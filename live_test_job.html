The Story Behind the Role

What is the most significant challenge this AI Architect will tackle in the first six months?

The key challenge will be to move beyond isolated experiments and build a cohesive, enterprise-wide AI roadmap. You won't just be designing systems; you'll be partnering with various departments to understand their unique needs, identify high-impact opportunities, and create a unified strategy that balances immediate wins with long-term, scalable transformation. It's a role that requires as much strategic diplomacy as it does technical expertise.

What's a quality you're looking for in a candidate that isn't explicitly listed in the qualifications?

We're looking for a 'pragmatic innovator.' The ideal candidate is someone who is deeply knowledgeable about cutting-edge AI but is equally skilled at applying it to real-world business problems. It’s not about using the fanciest new tool, but about selecting and implementing the *right* tool to drive measurable business value, improve security, and ensure compliance. We need a builder who can also think like a business strategist.

How does this role directly contribute to Ascend's core mission?

Ascend's mission is to drive business transformation for our clients. This role is the engine for that mission in the AI era. By shaping our internal AI strategy and building scalable solutions, you will directly empower our teams to be more efficient and innovative. This translates into more powerful, intelligent, and secure services for our clients, solidifying our position as a forward-thinking technology partner.

PURPOSE: The AI Architect shapes Ascend's enterprise AI strategy and roadmap through hands-on innovation and strategic design. This role partners with teams to identify and prioritize AI opportunities, turn grassroots experimentation into scalable solutions, and ensure outcomes are secure, compliant, and impactful. By leading through influence, the AI Architect accelerates responsible AI adoption across the company and drives measurable business transformation.

What You'll Do

  • Shape and evolve the company's AI strategy in alignment with business priorities.
  • Define vision, principles, and success metrics for AI adoption.
  • Act as an internal advocate and thought leader for responsible AI innovation.
  • Partner with departmental AI Champions to identify and prioritize high-value use cases.
  • Build and maintain a roadmap balancing quick wins with long-term transformation.
  • Stay current on AI/automation trends and industry benchmarks.
  • Prioritize initiatives based on business value, feasibility, and risk.
  • Prototype, test, and scale AI solutions across departments.
  • Ensure solutions are secure, compliant, and aligned with governance standards.
  • Educate internal teams on AI capabilities, use cases, and best practices.
  • Develop training, playbooks, and resources to build AI fluency across the company.
  • Define and track KPIs to measure effectiveness and ROI.
  • Use insights to refine strategy and accelerate adoption.
  • Report progress and outcomes to leadership.

What You'll Need

  • Experience with Generative AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, etc.).
  • Familiarity with enterprise AI platforms (ServiceNow Now Assist, Salesforce Einstein, Microsoft Copilot, Azure AI Foundry).
  • Skilled in prompt engineering, workflow design, and reusable playbooks.
  • Ability to prototype solutions using APIs, no/low-code, and automation tools.
  • Knowledge of RPA, workflow automation, and ITSM/CRM integrations.
  • Awareness of deployment practices to scale AI solutions securely and compliantly.

Application Insights
