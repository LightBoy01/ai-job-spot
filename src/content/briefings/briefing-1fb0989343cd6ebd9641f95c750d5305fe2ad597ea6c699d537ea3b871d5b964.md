### Summary\nAI vendors are increasingly publishing detailed threat intelligence on how their platforms are exploited, a significant shift from the historical lack of transparency in breach disclosures. However, this new openness has met with a divided response within the cybersecurity community; some professionals view it as a valuable learning resource, while others dismiss it as self-serving marketing.\n\n### Why It Matters\nThis trend represents a pivotal moment for AI security, offering critical real-world threat intelligence that directly impacts the security posture of AI deployments across all organizations. For AI professionals, these disclosures provide actionable insights into specific attack methodologies, enabling proactive hardening of systems, improved threat modeling, and more effective incident response tailored to AI-specific risks. The split reaction underscores a broader industry challenge: overcoming skepticism to leverage vendor transparency for collective defense. By critically engaging with this information, rather than dismissing it, the AI community can accelerate its maturity, moving security discussions from theoretical risks to practical, data-driven defense strategies. Embracing this collaborative learning is essential for building resilient and trustworthy AI ecosystems, preventing a fragmented security landscape where critical lessons are repeatedly re-learned.
