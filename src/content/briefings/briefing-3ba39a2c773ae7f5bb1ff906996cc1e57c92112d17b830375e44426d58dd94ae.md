<p>Welcome to our latest roundup of what's going on in the indie game space. This week saw the arrival of some fairly high-profile ports, a relaxing garden creation game and much more. I've even managed to tear myself away from <em>Football Manager 26</em> for long enough to tell you about some of them.</p><p>Barely a week goes by without a fun theme event taking place on Steam and one that runs until November 10 is also going down on the <a class="link" href="https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/sales-and-deals/fusion-festival/#sort=df&amp;p=0" data-i13n="cpos:1;pos:1">Nintendo eShop</a>. The <a class="link" href="https://store.steampowered.com/curator/45063983/sale/MetroidvaniaFusionFestival" data-i13n="cpos:2;pos:1">Metroidvania Fusion Festival</a> is a celebration of — what else? — the broad spectrum of Metroidvania games. It features 245 projects, including upcoming games and demos, and a sale with discounts of up to 90 percent.&nbsp;</p><p>I've grabbed a few demos to check out this weekend, including <a class="link" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/2878580/Key_Fairy/" data-i13n="cpos:3;pos:1"><em>Key Fairy</em></a> (a pacifist bullet-hell game that sees you grappling and dancing your way around enemies), <a class="link" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/2184080/Echo_Weaver/" data-i13n="cpos:4;pos:1"><em>Echo Weaver</em></a> (a time-loop puzzler that I've <a class="link" href="https://www.engadget.com/gaming/playdate-season-two-spray-paint-simulator-and-other-new-indie-games-worth-checking-out-110035699.html" data-i13n="cpos:5;pos:1">mentioned before</a>) and 2D platformer <a class="link" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1587380/Little_Ghost/" data-i13n="cpos:6;pos:1">Little Ghost</a>, in which you explore a mixed media world as a cute specter.</p><p>In terms of deals, <a class="link" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/774361/Blasphemous" data-i13n="cpos:7;pos:1"><em>Blasphemous</em></a> is 75 percent off, <a class="link" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/2114740/Blasphemous_2/" data-i13n="cpos:8;pos:1"><em>Blasphemous II</em></a> is half off, 2D action game <a class="link" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1580180/Gunbrella/" data-i13n="cpos:9;pos:1"><em>Gunbrella</em></a> (which I've had on my wishlist for years) is 65 percent off and body horror delight <a class="link" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/953490/CARRION/" data-i13n="cpos:10;pos:1"><em>Carrion</em></a> is 22 percent off. I can wholeheartedly recommend <a class="link" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1733250/Turbo_Kid/" data-i13n="cpos:11;pos:1"><em>Turbo Kid</em></a>, a quasi-sequel to one of my favorite films of the same name. That's 35 percent off in this sale.</p><p>Elsewhere this week, I read a solid profile of developer Lente Cuenen in the <a class="link" href="https://www.nytimes.com/2025/11/02/arts/spilled-lente-cuenen.html" data-i13n="cpos:12;pos:1"><em>The New York Times</em></a>. Cuenen is the developer of <a class="link" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/2240080/Spilled/" data-i13n="cpos:13;pos:1"><em>Spilled!</em></a>, a relaxing waterway cleanup game I've been meaning to check out for a while. The arcade action game debuted on Meta Quest and Apple Vision Pro last year. It made the leap to <a class="link" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/2702300/THRASHER" data-i13n="cpos:22;pos:1">Steam</a> (and SteamVR) this week, so you can now play it on a flatscreen for the first time.</p><p><em>Thrasher </em>is a very trippy-looking game from developer Puddle and publisher Creature Label. It seems like quite the wild ride and it was named Apple's Vision Pro game of the year for 2024. For what it’s worth. the team behind <em>Thrasher </em>says that the game runs at 90 fps on Steam Deck.</p><div><div style="left:0;width:100%;height:0;position:relative;padding-bottom:56.25%;"></div></div><p>Here's a completely different change of pace. <em>Dream Garden </em>is all about creating a relaxing garden space for yourself. You can use hills, rivers, ponds and pebbles to shape the landscape, then add decorations. With the rake tool, you can trace patterns in the sand.&nbsp;</p><p>There are no limits or timers in this Japanese-inspired game from the duo at Campfire Studio. <em>Dream Garden </em>is out now on <a class="link" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/3367600/Dream_Garden/" data-i13n="cpos:23;pos:1">Steam</a>.</p><div><div style="left:0;width:100%;height:0;position:relative;padding-bottom:56.25%;"></div></div><p><em>The Falconeer </em>first took flight five years ago — it was an <a class="link" href="https://www.engadget.com/the-falconeer-xbox-series-x-day-one-160037062.html" data-i13n="cpos:24;pos:1">Xbox Series X/S launch title</a>, fact fans. A new edition for Steam called <em>The Falconeer: Revolution Remaster </em>is a complete rework, as solo developer Tomas Sala rebuilt the game from the ground up. It features new tech, overhauled and expanded environments, gameplay upgrades and all of the previous DLC.</p><p>I'm glad to hear that Sala has improved the flight mechanics. I found it a bit tough to get to grips with the aerial combat when I played <em>The Falconeer</em> back in 2020, but I enjoyed it otherwise.&nbsp;</p><p>The remastered version (which looks much grander in scope than the original game) is available as a free upgrade for existing owners on <a class="link" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1135260/The_Falconeer_Revolution_Remaster/" data-i13n="cpos:25;pos:1">Steam</a>.
