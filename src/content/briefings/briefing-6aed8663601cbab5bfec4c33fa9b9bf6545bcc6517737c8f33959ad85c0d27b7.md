<p>It's been a <a data-i13n="cpos:1;pos:1" href="https://www.engadget.com/skate-story-hands-on-kick-push-shatter-120051976.html">long road to get here</a>, but the surreal skateboarding sim <em>Skate Story</em> <a data-i13n="cpos:2;pos:1" href="https://www.engadget.com/gaming/skate-story-finally-arrives-on-december-8-160911918.html">will actually be available to play</a> on December 8. The title will also be a <a data-i13n="cpos:3;pos:1" href="https://blog.playstation.com/2025/11/24/skate-story-launches-into-the-playstation-plus-game-catalog-on-dec-8-hands-on-report/">day-one Game Catalog release for PlayStation Plus subscribers</a>, which is a great perk for Sony fans. It'll be available on other platforms, like Steam and Nintendo Switch, but those versions cost $20. The PS5 version is free for PS Plus subscribers and it's not currently coming to Xbox.</p>
<div id="1bb91abbb2ee4cb681aa4247a26914e5"></div>
<p>For the uninitiated, <em>Skate Story</em> can only be described as a Lynchian take on skateboarding. Players control a glass avatar and they perform tricks to, well, swallow the moon and defeat the devil. It was announced all the way back in 2020. We got to play it last year and came away impressed. The game was made by Sam Eng, <a data-i13n="cpos:4;pos:1" href="https://switchplayer.net/2018/12/03/zarvot-review/">who was behind the indie shooter <em>Zarvot</em></a>.</p>
<span id="end-legacy-contents"></span><p>It's also being published by Devolver Digital, a company that knows its way around unique gameplay concepts. It published the <a data-i13n="cpos:5;pos:1" href="https://www.engadget.com/gaming/cult-of-the-lambs-next-dlc-is-woolhaven-out-in-early-2026-225129643.html">utterly sublime</a> <em>Cult of the Lamb</em> and <a data-i13n="cpos:6;pos:1" href="https://www.engadget.com/gaming/ball-x-pits-deeply-satisfying-grind-keeps-me-coming-back-for-more-171000754.html">one of our recent favorites</a>, <em>Ball x Pit</em>. The former lets you feed poop to cult followers, which makes performing kickflips as a glass-bodied demon seem downright normal.</p>
<div id="de555d529b6b41e0a0b8e49d15139bc0"></div>
<p>Looking to see what all of the fuss is about? There's a <a data-i13n="cpos:7;pos:1" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1263240/Skate_Story/">demo right now on Steam</a>. The game's also available for purchase on the PS5, for those who don't wanna pony up for a PS Plus subscription.</p>This article originally appeared on Engadget at https://www.engadget.com/gaming/playstation/skate-story-grinds-its-way-to-playstation-plus-on-december-8-193034939.html?src=rss
