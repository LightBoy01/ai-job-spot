### Summary\\nThe proliferation of AI agents is transforming computational paradigms, revealing that traditional cloud infrastructure, including serverless architectures, is inadequate for their unique requirements. This challenge is giving rise to specialized 'agent-native platforms' designed to offer efficient and adaptive solutions specifically tailored for intelligent, agent-driven applications.\\n\\n### Why It Matters\\nThis shift signals a critical inflection point for AI development and deployment. For AI professionals, it highlights the growing inadequacy of existing cloud paradigms, like serverless functions, which are primarily designed for stateless, event-driven, and often short-lived computations. AI agents, by contrast, often require persistent state, long-running processes, dynamic resource allocation, and complex inter-agent communication, making them a poor fit for traditional serverless models. The emergence of agent-native platforms isn't just an optimization; it represents a fundamental re-architecture of how intelligent systems are built and operated. Professionals must adapt by understanding these new architectural patterns, tools, and deployment strategies. Businesses that embrace these platforms early will gain a significant competitive advantage in deploying more robust, scalable, and sophisticated AI applications, while cloud providers are presented with a massive opportunity to innovate and capture this burgeoning market. This trend underscores the increasing maturity of AI, moving beyond simple model deployment to the orchestration of complex, autonomous systems that demand a new class of computational backbone.
