### Summary
Dai Nippon Printing (DNP) successfully rolled out ChatGPT Enterprise across ten critical departments in just three months, achieving remarkable operational improvements. This rapid deployment resulted in a 95% reduction in patent research time, a tenfold increase in processing volume, and an 87% automation rate. The initiative also maintained 100% weekly active user engagement and facilitated 70% knowledge reuse, showcasing highly effective integration of advanced AI into core business functions and demonstrating immediate, measurable impact.

This case study moves beyond theoretical discussions, providing tangible proof of what's possible when cutting-edge LLMs are integrated with agility and purpose.
