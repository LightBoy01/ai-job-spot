### Summary
The Ringer Movies podcast episode features Sean and Amanda discussing the potential Warner Bros. Discovery sale, speculating on mergers with Paramount, Comcast, or Netflix. They then offer a critical take on Colleen Hoover's new adaptation, *Regretting You*. The hosts also delve into Joachim Trier's film *Sentimental Value*, debating its varying emotional impact on viewers. In the final segment, Trier himself joins the conversation to reveal the personal narrative woven into Stellan Skarsgård's character, the importance of observation in character development, and his memorable experience filming in his childhood neighborhood.
