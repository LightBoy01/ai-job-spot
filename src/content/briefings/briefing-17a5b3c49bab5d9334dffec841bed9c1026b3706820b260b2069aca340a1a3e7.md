### Summary\nThe 28th European Conference on Artificial Intelligence (ECAI-2025) convened in Bologna, Italy, from October 25-30, 2025. The initial two days, October 25-26, were dedicated to pre-conference activities including workshops, tutorials, and a doctoral consortium, leading into the main conference program which ran from October 27-30. This briefing provides a curated collection of social media posts from attendees, aiming to offer a dynamic snapshot and a \"flavor\" of the event's key discussions and atmosphere.\n\n### Why It Matters\nThis social media digest of ECAI 2025 offers a crucial, real-time pulse on the European AI landscape, providing insights far beyond a simple event recap. For professionals in the AI space, it serves as an unfiltered lens into the most debated topics, emerging research frontiers, and prevailing industry sentiment directly from the conference floor. This \"ground-level\" perspective is invaluable for quickly identifying nascent trends, understanding the practical implications and perceived value of academic research, and gauging the community's engagement with specific AI applications or ethical concerns. It empowers practitioners and researchers alike to assess which ideas are gaining traction, spot potential collaborators, and stay ahead of the curve in a rapidly evolving field, often before official proceedings or papers are widely published. Essentially, it provides a collective intelligence snapshot, highlighting what truly resonated with and energized the AI community at one of Europe's most significant AI gatherings.
