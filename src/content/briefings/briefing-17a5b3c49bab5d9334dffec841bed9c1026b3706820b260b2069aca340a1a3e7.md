### Summary\\nThe 28th European Conference on Artificial Intelligence (ECAI-2025) took place in Bologna, Italy, from October 25-30, 2025. The event's initial two days were dedicated to workshops, tutorials, and a doctoral consortium, with the main conference running from October 27-30. This briefing serves as an introduction to a collection of social media posts, aiming to provide a snapshot of the conference experience from attendees' perspectives.\\n\\n### Why It Matters\\nFor professionals in the rapidly evolving AI landscape, events like ECAI-2025 are far more than just academic gatherings; they are critical pulse checks on the industry's direction, emerging trends, and collaborative opportunities within the European context. This particular \"social media round up\" approach underscores a significant shift in how information and sentiment are shared and consumed in fast-paced fields like AI. Beyond formal paper presentations, the immediate, often unfiltered, reactions and discussions on social media offer a real-time barometer of what truly resonated with attendees, which topics sparked the most debate, and what new research or applications are gaining traction. Such insights are invaluable for anticipating future research funding priorities, understanding evolving ethical considerations specific to European AI policy, and identifying potential collaborators or talent. Furthermore, the inclusion of workshops, tutorials, and a doctoral consortium highlights the commitment to nurturing the next generation of AI researchers and ensuring continuous knowledge transfer. For an AI professional, monitoring these events, even through informal channels, is essential for staying abreast of the cutting edge, validating strategic directions, and maintaining a competitive advantage in a global AI economy.
