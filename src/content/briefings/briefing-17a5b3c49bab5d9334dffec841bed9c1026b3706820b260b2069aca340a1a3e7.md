### Summary\nThe 28th European Conference on Artificial Intelligence (ECAI-2025) convened in Bologna, Italy, from October 25-30, 2025. The initial two days, October 25-26, were dedicated to workshops, tutorials, and a doctoral consortium, leading into the main conference program which ran from October 27-30. This briefing indicates an intention to capture the essence of the event through a collection of social media posts from attendees.\n\n### Why It Matters\nECAI stands as a cornerstone event in the European AI ecosystem, providing a critical forum for the continent's leading researchers, academics, and industry professionals. For anyone deeply involved in the AI space, understanding the pulse of such a major conference is essential for discerning the latest breakthroughs, identifying emerging research trajectories, and anticipating future challenges and opportunities in the field. Beyond the formal presentations, the explicit mention of leveraging social media posts highlights a significant trend: the democratization of conference insights. This approach allows for a broader, more immediate dissemination of key ideas, diverse perspectives, and real-time engagement that extends beyond physical attendance. It underscores the growing importance of informal, community-driven communication channels in shaping and reflecting the discourse around AI's ethical implications, technological advancements, and societal impact. Professionals can leverage these aggregated insights to stay abreast of European-specific AI policy discussions, research funding priorities, and collaborative opportunities, ensuring they remain at the forefront of a rapidly evolving global AI landscape.
