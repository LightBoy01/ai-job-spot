It's quick, ultra-maneuverable and beginner-friendly. The learning curve here isn't that steep.</p> <span id="end-legacy-contents"></span>
<p>It boasts four propeller guards, for safety, and the built-in 4K camera certainly gets the job done. It's extremely light, weighing just 135 grams. This makes it easy to transport, sure, but also means that the Neo is exempt from drone license requirements.</p> 
<p>This version doesn't ship with a dedicated controller, though there is a combo pack for that, but that doesn't really matter here. The Neo can be maneuvered by hand gestures, human voice, mobile app or standard RC controller.</p>
