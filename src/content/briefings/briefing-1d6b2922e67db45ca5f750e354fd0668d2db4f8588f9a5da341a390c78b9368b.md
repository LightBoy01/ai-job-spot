### Summary\
Ubisoft CEO Yves Guillemot clarified recent market speculation, confirming that Chinese tech giant Tencent is neither pursuing a full acquisition of Ubisoft nor withdrawing from their existing $1.25 billion partnership. The recent delay in Ubisoft's H1 2025-2026 financial report was attributed to new auditors implementing changes in the company's fiscal accounting methods, rather than any corporate instability or deal changes. The underlying context, hinted at by the original article's title, suggests that the Assassin's Creed franchise continues to be a critical performance driver for Ubisoft.\
