This is a redesign of the OnePlus 15, which is a device <a data-i13n="cpos:2;pos:1" href="https://www.engadget.com/mobile/smartphones/oneplus-15-review-a-great-phone-if-photography-isnt-a-priority-143000489.html"><ins>we rather enjoyed</ins></a>. The 15R will presumably be a budget-friendly version of the flagship 15, <a data-i13n="cpos:3;pos:1" href="https://www.androidcentral.com/phones/oneplus/i-dont-believe-any-phone-delivers-better-value-than-the-oneplus-13r"><ins>based on past releases</ins></a>, but we don't have a price just yet. It is, however, being referred to as "the ultimate value high quality flagship device."</p>
<p>The company hasn't dropped any real specs just yet, but we do know that this handset prioritizes durability. As a matter of fact, OnePlus says the 15R has "the industry's most complete durability ratings of IP66, IP68, IP69 and IP69K." This phone should take a licking and keep on ticking.</p>

<p>It'll be available in two colors. There's a black one and a mint green design. The phone launches on December 17, which doesn't leave OnePlus a lot of time to provide specs and pricing information. Only customers in Europe, India and North America will be able to buy the handset at first.</p>
<figure><img src="https://s.yimg.com/os/creatr-uploaded-images/2025-11/6316fbf0-c951-11f0-a6bf-4c6258cf16ba" data-crop-orig-src="https://s.yimg.com/os/creatr-uploaded-images/2025-11/6316fbf0-c951-11f0-a6bf-4c6258cf16ba" style="height:1414px;width:2282px;" alt="Three tablets." data-uuid="dcdd4053-6fb1-3295-b7b5-78f1a9535617"><figcaption></figcaption><div class="photo-credit">OnePlus </div></figure>
<p>OnePlus also just revealed the Pad Go 2, which looks like a decent enough mid-range tablet. It boasts an anti-glare finish and is available in both black and purple. This will be the first tablet from the company to offer 5G connectivity. It comes with an integrated stylus, which has been "designed to enhance productivity and precision for note-taking and creative work." It also drops on December 17 in Europe, India and North America.</p>

<p>The OnePlus Watch Lite is a budget-friendly version of its <a data-i13n="cpos:4;pos:1" href="https://www.engadget.com/oneplus-just-unveiled-a-new-full-sized-tablet-with-some-decent-specs-140023477.html"><ins>flagship smartwatch</ins></a>, offering top-tier "health and fitness features to an even more accessible price point." We don't have that price point just yet.
