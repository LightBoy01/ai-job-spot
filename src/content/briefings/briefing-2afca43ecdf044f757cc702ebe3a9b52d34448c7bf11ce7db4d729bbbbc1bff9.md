### Summary\
OnePlus has announced three new devices launching December 17: the budget-friendly and highly durable OnePlus 15R smartphone, featuring industry-leading IP durability ratings and aimed at the \"ultimate value\" segment; the mid-range OnePlus Pad Go 2 tablet, which introduces 5G connectivity and an integrated stylus for productivity; and the OnePlus Watch Lite, a more accessible version of its flagship smartwatch with advanced health and fitness tracking. While specific pricing and full specs are pending, all devices target enhanced value and specific markets, with the 15R and Pad Go 2 launching in Europe, India, and North America, and the Watch Lite exclusively in Europe.\
\
