<p>Apple's AirTag is a small tracker that helps you monitor your essentials from keys and wallets to luggage and backpacks. To make the most of it, you'll want the right accessories to keep it secure and easy to attach. The best AirTag accessories protect your tracker, add style and make it easier to fit it into your daily routine.<br><br>There's a wide range of options depending on how you use your AirTag. Some holders are slim enough to slip into a wallet while others are rugged enough to clip onto a bike or a pet collar. You can find leather key rings that match your Apple gear, silicone loops for gym bags and hard cases that stand up to travel or outdoor use. Many come in different colors and finishes so you can match them to your style while keeping your items easy to find.<br><br>From minimalist inserts to heavy-duty mounts, these AirTag accessories combine form and function to keep your tracker protected and your belongings within reach.</p>

<h2 id="jump-link-airtag-holder-faqs">AirTag holder FAQs</h2> 
<h3 id="jump-link-why-do-airtags-need-a-holder">Why do AirTags need a holder?</h3> 
<p>AirTags need a holder because they do not have built-in keyring holes like Tile, Chipolo and other Bluetooth trackers do.</p> <span id="end-legacy-contents"></span>
<h3 id="jump-link-how-do-you-attach-an-airtag-to-things">How do you attach an AirTag to things?</h3> 
<p>You'll need a holder or case to attach an AirTag to your stuff. If you're comfortable slipping an AirTag into an interior pocket of a bag or coat, you can do so without an extra accessory. But if you want to use one to keep track of your keys, wallet, backpack or even your pet on their collar, you'll need an accessory that can accommodate that use case.</p> 
<h3 id="jump-link-do-airtag-holders-affect-tracking-performance">Do AirTag holders affect tracking performance?</h3> 
<p>No, AirTag holders should not affect tracking performance.</p> 
<h3 id="jump-link-do-airtag-holders-protect-against-scratches-and-impacts">Do AirTag holders protect against scratches and impacts?</h3> 
<p>Yes, AirTag holders can protect against scratches and impacts. Many AirTag cases encircle the edge of the tracker only, leave the two disk sides exposed.
