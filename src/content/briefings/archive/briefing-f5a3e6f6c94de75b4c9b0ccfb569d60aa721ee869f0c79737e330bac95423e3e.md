Yet Europe isn’t capital-poor: households save €1.4tn a year, nearly twice as much as in America. Still, very little of that money finds its way into startups, despite a plethora of incentives like the UK’s EIS tax relief for business angels.&nbsp;&nbsp; Even when funding is available, Europe’s venture capital firms are slow and cautious.
