### Summary\nSony is offering significant Black Friday discounts on all PlayStation 5 models, including the Digital Edition ($399), standard PS5 ($449), and the high-performance PS5 Pro ($649), each receiving a $100 price reduction. These deals are available directly from Sony and major retailers like Amazon and Walmart. The article highlights the consoles' core features, such as fast load times, smooth frame rates, and crisp visuals powered by custom AMD Zen 2 and RDNA 2 architecture. Beyond consoles, discounts extend to accessories like the DualSense Wireless Controller ($55) and the premium DualSense Edge controller ($169), as well as the PlayStation Portal remote player ($179). Increased console sales mean a larger audience experiencing these AI-driven features, thereby driving demand for more advanced in-game AI capabilities and accelerating the real-time deployment of complex AI models in consumer products.
