In the era of AI agents, most organizations could benefit from AX (agent experience), the discipline to prepare an organization to successfully integrate agents as collaborative partners with the human workforce. Learn more about these five specific AX skills: knowledge curation, change management, critical thinking, interaction skills, and agent oversight.
