<img src="https://res.infoq.com/news/2025/11/apple-pico-banana-image-editing/en/headerimage/apple-pico-banana-400-1762171306715.jpeg"><p>Pico-Banana-400K is a curated dataset of 400,000 images developed by Apple researchers to make it easier to create text-guided image editing models. The images were generated using Google's Nano-Banana to modify real photographs from the Open Images collecion and were then filtered using Gemini-2.5-Pro based on their overall quality and prompt compliance.</p> <i>By Sergio De Simone</i>
