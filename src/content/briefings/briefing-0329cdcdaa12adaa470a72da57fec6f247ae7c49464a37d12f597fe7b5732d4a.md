### Summary\nThis briefing announces a series of free, virtual AI and machine learning seminars scheduled from November 3 to December 31, 2025. These accessible online events cover diverse topics, exemplified by an early session on November 3, 2025, featuring Agni Orfanoudaki discussing \"Machine Learning Informed POMDPs for Intensive Stroke Care.\"\n\n### Why It Matters\nFor professionals in the AI space, these seminars represent an invaluable opportunity for continuous learning and professional development in a rapidly evolving field. Staying current with cutting-edge research, methodologies like POMDPs, and their applications—such as in critical healthcare scenarios like stroke care—is crucial for driving innovation and maintaining competitive relevance. Beyond specific technical insights, these free, virtual events democratize access to expert knowledge, allowing a global audience to engage with leading thinkers and emerging trends without geographical or financial barriers. Attending such seminars is not just about gaining knowledge; it's about spotting the next big wave in AI, understanding how academic breakthroughs translate into real-world impact, and fostering a culture of lifelong learning essential for anyone shaping the future of artificial intelligence.
