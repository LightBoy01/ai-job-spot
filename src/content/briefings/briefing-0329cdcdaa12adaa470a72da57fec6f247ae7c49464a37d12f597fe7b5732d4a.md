### Summary\nThis briefing highlights a series of free, virtual seminars focused on AI and machine learning, scheduled to run from November 3 to December 31, 2025. A notable event detailed is \"To Scan or Not to Scan? Machine Learning Informed POMDPs for Intensive Stroke Care,\" presented by Agni Orfanoudaki on November 3, 2025.\n\n### Why It Matters\nThese virtual seminars serve as a crucial conduit for AI professionals to remain at the forefront of a rapidly evolving industry. By offering free and accessible online events, they significantly democratize access to cutting-edge research, methodologies, and thought leadership from around the globe. This enables a wider professional audience, irrespective of geographical location or financial constraints, to engage with the latest advancements. The highlighted seminar on AI-informed POMDPs for stroke care exemplifies the increasing application of sophisticated machine learning techniques in critical, real-world domains like healthcare. For professionals, participating in such events is not just about staying informed; it's an invaluable opportunity for continuous learning, identifying emerging trends, and potentially fostering new ideas or collaborative ventures, which are all essential for driving innovation and ensuring career growth within the dynamic AI landscape.
