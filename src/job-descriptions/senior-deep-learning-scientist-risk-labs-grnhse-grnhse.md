The Role
<p>Since Block's inception, our innovative and technology-forward approach to risk management and customer protection has been fundamental to how we invent and build financial products. The Risk team at Block continues this legacy through a sophisticated, technology and science-led approach to protecting our customers and their funds. Our interdisciplinary structure combines Product Development, Science teams (specializing in modeling, analytics, and data science), Operations and key partners including Legal, Compliance and Policy, all working in concert to identify, assess, and solve complex risk challenges across access, fraud prevention and compliance.</p>
<p>This role is part of our Risk Labs team - this team’s mission is aimed at developing advanced deep learning and reinforcement learning based signals and learned representations for machine learning models. In this role, you’ll explore, develop and implement alternatives to traditional feature based ML with state-of-the-art models for solving risk problems. This team works across a wide-swath of the Risk organization touching a variety of use cases and collaborating with a diverse set of teams.</p>

What You'll Be Up To
<ul>
<li>Reporting to the lead of the Risk Labs Modeling team</li>
<li>Dreaming up, designing, and running big experiments to quickly test out cool (and practical) ideas</li>
<li>Applying the latest theoretical breakthroughs to make our products even better</li>
<li>Building experiments, prototypes, and architectural designs across computer science fields like ML, data mining, NLP, and performance analysis</li>
<li>Contributing to the growth of new initiatives by sharing emerging trends and best practices, fostering a collaborative and innovative environment</li>
<li>Taking the lead on defining data structures, frameworks, designs, and evaluation metrics for your research solutions – you'll have a lot of autonomy here</li>
<li>Teaming up with folks inside and outside Block to find new research areas and shape our long-term strategies</li>
<li>Running experiments for your research, using simulations and prototypes to see what happens</li>
<li>Refining how we do research, solidifying your hypotheses, and helping evolve our practices with minimal hand-holding</li>
</ul>
