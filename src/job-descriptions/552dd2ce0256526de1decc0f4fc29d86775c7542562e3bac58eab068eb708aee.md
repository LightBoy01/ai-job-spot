Koch Companies is looking for an **Application Developer** who’s ready to take ownership of high-impact projects that power our transportation, warehousing, and logistics operations. You’ll build tools from the ground up, modernize existing systems, and collaborate with a small, experienced team to solve real business problems. This is a hands-on development role! **Expect to code in C# (Windows service applications and WinForms), MVC, JSON API, and modern web technologies**. We're also looking for someone who stays current and is already **experimenting with or implementing AI in their work**. Your insights will help shape how we bring next-gen solutions into our business. This role starts in-office for onboarding and transitions to a hybrid schedule (3 days in, 2 days remote) once you’re up to speed.

**Location**: This position is onsite at our corporate office in **Golden Valley, MN** | Hybrid (after 90 days)

**Who are you?**

You’re a self-driven developer who thrives in a fast-paced environment and enjoys solving meaningful problems. You take full ownership of your work and don’t need constant direction to meet goals or deadlines. You’re collaborative, but you’re also comfortable working independently when the task calls for it.

You pick up new tools quickly and adapt when priorities shift. You’re already exploring or leveraging AI in your personal or professional life and are eager to help implement next-gen technology in real business environments.

You care about building clean, maintainable code that aligns with business needs. You’re the kind of person who speaks up, asks questions, and contributes ideas that move the team forward.

**What you'll do:**

As an Application Developer, you'll:

*   Lead impactful projects like rebuilding our customer portal from scratch
    
*   Develop API integrations for warehousing solutions
    
*   Create user interfaces for EDI systems
    
*   Write modern, efficient code in C#, MVC, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Angular, and APIs
    
*   Work closely with SQL DBAs to streamline application–database communication
    
*   Troubleshoot and resolve software issues
    
*   Join the IT helpdesk on-call rotation (approx. 3–4 weeks per year)
    
*   Stay current with industry standards and evaluate AI tools or automations that can improve performance
    

**Knowledge and Skills to be successful:**

*   Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science or related field.
*   3+ years of software development experience.
*   Strong proficiency with C#, MVC, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Angular, and SOAP/RESTful APIs.
*   Experience working with SQL databases.
*   Experience developing mobile or web applications 
*   Solid troubleshooting, analytical, and problem-solving abilities.
*   Strong problem-solving and project management skills.
*   Excellent interpersonal and communication skills.
*   A forward-thinking mindset with an interest in leveraging AI tools and automation to improve development workflows and business efficiency

Preferred, but not required:

*   Background in transportation or warehousing
    
*   Project management experience
    
*   Programming certifications
    
*   Prior experience integrating or piloting AI-based technologies in development work
