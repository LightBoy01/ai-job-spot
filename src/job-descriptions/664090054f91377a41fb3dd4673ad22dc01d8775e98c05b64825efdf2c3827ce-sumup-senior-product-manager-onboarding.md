At SumUp, the **Lifecycle tribe** shapes the very first moments merchants have with our products, and every meaningful interaction that follows. We design the journeys that help merchants discover, onboard, and engage with SumUp across web and mobile, ensuring their path from signup to success is simple, compliant, and genuinely delightful.

As part of this team, you'll work at the heart of our ecosystem, tackling complex cross-market challenges, collaborating with Engineering, Design, Compliance, and Operations, and building scalable systems that drive adoption and long-term engagement.

Join us as a **Senior Product Manager** to craft experiences that matter and deliver real impact for millions of merchants worldwide.

**What You'll Do**

*   **Own the Strategy:** Own the strategy and roadmap for merchant onboarding, product onboarding, upsell/cross-sell, and engagement features.
*   **Develop domain expertise:** Become the go-to expert for compliance regulations, merchant profiles, verification methods, SumUp product ecosystem and technical product architecture.
*   **Drive Product Delivery:** Contribute to the product roadmap and squad-level OKRs, supporting the development and delivery of in-flight features while ensuring smooth execution.
*   **Build Partnerships:** Work closely with other product leaders and stakeholders across Operations, Compliance, Identity and Verification to navigate trade-offs and build scalable, compliant systems.
*   **Communicate with Impact:** Clearly communicate your product vision, priorities, and progress to senior leadership and stakeholders, ensuring alignment on investment decisions.
*   **Understand the User:** Engage with key stakeholders across all markets and acquisition channels to resolve friction points, identify user problems, and translate insights into actionable product requirements that improve the merchant experience.
*   **Prioritise for Impact:** Use data and insights to identify gaps and opportunities and make informed prioritisation decisions, ensuring focus on high-value outcomes within strict compliance timelines.

**You'll be great for this role if you have**

*   Demonstrated success delivering customer-facing products in complex, multi-market environments. 
*   Ability to navigate multi-system environments and drive a clear, strategic product roadmap that accounts for complex dependencies.
*   Solid technical fluency that enables you to partner closely with engineers; comfortable understanding system behavior, APIs, and contributing to solution discussions.
*   Solid data analysis skills to uncover opportunities, identify gaps and guide evidence-based product decisions.
*   Proven track record of building trust, aligning stakeholders across markets, and engaging senior leaders to ensure teams have the clarity and resources needed to deliver.

**Nice to Have**

*   A design background or experience designing complex product systems and user journeys.
*   A strong understanding of KYC topics within the onboarding domain.
*   Fintech product experience.
