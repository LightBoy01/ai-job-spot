<p>The Team &amp; Role</p>
<p>This position leads the technical and data analytics process for Fraud tools on the subscription payment platform.&nbsp; The role entails the comprehensive analysis of extensive datasets to derive actionable intelligence and provide prompt, high-level support to stakeholders. Key responsibilities encompass meticulous data interrogation, detailed reporting, and timely response to internal and client support inquiries. A background in finance or credit cards payment flows, with demonstrable experience in payment fraud, is highly desirable. The successful candidate will possess exceptional attention to detail, robust self-discipline, and the capacity to meet rigorous deadlines within a dynamic environment. Strong client-facing communication skills are imperative, involving direct client engagement to present analytical findings, lead strategic discussions, and ensure comprehensive fulfillment of their needs. This role also requires coordination with internal resources and the strategic guidance of tool capabilities integration into the payment platform.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>This is a remote position, so you’ll be working remotely while occasionally getting together with your team for office visits, events, or offsites. Of course, you’re welcome to come into the office more often if you’re nearby.&nbsp;</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>What you’ll do</p>
<ul>
<li>Conduct in-depth analysis of data pertaining to payment systems, credit card transactions, and fraud detection to discern trends, patterns, and actionable insights.</li>
<li>Develop and optimize Python scripts, SQL queries, and SQL scripts for sophisticated data manipulation, extraction, and analysis.</li>
<li>Utilize SQL Server Management Studio (or equivalent software) for advanced database management and complex query execution.</li>
<li>Generate comprehensive data reports and visualizations for internal stakeholders and clients.</li>
<li>Engage in collaborative efforts with cross-functional teams to facilitate data-driven strategic decision-making.</li>
<li>Lead formal client meetings and effectively communicate data insights with clarity and precision, ensuring utmost client satisfaction.&nbsp; Comfortable with various levels of client interactions up to and including C level decision makers.</li>
<li>Rigorous analysis of data related to payment systems, credit card transactions, and fraud detection.</li>
<li>Manage and adhere to Service Level Agreements (SLAs) concerning data-related support tickets from clients and internal teams.</li>
<li>Ensure the integrity and reliability of data models, dashboards, and reports, concurrently maintaining stringent customer data security protocols.</li>
<li>Lead team of Data analysts as capacity scales</li>
<li>Implement AI programming techniques to construct predictive models and enhance analytical capabilities.</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Your experience</p>
<ul>
<li>Bachelor’s degree in Data Sciences, Finance, Computer Science, or a closely related discipline (Master’s degree preferred).</li>
<li>Seven (7) or more years of professional experience in data analysis, ideally within the finance, credit card, or payment systems sector.</li>
<li>Extensive familiarity with Rule-Based Risk modeling and Payment Fraud tools.</li>
<li>Superior time management and self-discipline, with a proven ability to consistently meet demanding deadlines.</li>
<li>Substantial experience with data analysis tools, including Python, SQL, and SQL Server Management Studio (or similar).</li>
<li>Familiarity with AI programming and machine learning applications.</li>
<li>Proficiency in database management systems, such as MySQL, PostgreSQL, JSON,and NoSQL.</li>
<li>Demonstrated ability to translate intricate data into actionable insights and articulate these findings in a clear, concise, and professional manner.</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Nice to haves:</p>
<ul>
<li>Experience with machine learning or predictive analytics.</li>
<li>Proficiency with business intelligence tools like Tableau and Power BI.</li>
<li>Advanced knowledge of fraud analytics or payment system regulatory compliance.</li>
</ul>
