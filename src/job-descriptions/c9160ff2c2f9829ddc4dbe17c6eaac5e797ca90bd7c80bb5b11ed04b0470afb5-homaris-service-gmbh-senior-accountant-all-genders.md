Working closely with existing accountants, you will contribute to the correct execution and booking of all necessary payments throughout the company. By leveraging modern software solutions, you will actively accelerate processes. You will play a central role within the company, maintaining constant communication and exchange with other departments.\\n\\nHomaris champions diversity and encourages applications from individuals of all genders, cultures, and backgrounds. We also provide attractive benefits such as employer contributions to company pension schemes, a monthly tax-free digital voucher, subsidized healthy lunches, a Deutschlandticket for public transport, Jobrad leasing, employee discounts on Homaris apartments, modern work equipment, and complimentary mental health coaching for you and your family.
