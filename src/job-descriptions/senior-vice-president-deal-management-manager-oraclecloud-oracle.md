<p>We’re seeking a future team member for the role of&nbsp;<b>Senior Vice President, Deal Management Manager</b>&nbsp;to join our&nbsp;<b>Wealth Services Platform</b>&nbsp;<b>RFP</b> team. This role is located in&nbsp;<i>Lake Mary, Florida and Jersey City, NJ</i></p>
<p><b>In this role, you’ll make an impact in the following ways:</b></p>
<ul>
 <li>Lead, mentor, and grow the RFP team, driving performance, accountability, and professional development.</li>
 <li>Define and execute the overall RFP strategy aligned with business growth objectives.</li>
 <li>Partner with senior business leaders, product teams, and client relationship managers to position the firm competitively in RFP responses.</li>
 <li>Oversee the end-to-end RFP lifecycle: intake, analysis, response development, quality review, submission, and post-mortem feedback.</li>
 <li>Ensure responses are accurate, compliant, and differentiated, reflecting the firm’s strengths in wealth and financial services.</li>
 <li>Maintain a centralized knowledge base and content library for efficient and consistent RFP production.</li>
 <li>Own and manage RFPs for top opportunities, personally leading process and quality assurance for BAU RFPs as a player/coach.</li>
 <li>Implement and leverage AI-driven RFP automation tools (e.g., RFPIO, Loopio, or custom AI solutions) to increase efficiency and accuracy.</li>
 <li>Explore opportunities to improve generative AI and natural language processing for drafting, content tagging, and knowledge management.</li>
 <li>Collaborate with AI Hub and innovation teams to integrate AI, data analytics, and workflow automation into the RFP process.</li>
 <li>Act as a strategic advisor to business development and client coverage teams on proposal positioning.</li>
 <li>Partner with subject matter experts (investment, operations, compliance, and technology) to ensure responses are aligned with client needs.</li>
 <li>Represent the RFP team in senior leadership meetings and cross-functional committees.</li>
 <li>Establish KPIs and reporting to measure RFP success rates, turnaround times, and client feedback.</li>
 <li>Continuously improve processes, templates, and content management through technology and best practices.</li>
 <li>Ensure compliance with regulatory, legal, and brand standards.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>To be successful in this role, we’re seeking the following:</b></p>
<ul>
 <li>8+ years of experience in RFP management within financial services, asset/wealth management, or banking.</li>
 <li>Proven track record of leading and scaling RFP teams.</li>
 <li>Strong understanding of wealth management products, investment solutions, and financial regulations.</li>
 <li>Expertise in AI/ML applications, automation tools, and knowledge management systems.</li>
 <li>Exceptional project management, writing, and communication skills.</li>
 <li>Strong stakeholder management and ability to influence senior executives.</li>
 <li>Strategic thinker with an innovation mindset.</li>
 <li>Attention to detail and deep technical acumen in AI and automation tools.</li>
 <li>Exceptional leadership, coaching abilities, and people development skills.</li>
 <li>Ability to translate complex financial concepts into client-centric messaging.</li>
 <li>Resilient, agile, highly collaborative, and able to work under pressure with tight deadlines.</li>
 <li>Voracious learner.</li>
</ul>
