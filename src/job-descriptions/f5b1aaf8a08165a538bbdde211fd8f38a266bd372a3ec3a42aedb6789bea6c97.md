**Senior Power Electronics Engineer**
**Responsibilities**
1.  **Power Electronics Product Development**
*   Responsible for solution design, component selection, and circuit development of power electronics products.
*   Responsible for topology design, magnetic component design, and thermal design to ensure system performance and reliability.
1.  **Technical Verification & Testing**
*   Organize and perform electrical performance, safety, and EMC testing; drive prototype verification and functional debugging.
*   Analyze and resolve electrical, thermal, and EMI/EMC issues during the development process.
1.  **Standards & Compliance**
*   Familiar with and able to apply international/domestic power electronics standards (e.g., IEC, UL, GB), ensuring designs meet safety and quality requirements.
*   Keep track of cutting-edge technologies and component trends in the power electronics industry and apply them in product development.
1.  **Team Support & Guidance**
*   Provide technical training and guidance for junior and mid-level engineers.
*   Participate in technical reviews and patent writing to enhance the team’s overall R&D capability.
**Requirements**
*   Bachelor’s degree or above in Power Electronics, Automation, or related majors.
*   Over 5 years of experience in power electronics product R&D; experience in telecom power supply, AI server power, HVDC, PV/energy storage inverters, or EV chargers is preferred.
*   Proficient in common power electronics topologies (e.g., Buck/Boost, LLC, Full Bridge, Three-Level), with expertise in magnetic component design and thermal simulation.
*   Familiar with power semiconductor devices (IGBT, SiC, GaN) and their driver design.
*   Experience with DSP applications, hardware-software co-debugging; familiarity with Matlab, Mathcad is a plus.
*   Good English communication skills, able to work smoothly with overseas R&D teams.
*   Strong innovation, analytical, and problem-solving abilities.
