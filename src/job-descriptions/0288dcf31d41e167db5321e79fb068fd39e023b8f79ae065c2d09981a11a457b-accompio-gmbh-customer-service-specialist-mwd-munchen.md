### Description
accompio is a Private-Equity-backed IT group, consisting of approximately 700 specialists across 20 locations in Germany, Hungary, and Bulgaria. We are building a strong powerhouse of experts to provide new perspectives for our customers and employees. We support companies in their digitalization journey, making their processes future-proof, from IT infrastructure and cloud solutions to DevOps and Cyber Security. We leverage modern technologies like container platforms, Public Cloud Services, Next-Gen Security Platforms, and Infrastructure Automation to create scalable systems with the highest quality and performance.

We are currently seeking a full-time Customer Service Specialist (m/f/d) for our Grasbrunn near Munich location. In this role, you will be the first point of contact for our customers, ensuring professional initial consultation, coordinating inquiries, documenting information in our ticket system, and resolving simple IT issues like password resets or basic Office 365 and Active Directory tasks.

### Responsibilities
*   Be the first voice for our customers, taking incoming calls and providing professional, friendly initial consultations.
*   Ensure the telephone availability of our service hotline and coordinate incoming requests in a structured and reliable manner.
*   Record customer inquiries, prioritize them, and meticulously document all important information in the ticket system.
*   Create and maintain tickets, forward them to internal departments, and actively track their processing status.
*   Ideally, independently resolve simple IT issues such as password resets, basic Office 365 topics, or simple Active Directory tasks.
*   Keep our customers informed about the status and progress of their inquiries, communicating clearly and understandably.

### Qualifications
*   You communicate clearly, friendly, and confidently.
*   You are service-oriented, take responsibility, and genuinely want to help customers.
*   You are empathetic and professional, and can explain technical information understandably.
*   You work in a structured manner and are familiar with CRM or ticket systems.
*   Ideally, you have experience in Customer Service, IT Support, or Service Desk, or possess a strong IT affinity.
*   You have basic IT knowledge, e.g., Office 365 – and ideally Active Directory (e.g., creating users, resetting passwords).
