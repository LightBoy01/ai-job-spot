**Intern - Autonomation Engineer**

(Based at Katunayake)

MAS Active is a division of the larger MAS Group, specializing in manufacturing athleisure and sportswear designed for agility, comfort, and performance. MAS Active has set up operations across Sri Lanka, the Dominican Republic, and Italy. Serving top brands like lululemon, Patagonia, Lacoste, Ralph Lauren, and Gymshark, MAS Active blends high-performance sportswear with fashion, and is innovating in the space of wearable products that track fitness metrics and workout analytics.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Design and develop digital automation solutions for manual operations.
*   Implementation and maintenance of projects both hardware and software.
*   Carry out PCB designing and electrical wiring for developed machines.
*   Machine assembly and testing.
*   Support ongoing automation projects.
*   Maintaining 5S/Workshop Safety standards.

**Knowledge and Skills**

*   Undergraduate or Graduate in Electronics or Mechatronics engineering or Autonomation.
*   Basic electronics and electrical knowledge along with sensors and actuators.
*   Knowledge in Embedded systems, Arduino, PLC and HMI Programming.
*   Knowledge in IOT, RPA, AI and ML, Cloud Computing, Microsoft Power Platform.
*   Machine design knowledge and manufacturing knowledge.
