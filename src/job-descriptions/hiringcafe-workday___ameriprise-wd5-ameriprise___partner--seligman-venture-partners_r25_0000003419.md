Investing Partner for the for the Venture Capital team will focus on investing in early to mid-stage investments in cloud infrastructure, artificial intelligence and machine learning, and cyber security companies.

**Responsibilities**

*   Lead sourcing and execution of venture investments.
*   Represent the firm, conferences, and thought leadership forums. Serve as a board member of technology companies, providing both business and technical advice to portfolio company founders.
*   Coordinate with legal, finance, and external advisors to finalize deals.
*   Oversee portfolio management, including performance tracking, governance, and exit strategies.

  
**Requirements**

*   5+ years' experience in operational roles in a significant public or private technology company.
*   5+ years' experience in technology company.
*   5+ years' experience investing at a venture capital firm .
*   Deep domain knowledge of technology sub-sectors (cloud infrastructure, artificial intelligence and machine learning, and cyber security companies) gained through 5+ years of experience working in the technology industry and through education in related technical disciplines of computer science and software engineering / coding.
*   Must be comfortable with analyzing the investment merits of private technology companies, valuing those companies in a disciplined manner, and generating strong investment returns for stakeholders. In addition, the Investing Partner needs to be well-spoken and persuasive to be chosen to lead funding rounds and assume board positions in private technology companies. An ability to network with founders and high-level tech industry talent through social and education events is also a necessity. Preference will be accorded to candidates with 5+ years of experience working at a VC firm.
