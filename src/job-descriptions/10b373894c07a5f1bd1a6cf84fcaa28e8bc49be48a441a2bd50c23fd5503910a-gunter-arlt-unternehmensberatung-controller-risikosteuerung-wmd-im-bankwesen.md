### Description\nDo you love numbers, analyses, and risk management? Would you like to work in a dynamic financial environment and actively shape the control of central processes? Then you are in the right place!\n\nThis well-established financial institution, which serves small and medium-sized enterprises and commercial real estate clients, places great importance on collaborative partnerships, agility, integrity, and credibility. Its service portfolio includes corporate and real estate financing, account-based investments, industry-specific insurance services, and comprehensive financial consulting.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Creation of meaningful risk reports, analyses, and evaluations for various internal target groups.\n- Continuous monitoring of the risk situation, risk-bearing capacity, and adherence to defined limits.\n- Preparation of decision-relevant information for management.\n- Analysis and tracking of operational and strategic goal achievement.\n- Collaboration in the annual risk inventory as well as in the validation and further development of existing methods and procedures.\n- Support in business and capital planning, internal and external audits, and regulatory reporting.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Solid knowledge of banking applications VR-Control and agree21, and proficient use of Microsoft 365.\n- Analytical, conceptual, and solution-oriented thinking.\n- Openness to change and enjoyment of actively shaping processes.\n- Independent, structured, and precise working method.\n- High commitment, flexibility, and team orientation.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an analytical and detail-oriented individual passionate about risk management. The role offers diverse and responsible tasks with significant development potential within a dynamic financial institution. You'll join a collaborative and appreciative team that values integrity, agility, and continuous personal and professional growth. The company demonstrates strong commitment to employee well-being, offering an attractive, performance-oriented compensation package, 30 days of vacation, company health insurance, mobile and flexible working hours, a company restaurant, an employee assistance program, and even a company bike scheme. If you're looking to actively shape central processes and grow your expertise in a supportive environment, this role is for you.
