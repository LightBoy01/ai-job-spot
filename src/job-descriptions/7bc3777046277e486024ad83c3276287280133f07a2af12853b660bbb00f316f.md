**Our Opportunity:**
We are seeking a dynamic **Senior Manager of Cloud Engineering & Operations** with substantial experience in cloud infrastructure and a passion for generative AI technologies. This role will be responsible for the architecture, deployment, management, and continuous improvement of our cloud infrastructure. This role bridges cloud operations excellence with next-gen AI innovation, driving scalable, secure, and cost-efficient AI infrastructure. The ideal candidate will possess in-depth expertise in cloud platforms (AWS/GCP, Azure), strong leadership capabilities, and a passion for driving operational excellence, automation, security, and cost optimization.
**Who you’ll report to:** VP Cloud Engineering
**Location:**  San Jose, California – Bay Area /   hybrid, two days in the office
**You'll make an impact by being responsible for:**
*   Lead and grow a high-performing cloud engineering team focused on building and maintaining scalable cloud infrastructure for AI workloads, including generative AI models.
*   Mentor the Cloud Operations team, fostering a culture of collaboration, accountability, and continuous improvement.
*   Oversee the design, implementation, and maintenance of scalable, secure, and highly available cloud infrastructure.
*   Develop and enforce cloud governance, operational best practices, and compliance policies.
*   Drive automation across deployment pipelines, monitoring, and infrastructure management (IaC, CI/CD).
*   Manage cloud cost optimization strategies to ensure efficient usage of cloud resources.
*   Collaborate with DevOps, Security, Development, and Product teams to align cloud operations with business objectives.
*   Implement robust monitoring, alerting, and incident management processes to ensure high service uptime and reliability.
*   Ensure robust security, compliance, and monitoring frameworks for AI infrastructure.
*   Stay up to date with generative AI advancements and translate new capabilities into infrastructure improvements.
*   Lead capacity planning, disaster recovery planning, and performance tuning initiatives.
*   Stay current on emerging cloud technologies, trends, and best practices to continuously improve architecture and operations.
*   Define and report KPIs and metrics for system performance, uptime, security, and operational efficiency.
**You will be a great fit if you have:**
*   Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
*   10+ years of experience in IT infrastructure or cloud operations, with 3–5 years in a leadership role.
*   Proven experience managing AWS/ GCP, Azure environments in a large-scale production setting.
*   Strong understanding of DevOps practices, Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) tools (Terraform, CloudFormation, etc.), and CI/CD tools (Jenkins, GitLab, etc.).
*   Deep knowledge of cloud security, compliance, cost management, and performance optimization.
*   Experience managing GPU/TPU clusters or similar accelerators for AI workloads.
*   Knowledge of frameworks and tools like TensorFlow, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, or equivalent.
*   Hands-on experience leading high-performing engineering teams in 24x7 production environments, with a strong focus on incident management, root cause analysis (RCA), and continuous service reliability improvement.
*   Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
