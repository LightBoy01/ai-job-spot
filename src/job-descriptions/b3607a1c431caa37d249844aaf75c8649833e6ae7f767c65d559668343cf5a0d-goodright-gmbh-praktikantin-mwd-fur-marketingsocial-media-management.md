### Description\nAre you looking for an exciting workplace where you can truly make a difference? Then Pflegewächter (Goodright GmbH) is the right place for you! We are a small but dedicated social-tech startup committed to making elderly care understandable and accessible. Our goal is to challenge outdated systems, enabling those in need of care to live self-determined and dignified lives, while relieving their caregivers. You'll work in a young and dynamic team that collaborates digitally from various locations. If you're eager to gain direct practical experience, shape things, make the world a better place, and take on responsibility, then we look forward to getting to know you and working together on our mission! This role is ideal for gaining initial insights into the exciting world of marketing and social media management, with a duration of at least 6 weeks, up to 6 months. We offer societal added value, flexible working hours with co-working/office options in Hamburg, Lüneburg, Munich, and Hanover, a long-term perspective, a company laptop, pet-friendly offices, home office options, and team events.\n\n### Responsibilities\nYour tasks with us include supporting marketing & social media management, specifically:\n\n*   Creating and maintaining content for our social media channels.\n*   Answering customer inquiries via email and social media channels.\n*   Creating and optimizing high-quality B2C content according to SEO principles.\n\n### Qualifications\nYour profile:\n\n*   You are studying Media, Communication, or Marketing Management (or something comparable).\n*   Initial experience with **Social Media Management** is an advantage, but not a must.\n*   You are interested in **SEO** and **B2C communication**.\n*   You are keen to contribute **responsibly and independently** within a young team.\n*   You are reliable, open to new ideas, and find it exciting to truly make a difference in a **social-tech startup**.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic entry-level opportunity for students or recent graduates passionate about social impact and digital marketing. The emphasis on independent work within a supportive, dynamic, and digitally connected team makes it an ideal environment for hands-on learning and professional growth. The flexible work arrangements and additional perks like pet-friendly offices and team events highlight a modern, employee-focused culture. If you're looking for a role where your work truly matters and you can develop valuable skills, this is a perfect fit.
