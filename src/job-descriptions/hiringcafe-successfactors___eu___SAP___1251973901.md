**What You’ll do**

As a Software Engineer, you will have the opportunity to be part of an agile team that will:

*   Establish a trusted partnership with our internal business and technical counterparts and act as “ONE TEAM” from portfolio to delivery and then operation
*   Drive continuous improvements in IT Solution and Service quality, focusing on stability, usability, maintainability, and ultimately customer satisfaction
*   Develop skills and competency in the latest business models, solutions, and technologies.
*   Assist in the design, development, and testing of Cloud Native applications
*   Develop and maintain technical documentation such as functional specifications, design documents, and test scripts
*   Participate in code reviews to ensure strict adherence to coding standards and best practices

**What you bring**

We seek people with distinctive academic credentials, strategic thinking, superior problem-solving skills, and a strong professional record of accomplishment of results. In addition, we place high value on relevant personal qualities: resourcefulness, tenacity, high-energy and self-confidence. Desired skills are:

*   A bachelor's or master's degree in computer science or related fields
*   1 -3 years of professional work experience
*   Comprehensive understanding of the software development lifecycle, Agile (SAFe), and methodologies such as Domain-Driven Design (DDD) and Test-Driven Development (TDD).
*   Experience in **Node.js, JavaScript, SQL**
*   Knowledge of **UI development** with additional skills in HTML5 and CSS.
*   Experience with **cloud platforms** such as **Cloud Foundry**
*   Nice to have - event**\-driven architectures**, API integrations, and GENAI-based solutions
*   Knowledge of **GenAI, Prompt Engineering, and Large Language Models (LLMs)** to optimize workflows and generate automated insights would be considered a plus
*   Skilled in **CI/CD pipeline setup** and using **Git** for version control
*   Ability to work effectively in **international, cross-functional teams**, with excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
*   Independent and team-oriented work ethic, strong analytical skills, and problem-solving abilities.
*   Quick learning aptitude and adaptability for new technologies and methodologies.
*   Strong interpersonal and verbal/written communication skills in English.
