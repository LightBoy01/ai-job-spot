**About Highspot**

Highspot is pioneering the category that is fundamentally changing the way companies increase sales productivity. On a mission to transform the way millions of people work with sales enablement, Highspot is committed to building breakthrough software with a spark of magic. We believe a great place to work is about more than the work – it’s about what the company stands for, and how it authentically represents its values in the real world. Our goal is to create a culture where everyone feels a deep sense of belonging and is empowered to be an agent of change, with the ability to transform themselves, their workplace, and their world.

  

**Design at Highspot**

We are a collective of global makers who infuse our design craft into everything we do. We contribute and drive impact across all creative functions; from product R&D to events, design language to design systems, from marketing initiatives and everything brand, our teams focus diligently on building the best Highspot Experience, creating lasting impact for our current and future customers.

  

**About the Role**

We are seeking a Senior Product Designer that has a perfect balance of skills to demonstrate Design excellence, the energy to make it happen, and has the design-maturity to influence for impact while managing ambiguity.

  

You have experience with moulding designs from initial ideation, through rapid iteration, to pixel-perfect implementation. You should have a passion for usability, an eye for visual consistency, and the ability to synthesize complexity into its essential parts. You are interested and have experience in the unique challenges of designing for AI, agentic platforms, and machine learning. You are an effective diplomat with strong communication and operational skills who is willing to advocate for the position of customers in the face of senior balancing points from Product Management, Design and Engineering leadership. “World-class” is the bar and you have the desire to take us there!

**Responsibilities**  
*   Ability to manage ambiguous and complex product areas to deliver strong work on tight schedules
*   Experience working across multiple projects at once
*   Identify and prioritize usage scenarios through customer research and competitive analysis
*   Analyze user feedback and activity to develop personas
*   Create wireframes, storyboards, and user flows
*   Design functional prototypes to prove out interaction models
*   Strong partnership with engineering and product management to drive shared vision and execution of product plans
  
  
**Required Qualifications**  
*   Degree in human-computer interaction, interaction design, visual design, or equivalent work experience
*   5+ years of experience in designing world-class software
*   Expert knowledge of user-centred design principles
*   Fluency and expertise with Figma, and industry standard design tools
*   Solid portfolio of work spanning multiple projects
*   Experience with UX for enterprise software products, channels, or marketplaces a plus
*   Experience working with agile development teams (agile/scrum/kanban)
*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills
*   Excellent interpersonal and collaborative skills
*   Bonus: Prior experience with designing for AI and agentic platforms
