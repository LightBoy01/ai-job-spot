FAQs:
-----

  

### How does this work? Is this a job application?

We are scaling every team over the coming months — commercial, engineering, ML, compliance. We know that the very best aren’t on the market — so we are looking for people who are ready to make a jump.

  

### Will you get back to me?

Probably not. That sounds harsh, but the last time we opened applications like this we received thousands of CVs. We are scaling fast to deliver AI healthcare at scale — we simply don’t have the time to reach out to every individual. But if we think you’re a fit, we will be beating your door down.

  

### What specific skills are you looking for?

Generally — extreme grit, deep curiosity, an insane bias to action, and genuinely world-class performance. That will depend on your sector and stage of life — we don’t just want senior hires or juniors. You might have won the Gold Medal in the Math Olympiad at school or founded a company and scaled it to >$1m ARR or invented a new algorithm for the detection of minerals from space, or achieved one of a million other wonderful human feats — you know what it is, so tell us. We are looking for people who climb the mountain just because it’s there and can show us that, whatever stage of life or industry they are coming from.

  

### Can I work remotely?

Truly, for the right talent, we know it doesn’t matter where you work. However, we also recognize that this approach works better for some roles than others. Tell us your preferences in the application form, but generally, London-based candidates or those willing to move will be higher on our priority list to meet.
