We're Celonis, the global leader in Process Mining technology and one of the world's fastest-growing SaaS firms. We believe there is a massive opportunity to unlock productivity by placing data and intelligence at the core of business processes - and for that, we need you to join us.

**The Role:**

As a **Supply Chain Optimization Deployment  Architect (SCODA)**, you'll be the bridge between our customers' toughest supply chain challenges and our powerful Process Intelligence platform. Working directly with Celonis' strategic customers throughout the customer lifecycle  in both, presales and post-sales,  you'll use your deep domain knowledge to design, optimize, and scale innovative supply chain solutions. Your goal is to pinpoint customer pain points, align our offerings with their strategic supply chain vision, and ensure successful project execution and clear value. You'll proactively identify key use cases that transform customer supply chains, and standardize these solutions in collaboration with internal teams to boost adoption across our customer base.

This role can be based out of our Stockholm or Copenhagen offices. We're looking for individuals with hands-on experience in delivering solutions to customers.

**The work you’ll do:**

*   **Design and architect a comprehensive Supply Chain Vision** with customers and internal account teams, identifying and solving critical challenges at the core of their supply chains and businesses.
*   **Collaborate closely with customers' supply chain experts** to define a target state where the Celonis platform, encompassing Process Intelligence, AI, and automation, delivers tangible value to their operations.
*   **Provide presales support**, helping to position and articulate the strategic value of Celonis's supply chain capabilities to prospective customers.
*   **Provide expertise and domain knowledge in the delivery phase** by providing guidance on solution architecture to ongoing projects in , participating in critical project phases, assessing implementation progress, and proactively contributing additional use case ideas to ensure projects achieve their full potential.
*   **Collect and share compelling Supply Chain success stories** across the organization and with our customers, showcasing the impact of our solutions.
*   **Closely collaborate with Product & Engineering and the Business Apps Teams** to inform and shape future product development based on valuable customer feedback and internal experiences.
*   **Train internal teams** and empower them to effectively position and deploy Celonis's supply chain solutions.

**The preferred qualifications you need:**

*   **Minimum 5 years experience in Supply Chain Management** for enterprises, with a strong portfolio demonstrating successful and diverse use cases that have solved real-world problems.
*   **Excellent problem-solving skills**, analytical thinking, and meticulous attention to detail, coupled with a track record of delivering high-quality solutions, often under demanding deadlines.
*   **Experience with the implementation and roll-out of Supply Chain Software** or Enterprise Resource Planning tools is highly desirable.
*   **Strong communication and collaboration skills**, with the ability to translate complex supply chain concepts into clear, actionable insights for diverse stakeholder groups, including non-technical audiences.
*   **Strong visionary architectural mindset** to develop scalable and easily extensible solutions that address long-term customer needs.
*   **Bachelor's degree or higher in Supply Chain Management, Business, or a related field**
*   Written and spoken fluency in English and a Nordics language

**About Us:**

Celonis makes processes work for people, companies and the planet. The Celonis Process Intelligence Platform uses industry-leading process mining and AI technology and augments it with business context to give customers a living digital twin of their business operation. It’s system-agnostic and without bias, and provides everyone with a common language for understanding and improving businesses. Celonis enables its customers to continuously realize significant value across the top, bottom, and green line. Celonis is headquartered in Munich, Germany, and New York City, USA, with more than 20 offices worldwide.
