Job Title:

Internship: Software Development in Suzhou R&D Center

Job Description:

Are you a highly motivated and proactive individual looking to gain hands-on experience in cutting-edge AI technology within an innovative R&D environment? Join our team as an intern and contribute to exciting projects shaping the future of the aviation industry and AI software development.

Location: Suzhou R&D Centre

Duration: 6 months

Start Date: June 2025

### Your Tasks Would Be:

*   Software/Algorithm Development Support:
    
    *   Support the Computer Vision Data Labeling to train the model for high accuracy,
        
    *   Support development tools/ scripts for data management, processing and analysis.
        
*   Lab Management Support:
    
    *   Support the maintenance of the R&D lab, including the user accounts, workstations, development tools, devices…
        
    *   Support 3D printing of the prototypes (could learn after join the team)
        

### What You Will Gain:

*   In-depth Insights: Gain valuable insights into the operations and processes of our state-of-the-art R&D centre.
    
*   Meaningful Responsibility: Take ownership of small-scale, short-term projects from inception to completion.
    
*   Specialized Knowledge: Develop foundational knowledge in key areas such as Computer Vision, Artificial Intelligence, fast prototyping.
    

Qualifications:

*   Basic knowledge of Software Engineering, experience with Python programming
    
*   Basic knowledge of AI, especially computer vision related technologies
    
*   Ability to think conceptually and strategically, with a passion for future-oriented design.
    
*   Communication, presentation, and interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively in an international team environment
    
*   A proactive and self-driven approach with a strong desire to learn and find solutions
    
*   A highly motivated, creative, and open-minded spirit with a "can-do" attitude.
    
*   Fluent English and Chinese for seamless communication.
    

This job requires an awareness of any potential compliance risks and a commitment to act with integrity, as the foundation for the Company’s success, reputation and sustainable growth.
