### Description\nOn behalf of our partner company in Munich, we are seeking an **HR Manager / Legal Counsel (m/f/d)** to join their team as soon as possible. In this pivotal role, you will be responsible for handling national and international employment law matters, including tax and social security law, and will play a key part in supporting the HR-Director with international projects and HR-related initiatives.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Manage national and international employment law matters, including tax and social security law.\n- Support the HR-Director in planning, coordinating, and implementing international projects and HR measures, ensuring compliance with legal, collective bargaining, and statutory regulations.\n- Represent the company in labor court proceedings, draft guidelines, works council agreements, and employment contract templates, ensuring their legal conformity.\n- Collaborate with the works council and coordinate/support employee secondments and international expatriates.\n- Advise and train managers and HR colleagues on all employment law topics, actively drive the further development of HR processes, and work closely with the HR development department.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Hold a qualification as a fully qualified lawyer (Volljurist), business lawyer (Wirtschaftsrechtler), or a comparable qualification with a focus on employment law.\n- Possess several years of professional experience in HR with an international scope.\n- Ideally have profound knowledge in tax and social security law, as well as experience with collective bargaining agreements (e.g., in the metal and electrical industry).\n- Demonstrate good user knowledge of MS Office.\n- Exhibit strong communication skills, diplomatic acumen, assertiveness, and intercultural competence.\n- Command very good German and good English skills, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a seasoned employment law professional looking to make a significant impact within a dynamic international company in Munich. The role offers a comprehensive scope, from advising on complex legal matters and representing the company in court to actively shaping HR processes and collaborating on global initiatives. With a strong emphasis on professional development, flexible working hours, and the option for home office, the company clearly values its employees' growth and work-life balance. Located centrally in Munich, this permanent position provides a modern work environment and a clear path for long-term career stability and continuous learning.
