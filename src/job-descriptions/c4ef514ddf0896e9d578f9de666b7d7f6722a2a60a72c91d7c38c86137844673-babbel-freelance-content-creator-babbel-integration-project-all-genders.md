### Description\nBabbel, a market leader in app-based language learning with over 600 team members from 50+ countries, is committed to making language learning accessible. The company is currently building a blended learning model for BAMF-accredited integration courses (A1–B1), combining teacher-led instruction with structured digital self-study.\n\nThis Freelance Content Creator role is pivotal to this project. You will be a detail-oriented individual responsible for transforming existing curriculum plans, activity specifications, and pedagogical guidelines into high-quality, engaging, and learner-ready digital activities within Babbel's Learning Management System (LMS) and potentially other learning modes like an AI speaking coach. This is an execution-heavy position focused on high-volume production to support the launch of Babbel’s blended learning integration course by March 2026.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Build LMS-based activities (reading, listening, grammar, writing, vocab, quizzes, tasks) according to precise templates and didactical guidelines.\n*   Translate curriculum specifications into structured learning flows inside the LMS.\n*   Produce, edit, and upload content (text, micro-dialogues, scripts, prompts, audio placeholders).\n*   Ensure all activities meet CEFR A1–B1 learning objectives and BAMF expectations.\n*   Work closely with the Curriculum Manager to deliver weekly content packages.\n*   Maintain consistency in tone, level, and format across all created materials.\n*   Track production progress and deliver high-quality content on tight timelines.\n\n### Qualifications\n**Essential:**\n*   Experience designing or building content inside an LMS (e.g., Moodle, Canvas, LearnDash, Thinkific, proprietary systems).\n*   Experience creating digital learning activities (e.g., quizzes, dialogues, scaffolding tasks, microlearning practice).\n*   Strong ability to follow detailed templates, instructions, and structured workflows.\n*   Familiarity with CEFR levels, especially A1–B1.\n*   Excellent written German skills (C1+) and strong command of English.\n*   Ability to execute quickly and reliably; all A1–B1 content needs to be produced by March 2026.\n*   Strong attention to detail and quality standards.\n\n**Nice to Have:**\n*   Experience in adult language education or DaF/DaZ contexts.\n*   Experience creating content for integration courses (BAMF, telc, DTZ).\n*   Familiarity with AI-based content formats (prompts, scenario writing, conversation design).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis freelance Content Creator role at Babbel offers a unique opportunity to contribute significantly to a high-impact blended learning initiative. It's ideal for a detail-oriented and efficient individual with a strong background in LMS content creation and a solid understanding of CEFR levels, particularly A1-B1 German. The role is challenging due to tight timelines and high-volume production, but it promises direct impact on a new product launch. Babbel fosters a vibrant, international, and inclusive startup culture that emphasizes growth, continuous learning, and work-life balance, making it an attractive environment for passionate educators and content specialists looking to make a difference in language learning. The company actively seeks diverse candidates and values individual growth, even for freelance contributors.
