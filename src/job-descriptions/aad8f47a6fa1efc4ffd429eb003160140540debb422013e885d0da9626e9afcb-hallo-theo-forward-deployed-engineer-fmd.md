### Description\nAs a Forward Deployed Engineer at hallo theo, you are the crucial link between product, engineering, and operations, directly impacting property management teams across Germany. This role involves transforming real-world challenges into scalable, production-ready solutions and feeding those learnings back into the core product. You'll work on a product with significant customer impact, collaborating closely with various teams, shaping deployment strategies, and growing into a key voice in connecting our product to the real world.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Own end-to-end solutions for our property management teams across Germany – from discovery and solution design through implementation, rollout, monitoring and iteration.\n- Design and build integrations and extensions on top of our platform (APIs, data pipelines, configuration, custom logic) that are reliable, secure and maintainable.\n- Serve as the technical partner for our regional teams, collaborating with Product and Operations to clarify requirements, manage risks and keep projects on track.\n- Translate frontline operational challenges into product insights – shaping our roadmap with clear problem statements, edge cases and data-backed learnings from the field.\n- Continuously improve our playbooks and tooling, so future rollouts are faster, safer and more repeatable.\n\n### Qualifications\n**Minimum:**\n- 5–7 years of experience as a Product Engineer / Software Engineer working on production systems (ideally in SaaS or platform products).\n- Strong experience with modern web backends and APIs (e.g. REST/GraphQL, event-driven systems, cloud-native architectures).\n- Proven track record of customer-facing work: implementations, integrations, solution engineering or similar.\n- Ability to scope and slice work, manage technical trade-offs and communicate clearly with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.\n- Comfortable working in discovery mode: ambiguity, incomplete specs, iterating quickly with customers.\n- Native-level or very strong German (min. C1) and very good English.\n**Nice to Haves:**\n- Experience in B2B software with integrations into CRM, ERP, HR, payments or other core systems.\n- Experience in startups / scale-ups where processes and tooling are still evolving.\n- Prior experience as Solutions Engineer / Implementation Engineer / \"Forward Deployed\" / Field Engineer.\n- Familiarity with analytics & instrumentation to measure adoption and impact of what you ship.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting opportunity for a seasoned Product or Software Engineer who thrives on direct customer interaction and shaping product strategy. The role offers significant autonomy to build end-to-end solutions and influence the core platform by bringing real-world insights from property management teams. Strong German language skills are essential, as is comfort with ambiguity and a proactive approach to problem-solving. It's a chance to be a pivotal bridge between technology and customer success in a fast-evolving scale-up environment.
