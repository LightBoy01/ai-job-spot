### Description\nRoger is specialized software designed to increase revenue and reduce effort in dental practices. With our AI assistant, we enable dental practices to achieve GDPR-compliant WhatsApp and SMS communication, automated appointment reminders, efficient treatment cost plan tracking, as well as offer modules like factoring, external dental practice administration (ZMV), and external reception.\n\nIn this role, you will build and scale our growth engine. You will combine deep product understanding with intelligent automation to transform leads from “interested” to “enthusiastic”. Your goal is not just to generate incoming leads, but to convert them into happy customers through sharp messaging and automated journeys.\n\n### Responsibilities\n**1. Product Marketing & Product Understanding**\n* Understand the product in detail and translate it into compelling messaging and positioning strategies.\n* Collaborate closely with Product & Sales to understand customer segments, use cases, and pain points.\n* Develop product storylines precisely tailored to the target audience.\n\n**2. CRM & Marketing Automation (HubSpot-first)**\n* Build your own HubSpot funnel, including lead scoring, nurturing sequences, trigger automation, and segmentation.\n* Develop a consistent lead experience – no matter which channel the lead comes from.\n* Utilize tools like Apollo, HubSpot & AI-native automations to efficiently scale processes.\n\n**3. Growth & Funnel Ownership**\n* Think holistically in Full-Funnel Growth: Awareness → Activation → Conversion → Retention.\n* Analyze and optimize conversion rates across all funnel stages.\n* Develop new growth hypotheses, experiments & A/B tests.\n\n**4. Lead Management**\n* Establish a clear process for: “What do I do with incoming leads?”\n* Build automated playbooks & journeys that guide leads based on segment, intent, and behavior.\n* Coordinate closely with Sales to efficiently hand over sales-ready leads.\n\n### Qualifications\n* At least 2 years of experience in Product Marketing, Growth, or CRM Automation.\n* Strong technical understanding, data-driven, AI-savvy.\n* Experience with Apollo, HubSpot, and modern AI marketing tools.\n* Ability to quickly grasp complex products and customer segments.\n* Startup mentality, ownership, eagerness to experiment.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a proactive and data-driven Growth Marketing professional to make a significant impact within a rapidly growing HealthTech startup. You'll have the autonomy to build and scale critical marketing infrastructure, directly influencing revenue. The role offers a chance to work with a modern tech stack, contribute to a product creating real value in healthcare digitalization, and thrive in an ambitious culture with flat hierarchies and a strong emphasis on experimentation and ownership. If you're ready to drive growth and take real responsibility, Roger offers a dynamic environment to shape their success story.
