### Description\nOur partner company in Munich is seeking a **Disponent International Logistics (m/w/d)** to start as soon as possible. In this role, you will be responsible for the planning and control of goods disposition, acting as a crucial link between suppliers, customers, and transport companies. This is a long-term opportunity within a modern environment that values work-life balance and employee development.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- As a **Disponent International Logistics (m/w/d)**, you will plan and control the disposition of goods between suppliers, customers, and transport companies.\n- You will manage communication within order processing in German and English, ensuring transparent operations.\n- You will independently negotiate transport prices and optimize cost structures in the transport sector.\n- Additionally, you will acquire new transport companies, expand the service provider network, and secure the service portfolio.\n- You will be responsible for the creation, control, and maintenance of all transport-relevant documents (e.g., waybills, shipping documents, customs documents), ensuring compliance and quality.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You have a degree as a freight forwarding agent (Speditionskaufmann (m/w/d)), merchant for freight forwarding and logistics services (Kaufmann für Spedition und Logistikdienstleistung (m/w/d)), or a comparable qualification.\n- Ideally, you have professional experience in a comparable position in dispatch, transport logistics, or order processing.\n- You possess sound PC skills, especially in MS Office.\n- You impress with organizational, coordination, and planning skills, as well as a structured way of working.\n- Excellent German and good English skills distinguish you.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent long-term opportunity for a logistics professional who thrives in a dynamic international environment. The company offers a supportive and appreciative work culture with a \"Du\"-culture, flexible working hours for a healthy work-life balance, and significant investment in employee growth through various training and development programs. Located centrally in Munich, this role promises a modern work setting where your contributions are valued.
