We’re looking to bring on board a **visionary, generalist profile** with a strong passion for **Artificial Intelligence**, to drive transformation and innovation across the business. Your mission will be to act as a **catalyst**, identifying high-impact opportunities where AI can modernize operations, create new value propositions, and optimize existing products.

You will lead a **technical team** responsible for supporting this transformation and working closely with them to coordinate the impact and implementation of optimization strategies across current and future workflows—applying the most effective automation solutions.

Responsibilities:

*   Proactively identify and assess opportunities to implement AI-powered innovation and automation solutions across different business areas.
*   Translate business needs and AI capabilities into concrete use cases, define product/project roadmaps, and prioritize initiatives based on potential impact and feasibility.
*   Drive AI initiatives from ideation and conceptual design to implementation oversight, collaborating closely with technical, product, and business teams.
*   Act as a change agent—helping teams adopt new AI tools and processes, ensuring smooth transitions and maximizing value.
*   Collaborate with department leaders, product managers, engineers, and other stakeholders to align AI initiatives with company strategy.
*   Promote rapid iteration and execution, regularly delivering prototypes and updates to demonstrate value and gather feedback.
*   Stay up to date on the latest trends, tools, and best practices in AI, continuously evaluating their applicability.
*   Lead a specialized automation team with close collaboration across development departments.

**Requirements**

*   **Proven Experience in High-Impact Roles**: You’ve previously worked in roles such as Product Management, Growth, Entrepreneurship, Innovation Consulting, Operations, Automation, or similar—where you’ve conceptualized, launched, and/or scaled meaningful products, projects, or initiatives.
*   **Technical Team Leadership**: Experience managing technical teams and aligning with cross-functional departments. Skilled in performance tracking and team motivation.
*   **Hands-On Passion and Knowledge in AI**: You have a clear understanding of current AI trends and the capabilities of various models/tools (LLMs, generative AI, etc.), and how they can be applied to solve business problems and create value.
*   **Builder & Executor Mindset**: You’re action-oriented—you don’t just strategize, you drive things forward and deliver tangible results.
*   **Strategic Vision & Analytical Thinking**: You can see the big picture, identify key levers, and make data-driven decisions.
*   **Leadership & Collaboration**: You’re able to lead cross-functional initiatives, motivate others, and work effectively with diverse profiles (engineering, marketing, operations, etc.).
*   **Strong Communication Skills**: You can clearly and persuasively explain complex ideas to both technical and non-technical audiences.
*   **Technical Fluency**: While you don’t need to be a programmer, you should understand the fundamentals of AI solutions and communicate effectively with engineers.
*   **Proactivity, Curiosity & Adaptability**: You’re self-motivated, fast-learning, and thrive in dynamic, evolving environments. You see opportunity where others see problems.

### **Nice to Have (Not Mandatory)**

*   Experience working in large organizations or driving change in established environments.
*   Prior application of AI in specific business contexts (e.g., workflow automation, e-commerce personalization, marketing optimization, etc.).
*   Familiarity with **Agile product development methodologies**.
*   Experience with modern tools across real-world projects, including:

*   **Data/BI**: Tableau, Qlik, PowerBI

*   **Workflow Automation**: n8n, Zapier, Make

*   **Digital Marketing & Analytics**: Google Ads, GA4, Firebase, AppsFlyer, Amplitude
*   **Design & Rapid Prototyping**: Figma, Canva
