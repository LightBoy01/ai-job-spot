### Description\nJoin a young, dynamic, and rapidly growing promotional products agency based in Hamburg. We are setting new standards in the industry with our webshop, Promobedarf.de, where we supply a wide range of promotional items quickly and reliably for every event. Our core philosophy is to simplify complex individual requirements, providing customers with swift, effective solutions that contribute to their success. We pride ourselves on our customer-centric approach, ensuring every client has a direct and immediate point of contact with our team of highly competent professionals. We foster an agile, future-oriented, and unbureaucratic work environment where honesty, directness, and mutual helpfulness are highly valued.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Receive and process customer inquiries via phone or in writing.\n*   Calculate and prepare detailed offers.\n*   Record and process customer orders efficiently.\n*   Review and prepare print data for production.\n*   Monitor the entire order processing workflow and ensure timely deliveries.\n*   Keep customers informed about the progress of their orders.\n*   Provide comprehensive advice and support to customers.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   General higher education entrance qualification (Abitur).\n*   A completed vocational training or apprenticeship in one of the following fields, or a similar relevant qualification:\n    *   Media Designer (Digital and Print) with a focus on consulting and planning.\n    *   Media Consultant (Audiovisual Media).\n    *   Marketing Communication Specialist.\n    *   Dialog Marketing Specialist.\n    *   Certified Media Specialist (Fachwirt/in Medien).\n    *   Digital and Print Media Specialist.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a dedicated and customer-focused individual to join a vibrant and growing agency in Hamburg. The role offers a comprehensive immersion into the world of promotional products, printing, and production processes. You'll be part of a friendly, open-minded, and highly motivated team that genuinely values collaboration and direct communication. With an emphasis on continuous learning and the opportunity to quickly implement your ideas, this position is ideal for someone looking to make a tangible impact and develop their career within a supportive and agile environment. The company also sweetens the deal with a loft-style open-plan office and complimentary perks like fresh fruit and coffee.
