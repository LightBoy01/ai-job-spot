At TechBiz Global, we are providing recruitment service to our TOP clients from our portfolio. We are currently seeking a QA Engineer to join one of our **clients' teams**. If you're looking for an exciting opportunity to grow in a innovative environment, this could be the perfect fit for you.

  
  

**Job Description: Junior / Intermediate QA Engineer**

**Start Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Commitment:** 12 days per month (approx. 3 days/week)  
**Duration:** Indefinite period (quarterly contract renewals)  
**Location:** Remote  
**Level:** Junior or Intermediate

We are looking for a motivated Junior or Intermediate QA Engineer to help scale up testing capacity. You will collaborate closely with developers, product owners, and other QA team members to ensure product quality across all stages of the development lifecycle.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Write, maintain, and execute comprehensive **test cases**, **test plans**, and **bug reports**.
    
*   Perform **functional**, **regression**, **integration**, and **exploratory** testing.
    
*   Work with the QA lead and engineering team to identify, reproduce, and track software defects.
    
*   Contribute to continuous improvement of QA processes and best practices.
    
*   Assist with early-stage **test automation** tasks, including basic scripting and framework usage.
    
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand requirements and ensure high-quality deliverables.
    
*   Participate in release validation and support ongoing quality initiatives.
    
*   (Optional/Preferred) Conduct **Accessibility testing**, ensuring compliance with relevant guidelines (e.g., WCAG).
    

**Required Skills & Qualifications**

*   Experience creating and executing **test cases**, **test plans**, and **bug reports**.
    
*   Basic knowledge of **test automation concepts** and scripting languages—**Python preferred**.
    
*   Strong **analytical** and **problem-solving** skills with a high attention to detail.
    
*   Good **communication skills** and the ability to work effectively in a collaborative team environment.
    
*   Ability to work independently and manage time within a part-time schedule.
    

**Nice to Have**

*   Experience with Accessibility testing (e.g., WCAG, screen readers, keyboard navigation).
    
*   Familiarity with test automation frameworks or CI/CD pipelines.
    
*   Experience with issue-tracking or test-management tools (e.g., Jira, TestRail).
    

**What We Offer**

*   Flexible part-time engagement (approx. 3 days/week).
    
*   Opportunity to influence and improve QA practices in a growing team.
    
*   Long-term collaboration through quarterly renewable contracts.
    
*   Supportive, team-oriented work environment.
