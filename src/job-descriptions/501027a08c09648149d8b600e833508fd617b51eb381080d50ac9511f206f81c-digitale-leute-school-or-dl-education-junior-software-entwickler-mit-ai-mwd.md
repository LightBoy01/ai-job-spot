Through a diverse ecosystem of conferences, bootcamps, and a thriving community, we facilitate networking and knowledge exchange among leaders in the digital industry. We proudly collaborate with esteemed partners such as Airbnb, Porsche Digital, Atlassian, and Rewe Digital to collectively shape the future of software development.\n\nOur Digitale Leute School offers highly practical and cutting-edge continuing education for the tech sector. A flagship offering is our AI Software Engineering Bootcamp, meticulously designed to enable experienced software developers to achieve their next significant career milestone. This intensive program transforms participants into highly influential AI Software Engineers. Taught by expert trainers from leading tech companies, the bootcamp delivers top-tier AI knowledge seamlessly integrated with essential product management methodologies. Crucially, we provide active support in participants' job searches through our powerful hiring network, and for eligible individuals with a Bildungsgutschein, participation can be entirely free.\n\n### Responsibilities\nUpon successful completion of the AI Software Engineering Bootcamp, participants will be equipped to:\n- Transition from a traditional developer role to a strategic co-creator, significantly increasing their strategic influence in product development as AI handles repetitive coding tasks.\n- Expertly analyze business models and user needs, formulate data-driven hypotheses, and translate these insights into comprehensive roadmaps and product-oriented backlogs.\n- Develop robust and sustainable software solutions, grounded in proven architectural principles and modern design patterns, ensuring long-term viability in the AI era.\n- Proactively identify and manage risks associated with AI deployment, meticulously oversee AI decisions, and maintain vigilant control over costs, API limits, and system performance.\n- Leverage modern AI tools to efficiently develop stable prototypes or complete Minimum Viable Products (MVPs), automate testing procedures, and ensure secure deployment strategies.\n- Maintain a keen oversight of AI decisions, rigorously ensuring quality, strict adherence to defined requirements, and comprehensive observability in live production environments.\n- Strategically integrate and orchestrate AI as a seamless team member, developing specialized agents for various tasks and managing their collaborative workflows to enhance team productivity.\n- Automate complex development workflows and seamlessly integrate critical enterprise data using established frameworks like LangChain or MCP to deliver truly valuable and impactful AI features.\n\n### Qualifications\nWe are seeking ambitious individuals who possess the following qualities and experiences:\n- A profound passion for technology and an innate drive to develop innovative products that deeply engage and delight users.\n- Solid foundational knowledge and practical experience in modern software development practices.\n- An inherent curiosity, high ambition, and a proactive commitment to continuous professional and personal development.\n- A genuine enjoyment of agile methodologies (such as Scrum and Kanban) and a strong preference for collaborative teamwork environments.\n- The ability to deeply empathize with users and stakeholders, consistently demonstrating a product-centric perspective when conceptualizing and implementing technical solutions.\n- A readiness and eagerness to immerse themselves in complex, rapidly evolving fields, including advanced AI development and sophisticated agentic workflows.\n- A dynamic mindset that embraces changes in tech stacks and the adoption of new tools as motivating factors for growth, rather than sources of uncertainty.\n- A strong desire to think creatively and beyond conventional boundaries, coupled with a willingness to take on significant technical and product-strategic responsibility.\n- A recommended German language proficiency of B2 is beneficial for optimal participation
