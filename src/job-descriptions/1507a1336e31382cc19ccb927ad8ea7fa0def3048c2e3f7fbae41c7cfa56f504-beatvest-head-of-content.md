### Description\nAre you passionate about high-quality content, smart automation, and want to shape the future of financial education with AI? Welcome to beatvest.\n\nbeatvest is the leading platform for certified, gamified financial education – and a partner for banks and financial service providers looking to activate their customers, build trust, and unlock product potential. Our B2B SaaS solution integrates directly into existing digital banking experiences, transforming inactive app users into informed investors.\n\nAs Head of Content, you will lead the content strategy for our B2B partners and develop learning content that not only informs users but sustainably motivates them to act, be it through ETF investments, savings plans, or other financial products. You bring data-driven thinking, a strong sense for UX, and a high demand for quality, utilizing AI tools not as a buzzword but as a real lever for scaling. The role is full-time and remote, starting immediately.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- You will develop and be responsible for the strategic direction of beatvest's content platform – with a special focus on financial education formats integrated into our B2B partners' systems.\n- You will manage the entire content lifecycle – from conception to production and evaluation.\n- You will develop new content features and learning formats that measurably activate users and promote product closures.\n- You will use state-of-the-art AI tools and automation solutions (e.g., n8n) for content scaling without compromising quality and factual accuracy.\n- You will collaborate with customer support to analyze user feedback and design content even more effectively.\n- You are responsible for stakeholder management, working closely with customers, external education experts, and the product team.\n- You will present new formats and content concepts to senior stakeholders of large corporations, both internally and externally.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Excellent German language skills and fluent English; you can prepare content for target audiences in both languages.\n- Proven experience in the financial sector (e.g., financial journalism, banking, FinTech, corporate finance, etc.); you immediately recognize high-quality financial content.\n- You think data-driven and bring experience with AI models and automation tools.\n- You have a good eye for visualizations and excellent PowerPoint skills, ideally with experience presenting to top-level management.\n- You have experience working cross-functionally, e.g., with product, marketing, sales, or support teams.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a pivotal role at beatvest, offering a unique opportunity to be a key player in scaling a B2B content platform with direct impact on major financial institutions. You'll join a highly motivated, multidisciplinary team dedicated to financial education and wellness, shaping certified, evidence-based content that truly activates millions of users. The company emphasizes innovation, user feedback, speed, and impact, making it an ideal environment for a data-driven content leader who wants to leverage cutting-edge AI and automation to make a tangible difference in a crucial future market. The remote setup provides flexibility, while the emphasis on impactful, white-label integrations into banking apps ensures wide reach.
