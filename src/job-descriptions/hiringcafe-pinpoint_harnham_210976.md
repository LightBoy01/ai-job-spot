**New York City - Manhattan (Financial District)**

**Clear uncapped commission structure + 401k + health + PTO**

**Starting as soon as possible**

  

Harnham New York, has a startup feel and close knit team with a huge opportunity to come in, work hard and build up a successful sales career. The Harnham Group is the largest Data and Analytics staffing firm globally with offices in New York, Phoenix London and Amsterdam. Although Harnham is a very specialist company and work on niche roles, we work with clients within a range of industries including cutting-edge startups, well known social media brands, ecommerce retailers and international financial companies.

  

**Benefits:**

Harnham offers a **competitive salary**, **unlimited commission**, a bonus structure, 401k, health cover, and travel perks.

On top of the commission, top performing consultants are rewarded with monthly lunch clubs, trips away, luxury Handbag/watch raffle, early finishes and prizes during the annual awards night.

There is a range of wellbeing benefits including mental health support, wellness allowance, extended lunch breaks midweek to fit in a gym class. _As well as this, we offer hybrid working (4 days in office) after 6 months full time in the office._

  

We're looking for individuals who are excited by a career in sales/recruitment specifically and are ambitious to be successful, whether that is in terms of progressing up the career ladder or reaping the rewards of commission and bonuses!

The role:
---------

This is a **full 360 recruitment** consultant role where you will be responsible for managing the end to end cycle from sourcing jobs and sourcing candidates.

*   The job involves **business development**, building relationships with clients, outbound emails and calling as well as nurturing existing client relations. As this is a **sales** career expect to be working in an environment that is very target-driven; you'll be competing with your co-workers to meet and exceed targets.
*   You’ll be working towards KPIs and targets to generate revenue for yourself!
*   50% of the role is chasing leads, reaching out to hiring managers and clients who could be hiring and understanding their pain points to then sell Harnham’s talent solutions.
*   The other 50% of the role is focused on candidate sourcing and managing the interview processes
*   Recruitment can be such a **rewarding** career in terms of long term career development and financial control. However, it is very autonomous and merit based so to get the most of out this successful recruiters have a tenacious work ethic and clear ambitions!
