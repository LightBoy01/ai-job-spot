<p><strong>The Role:</strong></p>
<p> </p>
<p>Project Manager, Project Delivery and Support </p>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>About the Team:</strong></p>
<p> </p>
<p>Project Delivery &amp; Support (PD&amp;S) is a centralized project management office that supports various technology teams across the bank. As a Project Manager in PD&amp;S, you will lead a variety of projects across business lines and technologies, using various project management methodologies and tools. You will work in a culturally diverse environment while providing our Technology and Business partners with best-in-class project management expertise.</p>
<p>Come join our innovative and results oriented team!</p>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>Is this role right for you?</strong></p>
<p> </p>
<p>• Manage and ensure the successful delivery of complex projects involving multiple teams from different groups across the bank, as well as external vendors when required</p>
<p>• Is accountable for planning the end-to-end design of significant processes and projects (the full journey)</p>
<p>• Demonstrates ability to develop and maintain high functioning project teams</p>
<p>• Influences business project decisions with direct impact on financials or operating results</p>
<p>• Provides technical advice and leads the development of the roadmap within the context of a technical specialty</p>
<p>• Ability to independently run executive Steerco meetings with Group Heads, EVPs, and SVPs</p>
<p>• Champions a customer focused culture to deepen client relationships and leverage broader Bank relationships, systems and knowledge Recommends technical strategy within a technical domain, may investigate and evaluate new technologies</p>
<p>• Work with executive sponsors and business managers to prioritize requirements, issues and deliverables</p>
<p>• Create and maintain risk/issue logs and escalate issues to stakeholders and steering committee as appropriate</p>
<p>• Defines and maintains the roadmap and project budget</p>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>Do you have the skills that will enable you to succeed?</strong></p>
<p> </p>
<p>• 5-7 years of experience as a Project Manager, managing large complex projects, that have significant IT and operational components, preferably in the financial services industry</p>
<p>• Excellent track record managing projects of high/medium complexity, high/medium risk and budgets (up to $10 Million+) dealing with multiple stakeholders</p>
<p>• Strong communication (verbal/written/presentation). Mature interpersonal skills with the ability to work with, and influence, all types of professionals</p>
<p>• Knowledge of project management tools and techniques (e.g., CA PPM, MS Projects, JIRA, Visio, Agile/Scrum, Waterfall etc.)</p>
<p>• Delivery experience in 1 or more the following: Infrastructure, Application Development, Cloud, AI, Regulatory Projects, Agile experience preferred.</p>
<p>• Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Engineering, Business, or related discipline</p>
<p>• Must Haves: PMP certification or working towards</p>
<p>• Nice to Haves: Scrum Master certification (CSM), Professional ScrumMaster certification (PSM) and/or master’s degree (MBA), Spanish preferred</p>
