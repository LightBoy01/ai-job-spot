PandaDoc empowers growing organizations to thrive through our all-in-one document automation platform. With over 40+ cross-functional teams across Engineering, Product, and Design, we're scaling our operations to deliver innovative solutions with speed, quality, and customer focus.  
This role operates within a product-centric environment where engineering operations directly support business outcomes and customer value delivery, you will work across both discovery and delivery part, improving end-to-end processes.

About the Role
--------------

We are hiring a **Middle Engineering Operations Specialist** to support operational excellence across our Engineering, Product, and Design (EPD) organization. Reporting to the **Senior Engineering Operations Manager**, you will play an essential hands-on role in facilitating team ceremonies, maintaining operational systems, and coordinating project initiatives across our 40+ cross-functional teams.

You will coordinate organization-wide initiatives and help teams adopt best practices that enable them to deliver high-quality, customer-centric solutions with speed and autonomy.

Key Responsibilities
--------------------

### Team Support&Tool Management

*   Support teams following Scrum framework and help remove impediments to their progress
*   Maintain Jira hygiene across all teams - ensuring consistent naming conventions, proper ticket categorization, and clean backlogs
*   Configure and optimize team boards, filters, and workflows in Jira
*   Identify opportunities for AI-optimization and suggest improvements to reduce manual work
*   Maintain JIRA dashboards and reports on team velocity, sprint completion, and delivery health

### Project Coordination

*   Support the coordination of cross-functional initiatives by tracking action items and dependencies
*   Risks management: flag risks or blockers to senior team members

### Process Documentation & Support

*   Document team processes, decisions, and standard operating procedures
*   Maintain up-to-date runbooks and operational guides
*   Help teams adopt established best practices and operational standards

### Continuous Improvement

*   Collect feedback from teams about process pain points and improvement ideas
*   Participate in process improvement initiatives led by senior team members
*   Support the rollout of new tools, processes, or practices across teams
*   Contribute to communities of practice and knowledge sharing sessions

Required Qualifications
-----------------------

### Must-Haves

*   **Experience:** 2+ years of hands-on professional experience in project coordination, Scrum Master role, or Engineering operations
*   **Agile Knowledge:** Solid understanding of Scrum framework and Agile principles
*   **Tool Expertise:** Strong experience with Jira administration
*   **SDLC Understanding:** Understanding of software development lifecycle and engineering practices
*   **DevOps Awareness:** Familiarity with CI/CD concepts and DevOps fundamentals
*   **Organizational Skills:** Excellent organizational skills with high attention to detail
*   **Communication:** Strong written and verbal communication skills
*   **AI Tools:** Ability to work with AI tools to create basic automation scripts
*   **Language:** English proficiency at B2 level

### Nice to Haves

*   Scrum Master certification (CSM, PSM)
*   Experience working in SaaS/Product companies
*   Familiarity with engineering metrics (velocity, cycle time, DORA metrics)
