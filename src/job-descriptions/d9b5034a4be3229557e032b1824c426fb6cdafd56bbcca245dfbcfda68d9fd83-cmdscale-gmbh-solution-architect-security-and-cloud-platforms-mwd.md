### Description\nThis role is for a **Solution Architect - Security & Cloud Platforms** who sees cloud architecture as more than just technology. You will actively shape security standards, design scalable platforms, and drive secure digital transformation.\n\nYou will act as the crucial interface between business, security, and technology, developing secure and scalable cloud architectures. Your expertise will be vital in advising on regulatory requirements and embedding security-by-design into all projects.\n\nKey responsibilities include migrating legacy systems to modern cloud environments, building future-proof platforms, and empowering DevOps teams with clear architectural and security concepts. The core challenge is to enable secure, traceable, and compliance-driven communication between open cloud environments and highly secure areas, especially within Multi-Cloud-Mesh architectures.\n\nThe company offers direct project involvement, significant autonomy, an open culture, strong team support, and exceptional flexibility, including remote work options and a focus on work-life balance.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Develop solutions enabling secure, traceable, and compliance-driven communication between open cloud environments and highly secure areas, especially for Multi-Cloud-Mesh architectures.\n*   Define data classification and protection needs, ensuring secure transfer to highly sensitive areas, including robust auditing and logging.\n*   Design secure network and communication architectures, including Multi-Cloud-Mesh structures and scalable network topologies.\n*   Implement secure mechanisms for data transfer, synchronization, and workload orchestration.\n*   Develop and integrate access and identity management solutions, including identity separation, trust anchors, and Zero-Trust approaches.\n*   Ensure the use of trusted platform and component bases, incorporating certified components, HSMs, and Customer-Managed Keys.\n*   Create concepts for monitoring, patch management, emergency, and recovery for operational and lifecycle management.\n*   Guarantee compliance with regulatory requirements (ISO 27001, IT-Grundschutz, VSA) and full auditability.\n*   Define operational models and responsibilities, including Shared-Responsibility models in multi-cloud contexts.\n*   Ensure interoperability and extensibility across multiple hyperscalers and through modular design.\n*   Develop user and usage scenarios for business applications, crisis operations, and mobile access.\n*   Lead architecture and concept development for Cloud and Hybrid platforms (AWS, OpenShift, OpenStack, etc.).\n*   Design Security Architectures focusing on Zero-Trust, Identity & Access Management, and Compliance.\n*   Craft Platform Designs, including Landing Zone, Service Mesh, and Marketplace solutions.\n*   Optionally, drive DevSecOps approaches (Infrastructure as Code, Policy as Code, secure software supply chains).\n*   Oversee the migration of Legacy systems into scalable Cloud environments.\n*   Provide expert consulting on regulatory compliance and Security Governance.\n*   Establish maturity models for Cloud and Security platforms.\n*   Empower Developer and DevOps teams through clear architecture and security concepts.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   **Architecture & Design Competence:** Proven experience in cloud and security architectures, combining strategic understanding with a hands-on mentality.\n*   **Technical Expertise & Decisiveness:** Ability to understand, structure, and implement complex technical concepts; self-starter in new topics with a strong overview.\n*   **Regulatory Knowledge:** Deep understanding of Compliance & Security Governance (ISO 27001, IT-GS, VSA).\n*   **Communication Skills:** Fluent in professional German and English, capable of clear and confident communication with both teams and clients.\n*   **Analytical & Solution-Oriented Thinking:** Passion for innovative technologies and the ability to apply them purposefully in consulting and development processes.\n*   **Background Check:** Willingness to provide a current police clearance certificate for work in security-relevant IT environments.\n\n**Relevant Technologies (Orientation, not exhaustive):**\n*   **Backend Languages:** Go, NodeJS, TypeScript, C#, .NET, Python, Rust\n*   **Databases:** Open Source Databases, PostgreSQL, MongoDB\n*   **Frontend & Frameworks:** React, Vue.js, Typescript, Svelte, Angular\n*   **Data Visualization & Monitoring:** Grafana, Power BI, Prometheus\n*   **Cloud Providers:** AWS, Azure, Google Cloud\n*   **Container Technologies:** Docker, Kubernetes\n*   **Security & Compliance Tools:** IAM Systems, Zero-Trust Frameworks, Policy-as-Code\n*   **Automation & IaC:** Terraform, Ansible, GitOps\n*   **Monitoring & Observability:** Grafana,
