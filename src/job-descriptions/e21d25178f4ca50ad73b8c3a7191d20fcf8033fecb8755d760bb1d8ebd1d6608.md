**Job Description:**
**Roles & Responsibilities :**
**Overview:**
We are seeking an experienced leader to drive Bosch’s AI, machine learning, and automation initiatives. This role requires a hands-on technologist with deep expertise in AI/ML development, capable of leading teams, managing products, and collaborating closely with clients to deliver scalable, innovative solutions that align with business goals and accelerate growth.
**Key Responsibilities:**
**Strategic Leadership:**
*   Define and own the AI and automation strategy aligned with Bosch’s business and product objectives.
*   Evaluate and adopt emerging technologies to boost competitive advantage and client outcomes.
*   Partner with business leaders to identify and execute AI-driven transformation opportunities.
*   Ensure AI initiatives deliver measurable business value including revenue growth and cost efficiency.
**Architecture & Systems Design:**
*   Architect scalable, modular, and secure AI/ML solutions tailored to client and business needs.
*   Oversee development and deployment of robust data pipelines, APIs, and AI models.
*   Make informed decisions on infrastructure, tools, and frameworks to future-proof systems.
*   Continuously assess new technologies to enhance and expand product capabilities.
**Team Leadership & Execution:**
*   Build, mentor, and lead a high-performing team of data scientists, ML engineers, and developers.
*   Actively participate in model development, code reviews, and performance tuning.
*   Foster a culture of innovation, continuous learning, and engineering excellence.
*   Guide resolution of complex technical challenges ensuring smooth project delivery.
**Process & Quality Management:**
*   Implement and refine development standards, testing protocols, and release processes.
*   Maintain clear documentation of technical workflows and product features.
*   Drive agile practices tailored to team needs and project demands.
*   Establish SOPs for development, deployment, and ongoing maintenance.
*   Track and analyze development metrics to optimize quality and velocity.
**Client Engagement & Product Development:**
*   Collaborate with client teams to translate business requirements into technical solutions.
*   Lead delivery of custom AI applications ensuring high quality and timely execution.
*   Define product roadmaps, prioritizing features based on impact and feasibility.
*   Maintain alignment with client expectations through continuous feedback and iteration.
*   Balance innovation with system stability and technical debt management.

*   15+ years in technology leadership roles with proven delivery success.

*   Experience designing scalable AI architectures on cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure).
*   Ability to communicate complex technical topics clearly to business and client stakeholders.
