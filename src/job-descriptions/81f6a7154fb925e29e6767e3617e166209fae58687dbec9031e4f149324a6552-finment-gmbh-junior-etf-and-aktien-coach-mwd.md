### Description\nFinMent is a rapidly growing, state-certified FinTech startup based in Berlin, dedicated to democratizing access to institutional investment knowledge. We empower individuals with innovative technology, proven financial expertise, and exclusive strategies previously reserved for high-net-worth investors.\n\nWe are seeking a highly motivated Junior Investment Coach to join our team. In this unique role, you will guide our investor community, helping them achieve financial literacy and independence. This position offers an unparalleled opportunity to kickstart your career in finance, combined with a fully sponsored dual study program at a prestigious private university and exclusive access to our high-value investment products.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Engage directly with clients, coaching our investor community on questions and challenges in stock trading.\n- Conduct individual and group sessions on personal finance, investment strategies, and trading psychology.\n- Support clients in building their own trading routines and investment structures.\n- Collaborate closely with the Support, Coaching, and Analysis teams, actively contributing to overall team success.\n\n### Qualifications\n- High motivation and a strong desire to learn about financial topics, with an ambition to develop into a financial specialist.\n- 6 months to 1 year of practical experience in coaching and customer support, gained through training, internships, or similar activities.\n- Proven self-motivation and a passion for clean, agile process and project management.\n- Demonstrated willingness to take responsibility for your area, even outside your comfort zone, acting result-oriented and swiftly in execution.\n- Eagerness to continuously learn and develop, both professionally and personally, actively seeking feedback and keeping team success in mind.\n- Very strong analytical and structural thinking abilities to thoroughly consider scenarios and implement solutions effectively.\n- Ideally native-level German proficiency and good conversational English.\n- Practical working experience with common financial and chart software (e.g., TWS, Tradingview) is a plus.\n- Don't worry if you don't meet every single requirement; we encourage you to apply if you're passionate about working with us!\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for anyone passionate about finance and personal development. The culture fosters continuous learning, responsibility, and collaboration, making it ideal for proactive individuals eager to make a significant contribution in a dynamic FinTech environment.
