### Description\nThis role is for someone passionate about quality and safety on civil engineering construction sites. You will serve as a crucial interface between clients, municipalities, and contracted civil engineering companies. The company prides itself on being a solution-oriented and innovative team, inviting you to become a part of their motivated crew and drive success together.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Take full responsibility for construction sites, from technical execution to final acceptance.\n- Ensure compliance with all legal requirements and construction plans on site.\n- Coordinate and lead construction meetings, meticulously documenting proceedings.\n- Prepare and review measurements, ensuring the efficient and economical use of construction materials and equipment.\n- Continuously monitor quality and construction progress, documenting all work thoroughly.\n- Manage and coordinate external contractors, verifying their measurements.\n- Support the application for permits and facilitate communication with authorities.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A certified foreman (Polier), technician, master in road or civil engineering, or a civil engineer (m/w/d), or possess a comparable education/degree (e.g., in landscape and gardening).\n- Possess many years of professional experience in civil engineering, road construction, or comparable experience in landscape and gardening.\n- Proficient in MS Office applications.\n- Knowledge of relevant regulations, including DIN18300, ZTV, RSA21, and VOB.\n- Excellent German language skills and a minimum of a Class B driver's license.\n- Willingness to travel is required, though no weekend travel is expected.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a comprehensive and highly responsible role for an experienced civil engineering professional who thrives in a dynamic environment. The company emphasizes teamwork, innovation, and a motivated culture with \"start-up flair\". They clearly value their employees by offering attractive benefits such as a company car (with private use), flexible working hours, mobile work options, and comprehensive support for travel. The focus on work-life balance, highlighted by no weekend travel and dedicated travel management support, makes this an appealing opportunity for someone looking to lead significant projects while enjoying modern employment conditions and a vibrant company culture.
