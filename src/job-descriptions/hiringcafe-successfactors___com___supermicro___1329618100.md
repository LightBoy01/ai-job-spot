**Essential Duties and Responsibilities:**
------------------------------------------

This position includes, but is not limited to, the following responsibilities:

*   Develop and execute comprehensive strategies while collaborating closely with marketing, engineering, and business development teams to deliver complete, end-to-end solutions for customers.
*   Leverage a strong technical background to create solution-oriented product requirements, focusing on hyperscale datacenters, cloud computing, HPC, virtualization, software-defined storage, and AI infrastructure.
*   Engage with customers at all organizational levels—including executives, engineering teams, and product marketing staff—to communicate Supermicro’s capabilities, address challenges, and gather customer requirements.
*   Consult with clients to assess their specific needs before initiating or recommending solutions.
*   Act as a creative thinker and problem solver, developing innovative strategies and customized solutions for customer projects.
*   Build strong, credible relationships with customers, including executive stakeholders, ensuring alignment with their goals and objectives.
*   Escalate critical issues to senior management and key vendors when necessary to ensure timely resolutions.
*   Provide technical training to internal teams, sales representatives, and customers to enhance their understanding of Supermicro’s solutions.
*   Offer third-level customer support to both internal teams and external clients as required.

**Qualifications:**
-------------------

*   Master’s degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or Computer Engineering.
*   A minimum of 10 years of experience in system/server/datacenter architecture.
*   Proven leadership abilities, including the capacity to lead global, cross-functional teams and drive product excellence.
*   Strong experience in product marketing and/or product management, with demonstrated success managing end-to-end product development cycles, from order to delivery, implementation, and customer qualification.
*   Deep understanding of computer architecture and vertical domain expertise.
*   Exceptional communication and presentation skills, with the ability to engage effectively with customers, promote solutions, and gather feedback.
*   Proficient in Linux operating systems such as Ubuntu, SUSE, Red Hat, and CentOS.
*   Experienced in virtualization, containerization, and storage technologies (e.g., VMware, VSAN, HCI, KVM, Docker, Kubernetes, CEPH, SDS).
*   Hands-on experience with AI, GPU technologies, and CUDA, with the ability to support proof-of-concept (POC) initiatives.
*   Strong understanding of market trends and the ability to anticipate and respond effectively to customer needs.
*   Fluent in both English and Chinese (written and spoken).
*   Engineering background is a plus.

**Key Skills:**

*   Strong presentation, teamwork, communication, and negotiation skills.
*   Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities.
*   Self-motivation and customer service orientation.
*   Leadership skills with keen attention to detail.

**Additional Requirements:**

Ability to travel up to 25% of the time.
