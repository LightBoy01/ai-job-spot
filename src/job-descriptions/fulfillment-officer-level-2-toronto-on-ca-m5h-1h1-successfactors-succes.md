<p>The incumbent will be responsible for certain data and limit management activities in support of Corp/FI Credit adjudication units.  In collaboration with the credit officers and CCRA Analysts, the CCRA Fulfillment Officer will ensure data integrity and compliance with internal and regulatory requirements.  This is processed via the Bank’s business support credit tools (e.g. CLens, C360, et al).</p>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>Is this role right for you? In this role, you will:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Ensure credit tools captures accurately reflects the authorizations provided by Credit on a timely basis.  Safeguard the integrity of the database by ensuring consistent and accurate input for each request. For IB &amp; Commercial portfolio, Fulfillment Officer assigned ensures that the data input in C360 is accurate as authorized Credit Presentation.</li>
<li>Ensure consistency between the authorization document and the final data credit tools (or C360 for IB/Commercial entities) input changes before final release into the system, if applies.</li>
<li>Proactively looking for ways to improve efficiencies within the unit as well as with partners whom we interact. </li>
<li>Assist Analysts (and/or management) only periodically when workload escalates (e.g. clean-up tasks, Log updates, cross portfolio coverage, projects, et al) as assigned by Analyst or management.</li>
<li>Expanded role* covering not only credit fulfillment roles as assigned by management team but leading various Fulfillment tasks as assigned by management:</li>
