Overview:
<p>How you can make a difference  </p><p>We are seeking a Senior Workforce Strategist to help lead our enterprise AI and workforce transformation agenda. This role is responsible for shaping the future of work at HealthEquity, ensuring that our 3,000-person workforce is empowered, AI-enabled, and aligned to deliver on business strategy.</p><p> </p><p>The Senior Workforce Strategist will partner with senior leaders across all Business Units to:</p><ul><li>Define enterprise workforce strategies in the context of AI and digital transformation.</li><li>Develop workforce plans that anticipate role evolution, skill needs, and capacity shifts.</li><li>Translate business strategy into actionable talent strategies, with measurable outcomes.</li><li>Serve as a thought leader in workforce modernization and organizational design.</li></ul><p> </p><p> </p><p>What you’ll be doing </p><p> Key Responsibilities</p><ul><li>Workforce Strategy Design: Build enterprise-wide workforce strategies aligned to AI adoption, business growth, and future skills needs.</li><li>AI Transformation Integration: Partner with the Enterprise AI program team to align workforce strategy with automation, augmentation, and upskilling initiatives.</li><li>Enterprise AI Roadmap Development: Collaborate with senior leaders across to define requirements and build a multi-year enterprise AI roadmap that links business priorities, workforce shifts, and technology investments.</li><li>Leadership Enablement: Advise executives on workforce implications of AI adoption, including role design, change management, and reskilling.</li><li>Metrics &amp; Reporting: Define and track KPIs such as enablement and AI adoption rates.</li><li>Culture &amp; Change Leadership: Drive messaging and programs that reinforce curiosity, growth, and responsible AI adoption.</li></ul><p> </p><p>What you will need to be successful</p><p>Qualifications</p><ul><li>8–10+ years of experience in workforce strategy, organizational design, HR strategy, or related consulting.</li><li>Strong understanding of AI/automation trends and their workforce implications.</li><li>Experience in enterprise-wide transformation programs.</li><li>Exceptional analytical and communication skills.</li><li>Ability to influence and partner at the senior leadership level.</li></ul><p> </p><p>Education</p><ul><li>Bachelor’s degree required.</li><li>Master’s degree in Organizational Development (preferred), Human Resources, Business Administration, or a related field strongly desired.</li></ul><p> </p><p>#LI-Remote</p><p>This is a remote position.</p>
