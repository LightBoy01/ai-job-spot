Career Opportunities: Generative AI Engineer (4564)
===================================================

               

Requisition ID **4564** - Posted  - **Yazılım** - Location (1)

[  Job Description Print Preview](javascript:void\(0\))

  

 

We are looking for a **“Generative AI Engineer**” who will be able to work with enthusiasm to implement solutions in the field of development to join our **Data and Artificial Intelligence Applications team.**

We believe that highly competitive solutions can only be developed by motivated and enthusiastic teams. That’s why our team members have been selected among the top engineers and specialists in the market.

We are experts in developing solutions for financial organizations. If you want to be part of this dynamic team, come & join us and share our goal that is to take the technology to the next level with creative, fast, efficient, and secure solutions.

**Here are the details of our job posting:**

**What makes you a good fit:**

*   Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science (or equivalent experience)
*   At least 3+ years of software development experience
*   Strong coding skills with Python
*   Excellent knowledge of Django, Flask, FastAPI technologies
*   Extensive experience with SQLAlchemy, Alembic migrations, and designing robust Pydantic schemas for data validation and serialization in Python-based applications.
*   Experience working with cloud-based AI providers like OpenAI, Azure, Google, Meta, etc.
*   Deep knowledge of Langchain framework and its modules such as Retrieval, Chains, Agents, Memory
*   Experience in working with Multi-Agent frameworks (e.g., CrewAI, LangGraph, AutoGen, Magnetic-One ).
*   Experience in Prompt Engineering.
*   Proven experience in designing and developing production-level prompts tailored for various AI models and use cases
*   Solid understanding of the vector databases(Pinecone, Chroma, Qdrant, Faiss)
*   Hands-on experience with implementing production-grade guardrails for AI systems to ensure reliability, safety, and ethical compliance.
*   Possesses a solid perspective on Agent-Oriented Programming (AOP) and its application in designing and developing intelligent, autonomous systems.
*   In-depth knowledge of working with containerized cloud environments (Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, GCP, Azure, Databricks, etc) and associated machine learning products, e.g. Amazon SageMaker, Azure ML

**What you’ll experience working at Softtech:**

*   Implement and develop Generative AI solutions for business needs. 
*   Work with large language models (LLMs) or small language models (SLMs) to create innovative applications and solutions.
*   Develop and integrate AI-related APIs from providers like Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI. 
*   Write clean, scalable, and efficient code following best practices in AI development. 
*   Collaborate with senior engineers and architects to ensure the robustness and scalability of AI solutions. 
*   Conduct unit tests and debugging to ensure software reliability. 
*   Stay up to date with emerging AI technologies and frameworks, applying them where relevant. 
*   Participate in team discussions and contribute to knowledge sharing.

**What we offer you:**  
  

*   Private medical insurance and group life insurance

*   Company contributory in individual retirement or cumulative life insurance 3 months after working with us
*   Employee and Employer Contribution Plan for individual retirement support (Optional)
*   Monthly meal payment
*   Flexible remote working support
*   Transportation fee or shuttle option according to your preference
*   Covered travel and accommodation expenses if necessary
*   Employee discounts – including a huge number of retailers via İş Grubu Ailem
*   Continuously learning and development opportunities
*   Health and well-being seminars and initiatives via Softtech Academy
*   Employee support program through a corporate consulting firm
*   Support for postgraduate education, including administrative leave and financial incentives for those pursuing a Master's or PhD degree
*   Study leave and support for studying for a qualification or other accredited development relevant to your role
*   Attending to technical congress and conferences
*   Celebrate your birthday with a day off

**About us:**

As one of Turkey's leading technology companies, we have been operating at a total of 6 different locations in Turkey and USA with more than 1600 employees since 2006.

With the strength of our experience in banking and finance industry and experienced human resources, we are developing customer-oriented solutions for our business partners operating in diverse areas.

Our mission is to transform all our customers including mainly İş Bankası into digital companies by using our efficient and high-energy ecosystem.

We recognize that our people are our strength and the diverse talents. We are an equal opportunity employer and welcome applications from any individuals without any restrictions at our company.
