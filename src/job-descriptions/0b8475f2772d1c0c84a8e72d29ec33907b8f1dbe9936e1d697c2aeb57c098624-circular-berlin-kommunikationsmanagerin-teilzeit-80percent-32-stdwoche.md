### Description\\nCircular Berlin is a non-profit organization dedicated to advancing Berlin's transition towards a circular city. We integrate knowledge, practical projects, and community engagement, making our experiences accessible worldwide. We are looking for an experienced Communication Manager (d/w/m) to lead and implement Circular Berlin's external communication strategy. Starting in March 2026, this position requires strong strategic and communication skills, reports directly to the Executive Board, and coordinates a small operational communication team. The role is a part-time position (80%, 32 hours/week) with an initial fixed-term contract of 2 years, with the possibility of extension. The ideal candidate will be based in Berlin and fluent in both German and English.\\n\\n### Responsibilities\\nThis role is divided into three key areas:\\n\\n**1. Strategy (approx. 20% of working time)**\\n*   Develop a deep understanding of Circular Berlin's identity, mission, values, and stakeholders, translating this into effective stakeholder engagement strategies and impactful storytelling.\\n*   Formulate cohesive and actionable strategies for various communication channels, including social media, newsletters, the website, blog, campaigns, strategic event participation, and press/media relations.\\n*   Define annual objectives and KPI targets for all communication activities.\\n*   Provide strategic coordination for the communication team (one part-time employee + volunteers).\\n*   Strategically represent Circular Berlin at public events, networking meetings, and conferences.\\n\\n**2. Management (approx. 50% of working time)**\\n*   Internally manage a European project focused on developing an international campaign for Urban Resource Centers across various European regions. This includes coordinating with European partners and external agencies, managing the project budget, and overseeing campaign implementation (approximately one day per week).\\n*   Create and maintain a comprehensive communication calendar for all Circular Berlin projects.\\n*   Monitor and evaluate communication campaigns and engagement initiatives, adapting strategies based on stakeholder feedback, fundraising outcomes, and analytics.\\n*   Maintain the internal stakeholder database and progressively migrate it to a CRM system.\\n*   Ensure consistent adherence to Circular Berlin's visual identity across all channels and projects.\\n\\n**3. Operational Implementation (approx. 30% of working time)**\\n*   Execute all necessary communication activities for projects in collaboration with the team.\\n*   Create content in German and English, including social media posts, newsletters, website texts, event descriptions, and press releases.\\n*   Produce visual content aligned with the corporate identity, such as graphics, videos, presentations, and layouts for reports and whitepapers.\\n*   Plan and execute specific communication campaigns jointly with team members.\\n\\n### Qualifications\\n**What you bring to Circular Berlin:**\\n*   Proven experience (5+ years) in communication roles within corporate or NGO environments, including at least 3 years in leading multi-stakeholder projects, team leadership, and budget responsibility.\\n*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills in German and English (native or C2 level), encompassing strong storytelling and impact messaging.\\n*   Proficient graphic and design skills, with experience in creating engaging visual communication materials.\\n*   Understanding of global media landscapes and social media platforms; curiosity for new tools to optimize and automate communication processes.\\n*   Ability to structuredly involve and update internal stakeholders and team members.\\n*   Passion for strategic planning and operational implementation, with strong task prioritization abilities.\\n*   Capability to thrive in agile environments and flexibly adapt to changes.\\n*   Residency in Berlin or surrounding area; willingness to work at least 2 days per week in the office.\\n*   Experience with tools such as Figma, Canva, WordPress, Google Suite, Zoom, Slack, and Notion.\\n*   Solid knowledge of the Circular Economy and enthusiasm for the topic will be highly valued in the selection process.\\n*   An existing network of relevant stakeholders in media, communication, or NGOs is an advantage.\\n*   Experience in fundraising communication for climate initiatives is an advantage.\\n\\n### Author's Take\\nThis is a truly impactful role for an experienced Communication Manager passionate about sustainability and the circular economy.
