Company:

Mercer

Description:
------------

We are seeking a talented individual to join our US Health Specialty Practice team at Mercer. This role will be based in New York. This is a hybrid role that has a requirement of working at least three days a week in the office.  
Mercer is seeking a strategic and results-driven Digital Products and AI Leader to shape and deliver digital product solutions for Mercer’s US Health business. This role will lead cross-functional collaboration across business, data science, actuarial, research, and technology teams to create commercially impactful technology-driven offerings.

We will count on you to:

*   Lead the development, roadmap, and delivery of digital products structuring releases against key features and milestones within Mercer’s US Health business, translating strategy into actionable product initiatives
    
*   Inventory and manage data assets and partnerships
    
*   Manage stakeholder engagement across a matrixed organization, including actuaries, consultants, and technology teams to build alignment and ensure relevance
    
*   Perform market analysis and user research to identify opportunities, understand customer needs, and evaluate competition
    
*   Translate business and client challenges into solution hypotheses and product requirements that deliver intuitive, scalable, and client-centric solutions, working through ambiguity and iterating in collaboration with cross-functional teams
    
*   Design and deploy intelligent product features using AI, generative models, and data modeling to translate highly complex data into meaningful, valuable insights
    

What you need to have:

*   Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field (e.g., Computer Science, Business, Economics); MBA or advanced degree a plus
    
*   10+ years of experience in Product Management, with a track record of leading B2B or platform-based SaaS products from concept to scale
    
*   Experience in global consulting or insurance organizations
    
*   Familiarity with relevant technologies, platforms, and data analysis methodologies
    

What makes you stand out:

*   Familiarity with AI/LLM-based product design, B2B platform design, and intelligent automation
    
*   Certifications in Agile, Scrum, or Product Leadership (CSPO, CSP-PO, SAFe, etc.)
    
*   Comfortable driving transformation within complex organizations
    

Why join our team:

*   We help you be your best through professional development opportunities, interesting work and supportive leaders.
    
*   We foster a vibrant and inclusive culture where you can work with talented colleagues to create new solutions and have impact for colleagues, clients and communities.
    
*   Our scale enables us to provide a range of career opportunities, as well as benefits and rewards to enhance your well-being.
    

Mercer, a business of Marsh McLennan (NYSE: MMC), is a global leader in helping clients realize their investment objectives, shape the future of work and enhance health and retirement outcomes for their people. Marsh McLennan is a global leader in risk, strategy and people, advising clients in 130 countries across four businesses: Marsh, Guy Carpenter, Mercer and Oliver Wyman. With annual revenue of $24 billion and more than 90,000 colleagues, Marsh McLennan helps build the confidence to thrive through the power of perspective.
