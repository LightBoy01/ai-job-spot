### Description\nGFN GmbH is an IT-specialized education provider in Germany, dedicated to supporting individuals nationwide in their further education and career reorientation. This 12-month trainee program is your springboard to becoming a certified trainer, culminating in the IHK instructor qualification. You'll gain hands-on experience and proactively shape the world of professional retraining and further education from day one. We believe in your potential and your strengths!\n\n### Responsibilities\nFrom day one, you will receive optimal preparation for your future role as a trainer (m/f/x) at GFN. Our experienced colleagues will provide professional, methodological, and didactical guidance on your journey to becoming a trainer. Subsequently, you will take over online and in-person training sessions in your specialized field.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   You are our top trainee (m/f/x) if you are characterized by a reliable and team-oriented working style.\n*   Have you always been good at explaining things to others and enjoy being in the spotlight? Are you distinguished by your affinity and enthusiasm for IT topics?\n*   Do you bring a technical background (e.g., from further training, a technical degree, a relevant apprenticeship, or professional experience)?\n*   Do you have very good German language skills (at least C1)?\n\nEven if you don't have a completed IT degree, please apply – we look forward to your individual qualifications.\nPlease write a few sentences about your motivation to become a trainer (m/f/x) and (optional) about your previous experience as a trainer (m/f/x), tutor (m/f/x), coach (m/f/x), or in a similar role in the education sector.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for individuals passionate about both IT and education. The comprehensive 12-month paid trainee program, complete with IHK certification preparation, strong mentorship through a \"Tandem Partner,\" and regular peer exchange, offers a clear and supportive path into a rewarding career as a trainer. GFN stands out with its commitment to employee well-being, evidenced by an impressive benefits package including 30 vacation days, Jobrad leasing, EGYM Wellpass, an internet allowance, and a subsidized Deutschland-Ticket. The company actively fosters a prejudice-free, appreciative, and diverse culture, making it an ideal environment for those looking to grow professionally and make a tangible impact. This role is perfect for someone eager to learn, contribute, and thrive within a supportive community.
