### Description\nAre you a university student with a solid understanding of marketing strategies and design tools? Do you have a burning desire to apply your skills to a real project, working independently yet guided by experienced colleagues? Is your university requiring a mandatory internship where you truly want to gain valuable experience? If so, you are exactly the person we are looking for! This internship offers a unique opportunity to shape our digital presence and gain hands-on experience in a dynamic startup environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Conceive, design, and implement the new corporate website (layout, UX, accessibility, responsive design).\n- Contribute to the content plan for the website (texts, images, videos, landing pages).\n- Create wireframes/mockups and closely coordinate with development and marketing teams.\n- Design graphic elements for the website (headers, icons, banners, CTA modules).\n- Support SEO-relevant aspects and performance optimization.\n- Maintain content assets, media databases, and style guides.\n\n### Qualifications\nYou are a great fit if:\n- You have a passion for customer-centric work and strong communication skills in German.\n- You are currently pursuing a degree in Media Informatics, Media Studies, Visual Communication, Marketing, or a similar field.\n- Reliability and commitment are among your key characteristics.\n- You are available for a full-time (or near full-time) internship for 2-6 months.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional internship for an ambitious student looking to make a tangible impact. You'll be given significant responsibility and ample room for your own ideas, all while receiving professional guidance from a seasoned team. It's a fantastic chance to dive deep into the world of smart healthcare prevention and experience a true startup atmosphere characterized by passion, rhythm, and a collaborative spirit. This role promises hands-on experience, real project ownership, and invaluable insights into a growing industry.
