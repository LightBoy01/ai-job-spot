**Company Description:**

We are SGS – the world's leading testing, inspection and certification company. With 99,500 employees across 2,500 locations, we generate a unique and massive dataset on global trade, product quality, and sustainability. We are now launching a new Digital Hub in Madrid to transform this data into intelligent products and services. This is your opportunity to help build the AI infrastructure that will redefine our industry.

**Job Description:**

We're looking for an experienced AI Engineer to help build intelligent systems that leverage one of the world's most unique industrial datasets. In this role, you'll work on production-grade Generative AI solutions, developing and deploying AI systems that solve real business challenges across SGS's global operations.

As a Senior AI Engineer in our new Digital Hub, you will design, build, and deploy advanced AI solutions that automate and enhance critical business processes. You'll work with cutting-edge Generative AI technologies, collaborate with cross-functional teams, and contribute to establishing our AI engineering best practices.

**What You'll Build and Own:**

*   **Develop Production AI Solutions:** Design and implement Generative AI applications using LLMs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and agentic workflows to solve business-critical problems
*   **Model Development & Optimization:** Work with both commercial and open-source models (e.g., GPT-4, Claude, Llama, Mistral), including fine-tuning on proprietary data when beneficial
*   **Build Intelligent Systems:** Create AI-powered workflows and agents that can interact with tools, APIs, and databases to automate complex tasks like report analysis, compliance checks, and document generation
*   **Implement AI Infrastructure:** Build and maintain robust AI/ML pipelines using modern frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex, etc.) and MLOps tools, ensuring scalability and reliability
*   **Collaborate Across Teams:** Work closely with product managers, data engineers, and domain experts to translate business requirements into technical solutions
*   **Drive Best Practices:** Contribute to establishing engineering standards, evaluation frameworks, and reusable components for AI development across the organization

**Qualifications:**

**Required Skills & Experience:**

*   **Experience:** 5+ years in software engineering with 2+ years focused on AI/ML, including recent hands-on experience with LLMs and Generative AI
*   **Generative AI Expertise:** Proven track record building and deploying LLM-based applications, including prompt engineering, RAG systems, and model evaluation
*   **AI Frameworks:** Experience with frameworks like LangChain, LlamaIndex, or similar tools for building LLM applications
*   **Programming:** Strong Python skills and experience with ML libraries (PyTorch, TensorFlow, scikit-learn)
*   **Infrastructure:** Hands-on experience with vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant, Chroma), containerization (Docker), and cloud platforms (GCP, AWS, or Azure)
*   **Data Engineering:** Ability to work with unstructured data, build data pipelines, and create robust APIs (FastAPI, Flask)

**Nice to Have:**

*   Experience fine-tuning or training models from scratch
*   Familiarity with Kubernetes and MLOps platforms
*   Knowledge of agent frameworks and multi-step reasoning systems
*   Experience in regulated industries or with compliance-related applications
*   Contributions to open-source AI projects
