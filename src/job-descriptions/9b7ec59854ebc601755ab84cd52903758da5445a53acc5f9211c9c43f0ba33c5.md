**Ready to design products that make a real impact for global users?**
**Are you excited to promote user-centred design across our culture?**
**Join our team supporting customers in cloud, security, delivery solutions.**
Akamai seeks a Senior User Experience Designer to design for enterprise cloud, security, and delivery products. Collaborate within cross-functional teams to create impactful experiences that empower users and deliver measurable results.
**What you'll do as a Senior UX Designer**
You will play a significant role in leading the design for major products and features. You'll be working closely with product owners and lead design efforts from concept through launch. You will have a proven track record of shipping great products that users love.
As a User Experience Designer Senior, you will be responsible for:
*   Leading end-to-end design efforts for complex features and products, from concept through delivery
*   Collaborating closely with product managers, engineers, researchers, and other designers
*   Translating business and user requirements into intuitive workflows, wireframes, mock-ups and prototypes
*   Facilitating workshops, design reviews, and feedback sessions with stakeholders
*   Using data and research (quantitative and qualitative) to inform design decisions
*   Contributing to and evolve our design system, ensuring consistency and accessibility
*   Mentoring junior designers and help drive best practices within the team
**Do what you love**
To be successful in this role you will:
*   Possess 5-8y of experience in UX/UI design within B2B Saas enterpriseproduct environments documented in the submitted portfolio.
*   Showcase a portfolio demonstrating user-focused design solutions for intricate challenges.
*   Contribute to and design effectively within a comprehensive design system.
*   Demonstrate expertise designing accessible, inclusive user experiences.
*   Demonstrate initiative while embracing ongoing learning and personal growth.
*   Conduct or collaborate on user research initiatives.
*   Gain experience in data visualisation or dashboard design.
*   Explore emerging technologies to elevate user experience with AI-enhanced solutions.
