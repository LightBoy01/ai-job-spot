**Location:** Remote (USA only) **Employment Type:** Full-time

### About the Role

Our client, a company evolving to empower product teams with both expert training and **AI-powered tools**, is looking for a **Senior AI Engineer**. You'll play a key role in shaping how their platform uses Large Language Models (LLMs) to deliver customer value. This is a highly collaborative, product-focused role where you will turn cutting-edge AI capabilities into practical features for their Insight Analytics platform.

This is a **fully remote** position, so our client requires someone who is self-motivated and excels at clear, asynchronous communication.

### What You'll Own

*   **Prompt Engineering:** Design, experiment with, and refine prompts and system instructions to maximize the effectiveness and reliability of LLMs.
*   **LLM-Powered Features:** Collaborate with product and engineering teams to develop and integrate new backend features, enhancing the platform with intelligent capabilities.
*   **Continuous Improvement:** Continuously evaluate LLM outputs via internal testing and user feedback, iterating on prompts, model settings, and data pipelines.
*   **Model Evaluation:** Design and implement evaluation frameworks, monitor key metrics, and develop automated testing pipelines to ensure consistent quality.
*   **Coding & Integration:** Write and maintain product code (primarily **TypeScript**, with some **Python**) to implement LLM interactions, including building **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** pipelines and handling vector embeddings. A majority of your work will be in-code, shipping product.

**Requirements**

*   **3+ years** of experience in software engineering and AI/LLM development (open to less experience if pedigree is strong).
*   Strong background in building and consuming APIs, working with databases, and cloud services.
