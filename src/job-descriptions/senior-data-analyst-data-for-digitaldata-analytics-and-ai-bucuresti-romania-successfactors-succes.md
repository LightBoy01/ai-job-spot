<b>About our team</b>

<p>BCR is the place where you learn, grow, and contribute to shaping the future of banking. We are recognized as a school of excellence in banking, a top employer in the banking sector, and a team open to learning, where every colleague feels seen and valued.</p>
<p>We are an organization that values people and supports them in having a life that brings fulfillment, security, and balance.</p>
<p>The Data, Analytics &amp; AI Division is in charge to transform BCR in a data driven organization being responsible for end-to-end delivery of AI, BI &amp; Advanced analytics solutions, data governance and quality. We combine business and technical knowledge at the same time, and we coordinate and define data strategy for the entire organization.</p>
<p>Therefore, if you are a team player and you know what a data warehouse or a data lake is and most important, you know how to design, model, store and extract the business relevant information from them, you may find our team to be perfect for you.</p>


<b>How you will contribute</b>

<ul>
<li>Be a trusted partner for the business representatives and collaborate closely with them. Identify their business needs and use your BI expertise to transform their needs into high level features;</li>
<li>Discover the current business and data process flow and design the feature solution that fits both the system architecture standards and the business owner expectations;</li>
<li>Define the feature acceptance criteria and design the Q&amp;A testing strategy and plan;</li>
<li>Document de technical implementation steps and collaborate with the ETL developers during the feature development and Q&amp;A validation phase;</li>
<li>Be passionate, creative, eager to learn and not the least “Have fun in your daily journey”.</li>
</ul>


<b>What will help you be successful</b>

<ul>
<li>Bachelor's degree in economics, technical, mathematics, computer science;</li>
<li>Good knowledge regarding banking products and processes;</li>
<li>Basic knowledge of Data Management;</li>
<li>Solid skills regarding DWH architecture – physicals structures, data integration, reporting;</li>
<li>Oracle Database SQL Certified Associate Certification or at least 1 year of day-to-day work experience with Oracle SQL;</li>
<li>Databricks knowledge</li>
<li>Knowledge of scrum methodology and experience working in an agile collaborative environment is a plus;</li>
<li>Good knowledge regarding implementation steps of BI / IT solutions;</li>
<li>Good communication skills, self-organized, opened to change, team player.</li>
</ul>
