### Description\nOur client is a globally recognized brand manufacturer for timeless kitchen design and one of the leading, style-defining brands in the international luxury segment. As an innovator, the company sets international standards for the highest precision and innovative functionality. Every detail is a conscious decision, every component the intelligent implementation of a design vision.\n\nTo continue this path as an innovator, they are seeking a Product Developer who will continuously advance what is technically feasible and translate the client's design language into functional, innovative products at the interface between design and modern, individualized series production.\n\n### Responsibilities\n**Your Mission: Drive Innovation & Shape the Future**\n\nAs part of a team of four colleagues, you will be among the technical pioneers behind the products of tomorrow.\n\n*   **Holistic Product Development:** You are responsible for the entire development process for your projects – from the initial idea to series production. You will develop sophisticated system furniture and assemblies and elegantly integrate forward-looking fittings and intelligent materials into the client's modular system.\n*   **Technical Design & Feasibility:** You serve as the interface between design and engineering. You translate aesthetic visions into intelligent, manufacturable technical concepts.\n*   **Innovation:** You proactively identify new elements and materials (e.g., wood, metal, glass, hybrid materials) and assess their applicability for future product generations.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   **Professional Expertise:** You hold a degree (Master / Dipl.-Ing.) in Wood Technology or a comparable engineering qualification. You combine practical understanding with technical precision and a passion for outstanding design.\n*   **Mindset:** You are not satisfied with the first best solution but seek smart, innovative answers to customer wishes and technical challenges.\n*   **CAD / IT Skills:** You are proficient in using a common 3D CAD system (preferably SolidWorks) as well as PDM systems.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a truly exciting opportunity for a Senior Product Developer to make a significant impact within a globally renowned luxury brand. The role offers the rare chance to not just contribute, but to fundamentally shape the technical signature of the next decade in kitchen design. The company fosters an open and appreciative team culture, deeply committed to innovation and the pursuit of perfect detail. With a secure position at a premium brand, candidates can expect excellent prospects for personal and professional growth. This role is ideal for someone passionate about blending exquisite design with technical innovation and precision engineering.
