**Job Description:**

**Location:** NYC (Hybrid – at least 3 days onsite/week)  
**Visa Sponsorship:** We offer sponsorship under specific treaty-based U.S. work visas, including TN (for eligible Canadian and Mexican citizens). We do not support or transfer other visa types (e.g., H-1B).

Our client is an early-stage startup building a scalable, AI-powered platform to revolutionize procurement. As a Senior Full Stack Software Engineer, you’ll join the founding engineering team, working closely with AI/ML experts to deliver innovative features and shape the technical roadmap.

**Role and responsibilities**

*   Design, build, and scale core platform features across front-end (React/TypeScript) and back-end (NestJS/Node.js).
*   Manage and optimize AWS infrastructure including containers, databases, networking, and monitoring.
*   Integrate AI/LLM-powered services, APIs, and data pipelines into production-ready applications.
*   Collaborate with AI/ML engineers to connect models with scalable workflows.
*   Lead technical discussions, contribute to system architecture, and drive best practices.
*   Ensure high-quality code through reviews, testing, and engineering standards.
*   Mentor junior engineers and help shape team culture and processes.
*   Influence product roadmap, making scalability and build-vs-buy trade-offs.

**Qualifications:**

**Must have:**

*   6+ years of full-stack engineering experience.
*   Advanced proficiency in JavaScript/TypeScript, React, and modern front-end architectures.
*   Strong back-end expertise with Node.js/NestJS, RESTful APIs, and async flows.
*   Hands-on experience with PostgreSQL or similar databases.
*   Proven ability to manage and scale AWS infrastructure.
*   Experience integrating AI/LLM APIs into production systems.
*   Familiarity with infrastructure as code (Terraform, CDK, or CloudFormation).
*   Experience leading projects or mentoring engineers.
*   Excellent communication and collaboration skills.

**Nice to have**

*   Experience in fast-paced startup environments.
*   Prior work with data pipelines or MLOps.
*   Contributions to open-source or tech communities.
