### Description
Our client is an established financial services company operating within an international mobility group. The company actively supports the transition to new mobility with innovative solutions, particularly in the fields of electromobility and flexible usage concepts. The internal work culture is characterized by openness, significant scope for empowerment and design, and a clear focus on continuous change and digitalization.

You can expect a modern work environment with flexible working hour models and the possibility of mobile working. An internal training academy offers diverse professional and personal development opportunities. Additionally, you will benefit from attractive supplementary benefits such as a vehicle leasing program and an employer-funded company pension scheme.

### Responsibilities
*   Provide strategic and operational HR support to assigned departments, acting as a competent contact person for managers and employees.
*   Advise on all HR-relevant topics throughout the employee lifecycle, from workforce planning and recruiting to development and separation.
*   Proactively co-design and implement HR initiatives, change processes, and organizational development strategies.
*   Analyze personnel structures, skills, and needs, deriving actionable measures based on KPIs and HR data.
*   Optimize and digitalize HR processes in close coordination with relevant interfaces across the organization.
*   Take ownership and independently manage special topics and projects within the HR domain.

### Qualifications
*   Completed degree with a focus on Human Resources, Business Psychology, Business Administration, or a similar field.
*   Several years of professional experience as an HR Business Partner, ideally within an international corporate environment.
*   Sound knowledge of labor law, social security law, and tax law.
*   Demonstrated strong consulting expertise, a proactive "hands-on" mentality, and a genuine enjoyment of shaping and designing initiatives.
*   An independent, structured way of working, coupled with a strong service and solution-oriented approach.
*   Excellent communication skills in both German and English, along with proficient use of modern HR tools.

### Author's Take
This role offers a unique opportunity for a seasoned HR Business Partner to directly influence the strategic direction of an innovative financial services company within the dynamic international mobility sector. With a culture that champions openness, empowerment, and continuous learning, you won't just be supporting HR functions – you'll be actively shaping the future workforce, driving digitalization, and fostering a modern, flexible work environment. This is ideal for someone who thrives on strategic challenges, data-driven decisions, and a hands-on approach to organizational change and development.
