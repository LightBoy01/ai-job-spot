<p><strong>AI Developer – Microsoft Workflow &amp; AI Integration</strong></p>
<p><strong>Position Overview</strong></p>
<p>We are seeking a hands-on <strong>AI Automation Developer</strong> with deep experience in the <strong>Microsoft Tech Stack</strong> and a strong understanding of <strong>AI applications</strong>. This role will focus on designing scalable, repeatable automation patterns that integrate <strong>AI agents</strong> (e.g., Copilot) with Microsoft workflows such as <strong>Power Automate</strong>, enabling intelligent batch processing of documents and data. The developer will collaborate closely with HACH leadership from ideation through production deployment. The developer will work cross-functional collaboration with data scientists, business analysts, and IT teams.</p>


 &nbsp;

<p><strong>Key Responsibilities</strong></p>
<p><strong>AI-Driven Automation Development</strong></p>
<ul>
 <li>Architect and implement Microsoft workflows that ingest, reason over, and act on large volumes of structured and unstructured data.</li>
 <li>Design and prompt-engineer Copilot agents to generate summaries, reports, and formatted outputs based on HACH specifications.</li>
 <li>Build seamless pipelines between <strong>Power Automate</strong>, <strong>Copilot Studio</strong>, and mission-critical systems.</li>
 <li>Develop reusable automation patterns for document processing, classification, and enrichment using AI.</li>
 <li>The emphasis will be designing solutions that scale across departments or business units.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>DevOps &amp; Lifecycle Management</strong></p>
<ul>
 <li>Create promotion pipelines with version control, automated testing, and rollback strategies.</li>
 <li>Define branching and release strategies aligned with HACH’s SDLC.</li>
 <li>Ensure robust unit testing and maintain documentation in accordance with HACH standards.</li>
</ul>


 &nbsp;

<p><strong>Must-Have Qualifications</strong></p>
<ul>
 <li><strong>3–4+ years</strong> of experience in automation development using Microsoft technologies.</li>
 <li><strong>1+ year</strong> of hands-on experience with <strong>Copilot Studio</strong>, including building and deploying agents in production.</li>
 <li>Proven track record of end-to-end delivery of Microsoft automation solutions.</li>
 <li>Strong understanding of <strong>Generative AI / LLMs</strong> and their integration into enterprise workflows.</li>
 <li>Excellent communication skills with the ability to translate technical concepts for non-technical stakeholders.</li>
</ul>
