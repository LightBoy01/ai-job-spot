**Job Description:**

**Your Career**

We are seeking a Principal Software Engineer with deep expertise in designing, building, and scaling AI-powered platforms. In this role, you will help shape the future of our agentic platform by leveraging advanced machine learning (ML) techniques to tackle complex, large-scale challenges and deliver impactful customer experiences.

As a technical leader, you will take full ownership of key product features—leading architecture, design, and implementation—while working closely with cross-functional teams. Your expertise and insights will be instrumental in guiding the technical vision of our platform. We highly value innovation, initiative, and a strong sense of ownership, and we are looking for someone who thrives on pushing the boundaries of what’s possible with secure, cloud-native, and scalable AI agents.

**This role is located at our Santa Clara Headquarters Campus 3 days a week.**

**Your Impact**

*   Responsible for the complete software development life cycle including requirement analysis, design, development, deployment and support
*   Write clean, testable, readable, scalable and maintainable Python / Go lang code that scales and performs well for thousands of customers
*   Participate actively and contribute to design and development discussions
*   Develop solid understanding and be able to explain advanced Cloud Computing and Cloud Security concepts to others
*   Be able to clearly communicate goals and desired outcomes to internal project teams

**Qualifications:**

**Your Experience** 

*   Extensive hands-on programming skills in one - Python, Go
*   Experience with cloud-native service development stack on GCP
*   Strong knowledge of databases SQL
*   Experience building scalable REST API services, CI/CD pipelines
*   Experience with GCP strongly desired
*   Ability to explain the architecture and design with complete rationale behind design decisions
*   Excellent written and communication skills - passion for learning, collaborating with others
