Career Opportunities: Master Thesis: Transformer World Models for AI Agents (773638)
====================================================================================

Requisition ID **773638** - Posted  - Country/Area (1) - state/province (1)

Join our Team
-------------

**About us:**

Ericsson Research is pioneering AI-driven automation for complex telecommunications systems, aiming to improve network efficiency, sustainability, and user experience worldwide. Our AI team focuses on developing advanced transformer world models to enable autonomous AI agents in mobile networks. By combining cutting-edge machine learning with telecom expertise, we strive to push the limits of intelligent network automation.

**What you will do:**

*   Explore transformer-based world models to accelerate AI agent training and enable reusability across multiple tasks and agents.  
      
    
*   Develop model-based reinforcement learning algorithms using transformer environment models.  
      
    
*   Investigate extensions for multi-agent and multi-task learning scenarios.  
      
    
*   Evaluate algorithms using both custom simulators and real network data.  
      
    
*   Collaborate closely with AI researchers in a dynamic research environment.  
      
    
*   Contribute to innovative research that improves automation in mobile networks.

**The skills you will bring:**

*   Currently pursuing a Master’s degree with most coursework completed and strong academic performance.  
      
    
*   Excellent programming skills in Python.  
      
    
*   Hands-on experience with deep learning frameworks.  
      
    
*   Solid knowledge and genuine interest in reinforcement learning (practical experience is a plus).  
      
    
*   Creative mindset, curiosity, and the ability to learn quickly.  
      
    
*   Strong communication skills and a collaborative approach to teamwork.  
      
    
*   Proficiency in written and spoken English, with the ability to excel in an international team.

**Application:**  
Please submit your application with a resume, a short cover letter and academic transcripts from both your BSc and your MSc.

**Why join Ericsson?**

At Ericsson, you´ll have an outstanding opportunity. The chance to use your skills and imagination to push the boundaries of what´s possible. To build solutions never seen before to some of the world’s toughest problems. You´ll be challenged, but you won’t be alone.
