### **Job Overview**

We’re looking for a **Sales & Finance Operations Intern** who is passionate about data, AI, and business. In this role, you’ll work closely with our sales and finance teams to build dashboards, analyze data, create workflows, and explore AI-driven solutions to real business problems. This internship is designed for students or early-career professionals who want to develop a career at the intersection of **data, AI, and business strategy**.

### **Key Responsibilities**

*   Build dashboards and reports to track sales and financial performance.
*   Analyze data to identify trends, gaps, and opportunities for improvement.
*   Support workflow automation and process optimization across sales and finance.
*   Assist in designing and testing AI-powered agents to solve business use cases.
*   Work with stakeholders to understand sales processes, challenges, and opportunities.
*   Contribute to building scalable solutions that improve decision-making and efficiency.

### **Requirements**

*   Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in **Business, Data Analytics, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field**.
*   Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
*   Interest in **sales processes, finance operations, and business strategy**.
*   Basic knowledge of tools like **Google Sheets, SQL, Python, or BI platforms** (e.g., Tableau, Power BI, Looker) is a plus.
*   Familiarity or curiosity about **AI tools and automation**.
*   Strong communication skills and ability to work collaboratively.

### **What You’ll Gain**

*   Hands-on experience at the intersection of **sales, finance, data, and AI**.
*   Exposure to building real-world dashboards, workflows, and AI solutions.
*   Mentorship from experienced Sales and Finance Ops professionals.
*   A chance to shape processes in a **fast-growing SaaS company**.
*   Opportunity for full-time consideration upon successful completion.
