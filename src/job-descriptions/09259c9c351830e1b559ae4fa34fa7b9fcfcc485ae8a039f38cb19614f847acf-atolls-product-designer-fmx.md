### Description\nAtolls is the world's largest community-driven shopping platform, active in 20+ markets, helping millions make smarter spending decisions. Our 1,000+ team across 10 countries builds global-scale products where you'll have real ownership and see your impact.\n\nWe are looking for a **Midweight Product Designer** to join the **Atolls Product Design Team**. You will contribute to creating engaging digital experiences by designing intuitive, scalable, and impactful solutions. Reporting to the domain Head of Design, you'll work closely with Product Managers, Engineers, and Researchers to ensure designs are user-focused, business-driven, and aligned with strategic goals. The ideal candidate will have 4+ years of experience in digital product design, preferably in a **SaaS environment**, with a strong foundation in user-centered design, problem-solving, and collaboration.\n\n**Our Benefits:**\nAt Atolls, we believe in nurturing both your professional and personal growth. You can expect:\n- A culture that values personal and professional development, with internal mobility opportunities.\n- A supportive and open-minded team that embraces diverse perspectives and innovative ideas.\n- 32 days of paid vacation plus your birthday off, giving you the time you need to recharge.\n- A flexible hybrid working scheme to balance work and life.\n- Access to a learning budget and internal training to help you grow in your role.\n- Mental health coaching to support your well-being.\n- Regular global and local get-togethers to celebrate successes and build connections.\n- The possibility of taking a sabbatical after two years with the company.\n- A cloud-based company setup, providing flexibility and collaboration opportunities no matter where you are.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **User-Centric Problem Solving**: Leverage qualitative and quantitative insights to deeply understand user needs, pain points, and behaviors, translating them into thoughtful and effective design solutions.\n- **Experience Design**: Craft seamless user flows, intuitive interfaces, and engaging interactions that enhance the overall product experience.\n- **Iterative Improvement**: Validate and refine design proposals using user feedback, usability testing, and analytics to ensure continuous improvement and measurable impact.\n- **Collaboration**: Work closely with Product Managers and Engineers to align design initiatives with product goals, ensuring solutions balance user needs with business objectives.\n- **Brand & Design System Alignment**: Design experiences consistent with our brand identity, leveraging and contributing to our dynamic design system to maintain consistency across products.\n- **Execution & Delivery**: Confidently manage your own workload while ensuring high standards of execution, from conceptual wireframes to pixel-perfect UI designs.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **4+ years of experience** in digital product design, preferably within a SaaS environment.\n- A **strong portfolio** demonstrating design craft, problem-solving, and attention to detail.\n- Proven experience designing for **web and native applications**, balancing aesthetics with functionality.\n- Experience with **Figma**, **design systems**, variables and design tokens.\n- A passion for **user-centric design**, with a keen ability to interpret user profiles, customer journeys, and stakeholder needs to create informed and impactful experiences.\n- Confident knowledge of **design techniques and best practices**, staying up to date with the latest UX/UI trends and technologies.\n- Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with fluency in **English (written and verbal)**.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a Mid-Senior Product Designer who thrives in a fast-paced, global, and highly collaborative environment. Atolls offers a significant chance to impact millions of users daily on a platform with massive scale. The company clearly values employee well-being and growth, evidenced by generous benefits like extensive vacation, hybrid work flexibility, learning budgets, mental health support, and even sabbaticals. If you're passionate about user-centric design, enjoy working with a dynamic design system, and want to contribute to a product that truly makes a difference in people's shopping decisions, this role at Atolls provides a compelling mix of challenge, impact, and support.
