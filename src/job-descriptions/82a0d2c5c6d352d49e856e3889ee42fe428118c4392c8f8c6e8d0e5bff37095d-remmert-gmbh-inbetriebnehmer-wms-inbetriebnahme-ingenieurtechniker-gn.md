### Description\nRemmert is a family-owned company specializing in automated storage and logistics systems for sheet metal, long goods, and workpieces. Since 1945, we've focused on space-saving storage and fully automated material handling. We operate in the future-oriented machine building, automation, and logistics industry. We foster a culture of growth, open communication, and quick decision-making. As we continuously grow, we are looking for dedicated colleagues to join our team.\n\nAs an **Inbetriebnehmer (Commissioning Engineer)**, you will be based at our headquarters in Löhne and travel internationally to customer sites approximately 50% of the time (around 100 overnight stays per year). This role involves ensuring the smooth operation of our cutting-edge software and systems worldwide.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Commissioning of software at national and international customer sites.\n*   Installation, simulation, and testing of software.\n*   Conducting training sessions for customers.\n*   Configuration of databases and software.\n*   Leading customer workshops for process definition and optimization.\n*   Rolling out updates.\n*   Exchanging and adjusting electrical components.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed vocational training in automation or information technology, or a comparable qualification.\n*   Good knowledge of application installations, especially Windows.\n*   Open to career starters (\"Berufseinsteiger\") or candidates with relevant professional experience.\n*   Affinity for control technology is desirable.\n*   Strong communication skills in German and English.\n*   Independent and reliable way of working.\n*   Possession of a Class B driving license.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting opportunity for an Inbetriebnehmer looking for a dynamic role with significant international travel and direct customer engagement. Remmert, as a long-standing family-owned company, offers a remarkably supportive environment with excellent benefits, including profit sharing, flexible work (hybrid), extensive professional development, and a strong focus on employee well-being and a positive work-life balance. Whether you're a recent graduate or an experienced professional, the emphasis on growth and open communication makes this a compelling place to build a long-term career in a cutting-edge industry. The chance to work with automated logistics systems globally, coupled with a generous benefits package and a culture that truly invests in its people, makes this a standout position.
