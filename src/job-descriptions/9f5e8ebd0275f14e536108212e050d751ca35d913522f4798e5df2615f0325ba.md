**Job Description:**

*   Design engaging visuals for **social media** (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube) — posts, carousels, GIFs, and short videos.
*   Create promotional materials for product launches, client showcases, and marketing campaigns.
*   Develop **mockups** and creative assets for UI/UX case studies and internal branding.
*   Collaborate with the marketing, content, and video teams to maintain **brand consistency**.
*   Support **video editing, motion graphics, and reel creation** when needed.
*   Assist with **photoshoots and video sessions** — experience in these areas will be a big plus.
*   Experiment with **AI-based design tools (Firefly, Midjourney, Runway)** to push creative boundaries.

**Qualifications:**

*   1–2 years of experience in **graphic design or digital content creation**.
*   Proficiency in **Adobe Creative Suite** (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects).
*   Strong **sense of visual balance, color, and typography**.
*   Understanding of **social media design** trends and formats.
*   Great **communication skills** and **attention to detail**.
*   Comfort using **AI-assisted creative tools**.

**Bonus Points If You Have**

*   Experience in **photography, videography, and video editing** — candidates with these skills will get preference.
*   Familiarity with **Figma and UI presentation**.
*   Ability to create **motion graphics** or **short-form video ads**
