### Description
We are looking for a dedicated Marketing Manager for our client in the field of plant-based health ingredients with strict quality standards. In this role, you will be responsible for Meta Ads, Google Ads, SEO tools, and E-commerce. The position requires proven online marketing experience and proficient use of Microsoft Office 365, alongside excellent German (C1–C2) and good English (B2–C1) language skills. The place of employment is in the greater Dortmund area.

### Responsibilities
- Develop, plan, and implement cross-media marketing campaigns (online and offline) for end consumers and pharmacies.
- Manage creative, media, and digital agencies, as well as external service providers.
- Develop and control effective advertising campaigns.
- Conduct thorough market, competitor, and target group analyses.
- Take responsibility for product strategy, brand management, and budget control.
- Collaborate closely with sales, product development, and management.

### Qualifications
- Completed degree in Marketing, Communications, or Business Administration.
- Minimum 2 years of professional experience in marketing or product management, ideally in the FMCG/Healthcare sector.
- Proven expertise in the conception and management of integrated campaigns (TV, Print, Digital).
- Very good knowledge of online marketing, including performance channels (Google Ads, Meta Ads), SEA/SEO, and Social Media.
- Experience in Amazon E-commerce marketing is desirable.
- A high degree of reliability, responsibility, and commitment.
