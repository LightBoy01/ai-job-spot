About the role
--------------

You’ll get to make an impact like never before – both within Reckitt and across the FMCG market.

As a Senior Business Analyst, you’ll be a key partner to the Product Manager and the Product Team, helping to shape and deliver the product strategy through continuous, incremental improvements. You’ll work closely with stakeholders and squad members to capture and refine requirements into actionable user stories that drive real business value.

You’ll facilitate backlog and story refinement sessions, ensuring that stories are clear, testable, and aligned with business goals. You’ll design business scenarios and use cases, and collaborate with the team to elaborate on requirements and validate that they are fulfilled by product increments.

You’ll also be instrumental in driving product adoption by delivering training and support to end users and other key stakeholders.

**You will succeed because you have experience of...**

…working with **detail-oriented stakeholders** who have high expectations for the **quality and impact of product deliverables**. You thrive in ambiguity and can navigate complex matrix structures involving diverse teams across countries and companies, always driven by a **strong sense of ownership** and a focus on getting things done.

You’re commercially astute and skilled at **translating complex data into clear, actionable insights** for both business and technical audiences. You’re passionate about technology and can confidently communicate its benefits to IT and non-technical colleagues alike.

You’re a thoughtful listener who builds trust and simplifies complexity. Above all, you’re a **proactive leader** with a **strong understanding of AI capabilities**, able to inspire teams and stakeholders while continuously seeking new ways to deliver lasting value.

Your responsibilities
---------------------

*   **Eliciting, analysing, documenting, and testing product requirements** – including features and user stories – by defining functional test protocols and monitoring test execution.
*   **Preparing and delivering product user guides and training materials** to end users and support teams to drive adoption and effective usage.
*   **Designing business scenarios, process flows, and use cases** that maximise product value and adoption.
*   **Identifying and integrating relevant security, architectural, legal, and compliance requirements** into AI product development, ensuring alignment with industry best practices.
*   **Collaborating closely with the Product Team and business stakeholders** to elaborate and refine product requirements.
*   **Supporting the Product Manager** in shaping and executing the product strategy.
*   **Partnering with Data Scientists** to co-develop bespoke analytical models that address specific business needs.
*   **Coordinating with Architects, and Platform Team members** to ensure alignment on product requirements and delivery.
*   **Writing prompts and guiding the development team functionally**, ensuring clarity and relevance of AI interactions.
*   **Managing relationships with selected vendors and suppliers** involved in development, testing, and support services.
*   **Defining KPIs and test protocols** in collaboration with the Product Manager, and supporting the measurement of solution value with end users.
*   **Monitoring and reporting on product metrics**, including utilisation, satisfaction (e.g., NPS), adoption rates, usage intensity, training effectiveness, and stakeholder feedback.
