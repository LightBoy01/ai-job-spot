**Job Description:**

**ABOUT THE ROLE**

We are seeking a **Staff Software Engineer** to serve as the technical anchor of our internal innovation team. This role combines deep technical expertise with leadership across architecture, product thinking, and stakeholder engagement.

You’ll guide a cross-disciplinary team, mentor engineers across levels, and partner closely with Engineering Leadership and business stakeholders to turn high-level opportunities into working platforms. The role is hands-on but also highly influential: you’ll ensure technical excellence while balancing speed with rigor—shipping solutions quickly while laying down patterns that can scale if adopted long-term.

You’ll also help define how projects are structured, built, and transitioned for production support, ensuring solutions deliver both immediate impact and lasting value.

**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

As a **Staff Software Engineer** on our team, you will:

*   **Catalyst for Innovation** – Lead the design and development of platforms that solve high-impact problems where no off-the-shelf solution exists.
*   **Architect & Builder** – Translate ambiguous business needs into clear technical direction, delivering production-grade systems from prototype to platform.
*   **Scale with Confidence** – Design systems that can evolve rapidly while maintaining reliability, scalability, and long-term maintainability.
*   **Mentor & Elevate** – Guide engineers across levels, setting standards for engineering excellence and fostering a culture of technical depth.
*   **Cross-Functional Collaboration** – Partner with product, design, AI/ML, and business stakeholders to define scope, clarify tradeoffs, and ensure success.
*   **From Zero to Platform** – Rapid prototyping and driving projects from concept to delivery, ensuring ongoing ownership of the platforms.
*   **Operational Excellence** – Embed best practices in CI/CD, observability, and security from the start, ensuring production-readiness and support.

**Qualifications:**

**WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR**

*   8+ years of professional software engineering experience, with 2–3 years at Staff/Principal level or equivalent.
*   Deep expertise in distributed systems, APIs, or full-stack platform development.
*   Hands-on experience with cloud-native environments (containers, serverless, CI/CD pipelines).
*   Familiarity with AI/ML systems (LLMs, RAG, applied ML).
*   Ability to lead technical direction while remaining hands-on.
*   Strong communicator, able to explain tradeoffs to executives and mentor engineers.
*   Comfortable working in ambiguous, high-autonomy environments.
*   Track record of delivering complex solutions that align technical strategy with business needs.

**BONUS POINTS FOR**

*   Experience with other large entertainment focused platforms, centered around being consumer-facing at a large scale
*   Familiarity and hands-on exposure with MCP Servers and highly accessible and available API platforms
*   Knowledge of microservices and event-driven architectures at enterprise scale
*   Cloud-native, global scaling experience (AWS and their various tool offerings)
*   Understanding of the mindset of “Fail Fast, Learn Fast” and continuous improvement
