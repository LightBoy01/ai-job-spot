**Requirements**

**Do what you love:**

*   **AI Roadmap Execution & Automation Strategy:** Develop and operationalize a clear AI roadmap focused on identifying automation opportunities across business processes. Align initiatives with IT strategy and business goals.

*   **Technical Problem Solving & Solution Design**: Analyze inefficiencies and procedural bottlenecks. Design and prototype AI-driven automation solutions using a solid understanding of development principles and system architecture.

*   **Cross-Functional Collaboration & Requirements Engineering:** Partner with business units to gather requirements, assess feasibility, and co-create automation solutions that deliver measurable impact. Translate business needs into technical specifications.

*   **Technology Evaluation & Adoption**: Scout emerging AI and automation technologies. Lead pilots and proof-of-concepts to validate technical fit and business value before scaling adoption.

*   **Project Delivery & Technical Oversight**: Manage AI and automation projects from concept to deployment. Ensure timely delivery, code quality, and integration with existing systems. Collaborate with developers and data scientists as needed.

*   **AI & Automation Governance**: Promote ethical and compliant use of AI and automation tools. Ensure adherence to data privacy regulations, internal policies, and industry standards.

*   **Stakeholder Communication & Reporting**: Provide regular updates to the CTO and key stakeholders on automation progress, technical challenges, and strategic opportunities. Ensure transparency and alignment across teams.
