<p> </p><p><strong>Summary</strong></p>
<p>Gainwell Technologies is seeking a highly skilled AI Engineer to design, develop, and deploy advanced AI solutions, including Generative AI (GenAI), across our healthcare technology platforms. This role involves building and optimizing AI technologies, integrating them into existing systems, and ensuring their effectiveness in improving healthcare outcomes and operational efficiency while maintaining compliance with industry standards. </p>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>Your role in our mission</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>AI Model Development – Design, build, and train machine learning and deep learning models, including GenAI, NLP, and predictive analytics solutions for healthcare applications.</li>
<li>End-to-End AI Solution Deployment – Develop, test, and deploy AI solutions in cloud and on-premise environments, ensuring reliability, scalability, and real-world impact.</li>
<li>Data Engineering &amp; Processing – Work with large healthcare datasets, performing data preprocessing, feature engineering, and model training while ensuring compliance with HIPAA and other regulatory standards.</li>
<li>System Integration – Implement and optimize AI models within Gainwell’s existing technology stack, collaborating with software engineers to ensure seamless integration.</li>
<li>Performance Optimization – Continuously monitor, refine, and optimize AI models for accuracy, efficiency, and speed, leveraging MLOps best practices.</li>
<li>AI Research &amp; Innovation – Stay updated with the latest AI/ML advancements, exploring new technologies and methodologies to enhance solution effectiveness.</li>
<li>Compliance &amp; Security – Ensure AI implementations adhere to healthcare industry regulations, ethical AI principles, and data privacy standards.</li>
<li>Automation &amp; Workflow Enhancement – Identify opportunities to automate workflows and optimize business processes using AI-driven solutions.</li>
<li>Experience developing, deploying and finetuning LLMs (GPT, Gemini, Claude or similar) for real world applications including prompt engineering, model optimization and inference efficiency.</li>
<li>Strong programming skills in Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, and other AI frameworks.</li>
<li>Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to translate business challenges into AI-driven solutions.</li>
</ul>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>What we're looking for</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Advanced Education</strong>: Master’s or Ph.D. in Computer Science, AI, Data Science, or a related field.</li>
<li><strong>Extensive AI/ML Experience</strong>: 5+ years in AI/ML engineering, including hands-on work with GenAI, NLP, deep learning, and computer vision.</li>
<li><strong>Technical Proficiency</strong>: Strong coding skills in Python and frameworks like TensorFlow and PyTorch; experienced with LLMs (e.g., GPT, Gemini, Claude) including prompt engineering and optimization.</li>
<li><strong>Scalable Deployment Skills</strong>: Familiar with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP), MLOps practices, and big data tools (Spark, Hadoop, SQL/NoSQL).</li>
<li><strong>Domain Knowledge</strong>: Strong problem-solving abilities with a plus for experience in healthcare AI and understanding of regulatory standards like HIPAA and CMS.</li>
</ul>
