**The Company**

Tutor Intelligence is building the technology and processes to let robots go where they’ve never gone before: the average American factory. We understand that general-purpose and generally-intelligent robots are going to be built _in our lifetimes_, and we’re combining human and artificial intelligence to lead the charge. As an MIT-founded AI software company who deploys its inventions directly into the facilities that need them, on state-of-the-art hardware, every role at Tutor has a direct impact on the global, physical economy.

  

**The Role**

We are looking for an ambitious, generalist recruiter with a technical background to grow our org from ~50 to 100+ FTEs across the full robotics stack: research & engineering talent, GTM, and operations. As the first full-time recruiter at Tutor Intelligence, you will work directly with the CEO and CTO to build pipeline, evaluate candidates, and take a drivers seat in scaling the organization.

**Requirements**  
*   3–5+ years experience recruiting across both technical (engineering, research, product) and GTM (sales, marketing, operations) roles
*   Strong technical literacy; able to understand robotics, AI/ML, or adjacent deep-tech fields enough to screen candidates effectively
*   Prior experience at an early-stage startup or fast-growing company; comfortable scaling from ~50 → 100+ employees
*   Proven success sourcing and closing candidates across the full stack: cold outbound, campaigns, referrals, inbound funnel
*   Fluency with modern sourcing tools (LinkedIn Recruiter, Gem, Lever, etc.), outbound email automation, and CRM best practices
*   Track record of high-ownership execution: able to take a role from definition to closed hire with minimal hand-holding
*   Ability to context-switch between executive recruiting, senior engineers, and GTM hires without losing momentum
*   Strong written communication skills; able to craft outreach that resonates with both technical and commercial talent
*   Ambitious, growth-oriented mindset; looking to scale your own career alongside the company’s trajectory
*   Comfortable reporting directly to founders, influencing hiring strategy, and building recruiting processes from scratch
  
  
**Nice to Haves**  
*   Experience recruiting in robotics, automation, or other frontier technology fields
*   Exposure to employer branding, content, or community-building to attract top talent
*   Built or scaled recruiting processes, playbooks, or teams from scratch
