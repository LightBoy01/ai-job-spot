**Job Description:**

At ServiceNow, our technology makes the world work for everyone—and our people make it possible. We are seeking a highly experienced and proactive Executive Assistant to support the **Senior Vice President of Global People Services and the Vice President of Operations Service & Delivery.**

The ideal candidate anticipates needs, brings structure to complexity, and creates space for the SVP and VP to focus on leading at scale. This is a high-impact role requiring exceptional judgment, professionalism, and the ability to thrive in a fast-paced, global environment.

What you get to do in this role:

*   Provide high-level administrative and strategic support to the SVP, Global People Services and VP, Operations Service & Delivery
*   Proactively manage a complex, rapidly evolving calendar—prioritizing time with intention and clarity
*   Anticipate the executives’ needs and take initiative to remove roadblocks, streamline decision-making, and keep priorities moving
*   Coordinate demanding global travel and logistics with precision and foresight, ensuring seamless execution and contingency planning
*   Prepare briefing materials, pre-reads, and communications for internal meetings, customer engagements, and executive forums
*   Track key action items and follow-ups, ensuring accountability and visibility across the leadership team
*   Use judgment to move at appropriate pace, remove blockers and expedite Global People Services’ team performance
*   Handle confidential information with the highest level of discretion and professionalism
*   Serve as a liaison between the SVP, VP and internal stakeholders, partners, and customers—modeling ServiceNow’s values in every interaction
*   Partner with the Chief of Staff and senior leaders to support team operations, communications, and culture initiatives

**Qualifications:**

**To be successful in this role you have:**

*   Experience in leveraging or critically thinking about how to integrate AI into work processes, decision-making, or problem-solving. This may include using AI-powered tools, automating workflows, analyzing AI-driven insights, or exploring AI's potential impact on the function or industry.
*   10+ years of experience supporting a senior executive (SVP level or above) in a global, fast-paced organization; experience in enterprise software, sales, or HR strategy functions strongly preferred
*   Demonstrated ability to anticipate needs, exercise sound judgment, and operate independently with a high degree of ownership
*   Track record of prowess in navigating organizations, including building relationships with peer EAs and team members to help overcome organizational silos or barriers
*   Exceptional organizational and time management skills; ability to manage multiple priorities and pivot quickly
*   Exceptional attention to detail and follow up
*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills; executive presence and professionalism in all interactions
*   Experience coordinating team offsites and events
*   Flexibility on working hours with ability to accommodate global teams and meetings, on occasion
*   Proficiency in Microsoft Suite, Zoom, and enterprise expense/travel tools such as Concur
*   A high level of emotional intelligence, discretion, and commitment to confidentiality
*   A collaborative, low-ego approach and a strong sense of purpose in enabling the success of others
