<p>Keysight is on the forefront of technology innovation, delivering breakthroughs and trusted insights in electronic design, simulation, prototyping, test, manufacturing, and optimization. Learn more about what we do.</p><p> </p><p>Our award-winning culture embraces a bold vision of where technology can take us and a passion for tackling challenging problems with industry-first solutions. We believe that when people feel a sense of belonging, they can be more creative, innovative, and thrive at all points in their careers.</p><p> </p><p>Join Keysight’s central Data &amp; Analytics organization as a key contributor to the Growth Insights team. <ul><li>Analyze Growth Opportunities: Collaborate with business stakeholders to explore complex growth challenges using data analytics, predictive modeling, and Gen AI tools to generate actionable insights.</li><li>Develop AI-Enhanced Dashboards: Build and maintain dynamic, Gen AI-powered dashboards and reporting tools that provide foresight and prescriptive recommendations to business teams.</li><li>Support ML Model Deployment: Assist in the implementation and monitoring of machine learning models for use cases such as sales recommendations, churn prediction, and financial forecasting.</li><li>Enable Agentic AI Solutions: Contribute to the development and testing of Agentic AI workflows that automate growth-related tasks across platforms like Salesforce, Hubspot, Eloqua, and Oracle Cloud.</li><li>Collaborate on Data Infrastructure: Work with Data Engineering to ensure data pipelines and architecture support scalable, AI-native analytics solutions.</li><li>Promote Data Literacy: Help maintain the enterprise data catalog and support internal data literacy initiatives by enabling self-service analytics and sharing best practices.</li><li>Deliver Executive Insights: Prepare data visualizations and summaries that support strategic decision-making by senior leadership, including the Chief Data &amp; Analytics Officer.</li></ul>

<ul><li>Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in a STEM field (e.g., Computer Science, Engineering, Statistics, Mathematics)</li><li>5+ years of experience in Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, or Data Science</li><li>Proficient in Python or R, and SQL queries</li><li>Highly experienced with BI tools such as Power BI and Tableau</li><li>Familiarity with Generative AI and Machine Learning concepts and tools</li><li>Strong analytical skills to translate data into business insights</li><li>Strong communications skills and affinity for data storytelling and data storyboards to communicate the 'why'</li><li>Experience working in cross-functional teams and managing multiple priorities</li></ul><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p>Careers Privacy Statement***Keysight is an Equal Opportunity Employer.***</p>
