### Description\nLearning Digital is a certified educational institution offering government-funded advanced training programs that can be completed flexibly from anywhere. Our modular and innovative further education concept is tailored to personal needs, aiming to enhance qualifications and improve career opportunities. Further education should empower people with joy and increase satisfaction – in a contemporary and future-oriented way, because the future of learning is digital.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Independent preparation and execution of courses.\n*   Imparting scientifically sound knowledge with strong practical relevance, following the Dual Study teaching concept.\n*   Prompt communication and individual support for students regarding all questions about their course content.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Extensive professional experience, including at least three years of relevant practical experience outside of academia.\n*   Pedagogical aptitude and didactic qualification, ideally demonstrated by experience in academic teaching.\n*   Enjoy working with students.\n*   Skills in Scrum.\n*   Comfortable working in a digital environment and quickly adapting to new systems, applications, and processes.\n*   Enjoys working in a team, offers advice when asked, and is also open to receiving support.\n*   Responsible, reliable, and possesses a high degree of creativity & conceptual thinking.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at Learning Digital offers a unique opportunity for experienced educators to shape the future of digital learning. With significant autonomy and a supportive, team-oriented culture, you'll be empowered to deliver highly relevant and practical knowledge to students across Germany. The emphasis on flexibility and innovation means you'll be constantly engaged in a forward-thinking environment that values both individual contribution and collaborative success. If you're passionate about empowering learners and thrive in a digital-first, responsible setting, this is an excellent fit.
