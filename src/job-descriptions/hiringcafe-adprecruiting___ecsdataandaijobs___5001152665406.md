ECS is seeking a **_Senior Data Scientist_** to work **_remotely_**.  

ECS is seeking an organized, efficient, detail-oriented Senior Data Scientist to join our team. **Responsibilities:** 

1.  Leverage AI expertise to advise and create solutions that are innovative, secure, accurate, and maintainable.
2.  Contribute to an agile team as the lead data scientist, promoting metadata normalization & visual graph navigation of document relations. 
3.  Decipher customer needs and quickly adapt the AI prompt engineering.
4.  Build prototypes quickly for rapid feedback from customers.
5.  Command of Vector stores for Semantic Search and other use cases.
