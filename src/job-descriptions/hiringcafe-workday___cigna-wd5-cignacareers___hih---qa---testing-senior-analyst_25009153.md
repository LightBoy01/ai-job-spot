**This position will:**

*   Ensure the quality, reliability, and compliance of Gen AI/Agentic AI solutions, leveraging AWS for data engineering and Azure OpenAI for LLM-based applications
*   Design, develop, and execute comprehensive test plans for AI/ML models, data pipelines, and system integrations
*   Collaborate with architects, developers, and data scientists to validate requirements and solution outputs
*   Maintain high standards for test documentation, defect tracking, and reporting
*   Advocate for best practices in AI/ML testing, including fairness, bias, and explainability
*   Continuously improve testing processes and tools to enhance solution quality
*   Coordinate with ServiceNow and Workday teams to validate end-to-end workflows and ensure seamless integration of AI components

**Responsibilities:**

Participate in joint testing efforts, focusing on the interfaces and data exchanges between Gen AI solutions, ServiceNow, and Workday< >Develop and maintain automated and manual test cases for Gen AI/Agentic AI use casesValidate outputs from Azure OpenAI LLMs for accuracy, relevance, fairness, and bias, using both quantitative and qualitative methodsTest AWS-based data pipelines for data integrity, transformation accuracy, and performance under various scenariosPerform end-to-end testing of APIs and microservices that integrate AWS data sources with Azure OpenAI LLMs and HR systemsConduct regression, performance, security, and compliance testing of AI solutions to ensure robustness and reliabilityDocument test results, track defects, and collaborate with developers to resolve issues efficientlyEnsure comprehensive test coverage for HR system integrations (e.g., Workday) and business-critical workflowsParticipate in design and code reviews to provide a quality perspective and identify potential risks earlyWork with ServiceNow and Workday teams to validate data exchanges and workflow triggers, ensuring AI outputs are correctly consumed and acted upon by downstream systemsSupport user acceptance testing (UAT) and integration testing phases involving ServiceNow and Workday and other HR systems
