You will execute projects independently and provide second-level support, contributing significantly to client success and system security.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   In-depth training and development in SAP authorization systems.\n*   Independent execution of projects related to SAP authorization management.\n*   Client support across various industries in SAP authorization management.\n*   Providing second-level support for authorization issues.\n*   Analyzing authorization requirements and defining solutions.\n*   Performing authorization administration tasks.\n*   Developing solutions for complex role and authorization management questions.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Initial experience as an SAP consultant or user in SAP.\n*   Desirable: Experience in authorization administration.\n*   Curiosity to explore new cities and clients (20% travel readiness).\n*   Very good German language skills.\n*   Understanding of business processes within companies.\n*   Ability to transform business processes into the SAP system.\n*   Will and ambition to develop into an expert in SAP Security.\n*   Ability to communicate solutions clearly and understandably to clients.\n*   Confident demeanor towards colleagues and clients.\n*   Reliability.\n*   Independent & structured way of working.\n*   Team player.\n*   Degree or vocational training in Computer Science, Business Administration, Business Informatics, or similar fields.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced SAP professional eager to specialize and grow into an SAP Security expert.
