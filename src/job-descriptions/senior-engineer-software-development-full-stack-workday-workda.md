Job Overview:
​
Our company is seeking a skilled senior software developer to help with the development of many projects that span different technologies and requirements.  You will be working with a motivated team in a fast paced environment.<p></p>
A senior developer&#39;s day involves attending a morning stand-up meeting, reviewing code, working on complex coding tasks, collaborating with team members, and participating in design and architecture discussions. They also attend meetings like sprint planning and retrospectives, monitor project progress, deploy code, and mentor junior developers. By the end of the day, they review their progress, update documentation, and prepare for the next day&#39;s tasks.

Responsibilities:<ul><li>
Work on a team that is responsible for the design, implementation, and development of dynamic account opening and account maintenance applications.</li><li>
Explore and implement AI based solutions to enhance the account opening and client onboarding systems.</li><li>
Provide technical leadership to teammates through technical design and implementation of industry best practices adhering to LPL’s modernization practices, cloud practices, SDLC, and release management processes.</li><li>
Develop positive relationships with business partners to understand their needs, manage expectations, deliver value-add solutions and support translation of problem statements into system requirements.</li><li>
Assess opportunities for application and process improvements and show success through KPIs.</li><li>
Full application life-cycle coordination and management of deliverables in alignment with best practices and organizational compliance goals</li><li>
Collaborate with other shared services teams (technical and non-technical) to ensure solutions and platforms are both functional and sustainable.</li><li>
Use customer and business feedback to inform roadmaps and non-functional goals.</li></ul>

What are we looking for?
<p></p>
We want strong collaborators who can deliver a world-class client experience. We are looking for people who thrive in a fast-paced environment, are client-focused, team oriented, and are able to execute in a way that encourages creativity and continuous improvement.

Desired Skills and Competencies:<ul><li>
4&#43; years of hands on full-stack development and a desire to learn.</li><li>
Proficiency in .NET Framework: Strong understanding of .NET Core and .NET Framework.</li><li>
Ability to create and consume RESTful services.</li><li>
Strong understanding of Angular concepts such as components, modules, services, and dependency injection.</li><li>
Understanding responsive design principles to ensure applications work on various devices.</li><li>
Knowledge of core AWS services such as EC2, S3, RDS, and Lambda.</li><li>
Experience with IaC tools like AWS CloudFormation or Terraform.</li><li>
Utilizing AI-powered tools like Copilot to generate code snippets, boilerplate code, and entire functions based on natural language descriptions.</li><li>
Leveraging AI to provide intelligent code completions, reducing the time spent on writing repetitive code.</li><li>
Using AI to identify potential bugs and suggest fixes in real-time as you write code.</li><li>
Code Refactoring: Employing AI to suggest and automate code refactoring, improving code quality and maintainability.</li><li>
Documentation Generation: Utilizing AI to generate documentation for code, including comments, function descriptions, and usage examples.</li><li>
CI/CD using GitHub repositories.</li><li>
Excellent verbal and written communication skills, both technical and non-technical.</li><li>
Problem-Solving: Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to debug and resolve issues efficiently.</li><li>
Capable of effectively planning, prioritizing and executing tasks utilizing resources and tools.</li></ul>
