🚀 **Sales Manager – Berlin (On-Site / DACH Region)**

  
Ready to take your sales career to the next level in one of Europe's fastest-growing fintech companies?

Join our energetic team in Berlin and help small businesses across the DACH region grow by providing smarter payment solutions.

At **SumUp**, we empower over 4 million merchants in 30+ countries to get paid and run their business easily — from card readers and POS systems to invoicing, business accounts, and more.

As a **Sales Manager**, you'll be at the forefront of our growth, engaging directly with merchants, building relationships, and helping them access the right SumUp solutions for their business.

  

### 🎯 **What You'll Be Doing**

💼 Own your sales pipeline end-to-end: qualify leads, engage prospects via phone and email, and convert them into loyal customers

🤝 Build strong, lasting relationships with small businesses by understanding their needs and showing how SumUp's ecosystem makes their operations simpler

🎤 Deliver compelling product consultations and demos — turning interest into adoption of our card readers, POS solutions, and software products

🔥 Drive results by meeting and exceeding ambitious sales KPIs in a fast-paced, performance-oriented environment

📊 Track your progress, share insights with the team, and contribute to a culture of continuous improvement

🧠 Collaborate with your team, share best practices, and help shape a high-energy sales culture in the Berlin office

  

### 👀 **You'll Be a Great Fit If You**

*   Have proven sales experience (POS, SaaS, hospitality, retail, or related industries a plus)
    
*   Are fluent in **German (C1+)** and strong in English for internal collaboration
    
*   Are confident, solution-oriented, and enjoy consultative selling
    
*   Thrive on targets and are motivated by measurable success
    
*   Have excellent communication and interpersonal skills
    
*   Are self-driven, resilient, and excited to grow in a fast-paced environment
