**Would you like to work in a dynamic sales-growth role?**
**Would you like to help shape the future of AI application security?**
**Join our Security Sales Team!**
We work with world leading companies in every major industry to make the internet fast and secure. We collaborate with customers and prospects, aligning solutions to business needs. We build relationships and insights to maximize product value through the full scope of our global platform.
**Become a Firewall for AI Sales Specialist!**
As a Security Sales Specialist, you will be afforded a flexibility not often seen in sales. You will help shape a region, target accounts, and help design the go-to-market strategy. In this role, you will be a sales professional with an entrepreneurial spirit.
As a Senior Security Sales Specialist, you will be responsible for:
*   Being an advisor: innovating with customers, developing proofs of concept (PoCs), and bringing AI solutions to life.
*   Working closely with enablement, marketing and product teams to help refine sales motions and influence product strategy.
*   Managing the sales cycle from outreach, qualification, needs analysis, product demonstration, Proof of concept (PoC), negotiation and close.
*   Being accountable for your sales target and overachieving on that sales target.
*   Building customer relationships and qualifying opportunities such that the sales forecast is accurate.
**Do what you love**
To be successful in this role you will:
*   Have deep domain experience in LLMs, abuse prevention, and/or AI/ML-related threat defense.
*   Have 10+ years' sales experience in cybersecurity and show evidence of success and progression in the role.
*   Display history of consistently exceeding individual sales goals and targets.
*   Have experience navigating complex organizations as well as having Advanced English
*   Have demonstrated the ability to close new customer acquisition.
