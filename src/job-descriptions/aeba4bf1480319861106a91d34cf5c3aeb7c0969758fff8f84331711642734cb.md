**Project title:** Shaping functional strategy with AI to drive growth & efficiency

**Project scope/overview:**

This project focuses on identifying and driving opportunities where AI can accelerate Airwallex’s growth across the customer lifecycle, and internal efficiency. The intern will work closely with domain-specific teams such as Sales & Marketing, LRC, or Finance to evaluate how AI-powered tools can be embedded into the current journeys.

**Detailed Outline:**

**Tasks/deliverables:**

1.  **Identify opportunities:** Map potential AI use cases to current workflows and identify opportunities for further AI transformation
    
2.  **Pilot design:** Work with functional teams to design 2 to 3 pilot experiments using AI in GTM workflows.
    
3.  **Pilot execution:** Run pilots with selected pilot groups (e.g., SDR team for outreach, marketing team for GEO content) and track pilot performance against success metrics
    
4.  **Insights synthesis:** Analyse pilot results to capture learnings on effectiveness, adoption, and business impact (e.g., uplift in conversions, reduced sales lifecycle).
    
5.  **GTM growth strategy recommendations:** Translate pilot learnings into strategic recommendations for the teams e.g., scaling specific AI tools, transforming the SDR journey
    

**Project Milestones**:

1.  **Pilot design sign-off:** Alignment with key stakeholders on pilot scope, timeline, and success metrics.
    
2.  **Pilot kick-off:** Launch of pilots with selected teams and tracking mechanisms activated.
    
3.  **Mid-pilot check-in**: Interim review of early results and adoption feedback
    
4.  **End-of-pilot recommendation:** Presentation of findings and strategic recommendations to leadership, including scaling options or alternate options.
    

**Expected learning:**

*   **Using AI as a growth driver**: Develop a clear understanding of how AI-powered tools can be applied to accelerate growth and efficiency.
    
*   **Data-driven strategy & decision making**: Gain experience in analysing pilot outcomes using both quantitative metrics (e.g., conversion uplift, adoption rates, efficiency gains) and qualitative feedback, then linking results to strategic business recommendations.
    
*   **Strategic GTM planning**: Apply concepts from business strategy courses to recommend how AI can reshape strategy for long-term growth, and transform internal processes for efficiency.
    

**Planned role & responsibilities**

*   Participate in team standups, strategy discussions, and planning sessions to understand priorities and progress.
    
*   Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders and report progress on pilot metrics
    
*   Deliver interim project updates (bi-weekly check-ins) and a final showcase presentation to leadership.
    
*   Document insights and recommendations for scaling AI-powered initiatives.
