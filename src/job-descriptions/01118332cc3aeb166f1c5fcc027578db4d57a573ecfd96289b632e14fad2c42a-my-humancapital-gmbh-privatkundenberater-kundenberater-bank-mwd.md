### Description\nThis full-time, permanent position based in Frankfurt am Main, Germany, seeks a dedicated Private Client Advisor / Bank Client Advisor to provide holistic financial guidance to a loyal client base. The role involves developing personalized financial plans covering accounts, loans, investments, and retirement products, alongside actively acquiring new clients and expanding existing relationships. You will leverage modern digital tools and collaborate closely with internal teams to optimize the advisory experience. This role offers long-term stability with a market leader, excellent work-life balance including 32 days of holiday, a positive team culture, and extensive development opportunities.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Provide holistic and individualized financial advice to a fixed client base, engaging personally, telephonically, and digitally.\n- Advise clients on checking accounts, loans, investments (securities), and pension products, developing tailored financial plans.\n- Take responsibility for active new client acquisition and the expansion of existing client relationships, aligned with their financial goals.\n- Co-design target-group-oriented marketing campaigns and utilize modern advisory tools and digital channels to optimize client experiences.\n- Document advisory discussions and results diligently, collaborate closely with internal teams, and contribute to the continuous development of advisory processes.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed vocational training as a Bankkaufmann (m/w/d) or a comparable qualification (e.g., Financial Advisor, Bank Client Advisor, Insurance and Finance Specialist).\n- Initial professional experience in private client advisory or banking sales, ideally including securities and credit products.\n- Good knowledge of account management, digital advisory formats, and ideally experience with financial and advisory tools.\n- Proven sales talent, strong communication skills, customer orientation, and a solution-oriented, structured working approach.\n- Excellent German language skills (very good fluency).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for an ambitious financial advisor with initial experience to join a crisis-proof market leader in Frankfurt. The company truly invests in its employees, offering a rare combination of 32 days of holiday, a supportive \"Du-Kultur\" with high trust from leadership, and abundant career development paths. If you thrive on building long-term client relationships and value a modern, collaborative environment, this role provides both professional growth and excellent work-life balance.
