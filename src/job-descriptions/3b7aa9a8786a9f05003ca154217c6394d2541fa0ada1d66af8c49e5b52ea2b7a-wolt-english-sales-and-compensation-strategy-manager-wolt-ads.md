### Description\nWolt is a dynamic technology company known for its delivery platform, expanding rapidly into a \"delivery of everything\" model across 500 cities in 30 countries. Following its merger with DoorDash, Wolt is now embarking on building a critical Retail Media ecosystem globally, aiming to connect brands with millions of shoppers through in-app and off-app advertising. This role is for a Sales and Compensation Strategy Manager within the Global Sales Strategy & Operations Team, crucial for designing and executing global Ads Sales incentive programs. It's a strategic, execution-focused position involving deep analysis of sales performance, supporting incentive plan creation, and driving process improvements through automation and standardization. Wolt emphasizes a challenging yet exciting, high-ownership environment where individuals can learn, build, and ship more, with significant leadership exposure and global impact.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Own and execute the incentive design for Ads Sales globally at Wolt to drive top-line revenue.\n- Work closely with sales leadership, sales strategy, finance, and product teams to drive the development, implementation, and administration of new sales incentive structures.\n- Build and maintain complex forecast models that drive the sales organization's goals.\n- Continuously assess business performance, drive diagnostics, and deliver data-driven recommendations on ways to improve output.\n- Set and track accurate quota targets across various sales organizations that align with our strategic topline and company goals.\n- Create compelling and accurate visualization reports that track sales commissions while providing insights into business performance.\n- Focus on improving standardization and automation of all processes to do with quota, incentive design, and attainment/commissions tracking.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 4-5 years of experience in sales operations, business planning, analytics, or a related field.\n- Excellent analytical thinking skills, with the ability to deliver actionable recommendations from complex datasets.\n- Strong preference for SQL experience and experience with CRMs (e.g., Salesforce, PipeDrive, Hubspot).\n- Excellent communication skills and proven ability to work effectively across multiple teams.\n- A proactive, problem-solving mindset, capable of working independently in a fast-paced environment.\n- Demonstrated interest in global sales strategy and working on high-impact projects with leadership exposure.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a Mid-Senior level individual contributor to significantly impact Wolt's rapidly growing Retail Media business on a global scale. The role offers high ownership in designing critical incentive programs and direct exposure to senior leadership, making it ideal for a highly analytical and proactive professional. Expect a challenging, high-energy environment that promises extensive learning, rapid growth, and the chance to build scalable systems that are central to Wolt's strategic expansion.
