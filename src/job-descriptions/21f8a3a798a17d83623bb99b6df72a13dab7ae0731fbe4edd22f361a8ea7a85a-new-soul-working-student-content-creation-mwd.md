### Description\\nJoin new:soul, a pioneering brand on a mission to promote self-care and mindfulness across Europe, building the leading name for massage at the intersection of wellness and technology. As a key member of our dynamic team, you will be instrumental in shaping our brand's voice and visual identity through compelling, performance-focused content. This role offers a steep learning curve, significant personal and professional development within an agile environment, and the opportunity to work closely with our founders in a modern office located in the heart of Kollwitz-Kiez.\\n\\n### Responsibilities\\n- Develop creative, brand-compliant content ideas for various platforms, with a strong focus on performance ads.\\n- Produce high-quality textual, visual, and multimedia content (Reels, short videos, UGC) that reflects our brand voice.\\n- Conduct content production in our studios – both in front of and behind the camera – and collaborate with external creators for photo and video shoots.\\n- Write and edit texts with a focus on correctness, tonality, and style; adapt content for specific platforms to maximize performance and engagement.\\n- Contribute to visual storytelling by bringing in your ideas and helping us continuously evolve the new:soul brand.\\n\\n### Qualifications\\n- Native German proficiency and fluent English skills, both written and spoken.\\n- Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple projects and deadlines concurrently.\\n- Excellent writing and editing skills with a keen eye for detail, tonality, and style.\\n- Experience in video editing and creating short-form content (Reels, TikTok, etc.).\\n- A strong sense of aesthetics, brand communication, and storytelling – and a passion for shaping a young, rapidly growing brand.\\n\\n### Author's Take\\nThis is an exciting opportunity for a creative and hands-on Content Creator to make a significant impact at a rapidly growing wellness-tech brand. You'll enjoy a unique blend of strategic content development and practical production, directly influencing new:soul's brand presence and performance. Working closely with founders, you'll benefit from a fast-paced, agile environment and contribute to a meaningful mission, all while enjoying attractive benefits like discounted self-care services.
