### Description\nJoin Vanessa Albert, founder of the successful Instagram account Better-ness (158K+ Followers), as her **Social Media Creative (all genders)**. In this pivotal role, you will be my right hand, shaping the future of health content focused on **sugar-free nutrition, longevity, and biohacking**. I'm seeking a **creative and structured individual** who is eager to produce engaging content, think strategically, introduce fresh ideas, and collaboratively evolve the brand. This is a hybrid, part-time position (10-12 hours/week) based in Munich, offering significant influence and growth.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Content Planning & Execution:** Independently plan projects, including research, scriptwriting, filming, and editing videos.\n- **Idea & Concept Development:** Develop innovative concepts, monitor trends, and continuously enhance the aesthetic appeal of content.\n- **AI-Powered Efficiency:** Utilize AI tools of your choice to optimize workflow and production processes.\n- **Social Media Content Creation:** Produce engaging Feed Posts, Reels, and Stories.\n- **Daily Tasks:** Conduct research, write scripts, edit videos, and generate new ideas.\n- **Structured Production:** Consistently produce 3 videos per week, complemented by 2 hours of dedicated research.\n- **Collaboration & Planning:** Participate in regular meetings for content and topic planning.\n- **No Community Management:** This role does not involve managing comments or direct messages.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Education/Experience:** A degree or vocational training in media, communication, design, or comparable practical experience in social media content creation.\n- **Language Skills:** Fluent in German, with at least B2 level English proficiency.\n- **Content Tools Proficiency:** Confident in using CapCut Pro and current social media tools, with basic knowledge of AI tools for script creation, editing, and workflow optimization.\n- **Creative Understanding:** A strong sense for trends, aesthetics, and performance-focused storytelling – from initial content idea to visual implementation.\n- **Structured Work Ethic:** Self-motivated, reliable, and committed to high-quality visual design and brand presentation.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity to become the creative backbone of Better-ness, working directly with founder Vanessa Albert. If you're passionate about health, wellness, and content creation, you'll thrive in an environment that champions creative freedom, ownership, and direct impact on a growing community. We offer a flexible, hybrid part-time setup (10-12 hours/week) that respects your work-life balance while challenging you professionally. You'll benefit from clear structures, continuous professional development, and the chance to expand your network within the health and wellness industry. Embrace cutting-edge tools, dive deep into topics like longevity and biohacking, and contribute your unique vision. We believe in working 'auf Augenhöhe' – with open communication, honest feedback, and a genuine desire to see your ideas come to life. This role promises not just a job, but a long-term creative partnership where your skills will continuously evolve and make a tangible difference.
