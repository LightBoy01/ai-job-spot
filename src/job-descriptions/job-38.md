# Remote AI Prompt Engineer

 
### Description 

ActiveCampaign is seeking a talented Remote AI Prompt Engineer to join our innovative team. In this role, you will be responsible for designing, developing, and optimizing prompts for Large Language Models (LLMs) to enhance our marketing automation and customer experience platforms. You will work closely with our AI research and product teams to ensure our LLM-powered features are effective, accurate, and aligned with business goals. 
### Responsibilities 

- Design, develop, and test prompts for various LLM applications, focusing on marketing and customer engagement.
- Analyze and interpret LLM outputs to refine prompt strategies and improve model performance.
- Collaborate with AI researchers and product managers to integrate LLM capabilities into new and existing features.
- Stay up-to-date with the latest advancements in prompt engineering, LLMs, and natural language processing (NLP).
- Contribute to the development of best practices and tools for prompt engineering within the organization. 
### Qualifications 

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Linguistics, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
- 2+ years of experience in prompt engineering, NLP, or a related AI/ML role.
- Strong understanding of Large Language Models (LLMs) and their applications.
- Excellent writing and communication skills, with the ability to craft clear and effective prompts.
- Analytical mindset with strong problem-solving abilities.
- Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a remote, fast-paced environment.
