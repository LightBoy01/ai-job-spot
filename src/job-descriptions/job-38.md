# Remote AI Prompt Engineer

- **Company:** ActiveCampaign
- **Location:** Remote
- **Job Level:** Mid-Senior
- **Application Link:** https://www.activecampaign.com/careers

---

### Description

ActiveCampaign is hiring a Remote AI Prompt Engineer to optimize the performance of our AI language models. You will design, test, and refine prompts to ensure our AI tools deliver accurate, relevant, and high-quality outputs. This role involves close collaboration with data scientists, software engineers, and product managers.

### Responsibilities

- Develop and iterate on prompts for large language models.
- Evaluate and improve AI model responses through prompt engineering techniques.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate AI into products.
- Stay updated on the latest advancements in prompt engineering and LLMs.

### Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Linguistics, or a related field.
- Experience with large language models and prompt engineering.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Excellent communication and collaboration abilities.
