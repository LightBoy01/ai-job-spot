📌 What We’re Looking For: Automation Data Scientist  

We require a Data Scientist to help us automate the process discovery cycle, to empower us to make data driven prioritization  decisions in regards to the automation projects and build a process performance monitoring system once the automation has been delivered to production.

  

_What You Will Do :_ 

*   Designing, selecting, and engineering features, and building, evaluating, and optimizing predictive and generative models using state‑of‑the‑art machine‑learning techniques.  
    
*   Developing, deploying, and maintaining machine‑learning models and pipelines in production environments, ensuring scalability, reliability, and low latency.  
    
*   Monitoring model performance, detecting drift, and implementing automated retraining and governance strategies.  
    
*   Researching and prototyping advanced statistical and machine‑learning methods to generate actionable insights and drive product innovation.  
      
      
    
*   Collaborating with cross‑functional teams (product, engineering, design, business) to translate business problems into data‑science solutions with measurable impact.  
    
*   Communicating complex technical results and insights to technical and non‑technical stakeholders.  
    
*   Keeping up to date with the latest technology trends in data science, machine learning, and large language models, and actively pursuing continuous learning opportunities.

  

_What You Will Need :_

*   2+ years of practical experience as a Data Scientist.  
    
*   Strong problem‑solving skills with an emphasis on product development and delivery.  
    
*   Practical experience with NLP, large language models (LLMs), computer vision, multimodal modeling, clustering, and decision‑tree algorithms.  
    
*   Hands‑on experience with data‑science and machine‑learning libraries and frameworks such as Python (pandas, NumPy, scikit‑learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch) and related tooling (MLflow, Airflow, Spark) for experimentation and productionization.  
    
*   Excellent programming skills in Python, including writing clean, modular, well‑documented code, adhering to software‑engineering best practices (Git version control, unit testing, code reviews, and CI/CD pipelines).  
    
*   Ability to understand various data structures and common methods in data transformation and analysis.  
    
*   Data‑oriented personality with a passion for empirical, evidence‑based decision‑making.  
    
*   Great written and verbal communication skills, capable of translating complex analyses into clear business recommendations.  
      
    
*   A self‑starter mindset, able to work independently while proactively collaborating with cross‑functional teams.
