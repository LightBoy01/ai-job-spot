**Launch Your Career with the Allianz Benelux Young Graduate Program!** **Are you ready to transform your academic achievements into professional success? Join Allianz Benelux's Young Graduate Program and embark on a journey to become a leader in the insurance industry.**

The Allianz Benelux Young Graduate Program offers a unique opportunity for driven graduates to gain hands-on experience and develop their skills in a dynamic, international environment. Over 18 months, you will rotate through three different departments, gaining exposure to various business domains and contributing to strategic initiatives. This program is designed for technical profiles with a Master's degree in Data, Actuarial Sciences, Econometrics, Business Administration, Technology, or AI-related field.

**What We Offer**

*   **Comprehensive Development:** Engage in a structured program with three rotations across Benelux.
*   **International Exposure:** Work at a Benelux level, gaining insights into global operations.
*   **Mentorship:** Benefit from guidance by experienced professionals and strong mentors.
*   **Career Growth:** Set the foundation for a versatile and exciting career in the insurance industry.
*   **Flexibility:** Choose between different areas, with potential landing positions in Portfolio Steering, Claims Data, Actuary Pricing, Corporate Actuary, Risk Management, and Transformation Office.

**Your Responsibilities**

*   Participate in rotations across different departments.
*   Engage in data analysis, risk assessments, and customer improvement projects.
*   Collaborate with colleagues to drive innovation and create impact from day one.
*   Develop a deep understanding of Allianz's operations and strategic objectives

**Your Profile**

You hold a Master's degree in a quantitative field such as statistics, econometrics, mathematics, actuarial sciences, or a background in business administration, economics, technology, or AI. Additional relevant experience (as student/intern) is a plus. You are detail-oriented, analytical, and possess programming skills (SAS, Python, etc.). You are a team player, customer-oriented, flexible, creative, and driven by passion. You communicate easily and speak fluent English, with a strong recommendation for proficiency in Dutch and/or French. At Allianz, we're always on the lookout for individuals who are enthusiastic about AI and Data Analytics to shape the future and drive innovation across our organization.
