**Title:** **Engineer, Optical Proximity Correction**
**About** **GlobalFoundries**
GlobalFoundries is a leading full-service semiconductor foundry providing a unique combination of design, development, and fabrication services to some of the world’s most inspired technology companies. With a global manufacturing footprint spanning three continents, GlobalFoundries makes possible the technologies and systems that transform industries and give customers the power to shape their markets. For more information, visit [www.gf.com](http://www.gf.com/).
**Introduction:**
We are seeking a technically skilled and motivated AI/ML Engineer to join our team in enhancing Optical Proximity Correction (OPC) processes through advanced automation and predictive modeling. This role involves developing AI/ML solutions, automating data workflows, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to drive innovation in semiconductor manufacturing.
**Your Job**:
*   Develop and deploy AI/ML models to predict and mitigate OPC weak points.
*   Automate data collection, analysis, and reporting using Python, MySQL, Tcl, Perl, and Power BI.
*   Maintain and enhance internal websites using JavaScript, PHP, and HTML.
*   Manage and optimize MySQL databases to support OPC data pipelines and tool integration.
*   Collaborate with OPC engineers and GFIT teams to align technical solutions with business objectives.
*   Balance and prioritize multiple high-impact projects effectively.
*   Ensure robustness, scalability, and compliance of all deployed solutions.
*   Coach and mentor student interns or junior team members as needed.
**Other Responsibilities:**
*   Perform all activities in a safe and responsible manner and support all Environmental, Health, Safety & Security requirements and programs
**Required Qualifications****:**
*   Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
*   Strong programming skills in Python, MySQL, Tcl, Perl, and csh scripting.
*   Experience with AI/ML frameworks such as XGBoost, TensorFlow, or Scikit-learn.
*   Proficiency in MySQL and database design for high-volume data environments.
*   Hands-on experience with automation and data visualization tools (e.g., Power BI).
*   Familiarity with semiconductor manufacturing processes, particularly OPC.
*   Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills.
*   Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced, high-tech environment.
*   Effective communication skills for engineering-level collaboration.
