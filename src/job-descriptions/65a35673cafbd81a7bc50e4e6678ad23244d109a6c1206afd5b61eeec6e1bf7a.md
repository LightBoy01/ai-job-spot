Career Opportunities: Sr. Account Executive (49692)
===================================================

Requisition ID **49692** - Posted  - **GTM - CROSS PG - INTL SALES**

**OPENTEXT - THE INFORMATION COMPANY**

OpenText is a global leader in information management, where innovation, creativity, and collaboration are the key components of our corporate culture. As a member of our team, you will have the opportunity to partner with the most highly regarded companies in the world, tackle complex issues, and contribute to projects that shape the future of digital transformation.

**AI-First. Future-Driven. Human-Centered.**

At OpenText, AI is at the heart of everything we do—powering innovation, transforming work, and empowering digital knowledge workers. We're hiring talent that AI can't replace to help us shape the future of information management. Join us.

**About the role:**  
The Analytics Account Executive, experienced sales rep responsible for delivering Analytics and AI software(Vertica/Analytics Database and IDOL/Knowledge Discovery)  sales in Taiwan.  The primary opportunity is selling into whitespace market. This software sales rep will build strong relationships by networking within the account base and leverage the broader team to identify contacts and sales opportunities. Focused on selling OpenText Anlytics&AI products. This senior sales rep will develop relationships with the highest level of IT and business management to communicate our value proposition and deliver software sales.

**Responsibilities:**  
Achieves or exceeds sales quota.  
Use expertise to seek out new opportunities and expand and enhance existing opportunities to build the pipeline in and drive pursuit.  
Establish a professional, working, and consultative, relationship with the client, up to and including the C-level for mid-to-large accounts, by developing a core understanding of the unique business needs of the client within their industry.  
Maintains knowledge of competitors in account to strategically position the company's products and services better.  
Build and maintain partner ecosystem to scale business.   
Directs or coordinates supporting sales activities.

**Education and Experience Required:**  
Directly related previous work experience.  
Demonstrated achievement of progressively higher quota, diversity of business customer, and higher level customer interface.  
Extensive selling experience within telco, financial industries or public sectors.  
Experience on analytics products like data warehouse, enterprise search, Hadoop/Datalake, BI, ETL, ML.  
Typically more than 5 years of advanced sales experience.  
Above 2-3 years of product sales in the desired specialty (Big Data, Data warehouse, Business Intelligence, Enterprise Search, NLP, Predictive Analytics etc.)

**Knowledge & Skills**  
Self-driven, proactive working attitude.   
Knows and understands the company sales tools and processes.  
Listens to customer needs and tailors messages to customer based on their needs and ties-in additional upgrades or extended products/services  
Consistently demonstrates proactive activities within accounts to uncover new business and take ownership for developing early-stage opportunities.  
Exhibits thorough knowledge of the portfolio, observed via customer conversations and product discussions.  
Good partner management skills, familiar with partner network related to analytics solutions in Taiwan.   
Consistently meets or exceeds metrics related to the role set by the direct reporting manager.
