**About this role:**

Corporate Strategy is a small, highly leveraged team that is at the forefront of exploring new growth opportunities for Gartner’s diverse businesses. This entry-level role is geared toward high-potential individuals that have recently completed their undergraduate program and have a strong interest in business. The individual selected for this role will join a world class team that helps shape strategic decision making at Gartner.

**What you'll do:**

This role will support the Corporate Strategy team as it helps senior executives better understand the quickly evolving business landscape. The team’s mandate includes covering developments in markets where Gartner participates, searching for new acquisition targets and business opportunities, supporting long range planning activities, and regularly presenting to senior audiences as subject matter experts on markets and competition.

The role will include/develop:

*   Research and analyze developments in the information services marketplace as well as broader technology trends like Artificial Intelligence
*   Search for businesses and capabilities that will help take Gartner continue its double-digit growth trajectory
*   Structure and analyze ambiguous strategic problems
*   Exposure to senior finance leaders, senior business unit leaders and executive management as part of cross-functional project teams
*   A network of contacts and relationships throughout the organization
*   A broad understanding of the organization’s business units and operations
*   A strong foundation of investment research and business analysis skills

The primary locations for assignments are our Stamford, Connecticut headquarters. This is a hybrid opportunity.

**What you'll need:**

*   0-2 years’ working in corporate strategy or relevant experience
*   Undergraduate degree in finance, business, economics, statistics, or related field
*   Demonstrated intellectual curiosity and interest in business
*   Ability to solve complex and ambiguous problems
*   Strong communication skills
*   Ability to synthesize both qualitative and quantitative inputs in business decisions
*   Excellent interpersonal and communication (written and verbal) skills, including the ability to build relationships with, collaborate, and influence associates across all levels of the company
*   Must be a fast learner and comfortable managing unexpected change
*   Willingness to embrace best practices and feedback as a means of continuous improvement
*   High standards and a commitment to excellence with high attention to detail
*   High level of integrity
*   Excellent knowledge of MS Excel and PowerPoint as well as experience with generative AI tools like ChatGPT or NotebookLM
