**Job Description:**

The Senior Appian Engineer must have at least 10+ years of experience. As the senior Appian Engineer, you will be the key member of the Information Technology team and will be responsible for leading design, implementation, driving technology innovation initiatives and provide technical support. Banking domain experience is a must. Pega experience is highly desirable.  
  
Researching, analyzing, designing, estimating, planning, proposing, and delivering Appian solutions that are appropriate for the business and technology strategies  
Manage key stakeholders - Collaborate with Product owners on translating business requirements and operational strategy into a long-term, executable solution plan and roadmap  
Work with IT Leadership to keep solutions in alignment with the existing landscape and future IT roadmap  
Be able to understand Pega implementation, codebase and be able to build similar functionalities in Appian  
Experience on CI/CD Pipelines, Deployment methods and Integrations with external Systems  
Experience on designing and building Appian Applications, meeting application Performance requirements  
Experience in Agile methodologies, especially Scrum  
Knowledge on Automation Testing, Appian RPA, Java, API and AI capabilities  
Experience with public cloud platforms and application development platforms (Microsoft Azure, AWS, CGP, etc.)  
Actively monitor new and emerging technologies, trends, practices, and solutions and assess their applicability to business initiatives and strategies  
Excellent interpersonal and communication skills and proven ability to work effectively with all organizational levels
