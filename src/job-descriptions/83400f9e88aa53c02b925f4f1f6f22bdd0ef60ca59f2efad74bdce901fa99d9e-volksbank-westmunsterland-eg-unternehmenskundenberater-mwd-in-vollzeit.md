### Description\nWith joy at work and responsibility in action: this is how we want to operate, and as a \"TOP Employer\", our bank provides the best conditions for it. With approximately 650 dedicated employees and a balance sheet total of 5.0 billion Euros, we are a large regional bank in Westmünsterland. We are committed to a healthy economy, sustainable quality of life, and personal-digital banking. We are large enough to accompany members and clients through even the most demanding topics, yet agile, autonomous, and fresh enough to quickly implement innovation and impulses. This Senior Corporate Client Advisor role is an opportunity to independently manage key client relationships and drive complex financing projects within our vibrant regional market.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Independently manage corporate clients and serve as the central point of contact for all matters concerning financing, liquidity, and strategic development.\n- Demonstrate strengths in acquisition and sales-oriented, holistic client support, developing customized financing concepts in collaboration with all relevant product experts and cooperation partners.\n- As a bank representative, cultivate diverse contacts and continuously expand the customer base, involving all product experts and cooperation partners. Bear responsibility for the results of your client portfolio, considering risk-policy aspects of business relationships.\n- Structure individual financing solutions and oversee challenging engagements with a keen sense for risk, return, and client benefit.\n- Coordinate complex financing projects and contribute expertise to the analysis of corporate key figures and business models.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Several years of experience in advising large companies, covering all relevant areas holistically.\n- In addition to a banking-specific education, you have completed a degree in economics (e.g., business administration, banking, commercial law) or possess a comparable qualification.\n- Profound knowledge in structuring complex financings, balance sheet analysis, and assessing corporate risks.\n- You impress with your obliging manner, strong communication skills, team spirit, and high self-motivation.\n- Client-benefit-oriented presentation of solutions, strong goal orientation, and closing confidence are among your strengths.\n- Your working style is characterized by responsibility, reliability, and professional demeanor.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a solid opportunity for an experienced Corporate Client Advisor looking for a responsible, impactful role in a stable, regionally-focused bank known for its supportive culture, work-life balance, and commitment to employee development.
