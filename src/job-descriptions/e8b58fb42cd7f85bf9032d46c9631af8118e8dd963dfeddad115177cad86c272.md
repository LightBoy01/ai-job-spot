At realfast, we're building the future of enterprise AI—not by replacing humans, but by amplifying what makes them exceptional. Founded by proven operators with decades of scaling experience, we're solving the "last mile problem" in enterprise AI: the gap between impressive demos and production deployment. Our AI agents work alongside Salesforce developers to eliminate tedious work like unit testing and configuration management, freeing them to focus on architecture, strategy, and the human interactions that drive real business value.

We're backed by PeakXV Partners (formerly Sequoia India and SEA) and RTP Global, and we're building AI that makes humans more human, not obsolete. If you're excited about building production-grade AI that delivers measurable results and want to be part of transforming how enterprise software gets built, we'd love to meet you.

We are seeking an experienced Salesforce Technical Lead who will serve dual roles as a hands-on technical leader and solution architect. This individual will guide our Salesforce development team while designing enterprise-level solutions that align with business objectives and technical best practices.

At [realfast.ai](http://realfast.ai), we believe in the transformative potential of AI to positively impact businesses and lives. As an AI-first organization, we leverage AI for both internal processes and delivering exceptional value to our customers.

What you'll do
--------------

*   Lead a team of Salesforce developers, providing mentorship, code reviews, and technical guidance
    
*   Design end-to-end Salesforce solutions across Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, and platform capabilities
    
*   Translate business requirements into technical architecture documents, data models, and integration designs
    
*   Hands-on development of complex Apex classes, triggers, Lightning Web Components, and integrations
    
*   Implement CI/CD pipelines and deployment strategies using DevOps tools
    
*   Collaborate with stakeholders to present technical concepts and coordinate cross-platform initiatives
    
*   Define security models, sharing rules, data governance strategies and troubleshoot production issues to optimize platform performance
    
*   Establish and enforce development standards, best practices, and governance frameworks
    
*   Evaluate and recommend appropriate Salesforce products and third-party solutions for enterprise requirements
    

What you'll bring
-----------------

*   **7+ years of Salesforce development experience** with 3+ years in technical lead or architect role
    
*   **Active Salesforce certifications**: Platform Developer II, Application Architect or System Architect (Technical Architect preferred)
    
*   **Deep expertise** in Apex, SOQL, Lightning Web Components, and Salesforce APIs
    
*   **Strong experience** with integration patterns (REST, SOAP, Platform Events, CDC)
    
*   **Proficiency** with DevOps tools, version control systems (Git), and Agile/Scrum methodologies
    
*   **Knowledge** of middleware platforms (MuleSoft, Boomi, Workato) and enterprise integration patterns
    
*   **Exposure to AI development tools** - required for our AI-first approach
    
*   **Excellent communication and leadership skills** to present technical strategies to stakeholders and mentor technical talent in ambiguous situations
    
*   **Experience with data migration tools and ETL processes** for complex enterprise data transformations
    
*   **Understanding of JavaScript frameworks and modern web development** with familiarity in CPQ, Experience Cloud, or Field Service
    

This role is for you if
-----------------------

*   You're deeply curious about emerging technologies and driven to continuously learn and innovate
    
*   You take the time to dig deep and understand the "why" behind business requirements before architecting solutions
    
*   You thrive in leading cross-functional teams and can influence without authority
    
*   You focus on delivering enterprise-grade solutions that balance quality with business constraints
    
*   You can handle complex requirements and architect complete solutions independently
    
*   You're excited to push the boundaries of what's possible with AI-enhanced Salesforce implementations
    

This role is NOT for you if
---------------------------

*   You prefer individual contributor roles without leadership responsibilities
    
*   You haven't explored AI tools and their application to enterprise software development
    
*   You shy away from taking ownership of end-to-end technical delivery and team outcomes
    
*   You need highly prescriptive requirements and struggle with ambiguous problem-solving
