**The opportunity**
Join our MENA Technology team as an AI Engineer and take your career to the next level. As a member of the Digital and Emerging Technologies team, you will contribute to the development and deployment of innovative AI solutions. This is a great opportunity to apply your skills and knowledge in a dynamic and challenging environment.
We are seeking a talented and motivated AI Engineer who has a strong foundation in computer science or a related field and has experience working with AI algorithms and techniques. You should be comfortable working independently and as part of a team and have a passion for solving complex problems using AI.
**Your key responsibilities**
*   Design, develop, and train machine learning models using various algorithms and techniques (e.g., neural networks, deep learning, reinforcement learning).
*   Adapt pre-trained Gen AI models / fine-tune Gen AI models to specific tasks or domains, ensuring optimal performance.
*   Develop effective prompts and techniques to guide Gen AI models and generate high-quality outputs.
*   Optimize AI algorithms for performance, efficiency, and scalability.
*   Stay updated on the latest advancements in AI and explore new techniques and applications.
*   Work closely with other teams (e.g., data scientists, software engineers) to integrate AI solutions into existing products or services.
**Skills and attributes for success**
**To qualify for the role you must have**
*   B.E/ B.Tech/ MCA/ MS /M.Tech or equivalent degree in Computer Science discipline.
*   Minimum 1 – 3 years of relevant experience in the field of AI / Data Science.
*   Strong programming skills, preferably in Python, and experience with machine learning frameworks.
*   Experience / Exposure in cloud services like Azure for deploying machine learning models and leveraging cloud-based machine learning services.
*   Experience / Exposure in developing Generative AI solutions using Large Language models preferably Azure OpenAI.
*   Familiarity with relational databases like SQL Server / My SQL / PostgreSQL.
*   Familiarity with Docker, Kubernetes, or similar tools for containerization and orchestration of machine learning applications.
*   Knowledge / Experience in AI frameworks and technologies, such as GANs, transformers, and reinforcement learning.
*   Excellent problem-solving skills, attention to detail and accuracy.
*   Effective communication and collaboration skills.
**Ideally, you’ll also have**
*   Industry recognized certifications on AI / ML / Deep Learning.
**What we look for**
*   A self-starter, independent-thinker, curious and creative person who will demonstrate a deep commitment to innovation and an unwavering dedication to shaping the future of AI technology.
