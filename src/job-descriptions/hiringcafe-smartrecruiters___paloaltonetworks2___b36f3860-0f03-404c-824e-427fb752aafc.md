**Job Description:**

**Your Career**

We are seeking Test Engineers with a strong Automation First Mindset as we scale the Prisma SASE Test team.

We’re seeking hands-on engineers who take ownership and solve problems end to end. Collaboration and teamwork are at the foundation of our culture and we need engineers who can communicate l and work well with others towards achieving a common goal.

**Your Impact**

*   Deliver AI-driven test automation to accelerate validation of Prisma SASE functionality, scale, and reliability that replicate real‑world network and multi‑cloud scenarios
*   Design and implement agentic features  (prompting, tool‑use, RAG) to re-imagine  existing test engineering workflows, RCAs  and automation frameworks capabilities.
*   Build evaluation harnesses and integrate into CI/CD to enforce quality/safety gates and publish clear dashboards
*   Partner with Dev/PM/SRE to embed quality in design and throughout the feature lifecycle
*   Incorporate Customer & Field feedback to continuously improve test strategy, automation, and regression infrastructure

**Qualifications:**

**Your Experience** 

*   Strong automation skills in Python or TypeScript; Playwright/Pytest preferred
*   Experience with RAG or Agentic Frameworks is a huge plus along with experience with AI Driven development with tools such as Cursor, Codex or Claude Code.
*   CI/CD familiarity (GitHub/GitLab/Jenkins) and basic dashboarding/reporting
*   Cloud fundamentals (AWS/Azure/GCP) and solid software/QA foundations
*   Experience with TCP/IP Networking, SD-WAN or Network/Data Security Technologies such as VPNs, Proxy or DLP/CASB
*   Ability to learn quickly and thrive in a fast‑paced, collaborative environment
*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science/Engineering/Networking or equivalent military experience along with 6 plus years of work experience
