### Description
This audit assistant role involves working on diverse projects within auditing, engaging directly with clients of varying industries and sizes. You will join a seasoned yet family-like team of approximately 150 professionals across five locations. The company, recognized as a DATEV Digitale Kanzlei, leverages modern digital tools and clear processes to ensure efficient, structured, and flexible workflows. Your responsibilities will be tailored to your strengths, offering early responsibility and continuous professional development in collaboration with experienced colleagues. The firm values individual contributions and provides a supportive environment for growth.

### Responsibilities
- Assist in the audit of annual and consolidated financial statements.
- Support due diligence reviews and company valuations.
- Prepare audit reports and documentation.
- Process business-related special topics and tax questions.
- Contribute to the further development of audit processes and digital solutions.
- Undertake additional tasks that align with your interests and strengths.

### Qualifications
- Completed degree in Economics (Bachelor or Master), ideally with a focus on Auditing, Accounting, or Taxation.
- Initial practical experience in taxation (e.g., through internships or working student positions) is desirable but not mandatory.
- A structured, independent working style and a willingness to learn new topics.
- Strong communication skills, reliability, and good interpersonal skills for team and client interactions.
- DATEV software knowledge is desirable but not a mandatory prerequisite.

The company prioritizes individuals who are eager to apply their knowledge, work meticulously, and continuously develop professionally, rather than seeking perfect résumés. Whether you are a recent graduate or bring initial practical experience, you will find an environment that supports your growth and allows you to take on responsibility.
