### Description\nWe are an e-commerce company in the pest control sector and are looking for support in expanding our SEO content. Our focus is on high-quality, informative blog articles that offer practical solutions to households – partly with the support of modern AI tools.\n\nThis is a 3-month unpaid internship with a flexible start, offering 100% remote work. You'll gain practical SEO experience, insight into content processes, keyword research, and performance analysis, with the opportunity to work independently on topics and see direct implementation.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Research on topics related to pests, pantry protection, home & garden.\n- Writing SEO-optimized blog articles (including keyword research and structuring).\n- Using AI tools for idea generation, outlining, and draft text creation.\n- Optimizing existing content (meta-data, internal linking, structure).\n- Creating content briefings and simple editorial plans.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Interest in SEO, content marketing, and digital tools.\n- Very good German language skills.\n- Structured and reliable way of working.\n- First experience in writing texts or blog articles (advantageous, not mandatory).\n- Motivation to quickly familiarize oneself with new topics.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent entry-level opportunity for aspiring content creators passionate about SEO and digital marketing. As an unpaid internship, it offers unparalleled hands-on experience in a remote, dynamic e-commerce environment. You'll gain practical skills in crafting SEO-optimized content, leveraging AI tools, and seeing your work directly impact a growing business. It's ideal for self-starters eager to learn, contribute meaningfully, and build a strong portfolio in a flexible setting.
