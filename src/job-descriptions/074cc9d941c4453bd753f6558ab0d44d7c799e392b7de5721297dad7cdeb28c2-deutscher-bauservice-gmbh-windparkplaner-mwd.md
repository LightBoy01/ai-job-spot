### Description\nJoin a rapidly growing leader in services for engineering firms and civil engineering companies, focusing on expanding existing wind energy projects across Germany. We are seeking an experienced Project Engineer who is passionate about renewable energy, possesses strong project management skills, and thrives in a dynamic environment. If you're ready to take on significant responsibility for large-scale wind power initiatives from conception to completion, we encourage you to apply and contribute to our dedicated team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Take full responsibility for wind energy projects, from initial consultation to contract completion.\n- Create site plans, concepts, and planning variants for potential wind farms, including development concepts.\n- Provide comprehensive support and consulting to clients.\n- Conduct draft and approval planning for wind farm projects.\n- Technically assess projects, ensuring compliance with legal regulations and necessary planning steps for approval.\n- Prepare and submit approval applications.\n- Act as a primary point of contact for approval authorities, experts, clients, and for all technical inquiries.\n- Present project plans and information at municipal council meetings and public assemblies.\n- Process information material for clients.\n- Manage technical documents and project documentation.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A completed degree in Architecture, Geography, Renewable Energies, Civil Engineering, or a completed apprenticeship as a Construction Draftsman with relevant professional experience, or a similar qualification.\n- You are an organizational talent, a team player, and eager to take on responsibility.\n- Experience in customer care and dealing with approval authorities is desirable.\n- Possess very good communication and negotiation skills.\n- Experience with CAD or GIS programs is required; experience with WindPRO and Windographer is a plus.\n- Good proficiency in MS-Office.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role offers a unique opportunity to significantly impact Germany's renewable energy landscape within a company that truly values its employees. With a vibrant \"start-up flair,\" you can expect an agile, collaborative, and empowering work environment. The comprehensive benefits package, including flexible working hours, mobile work options, full office catering, and numerous team events, underscores a strong commitment to work-life balance, well-being, and team cohesion. This is an ideal position for a driven Project Engineer looking for ownership, growth, and a supportive culture in a rapidly expanding sector.
