 The Security Specialist oversees end-to-end security for our AI-powered SaaS platform, ensuring protection against threats and compliance with industry regulations. This role involves risk assessments, incident response, compliance audits, and implementing security best practices.

**Responsibilities:**

· Develop comprehensive security policies and procedures.

· Perform security assessments and penetration testing.

· Implement and manage security controls (firewalls, IDS/IPS, SIEM).

· Collaborate with engineering and DevOps to integrate DevSecOps practices.

· Ensure regulatory compliance (GDPR, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, SOC 2).

· Manage interactions with security auditors and regulatory bodies.

· Maintain awareness of cybersecurity trends, particularly around AI/ML.

**Qualifications:**

· 5–10 years of cybersecurity experience.

· Expertise in cloud, network, and application security.

· Familiarity with OWASP, threat modeling, IAM, and authentication frameworks.

· Experience implementing monitoring/logging security solutions.

· Strong analytical and incident response skills.

**Preferred Experience:**

· Experience in regulated industries (healthcare, Finserv, retail, SLED).

· Familiarity securing AI/ML environments and handling sensitive data.

· Relevant certifications (CISSP, CISM, CEH, OSCP).
