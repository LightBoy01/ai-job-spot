Business Function

Responsibilities

Develop and implement a comprehensive governance framework for the responsible and ethical use of generative AI (Gen-AI) technologies within DBS Bank's operations and service offerings

Establish policies, guidelines, and control measures to ensure compliance with relevant regulations, industry standards, and best practices related to Gen-AI deployment

Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including technology, risk, compliance, and business units, to identify and mitigate potential risks and challenges associated with the use of Gen-AI

Provide subject matter expertise and advisory support to business leaders and project teams on the appropriate application of Gen-AI, including considerations around AI literacy and prompt engineering

Monitor the evolving landscape of Gen-AI technologies, regulations, and industry trends, and recommend strategic initiatives to maintain DBS Bank's position as a leader in responsible AI governance

Develop and deliver training programs to enhance the AI literacy of DBS Bank employees, empowering them to effectively leverage Gen-AI tools and techniques

Represent DBS Bank in industry forums, regulatory discussions, and external collaborations related to Gen-AI governance and responsible AI practices

Requirements

Advanced degree in computer science, data science, or a related technical field, with a strong understanding of AI/ML principles and applications

Minimum 8 years of experience in a leadership role, with a proven track record in developing and implementing AI governance frameworks within the banking or financial services industry

Demonstrated expertise in prompt engineering, including the ability to design, test, and optimize prompts for various Gen-AI use cases

Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to navigate complex regulatory and technological landscapes

Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to effectively present technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences

Familiarity with relevant regulations, industry standards, and best practices related to AI governance, data privacy, and ethical AI

Passion for driving innovation and fostering a culture of responsible AI adoption within the organization

Proficient in Mandarin
