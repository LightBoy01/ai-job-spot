*   **List Comparisons**: Developing AI tools to automate the comparison of lists for accuracy and consistency.
*   **Document review:** Finding AI solutions to facilitate and accelerate document review
*   **Any other efficiency that can be gained !** There will be creative space within this internship to identify and propose innovative solutions where current inefficiencies exist.
*   **Quality operations activities:** as part of this internship, you will have the chance to work first-hand on quality operations activities in order to better develop AI solutions and to promote your learning of the field

Your role:

*   Collaborate with a close-knit team of 8-10 employees to understand project requirements and objectives

*   Conduct testing and validation by customers of AI tools to ensure functionality and reliability
*   Document processes and results for future reference
