# Research Engineer, Science (LLM)

- **Company:** Google DeepMind
- **Location:** London, UK
- **Job Level:** Research Engineer
- **Employee Role:** Individual Contributor
- **Application Link:** <https://deepmind.google/careers/>

---

### Description

<p>A Google DeepMind Research Engineer, Science (LLM) is a pivotal role focused on advancing scientific discovery through the application of large language models (LLMs) and machine learning techniques. This interdisciplinary position involves collaborating with researchers and software engineers to develop and execute experiments exploring new AI applications, particularly within scientific domains like biology, physics, and mathematics.</p>

### Responsibilities

- Rapidly prototype machine learning techniques for scientific problems.
- Conduct exploratory analysis to guide experimentation and research directions.
- Design and implement scalable infrastructure and procedures for training and evaluating modern machine learning systems.
- Develop tools, libraries, and frameworks to accelerate research.
- Report and present software developments, experimental results, and data analysis.
- Collaborate with internal and external scientific domain experts.

### Qualifications

- A Master's degree in computer science, electrical engineering, science, mathematics, or equivalent industry experience.
- Proven experience working with large language models (LLMs).
- Experience with at least one programming language, preferably Python or C++.
- Knowledge of linear algebra, calculus, and statistics.
- Experience working with large and noisy datasets, including exploring, analyzing, and visualizing data.
- Familiarity with machine learning and scientific libraries such as Jax, PyTorch, TensorFlow, NumPy, or Pandas.
