# Research Engineer, Science (LLM)

### Description

Google DeepMind is on a mission to solve intelligence to advance science and benefit humanity. We are looking for a Research Engineer to join our Science team and contribute to our work on Large Language Models (LLMs) for scientific discovery. In this role, you will collaborate with world-class researchers to build and train large-scale models that can understand and reason about complex scientific data, from genomics to climate science. This is a unique opportunity to work on some of the most ambitious and scientifically important problems of our time.

### Responsibilities

- Design, implement, and scale up large-scale machine learning experiments, particularly with LLMs.
- Develop and optimize training and inference code using frameworks like JAX and TensorFlow.
- Work with massive scientific datasets, ensuring data quality and building efficient data pipelines.
- Collaborate closely with research scientists to prototype new ideas, understand experimental results, and push the boundaries of scientific AI.
- Contribute to the engineering of robust, reproducible, and scalable research infrastructure.
- Stay current with the latest literature and engineering practices in large-scale machine learning.

### Qualifications

- MS or PhD in Computer Science, or equivalent practical experience.
- Strong software engineering skills with a proven track record of building and shipping high-quality code.
- Experience with deep learning frameworks such as JAX, TensorFlow, or PyTorch.
- Experience in training and working with Large Language Models (LLMs) is highly desirable.
- A passion for science and a strong desire to apply AI to solve real-world scientific challenges.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Ability to work in a fast-paced, collaborative, and research-oriented environment.
