# Machine Learning Engineer

### Description

Scale AI is at the forefront of accelerating the development of AI applications. We are looking for a Machine Learning Engineer to join our dynamic team and work on the next generation of our data labeling and model training platform. As a Machine Learning Engineer at Scale, you will be responsible for designing, building, and deploying machine learning models that power our products and delight our customers. You will have the opportunity to work with large-scale datasets and cutting-edge technologies, including Large Language Models (LLMs) and Natural Language Processing (NLP).

### Responsibilities

- Design, build, and maintain scalable machine learning pipelines for training and inference.
- Develop and productionize machine learning models for a variety of tasks, including classification, entity recognition, and data generation.
- Collaborate with product managers, software engineers, and other stakeholders to define requirements and deliver high-impact solutions.
- Stay up-to-date with the latest research and best practices in machine learning and apply them to our products.
- Analyze and interpret complex datasets to drive product decisions and improve model performance.
- Write clean, maintainable, and well-documented code.

### Qualifications

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
- 3+ years of experience in a machine learning or data science role.
- Strong programming skills in Python and experience with machine learning libraries such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, and scikit-learn.
- Experience with NLP and LLMs is a strong plus.
- Experience with cloud computing platforms such as AWS, GCP, or Azure.
- Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
- Strong communication and collaboration skills.
