### Description\nTekpoint GmbH is an internationally leading distributor in Consumer Electronics and Micromobility, passionately building, developing, and supporting brands. Our goal is to achieve sustainable growth and cultivate strong partnerships with our brand partners. We are looking for an experienced and motivated Brand Manager (m/w/d) to join our team, who will leverage expertise and creativity to manage and further develop our brands.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Strategic Brand Management:** Develop and implement strategies for the advancement of defined brands within our portfolio.\n*   **Strategic Planning:** Develop long-term brand strategies based on market and target group analyses.\n*   **Communication:** Coordinate all brand-related measures, both internally and externally.\n*   **Interface Management:** Close collaboration with internal departments (e.g., Sales, Marketing, Controlling) and external brand partners to ensure optimal cooperation.\n*   **Product Portfolio Management:** Strategic planning and optimization of the product portfolio.\n*   **Price and Sales Strategy:** Develop and implement pricing strategies, and support the planning and implementation of sales measures.\n*   **Budget and Quantity Planning:** Create forecasts and plan budgets in collaboration with relevant departments.\n*   **Analysis and Optimization:** Monitor brand performance using KPIs and derive optimization measures.\n*   **Market Analysis:** Conduct market and competitor analyses to identify trends and derive recommendations for action.\n*   **Positioning and Further Development:** Ensure clear brand positioning and identify new growth opportunities.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   A successfully completed degree in Business Administration, or a comparable qualification with a focus on Consumer Electronics / Micromobility.\n*   Several years of experience in Category Management, Brand Development, or Brand Management, ideally in an international environment.\n*   Strategic mindset and strong analytical skills.\n*   Strong communication and negotiation skills, as well as team spirit.\n*   Independent, structured, and solution-oriented working style.\n*   Proficient in MS Office and ideally knowledge of market analysis tools.\n*   Fluent in spoken and written German and English.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a compelling opportunity for an experienced Brand Manager eager to make a significant strategic impact within the dynamic Consumer Electronics and Micromobility sectors. Tekpoint GmbH offers a role with considerable creative freedom and responsibility, emphasizing brand development and sustainable growth. The company prides itself on a supportive, motivated team and provides excellent professional development prospects, a modern work environment, attractive benefits including home office flexibility, and engaging company events. If you are passionate about building and nurturing brands in an innovative international setting, this role in Düsseldorf offers a superb platform for career growth.
