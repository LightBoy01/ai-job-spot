### Description

As an Inside Sales Coordinator at NOV, you will be at the center of our customer engagement strategy. This role is perfect for a detail-oriented and collaborative individual who thrives on delivering exceptional service. You will be instrumental in supporting our sales teams, building strong client relationships, and ensuring a seamless order fulfillment process. By collaborating with Account Managers, Engineering, Supply Chain, and Planning, you will provide comprehensive solutions to both new and existing customers, leveraging the latest digital tools to drive success.

### Responsibilities

- Act as a trusted partner to customers by preparing accurate quotes, processing sales orders, and providing timely and proactive updates on order status, pricing, and delivery.
- Meticulously compare customer purchase orders against entered orders to ensure all specifications, terms, and conditions are met.
- Verify that purchase orders align with NOV XL Systems' delivery, financial, and commercial requirements.
- Collaborate with Product Engineering and Marketing to ensure all technical requirements are fulfilled for quotations.
- Navigate customer portals for bids, track sales opportunities, and maintain complete and accurate records.
- Apply strong math and problem-solving skills to accurately calculate pricing, discounts, and delivery timelines.
- Communicate with Planning and Scheduling departments on current and potential orders to maintain accuracy and ensure on-time delivery.
- Generate insightful reports to support sales forecasting and strategic decision-making.
- Contribute to a culture of continuous improvement by identifying and suggesting process enhancements and embracing new technologies.

### Qualifications

- High School diploma or equivalent is required.
- Solid mathematical and analytical skills for pricing, reporting, and ensuring order accuracy.
- Proficiency in Microsoft 365, including Outlook, SharePoint, and OneDrive.
- Strong experience with Excel (formulas, pivot tables) or similar tools for managing sales data.
- Experience in the oilfield service industry and knowledge of relevant oil and gas products is preferred.
- Previous experience in an Inside Sales role is a plus.
- Experience with SyteLine and Microsoft Dynamics CRM is desirable.
- A positive, detail-oriented, and service-focused attitude, with the ability to work independently and as part of a team.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to interact professionally with all levels of the organization.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "This sounds like a vital support role, but how does an Inside Sales Coordinator truly influence the success of a major deal or a client relationship?"**

That's a great question, and it gets to the heart of what makes this role so critical. Think of our Account Managers as the scouts who find the path, but the Inside Sales Coordinator is the one who builds the bridge. I remember a recent deal where a client had a highly specific technical requirement on a tight deadline. While the Account Manager was managing the relationship, it was the Coordinator who dug in with Engineering to confirm feasibility, coordinated with Supply Chain to check material availability, and kept the client updated at every step. They didn't just process the order; they quarterbacked the internal effort that made the sale possible. In this position, you're not just supporting the team; you're the connective tissue that ensures we deliver on our promises.

**A candidate might also ask: "The description mentions embracing new technologies like AI. What does that actually look like for this role, and how does the team support learning and development?"**

We're really passionate about not just keeping up, but staying ahead. For a coordinator, this isn't about becoming a programmer; it's about using smart tools to be more effective. For example, we're exploring AI to help draft initial quotes based on historical data, freeing you up to focus on the more complex, problem-solving aspects of a client's request. We don't just throw new tech at you; we have regular 'lunch and learn' sessions, and everyone is encouraged to experiment and share what works. Your manager won’t just assign you tasks; they'll ask, 'Is there a better, faster way we could be doing this?' It's a culture of continuous improvement, not just as a buzzword, but in our daily work. We want you to grow with us.
