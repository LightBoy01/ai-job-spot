### Description\
Beatvest is the leading platform for certified, gamified financial education, aiming to empower banks and financial service providers to activate their users, build trust, and unlock the full potential of their product landscape. Our B2B SaaS solution integrates directly into existing banking offerings, transforming inactive app users into informed investors.\
\
As a Founder's Associate (Werkstudent:in), you will work closely with one of our founders, gaining unique insight into the strategic and operational processes of a rapidly growing FinTech startup. You will co-create, co-think, and actively contribute with the goal of helping more people overcome their fear of investing and achieve the financial future they deserve.\
\
This position is **Remote (flexible)**, offering **part-time** hours during the semester with the possibility of **full-time** during semester breaks.\
\
### Responsibilities\
- **Tooling & Automation**: Build and maintain automations (e.g., with n8n, Airtable, Zapier) to enhance internal workflows and B2B client setups.\
- **Product Development & Rollouts**: Support the development and deployment of new product flows, from concept and data modeling to implementation in partner environments.\
- **Data-Driven Insights**: Analyze User-KPIs, funnel performance, and investment conversions for banking partners.\
- **Collaboration with Tech & Content**: Work closely with engineers and the content team to link new features or learning paths with concrete business goals.\
- **Presentations & Pitch-Decks**: Create high-quality B2B presentations for banks and partners in both German and English.\
\
### Qualifications\
- **Education**: Currently pursuing a degree in Data Science, Business Informatics, Business Administration (BWL), Business Mathematics, or a comparable field.\
- **Language Skills**: Very good proficiency in German and English (B2/C1+), both written and verbal.\
- **Domain Interest**: Strong enthusiasm for financial markets and financial literacy.\
- **Technical Skills**: Experience with automation tools (e.g., n8n, Airtable, Make, Zapier) or a strong interest in learning them.\
- **Analytical Acumen**: Strong analytical skills; initial experience with SQL, Notion, or BI tools is a plus.\
- **Presentation Skills**: Excellent PowerPoint skills and a keen eye for presentation design.\
- **Work Ethic**: Structured, independent work style and a hands-on mentality.\
