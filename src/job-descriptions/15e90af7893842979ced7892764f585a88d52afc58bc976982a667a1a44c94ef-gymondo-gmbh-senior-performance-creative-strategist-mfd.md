### Description
At 7NXT (Gymondo, 7Mind, BuddyFit), we are building a next-generation Creative Engine aimed at shaping how millions discover and build healthier routines, both physically and mentally. As our Senior Performance Creative Strategist, you will be instrumental in defining the creative systems behind our growth. This role combines storytelling, creative direction, performance insights, behavioral science, and AI-driven workflows to transform ideas into scalable frameworks that drive both creative excellence and subscription growth.

Your core mission is to create a scalable, AI-enabled creative system that elevates both the creative quality and performance of our paid social presence, primarily across Meta and TikTok. If you are passionate about developing ideas, shaping narratives, crafting angles, and turning them into high-performing creatives, this role offers significant space, ownership, and creative influence to achieve just that. Your work will not only enhance ad performance but also fundamentally shape how our brands appear creatively across various platforms.

### Responsibilities
-   **Turn insights into ideas:** Translate platform signals and human insights into clear creative hypotheses, build repeatable creative frameworks (angles, hooks, narratives, visual logic), and use behavioral science (identity cues, self-efficacy, fresh start effect, loss aversion) to shape messaging that resonates and reduces friction.
-   **Accelerate creation with AI:** Leverage AI to explore creative angles, themes, and ad families, generate hook, copy, and visual variations, create mood boards, storyboards, and visual references, and support designers/editors in fast variant production. This also includes developing shared prompt libraries and AI best practices to help the broader team adopt efficient and modern workflows.
-   **Build creative systems that scale:** Develop ad families rather than isolated assets, systemize creative logic across brands, formats, markets, and languages, and maintain a shared Creative Insights + AI Playbook used across the organization.
-   **Collaborate with clarity (no people management):** Work closely with Performance Marketing on priorities and opportunities, deliver structured and inspiring briefs for campaign assets to designers, video editors, and influencer teams that align with campaign goals and deadlines. Align concepts and narratives with Product Marketing, Web, and Brand across the full conversion journey, and give feedback and approve creative content for Performance campaign launches.
-   **Make creative match the user journey:** Ensure narrative and visual consistency from ad to landing page to onboarding, partner with Web & Product to map creative angles to the right conversion paths, and ensure all creative assets meet platform requirements (safe zones, channel best practices etc.), branding guidelines, and campaign objectives.
-   **Raise creative quality through structured experimentation:** Set the standard for what great short-form creative looks like at 7NXT, build testing roadmaps (angle → hook → format → CTA), and stay ahead of cultural moments, formats, and platform trends.

### Qualifications
-   3–6+ years of experience in creative strategy, performance creative, or paid social creative.
-   Deep expertise with Meta (required), plus familiarity with TikTok & YouTube Shorts.
-   Strong ability to craft scroll-stopping hooks, angles, and narratives.
-   Hands-on experience using AI tools for ideation, variant creation, and storyboarding.
-   Confidence interpreting creative & performance insights.
-   Understanding of behavioral science in motivation and habit-building categories.
-   Experience across web acquisition flows, landing pages, web flows, app store messaging, and onboarding.
-   Demonstrated curiosity, creative intuition, and analytical sensitivity, with a desire for ideas to move both people and metrics.

**Nice-to-haves:**
-   Background in fitness, wellbeing, or habit-building storytelling.
-   Experience scripting or storyboarding UGC/talent-led creative.
-   Experience working across multiple brands or markets.
