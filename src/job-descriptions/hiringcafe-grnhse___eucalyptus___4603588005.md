**About Euc**
-------------

[**We’re making good health last a lifetime**](https://www.eucalyptus.health/mission)

More than 1 billion people globally live with obesity, a major leading indicator of many preventable chronic diseases such as diabetes and heart disease.

Eucalyptus is the company behind [Juniper](https://www.myjuniper.co.uk/) and [Compound](http://www.compound.co), two of the world’s largest weight management programmes combining GLP-1 medication with a tailored nutrition and exercise programme, supported by our multidisciplinary care team of prescribers, health coaches, dietitians, nurses and pharmacists.

Our [published clinical research](https://www.eucalyptus.health/research#published-research) demonstrates that our combined approach to weight management and lifestyle change increases the likelihood of our patients losing significant weight during their treatment with Juniper by four times.

**Since launching, we’ve grown fast to support millions of patients. In the last 12 months:**

*   We grew the size of our patient base by 10x in the UK, received selective [NICE endorsement](https://www.eucalyptus.health/blog/the-opportunity-for-australian-telehealth-standards) for our holistic approach to obesity management, and grew our team from 50 to 200.
*   Globally, we grew revenue by >120% YoY, while reducing cash burned by 90% YoY, with over $100M USD raised from global investors such as BOND, NewView, Blackbird and Airtree - early backers of companies like Canva, Stripe, Uber and Airbnb.

### **About The Role**

Clinical Operations is at the heart of what we do as a healthcare business. As our patient base expands across a growing range of conditions and treatments, we are seeking an experienced Clinical Operations Manager to support the safe and effective scaling of this function.

In this role, you’ll take charge of our UK prescriber workforce (100+ FTE and rapidly growing) - the clinicians at the core of delivering safe, effective, and world-class care to thousands of patients every day. You’ll ensure they are supported, empowered, and consistently performing at their best. Alongside managing workforce planning and rota design, you’ll drive performance, uphold clinical standards, and embed the systems and oversight that enable scale. This is a pivotal role in building a prescriber function that is both resilient today and ready for the future of digital healthcare.

You’ll combine people leadership with operational execution. You’ll use data and technology to monitor performance, resolve challenges quickly, and identify opportunities to improve safety, efficiency, and patient outcomes. With new clinics and treatments launching in 2025 and beyond, you’ll play a central role in ensuring our prescriber workforce is well-prepared and able to deliver at scale.

This role will report directly to the Director of Clinical Operations UK, Alice Pan and will work closely with:

*   Guy Wallace, Head of Supply Chain & Fulfilment
*   Gianluca Uberti, VP Operations
*   Clinical, Product, Operations, and Data leaders across the UK and the global team

While this role is focused on the UK, it has clear potential to grow into a cross-market leadership role as we expand across Europe and beyond.

### **What You’ll Be Responsible For**

*   **Lead and inspire our prescriber workforce** - Take ownership of the UK prescriber team, driving engagement, setting high standards, and ensuring every clinician is empowered to deliver outstanding patient care with the right tools and tech at their fingertips.
*   **Elevate performance and quality at scale** - Harness data, automation, and real-time feedback loops to raise the bar on clinical outcomes, ensuring our workforce is consistently safe, effective, and world-class.
*   **Build systems that power growth** - Design and refine processes that make scheduling, onboarding, and workforce management seamless and scalable, leveraging automation and technology to unlock efficiency and prepare us for future expansion.
*   **Be the glue across teams** - Partner with Clinical, Operations, Product, and Data leaders to solve problems fast, embed tech-enabled workflows, and launch new services smoothly - creating an environment where prescribers thrive.

### **What We Expect From You**

*   You take accountability for prescriber scheduling, performance, and development, ensuring care is delivered safely, efficiently, and at scale.
*   You uphold high clinical standards through clear performance management, audits, and coaching, while balancing commercial goals on efficiency, cost, and conversion.
*   You bring structure, clarity, and data-driven decision-making to clinical operations, leveraging automation and AI to streamline processes and remove bottlenecks.
*   You play a key role in rolling out new services across our brands, ensuring prescribers are trained, workflows are ready, and patients receive safe, timely, and effective care from day one.
