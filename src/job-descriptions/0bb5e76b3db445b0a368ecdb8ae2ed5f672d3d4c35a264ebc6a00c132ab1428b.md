### Description

The Endpoint & Integration Engineer is a hybrid role responsible for the ownership, maintenance, and security of all endpoint management systems and tooling across the organization. This role ensures a seamless end-user experience through automation, standardization, and proactive support, while enforcing security policies and managing key technology systems that support endpoint security, patching, and remote monitoring. In addition to endpoint management, this position leads the design and implementation of integrations between internal employee systems and customer-facing portals, ensuring data flows smoothly across platforms. The ideal candidate is a hands-on technical expert with strong troubleshooting skills, a security-first mindset, and a deep understanding of both Windows and Apple ecosystems.

### Responsibilities

**Endpoint Management & Security:**
- Assume full accountability and ownership over assigned endpoint systems and tools, including lifecycle maintenance, monitoring, security hardening, and performance tuning.
- Administer and optimize tools such as Kaseya VSA (RMM), Datto AV/EDR/DattoDNS, ScreenConnect, and VulScan.
- Lead imaging and deployment processes using Autopilot, SmartDeploy, and Apple Business Manager.
- Manage mobile devices (Android/iOS) using InTune and Addigy.
- Maintain patching schedules, remediation protocols, and automated responses to threats.
- Configure and manage Zero Trust Endpoint (Threatlocker, AutoElevate) and Zero Trust Network policies (Microsoft GSA).
- Manage and enforce Azure Conditional Access policies and Cloud GPOs.

**Systems Integration & Automation:**
- Own technical configurations for CloudRadial, ConnectBooster, and ITGlue.
- Evaluate and integrate core business systems (PSA, ticketing, billing) to streamline internal workflows.
- Utilize scripting, APIs, and third-party tools to extract, transform, and present data for reporting and analysis.
- Maintain and enhance integrations across KaseyaOne, ITGlue, Network Glue, and other automation platforms.
- Leverage AI tools to improve documentation, diagnostics, and company workflows.

**Product Ownership & Operations:**
- Serve as the primary owner for key platforms including Kaseya VSA, Datto AV/EDR, Intune, Addigy, AutoElevate, Entra ID SSO, and CoPilot.
- Proactively make system adjustments, recommendations, and improvements.
- Own vendor relationships, including solution design, contract scoping, and pricing.
- Participate in an on-call rotation (Tier 1 NOC/Tier 2 SOC) every 2-3 months and serve as the Tier 3 expert for your designated systems.
- Travel to client sites is expected up to 20% of the time.

### Qualifications

**Required Skills & Experience:**
- Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering or a related field; Master's degree preferred.
- 3+ years of experience in endpoint management or IT systems engineering.
- Proven ability to work in a fast-paced environment and manage shifting priorities.
- Strong problem-solving skills and experience with scripting (PowerShell, Bash, or similar) for automation.
- Hands-on experience with Microsoft Azure, Conditional Access, and GPO management.
- Knowledge of zero trust security principles and platforms (e.g., Threatlocker, AutoElevate).
- Direct experience with Kaseya RMM, Datto products, InTune, Addigy, and Apple Business Manager.
- Excellent documentation, communication, and time-management skills.

**Preferred Certifications:**
- Microsoft Certified: Endpoint Administrator Associate
- Kaseya Certified Administrator
- Datto Certified Specialist
- CompTIA Security+, CySA+
- Apple Certified Support Professional (ACSP)

### Author's Take

**1. This role is a unique hybrid of endpoint management and systems integration. How do these two functions intersect to drive the most significant impact for the organization?**

That's a great question because it gets to the heart of why this role exists. Think of it this way: endpoint management is about building a strong, secure foundation. It's ensuring every device is stable, patched, and protected. That's crucial, but it can be reactive. The integration piece is where we build the 'smart home' on top of that foundation. It's about making our systems talk to each other, automating workflows, and turning raw data from our endpoint tools into strategic insights. The real impact comes when you, in this role, use your deep knowledge of the endpoints to design an integration that, for example, automatically isolates a threatened device, creates a high-priority ticket with diagnostic data already attached, and alerts the right team—all before a user even reports a problem. You’re not just a device manager; you’re the architect of a proactive, self-healing IT ecosystem. You’re bridging the gap between keeping the lights on and defining how brightly they shine.

**2. The description emphasizes 'full accountability and ownership' over key systems. What does that autonomy look like in practice, and how does the team culture support someone in making significant improvements?**

'Ownership' here isn't a corporate buzzword; it's our philosophy. It means you are the go-to expert and the primary driver for the platforms you manage. In practice, this looks like you identifying that a current tool isn't scaling and being empowered to research, pilot, and build the business case for its replacement. It's you writing a PowerShell script to automate a tedious onboarding process and seeing it become the new company standard. Our culture isn't about top-down directives; it's about trusting our experts. You won't be handed a 100-step instruction manual. You'll be given a goal, like 'improve our patching compliance from 95% to 99%,' and the freedom to architect the solution. You're supported by a team of peers who are also owners of their domains, so there's a deep well of knowledge to draw from, but you are in the driver's seat for your technologies. We hire people we trust to make smart decisions, and then we get out of their way so they can.
