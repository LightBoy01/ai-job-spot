**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------

**Role Purpose**  
Lead internal innovation projects and act as an AI Champion to accelerate AI adoption across the organization. This role combines project management, AI use case discovery, hands-on solution exploration, and technical enablement, overseeing tech support operations (prompting skills, tools, licenses) following FY26 strategic innovation workstream plan.

**Key Responsibilities:**

**A. Internal Project Management**   
\- Plan, execute, and monitor internal innovation projects (AI adoption, automation, AI development, Low-code & No-code projects).  
\- Manage scope, schedule, measurement, quality, risk, and internal stakeholders.  
\- Prepare project reports and updates for leadership.

**B. AI Champion & Use Case Lead**   
\- Facilitate use case intake workshops on Champion Meet-up Meeting; evaluate and prioritize AI/GenAI opportunities.   
\- Conduct hands-on POCs using GenAI Chat, Microsoft Copilot, Power Platform, Power BI, Copilot Studio...  
\- Develop and maintain prompt libraries, prompt engineering guidelines, and reusable templates.  
\- Ensure compliance with Responsible AI and internal governance frameworks.

**C. Technical Support & Tools Management**   
\- Oversee prompting skill development and provide guidance to internal users.  
\- Manage AI tools, licenses, and access control (e.g., Copilot, Azure OpenAI, Power Platform).  
\- Coordinate with champions, IT and vendors for license renewals, provisioning, and troubleshooting.

**D. Team Leadership & Enablement**   
\- Supervise and mentor a small team for Tech Support, Innovation Lab.  
\- Organize training sessions, office hours, and enablement programs for employees.  
\- Collect feedback, measure adoption, and continuously improve internal AI capabilities.

**Qualifications & Experiences**
--------------------------------

*   2-4 years of experience in project management (preferably in technology or innovation projects).
*   Proven experience with AI/automation POCs or deployments.
*   Knowledge of PMBOK or Agile methodologies.
*   Hands-on experience with Microsoft Copilot, Azure OpenAI, Power Platform, Power BI.
*   Familiarity with license management and tech support processes.
*   Excellent communication skills in English and Vietnamese.
