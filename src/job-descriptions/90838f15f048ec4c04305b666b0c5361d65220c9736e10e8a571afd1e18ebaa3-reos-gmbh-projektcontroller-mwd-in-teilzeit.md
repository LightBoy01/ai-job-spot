### Description\\nThis role is for a proactive Controller responsible for supporting the company's subsidiaries in implementing cost accounting and developing robust controlling processes. You will be a central point of contact, ensuring financial transparency, coordinating intercompany activities, managing grants, and providing critical financial analyses to enable data-driven decisions. Your expertise will be vital in shaping the future of controlling across the organization.\\n\\n### Responsibilities\\n- Act as a central point of contact for subsidiaries, actively supporting the introduction of cost accounting in close collaboration with financial accounting to deliver precise evaluations.\\n- Take responsibility for analyses and evaluations in Property Management Controlling, ensuring transparent representation of cost centers and properties to facilitate informed decisions.\\n- Coordinate intercompany reconciliations, ensuring all processes run smoothly and efficiently.\\n- Oversee and monitor funding, managing everything from application and disbursement to reporting, ensuring complete documentation and verification.\\n- Provide support in Financial Controlling as needed.\\n- Contribute with your expertise to the further development and establishment of controlling within our subsidiaries.\\n\\n### Qualifications\\n- A completed degree in Business Administration with a focus on Finance and Controlling.\\n- Proven relevant professional experience in controlling.\\n- Expert proficiency in MS Excel for complex data analyses.\\n- Experience with ERP systems, ideally LucaNet.\\n- A strong ability to work independently with innovative problem-solving approaches for process development.\\n- Excellent analytical skills and the ability to clearly present complex issues.\\n- Fluent German language skills (negotiation level).\\n\\n### Author's Take\\nThis is a compelling opportunity for a motivated Mid-Senior Controller to significantly impact financial strategy and process development across a dynamic group of subsidiaries. The company fosters a highly supportive and innovative culture, emphasizing teamwork, continuous learning, and employee well-being.
