****About the Role****
War Room is a performance marketing agency that thrives on helping clients dominate digital advertising. We’ve built our reputation by combining cutting-edge technology with expert campaign strategy. We’re taking the next leap forward with our proprietary AI-powered platform Kedet. Kedet joins multiple demand-side platforms, measurement, and verification technologies into one unified platform. Learn more about Kedet here: [Meet Kedet](https://www.warroominc.com/meet-kedet/).
We are looking for a Fractional Product Manager to help us shape the vision, strategy, and execution of Kedet. This role will bring both strategic leadership and hands-on execution, setting the foundation for product management at War Room****.****
****What You’ll Be Doing****
### Strategic Leadership
*   Define and own the product vision and roadmap for Kedet.
*   Partner with leadership to identify market opportunities and competitive differentiation.
*   Ensure Kedet’s roadmap aligns with client needs, agency growth, and future integrations.
### Tactical Execution
*   Translate business requirements into clear user stories, wireframes, and product specifications.
*   Work closely with engineering and design teams to prioritize, build, and release features.
*   Manage the product backlog and facilitate sprint planning.
*   Establish feedback loops with internal stakeholders, clients, and end users to refine Kedet.
*   Monitor product performance, adoption, and ROI, making data-informed decisions.
### Process & Foundation
*   Introduce product management best practices to War Room (roadmaps, backlog grooming, release planning, etc.).
*   Build internal alignment around Kedet’s product strategy and priorities.
*   Lay the groundwork for scaling Kedet as both a product and a business driver.
****Skills + Experience****
*   5–10 years of product management experience, ideally with SaaS, AdTech, MarTech, or AI-driven platforms.
*   Strong background in digital marketing ecosystems, DSP integrations, or programmatic advertising.
*   Proven ability to work in fractional, consulting, or early-stage environments, balancing strategy with execution.
*   Excellent communication skills, able to bridge technical and non-technical teams.
*   Track record of introducing product practices and building roadmaps from zero-to-one.
*   Entrepreneurial, adaptable, and energized by building something new.
****Nice to Have****
--------------------
*   Experience working with performance marketing agencies or platforms.
*   Knowledge of machine learning/AI applications in advertising.
*   Familiarity with multi-sided platforms and client-facing tools.
****Engagement Details****
--------------------------
*   Flexible fractional role: estimated 15–20 hours/week to start.
*   Initial contract of 6 months with potential to extend or expand.
