### Description\nGFN GmbH is an IT-specialized education provider across Germany, supporting individuals in their professional development and reorientation. We are looking for a **Head of Payer Management (m/f/x)** to join our team.\n\nIdeally, you bring initial experience in the further education sector, preferably in publicly funded training. You enjoy working with people, approach them openly, listen actively, and possess strong empathy. If you thrive in a dynamic small team where things never get boring, then you are the right fit for us! This strategic role focuses on the development and optimization of our payer management systems, driving growth, and embracing digital transformation, playing a key part in our journey towards a digitally driven educational ecosystem.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Modern Reporting System Development:** Analyze current/target states per location (STO), market share and revenue development; provide regular reporting to location, regional, and business unit management.\n*   **Strategic Control:** Develop and implement individual growth strategies per location, focusing on expanding or securing market shares.\n*   **Proactive Market Development:** Identify and strategically approach new payers (KST) in collaboration with location managers; conduct nationwide on-site visits as required.\n*   **Performance Measurement & Best Practices:** Establish matrix and checklists to derive success factors, transfer them to new locations and payers, and track measures.\n*   **Support & Guidance:** Participate in payer meetings (e.g., AfA/JC) and assist at in-house and trade fairs.\n*   **Innovation Management:** Further develop the database for payer management; initiate B2G sales and marketing activities (outbound calls, email marketing).\n*   **Digitalization & Efficiency:** Promote digital processes in sales and participant support; utilize AI-powered learning platforms and predictive analytics for success measurement.\n*   **Representation Function:** Serve as backup/representative for the management of new locations, working closely with the \"New Locations\" leadership team.\n*   **Growth & Transformation:** Support the rollout of new digital learning formats, contribute to the automation and \"agentification\" of work processes, and identify leverage factors for acquiring and retaining new payers.\n*   **Quality & Innovation Focus:** Convey best practices to location and regional management, actively shape the transformation into a digitally driven educational ecosystem.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed degree in Business Administration, Education Management, Business Informatics, or an equivalent qualification.\n*   Several years of experience in strategic project management, ideally within the education or further education sector.\n*   Strong analytical skills and experience in building reporting and controlling systems.\n*   High affinity for digitalization, data analysis, and modern learning technologies (e.g., AI-powered platforms, micro-learning).\n*   Excellent communication skills, persuasiveness, and willingness to travel for nationwide assignments.\n*   Experience in collaborating with payers (e.g., AfA, JC) and managing complex stakeholder structures.\n*   Self-initiative, innovative spirit, and the ability to actively drive change.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a highly strategic and impactful leadership role for someone passionate about leveraging data and digital innovation to drive growth in the education sector. GFN GmbH offers a dynamic environment where your expertise in payer management, reporting, and strategic development will be crucial for the company's digital transformation. The culture emphasizes a supportive, diverse, and appreciative collaboration, complemented by excellent benefits that prioritize employee well-being and mobility, such as 30 vacation days, Jobrad-Leasing, an EGYM Wellpass subsidy, internet allowance, and a heavily subsidized Deutschland-Ticket. If you're an analytical, communicative, and proactive leader ready to shape the future of educational services, this opportunity provides a platform for significant contribution and professional growth.
