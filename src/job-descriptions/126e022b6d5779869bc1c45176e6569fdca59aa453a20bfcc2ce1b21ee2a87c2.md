Our ~15,000 employees create world-class solutions in communications, 5G, automotive, energy, quantum, aerospace, defense, and semiconductor markets for customers in over 100 countries. Our [award-winning](https://jobs.keysight.com/awards) culture embraces a bold vision of where technology can take us and a passion for tackling challenging problems with industry-first solutions. We believe that when people feel a sense of belonging, they can be more creative, innovative, and thrive at all points in their careers.

Responsibilities:

Want to be at the cutting edge of Research and Development in cellular Networks and AI? Join the Milan team, which is taking world-leading Keysight electronics products from wild ideas to proof of concept. 

As a student in Telecom, Software, or AI Engineering, you will be involved in the transformation of a test and measurement idea into the development of a proof of concept. You will be collaborating with international colleagues from the 5G & 6G research facilities in Europe, where a young & dynamic team is settled.

You understand or want to learn more about 6G? Do you want to apply your skills in solving real 6G challenges? Then this internship is for you!

You will solve a broad range of Telecom problems in the 6G network test space. The work is of varying scope and complexity and will expose you to what engineers do daily in a research lab. We have different topics ranging from executing 5G/6G tests on our partners’ networks, running experiments to assess network performance, testbeds to create datasets for AI training, maintaining complex software/cloud/communication systems, running experimentation to assess AI performance, etc.

The amount of work hours and weekly schedule is flexible and takes into account your study requirements.

The project you work on will be part of an existing team project where you can cooperate with experienced engineers from multiple Keysight R&D centers all around Europe. The work can also be combined with your Bachelor's or Master's thesis projects as part of your education.

Qualifications:

*   Enthusiastic Bachelor's or Master's level student
    
*   Fluent in English (written and oral)
    
*   Passionate about working on advanced test and measurement challenges
    
*   Aspiring Telecom, Software, or AI/ML engineer eager to apply academic knowledge in real conditions
    
*   Interested in investigating cutting-edge testing methodologies in cellular systems
    
*   Must be studying at one of the local Milan universities
    
*   No relocation package or relocation support offered
