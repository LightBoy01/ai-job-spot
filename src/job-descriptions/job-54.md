As an AI Application Support Engineer at LSEG, you will play a critical role in ensuring the smooth operation and high-quality user experience of our AI-powered applications and platforms. Based in our dynamic Bangalore office, you will be a key member of our global team, providing expert support for a large-scale financial software product. This is an exciting opportunity to work at the intersection of finance and artificial intelligence, with a focus on problem-solving, innovation, and customer service.

### Responsibilities
- Provide technical support for AI-based applications, identifying and resolving issues reported by users.
- Analyse, troubleshoot, and resolve technical problems in a timely manner, ensuring minimal impact on users.
- Monitor the performance of AI applications and address potential areas of concern before they escalate.
- Ability to and assist in enhancing the performance, scalability, and usability of AI applications.
- Respond to user inquiries related to the application’s AI features, providing guidance, documentation, and solutions.
- Conduct root cause analysis on recurring problems and provide insights to the development team for future improvements.
- Create and maintain user guides, FAQs, and other support documentation for customers.
- Perform regular system updates, patches, and maintenance to ensure smooth operation of the AI Insight application.
- Stay up to date with the latest AI technologies, trends, and standard methodologies.
- Handle client critical issues and manage incidents to resolution, ensuring customer satisfaction.
- Provides Level 2 24x7 technical and production support, including out-of-hours and on-call support.

### Qualifications
- 4 to 6 years of professional experience in Cloud Computing technologies (AWS or Azure) and SRE practices.
- Bachelor's degree in computer science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field (or equivalent experience).
- Proven experience in technical support or application support, preferably in AI-based applications.
- Experience with AI/ML technologies, ML OPS, frameworks, and tools (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch, etc.).
- Excellent problem-solving, and analytical skills.
- Experience with programming languages like Python, Java, or similar.
- Solid understanding of cloud services and platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, GCP).
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills with a focus on customer service.
- Ability to manage multiple support cases and prioritize tasks effectively.
- Experience with Observability practices with logging, metrics, tracing, and alerting.
- Familiarity with DevOps practices and tools (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes).
- Understanding of natural language processing (NLP) or computer vision applications.
- Experience in solving distributed systems and cloud-based application problems.
- Experience in Financial or Investment Banking environments is preferred.
- Experience working with machine learning models and deploying AI solutions is a plus.
- Certification in cloud platforms or relevant AI technologies is a plus.
- Hands on Experience with one of the following cloud platforms: Azure, AWS, or GCP.
