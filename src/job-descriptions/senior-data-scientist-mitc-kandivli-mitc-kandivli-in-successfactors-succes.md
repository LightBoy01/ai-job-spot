<b>About the Team</b>

<p>Mahindra AI Division is the AI innovation arm within Mahindra Group. The division experiments with state-of-the-art AI technologies and is focused on developing and delivering cutting-edge AI products to the group. With the division’s team-expansion plan, there is an excellent opportunity for your career growth in the team.</p>


<b>Responsibilities</b>

<ul>
<li>Provide data science and AI expertise to design, build, and deliver scalable ML and deep learning solutions.</li>
<li>Work extensively with <strong>tabular data</strong> to develop predictive models, perform feature engineering, and optimize performance for real-world applications.</li>
<li>Apply <strong>conventional machine learning techniques</strong> (e.g., regression, classification, ensemble methods) alongside advanced deep learning methods.</li>
<li>Build and optimize <strong>deep learning models</strong> for structured and unstructured data, including text and audio.</li>
<li>Lead development of solutions for <strong>audio data</strong>, including classification, recognition, and signal processing tasks.</li>
<li><strong>Design, fine-tune, and deploy LLMs (Large Language Models), SLMs (Small Language Models), and other Generative AI solutions</strong> for diverse business applications.</li>
<li>Translate business problems into well-structured ML/AI problems and deliver actionable insights.</li>
<li>Deploy production-grade models with a focus on efficiency, scalability, and maintainability.</li>
<li>Collaborate with cross-functional teams including product managers, engineers, and domain experts to deliver high-impact projects.</li>
<li>Mentor junior data scientists and interns, fostering innovation and best practices.</li>
</ul>


<b>Qualification Requirements:</b>

<p><strong>Educational:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Bachelor’s degree with at least 7 years of experience or Master’s with at least 4 years of experience in Data Science/ML/AI.</li>
<li>Preferred to have degree from a Tier-1/2 institute (IIT/IISc/NITs if studied in India) or a globally top-ranked university (as per QS).</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Technical:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Proven expertise working with <strong>large-scale tabular data</strong> and building machine learning models.</li>
<li>Strong knowledge of <strong>conventional ML algorithms</strong> (tree-based models, SVMs, clustering, etc.).</li>
<li>Hands-on experience with <strong>deep learning frameworks</strong> such as TensorFlow / PyTorch.</li>
<li><strong>Mandatory expertise in LLMs, SLMs, and Generative AI</strong>, including fine-tuning, prompt engineering, and deployment.</li>
<li>Proficiency in <strong>Python</strong> and libraries such as scikit-learn, pandas, NumPy.</li>
<li>Exposure to model deployment frameworks and tools (ONNX, Triton, TensorRT, FastAPI, etc.).</li>
<li>Familiarity with cloud platforms (Azure/GCP) for training and deploying ML solutions.</li>
<li>Experience with Databricks is a plus.</li>
<li>Strong understanding of MLOps practices, reproducibility, and model monitoring.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Other:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.</li>
<li>Demonstrated experience working collaboratively in cross-functional teams.</li>
<li>Ability to balance research and production priorities while meeting deadlines.</li>
<li>Passionate about applying AI/ML to solve diverse real-world problems, especially at the intersection of <strong>GenAI and enterprise AI adoption</strong>.</li>
</ul>
