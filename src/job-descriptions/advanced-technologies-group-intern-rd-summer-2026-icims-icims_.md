<p><strong>JOB OBJECTIVE:</strong> The Advanced Technologies Group (ATG) at Promega is a multidisciplinary R&amp;D team, united by the goal of creating innovative technologies for the study of molecular interactions, gene editing, protein biochemistry and cellular health. As an intern with ATG you will serve as a valued member of our team, working collaboratively and creatively to develop novel, cutting-edge gene editing technologies. You will gain experience in CRISPR-based gene editing, rational and AI-guided protein redesign, mammalian cell culturing and computational data analysis. You will read foundational and current scientific literature in order to contribute creative, original ideas to advance your project.
