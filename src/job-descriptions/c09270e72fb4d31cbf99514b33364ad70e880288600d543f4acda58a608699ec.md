**Solutions Sales Specialist – Optimisation and Governance**  

This is a hybrid opportunity where you’ll work 3 days in the Sydney office, 2 days a week from home.

  
Now is the time to bring your **solution sales** expertise to a permanent role for a **Fortune 500 Solutions Integrator** where success and fulfillment go hand in hand. You’ll be at the forefront of helping businesses transform their cloud, data, AI, cybersecurity, and analytics to ensure they are future-ready.

As a **Solutions Sales Specialist with Insight,** you’ll partner with sales and delivery teams to develop compelling solution strategies that align with customer objectives and trends. We’ll count on you to lead customer delivery sessions and develop tailored recommendations.  Along the way, you’ll Lead the qualification of your relevant practice’s opportunities to support bid no bid decisions. Develop and deliver high-impact proposals, executive-ready presentations, and technical demonstrations. Contribute to sales enablement through the creation and operationalization of reusable assets, frameworks, and programs. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to align technical and commercial messaging across customer touchpoints.
