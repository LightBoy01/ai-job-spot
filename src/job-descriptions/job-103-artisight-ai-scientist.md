Artisight is seeking an AI Scientist with deep expertise in Vision AI to join our team. In this role, you will work at the intersection of research and application—designing novel vision models, collaborating with cross-functional teams, and deploying these technologies into real-world healthcare environments to power our smart hospital platform.

This is a high-impact role for a candidate with a Master’s or PhD in a related field and a proven research track record.

### Responsibilities

- Research, design, and implement cutting-edge computer vision models (image classification, object detection, segmentation, video understanding).
- Develop and optimize generative vision models (e.g., text-to-image, text-to-video).
- Train, fine-tune, and evaluate large-scale vision foundation models for healthcare-specific applications.
- Collaborate with AI scientists, engineers, and product teams to integrate vision AI capabilities into Artisight’s platform.
- Stay at the forefront of vision AI and multimodal learning research, bringing innovations into production.

### Qualifications

- M.S. or Ph.D. in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Applied AI, or a related discipline.
- Demonstrated expertise in computer vision research, evidenced by open-source contributions or peer-reviewed publications (e.g., CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR).
- Hands-on experience with image/video classification, object detection, segmentation, and/or generative models.
- Strong knowledge of deep learning methods like transformers, diffusion models, and CNNs.
- Proficiency in PyTorch or TensorFlow for large-scale model training.
- **Nice to have:** Experience with multimodal learning (vision + audio + text), 3D vision, medical imaging, or real-time video analysis.
