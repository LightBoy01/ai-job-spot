**Who are we?**

UpGuard’s mission is to make life easier for security teams. We meticulously create robust solutions that enable our customers to identify, assess, and remediate cybersecurity risk across their attack surface, vendor ecosystem, workforce, and trust relationships. Our integrated cyber risk posture management platform combines comprehensive security ratings, instant risk assessments, templated security questionnaires, threat intelligence capabilities, and agentic AI to give organizations a holistic view of their risk surface.

  

Our Product team at UpGuard comprises of our Product Management, Product Support, Engineering, Design, Data Leaks Detection, and Third Party Risk Management Services. Our Product team’s vision is to become the leader in the Cyber VRM category by providing a best-in-class SaaS platform for organizations to manage their third-party risk and external attack surface. Our purpose as a Product team is simply to build a product that enables this. We aim to build a product that users love and come to rely on, and one that regularly and consistently improves.

  

**Where does this role fit in?**

  

We're entering a new phase of growth, expanding our product suite and user base and product design is a key strategic differentiator in this evolution. As VP of Product Design, you'll sit at the heart of this transformation, reporting to the Chief Product Officer and leading a growing team of designers and a UX writer. You'll scale our design system, drive the adoption of AI tools to improve efficiency, and help shape a unified, world class experience across every touchpoint in close partnership with Product, Engineering, and Brand.

  

Reporting to the Chief Product Officer, you'll lead a team of five Product Designers (with plans to grow) and a UX Writer. You'll be responsible for scaling our design system into a robust foundation that enables consistency and speed, while championing AI-assisted design tooling to reduce repetitive work and maximise time spent on solving user problems. You'll also partner closely with the VP of Product, VP of Engineering, and Brand & Marketing Design to ensure a unified, world class experience across all touchpoints.

**Drive Design Vision & Strategy**  
*   Craft and socialise a compelling design vision that aligns with UpGuard’s product strategy and brand.
*   Establish a design‑system roadmap that scales across multiple products and future use cases.
*   Define success metrics e.g. adoption of the design system, usability/NPS increases, reduction in design‑to‑dev cycle time and report progress to executives.

  

  
  
**Build & Scale a Modern Design System**  
*   Lead and accelerate the evolution of our token‑driven Figma design system, formalising governance, documentation, and contributor workflows.
*   Formalise design‑ops processes (versioning, governance, documentation) so patterns remain consistent as the suite grows.
*   Ensure collaboration with Brand & Marketing Design to keep visual language unified.
  
  
**Lead & Empower the Design Team**  
*   Hire, mentor, and grow a high‑performing team of Product Designers.
*   Provide coaching, clear career paths, and effective feedback loops so designers produce their best work.
*   Foster a culture of rapid prototyping, user testing, and measurable learning.

  

  
  
**Integrate AI Into the Design Workflow**  
*   Identify and deploy generative‑AI tools (e.g. for ideation, content, asset generation) that streamline designer workflows.
*   Partner with Engineering and Product teams to experiment with AI‑driven UX patterns inside the product itself.

  

  
  
**Cross‑Functional Leadership & Execution**  
*   Collaborate daily with Product, Engineering, Customer Success, and Support to ship features that delight users.
*   Translate complex cybersecurity concepts into simple, intuitive experiences.
*   Balance strategic initiatives with hands‑on design reviews and execution oversight.

  

  
  
**What do we need from you?**  
*   **Proven leadership:** Multiple years leading product‑design teams (Director/Head/VP level), ideally in a high‑growth B2B software environment, but we value exceptional accomplishments regardless of sector.
*   **Track record of impact:** Clear examples where your leadership elevated product usability, visual quality, or speed of delivery.
*   **Design‑system expertise:** Experience building or scaling a component library and token system in Figma (or similar).
*   **AI mindset:** Familiarity with generative‑AI tools and a passion for integrating them into design workflows.
*   **Collaboration skills:** Excellent communication and influence across Product, Engineering, and Brand teams.
*   **User‑centred craft:** Ability to pair data‑driven insights with strong product‑thinking and interaction‑design chops.
*   **Domain exposure:** Experience in B2B SaaS is highly valued; cybersecurity familiarity is a bonus but not mandatory.
*   **Location:** Must be based on Australia’s east coast (AEST/AEDT time zone) and able to work remotely or from our optional offices.
  
  
**What would give you an edge?**  
*   Demonstrated success introducing AI‑assisted processes that measurably improved design velocity or quality.
*   Experience scaling design orgs from single‑product to multi‑product platforms.
*   Contributions to open‑source or public design systems.
*   Familiarity with accessibility best practices and inclusive‑design methodologies.
