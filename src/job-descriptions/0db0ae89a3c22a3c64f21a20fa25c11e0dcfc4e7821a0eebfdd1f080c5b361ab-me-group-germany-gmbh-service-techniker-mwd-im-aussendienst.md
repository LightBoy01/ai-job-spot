### Description\nThe Me Group Germany GmbH, a 100% subsidiary of the globally active ME-Group, is looking for a dedicated Service Technician. As a key player for their Fotofix and Revolution Waschstationen brands in Germany, you'll be joining a dynamic company that values innovation and collaboration. If you're eager to contribute, drive change, and help shape the future, this full-time role in the Kassel, Göttingen, and Fulda region offers an exciting opportunity to be part of a team that's always evolving.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Support and regular inspection of our automated machines.\n- Performance of maintenance, repair, and upkeep tasks.\n- Cleaning and refilling of machines with consumables.\n- Installation and deinstallation of various machine types.\n- Direct point of contact for our customers on-site.\n- Implementation of promotional activities on the machines.\n- Digital documentation of all activities via our service app.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Flexibility in field service and daily planning.\n- Completed technical training (e.g., electrician or technician) is advantageous.\n- Reliable, independent, and structured working method.\n- Team spirit and sense of responsibility.\n- Proficient use of PC and mobile applications.\n- Residence within the service area.\n- Driver's license class B and good German language skills required.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at Me Group Germany GmbH offers a fantastic opportunity for a proactive Service Technician to be at the forefront of customer interaction and operational excellence. The company emphasizes a dynamic work environment with flat hierarchies and significant growth potential, evidenced by their commitment to continuous development through intensive onboarding and targeted training. Benefits like Wellpass membership, corporate discounts, and a bonus program highlight a focus on employee well-being and engagement. It's an ideal fit for someone seeking autonomy, diverse responsibilities, and a chance to directly impact customer satisfaction within a supportive, forward-thinking organization.
