<p>The Nuclear Company is the fastest growing startup in the nuclear and energy space creating a never before seen fleet-scale approach to building nuclear reactors. Through its design-once, build-many approach and coalition building across communities, regulators, and financial stakeholders, The Nuclear Company is committed to delivering safe and reliable electricity at the lowest cost, while catalyzing the nuclear industry toward rapid development in America and globally.</p>
<p><strong>About the role:&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p>We are seeking a highly skilled Talent Sourcer/Researcher to join our Talent Acquisition team. The ideal candidate is an expert in leveraging AI tools, data analytics, and emerging technologies to source and build robust candidate pipelines across diverse skill sets. This role requires strong project management skills, proficiency in social media sourcing, and a proven track record of using AI-driven recruiting strategies to attract top talent.</p>
<p><strong>Responsibilities:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Utilize AI-powered tools (e.g., LinkedIn Recruiter, AI sourcing platforms, chatbots) to identify, engage, and attract high-quality candidates.</li>
<li>Leverage data analytics to optimize sourcing strategies, track pipeline metrics, and improve recruitment outcomes.</li>
<li>Build and maintain diverse candidate pipelines across multiple skill sets, ensuring a steady flow of qualified talent for current and future roles.</li>
<li>Execute innovative sourcing campaigns on platforms like LinkedIn, X, GitHub, and niche communities to engage passive and active candidates.</li>
<li>Lead sourcing projects, coordinate with hiring managers, and manage timelines to meet recruitment goals efficiently.</li>
<li>Stay ahead of industry trends by adopting and integrating new AI tools, sourcing platforms, and recruitment technologies.</li>
<li>Partner with the Talent Acquisition team and hiring managers to align sourcing strategies with organizational needs and diversity goals.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Experience</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Business, or a related field (or equivalent experience).</li>
<li>7-10+ years of experience in talent sourcing or recruiting, with a focus on AI-driven and data-informed strategies.</li>
<li>Experience using AI-powered sourcing platforms and chatbots to identify and engage candidates.</li>
<li>Familiarity with data analytics for recruitment, including sourcing metrics and pipeline tracking.</li>
<li>Proven success in sourcing candidates through social media platforms like LinkedIn, X, and professional networks.</li>
<li>Demonstrated ability to manage multiple sourcing projects, prioritize tasks, and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment.</li>
<li>Experience researching and evaluating new sourcing tools and technologies using data-driven insights to enhance recruitment strategies.</li>
<li>Ability to research talent markets and pools, leveraging data to identify trends, skill availability, and competitive hiring landscapes.</li>
<li>Knowledge of niche job boards, diversity sourcing strategies, or industry-specific recruiting is a plus.</li>
</ul>
