### Description\nimmocloud is rapidly digitizing property management for landlords and administrators, serving over **15,000 customers** and experiencing strong growth. We are seeking an operative Marketing Manager to elevate our marketing visibility, modernity, and consistency across all channels. This hybrid role involves hands-on content production and the optimization of existing marketing channels.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Maintain, develop, and optimize our website (WordPress, landing pages, A/B tests, technical SEO).\n*   Plan, create, and publish content on Instagram, LinkedIn, and YouTube, including formats, Reels, and videos (preparation and post-production).\n*   Take responsibility for our online magazine, including topic research, SEO optimization, and collaboration with our SEO agency.\n*   Conceive and send newsletters, analyze their performance, and optimize customer journeys.\n*   Operate as a hybrid role: hands-on content production combined with the optimization of existing channels.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   At least **3 years of experience** in online marketing or content creation.\n*   Highly proficient in WordPress, social media, and copywriting.\n*   Experience with video editing/production (e.g., CapCut, DaVinci) and basic SEO knowledge (e.g., Sistrix).\n*   Proficiency with newsletter tools (e.g., Brevo/Mailchimp) and design skills (e.g., Canva).\n*   Creative, trend-affine, with a hands-on mentality – you love content, take responsibility, and bring your own ideas to the table.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a proactive and creative Marketing Manager to make a significant impact within a fast-growing company. If you thrive on autonomy and love to see your ideas come to life, you'll find ample space for design and influence here. The hybrid work model, generous vacation, and a supportive team further sweeten the deal, making it ideal for someone who wants to genuinely \\\"do\\\" marketing and drive tangible results.
