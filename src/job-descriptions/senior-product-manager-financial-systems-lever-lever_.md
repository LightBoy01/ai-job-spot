<br><br><br>About the Role 

We’re building the financial backbone for modern real estate,  a system where data flows intelligently, ledgers stay audit-ready, and financial operations scale effortlessly.

This is your chance to build from first principles: a high-trust accounting infrastructure inside a powerful graph-based platform. You’ll design the interfaces and system logic that power rent flows, payables, inter-company transactions, and reconciliations, with built-in intelligence, not endless spreadsheets.

This role isn’t tucked away in the back office. You’ll sit at the intersection of product, finance, data, and AI, owning the systems that bring clarity, integrity, and automation to every financial decision.
