**⭐ About daydream**
--------------------

_**daydream automates programmatic SEO, end-to-end.**_

We believe the future of SEO lies in automation, which is why daydream leverages a combination of AI-enabled services and software to execute SEO growth playbooks. Our platform helps businesses identify search patterns, create high-quality content at scale, and continuously optimize it automatically—empowering companies like Rho, Clay, Beacons, and dozens more to rapidly scale their growth.

We've raised $6.2M from leading investors like First Round Capital, Basis Set Ventures, WndrCo, SOMA Capital, current and former Heads of Growth from Notion, Airtable, Webflow, and Dropbox, and notable angels such as Lenny Rachitsky and Eli Schwartz.

**🔧 The problem we’re trying to solve**
----------------------------------------

Every company needs to do two things:

1.  Build a product.
    
2.  Acquire customers.
    

There are three ways to acquire customers — sales, product-led growth, and growth marketing. Of these three, growth marketing is particularly hard to staff for because great internal talent is find (ex. in SEO, there are probably < 20 people who we would consider world-class), and time-consuming to ramp.

Then there’s the agencies, but agencies are often slow, lack strong technical talent, and in general are associated with poor quality of outcomes.

**🧑‍💻 How we’re solving this problem**
----------------------------------------

daydream is different — we’ve built a vertically integrated software + services stack with a singular goal in mind — deliver outstanding outcomes for every customer we service, repeatedly and consistently in a fraction of the time they expect. While we’ve started with SEO, we expect to expand to all other major growth marketing channels over the near future.

**👷🏻‍♀️** **What You’ll Be Doing**
------------------------------------

Daydream runs a **forward-deployed growth model**—our Growth Leads are working directly with our customers' teams, delivering SEO execution as a high-impact extension of their in-house team. This is not a passive agency model. It’s hands-on and deeply strategic.

As Head of Growth, you'll own the performance, structure, and scale of that entire delivery engine. This is a pivotal leadership role—responsible for making sure every Growth Lead is set up to drive outcomes at scale, across dozens of fast-growing startups.

*   **Own the forward-deployed team:** Manage, mentor, and scale a team of Growth Leads and Growth Team Leads delivering embedded SEO execution.
    
*   **Drive performance:** Ensure quality, consistency, and impact across all client engagements as you are leading growth for a portfolio of clients rather than a product.
    
*   **Develop team structure:** Set up Growth Leads for success through onboarding, training, and day-to-day systems—while supporting Growth Team Leads in managing and developing their pods of Growth Leads.
    
*   **Set strategy:** Define how we hire, train, and operate across our growth delivery function.
    
*   **Interface with key clients:** Act as the executive partner on high-priority accounts.
    
*   **Systematize scale:** Build processes, playbooks, and operational infrastructure to grow efficiently.
    
*   **Work cross-functionally:** Partner with GTM, product, and engineering to continuously improve our growth stack.
