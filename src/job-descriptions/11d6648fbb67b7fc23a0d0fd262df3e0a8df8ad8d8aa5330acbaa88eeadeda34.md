**Required Skills and Experience**
*   At least 3 years of experience in designing and implementing AI, ML, and Data-driven solutions, preferably on Public Cloud platforms, with a focus on Machine Learning, Generative AI, and Data Platforms
*   Concrete, hands-on experience in designing and delivering scalable AI/ML solutions or platforms using modern paradigms (MLOps, Lakehouse, Data Mesh, AI Factories, Vector Databases) aligned to customer business needs
*   Proven ability to analyze business processes and translate them into AI-enabled solutions within Public Cloud Hyperscaler environments
*   Ability to define AI/ML architectural solutions with key requirements such as performance, security, explainability, and reliability
*   Solid understanding of Cloud-native AI and Data services, such as: Azure ML, Azure OpenAI, Databricks, Azure Data Lake, Azure Synapse, AWS SageMaker, Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, BigQuery, LangChain, Vector DBs (Chroma, Pinecone, Milvus)
*   Knowledge of AI/ML frameworks and tools, including but not limited to: TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, Hugging Face Transformers, OpenAI APIs, Ollama, LangChain
*   Experience with Data Engineering, pipelines, and orchestration tools: Databricks, Airflow, Spark, dbt
*   Proficiency in programming languages relevant to AI/ML and Data pipelines: Python (mandatory), SQL, optionally Scala or Java
*   Knowledge of prompt engineering techniques and best practices for Large Language Models (LLMs) and Generative AI solutions
*   Familiarity with version control and CI/CD processes for AI/ML projects (Git, MLflow, DVC, etc.)
**Preferred Skills and Experience**
*   Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or a related field
*   Minimum 5 years of experience in delivering AI/ML/Data consulting services for enterprise customers across multiple industries
*   Experience with production-grade AI/ML deployments, including MLOps pipelines and monitoring of AI solutions
*   Familiarity with regulatory, ethical, and security considerations for AI systems in enterprise environments
