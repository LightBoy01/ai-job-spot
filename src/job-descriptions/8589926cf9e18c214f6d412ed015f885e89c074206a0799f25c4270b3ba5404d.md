**Requirements**

We are seeking a seasoned technologist who can bridge **developer advocacy** and **Solution Architecture**. In this hybrid role, you will both **educate and empower developer communities** while also **advising enterprise customers** on large-scale Data, AI, and Cloud transformations.

You’ll work hands-on with cutting-edge technologies ranging from **Big Data platforms (Databricks, Snowflake, Hadoop, Kafka, MongoDB, etc.)** to **LLMs, AI/ML, and distributed systems**, while representing our platform in communities, conferences, and strategic customer engagements.

This role is ideal for someone who thrives in both **community-facing developer advocacy** and **strategic solution architecture consulting**, helping to build the future of data-driven applications.  

**Key Responsibilities:**

**Developer Advocacy & Community Building**  

*   Create authentic technical resources: open source demos, code samples, documentation, how-to guides, tutorials, and blogs.
*   Build awareness and motivate developers to build applications, data pipelines, and data science workflows on our platform.
*   Engage in communities like **Stack Overflow, GitHub, Slack, Reddit, Discord** to support developers and identify new resources.
*   Deliver impactful talks at **conferences, meetups, and events**, showcasing solutions, best practices, and platform capabilities.
*   Collaborate with product teams to communicate developer needs and influence roadmap.
*   Define and own success metrics for developer engagement, content reach, and community growth.

**Solution Architecture & Customer Success**  **:**

*   Partner with enterprise customers as a **trusted technical advisor**, guiding their Data & AI transformation initiatives.
*   Architect and implement scalable solutions across **data engineering, data science, analytics, and ML/AI workflows**.
*   Lead customer workshops, proof-of-concepts, migrations, and complex delivery programs.
*   Consult on cloud-native architectures, integrations, and data governance/security (Unity Catalog, RBAC).
*   Advise customers on competitive landscapes (Snowflake, Databricks, EMR, Sagemaker, MongoDB Atlas, etc.).
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams: product engineering, marketing, customer success, and partner organizations.
*   Mentor internal teams and customers on **best practices, distributed computing, and modern data architectures**.  
    

**Required Skills:**  
**Must-Have:**

*   **7–10+ years** of experience in **Developer Relations, Developer Advocacy, or Solution Architecture**.
*   Proven expertise in **Big Data technologies** (Apache Spark, Kafka, Data Lakes, Hadoop, Cassandra, Delta Lake, Trino).
*   Strong coding experience in at least two of: **Python, PySpark, Java, Scala, JavaScript, Go, Ruby, R, or C#**.
*   Hands-on experience with **LLMs and AI/ML platforms** (Langchain, LlamaIndex, HuggingFace, mistral, Pinecone, Weaviate, MLflow).
*   Proficiency with **data platforms and cloud ecosystems**: Databricks Lakehouse, Snowflake, MongoDB, Postgres, NoSQL (MongoDB, Redis, HBase), and at least one of **AWS, Azure, or GCP**.
*   Strong background in **SQL, Spark SQL, Spark Streaming, distributed computing, and cloud-native architectures**.
*   Knowledge of **MLOps, GenAI solutions, and scalable production deployments** is a plus.
*   Exceptional **communication, technical writing, whiteboarding, and presentation skills**.
*   Experience working with **cross-functional teams** and in **customer-facing roles**.
*   Willingness to travel (~30%) for conferences, events, and onsite customer engagements.
