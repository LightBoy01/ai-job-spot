Description

At RFG Advisory, words move people—and we’re hiring a Copywriter who can turn insight into action. You’ll shape our brand voice, build high-performing content across B2B (corporate) and B2C (advisor) audiences, and level up our search footprint with AEO/SEO and AI-powered workflows.  

_**Please note, this role requires prior experience in financial services. Only applicants with relevant experience will be considered.**_

  

**Location**

*   This position is located in Birmingham, AL. We offer a hybrid schedule 3 days in-office, 2 days remote.
*   Remote work will be considered for the right candidate. If remote, occasional travel to Birmingham will be required. 

**What You’ll Do** 

**Content Creation & Strategy**

*   Own and evolve the RFG Writing Style Guide to ensure clarity, consistency, and brand alignment.
*   Develop high-impact content across formats: website pages, blogs, emails, social media, ads, brochures, presentations, video/podcast scripts, and more.
*   Collaborate with design, marketing, and leadership to turn strategy into polished, high-performing assets.

**SEO & Organic Growth**

*   Lead AEO/SEO strategy: build topic maps, conduct keyword and intent research, and optimize on-page elements (titles, meta, schema).
*   Improve visibility across search engines and answer engines (e.g., People Also Ask, AI Overviews, Featured Snippets).

**Editorial Operations**

*   Manage content calendars and coordinate with B2B and Advisor Marketing teams.
*   Align content with HubSpot campaigns, KPIs, and broader marketing goals.

**AI-Enhanced Writing**

*   Draft elite AI prompts and maintain custom GPT instructions to generate repeatable, on-brand content.
*   QA AI-generated outputs and fine-tune tone, voice, and structure.

**Compliance Collaboration**

*   Package content for compliance review and partner with the compliance team to ensure fast, accurate approvals.

**Analytics & Optimization**

*   Partner with analytics and vendor teams (HubSpot, GA4, Search Console) to monitor performance and evolve the content roadmap.

**Key Workstreams**

*   **Corporate/B2B:** Thought leadership blogs, market insights, case studies, event kits, capabilities collateral, and WordPress/Yoast website updates.
*   **Advisor/B2C:** Pathfinder content, brand guides, microsites, and advisor-deployable assets.
*   **Search & Reporting:** Organic performance analysis and roadmap evolution.

 At RFG, you won’t just write—you’ll lead with a servant heart, iterate to excellence, and execute relentlessly. Your work will have visible impact every day. Join a team that values innovation, collaboration, and purpose-driven growth.

Requirements

*   3+ years of copywriting/content experience (financial services a plus).
