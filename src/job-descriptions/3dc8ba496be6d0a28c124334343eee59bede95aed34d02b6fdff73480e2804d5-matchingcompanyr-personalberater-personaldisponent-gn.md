### Description\nThe MatchingCompany® is your partner for professional and sustainable placement in the HR sector. With over 25 years of industry experience, we are specialists in nationwide placement of technical and leadership positions in People Management. We are seeking **HR Consultants / Disponents (gn)** for temporary employment (ANÜ) of industrial-technical personnel on behalf of a client at our Osnabrück location. This 360° role combines recruitment, client acquisition, and comprehensive personnel management.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Recruiting new employees for temporary assignments at client companies.\n- Independently managing the entire personnel administration and support process.\n- Providing sustainable support for existing clients and actively acquiring new clients.\n- Sourcing candidates through various channels, managing contract agreements, and providing personal support.\n- Appropriately assigning employees (gn) according to defined client requirements.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed vocational training or degree.\n- Proven experience in the HR services industry (temporary employment), specifically within the industrial-technical sector.\n- Fluent German language skills, both written and spoken.\n- Enjoyment of sales and a collaborative team spirit.\n- High degree of personal responsibility and a strong service-oriented mindset.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at The MatchingCompany offers a fantastic opportunity for an experienced HR professional to thrive as a 360° consultant. With a focus on the industrial-technical sector, you'll manage the full recruitment lifecycle, from client acquisition to candidate placement and support. The company values independence, teamwork, and provides exceptional benefits including a permanent contract, over 30 days of holiday, performance-based pay, and extensive development opportunities. It's an ideal environment for those seeking a challenging yet supportive career where impact and growth are genuinely fostered.
