**Job Description:**

As Teamlead Brand Marketing Campaigns & Cooperations (m/f/d), you will **shape Breuninger’s international brand presence** by leading the planning, development and execution of our **360° brand campaigns and strategic collaborations**. 

Working closely with internal stakeholders (Creative, Media, Buying, CRM, Analytics) as well as external brand partners and agencies, you will ensure that Breuninger is positioned as a leading international omnichannel luxury fashion retailer.  

The ideal candidate (m/f/d) is a highly trained marketing professional, experienced in brand and campaign management and communication planning with a passion for lifestyle and fashion. You must have strong strategic and execution skills, ensuring that the cross-functional team continuously strives for excellence and delivers ambitious and impactful campaigns. You are an extraordinary communicator and a stakeholder management champion. 

*   Lead your team in developing and **executing 360° campaigns** that elevate Breuninger’s brand internationally and strengthen our positioning as the No. 1 omnichannel player in the DACH region and beyond. Manage and mentor two local Brand Marketing managers (Austria/Switzerland and Poland/CEE) as part of your broader team, ensuring seamless integration of market needs into our overarching communication strategy 
*   Deliver impactful 360° brand campaigns and collaborations across all touchpoints (digital & offline), ensuring **coherent, and premium brand experience** 
*   Evaluate **marketing activities and collaborations** on a KPI level, optimizing for the maximum return on brand and investment 
*   Build and nurture **strong relationships with cross-functional stakeholders** and partner brands 
*   Foster a culture of excellence and efficiency in your team, driving continuous process improvements and automation 
*   Guide international markets by **establishing structures and processes** that strengthen brand awareness in focus regions  
*   **Manage and develop partnerships** tailored to relevant market and consumer segments 
*   Integrate **AI applications** into planning and campaign development processes

**Qualifications:**

*   Bachelor’s or Master's degree in Marketing, Business or related field 
*   8+ years’ experience in (brand) marketing ideally in consumer-facing lifestyle or fashion environments 
*   Proven leadership experience and track record in people management  
*   **Deep understanding of 360° channel orchestration** including all digital and offline touchpoints 
*   Strong expertise in leveraging AI applications in campaign planning  
*   Excellent communication and presentation skills in both German and English 
*   Strong project management capabilities, paired with a solution-oriented, proactive mindset 
*   High emotional intelligence, able to navigate complex processes and diverse stakeholders with ease  
*   Hands-on, ambitious and passionate about luxury, lifestyle and fashion  
*   Affinity for complex proccesses and continous improvements across all involved parties, striving for best results 
*   **Fluency in German and English**
