<p><strong>Job Summary</strong></p>
<p>As the Team Lead for Platform Engineering at Unframe, you’ll play a critical role in shaping the foundation of our AI platform. You’ll lead a team of Full Stack Engineers focused on building reusable AI services, scalable APIs, a dynamic UI engine, and shared infrastructure that powers every solution we deliver. This is a hands-on leadership role - you’ll guide architecture and execution while mentoring engineers and ensuring technical excellence.</p>
<p><strong>Responsibilities</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Lead and mentor a team of Full Stack Engineers, fostering a collaborative, high-performance culture.</li>
<li>Drive the design and implementation of core AI building blocks, backend services, and frontend components.</li>
<li>Own platform-wide features such as user management, multi-tenant abstractions, and solution dashboards.</li>
<li>Collaborate cross-functionally with Product, AI, and Solutions teams to ensure the platform meets business and technical goals.</li>
<li>Define and enforce best practices for code quality, testing, CI/CD, and system architecture.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Requirements</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>6+ years of experience in software engineering, including 3+ years in a technical leadership or team lead role.</li>
<li>Background in AI/ML, LLMs, or prompt-based applications.</li>
<li>Proven ability to design scalable APIs, services, and reusable UI components.</li>
<li>Strong proficiency in modern frontend and backend frameworks (e.g. React, Node.js, Python, etc.).</li>
<li>Experience building platforms or working in complex, multi-solution product environments.</li>
<li>Strong communication and collaboration skills, with experience leading cross-functional initiatives.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Nice to Have</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Experience with design systems, multi-tenant architecture, or enterprise-grade platforms.</li>
<li>Familiarity with backend performance optimization, observability, and database design.</li>
</ul>
<p>If you’re a strong, independent builder who thrives on ownership, cares deeply about quality, and wants to have a real impact — we’d love to meet you.</p>
