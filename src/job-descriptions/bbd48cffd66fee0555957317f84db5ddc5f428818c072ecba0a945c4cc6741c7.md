**Job Overview**
The **Senior Director, Studio Creative Innovation** will lead the transformation of Macy's in-house photo and video studio into a cutting-edge, future-ready storytelling hub. This role reimagines a legacy content engine by integrating AI-enabled workflows, experiential storytelling, and high-impact brand visuals meet operational excellence.
More than a leadership position, this is a creative reinvention mandate. The Senior Director brings bold vision, cultural fluency, and the ability to translate creative energy into measurable business impact across e-commerce, social, editorial, and marketing channels.
**What You Will Do**
**Studio Reinvention & Vision**
*   Reimagine the creative studio as an innovation lab that blends speed, scale, and storytelling.
*   Define a bold, modern vision for how content is captured, created, edited, and distributed across all customer touchpoints.
*   Integrate emerging technologies - including AI, CGI, real-time content capture, and automation -  to streamline workflows and future-proof production capabilities.
**Creative Direction & Storytelling Leadership**
*   Lead end-to-end production for photography and video—from concept through post-production—across still life, on-figure, campaign, social, and experiential content.
*   Set and uphold a high creative standard to ensure all content is emotionally resonant, culturally relevant, and visually magnetic.
*   Collaborate with brand creative, marketing, and merchandising teams to translate seasonal narratives, trend stories, and key launches into compelling visual storytelling.
**Fashion Photography Excellence**
*   Define and elevate the creative vision for Fashion and Beauty photography, casting, and styling.
*   Ensure that all on-set production and final executions reflect a distinctive, high-impact aesthetic that positions Macy’s and its Private Brands as fashion leaders.
**Operational Innovation**
*   Redesign studio systems, processes, and workflows to enable a nimble, efficient, and scalable content production ecosystem.
*   Build flexible production models that balance in-house and external capabilities, with a focus on agility and cost optimization.
*   Oversee studio resourcing, budgeting, vendor relationships, and technology implementation with a startup mindset.
**Cross-Functional & Cultural Leadership**
*   Serve as a trusted creative partner to executives and stakeholders across brand, digital, creative, and merchandising teams.
*   Inspire, mentor, and mobilize a high-performing team spanning art direction, styling, production, editing, and post-production.
*   Foster a studio culture grounded in experimentation, inclusivity, and bold creativity.
**Entrepreneurial & Commercial Growth**
*   Unlock new business opportunities by exploring monetization strategies, brand partnerships, and co-branded productions.
*   Position the studio as a strategic, revenue-generating asset that drives not only marketing outcomes but cultural relevance for the Macy’s brand.
*   Foster an environment of acceptance and respect that strengthens relationships, and ensures authentic connections with colleagues, customers, and communities.
*   In addition to the essential duties mentioned above, other duties may be assigned.
**Skills You Will Need**
**Creative Transformation & Vision** – Proven ability to lead large-scale creative reinvention by transforming legacy studio systems into agile, tech-forward, innovation hubs.
**Omnichannel Storytelling & Content Strategy** – Deep expertise in developing emotionally resonant, culturally relevant content across e-commerce, social, editorial, and experiential channels.
**Fashion & Lifestyle Aesthetic Expertise** – Strong fashion and beauty sensibility, with a clear understanding of casting, styling, and photography direction that elevates brand identity.
**Emerging Technology Integration** – Familiarity with AI, CGI, real-time content capture, and automation tools to streamline production and future-proof workflows.
**Creative Direction & Execution** – Experienced in leading end-to-end photography and video production across campaign, still life, on-figure, social, and experiential content formats.
**Studio Operations & Workflow Optimization** – Skilled in redesigning systems and processes to drive speed, scale, and efficiency within a complex production environment.
**Budget & Vendor Management** – Capable of managing resources, budgets, and vendor partnerships with a startup mindset and focus on agility and cost optimization.
**Cross-Functional Leadership & Influence** – Trusted partner to senior leaders across brand, digital, creative, and merchandising with the ability to build alignment and drive shared outcomes.
**Team Leadership & Culture Building** – Inspires and mentors a high-performing creative team while fostering a studio culture centered on experimentation, inclusion, and excellence.
**Entrepreneurial Thinking & Commercial Acumen** – Strategic thinker who identifies new revenue opportunities through monetization, brand partnerships, and co-branded content.
**Creative-Commercial Balance** – Combines artistic vision with business impact, using brand storytelling as a driver of both cultural relevance and financial growth.
**Trendsetting & Innovation Mindset** – A culture shaper who doesn't just follow trends but sets them—connecting creative strategy with innovation and market leadership.
