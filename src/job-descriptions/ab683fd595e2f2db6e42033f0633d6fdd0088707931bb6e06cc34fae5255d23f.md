Our solution is a Digital Management Platform for Technical Processes in the Maritime Industry

This is a unique opportunity to join an exciting tech scale-up company where you will play a key role in shaping the future of our advanced data platform.

By fostering collaboration and streamlining processes, we aim to enhance stakeholder value by cutting costs and ensuring reliability in project planning and execution.
