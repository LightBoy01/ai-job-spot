The company emphasizes a strong 'Teamspirit' with flat hierarchies and quick decision-making, complemented by regular company events. New hires benefit from professional onboarding and ample opportunities for further education and training. The role offers a valuable blend of administrative precision and strategic involvement in HR process improvement within a supportive and forward-thinking environment.
