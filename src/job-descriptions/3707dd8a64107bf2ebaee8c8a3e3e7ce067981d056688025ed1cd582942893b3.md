UL Solutions is seeking an **AI Safety Scientist** to join our Center of Excellence in AI Safety. This role is pivotal in ensuring that AI products developed by our clients meet the highest standards of safety, reliability, and ethical compliance.

### Responsibilities

*   Work closely with customers, who develop AI products and applications, and evaluate and assess the safety of AI products by analyzing the performance and compliances to the requirements of AI safety principles such as reliability, robustness, risk management, transparency, explainability, trustworthiness, and bias management 
*   Work closely with customers, who develop AI products and applications, in assessing, evaluating, testing and certifying for the intended function and safety of the AI products to meet their safety requirements. 
*   Train junior members of the Center of Excellence (CoE) of AI Safety in evaluating testing and certifying AI products for safety, and in AI standards and regulations.
*   Certify AI products for safety compliance to AI safety standards and regulations by performing the evaluation, creating necessary documentation, communicating with the customer, and performing project management related tasks.
*   Maintains/improves technical knowledge about and compliance to AI standards (e.g., ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42, IEEE-SA) and regulations (e.g., EU GDPR, EU AI Act and U.S. AI Executive Order).
*   Contributes to the overall revenue performance of UL Solutions
*   Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams to ensure adherence of AI products to AI safety standards, regulations, and best practices.

### Qualifications

*   Bachelor’s, Master’s or Ph.D. degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or Engineering. 
*   5+ years of industrial experience in developing AI/ML models, applications, and systems.
*   Experience in industries related to Testing, Inspection, and Certification (TIC) and standard and regulatory bodies is a plus.
