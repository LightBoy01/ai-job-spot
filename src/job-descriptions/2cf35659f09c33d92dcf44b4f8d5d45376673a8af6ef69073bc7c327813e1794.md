We believe in flexibility for you to explore your passions while making an impact through meaningful work within our inclusive workforce. That’s what _**#LifeAtCummins**_ is all about

We are looking for a motivated placement engineer to join our **Engine Sub-System Team focusing on AI, Digital, and Zero Fuel Burn Testing**. During your placement, you will learn how a major global organization operates, gaining the tools and exposure you will need to launch your career in the industry and power your potential!

This year-long industrial placement offers an opportunity to gain hands-on engineering experience, working at the intersection of advanced digital technologies and sustainable internal combustion engine system development. The successful candidate will contribute to projects that enhance engineering efficiency through AI/Digital tools and accelerate mechanical validation of components using innovative rig testing methods to replicate engine running conditions without burning fuel in a engine test cell.

This role will give you an opportunity to work on different sub-systems of the engine and you will interact with a wide range of global and local cross functional groups and departments to co-ordinate project delivery.

This role is available to candidates who qualify for a placement year and will commence Summer of 2026.

### Responsibilities

In this role, you will make an impact in the following ways: 

*   Support AI & Digital Engineering Tool Development: Assist in designing, testing, and deploying digital and AI-oriented tools to optimise engine sub-system component design, investigate & testing and project/resource management.
*   Contribute to Zero Fuel Burn Testing Component Validation: Participate in the design, setup, execution, and monitoring of advanced component rig validation tests aimed at reducing the time required in an engine test cell & fuel burn.
*   Data Evaluation & Reporting: Collect, process, and investigate experimental and simulation data, preparing clear technical reports and visualisations to support engineering decision-making.

### Qualifications

To be successful in this role you will need the following: 

*   We are looking for a wide range of applicants studying towards an Engineering, Mathematics, Physics or other related STEM degree. Preference for Mechanical, Aerospace, Motorsport or other suitably related Engineering discipline, typically halfway through the program.
*   Demonstrated ability to work collaboratively in technical teams and communicate effectively in written and verbal formats.
*   Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with ability to interpret technical data.
*   Interest in internal combustion engines and their operation
*   Familiarity with using AI and digital tools to help complete engineering related tasks.
*   Prior exposure to engine testing, propulsion systems, or sustainable energy technologies through coursework, projects, or internships.
