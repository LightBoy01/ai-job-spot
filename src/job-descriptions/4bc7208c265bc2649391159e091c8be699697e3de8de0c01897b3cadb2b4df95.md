**About this role:**
Reporting to the Senior Marketing Project Manager in our In-House Agency ([wellocreative.com](https://wellocreative.com)), this role collaborates with key stakeholders to execute project plans and deliver creative assets across marketing channels.  This position offers a hybrid work schedule and is based in either **Charlotte, NC** or **Minneapolis, MN**.  The Wells Fargo job profile is **Marketing Production Consultant**.
****In this role, you will:****
*   Participate in the planning and management of project timelines for marketing campaigns across multiple platforms, with a focus on digital and specialized channels
*   Evaluate marketing project goals, monitor progress, and ensure delivery of key milestones
*   Collaborate with stakeholders to align on strategies and execution plans
*   Coordinate with internal teams and external partners to resolve scheduling conflicts and adjust timelines as needed
*   Assign tasks, manage deadlines, and ensure timely completion of all project phases
*   Negotiate timelines, troubleshoot issues, and implement necessary changes
*   Ensure all marketing campaigns comply with brand standards and legal requirements
*   Partner with vendors to support campaign execution and delivery
Depending on the volume of applications received, this job posting may be removed prior to the indicated close date.
****Required Qualifications:****
*   2+ years of Marketing Production experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education
****Desired Qualifications:****
*   Experience executing digital marketing projects and concurrent work streams
*   Experience executing multi-channel marketing campaigns across paid, owned and earned channels
*   Experience leveraging automation and AI tools to optimize workflows
*   Experience applying Agile methodologies within an agency environment
*   Experience collaborating with cross-functional teams and external vendors
*   Experience consulting and influencing senior leaders, partners, and peers
