We are seeking a talented Backend Engineer to help bring advanced AI/ML features to our travel and expense management platform. This position is perfect for a strong backend developer who’s excited to work with the latest AI/ML technologies in a fast-moving, cross-functional environment.  
About the Position  
As a Backend Engineer, you’ll design and build high-performance backend systems that enable large language models (LLMs), retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and conversational assistants in our consumer products. You will collaborate closely with experts in AI/LLM and product teams to translate concepts into robust, scalable solutions for our end users.

**What You’ll Do:**

*   Build and maintain scalable backend APIs and microservices supporting AI/LLM-powered features
*   Connect backend systems to AI/LLM services and platforms
*   Work with AI/LLM specialists to deliver production-ready, machine learning-driven functionality
*   Ensure all systems are reliable, high-performing, and secure
*   Partner with product and engineering teams to define requirements and deliver innovative features

**What We’re Looking For:**

*   5+ years of backend development experience
*   Proficiency in at least one backend programming language (such as Java)
*   Proven ability to develop and support production-grade APIs and distributed services
*   Strong understanding of data structures, algorithms, and distributed computing
*   Experience working with SQL and NoSQL database systems
*   History of shipping scalable, consumer-facing products
*   Hands-on experience with AI/ML platforms and libraries (OpenAI, Hugging Face, etc.)
*   Exposure to LLMs, RAG, or conversation-based AI/ML applications
*   Familiarity with vector databases and embedding-based solutions
*   Solid background with public cloud services (AWS, GCP, Azure)
*   Understanding of AI/ML concepts and deployment patterns
