<p><strong>The Role</strong></p>
<p>Nanonets is currently seeking an Account Executive (Enterprise – US) based in the Bay Area, CA. In this role, you will be responsible for acquiring and growing enterprise accounts across the US market. You will partner with senior decision-makers, drive large and complex deal cycles, and play a pivotal role in Nanonets’ next phase of growth in the US.</p>
<p><strong>Roles&nbsp;and&nbsp;Responsibilities&nbsp;</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Own&nbsp;the&nbsp;full&nbsp;sales&nbsp;cycle&nbsp;for&nbsp;enterprise&nbsp;accounts&nbsp;in&nbsp;the&nbsp;US&nbsp;—&nbsp;from&nbsp;prospecting&nbsp;to&nbsp;negotiation&nbsp;and&nbsp;closing.</li>
<li>Build&nbsp;and&nbsp;manage&nbsp;strong,&nbsp;trust-based&nbsp;relationships&nbsp;with&nbsp;senior&nbsp;executives&nbsp;and&nbsp;key&nbsp;stakeholders&nbsp;at&nbsp;Fortune&nbsp;1000&nbsp;companies.</li>
<li>Drive&nbsp;pipeline&nbsp;generation&nbsp;through&nbsp;outbound&nbsp;prospecting,&nbsp;events,&nbsp;and&nbsp;networking,&nbsp;in&nbsp;addition&nbsp;to&nbsp;managing&nbsp;inbound&nbsp;leads.</li>
<li>Understand&nbsp;customer&nbsp;challenges&nbsp;deeply&nbsp;and&nbsp;map&nbsp;Nanonets’&nbsp;solutions&nbsp;to&nbsp;their&nbsp;needs.</li>
<li>Collaborate&nbsp;cross-functionally&nbsp;with&nbsp;solutions&nbsp;engineering,&nbsp;product,&nbsp;and&nbsp;leadership&nbsp;teams&nbsp;to&nbsp;deliver&nbsp;value&nbsp;to&nbsp;enterprise&nbsp;customers.</li>
<li>Track&nbsp;and&nbsp;coordinate&nbsp;all&nbsp;account&nbsp;activities&nbsp;to&nbsp;ensure&nbsp;seamless&nbsp;execution&nbsp;and&nbsp;growth.</li>
<li>Consistently&nbsp;meet&nbsp;or&nbsp;exceed&nbsp;sales&nbsp;quotas&nbsp;in&nbsp;a&nbsp;fast-paced,&nbsp;high-growth&nbsp;environment.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Requirements&nbsp;and&nbsp;Skills&nbsp;</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>4+&nbsp;years&nbsp;of&nbsp;experience&nbsp;selling&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>enterprise&nbsp;accounts&nbsp;in&nbsp;the&nbsp;US</strong>&nbsp;(preferably&nbsp;SaaS/AI/automation&nbsp;solutions).</li>
<li>Proven&nbsp;track&nbsp;record&nbsp;of&nbsp;building&nbsp;and&nbsp;closing&nbsp;complex&nbsp;enterprise&nbsp;deals&nbsp;</li>
<li>Strong&nbsp;experience&nbsp;with&nbsp;outbound&nbsp;prospecting,&nbsp;pipeline&nbsp;building,&nbsp;and&nbsp;navigating&nbsp;long&nbsp;sales&nbsp;cycles.</li>
<li>Ability&nbsp;to&nbsp;engage&nbsp;with&nbsp;senior&nbsp;executives&nbsp;and&nbsp;clearly&nbsp;articulate&nbsp;Nanonets’&nbsp;value&nbsp;proposition.</li>
