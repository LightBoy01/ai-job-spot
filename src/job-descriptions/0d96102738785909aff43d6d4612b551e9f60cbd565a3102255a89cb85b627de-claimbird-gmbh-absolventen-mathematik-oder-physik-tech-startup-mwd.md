### Description
We are a tech startup specializing in automation for claims management. Our technologies deliver high-quality, real-time results in the detection and evaluation of claims. With the help of artificial intelligence, claims are automatically reviewed and evaluated. The benefit: time and cost savings – and more satisfied customers.

We offer diverse and challenging tasks, flat hierarchies with short decision-making paths and creative freedom, a motivated and innovative environment, a friendly and trusting work atmosphere, flexible working options (including a 4-day week or part-time upon request), and the latest hardware with free choice of OS and IDE at a modern workplace in the heart of Hanover.

Are you eager to build and implement new projects and be part of a young, aspiring company? Do you enjoy working independently in a dynamic team? Then you are the right person for us, and we look forward to your application! Should you not meet all the mentioned requirements, do not hesitate to apply anyway.

### Responsibilities
- Actively participate in the development of innovative, new products in the insurance and mobility sectors.
- Further develop our existing products using modern web technologies for various end devices.
- Accompany technologically and analytically challenging projects with renowned clients.
- Analyze customer requirements and identify new use cases.

### Qualifications
- Experience with Node.js, web frameworks like React, React Native, or Swift, and familiarity with testing environments (e.g., Mocha, Cypress, Playwright).
- Proficiency in TypeScript, Python, Rust, or SwiftUI.
- Ideally, experience with databases (No-SQL or SQL) as well as with RESTful APIs or GraphQL.
- Enjoy working in a team and familiarity with Agile methods and procedures.
- Completed degree in Mathematics, Physics, or a technical-scientific field.
