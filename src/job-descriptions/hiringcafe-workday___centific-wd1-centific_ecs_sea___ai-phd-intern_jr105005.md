**About Job**

*   As a PhD Intern, you would work in an exciting and fast-paced environment, collaborating closely with teams across the company, including Research. You will work as part of an organization that brings together talent in the areas of large language models, recommender systems, machine learning, deep learning, graph learning, information retrieval, software engineering, and responsible AI. We value and encourage diversity in the belief that it leads to both great workplaces and great products.

**Responsibilities:**

*   Conduct R&D in areas such as Deep Reinforcement Learning, Large Language Models (LLMs), Natural Language Processing (NLP), Information Retrieval, and Machine Learning.
*   Contribute to LLM fine-tuning, including Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) using Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO).
*   Apply post-training techniques to enhance model alignment, safety, and performance.
*   Collaborate with internal teams such as Research, Turing, and AI product groups to bring innovations to real-world applications.
*   Drive innovation through experimentation, collaboration, and technical excellence.
