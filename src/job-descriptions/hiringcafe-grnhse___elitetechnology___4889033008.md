Elite Technology is a leading, independent technology company that helps law firms drive profitability through improved operational effectiveness, increased efficiency, and greater visibility across their business. Our cutting-edge digital financial management and business operations solutions drive success for firms around the globe by improving finance and accounting operations, including billing, invoicing, payments, and financial reporting. By bringing together data, insights, and tools, we enable law firm leaders to make informed business decisions to build and manage their businesses more effectively and efficiently.

**Job Description:**

Our Senior Manager, Go-To-Market Content will play a key role in scaling how we show up in the market across every channel and funnel stage. This position is ideal for a high-output, systems-driven content leader who is also a hands-on creator. You’ll be responsible for architecting a content engine that transforms product messaging, customer research, and the product roadmap into channel-specific assets, leverages AI for scale and consistency, and drives measurable results against GTM and marketing goals.

**What You’ll Do:**

*   Build a high-leverage content engine that supports campaign, lifecycle, and brand goals without dependency on manual workflows.
*   Translate product messaging into scalable content across web, email, ads, events, and partner enablement assets.
*   Use AI tools and prompt systems to accelerate consistent, high-performing content creation.
*   Develop modular content frameworks adaptable by segment, buying stage, or channel.
*   Own development of organic social content (primarily LinkedIn) including thought leadership, company posts, and audience-building series.
*   Collaborate with executives and SMEs on ghostwritten content that amplifies reach and point of view.
*   Monitor and analyze performance to drive audience engagement and optimize messaging.
*   Extend the reach of top-performing content across channels and teams.
*   Design and execute content experiments (messaging variants, formats, hooks) to optimize performance.
*   Define, track, and report on metrics at the content level, including engagement, conversion, pipeline contribution, and retention.
*   Work with analytics to extract insights and iterate based on results.
*   Develop templates and playbooks that reduce friction and improve campaign execution speed.
*   Personally write and produce a wide range of content, including campaign assets, website copy, ads, scripts, and social posts.
*   Create thought leadership content shaped by market insight, not just internal input, with a focus on clarity and utility.
*   Maintain a consistent brand voice across all content formats and customer journey stages.
*   Showcase a portfolio or examples of content that drove measurable outcomes.
*   Perform other duties as assigned to support the overall goals and objectives of the department/company.

**What You’ll Need:**

*   8+ years of experience in B2B content or marketing roles, ideally in SaaS or complex solution categories.
*   Proven track record of creating high-performing content across formats and channels with measurable impact.
*   Demonstrated ability to develop thought leadership that builds audience and credibility (e.g., LinkedIn, blogs, earned media).
*   Demonstrated ability to write short-form ad and campaign copy that performs.
*   Deep experience with AI tools and agents for drafting, editing, and scaling assets.
*   Strong instincts for content performance across channels and scaling for reach and ROI.
*   Excellent editorial judgment, fast writing ability, and focus on clarity and impact.
*   Strong business acumen balanced with creative exploration.
*   High accountability and an owner’s mindset.
*   Must provide examples of written work and discuss their strategic purpose and results.
