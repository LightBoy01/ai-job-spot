### Description\nJoin Biomasse-Kraftwerk Fechenheim GmbH, a modern energy recovery plant dedicated to the energy transition, located in Frankfurt am Main. We process approximately 120,000 tons of woody biomass waste annually, providing reliable, cost-effective, and environmentally friendly energy. With significant facility expansions in planning, we are seeking skilled individuals, or those eager to become so, to help build a largely new team from the ground up. This is a unique opportunity to contribute to our future success and be part of a growing, forward-thinking organization.\n\nWe are looking for an **Anlagenfahrer / Kraftwerker (Plant Operator / Power Plant Worker) for Shift Service** to join us as soon as possible, playing a crucial role in the operation and optimization of our biomass power plant.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Monitor, operate, and control the power plant from the control room.\n- Analyze and eliminate malfunctions in the entire plant and control technology.\n- Be responsible for the maintenance of operating systems.\n- Be responsible for plant optimization.\n- Independently carry out plant disconnections.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed vocational training as a power plant operator, certified boiler attendant, industrial mechanic, electrician, mechatronics technician, or chemical technician.\n- Interest in further training as a power plant operator or operations engineer.\n- Engaged, reliable, and team-oriented work ethic, along with physical resilience.\n- Communicative personality.\n- MS-Office knowledge is an advantage.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a dedicated individual to join a critical player in Germany's energy transition. Biomasse-Kraftwerk Fechenheim GmbH offers a stable, future-proof, and permanent position with excellent benefits, including 30+ days of vacation, an attractive salary package (though not specified), and comprehensive opportunities for professional development, such as becoming a Power Plant Master. The emphasis on team building, continuous learning, and contributing to a sustainable future makes this a compelling role for someone looking for impact and long-term career growth in a supportive environment.
