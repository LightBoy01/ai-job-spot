<p><strong>The Opportunity:</strong></p>
<p>We are currently seeking a talented and innovative <strong>Controlling Intern</strong> to join our Organization's Finance team in the UAE, based in Dubai. The Intern will have the opportunity to work closely with experienced professionals who will guide you through real-world, hands-on experiences. This internship will provide valuable insights into various aspects of finance and contribute directly to the success of our company.</p>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>Role &amp; Responsibilities:</strong></p>
<p><strong> </strong></p>
<p><strong>Reporting and dashboard development</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Design, develop, and maintain interactive Power BI dashboards and reports to visualize key financial performance indicators (KPIs) for senior management.</li>
<li>Collaborate with finance and business professionals to define reporting requirements and translate business needs into functional, visually appealing dashboards.</li>
<li>Clean, and transform financial and operational datasets from various sources using tools such as Power Query to ensure data accuracy and consistency.</li>
<li>Use Data Analysis Expressions (DAX) to create advanced calculations and metrics within Power BI models.</li>
</ul>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>Process automation and improvement</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Evaluate existing finance business processes to identify opportunities for automation and efficiency gains.</li>
<li>Develop and implement automated workflows using Power Automate to streamline repetitive tasks, such as data collection, and report distribution.</li>
</ul>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>Data analysis and management</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Perform statistical and exploratory data analysis to identify trends, patterns, and anomalies in large financial datasets.</li>
<li>Support data cleansing initiatives to improve the overall quality of data used in reporting and analysis.</li>
<li>Assist in the management and maintenance of data models to support scalable business intelligence reporting.</li>
<li>Share emerging technologies in data analytics and business intelligence to propose new solutions</li>
</ul>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>Communication and collaboration</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Present findings and insights from data analysis and dashboards to both technical and non-technical audiences, including senior management.</li>
<li>Work closely with finance, and other business units to integrate data insights into business strategies and support strategic decision-making.</li>
<li>Communicate project progress, challenges, and deliverables to a supervisor, and participate actively in team meetings.</li>
<li>Document technical and functional details of all developed reports and automated processes.</li>
</ul>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>Requirements:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Bachelor’s or Master’s degree with major in IT, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence</li>
<li>Finance experience preferred</li>
<li>Strong technical skills in Power BI, Power Automate, Power Query</li>
<li>Knowledge in SAP and Programming skills would be a plus.</li>
<li>Advanced Excel skills</li>
<li>Strong analytical skills</li>
<li>Excellent communication and interpersonal skills. </li>
<li>Strong work ethic and a proactive attitude with a genuine willingness to learn.</li>
<li>Fluency in spoken and written English</li>
<li>Valid UAE residence visa holder</li>
</ul>
