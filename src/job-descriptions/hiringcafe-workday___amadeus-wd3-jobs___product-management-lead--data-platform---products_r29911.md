**Job Title**

Product Management Lead, Data Platform & Products

Job Title: Software Development, Associate Manager

Position type: Permanent  
Location: Bogotá, Hybrid  
Job ID: R29911

Job family: Product/Solution Management

Job Profile: Product Management Principal Specialist

**About the business area**

This is your opportunity to lead the creation of **data-driven and AI-powered products** that will transform how airlines harness insights, make decisions, and deliver exceptional travel experiences. You’ll play a leading role in building products that are **strategic to Navitaire’s future** and **game-changing for our customers**.

**Summary of the Role:**
========================

As a **Product Manager for Data & AI Products**, you will own a portfolio of data-centric offerings — ranging from APIs and data services to advanced analytics and AI-powered applications. You will define vision and strategy, guide product development, and deliver measurable value to airlines.

You’ll work at the intersection of product strategy, data science, and airline operations, collaborating closely with R&D, data engineering, customer success, and business leaders. This role is ideal for someone who thrives on solving complex customer problems with data and who wants to shape the next generation of travel technology.

In this role you'll:
====================

Strategy & Vision
-----------------

*   Define and own the **data & AI product roadmap,** aligned with Navitaire's goals and customer needs.
*   **Translate strategy into actionable plans,** with clear objectives, measurable outcomes, and timelines.
*   Drive **co-innovation with airlines,** transforming customer challenges into validated product concepts, MVPs, and scalable solutions.

Execution & Delivery
--------------------

*   Lead the **end-to-end product lifecycle,** from ideation to launch and iteration.
*   **Collaborate with engineering and data science teams** to ensure features are technically sound, scalable, and deliver customer impact.
*   **Act as Product Owner** where applicable, maximizing agile team output.

Customer & Market Leadership
----------------------------

*   Conduct **market research and competitive analysis** to identify opportunities and sharpen positioning.
*   Partner with Product Marketing to **craft compelling value propositions** and go-to-market strategies.
*   **Embed best practices** in usability, scalability, and data quality into product design.

Operational Excellence
----------------------

*   Monitor product usage and KPIs, using insights to **drive continuous improvement.**
*   **Support resource planning** and alignment with R&D capacity.
*   **Guide technology partnerships** and joint innovation opportunities.

About the ideal candidate:
==========================

Background & Experience
-----------------------

*   Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Data Science, Business, or related field.
*   **3+ years in product management** or leadership in data/AI product development, with a track record of delivering successful products.
*   **Strong knowledge** of modern data and AI ecosystems, including:
    *   SQL
    *   Python
    *   Polars/Pandas
    *   RESTful APIs
    *   Cloud Platforms (Azure, GCP, AWS)
    *   Data Warehousing & Data Lakes (Snowflake, BigQuery, Delta Lake)
    *   Data Processing & Pipelines (Spark, debt, Airflow)
    *   Event Streaming (Kafka, Azure Event Hubs)
    *   NLP
    *   Foundational Models
    *   Prompt Engineering
    *   RAG
    *   MCP
    *   Applied AI/ML Use Cases

Skills & Mindset
----------------

*   **Excellent storytelling and communication skills** to influence diverse stakeholders.
*   **Data-driven decision maker,** skilled in using KPIs, telemetry, and customer insights.
*   Familiarity with **Agile,** Lean, and SAFe practices.
*   Passion for travel technology; **airline industry experience is a plus.**
*   Ability to travel ~10%.
