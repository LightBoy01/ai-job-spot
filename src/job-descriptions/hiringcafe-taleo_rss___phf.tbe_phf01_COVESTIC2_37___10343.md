**Role Summary**

We are seeking a Principal Consultant with deep expertise in enterprise technology strategy, digital transformation, and data-driven business innovation. This role is ideal for a seasoned professional with a track record of delivering strategic impact across complex client environments — particularly within Fortune 500 organizations and large-scale transformation initiatives.  
As a trusted advisor to client executives, you will lead high-value consulting engagements in areas such as AI/ML, cloud modernization, advanced analytics, and digital experience platforms. You’ll shape technology roadmaps, architect solutions, and align cross-functional teams to deliver measurable business outcomes.

**Responsibilities & Tasks**

Client Strategy & Advisory Leadership  
• Partner with C-level stakeholders to understand business goals and define transformation strategies.  
• Lead strategic engagements across the full consulting lifecycle — from assessment to roadmap to implementation oversight.  
• Translate business objectives into actionable architectures leveraging data, cloud, and intelligent automation.  
Solution Strategy & Pre-Sales Support  
• Design and present enterprise-grade solutions that address complex business challenges.  
• Collaborate with sales, product, and delivery teams to develop proposals, pricing models, and client-facing materials.  
• Lead workshops and strategic planning sessions to shape long-term technology partnerships.  
Engagement Oversight & Delivery Alignment  
• Provide strategic oversight to ongoing client programs, ensuring execution aligns with the intended outcomes.  
• Serve as the senior consulting point of contact for solution direction and client relationship continuity.  
• Guide internal teams through knowledge transfer, solution hand-off, and engagement wrap-up.  
Thought Leadership & Practice Development  
• Contribute to consulting methodologies, reusable frameworks, and knowledge-sharing initiatives.  
• Mentor junior consultants and provide domain leadership across cross-functional teams.  
• Monitor market trends and emerging technologies to keep offerings relevant and competitive.

**Experience, Skills and Qualifications Required**

Key Qualifications:  
• 15+ years of experience in technology consulting, enterprise architecture, or strategic business development.  
• Proven experience leading consulting engagements for large enterprise clients across verticals like Telecom, Media, Financial Services, and Retail.  
• Strong understanding of cloud platforms (e.g., Oracle, AWS, Azure), AI/ML, data analytics, and enterprise modernization.  
• Ability to lead cross-functional solution teams and drive consultative selling in complex environments.  
• Exceptional communication skills and executive presence.  
  
Preferred Background:  
• Previous roles in major consulting firms or global system integrators (e.g., HCLTech, Tech Mahindra, Cognizant, Tata Consulting Services, Accenture, Deloitte).  
• Experience developing go-to-market strategies and digital product offerings.  
• Familiarity with enterprise data platforms, SaaS models, and customer experience technologies.  
• Background in high-value content and consumer analytics solutions.
