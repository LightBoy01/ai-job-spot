### Description
At American Express, our mission is to elevate the customer experience daily by delivering unparalleled products and services. With the right backing, people and businesses have the power to progress in incredible ways. When you join Team Amex, you become part of a global and diverse community with an unwavering commitment to back our customers, communities, and each other.

We are looking for a Manager in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML), specializing in Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Generative AI. This role is pivotal in advancing our data science capabilities to bolster enterprise-wide initiatives. As a Manager for NLP and Generative AI, you will be instrumental in developing and implementing cutting-edge solutions that address significant business challenges. Working with cross-functional teams, your expertise will directly impact our strategic goals by enhancing product offerings, streamlining operations, and driving innovation in customer interactions.

### Responsibilities
*   Lead the design and development of NLP and Generative AI projects, ensuring alignment with business objectives and scalability across the organization.
*   Conduct applied research in NLP to identify and deploy advanced techniques that enhance our data science capabilities, focusing on delivering practical business solutions.
*   Foster a culture of innovation by staying abreast of the latest trends in AI and NLP, and actively sharing knowledge to contribute to the team's collective growth.
*   Manage project timelines, resources, and deliverables using agile methodologies, ensuring efficient progress from conception to deployment.
*   Collaborate with the Technology team to integrate NLP solutions into our existing systems and platforms, enhancing functionality and user engagement.

### Qualifications
*   Ph.D. degree in a quantitative discipline (e.g., Computer Science, Engineering, Statistics, Mathematics).
*   More than 3 years of experience in machine learning, NLP, and Generative AI, with a demonstrated ability to manage projects from inception to completion.
*   Proven expertise in applying deep learning and NLP techniques to solve complex problems, with a portfolio of projects that have delivered tangible business outcomes.
*   Solid understanding of Large Language Models and practical experience with Generative AI.
*   Solid understanding of agile project management principles, with experience leading teams and managing projects in a fast-paced environment.
*   Excellent communication and leadership skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with both technical and non-technical teams.
*   Strong problem-solving abilities, with a creative and analytical approach to tackling challenges and driving results.

### Author's Take
**1. What is the broader strategic impact this role is expected to have on American Express beyond the immediate project deliverables?**

"That's a great question because it gets to the heart of why this role is so critical right now. Historically, we've had pockets of excellence in AI, but this position is about being a force multiplier. You're not just building a model for a single use case; you're helping build the foundation for how Amex leverages conversational and generative AI across the entire enterprise. Think about it: the work you lead could fundamentally reshape our customer service bots, create hyper-personalized marketing content at scale, or even help our risk teams identify emerging trends from unstructured data faster than ever before. This role is about moving from reactive problem-solving to proactively defining the future of our customer and colleague experiences. Your success will be measured not just by the models you ship, but by how you elevate the AI maturity of the entire organization."

**2. The description mentions collaboration with Technology, Marketing, and Risk. Can you describe what that collaboration looks like day-to-day and how the team navigates differing priorities?**

"Absolutely. Collaboration here isn't just a buzzword on a job description; it's our operating model. We work in cross-functional pods where you, as an AI manager, would be at the table from day one with product owners, engineers, and business stakeholders. Your day might start with a stand-up with the tech team to unblock a data pipeline issue, move to a strategy session with Marketing to brainstorm how GenAI can improve a new campaign, and end with a demo of a new prototype for the Servicing team. The key is that we don't operate in silos. You're not just handed a set of requirements to build. You're a strategic partner, a translator between the art of the possible and the reality of the business need. Navigating priorities is a constant conversation, but because we're all focused on the same enterprise-level outcomes, it's a collaborative process of alignment, not a tug-of-war. Your ability to communicate complex ideas clearly and build relationships is just as important as your technical chops."
