### Description\nKickdown is Germany's leading auction platform for classic cars and collector vehicles, offering a full-service approach. We provide comprehensive support to sellers and maximum transparency and security to buyers. While our supply side is thriving, we are now focused on significantly scaling our buyer acquisition, revenue, and sell-through rate.\n\nFor this mission, we are seeking a highly driven individual to develop and operationally implement a holistic marketing strategy across all relevant channels, with a clear performance focus. This is a unique \"green field\" opportunity in a small, ambitious team with a true passion for special vehicles, offering high responsibility and creative freedom.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop a holistic marketing and growth strategy across all relevant channels (digital and selected offline channels).\n- Take full responsibility for our digital performance channels with a buyer focus, including Meta / Instagram Ads and Google Ads (Search, potentially YouTube/Display).\n- Audit and optimize the existing setup, planning and implementing a scalable test and growth roadmap.\n- Build and further develop tracking, attribution, and reporting (especially revenue, sell rate, and buyer KPIs).\n- Build and utilize CRM/newsletter flows (e.g., with Klaviyo or similar) for buyer activation and reactivation).\n- Conceive and integrate additional channels into the overall strategy, such as print cooperations, magazines, partner mailings, and events, trade fairs, and community formats within the classic car environment.\n- Collaborate closely with C-level executives.\n- Manage and challenge external partners/agencies.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 5–8+ years of experience in marketing with a strong focus on performance / growth.\n- Proven experience in developing and implementing cross-channel marketing strategies.\n- Ideally, a background in marketplaces / platforms and/or startups / scale-ups.\n- Highly proficient in Meta Ads, Google Ads, tracking (e.g., GA4, Tag Manager), and at least one CRM/newsletter tool (e.g., Klaviyo).\n- Experience with offline or supplementary channels (e.g., print, events, trade fairs) is a plus.\n- Strongly KPI- and revenue-oriented, with a clear eye for cost/benefit and efficiency.\n- Possess a \"doer mentality\": you implement things independently, don't wait for instructions, and don't need a large team behind you.\n- Fluent in German, proficient in English; affinity for cars/classic cars is a plus but not a requirement.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a senior performance marketer to make a profound impact in a growing, niche market. You'll be joining a small, passionate team where your expertise will directly shape the company's growth trajectory. The \"green field\" aspect means immense autonomy to innovate and implement your vision from scratch, working closely with leadership. If you thrive on high responsibility, direct influence on revenue, and a clear set of goals in a dynamic startup environment, this role offers an unparalleled chance to truly own and drive a critical part of the business.
