### Description
This Düsseldorf-based recruitment consultancy, with a successful history, specializes in placing IT and ERP specialists. Our mission is to connect companies with talent that truly makes a difference. We are seeking an engaged **Junior ERP Recruiting Consultant (m/w/d)** to join our team. This role is ideal for someone with **2-3 years of experience in 180° Recruiting** who is eager to transition into **360° Recruiting**. Ideally, you will have prior recruiting experience with **ERP systems** such as Microsoft NAV/Business Central, SAP, ABAS, SAGE, or similar solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Gradually build your role in **360° Recruiting**, from client support to successful placements.
- Independently manage and develop **candidate relationships** within the ERP sector.
- Identify and approach specialists through various recruiting channels (Active Sourcing, Talent Pools, networks, etc.).
- Conduct interviews, advise, and guide candidates through the entire recruitment process.
- Continuously **monitor the market** for Microsoft NAV/BC, SAP, ABAS, SAGE, and other ERP solutions.
- Support **needs assessment, client communication, and process coordination**.
- Progressively take over **client acquisition**, contract negotiations, and steer the entire placement process.

### Qualifications
- **2-3 years of experience in 180° Recruiting** within the IT/ERP environment.
- Knowledge or initial specialization in recruiting for **ERP systems** such as Microsoft NAV/Business Central, SAP, ABAS, SAGE, or similar solutions.
- Strong communication and consulting skills.
- Proactive work style and enjoyment of networking.
- Goal-oriented, skilled in negotiation, and self-motivated.
- Very good German language skills (C1).
