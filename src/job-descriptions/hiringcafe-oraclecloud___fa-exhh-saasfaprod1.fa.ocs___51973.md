**What you’ll be doing:**

*   Build & Optimize Pipelines – Assist in developing ETL/ELT workflows using Azure Data Factory, Python, SQL, and PySpark.
*   Data Transformation – Implement business logic and data modeling within Databricks and Delta Lake.
*   Workflow Orchestration – Work with tools such as Airflow or Tidal to schedule, monitor, and troubleshoot jobs.
*   SQL Development – Write, debug, and optimize queries for reporting, validation, and transformation tasks.
*   Automation & Validation – Contribute to Python/PySpark scripts for ETL, data validation, and automation.
*   AI/ML Data Prep – Prepare datasets for ML models (feature extraction, cleansing, transformation).
*   Model Integration – Support experiments by integrating ML model outputs into pipelines and dashboards.
*   Documentation & Quality – Participate in validation, reconciliation, and documentation to ensure accuracy and consistency.

**What’s needed - Basic Qualifications:**

*   Pursuing a master’s degree, enrolled as a full-time student carrying a minimum of 12 college credits
*   Strong foundation in SQL and Python programming.
*   Good understanding of data engineering concepts
*   Understanding and Interest in AI/ML and applying data to solve real business problems
*   Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills
*   Team-oriented, eager to learn, and adaptable

*   Enjoy working in teams and being highly communicative
