### Description\nThis 18-month \"Young Talents Launchpad\" program is designed for recent Bachelor's or Master's graduates seeking orientation and practical experience in medical technology. You will gain valuable insights and hands-on experience by rotating through three stations across two distinct companies: an innovative startup (medical magnesium) and a family-owned global player (KLS Martin). The program offers individual mentoring, extensive networking opportunities, and attractive remuneration.\n\nThe program is structured into the following parts:\n*   **Station 1: Product Development & Project Management at medical magnesium (Aachen)**: Dive into the world of bioabsorbable implants, working on innovative solutions for orthopedics and trauma surgery.\n*   **Station 2: Technical Sales at KLS Martin (Tuttlingen)**: Engage with customers and surgical procedures, understand the market, and represent products at specialist congresses.\n*   **Station 3: Research & Development at KLS Martin Biocenter (Mühlheim a.d. Donau/Tuttlingen)**: Gain hands-on experience in preclinical investigations and the manufacturing of Class III medical devices.\n\n### Responsibilities\nWithin the framework of these stations, you will be entrusted with diverse tasks, providing insight into many aspects of a medical product's lifecycle. Throughout the 18 months, you will consistently face new challenges, including:\n*   Contributing to development and research projects for bioabsorbable implants, among others.\n*   Supporting the market authorization of Class III medical devices.\n*   Analyzing customer feedback and identifying new market potentials.\n*   Accompanying surgeries and customer appointments in technical sales.\n*   Preparing product presentations and participating in specialist congresses.\n*   Gaining hands-on experience in preclinical investigations and manufacturing of medical devices.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed Bachelor's or Master's degree (Engineering, Natural Sciences, or comparable field).\n*   Strong interest in medical technology and innovation projects.\n*   Openness to different areas of responsibility.\n*   Willingness to be on-site at the respective locations for each stage.\n*   Strong communication skills and team spirit.\n*   Good German and English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis \"Young Talents Launchpad\" is an exceptional opportunity for ambitious graduates eager to kickstart their career in MedTech. The unique structure of rotating between an innovative startup and a global player provides an unparalleled breadth of experience in product development, sales, and R&D. With individual mentoring, a robust professional network, and exposure to cutting-edge medical devices, this 18-month program is more than just a job – it's a comprehensive foundation that truly opens doors and accelerates professional growth in the medical technology industry.
