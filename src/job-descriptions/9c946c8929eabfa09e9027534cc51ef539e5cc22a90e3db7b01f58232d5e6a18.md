****About the role****

We are looking for an experienced ****Senior Full Stack Software Engineer**** to join our talented team and contribute to building cutting-edge software solutions.

  

****What you'll do****

As a Senior Full Stack Software Engineer at Mozn, you'll be:

*   Leading the design and implementation of high-quality software solutions across both front-end and back-end layers
*   Architecting scalable and robust systems while ensuring optimal performance and maintainability
*   Developing and maintaining complex web applications using Python or GO, React, and TypeScript, while applying best practices in modern software development
*   Designing and implementing RESTful APIs, microservices, and other backend services in a way that ensures scalability, security, and performance
*   Working closely with UX/UI designers to ensure seamless integration between front-end and back-end components, delivering a highly functional and responsive user experience
*   Collaborating with product managers, stakeholders, and cross-functional teams to understand requirements and translate them into effective technical solutions
*   Conducting thorough code reviews to maintain code quality, share knowledge, and mentor junior team members
*   Maintaining comprehensive documentation of the code, deployment process, encountered issues, and their resolutions, promoting clear understanding across the team
*   Troubleshooting, debugging, and optimizing existing applications, ensuring smooth and seamless user experiences
*   Taking ownership of the deployment process, monitor live systems, and troubleshoot production issues when needed
*   Mentoring and guiding junior engineers, fostering an environment of knowledge sharing and professional development
*   Participating in strategic planning, defining technical priorities, and identifying opportunities for process improvement within the engineering team
*   Cultivating a culture of continuous improvement by encouraging collaboration, innovation, and adherence to quality standards within the team

****Qualifications****

*   Bachelor's degree in computer science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent work experience
*   8+ years of professional experience as a Full Stack Software Engineer or similar role, with proven expertise in both front-end and back-end technologies
*   Experience working with algorithms, data structures, complexity analysis, and object-oriented software design.
*   Strong proficiency in Python or GO and experience building robust, scalable systems
*   Solid experience with front-end frameworks, particularly React and TypeScript, and an understanding of responsive design principles
*   Strong knowledge of relational databases (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL).
*   Strong knowledge of RESTful API design
*   Strong knowledge of AI Native/Agentic tech stacks
*   Proficient with modern software development tools and practices, including version control systems (Git), build systems, and testing frameworks
*   Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders
*   Experience mentoring junior engineers and fostering a collaborative team culture
*   Ability to manage time effectively, prioritize tasks, and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment
*   Enthusiasm for tackling complex problems and delivering high-impact software solutions.

### Preferred Additional Skills

*   Familiarity with event-driven architecture and messaging systems (e.g., Kafka, RabbitMQ)
*   Knowledge of containerization (e.g., Docker) and orchestration tools like Kubernetes
*   Experience with observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, or ELK Stack
*   Understanding of security best practices and experience securing web applications
*   Experience with building efficient, scalable back-end services
*   Familiarity with cloud platforms (eg. AWS, Azure, GCP, OCI)
*   Knowledge of microservice architecture
