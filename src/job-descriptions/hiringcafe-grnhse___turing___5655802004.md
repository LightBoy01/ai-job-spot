**About Turing**

Based in San Francisco, California, Turing is the world’s leading research accelerator for frontier AI labs and a trusted partner for global enterprises looking to deploy advanced AI systems. Turing accelerates frontier research with high-quality data, specialized talent, and training pipelines that advance thinking, reasoning, coding, multimodality, and STEM. For enterprises, Turing builds proprietary intelligence systems that integrate AI into mission-critical workflows, unlock transformative outcomes, and drive lasting competitive advantage.

Recognized by Forbes, The Information, and Fast Company among the world’s top innovators, Turing’s leadership team includes AI technologists from Meta, Google, Microsoft, Apple, Amazon, McKinsey, Bain, Stanford, Caltech, and MIT. Learn more at [www.turing.com](http://www.turing.com/)

### **About the Role**

We are hiring a General Manager (GM) with a founder’s mindset and operator’s discipline to lead strategic accounts across frontier AI labs. This role carries end-to-end P&L accountability — you will act as the CEO of your accounts, driving financial outcomes, deepening technical partnerships, and ensuring delivery excellence.

The ideal candidate brings entrepreneurial ownership, technical fluency, and investment-level commercial instincts. You know how to build in ambiguity, create structure where none exists, and translate cutting-edge research needs into business growth.

### **Responsibilities**

*   Own full P&L accountability for assigned frontier AI lab accounts
*   Expand and deepen existing partnerships, structuring new engagements that align with customer priorities and Turing’s financial goals
*   Shape, scope, and launch multi-million-dollar programs across RLHF, evaluation, scaling, data pipelines, and agentic workflows
*   Lead executive-level commercial discussions with research leadership and technical sponsors
*   Translate ambiguous or early-stage research objectives into executable programs with clear scope, milestones, and ROI
*   Oversee health and profitability of all active programs, ensuring responsiveness to shifting technical requirements
*   Partner with internal teams to align delivery capabilities with evolving customer needs, without direct ownership of delivery execution
*   Capture customer and technical signals to inform Turing’s service design, roadmap, and AGI infrastructure strategy
*   Act as trusted technical-commercial partner for researchers, infra engineers, and executives inside customer labs  
      
    

### **Requirements**

*   Founder/operator background with direct experience owning budgets, scaling products, or exiting startups
*   Demonstrated ability to expand and grow high-value accounts in complex, research-driven environments
*   Strong technical fluency in generative AI, foundation models, and adjacent workflows (CoT, RAG, alignment, evaluation, safety)
*   Comfort engaging both researchers and C-level stakeholders, flexing between technical depth and executive clarity
*   Track record of translating zero-to-one opportunities into scalable, repeatable programs inside existing customer bases
*   Highly organized and resilient, able to manage concurrent workstreams and account-level P&L responsibilities
*   Commercial instincts that balance growth, profitability, and long-term strategic alignment
