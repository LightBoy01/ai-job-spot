If you have a proven track record in project management, possess a practical mindset, and maintain clarity even in stressful situations, you're the experienced professional we're looking for. We pride ourselves on a dynamic \"start-up flair\" and a strong commitment to employee well-being, offering flexible working hours, mobile work options, and a comprehensive benefits package including clients.\\n- Drive the draft and approval planning processes for wind farm projects.\\n- Conduct thorough technical assessments of projects, ensuring compliance with legal regulations and all all project-related technical inquiries.\\n- Deliver compelling presentations to municipal councils and at public citizen meetings.\\n- Process and develop informative materials for clients.\\n- Efficiently manage technical documents and comprehensive project documentation.\\n\\n### Qualifications\\n- Completed degree in Architecture, Geography, Renewable Energies, Civil Engineering, or a
