**What you’ll need to succeed as Senior Scientist, Data Science at XPO**

Minimum qualifications:

*   Bachelor’s degree or equivalent related work or military experience
*   2 years of experience in data science, operations research, or data analysis with a focus on AI-driven analytics, statistical modeling, machine learning, forecasting, or optimization
*   Experience in at least one programming language such as Python, R, SQL, SAS, Spark, Java, etc., with practical applications in AI or ML projects
*   Familiarity with AI techniques and machine learning algorithms, including supervised and unsupervised learning
*   Strong communication skills with a knack for translating complex AI concepts into clear business insights

Preferred qualifications:

*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Engineering, Economics, or any related quantitative discipline
*   A Master’s or PhD in Computer Science, AI, Data Science, or a related quantitative field

*   years of hands-on experience in developing and deploying optimization solutions
*   5+ years’ experience in pricing optimization, demand forecasting, utilizing statistical modeling, machine learning, and reinforcement learning techniques.
*   Experience with causal inference and measurement methods for analyzing the impact of pricing algorithms on revenue
*   Experience with reinforcement learning for pricing optimization, decision-making, and consumer behavior modeling

*   Proven track record of delivering AI-enabled insights that improved operations, financial performance, or customer experience
*   Strong foundation in statistics including hypothesis testing, anomaly detection, and statistical inference, as applied in AI model validation
*   Hands-on experience building and deploying AI models, including supervised, unsupervised, and deep learning methods

What you’ll do on a typical day:

*   Leverage Machine Learning, AI, statistical and/or optimization methods to solve various problems in Pricing, Sales, and Finance in order to optimize revenue for the company
*   Work with a team of data scientists and engineers to design, test and implement models into in-house software solutions
*   You will be a thought leader for the practice of Data Science and Analytics across the organization
*   Explore and research cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence techniques and statistical methods, to identify opportunities to drive process improvements through analytics and automation
*   Conduct and present ad-hoc analyses to senior leaders within the business and technology organizations
*   Communicate with technical and business-oriented audiences
*   Serve as a subject matter expert in applying AI techniques to logistics and transportation challenges
*   Leverage advanced data science methods, including machine learning, deep learning, and optimization, to enhance business performance
*   Perform analytics ranging from predictive to prescriptive and cognitive, aligning with AI maturity levels based on project needs
*   Combine technical skills in AI and data science with strong business acumen to deliver real-world impact at XPO
