At Dapo Talent Partners, we believe great careers are built on great matches. We partner with forward-thinking companies to design and deliver talent strategies that align with business goals, culture, and long-term success.

We are partnering with a hidden champion in the **Aviation & Air Traffic Control** sector. They have a 60+ year history (roots in legendary aerospace firms) and a massive global footprint. They build the systems that ensure millions of passengers take off and land safely every day.

We are looking for a **Linux-focused IT Engineer** to join their System Engineering team. You aren’t just closing tickets here; you are building and commissioning High Availability (HA) clusters that _cannot_ fail.

Tasks
-----

• **Build the Foundation:** You’ll install and configure Linux-based clusters (RedHat) for High Availability services.

• **Network Engineering:** You’ll handle the configuration of network components (Cisco/HP) and serial connections.

• **Go Where the Action Is:** This role involves approx. 20% worldwide travel. You will go on-site to customers (airports/control centers) to commission the systems you helped build.

• **Knowledge Transfer:** You’ll train client admins on how to handle the systems you deploy.

Requirements
------------

**The Tech Stack & Environment**

• **OS**: Linux (Deep expertise required, ideally RedHat) & Windows Server (2019+).

• **Hardware/Net**: Cisco, HP, Active Directory.

• **Methodology:** You’ll work in a Scrum team of ~4 direct colleagues, collaborating with Software Engineering and Integration teams.

**Who you are**

• **The Linux Pro:** You have 3–5 years of hands-on experience. You are comfortable in a shell environment and understand HA architecture.

• **Bilingual**: You speak **German (C1)** and English (C1) fluently. This is non-negotiable due to the safety-critical nature of the documentation and communication.

• **The Road Warrior:** You are excited by the idea of traveling internationally (20%) to see your systems go live.

• **Education:** You have a technical education (CS degree or equivalent professional experience).

Benefits
--------

• **Salary**: €50,000 – €70,000 gross p.a. (Fixed salary).

• **Contract**: Permanent employment (Unbefristet).

• **Flexibility**: Hybrid setup (mobile working up to 60% / 2–3 days home office) + Flexitime.

• **Perks**: 30 days vacation, JobRad (bike leasing), Wellpass, and corporate benefits.

• **Location**: Based in the beautiful Lake Constance (Friedrichshafen) region.
