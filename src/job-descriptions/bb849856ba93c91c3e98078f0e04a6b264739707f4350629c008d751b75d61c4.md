**Senior Corporate Recruiter**

We have an opportunity for a **Senior** **Corporate Recruiter** to join our Talent Acquisition team **in** **Mexico City**, reporting to the Manager, Talent Acquisition. In this pivotal role, you will be the first member of our People & Places team on the ground at our new Center of Excellence. As such, you will be expected to wear multiple hats and support various departments in establishing their local presence. 

You will lead and manage the end-to-end recruitment process, partnering closely with hiring managers to understand and anticipate their evolving talent needs. Your focus will be on delivering top-tier candidates who align with the organization’s goals and culture. This role also involves close collaboration with senior leaders to ensure strategic hiring decisions that drive business performance. You will help shape the hiring agenda to support broader organizational objectives, while maintaining strong, transparent communication with stakeholders and employees across the business. 

**Be a Contributor — What You’ll Do**  

*   **Design and execute strategic recruiting initiatives** to support hiring in a newly established location for the company 

*   **Manage the full-cycle recruitment process** across multiple business units and levels, ensuring alignment with staffing goals and timelines 

*   **Develop and implement employment marketing strategies** to attract passive candidates and build a strong talent pipeline 

*   **Partner with hiring managers and interviewers** to ensure job requirements are clearly defined and candidates are evaluated against appropriate criteria 

*   **Collaborate closely with Hiring Managers and HR Business Partners** to optimize the effectiveness and consistency of the recruiting process 

*   **Maintain regular communication** with hiring managers and candidates to ensure timely progress and transparency throughout the recruitment lifecycle 

*   **Engage in cross-functional staffing activities and projects**, contributing to broader organizational talent initiatives 

*   **Establish and manage search strategies**, including vendor selection and performance metrics, to ensure high-quality and cost-effective hiring outcomes 

*   **On-site presence required:** Work from the office three days per week to support the growth of this new site and actively collaborate with internal stakeholders across departments 

**Be Prepared — What You Bring**  

*   **Exceptional interpersonal and communication skills**, with a strong track record of contributing to team-based projects 

*   **10+ years of experience** managing the full-cycle recruitment process across diverse roles and business units 

*   **Thorough understanding of the end-to-end recruitment lifecycle**, including compliance with local employment laws and regulations 

*   **Proficient in using a variety of hiring tools**, including Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), Candidate Relationship Management (CRM) platforms, and job boards 

*   **Experienced in talent sourcing and resume mapping** from a wide range of channels to build robust candidate pipelines 

*   **Strong vendor management capabilities**, including oversight of search firms and job board partnerships 

*   **Advanced computer proficiency**, particularly in Microsoft Office Suite and modern AI enabled tools  

*   **Preferred experience in general HR functions**, contributing to broader people initiatives beyond recruitment 

*   **Bachelor’s and/or Master’s degree** in Business Administration or Human Resources strongly preferred 

**Be Successful — Your Path**  

**First 90 Days:** Immerse yourself in our culture, connect with mentors (Blox Buddies), and map the systems and meet with key stakeholders that rely on your work. Discuss and create short/long term goals  

**Six Months:**   

*   Develop strong relationships and partner with the hiring managers, business leaders, and HR  

*   Partner with managers to forecast and develop plans for key staffing initiatives  

*   Track and report key metrics designed to measure and predict staffing activity     

**One Year:**   

*   Develop key relationships with Mexico City employees and cross-functional global teams  

*   Actively participate in all staffing related activities and engage in cross-functional projects  

*   Plan strategic recruitment initiatives to hire diverse talent in Mexico City  

*   Be part of multiple projects within People & Places
