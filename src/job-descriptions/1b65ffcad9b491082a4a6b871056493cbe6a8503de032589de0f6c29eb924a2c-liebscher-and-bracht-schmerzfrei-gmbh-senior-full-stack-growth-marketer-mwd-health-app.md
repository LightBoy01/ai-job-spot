### Description\nLiebscher & Bracht stands for smart solutions in health, pain relief, and regeneration. As a leading D2C Health Brand in Germany, with millions of followers and a strong community, they are known for high-quality, effective, and unique products. Their team is driven by a pioneering spirit, ownership, and a commitment to continuous growth.\n\nAs a **Full-Stack Growth Marketer (m/f/d)** for their Health App, which already boasts over 1 million downloads, you will take full responsibility for its growth. You will strategize and manage the entire funnel – from strategy, media buying, and performance marketing to conversion optimization, retention, and overall app experience.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop and implement holistic growth strategies across all channels (Paid, CRM, Influencer, E-Mail, SEO, Content).\n- Plan and execute campaigns, product stories, and landing pages that effectively blend brand messaging with performance goals.\n- Manage budgets, KPIs, and external partners such as agencies and freelancers.\n- Build data-driven funnels, automations, and customer journeys to optimize user engagement and conversion.\n- Collaborate closely with Product, Brand, Design, and Tech teams to ensure maximum brand impact and a seamless user experience.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A minimum of 3 years of experience in online or performance marketing, ideally within a D2C or e-commerce environment.\n- Strong hands-on expertise in Paid Media Buying, funnel optimization, copywriting, and conversion strategies.\n- A solid understanding of offer structures, upsells, downsells, and data-driven revenue growth techniques.\n- Deep know-how in CRM (Customer Relationship Management), Attribution, and Analytics.\n- Proven experience in cross-functional leadership and managing budgets responsibly.\n- A genuine passion for health, regeneration, performance, and brand building.\n- Possess a true Growth Mindset and a strong desire to take on significant responsibility.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at Liebscher & Bracht offers an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned Full-Stack Growth Marketer to drive the entire growth strategy for a successful health app within a prominent D2C brand. The company fosters a high-ownership environment with agile structures and flat hierarchies, encouraging a pioneering spirit. With a generous professional development budget, flexible working arrangements including extensive vacation and \"Workation\" days, and a strong team culture, this position is ideal for an autonomous, data-driven professional passionate about health and continuous growth.
