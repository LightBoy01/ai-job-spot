**Life at UiPath**
==================

The people at UiPath believe in the transformative power of automation to change how the world works. We’re committed to creating category-leading enterprise software that unleashes that power.

To make that happen, we need people who are curious, self-propelled, generous, and genuine. People who love being part of a fast-moving, fast-thinking growth company. And people who care—about each other, about UiPath, and about our larger purpose.

Could that be you?

As we continue expanding our platform and impact, we’ve welcomed Peak into the UiPath family - a company whose vision and values align closely with our own.

Peak brings deep expertise in decision intelligence and AI-powered optimization, helping global businesses like Nike, Heidelberg Materials, and Boohoo drive real-world outcomes across inventory, pricing, and operations. Our AI platform is already delivering measurable commercial value, and together, we’re scaling that impact even further.

**Your mission**
================

As a **Senior Product Manager**, you’ll be at the forefront of shaping how businesses unlock real value through applied AI. You’ll join a high-performing, cross-functional team building next-generation products in a rapidly evolving space - one where innovation, learning, and impact are daily drivers.

You’ll be responsible for identifying and prioritizing the most valuable product opportunities, driving discovery and delivery, and collaborating across Peak and UiPath teams to bring transformative solutions to life. From influencing go-to-market strategy to driving customer adoption, you’ll help shape a product vision that excites teams and delivers commercial impact.

**What you'll do at UiPath**
============================

*   Discover the most valuable opportunities, and collaborate with our customers and various functions across the business including Data Science, GTM, Sales, and Marketing, to develop and launch powerful products and capabilities to market
    
*   Lead a cross-functional squad comprised of machine learning engineers, data scientists and product designers, and demonstrate agile best practices
    
*   Build and maintain relationships with key stakeholders, influencing decision making throughout the product life cycle
    
*   Prioritise and own the product roadmap & backlogs your products
    
*   Define and drive the product vision and strategy for your product area, working with leadership and your team to set objectives and key results that drive Peak to valuable outcomes
    
*   Lead ideation, testing & learning through rapid prototyping and user testing
    
*   Accelerate value add from Peak products and drive adoption
    
*   Research and understand market trends, competitors and new technologies to identify new capabilities with data science research teams.
    

**What you'll bring to the team**
=================================

*   Prior experience working as a Product Manager is essential, preferably from a product-led B2B SaaS company
    
*   Experience delivering features to users built on GenAI technologies
    
*   Subject matter expertise in the value chains of physical product companies would be highly advantageous, particularly inventory and pricing optimisation
    
*   Have worked with or in close proximity to technical teams working to solve complex problems and, essentially, be able to simplify and communicate to non-technical teams
    
*   Proven track record of using data and insights to influence decision making and drive and measure incremental value
    
*   Experience with collaborating and managing exec level stakeholders
    

Maybe you don’t tick all the boxes above—but still think you’d be great for the job? Go ahead, apply anyway. Please. Because we know that experience comes in all shapes and sizes—and passion can’t be learned.
