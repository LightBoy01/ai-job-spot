**Job Description:**

**Get to Know the Team**

You'll join the **Commercial (Non-Merchant) Enterprise & Channel team** at Grab Indonesia. This team builds partnerships with some of the country's most strategic clients, including those from the government, State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs), telecommunications companies (Telcos), and Conglomerates.

Our mission? To unlock growth opportunities for Grab while creating long-term value for Indonesia's digital economy. We are a collaborative, fast moving, and impact driven team that works across departments to deliver both revenue and ecosystem credibility.

**Get to Know the Role**

As **Head of Commercial, Government & Key Industries**, you will lead our commercial strategy and client partnerships across Government, SOEs, Telcos, and Conglomerates. You will manage a high-performing team of Leads and Key Account Managers, ensuring disciplined execution of quarterly commercial goals while shaping long-term strategic partnerships. You will report to the Director of B2B Commercial, and the work arrangement is full on-site. You will:

*   Influence at the **highest levels** (C-levels, ministries, and boardrooms).
*   Promote **multi-product adoption** (GrabCar Premium/HVR, Grab for Business, GrabFood, GrabMart, and ESG/EV programs).
*   Be a **key voice in shaping Grab's role in Indonesia's digital and regulatory ecosystem**.
*   Build and grow a talented team in one of Grab's most critical verticals.

**The Critical Tasks You Will Perform**

1.  **Define and execute the commercial strategy** for Government, SOEs, Oil & Telco, and Conglomerates, aligned with Grab's Non-MEX Enterprise goals.
2.  **Lead senior-level negotiations and partnerships**, ensuring win-win outcomes that balance Grab's growth with client needs.
3.  **Coach and inspire a team** of Leads and KAMs, building capabilities in consultative partnership and complex deal structuring.
4.  **Use AI tools** for research, data analysis, and productivity — improving pipeline accuracy, forecasting, and client insights.
5.  **Shape Grab's external positioning** by representing us at industry forums, client meetings, and public sector engagements.

**Qualifications:**

**What Essential Skills You Will Need**

1.  **10+ years of commercial leadership experience**, with a track record in Government, SOEs, Telcos, or Conglomerates.
2.  Experience advising and **influencing C-suite executives and government ministers**.
3.  **Commercial acumen and negotiation skills** to structure multi-million-dollar, multi-stakeholder partnerships.
4.  **Leadership capability** to manage and grow high-achieving teams
5.  **Strategic thinking** to balance short-term revenue with long-term ecosystem value.
6.  **Hands-on familiarity with AI tools** for data analysis, client insights, and productivity enhancement.
7.  Adaptability to work in a **fast moving and matrix environment**.
