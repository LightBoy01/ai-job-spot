🚀 **Become our new DevOps Engineer (m/f/d)**

As a DevOps Engineer, you will play a key role in designing, building, and operating secure, scalable, and automated cloud infrastructures on Azure. You will work end-to-end across projects, collaborating with developers, architects, and data teams to create efficient delivery pipelines and highly reliable environments.

#### **Your responsibilities**

*   Design, build, and maintain secure and scalable cloud infrastructure on Microsoft Azure
    
*   Implement and manage Infrastructure as Code using tools such as **Terraform** or **Bicep**
    
*   Build and operate CI/CD pipelines (preferably **Azure DevOps**) for automated build, test, and deployment processes
    
*   Set up and manage **IAM**, access control, and security policies following best practices
    
*   Ensure cloud **governance**, compliance, and alignment with organizational guidelines
    
*   Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting solutions for infrastructure and applications
    
*   Collaborate with Engineering, QA, and Security teams to ensure smooth deployments and reliable operations
    
*   Contribute to documentation, standardization, and continuous improvement of infrastructure processes
    

  
  

#### **Your profile**

*   Several years of experience as a DevOps Engineer, Cloud Engineer, or similar role
    
*   Strong hands-on experience with **Microsoft Azure** (mandatory)
    
*   Advanced experience with IaC tools such as **Terraform** and/or **Bicep**
    
*   Proven experience building and maintaining **CI/CD pipelines** (preferably Azure DevOps)
    
*   Strong understanding of **IAM**, access management, and security/governance principles
    
*   Experience with **monitoring, logging, and observability** solutions
    
*   A high level of ownership, reliability, and quality awareness
    
*   Good communication skills and ability to work effectively with cross-functional teams
    

#### **Why join us**
