Our client, an international manufacturing company, is entering the implementation phase of its Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations rollout.

Following the analysis and preparation phase in late 2025, the project will intensify in early 2026 with a strong focus on **Finance** and **Warehouse Management (WMS)** setup and rollout .

To strengthen the project team, we are looking for a **Nearshore Senior D365FO SCM/WMS Consultant**. The consultant will be responsible for driving the **Logistics & Warehouse Management rollout** throughout 2026 and supporting Finance setup activities.

The project targets a **Go-Live in July 2026** and requires a consultant with fluent German skills and willingness to travel onsite to Germany regularly.

**Location:** Hybrid (Remote + Travel to Germany)  
**Start:** January 2026  
**Contract:** Freelance  
**Duration:** 3–6 months (extension possible)  
**Languages:** German (fluent is a must have), English (very good), French a plus

Tasks
-----

*   Lead the **D365FO WMS / Supply Chain Management (SCM)** implementation within the rollout stream
*   Configure, test, and operationalize the **Warehouse Management System (WMS)** end-to-end
*   Support the Finance team with initial configuration and integration tasks
*   Work closely with the Project Lead (4 days/week) and coordinate with local and nearshore resources
*   Participate in hybrid workshops, functional deep dives, and onsite training sessions
*   Contribute to project planning, milestone tracking, and progress reporting
*   Prepare for and support the **Go-Live in July 2026**, including cutover and hypercare
*   Ensure proper documentation, knowledge transfer, and process handover

Requirements
------------

*   **5+ years of hands-on experience** with Microsoft **Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations**
*   Strong functional expertise in **SCM, Logistics, Warehouse Management (WMS/EWM)**
*   Proven experience in **D365FO WMS configuration and rollout**
*   Good understanding of Finance-related processes and Logistics–Finance integration
*   Practical experience in German-speaking implementations!!
*   **Fluent German (mandatory)!!**
*   Very good English; French is an advantage
*   Willingness to travel to Germany on a regular basis (10 days per month, travel expenses will be paid according to company travel policy)
