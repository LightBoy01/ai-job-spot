### Description\nGreenfield Technology, founded in 2018, specializes in developing innovative software solutions for enterprises, public authorities, and institutions, leveraging the advanced Low-Code platform from Pega. We are dedicated to driving digital transformation in Germany, enabling modern and efficient work processes. We collaborate closely with our clients throughout the entire software lifecycle, from initial concept to successful implementation. We foster a culture of trust, mutual respect, and collaboration, where individual growth and team success are paramount, all while working with future-oriented technologies. This role offers the unique opportunity to contribute significantly to the digitalization of Germany.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop and maintain applications for large enterprises and the public sector.\n- Utilize the innovative Pega Low-Code platform.\n- Support digital transformation by analyzing, conceptualizing, and implementing digitalization solutions for new or existing business and communication processes.\n- Identify, review, and evaluate automation potentials.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A degree in Business Informatics, Computer Science, Mathematics, or a similar field.\n- A strong preference for Low-Code development over traditional programming, coupled with a desire for \"customer-facing\" software development.\n- Initial practical experience with object-oriented programming languages such as Java.\n- Understanding of frontend technologies like HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and ReactJS.\n- Preferably, initial exposure to BPMN and webservice technologies such as REST or SOAP.\n- An understanding of business processes.\n- Strong self-initiative, high motivation, and enjoyment of collaborating in agile project teams.\n- Fluent German and English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for a passionate individual looking to kickstart their career in digital transformation, specifically within the impactful realm of Low-Code development using Pega. Greenfield Technology offers a vibrant, trust-based culture with a strong emphasis on continuous learning through their Pega Academy and certification support. The role promises significant impact, contributing directly to Germany's digitalization efforts. Beyond the meaningful work, the company stands out with its commitment to employee well-being, offering flexible remote work options (including workation), 30 days of annual leave, private health insurance, and engaging regular team events. If you're a recent graduate with a knack for problem-solving, a desire to work with cutting-edge low-code platforms, and thrive in a collaborative, agile environment, this is a prime position to make a real difference from day one.
