  Our Audit Intelligence & Steering team is looking for YOU!   

**We are Uniper**

At Uniper, we are pro-actively transforming the world of energy whilst at the same time ensuring security of energy supply. As an internationally operating company, we work in very diverse teams with the greatest possible working time flexibility for our employees. Our corporate culture is characterized by equal opportunities, mutual appreciation, and respect. With us, you will be able to develop new business models, work on technological solutions for a modern, sustainable, and future-oriented energy supply as well as pro-actively help to shape changes. Interested? Then we will look forward to meeting you!

**Your responsibilities**

**Being part of an international high profile team with the unique opportunity to gain insights into Uniper and into the operations of Internal Audit.Work in a diverse team with colleagues having different professional backgrounds. In Internal Audit, we are committed to people development.Your primary project would be to assist the Internal Audit team in continuing its Data journey by supporting the implementation of our Technology Strategy and incorporating the same into audit approach and procedures.** 

*   Interact with Internal Audit Leaders and understand their specific needs
*   Network with Data and Technology Experts across Uniper to share knowledge
*   Support the implementation of the Data Analytics solutions
*   As part of the Audit Intelligence and Steering team you will additionally support: 
    
    *   Digitalisation of the Internal Audit function
    
    *   Operation and administration of the Audit Management System
    
    *   Implementation of sustainable and pragmatic technology solutions to improve Audit operations
    
    *   Support in development of relevant department guidelines, framework documents and user manuals for digital solutions
    
    *   Development of training material and upskilling of colleagues on roll-out of new digital solutions
    
    *   Support in quarterly reporting process, including support in translating reports into German
    
    *   Contribute to the development of Copilot agents to support Internal Audit’s digitalization and automation efforts

 

**Your profile**

  

*   Ongoing university studies in energy management, (business) informatics or data analytics, (industrial) engineering, (business) mathematics, business administration, economics or similar
*   Ability to apply and advise on current analytical and quantitative tools and modelling techniques to gain business insights, solve complex business problems and improve the data basis for decision making
*   Profound MS Office knowledge (PowerPoint and Excel) and experience with dashboard tools like Tableau, Power Bi, Qlik or similar
*   Affinity to data science and the analytical ecosystem. Programming knowledge: Python, R and / or SQL as well as first experiences with Power BI, understanding of Databases, APIs and Power Automationwould be advantageous
*   Team player (m/f/d) with a high degree of self-motivation, assertiveness and persuasiveness
*   Critical judgement and entrepreneurial thinking
*   Motivation to develop in the Artificial Intelligence (AI) domain is highly valued
*   Affinity with modern tools such as Microsoft Copilot or ChatGPT would be highly desirable
*   Business fluent in English and German, other language skills a plus
