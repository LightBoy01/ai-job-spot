Front brings core support channels into a modern, intuitive workspace where teams can collaborate on requests, automate manual processes, and delight customers across their entire lifecycle. Front’s flexible workflows, AI features, and customer intelligence provide the efficiency and insights to keep entire organizations customer-first, every day. AI is transforming customer support and, with over 12 years of experience building customer operations solutions, Front is in a unique position to shape this transformation. The AI innovation team at Front leverages AI and ML methodologies to build functionality across the entire breadth of the product. Our workflow automations, agent assistance and operational insights have only scratched the surface, and user engagement is already extremely promising. This role would have the opportunity to get in at the ground floor and significantly influence the direction of the product as we scale.

**What will you be doing?**

*   Collaborate closely with product managers, designers, and fellow engineers to build and enhance product features.
    
*   Develop in a JavaScript environment, with a strong focus on TypeScript, Node.js, and React.
    
*   Contribute to the deployment of AI-powered features for our users (e.g., AI Tagging, AI Answers, etc.) [See examples](https://front.com/blog/customer-service-agent-assistance-ai)
    
*   Take ownership of features end-to-end, often with minimal cross-functional involvement.
    
*   Nothing is off-limits on the backend, including, but not limited to, infrastructure, search, and persistence layers.
    

**What skills and experience do you need?**

Must have:

*   Proven industry experience building fast, reliable, and real-time web applications.
    
*   Product-minded: Passionate about delivering exceptional user experiences.
    
*   Ability to think from first principles, evaluate trade-offs between technical solutions, and make pragmatic technical decisions.
    
*   High learning agility - you’re excited when you encounter a problem you haven’t seen before.
    
*   Strong communication skills that illustrate a clear, structured thought process.
    

Should have:

*   Familiarity with the full-stack architectural patterns of large-scale web applications.
    
*   Broad experience working with various persistence layers (SQL/NoSQL, search, graph, cache).
    
*   Knowledgeable about modern web technologies and familiarity with IaC. Strong appetite for keeping up with the latest technologies and methodologies.
    
*   Keen interest in AI and ML technologies.
    

Nice to have:

*   Experience with AI technologies (e.g., LLMs, Vector Databases, etc.)
    
*   Familiarity with managing complex data models at scale.
