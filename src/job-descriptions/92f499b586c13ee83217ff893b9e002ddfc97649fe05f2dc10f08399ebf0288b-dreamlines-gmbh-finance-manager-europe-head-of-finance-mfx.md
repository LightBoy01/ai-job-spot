### Description\nWe are seeking an experienced Finance Manager Europe (or Head of Finance) to lead our European finance operations, playing a key role in shaping our financial strategy and processes. This is a pivotal leadership position based in Germany, offering a flexible hybrid/remote working model with occasional travel to Hamburg.\n\nIn this role, you will oversee comprehensive accounting, consolidation, and reporting activities, while also driving continuous improvement and automation across our international finance function. You will be responsible for managing and developing the European finance team, collaborating with global stakeholders, and ensuring strict compliance with local and international accounting standards. This opportunity offers significant impact, allowing you to drive digital transformation within a growing global business in a collaborative and international environment with short decision paths.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Full responsibility for monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements in accordance with HGB (in-house) and IFRS (managed via external partner).\n- Lead, mentor, and develop the European finance team.\n- Manage the ongoing partnership and performance of the outsourced Shared Service Center (SSC) in Asia.\n- Oversee the audit process, collaborating closely with auditors and tax advisors to ensure full compliance with statutory and international tax requirements.\n- Drive the implementation and integration of LucaNet for consolidation and reporting.\n- Ensure accurate group consolidation across all international entities (Germany, UK, Australia, and the Philippines).\n- Partner cross-functionally with local and global teams to align financial processes and improve efficiency.\n- Lead process optimization and automation initiatives within the finance function.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Degree in Finance, Accounting, or a related field; CPA/ACCA or comparable qualification is a plus.\n- Proven experience in group accounting and consolidation, ideally in an international environment.\n- Strong month-end closing and financial reporting skills.\n- Practical experience in audit management and preparation of statutory financial statements (HGB; IFRS exposure is an advantage).\n- Sound knowledge of SAP (FI, SD, MM).\n- Experience in finance process improvement, digitalization, and automation.\n- Strong tax knowledge and experience working with tax advisors.\n- Excellent communication skills in English and German (business fluent).\n- Willingness to travel to Hamburg approximately once per month (travel expenses covered).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a pivotal Senior Finance Manager role for a seasoned professional eager to shape international finance strategy and drive digital transformation. It offers significant autonomy, a clear leadership mandate, and the chance to make a tangible impact across a growing global business. The flexible hybrid model, coupled with generous travel benefits, employee discounts, and a collaborative international culture, makes this an exceptionally attractive opportunity for those looking to combine strategic influence with work-life balance and a dynamic career path in a forward-thinking organization.
