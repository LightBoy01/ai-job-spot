**DESCRIPTION:**

The Senior Machine Learning Engineer (EU) is expected to lead and contribute to all parts of the software development lifecycle, including architecture, design, development, documentation, testing, and operations. As a member of a rapidly evolving engineering organization, the Senior Machine Learning Engineer will need to be able to cross functions easily and collaborate with other highly effective individuals, including members of the C-suite.

This is a challenging engineering role that offers a great opportunity to grow with Aetion and make a difference by ​​helping expand the use of Aetion's products.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:** 

*   Use a variety of machine learning methods to solve healthcare-related problems using cloud-based technologies.
*   Transform innovative AI prototypes into production-ready solutions through rigorous engineering practices.
*   Work cross functionally with engineers to integrate AI/ML features into our applications.
*   Work alongside science and product to identify and solve high-value machine learning use-cases.
*   Maintain and monitor existing AI/ML functionality.
*   Research topics in machine learning and AI to stay up-to-date on emerging trends in the field.

**QUALIFICATIONS:** 

**Required**

*   A BSc/MSc/PhD degree (or equivalent) in mathematics, data science, computer science, statistics, electrical engineering, bioinformatics, biomedical engineering or a related STEM field.
*   6+ years of relevant work experience.
*   Expert in Python programming for full data science lifecycle (ETL, data exploration, cleaning/pre-processing, feature engineering, model training, evaluation, deployment, monitoring). Tech stack: SQL, Scikit-learn, Numpy, Pandas, Pytorch, Transformers. 
*   Experience with LLMs: prompt engineering, RAG, fine-tuning. 
*   Highly analytical, problem-solving mindset with a demonstrated ability to work in cross-functional teams and independently solving ill-defined problems on data from heterogeneous sources.
*   Interested in partnering with product teams to solve complex business problems with AI/ML.
*   Strong communication skills, in fluent English, with ability to explain complex concepts to a variety of audiences.
*   Experience with version control software (e.g., Git)
*   Experience working with containerized compute (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes)
*   Experience working with cloud platforms (e.g., AWS)

**Preferred**

*   Experience in the healthcare industry or experience working with large, complex medical / healthcare data (e.g., insurance claims, EHR, CDISC).
*   Experience with AWS Bedrock and/or other LLM platforms.
*   Experience with MLOps.
*   Experience with software engineering best practices.
