We collaborate with partners like Airbnb, Porsche Digital, Atlassian, and Rewe Digital to shape the future of software development.\n\nOur Digitale Leute School offers practical advanced training for the tech industry, with a highlight being our AI Software Engineering Bootcamp. This program is an ideal opportunity for software developers to take their next career step. Our trainers, hailing from leading tech companies, impart cutting-edge AI know-how combined with product management methodologies specifically tailored for developers.\n\nAfter the bootcamp, we actively support your job search through our robust hiring network. For job seekers holding an education voucher, participation is entirely free.\n\nThe next course is starting soon, and there are still open spots! This program includes:\n-   3 months of remote/online training to become an AI Software Engineer.\n-   Support in finding a company for a job entry during or after the bootcamp.\n-   The training is compatible with current employment.\n-   You will receive a mentor to guide you through the program and simulate work within product teams.\n-   Upon completion, you can join product teams as a certified AI Software Engineer.\n\nYou will receive a certificate, all course materials, and access to a broad professional network. We recommend a German language proficiency of B2 for this course.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a participant in the AI Software Engineering Bootcamp, you will delve into comprehensive topics designed to transform your role and capabilities:\n\n-   **From Developer to Co-Creator**: As AI takes over repetitive coding tasks, your strategic influence in product development will grow. You will learn to analyze business models and user needs, formulate hypotheses, and translate your insights into roadmaps and product-related backlogs.\n-   **Architecture & Responsibility in the AI Age**: You will develop software that is not only functional but also structured and long-term sustainable, based on proven architectural principles and modern patterns. This includes identifying risks in AI deployment, monitoring AI decisions, and keeping an eye on costs, API limits, and system performance.\n-   **AI-Driven Development & Deployment**: You will utilize modern AI tools to develop stable prototypes or complete MVPs, automate testing, and deploy securely. This involves monitoring AI decisions – from quality and adherence to your requirements to observability in live operation.\n-   **Orchestrate AI as a Team Member**: You will strategically deploy AI within a team, develop agents for various tasks, and orchestrate their collaboration. This includes automating workflows and integrating corporate data via established frameworks like LangChain or MCP for creating truly valuable AI features.\n\n### Qualifications\nWe are looking for ambitious software developers who meet the following criteria:\n\n-   You have a passion for technology and enjoy developing products that delight users.\n-   You bring solid knowledge in modern software development.\n-   You are curious, ambitious, and actively seek ways to develop yourself professionally.\n-   You enjoy agile methodologies (Scrum, Kanban) and collaborative teamwork.\n-   You can empathize well with users and stakeholders and understand how to think about technical solutions from a product perspective.\n-   You are ready to immerse yourself in complex, rapidly evolving topics such as AI development and agentic workflows.\n-   You are continuously evolving: changes in tech stacks and new tools motivate you rather than unsettle you.\n-   You enjoy thinking outside the box and taking on both technical and product strategic responsibility.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis AI Software Engineering Bootcamp from Digitale Leute offers a compelling pathway for experienced developers to elevate their careers and specialize in the rapidly evolving field of AI. The program's strong focus on product management, strategic influence, and practical application, combined with expert trainers from leading tech companies, provides a robust foundation. The significant emphasis on mentorship, simulated team environments, and active job search support through a powerful hiring network makes this an exceptional opportunity for career advancement.
