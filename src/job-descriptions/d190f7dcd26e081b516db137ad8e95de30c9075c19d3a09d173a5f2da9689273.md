**Job Description:**

TheLoops, an IFS company, is the first enterprise-grade AI Agent platform built for SaaS, CX, and mission-critical industries. Our AI Agents are secure, always-learning digital coworkers that work 24/7 to drive measurable business outcomes. As the company grows, it is looking for mission-driven individuals to help deliver the future of AI-powered operations.

At TheLoops, they:

*   **Lead with Curiosity:** They ask questions, learn, and communicate with respect for each other.
*   **Learn by Making:** They iterate fast to deliver tangible solutions with humility.
*   **Listen to the Loop:** They use data and feedback to map their path to success and measure it with autonomy and empathy.

We are looking for a hands-on, technically-driven leader to build and guide a brand-new team in Sri Lanka comprising of TAMs, FDEs and other Engineer Roles. This is a unique opportunity to blend your technical expertise with leadership, acting as a critical bridge between our US teams and a budding local talent pool. You will be instrumental in fostering a collaborative, start-up-like culture within a larger enterprise, ensuring our local team is equipped to deliver high-impact AI solutions directly to our customers. You will be a mentor, a coach, and a technical expert all in one.

**What You'll Do**

*   **Team Leadership & Development:**
    *   Recruit, interview, and onboard local technical talent, building a high-performing TAM, FDE & other roles from the ground up.
    *   Provide continuous coaching, mentorship, and technical guidance to team members, helping them grow their skills and navigate complex challenges.
    *   Foster a culture of ownership, curiosity, and rapid iteration within the local team.
*   **Technical Guidance & Execution:**
    *   Maintain a deep understanding of IFS & TheLoops platform and complex technical language to effectively guide your team.
    *   Ensure the technical quality and on-time delivery of customer projects.
    *   Act as a technical escalation point, helping your team troubleshoot difficult deployments and optimize agent performance.
*   **Knowledge Transfer & Collaboration:**
    *   Design and execute a comprehensive knowledge transfer program to seamlessly integrate the local team with the remote US team.
    *   Serve as a key liaison, facilitating clear communication and collaboration between our US-based Product, Engineering, and Customer Success teams and your local team.
    *   Regularly share local team insights and customer feedback to influence the product roadmap.

**Qualifications:**

*   A minimum of 5 years of experience in a software engineering, solutions engineering, or ML deployment role, with technical leadership experience.
*   Knowledge of Python, AI/ML concepts and frameworks (e.g., LLMs, RAG systems, vector databases).
*   Exceptional communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to translate complex technical concepts for diverse audiences.
*   A passion for coaching and mentoring junior engineers and a desire to help others grow.
*   Proven ability to operate autonomously and adapt quickly in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
*   A strong understanding of the full software development lifecycle and experience with enterprise systems, APIs, and complex integrations.
