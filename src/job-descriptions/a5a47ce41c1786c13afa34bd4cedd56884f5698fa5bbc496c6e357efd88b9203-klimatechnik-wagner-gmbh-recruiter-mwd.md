We develop tailor-made solutions for private and commercial customers that combine maximum comfort and energy efficiency. With commitment and expertise, we create individual concepts that **WORK** – true to our motto: Together we **CREATE IMPACT**! Become part of our team and shape the future of climate technology with us!\n\nWe are looking for a dedicated Recruiting Specialist to take responsibility for our entire talent acquisition process. You'll join a motivated team that values collaboration, continuous learning, and a supportive environment. As a stable company with 75 years of history, we are committed to employee development and well-being, offering individual training opportunities and support in personal emergencies.
