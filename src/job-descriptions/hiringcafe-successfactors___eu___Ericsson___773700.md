Career Opportunities: Solution Architect (773700)
=================================================

Requisition ID **773700** - Posted  - Country/Area (1) - state/province (1)

Grow with us
------------

**About this opportunity:**

We are currently seeking dedicated and results-oriented individuals to join our team as a Solution Architect. The Solution Architect will assume a critical role in analyzing, creating, and implementing technically feasible solutions that align with our customer specifications and business needs. This role provides an exciting opportunity to contribute to one of the world's leading technology companies with a commitment to innovation and customer satisfaction.

The Solution Architect will basically work as Service CSR (Customer Solution Respobsible) as one of key member in core3(Process Role). The team consists of 12 members, 7 of whom are working as Service CSR. Each CSR will have the individual opportunity to secure contract from customers. We expect the candidate to bring new or fresh ideas to help drive growth in the telecom industry.

Join our Team
-------------

**What you will do:**

\- Analyzing customer's business environment, technology, and non-functional requirements.

\- Developing and implementing potential solutions in collaboration with the Core 3 team.

\- Crafting interactive technical presentations to illustrate proposed solutions.

\- Conducting presentations directly to the customer, exhibiting expertise and communication skills.

\- Investigating current customer networking structures and identifying areas of improvement.

\- Providing technical support and vital inputs to the delivery team for seamless implementation.

The skills you bring:
---------------------

**Must have**

*   AI, Cloud Native, IT, Data analysis, RAN, Cloud RAN, XaaS
*   Third Party & Subcontractor Handling
*   Consultative Capability
*   Continuous Improvement
*   Strong Customer Interaction Skills
*   Experience with Diverse Customers/Markets
*   Market Insight
*   Leadership Skills
*   Project Management Skills
*   Proficiency in English (business level) and Japanese (native level)
*   Growth mindset
*   Product Knowledge.
*   Hardware, Architecture, Virtual Environment, Technology, Protocol and Interface
*   Automation
*   Solution Delivery
*   Solution Architect
*   Financial Acumen
*   Customer Solution Design
*   Business Acumen
*   Service Delivery Models and Strategy
*   3-5 years of experience in telecom as a Project Manager, Project Leader, or in Sales

Nice to have
------------

*   Project Implementation
*   Process and Tools Management
*   Knowledge sharing and learning

**Can be developed on the job:**

*   Documentation
*   Ericsson Portfolio
*   Customer Business Process

What kind of challenges should this person naturally enjoy?
-----------------------------------------------------------

To take full ownership of assigned opportunities from pre-sales through to delivery, identify areas for improvement, and propose enhanced services in subsequent opportunities, thereby contributing to the company's vision and core values in a professional manner.

What measurable outcomes would indicate success, and what impact would this person have?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Within 3 months, they will master Ericsson and customer processes, negotiate service scopes, create plans, perform costing and risk analysis, and address challenges. By 6 months, they’ll gather insights to inform service portfolio improvements globally. After 12 months, they will serve as a role model, fostering growth among peers and new team members.

What might a persons career path look like in this role?
--------------------------------------------------------

A new joiner could start by building experience in this position, then move into other roles like Sales, Project/Program Manager, Line Manager, or cross-functional leadership. They may also work with global projects to expand skills and networks. Attractive points include working with cutting-edge telecom technology, a diverse and international workplace, continuous learning programs, and flexible work arrangements - all supporting long-term career growth and satisfaction.

**Why join Ericsson?**

At Ericsson, you´ll have an outstanding opportunity. The chance to use your skills and imagination to push the boundaries of what´s possible. To build solutions never seen before to some of the world’s toughest problems. You´ll be challenged, but you won’t be alone. You´ll be joining a team of diverse innovators, all driven to go beyond the status quo to craft what comes next.
