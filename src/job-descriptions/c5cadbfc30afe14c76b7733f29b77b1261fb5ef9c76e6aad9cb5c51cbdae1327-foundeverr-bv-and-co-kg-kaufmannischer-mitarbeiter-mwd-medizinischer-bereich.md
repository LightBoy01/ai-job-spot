### Description\nAre you looking for a back-office role where you can primarily provide written support to customers of a medical device manufacturer? Then we are looking for you as a **Commercial Employee in the Medical Field (m/f/d)** for Foundever!\n\nThis position starts on **January 5, 2026**, located in **Lutherstadt Wittenberg**, requiring **40 hours** per week. Working hours are Monday to Friday between 08:00 and 18:00, with no weekend work. The compensation includes a fixed salary, performance bonus, and additional allowances.\n\nFoundever offers the opportunity for **hybrid work** (home office on Mondays and Fridays) after successful onboarding. This is initially a **temporary contract for one year** with the option of extension to permanent employment.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a Commercial Employee, your key responsibilities will include:\n- Processing all incoming hospital inquiries (written and by phone) regarding order processing, pricing, invoicing, service requests, and customer complaints.\n- Tracking of delivery and payment arrears.\n- Performing invoicing, including electronic billing.\n- Issuing credit notes for returns and complaints.\n\n### Qualifications\nTo succeed in this role, you should possess:\n- Commercial vocational training and ideally prior experience in customer service.\n- Fluent German in spoken and written form (C2-Level).\n- Good English skills are desirable.\n- Very good PC and Microsoft Office skills.\n- Enjoyment of communication and telephony.\n- SAP knowledge is an advantage, but not a must.\n- Composure in stressful situations.\n\n### Author's Take\nFoundever is a global leader in the Customer Experience (CX) industry, with 150,000 employees worldwide, providing innovative CX solutions for over 800 leading brands. They pride themselves on creating special moments in everyday work and fostering a diverse and inclusive environment where \"All people are welcome at Foundever®!\"\n\nThis role offers significant support and and growth opportunities, including direct mentoring and feedback from colleagues and superiors. Foundever is committed to internal career progression, with 84% of promotion jobs and 93% of management positions filled internally, including international advancement through global German-speaking projects.\n\nBeyond professional growth, the company provides an excellent benefits package designed for employee well-being and satisfaction. This includes various team events like summer parties, exclusive employee perks such as shopping discounts, bike leasing, maximum capital-forming benefits, a company pension scheme, and a gift card with regular top-ups. Additionally, comprehensive health and well-being programs, a global wellness program, a dedicated fitness app, and Wellhub discounts are available. Employees also enjoy a generous holiday allowance of 27-31 days, depending on tenure, plus special leave.\n\nThe application process is straightforward:\n1. Our friendly Recruiting Team will contact you by phone to introduce themselves and discuss further details.\n2. Following a successful aptitude test, a video interview will be conducted with your recruiter and a project manager (Team Manager or Operations Manager).\n3. Upon positive completion of these steps, you will receive a warm welcome to the Foundever team!
