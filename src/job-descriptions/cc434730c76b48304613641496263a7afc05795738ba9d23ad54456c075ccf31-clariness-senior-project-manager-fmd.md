**Clariness is looking for a Senior Project Manager (f/m/d) based in Berlin, Hamburg or London.  
****\- Full-time; unlimited -**

**About the role:** 

In this role, you will play a key part in driving the successful delivery of complex, multi-country projects at Clariness. Acting as a central coordination point, you will ensure that timelines, documentation, and communication flow seamlessly across teams and stakeholders. You will support junior coordinators in their day-to-day tasks, contribute to continuous process improvements, and help maintain high operational standards across all project activities.

**As a Senior Project Manager (f/m/d), you would be responsible for:**

*   Supporting the planning and coordination of complex, multi-country projects, ensuring timelines, deliverables, and documentation are aligned across all stakeholders. 
*   Providing guidance and day-to-day support to junior coordinators, helping them navigate processes, improve efficiency, and deliver high-quality work. 
*   Maintaining consistency and accuracy in project documentation, schedules, and budget tracking to ensure smooth project delivery.
*   Collaborating closely with cross-functional teams to identify workflow gaps and contribute to the development of more scalable and efficient ways of working.
*   Conducting internal quality checks to ensure all project materials meet Clariness' standards as well as regulatory and client requirements.
*   Supporting the implementation and optimization of project management tools and systems to enhance transparency, efficiency, and team performance.

**We would like you to have:** 

*   Successfully completed relevant studies, ideally in Project Management, Business Administration, Healthcare/Clinical fields, Public Health, Health Informatics, Market Research, Biostatistics, or a comparable discipline. 
*   3–5 years of experience in project coordination or project management, ideally in a fast-paced or cross-functional environment. 
*   Strong working knowledge of project management tools (e.g., MS Project, Jira, PowerPoint) and the ability to quickly adapt to new systems. 
*   Proven experience supporting or mentoring team members, with a collaborative and solutions-oriented mindset.
*   Excellent problem-solving skills, the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously, and a structured approach to delivering high-quality work.

**You may ask now, why should I work for you? Let us give you a few reasons – and you'll learn more during the process.**

*   A varied and exciting job with a lot of personal responsibility in an international company, where you can develop and expand your skills.
*   Professional and personal development opportunities - incl. personal development budget.
*   Flexible working hours and mobile working.
*   Regular team events and open feedback culture.
*   A versatile field of activity and challenging projects.
*   A supportive and open company culture, providing the opportunity to collaborate with a diverse and professional team.
*   Impact healthcare by accelerating medical innovation through improved access to clinical trials, potentially bringing needed treatments to patients faster.
