**Thesis/Internship/Working Student: Artificial Intelligence & AI Agent Systems**

**\_\_\_\_\_\_**

Students   —  Working Student; Internship; Thesis 

Berlin

This challenge awaits you:
--------------------------

Artificial intelligence is changing our world—and you can be at the forefront of this change with us! Become part of our innovation team and work on exciting projects related to the development and implementation of AI-supported systems. In this technical role, you can apply your programming skills and interest in modern AI frameworks to accompany innovative use cases from the initial idea to a functioning prototype. You can expect a motivated team of developers, researchers, and students with whom you will jointly design, test, and optimize solutions in the field of AI agent systems. We offer you the opportunity to dive deep into the AI stack and work with state-of-the-art frameworks. An environment that offers space for experimental work, idea development, and hands-on coding. Collaboration with an interdisciplinary team from research and development. Flexible working hours that can be combined with your studies.

Your Tasks:
-----------

*   You evaluate specific AI use cases for their technical feasibility
*   You work hands-on with modern AI frameworks and test their suitability for our use cases
*   You will assist in the development of AI agent systems and implement them using Python and existing AI tools
*   You will research, analyze, and prepare current trends and technical solutions in the field of generative AI and autonomous agents
*   You will support our in-house AI training courses as a tutor and help to spread knowledge within the team.

Necessary Skills:
-----------------

*   Current studies in computer science, artificial intelligence, data science, or comparable technical fields
*   Good knowledge of Python and initial experience with AI/ML frameworks (e.g., PyTorch, TensorFlow, LangChain, OpenAI API) and cloud-native work (MS Azure)
*   Interest in generative AI, multi-agent systems, and current developments in the field of LLMs
*   Independent and structured way of working, combined with a desire for experimental work and prototyping
*   German language skills (at least C1) and English language skills (at least B1).

Desired Skills:
---------------

*   A high degree of initiative and interest in future technologies
*   Analytical thinking skills and the ability to grasp complex relationships and communicate them clearly
*   Teamwork and communication skills, as well as a structured and reliable approach to work

Remuneration is based on our collective bargaining agreement. The current hourly wage for this position is EUR 17.52. The internship/thesis is remunerated with a monthly salary of EUR 979.

You won’t just be working anywhere as a student at IAV. You’ll be right in the middle of it all. Real projects. Exciting future tasks. Completely integrated and side-by-side with IAV experts. Lots of responsibility and at the same time lots of freedom, combining university and work. The result is the best prospects for your professional development. And attractive compensation in accordance with our company wage agreements.  
Diversity and equal opportunity are important to us. What matters to us is the individual, with his or her character and strengths.
