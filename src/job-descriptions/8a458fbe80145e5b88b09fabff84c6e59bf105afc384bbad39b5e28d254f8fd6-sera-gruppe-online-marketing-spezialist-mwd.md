### Description\nThe company is a traditional, medium-sized group with over 300 colleagues across 6 international locations, committed to innovative products and services worldwide. They are currently undergoing significant international growth and a transformation towards becoming a modern, value-oriented, and people-focused organization with a flat hierarchy, a culture of trust, and short decision-making paths.\n\nAs a Digital Marketing Manager, you will be pivotal in this transformation, focusing on generating concrete results rather than just reach. You will be responsible for building and optimizing end-to-end digital funnels, from initial awareness to qualified lead conversion, ultimately transforming traffic into leads and leads into customers.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Build, implement, and continuously optimize end-to-end marketing funnels.\n*   Conceive and implement conversion-optimized Landing Pages in TYPO3, including A/B testing, UX optimization, tracking, and reporting.\n*   Plan and manage lead generation campaigns across search engines, social media, newsletters, and paid channels.\n*   Develop and oversee marketing automations, such as email flows, lead scoring, and re-engagement processes.\n*   Be responsible for performance monitoring using tools like Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, and Search Console, with a strong focus on conversion KPIs.\n*   Collaborate closely with sales and product marketing to ensure measurable integration of leads and sales efforts.\n*   Manage and edit content for digital channels, including the website, social media, and newsletters.\n*   Implement SEO optimization and develop content strategies based on data-driven tools such as Similarweb, SEMrush, or Sistrix.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Successfully completed training or studies in marketing, communication, media, or a comparable qualification.\n*   Several years of professional experience in marketing, ideally with a background in collaborating with sales.\n*   Ability to work in a structured yet creative manner, maintaining an overview while tackling new tasks with a strong hands-on mentality.\n*   Proficiency in working with websites, social media, and common design and graphic tools.\n*   Confident communication skills in both German and English, with comfort working in an international environment.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a results-driven Digital Marketing Manager looking to make a significant impact within a company undergoing an exciting digital transformation. The role offers high autonomy, a collaborative team environment, and the chance to directly contribute to international growth. If you thrive on converting traffic into tangible business value and appreciate a culture of trust and open communication, this position could be an excellent fit. The emphasis on hands-on work, data-driven decisions, and end-to-end funnel optimization means you'll be able to see the direct results of your efforts in a supportive and innovative setting.
