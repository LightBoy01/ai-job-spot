<p><em>Temus was established by Temasek in partnership with UST, to provide digital transformation solutions for the private and public sectors as we aspire to be a strategic partner in realising the Singapore Government’s Smart Nation vision. We are headquartered in Singapore and have more than 400 employees across a wide range of disciplines in strategy, design, architecture, technology, data &amp; AI.</em></p><p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Our Purpose</p>
<ul>
<li>Place client value and human experience at the centre of everything we do</li>
<li>Use AI and data to deliver material impact and drive disruptive transformation across public and private sectors</li>
<li>Maintain public trust in AI and data protection ensuring fairness, ethics, accountability, and transparency</li>
</ul>
<p>Your Role</p>
<ul>
<li>Harness your expertise in AI engineering, delivery, and governance to solve complex problems and deliver transformative change for clients and citizens</li>
<li>Make an outstanding contribution to a world-class AI and data team</li>
<li>Develop and maintain relationships with a broad range of clients, colleagues, and partners across a variety of contexts and formats</li>
<li>Invest continuously in building and extending your knowledge and skills</li>
</ul>
<p>Your Profile</p>
<ul>
<li>Minimum 7 years of relevant working experience in fields related to artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, software engineering</li>
<li>Broad knowledge of data science and machine learning: NLP, computer vision, recommendation systems, reinforcement learning and time series forecasting</li>
<li>Completed at least one hands-on AI development and deployment end-to-end</li>
<li>Exposure to software engineering and CI/CD principles: containerization, Infrastructure/configuration as code, model, and data versioning</li>
<li>Exposure to a broad range of AI, data, and development tools: Jupyter, TensorFlow, PyTorch, DataRobot, H2O, Hugging Face, AWS Sagemaker, Google Vertex AI, Azure ML, MLFlow, Ignazio, Dataiku, IBM-360, MS Fair toolkit, Google PAIR, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, Ansible and Datadog</li>
<li>Exposure to one of more of cloud services: AWS, Azure or GCP</li>
<li>Ability to rapidly acquire new AI and data skills</li>
<li>Strong ability to communicate with, develop and maintain relationships with a broad range of clients, colleagues, and partners across a variety of contexts and formats.</li>
<li>Bachelor’s or master’s degree in any quantitative field</li>
<li>Track record on data science competitions such as Kaggle is highly desirable</li>
</ul>
