# AI Content Creator

### Description

Marketing Architects is a leading all-inclusive advertising agency, and we are looking for a creative and tech-savvy AI Content Creator to join our team. In this role, you will be responsible for leveraging generative AI tools to create high-quality marketing content, from ad copy and social media posts to video scripts and blog articles. You will work closely with our creative and marketing teams to develop and execute innovative campaigns that drive results for our clients.

### Responsibilities

- Use generative AI tools to create a wide range of marketing content.
- Edit and refine AI-generated content to ensure it is on-brand, engaging, and effective.
- Collaborate with creative directors, copywriters, and designers to develop content strategies.
- Stay up-to-date with the latest trends and best practices in AI-powered content creation.
- Analyze content performance and use data to inform future content strategies.
- Experiment with new AI tools and techniques to push the boundaries of creativity.

### Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, or a related field.
- 1+ years of experience in content creation or copywriting.
- Experience with generative AI tools for text and image generation is a strong plus.
- Excellent writing, editing, and proofreading skills.
- A creative mindset and a passion for storytelling.
- Ability to work in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.
