**Practice Client Leader**

**Roles and Responsibilities**

**Solution Offerings Development:**

*   Define and develop Salesforce solution offerings, accelerators, and industry-specific templates that align with market trends and client needs.
*   Collaborate with technical teams to create innovative, scalable, and repeatable Salesforce solutions.
*   Identify opportunities to integrate AI (e.g., Einstein GPT), data analytics, and emerging Salesforce technologies into offerings.

**Go-To-Market Strategy:**

*   Develop and execute a comprehensive GTM strategy for Salesforce solutions across key industries.
*   Partner with marketing and sales teams to create compelling value propositions, messaging, and campaigns.
*   Drive the launch of new solutions, ensuring successful adoption and pipeline generation.

**Sales Enablement and Support:**

*   Train and enable sales teams to articulate the value of Salesforce offerings and position solutions effectively with clients.
*   Create sales playbooks, collateral, and ROI calculators tailored to target industries.
*   Act as a subject matter expert in key client engagements, RFPs, and executive presentations.

**Partnership and Ecosystem Collaboration:**

*   Build strong relationships with Salesforce account executives, ISVs, and AppExchange partners to drive co-selling opportunities.
*   Collaborate with Salesforce to align on joint GTM initiatives and leverage ecosystem resources.

**Market Analysis and Innovation:**

*   Monitor Salesforce market trends, competitive landscape, and emerging client needs to inform solution development.
*   Continuously identify opportunities to enhance and differentiate your company’s Salesforce offerings portfolio.

**Revenue Growth and Pipeline Acceleration:**

*   Work closely with sales leadership to align offerings with pipeline and revenue growth goals.
*   Track and report on the success of Salesforce GTM initiatives, adjusting strategies as needed to achieve targets.

**Qualifications**

*   12+ years of experience in Salesforce consulting, solution development, or go-to-market leadership roles

*   Deep understanding of Salesforce platform capabilities, including Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Marketing Cloud, and industry clouds
*   Techno-functional SME on at least one industry cloud such as FSC, Health Cloud, LS Cloud, or Communications Cloud
*   Proven track record of developing and executing successful GTM strategies that drive pipeline growth and revenue.
*   Experience working within the Salesforce ecosystem, including partnerships with Salesforce account executives and ISVs.
*   Strong leadership and communication skills with the ability to influence cross-functional teams and executives.
*   Analytical mindset with the ability to use data to inform decisions and measure success.
*   Experience in working effectively in a multi-national consulting firm with a globally distributed team

**Preferred Skills**

*   Expertise in AI/ML solutions and their integration with Salesforce (e.g., Agentforce).
*   Industry knowledge in verticals such as financial services, healthcare, telco, high tech, and education
*   Strong understanding on data architecture, and Salesforce Data Cloud
*   Salesforce certifications (e.g., Application Architect, System Architect, Data Cloud Consultant, AI Specialist)
