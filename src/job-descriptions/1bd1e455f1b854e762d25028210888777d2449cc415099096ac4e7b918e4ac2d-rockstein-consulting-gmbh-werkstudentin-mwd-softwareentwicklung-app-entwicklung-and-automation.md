If you are currently enrolled in a university program, have a keen interest in artificial intelligence, and are eager to gain insights into strategic consulting, we encourage you to apply. This role offers 100% remote flexibility, with an optional office presence in Monheim am Rhein. You'll join an agile startup environment, working closely with management and software developers, contributing to innovative technologies and future-oriented business models.\n\n### Developing and enhancing an **iOS app** (e.g., using Swift/SwiftUI or a cross-platform framework like React Native/Flutter).\n    *   Connecting the app to backends, APIs, and databases.\n*   **Web Development / Frontend:**\n    *   Building modern **web frontends** for landing pages, dashboards, or internal tools.\n    *   Implementing UI/UX concepts into clean, responsive code.\n*   **Automation & n8n:**\n    *   Conceiving and implementing **n8n workflows** to automate business processes.\n    *   Developing and optimizing **AI agents** through prompt engineering and integrating them into various systems via **API**.\n    *   Maintaining and optimizing existing automations.\n\n### Qualifications\nWe are looking for a candidate who meets the following criteria:\n\n*   Currently an enrolled student in **Computer Science, Business Informatics**, or a comparable degree program.\n*   Possesses **practical programming experience** and is confident in independently implementing features, preferably with:\n    *   A **frontend language** (JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML/CSS) and\n    *   Either **iOS (Swift/SwiftUI)** or a **cross-platform framework** (e.g., React Native, Flutter).\n*   Has a fundamental understanding of:\n    *   **APIs (REST, JSON)**\n    *   **Python**\n    *   **Git** (e.g., GitHub/GitLab)\n*   Experience with **n8n** or other automation workflows.\n*   Strong interest in **AI, automation, and modern tech stacks**.\n*   Structured and independent working style, coupled with a pragmatic \"Getting-Things-Done\" mindset.\n*   Very good German and English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a proactive and tech-savvy student to dive deep into the world of AI strategy, app, and web development
