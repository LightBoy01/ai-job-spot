#### About this Position

Take the chance to actively shape strategic marketing projects at Henkel Construction, gain deep insights into market analysis, strategy development and innovative business concepts in an international environment.  
  

#### What you´ll do

*   Enjoy the unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience in high level strategy  
    projects for Henkel Construction
*   Support market research and data analysis to develop strategies on business unit level 
*   Develop innovative concepts that contribute to the overall strategic goals of the  
    company
*   Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams and stakeholders to ensure alignment  
    and successful implementation of strategic plans

#### What makes you a good fit

*   Active student of business administration preferably with focus on Marketing and/or Strategy
*   First practical experience acquired through internships
*   Excellent English language skills and German is an advantage
*   Advanced MS-Office skills (Excel, Power Point)
*   Knowledge of AI tools and Project management will be beneficial
*   Good team player with ability to plan work and set priorities

*   Analytical skills, reliability and high level of self organization to handle projects independently of advantage
