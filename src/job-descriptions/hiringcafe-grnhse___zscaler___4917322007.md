About **Zscaler**

Serving thousands of enterprise customers around the world including 45% of Fortune 500 companies, Zscaler (NASDAQ: ZS) was founded in 2007 with a mission to make the cloud a safe place to do business and a more enjoyable experience for enterprise users. As the operator of the world’s largest security cloud, Zscaler accelerates digital transformation so enterprises can be more agile, efficient, resilient, and secure. The pioneering, AI-powered Zscaler Zero Trust Exchange™ platform, which is found in our SASE and SSE offerings, protects thousands of enterprise customers from cyberattacks and data loss by securely connecting users, devices, and applications in any location.

Named a Best Workplace in Technology by Fortune and others, Zscaler fosters an inclusive and supportive culture that is home to some of the brightest minds in the industry. If you thrive in an environment that is fast-paced and collaborative, and you are passionate about building and innovating for the greater good, come make your next move with Zscaler. 

Our general and administrative teams help to support and scale our great company. Whether striving to grow our workforce, nurture an amazing culture and work environment, support our financial and legal operations, or maintain our global infrastructure, the G&A team provides a strong foundation for growth. Put your passion, drive and expertise to work with the world’s cloud security leader.

We’re looking for an experienced **Senior Manager, Legal Operations** to join our Legal & Compliance team. Reporting to the Director, Legal Operations, you will be responsible for:

*   Leading legal operations projects, including technology implementations, process optimization, and cross-functional collaboration to deliver impactful results
*   Optimizing systems such as Ironclad, e-billing platforms, ServiceNow, and e-discovery tools, and driving adoption of AI-based technologies for automation and workflow enhancements
*   Analyzing data and creating dashboards to monitor team performance, system usage metrics, and overall efficiency
*   Developing and delivering training programs for new processes and tools, fostering user adoption and continuous improvement
*   Building strong relationships with stakeholders across Legal, Compliance, IT, Finance, and business teams to enhance collaboration and support organizational goals

**What We’re Looking for (Minimum Qualifications)**

*   7+ years in legal operations or project management in a corporate legal department
*   Strong project management skills managing multiple complex initiatives
*   Experience with technology evaluation, legal tech platforms, and implementations
*   Demonstrated ability to gather data, create dashboards and reports for trend analysis
*   Change management expertise, specifically related to legal workflows and process improvements

**What Will Make You Stand Out (Preferred Qualifications)**

*   Experience in high-growth companies with evolving legal operations needs
*   Background in AI tools and automation technologies for contract review and workflow efficiency
*   Proven ability to manage vendor relationships and legal tech implementations
