At **myoncare**, we don’t just develop software – we are building an **international health ecosystem that redefines care**. Our platform connects patients, clinics, doctors, and partners within a single, intelligent system, creating something unprecedented: **continuous, secure, and personalized care around the clock.**

As an **Expert Frontend Developer**, you’ll create intuitive, secure interfaces for complex healthcare data, shape our architecture and component libraries, and drive the full development lifecycle. Your work will directly impact how patients and healthcare professionals experience care every day.

If you’re excited about building meaningful software, shaping innovation, and making a real difference in healthcare, this is your chance to join a team that’s transforming the future.

Tasks
-----

*   **Frontend Development:** Implement and maintain a web UI component library based on React
*   **Business & Data Workflows:** Design, implement, and support business workflows; structure, analyze, and process data to meet requirements and solve problems
*   **Software Quality & Lifecycle:** Perform unit testing, provide clear documentation, manage integration and migration, and work along the full software development lifecycle (SDLC)
*   **Continuous Improvement:** Stay up to date with industry trends, improve knowledge of new technologies, and contribute innovative ideas and features to our products
*   **Problem Solving & Maintenance:** Analyze system flows, investigate issues, optimize data usage and processes, and perform bug fixing
*   **Leadership & Architecture:** Take on leadership responsibilities within a Scrum team, enforce clean and secure coding practices, and provide architectural solutions for internal and external systems

Requirements
------------

**Technical Requirements**

*   Degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field
*   **6+** years of experience as a Frontend Developer
*   Excellent knowledge of **JavaScript** and **React**
*   Experience with **frameworks and systems** such as **GIT**
*   Experience with **REST APIs** and state management (**Redux**)
*   Strong understanding of **clean, secure, and maintainable coding practices**
*   Knowledge of **Scrum** and agile development methodologies

**Soft Skills**

*   Team-oriented, reliable, and proactive character
*   Strong **problem-solving** and **analytical thinking** abilities
*   Very good **presentation skills**
*   Strong **organizational and leadership** capabilities
*   Effective **communication skills** in English (verbal and written)
