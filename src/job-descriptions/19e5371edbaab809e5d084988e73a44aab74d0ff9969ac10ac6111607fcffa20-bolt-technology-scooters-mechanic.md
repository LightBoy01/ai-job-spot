In this critical role, you will be responsible for quickly identifying, fixing, and performing thorough safety tests on e-scooters, ensuring every ride is safe, reliable, and contributes to a top-notch customer experience.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Conduct general repair work and check-ups to ensure the highest quality for e-scooters.\n- Inspect e-scooters in use and diagnose problems within various product components.\n- Test e-scooters rigorously before release to uphold quality and safety standards.\n- Prepare new e-scooters for service, including unpacking, quality checks, equipment installation, safety assurance, and troubleshooting.\n- Assist with broader warehouse operations, including inventory and battery management, shipments, and general housekeeping.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Demonstrated high level of attentiveness and accuracy in work.\n- Proficiency in operating various hand and power tools.\n- Proactive approach and willingness to take ownership of challenges in a hands-on capacity.\n- Experience in repairing mechanical devices, particularly in automotive or electronic repair fields.\n- Solid local language skills and experience using smartphones.\n- English language skills and a forklift license are considered advantageous.\n\n_Experience is valuable, but we prioritize drive, intelligence, and integrity.
