<p><strong>What You'll Do:</strong></p>
<p>As a member of the AI&amp;D product management team; your primary focus will be to work with the foundational businesses, define the strategy, manage requirements documents, manage the roadmap, and execute on the backlog across AI&amp;D products portfolio and enterprise foundational initiatives.<br><br>• Strategy: Work with foundational businesses and AI&amp;D teams to develop vision and drive effective outcomes across the enterprise<br>• Ownership: Align with strategy for various business teams and create and drive a portfolio of AI &amp; Data cross-functional initiatives to deliver on core elements of the business strategy<br>• Definition: Articulate business problems, gather evidence from users and outside sources, clarify potential solutions with assumptions, guiding principles, strategic connections<br>• Roadmap: Define the roadmap and prioritization across the portfolio and create functional &amp; non-functional requirements and user stories to manage product backlog and execution through scrum teams<br>• Delivery: Partner with AI&amp;D cross functional teams and Business Unit product managers and technology partners to ensure high-quality, efficient execution of key initiatives in a scaled agile delivery framework<br>• Communication: Serve as primary evangelist of AI&amp;D strategy and product capabilities with internal, business, and executive stakeholders<br>• Outcomes: Drive outcomes aligned with business teams and AI&amp;D OKRs and report key metrics driving product effectiveness, utilization, and satisfaction<br><br><strong>What You’ll Bring:</strong></p>
<p>You will bring relevant product management industry experience, having worked on enterprise data platforms, collaborated with cross-functional teams and multiple business units, AI models and services and analytics &amp; reporting for the enterprise.<br><br>Required Skills:<br>• Bachelor’s OR master’s degree in a technical field (Engineering/Computer Science or related) or business-related discipline<br>• 3+ years of industry experience with related product management, design, or business-facing product development experience<br>• 3+ years of developing and managing roadmaps, writing product specifications, and managing agile processes.<br>• 3+ years of experience working with data &amp; analytics products and experience with cloud technologies, data governance, data science and analytics.<br>• Proven experience in shipping AI-based products from ideation to launch and understanding of LLMs.<br>• Strong written and oral communication skills, including experience in developing influential presentations. Ability and executive presence to communicate persuasively with stakeholders at all levels in the organization as well as external partners.<br>• Demonstrated success in creating measurable business benefit from products and analytics while interacting with cross functional stakeholders in a complex organization.<br><br>Preferred Skills:<br><br>• Strong organization skills with the ability to collaborate across multiple business units and functions, including Technology, Data and Compliance, and third-party data and technology partners.<br>• Fluency in technical delivery, including scaled agile processes and product management tools like Confluence, Jira.</p><p>​</p>
