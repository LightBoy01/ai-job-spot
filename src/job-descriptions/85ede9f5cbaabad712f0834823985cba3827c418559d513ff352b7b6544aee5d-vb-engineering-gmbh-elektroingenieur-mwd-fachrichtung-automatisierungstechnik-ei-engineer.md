### Description\nVB-Engineering GmbH is a young, modern, and future-oriented company specializing in high-quality engineering services for industrial and maritime facilities. Efficiency, transparency, quality, and adherence to deadlines are core to our company philosophy. We specialize in process engineering, energy-generating plants, and marine operating technology. Our expertise not only boosts efficiency but also enhances the safety and performance of these systems. We are looking for an experienced, motivated, and professionally and personally convincing Electrical Engineer (m/f/d) specializing in Automation Technology to join our interdisciplinary team of engineers, technicians, and specialists as soon as possible.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Independently manage automation projects in chemical, petrochemical, and power plant sectors.\n- Create and maintain circuit diagrams, cable lists, I/O lists, and P&ID flow diagrams.\n- Select and specify electrical equipment and automation components.\n- Support commissioning and loop checks.\n- Coordinate with internal and external project partners and clients.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in Electrical Engineering with a focus on Automation Technology or a comparable qualification.\n- Profound knowledge of relevant standards and guidelines (e.g., VDE, IEC, ATEX).\n- Experience in project execution.\n- Structured, independent working style and strong teamwork skills.\n- Proficiency with CAD tools (e.g., EPLAN, AutoCAD) would be an advantage.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced Automation Electrical Engineer to join a dynamic, future-oriented company at the forefront of industrial and maritime engineering. VB-Engineering fosters a collegial environment with flat hierarchies, offering challenging projects in growth sectors and robust long-term development prospects. The role promises significant impact, contributing directly to enhancing the efficiency, safety, and performance of critical infrastructure. Furthermore, the company supports professional growth through continuous training, flexible working hours, and home office options, demonstrating a strong commitment to employee well-being and career advancement.
