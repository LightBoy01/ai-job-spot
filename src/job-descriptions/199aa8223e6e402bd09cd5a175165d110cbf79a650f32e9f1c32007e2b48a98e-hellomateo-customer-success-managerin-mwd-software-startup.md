### Description\nWe at **hellomateo** are building the next generation of marketing and service technology for German SMEs. Our software combines AI, automation, and user-centric design – and is growing rapidly.🐨\n\nIn this role, you are the interface between product and customer. You guide new customers through their onboarding and help existing customers maximize the value of our software through training.\n\nYou will work closely with Sales, Product, and Support – with a high degree of autonomy, a clear vision, and the focus on turning satisfied customers into true fans.📈\n\n### Responsibilities\nIn these areas, you will make a difference:\n- **🧑‍💻 Conducting Onboardings:** Ensure customers can quickly and effectively use our platform.\n- 👩‍🏫 **Delivering Training Sessions:** Help our customers better understand the software to get the most out of hellomateo.\n- **👥 Solution-oriented daily support:** pragmatic, fast, reliable.\n- **📈 Continuous optimization of the customer journey** based on user data and feedback.\n- **🔎 Identification of up- and cross-selling potentials** in close cooperation with Sales.\n- **🤝 Close collaboration with Product & Tech:** Bring the customer's voice to the team.\n- **📄 Documentation and analysis of user feedback and KPIs** to improve our services.\n\n### Qualifications\nThe basic prerequisite for this role is your communication skills – you should enjoy daily customer contact. Even if you don't have prior experience in Customer Success, let's start your Customer Success career together! 🚀\n\nWhat you should bring:\n- Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal.\n- Excellent German language skills (C2) and English language skills (B2/C1).\n- Ability to convey complex information clearly and understandably.\n- Team player and ability to collaborate effectively with various departments.\n- Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously.\n- Understanding of systems and software.\n- Experience in Customer Success, customer support, or a similar role is an advantage.\n\nEven if you can't tick every checkbox – apply! Much more important than fulfilling every single qualification is that you are eager to hit the ground running with us. Together we grow with the challenges! 📈🐨\n\n### Author's Take\nhellomateo offers a dynamic Customer Success role where you'll directly impact user satisfaction and product development. This is an excellent opportunity for highly communicative individuals, including career starters, to grow within a collaborative, office-first environment that emphasizes team events, personal ownership, and continuous learning. Your future colleagues highlight the importance of enthusiasm for the challenge and a commitment that goes beyond just a job, valuing proactive team players who also contribute to a positive office atmosphere. Beyond competitive compensation, benefits like Wellhub/Deutschland-Ticket, weekly team meals, and social events underscore hellomateo's dedication to team well-being and strong internal bonds.
