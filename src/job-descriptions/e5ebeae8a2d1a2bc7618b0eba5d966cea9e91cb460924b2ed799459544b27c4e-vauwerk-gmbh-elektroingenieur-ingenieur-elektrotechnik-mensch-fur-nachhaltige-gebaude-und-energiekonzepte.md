### Description\nVAUWERK GmbH is a future-oriented team actively shaping the sustainable development of our region. Starting as a classic project developer, we have evolved into a versatile company focusing on planning, architecture, and technical building equipment (TGA). Today, we are experts in smart energy concepts and modern building systems, combining innovation, functionality, and sustainability. Our goal is to create future-proof residential and commercial districts that intelligently connect people, energy, and the environment. If you are eager to work on real future projects and contribute to sustainable solutions, you are exactly right with us.\n\nAs an Electrical Engineer, you don't just plan systems – you create solutions that make buildings smarter and more sustainable.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Planning and design of electrical systems for energy-efficient buildings.\n*   Development of modern supply concepts including PV systems, storage, and charging infrastructure.\n*   Participation in service phases 1-9 in the field of electrical engineering.\n*   Creation of technical documentation (bills of quantities, wiring diagrams, calculations).\n*   Coordination with clients and other disciplines – from concept to commissioning.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Degree in Electrical Engineering, Energy Technology, Building Services Engineering, or Supply Engineering.\n*   Alternatively, an electrical engineering apprenticeship with further training as a specialist planner, technician, or master craftsman.\n*   Experience in planning electro-technical systems in a construction environment.\n*   Good knowledge of current standards as well as planning and calculation tools.\n*   Interest in sustainable power supply and innovative building technology.\n*   Enjoyment of interdisciplinary collaboration and personal responsibility.\n*   German language skills at least C1 level.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at VAUWERK GmbH offers a unique opportunity for an Electrical Engineer passionate about sustainability and innovative building solutions. The company stands out with its commitment to creating smart, energy-efficient futures and provides a truly supportive and forward-thinking environment. If you're looking for a role where your work has real-world impact within a tight-knit and dynamic team, this is an excellent opportunity.
