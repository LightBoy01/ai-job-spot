# AI Automation Engineer

### Description

This is an exciting opportunity to join a fast-growing Techstars portfolio company that is revolutionizing a major industry. We are looking for an AI Automation Engineer to help us build and scale our intelligent automation platform. In this role, you will be responsible for designing, developing, and deploying AI-powered automation solutions that streamline our internal processes and enhance our product offerings. This is a unique opportunity to get in on the ground floor of a promising startup and make a significant impact on its success.

### Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and maintain AI-powered automation workflows.
- Integrate our automation platform with various third-party applications and services.
- Work with our product and engineering teams to identify new opportunities for automation.
- Analyze and optimize the performance of our automation solutions.
- Stay up-to-date with the latest trends and technologies in AI and automation.

### Qualifications

- 3+ years of experience in software development, with a focus on automation.
- Experience with Robotic Process Automation (RPA) tools and techniques is a plus.
- Strong programming skills in Python or a similar language.
- Experience with APIs and system integration.
- Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
- A passion for startups and a desire to work in a fast-paced environment.
