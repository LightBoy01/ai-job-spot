### Description
Are safety on construction sites and in road traffic familiar concepts to you? Does participating in construction meetings and acceptance procedures belong to your daily business? Do you immediately recognize when construction sites are not properly secured or when specifications haven't been met?

If so, you are exactly right with us!

We are successful because we act as a team, solution-oriented, and innovatively. Become part of our motivated team and take off with us! This role is crucial for the expansion of our fiber optic network, ensuring quality, safety, and seamless coordination across various projects nationwide.

### Responsibilities
*   Steering and monitoring of construction projects in fiber optic expansion.
*   Coordination and supervision of on-site activities, involving both internal employees and external service providers.
*   Regular exchange with responsible authorities and offices.
*   Ensuring quality and documentation standards.
*   Acting as an interface between clients, contractors, and the company.
*   General documentation and construction acceptances, as well as participation in construction meetings.

### Qualifications
*   A completed degree or vocational training in a technical field, construction electrical engineering, civil engineering, or road construction is advantageous, or proven experience as a Construction Technician (m/f/d).
*   Initial experience as a Construction Manager/Foreman (m/f/d) in fiber optic or utility construction.
*   An independent working style, a high degree of commitment, and assertiveness.
*   Driver's license Class B.
*   Nationwide willingness to travel within Germany.
