### Description\nGC Corporation, founded in Japan in 1921, is a global leader in the development, manufacture, and distribution of a wide range of dental products. With its European head office in Leuven, Belgium, GC Europe NV oversees operations across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. The company is seeking a dedicated Course & Event Manager Specialist to join its team in Breckerfeld, Germany. This role is crucial for executing and managing clinical education programs and events across the DACH region, ensuring high-quality delivery and strategic alignment with regional standards.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Execute and manage clinical education programs in DACH (courses, congresses, workshops, and events), ensuring timely and high-quality delivery.\n- Collaborate closely with the Clinical Education Professional Manager EMEA to align local execution with regional strategy and standards.\n- Coordinate all logistical and operational aspects of educational events, including venue setup, equipment, materials, faculty scheduling, and participant communication.\n- Work with Sales teams to understand customer needs, identify training opportunities, and integrate feedback into educational offerings for traditional Ortho and Mov'Aligners.\n- Contribute to the development and customization of course agendas and content to address local clinical practices, trends, and business priorities.\n- Build and maintain strong relationships with universities, professors, and academic partners to support clinical education and early engagement.\n- Maintain close contact with key customers and use their personal networks to spread information and use them as influencers.\n- Manage relationships with key opinion leaders (KOLs) and faculty involved in educational initiatives.\n- Monitor and report on program effectiveness, participant feedback, and continuous improvement opportunities.\n- Collaborate cross-functionally with Marketing, Product Management, and Training & Education teams to ensure alignment and maximize impact.\n- Share success stories and solutions for improvements.\n- Create and manage purchase orders (POs) and coordinate vendor selection and onboarding as needed.\n- Track and monitor expenses to ensure all programs stay within allocated budgets.\n- Ensure compliance with internal policies, local regulations, and industry standards related to clinical education.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 4–6 years of experience in clinical education, medical training, event coordination, or healthcare-related project management—preferably in Ortho or medical device, pharmaceutical, or healthcare industry.\n- Proven track record in the execution and coordination of clinical courses, workshops, and congresses, including both in-person and virtual formats.\n- Experience working with healthcare professionals, universities, and KOLs, with strong stakeholder management and relationship-building skills.\n- Demonstrated ability to collaborate cross-functionally with sales, marketing, and regional education teams.\n- Solid understanding of clinical environments, customer training needs, and healthcare industry trends.\n- Experience managing budgets, purchase orders, and invoicing, with strong attention to administrative detail.\n- Comfortable working in a fast-paced, small team with shifting priorities and tight timelines.\n- Experience with CRM systems, event management tools, and Microsoft Office Suite; knowledge of virtual training platforms (e.g., Zoom, Teams) is a plus.\n- Capable of adapting to challenging situations, problem-solving, and possessing a positive attitude is a plus.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting opportunity for an experienced Course & Event Manager Specialist to make a significant impact within GC Corporation's growing DACH region. The role offers a blend of strategic collaboration and hands-on execution, allowing you to shape clinical education programs for a leading global dental products company. With a competitive benefits package, flexible working arrangements including remote options, and a supportive, multicultural environment that prioritizes respect and team spirit, GC Europe clearly values its employees. You'll have ample opportunities for professional development and the chance to contribute to a healthier world, making this a well-rounded and appealing position for a dedicated professional.
