----------------

*   12+ years of experience in designing and delivering AI/ML solutions with a strong foundation in machine learning, deep learning, NLP, and generative AI.
*   Proven expertise in architecting scalable AI systems, collaborating with cross-functional teams, and aligning technical solutions with strategic business goals.
*   Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills with hands-on proficiency in deep learning frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch), LLMs (GPT, BERT, Gemini), and cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure).
*   Adept at thriving in dynamic environments while driving innovation, technical leadership, and continuous improvement in AI/ML solution delivery.
