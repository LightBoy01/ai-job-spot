Would you like to be part of our new adventure? Vodafone Group launched its new technological HUB in 2021, an international center of excellence dedicated to research and development of technical solutions, such as Secure Networks, 5G and 6G development, Open RAN, IoT, MPN & MEC and UCC for Vodafone Business, platforms and enterprise solutions.
This vacancy is part of VOIS Spain, legal entity part of Vodafone Group.
Come and join us to create the future together!
For the role of Global Product Manager- MPN (Mobile Private Networks) we are looking for candidates with a broad range of skills.
The responsibilities include:
*   Own the end-to-end product strategy and roadmap for Edge Portfolio offerings, with a focus on MPN and AI-driven solutions.
*   Lead cross-functional collaboration with Engineering, Sales, Marketing, and Regulatory teams to deliver product initiatives on time and within scope.
*   Represent Vodafone in strategic partnerships and workshops, including engagements with network equipment vendors and other key industry players.
*   Drive customer-centric innovation by translating market insights and client feedback into actionable product features.
*   Represent Product Management in governance calls and deal clinics to ensure product performance and commercial alignment.
*   Oversee risk tracking and compliance for enterprise deployments, including security requirement reviews and regulatory launches.
*   Act as a key point of contact for internal stakeholders and external partners, facilitating workshops, catch-up calls, and roadmap alignment sessions.
**What you bring**
------------------
*   Proven experience in global product management within the telecoms, technology sector.
*   Strong understanding of Edge computing, MPN, AI chatbot integration, and enterprise security frameworks desirable.
*   Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with experience leading cross-functional teams.
*   Strategic thinker with a hands-on approach to execution and problem-solving.
*   Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Business, or a related field; MBA or equivalent preferred.
*   Experience working in multinational environments and managing complex product portfolios.
*   Familiarity with regulatory processes and enterprise client engagement is a plus.
*   Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe) certifications are a plus
