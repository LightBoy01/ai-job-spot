### Description
Are you from the banking industry, with experience in accounting, regulatory reporting, or annual financial statements, or have you already gained extensive knowledge in the financial services sector? Do you want to apply and develop your professional expertise in demanding IT projects? Do you enjoy taking responsibility and preparing complex technical issues so that development teams can implement them efficiently? Then you are exactly right with us!

CPU Consulting & Software GmbH has been a successful IT service provider in the banking environment for more than four decades in the Professional Services area. As a subsidiary of the CPU Group, we focus our competencies on the successful implementation of software projects for our customers on-site.

### Responsibilities
- Recording, analysis, and evaluation of technical requirements related to the HGB single-entity financial statements of credit cooperatives.
- Review of technical and customer requirements, considering regulatory specifications.
- Creation of technical concepts, user stories, specifications, and technical decision bases.
- Translation of complex technical content into clear technical implementation requirements.
- Coordination with specialist departments, developers, test teams, and external partners.
- Participation in agile projects and assumption of sub-tasks / work packages.
- Preparation of presentations, reports, and analyses (Excel, PowerPoint).
- Technical support for tests, including derivation and documentation of test cases.

### Qualifications
- Completed studies (Business Administration, Business Informatics) and/or several years of professional experience in the banking environment.
- Solid knowledge in accounting according to HGB, ideally with a focus on credit cooperatives.
- Experience in regulatory reporting or annual financial statement processes.
- High IT affinity and understanding of the interface between technical departments and software development.
- Initial experience in requirements analysis, process modeling, or technical conception.
- Structured, analytical, and reliable way of working.
- Strong communication skills, team orientation, and quality awareness.
- Very good German language skills, both written and spoken.
- Advantage: Experience in agile projects or in the test/QA environment.
