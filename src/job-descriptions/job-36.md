# AI Automation Specialist

 
### Description 

FAM Brands is seeking an AI Automation Specialist to join our team in Los Angeles, CA. In this role, you will be responsible for designing, developing, and implementing intelligent automation solutions that streamline business processes and enhance operational efficiency. You will work with various departments to identify automation opportunities, leverage AI tools, and drive digital transformation initiatives across the organization. 
### Responsibilities 

- Identify and analyze business processes suitable for AI-driven automation.
- Design, develop, and deploy automation workflows using AI and Robotic Process Automation (RPA) tools.
- Collaborate with stakeholders to gather requirements and ensure automation solutions meet business needs.
- Monitor and optimize the performance of automated processes, ensuring accuracy and efficiency.
- Stay abreast of the latest advancements in AI and automation technologies and apply them to improve operations. 
### Qualifications 

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Business Process Management, or a related field.
- 1+ years of experience in business process automation or AI automation.
- Familiarity with RPA platforms (e.g., UiPath, Automation Anywhere, Blue Prism) is a plus.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate business needs into technical solutions.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
- A passion for leveraging technology to drive efficiency and innovation.
