# AI Automation Specialist

- **Company:** FAM Brands
- **Location:** Los Angeles, CA
- **Job Level:** Entry-Level
- **Application Link:** https://www.fambrands.com/careers

---

### Description

FAM Brands is looking for an AI Automation Specialist to join our team. This role is ideal for new or upcoming college graduates with a strong interest in AI and automation tools. You will be involved in identifying, designing, and implementing AI-driven automation solutions to streamline business processes across various departments.

### Responsibilities

- Identify opportunities for AI-driven automation within business processes.
- Assist in the design and development of automation solutions using AI tools.
- Implement and test automated workflows.
- Collaborate with teams to integrate automation into existing systems.

### Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field (or currently pursuing).
- Strong interest in AI, machine learning, and automation.
- Familiarity with RPA tools (e.g., UiPath, Automation Anywhere) is a plus.
- Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
