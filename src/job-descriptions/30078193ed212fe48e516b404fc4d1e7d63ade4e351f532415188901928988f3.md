**What You'll Do**  
*   Lead end-to-end AI implementations, including configuration, testing, and deployment for Voice Agent, Co-Pilot, and related AI solutions.
*   Design and maintain AI playbooks and best practices for prompt design, conversation flow, and customer configuration.
*   Serve as the AI subject-matter expert during customer onboarding and post-go-live optimization cycles.
*   Partner with Product and Engineering to pilot new AI features, provide structured feedback, and identify enhancements.
*   Analyze AI performance by monitoring accuracy, adoption, and user feedback to inform future iterations.
*   Collaborate cross-functionally with Implementation PMs, TAMs, and PSEs to integrate AI capabilities into the broader customer journey.
*   Train customer teams on self-service management of AI tools, including prompt iteration and reporting.
*   Build structure and repeatability by documenting configurations, processes, and insights that scale across all implementations.
  
  
**What You Bring**  
*   3–5 years experience in Professional Services, Solutions Engineering, or AI/automation delivery within a SaaS company.
*   Proven track record leading technical customer implementations from kickoff to go-live.
*   Strong understanding of LLMs, conversational AI, and automation platforms such as OpenAI, LangChain, or Dialogflow.
*   Experience with prompt design, workflow configuration, and structured data (JSON, APIs).
*   Excellent communicator who can simplify complex AI concepts for customers and internal stakeholders.
*   Highly organized and detail-oriented, with a focus on process rigor.
*   Curious, adaptable, and driven to experiment with emerging technologies.
*   Startup mindset with the ability to balance structure and speed.
