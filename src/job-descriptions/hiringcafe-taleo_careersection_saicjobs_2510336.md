Join SAIC in supporting our enterprise data and analytics initiatives. As a Data Scientist, you’ll advance our customers’ analytics and AI mission. As a Data Scientist, you’ll design predictive models, apply AI/ML algorithms, and deliver insights that improve operational readiness and decision-making.

**Duties and Responsibilities:**

*   Design predictive models and simulations
*   Develop AI/ML algorithms
*   Perform statistical analysis and data mining
*   Deliver actionable insights to mission stakeholders

  

**Required Qualifications:**

*   Active Secret Clearance, or ability to acquire one
*   Master’s degree and 7+ years, Bachelor’s degree in data science, statistics, computer science, or related, or equivalent and 10+ years of related experience
*   5+ years of data science/AI/ML experience
*   Proficient in Python, R, Jupyter, TensorFlow, PyTorch
*   Strong writing and presentation skills

**Preferred Qualifications:**

*   DoD/IC data science experience
*   Cloud certification (AWS, Azure)
