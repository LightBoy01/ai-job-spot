**Forvia, a sustainable mobility technology leader**
----------------------------------------------------

We pioneer technology for mobility experience that matter to people.

**Your mission, roles and responsibilities**
--------------------------------------------

IT at Forvia is a key player in our ongoing strategy into a “Digital Transformation.” IT also plays a major role in guaranteeing the service and support that ensures our business succeeds in our fast-moving world.  
Each day, IT teams are tackling critical challenges to help build Forvia’s competitive advantage throughout Digital Transformation. Examples include developing mobile apps, harnessing and applying Big Data, engaging in predictive analysis, leveraging the cloud and contributing to Forvia’s cultural shift to become a digitally advanced company. IT also plays a lead role in ensuring continuous optimization and cybersecurity of our company processes.

Our IT Department - Global Information Technologies (GIT) is looking for a I**BP SAP Internal Consultant** to be based in Santa Maria da Feira (Portugal).  
The I**BP SAP internal Consultant’s** role is to support and enrich our IBP SAP core model in the Supply Chain domain. You will support the global organization in the implementation of processes and tools, and act as a IBP expert bridge between the Business stakeholders and GIT for IBP and Supply Chain business processes. You will contribute to innovation and improvement opportunities in the Supply Chain domain (Machine learning, artificial intelligence, Digital Transformation, IBP roadmap innovations). Main Missions and Results:

*   Act as internal SAP expert link for your domain : lead the implementation and continuous improvement of the corresponding modules/tools in close cooperation with Users Community and Technical Teams; Support the Functional Design Team Leader in developing and enhancing the IBP core system.
*   Be the functional expert for worldwide used tools and workflows;
*   Review and analyze the effectiveness and efficiency of existing systems and develop strategies for improving or further leveraging these systems.
*   Manage Service requests, support Supply Chain on Incident reporting when necessary, understanding the incident root cause and helping to define corrective actions.
*   Participate in Projects and Conduct meetings with decision makers, system owners, and end users to define requirements and system goals, and identify and resolve system issues.; Support integration activities for data migration, interfaces and data warehouse.
*   Coordinate and perform in-depth tests, including end-user reviews, for modified and new applications & functionalities, and other post-implementation support.
*   Create business requirement documents and technical specifications for Supply Chain areas, planning, and monitoring change requests process within Global Information Systems.

**Your profile and competencies to succeed**
--------------------------------------------

**What you bring with you:**

*   An analytical mindset and ability to quickly establish successful cooperation with others.
*   A strong customer orientation and experience on technical topics and know-how on providing a service of excellence.
*   Ability to adapt to shifting priorities, demands and timelines together with good problem-solving capabilities
*   Ability to conduct research into systems issues

  
**What we are looking for:**

*   A degree in economics, (industrial-)engineering, informatics or equivalent qualification;
*   At least 2 years of professional experience in SAP IBP Module, ideally as an IBP Consultant or part of an SAP Support chain (with customizing and hands-on experience in the key IBP SAP functionalities);
*   Expertise in at least one of the following Supply Chain processes: Demand planning, Sales&Operations planning, Production Planning and Scheduling) would be preferred;
*   Experience in project management or project mode is appreciated.
*   Fluency in English (mandatory);
*   Strong customer orientation and Good communication skills preferably in an international environment;
*   Experience working in a team-oriented, collaborative environment is a plus
