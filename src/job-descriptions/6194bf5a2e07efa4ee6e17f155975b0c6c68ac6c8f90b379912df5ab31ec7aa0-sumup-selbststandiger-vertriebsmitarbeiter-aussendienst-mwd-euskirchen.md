### Description
Are you ready to take charge of your career, define your own success, and manage your time freely? SumUp empowers small businesses with smart and user-friendly financial solutions, from mobile card payments to comprehensive POS systems. Our mission is to strengthen entrepreneurs, allowing them to focus entirely on their core business.

As a **freelance Field Sales Consultant** at SumUp, you become your own boss, with the opportunity to earn attractive, unlimited commissions. Your core responsibility will be to build relationships with local businesses, demonstrating how SumUp's solutions can simplify their operations. You'll be directly selling our range of products, including mobile card terminals and complete payment systems, equipped with everything you need to succeed. This role offers 100% performance-based compensation, meaning your income is directly tied to your efforts, with no upper limit. You'll also benefit from maximum flexibility, allowing you to work when and where you want, and access to top-tier products with high market potential. Importantly, SumUp provides comprehensive training and sales support, ensuring you're never left alone on your journey.

### Responsibilities
*   **Active New Customer Acquisition**: Identify and win local businesses as new SumUp customers.
*   **Personal Meetings and Consulting**: Present our products and demonstrate their benefits through direct interaction.
*   **Customer Relationship Building**: Secure long-term partnerships by providing excellent service and support.
*   **Independent Client and Lead Management**: Take full ownership of how you build and grow your client base.

### Qualifications
*   Passion for sales and strong self-motivation.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer service is an advantage.
*   Excellent communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Very good German language skills.
