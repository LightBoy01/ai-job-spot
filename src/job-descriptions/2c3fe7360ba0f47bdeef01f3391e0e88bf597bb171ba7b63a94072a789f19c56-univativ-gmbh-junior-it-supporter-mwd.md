### Description\nUnivativ is a project and personnel service provider that offers exciting job opportunities to students and graduates at renowned companies. Your career is our mission, and your development is important to us.\n\nWe are looking for an Entry-Level IT Support Specialist to join our client's dynamic and engaged team in Mannheim, starting as soon as possible, ideally by December 1, 2025. This full-time (40 hours/week) role is perfect if you have initial experience in user and customer consulting and knowledge of common databases and servers. You will gain comprehensive industry insights, work with various partner firms, and earn an annual salary between 38,000 € and 48,000 €.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Take over 1st and 2nd-level support.\n- Install and maintain workstation systems in the client area.\n- Document incidents and resolutions in the ticketing system.\n- Install hardware components.\n- Support IT projects such as rollouts and upgrades.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics, or completed IT vocational training (e.g., IT Specialist for System Integration).\n- Initial experience in support and a good understanding of IT concepts.\n- Experience with ticketing systems.\n- Fluent German language skills.\n- Structured and independent work ethic, coupled with a professional approach to customer interaction.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent entry-level opportunity for aspiring IT professionals looking to kickstart their career in IT support within a dynamic environment. Univativ prioritizes your development, offering a competitive salary, the flexibility of remote work, and significant growth potential through their e-Academy and individual coaching programs. It's a chance to immerse yourself in the IT industry, work with diverse clients, and build a strong foundation for future specialization. If you're a proactive problem-solver with a passion for IT and customer service, this role provides a clear path for professional advancement and continuous learning.
