### Description
This role at Neuraum Ventures is a dynamic blend of business strategy and hands-on product execution. As a Product Lead, you will own the entire product lifecycle, from initial discovery through go-to-market and continuous optimization. You will define and implement the product vision and strategy, leveraging data and deep customer understanding to drive value. This involves leading a high-performing product development team, collaborating closely with developers, and crafting effective go-to-market plans with the account management team to ensure successful product launches, scaling, and value realization within the B2B SaaS space.

### Responsibilities
- **Customer & Market Voice**: Act as the voice of the customer, gathering, prioritizing, and managing insights. Conduct user research during ideation and design to meet customer needs.
- **Execution & Delivery**: Manage the full product lifecycle, from idea approval through design, development, release, and impact analysis, ensuring smooth and effective execution.
- **Go-to-Market**: Lead all aspects of product release, onboarding, sales support, and customer engagement to ensure seamless launches and adoption.
- **Product Strategy & Roadmap**: Collaborate with the Head of Product to define the product vision, strategy, and roadmap for your area of responsibility, aligning with company goals and customer demands.
- **Data-Led Decision Making**: Utilize data for informed decisions, conducting thorough analysis on user behavior to assess product performance and identify future development opportunities.

### Qualifications
- At least 4 years of experience owning a product or a significant part of one, with a strong focus on business, strategy, and analysis.
- Significant experience in go-to-market strategies (launching, scaling, sales engagement, onboarding), ideally with B2B SaaS Products.
- Technical understanding to collaborate directly with front-end and back-end developers.
- Data-driven proficiency in tools like Metabase, Tableau, or PowerBI, with an interest in AI technologies.
- An entrepreneurial thinker and go-getter, eager to tackle complex product challenges with creative and innovative ideas.
- A numbers-driven systems thinker, favoring a pragmatic and outcome-oriented process management style.
- Enjoys working on complex challenges in a collaborative, non-hierarchical setup, not afraid to step outside typical role confines.
- A positive spirit that fits seamlessly with a friendly team believing in a positive and fun work environment.
- Fluent in English and German (C2 level required for working with the German market).
