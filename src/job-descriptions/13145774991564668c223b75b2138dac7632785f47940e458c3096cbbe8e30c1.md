**TransferGo** is a growing fintech scale-up on a mission to make the world a fairer place for migrants. We strive to provide tailored, more affordable financial services that make people's hard-earned money go further. Now in our 13th year, with close to 400 employees in offices across Europe and the UK, we’re crafting a brilliant, relevant product that makes a difference in people's lives and the well-being of their families. 

We’ve come this far by building a talented, diverse workforce on a fair culture and our strong values. Having this strong team of employees, we can serve those who really need our product to make their lives better. 

We’re hiring a Junior Android Developer to strengthen our team and bring fresh perspectives. You’ll start by getting up to speed with our codebase and AI-powered workflows, then quickly contribute to UI improvements, bug fixes, and new features.

Our mission is to make every new user's first moments seamless and exciting. We own the registration flow and the journey to a customer’s first transaction — the “front door” to our product.

**Why you’ll enjoy working with us:**

*   **Direct Impact:** Your work will shape our most critical metrics: user growth and conversion.
*   **Modern Stack:** Kotlin-first development with Jetpack Compose and MVVM architecture.
*   **AI-Driven Development:** You’ll learn to leverage AI tools daily to build faster, smarter, and better.
*   **High Visibility:** The features you build will be seen by every new customer — and by our leadership team.

You’ll work closely with mid and senior developers, designers, product managers, and data analysts, learning first-hand how great products are built.

**What you’ll do**

*   **Utilise AI Development Tools:** Heavily leverage AI tools from your first day to accelerate development, generate code, write tests more effectively.
*   **Develop Features:** Write and maintain clean, efficient Kotlin code to build new application features.
*   **Implement UI:** Translate UI/UX designs into functional, high-quality interfaces using Jetpack Compose.
*   **Test & Debug:** Identify, troubleshoot, and fix bugs and other issues, while also writing unit tests for your code.
*   **Integrate APIs:** Connect the app to backend services by implementing and consuming RESTful APIs.
*   **Collaborate:** Use Git for version control, and work with the team to improve the product.

**What You’ll Bring**

**Must-haves:**

*   **Kotlin & Android Basics:** You have foundational knowledge of Kotlin and core Android concepts from personal projects or coursework. You don't need to be an expert, but you should understand the fundamentals.
*   **Familiarity with Jetpack Compose:** You have been introduced to Jetpack Compose and understand the basic idea of building UI with composable functions.
*   **Essential Tools:** You know how to use Git for version control and are comfortable finding your way around Android Studio.
*   **AI-Driven Eagerness to Learn:** Most importantly, you have a strong desire to learn. This includes a specific curiosity for mastering AI development tools, seeing them as essential for accelerating your growth and becoming a highly effective Android developer.

**Nice-to-haves:**

*   Experience with modularisation.
*   JetpackCompose.

**You are:**

*   Curious and eager to learn.
*   Proactive and able to find solutions independently.
*   Collaborative and open to feedback.
*   User-focused, with empathy for creating seamless experiences.
*   Resilient, humble, and growth-oriented.
