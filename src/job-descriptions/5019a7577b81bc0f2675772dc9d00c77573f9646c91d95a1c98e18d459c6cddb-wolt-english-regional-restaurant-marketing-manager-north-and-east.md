### Description\nWolt, a fast-growing tech company, is on a mission to make cities better places to live by connecting people with the best of their neighborhoods through a comprehensive delivery platform. Since its founding in 2014 and joining forces with DoorDash in 2022, Wolt operates in over 500 cities across 30 countries. The company fosters an exciting, challenging, and fun environment where self-starters with an entrepreneurial spirit can learn, build, and ship more.\n\nIn Germany, the marketing team is composed of passionate, impact-driven professionals. This role, **Regional Restaurant Marketing Manager**, offers a unique hybrid opportunity to shape Wolt's restaurant marketing strategy across **North and East Germany**. You will be at the forefront of strengthening Wolt's brand and commercial success by building impactful, on-the-ground restaurant marketing strategies tailored to the gastronomy scenes in cities like Hamburg, Leipzig, and Dresden, focusing on local market insights and partner collaboration to drive visibility and growth for key SMB Restaurant Partners.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a Regional Restaurant Marketing Manager, your day-to-day will involve:\n\n**Local Market Strategy & Partner Activation:**\n*   Developing and executing tailored restaurant marketing strategies across cities in North and East Germany, leveraging local market expertise.\n*   Identifying and capitalizing on hyper-local marketing opportunities such as city festivals, seasonal events, and cultural moments.\n*   Building and leveraging existing relationships with top-tier restaurant partners in the region to co-create exciting marketing activations.\n\n**Hands-On Campaign Execution:**\n*   Activating restaurant partners through on-the-ground support, from campaign planning to execution, to drive customer demand and visibility.\n*   Executing 360 marketing plans, including advertising across various channels (paid, owned, earned), promotions, and co-branded initiatives.\n*   Collaborating closely with the local Berlin-based Marketing team and agencies to adapt national strategies into localized initiatives.\n*   Managing regional/partner marketing budgets, ensuring efficient resource allocation and adherence to financial targets.\n\n**Partner Relationship Management:**\n*   Acting as a trusted marketing advisor to key restaurant partners, helping them understand and effectively use Wolt's marketing tools.\n*   Collaborating closely with account management and local sales teams to align on priorities, performance goals, and joint marketing opportunities.\n\n**Performance Reporting, Data Analysis & Insights:**\n*   Tracking, analyzing, optimizing, and reporting on marketing performance metrics at the regional and partner level.\n*   Utilizing data-based insights to optimize marketing strategies and improve return on investment (ROI).\n*   Providing feedback from the field to continuously improve and shape local marketing strategies.\n\n### Qualifications\nWe are looking for a candidate who meets the following expectations:\n\n*   Bachelor's or Master's degree in Marketing with 6-8 years of marketing experience and a proven track record in campaign execution and partner collaboration.\n*   Strong ties to the gastronomy scene in North and East Germany, acquired through previous roles in food-tech, e-commerce/marketplace, hospitality, FMCG, or lifestyle/urban marketing, are a significant plus.\n*   Demonstrated strategic thinking and a results-oriented mindset.\n*   A hands-on attitude, comfortable moving from strategy to execution, coordinating photoshoots, setting up pop-up activations, or meeting partners face-to-face.\n*   Bonus: Previous experience in sales or account management.\n*   Native German speaking and business fluent in English.\n*   Awareness of compliance and adaptability in a dynamic marketing landscape.\n*   Willingness to travel regularly in North and East Germany, with monthly visits to Berlin HQ.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned marketing professional to make a tangible impact at Wolt, a globally scaling tech company. The role offers the unique blend of strategic development and hands-on execution within a dynamic, fast-paced environment. You'll gain significant professional growth by building new vertical strategies and collaborating with a team of experts. Wolt emphasizes a culture of ownership, impact, and continuous learning, supported by flexible working options and a dog-friendly office. If you're a self-starter passionate about local gastronomy and driving growth, this role promises exciting challenges, immediate impact, and the chance to contribute to something truly exceptional.
