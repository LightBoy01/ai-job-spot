### Description
DeltaValue GmbH is actively seeking new team members for the role of Investment Analyst in the capital markets sector. As a state-certified and approved training company, we are dedicated to imparting knowledge about successful capital investment to our clients. Each year, we support hundreds of clients from Germany, Austria, and Switzerland on their financial journey, establishing ourselves as a leading provider in this field.

As an Investment Analyst, you will play a crucial role in supporting our research processes, overseeing and developing our products, structuring client portfolios, and evaluating specific trading ideas across stocks, options, and futures. Furthermore, you will engage directly with our clients to support the implementation and understanding of our strategies.

### Responsibilities
*   Perform regular stock screenings and company analyses.
*   Oversee our strategies in stock, options, and futures trading.
*   Support clients with portfolio structuring and asset allocation.
*   Assist our customer base with technical and didactic questions regarding the implementation of our strategies.
*   Create presentations, reports, evaluations, checklists, and guides for clients using standard Office applications.
*   Continuously develop personal knowledge and skills within the broader field of "Capital Markets."

### Qualifications
*   Quick comprehension and a strong sense of precision.
*   The ability to follow existing processes and execute them reliably.
*   Motivation to consistently deliver your best for our clients.
*   Professional demeanor and a stylish appearance.
*   Fluent German, both written and spoken.
*   Experience in personal capital investment on the stock market.
*   A completed degree in Finance or Asset Management is advantageous.
*   Experience in customer support is advantageous.
