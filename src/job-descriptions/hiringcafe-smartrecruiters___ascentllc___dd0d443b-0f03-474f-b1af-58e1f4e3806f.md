**Ascent** is a leading provider of comprehensive solutions for the planning, design, engineering, development, and operation of data centres and other mission critical facilities. Headquartered in Saint Louis, MO, Ascent specializes in powerful, efficient, and technologically advanced mission critical facilities and offers clients a broad range of delivery models spanning shell & core, build-to-suit, turnkey, and outsourced critical systems operations. Founded in 1998, Ascent provides a full spectrum of fast track services that include project programming, design management, facility assessment, development and construction management, commissioning, and ongoing maintenance operations. Today, Ascent focuses on providing clients with innovative solutions and services yielding the highest levels of operational autonomy, efficiency, and control. Ascent’s clients include some of the most demanding mission critical organizations spanning multiple industries, Fortune 500 firms, and service providers. Our business is growing fast and we are currently seeking a talented _**Project Superintendent – Data Centre / Industrial MEP**_ to join our team in Cambridge, Ontario on a 54 MW Expansion for AI Build.

**Job Description:**

**OPPORTUNITY DETAILS:**

**This will be a unique opportunity to join a team of technical subject matter experts delivering 54MW in the AI driven mission critical space, including complex power configurations and new industry technology involving liquid to chip cooling solutions.  This deployment will be one of the first major AI deliveries and one of the largest consumers of power in the region.**

**This is a project role with expected duration of 9-12 months.** 

**TITLE:** 

On-Site Superintendent – Data Centre / Industrial MEP Expansion - 9 - 12 Mos. Project 

**LOCATION:** 

Cambridge, ON Canada - On-Site

On-site Position - Must be able to report daily in person to the Data Centre location in Cambridge, ON. 

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

*   Responsible for field operations for assigned projects related to MEP
*   Directs the day-to-day onsite supervision of the field labor force, delegating work appropriately
*   Inspects Contractor or Subcontractor operations to ensure compliance with contract
*   Maintains good working relationships with clients, vendors, contractors/subcontractors, internal departments, and engineers
*   Works closely with the Construction Project Manager to ensure timely and profitable completion of each project
*   Works with the Project Manager to develop and maintain the site logistics plan
*   Reviews and approves Trade Contractor Payment Applications with Project Manager
*   Coordinates site testing and inspection efforts, including conducting quality inspections
*   Monitors costs, including labor, time and material
*   Demonstrates commitment to an injury-free environment, including communication of safety standards to labor force
*   Manages and monitors schedules to ensure all activities meet deadlines given
*   Prepares and submits Superintendent’s Daily Reports
*   Attends and participates in project meetings, including subcontractor meetings

**Qualifications:**

*   5+ years of experience as a Field Superintendent, Foreman, Lead, etc. or other related work and / or trade experience
*   Industry experience working with construction of data centres, industrial, healthcare or other mission critical facilities preferred
*   Working knowledge of Microsoft Office
*   High School Diploma or equivalent required
*   Strong organization, time management, and verbal/written communication skills
*   Must possess strong decision making, problem solving, leadership and management abilities
*   Familiarity with and ability to read and understand drawings and specifications
*   Working knowledge of terms and conditions of project contracts, subcontracts, and purchase orders
*   Ability to maintain discretion and confidentiality at all times
*   Ability to work independently and as a member of a cross functional team
*   Ability to travel as required by the project / client

**Additional Information:**

**WORKING CONDITIONS:**

*   Walking and/or standing for long periods of time will be required
*   Bending, stooping and lifting may be required
*   Periods of construction / equipment sound and noise where protective equipment will be required
*   Required to spend time concentrating which will require attention to detail and high levels of accuracy; and
*   Required to meet a number of deadlines which may cause stress

**Applicants must be legally entitled to work in Canada. Visa Sponsorship is not available for this role.** 

This position reports to the Sr. Site Superintendent.
