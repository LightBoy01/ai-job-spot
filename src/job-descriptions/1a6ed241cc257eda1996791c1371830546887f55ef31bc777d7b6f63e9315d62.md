**AI Solution Sales, Business Development Manager**
**Seoul, Korea**
At Dell Technologies, we create the extraordinary. Our AI Sales Biz Dev Executives are the experts who sell AI innovation to the world. Responsible for AI business growth & sales enablement, they get to know Dell Technology’s best of breed infrastructure solutions, Services capabilities, eco-system partnerships and GTM strategy to help drive large complex & transformational opportunities. Sales enablement is integral part of the role to train core sales & presales teams. Our core sales teams will rely on them for AI solutions advice during the sales process and advise customers on everything from AI solutions, services capabilities, manage eco-system partnerships, value proposition & TCO, through to commercials.
**Role Description:**
As an AI business development executive, you will proactively identify, qualify and work in partnership with sales executives in a matrix environment to sell comprehensive AI solutions based on a thorough understanding of both short- and long-term customer requirements. This includes, but is not limited to:
*   Discovery of customer requirements, in partnership with core sales
*   Jointly develop new accounts and expand existing accounts
*   Develop and implement AI solutions sales strategies in key accounts
*   Maintain effective customer relationships with key decision makers & influencers
*   Engage in customer presentations, focused seminars, solutions workshops & EBC
*   Competitive differentiation of Dell & eco-system partner AI solutions
*   Solution proposal development
*   Support customer RFI/RFP response
*   Sales support for customer product evaluations and Proof of Concept engagements
*   Field sales enablement, evangelize use-cases & best practices sharing
**Role Requirements:**
*   15+ years of related sales business development experience
*   Experience in training core sales and other specialty presales teams
*   Collaborate and work closely with Engineering teams, eco-system partners given the cross-functional nature of the role
*   10-12 years of deep technical expertise across multiple disciplines. Understanding of hardware designs for Servers, Storage, Networking of HPC and AI systems. Specific vertical experiences will also be a plus
*   Solution-selling skills with maniacal focus on business outcomes, solving customer problems, TCO and accounting techniques; comfortable talking about Server, Storage, Networking and Services offerings
*   Relationship-minded and build trust with key influencers in LOB, experienced in selling outside traditional IT
*   Use-case evangelist, develop POV on infrastructure with BU and Sales, bring credibility to Dell, able to enable Sales and provide Product Group feedback to refine offers
*   Strong local language & English communication skills
*   Minimum a bachelor’s degree in related field (desired)
