**Job Description Summary**
As part of the Project Management Office, you will help ensure flawless management and delivery of new product development, some E2E (consumer/customer impacts), sustainability, and other key projects. Working under the guidance of a Sr. Manager, you will help lead project teams to deliver projects on time and within budget, utilizing resources effectively and driving collaboration across functions.
The ideal candidate is detail-oriented and results-driven, thriving in cross-functional and multicultural team environments while managing multiple priorities. They possess strong organizational and analytical abilities, robust communication skills, and confidence with Microsoft Office and quickly learning new tools. With a proactive and flexible mindset, the candidate leads with empathy and adaptability.
**Essential Duties and Responsibilities**
*   **Project Planning and Documentation (30 %)**
*   Build project plans and monitor progress against timelines and budgets.
*   Assist in planning and scheduling project timelines and milestones.
*   Prepare and maintain project documentation (meeting minutes, action lists, plans etc.).
*   **Project Execution and Teams coordination (30%)**
*   Lead and organize cross-functional project teams.
*   Facilitate efficient meetings (project updates, risk assessments, etc.).
*   Direct project tasks and delegate responsibilities to team members.
*   Adapt project management processes for various priorities.
*   **Communication and Stakeholder management (20%)**
*   Communicate project updates, risks, and deviations to team members and stakeholders to ensure alignment and support.
*   Present critical topics and updates during Stage Gate and leadership meetings.
*   **Performance tracking & Continuous improvement (15%)**
*   Measure project performance using KPIs and analyze results.
*   Conduct post-project reviews, document key learnings and improvements need.
*   Maintain and improve project documentation (spreadsheets, process maps, etc.).
*   **Training and Support (5%)**
*   Support team members to encourage collaboration and ensure project success.
**Qualifications**
Minimum degree and experience:
*   Bachelor’s degree in engineering, Supply Chain/Operations Management, Business Management, or a related field is required.
*   Certified associate in project management (CAPM) preferred \[not required\]
*   At least 3 years of work experience required – in CPG and/or food manufacturing is a plus.
**Skills and Competencies**
**Team Collaboration & Leadership**
*   Results-oriented team player: Thrive in a diverse, multicultural, and fast-paced environment, actively seeking to support colleagues and achieve group objectives.
*   Lead by example: Demonstrate integrity, initiative, and a collaborative spirit; inspire trust and motivate project team members.
*   Empathetic cross-functional partner: Listen actively, maintain humility, and show understanding of team challenges; work cross-functionally to identify and implement solutions that serve the overall business objectives.
**2\. Communication & Stakeholder Management**
*   Effective communicator: Convey ideas and project updates clearly and professionally, both verbally and in writing, adapting style to audience and context.
*   Stakeholder engagement: Collaborate with internal stakeholders at all levels to gather requirements, share updates, and ensure ongoing alignment throughout the project lifecycle.
**3\. Organizational & Analytical Excellence**
*   Strong organizational skills: Maintain detailed project plans, schedules, and documentation with a high level of rigor and attention to detail.
*   Analytical mindset: Apply critical thinking to evaluate complex problems, analyze data, and propose actionable solutions.
*   Business acumen and growth mindset: Approach challenges with a forward-thinking attitude, identifying opportunities to support business priorities and drive continuous improvement.
**4\. Time Management & Flexibility**
*   Manage competing priorities: Assess urgency and importance across multiple assignments, efficiently prioritize workload, and consistently meet deadlines.
*   Flexible and quick learner: Adapt rapidly to changing project scopes, timelines, and priorities, remaining effective under pressure and open to new ways of working.
**5\. Technical Proficiency**
*   Microsoft Office expertise: Utilize MS Excel for project tracking and data analysis, MS Planner for Gantt charts and scheduling, and other MS Office tools for documentation and presentations.
*   Embrace technology and AI: Learn and leverage new tools and systems as needed to increase efficiency and project visibility.
