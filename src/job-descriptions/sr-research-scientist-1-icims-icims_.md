Job Description:
<p><strong>OUR TEAM:</strong>  We are a highly collaborative group of scientists dedicated to advancing the study of cell biology and cell health. Our research focuses on developing innovative tools to explore altered cell health and inflammatory processes, including senescence. By combining Promega’s powerful technologies with our deep expertise in assay development, we strive to meet the evolving needs of the research community. Looking ahead, we are expanding our use of cutting-edge AI/ML approaches to analyze complex data sets (e.g., transcriptomics, proteomics). These efforts aim to uncover new biological insights, build predictive models, and deliver next-generation assay systems and analysis tools that empower scientists across disciplines—from basic research to drug discovery.</p><p> </p><p><strong>YOUR ROLE:</strong>  As a Senior Scientist, you will play a pivotal role in uniting experimental assay development with advanced computational approaches to deliver biologically meaningful and analytically robust solutions. You will design and execute sophisticated experimental strategies, generate large-scale data sets, and harness AI/ML tools to analyze data, build predictive models, and uncover actionable insights. In this role, you will collaborate closely with cross-functional teams—including experts in transcriptomics, proteomics, quantitative biology, computational modeling, and assay development—to ensure our products integrate both biological depth and technological innovation. You will drive near-term assay development while laying the groundwork for transformative, systems-level capabilities that support Promega’s long-term strategic vision. Beyond project execution, you will have the opportunity to lead initiatives, mentor colleagues, and champion innovative ideas that position predictive modeling as a cornerstone of future product innovation.</p><p> </p><p><strong>YOUR EXPERIENCE:  </strong>You are a scientist with advanced training (Ph.D. or M.S. with substantial experience) in biochemistry, molecular biology, computational biology, or a related discipline, with proven expertise at the intersection of experimental and computational science.
