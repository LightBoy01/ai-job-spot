### Description\nIn the role of Online Marketing Manager, you will be responsible for calculating and managing the advertising budgets for our clients. Your primary goal is to achieve the best possible visibility within a given budget on platforms like Amazon. This involves regularly preparing detailed evaluations to communicate progress and results to clients, as well as strategically positioning products in desired advertising slots to maximize impact.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Strategy Development:** Develop SEA (Search Engine Advertising) strategies in close cooperation with our Account Managers to optimally exploit and expand advertising potential on Amazon.\n*   **Keyword Research & SEO Optimization:** Conduct comprehensive keyword research and perform on-page and off-page analyses to continuously improve campaign performance.\n*   **Campaign Optimization:** Maximize campaign potential through ongoing optimization and diligent monitoring of campaigns to achieve the best possible results.\n*   **Data Analysis & Action Planning:** Analyze campaign data using various analytical tools and derive concrete measures to enhance overall performance.\n*   **Reporting:** Set up and maintain clear, transparent reports to document campaign results and successes for clients.\n*   **Amazon Seller Central Management:** Utilize and manage Amazon Seller Central for effective monitoring and control of advertising campaigns.\n*   **PPC Optimization:** Adjust and optimize PPC (Pay-Per-Click) prices based on predefined strategies to maximize the efficiency and ROI of advertising efforts.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   **Commercial Education:** You have completed a commercial apprenticeship, ideally as a merchant for e-commerce or in a similar field.\n*   **Independent Work Style:** You are proactive and able to & Communication Skills:** You enjoy working collaboratively in a team and can express yourself clearly and precisely both in writing and orally.\n*   **Analytical Thinking:** You demonstrate a strong
