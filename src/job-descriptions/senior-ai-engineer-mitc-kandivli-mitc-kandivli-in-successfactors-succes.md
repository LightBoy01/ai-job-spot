About the Team:

<p>Mahindra AI Division is the AI innovation arm within Mahindra Group. The division experiments with state-of-the-art AI technologies and is focused on developing and delivering cutting-edge AI products to the group. With the division’s team-expansion plan, there is an excellent opportunity for your career growth in the team.</p>


<b>Responsibilities:</b>

<ul>
<li>Design, develop, and maintain <strong>backend services and APIs</strong> to support AI/ML applications at scale.</li>
<li>Productionize ML and deep learning models, ensuring efficiency, reliability, and low-latency inference.</li>
<li>Build scalable <strong>microservices and REST/gRPC APIs</strong> for deploying AI solutions in enterprise environments.</li>
<li>Optimize AI model serving using frameworks such as <strong>TensorRT, ONNX Runtime, Triton Inference Server, or FastAPI</strong>.</li>
<li>Implement MLOps practices including model versioning, monitoring, and automated deployments.</li>
<li>Collaborate with AI researchers, data scientists, and product managers to translate prototypes into production-ready systems.</li>
<li>Ensure robustness, fault tolerance, and security in backend AI systems.</li>
<li>Integrate AI services with enterprise data platforms, cloud systems, and third-party APIs.</li>
<li>Contribute to architecture discussions, design reviews, and performance tuning.</li>
<li>Mentor junior engineers and contribute to best practices in AI software engineering.</li>
</ul>


<b>Educational &amp; Experience requirements</b>

<ul>
<li>Bachelor’s degree with at least 7 years of experience or Master’s with at least 4 years of experience in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, or related fields.</li>
<li>Preferred to have degree from a Tier-1/2 institute (IIT/IISc/NITs if studied in India) or a globally top-ranked university (as per QS).</li>
</ul>


<b>Technical Requirements</b>

<ul>
<li>Strong proficiency in <strong>Python</strong> and backend frameworks (FastAPI).</li>
<li>Expertise in Prompt engineering and working with various LLMs<strong>.</strong></li>
<li>Experience in <strong>productionizing AI/ML models</strong> with efficient inference pipelines.</li>
<li>Hands-on experience with <strong>model deployment frameworks</strong> (Triton, TensorRT, TorchServe, ONNX Runtime).</li>
<li>Knowledge of <strong>cloud platforms</strong> (Azure, GCP) and container technologies (<strong>Docker</strong>).</li>
<li>Strong experience with <strong>microservices architecture, CI/CD pipelines, and monitoring tools</strong> (Prometheus, Grafana).</li>
<li>Familiarity with <strong>databases</strong> (SQL/NoSQL) and scalable data storage solutions.</li>
<li>Exposure to <strong>LLMs, SLMs, and GenAI model integration</strong> into backend systems is a strong plus.</li>
<li>Understanding of security, authentication, and performance optimization in large-scale systems.</li>
<li>Experience with version control (Git) and Agile development practices.</li>
</ul>


<b>Behavioral Requirements</b>
