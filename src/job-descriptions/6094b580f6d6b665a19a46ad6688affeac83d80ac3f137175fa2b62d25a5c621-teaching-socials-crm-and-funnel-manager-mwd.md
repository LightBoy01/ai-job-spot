### Description\nTeaching Socials is one of the fastest-growing consulting firms in the DACH region, specializing in supporting Personal Brands in the Finance & Real Estate sectors. We integrate reach, lead generation, and sales into a cohesive system. As the CRM & Funnel Manager, you will be at the heart of this system, taking end-to-end ownership of the CRM architecture. Your primary objective is to drive sales speed, minimize process friction, and ensure complete data transparency, directly contributing to measurable revenue impact through clean data, smart automations, and clear dashboards.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **CRM Strategy & Management:** Architect and manage CRM systems (Close, HubSpot, Pipedrive), defining pipelines, stages, properties, lead-routing, SLA rules, and ensuring robust data hygiene.\n*   **Automation & AI:** Design and implement complex automation workflows using Make/Zapier, including webhooks, lead scoring, auto-tasks, and multi-channel email/WhatsApp sequences.\n*   **System Integrations:** Establish and maintain bidirectional synchronizations and deduplicated datasets across various platforms such as Typeform, ManyChat, Calendly, Payment and Ads Platforms, Airtable, and Notion.\n*   **Analytics & Reporting:** Develop comprehensive conversion chain analysis and build dynamic dashboards in Excel/Sheets/Airtable, complete with actionable alerts.\n*   **Process Optimization:** Collaborate closely with Sales and Account Management teams to identify bottlenecks, create Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and playbooks, and conduct A/B tests to enhance efficiency and conversion rates.\n*   **Training & Enablement:** Provide internal enablement for Sales and Account Management teams through short guides, Looms, and playbooks, ensuring they are proficient and confident in using CRM systems.\n*   **Customer Workshops:** Conduct practical workshops and create short Loom guides for clients on CRM setup (Close/HubSpot), best practices, KPI dashboards, and daily utilization, including Q&A.\n*   **Funnel Building:** Develop landing pages/one-pagers (e.g., with Elementor), implement Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) tests, set up tracking, and ensure seamless handover to the CRM.\n*   **Google Workspace Management:** Manage structure, access rights, and automations within Google Sheets/Drive, and maintain a comprehensive template library.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   **Experience:** 3-5+ years of hands-on experience in RevOps, CRM, and/or Automation, ideally within Coaching, Finance, or Real Estate industries.\n*   **CRM Expertise:** Deep CRM know-how, with a strong preference for Close and experience with HubSpot.\n*   **Automation Proficiency:** Expert-level skills in Make or Zapier, including APIs, webhooks, error handling, and versioning.\n*   **Data & Analytics:** Very strong Excel/Sheets skills (Pivot, Query, Regex, Modeling) and extensive experience in dashboarding.\n*   **AI Integration:** Practical experience with AI applications for workflow acceleration (e.g., auto-tags, notes, scoring).\n*   **Data Mindset:** A clear understanding of data best practices including deduplication, mappings, schemas, and naming conventions.\n*   **Communication & Documentation:** Ability to create structured documentation (SOPs, Runbooks) and possess clear communication skills (DE C1+).\n*   **Profile:** A strong system thinker, meticulous in documentation, understands both sales and marketing perspectives, and embodies a \"builder-type\" mentality – quickly developing V1s, measuring results, and iterating.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a pivotal role for a highly proactive and technically adept CRM & Funnel Manager who thrives on ownership and direct impact within a fast-paced, growth-oriented environment. Teaching Socials is explicitly looking for someone who goes beyond mere tool operation; they expect a strategic builder who can architect, automate, analyze, and optimize end-to-end processes to drive tangible revenue growth. If you are a hands-on problem-solver with a strong data mindset, a knack for automation, and the ability to empower others, this role offers a significant opportunity to shape the core operational engine of a rapidly expanding consultancy. This isn't for those who prefer pure admin tasks or just reporting; it's for someone ready to build, measure, and iterate with a clear focus on results.
