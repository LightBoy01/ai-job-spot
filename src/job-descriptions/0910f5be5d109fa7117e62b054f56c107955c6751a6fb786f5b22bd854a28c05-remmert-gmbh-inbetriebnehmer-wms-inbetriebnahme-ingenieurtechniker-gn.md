### Description\nRemmert specializes in automated storage and logistics systems, providing innovative solutions for space-saving storage and fully automatic movement of materials. As a family-run company since 1945, we thrive in the future-oriented industry of Mechanical Engineering, Automation, and Logistics, fostering an environment of continuous development, open communication, and quick decision-making.\n\nWe are continuously growing and seeking a dedicated **Commissioning Engineer (gn)** to join our team. Based in Löhne, this role involves significant international travel (approx. 50% or 100 overnight stays annually) to customer sites worldwide, offering a dynamic and impactful opportunity.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Commissioning of software at national and international customer sites.\n- Installation, simulation, and testing of software.\n- Conducting training sessions for customers.\n- Configuration of databases and software systems.\n- Leading customer workshops for process definition and optimization.\n- Roll-out of software updates.\n- Replacement and adjustment of electrical components.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed vocational training in automation or information technology, or a comparable qualification.\n- Good knowledge of application installations, particularly within Windows environments.\n- Open to career starters or individuals with professional experience in a relevant field.\n- Affinity for control technology is desirable.\n- Strong communication skills in both German and English.\n- Independent and reliable work ethic.\n- Possession of a Class B driving license.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a motivated and technically-minded individual, from those starting their career to experienced professionals, to join a stable, family-owned leader in automated logistics. The role offers significant international exposure and a direct impact on customer success, balanced with a highly supportive company culture. Remmert prioritizes employee well-being, continuous development through structured training and regular reviews, and a strong sense of community with generous benefits including hybrid work, profit sharing, health management, and various employee events. If you're looking for a long-term, engaging role with global reach and a commitment to your growth, this could be your ideal next step.
