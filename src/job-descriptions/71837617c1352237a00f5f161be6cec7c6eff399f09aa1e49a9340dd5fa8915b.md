Your Team Responsibilities
--------------------------

The AI Engineering team is responsible for **acquiring, processing, and transforming unstructured market data** into structured, high-quality datasets for downstream consumption. Core responsibilities include:

*   **Data Acquisition**: Ingest unstructured data from financial documents, news, and websites at scale.
    
*   **AI/ML Processing**: Use ML, Generative AI, and Agentic AI to extract, validate, and enrich critical information.
    
*   **Structuring & Mapping**: Convert raw data into standardized formats and map entities for analytics and indices.
    
*   **Platform Engineering**: Build and operate AI platforms (DAP, semantic search, Vertex AI pipelines) with strong DevOps practices.
    
*   **Data Delivery**: Expose curated datasets via APIs and pipelines to support index creation, custom products, and analytics.
    
*   **Innovation**: Continuously improve accuracy, coverage, and automation using emerging AI/ML techniques.
    

Your Key Responsibilities
-------------------------

*   Desing and develop python and Agentic AI, LLMs , AI ML based systems
*   Deploy the application on GCP cloud through devops
*   Manage and maintain the application through various dashboards.
*   Create and use ML models/LLMs to consume unstructured data to classify, process and parse and make available to downstream

Your skills and experience that will help you excel
---------------------------------------------------

Tech : Python, Gen AI, LLMs, Agentic AI, Machine leaning, GCP, Azure cloud, Devops, NLP, Transformersm, Vector DBs
