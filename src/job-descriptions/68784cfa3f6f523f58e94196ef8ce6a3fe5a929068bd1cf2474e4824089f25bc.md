Perion is a global advertising technology company delivering solutions to the biggest brands and publishers around the globe across search, social media and display, video, CTV, and programmatic DOOH.
Home to an award-winning technology solution –– with our unique data-driven AI/ML based technologies, we deliver and optimize hundreds of terabytes of data and billions of events per day. We’re working with dozens of sources to provide a superior experience across screens and platforms, including mobile, video, social and native.
### **The Role**
We’re looking for a **multi-talented, lightning-fast Brand Designer** to join our growing team. You’ll bring ideas to life across a wide range of formats—PowerPoint, web, video, and beyond—and play a key role in shaping how Perion shows up in the world. If you live for smart design, love a fast-paced challenge, and know how to make complex things look simple and beautiful, keep reading.
### **What You’ll Do**
*   **Design jaw-dropping PowerPoint decks** that sell, persuade, and inspire—both client-facing and internal.
*   **Create, update, and maintain web pages** (experience in web design and custom HTML a must).
*   **Produce motion graphics and short videos** using After Effects and similar tools for product demos, marketing, and social.
*   **Collaborate with marketing, product, and sales teams** to bring our story to life across digital touchpoints.
*   **Push boundaries with AI tools** to accelerate production and experiment with creative solutions.
*   Build and iterate quickly with **Figma, Adobe Creative Suite**, **Midjourney,** and other similar platform tools.
### **What You Bring**
*   4–6 years of experience in a design or creative role, preferably within ad tech, digital media, or a fast-paced tech company or agency.
*   Expert-level PowerPoint design skills.
*   Proficient in web design and basic front-end development (HTML/CSS).
*   Strong skills in motion design/video editing, especially with After Effects.
*   Fluent in Figma and Adobe Creative Cloud (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, etc.).
*   Comfortable using or exploring AI creative tools (e.g., image/video generation, content automation).
*   Quick turnaround times during busy seasons — you work smart, move fast, and can juggle multiple projects.
*   Strong portfolio that shows you’re a creative thinker, visual storyteller, and design unicorn.
**Soft Skills That Set You Apart**
*   Self-starter mindset — you take ownership of your work and are naturally curious about trying new tools, processes, and ideas.
*   Adaptable and collaborative — you thrive in hybrid environments, whether in-office or remote, and enjoy working with global teammates across time zones.
*   Clear communicator with a proactive, solutions-first attitude.
### **Bonus Points**
*   Experience in B2B or SaaS marketing.
*   Familiarity with ad tech platforms or digital advertising trends.
*   Animation, 3D, UX design skills a plus.
*   WordPress experience an advantage.
### **Why Perion?**
*   Work at the intersection of creativity and technology in a high-growth company.
*   Join a team that values speed, smarts, and standout design.
*   Competitive salary, benefits, and flexibility
