Role Overview

The Vice President - Engineering is a strategic and hands-on leader responsible for driving the end-to-end development and delivery of innovative, AI-driven products, manage IT services delivery to its clients. This role demands a deep technical background, hands-on coding ability, and a proven track record of leveraging AI to build scalable and cutting-edge solutions. The ideal candidate will balance technical leadership with the ability to lead and inspire high-performing teams.

  

Key Responsibilities

*   Strategic Leadership:
    *   Define and execute the product engineering strategy, with a strong emphasis on AI and emerging technologies.
    *   Partner with executive leadership and Architecting teams to shape the company’s technical vision and align it with business objectives.
    *   Leverage emerging technologies to build a single technology platform for all products, using no code, low code philosophy
    *   Strong believer in AI to improve team productivity and ability to think big 
*   Hands-On Product Development:
    *   Actively contribute to architectural design and, where necessary, coding efforts to accelerate key projects.
    *   Oversee the design, development, and deployment of scalable, secure, and high-quality software products.
*   Hands-On Service Delivery:
    *   Engage with clients, understand their vision, deliver to their vision
    *   Wow clients with thought leadership and world class delivery
*   Team Leadership:
    *   Build, mentor, and lead a high-performing, multidisciplinary engineering team.
    *   Foster a culture of technical excellence, innovation, and accountability.
    *   Ensure team alignment with the organization's AI-driven product strategy.
*   Technology and Process Excellence:
    *   Implement and advocate best practices in software development, architecture.
    *   Ensure robust design, testing, and quality assurance methodologies across all product lines.
    *   Stay at the forefront of AI advancements and integrate cutting-edge techniques into product engineering processes.
*   Operational Efficiency:
    *   Streamline engineering workflows to improve speed to market and maximize productivity without compromising quality.
    *   Establish KPIs and metrics to track product engineering success and continuously improve outcomes.
    *   Efficiently manage resources, budgets, and timelines to align with strategic goals.

  

Technical Skills

*   Core Expertise:
    *   Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and building scalable cloud-native solutions.
*   DevOps and Automation:
    *   Knowledge of CI/CD pipelines and automation tools.
    *   Familiarity with containerization and orchestration tools like Kubernetes.
*   Cybersecurity and Compliance:
    *   Understanding of secure software development practices.
    *   Knowledge of cybersecurity principles and compliance standards (e.g., GDPR, ISO 27001).
*   Architectural Expertise:
    *   Ability to design scalable, microservices-based architectures.
    *   Hands-on experience with API design and integration.

  

Qualifications

*   Education:
    *   Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Engineering, or a related field; MBA or advanced degree preferred.
*   Experience:
    *   15+ years of experience in product engineering, with a minimum of 5 years in leadership roles.
    *   Proven hands-on experience in building and deploying AI/ML-driven solutions.
    *   Strong track record of delivering large-scale, cloud-native software products in a fast-paced environment.
*   Leadership Skills:
    *   Exceptional technical leadership with the ability to inspire and mentor engineers.
    *   Strong communication and collaboration skills to influence stakeholders and executive teams.

  

Key Competencies

*   Visionary thinking with a focus on AI and future-ready technologies.
*   Proven ability to lead by example, with a strong hands-on approach to technical challenges.
*   Deep understanding of the intersection of business needs and technical solutions.
*   Adaptability to evolving priorities in a fast-paced environment.
