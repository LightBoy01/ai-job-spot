**Company Description:**

Wix makes it possible for anyone to succeed online. Since 2006, we’ve grown to around 5,300 employees in 22 countries, launched over 40 products, and serve over 282 million users and their visitors worldwide. At Wix, we push you to innovate, evolve in non-traditional ways, and collaborate. We operate in small teams that work closely together at a high level. 

Although we’re already industry leaders in web creation and business, our work is nowhere near done. Our goal is to become the main engine of the Internet so we can create a more open web for all.

We're proud to be an equal opportunity employer. Wix was built around the idea that everyone has the right to be successful, online. This same vision defines us as an employer: creating a work environment where everyone is welcome, and anyone has the right to succeed.

**Job Description:**

As a frontend developer at Wix Components, you'll play a key role in shaping the Wix platform by developing robust infrastructure that enables the creation of high-quality, reusable components, which enhance the user experience for millions of users globally. In your day-to-day, you will: 

• Shape and develop Wix’s Components Platform by building a powerful infrastructure that extends Wix's capabilities

• Develop high-quality, reusable components that enhance the Wix platform 

• Collaborate with Product Managers, UX Designers, and Product Designers to meet and exceed user needs 

• Design and maintain the infrastructure supporting component integration across the Wix ecosystem

• Incorporate AI-powered tools into your development workflow to boost productivity, from code generation and testing to documentation

• Influence millions of Wix users who have built over 500 million websites, serving billions of users globally.

**Qualifications:**

Requirements

*   5+ years of experience in frontend development 
    
*   Experience in developing high-quality UI and providing an exceptional developer experience
    
*   Strong proficiency in modern web technologies, including JavaScript, TypeScript
    
*   Excellent communication skills, adept at working with other engineering teams, product, and diverse stakeholders, to achieve shared goals
    
*   Open-minded, ready to tackle challenges, and comfortable to leverage AI to enhance quality and productivity 
    
*   Passionate about technology, UI, and innovation
    

**Additional Information:**

We are the Wix Components Group. We create the building blocks that allow Wix’s internal organization and hundreds of millions of users to design and build products, sites, and apps. Our team designs and maintains the core UI components that form the foundation of every Wix site and application. We also develop cross-cutting features, such as component CSS customization, RTL support, accessibility enhancements and many more.

Our products are user-facing, and our challenge is to ensure everything we create works seamlessly across all platforms and provides the best value to all users. We've been focusing on shaping a platformized infrastructure that extends Wix’s capabilities and supports innovative component development. This open platform approach is a unique opportunity to be part of a team that's redefining how developers integrate with Wix.

As part of the Wix Frontend Development (FED) Guild, we collaborate closely with product managers, UX designers, and developers across Wix to ensure seamless integration and exceptional performance across the Wix ecosystem.
