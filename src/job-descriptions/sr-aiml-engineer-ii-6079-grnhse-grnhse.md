<p>If you think you can see yourself delivering our mission and pursuing our goals with us, then check out the job description below!</p>
<p><strong>What you’ll do:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>You'll analyze complex datasets to assess performance or find new patterns</li>
<li>You'll build, train, operationalize and maintain AI/ML models in production</li>
<li>You'll apply machine learning techniques to data sets related to immigration</li>
<li>You'll present results to a diverse audience in presentation or report form</li>
<li>You'll support architectural leadership, technical support, and advisement services to ensure identity management system technologies are integrated and meeting the appropriate security requirements</li>
<li>You’ll support leadership who engage with senior level executives at a public facing Federal agency and provide subject matter expertise in security architecture and other key domain areas</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>What you’ll need to succeed:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Minimum 7 years of experience</li>
<li>Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience</li>
<li>US citizenship is required for this position in order to obtain and maintain a TS/SCI with CI Polygraph</li>
<li>You have experience using open-source models to the largest extent possible or provide a comparable analysis and justification for using proprietary models</li>
<li>Strong experience with one or more of the following AI/ML methods: Generative AI/LLMs, NLP, RAG, and neural networks.</li>
<li>At least 5 years of experience with one or more of the following technologies: Python, R, Anaconda, Numpy, TensorFlow, Keras, SciKit-Learn, and Postgres</li>
<li>You have at least 5 years of experience designing, implementing, and maintaining data architecture and services to be used for AI/ML. Additionally, able to maintain the AI/ML models through user stories and DevSecOps CI/CD pipeline including any optimization and evaluation of alternatives</li>
<li>Strong experience designing, developing, and deploying AI/ML solutions into classified AWS cloud environments at IL6+.</li>
<li>You have the ability to perform data analytics on program related or system related activities. This will include assessing performance and manual processes implementing methods/algorithms to automate and optimize</li>
</ul>
