**Job Summary**

As the Team Lead for Platform Engineering at Unframe, you’ll play a critical role in shaping the foundation of our AI platform. You’ll lead a team of Full Stack Engineers focused on building reusable AI services, scalable APIs, a dynamic UI engine, and shared infrastructure that powers every solution we deliver. This is a hands-on leadership role - you’ll guide architecture and execution while mentoring engineers and ensuring technical excellence.

**Responsibilities**

*   Lead and mentor a team of Full Stack Engineers, fostering a collaborative, high-performance culture.
*   Drive the design and implementation of core AI building blocks, backend services, and frontend components.
*   Own platform-wide features such as user management, multi-tenant abstractions, and solution dashboards.
*   Collaborate cross-functionally with Product, AI, and Solutions teams to ensure the platform meets business and technical goals.
*   Define and enforce best practices for code quality, testing, CI/CD, and system architecture.

**Requirements**

*   6+ years of experience in software engineering, including 3+ years in a technical leadership or team lead role.
*   Background in AI/ML, LLMs, or prompt-based applications.
*   Proven ability to design scalable APIs, services, and reusable UI components.
*   Strong proficiency in modern frontend and backend frameworks (e.g. React, Node.js, Python, etc.).
*   Experience building platforms or working in complex, multi-solution product environments.
*   Strong communication and collaboration skills, with experience leading cross-functional initiatives.

**Nice to Have**

*   Experience with design systems, multi-tenant architecture, or enterprise-grade platforms.
*   Familiarity with backend performance optimization, observability, and database design.

If you’re a strong, independent builder who thrives on ownership, cares deeply about quality, and wants to have a real impact — we’d love to meet you.
