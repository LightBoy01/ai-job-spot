### Description
As a Creative Strategist, you will develop high-performing ideas and concepts for our advertising campaigns. You will analyze target audiences, identify the best creative angles, and work closely with Copy and Design to develop strong hooks, ads, videos, and landing page concepts. Your work ensures our campaigns scale, acquire new customers, and our brands – especially _nedura_ – grow creatively and effectively. We are looking for someone to start immediately.

### Responsibilities
- Developing creative concepts for Meta, Google, and Native Ads.
- Analyzing target audiences, market trends, and data to derive strong creative angles.
- Collaborating with Copy & Design to create high-performing ads, hooks, headlines, and video concepts, with a focus on Direct Response.
- Conceptualizing compelling advertorials and conversion-oriented landing pages.
- Strategically supporting complete funnels (Top-, Middle-, Bottom-of-Funnel).
- Planning and evaluating A/B tests – continuous optimization of creatives.

### Qualifications
- 2-3 years of experience in Creative Strategy, Performance Marketing, or Direct Response.
- Strong understanding of customer psychology, messaging, and sales-boosting creatives.
- Experience in creating or optimizing ads (Meta, Google, Native) – including hooks, headlines, and concepts.
- Ability to derive creative decisions from data (CTR, CVR, scroll-stoppers, hook performance).
- Basic understanding of funnel structures (TOF/MOF/BOF) and conversion mechanics.
- Experience collaborating with design and copy teams.
- Creative thinking combined with a structured, reliable working approach.
- Bonus: Experience in D2C, dietary supplements, or the health sector.
