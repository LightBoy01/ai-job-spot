**Digitalization and energy transition in one sentence?** That’s what we do at Green Fusion!

Our software optimizes heating systems in the real estate sector, **helping to combat climate change through digitalization and automation. We reduce emissions and energy consumption, actively advancing the energy transition.**

As a **(Senior) Process Operations Manager in Engineering,** you will own the end-to-end building-optimization process—bringing clarity, speed, and predictability to a critical cross-functional workflow as we scale.

Tasks
-----

**In this role, you will:**

*   **Own the end-to-end optimization funnel** — spanning remote and on-site workflows — including readiness, prioritization, execution, and verification.
*   **Maintain the single source of truth for optimization** and lead weekly funnel reviews, sequencing, and progress tracking.
*   **Manage approvals, exceptions, and cross-team escalations** with clearly defined SLAs and timely communication.
*   **Publish and communicate weekly KPIs**, including throughput, approval times, data completeness, and exceptions.
*   **Support annual, quarterly, and weekly planning** by producing data-driven forecasting for optimization throughput.
*   **Partner with Sales and Customer Success** to ensure all new deals are properly onboarded into the optimization funnel — validating readiness, controller coverage, and constraints, and ensuring a smooth transition into execution.
*   **Collaborate with System Engineering and Product** to assess technical feasibility, resolve blockers, define next steps, and identify opportunities to improve or automate components of the optimization process while maintaining day-to-day operational ownership.

Requirements
------------

We know that nobody fits a job description 100%. If you see yourself in most of these points and are passionate about our mission, we’d love to hear from you!

*   You have **4–6+ years of experience** in operations, delivery management, or process ownership within a tech, SaaS, or IoT environment.
*   You **excel at owning complex cross-functional processes end-to-end** — from onboarding through planning, execution, and verification.
*   You bring **strong analytical skills** and are comfortable working with data, including funnel KPIs, dashboards, and reporting.
*   You **communicate effectively and can translate seamlessly** between Product, Engineering, Customer Success, Sales, and Operations.
*   You can **independently** prioritize, sequence work, and make thoughtful trade-off decisions.
*   You **navigate changing environments** with ease and bring clear structure to the work.
*   You **stay effective** during high-volume periods and **act with urgency** when needed.
