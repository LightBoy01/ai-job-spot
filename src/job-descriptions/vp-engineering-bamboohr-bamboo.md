<p>Role Overview</p>
<p>The Vice President - Engineering is a strategic and hands-on leader responsible for driving the end-to-end development and delivery of innovative, AI-driven products, manage IT services delivery to its clients. This role demands a deep technical background, hands-on coding ability, and a proven track record of leveraging AI to build scalable and cutting-edge solutions. The ideal candidate will balance technical leadership with the ability to lead and inspire high-performing teams.</p>
<p><br></p>
<p>Key Responsibilities</p>
<ul>
<li>Strategic Leadership:
<ul>
<li>Define and execute the product engineering strategy, with a strong emphasis on AI and emerging technologies.</li>
<li>Partner with executive leadership and Architecting teams to shape the company’s technical vision and align it with business objectives.</li>
<li>Leverage emerging technologies to build a single technology platform for all products, using no code, low code philosophy</li>
<li>Strong believer in AI to improve team productivity and ability to think big </li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Hands-On Product Development:
<ul>
<li>Actively contribute to architectural design and, where necessary, coding efforts to accelerate key projects.</li>
<li>Oversee the design, development, and deployment of scalable, secure, and high-quality software products.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Hands-On Service Delivery:
<ul>
<li>Engage with clients, understand their vision, deliver to their vision</li>
<li>Wow clients with thought leadership and world class delivery</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Team Leadership:
<ul>
<li>Build, mentor, and lead a high-performing, multidisciplinary engineering team.</li>
<li>Foster a culture of technical excellence, innovation, and accountability.</li>
<li>Ensure team alignment with the organization's AI-driven product strategy.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Technology and Process Excellence:
<ul>
<li>Implement and advocate best practices in software development, architecture.</li>
<li>Ensure robust design, testing, and quality assurance methodologies across all product lines.</li>
<li>Stay at the forefront of AI advancements and integrate cutting-edge techniques into product engineering processes.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Operational Efficiency:
<ul>
<li>Streamline engineering workflows to improve speed to market and maximize productivity without compromising quality.</li>
<li>Establish KPIs and metrics to track product engineering success and continuously improve outcomes.</li>
<li>Efficiently manage resources, budgets, and timelines to align with strategic goals.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
