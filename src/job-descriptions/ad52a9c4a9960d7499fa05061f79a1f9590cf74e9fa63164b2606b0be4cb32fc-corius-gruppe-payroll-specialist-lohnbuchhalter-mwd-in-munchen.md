### Description\nAre you an experienced Payroll Specialist with extensive practical knowledge of DATEV LODAS, seeking a new challenge in a future-proof industry? We are looking for an independent and service-oriented professional to strengthen our HR department as a **Payroll Specialist / Lohnbuchhalter:in**. This is a permanent position available in full or part-time (min. 25 hours/week), starting as soon as possible. CORIUS is a rapidly growing, supra-regional network of partner practices and clinics, committed to medical excellence in high-quality dermatological care. Innovative, research-driven, and holistic, we advance dermatological services for the well-being of our patients, always at the forefront of current medical and aesthetic findings. We are expanding quickly with many new ideas and projects across Germany, Switzerland, and Europe.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Independently perform payroll accounting for several CORIUS practices using DATEV LODAS, ensuring continuous updates of master and movement data.\n- Reliably manage reporting (Meldewesen) and create certificates. Correspond with health insurance companies, authorities, and social security institutions.\n- Serve as the expert contact person for all payroll-related questions for our HR team and other internal interfaces (e.g., Accounting).\n- Create statistics and maintain internal databases and tables.\n- Provide support to the HR team with administrative tasks as needed or as a substitute, such as managing absences, and preparing employment references, certificates, and contracts.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed commercial training or a degree (e.g., in accounting/tax consulting) and possess several years of professional experience in independently performing payroll accounting.\n- At least three years of practical experience with our payroll program DATEV LODAS.\n- Excellent knowledge of payroll tax, social security, and labor law, continuously staying up-to-date with relevant regulations.\n- Confident communication skills when interacting with diverse target groups and various hierarchical levels.\n- A self-reliant, structured, goal-oriented, and solution-focused working style.\n- Ideally, practical experience in HR administration and preparing related documents (e.g., employment contracts and references).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a compelling opportunity for a seasoned Payroll Specialist to join a rapidly expanding healthcare group. The role offers significant autonomy within a broad scope of responsibilities, coupled with a supportive and collaborative team environment. CORIUS prioritizes employee well-being with excellent work-life balance benefits, including hybrid work, 30 days of holiday, flexible hours, and comprehensive benefits like bike leasing. The company is committed to professional growth through individual training options, making it ideal for someone looking to apply their expertise and evolve within a dynamic organization.
