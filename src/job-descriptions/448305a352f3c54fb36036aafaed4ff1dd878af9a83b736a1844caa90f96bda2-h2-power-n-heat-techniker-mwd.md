### Description\nH2 Power 'n' Heat GmbH is an innovative energy startup based in the Berlin metropolitan region, dedicated to pioneering green hydrogen solutions. Our flagship product, the Power Box, is a compact, modular fuel cell system designed to replace diesel generators with an emissions-free, efficient, and quiet alternative. This technology is already being deployed in mobile and stationary applications, from construction sites and events to remote buildings.\n\nAfter three years of intensive development and a successful market launch, we are expanding our test and development environment. We are looking for a technically strong Test Technician to strengthen our team at the test stand. This is a unique opportunity to work on future-proof systems, building and maintaining our fuel cell test stands, testing prototypes, and seeing your work directly impact real-world applications.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a Test Technician (m/w/d), you will support us with the following tasks:\n\n-   Preparation and execution of test stand experiments\n-   Accompanying documentation for experiments\n-   Collaboration on the automation of test runs\n-   Installation and commissioning of measurement technology\n-   Conversion and repair work in the test stand area\n-   Construction of prototype test vehicles, including wiring\n-   Maintenance of test setups and customer systems\n\nYou will spend approximately 70-80% of your time practically at the test stand / on the system, and 20-30% on PC-based tasks and documentation.\n\nAdditionally, you will benefit from:\n-   Close collaboration with our engineering team\n-   Insight into the development of modern fuel cell technology\n\n### Qualifications\nYou have successfully completed vocational training as a Technician, Mechatronics Technician, Plant or Industrial Mechanic, or Automotive Mechanic, and can demonstrate initial professional experience. Ideally, you bring the following:\n\n-   Very good technical understanding combined with manual dexterity\n-   Basic knowledge of electrical engineering\n-   Basic knowledge in the use of application and measurement technology\n-   Willingness for independent \"on-the-job\" further training\n-   Strong communication skills and high team orientation\n-   Sense of responsibility and ability to work independently\n-   Willingness to travel for maintenance tasks\n\n**Not a must, but a plus:**\n-   Experience with test stands or test setups\n-   Experience in handling safety-relevant systems\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a motivated Test Technician to join a dynamic and innovative energy startup at the forefront of green hydrogen technology. H2 Power 'n' Heat GmbH offers a clear mission to replace diesel with scalable H2 solutions, providing a role with tangible impact and direct influence on product development. The company fosters a collaborative environment with short decision paths, transparent communication, and a strong focus on employee growth and development within the technical field. You'll work with modern equipment, benefit from employee perks like JobRad-Leasing and BVG-Jobticket, and enjoy a healthy work-life balance with no shift or weekend work. If you're passionate about sustainable technology and eager to make a real difference, this role offers a performance-based compensation package with excellent development perspectives in the vibrant Berlin startup ecosystem.
