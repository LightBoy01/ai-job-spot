Job Description
---------------
Capgemini is seeking a Director level executive to
*   **Delivery Management**: Lead and manage the delivery of multiple projects/programs across geographies or business units, ensuring timely, quality, and cost-effective execution.
*   **AI-Enabled Program Oversight:** Drive the use of AI models, intelligent automation, predictive analytics, and generative AI to enhance delivery speed, quality, and decision-making. Collaborate with AI/ML teams to ensure solution feasibility, deployment readiness, and business impact realization.
*   **Client Engagement:** Serve as the primary point of contact for clients on delivery matters. Build strong client relationships, manage expectations, and ensure customer satisfaction.
*   **Team Leadership:** Lead and mentor project managers, tech leads, and other delivery staff. Foster a culture of ownership, accountability, and continuous improvement.
*   **Governance & Reporting:** Define and enforce delivery governance frameworks. Track key delivery metrics (e.g., velocity, quality, defect leakage, SLA adherence) and provide regular updates to leadership.
*   **Risk & Issue Management:** Proactively identify risks and issues in delivery. Lead mitigation strategies and communicate impacts effectively to internal and external stakeholders.
*   **Process Excellence:** Drive adoption of Agile, DevOps, and other delivery best practices. Continuously improve delivery efficiency, quality, and responsiveness.
*   **Financial Oversight:** Manage budgets, forecasts, and profitability of delivery engagements. Ensure resource optimization and cost control.
*   **Collaboration:** Work closely with Sales, Pre-sales, Practice, HR, and Finance teams to support growth, staffing, and operational alignment.
Our Ideal Candidate
-------------------
They will have 15-20 years of experience with a strong project track record and demonstrated P&L contribution to sales. A Bachelor’s degree is required; MBA is strongly preferred.
This person is motivated to deliver high-quality work to drive customer satisfaction. They use their intellect, insight, and ability to build creative solutions with clients and colleagues while embracing a collaborative mindset. Key qualifications include:
• **Domain:** Banking, Financial Services & Insurance only
• Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or related field; MBA is a plus
• 3-5 years in a leadership or executive delivery role
• Proven track record of managing complex, large-scale technology programs or portfolios
• Excellent client-facing, communication, and negotiation skills
• Deep understanding of project delivery methodologies (Agile, Scrum, Waterfall, Hybrid)
• Strong knowledge of SDLC, quality frameworks, and IT operations
• Ability to manage geographically distributed teams
• Experience in AI
**Nice to Have:**
• PMP / SAFe / ITIL or similar certifications
• Experience in cloud migration, digital transformation, or product implementation
• Prior experience working in a global delivery model (onsite-offshore)
