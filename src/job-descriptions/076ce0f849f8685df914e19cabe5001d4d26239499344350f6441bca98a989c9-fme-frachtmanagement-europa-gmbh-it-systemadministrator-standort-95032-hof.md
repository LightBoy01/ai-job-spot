### Description
This is a family-run, medium-sized forwarding company with locations in Cologne, Leipzig, Dortmund, Hof, and Thörey. We specialize in organizing Europe-wide partial and full loads, providing comprehensive transport solutions to our clients. In 2024, we organized approximately 60,000 direct transports, achieving a turnover of €37 million. We are continuously growing and have been repeatedly recognized by FOCUS magazine as a \\\"**TOP Employer**\\\" and, for the fifth consecutive time, as a \\\"**Growth Champion**\\\". As an IT System Administrator, you will operate within flat hierarchies with significant scope for creativity, playing a central role in ensuring that 160 colleagues across all locations can work reliably.

### Responsibilities
The main location for this role is 95032 Hof, with occasional deployments to other branches in Leipzig and 99334 Thörey (approximately twice per quarter).

- Administer and support the Microsoft 365 environment, including Azure and Intune.
- Manage the local Active Directory and server infrastructure.
- Set up, configure, and maintain VPN connections and networks.
- Provide support for the 3CX telephone system.
- Perform Endpoint Management using NinjaOne.
- Administer the vSphere virtualization environment.
- Support and maintain MS SQL databases.
- Deliver First-Level-Support to approximately 160 users.
- Handle user administration, PC setup, and system maintenance.
- Take on Quality Management (QM) and security tasks.
- Conduct device testing and IT security controls at branch locations.

### Qualifications
- Completed IT training or a comparable qualification.
- Profound knowledge in Microsoft 365, Azure, and Intune.
- Experience with Active Directory and Windows Server environments.
- Knowledge of VPN and network administration.
- Experience with (3CX) telephone systems.
- Knowledge of NinjaOne or comparable RMM tools.
- Experience with VMware vSphere.
- Basic knowledge of MS SQL databases.
- Experience with MailStore (email archiving).
- Driving license Class B and willingness to travel for assignments in Hof/Leipzig.
- Service orientation, a structured working approach, and clear communication – even with non-IT colleagues.

**Nice-to-have (Bonus Skills):**
- Experience with logistics software.
- Basic knowledge of DATEV.
- Experience in using AI tools in daily work.
- Interest in automation (e.g., n8n).
- Initial exposure to Power BI.
- Experience with HubSpot or similar CRM systems.
