**What you’ll do:**

Position title: SAP iXp Intern – AI Demo Build & Joule Agent Developer

Location: Houston Office or Remote

Contract Duration: 6 months

Working Hours: 20-40 hours/week

As an SAP Business AI iXp Intern, you will be part of the Solution & Industries Experience Demo Build Team. Your focus will be on documenting, testing, and developing Business AI scenarios and Joule use cases that showcase SAP’s next-generation AI capabilities. Key deliverables include:

• Well-structured documentation of AI-driven demo scenarios.

• Testing and validation of Joule-based skills and workflows.

• Creation of custom Joule skills to extend SAP’s AI assistant.

• Contribution to building and refining AI Agents for industry-relevant use cases.

This role directly contributes to customer-facing demos that highlight SAP’s differentiation in the AI space. The intern’s work will:

• Enable compelling demonstrations of SAP Business AI and Joule to customers and partners.

• Drive customer confidence in SAP’s AI roadmap by showcasing practical and impactful scenarios.

• Accelerate solution adoption by helping create demo-ready content that resonates with industry-specific business challenges.

**What you bring:**

We’re looking for someone who takes initiative, perseveres, and stays curious. You enjoy working on innovative projects and are energized by continuous learning.

• Educational requirements: Min. Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Science, Business, or related field.

• Eligibility: Must be currently enrolled, or recently graduated (start date must be within 6 months of graduation date) from a coding academy/bootcamp, apprenticeship, associate, bachelor’s, master’s or JD/PhD program

• Strong communication skills (both written and oral).

• Interest in AI, machine learning, and enterprise applications.

• Familiarity with SAP solutions or willingness to learn.

• Exposure to conversational AI/chatbots, or experience with scripting (Python, JavaScript, or equivalent).

• Knowledge of ABAP, BTP is preferred

• Analytical mindset with the ability to document clearly and test systematically.

• Strong interpersonal and collaboration skills.

• Ability to learn quickly and contribute to global demo initiatives.
