### Description\nBW PARTNER is a leading, independent auditing and tax consulting firm in Baden-Württemberg, home to over 300 professionals. They pride themselves on delivering high-level consulting while fostering a supportive and cohesive environment. This dual study program in Business and Tax Law, in cooperation with DHBW Stuttgart, offers a unique opportunity to build a strong foundation for your career. Starting on 01.10.2026, the program combines practical phases within the firm with theoretical studies at the university, providing a diverse and exciting mix of activities.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a dual student, you will be involved in a variety of tasks, gaining hands-on experience across different areas (depending on the season and project load):\n- Support in design consulting and business administration consulting.\n- Assistance with annual financial statements and corporate tax returns.\n- Contribution to special tax projects, for instance, in international tax law or in the field of inheritance and gifts.\n- Undertaking diverse research activities related to tax matters.\n\n### Qualifications\nTo succeed in this role and integrate as a valuable team member, BW PARTNER looks for individuals who possess:\n- A successfully completed Abitur (German higher education entrance qualification) or equivalent Fachhochschulreife.\n- A strong understanding of economic contexts and a good aptitude for working with numbers.\n- An appreciation for a personal and respectful interaction style within a modern work environment.\n- The ability to handle trust and freedom responsibly, consistently delivering good performance.\n- An open and positive attitude towards digitalization and continuous changes.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis dual study program at BW PARTNER offers an exceptional entry point into the world of tax consulting and auditing. The company's commitment to employee development is evident through its robust mentorship program, emphasis on continuous learning, and supportive 'Duz-Kultur'. Students benefit from a highly integrated curriculum that blends theoretical knowledge with practical, client-facing experience from day one. With attractive compensation, flexible working arrangements (including home office options), and a comprehensive benefits package, BW PARTNER clearly values the well-being and long-term career growth of its team members. This is an ideal opportunity for ambitious students seeking a modern, collaborative, and forward-thinking environment to kickstart their professional journey.
