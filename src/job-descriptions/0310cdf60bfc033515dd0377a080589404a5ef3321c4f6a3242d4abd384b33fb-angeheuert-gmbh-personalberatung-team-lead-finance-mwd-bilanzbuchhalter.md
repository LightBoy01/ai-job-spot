### Description\nJoin a rapidly growing company near Trier, Föhren, deeply committed to renewable energies. As **Team Lead Finance**, you will take full responsibility for the entire financial accounting, from specialist leadership to process development. This is a dynamic role offering diverse tasks with a strong focus on balance sheets within a sustainable environment characterized by flat hierarchies. We value long-term development, modern working conditions, and personal freedom. This position offers flexible, hybrid work options, including part-time from 30 hours, ensuring a healthy work-life balance.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Lead the financial accounting department in accordance with HGB, bringing your expertise to professionalize structures, digitize processes, and further develop your team.\n- Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements.\n- Act as the primary contact for tax advisors, banks, and auditors.\n- Oversee consolidation, intercompany reconciliations, and ensure tax compliance.\n- Continuously develop accounting processes and implement digital tools.\n- Collaborate closely with the commercial director and adjacent departments.\n- **Optional:** Contribute to controlling activities such as cost center and project controlling.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed commercial training or a degree in finance.\n- Successfully passed the certified accountant (Bilanzbuchhalter) examination.\n- Approximately 10 years of professional experience in accounting, with strong closing certainty according to HGB.\n- A genuine passion for team leadership and team development.\n- Proven experience with consolidation, intercompany processes, and ERP systems (e.g., SAP).\n- Analytical mindset, precise working methods, and a strong drive for continuous improvement.\n- **Nice to have:** Experience with IFRS, project controlling, group structures, or prior work in the renewable energy sector.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for an experienced and passionate finance professional to step into a **Lead** role within a highly impactful and rapidly expanding company in the renewable energy sector. The position uniquely blends strategic financial leadership with hands-on process optimization and team development. The company culture is genuinely forward-thinking, emphasizing flexible work (hybrid, part-time), continuous learning, and a supportive, familial atmosphere evident in perks like welcome office dogs and regular team events. With a clear mission for 100% green energy and a generous benefits package including a competitive salary, comprehensive wellness programs, and a buddy onboarding system, this role offers both professional challenge and exceptional personal fulfillment. If you're ready to lead, innovate, and contribute to a sustainable future, this is an opportunity not to be missed.
