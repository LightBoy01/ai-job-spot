**Seeking Engineering Interns!** We are seeking a talented and motivated individual to join our Verification Test System Design Organization as a Systems Engineer - Test Architect. In this role, you will be responsible for creating system architectures to support verification and test of engine development programs. You will work closely with cross-functional engineering teams to create and integrate models as part of the systems engineering development process. 

**What You Will Do**

*   Responsibilities may include working on tasks related to commercial engines, military engines or both depending on business need. May be required to obtain a security clearance. ​
*   Developing familiarity with Model-Based Systems Engineering, SysML (graphical modeling language) and Magic Grid methodology
*   Gaining exposure to jet propulsion design and test basics
*   Learning, refining, and creating Verification and Validation MBSE frameworks and methods – can include optimizing with coded scripts or AI tools
*   Supporting the creation of Verification/Validation models for programs as needed
*   Creating model libraries to support future engine program modeling efforts
*   Being a change agent promoting disciplined, model-based systems engineering within the Validation organization and throughout Pratt & Whitney

**What You Will Learn**

*   Engineering Interns at Pratt & Whitney work on challenging, meaningful, and impactful projects across our business.
*   Our intern program seeks to help you develop an understanding of the aerospace industry and learn about our company and its operations.
*   While you benefit from on-the-job training and practical experience, you are expected to contribute fresh ideas and participate as a productive member of the department. Your growth as a professional and the difference you make will be on display every day.

**Qualifications You Must Have**

*   Enrolled in a degree program in Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, or a related field
*   Minimum Cumulative GPA of 3.0

**Qualifications We Prefer**

*   Experience in systems engineering discipline
*   Experience with MBSE, SysML and/or programming experience in Java or similar. 
*   Experience integrating Artificial Intelligence into engineering tool suites or processes. 
*   Experience with aircraft engine systems and validation processes.
