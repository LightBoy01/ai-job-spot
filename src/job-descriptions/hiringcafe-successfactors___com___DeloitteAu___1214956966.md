**What will your typical day look like?**  
 

As a Snr Consultant in our Data and AI team, you will - Lead the development and delivery of advanced analytics & data solutions for our clients.

**About the team**  
 

Our diverse Data & AI team comprises over 500 specialist practitioners across Australia, each of whom are constantly curious and excited about combining their business acumen and technological expertise to create data-centric solutions that help solve complex problems and transform or reinvent our client’s business.

**Enough about us, let’s talk about you.**  
 

You are someone with experience and skillsets that may include:

*   Experience as a Data Analyst, Business Analyst, Reporting Analyst or Data Scientist in a commercial environment.
*   Experience with designing, developing, and deploying reporting solutions in Tableau, power BI, QLIK or similar.
*   Experience using scripting and query languages such as Python, SQL and R.  
*   Experience with SQL Server, DB2, PostgreSQL, Oracle, or similar databasing technologies.  
*   Experience with Analytics solutions on AWS, Azure, GCP or Snowflake will be highly regarded.
*   Experience in a commercial consulting environment will be well regarded but is not essential.
*   Experience with stakeholder engagement and/or project management.
*   The ability to translate Analytics / Technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
*   Strong written and verbal communication skills.
