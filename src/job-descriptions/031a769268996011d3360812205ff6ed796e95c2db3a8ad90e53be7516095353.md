### Description

As a Senior Data Scientist at the Dutch Food and Consumer Product Safety Authority (NVWA), you will play a pivotal role in advancing our mission to protect food and product safety, animal welfare, plant health, and nature. We are seeking an experienced statistical expert to join our Data Science Cluster at the Bureau for Risk Assessment & Research (BuRO). Your work will focus on giving a significant boost to data-driven practices within the organization, particularly in the public interest domains of Nature & Environment and Product Safety.

In this role, you will immerse yourself in (big) data to analyze, research, experiment, and develop innovative solutions. You will leverage diverse internal and external data sources to anticipate societal developments and enable risk-oriented work. This is not a role where you step into a fully established system; instead, you will join a dynamic and developing environment that offers ample space for personal initiative and a pioneering spirit to help us reach the next level of data maturity.

### Responsibilities

*   Execute complex, knowledge-intensive data processing and advanced statistical analyses using a variety of structured and unstructured data sources.
*   Take the lead in statistical analysis projects, actively involving and collaborating with colleagues to enhance the collective expertise of the Data Science Cluster.
*   Coach and mentor less experienced data scientists within BuRO, helping them grow their skills in applied statistics.
*   Actively contribute to raising the data literacy and knowledge level of other departments and teams within the NVWA.
*   Proactively identify, evaluate, and introduce new developments and technologies from the statistical and data science fields into the organization.
*   Contribute to the full lifecycle of statistical research projects, from initial setup and planning to execution and evaluation.
*   Ensure the responsible and ethical application of data science and AI, showing sensitivity to ethical considerations.
*   Act as a key contact for external partners, including universities, while actively maintaining and expanding a relevant professional network.

### Qualifications

*   A completed Master's or Doctoral degree in a relevant field such as Artificial Intelligence, (Applied) Mathematics, Econometrics, Criminology, or Information Technology.
*   A minimum of 5 years of relevant work experience as a Data Scientist or scientific researcher, with demonstrable experience in setting up and executing quantitative research and data science projects.
*   Deep knowledge of Artificial Intelligence (AI), machine learning techniques, and statistical methodologies. Desirable knowledge includes causal inference and Bayesian statistics.
*   Proficiency in relevant programming languages and tools such as SAS, Python, R, Git, and Azure ML Studio.
*   Knowledge of Natural Language Processing (NLP), neural networks, MLOps, and applied mathematics is considered a significant plus.
*   Excellent communication skills with the ability to translate business problems into data science solutions and convey complex results to non-technical stakeholders.
*   An enthusiastic, optimistic, and proactive personality with the ability to motivate and inspire colleagues.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "The description emphasizes boosting data-driven work to protect public interests. Can you share a concrete example of a project where data science made a real-world impact at the NVWA?"**

> Of course. One of our recent successes involved tackling fraudulent online sellers of unauthorized pesticides. In the past, this was like finding a needle in a haystack. But a small team within our cluster developed a model that scrapes web shops and social media, using NLP to identify suspicious language and network analysis to link seemingly separate sellers back to a single source. Instead of reacting to complaints, we can now proactively identify major offenders. The first time we used it, we were able to coordinate with inspectors to shut down a network that was distributing harmful chemicals to unknowing gardeners. It was a fantastic moment because it wasn't just about code; it was about directly preventing environmental damage and protecting public health. This role is about creating more stories like that.

**Another candidate might ask: "The role is described as having a 'pioneering spirit' in an environment that isn't a 'made bed.' What are the biggest challenges the team is currently facing, and what would a successful first year in this role look like?"**

> That's a great question because it gets to the heart of the opportunity here. Our biggest challenge isn't a lack of ambition or data; it's about connecting the dots and maturing our practices across a large, diverse organization. Some departments are incredibly data-savvy, while others are just starting their journey. So, a successful first year for you wouldn't just be about delivering a brilliant predictive model in isolation. Success would be leading a key project—maybe on causal inference to measure the real impact of our inspections—while simultaneously becoming the go-to person for statistical advice. It would be seeing the junior data scientists you've mentored start to lead their own analyses with confidence. Your success will be measured by two things: the innovative projects you deliver and the tangible uplift in data science capability you help create across the NVWA. We're looking for someone who wants to build not just models, but a data-driven culture.
