**A Day in the Life**
=====================
At Medtronic, we’re revolutionizing healthcare by leveraging Generative AI to drive innovation in medical device development, clinical decision support, and operational efficiencies. We are seeking a Senior Data Scientist specializing in Generative AI to lead the design, development, and application of cutting-edge AI solutions, focusing on transforming how we analyze, interpret, and utilize healthcare data.
**Responsibilities may include the following and other duties may be assigned**
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including clinicians, engineers, and product managers, to identify opportunities for applying generative AI to solve complex problems in healthcare and medical device applications.
*   Develop and fine-tune generative AI models, including LLMs (LLaMA, GPT, T5, BERT, MedBERT) and other transformer-based architectures, for applications such as summarization, entity extraction, or knowledge generation.
*   Apply Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) techniques to combine generative AI with structured and unstructured knowledge bases for clinical decision support and operational insights.
*   Design and implement pipelines for preprocessing diverse data sources (e.g., clinical notes, EHRs, sensor data) to prepare high-quality datasets for generative AI model training and evaluation.
*   Research and apply domain-specific adaptations of generative AI models, ensuring solutions meet clinical, operational, and business needs.
*   Stay current with the latest advancements in open-source generative AI models and frameworks, ensuring integration of cutting-edge techniques into healthcare-focused solutions.
*   Develop scalable and secure deployment strategies for generative AI applications using cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP).
*   Conduct exploratory data analysis (EDA) to validate data quality and assess suitability for generative model applications.
*   Document methodologies, experimental results, and recommendations to align with regulatory and organizational standards.
*   Mentor and guide junior data scientists, fostering expertise in generative AI techniques and healthcare applications.
**Required Knowledge and Experience**
*   8+ years of experience in AI, data science, or machine learning, with at least 5+ years focused on generative AI.
*   Strong expertise in open-source generative AI models, including but not limited to GPT, LLaMA, T5, BERT, and MedBERT, and experience fine-tuning these models for domain-specific applications.
*   Proficiency in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and integrating generative AI with knowledge graphs, structured data, or enterprise databases and Microsoft Copilot Studio
*   Deep understanding of transformer-based architectures and their applications in text generation, summarization, and knowledge extraction.
*   Proficiency in Python and machine learning frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, or Hugging Face.
*   Experience building pipelines for preprocessing and managing large-scale text datasets.
*   Strong problem-solving skills and ability to translate business needs into data-driven solutions.
*   Excellent communication and collaboration skills to deliver impactful AI solutions across interdisciplinary teams.
*   Bachelors / Master’s of Engineering in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related technical field.
*   8+ years of professional experience in data science or AI, with significant contributions to projects involving generative AI.
