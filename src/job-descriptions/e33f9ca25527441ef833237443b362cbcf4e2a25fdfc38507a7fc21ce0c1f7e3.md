**This role is for one of the Weekday's clients  
  
**We are partnering with a leading AI research group to engage **Mathematics PhD experts** for a groundbreaking project aimed at enhancing AI systems’ reasoning and problem-solving capabilities. Participants will apply their deep mathematical expertise to evaluate, refine, and validate complex mathematical reasoning produced by advanced AI models.

This opportunity is ideal for **recent PhD graduates (within the past 4 years)** or **current PhD candidates (at least 3 years into their program)** from top-tier mathematics programs who are passionate about mathematical rigor, logical reasoning, and advancing AI research.

**Requirements**

### **Key Responsibilities**

*   Solve and assess advanced mathematics problems across various domains.
*   Review and critique AI-generated proofs and solutions for logical accuracy and rigor.
*   Provide structured, detailed feedback to help improve the model’s mathematical reasoning and coherence.

### **Ideal Qualifications**

*   **PhD in Mathematics (completed within the past 4 years)** OR **PhD candidate at least 3 years into program** (typically ≥4th year post-undergrad or ≥3rd year with a master’s).
*   Enrolled in or graduated from a **top 30 mathematics program**.
*   Active involvement in **academic or research activities** preferred.
*   Exceptional skills in **advanced problem-solving, theorem-based reasoning, and written explanation**.
*   Ability to identify logical inconsistencies and communicate findings clearly and precisely.

### **More About the Opportunity**

*   **Remote and asynchronous** — work on your own schedule.
*   **Flexible, project-based engagement** lasting **5–6 weeks**, with potential for extension or future projects.

### **Compensation & Contract Terms**

*   **Competitive hourly rate**, aligned with experience and academic background.
*   **Independent contractor** position.
*   **Weekly payments** processed via Stripe Connect.
