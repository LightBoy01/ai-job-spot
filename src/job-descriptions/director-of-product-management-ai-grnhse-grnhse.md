<p><strong>About the Role</strong></p>
<p>OpenLoop is looking for a <strong>Director of Product Management, AI</strong> to join our team remotely or at HQ in Des Moines, IA. In this role, you will be responsible for unifying our B2B and B2C AI capabilities into a cohesive strategy, ensuring our AI products drive meaningful revenue growth, partner adoption, and market differentiation. The ideal candidate is both a product strategist and a go-to-market operator—able to define the long-term roadmap while building the commercial materials, positioning, and integration models that unlock near-term results.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>What You'll Do</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Define and evolve the AI product portfolio across B2B and B2C capabilities, aligning with OpenLoop’s mission and revenue objectives.</li>
<li>Develop compelling GTM strategies, messaging, and sales enablement materials that accelerate adoption and revenue growth.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Build and scale partnerships with healthcare organizations, technology vendors, and channel partners to expand reach and deliver&nbsp;value-added solutions.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Collaborate closely with sales, marketing, and&nbsp;customer success to drive product adoption, expand existing accounts, and identify new revenue streams.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Guide product managers and engineering teams in prioritization, execution, and alignment to strategic goals—ensuring the roadmap delivers both near-term impact and long-term value.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Partner with engineering, design, and operations to ensure successful delivery of AI products that meet customer and market needs.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Conduct ongoing competitive analysis, market research, and customer feedback loops to inform strategy and prioritize investments.</li>
<li>Lead and mentor a team of product managers, instilling a culture of ownership, accountability, and customer focus.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Other duties as assigned.</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Who You Are</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Bachelor's Degree in Business Administration, Engineering, Computer Science or related field.</li>
<li>10+ years of product management experience, with 4+ years in leadership roles.</li>
<li>Proven success developing and launching AI-driven products, ideally across both B2B and B2C markets. </li>
<li>Strong track record of building and executing go-to-market strategies tied to measurable revenue growth. </li>
<li>Experience integrating products into partner ecosystems and building scalable partner models. </li>
<li>Ability to guide and align product managers and engineering teams around strategic priorities and roadmap execution. </li>
<li>Exceptional communication skills with the ability to craft compelling narratives for executives, partners, and customers. </li>
<li>Strong analytical mindset; able to balance data-driven decision making with intuition and vision. </li>
<li>Background in healthcare, health-tech, or adjacent industries preferred.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
