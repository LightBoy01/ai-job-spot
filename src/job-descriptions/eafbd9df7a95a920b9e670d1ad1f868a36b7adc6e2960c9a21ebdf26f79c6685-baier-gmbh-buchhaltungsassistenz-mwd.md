### Description\nJoin our growing family-owned company that values motivated individuals and offers excellent working conditions and attractive benefits. We are looking for a dedicated professional to strengthen our accounting and administration team, starting immediately in a full-time capacity. This versatile role involves supporting various specialized areas based on your skills and qualifications.\n\n### Responsibilities\nDepending on your skills and qualifications, your responsibilities will cover a wide range of tasks, including but not limited to:\n- **Financial Accounting:** Managing accounts payable and receivable, invoice verification, payment processing, dunning, cash and bank postings, fixed asset accounting, account reconciliation, and assisting with monthly and annual financial statements (HGB).\n- **Payroll/HR Administration:** Maintaining master data and time accounts, certificate management, and preparing payroll in collaboration with tax advisors/payroll office.\n- **International Secondments:** Applying for and managing A1 certificates, and coordinating reporting and compliance requirements for international assignments (EU/EFTA).\n- **Customs & Foreign Trade:** Supporting customs clearance (e.g., EORI, ATLAS in cooperation with service providers/forwarders), managing supplier declarations/preference certificates, and goods tariff classification.\n- **Reporting & Statistics:** Handling Intrastat, recapitulative statements (ZM), reports to authorities/associations (e.g., Federal Statistical Office), and managing operational key figures.\n- **Organization & Processes:** Maintaining master data, document management, creating evaluations/reports, and serving as an interface to purchasing, sales, project, and assembly departments.\n\n### Qualifications\n**You should have:**\n- A completed commercial apprenticeship, such as Industrial Clerk, Office Management Clerk, Tax Assistant, or Shipping and Logistics Services Clerk.\n- Ideally, additional qualifications in Financial or Balance Sheet Accounting, or comparable qualification through extensive professional experience.\n- Several years of experience in financial accounting, and ideally in payroll/HR administration.\n- Practical experience with ERP and accounting software (e.g., DATEV, SAP, Microsoft Dynamics, Lexware or similar) and very good Excel skills.\n- Advantageous: Knowledge of customs/foreign trade processes (ATLAS, preferences, tariffing) and reporting (Intrastat/ZM).\n- Secure handling of German taxes/duties in daily business (especially VAT).\n- Good German language skills (minimum C1) and English is an advantage.\n\n**You are:**\n- Reliable, structured, and precise, with a high sense of responsibility and discretion.\n- Independent, proactive, and solution-oriented in your work approach.\n- Communicative and a team player, coordinating confidently between departments, service providers, and authorities.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a diligent and experienced accounting and administration professional to join a stable, family-owned company. The role offers remarkable breadth, touching upon everything from core financial accounting and HR to international compliance and customs. What truly stands out are the employee-centric benefits: significant autonomy and scope for initiative, flexible working hours including mobile work options, flat hierarchies, and generous vacation days (up to 35!). The company clearly invests in its people, providing ample opportunities for continuous professional development in specialized areas. If you thrive in a supportive yet challenging environment where your diverse skills are valued and you can directly contribute to healthy company growth, this role provides an excellent long-term career path.
