**Job Description:**
DXC Technology (NYSE: DXC) is the world’s leading independent, end-to-end IT services company, helping clients harness the power of innovation to thrive on change. Created by the merger of CSC and the Enterprise Services business of Hewlett Packard Enterprise, DXC Technology serves nearly 6,000 private and public sector clients across 70 countries. The company’s technology independence, global talent and extensive partner alliance combine to deliver powerful next-generation IT services and solutions. DXC Technology is recognized among the best corporate citizens globally. For more information, visit [www.dxc.technology](http://www.dxc.technology/)
Are you a recent graduate or currently studying and eager to dive into the world of cloud technologies and DevOps? Join our Data & AI Platforms organization as a Junior Cloud DevOps Engineer and embark on a journey to become an AIOps expert. This role offers an excellent opportunity to work with cutting-edge Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) and Platform as a Service (PaaS) technologies, with a focus on public clouds like Azure.
If you're passionate about cloud technologies and eager to grow in the DevOps field, we'd love to hear from you!
**We are currently looking for:**
**Junior Cloud Devops Engineer**
================================
**You will be responsible for:**
*   Engage in site reliability engineering and platform automation.
*   Develop and maintain CI/CD pipelines.
*   Contribute to the DXC expert team, working on Big Data and cloud-native solutions (AWS, Azure, GCP).
*   Embrace an automate-everything mindset, delivering source code for large-scale deployments and on-premises to public cloud migrations.
*   Manage configurations for large multinational customers.
*   Support AI/ML engineering teams by enabling scalable infrastructure for MLOps workflows and GenAI experimentation.
*   Collaborate on integrating AI-driven tools and services into DevOps pipelines.
​**We require:**
*   Understanding of Docker, Git, and Azure cloud technologies.
*   Knowledge of DevOps principles.
*   Practical Linux skills and scripting capabilities in Bash, Python, or Ruby.
*   Strong motivation, ownership, and drive to get things done.
*   Basic understanding of AI/ML.
**Nice to have:**
*   Understanding of IaC tools such as Terraform, Bicep, ARM Templates.
*   Understanding of containerization platforms such as Kubernetes, Azure Container Apps, Docker, Docker Compose.
*   Certifications that confirm your skills.
*   Exposure to GenAI frameworks (e.g., LangChain, Hugging Face Transformers) or cloud-native AI services (e.g., Azure OpenAI, Amazon Bedrock).
*   Experience supporting data science or ML teams in a DevOps or MLOps capacity.
**Why Join Us?**

*   Gain hands-on experience with the latest cloud technologies.
*   Develop your skills and advance your career in a supportive environment.
