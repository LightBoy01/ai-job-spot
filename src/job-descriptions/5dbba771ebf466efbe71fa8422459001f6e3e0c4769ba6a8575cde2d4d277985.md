**Introduction**
----------------

The candidate will contribute to the development of artificial intelligence and machine learning solutions to support internal audit activities in the banking sector. Specifically:  
\- Collaborate with internal teams to build data-driven models for audit controls  
\- Analyze structured and unstructured datasets  
\- Develop machine learning algorithms to improve decision-making processes  
\- Support automation and digitalization of audit activities  
\- Document and share developed solutions with the team  
\- Participate in innovative projects applying AI to internal control, both traditional and generative AI
