### Description\nJoin the ride! Did you always want to work for a brand that moves all your friends? If you’re driven by creativity, strategy, and the thrill of shaping one of Germany’s most loved mobility brands, then hop in, your journey starts here. As Head of Brand & Community Management, you are the architect of the MILES brand – you define, develop, and activate it across all touchpoints to foster community-driven growth, loyalty, and love for MILES.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Responsibility for the MILES brand strategy and positioning, aligning it with the corporate vision and commercial goals.\n- Define and develop the brand architecture across our entire portfolio (Carsharing, Subscription, B2B) to ensure consistency and clarity.\n- Develop and implement integrated brand plans that strengthen brand awareness and emotional connection – with a focus on ROI and long-term brand value.\n- Lead creative direction and storytelling, shaping campaigns, content, and visual identity in close collaboration with our internal design and content teams as well as agency partners.\n- Preserve and further develop our look, feel, and tone of voice, so that every message and every interaction reflects the personality and purpose of MILES.\n- Track and manage brand performance metrics such as top-of-mind, aided/unaided brand awareness, consideration, and brand sentiment.\n- Leverage the strength of our community: Build authentic relationships with our users and promote initiatives that turn customers into brand ambassadors.\n- Lead and inspire a cross-functional team of design, creatives, content and foster collaboration with CRM, performance marketing and other teams within the MILES organization.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 5+ years of experience in brand strategy, communication or creative leadership, including at least 3 years in a digital-first or mobility/lifestyle environment.\n- Proven track record in building and scaling strong consumer brands – ideally in a multi-market context.\n- Deep understanding of integrated brand management – from storytelling and design systems to data-driven brand tracking.\n- Experience in agency steering, creative briefing, and campaign execution.\n- Familiarity with brand performance metrics and the ability to interpret data for decision-making.\n- Strong sense for culture, design, and communication trends, and the ability to translate insights into concrete actions.\n- Excellent stakeholder management and the ability to unite creative vision with business priorities.\n- Fluent English and German language skills (written and spoken).\n\n### Author's Take\nMILES offers a vibrant and supportive environment where you can truly make an impact on a beloved mobility brand. Beyond a competitive package that includes mobility credits or a Germany ticket, subsidized fitness (Urban Sports Club), and attractive retirement plans, you'll be part of a diverse team of over 50 nationalities, operating in an English-speaking corporate culture. The company prioritizes personal growth through continuous learning and knowledge exchange, fostering a collaborative atmosphere. What truly sets MILES apart is its strong commitment to social impact, actively investing a portion of its revenue in regional and social projects (#milescharity). This role is perfect for a strategic and creative leader who wants to shape a brand with purpose and drive community engagement.
