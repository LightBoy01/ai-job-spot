Harnham is a global leader in **Data & Analytics recruitment**, providing talent solutions the UK, Europe, and the U.S. Since 2006, we’ve built a reputation for deep market knowledge, outstanding delivery, and strong client relationships across Data Science, Data Engineering, Analytics, and AI.

We’ve helped shape the careers of over **15,000 Data professionals** and continue to grow across global markets and that growth starts with hiring the best people into our teams.

  

**AI & Systems Implementation Lead**

The recruitment industry is moving at an exceptional pace, with technology, AI, and data now playing a crucial role in driving businesses forward.

We’ve achieved this by keeping a few things at the heart of our culture: our belief that _“what gets us here won’t get us there”_ and our commitment to putting clients and candidates first.

  

Now, we’re ready for the next stage of growth — and we’ve recognised the need for a standalone role that bridges innovation, implementation, and strategy.

We’re looking for someone with experience in data, AI, and implementation who can connect the dots between cutting-edge technology and real-world business improvement. If you’ve found yourself asking _“what’s next?”_ in your career, this could be the answer.

Key Responsibilities
--------------------

**The Role:**

As an **AI & Implementation Lead**, you’ll report directly to the Group CEO and work closely with senior stakeholders across the business. **Your mission: to explore, evaluate, and implement AI and data-led solutions** that make our people more effective, our processes more efficient, and our business more future-ready.

Essentially, the role will suit someone who understands agency recruitment inside out but perhaps also has been involved in projects at work around data, CRM, strategy or lead initiatives as part of their role.

  

Some of the key elements that you'll get to touch on in this position include:

*   Employee Experience
*   Systems Analysis, Strategy & Improvement
*   Process Analysis & Improvement
*   Guidance around best practice on policies, procedures, training and materials
*   Onboarding Support
