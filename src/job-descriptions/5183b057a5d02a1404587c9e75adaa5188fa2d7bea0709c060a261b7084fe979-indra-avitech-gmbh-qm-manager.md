Your mission
------------

*   Responsibility for the continuous improvement of quality management systems and processes according to ISO 9001 or similar standards
*   Ensuring compliance with legal, normative and internal quality requirements
*   Planning, conducting and following up on internal and external audits
*   Accompaniment and monitoring of quality processes according to the V-model, especially in the field of software and system development
*   Analysis of quality problems and initiation of corrective and preventive measures
*   Support and advice to the specialist departments in all matters of quality management
*   Active approach and implementation of new ISO standards, in particular ISO 27001\*\* (information security management), in order to adapt to changing standards and market requirements
*   Work closely with the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)\*\* to integrate information security requirements into quality processes and jointly mitigate information security risks
*   Development of training and awareness-raising measures to promote quality and safety awareness in the company
*   Working with suppliers and customers to ensure and optimize product quality
*   Creation and evaluation of quality indicators and reporting to the management

Your profile
------------

*   Completed studies in the field of quality management, engineering or a comparable qualification
*   Several years of professional experience in quality management, ideally in an industrial environment
*   Sound knowledge of relevant norms and standards (e.g. B. ISO 9001, ISO 14001, etc.)
*   Knowledge and experience in the implementation of ISO 27001\*\* and other new ISO standards
*   Experience working with a CISO\*\* or similar functions to ensure compliance with information security policies
*   Experience in conducting audits (internal and external)
*   Analytical thinking and solution-oriented way of working
*   High communication and assertiveness skills
*   Very good knowledge of German and English, both written and spoken
*   Willingness to travel (approx. 10%)
