### Description\nThis role is for a proactive Quality Management Specialist responsible for the continuous improvement and compliance of quality management systems, particularly focusing on ISO 9001 and the implementation of ISO 27001 for information security. The position involves planning and conducting audits, analyzing quality issues, supporting specialist departments, and collaborating with the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), as well as external suppliers and customers to ensure product quality and security.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Responsibility for the continuous improvement of quality management systems and processes according to ISO 9001 or similar standards\n- Ensuring compliance with legal, normative and internal quality requirements\n- Planning, conducting and following up on internal and external audits\n- Accompaniment and monitoring of quality processes according to the V-model, especially in the field of software and system development\n- Analysis of quality problems and initiation of corrective and preventive measures\n- Support and advice to the specialist departments in all matters of quality management\n- Active approach and implementation of new ISO standards, in particular ISO 27001 (information security management), in order to adapt to changing standards and market requirements\n- Work closely with the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) to integrate information security requirements into quality processes and jointly mitigate information security risks\n- Development of training and awareness-raising measures to promote quality and safety awareness in the company\n- Working with suppliers and customers to ensure and optimize product quality\n- Creation and evaluation of quality indicators and reporting to the management\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed studies in the field of quality management, engineering or a comparable qualification\n- Several years of professional experience in quality management, ideally in an industrial environment\n- Sound knowledge of relevant norms and standards (e.g., ISO 9001, ISO 14001, etc.)\n- Knowledge and experience in the implementation of ISO 27001 and other new ISO standards\n- Experience working with a CISO or similar functions to ensure compliance with information security policies\n- Experience in conducting audits (internal and external)\n- Analytical thinking and solution-oriented way of working\n- High communication and assertiveness skills\n- Very good knowledge of German and English, both written and spoken\n- Willingness to travel (approx. 10%)\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a dedicated Quality Management professional to significantly impact a company's quality and security posture. The role offers the chance to lead ISO 27001 implementation and collaborate closely with the CISO. The company culture is highly supportive, emphasizing work-life balance through flexible hours, mobile working, and generous holidays, alongside robust opportunities for individual growth and training within an international team. This position is ideal for someone looking for a challenging yet balanced role with a clear path for influence and development.
