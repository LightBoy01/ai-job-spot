### Description\neBazaaris is a leading Retail Media agency focusing on Amazon and online pharmacies, dedicated to helping successful brands unlock their full marketplace potential from content optimization to strategic brand management. We are seeking an Amazon Account Manager (m/w/d) with an OTC or FMCG background to strengthen our team. In this role, you will be responsible for supporting our partners and developing sustainable growth strategies. Leveraging your industry know-how, you will play a crucial part in optimally positioning brands within the health and consumer goods sectors on Amazon and in online pharmacies. This position offers challenging projects with strong brands and significant advertising budgets.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Independently manage and develop Healthcare and FMCG accounts on Amazon & in online pharmacies.\n*   Plan product detail pages, A+ Content, Brand Store, and Brand Story with the objective of sustainably increasing visibility, conversion, and brand building.\n*   Conceive and implement Retail Media campaigns (Sponsored Ads, DSP, CTV) in collaboration with our Advertising team.\n*   Analyze market, competitor, and performance data, identify growth potentials, and derive concrete optimization measures.\n*   Serve as the interface between client, Content, Advertising, Operations, and BI, ensuring seamless collaboration across all functions.\n*   Create reports, derive actionable recommendations, and continuously optimize brand performance.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Possess initial experience in e-commerce, online marketing, or account management, ideally within the OTC / FMCG / Healthcare sectors.\n*   Demonstrate an understanding of digital retail structures, as well as brand and assortment management in the pharmacy or marketplace environment.\n*   Show knowledge of or interest in Amazon (Vendor Central, Seller Central, Ads) – this is a significant advantage.\n*   Exhibit strong analytical skills and a data-driven mindset, capable of translating numbers into concrete actions.\n*   Work independently, in a structured, and solution-oriented manner with a high standard of quality.\n*   Command very good German and English language skills, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an ambitious Account Manager with initial experience in e-commerce, particularly in OTC/FMCG/Healthcare. The role offers significant autonomy in managing key accounts and shaping growth strategies on Amazon and online pharmacies. eBazaaris fosters a results-oriented culture with flexible working arrangements (remote/Berlin), prioritizing continuous learning and career development, complemented by engaging team events. It's a great fit for someone looking to make a tangible impact with strong brands in a dynamic environment.
