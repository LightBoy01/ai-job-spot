### Description\nRemmert specializes in automated storage and logistics systems, focusing on space-efficient storage and fully automatic movement of sheet metal, long goods, and workpieces. Operating in the future-oriented industry of mechanical engineering, automation, and logistics, Remmert is a continuously growing, family-run company since 1945. They prioritize development perspectives, communication at eye level, and short decision paths.\n\nEmployees at Remmert benefit from an attractive package that includes an unlimited employment contract, 30 days of annual leave, options for mobile working and flexible hours, and special bonuses such as profit sharing, employee referral programs, e-bike leasing, attendance bonuses, and gym subsidies. The company provides modern equipment like laptops, mobile phones, and height-adjustable desks, along with complimentary coffee, fruits, and vegetables. Remmert is committed to fostering professional growth through regular exchanges, annual performance reviews, and a comprehensive catalog of continuing education opportunities for all professional groups. The family-oriented culture is further enhanced by regular employee events, including summer outings, hiking days, and Christmas celebrations for colleagues and their families. Additionally, the company offers robust support for occupational pension schemes with attractive employer contributions and promotes employee well-being through a comprehensive health management program, including health checks, preventative measures during work hours, running groups, and participation in company runs.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Requirements Determination & Procurement Planning:** Close collaboration with sales, engineering, and project management to derive technical specifications, create bills of materials, and establish procurement schedules.\n*   **Supplier Selection & Development:** Identify, evaluate, and select suitable suppliers, considering quality, ESG, and certification standards. Build and maintain long-term supplier relationships.\n*   **Contract & Price Negotiations:** Draft and negotiate framework agreements, price structures, and payment terms. Ensure cost-optimized procurement while considering technical requirements.\n*   **Technical Risk & Quality Management:** Review technical specifications, standards, and compatibility. Collaborate with quality assurance and manage deviations.\n*   **Supply Chain Tracking & Deadline Management:** Monitor delivery dates, coordinate deliveries, and support assembly and installation processes.\n*   **Cost Efficiency & Budget Control:** Create cost overviews, conduct price analyses, and participate in budget planning.\n*   **Documentation & Reporting:** Maintain comprehensive procurement documentation and provide regular status reports to project management.\n*   **Process Optimization & Digitalization:** Contribute to the further development of procurement processes and leverage digital tools for supplier evaluation.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed technical training and commercial further education with a focus on mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, or a comparable qualification; ideally, a degree in Industrial Engineering, Supply Chain Management, or relevant specialized training.\n*   Sound knowledge in mechanical engineering, ideally in the areas of shelving steel construction, load carriers, conveyor technology, and automation components.\n*   Proven experience in project purchasing, especially during the project planning phase.\n*   Strong negotiation skills, knowledge of contract law, and expertise in supplier management.\n*   SAP knowledge (order processing, invoice workflow) is an advantage.\n*   A team player with a structured way of working and a high degree of initiative.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at Remmert offers a unique opportunity to join a stable, family-run company that is a leader in automated logistics systems. As a Project Purchaser, you'll play a critical role in ensuring the quality and cost-effectiveness of their innovative solutions, working within a collaborative environment that values open communication and quick decision-making. The company strongly supports employee growth and well-being, providing excellent benefits, flexible working arrangements, and extensive development opportunities. If you're a long-term oriented individual seeking to make a tangible impact in a specialized, future-proof industry, Remmert offers a rewarding career path where you can truly grow with the team.
