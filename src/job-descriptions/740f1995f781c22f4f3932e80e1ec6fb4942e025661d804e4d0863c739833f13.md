Wix makes it possible for anyone to succeed online. At Wix, we push you to innovate, evolve in non-traditional ways, and collaborate. We operate in small teams that work closely together at a high level.

**Job Description:**

As a backend engineer at Wix Analytics, you'll focus on producing scalable, high-quality code while collaborating with product managers and creating APIs. You'll be part of a dynamic team tackling challenges in backend development and contributing to the success of Wix Analytics.

In your day-to-day, you will: 

*   Produce scalable, high-quality code 
*   Collaborate with product managers 
*   Create APIs 

**Qualifications:**

*   2+ years of production experience in backend development 
*   Proficiency in Node.js, familiarity with Java is a plus 
*   Ability to work with large codebases 
*   Knowledge of AI development tools 
*   Ability to work quickly in a fast-changing environment 
*   Product-focused mindset
