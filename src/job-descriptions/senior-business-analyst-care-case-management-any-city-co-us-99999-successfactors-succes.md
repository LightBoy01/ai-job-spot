<b>Summary</b>

<p>As a <strong>Senior Professional Business Analyst </strong>at Gainwell, you can contribute your skills as we harness the power of technology to help our clients improve the health and well-being of the members they serve — a community’s most vulnerable. Connect your passion with purpose, teaming with people who thrive on finding innovative solutions to some of healthcare’s biggest challenges. Here are the details on this position.</p>


<b>Your role in our mission</b>

<p>Play a critical part in ensuring Gainwell is meeting our clients’ objectives in important areas.</p>
<ul>
<li>Understand new Case and Care Management Policies provided to Gainwell by our Customer. Generate business and/or data specifications in support of evidence requirements for each component.</li>
<li>Apply knowledge of CMS policies and regulatory requirements associated with Case and Care Management and Population Health Management when partnering with our Customer to develop functional requirements for system changes; keep abreast of CMS policy and regulatory changes.</li>
<li>Facilitate design sessions with our Customer, our developers and third-party vendors, and generate design specification documents.</li>
<li>Enter approved requirements into ALM.</li>
<li>Partner with development team and clarify requirements as needed.</li>
<li>Help verify that all requirements have been met by running ALM requirements traceability (RTM) reports.</li>
<li>Partner with test team and support generation of user stories/test scenarios and supporting test cases, for system testing, system integration testing and end-to-end testing, that align to each requirement and to generate test data needed to perform testing.</li>
<li>Help develop test scenarios and cases as needed for Model Office and End-to-End Testing. This will include building the necessary data sets.</li>
<li>Support development of user training collateral designed to support Policy and functional requirements and participate in end-user training activities as needed.</li>
</ul>


<b>Other role expectations</b>

<ul>
<li>Support UAT activities performed by our Customer.</li>
<li>Participate in end-to-end functional testing as needed.</li>
<li>Communicate with the Customer (internal and external) and project team, measuring and sharing project metrics/statuses, identifying and documenting risks, and recommending changes as needed.</li>
<li>Assist with Operational Readiness activities including any operational change management activities.</li>
<li>Serve as the subject matter expert for end-to-end work and data flow for the Care and Case Management application ecosystem</li>
<li>Build strong working relationships with the Gainwell team and Client stakeholders</li>
<li>Assist with the overall maintenance of the RTM for each project</li>
</ul>


<b>What we're looking for</b>

<ul>
<li>5+ years of experience as a Business Analyst, including 3+ years in Medicaid programs and 3+ years in multi-vendor environments.</li>
<li>Proven ability to interpret high-level requirements and translate them into detailed functional and technical specifications.</li>
<li>Strong client communication skills, with the ability to translate customer requests into actionable objectives and present effectively to business leaders, clients, and technical teams.</li>
<li>Leadership experience in mentoring and guiding junior colleagues.</li>
<li>Hands-on experience with requirements traceability management (RTM), test scenario creation, and test case execution.</li>
<li>Advanced proficiency in Excel (including pivot tables), and working knowledge of Word, PowerPoint, ALM, and AI tools.</li>
<li>Strong technical writing skills for documentation, reporting, and communication across stakeholders.</li>
</ul>


<b>Additional skills and experience</b>

<ul>
<li>2+ years of experience supporting or working within Healthcare Case Management</li>
<li>Experience writing/executing SQL queries</li>
<li>Proficiency in Visio</li>
<li>Certifications such as CompTIA A+, Network+, or equivalent</li>
<li>Experience working under NIST 800-53 or similar compliance frameworks</li>
<li>Previous experience with VeloCloud Router hardware</li>
</ul>
