### Description\nJoin Solano and take charge of accounting for a diverse real estate portfolio. This role offers the opportunity to work in a structured and independent manner, where your contributions are highly valued. We provide a modern work environment, efficient decision-making processes, and flexible working arrangements to support your success.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Book all ongoing business transactions, including service charges and rents.\n*   Manage accounts receivable and accounts payable, encompassing payment transactions and the dunning process.\n*   Monitor accounts, open items, and overall liquidity.\n*   Serve as a subject matter expert, communicating with owners, tenants, and service providers, and assisting tax advisors.\n*   Prepare utility cost statements, heating cost notifications, and comprehensive financial reports.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Successfully completed commercial training with several years of professional experience in accounting.\n*   Experience in the real estate sector is advantageous, but not strictly required as training can be provided.\n*   Excellent understanding of numbers and strong proficiency in common MS Office applications, especially Excel.\n*   Good German language skills, both written and spoken, are essential.\n*   A hands-on mentality combined with an independent and responsible work approach.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a compelling opportunity for an experienced accountant looking for autonomy and growth within the German real estate sector. Solano offers a supportive and modern workplace with flexible hours and mobile working options, indicating a strong commitment to work-life balance. The emphasis on continuous development and a flat hierarchy suggests a forward-thinking environment where your ideas are valued. If you thrive on independent work and are eager to make a direct impact, this role provides an excellent platform for a long-term career.
