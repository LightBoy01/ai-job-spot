**Purpose & Overall Relevance for the Organization:** 

Drive the application of artificial intelligence and advanced analytics to generate actionable business insights across markets and functions. Lead the development of predictive and prescriptive models that support strategic decision-making and operational efficiency. Act as a strategic enabler between data science capabilities and business stakeholders, ensuring that AI-driven solutions are aligned with business goals and deliver measurable impact.

**Key Responsibilities:** 

**AI-Driven Analytics & Business Insights**

*   Design and implement machine learning models for forecasting, segmentation, anomaly detection, and optimization.
*   Translate business challenges into analytical frameworks and AI solutions.
*   Develop and maintain advanced dashboards and tools that integrate AI outputs with business KPIs.
*   Ensure explainability and usability of AI models for non-technical stakeholders.

**Data Strategy & Integration**

*   Collaborate with data engineering teams to ensure access to clean, structured, and relevant data for modeling.
*   Integrate internal and external data sources to enrich analytical capabilities.
*   Promote data governance and ethical AI practices in model development and deployment.

**Stakeholder Engagement & Enablement**

*   Partner with commercial, operations, finance, and digital teams to identify opportunities for AI application.
*   Facilitate workshops and training sessions to increase data literacy and adoption of AI tools.
*   Communicate insights and model results effectively to senior leadership and cross-functional teams.

**Innovation & Continuous Improvement**

*   Stay current with emerging AI technologies, tools, and methodologies.
*   Evaluate and improve model performance, automation, and scalability.
*   Foster a culture of experimentation and innovation within the analytics team.

**Requisite Education and Experience / Minimum Qualifications:**

*   Bachelor’s degree in Business, Finance, Economics, Mathematics, Data Science, or a related field
*   2+ years of experience in AI, Data Science, or Business Analytics
*   Proficiency in Python and ML libraries (e.g., scikit-learn, TensorFlow)
*   Experience with Power BI and data storytelling
*   Familiarity with cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, GCP)

**Knowledge & Soft Skills:**

*   Strong understanding of machine learning techniques and statistical modeling
*   Ability to distill complex data into actionable insights and communicate them effectively to diverse stakeholders
*   Strong data storytelling skills, with a clear focus on connecting analytics to business impact
*   Comfortable communicating technical concepts in simple, business-relevant terms
*   Strategic thinker and collaborative mindset, capable of working with both technical and commercial teams
*   Ability to manage multiple priorities and projects simultaneously
*   Attention to detail, accuracy, and rigor in analysis
*   Experience implementing analytics projects or processes
*   People management experience is a plus
*   Fluent in English; Spanish and/or Portuguese is a plus
*   Passionate about leveraging AI to drive business impact
