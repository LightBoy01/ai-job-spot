Team Overview:
<p>We’re looking for a highly driven and strategic <strong>CX &amp; AI Enablement Manager</strong> to join our CX Quality Assurance team. This role will serve as a central pillar in defining and scaling quality standards across all business units and regions. You’ll work cross-functionally to align global QA practices, lead strategic initiatives, and implement innovative solutions to improve customer experience quality at scale.</p>
<p>This role requires business-oriented ownership and excellent communication skills to ensure effectiveness of the QA strategy. In the end, we are looking for a strong, committed and creative performer that is able to transmit the DiDi experience to our end users while leading an integrated team.</p>

Role Responsibilities:
<ul>
<li>Drive the development and centralization of global QA standards, applying AI as a foundational lever.</li>
<li>Lead end-to-end project management for global QA initiatives, including process standardization, tool implementation, and cross-regional alignment.</li>
<li>Explore, test, and deploy innovative technologies, including AI solutions, to enhance Quality Assurance operations.</li>
<li>Act as a strategic partner to regional QA teams and other CX functions, ensuring consistency, alignment, and operational excellence.</li>
<li>Monitor performance and impact of global QA strategies, continuously identifying areas for optimization.</li>
<li>Collaborate with Product and Data teams to develop scalable quality solutions.</li>
</ul>
