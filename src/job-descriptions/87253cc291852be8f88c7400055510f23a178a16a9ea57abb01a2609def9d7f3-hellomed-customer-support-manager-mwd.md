### Description\nhellomed is the digital pharmacy for caregivers, simplifying long-term medication management. Our vision is to prevent 250,000 annual hospitalizations due to medication errors and save 1.2 billion Euros in associated costs by 2030. Through our innovative hellomedOS software, we empower nursing staff, making daily medication care for patients safer and less burdensome. We are a vibrant team of pharmacists, care experts, and developers, supported by prominent investors, and our product is loved by caregivers across Germany. Join us in completely reinventing medication supply in nursing care for tomorrow!\n\nThis role is for a dedicated and customer-focused individual to serve as a primary contact, problem-solver, and process improver within our Customer Operations team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- You will be the primary point of contact for our care companies, handling all inquiries related to orders, deliveries, prescription processing, and general questions – reliably, clearly, and always aiming to significantly ease caregivers' daily routines.\n- You will coordinate complex pharmaceutical questions and systematically escalate them to our 2nd Level Support (PTAs/pharmacists) to ensure safe and professional care.\n- You will actively shape the expansion of our ticket and CRM systems, helping to build transparent, efficient, and error-free internal processes.\n- You will develop practical FAQs, guidelines, and process improvements based on daily customer communication, directly contributing to enhancing service quality.\n- Through your work, you will ensure that master data and documents for new and existing patients are complete, correct, and current – forming the foundation for secure medication provision.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Service Experience:** You have experience in customer service. You understand the daily challenges of our care customers and can provide reliable, fast, and solution-oriented support.\n- **Customer Orientation:** You possess a strong service mindset and find it easy to address customer concerns, providing answers that genuinely help in their daily care routines.\n- **Communication Skills:** You can explain complex matters clearly and understandably – both in writing and over the phone. Even in demanding situations, you remain confident, friendly, and committed.\n- **Structured Work Style:** You work meticulously, think in processes, and maintain an overview even with many parallel inquiries. Reliability and accuracy are among your strengths.\n- **Digital Competence:** You have a good understanding for digital applications and enjoy working with modern ticketing systems, CRM tools, and web platforms. You quickly and confidently learn new systems.\n- **Team Player:** You enjoy collaborating with various teams such as 2nd-Level Support, Customer Success, and Operations, actively contributing to ensuring a seamless supply experience.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting opportunity to join a mission-driven company that is genuinely impacting healthcare by preventing medication errors. The role offers a unique blend of direct customer interaction and strategic input, allowing you to not only solve immediate problems but also shape the systems and processes that improve efficiency and service quality. hellomed fosters a supportive and flexible culture, providing an attractive modern office in Berlin, above-average pay, flexible working hours with home office options, and a strong emphasis on personal development and team cohesion. If you're a customer-focused individual with a knack for digital tools and a desire to make a tangible difference, this role offers a steep learning curve and ample freedom to contribute your ideas in a vibrant and innovative team environment.
