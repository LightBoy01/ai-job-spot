Job Responsibilities:

*   Architect, develop, and deploy agentic automation bots to streamline insurance operations, ensuring robust compliance and operational excellence.
*   Design, implement, and optimise predictive modelling pipelines leveraging advanced machine learning to drive actionable business insights and risk stratification.
*   Collaborate cross-functionally to translate business requirements into scalable AI solutions, maintaining rigorous model governance and continuous performance monitoring.
*   Lead initiatives to enhance operational customer experience, utilising AI-driven verbatim analytics to inform workflow improvements and service design.
*   Formulate and execute the operations AI strategy, ensuring alignment with enterprise objectives and regulatory frameworks.
*   Champion the adoption of no-code/low-code AI solutions, accelerating delivery of bespoke automation tailored to operational needs.
*   Maintain oversight of AI solution lifecycle, ensuring continuous improvement, ethical standards, and measurable business impact.

Job Requirements:

*   Bachelor's degree in Business Analytics, Data Science, Computer Science, Engineering, or related quantitative field. Master's degree preferred.
*   Minimum 4 years of progressive experience in business analytics, AI implementation, or operations optimization within financial services. Proven track record of deploying machine learning solutions and workflow automation in regulated environments.
*   Advanced proficiency in data analytics tools (Python, R, SQL, Tableau/Power BI)
*   Experience with AI/ML platforms and Natural Language Processing
*   Knowledge of API integration and workflow automation tools
*   Familiarity with cloud platforms and data governance frameworks
*   Financial Industry operations and customer lifecycle management
*   Understanding of Hong Kong regulatory requirements (PDPO, insurance regulations)
*   Experience with customer touchpoint optimization and conversion analytics
