### Description\nOur client, a privately owned, medium-sized company based near Leipzig, is seeking a dedicated **Technician (m/f/d)** in **Building Automation / HVAC Systems**. For many years, the company has been a reliable partner for public clients and industrial customers in Central Germany, known for delivering **tailored automation solutions, short decision-making paths, and genuine team spirit.** This role is a key position, contributing to the programming, commissioning, and maintenance of advanced building automation projects, with a significant focus on HVAC systems.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Programming and commissioning of building automation projects, especially in the HVAC sector.\n- Execution of installation, maintenance, and repair work on measurement, control, and regulation technology.\n- Troubleshooting, fault rectification, and technical optimizations.\n- Documentation, creation of test and service protocols.\n- Training and instruction of users at the customer's site.\n- Decentralized work – optionally from home office or directly at the customer's site.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed vocational training in **Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, Automation Technology, Computer Science, or comparable fields.**\n- Ideally, initial experience in **MSR (measurement, control, and regulation), DDC (direct digital control), or building automation systems.**\n- Basic knowledge of HVAC technology and good technical understanding.\n- Interest in IT and network technology, programming, or digitalization of technical systems.\n- Independent, structured way of working and enjoyment of teamwork.\n- Class B driver's license and willingness to travel within the specified regions.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a skilled technician looking to advance their career within a stable, reputable company. The role offers a perfect blend of technical challenge and collaborative teamwork, allowing for significant impact on diverse projects. The company's commitment to employee well-being is evident through its flat hierarchies, open communication, and an attractive benefits package that includes a permanent contract, 30 days of vacation, a competitive salary with a 13th month, a company car (also for private use), comprehensive equipment, and robust pension plans. Furthermore, the strong emphasis on thorough onboarding and individual professional development ensures long-term career growth. The flexibility of working from home or directly with clients adds to the appeal, making this a truly modern and supportive work environment.
