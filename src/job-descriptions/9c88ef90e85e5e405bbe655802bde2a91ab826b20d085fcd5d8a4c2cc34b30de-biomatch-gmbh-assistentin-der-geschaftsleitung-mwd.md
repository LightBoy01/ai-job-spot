### Description
This role provides essential administrative and project support to the Head of Finance and other key departments within a dynamic and modern work environment. You will be instrumental in coordinating complex schedules, preparing strategic presentations, and managing various financial projects, contributing directly to the operational and strategic success of the organization. The position offers a hybrid work model and flexible hours, emphasizing work-life balance and a collaborative team spirit.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate appointments, travel arrangements, and overarching administrative processes.
- Prepare and design impactful presentations in both German and English.
- Provide operational and strategic support to the Head of Finance.
- Organize and oversee various sub-projects within the finance environment.
- Gather information and create concise analyses and evaluations.
- Support the Purchasing, IT, and Financial Administration departments.
- Maintain and enter relevant data into the ERP system.

### Qualifications
- Completed commercial vocational training or a comparable qualification.
- Proven professional experience in an assistant or support role at a management level.
- Proficiency in MS Office, particularly Excel, PowerPoint, and Word.
- Desirable: Initial experience with modern AI tools (e.g., Copilot).
- Very good command of written and spoken English.
- Structured way of working, reliability, and strong organizational talent.
- Communicative personality with a service-oriented mindset and team spirit.
