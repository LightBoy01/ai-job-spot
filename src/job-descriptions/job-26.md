# Prompt & Context Engineer

### Description

NTT DATA is a top 10 global IT services provider, and we are looking for a skilled Prompt & Context Engineer to join our team. In this role, you will be responsible for designing, developing, and optimizing the prompts and contextual information that power our clients' Large Language Model (LLM) applications. You will work closely with our data scientists, AI strategists, and clients to ensure that our LLM-based solutions are accurate, reliable, and aligned with business goals. This is a unique opportunity to specialize in the emerging field of prompt engineering and make a significant impact on the future of AI.

### Responsibilities

- Design and develop effective prompts for a wide range of LLM applications.
- Curate and structure the contextual information that is provided to LLMs.
- Analyze and debug LLM performance to identify and address issues.
- Develop and implement strategies for testing and evaluating prompts and context.
- Stay up-to-date with the latest research and best practices in prompt engineering.
- Collaborate with clients and internal teams to define requirements and deliver high-quality solutions.

### Qualifications

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Linguistics, or a related field.
- 3+ years of experience in a technical role, such as software engineer, data scientist, or linguist.
- Strong understanding of natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning concepts.
- Experience with LLMs and prompt engineering is a strong plus.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Strong communication and collaboration skills.
