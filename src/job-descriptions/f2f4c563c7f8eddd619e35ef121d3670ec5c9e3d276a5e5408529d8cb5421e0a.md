Career Opportunities: Lead Solutions Consultant (49779)
=======================================================

Requisition ID **49779** - Posted  - **Int'l Sales - Solution Consulting**

**OPENTEXT - THE INFORMATION COMPANY**

OpenText is a global leader in information management, where innovation, creativity, and collaboration are the key components of our corporate culture. As a member of our team, you will have the opportunity to partner with the most highly regarded companies in the world, tackle complex issues, and contribute to projects that shape the future of digital transformation.

**AI-First. Future-Driven. Human-Centered.**

At OpenText, AI is at the heart of everything we do—powering innovation, transforming work, and empowering digital knowledge workers. We're hiring talent that AI can't replace to help us shape the future of information management. Join us.

YOUR IMPACT
===========

As a **Lead Solution Consultant** within OpenText’s **Cybersecurity Solutions** team, you will be at the forefront of helping customers and partners build resilience against cyber threats. You will secure technical wins, promote OpenText solutions, and guide organizations in safeguarding their critical assets while ensuring compliance and continuity amid unforeseen cyber events. Leveraging your deep technical expertise, you will deliver impactful sales presentations, lead solution demonstrations, conduct technical evaluations (POCs), and serve as a trusted advisor to both technical and executive stakeholders.

WHAT THE ROLE OFFERS
====================

*   The opportunity to become a **subject matter expert** in OpenText’s cybersecurity solution portfolios, including Application Security, Security Operations, Identity and Access Management, and Data Security—with training and mentorship provided.
    
*   A **highly collaborative role** working closely with Account Executives, Solutions Consultants, partners, and customers to drive revenue growth and customer success.
    
*   **Ownership of technical solutioning**, including conducting deep-dive assessments, leading demonstrations, and designing tailored cybersecurity solutions.
    
*   The chance to **shape customer strategies** around Microsoft 365, Azure AD, encryption, data loss prevention, and cloud-based data security practices.
    
*   A **dynamic, global environment** offering opportunities to influence product direction, contribute to cross-sell initiatives, and conduct customer business reviews.
    

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED
========================

**Education & Experience**

*   Bachelor’s degree (advanced coursework preferred) and **12+ years of relevant experience**.
    
*   CISSP certification is desired.
    

**Technical Skills**

*   Deep understanding of the **IT data and cyber threat landscape**.
    
*   Hands-on experience with **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** and **Machine Learning (ML)** solutions, including awareness of potential AI-related security threats.
    
*   Proficiency in **network technologies, protocols, administration, virtualization** (VMWare, Hyper-V, KVM), and **SaaS services** (O365, Salesforce, Dynamics).
    
*   Expertise in **data security and protection**—including encryption, DLP, threat intelligence, and incident response.
    
*   Knowledge of **compliance standards and regulations**: GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, HITRUST, SOC2, PCI-DSS, ISO 27001, FedRAMP, DKIM/DMARC.
    
*   Proficiency with **threat detection and mitigation tools** such as SIEM, XDR, and EDR.
    
*   Experience with **Identity and Access Management (IAM)**, governance, and administration.
    
*   Familiarity with **data governance and protection practices**.
    
*   Working knowledge of **Application Security**, API integration, **container security**, and **Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC)**.
    
*   Competency with **cloud platforms** (AWS, Azure, GCP).
    
*   **Scripting proficiency** in Python, Bash, or PowerShell.
    

**Leadership & Problem-Solving**

*   Demonstrated ability to act independently, make critical decisions, and design innovative technical solutions.
    
*   A **consultative approach** with proven success influencing both technical and business stakeholders, including C-level executives.
    

**Other Attributes**

*   Exceptional written and verbal communication skills.
    
*   Strong analytical, decision-making, and problem-solving abilities.
    
*   Collaborative, adaptable, and globally minded team player.
    

SALARY STATEMENT
================

Compensation for this role will be competitive and based on experience, skill level, and market conditions. In addition to base pay, OpenText offers performance-based incentives, comprehensive benefits, and career growth opportunities within a global organization.
