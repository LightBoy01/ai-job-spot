### Description\nAre you a creative and motivated Content Creator (m/w/d) who loves planning, shooting, editing, and staging content for Instagram? This is your chance to build a channel \"from zero to something\" and leave your unique creative mark. We are looking for someone to develop a new Instagram channel with a dermatologist in Würzburg, focusing on high-quality video content, added value, and community engagement. This role is not about product sales or practice advertising, but rather about conveying:\n*   Understandable dermatological knowledge (derma, allergies, etc.)\n*   Visually high-quality production\n*   With humor, empathy, and aesthetics\n*   Serious, but approachable\nThis position is ideal as a part-time job or freelance role, offering 4-8 flexible hours per week.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs our Content Creator, you will be responsible for:\n*   **Content Development:**\n    *   Conceiving format ideas\n    *   Providing feedback\n    *   Building a recognizable visual style\n*   **On-site Filming in Würzburg:**\n    *   Planning and execution of film shoots\n    *   Setting up camera/smartphone, lighting, audio (equipment provided)\n*   **Editing & Post-production:**\n    *   Editing Reels (CapCut / Premiere / Final Cut)\n    *   Adding music, subtitles, hooks, and dynamic elements\n\n### Qualifications\nYou are the perfect fit if you:\n*   Are confident in filming and editing\n*   Have an eye for aesthetics and visual language\n*   Understand hooks and storytelling\n*   Can work independently\n*   Enjoy giving feedback\n*   Sensibly apply trends\n*   Are located in Würzburg\nThis role is ideal for students in Media Communications, Film/Media Production, Communication Design, Journalism, Marketing/Social Media, or talented creators with relevant experience.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a truly special opportunity for an aspiring or experienced Content Creator to take full creative control and build a significant social media presence from the ground up. You'll collaborate directly with a dermatology expert, gaining unique insights and creating impactful, educational content. The role offers remarkable flexibility, the chance to develop a strong professional portfolio, and clear pathways for long-term positioning, potentially evolving into a Social Video Producer. It's a role where your creativity genuinely makes a difference and helps shape a new brand identity.
