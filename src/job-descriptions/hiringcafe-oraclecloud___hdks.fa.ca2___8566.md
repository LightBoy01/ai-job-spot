Reporting to the AVP, Product and Underwriting, the role is responsible for leading strategic development and execution of underwriting policies, practices, and risk management for direct-to-consumer home and auto insurance products. This role ensures profitable growth, regulatory compliance, and competitive customer-centric products while fostering innovation in a digital-first environment.

**What can you expect from this role?**

**Strategic Leadership**

*   Develop and implement underwriting strategies aligned with company operational plan and market trends.
*   Lead underwriting initiatives to optimize pricing, risk selection, and portfolio performance.
*   Monitor competitive landscape and regulatory changes to inform product innovation and differentiation.
*   Collaborate with executive leadership on product development and market expansion.

**Operational Oversight**

*   Oversee day-to-day underwriting philosophy, ensuring efficiency, accuracy, and compliance.
*   Monitor underwriting guidelines, workflows, and quality assurance protocols.
*   Set performance goals, conduct evaluations, and implement training programs.
*   Foster a culture of accountability, innovation, and continuous improvement.

**Technology & Innovation**

*   Partner with product and tech teams to enhance underwriting automation and digital tools.
*   Leverage data analytics and AI to improve risk assessment and decision-making.
*   Drive adoption of modern underwriting platforms and customer-centric solutions.

**Stakeholder Collaboration**

*   Work cross-functionally with digital, product, portfolio management, pricing, claims, marketing, analytics and legal teams.
*   Represent underwriting in strategic planning, regulatory discussions, and partner engagements.
*   Provide insights and reporting to senior leadership on underwriting performance and competitive landscape.

**What do you bring to the role?**

*   Bachelor’s degree in business, Insurance, Actuarial Science, or related field (MBA or CIP/FCIP designation is an asset)
*   8+ years of progressive experience in personal insurance, with a strong focus on product management and underwriting 
*   Proven leadership experience in a direct-to-consumer or digital insurance environment
*   Strong collaboration skills, strategic thinking and stakeholder management
*   Deep understanding of home and auto insurance products, pricing, and regulatory frameworks
*   Excellent analytical and problem solving with the ability to select and implement best solution
*   Advanced decision-making skills 
*   Strong proficiency in Microsoft Office suite of products 

**Key Competencies**

*   Strategic Thinking & Execution
*   Risk Management & Compliance
*   Leadership & Team Development
*   Customer-Centric Mindset
*   Digital Fluency & Innovation
*   Collaboration & Influence
