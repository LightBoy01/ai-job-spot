We are seeking a detail-oriented and organized Data Entry Clerk to accurately input, update, and maintain company information in various databases and systems. The ideal candidate will be reliable, tech-savvy, and able to manage large volumes of data with speed and accuracy.

Tasks
-----

**Responsibilities**

*   Enter, update, and verify data in company systems and databases
*   Review data for errors, inconsistencies, or missing information
*   Maintain data confidentiality and security
*   Retrieve data for reports and analysis when requested
*   Prepare and sort documents for data entry
*   Generate regular data reports or summaries
*   Assist with administrative tasks as needed

Requirements
------------

**Qualifications**

*   High school diploma or equivalent (Associate degree preferred)
*   Proven experience in data entry or administrative support
*   Strong typing skills (recommended: 45+ WPM)
*   Excellent attention to detail and accuracy
*   Proficiency with MS Office (Excel, Word) and data management software
*   Ability to work independently and meet deadlines
*   Strong organizational and time-management skills

**Preferred Skills (Optional)**

*   Experience with CRM or ERP systems
*   Knowledge of spreadsheets, formulas, and data validation
*   Basic understanding of data confidentiality and data protection principles
