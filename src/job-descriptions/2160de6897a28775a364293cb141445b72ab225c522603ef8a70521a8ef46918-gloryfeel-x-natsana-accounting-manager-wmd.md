### Description\nnatsana unites four of the most successful and fastest-growing brands in high-quality food supplements, including natural elements, Nature Love, gloryfeel, and Feel Natural, under one roof. With a strong partnership with Bayer AG since 2025, natsana is a true powerhouse for quality, innovation, and growth, driven by a mission to make health and well-being accessible to all through natural, scientifically-backed products.\n\nWe are a dynamic, ambitious, and energetic company with a culture founded on appreciation, teamwork, and transparent communication. We seek proactive co-creators who are eager to take responsibility, challenge the status quo, and find joy in their work. If you are passionate about making a real impact and thrive in an environment that values fun and flexibility, you belong with our natsana Allstars-Team.\n\nWe are looking for an **Accounting Manager (w/m/d)** to join us on a full-time, permanent basis. This role offers flexible working hours and operates under a hybrid model, allowing work from our Düsseldorf or Hamburg offices, or home office. You will report directly to the Director Finance Controlling within our Finance & Accounting team.\n\nAs a central part of our Finance team, you will play a key role in operational accounting, ensuring efficient, digitally supported processes. You will collaborate closely with various departments like Sales, Purchasing, and Controlling, and actively support monthly and annual financial statements.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Accounting Support and Pre-Posting:**\n  - Independent pre-posting of incoming and outgoing invoices.\n  - Booking of business transactions using DATEV Unternehmen online.\n- **Digitalization and System Maintenance:**\n  - Support and further development of the ERP system regarding invoicing and accounting processes.\n  - Assistance in setting up and maintaining booking logic and workflows within the ERP system.\n- **Invoice and Payment Approval:**\n  - Processing invoice runs via digital approval tools like Candis.\n  - Maintaining creditor and debtor master data.\n- **Retail Sales Accounting:**\n  - Processing retail sales, including cash reconciliations, payment matching, and revenue posting.\n  - Acting as the interface between retail sales and finance.\n- **Month-End and Year-End Preparations:**\n  - Support in the preparation of monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements in accordance with HGB (German Commercial Code).\n  - Creation of accruals, deferrals, and booking vouchers in coordination with the tax advisor or auditor.\n- **Additional Tasks:**\n  - Participation in internal projects for process optimization in accounting.\n  - Support for tax audits and internal audits.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed commercial training (e.g., Industrial Clerk, Tax Clerk, Office Clerk) or comparable qualification, with at least 2 years of relevant professional experience in accounting.\n- Good accounting knowledge; experience with DATEV Unternehmen online is a plus.\n- Proficient in using digital tools and ERP systems; experience in system maintenance and support is a plus.\n- Experience with digital approval tools like Candis is desirable.\n- Structured, independent, and precise way of working.\n- Team player, responsible, and highly service-oriented.\n- Proficient in MS Office, especially Excel.\n- German language skills at a minimum C-level.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a detail-oriented Accounting professional with at least two years of experience who is eager to drive digitalization and process optimization within a fast-growing, values-driven company. natsana offers an exceptionally supportive and flexible hybrid work environment, generous vacation, and a strong commitment to employee development. If you thrive on challenging the status quo and contributing to a dynamic team, this role provides a compelling blend of responsibility and growth within a company that truly makes an impact.
