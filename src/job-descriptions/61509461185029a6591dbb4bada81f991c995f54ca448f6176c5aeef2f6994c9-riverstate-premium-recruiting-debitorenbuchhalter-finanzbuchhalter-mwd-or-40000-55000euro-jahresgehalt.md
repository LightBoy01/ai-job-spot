### Description\nJoin an open and experienced finance team at a company dedicated to a healthy environment, characterized by a vibrant Scandinavian-inspired culture. This role as a Debtor/Financial Accountant offers a dynamic work environment where your expertise is valued, your opinion heard, and direct collaboration is key, both internally and externally. The company prides itself on modern structures, efficient decision-making, and strong support for work-life balance through flexible hours and home office options, alongside attractive benefits. Even if you're early in your career, this is an opportunity to grow within a meaningful and stable industry.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Independently manage accounts receivable, including dunning processes.\n- Monitor, analyze, and post open items.\n- Resolve payment discrepancies through direct customer contact.\n- Conduct collection and insolvency procedures.\n- Post incoming payments and credit notes, maintaining debtor accounts.\n- Assist with monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements.\n- Analyze payment behavior and contribute to optimization projects.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed commercial vocational training or equivalent.\n- Good German language skills, both written and spoken.\n- Proficient in MS Office applications.\n- Professional experience in receivables management or dunning is a plus (beginners are also very welcome).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a compelling opportunity for a motivated individual looking to make an impact within a supportive and forward-thinking organization. The blend of a strong environmental mission, a positive Scandinavian work culture, and excellent benefits like flexible work and home office options, creates an attractive package. The inclusive approach, welcoming both experienced professionals and beginners, signals a commitment to growth and mentorship. If you thrive in a collaborative environment where your accounting skills contribute to a greater purpose, this role offers both stability and genuine development prospects.
