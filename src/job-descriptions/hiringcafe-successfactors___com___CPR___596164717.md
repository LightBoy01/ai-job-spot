**PURPOSE OF THE POSITION:**

The coordinator is responsible for managing day-to-day interactions with key passenger accounts in the east and for supporting operational oversight and planning, contract development, stakeholder engagement with third party passenger service providers.

**POSITION ACCOUNTABILITIES:**

*   Serve as the primary liaison for passenger service providers operating on CPKC’s network.
*   Responsible for tracking passenger performance metrics, documenting and resolving performance and safety incidents
*   Ensure adherence to contractual obligations by both parties, and advising on performance and efficiency improvements
*   Track special movement requests and other billable passenger services and ensure timely and accurate billing
*   Collaborate with engineering teams to develop capital plans for railway plant maintenance
*   Assist in operational planning and project oversight
*   Attend cross functional meetings and work directly with internal and external stakeholders
*   Work closely with stakeholders to develop and implement cost -recovery initiatives

**POSITION REQUIREMENTS:**

*   Bachelor's Degree required
*   3 years' experience in freight or commuter railway operations or other transportation logistics
*   Valid driver’s license
*   Ability to work collaboratively with multiple stakeholders
*   Proficiency in Microsoft Office
*   Contract negotiation, account management, or other external facing roles
*   SAP, accounting software or basic AI (Artificial Intelligence) prompting
