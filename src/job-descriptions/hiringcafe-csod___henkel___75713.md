#### About this Position

At Henkel, you’ll be part of an organization that’s shaping the future through innovation, sustainability and collaboration. With our trusted brands like Persil®, ‘all®, Loctite®, Snuggle®, and Schwarzkopf® and our cutting-edge technologies, you’ll have countless opportunities to explore new paths and grow.   
  
This position is with our Adhesive Technologies business unit – where we empower our people to transform industries and provide our customers with a competitive advantage through adhesives, sealants and functional coatings.   
  
**Dare to learn new skills, advance in your career and make an impact at Henkel.**    

#### What you´ll do

We are looking for a visionary Digital Transformation Leader with strong experience in innovation environments to scope, prioritize, and implement the digital modernization of our innovation functions. As a global leader in adhesives, sealants & surface treatments, we seek to accelerate innovation cycles, enhance product development and application engineering capabilities while strengthening collaboration and knowledge sharing across all functions. This role requires a strong blend of strategic thinking, technical expertise and change management skills to lead cross-functional teams.

**Responsibilities:**

*   Develop and lead Henkel’s Industrial Adhesive SBUs digital transformation strategy in alignment with business goals, focusing on operational excellence, innovation and customer value. 
*   Augment cross-functional collaboration between Henkel’s IT, centralized digital innovation teams and the Industrial adhesive SBUs Innovation and commercial teams. 
*   Champion the adoption of digital tools, data platforms, advanced modeling, predictive analytics and AI/ML models to increase the effectiveness of innovation functions. 
*   Oversee and drive change management strategies for digital initiatives, ensuring stakeholder engagement, effective communication, and smooth adoption. 
*   Consolidate and streamline internal access to product and application knowledge for customer support and innovation.
*   Continuously monitor technological trends and assess their relevance to the business. If needed, build partnerships with external vendors, consultants, and tech providers. 
*   Champion a digital-first mindset across the organization and collaborate with leadership teams to drive cultural and operational change. 
*   Develop upskilling/training programs with global counterparts and create communication strategies to support digital adoption across all functions interacting with innovation teams. 
*   Increase customer technical intimacy through digital solutions, CRM enhancements, and data-driven service models. 
*   Track and report performance metrics, success factors, and KPIs related to digital transformation.

#### What makes you a good fit

**Education and Experience:** 

*   Master’s or PhD in Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, Materials Science, Data Science, or a related technical discipline.
*   10+ years of experience in R&D leadership, digital transformation, project management or innovation strategy within the adhesives, specialty chemicals, or process industries. 
*   Expertise in understanding digital tools used in research environments: ELNs, LIMS, modeling software, data lakes, and AI/ML platforms.
*   Proven track record of managing complex, cross-functional digital programs and teams across global organizations.

**Skills and Competencies:**

*   Strong stakeholder management skills and the ability to communicate technical concepts to non-technical teams are important.
*   Strong leadership, communication, and influencing skills at all levels of the business.
*   Passion for driving innovation, customer-centric design, and continuous improvement through digital technology.
