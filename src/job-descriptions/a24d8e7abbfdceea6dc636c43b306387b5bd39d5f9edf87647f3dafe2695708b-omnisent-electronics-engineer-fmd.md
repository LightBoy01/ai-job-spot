**Founded in late 2024, Omnisent is a Munich-based acoustic AI company developing next-generation sensing systems that turn sound into real-time intelligence. The team combines deep expertise in physics, machine learning, and mechanical engineering — united by a mission to unlock the untapped intelligence of the acoustic world.**

At the core of Omnisent’s innovation are ultra-low-power sonic devices and the company’s Large Acoustic Model (LAM) — a foundation model trained on the complex world of non-speech audio. This unique combination enables the real-time detection, classification, and localization of acoustic events — whether identifying drones in contested airspace or tracing air leaks in manufacturing systems.

Tasks
-----

*   **Design and optimize** ultra-low-noise, low-power mixed-signal electronics for advanced sensing systems.
*   **Develop analog and mixed-signal front-ends**, including amplification, filtering, and ADC interfacing, with a focus on precision, stability, and efficiency.
*   **Lead PCB layout** with emphasis on EMI/EMC-aware design, grounding strategies, and robust signal integrity.
*   **Prototype and manufacture PCBs in-house**, including assembly, bring-up, and debugging.
*   **Define and execute product-level electronics testing**, including noise analysis, performance validation, and system-level integration tests.
*   **Ensure designs align with compliance standards**, and coordinate with external testing houses as needed.
*   **Play a key role in DFM/DFT refinement** and electronics-focused product lifecycle management.
*   **Collaborate with EMS partners** on NPI builds, production feedback, and yield optimization.
*   **Work closely with RF and mechanical teams** to ensure clean integration of electronics into compact, ruggedized hardware.

Requirements
------------

**Basic Requirements:**

*   **Bachelor’s or Master’s degree** in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, or a related field.
*   **3+ years of experience** in analog/mixed-signal design and PCB development.
*   **Strong knowledge** of low-noise analog design, grounding, shielding, and EMI/EMC principles.
*   **Proficiency with PCB design software** (Altium, KiCad, etc.).
*   **Hands-on experience** with test equipment (oscilloscopes, analyzers, signal generators).
*   **Understanding of compliance standards** and designing electronics for certification.
*   **Experience with DFM/DFT** and taking hardware into production.
*   **Strong communication skills** in English (German a plus).
*   **EU work authorization.**

**Preferred Requirements:**

*   **Experience with precision sensing systems.**
*   **Prior collaboration** with EMS partners on production builds.
*   **Familiarity with formal product lifecycle processes** (PLM, ECO workflows).
*   **Experience participating** in compliance/EMC test campaigns.
*   **Startup experience** with a desire to help build a category-defining product.
