### Description\nAs a Software Implementation Engineer at Quantum Maritime, you will be a pivotal member of our rapidly expanding Quantum Maritime Engineering Team, based in Rostock. Your primary mission is to integrate Quantum Systems' advanced software modules into our Unmanned Surface Vessels (USVs) and Unmanned Underwater Vessels (UUVs). This involves working closely with hardware and software development stakeholders to embed core technology and integrate maritime products into the #MOSAIC System. This role is crucial in solidifying Quantum Systems' position as the benchmark for sea and underwater data collection solutions, requiring a candidate who thrives in high-pressure, innovative environments and is eager to shape the future of maritime operations.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Implement Software routines on maritime hardware\n- Adopt hardware interfaces to software\n- Test software routines on maritime hardware\n- Adopt software from UAVs to control USVs and UUVs\n- Implement swarm functionalities on the USVs and UUVs\n- Interact with the software development of Quantum Systems\n\n### Qualifications\n- A degree in the field of hardware-based software engineering\n- At least 5 years of professional experience in implementing software in autonomous vehicles\n- A strong interest in bringing technology to the next level\n- An open, communicative, very friendly, and competent manner, making it easy for you to be seen as an internal expert\n- Highly self-motivated and able to drive your area of responsibility forward independently\n- Fluent communication in English\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an experienced Software Implementation Engineer passionate about cutting-edge autonomous vehicle technology, specifically in the maritime domain. Quantum Systems is a globally recognized organization at the forefront of UUV innovation, offering a fast-paced, agile environment where individual contributions make a tangible impact. The role promises significant professional growth, working alongside ambitious industry pioneers to redefine mission versatility and performance for defense, security, and public sector clients worldwide. If you're driven, an expert in your field, and eager to shape the future of reconnaissance, this role offers a chance to be at the heart of truly next-generation solutions.
