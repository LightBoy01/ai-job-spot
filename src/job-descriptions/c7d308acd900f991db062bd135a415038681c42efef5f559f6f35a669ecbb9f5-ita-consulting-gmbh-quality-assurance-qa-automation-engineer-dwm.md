### Description\nAs a Quality Assurance (QA) Automation Engineer (d/w/m) at LetMeShip, you will ensure our web applications run smoothly and meet our high quality standards. You will optimize testing processes and develop automated tests to continuously improve our platform. We offer an international and familial work environment, where a \"People first\" culture isn't just a motto, but a lived reality, emphasizing employee well-being and a supportive atmosphere.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Perform manual tests to ensure the quality of our website and applications.\n- Develop and implement comprehensive test strategies.\n- Build and maintain automated test cases for long-term efficiency improvement.\n- Collaborate with developers, designers, and product owners throughout the SDLC to define acceptance criteria, test cases, and prioritize quality.\n- Analyze bugs, document test results, and create meaningful test reports.\n- Support the strengthening and further development of a quality mindset within the team.\n- Ensure compliance with quality standards and continuously optimize testing processes.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Several years of experience in testing, ideally in web applications.\n- Knowledge in the development and implementation of test strategies.\n- Basic understanding of HTML, CSS, and common browser development tools.\n- Experience with Playwright or comparable test automation frameworks like Selenium or Cypress.\n- Experience in API testing (REST/SOAP).\n- Enjoy sharing your testing knowledge with the team and working in a cross-functional team.\n- Analytical thinking, as well as a meticulous and structured working approach.\n- Team spirit and self-initiative are natural for you.\n- Excellent communication skills to clearly explain problems and constructively challenge decisions when interacting with Product Managers and Software Developers.\n- Fluent English skills, as you will be working in an international team.\n- You regularly question existing processes and strive to continuously improve yourself and your work.\n\n### Author's Take\nJoin a company with 25 years of experience that maintains the dynamic spirit of a startup. This role offers excellent benefits designed to support a healthy work-life balance and personal growth. You'll enjoy 30 days of vacation, flexible hours with hybrid work options, and transparent overtime compensation. Financial perks include a Deutschlandticket subsidy or garage parking contribution, a monthly €50 SpenditCard, and corporate benefits with attractive discounts. Your future is secured with company pension schemes, and support is available through the Fürstenberg Institute. You’ll also have the opportunity for workation. A structured onboarding process ensures you feel welcome and confident from day one, allowing you to quickly contribute and reach your full potential.
