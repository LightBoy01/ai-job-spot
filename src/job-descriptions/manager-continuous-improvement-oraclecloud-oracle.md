<p>This position is a key member of a regional business transformation team, driving an end-to-end analysis of a functional Business Operations &amp; performance measures, identifying opportunities for profitable growth, and driving business transformation leveraging the Continuous Improvement (CI) principles, system thinking, and big data analytics techniques (adopting AI). Additionally, this leadership role requires operational experience in business financials, functions, and demonstrated ability to collaboratively work functions in order to drive change.&nbsp;This role will be a member of a regional team that is responsible for driving business analytics, use of Business Transformation toolset with the operating functions, project management of the business transformation projects and implementing appropriate operating cadence for the business.</p><p>Grade : 50</p><p>Internal Tittle: Manager, Business Transformation</p><p>Preferred Location: Preference will be given to candidates residing in close proximity (Near Site) to one of our major AmeriPath labs located in either the Southwest and Southeast territory.</p>
Responsibilities
<ul><li>Owns the learning, application, and adoption of Business Transformation principles and practices for a particular function within a regional business with the goal of building localized self-sufficiency, sustainability, and scalability of improvement effort within the function.</li><li>Drives the use of big data analytics tools (adopting AI) to tackle system level problems.</li><li>Works with functional leaders as well as team members to drive a cultural change shift from dashboards and analytics towards actions and results.</li><li>Owns a portfolio of initiatives (i) strategic business transformation projects, and (ii)operational execution for a regional function &nbsp;that result in large-scale improvement across designated functions&nbsp;and businesses.</li><li>Demonstrated understanding of continuous improvement, process management, or system thinking (value stream thinking).</li><li>Proven track record in facilitating cross functional discussions, targeted workshops and Kaizens to achieve strategic &amp; operational objectives. ​</li><li>General knowledge of Hoshin and breakthrough planning.</li><li>Demonstrates expertise in business transformation projects using program management principles and tools, and has a track record of success leading and influencing at the mid to senior leadership level without direct authority in driving change,&nbsp;</li><li>Captures, quantifies, and reapplies best-in-class practices within a regional function such as laboratories, Logistics or Patient Services.</li><li>Acts as a change agent to guide teams toward success.</li><li>Works with local team members to identify and prioritize continuous improvement opportunities for a regional functional business.&nbsp;Works with functional and site leaders as a “thought leader” to create future state improvement roadmaps with clear executable plans that are in line with the business strategies and improvement needs.</li><li>Contributes toward building a grass-roots culture of continuous improvement, use of big data analytics techniques, system thinking, and cross-functional collaboration.</li></ul>
