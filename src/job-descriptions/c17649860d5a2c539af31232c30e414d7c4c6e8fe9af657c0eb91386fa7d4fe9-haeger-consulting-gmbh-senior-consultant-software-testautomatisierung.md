### Description
This role is for a passionate Software Quality Consultant focused on elevating quality standards for clients through test automation and innovative solutions. You will be instrumental in designing test architectures, implementing modern automation tools, and advising clients on best practices in quality assurance within diverse IT landscapes like agile, DevOps, Cloud, and Mobile testing environments.

### Responsibilities
- Take responsibility for test architectures and automation, conceptualizing and maintaining automation frameworks, ensuring quality for business-critical applications, and advancing test processes.
- Implement modern automation solutions using tools like Playwright, REST Assured, Cucumber, Tricentis Tosca, or Cypress, integrating them seamlessly into CI/CD pipelines.
- Plan and manage testing activities, advise clients, coordinate test efforts, lead smaller teams, and support project/specialist teams in developing test strategies, quality assurance, and best practices.
- Analyze and report test results, evaluate outcomes, document deviations, monitor test progress, and initiate optimizations.
- Work on a variety of topics, bringing your expertise to agile teams, DevOps environments, Cloud, or Mobile testing, staying at the technological forefront.

### Qualifications
- A minimum of 2–4 years of practical experience in software testing, test automation, or quality assurance, ideally in a consulting role.
- Proficiency in multiple programming languages such as Java, Python, JavaScript, or C#, and confidence in using testing and performance tools like Playwright, JUnit, REST Assured, Tricentis Tosca, JMeter, or LoadRunner.
- Familiarity with modern test concepts and standards, including agile testing, risk-based strategies, or exploratory test design; an ISTQB certification is a plus.
- Clear and audience-appropriate communication skills when interacting with developers, Product Owners, or stakeholders, capable of understanding requirements, presenting results, and providing recommendations. Experience with REST/SOAP APIs is a plus.
- Demonstrated team spirit and leadership experience, having led smaller teams or professionally guided and developed colleagues.
- Native German speaker with no issues communicating in English.
