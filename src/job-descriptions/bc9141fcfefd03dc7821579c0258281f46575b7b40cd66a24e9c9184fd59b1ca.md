****About the role****

We are seeking a high-performance Engineering Manager with proven experience to lead a brand new Agentic AI Squad of Engineers/data scientists building Agentic AI capabilites at the core of all our tools.

  

****What you'll do****

  

****As an Engineering Manager, your daily workload might include:****

*   Hiring, leading, mentoring, and managing the best in class group of product Engineers, to build the best-in-class products within the AML/AF space, cultivating collaboration and innovation
*   Providing guidance, conducting performance reviews, and supporting career development for engineers
*   Overseeing the design, development, testing, and deployment of our SaaS product, with Agentic AI front and Centre, ensuring timely delivery and high-quality standards
*   Collaborating with other stakeholders to create engineering objectives for your team that align with business goals
*   Working closely with product teams to help steer both the functional and technical roadmap with focus on the end user
*   Driving scalable architecture and design solutions, ensuring performance, reliability, and maintainability of the SaaS product
*   Ensuring best practices for coding, security, testing, and performance optimization are followed
*   Staying updated with domain trends, technologies, promote its adoption across the tech stack and development process to drive innovation and efficiency
*   Developing and implementing policies, standards, and procedures for your engineering team that empowers them to be high performing

****Qualifications****

*   BS/MS from a top school in Computer Science or any related field
*   12+ years of software engineering experience, of which 5+ years managing teams, preferable B2B SaaS software
*   Previous experience in AML or Fraud or Banking (Nice to have)
*   Min of 1-2 years' experience with GEN AI/Agentic AI technologies building pilots or production ready systems
*   Proven track record of managing and scaling cross functional engineering teams at speed
*   Proven experience of creating a culture of data driven decision making
*   Proven experience in collaborating in an organizational unit of several independent engineering teams
*   Excellent communication, leadership, and organizational skills
*   Proven expertise in architecting and managing large-scale, complex distributed software systems
