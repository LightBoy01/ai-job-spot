### Description\nAre you an expert in large-scale battery storage and renewable energies? Then we need you!\n\nJoin a strong company dedicated to implementing solar and wind energy projects across the country. We are looking for you to plan and execute mega-projects in Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).\n\nAs a **Project Developer (m/f/d) Battery Storage**, you will be responsible for the entire project management of future battery storage projects, from site acquisition to turnkey handover. Based at our company's location in **Berlin**, you'll be developing tomorrow's projects. Beyond your desire to be a vital part of this groundbreaking vision, you should also be willing to travel, as regular visits to the projected battery storage sites are part of your duties.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- You will be responsible for identifying, coordinating, and managing battery storage projects.\n- You will accompany BESS projects from conception to turnkey handover.\n- You will conduct site analyses, secure land, and develop planning concepts.\n- You will assess project risks and oversee approval procedures.\n- You will report regularly on project status and collaborate closely with internal departments.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Relevant professional experience in a comparable position and with battery storage systems, for example, as a Project Developer in the fields of photovoltaics, solar, wind, or renewable energies.\n- Completed technical degree, for example, in Electrical Engineering, Energy Technology, Renewable Energies, Mechanical Engineering, or Industrial Engineering.\n- Experience with project management tools and MS Office.\n- Good understanding of business administration contexts.\n- Your communication skills make you a strong negotiation partner.\n- Fluent German and good English language skills.\n- Willingness to travel nationwide to visit project sites.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned Project Developer passionate about renewable energy to lead the charge on Germany's critical battery storage infrastructure. The company offers not only a competitive and regularly adjusted salary but also embraces modern work flexibility, extensive benefits including 30 days of leave, paid continuous education, and a supportive, state-of-the-art office environment. If you're ready to make a significant impact on sustainable energy development, this role in a forward-thinking Berlin-based company is for you.
