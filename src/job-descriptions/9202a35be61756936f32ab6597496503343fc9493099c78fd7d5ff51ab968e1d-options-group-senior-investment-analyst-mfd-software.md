Tasks
-----

This role focuses on growth investments in the **software** sector\*\*,\*\* providing an exciting opportunity to work with innovative companies and technologies:

*   Conduct comprehensive industry research to identify **majority and minority** investment opportunities in the **software** sector:
*   **Assist in the due diligence proces**s by analyzing and evaluating potential investments
*   Building and maintaining detailed **financial LBO models**
*   Monitor the **performance of portfolio companies**, providing regular updates and strategic advice to senior management
*   Assist in the preparation of **investment memorandums** for presentation to the investment committee
*   Communicate with executives of portfolio companies

Requirements
------------

*   Bachelor's degree in Finance, Economics, Business, or a related field
*   Professional work experience in private equity, investment banking, or growth equity
*   Experience with the **software industry** and a keen interest in technology trends
*   Ability to work independently and as part of a team in a fast-paced environment
*   Fluent German and English

*   Participatition in the carry scheme
