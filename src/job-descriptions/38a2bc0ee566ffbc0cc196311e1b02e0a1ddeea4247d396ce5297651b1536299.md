About the mission

Leadtech is on a mission to redefine how people interact with artificial intelligence. We are on a mission to redefine how people interact with artificial intelligence. Our goal is to create an accessible, user-friendly platform that empowers professionals and creators to harness the potential of AI across their daily workflows. By simplifying access and integration, we aim to make AI more practical, scalable, and impactful for a wide range of users. From day one, we are focused on delivering a profitable, standalone product with carefully curated capabilities and strong growth potential.

The Role

We are looking for a Project Lead with an entrepreneurial mindset to take ownership of this AI line product strategy and execution. This is not just a product role — it’s an opportunity to help shape the company from the ground up with a new external Consumer AI Product.

You will lead the development of our product, dedicated team, collaborating closely with cross-functional stakeholders. Over time, you’ll build and scale the team around you, defining the roadmap and driving the evolution of the product into a full-scale ecosystem and marketplace.

This role is ideal for someone who thrives in ambiguity, is excited to build from 0→1, and wants to make a tangible impact in one of the fastest-moving industries in the world.

Key Responsibilities

*   Lead Product  development – take product from concept to market launch with a small, agile team backed and supported by a large group organisation.
*   Define the product vision, strategy, and roadmap, aligned with business goals of rapid adoption and profitability.
*   Collaborate with engineering to design a modular, scalable architecture for integrating multiple AI models and APIs.
*   Build strong relationships with early users to validate assumptions and drive user-centric product decisions.
*   Establish success metrics and use data-driven insights to iterate quickly.
*   Over time, recruit, lead, and inspire a product team, growing the function as the company scales.
*   Partner with leadership on go-to-market strategy, monetization strategies, user metrics, and growth loops.
*   Act as a bridge between vision and execution, ensuring alignment across technical, business, and user needs.

**Requirements**

About You

*   Entrepreneurial background – founder, early-stage startup experience, or track record of building products from scratch.
*   Proven record of shipping AI products fast with quick iterations

*   5+ years of product management / product leadership experience in SaaS, AI, developer tools, or platforms.
*   Demonstrated ability to take an AI product from idea to MVP launch and beyond.
*   Strong technical fluency – comfortable working with engineers, APIs, and LLMs, with the ability to evaluate trade-offs.

*   Proven experience working in small, cross-functional teams, wearing multiple hats, and moving fast.
*   Strategic thinker with a hands-on, execution-first approach.
*   Strong leadership and communication skills, with the ability to inspire teams and align stakeholders.
*   A passion in A.I: Knowledge of the current trends in A.I Products, and where the industry is going from your insights. 

Nice to Have

*   Experience in AI/ML products, LLMs, or generative AI tools.
*   Background in freemium SaaS or subscription business models.
*   Exposure to platform ecosystems, SDKs, or marketplaces.
*   Familiarity with content creation, productivity, or workflow tools.
