### Description
Kohnle & Partner stands out as a distinctive tax firm, embodying modernity, digital processes, and dynamic growth. For over 45 years, our commitment to providing top working conditions, fostering a highly motivated team, and housing leading experts in high-quality offices has been the cornerstone of our success. We are recognized as one of the leading firms in the Baden region, serving companies and private individuals as competent partners, passionate problem-solvers, and innovative forward-thinkers.

Join our team at one of our locations in Karlsruhe, Weingarten, Offenburg, or Karlsbad. As a Tax Assistant (Steuerfachangestellter), you'll be empowered to do more than just juggle numbers. We are dedicated to supporting your personal and professional development through your training, enabling you to comprehensively and competently assist our clients with their tax and business management needs in the future.

### Responsibilities
*   Preparation of small tax returns and diverse financial accounting tasks.
*   Preparation and support for the creation of annual financial statements.
*   Review of tax assessments and correspondence with the tax office.
*   Conducting research activities.
*   Support for digital transformation initiatives.
*   Opportunity to engage in other areas that interest you and leverage your strengths.

### Qualifications
*   You are communicative and enjoy working with people.
*   You follow through on your commitments.
*   You are open to new ideas and changes.
*   You have high standards for the quality of your work.

**Desirable:**
*   A-levels (Fachhochschulreife / allgemeine Hochschulreife) or a secondary school leaving certificate (Realschulabschluss) with good grades.
*   Initial experience with MS-Office applications, especially Word and Excel.
