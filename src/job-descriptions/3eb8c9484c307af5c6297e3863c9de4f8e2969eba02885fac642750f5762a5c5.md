We currently have a career opportunity for a **Director** to join our team located in NYC. This role will have at least 25% travel.

  
 As a Director at Perficient, you will play a crucial role on the leadership team of our digital consultancy practice. You will collaborate with the sales team, industry partners, and subject matter experts to deliver and expand digital solutions that transform how our clients connect with their customers and grow their business. This will involve working with cross-functional teams to drive results and financial performance. Additionally, you will foster an engaging colleague culture that inspires strong team and individual performance, in line with Perficient's People Promise.

### Responsibilities

*   **Client Engagement & Sales Enablement**
    *   Serve as an industry-facing expert during sales pursuits and client meetings, helping articulate value propositions that resonate with banking clients.
    *   Collaborate closely with account teams and solution architects to shape proposals, presentations, and strategic roadmaps.
    *   Identify and open new opportunities through trusted advisor relationships and strategic insight.
*   **Thought Leadership**
    *   Represent the firm as a thought leader in the retail and commercial banking space through whitepapers, industry events, webinars, and roundtables.
    *   Monitor industry trends, regulatory changes, and innovation to ensure our offerings remain cutting-edge and relevant.
*   **Solution Development & Offerings**
    *   Partner with platform teams (Salesforce, Microsoft, Cloud providers) to co-create targeted financial services solutions aligned with client needs.
    *   Provide subject matter input into the development of new service offerings and go-to-market strategies.
*   **Consulting & Advisory Support**
    *   Advise clients on transformation strategy, digital operating models, customer engagement, and back-office modernization.
    *   Lead or support delivery teams with insights and direction grounded in real-world banking experience.

### Qualifications

*   10+ years of experience in retail and/or commercial banking, ideally including time spent in consulting or advisory roles.
    
*   Proven success in client-facing roles, including business development, pre-sales, or solutioning.
    
*   Strong understanding of digital transformation trends, including core modernization, open banking, customer experience, and embedded finance.
    
*   Demonstrated ability to leverage AI tools to enhance productivity, streamline workflows, and support data-informed task execution. 
*   Familiarity with AI-enhanced platforms is a plus. 
*   A solid understanding of AI capabilities and limitations including ethical considerations is expected
*   Experience with industry platforms and ecosystems (Salesforce, Microsoft, major cloud platforms).
    
*   Excellent communication skills with the ability to engage C-level executives.
    
*   Strategic thinker with a hands-on ability to develop actionable plans.
