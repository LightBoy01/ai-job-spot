**Job Description:**

As an Entry-Level Software Developer, you will play a key role in the development and maintenance of software solutions that support and enhance our 24/7 manufacturing operations. You will be part of a dynamic team responsible for the Manufacturing Execution System (MES)—a mission-critical platform that manages workflows, logistics, recipes, and specifications across the plant.

This is an excellent opportunity for a motivated individual who is eager to learn and explore AI and emerging technologies to help modernize and optimize manufacturing systems. You’ll gain hands-on experience in a fast-paced industrial environment while contributing to digital transformation initiatives.

**ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:**

*   Develop, test, and maintain software applications that support production efficiency, logistics control, and system reliability.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather requirements and deliver effective technical solutions.
*   Assist in integrating AI, automation, and other emerging technologies into MES and related systems.
*   Monitor and troubleshoot software issues in a 24/7 operational setting.
*   Participate in code reviews and contribute to continuous improvement efforts.
*   Document technical processes, configurations, and updates.
*   Stay current with industry trends and proactively suggest innovative solutions.

**Qualifications:**

REQUIRED:

*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
*   Familiarity with programming languages such as C#, Python, or JavaScript.
*   Basic understanding of software development principles and version control (e.g., Git).
*   Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
*   Effective communication and collaboration abilities.
*   Willingness to learn and explore AI, automation, and new technologies relevant to manufacturing.

PREFFERED:

*   Experience with databases (SQL or NoSQL).
*   Knowledge of Agile or DevOps practices.
*   Internship or project experience in software development.
*   Interest in AI applications like predictive maintenance, process optimization, or computer vision.
