**Regulatory Affairs Specialist, GEM – 1 Year Contract**
As a Regulatory Affairs Specialist, you will work within the Regulatory Affairs team, focusing on supporting daily regulatory operations throughout the region to help achieve regional business objectives. Your role will involve providing regulatory support to all divisions and regions within the EMEA area.
_This role will be 12 Months Temporary Contracted._
**Your responsibilities will include**:
*   Support the GEM region in analysis and maintenance of Regulatory Distribution Controls, including assessing current controls and drivers for change, collaborating with local teams on appropriate controls, and requesting control modifications
*   Support data analysis for the GEM RA Region
*   Support Submission preparation/tender/reimbursement activities through provision of documentation/information as requested.
*   Collaborate with country regulatory affairs to gather information related to continued improvement of global regulatory processes
*   Support introduction of efficiencies into local RA processes using digital tools, AI.
**What are we looking for:**
*   Education – degree level in a scientific discipline or equivalent work experience
*   0-2 years professional experience
*   Experience using Microsoft Office applications, experience in use of databases/applications, familiarity with working in a regulated environment.
