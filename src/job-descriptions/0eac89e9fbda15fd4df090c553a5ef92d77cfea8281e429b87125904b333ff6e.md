### Description

As the Director of AI Systems Tooling & Optimization at NBCUniversal, you will be at the forefront of shaping our technological future, particularly with the upcoming spin-off of a new, publicly traded company, 'Versant'. This leadership role is pivotal in evaluating, enhancing, and integrating our entire suite of analytics tools, AI/BI platforms, and agentic integrations. You will partner closely with our data science, analytics, and engineering teams to assess emerging technologies, optimize existing platforms, and ensure our systems deliver measurable business value. Your mission is to identify opportunities for automation and insight acceleration, establishing the governance and best practices that will power a new era of data-driven decision-making for a leading portfolio of news, sports, and entertainment brands.

### Responsibilities

*   Evaluate, select, and scale AI/BI and analytics technologies with a proven return on investment.
*   Optimize the performance, usability, and adoption of existing tools and platforms.
*   Partner with cross-functional teams to design, test, and integrate AI Agents and other advanced capabilities into core workflows.
*   Identify gaps and recommend new solutions across the analytics and systems tooling stack, including non-AI technologies.
*   Establish robust governance frameworks, usage guidelines, and performance KPIs for all systems and tools.
*   Lead change management initiatives and training programs to drive the adoption of new capabilities across the organization.
*   Continuously monitor industry trends to maintain a competitive and innovative tooling ecosystem.

### Qualifications

*   A Bachelor’s degree in a technical field.
*   Proficiency in SQL and experience programming in Python or a similar language.
*   Experience working with CI/CD managed systems, including source control (e.g., Git/GitHub) and Infrastructure as Code (e.g., Terraform).
*   Proven leadership in systems, analytics, or AI/BI program management with experience optimizing and integrating diverse platforms.
*   Strong technical and business acumen to effectively bridge the gap between data teams and business stakeholders.
*   Hands-on experience with agent platforms such as Microsoft Copilot Studio, Google Vertex AI, or similar tools for deploying and optimizing AI-driven solutions.
*   Demonstrated expertise in agent orchestration using frameworks like CrewAI or Google ADK to coordinate multi-agent workflows.
*   Skilled in driving organizational adoption of new tools and methodologies through effective change management.

### Author's Take

**Candidate Question:** "Given the planned spin-off of 'Versant', how does this role go beyond maintaining existing systems to actively shaping the technological identity of the new company?"

**Insider's Answer:** "That's the core of the opportunity. You're not just inheriting a legacy stack; you're essentially being handed the architect's pen for a multi-billion dollar, publicly-traded company from its inception. While we have incredible assets like USA Network and CNBC, 'Versant' needs its own distinct, modern, and agile data infrastructure to compete. This role is tasked with defining that. You'll be the one deciding which AI platforms will give our news teams an edge, which BI tools will empower our ad sales leaders, and how we automate processes to be leaner than our competitors. It’s less about upkeep and more about building a strategic arsenal of technology that will be baked into the company's DNA from day one."

**Candidate Question:** "This role involves extensive collaboration with data science, analytics, and engineering. What is the current dynamic between these teams, and what's the biggest opportunity for a new leader to foster innovation among them?"

**Insider's Answer:** "Right now, our teams are brilliant but operate in high-performing silos. The data scientists are building incredible models, and the engineers are creating robust platforms, but there isn't always a central vision connecting their toolsets. The biggest opportunity here is to be the catalyst—the translator who connects the potential of a new AI agent to a tangible business problem that the analytics team is trying to solve. Your success won't just be measured by the tech you implement, but by the 'aha!' moments you create between teams. We need a leader who can build a unified ecosystem where an idea from one team can be seamlessly prototyped, tested, and scaled by another, transforming how we work and driving real, measurable value."
