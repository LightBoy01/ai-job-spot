Your tasks
----------

Do you love interacting with people and finding solutions to their problems? Are you passionate about delivering excellent support and ensuring a smooth experience for customers? Do you thrive in a fast-paced environment and enjoy working in an international team? Then this role is perfect for you!

Your tasks are as follows:
--------------------------

*   Customer inquiries via email and phone are handled with care and efficiency, ensuring a positive experience.
*   Provide customers with replacement orders, refunds, or general assistance.
*   Monitor and analyze customer reviews across different channels.
*   Collaborate with internal teams to improve customer satisfaction.

Your profile
------------

*   You have a degree or relevant training in a related field (beneficial but not mandatory).
*   You already have experience in customer support or a similar role (a plus but not required).
*   You speak French and German on a excellent level, English is a plus.
*   You are located in Berlin or willing to relocate for the job.
*   You are proficient in customer support tools and software.
*   You work in a structured and solution-oriented way.
*   You have a proactive mindset and strong customer orientation.
*   You excel at multitasking and prioritizing in a dynamic environment.

What you can expect from us
---------------------------

*   Our diverse team is ready to be **enriched and** **inspired by your ideas**: help building the future of food retailing in our fast-growing start-up!
*   **Extensive Onboardings** which will prepare you for your various and challenging tasks
*   Flexible working hours and home office options to ensure a **good work-life balance**
*   A beautiful and **modern office in Berlin Schöneberg,** easy to reach by public transport – and we cover the costs of your monthly **BVG (public transport Berlin) ticket**
*   Find your way (back) to the gym thanks to fitness **cooperations with Urban Sports Club or FitX**
*   Optimum performance with either **a Windows laptop or MacBook.**
*   **Well equipped office kitchen** with fresh fruit, delicious KoRo snacks, coffee and tea
*   **20 % discount in our KoRo online store**
*   **Regular team events** and joint activities as well as legendary company parties
