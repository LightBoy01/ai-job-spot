​Supply​

Our supply chain is the backbone of our business. It's how we get our trusted products to people all over the world, safely and efficiently. And it's our talented and passionate teams that make this happen.

If you're looking for a career in supply chain, there's no better place to be than Reckitt. We offer a variety of exciting opportunities in all areas of the supply chain, from planning and procurement to manufacturing and logistics. You could join our Global Supply Planning team to develop and implement our global supply strategy, or work with our Procurement Centre of Excellence team to negotiate and manage our supplier relationships.

Our Manufacturing Excellence team help us improve the efficiency, quality and safety of our manufacturing operations, and our Logistics Excellence team develop new and innovative ways to distribute our products to customers.

Your responsibilities

<p>1. Strategic Sourcing &amp; Execution</p>
<ul>
<li>Design and implement sourcing strategies aligned with global objectives.</li>
<li>Manage a robust pipeline of procurement projects with strong execution, negotiation, and contract management.</li>
</ul>
<p>2. Financial Ownership</p>
<ul>
<li>Drive annual budget planning and spend forecasting.</li>
<li>Lead monthly/quarterly financial reviews and corrective action planning.</li>
<li>Optimize cost of goods through tenders, RFQs, and benchmarking.</li>
<li>Deliver Net Working Capital (NWC) improvements.</li>
</ul>
<p>3. Stakeholder Collaboration</p>
<ul>
<li>Influence internal stakeholders to align with procurement goals.</li>
<li>Partner cross-functionally to meet business needs and maximize value.</li>
</ul>
<p>4. Supplier &amp; Category Management</p>
<ul>
<li>Manage procurement of packaging materials within assigned category.</li>
<li>Monitor supplier performance against KPI’s: cost, quality, service, and cash.</li>
</ul>
<p>5. Innovation &amp; Market Intelligence</p>
<ul>
<li>Collaborate with suppliers to drive innovation and sustainability.</li>
<li>Leverage market insights to inform sourcing decisions.</li>
<li>Support upstream initiatives and material optimization projects.</li>
</ul>
<p>6. Risk &amp; Compliance</p>
<ul>
<li>Assess and mitigate supplier risks; lead contingency planning.</li>
<li>Ensure contracts meet legal standards and pricing compliance.</li>
</ul>


The experience we're looking for

<ul>
<li>Experience: Minimum 5 years in strategic procurement, preferably with commodity/category expertise.</li>
<li>Education: Bachelor’s degree in engineering, Finance, Business, or related field; Master’s degree is a plus.</li>
<li>Contract &amp; Negotiation Expertise: Skilled in developing high-value contracts and managing complex negotiations with a balance of assertiveness and diplomacy.</li>
<li>Supplier &amp; Category Management: Proven ability to manage supplier performance and drive continuous improvement across tactical and strategic supply bases.</li>
<li>Financial &amp; Analytical Acumen: Strong understanding of cost structures, budgeting, forecasting, and data-driven decision-making.</li>
<li>Project Management: Demonstrated success in leading procurement and cross-functional projects from concept to execution.</li>
<li>Digital Proficiency: Advanced skills in MS Excel, PowerPoint, Power BI, and familiarity with AI tools.</li>
<li>Communication &amp; Influence: Excellent verbal, written, and presentation skills with the ability to navigate stakeholder interests and resolve conflicts effectively.</li>
</ul>
