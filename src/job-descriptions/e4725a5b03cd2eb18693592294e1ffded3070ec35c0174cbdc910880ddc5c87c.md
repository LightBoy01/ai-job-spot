**Company Description:**

Bench by Mainstreet is on a mission to make back-office solutions affordable and accessible for small businesses across the U.S. Since 2012, we’ve supported 35,000+ entrepreneurs with bookkeeping, tax, and financial services—giving them the tools they need to succeed and grow.

We’re looking for a Marketing, Content & Social Media Manager (or agency partner) who can take full ownership of our content calendar and lead campaign execution across channels. The ideal fit brings a balance of strategic organization (managing campaigns and calendars) and creative execution (design, storytelling, short-form video).

**Job Description:**

**Social Media & Content Manager (Open to Agencies, FTE, or Contractors — Global)**

Whether individual or agency, you’ll manage our end-to-end content calendar and ensure that campaigns, social media, blogs, and email activities ladder up to a cohesive, data-driven plan. You’ll also produce creative content across LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and Twitter/X—with a strong focus on design, Reels/short-form video, storytelling, and innovative use of AI-driven tools to accelerate impact.

**What You’ll Do**

**Content Calendar & Campaigns**

*   Build and manage a unified content and campaign calendar.
    
*   Coordinate with marketing, product, and customer teams to align activities.
    
*   Ensure content deadlines and deliverables are met across all channels.
    
*   Define and manage **AI agents** to help create, repurpose, and distribute content (with human oversight for quality control).
    
*   Align storytelling and creative direction across **social media, email, and paid ads** to ensure message consistency.
    

**Social Media Management & Storytelling**

*   Own Bench by Mainstreet’s social presence across all major platforms.
    
*   Create content that tells small business success stories and highlights customer value.
    
*   **Collect and publish customer testimonials** as part of ongoing brand storytelling.
    
*   Produce and edit short-form video content (Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts) that drives engagement and conversions.
    
*   Leverage **cutting-edge AI tools**, data, and insights to optimize creative output and personalize campaigns.
    

**Creative & Design Execution**

*   Ideate, design, and deliver visually compelling creative assets.
    
*   Use tools like **Canva, Adobe Suite, CapCut, or Figma** to create standout graphics, carousels, and video content.
    
*   Incorporate **AI-assisted design workflows** for faster iteration and brand consistency.
    
*   Maintain brand voice and visual identity consistently across all touchpoints.
    
*   Conceptualize and execute **guerrilla marketing campaigns** that capture attention and drive organic reach.
    

**Cross-Functional Collaboration**

*   Partner with sales, customer success, and product teams to source stories, insights, and milestones.
    
*   Translate internal wins and partnerships into external-facing campaigns that resonate with SMB audiences.
    
*   Collaborate on initiatives to **launch a new brand from the ground up**, shaping its digital voice and community presence.
    

**Performance & Reporting**

*   Monitor engagement, reach, and conversion across all content and campaigns.
    
*   Provide regular insights and data-backed recommendations for optimization.
    
*   Demonstrate **proven conversion rates** and ability to connect creative campaigns directly to sales outcomes.
    

**Qualifications:**

**What We’re Looking For**

*   3–5+ years in marketing, content, or social media management (or agency equivalent).
    
*   Proven success managing multi-channel social media and content campaigns.
    
*   Strong portfolio demonstrating creative storytelling, design skills, and short-form video.
    
*   Experience using **AI tools** to scale content creation and performance.
    
*   Excellent writing, editing, and storytelling ability.
    
*   Proficiency with creative/design platforms (Canva, Adobe Suite, CapCut, Figma, etc.).
    
*   Highly organized, collaborative, and able to balance strategy with execution.
    

**Nice-to-Haves**

*   Experience working with SMBs, SaaS, or fintech brands.
    
*   Exposure to paid social campaigns and analytics.
    
*   Photography, video production, or motion design experience.
    
*   Experience launching or growing new brands and online communities.
