<p><strong>About the Role</strong></p>
<p>We are seeking a motivated and adaptable AI Data Engineer with a strong interest in data infrastructure. This role will play a crucial role in scaling data sets that we use for training our AI models. The ideal candidate will be passionate about building robust and efficient data pipelines, ensuring data quality, and contributing to the advancement of our AI capabilities.</p>
<p><strong>Responsibilities:</strong></p>
<p><strong>Large-Scale Data Collection (millions of records):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Gather data through web scraping or utilize existing open datasets.</li>
<li>Alternatively, leverage Nanonets' internal datasets via our APIs.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Dataset Filtering, Cleaning, and Validation:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Use foundational models or rule-based approaches to filter and clean datasets.</li>
<li>Ensure dataset integrity through comprehensive validation.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Data Visualization:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Work with tools like Streamlit or Gradio for effective data visualization.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Benchmarking:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Run existing benchmarks on models to assess their performance.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Model Migration:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Migrate models from older versions to newer ones based on benchmark results.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Collaboration with Annotators:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Coordinate with annotators to ensure accurate and consistent data labeling.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Qualifications:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>B.E/B.Tech from a reputed school.</li>
<li>0-3 years of experience.</li>
<li>Strong hands-on experience in Python</li>
<li>Good written and verbal communication skills.</li>
<li>Strong interpersonal skills, with the ability to build rapport.</li>
</ul>
