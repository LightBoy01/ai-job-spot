**Enterprise Architect**

**Base Location:** London, Leeds, Manchester or Birmingham plus network of 20 offices nationally:

[www.kpmg88careers.co.uk/experienced-professional/#LeBlender.OfficeLocations](http://www.kpmg88careers.co.uk/experienced-professional/#LeBlender.OfficeLocations)

The KPMG Technology Strategy practice is a cornerstone of our business.  Operating from across or UK offices we do work that matters, serving the country with diligence and expertise.

KPMG is one of the world's largest and most respected consultancies.  We've supported the UK through times of war and peace, prosperity and recession, political and regulatory upheaval. We've proudly stood beside the institutions and businesses which make the UK what it is.

By encompassing a breadth of areas we deliver collaborative team engagements, working together across these capabilities to help our clients get the best from their IT services, we become immersed in our clients' organisations, applying sector knowledge and technology solutions to deliver the best possible outcomes and get it right first time.

**Why Join KPMG as an Enterprise Architect**

KPMG’s Technology Strategy practice helps clients transform the way that CIOs and their management teams manage and deliver IT services to their customers. We help our clients to understand and exploit the constantly changing technology ecosystem to deliver value, agility and efficiency. In collaboration with other specialists across KPMG, we support our clients through digital transformation and enable them to transform their business through technology-enabled change.

**What will you be doing?**

Working with KPMG colleagues, client teams and with C Level client resources to:

*   Provide credible advice to clients on how to operate IT in the most effective manner, including commercial structure and cost control
*   Develop IT strategies collaboratively with client teams
*   Lead client engagements end to end
*   Design IT operating models that address client challenges
*   Identify emerging trends in the market and develop products and services that KPMG can leverage within our client base
*   Champion the use of our global propositions e.g. AI

**What will you need to do it?**

As an Enterprise Architect, working in either a major Consultancy or with major Public or Private sector organisations, you will have Digital experience in at least two of the following:

*   Developing IT strategies in complex environments, with a focus on leveraging Cloud/ SAP and other emerging technologies
*   IT Operating model design across several organisations
*   IT Cost optimisation
*   Agile delivery methodologies, particularly at scale in large organisations
*   Be self-sufficient, with proven leadership experience delivering large technology programmes/projects
*   Tenacious, passionate and thorough in approach and style; able to adjust communication styles from C level to junior level

**Skills we’d love to see/Amazing Extras:**  
 

*   Consulting experience with large organisations and complex technology transformations
*   A passion for emerging technology and driving business value around Digital technology areas such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Platforms, Integration, Data, User Experience

To discuss this or wider Consulting roles with our recruitment team, all you need to do is apply, create a profile, upload your CV and begin to make your mark with KPMG.
