**Welcome to Maastricht University!**
The world is changing rapidly, and so is procurement. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is reshaping how organizations collaborate, make decisions, and develop talent. At the Graduate School of Business and Economics (GSBE), you will explore how AI transforms procurement leadership, buyer–supplier relationships, and the skills professionals need to thrive in the future.
**PhD Candidate: The Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Procurement
**
*   **Our goal:** To investigate how emerging AI technologies redefine procurement strategies, leadership practices, and workforce development.
*   **Your colleagues:** You will join an interdisciplinary research team at the School of Business and Economics (SBE), working closely with industry partners such as NEVI, DEXLab, UMIO, and Brightlands. Supervision by Prof. Dr. Frank Rozemeijer, Prof. Dr. Dominik Mahr, and Dr. Jonas Heller.
As a PhD candidate, you will conduct original research into the ways AI is transforming procurement across industries. You will study leadership challenges, digital transformation of procurement tasks, and organizational interventions to support workforce upskilling.
Your research will combine a variety of methods—from interviews and longitudinal field studies to experimental designs and intervention evaluations. Along the way, you will engage with a vibrant community of practice, bringing together academia, industry, and public-sector stakeholders.
Academic responsibilities include publishing in peer-reviewed journals, presenting at leading conferences, and contributing to the broader academic and practitioner communities around digital procurement transformation. You will also take PhD coursework to strengthen your skills and contribute to teaching activities at SBE.
