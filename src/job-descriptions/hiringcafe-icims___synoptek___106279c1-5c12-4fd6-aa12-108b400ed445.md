Overview:

**Position Overview**  
This is an amazing opportunity to work within one of the fastest growing Managed Services Providers. We  
are a company with a heart and soul dedicated to the ongoing success and growth of our employees and  
continued business success of the customers we support. We foster a fun and connected environment  
with employee benefits extending beyond general compensation and into company sponsored events and  
an invested culture of learning.

  
The Dynamics 365 Development Lead is responsible for spearheading the digital transformation of one of  
our largest customers. Additionally, the person in this role will build and lead a client-side development  
team, ensuring seamless implementation of Dynamics 365 CE & Finance & Supply Chain, Power  
Platform, Azure, microservices, and third-party applications while driving Agile best practices for iterative  
development and continuous improvement

\*\*Please note this is a 6-9 month contract role

**Duties and Responsibilities**

*   Key Responsibilities
    *   Mentor, manage & upskill a high-performing Dynamics 365 development team both on  
        Partner Side & Client side.
    *   Define team structure, roles, and responsibilities for development team.
    *   Establish Agile development methodologies, including Scrum, Kanban, and iterative delivery.
    *   Establish best practices for DevOps, CI/CD pipelines, and cloud-based deployments.
    *   Lead the development of Dynamics 365 CE & Finance & Supply Chain solutions.
    *   Review and validate integration strategies for Power Platform, Azure, ISV solutions, and  
        third-party applications.
    *   Oversee data migration, automation, and optimization using Dataverse, Power BI, and  
        Fabric.
    *   Implement AI-driven automation to enhance productivity and business intelligence.
    *   Develop and execute a test strategy & automation framework for quality assurance.
    *   Ensure security, compliance, and performance optimization across platforms.  
          
        
*    Agile Development Framework Responsibilities
    *   Lead Agile ceremonies including daily stand-ups, sprint planning, retrospectives, and backlog  
        grooming.
    *   Define and track KPIs for Agile delivery, ensuring velocity, efficiency, and continuous  
        improvement.
    *   Foster a collaborative development culture with cross-functional teams.
    *   Implement CI/CD pipelines for automated deployments and iterative releases.
    *   Ensure regular feedback loops with stakeholders to refine requirements and enhance user  
        adoption.
    *   Building the Client-Side Development Team
    *   Define strategy to onboard skilled developers, architects, and analysts.
    *   Establish training programs to upskill team members in Dynamics 365, Power Platform, and  
        Azure.
    *   Promote interdepartmental synergy to support agile collaboration
    *   Implement code review, testing, and deployment standards for quality assurance.
    *   Ensure cross-functional collaboration between developers, business analysts, and IT teams
*   This job description is not designed to be a comprehensive list of the duties and responsibilities  
    required of the employee in this position, as duties, responsibilities, and activities may change at any  
    time with or without notice

**Education**

*   Bachelor’s degree in a related field from an accredited college or university.
*   Microsoft certifications in Dynamics 365, Azure, and Power Platform (preferred).  
    Job-related Experience
*   Customarily has at least 8 years of experience in Dynamics 365 development.
*   Expertise in Dynamics 365 CE, Finance & Supply Chain, Power Platform, Azure, and AI automation.
*   Strong understanding of API management, microservices, and third-party integrations.
*   Experience with Azure services for cloud-based solutions.
*   Expertise in test strategy & automation for quality assurance and system reliability.
