Energy Competence Centre GmbH is looking for a dedicated Intern (m/f/d) for a “Pflichtpraktikum” (mandatory internship as part of their study program), full-time or part-time in Berlin-Charlottenburg. We offer a down-to-earth working environment with flat hierarchies.

Tasks
-----

*   support of the project development and business development team (PD and BD team) in the development and advancement of various infrastructure projects
*   support in researching and conducting studies in relevant technologies for ongoing projects
*   assistance in preparing engineering-related calculations, design drawings, specifications, and visual aids
*   support of the PD-Team in the day-to-day tasks on our infrastructure projects (gathering data, communication with selected stakeholders, taking minutes of meetings)
*   supporting the company management and the PD team in day-to-day business tasks

Requirements
------------

*   Student in engineering, architecture, geography or similar
*   Experience with Geographic Information Systems is highly appreciated
*   Business fluent in English
*   First professional experience is a plus
*   Experience in the field of renewable energies is a plus
*   Other languages, especially Arabic, French and/or German are a plus
*   Availability for occasional travel
*   Team spirit and flexibility, independent working style and reliability.

Benefits
--------

• Possibility for home office

• Flexible working hours

• Free fresh fruit, coffee, tea, mineral water

• Ergonomic workstations

• Full-time or Part-time position (20 – 40 hours / week)
