### About BizzyCar

BizzyCar is a B2B SaaS company transforming the automotive care experience for consumers, dealerships, and manufacturers. Our mission is to simplify car repairs, enabling consumers to resolve issues like recalls without leaving home or work. We are solving a $22B industry challenge, with over 70 million cars on the road having unresolved recalls.

### About the role

BizzyCar is seeking a talented and driven AI Engineer to join our team and play a pivotal role in developing and enhancing our AI-powered toolset. You will be responsible for designing, implementing, and optimizing AI models and algorithms that directly impact our customer experience and operational efficiency. This role demands a strong understanding of machine learning principles, software development best practices, and a passion for creating innovative solutions.

### Responsibilities

- **Develop and Implement AI Models:** Design, build, and deploy machine learning models for various applications, including customer interaction, predictive analytics, and process automation.
- **Enhance Existing AI Toolset:** Improve the performance, accuracy, and scalability of our existing AI algorithms and models, measure performance and maintain models.
- **Consumer Interaction AI Development:** Create and refine AI solutions that enhance customer engagement, such as chatbots, personalized recommendations, and sentiment analysis tools.
- **Data Analysis and Preprocessing:** Extract, clean, and preprocess large datasets for model training and evaluation, ensuring data quality and integrity.
- **Collaborate and Communicate:** Work closely with product managers, data scientists, and software engineers to define requirements, communicate progress, and deliver high-quality AI solutions.

### Qualifications

- Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, or a related field.
- Minimum of 2+ years of experience in AI/machine learning development, with a focus on consumer-facing communication solutions.
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, and experience with machine learning libraries like TensorFlow, PyTorch, or scikit-learn.
- Experience with natural language processing (NLP), conversational AI, or related technologies.
- Strong understanding of software development principles, version control (Git), and cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP).

We value our employees’ time and efforts. Our commitment to your success is enhanced by our competitive compensation and benefits package including paid time off, medical, dental, 401k, vision and equity benefits and future growth opportunities within the company.
