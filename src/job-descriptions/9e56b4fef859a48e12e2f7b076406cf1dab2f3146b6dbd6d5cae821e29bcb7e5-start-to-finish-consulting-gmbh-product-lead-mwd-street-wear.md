Tasks
-----

**About the Role**

The Product Lead will be responsible for managing the full lifecycle from sample development to mass production across all apparel categories. You will professionalize the production setup, accelerate timelines, and expand the company’s international manufacturing network. This position requires deep experience in textile production, strong operational execution, and the ability to scale complex supply chains.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Manage the full process from sample development to large-scale production
*   Oversee sampling for core products (T-shirts, hoodies, sweaters, caps) and growing categories (denim, outerwear, accessories)
*   Coordinate complex printing, embroidery, washing, dyeing, and finishing processes
*   Ensure consistent Sample → Production timelines of 1–2 months
*   Build new production lines and expand the international supplier network (Asia, Uzbekistan, Southeast Asia)
*   Manage offshore factories, production partners, and external agencies
*   Oversee purchasing, supply chain planning, cost control, and quality assurance
*   Work closely with Design, Marketing, and Operations on feasibility and timelines
*   Support the development of new materials and technical product categories
*   Establish documentation, compliance, and production standards
*   Travel internationally to production partners as needed

Requirements
------------

*   Several years of experience in apparel production, garment technology, or textile manufacturing
*   Strong end-to-end understanding of fabrics, construction, printing, and finishing
*   Proven ability to manage **high-volume productions** (5,000–30,000+ units)
*   Experience working with international suppliers and offshore manufacturing
*   Track record of establishing or scaling new production setups is a strong plus
*   Excellent supplier management and project coordination skills
*   Ability to manage multiple timelines and product categories simultaneously
