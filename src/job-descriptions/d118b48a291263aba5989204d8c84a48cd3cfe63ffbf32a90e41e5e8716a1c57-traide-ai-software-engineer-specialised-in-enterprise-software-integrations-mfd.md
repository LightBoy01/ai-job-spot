### Description\nAt traide AI, we empower customs specialists with smarter, faster workflows. As a TypeScript Engineer focused on integrations, you will be instrumental in building the critical bridges connecting our AI-powered platform with customer systems, data pipelines, and third-party services. You'll join a calm yet ambitious VC-backed B2B startup, where your expertise will directly shape global trade operations. This role is ideal for engineers who thrive on solving complex data-exchange challenges, value clean TypeScript architecture, and seek significant ownership in a dynamic, fast-moving environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n-   **Integration & Platform Development**\n    -   Design, implement, and maintain integrations between our platform and external systems (APIs, ERPs, customs software, data sources).\n    -   Own integration workflows end-to-end: from technical discovery to production deployment.\n    -   Build TypeScript packages, services, and internal tooling that make integrations reliable, observable, and maintainable.\n    -   Extend our GraphQL/REST API surface and backend services where needed to support integration use cases.\n-   **Cross-Functional Collaboration**\n    -   Work with customer-facing teams to understand integration requirements and translate them into robust technical solutions.\n    -   Collaborate with AI engineers to connect model outputs with downstream systems.\n    -   Contribute technical input to product decisions involving data flows, system boundaries, and platform capabilities.\n-   **Code Quality & Reliability**\n    -   Ensure integrations are resilient, well-typed, observable, and easy to operate.\n    -   Identify bottlenecks in data flows and improve performance and reliability.\n    -   Maintain and evolve automated tests for integration logic and platform services.\n    -   Improve internal tooling and CI/CD processes to streamline integration development.\n-   **Knowledge Sharing**\n    -   Participate in code reviews and documentation to spread best practices across the team.\n    -   Help define standards for integrations, API design, error handling, and operational readiness.\n\n### Qualifications\n-   5+ years of professional software engineering experience, ideally building or maintaining integrations, backend services, or data pipelines.\n-   Strong proficiency in TypeScript across backend and shared codebases.\n-   Experience with HTTP APIs (GraphQL/REST), Node.js services, and relational databases such as Postgres.\n-   Familiarity with enterprise software solutions like ERP, PIM, Document Storage, etc.\n-   Ability to design clear integration architectures (webhooks, polling, messaging, auth flows, data mapping).\n-   Experience with API clients, SDKs, or integration frameworks is a strong plus.\n-   Comfortable working with partners and internal teams to clarify ambiguous or evolving requirements.\n-   You embrace AI tooling and modern developer workflows.\n-   You value code quality, operational excellence, and collaborative problem-solving.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a seasoned TypeScript Engineer to make a significant impact at a VC-backed startup that's truly transforming global trade. You'll gain end-to-end ownership over critical integrations, working with cutting-edge AI technologies in a collaborative, flexible, and growth-oriented environment. The company values individual responsibility, continuous learning, and offers a flexible hybrid work model, fostering an inclusive culture where your contributions are genuinely valued. If you're passionate about complex problem-solving, clean architecture, and driving real-world operational change, this role at traide AI is a compelling next step.
