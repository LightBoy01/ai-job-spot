At traide AI, we help customs specialists achieve smarter, faster workflows. Integrations play a critical role in making that possible. As a TypeScript Engineer focused on integrations, you’ll build the bridges that connect our AI-powered platform with customer systems, data pipelines, and third-party services.

You’ll join a calm but ambitious VC-backed B2B startup where your work shapes how global trade teams operate. Collaborate closely with product, AI engineering, and customer-facing teams to design and ship reliable, well-structured integrations that power real operational impact.

If you enjoy solving complex data-exchange problems, care about clean TypeScript architecture, and want ownership in a fast-moving environment, we’d love to talk.

Tasks
-----

**Integration & Platform Development**

*   Design, implement, and maintain integrations between our platform and external systems (APIs, ERPs, customs software, data sources).
*   Own integration workflows end-to-end: from technical discovery to production deployment.
*   Build TypeScript packages, services, and internal tooling that make integrations reliable, observable, and maintainable.
*   Extend our GraphQL/REST API surface and backend services where needed to support integration use cases.

**Cross-Functional Collaboration**

*   Work with customer-facing teams to understand integration requirements and translate them into robust technical solutions.
*   Collaborate with AI engineers to connect model outputs with downstream systems.
*   Contribute technical input to product decisions involving data flows, system boundaries, and platform capabilities.

**Code Quality & Reliability**

*   Ensure integrations are resilient, well-typed, observable, and easy to operate.
*   Identify bottlenecks in data flows and improve performance and reliability.
*   Maintain and evolve automated tests for integration logic and platform services.
*   Improve internal tooling and CI/CD processes to streamline integration development.

**Knowledge Sharing**

*   Participate in code reviews and documentation to spread best practices across the team.
*   Help define standards for integrations, API design, error handling, and operational readiness.

Requirements
------------

*   5+ years of professional software engineering experience, ideally building or maintaining integrations, backend services, or data pipelines.
*   Strong proficiency in TypeScript across backend and shared codebases.
*   Experience with HTTP APIs (GraphQL/REST), Node.js services, and relational databases such as Postgres.
*   Familiarity with enterprise software solutions like ERP, PIM, Document Storage, etc.
*   Ability to design clear integration architectures (webhooks, polling, messaging, auth flows, data mapping).
*   Experience with API clients, SDKs, or integration frameworks is a strong plus.
*   Comfortable working with partners and internal teams to clarify ambiguous or evolving requirements.
*   You embrace AI tooling and modern developer workflows.
*   You value code quality, operational excellence, and collaborative problem-solving.
