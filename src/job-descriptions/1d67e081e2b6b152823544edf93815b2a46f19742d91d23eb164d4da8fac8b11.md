What You Will Get to Do:
In addition to general technical directorial skills, candidates must have the following:
Technical Leadership
*   Define and oversee technical strategy and execution across programs and proposals.
*   Lead design and delivery of AI-driven solutions, enterprise software, and system integrations.
*   Serve as a subject matter expert in emerging technologies, shaping innovative client solutions.
Business Development
*   Collaborate with BD teams to identify, qualify, and pursue new opportunities.
*   Lead technical solutioning for RFPs, RFIs, white papers, and AI/software-focused pursuits.
*   Build and maintain strong client, partner, and stakeholder relationships.
Proposal Development
*   Lead technical volumes, ensuring AI, software, and system solutions align with client needs and win themes.
*   Partner with capture, pricing, and SMEs to develop compliant, compelling proposals.
*   Present technical and software solutions during orals and client briefings.
Team Mentorship
*   Mentor engineers, software developers, and solution architects, fostering innovation and excellence.
*   Support hiring and talent development across AI, software, and technical teams.
Qualifications You Will Bring:
Required
*   Bachelor’s or Master’s in Computer Science, Cybersecurity, Engineering, or related field.
*   10+ years in technical leadership, preferably in federal or enterprise environments.
*   Demonstrated success in business development, proposal strategy, and delivery of AI and software solutions.
*   Strong understanding of the DoD acquisition lifecycle, RFP structures, and government contracting.
*   Expertise in software development lifecycles, AI/ML frameworks, capture planning, technical writing, and proposal tools.
*   Excellent communication, client-facing, and leadership skills.
*   Ability to thrive in fast-paced, deadline-driven environments.
Preferred
*   Experience supporting IDIQs, GWACs, or large multi-award contracts.
*   Familiarity with Salesforce, GovWin, SharePoint, MS Teams, and modern AI/software development collaboration tools.
