### Description
Rügamer & Steiner is a leading E-Mail Marketing Agency for the top 10% of E-Commerce companies in Germany, Austria, and Switzerland. Having grown from 0 to over 18 creative minds in the last 5 years, the company is now aiming for significant expansion, targeting 30-40 employees by 2026, including their first international team members. To meet increasing client demand and facilitate this growth, we are seeking a dedicated HR & Recruiting Manager.

This role is integral to a dynamic startup environment, focusing on building and optimizing structures and processes across HR, Recruiting, and Executive Assistant functions. You will be instrumental in shaping our people strategy, ensuring we attract top talent and support our team's development as we scale.

### Responsibilities
- **Recruiting:**
  - Identify and attract national and international talents in eCommerce, Marketing, and Sales.
  - Manage the entire candidate journey, from application through initial screening, interviews, and case studies.
  - Develop and execute Employer Branding strategies to enhance candidate attraction.
  - Contribute to the ambitious goal of growing the team to 30-40 employees by 2026, with a focus on international recruitment requiring strong English language skills.
- **HR:**
  - Serve as the primary contact for the team on all HR and People-related matters.
  - Establish and ensure HR Compliance.
  - Administer all contract documents and employee data.
  - Drive People Development: Create roadmaps for employee growth, collaborate with leaders on performance oversight, and support individual development.
  - Oversee all onboarding processes to ensure a seamless new hire experience.
- **Executive Assistant:**
  - Design and optimize processes across Recruiting, HR, and Backoffice operations.
  - Assist the management team with travel management, event planning, and general administrative tasks in collaboration with the Team Assistant.
  - Prepare payroll data in coordination with the external payroll office.

### Qualifications
- A genuine passion for communicating with applicants, fostering employer branding, and driving HR and People processes forward.
- An experienced HR Manager with a minimum of 3-4 years of professional experience, specifically focusing on Recruiting and HR (Compliance) in both national and international contexts.
- A proactive \"Macher:in\" (doer) with exceptional organizational skills.
- Proven experience in HR Administration and Payroll processes.
- Demonstrated leadership competencies to develop and advance all employees within the organization.
