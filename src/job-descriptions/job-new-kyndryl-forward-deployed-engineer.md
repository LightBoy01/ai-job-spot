# Forward Deployed Engineer - Agentic AI

### Description

As a Forward Deployed Engineer for Agentic AI, you will be the key link between business problems and innovative AI solutions. You will design, build, and deploy production-grade AI systems, focusing on Agentic and Generative AI frameworks. The role requires deep expertise in AI architectures, model optimization, and a strategic mindset to transform business processes and drive significant efficiency gains.

### Responsibilities

- Advanced Architecture Design: Design Generative AI systems using GANs, VAEs, and transformer-based models. Architect Agentic AI frameworks using tools like LangChain, Hugging Face, and vector databases.
- Model Optimization & Innovation: Develop new techniques for LLM/SLM fine-tuning and optimize inference performance for real-time applications. Lead R&D initiatives in neurosymbolic AI and ethical guardrails.
- Strategic Team Player: Collaborate with high-performance teams to deploy AI systems that redefine industry standards and publish whitepapers or patents on new AI architectures.

### Qualifications

- **Required:**
- PhD in Machine Learning, Computational Neuroscience, or AI Ethics (or an MSc with 8+ years of experience).
- 7+ years of experience building production-grade AI systems.
- Extensive experience with Generative AI (PyTorch Lightning, Hugging Face, etc.) and Agentic AI (LangChain, vector databases, etc.).
- Proven ability to design multi-agent systems.
- A portfolio demonstrating 10x efficiency gains through AI-driven process reinvention.
- Fluency in ethical AI frameworks.
