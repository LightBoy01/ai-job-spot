### Description
Pitchview is a digital platform that enables companies to shape their customer relationships more professionally and personally – whether in sales, marketing, or key account management. Our clients are leading companies in the pharmaceutical industry and financial sector, who use Pitchview to create interactive presentations, shared spaces, and eDetailings for their target groups.

### Responsibilities
As a working student, you will support our marketing and sales team in expanding our presence on LinkedIn and generating new leads. You will work on real projects – from idea generation to implementation.

*   Contribute to **LinkedIn social media strategies** (content, targeting, campaign planning).
*   **Create banners, visuals, and promotional materials** (e.g., with Canva, Figma, or Adobe Express).
*   **Set up and manage LinkedIn campaigns** (organic and paid).
*   **Market webinars** – from invitation to lead generation.
*   **Conduct active LinkedIn outreach** for participant and lead acquisition.
*   Support **sales** – with **appointment preparation and follow-up**.
*   Contribute to the **maintenance and updating of the CRM system, marketing automation system, and our website**.
*   Assist with **campaign evaluation** (analytics, KPIs, reporting).

### Qualifications
*   You are studying Marketing, Media, Communication, Business Administration, or a comparable field.
*   You have a good feel for **social media trends and B2B communication**.
*   Experience with **LinkedIn**, **LinkedIn Ads Manager, marketing automation systems, or CRM systems** is a plus.
*   You work in a **structured, creative, and independent** manner.
*   You are eager to contribute to a growing SaaS company and learn about both **marketing and sales processes**.
