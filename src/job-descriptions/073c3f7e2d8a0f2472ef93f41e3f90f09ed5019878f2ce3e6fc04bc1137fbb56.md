### Description

As an AI Engineer at DXC Technology, you will be at the forefront of building and deploying scalable AI and machine learning solutions that solve real-world challenges. You will work on a variety of applications, including NLP, computer vision, and cutting-edge Generative AI. At DXC, we believe strong connections and community are key to our success. Our work model prioritizes in-person collaboration while offering flexibility to support wellbeing, productivity, and individual work styles in an inclusive environment where everyone can thrive.

### Responsibilities

**Core Responsibilities:**

*   **Model Development & Deployment:** Build scalable AI/ML models for real-world applications, including NLP, computer vision, and GenAI use cases.
*   **Data Engineering:** Preprocess large-scale structured and unstructured datasets, optimize data pipelines, and extract meaningful insights.
*   **MLOps & Automation:** Implement best practices for the training, deployment, and monitoring of AI systems. Integrate CI/CD pipelines, manage containers (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes), and automate deployment and scaling processes using tools like MLflow and major cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP).

**Additional Responsibilities (Good to Have):**

*   **Collaboration:** Work closely with cross-functional teams, including software engineers, data scientists, and product managers, to seamlessly integrate AI capabilities into our products.
*   **Research & Innovation:** Stay current with the latest AI trends, research papers, and emerging tools such as LangChain, RAG, and new LLMs.
*   **Mentorship:** Guide junior engineers and actively contribute to the team's growth and skill development.

### Qualifications

*   **Programming:** Strong proficiency in Python, with hands-on experience in frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, and Scikit-learn, as well as data manipulation libraries (Pandas, NumPy).
*   **AI Concepts:** A deep understanding of supervised/unsupervised learning, Natural Language Processing (NLP), deep learning, and Generative AI.
*   **Tools & Frameworks:** Experience with vector databases, containerization (Docker), orchestration (Kubernetes), and prompt engineering.
*   **Soft Skills:** Excellent critical thinking, stakeholder engagement, conflict management, and team-building abilities.

### Author's Take

**A potential candidate asks: "Beyond the listed responsibilities, what does a major success story for this role look like in the first year?"**

That's a great question because it gets to the heart of the impact you can make here. Honestly, a huge win would be taking one of our promising GenAI proof-of-concepts and owning its journey into a fully-fledged, production-grade system. We have a project right now that uses RAG to help our internal teams find complex information buried in thousands of documents. It works, but it's not yet scalable or robust. In your first year, you wouldn't just be tweaking a model; you'd be the architect of its MLOps framework. You'd build the automated deployment pipeline, set up the monitoring to catch model drift, and work with the product team to see your work directly cut down research time for hundreds of colleagues. Success isn't just a successful deployment; it's seeing the metrics prove that your system became an indispensable tool for the business.

**Another candidate asks: "The description mentions staying updated with AI trends. How does the team balance shipping features with genuine research and innovation?"**

We're very intentional about this because we know the field moves incredibly fast. We actively avoid becoming a feature factory. We have a 'Pathfinder Program' where every engineer gets dedicated time each quarter to work on a passion project or experiment with a new technology, like a new vector database or a novel prompting technique. The only rule is that you have to present your findings back to the team. It's not just about reading papers; it's about building things. This has led to some of our best breakthroughs and keeps our skills sharp. It ensures that when we commit to a new technology for a major project, it's a decision backed by hands-on experience, not just hype.
