Do you want to take the first step in making Filipinos’ lives better everyday? Here in GCash we want to stay at the forefront of the FinTech industry by creating innovative, meaningful, and convenient financial solutions for the nation! G ka ba? Join the G Nation today!

**You will be responsible for the following:**

*   Drives the shared vision and objectives on the use of data and AI in the assigned business Unit building strategic relationships with the unit’s key pillars to achieve their business goals.
    
*   Responsible for guiding customers through the successful adoption and implementation of Data and AI solutions. 
    
*   Works closely with their assigned business/enabling units to understand their business needs, providing technical expertise, and ensuring that they achieve their desired outcomes with the company's Data and AI products. 
    
*   Design initiatives, programs and products with the function leads and cross functional teams (Tribes) to enable the organization to realize the optimal business value derivable from the organization’s Data and AI assets.
    
*   Develop comprehensive studies using internal and/or external data to address specific business questions and challenges, and provide recommendations for next steps.
    
*   Promotes Gcash’s Data and AI strategy, fosters a culture of compliance to data privacy regulations and Data and AI Governance Framework
    
*   Accountable for the quality, accessibility, analysis and management of data outputs that drive innovations, actionable intelligence and tangible value to their assigned business unit.
    

**We are looking for:**

*   Highly skilled in influencing and engaging stakeholders to secure their buy-in and support.
    
*   Strong business acumen, to effectively identify the value of Data and AI in the business context.
    
*   Must understand the key operational metrics of their assigned business unit, and their impact on profit and loss,revenue, customer retention, and so on.
    
*   General technical fluency – understanding in quantitative analytics and structured problem solving.
    
*   May have a formal STEM (science, technology, engineering, and mathematics) background or self-taught knowledge in a STEM field.
    
*   A mastery of process-management skills -- should be able to direct an analytics initiative from ideation through production and adoption and understand the life cycle of an analytics initiative and the common pitfalls.
    
*   Entrepreneurial mind-set -- enthusiasm, commitment, and business savvy to navigate potential technical, and organizational roadblocks that may emerge.
    
*   SQL skill is a must. Python would be a bonus. 
    
*   General Knowledge on the following
    
*   General Relational Database Concepts
    
*   General Data Visualization, Data Storytelling, and tool know-how (Tableau, PowerBI, Qlickview, AWS Quicksight, etc.)
    
*   General Statistical Modeling Techniques (i.e. Regression, K-means segmentation, forecasting, etc.)
    
*   General know how on data management programming languages (SQL, R, Python, etc)
    
*   General Datawarehousing and Data Modeling concepts (Fact & Dimension tables, Slowly Changing Dimensions (SCD), etc.)
    

**What We Offer**

Opportunity for career growth and development in the #1 FinTech company in the country Working with a dynamic and highly collaborative team who want to change the game A company that values their people with highly competitive and flexible compensation and benefits package
