**Purpose of Role**
-------------------

As the **Director, Global Benefits & Wellness**, you'll be a key leader on Under Armour’s Total Rewards team, shaping the future of our global benefits and wellness programs. You'll architect our strategy, drive innovation, and evolve our offerings to ensure our programs, policies and processes attract and retain world-class talent. Reporting to the VP, Rewards and Operations, you'll play a vital role in enhancing our teammates' wellbeing by delivering a compelling, impactful, and competitive benefits experience.

**Your Impact**
---------------

*   Architect the design and development of our next-generation health, wellness, retirement, and leave programs in 30+ countries across the Americas, EMEA, and APAC
*   Leverage data, technology, and analytics to optimize program effectiveness, balance costs, and personalize the teammate experience
*   Develop and manage vendor partnerships to ensure best-in-class service and value, including negotiations and platform integration
*   Champion the teammate experience by ensuring benefits are inclusive, accessible, and aligned with life-stage needs and diverse populations
*   Innovate by evaluating emerging technologies and point-solutions and integrating them into benefit roadmap, delivery and communications
*   Partner with cross-functional teams (e.g., Finance, Legal, Procurement) to ensure compliance, fiscal stewardship, and alignment with business objectives
*   Measure impact through KPIs, teammate feedback, financial analysis, and benchmarking to continuously improve program effectiveness and ROI
*   Lead, coach, and develop a high-performing team of direct and indirect reports, fostering a culture of accountability and innovation

**Qualifications**
------------------

*   Deep expertise leading and scaling complex benefits programs in a global, matrixed organization
*   Strong functional expertise of the U.S. healthcare system, wellness sector, retirement industry, and regulatory frameworks (e.g., ACA, ERISA, HIPAA)
*   Strategic and analytical mindset with a strong command of vendor management, contract negotiation, and financial stewardship
*   Ability to be a hands-on leader who can execute and collaborate effectively with stakeholders at all levels
*   Familiarity with AI, automation, and digital health tools in the context of HR and benefits
*   Commitment to equity, teammate-centric design, and evidence-based decision-making
