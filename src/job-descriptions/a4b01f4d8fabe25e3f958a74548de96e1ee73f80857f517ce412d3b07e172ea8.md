**Make your mark for patients**

We are looking for a **Head of Asset Learning** who is strategic, innovative, and collaborative to join our **Talent Acquisition & Learning team,** based in our HQ office, in Brussels, Belgium.

**About the Role**

You will lead a team to drive a globally aligned, locally relevant, and impact-driven asset learning strategy. Acting as a key integrator, you will ensure strong connectivity between asset learning, stakeholder learning, and patient impact priorities. Your focus will be on shaping innovative, digital-first learning solutions, enhancing modular content creation, and fostering cross-functional collaboration across Commercial, Medical, Access, and regional teams.

**Who You’ll Work With**

You will collaborate with stakeholders across Commercial, Medical, and Access teams, ensuring learning strategies align with business priorities. As part of a global team, you will integrate global and local learning needs, optimizing efficiency and alignment across markets.

**What You’ll Do**

*   Lead and develop a high-performing team, fostering innovation, agility, and collaboration while ensuring asset learning aligns with business priorities and patient impact goals.
*   Oversee global asset learning strategies, ensuring consistency across global, regional, and local levels while enabling flexibility for market-specific adaptations.
*   Own and manage the global asset learning budget, ensuring optimal resource allocation, cost-efficiency, and ROI. 
*   Drive digital learning transformation, leveraging AI-driven automation and data insights to enhance accessibility, efficiency, and impact across global learning platforms.
*   Define and lead the global asset learning vision, aligning with enterprise-wide strategic priorities and business outcomes. 
*   Serve as a key advisor to asset leadership teams and PSLTs  on learning strategy, capability development, and organizational transformation. 
*   Streamline content creation and approval processes, implementing scalable solutions to improve learning efficiency while maintaining quality and compliance standards.
*   Facilitate cross-functional collaboration to optimize resource utilization and ensure alignment between asset learning, commercial, and medical learning teams.
*   Drive alignment between asset-specific and enterprise-wide learning strategies, ensuring synergies across Commercial, Medical, Access, and R&D. 
*   Establish and lead governance frameworks to ensure consistency, compliance, and quality across all asset learning programs and processes.

**Interested? For this role, we’re looking for the following education, experience, and skills:**

*   Proven leadership experience in learning, capability-building, or organizational development within a pharmaceutical or life sciences environment, driving innovation and impact.
*   Strong track record in managing global teams, aligning cross-functional priorities, and navigating complex stakeholder landscapes in a fast-paced, matrixed environment.
*   Expertise in digital learning transformation, AI-driven automation, and modular content design, ensuring scalable, flexible, and technology-driven learning solutions.
*   Proven experience of process and technology excellence, driving continuous improvement and operational efficiency through innovative, scalable, and data-driven solutions. 
*   Strategic mindset with the ability to manage ambiguity, execute efficiently, and integrate global-to-local learning strategies for business and patient impact.
*   Strong business acumen, excellent communication, and influencing skills to engage stakeholders and drive alignment across functions and geographies.
*   Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in a relevant field, with a strong background in learning, organizational development, or digital learning innovation.
