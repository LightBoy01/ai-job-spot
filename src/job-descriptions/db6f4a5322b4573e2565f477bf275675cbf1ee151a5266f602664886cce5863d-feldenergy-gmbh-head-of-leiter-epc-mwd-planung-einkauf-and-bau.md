### Description\nWith our innovative Agri-PV solutions, we bring agriculture and energy generation together. Our goal: dual land use, opening new perspectives for farmers, and actively shaping the energy transition. After successful market entry, we are now starting to build our own EPC (Engineering, Procurement & Construction) department. For this, we are looking for a committed personality to scale this area with us.\n\nAs **Head of / Leiter EPC (m/w/d)**, you take overall responsibility for the technical realization of our Agri-PV projects – from kick-off after building permit to successful commissioning. You design processes, build a powerful EPC team, and ensure efficient, scalable implementation structures.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Operational leadership of the EPC department\n- Management and optimization of interfaces between engineering, procurement, construction management, and partners\n- Selection, evaluation, and management of external service providers and subcontractors\n- Ensuring schedule, budget, quality, and compliance in implementation\n- Development of standardized processes and tools for efficient project execution\n- Participation in technical decisions (e.g., module selection, substructures, connection solutions)\n- Continuous market screening for new innovative and cost-efficient solutions\n- Support for tenders, contract negotiations, and technical due diligence\n\n### Qualifications\n- Several years of experience and a strong network in EPC or project management of PV or infrastructure projects (ideally Agri-PV or ground-mounted)\n- Knowledge in construction management, grid connection, procurement processes, and project controlling\n- High-quality standards, entrepreneurial thinking, and hands-on mentality\n- Experience in building or scaling teams or departments is an advantage\n- Proficient in relevant tools (e.g., MS Project, AutoCAD, PVSol, ERP systems)\n- Strong communication skills, willingness to travel, fluent in German\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a truly pivotal opportunity for a seasoned EPC leader to make a significant impact on the energy transition. Joining a company that's pioneering Agri-PV solutions, you'll not only build and scale a critical department but also contribute to a sustainable future. The company offers a vibrant culture centered on innovation, collaboration, and employee empowerment, evident through profit sharing, continuous learning opportunities, and a supportive environment. With 27 days of vacation, regular team events, and modern tools, they clearly prioritize employee well-being alongside ambitious goals. If you're looking to lead a high-growth initiative with strong backing and a clear mission, this role offers a unique blend of challenge and reward.
