**Founded in late 2024, Omnisent is a Munich-based acoustic AI company developing next-generation sensing systems that turn sound into real-time intelligence. The team combines deep expertise in physics, machine learning, and mechanical engineering — united by a mission to unlock the untapped intelligence of the acoustic world.**

At the core of Omnisent’s innovation are ultra-low-power sonic devices and the company’s Large Acoustic Model (LAM) — a foundation model trained on the complex world of non-speech audio. This unique combination enables the real-time detection, classification, and localization of acoustic events — whether identifying drones in contested airspace or tracing air leaks in manufacturing systems.

Tasks
-----

*   **Design, assemble, and modify** aerial and robotic platforms used for system testing.
*   **Build mechanical test rigs and fixtures** for acoustic, RF, and structural evaluations.
*   **Plan and execute experiments** in controlled lab environments, including data collection and documentation.
*   **Rapidly prototype** using FDM, SLA, SLS, and subtractive methods such as CNC machining and waterjet cutting.
*   **Contribute to enclosure and component designs** that balance acoustics, thermals, durability, and manufacturability.
*   **Own product lifecycle tasks**, including BOM maintenance, revision tracking, and test documentation.
*   **Prepare fixtures and prototypes** for external testing houses and manufacturing partners.
*   **Collaborate with cross-functional teams** to help bring a deep-tech product from concept to real-world deployment.

Requirements
------------

**Basic Requirements:**

*   **Bachelor’s or Master’s degree** in Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, or a related discipline.
*   **Strong foundations** in mechanical design and manufacturing processes.
*   **Proficiency with CAD tools** (SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Onshape, etc.).
*   **Hands-on, practical mindset** with experience in prototyping and testing.
*   **Organized working style** with attention to documentation and design traceability.
*   **Strong communication skills** in English (German a plus).
*   **EU work authorization.**

**Preferred Requirements:**

*   **Experience working on drones, robots, or electromechanical systems.**
*   **Exposure to FEA or simulation tools.**
*   **Experience collaborating with testing labs** or external engineering partners.
*   **Interest in large-scale sensing** and advanced monitoring technologies.
