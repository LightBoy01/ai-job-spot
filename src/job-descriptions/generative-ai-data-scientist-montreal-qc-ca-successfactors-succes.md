<b>Summary</b>

<p> </p>
<p>Are you passionate about language and harnessing its power with cutting-edge AI? Do you thrive in a dynamic environment where innovation drives the future? If so, we want you!</p>


<b>Key Responsibilities</b>

<p> </p>
<ul>
<li>Design, train, and deploy NLP models for various applications within the telecommunications domain. This could include tasks like sentiment analysis, topic modeling, chatbot development, and more.</li>
<li>Work collaboratively with engineers, product managers, and data analysts to translate business needs into actionable NLP solutions.</li>
<li>Explore and implement cutting-edge Generative AI (GenAI) techniques to create innovative applications for our telecommunications services.</li>
<li>Continuously monitor and evaluate the performance of NLP models, identifying areas for improvement and optimization.</li>
<li>Participate in research initiatives to stay ahead of the curve in NLP and GenAI advancements.</li>
<li>Clearly document your work and present findings to both technical and non-technical audiences.</li>
</ul>


<b>Critical Qualifications</b>

<p> </p>
<ul>
<li>Bachelors or Master's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Linguistics or a related field.</li>
<li>Experience with Large Language Models (LLMs) and Generative AI (GenAI) technology, including but not limited to OpenAI API, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Langchain, and HuggingFace Transformers.</li>
<li>Research, design, build, and train innovative applications of LLMs.</li>
<li>Proficient in Python programming, with experience utilizing data manipulation libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, and Scikit-learn.</li>
<li>Ability to write and optimize complex SQL queries for data analysis and manipulation. Experience working with various SQL databases, including but not limited to MySQL, PostgreSQL, and </li>
<li>BigQuery. Understanding of relational database concepts and data modeling is essential.</li>
<li>Proven experience in designing, training, and deploying NLP models using frameworks like TensorFlow or PyTorch.</li>
<li>Strong understanding of machine learning algorithms and their application in NLP tasks.</li>
<li>Experience with version control (Git) and testing methodologies (unit/integration)</li>
<li>Experience designing and implementing RESTful APIs for backend applications, with a strong focus on data-centric services</li>
<li>Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to explain complex technical concepts to diverse audiences.</li>
<li>A passion for innovation and a strong desire to push the boundaries of NLP with GenAI.</li>
</ul>
<p> </p>


Preferred Qualification

<p> </p>
<ul>
<li>Experience working within the telecommunications industry.</li>
<li>Knowledge of cloud computing platforms like GCP, AWS or Azure.</li>
<li>Familiarity with GenAI techniques like text generation, summarization, or machine translation.</li>
<li>Experience with prompt engineering.</li>
</ul>
