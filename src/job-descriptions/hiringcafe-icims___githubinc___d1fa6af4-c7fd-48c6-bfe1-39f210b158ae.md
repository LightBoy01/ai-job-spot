Overview:

As the global home for all developers, GitHub is the complete AI-powered developer platform to build, scale, and deliver secure software. Over 150+ million developers, including more than 90% of the Fortune 100 companies, use GitHub to collaborate and experiment across 420+ million repositories. With all the collaborative features of GitHub, it has never been easier for individuals and teams to write faster, better code.

With over 20M users, GitHub Copilot is widely recognized as the most used AI developer tool in the world, while GitHub is used by over 150M users and thousands of enterprises. In this role, you will be bringing the power of Copilot to the GitHub platform through AI-native experiences designed to help developers be more productive and collaborate with AI as a new team member.

This position requires a product manager with experience building new products and finding product market fit. The ideal candidate is a technical or design-oriented, hands on product manager that is familiar with AI products and techniques, with a deep attention to detail. You will work closely with engineering, design and marketing partners across GitHub to research and launch new experiences, and drive strategic discussions with teams and executives across the business to generate energy and alignment on goals.

Responsibilities:

Analysis of Customer & Market Signals

*   Identify and prioritize developer needs through customer engagement, feedback, and market trends.
    
*   Anticipate and mitigate blockers to ensure seamless developer workflows.
    

Product/Service Definition

*   Define and drive GitHub’s multi-model AI strategy and platform roadmap.
    
*   Influence senior stakeholders and provide thought leadership across GitHub and Microsoft.
    

Product/Service Development

*   Partner with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and emerging AI providers to evaluate and integrate cutting-edge models.
    
*   Collaborate with applied science teams to translate research into scalable platform features.
    

Go-To-Market & Delivery

*   Lead growth experiments and adoption campaigns to validate product direction and scale usage.
    
*   Communicate product vision through executive briefings, blog posts, and developer community channels.
    

Performance & Impact

*   Use data and customer insights to prioritize investments and measure platform impact.
    
*   Drive systematic, metrics-based decision-making for continuous product improvement
    

Qualifications:

**Required Qualifications:**

*   8+ years experience in product/service/project/program management, software development, product design, or related field
    *   OR Bachelor's Degree in related field AND 6+ years experience in product/service/project/program management, software development, product design, or related field
    *   OR equivalent experience
*   3+ years of experience working across AI/ML experiences, developer tooling, or new product development.
*   Experience in leading cross-functional teams and influencing senior stakeholders.
    

**Preferred Qualifications:**

*   Master’s degree in Business, Computer Science, or related field.
    
*   10+ years experience in product management or related disciplines.
    
*   Experience collaborating with AI research teams and applying emerging model capabilities to customer scenarios.
