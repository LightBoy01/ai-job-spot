Overview:

[Keysight](https://www.keysight.com/us/en/about.html) is on the forefront of technology innovation, delivering breakthroughs and trusted insights in electronic design, simulation, prototyping, test, manufacturing, and optimization. Our ~15,000 employees create world-class solutions in communications, 5G, automotive, energy, quantum, aerospace, defense, and semiconductor markets for customers in over 100 countries. Learn more [about what we do.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykxkSz5DlPo&t=16s)

Our [award-winning](https://jobs.keysight.com/awards) culture embraces a bold vision of where technology can take us and a passion for tackling challenging problems with industry-first solutions. We believe that when people feel a sense of belonging, they can be more creative, innovative, and thrive at all points in their careers.

Join Keysight’s central Data & Analytics organization as a key contributor to the Growth Insights team. In this mid-level role, you will apply advanced analytics, Business Intelligence, and Generative AI techniques to uncover insights and drive data-informed growth strategies across Keysight’s hardware, software, and services portfolio.

You will work cross-functionally with product, sales, marketing, and strategy teams to support enterprise-wide growth initiatives, reporting to a senior leader within the Data & Analytics organization.

Responsibilities:

*   Analyze Growth Opportunities: Collaborate with business stakeholders to explore complex growth challenges using data analytics, predictive modeling, and Gen AI tools to generate actionable insights.
*   Develop AI-Enhanced Dashboards: Build and maintain dynamic, Gen AI-powered dashboards and reporting tools that provide foresight and prescriptive recommendations to business teams.
*   Support ML Model Deployment: Assist in the implementation and monitoring of machine learning models for use cases such as sales recommendations, churn prediction, and financial forecasting.
*   Enable Agentic AI Solutions: Contribute to the development and testing of Agentic AI workflows that automate growth-related tasks across platforms like Salesforce, Hubspot, Eloqua, and Oracle Cloud.
*   Collaborate on Data Infrastructure: Work with Data Engineering to ensure data pipelines and architecture support scalable, AI-native analytics solutions.
*   Promote Data Literacy: Help maintain the enterprise data catalog and support internal data literacy initiatives by enabling self-service analytics and sharing best practices.
*   Deliver Executive Insights: Prepare data visualizations and summaries that support strategic decision-making by senior leadership, including the Chief Data & Analytics Officer.

Qualifications:

*   Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in a STEM field (e.g., Computer Science, Engineering, Statistics, Mathematics)
*   5+ years of experience in Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, or Data Science
*   Proficient in Python or R, and SQL queries
*   Highly experienced with BI tools such as Power BI and Tableau
*   Familiarity with Generative AI and Machine Learning concepts and tools
*   Strong analytical skills to translate data into business insights
*   Strong communications skills and affinity for data storytelling and data storyboards to communicate the 'why'
*   Experience working in cross-functional teams and managing multiple priorities
