About the role:
<p>Developers face challenges deploying trained machine learning models due to fragmented, incomplete solutions necessitating extensive adjustments and model-specific optimizations. Modular's next-generation platform aims to revolutionize AI development and deployment. As the <strong>Cloud Inference Tech Lead</strong> you will focus your efforts on a critical component of Modular's enterprise offering, focusing on a GenAI cluster-level inference product. You will use your skills and background to design and implement key features and provide a technical leadership and mentorship to others in the engineering and product organization influencing the strategic direction and helping facilitate tactical decisions. Mammoth is our Kubernetes-native cluster solution that ensures reliable, effortless deployment of customer’s GenAI models with top-tier large-scale performance, independent of using different types of GPUs or other accelerator hardware.</p>
<p>Join us in shaping the future of AI infrastructure at scale—your work will directly power the next generation of AI applications used by millions of people worldwide.</p>
<p><strong>LOCATION:&nbsp;</strong>Candidates based in the <strong>US or Canada </strong>are welcome to apply. You can work in our office in Los Altos, CA or remotely from home. Onboarding for new hires is conducted in-person in our Los Altos, CA office.</p>
