As the AI Enablement & Knowledge Specialist, you will lead Branch’s AI-first content strategy, ensuring all knowledge is accurate, consistent, and optimized for automation. You will partner with subject matter experts and AI specialists to govern and maintain the centralized knowledge base that powers our automated support channels.

The ideal candidate is a content governance expert who understands both customer experience and AI optimization, with a proven ability to connect people, processes, and technology for maximum operational impact.

### Responsibilities

- **Content Governance & Standards:**
  - Maintain and optimize the AI knowledge base, ensuring consistent content formats, tone, and metadata.
  - Develop and enforce governance standards for all AI-ready content.
  - Maintain centralized templates and tagging protocols for SOPs, playbooks, and learning materials.
- **Collaboration & Facilitation:**
  - Guide subject matter experts in creating content with AI-enabled tools.
  - Support content production across teams, focusing on bot-specific SOPs and playbooks.
  - Work with the AI Systems & Automation Specialist to operationalize knowledge within automated workflows.
- **AI-First Content Strategy:**
  - Lead the integration of AI into onboarding, SOP creation, and microlearning.
  - Review AI data for accuracy and accessibility.
  - Contribute to the development of data points for AI conversations.
- **Performance Management & Optimization:**
  - Monitor AI resolution rates to identify and close knowledge gaps.
  - Use analytics to measure content performance and drive updates.

### Qualifications

- 5+ years in knowledge management or a related operational role.
- Strong background in content governance, taxonomy, and metadata management.
- Experience with AI-powered knowledge platforms and automation tools.
- Excellent facilitation skills for guiding cross-functional contributors.
- Strong project management and organizational skills.
- Familiarity with conversational AI optimization and chatbot/voice automation.
