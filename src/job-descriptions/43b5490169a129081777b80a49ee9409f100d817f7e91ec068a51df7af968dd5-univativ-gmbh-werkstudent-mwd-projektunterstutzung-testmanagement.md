### Description\nThis part-time student trainee (Werkstudent) position in IT Project Management at univativ offers a unique opportunity to gain practical experience in the multimedia industry in Frankfurt am Main. You will actively support project management activities, monitor budgets, report on project status, act as a contact person for software-related questions, and assist the project lead with smaller sub-projects. This role is ideal for technically savvy individuals passionate about modern work environments and innovative topics, providing a promising career entry point with ample development opportunities through the univativ academy.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Communicate with key users to gather requirements and details for creating test cases.\n- Develop and document test cases, emphasizing completeness and quality.\n- Maintain and update Excel templates for Jira.\n- Create and manage Jira projects for test execution.\n- Support the Test Manager in all test management activities, including progress tracking and reporting.\n- Collaborate with the project team using Wrike and SharePoint for documentation and coordination.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Basic understanding of software testing and test management processes.\n- Familiarity with Jira or a strong willingness to quickly learn.\n- Excellent organizational and communication skills.\n- Very good knowledge of MS Excel.\n- Fluent in English (negotiation level).\n- Fluent in German.\n- Experience with Jira, Excel spreadsheets, Microsoft 365, Wrike and SharePoint.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent entry-level opportunity for students looking to kickstart their career in IT project management and software testing within the dynamic multimedia sector. univativ clearly prioritizes the development of its student workforce, offering comprehensive training, mentorship, and a supportive environment for professional growth. The hands-on experience with industry-standard tools like Jira, Wrike, and SharePoint will be invaluable. The 16-20 hours per week schedule makes it highly compatible with academic commitments. This role provides a solid foundation for future career advancement, especially for those passionate about quality assurance and project coordination.
