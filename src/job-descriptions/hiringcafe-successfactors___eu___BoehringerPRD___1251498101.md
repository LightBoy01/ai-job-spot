**Tasks & responsibilities:**

*   Define, develop, implement, enhance and maintain systems that support EHS processes such as: SAP Incident, Audit and Risk management, SAP Product Stewardship, Integrated Health Management Systems, Occupational  Safety, Regulatory Compliance, Product Compliance & Safety and much more!
*   Support and work with innovative initiatives such as leveraging AI within our field, helping to shape the future roadmap of such technologies within the company.
*   You will collaborate closely with Business Key Users and IT professionals to prioritize and plan the implementation of changes and projects.

*   You’ve earned a bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field, like Business Informatics.
*   Professional experience of <3 years working within IT as a system engineer/analyst. Experience within EHS area is desirable but not mandatory!
*   Pharma industry knowledge is a plus.
*   Understanding of basic IT concepts, architecture and terminology, such as databases, servers, web services, cloud-based set ups, APIs, amongst others.
*   You’re a natural problem-solver with a results-driven attitude, innovative thinking, and top-notch communication skills.
*   If you are a true team player who loves collaborating and working with others, you'll fit right in!

*   Willing to travel occasionally? Pack your bags and join us on this exciting journey!

*   We are an international team; therefore, you should be fluent in English, both written and spoken. German skills would also be a plus!
