**What will your typical day look like?**
-----------------------------------------

We are seeking a Senior Manager for our Technology & Transformation (T&T) department, focusing on the Future of Service offering within our national practice. As a Senior Manager, you will support the evolution and delivery of our service offerings, contribute to team growth, and help establish and maintain relationships with clients as a trusted advisor. You will deliver consulting engagements as a project manager and functional leader using proven methodologies, ensuring client satisfaction throughout the process. You will also participate in consulting sales activities, including drafting service proposals and supporting business development initiatives.

**About the team**
------------------

You will be part of a dynamic and innovative team passionate about technology and delivering exceptional value to clients. The T&T team brings together professionals from diverse backgrounds who thrive in a fast-paced, entrepreneurial environment. If you are eager to learn, grow, and make an impact through technology-enabled transformation, this is the place for you.

**Enough about us, let’s talk about you**
-----------------------------------------

A seasoned leader with over 10+ years of consulting experience, specializing in transforming customer experience strategies and driving digital channel optimization for enterprise organizations. Demonstrates deep expertise in contact center operations and is recognized for enabling next-generation Contact Center as a Service (CCaaS) solutions, including Genesys, Five9, and NICE. Adept at aligning technology investments with business outcomes, fostering innovation, and delivering measurable improvements in customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, and digital engagement.

Core Competencies: Customer Experience Strategy & Transformation, Digital Channel Optimization, Contact Center Operations & Performance Improvement, CCaaS Platform Selection, Implementation & Integration, Business Development & Enterprise Growth, Leading Complex Pursuits & Strategic Deal Shaping, Change Management & Stakeholder Engagement, Workforce Optimization & Automation (RPA, AI, WFM), Data-Driven Insights & KPI Frameworks

Professional Experience Highlights:  
•    Led multi-year CX transformation programs for enterprise clients, resulting in double-digit NPS and CSAT improvements.  
•    Directed large-scale CCaaS migrations, successfully transitioning legacy environments to cloud-based platforms and reducing total cost of ownership.  
•    Developed digital self-service strategies, increasing digital containment rates and reducing call volumes.  
•    Advised on vendor selection, RFP management, and technology roadmaps for global contact center modernization initiatives.  
•    Implemented advanced analytics and reporting solutions to enable real-time performance monitoring and continuous improvement.  
•    Drove business development efforts, cultivating executive client relationships, identifying new opportunities, and expanding service offerings.  
•    Led complex enterprise pursuits, including RFP responses, solution development, and contract negotiations, resulting in significant new business wins and long-term client partnerships.

Industry Experience: Financial Services, Retail, and/or Public Sector
