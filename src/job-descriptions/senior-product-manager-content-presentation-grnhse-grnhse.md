About Tubi:
<p>Boldly built for every fandom, Tubi is a free streaming service that entertains over 100 million monthly active users. Tubi offers the world's largest collection of Hollywood movies and TV shows, thousands of creator-led stories and hundreds of Tubi Originals made for the most passionate fans. Headquartered in San Francisco and founded in 2014, Tubi is part of Tubi Media Group, a division of Fox Corporation.</p>
<strong>About the role</strong>
<p>We are looking for a strategic and technically adept Senior Product Manager to lead the development of our content presentation, crafting intelligent, personalized user experiences powered by cutting-edge AI. This role is high-impact, owning a complex product area and a chance to shape the future of how users discover content and interact with our platform, ensuring their experiences are hyper-personalized.</p>
<p>If you are passionate about creating engaging user experiences, leveraging data and AI/ML models, through rapid experimentation and iteration, this opportunity is for you!&nbsp;</p>
<p>This is a hybrid role based out of our San Francisco office.</p>

<strong>What you’ll do:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Define and drive the product vision and roadmap creating compelling assets for our content, how they are presented, helping convert browsing into viewership.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Leverage ML/AI and deep user understanding and design impacting our topline company goals and metrics.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Design experiences, AI-generated assets, and ML models helping viewers easily explore and make decisions on what to watch next.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Foster a cohesive and high-performing cross-functional team by leading and collaborating with XFN teams including product, engineering, design, and data science, as well as closely work with the content teams.</li>
<li>Own the ML and AI models, ensuring they effectively power personalized recommendations across various user touchpoints.</li>
<li>Implement a high rate of experimentation to validate hypotheses, learn from user behavior, and iterate on product features.</li>
</ul>

<strong>Your background:</strong>
<ul>
<li>6+ years experience in product management, with some background in AI/ML-driven personalization and content discovery.</li>
<li>Proven track record of leading cross-functional teams in a fast-paced, data-driven environment and delivering complex, technical, and user-first experiences.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Deep understanding of UX principles, with a passion for the finer details.</li>
<li>Strong expertise in product experimentation, including A/B testing and iterative development, with the ability to break down a big picture vision into testable hypotheses.</li>
<li>Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills; proven ability to influence across technical and non-technical teams.</li>
<li>Experience in the consumer video or entertainment industry is a plus.</li>
<li>Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field; advanced degree a plus.</li>
<li>Passion for delivering exceptional user experiences through innovative technology solutions.</li>
</ul>
