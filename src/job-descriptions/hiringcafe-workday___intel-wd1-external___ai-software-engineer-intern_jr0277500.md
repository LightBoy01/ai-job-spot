Artificial Intelligence (AI) is changing our lives and is present everywhere. Intel provides whole software/hardware AI solutions to seamlessly integrate into customers' business.  
We are looking for a passionate Graduate Technical Intern who will help us to work on AI/LLM based solution for automatical code development and bug fixing. Work in our team includes,

*   Design and develop new features for AI based coding system;

*   Apply new and innovative ideas to the solution

*   Master's or Ph.D. student in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, or related fields
*   Good programming skills of modern C++ and Python
*   Know foundations of Deep Learning theory and some hands-on experience Communicate in English fluently (both written)
*   Are passionate on solving problems and positive thinker
*   Experience of LLM and model finetuning is a good plus
*   At least 3 days per week, able to commit to 6m above internship
