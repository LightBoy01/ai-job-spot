**As a Procurement IT & Facilities Assistant, your tasks will include the following:**

*   Support end-to-end procurement activities for IT hardware, software, and facility-related services (e.g., maintenance, cleaning, office supplies).
*   Source and procure facilities-related goods and services (such as office furniture, maintenance materials, cleaning supplies, and utilities) by identifying qualified suppliers, obtaining quotations, evaluating offers, and ensuring cost-effective and timely delivery in line with company procurement policies
*   Prepare and process purchase requests, purchase orders, and related documentation in accordance with company procurement policies.
*   Assist in the sourcing and vendor evaluation process by collecting quotations, comparing offers, and preparing evaluation summaries.
*   Coordinate with suppliers and service providers for order confirmations, delivery schedules, installations, and issue resolution.
*   Maintain and update supplier databases, contracts, and digital procurement records.
*   Support budget tracking and cost analysis for IT and facilities categories.
*   ·Coordinate license renewals and ensure compliance with software agreements
*   Track invoices, payments, and delivery documentation in coordination with Finance.
*   Ensure all procurement and facilities processes comply with internal policies, ethical standards, and approval workflows.

**Working on Home Office (HO) Secure English Language Tests (SELTs)**

Any person who is engaged by PeopleCert to work on the SELT service must undergo a Background Check (the results of which must be acceptable to PeopleCert and the HO) prior to commencing their SELT duties. All SELT personnel will be required to complete a declaration (provided by PeopleCert) where the existence of any criminal record and/or bankruptcy must be declared.

If working on the SELT service in the UK, background checks will include:

*   A basic or enhanced Disclosure Barring Service (DBS) check

*   Right to Work in the UK check (including nationality, identity and place of residence)

*   HO security check (Baseline Personnel Security Standard (BPSS) or Counter Terrorist Check (CTC)

*   Financial background check

*   Employment reference check.

*   If working on the SELT service anywhere in the world (outside of the UK) personnel will undergo background checks that are equivalent to those stated for the UK.

In addition, if personnel are required to speak to SELT candidates they must be appropriately skilled in English language and, where SELT services are provided anywhere in the world (outside of the UK), the official language of the relevant country.
