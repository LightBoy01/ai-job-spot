### Description\nThe MatchingCompany® is a specialist in professional and sustainable recruitment, boasting over 25 years of industry experience in placing skilled and executive personnel nationwide, particularly in People Management. We are seeking an operational leader with profound industry knowledge to take on the pivotal role of Branch Director for the Medical and Nursing sectors at our Aachen location.\n\nThis full-time, permanent position offers an exceptional opportunity for an entrepreneurial leader to drive significant impact. You will benefit from performance-based compensation, annual bonuses, participation in company success, and a minimum of 30 days holiday, alongside extensive onboarding, continuous professional development, and substantial room for shaping your role and career path. We also provide comprehensive corporate benefits focusing on health, family, and pension provisions.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Overall responsibility for the branch and acting as an external \"ambassador\".\n- Accountable leadership of operational business, including budget and revenue responsibility.\n- New customer acquisition, as well as the support and expansion of existing customer relationships.\n- Organizational, administrative, and personnel management of the team covering personnel and client consulting (disposition).\n- Planning and control of corresponding sales activities.\n- Coordination of collaboration with all local departments and the head office.\n- Ensuring compliance with all labor law, HR-related, and legal regulations.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed vocational training or degree.\n- Several years of experience in the personnel services industry, specifically in temporary employment and direct placement of skilled and executive personnel in the medical, nursing, and ideally, pedagogical fields.\n- Business and human resources knowledge, especially in leading employees.\n- Fluent German language skills, both written and spoken.\n- High sales affinity, goal-orientation, and a proactive working style.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a seasoned leader in personnel services to take the helm of a thriving branch. With comprehensive benefits, a focus on continuous learning, and a clear path to impact, this position is ideal for someone ready to leverage their expertise to drive both business success and team development within a stable and professional environment.
