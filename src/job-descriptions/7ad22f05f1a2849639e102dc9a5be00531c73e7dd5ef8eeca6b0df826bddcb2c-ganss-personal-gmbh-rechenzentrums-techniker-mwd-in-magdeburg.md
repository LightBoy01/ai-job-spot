### Description\nThis is an exciting opportunity to join a successful company as a Data Cabling Specialist/Project Coordinator. You will be responsible for the planning, execution, and maintenance of critical data center infrastructure, leading small to medium projects, and performing essential troubleshooting. The role offers a modern IT workplace, above-average compensation, and significant opportunities for professional and personal growth within a dynamic team environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Plan, execute, test, measure, and document patch and infrastructure cabling in customer data centers.\n- Plan and coordinate small and medium-sized projects, and lead internal and external specialist personnel.\n- Perform error analysis and basic troubleshooting for fiber optic (LWL) and copper data cabling in collaboration with quality management.\n- Execute fiber optic splicing.\n- Install and modify active components according to customer instructions.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed technical vocational training or equivalent experience in a similar field.\n- Knowledge of data cabling (passive fiber optic and copper).\n- Experience in cable installation and cable assembly.\n- Demonstrated proactiveness, independent and flexible work style, strong teamwork ability, and organizational skills.\n- Good proficiency in German and English.\n- Possession of a valid driver's license and a police clearance certificate without entries.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role is perfect for a skilled data cabling professional who is ready to take on more responsibility, including project coordination and team leadership. The company values growth and offers a strong compensation package. If you thrive in a hands-on technical role that also demands organizational prowess and collaboration, and you're looking for continuous development in a vital field, this could be your next great career move.
