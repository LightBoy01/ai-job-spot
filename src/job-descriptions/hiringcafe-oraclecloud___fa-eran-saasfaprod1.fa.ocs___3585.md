**Your Team**

The Automation Enablement Manager sits in the Technology Transformation & Operations team reporting to the Senior Manager, Automation and AI Strategy & Governance.

**Your Responsibilities**

This role manages and prioritises Automation and AI initiatives with cutting-edge technology. You will facilitate prioritisation discussions with senior stakeholders across the business.

*   Act as the primary point of contact for all internal intelligent automation initiatives including GenAI initiatives
*   Track progress and report on internal initiative status, risks, and dependencies
*   Present initiative updates and recommendations to senior leadership and governance forums.
*   Coordinate onboarding of new tools and solutions, ensuring alignment with business needs.
*   Lead cross-functional assessments involving Cybersecurity, Risk, Privacy, Legal, Data and Compliance teams.
*   Ensure all onboarding of new Automation and GenAI tools and solutions meet internal governance and regulatory requirements.
*   Manage documentation and approvals required for initiative execution.
*   Ensure initiatives comply with enterprise risk management frameworks.
*   Escalate and resolve issues related to risk, privacy, or compliance in a timely manner.

**Your Capability and Experience**

*   3-5 years in technology project and delivery management roles.
*   A high degree of autonomy and influence across the organisation.
*   A strong understanding of emerging technologies such as generative AI, machine learning, or automation. 
*   A passion for teaching technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
