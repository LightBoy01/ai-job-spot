### Description\nRadisson Hotel Group (RHG) is seeking a strategic and results-driven Director of Sales (m/w/d) to be based in Hamburg. This pivotal role involves leading and managing all sales initiatives for a diverse portfolio of 3 brands and 16 hotels across Germany, Belgium, Switzerland, and Austria. The successful candidate will be passionate about delivering exceptional customer experiences, driving performance, and inspiring a dynamic sales team to achieve outstanding results and elevate RHG's market presence.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Lead and manage all sales initiatives for a portfolio of 3 brands and 16 hotels in Germany, Belgium, Switzerland and Austria.\n- Coach, mentor, and motivate a small but dynamic team of 3 Regional Sales Managers / Account Managers to achieve outstanding results.\n- Maximize revenue growth by implementing strategic sales activities aligned with business objectives.\n- Identify and drive new business opportunities to expand market presence.\n- Build and nurture strong relationships with internal and external stakeholders to maximize sales potential.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Proven experience in a leadership role within hotel sales, with a strong track record of driving performance.\n- Commercially astute, highly organized, and able to thrive in a fast-paced environment.\n- Ambitious and results-oriented, with a passion for increasing productivity and sales volume.\n- Excellent communicator and negotiator, with the ability to build lasting partnerships.\n- Business fluency in both German and English.\n- A clear thinker with attention to detail, able to develop and execute sales strategies effectively.\n- Passionate about creating a positive impact and enhancing the hotel's reputation within the community.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting leadership opportunity for an experienced hotel sales professional looking to make a significant impact across a multi-national portfolio. RHG offers a supportive, inclusive, and growth-oriented culture that truly values its employees, often referred to as 'Moment Makers'. Beyond competitive compensation, perks include a flexible working model, exclusive hotel discounts, wellness subsidies, extensive training through the Radisson Academy, and engaging team events. If you're a strategic leader with an entrepreneurial spirit, eager to coach a dynamic team and drive revenue, this role provides a strong platform for professional growth and making memorable moments.
