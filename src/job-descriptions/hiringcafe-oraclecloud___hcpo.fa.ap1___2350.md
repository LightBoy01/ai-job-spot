**Purpose of role:**

As Australia’s most trusted manufacturer of natural health products 15 years running, and one of the Financial Reviews Best Places to Work in 2023, it couldn’t be a better time to join a truly purpose-led brand that has wellbeing and workplace flexibility at the heart of our culture! We are hiring for Full Stack Developer to be part of our Blackmores AI Health Lab.

The Blackmores AI Health Lab is a greenfield initiative aimed at leveraging advanced AI to drive innovation, personalize health experiences, and support evidence-based decision-making. The Full-stack developer will be responsible for developing backend services, frontend interfaces, APIs, and supporting programs that deliver predictive, generative, and agentic AI capabilities to users. You will work closely with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless integration, usability, and performance of AI-powered applications.

A pragmatic builder, you will translate AI prototypes into production-grade applications, ensuring scalability, security, and maintainability. implement the models and generative and agentic systems required to deliver those outcomes. 

This role reports to our AI Principal Lead and will be expected to work out from either our Surry Hills or Warriewood Office three times and two days from home weekly. 

**Responsibilities include:** 

*   Technical Execution: Build and maintain backend services, frontend interfaces, webapp development and APIs for AI applications.
    
*   Infrastructure & Automation: Implement CI/CD workflows and containerized environments for application deployment.
    
*   Collaboration: Partner with ML engineers, data scientists, and product teams to translate AI models into usable applications.
    
*   Solution Orientation: Apply Minimal Viable Product (MVP) and agile principles to rapidly iterate and deliver functional AI-powered applications.
    
*   Mentorship: Support junior engineers and cross-functional teams in best practices for software development.
    

**What we offer?** 

*   A role that designs and develop backend, frontend, APIs, and applications that deliver AI-powered solutions to users in a scalable and user-friendly manner
    
*   Great benefits to support your health and wellbeing, including access to discounted products 
    
*   Mental health support for you and your family through our Sonder assistance program 
    

**Who are you?** 

*   Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Engineering, or a related field; advanced degree preferred.
    
*   At least 3 years of work experience in building and deploying applications wrapping AI solutions using Streamlit, Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, React, Next.js or similar frontend frameworks.
*   Experience in building APIs with Rest, Flask/FastAPI or similar framework.
*   Experience with Docker, CI/CD pipelines (GitHub), and cloud-native deployment tools.
*   Proven ability to work in agile environments and collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams.
    
*   Strong analytical skills with a commitment to building scalable, reliable, and compliant software
    

Blackmores Group is a leading natural health company with proud Australian heritage, with extensive reach across Asia Pacific.  Founded by visionary naturopath Maurice Blackmore in 1932, our vision is to connect every person on earth to the healing power of nature by combining our knowledge of nature and science to deliver quality health solutions to people and their pets everywhere, every day.  
  
Blackmores Group operates in over 12 markets, with headquarters and a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in Australia, and offices across South East Asia and China.
