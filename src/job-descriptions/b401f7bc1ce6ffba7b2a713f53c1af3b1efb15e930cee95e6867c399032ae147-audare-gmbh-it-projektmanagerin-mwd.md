### Description\nDo you want to take responsibility, work directly on exciting retail projects, and collaborate with management at eye level? Then Audare is the right place for you! You'll shape IT projects from start to finish – from planning new branches and rolling out POS systems to optimizing existing processes. You're not just an implementer, but also an idea generator: your solutions directly influence our customers' success and our company's development.\n\nAudare is a young consulting and implementation team focused on digital processes in retail. Our mission is to help large retailers make their IT infrastructure stable, efficient, and future-proof. From POS systems to networks and branch projects, we are close to the business, pragmatic in our approach, and always strive to simplify processes and improve them sustainably.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- You coordinate branch projects from opening to closing – covering everything from IT rollouts to network connections.\n- You support the development and standardization of Point-of-Sale (POS) systems and ensure smooth rollouts.\n- You manage external partners and service providers, ensuring deadlines and results are consistently met.\n- You handle 2nd-level support cases and maintain a clear overview, even during escalations.\n- You meticulously document processes and results, contributing significantly to the team's knowledge base.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You have gained initial experience in IT projects (e.g., rollouts, support, or system integration).\n- You bring a basic understanding of networks and Windows environments.\n- You work in a structured, reliable manner and communicate clearly with colleagues, customers, and partners.\n- You are hands-on, solution-oriented, and eager to learn – driven by a desire for professional and personal development.\n- You show strong initiative and are willing to take on responsibility.\n- You enjoy making processes leaner and smarter.\n- You are keen to make a visible impact within a small, agile team.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at Audare offers a fantastic opportunity for a motivated individual with foundational IT experience to make a tangible impact within the retail sector. You'll work directly with company founders, gaining invaluable insights and a steep learning curve. The emphasis is on active contribution and idea generation, allowing you to truly shape processes and solutions rather than just executing them. It's an ideal fit for someone who thrives on responsibility, enjoys problem-solving, and wants to see their contributions visibly drive success in a dynamic, supportive small team environment.
