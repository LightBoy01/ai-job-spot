### Description\\nDigitale Leute is a leading platform connecting specialists and executives in product management, UX design, and engineering within the digital industry through conferences, bootcamps, and a vibrant community. In collaboration with partners like Airbnb, Porsche Digital, Atlassian, and Rewe Digital, we are actively shaping the future of software development.\\n\\nOur Digitale Leute School offers highly practical training programs for the tech sector, with a standout offering being our **Product Design Bootcamp**. This 4-month online course, conducted primarily in German, is an ideal opportunity for individuals seeking to start or transition into a career as a UX/UI Designer. It is also suitable for those looking for a part-time program. Our trainers are seasoned professionals from top tech companies, ensuring you receive up-to-date, industry-relevant knowledge. Participants will earn a **Certificate as a Product Designer / UX/UI Designer**.\\n\\nWe are committed to your success, providing **personal mentoring, close supervision, and working in simulated product teams** to mirror real-world experience. Crucially, upon completion, we **actively support your job search** through our extensive hiring network, assisting with applications and entry into digital product teams. For eligible job seekers with an education voucher (Bildungsgutschein), participation can be completely free. The next course starts soon, with limited places still available.\\n\\n### Responsibilities\\n**As a participant in the Product Design Bootcamp, you will immerse yourself in core aspects of UX/UI Design and learn to:**\\n- **User Research:** Understand fundamental user needs and behaviors.\\n- **Prototyping & Wireframing:** Develop visual and interactive representations of ideas.\\n- **Usability-Tests:** Conduct tests, gather feedback, and iterate on design solutions for improvement.\\n- **Interface Design:** Create modern, intuitive, and user-friendly interfaces.\\n- **Agile Methods:** Work effectively within a team using methodologies like Scrum and Kanban.\\n- **Practical Project Work:** Apply learned concepts by working on projects structured like those in real product teams.\\n\\n### Qualifications\\n**To thrive in this bootcamp and launch your UX/UI Design career, you should possess:**\\n- A strong enthusiasm for design, technology, and digital products.\\n- A team-oriented mindset, strong communication skills, and openness to feedback.\\n- Good German language skills, as the course and projects are predominantly conducted in German.\\n- This program welcomes career changers, juniors, or individuals looking to switch fields – **no prior design knowledge is required**, just motivation and a strong willingness to learn.\\n\\n### Author's Take\\nThis bootcamp from Digitale Leute offers an exceptional launchpad for aspiring UX/UI Designers.
