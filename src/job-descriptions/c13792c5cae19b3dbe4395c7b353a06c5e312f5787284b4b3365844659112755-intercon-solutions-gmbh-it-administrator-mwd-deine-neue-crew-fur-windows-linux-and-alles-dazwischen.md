### Description\nThe company, located in the heart of Hamburg, is seeking an IT Administrator to ensure their systems are reliable, robust, and future-proof. This role is for someone passionate about stable IT environments and continuous improvement across Windows, Linux, and network infrastructures. You'll be instrumental in both maintaining and modernizing the IT landscape, making systems truly dependable and ready for any challenge, like a seasoned Hanseat.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Serve as the primary point of contact for the entire IT infrastructure.\n*   Administer and maintain Windows and Linux servers.\n*   Manage networks (LAN, WAN, VPN).\n*   Provide 1st and 2nd-level support for colleagues.\n*   Continuously develop and modernize the IT landscape.\n*   Protect systems through firewalls, antivirus, and backup strategies.\n*   Set up and maintain workstations and hardware.\n*   Document systems in a clear and understandable manner, avoiding cryptic jargon.\n*   Collaborate on IT customer projects (e.g., with VMware or Docker).\n*   Perform vulnerability analysis and remediation.\n*   Procure quotes and evaluate IT investments.\n*   Support ISO certification audits (27001 & 9001).\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed vocational training as an IT Specialist in System Integration, a degree in Computer Science, or equivalent.\n*   Solid experience with Windows and Linux administration.\n*   Knowledge of network technologies (TCP/IP, Routing, Switching).\n*   Experience with virtualization (VMware, Docker & Co.).\n*   Bonus points for experience with ISO 27001 & ISO 9001.\n*   Structured way of working, solution-oriented approach, and enjoyment of troubleshooting.\n*   High service orientation and strong communication skills.\n*   Good command of German and English.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a dedicated IT Administrator to join a vibrant and supportive team in the heart of Hamburg. The company cultivates a highly collaborative culture, offering substantial creative freedom ('Gestaltungsspielraum') and genuine professional development opportunities that go beyond the usual. With flexible working hours, home office options, and a clear commitment to providing modern tools and stable systems, they evidently prioritize work-life balance and employee empowerment. This role is more than just daily administration; it's about actively shaping a robust IT future and contributing directly to the company's success and security certifications. If you're looking for a secure, long-term position that promises stability, growth, and a team that 'functions together and occasionally laughs,' this role is definitely worth exploring.
