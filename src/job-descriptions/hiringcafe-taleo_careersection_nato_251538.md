**Post Context**

ACT contributes to preserving the peace, security and territorial integrity of Alliance member states by leading, at Strategic Command level, Warfare Development required to enhance NATO’s posture, military structures, forces, capabilities and doctrines.

The Capability Development Directorate (CAPDEV) comprises two Divisions—Requirements and Capabilities. The Directorate supports SACT in his Capabilities Requirement Authority (CRA) role. It is responsible for a holistic through lifecycle Capability Development approach that infuses innovation and transformative efforts that are an integral part of the Warfare Development. This includes responsibilities for elicitation, development, capture and collection, quality review, traceability and visibility of capability requirements.

The Requirements Division executes requirements management for NATO capabilities. It develops capability requirements, provides traceability and quality assurance of capability requirements, performs gap analysis and supports interoperability and implementation options across the DOTMLPFI spectrum.

The Federated Interoperability Branch supports and enables the delivery of coherent and interoperable capabilities by developing and verifying the associated capability specifications in order to ensure Day Zero interoperability of people, processes, and technologies.

Within the FI Branch, the Principal IV&V Requirements Manager will lead the requirements gathering, analysis, and validation process with ACO and SHAPE as the critical customer for interoperable C2 capabilities. The Principal IV&V Requirements Manager will put in place the necessary policies, architectures and systems to enable year-round and continual C3 interoperability testing collaborating closely with Enterprise stakeholders and Partners.

**Principal Duties:** 

*   Leadership: Lead and take part in diverse multinational work groups and activities related to the management and communication of continual year-round IV&V test events. This may include deputizing for higher-grade staff, as required.
*   Resource Management: Contribute to the budgetary and human resources efficiently to ensure the Section’s optimal performance in the execution of IV&V activities and events.
*   Operations Oversight: Contribute to the day-to-day development and expansion of C3 testing activities at the Command, contribute to information management using IV&V tools and conduct routine liaison with NATO and nations to ensure participation in IV&V activities and events.
*   Performance Evaluation: Contribute information to measure the effectiveness if C3 interoperability activities.
*   Risk Management: Identify, report and mitigate potential risks associated with NATO and national participation in C3 Interoperability test programs, ensuring continuity of essential functions and real-life support to IV&V events.
*   Strategic Planning: Contribute to the prioritization of C3 Interoperability test events, aligning with NATO's DT Vision, Strategic Objectives, Deliverables and Outcomes.
*   Stakeholder Collaboration: Routine stakeholder engagement with NATO Enterprise, member nations, academic institutions, industry partners, and other stakeholders to improve awareness, communicate the benefits and provide access to C3 interoperability testing activities.
*   Communication: Effectively deliver briefings and communicate with ACT leadership, subordinates, and external stakeholders.  Convey complex information and expectations in a clear and concise manner.
