**Dealer Sentiment & Measurement Analyst - Global Dealer Engagement** 

We are the movers of the world and the makers of the future. We get up every day, roll up our sleeves and build a better world -- together. At Ford, we’re all a part of something bigger than ourselves. Are you ready to change the way the world moves? 

Our Global Dealer Engagement team builds successful partnerships with our 9,500 dealers and helps them unlock their full potential and increase customer satisfaction. We focus on training and productivity, governance, customer sentiment, diversity, community, retail excellence, systems and process modernization, and rewards and recognition to help our dealers differentiate themselves from the competition and maximize their strengths.

### Responsibilities

**What you'll do...**

The Dealer Sentiment & Measurement Analyst is responsible for contributing to data and analytic reporting for Global Dealer Engagement (GDE) and supporting teams for Sentiment and Benchmarking Studies through implementation of static visuals in Excel/PPT and dynamic dashboards in various dashboards and highly interested in creating AI driven closed database search engines for Ford internal use. The ideal candidate must be relentlessly curious and possess a desire to understand and anticipate the data required to analyze trends in data that might point to a risk or opportunity of how we might better manage our relationship with our dealership globally.

GDE is aligned around a mindset that Ford and its dealers will “win together” by accelerating to catch and surpass competitors in automotive sales and service (both traditional and non-traditional).

We are looking to build a diverse team that brings together multiple backgrounds and experiences to create diversity of thought. Success will require a mix of talent that collectively understands the customer (as people), dealers (as partners), service, credit, marketing & sales, product development, etc.

*   Help deliver a comprehensive closed database of global dealer sentiment studies powered by GENAI in a format useable by all Ford stakeholders
*   Structure SmartSheets for GDE Pillars (Education & Training; Dealer Culture; others) for in-house project management
*   Help bring to life measurement initiatives in a visual format understandable at the executive level.
*   Assist in coordinating projects and deliverables for the Chief Global Dealer Experience Officer as necessary
*   Collaborating with global partners to support the development of transformative dealer experiences
*   Building and managing network of external resources to deeply learn and gain access to emerging best-in-class auto and non-auto franchise-models and technologies

### Qualifications

**You'll have...**

*   Associate’s degree or equivalent combination of relevant education and experience.
*   5+ years of experience with advanced Excel capabilities (n-gram, macros, coding)
*   5+ years of experience with Qlik Sense & Smartsheets & Power Bi
*   2+ years of technical experience with LLM/AI Tools and prompt engineering

**Even better, you may have...**

*   Experience working on global teams
*   Dealer experience is preferred
*   Strong interest in advocating for and delivering data to improve dealer sentiment and customer experience
*   Comfortable working in whitespace; ability to use variety of frameworks to provide structure creatively
*   Experience cultivating information and data from a variety of sources and synthesizing it into a cohesive story
*   Detail-oriented with ability to balance competing priorities and problem-solve in real time
*   Ability to build strong cross functional relationships and collaborate with peers and partners globally
*   Understanding of Ford channel complexities (dealer, contact center, web, app, vehicle)
*   Familiar leveraging management consulting structured problem-solving frameworks
*   Persuasive in making a case for change leading to action

**Key Leadership Behaviors**

*   Excellence: Understand the customer value proposition and ensure all aspects of the strategy support improvement
*   Focus: Create a clear vision for the future of automotive retail at Ford through research and benchmarking, especially through direct engagement with market leaders and dealers
*   Collaboration: Intense curiosity to learn and seek to understand by going to where the work is done. Engaging others and bringing them along on the journey to imagine “the art of the possible”
