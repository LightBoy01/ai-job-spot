As an IT Security Manager, your main responsibility will be to implement and ensure compliance with security standards across both on-premise networks and cloud environments.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Further development and securing of the on-premise network landscape, including the transition to the cloud.\n*   Conducting penetration tests to identify vulnerabilities.\n*   Creating security policies and implementing guidelines.\n*   Providing security awareness training for employees.\n*   Implementing firewalls.\n*   Incident Management.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed degree in IT Security or Business Informatics, OR approximately 5 years of professional experience in the field.\n*   Experience in securing on-premise networks and cloud structures, as well as implementing security-relevant guidelines.\n*   High motivation, self-initiative, and analytical thinking.\n*   Fluent German language skills and good English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role offers a fantastic blend of strategic development and hands-on implementation in a critical domain. The company prioritizes employee well-being with flexible hours, ample vacation, and comprehensive wellness benefits, coupled with strong support for professional growth. It's a key position within a large, stable European leader, perfect for a security professional looking to make a significant impact.
