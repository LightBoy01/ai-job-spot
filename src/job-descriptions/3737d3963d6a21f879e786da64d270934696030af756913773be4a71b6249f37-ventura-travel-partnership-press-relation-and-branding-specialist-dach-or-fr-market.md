_Join us at Ventura TRAVEL – a group of specialized travel brands that unleash the adventurer in every person! As the Marketing team, our mission is understanding our customer's needs and wishes._

As our **Partnerships, PR & Branding Specialist,** you will build and secure strategic partnerships and PR opportunities that expand the reach of our travel brands. In doing so, you will have the opportunity to strengthen Ventura TRAVEL's brand awareness, grow our visibility in your market and drive measurable impact on branded search and partnership funding.

You will report directly to our Marketing Director and work in an international marketing team of +10 highly motivated, skilled and passionate colleagues. We thrive on diverse perspectives and value people who bring fresh ideas and strong ownership. Check out our video to learn more about who we are and how we work.

**Please note: We only consider applications that have a CV in English.** 

_On a daily basis, you will_
----------------------------

*   Identify, pitch and secure strategic partnerships with tourism boards, travel companies and relevant consumer brands
*   Manage partnership pipelines end to end, from first outreach to execution
*   Raise budgets and funds for joint initiatives that increase reach and visibility
*   Build relationships with journalists, bloggers and media to secure ongoing coverage
*   Drive PR outreach to increase mentions, visibility and award opportunities
*   Identify and apply to relevant travel and marketing awards
*   Proactively secure high quality backlinks to strengthen SEO and brand authority
*   Track KPIs such as branded search volume, media reach and partnership value

_The basics you bring along_
----------------------------

*   Strong communication and relationship building skills
*   Confident networker who enjoys outreach and connecting with people
*   Clear understanding of how brand awareness supports business growth
*   Ability to independently identify opportunities and turn them into measurable impact
*   Structured way of working, clear prioritization and focus on results
*   High motivation to drive visibility, media presence and awards
*   Fluency in German or in French
*   Identify opportunities to apply AI and take initiative to implement solutions.

_You can impress us even more with_
-----------------------------------

*   Existing contacts within tourism boards, media or relevant consumer brands
*   Background and proven success in PR
*   Longstanding tourism background and strong network within the German tour operator landscape
