### Description\nThis is an exciting opportunity for a motivated individual to kickstart a career in Cloud and DevOps engineering. In this role, you will gain hands-on experience building, deploying, and operating cloud services across AWS, Azure, and GCP, while actively contributing to automation, containerization, and migration projects.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Build, deploy and operate cloud services on AWS/Azure/GCP with guidance.\n- Create simple CI/CD pipelines and improve release processes.\n- Automate routine tasks with Terraform/Ansible or scripts (Python/Bash).\n- Containerize apps with Docker and learn the basics of Kubernetes.\n- Monitor systems, troubleshoot incidents and document what you learn.\n- Join migration projects from on-prem to cloud and follow security best practices.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Curiosity about cloud/DevOps and willingness to learn fast.\n- Basic hands-on with ANY of: AWS, Azure, GCP, Linux, Git, Docker, Python/Bash or CI/CD tools.\n- Communicate clearly in English (German a plus).\n- Eligible to work in Germany.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent entry-point for anyone eager to dive deep into Cloud and DevOps. The explicit mention of \"guidance\" and \"learning the basics\" alongside \"basic hands-on\" requirements highlights a strong commitment to professional development. If you're driven to learn rapidly and want practical experience across multiple cloud providers, this role offers a clear path to becoming a proficient Cloud/DevOps Engineer.
