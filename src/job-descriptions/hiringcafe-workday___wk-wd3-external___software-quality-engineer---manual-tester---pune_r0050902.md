**About the Role:**

As a Senior Software Quality Assurance Analyst, you will contribute significantly to the reliability and performance of our software products. Your role will involve more complex testing activities, a higher level of defect analysis, and active participation in enhancing our QA methodologies. You will be pivotal in ensuring our products meet the highest standards of quality.

**Responsibilities:**

• Design, execute, and document complex test cases.

• Perform in-depth root cause analysis of defects.

• Develop and enhance test plans and test cases.

• Conduct performance, load, and stress testing.

• Automate repetitive test cases where applicable.

• Assist in implementing and maintaining QA tools.

• Design and execute comprehensive manual test plans for complex systems, including financial transactions, or API integrations.

using tools like Selenium, Cypress, or Postman.

• Validate API functionality, performance, and security across microservices and third-party integrations.

•  Utilize AI-powered QA tools for test case generation, defect prediction, and intelligent reporting

• Coordinate with cross-functional teams to ensure comprehensive testing.

• Mentor junior QA team members.

• Participate in code reviews from a QA perspective.

• Propose and drive improvements in QA processes and standards.

**Skills:**

• Advanced Test Case Execution: Comprehensive testing capabilities.

• Root Cause Analysis: Thorough investigation of defects.

• Test Automation: Familiarity with tools like Selenium.

• Experience testing APIs (REST, GraphQL) and understanding of JSON, XML, and authentication protocols.

Experience in Manual Testing - 3+ Years

• **Hands-on experience with AI tools in QA (e.g., Testim, Mabl, or GitHub Copilot)**

• Performance Testing: Understanding performance testing tools and methods.

• Collaboration: Effective interaction with cross-functional teams.

• Documentation: Advanced skills in preparing test documentation.

• Bug Tracking: Detailed and efficient use of tracking systems.

• Continuous Improvement: Proactive in enhancing QA processes.

• Requires in-depth conceptual and practical knowledge in primary technical job family and knowledge of related technical job families; has worked with a range of technologies

• Has knowledge of best practices and aware of the competition and market differentiators

• Knowledge of CI/CD tools (e.g., Jenkins, GitHub Actions) and version control systems.

• Solves complex technical problems; exercises judgment based on the analysis of multiple sources of information

• Works independently, receives minimal guidance; may lead projects or may have accountability for on-going activities

• Impacts a range project activities within own team and other related teams; acts as a resource for colleagues
