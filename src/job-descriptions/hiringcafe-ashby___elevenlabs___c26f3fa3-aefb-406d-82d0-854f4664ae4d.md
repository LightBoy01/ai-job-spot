About ElevenLabs
----------------

ElevenLabs is a research and product company defining the frontier of audio AI. Millions of people use our technology to read articles, voice over videos, and restore voices lost to disability. Leading developers and enterprises worldwide use ElevenLabs to build intelligent agents for support, sales, and education.

We launched in January 2023 with the first AI model to cross the threshold of human-like speech. In January 2025, we raised a $180 million Series C round, valuing the company at $3.3 billion. By September 2025, that valuation doubled to $6.6 billion as we surpassed $200 million ARR in under three years.

Our mission is to build the most important audio AI platform in the world, solve AI audio intelligence, and make information accessible in any voice, language, or sound.

Our core offerings are our Creative Platform and the Agents Platform, powered by proprietary Text to Speech, Speech to Text, and conversational AI models.

We are just getting started. If you want to work hard and create lasting impact, we would like to hear from you.

**About the Role**
------------------

We are seeking an experienced and dynamic leader to spearhead ElevenLabs’ public policy and government affairs efforts globally. The Public Policy Lead will drive our public policy and public sector engagement strategies globally, represent ElevenLabs in conversations with policymakers and other stakeholders, monitor and track regulatory developments that impact the company, and help shape frameworks that promote innovation _and_ the responsible use of AI.

**In this role, you will:**

*   Lead ElevenLabs engagement with government bodies and officials globally, with a focus on AI governance, safety, copyright, privacy, transparency, data protection, competition, and/or digital rights.
    
*   Build and maintain strong relationships with policymakers, regulators, industry associations, and civil society groups.
    
*   Monitor, analyze, and advise on emerging regulations impacting the company.
    
*   Represent ElevenLabs at conferences and industry events, advancing the company’s public policy goals.
    
*   Support the development of internal policies by our Legal, Safety, Security, and other teams.
    
*   Provide strategic counsel to company leadership on global policy trends and their business implications.
    

**Who you are:**

*   You’re genuinely interested in the future of AI and emerging technologies, bringing curiosity and enthusiasm to your role.
    
*   You stay up-to-date on AI regulation and industry trends, and are eager to apply this expertise to cutting-edge products.
    
*   You thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic startup environment, working cross-functionally with teams across the organization to support and scale a growing business.
    
*   You’re comfortable working independently and handling ambiguity, excelling in situations where you must make informed decisions quickly.
    
*   You bring a proactive, solution-oriented approach to each project, focused on moving the business forward.
    

**What you bring:**

*   8+ years’ experience in public and private sector (in-house, firm) policy roles, with a focus on AI and tech.
    
*   Strong knowledge of the global AI policy landscape, including general AI governance, safety, copyright, privacy, transparency, data protection, competition, and digital rights.
    
*   Demonstrated success in designing, advocating for, and influencing policy outcomes, as well as coalition building.
    
*   Public policy experience both within and outside the U.S.
    
*   Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, including when briefing government officials and other policymakers.
    

**Location**

This role is remote and can be executed in the United States.
