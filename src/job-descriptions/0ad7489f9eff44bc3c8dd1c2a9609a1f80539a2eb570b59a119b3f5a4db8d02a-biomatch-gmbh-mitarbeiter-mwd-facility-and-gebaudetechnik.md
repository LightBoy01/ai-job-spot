### Description\
Join our Facility Management team in Planegg as a dedicated Employee for Facility & Building Technology. This role is offered on a full-time or part-time basis with an immediate start, contributing significantly to the operational excellence and safety of our technical, laboratory, and office environments.\
\
### Responsibilities\
- Support in technical building operations and laboratory/building infrastructure.\
- Coordination and accompaniment of external service providers (e.g., electrical, climate/cooling, cleaning, maintenance).\
- Execution of minor repairs, manual work, and general caretaker duties.\
- Assistance with maintenance, technical inspections, and safety-relevant controls.\
- Regular inspections in laboratory, technical, and office areas to ensure order, functionality, and safety.\
- Support with moves, renovations, room changes, and internal projects.\
- Taking over simple logistical tasks, such as material provision, and support for events and site logistics.\
- Collaboration on inventories and warehouse activities.\
- Documentation of all activities, disruptions, and identified defects.\
- Contribution to the adherence of safety, hygiene, and quality standards.\
- Utilization of basic IT knowledge to support technical and administrative processes.\
\
### Qualifications\
- Technical or craft training (e.g., electrical, building services, mechanics, locksmithing).\
- Initial experience in technical service, ideally in a laboratory environment.\
- Basic knowledge in electrical or building technology.\
- Meticulous and responsible work ethic.\
- Very good German language skills; basic English skills are helpful.\
\
