Responsibilities:

This internship offers a unique opportunity to drive innovation in advanced test and measurement challenges, working on cutting-edge projects in a collaborative environment. We are seeking an enthusiastic Bachelor’s or Master’s level student with a passion for solving complex technical problems and applying academic knowledge in real-world conditions.

**About the Role**

As a future Software, or AI/ML engineer, you will have the chance to tackle test methodology challenges and contribute to instrument driver development. Projects will focus on:

*   Test development, automation and execution
*   Building application and Test Infrastructure

**Basic Qualifications**

Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field

*   Practical experience in software development
*   Fluent in English (written and oral communication)
*   Interest in test methodologies and problem-solving

Qualifications:

**Preferred Qualifications**

*   Undergraduate student pursuing an B.S. or M.S. in a relevant technical field
*   Familiarity with Large Language Models (LLMs) or Generative AI methodologies
*   Exposure to test automation

**Technical Skills**

*   Familiarity with basic programming skills such as Python, C, or C#.
*   Hands-on expertise with any LLMs or Generative AI methodologies is a plus
*   Comfort with software development tools and practices
*   Strong problem-solving skills and eagerness to learn new technologies

**Who You Are**

We’re looking for a proactive, self-motivated individual with a proven ability to deliver complex software features. You are passionate about AI/ML technologies, thrive in collaborative environments, and value diverse perspectives. Your enthusiasm for innovation, teamwork, and excellent communication skills will help you succeed in this role.

**Why Join Us?**

This internship provides hands-on experience in a dynamic, innovative environment where you can apply your academic knowledge to real-world challenges. You’ll work alongside talented professionals, contribute to impactful projects, and grow your skills in a supportive, collaborative setting.
