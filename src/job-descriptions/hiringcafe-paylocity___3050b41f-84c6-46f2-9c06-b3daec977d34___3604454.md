Our in-house risk managers are experts when it comes to understanding the unique intricacies of insuring successful customers, ensuring proper coverage, loss prevention and emergency preparations.

We are looking for the best and the brightest talent inspired to protect what matters most to our customers – a team, a culture and philosophy to be the best at what we do.

We are seeking an experienced AI Engineer to join our dynamic team and drive the design, development, and deployment of cutting-edge artificial intelligence solutions. The ideal candidate has a strong foundation in machine learning, deep learning, and large-scale data systems, with hands-on experience in building production-grade AI applications. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams including data engineers, product managers, and software developers to deliver impactful AI-driven applications.

  

**Job Responsibilities include but are not limited to:**

· Design, develop, and deploy AI/ML models for enterprise-grade applications.

· Build end-to-end ML pipelines including training, evaluation, optimization, deployment, and monitoring.

· Architect and develop agentic AI systems including multi-agent workflows, RAG chatbots, and intelligent automation.

· Build RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) applications tailored to insurance workflows.

· Develop multi-agent orchestration systems for complex P&C processes.

· Drive company-wide AI adoption by identifying high-impact use cases across P&C insurance functions.

· Conduct experimentation and research with state-of-the-art AI techniques and frameworks.

· Establish AI development standards, best practices, and governance frameworks.

· Collaborate with our Python-strong data engineering team on backend integrations.

Requirements

· Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, or a related field (PhD preferred).

· 5+ years of professional experience in AI/ML engineering, with a proven track record of building and deploying production-grade AI systems.

· Expertise in Python with proficiency in leading AI/ML frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, Hugging Face, and Scikit-learn.

· Hands-on experience with MLOps platforms, including Kubeflow, MLflow, and Azure Machine Learning (Vertex AI/SageMaker acceptable).

· Strong applied experience with LLMs (Large Language Models), RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) techniques, and vector databases (e.g., Pinecone, Weaviate, FAISS).

· Practical experience with agent frameworks, workflow orchestration, and multi-agent systems.

· Proficiency in full-stack development, including web frameworks, backend systems, API development (REST, GraphQL), and deployment on cloud infrastructure (Azure strongly preferred; AWS/GCP acceptable).

· Solid knowledge of containerization and orchestration tools such as Docker and Kubernetes.

· Familiarity with AI security, compliance, and governance standards, particularly for regulated industries.

· Hands-on experience with multiple database platforms, including SQL Server, Oracle, and Snowflake.

· Ability to thrive in ambiguity, demonstrating strong ownership, adaptability, and problem-solving in fast-paced, high-trust environments.

· Domain knowledge in Property & Casualty (P&C) insurance or experience addressing business problems in regulated industries is a strong plus.
