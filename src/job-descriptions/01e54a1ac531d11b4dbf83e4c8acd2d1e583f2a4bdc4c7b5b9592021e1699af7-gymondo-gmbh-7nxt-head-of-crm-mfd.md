### Description\nWe empower people to lead a fit and healthy life through Gymondo, 7Mind, and BuddyFit, supporting millions in building healthier routines. As Head of CRM (m/f/d) at 7NXT, you will be a central Marketing leader, overseeing the entire user lifecycle across our three leading wellbeing brands. This strategic role involves designing behavior-science-informed CRM strategies, leading a high-performing team, and leveraging Braze as a powerful growth engine to achieve measurable business impact through first activation to long-term retention.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Build a behaviour-science-driven lifecycle strategy:** Utilize principles of behavioral psychology and habit formation to influence user behavior, transforming insights into journeys that reduce friction, overcome resistance, and increase long-term adherence.\n- **Lead CRM for three brands with different motivations & usage patterns:** Develop a modular CRM system supporting Gymondo’s daily workouts, BuddyFit’s live-class dynamics, and 7Mind’s mental health routines, identifying cross-brand synergies while maintaining clear brand identity.\n- **Own Braze as a strategic growth platform:** Lead the roadmap for Braze experimentation, segmentation, data flows, and channel orchestration (email, push, in-app, SMS), collaborating closely with Product, Data & Engineering to ensure robust, real-time, and insight-rich CRM data foundations.\n- **Drive a strong test-and-learn culture:** Run structured experiments (A/B, multivariate, holdout tests) to continuously improve onboarding, activation, and retention KPIs, establishing dashboards and performance rituals.\n- **Build and lead a high-performing world-class central CRM team:** Hire, coach, and develop lifecycle marketers, CRM specialists, and automation experts, strengthening collaboration with Product, Brand, Creative, and Paid Media.\n- **Deliver measurable impact:** Be accountable for onboarding conversion, activation rates, weekly & monthly engagement, retention & churn, incremental revenue from CRM, audience health, and channel performance.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 10+ years of experience in CRM / lifecycle marketing within subscription-based consumer apps in the health and wellness vertical (digital fitness, nutrition, mental wellbeing, self development, habits and goal setting, digital therapy).\n- Deep understanding of behavior-change cycles, addressing motivation dips and habit formation challenges.\n- Expertise in Braze with strong knowledge of segmentation, customer journey orchestration, Canvas Flow, Liquid, automation, and triggers.\n- Strong ability to design communications that support habit formation, reduce drop-off points, and guide users through moments of low motivation.\n- Analytical and impact-focused: able to interpret cohorts, isolate signals from noise, and measure incremental impact.\n- Comfortable crafting journeys, defining data requirements, and presenting strategy.\n- Skilled at creating clarity and building structure in a multi-brand environment.\n- Ability to simplify complexity and build scalable CRM systems.\n- Excellent English skills (German is a plus).\n- A passion for wellbeing, fitness, or mental health.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a pivotal Lead-level role for an experienced CRM professional eager to make a significant impact across three leading European wellbeing brands. 7NXT champions a high-autonomy, performance-driven culture that values empowerment, transparency, and continuous experimentation. You'll lead a team, shape a modern CRM ecosystem powered by behavioral science and real-time data, and have direct influence from day one. The company fosters a supportive environment with excellent benefits, including 30 days of vacation, a hybrid working model (3 days in office, 2 WFH), 4 weeks of remote work abroad, comprehensive wellness perks (Urban Sports Club, Nilo Health, free product access), and a generous €1,000 annual training budget. They are genuinely committed to diversity, inclusion, and personal growth, making it an ideal place for someone passionate about health and wellbeing to thrive.
