****About the role****

We are seeking a ****future-ready Digital Marketing Manager**** to lead Mozn’s digital marketing strategy across all three business units — ****FOCAL****, ****OSOS****, and ****Mozn Solutions****. This role will manage the full spectrum of online marketing initiatives to build brand presence, generate demand, and support revenue growth in a rapidly evolving AI-powered world.

The ideal candidate is a ****tech-savvy, data-driven marketer**** with deep experience in B2B SaaS/AI, an appetite for continuous learning, and the ability to stay ahead of marketing trends by experimenting with emerging AI tools and technologies.

  

****What you'll do****

****Digital Marketing Strategy & Execution****

*   Develop, implement, and manage multi-channel digital marketing campaigns across Mozn’s websites, landing pages, and online platforms.
*   Own and manage the Mozn and FOCAL websites end-to-end, including content updates, UX optimization, web development projects, integrations, and performance tracking.
*   Plan and execute inbound and outbound lead generation campaigns across paid, owned, and earned channels.
*   Manage paid advertising campaigns (Google, LinkedIn, YouTube, Meta, Bing, programmatic..) from strategy to execution, optimization, and reporting.
*   Set up and manage marketing automation workflows (HubSpot / Salesforce) for lead nurturing, newsletters, and account-based campaigns.
*   Maintain and optimize corporate and product websites for user experience, SEO, and lead generation.
*   Ensure brand consistency and messaging alignment across all digital touchpoints.

****Social Media Management****

*   Oversee Mozn’s social media presence across LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and other relevant platforms, ensuring alignment with brand strategy, audience engagement, and campaign integration.

****SEO, SEM & Optimization****

*   Lead SEO and SEM strategies to improve visibility, rankings, and conversion rates across all Mozn websites and microsites.
*   Implement conversion rate optimization (CRO) best practices and personalization strategies to increase engagement and lead quality.

****Analytics, AI & Experimentation****

*   Monitor and report on KPIs / OKRs — including MQL-to-SQL conversion rates, channel ROI, pipeline influence, and campaign performance.
*   Build Dashboards and Prepare Reports on performance on Salesforce.
*   Leverage Salesforce CRM, HubSpot, and advanced analytics dashboards to track marketing pipeline generation, pipeline influence, omni-channel attribution, and customer journey insights.
*   Leverage predictive analytics and machine learning insights for campaign targeting and personalization.
*   Drive a culture of experimentation by designing and executing A/B and multivariate tests to optimize messaging, creative, and conversion rates.

****Innovation & Continuous Learning****

*   Continuously research, evaluate, and pilot emerging AI-driven marketing technologies, and automations ensuring Mozn remains ahead of industry trends.
*   Integrate AI-powered marketing solutions, including generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, AI video platforms) for content creation, personalization, and campaign optimization.
*   Build and maintain an integrated marketing technology stack, ensuring seamless data flow across CRM, automation, analytics, and personalization tools.
*   Stay engaged in AI marketing communities, industry events, and certifications to ensure strategies remain cutting-edge.

****Collaboration & Leadership****

*   Work closely with Product Marketing, Field Marketing, Channel Marketing, Sales, and Corporate Communications to align campaigns with go-to-market priorities.
*   Manage vendor relationships, freelancers, and agency partners as needed.
*   Support and mentor digital marketing team members, fostering a culture of innovation and continuous improvement.

  

****Qualifications****

*   Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Marketing, Business, or STEM.
*   Certifications in Digital Marketing, HubSpot, or Google Analytics are a plus.
*   7+ years of B2B digital marketing experience, preferably in AI, SaaS, or enterprise technology.
*   Proven track record in lead generation, SEO, SEM, paid media, email marketing, and marketing automation.
*   Hands-on experience with leading marketing, advertising, and web platforms such as Webflow, HubSpot, LinkedIn Ads, Meta Ads, Semrush, and AI-powered creative tools.
*   Familiarity with AI and data concepts, including large language models, predictive analytics, and marketing AI frameworks.
*   Proficiency in managing campaigns for both Arabic and English audiences.
*   Strong analytical skills with experience in marketing KPIs, ROI measurement, and pipeline attribution.
*   Experience working with Customer Data Platforms (CDPs), API integrations, and advanced marketing analytics tools.
*   Track record of running structured experimentation and applying growth marketing techniques to drive measurable impact.
*   Experience in GCC markets, with knowledge of local digital channels and cultural nuances.
*   Fluency in English is required; proficiency in Arabic is a strong plus, enabling deeper engagement and personalization for regional audiences.
*   Proactive in delivering measurable results and driving meaningful change through high-quality work (****Impact****).
*   Strong collaboration and communication skills; able to work effectively across functions and cultures (****People****).
*   Committed to continuous learning, innovation, and maintaining the highest standards in all deliverables (****Excellence****).
*   Self-motivated with the ability to take full responsibility for tasks, from conception to delivery, with minimal supervision (****Ownership****).
*   Flexible and adaptable to evolving priorities in a fast-paced, high-growth environment.
