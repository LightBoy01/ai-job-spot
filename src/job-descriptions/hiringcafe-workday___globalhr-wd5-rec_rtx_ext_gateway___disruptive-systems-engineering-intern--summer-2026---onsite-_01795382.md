Collins Aerospace Mission Systems Advanced Technology & Capabilities (ATC) Engineering seeks a **Intern Engineer** to join the **Disruptive Systems Technology** Team. Our cross-discipline team solves defense customers’ toughest problems through rapidly developing, integrating, and demonstrating systems that employ cutting-edge signal processing and networking innovations.  We pride ourselves on creativity, facing challenging and complex problems head on, and pushing the limits of the possible – all to provide the Warfighter with the most responsive and disruptive capabilities that navigate the intersection of communications; position, navigation & timing (PNT); cyber space; and other radio frequency (RF) systems.

ATC focuses on cutting-edge Department of Defense research and development projects. As an ATC engineer, you will continually advance the state of the art through the creation of innovative system concepts and technological capabilities.  As concepts / capabilities are proven, matured, and demonstrated, you will collaborate with business unit engineers in the adoption and transition of technologies into the next generation of Collins Aerospace products and services. 

Within a group of highly innovative engineers, this role provides leadership over low TRL (Technology Readiness Level <6) programs to ensure all the key performance characteristics are consistently met and that valuable IP is captured and protected as part of the Invention Disclosure process. The effective candidate will have experience both capturing pursuits and bringing the program’s vision to reality, including technical leadership and execution. 

**What You Will Do:**

*   Design, code, test, integrate, and document software solutions
    
*   Collaborate with project managers and other professionals within Engineering
    
*   Assist in design and development of innovative solutions for communication, navigation, radar and electronic warfare applications.
    
*   Collaborate closely across multi-disciplinary teams to rapidly identify and implement innovative, effective solutions.
    
*   Contribute to technical presentations that articulate design approaches and their relevance to project objectives.
    

**What You Will Learn:**

*   You will become familiar with the specific technologies, programming languages, and tools used within the aerospace and defense industry.
    
*   You will develop skills in working within interdisciplinary teams, communicating effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders, and presenting their work.
    
*   You will gain an understanding of the products and systems developed by Collins Aerospace and the Disruptive Technologies Team.
    

**Qualifications You Must Have:**

*   The ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. government issued security clearance is required

**Qualifications We Prefer:**

*   Experience with modern programming languages such as Python and C/C++..
    
*   Experience with implementing best practices of software development on small to medium scale projects.
    
*   Familiar with embedded software development and software/hardware integration.
    
*   Familiar in software development, test, and deployment in languages such as Python and C/C++.
    
*   Familiar with software defined radios (SDRs).
    
*   Familiar with Object Oriented Design/Development and multithreading
    
*   Familiar with Agile/SCRUM, DevOps, and test automation practices.
    
*   Familiar with using configuration management and defect tracking tools (Git, JIRA, etc.).
