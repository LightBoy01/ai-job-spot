**About this opportunity:  
  
**

This master’s thesis provides an opportunity to design and evaluate an intelligent orchestration layer for refining outputs from Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems at Ericsson. You will develop and implement a multi-agent framework to interpret, validate, and explain RAG outputs, focusing on modularity, reusability, and integration with existing Ericsson AI pipelines. The work involves benchmarking improvements in quality, accuracy, and explainability, and collaborating with Ericsson’s research teams.  
  

**What you will do:  
  
**

*   Investigate and compare current RAG systems and orchestration frameworks with respect to their operational characteristics and modular agent design. This includes both theoretical models, empirical data, and commercial products. 
*   Model the orchestration layer in a suitable agent framework, introducing temperature or other relevant parameters if applicable. (Existing RAG models and pipelines will be used as the baseline; your work will add the orchestration layer on top).
*   Simulate and benchmark system behaviour with and without the orchestration layer, using structured evaluation matrices. Optionally, compare with simplified lab set-ups or real-world use cases (e.g., diagnostics, root cause analysis).
*   Integrate and collaborate with Ericsson’s AI and RAG teams to ensure compatibility and operational value.   
      
    

**The skills you bring:  
  
**

*   Currently pursuing a master’s degree in computer science, electrical engineering, or a related field.
*   Good knowledge of AI, multi-agent systems, and orchestration frameworks.
*   Experience with Python and agent frameworks
*   Basic understanding of RAG systems and post-processing in AI pipelines.
