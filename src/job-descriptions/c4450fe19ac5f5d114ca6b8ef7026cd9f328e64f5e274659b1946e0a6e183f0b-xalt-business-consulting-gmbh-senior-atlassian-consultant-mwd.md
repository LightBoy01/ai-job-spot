### Description
Are you as enthusiastic about **Atlassian tools** like **Jira, Confluence, Goals, and Rovo** as we are? Do you possess excellent teamwork skills and shine in communication? Then let's collaborate, either remotely or from our offices in Munich or Leipzig, and become a **Senior Atlassian Consultant (m/f/d)** as part of our team for innovative and exciting projects! You will support our clients in implementing Atlassian tools, designing Confluence intranets, and developing Jira workflows. Become our ambassador for Atlassian services and help us not just sell products, but find the best possible solution – one that we would use ourselves.

### Responsibilities
You will support our clients in various areas such as agile collaboration, ITSM, service management, etc., depending on your strengths.
- You will advise our clients on the selection and integration of suitable Atlassian products into their existing infrastructures.
- You will analyze our clients' requirements and business processes and identify opportunities for leveraging Atlassian tools.
- You will develop collaborative intranets based on Confluence.
- You will design Jira workflows to digitalize business processes.
- You will lead projects and coordinate our internal development team to extend the functionalities of Atlassian tools through apps and macros.

### Qualifications
- You have successfully completed a university degree in (Business) Informatics, Business Administration, or a similar field, or possess equivalent training.
- You have extensive practical experience in applying Atlassian products such as Jira and Confluence.
- You have successfully executed projects with clients in German-speaking regions and strive to understand and continuously optimize our clients' processes and systems. You place great importance on service orientation and client satisfaction.
- You value high quality, are dedicated, and work independently.
- Thanks to your strong communication skills and team orientation, you significantly enrich our team.
- You possess excellent German and English language skills.
- You are interested in an Atlassian certification or already hold existing certificates that you wish to further develop with our support.
- You have extensive practical experience in administering Jira, Confluence, or other Atlassian tools like Bitbucket and Bamboo.
- You have experience in independently managing projects and in project management within an IT environment.
