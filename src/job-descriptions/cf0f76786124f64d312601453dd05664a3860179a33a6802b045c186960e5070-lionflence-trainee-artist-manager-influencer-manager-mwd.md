### Description\nLionflence, a young and successful company specializing in online marketing, artist management, brand building, and proprietary software development, is seeking a motivated individual to join their team. With main offices in Mülheim an der Ruhr and Berlin, Lionflence offers a comprehensive 360° service package that goes beyond typical marketing agency offerings.\n\nThis exciting and challenging role in Artist Management focuses on Media/Social Media, Influencer Marketing, and related technologies. You will be integrated into a highly motivated and dedicated team, benefiting from flat hierarchies and flexible structures that encourage independent work and active participation. We prioritize a healthy work-life balance, offering 30 days of vacation, and foster a culture of open communication, constructive feedback, and fun at work. You'll have the opportunity to actively shape your work and contribute to both professional and cultural topics.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs an integral part of our Artist Management team, your tasks will include:\n- Supporting the coordination of our internal talents.\n- Assisting in the search for new cooperation partners.\n- Learning how to prepare requests and offers.\n- Gaining comprehensive knowledge of market prices and negotiation strategies, with initial opportunities to demonstrate your negotiation skills (in consultation), including with providers and service partners.\n- Learning about contract drafting in this industry, identifying common pitfalls, and beginning to review initial contracts.\n- Supporting Artist Managers in developing concepts and content formats for talents and assisting in their realization.\n- Acting as an interface between talents and our partners, planning their engagements, and ensuring their well-being.\n- Identifying and acquiring potential new cooperation partners and future formats and broadcasts.\n- In your first year, you will take on a supportive role, and depending on your development, you will be gradually introduced to more responsibilities.\n\n### Qualifications\nTo be successful in this role, you should bring:\n- A minimum of an internship, working student position, and/or training in Influencer Marketing or Online Marketing, e.g., in a digital, social media, or PR agency.\n- Interest and a keen sense for the latest digital and consumer trends.\n- Proficient use of all common social media networks and online communities.\n- Ideally, experience in the implementation and/or conceptualization of social media and influencer marketing campaigns.\n- High creativity and motivation.\n- Fluent German and English language skills, both written and spoken.\n- A strong interest in supporting artists and a passion for organization.\n- An open-minded, communicative, and team-oriented personality with strong interpersonal skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding entry-level opportunity for an ambitious individual passionate about influencer marketing and artist management. Lionflence offers a highly supportive environment focused on learning and development, where you'll be actively mentored and progressively entrusted with more responsibility. The strong emphasis on flat hierarchies, team collaboration, 30 days of vacation, and proprietary software highlights a company that values both professional growth and employee well-being. If you're a proactive team player eager to make a tangible impact in a dynamic industry, this role provides an excellent foundation to kickstart your career.
