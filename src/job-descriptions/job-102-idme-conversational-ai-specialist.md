As the Conversational AI Specialist at ID.me, you will be responsible for designing, managing, and advancing our chatbot ecosystem. This role blends technical execution with conversational design to deliver seamless, scalable support experiences for our 140 million+ users. You will lead chatbot flow development, own performance metrics, and prototype new use cases to expand automation across the business.
This is a fully onsite position based at one of our headquarters in McLean, VA, Tampa, FL, or Mountain View, CA.
### Responsibilities
- Manage chatbot initiatives end-to-end, including flow design, testing, and iteration.
- Enhance chatbot performance by improving resolution rates and streamlining escalations.
- Track, analyze, and report on chatbot KPIs (e.g., containment rate, resolution rate) to drive continuous improvements.
- Prototype and evaluate new use cases to expand chatbot automation.
- Collaborate cross-functionally with Support, Product, and Engineering teams.
### Qualifications
- Bachelor’s degree in Business, Communications, Computer Science, or a related field.
- 1+ years of professional experience with chatbot platforms, conversational AI, or automation workflows.
- 1+ years of experience using analytics to track KPIs and drive improvements.
- Strong project management skills and the ability to thrive in a fast-paced, onsite team environment.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
- **Preferred:** Experience with LLM prompt engineering, RAG, and/or basic JavaScript.
