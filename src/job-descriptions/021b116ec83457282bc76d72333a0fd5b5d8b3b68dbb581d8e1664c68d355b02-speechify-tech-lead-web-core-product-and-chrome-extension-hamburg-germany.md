### Description
Speechify's mission is to ensure reading is never a barrier to learning. Over 50 million people utilize Speechify's text-to-speech products across iOS, Android, Mac, Chrome Extension, and Web apps, transforming text into audio for faster reading and better retention. Recognized by Google as \"Chrome Extension of the Year\" and Apple as \"App of the Day,\" Speechify is a leader in its field.

With nearly 200 distributed team members globally, Speechify operates 100% remotely, fostering a flat organization where technical excellence and consistent results can lead to leadership. This is a key role for a strategic individual passionate about product decisions, thriving in fast-paced environments, and experienced in building delightful user experiences. The company values work ethic, solid communication, and an obsession with winning. The interview process is designed for completion within one week.

### Responsibilities
- Actively ship production code to Speechify's web products.
- Work closely and collaboratively with a dedicated product team.
- Participate in product discussions to strategically shape the product roadmap.
- Contribute to new and exciting features that will positively impact millions of lives.

### Qualifications
- Proven experience in building and shipping products that have successfully scaled to thousands or millions of users.
- A strong sense of customer obsession, with a passion for the field and a desire to build high-quality products that genuinely serve customer needs.
- Ability to work with speed, quickly generating ideas and making decisive calls on what to ship immediately versus what requires more time.
- Demonstrated focus in a high-growth startup environment, knowing when to engage with the busy remote team and when to work heads-down.
- Excellent collaboration skills, capable of inspiring, playing, and negotiating effectively with opinionated designers, marketers, and Product Managers.
- **Tech Stack:** Proficiency in Vanilla JS, ReactJS, Redux, Firebase, and Typescript.
