### Description

Shape a brighter financial future with us. At SoFi, we’re a next-generation financial services company and a national bank, using innovative, mobile-first technology to help our millions of members reach their financial goals. The industry is transforming, and we are at the forefront, proud that our work directly impacts people’s lives.

We are seeking a Staff Software Engineer to join our team in building next-generation applications that empower our front-line and back-office agents. With rapid advancements in AI, we are integrating automation and intelligent assistance into agent-facing tools to improve workflows, reduce friction, and elevate the member experience.

As a Staff Software Engineer, you will play a critical role in designing, developing, and deploying these applications. You’ll work across the stack with a strong emphasis on back-end development, delivering reliable, scalable, and high-performance services and APIs. You will collaborate closely with Product, Design, and Engineering partners to bring ideas from concept to production, all while mentoring junior engineers and helping to grow a culture of technical excellence.

### Responsibilities

*   Build and maintain modern applications using Java/Kotlin, Spring, relational databases, Kafka, React, and JavaScript/TypeScript.
*   Participate in architecture discussions, code reviews, and system design to drive high-quality, scalable solutions.
*   Deliver highly available and scalable services in a production environment.
*   Apply operational excellence best practices, including monitoring, alerting, and troubleshooting to ensure system reliability.
*   Experiment with and implement AI-driven features, such as context-aware responses and intelligent data retrieval.
*   Mentor other engineers, support the technical culture, and help grow the team.
*   Generate ideas for new initiatives and technologies to push our platform forward.
*   Communicate and collaborate effectively with project leads, product managers, and other software developers.

### Qualifications

*   Bachelor’s Degree in a technical field or equivalent relevant work experience.
*   8+ years of professional programming experience, ideally on a modern stack.
*   6+ years of strong experience with Java or Kotlin, including a solid understanding of relational databases and ORMs.
*   A willingness to roll up your sleeves, collaborate with others, and get stuff done.
*   Strong sense of responsibility and experience driving projects from inception to completion.
*   A pragmatic affinity for solving problems and shipping impactful features over polishing perfect code.
*   Experience working in a collaborative coding environment (specifically Git), refining designs, and managing pull requests.
*   Demonstrated experience mentoring junior developers.
*   Understanding of operational excellence and familiarity with monitoring tools such as DataDog.
*   An understanding of AI terminology is a plus.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "This role focuses on internal tools for agents. How does this work directly connect to SoFi's primary mission of helping members achieve financial independence?"**

That's a great question because it gets right to the heart of our strategy. Think of our support agents as the human connection to our digital-first platform. As we scale to millions more members, we can't just hire more people—we have to make each agent exponentially more effective. The AI-powered tools you'll be building aren't just about saving a few seconds on a call. They're about arming our agents with the right information and automated workflows at the exact right moment, turning a potentially stressful financial conversation for a member into a seamless, positive resolution. When our agents can solve complex problems faster and more accurately, our members build deeper trust in us. You’re not just optimizing a workflow; you're building the engine that allows us to deliver high-touch, empathetic service at an unprecedented scale, which is absolutely critical to our mission.

**Another great question would be: "The description mentions 'a culture of technical excellence.' What does that actually look like day-to-day, especially when balancing new feature development with the reliability required of a national bank?"**

That balance is the key challenge and what makes this role so interesting. 'Technical excellence' here isn't about gold-plating everything. It's about pragmatic, intelligent design. Day-to-day, this means our senior engineers are constantly leading discussions about trade-offs. For example, we might choose a simpler, more robust architecture for a critical service that handles member data, even if it's less 'cutting-edge,' because reliability is non-negotiable. But for a new AI-driven suggestion feature, we encourage rapid experimentation and iteration. You’ll be in design reviews where we debate whether a new service needs to be 99.999% available or if 99.9% is acceptable to ship faster and learn from real user feedback. It’s a culture of deep ownership where you're trusted to make informed decisions that align with both our product goals and our responsibilities as a financial institution.
