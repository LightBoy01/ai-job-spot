The **Senior Director, PMO & IT Strategy** leads enterprise-wide program management, strategic planning, and transformation initiatives across the **Global Business Systems & Technology** organization. This role ensures IT and Enterprise Applications investments deliver measurable business outcomes, accelerate automation and AI-driven initiatives, and support secure, scalable operations.

Reporting to the CIO, this leader partners closely with Finance, GTM, and cross-functional executives to shape the technology roadmap, oversee a complex portfolio of enterprise applications and transformation programs, and build a high-performance PMO practice with strong governance, delivery frameworks, and KPI-driven scorecards.

**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------

### **Strategic Leadership**

*   Serve as a key advisor to the CIO on **technology strategy, enterprise applications, and finance transformation initiatives** (e.g., AI-driven automation, Account Hierarchy transformation, and Salesforce CPQ, Zuora, NetSuite programs).
*   Lead **annual and multi-year IT & Enterprise Apps roadmap development**, intake, prioritization, and budget planning cycles in alignment with corporate OKRs.
*   Drive **portfolio scorecards and executive dashboards** to track ROI, delivery performance, and strategic alignment.
*   Identify opportunities to leverage **AI, automation, and process optimization** to accelerate business value and operational scalability.

### **Program Management Office (PMO)**

*   **Scale and lead a 7-member PMO team** of Project Managers, Scrum Masters, Technical Writers, and Analysts responsible for **70+ projects annually** across EA & PMO portfolios.
*   Build and operationalize **PMO Services Maturity frameworks**, including governance playbooks, portfolio KPIs, Jira/Confluence templates, and standardized UAT processes.
*   Oversee **cross-functional transformation programs**—from **AI-enabled modernization** to **Account Hierarchy UAT**—ensuring alignment with strategic priorities, budget, and delivery timelines.
*   Introduce and refine **agile delivery practices** and **phase-gate governance** that balance flexibility, control, and business stakeholder engagement.

### **Operational Excellence & Governance**

*   Implement **intake-to-delivery investment governance**, partnering with Finance and Enterprise Architecture to ensure resource and budget optimization.
*   Champion **risk management, change control, compliance, and audit-readiness** across enterprise technology programs.
*   Build **KPI-driven dashboards and scorecards** to monitor portfolio health, escalate risks early, and highlight value realization to executive stakeholders.

### **Leadership & Team Development**

*   Mentor, coach, and expand a **high-impact PMO team**, fostering a culture of **accountability, transparency, and continuous improvement**.
*   Establish **cross-functional steering committees and executive reporting cadences**, ensuring leadership has clear visibility into **progress, risks, dependencies, and business impact**.
*   Lead **UAT planning and execution frameworks**, including **multi-week onsite/virtual UAT cycles**, charters, communication plans, and tester enablement for major releases.

**Background**
--------------

**Required:**

*   10+ years of experience in **Enterprise Applications, IT Strategy, or Program Management**, including at least 5 years in senior leadership.
*   Proven track record of leading **enterprise-level application transformation programs** (Salesforce, CPQ, Zuora, NetSuite, AI).
*   Strong expertise in **portfolio governance, KPI dashboards, Jira/Confluence automation, and executive stakeholder engagement.**
*   Demonstrated success bridging **business and technology strategy** to drive operational excellence and measurable outcomes.
*   Proficiency in **UAT orchestration** and cross-functional delivery in hybrid onsite/remote environments.
*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Business Administration, or related field.

**Preferred:**

*   MBA or advanced degree in Business or Technology.
*   Certifications such as **PMP, PMI-PMOCP, ITIL, or Agile** credentials.
