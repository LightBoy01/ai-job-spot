### Description
For our partner company in Munich, we are looking for a **Project Manager Finance (m/w/d)** to join their team as soon as possible. In this pivotal role, you will be responsible for independently managing and coordinating financial projects, ensuring their timely and successful implementation. This position is based in the heart of Munich, offering an excellent opportunity to contribute to the company's financial strategy and operational efficiency.

### Responsibilities
*   Independently manage and coordinate financial projects, ensuring their punctual and successful implementation.
*   Create, monitor, and analyze budgets and forecasts to support the company's financial planning and control.
*   Evaluate financial key performance indicators (KPIs), identify optimization potential, and derive well-founded recommendations for management.
*   Ensure compliance with reporting, regulatory, and internal compliance requirements, contributing to the transparency and security of financial processes.
*   Act as a key interface between the finance department and other specialist departments, coordinating alignments and fostering efficient collaboration.

### Qualifications
*   A completed degree in Business Administration, Finance, Controlling, or a comparable qualification (e.g., certified tax clerk).
*   Proven experience in the finance sector and in project management (e.g., as a Project Manager, Project Leader, Financial Accountant, or similar position).
*   Solid knowledge in budgeting, forecasting, controlling, and financial analysis, coupled with proficiency in standard MS Office programs.
*   A structured, analytical, and solution-oriented working style, with the ability to maintain an overview in complex projects.
*   Very good German language skills.
