**Job Description**
-------------------

**Domain: Insurance**

**Role Overview:**  
The Web AI Engineer will be responsible for designing and implementing intelligent web based solutions that enhance digital capabilities across insurance platforms. This role requires strong expertise in artificial intelligence machine learning and full stack web development The engineer will contribute to automation personalization and predictive analytics for policy servicing claims management and customer engagement

**Key Responsibilities:**  
Develop and deploy AI powered web applications for insurance workflows including underwriting claims processing and customer servicing  
Implement machine learning models for risk assessment fraud detection and customer behavior analysis  
Integrate AI services with web platforms using APIs and microservices architecture  
Build responsive and scalable frontend interfaces using modern frameworks  
Design and maintain backend systems that support real time data processing and model inference  
Optimize performance reliability and scalability of AI enabled web components  
Ensure compliance with insurance regulatory standards and data privacy protocols  
Collaborate with data scientists architects and developers to align AI solutions with business objectives

**Required Skills:**  
Proficiency in Python JavaScript and frameworks such as React Angular or Vue  
Experience with machine learning libraries including TensorFlow PyTorch or Scikitlearn  
Strong understanding of RESTful APIs cloud services and containerization technologies  
Familiarity with insurance data models and domain specific use cases  
Ability to implement AI algorithms for classification regression clustering and recommendation systems  
Exposure to NLP techniques for document processing and chatbot development  
Experience with data pipelines model deployment and monitoring tools  
Knowledge of security and compliance standards relevant to insurance platforms

**Preferred Qualifications:**  
Background in AI applications for life health or general insurance products  
Certification in machine learning AI engineering or cloud architecture  
Experience with personalization engines and predictive analytics in insurance  
Familiarity with headless CMS and APIfirst design principles

**Work Environment:**  
Agile and innovation driven setting focused on intelligent insurance solutions  
Opportunity to lead AI initiatives and contribute to digital transformation programs  
Emphasis on technical excellence scalability and domain relevance
