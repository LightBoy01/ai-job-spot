### Description\nStudierenden Kunstmarkt is the largest platform for young art in Germany, working closely with art students and young artists to redefine the art market as transparent, fair, and digital. We are a fast-growing startup seeking a motivated individual to support our young team in **Finance & Controlling**. This role offers a unique opportunity to actively shape processes, think innovatively, and contribute to the success of a dynamic company in the art world.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Process daily financial and payment transactions.\n- Prepare and review bank transfers.\n- Issue invoices (for customers & artists).\n- Create offers.\n- Support in **Controlling** (key figures, reports, analyses).\n- Monitor incoming payments.\n- Serve as a contact person for our artists regarding inquiries.\n- Participate in **automations** & process optimizations.\n- Actively contribute ideas, suggest, and implement improvements.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Enrolled Bachelor's or Master's student in **Business Administration**, **Finance**, **Controlling**, **Accounting**, or a similar field.\n- Structured and reliable working style.\n- Eagerness to learn new things and delve into topics.\n- Enjoy taking responsibility and working independently.\n- Passion for numbers, processes, and team collaboration.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for a student passionate about finance, processes, and the vibrant art world. You'll gain invaluable hands-on experience in a rapidly scaling startup, where your ideas are not only welcomed but actively sought out. The company fosters a supportive and creative environment with a strong emphasis on learning and growth, offering real responsibilities and opportunities to contribute to automation and process improvements. With a flexible work model combining in-office collaboration in Leipzig and occasional remote work, this role truly provides a compelling blend of professional development and an engaging company culture.
