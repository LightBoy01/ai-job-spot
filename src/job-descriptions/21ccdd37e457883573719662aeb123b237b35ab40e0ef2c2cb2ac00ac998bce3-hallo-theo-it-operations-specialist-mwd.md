### Description\nAs an IT Operations Specialist (m/f/d), you will be responsible for the smooth operation of our internal IT systems. This includes everything from setting up and managing workstations, providing technical first-level support, implementing security guidelines, and automating processes, to collaborating with external partners.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Management and setup of workstations via our MDM system, as well as Microsoft 365 and Okta.\n- Technical First-Level Support (ticket system, remote access) and execution of On- and Offboardings in close coordination with HR.\n- Implementation and compliance with IT security guidelines such as encryption, patch management, and access controls.\n- Automation and optimization of IT processes, including integration of existing systems.\n- Documentation and inventory management of the IT infrastructure, and close collaboration with external IT partners.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed vocational training or university degree in IT or a comparable qualification.\n- Profound knowledge in cloud environments (Microsoft 365, Azure AD / Okta) and Mobile Device Management.\n- Experience in Windows/macOS administration, as well as in software distribution, patch management, and endpoint security.\n- Structured, independent work style with a high service orientation.\n- Very good German and good English language skills, and motivation to actively shape our IT department.\n- We welcome your application even if you don't (yet) meet every single point in the profile, provided you're eager to grow and develop with us.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role offers a significant opportunity for an IT Operations Specialist to have a direct impact within a dynamic, growing company. You'll enjoy considerable autonomy and a flat hierarchy, encouraging you to actively shape the IT department. The company emphasizes continuous development and offers a competitive salary along with extensive opportunities for professional growth, making it an ideal environment for someone looking to evolve their career in a supportive and impactful setting.
