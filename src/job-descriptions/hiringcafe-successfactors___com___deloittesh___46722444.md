**Your work profile.**

**Role Description:**

Data Scientist with strong expertise in Data Science, Statistics, Generative AI (GenAI), and Traditional Machine Learning. The ideal candidate will be responsible for designing, developing, and deploying data-driven solutions that deliver business impact. This role requires a blend of analytical thinking, statistical rigor, and practical machine learning experience, along with the ability to apply emerging GenAI techniques to real-world problems.

**Key Job Responsibilities**

*   Develop, train, and optimize Machine Learning and Deep Learning models for predictive and prescriptive analytics.
*   Apply advanced statistical techniques for hypothesis testing, feature engineering, and experimental design.
*   Design and implement Generative AI solutions (RAG pipelines, LLM fine-tuning, embeddings, prompt engineering) to solve domain-specific challenges.
*   Work with structured and unstructured data (text, images, etc.) to derive actionable insights.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams (engineering, product, business) to integrate models into scalable systems.
*   Automate data workflows, improve pipelines, and ensure high-quality data for modeling.
*   Communicate findings effectively through dashboards, reports, and presentations.

**Desired qualifications**

*   Strong foundation in **Statistics, Probability, and Linear Algebra**.
*   Hands-on experience with **Python**, and ML libraries such as **scikit-learn, Langchain, and Langgraph**.
*   Practical knowledge of **Generative AI** (LLMs, LangChain, Hugging Face, vector databases, prompt engineering).
*   Expertise in **Traditional ML algorithms** (Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forest, XGBoost, Clustering, NLP methods).
*   Experience in working with large datasets, SQL/NoSQL databases, and data preprocessing.
*   Familiarity with **cloud platforms (Azure)** and deployment frameworks (Docker, Kubernetes, FastAPI, Flask).
*   Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and communication skills.

**Way of working**
