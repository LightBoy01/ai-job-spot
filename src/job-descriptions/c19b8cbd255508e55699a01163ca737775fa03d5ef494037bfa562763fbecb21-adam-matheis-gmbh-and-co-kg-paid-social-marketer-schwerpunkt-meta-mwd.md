### Description
Schlafgut is evolving from the invention of the fitted sheet towards the sleep culture of tomorrow. We represent a family-owned, medium-sized business with 100 years of tradition, combined with a startup culture and ambitious goals. We aim to continue our successful company history and transform our well-known textile brand, recognized in both stationary and online retail, into the world's first and most successful holistic sleep brand. For this exciting journey, we are looking for you to join our **new office in Munich!**

### Responsibilities
*   Create and manage Paid Social Ads (structure, budgets, bidding, audiences, creatives).
*   Develop and implement growth strategies for brand awareness and customer acquisition (prospecting, retargeting, offer tests).
*   Practice Growth Hacking: rapidly test, learn, and iterate from hooks to landing page angles.
*   Develop and design creatives in collaboration with our Brand Team.
*   Analyze marketing KPIs and adjust strategies based on results.
*   Work closely with internal and external teams (Brand Marketing, Graphics, Agencies, etc.).

### Qualifications
*   Entrepreneurial-minded Paid Social Marketing Specialist with a hands-on mentality.
*   Sales experience in e-commerce; drive and proven cases are more important to us than years of experience.
*   Experience in managing service providers and agencies.
*   Strong analytical skills and experience with marketing analysis tools (Triple Whale or similar), GA4 is a plus.
*   Innovative thinking and motivation to test and implement new strategies and technologies.
*   Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal, in German and English.
*   Enthusiasm for AI, technologies, and tools, and their application in daily business.
