**Job Title**

Data Scientist 3

**Summary of the Role**
-----------------------

We’re looking for a Data Scientist to help transform data into actionable insights that support decision-making across the business. You’ll apply data science techniques such as machine learning, artificial intelligence, and statistical modeling to solve real-world challenges and contribute to shaping the future of travel.

**In This Role You’ll**
-----------------------

*   Use data science techniques—including machine learning, artificial intelligence, and statistical modeling—to identify and quantify business opportunities.
*   Collect, manage, and analyze large datasets to uncover patterns and generate insights.
*   Collaborate with data engineering teams to implement scalable data analysis algorithms.
*   Communicate complex findings and their implications clearly to business stakeholders.
*   Identify and integrate internal and external data sources to support fact-based decision-making.

**About the Ideal Candidate**
-----------------------------

*   6+ years of experience applying data science techniques such as machine learning, artificial intelligence, or high-performance computing.
    
*   Proficiency in managing and analyzing large, complex datasets using modern tools and techniques.
    
*   Ability to translate technical results into clear, actionable insights for non-technical audiences.
    
*   Familiarity with collaborative environments and contributing to communities of practice.
    
*   Comfortable working with moderate guidance and contributing to process improvements.
