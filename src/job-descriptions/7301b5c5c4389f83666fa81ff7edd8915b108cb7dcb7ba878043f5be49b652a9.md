**Your role**

*   Lead the planning, execution, and governance of AI-driven transformation projects within the Finance unit.
    
*   Oversee project scope, budget, timeline, and resource allocation, ensuring alignment with company and regulatory standards.
    
*   Establish and manage the PMO framework, ensuring structured project tracking, reporting, and risk mitigation.
    
*   Drive coordination among Finance, IT, and business stakeholders for smooth integration of AI solutions into legacy systems and manual processes.
    
*   Collaborate with Finance leadership, IT, and external vendors to align transformation initiatives with business needs.
    
*   Design, implement, and monitor change management activities to support adoption, including stakeholder engagement, communication planning, and training strategies.
    
*   Develop project documentation, risk assessments, and reporting mechanisms tailored to a highly regulated, listed company environment.
    
*   Anticipate and resolve project roadblocks, especially those arising from complex approval processes, legacy systems, and entrenched policy landscapes.
    
*   Drive change management strategies to overcome resistance and ensure smooth adoption of new processes and technologies.
    

**To succeed in your role**

*   Bachelor’s degree or above in Business Administration, Information Systems, Finance, or related disciplines.
    
*   Minimum 7 years of experience in project management, with at least 3 years leading large-scale transformation or technology-driven projects for Finance in sizeable or listed corporations.
    
*   Expertise in change management and business process re-engineering (BPR) to modernize legacy systems and workflows.
    
*   Proven track record in change management, stakeholder alignment, and conducting business process re-engineering within regulated organizations.
    
*   Demonstrated experience integrating AI or digital technologies into legacy operations is highly advantageous.
    
*   Familiarity with AI/automation in Finance (e.g., robotic process automation, predictive analytics) is highly desirable.
    
*   Strong understanding of Finance operations, compliance, and regulatory environments in Hong Kong.
    
*   Resilient leader capable of navigating complex approval processes and legacy mindsets.
    
*   Excellent organizational, leadership, and interpersonal skills, with fluency in English/Cantonese; Mandarin advantageous.
