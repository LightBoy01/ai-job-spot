You will define AI/ML architectures, lead marquee customer engagements, and evangelize cutting-edge solutions in Generative AI (GenAI), Large Language Models (LLMs), AI Agents, and Responsible AI frameworks. The role demands a unique balance of strategic vision, hands-on technical delivery, and market-facing thought leadership.

Core Competencies

*   Enterprise Architecture – Defining AI-enabled enterprise architectures that scale across industries.
*   Generative AI & Agents – Proven expertise in LLMs, multi-modal AI, AI agent orchestration, and prompt/flow engineering.
*   Cloud & Multi-cloud Strategy – Expert in Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI); experience with AWS, Azure, GCP highly valued.
*   AI/ML Engineering & MLOps – Strong grounding in ML frameworks (LangChain, HuggingFace, PyTorch, TensorFlow), CI/CD, observability, and governance.
*   AI Security & Responsible AI – Knowledge of data privacy, fairness, explainability, model risk & compliance frameworks.
*   Executive Engagement – Ability to communicate AI vision and ROI at board/executive level, while guiding technical teams on design/delivery.
*   Innovation & GTM – Strong orientation towards IP reuse, accelerators, and GTM initiatives (proposals, bid defense, executive workshops).

Qualifications & Experience

*   16–20 years in technology, with 8+ years in AI/ML, Data Science, or Advanced Analytics.
*   Demonstrated track record in enterprise-scale AI/ML adoption—from discovery to production rollout.
*   Proven hands-on expertise in GenAI, LLM fine-tuning, multi-modal AI, and intelligent agents.
*   Deep experience with cloud-native deployments, vector databases, RAG architectures, and hybrid data pipelines.
*   Strong expertise in MLOps, governance, and lifecycle management of AI models.
*   Experience in executive-facing roles (CxO briefings, board-level AI strategy sessions).
*   Bachelor’s/Master’s in Computer Science, Data Science, AI, or equivalent. PhD preferred but not mandatory.

Contributions to AI thought leadership: patents, whitepapers, conference talks, or AI community initiatives.

### Responsibilities

Key Responsibilities

*   Enterprise AI Leadership  
    Drive enterprise-wide AI/ML architecture strategy, ensuring alignment with business outcomes, data strategy, security, and compliance.
*   Solution Design & Delivery  
    Architect, prototype, and deliver production-grade AI systems across GenAI, LLMs, multi-modal AI, RAG pipelines, and intelligent agents.
*   CxO Engagement  
    Act as a trusted advisor to CIOs/CTOs/CDOs on AI adoption strategy, cloud-native transformation, and scaling AI beyond PoCs into production.
*   Hands-on Technical Authority  
    Remain deeply hands-on in architecting and integrating AI into enterprise landscapes (Fusion, EBS, custom apps, cloud-native stacks).
*   Governance & Standards  
    Establish AI architecture blueprints, reusable patterns, and governance frameworks (LLMOps, model risk management, AI security, EU AI Act compliance).
*   Thought Leadership  
    Represent the practice in customer-facing demos, conferences, and whitepapers; influence Oracle’s AI GTM and innovation roadmap.
*   Cross-Studio Collaboration  
    Partner with Cloud, Data & Analytics, and Security Studios to deliver end-to-end enterprise transformation solutions.
*   Market & Innovation Focus  
    Track global AI trends, vendor ecosystems (OpenAI, Cohere, Anthropic, HuggingFace), and continuously infuse market-leading innovation into delivery.
