### Description

We are seeking a Senior Performance Marketing Specialist to join our dynamic Performance Marketing team at 8am. This is a key, hands-on role focused on scaling our paid media programs, with a strong emphasis on direct media buying, managing industry partner sponsorships, and programmatic advertising. You will also contribute to a broader range of performance channels, including paid search, paid social, pay-per-lead, and affiliate marketing.

This position is ideal for a professional who thrives in B2B tech and SaaS environments, understands complex buyer journeys, and excels at crafting and optimizing campaigns that deliver measurable business impact. You will work closely with cross-functional teams—including content, product marketing, analytics, and sales—to ensure our campaigns reach the right audience with the right message at the right time.

At 8am, our vision is to power a world where professionals thrive. Founded in 2005, we provide a purpose-built business platform that helps over 250,000 legal, accounting, and other client-focused professionals simplify operations, ensure compliance, and fuel profitable growth. Recognized for 13 consecutive years on the Inc. 5000 list of fastest-growing companies, our team continues to expand as we pursue our mission.

### Responsibilities

*   Manage sponsorship agreements with key industry partners to maximize brand visibility, engagement, and lead generation.
*   Plan, execute, and optimize programmatic advertising campaigns across display, video, and native formats.
*   Support paid search, paid social, affiliate, and other performance marketing programs to drive MQL/SQL generation and brand-building efforts.
*   Collaborate with creative and content teams to develop compelling ad creative and landing pages optimized for conversions.
*   Monitor performance metrics, analyze campaign data, and provide actionable insights to improve ROI and CPA.
*   Manage budgets effectively, ensuring spend is optimized and forecasted accurately.
*   Stay ahead of industry trends, new platforms, and competitive activity to identify new growth opportunities.
*   Partner with analytics teams to develop reporting dashboards and track performance against KPIs.

### Qualifications

**Required Qualifications:**

*   5+ years of experience in performance marketing, with at least 2+ years in direct media buying and programmatic advertising.
*   Proven track record managing paid media campaigns in a B2B tech or SaaS company.
*   Strong understanding of the full-funnel buyer journey and how to tailor media strategies for awareness, consideration, and conversion.
*   Hands-on experience with direct buys with industry media partners and programmatic platforms (e.g., DV360, The Trade Desk).
*   Strong analytical skills, comfortable using multi-touch attribution insights to understand campaign performance.
*   Excellent negotiation, project management, and organizational skills.
*   Familiarity with marketing automation tools (e.g., Marketo) and CRM systems (Salesforce preferred).
*   Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, high-growth, cross-functional environment.
*   Demonstrated experience leveraging AI tools to improve workflows or enhance decision-making.

**Preferred Qualifications:**

*   Experience in the legal tech or professional services vertical.
*   Familiarity with additional marketing tools such as Qualified, Userled, or Clay.
*   A strong plus for hands-on experience with paid search (Google Ads, Microsoft Ads) and paid social (LinkedIn Ads, Meta Ads, Reddit Ads).
*   Knowledge of SEO/AEO best practices to complement paid media efforts.
*   Based in Austin, Texas.
