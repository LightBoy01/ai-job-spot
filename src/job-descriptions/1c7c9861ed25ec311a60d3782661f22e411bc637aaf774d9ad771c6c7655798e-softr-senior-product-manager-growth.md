### Description\nSoftr is a fast-growing, no-code AI-powered platform empowering over 1 million teams globally to build business apps like internal tools and client portals using their existing data sources. They are a fully remote, distributed, and diverse team focused on high impact and innovation, backed by top-tier investors.\n\nThey are seeking a deeply user-obsessed Product Manager (Growth) to shape the future of no-code. This role is for a builder and a closer who thrives in high-autonomy, high-impact environments, taking ownership of the end-to-end product lifecycle from discovery to iteration, and collaborating closely with engineering, design, and customer-facing teams.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Own the end-to-end product lifecycle—from discovery and ideation to launch, adoption, and iteration.\n- Develop a deep understanding of Softr's diverse users (creators, operations teams, small businesses, and startups).\n- Prioritize features that deliver significant impact, carefully balancing customer needs with key business goals.\n- Define clear product specifications and collaborate seamlessly with designers and engineers to bring solutions to life.\n- Analyze user behavior and product metrics rigorously to inform strategic roadmap decisions and continuous improvements.\n- Work cross-functionally to ensure smooth product releases and cohesive messaging across all relevant teams.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 3–6 years of experience in a Product Management role, ideally within a fast-paced tech startup or agency environment.\n- Proven experience in planning and conducting qualitative or quantitative research, such as in-depth interviews or surveys, to deeply understand customer problems.\n- Ability to drive actionable insights and and recommendations for the business and product roadmap based on data analysis.\n- Capable of identifying and prioritizing strategic and tactical product topics that will have the most significant impact on Softr's business and customers.\n- Demonstrated ability to drive customer-driven decisions, clear prioritization, and efficient execution in the face of ambiguity and complexity.\n- Deep expertise in working effectively with go-to-market teams, and especially engineering teams, to deliver substantial value.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an experienced Growth Product Manager to join a rapidly expanding, well-funded no-code platform that's genuinely changing how people build software. Softr offers a fully remote, high-ownership environment with zero bureaucracy, allowing for significant impact and direct collaboration with leadership. The role promises a competitive compensation package, equity, and a culture that values autonomy, growth, and team connection through annual retreats. If you're a builder passionate about empowering users and thrive on tangible results in a fast-paced, lean setting, this role presents a unique chance to shape a product with over a million users and contribute to a mission-critical industry.
