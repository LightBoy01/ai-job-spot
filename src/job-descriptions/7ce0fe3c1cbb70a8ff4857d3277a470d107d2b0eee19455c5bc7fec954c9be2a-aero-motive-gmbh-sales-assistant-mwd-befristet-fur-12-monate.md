### Description\nAero-Motive GmbH, based in Oberhaching near Munich, has been a global leader for over 50 years in producing industrial spring balancers and retractors. We drive efficiency and safety across various industries with our innovative solutions. We are seeking a highly motivated individual to join our team for a strategically important project, supporting key initiatives across sales, marketing, and IT.\n\nThis is a 12-month fixed-term position with the potential for extension, offering a future-oriented work environment with modern equipment and excellent accessibility by public transport and car. Join us in shaping our company's future.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Support in building structured sales processes (including preparation of documents and assistance for customer care).\n- Collaboration in implementing and maintaining the CRM system (data maintenance, process support).\n- Assistance in marketing (creation and maintenance of content, coordination of external service providers).\n- Participation in building and maintaining the website (content management, coordination with agencies).\n- Research and preparation of market and competitor information to support the sales strategy.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed commercial or technical education (e.g., mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, mechatronics, industrial engineering or similar).\n- Professional experience in sales, customer service, or as an application engineer is an advantage.\n- Enjoy customer contact, consulting, and independent work – both online and offline.\n- Technical interest, paired with a structured, communicative approach.\n- Fluent communication in German and English is a given for you.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a proactive and detail-oriented professional to make a significant impact within a globally active and innovative company. You'll contribute directly to strategic projects, gaining broad experience across sales, marketing, and IT. Aero-Motive offers a supportive, modern work environment and values independent contributors eager to shape the company's future. The potential for extension makes this an exciting entry point for a dedicated individual looking to grow their career.
