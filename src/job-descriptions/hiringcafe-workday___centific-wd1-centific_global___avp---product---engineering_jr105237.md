**About Centific**

Centific is a frontier AI data foundry that curates diverse, high-quality data, using our purpose-built technology platforms to empower the Magnificent Seven and our enterprise clients with safe, scalable AI deployment. Our team includes more than 150 PhDs and data scientists, along with more than 4,000 AI practitioners and engineers. We harness the power of an integrated solution ecosystem—comprising industry-leading partnerships and 1.8 million vertical domain experts in more than 230 markets—to create contextual, multilingual, pre-trained datasets; fine-tuned, industry-specific LLMs; and RAG pipelines supported by vector databases. Our zero-distance innovation™ solutions for GenAI can reduce GenAI costs by up to 80% and bring solutions to market 50% faster.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between AI creators and industry leaders by bringing best practices in GenAI to unicorn innovators and enterprise customers. We aim to help these organizations unlock significant business value by deploying GenAI at scale, helping to ensure they stay at the forefront of technological advancement and maintain a competitive edge in their respective markets.

**About Job**

**Role Overview**

We are seeking a proven **product and engineering leader to** **scale OneForma globally**. This leader will own the technical vision, strategy, and execution for a platform that:

*   Processes **millions of expert interactions** across global markets
*   Powers **real-time AI-driven matching** and workflow orchestration
*   Ensures **enterprise-grade security** for the world’s most sensitive AI data
*   Supports **autonomous payments and compliance** across 100+ countries

The ideal candidate has scaled two-sided platforms or SaaS/data ecosystems, knows how to balance **innovation with governance**, and can lead **globally distributed engineering teams** with discipline and impact.

**What You’ll Build**

**Platform Innovation**

*   Architect next-gen matching algorithms that connect AI projects with expert talent in real-time.
*   Build workflow systems that scale expert management from thousands to millions.
*   Create fraud prevention and trust/safety systems that protect billions in AI training investments.
*   Design payment and compliance infrastructure that operates seamlessly across global markets.

**Scale & Execution**

*   Lead multiple engineering and product teams to deliver enterprise-grade platform features with consistency and reliability.
*   Build scalable architectures and processes that support high transaction volume, onboarding, workflow automation, and payments.
*   Ensure operational excellence across release management, QA, uptime, and customer SLAs.
*   Implement metrics, dashboards, and governance to monitor and improve performance at scale.

**Technical Leadership**

*   Drive technical decisions for distributed systems handling massive concurrent workflows.
*   Integrate ML/AI into production platforms for personalization, matching, and quality prediction.
*   Establish engineering culture and practices that attract and retain top-tier talent.
*   Mentor engineering leaders and ICs across globally distributed teams.

**Global Scale & Impact**

*   Partner with Centific’s executive leadership on long-term platform strategy and hypergrowth roadmap.
*   Unlock new markets and enterprise use cases in the rapidly evolving AI economy.
*   Shape operational excellence frameworks that support **10x growth** while maintaining enterprise reliability.

**Qualifications**

*   10+ years of product and engineering experience with increasing leadership responsibilities.
*   5+ years leading multi-team engineering organizations at Senior Director or equivalent level.
*   Proven success scaling two-sided platforms, talent marketplaces, or enterprise SaaS/data ecosystems.
*   Strong technical depth in distributed systems, workflow automation, cloud-native infrastructure (AWS/GCP/Azure).
*   Experience integrating AI/ML into platforms at scale (matching, personalization, fraud detection).
*   Track record scaling globally distributed engineering teams through hypergrowth.
*   Strong focus on **operational excellence**: security, reliability, scalability, and governance.
*   Ability to translate complex technical challenges into clear business impact.

**Why This Role Matters**

You’re not just scaling existing technology — you’re helping define the **infrastructure of the AI economy**. The systems you build will:

*   Enable breakthrough AI models
*   Support millions of expert livelihoods
*   Shape how Fortune 500 enterprises deliver their most strategic AI initiatives

This is your opportunity to:

*   Lead a world-class engineering organization through a period of **explosive growth**
*   Build a platform that becomes **essential infrastructure** for the AI industry
*   Work directly with Centific’s executive leadership to shape **company strategy**
