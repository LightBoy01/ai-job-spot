<p><strong>Tec de Monterrey – School of Engineering and Sciences, Monterrey Campus    </strong></p>



<p><strong>About Tec de Monterrey    </strong></p>



<p>Tec de Monterrey is ranked the No. 1 private university in Mexico and among the top 50 private universities worldwide. We rank in the top 10 in Latin America (QS World University Rankings) and the top 4 in the region (Times Higher Education) and 185 worldwide (QS World University Rankings). We educate over 80,000 students across 31 campuses and 16 international liaison offices, driven by our vision to develop leaders with entrepreneurial spirit, humanistic values, and global competitiveness.  </p>



<p>Disruption is the new normal, and at Tecnológico de Monterrey, every morning we wake up with the responsibility of transforming our surroundings. Here, you’ll find your purpose, seize innovative opportunities, and be part of a collaborative culture based on inclusion, meritocracy, and respect.   </p>



<p><strong>Position Overview   </strong> </p>



<p>We invite applications for a Full-Time Professor in Industrial Engineering (IE) (Assistant, Associate, or Full Professor rank) specializing in Artificial Intelligence (AI) applications. Ideal candidates will apply AI methodologies to optimize supply chain management, logistics, data analytics, or operational excellence (with a focus on Quality). The role requires driving high-impact research, securing competitive funding, and delivering outstanding AI-enhanced curricula at undergraduate and graduate levels.    </p>
