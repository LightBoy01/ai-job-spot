Join the Revolution in AI-Powered Energy Management!

At etalytics, we develop intelligent software that helps industrial companies use energy more efficiently, reduce CO₂ emissions, and cut costs. With cutting-edge IoT, data analytics, and AI technologies, we’re actively shaping the future of sustainable energy systems.

Are you studying computer science or a related field and looking for a hands-on internship where you can work with real infrastructure and modern DevOps tools? Join us for a **3-month full-time internship in DevOps Engineering** with option for working student contract afterwards and help us build reliable, scalable, and automated systems!

Tasks
-----

**Infrastructure Basics (Hands-on):**

Support in setting up and operating applications and Kubernetes environments — both on-prem and in the cloud. You’ll learn GitOps workflows and work with tools such as ArgoCD or Flux.

**Deployment Support:**

Assist our developers in preparing and executing software releases.

**System Monitoring:**

Create Grafana dashboards, work with Prometheus, and help visualize key application and infrastructure metrics.

**Troubleshooting & Reliability:**

Analyze basic issues around logs, performance, and scalability, and support the identification and resolution of root causes.

**Automation:**

Write small scripts, automate recurring tasks, and help improve our CI/CD workflows (GitHub Actions / GitLab CI).

**Collaboration:**

Work closely with our development, QA, and customer success teams to ensure stable and user-friendly services.

Requirements
------------

**Basic Kubernetes Knowledge:**

You have first experience working with Kubernetes from university projects, a previous internship, or your own setups.

**Infrastructure as Code (Basics):**

Some exposure to IaC tools (e.g., ArgoCD, Flux, Helm, or Terraform) is a plus.

**Monitoring Interest:**

You’re eager to learn how to work with Grafana, Prometheus, and observability concepts.

**CI/CD Foundations:**

You’re familiar with modern CI/CD pipelines (e.g., GitHub Actions or GitLab CI).

**Linux & Networking:**

You’re comfortable using Linux and understand basic networking fundamentals.

**Problem-Solving Mindset:**

You approach debugging and troubleshooting methodically and enjoy figuring out how systems behave.
