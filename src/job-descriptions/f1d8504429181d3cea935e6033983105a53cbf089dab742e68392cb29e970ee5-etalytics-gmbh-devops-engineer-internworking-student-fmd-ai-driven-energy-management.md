### Description\nJoin etalytics and be part of the revolution in AI-powered energy management! We develop intelligent software leveraging cutting-edge IoT, data analytics, and AI to help industrial companies achieve greater energy efficiency, reduce CO₂ emissions, and cut costs.\n\nWe are seeking a proactive student in computer science or a related field for a **3-month full-time internship in DevOps Engineering**. This is a hands-on opportunity to work with real infrastructure and modern DevOps tools, with the potential for a working student contract afterward. You will play a crucial role in building reliable, scalable, and automated systems for a sustainable future.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Infrastructure Basics (Hands-on):** Support in setting up and operating applications and Kubernetes environments, both on-prem and in the cloud. Learn GitOps workflows and work with tools such as ArgoCD or Flux.\n- **Deployment Support:** Assist our developers in preparing and executing software releases.\n- **System Monitoring:** Create Grafana dashboards, work with Prometheus, and help visualize key application and infrastructure metrics.\n- **Troubleshooting & Reliability:** Analyze basic issues around logs, performance, and scalability, and support the identification and resolution of root causes.\n- **Automation:** Write small scripts, automate recurring tasks, and help improve our CI/CD workflows (GitHub Actions / GitLab CI).\n- **Collaboration:** Work closely with our development, QA, and customer success teams to ensure stable and user-friendly services.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Basic Kubernetes Knowledge:** First experience working with Kubernetes from university projects, a previous internship, or your own setups.\n- **Infrastructure as Code (Basics):** Some exposure to IaC tools (e.g., ArgoCD, Flux, Helm, or Terraform) is a plus.\n- **Monitoring Interest:** Eagerness to learn how to work with Grafana, Prometheus, and observability concepts.\n- **CI/CD Foundations:** Familiarity with modern CI/CD pipelines (e.g., GitHub Actions or GitLab CI).\n- **Linux & Networking:** Comfortable using Linux and understanding basic networking fundamentals.\n- **Problem-Solving Mindset:** A methodical approach to debugging and troubleshooting, enjoying figuring out how systems behave.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an aspiring DevOps Engineer to gain practical experience in a high-impact, purpose-driven environment. etalytics offers a supportive and collaborative culture with excellent development opportunities and a high level of personal responsibility, even for interns. You'll benefit from hybrid working options, flexible hours, and regular team events within an international team. If you're eager to learn, contribute to sustainable energy solutions, and build a strong foundation in modern DevOps practices, etalytics in Darmstadt is the place for you.
