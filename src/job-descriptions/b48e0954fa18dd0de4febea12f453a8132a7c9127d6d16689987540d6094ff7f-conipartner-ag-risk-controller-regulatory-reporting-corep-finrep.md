### Description

coni + partner, a consulting firm established in 1993, is recruiting on behalf of an international bank located in Zurich. Our client is seeking a dedicated and skilled Accounting Specialist to join their Group Risks department as a **Risk Controller Regulatory Reporting (COREP, FINREP)**. This is a crucial role within a small, specialized team, focusing on the preparation, analysis, and monitoring of regulatory reports to ensure the bank's compliance and financial stability.

### Responsibilities

*   Work as a key member of a small specialist team within Group Risks.
*   Prepare monthly or quarterly Regulatory Reports (COREP and FINREP) for senior management and financial market authorities.
*   Determine and control Capital Requirements in accordance with Basel III, CRR, and CRD.
*   Analyze and assess capital adequacy.
*   Develop and maintain the bank's Recovery Plan.
*   Monitor regulatory developments pertaining to Basel III Pillar 1.
*   Evaluate new regulatory requirements, conduct impact analyses, and contribute to their implementation.
*   Generate statistics based on regulatory provisions.
*   Continuously monitor data quality within systems and optimize COREP and FINREP reporting processes.
*   Drive the further development of systems and lead projects with regulatory content.

### Qualifications

*   A university degree in Quantitative Finance, Economics, or Accounting.
*   Proven professional experience in auditing, consulting, or within a banking environment.
*   Solid knowledge of Basel III Pillar 1 and Regulatory Reporting frameworks.
*   Familiarity with CRD and the ICAAP process, which determines the amount and quality of capital in relation to the risk profile.
*   A strong interest in Risk Management Frameworks and Stress Testing.
*   Keen interest in Regulatory Reporting, the analysis of financial figures, and their presentation.
*   Demonstrated structured, independent, and precise working methodology.
*   Possess an analytical and networked mindset.
*   Exhibit initiative, responsibility, and a team-oriented personality.
*   A natural aptitude for IT applications, coupled with proficiency in MS-Office.
*   Fluent in both German and English.
