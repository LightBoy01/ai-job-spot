Tasks
-----

**Your Mission**

We are looking for you to be the new, experienced **PR Manager (m/f/d)** for our client!

You strengthen **their** reputation, build exciting relationships with the media, and make sure **their** innovative story resonates. You will work closely with **their** Marketing and Branding teams to disseminate **their** message consistently and effectively.

**Your Tasks**

*   You develop a sharp PR strategy that precisely supports **our client's** corporate goals and sets **them** apart from the competition.
*   You maintain and expand your network of tech journalists, industry influencers, and relevant media nationally and internationally.
*   You are responsible for writing compelling press releases, expert articles, sharp Q&As, and placing the expert topics.
*   Together with the Branding team, you ensure the corporate message is clear, consistent, and strong.
*   You are prepared and know how to communicate confidently and quickly in critical situations.
*   You measure the success of your PR activities and know how to report and optimize the results.

**Your Profile**

*   **Minimum of 5 to 7 years** of professional experience in Public Relations, ideally in the Tech industry (B2B Software, AI, SaaS).

*   An excellent network in the media landscape (Tech and Business) is a major advantage.
