**Location 📍:** Sydney, AU, Hybrid - 3 days a week in office

**About Us** 🚀

At Relevance AI, our mission is to empower anyone to delegate work to the AI workforce.

We’re building a new type of automation platform, for anyone to create and use AI agents that can replicate human quality work, decision making and collaboration.

We are scaling fast to meet unprecedented customer demand and we need exceptional talent to help us grow. If you’re ready to be part of a trailblazing company at the cutting edge of AI, this is the place for you.

**The Role 🥳**

We’re hiring for multiple Software Engineers to join our growing team in Sydney. We were named Startup Of The Year by Startup Daily. This is your chance to be a hands-on contributor in a collaborative, high-performance environment where you can grow your career quickly.

At Relevance AI, your work will reach customers within hours, offering a rare opportunity to see the impact of your contributions in real-time. You’ll be supported by tenured teammates and seniors who are still very hands-on. Our rituals include daily stand-ups, regular knowledge-sharing sessions, and a culture that values deliberate, thoughtful scaling. We focus on doing things right, not just fast.

You’ll work across our modern tech stack, which we’re constantly optimising to move and iterate faster. Our supportive, fast-paced culture fosters collaboration, curiosity, and creativity, making it an environment where innovation thrives and where your ideas and expertise will be highly valued.

**Your Impact 💥**

*   Build and deliver impactful features across our stack, contributing to both performant frontend and scalable backend systems, and cloud infrastructure.
    
*   Contribute to innovative AI solutions, helping design and implement new features that improve the customer experience.
    
*   Help shape our engineering culture by sharing ideas, participating in code reviews, and supporting best practices as we grow.
    
*   Uphold high standards for code quality, performance, and scalability, and help ensure these standards are met in your work and through peer reviews.
    
*   Support and learn from your peers by sharing knowledge and offering feedback, fostering a collaborative and growth-oriented environment.
    

**Our Tech Stack (so far) 💻**

We leverage a modern, scalable, and developer-friendly technology stack to build robust web applications:

*   Frontend: Vue.js, Nuxt, Typescript
    
*   Backend: Express.js, Node.js, Typescript
    
*   Database: MongoDB, PostgreSQL
    
*   Cloud & Deployment: Vercel, AWS
    

**What We’re Looking For** 🧠

*   2+ years of hands-on software engineering experience, ideally within fast-paced, high-growth tech environments.
    
*   Expertise in our tech stack or a proven ability to quickly master new technologies.
    
*   Thrive in dynamic, high-performance start up settings and comfortable with ambiguity, rapid iteration, and taking initiative.
    
*   Demonstrated ability to take full ownership of projects, solve complex problems, and consistently deliver high-quality, impactful results.
    
*   Deep interest in artificial intelligence and excitement to build innovative, category-defining products.
    
*   Regularly leverage AI tools and technologies in your workflow, and stay up-to-date with the latest advancements to boost productivity and innovation.
