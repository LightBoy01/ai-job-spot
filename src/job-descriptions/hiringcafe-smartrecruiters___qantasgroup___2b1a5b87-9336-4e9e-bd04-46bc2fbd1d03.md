**Company Description:**

Building on more than 100 years of history and experience, QantasLink is a consolidated network of regional airlines that connects passengers and communities across Australia. QantasLink is made up of four subsidiary organisations – Eastern Airlines, Sunstate Airlines, National Jet Systems and Network Aviation. Operating as an end-to-end airline, QantasLink harnesses the strength of its regional partnerships and a strong sense of family, to provide meaningful career paths for its more than 2,500 people with opportunities that might take you to exciting destinations across QantasLink or the Qantas Group.

**Job Description:**

*   **Play a key role in enhancing our SMS through advanced analytics and AI lead innovation**
*   **Help deliver safety improvements and enhanced performance for QantasLink**
*   **Full-time permanent position based in Mascot, Sydney**

We’ve always been pioneers, and we’re proud to see that spirit in our people today. Our resilience has helped us through good and tough times, and it still drives us to keep pushing the challenging expectations today.

Our Regional businesses are an important part of the Qantas Group so no matter where you fit in we come together with pride to deliver the best and safest experience for our customers.  The success we’ve achieved hasn’t been a matter of luck.  It’s a direct result of our strategic leadership.  This means taking accountability for driving results, being competitive and influencing outcomes.

As **Principal Safety Specialist**, you’ll play a key role in enhancing the effectiveness of our Safety Management System (SMS) through advanced analytics, digital transformation, and AI-enabled innovation.

You’ll act as a bridge between operational safety teams, Group Safety Data Analytics, and leadership, ensuring safety data translates into meaningful, actionable outcomes. This role supports employees across National Jet Systems (NJS), Eastern Australia Airlines (EAA), Sunstate Airlines (SSA), and Alliance QQ ACMI operations.

**What you’ll bring to the role:**

*   5+ years’ experience in airline safety management or technical data analytics.
*   Bachelor’s degree in Aviation Safety, Engineering, Data Analytics or related discipline.
*   Strong understanding of SMS, CASA regulations, ICAO Annex 19, and IATA IOSA.
*   Hands-on experience with Intelex or other SMS software is preferable.
*   Proficiency in Power BI (DAX, data modelling, dashboard creation).
*   Awareness of AI/ML applications and digital transformation in aviation.
*   Excellent communication and stakeholder engagement skills.
*   Ability to interpret complex data and present insights clearly.
