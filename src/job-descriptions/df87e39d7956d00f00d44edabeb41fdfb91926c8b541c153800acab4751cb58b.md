Reporting to the Global Process Owner Source-to-Pay (S2P), the **Requisition to Receipt Global Process Lead** is responsible for leading the global strategy, design, implementation, and continuous improvement of the Procure-to-Pay (P2P) process, specifically focusing on the requisition to receipt phase. In this role, you will ensures efficient and compliant procurement processes typically driving the organisations transactional matching strategy to ensure payment commensurate to goods received.
### **Responsibilities**
**Process Design & Optimization:**
*   Design fit for purpose requisition to receipt processes, ensuring first time match rates and eliminating manual interventions where possible
*   Identify opportunities for process improvements and implement solutions to enhance efficiency and effectiveness
*   Operate to, and promote, standardized and harmonized ways of working to ensure global consistency in the execution of requisition to receipt processes across all global locations
*   Develop and maintain global requisition to receipt policies, procedures, and best practices
**Stakeholder Management & Customer Centricity:**
*   Lead cross-functional teams to improve requisition to receipt efficiency and effectiveness
*   Collaborate effectively with stakeholders across various departments and geographical locations to gather requirements, manage expectations, and ensure buy-in for process improvements
*   Provide guidance on how to best translate procurement processes into transactional matching to ensure minimal exceptions in the invoicing and payment processes
*   Develop and maintain strong stakeholder relationships across Procurement, Finance, Legal, GBUs, and Corporate Functions
**Technology & Tooling:**
*   Implement and manage technology solutions to support the requisition to receipt process
*   Work with IT and other relevant teams to select, implement, and maintain tools used to support a streamlined requisition to receipt process. This includes evaluating existing systems and recommending improvements or replacements
*   Understand and leverage AI technologies and future possibilities to enhance the S2P function, driving innovation and strategic advantages
*   Ensure data integrity within S2P and contribute to Data Governance initiatives
*   Lead initiatives to drive best solution usage, user experience and automation as part of continuous improvement
**Risk Management & Compliance:**
*   Ensure Internal Control and compliance requirements are embedded in requisition to receipt process design
*   Identify and mitigate risks associated with approval workflows, ensuring compliance with relevant regulations and internal policies
*   Monitor compliance with relevant regulations and policies, establish periodic self-auditing frameworks to proactively identify gaps
**Change, Training & Documentation:**
*   Develop and deliver training materials and documentation to support the effective implementation and use of requisition to receipt processes and new technologies
*   Lead change management efforts to ensure successful adoption of new processes and technologies across the organization
**Metrics & Reporting:**
*   Establish clear Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to manage and report on requisition to receipt process
*   Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and reporting on the effectiveness of the processes, identifying areas for improvement and tracking progress against targets
*   Measure first time match rate and implement corrective actions
*   Implement continuous improvement initiatives based on performance data and track value generated from continuous improvement projects
*   Evaluate requisition to receipt process maturity & performance through benchmarking
