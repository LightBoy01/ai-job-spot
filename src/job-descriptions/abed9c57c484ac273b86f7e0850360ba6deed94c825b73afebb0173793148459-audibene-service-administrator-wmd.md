### Description\nAt audibene, we believe everyone should hear well to live well, and we're driven by the goal of improving our customers' lives. We're seeking a proactive and detail-oriented Service Administrator to join our Product & Engineering Team in Berlin. \n\nIn this pivotal role, you will be responsible for ensuring data quality across our internal systems, maintaining seamless integrations with partners and core platforms like Salesforce and Business Central, and supporting critical business processes such as customer and contract management. You'll collaborate closely with Product Managers, Engineers, Designers, and various internal and external stakeholders to uphold and evolve the operational backbone of our rapidly growing company. This position is ideal for individuals who thrive at the intersection of system administration, data management, and process design, and are eager to actively shape our digital ecosystem.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Manage and maintain business-critical data, including customer information, contracts, and process data within internal systems.\n- Ensure seamless integration and synchronization across Salesforce, Business Central, and custom-developed microservices.\n- Monitor and validate data flows, identify discrepancies, and coordinate resolutions with Engineering and Product teams.\n- Provide direct support to external partners, managing access, resolving issues, and tracking feedback.\n- Create and track bug reports or enhancement requests based on input from partners and internal stakeholders.\n- Collaborate with Product, Operations, and Engineering teams to define administrative processes and data workflows.\n- Contribute to the design of administrative tools, dashboards, or lightweight frontends for enhanced data overview and control.\n- Drive best practices for data quality, operational excellence, and process scalability.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Proven experience in system administration, data operations, or integration management within a multi-system environment (e.g., Salesforce, ERP, or similar).\n- Strong understanding of data flows, APIs, and integrations; comfortable driving technical topics with stakeholders.\n- Exceptional attention to detail, reliability, and organizational skills.\n- Strong communication skills, enabling effective collaboration with both internal stakeholders and external partners.\n- Proactive work ethic – able to identify problems early and drive process improvements.\n- Familiarity with SQL, REST APIs, or basic scripting for data validation or analysis.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting opportunity for a meticulous and proactive Service Administrator to play a crucial role in a rapidly expanding global health-tech company. You'll be at the heart of audibene's operational excellence, ensuring the data integrity and seamless integrations that directly contribute to improving customers' lives. The company offers a supportive, collaborative, and growth-oriented environment with excellent benefits, including hybrid work, professional development, wellness programs, and a dog-friendly office. If you're passionate about data quality and system integration, and want to make a tangible impact, this role offers a fantastic platform for growth and contribution.
