Job Responsibilities:
1. Knowledge Management
1) Knowledge Capture &amp; Structuring:
  - Identify and categorize different types of procurement knowledge (structured, unstructured, tacit/tribal knowledge).  
  - Design and implement frameworks to document, store, and retrieve critical procurement knowledge (e.g., supplier best practices, negotiation strategies, market intelligence).  
  - Convert unstructured data (emails, reports, expert insights) into structured, searchable formats (wikis, FAQs, decision trees).  
2) Knowledge Sharing &amp; Enablement:
  - Develop and maintain a centralized procurement knowledge hub (e.g., SharePoint, Confluence, or custom platforms).  
  - Work with procurement teams to embed knowledge-sharing practices into daily workflows.  
  - Train stakeholders on knowledge management tools and best practices.  
2. Data Governance &amp; Integration
1) Data Quality &amp; Connectivity:
  - Analyze procurement data flows from source systems (ERP, P2P tools) to end-user dashboards, identifying gaps and inconsistencies.  
  - Define data standards, taxonomies, and ownership models for procurement data (e.g., spend, supplier, contract data).  
  - Resolve data fragmentation issues by mapping and integrating siloed datasets. 
2) Data Accessibility &amp; Usability:
  - Collaborate with IT and analytics teams to design intuitive data interfaces (dashboards, reports, self-service tools).  
  - Ensure data lineage and metadata management for traceability and compliance. 
  - Implement data validation rules and monitoring to improve accuracy and completeness.  

Job Requirements:
1. Procurement Expertise:
  - 10+ years in procurement/supply chain with hands-on experience in sourcing, contracting, or supplier management. 
 - Deep understanding of procurement processes and pain points in data/knowledge gaps.  
2. Knowledge &amp; Data Management Skills:
  - Proven experience in knowledge management (capturing tacit knowledge, content curation, taxonomy design).  
  - Strong background in data governance, master data management (MDM), and data quality frameworks.  
  - Proficiency in data tools (SQL, Excel, Power BI, Alteryx) and collaboration platforms (SharePoint, Confluence).  
3. Analytical &amp; Problem-Solving Abilities:
  - Ability to analyze complex data sets, identify root causes of data issues, and recommend solutions.  
  - Experience with process mapping and data lineage documentation.  
4. Preferred: 
- Familiarity with AI/ML for knowledge mining (e.g., NLP for contract analysis, chatbots for Q&amp;A).  
- Certifications in data governance (e.g., DAMA, CDMP) or procurement (CPSM, CSCP). 
- Experience in consulting industry with hands on practice in data governance and knowledge management to customers
