### Description
The think Group is seeking a dedicated **Technical Services Employee (m/w/d)** to join our Technical Services department in **Bochum** at the earliest possible date. In this vital role, you will contribute to the smooth operation of our IT infrastructure, ensuring our internal systems run efficiently.

As part of the think Group, you can look forward to a supportive and growth-oriented environment. We offer:
*   Comprehensive onboarding complemented by our Buddy program.
*   An annual budget for further training and professional development.
*   A "Mitarbeiter werben Mitarbeiter" bonus of €1,000 for successful employee referrals.
*   A €200 employer subsidy for your workplace glasses.
*   Free lunch on "Healthy Wednesday."
*   Employer contribution to capital formation.
*   A high degree of personal development and individual support.
*   Training opportunities in our in-house fitness studio.
*   Bike leasing offers for you and your family.
*   Employee discounts and benefits with numerous cooperation partners.
*   Shared leisure activities, fostering a strong team spirit that extends beyond work.

We value team cohesion and look forward to welcoming you into a team that truly sticks together.

### Responsibilities
*   Maintenance and repair of IT components.
*   Conversion and expansion of workplace hardware.
*   Installation and configuration of hardware in client and server environments.
*   Independent software installation.
*   Troubleshooting hardware and software problems and processing tickets within defined service levels.
*   Autonomous implementation of installation changes and, if necessary, software configurations.

### Qualifications
*   Completed vocational training (e.g., IT systems electronics technician, information electronics technician, or mechatronics technician) or an equivalent qualification.
*   Initial experience in the repair of IT components and in recording/implementing customer requirements in rollouts.
*   Structured working style and strong teamwork skills.
*   Relevant manufacturer certifications are an advantage.
*   Driver's license B for cars/small buses (alt: "FS 3") is desirable.
