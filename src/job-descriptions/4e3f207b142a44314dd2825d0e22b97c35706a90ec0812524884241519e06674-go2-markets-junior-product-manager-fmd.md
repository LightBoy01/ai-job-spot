We are seeking a highly motivated **Junior Product Manager** to join our **Product & IT team in Berlin**. Are you eager to learn and contribute to our mission for a more sustainable future?

GO2 Markets connects buyers and sellers worldwide to facilitate the trading of environmental products and provides advisory services to help organizations develop and implement sustainability strategies.

As our **Junior Product Manager**, you will support the development and growth of our internal platforms, **GO2+** and **GO2 Zero**. You will coordinate development efforts, assist in prioritizing features, and ensure the timely delivery of solutions that meet client expectations. Your contributions will help shape our products and drive the success of our sustainability mission.

This is a **full-time, Berlin-based position, available for an immediate start.**

**Your Mission**  
Support the delivery and improvement of GO2’s internal products, driving clear coordination, quality execution, and incremental product ownership.

*   Help deliver **key GO2+ features on schedule** by coordinating with developers and external teams.
    
*   Maintain a **well-organized backlog** and write clear, actionable user stories.
    
*   Support **QA and testing** to ensure features meet requirements.
    
*   Collect and structure **feedback from Markets**, feeding it into prioritization and planning.
    
*   Enhance **communication and workflows** across Product, Tech, and Markets teams.
    

*   Build deep **knowledge of GO2+** and contribute to small product decisions.
    

  
  

**What You Will Bring**

*   Bachelor’s degree in **Business, Engineering, Computer Science, Sustainability**, or a related field.
    
*   2–3 years of experience in **product management, project coordination**, or a similar role.
    
*   Proven ability to turn **analytics and user insights** into clear product requirements and actionable user stories.
    
*   Solid understanding of **core product management practices** and strong documentation skills.
    
*   Experience with product and collaboration tools such as **Jira, Confluence, Figma, and Mixpanel**.
    
*   Excellent **organizational skills** with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
    
*   Comfortable collaborating with **developers**, understanding technical constraints, and supporting timely feature delivery.
    
*   Strong **communication and cross-functional collaboration** skills across Product, Tech, and Markets teams.
    
*   Analytical, detail-oriented, and eager to **learn complex systems** and GO2’s business domain.
    
*   Experience or strong interest in **AI-driven solutions** and innovative product development.
    

**What We Offer** 

*   A spacious, dog-friendly office loft by the canal in Berlin-Kreuzberg, designed for collaboration and comfort. 
    

*   Flexible work arrangements, including remote work options and the ability to plan workations. 
    

*   A purpose-driven environment where you can grow as a Sustainability Ambassador and contribute to meaningful projects. 
    

*   An international and diverse team that values ownership, collaboration, and professional growth. 
    

*   Annual learning budget and regular internal workshops to support your continuous development. 
    

*   Sustainable commuting benefits, including the Deutschland Ticket and Job Bike program. 
    

*   Access to a city-wide gym membership and mental health support through Nilo.health. 
    

*   Complimentary drinks, fresh fruit, and snacks, along with regular team events and volunteering opportunities.
