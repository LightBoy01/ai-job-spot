This role focuses on using artificial intelligence to improve how digital content is created and delivered. The specialist will work with AI tools to automate content production and manage requests from different regions, mainly Europe and Asia-Pacific, with a focus on the Animal Health business.

<strong>Tasks and responsibilities</strong>
<ul>
<li>Develop, implement, and improve AI models for content creation (websites, emails, banners).</li>
<li>Manage the technical production of digital assets and coordinate with external vendors.</li>
<li>Integrate systems used for content planning, review (MLR), asset management, and omnichannel distribution.</li>
<li>Handle content production requests from EU and APAC regions.</li>
<li>Act as a point of contact for issue resolution and stakeholder communication across markets.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Requirements</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>AI &amp; Machine Learning Expertise:<br>
<ul>
<li>Experience with large language models (LLMs), LangChain, LlamaIndex, and agent-based AI frameworks.</li>
<li>Knowledge of prompt engineering and the full AI model lifecycle (training, deployment, monitoring, retraining).</li>
<li>Familiarity with multimodal AI models for text, image, and video content.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Cloud &amp; DevOps Skills:<br>
<ul>
<li>Hands-on experience with AWS AI/ML services (e.g., SageMaker, Bedrock, Comprehend, Rekognition).</li>
<li>Experience with Azure AI tools and hybrid cloud environments.</li>
<li>Proficiency in MLOps practices, containerization (Docker/Kubernetes), and CI/CD pipelines.</li>
<li>Strong programming skills in Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, and SQL.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Pharma &amp; Content Domain Knowledge:
<ul>
<li>Understanding of content production processes and pharmaceutical compliance standards.</li>
<li>Experience with omnichannel content strategies and tools used in pharma marketing and medical affairs.</li>
<li>Knowledge of content tagging, MLR workflows, metadata management, and personalization engines.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
