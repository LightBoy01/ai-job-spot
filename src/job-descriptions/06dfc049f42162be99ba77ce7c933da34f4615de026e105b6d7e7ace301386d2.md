### Description

As a Senior Process Engineer, you will be a strategic professional at the forefront of business transformation. This is a seasoned role for an expert in process improvement and data analysis who will partner with global leaders to standardize, document, and optimize one of our seven core global processes. You will be instrumental in analyzing complex data, identifying opportunities for enhancement, and leading initiatives that drive efficiency and elevate customer satisfaction. A key aspect of this position involves harnessing the power of Artificial Intelligence; you will design, develop, and refine prompts for our internal AI tools to generate actionable outputs that deliver significant improvements in productivity and cost-effectiveness.

### Responsibilities

*   Establish a strong partnership with the Global Process Lead (GPL) and Cross-Service Center (CSC) Process Leads to drive process standardization, improvement, and adoption.
*   Evaluate process improvement opportunities, design efficient solutions (both tech and non-tech), and conduct cost/benefit analyses.
*   Collaborate with Systems Operations on technology enhancements, ensuring seamless delivery and implementation while identifying and resolving issues.
*   Conduct regular process reviews to ensure adherence to standards, manage an exception inventory, and drive the elimination of exceptions.
*   Support the GPL in driving productivity initiatives and fostering a culture of continuous improvement.
*   Lead the implementation of key transformation and process-related initiatives, ensuring accountability and timely delivery.
*   Define project scope, objectives, and deliverables in collaboration with the GPL and key stakeholders.
*   Effectively communicate project expectations, proactively track milestones, and manage dependencies.
*   Develop and deliver progress reports and executive presentations to senior leadership on key initiatives.
*   Collaborate with operations teams to identify bottlenecks and inefficiencies where AI can provide significant value, translating business needs into effective AI prompt strategies.
*   Assess and identify opportunities for innovation and explore new sources of growth.
*   Appropriately assess risk in all business decisions, ensuring compliance with laws, regulations, and company policy.

### Qualifications

*   **Experience:** 6-10 years of experience in Process Engineering, Transformation, and Lean methodologies is required.
*   **Domain Knowledge:** Strong knowledge of the end-to-end Procure-to-Pay (P2P) Accounts Payable process, with a deep understanding of supplier onboarding.
*   **Leadership & Influence:** Proven ability to influence and lead in a matrixed environment, motivating diverse groups and effectively presenting to senior stakeholders.
*   **Project Management:** Advanced program change and implementation skills with a strong record of project management, multitasking, and prioritization in a fast-paced environment.
*   **Analytical Skills:** Proven executive-level problem-solving, critical thinking, and root-cause analysis capabilities.
*   **AI Acumen:** Demonstrated ability to drive tangible improvements in efficiency or cost reduction through the application of AI.
*   **Personal Attributes:** Self-starter with a results-oriented, positive, and adaptable attitude. Exceptional interpersonal and communication skills are essential.
*   **Education:** Bachelor’s/University degree is required; a Master’s degree is preferred.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask:** "The description mentions leveraging AI and refining prompts. Can you tell me more about the current state of AI adoption for this process and the level of autonomy this role will have in shaping new AI-driven strategies?"

**Here's my take:** That's a fantastic question, and it gets right to the heart of why this role is so exciting. We're at a pivotal moment. We've moved beyond simple experimentation and now have some powerful internal AI tools at our disposal. However, we are not a top-down organization where strategies are simply handed down. We are looking for the person in this role to be a pioneer. You won't just be executing a pre-defined plan; you'll be on the ground, collaborating with operations, seeing the inefficiencies firsthand, and then translating those insights into sophisticated AI prompts that solve real-world problems. Your success will directly shape our AI playbook for this entire global process, making a highly visible impact on our productivity and cost-effectiveness from day one.

**A candidate might also ask:** "Given this role involves influencing change across global teams without direct authority, how is success truly measured, and what kind of support can I expect when navigating complex stakeholder relationships?"

**Here's my take:** That’s the core challenge and, frankly, the biggest opportunity. A 'win' here isn't just a completed project on a chart. A true win is when a process you've redesigned is adopted globally, and you start getting feedback from regional teams about how it's saved them time or eliminated a major headache. Success is measured by adoption and tangible efficiency gains. As for support, think of the Global Process Lead as your dedicated executive sponsor and partner. They help clear roadblocks at a senior level, while you own the execution and relationship-building on the ground. We value collaboration over hierarchy, so your data-driven proposals and expertise will be your most powerful tools. Your first year will be about building that trust, securing a few key process improvement wins, and establishing yourself as the go-to expert for transforming this process.
