Our comprehensive messaging suite empowers small and medium-sized businesses to connect with customers across various channels like WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, and Instagram, enabling modern solutions for payments, reviews, and sales. Backed by over $16.5 million in capital from leading Venture Capital funds, Superchat is growing rapidly and building an impactful platform.\n\nThis is an on-site role based in our beautiful office in the heart of Berlin (Prenzlauer Berg), offering a hybrid work model with two days home office per week. We foster an inclusive environment and encourage diverse perspectives. If you're looking to relocate, we offer comprehensive assistance to help you settle in.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Take ownership by building and delivering new features to our customers independently.\n- Improve the performance of our platform to enhance the user experience.\n- Participate in discussions about the long-term architecture of Superchat.\n- Shape internal development processes and best practices to ensure the engineering team scales effectively.\n- Bring your own ideas to the table to help shape our product roadmap.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You have 3+ years of hands-on experience in backend development, preferably within the JVM ecosystem (Kotlin with Quarkus Framework, but Java/Spring Boot experience is also valued).\n- Experience with AWS (or other cloud providers) and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform).\n- A strong understanding of version control systems, particularly Git.\n- Ability to plan, manage, and deliver projects independently and on time.\n- Professionally fluent in written and spoken English.\n- Familiarity with our tech stack components is a plus: RDS with Postgres, S3, SQS, SNS, SES, Lambda, Cloudfront, OpenSearch, LangGraph (AI infrastructure), GitHub Actions for CI/CD, Linear, LaunchDarkly, Doppler, DataDog / Sentry / BetterUptime.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a compelling opportunity for a skilled Backend Developer looking to make a significant impact in a fast-paced, innovative startup. The role emphasizes ownership and independent delivery, allowing developers to directly influence the product and its architecture. With a strong commitment to personal growth through a dedicated budget, a healthy work-life balance (hybrid model, generous vacation), and a vibrant culture in central Berlin with relocation support, Superchat clearly values its employees. This role is ideal for someone passionate about building scalable solutions that genuinely empower small and medium businesses.
