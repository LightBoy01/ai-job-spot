**Company Description:**

Visa is a world leader in payments and technology, with over 259 billion payments transactions flowing safely between consumers, merchants, financial institutions, and government entities in more than 200 countries and territories each year. Our mission is to connect the world through the most innovative, convenient, reliable, and secure payments network, enabling individuals, businesses, and economies to thrive while driven by a common purpose – to uplift everyone, everywhere by being the best way to pay and be paid.

Make an impact with a purpose-driven industry leader. Join us today and experience Life at Visa.

**Job Description:**

We are seeking a candidate with a demonstrated ability to lead by influence with a record of successfully delivering modern, large-scale, complex services and products with the highest levels of security and reliability. This is accomplished by leading multiple large diverse technical teams at all levels and closely collaborate with program and product teams. 

Your mission is to provide hands-on leadership to global Payment Processing teams and build a world-class team of engineers. The focus is on defining, executing, and delivering functional and non-functional features at scale quickly and promoting a diverse culture of cross-functional collaboration and engineering excellence. Be an idea leader and bring industry best practices to benefit the team and the wider organization. The ability to synthesize and simplify complex needs including business capabilities and operational efficiencies while conforming to regulatory, security and privacy requirements is required.

As a Senior Director, Payment Processing in Acceptance Solutions, you will pave the way for our most important asset, our engineering teams, to deliver amazing features of the highest quality. You will be working with colleagues, who will help, support and challenge you every day.

You will provide technical leadership, foster innovation, set engineering excellence standards, and collaborate with business teams to develop cutting-edge products and solutions. You will build the strategy for your teams to architect, design and deliver the next wave of enterprise FinTech innovation.

If this sounds exciting, we would love to chat and tell you more about our work culture and environment.

Be a part of our mission to shape the future of secure payments.

Essential Functions:

*   Drive the design, implementation, and deployment of secure, reliable, robust and interoperable products.
*   Provide leadership across the engineering organization to ensure solutions are built in alignment with platform principles and standards of six nines availability.
*   Define technical strategy for the payment processing platform that aligns with the product vision and accelerates innovation.
*   Drive strategic impact by delivering fit-for-purpose products with an accelerated time to market.
*   Leverage AI/ML technologies in bringing productivity across the SDLC phases and develop innovative business solutions.
*   Partner with Product, Regional teams on requirements and prioritization. Influence product strategy and roadmap.
*   Lead a multi-disciplinary team of software developers, data engineers, AI & ML experts, test engineers, release engineers.
*   Develop and nurture the engineering team by hiring critical talent, driving retention, motivating, and mentoring staff, and driving career growth for team members. Build an inclusive team culture, and a culture of ownership and accountability.
*   Ensure strong program management and governance practices for your team.
*   Forge strong partnerships with key stakeholders across Technology & Business.
*   Role model Visa Leadership Principles (VLPs).

This is a hybrid position. Expectation of days in the office will be confirmed by your Hiring Manager.
