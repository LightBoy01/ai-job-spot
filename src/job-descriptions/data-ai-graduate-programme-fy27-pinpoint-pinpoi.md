What We're Looking For
  

We are looking for students from academic disciplines in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics).&nbsp;<br><br>
The ideal Analytics candidate will be familiar with any number of the following analytics skills:<ul><li>Knowledge of how data can be used to solve business problems&nbsp;</li><li>Cloud data platform experience/knowledge (AWS, MS Azure, Google Cloud Platform)</li><li>Data science knowledge with any of Python, SAS, R, Scala, PySpark or other relevant coding language and data science technologies</li><li>Knowledge of machine learning modelling techniques</li><li>Knowledge of data warehouse tools and database technologies e.g., SQL, ETL, No-SQL and other Big Data technologies</li><li>Experience in/knowledge of data visualisation/reporting tools such as Tableau, Qlik, Looker or Power BI</li></ul>
Familiarity in every skill is not required as candidates will be aligned to one of the capability areas (Data Science, Data Engineering, Data Visualisation, Data &amp; AI Strategy) based on their knowledge.
<br>
All candidates will have a minimum 2:1 degree, obtained or predicted.&nbsp;<br><br>As well as demonstrate the following:<ul><li>Strong teamwork skills</li><li>Problem solving skills</li><li>Strong verbal/written communication &amp; data presentation skills</li><li>Ability to adapt and learn in a continuously changing environment</li><li>Experience in leadership roles in extra-curricular activities</li><li>Creativity, initiative, and a growth mindset</li></ul>
