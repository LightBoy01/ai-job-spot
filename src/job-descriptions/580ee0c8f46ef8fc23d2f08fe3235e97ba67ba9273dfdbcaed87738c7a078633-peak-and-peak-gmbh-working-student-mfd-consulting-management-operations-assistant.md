Consulting Management is looking for dedicated support to help us stay on track with deadlines, drive growth initiatives, and ensure that nothing falls through the cracks. We are seeking a working student who can actively monitor ongoing topics, support us in preparing presentations, and keep momentum high across various projects.

This role combines elements of **project coordination** and **executive assistance**, offering a unique opportunity to work closely with senior leaders, gain deep insights into consulting operations, and contribute directly to the success of a high-performing team.

Tasks
-----

The Assistant to the Leadership Team will focus on three core areas:

1.  Project and Initiative Coordination

*   Track Tasks: Maintain a centralized dashboard of the Leadership Team's key strategic initiatives and tasks, track milestones, deadlines, and dependencies across departments.
*   Supporting Materials: Assist in the preparation, formatting, and distribution of confidential documents, presentations, and reports for meetings and board sessions.
*   Research: Conduct research on initiatives as requested, summarizing findings into concise, actionable briefs.

2\. Executive Administrative Support

*   Meeting Management: Manage complex calendars for consulting management leaders, scheduling internal and external meetings, and managing conflicts with discretion.
*   Documentation: Attend key strategy meetings to accurately capture high-level decisions, action items, and next steps, ensuring accountability follow-up.
*   Communication Drafts: Draft and edit internal communications, memos, and emails on behalf of the leadership team, ensuring professional quality and alignment with company voice.
*   Travel and Logistics: Arrange domestic and international travel logistics, including flights, accommodation, and detailed itineraries for the consulting management leaders.
*   Support the extended leadership team in additional initiatives and responsibilities.

3\. Cross-Departmental Liaison

*   Act as the primary point of contact between the Consulting Management Team and extended leadership team for information gathering and alignment requests.
*   Manage confidential files and proprietary data with the utmost discretion and adherence to company policies.
*   Assist in planning and executing internal company events, off-sites, and executive team social gatherings.

Requirements
------------

Currently enrolled in an undergraduate or postgraduate degree program (e.g., Business Administration, Management, Technology, Communications, Finance).

*   Strong proficiency in the Microsoft Office Suite or Google Workspace.
*   Exceptional organizational skills and meticulous attention to detail.
*   Demonstrated ability to maintain strict confidentiality and exercise professional judgment.

Nice to Have

Prior experience in an office environment, volunteer leadership, or project coordination role is a plus.

*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English, with proven editing and drafting abilities.
    
*   Ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously and thrive in a dynamic, deadline-driven environment.
    

Please note that you need to be located in Germany or Greece, you need to have a valid enrollment from a university and a valid working visa.
