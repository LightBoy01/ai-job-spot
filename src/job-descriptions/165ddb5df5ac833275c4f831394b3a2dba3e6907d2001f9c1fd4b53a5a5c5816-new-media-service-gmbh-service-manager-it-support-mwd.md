### Description\nNew Media Service GmbH, a modern IT system house with locations in Singen (Hohentwiel) and Weinheim (Bergstraße), is seeking a dedicated individual. Our consultants operate across Germany, Austria, and Switzerland, both remotely and on-site. We are looking for a proactive professional for a hybrid role that combines operational IT support with strategic team leadership. You will be instrumental in building a high-performing support team, optimizing processes, and serving as the central point of contact for our customers.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **IT Service Management & Process Ownership:**\n    - Steer and prioritize incoming second-level IT support requests.\n    - Ensure smooth IT support operations.\n    - Analyze, optimize, and further develop existing support processes.\n    - Build and maintain the internal knowledge base.\n    - Create SLA reports, trend analyses, and performance indicators.\n    - Contribute to the further development of ITIL-based processes.\n- **Team Leadership & Support Area Development:**\n    - Provide technical and organizational leadership for the IT Support Team.\n    - Build a high-performing, structured support area.\n    - Coach, develop, and ensure quality control of the team.\n    - Plan employee deployment and coordinate training.\n    - Develop and enforce Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).\n- **Customer Care & Communication:**\n    - Professionally conduct and moderate regular client meetings (Jour-Fixes).\n    - Manage escalations and ensure close coordination with clients and internal stakeholders.\n    - Act as an interface to Infrastructure, Development, and Consulting teams.\n- **Operational Daily Support:**\n    - Provide second-level support for areas like Microsoft 365, networks, clients, and security.\n    - Troubleshoot complex support cases.\n    - Hand over critical tickets to specialists or manufacturers.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Several years of experience in IT support and IT Service Management.\n- Deep understanding of ITIL processes and ticketing systems.\n- Strong communication, moderation, and customer-oriented skills.\n- Experience in team leadership or project management is an advantage.\n- Structured, customer-focused, and solution-oriented work approach.\n- Very good German and good English language skills.\n- Conscientiousness, independence, and strong motivation.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an experienced IT Support professional who is eager to step into a leadership role and make a significant impact. The company offers a secure position in a growing Cloud Solution Provider, providing substantial scope for shaping the new support area. With a modern work environment, flat hierarchies, flexible working hours, home office options, and excellent team events, the culture strongly supports work-life balance and professional growth through individual training programs and profit sharing. It’s an ideal role for someone who thrives on building, optimizing, and leading within a dynamic and supportive environment.
