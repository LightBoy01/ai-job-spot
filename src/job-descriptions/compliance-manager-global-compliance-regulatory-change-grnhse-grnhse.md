Our focus is on building the restaurant platform that helps restaurants adapt, take control, and get back to what they do best: building the businesses they love. <p><strong><em>Bready</em></strong><strong>* to make a change?</strong></p>
<p>The Toast Enterprise Risk and Compliance Management Program provides a coordinated, company-wide approach to identify, measure, monitor, mitigate, and treat risks while enabling the business to innovate and deliver quality products and solutions to serve our customers.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Toast seeks an experienced Compliance Manager to support the oversight and execution of its Global Compliance Testing, Regulatory Change Management, and Compliance Issue Management programs within the Enterprise Risk &amp; Compliance department. This role will manage and perform compliance testing activities, assess and track regulatory changes, and oversee the timely and effective remediation of compliance issues.&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong><em>Work Mode: </em></strong><em>Hybrid (WFO twice a week in Bangalore office)</em><strong><em><br></em></strong><strong><em>Work Location: </em></strong><em>Bangalore or Willing to relocate</em><em><br></em><strong><em>Work Timings:</em></strong><em> 2PM - 11PM (IST</em></p>
<p><strong><br></strong><strong>About this roll*&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p>Global Compliance&nbsp; Testing</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Control &amp; Transaction Testing – </strong>Support the oversight and execution of compliance testing activities assigned by US-based Global Compliance Testing Manager.</li>
<li><strong>Reporting – </strong>Provide accurate and timely updates on assigned work to the US-based Global Compliance Testing Manager.</li>
</ul>
<p>Regulatory Change Management</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Regulatory Change Oversight - </strong>Manage alert monitoring, applicability/impact assessments, and implementation tracking.</li>
<li><strong>Compliance Obligations Inventory – </strong>Oversee translation of applicable laws and regulations into discrete compliance requirements and ensure accurate entry into the enterprise GRC system.</li>
</ul>
<p>Compliance Issue Management</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Centralized Intake Process –</strong> Support the implementation and day-to-day oversight of the centralized intake process for compliance issues and ensure issues are inputted into the enterprise GRC system.</li>
<li><strong>Monitoring &amp; Validation -&nbsp; </strong>Manage updates from Compliance Leads on open issues to ensure progress aligns with remediation timelines and SLAs. Oversee validation of issues.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
<p>Other Responsibilities</p>
<ul>
<li>Help supervise Senior Analyst based in Bangalore, ensuring adherence to methodology, documentation, and quality standards.</li>
<li>Support preparation of various reports to Compliance Leadership and governance committee.</li>
<li>Leverage AI-enabled tools and other technology, where appropriate, to support efficiency in day-to-day compliance work.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Do you have the right </strong><strong><em>ingredients*</em></strong><strong>? </strong><strong>&nbsp;(Requirements)</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Bachelor's Degree required.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Minimum 6–8 years in regulatory compliance, risk management, or audit, preferably in FinTech, banking, technology, or consulting.</li>
<li>Familiarity using enterprise GRC systems</li>
<li>Experience in one or more of the following:&nbsp;</li>
<ul>
<li>Regulatory change processes such as tracking developments or maintaining regulatory inventories.</li>
<li>Second-line compliance testing, including drafting test plans, executing testing,&nbsp; or documenting issues.</li>
<li>Supporting or managing issue management frameworks (e.g., logging, tracking, or validating remediation).</li>
</ul>
<li>Familiarity with U.S. and/or global laws and regulations impacting Fintechs similar to Toast&nbsp; in areas such as (but not limited to): financial crimes, payments, lending, or consumer protection.</li>
<li>Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to challenge, influence, and collaborate effectively across functions.</li>
<li>Experience supervising junior team members, including setting direction, reviewing deliverables, and giving constructive feedback.</li>
<li>Proficiency in Google Workspace for organizing information, maintaining trackers, and preparing deliverables; experience using and customizing enterprise AI tools to streamline manual or repetitive tasks.</li>
<li>Analytical mindset, attention to detail, and ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment.</li>
</ul>
