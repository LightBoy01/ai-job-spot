**About the role :** 

We are seeking an experienced and motivated Individual who have proven expertise in collections and account follow-ups. The ideal candidate will be confident in handling a high volume of calls and emails, with hands-on experience using leading ERP and CRM applications. This role requires strong individual performance, attention to detail, and an active interest in leveraging AI-based solutions for process stabilization and improvement.

**Shift time : 7 PM IST to 4 AM IST**  

**What You'll Do:**

*   Make collection calls to customers/accounts.
    
*   Send and track collection-related emails daily.
    
*   Manage end-to-end collections activities, including follow-ups, dispute resolution, and payment status updates.
    
*   Ensure timely escalation of overdue accounts and maintain accurate records in systems.
    
*   Utilize applications like SAP, Salesforce, and HRC for account tracking and reporting.
    
*   Partner with internal teams to stabilize collection processes and explore AI-driven improvements.
    
*   Deliver results as an individual contributor, ensuring productivity and quality standards are consistently met.
    

**What You Bring:**

*   Educational Qualification: Bachelors in Commerce or other relevant Degree
    
*   5 to 7 years of experience in B2B or B2C collections, preferably in an international process.
    
*   Adaptable to work in night shift 
*   Strong communication skills with the ability to handle challenging conversations.
    
*   Proficiency in SAP / Salesforce /  High Radius (or similar ERP/CRM platforms).
    
*   Strong interest in collection process stabilization using AI tools/technologies.
    
*   Highly self-driven and able to work with minimal supervision.
    

**Preferred Skills:**

*   Exposure to global client accounts and cross-cultural communication.
    
*   Analytical skills to track collection trends and suggest improvements.
    
*   Familiarity with productivity and reporting dashboards
