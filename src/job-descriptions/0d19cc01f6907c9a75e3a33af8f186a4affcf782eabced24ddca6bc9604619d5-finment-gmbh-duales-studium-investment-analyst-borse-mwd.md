### Description\nFinMent is a fast-growing, double government-certified FinTech startup in Berlin, dedicated to democratizing access to institutional investment knowledge and strategies for everyone. Our mission is: \"Let your money work even more efficiently for you!\" We achieve this through innovative technologies, verified financial knowledge, and exclusive strategies previously reserved for institutional investors. This unique opportunity offers you a chance to kickstart your career in a profitable FinTech startup as part of a dual study program, combining practical work with a Bachelor's or Master's degree at a renowned private university. As part of our inner circle, you'll also gain free access to our products, enabling you to generate a regular stock market income and achieve financial freedom, just like our clients.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a core member of our team, you will be involved in the analysis, development, and implementation of financial and investment content, while comprehensively supporting our customers and community. You will have the opportunity to independently implement your ideas.\n- **Market Analysis & Research:** Observe markets, analyze securities, and develop well-founded investment ideas that actively support our trading team and customers.\n- **Customer & Community Support:** Act as the direct point of contact for our customers and investor community regarding questions, problems, and stock market trading.\n- **Campaigns & Customer Loyalty:** Plan and accompany measures for customer loyalty, satisfaction, and referrals, with a focus on long-term relationships.\n- **Content Creation & Knowledge Transfer:** Develop understandable content such as explainer videos and guides to convey financial topics simply and practically.\n- **Strategies & Further Development:** Contribute your own ideas and actively help improve our trading system and develop new investment strategies.\n\n### Qualifications\nWe are looking for an ambitious individual with a thirst for financial knowledge, eager to develop into a specialist in the financial sector.\n- **Experience:** You have 6 months to 1 year of practical experience in customer support, ideally through internships or similar activities.\n- **Passion:** You are enthusiastic about this exact job! You have strong self-motivation and a passion for clean, agile process and project management.\n- **Startup Responsibility:** You have demonstrable experience taking responsibility for your topic area, even outside your comfort zone. You tackled things without hesitation, were results-oriented, and quick in execution.\n- **Personal Growth:** You are eager to learn and develop, whether in your job, your sector, or privately! You actively seek feedback and always keep the team's success in mind.\n- **Smart:** You possess very strong analytical and structural skills to clearly think through scenarios in advance and then implement them in a structured manner.\n- **Language:** Your German is ideally at \"native speaker\" level. Your English is good for conversations.\n- **Tools:** Ideally, you have practical working experience with common financial and charting software (e.g., TWS, Tradingview).\n*Please note: You don't always meet all requirements. That shouldn't stop you. If you are truly passionate about working with us, we look forward to hearing from you!*\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional entry-level opportunity within a dynamic, fast-growing FinTech startup that genuinely invests in its people. FinMent offers a unique dual study program where you can earn a Bachelor's or Master's degree at a prestigious private university, with all tuition fees covered by a generous 100,000€ scholarship, alongside a very good monthly salary.
