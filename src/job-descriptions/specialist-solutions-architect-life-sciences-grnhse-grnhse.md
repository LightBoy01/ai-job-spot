The role
<p>We are looking for an experienced <strong>Specialist Solutions Architect (Life Sciences)</strong> to support our customers in academia, biotech, and pharmaceutical industries as they harness Nebius services for cutting-edge scientific research and innovation. This role is ideal for someone with a strong academic background—preferably a PhD—in a life sciences or related discipline, and a deep interest in applying AI and high-performance computing (HPC) to real-world scientific challenges.</p>
<p>In this role, you will act as a trusted advisor, collaborating closely with research teams, data scientists, and bioinformaticians to architect scalable AI and data solutions for complex scientific workloads—from genomics and molecular modeling to large-scale simulations and machine learning-driven drug discovery.</p>
<p>You’re welcome to work in our office in <strong>Amsterdam or remotely from any EU country.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Your responsibilities will include:&nbsp;</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Design tailored solutions for life sciences customers that meet both scientific and business objectives.</li>
<li>Guide researchers and technical stakeholders in leveraging Nebius infrastructure for AI/ML, HPC, and data-intensive workflows.</li>
<li>Build and maintain long-term, trust-based relationships with academic and scientific institutions.</li>
<li>Deliver technical presentations, write whitepapers, and host webinars/workshops for audiences across the spectrum of scientific expertise.</li>
<li>Collaborate with Nebius engineering and product teams to relay feedback from the scientific community and influence feature development.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>We expect you to have:&nbsp;</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>PhD or equivalent research experience in bioinformatics, computational biology, cheminformatics, structural biology, or a related scientific field.</li>
<li>3+ years of experience with cloud technologies, preferably in MLOps, scientific computing, or AI/ML engineering roles.</li>
<li>Strong understanding of life sciences workflows (e.g., omics analysis, molecular dynamics, protein structure prediction, AI/ML in drug discovery).</li>
<li>Proven experience in distributed training or simulation workloads (multi-node, multi-GPU environments).</li>
<li>Hands-on knowledge of scientific computing libraries and ML frameworks such as PyTorch, JAX, or TensorFlow.</li>
<li>Excellent communication skills, especially in explaining technical concepts to scientific audiences.</li>
<li>Bonus Points</li>
<li>Experience deploying inference or simulation infrastructure in production or research environments.</li>
<li>Ability to transition scientific prototypes or pipelines from proof-of-concept to scalable production systems.</li>
<li>Familiarity with open-source tools and datasets commonly used in life sciences research.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Preferred Tooling: </strong></p>
