### Description\nJoin the think Group in Bochum as a Microsoft Azure / Intune Specialist (m/f/d) at the earliest possible date. This role involves planning, implementing, and operating cutting-edge Microsoft Azure and Intune solutions for our diverse client base, ensuring robust cloud infrastructures and efficient mobile device management. We are seeking a proactive team member to contribute to our dynamic environment and grow with us.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Planning, implementation, and operation of Microsoft Azure and Microsoft Intune solutions for our clients\n- Consulting on and implementing M365 services, Azure AD, Conditional Access, Autopilot, Endpoint Manager, and more\n- Administration and optimization of cloud infrastructures in Azure (IaaS/PaaS) including networks, storage, security, and identity management\n- Integration and management of mobile devices (MDM/MAM) with Microsoft Intune\n- Execution of cloud migration, including analysis, conception, and accompanying implementation\n- Creation of technical documentation and concepts\n- 2nd-level support and troubleshooting for complex Microsoft Cloud inquiries\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed technical degree or comparable vocational training (e.g., IT Specialist for System Integration)\n- Solid knowledge in the Microsoft Cloud environment, especially Azure, Microsoft Intune, Azure AD, and Microsoft 365\n- Experience in implementing endpoint management strategies with Intune and Autopilot\n- Knowledge in Azure networking services, security, monitoring, and identity management\n- Good PowerShell skills for automation and scripting\n- Solution-oriented approach, team spirit, and customer orientation\n- Very good German and English language skills, both written and spoken\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a dedicated Microsoft Azure / Intune Specialist to join a highly collaborative team focused on innovative cloud solutions. The think Group strongly values employee well-being and growth, offering excellent work-life balance through flexible home office, annual workation (up to 4 weeks mobile work from European countries), and a substantial annual budget for continuous professional development. Beyond the technical challenges, you'll find a supportive environment with a strong team spirit, comprehensive onboarding, and numerous benefits, including health initiatives (free lunch on Healthy Wednesday, fitness studio access), bike leasing, and various personal discounts. They are looking for someone who thrives on solving complex problems for clients and wants to grow within a dynamic, forward-thinking company.
