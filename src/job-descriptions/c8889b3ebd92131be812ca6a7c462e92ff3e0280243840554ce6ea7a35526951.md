**About the role**
==================

We are looking for a designer skilled in both 2D static graphics (banner ads) and motion graphics (classic video editing and graphical animation) to join our performance marketing team. This role is essential to our marketing (UA) team and requires collaboration on a variety of creative projects, contributing to advertising campaigns across different platforms.

**Key Responsibilities**
========================

*   Create 2D banner and motion ads for products on platforms like Meta, TikTok, and YouTube.
    
*   Stay up to date with Instagram and TikTok trends, with a strong sense of timing, spacing, and dynamics in video content.
    
*   Generate images using AI tools.
    
*   Participate in creative marketing team meetings and propose your own ideas.
    

**Qualifications**
==================

*   3+ years of experience as a 2D Graphic and Motion Designer.
    
*   Proficiency in Adobe software (Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Premiere Pro).
    
*   Strong skills in both static graphics and motion design.
    
*   Basic knowledge of color theory, composition, and typography.
    
*   For video content, a solid understanding of timing, spacing, and animation dynamics;
    
*   Experience with AI image generators (understanding how to formulate prompts for optimal results).
    
*   Creativity and ability to generate and develop new ideas.
    
*   English language proficiency at B2 level.
    
*   Portfolio with 2D banners and motion works required.
    

**Nice to have**
================

*   Knowledge and understanding of TikTok trends.
    
*   Keeping up with updates in the AI industry.
