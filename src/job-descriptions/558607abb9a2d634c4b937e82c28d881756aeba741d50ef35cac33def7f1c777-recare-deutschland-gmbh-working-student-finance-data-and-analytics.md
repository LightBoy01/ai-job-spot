### Description\nRecare is a leading German HealthTech company dedicated to reshaping discharge management with technology-driven, patient-centered, and bureaucracy-free solutions. Through its market-leading SaaS platform and AI innovations, Recare connects two-thirds of all German hospitals with over 650 rehabilitation clinics and 25,000 nursing and homecare providers, significantly simplifying processes and relieving healthcare professionals. With approximately 90 employees and growing, Recare seeks individuals passionate about improving the healthcare system.\n\nAs a Working Student in Finance Data & Analytics, you'll join a team that values purpose-driven work, where your contributions directly impact patients and professionals. Recare fosters a high-performance, respectful culture with strong team dynamics, emphasizing mutual respect and loyalty. The company offers a remote-friendly environment (100% remote Germany-wide) with flexible working hours, accommodating studies and allowing for \"workations\" by arrangement. Professional development is prioritized, ensuring your success and job satisfaction, alongside benefits like an Edenred card and an extra day off for your birthday.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Build and maintain a weekly Finance KPI pack (e.g., AR aging/DSO, spend by cost center, revenue bridges) and ensure high data quality.\n*   Act as a hands-on analyst, transforming raw exports (e.g., DATEV, CRM) into clean tables and actionable insights.\n*   Shape lightweight data pipelines (SQL/Sheets), definitions, and documentation to enable stakeholders to self-serve key views.\n*   Support FP&A on forecasts, ad-hoc deep dives, and tool introductions/data migrations (mapping, test cases, reconciliation).\n*   Ensure smooth month-/year-end support with simple reconciliations.\n*   Develop dashboards in Looker and small automations to reduce manual work.\n*   Analyze revenue, usage, and spend trends, communicating findings clearly.\n*   Collaborate with cross-functional analysts (Product, Sales, Marketing, Revenue Ops) to align on metrics and data standards.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's/Master's program (Business/Econ/Data/CS/Math or similar), with 15–20 hours/week available.\n*   Strong command of SQL and advanced skills in Excel/Google Sheets.\n*   Curiosity, structured problem-solving abilities, reliability, and discretion with financial data (GDPR aware).\n*   System-oriented thinker, appreciative of tidy schemas, clear definitions, and reproducible work.\n*   Clear communication in English (B2+); German is a plus.\n*   Tech-affine; experience with Looker (or similar BI tools), Python, Google AppScript, or cloud warehouses (Snowflake) is a plus.\n*   Exposure to Accounting platforms (DATEV) and CRM systems (Salesforce) is ideal.\n*   Nice to have: familiarity with Accounting and FP&A concepts (accruals, AR aging, deferred revenue).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an ambitious working student eager to kickstart a career in finance data analytics within the impactful HealthTech sector. You'll gain practical, hands-on experience with critical financial data, build essential reporting dashboards, and contribute directly to a company actively improving healthcare. The emphasis on professional development, flexible remote work, and a supportive, high-performance culture makes this an ideal environment to apply your SQL and analytical skills while still pursuing your studies. If you're passionate about data, have a keen eye for detail, and want your work to truly matter, Recare offers a unique growth trajectory.
