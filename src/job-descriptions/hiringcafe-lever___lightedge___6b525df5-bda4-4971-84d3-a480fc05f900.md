LightEdge is seeking a seasoned Director of IT Program Management to drive enterprise-level technology initiatives and oversee the strategic alignment of IT programs with company objectives. This leader will be responsible for guiding cross-functional technology programs, optimizing business-critical processes such as Quote-to-Cash, Onboarding/Offboarding, AI adoption, and Master Data Management, and ensuring disciplined governance across the IT portfolio. Reporting directly to the CIO, this role will play a pivotal part in translating vision into execution, ensuring technology delivery is streamlined, value-driven, and future-ready.  
  
**Responsibilities:**  
*     
    

**Strategic Leadership**

*   Define and champion the IT Program Management function, setting the vision, strategy, and standards for program execution and governance.
*   Partner with the CIO and executive leadership to prioritize IT investments that enable business growth and operational efficiency.
*   Collaborate closely with the Operations PMO to ensure alignment, eliminate duplication, and drive coordinated delivery of enterprise-wide initiatives.
*   Act as a change agent, ensuring that IT programs deliver transformative impact across the enterprise.

**Core Business Process Enablement**

*   Direct technology enablement for mission-critical processes:
*   **Quote-to-Cash (QTC):** Drive end-to-end integration and automation across sales, billing, and finance platforms.
*   **Onboarding & Offboarding:** Modernize and streamline processes for both employees and customers to enhance experience and compliance.
*   **AI Adoption:** Lead integration of AI into business and IT workflows to drive innovation and efficiency.
*   **Master Data Management (MDM):** Establish governance and accountability to ensure trusted, high-quality data across the organization.

**Portfolio & Governance Oversight**

*   Establish and oversee the IT portfolio management framework, ensuring that all initiatives are strategically prioritized, transparent, and delivering measurable business outcomes.
*   Implement and refine intake and demand management processes that balance business needs with IT capacity.
*   Govern delivery practices across the IT organization to ensure alignment, scalability, and risk management.

**Program Delivery & Execution**

*   Oversee execution of cross-functional IT initiatives, ensuring scope, schedule, budget, and quality are consistently met.
*   Manage interdependencies across programs and identify opportunities for synergies.
*   Drive adoption of program management best practices and continuous improvement in delivery methodologies.

**Executive & Stakeholder Engagement**

*   Serve as the primary liaison between IT and business leadership on all program and portfolio matters.
*   Ensure effective communication, transparency, and reporting of IT program performance at the executive and board levels.
*   Build strong partnerships across business units, including alignment with theOperations PMO, to ensure enterprise-wide coordination and unified delivery.
  
  
**Education and Experience:**  
*   Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Business, or related field (Master’s preferred).
*   10+ years of experience in IT program and portfolio management, with at least 5 years in a senior leadership role.
*   Proven expertise in leading enterprise-scale IT initiatives and business process transformation.
*   Strong knowledge of enterprise applications and business processes across finance, HR, operations, and sales.
*   Demonstrated success in establishing IT governance, portfolio management, and intake frameworks.
*   Experience with AI initiatives and master data management is highly desirable.
*   Executive presence with exceptional communication, stakeholder engagement, and leadership skills.
*   Professional certifications such as PMP, PgMP, or SAFe Agilist strongly preferred.
