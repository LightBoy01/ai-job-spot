#### **Director, Clinical Quality & Analytics** 

#### The Director, Clinical Quality & Analytics, will combine operational rigor, advanced analytics, and clinical curiosity to ensure we deliver safe, high-quality, and patient-centered care. This role is ideal for someone who thrives on turning complexity into clarity, loves building scalable systems, and believes that data is the currency of good decisions.

*   Shape the future of clinical quality and innovation at Midi.  
      
    
*   Lead analytics at the intersection of AI, clinical operations, and patient experience.  
      
    
*   Build scalable systems that improve patient outcomes and clinician success.  
      
    

#### **The Difference You’ll Make:**

#### _Clinical AI & Innovation_ 

*   Own analytics around scribe AI tools (speech-to-text transcription + Script AI insights), ensuring outputs become clear reports and dashboards.  
      
    
*   Develop analytics on clinician–patient interactions, training opportunities, operational/tech barriers, and clinical trends.  
      
    
*   Lead process improvement (PI) initiatives to strengthen quality, reduce variation, and improve outcomes.  
      
    
*   Partner with Product and Operations teams to integrate AI-driven findings into workflows.  
      
    
*   Build and maintain real-time dashboards with HEDIS and quality metrics for immediate visibility into performance and outcomes.  
      
    

#### _Quality Program Oversight_ 

*   Own Midi’s Quality program, including chart audits, expansion of automated audits, and oversight of compliance with standards.  
      
    
*   Monitor patient safety, adverse events, and near-miss reporting—integrating findings into continuous improvement.  
      
    
*   Lead weekly AI-powered Practice Pattern reviews, ensuring clinicians adhere to protocols and identifying improvement opportunities.  
      
    
*   Partner with Learning & Development to evaluate the Onboarding process with quantitative data aligned to practice patterns.  
      
    

#### _Clinical Protocols & Program Development_ 

*   Collaborate with clinical leadership on evidence-based clinical protocols, ensuring alignment with patient-centered care.  
      
    
*   Design algorithms of care—structured pathways and decision rules that improve outcomes and consistency.  
      
    
*   Tailor protocols to distinct clinical services (menopause, weight, primary care, and future complex care).  
      
    
*   Maintain rigorous documentation and communicate program status/results to stakeholders.  
      
    

#### _Cross-Functional Leadership_ 

*   Partner with Product, Operations, and Clinical leaders to ensure program success.  
      
    
*   Bridge technical and clinical teams, fostering agile development and rapid implementation.  
      
    
*   Contribute to strategic planning as Midi expands into complex care while maintaining excellence in menopause, weight, and midlife primary care.  
      
    

#### _Data & Analytics_ 

*   Lead with the philosophy: data drives science, and science drives decisions.  
      
    
*   Use data to allocate resources, identify inflection points of value, and pivot programs toward better outcomes.  
      
    
*   Build dashboards and frameworks that translate data into actionable insights for leadership, clinicians, and partners.

#### **What You’ll Need to Succeed :**

*   8+ years of experience in clinical operations, health analytics, quality management, or health services research.  
      
    
*   Demonstrated success in leading data-rich clinical programs and translating findings into operational improvements.  
      
    
*   Highly energetic, innovative, and skilled at reducing entropy—able to design simple solutions to complex challenges.  
      
    
*   Proven ability to bridge technical and clinical teams, driving agile and scalable innovation.  
      
    
*   Obsessed with time and efficiency: respects deadlines, starts on time, and finishes on time.  
      
    
*   Holds self and colleagues accountable, thrives in fast-paced environments, and avoids stagnation.  
      
    
*   Deep intellectual curiosity—if given unlimited time, you’d spend it acquiring more knowledge.
