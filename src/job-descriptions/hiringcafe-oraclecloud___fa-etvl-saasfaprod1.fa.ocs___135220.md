**AI Developer – Microsoft Workflow & AI Integration**

**Position Overview**

We are seeking a hands-on **AI Automation Developer** with deep experience in the **Microsoft Tech Stack** and a strong understanding of **AI applications**. This role will focus on designing scalable, repeatable automation patterns that integrate **AI agents** (e.g., Copilot) with Microsoft workflows such as **Power Automate**, enabling intelligent batch processing of documents and data. The developer will collaborate closely with HACH leadership from ideation through production deployment. The developer will work cross-functional collaboration with data scientists, business analysts, and IT teams.

**Key Responsibilities**

**AI-Driven Automation Development**

*   Architect and implement Microsoft workflows that ingest, reason over, and act on large volumes of structured and unstructured data.
*   Design and prompt-engineer Copilot agents to generate summaries, reports, and formatted outputs based on HACH specifications.
*   Build seamless pipelines between **Power Automate**, **Copilot Studio**, and mission-critical systems.
*   Develop reusable automation patterns for document processing, classification, and enrichment using AI.
*   The emphasis will be designing solutions that scale across departments or business units.

**DevOps & Lifecycle Management**

*   Create promotion pipelines with version control, automated testing, and rollback strategies.
*   Define branching and release strategies aligned with HACH’s SDLC.
*   Ensure robust unit testing and maintain documentation in accordance with HACH standards.

**Must-Have Qualifications**

*   **3–4+ years** of experience in automation development using Microsoft technologies.
*   **1+ year** of hands-on experience with **Copilot Studio**, including building and deploying agents in production.
*   Proven track record of end-to-end delivery of Microsoft automation solutions.
*   Strong understanding of **Generative AI / LLMs** and their integration into enterprise workflows.
*   Excellent communication skills with the ability to translate technical concepts for non-technical stakeholders.
