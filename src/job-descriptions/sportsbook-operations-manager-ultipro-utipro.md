<p><strong>Who We Are<br><br></strong>Bally's Corporation is a global casino-entertainment company with a growing omni-channel presence. It currently owns and manages 15 casinos across 10 states, a golf course in New York, a horse racetrack in Colorado, and has access to OSB licenses in 18 states. It also owns Bally's Interactive International, formerly Gamesys Group, a leading, global, online gaming operator, Bally Bet, a first-in-class sports betting platform, and Bally Casino, a growing iCasino platform.<br><br>With 10,600 employees, the Company's casino operations include approximately 15,300 slot machines, 580 table games and 3,800 hotel rooms. Upon completing the construction of a permanent casino facility in Chicago, IL, and a land-based casino near the Nittany Mall in State College, PA, Bally's will own and/or manage 16 casinos across 11 states. Bally’s also has rights to developable land in Las Vegas post the closure of the Tropicana.<br><br><strong>Well, what about the team?<br><br></strong>As Sportsbook Operations Manager for International jurisdictions, you are accountable for leading and delivering best-in-class sports content and operational performance across both existing and future markets we enter.</p>
<p>Your objective is to drive engagement through timely, localised, and exciting content, while pioneering AI-driven tools and workflows that scale operations efficiently and intelligently.</p>
<p>Reporting to the VP Product Sports, you will champion the Sports domain, build operational excellence, and drive content innovation.</p>
<p>A key part of your role is to build internal excitement around key sporting events and ensuring our sportsbook consistently delivers relevant and commercially impactful experiences.</p>
<p>You will lead the adoption of AI-powered content and automation tools within the team and play a central role in our journey toward becoming the first fully AI-driven sportsbook.  You will demonstrate strong leadership and communication skills, proactively aligning with Marketing, Product, Compliance, and Trading to ensure seamless delivery of sports experiences that excite our players.</p>
<p>Your team will be known for delivering high-quality, localised sportsbook content at scale, and for being agile, innovative, and insight-led in how they work.<br><br><strong>So, what will you be doing?<br><br>Champion the Sporting Calendar</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Build internal momentum around major sporting events by championing them across Product, Marketing and Tech.</li>
<li>Lead the creation and management of the sports calendar for all jurisdictions — surfacing key moments and ensuring timely coverage and promotion.</li>
<li>Work with cross-functional teams to ensure major sporting events are prioritised across relevant teams.</li>
<li>Define with insights what sporting events are more important on specific brands</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Deliver and Scale Sportsbook Operations</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Oversee the daily creation and planning of betting specials, boosts &amp; promotions cards.</li>
<li>Ensure all content is localised appropriately for each market, meeting regulatory and customer expectations.</li>
<li>Monitor sportsbook performance and user engagement, identifying areas of friction or opportunity and recommending operational improvements.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Lead the AI-Powered Sportsbook Transition</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Champion the use of AI tools for content generation, personalisation, event prioritisation, and campaign automation.</li>
<li>Collaborate with Product, Data team, and Tech to refine, test, and scale AI-led content solutions, and lead on the operationalisation of AI tools post delivery.</li>
<li>Feed Content tools and logic requirements into Product and Tech teams and assist with QA through to successful release of new enhancements and features</li>
<li>Reduce reliance on manual content operations while maintaining quality and market relevance.</li>
<li>Use AI tools to create innovative new content experiences that create a key differentiator for our sportsbook product.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Own Operational Standards and Continuous Improvement</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Set and maintain high operational standards across sports content delivery.</li>
<li>Provide regular reporting on performance, engagement, and the effectiveness of AI-driven initiatives.</li>
<li>Identify and establish ways of working with other teams.</li>
<li>Work with third parties and ensure day to day running of third party integrations is seamless</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Collaborate Cross-Functionally</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Build strong working relationships with local and global teams including Product, Trading, Compliance, and Brand.</li>
<li>Ensure alignment between regional content delivery and broader business objectives.</li>
<li>Share market insights and advocate for market-specific needs in strategic discussions.<strong> </strong></li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Lead and Develop a High-Performing Team</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Line manage a small team of Sportsbook Content Operators.</li>
<li>Set objectives and personal development plans aligned to both individual and business goals.</li>
<li>Develop a high-performance culture that emphasises accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement.</li>
<li>Ensure the team is upskilled in AI tools, localisation best practices, and industry trends to support our shift towards automated, scalable operations.</li>
<li>Conduct regular 1:1s and performance reviews to drive individual and team success.</li>
<li>Champion knowledge sharing and collaboration within the team, encouraging proactive communication and cross-market learning.</li>
</ul>
