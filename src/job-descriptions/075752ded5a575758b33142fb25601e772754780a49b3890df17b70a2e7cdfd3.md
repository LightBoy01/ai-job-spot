YOUR MISSION

We are looking for a highly-skilled **Senior Frontend Engineer** to join one of our development teams. In this role, you will be responsible for developing and implementing user interface components using React.js with technologies such as TypeScript, GraphQL, Jest, Cypress and Vite, as we build the future of AI-powered digital advertising. You will also be responsible for testing, profiling and improving front-end performance and documenting our front-end codebase, and mentoring your team-mates, planning tasks and estimating workloads.  
  
You'll be working within a cross-disciplinary team under the Engineering Manager to contribute to our multi-tenant SaaS product. This is a high-impact role for someone who thrives on elevating engineering standards, and driving a forward-thinking, **AI-first** approach across the organization.   
  
To ensure success as a senior frontend engineer, you should have in-depth knowledge of React concepts, excellent front-end coding skills, and a great understanding of progressive web applications. Ultimately, a top-class Senior Frontend Engineer should be able to design, plan and build modern user interface components to enhance application performance and coach other team-mates to achieve the same.
