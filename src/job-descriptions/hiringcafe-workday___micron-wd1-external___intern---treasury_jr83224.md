**Our vision is to transform how the world uses information to enrich life for _all_.**

Micron Technology is a world leader in innovating memory and storage solutions that accelerate the transformation of information into intelligence, inspiring the world to learn, communicate and advance faster than ever.

Micron’s Treasury internship offers a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience across multiple Treasury functions, including Cash & Investment Management, Foreign Exchange, Capital Markets, Credit & Collections, and Risk Management & Insurance. Interns will rotate through these areas, working closely with mentors and contributing to both daily operations and project-based initiatives.  The ideal candidate for this role is someone who can leverage programming skills to drive process improvement and automation throughout the internship.  We are also looking for candidates who have strong communication skills, ability to engage well with others in group settings, a self-starter, and someone who is interested in pursuing a career in corporate finance after college.

**Responsibilities include, but are not limited to:**  
•    Treasury operations  
•    Counterparty risk analysis  
•    Net interest forecasting  
•    Support automation and process improvement initiatives using programming languages, business intelligence tools, and AI-driven agents

•    Foreign exchange, interest rate, and commodity analysis

•    Capital markets  
•    Credit & collections  
•    Overview of underwriting  
•    Risk management  
•    Insurance

•    Ad-hoc and project-specific research

**Qualifications:**  
**Successful candidates for this position will have:**  
•    Experience using business intelligence tools (e.g. Power BI, Tableau)  
•    Experience using programming languages (e.g. Python, R, SQL) and AI-driven scripting agents  
•    Excellent oral and written communication skills with the ability to communicate technical concepts to audiences  
•    Strong interpersonal skills and ability to collaborate effectively in cross-functional teams

•    The ability to be highly organized and detail-oriented, with a strong focus on accuracy  
•    Good analytical and problem-solving skills  
•    The ability to follow comprehensive instructions, work with minimal supervision, prioritize and multi-task effectively, and be diligent and eager to learn

**Education:**  
Working towards Bachelor's Degree in Business, Finance, Accounting, Economics, Information Systems, Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field
