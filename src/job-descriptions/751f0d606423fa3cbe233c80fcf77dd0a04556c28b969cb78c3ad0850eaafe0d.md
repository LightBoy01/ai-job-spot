**Summary:**
Join our Global IT Organization as we help shape the future through digital, data, and AI. We are seeking an innovative and strategic leader to join our Global IT Organization as the Director IT, Digital Partner – Manufacturing, Supply Chain, and SAP COE. This is a pivotal role in accelerating our digital transformation journey through a multi-year SAP S/4HANA business transformation leveraging the latest innovations in SAP S/4HANA. In addition, the role will explore and implement advanced technologies like IIoT, digital twin, real-time predictive analytics and AI to transform our manufacturing environments and our global supply chain network.
Reporting directly to the Global Chief Information Officer, you will collaborate with senior leaders across manufacturing, supply chain and our global IT organization to reimagine the future of our business, creating unparalleled business value.
This role will lead our multi-year global SAP S/4HANA program as well as lead an enterprise culture shift toward a digital-first, AI-first, data-driven culture. You will help evolve the IT landscape to support seamless end-to-end business processes, efficient, resilient, and agile operations aligned with industry 5.0 standards to respond swiftly to market changes and disruptions while maintaining high standards of quality and sustainability. You will inspire a high-performing global IT team to deliver continuous business value.
This role builds deep, trusted relationships with business leaders across the core ERP functional areas (Order Management, Supply Chain, Planning, Manufacturing, Engineering, Procurement), understanding their operating environments, key strategies and tactics while providing ERP and other technology recommendations to business leaders to maximize value. Hollister operates in a hybrid work enviornment. This position is to be based out of the Libertyville, IL office.

**About Hollister Incorporated**
Hollister Incorporated is an independent, employee-owned company that develops, manufactures and markets healthcare products worldwide. The company spearheads the advancement of innovative products for ostomy care, continence care and critical care, and also creates educational support materials for patients and healthcare professionals. Headquartered in Libertyville, Illinois, Hollister has manufacturing and distribution centers on three continents and sells in nearly 80 countries. Hollister is a wholly owned subsidiary of The Firm of John Dickinson Schneider, Inc., and is guided both by its Mission to make life more rewarding and dignified for people who use our products and services, as well as its Vision to grow and prosper as an independent, employee-owned company, and in the process, to become better human beings.
