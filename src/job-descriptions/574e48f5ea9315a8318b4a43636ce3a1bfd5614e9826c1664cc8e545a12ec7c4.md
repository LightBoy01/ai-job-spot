**Your Impact at Brevo:**  
*   **Build personalization systems** that adapt our platform to each customer's unique context and needs
*   **Optimize RAG architectures** for knowledge retrieval, embedding strategies, and multi-modal data processing
*   **Deploy ML models to production** using Kubernetes and modern MLOps practices on GCP
*   **Create intelligent targeting models** for audience selection and content optimization
*   **Monitor and measure impact** of AI features, ensuring ROI and performance standards
*   **Collaborate with product teams** to identify and prototype new AI capabilities
  
  
**Who you are:**  
*   5+ years of Python experience in production environments
*   Strong experience with LLMs and RAG systems (vector databases, embeddings, retrieval optimization)
*   Proven track record deploying and monitoring ML models at scale
*   Experience with cloud platforms (GCP preferred) and Kubernetes
*   Ability to work with diverse data types (text, web content, structured data)
