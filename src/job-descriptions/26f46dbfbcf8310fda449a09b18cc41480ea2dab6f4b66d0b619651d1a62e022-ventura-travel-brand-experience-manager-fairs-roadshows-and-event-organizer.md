Join us at Ventura TRAVEL – a group of specialized travel brands that unleash the adventurer in every person!

[Ventura TRAVEL](http://www.venturatravel.org/) was founded to empower intrapreneurs to build up leading niche travel brands, focused on authentic and responsible travel experiences. We are strongly committed to sustainable tourism principles, to mitigate the environmental and cultural footprint of tourism. As a holding enterprise for other tourism companies and through our head office in Berlin, Germany, we take care of all the across company and international issues concerning all our subsidiary brands. All our brands actively live and share our core values to contribute to a business for the good cause.

_On a daily basis, you will_
----------------------------

*   Plan and execute our full offline event calendar, including major fairs, roadshows and smaller branded events.
*   Coordinate all logistics: venues, materials, shipments, vendors, travel plans, and on site setup.
*   Own event execution from start to finish: preparation, booth setup, guest flow, lead capture and post event reporting.
*   Ensure consistent quality across all events, keeping our brand experience professional, engaging and aligned with our identity.
*   Manage event budgets, negotiate with external partners and track all operational details.
*   Travel frequently across Europe to lead events on site and represent our brands.

_The basics you bring along_
----------------------------

*   Proven experience in event management, trade fairs or roadshow organization.
*   Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple complex logistics streams in parallel.
*   Hands on mindset: comfortable taking full ownership, solving problems quickly and working independently.
*   Excellent communication skills and confidence engaging with partners, suppliers and event attendees.
*   Fluency in either German or French to run events in those markets.
*   Ability to travel frequently within Europe and work flexible hours during events.

_You can impress us even more with_
-----------------------------------

*   Experience in the travel or tourism industry.
*   A strong supplier network for fairs, event materials or exhibition logistics.
*   Knowledge of branding, booth design or experiential marketing.
*   Experience measuring event impact through lead generation, branded search or conversion tracking.
*   Familiarity with CRM or lead management tools.
