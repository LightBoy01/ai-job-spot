Idana is an excellently funded, high-growth HealthTech startup from Freiburg. Our software digitalizes medical history and patient forms in medical practices – ensuring better care and greater efficiency. Thousands of doctors and medical assistants work with Idana every day. We are a startup in the scaling phase – with us, you can grow and shape the future of healthcare.

Tasks
-----

You will join a mature, high-trust engineering team of 4 Fullstack Engineers. We don't just write code; we own our services end-to-end on **Google Cloud Platform**. We believe in "You build it, you run it," peer reviews, and collective architectural decisions.

We are looking for a **Senior Developer** to complement our team. We don't believe in silos—everyone touches the full stack—but we need specific expertise to drive our upcoming challenges. Depending on your strengths, you will fit into one of two profiles:

**Option A: The "Frontend Architect" (Focus Vue/TS)**

*   You love the browser. You dive into the backend, but your superpower is structuring complex frontends.
*   **Key Challenge:** You will develop of our new **Design System** to ensure reusable components across our distributed frontends.

**Option B: The "Cloud & Backend Driver" (Focus Go/Infra)**

*   You love the engine room. You build UIs, but your heart beats for distributed systems and automation.
*   **Key Challenge:** You help us evolve our backend (currently a mix of TypeScript & Go) and strengthen our **Infrastructure as Code** setup with **Terraform** on GCP.

**Regardless of the profile, your core role is:**

*   **Sparring Partner:** You act as a technical sparring partner. We challenge each other in code reviews and architectural discussions to learn, grow, and improve as a team.
*   **Ownership:** You take responsibility for your features—from the first line of code to the deployment pipeline (GitHub Actions) and monitoring.
*   **Collaboration:** You actively participate in architectural decisions. We decide collectively—often one person drives a topic, but we review and refine the solution together to ensure high quality and shared ownership.

Requirements
------------

*   **Experience:** You have several years of experience in Software Engineering and a deep understanding of modern web architectures.
*   **Tech Stack:** You are proficient in either **Vue.js/TypeScript** or **Go/GCP/Terraform**. You don't need to be a master of everything on Day 1, but you must be willing to learn the rest.
*   **Language:** You are fluent in **English** (Team language). German is a plus, but not required.
*   **Mindset:** You fit into a team where "we" is more important than "I". You value clean code, maintainability, and honest feedback.
*   **Location:** Ideally, you are based in/around **Freiburg** (Hybrid) or are willing to visit for team events 1-2 times a year. **Full Remote** is possible.

Benefits
--------

**Real Engineering Culture:**

*   Monthly **"Hack & Innovation Days"** to explore new tech.
*   **Lean Processes:** Scrum with as few meetings as possible. We value focus time.
*   **Modern Stack:** Google Cloud, Terraform, Go, Vue 3 + TS, GitHub Actions.

**Company Benefits:**

*   A central role in a meaningful HealthTech startup.
*   **30 days vacation**, flexible working hours, and 100% remote options.
*   Monthly non-cash benefit (50 €) and allowance for company pension scheme.
*   Clearly defined goals & development with OKRs, scorecards & coaching.
*   Regular team events in Freiburg & genuine togetherness.

**Sounds exciting?** A perfect profile is not crucial to us – what matters more is that you want to take responsibility, fit into the team, and have the desire to advance digitalization in healthcare together with us.
