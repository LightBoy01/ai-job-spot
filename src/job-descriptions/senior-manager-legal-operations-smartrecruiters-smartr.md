<strong>Job Description:</strong><p>We are looking for a results-driven, strategically-focused&#xa0;<strong>Senior Manager, Legal Operations</strong>&#xa0;- Business &amp; Finance to join Procore’s Legal, Regulatory &amp; Compliance (LRC) Department. This role is for a versatile leader who has seen what excellence looks like at scale and is driven by a healthy disdain for the status quo. You will be empowered to both successfully navigate the complexities of the present and build a more efficient, data-driven future.</p><p>As a<strong>&#xa0;Senior Manager, Legal Operations,&#xa0;</strong>you will be a key member of the Legal Operations team, working closely with various internal stakeholders, including LRC, Finance, IT, Procurement, HR, and other internal operations leaders. If you are a proactive leader and culture-carrier who thrives on developing talent and shaping a vital business function, we want to hear from you.</p><p>This position reports to our Vice President, Legal Operations. narratives for LRC leadership by evolving the caliber of financial reports and dashboards.</p></li><li><p>Champion Tech-Enabled Operational Excellence: Proactively identify, design, and build the
