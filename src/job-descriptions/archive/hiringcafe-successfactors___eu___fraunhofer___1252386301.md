**Future. Discover. Together.**
If you're passionate about startups, we're excited to introduce you to this fantastic opportunity at HHI. Our vision? To accelerate the creation of groundbreaking, technology-driven startups. To do this, we rely on talent like YOU - with a mix of deep-tech projects, financial support, professional space, and a strong network of mentors and coaches.
As part of our project with the startup Neptune, we are looking for a working student Unity developer.
As part of the project, we have set ourselves the task of revolutionizing the way children learn music! Supported by a leading research institute, we are developing an innovative app that combines AI, motion detection and rhythm games to make music education interactive, fun and accessible. Imagine kids playing with real and virtual instruments while an intelligent AI supports them with every note and helps them improve. If you're excited about using technology to inspire young minds, then you've come to the right place. Become a part of our team and join us on our journey of research and innovation!
**What you will do**
*   Support with development breathtaking motion detection features with Unity and AI, allowing children to play music through movement and interaction with real instruments
*   Support with the creation of sound and pitch recognition systems that enable children to always hit the right notes as they play
*   Collaborate with a creative and dedicated team to build smooth, addictive game mechanics that will keep kids engaged and eager to keep playing
**What you bring to the table**
*   Enrolled student in a relevant field of study
*   Unity Experience: You are proficient in Unity and C# and love creating interactive experiences
*   AI & Motion Wizard: You are familiar with tools like MediaPipe, Lightbuzz, Kinect, or OpenCV and excited about integrating AI to enhance motion detection
*   Sound Knowledge: Do you have experience with sound and pitch recognition?
*   Startup Mentality: You thrive in a fast-paced, creative environment where new ideas are always welcome
*   Music Enthusiast: Do you love music? Even better! We are all about blending technology with creativity
*   You should have strong English skills, both written and spoken; knowledge of German is an advantage but not mandatory
**What you can expect**
*   Fascinating challenges in a scientific and entrepreneurial setting
*   Attractive salary
*   Modern  and excellently equipped workspace in central location
*   Great and cooperative working atmosphere in an international team
*   Flexible working hours
*   Opportunities to work from home
Your contract at Fraunhofer HHI is limited to September 30, 2026, as part of the cooperation and joint project with the startup NepTune.
The monthly working time is 80 hours. This position is also available on a part-time basis.
