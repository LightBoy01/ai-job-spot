### Description
This is an exciting opportunity to join a family-owned, internationally active mid-sized company headquartered near Koblenz, Germany. For nearly 80 years, our client has been a leader in fall protection and safety solutions for sports and industry. With approximately 950 employees worldwide (500 at the main site) and operations in six countries, the company embodies tradition, innovation, and international growth.

We are seeking an experienced Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Software Developer / Technical Consultant (m/f/d) for a permanent position, to further expand our ERP team. This role offers an attractive salary package, flexible working hours with a 40-hour week and 30 days of annual leave, as well as the option for up to two days of home office per week. Expect a familial company culture with flat hierarchies, open communication, and diverse benefits including occupational pension, JobRad, employee discounts, holiday and Christmas bonuses, modern offices, a canteen, and regular training opportunities. Minimal travel is required.

### Responsibilities
*   Actively participate in the international rollout of Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (BC23).
*   Maintain, further develop, and support the ERP application.
*   Analyze functional requirements and translate them into technical solutions.
*   Independently develop solutions using AL (and C/AL for existing NAV environments).
*   Design and implement customizations and extensions.
*   Optimize and automate business processes.
*   Collaborate closely with internal NAV developers and the IT department, particularly on topics related to SQL Server, .NET, and C#.
*   Provide 2nd-level support, conduct user training, and create documentation.

### Qualifications
*   A degree in (Business) Informatics, a qualification in software development, or equivalent professional experience.
*   Profound knowledge of Microsoft Dynamics NAV / Business Central and experience with AL and C/AL development.
*   Proficiency in SQL, databases, object-oriented programming, and Microsoft Office.
*   Understanding of commercial processes and their mapping within an ERP system.
*   Independent, structured, and solution-oriented work style.
*   Strong team player with a motivation for continuous learning and excellent communication skills.
*   Very good German and good English language skills, both written and spoken.
