**Job Description:**
Visa Technology & Operations LLC, a Visa Inc. company, needs a Machine Learning Engineer – Sr. Consultant level (multiple openings) in Austin, TX to:
*   Develop and maintain critical risk and fraud-detection applications and services, including idea generation, architecture, design, development, and testing.
*   Design, implement, and manage machine learning-based security solutions to prevent customer attacks, ensuring continuous performance monitoring and issue mitigation.
*   Continuously iterate on machine learning models to enhance precision and recall, optimizing them to meet performance criteria in terms of accuracy and latency.
*   Lead the end-to-end development process to deliver high-quality solutions that meet specifications, align with the architectural vision, and adhere to all applicable standards, covering all phases of solution development.
*   Collaborate with project team members to develop and implement new data-driven AI/ML-based business solutions, working with senior technical staff and Project Managers to identify, document, plan, track, and manage risks and issues.
*   Establish data governance and data management frameworks to efficiently organize and operationalize machine learning pipelines.
*   Construct ETL pipelines using technologies like Spark, Python, HIVE, or Scala to process transaction and account-level data and standardize data fields from diverse sources.
*   Experiment with and develop custom algorithms using state-of-the-art techniques for modeling and scoring, leveraging machine learning, data mining, and statistical methodologies.
*   Communicate status, issues, and risks in a detailed and timely manner to relevant stakeholders.
*   Present technical solutions in business terms to non-technical stakeholders.
*   Provide coaching and mentoring to junior team members to enhance their technical skills and professional development.
*   Drive the development effort end-to-end, collaborating closely with senior technical staff and Project Managers to ensure successful project delivery.
*   Position reports to the Austin, Texas office and may allow for partial telecommuting.
**Qualifications:**
Basic Qualifications:
*   Employer will accept a PhD in Computer Science, Mathematics or related field, and 2 years of experience in the job offered or in a machine learning engineer or research scientist-related occupation.
*   Position requires experience in the following skills:
*   Machine learning;
*   Deep learning;
*   Artificial Intelligence;
*   Generative AI;
*   Statistics;
*   Python;
*   Pytorch;
*   Spark;
*   SQL; and
*   Creating, implementing, and optimizing advanced AI models.
