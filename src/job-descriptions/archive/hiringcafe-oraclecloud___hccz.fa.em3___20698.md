**Role:** Product Owner
**Location: Madrid, Spain**
**Type:** Full-time, Permanent
We are looking for 3 full-time Product Owners to join our growing team developing a cutting-edge, AI-powered platform to improve English communication in the workplace. Each role will focus on a different area of the learner and customer journey and will work cross-functionally with engineering, design, AI researchers, and product leadership.
**About the Roles**
Each Product Owner will own a dedicated part of the product portfolio:
*   **Product Owner – Digital Language Tutor:** Owns the learner’s real-time practice journey through AI conversation.
*   **Product Owner – Dashboards:** Owns data modeling, admin/learner dashboards, and visualisation of learner progress and ROI.
*   **Product Owner – Speaking Assessment:** Leads AI-driven feedback, diagnostics, and learner modelling. Suited to a platform- and data-oriented PO who thrives on algorithmic products and infrastructure logic.
Each PO role comes with strong ownership and the opportunity to shape the product from design through delivery.
**What You'll Do**
*   Translate vision and user needs into clear, actionable product requirements
*   Own and refine the product backlog; define epics and user stories
*   Work closely with tech leads and designers to plan and deliver features
*   Define success metrics, prioritise delivery, and communicate progress
*   Collaborate across AI, UX, learning design, and commercial teams
*   Ensure consistency, scalability, and measurable learner outcomes
**What We’re Looking For**
*   5+ years’ experience as a Product Owner or Product Manager delivering tech products
*   Strong decision-making skills and ownership mindset
*   Confident working with cross-functional teams in an Agile environment
*   Clear, professional communication in English
*   Comfortable navigating ambiguity and shifting priorities
*   Experience with AI, EdTech, or enterprise SaaS is a plus
