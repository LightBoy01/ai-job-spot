**About the Role**
The Business Efficiency Officer will play a key role in shaping and modernising our frontline Waste and Cleansing service, ensuring they deliver best value and reflect the Council’s commitment to putting citizens first. This role leads the design and development of operational and digital solutions that enhance service efficiency, optimise resource use, and improve customer experience across Nottingham City.
The postholder is responsible for the procurement, implementation, and ongoing management of systems and digital platforms that support end-to-end service delivery—from resident engagement to frontline operations. These solutions must be secure, compliant with legislation, and rigorously tested to meet high standards of performance and reliability. By embedding streamlined reporting and response processes, the Business Efficiency Officer ensures services are responsive, data-driven, and aligned with the Council’s strategic priorities.
**About You**
**The ideal candidate will have:**
·         **Proven experience in high-level decision-making and strategic advisory**, with the ability to champion innovative digital solutions and deputise for senior officers when required
·         **Strong project management and analytical skills**, including the ability to scope, design, and deliver technical and non-technical digital projects, while identifying and managing risks, issues, and dependencies.
·         **Demonstrated ability to lead cross-functional collaboration**, working effectively with user researchers, product managers, analysts, and delivery teams to drive service improvements through technology.
·         **Expertise in emerging technologies**, including AI, machine learning, automation, and web bots, with a track record of implementing these tools to enhance customer and colleague experiences
·         **Advanced technical and development skills**, with experience designing and building complex digital solutions, mentoring others, and developing data dashboards to support performance insights and decision-making.
