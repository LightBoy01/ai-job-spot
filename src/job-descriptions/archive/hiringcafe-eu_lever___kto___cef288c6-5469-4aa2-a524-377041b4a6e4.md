Summary We are looking for a Senior AI Video Producer/Editor to join our creative team. This professional will be responsible for applying Artificial Intelligence tools in video creation, developing high-quality prompts, and performing edits when necessary. Additionally, they will work on various audiovisual fronts: from editing and animating diverse content to participating in on-site productions, with responsibilities in coordination and approval. The ideal candidate should have proven experience, a portfolio showcasing AI-driven video projects, and a strong background in audiovisual editing and production. We seek someone with creative autonomy, strategic vision, and the ability to collaborate with different areas of the company.
**Main Responsibilities**
*   Create and develop advanced AI video prompts, ensuring high aesthetic and technical quality.
*   Edit AI-generated or recorded audiovisual content, refining narrative, pacing, and visual language.
*   Edit standalone videos for campaigns, social media, and other brand channels.
*   Perform layout animation and motion graphics when needed.
*   Participate in on-site audiovisual productions, providing creative and technical support.
*   Coordinate and approve audiovisual materials, ensuring consistency with the brand identity.
*   Collaborate with designers, producers, and creatives, bringing innovative solutions.
**Requirements**
*   Proven experience as an editor, filmmaker, or similar role at a senior level.
*   Advanced knowledge of AI video tools (e.g., Runway, Pika, Sora, among others).
*   Portfolio demonstrating creative use of AI in audiovisual projects.
*   Proficiency in video editing software (Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut, or similar).
*   Knowledge of motion design and layout animation.
*   Experience in audiovisual productions with a directorial and coordination perspective.
*   Ability to work autonomously and collaboratively, delivering consistent results.
