As a **Transformation Expert** you are a member of the Telenet Transformation team working on the Telenet Operating Model and Agile Way of Working, both from a design and transformation perspective. You play an active role in ensuring the **Telenet Operating Model** and Agile Way of Working are future-proof, fit for purpose and adopted in the organization. You are part of a small but impactful team of approximately 10 Transformation Experts, reporting to the Agility Support-as-a-Service Manager.
**Your responsibilities as a Transformation Expert**
*   **You design and continuously refine the Telenet Agile Way of Working.**
*   You keep track of trends and evolutions in the domains of Operating models, Digital Transformation, AI, technology, and agile ways of working; and assess the opportunities and implications for Telenet
*   You actively engage with the stakeholder community in the organization and **identify improvement opportunities**. You prioritize next steps and lead the development of guiding principles and working processes.
*   Furthermore, you identify and set up the metrics and indicators that let to measure the adoption and effectiveness of the transformation initiatives
*   **As a Transformation Expert, you take a leading role in the adoption of the Telenet Agile Way of Working,** designing and implementing the most effective change approach, making use of newsletters, trainings, guilds, in-depth coaching, etc.
*   **In case of more disruptive changes which go beyond the continuous refinement of our way of working, you are part of the core Transformation team** actively involved in the design of the **Operating Model** and implementation of these changes.
**What we have to offer**
You will be joining a company that invests fully in you and adapts your work environment optimally to what you need. This allows you to develop while taking care of your work-life balance. What else are we throwing into the fray?
*   You will have every opportunity to further develop your expertise. We stimulate your personal growth with internal mobility opportunities and extensive learning opportunities, but also give you the chance to press the pause button after a while or take a completely different path.
*   A varied job in which you achieve tangible results and make your mark.
*   A competitive gross monthly salary that we complement with benefits (ex. homeworking policy, company car, specific benefits such as meal vouchers, hospitalization insurance, telephone subscription, discounts on Telenet products, smartphone, etc.).
*   The fine company of colleagues who are as enthusiastic as you are.
**Our ideal Transformation Expert**
*   You have a minimum 5 years of demonstrable experience of Transformation Programs aiming at generating flow at enterprise level
*   You have experience with Agile Methodologies for Agile @ Scale (SAFE, Spotify, Less)
*   You are familiar with systemic Operating Model thinking and approaches such as Team Topologies
*   You have experience with Outcome based working
*   You have experience with Data driven transformation, experience with Artificial Intelligence is a plus
*   You're familiar with working with stakeholders at various levels in the organization, ranging from Executive Leadership team to members in a Flow team
*   You have demonstrable experience with how technical Software Development Practices can contribute to generating flow (e.g. CI/CD; test automation; ..)
*   You have a "can do" attitude, and prepared to take ownership
