**Senior Rust Engineer - Fullstack & LLM Evaluation**
**Location:** Remote — US, Canada, Australia, Western Europe (eligible countries only)
**Contract Type:** Contractor (no medical/paid leave)
**Duration:** 1 month (extensions possible)
**Commitment:** Flexible — minimum 10 hrs/week, up to 40 hrs/week (with partial PST overlap required)
**Experience Required:** 5–13 years overall, with 4+ years in Rust
**About the Role:**
We are seeking **Senior Rust Engineers** to contribute to building advanced datasets and evaluation tools that improve how Large Language Models (LLMs) interact with real-world software repositories.
This is a unique opportunity to work at the intersection of **full-stack engineering, open-source ecosystems, and AI research.**
**What You’ll Do:**
*   Work on diverse projects, improving LLM reasoning across real software engineering tasks.
*   Deliver end-to-end use cases (e.g., automation agents, coding copilots, creative assistants).
*   Review and rank multiple model-generated code responses per task.
*   Evaluate code diffs for correctness, maintainability, style, and efficiency.
*   Provide structured feedback and clear rationales for evaluation decisions.
*   Collaborate with AI researchers and engineers on frontier challenges.
**Must-Have Skills:**
*   **5+ years of overall software engineering experience.**
*   **4+ years of hands-on Rust development experience.**
*   Prior full-time experience at a **top-tier tech company** in the US, Canada, Australia, or Western Europe (contractor/part-time experience not eligible).
*   Proven expertise in **full-stack development, software architecture, debugging, and code review practices.**
*   Strong ability to assess and evaluate code diffs for quality and efficiency.
*   LinkedIn profile link (must be a **verified account**).
**Nice-to-Have Skills:**
*   A graduate of a top global university.
*   Open-source contributions or experience with large-scale AI/LLM projects.
**Engagement Details:**
*   **Type:** Contractor (independent, no benefits).
*   **Duration:** 1 month (with potential extensions).
*   **Hours:** 10–40 hrs/week, with partial overlap with Pacific Standard Time.
*   **Eligibility:** Only citizens/GC/valid work permit holders in the US, Canada, Australia, or Western Europe. H-1B not eligible.
