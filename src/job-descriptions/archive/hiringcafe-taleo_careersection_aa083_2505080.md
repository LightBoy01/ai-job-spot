**JOB DESCRIPTION:**

The postdoctoral fellow will conduct, report and present research in his/her field of study under the supervision of a faculty mentor. This is a training position and is not expected to be a permanent position.

**ESSENTIAL JOB FUNCTIONS:**

*   Plan and perform experiments independently
    
*   Prepare reports
    
*   Write protocol(s)
    
*   Interact with research participants and graduate students
    
*   Report data at meetings and present research
    
*   Assist with grant writing
    
*   Disseminate research through manuscript publications
    

**MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS:**

Ph.D., M.D. or Doctoral equivalent in any field and two (2) years of completed Postdoctoral Fellow research experience

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:**

Expertise in engineering/technical sciences related to wearable sensors or home-based ambient sensors, AI/Cloud Computing, or Geriatrics/Aging-in-place with at least two years of completed postdoctoral fellow research. Experience in experiments preferred.

**SALARY:**

Commensurate with experience.
