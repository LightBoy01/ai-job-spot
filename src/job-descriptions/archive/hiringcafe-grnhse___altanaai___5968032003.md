**The Opportunity at Altana**

This role will be responsible for managing and optimizing the underlying data, engineering, and infrastructure supporting Altana’s primary product, the Atlas. Reporting directly to the VP of Engineering, you will lead several teams of talented engineers and data scientists responsible for building the core knowledge graph platform, partner integrations and patterns necessary to power the most comprehensive supply chain understanding in the world.

**Your Responsibilities**

*   Grow, direct and manage a multi-functional team of software engineers, data engineers, and ML engineers
*   Design the organization, process, and tools to achieve the overall goals
*   Engage in partnership integration discussions, lead technical design discussions, and product integrations to help expand Altana’s business offerings
*   Build both a functional production environment, and foster the environment of experimentation and collaboration to push the boundaries of insight capable on top of the supply chain
*   Lead the design, development, and testing of data applications, services, interfaces, ETL processes, reporting, and other workflow initiatives.
*   Partner with our Founders, CTO, and VP of Machine Learning to design and drive the integration of AI and ML algorithms and methodologies into our platform.
*   Create and maintain optimal data pipeline architecture to support the efficient processing of data.
*   Build, scale, and develop a high-functioning platform engineering team, fostering a culture of technical excellence, problem-solving, and collaboration

**About You**

*   Experience building, leading, and developing best-in-class engineering teams, especially in rapidly scaling environment, and have led or been part of a rapidly scaling organization
*   10+ years of real-world experience developing or managing complex engineering systems
*   Track record of success tackling large-scale data engineering, data optimization, and data architecture challenges, particularly in fast-paced ML/AI-enabled products and services
*   Conversant in knowledge graphs, federated learning systems, graph algorithms, deep learning, and network analysis.
*   Experience with large scale data processing systems such as spark, dask/ray, hadoop, etc.
*   A hands-on leader who leads from the front, and is willing to help the team deliver
*   Strong sense of shared ownership and a collaborative disposition
*   Strong written and verbal communication skills
*   Ability to navigate up and down the conceptual stack from customer needs to data storage optimizations
*   You don’t hesitate to go to the dentist during the work day, but you’re also the person that’s on your laptop working on the team’s hardest problems to relax
*   Not required, but a plus if you've worked with supply chain data, company data, have familiarity with B2B economic modeling
