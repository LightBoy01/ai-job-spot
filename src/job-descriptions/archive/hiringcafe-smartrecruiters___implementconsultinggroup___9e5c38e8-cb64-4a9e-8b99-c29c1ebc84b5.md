**Company Description:**
**Job Description:**
Do you want to kickstart your career where technology meets real-world impact? In The Tech Collective (powered by Implement), we are currently seeking junior consultants who want to kick-start their careers by working part-time while they study. If you’re passionate about tech, data, AI, and digital transformation – and want to learn fast while creating tangible client value – we’d love to meet you.
**What you’ll do**
You’ll be part of cross-functional teams delivering high-impact projects across industries, for both public and private clients. Your work will include but not be limited to:
*   Driving digital transformation projects from strategy to implementation
*   Shaping actionable AI and data strategies, use-case discovery, and roadmaps
*   Developing data centric solutions to support the activation af data in new contexts
*   Building proof-of-concepts with generative AI and APIs, and helping integrate solutions into client ecosystems
*   Facilitating workshops, preparing clear executive materials, and enabling leaders to drive change
You’ll start as a generalist with room to explore and the option to grow into tracks such as AI advisory, data architecture, AI engineering, software development, or digital strategy.
**What you bring**
If you’re a nerdy person with a collaborative mindset, we’d love to meet you. And if you’re also proactive, empathetic, and comfortable taking responsibility, you’ll fit right in at The Tech Collective.
On top of that, we’d prefer if you:
*    Are currently studying a relevant bachelor’s or master’s degree (e.g., business, IT, engineering, computer science, or similar)
*   Have strong analytical skills and a genuine interest in how technology drives business value
*   Show curiosity for AI and modern digital solutions (it will be considered a bonus but not a must if you’ve tried LLMs, Python, or cloud tools)
*   Have strong communication skills, with the ability to structure ideas clearly and turn complexity into clarity
**What we offer**
Join a fast-growing collective that blends nerdiness, energy, and a genuine interest in people. You’ll gain early client exposure, a steep learning curve, and close sparring with experienced consultants. We offer internal training, keynotes, and hands-on learning across AI, data, cyber, and digital strategy.
Our culture is built on high autonomy with responsibility, co-creation, and active knowledge sharing. You’ll work on projects across industries and disciplines – from strategy rooms to solution pilots with leading technologies.
**About The Tech Collective**
The Tech Collective is a fast-growing consulting company powered by Implement Consulting Group. We help organisations unlock the potential of data, AI, and analytics to become truly digital by combining cutting-edge tech with human-centric change.
Ready to shape the digital future with us? Please apply before the deadline on October 26th 2025.We’ll get back to you as soon as possible after the application deadline.
