**Business Developers Manager - Paris**
=======================================
Groupon is a marketplace where customers discover new experiences and services everyday and local businesses thrive. To date we have worked with over a million merchant partners worldwide, connecting over 16 million customers with deals across various categories. In a world often dominated by e-commerce giants, we stand out as one of the few platforms uniquely committed to helping local businesses succeed on a performance basis.
Groupon is on a radical journey to transform our business with relentless pursuit of results. Even with thousands of employees spread across multiple continents, we still maintain a culture that inspires innovation, rewards risk-taking and celebrates success. The impact here can be immediate due to our scale and the speed of our transformation. We're a "best of both worlds" kind of company. We're big enough to have the resources and scale, but small enough that a single person has a surprising amount of autonomy and can make a meaningful impact.
At Groupon, we connect people to great local businesses, experiences, and destinations—helping them get the most out of their city 🌆. Our marketplace supports small businesses and strengthens communities by driving traffic, awareness, and revenue through tailored campaigns 💼
As a BD Manager in our Business Development team, you will lead a team of Sales Business Developers responsible for bringing new merchants onto the platform 🤝. You’ll drive performance, coach and mentor your team, and play a key role in scaling our merchant partnerships 📈.
### **🛠️ What You'll Do**
*    Lead, coach, and develop a team of Sales Business Developers to exceed sales targets
*   Monitor performance, pipeline activity, and deal quality
*   Set clear expectations and hold your team accountable for results
*   Drive category knowledge and strategic campaign development
*   Train and support your team with tools, strategy, and insights
*   Share best practices and collaborate across the business
### **🌟We’d love to hear from you if you have**
*    3+ years of experience leading B2B sales teams
*   A proven track record of growing teams and exceeding goals
*   Strong coaching, motivation, performance management, and delegation skills
*   Data-driven decision-making ability
*   Excellent communication, adaptability, and a strong AI mindset
*   A genuine passion for helping local businesses grow
### **🌍 Why Join Groupon?**
*   Work with a fun, diverse, and international team
*   Be part of a company where innovation is encouraged and impact is real
*   Enjoy a flexible, hybrid work model (3 days in our office)
