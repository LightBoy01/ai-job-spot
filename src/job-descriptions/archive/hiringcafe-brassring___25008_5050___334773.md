### Your role
We’re looking for a HOLT Data Products Analyst to:
• Program and maintain standard and bespoke HOLT data files for incorporation in the investment process of clients
• Develop standard access and testing code for delivery to clients alongside the HOLT data
• Conduct quantitative research on HOLT data and alpha signals
• Develop and maintain the HOLT Management Inventive/Governance machine learning algorithms
• Collaborate with the HOLT Model Development group on development of the HOLT model and tools infrastructure
• Collaborate with internal partners across UBS Data Intelligence and UBS as a whole
• Develop subject matter expertise to represent the HOLT Data Products offering to clients

### Your team
You’ll be working in the HOLT Data Products Team which works to help HOLT clients incorporate our industry leading HOLT fundamental dataset into their investment process. You will be working alongside the HOLT Model Development Team which is tasked with maintaining and developing the HOLT Model and Data infrastructure.
