**Our Team**
------------
As a designer at BuildASign, you'd be part of a dynamic creative team that fuels our mission to empower individuals and businesses to share their stories through custom-printed products. We're a leading online custom printing provider, operating not just BuildASign.com, but also EasyCanvasPrints.com, Alliedshirts.com, and our many “On The Cheap” brands, giving us a broad scope for design..Our work spans a wide range of custom products, including signs, banners, flags, magnets, apparel, and home decor, all designed to be personalized by our diverse customer base. Our work spans many marketing channels such as Email, Social, Affiliate Marketing, Search, Display, etc.
We’re looking for a highly skilled Senior Graphic Designer to join our remote creative team. This role is perfect for a proactive designer with strong technical abilities, aesthetic judgment, and the discipline to work independently. You’ll be responsible for producing high-quality marketing assets across digital, email, and website channels. Ideal candidates will also bring skills in 3D design and video editing, and know how to leverage AI tools for faster, smarter workflows
**What You Will Do**
--------------------
Design and execute a wide range of marketing assets, including:
*   Internal and promotional emails
*   Paid and organic social ads
*   SEM/search/display banners
*   Website product and marketing images
*   Develop and maintain reusable templates for seasonal campaigns, emails, and performance assets.
*   Produce high-quality product imagery using compositing techniques.
*   Collaborate with creative leads to ensure consistency with brand standards and campaign goals.
*   Utilize and refine 3D assets for use in static and animated product scenes.
*   Assist in web asset creation, including:
*   Hero images for landing pages and category pages
*   Product imagery across all formats and variations
*   Branded content for use across our sites and marketing campaigns
*   Designer and post-designer scenes and composite visuals
*   Sign collateral, such as downloadable templates, spec sheets, and usage guides
*   Edit or assist with short-form videos for ads or creator partnerships as needed.
*   Incorporate AI tools to increase efficiency, improve image generation, or assist in iteration and ideatio
