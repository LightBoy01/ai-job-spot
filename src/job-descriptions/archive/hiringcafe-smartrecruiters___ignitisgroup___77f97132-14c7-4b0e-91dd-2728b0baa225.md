**Job Description:**
We are currently looking for a new colleague to join the Digitalization and Data Analytics team at the Ignitis Group shared service center. In this role, you’ll be responsible for applying artificial intelligence technologies across the group to enhance its digital maturity and create exceptional experiences for our clients and employees. If that speaks to you, we’d be happy to welcome you to our team!
Take YOUR part in #EnergySmart!
**You will contribute to green and secure energy ecosystem creation by:**
*   Proposing and implementing artificial intelligence ideas that will transform tomorrow's digital customer and employee experiences
*   Developing, enhancing, and deploying automation solutions using artificial intelligence and machine learning methods to analyze data
*   Working with large datasets, including both structured and unstructured data
*   Monitoring market trends, analyzing artificial intelligence application opportunities, and implementing pilot projects for proposed ideas
*   Executing digitization projects of varying scales: from MVP solutions in small teams to broad technology applications at the group level
**Qualifications:**
*   Experience with LLMs and other artificial intelligence solutions, as well as assisting others in utilizing them effectively
*   At least 1 year of experience in project management and know the key actions that help to deliver successful projects
*   Strong programming skills (e.g., Python) for model development, data analysis, machine learning, and working with GenAI libraries
*   At least one year of experience in developing and applying artificial intelligence solutions in business environments
*   An understanding of the value created by rapid prototyping, MVPs, or pilot projects, with direct involvement in their implementation
