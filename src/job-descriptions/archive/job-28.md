# Data Scientist (AI/ML Focus)

### Description

Mind To Machine Connect is a technology consulting firm that helps businesses leverage the power of AI and machine learning. We are looking for a Data Scientist with a strong AI/ML focus to join our team. In this role, you will work with our clients to develop and implement custom machine learning solutions that solve their most pressing business problems. You will have the opportunity to work on a variety of projects, from predictive modeling and forecasting to natural language processing and computer vision.

### Responsibilities

- Work with clients to understand their data and business requirements.
- Develop and implement machine learning models to solve a variety of business problems.
- Analyze and interpret data to identify trends and insights.
- Communicate findings and recommendations to both technical and non-technical audiences.
- Stay up-to-date with the latest trends and technologies in data science and machine learning.

### Qualifications

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Science, Computer Science, or a related field.
- 3+ years of experience in data science or a related role.
- Strong programming skills in Python and experience with machine learning libraries such as scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or PyTorch.
- Proficiency in SQL and experience with relational databases.
- Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
- Strong communication and consulting skills.
