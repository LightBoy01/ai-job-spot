**Job Description:**
**Anticipated Contract End Date/Length:** 3 months
**Work set up:** On-Site
Our client in the global professional services industry is looking for a highly skilled Dialogflow CX Engineer to join a dynamic team supporting a leading client in the TMTS sector. This role is ideal for a developer with deep expertise in Dialogflow CX and a strong background in conversational AI architecture. The successful candidate will contribute to the design, development, and deployment of intelligent virtual agents and conversational interfaces using Google Cloud technologies.
**What you will do:**
*   Design and develop conversational flows using Dialogflow CX.
*   Architect and implement scalable Conversational AI solutions.
*   Integrate Dialogflow agents with backend systems via Apigee and other APIs.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deploy CCAI solutions.
*   Leverage Google Cloud Platform tools to optimize performance and scalability.
*   Apply GenAI capabilities to enhance agent intelligence and user experience.
*   Ensure compliance with security protocols and maintain BPSS clearance standards.
*   Participate in technical interviews and contribute to solution design discussions.
**Qualifications:**
*   Proven experience as a Dialogflow CX Engineer or Conversational AI Developer.
*   Strong proficiency in Dialogflow CX, Google Cloud, Apigee, and CCAI.
*   Familiarity with GenAI and agent-based architectures.
*   Background in software engineering within the Application Server Technologies domain.
*   Ability to work on-site in the UK and comply with BPSS clearance requirements.
*   Excellent communication skills in English and ability to collaborate in a fast-paced environment.
*   Must operate under PAYE via Umbrella structure.
**Additional Information:**
Candidates must be legally authorized to live and work in the country the position is based in, without requiring sponsorship.
