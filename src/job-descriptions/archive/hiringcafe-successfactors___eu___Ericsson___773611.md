**About this opportunity:**
Ready to make your master’s thesis part of the next big leap in supply chain technology? This thesis project offers the chance to explore Agentic AI — autonomous, goal-oriented AI systems — and apply them to revolutionize operational processes. You will focus on enhancing Ericsson’s Excess Management process, a critical initiative currently under development, by using AI-powered process redesign and organizational transformation. Collaborating with supply chain innovators, AI specialists, and strategy leaders, you will bridge academic research with impactful, real-world applications.
**What you will do:**
*   Research best practices for integrating Agentic AI into business processes and organizational design.
*   Analyze current and evolving supply chain workflows, with an emphasis on Excess Management.
*   Identify inefficiencies and propose AI-powered redesign solutions.
*   Contribute to the development of Ericsson’s AI-driven supply chain roadmap.
*   Translate technical AI capabilities into strategic business outcomes.
**The skills you bring:**
*   Strong understanding of supply chain processes and workflows.
*   Knowledge of AI concepts and data-driven applications.
*   Excellent research and analytical skills.
*   Ability to connect technical insights to strategic objectives.
*   Curious and collaborative mindset, with confidence in handling both qualitative and quantitative analysis.
If you’re ready to take your research from theory to impact, apply today and help shape the future of AI-powered supply chain solutions.
**Why join Ericsson?**
At Ericsson, you´ll have an outstanding opportunity. The chance to use your skills and imagination to push the boundaries of what´s possible. To build solutions never seen before to some of the world’s toughest problems. You´ll be challenged, but you won’t be alone. You´ll be joining a team of diverse innovators, all driven to go beyond the status quo to craft what comes next.
