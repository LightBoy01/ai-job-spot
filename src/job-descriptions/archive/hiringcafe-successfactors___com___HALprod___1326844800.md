**Job Duties**

**Role Overview:**
We are looking for a proactive Frontend Developer with strong TypeScript and React skills to join a motivated, focused team bringing AI/agentic integration solutions to life. You will play a key role in building the UI layer that connects agents and LLM integrations to our platform, with a strong emphasis on seamless integration and great user experience.
**Key Responsibilities:**
● Develop and maintain responsive UIs in React (TypeScript), ensuring excellent UX and design consistency.
● Collaborate closely with backend and AI/agentic teams to integrate LLM-powered features into our platform.
● Translate product requirements into clean, maintainable, and scalable code.
● Work proactively with designers and product managers to reﬁne user experience and workﬂows.
● Ensure performance, reliability, and security of the applications.
**Requirements:**
● Strong professional experience with TypeScript and React (7+ years preferred).
● Understanding of RESTful APIs, GraphQL, and integration best practices.
● Knowledge of modern frontend tooling (Webpack, Vite, etc.).
● Proactive mindset with strong communication and problem-solving skills.
● Bachelors degree in Computer Science or similar discipline
**Nice to Have:**
● Experience with Node.js for backend integration.
● Exposure to Angular framework.
● Knowledge of UI/UX design principles and accessibility best practices.
● Familiarity with cloud services (AWS, GCP, Azure).
