# GenAI Model Risk Data Scientist

### Description

Citi is seeking a GenAI Model Risk Data Scientist to join our team in New York, NY. In this critical role, you will be responsible for assessing and managing the risks associated with Generative AI models used across various financial applications. You will develop and implement robust methodologies for model validation, performance monitoring, and risk quantification, ensuring the safe and responsible deployment of cutting-edge AI technologies in a highly regulated environment.

### Responsibilities

- Develop and implement quantitative methodologies for validating Generative AI models, including LLMs and other GenAI techniques.
- Conduct independent model reviews and risk assessments, identifying potential vulnerabilities and biases.
- Design and execute stress testing scenarios to evaluate model performance under extreme conditions.
- Collaborate with model development teams, risk management, and regulatory bodies to ensure compliance.
- Communicate complex model risk concepts and findings to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Stay abreast of the latest advancements in Generative AI and model risk management in finance.

### Qualifications

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in a quantitative field such as Financial Engineering, Data Science, Statistics, or Computer Science.
- 3+ years of experience in model risk management, quantitative analysis, or data science within the financial services industry.
- Strong understanding of Generative AI models (e.g., LLMs, GANs) and their applications.
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Python or R, and experience with relevant libraries.
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
- Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, highly regulated environment.
