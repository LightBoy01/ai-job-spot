**What is the role about ?**
We are seeking a **Senior Manager – Techstack** who will own the design, implementation, and optimization of InMobi’s GTM technology strategy with an **AI-first mindset**. This role will serve as the **architect and operator** of our revenue technology ecosystem, ensuring scalability, automation, and intelligence across the **Lead-to-Cash process**.
You will drive adoption of AI-driven workflows, enable revenue teams with actionable intelligence, and continuously align our RevTech stack to business priorities. This role is pivotal in building the foundation for **predictable revenue growth, forecasting accuracy, and operational efficiency.**
**What will you be doing ?**
1.  **Techstack Strategy & Architecture**
*   Define and execute the **long-term GTM tech strategy**, ensuring scalability, interoperability, and AI-first adoption.
*   Own the end-to-end revenue technology ecosystem (Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, CaptivateIQ, Operative/OMS, analytics tools, AI platforms).
*   Partner with IT, Product, and Security teams to ensure compliance, governance, and cost efficiency.
1.  **AI-First Enablement**
*   Integrate **AI-driven capabilities** into core GTM workflows (forecasting, lead scoring, deal insights, territory planning, churn prediction, incentive optimization).
*   Evaluate and onboard **GenAI and ML-based tools** that improve sales productivity, marketing attribution, and revenue forecasting.
*   Build automation and decisioning layers to eliminate manual effort and enable predictive, self-serve insights for GTM teams.
1.  **Business Partnership**
*   Work closely with Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, and Finance leadership to **translate GTM priorities into tech requirements**.
*   Serve as the **RevTech advisor** to senior leadership, influencing investment decisions with ROI-driven recommendations.
*   Drive adoption through structured change management, ensuring tools deliver business impact (not just reporting).
1.  **Revenue Intelligence & Analytics**
*   Partner with the Revenue Intelligence team to ensure **data flows seamlessly** across systems for accurate dashboards, KPIs, and forecasting.
*   Enable **end-to-end visibility** from lead generation to bookings, renewal, and expansion.
*   Ensure marketing and sales funnel tracking, event ROI, and incentive planning are supported by the techstack.
1.  **Governance & Optimization**
*   Establish **standards, documentation, and governance** for tool usage, ensuring a unified source of truth.
*   Monitor and optimize tool usage, adoption, and spend – ensuring alignment with AOP.
*   Continuously benchmark against best-in-class RevTech stacks in SaaS and AdTech.
**What is expected of you?**
*   10+ years of experience in **Revenue Operations / Sales Ops / Marketing Ops / Business Systems**, with at least 4+ years managing RevTech stack strategy.
*   Proven track record of **driving AI-led automation** in GTM workflows.
*   Deep expertise in Salesforce ecosystem; strong exposure to HubSpot, Outreach, Gong, CaptivateIQ, Operative, or similar.
*   Strong understanding of **lead-to-cash processes** in SaaS/AdTech, including pipeline management, forecasting, commissions, and customer lifecycle.
*   Analytical mindset with ability to evaluate technology ROI and drive adoption across global teams.
*   Strong stakeholder management skills with the ability to influence senior leadership.
*   Exposure to **AI/ML technologies** (predictive analytics, GenAI, workflow automation) is a must.
