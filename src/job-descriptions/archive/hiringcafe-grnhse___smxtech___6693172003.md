SMX is seeking a **Senior AI Integration Specialist** to design, integrate, and optimize AI/ML solutions for Army Intelligence workflows. This role will enhance decision support and automation while ensuring compliance, security, and mission readiness.
**This is a full-time onsite position.**
**Essential Duties & Responsibilities**
AI Solution Design and Implementation
*   Design, develop, and implement AI-based solutions tailored to military intelligence workflows and operational needs.
*   Prototype and validate AI-powered workflows for data analysis, decision support, and automation.
System Integration and Optimization
*   Coordinate with external DoD entities and 3rd party vendors to Integrate AI technologies into existing Army Intelligence systems and digital platforms, ensuring seamless operation and minimal disruption.
*   Optimize AI integrations for performance, scalability, and security within cloud computing environments (AWS, Azure, …).
Collaboration and Stakeholder Engagement
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including military personnel, data scientists, engineers, and intelligence analysts, to translate mission requirements into technical solutions.
*   Liaise between technical teams and military leadership to ensure alignment with operational goals.
Deployment & Monitoring
*   Employ Agile methodologies for project management.
*   Manage deployment pipelines, containerization, and CI/CD workflows for AI services in the cloud.
*   Implement robust monitoring, observability, and performance tracking for AI systems.
Training & Support
*   Provide training and technical support to military personnel to ensure effective adoption and utilization of AI technologies.
*   Document integration architecture, processes, and operational dependencies.
Compliance & Security
*   Ensure compliance with military security, privacy, and governance standards for AI systems.
*   Stay updated on advancements in AI and recommend improvements to existing systems.
**Required Skills, Experience & Education**
Security
*   Active TS security clearance and eligible for SCI and NATO read-on prior to starting work
*   Meet all requirements to receive a privileged user account on a TS/SCI information system (e.g. Army Cloud Computing Service Provider) prior to starting work. The requirements are currently defined in DoDD 8140.01.
Education and Experience
*   Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, or a related technical field.
*   3+ years of experience with AI/ML integration, systems engineering, or software development.
Technical Expertise
*   Strong understanding of AI technologies, machine learning models, and frameworks.
*   Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, Java, or similar.
*   Experience with API integration, cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, …), and workflow orchestration tools
*   Familiarity with containerization (Docker, Kubernetes) and CI/CD pipelines.
Military Operations Knowledge
*   Familiarity with military operations, intelligence workflows, and digital platforms.
*   Ability to work within the constraints of military security and compliance standards.
Problem-Solving & Communication
*   Strong analytical and problem-solving skills to troubleshoot and optimize AI integrations.
*   Excellent communication and interpersonal skills for effective collaboration with diverse teams.
**Desired Skills/Experience**
*   Experience with AI platforms such as OpenAI, Hugging Face, or Amazon SageMaker.
*   Knowledge of MLOps tools and LLM-based applications.
*   Background in data privacy, ethical AI deployment, or regulated industries.
*   AWS Certified Security – Specialty or equivalent cloud certifications.
*   Security+ certified.
