**Job Description:**
We are seeking a **UI/UX Designer** with exceptionally strong **UI design skills**, who thrives in fast-paced, ever-evolving environments and is passionate about creating high-impact digital experiences enhanced by the latest AI design technologies.
At the start, you will focus on designing **installation experiences for a global conference**, producing **compelling generative AI-driven graphics and animations** that engage and inspire. Beyond this, you must also be able to design **best-in-class business intelligence applications** spanning desktop and mobile, ensuring seamless, intuitive, and elegant interfaces that elevate user experience.
This is not a role for traditional designers—we’re looking for someone who moves fast, adapts faster, and consistently delivers cutting-edge design solutions.
**Key Responsibilities**
*   Create installation design experiences for a global conference, including generative AI graphics and animations.
*   Support visually engaging marketing deliverables (e.g., product mockups, feature highlights, social media assets, event visuals) using Figma, Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, Canva, and generative AI tools.
*   Design modern, visually striking, user-centered interfaces with a strong emphasis on UI quality and detail.
*   Design best-in-class business intelligence applications across desktop and mobile, ensuring usability and scalability.
*   Translate complex problems and user needs into wireframes, mockups, prototypes, and polished UI designs.
*   Rapidly prototype product concepts using Figma and AI tools (e.g., Sora, RunwayML, Midjourney).
*   Collaborate with product and engineering teams.
*   Conduct user research and usability testing, incorporating insights into design iterations.
*   Contribute to and advance design systems, ensuring consistency across platforms.
**Required Qualifications**
*   2+ years of experience in UI/UX or Product Design with a portfolio highlighting outstanding **UI design skills** across desktop and mobile applications.
*   Advanced proficiency in Figma and familiarity with motion/video tooling (e.g., After Effects, Lottie, Runway).
*   Hands-on experience with generative AI tools (Midjourney, RunwayML, Sora, or similar).
*   Strong grasp of accessibility, responsive design, and modern UX principles.
*   Experience designing data-rich, enterprise, or business intelligence applications is highly valued.
*   Comfortable working in agile, iterative environments with the ability to pivot quickly while maintaining design excellence.
*   Excellent communication skills with the ability to clearly explain design decisions and collaborate across functions.
**Preferred Qualifications**
*   Excellent communication skills and proven ability to collaborate effectively within a multinational team operating across three time zones.
*   Motion, video, or 3D design expertise (e.g., WebGL, Spline, Unreal Engine).
*   Background in enterprise SaaS or analytics platforms.
*   Passion for AI and Bitcoin.
*   Work onsite from the Warsaw office 3 days a week.
