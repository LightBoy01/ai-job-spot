**Let's start with the role**
As a **Product Owner** of one of our AI product teams, you will drive the vision and strategy for new and existing products that the team is responsible for. You will support the development of AI-powered products by applying product and agile best practices to maximize the business value of the team's deliverables. This is a unique role that combines product ownership skills with knowledge of the machine learning / AI product lifecycle.
**As an AI Product Owner you will:**
*   Contribute to the product vision and strategy;
*   Collaborate with stakeholders to obtain domain expertise and gather business and customer requirements;
*   Create and maintain the ΑΙ roadmap, ensuring impact-driven prioritization, problem validation and alignment with internal stakeholders;
*   Facilitate and lead the discovery process;
*   Define and track measures of success in conjunction with product analysts;
*   Provide vision and direction to the Agile development team;
*   Create PRDs, epics, experiments and associated documentation;
*   Manage the product backlog and team priorities;
*   Conduct market research and competitive analysis to identify trends and opportunities;
*   Support the team and organization by applying both product and agile best practices;
*   Communicate product updates.
**What you’ll bring**
*   Working experience in a product role within technology, ideally in a B2C online business;
*   Understanding of the machine learning/AI product lifecycle;
*   Solid experience with agile methodologies;
*   Proven ability to define a set of requirements that pinpoint customer/business challenges and identify improvements to address them;
*   Ability to communicate clearly and concisely across technology and business teams;
*   Previous experience with product lean startup or design thinking will be considered a plus;
