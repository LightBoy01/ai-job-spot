**Job Description:**
**Job Description:**
We are driving innovation in artificial intelligence by building scalable, intelligent solutions that empower businesses to solve complex problems. We are seeking a **Principal Test Engineer with deep hands-on experience in AI testing** to lead quality assurance efforts, improve model performance, and ensure robust, trustworthy AI solutions.
**Key Responsibilities:**
*   Lead the development of comprehensive test strategies and frameworks for AI-powered systems, models, and applications.
*   Collaborate with AI research teams, data engineers, and software developers to define test requirements and coverage for AI models and data pipelines.
*   Design and implement test cases that validate model accuracy, fairness, robustness, interpretability, and edge-case handling.
*   Identify, troubleshoot, and resolve issues related to data quality, model drift, and performance degradation.
*   Develop automated testing pipelines that integrate with continuous integration and delivery (CI/CD) environments.
*   Ensure that AI systems meet ethical, compliance, and privacy standards through rigorous validation and testing.
*   Mentor and coach team members in AI testing methodologies and best practices.
*   Evaluate and recommend tools, platforms, and frameworks that enhance AI testing capabilities.
*   Stay updated with industry trends, emerging AI technologies, and testing innovations to continuously improve testing stratey
*   Drive and take ownership of Stress, Scalability, and Performance (SSP) testing, including planning, execution, defect management, and reporting.
*   Lead the design, development, and maintenance of operations dashboards for web applications to ensure performance visibility and actionable insights.
**Qualifications:**
**Qualifications:**
*   Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, or a related field.
*   8+ years of experience in software testing, with at least 3 years focused on AI system testing.
*   Hands-on experience testing machine learning and AI models, including validation of datasets, algorithms, and model outputs.
*   Strong knowledge of AI techniques such as supervised learning, unsupervised learning, neural networks, and reinforcement learning.
*   Familiarity with AI frameworks such as PyTorch, scikit-learn, or equivalent.
*   Proficiency in scripting languages like Python, R, or others used in AI testing.
*   Understanding of metrics for model evaluation (precision, recall, F1 score, AUC, etc.).
*   Experience with automation frameworks, test orchestration tools, and cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP).
*   Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
**Preferred Qualifications:**
*   Experience with bias detection and mitigation, fairness testing, and explainability frameworks.
*   Knowledge of MLOps and embedding testing within AI deployment pipelines.
*   Familiarity with regulatory requirements related to AI systems
**Work Environment:**
You will be part of a fast-paced, collaborative environment where quality is a shared responsibility. This role provides opportunities to define testing best practices across the organization and influence the technical roadmap for QA.
