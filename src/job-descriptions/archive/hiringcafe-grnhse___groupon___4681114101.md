_Rebuild the Future of Local Commerce with AI_
At Groupon, we’re in full transformation mode. Our mission: become a true AI-First company, where every customer, merchant, and employee feels the impact of intelligent automation.
**The Opportunity:**
We’re seeking a hands-on Director of AI and Automation to drive the design, build, and scaling of real-world AI applications. You’ll launch projects that boost retention and experience for millions of users and merchants, while fast-tracking automation throughout our business.
**Where You’ll Have Impact:**
*   **Lead and execute** our global AI strategy—prioritize what really works, pilot, scale, and measure success.
*   **Own delivery** of AI-powered solutions: from NLP and automated recommendations to cutting-edge workflow automation and agent orchestration.
*   **Work cross-functionally** with agile teams in Product, Engineering, Data, Infrastructure, and Ops to turn business needs into shipped solutions.
*   **Champion responsible AI:** ensure every solution is secure, GDPR/AI Act-compliant, and measurable in ROI.
*   **Drive transformation:** Set up robust frameworks for experimentation, evaluation, and rapid scaling of what delivers real value.
**About You:**
*   8+ years hands-on experience in real-world AI/ML delivery (production systems, not just models or prototypes)
*   Strong background in both process automation and machine learning—leveraging platforms such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, UiPath, Blue Prism, RPA
*   Cloud ecosystem fluency: GCP, AWS
*   Experience with modern AI tooling (vector stores, RAG frameworks, agent orchestration)
*   Bias for action and measurable business results—not AI “slideware”
*   Thrive leading lean, agile project teams and influencing cross-company stakeholders
*   Fluent English; able to collaborate across the globe, with major locations in the USA, Europe and India
**Why Groupon?**
*   Real executive sponsorship for AI-First transformation
*   Build what matters: your work will be shipped, used, and matter from day one
*   Move at scale-up speed, not corporate pace—your ideas will shape the company
*   High autonomy, smart collaborators, global reach
