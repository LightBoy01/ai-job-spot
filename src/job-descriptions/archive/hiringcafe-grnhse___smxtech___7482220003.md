The Naval Postgraduate School (NPS) is seeking a motivated and enthusiastic Junior AI Application Developer to support the design, development, and deployment of intelligent applications. This role is ideal for recent graduates or early-career professionals who are eager to apply their programming and machine learning skills while continuing to learn and grow in a fast-paced environment. The ideal candidate is curious, collaborative, and passionate about applying AI to solve real-world problems.
**This is a temporary position.**
**Essential Duties & Responsibilities**
*   Design, develop, and implement AI-based applications from concept to deployment.
*   Build, train, and evaluate machine learning and deep learning models.
*   Collect, preprocess, and analyze large datasets to ensure data quality.
*   Integrate AI models into production systems using APIs, microservices, and cloud platforms.
*   Collaborate with product managers, data engineers, and domain experts to deliver AI solutions aligned with business goals.
*   Monitor model performance, retrain, and optimize for accuracy, scalability, and efficiency.
*   Stay current with emerging AI technologies, tools, and best practices.
*   Ensure AI solutions follow ethical, responsible, and secure AI principles.
**Required Skills & Experience**
*   Education: Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Data Science, Engineering, or related field (or equivalent practical experience).
*   Programming: Strong foundation in Python; familiarity with Java, C++, or JavaScript is a plus.
*   Mathematics & Statistics: Basic understanding of linear algebra, calculus, and probability/statistics.
*   Machine Learning: Coursework or project experience with supervised/unsupervised learning, or exposure to NLP/computer vision.
*   Frameworks: Familiarity with at least one ML library such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, or scikit-learn.
*   Data Handling: Exposure to SQL and data manipulation tools (Pandas, NumPy).
*   Collaboration: Strong communication skills and eagerness to learn from peers and mentors.
**Desired Skills & Experience**
*   Internship, project, or coursework involving AI/ML.
*   Familiarity with version control (Git).
*   Exposure to cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure).
*   Basic knowledge of APIs and web services.
*   Working Knowledge of Wargaming and other DoD concepts uses of AI applications
