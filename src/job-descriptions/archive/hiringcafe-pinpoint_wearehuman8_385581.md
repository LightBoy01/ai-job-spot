If you are driven by a deep curiosity about human behavior and are motivated to turn data into a decisive marketing strategy, join us as an Insight Manager at Human8.
You will be the architect of insight - transforming numbers into compelling narratives and recommendations that guide our clients. Success requires fluent management of diverse projects and clients, all while coordinating effectively with your team.
We are a team of collaborators and are looking for individuals who find their energy in working closely with others to create great work and achieve shared goals.
_Kindly note that we are part of a global team, please submit your CV in English._
Key Responsibilities
--------------------
**Impactful client delivery**
*   You’re able to interpret client needs and different sources of information to come up with a sharp and focused project design. You collaborate with clients to ensure we’ll capture data and insights that will meet their objectives.
*   You have a good understanding of various quantitative research methodologies. You know how to apply these to get rich and relevant insights.
*   You are an independent project manager and proactive communicator. You thrive in knowing the ins and outs and push the project forward.
*   You plan and coordinate data and insight analyses and take the lead in the interpretation and reporting on the findings of quantitative research projects.
*   You are comfortable to support seniors in translating your insights and recommendations into tangible marketing deliverables.
*   You are a storyteller. You can shape the insights into compelling outputs that resonate with client audiences.
*   You actively use AI to work more efficiently and effectively. You learn how to use AI in a business relevant way, to contextualize your work and add value to the client.
*   You can use different resources and apply behavioural frameworks and models. You can use these to contextualize client briefs and deliverables.
*   You showcase excellent presentation skills and you’re able to prepare client workshops / brainstorms and attend in a moderating or supportive role.
**Account & commercial Management**
*   You’re excellent at building good working relationships with clients and you learn to flex style as needed.
*   You’re attentive to client needs and are eager to know their business and industry better.
*   You understand our unique selling arguments of our solutions and supportive methods we offer and how we position vs. competitors.
*   You are excited to start writing proposals for your clients or support your senior team members in doing so.
Skills, Knowledge and Expertise
-------------------------------
*   A bachelor's degree in a related field (e.g., social science, psychology, business, economics) or equivalent practical experience.
*   At least 5 years of professional research experience.
*   **Curiosity and Eagerness**: A desire to learn and understand complex human behavior.
*   **Proactive and organized:** Being able to juggle a lot of moving parts.
*   **Communication**: Clear and pro-active communication within the team and towards clients.
*   **Adaptability**: Willingness to adapt to changing project requirements and rapidly evolving industry.
*   **Analytical:** A high affinity with data interpretation and analysis.
*   **Innovative**: Actively experimenting with AI, eager to learn and use new technologies to bring added value for the client.
*   **Collaborative**: You love to achieve and win together!
Benefits
--------
*   You will join a pioneering company in a dynamic and innovative industry that is re-inventing itself.
*   You will grow as a marketing insights consultant, and thrive on the cross road of AI capabilities and deep human understanding.
*   Aspirational brands will seek your guidance.
*   Competitive salary and local benefits.
