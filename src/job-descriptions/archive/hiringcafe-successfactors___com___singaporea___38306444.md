**Job Description**
-------------------
The successful incumber will be part of the Information Security Department withing the Information Technology Division.
**Key Responsibilities**
*   Design and implement security automation workflows to improve efficiency, consistency, and scalability across engineering and operations.
*   Leverage Generative AI to support automation, accelerate decision-making, and improve outcomes across multiple Information Security domains.
*   Develop and integrate cloud-native security controls, ensuring alignment with enterprise architecture, governance, and operational requirements.
*   Contribute to DevSecOps practices and collaborate with offshore development teams to support secure development environments.
*   Lead end-to-end deployment of security tools, including requirement gathering, RFQ development, vendor evaluation, proof-of-concept (POC), implementation, and handover.
*   Maintain and enhance cybersecurity toolsets through regular tuning, upgrades, and lifecycle refresh planning.
*   Support ad-hoc assignments as required by the Cyber Engineering or wider Information Security team.
**Requirements**
*   Degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related discipline.
*   At least 5 years of experience in cybersecurity engineering or equivalent roles.
*   Experience with scripting and automation (e.g., Python, PowerShell, Bash).
*   Hands-on knowledge of cloud platforms and cloud-native security controls.
*   Familiarity with Generative AI tools and their application in security contexts.
*   Experience in managing cybersecurity tool lifecycles and leading solution deployments, including RFQs.
*   Experience working in DevSecOps environments and with offshore development teams is an advantage.
*   Industry certifications such as CISSP, AWS Security Specialty, or equivalent are preferred.
*   Strong interpersonal skills and the ability to work independently across multiple teams.
