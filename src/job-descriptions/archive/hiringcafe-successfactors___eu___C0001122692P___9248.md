**Thesis opportunity at DeLaval!**
==================================
Would you like to write your thesis in collaboration with a leading company in milking equipment and solutions? We are now offering an exciting opportunity for students who want to put their theoretical knowledge into practice and contribute to the development of DeLaval.
**About the thesis project
**We have identified a few possible thesis areas within Project management, production engineering, data science and software/embedded engineering, such as:
*   Trends in project management and project work – what new methods and approaches exist, and which of these would suit us?
However, we are also open to shaping the scope together with you, depending on your background, interests, and academic requirements. Our aim is to create a project that is valuable both for your studies and for us as a company.
**We are looking for students who:**
*   Are currently studying within engineering at a university
*   Have an interest in production, AI, automation and/or robotics
*   Are analytical, curious, and able to work independently
**Application**
Please send your CV, a short personal introduction, and if possible, a brief outline of how you would approach the project.
We look forward to hearing from you!
**About DeLaval**
Our vision is to make sustainable food production possible. As a company built on innovation, we constantly work to find ways of helping our customers, dairy farmers, do more with less by providing world-leading milking equipment and solutions.
Today, DeLaval has approximately 4,500 passionate professionals operating in more than 100 markets around the world. DeLaval is part of the Tetra Laval Group. See more at corporate.delaval.co
