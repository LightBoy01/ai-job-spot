Hey there!
We’re [Fever](https://feverup.com/), the world’s leading tech platform for culture and live entertainment,
Our mission? To democratize access to culture and entertainment. With our proprietary cutting-edge technology and data-driven approach, we’re revolutionizing the way people engage with live entertainment.
Every month, our platform inspires over 300 million people in +40 countries (and counting) to discover unforgettable experiences while also empowering event creators with our data and technology, helping them scale, innovate, and enhance their events to reach new audiences.
Our results? We’ve teamed up with major industry leaders like Netflix, F.C. Barcelona, and Primavera Sound, presented international award-winning experiences, and are backed by several leading global investors! Impressive, right?
To achieve our mission, we are looking for bar-raisers with a hands-on mindset who are eager to help shape the future of entertainment!
Ready to be part of the experience?
Now, let’s discuss this role and what you will do to help achieve Fever’s mission.
**About the role**
*   Lead and scale a high-performing team of creatives focused on digital marketing content.
*   Mentor and supervise team members to accelerate their creative growth and technical mastery across design, video, and AI tools.
*   Drive creative ideation and asset execution for paid social campaigns (Meta, TikTok, YouTube, and more), with a strong focus on performance and speed.
*   Develop and maintain scalable creative systems (AI workflows, asset libraries, modular templates, versioning protocols).
*   Use data and testing results to build a fast iteration loop, optimizing visuals and narratives based on actual campaign performance.
*   Act as the internal reference for AI creative tools and workflows (Runway, Sora, Midjourney, Veo, ChatGPT, etc.), fostering experimentation and training others.
*   Collaborate cross-functionally with internal teams to align creative execution with growth objectives and KPIs.
*   Build creative frameworks that balance quality, speed, and results.
**About you:**
*   6+ years of professional experience in performance-focused creative production - digital design, video editing, or creative teams, focused on creativity, growth and efficiency.
*   Fluent in English and Spanish (additional languages are a plus).
*   Strong creative leadership experience: mentoring, managing feedback loops, and scaling teams with a growth mindset.
*   Advanced knowledge of Adobe Creative Suite, Figma, and AI design tools (Midjourney, Runway, Sora, etc.).
*   Experience creating assets for high-performance digital campaigns on Meta, TikTok, and YouTube.
*   You thrive in fast-paced, iterative, data-driven environments (agency, startup, or in-house growth team experience is a plus).
*   Solid understanding of analytics and creative testing (CTR, hook rate, retention curves, etc.) and how they inform creative decisions.
*   Experience building and maintaining scalable design systems and asset management libraries (LucidLink, Cloudinary, etc.).
**You will thrive here if you:**
*   Think like a growth strategist but create like a top-tier designer
*   Have a passion for turning performance insights into better, smarter creative.
*   Can mentor profiles with empathy and high standards.
*   Know how to deliver high-impact visuals at scale without burning out your team.
*   Enjoy experimenting with AI tools and driving creative innovation across formats.
*   Are obsessed with testing and always looking for ways to optimize
*   Can balance creative excellence with tight deadlines and shifting priorities.
*   Feel confident setting clear creative goals, giving structured feedback, and aligning stakeholders around a shared vision.
