Inviting applications for the role of **Vice President- Responsible AI – Agentic AI!**
Overview
In this role, you'll be part of Genpact's transformation under Genpact Next, as we lead the shift to Agentic AI Solutions—domain-specific, autonomous systems that redefine how we deliver value to clients. You'll help drive the adoption of innovations like the Genpact AP Suite in finance and accounting, with more Agentic AI products set to expand across service lines.
As the Leader of Responsible AI (RAI), this role will strategically shape and grow Genpact’s Responsible AI practice, elevating our brand’s visibility and establishing thought leadership in ethical AI applications and building assets that are integral to our AI solutions. This role will collaborate across functions to embed responsible AI principles into client projects, increasing our competitive edge and client success rates.
Responsibilities:
Develop and lead a scalable RAI strategy aligned with Genpact’s values and market positioning
Build, mentor, and lead a specialized team focused on creating and managing RAI assets, methodologies and frameworks
Drive thought leadership by publishing high-quality insights, presenting at industry events, and actively engaging with academic and industry communities (e.g., tech partner RAI leaders, standards setting government agencies, think tanks and trade organizations leading industry practices and benchmarks)
Collaborate with sales, solutions and delivery teams to enhance client proposals and engagements, improving win rates and client satisfaction
Build and embed Genpact RAI into our solutions, working with Advanced Technology Solutions teams
Foster a collaborative culture that prioritizes Genpact-wide growth and knowledge sharing of RAI work working closely with the Strategy, Agentic AI, Technology & Innovation and Legal teams
Monitor the RAI environment and report our work in progress to the Responsible AI Committee
Qualifications we seek in you!
Minimum Qualifications
experience in AI, ethics, or related technology advisory and/or applied research role
Proven track record in developing and implementing RAI strategies, tools, and frameworks
Demonstrated ability to engage with senior stakeholders and external clients effectively
Strong or rising industry presence, including thought leadership through publications, speaking engagements, or professional forums
Exceptional interpersonal skills with a collaborative, team-first mindset
