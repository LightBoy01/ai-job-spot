Department Description
----------------------
You will be part of the Data and Reporting Governance AT team, a key department within the Data Management AT area at Erste Bank. We are looking for a motivated professional to help shape the future of AI governance across Erste Bank and the Sparkassen network.
**Your Tasks**
--------------
*   Lead the implementation of an AI Governance Framework across Erste Bank and Sparkassen
*   Assess (Generative) AI models and applications in alignment with internal governance standards, regulatory requirements, and ethical guidelines
*   Ensure AI usage complies with Erste Group’s risk profile
*   Perform control and oversight duties as part of a second line of defense function, including establishing regular monitoring mechanisms
*   Review and validate AI initiatives, delivering clear assessments and governance outcomes to stakeholders
*   Foster cross-functional collaboration with internal teams such as Data Scientists, Engineers, and Business Units
**Your Background**
-------------------
*   University degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field
*   Solid experience with Generative AI models and a strong understanding of the AI/model development lifecycle, including explainability and monitoring
*   Minimum of 3 years of relevant professional experience
*   Excellent communication skills, with the ability to engage both technical and non-technical stakeholders
*   Strong analytical mindset with the ability to identify risks and clearly explain complex topics
*   Proactive and self-driven approach to learning and knowledge sharing
*   Responsible and growth-oriented attitude
*   Fluency in German and English; knowledge of a CEE language is a plus
