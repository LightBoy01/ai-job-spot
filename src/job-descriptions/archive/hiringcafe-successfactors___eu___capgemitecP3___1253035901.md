**What you will be doing ?**
----------------------------
We are seeking to grow our Customer Transformation consulting practice by hiring a dynamic **Marketing Strategy & Transformation Manager** to help us lead and accelerate the growth of Martech enabled transformation and address the growing complexity of AI Marketing solutions. The individual we are looking for must have a sound understanding of **Marketing or Advertising Technology** with a proven ability to shape, sell and deliver innovative marketing strategy programmes of work.
You should have some of the following experience:
*   10 + experience, preferably in a consultancy and/or agency environment.
*   Degree educated or equivalent.
*   Previous experience in shaping, selling and delivering Marketing Transformations.
*   You must have broad MarTech or AdTech knowledge meaning you’re capable of delivering Marketing Technology solutions to clients.
*   You understand how to turn a set of business objectives into capabilities and map those capabilities to technical solutions.
*   You must also have hands on experience in driving digital transformation more broadly, in areas such as building marketing strategies, designing future-fit marketing operations, and activating content, data & technology to deliver growth.
**An overview of the role:**
----------------------------
This role is an exciting blend of management consulting skills, mixed with Marketing Technology, Strategy and Operations.
*   You will define modern Marketing Technology and Advertising Technology (Martech) ecosystems for clients, delivering impactful solutions that improve ROI and enable their marketing strategies, whether focused on productivity, optimisation, or engagement.
*   You will advise tier-one organizations at the cutting edge of the industry, delivering and embedding Marketing Services and Martech capabilities alongside supporting processes, operating models, and governance frameworks.
*   You will analyze and optimize clients’ Marketing Services and Martech stacks, identifying inefficiencies and designing solutions to enhance marketing operations and maximize ROI.
*   You will provide objective insights into the strengths and weaknesses of products, tools, and approaches within the Marketing Services and Martech space.
*   You will ensure you are informed of best practices, emerging technology trends, and innovations in digital media, data integration, data platforms, marketing automation, digital asset management, content management, and social media.
*   You will help organisations unlock the potential of AI by defining pilots, scaling approaches, and embedding AI into their marketing practices.
*   You will have industry experience in one or of more of: Financial Services, Consumer Products, Retail, Technology, Utilities, Telco, Public Sector
