**Our Team**
A Photography Assistant plays a vital role in supporting photographers throughout the creative and technical process of a shoot. From preparing products and props to organizing equipment, setting up the studio, and managing logistics, the assistant ensures that everything runs smoothly behind the scenes.
Beyond execution, the role requires attention to detail, adaptability, and teamwork. Today, curiosity about new tools such as Artificial Intelligence also adds value, opening new ways to enhance efficiency, creativity, and innovation within the photography workflow
**What you will do**
● Assist the Photographer throughout the shoot process (studio and lifestyle).
● Preparation of products for shooting (steaming, ironing, ensuring compliance with delivery forms).
● Props management: selection, booking, and coordination of delivery/returns with the warehouse.
● Studio setup & styling assistance, ensuring smooth execution during shoots.
● Product identification and shoot scripting during sessions.
● Maintain studio organization and manage equipment inventory.
● Handle logistics during lifestyle shoots (transportation, equipment, props, and production-related tasks).
● Contribute to reviewing briefs with the photographer and supervisor.
● Project coordination using tools such as Jira.
● Prepare keywords and file naming conventions for image management.
● Upload and manage assets on OTMM and Box platforms.
● Perform basic photography tasks (shooting Tier4/Tier6 images, cropping, color correction, silhouetting, and basic retouching)
● Stay engaged in AI-driven tools and experiment with how they can support or optimize photography and post-production processes
**Your Qualifications**
● Strong attention to detail and organizational skills
● Basic photography and retouching knowledge
● Ability to work collaboratively within a fast-paced studio environment
● Familiarity with project management tools (e.g., Jira) is a plus
● Curiosity and openness to learning about AI applications in photography, from generating creative concepts to streamlining workflows
