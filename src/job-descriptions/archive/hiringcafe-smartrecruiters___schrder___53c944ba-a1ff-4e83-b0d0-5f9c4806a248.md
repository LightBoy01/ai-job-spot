**Job Description:**
We are seeking a versatile **Full-Stack Engineer** to join our team. This role combines robust software engineering skills with a growing focus on **AI development**, offering the opportunity to work across the stack—from front-end interfaces to back-end services and intelligent systems.
You will be building scalable applications using **.NET, Python, and Angular**, while also contributing to the design and implementation of **AI-powered features** that enhance our products and services.
**Key Responsibilities**
*   Design, develop, and maintain full-stack web applications using **Angular (front-end)** and **.NET/Python (back-end)**.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define, design, and ship new features.
*   Integrate AI/ML models into production systems, working closely with data scientists and AI engineers.
*   Package, deploy, and manage applications using Docker and containerization best practices.
*   Build and consume RESTful APIs and microservices.
*   Ensure code quality through unit testing, integration testing, and code reviews.
*   Optimize application performance and scalability.
*   Stay current with emerging technologies and propose innovative solutions.
**Qualifications:**
*   Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field.
*   3+ years of experience in full-stack development.
*   Strong proficiency in:
*   **Front-end:** Angular, TypeScript, HTML/CSS
*   **Back-end:** .NET (C#), Python
*   **Databases:** SQL Server, PostgreSQL, Mongo, or similar
*   Experience with AI/ML frameworks (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn).
*   Familiarity with cloud platforms (Azure, AWS).
*   Understanding of CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices.
**Required Soft Skills**
*   **Problem-solving mindset:** Ability to break down complex challenges and propose effective solutions.
*   **Communication skills:** Clear and concise communication with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
*   **Team collaboration:** Strong interpersonal skills and a collaborative attitude in cross-functional teams.
*   **Adaptability:** Comfortable working in a fast-paced, evolving environment with changing priorities.
*   **Curiosity and learning agility:** Eagerness to explore new technologies, especially in AI and machine learning.
*   **Accountability:** Ownership of tasks and commitment to delivering high-quality work.
*   **Nice to Have**
*   Experience deploying AI models in production environments.
*   Knowledge of MLOps practices.
*   Exposure to data engineering and data pipelines.
*   Contributions to open-source projects or technical communities.
*   IoT Protocols & Messaging: Experience with protocols like MQTT, AMQP, CoAP, or WebSockets for device-to-cloud and device-to-device communication.
