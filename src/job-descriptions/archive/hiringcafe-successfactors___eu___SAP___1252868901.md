**About the Team**
We’re the AI Incubation team, a global group that thrives on a mix of on-site collaboration and virtual teamwork. Our culture is open, fast-paced, and innovative, with a strong focus on learning, experimentation, and rapid delivery. To accelerate our mission, we are looking for a seasoned and pragmatic Senior Developer to significantly increase our engineering horsepower and deliver a continuous stream of AI solutions.
**What You’ll Do**
As a Senior Developer, you will be a core engine of our technical delivery. Your primary mission is to write excellent code and build robust, incremental AI solutions. You will be a hands-on practitioner, responsible for turning our team's innovative designs and research into functional, high-quality software at a rapid pace.
In this role, you’ll:
*   **Develop and Implement AI Solutions:** Focus on hands-on development to build, test, and refine high-impact AI prototypes, with a strong emphasis on Generative AI, LLMs, and multimodal systems.
*   **Write Exceptional Code:** Consistently deliver clean, efficient, and scalable code, primarily in Python, that serves as a benchmark for quality on the team.
*   **Deliver End-to-End Functionality:** Take well-defined features and requirements and implement them from the data pipeline through to the backend service, ensuring a fully functional result.
*   **Rapidly Iterate and Deliver:** Work in fast-paced sprints to build and release incremental improvements and features, transforming research findings into tangible application updates.
*   **Collaborate on Technical Solutions:** Work closely with architects and researchers to understand requirements and provide expert input on implementation details, ensuring our solutions are robust and well-crafted.
*   **Contribute to Code Quality:** Participate actively in code reviews, offering constructive feedback and embracing opportunities to improve the team's collective codebase.
*   **Deploy and Maintain Applications:** Utilize your expertise in cloud platforms and containerization to deploy, monitor, and maintain our applications and experiments.
**Who You Are**
We are looking for a hands-on, pragmatic, and experienced developer who is passionate about building with AI. You are a natural problem-solver who thrives on writing code and seeing it come to life in a dynamic, forward-thinking environment.
*   **An Experienced Coder:** You have significant professional experience in software development, with proven expertise in **Python** and its ecosystem.
*   **A Hands-on AI Practitioner:** You have demonstrable experience building and working with Machine Learning models and systems. Direct experience with **Large Language Models (LLMs), retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and frameworks like LangChain, TensorFlow, or PyTorch** is a major plus.
*   **A Cloud Native:** You possess strong experience with at least one major cloud platform (**AWS, GCP, or Azure**) and are proficient with containerization technologies (**Docker, Kubernetes**).
*   **A Pragmatic Builder:** You are a ‘doer’ at heart. You thrive on turning complex requirements into simple, elegant, and working code. You have a strong "get it done" attitude.
*   **A Collaborative Teammate:** You have excellent communication skills in **English** and are a strong individual contributor who also excels in a team environment. You enjoy sharing knowledge and helping the team succeed through your work.
*   **Pragmatic and Adaptable:** You are comfortable with ambiguity and can navigate a rapidly evolving technology landscape, making smart implementation decisions to move projects forward.
**What You'll Gain in This Role**
*   **Deep, Hands-on Experience:** The opportunity to focus purely on building with the latest advancements in AI before they become mainstream.
*   **Tangible Impact:** A key role where your direct contribution of code translates immediately into progress and functional prototypes.
*   **A Culture of Speed:** A collaborative, low-bureaucracy environment where you are empowered to build, ship, and iterate quickly without being bogged down.
*   **Continuous Learning:** Stay at the peak of your technical craft by working alongside top researchers and engineers on novel challenges.
