**Senior Director – Data & AI product management Lead**
**Barcelona, Spain.**
**Role Overview**
Lead the global Data + AI product portfolio with a strategic, product-driven mindset. This role bridges business needs and technical delivery, ensuring impactful, scalable solutions that drive measurable value across the enterprise.
**Your Key Responsibilities**
*   Define and maintain the integrated roadmap of Data + AI products and initiatives. Engage with the business platforms to identify opportunities, scope projects, and prioritize based on strategic impact and feasibility.
*   Oversee the execution of Data + AI projects from inception to delivery. Set up proper project governance, define milestones, success criteria, and risk mitigation plans.
*   Ensure each data/AI product is built on a sound solution architecture and adheres to enterprise standards. Review solution designs for scalability, security, and integration with existing systems.
*   Coordinate resource deployment across Data + AI teams. Interface with peer leaders to understand capacity and assign teams to projects.
*   Guide teams to develop data/AI solutions with a product mindset. Establish principles of product management, encourage building reusable components, and ensure considerations like user experience and change management.
*   Serve as the primary liaison between Data + AI delivery teams and business stakeholders. Manage expectations, gather feedback, and ensure satisfaction.
**We offer.**
*   End-to-end ownership – shape and deliver the full lifecycle of Data + AI products across diverse business platforms.
*   Strategic influence – work at the intersection of business and technology, guiding high-impact initiatives from vision to value.
*   A global playground – lead cross-functional teams and collaborate with internal and external partners on cutting-edge solutions.
*   A culture of innovation – explore emerging technologies and drive adoption of modern tools and practices.
*   Growth through leadership – develop your expertise while mentoring a team of product professionals in a high-visibility role.
*   Purpose-led impact – contribute to data-driven transformation that supports sustainability, health, and innovation.
**You bring:**
*   Product leadership – Extensive experience in data roles with a solid foundation in product management and delivery.
*   Strategic thinking – ability to define roadmaps, prioritise initiatives, and align stakeholders around shared goals.
*   A strong sense of accountability – you take initiative, speak up, and see things through. You are responsible and focus on what matters.
*   An innovator’s instinct – you are always looking for better, smarter, more sustainable ways to make an impact.
*   A collaborative spirit – you’re at your best when working across teams, functions and countries.
*   Curiosity and an open mind – because you believe progress starts with asking, “Is there a better way?”
