**Job Description:**
Project is T&M based Team Extension to join existing team located in Germany. The scope is to gather and prepare data for internal clients requesting simulation data for Autonomous Vehicles test scenarios.
**Tasks:**
*   **Data selection and extraction** – retrieving images and video clips from internal datasets using tools such as Nvidia Data Scout, based on text/image queries and distribution requirements (e.g., day/night, rural/urban, road/motorway).
*   **Data augmentation and scaling** – applying upsampling techniques (time-based, GPS-based, H3 cell-based) to increase dataset volume and enrich data coverage.
*   **Curation management** – tracking progress in Corner Case Management (CCM), creating new corner cases with defined requirements (e.g., target volume, specific sign, reference images).
*   **Dataset preparation** – structuring curated results into CSV files, integrating outputs from Data Scout/Databricks, and preparing datasets for downstream processes.
*   **Quality control and data pipeline management** – initiating Relevance Check requests with external teams and managing data queuing for labeling and further processing.
**Qualifications:**
*   Proficient in **SQL** for data querying, analysis, and dataset preparation.
*   Experienced with **Databricks**, including leveraging the built-in AI assistant for data exploration and workflow optimization.
*   Skilled in **Python** for ad-hoc scripting, automation, and data manipulation.
*   Familiar with **Cursor AI assistant** to enhance productivity and streamline data-related tasks.
*   Strong **self-organization and documentation skills**, maintaining independent notes and progress tracking to complement limited CCM visibility.
