In Grab, purpose gives us joy and habits build excellence, while harnessing the power of Technology and AI to deliver the mission of driving Southeast Asia forward by economically empowering everyone, with heart, hunger, honour, and humility.
**Job Description:**
**Get to Know the Team**
The Fulfilment Tech Family drives Grab's mission to out-serve customers and partners across Southeast Asia's diverse marketplaces. We build high-throughput, real-time distributed systems powered by advanced machine learning, handling hundreds of millions of requests daily. Our goal? To deliver exceptional products and experiences that boost driver partner engagement, maximize efficiency in fulfilling customer orders—rain or shine—and create thriving marketplaces with optimal pricing loved by both partners and customers.
**Get to Know the Role**
We're looking for a Senior Full-stack Engineer to join our dynamic Fulfilment Tech Family.
You will create, implement, and scale innovative solutions leveraging advanced AI and machine learning technologies to enhance driver partner experiences and optimize marketplace efficiency.
You will report to the Engineering Manager and work onsite at Grab Office.
**The Critical Tasks You Will Perform**
*   Develop AI agents and chatbots using LLMs, LangChain, and LangGraph to enhance driver experiences and solve real-world challenges.
*   Build scalable full-stack solutions, including cloud-based backends and efficient web interfaces.
*   Write high-quality code in modern languages (e.g., Python, Go) and frameworks, ensuring scalability and efficiency. Conduct code/design reviews.
*   Deploy machine learning pipelines and integrate AI agents with fulfilment systems.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to implement features and assess business impact. Ensure smooth mobile integration.
*   Design and manage infrastructure with Terraform and cloud platforms like AWS/Azure.
*   Participate in on-call rotations, perform root cause analysis, and contribute to post-mortems.
*   Work in a regional setup, collaborating across teams and cultures.
**Qualifications:**
**What Essential Skills You Will Need**
*   Strong foundation in computer science: data structures, algorithms, multi-threading, distributed systems, relational/non-relational databases.
*   Expertise in LLMs, LangChain, LangGraph for conversational AI development.
*   Proficient in backend languages (e.g., Go, Python, Java) with focus on Go and Python.
*   Experience with modern frontend frameworks (e.g., Next.js), SSR concepts (SSR/SSG), and protocols (WebSocket, Streamable HTTP, gRPC).
*   5+ years of cloud platform experience (AWS/Azure) and scalable backend deployment.
*   Advocate for shift-left testing with unit, integration, and automated testing.
*   5+ years in real-time data processing with Flink/Kafka or similar tools.
*   Experienced in stakeholder collaboration across teams/regions.
*   Strong problem-solving and system design skills for scalability and stability.
*   Proficient in creating concise technical documentation for cross-team collaboration.
