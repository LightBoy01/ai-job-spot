Global Sales and Network is one of the five divisions within Mercedes-Benz Cars Sales and includes, among other things, sales in over 100 linguistically, geographically, and economically diverse countries. Our markets range from South America through Europe, Africa, the Middle East, to Asia and Australia.
In our department, you will have the opportunity to gain insights into the exciting world of Business Intelligence and data. Additionally, cutting-edge topics such as AI, Data Management, Data Science, and Data Quality will be part of your experience. As the central unit, we are responsible for managing all markets except the USA and China, which includes tasks such as developing AI use cases in the sales sector, regular communication with individual markets, and implementing sales projects. Our team serves as the interface between the external organization and the central unit.
What we offer you:
*   The chance to participate in various data science projects in international collaboration with internal and external partners
*   Insight into AI strategy and data management topics from the perspective of the headquarters and in close cooperation with the markets
*   Insights into future-relevant technologies and tools, such as Data Lake, Databricks, PowerBI
*   Insights into global sales processes related to data and lead management
*   Creative freedom to contribute your own ideas to strengthen the data community, with the goal of enhancing data culture and data stewardship
*   The opportunity to gain experience with digital sales tools and to help shape the digitization of our sales processes
*   An environment that supports and actively encourages interdisciplinary and independent work
These challenges await you:
*   Collaboration in an interdisciplinary project environment with direct contact to the specialist departments
*   Participation in data science projects in close cooperation with our business partners
*   Support in data quality assurance and data validation through analysis of data and digital KPIs in reports
*   Participation in the implementation of guidelines and processes in the area of data management within the Mercedes-Benz Group
*   Project-supporting activities such as the preparation and follow-up of project meetings, as well as the creation of presentations, decision-making, and communication materials
*   Support in the development of the data community in the social intranet, as well as participation and planning of data stewardship activities
The activity can begin from February 2026.
*   Studies in the field of Economics, Business Administration, Business Informatics, or a comparable field of study
*   Proficient German and English language skills, both written and spoken
*   Proficient use of MS Office, as well as affinity for IT and the internet - PowerBI knowledge is an advantage; SQL & Python knowledge is an advantage; Adobe Photoshop or Adobe Illustrator knowledge is an advantage
*   Affinity for data and interest in technology topics such as AI, Data Catalog, Data & Analytics, Data Governance, Data Science, Data Management, and Data Quality
*   Strong drive for creativity and creative problem-solving skills
*   Independent and responsible work attitude, intercultural competence, commitment and initiative
*   Strong analytical and conceptual skills, methodical work approach with high creativity, communication and teamwork skills
Please note that your internship at this location must be mandatory.
