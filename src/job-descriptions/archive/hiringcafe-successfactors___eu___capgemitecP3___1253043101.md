Lead Change. Inspire Growth. Join Capgemini Invent’s CPR team
-------------------------------------------------------------
At Capgemini Invent, we recognize the need for transformative company consulting. For over fifty years, we’ve honed our reputation as a leading management consultancy by combining strategy, technology, data science, creative design, and engineering expertise with an inventive mindset. Join us to drive transformation tailored to our clients’ challenges of today and tomorrow validated by data, powered by creativity, and underpinned by purposeful technology.
YOUR ROLE
---------
At Capgemini Invent, we help our clients imagine and build what's next. As the innovation and transformation powerhouse of the Capgemini Group, we combine strategy, technology, data science, and creative design to solve the most complex business and technology challenges. We are guided by our core values: honesty, boldness, trust, freedom, team spirit, modesty, and fun—and we live them every day. We are now looking for a Manager or Senior Manager to join our Consumer Products & Retail (CPR) division in Sweden. This is a client-facing role for someone who thrives on driving transformation, delivering impact, and building trusted relationships. In this role, you will:
*   Lead strategic transformation projects for top-tier consumer brands and retailers.
*   Act as a trusted advisor to clients, shaping their digital, operational, and sustainability agendas.
*   Develop and strengthen collaboration and relationships with our key clients.
*   Drive business development and contribute to the growth of our CPR practice.
*   Lead and mentor teams, fostering a collaborative and inclusive culture.
*   Collaborate across Capgemini Invent’s global network to deliver innovative solutions.
YOUR PROFILE
------------
*   Strong consulting background within consumer products or retail, combined with expertise in one of the following areas: marketing, business technology, supply chain, or data and AI.
*   Demonstrated success in client-facing roles, with strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
*   Experience leading teams and delivering complex projects with measurable impact.
*   A passion for innovation, sustainability, and digital transformation.
*   Strong academic foundation (Master’s degree or equivalent) in business, engineering, economics, or related fields.
*   Fluent in Swedish and English, both written and spoken.
