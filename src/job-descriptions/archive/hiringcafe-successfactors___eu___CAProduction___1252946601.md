**The future looks like you**
-----------------------------
With over 1,300 stores in 17 European countries and more than 25,000 employees, C&A is one of Europe’s leading fashion retailers. Every day, C&A welcomes millions of visitors to its stores in Europe and to its online shop. C&A offers quality and long-lasting fashion at affordable prices for
the many. We are building on more than 180 years of experience in the fashion industry and continuously modernize the company to become an omnichannel fashion retailer.
The Business Navigation team plays a critical role in supporting the C&A retail selling organization by providing financial insights, analysis, and strategic guidance. This position partners closely with business leaders to drive informed decision-making, optimize performance, and ensure sustainable, profitable growth. It offers a dynamic opportunity for finance professionals to contribute to the success of C&A.
**Your responsibilities**
-------------------------
· Co-pilot to the business leaders of the Selling organization with focus on online and OMNI
· Enable fact-based decision-making and performance management for the business leaders by generating insights, identifying opportunities and improvement areas
· Be a sparring partner for the Selling team and Business Navigation leadership
· Execute and manage the budget, planning, and forecasting processes
· Enhance reporting and planning processes by leveraging AI-powered tools
· Help to develop One C&A KPI dashboard based on Microstrategy
· Develop business cases, with clear metrics, benchmarks, and threshold
**What you bring**
------------------
· Bachelor’s / Master’s degree
· Minimum of 5 years of experience in commercial business controlling / analysis for offline and / or e-commerce business
· Preferably with exposure to fashion, consumer goods, or other retail industries
· Experienced user in Excel and PowerPoint, business intelligence tools like Microstrategy
· Proficiency in AI-powered tools and platforms to enhance productivity, problem-solving, and decision-making
· Positive and proactive mindset with a “hands-on” mentality
· Good leadership and communication skills
· Fluent in English
