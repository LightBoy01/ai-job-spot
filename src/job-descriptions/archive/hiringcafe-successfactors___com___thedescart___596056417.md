**We’re growing fast and invite you to join our amazing team.**
We’re looking for a **Senior Product Manager** to play a pivotal role in shaping and scaling our eCommerce SaaS platform. This isn’t just about managing features—it’s about owning outcomes across adoption, retention, monetization, and customer value. You’ll work hands-on across the product lifecycle, from discovery through delivery, partnering closely with Engineering, Design, and Go-to-Market teams to drive measurable business and customer impact.
**What You’ll Do**
*   **Own the Product Lifecycle**: Drive product strategy and execution end-to-end, from discovery and validation to launch and iteration.
*   **Deliver Business Impact**: Define success metrics and deliver against adoption, retention, monetization, and platform scalability goals.
*   **Collaborate Cross-Functionally**: Partner with Sales, Marketing, and Customer Success to shape pricing, packaging, and go-to-market readiness.
*   **Build with Scale in Mind**: Optimize internal workflows and leverage AI and automation to improve both product efficiency and customer outcomes.
*   **Execute with Discipline**: Prioritize ruthlessly, translate strategy into clear requirements, and work tightly with Engineering to deliver with quality and speed.
**What You Bring**
*   5–7+ years of experience in **B2B SaaS product management**, ideally with significant eCommerce platform exposure.
*   Hands-on track record of delivering outcomes across **growth, retention, monetization, or efficiency**.
*   Strong commercial and technical acumen in integration-heavy SaaS environments.
*   Familiarity with **AI-powered tools, data-driven decision making, and workflow automation**.
*   Excellent communicator who thrives in cross-functional settings and can balance strategic vision with tactical execution.
*   Alignment with **Descartes Aptitudes**:
*   **Smarts** – structured problem-solving and sound judgment.
*   **Curiosity** – eagerness to explore, learn, and challenge assumptions.
*   **Coachability** – openness to feedback and continual growth.
*   **Work Ethic** – persistence and follow-through in driving outcomes.
*   **Track Record** – proven history of delivering results in SaaS/eCommerce.
*   **Responsibility** – ownership mindset and accountability for impact.
*   **Honesty** – integrity, transparency, and customer-first thinking.
**What’s In It for You**
*   **Remote-First Flexibility**: Work from where you’re most productive, with the flexibility to balance life and work.
*   **Growth Opportunities**: Expand your skills and career path in a high-growth SaaS environment with mentorship and leadership visibility.
*   **Impact You Can See**: Drive initiatives that directly influence customer success, platform growth, and company performance.
*   **Collaborative Culture**: Join a supportive team that values ownership, transparency, and continuous learning.
*   **Autonomy with Support**: Take true ownership of your product area while having the backing of leadership and cross-functional peers.

You’ll own a high-impact product area with real autonomy and visibility. We value clarity, customer empathy, and results. If you’re driven to build, innovate, and deliver meaningful outcomes—we want to hear from you.
