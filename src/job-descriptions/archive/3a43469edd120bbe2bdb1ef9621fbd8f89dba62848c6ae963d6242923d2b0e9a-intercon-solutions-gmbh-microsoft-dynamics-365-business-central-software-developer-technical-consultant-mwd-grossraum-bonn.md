### Description

Our client is a family-owned, internationally active mid-sized company with headquarters in the Koblenz area, Germany. For nearly 80 years, the company has been a leading manufacturer of fall protection equipment and safety solutions for sports and industry. With approximately 950 employees worldwide (around 500 at the main headquarters) and branches and production facilities in six countries, the company seamlessly blends tradition, innovation, and international growth.

To further expand their ERP team, they are immediately seeking an experienced **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Software Developer / Technical Consultant (m/f/d)** for a permanent position. This role offers an attractive salary package ranging from **€60,000 to €80,000 p.a.**, depending on experience, along with flexible working hours, home office options, and a supportive, familial company culture.

### Responsibilities

*   Actively participate in the international rollout of Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (BC23).
*   Provide support, further development, and maintenance of the ERP application.
*   Analyze functional requirements and translate them into robust technical solutions.
*   Independently develop in AL (and C/AL for the existing NAV environment).
*   Concept and realize system adaptations and extensions.
*   Optimize and automate business processes to enhance efficiency.
*   Collaborate closely with internal NAV developers and the IT department, particularly on topics related to SQL Server, .NET, and C#.
*   Offer 2nd-level support, conduct user training, and create comprehensive documentation.

### Qualifications

*   Completed degree in (Business) Informatics, a qualification in software development, or equivalent professional experience.
*   Profound knowledge of Microsoft Dynamics NAV / Business Central and practical experience with AL and C/AL development.
*   Familiarity with SQL, databases, object-oriented programming, and Microsoft Office applications.
*   Strong understanding of commercial processes and their mapping within ERP systems.
*   Possess an independent, structured, and solution-oriented working style.
*   Demonstrate strong teamwork skills, a motivation for continuous learning, and excellent communication abilities.
*   Very good German and good English language skills, both written and spoken.
