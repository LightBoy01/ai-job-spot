**Job Description:**
**Team Overview:**
The Premium AI teams work on products at LinkedIn which impact millions of members on the platform. On the Premium AI team, you will be working on exciting products and features related to enabling individuals and small businesses who are looking to optimize their leverage of LinkedIn. Technical work includes Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing (NLP), Generative AI (GAI), Semantic Search, Large Language Models (LLMs), and multi-task learning. This team works closely with other AI teams across LinkedIn.
**Location:**
At LinkedIn, our approach to flexible work is centered on trust and optimized for culture, connection, clarity, and the evolving needs of our business. The work location of this role is hybrid, meaning it will be performed both from home and from a LinkedIn office on select days, as determined by the business needs of the team.
This role will be based in Sunnyvale, CA.
**Responsibilities:**
• Directly lead a team of 7-8 Engineers, including Senior and Staff (Technical Lead) Engineers.
• Collaborate cross-functionally with Applications Engineers, Product, Infrastructure, and foundational teams to align on initiatives.
• Participate in key technical and design discussions with technical leads in the team.
• Define and leverage best practices for engineering and research to ensure optimal productivity and drive faster iterations.
• Attract world class talent and provide technical guidance, career development, and mentoring to team members.
• Operate best engineering and scientific practices & processes to ensure productivity of the team and drive faster iterations via A/B experiments.
**Qualifications:**
**Basic Qualifications:**
• 5+ years of related industry experience in Machine Learning, development, and algorithm related solutions
• BA/BS in Computer Science or other technical discipline, or related practical technical experience
• 1+ year(s) of management experience leading a AI/ML engineering team, or 1+ year(s) of Staff level AI/ML engineering experience with management training
**Preferred Qualifications:**
• 2+ years of experience in a leadership role within the AI or Machine Learning domain, including at least one year of direct people leadership.
• 7+ years of industry experience in AI, Machine Learning, or related disciplines.
• MS or PhD in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Statistics or related fields
• Experience building customer-facing AI solutions.
**Suggested Skills:**
• Team Management
• Semantic Search
• Deep Learning
• AI Models (LLMs)
• Technical Leadership
