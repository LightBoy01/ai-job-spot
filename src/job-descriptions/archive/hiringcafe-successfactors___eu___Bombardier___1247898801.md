**During your internship, you will contribute to:**
---------------------------------------------------
*   Participate in and lead a research project in AI on the topic of SADS (Synthetic Air Data System) to strengthen redundancy and ensure reliability of in-flight information.
*   Provide AI expertise and contribute to large-scale project related to aircraft wing surface icing
*   Evaluate and challenge AI techniques applied to the modeling of on-board systems
*   Develop new approaches for ice detection using AI tools.
### **What we are looking for:**
*   You enjoy teamwork and collaboration.
*   You are proficient in programming (C++, C# and/or Python) and familiar with AI development environments as well as with the most recent AI libraries.
*   You are comfortable working in an R&D environment, enjoy taking the lead on projects and are a source of innovative ideas.
*   You are a third- or fourth-year university student, or you are pursuing a master’s degree with a strong interest in aerospace engineering, product development, and continuous improvement.
*   You have good communication skills in both French and English.
