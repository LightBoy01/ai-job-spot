**Not a Job. A Mission.**
Created in 2007 from the creators of Kali Linux, OffSec (formerly Offensive Security) is the powerhouse behind the world’s most respected cybersecurity training and certifications. We don’t just teach theory—we live by our TRY HARDER ethos, delivering hands-on, real-world learning that builds elite defenders and closes the global infosec talent gap.
At OffSec, you’ll be part of a mission-driven team shaping the future of cybersecurity. With colleagues across 45+ countries, our community thrives on diversity, expertise, and passion—ranging from published authors and keynote speakers to government advisors and private-sector innovators.
Work from anywhere. Collaborate with brilliant minds. Push boundaries. If you’re ready to grow, make an impact, and be part of something bigger—JOIN US!
**The Role**
Ready to turn your hard-earned cybersecurity mastery into world-class training that inspires and equips the next generation of defenders? Offensive Security is hunting for a Senior Content Engineer who’s equal parts cyber sorcerer, technical tinkerer, and content visionary.
This isn’t your average “sit behind a desk” gig. You’ll be:
*   Crafting high-impact, hands-on labs that make learners sweat (in the best way).
*   Weaving cutting-edge attack and defense scenarios into unforgettable training.
*   Shaping the future of OffSec’s legendary certifications.
*   Leading development of advanced and real world defensive training content and exercises.
*   Leading, mentoring, and pushing your teammates to go further, faster, smarter.
If you crave a challenge, thrive in high-stakes environments, and love blending hardcore technical skill with creative content engineering—this role was built for you.
**What You’ll Be Doing**
_**Content Development**_
*   Design and develop immersive cybersecurity training content, focusing on detection, prevention, and security analysis — equal parts brutal and brilliant.
*   Mentor teammates, set the technical bar high, and refine content for maximum clarity and accuracy.
*   Collaborate across teams to keep labs and learning material fresh, relevant, and seamless.
_**Content Engineering**_
*   Build and oversee next-level training environments with VMs, cloud infrastructure, and automation.
*   Develop and test complex and stable lab environments that provide a great learner experience.
*   Troubleshoot like a pro—solving complex lab integration issues with precision and flair.
_**Applied Technical Expertise**_
*   Create realistic, scenario-driven challenges that push learners out of their comfort zones.
*   Validate, test, and enhance training environments with your security analysis and DFIR chops.
*   Stay on the bleeding edge—bring emerging threats, adversarial tactics, and defense strategies into our content.
**What You Bring**
*   7+ years of cybersecurity + VM creation + content development experience.
*   Expert wizardry in Microsoft Azure & Active Directory attack techniques, detections, and threat hunting (enumeration, escalation, lateral movement, persistence).
*   Experienced in AI-assisted code development of security tooling and proof of concepts.
*   Battle-tested in both bypassing AV, EDR, and modern defenses, as well as detection of bypass techniques.
*   Killer communication skills—able to make the complex sound clear and captivating.
*   Sharp research instincts: you validate, test, and translate threats into learning gold.
*   Leadership vibes: proven mentor, project driver, and process improver.
*   Comfortable working across global teams, cultures, and time zones.
**Working conditions**
This role is a full-time position. Work hours for this position are flexible, and will be performed remotely from anywhere in the world.
