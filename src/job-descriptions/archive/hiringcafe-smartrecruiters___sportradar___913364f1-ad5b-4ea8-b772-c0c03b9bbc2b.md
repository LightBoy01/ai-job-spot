**Company Description:**
We’re the world’s leading sports technology company, at the intersection between sports, media, and betting. More than 1,700 sports federations, media outlets, betting operators, and consumer platforms across 120 countries rely on our know-how and technology to boost their business.
**Job Description:**
**ABOUT US:**
We are looking for a talented **AI Engineer** to join our **Content Studio** team. Content Studio builds next-generation AI solutions that transform live sports data into engaging narratives — including automated match reports, previews, and sports articles. This role combines hands-on software engineering with cutting-edge applied AI research, giving you the opportunity to shape how millions of fans consume sports content.
**Why us?**
*   Be part of the **core AI engineering team** building the future of sports storytelling.
*   Work with cutting-edge **LLM and agent frameworks** in real production environments.
*   Shape a product that reaches **global sports fans** every day.
**THE CHALLENGE:**
*   **Build full-stack AI applications**: Develop scalable, user-facing tools for editors and automated content pipelines.
*   **Integrate AI frameworks**: Design and implement agentic workflows that bring sports data to life.
*   **AI prompting & evaluation**: Craft, test, and refine prompt strategies for **data-to-text generation**, ensuring factual accuracy and high-quality output.
*   **Model evaluation & iteration**: Run systematic evaluations of LLM output using both rule-based and human-in-the-loop feedback.
*   **Collaborate cross-functionally**: Partner with product managers, data scientists, and editorial teams to align AI capabilities with sports storytelling needs.
*   **Deploy and scale**: Deliver production-ready services on cloud platforms with strong monitoring, testing, and performance optimization.
**Technologies We Use:**
*   **Full-stack development**: **Python**, **JavaScript** (React, Node.js).
*   **AI frameworks**: **LangGraph**, **LangChain**, Google Agent Developer Kit (ADK).
*   **Prompting & evaluation**: **LLM prompting strategies**, evaluation pipelines.
*   **Cloud & DevOps**: **Google Cloud Platform** (**GCP**) and modern CI/CD workflows.
*   **Domain**: Data-to-text generation, sports data **APIs**, automated content systems.
**ABOUT YOU:**
*   Solid foundation as a **Full-Stack Developer** (**Python**, **JavaScript**) with strong interest in AI-driven applications.
*   **Self-driven and self-motivated**: able to take ownership, explore new ideas, and contribute without constant direction.
*   Comfortable with ambiguity — you can identify what needs doing and drive it forward with **initiative and creativity**.
*   Curiosity and adaptability to **learn emerging AI frameworks and tools** quickly.
*   Practical problem-solving mindset with focus on building **production-ready systems**.
*   Strong collaboration skills — you know when to align with the team, seek guidance, and share progress.
*   **Bonus**: experience in sports tech, media automation, or real-time data pipelines.
**OUR OFFER:**
*   A **collaborative environment** with colleagues from all over the world (Engineering offices in Europe, Asia and US) including various **social events** and **teambuilding**.
*   **Flexibility** to manage your workday and tasks with **autonomy**.
*   A balance of **structure** and **autonomy** to tackle your daily tasks.
*   **Vibrant** and **inclusive community**, including **Women in Tech** and **Pride groups** which welcome all participants.
*   **Global Employee Assistance Programme.**
*   **Calm** and **Reulay app** (leading well-being apps designed to support focus, quality rest, mindfulness, and long-term mental resilience).
*   **Online training** **videos**.
*   **Flexible working hours.**
_While we appreciate the flexibility and benefits of working from home, we strongly believe that coming together in person fosters stronger connections, encourages collaboration, and drives innovation—both as individuals and as a company. The energy, shared ideas, and team support we experience in the office strengthen the foundation of our success and culture. For this reason, we are an office-first business operating on a hybrid model, with team members working in the office three days a week to build relationships, exchange ideas, and grow together._
**OUR RECRUITMENT PROCESS:**
*   **Initial Screening:** A quick chat with our Talent Acquisition Partner to understand your background and expectations.
*   **Interview** (**with the Hiring manager**): A short (30 - 45 minute, online) interview with the hiring manager.
*   **First Technical Interview (with the team):** Meet with the Technical team (45 minutes, online) and do a live coding challenge - basic coding and algorithmic questions.
*   **Second Technical Interview (with the team):** Meet with the Technical team (45 minutes, online) and do a live coding challenge - building a full project (AI).
*   **Finals Steps**: Receive feedback and, if successful, an offer!
