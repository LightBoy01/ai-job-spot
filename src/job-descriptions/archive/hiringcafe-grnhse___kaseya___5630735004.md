**Summary**
**As a Software Engineering Manager, you’ll lead a team of engineers in delivering scalable and secure SaaS solutions, drive Agile adoption and AI/ML integration, and foster a culture of quality, accountability, and innovation while ensuring compliance and operational excellence.**
**Responsibilities**
*   **Manage and mentor a team of software engineers, ensuring delivery of high-quality features in a modern SaaS environment.**
*   **Drive adoption of Agile practices, continuous integration, and test automation across the team.**
*   **Collaborate closely with product and QA partners to align engineering priorities with customer needs.**
*   **Support the integration of AI/ML into product features and engineering workflows.**
*   **Promote a culture of quality, ownership, and accountability within the team.**
*   **Oversee release readiness, incident response, and continuous improvement initiatives.**
*   **Ensure secure coding practices and adherence to compliance standards (SOC 2, GDPR, etc.).**
*   **Provide input into technical architecture and implementation while enabling engineers to take ownership.**
**Requirements**
*   **5+ years of experience leading software engineering teams with direct people management.**
*   **Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or equivalent experience.**
*   **Proven track record delivering cloud-native SaaS products.**
*   **Strong understanding of Agile methodologies, CI/CD, and DevOps principles.**
*   **Experience with observability tools, incident management, and performance optimization.**
*   **Familiarity with AI/ML technologies is a plus.**
*   **Strong communication skills with a passion for coaching engineers.**
*   **Ability to manage team schedules, balance priorities, and deliver on commitments.**
**Tech Stack**
*   **Backend: Ruby on Rails, .NET Core/Framework**
*   **Frontend: React, Angular**
*   **Databases: PostgreSQL, MS SQL, MySQL**
*   **Infrastructure: AWS and Azure**
*   **Version Control: Git**
*   **Testing: Unit testing, TDD/BDD encouraged**
