**What's the Role?**
====================
Still interested? Awesome. Here's the deal:
Our Senior UX/UI Designer sits at the intersection of strategy and execution, serving as the bridge between product vision and beautiful, functional user experiences. You'll be the design voice in critical product decisions, working closely with our Vietnam design squad to ensure every pixel serves a purpose and every interaction feels effortless.
This isn't just about creating pretty interfaces (though you'll do that too). You'll be translating complex product requirements into clear design briefs, facilitating cross-functional collaboration across time zones, and championing a design system that scales with our rapidly growing product. From discovery sessions with PMs to final handoffs with engineering, you'll ensure design quality never gets lost in translation.
At Creative Force, design isn't an afterthought - it's integral to how we solve complex creative operations challenges for the world's biggest brands. As our Senior UX/UI Designer, you'll help shape not just what our product looks like, but how it feels to use it every day. And as AI continues to reshape our industry, you'll be at the forefront of exploring how these new capabilities can enhance both our design process and our users' creative workflows.
**Senior UX/UI Designer Self-Assessment**
=========================================
Normally, this is where you'd find a three-page-long list of required skills, including but not limited to: fire dancing, speed stacking, and knife-forging.
But it's 2025, so we aren't going to do that to you.
We do know, however, what characteristics we believe make a kick-ass Senior UX/UI Designer. To help you evaluate if you'd be a good fit for this role, we've provided them below.
### **You're a Cross-Functional Facilitator**
You excel at running design kickoffs, reviews, and planning sessions that actually move the needle. You write crisp design briefs that translate product intent into actionable design work. Most importantly, you know how to coordinate async collaboration across different time zones and cultures, ensuring everyone from PMs to engineers to your Vietnam design team stays aligned and inspired.
### **You Have Strong UX Craft with Systems Thinking**
You're not just pushing pixels - you understand interaction design, information architecture, and visual hierarchy at a deep level. Your Figma skills are expert-level, and you've built or maintained design systems that actually work in code. You think systematically about how design decisions ripple across the entire product experience.
### **You Balance Product Sense with Design Vision**
You can prioritize ruthlessly, negotiate trade-offs intelligently, and define what success looks like for both users and the business. Even without direct line management, you know how to align and inspire cross-functional teams toward shared outcomes. You see the big picture while never losing sight of the details that matter.
### **You're Ready to Elevate Quality and Embrace AI**
You have an eye for what makes great design great, and you're not afraid to push back when standards slip. You introduce lightweight validation methods, help define acceptance criteria that protect design intent, and ensure that what gets built matches what was designed. You're also AI curious - excited about exploring how AI can enhance both your design process and our users' creative workflows, whether that's experimenting with AI-powered design tools or identifying opportunities where automation can free up time for more strategic thinking.
