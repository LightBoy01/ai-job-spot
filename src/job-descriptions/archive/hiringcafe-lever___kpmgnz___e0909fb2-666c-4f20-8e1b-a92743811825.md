**The opportunity:**
*   This opportunity is for a Senior Technical Business Analyst seconded to the AI Team where you will partner with business leaders and engineers to translate complex processes and policies into robust, testable requirements for AI-enabled solutions. You will lead structured discovery, define evaluation and acceptance criteria, support solution design and champion  governance so solutions are safe, compliant, and deliver measurable outcomes.
**Day to day you will:**
*   Lead discovery to map current and future state processes, decision points, and escalation paths, producing clear and reusable artefacts.
*   Translate business rules and policy into machine-readable specifications (e.g., decision tables, structured requirements, prompt/skill specifications).
*   Define evaluation datasets, success metrics, and adversarial tests to validate quality, safety, and reliability.
*   Partner with engineers to shape solution options, integrations, non-functional requirements, and test plans.
*   Prepare governance inputs (risk assessment, data classification, access/RBAC, privacy considerations) and track approvals to completion.
*   Plan and run UAT and pilot phases; coordinate training, comms and change activities to drive adoption.
*   Measure and report business impact (time, quality, cost) and agent performance (success and intervention rates).
*   Maintain a groomed backlog, RAID, and stakeholder communications, ensuring alignment and momentum.
**About you:**
*   AI first in your approach to work.
*   5+ years’ experience as a Business Analyst (or Product BA) delivering technology-enabled change in complex environments.
*   Outstanding facilitation, requirements elicitation, and documentation skills; able to turn ambiguity into crisp, testable acceptance criteria.
*   Proven experience with AI/automation or data-driven solutions, including defining evaluation sets and measurable outcomes.
*   Working knowledge of enterprise platforms and data (e.g., Power Platform, Azure, ServiceNow, SharePoint/Fabric), APIs/connectors, and identity/RBAC concepts.
*   Familiar with AI governance, risk and compliance processes (privacy, data protection, DLP/labeling) and how they influence design and rollout.
*   Comfortable partnering closely with engineers and product owners; excellent communication with senior stakeholders.
*   A growth mindset: curious, pragmatic, and outcome focused.
*   Confidence with LLM/agent concepts (human-in-the-loop, prompt patterns, tool/skill design, telemetry, cost controls).
