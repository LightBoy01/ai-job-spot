**Job Description:**
We are seeking a highly skilled Senior AI Software Engineer with deep expertise in Python and a proven track record of building production-grade systems. This role is at the intersection of software engineering and applied AI, where you’ll design and deliver end-to-end AI applications, integrate large language models (LLMs), and provide technical leadership across teams.
The ideal candidate is a strong engineer first and foremost, with the ability to write clean, scalable, and maintainable code in Python, while also keeping pace with the fast-moving AI landscape.
**Responsibilities**
*   **End-to-End Development**: Design, implement, and deploy AI-powered applications with a strong emphasis on backend Python engineering.
*   **Backend Systems**: Build and maintain production services using Python (FastAPI, SQL), ensuring modularity, reliability, and scalability.
*   **AI/LLM Integration**: Apply deep technical skills in LLM development (prompt engineering, RAG, vector databases, agentic frameworks) to real-world problems.
*   **Engineering Best Practices**: Drive high standards in CI/CD, testing, version control, and code quality to ensure production readiness.
*   **Standardisation & Reuse**: Establish reusable frameworks and components to accelerate AI solution development across teams.
*   **Technical Leadership**: Provide architectural direction, perform in-depth code reviews, and act as a trusted consultant to internal teams.
*   **Mentorship**: Mentor junior engineers, fostering strong engineering skills and AI literacy.
*   **Continuous Learning**: Stay at the forefront of both Python engineering practices and emerging AI technologies.
**Required Skills & Experience**
*   **Education**: MSc (or equivalent) in Computer Science, AI, or related field.
*   **Experience**:
*   5+ years of professional experience as a software engineer with strong Python development skills.
*   2–3 years hands-on experience with Generative AI/LLMs.
*   **Technical Strengths**:
*   Advanced Python programming skills (data structures, concurrency, performance optimization).
*   Backend frameworks (e.g., FastAPI) and relational databases (SQL).
*   Strong understanding of LLMs, embeddings, prompt engineering, and agentic frameworks.
*   Expertise in CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, and production deployments.
*   Experience with cloud platforms (Azure, GCP, AWS).
*   **Soft Skills**:
*   Excellent communicator, able to explain complex systems to diverse audiences.
*   Strong leadership and mentoring abilities.
**Nice-to-Have Skills**
