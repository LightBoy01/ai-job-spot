Caring, teamwork, and courage are the values that guide everything we do. Responsibility is a core of our operations. **Data, Analytics and AI superstars for gambling industry**
Are you a superstar in data, analytics or AI and have gambling industry experience?
We are looking for new **Data, Analytics and AI Superstars** to join Veikkaus’ growing **Data & AI unit** - and perhaps you’re the one we are looking for!
We are looking for skilled, curious and passionate people, who would be playing key roles in developing Veikkaus towards becoming the most data-driven gaming company in the world.
We’re dedicated to developing entertaining gambling experiences, by being responsible at the same time. At Veikkaus, you’ll find meaningful roles, opportunities to grow, and a workplace where care, collaboration, and courage shape everyday life. We believe everyone should be able to develop, succeed, and be themselves – while building shared success together. For a better game – and a better working life.
**If you can answer ‘yes’ to some of the following questions** (not all required) and you have experience in the gambling industry or a related field, you might be the superstar we are looking for. We encourage you to apply or contact us for further information.
**Essential Skills in Data and Analytics:**
*   Can you create data visualization and tell stories with the data? (e.g. Power BI)
*   Can you query and build the data for decision-making to an understandable and usable form? (e.g SQL, Python)
*   Can you use statistical analysis and modeling to uncover trends and patterns to create actionable insights?
*   Can you utilize machine learning and predictive analytics to personalize experience or optimize performance?
*   Can you create and utilize customer segmentation and behavioral analytics to improve customer engagement?
*   Can you design and execute experimentation to validate ideas and optimize the outcomes?
*   Can you build data pipelines, develop data quality and usability of data assets?
*   Can you support strategic thinking and business development with data-driven decision making?
*   Can you use data, analytics and AI for safe and sustainable gambling experiences?
*   Can you translate complex data and analytics into clear insights for the stakeholders?
Still thinking about if Veikkaus could be your place? Don’t hesitate to contact us to have more information if you’re interested!
**We offer you:** A wealth of data, interesting and challenging tasks, and competitive employee benefits. In addition, you will get to work with talented and considerate colleagues, and acquire new skills, especially about utilizing data and developing digital business. Our work is flexible across team boundaries, which is why we actively communicate what we are working on both at the unit and company levels. Veikkaus has a special social mission to prevent gambling-related issues that makes our work more meaningful.
We genuinely aim to be a responsible employer, offering engaging tasks and a healthy work–life balance. It is important to us that everyone can come to work as they are. We value diverse backgrounds, skills, and perspectives, and believe that success comes from working in diverse teams. We encourage individuals from various backgrounds to apply for our jobs.
**This position is located in Finland and requires living in Finland**. The office is in Pohjois-Haaga, Helsinki. Veikkaus prefers a flexible hybrid work model between a remote workplace and an office.
