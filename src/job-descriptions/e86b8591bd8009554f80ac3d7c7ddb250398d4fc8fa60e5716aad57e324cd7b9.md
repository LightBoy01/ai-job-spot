Responsibilities
----------------

Are you an experienced cloud engineer with a passion for AI innovation? We’re hiring a **Senior Cloud AI Engineer** to join our growing team!  In this role, you’ll take the lead in designing and managing cloud-based AI systems with a focus on the Microsoft Cloud Platform (MCP) and natural language interface development. You’ll work alongside cross-functional teams to build smart, scalable, and secure solutions that deliver real value to our customers.

This is a great opportunity to apply your technical skills in a fast-paced, forward-thinking environment—while driving the future of AI-powered applications in the cloud.

**Roles and Responsibilities:** 

*   Develop and maintain cloud Agentic AI solutions in accordance with best practices
*   Deploys, debugs, and executes initiatives related to cloud computing, including modular, container base solutions
*   Design, develop, and maintain cloud-based systems, ensuring efficient data storage and adherence to security policies
*   Collaborate with engineering and development teams to evaluate and identify optimal cloud solutions
*   Interact with customers, provide cloud support, and make recommendations based on customer needs
*   Modify and improve existing enterprise cloud solutions
*   Educate customer developer teams on the implementation of new cloud technologies and initiatives

Qualifications
--------------

**Required Qualifications:**

*   Minimum of 12 years of experience with BS/BA OR 16 years of experience with a HS Diploma
*   Proven work experience as a Cloud Engineer or similar role
*   Must have experience working with Cloud Artificial Intelligence offerings as well as Agentic AI MCP's
*   Must have Microsoft Azure and Amazon AWS experience
*   Relevant training and/or certifications
*   Troubleshooting and analytical skills
*   Strong communication and collaboration skills
*    Must hold a current Public Trust Clearance.
*   Must be a US Citizen

**Preferred Qualifications**:

*   Container technologies such as Kubernetes is preferred
*    DevOps CI/CID pipeline development experience preferred
