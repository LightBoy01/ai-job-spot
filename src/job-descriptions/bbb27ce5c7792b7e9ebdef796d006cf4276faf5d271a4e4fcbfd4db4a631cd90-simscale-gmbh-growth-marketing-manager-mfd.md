SimScale is an engineering simulation platform that is revolutionizing the way engineers, designers, scientists, and architects design products. The SimScale platform is accessible completely via a web browser, with an easy-to-use interface which supports numerous simulation types including Structural Mechanics, Fluid Dynamics and Thermodynamics.

By harnessing the power of the cloud for simulation, SimScale eliminates the hurdles that accompany traditional simulation tools: high installation costs, licensing fees, deployment of high-performance computing hardware, mandatory updates and maintenance. Users always have the latest version of SimScale.

**Your Opportunity**

As a key member of SimScale’s marketing organization, the Growth Marketing Manager plays a pivotal role in driving rapid ARR growth across our global markets. This position is responsible for defining and executing integrated marketing campaigns aimed at capturing net new customers. By leveraging a full-funnel marketing approach, you will utilize demand-generation tactics, lead global campaign execution, and manage social media with a specific focus on driving engagement and conversions within our Strategic Account segment. This role succeeds by increasing reach into and engagement of senior buyers within our target ICP sectors in a manner that motivates change, compels action, and accelerates conversion to pipeline, so as to help drive SimScale’s growth globally.

*   Define and orchestrate end-to-end integrated marketing campaigns targeting net new customers within our strategic account segment.
*   Own campaign strategy and execution, from ideation to launch to performance optimization across email, webinars, social, partnerships, and emerging channels.
*   Collaborate with the Paid Media Manager to align on messaging, audiences, and funnel performance.
*   Collaborate cross-functionally with content, product marketing, and sales to ensure alignment and impact.
*   Identify, test and scale new growth channels based on where our target audience is (Reddit, YouTube, niche communities, co-marketing partnerships).
*   Introduce and operationalize AI tooling and automation workflows where they improve campaign efficiency, personalization, and execution velocity.
*   Analyze trends, develop actionable insights to drive pipeline growth.
