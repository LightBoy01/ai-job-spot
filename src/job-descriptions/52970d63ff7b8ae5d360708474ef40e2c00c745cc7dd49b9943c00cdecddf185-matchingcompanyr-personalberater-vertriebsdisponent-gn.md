For our client in Leipzig, we are seeking a dedicated **Personalberater / Vertriebsdisponent (gn)** to manage the temporary placement (ANÜ) of industrial-technical personnel.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Sustainable key account management and active new customer acquisition.\n- Recruitment of new employees for temporary assignments at client companies.\n- Independent management of the entire personnel process.\n- Sourcing applicants (gn) through various channels, contract negotiation, personal support, etc.\n- Appropriate placement of employees (gn) according to defined client requirements.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed vocational training.\n- Experience in the personnel services industry (temporary employment) within the industrial-technical sector.\n- Experience and enjoyment of active sales for new customer acquisition.\n- Fluent German language skills, both written and spoken.\n- High level of personal responsibility and a strong service-oriented mindset.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced recruitment consultant with a passion for 360° sales and HR within the industrial-technical sector. MatchingCompany® offers a stable, long-term career with attractive performance-based compensation, generous vacation, and comprehensive benefits focusing on health, family, and professional development. The role provides significant autonomy and ample opportunities for continuous learning and individual growth within a supportive environment.
