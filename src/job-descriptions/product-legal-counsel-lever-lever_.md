<b>Role Summary&nbsp;</b>
<br>
We are looking for an experienced Legal Counsel to support Mistral AI’s compliance by being the product and engineering teams' closest partner and advisor.
<br>
Play a pivotal role in shaping AI compliance—tackle cutting-edge regulations, influence the future of responsible AI, and collaborate directly with our engineering, science, and product teams at the heart of a rapidly evolving industry.
<br>
The legal team currently comprises 10 persons across Paris and Palo Alto, covering a spectrum of competences across regulatory, IP, contracts and corporate.
<br>
<br>
<b>What you will do</b>
<br>
•&nbsp;Collaborate with cross-functional teams in close partnership with technical and product teams to ensure legal and regulatory compliance in the deployment of AI technologies, with a specific focus on consumer law (DSA, online platforms, etc.).
•&nbsp;Stay updated on the latest AI-related legal and regulatory developments, and provide practical guidance to the organization on potential risks, solutions and opportunities.
•&nbsp;Provide training and support to the business on legal and compliance matters related to AI.
•&nbsp;Help drafting terms and other contractual materials, or negotiate contracts with upstream partners or suppliers, including but not limited to AI-related service agreements.
•&nbsp;Support team on other legal topics as needed.&nbsp;
<br>
<br>
<b>Qualifications and skills</b>
<br>
• Master degree in law, a bar admission is a plus, as well as an experience in a fast-growing environment
• 3 to 5 years of experience in a law firm or in a tech company, in IT law, with a focus on consumer law (DSA, online platform regulations, etc.)
• An understanding of artificial intelligence technologies and their applications is essential
• Solution oriented approach: the candidate should be able to identify compliance issues and develop effective strategies to address and resolve them. They should be capable of implementing practical solutions
• Solid project management skills
• Ability to analyze complex legal and technical issues related to AI and to identify potential legal risks and solutions is important.
• Candidates should be detail-oriented and have strong problem-solving skills
• Excellent written and verbal communication; Strong ability to present complex legal concepts in a clear and understandable manner
• Business oriented
• An international experience is a strong plus
• Ability and willingness to continuously learn and update their skills and knowledge.
• Ability to work independently and in a team-oriented, collaborative environment.
• Proactive, flexible, and able to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment
