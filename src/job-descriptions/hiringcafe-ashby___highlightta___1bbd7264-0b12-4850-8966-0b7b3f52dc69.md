**About the Role**

We’re supporting a leading SaaS company in the search for a **Vice President of Product** to lead the end-to-end strategy and execution of its flagship B2B platform. This high-impact role will oversee the core application layer, reimagine the customer experience with an AI-first approach, and build a scalable, next-generation product organization.

In this executive leadership role, you’ll own the roadmap, drive AI innovation, and guide a cross-functional team in transforming the platform into the dominant solution in its market. With direct influence over the platform experience, this role offers the rare opportunity to shape the long-term product vision while delivering fast, measurable impact.

This is an ideal opportunity for a proven product leader who thrives in high-growth SaaS environments, has scaled platforms end-to-end, and is comfortable balancing strategic vision with day-to-day execution.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Own and drive the product vision, roadmap, and performance for the core SaaS platform.
    
*   Lead the application layer experience across web and front-end capabilities.
    
*   Champion AI-first thinking and identify opportunities for transformative innovation.
    
*   Build and lead a high-performing team of product managers and future leaders.
    
*   Collaborate with engineering, design, GTM, and customer teams to ensure cross-functional success.
    
*   Influence stakeholders and peers without direct authority.
    
*   Balance short-term execution with long-term strategy and innovation.
    
*   Represent product direction and progress with senior executives and partners.  
    

**What You Bring**

*   12+ years in B2B SaaS product management, with at least 5 years in a senior or VP-level leadership role.
    
*   Experience owning complex, scalable, customer-facing platform products.
    
*   Proven success in delivering AI-driven or data-rich solutions.
    
*   Strong technical fluency and ability to lead architecture and API conversations.
    
*   Background in fintech or financial SaaS is a strong asset.
    
*   Experience in customer-facing or go-to-market roles preferred.
    
*   Executive presence, resilience, and a clear bias for action.
    
*   Demonstrated conviction in shaping and pursuing a well-founded vision, informed by data, research, and strategic insight.
