About this opportunity
Ericsson is a global leader in telecommunications and technology, dedicated to driving innovation and shaping the future of communication. With a commitment to pushing the boundaries of what's possible, Ericsson is at the forefront of creating solutions that address the world's most pressing challenges. As Automation Architect at our Noida location, you will play a key role in crafting innovative and scalable solutions that integrate Java and Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning (AI/ML) technologies. This position requires a top-tier professional with a strong background in High-Level Design (HLD) and a deep understanding of both Java and AI/ML domains. You will be responsible for designing end-to-end technical solutions that meet customer needs and chip in to the overall success of Ericsson.
What you will do
•    Lead the definition and crafting of solution offerings, focusing on end-to-end technical solutions that incorporate Java and AI/ML technologies.
•    Conduct thorough analysis of customer technology, define business requirements, and actively participate in risk analysis.
•    Collaborate with multi-functional teams to generate potential activities and innovative solutions.
•    Develop comprehensive technical presentations and proposals, delivering compelling insights during customer presentations.
•    Provide valuable feedback to management to enhance solution quality and overall performance.
•    Actively participate in knowledge transfer, documentation, and information sharing within the team.
•    Stay abreast of new technologies and advancements in Java and AI/ML domains, sharing relevant insights to cultivate team competence.
•    Drive the development of balanced and reusable solutions.
•    Take ownership of High-Level Design (HLD) activities, ensuring the delivery of robust architectural solutions.
You will bring
•    Minimum 7 years into  Core JAVA, Jscript, Java Enable, Groovy.
•    Proficiency in Java programming language and practical experience in implementing AI/ML solutions.
•   Proficient into backend development, Java threading, oops concept of designing and principal related to same.
