We are hiring globally!

  

  

We are looking for **AI** **Data Specialists** to support the improvement of AI-generated content in **English**.

**Job Type:** Freelance

**Location:** Remote

**_Work Schedule:_** Part-time - 10+ hours per week. Flexible - work whenever you want.

**Start Date:** Immediately

**Duration:** Until the end of December 2025 (an extension is possible)

**Rate:** 18.5 USD 

**Help Shape the Future of AI**

Are you a student, recent graduate, stay-at-home parent, gig worker, or professional seeking flexible remote work? Are you interested in shaping the development and safety of today’s AI models?

**What You’ll Do**

The role involves performing diverse data-related tasks, including:

  

\- Data collection, evaluation, and annotation.

\- Pairwise comparisons.

\- Counting tasks.

\- Object tagging and labeling across different content types (audio, video, images, or collected data)

  

⚠️**_Important:_** _The use of AI tools or Large Language Models (LLMs) to complete assigned tasks is strictly prohibited. Any use of such tools will be considered a violation of the project requirements, resulting in non-payment and immediate removal from the project._

**What we’re looking for:**

_\- English Proficiency:_ Advanced proficiency in English at B2, C1, C2, or Native level.

_\- AI & Data Capabilities (Preferred):_ Experience in one or more of the following areas: machine learning tasks, data collection and preprocessing, data evaluation and quality assurance, and data annotation and labeling.

**What We Offer**

\- 100% remote work

\- Flexible schedule

\- Opportunity to earn extra income

\- Timely payments

\- The job is ideal for students, part-timers, or stay-at-home parents

**Please apply using a GMAIL account if you have one.**
