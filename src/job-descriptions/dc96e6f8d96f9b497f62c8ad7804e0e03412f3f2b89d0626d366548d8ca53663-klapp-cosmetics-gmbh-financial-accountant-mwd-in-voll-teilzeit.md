### Description\nKLAPP Cosmetics is a pioneer in the world of beauty, synonymous with innovative cosmetic treatments that are ahead of their time, embodying \"Made in Germany\" as a promise of quality. With over 45 years of experience and continuous research and development of new high-tech active ingredients and methods, we consistently achieve the highest treatment satisfaction. We are seeking a dedicated and experienced Bilanzbuchhalter (Certified Accountant) to join our team, playing a crucial role in ensuring the financial integrity and strategic reporting of the company.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Preparation of monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements according to HGB (German Commercial Code)\n*   Responsibility for the proper recording of all business transactions\n*   Maintenance and reconciliation of general ledger, accounts receivable, and accounts payable\n*   Conducting analyses and providing reports for management\n*   Serving as the primary contact person for auditors, tax advisors, and authorities\n*   Further development of accounting processes and internal control systems\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed commercial training and further education as a certified accountant (Bilanzbuchhalter IHK) or a comparable qualification\n*   Several years of professional experience in accounting, ideally within a medium-sized or international company\n*   Very good knowledge of German accounting law (HGB)\n*   Good knowledge of German tax and commercial law (HGB)\n*   Proficient in accounting software (e.g., Business Central) and MS Office, especially Excel\n*   Analytical mindset and strong numerical comprehension\n*   Independent, structured, and solution-oriented work ethic\n*   Team player with strong communication skills\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a compelling opportunity for a skilled Bilanzbuchhalter to join a stable yet innovative company with a strong brand and a commitment to quality.
