### Description\nunivativ is a project and personnel service provider dedicated to connecting students and graduates with exciting opportunities at renowned companies. Your career is our mission, and your development is at the heart of what we do.\n\nAre you passionate about successfully steering vehicle projects and reliably achieving milestones? As a Project Manager (m/f/d) for Vehicle Control, you will accompany the entire process from order to target market, ensuring smooth operations. Together with the team, you will optimize processes, create transparency, and actively contribute to project success.\n\nOur client, a global player in the automotive industry located in Affalterbach, is currently seeking project-based reinforcement. This role is a full-time (40 hours/week) position, starting as soon as possible, with an initial contract until December 1, 2026, and an option for extension. You can expect a broad spectrum of tasks and a friendly working atmosphere. This is a chance to become part of univativ and turn your career aspirations into reality!\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Ensuring transparency and timely milestone achievement through continuous tracking and KPI monitoring – from order placement to target market delivery.\n*   Early assessment and active management of risks in close collaboration with interface areas.\n*   Initiating and implementing appropriate countermeasures for schedule deviations, as well as escalating issues to relevant committees.\n*   Cross-departmental coordination on certification-relevant activities and creating transparency regarding their impact on timelines.\n*   Co-designing and further developing new, cross-divisional processes, tools, and methods for efficient vehicle control.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   A completed degree in engineering, industrial engineering, business administration, or a comparable qualification.\n*   Profound process and system knowledge in vehicle control and action management, particularly in GO, ZASM, and INTAKT.\n*   Experience in strategic, conceptual work, and in site- or plant-wide projects is advantageous.\n*   Demonstrated strong sense of self-responsibility, analytical and structured working methods, and the ability to constructively resolve conflicts.\n*   At least business fluent German language skills and good English language skills, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an experienced Project Manager passionate about the automotive industry. You'll be joining a global player and taking on a critical role in steering vehicle projects from end-to-end. univativ emphasizes career growth, offering extensive professional development opportunities, including various certifications and individualized training, to help you become an expert. Beyond a competitive salary, you'll benefit from a supportive atmosphere, gain valuable experience, and expand your professional network across diverse client projects. If you thrive on optimizing processes, managing complex timelines, and contributing to cutting-edge vehicle development, this project-based role offers significant impact and growth potential.
