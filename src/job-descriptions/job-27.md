# Machine Learning Engineer, Core Products

### Description

Roblox is ushering in the next generation of entertainment, allowing people to imagine, create, and play together in immersive, user-generated 3D worlds. We are looking for a Machine Learning Engineer to join our Core Products team and help us build the platform that powers our metaverse. In this role, you will work on a variety of machine learning problems, from content discovery and recommendation to trust and safety. This is a unique opportunity to work on a massive scale and make a significant impact on the future of entertainment.

### Responsibilities

- Design, build, and deploy machine learning models that power core features of the Roblox platform.
- Develop and maintain the infrastructure for training and serving models at scale.
- Collaborate with product managers, engineers, and designers to deliver high-impact solutions.
- Research and apply the latest techniques in machine learning, with a focus on deep learning and NLP.
- Analyze large-scale data to understand user behavior and improve model performance.

### Qualifications

- 5+ years of experience in machine learning or a related field.
- Strong programming skills in Python and experience with machine learning frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- Experience with deep learning and NLP is a strong plus.
- Experience with large-scale data processing and distributed systems.
- Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
- A passion for gaming and the metaverse.
