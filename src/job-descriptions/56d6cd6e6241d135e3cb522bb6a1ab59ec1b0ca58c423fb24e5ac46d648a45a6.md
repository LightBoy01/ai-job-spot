**PeopleCert** is looking for a **Senior** **Copywriter,** responsible for writing high-impact copy that brings our brands to life through multiple comms channels and audiences. With a strategic mindset and an ability to translate information into communication, the jobholder crafts compelling storytelling as well as sharp messaging that resonates with a global audience of professionals and enterprises.  We're looking for a talented, creative thinker with a penchant for smart concepts that tell a story through beautiful writing.  **As a Senior Copywriter, your tasks will include the following:**

*   Craft engaging and persuasive copy for a wide range of external channels, including websites, landing pages, social media, email campaigns, newsletters, digital and print advertising, and product promotional materials.

*   Lead the development of impactful communication concepts, generating original and strategic ideas that will drive engagement and results. 
*   Write clear and compelling content for internal communications and employer branding, helping to articulate company culture, values, and initiatives. 
*   Collaborate closely with design, digital, product marketing, and brand management teams to ensure cohesive storytelling and campaign execution.
*   Uphold and evolves the PeopleCert brand voice and tone across all communications. 
*   Edit and refines copy written by others to ensure quality and accuracy.
*   Contribute to naming, taglines, campaign concepts and other creative ideas that drive awareness and brand affinity. 
*   Champion creativity and pushes the boundaries of how we communicate with our audiences

**Working on Home Office (HO) Secure English Language Tests (SELTs)**

Any person who is engaged by PeopleCert to work on the SELT service must undergo a Background Check (the results of which must be acceptable to PeopleCert and the HO) prior to commencing their SELT duties. All SELT personnel will be required to complete a declaration (provided by PeopleCert) where the existence of any criminal record and/or bankruptcy must be declared.

If working on the SELT service in the UK, background checks will include:

*   A basic or enhanced Disclosure Barring Service (DBS) check

*   Right to Work in the UK check (including nationality, identity and place of residence)

*   HO security check (Baseline Personnel Security Standard (BPSS) or Counter Terrorist Check (CTC)

*   Financial background check

*   Employment reference check.

*   If working on the SELT service anywhere in the world (outside of the UK) personnel will undergo background checks that are equivalent to those stated for the UK.

In addition, if personnel are required to speak to SELT candidates they must be appropriately skilled in English language and, where SELT services are provided anywhere in the world (outside of the UK), the official language of the relevant country.
