The Senior Manager, GTM Technology is responsible for defining, executing, and optimizing PandaDoc’s GTM technology strategy to power PLG and sales-assisted motions. This leader ensures that AI-driven automation, scalable system architecture, and actionable data intelligence fuel predictable growth and operational efficiency across the full customer lifecycle.

Leading a team of RevTech experts, this role bridges technology and business strategy across the entire pre-lead-to-cash lifecycle, aligning investments with GTM priorities, breaking down silos, and enabling revenue teams to drive sustainable, scalable growth.

**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------

*   **Strategy & Roadmap:** Own the GTM systems roadmap (CRM, Marketing Automation, Customer Success, CPQ, CLM, Billing) aligned with growth goals. Translate business needs into scalable, technology-enabled workflows.
*   **Tech Stack Leadership:** Manage Salesforce, HubSpot/Marketo, Gainsight, Salesloft/Outreach, LeanData/ChiliPiper, Gong, and other platforms. Ensure seamless integrations and data flow for a unified revenue view.
*   **System Optimization:** Design and implement scalable, secure systems. Drive automation-first initiatives in lead routing, pipeline governance, forecasting, and customer engagement.
*   **Data & Intelligence:** Establish governance standards. Partner with Revenue Strategy on AI forecasting, churn prediction, and pipeline analytics. Deliver trusted insights for GTM execution.
*   **Team & Collaboration:** Lead a high-performing RevTech team. Act as the primary liaison between GTM teams and technical stakeholders. Foster a culture of accountability and innovation.
*   **Vendor & Budget:** Own vendor management, contracts, and ROI analysis. Oversee tech budgets and track emerging AI and revenue intelligence trends.

**Required Experience**
-----------------------

*   5+ years in Revenue Technology, RevOps, or Business Systems leadership within SaaS/subscription businesses.
*   Proven experience scaling GTM tech stacks.
*   Strong expertise in data architecture, integrations, and multi-system interoperability.
*   Experience supporting PLG and sales-assisted motions across omnichannel strategies.
*   Hands-on expertise with AI-driven automation and workflow orchestration (e.g., Workato, Zapier, MuleSoft, Tray.io).

**Skills & Competencies**
-------------------------

**Technical**

*   Deep expertise in Salesforce and integration frameworks.
*   Knowledge of AI-driven forecasting, lead scoring, and automation.

**Leadership**

*   Strong ability to translate business needs into scalable solutions.
*   Excellent communication skills with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
*   Experience leading, mentoring, and scaling technical teams.
*   Strategic problem-solver with a bias for execution.
