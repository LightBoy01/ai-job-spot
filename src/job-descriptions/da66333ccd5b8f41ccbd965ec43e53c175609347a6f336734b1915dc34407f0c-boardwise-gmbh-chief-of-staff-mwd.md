### Description\nAre you a quick thinker who loves numbers and wants to take on responsibility close to the C-level? Do you want to develop yourself further and have clear goals? Then this role could be your next big step. As Chief of Staff, you will work directly with our CFO and the executive management, actively shaping the strategy and operations of our rapidly growing company.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   You drive strategic and operational projects in close coordination with the C-level.\n*   You define focus measures and implement them independently and quickly.\n*   Support the CFO on operational and strategic Finance and BI topics.\n*   Preparation of analyses, reports, dashboards, presentations, and preliminary accounting.\n*   Structuring and coordination of meetings, decisions, and finance projects.\n*   Independent processing of administrative topics in the finance area.\n*   Close collaboration with key areas of the company.\n*   Active role in optimizing our processes and reporting structures.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Degree in Business Administration, Finance, Accounting, or comparable field.\n*   1-2 years of experience in an analytical, finance, or BI-related role.\n*   Very strong Excel and analysis skills; experience with BI/reporting tools (e.g., PowerBI, Looker, Tableau) is a plus.\n*   Excellent English, both written and spoken.\n*   Basic knowledge of process development is desirable.\n*   Quick, smart, curious – you recognize connections, think ahead, and work solution-oriented.\n*   Enjoy numbers, structure, efficiency, and a high-quality standard.\n*   Professional demeanor, discretion, and confident communication.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an ambitious individual with 1-2 years of experience to gain direct exposure to C-level strategy and operations within a fast-growing, data-driven company. The role offers significant autonomy, professional development, and the chance to drive impactful projects from day one. Ideal for someone who thrives in a remote-first, collaborative, and results-oriented environment, eager to shape the financial and operational future of an evolving business.
