**Ready for an exciting opportunity?  
**  
If you already hold a degree and have at least two years of work experience, our Career Changer Apprenticeship could be your next big move.  Whether you're looking to transition into a new industry or build on your existing skills in a fresh direction, this program is designed to support ambitious professionals ready for change.  
   
**Register your interest today** and take the first step toward a rewarding new career path.  
  
Design and build software solutions that harness the power of AI tools and modern development practices. You’ll gain hands-on experience in creating applications, platforms, and services that integrate AI capabilities to solve real-world business challenges and deliver meaningful impact.  
  
**To be eligible for the programme,** **you will need a 2:2 degree in a non-technical subject or equivalent Level 6 qualification (NVQ, Diploma, Certificate, or Award), GCSE Maths and English at Grade 4–9 (A\*–C), and 2 years of work experience in any field.  
  
This programme is available in Manchester and Newcastle.**
