**About this opportunity  
  
**

Are you passionate about integrated circuit design? Do you want to make a tangible impact on the next generation of Application-Specific Integrated Circuits (ASICs)? Here is your chance! We invite applications for a Master thesis project focused on thermal-aware floorplan practices for ASICs—a topic at the intersection of innovation, efficiency, and reliability.  
  
With the rise of high-performance and energy-efficient ASICs in everything from AI accelerators to consumer electronics, mastering the art of thermally-aware floorplan is both a technical necessity and a strategic advantage. Your work will help shape methodologies embraced by industry leaders and push the boundaries of what’s possible in modern chip design.

Advancements in semiconductor technology have ushered in a new era of miniaturization, with technology nodes shrinking to unprecedented scales. While these innovations enable higher integration and performance per unit area, they also introduce significant challenges associated with power consumption, area efficiency, and—critically—power density. As device features become more compact, the local heat generation can increase dramatically, leading to hotspots that threaten performance, reliability, and even the lifespan of modern ASICs.  
  

Thermal management is no longer a peripheral concern; it is central to both floorplan and the holistic design process. Traditional approaches to floorplan, often driven primarily by logic placement and interconnect minimization, must now integrate sophisticated strategies to mitigate thermal issues. This Master thesis aims to address these emerging challenges, contributing to a body of knowledge that is vital for the future of ASIC development.

**What you will do  
  
**

· Survey and Analyze prior projects and literature in ASIC floorplan, focusing on the evolution of thermal-aware methodologies.

· Develop and Implement optimization algorithms tailored for thermal management, balancing power, area, and performance constraints.

· Leverage AI Agents and Tools to intelligently explore the design space, evaluating a multitude of floorplan alternatives for thermal efficiency.

· Compile a Comprehensive List of best practices and actionable guidelines for thermal-friendly floorplan, building a resource for future designers.  
  

**The skills you bring  
  
**

This Master thesis is suitable for final-year Master’s students specializing in:

· Electronics

· Microelectronics

· Computer Engineering

· Or closely related fields  
  

You should have a strong interest in IC design, optimization techniques, and a willingness to engage with cutting-edge AI tools.  
  

**Thesis Highlights  
  
**

· Tackle Real-World Challenges: Engage with industry-relevant problems at advanced technology nodes, including power density and heat dissipation.

· Innovative Algorithms: Experiment with and benchmark both classic and modern optimization strategies for thermal management.

· AI-Driven Exploration: Utilize intelligent agents and toolchains to automate and enhance the search for optimal floorplan solutions.

· Practical Outcomes: Produce a detailed compendium of best practices for thermal-friendly design, with examples, illustrations, and clear recommendations.
