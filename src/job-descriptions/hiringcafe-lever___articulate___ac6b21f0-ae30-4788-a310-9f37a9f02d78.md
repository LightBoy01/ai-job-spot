**What You'll Do:**  
*   **Operate with a high degree of autonomy:** Set direction and make strategic decisions that shape the future of distribution at Articulate, while serving as a mentor and strategic resource for peers and junior team members.
*   **Develop vision and strategy:** Work as part of an empowered product team, owning the distribution product area’s strategic direction.
*   **Deliver our roadmap:** Translate the vision into actionable roadmaps, working flexibly and iteratively with key partners including tripod/triad leads (design, engineering), product marketing, and data to define, prioritize, implement, and deliver customer value. Anticipate risks, develop proactive solutions, and drive efficiency across cross-functional teams to ensure on-time, high-quality delivery.
*   **Lead continuous discovery:** Deeply understand our users, their needs, and their pain points. Gather and analyze qualitative and quantitative data to identify trends, opportunities, and areas for improvement.
*   **Define and analyze key metrics:** Establish clear goals and track progress towards them. Use data to measure outcomes and identify areas for optimization.
*   **Collaborate cross-functionally:** Work closely with go-to-market teams and cross-functional partners across the business to deliver powerful and intuitive experiences for our customers.
*   **Stay ahead of the curve:** Conduct market research and competitive analysis to identify emerging trends and opportunities across localization, AI, and Learning & Development.
*   **Create visibility and alignment:** Clearly communicate product strategy, roadmap updates, and progress to stakeholders at all levels, from your team to C-level executives.
  
  
**What You Should Have:**  
*   8+ years working on technology-powered products as a Product Manager or directly related experience (e.g. product designer, engineer, data analyst, data scientist, or user researcher). 
*   Strong analytical abilities, with the capability to gather and use both qualitative and quantitative data to inform hypotheses, guide strategy, make product decisions, and measure success.
*   Excellent communication skills, with experience managing and influencing stakeholders, and comfort presenting to C-level executives.
*   A history of leading cross-functional teams and influencing others without direct authority, driving success through collaboration.
*   Familiarity with modern product discovery techniques, with a focus on delivering products customers love.
*   Understanding of AI and potential applications.
  
  
**Nice to Haves:**  
*   Background in B2B SaaS products, ideally in the Learning & Development or EdTech industry.
*   Experience launching and growing new B2B SaaS products.
  
  

About us

  

Articulate Global, LLC, is the leading SaaS provider of creator platforms for online workplace training. Founded by Adam Schwartz in 2002, Articulate provides creator tools and services that make it simple for enterprises and SMBs to develop, deliver, and analyze online workplace training that’s engaging and effective.  Increasingly, organizations must reskill employees for ever-changing remote and hybrid work environments, create learning cultures that attract and retain employees in a tight labor market, and use training to build more equitable, empowering, and engaging workplaces. Articulate helps organizations address these critical business needs with its creator platform for workplace training. Articulate 360—a suite of creator tools for online courses—was named the 7th most-loved product in the world by TrustRadius in 2021. And [Rise](http://rise.com/)—an all-in-one online training system that makes online training easy to create, enjoyable to take, and simple to manage—is the first creator platform for SMBs and departments within the enterprise. Articulate has more than 118,000 customers in 170 countries and counts all 100 of the Fortune 100 companies as customers.
