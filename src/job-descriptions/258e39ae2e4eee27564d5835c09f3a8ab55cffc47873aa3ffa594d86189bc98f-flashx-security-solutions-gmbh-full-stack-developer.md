### Description
FlashX protects small and medium-sized enterprises from identity theft – by far the most common entry point for hackers.

With FlashX, a multi-tenant SaaS Identity and Access Management (IAM) platform from Germany that is already in productive use at numerous SMEs, we prevent stolen login credentials or weak multi-factor authentication (MFA) from causing significant damage. FlashX combines password/access controls, MFA, roles, and audits in an easy-to-use interface. Hosting in the Open Telekom Cloud (Deutsche Telekom) ensures the highest data protection standards and data sovereignty. Zero knowledge and GDPR compliant by design.

Compliance guidelines such as ISO 27001, BSI IT Baseline Protection, NIS2, and KRITIS mandate the use of IAM.

Implementation in minutes – independently or with trusted IT managed service partners.

We're looking for a skilled Full-Stack Developer with strong experience in Laravel (PHP) as the main backend framework, plus knowledge of browser extension development. You will take over the day-to-day development of our platform, including bug fixes, feature rollouts, QA, and small improvements. This role fills a critical gap between our current frontend and backend specialists – acting as the go-to dev for daily updates and ongoing platform evolution.

### Responsibilities
*   Handle daily bug fixes, UI tweaks, and feature requests
*   Maintain the Laravel core and connect it with browser extensions
*   Work across backend and frontend (React.js) when needed
*   Implement security features, including basic encryption and compliance logic
*   Assist with in-house QA, testing, and tracking
*   Collaborate with our frontend & DevOps team (React/Angular + Kubernetes/CI)

### Qualifications
To excel in this role, you should possess a strong foundation in full-stack development with a keen eye for security:

**Core Technical Skills:**
*   Deep experience maintaining and building Laravel (PHP) applications
*   Proven experience building or maintaining Chrome/Edge browser extensions
*   Comfortable working with React.js components for frontend development
*   Proficiency with MySQL (Laravel Eloquent ORM)
*   Basic encryption knowledge, capable of handling secure data logic and privacy enforcement
*   Familiarity with Node.js (for potential backend microservices in future phases)
*   Experience with QA testing, bug reporting, and Git
*   Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, Jira, GitHub, and Agile methodologies

**Bonus Skills:**
*   TypeScript, Tailwind, or Vue (not core to the stack but beneficial)

**Key Attributes:**
*   A Laravel 'pro' who can deliver independently and take ownership
*   Comfortable navigating browser extension APIs
*   Understands security best practices implicitly
*   Ability to start immediately on a contract basis
