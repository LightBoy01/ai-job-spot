### Description\nJoin Wolt, a dynamic technology company transforming local commerce, from restaurant food to \"almost everything\" delivery across 500+ cities in 30 countries. Following its merger with DoorDash, Wolt continues its ambitious global expansion.\n\nThis specific role is for a **Store Manager** within Wolt Market, our innovative grocery store concept focused on unparalleled convenience and customer experience. As a key player in our German growth, you will lead one of our stores, having a strong influence on city operations and directly shaping the future of grocery shopping. This is a unique opportunity to contribute significantly to one of Europe's fastest-growing companies.\n\n*Please note: This is a fixed-term position for 12 months, starting from the beginning of March 2026.*\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a Store Manager, you will be instrumental in the day-to-day success and strategic growth of your Wolt Market store. Your core responsibilities include:\n\n*   **Team Leadership & Development**:\n    *   Lead, grow, and motivate a team, including 2-4 Shift Leads, guiding, coaching, and developing them according to Wolt's leadership values.\n    *   Ensure Shift Leads are equipped with the necessary knowledge and tools for smooth day-to-day operations, including freshness, inventory, and stock management.\n    *   Manage the entire employee lifecycle for your team, including hiring, training, providing daily feedback, and conducting bi-annual 1:1 performance reviews and goal setting.\n    *   Foster a positive and productive work environment, organizing team events a couple of times a year.\n*   **Operational Excellence**:\n    *   Collaborate closely with the Operations Team to continuously improve store processes and ensure fast delivery and fluent operations.\n    *   Actively participate in internal and external meetings to drive operational efficiency.\n    *   Monitor shrink levels and implement strategies to control food waste effectively.\n    *   Manage shift scheduling efficiently to meet operational demands.\n    *   Oversee the handling of incoming goods and store orders, ensuring optimal stock levels.\n    *   Ensure the store and colleagues have all necessary resources for rapid growth, from office supplies and IT devices to new fridges.\n*   **Cross-Functional Collaboration**:\n    *   Work in close collaboration and communication with other Wolt functions to ensure an optimal selection and range of products for sale.\n\n### Qualifications\nTo succeed in this challenging and rewarding role, we are looking for a candidate who possesses:\n\n*   Proven leadership experience with a demonstrated ability to motivate and develop teams.\n*   Experience working in a grocery store environment is a significant plus.\n*   A fast learner with excellent multitasking abilities.\n*   Proactive, service-minded, and possessing a strong \"can-do\" attitude.\n*   Comfortable and thrives in a fast-paced, entrepreneurial environment.\n*   Flexibility to work during days, evenings, and weekends as required by store operations.\n*   Data-driven and analytical mindset, capable of using data to inform decisions and drive improvements.\n*   Fluent in English; proficiency in German is considered a valuable asset.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a high-impact, hands-on leadership opportunity within Wolt Market, a rapidly expanding division of a global tech company. As a Store Manager, you'll be at the forefront of defining the future of grocery delivery in Germany, leading a team and optimizing critical operations. While the role is demanding and requires an entrepreneurial spirit, it offers unparalleled opportunities for professional growth and the chance to make a tangible difference. The fixed-term nature of 12 months should be noted, but for an ambitious and data-driven operational leader, this role promises an intense learning curve and significant contributions that could open doors to further opportunities within Wolt’s fast-paced ecosystem. It's an ideal fit for someone passionate about operational excellence, team development, and customer satisfaction in a dynamic environment.
