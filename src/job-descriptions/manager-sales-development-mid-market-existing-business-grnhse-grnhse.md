If you are ready to take a team of diverse, mission-driven sales professionals to the next level, we want to speak with you!</p>
<p><strong>What You Will Do:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Build and train members of the Mid Market Sales Development team to identify, contact, and create qualified opportunities&nbsp;</li>
<li>Help to define performance metrics by which SDRs will be measured</li>
<li>Coach SDRs through shadowing, role plays, process improvement, and performance reporting in recurring 1:1’s</li>
<li>Partner with the marketing, Account Management teams to develop campaigns that align to their respective goals based on a territory plan</li>
<li>Leverage creative marketing and sales tactics to help the SDR team prospect and engage with multiple buyer personas to introduce Zoominfo’s value</li>
<li>Manage SDR reports and dashboards to ensure the results they deliver can be easily understood by stakeholders throughout the organization</li>
<li>Iterate on the sales development process, methodology, campaigns, hiring profiles, training and enablement with respect to the Zoominfo core values</li>
<li>Assist with recruiting, hiring, and onboarding new Sales Development Representatives</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>What You Bring:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>1+ years management experience in Mid-Market or Enterprise sales or sales development that demonstrates dependability, work ethic, and strong desire to lead a successful sales team</li>
<li>A proven track record of delivering sales pipeline at a fast growing company with multiple sales segments within an outbound model</li>
<li>A deep understanding of the outbound SDR motion and how to motivate early career sellers.</li>
<li>A drive to continue innovation and evolution of the SDR role through usage of AI and other tools and process changes.</li>
<li>Experience selling a GTM solution is a major plus!</li>
<li>Ability to attract, retain, and motivate exceptional team members</li>
<li>Experience prospecting into Mid-Market accounts (100 - 1000 employee headcount orgs)</li>
<li>Excellent active listening skills with genuine curiosity used to easily collaborate with both prospects and colleagues</li>
<li>A passion for customers and the desire to build authentic relationships</li>
<li>Have a general understanding of ZoomInfo offerings</li>
<li>A broad knowledge of the sales technology ecosystem, including Salesforce</li>
<li>Experience with prospecting tools like Outreach, ZoomInfo</li>
</ul>
