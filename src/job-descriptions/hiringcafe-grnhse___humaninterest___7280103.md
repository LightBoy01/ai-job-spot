**About the role**

The Productivity Tools Program Manager is responsible for the strategic oversight and lifecycle management of the Revenue organization’s technology stack. This role combines tools strategy with ownership of initiatives to select, configure, and apply the software to maximum effect. Areas of focus include data analysis, vendor negotiations, strategies for tool adoption, and continual optimization. They will be the primary driver for identifying and investigating new solutions, managing SaaS procurement, and programs to ensure that our productivity tools directly support strategic objectives.

**What you get to do every day**

*   Serve as the strategic lead and subject matter expert for productivity tools used by the Revenue organization as we research, evaluate, implement, and decommission SaaS products.
*   Own and execute programs that contribute to successful tool adoption, ensuring seamless transitions, optimal configuration, and maximal return on software investments.
*   Revise workflows to incorporate new features and AI efficiencies. Train agents and tune large language models to enhance productivity among end-users.
*   Maintain a tool stack by tracking renewal dates, assessing vendor performance and leading negotiations to secure favorable terms and pricing.
*   Conduct deep analysis of tool usage and outcomes to develop strategic insights that justify spending, inform decision-making, and highlight opportunities for greater efficiency.
*   Propose adoption of new tools and renewal of existing contracts, outlining business cases, ROI, and integration plans in written reports to leadership.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional leadership to align the productivity tools roadmap with broader organizational goals and address complex business challenges. 
*   Oversee user assistance, account provisioning, and enablement support, delegating tasks to a specialist or administrator while remaining focused on strategic guidance.
*   Other duties as assigned by the business.

**What you bring to the role**

*   Demonstrated senior-level experience in SaaS management, vendor relations, or IT strategy, including successful contract negotiations.
*   A history of effective project management, including roadmapping, task delegation, progress tracking, and ultimately achieving desired business outcomes.
*   Proven experience leveraging AI tools to enhance work efficiency. This includes expertise in refining prompts, training AI agents, and effectively manipulating Large Language Models (LLMs) to consistently produce valuable outputs.
*   Advanced proficiency in data analysis and reporting, capable of translating complex data from tool logs and system reports into clear, actionable business insights.
*   The capability to create and present concise business proposals to senior leadership.
*   Deep expertise in the administration and deployment of core business tools, such as CRM and sales enablement platforms.
*   Exceptional communication and negotiation skills, with the ability to influence outcomes and build strong relationships with both vendors and internal stakeholders.
*   Superior ability to organize and prioritize, effectively managing multiple high-stakes initiatives with minimal supervision.
