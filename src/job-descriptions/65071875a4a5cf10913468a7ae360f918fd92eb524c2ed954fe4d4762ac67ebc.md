**RWE Supply & Trading Asia-Pacific Pte. Ltd., Human Resources**

**To start as soon as possible**

**3months Internship**

**About the role**

We are looking for a detail-oriented **HR Documentation Intern** to support our HR Operations team. The intern will help gather and organise initial insights on employment regulations and HR-related requirements across **Singapore, Australia, China, Japan, India, and Indonesia**. This role involves using web searches and AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT) to compile structured summaries that will serve as a starting point for deeper analysis by the HR team.

*   Use web searches and AI tools to obtain preliminary information on HR regulations and employment practices in the assigned countries.
*   Document insights on HR processes (e.g., hiring, onboarding, payroll, termination, statutory benefits, compliance requirements).
*   Organise information into clear and structured templates for internal use.
*   Flag areas where information may be incomplete, inconsistent, or require further expert validation.
*   Support the HR team with other documentation or administrative tasks as needed.

**Job requirements and experience**

*   Currently pursuing a degree in **Human Resources, Business, Law, or a related field**.
*   Strong ability to search, read, and summarise information clearly and concisely.
*   Comfortable using AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT) and online resources for fact-finding.
*   Good attention to detail and organisational skills.
*   Interest in international HR operations and employment regulations.
*   Proficient in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint).

**Advantageous, but not essential**

*   Ability to read and write Chinese or Japanese will be advantageous.
