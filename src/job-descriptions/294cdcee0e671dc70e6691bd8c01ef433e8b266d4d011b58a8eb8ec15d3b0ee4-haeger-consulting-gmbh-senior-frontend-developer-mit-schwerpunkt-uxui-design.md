### Description\nJoin our dynamic and creative team as a Senior Frontend Developer, where you'll shape modern web projects using cutting-edge technologies like Angular or Vue. In this pivotal role, you'll not only develop high-quality, user-centric web applications with a keen eye for UX, design, and technical excellence but also act as a trusted advisor to our clients. You'll work in an agile environment that values innovation, collaboration, and continuous improvement, offering ample opportunities for personal and professional growth. We emphasize a healthy work-life balance through flexible hours and remote work options, fostering a supportive and collegial team spirit.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Implement modern web projects with Angular or Vue in close collaboration with our clients.\n- Advise client teams on the selection and integration of UI component libraries such as Material-UI (MUI) or Angular Material.\n- Develop high-quality, user-centric web applications focusing on UX, design, and technical excellence, always prioritizing client needs.\n- Ensure an excellent user experience and appealing UI design, actively contributing suggestions for improvement.\n- Optimize client applications regarding performance, responsiveness, and maintainability.\n- Work closely with backend and client teams to develop holistic solutions, taking on minor UX/design tasks as needed.\n- Uphold clean code principles, regularly conduct refactorings, and contribute to the continuous improvement of the codebase.\n- Be responsible for the maintenance and further development of existing client applications.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You have at least 5 years of experience in frontend development, ideally in client projects, and possess profound knowledge in UX/UI design.\n- You are proficient in HTML5, CSS3, SCSS, JavaScript, and TypeScript, and have expert knowledge of Angular or Vue frameworks.\n- You have experience with UI component libraries such as MUI, Angular Material, or similar, and are familiar with UX principles and responsive web design.\n- You work confidently with design systems and tools like Figma or Adobe XD.\n- You have experience with state management solutions such as Redux, Vuex, or NgRx.\n- You confidently integrate RESTful or GraphQL APIs and test your applications with Jest, Cypress, or comparable tools.\n- You utilize modern tooling environments like Webpack, npm, or yarn.\n- You enjoy working in a team, are communicative, and bring a consulting mindset.\n- You speak German at a native level and can communicate effortlessly in English.\n- Ideally, you have experience with Node.js (e.g., for SSR, tooling, or full-stack scenarios) and knowledge of implementing accessible web applications.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a Senior Frontend Developer passionate about crafting exceptional digital experiences and advising clients. The company truly walks the talk when it comes to fostering a collaborative, flexible, and growth-oriented environment. You'll thrive here if you enjoy challenging client projects, continuous learning, and being part of a team that genuinely values cohesion and innovative thinking. With flexible working hours and remote options, a strong emphasis on knowledge transfer, and an attractive compensation package, this role offers a compelling blend of professional challenge and personal well-being.
