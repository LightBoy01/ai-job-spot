<p><strong>Your Opportunity</strong></p>
<p> </p>
<p>We are seeking a visionary technology leader to define and drive our enterprise-wide AI strategy. This role is pivotal in transforming how we work, serve customers, and deliver value—through the integration of AI, automation, and intelligent workflows. As a senior member of the IT leadership team, you will operate across functions to identify opportunities, build capabilities, and embed AI-driven thinking into the company’s strategic and operational core.</p>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>Your Impact</strong></p>
<p> </p>
<ul>
<li>You will create clarity in uncertainty by translating complex AI trends into actionable strategies that align with business priorities.</li>
<li>Know your business by partnering with process owners and functional leaders to reimagine workflows using AI, automation, and intelligent decisioning.</li>
<li>Communicate to inspire action by evangelizing AI’s potential across the enterprise, influencing stakeholders at all levels, and fostering a culture of innovation and responsible enablement.</li>
</ul>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>Key Responsibilities</strong></p>
<p> </p>
<ul>
<li><strong>AI Strategy Leadership</strong>: Define and evolve the company-wide AI and automation roadmap.</li>
<li><strong>Enablement &amp; Governance</strong>: Build learning programs, governance frameworks, and tools that empower business units to adopt AI responsibly and effectively.</li>
<li><strong>Process Transformation</strong>: Lead cross-functional efforts to redesign workflows using AI and automation.</li>
<li><strong>Technology Evaluation</strong>: Stay current on AI trends and implement relevant technologies (e.g., LLMs, RPA, ML platforms).</li>
<li><strong>Use Case Development</strong>: Identify high-impact opportunities, pilot solutions, and scale successful initiatives.</li>
<li><strong>Impact Measurement</strong>: Define and track metrics to measure the value and ROI of AI initiatives.</li>
<li><strong>Stakeholder Engagement</strong>: Serve as a trusted advisor to executives and educate teams on AI’s role and value.</li>
<li><strong>Team Building</strong>: Recruit and lead a multidisciplinary team focused on AI strategy, data science, and automation engineering.</li>
<li><strong>Ethics &amp; Risk Oversight</strong>: Establish AI ethics, security, and compliance guidelines in partnership with Legal, Security, and Data Governance.</li>
<li><strong>Vendor Management</strong>: Manage relationships with key AI partners and evaluate build vs. buy decisions.</li>
</ul>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>Your Qualifications</strong></p>
<p> </p>
<ul>
<li>5+ years in technology, innovation, or transformation roles, with 3–5 years in AI/ML or intelligent automation.</li>
<li>Proven success in implementing enterprise AI strategies that deliver measurable business value.</li>
<li>Strong foundation in business process engineering and automation platforms.</li>
<li>Familiarity with AI technologies including NLP, generative AI, ML, and computer vision.</li>
<li>Experience collaborating across business, engineering, and product teams.</li>
<li>Executive presence and ability to influence at all levels.</li>
<li>Knowledge of AI ethics and governance frameworks is a plus.</li>
<li>Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Data Science, Business, or related field.</li>
<li>#VH-1</li>
</ul>
