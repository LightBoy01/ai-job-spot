Headquartered in Tokyo, Japan, Evident employs around 4,500 people at a total of 57 locations worldwide. Evident Europe, headquartered in Hamburg, Germany, employs approximately 770 people in 16 countries. The product portfolio includes a wide range of microscope systems used in clinical diagnostics, medical and basic research, as well as in various industrial areas. In addition, Evident offers videoscopes, products for non-destructive testing techniques and for X-ray fluorescence analysis.

Tasks
-----

*   Lead a team of Product Master Data Specialists:
    
*   Manage the daily execution of product data tasks for the Americas and EMEA region, in line with business priorities
    
*   Monitor and balance the team’s workload and ensure availability
    
*   Own business critical KPIs including the item creation cycle time
    
*   Own the global end-to-end Product Master Data Management (P-MDM) process:
    
*   Ensure effective integration with the New Product Development process, and all major business processes
    
*   Coordinate and closely collaborate with the community of process stakeholders
    
*   Set-up and roll-out a set of operational KPI, covering the process end-to-end
    
*   Continuously strive for improvement & process automation to increase efficiency and quality in master data management
    
*   Partner with Master Data Governance and other IT functions on process design, requirements, and roadmapDrive further harmonization of global P-MDM processes and data standards in collaboration with other functions
