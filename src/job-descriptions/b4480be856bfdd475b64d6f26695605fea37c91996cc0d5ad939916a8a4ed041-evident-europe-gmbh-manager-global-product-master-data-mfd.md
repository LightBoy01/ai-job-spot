### Description
Evident, headquartered in Tokyo, Japan, with a significant European presence in Hamburg, Germany, is a global leader in scientific solutions. Employing around 4,500 people worldwide across 57 locations, Evident Europe contributes approximately 770 employees in 16 countries. Our diverse product portfolio spans advanced microscope systems for clinical diagnostics, medical and basic research, industrial applications, as well as videoscopes, non-destructive testing equipment, and X-ray fluorescence analysis. We are seeking a dedicated and experienced leader to join our team as a Team Lead Product Master Data Management (P-MDM) to drive efficiency and quality across our global product data landscape.

### Responsibilities
- Lead a team of Product Master Data Specialists, managing daily execution of product data tasks for the Americas and EMEA region, in line with business priorities.
- Monitor and balance the team’s workload and ensure availability.
- Own business critical KPIs, including the item creation cycle time.
- Own the global end-to-end Product Master Data Management (P-MDM) process, ensuring effective integration with the New Product Development process and all major business processes.
- Coordinate and closely collaborate with the community of process stakeholders.
- Set-up and roll-out a set of operational KPIs, covering the process end-to-end.
- Continuously strive for improvement & process automation to increase efficiency and quality in master data management.
- Partner with Master Data Governance and other IT functions on process design, requirements, and roadmap.
- Drive further harmonization of global P-MDM processes and data standards in collaboration with other functions.

### Qualifications
- University Degree.
- Experienced in managing product master data.
- Demonstrated strong experience in project management.
- Pronounced analytical and conceptual skills, very data-oriented approach.
- Exceptional end-to-end process understanding and mindset.
- Strong solution orientation and proactive behavior.
- Ability to matrix well across all functions.
- \"Hands-on mentality\" and flexibility to take operational tasks within the team.
- Strong command of English.
- Team player with strong communication and interaction skills.
