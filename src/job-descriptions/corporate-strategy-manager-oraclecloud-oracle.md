<p><strong>What is the role of Corporate Strategy at Definity Financial?</strong></p>
<p>This role will be joining the Corporate Strategy team that focuses on Strategy Development &amp; Innovation. The core mandate of this team is as follows:&nbsp;</p>
<ul>
 <li>In-house strategy consultants that bring unbiased and unique perspectives through excelling in problem solving, storytelling, and connecting the dots between markets, customers, products, and enterprise strategies</li>
 <li>Monitor external environment, macroeconomic trends and industry forces impacting the business</li>
 <li>Operate across all lines of businesses (Personal, Commercial), distribution channels (broker, MGAs, direct), core operations (Claims, Sales &amp; Distribution) and support functions</li>
 <li>Support leaders to develop and refine corporate and business line strategies and formulate point-of-views to respond to and proactively prepare for market developments</li>
 <li>Lead development for strategies that impact multiple P&amp;Ls / functions; support strategy development across owned subsidiaries</li>
 <li>Advise ELT &amp; Board on enterprise response to known/emerging strategic risks</li>
 <li>Lead a cross-functional team that guides enterprise innovation efforts</li>
 <li>Lead communication of Corporate Strategy to employees and investors</li>
 <li>Support transition of developed strategies into execution</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>What can you expect in this role?</strong></p>
<ul>
 <li>Act as an “in-house management consultant” – apply strategic thinking to solve complex Corporate Strategy problems</li>
 <li>Be constantly challenged to solve problems that each require unique problem-solving methods and frameworks</li>
 <li>Independently drive analysis to arrive at options and recommendations using a hypothesis and data driven approach</li>
 <li>Support deliverables that shape decision-making by the executive team and Board of Directors</li>
 <li>Engage with a wide spectrum of functional executives and senior leaders across the organization&nbsp;</li>
 <li>Support the enterprise innovation agenda by leading the research &amp; development of POVs on emerging / new to company ideas working with a cross-functional team&nbsp;</li>
 <li>Develop presentation materials that communicate information and ideas effectively and simply&nbsp;</li>
 <li>Do all this in a fun and supportive team led by leaders who encourage pursuit of your passions and ambitions; be an active contributor to shape the high-performance team culture&nbsp;</li>
 <li>Work-life balance and flexibility with no overnight travel; hybrid flexibility with minimum of 3 days / week in-office presence required</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>What do you bring to the role?</strong></p>
<ul>
 <li>7+ years of strategy experience gained at a top-tier management consulting firm, internal corporate strategy role, or equivalent; experience in both consulting and in-house environments is a strong asset</li>
 <li>Experience in P&amp;C insurance or broader financial services is preferred but not required; ability to quickly learn industry dynamics is essential</li>
 <li>Proven track record in evaluating new business opportunities, developing go-to-market strategies, and conducting business model assessments</li>
 <li>Demonstrated ability to build business cases and translate strategic recommendations into actionable plans</li>
 <li>Strong business acumen with a demonstrated aptitude for strategic, analytical, and critical thinking in ambiguous and fast-changing environments</li>
 <li>Experience in leading and influencing cross-functional teams, with the ability to challenge perspectives and facilitate robust strategic discussions</li>
 <li>Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to craft compelling presentations and executive-level materials</li>
 <li>Acute attention to detail and a commitment to producing high-quality, iterative work products</li>
 <li>Ability to manage multiple priorities, meet deadlines, and perform under pressure in a dynamic environment</li>
 <li>High degree of personal integrity, discretion, and professionalism in handling sensitive and confidential information</li>
 <li>Expert proficiency in Microsoft PowerPoint and Excel is required; familiarity with strategy tools (e.g., market sizing, competitive analysis, financial modeling) and AI tools for strategy development is a plus</li>
 <li>Experience presenting to senior executives and/or boards is a strong asset</li>
</ul>
<p>Salary range is $77,100 - $142,000</p>
