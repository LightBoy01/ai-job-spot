Are you looking to kick-start your marketing career in a role that combines creativity, data, and cutting-edge technology? At Everbridge, we have a fantastic opportunity for an early-career marketer to join our Global Demand Center as a Demand Generation Marketing Associate. This is a remote/homebased role to be based anywhere in the UK. In this role, you’ll partner with our EMEA go-to-market teams to design and deliver multichannel campaigns that drive measurable pipeline and revenue. You’ll gain hands-on experience with leading marketing tools such as Marketo, Salesforce, and AI platforms, giving you a strong foundation for a career in demand generation or marketing operations.  
  
**What you'll do:**  
*   Build and deliver campaigns in Marketo, Salesforce, and AI tools, including emails, nurture tracks, and landing pages.
*   Manage list segmentation, data uploads, and database hygiene to maintain accuracy and integrity.
*   Support promotion of digital and in-person events through multichannel campaign execution.
*   Analyze campaign performance and reporting data, providing insights to improve ROI and pipeline contribution.
*   Contribute to the optimization of lead scoring models and lead management processes.
*   Troubleshoot and resolve campaign and workflow issues.
*   Learn, develop, and grow your skills in MarTech, Salesforce, and AI tools to modernize and optimize demand generation programs.
*   Collaborate with marketing teams and external agencies to ensure consistent execution and messaging across campaigns.
  
  
**What you'll bring:**  
*   1+ years of B2B SaaS demand generation experience.
*   Hands-on experience executing campaigns in Marketo and Salesforce, with an understanding of data workflows.
*   Familiarity with AI, MarTech, or analytics tools, with a drive for continuous learning to modernize and optimize demand generation.
*   Strong project coordination skills—comfortable managing timelines, tasks, and stakeholders to keep campaigns on track.
*   Excellent attention to detail, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills.
*   Strong communication skills; English fluency (written, verbal, and presentation).
*   Curiosity and ambition to grow into more advanced demand generation/marketing operations roles.
*   Experience working with remote, global teams is desirable but not essential.
*   Bachelor or Postgraduate Degree in a STEM subject (or equivalent) preferred.
