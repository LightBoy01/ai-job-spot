**About Zulubot:**
Zulubot is the integrated production studio of Zulu Alpha Kilo. With a multidisciplinary team of editors, developers, AI specialists, motion designers, graphic artists, and producers, Zulubot brings ideas to life through nimble, full-spectrum production, from live shoots to post-production, motion design, creator collaborations, and AI powered content solutions.
Born out of client demand for faster, more nimble production, Zulubot was launched a decade ago to meet the needs of modern content creation. What began as a future-forward idea is now a 30+ person strong studio that includes edit suites, an audio suite, photography capabilities, and a multidisciplinary team of editors, developers, motion designers, graphic artists, and producers.
Over the past decade, Zulubot has helped bring to life some of the agency’s most innovative and talked-about work. From producing and building more than 100 AR lenses for brands on Snapchat, to editing documentary films like the award-winning “Harley-Davidson: Common Ground” and Tim Hortons’ “The Away Game”, Zulubot has always pushed the boundaries of content and craft. For New York-based SingleCut Beersmiths, the team created Notes IPA—the first beer that teaches you how to play guitar using augmented reality.
With over 10 years under our belt, we’re looking to take Zulubot to the next level by expanding our AI capabilities and imbedding AI creation within our team.
**Position Overview:**
As our Digital Project Manager/Product Owner, you’ll oversee digital projects from concept to delivery, ensuring work is resourced, scheduled, and executed at the highest level. You’ll be the go-to contact for both internal teams and clients, balancing creative vision with technical execution. This role is a 3-month contact role with potential to grow into a full time position.
Project types include:
*   Websites (WordPress, React, Next.js)
*   AI-powered experiences
*   AR lenses and 3D content
*   Innovation/emerging technology focused campaigns
*   Interactive and experiential marketing projects
#### **Key** **Responsibilities:**
*   Own the process: Manage incoming digital projects, assess feasibility, create budgets and detailed workback schedules.
*   Resource smartly: Prioritize in-house talent, supplement with freelancers or vendors as needed.
*   Manage multiple projects: Manage digital projects, have daily check ins with them team to make sure all projects are on track
*   Lead production: Partner with agency producers, account managers, and creatives to deliver on time, on budget, and on brief.
*   Inspire innovation: Champion emerging tech opportunities and integrate new tools/processes into our workflow.
*   Grow the network: Maintain and expand a roster of top-tier digital innovation freelancers.
#### What We’re Looking For
*   Proven experience leading digital production for websites, AI/AR, and emerging tech projects.
*   Entrepreneurial mindset, taking ownership from pitch to delivery.
*   Track record of delivering design, development, 3D, and content creation projects for major brands.
*   Strong client-facing communication skills with the ability to translate complex digital concepts.
*   Passion for continuous learning and staying ahead of industry trends.
#### Qualifications
*   5+ years managing digital projects in an agency, brand, or platform environment.
*   Solid understanding of design, development, and digital content workflows.
*   Experience with platforms like WordPress, React, Next.js, AR/3D, and campaign websites.
*   Skilled in budgeting, scheduling, and resource management.
*   Experience working with large brands and navigating complex approval processes.
*   Nice to have: Scrum Master (CSM), Product Owner (CSPO), or similar Agile certification.
