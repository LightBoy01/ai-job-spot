<p>C3 AI (NYSE: AI), is the Enterprise AI application software company. C3 AI delivers a family of fully integrated products including the C3 Agentic AI Platform, an end-to-end platform for developing, deploying, and operating enterprise AI applications, C3 AI applications, a portfolio of industry-specific SaaS enterprise AI applications that enable the digital transformation of organizations globally, and C3 Generative AI, a suite of domain-specific generative AI offerings for the enterprise.&nbsp;Learn more at: <strong>C3 AI</strong></p><p>C3 AI is looking for a manager to provide leadership and direction to the applications software engineering team of bright and ambitious self-starters like yourself. You will be part of a fast-moving organization with highly motivated engineers who tackle challenging and impactful problems. This management position is highly visible, will require frequent communication and interactions with the senior leadership team, and will require a driven individual taking on the responsibilities to develop and shape the product roadmap and strategic team goals.</p>
<p><strong>&nbsp;Responsibilities:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Be passionate about driving and leading your team to success and help them to excel.</li>
<li>Grow the Applications Engineering team by hiring, developing, training, and mentoring internal and external engineering team members.</li>
<li>Communicate regularly with senior management, technical architects, and other teams across C3 AI.</li>
<li>Lead the release and sprint planning efforts: prioritize tasks for the Applications Engineering team and ensure products meet internal and external timeline commitments.</li>
<li>Drive C3 AI Applications R&amp;D efforts: stay up to date with the latest relevant technical innovation and lead the development to drive new innovation into C3 AI products.</li>
<li>Have the ability to be a hands-on contributor during the entire development cycle from conception and design through release.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Qualifications:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Bachelor’s degree in computer science or related field; advanced degree preferred.</li>
<li>3 or more years of work experience in an analytics software company.</li>
<li>Significant leadership experience managing highly technical teams.</li>
<li>Thrive in a fast-paced environment with a willingness to dive deep and to get things done.</li>
<li>Demonstrated project management experience.</li>
<li>High level of expertise in agile software development.</li>
<li>End-to-end ownership of projects.</li>
<li>Stellar listening, verbal communication, and written communication skills.</li>
<li>Extensive software design and architecture skills.</li>
<li>Thorough knowledge of data structures, algorithms, profiling/optimization, and object-oriented and functional programming.</li>
<li>Strong programming skills, ideally JavaScript.</li>
<li>Experience with building high-performance, large-scale, and user-friendly software applications and tools.</li>
<li>A disciplined approach to testing and quality assurance.</li>
</ul>
