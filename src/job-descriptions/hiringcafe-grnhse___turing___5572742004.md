**Job Summary**
---------------

Turing is seeking a talented and detail-oriented Graphic Designer, Integrated Marketing to produce visually compelling designs across diverse marketing channels. This role focuses on creating on-brand advertising campaigns, presentation templates, brand guidelines, digital assets, and integrated marketing initiatives.

Collaborating closely with marketing designers embedded across business units, you’ll maintain and evolve Turing’s visual identity, leveraging the latest creative tools and staying ahead of design trends to deliver exceptional brand experiences.

**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------

*   **Design and Production:** Create compelling graphics and creative assets for integrated marketing initiatives including advertising campaigns, social media, email, presentations, digital content, and events.
*   **Brand Consistency:** Support and evolve Turing’s visual design system and brand guidelines, maintaining templates, design libraries, and visual standards across internal and external communications.
*   **Collaboration and Alignment:** Partner closely with embedded marketing designers, copywriters, and internal stakeholders to ensure cohesive visual storytelling and consistent brand execution across teams.
*   **Presentation and Asset Development:** Develop polished, professional, end-to-end presentation templates that minimize the need for end-user customization (Google Slides, PowerPoint); and creation of other marketing collateral that clearly communicate brand messaging and positioning.
*   **Market and Trend Awareness:** Stay current with the latest design trends, tools, and technologies to continuously improve creative quality, innovation, and execution speed.

**Requirements**
----------------

*   5 years of graphic design experience, preferably in technology, AI, SaaS, or similar industries.
*   Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign), Figma, and Canva
*   Strong working knowledge of presentation tools (Google Slides and PowerPoint). Deep understanding of master slides, theme building, and layout systems in both platforms.
*   Experience leveraging generative AI tools for design (e.g., Midjourney, Runway, Adobe Firefly) to enhance creative output and efficiency.  
      
    
*   Strong understanding of branding, visual storytelling, and integrated marketing principles.
*   Demonstrated experience with design systems, brand guidelines, and visual identity management across diverse channels.
*   Excellent collaboration, organizational, and communication skills, able to handle multiple projects simultaneously.
*   Self-motivated, proactive learner comfortable working remotely in a dynamic, fast-paced environment.
*   Proven experience designing client-facing presentations, such as pitch decks and sales materials, with a focus on clear visual storytelling, data visualization, and adherence to brand standards.

**Preferred Qualifications**
----------------------------

*   Experience designing within AI, tech, or rapidly scaling startup environments.
*   Familiarity with motion graphics, video editing software (After Effects, Premiere Pro), or AI-driven motion tools.
*   Portfolio showcasing integrated marketing campaigns, presentation materials (powerpoint and/or google slides), and consistent brand execution across channels.
*   Familiarity with global and multi-audience brand challenges.
