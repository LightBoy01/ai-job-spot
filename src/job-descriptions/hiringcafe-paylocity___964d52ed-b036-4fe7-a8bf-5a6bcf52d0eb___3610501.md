Description

Tivly helps more than a million small-business owners each year get the insurance they need—fast. We believe a small, senior team super-charged by AI out-delivers a large org. Cursor, Claude, Copilot, and Blitzy are your everyday co-workers, so you’ll spend far more time shipping code than sitting in meetings.

  

  

  

**WHY THIS ROLE IS DIFFERENT**

*   70–80% hands-on coding; zero head-count KPI. We grow output with AI, not people.
*   Direct access to executives — shape product and architecture without inheriting a hiring budget.
*   AI at every layer: Blitzy for repo-wide refactors, Cursor/Claude/Copilot for the inner loop, CodeRabbit for automated PR reviews.
*   $3,000 annual AI stipend + paid seats for any tool you need.
*   10% exploration time each sprint to try new agents, write internal posts, or contribute to OSS.

  

  

**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

*   Design, build, and ship features across Laravel, PHP/Laravel for front-end and back-end on AWS.
*   Orchestrate large Blitzy runs for sweeping refactors; review and harden the resulting PRs.
*   Establish AI-assisted development standards—prompt patterns, validation gates, automated testing.
*   Drive technical strategy with Product, ensuring we solve the right problems, not just write more lines of code.
*   Automate CI/CD and infrastructure with Docker, Kubernetes, and Terraform so deployment stays self-service and fast.

  

Requirements

  

  

**YOU’LL THRIVE HERE IF YOU**

*   Bring 8+ years of production web experience and have nuanced points of view on various AI coding tools.
*   Default to writing tests even when AI drafts the first pass.
*   Have been an engineering management leader before, but would prefer to focus on technical leadership and individual contribution.
*   Communicate crisply—concise RFCs, Loom walk-throughs, thoughtful code reviews.
*   Stay current on AI research, blogs, and podcasts, and can cite a recent insight that changed your perspective.

  

  

**OUR STACK**

*   Front-end: Laravel, React
*   Back-end: PHP/Laravel 
*   Data: MySQL
*   DevOps: AWS, Docker
*   Gen-AI: Blitzy, Cursor, Claude Code, CodeRabbit

  

  

**COMPENSATION & PERKS**

*   Competitive salary
*   Health, dental, vision, and 401(k) with match
*   15 paid PTO days per year and  company holidays
*   Company-sponsored annual off-site (travel & lodging covered)
*   Remote-first culture with flexible hours across US time zones
