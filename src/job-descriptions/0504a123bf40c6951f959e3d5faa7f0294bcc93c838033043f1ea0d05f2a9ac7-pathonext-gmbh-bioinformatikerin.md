This role is crucial for advancing our diagnostic capabilities through the development and optimization of cutting-edge bioinformatic analysis procedures and NGS workflows.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop, validate, and verify bioinformatic analysis procedures in accordance with the requirements of an accredited laboratory.\n- Implement and optimize automated Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) workflows for efficient and quality-assured data processing.\n- Analyze and interpret NGS data in a diagnostic environment, and conduct exploratory data analysis for research and development projects.\n\n### Qualifications\n- University degree in Bioinformatics, Computational Biology, Statistics, Computer Science, Biology, or a related field.\n- Professional experience in NGS data analysis.\n- Good programming skills (Python, R, bash).\n- Experience in Multi-omics, Machine Learning, and Workflow Management Systems (nextflow, snakemake) is a plus.\n- Ideally, prior knowledge of CLC Genomics Workbench (QIAGEN) software.\n- Good communication and organizational skills.\n- Good written and spoken English and German language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a Bioinformatician to make a tangible impact in a modern, DAkkS accredited laboratory. The company offers a vibrant, international environment with a strong commitment to professional growth and work-life balance through family-friendly hours and a culture of open communication. If you're passionate about pushing the boundaries of diagnostic bioinformatics and value a supportive team, this role offers a clear path to a fulfilling career.
