The Conversational AI Solution Specialist is responsible for the seamless operation and performance enhancement of dedicated CAI solutions within an assigned BU. Alongside these operational duties, they play a crucial role in facilitating the roll-out of new CAI solutions, troubleshooting, risk mitigation, and providing first-line support for Incident and Bug resolution.

**What you do**

*   Facilitate identification of new CAI solutions by consulting on local requirements, specifications, and limitations.
*   Conduct initial analysis for potential solution improvements.
*   Secure stakeholder buy-in through collection of necessary approvals.
*   Prepare successful CAI solution roll-out with actions including privacy impact assessments, customer journey gap assessment, and solution requirements document updates.
*   Execute localization and configuration of provided global CAI assets as per defined local CAI solution requirements.
*   Set up NLU structure, ensuring optimized intent recognition of the CAI solution.
*   Perform CAI solution staging and deployment following best practices and quality standards.
*   Prepare and conduct functional testing and UAT and generate insightful results.
*   Conduct regular performance testing and monitoring of CAI solutions, engaging in a continuous improvement and enhancement process.
*   Adjust global set of KPIs for CAI analytics to local requirements, implement and test local KPIs and regularly review them for accuracy and reliability.
*   Adhere to globally defined technical standards and best practices.
*   Execute local access management and support infrastructure upgrades & testing.
*   Ensure and regularly update the local Solution Design Document (SDD) for compliance and relevance.
*   Execute project management tasks for CAI solution roll-outs, including defining project timeline, milestones, deliverables, and responsibilities.
*   Collaborate with CAI Regional Lead in hosting approval meetings and conducting the Go-Live process.
*   Open incidents with all priority levels as needed using provided templates and documentation, supporting resolution when required.

  
**What you bring**

*   Basic understanding of Conversational AI frameworks and technologies like STT, TTS, SSML and NLP.
*   Demonstrated successful track record in working with a Conversational AI software, such as Cognigy.
*   Good understanding of business environment and business processes.
*   High problem solution competencies, including analytical mindset and skills to analyze CAI solutions und underlying business processes to derive potential improvements .
*   Knowledge in programing languages like JavaScript or Python beneficial.
*   Experience in working in multicultural and diverse teams.
*   Proven experience and knowledge of IT organizations and teams.
*   Good coordination & communication skills
*   Proficient in English, with strong verbal and written communication skills.
*   Experience in agile methodologies and usage of state-of-the-art project management tools.
