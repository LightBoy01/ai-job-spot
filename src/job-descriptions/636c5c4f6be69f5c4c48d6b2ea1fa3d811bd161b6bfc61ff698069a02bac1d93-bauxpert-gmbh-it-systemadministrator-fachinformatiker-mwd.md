### Description\nWe are the central hub for a strong group of North German building material dealers – hands-on, down-to-earth, reliable. 19 medium-sized companies operate across 36 locations with approximately 1,000 employees, serving our customers. We generate an annual turnover of approximately € 400 million. We provide a central IT infrastructure for processing all business processes to a significant portion of our shareholders and operate our own data center for this purpose, currently serving around 620 users.\n\nTo strengthen our IT team in Bad Bramstedt near Hamburg, we are looking for an engaged new colleague (m/f/d) who enjoys actively shaping our IT infrastructure and reliably keeping IT operations running.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   With your support, the smooth operation and further development of the central IT infrastructure based on Microsoft systems will be ensured.\n*   You install and maintain applications in the data center and technically provide them.\n*   You independently process support tickets.\n*   You perform error diagnostics and troubleshoot technical issues.\n*   You take on your own - and support joint - IT projects.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Successfully completed training in the IT sector (e.g., IT specialist for system integration, system electronics technician) or comparable qualification.\n*   Professional experience in application and/or system administration.\n*   In-depth knowledge of Microsoft Server operating systems (2016-2025) and server virtualization (Hyper-V).\n*   You also possess knowledge in at least one of these areas:\n    *   Microsoft Exchange SE\n    *   Veeam-Backup\n    *   Network configuration in WAN, LAN, Routing, and Firewall\n    *   Citrix products\n*   You enjoy working independently and goal-oriented.\n*   You have good team and communication skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an IT Administrator eager to make a tangible impact within a robust and growing group of companies. Beyond routine maintenance, the role offers significant scope for actively shaping the IT landscape, supported by a culture that values quick decisions, personal development, and a strong work-life balance through flexible hours and remote options. The chance to work with modern technologies in an owned data center, coupled with generous benefits, makes this a highly appealing long-term prospect.
