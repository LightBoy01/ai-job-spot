### Description\nThis pivotal role as Head of Construction (m/w/d) offers a unique opportunity to strategically shape the technical evolution of a high-quality residential real estate portfolio for a successful, boutique-style asset management firm. Based in Berlin, you will lead an experienced team of approximately eight technical professionals, overseeing all construction-related activities, including modernizations, maintenance strategies, and value-enhancing CapEx projects. The company specializes in tailored investment solutions, focusing on sustainable value preservation, high quality, and responsible portfolio management within the Berlin residential market and selected nationwide investments. This position provides significant autonomy to drive technical development and secure long-term value in a dynamic, growth-oriented, and personally-driven environment with short decision-making paths.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Strategic Oversight**: Drive all technical measures within the residential portfolio, encompassing modernizations, maintenance strategies, energy optimizations, and value-adding CapEx projects.\n*   **Team Leadership**: Lead, develop, and manage a team of approximately eight technical professionals, including Technical Asset Managers and Technical Project Managers.\n*   **Project Coordination**: Coordinate and ensure the quality of renovation and expansion projects, specifically apartment renovations, existing property modernizations, attic conversions, and structural renewal measures.\n*   **Budget Management**: Establish, plan, and control CapEx budgets, including prioritizing measures and monitoring economic success.\n*   **Compliance Assurance**: Ensure adherence to all technical, building law, and regulatory requirements across the entire portfolio.\n*   **External Stakeholder Management**: Manage external service providers, architects, engineers, and specialist planners, from tendering to acceptance.\n*   **Process Development**: Continuously develop technical standards, processes, and quality guidelines to secure the long-term value of the portfolio.\n*   **Cross-Functional Collaboration**: Collaborate closely with commercial and strategic asset management teams to align measures with portfolio objectives.\n*   **Reporting & Recommendations**: Provide regular reports to the management board and offer well-founded decision and investment recommendations.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   **Education**: Completed degree in Civil Engineering, Architecture, Building Services Engineering, or a comparable technical qualification.\n*   **Experience**: At least 8–12 years of professional experience in technical management of residential properties, ideally in a leading or deputy management position.\n*   **Expertise**: Profound expertise in existing property renovation, modernization, maintenance strategies, attic conversions, and CapEx control.\n*   **Leadership**: Proven experience in leading technical teams and managing complex existing property portfolios.\n*   **Skills**: Strong competence in budgeting, assessing technical risks, and managing external partners (architects, engineers, specialist firms).\n*   **Work Style**: Distinct decision-making ability, strategic thinking, and a solution-oriented, hands-on work style.\n*   **Communication**: High communication and negotiation skills, combined with a professional demeanor and confident stakeholder management.\n\n### Author's
