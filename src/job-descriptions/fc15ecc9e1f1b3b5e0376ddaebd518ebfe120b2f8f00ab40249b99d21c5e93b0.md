Career Opportunities: System Architect (773528)

Requisition ID **773528** - Posted  - Country/Area (1) - state/province (1)

Join our Team

**About this opportunity**

Within Cognitive Software Engineering, we offer the opportunity to collaborate with highly qualified teams across the globe and contribute to success stories for our customers.

As a SaaS System Architect, you will design and develop scalable and high-performing distributed cloud-based applications.  
Your deep understanding of software architecture will be essential in supporting multiple applications and customer scenarios, ensuring a seamless transition from development to production and maintenance.

You will work closely with cross-functional teams, including product owners, domain experts, and technical leaders, to ensure reliability, scalability, and architectural excellence across our solutions.

**What you will do**

*   Design and drive system architecture implementation based on product needs
*   Assess impacts, dependencies, and risks for all requirements
*   Provide clear feature specifications and time estimates to development teams
*   Ensure architectural compliance with Ericsson standards and practices
*   Collaborate with other system architects to harmonize methodologies
*   Mentor and guide technical leaders within the domain
*   Anticipate and adapt to emerging IT and cloud technology trends
*   Provide thought leadership within and beyond the unit

**You will bring**

*   A Bachelor’s degree (MSc is a plus) in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent experience (10+ years in software/cloud architecture)
*   Proven track record as a technical reference in system architecture
*   Expertise in designing and developing highly scalable cloud applications on hyperscale cloud providers (e.g., AWS, Azure, GCP)
*   Experience in building applications with high availability, scalability, resiliency, observability, and disaster recovery
*   Strong proficiency in Python, Java, or Go, and in workflow management tools (e.g., Prefect, Argo)
*   Solid understanding of IAM, security, and privacy integration for enterprise-grade applications
*   Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, microservices, and event-driven architectures
*   Strong understanding of API design and security best practices
*   Ability to lead by example in programming and documentation standards, and to communicate complex topics effectively to stakeholders
*   Collaborative, self-driven, and solution-oriented mindset
*   Hands-on experience with CI/CD/CT practices
*   Exposure to AI/ML algorithms and AI-centric products is a strong advantage

**Why join Ericsson?**

At Ericsson, you´ll have an outstanding opportunity. The chance to use your skills and imagination to push the boundaries of what´s possible. To build solutions never seen before to some of the world’s toughest problems. You´ll be challenged, but you won’t be alone. You´ll be joining a team of diverse innovators, all driven to go beyond the status quo to craft what comes next.
