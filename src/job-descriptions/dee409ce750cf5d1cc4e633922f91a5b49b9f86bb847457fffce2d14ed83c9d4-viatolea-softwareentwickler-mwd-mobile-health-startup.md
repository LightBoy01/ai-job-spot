### Description\
Join a growing startup in Nuremberg, Germany, that's making a real difference in people's lives. We've developed viatolea, an innovative app designed to help individuals with food intolerances manage their symptoms effectively. By combining modern technology with expert knowledge, our app uses a nutrition and symptom diary to identify individual intolerances and then guides users through a personalized dietary adjustment plan. As a registered medical product, viatolea offers a reliable and impactful solution.\
\
### Responsibilities\
You are not just a developer; you are an **architect, decision-maker, and doer**. You will assume technical responsibility for our platform and app, shaping its architecture, making crucial technology decisions, and building a product with genuine impact. This role is for someone eager to achieve more than just writing code.\
\
*   **App Development with Flutter**: Design and implement new features, and extend existing functionalities of our app.\
*   **Backend Development and Database**: Be responsible for building, further developing, and maintaining our backend and associated databases.\
*   **Data Analytics**: Support the ongoing development of our algorithms and empower the team in leveraging data-driven insights.\
\
### Qualifications\
*   Completed Master's degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics, or a related field.\
*   Fluent German language skills (at least C1) are required, as the app targets the German market.\
*   **Experience**: Several years of practical experience in Flutter and Full-Stack development, including database know-how.\
*   **Leadership Mindset**: You think like a Tech Lead, take ownership, and make decisions.\
*   **Hands-on**: You tackle problems, find solutions, and don't shy away from challenges.\
*   **Startup Mentality**: You want to create and shape, not just administer. Corporate structures are not your thing.\
*   **Bonus Points**: Experience in building scalable architectures, DevOps, CI/CD, Security.\
\
### Author's Take\
This is an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned Full-Stack/Flutter professional with a strong leadership inclination to join a purpose-driven startup.
