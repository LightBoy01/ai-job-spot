### Description
Do you have several years of agency experience and love to powerfully showcase brands across all channels – from classic ATL communication to BTL, Digital, Social, and Online? Do you feel at home in integrated 360° campaigns, keeping an eye on budgets and KPIs, as well as creative quality and timing? As the central point of contact for your clients, you will be responsible for the strategy, implementation, and management of the entire campaign process.

We are looking for a motivated and committed **Senior Account Manager (all genders)** for our Hamburg location, to start as soon as possible.

### Responsibilities
- Be the central point of contact for your clients, strategically and operationally guiding them through integrated 360° campaigns (ATL, BTL, Digital, Social, Online).
- Be responsible for the entire campaign process – from briefing, strategy, and creation to production, media coordination, roll-out, and optimization.
- Proactively drive key topics in brand strategic client consulting, thinking along the customer journey and keeping goals and KPIs clearly in sight.
- Take on budget responsibility: planning, controlling, forecasting, and monitoring economic performance fall within your area of responsibility.
- Be a sparring partner for your consulting team and support their further development.
- Orchestrate interdisciplinary teams (e.g., strategy, creation, media, tech) and ensure smooth collaboration across all disciplines.
- Manage interfaces with external service providers, partner agencies, and production companies, ensuring quality, timing, and budget compliance.

### Qualifications
- Several years of experience in a consulting position, ideally agency-side, with responsibility for integrated 360° communication projects.
- Strong expertise in the development, implementation, and production processes of communication concepts across all channels – ATL, BTL, Digital/Online, Social – and a good sense for strong creation and brand management.
- Proficient in budget and project controlling (budget planning, forecast, profitability analysis) and able to interpret key figures and derive measures from them.
- Knowledge of complex campaign processes and a structured, solution-oriented, and proactive way of working.
- A true team player, ideally with experience in leading and developing a team.
- Confident client communication, presentation security, and negotiation skills characterize you.
- Motivate the internal team with your positive, clear manner and good briefings.
- Language proficiency: You are fluent in spoken and written German and communicate confidently in English.
- A quick grasp, high affinity for numbers, and enjoyment of data-driven optimization complete your profile.
