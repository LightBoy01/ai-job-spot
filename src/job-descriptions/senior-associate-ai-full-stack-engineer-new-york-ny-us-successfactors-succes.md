<p> </p>
<p> </p><p>We are seeking an experienced AI Full Stack Engineer to design, build, and deploy next-generation AI-powered applications. This role operates at the intersection of machine learning, agentic AI, and full-stack development, delivering scalable solutions that embed intelligence directly into business workflows.</p>
<p>The AI Full Stack Engineer will partner with product managers, data scientists, architects, and DevOps teams to translate ambiguous business problems into production-ready systems. You will work across the full stack — from AI/ML model development and deployment to frontend user experiences and backend services — ensuring that solutions are performant, secure, and aligned with enterprise standards.</p>
<p>This position is ideal for engineers with strong software craftsmanship, applied AI/ML experience, and a passion for agentic AI systems and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures.</p>

What You'll Do:
<ul>
<li>Design and implement end-to-end AI-powered applications, from data pipelines and ML models to APIs and user interfaces.</li>
<li>Collaborate with product managers and stakeholders to translate business problems into technical specifications.</li>
<li>Build and optimize RAG systems leveraging embeddings, vector databases, and document pipelines.</li>
<li>Develop AI agents with autonomous reasoning, multi-step task execution, and external tool/API integration.</li>
<li>Implement Agentic AI protocols including Model Context Protocol (MCP) and multi-agent orchestration frameworks.</li>
<li>Build scalable frontend applications using React, Angular, or Vue, and backend services with Python, Node.js, Java, or Go.</li>
<li>Design and maintain SQL and NoSQL databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, Redis) for structured and unstructured data.</li>
<li>Deploy, monitor, and scale applications on cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure) with Docker and Kubernetes.</li>
<li>Implement CI/CD pipelines, version control, and automated testing for reliable delivery.</li>
<li>Ensure system reliability through monitoring, observability, drift detection, and alerting for ML models in production.</li>
<li>Collaborate with data scientists, DevOps, and cross-functional teams to accelerate AI feature delivery.</li>
<li>Mentor junior developers and contribute to a culture of engineering excellence.</li>
</ul>

What You'll Bring:
<ul>
<li>Master’s degree in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Data Science, Software Engineering, or related technical field.</li>
<li>4+ years of professional software development experience, with at least 2+ years focused on AI/ML application development and deployment.</li>
<li>Proficiency in modern frontend frameworks (React, Angular, Vue), backend development (Python, Node.js, Java, Go), and RESTful APIs.</li>
<li>Strong experience with SQL and NoSQL databases.</li>
<li>Hands-on experience deploying applications to AWS, GCP, or Azure.</li>
<li>Proven expertise with Git, CI/CD pipelines, Docker, and Kubernetes.</li>
<li>Solid understanding of ML pipeline automation, model versioning, and production deployment.</li>
<li>Strong collaboration and communication skills to work with technical and business partners.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Preferred / Desired Skills</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Experience implementing RAG systems and working with vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, ChromaDB).</li>
<li>Proficiency in agent development frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex, Strands) and multi-agent orchestration.</li>
<li>Familiarity with real-time AI systems, streaming pipelines, and low-latency inference using Kafka or Redis Streams.</li>
<li>Knowledge of monitoring ML systems for drift, performance degradation, and automated alerting.</li>
<li>Exposure to insurance or financial services technology solutions.</li>
<li>Demonstrated ability to lead technical projects and mentor junior engineers.</li>
<li>Strong portfolio of AI-enabled full-stack applications in production.</li>
</ul>
