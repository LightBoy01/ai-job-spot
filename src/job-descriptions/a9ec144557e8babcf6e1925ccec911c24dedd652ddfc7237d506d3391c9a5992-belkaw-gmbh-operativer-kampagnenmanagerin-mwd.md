### Description\nAs an **Operational Campaign Manager (m/f/d)** for our Market & Communications division, you will be responsible for the successful implementation of sales measures, promotions, and campaigns across our commodity sectors (electricity and gas) and for our energy services, such as PV and heat pumps.\n\nYour focus will be on operational execution – from address acquisition and managing external service providers to performance monitoring. You will work closely with strategic marketing, product management, and customer service to ensure campaigns are efficiently implemented and continuously improved. At BELKAW, you will actively shape the energy supply in our region and contribute your ideas directly.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Address and Target Group Management:** Selection, procurement, and preparation of relevant addresses, as well as segmentation and prioritization of target groups. Establishment of structured processes for customer acquisition, re-acquisition, and cross-selling.\n- **Campaign Implementation:** Planning, organization, and operational execution of marketing and sales activities. Management of external service providers such as printing companies, address providers, or postal services. Responsibility for the customer referral program, including data reconciliation, premium processing, and communication.\n- **Sales Partner Network:** Development, maintenance, and support of sales partners (e.g., associations, retail, brokers, craft businesses). Provision of information and materials, as well as coordination of cooperation and commission processing.\n- **Internal Communication & Service Support:** Preparation of information for internal teams, conducting training, and ensuring clear communication on campaign content.\n- **Controlling & Reporting:** Analysis of relevant KPIs, creation of reports, and derivation of optimization measures for the continuous improvement of campaigns.\n- **Collaboration & Interfaces:** Close coordination with marketing, product management, and customer service, as well as participation in the further development of our marketing processes.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed vocational training as a Marketing Communications Clerk, E-Commerce Clerk, or Office Management Clerk with a focus on marketing/sales.\n- Alternatively, a completed degree in Marketing, Business Administration, Communication, or a comparable qualification with additional certification.\n- Experience in campaign or sales management, ideally in the B2C energy or telecommunications sector.\n- Very good organizational skills and a good understanding of processes.\n- Analytical thinking and experience in KPI analysis and reporting.\n- Strong communication skills and confident demeanor in dealing with internal and external partners.\n- Independent, structured, and solution-oriented way of working.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a comprehensive Mid-Senior level role for an \"Operational Campaign Manager\" at BELKAW, an energy provider in Germany. The position offers a significant opportunity to actively shape the regional energy supply, focusing on both traditional commodities and innovative services like solar PV and heat pumps. The company fosters a highly supportive and collaborative culture, emphasizing an \"open learning culture\" and a strong \"we-feeling\". With attractive benefits, flexible working models (up to 50% home office), and short communication paths, it's an ideal environment for a proactive and analytical individual contributor seeking impact and growth within a future-proof industry.
