Join us in the challenge of developing optimal usability for our employees, clients, and ticket buyers, and actively contribute your optimization suggestions.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Utilize and maintain internal project management tools.\n*   Support your team members with testing and implementing various applications.\n*   Participate in the creation and optimization of new processes and structures.\n*   Collaborate on the implementation of new projects and use your acquired programming skills for the independent creation of smaller applications.\n*   Work independently with other apprentices on your own apprentice project, supervised by your trainer.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   You have a secondary school leaving certificate (or higher) with good math grades, or perhaps you're a university dropout curious about practical experience.\n*   You have a strong interest in application-oriented IT and can demonstrate initial practical experience gained through internships, studies, or personal projects beyond school.\n*   You are confident in working with a Mac and common software applications.\n*   You bring quick comprehension, analytical thinking, and strong motivation.\n*   You are a team player who also wants to work independently and responsibly under guidance.\n*   You speak good German and English.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional entry-level IT specialist apprenticeship designed for proactive individuals eager to dive into real-world projects from day one.
