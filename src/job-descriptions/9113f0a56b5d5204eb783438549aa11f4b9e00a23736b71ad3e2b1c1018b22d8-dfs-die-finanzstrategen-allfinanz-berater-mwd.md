### Description\nDFS – Die Finanzstrategen is an internationally active group specializing in comprehensive financial strategies for high-net-worth private clients, doctors, entrepreneurs, and institutions in the DACH region. As an All-Finance Consultant, you will play a pivotal role in developing strategic concepts, complementing existing wealth management with professional risk analysis and provident planning. The firm prides itself on a 360° approach, integrating capital investment, financing, and provident solutions. Supported by an in-house marketing company in Dubai, new leads are continuously generated through a Setter – Closer system, allowing you to focus on the long-term strategic care of these valuable clients and an existing portfolio.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Analyze and optimize insurance portfolios for existing Private Banking clients.\n- Develop tailored solutions for occupational disability, corporate pensions, and asset protection.\n- Provide compliance-driven advice in accordance with FIDLEG, VersVermV, and MaRisk.\n- Collaborate with our wealth management team on holistic client concepts.\n- Manage an existing portfolio of approximately 150 high-net-worth private clients.\n\n### Qualifications\n**Required:**\n- Successfully completed training as a bank or insurance clerk, financial specialist (IHK), or a comparable qualification.\n- At least 5 years of proven experience advising high-net-worth private clients (consulting sum of €250,000+).\n- Comprehensive knowledge across investment, insurance, and provident planning sectors.\n- Demonstrable success in all-finance consulting.\n- Native-level proficiency in German, both written and spoken.\n\n**Preferred:**\n- Knowledge in corporate pensions (bAV).\n- Experience with succession planning.\n- Understanding of holding companies and foundations.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned All-Finance Consultant looking to make a significant impact within an international and growth-oriented financial group. The role offers a unique blend of strategic client advising, substantial earning potential through a competitive variable compensation model that builds passive income, and a clear path for professional development, including company-funded certifications (CFP, CIIA) and potential international assignments. With an existing portfolio of high-net-worth clients, a robust lead generation system, and a supportive culture featuring hybrid work models and flat hierarchies, DFS provides an environment where top-tier professionals can truly thrive and achieve long-term career success.
