**Company Description:**

Have you ever wondered why your favorite apps, social media content, and video games load in the blink of an eye? It's likely because of Gcore behind the scenes!  
  
Join a team that collaborates with industry giants like Intel, Dell, NVIDIA, Graphcore, and Equinix to accelerate AI training, provide cutting-edge cloud services, and optimize content delivery.  
If you are passionate about transforming the internet and contributing to cutting-edge innovations, come join us at Gcore!

**We are over 550 professionals and currently looking for a** **Marketing & Comms Specialist to join our Brand Marketing Team** and strengthen our brand presence both globally and in the DACH region.  
With AI now at the heart of our marketing strategy, this role offers the opportunity to pioneer AI-powered brand initiatives, experiment with the latest tools, and set new standards for data-driven storytelling and customer engagement.  
You will not only implement proven brand strategies but also help shape how AI transforms the way we create, communicate, and connect with audiences worldwide.

**Job Description:**

**Your Responsibilities:**

*   Implement the global brand strategy and develop and execute regional marketing initiatives in the DACH region
*   Collaborate with international marketing teams and regional enterprise sales teams
*   Create and adapt image content for various channels, leveraging AI tools for faster and higher-quality production where applicable
*   Contribute to messaging, product positioning, and the development of use cases for different verticals
*   Plan and execute communications and marketing campaigns
*   Support PR and analyst relations activities, coordinating with agencies and media partners, and exploring AI-enabled media monitoring and sentiment analysis
*   Support events and sponsorship activities with targeted communications measures
*   Assist with executive branding and thought leadership initiatives
*   Creation of pitch decks and sales presentations
*   Manage external partners (agencies, freelancers, consultants)
*   Monitor and report on relevant KPIs.

**Qualifications:**

**We Expect You to Have:**

*   A successfully completed degree, preferably in Business Administration or Communications
*   Several years of experience in B2B marketing or communications, ideally in the tech, cloud, or AI industry
*   Native-level German skills and excellent English, both written and spoken
*   Experience in creating image content (video, photo, graphics), ideally with knowledge of AI-based creative tools
*   International project experience and understanding of market requirements in the DACH region
*   Experience in creating professional presentations
*   Strong organizational skills, creativity, and initiative
*   Experience working with agencies and external partners
*   Structured, proactive approach and a team-oriented mindset.
