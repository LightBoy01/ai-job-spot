Description

**Vice President of Sales**

Transformational leadership opportunity with a third-generation, family-owned brand.  
  
 

**About Us**  
We are a third-generation, family-owned company known for high-quality outerwear, timeless style, and strong community engagement. Since 1983, we’ve built a reputation for craftsmanship and customer relationships that last. Today, we are modernizing our go-to-market strategies, scaling growth opportunities, and investing in leadership to position the company for long-term success. Our culture is built on four core values — Caring, Integrity, Pride, and Growth Mindset — which guide how we work with customers, partners, and each other.  
  
 

**The Role**  
We’re seeking a **transformational VP of Sales** — a leader who can bring a fresh perspective, strengthen an already strong team, and drive organizational growth through internal transformation.

  

· Reports directly to the President and sits on the senior leadership team.

· Oversees two Sales Directors managing ~30 reps across Promotional Products, Retail, College, Sporting Goods, and Uniform channels.

· Leads efforts to modernize sales processes, strengthen accountability, develop inside sales, and drive cross-functional alignment with Customer Service and other departments.  
  
 

**Location:** Sharon, MA (local preferred; relocation assistance available if needed)  
**Travel:** ~30-40% (varies based on trade shows, customer needs, and field support)  
  
 

**What You’ll Do**

*   **Drive Growth:** Lead the next stage of Charles      River’s growth by bringing new ideas, modern practices, and a fresh      perspective to the sales organization.
*   **Set Go-to-Market Direction:** Design and execute      channel-specific strategies that identify long-term growth opportunities      and priorities.
*   **Elevate Sales Processes:** Redesign and implement modern,      customer-centric sales processes — including CRM adoption, pipeline      management, accountability systems, and consultative selling.
*   **Champion Customer Experience:** Transform the customer journey by      working cross-functionally to ensure seamless integration between sales,      marketing, customer service, and operations.
*   **Develop & Support the Team:** Coach, mentor, and empower the sales      team; strengthen accountability while building a culture of pride and      performance.
*   **Harness Technology & Data:** Champion the use of CRM,      analytics, and emerging AI tools to improve forecasting, increase sales      productivity, and inform smarter decision-making.
*   **Advance Strategic Accounts:** Lead strategy and implementation      for key accounts, strengthening Charles River’s external presence,      deepening partnerships, and driving sustainable growth.
*   **Expand Retail & Emerging      Channels:** Grow opportunities in resort,      e-commerce, and other evolving markets.
*   **Optimize Sales Structure &      Coverage:** Assess and refine territories,      account assignments, and channel strategies to ensure balanced coverage,      efficiency, and growth potential.
*   **Strengthen Customer      Relationships:** Build and maintain executive-level relationships with customers and      industry partners, representing Charles River as a trusted advisor.
*   **Unify Across Functions:** Partner with Marketing, Product,      IT, and Customer Service to align growth strategies and execution.
*   **Deliver Profitable Growth:** Accelerate top-line revenue while      safeguarding margins to ensure sustainable, long-term profitability.
*   **Represent the Brand:** Serve as a visible ambassador at trade shows, industry events, and key account meetings. 

Requirements

**Why You’ll Want to Work Here**

*   **The Company:** A third-generation,      family-owned business with a proud history and story. Under new      leadership, we are modernizing while staying true to our roots.
*   **The Reputation:** Customers consistently      praise the quality, design, and value of our apparel — trusted for over 40      years.
*   **The Opportunity:** This is a newly structured,      high-impact role with broad scope and influence. You’ll help shape the      company’s future direction and growth.
*   **The People:** Passionate, motivated      colleagues who care deeply about the company, the customers, and each      other. You’ll build relationships that last a lifetime.  
      
     

**Who You Are**

  

· **Empowering Leader** ? Proven ability to modernize and unify a traditional sales org; leads through trust and influence.

**·** **Change Agent ? Experienced in driving transformation and bringing fresh perspective to shift cycles and accelerate growth.**

**·** **Growth Mindset** ? Curious, adaptable, and energized by transformation; sees challenges as opportunities.

· **Tech & Data Fluent ?** Skilled in CRM, analytics, and emerging AI tools to improve forecasting, sales productivity, and customer experience.

· **People Developer** ? Strong coach and mentor with a track record of building teams and elevating talent.

· **Collaborative & Low Ego** ? Works seamlessly across departments; puts customers and company first.

· **Hands-On Operator** ? Strategic yet engaged in pipeline reviews, customer insights, and sales activity where needed.

· **Metrics-Driven** ? Excels at setting KPIs, dashboards, and clear accountability systems.

· **Industry Knowledge** ? Experience in the promotional products industry preferred; adjacent industries (apparel, consumer goods, wholesale/distribution) also valued if paired with channel/distributor expertise.
