**About Role**
--------------

We are seeking an experienced Salesforce Quality Analyst who will serve as both a hands-on QA practitioner and a founding QA team member. This individual will participate in building the QA organization while actively delivering testing for multiple Salesforce implementation, Agentforce, and AI transformation projects. As a founding QA team member, you'll help establish quality assurance practices from the ground up while ensuring exceptional quality delivery across our client engagements.

You'll contribute to engagements that include:

*   AI-related projects: Testing Agentforce implementations, Data Cloud integrations, and AI-powered Salesforce solutions.
    
*   Salesforce projects: Quality assurance across Sales, Service, Experience, Platform, Industry Clouds, and products like Marketing Cloud, Tableau, MuleSoft, and Slack.
    
*   Enterprise integrations: Testing complex integrations between Salesforce and other enterprise systems.
    

**What you'll do**
------------------

*   Establish and build the QA function from scratch, defining processes, standards, and best practices.
    
*   Execute end-to-end testing for 3-4 concurrent Salesforce projects.
    
*   Create comprehensive test plans, test cases, and execution strategies from business requirements.
    
*   Perform functional, integration, regression, and UAT testing across multiple Salesforce clouds.
    
*   Write basic automation scripts for repetitive manual testing tasks and regression suites.
    
*   Validate data migrations, integrations, and system configurations.
    
*   Partner with developers, solution architects, and business analysts to understand requirements.
    
*   Facilitate UAT sessions with business users and gather feedback.
    
*   Leverage AI tools and LLMs to enhance testing efficiency and coverage.
    
*   Build and mentor a team of QA engineers as the function scales.
    
*   Define QA metrics, KPIs, and quality gates for project delivery.
    

**What you'll bring**
---------------------

*   8-10 years of quality assurance experience with at least 5 years in Salesforce testing
    
*   Proven experience building QA organizations or functions from scratch in previous roles
    
*   Hands-on expertise in manual testing with basic automation scripting capabilities (JavaScript, Python, or similar)
    
*   Admin-level understanding of Salesforce platform and at least 2-3 clouds (Sales, Service, Experience)
    
*   Strong understanding of Salesforce integrations, enterprise system testing, and deployment processes
    
*   Experience with both small-scale implementations and large enterprise projects
    
*   Proficiency with test management tools (JIRA, TestRail, Zephyr) and Salesforce-specific testing tools (Provar, Copado Testing)
    
*   Experience with modern automation frameworks (Playwright, Selenium, Cypress) and API testing (REST, SOAP)
    
*   Understanding of data migration testing, ETL validation, and sandbox management
    
*   Comfort with AI tools and ability to integrate them into QA workflows
    
*   Exceptional communication skills and strong problem-solving abilities with attention to detail
    
*   Ability to context-switch effectively across multiple projects
