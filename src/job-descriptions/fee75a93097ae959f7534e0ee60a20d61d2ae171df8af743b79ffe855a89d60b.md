**About the role**
------------------

Join our Cybersecurity Operations team as a Cyber Security Developer, where you'll lead automation, integration, and AI-driven enhancements across our security landscape. This role blends hands-on development with strategic innovation to strengthen our operational resilience.

**Key responsibilities**
------------------------

*   Design and implement cybersecurity automation workflows (e.g., user remediation, containment, proactive response).
*   Develop integrations between security tools to enable seamless data exchange and automated decision-making.
*   Apply modern software engineering practices including version control, documentation, and testing.
*   Collaborate with analysts and engineers to translate operational needs into scalable solutions.
*   Act as a technical bridge between detection engineering and incident response teams.
*   Leverage AI for controlled, intelligent decision-making in security operations.
*   Champion DevOps principles within the cybersecurity team using platforms like Azure DevOps.
*   Explore and implement hyperautomation platforms with deep AI integrations.

**Job requirements**
--------------------

*   Minimum 2 years of hands-on experience in cybersecurity development within complex IT environments.
*   Proven track record in building automation workflows, ideally in a security context.
*   Strong grasp of DevOps and CI/CD pipeline concepts.
*   Experience with tools such as Azure DevOps, Git, or similar.
*   Familiarity with AI technologies and their integration into technical workflows.
*   Exposure to hyperautomation platforms and cybersecurity-specific AI agents.
*   Excellent communication and problem-solving skills; able to work independently and strategically.
*   Ability to operate autonomously and advise on development strategy and tooling.
