### Description\nNationwide rollout of a central document management and collaboration platform for a federal authority with tens of thousands of users. The solution will become the mandatory standard for secure document management, workflow automation, and knowledge management throughout the entire enterprise.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Responsible planning, coordination, and execution of sub-projects within the document management program.\n- Mapping and maintenance of the sub-project in MS Project, SharePoint, and Atlassian Jira.\n- Sub-project controlling regarding deadlines, milestones, requirements, budget, and deliverables.\n- Management of dependencies, changes, configurations, and risks.\n- Stakeholder management and resolution of goal and interest conflicts.\n- Committee work, communication, and reporting.\n- Administrative tasks (appointment coordination, quality assurance, on-/offboarding, list maintenance).\n- Creation of specification documents and support of project management on special topics.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed (Business) Informatics degree, IT Bachelor, or recognized IT vocational training (alternatively, 6 years of relevant IT professional experience).\n- At least 5 years of project management experience in medium to large, complex IT projects.\n- Very good German (C1 CEFR).\n- Profound practical experience with MS Project, Jira, SharePoint, and the entire Microsoft Office suite.\n- Demonstrable experience in classic project controlling (deadlines, milestones, budget, risks).\n- Confident stakeholder and conflict management in large organizations.\n- High reliability, structured way of working, and very good coordination skills.\n- Excellent communication and presentation skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a highly impactful Senior Project Lead role, vital for the digitalization of a federal authority. It requires robust project management skills, particularly in stakeholder and conflict resolution, within a complex, large-scale environment. The opportunity to shape a mandatory standard for tens of thousands of users makes it uniquely challenging and rewarding for a structured and reliable professional.
