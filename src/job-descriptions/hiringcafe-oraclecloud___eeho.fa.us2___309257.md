What We Offer

*   Work on impactful AI Project & Initiatives involving MCP, GenAI, and AI Agents.
*   Mentorship from senior AI professionals working across LLMs, OCI, and MLOps pipelines.
*   A culture of innovation, learning, and practical experimentation.
*   Clear path to grow into AI Specialist or Technical Lead roles based on delivery excellence.

### Responsibilities

Key Responsibilities

*   Build and deliver AI Agents using GenAI frameworks, orchestrated through Model Context Protocol (MCP) to enable multi-step reasoning and tool invocation.
*   Engineer prompt-based workflows, integrating LLMs with enterprise applications through REST APIs and OCI components.
*   Develop and deploy AI models on OCI Functions, containerize them using OKE, and scale them through automated pipelines.
*   Contribute to GenAI use case delivery across natural language querying, document intelligence, and process automation.
*   Support MLOps practices for model versioning, continuous deployment, and monitoring in production.
*   Write and optimize SQL queries and manage integration with structured data sources.
*   Participate in system design, performance tuning, and end-user experience optimization for AI features.

Required Qualifications

*   Master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, or a related field.
*   1–4 years of experience in Python development with exposure to ML/AI pipelines.
*   Hands-on familiarity with Model Context Protocol (MCP) and AI Agent orchestration frameworks.
*   Experience building solutions on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) including Functions, OKE, and storage services.
*   Strong understanding of prompt engineering, LLM APIs (OpenAI, Cohere, etc.), and embeddings.
*   Proficiency with SQL, REST APIs, and containerized development (Docker/K8s).
*   A passion for continuous learning, prototyping, and implementing state-of-the-art GenAI capabilities.

Ideal Candidate Traits

*   Builder’s mindset with strong curiosity for emerging AI technologies and techniques.
*   Ability to research, evaluate, and implement latest AI papers, tools, or APIs quickly.
*   Comfortable switching contexts between coding, system debugging, and conceptual solutioning.
*   Collaborative and proactive in team environments, with a strong sense of ownership.

Interview Process

*   Round 1: Deep coding test – Python, logic, and ML patterns
*   Round 2: Technical interview – MCP, AI Agent flow, prompt design, OCI skills
*   Round 3: Solution walkthrough – Design and delivery thinking
*   Round 4 (Optional): Culture and growth alignment
