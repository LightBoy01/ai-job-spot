### Description
For over 28 years, requisimus has been passionate about all IT disciplines, from software development and embedded systems to IT consulting. For our client in the automotive industry, we are looking for a responsible qualified electrician (m/w/x) for high-voltage systems, starting in January.

### Responsibilities
- Ensuring the execution of risk assessments before commencing work on vehicle high-voltage drive components and systems.
- Documentation and creation of handover protocols.
- Being the point of contact for high-voltage topics.
- Identifying training needs and developing instruction plans.
- Coordinating relevant topics with high-voltage safety.
- Organizing training sessions.
- Adapting work instructions.
- Supporting audits.
- Conducting area controls (especially regarding protective measures).
- Ensuring regular maintenance, inspection, and, if necessary, disposal of protective equipment.

### Qualifications
- Qualified electrician degree.
- At least 3 years of experience in vehicle high-voltage systems.
- Experience with DGUV Regulation 3 or DIN VDE 0105-100.
- Very good German language skills, both written and spoken.
