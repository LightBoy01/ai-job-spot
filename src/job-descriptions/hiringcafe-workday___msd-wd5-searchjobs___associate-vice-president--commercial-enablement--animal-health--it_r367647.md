**Job Description**

The Associate Vice President, Commercial Enablement, Animal Health IT, will be accountable for all aspects of our company's Animal Health’s Sales and Marketing technology initiatives, including global commercial stakeholder management, demand management and prioritization, business case development, solutions architecture and design, platform roadmap development, solutions delivery and, ultimately, system adoption, scaling and business outcome realization across all markets worldwide.

The Global Commercial IT Leader will set and achieve aggressive business value realization targets across a wide range of technology-enabled commercial initiatives including Global Web Site Redesign and Management, Customer Care, Customer Onboarding, eLearning, Pet Owner Engagement, Field Effectiveness, Remote Customer Engagement, Multi-channel Digital Marketing, Lead Generation and Management, Pricing and Rebates Management, and Commercial Data and Analytics Services among others.  The successful candidate will be responsible for developing and driving the Global CRM Harmonization initiative and for developing commercial technology integration strategies and solutions for the Animal Health Intelligence business and other businesses to be acquired in the future.  

The Animal Health Global Commercial IT Leader will work in a highly collaborative capacity with global IT leaders across the organization: from Data and Application Architecture, Networking, Security, Risk Management, Cloud Computing, SAP, eCommerce and other disciplines.  The successful candidate will operate in a highly consultative capacity with General Managers, Marketing and Sales leaders around the globe to qualify and prioritize demand, to develop market-tailored global solutions, to provide post-installation process and technology hyper-care and to drive technology adoption and business value realization.

The Animal Health Global Commercial IT Leader will staff, develop and inspire a global team person team of top talent with deep expertise in Sales and Marketing technologies, business processes, challenges and solutions.  The selected candidate will be a critical contributor to Animal Health’s ambitions and, as such, will participate on the Chief Commercial Officer’s extended leadership team.  The position reports to the Chief Information Officer (CIO), Animal Health.

**Primary Responsibilities:** 

*   Representation of Global IT organizational priorities and alignment of these priorities with Animal Health (AH) business strategy as part of AH Enterprise Priority Executive Committee.
    
*   Co-creation of Global Commercial Digital strategy as part of the Chief Commercial Officer’s leadership team.
    
*   Development and execution of commercial IT strategies to digitally enable and accelerate Sales and Marketing objectives – revenue, market share and profitability growth, customer engagement and demand generation expansion, brand awareness and reputation acceleration.
    
*   Management and prioritization of global demand for commercial platforms and services
    
*   Development of business cases and securing of capital and operating funds to support execution
    
*   Creation, communication and execution of clear commercial IT solutions roadmaps
    
*   Development and delivery of efficient, reusable component application and data architectures in conjunction with the organizational Chief Technology Office (CTO)
    
*   Collaboration with global IT infrastructure teams ensuring proper compliance, security and architectural alignment with company standards
    
*   Establishment and execution of market launch timelines aligned with commercial business partner objectives and market readiness evaluations
    
*   Development, delivery and application of market enablement tools and capabilities assuring rapid and effective market adoption of delivered technologies
    
*   Design and delivery of post-installation hyper-care services to assure proper sustainment of delivered solutions
    
*   Creation, management and reporting of key transactional and financial success metrics for delivered platforms and services
    
*   Identification, prototyping and evaluation of new innovations in the commercial space in conjunction with key business partners
    
*   Effective recruitment, development and inspiration of top talent
    
*   Oversite and championing of a diverse and inclusive work environment where all individuals share equally in opportunities to contribute, grow, advance and thrive.
