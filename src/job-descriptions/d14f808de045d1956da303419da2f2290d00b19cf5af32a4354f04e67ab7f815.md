**About the role:** 

We are seeking a seasoned Video Specialist with a strong background in video production, motion design, team leadership, and project management. The ideal candidate brings a blend of solid understanding of the Saudi culture and market, combined with creative expertise

and technical skill, with advanced knowledge in AI tools for content creation. This role requires a strategic thinker who is also hands-on, with proven experience in managing end-to-end video production workflows. 

The ideal candidate will be able to communicate and align with teams within the same office and across regional offices.

**Responsibilities:** 

*    Execute end-to-end video production, from concept development to final delivery, ensuring alignment with brand guidelines and objectives.
*   Collaborate closely with the Video Team Manager, designers, and content teams to develop creative treatments and storyboards.
*   Capture, edit, and deliver engaging video content created manually and with AI tools for: 
*   **Social Media:** Snapchat, X, Meta, YouTube, and TikTok
*   **Retail Screens & Office Displays:** AppSpace content for internal/external communications  
*   **Outdoor Campaigns:** Digital Out-of-Home (DOOH) creative 
*   **Brand & Corporate Videos:** Storytelling-driven content aligned with brand identity 
*   **Travel & Partnership Videos:** Destination features, campaigns, and collaborations
*   Produce high-quality event coverage, product explainers, and promotional shoots.
*   Leverage AI-powered tools to accelerate editing, enhance visual quality, and optimize production processes.

**Profile requirements:**

*   Bachelor’s degree in Film, Media Production, or a related field.
*   Good understanding of the Saudi culture and experience in the Saudi market
*   5+ years of experience in videography, video editing, and motion design.
*   Proficiency with industry-standard software (Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, etc.).
*   Experience as a team lead and project management
*   Worked on projects across departments
*   Experience producing content across diverse formats and platforms.
*   Working knowledge of AI-driven tools for video enhancement and automation.
*   Strong visual storytelling skills with attention to detail.
*   Ability to manage multiple projects in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment.
*   Advanced video editing (Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve) 
*   Very strong motion graphics capability (After Effects)
*   Solid knowledge of camera operation ( Sony mirrorless camera) 
*   Confident with lighting setups and audio recording for events
*   Skilled in video formats, codecs, and workflow optimization
*   AI & Automation Tools: VEO 2/3, Runway ML, Adobe Firefly, or any other relevant AI tools
*   Willing to travel for shoots if required.
