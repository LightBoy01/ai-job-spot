### Description\nThis role involves the meticulous preparation of various tax declarations and financial statements, management of tax-related documentation, and direct correspondence with tax authorities and advisors. A key focus will be on real estate-specific tax matters, contributing to the financial health and compliance of an internationally growing company.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Preparation of corporate income tax and sales tax annual returns.\n- Preparation of tax balance sheets and profit and loss statements.\n- Differentiation of maintenance expenses and production costs.\n- Maintenance of monitoring sheets according to § 15a UStG (German VAT Act).\n- Preparation of e-balance sheets.\n- Correspondence with tax authorities and tax advisors.\n- Preparation and submission of sales tax pre-declarations.\n- Preparation of annual financial statements according to HGB (German Commercial Code) for smaller companies.\n- Collaboration on special tax projects.\n- Point of contact for daily business questions.\n- Organizational tasks.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed training as a tax clerk (Steuerfachangestellte) or further education as a tax specialist (Steuerfachwirt).\n- Initial to several years of experience in managing real estate-related tax topics.\n- Good to very good knowledge of MS Office, especially Excel.\n- Careful, conscientious, and independent way of working.\n- Team player with a willingness for continuous further training.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis position offers a secure role within an internationally growing company, emphasizing a supportive and innovative culture with flat hierarchies and a \"Du\"-culture. Candidates can expect significant development opportunities through internal training (Sirius Academy), a buddy program for seamless integration, flexible working hours for excellent work-life balance, and regular team events, fostering both professional and personal growth.
