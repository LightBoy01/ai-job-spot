### Description\nJoin a rapidly growing company focused on renewable energies near Trier, Germany. As a **Head of Financial Accounting with a Passion (m/f/d)**, you will take responsibility for the entire financial accounting function, from technical leadership to process development. This role offers a diverse range of tasks with a focus on balance sheet accounting, a sustainable environment with flat hierarchies, and an employer who values long-term development, modern working conditions, and personal freedom.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Lead the financial accounting department according to HGB, contributing your expertise to professionalize structures, digitize processes, and develop your team.\n- Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements.\n- Act as the primary contact person for tax advisors, banks, and auditors.\n- Oversee consolidation, intercompany reconciliations, and tax compliance.\n- Continuously develop accounting processes and digital tools.\n- Collaborate closely with the commercial director and other related departments.\n- **Optional:** Participate in controlling activities (cost centers, project controlling).\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed commercial training or a degree in finance.\n- Successfully passed the **Balance Accountant (Bilanzbuchhalter)** examination.\n- Approximately **10 years of professional experience** in accounting, with solid expertise in HGB financial statements.\n- Team leadership is a passion of yours? This is the perfect opportunity!\n- Experience with **consolidation, intercompany processes, and ERP systems** (e.g., SAP).\n- Analytical mindset, precise working style, and a desire for continuous improvement.\n- **Nice to have:** Experience with IFRS, project controlling, group structures, or within the renewable energy sector.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a seasoned and passionate Head of Financial Accounting to make a significant impact within a rapidly expanding company dedicated to renewable energy. The role offers true leadership, allowing you to drive digitalization and process improvements while mentoring a team. What truly sets this position apart is the company's commitment to its employees: a generous salary, flexible hybrid work arrangements (including part-time options), extensive professional development, and a genuinely familial culture. With flat hierarchies, team events, a dog-friendly office, and even \"Bier um 4\" on Fridays, it’s clear this employer prioritizes both professional challenge and personal well-being. If you're looking to combine your financial expertise with a sustainable mission and a supportive environment, this role is a perfect match.
