### Description
This is a highly impactful role taking end-to-end responsibility for marketplace performance on platforms like Amazon and eBay. You will drive sustainable growth by optimizing existing setups, identifying new strategic platforms, and making data-driven decisions. The role involves continuous optimization of listing quality, content, and pricing, acting as a central interface for cross-functional teams, and orchestrating initiatives to identify and leverage structural growth opportunities.

### Responsibilities
- Take end-to-end ownership for marketplace performance on Amazon and eBay, ensuring a sustainable growth agenda.
- Drive the development of existing marketplace setups and systematically identify new strategic platforms.
- Utilize deep expertise in Amazon Seller Central, Vendor Central, and comparable systems to continuously optimize listing quality, content, and pricing.
- Make data-based decisions by monitoring all relevant KPIs (Conversion, CTR, BuyBox, Returns, Margin) and deriving concrete optimization impulses.
- Segment target groups, develop keyword and content strategies, and ensure performance-oriented implementation.
- Act as a central interface to merchandise management, pricing, logistics, marketing, and IT, with clear ownership for marketplace results.
- Identify structural growth levers (assortment, process, content, logistics) and orchestrate cross-departmental initiatives together with stakeholders.

### Qualifications
- Successfully completed studies (e.g., Business Administration, Business Informatics, eCommerce) or an equivalent qualification.
- Several years of robust experience in marketplace management, ideally in the Home & Living or DIY/hardware store environment.
- Strong analytical competence, KPI proficiency, and confident handling of performance, pricing, and listing data.
- Experience in managing larger product catalogs, complex variant logics, and marketplace-specific compliance requirements.
- High implementation speed, an ownership mindset, and the ability to drive topics structured, independently, and customer-centrically.
- Team player mentality, combined with a clear quality and results focus.
