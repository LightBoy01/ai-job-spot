<p>CoreWeave powers the creation and delivery of the intelligence that drives innovation.&nbsp;</p><p><strong>What You’ll Do:</strong><strong><br></strong><strong><br></strong>At CoreWeave, we’re scaling fast, and we believe AI, automation, and modern software should scale with us. As the Integrations and AI Manager, you sit at the intersection of business systems, intelligent agents, and internal product development. Your mission is to simplify and accelerate how work gets done across the company by building powerful internal tools, orchestrating enterprise integrations, and deploying AI agents that serve teams across GTM, Finance, Supply Chain, Engineering, Security, and Data Center Operations.</p>
<p>You’ll manage and deliver first-party internal products that enable high-impact business functions like supply chain, finance, and data center operations. You’ll also own and advocate and democratize integrations platform adoption driving business outcomes. You’ll also drive the transformational execution of agentic workforce.</p>
<p>You won’t just lead you’ll do. You’re a hands-on builder, just as comfortable writing code as you are shaping strategy. You thrive in fast-paced environments, are quick to roll up your sleeves, and you know how to drive execution without over-engineering.<br><br><strong>About the role:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Build and manage a portfolio of AI agents, internal tools, and automations across business domains, aligned to real workflows and measurable outcomes.</li>
<li>Own and evolve CoreWeave’s integration platform, ensuring seamless connectivity between systems like ERP, CRM, Billing, DCIM, PDM, Procurement, and our data warehouse.</li>
<li>Design, deliver, and support first-party internal products that power key business operations—using React/TypeScript on the front end and Node.js/Go on the backend.</li>
<li>Evaluate and integrate GenAI platforms and agentic frameworks such as OpenAI, Claude, Cohere, Gemini, Cursor, Crew, and others—selecting the right approach for the use case.</li>
<li>Collaborate closely with stakeholders across GTM, Finance, Supply Chain, Engineering, and Security to surface the highest-impact opportunities for simplification, automation, and AI-driven augmentation.</li>
<li>Ensure secure, scalable, and performant integrations and applications, aligned with modern engineering and security best practices.</li>
<li>Define and measure product and agent success with clear KPIs around performance, usability, reliability, and business value.</li>
<li>Lead by example in a hands-on way—writing code, reviewing pull requests, and participating in design and architecture discussions.</li>
<li>Operate with urgency and focus in a high-growth environment, knowing when to ship fast and when to go deep.</li>
</ul>
