You will lead initiatives that harness data, analytics, and AI to deliver actionable insights for enterprise customers, helping them make smarter revenue decisions. If you have a passion for revenue management, experience with B2B enterprise and/or AI-driven technologies, and a knack for translating market insights into impactful product strategies, we invite you to join us in shaping the future of intelligent revenue optimization.

**Responsibilities**  
*   Lead cross-functional teams in the end-to-end product development lifecycle, integrating AI-driven insights to enhance revenue management capabilities.
*   Define and communicate a compelling product vision, strategy, and roadmap aligned with business objectives and enterprise customer needs.
*   Collaborate with engineering, design, marketing, and sales teams to develop and deliver high-impact features that drive revenue intelligence adoption.
*   Conduct in-depth market research, competitor analysis, and customer feedback assessments to identify growth opportunities and product innovations.
*   Prioritize and manage the product backlog, leveraging data analytics to inform product decisions and optimize revenue-related features.
*   Develop detailed product requirements, user stories, and specifications for development teams across multiple geographies.
*   Advocate for the product internally and externally, presenting revenue intelligence capabilities to stakeholders, customers, and at industry events.
*   Champion agile development methodologies, ensuring continuous iteration and improvement in product releases.
*   Monitor product performance metrics, analyze user behavior, and implement enhancements based on key insights.
*   Stay at the forefront of industry trends, emerging AI technologies, and best practices in revenue intelligence and enterprise software.
  
  
**Minimum Qualifications**  
*   Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, Finance, Law, or a related field.
*   5+ years of experience in product management or related roles within the software industry.
*   **Experience with revenue intelligence, pricing optimization, or financial analytics products**, including advanced pricing models in hospitality, travel, or fintech.
*   **Fluent in applied AI and LLMs across the SDLC**, with expertise in advanced prompting, context engineering, agentic tool use, and designing AI-augmented workflows that drive automation, precision, and scale.
*   Strong understanding of software development processes and methodologies.
*   Proven ability to navigate enterprise sales cycles and drive adoption of revenue management solutions.
*   Exceptional communication skills with the ability to present complex revenue intelligence concepts clearly and persuasively.
*   Strong analytical skills with experience leveraging data to drive product decisions.
*   Expertise in Agile/Scrum methodologies and product management tools.
*   Experience working with cross-functional, globally distributed teams and enterprise customers.
  
  
**Preferred Qualifications**  
*   MBA or an advanced technical degree (Master’s in Computer Science or equivalent).
*   3+ years of experience as a software engineer or in a related technical role.
*   **Experience using ML models and algorithms to develop advanced revenue models.**
*   **Experience using ML or predictive models to develop advanced forecasting models and products.**
