Zenoti provides an all-in-one, cloud-based software solution for the beauty and wellness industry. Our solution allows users to seamlessly manage every aspect of the business in a comprehensive mobile solution: online appointment bookings, POS, CRM, employee management, inventory management, built-in marketing programs and more. Zenoti helps clients streamline their systems and reduce costs, while simultaneously improving customer retention and spending. Our platform is engineered for reliability and scale and harnesses the power of enterprise-level technology for businesses of all sizes

Zenoti powers more than 30,000 salons, spas, medspas and fitness studios in over 50 countries. This includes a vast portfolio of global brands, such as European Wax Center, Hand & Stone, Massage Heights, Rush Hair & Beauty, Sono Bello, Profile by Sanford, Hair Cuttery, CorePower Yoga and TONI&GUY.

To learn more about Zenoti visit: https://www.zenoti.com

We are looking for a passionate **Full Stack Developer** with expertise in **AI/ML, Azure, Python, Next.js/React**, and **cloud automation** to design and build scalable, cloud-native applications. You will work on both front-end and back-end systems, integrate AI/ML models, and contribute to automation-driven product development.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Develop full stack applications with **Python (backend)** and **Next.js/React (frontend)**.
*   Build, deploy, and optimize **AI/ML models** in production using **Azure ML** and related services.
*   Design and implement **REST/GraphQL APIs**, microservices, and cloud-native solutions.
*   Work on **infrastructure & cloud automation projects** (Azure DevOps, IaC with Terraform/Bicep/ARM templates).
*   Leverage **Azure cloud services** (App Services, Functions, AKS, Cosmos DB, Blob Storage).
*   Ensure performance, security, and scalability of applications.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams in an **Agile environment**.

**Required Skills**

*   Strong programming in **Python** (Django/FastAPI/Flask).
*   Experience with **AI/ML frameworks** (TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn).
*   Proficiency in **React.js/Next.js**.
*   Hands-on experience with **Azure Cloud & automation** (Azure DevOps, IaC tools, Kubernetes).
*   Strong knowledge of **SQL & NoSQL databases**.
*   Familiarity with **Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines**.

**Nice to Have**

*   Experience with **MLOps practices** and **Generative AI (LLMs, LangChain, etc.)**.
*   Knowledge of **serverless and event-driven architectures**.

**Education**

*   Bachelor’s/Master’s in **Computer Science, Engineering, or related fields**.
