<p>📌 What We’re Looking For: Automation Data Scientist<br></p>
<p>We require a Data Scientist to help us automate the process discovery cycle, to empower us to make data driven prioritization  decisions in regards to the automation projects and build a process performance monitoring system once the automation has been delivered to production.</p>
<p><br></p>
<p><em>What You Will Do : </em></p>
<ul>
<li>Designing, selecting, and engineering features, and building, evaluating, and optimizing predictive and generative models using state‑of‑the‑art machine‑learning techniques.<br></li>
<li>Developing, deploying, and maintaining machine‑learning models and pipelines in production environments, ensuring scalability, reliability, and low latency.<br></li>
<li>Monitoring model performance, detecting drift, and implementing automated retraining and governance strategies.<br></li>
<li>Researching and prototyping advanced statistical and machine‑learning methods to generate actionable insights and drive product innovation.<br><br><br></li>
<li>Collaborating with cross‑functional teams (product, engineering, design, business) to translate business problems into data‑science solutions with measurable impact.<br></li>
<li>Communicating complex technical results and insights to technical and non‑technical stakeholders.<br></li>
<li>Keeping up to date with the latest technology trends in data science, machine learning, and large language models, and actively pursuing continuous learning opportunities.</li>
</ul>
<p><br></p>
<p><em>What You Will Need :</em></p>
<ul>
<li>2+ years of practical experience as a Data Scientist.<br></li>
<li>Strong problem‑solving skills with an emphasis on product development and delivery.<br></li>
<li>Practical experience with NLP, large language models (LLMs), computer vision, multimodal modeling, clustering, and decision‑tree algorithms.<br></li>
<li>Hands‑on experience with data‑science and machine‑learning libraries and frameworks such as Python (pandas, NumPy, scikit‑learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch) and related tooling (MLflow, Airflow, Spark) for experimentation and productionization.<br></li>
<li>Excellent programming skills in Python, including writing clean, modular, well‑documented code, adhering to software‑engineering best practices (Git version control, unit testing, code reviews, and CI/CD pipelines).<br></li>
<li>Ability to understand various data structures and common methods in data transformation and analysis.<br></li>
<li>Data‑oriented personality with a passion for empirical, evidence‑based decision‑making.<br></li>
<li>Great written and verbal communication skills, capable of translating complex analyses into clear business recommendations.<br><br></li>
<li>A self‑starter mindset, able to work independently while proactively collaborating with cross‑functional teams.</li>
</ul>
