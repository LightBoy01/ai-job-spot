We’re expanding our existing team for one of our core products at **Shiftmove**, and we’re looking for **three talented Software Engineers** to join us!

As a Software Engineer, you’ll co-own the design and development of solutions to some of the most complex challenges faced by companies managing large fleets of vehicles.

We’re looking for engineers with a strong **specialization in either frontend or backend**, but we value curiosity and flexibility to work across the stack. Our goal is to build a rounded, capable team where everyone continuously expands their technical skills and impact.

If you’re thoughtful, collaborative, and eager to grow — we’d love to meet you!

What You Will Do
----------------

*   You will be part of a highly collaborative cross-functional product team alongside a dedicated Product Manager
*   You'll contribute actively to product discussions, gaining valuable insights and offering innovative solutions to address user needs
*   You deploy code to production, relying on our highly automated continuous integration and continuous delivery infrastructure
*   You will also play a key role in the development of key features such as Documents LLM based readout, Configurable User Permissions, Mileage readout from OEMs cloud, and more
*   You will actively participate in the engineering decision-making process by engaging in discussions, contributing to, and submitting Request for Comments (RFCs)
*   We are a team, you will be part of knowledge sharing, and contributing innovative ideas to enhance our product and customer experience

OUR TECH STACK
--------------

*   Backend: Java, Spring Boot
*   Front-end: Angular, Typescript, NGXS, RXJS and Storybook
*   CI/CD: AWS CodePipeline/CodeBuild
*   Cloud: Amazon Web Services
*   AI: TensorFlow, Python, SpringAI, Gemini

YOUR PROFILE
------------

*   Solid CS foundation and hands-on experience as Software Engineer.
*   Experience with any of the following frameworks Angular, SpringBoot or similar.
*   Experience with Java or Python, or any other OOP language
*   Fast learner, ready to pick up new tech stacks
*   You’re Ambitious and value knowledge sharing and mentorship

Why should you join us?

*   **Real impact:** work on products used by thousands of customers across Europe.

*   Mental health support: access to [Nilo.health](http://nilo.health/) platform if you could use a session with a psychologist.

*   **12 weeks of remote work from any country or continent you want!**
*   The choice of a range of Company Benefits, such as an Urban Sports Club membership, JobRad, or a BVG ticket grant
*   Hybrid setup: 2 days/week in our **Berlin** office, flexible remote options otherwise.
*   A dog-friendly office and plenty of flexibility to take care of what matters most
