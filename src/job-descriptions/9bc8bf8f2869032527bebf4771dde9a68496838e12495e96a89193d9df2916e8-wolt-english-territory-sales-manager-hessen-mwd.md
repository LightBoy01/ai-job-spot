Having launched in Germany in August 2020, Wolt is now significantly expanding its operations. This role is crucial for driving the acquisition of new restaurant partners across Germany, actively shaping Wolt's regional development. Working at Wolt is described as challenging and exciting, offering ample opportunities for learning, building, and shipping, ideal for self-starters with an entrepreneurial spirit.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop and actively shape Wolt's presence in your assigned region.\n- Be prepared for extensive daily travel (up to 300km) including occasional overnight stays.\n- Identify potential restaurant partnerships and successfully close sales deals to meet ambitious sales targets.\n- Independently organize and manage your sales pipeline, building strong relationships with key decision-makers at restaurants.\n- Conduct compelling in-person sales presentations to restaurant owners and managers, effectively showcasing Wolt's service.\n- Transform your assigned area into a thriving \"Wolt region\" by maximizing partner acquisition.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You bring 4+ years of proven experience in sales, or have demonstrated strong sales talent across the entire sales cycle.\n- You are characterized by exceptional friendliness and strong persuasive communication skills.\n- Possess high self-motivation, strong goal-orientation, and an outgoing, confident personality.\n- Your excellent communication and presentation skills enable you to be convincing and self-assured in various settings.\n- Hold a completed university degree or an equivalent qualification through a completed apprenticeship combined with relevant professional experience.\n- You are fluent in both German and English, verbally and in writing.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding Mid-Senior Field Sales opportunity for a driven individual ready to make a significant impact on Wolt's expansion in Germany. Joining a dynamic, international team, you'll be empowered with substantial autonomy to develop your region and acquire key restaurant partners. The role demands extensive travel and a self-starter mindset, but in return offers a highly competitive compensation package with performance bonuses, excellent growth prospects, and a supportive culture focused on learning, collaboration, and work-life balance. Ideal for a seasoned sales professional eager to leverage their entrepreneurial spirit within a rapidly growing tech environment.
