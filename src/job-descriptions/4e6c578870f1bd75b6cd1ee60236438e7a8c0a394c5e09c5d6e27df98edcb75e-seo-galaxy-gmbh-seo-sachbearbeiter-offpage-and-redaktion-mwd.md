### Description\nSEO Galaxy is a modern SEO agency with its own SEO/Linkbuilding marketplace, dedicated to helping companies achieve visibility, authority, and growth efficiently, data-driven, and with clear processes. We are looking for a reliable, communicative personality who enjoys SEO, texts, and organization to manage and expand our link-building operations within an open, structured environment that values quality, communication, and independence.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Handling and managing link-building orders on our backlink marketplace\n- Communicating with publishers regarding placements, availabilities, and inquiries\n- Outreach, acquisition, review, and evaluation of domains based on relevant SEO metrics\n- Reading, correcting, and approving SEO texts and guest articles\n- Written communication with clients & publishers regarding status, feedback, and requirements\n- Careful organizational work: deadlines, documentation, and clean workflow management\n- Expansion of publisher networks and support for new link-building products\n\n### Qualifications\n- Experience in off-page SEO or link building\n- Confident handling of SEO metrics (DR, Trust Flow, Traffic, Relevance, etc.)\n- Very good written expression and editorial skills\n- Experience in reading, reviewing, and optimizing texts (proofreading)\n- Structured, reliable, and independent working style\n- Enjoyment of both written and telephone communication with clients and publishers\n- Advantageous: Experience with tools like Ahrefs or SISTRIX\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at SEO Galaxy offers a fantastic opportunity for an experienced SEO professional to thrive in a 100% remote, flexible, and growth-oriented environment. The company emphasizes clear processes, flat hierarchies, and individual autonomy, while also providing ample opportunities for professional development and contribution to new initiatives. If you're a self-starter with a passion for link building and enjoy being part of a supportive, modern agency, this could be an excellent fit for you.
