INFUSE is on a mission to power innovation and excellence in the global demand generation landscape. As part of our continued growth and commitment to cutting-edge solutions, we’re expanding our **Intelligent Automation Department** and looking for an **AI Engineer** with a strong track record in AI/ML.

* * *

**What You’ll Be Working On:**

*   Design, train, and deploy ML/AI models with a focus on voice recognition, natural language processing, LLMs, and multi-label text classification.
*   Build and maintain APIs/pipelines (Python/Flask/Quart, async) and n8n workflows (nodes, triggers, error handling, retries, observability).
*   MLOps: experiments, versioning, CI/CD, monitoring (quality, drift, latency, cost), rollback.  
    Optimize performance & spend.
*   Work with multi-modal data and text pipelines for automation and intelligent decision-making.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate AI into internal products and tools.
*   Build robust, scalable systems that can be adapted for various use cases across departments.

* * *

**Requirements:**

*   3+ years of hands-on commercial experience in **AI/ML development**.
*   Strong knowledge of **Python** (asyncio), **FastAPI**, **pandas/NumPy**, **scikit-learn**, and basic experience with **PyTorch** or **TensorFlow**.
*   Experience with **n8n** workflows, including webhooks, retries, and error handling, including parallelization, queueing, rate limiting, and security best practices.
*   **Data processing**: ETL, data cleaning, feature engineering, and evaluation set preparation(formats: CSV, JSON, Parquet).
*   Proficiency in **SQL** (PostgreSQL), **Redis**, and **message queues** (RabbitMQ, Redis Streams).
*   Solid understanding of **Docker**, **Docker Compose**, **Linux basics**, and **TLS/secrets management**.
*   Familiarity with **MLOps principles** — experiments, versioning, CI/CD pipelines (e.g., MLflow, MinIO, or similar), and monitoring using Prometheus and Grafana.
*   Strong command of **Git** and **object-oriented programming (OOP)**.
*   Experience in **documenting the development process** and creating related technical documentation.
*   **English** level B2 or higher.

**Would be a plus:**

*   Experience with retrieval-augmented generation **(RAG)**, **vector databases** and **search** (e.g., pgvector, Qdrant), **embeddings**, **guardrail techniques**, and familiarity with **hardware accelerators** (e.g., Groq).
*   Background in **server and infrastructure management**: deploying on closed environments, SSL configuration, backups, and role-based access control.
*   Familiarity with **RPA** (Robotic Process Automation) technologies.
