  That's a very keen observation, and it highlights one of the most exciting,
  yet challenging, aspects of our work. Integrating with real-world enterprise
  systems is rarely a clean process. We often deal with incomplete data,
  inconsistent formats, archaic APIs, and systems that weren't designed for AI
  agents to interact with them. Our approach is multi-faceted: first, we invest
  heavily in robust data validation and cleaning pipelines, treating data
  quality as a first-class citizen. Second, we design our agents with extreme
  resilience and error handling, anticipating failure modes and building
  sophisticated recovery mechanisms. We leverage tools like LangGraph to
  orchestrate complex flows, making them debuggable and observable. Finally, and
  crucially, we embrace an iterative, 'builder mindset'. We start with simpler
  interactions, learn from the 'messiness,' and incrementally refine our agents,
  often collaborating directly with client IT teams to understand the
  intricacies of their systems. It's about engineering pragmatism and a
  commitment to making agents trustworthy, not just 'magic'.

### Description\nZalion is an international AI start-up revolutionizing industrial procurement by using cutting-edge AI agents to automate manual Source-to-Pay processes. This frees up procurement teams for more strategic work.\n\nThe company is already live with some of the largest companies in Europe and the US, demonstrating tangible impact. Zalion fosters a culture of passion, efficiency, and rapid progress, where employees \"love what we do,\" \"get stuff done,\" and \"progress fast.\"\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Own and ship agent-powered features end-to-end, from initial idea and prototype through to production rollout and iteration.\n- Design, build, and deploy AI agents into production environments that interact with real systems such as ERPs, email platforms, and supplier portals, delivering measurable outcomes.\n- Work effectively with both structured and unstructured data, including RFQs, emails, quotes, and contracts, to power agent memory, retrieval mechanisms, and decision-making processes.\n- Build scalable memory, reasoning, and orchestration pipelines, leveraging tools like LangChain/LangGraph and robust evaluation frameworks.\n- Integrate with external APIs and legacy systems, navigating real-world complexities such as data messiness, rate limits, and various failure modes.\n- Contribute to the development and maintenance of Zalion's cloud infrastructure to support scalable agentic workloads, focusing on observability, CI/CD practices, reliability, and cost-awareness.\n- Solve complex problems related to context management, latency, robustness, and guardrails, ensuring AI agents are debuggable and trustworthy, rather than opaque.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Strong proficiency in Python and a proven track record of writing clean, maintainable, and well-tested backend code.\n- Solid experience in designing and building RESTful APIs and data models for production environments.\n- Experience with key data engineering concepts, including ETL processes, data quality assurance, and performance optimization, alongside strong backend architecture skills.\n- Hands-on experience building a real application or system with Large Language Models (LLMs) from providers like OpenAI, Claude, etc. (concrete side projects are welcome).\n- Comfort working in cloud environments (ideally AWS) with practical experience in CI/CD pipelines, logging, and monitoring.\n- Possess a true \"builder mindset\" – a preference for shipping small, iterating quickly, and taking full end-to-end ownership of your work.\n- Comfortable with ambiguity and demonstrating strong ownership: you proactively identify problems, propose solutions, and drive them to completion without requiring perfect specifications.\n- Roughly 4+ years of professional experience, including at least one role where you were solely responsible for a production service, subsystem, or a major feature.\n\n**Tech Stack You'll Work With:**\n- **Python**: LangChain, LangGraph, LangSmith, FastAPI\n- **AWS**: Bedrock, ECS Fargate, Lambda, S3, SQS/SNS\n- **Frontend**: Next.js, React, TanStack Query/Router\n- **DevOps/Monitoring**: GitHub CI/CD, Sentry, Grafana, Prometheus\n- **Databases**: Postgres, Redis\n- **AI Infrastructure**: Large Language Models (LLMs), evaluation frameworks, tracing, guardrails\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a seasoned AI/ML Backend Engineer who thrives on autonomy and making a direct impact.
