### Description

At Cloudera, we empower people to transform complex data into clear and actionable insights. As the preferred data partner for top companies in nearly every industry, we manage as much data as the hyperscalers. Powered by the relentless innovation of the open-source community, Cloudera advances digital transformation for the world’s largest enterprises.

We are seeking a proactive, collaborative, and customer-oriented Solutions Architect to join our Data Services adoption and acceleration team. In this role, you will own and evangelize reference enterprise data architectures, collaborating directly with our customers to build strong, trusted advisor relationships. If you're looking for a role that encourages creative freedom, lets you influence our product roadmap, and allows you to impact our most interesting use cases, this is the place for you!

### Responsibilities

*   Act as a product specialist and trusted advisor to help shape customer decisions to adopt the Cloudera Data Platform (CDP).
*   Build utilities, provide innovative solutions, and develop reference architectures for our customers.
*   Provide expert-level technical assistance to customers, addressing challenges and offering solutions to optimize their use of Cloudera's products.
*   Collaborate closely with sales, product, and engineering teams to ensure alignment with customer needs and the delivery of tailored solutions.
*   Gather and communicate customer feedback to internal teams, contributing to product improvements and the development of new features.
*   Contribute to the Cloudera community through blogs, meetups, industry events, and by adding to both internal and external knowledge repositories.
*   Understand and leverage advancements in AI and Hybrid Cloud to better serve clients and address their unique needs.

### Qualifications

*   8+ years of professional experience in building utilities, providing solutions, creating reference architectures, and driving product adoption.
*   5+ years of hands-on experience with Kubernetes, RedHat OpenShift, Rancher, Docker, or similar technologies.
*   Proficiency and hands-on experience with Linux/RHEL.
*   Experience with Amazon AWS, Microsoft Azure, and/or Google Cloud, including relevant skills and certifications.
*   Strong experience with SDS and SDN components within the Kubernetes ecosystem.
*   Hands-on knowledge of the data ecosystem, including AI/ML, data warehousing, data engineering, and data-in-motion technologies.
*   Experience with Java/Golang (Go), Python, or other scripting languages.
*   Familiarity with NVIDIA GPU technologies.
*   A Bachelor's or Master's degree from an accredited university is required.
*   A genuine passion for your work and a strong drive for success.

*Please note: This role is not eligible for immigration sponsorship.*

### Author's Take

**Candidate Question:** *The description mentions the ability to "influence the product roadmap." Can you give me a real-world example of how a Solutions Architect's feedback from a customer engagement directly led to a change in the platform?*

**Insider's Answer:** "That's a great question because it gets to the heart of how impactful this role is. Just last quarter, one of our architects was working with a major financial services client struggling with a very specific, niche data lineage requirement for regulatory compliance. Our platform was close, but not perfect for their use case. Instead of just creating a workaround, our architect documented the challenge, prototyped a potential API enhancement using Go, and presented it directly to the Data Engineering product team. The PM and engineers were so impressed with the clarity of the problem and the elegance of the proposed solution that they fast-tracked it. What started as a customer-specific problem is now a core feature in our latest CDP release. That's the kind of direct line you have from the field to the core product here—your expertise doesn't just solve one customer's problem, it shapes the future of the platform for everyone."

**Candidate Question:** *The job description mentions 'creative freedom'. How does the team balance creating standardized, scalable reference architectures with the need for unique, innovative solutions for specific customer challenges?*

**Insider's Answer:** "We think of it less as a balance and more as a cycle. We start with a strong foundation of standardized 'playbooks' and reference architectures—these are our best practices, battle-tested and ready to deploy. This ensures consistency and quality. But we know that no two customers are alike. About 70% of your time might be spent adapting and implementing these patterns, but the other 30% is what we call 'innovation time.' This is where your creative freedom comes in. When you hit a novel challenge, you're encouraged to go off-script, collaborate with other architects, and build something new. The best of these custom solutions are then refined, documented, and absorbed back into our core set of playbooks. It's a system that allows you to be both a disciplined architect and a creative problem-solver, ensuring that your most innovative work helps elevate the entire team."
