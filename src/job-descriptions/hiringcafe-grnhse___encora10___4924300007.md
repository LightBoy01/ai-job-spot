**Job Summary:**  
We are seeking a highly experienced **AI Architect** to lead the design, development, and implementation of AI/ML-driven solutions for global clients. The ideal candidate has a strong background in machine learning, cloud-native architectures, and enterprise-grade AI adoption. As a nearshore leader, you will collaborate with product owners, data scientists, and engineering teams to translate business needs into scalable AI solutions while ensuring compliance, performance, and innovation.

* * *

**Responsibilities and Duties:**

*   Lead the architecture and design of AI/ML platforms and solutions, ensuring scalability, security, and performance.
    
*   Partner with stakeholders to identify AI/ML opportunities and define adoption roadmaps.
    
*   Guide the selection of AI frameworks, libraries, and tools (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch, Hugging Face, LangChain).
    
*   Collaborate with data engineers to establish secure, high-quality pipelines for model training and inference.
    
*   Define and implement AI/ML governance practices, including MLOps, retraining, monitoring, versioning, and bias detection.
    
*   Provide technical leadership to data scientists, ML engineers, and software developers.
    
*   Drive AI integration into enterprise systems leveraging cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP).
    
*   Stay current with emerging AI/GenAI trends and advise clients on adoption strategies.
    
*   Ensure compliance with data privacy, ethical AI principles, and regulatory standards.
    
*   Act as a bridge between nearshore teams and global client stakeholders for alignment and delivery.
    

* * *

**Qualifications and Skills:**

*   10+ years of IT experience, including 5+ years in AI/ML solution architecture.
    
*   Expertise in machine learning, deep learning, NLP, and GenAI solutions.
    
*   Hands-on experience with cloud AI services (AWS Sagemaker, Azure ML, GCP Vertex AI).
    
*   Strong proficiency in Python, SQL, and distributed data processing (Spark, Databricks, Ray).
    
*   Demonstrated ability to design and deliver enterprise AI solutions at scale.
    
*   Excellent English communication skills, with the ability to present AI concepts to non-technical audiences.
    

* * *

**Role-specific Requirements:**

*   Experience with MLOps frameworks (MLflow, Kubeflow, SageMaker pipelines, DataRobot).
    
*   Understanding of microservices, APIs, and AI application integration.
    
*   Knowledge of AI governance, compliance, and responsible AI frameworks.
