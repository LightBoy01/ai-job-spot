### Description\n[Zenjob](https://www.zenjob.com/en/) is an AI-powered platform for flexible staffing solutions, efficiently connecting businesses and temporary workers since 2015. Over 40,000 students and 1,000 partner companies across logistics, retail, and hospitality in major German cities trust Zenjob to find or provide flexible work.\n\nAs Team Lead Key Account Management (m/w/x), you will play a central role in the support and development of our nationwide key accounts. In addition to leading a growing team, you will be responsible for your own highly relevant key account portfolio with a monthly revenue volume of €1-2 million. You will combine operational excellence, strategic guidance, and leadership skills to ensure sustainable growth, successful roll-outs, and high customer satisfaction.\n\nWorking closely with the Head of Key Account Management and cross-functional teams, you will drive the optimization of our Retail Vertical and internal processes. Through data-driven decisions, clear prioritization, and strong customer relationships, you will actively shape the future of our retail business.\n\n### Responsibilities\n**Leadership & Team Development (approx. 50%)**\n- Lead, coach, and develop a growing team of Key Account Managers and Account Development Representatives.\n- Conduct regular 1:1s, coaching sessions, performance reviews, and provide continuous feedback.\n- Set clear team goals, priorities, and workflows.\n- Monitor KPIs, including pipeline, risk, and performance analyses.\n- Support your team in customer meetings, strategy development, and escalation cases.\n\n**Own Key Account Portfolio (approx. 30%)**\n- Act as a strategic point of contact for nationwide retail key accounts.\n- Responsible for growth, retention, and data-based forecasting for your portfolio.\n- Expand existing accounts through structured roll-out strategies: new regions, new job profiles, new use cases.\n- Acquire new large retail customers.\n- Conduct Quarterly Meetings, create account plans, and proactively prevent churn.\n- Close collaboration with headquarters and regional stakeholders.\n\n**Strategic Projects & Process Optimization (approx. 20%)**\n- Further develop the Retail Vertical and our scale-up strategies.\n- Develop training programs for KAMs and ADRs.\n- Jointly create new roll-out concepts, GTM strategies, and marketing measures.\n- Improve tools and processes.\n- Represent the Retail Vertical in cross-functional initiatives with Finance, Product, Operations, and Marketing.\n\n**Success Metrics for this Role:**\n- Increasing portfolio revenue (CLV).\n- Increasing customer retention.\n- Number of new booking locations and new customers.\n- High customer contact rate (meetings, calls) for your team and your own accounts.\n\n### Qualifications\n**What you bring:**\n- Experience in leading or managing Account Management, Customer Success, or B2B Sales teams.\n- Strong commercial understanding in customer relations, expansion, sales, and forecasting.\n- Proven ability to implement processes, playbooks, and standards at team or departmental level.\n- Strong data-driven decision-making ability; confident use of KPIs, dashboards, and forecasts.\n- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.\n- Ability to increase performance and develop talent through coaching.\n- Willingness to travel (1-3 roadshows per quarter in a region in Germany).\n- Very good German and English language skills.\n\n**Desirable:**\n- Experience in the retail sector, ideally in staffing or temporary employment services.\n- Knowledge of CLM/lifecycle strategies, segmentation, or complex sales processes.\n- Experience with CRM tools (e.g., Salesforce).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a highly impactful leadership role for an experienced professional passionate about retail and scaling a fast-growing platform. Zenjob offers a vibrant, innovative, and deeply collaborative culture where your strategic input directly shapes the future of their Retail Vertical. With a strong focus on professional development, generous benefits (including a company car and hybrid work model), and a supportive team environment, this role provides an excellent opportunity to drive significant growth and make a tangible difference in a key sector.
