**Your future team**

Join Deloitte Luxembourg’s **Intelligent Automation & Agentic AI team**, where innovation meets problem-solving. Partner with clients to design, configure, and implement cutting-edge solutions that redefine business processes and drive the future of work. Together, we leverage automation platforms, GenAI, AI agents, and document management systems to create impactful solutions that meet unique organizational needs.

**Your advantages of being one of us**

1.  **Career Growth, Your Way**: Build a strong foundation for your career with top-notch training programs, certifications, and mentorship opportunities provided by experienced architects and technical leads.
2.  **Innovative Work That Matters**: Gain exposure to groundbreaking technologies like GenAI, intelligent agents, and low-code platforms that create tangible business results.
3.  **Inclusive, Flexible Culture**: Work in an agile, team-driven environment where colleagues inspire and support one another while maintaining a healthy work-life balance.
4.  **Global Impact, Local Connections**: Collaborate with top-level clients in Luxembourg and international networks for wide-reaching and impactful project opportunities.
5.  **Practice Makes Perfect**: Build hands-on technical expertise through real-world client projects while driving innovation.

**Your contribution to our success**

1.  **Technical Configuration & Implementation**: Configure automation workflows, bots, and AI-driven components tailored to each client’s needs. Implement document management rules, integrate APIs and cloud services, and test solutions while ensuring performance and compliance.
2.  **Support Delivery Activities**: Engage in proof-of-concepts, pilots, UAT (User Acceptance Testing), and production rollouts to ensure seamless delivery and client satisfaction. Document processes to maintain traceability and standards.
3.  **Learning & Innovation**: Explore emerging technologies like GenAI, Agentic AI, and low-code platforms. Participate in internal training, knowledge-sharing sessions, and hands-on innovations for accelerated delivery.
4.  **Collaboration & Teamwork**: Be an integral part of our agile team by collaborating with Technical Leads, Architects, and Functional Leads. Partner effectively with nearshore teams to support implementation tasks and project success.

**Your skills**

**Must-have skills:**

*   *   Master’s degree in **Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems**, or equivalent experience.
    *   4+ years of hands-on or academic experience with **automation, software development**, or **AI**.
    *   Familiarity with **automation platforms** or low-code solutions (specific tools will be trained as needed).

**Nice-to-have skills:**

*   *   Interest or previous exposure to **document management systems** (e.g., SharePoint, Documentum, OpenText).
    *   Curiosity about emerging technologies, including **GenAI, LLMs, chatbots**, and intelligent agents.
    *   Basic programming or scripting skills (**Python, Java, .NET**, or similar).
