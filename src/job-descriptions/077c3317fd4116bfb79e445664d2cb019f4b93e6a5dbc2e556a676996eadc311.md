### Description

Wix makes it possible for anyone to succeed online, and we're looking for a Communications Manager in Tokyo to help us tell that story in Japan. As a key member of our Japan Marketing and Communications team, you will work to establish and maintain Wix's public image, drive engagement with media and influencers, and align local PR activities with our global strategies. You'll be joining a dynamic and collaborative team that operates at a high level, supporting Wix's mission to become the main engine of the Internet.

Our goal is to create a more open web for all, and this role is crucial to empowering individuals, businesses, and organizations across Japan to thrive online. We value innovation, non-traditional thinking, and a welcoming environment where anyone has the right to succeed.

### Responsibilities

*   Build and maintain strong relationships with industry media, tech journalists, and social influencers in Japan.
*   Manage Wix's corporate social media presence, publishing engaging content that resonates with the local audience.
*   Plan and execute PR campaigns around online and offline events, including providing speaking support for executives.
*   Write and distribute compelling press releases, monitor media coverage, and report on key performance indicators (KPIs).
*   Coordinate closely with our global headquarters to ensure local PR activities are aligned with Wix's worldwide strategy.

### Qualifications

*   3–5+ years of experience in Communications or Public Relations, gained either in a PR agency or in-house corporate environment.
*   Proven experience with press release distribution platforms and media list management.
*   Familiarity with SEO and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) strategies for PR campaigns.
*   Proficiency with AI/Generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini) for research, planning, and content creation.
*   An adaptable and collaborative team player with a proactive mindset and a strong curiosity for technology.
*   Keen crisis awareness and a sharp eye for information accuracy and novel insights relevant to the local market.
*   Language Requirements: Native-level Japanese and Advanced Business-level English.
*   Experience in video content creation (e.g., short-form or event highlight videos) is a strong advantage.
*   Experience contributing to thought leadership initiatives (e.g., industry reports, whitepapers, keynote support) is also a plus.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "How much autonomy does the Japan team have to create local campaigns versus executing a global strategy?"**

That's a great question, and it gets to the heart of how we operate. Think of it less as a top-down directive and more as a partnership. Our global team provides a powerful framework and incredible resources, but we're not just localizing—we're creating. We absolutely count on the Japan team to be our cultural experts and innovators on the ground. For instance, a recent successful campaign targeting local artisans was born entirely from a casual chat within the Tokyo team about a unique trend we were seeing. We pitched it, got the green light, and ran with it. So, while you'll align with the global mission, your creative freedom to build narratives that genuinely connect with the Japanese audience isn't just permitted; it's expected and celebrated.

**Another great question would be: "Beyond media mentions and KPIs, what does a real 'win' look like for this role and for the team?"**

This role is about so much more than just the numbers on a report. A true win for us is seeing the direct impact of our work on a user's life. I'll never forget the time we secured a feature story for a small, family-owned business that used Wix to pivot online during a tough time. The owner later wrote to us, explaining how that single piece of press not only tripled their orders but gave them the confidence to hire their first employee. That's the win. It’s seeing our platform empower someone's dream. When that happens, the entire team—both here and globally—feels that success. We're not just promoting a product; we're amplifying the stories of creators and entrepreneurs across Japan.
