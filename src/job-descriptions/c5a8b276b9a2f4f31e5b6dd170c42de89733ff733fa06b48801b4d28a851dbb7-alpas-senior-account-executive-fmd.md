We’re not just building a product - we’re building the **world’s first global supplier search engine**. Now we’re looking for a **Senior Account Executive** who’s ready to take ownership, close B2B SaaS deals with top-tier enterprise customers, and have a real impact from day one.

Aufgaben
--------

*   **Drive growth** - Take charge of the sales cycle from lead generation through closing
*   **Consult & inspire** - Understand client needs and show them how our solution can transform their procurement
*   **Negotiate & close** - Lead contract negotiations with enterprise clients to win strategic deals
*   **Collaborate across teams** - Work hand-in-hand with Customer Success and Product
*   **Build your network** - Represent us at industry events and expand our reach
*   **Leverage data** - Use analytics to identify opportunities and improve processes

Qualifikation
-------------

*   3+ years of work experience in start-up environment or similar (e.g. consulting)
*   Proven track record of successful project management and customer communication
*   Exposure to top-tier professional environments
*   Affinity for tech and data science topics
*   Native-level German and excellent English
*   Proficiency in Microsoft Office
*   Bonus: Knowledge of procurement and supply chain topics

Benefits
--------

*   **Make an impact** - Shape the future of digital procurement in a fast-growing tech start-up
*   **High ownership** - Have the freedom of managing your own projects
*   **Steep learning curve** - Direct collaboration with our founder
*   **Hybrid work culture** - Enjoy the combination of our beautiful office in Berlin, Prenzlauer Berg, Home Office and remote work options
*   **Strong culture** - A diverse and supportive team with regular events that are actually fun

WHAT WE VALUE

*   Honest, fast, and open collaboration as well as strong communication skills
*   Resourceful self-starters who hold themselves to high standards, have attention to detail, are intrinsically motivated & eager to learn on a daily basis
*   Proactive hands-on working style with passion for customer-facing tasks
*   Team members who are excited about our mission & tech
*   If you’re thrilled to have a business impact and like the startup spirit, we’d love to talk to you!
