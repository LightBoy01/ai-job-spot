### **What You’ll Do:**

As the SVP of Product Management, you will define, own, and execute the product vision and strategy for CoreWeave’s next-generation AI cloud infrastructure products—including AI/ML services, GPU & CPU compute, storage, and networking. You will lead and scale a high-impact product organization, working closely with engineering, GTM, and executive leadership to deliver cloud services purpose-built for AI/ML workloads.

The ideal candidate combines **startup grit and zero-to-one product building experience** with the ability to scale products to **cloud-scale adoption**. You should have a track record of bringing new technologies to market in high-growth environments and operating at scale within cloud or hyperscale infrastructure. Above all, you are passionate about AI and cloud technologies, with a deep understanding of what it takes to build and scale products that developers, researchers, and enterprises love.

### **About the role:**

*   **Product Strategy & Vision:** Define and drive the long-term strategy for CoreWeave’s AI/ML, compute, storage, and networking cloud services, aligned with the company’s mission to serve AI and high-performance workloads.
*   **Zero-to-One Product Leadership:** Lead the creation and launch of new products and services from concept to scale, with a bias toward innovation, rapid iteration, and customer validation. Apply startup and high-growth best practices to accelerate product-market fit in new categories while laying the foundation for future scale.
*   **Cloud Infrastructure Expertise & Technical Product Ownership:** Oversee roadmap development and delivery of products such as AI/ML training and inference services, GPU/CPU instance families, NVMe and object storage, and high-performance networking fabrics (InfiniBand, low-latency interconnects). Stay ahead of advancements in AI/ML workloads and their implications on infrastructure, shaping product direction through deep technical and market insights.
*   **Team Leadership**: Build, lead, and mentor a high-caliber team of product managers across infrastructure domains. Establish a culture of technical excellence, customer obsession, and delivery accountability.
*   **Customer-Centric Development:** Partner with a wide range of strategic customers, engineering, and field teams to define and prioritize product features and infrastructure capabilities tailored to the needs of AI researchers, model training teams, and real-time inference providers to create highly differentiated products.
*   **Market Differentiation & Positioning:** Ensure CoreWeave’s AI cloud is differentiated through performance, cost-efficiency, scalability, and developer experience—specifically for AI workloads
*   **Operational Excellence:** Partner with engineering, infrastructure, and SRE teams to ensure product SLAs, uptime, provisioning speed, and resource availability meet the demands of large-scale AI workloads.
*   **Cross-Functional Leadership:** Collaborate with finance, marketing, customer success, and sales to ensure successful product launches, pricing models, and customer engagement strategies.

### **Who You Are:**

*   **15+ years** in product management leadership roles in cloud infrastructure, including at least **7+ years** leading PM teams across compute, storage, or networking domains.
*   Proven track record building zero-to-one products and scaling them in startup or high-growth environments, as well as operating at scale within a cloud provider or hyperscaler.
*   Deep technical understanding of AI/ML services, GPU/CPU resource provisioning, software-defined networking, storage systems (block, object, file), and orchestration platforms (Kubernetes, Slurm, etc.).
*   Experience serving AI/ML use cases, including familiarity with model training workflows, inference performance bottlenecks, and workload optimization patterns.
*   Results-Oriented: Proven track record of driving business growth through innovative product solutions in the AI and cloud space.
*   Customer-obsessed mindset, with direct experience engaging with developers, researchers, and platform teams building AI-native applications.
*   Excellent leadership, communication, and execution skills. Ability to operate in a fast-paced, ambiguous startup environment and scale product operations effectively.
*   Education: Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field. MBA or equivalent experience is a plus.
