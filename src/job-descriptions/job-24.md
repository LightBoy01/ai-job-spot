# Chatbot Developer, Dialogflow

### Description

Turing is helping companies build their engineering dream teams by connecting them with the world's best remote developers. We are looking for a talented Chatbot Developer with expertise in Google Dialogflow to join our network. As a Turing developer, you will have the opportunity to work on challenging projects for leading companies, all from the comfort of your home. If you are passionate about conversational AI and have a strong background in Dialogflow, we encourage you to apply.

### Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and deploy engaging and effective chatbots using Google Dialogflow.
- Integrate chatbots with various messaging platforms and backend systems.
- Implement natural language understanding (NLU) models to improve chatbot performance.
- Analyze chatbot data to identify areas for improvement and optimize the user experience.
- Collaborate with clients and internal teams to define requirements and deliver high-quality solutions.
- Stay up-to-date with the latest features and best practices for Dialogflow and conversational AI.

### Qualifications

- 3+ years of experience in chatbot development, with a focus on Google Dialogflow.
- Strong understanding of conversational AI concepts and principles.
- Experience with JavaScript/Node.js for building webhooks and integrations.
- Familiarity with cloud platforms such as Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
- Excellent problem-solving and communication skills.
- Ability to work independently and as part of a remote team.
