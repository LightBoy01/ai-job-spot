### About the Role

We are seeking a data-driven, business-savvy Senior Product Analyst specialized in creating and optimizing interactive forms within digital platforms. You will play a key role in designing seamless, personalized user flows for a travel website, leveraging advanced analytics and AI techniques to continuously enhance the user experience and maximize conversions.

### **Your Mission**

*   Measure and analyze user behavior using data tools such as Amplitude to identify friction points and abandonment rates.
*   Align product strategy with clear business KPIs and prioritize initiatives on the product roadmap.
*   Design and implement smart and dynamic forms.
*   Apply AI and machine learning (e.g., predictive models, intelligent autocomplete, segmentation) to improve form efficiency and user experience.
*   Collaborate closely with UX/UI and development teams to build scalable, adaptive flows (e.g., progressive, multistep, or conditional forms).
*   Propose A/B tests and experiments to validate new features or form variations.
*   Stay informed about best practices in travel industry UX, data personalization, and smart experiences.  
    

**Requirements**

*   4+ years of experience as a Product Analyst, preferably in B2C platforms, travel, e‑commerce, or SaaS.
*   Hands‑on experience in designing and optimizing web forms and conversion flows.
*   Fluent in both spoken and written English and Spanish.
*   Solid business and technical acumen.
*   Knowledge of experimentation frameworks: A/B testing, funnel analysis, cohorts, heatmaps, etc.
*   Familiarity with applied AI tools and frameworks (e.g., integration with LLMs, personalized recommendations, conversational assistants, scoring systems).
*   Experience working with multidisciplinary teams: data, engineering, design, and business.
*   User‑centric mindset, data‑driven decision‑making, and problem‑solving orientation.
*   Process‑oriented, team‑spirited, and performance‑driven.
*   Excellent organizational and time‑management skills.
*   A can‑do attitude with the ability to juggle multiple priorities simultaneously.  
      
    

**Nice to have:**

*   Prior experience in travel‑tech or online reservation platforms.
*   Familiarity with automation tools (Zapier, n8n, Make) or low‑code form platforms (Typeform, Jotform, Tally, etc.).
*   Experience with content recommendation or personalization engines (e.g., “recommended trips,” dynamic pricing adjustments).
