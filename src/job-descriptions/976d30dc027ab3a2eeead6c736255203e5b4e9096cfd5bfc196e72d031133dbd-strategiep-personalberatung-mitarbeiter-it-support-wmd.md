### Description\nOur client is a leading company in the development and production of high-precision measuring devices for the process industry. With over 50 years of experience, the company offers tailored solutions for measuring pressure, temperature, and level. Quality, customer proximity, and technological innovation are central to their work. Headquartered near Oldenburg, Germany, their products are distributed worldwide.\n\nWe are looking for an **IT Support Specialist (m/f/d)** for 1st-Level Support to strengthen our client's team immediately. This role is crucial as the first point of contact for IT-related inquiries, ensuring smooth operations and a positive user experience across the organization.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Act as the first point of contact for IT disruptions, service requests, and problems.\n- Structure and document inquiries, escalating complex tickets to higher support levels.\n- Provide support to users in the use of standard software and in the employed systems.\n- Set up and configure end devices such as PCs, laptops, and smartphones.\n- Maintain user accounts and manage access rights.\n- Create user-friendly instructions for various IT topics.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Relevant experience in 1st-Level Support or comparable IT experience.\n- Good knowledge of Windows and/or Linux systems.\n- Experience in setting up end devices.\n- Proficiency in managing user accounts.\n- Basic knowledge of user administration.\n- Communicative, helpful, and solution-oriented.\n- Reliable and friendly, even in stressful situations.\n- Fluent German in written and spoken word.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a Junior IT professional who is passionate about helping others and wants to be the vital first point of contact in a technologically innovative company. The role offers a clear path for impact by ensuring colleagues have the tools they need to succeed. With attractive benefits, including potential home office and flexible working hours, the company fosters a collaborative culture with a strong emphasis on individual appreciation and growth. If you're a German-speaking, service-minded IT enthusiast looking to contribute to a successful, global player, this role could be a perfect fit.
