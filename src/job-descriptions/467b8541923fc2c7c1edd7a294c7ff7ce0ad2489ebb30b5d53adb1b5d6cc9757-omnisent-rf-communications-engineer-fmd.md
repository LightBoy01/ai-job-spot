**Founded in late 2024, Omnisent is a Munich-based acoustic AI company developing next-generation sensing systems that turn sound into real-time intelligence. The team combines deep expertise in physics, machine learning, and mechanical engineering — united by a mission to unlock the untapped intelligence of the acoustic world.**

At the core of Omnisent’s innovation are ultra-low-power sonic devices and the company’s Large Acoustic Model (LAM) — a foundation model trained on the complex world of non-speech audio. This unique combination enables the real-time detection, classification, and localization of acoustic events — whether identifying drones in contested airspace or tracing air leaks in manufacturing systems.

Tasks
-----

*   **Architect the RF backbone** of a long-lived distributed sensing network for low-power, wide-area acoustic monitoring.
*   **Design and optimize sub-GHz / LPWAN communication** for multi-year battery operation across large distributed deployments.
*   **Develop robust mesh or hybrid mesh–LPWAN strategies** with self-healing, redundancy, and resilience in challenging environments.
*   **Design and integrate embedded antennas** into compact, ruggedized housings, collaborating closely with mechanical and electronics teams.
*   **Implement interference-tolerant communication techniques** suitable for real-world, noisy RF conditions.
*   **Ensure regulatory compliance** with ETSI/FCC/RED and regional spectrum requirements.
*   **Co-design communication strategies** with firmware teams to maximize power efficiency and reliability.
*   **Lead RF prototyping and validation** including lab testing and DFM-focused iteration for smooth production handoff.
*   **Collaborate with EMS partners** on manufacturing, test planning, and quality optimization.
*   **Conduct real-world RF testing** to evaluate range, performance, and environmental impacts.
*   **Explore emerging LPWAN and sub-GHz technologies** to keep Omnisent’s platform at the forefront of innovation.

Requirements
------------

**Basic Requirements:**

*   **Bachelor’s or Master’s degree** in Electrical Engineering, Communications Engineering, RF Engineering, or a closely related field.
*   **3+ years of experience** in RF or wireless communications engineering.
*   **Hands-on experience** with sub-GHz or LPWAN technologies.
*   **Strong understanding** of link budgets, modulation schemes, antennas, and RF propagation.
*   **Skilled with RF lab tools** (spectrum analyzers, VNAs, signal generators, etc.).
*   **Familiarity with ETSI/RED or FCC frameworks** and designing for successful compliance testing.
*   **Comfortable collaborating cross-functionally** with firmware, electronics, mechanical, and cloud teams.
*   **Strong communication skills** in English (German a plus).
*   **EU work authorization.**

**Preferred Requirements:**

*   **Experience taking RF systems** from prototype to field deployment.
*   **Background in interference mitigation** or robust communication techniques.
*   **Experience with DFM, DFT, and EMS collaboration.**
*   **Background in mission-critical systems** or industrial-grade platforms.
*   **Startup experience** and a desire to help build something truly groundbreaking.
