### Description
Join hey contact heroes, the leading \"work at home\" service provider, and become a hero! We are committed to inspiring both our employees and clients daily. We are a young, dynamic, and rapidly growing company in the Customer Service sector, distinguishing ourselves through 100% agile working. This means there is no fixed workplace; all colleagues work exclusively from their home offices. Our mission is to help our clients become market leaders, driven by high engagement, passion, flexible resources, extensive expertise, and our international team structure. We are looking for experienced Customer Advisors to join our dedicated team.

### Responsibilities
- Serve as the central point of contact, handling incoming customer inquiries via telephone and striving to resolve them in a customer-oriented manner using provided information and systems.
- Responsibilities include general product consultation as well as actively selling products from the 1&1 product portfolio.
- Work within a shift system, Monday to Sunday, between 7:00 AM and 11:00 PM.

### Qualifications
- A minimum of 6 months demonstrable prior experience as a customer advisor in a project for a mobile communications provider.
- Strong affinity for telecommunications and sales.
- Very good German language skills (oral and written – minimum C1 level).
- Absolutely proficient in PC usage.
- Willingness to work in shifts and weekend services (Monday to Sunday, between 7:00 AM and 11:00 PM) are not an issue for you.
- Possess a friendly demeanor and respectful communication skills.
- Love making diverse customer personalities happy.
- Permanent residence and workplace located in Germany.
- Capable of working effectively from a home office.

### Author's Take
This is a compelling opportunity for customer service professionals with at least 6 months of experience in the mobile sector who are looking for a fully remote role. hey contact heroes offers a dynamic, agile, and supportive work environment, providing all necessary home office equipment, a monthly home office allowance, and clear pathways for career development and increasing remuneration. If you're passionate about telecommunications and sales, thrive in a remote setting, and are comfortable with shift work, this role provides excellent growth potential within a rapidly expanding company that values its 'heroes' and their contributions.
