<strong>About The Role</strong>
<p>OpenLoop is looking for a strategic and hands-on Director of Engineering to join our team in our <strong>Nashville, TN office**</strong>.&nbsp; In this role, you will be responsible for driving the development of our technology roadmap, lead high-performing engineering teams, and integrating advanced and scalable solutions into our digital health platform.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>**Please Note:&nbsp; We’re growing and opening a new office in Nashville, TN (address TBD). This role will be onsite full-time, Monday through Friday. Until the new office is ready, you’ll be able to work remotely from home, with the expectation of moving onsite once the space is open.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>

<strong>What You’ll Do:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Provide strategic leadership and technical oversight across engineering functions (platform, infrastructure, product development &amp; DevOps).</li>
<li>Drive the development and implementation of scalable, secure, and compliant healthcare software solutions.</li>
<li>Partner cross-functionally with Product, Design, Clinical, and Data teams to define and execute the technical vision.</li>
<li>Partner with our teams to build and scale AI-driven features that enhance clinical workflows, patient engagement, and operational efficiency.</li>
<li>Foster a culture of innovation, ownership, and continuous improvement.</li>
<li>Lead hiring, mentoring, and development of engineering talent, promoting diversity and inclusion.</li>
<li>Ensure adherence to security, privacy, and healthcare compliance standards (HIPAA, SOC 2, etc.).</li>
<li>Manage vendor relationships and evaluate build vs. buy decisions for critical technologies.</li>
<li>Other duties as assigned.</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>

<strong>Who You Are:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field.</li>
<li>8-10+ years of software engineering experience, with 5+ in engineering leadership roles.</li>
<li>Proven success leading and scaling engineering teams in high-growth startups or tech-forward healthcare environments.</li>
<li>Experience implementing AI/ML solutions, especially in healthcare or enterprise software.</li>
<li>Deep understanding of cloud-based architecture (e.g., AWS, GCP), modern frameworks, and API design.</li>
<li>Experience with distributed systems, serverless technologies, and event-driven architectures.</li>
<li>Experience with healthcare interoperability standards (e.g., HL7, FHIR) is a plus.</li>
<li>Strong communication skills with the ability to translate technical strategy into business outcomes.</li>
<li>Passionate about improving healthcare delivery through technology.</li>
</ul>
