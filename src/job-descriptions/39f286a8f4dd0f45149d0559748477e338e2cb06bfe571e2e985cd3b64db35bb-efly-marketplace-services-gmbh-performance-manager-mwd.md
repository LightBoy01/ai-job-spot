### Description

Are you a data-driven individual passionate about optimizing advertising performance? We are looking for a **Performance Manager** to join our dynamic team and drive the success of our clients' Amazon advertising campaigns. In this role, you will be responsible for calculating and managing advertising budgets, with the ultimate goal of achieving the best possible visibility for products within defined budgets. You will regularly create detailed evaluations for clients and strategically position products on desired advertising spaces. Join a young, motivated team with flat hierarchies and an open communication culture, where your growth is supported through continuous learning opportunities and a flexible hybrid work model.

### Responsibilities

*   **Strategy Development:** Develop comprehensive SEA strategies in close collaboration with our Account Managers to optimally leverage and expand advertising potential on Amazon.
*   **Keyword Research & SEO Optimization:** Conduct in-depth keyword research and perform on-page and off-page analyses to continuously improve campaign performance.
*   **Campaign Optimization:** Maximize campaign potential through ongoing optimization and monitoring to achieve the best possible results.
*   **Data Analysis & Action Derivation:** Analyze campaign data using various analytical tools and derive concrete measures to enhance performance.
*   **Reporting:** Establish and maintain comprehensive reports to transparently document campaign results and successes for clients.
*   **Amazon Seller Central Management:** Utilize and manage Amazon Seller Central for monitoring and controlling campaigns.
*   **PPC Optimization:** Adjust and optimize PPC prices based on predefined strategies to maximize the efficiency and ROI of advertising measures.

### Qualifications

*   **Technical Know-how:** Excellent proficiency in Excel and PowerPoint is essential for professionally creating campaign analyses and presentations.
*   **Team & Communication Skills:** You enjoy working in a team and can express yourself clearly and precisely, both in writing and verbally.
*   **Analytical Thinking:** You possess a strong understanding of numbers and the ability to make data-driven decisions.
*   **Structured Communication:** Your written and verbal communication is clear, concise, and well-organized.
*   **Independent Work Style:** You work autonomously and are capable of efficiently managing tasks independently.
*   **Quick Comprehension:** You have the ability to quickly grasp new topics and understand complex relationships.
