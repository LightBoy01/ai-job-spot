### **Digital Content Generalist III**

### Are you looking for a new direction for your legal career?

### Do you have great research and writing skills?

### **About the Role**

### The Senior Commissioning Editor will proactively acquire and develop research content for print and online platforms in line with Legal Markets' strategic direction.

### **About the Team**

### LexisNexis Legal & Professional, which serves customers in more than 150 countries with 11,800 employees worldwide, is part of RELX (www.relx.com), a global provider of information-based analytics and decision tools for professional and business customers. Our company has been a long-time leader in deploying AI and advanced technologies to the legal market to improve productivity and transform the overall business and practice of law, deploying ethical and powerful generative AI solutions with a flexible, multi-model approach that prioritizes using the best model from today’s top model creators for each individual legal use case. The company employs over 2,000 technologists, data scientists, and experts to develop, test, and validate solutions in line with RELX Responsible AI Principles (https://stories.relx.com/responsible-ai-principles/index.html).

### **Responsibilities**

*   ### Commission and manage high-quality content across multiple sectors, ensuring timely delivery from the best authors and providers.
    
*   ### Drive growth by identifying new titles and editions, hitting commissioning and revenue targets.
    
*   ### Develop and present clear, commercially viable business cases in collaboration with authors and stakeholders.
    
*   ### Oversee the full commissioning process, managing budgets, schedules, and quality outputs.
    
*   ### Manage portfolios and publications to meet revenue and cost targets, contributing to budgeting and forecasting.
    
*   ### Negotiate and secure author contracts, including delivery schedules and remuneration.
    
*   ### Build strong, lasting relationships with authors to support current and future commissioning.
    
*   ### Work closely with internal teams to align commissioning plans with wider business strategy.
    

### **Requirements**

*   ### Recognised expertise in your practice area, with the ability to stay ahead of market and industry trends.
    
*   ### Skilled in gathering and interpreting market intelligence, competitor activity, and customer feedback to inform strategy.
    
*   ### Proven track record in shaping sales and marketing plans that drive product growth and customer engagement.
    
*   ### Commercially minded, able to spot opportunities, respond to market needs, and keep portfolios competitive.
    
*   ### Confident using technology and AI tools to streamline research, workflows, and decision-making.
    
*   ### Strong financial awareness with experience managing budgets, revenue, and costs while delivering to time and quality.
    
*   ### Excellent communicator and influencer, able to build lasting relationships and negotiate effectively with stakeholders.
    
*   ### Self-starter with sound judgment, problem-solving skills, and the ability to coach others and lead projects.
