As a Working Student in Quality Control, you will be directly involved in ensuring that every drone leaving our production line meets the highest quality standards. You will gain hands-on experience in a high-tech manufacturing environment, learn how professional quality assurance works, and collaborate with several departments to continuously improve our processes.

Your work will have a real impact: you help identify issues early, improve workflows, and ensure our customers receive perfectly functioning products.

  

#### **What is your Day to Day Mission:**

*   **Incoming inspection**: You check raw parts and components from suppliers - verifying dimensions, surface quality, functionality, and overall compliance with specifications.
    
*   **Outgoing inspection**: You ensure that finished products meet all requirements before they are shipped to customers.
    
*   **Process improvement**: You support projects aimed at making our inspection procedures faster, more reliable, and more efficient.
    
*   **Documentation**: You create and update work instructions so the entire operations team works with clear and up-to-date guidelines.
    
*   **Nonconformity management**: You help analyze defects, document issues, and work with other teams to identify root causes and define corrective actions.
    

  
  

#### **What you bring to the team:**

*   You are currently studying Process Management, Quality Management or a similar field.
    
*   You already have initial practical experience in Quality Control or Quality Assurance.
    
*   You have a personal interest in drones or model aviation.
    
*   Your open, communicative and friendly personality makes it easy for you to become a valued internal expert.
    
*   You are highly self-motivated and able to take responsibility for your work independently.
    
*   Communicating in English is natural and comfortable for you.
