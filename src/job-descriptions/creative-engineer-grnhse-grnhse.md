<p><strong>YOUR ROLE:</strong></p>
<p>RAPP is seeking a&nbsp;<strong>Creative Engineer</strong> who thrives at the intersection of design and execution. In this role, you will focus on building <strong>scalable component libraries</strong> within <strong>connected design systems</strong>, using <strong>atomic design theory and principles</strong> as your guide. You will collaborate with designers, art directors, and creative teams to ensure consistent, efficient, and innovative execution of design systems across projects. Mastery of tools like <strong>Figma</strong> and a passion for systems thinking are essential for success in this role.</p>
<p><strong>KEY COMPETENCIES:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Comprehension</strong>: Understand design systems, their components, and their purpose. Interpret briefs and apply atomic design principles to execute scalable solutions.</li>
<li><strong>Ideation</strong>: Partner with creative teams to identify opportunities for improvement and scalability within design systems.</li>
<li><strong>Craft &amp; Execution</strong>: Build and maintain scalable, modular component libraries in design tools like Figma, ensuring accuracy and consistency.</li>
<li><strong>Iteration &amp; Elevation</strong>: Continuously refine and improve design systems and components based on feedback, team insights, and emerging best practices.</li>
<li><strong>Directing &amp; Managing</strong>: Manage workflows effectively, ensuring deliverables are on time, on brand, and aligned with project goals.</li>
<li><strong>Storytelling &amp; Selling</strong>: Clearly articulate the value and utility of design systems to team members and stakeholders, demonstrating their impact on creative execution.</li>
<li><strong>Business Contribution</strong>: Deliver solutions that improve efficiency, consistency, and scalability for both the agency and its clients.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Build and maintain <strong>scalable component libraries</strong> using tools like Figma, ensuring alignment with design system standards and atomic design principles.</li>
<li>Work collaboratively with designers, art directors, and creative leads to ensure systems are executed consistently across projects and campaigns.</li>
<li>Translate design concepts into reusable components that can function across diverse platforms and channels.</li>
<li>Ensure that design systems are user-friendly, accessible, and adaptable to evolving client needs.</li>
<li>Partner with creative teams to identify gaps or inefficiencies within existing design systems and propose solutions to improve scalability and usability.</li>
<li>Stay informed on industry trends, best practices, and emerging technologies related to design systems and component-based design.</li>
<li>Provide guidance and support to creative teams on how to effectively use and implement design systems in their work.</li>
<li>Contribute to quality assurance by testing components for usability, accuracy, and consistency.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>SUCCESS CRITERIA</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Creation of scalable, modular component libraries that foster consistency and efficiency across projects.</li>
<li>Effective collaboration with creative teams to elevate the execution and usability of design systems.</li>
<li>Proactive identification of opportunities to refine and improve design systems based on feedback and emerging trends.</li>
<li>Thoughtful communication of design system principles to stakeholders, fostering confidence and understanding.</li>
<li>Consistently meeting deadlines while delivering high-quality, on-brand components.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Experience</strong>: 2-5 years in a creative engineering or design systems role, with a portfolio showcasing component-based design work.</li>
<li><strong>Technical Proficiency</strong>:
<ul>
<li><strong>Mastery of Figma</strong> is required, with expertise in building and managing scalable component libraries.</li>
<li>Familiarity with prototyping tools like Adobe XD or Sketch is a plus.</li>
<li>Knowledge of atomic design principles and their application in design systems.</li>
<li>Curiosity about and interest in experimenting with <strong>AI tools</strong> and emerging design technologies.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Systems Thinking</strong>: Ability to break down complex design systems into modular, reusable components.</li>
<li><strong>Collaboration</strong>: Proven ability to work with cross-functional creative teams and contribute to a culture of innovation and inclusivity.</li>
<li><strong>Communication</strong>: Ability to articulate the value and functionality of design systems to both technical and creative stakeholders.</li>
<li><strong>Adaptability</strong>: Thrive under tight deadlines, manage competing priorities, and embrace new challenges with enthusiasm.</li>
</ul>
