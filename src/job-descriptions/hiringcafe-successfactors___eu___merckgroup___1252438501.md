**Start date: January 2026**

**Duration: 6 - 12 months** 

**Localisation: Corsir-sur-Vevey**

**Role level: Internship**

Main project:

The primary focus of this internship will be on developing and implementing a capacity management model. The intern will work on projects that include:

*   **Training** on current capacity management tool used by other departments on site
*   **Observation and Measurement** of current quality operations activities in order to develop and build a capacity model in the context of the ongoing work in other departments
*   **Testing and Implementation** of the model with adaptations as needed

Your Role: 

*   Collaborate with a close-knit team of 8-10 employees to understand project requirements and objectives.
*   Develop the capacity management tool.
*   Conduct testing and validation by team of the tool to ensure functionality and reliability.
*   Document processes and results for future reference.

Your profil:

*   Currently pursuing a degree in Supply Chain, Industrial Engineering, or a related field.
*   Familiarity with AI and machine learning concepts is a plus.
*   Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
*   Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal.
*   Fluent in English

Benefits:

*   Gain hands-on experience in quality operations and capacity management.
*   Work in a collaborative and innovative environment.
*   Opportunity to contribute to meaningful projects that impact the organization.
