### Description

Matera is on a mission to empower apartment owners, enabling them to manage their properties transparently and easily themselves, or entrusting comprehensive management to us, ultimately saving them significant time and money. Established in France in 2017, we've already improved the lives of over 100,000 property owners daily. Since 2021, our ambitious team has also made a great start in Germany.

We are looking for an **Accounting Manager** with a passion for accounting to join our team in Berlin. You'll have the flexibility to work from our office or embrace a hybrid model, on a full-time basis, starting from September or within the next four months.

### Responsibilities

Your role at Matera will involve:

*   Managing the accounting for our customers (you'll receive structured training upon joining).
*   Transferring accounting results to the Matera platform and configuring it individually for each client.
*   Providing customers with an overview of their current accounting status and explaining their financial position.
*   Actively contributing to the further development of our product by bringing improvement suggestions to our Product and Tech team.

Curious for a deeper dive into the tasks? You can find insights in an **interview with Jorge** from our team!

### Qualifications

**What you bring to the table:**

*   You enjoy working with numbers.
*   You are highly precise and meticulous, even in detail.
*   Initial experience or knowledge in accounting is a plus.
*   You speak German at a good level and can communicate confidently in English.

*At Matera, we guarantee equal opportunities and diversity. Here, you are welcome exactly as you are!*
