**Key Responsibilities  
**  

Own discrete transformations from framing to handover:  
  

Frame & plan  
  

·       Clarify objectives, success metrics, scope, constraints, and stakeholders.

·       Build lightweight business cases: size impact, estimate cost/benefit, outline ROI with guidance.

·       Plan delivery: milestones, RAID, dependencies, resources, and succinct status comms.

Discover & design  
  

·       Run discovery: map current state (SIPOC, BPMN‑lite), identify root causes, and propose future‑state designs.

·       Define hypotheses and test plans; choose the smallest viable experiment to learn fastest.

Implement & land change  
  

·       Roll up sleeves on configuration, workflow design, and data setup; or coordinate vendors/contractors.

·       Manage change: stakeholder mapping, comms and training plans, UAT coordination, adoption measurement.

·       Baseline KPIs, instrument dashboards, run pilots/A‑B tests, and capture lessons learned.

Continuously improve  
  

·       Maintain playbooks, templates, and standards; raise the bar every project.

·       Share wins and learnings; enable others to repeat success.
