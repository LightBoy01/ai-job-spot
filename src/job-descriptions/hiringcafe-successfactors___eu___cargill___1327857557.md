Job Purpose and Impact
----------------------

*   The Senior Consultant, Application Development job builds, maintains, integrates and implements software applications within the organization. This position requires strong expertise in SAP system with preference with the ACM and/or MM/SD modules. With minimal supervision, this job performs complex software application testing, quality assurance, configuration, installation and support to ensure smooth, stable and timely implementation of SAP software and updates to installed applications. 

Key Accountabilities
--------------------

*   APPLICATION CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT (SAP/ACM): Builds complex application configurations to meet user and business requirements while identifying improvements to complex application support processes and procedures.
*   APPLICATION DEVELOPMENT & DEPLOYMENT (SAP): Performs advanced programming, configuration, testing and deployment of fixes or updates for application version releases.
*   USER COMMUNICATION & SUPPORT: Plays a key role and keeps an open channel of communication with users to respond to complex application support requests and needs.
*   APPLICATION SUPPORT (SAP): Conducts complex technical application support activities to deliver on business outcomes.
*   STAKEHOLDER MANAGEMENT: Partners with multi-functional teams, including developers, product managers and business partners to ensure configurations align with standards and project goals.
*   INCIDENT & REQUEST MANAGEMENT: Leads prioritization of incoming incident tickets and user requests.
*   VENDOR MANAGEMENT: Handles positive relationships with software vendors and negotiates complex contracts. 

Qualifications
--------------

*   Minimum requirement of 4 years of relevant work experience. Typically reflects 5 years or more of relevant experience.

Preferred: 

*   Proven experience delivering complex, custom-built software solutions, either from a business-facing or technical role, ideally within a product-centric delivery model.
*   Industry experience in Agriculture or Commodity Trading, with an understanding of the unique challenges and opportunities in these domains.
*   Hands-on experience or strong willingness to learn modern development platforms, including:
*   Back-end: SAP HANA Studio, SAP ABAP, Azure Functions, .NET Core
*   Front-end/UI: React, UI5, TypeScript, Fiori
*   AI/ML & Data: Django/Python, Azure ML, KNIME
*   Experience using advanced analytics and visualization tools, such as KNIME, Power BI, Tableau, or similar platforms, to support data-driven decision-making.
*   Demonstrated design and architecture principles including,  
*   Collaborating with solution architects and engineering teams to define and evolve component-based architectures that support scalable, reusable, and maintainable solutions.
*   Applying design thinking methodologies to deeply understand user needs, pain points, and workflows—translating insights into product features that deliver real business value.
*   Partnering with UX designers and business stakeholders to develop and refine personas, ensuring product decisions are grounded in user empathy and behavioral data.
*   Demonstrated success delivering or managing software products in a DevOps environment, with a focus on continuous integration, automated testing, and iterative delivery—moving beyond traditional RICEF models.
