# Generative AI Artist (Video)

### Description

Runway is building the next generation of creative tools, and we are looking for a talented Generative AI Artist with a focus on video to join our team. In this role, you will be responsible for creating stunning and innovative video content using our cutting-edge generative AI technology. You will work closely with our research and product teams to push the boundaries of what's possible with AI-powered video creation. This is a unique opportunity to be at the forefront of the creative AI revolution and shape the future of video.

### Responsibilities

- Create compelling video content using Runway's generative AI tools.
- Collaborate with our research and product teams to provide feedback and help improve our products.
- Experiment with new techniques and workflows for AI-powered video creation.
- Stay up-to-date with the latest trends in generative art and AI.
- Showcase your work and represent Runway at industry events and conferences.

### Qualifications

- A strong portfolio of creative work, with a focus on video.
- Experience with generative AI tools and techniques is a strong plus.
- Proficiency in video editing software such as Adobe Premiere Pro or Final Cut Pro.
- A creative mindset and a passion for storytelling.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
