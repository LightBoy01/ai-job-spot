Bolt is a rapidly growing tech company driven by its mission to \"make cities for people, not cars,\" fostering an inclusive environment where everyone is welcome.\n\nAs a Senior Android Engineer, you will be instrumental in designing, developing, and deploying robust mobile solutions to enhance Bolt's ride-hailing functionality. This role offers a unique opportunity for growth: mobile engineers at Bolt are encouraged to onboard to back-end development within their first year, paving a path to becoming a full-stack engineer. You'll also leverage a strong product-thinking inclination to shape and drive exciting product initiatives in close collaboration with Product Managers and your team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Designing, developing, A/B testing, and deploying solutions that improve our user journey.\n- Working closely with back-end engineers to define and implement API integrations for micro-mobility-related business logic.\n- Testing your solutions thoroughly to ensure they meet their intended goals, including building meaningful unit tests and collaborating with QA testers on usual and edge case scenarios.\n- Building a deep understanding of our products and their impact on business metrics and end-users, while contributing ideas and constructive feedback to our product roadmap.\n- Leading feature developments from idea generation through to bringing positive, validated impact to the end users.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Great at making data-driven decisions that positively impact the business; experience in product companies with a bias for properly experimenting their product hypotheses is a plus.\n- A strong drive for leading initiatives and features to completion, including monitoring and ensuring their success post-delivery.\n- Experience testing and releasing mobile changes while ensuring quality across multiple Android versions, with proficiency in unit testing and automation testing frameworks.\n- Strong principles towards writing clean, simple, secure, and maintainable code.\n- A solid understanding of algorithms and data structures.\n- Bolt values drive, intelligence, and integrity; applicants are encouraged to apply even if they don't tick every box.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a highly impactful Senior Android Engineering role at Bolt, a company with a strong mission to redefine urban mobility.
