**About HeyGen**

At HeyGen, our mission is to make visual storytelling accessible to all. Over the last decade, visual content has become the preferred method of information creation, consumption, and retention. But the ability to create such content, in particular videos, continues to be costly and challenging to scale. Our ambition is to build technology that equips more people with the power to reach, captivate, and inspire audiences.  
Learn more at [www.heygen.com](https://www.heygen.com).  Visit our Mission and Culture doc [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1JQ6k6Ce8VRE60zO09Uc94JVaxRWA2xC1-IbIFAAHPvs/edit?usp=sharing). 

**About the Role**We’re seeking a Product Manager Intern to FTE with excellent product sense and exceptional work ethic, someone who can identify pain points, develop solutions, and help execute cross-functional plans quickly and reliably. The ideal candidate has strong UX skills, is dynamic, a fast learner, and highly organized. You should feel confident working across design, data analysis, and technical concepts, and excel at communication and cross-functional collaboration. This is a high-impact, hands-on role offering meaningful ownership and a clear pathway to a full-time position for the right person.

**Highlights**

*   Work with a smart, energetic team focused on personal and professional development.
*   Take on significant responsibilities and make a meaningful impact on cutting-edge AI products.
*   Collaborate directly with founders and experienced professionals to create a world-class brand and product experience.

**Responsibilities**

*   Innovate the Creation Experience: Research and propose ideas to improve AI-powered content creation tools, including avatars and videos. Collaborate with the team to design and test new solutions that enhance user experience.
*   Engage with Customers: Conduct interviews with existing and potential customers to gather insights. Translate their needs into actionable product improvements.
*   Identify Core Problems: Use your research skills to analyze both qualitative and quantitative data, pinpointing the most critical challenges in AI-driven content creation.
*   Support Product Initiatives: Contribute to product development efforts by defining goals, creating mockups, and working with designers and developers to implement updates.
*   Stay Market-Savvy: Monitor industry trends, competitor advancements, and emerging technologies to keep the team informed and competitive.

**Requirements**

*   Recent graduate or in final term of bachelor's or master’s degree in Computer Science, Design, Business, or a related field.
*   Demonstrated interest in product management, AI, or content creation tools through coursework, projects, or internships.
*   Team Player: Excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively in a fast-paced environment.
*   Tech-Savvy: Basic understanding of software development or AI concepts, with a willingness to learn and adapt quickly.
*   Detail-Oriented: Strong organizational skills with attention to detail in research and execution.
*   Fast Learner: Comfortable picking up new tools, systems, and practices with minimal guidance.

**Bonus Points**

*   Experience with creative tools or content creation (e.g., video editing, design software).
*   You are a content creator passionate about storytelling.

**Compensation:**This is a paid internship with an opportunity to convert to full time employment.  HeyGen considers factors such as scope and responsibilities of the position, candidate's work experience, education/training, key skills, and internal equity, as well as location, market and business considerations when extending an offer.  As part of our total rewards package, HeyGen offers comprehensive benefits including a 401k plan, health benefits, generous PTO, a parental leave program and emotional health resources.
