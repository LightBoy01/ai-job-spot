### Description
Speechify is a leading platform making the world's information accessible via audio, enabling users to listen to articles, documents, and books at their own pace. As the #1 in its category and experiencing exponential growth, Speechify is transforming how people consume information, particularly benefiting those with learning differences.

The company is seeking a Senior Software Engineer to join its Core Experiences Team. This pivotal role focuses on building and maintaining the foundational services and SDKs that power Speechify's product experience across all platforms. It's ideal for a strategic thinker passionate about designing clear, reliable APIs and simple systems that directly enhance user experience, working at the crucial intersection of product and infrastructure.

### Responsibilities
- Collaborate closely with Speechify's Product Teams to define project scope, identify opportunities to reduce scope for faster customer feedback, navigate integration/UX tradeoffs across various product teams, and deliver robust solutions via the cross-platform SDK.
- Optimize existing functionality, such as improving loading times and parsing intelligence, by designing more efficient abstractions and refining algorithms.
- Develop and deploy cloud functions, lightweight backend services, and jobs primarily using Kotlin.

### Qualifications
- **Systems Thinking:** Possess an end-to-end design approach to system optimization and a strong belief in the value of simplicity.
- **Speed:** Ability to quickly generate ideas and options, and adept at deciding what can ship immediately versus what requires more development time.
- **Focus:** Capability to engage effectively or concentrate deeply as needed, thriving in a fast-paced, remote, high-growth startup environment.
- **Collaboration:** Skilled at inspiring, engaging, and negotiating with diverse and opinionated designers, marketers, and product managers.
- **Preferred Tech Stack:**
    - Proficiency in Kotlin Multiplatform (Java experience is acceptable, but the role primarily uses Kotlin Multiplatform).
    - Prior experience with Google Cloud Platform (GCP) is a plus.
