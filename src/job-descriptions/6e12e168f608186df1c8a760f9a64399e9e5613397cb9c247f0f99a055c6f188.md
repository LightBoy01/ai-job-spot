**YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES**

We are looking for a motivated and curious **Junior AI Automation Engineer** to join our team. This role is ideal for someone early in their career, with strong technical fundamentals and an eagerness to learn how to build intelligent agents and automate business processes using modern AI technologies. You will collaborate with consultants and business stakeholders to design, prototype, and implement automation solutions that drive efficiency and innovation across the company.

*   Design, develop, and maintain AI-powered automation workflows and intelligent agents
*   Build integrations between AI models (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, open-source LLMs) and enterprise tools (Power Automate, APIs, CRMs, databases, collaboration platforms)
*   Assist in rapid prototyping of new automation concepts and help scale them into production-ready solutions
*   Conduct testing, debugging, and performance optimization of automation scripts and agents
*   Stay up-to-date with emerging AI frameworks, libraries, and tools to bring innovative solutions to the team
*   Document processes, tools, and workflows to support knowledge sharing

**YOUR QUALIFICATIONS:**

*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Data Science, or a related technical field
*   Strong programming skills in Python or JavaScript (experience with APIs, automation, or scripting preferred)
*   Familiarity with AI/ML concepts (natural language processing, large language models, prompt engineering)
*   Experience with RESTful APIs, webhooks, or workflow automation tools (e.g., Zapier, Airflow, Make, LangChain)
*   Basic understanding of cloud environments (AWS, Azure, GCP) and version control (Git)
*   Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to learn new technologies quickly
*   Strong communication and collaboration skills in English 

**PREFERRED (NICE-TO-HAVE) SKILLS**

*   Coursework or internship experience in AI/ML, data pipelines, or intelligent automation
*   Exposure to tools like LangChain, RAG pipelines, or vector databases
*   Experience with databases (SQL/NoSQL) and basic data engineering practices
*   Familiarity with containerization and deployment workflows
*   Understanding of business process automation and workflow optimization
