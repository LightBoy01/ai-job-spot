PagerDuty, Inc. (NYSE:PD) is a global leader in digital operations management. Trusted by nearly half of both the Fortune 500 and the Forbes AI 50, as well as approximately two-thirds of the Fortune 100, PagerDuty is essential for delivering always-on digital experiences to modern businesses.

[Join us.](https://careers.pagerduty.com/) At PagerDuty, you'll tackle complex problems, collaborate with kind and ambitious people, and help build a more equitable world—all in a flexible, award-winning workplace.

**Are you passionate** about building the next generation of _**AI-driven systems?**_ At PagerDuty, our PagerDuty Advance team is at the forefront of transforming digital operations with intelligent automation and cutting-edge agent technology. 

As a Senior Software Engineer, you’ll have the unique opportunity to shape the future of our AI platform, being a part of this newly developed AI First division, you will drive innovation from the ground up, and see your work make a real impact for thousands of customers worldwide. If you thrive on solving complex problems, love working with the latest AI tools, and want to be part of a collaborative team that’s redefining what’s possible, we want to hear from you!

#### **Key Responsibilities**

*   Design, build, and maintain robust backend systems with a focus on AI and agent/chatbot technologies.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver new features and drive innovation in digital operations.
*   Participate in the full software development lifecycle, from design and architecture to deployment and support.
*   Take part in on-call rotations to ensure high availability and reliability of our services.
*   Contribute to technical discussions, code reviews, and mentor other engineers.
*   Work with cutting-edge technologies in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.

#### **Basic Qualifications**

*   5+ years of professional software engineering experience, with a strong focus on backend development.
*   Recent (last 6-12 months) hands-on experience in AI or chatbot development.
*   Experience shipping code as part of a team in a production environment.
*   Solid understanding of distributed systems, APIs, and scalable backend architectures.
*   Willingness to participate in on-call rotations.
*   Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
*   Strong communication and collaboration skills.
*   Based in Lisbon, with the ability to work remotely in Portugal after 20 days.

#### **Preferred Qualifications**

*   Experience with LangGraph (strong plus, as it’s foundational to our AI/agent work).
*   Experience with Elixir for backend development.
*   Background as a Backend Engineer with AI experience, or as a Machine Learning Engineer with product development experience.
*   Experience working in SaaS or cloud-based environments.
*   Familiarity with modern DevOps practices and CI/CD pipelines.
*   Experience mentoring or leading other engineers.

PagerDuty is a flexible, hybrid workplace. We embrace and encourage in-person working as an integral part of our culture. Both our employees and external research tells us that co-located collaboration strengthens connections, drives innovation, and accelerates learning.
