### Description\nThis growing energy sector company, based in Braunschweig, develops innovative and sustainable solutions in heating, cooling, and power supply. We are seeking an experienced professional for a permanent direct placement in **Building Services / Energy and Building Technology (m/f/d) - Heating, Cooling, Energy Technology**.\n\nIn this versatile role, you will significantly contribute to the planning, development, and implementation of modern energy concepts, accompanying projects from the initial technical idea to successful commissioning.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop technical concepts in heating, cooling, and air conditioning technology – for both existing buildings and new construction projects.\n- Create cost, budget, and resource plans, and prepare decision-making bases.\n- Manage external service providers (engineering firms, construction companies) during planning and execution.\n- Ensure legally compliant contractual frameworks.\n- Be responsible for budget and schedule controlling across all project phases.\n- Support projects through commissioning, including optimization and validation of energy solutions during later operation.\n- Close coordination with internal and external stakeholders to ensure economically viable, sustainable, and technically optimized solutions.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Minimum 5 years of professional experience in planning, design, or project management in heating, cooling, air conditioning, or energy technology.\n- Profound experience in building services (TGA) planning and project development, ideally from a construction, energy, or infrastructure-related environment.\n- Ability to economically evaluate technical concepts and adapt them to client needs.\n- Interdisciplinary understanding of connecting heating/cooling systems with electrical power generation systems.\n- Communicative, confident, structured, and solution-oriented – especially when facing complex technical challenges.\n- Organizational skills and a professional, obliging demeanor.\n- Must-have: profound experience in heating / cooling / thermal energy / heat pumps (no career changers).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a pivotal role within a dynamic and growing company dedicated to sustainable energy solutions. It offers a unique opportunity for a seasoned TGA/Energy Technology specialist to drive projects from conceptualization to long-term operational optimization. The ideal candidate will leverage their deep expertise in heating, cooling, and energy systems, coupled with strong stakeholder management skills, to deliver innovative and economically viable solutions. This position is perfect for someone passionate about making a tangible impact on environmental sustainability and energy efficiency, thriving in a collaborative and technically challenging environment.
