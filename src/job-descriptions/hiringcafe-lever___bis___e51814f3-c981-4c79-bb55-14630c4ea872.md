**What You'll Do**  
*   **Craft high-quality, long-form and short-form content** including trend reports, articles, e-books, blog posts, and tip lists that drive engagement and thought leadership.
*   **Use AI tools** to brainstorm, generate, and refine content ensuring everything aligns with our brand voice and quality standards.
*   **Develop and test effective AI prompts** to produce factual, cited, brand-aligned, and impactful content.
*   **Track content performance** and clearly communicate how your writing and the use of AI has supported BIS’s brand visibility, lead generation, or campaign success.
*   **Identify fresh, timely content ideas** that resonate with our target audience and support marketing goals.
*   **Edit and proofread content** to ensure clarity, consistency, and alignment with our tone and standards.
*   **Occasionally interview BIS clients** to uncover meaningful insights and transform them into compelling customer stories, case studies, and spotlights
*   **Apply SEO best practices** to improve searchability and reach of your content.
*   **Collaborate with marketing team members** to support campaigns, social media initiatives, and editorial planning.
*   **Maintain and organize content calendars**, ensuring deadlines are met and launches go smoothly.
*   **Stay current with AI writing trends** and technologies, and integrate new tools and approaches where appropriate.
  
  
**Who You Are**  
*   **Have hands-on experience with WordPress**, including setup, management, front-end customization, back-end troubleshooting, and plugin configuration. 
*   **Have a keen eye for design and usability**, and naturally notice how layout, formatting, and structure impact the user experience. 
*   **Have a strong sense for what makes content engaging**, backed by some training or experience in marketing writing, branding, or communications. 
*   **Thrive in fast-paced, collaborative environments**, and bring strong communication skills to team projects and cross-functional work. 
*   **Are a self-starter** who takes initiative, identifies opportunities, and enjoys solving problems without waiting for instructions. 
*   **Welcome feedback** and use it as a tool for personal and professional growth. 
*   **Are quick to adopt new technologies and tools**, driving AI and other automation opportunities to enhance modern marketing workflows. 
*   **Communicate clearly and confidently**, both in writing and in conversation, across a range of audiences. 
*   **Have experience supporting events**, including planning logistics, managing registrations, and helping with promotion for both virtual and in-person formats. 
  
  
**Bonus Points If You Have**

*   Familiarity with marketing analytics dashboards (Google Analytics, HubSpot, etc.)
