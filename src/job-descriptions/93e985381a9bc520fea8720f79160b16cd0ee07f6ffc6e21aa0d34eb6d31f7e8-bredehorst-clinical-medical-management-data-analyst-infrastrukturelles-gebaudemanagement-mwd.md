### Description\nThis role is for a highly analytical and detail-oriented Data Analyst / Controller who will be instrumental in processing, standardizing, and optimizing client data. You will play a key role in improving data quality, identifying cost-saving potentials, and supporting the team with actionable insights. The company offers a vibrant work environment, excellent benefits, and a focus on employee well-being and growth.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Data Preparation**: Collect and prepare client data, visualize data, and perform benchmarks and price comparisons.\n- **Data Standardization**: Consolidate multiple differently formatted Excel files into a master file.\n- **Team Collaboration**: Prepare and communicate data insights to the team.\n- **Real Controlling**: Review data quality, perform plausibility checks, and ensure consistency.\n- **Automation**: Make processes smarter (e.g., grouping rooms) to relieve the team.\n- **Optimization**: Identify savings potential and create comparative analyses.\n- **Training**: Desirably provide training and further education to internal employees in Excel.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Excel Expert**: Very good knowledge including Pivot tables, automation (macros desirable).\n- **Data Affinity**: Enjoy complex data preparation and data analysis.\n- **Controlling Experience**: Relevant professional experience in data analysis/controlling.\n- **Structured Way of Working**: Logically check datasets and analyze sources of error.\n- **Communication Skills**: Internal coordination and communication with the team.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an experienced Data Analyst or Controller who thrives in a dynamic, startup-like environment. The role offers a perfect blend of hands-on data work, strategic optimization, and the chance to directly impact team efficiency and business growth. With a strong emphasis on modern tools, employee well-being through benefits like fitness memberships and public transport, and a culture that values individual contributions and flat hierarchies, this company clearly invests in its people. If you're passionate about data, love finding efficiencies, and enjoy sharing your knowledge, this role in Düsseldorf could be an excellent next step.
