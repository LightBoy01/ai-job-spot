### Description\nEvery. is on a mission to redefine convenience food by combining over 40 years of industry experience with startup agility, making tasty, healthy, and ecologically and socially sustainable food accessible to everyone, everywhere.\n\nThis pivotal role as an Accountant (m/f/d) is perfect for a highly organized and analytical finance professional. You will be instrumental in managing all aspects of financial operations, including monthly closings, payroll preparation, and debtor/creditor management. Beyond day-to-day accounting, you will drive the development of digital, automated financial processes, provide actionable insights to the founders, and play a key role in the financial setup for international market expansion, directly shaping the company's growth trajectory.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Take full responsibility for the autonomous preparation of monthly financial statements (in-house booking of receivables, payables, bank accounts, credit cards, and monthly closing entries via Datev ASP).\n- Prepare summary reports and VAT pre-declarations using DATEV ASP.\n- Manage debtor and creditor accounts, including the creation and monitoring of outgoing invoices and payment receipts, as well as ensuring timely payment of incoming invoices from suppliers and service providers.\n- Prepare monthly payroll data (e.g., for new hires, salary changes) in collaboration with the tax office.\n- Continuously develop and optimize digital, automated accounting and invoicing processes.\n- Support the financial and tax setup of our international organization as we expand into new markets.\n- Generate weekly, monthly, and quarterly analyses and reports, providing actionable insights for the founders.\n- Occasionally assist with Office Management tasks, such as preparing and controlling IT accessories for employees or booking fixed assets.\n\n### Qualifications\n**Must-haves:**\n- Fluent in German and English.\n- Proficient in booking business transactions within DATEV ASP.\n- Solid experience with DATEV Unternehmen Online.\n- Highly structured and meticulous work ethic.\n- Strong quantitative and analytical understanding.\n- Good to very good Excel skills.\n- Competent in evaluating and analyzing key performance indicators (KPIs).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an experienced and proactive finance professional to make a significant impact within a rapidly growing, mission-driven startup. The role offers substantial autonomy from day one, ample room for personal and professional growth, and the freedom to implement your ideas. You'll be directly contributing to strategic decisions and international expansion, all while being part of a passionate team that values fun, innovation, and sustainability in the heart of Berlin. If you're ready to shape the financial future of a company reinventing an entire industry, this role is for you.
