<p>The Alaka`ina Foundation Family of Companies (FOCs) is looking for a <strong>Sr. Data Scientist</strong> to support our government customer located at Kirtland AFB in <i><strong>Albuquerque, New Mexico</strong></i>.</p><p>Kāpili maintains a repository of over 20,000 films, 2,000,000 photographs, and 60,000,000 pages of documents dating back to The Manhattan Project.
