**Job Description:**

We are seeking a skilled and versatile **Data Scientist** to join us as a contractor with the potential to be hired full time. In this role, you’ll collaborate with practice leaders, engineers, and cross-functional stakeholders to solve complex business challenges using data science and AI-driven approaches. You’ll work on end-to-end data science initiatives, with opportunities to design and implement cutting-edge solutions.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Design, develop, and deploy machine learning and deep learning models for Rx procurement and refill planning.
*   Lead projects involving **time-series forecasting, optimization, and reinforcement learning** for large-scale healthcare operations.
*   Apply expertise in **Python, Databricks, Vertex AI, Snowflake, and SQL** to build scalable solutions.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate business problems into data-driven solutions.

**Qualifications:**

*   Master’s or PhD in Data Science, Computer Science, Applied Mathematics, Operations Research, or related field.
*   2+ years of hands-on experience in applied machine learning and advanced analytics.
*   Strong background in **time-series modeling, optimization, and reinforcement learning**.
*   Proficiency in **Python, SQL, Databricks, and Snowflake**; experience with **Vertex AI or equivalent cloud ML platforms** is a plus.
*   Solid understanding of healthcare or pharmaceutical supply chain processes preferred.
*   Excellent problem-solving and communication skills.
*   Specialty Rx Procurement - Machine Learning, Optimization 

**Additional Information:**

This is a contract role through the end of the year with the potential to be hired full time if the project is extended.  No C2C please.
