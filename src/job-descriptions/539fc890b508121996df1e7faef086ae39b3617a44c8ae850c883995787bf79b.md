We are looking for a **Senior Integration Developer** to join a high-impact project for **one of our clients based in Sweden**. This is a **fully remote** opportunity with **immediate start**, ideal for an experienced engineer who enjoys building real-time, cloud-native integrations across distributed platforms. You will design and implement a microservice that bridges a customer engagement platform with an AI system, working within a modern infrastructure built on **AWS, Kubernetes, and Azure-hosted services**.

### **Your key responsibilities will be:**

*   Develop and deploy a **microservice in Kubernetes (AWS)** to act as a shim layer between a customer experience platform and an AI service.
*   Implement logic to manage real-time communication, event routing, and external service integration.
*   Design and expose secure APIs, ensure low-latency response, and handle streaming data.
*   Collaborate with platform teams and architects to ensure seamless integration and DevOps compliance.
*   Implement observability and monitoring using modern tooling.
*   Maintain robust documentation of architecture, development, and operational processes.

**Requirements**

*   7+ years of professional experience in backend or microservices development.

*   Strong coding skills in **Java, Node.js, or Python**.
*   Deep knowledge of **Kubernetes (EKS preferred)**, including **Helm**, operators, manifests.
*   Experience with **CI/CD pipelines**, preferably using **GitOps** practices.
*   Strong working knowledge of **AWS services** (EKS, IAM, API Gateway, CloudWatch, etc.).
*   Familiarity with **Azure AI services** and APIs is a plus.
*   Understanding of **real-time APIs** and protocols (e.g., **WebSocket**, gRPC, event streaming).
*   Solid grasp of **cloud security**, data protection, and compliance standards.
*   Strong problem-solving ability and capacity to work independently.

### **You will be a great candidate for us if you:**

*   Have experience delivering **mission-critical integration services** in production.
