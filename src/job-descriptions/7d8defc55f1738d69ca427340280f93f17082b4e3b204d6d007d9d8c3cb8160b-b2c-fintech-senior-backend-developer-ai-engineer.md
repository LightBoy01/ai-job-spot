### Description\nWe are a Berlin-based, B2C fintech operating in stealth mode, founded by two repeat entrepreneurs (YC alum, exit) with prior 10+ year careers in investment banking and strategy consulting, respectively. Having recently secured investment, we are assembling a 15-person, exceptionally strong core team.\n\nAs a Senior Backend Developer / AI Engineer, you will collaborate closely with our CTO to build and evolve our AI-powered fintech product. With a solid core architecture in place, you will be instrumental in integrating advanced LLM capabilities, establishing robust AI pipelines, implementing intelligent automation, and shipping production-ready AI features. This role offers significant autonomy to design and implement cutting-edge solutions, directly shaping how we leverage AI to transform financial services.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Designing and implementing LLM-powered features and intelligent automation workflows.\n- Building robust AI pipelines with proper orchestration, error handling, and monitoring.\n- Developing RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) systems for financial data processing.\n- Implementing and optimizing vector search and embedding strategies.\n- Creating evaluation frameworks for LLM outputs and maintaining quality standards.\n- Building prompt management systems and optimizing LLM interactions for cost and performance.\n- Integrating multiple AI models and managing model versioning/deployment.\n- Ensuring AI safety, reliability, and compliance in financial applications.\n\n### Qualifications\nTo succeed in this role, you should possess:\n\n**Hard Requirements**\n- **LLM Framework Expertise**: Deep hands-on experience with LangChain.js, LangGraph for JavaScript, or similar Node.js-based LLM frameworks. You understand agent architectures, chain composition, memory management, and can build complex multi-step AI workflows in TypeScript/JavaScript.\n- **Workflow Orchestration**: Production experience with Temporal.io (TypeScript SDK), Bull/BullMQ, or similar Node.js-compatible orchestration solutions. You know how to build reliable, long-running workflows with proper error handling, retries, and state management.\n- **Vector Database & RAG Systems**: Proven experience implementing RAG architectures using vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant, or pgvector) with Node.js clients. You understand embedding models, similarity search, and hybrid retrieval strategies.\n- **Node.js & TypeScript Mastery**: Expert-level proficiency in Node.js and TypeScript with extensive experience integrating AI services (OpenAI, Anthropic, Cohere APIs). You're comfortable with streams, async patterns, WebSockets for real-time AI responses, and optimizing for latency.\n\n**Additional Technical Skills**\n- Experience with prompt engineering and prompt management systems accessible from Node.js.\n- Hands-on experience with AI model APIs and their Node.js SDKs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Hugging Face, Replicate).\n- Knowledge of AI observability tools with Node.js integration (LangSmith, Helicone, or custom solutions).\n- Proficiency with streaming architectures and Server-Sent Events (SSE) for real-time AI responses.\n- Understanding of AI safety, hallucination detection, and output validation techniques.\n- Familiarity with AI evaluation frameworks and automated testing of LLM outputs.\n\n**Mindset & Approach**\n- **AI-Native Thinking**: Ability to think in terms of probabilistic systems, understand LLM limitations, and design robust systems around non-deterministic outputs.\n- **Pragmatic Innovation**: Balancing cutting-edge AI capabilities with production requirements, knowing when to use simple solutions versus complex AI architectures.\n- **Ownership Mentality**: Proactive, resourceful, and accountable for your work—you don't wait to be told what to do.\n- **Team Player**: Collaborative, able to explain AI concepts to non-technical stakeholders, and committed to elevating those around you.\n- **Quality-Focused**: Understanding the importance of evaluation, testing, and monitoring in AI systems and building with observability in mind.\n- **Continuous Learner**: Stays current with the rapidly evolving AI landscape, experiments with new models and techniques, and is energized by pushing the boundaries of what's possible.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a Senior Backend / AI Engineer passionate about building transformative AI products in fintech. Working in a stealth startup with repeat founders means high impact, significant autonomy, and a chance to truly shape the product from an early stage. The role demands deep technical expertise in Node.js, TypeScript, and various LLM/AI frameworks, alongside a pragmatic and quality-focused mindset. Based in Berlin-Mitte with flexible remote work, the company offers a competitive salary (€80k-€110k+) and a culture that values ownership, fast decision-making, and continuous learning. If you're ready to tackle complex AI challenges and drive innovation in financial services, this role offers a truly unique platform.
