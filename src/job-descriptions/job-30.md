# Prompt Engineer

### Description

Anthropic is an AI safety and research company that's working to build reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We are looking for a talented and creative Prompt Engineer to join our team. In this role, you will be responsible for developing and refining the prompts that we use to interact with our large language models. You will work closely with our research and product teams to create prompts that are effective, safe, and aligned with our mission. This is a unique opportunity to work at the cutting edge of AI and make a significant contribution to the development of safe and beneficial AI.

### Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and test prompts for a variety of applications.
- Analyze and interpret the behavior of our language models to improve prompt design.
- Collaborate with researchers and engineers to develop new prompting techniques.
- Stay up-to-date with the latest research in prompt engineering and AI safety.
- Contribute to the development of tools and infrastructure for prompt engineering.

### Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in a relevant field, or equivalent practical experience.
- Strong understanding of natural language processing and machine learning concepts.
- Excellent writing and communication skills.
- A creative and analytical mindset.
- A passion for AI safety and a desire to make a positive impact on the world.
