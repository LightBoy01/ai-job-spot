<b>Role Summary</b>
<br>
We are seeking a German speaker Legal Counsel to join our Legal team specializing in Contracts &amp; Commercial matters with a primary focus on the DACH region (Germany, Austria and Switzerland). for the DACH region.
<br>
As a member of our legal team (based in Paris), you will be instrumental in:
• Supporting our business in drafting our contracts, agreements and terms to enable deal making and revenue generation, in particular (but not exclusively)&nbsp; in the DACH region.
• Drive contracts negotiations and help contracts adaptation to the evolving AI regulation and compliance
<br>
The role is based in Paris and reports into our senior commercial counsel. The legal team currently comprises 12 persons across Paris and Palo Alto, covering a spectrum of competences across compliance, IP, contracts and corporate.
<br>
<br>
<b>What you will do</b>
<br>
• Draft and provide strategic advice on a wide range of contracts, including but not limited to AI-related service agreements.
• Negotiate (markup, arguments, strategies) such contracts with suppliers, partners, customers and other stakeholders.
• With the support of other team members in their respective fields of expertise, stay updated on the latest products and AI-related legal and regulatory developments, including when coming from customers’ fields, and provide guidance to their translation into contract adaptation and negotiation.
• Collaborate with cross-functional teams in close partnership with technical and business teams to ensure legal and regulatory compliance in the deployment of AI technologies.
• Provide training and support to the business on legal and compliance matters related to AI, and on contract negotiation.
• Help on other legal topics as needed (labor law, corporate, competition law, etc.)
<br>
<br>
<b>About you</b>
<br>
<b>Background: </b>Master degree in law, a bar admission is a plus, as well as an experience in a fast-growing environment.
<b>Experience</b>: a minimum of 4-6 years of relevant legal experience, preferably in a related technology or AI-focused environment involving contract negotiation.
<b>Languages:</b> Fluency in German and English is required; French is a plus.
<b>Knowledge of Artificial Intelligence: </b>An understanding of artificial intelligence technologies and their applications is essential. Good knowledge of the relevant area of law, such as data privacy, intellectual property, contract law and liability, regulatory and compliance.
<b>Analytical Skills</b>: Ability to analyze complex legal and technical issues related to AI and to identify potential legal risks and solutions is important. Candidates should be detail-oriented and have strong problem-solving skills.
<b>Communication Skills:</b> Excellent written and verbal communication; Strong ability to present complex legal concepts in a clear and understandable manner. Business oriented.
<b>International mindset</b>: An international experience is a plus.
<b>Adaptability: </b>Given the rapidly evolving nature of AI technologies and the legal landscape surrounding them, candidates should be adaptable and willing to continuously learn and update their skills and knowledge. Ability to work independently and in a team-oriented, collaborative environment. Proactive, flexible, and able to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.&nbsp;
<b>Ethical Awareness: </b>A strong understanding of ethical considerations related to AI, such as fairness, transparency, accountability, and bias mitigation, is important.
