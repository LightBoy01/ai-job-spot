<p><strong>Company Overview</strong>&nbsp;<br>&nbsp;<br><strong>Join the leader transforming healthcare waste management across America.</strong>&nbsp;</p>
<p>MedPro Disposal is the nation's largest privately-held medical waste management company, serving 35,000+ providers across 48 states in a $2+ billion market. Since 2009, we've been the trusted partner helping healthcare facilities save up to 30% on waste management costs while maintaining 99% on-time service.&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>What we do:</strong> We lead with comprehensive medical waste disposal solutions, plus pharmaceutical waste management, secure document destruction, and compliance training services&mdash;all powered by innovative technology and a client-first approach.&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Why it matters:</strong> We handle the critical but complex challenge of medical waste management so healthcare providers can focus on what they do best: delivering exceptional patient care.&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Our impact:</strong> From physician practices to hospitals, dental offices to long-term care facilities, we're trusted advisors and strategic partners&mdash;not just vendors. We combine proven expertise with environmental responsibility and comprehensive protection to solve complex operational challenges.&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Ready to grow with us?</strong></p>
<p>Join a fast-scaling, mission-driven company where we Do The Right Thing, roll up our sleeves with a "figure it out" attitude, and win as a team. We're building something meaningful&mdash;making healthcare safer, more compliant, and more efficient&mdash;one client at a time.&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Position Overview:</strong></p>
<p>We&rsquo;re seeking a Go-to-Market Systems Specialist to own and optimize the platforms that power our revenue and operations teams. You&rsquo;ll serve as the connective tissue between departments&mdash;managing, improving, and aligning our CRM, marketing automation, and sales enablement tools to help MedPro scale its operations, improve pipeline velocity, and deliver a best-in-class customer experience.&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Key Responsibilities:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Serve as primary <strong>Salesforce administrator</strong> &mdash; manage configurations, user permissions, page layouts, validation rules, workflows, and automation.</li>
<li>Administer and enhance Go-To-Market platforms (Salesforce, Outreach, Instantly,&nbsp;Five9, Velocify, etc.).</li>
<li>Maintain and improve automation flows, campaign integrations, and CRM configurations.</li>
<li>Streamline workflows from lead generation through customer onboarding.</li>
<li>Partner with sales, marketing, and support to reduce friction and increase visibility across the funnel.</li>
<li>Build dashboards and reporting for KPIs such as lead conversion, campaign ROI, and onboarding velocity.</li>
<li>Maintain data hygiene, support accurate forecasting, and enforce data governance standards.</li>
<li>Oversee integrations between GTM tools and other business systems (billing, compliance, e-signature).</li>
<li>Support API-based workflows (Zapier, MuleSoft) to ensure seamless data flow.</li>
<li>Train teams on CRM and automation best practices.</li>
<li>Document system processes, updates, and best practices.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Required Qualifications:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Experience: 2-3+ years in Salesforce administration and GTM/revenue systems management, ideally in a B2B environment.</li>
<li>Technical Skills:</li>
<ul>
<li>Intermediate to Advanced Salesforce Admin skills (certification preferred).</li>
<li>Expertise in marketing automation platforms (Pardot/Marketing Cloud,HubSpot, Marketo).</li>
<li>Familiarity with analytics tools (Power BI, Tableau).</li>
<li>Understanding of revenue enablement tools (Gong, Outreach, SalesLoft).</li>
<li>API integration experience (Zapier, MuleSoft).</li>
<li>Proficiency in project management tools (Asana, Monday, etc.)</li>
</ul>
<li>Analytical Skills: Strong ability to interpret data and generate actionable insights.</li>
<li>Communication: Excellent cross-functional collaboration and user training skills.</li>
<li>Education: Bachelor&rsquo;s degree in Business, Information Systems, or equivalent experience.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Preferred Qualifications:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>CRM admin experience</li>
<li>Experience in healthcare or medical waste industries.</li>
<li>Familiarity with compliance tracking or billing systems.</li>
</ul>
