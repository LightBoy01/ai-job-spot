**EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT** 

Time Equities, Inc. (“TEI”), an international real estate investment and development firm, is seeking a proactive, ambitious, multitasking, detail-oriented professional to serve as an Executive Assistant to both the Head of Equity Strategy and the Director of Portfolio Strategy. Although this position will support, report to, and take directions from both these executives, there will also be team assignments as well as collaborate with the teams.

This role will provide a broad range of executive assistant responsibilities to keep these executives working at peak efficiency. This role has an impact. The ideal candidate has a positive team-oriented attitude, works well with others, has strong written and communication skills, a growth mindset, and strives for excellence and efficiency. The successful candidate will have the desire and ability to learn the real estate and company-specific aspects of the role.

**RESPONSIBILITIES** 

**The Equity Team**

*   Scheduling meetings, appointments, travel, media placements, etc.
*   Assisting the team and the Head of Equity Strategy in preparation for speaking assignments and other investor communications.
*   Organizing financial reports (both for TEI and personal).
*   Managing limited investor correspondence.
*   Helping to set up and organize remote offices.
*   Assisting with new business development and/or relationships when needed
*   Assisting in organizing various due diligence and offering materials for new and existing properties
*   Assistance with monthly team reports and annual business plan for the Equity Department
*   Assisting with marketing and team, and materials
*   Assist with some personal assistance work such as bill paying, managing emails, learning new AI and other tech programs, etc.

 **The Portfolio Management Team**

*   Process Deal memos, wire requests, form/compliance preparation, and scheduling.
*   Provide data collection and input for analyses, including historical acquisitions and UW peer reviews, portfolio performance, and loan information.
*   Portfolio overview: square footage, addresses, managers, purchase dates, operating info
*   Assist in management committee reporting.
*   Assist in the preparation of presentations.
*   Provide outreach and follow-ups, and team communications.
*   General organization

**REQUIREMENTS** 

 A good working knowledge of Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, and Excel is a bonus, would be a willingness to learn how to use AI for efficiency and other tech features.

*   A minimum of three (3) years of experience as an Executive Assistant.
*   Experience working in a real estate firm is a plus.

**BENEFITS AND PERKS:** 

*   A broad competitive compensation and benefits package including Medical, Dental, Vision, Life, Disability, supplemental disability, parental leave policies 401K & match, profit sharing, and broad voluntary and pre-tax benefits
*   Tuition reimbursement  
*   Employee assistance wellness programs and services, including mentorship, health advocate, EAP, counseling, financial planning, and legal consultation
*   Employee engagement, including the Women’s Equity and Wellness Committee 

**Salary Range: $80K to 90K**
