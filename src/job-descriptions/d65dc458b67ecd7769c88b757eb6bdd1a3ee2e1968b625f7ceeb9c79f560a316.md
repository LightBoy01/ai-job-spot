**Position Specific Description**
Join our dynamic team as an AI and Automation Intern, where you'll engage in projects that drive transformative change through artificial intelligence. Your role will focus on enhancing operational processes to boost efficiency and effectiveness in a fast-paced, innovative environment.
**Key Responsibilities:**
*   Automation Solutions Development: Collaborate with experienced team members on creating AI-driven automation solutions
*   Process Analysis: Evaluate operational workflows to pinpoint AI automation opportunities
*   AI Model Training: Engage in the training and refining of AI models under expert guidance
*   Prototype Testing: Assist in developing and testing prototypes to validate solution effectiveness
*   Data Analysis: Contribute to data collection, preprocessing, and analysis to inform strategic insights
*   Documentation: Maintain detailed documentation of processes, models, and solutions developed during your internship
**Qualifications and Skills:**
*   Pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, or related field
*   Proficiency in programming languages such as Python or R
*   Basic knowledge of AI and machine learning frameworks (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch)
*   Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities
*   Excellent communication and teamwork skills
Job Overview
------------
To be considered for an internship, you must be actively enrolled at a college or university as a full-time student seeking a bachelor’s degree or higher. You must also have a current cumulative GPA of 3.00 or higher (on a 0.00 to 4.00 scale).
