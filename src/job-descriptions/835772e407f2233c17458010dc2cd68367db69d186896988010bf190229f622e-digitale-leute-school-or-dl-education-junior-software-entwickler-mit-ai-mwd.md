### Description\nDigitale Leute offers an AI Software Engineering Bootcamp aimed at experienced software developers looking to advance their careers. The program provides practical, up-to-date AI knowledge combined with product management methods, taught by trainers from leading tech companies. It focuses on transforming developers into strategic co-creators who can influence product development in the AI era. Upon completion, participants receive a certificate and active support in job searching through Digitale Leute's strong hiring network. The bootcamp is also available for job seekers with a training voucher at no cost and can be completed alongside an existing job.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **From Developer to Co-creator**: Learn to analyze business models and user needs, formulate hypotheses, and translate insights into roadmaps and product backlogs, growing your strategic influence as AI handles repetitive coding tasks.\n- **Architecture & Responsibility in the AI Age**: Develop structured and sustainable software based on proven architectural principles, identifying risks in AI deployment, monitoring AI decisions, and managing costs, API limits, and system performance.\n- **AI-supported Development & Deployment**: Utilize modern AI tools to develop stable prototypes or MVPs, automate testing, and securely deploy, while maintaining oversight of AI decisions, quality, requirement adherence, and live observability.\n- **Orchestrate AI as a Team Member**: Strategically deploy AI within a team, develop agents for various tasks, and orchestrate their collaboration. Automate workflows and integrate corporate data via frameworks like LangChain or MCP to create valuable AI features.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Passion for technology and developing user-centric products.\n- Solid knowledge in modern software development.\n- Curiosity, ambition, and an active pursuit of professional development.\n- Enjoyment of agile methodologies (Scrum, Kanban) and teamwork.\n- Strong empathy for users and stakeholders, with an understanding of product-oriented technical solutions.\n- Readiness to delve into complex, rapidly evolving topics like AI development and Agentic Workflows.\n- Adaptability to changes in tech stacks and new tools.\n- Enthusiasm for thinking outside the box and taking on both technical and product-strategic responsibility.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis bootcamp offers a highly relevant and forward-thinking opportunity for software developers to future-proof their careers in the age of AI. It strategically blends advanced technical skills with crucial product management insights, preparing participants not just to code with AI, but to truly lead and innovate using it. The emphasis on mentorship, practical application, and a strong hiring network makes it a compelling pathway for significant career advancement and impact.
