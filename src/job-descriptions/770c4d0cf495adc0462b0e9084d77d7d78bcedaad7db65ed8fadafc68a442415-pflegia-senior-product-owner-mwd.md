### Description\nAt Pflegia, we're on a mission to revolutionize healthcare recruitment across Europe. We're building and operating an innovative job-matching platform that intelligently connects caregivers with healthcare employers, with the bold vision to become Europe's leading platform for nursing professions and actively fight the nursing crisis. As a Senior Product Manager, you will be instrumental in shaping this future, owning the end-to-end product lifecycle from ideation to delivery, deeply embedding AI to create impactful solutions, and ensuring our platform continuously evolves to meet the critical needs of the healthcare sector.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Analyze business requirements, breaking them down into clear roadmaps, timelines, epics, tickets, and tasks.\n- Document, analyze, and continuously improve the entire user journey across Pflegia and other products.\n- Create actionable concepts for new products or features, estimate efforts and ROI, and own the complete product process from ideation to delivery.\n- Proactively think about how AI can provide significant impact, design experiments with well-defined expected outcomes, and iterate based on verification.\n- Define, track, improve, and revise key performance indicators (KPIs) through continuous iteration.\n- Collaborate effectively with stakeholders, developers, and data teams, utilizing data-driven storytelling to align and convince all teams.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 5–8+ years of proven Product Ownership/Management experience.\n- Strong ability to define product vision, roadmaps, and priorities, expertly aligning business goals, customer needs, and technical constraints.\n- Expertise in writing clear epics, user stories, and acceptance criteria, along with documenting complicated workflows and funnels.\n- Experience with search, SEO (including AIEO, AEO, GEO), or matching/recommendation systems.\n- Demonstrated data-driven decision making (analytics, A/B testing, experimentation).\n- Experience building and scaling marketplaces, job boards, or recruitment platforms, and improving conversion funnels and user experience across both job seeker and employer journeys.\n- High empathy for healthcare professionals and familiarity with the needs of healthcare facilities, as well as healthcare hiring and recruitment workflows.\n- Strong stakeholder management and communication skills.\n- Proven track record working effectively with cross-functional agile teams.\n- Comfortable operating in a fast-paced scale-up environment with ambitious goals, demonstrating resilience under pressure.\n- Outstanding problem-solving, prioritization, and critical thinking skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned Senior Product Manager to make a profound impact at a purpose-driven scale-up. Pflegia offers a unique blend of mission-driven work fighting a critical societal challenge, coupled with the agility and autonomy of a flat-hierarchied startup. You'll have the chance to deeply embed AI, define product strategy from the ground up, and see your work directly contribute to improving healthcare. The company culture is highly collaborative, supportive, and growth-oriented, with empathic leadership and the flexibility of unlimited home office. If you're looking to own significant product initiatives and drive tangible change in a fast-paced, innovative environment, Pflegia provides an ideal platform for your expertise.
