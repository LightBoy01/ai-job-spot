**MLEU, CTO role:**

**Leading at Cognizant**

This is a Leadership role at Cognizant. We believe how you lead is as important as what you deliver. Cognizant leaders at every level: **Drive our business strategy** and inspire teams around our future. **Live the leadership behaviors**, leading themselves, others and the business. **Uphold our Values**, role modeling them in every action and decision. **Nurture our people and culture**, creating a workplace where all can thrive.

At Cognizant, leadership transcends titles and is embodied in actions and behaviors. We empower our leaders at every level to drive business strategy, inspire teams, uphold our values, and foster an inclusive culture.

**About the role**

As a CTO of MLEU you will be a valued member of the team and work collaboratively with key stakeholders and clients.  

**In this role, you will:**

Report to head of the Business Unit. As a CTO for the sector, you will be responsible for creating and executing a focused proactive market strategy for Cognizant’s services and solutions that enable our clients to modernize technology, reimagine processes and transform experiences so they stay ahead in a fast-changing world.

\-        As CTO for the Manufacturing division within the MLEU sector, you will be the strategic technology leader driving Cognizant’s AI-powered transformation agenda across North America.

\-        This role is responsible for shaping and executing a data-driven strategy that enables clients to unlock operational efficiency, accelerate innovation, and build resilient, intelligent enterprises. You’ll lead with vision, collaborate across service lines, and elevate Cognizant’s presence in the industrial and diversified manufacturing space.

\-        Explore possible new assets for acquisition to build future capability

\-        Engage with analysts/advisors to socialize and Credentialize Cognizant Manufacturing, Logistics, Energy and Utilities credentials and GTM'

\-        Consistently demonstrate the **Cognizant Way to Lead,** which means operating with **Personal Leadership** (building trust, collaboration, and inclusion), **Organizational Leadership** (driving vision and purpose, demonstrating a strategic and enterprise mindset, and creating and communicating a bold direction that inspires purpose), and **Business Leadership** (exemplifying client focus, managing ambiguity with accountability and results, and operating with financial acumen)

**What you need to have to be considered**

*   Develop and execute a forward-looking technology strategy leveraging AI, machine learning, and advanced analytics to transform client operations
*   Drive profitable growth through identification and closure of new, renewal, and expansion opportunities
*   Architect data-centric solutions that enable predictive maintenance, supply chain optimization, and intelligent automation
*   Collaborate with delivery, operations, and finance teams to monitor and optimize account performance metrics
*   Lead senior-level client engagements, shaping digital transformation roadmaps and aligning technology investments with business outcomes
*   Partner with Cognizant’s innovation hubs and service lines to co-create scalable solutions for manufacturing clients
*   Cultivate executive relationships with client decision-makers and influencers to drive strategic alignment
*   Ensure execution excellence and customer satisfaction across all revenue portfolios
*   Leverage Cognizant’s ecosystem of AI and cloud partners to accelerate solution delivery and market differentiation
*   Champion thought leadership and represent Cognizant at industry forums, client events, and strategic roundtables
*   Drive visibility and influence within the manufacturing sector through strategic storytelling, data-backed insights, and innovation showcases

·       Embodiment of the **Cognizant Way to Lead**: Leading Self, Leading Others, & Leading the Business

·       The embodiment of Cognizant’s Values of: Work as One, Dare to Innovate, Raise the Bar, Do The right Thing, & Own It

**This will help you succeed**

*   Responsible for driving AI strategy across Manufacturing, Logistics, Energy and Utilities
    
*   Socializing strategy with clients and tracking key outcomes/metrics from engagements.
    
*   Drive key customer forums 
    
*   Build partnerships/alliance ecosystem around AI
    
*   Assist in driving broader MLEU Strategy and partnerships
