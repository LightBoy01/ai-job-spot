### Description\nimmocloud is revolutionizing real estate management for landlords and administrators, serving over **15,000 customers** and experiencing strong growth. While most customers convert directly during the trial phase, for larger clients (ACV €1,000 - €20,000), our Sales Team makes the difference by advising them through the decision process.\n\nTo better leverage these opportunities and systematically scale our sales, we are seeking a **Team Lead Sales (m/f/d)** to lead, develop, and actively sell alongside our small sales team.\n\nYour core responsibility is to ensure that promising sales opportunities reliably convert into **successful deals**, and that our Sales Team has the right structures, playbooks, and routines to achieve this. Your role is divided into two main areas: leadership/coaching and active sales. In short, you lead not from a desk, but **from the front line**.\n\n### Responsibilities\n**1) Leadership, Coaching, and Sales Concept (approx. 30 - 40%)**\n- Provide technical leadership for our two Sales Managers.\n- Develop clear, repeatable sales playbooks, including discovery frameworks, call scripts, objection handling, closing strategies, and follow-up mechanisms around the trial phase.\n- Analyze pipeline, win rates, and deal cycles with the team, and derive concrete measures to increase performance.\n- Work closely with the CEO and Marketing to prioritize leads effectively and focus the team's capacity on the right customers.\n- Ensure that insights from sales – from customer requirements to recurring objections or complaints – are structured and fed back into the company to be utilized by Product and Support.\n\n**2) Active Sales & Building New Channels (approx. 60 - 70%)**\n- Take direct responsibility for a portion of customers, guiding them to close in a few clear touchpoints.\n- Test and establish outbound approaches (e.g., cold outreach, sequences, campaigns) so that we are not solely reliant on inbound leads.\n- Refine our positioning in discussions with decision-makers and build long-term customer relationships in the core segment.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Several years of experience in B2B SaaS sales, ideally with a trial or product-led motion and ACVs in the €1,000 - €20,000 range.\n- Initial experience in leading or technically managing a small sales team – or a very clear ambition to take this step now.\n- Demonstrable success in: conversion rate improvement, structured work with pipelines and KPIs, and short, focused sales cycles.\n- Strong conceptual sales skills: You can not only use playbooks, guidelines, and processes but also develop them yourself.\n- Ideally, industry knowledge in real estate or property management (e.g., rentals, homeowners' associations, property management), but at least a clear willingness to quickly and deeply familiarize yourself with this environment.\n- High self-motivation, ownership, and enjoyment in pragmatically testing and improving things.\n- Very good German, both spoken and written; English is a plus.\n- Willingness to be regularly in the Düsseldorf office every week (hybrid possible, purely remote not).\n- No field sales: This is a digital inside sales role without travel expenses or on-site appointments. Customer contact primarily occurs via video, phone, and email.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a dynamic and highly impactful role for an experienced B2B SaaS sales professional with leadership aspirations. It offers significant autonomy to build and optimize sales strategies, direct influence on company growth, and high visibility within a rapidly growing company. You'll be instrumental in both leading a small team to new heights and actively contributing to sales, bringing your insights directly to the CEO. The company fosters a collaborative environment, valuing proactive development and providing flexibility through a hybrid work model. With 30 vacation days and a commitment to fair compensation, immocloud offers an exciting opportunity for a driven individual to make a tangible difference in digitalizing the real estate industry.
