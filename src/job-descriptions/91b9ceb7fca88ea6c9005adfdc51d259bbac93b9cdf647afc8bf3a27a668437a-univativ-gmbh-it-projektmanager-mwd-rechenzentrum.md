### Description\nunivativ is a project and personnel service provider deeply committed to your career development and professional growth. Our client is a prominent company in the energy sector, offering an innovative environment where you can contribute to exciting tasks and continuously expand your knowledge. We are looking for an IT Project Manager (m/w/d) to support a critical and large-scale data center migration project. This role offers the opportunity to work 38 hours per week in Stuttgart, with the flexibility of remote work options and a competitive annual salary. We prioritize providing quick feedback on your application and empower your development through diverse further training opportunities via our e-academy, alongside attractive corporate benefits.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- You will be responsible for the planning, steering, and monitoring of a large data center migration project.\n- You will maintain project plans, key milestones, and status reports, ensuring transparency and progress tracking.\n- You will organize and accurately document project meetings, capturing decisions and action items.\n- The analysis of project key figures and performance metrics will also be a core part of your tasks.\n- You will effectively coordinate internal teams and external service providers to ensure project alignment and delivery.\n- Furthermore, you will provide crucial support in risk management and change request processes.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A completed degree in Business Informatics, Economics, or a comparable qualification is required.\n- Demonstrated experience in a Project Management Office (PMO) environment.\n- Good knowledge and practical application of established project management methods such as PMI and PRINCE2.\n- Relevant experience in an IT infrastructure environment, ideally with hands-on experience concerning data center systems.\n- Proficient and secure handling of project management tools like MS Project or MS Azure DevOps.\n- At least business-fluent German language skills, both in written and spoken communication.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an experienced project management professional to play a pivotal role in a critical data center migration for a leading company in the energy sector. Univativ offers a supportive environment deeply focused on your growth and development, with comprehensive training opportunities through their e-academy and the flexibility of remote work. If you're an organized communicator with a passion for complex IT infrastructure projects and strong PM skills, this role provides the perfect platform to make a significant impact and advance your career. The competitive salary and robust benefits package further sweeten the deal, making this a highly attractive position for those ready for their next challenge.
