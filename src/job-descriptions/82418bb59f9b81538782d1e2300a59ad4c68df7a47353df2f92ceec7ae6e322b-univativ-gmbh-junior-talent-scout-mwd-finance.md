### Description\nUnivativ is a project and personnel service provider dedicated to connecting students and graduates with exciting opportunities at renowned companies. Our mission is your career development, as your growth is at the core of what we do.\n\nIn today's competitive landscape, businesses face increasing challenges in attracting the \"richtigen\" (right) talent, while students and graduates find it overwhelming to discover the ideal employer and job amidst a vast array of possibilities. This is where univativ steps in – we're the bridge that makes both sides happy! Young professionals trust us as career advisors and companions, and approximately 280 companies, from startups to global corporations, partner with us because we truly understand their needs. In essence, we are expert \"match-makers\" for career starters and experienced professionals alike.\n\nWe are seeking new colleagues with exceptional \"match-making\" qualities to join our Hannover branch as a Recruiter (m/f/d) in the Finance sector. If you're a recruiting professional eager to infuse fresh energy into your career, or a university graduate with initial experience in HR consulting or a corporate HR department, you'll find your place with us. Regardless of your background, you should be keen to learn a great deal and have a strong interest in finance topics. Your journey with us begins with our commitment to growth and expanding our university networks – driven by fresh ideas, innovative processes, strong team spirit, and of course, you! You'll be part of the team from day one, learning our business through on-the-job experience and comprehensive training. You'll also benefit from the ongoing support of experienced colleagues. This is a full-time, permanent position based in our Hannover office, with options for remote work by arrangement.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Identify suitable candidates within the Finance & Legal sectors, ideally utilizing our internal talent pool or active sourcing strategies.\n*   Ensure service-oriented applicant management and continuously expand our talent pool.\n*   Understand client requirements, provide individual consultation as a competent point of contact, and successfully connect companies with employees.\n*   Create and publish engaging job advertisements for your specific roles to attract new talent.\n*   Focus on inspiring applicants about the opportunities at univativ, including conducting independent job interviews.\n*   Proactively coordinate with the Payroll department regarding onboarding, offboarding, and ongoing questions during employee assignments.\n*   Prioritize the satisfaction of your clients and employees throughout their project engagements.\n*   Collaborate closely with Key Account Managers to address client requests for vacancies at key accounts.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   We're looking for versatile individuals who find HR and recruiting exciting, with a particular interest in finance topics.\n*   You are a recruiting professional, a university graduate with initial HR experience, or a career changer with a connection to HR.\n*   Initial experience in the finance environment through your studies or practical work at banks, insurance companies, or other finance-related activities is ideal, but not a mandatory requirement.\n*   Your personality is key: You have a genuine enjoyment of interacting with people and the ability to vividly describe job opportunities and career paths, inspiring others.\n*   Equally important: You possess the ambition and perseverance to find the perfect match for every position.\n*   Furthermore, you are well-organized, independent, an enthusiastic team player, and excel with strong communication skills and a service-oriented mindset.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at univativ offers a fantastic opportunity for an ambitious individual to significantly impact both talent careers and client success. The company fosters a supportive and collaborative environment, emphasizing continuous learning and professional growth through structured training and mentorship. With a focus on diversity, open ideas, and a fun, team-oriented atmosphere – complete with a \"mini-table tennis table\" – univativ truly values its employees. The position provides a permanent contract, competitive salary with bonuses, attractive benefits like corporate shopping and bike leasing, and the flexibility of hybrid work, all within a centrally located office in Hannover. It's an ideal step for those passionate about HR, finance, and making a tangible difference.
