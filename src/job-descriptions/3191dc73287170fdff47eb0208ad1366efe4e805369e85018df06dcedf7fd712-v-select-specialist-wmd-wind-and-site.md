### Description\nOur client, a leading project developer and operator of wind energy and photovoltaic systems in Europe, is looking for a Wind & Site Specialist (m/f/d) to join their team in Flensburg immediately. In this role, you will be crucial in the independent planning and optimization of wind parks, contributing directly to the growth of renewable energy.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Independently plan wind parks using windPRO software.\n- Conduct turbulence, power, sound, and shadow calculations for wind parks in windPRO.\n- Create maps for the acquisition and project development team using GIS software, including importing cadastral data and land maps.\n- Ensure wind park planning aligns with manufacturer specifications.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A completed degree in Geography, Landscape Planning, Renewable Energies, or a similar field.\n- Initial professional experience in wind turbine planning using windPRO.\n- Initial practical experience with GIS software is an advantage.\n- Very good German and good English language skills.\n- A high degree of independence, reliability, and organizational strength.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an ambitious individual eager to make a tangible impact in the renewable energy sector. The role offers challenging and diverse tasks within a growth market focused on climate protection. The company truly values its employees, providing a supportive environment with flexible working hours, remote work options, 30 days of vacation, and extensive opportunities for international professional development.
