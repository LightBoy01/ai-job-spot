### **Digital Marketing Manager - New Markets** 

### **About SumUp**

We believe in the everyday hero — those who have the courage to follow their passion and the determination to realise their dreams.

Small business owners are at the heart of all we do, so we're creating powerful, easy-to-use financial solutions to help them run their businesses. With a founder's mentality and a _team-first_ attitude, our diverse teams across Europe, South America and the United States work together to ensure that small business owners can be successful doing what they love.

### **💡 Team Description**

You'll join our Global Expansion team, a cross-functional group driving SumUp's entry into new markets worldwide.

Our mission is to build and scale SumUp's presence in regions where millions of small businesses are still underserved with most recent or upcoming  launches in **Australia,** **Mexico, Canada and more countries to come.**

As Digital Marketing Manager, you'll define and execute the go-to-market and growth strategies for these new countries. You'll shape how we acquire, activate, and retain our first merchants, designing data-driven experiments, building scalable playbooks, and collaborating closely with product, partnerships, and data teams to accelerate traction from day one.

This is a hands-on, high-impact role for a strategic operator who thrives in ambiguity, moves fast, and enjoys building growth engines from scratch.

### **🚀 What You'll Do**

*   Lead go-to-market growth across new and emerging markets, driving user acquisition and early activation.
*   Develop and own the full-funnel growth strategy, including budgeting, forecasting, and performance tracking across multiple channels.
*   Manage and optimize paid acquisition campaigns (Google, Meta, Programmatic, Affiliates) to drive measurable results with lean budgets.
*   Build and execute CRM and lifecycle flows to improve onboarding, engagement, and retention in the first year post-launch.
*   Partner with Product, Data, and Finance to design reporting, attribution, and performance dashboards, optimizing for payback and ROI.
*   Run rapid tests and experiments to validate product-market fit, identify growth loops, and localize positioning per geography.
*   Lead external partners and agencies while documenting playbooks and learnings for future market rollouts.
*   Collaborate with local and global teams to ensure smooth transitions from pilot phase to full-scale operations.  
      
    

### **🙌 You'll Be Great for This Role If**

*   You have 5–8+ years of experience in growth and performance marketing, ideally within fintech, SaaS, or fast-paced digital businesses.
*   You're hands-on and analytical — comfortable setting up campaigns, tracking funnels, and working with Excel/Google Sheets,  GA4, Google Analytics and/or Firebase and BI dashboards (Tableau, Looker, Power BI or similar).
*   You've managed six-figure marketing budgets and can demonstrate measurable impact on acquisition and retention metrics.
*   You combine strategic thinking with operational grit — able to build frameworks while executing fast in ambiguous environments.
*   You're a natural collaborator, skilled at influencing cross-functional stakeholders across Product, Partnerships, and Data.
*   You thrive in international settings, adapting quickly to new markets and cultural contexts.
*   Bonus: experience launching products in emerging markets or regulated industries (payments, banking, e-commerce).
