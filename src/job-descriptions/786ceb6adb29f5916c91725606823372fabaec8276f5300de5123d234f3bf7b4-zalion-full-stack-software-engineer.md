**Zalion is an international AI start-up revolutionizing industrial procurement.** We use cutting-edge AI agents to automate manual Source-to-Pay processes, freeing up procurement teams for strategic work.

We are live with the largest companies in Europe & the US.

We love what we do. We get stuff done. We progress fast.

Tasks
-----

*   **Own and ship end-to-end features** – from API design and data models to polished UIs and production monitoring.
*   Build scalable backend APIs in **Python/FastAPI**
*   Ship high-quality frontends using **Next.js (React, TypeScript) + TanStack (Query/Router)**.
*   Design and evolve agent workflows in LangChain/LangGraph that are robust, observable, and debuggable (not just prompt hacks)
*   Work with both structured and unstructured procurement data (RFQs, emails, quotes, contracts)
*   Build data & system integrations (ERP, email, supplier systems)
*   Help shape our architecture, reliability, and engineering best practices as we grow
*   Collaborate directly with founders, PM, and customers to understand real procurement workflows and turn them into product.

Requirements
------------

*   Strong **Python** skills - clean, maintainable, well-tested code matters
*   Professional experience with React/Next.js and TypeScript
*   Experience in **data engineering** and backend architecture
*   Comfortable building and scaling **RESTful APIs**
*   Curiosity about how LLMs can be used in real-world settings
*   **A builder mindset** - you like to ship, measure, and iterate rather than bikeshed
*   Comfort with **ambiguity and ownership**: you spot problems, propose solutions, and drive them to completion without waiting for perfect specs
*   **4+ years** experience in relevant (Start-Up) roles

Benefits
--------

*   Build AI agents that actually _do_ things
*   Massive autonomy, zero bureaucracy
*   Immediate impact, your work ships fast to real customers
*   Modern stack, no legacy
*   Competitive salary + meaningful equity
*   High-end equipment

**Tech Stack You’ll Work With**

*   **Python** (FastAPI, LangChain, LangGraph, LangSmith)
*   **Next.js**, **React**, **TypeScript**, **TanStack Query/Router**
*   **AWS** (Bedrock, ECS Fargate, Lambda, SQS/SNS)
*   GitHub CI/CD , Sentry & Grafana & Prometheus
*   **Postgres,** **Redis**
*   Large Language Models (LLMs) and evaluation tooling
