### Description\nAt SumUp, we empower small businesses globally by providing simple, affordable tools for payments, finance, and customer relationships. We are a people-positive, agile, and innovative organization, guided by values like Founder's Mentality, Team First, and We Care, fostering human connection, autonomy, and continuous learning.\n\nThe Global Operations team at SumUp strives to build the best customer experience in fintech. As a Senior Analytics Engineer, you will be critical in creating a robust, high-quality data foundation. This foundation drives efficiency, powers AI-driven automation, and enables data-driven decisions for our merchants and support agents. You will be the expert responsible for building and maintaining our data engine using dbt and Snowflake, ensuring trusted and accessible data for analysts, product managers, and AI systems.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Serve as the dedicated data owner for the Operations Tribe, focusing on transforming raw data into reliable, scalable data products.\n- Own, develop, and maintain the Operations data structure (DAOPS) using expert-level SQL and dbt within Snowflake, managing over 200 data models and ensuring clear business logic.\n- Manage and optimize data pipeline orchestration (e.g., Airflow), including critical third-party ingestions (Trustpilot, NICE) and implementing resource/cost optimizations.\n- Drive self-service analytics by establishing best practices for data governance, quality, and accessibility, and creating comprehensive documentation and data marts for stakeholders.\n- Implement robust data governance, with a strong focus on PII management, access control, and quality checks to ensure report reliability.\n- Provide a perfectly structured data foundation for new AI tools aimed at automating tasks and enhancing efficiency in Support.\n- Architect solutions to upskill stakeholders and enable broad self-service, shifting from centralized requests to decentralized, high-quality data access.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 3-5+ years of proven experience in Analytics Engineering or Data Engineering roles.\n- Expert-level SQL skills for complex data transformations and optimization.\n- Strong hands-on experience with dbt for building production-grade data models.\n- Solid experience with Snowflake (or a comparable data warehouse) and pipeline orchestration tools like Airflow.\n- A track record of successfully working on integrations involving APIs, various data sources, and robust ETL/ELT processes.\n- Excellent documentation skills and a genuine passion for enabling self-service analytics.\n- A true team player attitude, thriving on cross-functional collaboration within a fast-paced environment.\n- Demonstrated self-drive, proactiveness, high adaptability, and a strong commitment to data governance, quality, and ownership.\n- A passion for AI Technology, with excitement to power SumUp's next generation of automated support tools.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis Senior Analytics Engineer role at SumUp offers a unique opportunity to directly impact millions of small businesses by building the foundational data infrastructure for AI-driven automation and critical business decisions. It's ideal for a highly skilled individual contributor who thrives in a complex, fast-paced environment and is passionate about data integrity, self-service enablement, and cutting-edge AI technology. SumUp cultivates a supportive, inclusive, and growth-oriented culture with a strong emphasis on continuous learning, offering excellent benefits like a dedicated L&D budget, generous time off, virtual stock options, and a 1-month sabbatical.
