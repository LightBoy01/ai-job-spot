Deloitte’s AI & Data team is transforming how businesses leverage cloud-native technologies to solve complex challenges in data, machine learning, and AI. As a Director, you will lead strategic initiatives, drive innovation, and deliver impactful solutions for our clients. 

As a senior member of the team, you will work with C-suite clients to advise on customer strategy, lead the development of AI-powered decisioning platforms, and oversee transformation initiatives that deliver measurable business outcomes. You will also play a critical role in **business development**, **practice leadership**, and **building high-performing teams**. 

**What will your typical day look like?** 

 **Client Advisory and Solution Design**

*   Advise CxO-level stakeholders on customer strategy, personalisation, marketing technology, and AI adoption.
*   Shape AI-enabled decisioning roadmaps across customer acquisition, engagement, retention, and service.
*   Design integrated solutions that combine strategy, data, technology, and operations.

**Sales and Business Development**

*   Identify and pursue new business opportunities across key industries (e.g., financial services, retail, insurance).
*   Lead end-to-end sales cycles, from opportunity identification through to commercial negotiation and close.
*   Build and maintain strong client relationships, acting as a trusted advisor.

**Delivery and Execution**

*   Oversee the successful delivery of customer decisioning projects, ensuring on-time, high-quality outcomes.
*   Lead cross-functional teams across data science, marketing technology, and change management.
*   Support clients in transitioning to operational ownership and continuous optimisation of decisioning platforms.

**Practice and Capability Leadership**

*   *   Build and scale a high-performing AI decisioning team with diverse capabilities.
    *   Contribute to thought leadership and innovation in customer AI, personalisation, and decisioning.
    *   Mentor and grow emerging leaders and specialists in the practice.

**About the team** 

Our AI & Data team comprises over 500 specialists across Australia, partnering with clients in both public and private sectors to deliver cutting-edge solutions. We collaborate with leading technology providers such as AWS, Microsoft, Google, Snowflake, and Databricks, ensuring we stay at the forefront of emerging AI and data capabilities.

**Enough about us, let’s talk about you.** 

We are looking for a Director with the following capabilities: 

*   12+ years’ experience in consulting, technology, or industry roles related to implementing and leveraging value from Data & AI technologies. 
    

*   Proven track record in selling and delivering large-scale, Data & AI programs 
    

*   Deep understanding of cloud and AI technologies and platforms (e.g. AWS, Azure, Databricks, Snowflake, Anthropic, etc) 
    

*   Strong business acumen with the ability to connect technical capabilities to commercial outcomes. 
    

*   Proven leadership of multi-disciplinary teams in a consulting or matrixed environment. 
    

*   Exceptional stakeholder management and executive communication skills. 
    

*   Passion for innovation, emerging technology, and creating measurable value for clients.
