The Security Operations team’s mission is to protect Robinhood and its customers by rapidly anticipating, detecting, and responding to security threats, while continuously assuring and strengthening our defences to minimize the impact of risks and ensure business continuity.
As a Vulnerability Management intern, you will be at the forefront of safeguarding our organization by identifying and mitigating critical vulnerabilities before they can be exploited. You'll play a hands-on role in our dynamic cybersecurity ecosystem, tackling complex challenges and working with the latest tools and technologies to stay one step ahead of potential threats.
**This role is based in our** **New York, Menlo Park, Bellevue, or Toronto** **office(s), with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.**
At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.
**What you’ll do**
------------------
*   Operate a wide range of industry-leading vulnerability scanners, their inputs and outputs, and their configurations.
*   Improve agentic AI systems to further scale vulnerability triage, context gathering, and remediation workflows.
*   Collaborate closely with service owners to remediate vulnerabilities that pose a risk to the company or its customers.
**What you bring**
------------------
*   Currently enrolled in a full-time, degree-seeking program with an expected graduation date in Winter 2026/Spring 2027.
*   Security research skills. A solid understanding of security concepts, how vulnerabilities are exploited, and how they are mitigated.
*   Strong technical foundation in coding (Python, Go preferred), as well as scripting and automation.
*   Solid familiarity with modern cloud environments and their unique security challenges, particularly AWS.
*   Hands-on experience with generative AI models, particularly agentic AI approaches.
