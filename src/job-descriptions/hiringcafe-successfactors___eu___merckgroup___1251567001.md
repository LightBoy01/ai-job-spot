**Your role:**

  
As a Senior System Engineer - Windows, you will play a pivotal role in supporting our global Windows Server environments, spanning from versions 2012 to 2025, including Hyper-V and VMware. Your expertise will be crucial in providing top-tier support for complex issues, ensuring that our systems maintain optimal performance and minimal downtime. You will conduct thorough problem management and root cause analysis to address recurring incidents effectively. In this position, you will scope project demands that arise from IT improvements or business needs, contributing to the overall enhancement of our infrastructure.

You will also create and maintain comprehensive documentation for processes, wikis, and knowledge articles to facilitate knowledge sharing. Implementing changes in accordance with global change management processes will be a key responsibility, ensuring compliance with Global Qualification standards. As part of your duties, you will offer on-call support, managing both emergencies and routine maintenance on a rotational basis. Your participation in audit readiness and remediation will represent our IT topics effectively. You will execute infrastructure projects that focus on security hardening and lifecycle management, while also managing global patch and vulnerability for Windows Servers and VMware infrastructure. This role offers an exciting opportunity to utilize AI technologies for predictive maintenance and advanced troubleshooting capabilities.

**Who you are:**

*   You have extensive proven experience in Windows Server environments, particularly in networking, storage, and security.
*   You are passionate about enhancing IT services through automation and AI technologies, with knowledge of Azure Arc and cloud technology.
*   You possess a strong understanding of hardware, including servers, blade servers, and related infrastructure.
*   You are committed to delivering reliable, secure, and efficient IT infrastructure services.
*   You focus on safeguarding against cyber threats and ensuring compliance with industry standards.
*   You have experience in lifecycle management and are adept at minimizing costs.
*   You are driven to enhance operational excellence through improved ITSM/ITIL processes and data quality.
*   You collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to align IT strategies with business objectives.
