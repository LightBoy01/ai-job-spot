### Description
Wolt is a fast-growing technology company on a mission to bring joy, simplicity, and earnings to neighborhoods worldwide by connecting people with local businesses. Having started with restaurant food delivery, Wolt is now expanding to deliver \"almost everything\" across over 500 cities in 30 countries. Following its merger with DoorDash in 2022, Wolt continues its ambitious global expansion.

Working at Wolt is described as exciting and challenging, offering significant opportunities for learning, building, and shipping, ideal for self-starters with an entrepreneurial spirit. The company emphasizes customer experience, local relevance, and a culture that empowers ownership and impact.

This role, **Regional Restaurant Marketing Manager**, is a unique hybrid opportunity to lead Wolt's restaurant marketing strategy across **South** and **Central** Germany, focusing on key cities like Munich, Frankfurt, and Stuttgart. It combines strategic marketing with a hands-on, partner-facing approach to drive visibility, growth, and customer engagement for key SMB Restaurant Partners on the Wolt platform. The position is based in the Munich or Frankfurt office, requiring regular travel within the region and monthly visits to Berlin HQ.

### Responsibilities
-   **Local Market Strategy & Partner Activation**: Develop and execute tailored restaurant marketing strategies for cities in South and Central Germany, leveraging local market expertise. Identify and capitalize on hyper-local marketing opportunities (e.g., city festivals, seasonal events). Build and nurture relationships with top-tier restaurant partners to co-create marketing activations.
-   **Hands-On Campaign Execution**: Provide on-the-ground support to activate restaurant partners, from campaign planning to execution, to boost customer demand and visibility. Execute 360 marketing plans, encompassing advertising across paid, owned, and earned channels, promotions, and co-branded initiatives. Collaborate with the local Berlin-based Marketing team and agencies to adapt national strategies into localized initiatives. Manage regional/partner marketing budgets efficiently, ensuring adherence to financial targets.
-   **Partner Relationship Management**: Serve as a trusted marketing advisor to key restaurant partners, guiding them in effectively utilizing Wolt's marketing tools. Work closely with account management and local sales teams to align on priorities, performance goals, and joint marketing opportunities.
-   **Performance Reporting, Data Analysis & Insights**: Track, analyze, optimize, and report on marketing performance metrics at both regional and partner levels. Utilize data-based insights to refine marketing strategies and enhance return on investment (ROI). Provide field feedback to continuously improve and shape local marketing strategies.

### Qualifications
-   Bachelor's or Master's degree in Marketing with 6-8 years of marketing experience, demonstrating a proven track record in campaign execution and partner collaboration.
-   Strong connections to the gastronomy scene in South and Central Germany are a significant advantage, gained through previous roles in food-tech, e-commerce/marketplace, hospitality, FMCG, or lifestyle/urban marketing.
-   Possesses strategic thinking, a results-oriented mindset, and a hands-on attitude, comfortable transitioning from strategy to execution (e.g., coordinating photo shoots, setting up pop-up activations, or meeting partners face-to-face).
-   Bonus: Previous experience in sales or account management is highly beneficial.
-   Native German speaker and business fluent in English.
-   Awareness of compliance regulations and adaptability within a dynamic marketing landscape.
-   Willingness to travel regularly within South and Central Germany and to Berlin HQ on a monthly basis.
