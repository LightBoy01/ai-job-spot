**Location 📍:** Sydney, AU, Hybrid - 3 days a week in office

**About Us** 🚀

At Relevance AI, our mission is to empower anyone to delegate work to the AI workforce.

We’re building a new type of automation platform, for anyone to create and use AI agents that can replicate human quality work, decision making and collaboration.

We are scaling fast to meet unprecedented customer demand and we need exceptional talent to help us grow. If you’re ready to be part of a trailblazing company at the cutting edge of AI, this is the place for you.

**The Role 🥳**

We’re hiring for a Staff Software Engineer to join our mission-critical Enterprise team in Sydney. You will have high visibility and high autonomy to take ownership of complex projects for our large-scale customers, mentor other engineers, and make a meaningful impact on a category-defining product.

At Relevance AI, your work will reach customers within hours, offering a rare opportunity to see the impact of your contributions in real-time. You’ll have autonomy and trust to lead key projects and mentor team members without being weighed down by unnecessary layers of approval.

You’ll work across our modern modern tech stack, which we’re constantly optimising to move and iterate faster. Our supportive, fast-paced culture fosters collaboration, curiosity, and creativity, making it an environment where innovation thrives and where your ideas and expertise will be highly valued.

**Your Impact 💥**

*   Architect, build, and deliver Enterprise-grade features across the entire stack from elegant, performant frontends to scalable, reliable backend systems and cloud infrastructure.
    
*   Take ownership of complex, mission-critical projects, delivering impactful features within your first month and guiding them from ideation to production.
    
*   Push the boundaries of AI capabilities, designing and implementing cutting-edge solutions for our Enterprise customers.
    
*   Play a pivotal role in shaping our engineering culture, processes, and how we work as we scale.
    
*   Mentor and coach engineers of all levels, fostering a collaborative, high-performance environment and helping others grow their technical and professional skills.
    
*   Set and uphold high standards for code quality, performance, and scalability, and ensure these standards are adopted across the team.
    
*   Collaborate closely with product, design, and leadership to align technical direction with business goals and drive the company’s success.
    

**Our Tech Stack (so far) 💻**

We leverage a modern, scalable, and developer-friendly technology stack to build robust web applications:

*   Frontend: Vue.js, Nuxt, Typescript
    
*   Backend: Express.js, Node.js, Typescript
    
*   Database: MongoDB, PostgreSQL
    
*   Cloud & Deployment: Vercel, AWS
    

**What We’re Looking For** 🧠

*   6+ years of hands-on software engineering experience, ideally within fast-paced, high-growth tech environments and building products that service large Enterprise customers.
    
*   Strong expertise in our tech stack or a proven ability to quickly master new technologies.
    
*   Thrive in dynamic, high-performance start up settings and comfortable with ambiguity, rapid iteration, and taking initiative.
    
*   Demonstrated customer-centric thinking, ability to take full ownership of projects, solve complex problems, and consistently deliver high-quality, impactful results.
    
*   Deep interest in artificial intelligence and excitement to build innovative, category-defining products.
    
*   Regularly leverage AI tools and technologies in your workflow, and stay up-to-date with the latest advancements to boost productivity and innovation.
    

**Bonus Points if You Have Any Of The Following ⭐**

*   Experience designing and building scalable and fault-tolerant systems for real-world users in enterprise environments.
    
*   Strong understanding of large scale per-customer deployments (including on-premise and cloud).
    
*   Knowledge of FGA/ RBAC access controls, AWS infrastructure, SSO/ OAuth systems, and platform analytics.
