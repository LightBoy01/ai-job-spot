### Description\nThe client is a leading cybersecurity company exclusively specialized in critical infrastructures. They operate one of Germany's first OT-SOCs, now with a team of 25 employees. Based on over a decade of operational experience and a strong focus on proprietary developments, the company monitors and protects over 120 highly critical infrastructures, such as energy producers and other KRITIS operators, with its innovative OT attack detection system. You will guide an experienced DevOps team, contribute to strategic product development, and work in a flexible, 100% remote-friendly environment with excellent work-life balance benefits, including 30 days of vacation.
