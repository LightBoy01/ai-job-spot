### Job Title

AI First Full Stack Tech Lead

### Job Description

**AI First Full Stack Tech Lead.**

**About the Role**

We are seeking a **highly skilled AI Tech Lead / Senior Developer** to drive the design, development, and integration of agentic AI solutions within Philips’ CRM landscape (Salesforce CRM, Microsoft Dynamics CRM, Conga CPQ, CLM). The role requires a strong foundation in AI/ML, LLMs, and agentic workflows, coupled with solid experience in enterprise-grade application development.

The ideal candidate will combine deep technical expertise (AI/ML, Python, Java, JavaScript, MCP, microservices, React.js) with domain knowledge in CRM and CPQ/CLM to design innovative solutions that improve automation, contract lifecycle management, quoting processes, and customer experience.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Lead the design and development of agentic AI systems capable of executing autonomous actions across CRM platforms.
*   Build and integrate AI/ML and LLM-based models into enterprise applications, leveraging frameworks such as OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, and LangChain.
*   Architect and implement MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers and tools to support AI-driven workflows.
*   Design AI-driven development and testing frameworks to accelerate innovation.
*   Collaborate with CRM teams (Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics) to align AI solutions with existing CPQ/CLM processes (contract lifecycle, framework contract management, quoting).
*   Implement microservices and APIs for seamless integration with enterprise applications.
*   Drive MLOps practices for model deployment, monitoring, and lifecycle management.
*   Ensure solutions adhere to enterprise-grade security, authentication, and data validation standards.
*   Mentor developers and contribute to building a strong AI capability foundation within Philips.

**Required Skills & Qualifications**

*   6 to 10+ years of experience in enterprise application development.
*   Strong programming expertise in:
    *   Python, Java, JavaScript (React.js)
    *   Spring Boot, Microservices, API Integration, CI/CD pipelines
    *   PostgreSQL or other relational databases
*   Proven hands-on experience in delivering AI-powered solutions using:
    *   LLMs (OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, LangChain)
    *   Vector databases, embeddings, semantic search/recommendation systems
    *   Designing agentic workflows and AI-driven automation
    *   MCP server and tool development
*   Experience in AI-driven development and testing.
*   Strong knowledge of MLOps practices for model deployment and monitoring.
*   Familiarity with CRM domains (Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics) and CPQ/CLM processes.
*   Experience integrating with third-party tools like Conga CPQ, Contract Lifecycle Management systems, and quoting frameworks.

**Desirable Skills**

*   Experience with CPQ/CLM domain implementation projects.
*   Knowledge of contract lifecycle management frameworks and enterprise quoting systems.
*   Exposure to hyperautomation tools (AI Builder, Power Automate, Flow Builder).
*   Prior experience in designing secure and scalable agentic AI architectures.

**Why Join Us**

*   Be part of Philips’ CRM & AI innovation journey to transform sales, contract, and customer engagement processes.
*   Work on cutting-edge AI/LLM and agentic workflow solutions with direct business impact.
*   Collaborate with a talented global team driving next-gen automation in healthcare and CRM domains.
