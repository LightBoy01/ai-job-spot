**The Opportunity at Altana**

We are seeking a Strategic Program Director to lead the delivery and adoption of a large-scale software solution within a federal agency. This role goes beyond traditional program management: you’ll act as the strategic owner for driving mission impact, ensuring technical delivery, and building lasting client partnerships.

The right candidate thrives at the intersection of federal mission needs and cutting-edge software, can earn trust with senior government stakeholders, and knows how to mobilize cross-functional teams to deliver results at scale.

**Your Responsibilities**

*   Coordinate the execution of program initiatives and tasks to meet the program's administrative, technical, and operational requirements
*   Lead the full lifecycle of software implementation, from requirements intake to deployment and adoption.
*   Define and drive program goals, KPIs, and success criteria tied to agency mission outcomes.
*   Anticipate risks, resolve issues, and ensure the program delivers measurable value.
*   Partner directly with federal customers to translate strategic objectives into technical solutions.
*   Develop a deep understanding of the agency’s mission and use that insight to guide solution roadmaps.
*   Influence client decision-makers and advocate for product adoption/expansion.
*   Orchestrate delivery across engineering, product, data science, and security stakeholders.
*   Support contract actions, proposals, and other program sustainment and growth efforts as needed.
*   Build enduring relationships that drive renewals, expansions, and follow-on work.
*   Deliver clear, compelling updates to both technical and non-technical audiences. 

**About You**

*   Prior experience at a high-growth software company
*   5+ years of experience leading enterprise software programs in the federal sector.
*   Track record of delivering mission-critical solutions with measurable impact.
*   Strong understanding of federal procurement and delivery frameworks (OTA, BAA, SBIR, etc.).
*   Ability to translate between technical teams and executive stakeholders.
*   Familiarity with delivering data platforms, AI/ML solutions, or SaaS at federal scale.
*   Background working with DHS, DoD, or large civilian agencies.
*   Ability to maintain a government security clearance.  Active Secret or TS/SCI clearance, a plus.

This role is based in Washington, DC, USA with hybrid work flexibility and candidates must be within commuting distance from our office.
