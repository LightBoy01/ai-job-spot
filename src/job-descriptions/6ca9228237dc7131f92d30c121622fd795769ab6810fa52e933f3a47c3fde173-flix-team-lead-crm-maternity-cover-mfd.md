At Flix, we offer a dynamic work environment with competitive pay, strong growth opportunities, and a tech-driven approach to making travel more accessible, sustainable, and affordable.  

AS a **Team Lead CRM (maternity cover) (m/f/d),** you will join our global CRM team at Flix on **1-year contract**. This is a critical role to shape the future of CRM as we expand our capabilities, redefine our toolset, and constantly optimize for maximum revenue, profit, growth, scalability, and efficiency.

**About the Role**

*   Lead a team of 3 CRM Tech Managers and 2 CRM Data Analysts, ensuring their professional and personal development
*   Enable your team to deliver results with clear business outcomes by planning quarterly OKRs and aligning with key stakeholders across departments and functions
*   Define and drive the global vision and strategy for your teams, ensuring alignment with business and department goals
*   Oversee the design, implementation, and optimization of CRM communication in Braze across email and app channels to improve customer journeys
*   Ensure data-driven decision making across the entire CRM team by identifying and prioritizing analytics needs
*   Be proficient in the usage of Braze for multichannel, lifecycle-based communication and support the team to ensure effective usage
*   Stay on top of emerging CRM technologies, automation best practices, AI possibilities and innovations to maintain a competitive edge

**About You**

*   5+ years as Team Lead, Product Owner or Senior Manager with CRM/marketing products
*   Proven expertise in CRM automations (strategy + operations), including channel orchestration, lifecycle journeys, and personalization
*   Strong experience with Braze is a must
*   Strong data analytics skills, with the ability to interpret performance data, define KPIs, and optimize CRM performance
*   Passion for customer-centricity and delivering high-quality products with well-defined SLAs and monitoring
*   Excellent communication, presentation, and leadership skills. Able to motivate teams and collaborate effectively across departments and functions
*   Fluent in English

We recognize that everyone carries a unique set of valuable skills and experiences. If you think you could have an impact even though you don't meet 100% of the requirements, we still encourage you to apply. We want to hear from you!
