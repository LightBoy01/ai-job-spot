### Description
Are you an enrolled student (m/f/d) in Electrical Engineering or Building Services Engineering, looking for a student part-time job of 15-20 hours per week in Munich? This position at Buro Happold might be exactly what you're looking for!

Shaping the future of entire cities, designing infrastructures and buildings to combat climate change – a career at Buro Happold means making a significant impact. With a globally renowned reputation, our employees and our commitment ensure we make a positive contribution to our clients, the environment, and entire communities. If you want to make a difference with us – this is your chance!

### Responsibilities
*   Collaborate in the interdisciplinary provision of planning services (according to HOAI) for electrical or mechanical trades (heating, cooling, ventilation, and plumbing).
*   Support our planning team in direct project work (preparation of calculations and technical documents, as well as BIM-based coordination between various trades).
*   Assist the team with construction supervision.

### Qualifications
*   Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Building Services Engineering, Building Energy Systems, Environmental Engineering, or a comparable field.
*   Initial knowledge of German standards and, ideally, planning experience in heating, cooling, ventilation, plumbing, electrical engineering, and measurement & control (M&R).
*   Strong communication skills in German; English language skills are an advantage.
*   Knowledge of CAD programs and construction site experience are an advantage.

This is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for a student passionate about sustainable engineering and making a tangible impact on the built environment.
