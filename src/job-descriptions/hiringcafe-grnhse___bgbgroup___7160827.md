**BGB Group** 

**Senior Associate Consultant, Healthcare Strategy** 

**Our Agency** 

BGB is a healthcare communications agency that offers a wide range of services, including traditional pharmaceutical advertising, promotional medical education, payer marketing, and consulting services. Known for excellence and professionalism, we’re hired as strategic and creative partners by our biopharmaceutical clients to drive category/brand awareness and growth. 

**Who We Are** 

At **BGB Group**, we don’t just analyse the healthcare landscape—we shape it. 

Our **Innovation x Intelligence (IxI) division** is a small, fast-moving team that works like a start-up—agile, curious, and ambitious—but with the **backing of two industry powerhouses: BGB Group and Kx Advisors**. That means you get the best of both worlds: the freedom to explore bold thinking, and the creative and strategic firepower to turn it into action. 

We bring together **strategists, consultants, behavioural scientists, innovation experts, and tech specialists** to tackle the big, thorny questions in healthcare—helping biopharma clients unlock smarter, braver strategies that improve the lives of people living with disease. 

We go deep—using bespoke AI-powered tools to uncover the unknowns that shift thinking, reframe decisions, and open up new possibilities. We operate like the start-up we are—pacey, responsive, and collaborative—so we're looking for someone who enjoys variety, thrives under pressure, and takes initiative without being prompted.

We’re a small team, and we’re all very different—but what unites us is this weird, shared joy in cracking hard problems that actually help people and doing so in unconventional ways.

Who This Role is For 

This role is for the question-askers. The culturally attuned. The ones who care. 

💭 You thrive in fast-moving environments, are energized by face-to-face time with clients, and aren't afraid to dive straight into complex problems—whether that's leading a presentation, moderating a workshop, or jumping in to move a project forward. 

🌍 You’re plugged into the world—curious about how brands behave, why people make the choices they do, and how culture influences commerce. You see **strategy and creativity as allies,** not opposites. You're comfortable with ambiguity, fueled by insight, and energized by finding a smarter way through.

🔍 You were _that_ kid in class—the one with another question, the one who got excited to be wrong because it meant learning something new. 

❤️ And, most importantly, you want to use your skills for something that matters. You’re energised by the idea of making life better for people living with disease—and helping healthcare brands live up to their potential. 

**The Role** 

As a **Senior** **Associate Consultant**, you’ll help the world’s top biopharma companies solve some of their biggest brand and business challenges. From redefining brand strategy to exploring patient behaviour, from future-scaping therapeutic categories to shaping commercial narratives—you’ll work on a wide spectrum of projects with real-world impact. 

And you won’t be doing it alone. You’ll join a collaborative, idea-rich team who’ll challenge your thinking, support your growth, and probably make you laugh along the way. 

This is a **remote role, based in the UK**, with the option to connect in person at either our **Manchester** or **London hubs**. 

What You’ll Do 

*   **Get Stuck into Research** – Conduct interviews, moderate focus groups, analyze reports, and connect dots across data sources.
*   **Own and Drive Projects** – Take responsibility for workstreams, managing timelines and deliverables to keep things moving at speed.
*   **Be Client-Confident** – Present insights and recommendations persuasively, lead discussions, and build strong relationships through regular client interaction.
*   **Go Beyond the Obvious** – Use custom-built AI tools to uncover hidden patterns, untapped insights, and strategic whitespace, building smart strategic recommendations that clients can actually use.
*   **Work across Disciplines** – Collaborate with behavioral scientists, creatives, technologists, and strategic leads across BGB Group and Kx Advisors.
*   **Step In and Step Up** – Anticipate needs, take work off other team members' plates where necessary, and keep projects on track without waiting for instruction.
*   **Be Part of the Culture** – Learn, share, experiment, support, question, and grow.

What You Bring 

*    A **good grade** in a relevant degree—life sciences, psychology, business, or a related field.
*   **2-5 years' experience** in a relevant space—this could mean healthcare consulting, research, pharma or biotech, medical comms, or behavioral science. We need individuals who can hit the ground running, and who are comfortable being present with clients
*   A **strategic and curious mindset** – You don’t stop at the first answer. You dig, question, and connect the dots.
*   A **self-starting attitude** – You take initiative, take ownership, stay organized, and can move work forward independently. 
*   **Strong collaboration skills** – You enjoy bouncing ideas, building on others’ thinking, and working as part of a team.
*   **Proven ability** to confidently engage with clients, whether in presentations, workshops, or one-on-one discussions.
*   **Comfortable working at pace**–able to quickly grasp new concepts, make decisions, and adapt to shifting priorities. 

Why Join Us? 

**Small team energy, big company support** – Backed by BGB and Kx, with access to world-class creative, insight, and innovation capabilities 

**High-impact work, early responsibility** – You’ll contribute from day one on projects that matter 

**A learning-first culture** – Mentorship, peer support, and space to grow 

**A genuinely fun, curious environment –** We take the work seriously, not ourselves 

**Creative meets strategic** – This is a space where brand thinking, behavioural science, and big data collide 

**A future-facing mission** – Everything we do aims to improve the lives of people living with disease 

**If you're ready to bring curiosity, strategy, and creativity together to reimagine healthcare—and make a real difference to people’s lives—let’s talk.**.
