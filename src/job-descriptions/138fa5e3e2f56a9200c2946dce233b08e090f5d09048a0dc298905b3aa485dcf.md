**We're Hiring: AI/ML Lead** 📍 _Location_: Remote or Hybrid (flexible) 💼 _Type_: Full-time
Are you a trailblazer in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning? Join us as an **AI/ML Lead** to architect cutting-edge Generative AI and ML solutions with strategic impact. We're seeking a seasoned professional with deep technical expertise, leadership skills, and a passion for innovation.
🚀 **Key Responsibilities**
**🛠️ Technical Leadership & Development**
*   Design and implement ML models: supervised, unsupervised, deep learning, and reinforcement learning.
*   Build GenAI solutions using RAG, Vector Databases, LangChain, Hugging Face, and Agentic AI frameworks.
*   Leverage frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, and scikit-learn for model development.
*   Apply advanced NLP techniques: text classification, RNNs, CNNs, and Transformer-based models (e.g., BERT).
*   Deliver robust end-to-end solutions: data preprocessing, feature engineering, model deployment, and monitoring.
**💻 Technical Proficiency**
*   Strong programming skills in Python.
*   Experience with cloud platforms, especially AWS (Sagemaker, Lambda, DynamoDB, S3, and related services).
**🤝 Team Mentorship & Coaching**
*   Lead and mentor data scientists and ML engineers.
*   Set technical direction aligned with business strategy.
*   Promote knowledge sharing, collaboration, and continuous learning.
**📊 Strategic Advisory & Collaboration**
*   Collaborate cross-functionally to embed AI/ML into business products and processes.
*   Offer strategic insights to stakeholders at all levels.
*   Communicate complex technical ideas clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.
🎓 **Qualifications**
*   Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field (or equivalent experience).
*   5-8 years of relevant experience in AI/ML/Generative AI.
*   12+ years of total work experience, with proven project and team leadership.
*   Expertise in GenAI technologies: RAG, Vector DBs, LangChain, Hugging Face, and Agentic AI.
*   Proficiency in core ML techniques and NLP with Transformer-based architectures.
*   Strong hands-on experience with Python and AWS services.
**⭐ Preferred**
*   Experience defining technical strategy for AI/ML projects.
*   Background in the insurance domain.
*   Demonstrated ability to mentor junior team members.
*   Proven success in managing AI/ML initiatives end-to-end.
