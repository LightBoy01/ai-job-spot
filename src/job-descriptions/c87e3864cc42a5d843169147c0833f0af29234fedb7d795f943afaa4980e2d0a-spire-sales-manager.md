As a Sales Manager, you are accountable for the success of your business within a region or vertical, owning the performance of the Spire space-based data portfolio. You are expected to easily gain a strong understanding of the external business environment. Your key focus is building partnerships with prospects, driving them through the sales process until they become existing customers. Overall, there is a strong expectation that you will periodically raise the bar on the customer experience and advocate on their behalf to internal functions.

The successful candidate will need to show off a clear professional growth throughout their career, backed up by a desire to continue to develop. You are ready to own customer activities across dimensions, from operations, to tactics, to strategy. The successful candidate has a perfect command of English, synthesizes complex topics in just a few sentences, and is able to see the long-term value to be created for the company. You are at ease collaborating with internal cross-functional teams, organizing them and giving direction. You will be collaborating in a very lean and agile team with opportunities to drive initiatives to generate new business pipeline and improve customer experience.

Daily Responsibilities:

*   Identify outbound prospects and their business needs and engage them in 1:1 dialogue.
*   Drive outbound New Business pipeline generation, meeting sales targets through rigorous prospection and opportunity qualification.
*   Manage the end-to-end customer experience across the entire Spire Sales Process.
*   Ensure that internal processes and products get updated based on customer and industry insights that you periodically gather.
*   You conduct financial analysis of business opportunities to meet and exceed revenue and profitability targets by creating a pipeline of accounts that you drive from prospecting to deal closure over an average of 90 days towards 6 digits sales targets. You take ownership and accountability of your quota targets and provide data-centric forecasting of deal outcomes.
*   You have a thorough understanding of the industry, business trends, events, and continually monitor both industry and competitor developments.
*   You understand customer needs and use that knowledge to advocate for, and drive delivery of, improvements that provide Spire customers with an unparalleled experience.
*   You build your own industry network – other companies look up to you to gather industry support.
*   You coordinate across cross-functional teams and communicate with internal and external stakeholders, all while meeting tight deadlines.
*   Have a complete understanding (inclusive of the strategic context) of internal and external variables that impact your business. This includes owning forecasting, analyzing data, understanding, and reporting on the business.
*   You will closely collaborate with SDRs and the Marketing Team to develop, execute, and provide feedback on marketing campaigns to drive new business.
*   Responsible for keeping accurate customer and pipeline data on CRM for reporting and forecasting.

Basic Qualifications:

*   Experience driving internal cross-team collaboration.
*   Experience using data to influence business decisions.
*   Experience with deal-level business analysis.
*   Demonstrated success and growth in previous roles.
*   Be self-starting and intellectually curious.
*   Track record of career growth.
*   Career growth expectations and growth mindset.
*   Prior experience in SaaS/Data sales.

Preferred Qualifications:

*   6+ years of sales, account management in an international environment.
*   Degree in business, commercial or sales, from a top tier university.
*   Experience driving strategic and complex initiatives through cross-functional teams.
*   Track record of interviewing sales professionals into the team that you're a part of.
*   Track record of buddying, coaching or mentoring other senior sales professionals

Spire operates a hybrid work model, and this position will require you to work a minimum of three days per week in the office.

Access to US export-controlled software and/or technology may be required for this role. If needed, Spire will arrange the necessary licenses—this is not something candidates need to have before applying. #LI-RK1

**About Spire**

_We_ _improve life on Earth with data from space._

Spire Global is a space-to-cloud analytics company that owns and operates the largest multi-purpose constellation of satellites. Its proprietary data and algorithms provide the most advanced maritime, aviation, and weather tracking in the world. In addition to its constellation, Spire's data infrastructure includes a global ground station network and 24/7 operations that provide real-time global coverage of every point on Earth.
