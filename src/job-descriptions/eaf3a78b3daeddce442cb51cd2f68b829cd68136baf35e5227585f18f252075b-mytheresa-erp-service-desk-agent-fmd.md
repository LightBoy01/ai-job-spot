To support our ERP Services team we are searching for a ERP Service Desk Agent (f/m/d) to be based in our Munich office.

Tasks
-----

**WHAT YOU WILL DO**

*   Providing 1st level support for ERP topics
*   Serve as the escalation point of contact for in-house users
*   Close collaboration with ERP end users, ERP specialists and other IT teams
*   Understanding reported issues, triaging new incidents and prioritizing created tickets
*   Focusing on user needs and finding proper solutions
*   Solving reported issues, independently and in close collaboration with 2nd level support

Requirements
------------

**WHO YOU ARE**

*   Degree in Business Informatics, Economics or other comparable qualification with atleast 2 years of experience in working with Service Desk and providing user support
*   Strong Experience in working with MS Dynamics NAV/Business Central
*   Basic understanding of business processes (finance, sales, purchase, inventory, warehouse management)
*   Ability to understand user requirements, prioritize and manage multiple tickets
*   Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English, German is a plus
*   Experience in working with agile environment, SCRUM, JIRA, Confluence, MS Office
