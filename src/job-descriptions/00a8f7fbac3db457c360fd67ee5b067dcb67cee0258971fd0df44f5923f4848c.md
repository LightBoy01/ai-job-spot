**Your future team**
--------------------

At Deloitte Luxembourg, we are looking for a **versatile and experienced IT professional** to join our Audit Digital Factory. This role is unique: it combines the **technical expertise of a Software Developer** with the **strategic mindset of a Project Manager & Product Owner**.

You will not only **design and develop cutting-edge digital solutions** (leveraging AI, automation, and cloud technologies), but also **lead projects from conception to delivery**, ensuring alignment with business needs and tangible impact for the Audit department.

This is an exciting opportunity for someone who thrives in both **hands-on coding** and **project leadership**, eager to shape the future of digital transformation within Deloitte.

Your advantages of being one of us
----------------------------------

*   **Career Growth, Your Way**  
    Access top-notch training and career development, with a clear progression path to help you level up at your own pace.
*   **Global Impact, Local Connections**  
    Work on international projects and collaborate with diverse teams, all while making an impact locally.
*   **Inclusive, Flexible Culture**  
    Embrace a culture where your voice matters, with flexible hours to balance work and life seamlessly.
*   **Build Your Network**  
    Gain access to an exclusive alumni network and attend industry events to connect and grow your professional circle

**Your contribution to our success**
------------------------------------

*   1.  **Lead and Deliver Projects**: Act as both a key developer and the project driver, managing requirements, planning, and delivery.
    2.  **Develop and Innovate**: Build scalable applications using Angular, .NET Core, C#, SQL Server, SSIS, and Python.
    3.  **AI & Automation**: Integrate AI and machine learning models to enhance processes, including NLP, predictive analytics, and intelligent automation.
    4.  **Bridge Business & Tech**: Act as the interface between stakeholders and the development team, transforming business needs into technical solutions.
    5.  **Ensure Agile Delivery**: Organize sprints, oversee progress, and actively participate in code reviews and daily stand-ups.

**Your skills**
---------------

*   1.  **Solid full-stack development experience**, ideally 4+ years. **Angular, .NET Core, C#, SQL Server, SSIS, Python** and **cloud platforms (Azure preferred)**.
    2.  Strong experience with **project management or product ownership** (Agile/Scrum environment).
    3.  Ability to **translate business requirements into technical specifications**.
    4.  Experience or knowledge in **AI/ML tools and frameworks** (TensorFlow, PyTorch, Azure AI, etc.).
    5.  Excellent communication and leadership skills, with a **proactive and solution-oriented mindset**.
