### Description\nAre you passionate about technology? So are we! To strengthen our expert team at our client, a leader in the radio and communication industry, we are looking for a Development Engineer (m/f/x) for ASIC Characterization to join K-tronik permanently. We look forward to getting to know you personally and working together with passion and team spirit on exciting projects and pioneering technologies.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Conduct characterizations and validations of mixed-signal ASICs, RFICs, and MMICs.\n- Develop, set up, and optimize measurement setups for analyzing high-frequency and mixed-signal parameters.\n- Perform and evaluate RF measurements on coaxial and wafer samples in the micro and millimeter-wave range.\n- Automate characterization measurements, data acquisition, and evaluation using MATLAB, Python, or comparable tools.\n- Analyze and document measurement results, deriving recommendations for design and test development.\n- Create cost and time estimates.\n- Support external test houses with series releases and qualification measurements.\n- Collaborate closely with design, test, packaging, production, and users to validate performance targets and ensure technological excellence.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in Electrical Engineering, Communication Technology, Microelectronics, or a comparable technical education with a focus on RF technology.\n- Strong knowledge of RF measurement technology, calibration, and de-embedding techniques.\n- Experience in characterizing semiconductor components (ASICs, MMICs, RF components).\n- Proficient in using measurement systems (e.g., VNA, SMU, oscilloscope, climate chamber, etc.).\n- Knowledge of automation and data analysis (Python, MATLAB, or similar).\n- Analytical thinking, structured working style, and enthusiasm for complex technical challenges.\n- Strong communication and teamwork skills, along with fluent German and English language proficiency.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at K-tronik offers a unique opportunity for an experienced Development Engineer to make a significant impact on cutting-edge communication technology. The company prides itself on a supportive, collegial, and friendly work environment, highlighted by flexible working hours, 30 days of annual leave, and regular team events. Recognized as a Kununu TOP Company, K-tronik also invests in its employees' growth through individual training opportunities and comprehensive 360-degree support, including career planning and relocation assistance. This is an ideal position for someone seeking technical challenges within a collaborative and employee-centric culture.
