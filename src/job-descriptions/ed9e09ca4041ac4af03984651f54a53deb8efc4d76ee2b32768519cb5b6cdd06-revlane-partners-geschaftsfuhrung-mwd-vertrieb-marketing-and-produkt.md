### Description
REVLANE Partners is seeking an entrepreneurial Managing Director for a client located in the Munich area. This company specializes in developing and producing high-quality interior design solutions, with a strong focus on natural greenery and sustainable materials. Following successful brand development, the business is undergoing a significant restructuring to foster growth, digitalization, and brand renewal. Your mission will be to lead the company into its next phase, rebuilding its sales and brand, developing scalable processes, and guiding a dedicated team with energy and exemplary leadership. The ultimate goal is to professionalize sales, sharpen the product portfolio, and strategically reposition the brand in the market.

### Responsibilities
- Hold overall responsibility for revenue, brand, and operational excellence.
- Actively expand the B2B business segment and establish long-term partnerships.
- Build a digital, predictable sales funnel utilizing tools like CRM, LinkedIn, and automation.
- Drive a lean, impact-oriented marketing strategy.
- Motivate, develop, and coach the team to achieve their full potential.
- Maintain an operative presence, actively participating in key initiatives and serving as the authentic face of the brand.

### Qualifications
- Proven track record of sales success in a B2B environment, ideally within the design, interior, or project business sectors.
- Possess a \"hunter\" mentality: strong closing skills, negotiation proficiency, and a distinct growth orientation.
- Experience with modern digital sales and marketing tools.
- Demonstrate an entrepreneurial mindset.
- Be hands-on and possess a motivating leadership style.
- Exhibit a strong leadership personality with a coaching mentality.
- *Desired (but not mandatory)*: Familiarity with commercial basics, including understanding the difference between a balance sheet and a profit & loss statement, and knowledge of a Business Management Analysis (BWA).
