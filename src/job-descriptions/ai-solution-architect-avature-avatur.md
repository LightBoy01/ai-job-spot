Job Responsibilities
- As a technical solution leader for Global Supply Chain AI transformation, design and implement AI applications, AI Agents.
- Analyze business pain points and design cross function end-to-end digital solutions with achievable target.
- Evaluate current supply chain use cases and design AI Agent related solution as technical owner and design the technical architecture.
- Leading a group to build domain agents/solutions, with knowledge of frameworks of LangChain, LangGraph and strong hands on development skill using Python,Java.
- Lead to implement tools to extend LLM functionality and capability to enhance agent via A2A, MCP and function call.
- Ensure solutions and deliverables align with business objectives and security/compliance.
- Ensure the advancement, safety and performance of the system design and development.
- Responsible for the full life-cycle development of AI use case, AI application.
- Measure the effectiveness of AI solution and make necessary adjustments.

Job Requirements
- Proven work experience as an AI solution Architect or AI Senior Developer.
- Deep understanding of architectures for AI Agent systems, integrating components such as planning, memory (short/long-term), tool usage, feedback loops.
- Hands-on experience with LangChain, LangGraph, or similar LLM frameworks
- Deep understanding on ReAct, RAG and experience of Agent development.
- Strong Architecture and programming skills on Python，Java, and better to have experience as full-stack developer or experience with VUE.
- Strong capability and proven experience on Technical Architect, Solution Architect and system Architect.
- Experience in performance optimization and efficiency improvement of ML models, deep learning models is preferred
- Deep understanding of AI related knowledge, be familiar with the application scenarios and basic principles of common AI technologies and understand the development trends of the AI industry.
- A bachelor's degree or above in computer science, artificial intelligence, data science, software engineering, mathematics, or a related field is required.
- 5-8 year on application development. 3 years in AI solution/Agent engineering, or deep experience in Agentic workflow engineering.
- Ability to have good communicate in English, both written and verbal.
- Experience of leading a dev group.
