### Description

At audius, you'll join a globally active team of approximately 900 employees, becoming an integral part of the digital transformation. Together, we craft tailored services, from consulting and implementation to ongoing operations. Our focus is not only on fostering innovation and customer orientation but also on actively promoting employee growth and development. Whether you're analytical, communicative, pragmatic, or creative, audius offers optimal workplaces and career opportunities for all personalities.

We are looking for a dedicated **IT Onsite Technician (m/f/d)** to take on a central role in our IT Workplace Lifecycle Services at our Stuttgart-Weilimdorf location. You will be instrumental in ensuring the smooth running of daily operations and guaranteeing that our Service Level Agreements (SLAs) are consistently met.

### Responsibilities

*   Manage all IT workplace requests within the IT Workplace Lifecycle.
*   Resolve hardware incidents and service requests efficiently.
*   Perform IMAC/D (Install, Move, Add, Change, Delete) services for mobile devices such as notebooks, smartphones, and their accessories.
*   Install and configure Windows 10/11 end-devices.
*   Provide support and consultation to users regarding hardware and software queries.
*   Accurately record and document all processes for maximum transparency and traceability.
*   Execute on-site delivery of client devices within the greater Stuttgart area using a company vehicle.

### Qualifications

*   Completed vocational training, e.g., as an IT Specialist for System Integration, or a comparable qualification.
*   Solid professional knowledge in IT (client hardware and software, network cabling).
*   Very good proficiency in Microsoft operating systems, including Windows 10/11.
*   Excellent hardware knowledge (notebooks, PCs, smartphones, and their accessories).
*   Experience with common applications (e.g., Microsoft Office, SCCM).
*   Proven experience in 1st- or 2nd-level support using ticket systems, adhering to ITIL standards.
*   Maintain a friendly and professional demeanor when interacting with customers.
*   Fluent German and good English language skills.
*   A Class B driving license is required.
