### Description

Citi is hiring a GenAI Model Risk Data Scientist for their Innovation Labs. In this role, you will use your data science expertise to operationalize cutting-edge AI capabilities, with a specific focus on ensuring that Generative AI developments meet stringent technical criteria and regulatory requirements to reduce risk.

### Responsibilities

- Assess and validate Generative AI models for risk.
- Develop and implement model risk management frameworks for GenAI.
- Collaborate with AI developers and compliance teams.
- Stay updated on emerging GenAI technologies and regulatory landscapes.

### Qualifications

- Master's or Ph.D. in a quantitative field (e.g., Data Science, Statistics, Financial Engineering).
- Strong experience in data science, machine learning, and risk modeling.
- Knowledge of Generative AI models (LLMs, GANs, etc.).
- Experience in financial services or a highly regulated industry.
