**About the role:**

Specialists - Graphic Design are part of the Creative Solutions team and are responsible for the development, design, and delivery of visual assets for offline and online marketing campaigns.

**Responsibilities:**

*   Design and adapt social media visuals, stories, covers, GIF posts, Instagram highlights, and key visuals aligned with campaign concepts.
*   Translate marketing briefs and content calendars into engaging, creative social media designs.
*   Collaborate closely with content writers, the video team, and relevant stakeholders to ensure cohesive design execution.
*   Maintain visual consistency across Instagram, X, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, and LinkedIn.
*   Create impactful Key Visuals for campaigns that align with brand and campaign objectives.
*   Apply good knowledge of illustrations to enhance creativity and storytelling across digital platforms.
*   Leverage a basic knowledge of AI tools to streamline design processes and explore new creative possibilities.
*   Stay up to date with social design trends, platform innovations, and AI-assisted design tools.
*   Ensure all assets adhere to brand guidelines, logo placement rules, and cultural context checks.
*   Optimize visuals for performance, ensuring legibility, readability, and impact across devices.
*   Work on print materials such as catalogs, flyers, posters, booklets, standees, pop-ups, megacoms, muppies, and other large-format graphics.
*   Create design assets to support various digital marketing campaigns across platforms.
*   Develop corporate collateral, including infographics, PowerPoint presentations, reports, and decks.
*   Support the team when needed on CRM, SMP, and Outdoor design tasks.

**Profile Requirements:**

*   3+ years of experience working in a design role
*   A degree in Arts or Design-related fields is preferred.
*   Strong sense of visual storytelling, typography, and layout.
*   Proficiency in social media design tools; familiarity with Canva, Midjourney, Firefly, and other AI tools is a plus.
*   Ability to create Key Visuals for campaigns, adapting them across platforms and formats.
*   Good knowledge of illustration techniques to support creative storytelling and campaign needs.
*   Knowledge of photo editing, basic animation, and motion graphics to enhance digital outputs.
*   Ability to follow brand guidelines and work effectively within established design systems.
*   Detail-oriented with strong organizational skills; able to manage tasks (e.g., via Trello) and meet deadlines.
*   Effective communication and collaboration skills, including working with cross-functional and remote teams.
*   Comfortable receiving and applying constructive feedback.
*   Basic understanding of digital marketing principles, CRM and SMP workflows, Google Ads, and how different social media platforms operate.
*   Proficiency with G Suite applications and other collaboration tools.
