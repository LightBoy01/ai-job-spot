### Description\nThe future of welding is here, and it's straight out of a sci-fi movie! At WeldNova, we're revolutionizing metal welding with our innovative WeldMagnet technology, making weld seams float as if guided by the Force. Our patented solution enables welding processes to be more stable, faster, and significantly more resource-efficient. Backed by years of scientific research, WeldMagnet is currently being tested in pilot projects with leading industrial partners.\n\nAs we embark on our next phase of development, we're expanding our dynamic team. We're seeking passionate and skilled colleagues eager to work on practical industrial applications and committed to bringing groundbreaking manufacturing technology to market readiness.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Independently develop, implement, and optimize PLC control software for magnetic systems, including the integration of sensor technology and peripherals.\n- Conceptualize and realize real-time data acquisition, visualization, and process control for welding operations under industrial conditions.\n- Set up, parameterize, and test interfaces to power electronics, safety components, and Human-Machine Interfaces (HMI).\n- Create functional descriptions, software documentation, and safety concepts in accordance with current standards.\n- Collaborate closely with product development, welding technology, and application departments to ensure seamless integration into overall systems.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A completed university degree (Master or Diploma) in Automation Technology, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, or Computer Science with strong academic performance, or alternatively a qualification as a State Certified Technician (m/f/d) in these fields.\n- Very strong knowledge of PLC programming (e.g., Siemens TIA Portal, Beckhoff TwinCAT, or B&R Automation Studio).\n- Solid expertise in control and regulation technology, as well as real-time communication protocols (EtherCAT, Profinet, OPC UA).\n- Good knowledge of integrating HMI/SCADA systems and industrial communication protocols.\n- Several years of experience in the development and commissioning of complex PLC systems in machine or plant construction is highly desirable.\n- Experience in implementing safety-oriented controls (SIL, Performance Level) and integrating measurement and testing systems is advantageous.\n- An independent, structured, and analytical approach to work, coupled with excellent team and communication skills.\n- A high sense of responsibility and initiative when tackling technically demanding tasks.\n- Very good written and spoken German and English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an experienced PLC Developer to join a pioneering company at the forefront of industrial innovation. WeldNova offers a rare chance to work on truly novel technology, translating scientific breakthroughs into real-world industrial applications. The culture is built around growth, ownership, and collaboration, making it ideal for proactive individuals who thrive in a dynamic, evolving environment. If you're passionate about shaping the future of manufacturing and eager to see your impact firsthand, this role provides significant creative freedom and professional development.
