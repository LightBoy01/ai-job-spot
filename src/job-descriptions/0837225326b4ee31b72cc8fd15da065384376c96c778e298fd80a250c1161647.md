### Description

For over 30 years, Aussie has been a cornerstone in helping more than 1.5 million Australians achieve their dream of homeownership. We're evolving beyond just loans, now offering comprehensive support for finding, buying, and owning property through services like buyer’s agents and conveyancing. Backed by a trusted brand, award-winning support, and cutting-edge technology, we empower our brokers to excel.

Our Aussie Store Brokers are trusted experts in their local communities. With brokers now settling nearly 80% of all home loans and our service offerings expanding, the demand and opportunities for our team have never been greater. We are currently seeking a driven mortgage professional to join our high-performing retail store in **Glenmore Park**.

### Responsibilities

*   Manage a dynamic flow of high-intent customers through walk-ins, online leads, and in-store appointments.
*   Provide expert guidance to customers throughout the entire home loan journey, from the initial search to settlement and beyond.
*   Focus on client relationships and community engagement by submitting deals to our dedicated in-house processing team, freeing you from tedious administrative tasks.
*   Leverage our expanded service offerings, including buyer’s agents and conveyancing, to generate additional referral income.

### Qualifications

*   A Certificate IV in Finance and Mortgage Broking (or a strong willingness to obtain it—we can help!).
*   A proven background in sales or customer service-oriented roles.
*   The drive and determination to actively contribute to the growth and success of your retail store.
*   A genuine passion for helping customers achieve their property ownership dreams.
*   Must hold unrestricted Australian working rights.

### Author's Take

**1. As a self-employed, commission-based broker, how does Aussie ensure a steady stream of quality leads, especially for someone new to the store?**

"That's the question everyone has, and honestly, it's where Aussie really stands apart. You're not just handed a phone book and told 'good luck.' The power of the brand is immense; people trust the name, so we get a significant amount of walk-in traffic and direct inquiries. Beyond that, our national marketing engine is a powerhouse, and the leads generated online—people who are actively looking for help via the Aussie App or website—are funneled directly to our local stores. The system is designed to connect you with high-intent customers, not cold leads. The culture here is also genuinely supportive. We all want the store to succeed, so it’s not uncommon for seasoned brokers to share overflow appointments when they’re swamped. You're building your own business, but you're never building it alone."

**2. The description mentions AI cutting admin 'from 15 hours a week to near zero.' Can you give me a practical example of how this actually helps me focus on clients?**

"It sounds like marketing fluff until you see it in action. Let me give you a real example from last week. I had a client who was a first-time homebuyer, incredibly nervous, and had documents scattered everywhere. Instead of a dozen back-and-forth emails, she just uploaded everything securely through the Aussie App on her phone. Our platform's AI instantly scanned the docs, flagged a missing payslip, and automatically sent her a polite reminder. In the 'old days,' that would have been three phone calls and two emails from me alone. While the system handled that, I was on the phone with her, calming her nerves about the auction process. The tech handles the clerical work—the chasing, the checking, the follow-ups—which frees up your mental energy and your time to be the human expert your client actually needs. It lets you focus on building trust and closing deals, which is where you actually make your money."
