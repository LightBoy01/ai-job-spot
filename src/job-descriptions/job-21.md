# AI Research Scientist (Model Development)

### Description

The Chan Zuckerberg Initiative (CZI) is dedicated to advancing human potential and promoting equal opportunity. We are seeking a creative and driven AI Research Scientist to join our team and contribute to building predictive models of cells. In this role, you will work at the intersection of machine learning and biology, developing novel models and algorithms to understand the fundamental principles of life. This is a unique opportunity to apply your AI expertise to make a significant impact on biomedical research.

### Responsibilities

- Design, train, and validate state-of-the-art machine learning models, particularly in the areas of computer vision and genomics.
- Develop novel methods for self-supervised and semi-supervised learning to leverage vast amounts of unlabeled biological data.
- Collaborate with biologists, computational scientists, and software engineers to build and refine models that can accurately predict cellular behavior.
- Analyze large-scale biological datasets, including microscopy images and single-cell sequencing data.
- Publish research findings in top-tier scientific journals and conferences.
- Contribute to open-source software and datasets to advance the field.

### Qualifications

- PhD in a quantitative field such as Computer Science, Computational Biology, or a related discipline.
- Strong publication record in machine learning or computational biology.
- Deep expertise in PyTorch and experience with modern deep learning techniques.
- Experience working with large-scale biological data is a plus.
- A collaborative and mission-driven mindset.
- Excellent communication skills and the ability to explain complex technical concepts to a diverse audience.
