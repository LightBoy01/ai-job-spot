**EY - GDS Consulting - AI And DATA – AI & Data Strategy Consultant – Manager**

**About the Role**

We are seeking a dynamic Manager to join our AI & Data PE Consulting team, focused on driving data-driven transformations for Private Equity (PE) clients and their portfolio companies. The ideal candidate will bring a unique blend of AI strategy consulting, data analytics expertise, private equity domain knowledge, and hands-on experience in leading cross-functional teams to deliver scalable and impactful solutions.

You will work closely with C-suite leaders, operating partners, and portfolio company executives to help them leverage GenAI, analytics, and data-driven platforms to unlock value, accelerate digital transformation, and create competitive advantage.

**Key Responsibilities**

**AI & Data Consulting for Private Equity**

*   Partner with PE Operating Partners and portfolio leadership teams to define and execute AI, GenAI, and analytics-driven strategies.
*   Develop and deploy AI implementation playbooks, frameworks, and accelerators that drive tangible value creation.
*   Assess technology cost optimization, data monetization, and digital transformation opportunities across portfolio companies.

**GenAI & Digital Transformation**

*   Lead GenAI-driven initiatives, including solution ideation, prototyping, and scaling across portfolios.
*   Build proprietary AI/ML accelerators to enhance business processes such as customer care, software development, predictive analytics, and operational automation.
*   Stay ahead of emerging trends in AI, LLMs, process mining, and advanced analytics to deliver cutting-edge solutions.

**Data & Analytics Strategy**

*   Architect data solutions leveraging cloud platforms, data engineering frameworks, and predictive analytics models to solve complex business challenges.
*   Guide portfolio companies on setting up data governance frameworks, data marketplaces, and analytics COEs.
*   Design end-to-end data transformation programs, from roadmap creation to solution implementation.

**Business Development & Client Management**

*   Work closely with PE account leaders to identify opportunities and develop tailored value propositions.
*   Support pursuit strategy, proposal development, and client orals for strategic PE accounts.
*   Represent the firm at global analyst briefings, industry forums, and thought leadership platforms.

**Team Leadership & Collaboration**

*   Manage and mentor cross-functional teams across consulting, product development, and data engineering.
*   Foster collaboration between AI product teams, analytics experts, and sector specialists to deliver integrated solutions.
*   Drive knowledge sharing and capability-building within the team.

**Key Requirements**

*   Educational Background
*   MBA / PGDM / Master’s degree in Data Science, AI, Analytics, or Business Management.
*   Engineering or quantitative background preferred.

**Professional Experience**

*   10–15 years of experience in AI, data analytics, digital transformation, and strategy consulting, preferably within private equity, M&A, or portfolio transformation contexts.
*   Proven track record in AI-driven transformation programs, including GenAI, predictive analytics, and process mining.
*   Experience in building and deploying accelerators, playbooks, or proprietary platforms for data-driven consulting.

**Technical Expertise**

*   Strong understanding of GenAI frameworks, cloud platforms (Azure / AWS / GCP), data visualization tools, and analytics solutions.
*   Exposure to ERP transformations (e.g., Dynamics 365, SAP, Oracle) and portfolio tech integrations is a plus.
*   Familiarity with process mining tools like Celonis, Signavio, or PAFnow.

**Soft Skills**

*   Exceptional client engagement and stakeholder management skills, especially with C-suite executives.
*   Strong problem-solving mindset with the ability to translate AI into business value.
*   Excellent communication, presentation, and leadership abilities.

**Preferred Certifications**

*   GenAI, AI/ML, or data analytics certifications (Google, Microsoft, AWS, Coursera, etc.)

**Why Join Us**

*   Be at the forefront of AI-driven innovation in the Private Equity sector.
*   Work with global PE clients and their portfolio companies to drive real business impact.
*   Collaborate with a team of AI experts, analytics leaders, and industry specialists in a highly entrepreneurial environment.
