Our Vision: **A new era of healthcare in Europe.**

At Every Health, we're building the first digital platform that finally makes healthcare simple, inclusive, and accessible. For the LGBTQ+ community and beyond 🏳️‍🌈

We're making prevention, diagnoses, and treatments more convenient than ever before.  
Backed by top investors such as Atlantic Labs, Atomico Angels, and Gaingels, we're building the leading platform for sexual health in Europe.

Our goal: Rethinking healthcare – digital, human, and for everyone.

Tasks
-----

As our in-house **Full Stack Engineer**, you will be one of the very early members of the technical product and engineering team, responsible for executing on our tech strategy as we scale under the leadership of the Engineering Lead. This is a hands-on role where you will write production-ready code while overtime, also contributing to scaling our technology and product verticals.

Key responsibilities include:

*   **Owning the implementation of our technical products** – Developing technical components for us to rapidly launch and execute on our tech and product roadmap.
*   **Hands-on development** – Writing high-quality, maintainable code in TypeScript (React, Node.js, PostgreSQL) and leading critical system improvements.
*   **Withholding best practices and standards** – Following company and industry best practices when it comes to CI/CD, logging, monitoring, security, and testing.
*   **Cross-functional collaboration** – Working closely with different stakeholders to build an outstanding digital patient experience.

Your work will directly influence the growth of our venture and help us provide essential healthcare access to more people.

Requirements
------------

You are excited to **build** and take ownership of technical projects and products that scale from thousands to hundreds of thousands of users. Beyond writing code, you want to shape the future of digital healthcare.

We’d love to hear from you if you:

*   Have 3+ **years of experience** in software engineering and full-stack development.
*   Are **hands-on with engineering** – experienced in **TypeScript, React, Node.js, PostgreSQL (AWS RDS), and AWS infrastructure**.
*   Enjoy **building scalable systems** and improving **logging, CI/CD, and testing** for smooth operations.
*   Have experience with **API integrations, connecting systems, and handling sensitive data securely**.
*   Have a **founder’s mindset** – taking ownership, moving fast, and balancing speed with quality.
*   Are fluent in **English** (German is a plus).

Most importantly, you thrive on solving meaningful problems and seeing your work make a tangible impact on people’s lives.
