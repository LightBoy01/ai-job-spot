**About AirOps**
----------------

Today thousands of leading brands and agencies use [AirOps](https://www.airops.com/) to win the battle for attention with content that both humans and agents love.

We’re building the platform and profession that will empower a million marketers to become modern leaders — not spectators — as AI reshapes how brands reach their audiences.

We’re backed by awesome investors, including Unusual Ventures, Wing VC, Founder Collective, XFund, Village Global, and Alt Capital, and we’re building a world-class team with in-person hubs in San Francisco, New York, and Montevideo, Uruguay.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Build AI growth workflows using AirOps that we can use both internally and to productize for customers in different verticals
    
*   Evolve our services delivery playbook and resourcing, moving between execution and strategy
    
*   Use AirOps platform to improve our internal and customer facing processes and prove out new use cases
    
*   Partner with our Growth, Marketing, Sales, and Product teams to build new templates and workflows aligned to product launches and customer expansion opportunities
    
*   Work cross functionally with engineering, design, CX, growth, and content team members to bring features to market quickly & iterate based on feedback
    
*   Monitor key metrics closely; analyze trends/patterns & recommend solutions to improve business outcomes; report findings/recommendations regularly to Exec team
    
*   Partner with design team on UX research initiatives aimed at improving customer engagement & conversions throughout the customer journey
    
*   Test the product and provide feedback to improve the customer experience and usability
    

**Experience**

*   2 to 6 years experience in Growth, BizOps, Product, Technical Account Mgmt, Analytics, Solution Engineering, or similar role at a high-growth startup; we 💚 former (or future aspiring) founders
    
*   Comfortable moving fast in a startup where roles and responsibilities are fluid
    
*   Track record of running complex, multi-threaded projects in a hybrid environment
    
*   Experience with LLMs and AI techniques, including prompt engineering and retrieval
    
*   Experience automating processes with workflow automation tools like Zapier
    
*   Strong analytical skills with experience using data to inform decision making
    
*   Excellent project management skills, including proficiency in project management tools like Linear, Airtable, and Asana
    
*   Strong interest in the intersection between AI and growth, and the emergent capabilities and applications of LLMs
    
*   Familiarity with Python and JavaScript preferred
    
*   Superb written and verbal communication skills.
