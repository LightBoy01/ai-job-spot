**Job Description:**

**About the Role**

As a Senior Software Engineer, you will lead the design and development of scalable, high-performance systems that solve complex business problems. You'll own critical components, drive technical decisions, and collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver reliable software while mentoring team members and balancing short-term goals with long-term sustainability.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Translate complex requirements into effective software solutions
*   Design, build, and maintain scalable, high-performance components and services
*   Take ownership of system components, driving implementation and managing technical debt
*   Ensure high availability and SLA compliance for services
*   Collaborate with data scientists and engineering leadership in an agile environment
*   Contribute to architectural roadmaps and capacity planning
*   Define and implement comprehensive testing practices
*   Champion continuous delivery practices and enhance team productivity
*   Balance delivery priorities with architectural sustainability
*   Design and run experiments to optimize algorithms and system performance

**Qualifications:**

**Skills and Experience:**

*   Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent experience
*   5+ years of software development experience
*   Proficiency in Java, Python, Rust, or Go
*   Strong AWS cloud platform experience
*   Understanding of machine learning, deep learning, and LLMs
*   Experience building scalable, resilient systems
*   Knowledge of CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices
*   Functional programming experience (preferred)

**Professional Skills:**

*   Strong problem-solving and analytical abilities
*   Excellent communication and collaboration skills
*   Cross-functional teamwork experience
*   Commitment to continuous learning and industry awareness
