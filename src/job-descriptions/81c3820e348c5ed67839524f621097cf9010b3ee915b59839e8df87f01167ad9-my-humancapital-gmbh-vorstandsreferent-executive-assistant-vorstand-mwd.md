### Description\nOur partner company in Munich is seeking a dedicated and proactive **Executive Assistant to the Board (m/w/d)** to join their team as soon as possible. In this pivotal role, you will act as a central interface to the Executive Board, accompanying strategic and operational topics at the highest corporate level within a dynamic and growth-oriented environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Act as the central interface to the Executive Board, managing strategic and operational topics.\n- Support the Executive Management in meetings, analyze relevant information, prepare decision templates, and independently drive projects across various business units.\n- Coordinate interdisciplinary teams, moderate alignment meetings, and ensure efficient and timely execution of parallel tasks.\n- Manage interface relations between the Executive Board, accounting, tax firms, and auditors, establishing clear processes and robust decision-making foundations.\n- Actively participate in budget planning, business plan development, and annual financial statements within the Finance and Controlling departments.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A completed degree in Economics, Law, or Finance, OR a completed commercial apprenticeship (e.g., Office Administrator, Tax Assistant), or an equivalent qualification.\n- Ideally, first professional experience within a board or management environment, controlling, or in project leadership roles.\n- Proficient in standard MS-Office applications, especially Excel, with the ability to clearly present complex information.\n- Possess a team-oriented approach, maintain an overview in stressful situations, and demonstrate a solution-oriented mindset.\n- Excellent German language skills are required.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an ambitious professional looking for direct exposure to executive-level decision-making and strategic initiatives. The role promises significant impact, professional growth, and a supportive culture that values work-life balance and continuous learning. It's perfect for someone who thrives in a fast-paced environment, can manage complex tasks, and is eager to contribute directly to a company's success. The combination of executive support, project leadership, and financial involvement makes this a comprehensive and highly rewarding position.
