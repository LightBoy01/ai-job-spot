Job Summary

As an AI Infrastructure Engineer, you’ll work on the core of Unframe - building reusable AI services, scalable APIs, dynamic UI engine, and shared infrastructure that powers every solution we deliver.

*   Design and implement core AI building blocks, backend services and frontend components.
*   Work on platform-wide features such as user management, abstractions, and dashboards.

*   4+ years experience in software engineering or research roles.
*   Deep understanding of prompt-based pipelines and LLM APIs.
*   Ability to build clean APIs, Services and reusable UI components.

Nice to Have

*   Background in AI/ML, LLMs, or prompt-based apps.
*   Experience with design systems or multi-tenant architecture.
*   Familiarity with backend performance optimization and DB architecture.

If you’re a strong, independent builder who thrives on ownership, cares deeply about quality, and wants to have a real impact — we’d love to meet you.
