### Description\nThis company is a successful consulting firm actively contributing to Germany's energy transition by developing and realizing its own wind and PV projects on secured land. They are seeking a highly responsible and experienced Project Manager to oversee the full development cycle of renewable energy projects. This is a critical role that will directly contribute to establishing renewable energies in Germany in the coming years.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Take overall responsibility for a project portfolio, encompassing the entire development cycle from land acquisition, commissioning studies, and optimal layout/turbine type analysis to permit applications and follow-up.\n- Prepare and accompany permitting procedures in accordance with the Federal Immission Control Act (BImSchG).\n- Conduct regular travel to potential wind and PV park locations for site assessments and stakeholder engagement.\n- Manage stakeholders throughout the project development lifecycle, including landowners, service providers, authorities, and municipalities.\n- Prepare strategic decisions for the management board, including comprehensive reporting on project progress and risks.\n- Monitor projects diligently regarding time schedules, budget adherence, and quality standards.\n- Proactively identify and manage project risks to ensure successful project delivery.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A successfully completed degree in Geography, Engineering, Economics, or a comparable field, or equivalent professional qualifications and experience.\n- Several years of professional experience in project development, with a strong preference for the wind or PV industry, specifically focusing on permitting planning.\n- In-depth knowledge of the economic and legal framework for wind energy, particularly in planning law (e.g., BauGB, BImSchG, ROG, EEG, TA Lärm, BNatSchG, WindBG).\n- Proven experience in interacting and negotiating with landowners, service providers, permitting authorities, and local municipalities.\n- Practical experience with Geographical Information Systems (GIS) in the context of wind and PV park development.\n- Proficient application skills in MS-Office are considered advantageous.\n- Business-fluent German language skills, both written and spoken, are essential.\n- Willingness to travel and possession of a German driver's license (Class B).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a seasoned Project Manager passionate about making a tangible impact on Germany's renewable energy landscape. The role offers comprehensive responsibility across the entire project lifecycle, demanding a blend of technical expertise, legal understanding, and strong stakeholder management skills. The company fosters a dynamic, flexible, and supportive environment, indicated by flexible working hours, mobile work options, and a 'start-up flair' with numerous social events and benefits. For someone looking to take ownership and drive significant projects in a critical and growing industry, this position offers both challenge and substantial reward.
