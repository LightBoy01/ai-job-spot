Are you passionate about supporting reinsurance claims management and building your expertise in a great team of claims experts? Join Swiss Re as a Claims Specialist, where you’ll learn everything about reinsurance claims handling, work with experienced colleagues, and deliver reliable service to our clients across the WSE region.  

**About the Role** 

As a Claims Specialist, you will be responsible for a claims portfolio, analyse underlying claims trends in the portfolio by using the latest technology and technically assess claims that need your attention. You share the insights that you gain with your stakeholders, like Underwriting and Reserving. You’ll work under the guidance of senior claims experts, gaining exposure to a variety of claims scenarios and developing your skills in an engaging environment. You build and own the client relationships for your respective markets and support with the global clients claims strategy and relationship management. 

Key Responsibilities: 

*   Manage your own claims portfolio under the guidance of senior claims experts. 
*   Monitor the movements in your responsibility by using the latest technology and evaluate the claims that need your attention together with senior claims experts and key case managers 
*   Share your claims insights with stakeholders across the value chain to improve the profitability of the business, 
*   Closely collaborate in cross functional teams, especially with Underwriting and Reserving 
*   Participate in client service activities, including claims reviews and training sessions, as required. 
*   Collaborate with expert networks and deepen your knowledge in P&C lines of business  
*   Contribute to industry events and client meetings. 
*   Support your claims team in the development of data driven portfolio analytics  

**About the Team** 

The WSE & MEA & Globals WSE team manages claims portfolios across diverse regions. In your role, you’ll work closely with experienced colleagues, contribute to team goals, and build foundational expertise in claims management. 

**About You** 

You are a detail-oriented professional with good analytical abilities and effective communication skills. You thrive in a collaborative environment and are eager to learn and contribute to team success. Your ability to support claims management and maintain strong client relationships sets you apart. 

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements: 

*   University degree or equivalent qualification. 
*   Minimum of 3 years of experience in the re-/insurance industry in Underwriting, Claims or Finance. 
*   Basic understanding of insurance principles and market practices. 
*   Ability to leverage artificial intelligence tools and technologies to enhance claims analysis and decision-making. 
*   Excellent communication skills with fluency in English and either French or Spanish, ideally both. 

These are additional nice to haves: 

*   Experience working in international or multicultural environments. 
*   Knowledge of multiple lines of business. 
*   Exposure to project coordination is beneficial. 
*   Digital literacy and experience with artificial intelligence projects. 
*   Ability to travel for client meetings, conferences, and team collaborations.
