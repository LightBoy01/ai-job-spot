*   Design **end-to-end product experiences** across mobile (iOS/Android) and web platforms.

*   Track record of designing for both **mobile and web** applications.

*   Curiosity about **AI-powered and LLM-driven interfaces.**

Brainly is the **#1 AI education tool in the world**, with a vision to give every student in the world access to personalized learning, no matter their background or resources.
