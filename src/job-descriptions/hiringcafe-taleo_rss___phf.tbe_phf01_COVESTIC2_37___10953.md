**About Us:**

We are a leading IT services and consulting firm at the forefront of **digital transformation**, **AI-driven innovation**, and **enterprise modernization**. Our mission is to empower global organizations with scalable, future-ready solutions that drive business outcomes.

**Job Summary:**

We are seeking a seasoned **Enterprise Architect** with a strong track record of **client-facing solutioning**, **technology roadmapping**, and leading **digital transformation initiatives**. This individual will work closely with clients and internal teams to align business objectives with modern technology solutions — including **AI, cloud, data platforms, SaaS, PaaS, IaaS, and enterprise systems**.

**Key Responsibilities:**

*   Engage directly with enterprise clients to understand business goals, IT landscapes, and transformation challenges.
*   Lead the creation of **enterprise-wide technology roadmaps** that align with digital strategy and future-state architectures.
*   Analyze enterprise business processes, top-down, to determine functional scope of change, leading to application and technology architecture recommendations to support that analysis.
*   Where appropriate, describe hybrid architectural options (SaaS, PaaS, custom code) to meet unique digital transformation requirements.
*   Architect end-to-end solutions leveraging **cloud, AI/ML, data analytics, integration platforms**, and legacy modernization.
*   Support the **pre-sales process** by designing innovative solutions, crafting proposals, presenting to stakeholders, and contributing to RFP responses.
*   Serve as a **strategic advisor** to CxO-level executives and technical stakeholders.
*   Ensure architectural integrity across all initiatives, balancing business, technical, and operational considerations.
*   Collaborate cross-functionally with engineering, product, and consulting teams to ensure solution feasibility and scalability.
*   Stay up to date with emerging technologies and industry trends to provide thought leadership and innovation guidance.

**Technical Expertise:**

*   **AI/ML & GenAI:**
    *   Hands-on with AI/ML platforms (SageMaker, Azure ML, Vertex AI) and GenAI solutions using LLMs (e.g., GPT, Claude, PaLM)
    *   Experience with RAG architectures, prompt engineering, vector databases (Pinecone, FAISS), and enterprise use cases (e.g., AI assistants, intelligent search)
    *   Strong grasp of MLOps, model lifecycle, and ethical AI practices
*   **Cloud & Application Architecture:**
    *   Deep expertise in AWS, Azure, or GCP
    *   Cloud-native design using Kubernetes, containers, serverless, and APIs
    *   Experience modernizing legacy systems and building microservices
*   **Integration & DevOps:**
    *   Proficient in API gateways, event-driven architectures (Kafka, Pub/Sub)
    *   Skilled in CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, CloudFormation), and DevSecOps
*   **Security & Compliance:**
    *   Enterprise security architecture (IAM, encryption, zero trust)
    *   Familiar with GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2 compliance standards
*   **Frameworks & Tools:**
    *   TOGAF, Zachman, ArchiMate, Lucidchart, Sparx EA

**Required Qualifications:**

*   10+ years of experience in IT architecture, with at least 5 years in an **Enterprise Architect** or similar strategic role.
*   Proven experience working in **client-facing roles**, particularly in **consulting or services environments**.
*   Demonstrated experience **solutioning large-scale deals**, ideally including digital transformation and AI-driven use cases.
*   Deep understanding of **cloud platforms** (AWS, Azure, GCP), **AI/ML**, **data architecture**, and modern integration patterns (APIs, microservices, event-driven).
*   Strong communication and presentation skills, with the ability to influence stakeholders at all levels.
*   Experience working in industries such as financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, or retail is a plus.
*   TOGAF, Zachman, or other architecture framework certifications are a plus.

**Preferred Qualifications:**

*   Experience with **AI/ML platforms and tools**, including GenAI, NLP, and MLOps.
*   Familiarity with enterprise applications (ERP, CRM, SCM) and modernization strategies.
*   Background in Agile/DevOps environments with an understanding of delivery methodologies.

**Why Join Us:**

*   Work on cutting-edge digital and AI transformation projects with Fortune 500 clients.
*   Collaborate with a team of passionate technologists and strategists.
*   Shape strategic technology direction for global enterprises.
*   Competitive compensation, remote flexibility, and career advancement opportunities.
