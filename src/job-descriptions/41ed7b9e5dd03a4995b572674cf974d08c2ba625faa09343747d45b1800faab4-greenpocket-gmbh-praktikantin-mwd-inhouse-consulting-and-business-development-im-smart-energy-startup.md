### Description\nJoin an agile, green startup specializing in intuitive, AI-driven energy management and visualization software. Our mission is to simplify energy consumption management for everyone – from businesses to private households – and empower energy providers and corporate clients to significantly reduce their energy consumption and CO2 footprint. With a team of 35 talented colleagues, we are actively shaping the digitalization of the energy sector towards a sustainable, climate-neutral future. We are seeking a proactive student with an interest in current trends in energy management, AI applications, and IoT to support the strategic development of our company.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Business Development:** Support by identifying current trends and evaluating them for potential new business models and future strategic alignment.\n- **Innovation Management:** Work directly with our CEO on the strategic development of our company and software products.\n- **Analytical Deep Dive:** Independently develop and evaluate use cases, analyzing their impact on existing business models.\n- **Market Insights:** Conduct independent analyses of market and competitor developments within the energy sector, presenting your findings directly to the CEO.\n- **Stakeholder Management:** Act as the key interface between Product Management, Finance, and Sales.\n- **Investor Relations:** Manage correspondence with investors, maintain documentation, control the financing round process, and qualify/analyze investors and markets in preparation for investor meetings.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Currently enrolled as a student in Energy Economics, Business or Communication Sciences, or a comparable field.\n- Ideally, you have gained initial practical experience in strategy or business development through internships (e.g., at a management consultancy or a strategic department within a corporation or startup).\n- Possess an analytical mindset coupled with a systematic and goal-oriented approach to work.\n- Confident in contacting external partners or investors via telephone.\n- Highly organized, proactive, self-motivated, ambitious, and able to quickly apply acquired knowledge in practice.\n- Very good German and English language skills, proficient in standard MS-Office applications.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for an ambitious student eager to make a tangible impact in the sustainable energy sector. The role offers unparalleled exposure, working directly with the CEO on critical strategic initiatives, business development, market analysis, and investor relations within a dynamic, agile green startup. Candidates will benefit from continuous learning, significant responsibility, and a supportive, flexible work environment that includes home office options and regular team-building activities. It's a chance to truly shape the future of energy management.
