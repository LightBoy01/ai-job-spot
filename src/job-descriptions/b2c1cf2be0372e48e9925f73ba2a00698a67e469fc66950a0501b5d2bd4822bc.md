🧰 Who We Are
-------------
Wakam is a **B2B2C insurance company** that creates **white-label insurance solutions** via its **Play&Plug®** technology platform for more than **80 partners**. We provide most of our insurance products through **API**, and host white-label insurance solutions via our Play&Plug technology platform.
With a footprint spanning **32 countries**, Wakam is the **European leader in digital and embedded insurance**.
Strongly committed to social responsibility, Wakam is a **mission-driven company** dedicated to **“enabling transparent and impactful insurance.”**
* * *
🚀 About the Team
-----------------
Join Wakam’s **Finance Office**—the engine behind strategic decision-making and operational excellence. We safeguard the company’s financial health through budgeting, forecasting, performance reporting, risk management, and compliance.
Within Finance, the **FP&A (Financial Planning & Analysis)** team partners closely with Commercial, Technical, and Reinsurance teams to turn data into actionable insight, forecast performance, and steer value creation across Wakam’s European footprint.
* * *
🎯 Your Mission
---------------
Reporting to the **Technical FP&A Team Lead**, you will own and optimize **Wakam SA’s technical margin**. Your scope includes:
*   **Quarterly closing** of the technical margin and variance analysis.
*   Building **budgets and forecasts** for the technical margin with Commercial, Technical, and Reinsurance stakeholders.
*   Producing **monthly performance reporting** with clear, decision-ready insights.
*   Contributing to the annual **cost accounting** build.
*   Supporting **new business cases** with robust financial modelling.
*   Driving **finance transformation projects** and delivering **ad-hoc analyses** that influence strategy.
* * *
🧠 What You Bring
-----------------
*   Master’s degree (Bac+5) from a Business/Engineering school, ideally with a **Finance/Insurance** focus.
*   \+ 8 years’ experience in **Technical FP&A within the insurance industry (mandatory).**
*   Advanced **Excel** skills and solid command of financial modelling and performance KPIs.
*   Proven ability to translate complex topics into **clear, concise insights** for senior stakeholders.
*   **Fluent French and English** (spoken and written).
*   Curiosity for **AI and automation** and how they can streamline workflows, reduce repetitive tasks, and enrich analysis.
* * *
