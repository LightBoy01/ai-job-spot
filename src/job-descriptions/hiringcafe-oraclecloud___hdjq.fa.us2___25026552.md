**In This Role, Your Responsibilities Will Be:**

**Digital Tool Strategy & Support**

*   Manage the lifecycle of digital tools such as sizing and selection platforms, customer portals, and online configurators.
*   Work with product and engineering teams to ensure tools are accurate, reliable, and meet market needs.
*   Capture user insights and analytics to improve tool usability, effectiveness, and adoption.

**Web Design & UX Oversight**

*   Manage the design and deployment of web interfaces for digital tools, ensuring they are visually appealing, user-friendly, and on-brand.
*   Partner with web developers and designers to deliver responsive and accessible experiences for all users.
*   Optimize landing pages and content strategies that support tool usage and conversions.

**AI & Emerging Technology**

*   Explore and integrate AI features such as personalized recommendations, chatbots, and predictive selection tools.
*   Collaborate with data science and IT teams to pilot and expand new technologies.

**Software & License Key Management**

*   Oversee the process for generating, distributing, and tracking software and transmitter license keys.
*   Ensure customers have smooth access to licensed tools and maintain accurate records within CRM or license management systems.
*   Coordinate with sales, support, and legal teams on compliance and renewal workflows.

**Cross-Functional Collaboration & Promotion**

*   Develop go-to-market plans for new or updated digital tools.
*   Coordinate internal training, customer communications, and marketing campaigns that drive engagement.
*   Supervise performance metrics and adapt strategies for continuous improvement.

**Who You Are:**

You are a strategic thinker who thrives in dynamic environments and adapts with confidence. Curious and tech-forward, you are eager to explore new tools and innovations that improve the way people work. You communicate effectively and enjoy collaborating with colleagues across different teams and backgrounds. With a strong customer focus, you are passionate about creating value and making digital tools more intuitive and engaging to use. Above all, you are collaborative, open-minded, and committed to delivering exceptional value and service.

  
**For This Role, You Will Need:**

*   Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Digital Media, Business, or a related field.
*   Minimum 5 years of experience in digital marketing, product marketing, or digital tool management.
*   Experience with web design tools (e.g., WordPress, Webflow, or Adobe Experience Manager) and basic knowledge of HTML/CSS.
*   Familiarity with AI platforms (e.g., OpenAI, Google AI, Microsoft Copilot) and their application in user experience.
*   Understanding of software license management practices or platforms.
*   Strong communication and project management skills.
*   Expected Travel approximately 20%
*    Legal authorization to work in the United States - Sponsorship will not be provided for this position. 

Preferred Qualifications That Set You Apart:

*   Strategic thinking creating a roadmap vision for digital tools and how they would contribute to growth initiatives
*   Strong project management skills working across multiple business units to drive results and complete projects on time and on budget
