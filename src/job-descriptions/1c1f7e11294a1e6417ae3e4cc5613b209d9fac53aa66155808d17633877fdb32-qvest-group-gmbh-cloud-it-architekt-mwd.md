### Description\nQvest Digital is seeking a Senior Software Architect & Consultant to advise clients, design robust software architectures, and drive digital transformation. This role involves participating in presales, supporting project launches, evolving existing IT systems, and leading workshops, all while contributing to the continuous improvement of client organizations and Qvest Digital itself.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Consult clients on suitable technologies and design resilient software architectures.\n*   Participate in the presales process (including estimations) and accompany subsequent projects.\n*   Support project starts by organizing framework parameters such as CI/CD pipelines, development environments, and target platforms.\n*   Analyze and develop existing IT systems as part of IT assessments or migrations (e.g., to the cloud).\n*   Plan and conduct requirements workshops.\n*   Accompany rollouts and maintenance until project handover to the client.\n*   Contribute to the further development and improvement of client organizations and Qvest Digital.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   **Required:**\n    *   5+ years of experience in customer projects as a Consultant or Software Architect.\n    *   Strong knowledge of one of the major cloud providers (AWS, Azure, GCP).\n    *   Experience across the software lifecycle process with methods like Requirements Engineering, Architecture Documentation, and Continuous Integration.\n    *   Belief that IT architecture is based on technical expertise, including programming experience.\n    *   Ability to navigate new contexts, markets, and corporate cultures with confidence.\n    *   Experience in presales and proposal creation.\n    *   Proficiency in interacting with various hierarchical levels and stakeholders.\n    *   Ability to conduct workshops with clients and explain complex technological topics understandably to non-experts.\n    *   Proficiency in German and English (C1 level or better).\n*   **Nice to Have:**\n    *   Active and networked within the community, giving presentations at conferences and events.\n    *   Certifications in relevant areas such as ISAQB-F, Cloud System Architect (AWS/Azure/GCP), ITIL 4, or PMI/Prince2.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a compelling opportunity for an experienced Software Architect looking for a dynamic consulting role. Qvest Digital champions a highly inclusive and flexible work environment, offering excellent benefits like extensive professional development (12 days/year), generous vacation (28+4 days), remote work options, and strong support for work-life balance (family-friendly, flexible hours). The company values diversity, emphasizing skills and experience over traditional application formalities, making it an attractive employer for those seeking continuous growth and a supportive culture.
