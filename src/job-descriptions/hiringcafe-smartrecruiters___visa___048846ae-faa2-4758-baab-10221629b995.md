**Job Description:**

Visa and its subsidiary companies have Identity, Authorization and Fraud data in multiple systems built and acquired over the years. These rich datasets when appropriately mined via Machine Learning/Artificial Intelligence, will provide network-agnostic tools to improve authorization rates for our merchants and partners in the ecosystem. Identity Graph brings these internal datasets & relevant external data together to build a holistic 360-degree view of all the individuals who interact with the ecosystem and empower multiple use cases in the identity-enabled fraud detection space. This role will focus on defining and executing the product strategy for Visa’s Identity Graph, working with a globally distributed team to do so.

The Product Manager will play a critical role is defining the roadmap, strategy, and vision for Identity Graph. They will work directly with engineering, design and other Visa product teams to define requirements for the product that meet the product vision and regulatory requirements. The ideal candidate should be able to quickly come up to speed on the data available within Visa and externally and be able to think critically and strategically about how to bring it together in way that empowers a holistic view of the individuals. They will be required to work directly with technical teams, business teams, and partners, therefore should be comfortable adapting their communication style appropriately for their audience. They will need to influence stakeholders without direct authority to execute their product strategy. They will be responsible for developing data driven analyses to drive the product vision and strategy. The product manager may also participate as a Visa representative to industry standard organizations, participating in working groups to help define industry best practices or specifications. This role requires a variety of skillsets across strategy, product management, product analytics, delivery, and operations.

Responsibilities

• Lead a team of data engineers to explore internal and external data for Visa’s Identity Graph

• Define data exploration strategies to drive product requirements • Define the right set of metrics that needs to be tracked for product health

• Lead the analytical thinking how can Identity Graph add value to various use cases and simulate the results

• Work with stakeholders across various geographical regions, Visa product teams, and functions (e.g. design, legal) to define requirements and prioritize features

• Manage and coordinate technology development and integration with internal engineering teams and external partners/vendors

• Provide subject matter expertise for internal, client, and partner sales efforts

• Collaborate with fellow product managers to develop business case and revenue forecasts for your product

• Understand the industry landscape, competitors, regulatory requirements that impact your product

This is a hybrid position. Expectation of days in office will be confirmed by your hiring manager.
