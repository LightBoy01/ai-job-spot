We leverage more than 17 years of support to stakeholders across the federal government, with established and growing capabilities across Intelligence, Analytics, Engineering, Mission Support, and Communications disciplines. Founded in 2008, our mission is to transform the way our customers approach constantly changing and complex problem sets by bringing to bear the latest in technology and the highest caliber of talent.</em></p>

As a leader in our space, we partner with firms in the private sector, academic institutions, and industry associations with a goal of continually building our expertise and capabilities for the benefit of our employees and the customers we support. Through all of this, we have built a vibrant corporate culture diverse in expertise and perspectives with a focus on collaboration and innovation. Our teams are at the frontier of the Nation's most complex and rewarding challenges. Join our team.</em></p><p><strong>Barbaricum is seeking a Senior Intelligence Analyst to support The U.S. Army Training and Doctrine Command (TRADOC) G-2. This individual will provide independent critical reviews, red teaming support, and strategic analysis to TRADOC HQ leaders and staff. This role supports TRADOC’s mission to address complex strategic challenges by identifying weaknesses, vulnerabilities, and gaps across DOTMLPF (Doctrine, Organization, Training, Materiel, Leadership and Education, Personnel, and Facilities) functions. The Senior Military Operations Analyst will deliver solutions to strategic issues, assess critical assumptions, and propose alternative perspectives to improve concepts, doctrine, and organizational designs.</strong></p>
<p>Responsibilities:</p>
<ul>
<li>Perform detailed mission analyses to scope and define the parameters of each requested critical review.</li>
<li>Edit and provide recommendations for products with proposed changes and comments based on documented sources.</li>
<li>Identify and address DOTMLPF (Doctrine, Organization, Training, Materiel, Leadership and Education, Personnel, and Facilities) weaknesses, gaps, and disconnects.</li>
<li>Conduct timely reviews of small products such as implementing directives, regulations, white papers, or briefings.</li>
<li>Deliver evaluations that challenge the assumptions, solutions, and content within five (5) workdays.</li>
<li>Deliver unbiased and comprehensive reviews of significant projects such as experiment constructs or war-game designs.</li>
<li>Analyze assumptions, solutions, structure, and content within the requested timeframe.</li>
<li>Provide insights, observations, and actionable recommendations to TRADOC leadership.</li>
<li>Present findings through clear briefings and/or edited products.</li>
</ul>
<p>Qualifications:</p>
<ul>
<li>Active DoD Secret Clearance.</li>
<li>8-12 years of experience.</li>
<li>Military Intelligence Experience – Extensive background in intelligence operations, analysis, and strategic decision-making within a military environment.</li>
<li>Artificial Intelligence &amp; Machine Learning – Expertise in applying AI/ML technologies in defense, intelligence, and operational contexts.</li>
<li>Instruction &amp; Training – Experience in designing and delivering instructional programs for military or defense professionals.</li>
<li>Capability Development – Strong background in developing, testing, and implementing new military capabilities to enhance operational effectiveness.</li>
<li>Exercises &amp; Experimentation – Proven experience in planning, conducting, and assessing military exercises, wargaming, and strategic experimentation.</li>
<li>Future Operational Environment (OE) &amp; Threat Concepts/Assessment – Knowledge in analyzing and forecasting future battlefield environments and potential threats.</li>
<li>Science &amp; Technology (S&amp;T) Acquisition Process (ARL) – Familiarity with the Army Research Laboratory (ARL) acquisition process and defense technology procurement.</li>
<li>Educational Background – PhD preferred; Master’s degree required in a relevant field (e.g., Intelligence Studies, National Security, Engineering, or Computer Science).</li>
<li>Communication Skills – Excellent oral and written communication abilities, with a strong capacity to deliver briefings, reports, and analysis to senior leadership.</li>
</ul>
