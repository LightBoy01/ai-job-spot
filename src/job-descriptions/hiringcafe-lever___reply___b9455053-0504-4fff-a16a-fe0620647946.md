Concept Reply is a leader in IoT solutions. We support and advise our customers in Automotive, Manufacturing, Smart Infrastructure and beyond in all aspects of Internet of Things (IoT) and Cloud Computing. With a background in engineering and a passion for cutting-edge technology, our team of experts is committed to delivering world-class results throughout the entire value chain -- from designing and developing customized IoT solutions to implementing and managing them seamlessly.  We are seeking a Data & AI Engineer to join our team and contribute to the development of innovative data-driven solutions. The ideal candidate will have a passion for data analysis, machine learning, and artificial intelligence, with a strong desire to learn and grow in a dynamic environment.  
  
**Responsibilities**  
*   Lead complex AI projects including a team of professionals
*   Support the planning and execution of data migration and integration initiatives
*   Assist in the design, development, and maintenance of data pipelines and ETL workflows
*   Work closely with data engineers and scientists to build and enhance machine learning models
*   Contribute to the development and deployment of AI-driven applications and services
*   Stay informed on emerging trends and advancements in data science and AI
  
  
**Minimum Requirements**  
*   3 years of professional experience in data engineering, AI/ML, or a related technical field
*   1 year of hands-on experience supporting complex AI-related projects
*   Strong understanding of programming principles and data structures
*   Proficiency in Python (3 yrs experience)
*   Strong knowledge in at least one of the following areas: Generative AI services (e.g., OpenAI, Bedrock) or Computer Vision
*   Experience with cloud platforms like AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud
*   Familiarity with version control systems like Git
*   Basic understanding of data analysis techniques and statistical methods
  
  
**Preferred Qualifications**  
*   Familiarity with machine learning frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch
*   Understanding of data preprocessing techniques and feature engineering
*   Experience with Snowflake
*   2 years of experience leading technical projects
