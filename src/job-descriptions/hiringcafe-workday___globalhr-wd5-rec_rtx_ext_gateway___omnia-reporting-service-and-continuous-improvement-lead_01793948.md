**Role: OMNIA Reporting Service and Continuous Improvement Lead**

Be at the forefront of transforming British Army training.  
  
**About us:**  
At OMNIA® Training, we’ve brought together some of the UK’s most innovative defence training organisations under one powerful mission: to transform the British Army’s training system and create the best-trained Army in the world.

OMNIA are redefining the British Army’s collective training. To do that, we are looking for the best and brightest minds from across the UK. We are are backed by British innovation and powered by world-class experts, like you. OMNIA is at the heart of the UK’s bold Land Industrial Strategy.

  
**The role:**  
This is more than a job — **it’s a mission**. You will be part of a high-impact, collaborative environment, where we expect everyone to live the values and standards of the British Army. Every person in our team plays a critical role in delivering OMNIA’s vision; designing, delivering, and transforming collective training so the British Army is ready to fight and win.

You’ll work in a matrix organisation and report operationally through OMNIA Training and functionally through the Raytheon UK **_Programme Management Team._** Ultimately, you’ll work for the British Army, championing innovation, and helping shape the future of military training.

**_Main Duties_**

The OMNIA Reporting Service and Continuous Improvement Lead is responsible for overseeing the delivery of the Reporting Service within the OMNIA Assure function. This role ensures the accurate, timely, and insightful reporting of Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), Management Information (Mis) and Performance Indicators (PIs) across the Army Collective Training System (ACTS). The Reporting Service and Continuous Improvement Lead will manage a team of analysts and collaborate closely with the Authority to support decision-making and continuous improvement. L

*   Lead the development and implementation of reporting frameworks aligned with ACTS governance structures
*   Manage the Reporting Service team, ensuring delivery of monthly, quarterly and annual reports in line with specified requirements.
*   Oversee the creation of dashboards and data visualisations
*   Ensure reporting aligns with ISO 9001 and IS 44001 standards for quality and collaboration
*   Support the Authority in monitoring the health of the collaborative relationships and training system performance
*   Provide reporting to the ACTS Governance forums
*   Maintain a centralised, secure repository of reports and assure accessibility for authorised stakeholders
*   Drive continuous improvement by integrating insights into the Quality Improvement and Action Plan
*   Develop strong relationships with the customers and subcontractors to ensure that they are meeting their commitments to the project.
