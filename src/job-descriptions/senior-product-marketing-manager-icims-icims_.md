Overview:
<p>At GitHub, we believe in the power of a collaborative community to create the future of software. Software is how we can make the world a better place, and we need a Senior Product Marketing Manager to pioneer and drive the market narrative for our enterprise, AI, and security solutions. This is a high-impact, highly cross-functional role where you will be instrumental in defining market strategy, influencing product direction, and leading go-to-market efforts that drive significant business growth.</p><p><br>You'll be the voice of our developers and customers, translating their needs into a compelling narrative that shapes our core messaging and positioning. This role is about more than just crafting a message—it’s about defining a new market category and directly influencing product adoption, pipeline creation, and revenue growth for our evolving AI, enterprise and developer platform.<br>This role offers a unique chance to shape the future of a product used by millions of developers worldwide. You’ll be at the intersection of AI, security, and developer tools, working in a highly collaborative, yet autonomous, environment with a small team where your strategic contributions have a direct, visible impact on the business and the industry.</p>

Responsibilities:
<ul><li><strong>Define and Evangelize the Market Narrative:</strong> Serve as the primary driver of GitHub's market position for specific products, defining compelling messaging and positioning that resonates with developers and C-level stakeholders.</li><li><strong>Lead Strategic Go-to-Market Initiatives: </strong>Support product launches and major GTM initiatives, from strategy definition to cross-functional execution. You'll support the launch framework, establish success metrics, and ensure alignment across marketing, sales, and product teams.</li><li><strong>Influence Product Launches:</strong> Act as a strategic partner to Product Management, using a deep understanding of our audience, market trends, and competitive landscape to inform the product roadmap, pricing, packaging, and naming.</li><li><strong>Be the Expert: </strong>Maintain a deep understanding of the developer ecosystem, trends around AI agents and AI developer tools and the competitive landscape to inform strategic decisions across the company.</li></ul>

Qualifications:
<p><strong>Required Qualifications:</strong></p><ul><li>7+ years experience in Marketing <br><ul><li>OR Bachelor's Degree in Marketing, Computer Science, Business or related field AND 5+ years experience in Marketing</li><li>OR Master's Degree in Marketing, Computer Science, Business or related field AND 3+ years experience in Marketing</li><li>OR equivalent experience.</li></ul></li><li>5+ years of experience in Business-to-Business (B2B) product marketing at a high-growth SaaS or AI company (or equivalent experience).</li><li>Experience marketing to developer audiences, translating highly technical concepts into compelling narratives for diverse stakeholders—from engineers to executives.</li><li>Experience leading multiple strategic product launches for B2B software in the SaaS or AI space.</li></ul><p><strong>Preferred Qualifications:</strong></p><ul><li>Strong understanding of B2B software business models, with a track record of developing programs that drive significant business growth and pipeline generation</li><li>Comfortable operating in fast-paced environments with a high degree of autonomy, a bias for action, and the ability to manage multiple complex projects simultaneously</li><li>Experience leading go-to-market strategies and products, including the creation and/or direction of demos for revenue, field, and event teams</li><li>Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to influence stakeholders at all levels, including senior leadership</li></ul>
