### Description
PLANFOX is dedicated to shaping the digital future of healthcare by developing smart, networked software solutions that simplify processes, improve information quality, and create more time for people. Born from a research cooperation with the University of Augsburg and rooted in the XITASO Group, we combine scientific expertise with technological know-how and a passion for digital health. As a Technical Implementation Specialist, you will be a key player in the successful deployment of our PLANFOX Patient Portal, ensuring seamless integration into hospital IT infrastructures and actively contributing to its further development.

### Responsibilities
- Take joint responsibility with the team for the technical implementation of our cloud solution's introduction projects: the PLANFOX Patient Portal.
- Configure the patient portal's interfaces to precisely match the customer's third-party systems.
- Act as an expert for the configuration and architecture of the patient portal.
- Conceive technical solutions for the further development of our product landscape in close coordination with the implementation and development teams.
- Serve as a knowledge holder for all questions regarding integration into the IT infrastructure and processes of hospitals.

### Qualifications
- Demonstrable practical experience in IT or network technology, regardless of academic degree, is paramount.
- Knowledge of medical communication standards, with a strong focus on HL7v2 and HL7 FHIR, complemented by experience in healthcare projects.
- Familiarity with network technologies (HTTPS, TLS, TCP/IP, IPSec) to enable seamless integration of our cloud solution into hospital IT networks.
- A strong desire to contribute your expertise and commitment to a growing startup, actively shaping PLANFOX's future development steps.
- Analytical thinking and strong organizational skills for effective application in customer projects.
- Professional demeanor with customers and partners, coupled with a customer-oriented and independent work ethic.
- Proficiency in German to ensure smooth communication with our predominantly German-speaking customers.
