**About the role**
As a Principal Finance Analyst, you will get to:
*   Serve as a strategic finance business partner to sales VPs, primarily supporting the Central and East enterprise/corporate sales teams.
*   Provide guidance and recommendations to department and Executive leadership on their forecasts, budgets and all financial aspects of their operational decisions
*   Lead and participate in biweekly and executive sales commit calls, challenging and validating sales forecasts, pipeline, and results.
*   Drive improvements in sales forecasting, financial modeling, and reporting, leveraging Power BI, Excel, and AI tools.
*   Support annual budgeting for OpEx, revenue, and gross profit; track and communicate results to business partners.
*   Perform process improvements and provide recommendations
*   Create internal reports and dashboards. You will be recognized internally as a subject matter expert
*   Proactively identify data insights and value-add opportunities for sales leaders, not just reporting but influencing business decisions.
*   Gather, organize, and present complex financial data and information to several diverse stakeholders/audiences, including executive team and board members
*   Performs various ad-hoc analysis for leaders and key decision makers
*   Collaborate with sales compensation and deal desk teams to align strategy, compensation, and deal shaping.
