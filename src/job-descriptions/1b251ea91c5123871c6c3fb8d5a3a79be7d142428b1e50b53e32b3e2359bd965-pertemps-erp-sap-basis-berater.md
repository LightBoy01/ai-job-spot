### Description\nOur client, an internationally active, family-run corporate group based in Germany, is actively seeking experienced SAP Basis Consultants (m/f/d) to strengthen their existing team. This full-time, hybrid position offers a permanent contract in North Rhine-Westphalia. The role focuses on the comprehensive technological support and strategic evolution of their SAP S/4HANA landscape and connected cloud systems.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Provide technological support for the SAP S/4HANA landscape and integrated cloud systems.\n- Coordinate external service providers effectively.\n- Administer, monitor, and fine-tune system performance.\n- Offer technological assistance to the internal SAP team.\n- Lead and manage technology-related projects such as upgrades, carve-in/out, and archiving initiatives.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Possess several years of professional experience as an SAP Basis Consultant.\n- Demonstrate excellent knowledge of SAP S/4HANA and SAP BTP.\n- Have experience with modern cloud technologies (advantageous).\n- Exhibit strong communication and project management skills.\n- Be fluent in both German and English.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a senior SAP Basis professional to join a stable, international family business that prioritizes employee growth and work-life balance. You'll gain significant influence over critical system landscapes, leading vital projects and leveraging your expertise in SAP S/4HANA and cloud technologies. With attractive benefits, continuous development, and flexible working models, this role is perfect for someone eager to make a tangible impact and advance their career.
