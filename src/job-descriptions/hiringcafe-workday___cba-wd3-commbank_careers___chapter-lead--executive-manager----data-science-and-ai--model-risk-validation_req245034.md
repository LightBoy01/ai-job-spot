**Chapter Lead (Executive Manager) - Data Science and AI, Model Risk Validation**

*   You’re passionate about leading high-performing teams and validating cutting-edge AI models
*    We’re transforming financial services through scalable and ethical AI innovation
*   Together, we’ll deliver robust model validation frameworks that drive trust and impact

**Do Work That Matters**

At CommBank, we’re redefining the future of banking through world-class AI innovation. As a Chapter Lead in our AI & General Model Validation team, you’ll lead a talented group of data scientists across Australia and India, driving excellence in model validation and tooling development.

You’ll play a key role in shaping the future of AI governance, ensuring our models are safe, scalable, and aligned with customer and regulatory expectations.

**See Yourself in Our Team**

CommBank is leading new-to-the-world innovations. As part of our team, you’ll be empowered to shape the future of AI in financial services, working alongside top-tier talent in a collaborative, inclusive environment. Model Risk & Validation is part of the Enterprise Risk function and provides considered challenge to improve modelling outcomes.

We’re seeking an exceptional AI Chapter Lead (Executive Manager) to shape the future of AI model validation at scale. You’ll be part of a collaborative, forward-thinking team committed to ethical AI, innovation, and continuous learning at scale.

**Your Impact**

*   Lead a team of data scientists across Australia and India
    *   Recruitment, retention, coaching, and career development
    *   Planning, monitoring, and finalising model validation tooling and activities
    *   Ensuring quality and consistency in deliverables
    *   Occasional international travel may be required

*   Drive strategic transformation
    *   Lead development of tooling to accelerate validation workflows
    *   Oversee validation activities of AI models and embed changes to model validation frameworks

*   Collaborate with key stakeholders
    *   BU/SU-aligned data scientists, model owners, and monitors
    *   Centralised AI teams and acceleration stream leads
    *   Internal audit and governance bodies.

**We are interested in people with:**

**Leadership & Vision**

*   Proven experience in leading high-performing multidisciplinary teams with Gen AI, data science, and software engineering skill sets. Experience in mentoring junior data scientists and leading technical initiatives.
*   Project management experience in agile environments.
*   Enthusiastic about developing automation tools to drive continuous workflow improvements.
*   Strong stakeholder management skills, including engagement with General Managers.
*   Exceptional communication skills and growth mindset.

**Technical Excellence**

*   Proven hands-on experience as a data scientist or machine learning engineer, with recent successful applications of Generative AI in a business context.
*   Deep expertise in modern AI frameworks includes prompt engineering, RAG, guardrail design, agentic architectures.
*   Proficiency with industry standard tools like LangChain/LangGraph, OpenAI Agents SDK and Semantic Kernel.
*   Strong background in designing and implementing end-to-end ML workflow automation including custom tool development.
*   Hands-on experience with version control and CI/CD pipelines such as GitHub Actions.
*   Experience with LLM evaluation frameworks is desirable.
*   Knowledge in traditional machine learning models including regression, random forest and xgboost is desirable.
*   Knowledge of model risk governance and validation processes is desirable.
*   Degree in Data Science, Engineering, Computer Science, Statistics, Actuarial Studies, or related field.

_The ideal candidate will have expertise across multiple areas above, though we don't expect mastery in every technology listed._
