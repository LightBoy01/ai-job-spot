### Description\nThe marketing world is constantly evolving, demanding a fresh perspective on content and channels. As our Content Producer (m/f/d) at SmartMakers, you will be instrumental in transforming complex IoT & Asset Tracking topics into engaging alternative content formats, primarily video and audio, to captivate our target audiences and establish us as a Thought Leader in our markets. Your expertise in content production, combined with our industry know-how, will be key to achieving this mission.\n\nSmartMakers is dedicated to bringing clarity and transparency to our customers' supply chain and logistics processes. Our easily implementable, comprehensive solutions for IoT-based asset tracking help over 250 renowned manufacturing companies reduce search times, increase productivity, shorten lead times, minimize inventory, and ultimately decrease capital commitment. We offer a flexible work environment focused on results, with options for both office and mobile work, primarily from our modern office in Raumfabrik Karlsruhe. We provide unlimited contracts, diverse and responsible tasks in a growing team with a startup charm, and ample opportunities for training-on-the-job and co-creation on innovative, future-oriented products.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Long-form Content Production:** Conceptualize, technically prepare, and professionally record webinars, training videos, expert interviews, and events.\n*   **Short-form Content Creation:** Develop and realize short, attention-grabbing videos.\n*   **Channel-Specific Optimization:** Adapt content to meet the requirements of various channels.\n*   **Post-production:** Execute professional editing, sound design, color correction, and add text overlays, intros/outros, music, and motion graphics.\n*   **Quality Assurance:** Ensure the quality, consistency, and relevance of all content while adhering to our corporate design guidelines.\n*   **Visual Design Support:** Provide occasional support in the conception and creation of graphics, animations, and similar designs.\n*   **Marketing Campaign Support:** Contribute from idea generation to deployment across different channels.\n*   **Content Planning:** Collaborate with the Go-To-Market (GTM) team to identify relevant content formats.\n*   **Trend Monitoring:** Continuously observe trends and best practices in modern content creation, including testing new approaches within our context.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Proficient handling of Adobe Creative Cloud tools and similar software.\n*   In-depth knowledge of professional camera, lighting, and audio equipment.\n*   A keen eye for image composition, rhythm, and the ability to stage content captivatingly.\n*   Strong hands-on mentality: You spot a trend, identify an application within our context, and build a first draft.\n*   Structured, independent working style, and the ability to propose improvements for content formats and processes.\n*   Good understanding of online marketing channels and communication concepts to create suitable content.\n*   Completed degree or vocational training in a commercial, digital, marketing, or communication field.\n*   Specialized knowledge in logistics or industry would be a significant plus.\n*   Very good German language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis Content Producer role at SmartMakers offers a fantastic opportunity for a creative and proactive individual to significantly impact the company's brand as a thought leader in the dynamic IoT and asset-tracking space. The emphasis on video and audio production, combined with the encouragement for trend observation and hands-on experimentation, means you're not just executing tasks but actively shaping the content strategy. The company fosters a supportive \"Du-Kultur\" with flat hierarchies, valuing individual contribution and providing flexibility in working hours and location. With an unlimited contract, diverse responsibilities, and training-on-the-job, it’s an ideal environment for someone looking for growth and a chance to make a tangible difference in the digital transformation of businesses.
