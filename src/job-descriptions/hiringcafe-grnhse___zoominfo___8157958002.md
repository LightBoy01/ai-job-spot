ZoomInfo is where careers accelerate. We move fast, think boldly, and empower you to do the best work of your life. You’ll be surrounded by teammates who care deeply, challenge each other, and celebrate wins. With tools that amplify your impact and a culture that backs your ambition, you won’t just contribute. You’ll make things happen–fast.

We’re looking for a Design Technologist to join our fast-paced design team at ZoomInfo. This role sits at the intersection of UX design, frontend development, and AI-driven prototyping. You’ll bring strong design foundations with tools like Figma, pair them with coding fluency in modern frameworks, and help us pioneer the next generation of AI-powered user experiences. If you’re excited about creating agentic experiences, experimenting with LLM-driven interaction models, and using tools like Claude Code, V0, or Lovable in your design process, this is the role for you.

**What You'll Do:**

*   Design and prototype interactive, AI-enhanced user experiences across ZoomInfo’s product ecosystem.
*   Use Figma and traditional design tools to create elegant, production-ready assets for the design system.
*   Build and test prototypes with modern web frameworks (React, Next.js, etc.).
*   Explore and integrate LLM-powered interaction patterns into user workflows.
*   Leverage AI-assisted design tools (like Cline, Claude Code, V0, Lovable) as part of everyday prototyping.
*   Contribute to design system components, bridging visuals and coded implementations.
*   Translate complex AI capabilities into intuitive, human-centered interfaces.  
      
    

**What You Bring:**

*   2+ years of design experience with tools like Figma, Sketch, or Adobe XD.
*   Hands-on experience with frontend coding (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, or similar).
*   Strong understanding of interaction design, prototyping, and usability principles.
*   Regular use of AI-driven prototyping tools (Claude Code, V0, Lovable, or similar).
*   Curiosity about and comfort with designing for language-model-powered experiences.
*   Portfolio demonstrating design craft _and_ technical implementation.

**Preferred Qualifications**

*   Experience designing LLM-powered or agentic user interfaces.
*   Comfort discussing AI UI paradigms and experimentation approaches.
*   Familiarity with data visualization or workflow design.
*   Experience with APIs, data integrations, or rapid prototyping environments.
*   Knowledge of design systems and coded component libraries.
*   Experience with motion design
