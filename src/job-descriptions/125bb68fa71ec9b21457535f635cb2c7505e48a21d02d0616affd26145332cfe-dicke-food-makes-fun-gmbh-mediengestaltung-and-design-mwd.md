### Description\nDie Dicke Gourmet Konzepte GmbH & Co. KG is a medium-sized, owner-managed company based in Wuppertal, Germany. We specialize in developing concepts for fresh food and supporting manufacturers of selected gourmet specialties in optimally presenting their products on the German market.\n\nTo strengthen our team, we are looking for an engaged personality in the field of media design and general design.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Design and edit image material, including product photos, social media visuals, and simple print layouts.\n- Develop appealing graphics, layouts, and digital assets for various channels.\n- Cut and prepare short video formats, such as product clips and social media content.\n- Maintain and further develop our website using CMS systems.\n- Support smaller marketing tasks, including content preparation and social media posts.\n- Plan and implement visual materials for trade fair appearances.\n- Accompany selected trade fairs, providing stand support and creating content on-site.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed vocational training or equivalent university experience in media design, graphic design, or a related field.\n- Proficient in using common graphic design tools (e.g., Adobe Creative Cloud, Canva, or alternatives).\n- Routine experience with CMS systems (e.g., WordPress, Webflow, or similar).\n- A strong eye for aesthetics, brand impact, and clear design principles.\n- Reliable, structured way of working with an enjoyment for independent tasks.\n- Experience with food or lifestyle products is welcome but not mandatory.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a creative individual to join a dynamic and growing company in the gourmet food sector. The role offers a diverse range of responsibilities, from digital content creation to event support, ensuring no two days are the same. With a motivated team, short decision-making paths, and direct collaboration with management, you'll have ample room to contribute your own creative impulses and see your work come to life. The company values a supportive atmosphere and provides thorough onboarding, making it an ideal environment for professional growth and impact.
