### Description\nOur medical technology client in the Wedel area is seeking qualified candidates for a permanent direct employment position (no temporary agency work) as a **Service and Commissioning Technician for Electronic Systems (m/f/d)**. This role involves maintaining and commissioning complex electronic, opto-electronic, electromechanical, and electropneumatic systems used in medical technology.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Independently test, repair, and maintain complex individual and series devices in optical measurement technology, electronic, electromechanical, or electropneumatic devices/systems.\n- Install, configure, commission, maintain, and repair plants and systems, including independent troubleshooting and rectification according to circuit, current, and system diagrams.\n- Define the scope of repairs, including determining working hours and material consumption.\n- Install, configure, and parameterize software.\n- Conduct training for operating and service personnel.\n- Analyze, eliminate weaknesses/errors in devices, systems, and installations and document them as a change proposal (ECR).\n- Produce spare parts according to bills of material, circuit, and wiring diagrams.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 3.5 years of specialized vocational training, typically as an electronics technician for devices and systems or as a mechatronics technician.\n- Several years of professional experience.\n- Quick comprehension of complex structures, strong ability to maintain, repair, or assemble systems even without service/production instructions.\n- Knowledge of analog and digital circuit technology, as well as microprocessor technology.\n- Open manner in dealing with changing colleagues and customers.\n- Confident customer-oriented appearance.\n- Car driver's license and high willingness to travel (DACH region, other EU countries).\n- Proficient in common Office tools.\n- Good English language skills, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an experienced and technically skilled Service and Commissioning Technician to join a medical technology company. The role offers significant autonomy, direct customer interaction, and a chance to work with complex, cutting-edge systems. If you thrive on problem-solving, continuous learning, and travel, and have a strong background in electronics or mechatronics, this position offers a challenging and rewarding career path. The requirement for good English skills highlights the international scope of the role and the company's global reach.
