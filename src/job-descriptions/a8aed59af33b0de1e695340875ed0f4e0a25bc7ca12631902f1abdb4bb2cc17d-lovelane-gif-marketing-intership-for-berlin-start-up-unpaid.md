Work for an innovative start-up that want to spread more love!

Our product

We want to bring more love into the world (and especially into relationships). Self-care was yesterday, couple-care is next! We are creating a game app for couples so that they can deepen their relationship while having a lot of fun.

Our vision is to become a platform for all couple-related topics.

Tasks
-----

Creating and posting gifs so that couples can find and use them when texting their partner. Gifs are a great way to build and expand our brand.

Check out our gifs by typing in "soynmochi" in whatsapp gif search or on tenor website.

You will work with the founder to:

*   Brainstorm and create texts for gifs
*   Research and define hashtags we should use to post gifs
*   Upload gifs to platforms tenor and giphy
*   Review gif performance and adjust our strategy

Requirements
------------

It doesn't matter what you study - we're more interested in actual hands-on experiences

*   You have some experience in social media marketing esp. around researching hashtags
*   You like to design things quickly and not necessarily perfectly
*   You are available for at least 2 months (full-time) or 3 months (part-time, at least 20 hours/week).

Benefits
--------

*   You have a lot of creative freedom
*   You will learn how a start-up works and gain your first practical experience
*   Very flexible and free way of working and working hours
*   Completely remote possible

As we are boostrapped, this is an **unpaid internship and there will be no option for a paid position unfortunately.**
