Perficient has a career opportunity for a Product Owner with Contact Center experience to join our Product Development Consulting Business Unit located in the United States. This role is primarily office-based at a local Perficient location, with travel and on-site client engagements expected as needed.

Job Overview:

The Product Owner will have complete responsibility for the day-to-day management of the engagement, including all aspects related to managing a development team, resulting in a successful delivery and a satisfied, reference-able client. 

The Product Owner role will focus on leading client interactions with Product Managers and business stakeholders, understanding both client business requirements as well as end-user functional requirements, and may manage multiple product owners that have delivery targeted towards a single product or solution. In situations where a Product Owner is supporting multiple delivery streams, that person has sole responsibility and ownership of the product in its entirety.

About Us:

Our Product Development team drives innovation by building digital solutions through a full end-to-end development process. Utilizing modern platforms like Azure, AWS, and Google Cloud Platform, we create customer-focused products, internal workflow tools, and mobile apps that elevate business operations across industries. With a clear focus on results and growth, our team is committed to doubling our global presence while delivering meaningful outcomes for renowned clients. We also embed AI into our solutions to enable smarter features, automate complex workflows, and deliver intuitive, data-driven user experiences.

### Responsibilities

*   Lead product vision and strategy for your product team.
*   Engage with stakeholders, clients, and product managers in scope definition and requirements refinement
*   Write robust user stories that show your understanding of the product, the technical limitations, personas and acceptance criteria..
*   Develop deep knowledge of users, formulating hypotheses & advocating product direction and roadmap.
*   Act as the subject matter expert for your product; including awareness of the related technology stack and how the market and our customers use and extract business value from your product(s).
*   Define product roadmaps and drive Planning Increment objectives.
*   Assess risks and benefits of product enhancements and gain alignment with stakeholders, clients, and product managers.
*   Analyze product metrics and define continuous improvement initiatives to increase the Product’s business value.
*   Introduce new approaches and tool sets that will allow the product development team to better understand the solution criteria and requirements.

### Qualifications

*   7+ years of working experience as a Product Owner who is used to working in a high performing Scrum environment. 
*   2+ years of consulting industry experience, with strong relationship management, and account management experience.
*   Strong experience with Twilio and chatbot contact centers, preferably with background in service with IVR.
*   Highly proficient in collaborating with project stakeholders, end-users, business partners, and technical team members to deliver high quality products.
*   Demonstrated ability to leverage AI tools to enhance productivity, streamline workflows, and support data-informed task execution. 
*   Familiarity with AI-enhanced platforms is a plus. 
*   A solid understanding of AI capabilities and limitations including ethical considerations is expected.
*   Experience working directly with creative team members and development engineers.
*   Solid presentation skills who is highly proficient in collaborating with project stakeholders, end-users, business partners, and technical team members to deliver high quality products.
*   Concrete understanding of writing high quality user stories.
*   Ability to define high-level business objectives and drive product roadmaps.
*   Comfort engaging with executive stakeholders in dialogue regarding product vision and business.
*   Bachelor’s degree or foreign equivalent required from an accredited institution.
*   Wide variety of technical knowledge of various systems (technology).
*   Experience in agile and scrum development organizations.
*   Certification - Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO) or Professional Scrum Product Owner (PSPO) is a plus.
*   Experience with identifying and reporting on key performance indicators to help visualize efficacy of product enhancements.
*   Expertise in product backlog tools.
*   Experience healthcare is a plus.
