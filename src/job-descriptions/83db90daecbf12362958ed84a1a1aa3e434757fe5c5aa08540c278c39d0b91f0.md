**Location:** San Francisco, CA (On-site) or Bangalore, India

**Employment Type:** Full-time

### About the Role

Our client is an innovative AI startup with $6.88 million in funding, dedicated to helping people land their dream jobs with tools like **AI Interview Copilot** and Mock Interviews. They are seeking a **Founding Fullstack Engineer** to build their next generation of consumer and business-facing products. This is a high-autonomy role for a strong builder who is comfortable writing scalable business logic on the backend and driving elegant frontend solutions, with a strong emphasis on leveraging **AI, LLMs, and agent systems**.

This role demands a high degree of independent technical decision-making and ownership across the entire product lifecycle.

### What You'll Do

*   **Write scalable business logic** in their cloud-native backend services.
*   **Drive and implement frontend solutions** for both consumer and B2B products.
*   **Contribute to and lead the product development process** from ideation and rapid prototyping to architecting and scaling solutions.
*   **Apply and adapt new AI knowledge/tech stack rapidly**, with a focus on LLMs and agent systems.
*   **Drive technical initiatives** based on business context, including research and technical design for long-term business OKRs.
*   **Maintain technical quality** through automated testing, code reviews, CI/CD pipelines, and handling on-call duties.
*   Perform day-to-day **DevOps** on cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, and Azure knowledge is optional).
*   **Drive projects without Tech Lead/CTO's guidance** with independent technical decisions.

**Requirements**

*   **1-6 years** of industry experience as a fullstack software engineer working on complex consumer or B2B products.
*   **Proven track record of building with AI**, ideally LLMs or agent systems, and shipping these products to production.
*   Experience writing business logic in cloud-native backend services at scale.
*   _Bonus points:_ Hackathon winner, open-source contributor, or a strong GitHub commit history (1000+ commits/year).

**Benefits**

*   **Salary:** $200K - $400K
*   **Equity:** 0.5% - 3% (Strong Founding Equity)
*   **Visa Sponsorship:** Available for most visa types.
*   **Work Policy:** **In-person** in San Francisco or India, Bangalore
*   **Benefits include:** Lunch and dinner provided, Gym membership, and Commute allowance
