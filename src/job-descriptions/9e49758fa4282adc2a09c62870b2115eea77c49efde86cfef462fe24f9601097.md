**About the role**
------------------
International SOS is the world’s leading medical & travel security services company operating from over 1,000 locations in more than 80 countries with more than 11,000 multi-cultural medical, security, and logistics experts.
Our global services include medical and risk planning, preventative programs, in-country expertise, emergency response for travelers, expatriates, and their dependents of over 70 percent of the Fortune 500 companies.
The International SOS Consulting business unit and regional practices focus on designing, marketing, and delivering advisory services, programs, and managed services to our clients. The overall delivery framework comprises of: Advisory, programs and Managed services.
This role will lead the strategic design and operational delivery of realistic crisis simulations within the organization, with a particular focus on tailoring training programs to specific risk profiles.
**Key responsibilities**
------------------------
*   **Strategic scenario design**
Lead the creation and refinement of realistic crisis simulations tailored to organizational risks and geopolitical contexts. Ensure scenarios incorporate AI-driven insights and meet client preparedness objectives.
*   **Training program development & delivery**
Co-develop and deliver modular training programs featuring immersive, AI-enhanced crisis exercises, adapted to client maturity and needs. Oversee session planning and quality assurance.
*   **Client engagement, content development & cross-functional collaboration**
Engage regularly with clients to tailor crisis scenarios to their specific risk environments. Support content creation using AI-generated multimedia and collaborate closely
with crisis management, security, and commercial teams to ensure integrated, client-focused service delivery. Assist commercial teams in aligning solutions with client needs
and business goals.
*   **Mastery of internal simulation platform & technology integration**
Become a proficient operator of our proprietary digital crisis simulation tool, using it to design and deliver highly realistic and interactive crisis scenarios. Research and
integrate new technologies and AI applications to continuously enhance simulation immersiveness and relevance.
*   **AI innovation, compliance & research**
Explore and implement emerging AI technologies to enrich simulation content and realism, including AI-based image and video generation. Ensure full compliance with legal
frameworks governing AI use, data protection, and intellectual property in all simulation activities.
*   **Administrative backup & operational support**
Serve as the administrative backup and provide daily operational support to the Crisis Management Practice, ensuring continuity of service delivery and effective coordination
of practice activities.
**Geographical Scope:** Europe & Africa
