**About the role**

We are looking for a forward thinking Oracle DBA who will contribute to the evolution of the current database platforms  and who  understands both  business and overall IT frameworks within a financially regulated environment.

**What you’ll do here:**

*   You will help manage all environments related to the Oracle ecosystem  here at Intact.
    
*   Advocate and promote the transformation of the existing database platforms to a product centric operating model
    
*   Participate in the development and Governance of best practices and guidelines for new cloud based database solutions.
    
*   Contribute in the development of automation activities to improve daily database operations on Cloud Solutions.
    
*   Partner with the software engineering teams to help implement and support continuous integration (CI) and continuous deployment (CD) processes.
    
*   Participate in the continuous improvement of processes by providing ideas and best practices for DataOps teams within the Organization.
    
*   Apply the best practices and guidelines of the organizational and departmental change management process in relation to databases.
    
*   Act as a Database security champion to guide users on best practices around data usage and security.
    
*   Part of the overall on call support team.
    

**What you bring to the table:**

*   Bachelor’s degree in computer science or related field or any combination of equivalent education and experience.
    
*   5+ years of experience a wide range database technologies .
    
*   Current knowledge and technical understanding with 19C or 23AI, and Linux operating systems.
    
*   Working Knowledge of other database technologies SQL or document databases.
    
*   Experience implementing foundational databases cloud services -  and their integration with networking, security, account/organizations, logging & monitoring, identity access management, etc.
    
*   Experience implementing infrastructure as code using cloud formation and/or Terraform.
    
*   Experience in the Insurance or Financial service domain; Understands or willing to learn about our different lines of business and their evolving data needs.
    
*   Interest with the latest advancements in AI and the ability to leverage AI database technologies effectively.
    
*   An excellent team player - willing to learn and collaborate with a diverse and talented group of  IT professionals.
    
*   Open and transparent - able to communicate with both business and IT teams(Architecture, Audit,  Infrastructure and software engineering) within the organization.
    
*   Organized, proactive and a catalyst for change, who is driven by challenges and delivering results.
    
*   Good Knowledge on security best practices and understand the impacts they can have on a data workflows,
    
*   Thrives working in a constantly evolving technological environment.
    
*   No Canadian work experience required however must be eligible to work in Canada.
