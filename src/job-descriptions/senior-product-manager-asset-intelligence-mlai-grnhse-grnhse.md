<p>MaintainX is the world's leading Asset and Work Intelligence platform for industrial and frontline environments. We are a modern IoT-enabled cloud-based tool for reliability, safety, and operations on physical equipment and facilities. MaintainX powers operational excellence for 12,000 businesses including Duracell, Univar Solutions Inc., Titan America, McDonald's, Brenntag, Cintas, Xylem, and Shell.</p>
<p>We recently completed a $150 million Series D funding round, bringing our total funding to $254 million and valuing the company at $2.5 billion.</p>
<p><strong>We are looking for a Senior Product Manager with deep AI/ML experience to lead our Asset Intelligence efforts.</strong> Equipment downtime is one of the largest cost drivers in manufacturing. When machines go down, production stops, leading to missed outputs, lost revenue and expensive, emergency maintenance. MaintainX helps manufacturing customers maintain their facilities and keep their critical equipment online and operating efficiently. Your mission is to lead a cross-functional team of data scientists, machine learning, and application engineers in transforming the vast data we collect into predictive insights that help customers prevent failures, keep their production online, and optimize maintenance strategies.</p>
<p><strong>What you’ll do:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Lead product development for ML-powered predictive maintenance or similar use cases, collaborating with Data Science, Engineering, and Design to build and ship production-ready solutions.</li>
<li>Design intuitive UX that turns model insights into clear, actionable decisions for Reliability Engineers, Maintenance Managers and frontline Technicians.</li>
<li>Build scalable, generalizable products that deliver predictive value across a diverse customer base.</li>
<li>Define product strategy and drive the execution of cross-functional teams.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Translate complex, ambiguous user needs into product opportunities grounded in data and user research.</li>
<li>Partner with Go-To-Market teams to launch impactful capabilities and drive adoption.</li>
<li>Inspire and align your cross-functional team around a shared vision, roadmap, and success metrics.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>About you:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Has 5+ years of product management experience with a strong track record shipping ML/AI-driven products, ideally in B2B or industrial settings.</li>
<li>Has led cross-functional teams including Data Science, ML Engineering, and UX from concept to delivery.</li>
<li>Translates technical model outputs into intuitive product features and insights for non-technical users.</li>
<li>Brings an ML platform mindset to design reusable capabilities that scale across a broad product surface.</li>
<li>Has built and shipped ML-driven products that scale across customers and use cases.</li>
<li>Thrives in fast-paced, ambiguous environments, balancing MVP speed with long-term thinking.</li>
<li>Communicates clearly and builds trust across levels and disciplines - from execs to frontline users.</li>
<li>Understands and builds for hands-on, industrial users; manufacturing industry exposure a plus.</li>
</ul>
