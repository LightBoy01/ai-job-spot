### **_Cherry Ventures is supporting our portfolio with this hire._**

### Berlin based - m/f/d - full-time

### **About Plato**

Plato is the AI-powered Sales Intelligence platform for B2B wholesale. We turn reactive sales teams into proactive revenue engines by surfacing hidden opportunities in their data: who’s at risk, who’s ready to buy more, and what products to recommend next.

Wholesale is unlike e-commerce or SaaS: sparse data, huge transactions, complex catalogs, and long-term relationships. Our models help sales reps know which of their 200 customers to call today - insights that can save a €2M account or unlock €50K in new monthly revenue.

### **The Role: Sr. Data Scientist**

Build ML/AI systems for industries from construction to steel wholesale. This is not off-the-shelf ML: you’ll design models where success = revenue impact, not just accuracy.

**You’ll work on:**

*   **Production AI at Scale**: ML models across millions of transactions, 20+ enterprises, 500K+ SKUs.
*   **End-to-End Ownership**: From definition to deployment, evaluation, and A/B testing.
*   **Complex Challenges**: Sparse data, extreme imbalance, multi-objective optimization, scaling from SMBs to enterprises.

### **What Makes You Successful**

*   Strong ML fundamentals, production experience, distributed computing, and solid engineering practices
*   Problem-solving from first principles, comfort with ambiguity, and business pragmatism
*   Proven record with irregular time series, hierarchical data, actionable insights, and multi-tenant ML  
      
    

**Bonus:** B2B analytics, recommendation systems, supply chain, Databricks/Snowflake, MLOps, European context, German language.

### **Tech Stack**

PySpark, SQL, Python, Databricks, scikit-learn, OpenAI, AWS, Terraform, MLflow.

### **How We Work**

*   High ownership, low process: you ship end-to-end
*   Speed over perfection: ship v0, learn, iterate
*   Direct and intense: collaborate closely with product, sales, and customers
*   Small team, big impact: your models influence millions in revenue  
      
    

### **What Makes This Role Unique**

*   **Business Impact**: Models directly shape revenue and customer conversations
*   **Depth + Context**: Build explainable AI aligned with wholesale realities
*   **Scale + Complexity**: Multi-tenant challenges across vastly different customers
