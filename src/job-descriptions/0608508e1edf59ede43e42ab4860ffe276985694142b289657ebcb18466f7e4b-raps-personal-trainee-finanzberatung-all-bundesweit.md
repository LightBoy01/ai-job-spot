### Description\nThis unique opportunity is for aspiring financial and life consultants who seek a career with real impact, both for themselves and their clients. As a Trainee, you'll embark on a journey towards self-employment, building meaningful relationships and crafting personalized financial, pension, and life planning solutions. You'll guide clients through critical life phases, from career planning to retirement, ensuring their financial strategies align perfectly with their life goals. This role offers an entry point to take charge of your future, provide genuine value, and work closely with people.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Participate in an intensive four-month Trainee program, receiving individual guidance and specialized academy training.\n- Progressively prepare for your role as an independent consultant through continuous training, coaching, and targeted development.\n- Develop and manage your own client base, providing long-term support from initial career planning to retirement solutions.\n- Collaborate with clients to develop tailor-made financial, pension, and life planning concepts.\n- Engage in continuous professional development through regular training and seminars to maintain up-to-date expertise.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A completed degree, ideally with an economic or similarly relevant focus, or professional training in finance or insurance.\n- Excellent German language skills (C2 level) and the ability to clearly explain complex topics.\n- A genuine enjoyment of interacting with people, conducting conversations, and identifying individual needs.\n- Interest in economic issues and a commitment to continuous personal and professional development.\n- Strong initiative, self-confidence, and a desire to independently shape your professional career.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for ambitious individuals passionate about making a tangible difference in people's lives through financial and life planning. The comprehensive trainee program, coupled with strong mentorship and the promise of autonomy, makes it ideal for those who value continuous learning, flexibility, and building a self-reliant career. The competitive earning potential after the initial training period adds to the attractiveness of this path. If you're looking for a career that offers both purpose and independence, this role provides the perfect foundation.
