### Description\nTREELAX Partner is a rapidly growing software company revolutionizing tree care through digitalization. We provide specialized software to tree care businesses across Germany, making their operations simpler, more efficient, and modern. We're looking for an enthusiastic individual who understands technology but prioritizes people, eager to make complex topics understandable and guide hands-on professionals smoothly into the digital world.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- You will support new partner companies during **onboarding**, explaining processes, setting up modules, and ensuring a smooth start with our system.\n- You will plan your appointments **flexibly** according to our partners' availability – sometimes early, sometimes later in the day (with 2-7 days' notice).\n- You will support existing partners with questions regarding our systems, finding **pragmatic solutions**, often with an open ear and a dose of patience.\n- You will prepare **technical topics** for our development team in India in English and keep all stakeholders informed.\n- You have **high standards for your work results** and are aware of your responsibility as a crucial interface to our partner companies.\n- You work **independently**: when you take something on, it gets done – without anyone needing to follow up.\n\n### Qualifications\n- High **technical understanding** and experience with digital tools or ERP systems (Odoo experience is a plus, not a must).\n- Enjoyment in **explaining things** – patiently, practically, and at eye level with our partners.\n- Good **English skills** to clearly communicate technical points to our international team.\n- **Quick responsiveness** in support – you know that even small things are crucial for our partners in their daily work. We promise our partners a maximum response time of 24 hours (Mon-Fri).\n- **Independence and quality awareness**: You think proactively, act reliably, and keep your promises.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity to join a fast-growing, remote-first software company at the forefront of digitalizing the tree care industry. You'll have significant **autonomy and creative freedom** to shape how digitalization is implemented. The role offers a unique chance to work closely with real businesses, directly witnessing the impact of your work, within a **motivated, small team** that values quality, openness, and mutual trust. If you are a proactive problem-solver with a passion for guiding users through technical challenges and seek a **long-term perspective** where you can grow and take on responsibility, this role is a perfect fit.
