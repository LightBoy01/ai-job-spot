**Job Details:**

Job Description:
-----------------
Overall Responsibilities:
*   Manage a group of products under the business segments to ensure factory meets commit and product priorities in the Production Scheduling horizon thru monitoring factory execution as needed.
*   Own to work out and communicate the factory recovery plan to stakeholders when there is factory performance issue or excursion
*   Connect to GPO for supply analysis, transactional activities needed and be able to link back the result to the factory or stakeholders for supply analysis needed.
*   This includes working with GPO process owner on process capability and enhancement.
*   Participate in line execution issue (induced by Planning) and work out the solutions to ensure smooth factory execution and no impact to order support
*   Factory facing person to support product xRB excursion management.
*   Adequate technical capability and coordination skills in managing business needs and customer requirement.
*   Apply lean and drive continuous improvement in site owned activities for improved efficiency.
