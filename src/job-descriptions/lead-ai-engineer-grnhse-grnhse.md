<p><strong>Your objectives</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Solve the hardest problems with AI to deliver social and economic value</li>

<ul>

<li>Exceptional expertise in AI engineering, machine learning and data science</li>
<li>Practical hands-on experience with a broad range of tools to assist in the AI development lifecycle: LLMs, Jupyter, PyTorch, Tensorflow, Hugging Face Transformers, AWS Bedrock, AWS Sagemaker, Google Vertex AI, Azure ML, MLFlow, Dataiku, MS Fair, Google PAIR, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, Ansible, Datadog etc.</li>

<li>Strong ability to develop and maintain relationships amongst clients, colleagues, and partners</li>
