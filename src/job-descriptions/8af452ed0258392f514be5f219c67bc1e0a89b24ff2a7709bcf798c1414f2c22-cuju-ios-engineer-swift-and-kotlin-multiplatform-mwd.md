About CUJU

Make the Unseen seen! Our mission is to reinvent scouting in football and build the #1 global scouting platform for identifying and developing talent. With the help of modern AI technology, CUJU enables every young football talent to be seen – regardless of origin, gender, or social background. We create fair opportunities and transparent pathways into professional sports. Our platform connects athletes, clubs, and organizations worldwide to rethink scouting – data-driven, objective, and mobile. Together, we are shaping the next generation of global talent scouting.

**Your Mission:** As an experienced iOS Engineer, you'll play a key role in refactoring and evolving CUJU's flagship iOS app. You'll collaborate on our transition to Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP), adopting Compose Multiplatform for a unified, cross-platform UI. This is an exciting opportunity to shape the technical direction of our mobile apps and deliver seamless, high-quality experiences for football talents worldwide.

Tasks
-----

*   App Refactoring & Modernization: Lead the refactoring of existing iOS features, adopting best practices in Swift, Kotlin Multiplatform, and modular architecture.
*   Kotlin Multiplatform Integration: Collaborate on integrating KMP into core application functionalities, particularly for login and authentication.
*   Compose Multiplatform Adoption: Support future UI modernization initiatives, leveraging
*   Compose Multiplatform to create consistent, responsive, and scalable UIs across platforms. Bring our Design System to life.
*   Collaboration & Innovation: Work closely with Android, backend, and product teams to deliver unified app experiences and actively participate in defining architectural decisions.
*   Quality Assurance: Ensure high-quality, performant, and reliable code through rigorous testing, code reviews, and continuous improvement practices.

Requirements
------------

*   5+ years of professional experience as an iOS Engineer, with strong expertise in Swift and native iOS development.
*   Interest and experience with Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP) or a keen desire to learn and apply it.
*   Familiarity or interest in modern UI development techniques, including SwiftUI and Jetpack Compose.
*   Solid understanding of MVVM, modular architecture, and mobile development best practices.
*   Experience with automated testing, CI/CD, and version control systems.
*   Strong collaboration skills, capable of effectively working with remote, cross-functional, and international teams.
*   Fluent English and passion for delivering exceptional user experiences.
*   Enthusiasm for sport-tech and innovation in athlete performance analytics.
