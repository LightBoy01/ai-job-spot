**About AirOps**
----------------

Today thousands of leading brands and agencies use [AirOps](https://www.airops.com/) to win the battle for attention with content that both humans and agents love.

We’re building the platform and profession that will empower a million marketers to become modern leaders — not spectators — as AI reshapes how brands reach their audiences.

We’re backed by awesome investors, including Unusual Ventures, Wing VC, Founder Collective, XFund, Village Global, and Alt Capital, and we’re building a world-class team with in-person hubs in San Francisco, New York, and Montevideo, Uruguay.

**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------

*   Build AI growth workflows using AirOps for strategic enterprise customers while establishing scalable best practices and templates that can be leveraged across the team
    
*   Recruit, hire, onboard, and coach a team of Forward Deployed Engineers, evolving our services delivery playbook and resourcing model
    
*   Own customer success metrics and expansion revenue targets across your team's accounts; analyze trends and report insights to the Executive team
    
*   Partner with Growth, Marketing, Sales, and Product teams to influence roadmap and build strategic workflows aligned to customer needs and market opportunities
    
*   Drive executive relationships with key accounts through QBRs, architectural reviews, and strategic planning on complex implementations
    

**Experience**
--------------

*   4+ years experience in Solutions Engineering, Technical Account Management, Product, or similar technical leadership role at a high-growth B2B SaaS startup; we 💚 former (or future aspiring) founders
    
*   Deep experience with LLMs and AI techniques, including prompt engineering and production deployments, plus expert-level workflow automation (Zapier, etc.)
    
*   Strong analytical skills with experience using data to inform decisions and excellent project management skills using tools like Linear, Airtable, and Asana
    
*   Superb written and verbal communication skills with proven ability to influence cross-functional stakeholders and executive customers
