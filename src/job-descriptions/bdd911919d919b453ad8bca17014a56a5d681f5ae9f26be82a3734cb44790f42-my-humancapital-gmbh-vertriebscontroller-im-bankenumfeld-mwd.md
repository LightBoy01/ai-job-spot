### Description\nIn cooperation with our partner company in Munich, we are seeking a \"Sales Controller in the Banking Environment (m/f/d)\" to start as soon as possible. In this role, you will be crucial in strengthening the sales strategy by providing data-driven reports, customer potential analyses, and forecasts.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Create data-based reports, customer potential analyses, and forecasts to strengthen the sales strategy.\n- Monitor sales key figures, analyze target-actual deviations, and support goal achievement.\n- Strategically advise sales management on defining relevant action fields and accompany target planning processes.\n- Continuously deliver control data and KPI analyses to optimize sales success.\n- Be responsible for the technical and content-related support of sales and success recording systems, including workflow optimization and data integration.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed training as a Bank Clerk (m/f/d), Savings Bank Clerk (m/f/d), further training as a Bank Specialist (m/f/d), or a comparable qualification in Controlling/Sales.\n- Sound experience in sales controlling, data analysis, and reporting, ideally within a savings bank or banking environment.\n- Proficient in handling databases, BI tools, or ERP systems, and experience with sales systems (ideally).\n- Analytical, structured, and results-oriented work style; strong communication skills, teamwork ability, and customer orientation.\n- Very good German language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic long-term opportunity for a motivated Sales Controller looking to make a significant impact within a banking environment in Munich. The company prides itself on a supportive, appreciative \"Du-Kultur\" where trust and positive collaboration are paramount. With flexible working hours, home office options, and a strong commitment to continuous professional development, this role offers an excellent work-life balance and ample opportunities for growth in a modern work setting.
