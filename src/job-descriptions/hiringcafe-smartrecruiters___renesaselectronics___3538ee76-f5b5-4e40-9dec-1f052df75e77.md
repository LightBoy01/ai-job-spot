**Job Description:**

**About the Role**

We are seeking a **visionary and hands-on System Architect** to define and develop **next-generation robotic system solutions** with a focus on **perception, computer vision, and AI/ML integration**.

In this role, you will shape the **intelligence layer of robotics**, driving architectures that combine **sensors, compute, and AI algorithms** to enable advanced perception, decision-making, and autonomy. You will bridge **application requirements, robotics research, and semiconductor technology**, ensuring our solutions meet the performance, safety, and scalability demands of industrial, service, and humanoid robots.

**Key Responsibilities**

**System Architecture**

*   Define **end-to-end perception and AI architectures** for robotic systems (industrial, service, humanoid).
*   Architect **sensor fusion platforms** combining camera, LiDAR, radar, IMU, tactile, and proximity sensors.
*   Specify **AI/ML pipelines** for object detection, recognition, SLAM, scene understanding, and motion planning.
*   Define **HW/SW partitioning** between edge AI accelerators, MCUs/MPUs, and cloud-based inference.
*   Ensure **scalability, real-time performance, and safety compliance** for mission-critical robotic tasks.

**AI/Perception Development**

*   Guide **integration of AI models** (CNNs, transformers, reinforcement learning) for perception and control.
*   Support **vision-based navigation** (V-SLAM, semantic mapping, visual odometry) and **3D scene reconstruction**.
*   Collaborate with AI researchers and engineers to **optimize models for embedded deployment**.
*   Drive **benchmarking and validation** of AI algorithms against real-world datasets.

**Collaboration & Strategy**

*   Align with **product lines, silicon teams, and application engineers** to ensure system-level consistency.
*   Define **reference designs, SRDs, PoCs, and evaluation platforms** for robotic perception and AI use cases.
*   Track **trends in robotics AI (foundation models, multimodal AI, generative AI)** and shape solution roadmaps.
*   Partner with **universities, startups, and ecosystem players** to accelerate innovation in robotic perception and intelligence.

**Qualifications:**

*   Master’s or Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Robotics, or related field.
*   8+ years of experience in **robotics, embedded vision, or AI systems architecture**.

*   Proven expertise in:
    *   **Computer vision & perception** (image processing, V-SLAM, 3D vision, semantic segmentation).
    *   **Machine learning & deep learning** for robotics (PyTorch, TensorFlow, ONNX).
    *   **Sensor fusion** (camera, LiDAR, radar, IMU, tactile).
    *   **Embedded AI accelerators and edge computing architectures**.
    *   **Middleware & frameworks** (ROS/ROS2, DDS, GStreamer, OpenCV).
*   Strong knowledge of **real-time systems, safety standards (ISO 26262/13849, IEC 61508), and cybersecurity**.
*   Proficiency with **simulation tools** (Gazebo, Isaac Sim, MATLAB/Simulink).
*   Excellent **cross-functional leadership and communication skills**.

**Nice to Have**

*   Background in **reinforcement learning for robotics** or **learning from demonstration**.
*   Familiarity with **tactile sensing, haptics, and dexterous manipulation**.
*   Experience with **generative AI or multimodal AI applied to robotics**.
*   Knowledge of **energy-efficient AI architectures** for mobile robots.
