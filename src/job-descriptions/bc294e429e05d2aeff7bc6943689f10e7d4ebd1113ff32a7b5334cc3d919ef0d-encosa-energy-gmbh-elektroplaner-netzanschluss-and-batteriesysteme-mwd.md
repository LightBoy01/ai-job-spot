### Description\nencosa's mission is \"energy cost savings\" by helping companies reduce electricity costs and avoid CO2 emissions through battery storage and intelligent software. As a full-service provider, encosa handles everything from planning, grid connection, and certification to installation and commissioning.\n\nAs an Electrical Planner for Grid Connection & Battery Systems (m/w/d), you will create the complete technical design package for these projects. You will prepare SLD (Single Line Diagrams), protection and earthing concepts, and all relevant calculations, ensuring all documentation is norm-compliant and receives approvals from grid operators and certification bodies.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop SLD, protection coordination, short-circuit, and selectivity calculations.\n- Create cable dimensioning, earthing, and lightning protection concepts.\n- Compile technical documentation for grid operators, certification, and expert opinions.\n- Define bills of materials, specifications, and interfaces to transformer stations, RZA (Relay Protection System), and remote control technology.\n- Conduct design reviews with internal and external partners.\n- Ensure process and quality assurance in planning.\n- Prepare response packages for technical inquiries from grid operators.\n- Maintain and improve standards, templates, and calculation tools.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Degree in Electrical Engineering, Electrical Power Systems, or a comparable qualification.\n- Experience in the detailed electrical planning of energy systems, ideally PV or BESS (Battery Energy Storage Systems).\n- Proficient in VDE-AR-N 4105, VDE-AR-N 4110, and protection technology.\n- Knowledge of VDE-AR-E 2510-2 and VDE-AR-E 2510-50 is an advantage.\n- Proficient in CAD, calculation software, and clean technical documentation.\n- Team-oriented, meticulous, and quality-conscious.\n- German language skills at least B2, English language skills at least B2.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a high-impact role at the forefront of the energy transition, offering significant technical depth and responsibility within a dynamic startup environment. encosa provides a clear path for professional growth towards Senior Engineer or Lead Electrical Design, supported by a flexible hybrid work model and an attractive compensation package including a Virtual Stock Option Program. It's an excellent opportunity for an experienced Electrical Planner passionate about renewable energy and meticulous design to make a tangible difference.
