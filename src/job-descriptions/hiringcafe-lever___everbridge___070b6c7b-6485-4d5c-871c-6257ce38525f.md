Are you ready to grow your marketing career in a role that blends collaboration, technology, and impact? We have an exciting opportunity for an early-career Partner Marketing Associate to join the Everbridge Global Demand Center. Based in the UK, you’ll work with our EMEA & APAC go-to-market teams and Everbridge’s channel partners to deliver joint multichannel campaigns and events that drive measurable pipeline and revenue. This is your chance to make an impact at Everbridge—a fast-paced, B2B SaaS, high-growth company with a mission that matters: keeping people safe and organizations running. Our solutions help organizations stay connected, resilient, and prepared in an unpredictable world. Our clients and partners include some of the most recognized global brands and organizations.  
  
**What you'll do:**  
*   Collaborate with channel partners to plan and execute joint multichannel campaigns and events.
*   Build and deliver partner campaigns in Marketo, Salesforce, and AI tools, including emails, landing pages, and nurture programs.
*   Support list segmentation, data uploads, and database hygiene to ensure campaign accuracy and integrity.
*   Partner with internal teams and channel partners to maintain consistent messaging and smooth lead flow across all campaigns.
*   Manage and maintain partner lead data, including scrubbing, uploads, accuracy, and lead scoring support.
*   Analyse partner campaign performance, delivering insights to improve ROI and pipeline contribution.
*   Troubleshoot campaign workflows and provide ad-hoc support when needed.
  
  
**What you'll bring:**  
*   1+ years of B2B SaaS partner or demand generation marketing experience.
*   Hands-on experience with Marketo and Salesforce.
*   Curiosity and interest in AI, MarTech, and analytics tools, with a growth mindset to modernize and optimize demand generation.
*   Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
*   Solid project coordination skills—able to manage timelines, tasks, and stakeholders to keep campaigns on track.
*   Excellent communication skills; English fluency (written, verbal, and presentation) is essential.
*   Fluency in German is a plus, but not required.
*   Experience working with remote, global teams is desirable.
*   Bachelor or Postgraduate Degree in Marketing, STEM, or related field (or equivalent) preferred.
