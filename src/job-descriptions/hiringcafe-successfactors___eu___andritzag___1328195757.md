The Artificial Intelligence Engineer designs state-of-the-art software for autonomous control of industrial processes.

  
They apply a broad range of AI techniques confidently, including both symbolic and machine learning methods. They develop proofs of concept and new software products. They are skilled programmers themselves, but also able to specify designs for dedicated software developers.

  
The Artificial Intelligence Engineer is part of a diverse multidisciplinary team. Strong collaboration skills are essential.

  
They are an adept communicator and contribute as a subject matter expert to multiple ANDRITZ business units.

Responsibilities may include, but are not necessarily limited to the following:

  
Design and deploy symbolic and machine learning based AI solutions using appropriate algorithms and techniques.

  
Create and maintain comprehensive technical documentation, including system architecture diagrams, design specifications, and developer guidelines.

  
Provide guidance and mentorship to colleagues, fostering a culture of learning and continuous improvement within the development team.

  
Collaborate with operations teams to ensure the seamless integration of AI systems into the overall infrastructure, considering deployment, monitoring, and maintenance.

  
Stay updated on industry trends, emerging technologies, and best practices to incorporate relevant advancements into the software architecture.

Bachelor’s degree in computer science, engineering, math, or similar.

Familiarity with data structures, data modeling, and software architecture.

  
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
