<b>About 3E:</b>
We are a mission-driven company with the purpose to enable a safer, more sustainable world! 
3E provides a comprehensive suite of data, analytics, and software solutions for enabling and improving workplace &amp; chemical safety, product stewardship, supply chain management and sustainability. With over 35 years of experience, and 15 locations across North America, Europe, and Asia, 3E helps more than 5,000 customers in various sectors to achieve safety, sustainability, and speed to market.

Are you ready to shape the future?  <b>Come join us!&nbsp;</b>

At 3E, we are committed to creating a safer, more sustainable world—and technology is key to delivering that mission. As a <b>Senior Technical Support Engineer</b>, you’ll combine hands-on technical expertise with a proactive, ownership-driven mindset.
