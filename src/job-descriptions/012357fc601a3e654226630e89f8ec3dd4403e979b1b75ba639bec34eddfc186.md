### Description

At KPMG New Zealand, we are driven by a commitment to fuel prosperity for all New Zealanders, a mission guided by our core values of Integrity, Courage, Excellence, and Together, for Better. We are seeking a Senior Technical Business Analyst to be seconded to our innovative AI Team. In this pivotal role, you will act as the bridge between our business leaders and engineers, translating complex processes and policies into robust, testable requirements for cutting-edge AI-enabled solutions. You will champion governance to ensure our solutions are safe, compliant, and deliver tangible, measurable outcomes for the firm and our clients.

### Responsibilities

*   Lead discovery sessions to map current and future state processes, decision points, and escalation paths, producing clear and reusable artifacts.
*   Translate intricate business rules and policies into machine-readable specifications such as decision tables, structured requirements, and prompt/skill specifications.
*   Define comprehensive evaluation datasets, success metrics, and adversarial tests to validate solution quality, safety, and reliability.
*   Partner closely with engineers to shape solution options, integrations, non-functional requirements, and test plans.
*   Prepare and manage governance inputs, including risk assessments, data classification, access/RBAC, and privacy considerations, tracking approvals to completion.
*   Plan and execute UAT and pilot phases, coordinating training, communications, and change management activities to drive user adoption.
*   Measure and report on business impact (time, quality, cost) and agent performance (success and intervention rates).
*   Maintain a well-groomed backlog, manage RAID logs, and facilitate stakeholder communications to ensure alignment and project momentum.

### Qualifications

*   An "AI first" approach to problem-solving and work.
*   5+ years of experience as a Business Analyst or Product BA delivering technology-enabled change in complex environments.
*   Outstanding facilitation, requirements elicitation, and documentation skills, with the ability to turn ambiguity into crisp, testable acceptance criteria.
*   Proven experience with AI/automation or data-driven solutions, including defining evaluation sets and measurable outcomes.
*   Working knowledge of enterprise platforms and data (e.g., Power Platform, Azure, ServiceNow, SharePoint/Fabric), APIs/connectors, and identity/RBAC concepts.
*   Familiarity with AI governance, risk, and compliance processes (privacy, data protection, DLP/labeling) and their influence on design and rollout.
*   Comfortable partnering closely with engineers and product owners, with excellent communication skills for engaging senior stakeholders.
*   A growth mindset: curious, pragmatic, and outcome-focused.
*   Confidence with LLM/agent concepts such as human-in-the-loop, prompt patterns, tool/skill design, telemetry, and cost controls.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "The role is described as a 'secondment' to the AI team. What does this mean for the role's stability and integration within the team's long-term strategy?"**

That's a great question, and it gets to the heart of how we're building our AI capability. Think of this less as a temporary assignment and more as a strategic embedding. We know that the best AI solutions aren't built in a technical vacuum; they're born from a deep understanding of the business. By seconding a senior BA into the AI team, we're ensuring that business context, process rigor, and user needs are woven into the fabric of every project from day one. You won't be an outsider looking in; you'll be a core part of the team, shaping the strategy and ensuring that what we build is not just technically impressive, but fundamentally valuable and practical. It’s our way of ensuring innovation is directly tied to impact, and your role is central to that fusion.

**Another great question would be: "With the emphasis on governance and safety, how does KPMG balance robust compliance with the speed and agility needed to innovate in the AI space?"**

It’s a critical balance, and our approach is to treat governance not as a gate, but as a guardrail. Our value 'Together, for Better' really comes to life here. The person in this role isn't just handing off requirements to a compliance team; they are the champion for responsible AI within the development lifecycle. You'll partner with our risk and privacy experts from the outset, designing safety and ethics into the solution rather than trying to bolt them on at the end. This collaborative approach actually accelerates innovation because it gives the engineering team the confidence to build and experiment within a framework they know is safe and compliant. We're not about moving fast and breaking things; we're about moving smart and building trust.
