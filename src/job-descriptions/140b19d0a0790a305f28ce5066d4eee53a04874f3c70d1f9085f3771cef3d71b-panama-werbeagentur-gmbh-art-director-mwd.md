### Description
Panama is an owner-managed creative agency based in Stuttgart, challenging conventional notions (even Wikipedia's!) by delivering exciting projects across all media for fantastic clients. We believe that every individual makes a difference, and we're looking for someone exceptional to join our team. You don't need a visa to join us – just your outstanding skills and a passion for exceptional communication. We are currently seeking an **ART DIRECTOR (M/F/D)** to strengthen our growing agency.

### Responsibilities
*   Independently conceptualize diverse communication measures (offline & online).
*   Develop extraordinary ideas.
*   Work autonomously within a collaborative team environment.
*   Support the supervision of film and photo productions.

### Qualifications
*   Minimum of 3 years of professional experience in a creative agency, working on national and international brands with a strong digital background.
*   Experience in shooting (film & photo).
*   Ideally, experience in the creation and implementation of campaigns in the retail sector.
*   Confident sense for typography and good design.
*   Good German and English language skills.
*   Proficient handling of Creative Suite programs.
*   A mind full of creative ideas.

### Author's Take
Joining Panama means stepping into a pivotal role within a continuously growing agency that truly values its people. The culture here fosters a unique blend of independent work supported by a strong, collaborative team, all within flat hierarchies and a pleasant atmosphere. You'll gain exposure to a diverse client portfolio, working on major brands and internationally deployed campaigns.

Panama is deeply committed to employee growth, offering dedicated support for professional and personal development through its in-house "Panama-Academy" training events. Beyond professional advancement, the agency champions work-life balance with flexible working models, including two days of mobile working per week and the option for "Workation." You'll also enjoy 30 days of vacation, comprehensive health management with sports courses, and various social perks like fresh fruit, coffee, and even after-work beers. The central Stuttgart location is easily accessible, and for sports enthusiasts, bike leasing and "Panama Atletico" sports groups (football, running, skiing trips) create a vibrant, community-focused environment. This is a place where you can make a real difference, grow your skills, and truly enjoy your work-life balance.
