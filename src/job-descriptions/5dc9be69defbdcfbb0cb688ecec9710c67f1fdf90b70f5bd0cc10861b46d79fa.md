**About the Role:**
This Lead AI Software Engineer role will focus primarily on Panorama’s generative AI products, Solara and Class Companion. Solara is a secure, district-customizable AI platform that helps educators quickly generate resources like intervention plans, family communications, and lesson ideas, all while safeguarding student privacy. Class Companion, integrated within Solara, brings real-time AI feedback and tutoring into classrooms, offering instant rubric-aligned feedback, individual student support, and educator insights. In this role, you will lead the design and implementation of AI-powered features across these products to support educators in improving student outcomes.
Our Lead Software Engineers are engineering leaders who use their technical skills and knowledge to support a squad of engineers through mentorship, collaboration, and role modeling. They serve as bridges among engineering and partners across product, design, and other Panorama teammates. They continuously work to align their squad with Panorama’s broader goals, and they contribute to the creation and evolution of those goals. Lead engineers work as engineers on a squad, and they influence the team by elevating voices, mentoring, modeling best practices, and building alignment towards key technical and organizational decisions.

*   Evaluate emerging technologies to inform and accelerate product innovation

*   Mentor teammates by sharing technical expertise, best practices, and domain knowledge

*   Share industry and Panorama best practices to strengthen squad strategy and improve delivery outcomes
