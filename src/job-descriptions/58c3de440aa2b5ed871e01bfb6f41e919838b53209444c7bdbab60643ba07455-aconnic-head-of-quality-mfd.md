### Description\nThis is a full-time, permanent position based in Hartmannsdorf, starting immediately. Are you seeking a responsible leadership role in an international company, with the chance to evolve existing structures and make your mark? Seize this opportunity to succeed our long-standing Head of Quality. Together with your team, you will ensure that quality is not just inspected, but truly lived within our organization.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Strategically and operationally lead the Quality Management, Quality Assurance, and Quality Control departments as Head of Quality.\n- Take responsibility for quality controls, initial sample inspections, and component acceptances.\n- Evaluate the quality of components and make well-founded decisions in case of quality deviations.\n- Systematically apply problem-solving methods such as Ishikawa or 8D reports.\n- Manage ESD (Electrostatic Discharge) including measurements, training, and calibrations.\n- Analyze and evaluate complaints, then initiate sustainable corrective measures.\n- Actively contribute to the further development and maintenance of our management system.\n- Create, maintain, and manage QS-specific documentation.\n- Lead, train, and develop your team both professionally and personally.\n- Plan and accompany internal and external audits, ensuring quality goals and budgets are met.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Hold a technical degree (e.g., Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics, or related fields) or a comparable qualification.\n- Possess at least 5 years of professional and leadership experience in quality management.\n- Bring profound knowledge of quality methods and standards (e.g., ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 50001).\n- Be proficient in written and spoken English.\n- Convince us with your strong communication skills, decisive nature, and team spirit.\n- Act self-initiatively, responsibly, and think entrepreneurially.\n- Be willing to travel occasionally to support our international sites.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for an experienced Quality leader to step into a pivotal role within a growing international telecommunications company. Taking over from a long-standing incumbent, you'll inherit a solid foundation while being empowered with significant scope to innovate, implement your ideas, and shape the future of quality. The culture appears highly supportive, emphasizing flat hierarchies, direct collaboration with management, and substantial investment in employee growth and well-being, including professional development and work-life balance benefits like sabbaticals. If you're passionate about leading a motivated team, driving strategic quality initiatives, and making a tangible impact, this role offers both responsibility and exceptional development potential.
