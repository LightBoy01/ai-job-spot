Lummus Technology is the global leader in developing technology solutions that make modern life possible and focus on a more sustainable, low carbon future. We license process technologies in clean fuels, renewables, petrochemicals, polymers, gas processing and supply lifecycle services, catalysts, proprietary equipment and digitalization to customers worldwide.
We are currently seeking a talented AI Engineer to join our Advanced Intelligence Group (AIG). The AIG will focus on exploring ways to utilize AI within Lummus Technology to empower our business units and fundamentally transform how our company operates. This position will work directly with our world-class experts in process engineering, design, and project management to solve their toughest challenges. Our goal is to augment our people, freeing them from repetitive work so they can focus on the high-value, creative problem-solving that has made Lummus a global leader. If you're driven to see your work come to life in tangible, industrial-scale applications and to weave your own thread of innovation into our company's future, we want to talk to you.
**
We are unable to consider sponsorship for authorization to work, for this role.  All applicants must be US citizens, or current green card holders.**
Key Responsibilities
--------------------
*   Analyze the core challenges of the business units and help translate those needs into technical specifications for AI solutions.
*   Build and deploy a wide range of machine learning and generative AI models to solve complex problems.
*   Support and maintain the data pipelines required for our AI systems, ensuring data quality and readiness for model training and inference.
*   Build out AI solutions and assist in the integration of AI models into existing Lummus business applications and workflows using APIs and other services to create seamless user experiences.
*   Fine-tune and maintain large language models (LLMs) and vision models on our proprietary data, with a focus on performance, accuracy, and IP security.
*   Support the full lifecycle of AI applications, from initial coding, prototyping, and experimentation to deployment, monitoring, and continuous improvement.
*   Foster innovation by researching new techniques and technologies to stay at the forefront of the rapidly evolving AI landscape.
Skills, Knowledge & Expertise
-----------------------------
*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering or other engineering discipline, Mathematics, Data Science, or a relevant field.
*   Minimum 3 years of experience in computer or software engineering.
*   Proficiency in Python and its data science ecosystem (Pandas, NumPy, others).
*   Experience with ML frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
*   Experience supporting modern GenAI stacks.
