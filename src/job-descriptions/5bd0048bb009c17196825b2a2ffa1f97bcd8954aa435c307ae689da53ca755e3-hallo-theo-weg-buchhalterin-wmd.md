### Description\nAs a WEG Accountant at hallo theo, you will take on a key role in an environment that rethinks real estate management. You will work in a highly digitized setup that radically simplifies processes, continuously improves them, and gives you space to develop, take responsibility, and achieve real impact.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Independently manage ongoing rental and WEG accounting, including posting all business transactions, reconciling accounts, and maintaining master data.\n- Fully process invoice and payment transactions, including verification/coding, payment runs, allocability, and preparation for invoice audits.\n- Prepare all relevant statements: service charge, operating cost, and utility bills, monthly and annual financial statements, business plans, income/expense statements, and VAT pre-registrations.\n- Take responsibility for owner and tenant changes, including deposit settlements and processing property transfers.\n- Act as the central contact person for WEG administrators, owners, advisory boards, service providers, and authorities, and manage an independent, fair, and service-oriented dunning process.\n- Actively shape our digital accounting processes: utilize modern tools, continuously optimize, and collaborate on projects to further develop our WEG and rental accounting.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed commercial vocational training or degree in Accounting, Finance, or Real Estate Management.\n- Initial experience in WEG, rental, or real estate accounting.\n- Knowledge of accounting/payment transactions, WEG law, real estate, and receivables management is an advantage.\n- Very good affinity for numbers, structured way of working, and high reliability.\n- Enjoy professional interaction with owners, tenants, and partners; service-oriented demeanor.\n- Experience with \"impower\" is a plus, but not a prerequisite.\n- You don't have to meet all points – what's more important to us is a \"Growth Mindset\" and the willingness to take responsibility.\n\n### Author's Take\nhallo theo offers an exciting opportunity for an experienced WEG Accountant eager to revolutionize real estate management through digitalization.
