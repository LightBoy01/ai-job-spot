**Req ID:** 343967 

NTT DATA strives to hire exceptional, innovative and passionate individuals who want to grow with us. If you want to be part of an inclusive, adaptable, and forward-thinking organization, apply now.

We are currently seeking a UX Designer with AI Focus to join our team in Bangalore, Karnātaka (IN-KA), India (IN).

Job Responsibilities:
---------------------

*   Conduct user research and surveys to inform the design process.
*   Develop wireframes and prototypes to effectively communicate design concepts.
*   Create user-centered designs that align with business goals and user needs.
*   Utilize expertise in using Figma and other design tools.
*   Apply information architecture techniques to organize and structure content.
*   Use visual communication skills to create engaging and intuitive interfaces.

Job Requirements:
-----------------

*   Proven experience in UX design with a focus on AI-driven applications.
*   Strong knowledge of information architecture, wireframing, prototyping, and visual communication techniques.
*   Proficiency in Figma and other industry-standard design tools.
*   Ability to conduct user research and translate findings into actionable design insights.
*   Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
*   Attention to detail and a strong sense of aesthetics.
