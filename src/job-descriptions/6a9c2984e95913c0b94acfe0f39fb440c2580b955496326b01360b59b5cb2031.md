**What you will do**

**AI Automation**

*   Proactively identify automation opportunities across all operational workflows
*   Design and implement automation solutions.

**Infrastructure Monitoring, Compliance & Deployment Management**

*   Collaborate with development teams to ensure seamless service operationalization from development to production
*   Implement comprehensive monitoring solutions with real-time alerting and observability
*   Maintain strict license compliance verification for all provisioned services
*   Lead up-front planning, scheduling, and deployment of releases to production environments following ITIL change management framework.

**Process management**

*   Design processes aligned with business objectives and industry best practices
*   Develop comprehensive adoption strategies with stakeholder engagement, training programs, and success metrics
*   Monitor process effectiveness through KPIs and analytics
*   Continuously optimize workflows through feedback analysis and iterative improvements to ensure consistent achievement of business goals and operational efficiency.

**What you bring**

Bachelor’s degree in computer science or a related field

\- Strong coding skills (Python)

 - Experience with AI-Powered automation

 - Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure or GCP)

 - Experience with REST APIs and microservices

 - Strong ability to work effectively across multiple teams and departments

 - Confident communication skills in English (both written and spoken)

 - Commitment to staying updated with latest technologies and industry best practices

 - Process-oriented person who seeks for the most efficient solutions

 - Analytical thinking and creative problem-solving abilities

 - Knowledge of ITIL framework and service management best practices
