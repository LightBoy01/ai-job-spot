<p>As a Product Designer, you’ll help leverage emerging technology to reimagine the experience of editing video. You’ll own the end-to-end design of new features and evolution of existing ones in the generative/agentic space.</p>
<p>You’ve got a passion for creating novel experiences and experience collaborating closely with user research, product management, engineering, and other designers to achieve the right mix of novelty and usefulness.</p>
<p>Our product is an intricate web of interconnected features, which means designers at Descript need strong&nbsp;<strong>systems design</strong> thinking. You’ll need to know how to find the right fit for new features in an existing product and have a strong intuition for when to push at system boundaries.</p>
<p>Join us to help build the future of creative tools.</p>

<strong>Responsibilities</strong>
<ul>
<li>Play a key role in a feature team, driving design decisions and collaborating with stakeholders and fellow designers to shape innovative video editing experiences</li>
<li>Set a high bar for craft, eye for detail, and deep empathy for users</li>
<li>Own the design process from concept through to execution</li>
<li>Bring clarity to problems that are broad and ambiguous in scope, integrating user, business, and technical constraints through structured thinking and effective communication</li>
<li>Contribute to the definition and expansion of our design system</li>
<li>Collaborate deeply with diverse stakeholders (product management, engineers, designers, executives)</li>
<li>Advocate for what you think is right, know when to disagree and commit</li>
</ul>

<strong>Requirements</strong>
<ul>
<li>4+ years as a designer, the majority of it spent in product</li>
<li>You have a portfolio demonstrating your ability to create net new features from scratch as well as your systems thinking</li>
<li>Exceptional interaction and visual design skills—the ability to create delight without sacrificing usability</li>
<li>Understanding of AI/ML and some experience designing for it</li>
<li>Experience designing web, mobile or desktop applications with workflows and systems design challenges</li>
<li>Experience on a fast-growing team and ability to work through ambiguity and create clarity</li>
<li>Clear, direct communication</li>
<li>Self-direction, you own your own work and deliver on your commitments</li>
<li>Contribute actively in team crit—know how to give and receive feedback well</li>
<li><strong>At least 2 of the following stand-out skills</strong></li>
</ul>

Stand-out skills
<ul>
<li>Past work building and/or working on a creation tool (e.g., word processor, design tool, audio or video editing tool)</li>
<li>Experience building or working on a mature design system</li>
<li>Expertise in accessibility</li>
<li>Comfort with designer-led user research</li>
<li>An interest in Vibe coding (or, hey, code coding) for prototyping</li>
<li>Finding new tools and processes (including AI) to improve the design process</li>
<li>UI animation skills</li>
</ul>
