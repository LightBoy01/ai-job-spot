Company Description
-------------------

Erste Group was founded in 1819 as the first Austrian savings bank and today it is one of the largest banking groups in Central and Eastern Europe (CEE). As an attractive employer, Erste Group offers interesting career opportunities in an international environment.

Department Description
----------------------

The team „ Group Total Rewards Management & Reporting“ is part of the department “Head of Group Remuneration, HR Legal, Digital & Reporting” and as such responsible for Job Architecture, Compensation & Benefits as well as HR Reporting. The team interacts as a center of competence with clients such as HR Business Partners, Managers as well as Members of the Executive Board.

**Your Tasks**
--------------

*   Dive into diverse HR topics, with a special focus on Rewards Management and Job Levelling frameworks
*   Create and enhance reports and dashboards on key HR metrics to support data-driven decisions
*   Support the preparation of presentations for various stakeholders
*   Collaborate and advise internal customers, working in close co-operation with other departments
*   Assist in local and group-wide projects, including job levelling and HR tool maintenance, ensuring data accuracy and usability across HR

**Your Background**
-------------------

*   Ongoing studies in economics, human resources or data analytics at a University/University of Applied Sciences
*   Ideally, you already have some work experience in HR
*   You have very good data analyzing skills, a hands-on mentality, and enjoyment in working with people, while handling confidential data with discretion
*   You are fluent in English and German
*   You have well advanced IT and MS Office skills (especially Excel/PowerPoint); experience with AI tools or programming is an advantage
