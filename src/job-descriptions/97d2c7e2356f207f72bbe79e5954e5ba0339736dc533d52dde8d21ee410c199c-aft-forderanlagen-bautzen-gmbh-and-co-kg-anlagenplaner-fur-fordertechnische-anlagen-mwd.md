### Description\nThe AFT-Group is a successful system partner for material flow solutions, operating internationally with renowned automotive manufacturers, in clinics, and across various industries. Our product spectrum encompasses floor conveyor technology, storage technology, and overhead conveyor technology. With 300 employees worldwide, we plan, sell, and construct new systems or expand existing facilities, always tailored to individual customer requirements. Through the development of innovative techniques, the AFT-Group has been a valued partner for intralogistics projects for over 30 years.\n\nAs a Plant Planner for Conveyor Systems in the Layout department, you will, under the guidance of a group leader, be responsible for the timely and technically accurate creation of layouts in the areas of conveyor technology, steel construction, and safety technology. Furthermore, you will actively support the planning, elaboration, and continuous development of projects, as well as the maintenance of our internal knowledge management.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Plan and develop conveyor systems for our customers in the automotive industry and other industrial sectors.\n- Create layouts and technical drawings using CAD software.\n- Coordinate and align with internal and external partners to ensure optimal solutions.\n- Monitor project progress and ensure compliance with deadlines and budgets.\n- Support during the commissioning and optimization of systems on-site.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in Mechanical Engineering or a comparable qualification.\n- Experience in the planning and project engineering of conveyor systems.\n- Good knowledge of CAD software (e.g., AutoCAD, SolidWorks).\n- Strong team player with good communication skills.\n- Willingness to undertake occasional business trips.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at AFT-Group offers a compelling opportunity for a skilled Plant Planner to contribute to diverse and exciting intralogistics projects within an innovative company. The organization prides itself on a collegiate environment with flat hierarchies, fostering both strong collaboration and individual growth. Candidates can expect to benefit from modern work tools, structured onboarding, and flexible working arrangements including home office options and flextime. The comprehensive benefits package also includes 30 days of holiday, an 'Erholungsbeihilfe' (recreational allowance), employee discounts, regular company events, and options for a Jobrad or monthly credit card allowance. AFT-Group is an equal opportunities employer, dedicated to creating an inclusive workplace where individual differences are valued and respected, encouraging all employees to reach their full potential.
