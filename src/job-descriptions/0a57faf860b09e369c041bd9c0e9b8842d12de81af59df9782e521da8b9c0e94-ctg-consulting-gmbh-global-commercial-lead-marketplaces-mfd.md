### Description\nZWILLING, a globally recognized premium brand in the kitchen and beauty segments, is seeking a Global Commercial Lead Marketplaces (m/f/d). Reporting directly to the Chief Commercial Officer in Solingen, this individual will be a key driver for global transformation and commercial growth worldwide, leveraging strong international communication skills and strategic expertise in online marketplaces.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Define and develop the global Amazon and marketplace strategy (channels, pricing, growth).\n- Establish global frameworks, tools, and processes for cross-regional coordination.\n- Advise on Vendor/Seller/Hybrid models and their impact on profitability.\n- Collaborate with regional leaders to review performance and ensure strategic alignment.\n- Serve as a strategic sparring partner to the CCO, leading a small team.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Extensive professional experience in Amazon channel management, ideally with practical experience in both Seller Central and Vendor Central models.\n- Successful experience managing complex international Amazon setups and coordinating across multiple regions.\n- In-depth knowledge of Amazon's commercial levers and policies (pricing, assortment, supply chain, advertising mechanisms, terms and conditions) as well as other relevant marketplaces.\n- Strong business acumen and financial understanding: ability to read P&L statements, analyze margin drivers, and align business models for profitability.\n- Proven ability to translate operational experience into strategic frameworks and global guidelines.\n- Highly pragmatic and hands-on personality with the ability to develop robust, implementable solutions rather than theoretical models.\n- Strong influencing and communication skills, ability to challenge and advise leaders without direct disciplinary authority.\n- Fluent English, additional languages are an advantage.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a highly visible and strategic role offering global impact within a secure, brand-strong company with an international innovation agenda. ZWILLING provides a modern workplace with a family-like and supportive culture, emphasizing clear growth strategies across E-commerce, Retail, and Marketplaces. The role comes with excellent benefits including home office options, flexible working hours, an integrated daycare, further training opportunities, and health programs, fostering both professional growth and work-life balance. It's an ideal opportunity for a pragmatic, influential leader to shape the future of a premium brand's global online presence.
