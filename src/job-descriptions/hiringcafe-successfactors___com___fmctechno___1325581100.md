Job Purpose
-----------

TechnipFMC is a global leader in the energy industry, specialized in subsea and surface technologies. With our proprietary technologies and production systems, integrated expertise, and comprehensive solutions, we are transforming our clients’ project economics. To learn more about how we are enhancing the performance of the world’s energy industry, go to [www.TechnipFMC.com](http://www.technipfmc.com/).   

**Automation Technology Engineer I** for individuals passionate about automation, robotics, and industrial technology. You will work closely with senior engineers to support, maintain, and enhance automation systems across various industries.

Essential Functions (Job Duties and Responsibilities):
------------------------------------------------------

*   Assist in the **design, development, and implementation** of automation systems.
*   Support **troubleshooting and maintenance** of automated equipment and control systems.
*   Collaborate with engineering teams to optimize **process automation and efficiency**.
*   Monitor and analyze **system performance**, identifying areas for improvement.
*   Conduct **testing and validation** of automation solutions.
*   Document technical processes, system configurations, and troubleshooting procedures.
*   Stay updated on emerging technologies and best practices in automation engineering.

Knowledge-Skills-Abilities (Competencies):
------------------------------------------

*   Experience with **industrial protocols** (Modbus, OPC, Ethernet/IP).
*   Knowledge of **machine learning and AI-driven automation**.
*   Hands-on experience with **automation tools and software**.
*   Basic understanding of **automation systems, robotics, and industrial control technologies**.
*   Familiarity with **PLC programming, SCADA systems, and industrial networking**.
*   Strong problem-solving skills and ability to work in a **fast-paced environment**.
*   Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.
