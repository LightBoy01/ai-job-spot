Description
QuaverEd is a company located on Music Row in Nashville, TN. We are seeking a motivated and detail-oriented **Entry-Level AI Specialist** to join our growing team. This role is ideal for recent graduates or early-career professionals who are passionate about artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data-driven problem-solving. As an AI Specialist, you’ll gain hands-on experience in developing, testing, and deploying AI-powered solutions while working alongside experienced AI professionals.
**Salary - Up to - $65,000**
###
### **Responsibilities**
*   Collaborate with cross-departmental teams to identify opportunities for AI-driven automation and efficiency opportunities.
*   Help design, develop, implement, and maintain AI flows, agents, and agent crews in QuaverEd
*   Use a combination of code-based tools (e.g., Python, AWS SDK) and no-code platforms (e.g., Zapier, CrewAI Studio) to deliver scalable solutions. Support data preparation, scrubbing, and labeling tasks to improve model performance.
*   Conduct research on emerging AI techniques, tools, and frameworks.
*   Document workflows and findings in a clear, repeatable manner for future iteration.
*   Stay curious and contribute to research on new AI tools, frameworks, and ethical best practices.
Requirements
*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Economics, Mathematics, or a related experience
*   Strong problem-solving and self-teaching ability; you’re excited to figure things out as you go.
*   Foundational understanding of ML, NLP, or LLMs (e.g., how ChatGPT or Claude works under the hood).
*   Experience with Python or other high end languages
*   Understanding of data analysis and visualization tools
*   Excellent communication skills and ability to work in a team environment
**Preferred Skills (Nice to Have)**
*   Coursework or projects in AI/ML.
*   Experience with AWS Bedrock, SageMaker, or CrewAI Studio.
*   Exposure to prompt engineering, embeddings, vector search, or Knowledge Bases.
*   Knowledge of natural language processing (NLP), computer vision, or reinforcement learning.
*   Experience with Git, APIs, JSON handling, or S3 buckets.
*   No-code/low-code tool usage (e.g., Zapier, Notion AI, Airtable Automations).
_**Please submit a resume and cover letter along with three references, as well as links to any relevant projects, GitHub repos, or portfolios (if available) to Quavered.com.**_
The role is _not_ a remote work position. All employees must live within commuting distance of Music Row in Nashville. We are currently requiring all team members to work at minimum 3 days a week in the office on the QuaverEd campus and allowing 2 days remote. This requirement is subject to change at the discretion of the team member's Supervisor and company policies.
_QuaverEd, Inc. offers an excellent benefits package including 80 hours of vacation per year with an increase after 3 years, 12 paid holidays, 60 hours of sick leave, subsidized health care up to $9,000 per year, subsidized life insurance, a 401(k) program with no participation fees, and a collegial, supporting atmosphere._
