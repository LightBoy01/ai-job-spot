**Location:** New York (highly preferred) or San Francisco

**Employment Type:** Full-time

**On-site:** In-person in New York or San Francisco

### About the Role

Our client is a global venture builder that empowers founders to launch and scale transformative healthcare companies. They are dedicated to launching AI-native healthcare ventures and are seeking a VP of Technology, AI Ventures to join their team. This role is for an exceptional leader who is passionate about the intersection of AI, venture building, and healthcare, with a deep, authentic motivation to transform global healthcare. You will be a trusted advisor to founders, providing credible technical advice and leveraging your deep understanding of the startup ecosystem.

### What You'll Do

*   **Partner with world-class founders** to build transformative health AI companies.
*   **Build and maintain strategic partnerships** with universities, tech companies, and investors.
*   **Develop market insights** on how emerging technologies will reshape healthcare.
*   **Provide expert product, technology, and commercial guidance** to our healthcare portfolio.
*   **Scale your impact** by integrating knowledge and relationships into LLM-enabled systems.
*   **Pursue bold healthcare AI theses** with full autonomy and committed capital.

**Requirements**

*   **8+ years of combined experience** in: founding or scaling health tech companies as a technical executive, building software/AI at early-stage startups, or investing at a top venture fund focused on AI or healthcare.
*   **Technical degree** (CS, math, physics, engineering) with current AI expertise.
*   **Active network** in the tech/startup/VC communities with credible relationships.
*   **Experience** in high-performing environments.
*   **Passion for startups** plus a deep commitment to another meaningful pursuit.

### Ideal Candidate Traits

*   **Trusted advisor** to successful tech and healthcare founders.
*   **Strong conviction** on AI-healthcare convergence with contrarian views.
*   **Proven track record** of making technical decisions in uncertain environments.
*   **Personal experience** building impactful LLM tools.
*   **Deep motivation** to transform global healthcare through technology.

**Benefits**

*   **Salary:** $198K - $240K (based on quality and experience).
*   **Equity:** Significant carried interest.
*   **Visa sponsorship** is not available for this role.
