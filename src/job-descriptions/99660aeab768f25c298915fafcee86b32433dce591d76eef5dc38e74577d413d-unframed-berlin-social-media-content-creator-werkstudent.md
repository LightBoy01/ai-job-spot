### Description\nUNFRÄMED Berlin is not a traditional beauty studio; we celebrate diversity, self-love, and individuality. We focus on enhancing natural beauty and making people feel good. Our services range from manicures and pedicures to lash & brow lifts, make-up, and high-quality skincare products.\nWe are looking for support to creatively and modernly develop our social media channels and online presence as a working student (Werkstudent:innenstelle).\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Create engaging content (photos, videos & texts) for our social media channels (e.g., Instagram, potentially TikTok).\n- Plan and maintain a content and posting calendar: regular posts, stories, Reels (if applicable), offers, campaigns, etc.\n- Support photo and video productions in the studio – e.g., beauty treatments, results, Before/After, Behind-the-Scenes.\n- Develop and implement ideas for campaigns, specials, or promotions (e.g., discount campaigns, Black Friday, product launches, offers).\n- Monitor social media trends, ensure appropriate tone, and represent our brand values: diversity, self-love, aesthetics, authenticity.\n- *Optional:* Interact with followers – respond to comments, community management, social engagement.\n- Collaborate closely with the team – both the studio team and those responsible for products, blog & shop – to align content and present a consistent brand image.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You are an enrolled student.\n- You have a good eye for aesthetics, trends, and social media. Photography, videography, or content creation are not new to you.\n- You work reliably, creatively, and independently.\n- You are flexible, interested, and open to beauty/lifestyle topics – ideally with initial experience in social media, content, photos/videos, or similar.\n- You possess basic knowledge of image and video editing or are willing to learn (e.g., Canva, smartphone, potentially Adobe or similar).\n- You are enthusiastic about being part of an inclusive, creative team that values diversity and individuality.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for a passionate and creative student to make a tangible impact on a brand with a truly unique and empowering mission. UNFRÄMED Berlin offers a vibrant, inclusive, and modern environment where your ideas are valued, and your creativity is given significant freedom. It's a chance to gain hands-on experience in social media and content creation within the beauty/lifestyle sector, collaborate closely with a supportive team, and actively shape the online presence of a company that genuinely celebrates individuality and self-love. If you're looking for a role where you can grow professionally while contributing to a positive message, this is it.
