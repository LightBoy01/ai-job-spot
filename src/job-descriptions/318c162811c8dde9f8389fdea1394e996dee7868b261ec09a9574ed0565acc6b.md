**Job Description:**

**Your role** 

We’re looking for a Data Engineer to join our AI research team. You’ll help build the data foundation that powers intelligent agents and real-time enterprise systems. This is a hands-on role where you’ll work with cutting-edge technologies in data architecture, semantic modeling, and AI integration. 

**Qualifications:**

**What you’ll do** 

*   Design scalable data platforms using Databricks, Delta Lake, and Spark 
*   Build real-time data pipelines with Kafka, Flink, or Spark Streaming 
*   Develop semantic models and knowledge graphs to support AI agents 
*   Enable AI systems to interact with ERP data through APIs and semantic layers 
*   Collaborate with AI engineers, architects, and product teams 
*   Ensure data quality, governance, and compliance (e.g., GDPR) 
*   Contribute to infrastructure using Azure, AWS, or GCP 

**What you bring** 

*   5+ years of experience in data architecture and engineering (Delta Lake, Databricks, Spark SQL). 
*   Strong skills in Python, SQL, and optionally Scala or Java 
*   Experience with cloud platforms and tools like Terraform, Airflow, Docker 
*   Proficiency in semantic & metadata modeling, including ontology engineering, metadata management, and knowledge graph construction. 
*   Experience with real-time data processing (Apache Kafka, Flink, Spark Streaming). 
*   Familiarity with AI/ML integration and semantic modeling 
*   A collaborative mindset and eagerness to learn and innovate 

**Nice to have** 

*   Knowledge of ERP systems or enterprise automation 
*   Experience with AI agent frameworks and protocols (e.g., MCP, A2A) 
*   Passion for responsible AI and data ethics
