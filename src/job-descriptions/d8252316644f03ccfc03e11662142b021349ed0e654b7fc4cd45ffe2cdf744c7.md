IDEMIA Secure Transactions, a division of IDEMIA Group, is the leading technology provider making it safer and easier to pay and connect.

With unmatched expertise in cryptography and credential issuance, IST is trusted by over 2,000 financial institutions, mobile operators, automotive manufacturers, and IoT providers worldwide. 

Every day, IST secures billions of essential transactions, ensuring the highest levels of data protection and convenience.

**Purpose**
-----------

We are seeking an HRIS Solution onwer **who** will lead a large perimeter within our HRIS landscape reporting to HRIS Director.  
In detail, you will manage autonomously the preparation & realization of recurrent campaigns like Performance & Compensation. You will be responsible for the excellence support level on your perimeter.  
You will be responsible to conduct innovative projects in relation to Talent, AI etc … and will be the project manager for these projects.

You will be in direct contact with the internal stakeholders and external vendor  
  

This role for IDEMIA Secure Transactions (IST) is based in Poland / Romania / Hungaria.

 

**Key Missions**
----------------

**Compensation & Performance solution owner**

*   Gather Business needs from HR and Key Stakeholders
*   Design and configure solutions in our HRIS to support campaign objectives in direct relation with our integrator
*   Coordinate testing, deployment and monitoring campaign execution
*   Analyze results and gather feedback for continuous improvement

 **HRIS projects lead**

*   Manage end to end delivery of critical HRIS projects across domains ( Compensation, Performance, Talent, LMS, AI …)
*   Define Project scope, milestones, budget and success metrics
*   Collaborate with technical teams, business partners and vendors
*   Ensure timely and quality delivery aligned with business priorities

**Guardian of excellence support**

*   Deliver timely and effective support to HR teams and end users in collaboration with support team
*   Collaborate with HRIS team to resolve incidents and improve processes
*   Maintain documentations, FAQs, and user guides to foster autonomy
*   Monitor system performance and user satisfaction metrics

**Change Management & User Adoption**

*   Develop and deliver tailored change management plans for system evolutions
*   Create and lead training sessions, webinars, and materials
*   Engage user communities and gather feedback to guide improvements
*   Support HR Teams in driving adoption across the organization

**Contribute to HRIS Strategy and roadmap**

*   Participate in defining the long-term HRIS vision in collaboration with leadership
*   Identify emerging needs and propose system enhancements or new tools
*   Benchmark best practices and market trends to guide strategic decisions
*   Help translate business strategy into actionable HRIS Initiatives

**Profile & Other Information**
-------------------------------

*   5+ years of experience in HRIS management, with hands-on expertise in SAP SuccessFactors (Performance & Goals, Compensation, LMS, etc.)
*   Strong project management experience, ideally in an international environment
*   Proven track record in leading full-cycle HRIS initiatives, including design, configuration, deployment, and change management
*   Solid understanding of core HR processes, especially Performance Management, Compensation, Talent Management, and Learning
*   Excellent communication and stakeholder engagement skills, both with business and technical teams
*   Fluent in English
*   Master’s degree in HR, Information Systems, Business Administration, or a related field
*   Certifications in SuccessFactors modules ( It is a plus)
