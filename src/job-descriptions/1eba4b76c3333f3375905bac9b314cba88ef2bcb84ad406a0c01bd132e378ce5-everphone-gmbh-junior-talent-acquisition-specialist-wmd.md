### Description\nEverphone is seeking a **Junior Talent Acquisition Specialist** (w/m/d) to dive directly into full-cycle recruiting. This role offers immediate responsibility and the chance to work with modern tools and high-quality diagnostic methods. You will grow professionally, understand the strategic impact of Talent Acquisition on business success, and thrive in a growing scale-up environment, celebrating successes and tackling challenges alongside the team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Full-Cycle Recruiting for various departments – from requirements analysis to interview conduction and final selection.\n- Independent Active Sourcing on LinkedIn and other relevant platforms, including identification, outreach, and qualification of talents.\n- Planning, organization, and participation in job fairs, as well as their follow-up.\n- Building and maintaining university cooperations (Campus Recruiting, presence at university days, target group-specific measures).\n- Professional candidate support throughout the entire process and ensuring a positive Candidate Experience.\n- Coordination with Hiring Managers and regular exchange on profile requirements, market conditions, and process progress.\n- Maintenance of the applicant tracking system (e.g., Personio) and ensuring structured data management.\n- Contribution to recruiting projects, e.g., process improvements or target group analyses.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed university degree – ideally with a focus on business psychology, HR/Human Resources, business administration, or a comparable field, OR a completed commercial apprenticeship with further qualification in HR.\n- First practical experience in recruiting, active sourcing, or an HR environment.\n- Experience in organizing and coordinating tasks or smaller projects.\n- Confident, professional demeanor in dealing with external candidates and internal Hiring Managers.\n- Ability to manage multiple recruiting tasks in parallel, set clear priorities, and reliably meet deadlines.\n- Proactive, thoughtful communication tailored to the needs of your contacts.\n- Interest in familiarizing yourself with modern diagnostic procedures, tools, and standards.\n- Confident handling of changing priorities and a high work pace – maintaining structure and focus even during intensive phases.\n- Very good German and good English skills (at least C1).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a dedicated junior professional to kickstart their career in Talent Acquisition within a dynamic and rapidly growing scale-up. Everphone deeply invests in its employees' well-being, offering comprehensive support for mental and physical health, generous vacation, and attractive financial perks like a monthly extra credit and mobility allowance. The company fosters a strong sense of community through regular social events, team lunches, and Employee Resource Groups. Crucially, there's a significant emphasis on continuous learning and professional development, with access to internal academies, language courses, and workshops. You'll be empowered to take on real responsibility from day one, contributing strategically to the company's success while benefiting from a highly supportive and engaging work environment.
