<b>Essential Duties and Responsibilities:</b>

<p>This position includes, but is not limited to, the following responsibilities:</p>
<ul>
<li>Develop and execute comprehensive strategies while collaborating closely with marketing, engineering, and business development teams to deliver complete, end-to-end solutions for customers.</li>
<li>Leverage a strong technical background to create solution-oriented product requirements, focusing on hyperscale datacenters, cloud computing, HPC, virtualization, software-defined storage, and AI infrastructure.</li>
<li>Engage with customers at all organizational levels—including executives, engineering teams, and product marketing staff—to communicate Supermicro’s capabilities, address challenges, and gather customer requirements.</li>
<li>Consult with clients to assess their specific needs before initiating or recommending solutions.</li>
