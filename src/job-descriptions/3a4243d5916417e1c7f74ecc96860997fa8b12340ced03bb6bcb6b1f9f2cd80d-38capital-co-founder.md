We’re building the most modern and scalable physiotherapy brand in Germany – and we’re doing it profitably from day one. Our goal: exceptional care, high-quality locations, and a playbook that can scale across cities. We’re now looking for a driven and entrepreneurial Co-Founder to help launch and scale our first studio – and build the foundation for something bigger.

Tasks
-----

As a Co-Founder, you’ll take full ownership from day one. Depending on your strengths, you might lead:

*   Launch of our brand, first studio and operational setup
*   Development of core processes, systems, and SOPs
*   Hiring and team management on-site
*   Optimization of the full patient journey
*   Coordination with partners (e.g. insurers, referral doctors, tech platforms)
*   Future expansion planning and execution

You be instrumental in shaping the culture, structure, and long-term strategy of the business.

Teams are highly welcome!!

Requirements
------------

We’re not looking for perfect resumes – we’re looking for builders.

That said, you should bring:

Strong operational mindset and bias for execution

*   Experience in fast-paced environments (startup, consulting, operations)
*   Passion for building things from scratch
*   Ability to manage people, processes, and partners
*   A founder mentality: hands-on, solution-oriented, and driven
*   No medical background required – just clarity, hustle, and ambition

Benefits
--------

True co-founder role with meaningful equity

*   Freedom to build, shape, and lead from day one
*   Access to capital, strategic support, and a strong company-building network
*   The chance to redefine an entire category
*   Real ownership, real impact, real entrepreneurship

Excited to build something from scratch and leave your mark on the health space?
