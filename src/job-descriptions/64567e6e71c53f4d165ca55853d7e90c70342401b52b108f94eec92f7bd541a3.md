**Position Specific Description**
Be part of an agile scrum team focused on delivering AI-powered and enabled IT solutions to our Power Generation Division. Learn technical and functional aspects of delivering end-to-end AI solutions in the AWS cloud ecosystem. Engage with our business partners in understanding their requirements and explore innovative IT solutions while enhancing existing digital solutions with generative AI capabilities.
**Technical/Functional Skills You Will Build:**
*   Design and develop responsive web application solutions with AI integration
*   Implementation of Generative AI and Machine Learning technologies
*   Engage and collaborate with business partners on AI solution requirements
*   Learn to work with various areas of IT: cybersecurity, infrastructure, application development, and AI/ML operations
**Preferred Skills:**
*   Strong interpersonal and collaboration skills
*   Understanding of web development principles and modern frameworks
*   Familiarity with coding languages such as C#, Python, JavaScript
*   Basic knowledge of AWS cloud services and AI/ML platforms
*   Proficiency with AI productivity tools (GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, OpenAI APIs)
*   Understanding of prompt engineering and AI workflow optimization
**Preferred Qualifications**:
*   Analytical Problem Solving with AI-assisted methodologies
*   Report Development and Data Visualization
*   Strong Listening Skills and Requirements Gathering
*   Team Player with cross-functional collaboration experience
*   Effective Written and Verbal Communications
*   Strategic Planning and Solution Architecture
*   Advanced proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite (especially Excel)
*   Cloud Systems, Web Technologies, and AI/ML Platforms
*   Information Programming and API Integration
*   Coding Standards and AI Development Best Practices
