Tracks & Fields is seeking an AI & Automation Engineer to develop on-premise AI systems. - Design and implement AI-driven automation solutions.

- Deploy and maintain AI systems in a production environment.
- Collaborate with product and engineering teams.

### Qualifications

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
- Proven experience in AI, machine learning, or automation engineering.
- Proficiency in Python and relevant AI/ML frameworks.
- Experience with on-premise deployments is a plus.
