### Description
As a **Marketing Manager**, you will calculate and manage our clients' advertising budgets. Your goal is to achieve the best possible visibility within a given budget. Regular evaluations are created to communicate work progress to clients. The strategic positioning of products in desired advertising slots also plays a significant role.🚀

### Responsibilities
*   **Develop SEA Strategies**: Collaborate with our Account Managers to develop targeted SEA strategies and ensure the full advertising potential on Amazon is leveraged.
*   **Keyword Research & Analysis**: Conduct comprehensive keyword research and manage on-page and off-page optimization to maximize product visibility.
*   **Campaign Optimization**: Your goal is to fully exploit campaign potential and continuously improve performance.
*   **Data-Driven Measures**: Create analyses and derive concrete measures for campaign development – always keeping an eye on success with the help of analytical tools.
*   **Reporting**: Prepare regular reports to transparently present campaign results to our clients.
*   **Amazon Seller Central Expertise**: Be proficient in using Amazon Seller Central to manage advertising campaigns.
*   **PPC Price Optimization**: Optimize PPC prices based on predefined strategies, ensuring optimal budget utilization.

### Qualifications
*   **Independent Work**: You are capable of working autonomously and managing projects independently.
*   **Quick Comprehension**: You have the ability to quickly grasp new topics and find solutions.
*   **Teamwork and Communication Skills**: You enjoy working in a team and possess strong communication skills – both internally and when interacting with clients.
*   **Analytical Understanding**: You love working with numbers and have a good understanding of analytical relationships.
*   **Structured Communication**: You communicate clearly and concisely – whether in writing or verbally.
*   **Excel and PowerPoint Proficiency**: You have excellent knowledge of Excel and PowerPoint and know how to effectively use these tools to present results and analyze data.
