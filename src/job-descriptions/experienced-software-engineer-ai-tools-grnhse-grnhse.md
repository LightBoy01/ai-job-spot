<p>Working as a coder at Hudson River Trading (HRT) means working on a small, highly productive, and efficient team. You may see "trading" in our name and assume you'll be relegated to some dark corner to code for the "business," but that could not be further from the truth!&nbsp;</p>
<p>At HRT, an automated trading firm based in NYC, the code you write *is* our business, so it is everyone's job to make sure our developers have everything they need to do their jobs well.</p>
<p>As a member of the AI Tools team, you will be responsible for increasing productivity across the company by integrating the newest AI tools into our users’ workflows. Whether it’s for a Python developer writing a web app, a C++ developer speeding up order execution, a researcher investigating trade signals, or even Business Development meeting with vendors - we are focused on deploying what works.</p>
<p>This is an exciting opportunity to help build and define the AI tooling ecosystem at HRT. You will have to navigate the rapidly evolving options in this space, and adapt them to our own internal systems. You might be fixing auth one day, comparing vendors the next, and coding a new MCP the third.&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Responsibilities</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Help us develop software that improves productivity for every office and department at HRT while using the latest technologies and techniques in software engineering and the broader AI tooling ecosystem</li>
<li>HRT has many high-impact areas where we are integrating AI tools; here’s what you could work on:</li>
<ul>
<li>Writing a custom MCP wrapper around internal resources</li>
<li>Fine-tuning a new code completion model</li>
<li>Deploying a new coding assistant across all developer and researcher environments</li>
<li>Developing evaluations and iterating on an autonomous coding agent</li>
<li>Collaborating with security to improve AI sandboxing</li>
<li>Presenting and educating users on best practices</li>
</ul>
</ul>
<p><strong>Profile</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>You’re one of the top devs in your current organization, as measured by productivity, technical capability, and helpfulness to other devs</li>
<li>Experience deploying and maintaining applications. You’ve managed an application from the design phase through the production phase</li>
<li>You’re comfortable with defining direction in an environment of ambiguity and uncertainty. The AI tooling space changes rapidly</li>
<li>You really like to work with people who challenge you and make you better at what you do</li>
<li>In your spare time you code, tinker, read, explore, break things, and have an insatiable curiosity for all things AI and computer-related</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Qualifications</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field</li>
<li>Python experience is required, daily use of most recent standards preferred</li>
<li>C++ and JavaScript (TypeScript, React) experience a plus</li>
<li>Experience with the AI tooling ecosystem (use coding assistants/agentic workflows, ideally have built your own customizations)</li>
<li>Superior design, debugging, and problem solving skills</li>
<li>Knowledge of UNIX operating systems (we use Linux), system/processor performance, and network communication</li>
</ul>
<p>The estimated base salary range for this position is $150,000 to $250,000 per year, based on job-related skills and experience. This role will also be eligible for discretionary performance-based bonuses and a competitive benefits package.</p>
