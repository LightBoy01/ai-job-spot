### Description\nAre you eager to not just discuss the heating transition, but to implement it with concrete projects? Then you've come to the right place! We – the Effizienzpioniere – are an innovative renovation partner providing services around energy consulting, planning, and funding. We accompany our customers holistically on their renovation journey and ensure a clear, efficient, and funding-optimized implementation. In doing so, we set standards in the industry and drive climate protection forward. For this, we are looking for you, a tech-savvy and experienced reinforcement for our Housing Industry Team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Planning and control of heating and heat pump projects in existing residential buildings, including district heating options, with a focus on multi-family homes in the housing industry.\n- Independent development, evaluation, and elaboration of modern heating concepts – from the initial idea through variant comparisons to the final solution approach (hydraulics, hot water preparation, system temperatures, structural restrictions).\n- Technical support for our energy efficiency experts in early consulting phases and participation in finding solutions.\n- Analysis of energetic data and existing buildings as a basis for integrated heating concepts and planning.\n- Close coordination with specialist companies (HVAC, electrical, refrigeration) and independent coordination of all project-relevant steps up to tendering.\n- Preparation of technical contributions for grant applications and close cooperation with our funding service during application and implementation.\n- Technical support for the implementation of the final concept as well as continuous coordination with customers, specialist companies, and internal interfaces.\n- Co-shaping of our planning standards and know-how in the new housing industry team to further expand our USP \"Consulting. Planning. Funding. All from one source.\"\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in Building, Energy, or HVAC Engineering or a comparable field of study.\n- Several years of experience in TGA/SHK projects, ideally with practical experience in existing buildings and multi-family homes.\n- Proficient in heating load calculation, hydraulics, system concepts, and common design and planning tools (e.g., Hottgenroth, Solar-Computer, Linear, manufacturer-specific heat pump design tools, or CAD).\n- Profound understanding of hot water preparation and integrated heating concepts – including the development and evaluation of various variants.\n- Experience in technical concept development regarding reviewing options, comparing them, and leading to a decision.\n- Structured, pragmatic way of working and strong technical implementation skills.\n- Experience in project-accompanying technical clarification, including queries and minor adjustments.\n- Solution-oriented, practical mindset and hands-on mentality – someone who truly gets things rolling.\n- Strong communication skills in dealing with craftsmen, customers, and internal teams.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for an experienced and proactive engineer passionate about the energy transition. The role offers significant autonomy, a chance to truly shape planning standards and processes in a growing business segment, and direct impact on climate protection projects. The company fosters a collaborative, low-hierarchy environment with flexible working conditions and excellent development prospects, making it ideal for someone with a hands-on, solution-oriented approach.
