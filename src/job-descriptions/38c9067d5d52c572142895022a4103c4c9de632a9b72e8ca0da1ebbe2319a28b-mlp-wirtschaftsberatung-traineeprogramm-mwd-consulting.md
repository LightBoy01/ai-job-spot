### Description\nMLP Wirtschaftsberatung offers ambitious talents the opportunity to get to know the diverse world of financial and management consulting through a six-month trainee program. Join us at our Hamburg location and actively shape the future of our clients – and your own.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Provide individual consultation: Together with your team, you will advise demanding private and business clients in the areas of asset accumulation, investment, real estate financing, and pension planning.\n- Build competence: You will acquire profound technical and sales knowledge to specifically develop your consulting skills.\n- Drive Business Development: You will independently take on projects and actively contribute your ideas to strategic issues.\n- Demonstrate entrepreneurial thinking: As a Consultant Trainee, you act like an entrepreneur within the company and contribute significantly to the development of new concepts and processes.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You have successfully completed your studies or are about to graduate with convincing achievements.\n- You are characterized by initiative, goal orientation, and entrepreneurial thinking.\n- You communicate confidently and empathetically – both within the team and in client dialogue.\n- Personal development is not a secondary goal for you, but part of your attitude.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding entry-level opportunity for ambitious graduates looking to kickstart a career in financial and management consulting. The six-month trainee program at MLP Wirtschaftsberatung in Hamburg offers hands-on experience, direct client interaction, and significant responsibility from day one. With a strong emphasis on mentorship, continuous learning, and an entrepreneurial mindset, candidates will develop both their technical expertise and business acumen. The company fosters a supportive, flexible, and dynamic environment with attractive, performance-based compensation, making it an ideal launchpad for future leaders in the industry.
