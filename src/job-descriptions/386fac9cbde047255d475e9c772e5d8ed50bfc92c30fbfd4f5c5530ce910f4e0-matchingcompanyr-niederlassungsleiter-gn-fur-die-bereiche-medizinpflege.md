### Description\nThe MatchingCompany® is your partner for professional and sustainable placement in the HR sector. With over 25 years of industry experience, we specialize in nationwide recruitment of specialists and managers in People Management. We are seeking a **Branch Manager for the Medicine and Care sectors** for our client in Magdeburg, a role involving overall responsibility for the branch and acting as its external ambassador.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Overall responsibility for the branch and serving as its \"ambassador\" to the outside world.\n- Responsible leadership of operational business, including budget and revenue responsibility.\n- New customer acquisition, as well as nurturing and expanding existing customer relationships.\n- Organizational, administrative, and personnel management of the team focused on HR and client consulting (disposition).\n- Planning and steering of relevant sales activities.\n- Coordination of collaboration with all local departments and the head office.\n- Ensuring compliance with all labor law, HR-related, and legal regulations.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed vocational training or equivalent education.\n- Several years of experience in the HR services industry, specifically in temporary staffing (Arbeitnehmerüberlassung) of specialists and managers from the Medicine, Care, and ideally also Pedagogy sectors.\n- Business administration and HR management knowledge, especially in leading employees.\n- Fluent German language skills, both written and spoken.\n- High sales affinity, goal-oriented, and a proactive work approach.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a highly impactful leadership role, offering significant autonomy and the chance to shape a branch's success within a specialized and growing market segment (medicine and care). The company values professional development, work-life balance, and employee well-being, providing a supportive environment for a proactive and experienced manager. It's an excellent opportunity for someone who thrives on operational leadership, sales, and team management, while contributing to a company with a long-standing reputation for professional and sustainable recruitment.
