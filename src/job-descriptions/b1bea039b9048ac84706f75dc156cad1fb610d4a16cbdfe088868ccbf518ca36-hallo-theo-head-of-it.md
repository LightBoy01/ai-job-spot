**Your Mission**

You’ll own our shift from local, server-centric setups to a cloud-first reality - and make it visible in colleagues’ day-to-day work across our sites. Aligned with the Director of IT, you drive end-to-end delivery: turn complex migrations into smooth cutovers, keep operations dependable, and uphold pragmatic, auditable platform standards. As a trusted sparring partner and hands-on lead (no line management), you mentor teammates, steer partners to outcomes, and practice thoughtful **extreme ownership** so the right improvements ship, stick, and scale.

Your Responsibilities
---------------------

**Projects: M&A Local-to-Cloud & Tenant Alignment**

*   Scope & deliver migrations (identities, devices, data/permissions, mail/collab) incl. waves, cutover, rollback, **hypercare**.
*   Execute **local-to-cloud** moves and quiet tenant alignment (incl. Microsoft) with clear acceptance & risk logs.
*   Provide decision-ready updates; hand over cleanly to run-ops with reusable playbooks.

IT Operations
-------------

*   Own ITSM (incident/request/change), catalogue/KB, KPI dashboard; turn signals into improvements.
*   Run endpoint lifecycle (zero-touch, baselines, encryption/patch, inventory/returns).
*   Ensure Day-1-ready **JML**, tidy collaboration spaces, light guardrails vs. shadow IT.

SysAdmin / Sys-Ops
------------------

*   Operate IDP/SSO (roles, access reviews, least-privilege, automated provisioning).
*   Maintain baselines, monitoring/alerts, stable integrations (HR ↔ IDP/ITSM/DMS).
*   Embed security hygiene (EDR, patch/backup rhythms, restore tests) and GDPR/NIS2 evidence.

Leadership & Enablement (hands-on, measured)
--------------------------------------------

*   Set near-term priorities; coach IT-Ops/Sys-Ops; steer partners to outcomes.
*   Raise the bar on documentation, runbooks, service quality, incident/post-mortems.
*   Partner with the Director of IT on data-driven sequencing (stabilise → standardise → scale).

Preferred Background
--------------------

**Must-have**

*   Senior experience spanning **migrations (local-to-cloud & tenant alignment)**, **IT Ops**, and **Sys-Ops** in hybrid/multi-site environments.
*   Proven lift on **SLA/MTTR/CSAT**, endpoint compliance, and **clean cutovers**; strong change/risk control and documentation habits.
*   Practical leadership of small internal teams and external partners (prioritisation, coaching, clear acceptance criteria).
*   Solid grasp of **permission models**, **retention/classification**, **access reviews**, backup/restore evidence, and privacy obligations.
*   Ability to distil complexity into **decision-ready options** and to drive adoption with non-IT stakeholders.
*   German fluent; English professional.

Nice-to-have
------------

*   **Document management** governance (permissions, retention, audit trails) and self-service enablement.
*   Light automation/scripting for provisioning/reporting; capacity/cost visibility and simple ROI framing.
*   Participation in **restore/DR** exercises; familiarity with branch/field operations and vendor consolidation. **Why join us**

At hallo theo you’ll see your work matter quickly: you’ll turn complex migrations into smooth cutovers, lift operational reliability, and keep platforms tidy and auditable across offices and on-site teams. You’ll coach a capable team, partner closely with leadership, and simplify how we work. We value clarity, craftsmanship, and results - and we expect thoughtful **extreme ownership** that balances autonomy with crisp alignment, so the right things ship and stick.
