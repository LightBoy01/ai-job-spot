**HRS AS A COMPANY**
--------------------

HRS is reinventing the way businesses stay, work and pay in today’s dynamic global marketplace. As a global technology company HRS is focused on leveraging technology and Artificial Intelligence to create fully-digital lodging and meeting experiences for corporations, their travelers and their lodging partners. The company’s platform-based solutions are reinventing end-to-end travel management and the traveler experience for transient, meetings and workforce segments for Fortune 500 companies worldwide. Beyond cost savings HRS clients gain from an unrivaled focus on essential aspects including traveler satisfaction, risk mitigation and savings.  

HRS is also recognized for its award-winning Green Stay Initiative, technology that helps corporate hotel programs achieve their NetZero targets.  
HRS works with world’s leading hotel chains, regional hospitality groups and payment providers.

**BUSINESS UNIT**
-----------------

**POSITION**
------------

As Product Marketing Manager, your mission is to drive the successful positioning, messaging, and go-to-market execution for our portfolio of solutions and platforms. You’ll work cross-functionally to ensure that our value proposition resonates with corporate clients and decision-makers across key segments. From strategy development to campaign execution, you’ll play a central role in connecting market needs with commercial success. 

You will: 

*   **Lead the product marketing strategy**, shaping how we engage the market and how our solution is perceived across geographies. 
    

*   Translate complex product innovations and AI features into compelling narratives that resonate with buyers and users alike. 
    

*   Connect commercial goals and technical systems with a unified marketing vision that drives measurable impact. 
    

**CHALLENGE**
-------------

This is a pivotal role in establishing a scalable, data-informed, and cohesive GTM foundation for the meetings segment — in close collaboration with Product, Sales, and Corporate Marketing teams. 

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

Define and lead the go-to-market approach for our platforms, adapted to our global footprint and customer segmentation. 

Develop clear, differentiated product messaging that communicates value to our target audience and reflects the technical innovation of our platforms. 

Drive sales enablement by equipping internal teams with the right materials, training, and positioning to win in the market. 

Plan and execute cross-channel lifecycle and demand generation campaigns in collaboration with the corporate marketing team. 

Build and manage impactful assets — thought leadership, digital content, use cases, product guides, webinars — tailored for meetings buyers. 

Align with product managers, sales teams, and data owners to ensure our GTM strategy is both scalable and impactful. 

Provide insights into market trends, buyer needs, and competitive positioning to sharpen our messaging and innovation roadmap. 

Track success metrics, gather feedback, and continuously optimize initiatives to maximize reach and revenue contribution. 

**FOR THIS EXCITING MISSION YOU ARE EQUIPPED WITH...**
------------------------------------------------------

*   Minimum 3 years of experience in B2B product marketing, ideally in SaaS, marketplace, or enterprise travel environments.
*   Proven ability to develop and execute go-to-market strategies that drive adoption and revenue across international markets.
*   Expertise in crafting clear, differentiated value propositions that resonate with B2B decision-makers and reflect product innovation.
*   Hands-on experience planning and delivering marketing campaigns, including messaging, content creation, and enablement materials.
*   Demonstrated ability to align sales and marketing activities, ensuring consistent messaging and seamless execution across functions.
*   Proficiency in conducting market research, analyzing buyer needs, and adapting strategies to match customer expectations.
*   Strong written and verbal communication skills to convey product value across a variety of channels and audiences.
*   Ability to work effectively with product, sales, and marketing teams to ensure launch readiness and ongoing alignment.
*   Comfort using performance data and insights to optimize marketing efforts and support strategic planning.
*   Experience with AI-driven platforms or contributing to marketing strategies in digitally transforming industries is a strong plus.
*   Comfort with ambiguity and change, with the ability to integrate evolving products into customer-facing narratives quickly.
*   Fluency in English (written and verbal); additional languages are a plus. 

**PERSPECTIVE**
---------------

Access to a global network of a globally united and mutually responsible “Tribe of Intrapreneurs” that is passionately dedicated to disrupting the travel industry and while doing so reinventing the ways how businesses stay, work and pay. 

Our entrepreneurial-driven environment of full ownership and execution focus offers you the playground to contribute to a greater mission, while growing personally and professionally throughout this unique journey. You will continuously learn from a radical culture of retrospectives and continuous improvement and actively contribute to making business life better, smarter and more sustainable.
