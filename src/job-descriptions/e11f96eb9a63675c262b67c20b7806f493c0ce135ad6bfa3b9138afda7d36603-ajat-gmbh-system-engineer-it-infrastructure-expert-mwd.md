### Description\nThis is a critical role for an experienced IT professional focusing on Endpoint Management (SCCM/Intune), Linux Administration, and robust Security across Windows and Linux environments, both on-premise and in AWS. The core mission revolves around automation, security, and system stability, acting as a provisioning and patch management expert and a crucial 3rd-level support escalation point. The ideal candidate will be a versatile expert who thrives in hybrid environments and makes no compromises on system stability and security.\n\n### Responsibilities\n-   **Modern Client Management & Provisioning**\n    -   Design and manage Zero-Touch-Rollouts, utilizing Windows Autopilot as the standard tool for quick and error-free client provisioning.\n    -   Responsible for creating and distributing complex software packages (MSI, MSIX, IntuneWin) within a hybrid SCCM and Microsoft Intune environment.\n-   **Cross-Platform Patch-Management (Windows & Linux)**\n    -   Guarantee system security through comprehensive and automated patch management for Windows Clients, Windows Servers, and Linux environments.\n    -   Routinely utilize SCCM and Patch My PC, with a strong emphasis on automating 3rd-party updates via Patch My PC.\n    -   Manage updates for both local systems and AWS instances (Windows & Linux).\n-   **Linux Administration & Server Hardening**\n    -   Administer, maintain, and harden primarily server-based Linux environments (Debian, Ubuntu, RHEL), ensuring their stable operation.\n    -   Apply best practices to harden both Windows and Linux servers against potential attacks.\n-   **Netzwerk-Security & 3rd-Level-Support**\n    -   As a subject matter expert, analyze complex network and security problems (Firewall rules, VPN, Conditional Access, Endpoint Protection).\n    -   Act as the final escalation instance (3rd-Level) for complex disruptions related to clients, servers, software distribution, and network connectivity.\n\n### Qualifications\n-   **Experience:** At least 5-6 years of professional experience in IT system administration, ideally within an enterprise environment.\n-   **Microsoft Endpoint Professional:** Profound knowledge of SCCM, proficient with Windows Autopilot, and skilled in packaging via Intune (MSI, MSIX, IntuneWin).\n-   **Essential Skill:** Expert in Patch My PC, as this is a core requirement for automating patch processes.\n-   **Linux Knowledge:** Good, practically proven knowledge in the administration and patching of Linux servers (Debian, Ubuntu, RHEL).\n-   **Security Mindset:** Deep knowledge of Endpoint Security and operating system hardening. Familiarity with terms like Conditional Access, VPN, and Firewall rule sets is essential.\n-   **Languages:** Native-level German (C1) is mandatory. Good English skills are also required.\n-   **Working Style:** Works analytically, structurally, and is eager to take on responsibility.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a highly critical and impactful Senior-level role for an IT professional with a robust background in endpoint management, Linux administration, and network security. The emphasis on automation, cross-platform patching, and 3rd-level expertise makes this a challenging yet rewarding opportunity.
