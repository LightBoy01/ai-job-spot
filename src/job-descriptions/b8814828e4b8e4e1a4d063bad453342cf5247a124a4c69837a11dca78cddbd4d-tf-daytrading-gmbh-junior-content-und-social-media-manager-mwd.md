### Description\nWe at TradingFreaks are a dynamic team with a mission: We transform ambitious professionals into profitable day traders. As an e-learning company based in Siegburg, we offer high-quality training and strategies in stock trading. With a strong focus on responsibility, growth, excellence, and team spirit, we are looking for reinforcement in content and social media. If you love creative content, want to hit the nerve of the target audience, and want to work in an environment that promotes innovation – then you've come to the right place!\n\nThis role offers exciting tasks with plenty of room for your own ideas and creativity, working in an ambitious team with flat hierarchies. You'll have a workplace in our modern office in Siegburg with very good transport connections, and after successful onboarding, up to 2 days of home office per week are possible. We are committed to your personal and professional development through feedback and targeted support, and you'll gain access to high-quality specialist knowledge in trading and investments.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Contribute to the development and continuous improvement of our social media content plan.\n- Creatively contribute with your own ideas, new formats, and trends on platforms like Instagram, TikTok, YouTube & Co.\n- Produce short videos such as Reels, Shorts, and TikToks.\n- Create visual content such as carousel posts, graphics, and infographics.\n- Community management: Actively interact with our target audience, answer comments, and foster dialogue.\n- Monitor relevant KPIs and create regular reports for success measurement and optimization.\n- Utilize AI tools for topic research, content ideation, and competitor analysis.\n- Structured storage and maintenance of scripts, files, and templates.\n\n### Qualifications\n- First relevant professional experience in social media, content creation, or comparable activities.\n- Creative flair for visual storytelling, trends, and target audience engagement.\n- Solid knowledge in video editing (cut, sound, effects, etc.).\n- Experience with graphic design tools (e.g., Canva, Adobe Creative Cloud or similar).\n- Organizational talent and an independent, structured way of working.\n- Proficient use of social media platforms and their formats.\n- Experience in community management is an advantage.\n- Enthusiasm for digital trends, financial topics, and the trading world is a plus.\n- Native-level German language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a dynamic opportunity for a creative Social Media & Content Creator to join an innovative e-learning company focused on empowering individuals in day trading. The role offers significant creative freedom, professional development, and the chance to directly impact the company's mission. The hybrid work model and flat hierarchies underscore a supportive and growth-oriented environment. If you're passionate about digital content, finance, and making a real difference, this is an excellent role to showcase your skills and grow. The company values hands-on portfolio examples, so be sure to include them in your application!
