<strong>Introducing Design at Trainline ✏️</strong><p><br>Empowering people to make greener travel choices is central to our focus in 2025 and beyond. <strong>As a Senior UX Designer at Trainline you will... 🚅</strong><ul><li><p>Join a team focused on delivering a best-in-class Search, Compare and Buy UX across the UK &amp; Europe, enhancing the product by integrating thoughtful, tech-enabled solutions that support customers throughout their booking experience.</p></li><li><p>Leverage emerging technologies, including AI, machine learning, and large language models, to anticipate customer needs and create more intuitive, personalised searching experiences, which are driven by data and meeting diverse customer needs across multiple markets.</p></li><li><p>Work closely with Product, Research, Data, and Engineering in a dynamic, fast-paced environment. To this end, success lies in your customer-centric mindset, your ability to question thinking and develop our relationships with carriers.</p><p>This is an outstanding opportunity to come in and shape a significant part of the search experience as well as boost your design career, working within one of the most mature and established UX Teams in Travel. </p><p>You should understand the power and importance of quantitative data to assess the impact of design, and draw insights to iterate and optimise experience, playing back your explorations with clear narratives, woven together with data and research.</p><p>You should be passionate about product design and looking for an opportunity to disrupt and define a product that is changing the way we travel and inspiring millions of people across the UK and Europe to make more environmentally sustainable travel choices every day.</p><p>We’re looking for creative big thinkers with small egos to achieve our committed plans.
