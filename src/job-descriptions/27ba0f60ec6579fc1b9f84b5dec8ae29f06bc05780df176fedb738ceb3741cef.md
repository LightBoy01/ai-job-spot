**SUMMARY  
**The Global Legal Operations department at Euronet Worldwide Inc. plays a key role in supporting the legal function by optimising processes, streamlining operations and increasing efficiencies. This is a dynamic and evolving role which would suit someone adaptable, proactive and interested in the wider business strategy of a corporate legal department.

We value culture, team collaboration and exercise an open-door policy. The successful candidate will receive comprehensive training to support their development and ensure confidence in delivering the responsibilities outlined below.

We are seeking a highly motivated and details-driven individual to join our growing legal team.

**ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES**

**Legal Technology & Automation:**

*   Assist with the implementation of the recently deployed Microsoft Copilot AI Tool across the Legal department and scaling adoption of legal-specific AI tools to enhance operational efficiency and innovation.
*   Test functionality, gather user feedback, and contribute to business cases or implementation plans.
*   Monitor the performance and user adoption of existing legal systems and propose enhancements.

**Legal Operations Projects:**

*   Participate in department-wide initiatives aimed at improving legal service delivery.
*   Help scope requirements, document workflows, test new solutions, and support change.
*   Take initiative in identifying recurring issues and proposing sustainable solutions.

**Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM):**

*   Monitor and triage incoming contract and legal service requests to ensure efficient prioritisation and processing from existing CLM.
*   Manage user permissions and onboarding within the system, ensuring compliance with internal access protocols.
*   Check contracts are properly signed by all parties before storing in the document repository system.

**Document Repository Management:**

*   Maintain and enhance the legal document repository, ensuring documents, and legal templates are accurately filed and easily retrievable.

**Legal Procedure & Business Support:**

*   Serve as a first point of contact for internal legal inquiries from various departments, providing guidance on contract procedures, escalation paths, and standard processes.
*   Maintain a consistent and professional service approach to support business teams across global regions.

**SharePoint Intranet Administration:**

*   Regularly update the legal department’s SharePoint intranet page to ensure that internal policies, guidance documents, FAQs, and resources are current and accessible.
*   Coordinate with IT or knowledge management teams as needed for improvements or troubleshooting.

**Reporting & Insights:**

*   Supporting in the preparation of ad hoc reports.
*   Supporting in the preparation of presentations for stakeholders that summarize trends, insights, and areas for improvement.

**E-Signature Coordination:**

*   Manage the processing of documents through e-signature platforms (e.g., Adobe Sign), ensuring proper routing, signer sequencing, and timely execution.
*   Maintain accurate records and support compliance with company signature policies.

**Training & Onboarding:**

*   Deliver introductory training for new hires on legal systems, templates, and engagement processes.
*   Develop quick-reference guides and materials to promote self-service and legal process literacy across business units.
