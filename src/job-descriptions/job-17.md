# Manager, Machine Learning (Generative AI & Content Understanding)

### Description

Netflix is seeking an experienced and innovative leader to manage a team of machine learning engineers and researchers focused on Generative AI and Content Understanding. In this role, you will be at the heart of how Netflix creates and recommends content, personalizes the user experience, and optimizes its global content strategy. You will lead a high-performing team to develop and deploy cutting-edge models that understand content at a deep level and generate new forms of creative assets.

### Responsibilities

- Lead, mentor, and grow a team of machine learning scientists and engineers, fostering a culture of innovation and high performance.
- Define and execute the research and development roadmap for generative AI and content understanding, aligning with Netflix's strategic goals.
- Oversee the end-to-end lifecycle of machine learning models, from ideation and data collection to deployment and in-production monitoring.
- Collaborate with cross-functional partners in product, engineering, and creative teams to identify new opportunities and translate business needs into technical solutions.
- Stay at the forefront of advancements in generative models, deep learning, and content analysis, and guide the team in applying them to Netflix's unique challenges.
- Communicate complex technical concepts and project outcomes to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

### Qualifications

- PhD or MS in Computer Science, Machine Learning, or a related field.
- 5+ years of experience in machine learning or a related role, with at least 2 years in a management or leadership position.
- Proven track record of leading teams to successfully ship machine learning models to production.
- Deep technical expertise in one or more of the following areas: Generative AI (e.g., GANs, VAEs, Transformers, Diffusion Models), Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, or Computer Vision.
- Excellent leadership and communication skills, with the ability to inspire and motivate a team.
- Passion for content, entertainment, and the Netflix user experience.
