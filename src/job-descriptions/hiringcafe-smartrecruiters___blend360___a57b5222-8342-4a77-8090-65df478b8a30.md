**Job Description:**

We are seeking a hands-on Technical Lead with strong AWS and Generative AI expertise to join our investment and financial services team.This role is ideal for a highly autonomous professional who can not only design solutions but also lead their technical implementation, ensuring robust and scalable AI-driven systems tailored for financial services use cases.

**What is this position about?** 

*   Lead the **technical implementation** of AI and cloud-based solutions.
    
*   Design, build, and optimize **Gen AI and agentic workflows** for financial applications.
    
*   Architect and manage systems leveraging **AWS services** to ensure scalability, performance, and security.
    
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to align business goals with technical execution.
    
*   Ensure best practices in testing, monitoring, and reliability across deployed systems.
    
*   Troubleshoot complex production issues and provide rapid solutions.
    

**Qualifications:**

**Must haves**

*   Proven **AWS experience**, including hands-on implementation.
    
*   Strong background in **Generative AI** and **agentic system design**.
    
*   Ability to work independently and take ownership of technical delivery.
    
*   Strong problem-solving skills in fast-paced environments.
    
*   Excellent teamwork and communication skills.
    

**Nice to haves**

*   **Financial services industry experience**, especially investment or banking systems.
    
*   Familiarity with data security and compliance requirements in finance.
    

**What about languages?**  
  
You will need excellent written and verbal English for clear and effective communication with the team.

**How much experience must I have?**

To thrive in this role, you should have **5+ years of experience** in cloud/AI technical roles.
