<strong>About Nabla</strong><p>We are a team of entrepreneurs, clinicians and engineers committed to bringing back joy to the practice of medicine.</p><p>Together with a community of clinician innovators, we’ve harnessed the best of machine learning science to develop Nabla: the leading AI assistant that’s restoring the human connection at the heart of healthcare. Today, over 85,000 clinicians across 130+ healthcare organizations trust Nabla to support how they deliver care every day.</p><p>We’re at the start of an ambitious journey: Ambient listening, dictation, coding, and command capabilities are all converging into a proactive assistant that intuitively streamlines clinical and financial workflows.</p><p>Backed by a recent $70M Series C, we’re hiring to build the next generation of clinical AI and improve the lives of clinicians and patients everywhere.</p><p>This is a great time to join us!</p>
<strong>Product at Nabla</strong><p>The product team is a tight-knit group of builders, designers, and strategists. Together with engineering, we define and deliver high-impact tools that delight clinicians and solve their most pressing pain points.</p><p></p>
<strong>Responsibilities</strong><ul><li><p><strong>Product discovery</strong>: Lead product discovery efforts by identifying and validating opportunities through usability studies, user research, and data analysis. Ensure decisions are data-driven and customer-centric.</p></li><li><p><strong>Roadmap prioritization</strong>: Identify elegant and simple product features, prioritize them based on business impact, and organize them into product roadmaps.</p></li><li><p><strong>Product execution</strong>: Own the product execution process, working closely with designers, engineers, and the broader team. Ensure high-quality product delivery with a focus on attention to detail and user delight.</p></li><li><p><strong>Strategic decision</strong>: Collaborate with the product and executive teams to contribute to strategic decisions that drive the company forward, ensuring that products align with overall business and user goals.</p><p></p></li></ul>
<strong>Your DNA:</strong><ul><li><p>3+ years of product management experience in a technical or engineering-heavy environment</p></li><li><p>Experience managing product lifecycles, ideally with exposure to both building new products (0→1) and scaling existing ones (1→n)</p></li><li><p>Familiarity with AI/ML systems, data infrastructure, or developer platforms (hands-on experience a strong plus)</p></li><li><p>Strong systems thinking — able to understand complex architectures, anticipate dependencies, and make sound tradeoffs</p></li><li><p>Some exposure to regulated or safety-critical industries (healthcare experience a plus)</p></li><li><p>Clear and structured communicator — comfortable explaining tradeoffs and decisions to both technical and non-technical audiences</p></li><li><p>Proven track record of collaborating with engineering, design, and other teams to ship impactful technical products</p><p></p></li></ul>
