If you live in Australia or New Zealand, you’ve likely heard of AMP. But at a time when society is changing, we are too. We’re now a nimbler business with new leadership and thinking. 

For us, these are exciting times. There’s a real potential for big thinkers to help us redefine what financial services could be. And turn our legacy into something even more positive and powerful for the future.

**Help people create their tomorrow, while you create yours**

We help people with their banking, super, retirement and finances. Through upturns, downturns, recessions, and major life transitions. Every day, we help people see and make more of their financial potential, so that they can create their tomorrow. And we’ve been doing it for over 170 years. 

If we do our job well, we genuinely add to the prosperity of our country and its people. 

**How you’ll make an impact**

AMP Bank is part of the AMP Group and this is a critical role that will help lead the Bank towards its next stage of sustainable growth. AMP Bank operates two divisions, both serving Australian domestic customers. One (Lending &Everyday Banking) is focused on Home Lending, primarily via Intermediated channel (brokers / planners) and the second is a new Digital only division (AMP Bank GO), serving mini business and consumers, primarily around their Everyday Banking needs.

The Head of Bank Strategy & Transformation is a critical role in ensuring the Bank delivers against its strategy This role leads the charge in turning strategy into action. You’ll drive alignment, uplift agile delivery, and ensure our change portfolio delivers real value – fast. With a sharp focus on outcomes, people leadership, and innovation, you’ll help shape the future of the Bank and how we deliver for our customers.

You’ll make an impact by:

*   Maximise value return via ongoing uplifts in agile centric ways of working across the end to end Bank portfolio
*   Provide leadership, support and coaching for all Bank change resources, including those within business roles.
*   Improving the sustainable practices and engagement of delivery teams
*   Championing & reinforcing a culture of continuous improvement
*   Ensure Portfolio metrics and analytics is in place and effective.
*   Ensure strong and active business ownership, adoption and realisation of benefits from the change investment
*   Foster and champion experimentation with new technologies, including AI across the Bank value streams

**What you will bring to the team**

*   Demonstrated experience (10+ years) providing leadership and direction of major transformational/business Projects / Change Program, involving strategic value and high impact for the organisation.
*   Demonstrated experience initiating & operating an Agile / lean way of working
*   Experience in fostering & developing usage of emerging technologies, especially within AI & Data Analytics domains
*   Financial Services industry experience is highly desirable.
*   Deep knowledge of modern Portfolio Management principles, approaches, techniques and tools.
*   Sound understanding of business strategy and architecture principles.
*   Proven results in large and or complex scale change.
*   Proven results in people leadership, talent management and working in matrix management structures.
*   Proven experience in developing an innovation pipeline and effecting breakthrough change.
*   Industry certification, including in Agile practices, required
