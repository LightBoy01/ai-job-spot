Currently Recruit 121 is seeking a **Business Process Manager** to support a global External Manufacturing & Supply organisation undergoing rapid expansion.

You will join a central team responsible for PMO and Master Data Operations for external manufacturing globally.

This is a highly collaborative role working with technical experts such as production planners and PMO specialists. Your key value is the ability to turn technical complexity into clear, business-friendly global operating procedures, SOPs, work instructions, and process documentation that can be used reliably across international operations.

Tasks
-----

*   Translate process mapping and gap assessments into clear, global business procedures
*   Write and maintain GOPs, SOPs, work instructions, and operational documentation
*   Collaborate with cross-functional experts to capture and standardise processes
*   Support global process alignment across external manufacturing partners
*   Drive change management initiatives and stakeholder engagement
*   Support KPI, metrics, and performance tracking where relevant

Requirements
------------

*   Strong experience in **process documentation** (GOPs, SOPs, work instructions)
*   **Business process management** with a track record in global standardisation
*   Background in **manufacturing or supply chain operations**
*   Experience with **SAP operations** (execution, planning, purchasing, goods movement)
*   **Process mapping** and **gap analysis** capability
*   Effective **change management** and stakeholder engagement skills
*   Excellent written and verbal communication in English
