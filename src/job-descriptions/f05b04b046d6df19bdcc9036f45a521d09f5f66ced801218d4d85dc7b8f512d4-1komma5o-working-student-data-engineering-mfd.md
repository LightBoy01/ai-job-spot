1KOMMA5°
--------

We are looking for a **Working Student in Data Engineering** to join our Heartbeat Tech team in Berlin, Hamburg, or Munich. 1KOMMA5° is creating Germany's largest one-stop-shop for the sale, installation, and servicing of solar panels, heat pumps, electricity solutions, and charging infrastructure. And the best part? They are all interconnected! The Heartbeat Tech team drives the development of our end customer app, helping users understand how they generate, store, and consume energy in their homes. You’ll get hands-on experience working at the intersection of software, hardware, and clean energy - and help build the reliable data foundation that empowers thousands of households across Europe.

Become a part of our mission and learn about our Product & Tech team!

Deine Position
--------------

Join our Heartbeat Tech team and help shape the data backbone supporting the digital experience of thousands of end customers using our energy management app. As a Working Student in Data Engineering, you will support the Energy Data team in building robust data flows and pipelines that enable users to track, understand, and optimize their home energy systems.

*   **Product Analytics:** create dashboards and reports for monitoring of our Heartbeat app
*   **Analyze Measurements and Systems:** Dive deep into measurements, pricing, and system data to calculate meaningful and transparent insights.
*   **Ensure Data Quality:** Write audit SQL queries to maintain and establish high data quality standards across our systems.
*   **Data Ownership:** Support the team in taking ownership of data flows across our systems, ensuring reliability and performance for the Heartbeat App.
*   **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Collaborate closely with product managers, engineers, designers, and developers to deliver data-driven solutions and address critical business questions.
