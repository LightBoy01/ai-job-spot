Ladder is looking for a rockstar **Team Manager** to help drive the quality, consistency, and performance of our coach-led fitness programs (we call them teams) on the Ladder app. Team Managers own the day-to-day systems, insights, and standards that ensure every team delivers an exceptional member experience while driving meaningful business results.

Our Team Managers are the engines behind the success of each Ladder coach — deeply embedded in their programs, trusted partners in strategy and execution, and champions of the member experience. You'll work directly with a cohort of Ladder coaches to make sure their programming is top-tier, their community is engaged, and their performance is moving the needle.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO**
------------------

#### **🚀 Ownership & Execution**

*   Own the day-to-day execution and performance of your assigned Ladder teams
*   Serve as the final line of defense for everything members see — programming, content, and community — ensuring it exceeds our quality standards
*   Review workout programming weekly, complete Ladder workouts to stay close to the user experience and engage in Team Chat (in-app community chats)

#### **🤝 Coach Partnership**

*   Serve as the primary liaison between Ladder and your assigned coaches, building high-trust, impact-focused relationships
*   Be a reliable, strategic partner — balancing support and accountability in service of growth, retention, and member experience
*   Support coaches in delivering best-in-class workout programming, community engagement, and performance improvements

#### **📊 Insights & Guidance**

*   Monitor Ladder team performance using platform metrics, survey data, and member feedback
*   Maintain a deep understanding of your team’s target user and ensure all programming and content aligns with their goals and preferences
*   Meet regularly with coaches to translate data and feedback into clear, actionable guidance that improves team outcomes

#### **🔄 Cross-Functional Collaboration**

*   Partner with internal teams across Marketing, Product, and Support to ensure alignment and worldclass execution
*   Surface key insights from the community to inform product development and feature improvements
*   Participate in team-level and platform-level initiatives to improve internal systems, tools, and workflows

**WHAT YOU BRING**
------------------

*   2+ years of experience in business operations, program/project management, or account management

*   Highly organized, detail-oriented, and outcomes-driven
*   Strong communication skills
*   Comfort working with data and transforming it into actionable insights
*   Able to independently build and analyze surveys to gather feedback and drive team-level improvements
*   Creative, curious, proactive, and scrappy — you love building
*   Experience in a startup or fast-paced, ownership-driven environment
*   Comfortable using AI tools to supercharge your workflows — or eager to learn
