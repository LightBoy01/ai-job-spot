<p><b>Visual Design Services Expert</b> </p>

<p> </p>
<p><strong>Your core tasks &amp; responsibilities: <br><br></strong></p>
<ul>
<li>You analyze requirements, develop concepts, and execute delivery in a timely manner of visual design projects of various complexity and nature (print, graphic, web design, video)</li>
<li>You work directly with multiple global customers on end-to-end project you develop based on received feedback </li>
<li>You collaborate with other creative/technical teams within the Visual Design Service (VDS) global team and ensure the high quality of delivered materials</li>
</ul>
<p> </p>
<p><strong>You will also do: <br><br></strong></p>
<ul>
<li>You perform and review quality checks with a high level of complexity </li>
<li>You support project coordinator in communication with stakeholders </li>
<li>You adapt and develop work delivered by creative agencies</li>
<li>You contribute to process development and innovation within the global team</li>
</ul>
<p> </p>
<p> <strong>Who You Are: <br><br></strong></p>
<ul>
<li>You feel comfortable working with imagery, layout and typography, and experience in both print and digital design</li>
<li>You have knowledge of graphic programs like (Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop and inDesign) </li>
<li>You speak and write in English at C1 </li>
<li>You have good customer service skills </li>
<li>You pay attention to detail, have proactive attitude, organizational skills, and high sense of responsibility</li>
<li>You are passionate for innovation, technology, you feel comfortable about using AI tools in your daily job</li>
<li>You have experience in international projects or teams </li>
<li>Experience in video projects and Adobe Premiere, After Effects software skills, beneficial</li>
<li><strong>Graduate of Graphic Design or any other related course OR extensive professional experience in the respective process</strong>  </li>
</ul>
