**Company Description:**

Design the operations domain for our clients through business models, processes, and the implementation of (technical) solutions to help them achieve their strategic objectives and guide them through their business transformation. At Deloitte. 

**Job Description:**

*   Analyze processes, operating models, and the broader ecosystem to identify opportunities for optimization and innovation;
*   design and implement smart, data-driven, and technology-driven solutions – such as automation and AI – to sustainably improve performance;
*   prepare client materials for workshops and client sessions and taking responsibility for the quality of work;
*   support project management activities and industry (i.e. Consumer; Energy, Resources & Industrials (ER&I) and Technology, Media & Telecommunication (TMT)) and/or market proposals;
*   organize internal meetings and projects and support broader team development initiatives.

**Qualifications:**

Naturally, you are solution-oriented and effectively communicate ideas that solve problems. More importantly, you bring your own qualities, perspective on the world, and personality with you. As a Junior Consultant, you will have:

*   A Master's degree from a reputable university with strong academic results;
*   experience with extracurricular activities or relevant experiences within student, study, or sports organizations;
*   a strong interest in consultancy, demonstrated through your studies, internships, or work experience;
*   affinity with one of the topics, while showing interest in the others: operating model, process improvement, cost transformation, AI & automation, business operations, and transformations;
*   an excellent command of written and spoken English (and preferably Dutch).

**Additional Information:**

As a Junior Consultant, you’ll work in a multidisciplinary team from Deloitte to deliver value to our clients across the full transformation lifecycle. This starts from defining an operations strategy to (re)designing, optimizing and implementing the operating model and core processes. Using strategic thinking and (digital) technologies as a starting point, you contribute to building future-proof solutions to ensure our clients operate more efficient, customer-oriented, and sustainable.

Together with your team you develop innovative and scalable solutions that help our clients on key topics such as future of retail, energy transition, or 5G. Within our team you will have the opportunity to work with and learn from industry experts with a broad scale of expertise. You'll work, learn, and grow in an innovative culture that thrives on shared success, diverse ways of thinking and offers countless opportunities.
