<p>Keysight is on the forefront of technology innovation, delivering breakthroughs and trusted insights in electronic design, simulation, prototyping, test, manufacturing, and optimization. Our ~15,000 employees create world-class solutions in communications, 5G, automotive, energy, quantum, aerospace, defense, and semiconductor markets for customers in over 100 countries. Learn more about what we do.</p><p> </p><p>Our award-winning culture embraces a bold vision of where technology can take us and a passion for tackling challenging problems with industry-first solutions. We believe that when people feel a sense of belonging, they can be more creative, innovative, and thrive at all points in their careers.</p><p> </p><p>Join Keysight’s central Data &amp; Analytics organization as a key contributor to the Growth Insights team. In this mid-level role, you will apply advanced analytics, Business Intelligence, and Generative AI techniques to uncover insights and drive data-informed growth strategies across Keysight’s hardware, software, and services portfolio.</p><p>You will work cross-functionally with product, sales, marketing, and strategy teams to support enterprise-wide growth initiatives, reporting to a senior leader within the Data &amp; Analytics organization.</p>

Responsibilities:
<ul><li>Analyze Growth Opportunities: Collaborate with business stakeholders to explore complex growth challenges using data analytics, predictive modeling, and Gen AI tools to generate actionable insights.</li><li>Develop AI-Enhanced Dashboards: Build and maintain dynamic, Gen AI-powered dashboards and reporting tools that provide foresight and prescriptive recommendations to business teams.</li><li>Support ML Model Deployment: Assist in the implementation and monitoring of machine learning models for use cases such as sales recommendations, churn prediction, and financial forecasting.</li><li>Enable Agentic AI Solutions: Contribute to the development and testing of Agentic AI workflows that automate growth-related tasks across platforms like Salesforce, Hubspot, Eloqua, and Oracle Cloud.</li><li>Collaborate on Data Infrastructure: Work with Data Engineering to ensure data pipelines and architecture support scalable, AI-native analytics solutions.</li><li>Promote Data Literacy: Help maintain the enterprise data catalog and support internal data literacy initiatives by enabling self-service analytics and sharing best practices.</li><li>Deliver Executive Insights: Prepare data visualizations and summaries that support strategic decision-making by senior leadership, including the Chief Data &amp; Analytics Officer.</li></ul>

Qualifications:
