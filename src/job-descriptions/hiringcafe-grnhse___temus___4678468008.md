_Temus was established by Temasek in partnership with UST, to provide digital transformation solutions for the private and public sectors as we aspire to be a strategic partner in realising the Singapore Government’s Smart Nation vision. We are headquartered in Singapore and have more than 400 employees across a wide range of disciplines in strategy, design, architecture, technology, data & AI._

**Your objectives**

*   Solve the hardest problems with AI to deliver social and economic value
*   Exceed client expectations becoming a trusted partner in enterprise transformation
*   Recruit, inspire, lead and mentor a team of the strongest AI engineers
*   Create a culture of excellence and lead with confidence, charisma, context, and humility working effectively at all levels
*   Create and deliver world-class thought leadership on AI, win the confidence of business leaders and drive business adoption of AI
*   Maintain public trust in AI ensuring fairness, ethics, accountability, and transparency

**Your background**

*   Exceptional expertise in AI engineering, machine learning and data science
*   Practical hands-on experience with a broad range of tools to assist in the AI development lifecycle: LLMs, Jupyter, PyTorch, Tensorflow, Hugging Face Transformers, AWS Bedrock, AWS Sagemaker, Google Vertex AI, Azure ML, etc.
*   15 years of experience delivering and managing AI solutions through incubation, proofs-of-concept, to deployment and commercialisation
*   Strong understanding of the marketplace for AI services with the ability to develop go-to-market plans supported by trusted partnerships
*   Ability to build client relationships, grow engagements and deliver economic value
*   Ability to develop and deliver client proposals, and build consensus supported by detailed analysis, deep expertise and effective communication
*   Strong track record of leading delivery of AI large scale solutions through incubation, proofs-of-concept, to deployment and commercialisation
*   Demonstrated ability to attract, inspire, lead, coach and mentor outstanding engineers
*   Demonstrated ability to create and deliver world-class AI thought leadership, engage senior leaders and become their trusted advisor
*   Talented speaker and educator with active engagement in the AI community taking and giving courses, following podcasts, reading books, attending meetups, and developing projects
*   A good sense of humour 😉
