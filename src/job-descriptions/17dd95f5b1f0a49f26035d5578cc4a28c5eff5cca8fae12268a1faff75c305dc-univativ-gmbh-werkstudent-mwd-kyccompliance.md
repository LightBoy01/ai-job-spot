### Description\nUnivativ connects talented students and graduates with exciting opportunities at leading companies. We are passionate about your career development. We're looking for a proactive Working Student (m/f/d) to join our team in Frankfurt am Main, supporting Project Managers in the organization, steering, and control of critical projects within the financial sector. This is a long-term opportunity, with an option for extension and potential takeover, requiring approximately 20 hours per week.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Collaborate closely with Onboarding, Legal, and Risk Management teams to ensure regulatory compliance and further develop compliance processes.\n*   Perform key tasks including reviewing and maintaining customer master data in accordance with legal requirements, and assisting in identifying and assessing potential risks in customer relationships.\n*   Support internal research, as well as the creation of reports and documentation.\n*   Work with various internal departments to ensure seamless information flow.\n*   Contribute to the further development and optimization of compliance processes to continuously improve efficiency and quality.\n*   Utilize a meticulous, structured, and reliable approach to ensure all legal and internal requirements are consistently met.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Currently enrolled as a student (m/f/d) in Business, Law, Finance, or a comparable field of study, with a strong interest in compliance, KYC, anti-money laundering, and regulatory topics.\n*   Possess a meticulous, structured, and reliable working style that contributes to efficient and precise process implementation.\n*   Proficient in MS Office, especially Excel, and accustomed to using these tools routinely in daily work.\n*   Very good German language skills (written and spoken) for clear and professional communication; good English skills are a plus.\n*   High sense of responsibility and a strong degree of discretion.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a dedicated student to gain hands-on experience in a critical compliance role within the finance sector in Frankfurt.
