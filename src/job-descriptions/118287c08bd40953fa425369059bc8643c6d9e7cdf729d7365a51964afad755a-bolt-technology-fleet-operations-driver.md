### Description\nBolt is seeking a motivated and hands-on Fleet Operations Driver to join their team, playing a crucial role in deploying e-scooters across the city and ensuring their readiness for users. As one of Europe and Africa's fastest-growing tech companies with over 150 million users in 45+ countries, Bolt is driven by its mission to create cities for people, not cars, fostering an inclusive environment where everyone is welcome.\n\nIn this role, you will be responsible for maintaining organized, clutter-free cities by effectively distributing e-scooters, ensuring they are sufficiently charged, safe to ride, and properly rebalanced. Your dedication to high safety standards will be key to providing customers with a top-notch transport experience.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Driving Bolt vans to collect and deploy e-scooters or swap batteries.\n- Ensuring e-scooters are parked neatly, keeping streets and sidewalks clear.\n- Searching for lost e-scooters, assessing their condition, and either returning them to the warehouse or making them available for use.\n- Performing minor repairs directly on the street to maximize fleet availability.\n- Assisting the Operations team in the warehouse as needed.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Must hold an EU B driving license and be proficient in operating cars with a manual transmission.\n- A skilled, trustworthy driver with excellent knowledge of the local area, committed to ensuring safe and secure journeys. Previous professional driving experience is advantageous.\n- Demonstrates a proactive, hands-on approach and a strong willingness to take ownership of challenges.\n- Solid local language skills and practical experience using smartphones and mechanical tools.\n- English skills are an advantage.\n- Bolt values drive, intelligence, and integrity, encouraging applications even if not all criteria are met.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an an excellent entry-level opportunity for a hands-on individual passionate about urban mobility and making a tangible impact. As a Fleet Operations Driver at Bolt, you'll be on the front lines of revolutionizing city transport, ensuring scooters are available, charged, and safe for a massive user base. Bolt offers a fast-paced, inclusive environment with a strong mission and clear paths for professional growth, valuing drive and integrity over just experience. You'll enjoy perks like Bolt credits for free rides and team events, all while contributing to a greener, more accessible city. If you're a skilled local driver looking for a dynamic role with a purpose, this could be your next move.
