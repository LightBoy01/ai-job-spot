### Description\nFintegra Group develops digital solutions at the intersection of tax law, wealth management, and technology. Our platforms and products support tax advisors and financial service providers in the automated processing of complex wealth data – securely, scalably, and using the latest technology.\n\nWe are looking for a **Senior UI Developer (m/f/d)** with high technical expertise, a clear presence in the Angular ecosystem, and a passion for modern architecture to strengthen our Frontend team. In this role, you will significantly shape the frontends of our enterprise applications, working closely with Backend, Product, and UX teams, and ensuring high technical quality in our development processes.\n\n*   **Location**: Nuremberg or fully remote\n*   **Start**: Immediately\n*   **Employment**: Full-time\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Development & maintenance of modern **Single Page Applications (SPA)** based on Angular (v20)\n*   Implementation of sophisticated UI components with **Angular Material** and **Bootstrap 5**\n*   State-management using **NgRx** (Store, Effects, Entity, DevTools)\n*   Implementation of **Authentication & Security** (JWT, Auth0)\n*   Ensuring testing & code quality\n*   Performance optimization & implementation of modern architectural principles\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Fluent English skills, ideally at business level\n*   Several years of experience in developing Angular applications\n*   Experience with **TypeScript**, **RxJS**, WebSocket communication, and reactive architectural patterns\n*   Very good knowledge and experience with Angular Material, Bootstrap & Custom Components\n*   Know-how in NgRx-based state-management approaches\n*   Knowledge of security concepts & JWT authentication\n*   Team orientation, strong communication skills & an eye for good UX/UI design\n*   Openness to new AI tools in application development\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a Senior UI Developer passionate about building sophisticated enterprise applications within the Angular ecosystem.
