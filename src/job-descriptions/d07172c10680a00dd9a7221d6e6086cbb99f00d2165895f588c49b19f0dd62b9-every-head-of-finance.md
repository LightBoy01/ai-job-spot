**We’re hiring: Head of Finance (Berlin)**

Every is building the default way to eat better during the week. We’re a DTC food subscription business, with proven product-market fit, ready for the next 10x jump. We don’t do retail — we build our own channel, our own customer relationship, and our own operating system.  
Finance is a core lever to keep doubling without chaos.  
That’s why we’re hiring a Head of Finance to own the full finance function, build the systems for scale, and give the leadership team crystal-clear numbers to run the business.

**What you’ll own**

*   Monthly + annual financial statements and clean close rhythm
*   In-house accounting in DATEVasp (with Finance Manager + tax advisor)
*   Finance operations end-to-end, automation, and process quality
*   FP&A: forecasting, budgeting, scenario planning
*   Liquidity, cashflow, and working capital management
*   Unit economics, cost steering, and cross-pillar analysis
*   Data analytics + reporting stack for core metrics (with Tech lead)
*   Risk/legal/insurance topics at a pragmatic scale-up level
*   Building and leading the finance team for the next stage
*   ERP selection + rollout (co-owned with Ops)

What success looks like (12–24 months)

*   Finance becomes boringly reliable: fast close, trusted numbers
*   A real planning cadence supports doubling
*   Cash and working capital are tightly controlled
*   Dashboards and unit economics drive decisions
*   Finance tooling is coherent and ERP-ready
*   Multi-country complexity (VAT, customs, cross-boarder transactions) managed without bureaucracy

**What we’re looking for**

*   7–10+ years in finance leadership roles (scale-up and/or FMCG/DTC preferred)
*   Excellent accounting, controlling, and FP&A knowledge
*   Builder mindset: process design, automation, systems
*   Comfortable in multi-country setups and high-growth ambiguity
*   Strong cash and unit-economics intuition
*   Tools: DATEVasp, Moss, Easybill, Pathway, BigQuery
*   Fluent in German & English

If this sounds like your mountain, please send:

*   CV
*   a few bullets on the most complex finance system you built or scaled
*   and why Every
