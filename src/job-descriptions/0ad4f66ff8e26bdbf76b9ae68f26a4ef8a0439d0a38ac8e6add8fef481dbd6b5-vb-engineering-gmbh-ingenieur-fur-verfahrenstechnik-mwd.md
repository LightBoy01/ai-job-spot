### Description\nVB-Engineering GmbH is a young, modern, and future-oriented company specializing in high-quality engineering services for industrial and maritime plants. Efficiency, transparency, quality, and adherence to deadlines are core to our company philosophy. We focus on process engineering, energy-generating plants, and marine operating technology, improving both efficiency and safety. We are seeking an experienced, motivated, and technically and personally convincing Process Engineer (w/m/d) to join our interdisciplinary team of engineers, technicians, and specialists as soon as possible.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Develop, optimize, and evaluate process engineering processes and plants.\n*   Create technical documentation, e.g., P&ID schematics, safety and explosion protection documents.\n*   Perform process and component validations.\n*   Manage project planning, scheduling, and cost control.\n*   Coordinate interfaces between involved trades.\n*   Support on-site commissioning and technical clarifications.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed degree in Process Engineering or a comparable qualification.\n*   Experience in industrial plant construction, ideally in the chemical, refinery, or general process industry.\n*   Knowledge in creating technical documentation.\n*   Proficiency in CAD systems, e.g., AutoCAD.\n*   Independent, structured work approach and strong teamwork skills.\n*   Good communication skills in German (and ideally English).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an experienced Process Engineer looking to make a significant impact within a dynamic and supportive environment. VB-Engineering prides itself on its innovative and collaborative culture, offering diverse projects with real technical depth. You'll have ample room for personal growth and initiative, supported by a helpful team. The company also values work-life balance, providing flexible working hours and home office possibilities, alongside a commitment to long-term career perspectives. If you're passionate about optimizing complex systems and driving technical excellence, this role offers a truly rewarding challenge.
