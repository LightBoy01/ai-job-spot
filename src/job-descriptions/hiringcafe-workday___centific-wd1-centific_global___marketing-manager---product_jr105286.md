Our team includes more than 150 PhDs and data scientists, along with more than 4,000 AI practitioners and engineers. **About Job**

**Location:** Redmond, WA / US Hybrid  
**Department:** Product Marketing  
**Reports To:** VP of Product Management  
**Employment Type:** Full-Time

**About the Role**
------------------

We are looking for a **Product Marketing Manager (PMM)** with a strong background in **localization** and a passion for or growing knowledge of **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** for our US Region. In this role, you will partner closely with **Product Managers, Marketing, Sales, and Leadership** to bring innovative AI-driven products to market globally, ensuring they resonate across languages, cultures, and regions.

You will lead and execute go-to-market strategies, craft compelling product narratives, and ensure localized messaging aligns with user needs. This role requires a strategic thinker with strong execution skills, excellent cross-functional collaboration, and the curiosity to thrive in a fast-evolving AI ecosystem.

**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------

**Go-to-Market Strategy & Execution**

*   Partner with Product Managers to build marketing plans based on product roadmaps, feature releases, and customer insights.
    
*   Own and drive the product go-to-market (GTM) strategy, from positioning and messaging to content creation and channel strategy.
    
*   Create launch playbooks and manage product launches to drive awareness, adoption, and revenue.
    

**Localization & Globalization**

*   Develop product marketing strategies with a **local-first mindset**, ensuring regional and language-specific nuances are captured across messaging and campaigns.
    
*   Collaborate with localization teams and regional stakeholders to adapt messaging, content, and experiences for different geographies.
    
*   Drive internal awareness of localization impact on user adoption, experience, and brand perception.
    

**AI & Emerging Technology Readiness**

*   Translate complex AI and machine learning features into clear, customer-friendly benefits.
    
*   Stay abreast of evolving trends in Generative AI, NLP, and LLMs to shape messaging, content, and value propositions.
    
*   Work with Product and Sales Enablement teams to educate internal stakeholders on new AI-powered capabilities.
    

**Product Positioning, Messaging & Enablement**

*   Craft compelling product positioning and messaging that resonates with target audiences across verticals and regions.
    
*   Produce customer-facing assets such as datasheets, presentations, web content, and demo scripts.
    
*   Enable sales teams with tools, pitch decks, competitive intel, and battle cards tailored to regional needs.
    

**Market Research & Insights**

*   Conduct user research, competitive analysis, and win/loss interviews to influence positioning and roadmap.
    
*   Monitor performance of campaigns and launches and iterate based on results and feedback.
    

**Qualifications**
------------------

*   5+ years in **Product Marketing**, ideally in a B2B SaaS, AI, or tech company.
    
*   Demonstrated experience in **localization strategy**, execution, and stakeholder alignment across global markets.
    
*   Proven ability to collaborate effectively with **Product, Engineering, Sales, and Regional Marketing** teams.
    
*   Strong writing and storytelling skills with ability to simplify complex concepts, especially around AI.
    
*   Strategic thinker with a hands-on approach and bias for action.
    

**Preferred**

*   Understanding of **AI technologies**, especially GenAI/LLMs/NLP, or the ability to learn quickly and translate technical capabilities into value-based messaging.
    
*   Experience launching products across **multi-lingual markets**.
    
*   Background in enterprise or platform products is a plus.
    

**Key Competencies**
--------------------

*   Strategic and creative mindset
    
*   Excellent verbal and written communication
    
*   Highly collaborative and adaptable
    
*   Detail-oriented with strong project management skills
    
*   Analytical and data-driven decision making
