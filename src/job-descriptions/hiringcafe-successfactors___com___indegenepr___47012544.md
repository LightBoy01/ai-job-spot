Must Have
---------

ROLE: Analyst - Gen AI

  
Description:

Key Responsibilities: The position will be directly responsible for the following:

• Drive innovation through state-of-the-art LLM integration and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) strategies using Dataiku and modern AI frameworks  
• Experience with LLM Mesh, RAG pipelines, vector databases, embeddings, prompt engineering, and integration  
• Build applications powered by LLMs (OpenAI, Claude, Mistral, etc.) using LangChain, LlamaIndex, and related GenAI frameworks.   
• Implement RAG pipelines with vector DBs (Pinecone, FAISS, pgvector, ChromaDB) for grounding LLM responses with internal knowledge   
• Develop multimodal AI solutions (text, audio, image) and build autonomous agents where relevant.   
• Continuously improve model reliability, relevance by tuning prompt flows   
• Deploy GenAI models on AWS/GCP/Azure using services like SageMaker, Bedrock, Vertex AI   
• Ideate and design innovative solution frameworks aligning to market trends and technological innovations. Translating healthcare clients' business needs into technical solutions meeting user needs and business goals  
• POC building, go to market strategy building by application of AI/Gen AI use cases wherever applicable. Integrate large language models (LLMs), computer vision, or other AI/ML techniques into business solutions.

Technical Experience  
•  Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, AI/ML, Data Science, or related field.  
•  2-4 years of experience in software development, with at least 2 years in AI/ML or GenAI. Experience with LLM Mesh.  (preferred  - Dataiku LLM Mesh)  
•  Deep understanding of digital channels, marketing tactics, brand strategies, and marketing analytics  
•  Hands-on experience with LLMs (OpenAI, Claude, LLaMA, etc.), vector databases, and GenAI frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex, etc.).   
•  Proficiency in Python, pyspark and/or Java/JavaScript/TypeScript.  
•  Strong understanding of cloud platforms (Azure/AWS/GCP), especially AI-related services.  
•  Experience with frontend frameworks (React, Angular) and backend APIs.  
•  Pharma and Healthcare domain understanding is a good to have  
    

Skills  
Primary Skill: Proficient in Dataiku LLM mesh and RAG approaches    
Sub Skill(s): AI/ML Development  
Additional Skill(s): TensorFlow, NLP, Pytorch, GenAI Fundamentals
