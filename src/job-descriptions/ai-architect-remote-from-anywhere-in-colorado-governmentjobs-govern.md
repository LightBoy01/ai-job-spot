Position Duties<p><br></p><p><br></p><p><strong><strong>IMPORTANT NOTE:</strong></strong>&nbsp;Please review your application to ensure completion. For the most equitable applicant experience, OIT’s hiring team considers only the contents of your application to review your qualifications. Please do not include any attachments (such as resume or cover letter) with your application as these items&nbsp;are not used by OIT’s hiring team. &nbsp;</p><br><br><br><p>Do you want to make our beautiful state even better? At OIT, we provide secure digital services that put Coloradans first. We seek a skilled and motivated AI Architect to support the State of Colorado’s efforts in adopting and implementing artificial intelligence (AI) across government agencies. You will play a key role in designing and deploying scalable AI and GenAI solutions that align with strategic business objectives. If you’re passionate about bridging advanced technology and practical business value—this is a great opportunity.</p><br><p>Some of the roles opportunities include:</p><br><p>Designing and developing scalable, secure, and high-value end-to-end AI/ML solutions and architectural patterns.</p><br><p>Evaluating, selecting, and championing the appropriate AI technologies, frameworks, and tools for each project.</p><br><p>Providing technical leadership and guidance to ensure AI solutions are implemented correctly and seamlessly integrated with existing systems.</p><br><p>Translating complex technical concepts and project requirements into actionable insights for non-technical stakeholders and managing stakeholder expectations. You will act as a critical link between business needs and technical realities.&nbsp;</p><br><p>Identifying and mitigating technical risks and ethical considerations while ensuring the security and maintainability of all AI solutions.</p><br><p>Helping stakeholders understand what's possible, the risks involved, and the potential impact on the business.</p><br><p>Navigating the rapidly evolving AI landscape to propose and implement solutions that address core business challenges.&nbsp;</p><br><p>Evaluating and leading deployment of tools and platforms for AI/ML, NLP, computer vision, and predictive analytics.</p><br><p>Ensuring seamless integration of AI with systems, following data security and compliance standards.</p><br><p>Collaborating with business units to define KPIs, measure ROI, and drive value from AI projects.</p><br><p>Managing end-to-end AI project delivery—from scope to launch—while guiding cross-functional teams.</p><br><p></p>
