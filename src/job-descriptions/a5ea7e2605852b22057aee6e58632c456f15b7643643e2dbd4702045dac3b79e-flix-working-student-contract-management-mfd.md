At Flix, we offer a dynamic work environment with competitive pay, strong growth opportunities, and a tech-driven approach to making travel more accessible, sustainable, and affordable.

We're looking for a motivated and driven **Working Student – Contract Management (m/f/d)** who will help us shape our team, drive the company to the next level, and have the most direct influence on our success.  

This role is **based in Munich** (hybrid model).  

**About the Role**

*   Be part of the Global Business Excellence team, reporting to the project lead and manager for the Global Bus Partner Contract
*   Support the company-wide harmonization and implementation of Bus Partner Contracts as well as the automation and streamlining of Contract Management at Flix
*   Create, customize, and finalize contracts with the support of the team and the Flix Legal team
*   Take care of new contract requirements and customization needs and integrate them into the contract templates  
    

**About You**

*   **Currently enrolled Bachelor or Master student** in Economics, Finance, Business Administration, Law,  or a similar program, preferably with an international orientation and at least two semesters ahead
*   **Fluent in English and German**, both at C1 level; any additional language is a plus.
*   Focused on details and motivated to delve deeply into contracts to deliver high-quality work
*   Self-reliant, reliable, and conscientious
*   Sound knowledge of MS Word, Excel, and PowerPoint
*   Communicative, engaged, and flexible
*   Strong analytical and problem-solving skills

We recognize that everyone carries a unique set of valuable skills and experiences. If you think you could have an impact even though you don't meet 100% of the requirements, we still encourage you to apply. We want to hear from you!  

**What We Offer**

*   **Travel perks**: 12 free Flix vouchers + 12 discount vouchers for friends & family. 
*   **Work from (M)Anywhere**: Depending on your role, work from another location for up to 60 days per year. 
*   **Hybrid work model**: We are an office-first company, but we offer flexibility to balance work and life. 
*   **Wellbeing support**: Access confidential 1:1 counselling, courses, and stress management for yourself and up to four family members. 
*   **Learning & Development**: Take advantage of language classes, training courses, and expert-led sessions to grow your skills. 
*   **Mentoring Program**: Connect with experienced colleagues to gain insights and accelerate your career.
