**Role:** Project Manager (SEO), APAC

**Location:** Mumbai, India

**Work timings:** 7:00am to 4:00pm

**About the role:** 

We are seeking a SEO Project Manager to own the APAC SEO strategy and performance. You will build and manage a robust keyword playbook, train stakeholders, maintain SEO reporting across paid and organic channels, and deliver market-level insights that drive measurable growth. This role partners closely with content, web, analytics, and paid media teams to improve discoverability, engagement, and conversion across priority topics.

**What you will be doing:** 

*   Think with Google Keyword Playbook creation and management: build, maintain, and govern a scalable, taxonomy-based keyword playbook tailored to APAC markets and focus topics.
*   Create and share training documents on how to make the best use of the keyword playbook; conduct live and recorded training sessions for content, SEO, paid media, and market stakeholders.
*   SEO dashboard maintenance and biweekly performance tracking across paid and organic channels; ensure data accuracy, consistency, and clear narratives for decision-making.
*   Produce APAC markets bi-monthly report commentary and end-of-project analysis with actionable recommendations.
*   Provide one monthly competitive insight package for APAC with SERP screenshots, focused on priority content topics for TWG and Ads.google.com.
*   Monitor performance of top-performing pages on TWG and Ads.google.com, especially during migrations; track technical health and SEO performance, propose key SEO considerations, and analyze site architecture for internal linking opportunities.
*   Conduct competitor gap analyses, identify keyword opportunities, and provide insights to optimize TWG and Ads-related content across the funnel.
*   Partner with content editors and product marketing to influence editorial roadmaps, briefs, and on-page optimization (titles, headers, internal links, schema, UX elements).
*   Collaborate with web and engineering teams to prioritize and validate technical SEO enhancements (CWV, crawl/indexation, hreflang, sitemaps, redirects, canonicalization).
*   Align paid and organic strategies to maximize share of voice on priority topics, optimize SERP real estate, and improve CTR.
*   Socialize insights and drive adoption of recommendations across global and in-market stakeholders; establish governance and QA processes.

**What you need to be great in this role:**

*   7+ years of hands-on SEO experience across technical, content, and analytics for enterprise websites
*   Strong command of keyword research, topic clustering, and content performance optimization.
*   Comfortable building and maintaining multi-source dashboards; skilled at translating data into clear stories and actions.
*   Excellent stakeholder management, training, and communication skills; able to influence cross-functional teams and senior leaders.
*   Solid understanding of how paid search and organic search interact; experience aligning strategies across channels.
*   Passion for and inquisitive about AI and new technologies
*   Understanding and knowledge of AI tools is beneficial, but ability to learn and digest benefits and features of AI tools is critical.
