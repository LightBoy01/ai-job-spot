**Intro to GF**
GlobalFoundries is a leading full-service semiconductor foundry providing a unique combination of design, development, and fabrication services to some of the world’s most inspired technology companies. With a global manufacturing footprint spanning three continents, GlobalFoundries makes possible the technologies and systems that transform industries and give customers the power to shape their markets. For more information, visit [www.gf.com](http://www.gf.com/).
**Why GF**
Be a part of our global team that is at the forefront of providing cutting-edge global technology solutions. We are seeking candidates with a strong passion for new technologies and advanced manufacturing, as we continue our Industry 4.0 Digitalization journey using technologies such as Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, robotics, big data analytics and more.
Your work will have a direct impact in our expanding Fab Operations, in building out our platforms of purpose-built technologies such as smart mobile devices, personal computing, home and industrial IoT, automotive and more. You will have access to the latest technological developments at GF Labs, with opportunities for patent submissions, presenting your ideas on a global platform, and enjoy GF-wide collaboration across our fabs and offices around the world.
**Why This Role**
As the ideal candidate, you will leverage your technical strength, analytical and problem-solving skills and play a vital role to fuel our growth in Process Engineering.  Your role will span across existing and new Fabs, improving processes and thereby wafer yields, increasing manufacturing productivity and lowering cost that are key to achieving Fab excellence. Coaching engineers will also be part of your job responsibilities.
**What You Will Do**
*   Support production line, SPC/FDC (statistical process control / fault detection classification) monitoring, perform process window study and provide improvement plan to improve process stability and performance.
*   Lead Process CIP (continuous improvement projects) in department to improve process robustness, cost and tool capacity throughput
*   Develop and characterize new process recipes for new/existing technologies and NPI (New Product Introduction), meeting qualification and reliability requirements
*   Improve process performance indices adopting big data analytic, statistical analysis and structured problem-solving methodologies
*   Collaborate/work with cross functional teams, cross fabs and OEM suppliers for BKM (best known method) convergence on recipes, material, method, and resolving complex integration issue
*   Drive company's production ramp schedule by process qualification, productivity improvement and new tool release.
*   Train, coach, and guide Engineers to support daily operational requirements and impart process knowledge, troubleshooting skills, risk assessment and decision making.
*   Opportunities for job rotation and overseas assignment.
*   Opportunities to file patents, author technical papers for publication and present at local or overseas seminars.
**What You Will Need**
*   MS/BS in Chemical Engineering, Electrical/Electronic Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Materials Science Engineering, Chemistry & Physics with minimum 3 years of relevant experience.
*   Diploma candidates with minimum 5 years of work experience in similar role.
*   Demonstrated ability in structured problem solving and root cause analysis with solution development.
*   Strong knowledge in SPC (Statistical Process Control), risk assessment, DOE (Design of Experiment) and 8D approaches.
*   Experience in big data analysis and use of AI/ML.
*   Strong technical depth and having patents and papers publication is a plus.
