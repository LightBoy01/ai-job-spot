We’re seeking a future team member for the role of **Senior Vice President, Deal Management Manager** to join our **Wealth Services Platform** **RFP** team. This role is located in _Lake Mary, Florida and Jersey City, NJ_

**In this role, you’ll make an impact in the following ways:**

*   Lead, mentor, and grow the RFP team, driving performance, accountability, and professional development.
*   Define and execute the overall RFP strategy aligned with business growth objectives.
*   Partner with senior business leaders, product teams, and client relationship managers to position the firm competitively in RFP responses.
*   Oversee the end-to-end RFP lifecycle: intake, analysis, response development, quality review, submission, and post-mortem feedback.
*   Ensure responses are accurate, compliant, and differentiated, reflecting the firm’s strengths in wealth and financial services.
*   Maintain a centralized knowledge base and content library for efficient and consistent RFP production.
*   Own and manage RFPs for top opportunities, personally leading process and quality assurance for BAU RFPs as a player/coach.
*   Implement and leverage AI-driven RFP automation tools (e.g., RFPIO, Loopio, or custom AI solutions) to increase efficiency and accuracy.
*   Explore opportunities to improve generative AI and natural language processing for drafting, content tagging, and knowledge management.
*   Collaborate with AI Hub and innovation teams to integrate AI, data analytics, and workflow automation into the RFP process.
*   Act as a strategic advisor to business development and client coverage teams on proposal positioning.
*   Partner with subject matter experts (investment, operations, compliance, and technology) to ensure responses are aligned with client needs.
*   Represent the RFP team in senior leadership meetings and cross-functional committees.
*   Establish KPIs and reporting to measure RFP success rates, turnaround times, and client feedback.
*   Continuously improve processes, templates, and content management through technology and best practices.
*   Ensure compliance with regulatory, legal, and brand standards.

**To be successful in this role, we’re seeking the following:**

*   8+ years of experience in RFP management within financial services, asset/wealth management, or banking.
*   Proven track record of leading and scaling RFP teams.
*   Strong understanding of wealth management products, investment solutions, and financial regulations.
*   Expertise in AI/ML applications, automation tools, and knowledge management systems.
*   Exceptional project management, writing, and communication skills.
*   Strong stakeholder management and ability to influence senior executives.
*   Strategic thinker with an innovation mindset.
*   Attention to detail and deep technical acumen in AI and automation tools.
*   Exceptional leadership, coaching abilities, and people development skills.
*   Ability to translate complex financial concepts into client-centric messaging.
*   Resilient, agile, highly collaborative, and able to work under pressure with tight deadlines.
*   Voracious learner.
