Overview:
<p>How you can make a difference  </p><p>We are seeking a Workforce Strategist to join our Enterprise AI and workforce transformation team. This role supports the design and delivery of workforce strategies that enable AI adoption, unlock productivity, and prepare our workforce for the future.The Workforce Strategist will work closely with HR, business unit leaders, and transformation teams to:</p><ul><li>Conduct workforce analysis.</li><li>Identify opportunities for AI-driven productivity and role evolution.</li><li>Support change management, training, and communication efforts.</li></ul><p> </p><p>What you’ll be doing </p><ul><li>Program Support: Support delivery of training, enablement, and adoption programs</li><li>Enterprise AI Roadmap Support: Partner with senior leaders and the Enterprise AI Core Team to gather requirements, document business needs, and contribute to the development of the enterprise AI roadmap.</li><li>Change &amp; Comms: Help design communications, toolkits, and resources to drive AI adoption and workforce readiness.</li><li>Metrics &amp; Tracking: Support tracking of workforce KPIs, including adoption and enablement</li><li>Research &amp; Insights: Stay current on workforce modernization and AI/automation trends, and bring insights into planning.</li></ul><p> </p><p>What you will need to be successful</p><p> </p><ul><li>3–5+ years of experience in workforce planning, HR strategy, organizational effectiveness, or related consulting.</li><li>Exposure to AI/automation concepts and enthusiasm for workforce innovation.</li><li>Strong analytical, organizational, and communication skills.</li><li>Experience working cross-functionally and driving projects to completion.</li></ul><p> </p><p>Education</p><ul><li>Bachelor’s degree required.</li><li>Master’s degree in Organizational Development (preferred), Human Resources, Business Administration, or a related field a plus.</li></ul><p> </p><p>#LI-Remote</p><p>This is a remote position.</p>
