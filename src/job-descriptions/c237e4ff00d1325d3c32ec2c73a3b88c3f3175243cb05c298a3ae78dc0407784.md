**The Role:**

*   _Business Analyst Responsibilities_
    *   _Engage with stakeholders to gather, analyse, and document business requirements for Agentic AI, Gen AI, and ML projects._
    *   _Translate complex AI business needs into functional specifications, user stories, and acceptance criteria._
    *   _Ensure alignment between business objectives and advanced AI/ML technical solutions._
    *   _Conduct gap analysis and recommend process improvements for AI-driven workflows._
    *   _Collaborate with data scientists, AI engineers, and development teams to define and refine project scope._
    *   _Support testing efforts by validating requirements and assisting with UAT, especially for AI/ML models and agentic systems._
    *   _Ensure compliance with SOC1/SOC2, data privacy, and internal security standards._
*   _Scrum Master Responsibilities_
    *   _Act as a servant leader and facilitate Agile ceremonies (Sprint Planning, Stand-ups, Retrospectives, etc.) for AI-focused teams._
    *   _Guide teams on Agile best practices and remove impediments, with a focus on AI/ML project delivery._
    *   _Foster a culture of continuous improvement and ensure adherence to Agile principles in AI development._
    *   _Enable communication and collaboration between business, technical, and AI research teams._
    *   _Track sprint progress and report key metrics to stakeholders, including AI model performance and delivery milestones._
    *   _Encourage team accountability and ensure timely delivery of sprint objectives in Agentic and Gen AI projects._

**The Requirements:**

*   _Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or related field._
*   _10–11 years of experience in Business Analysis and Agile Scrum Management, with at least 2–3 years in Agentic AI, Gen AI, or ML projects._
*   _Strong understanding of Agentic AI concepts, Generative AI, and Machine Learning lifecycle._
*   _Hands-on experience in delivering projects involving agentic systems, autonomous agents, or generative models._
*   _Proficiency in Agile frameworks (Scrum, Kanban) and SDLC, especially in AI/ML environments._
*   _Proficiency in Azure DevOps or similar Agile tools._
*   _Experience in stakeholder management and working with cross-functional teams, including AI/ML specialists._
*   _Ability to define requirements, create user stories, and manage backlogs for AI/ML projects._
*   _Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and communication skills._
*   _Certified Scrum Master (CSM/PSM) and/or Certified Business Analyst (CBAP) preferred._
*   _Experience with regulatory and ethical considerations in AI projects is a plus._
