### Description
tectake is a dynamic e-commerce company with a broad and steadily growing product range covering home, household, leisure, and hobby. Through continuous optimizations, we continue our successful development both nationally and internationally. To drive this success and the rapid growth of recent years into the future, we are looking for a **Customer Service Specialist (m/f/d)** in full-time for our headquarters in Igersheim.

At tectake, we believe in creating a supportive and engaging work environment. You'll enjoy the presence of **Office Dogs**, contributing to a relaxed atmosphere. We offer **Corporate Benefits**, including attractive employee discounts on our product range. We strongly support **Family, Friends, and Career** through flexible working hours and a modern workplace, ensuring an optimal balance between professional and private life. Our **Team Spirit** is fostered through joint sports events and team activities. Furthermore, we provide support for **further education and team building**, ergonomic workstations, company health management, and quick decision-making processes within an open corporate culture. Free catering options are also provided.

### Responsibilities
- Support customers from quotation requests to delivery inquiries and package delivery, providing optimal assistance via phone and email.
- Contribute process optimization suggestions and ideas to increase the efficiency of work processes.
- Process complaint and grievance cases as part of customer support.
- Professional handling of spare part deliveries, exchanges, and repairs.

### Qualifications
- Completed commercial vocational training and professional experience in direct customer contact.
- Initial experience in administration and customer support via email and telephone.
- Technical understanding to support customers with problem-solving.
- Independent, structured, and meticulous working style, as well as a quick grasp.
- Proficient in common Microsoft Office programs.
- Very good German language skills, both written and spoken.
- Strong service orientation and empathy in customer contact.
