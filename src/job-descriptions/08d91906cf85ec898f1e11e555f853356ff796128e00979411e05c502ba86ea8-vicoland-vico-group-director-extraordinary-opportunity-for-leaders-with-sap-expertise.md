### Description\nVicoland is revolutionizing the professional services market by introducing \"Virtual Companies\" – \"Vicos.\" Our AI-driven SaaS platform enables freelance experts to team up and operate like real companies, successfully competing for large enterprise projects. Already, over 100 Vicos, comprising thousands of freelancers, are delivering projects for enterprise clients such as BASF, Miele, Siemens, and Zeiss, as well as numerous SMEs. Forbes and Forrester have recognized Vicoland as a leading force in market transformation.\n\nOur SAP-certified platform provides the essential digital infrastructure for forming and operating Vicos, covering marketing, finance, legal, and organization. A significant focus of our continued growth is within SAP services, with many Vicos specializing in areas from SAP S/4HANA transformations to highly specific application modules. We are actively seeking an ambitious, SAP-savvy leader who currently heads projects and teams within a consulting firm or system integrator. This role offers an unparalleled opportunity to rise further and faster, taking a significant entrepreneurial step.\n\nAs the Vico Group Director, you will accelerate our growth by providing entrepreneurial guidance and mentoring to our top-performing Vicos. You will start by leading and growing a group of approximately 10 Vicos, which already serve enterprise clients and comprise several hundred experienced professionals. Working directly with Vico Leads (the Vicos' founders/bosses), you will act as \"an entrepreneur leading entrepreneurs,\" ensuring Vicos are strategically well-positioned and deliver the execution
