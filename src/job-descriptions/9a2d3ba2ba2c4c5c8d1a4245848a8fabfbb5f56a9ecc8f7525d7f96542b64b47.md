The Role
--------

You’ll be responsible for:

*   **Own end‑to‑end delivery** for multiple client projects—from onboarding and scheduling through to final content publication.
*   **Build and maintain ClickUp boards**, SOPs, templates, and an AI prompt library; monitor capacity, dependencies, and deadlines.
*   **Coordinate internal and freelance teams** (writers, designers, video editors), providing clear briefs and enforcing QA checklists.
*   **Publish approved content** on client websites and social channels (WordPress, Buffer, HubSpot, SproutSocial) as well as Content 10x platforms.
*   **Communicate with clients** on status, next steps, and issue‑resolution; cultivate trusted relationships that drive retention.
*   **Embed AI and automation** to streamline workflows without compromising quality.
*   **Line‑manage one Ops Assistant**, providing coaching, feedback, and development pathways.

The Person
----------

*   3–5 years’ project, operations, or client‑services experience (agency/B2B environment a plus but not essential).
*   Advanced user of ClickUp (Trello/Asana transferable). Comfortable with WordPress, Buffer/HubSpot/SproutSocial, Descript/Riverside.
*   Proficient with generative AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) for research, QA, and process automation.
*   Detail‑obsessed; spots typos, misaligned images, and broken links before the client does.
*   Confident communicator in English—written and verbal—with a knack for building rapport remotely.
