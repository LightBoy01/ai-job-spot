Career Opportunities: Product Manager (4881)
============================================

               

Requisition ID **4881** - Posted  - **Portföy Yönetimi** - Location (2)

[  Job Description Print Preview](javascript:void\(0\))

  

 

We are looking for a **“Product Manager”** who will be able to work with an enthusiasm for implementing pioneering steps on the subject of development solutions to join our **Product Management Team.**

We believe that highly competitive solutions can only be developed by motivated and enthusiastic teams. That’s why our team members have been selected among the top engineers and specialists in the market. We are experts in developing solutions for financial organizations. If you want to be part of this dynamic team, come & join us and share our goal that is to take the technology to the next level with creative, fast, efficient, and secure solutions.

**Here are the details of our job posting:**

**What makes you a good fit:**

*   Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Business Administration, Economics, or related fields
*   Minimum 5 years of experience in product management
*   Familiarity with start-up processes
*   Experience in data-driven decision-making, A/B testing, and hypothesis testing
*   Proven track record in launching new products and managing the product lifecycle
*   Hands-on experience with Jira, Confluence, Figma, and Miro
*   Knowledge of Generative AI technologies
*   Experience working with Agile methodologies
*   Excellent English communication skills
*   Strong strategic orientation and analytical thinking skills
*   Team player, customer-focused, and effective in high-energy, fast-paced environments

**What you’ll experience working at Softtech:**

*   Shape product vision, roadmap, and strategy through business cases, success metrics, customer feedback, market research, and competitive analysis
*   Ensure new ideas and products are aligned with company strategy and provide leadership in this direction
*   Define short-, mid-, and long-term goals for products and lead the preparation of roadmaps
*   Analyze market trends, develop new market entry strategies, and proactively manage risks
*   Guide market research, competitor analysis, and pricing strategies by ensuring the right methodologies and tools are applied
*   Monitor product performance via KPIs and customer feedback, ensuring continuous improvement
*   Manage the product lifecycle, considering cost, integration, profitability, and cash flow impact
*   Support marketing and sales activities
*   Coordinate with senior management during the product development process and escalate critical issues when necessary
*   Contribute to data-driven decision-making processes, lead A/B tests and experimental initiatives
*   Strengthen collaboration across teams, provide mentorship, and promote a culture of product management and continuous improvement
*   Provide guidance in preparing product documentation and ensure deliverables from the product management process are produced and maintained

**What we offer you:**  
  

*   Private medical insurance and group life insurance

*   Company contributory in individual retirement or cumulative life insurance 3 months after working with us
*   Employee and Employer Contribution Plan for individual retirement support (Optional)
*   Monthly meal payment
*   Flexible remote working support
*   Transportation fee or shuttle option according to your preference
*   Covered travel and accommodation expenses if necessary
*   Employee discounts – including a huge number of retailers via İş Grubu Ailem
*   Continuously learning and development opportunities
*   Health and well-being seminars and initiatives via Softtech Academy
*   Employee support program through a corporate consulting firm
*   Support for postgraduate education, including administrative leave and financial incentives for those pursuing a Master's or PhD degree
*   Study leave and support for studying for a qualification or other accredited development relevant to your role
*   Attending to technical congress and conferences
*   Celebrate your birthday with a day off

**About us:**

As one of Turkey's leading technology companies, we have been operating at a total of 6 different locations in Turkey and USA with more than 1600 employees since 2006.

With the strength of our experience in banking and finance industry and experienced human resources, we are developing customer-oriented solutions for our business partners operating in diverse areas.

Our mission is to transform all our customers including mainly İş Bankası into digital companies by using our efficient and high-energy ecosystem.

We recognize that our people are our strength and the diverse talents. We are an equal opportunity employer and welcome applications from any individuals without any restrictions at our company.
