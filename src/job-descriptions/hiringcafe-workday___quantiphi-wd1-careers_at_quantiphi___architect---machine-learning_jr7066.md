**Responsibilities:**

*   Define and design comprehensive end-to-end architecture for production-grade multi-agent systems that are reliable, well-governed and compliant with industry standards.
*   Establish architectural standards, patterns and best practices for multi-agent deployments.
*   Create technical roadmaps and architecture blue prints for enterprise-grade agentic solutions.
*   Standardize proof-of-concept Agent codebases into robust, scalable, production-ready solutions.
*   Establish testing frameworks for multi-agent system validation.
*   Design and implement robust deployment pipelines on AWS/Azure cloud platforms.
*   Ensure scalability, reliability and performance of multi-agent systems in production.
*   Establish monitoring, logging and alerting systems for production environments.
*   Implement security protocols and compliance measures for enterprise deployments.
*   Conduct risk assessments and develop mitigation strategies for production systems.
*   Ensure adherence to data privacy and regulatory requirements.
*   Conduct Technical Reviews and ensure code quality across all development phases.
*   Guide development teams on orchestration patterns, error handling, CI/CD readiness, and scalability.
*   Help customers with creating sustainable, enterprise-grade AI architectures that deliver measurable business value.
*   Serve as an advisory consultant to customers on their AI transformation Journey, collaborating with Technical specialists and Business Development teams.
*   Develop technical content, reference implementations, and presentations to enable customers fully leverage AI/ML and Generative AI on Azure/AWS.
*   Develop and execute strategic roadmaps for LLM-based solutions, including RAG (Retrieval- Augmented Generation), Agentic RAG, and Agent-driven workflows.
*   Guide AI model evaluation, MLOps pipeline integration, and fine-tuning strategies for scalable AI solutions.

**Skill Set Required:**

*   Proficiency in LangGraph, LangChain for agentic workflows.
*   Deep Technical Experience across the AI spectrum from traditional ML and deep learning to Generative AI and Agentic AI.
*   Hands-on experience with autonomous agent frameworks and developing multi-agent systems.
*   Must have experience with AWS or Azure (LLM hosting, GPU-based inference, cost optimization).
*   Mentoring team members for career progression & upskilling to drive better solution outcomes.
*   Excellent Communication, presentation & storytelling skills.
*   Experience managing large-scale AI projects leveraging LLMs.
