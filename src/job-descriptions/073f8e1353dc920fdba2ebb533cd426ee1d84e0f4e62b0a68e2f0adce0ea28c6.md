### Description

Gusto is a modern, online people platform dedicated to helping small businesses care for their teams through full-service payroll, health insurance, 401(k)s, and expert HR tools. Serving over 400,000 businesses nationwide from our offices in Denver, San Francisco, and New York, our mission is to create a world where work empowers a better life.

As the Head of Revenue Analytics, you will lead a critical function that uses data to measure, analyze, and optimize the drivers of our Go-to-Market (GTM) revenue. Reporting to the Head of Revenue Operations, you will be a key strategic partner to our GTM and Product teams. This role requires a leader who can blend high-level strategic vision with hands-on analytical work, guiding a talented team responsible for revenue forecasting, marketing analytics, and customer expansion insights to shape Gusto's growth strategy.

### Responsibilities

*   **Strategic Leadership:** Develop and execute a comprehensive revenue analytics strategy that aligns with GTM business goals, providing data and insights to inform executive decisions.
*   **Forecasting & Planning:** Lead the design and execution of revenue forecasts across acquisition, expansion, and retention, providing clear and actionable performance views to Finance and GTM leaders.
*   **Marketing Analytics:** Drive analysis of acquisition efficiency, ROI, attribution, and customer lifecycle value to optimize marketing spend and guide strategic investments.
*   **Customer & Retention Insights:** Deliver deep analysis on churn, retention, and customer lifetime value (CLV) to inform strategies for engagement, upsell, and long-term profitability.
*   **Insights and Recommendations:** Conduct deep-dive analyses to identify key revenue drivers and anomalies, translating complex data into clear, actionable recommendations for senior leadership.
*   **Quota & Budget Alignment:** Partner with Finance and GTM leaders to support quota setting, budget allocation, and performance tracking.
*   **Data Products & Infrastructure:** Build and scale datasets, dashboards, and tools that enable self-serve access to revenue insights while ensuring data quality and consistency.
*   **AI-Driven Analytics:** Evaluate and apply AI/ML techniques to enhance forecasting and customer lifecycle modeling, staying ahead of emerging tools to drive efficiency and accuracy.
*   **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Serve as a critical partner to Sales Ops, Marketing Ops, and Finance to align data and insights across the entire revenue funnel.
*   **People Empowerment:** Recruit, mentor, and develop a high-performing analytics team, fostering a culture of curiosity, analytical rigor, and operational excellence.

### Qualifications

*   12+ years of experience in analytics, forecasting, or revenue strategy, with at least 5+ years in a leadership role.
*   **Leadership with a Builder Mindset:** A dynamic leader who inspires teams while maintaining a “roll up your sleeves” attitude to build reports, run analyses, and troubleshoot.
*   **Analytical Expertise:** Strong skills in data analysis and visualization tools (e.g., SQL, Tableau, Power BI) and experience with financial and marketing forecasting models.
*   **SaaS/Growth Background:** A proven track record of building and leading analytics functions in a high-growth SaaS or technology environment.
*   **Technical Skills:** Familiarity with various data sources and technologies, including Salesforce, marketing automation platforms, and data warehouses.
*   **AI Fluency:** Awareness of how AI/ML is transforming analytics, with the ability to partner with data science teams to operationalize new approaches.
*   **Business Acumen:** Deep understanding of the entire customer lifecycle, from acquisition to retention, and its impact on revenue.
*   **Communication & Influence:** Excellent ability to distill complex data into clear, actionable insights for both technical and non-technical audiences.

**Compensation & Location:**
*   The annual cash compensation range for this role is **$201,200 - $235,000** in San Francisco & New York, and **$171,200 - $200,000** in Denver.
*   This is a hybrid role, with an expectation to work from one of our offices (Denver, San Francisco, or New York City) 2-3 days per week.

### Author's Take

**1. Beyond forecasting, can you give an example of a strategic decision that was directly shaped by the Revenue Analytics team's insights?**

"Absolutely. Last year, we were debating a significant investment in a new marketing channel. On the surface, the initial acquisition cost looked high. But our team dug deeper. They built a model that didn't just look at initial sign-up, but at the entire customer journey for that cohort—from their activation rate to their long-term expansion revenue and eventual churn profile. Their analysis revealed that while these customers were more expensive to acquire, their lifetime value was nearly 2.5x higher than our average. They became our stickiest and most profitable segment. This insight didn't just get the budget approved; it fundamentally shifted how our marketing and product teams think about 'valuable' customers. It's a perfect example of how this team moves beyond reporting the 'what' to explaining the 'why' and influencing the 'what's next.'"

**2. As the new Head of this team, what would be my biggest challenge in the first six months, and what kind of cross-functional support can I expect to tackle it?**

"That's a great question. Your biggest—and most exciting—challenge will be to create a unified narrative of the customer journey. We have rich data across marketing, sales, product usage, and support, but they sometimes live in different worlds. Your initial mission will be to weave these threads together into a single, cohesive story. The beauty of Gusto is how collaborative the culture is. This isn't a challenge you'd face alone. Your key partners in Data Engineering, Product, and Finance are not just stakeholders; they're genuinely invested in solving this with you. You can expect to be in a room (virtual or physical) with VPs from other departments who are just as eager to whiteboard solutions and allocate resources. The challenge is complex, but the support system is robust and genuinely non-siloed. We solve big problems as one team here."
