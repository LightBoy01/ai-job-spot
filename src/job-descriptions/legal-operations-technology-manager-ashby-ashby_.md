<strong>Overview</strong><p>Suzy, Inc. is an AI-first SaaS company looking for a Legal Operations &amp; Technology Manager who can help us supercharge how the legal, compliance, and security teams operate. This role is not about paper-pushing or waiting for instructions — it’s about building, streamlining, and automating the way these functions deliver value across the company.</p><p>You’ll report directly to the General Counsel, driving projects forward with a get-it-done, tech-forward mindset. If you thrive on learning new tools, building workflows from scratch, and collaborating cross-functionally, this is the role for you.</p><p>We’re open to candidates from diverse backgrounds — legal ops, customer success, project management, RevOps, compliance, etc. What matters most is your ability to:</p><ul><li><p>Learn and leverage new tech quickly (especially AI and automation tools).</p></li><li><p>Bring a self-starter, can-do, collaborative attitude.</p></li><li><p>Translate complex problems into simple, scalable processes.<br></p></li></ul>
Responsibilities<p>Tech &amp; Automation Focus</p><ul><li><p>Research, test, and deploy AI and legal tech tools to automate contract reviews, reporting, and workflows.</p></li><li><p>Build scalable processes (playbooks, trackers, dashboards) to reduce manual work.</p></li><li><p>Integrate Salesforce, shared drives, and other systems for contracts hygiene and metadata tracking.<br></p></li></ul><p>Core Ops Execution</p><ul><li><p>Maintain contracts archive (MSAs, addenda, amendments, termination notices).</p></li><li><p>Log and track contract metadata (term, renewal, governing law, AI permissions).</p></li><li><p>Support order form workflows and contract lifecycle processes.</p></li><li><p>Centralize NDAs and build a searchable tracker for key terms.<br></p></li></ul><p>Cross-Functional Partnering</p><ul><li><p>Document workflows and playbooks</p></li><li><p>Partner with Enablement, Sales, Tech, and Finance to streamline handoffs and processes.</p></li><li><p>Support compliance projects, IP portfolio tracking, and policy creation.<br></p></li></ul>
Qualifications<p>We care less about <em>where you’ve been</em> and more about <em>what you can do.</em> That said, success in this role likely requires:</p><ul><li><p>Demonstrated ability to adopt and master new technology (bonus if you’ve used AI tools, Salesforce, or contract management systems).</p></li><li><p>Strong project management and organizational skills.</p></li><li><p>Excellent communication and collaboration skills; people enjoy working with you.</p></li><li><p>A bias toward action: you can take an ambiguous problem, figure it out, and deliver a solution.</p></li><li><p>High attention to detail paired with a builder’s mindset.</p></li><li><p>Experience in legal ops, RevOps, customer success, or similar process/ops-heavy functions is a plus — but not required.<br></p></li></ul>
