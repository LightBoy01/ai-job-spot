### Description\nunivativ is a project and personnel service provider that offers students and graduates exciting jobs with renowned companies, with a strong focus on career development. This role is for a precise and organized Technical Planner (m/w/d) in Electrical Engineering based in Stuttgart, within the energy sector. You will be instrumental in ensuring that circuit diagrams are kept current, complete, and reliable, contributing directly to network planning. This is an opportunity to join an innovative team at a client in the energy industry, work on exciting projects, and continuously expand your knowledge.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- You will enter all changes from marked circuit diagrams into Ruplan.\n- The transfer of handwritten adjustments to the Ruplan-EVU module is also part of your tasks.\n- Additionally, you will obtain missing or supplementary information for correct updates.\n- You are responsible for carrying out the work within the internal platform.\n- The prospective expansion of tasks to include simple constructive activities rounds off your profile.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A completed technical education with further training as a Technician (m/w/d).\n- Sound practical experience with the CAE tool Ruplan, including the EVU module.\n- Proficient handling of redlining and updating technical plans.\n- Ideally, a basic understanding of electrical engineering.\n- At least business-fluent German language skills, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role presents a fantastic opportunity for someone passionate about precision and technical planning within the vital energy sector. With a competitive annual salary of €66,000 - €70,000 and up to three days of home office, it offers excellent work-life balance. univativ stands out with its strong commitment to employee growth, providing comprehensive career support, diverse on and off-the-job training, and opportunities to build a professional network across various clients. There's also an attractive \\\"Recruit a Friend\\\" bonus program. This is ideal for a proactive individual looking to make an impact on critical infrastructure while rapidly advancing their career.
