### Description\nIntegraleaf is a specialized consulting firm dedicated to supporting pharmaceutical companies in the cannabis market. Our mission is to ensure comprehensive quality standards and guide businesses to operate successfully and compliantly in the growing medical cannabis market. We are seeking a dedicated **Qualified Person (QP)**, full-time or part-time, to strengthen our team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Ensure the proper manufacturing and release of medicinal cannabis.\n- Fulfill the duties of a Qualified Person according to §15 AMG.\n- Take responsibility as a Responsible Person according to MedCanG.\n- Advise and train cannabis companies within and outside Europe.\n- Communicate and coordinate with relevant authorities.\n- Support and further develop quality standards and processes.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in Pharmacy and qualification as a Qualified Person (QP) according to §15 AMG.\n- Solution-oriented and independent working style.\n- High communication skills.\n- High degree of initiative and ability to work autonomously.\n- Strong communication skills in dealing with authorities and other stakeholders.\n- Willingness to undertake business trips within Germany, occasionally worldwide (approx. 2-10 times per year).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a pivotal role for a Qualified Person passionate about shaping the future of the medical cannabis industry. Integraleaf offers a truly modern and supportive work environment, characterized by full remote flexibility, trust-based leadership, and flat hierarchies. With 30 days of flexible annual leave, a dynamic and supportive team, and the prospect of profit sharing, this position offers an exceptional blend of professional challenge and personal well-being. It's an opportunity to make a significant impact while enjoying a highly autonomous and rewarding career.
