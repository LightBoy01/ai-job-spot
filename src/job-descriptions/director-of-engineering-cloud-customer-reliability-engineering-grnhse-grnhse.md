<p><strong>The Team and Role</strong></p>
<p>Zuora's Cloud &amp; Customer Reliability Engineering (CCRE) team is dedicated to amplifying cloud operations for engineering productivity and service availability. As the Director of Engineering for this team, you will report directly to the VP of Cloud and be responsible for driving the strategy and execution of our reliability, observability, and AI initiatives.</p>
<p>You will lead the team in its mission to achieve world-class reliability for our monetization platform by focusing on proactive measures, automation, and continuous improvement. This role requires a leader with a strong technical background and the ability to define and execute a forward-looking strategy that leverages AI and automation to scale operations.</p>
<p>This role can be remote from anywhere in the US. You'll be working remotely while occasionally getting together with your team for office visits, events, or offsites. Of course, you’re welcome to come into the office more often if you’re nearby.</p>
<p><strong>Key Responsibilities</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Lead and Innovate</strong>: Lead, mentor, and scale a global team of Cloud &amp; Customer Reliability Engineers, fostering a culture of accountability, innovation, and continuous learning. Drive the adoption of AI, ML, and agentic AI to enhance operational efficiency.</li>
<li><strong>Enhance Observability and Monitoring</strong>: Own the strategy for a unified, full-stack observability platform that provides end-to-end visibility across metrics, logs, and traces. You will oversee the new Zuora Monitoring Solution and its future enhancements.</li>
<li><strong>Drive Reliability</strong>: Champion a proactive operational model by implementing predictive monitoring and hyper-personalized anomaly detection for customers. Your goal is to detect and mitigate issues before they impact customers.</li>
<li><strong>Automate and Scale</strong>: Oversee the development and deployment of an agentic AI platform and specialized AI agents to automate tasks such as incident management, provisioning, and troubleshooting. The goal is to scale operations 10x with AI and reduce manual toil.</li>
<li><strong>Operational Excellence</strong>: Drive improvements in key metrics like Time to Mitigate (TTM), Time to Provision, and customer-reported incidents. Reduce alerts and incidents through automation and intelligent systems.</li>
<li><strong>Strategic Partnerships</strong>: Partner with other engineering teams to enable developer productivity and build a resilient, scalable, and customer-aligned platform.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong><br>Your Experience</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>10+ years of experience in site reliability engineering, cloud operations, or a related field in a SaaS environment.</li>
<li>Proven experience in leading and scaling high-performing teams responsible for reliability, monitoring, and observability.</li>
<li>Deep expertise in leveraging AI/ML, automation, and AIOps to solve complex operational challenges.</li>
<li>Strong background in developing and implementing full-stack observability solutions (metrics, logs, traces) at scale.</li>
<li>Demonstrated ability to drive strategic initiatives, define roadmaps, and deliver on key business outcomes.</li>
<li>Excellent leadership, communication, and interpersonal skills, with the ability to influence cross-functional stakeholders.</li>
<li>Experience with incident management, root cause analysis, and a data-driven approach to problem-solving.</li>
</ul>
