### Description
Aurivian GmbH is a young and innovative IT service provider specializing in Shopware 6 eCommerce solutions. As a strategic and operational partner, we support demanding client projects across the entire eCommerce value chain, from architecture to development, and scalable hosting and deployment landscapes.

We are seeking an experienced individual with deep technical expertise to primarily manage our infrastructure, deployments, and automations, while also actively contributing to backend development. This role is crucial for ensuring our Shopware projects run performantly, securely, and reliably.

### Responsibilities
As a DevOps & Backend Engineer, you will be responsible for the operation and automation of our development and hosting environments, and support our backend team in implementing complex client requirements. You will work closely with developers, project managers, and architects, significantly shaping our technical standards, workflows, and infrastructure.

*   **DevOps & Infrastructure (Main Focus)**
    *   Operating and maintaining Linux servers (Cloud & On-Prem).
    *   Managing staging and production systems for Shopware 6.
    *   Building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines (Bitbucket Pipelines, Azure DevOps).
    *   Automating infrastructure with tools like Docker, Ansible, Terraform.
    *   Managing and optimizing Reverse Proxies (NGINX, Traefik).
    *   Setting up and maintaining Monitoring & Logging (Grafana, Prometheus).
    *   Implementing Security Hardening, Backup/Recovery concepts, and performance optimizations.
    *   Supporting Incident Management and providing technical support for the Dev team.
    *   Technically co-designing our DevOps standards, tools, and automations.
*   **Backend Development (Supportive & Project-based)**
    *   Developing and enhancing Shopware 6 plugins and modules.
    *   Implementing individual client requirements in Backend/Fullstack.
    *   Integrating external systems via REST-APIs.
    *   Conducting Code Reviews and technical documentation.
    *   Providing technical consulting for project managers and clients.
    *   Supporting the mentoring of junior developers.
    *   Co-designing internal development and CI/CD processes.

### Qualifications
To excel in this role, you should possess:

*   **Must-have (DevOps Focus)**
    *   Excellent knowledge in Linux administration.
    *   Experience with Docker, Reverse Proxies, and deployment workflows.
    *   Proficiency with Git and CI/CD setups.
    *   Knowledge of network and server security.
    *   Experience with monitoring/logging tools.
    *   Proficiency in scripting languages.
*   **Backend Skills**
    *   Several years of experience in object-oriented PHP development.
    *   Very good knowledge of Symfony, Twig, MySQL.
    *   Experience with Shopware 6.
    *   Experience with REST-API design and system integration.
    *   Basic Frontend knowledge (HTML, CSS, JavaScript; Vue.js desirable).
*   **Personal Attributes**
    *   High quality standards & structured way of working.
    *   Strong communication skills, solution-oriented, and team player.
    *   Open to new technologies, automation, and continuous improvements.
    *   Mentor mentality & enjoyment of team collaboration.
