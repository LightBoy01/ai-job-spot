**Join us. Let’s care for tomorrow.**

At Allianz Global Investors we foster a culture of professionalism, fulfilment, and an inclusive working environment. Do you want to be part of a leading active asset management company? Then join us now as Sustainability Strategist in Hong Kong within the Sustainability Strategist team.

In this role, you will be responsible to work with the team heads and other team members. This role will have a strong cross-functional exposure, and he/she will work closely with Sales and Marketing & RFP teams, Portfolio Management, Product Development, Communication and other parts of Allianz Global Investors.

**What you will do**

*   Point person for sustainability-related requests from Sales, Products, Investments, RFP team, Communications and Marketing
*   Sustainability expert & ambassador on-the-ground to engage externally with clients, regulators, industry bodies, NGOs & media
*   Development & maintenance of sustainability-related materials, contents & language
*   Sustainability market research and staying on top of regional developments, serving as a key source of sustainability insights from an Asia perspective
*   Strategic sustainable product planning across public and private market funds
*   Support active promotion of sustainability throughout the company through delivering trainings
*   Support sales on sustainability topics and support pitch preparation
*   Speak at client meetings & conferences on sustainability topics

**What you bring** 

*   University degree in Finance or related field of study
*   Several years of relevant experience in sustainable investing, proven knowledge of industry practices and ESG trends
*   Experience in a similar, client-facing role
*   Solid knowledge and understanding of ESG products and competitor landscape
*   CFA Sustainable Investing Certificate, CESGA or similar certificates are a plus
*   Strong analytical, conceptual, communications, writing and presentation skills
*   Shown project management and relationship management skills
*   Strong interpersonal skills
*   High proficiency in English and Mandarin, written and spoken, other languages are a plus
*   Outstanding skills in Microsoft Power Point and Excel
*   Outstanding skills in organization, prioritization and time management
*   AI skills: Data reporting/ ChatGPT/ Microsoft CoPilot
