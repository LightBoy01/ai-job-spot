**Job Description:**

**Data Scientist with focus AI / GenAI / Agentic AI  
Project Description:**  
We are the central think tank and center of excellence for all areas of our technology unit with Data Science, AI and GenAI in terms of knowledge, projects and providing enablers. We have an overarching program with multiple sub-projects that span the whole lifecycle from Plan via Build to Run and operate processes, tools and projects.  
**Activity description and concrete tasks:**  
You will participate in our top-level high-priority innovation topics in the AI / GenAI / Agentic AI field. While implementing and developing the concrete use case you will be investing additional effort to create/productionize "enablers" based on this work so that future projects can be delivered more efficiently and empower more people throughout DT Technik to build their usecases.
