### Description\nBolt is a rapidly growing tech company in Europe and Africa, with over 150 million users across 45+ countries. We are driven by a mission to make cities more accessible for people, not just cars, fostering an inclusive environment where everyone is welcome.\n\nWe are actively seeking a motivated and hands-on Scooters Mechanic to play a crucial role in maintaining our e-scooter fleet at the highest possible quality. You will be responsible for quickly and efficiently identifying and repairing damages, performing thorough safety tests, and ensuring every ride is safe and reliable. Your dedication will directly contribute to providing customers with a top-notch transport experience and furthering our vision of urban mobility.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Ensuring the highest quality for our e-scooters by conducting general repair work and check-ups.\n- Inspecting e-scooters in use and identifying problems within the different parts of the product.\n- Testing e-scooters before releasing them to ensure their quality and safety standards.\n- Putting new e-scooters into service: unpacking, doing quality checks, adding necessary equipment, ensuring safety, and troubleshooting.\n- Assisting with warehouse operations, including inventory and battery management, shipments, and general housekeeping.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You demonstrate a high level of attentiveness and accuracy in your work.\n- You possess the knowledge and skills to operate various hand and power tools.\n- Your proactive approach and willingness to take ownership of challenges are reflected in your hands-on work.\n- You have experience in repairing mechanical devices, particularly in the automotive or electronic repair fields.\n- You have solid local language skills and experience using smartphones.\n- Having English skills as well as a forklift license is an advantage.\n\nWhile experience is valued, we prioritize drive, intelligence, and integrity. We encourage you to apply even if you don't meet every single qualification.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting opportunity for a mechanically-minded individual to make a tangible impact on urban mobility within one of Europe's fastest-growing tech companies.
