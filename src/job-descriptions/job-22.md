# Senior Machine Learning Engineer, Siri

### Description

Join the team that powers Siri and other intelligent features on Apple devices. We are looking for a passionate and experienced Senior Machine Learning Engineer to help us build the next generation of on-device machine learning solutions. You will work on a wide range of problems, from natural language understanding and speech recognition to personalization and context awareness. This is a unique opportunity to make a significant impact on the lives of millions of users worldwide.

### Responsibilities

- Design, implement, and optimize machine learning models for on-device deployment.
- Develop and maintain the infrastructure for training, evaluating, and deploying models at scale.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality, user-facing features.
- Research and apply the latest techniques in machine learning, with a focus on efficiency and performance.
- Analyze and debug complex systems to ensure they meet Apple's high standards for quality and privacy.

### Qualifications

- 5+ years of experience in machine learning, with a focus on deep learning.
- Strong programming skills in Python and/or C++.
- Experience with one or more deep learning frameworks, such as TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- Experience with on-device machine learning is a strong plus.
- Excellent problem-solving and debugging skills.
- A passion for building amazing products.
