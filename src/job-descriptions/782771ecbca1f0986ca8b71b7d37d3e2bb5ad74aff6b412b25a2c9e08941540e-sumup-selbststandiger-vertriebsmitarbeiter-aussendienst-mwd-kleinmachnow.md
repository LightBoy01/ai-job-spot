### Description
Are you an independent, self-motivated sales professional ready to shape your own success? SumUp empowers small businesses with smart and user-friendly financial solutions, from card payments to comprehensive POS systems, enabling them to focus entirely on their core business.

As a **freelance Field Sales Consultant**, you become your own boss, responsible for building relationships with local businesses and demonstrating how SumUp solutions can simplify their operations. This role offers attractive, unlimited commissions, allowing your income to directly reflect your dedication and effort. You'll benefit from maximum flexibility, working when and where you choose, along with access to top-tier products with high market potential and robust sales training and support from SumUp.

### Responsibilities
*   **Active New Customer Acquisition**: Identify and acquire local businesses for SumUp.
*   **Personal Meetings and Consultation**: Present SumUp products and demonstrate their benefits to potential clients.
*   **Customer Relationship Building**: Secure long-term partnerships through excellent service and ongoing support.
*   **Self-Managed Client and Lead Management**: Independently manage your customer base and sales leads, shaping your own business strategy.

### Qualifications
*   A passion for sales and strong self-motivation.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer service is an advantage.
*   Excellent communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Very good German language skills.
