### Responsibilities

**Your Role**:

We're looking for a technical Senior AI Developer to drive our artificial intelligence initiatives, with particular focus on developing and implementing innovative solutions using AWS/UiPath/Databricks, and conversational AI technologies.

*   Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to assess complex business processes and identify opportunities for optimization
*   Design and implement AI solutions using AWS services, UiPath, Databricks, and other cloud platforms
*   Develop and optimize machine learning models, including LLMs and conversational AI systems
*   Contribute to the establishment of technical standards, MLOps practices, and CI/CD pipelines for AI solutions
*   Participate in proof-of-concepts and pilot programs to drive innovation
*   Ensure AI solutions Adhere to regulatory requirements and ethical AI principles
*   Proficiency in Python, ML frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch), and cloud platforms (AWS/ Azure)
*   Contribute to the end-to-end AI lifecycle management, including model monitoring and maintenance
