<p><b>About the Role</b> </p>
<ul><li><p>As an AI Security Engineer within the AI Transformation team, you will play a crucial role in ensuring our AI products and solutions uphold the highest standards of security and compliance. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p>Collaborate closely with AI developers, DevOps engineers, and product teams to identify potential security vulnerabilities, implement best practices, and ensure compliance with regulatory standards including GDPR, SOC 2, and others. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p>Lead security assessments, penetration testing, and vulnerability management for AI systems to proactively mitigate risks. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p>Act as the security expert in designing, developing, and deploying secure AI and machine learning applications, with a focus on safeguarding personally identifiable information (PII). </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p>Stay ahead of emerging cybersecurity threats, privacy regulations, and compliance requirements to ensure that our AI solutions continuously meet and exceed market standards. </p></li></ul>

<ul><li><p>Document security protocols, conduct training sessions, and promote security awareness within the team and organization. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p>Engage with stakeholders across multiple disciplines to refine security policies and procedures specific to AI and ML products. </p></li></ul>
<p><b>Required Skills and Experience</b> </p>
<ul><li><p>Minimum of 5 years&#39; experience in cybersecurity, particularly focused on securing software and data-intensive products. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p>Proven expertise in GDPR compliance, SOC 2 audits, and handling of sensitive PII. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p>Strong experience in penetration testing, vulnerability assessments, and security incident response. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p>Proficiency with cybersecurity frameworks such as NIST, ISO 27001, or CIS Controls. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p>Hands-on experience with cloud security practices, especially within Microsoft Azure, including identity management, access controls, and secure networking configurations. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p>Deep understanding of AI/ML systems architecture and the associated security considerations. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p>Excellent communication skills, with the ability to clearly articulate complex security topics to both technical and non-technical audiences. </p></li></ul>
