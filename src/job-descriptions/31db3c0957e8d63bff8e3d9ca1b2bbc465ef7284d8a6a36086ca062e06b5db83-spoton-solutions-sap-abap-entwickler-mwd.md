This department is dedicated to further expanding S/4HANA, implementing new solutions, and embracing fresh ideas. You'll join a team that is open to new ideas, works pragmatically, and is motivated to collaboratively build future-proof processes.\n\nThis role offers an attractive salary of up to €80,000 annually, a modern work environment with flexible and hybrid working options, including home office. You'll have extensive opportunities for further training and development in key technologies like S/4HANA, Fiori, and ODATA, along with certifications. If you're passionate about leveraging cutting-edge S/4HANA technologies, have a desire to actively shape processes, and appreciate a culture that values autonomy, professional growth, and work-life balance through flexible work models, this role in Cologne offers a compelling career path. It’s a chance to truly make an impact and drive digital transformation.
