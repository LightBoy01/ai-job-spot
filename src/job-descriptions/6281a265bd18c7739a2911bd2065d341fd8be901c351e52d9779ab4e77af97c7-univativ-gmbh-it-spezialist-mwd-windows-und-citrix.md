### Description\nOur client, a federal company based in Berlin within the public service sector, is seeking an experienced IT Specialist (m/w/d) for Windows and Citrix environments. This permanent, full-time position offers the opportunity to work on critical IT systems, ensuring their reliable operation while actively contributing to infrastructure development. If you possess a hands-on mentality, enjoy solving technical challenges pragmatically, and are looking for a role where you can apply your expertise, take responsibility, and grow long-term, this is for you. You will play a vital role in ensuring applications are performant, secure, and always available, making a significant contribution to the success of IT operations.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Ensure central IT infrastructure services run stably, securely, and sustainably at all times. This includes focusing on topics like High Availability (HA) and Disaster Recovery (DR) and actively applying your technical know-how in operations.\n- Collaborate closely with colleagues to plan, coordinate, and implement changes in a structured manner.\n- Take responsibility in projects from conception to implementation, developing solutions that are efficient and sustainable.\n- Ensure reliable system functionality and traceable processes through targeted testing and clear documentation.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A completed degree in Computer Science or a comparable IT-focused education.\n- Initial to profound experience in the operation and support of IT infrastructures.\n- Good knowledge of Windows and Citrix system administration.\n- Interest in IT security standards and ideally initial exposure to BSI IT-Grundschutz (IT Baseline Protection).\n- Basic understanding of IT Service Management processes (e.g., Incident, Change, or Service Level Management).\n- Team spirit, self-initiative, and enjoyment in collaboratively developing solutions.\n- Willingness to occasionally participate in a structured on-call duty rotation.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role presents a compelling opportunity for an experienced IT Specialist looking for stability and continuous growth within the public sector. The position emphasizes both operational excellence and proactive infrastructure development, allowing you to make a tangible impact. Beyond a competitive salary, the company offers a robust benefits package including permanent employment, remote work options, special payments like Christmas bonus, company pension, a flexible work model, and 31 days of annual leave. With a strong focus on team collaboration and regular training, it's an ideal environment for long-term career development and a healthy work-life balance.
