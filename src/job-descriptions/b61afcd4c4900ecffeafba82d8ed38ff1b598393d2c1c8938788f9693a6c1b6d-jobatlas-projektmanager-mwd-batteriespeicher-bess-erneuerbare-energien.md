### Description\nAre you an expert in large-scale battery storage and renewable energies? Then we need you! Join a robust company specializing in renewable energy, implementing solar and wind projects nationwide. We are seeking a dedicated professional for the planning and implementation of our next-generation Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) 'mega-projects'. As a **Project Developer (m/w/d) Battery Storage**, you will be responsible for the entire project lifecycle, from initial site acquisition to turnkey handover. Based at our Leer headquarters, you'll be developing the projects of tomorrow. While passionate about contributing to this pioneering vision, you should also be willing to travel regularly to project sites across Germany.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Identify, coordinate, and manage battery storage projects.\n- Accompany BESS projects from the initial concept to turnkey handover.\n- Conduct site analyses, secure land, and develop comprehensive planning concepts.\n- Evaluate project risks and oversee approval procedures.\n- Report regularly on project status and collaborate closely with internal technical departments.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Relevant professional experience in a comparable position with battery storage systems, for example, as a Project Developer in photovoltaics, solar, wind, or other renewable energy sectors.\n- Completed technical degree in a relevant field such as Electrical Engineering, Energy Technology, Renewable Energies, Mechanical Engineering, or Industrial Engineering.\n- Proficiency in project management tools and MS Office applications.\n- Strong understanding of business administration and commercial contexts.\n- Excellent communication skills, making you a strong negotiation partner.\n- Fluent in German and proficient in English.\n- Willingness to undertake nationwide business travel to visit project sites.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an experienced Project Developer to make a significant impact in the rapidly expanding renewable energy sector. The company offers a highly supportive environment with a competitive, regularly adjusted salary, flexible working hours, and options for mobile work, demonstrating a strong commitment to work-life balance. With 30 days of annual leave, paid further education, and a modern office featuring fresh amenities, the benefits package is comprehensive. This role is perfect for a driven individual ready to lead groundbreaking BESS projects from conception to completion, contributing directly to a sustainable future.
