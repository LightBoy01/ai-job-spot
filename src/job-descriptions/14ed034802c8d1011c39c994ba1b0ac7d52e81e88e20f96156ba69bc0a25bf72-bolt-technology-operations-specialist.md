### Description\nWe're seeking a skilled Operations Specialist with a focus on Key Account Management (KAM) to expand and manage Bolt's fleet partner ecosystem in Germany. Bolt, one of Europe and Africa's fastest-growing tech companies with over 200 million customers across 50+ countries, is committed to creating an inclusive environment and working towards its ultimate mission: to make cities for people, not cars. This role is pivotal in building strong, performance-driven relationships with key fleets through regular business reviews, deal execution, and daily operational support. In parallel, you will ensure that driver and fleet operations remain compliant with German PHV regulations, upholding Bolt's reputation as a safe and reliable mobility provider. Working closely with senior leadership, this specialist will drive fleet performance, service quality, and city-level growth.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Key Account Management (Primary Focus):** Manage relationships with key fleet partners, including monthly business reviews, commercial negotiations, and performance improvement plans.\n- Monitor fleet KPIs (utilization, acceptance rate, ETAs, quality metrics) and implement targeted actions to optimize results.\n- Identify growth opportunities within the fleet base and support onboarding of new high-potential partners.\n- Contribute to process improvement, efficiency projects, and scaling best practices across cities.\n- Serve as the main contact for strategic accounts, ensuring clear communication and issue resolution.\n- Collaborate cross-functionally with Operations, Policy, and Legal teams to align partner performance with Bolt's broader goals.\n- Coordinate with local authorities on licensing queries and escalate any compliance risks.\n- Support smooth driver and fleet onboarding processes and maintain accurate documentation in line with local requirements.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 2–4 years' experience in operations, account management, or fleet management.\n- Excellent relationship-building and negotiation skills.\n- Strong data literacy and ability to translate insights into actions.\n- Fluent in German and English (written and spoken).\n- Proactive, structured, and able to work independently in a fast-paced, regulated environment.\n- We highly value drive, intelligence, and integrity; please consider applying even if you don't tick every box.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent Mid-Senior level opportunity for a proactive Operations Specialist with a knack for Key Account Management. Bolt offers a dynamic, inclusive, and mission-driven environment focused on making cities better, supported by a strong collaborative culture. The role is critical for driving both performance and regulatory compliance within their German fleet ecosystem, offering significant impact and direct collaboration with senior leadership. Bolt prioritizes employee growth and well-being, providing unique career development, stock options, wellness perks, a generous paid sabbatical after 5 years, and a flexible hybrid work model.
