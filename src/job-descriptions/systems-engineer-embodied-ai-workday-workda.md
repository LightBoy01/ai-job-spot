<p>We are seeking a highly independent Systems Engineer (Embodied AI) to lead the development of intelligent robotic systems within a dynamic, small robotics team. This role focusses on designing and deploying AI-driven robots capable of seamless interaction with built environments (e.g., lifts, turnstiles, doors) and navigating both dynamic and static obstacles in indoor as well as sheltered outdoor environments. The candidate will operate with minimal supervision, leveraging simulation platforms such as NVIDIA Isaac Sim or MuJoCo to drive innovation in embodied AI. The ideal candidate is a self-driven problem-solver with deep expertise in reinforcement learning, computer vision, sensor fusion, and sim-to-real deployment for resource-constrained devices.</p><p></p><p><b>Key Responsibilities:</b></p>
<ul><li><p><b>Develop Advanced Embodied AI Systems</b>: Design and implement AI models integrating perception, reasoning, and action to enable robots to interact with building infrastructure and navigate complex environments. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p><b>Apply Reinforcement Learning</b>: Use reinforcement learning to train robots for autonomous navigation, obstacle avoidance, and robust interaction in unpredictable real-world scenarios. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p><b>Implement Multi-Modal Perception</b>: Develop algorithms to fuse data from cameras, LiDAR, ultrasonic sensors, and tactile sensors for robust environmental understanding and precise interaction with physical systems. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p><b>Leverage Simulation Platforms</b>: Utilise NVIDIA Isaac Sim or MuJoCo to design, test, and train AI models, addressing the sim-to-real gap through techniques like domain randomisation or transfer learning. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p><b>Optimise for Edge Deployment</b>: Deploy AI models on resource-constrained edge devices (e.g., edge GPUs), ensuring low latency, minimal power consumption, and high reliability. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p><b>Collaborate on System Integration</b>: Work with systems engineers to integrate AI models with robotic platforms and building access systems for seamless functionality. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p><b>Lead and Innovate</b>: Provide technical leadership and mentorship while contributing to the team’s strategic vision and fostering innovation. </p></li></ul>
<ul><li><p><b>Stay Current with Advancements</b>: Stay at the forefront of embodied AI developments, including vision-language-action models and world models, to implement cutting-edge solutions. </p></li></ul>
