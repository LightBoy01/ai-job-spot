**Summary**
-----------

We are seeking a highly motivated and analytical Senior Data Scientist to join our growing team. The ideal candidate will have a strong foundation in data science principles and a passion for leveraging data to drive business decisions. This role offers an excellent opportunity to learn and grow within a dynamic and collaborative environment. 

  
The team’s portfolio focuses on anomaly/fraud detection and field services use cases. We take complicated business problems, dive deep into the data and processes associated with those problems, and create both classification/regression and generative/agentic solutions.

**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------

*   Be a part of the field services team. Learn deeply about how field services operates, the everyday problems that need solving, and create those solutions.
*   Be a contributor to the end to end AI/ML solution. Wrangle the data, create the model, define the parameters of productionization, and continuously improve it.
*   Have a strong understanding of AI/ML and be able to advocate for new ideas and changes to the current roadmap. We work closely as a team, so communication is key.
*   Be willing to innovate, particularly on complicated, undefined problems. We often are given an outcome, and it is our job to find the way to take the business there.
*   Proactive and results-oriented: Embraces challenges with enthusiasm and a positive "can-do" attitude, consistently exceeding expectations.
*   Innovative and solution-focused: Proactively identifies opportunities for improvement and contributes creative solutions to enhance efficiency and effectiveness. 
*   Customer-centric: Demonstrates a strong understanding of customer needs and leverages this knowledge to deliver exceptional service and build strong relationships. 
*   Analytical and detail-oriented: Meticulously gathers and analyzes information to effectively identify and resolve complex problems. 
*   Collaborative team player: Actively contributes to a positive team environment, fostering collaboration and contributing to shared success. 
*   Growth-minded: Actively seeks opportunities to develop new skills and apply them to enhance job performance and contribute to organizational goals.   
     

**Critical Qualifications**
---------------------------

*   Bachelor's degree in computer science, Statistics, Mathematics, Data Science, Engineering or a related field. Relevant experience may be considered in lieu of a degree. 
*   Approximately  5-10 years of relevant work experience in a data science or analytics role. 
*   Experience in a Cloud environment. GCP is preferred – but we recognise that cloud skills are transferable.
*   Proficiency in SQL and Python.
*   Familiarity with various data frameworks, especially Beam and Dataflow.
*   Experience with popular machine learning libraries (e.g., scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch)
*   Experience with a major Generative AI platform such as VertexAI Agent Space.

**Preferred Qualifications**
----------------------------

*   Experience in the telecommunications industry or applying technical problems to operational/supply chain problems.  

Adequate knowledge of French is required for positions in Quebec.
