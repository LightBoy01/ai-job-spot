Project Manager
---------------

**Department:** Production **Location:** Costa Rica  

[START YOUR APPLICATION](https://apply.hrmdirect.com/resumedirect/ApplyOnline/Apply.aspx?req_id=3532028&source=3532028-CJB-0)

**About us:**

TrustScale, an e2f company, helps businesses scale with confidence by providing high-quality data and AI solutions. We combine global expertise with cutting-edge technology to deliver training data and evaluation services that power next-generation AI applications.

  
**Summary:**

As a Project Manager, you will lead AI data collection and localization projects, driving them from inception to completion. You will be the pivot for cross-functional teams and ensure that our projects meet business objectives and are delivered on time.

  
**Responsibilities:**

*   Using Agile methodologies, plan and oversee the entire project lifecycle, from conception to delivery.
    
*   Manage relationships with internal stakeholders and external clients.
    
*   Ensure project milestones align with business objectives using Scrum practices.
    
*   Lead project teams, providing direction and support to project coordinators and Junior Project Managers.
    
*   Innovate and implement project management methodologies to enhance project efficiency.
    
*   Engage with stakeholders to report on project progress and address any issues.
    
*   Implement project standards and practices, learning from senior project managers to become more autonomous.
    

**Requirements:**

*   Minimum of 5 years experience in project management within AI or related fields in a fast-paced environment.
    
*   Bachelor's degree in Project Management, Computer Science, or a related field.
    
*   Strong leadership skills and experience managing sizable, diverse teams.
    
*   Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to manage complex projects.
    
*   Strong technical understanding of AI and machine learning.
    
*   Proven ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously in a dynamic environment.
    
*   Certification in Agile, Scrum, or similar methodologies.
    
*   Sense of urgency and accountability.
    
*   Excellent communication skills in English
