### Description
Lyn the Agency is a social media agency specializing in talent management, influencer marketing, and brand experiences. We work closely with selected creators, providing comprehensive, strategic, creative, and operational support. Our goal is to build sustainable careers and forge brand partnerships that are enjoyable and deliver genuine value.

As an employer, we believe in straightforward principles: short communication paths, clear decisions, and strong team spirit. We steer clear of internal politics and unnecessary drama. Everyone takes responsibility, contributes their own ideas, and actively helps develop the agency. We are experiencing healthy growth, have ambitious plans, and ensure every team member has a meaningful and fulfilling role. We are currently engaged in exciting projects with strong brands, expanding our event segment, and developing new creators, all while maintaining a strong company culture. We celebrate successes together, support each other, and cultivate an environment where everyone feels comfortable and enjoys their work. We invite you to become part of a young, ambitious team that drives significant change and is always eager to learn.

### Responsibilities
- Support in the day-to-day management of our creators.
- Research and preparation of brand collaborations.
- Assistance with briefings, reports, and contract negotiations.
- Communication with brands and agencies.
- Maintenance of internal overviews, campaign lists, and creator data.

### Qualifications
- Experience in dealing with creators and in talent management.
- Enthusiasm for social media and current trends.
- A keen sense for topics related to beauty, fashion, and lifestyle.
- Structured, dedicated, and proactive working style.
