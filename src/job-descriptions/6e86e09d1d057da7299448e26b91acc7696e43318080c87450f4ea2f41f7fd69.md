**Let's introduce ourselves**
-----------------------------

Picture yourself in an exciting industry where the future revolves around leading-edge electrified and self-driving cars seamlessly integrated into people's daily lives. But how can we create this future in a way that is both safe and sustainable? This is just one of the inspiring questions we're working to solve here at Volvo Cars. Join us and watch your ideas shape the future of mobility. 

**Passionate about secure, innovative products?**

Do you thrive where technology, security, and user experience meet?

Join Cybersecurity at Volvo Cars as a **Cyber Security Data & Analytics Product Manager** and help drive cutting-edge solutions.

You’ll be part of a diverse, collaborative team that values creativity, continuous learning, and fresh ideas. Together, we shape Volvo Cars’ cybersecurity strategy while exploring the latest technologies—supporting your growth every step of the way.

**What you'll do**
------------------

You’ll be responsible for the product vision and roadmap and align stakeholders around the vision. Own the product lifecycle from ideation to execution, ensuring seamless integration of security features in software applications. Moreover, you will collaborate closely with engineering and security teams to deliver top-notch solutions. Define and prioritize product roadmaps, ensuring alignment with security goals and customer needs. Analyze market trends, customer feedback, and emerging threats to stay ahead of the curve in application security. You will ensure the delivery of scalable and user-friendly security features. Last but most importantly you will foster a security-first mindset across the organization and promote best practices.

**What you'll bring**
---------------------

You bring proven experience in product management, ideally more than 5 years within the data analytics domain. A proactive problem-solver, you identify and address security challenges with a strategic mindset. With strong communication and leadership skills, you influence and educate stakeholders across all levels. You balance security, user experience, and business objectives effectively.

**Key Requirements:**

  
**Enterprise & Critical Infrastructure Experience**

*   Proven track record delivering products in enterprise or critical-infrastructure environments with a strong focus on security analytics
*   Deep familiarity with regulated environments (e.g., critical infrastructure, finance, government) and application of security-by-design principles throughout the product lifecycle

**Data Platform and technical knowledge** 

*   Strong ability to define product requirements for performance-optimized data pipelines that support analytics, ML prototyping, and large-scale database design
*   Understanding of data architecture with the ability to partner closely with engineering teams proficient in Snowflake, Airflow, and DBT
*   Ability to orchestrate scalable telemetry pipelines for high-volume data ingestion and transformation, ensuring product performance and reliability
*   Able to visualize and communicate architectures across cloud engineering, data engineering, and ML solution domains
*   Skilled at capturing and prioritizing requirements for cloud-native solutions in Microsoft Azure, including Infrastructure-as-Code practices with Terraform and ARM templates

**MLOps & Applied Machine Learning**

*   Experience driving the applied ML lifecycle: from model development to deployment, versioning, monitoring, and scaling into production (MLOps best practices)
*   Expertise in aligning ML-driven product features with real-world security defense contexts, including geospatial analytics, risk estimation, and advanced ML methods

**DevOps & Agile Delivery**

*   Familiarity with CI/CD workflows (e.g., GitHub Actions), containerization, and agile SDLC practices to accelerate product delivery
*   Skilled at embedding observability, privacy, compliance, and resilience requirements into product roadmaps

**Cybersecurity Analytics Focus**

*   Ability to lead cybersecurity analytics initiatives by prioritizing features for threat telemetry ingestion, correlation, enrichment, and vulnerability management
*   Strong orientation toward governance and compliance, ensuring robust data lineage and observability are in place for security analytics products

**Product Leadership & Strategy**

*   Proven ability to translate technical requirements into actionable product roadmaps, define measurable OKRs, and align engineering, security, and compliance teams
*   Strong stakeholder management skills to bridge between technical, business, and regulatory needs in high-stakes security environments
