### Description\nSchlafgut is an owner-managed company with a century of tradition, blending this heritage with a dynamic startup culture and ambitious goals. We're on a mission to evolve our well-known textile brand into the world's first and most successful holistic sleep brand. If you're an enrolled student eager to dive deep into IT, not just operate systems but truly understand and actively shape them, then this is your opportunity. We're looking for a proactive IT Working Student to join our team in Eislingen and help us build the future of our digital landscape.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Support in the administration and maintenance of various IT systems, including servers, networks, and user management.\n*   Actively participate in the maintenance and continuous development of our ERP system.\n*   Set up and configure user accounts, permissions, and workflows.\n*   Provide first-level support, analyzing and resolving IT-related issues in daily operations.\n*   Document IT processes and system adjustments thoroughly.\n*   Contribute to exciting IT projects, such as automations, interface developments, and system rollouts.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Currently an enrolled student in Computer Science, Business Informatics, IT Management, or a related field.\n*   Possess initial knowledge of Windows systems, networks, or ERP software.\n*   A strong interest in system administration, process optimization, and digital tools.\n*   Demonstrated analytical thinking, a structured approach to work, and a proactive attitude.\n*   Eager to take on responsibility and driven to find solutions rather than excuses.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent entry-level opportunity for an ambitious IT student to gain practical experience in a company that values both tradition and innovation. You'll be more than just a support role; you'll actively contribute to the digital transformation of a brand with global aspirations. The role offers hands-on involvement with core IT systems and projects, providing a solid foundation for a career in IT. If you're analytical, eager to learn, and ready to make an impact, Schlafgut offers a stimulating environment to grow your skills.
