### Description

At Diamond Braces, our mission is to improve lives by providing affordable, accessible, and amazing orthodontic care—what we call the 'AAA Experience.' We live by our “No Smile Left Behind” philosophy, and we're looking for a leader to help us elevate that commitment.

We are seeking an experienced Contract Client Relations Manager to design, launch, and manage a new, high-performance remote client relations team. This is a critical 90-day contract role, with the potential for extension, focused on building the operational foundation for our client outreach. You will be responsible for developing outbound and inbound KPIs, creating agent scorecards, implementing accountability systems, and building training programs that drive measurable results. A key part of this role will be leveraging your hands-on experience with NICE systems and integrating AI solutions to optimize our call center operations.

### Responsibilities

*   Build and manage a fully remote team of client relations agents.
*   Develop and implement KPIs for outbound and inbound calls, including clear agent scorecards and accountability measures.
*   Create and lead training programs designed to improve team performance and conversion rates.
*   Integrate AI solutions into daily workflows for call handling, reporting, and agent coaching.
*   Utilize the NICE platform to optimize call routing, analytics, and performance tracking.
*   Monitor team performance rigorously and enforce accountability through structured reporting and feedback.
*   Collaborate with marketing and operations teams to ensure client outreach strategies are aligned with broader organizational goals.

### Qualifications

*   A minimum of 5 years of experience in call center or client relations management.
*   Strong, hands-on expertise with NICE systems is required.
*   Demonstrated ability to create and manage KPIs, scorecards, and accountability systems from the ground up.
*   Proven experience with the successful integration of AI into call center operations.
*   Exceptional leadership, training, and coaching skills, particularly in a remote team environment.
*   A track record of driving measurable improvements in call center KPIs and sales conversions.
*   Excellent organizational and communication skills.

### Author's Take

**1. This role is about building a new team from scratch. What specific business challenge prompted this initiative, and what does true success look like after the initial 90-day contract?**

That's a great question because it gets right to the heart of this role's impact. We've grown rapidly by making orthodontic care accessible, but we know our next phase of growth depends on refining our 'AAA Experience' proactively. Right now, we're great at reacting to client needs, but we want to build a system that anticipates them. This team isn't just about handling calls; it's about creating a data-driven, strategic outreach engine. Success in the first 90 days isn't just about having a team in place taking calls. It’s about you presenting a dashboard with a clear, measurable baseline for performance—KPIs you designed, scorecards you implemented, and early results from the AI tools you integrated. True success is building the playbook that will define our client relations for the next five years.

**2. The job description mentions AI integration and NICE systems. How much autonomy will this role have to experiment with new technologies, and what is the company's broader philosophy on innovation?**

We see technology, especially AI, as a tool to empower our team, not replace the human touch that's central to our brand. This role is our lead pioneer in that effort. You won't be bogged down by red tape; you'll be expected to come in, assess our NICE capabilities, and immediately start piloting new AI-driven solutions for things like agent coaching and sentiment analysis. Our leadership is hungry for data and innovation. If you can build a business case for a new tool or process that will improve the agent experience or boost conversion rates, you'll get the support to make it happen. We want a builder and an innovator who is as excited about optimizing a workflow as they are about coaching a new team member to success.
