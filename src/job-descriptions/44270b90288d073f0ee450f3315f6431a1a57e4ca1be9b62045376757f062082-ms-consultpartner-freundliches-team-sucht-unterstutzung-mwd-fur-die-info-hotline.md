### Description\nJoin our friendly customer service team in Dresden, supporting an international mobile provider. This role is your chance to be the primary point of contact for customers, addressing their inquiries about mobile services, tariffs, and technical issues. After a thorough onboarding period, you'll have the flexibility to transition to working from your home office.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Provide friendly and professional telephone and written support to customers.\n- Act as the first point of contact for all questions related to mobile communication, tariffs, and services.\n- Clearly, professionally, and solution-orientedly answer contract and technical questions.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Excellent German language skills (written and spoken) to convey information clearly and friendly.\n- Good PC skills and a quick grasp of new information.\n- Willingness to work shifts, weekends, nights, and holidays.\n- Newcomers (\"Quereinsteiger\") to customer service are expressly welcome and will receive comprehensive training.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent entry-level opportunity for individuals looking to start a career in customer service, especially for those new to the field. The company places a strong emphasis on comprehensive training and provides a supportive team environment. The offer of a welcome bonus of up to 2,026 € and the flexibility to work from home after initial training are significant perks. While it requires flexibility for shift work, the role also highlights clear paths for individual development and continuous learning. It's ideal for someone with strong communication skills and a friendly demeanor who enjoys helping people.
