### Description\nNetzwerkbahn Sachsen GmbH, a young, nationwide railway transport company based in Dresden, is actively seeking full-time Train Conductors (w/m/d) for its locations in Stuttgart, Frankfurt am Main, Erfurt, and Berlin. As a future-proof and growing company, Netzwerkbahn Sachsen offers a varied and secure work environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Support the train driver in the safe preparation and execution of train journeys\n- Perform duties as a shunting assistant\n- Act as a contact person for passengers on the train\n- Conduct ticket inspections\n- Prepare documents for journeys (wagon list, brake slip)\n- Manage train formation and ensure operational safety in accordance with valid regulations (wagon technical inspection)\n- Perform brake tests\n- Provide customer advice and support on board\n- Take responsibility for order and safety on the trains\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed training as a Train Conductor (Zf) including brake test authorization and SRK Leader training component\n- Fitness for duty according to TfV (Train Driver Ordinance)\n- Interest and experience in the railway sector\n- Flexibility, willingness to travel and be deployed, and team-oriented action\n- Willingness to work in shifts, nights, and on weekends, as well as readiness for overnight stays away from home\n- Customer-oriented and well-groomed appearance\n- Fluent German language skills, English language skills advantageous\n- Willingness to undergo further training\n- Enjoy interacting with people\n\n### Author's Take\nNetzwerkbahn Sachsen offers a secure job in a future-oriented and growing company. They provide above-tariff pay, shift bonuses, and capital-forming benefits. The company fosters a cooperative and familial atmosphere with short decision-making paths, and ample opportunities for professional development. This is an excellent chance to contribute to a vital service and grow within a supportive environment.
