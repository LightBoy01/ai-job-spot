**Intern – Digitalization**

(Based at MDS, Ratmalana)

MAS Intimates, the largest division of MAS Holdings, provides design and manufacturing solutions for leading global lingerie brands. With design hubs in Hong Kong, London, and Sri Lanka, and manufacturing facilities across Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, India, Indonesia, and Kenya, it offers a diverse product portfolio, including bras, briefs, shapewear, performance wear, sleepwear, and wearable tech. Intimates’ customers include Victoria’s Secret, Gap, and Calvin Klein. The division's innovation teams collaborate with universities and experts to deliver consumer-driven, technology-led solutions.

Embark on a journey of change with MAS, by joining the MAS Intimates as Intern – Digitalization. This will be a great opportunity to further your subject knowledge and gain hands-on experience while accelerating your personal development.

**KEY RESPONSIBILITIES**

*   Assist in managing key projects under DPC - Digital Product Creation (PLM, Evangelist Program, Digital Co-Creation).
*   Support initiatives under DBM - Digital Business Models (Seamless Inventory, AI Project Follow-Up).
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams and customers.

**KNOWLEDGE AND SKILLS**

*   Undergraduate degree in Digitalization/ Project Management/ Mathematics/ Digital Innovation/ Data Science/ AI & Analytics.
*   Professional qualifications (CIMA, CIM, or BCS – Level 2 or higher) are preferred.
*   Prior experience in internships or working with cross-functional teams is a plus.
