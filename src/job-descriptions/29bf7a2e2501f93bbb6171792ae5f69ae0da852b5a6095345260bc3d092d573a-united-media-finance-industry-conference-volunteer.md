Our conferences attract major industry players like Blackstone, BlackRock, Apollo, and KKR.\n\nWe are seeking ambitious and talented individuals to join a team of 25+ peers to help us build, run, and deliver the leading French Private Equity Conference. Volunteers are crucial to the success of Private Equity Insights and its mission to foster the world's largest Private Equity community.\n\nJoin us at the Marriott Hotel City West in Munich on the 26th September 2025! A 2023 Milan's highlight video is available [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1w9amNARIROInji1jERo8KzgPLCfemnQM/view?usp=drive_link) for a sense of the conference atmosphere. More information can be found on our website [here](https://pe-insights.com/private-equity-conferences/france/).\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a Private Equity Conference Volunteer, you will play a vital role in ensuring the successful execution of the event. While specific tasks will be detailed during training, general responsibilities include:\n*   Actively contributing to the setup, running, and delivery of the conference.\n*   Assisting with various logistical aspects to ensure a smooth experience for 500+ attendees, including leaders, entrepreneurs, and investors.\n*   Engaging with event participants, providing information and support as needed.\n*   Attending a mandatory 2-hour training session and rehearsal the afternoon before the conference.\n*   Committing your time for the full day of the conference on September 26th, 2025.\n\n### Qualifications\nWe are looking for individuals who are:\n*   Ambitious and talented, with a proactive mindset.\n*   Eager to expand their professional network within the private equity sector.\n*   Interested in developing soft skills, including leadership, communication, and management.\n*   Enthusiastic about learning the intricacies of organizing a leading international finance event.\n*   Collaborative and able to work effectively as part of a 25+ person team.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis volunteer opportunity with Private Equity Insights offers an unparalleled chance for aspiring professionals and students to gain direct exposure to the elite world of private equity. The structured benefits, including individual management training, skill development in communication, and an exclusive volunteer dinner, highlight the organization's commitment to your growth. The insights gained from running a major conference, coupled with direct interaction with industry leaders, make this a truly unique and valuable experience.
