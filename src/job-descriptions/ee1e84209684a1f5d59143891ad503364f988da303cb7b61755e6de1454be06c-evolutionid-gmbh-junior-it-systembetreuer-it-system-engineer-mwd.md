### Description\nThis company is a leading software manufacturer in the field of Identity and Access Management (PIAM), providing a platform that controls digital (IT access) and physical (buildings, vehicles, machines) access rights. Their solutions are crucial for critical infrastructure, industry, aviation, and technology corporations, ensuring employees and external partners have fast and secure access.\n\nIn this varied role, you will be a key player, blending system administration, technical support, and project implementation for their cutting-edge PIAM solutions, ensuring their clients' operations run smoothly and securely.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Long-term Consulting Project with a Major Client:**\n    - Administer access and time tracking systems: Manage system administration, permissions, and regular data maintenance.\n    - Coordinate operations & troubleshooting: Support updates and upgrades, organize incident resolution, and align with specialist departments and technical service providers.\n    - Manage ID card and badge systems: Oversee the on-site ID system, maintain badge printers, and assist with the introduction of new production solutions.\n- **Technical Support for evolutionID Solutions:**\n    - Process support tickets: Analyze technical inquiries, resolve support cases, and assist customers with software, SaaS, and minor hardware issues.\n    - Support evolutionID modules in operation: Manage ID card, image, and RFID components, perform system maintenance, and ensure the stable operation of their solutions.\n- **Project Implementation & Planning:**\n    - Implement smaller software projects: Install, configure, and test applications, achieving quick and visible successes.\n    - Accompany project planning & development: Contribute to technical specifications, documentation, and process analyses, gradually growing into larger projects.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Relevant Professional Experience:** You have a technical or IT-related education or comparable experience, with practical experience in IT support or a technical environment being crucial.\n- **Technical Understanding:** You possess fundamental IT knowledge and are proficient with software tools. Knowledge of access or time tracking systems is a plus, but not a must.\n- **Problem-Solving Skills:** You analyze technical inquiries quickly and pragmatically, independently finding solutions even in new or ambiguous situations.\n- **Independent Work Style:** You work responsibly, hands-on, are eager to learn, and thrive in a dynamic, less bureaucratic environment.\n- **Strong Communication:** You present yourself professionally and communicate confidently – you are fluent in German or English, and have a solid grasp of the other language.\n- **Team & Interface Capability:** You enjoy collaborating with various internal teams and customers, navigating confidently in a role that spans support, technology, and project work.\n- **High On-site Presence & Mobility:** You are willing to perform the majority of your work in their new office at Leonrodplatz in Munich and ideally possess a Class B driver's license for occasional client appointments.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a highly dynamic and impactful role for a technically-minded individual who enjoys a blend of hands-on system administration, direct customer support, and tangible project work. The company offers a unique opportunity to grow professionally within a supportive, international, and non-bureaucratic environment where your ideas are valued, and your contributions genuinely matter. If you thrive on variety, problem-solving, and continuous learning in the critical and evolving field of Identity and Access Management, this position offers significant career development and the chance to make a real difference. The emphasis on collaboration, short decision paths, and direct interaction with both internal teams and clients makes it an exciting opportunity for someone eager to shape their career path and influence product evolution.
