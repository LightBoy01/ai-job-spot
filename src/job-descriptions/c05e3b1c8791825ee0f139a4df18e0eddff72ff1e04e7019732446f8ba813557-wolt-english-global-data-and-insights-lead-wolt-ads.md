### Description\nWolt, a technology company founded in 2014, specializes in the delivery of \"almost everything\" across over 500 cities in 30 countries. Having joined forces with DoorDash in 2022, Wolt is now embarking on an exciting journey to build one of the most important Retail Media ecosystems globally. This platform aims to empower brands to leverage consumer insights, connect with shoppers via in-app and off-app advertising, and deliver unforgettable brand experiences to millions of customers.\n\nWe are seeking a Global Data & Insights Lead to strategically shape and scale the commercial foundation of Wolt's Data & Insights products. This includes crucial offerings such as the upcoming Clean Room capability, self-serve insights dashboards, and new audience products that extend targeting and measurement beyond the Wolt app. In this high-impact role, you will transform complex data capabilities into simple, valuable commercial offerings for brands and agencies. You will collaborate closely with Product, Sales, PMM, Data Science, and Legal/Privacy teams to define packaging, pricing, positioning, and go-to-market plans. This is a significant opportunity for someone passionate about the intersection of product, commercial strategy, and data, eager to build something foundational for the future of Wolt Ads.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Commercial Strategy:**\n    - Shape the Data & Insights product portfolio, packaging, and key customer segments.\n    - Own pricing across subscription, usage, enterprise, and performance models.\n    - Define product rollout strategies, including when products should be self-serve versus managed across markets.\n    - Create clear, practical go-to-market materials such as sales plays, pitch decks, and demos.\n- **Product Partnership:**\n    - Translate customer and market input into sharp product requirements and priorities.\n    - Work closely with Product, Data Science, and Engineering on MVPs and success metrics.\n    - Test and scale new commercial ideas through pilots (e.g., pricing pilots, Clean Room trials).\n- **Go-to-Market & Sales:**\n    - Partner with Sales and Agencies to build effective packages and enterprise proposals.\n    - Support commercial discussions, SLAs, and terms for Clean Room and audience deals.\n    - Equip Sales, Account Managers, and Agencies with simple, effective enablement and training.\n- **Operations & Governance:**\n    - Define commercial KPIs, forecast Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR), and support revenue reporting.\n    - Collaborate with Finance on billing models and usage measurement.\n    - Ensure data products remain commercially consistent and aligned with privacy and consent requirements.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Experience & Background:**\n    - 5–8+ years of experience in product-commercial, product marketing, or commercial strategy roles within data/analytics, retail media, ad-tech, or publisher data.\n    - Proven experience commercializing data products, Clean Room solutions, or privacy-safe data sharing initiatives.\n- **Commercial Acumen:**\n    - Strong commercial skills including pricing, packaging, revenue modeling, and experience with enterprise agreements.\n    - Analytical mindset with confidence in pricing models, forecasts, and Business Intelligence (BI) tools.\n- **Collaboration & Communication:**\n    - Ability to work closely with Product and technical teams to shape roadmaps and MVPs.\n    - Comfortable collaborating with diverse stakeholders including Sales, Agencies, PMM, and Legal/Privacy.\n    - Clear communicator with strong customer-facing and internal storytelling skills.\n- **Work Style:**\n    - Hands-on, proactive, and comfortable navigating a fast-moving matrix environment.\n- **Nice to Have:**\n    - Experience with CPG advertisers/agencies or payment partners.\n    - Background in self-serve data tools or data monetization programs.\n    - Familiarity with Clean Room vendors, AppsFlyer, DMPs, and various data privacy frameworks.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a truly high-impact opportunity for a seasoned commercial strategist to define the future of Wolt's Retail Media and Data & Insights ecosystem across a vast international footprint of 30+ markets. You'll be joining a genuinely humble, friendly, and international culture that champions cross-functional collaboration and offers significant flexibility and autonomy. The role promises competitive compensation, equity, and the chance to build and scale a core global function from the ground up.\n\nSuccess in this position means driving significant adoption and revenue growth for new data products, establishing clear and scalable packaging/pricing models, and meaningfully influencing the product roadmap with commercial insights. Your first 90 days will be critical, involving deep engagement with customers and sales teams, proposing a three-tier packaging/pricing model, launching key pilots, and delivering essential sales enablement. Wolt is also at the forefront of leveraging AI/ML, and this role will actively contribute to using generative tools for pricing scenarios and sales collateral, while ensuring all AI applications remain privacy-safe, ethical, and transparent. If you thrive in an entrepreneurial, fast-paced environment where your work directly shapes the company's strategic direction and revenue, this role offers an unparalleled 'ride of your life'.
