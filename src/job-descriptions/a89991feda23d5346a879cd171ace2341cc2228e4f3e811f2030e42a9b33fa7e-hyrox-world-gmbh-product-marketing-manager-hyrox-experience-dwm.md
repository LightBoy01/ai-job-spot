HYROX is a global sports event series that combines running and functional fitness training in a unique race format. Founded in Germany in 2017, HYROX has evolved into a worldwide fitness movement with events across five continents, attracting tens of thousands of athletes every season.

We are looking for a **Product Marketing Manager (d/w/m) - HYROX Experience** to take ownership of our premium verticals: **Championships & Travel Experiences**. In this role, you act as the strategic hub, connecting our global brand vision with specific product executions. You are not just executing tasks; you are the "Product Owner" for the marketing lifecycle of our most prestigious events.

**YOUR KEY RESPONSIBILITIES**

**Strategy & Go-to-Market**

*   **Lead the marketing strategy** for HYROX Championships and the Travel/Cruises vertical. You define _who_ we target, _how_ we talk to them, and _where_ we reach them.
*   **Develop Go-to-Market plans** for new experience products, ensuring a seamless journey from launch to the event itself.
*   **Manage the marketing budget** for your products independently, ensuring efficient allocation across channels.

**Campaign Management & Content**

*   **Orchestrate integrated campaigns** (Social, CRM, Performance) by briefing and managing internal specialists and external partners. You ensure the right message hits the right channel at the right time.
*   **Oversee social media & content strategy** for your products. Instead of just posting, you define the editorial plan and ensure consistent storytelling.
*   **Coordinate the creation of assets**, working closely with the Brand & Design team to ensure all materials meet global CI guidelines.

**Partnerships & Experiences**

*   **Act as the central point of contact** for brand partners and influencers related to your products, managing sponsorship activations and exclusive influencer events.
*   **Manage Media & Partner Accreditation** on-site, ensuring a premium experience for our VIP guests and press partners.
*   **Collaborate with Operations, Ticketing, and Merchandising** to ensure the customer experience on-site matches the marketing promise.

**Analysis & Growth**

*   **Define and track KPIs** (e.g., ticket sales, CPA, engagement) and report results to the Global Senior Product Marketing Manager.
*   **Utilize CRM and data insights** to segment audiences effectively and increase customer retention for our travel products.

**YOUR PROFILE**

*   **3–6 years of experience** in Marketing, ideally with a background in **Sports, Tourism, or Event Marketing**.
*   **Strong Project Management DNA:** You are organized, structured, and familiar with tools like [Monday.com](http://Monday.com) or Asana. You know how to lead interdisciplinary teams without direct disciplinary power.
*   **Strategic & Operational Mix:** You can write a strategic plan, but you are also willing to roll up your sleeves on-site at a Championship race.
*   **Understanding of the Marketing Mix:** You don’t need to be an expert in every channel (SEO, PR, Social), but you must understand how they work together to steer specialists effectively.
*   **Communication Skills:** Fluent in English; you are a clear communicator who can negotiate with stakeholders and partners.
*   **Willingness to travel** (approx. 20-30%) to support our major Championship events globally.

**WHAT WE OFFER**

*   The opportunity to take on responsibility in a fast-growing, international company with a strong sports DNA.
*   Flat hierarchies, quick decision-making processes, and plenty of room for your ideas.
*   A motivated, international team with a genuine passion for sports and events.
*   A healthy work-life balance thanks to flexible, trust-based working hours.
*   A modern office in a top location – right in Hamburg Altona with excellent transport connections.
*   28 vacation days to recharge and reset.
*   A Wellpass membership and discounted lunch options in-house.
*   Your Deutschlandticket is fully covered by us
*   Employee discounts in our HYROX stores.
