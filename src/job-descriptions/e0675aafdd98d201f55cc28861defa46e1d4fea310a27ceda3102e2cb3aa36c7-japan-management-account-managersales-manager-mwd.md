### Description
Our client, an international company that develops and manufactures **drive systems and precision motors**, is looking for a dedicated Account Manager / Sales Manager to manage and expand the customer base in the European region. This position offers an international environment, a technical product portfolio, and direct customer contact.

### Responsibilities
*   Manage and develop new and existing customers within the sales territory.
*   Plan and conduct customer visits.
*   Act as an interface between customers and various company locations.
*   Inform customers about new product developments and offerings.
*   Support the implementation of the overall sales strategy.
*   Create detailed sales plans for the territory, including a comprehensive lead list.
*   Drive sales results within the territory to achieve agreed-upon KPIs.
*   Conduct customer and market analysis in collaboration with business units.
*   Organize and participate in trade fairs.

### Qualifications
*   Bachelor's degree, ideally in Engineering, or a comparable qualification.
*   More than 3 years of professional experience in sales of technically sophisticated products.
*   Technical understanding in areas such as mechanics, electronics, and aerodynamics.
*   Basic knowledge of business administration.
*   Fluent in English and German; additional languages are a plus.
