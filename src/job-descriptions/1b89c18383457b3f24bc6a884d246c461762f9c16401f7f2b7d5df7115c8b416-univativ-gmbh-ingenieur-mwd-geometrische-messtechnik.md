### Description
univativ is a project and personnel service provider dedicated to offering students and graduates exciting job opportunities with renowned companies, with a strong focus on fostering your career development. We are currently seeking a Geometric Measurement Engineer (m/w/d) to join our client, a global leader in the automotive industry, on a project basis in Affalterbach, with a potential extension until December 1, 2026.

In this role, you will apply your extensive expertise in geometric measurement technology to demanding development projects. You will take responsibility for complex 3D measurement tasks, collaborating closely with various technical departments to actively shape innovative solutions for drivetrain and vehicle components. You can expect a broad range of challenging tasks and a friendly work atmosphere, all while univativ supports your professional growth as a dedicated career companion.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and execute complex metrological test series using 3D measurement systems and other geometric measuring equipment for engine, drivetrain, and vehicle components.
- Operate and program 3D measurement systems, including simulation, execution, and evaluation using associated hardware and software.
- Analyze and evaluate measurement results, identifying and resolving errors using appropriate diagnostic systems.
- Prepare, set up, and provide measurement objects in close collaboration with development departments.
- Manage orders and data, including documentation and evaluation, within the relevant IT systems of the Drivetrain Operations department.

### Qualifications
- Completed technical education with a focus on geometric measurement technology, vehicle technology, or a comparable qualification.
- Several years of experience in 3D measurement technology, including the development, specification, and operational implementation of complex measurement tasks.
- Profound understanding of common measuring and testing devices, as well as typical measurement requirements for mechanical and electrical components in drivetrain and vehicle development.
- Proven ability in programming, operating, and applying 3D measuring machines, including associated hardware and software, and special measurement technology.
- Highly independent, analytical, and structured way of working combined with a strong focus on quality, solutions, and goals.
- At least business-fluent German language skills, both written and spoken.
