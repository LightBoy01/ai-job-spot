### Description\nThe MatchingCompany®, with over 25 years of industry experience, is a leading specialist in professional and sustainable recruitment within People Management across Germany. We are seeking a dedicated **Personnel Consultant / Disponent (m/f/d)** on behalf of our client for their Bielefeld location. In this pivotal 360° role, you will be responsible for the temporary placement (ANÜ) of industrial-technical personnel, playing a key part in connecting skilled individuals with suitable client companies.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a Personnel Consultant / Disponent, your core responsibilities will include:\n- Independently managing the entire personnel recruitment process for temporary assignments with client companies.\n- Actively recruiting new employees for placements within the framework of temporary employment.\n- Providing sustainable support for existing client accounts and actively acquiring new clients.\n- Sourcing qualified applicants through various channels, managing contract drafting, and providing personal support throughout their assignments.\n- Ensuring the precise placement of employees to meet specific client requirements.\n\n### Qualifications\nTo thrive in this role, you should possess:\n- A successfully completed vocational training or equivalent qualification.\n- Proven experience within the personnel services industry, specifically in temporary employment for the industrial-technical sector.\n- Fluent German language skills, both written and spoken.\n- A strong passion for sales and a collaborative team spirit.\n- A high degree of personal responsibility and a pronounced service-oriented mindset.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a proactive and service-oriented individual passionate about the HR and recruitment industry. The MatchingCompany® offers a dynamic environment where you can truly make an impact, connecting skilled industrial-technical professionals with leading companies. Beyond competitive, performance-based compensation and generous holiday allowance (minimum 30 days), the company is deeply committed to your professional development through comprehensive onboarding and continuous training. You'll enjoy significant autonomy and individual growth possibilities, supported by a collaborative team culture that values initiative and a strong service ethos. If you're ready to embrace a 360° consulting role with a focus on sustainable placements and client success, this could be your perfect match.
