**JOB TITLE:**  

The Senior Technical Project Manager will work closely with Engineering, QA, Ops and Marketing Science resources on developing & delivery state of the art AI enabled data products for key RAPP clients using proprietary RAPP technologies. 

**_The Opportunity_** 

*   Managing cross-functional highly skilled teams using agile methodologies. 

*   Helping to ship new features and enhancements for our proprietary, AI enabled, technologies. 

*   Getting hands on with engineering teams working on ETL pipelines and data lakes. 

*   Client facing providing updates and documentation for clients on progress, associated risks and dependencies. 

*   You’ll have autonomy but also supporting the Technical Programme Director and Product Manager on delivery and operations (scoping, backlog management, roadmap planning, etc). 

*   Work on key global Omnicom accounts – household names in the automative, CPG, pharmaceutical, finance & fashion sectors.  

**_Role Requirements_**  

*   Experience in an agency environment: with a track record in Martech and technical project delivery. Comfortable with AI, data and analytics projects. 

*   Self-starter: proactive, able to drive projects forward, prioritise effectively, focus on solutions, and meet deadlines. 

*   Strong organisational and project management skills: scoping, scheduling, team coordination, resource planning, and attention to detail. 

*   Comfortable in client-facing situations: working with global brands with strong expectation management skills. 

*   Project Manager experience in areas including: Agile principles (a scrum certified would be ideal) and proficient in delivery tools such as JIRA. Plus comfortable with budget, resource and vendor management. 

*   Proficiency and an understanding of marketing and media data: comfortable working with technical teams & data engineering resources (onshore & offshore). 

*   Interest in working with data and AI products: working closely with programme and product management. 

*   Someone who’s organised, detail-oriented: thrives in dynamic and complex multi-stakeholder environments. 

A collaborative and proactive problem solver: able to connect the right people and resources to achieve outcomes quickly. 

*   Experienced in different technologies & platforms: comfortable with managing teams that use Cloud Services (AWS), and who work with AI/ML and database technologies such as PostgresSQL and Databricks
