**About the role**

We're seeking a **Design Ops Lead** to facilitate and optimize Product Design processes at the Intact Lab. This role invites an experienced professional to implement best practices across teams, enhance cross-functional workflows, establish training programs, and foster a vibrant culture. Your leadership will be pivotal in making Intact Lab the premier destination for Design specialists.

**What you'll do here:**

*   **Process Optimization:** Analyze, design, and implement streamlined workflows that enhance efficiency and effectiveness, from discovery through delivery and beyond. Establish and standardize best practices and methodologies across Design teams.
    
*   **Tooling and Automation:** Assess, pilot and integrate AI tools to facilitate work tasks and transform processes. Administer Design software and ensure staff are well equipped and leveraging tools to their full potential.
    
*   **Learning and Development:** Establish and nurture our community of practice. Curate a training curriculum that supports product managers and stakeholders in enhancing their skills. Provide thought leadership and mentorship. Standardize design nomenclature.
    
*   **Cross-functional Collaboration:** Partner with Engineering, Product and Data teams to define cross-functional operating models. Facilitate workshops, meetings, and joint planning sessions to ensure alignment on goals and priorities.
    

**What you bring to the table:**

*   Minimum of 5 years experience in Product Design (UX/UI), including 2 in Design Ops.
    
*   Prior work in large enterprises.
    
*   Demonstrated ability to lead by influence and bring about change.
    
*   Ability to develop compelling presentations that secure buy-in and inspire stakeholders.
    
*   For candidates located in Quebec, bilingualism is required considering the necessity to interact on a regular basis with English-speaking colleagues across the country. 
    
*   No Canadian work experience required however must be eligible to work in Canada.
