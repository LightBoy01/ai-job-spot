# AI/ML Data Scientist

- **Company:** Sanofi
- **Location:** Cambridge, MA
- **Job Level:** Associate Director
- **Application Link:** https://www.sanofi.com/en/careers

---

### Description

Sanofi is seeking an AI/ML Data Scientist to join their AI and Computational Sciences team. This role focuses on transforming healthcare through the application of AI, working with diverse datasets including image, omics, wearable sensor data, and clinical data analytics. Drive innovation in areas like precision medicine, advanced omics analysis, and digital health.

### Responsibilities

- Develop and implement AI/ML models for healthcare applications.
- Analyze complex biological and clinical datasets.
- Collaborate with research scientists and clinicians.
- Contribute to the strategic direction of AI in healthcare.

### Qualifications

- Ph.D. or Master's degree in Computer Science, Bioinformatics, or a related field.
- Strong experience in machine learning, deep learning, and statistical modeling.
- Proficiency in Python and relevant libraries (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch).
- Experience with large-scale data analysis in a scientific domain.
