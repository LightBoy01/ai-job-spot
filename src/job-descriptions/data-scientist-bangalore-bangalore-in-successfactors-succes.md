<b>Role Overview:</b>

<p>We are seeking a highly skilled and motivated Senior Data Scientist with 3-5 years of experience in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML). The ideal candidate will be responsible for driving end-to-end problem-solving initiatives, from understanding the problem to developing predictive and prescriptive models, deploying solutions, and effectively communicating insights to stakeholders. Experience in time-series analysis and CAD/CAE will be considered a strong plus.</p>


Key Responsibilities:

<ul>
<li>Problem Understanding
<ul>
<li>Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify and understand business challenges.</li>
<li>Translate business problems into analytical tasks and define the problem scope.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Data Exploration and Preparation
<ul>
<li>Collect, clean, and preprocess large datasets from multiple sources.</li>
<li>Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) to extract meaningful insights.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Model Development and Time-Series Analysis
<ul>
<li>Design, develop, and optimize AI/ML models to solve complex problems.</li>
<li>Apply advanced techniques to analyze and forecast time-series data.</li>
<li>Implement end-to-end machine learning pipelines, from data ingestion to deployment.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Deployment and Scalability
<ul>
<li>Build and deploy machine learning models into production environments.</li>
<li>Ensure scalability, robustness, and efficiency of deployed models.</li>
<li>Implement MLOps practices for model lifecycle management.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Communication
<ul>
<li>Present technical findings and actionable insights to non-technical stakeholders.</li>
<li>Create visualizations and reports to communicate results effectively.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Continuous Improvement
<ul>
<li>Stay updated with the latest advancements in AI/ML technologies.</li>
<li>Experiment with new tools and techniques to enhance model performance</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>


<b>Experience</b>

<ul>
<li>3-5 years of hands-on experience in AI/ML model development, deployment, and lifecycle management.</li>
</ul>


<b>Qualifications</b>

<ul>
<li>Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, or related fields.</li>
</ul>


<b>Required Skills:</b>

<ul>
<li>Proficiency in programming languages like Python.</li>
<li>Strong knowledge of machine learning algorithms, deep learning frameworks (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch), and optimization techniques.</li>
<li>Experience working with time-series data analysis and forecasting techniques.</li>
<li>Expertise in deploying models using cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure) and containerization tools (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes).</li>
<li>Excellent communication and presentation skills to convey complex ideas clearly.</li>
