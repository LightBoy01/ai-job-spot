**Job Description Summary**
As Customer Experience & Digital Manager leads the CX & Digital Team in driving business transformation through customer-centric
strategies and digital innovation. Champions initiatives that accelerate both Growth and Launch Excellence by integrating emerging
technologies, innovative business models, and value-based partnerships.
**Job Description**
**Key Responsibilities:**
*   Navigates complexity within the CX & Digital Team and developing each team members by acting as a ICU leadership.
*   Develops strategies based on customer insights to enhance HCP experience and maximize brand engagement.
*   Leads deployment and adoption of new digital platforms and tools to elevate customer engagement and amplify brand presence for InMB products.
*   Executes new engagement strategies for new products to support Day 0 Access.
*   Transforms digital content generation processes across technologies (MarkTech), internal systems and the teams.
*   Acts as an accelerator and facilitator for the disruptive innovation approach and methodology across Türkiye and APMA.
*   Empowers teams to generate innovative ideas and provides the structure and support needed to transform those ideas into impactful initiatives aligned with our Growth Ambition.
*   Serves as a strategic representative of Novartis by cultivating high-impact partnerships with external stakeholders in the domains of digital health and artificial intelligence.
*   Continuously scans the external landscape to identify emerging trends and shifts, offering strategic insights and foresight to inform leadership decision-making.
*   Monitor and analyze customer feedback and insight, analyze the engagement KPIs to identify improvement opportunities.
**Key performance indicators:**
*   Digital engagement metrics
*   Customer satisfaction (CSAT), Net Promoter Score (NPS)
*   Content effectiveness, # of new Content, CTR, OR, saving
*   Project delivery timelines and impact on growth, MS, launch excellence
*   Cross-functional collaboration effectiveness and stakeholder feedback
*   Productivity, cost effectiveness
**Essential Requirements:**
*   At least 2 years of Marketing Experience
*   Customer Experience Strategy & Execution
*   People Manager Experience
*   Digital Transformation & Agile Project Management
*   Corporate Innovation
*   Digital Marketing
*   A Customer-Centric Mindset
*   Digital Marketing & Analytics
