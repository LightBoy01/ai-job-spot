MaintainX is the world's leading Asset and Work Intelligence platform for industrial and frontline environments. We are a modern IoT-enabled cloud-based tool for reliability, safety, and operations on physical equipment and facilities. MaintainX powers operational excellence for 12,000+ businesses including Duracell, Univar Solutions Inc., Titan America, McDonald's, Brenntag, Cintas, Xylem, and Shell.

We recently completed a $150 million Series D funding round, bringing our total funding to $254 million and valuing the company at $2.5 billion.

As the **AI Manager of Growth & Acquisition**, you will be responsible for driving the next wave of AI-powered marketing efficiency and scale. Sitting within the Growth Marketing team, this role focuses on front-end growth levers—content, targeting, and audiences—while partnering across Paid, SEO, and Lifecycle functions to deploy high-impact AI capabilities. The ideal candidate blends technical AI/ML fluency with strong growth marketing instincts, able to rapidly test, deploy, and scale acquisition plays that directly improve pipeline efficiency.

#### **What you'll do:**

*   **AI Personalization Engines** – Build and optimize AI-driven personalization across website experiences, ad targeting, and email journeys to improve conversion.
*   **Generative AI for Creative Scale** – Deploy AI tools for high-volume, high-quality content production (ads, landing pages, SEO content) that accelerate campaign velocity.
*   **Predictive Audience Modeling** – Run machine learning models to identify high-intent segments, create lookalike audiences, and drive precision in paid channels.
*   **AI Market Research & Intelligence** – Leverage AI to synthesize competitive intelligence, identify whitespace, and guide smarter targeting strategies.
*   **Cross-Functional Experimentation** – Partner with Paid Media, SEO, and Lifecycle Marketing teams to design and scale AI-driven acquisition experiments.
*   **Deliver outcomes that:** 

*   Increase efficiency of acquisition spend across paid and organic channels.
*   Improve visit-to-conversion rates through personalized and AI-optimized journeys.
*   Accelerate pipeline growth at the top of the funnel, fueling the company’s enterprise and mid-market expansion.

#### **What you’ll bring:**

*   **Speed to Execution:** You bias toward action, quickly moving from idea to test to scaled program.
*   **Curiosity with Rigor:** You explore AI tools and approaches creatively, but always measure impact and scalability.
*   **Experimentation Mindset:** You thrive in test-and-learn environments, designing bold experiments and doubling down on what works.
*   **Collaboration Across Functions:** You partner deeply with Paid, SEO, and Lifecycle teams to maximize the impact of AI-driven plays.
*   **Growth Orientation:** You care about pipeline, efficiency, and measurable business outcomes above vanity metrics.
*   **Responsible AI:** You champion ethical use of AI, ensuring accuracy, transparency, and trust in how we scale campaigns.

#### **About you:**

*   5+ years in growth marketing, digital acquisition, or marketing analytics, with hands-on experience in AI/ML applications.
*   Proven ability to use AI platforms (e.g., Jasper, SurferSEO, Clay, Demandbase, or equivalent) for campaign execution and audience modeling.
*   Experience with paid media optimization, SEO/SEM, and digital testing frameworks.
*   Strong analytical skills and comfort with predictive modeling, experimentation, and performance reporting.
*   Entrepreneurial mindset: thrives in test-and-learn environments, bias toward speed and execution.
