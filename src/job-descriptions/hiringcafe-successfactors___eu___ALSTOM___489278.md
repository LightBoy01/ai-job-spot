At Alstom, we understand transport networks and what moves people. From high-speed trains, metros, monorails, and trams, to turnkey systems, services, infrastructure, signalling and digital mobility, we offer our diverse customers the broadest portfolio in the industry. Every day, more than 80 000 colleagues lead the way to greener and smarter mobility worldwide, connecting cities as we reduce carbon and replace cars.

**Your Mission**

As Procurement Digital Transformation Director, you will lead the strategic evolution of Alstom’s global procurement digital landscape. You will define and execute the digital roadmap, drive adoption of cutting-edge tools including AI and GenAI, and ensure alignment with our 3-year strategic plan.

**Key Responsibilities**

Digital Transformation

*   Define strategy, standards, and goals for procurement processes and applications
*   Accountable for the procurement global digital roadmap and tool performance
*   Ensure alignment and adoption across the procurement community
*   Manage relationships with IS&T (Information Systems & Technology) towers

Team Management

*   Lead and develop the Business Process Owner team
*   Ensure target achievement and timely delivery
*   Promote ethical conduct and Alstom’s Code of Ethics

**What You Bring**

*   15–20 years of experience in digital transformation within large industrial companies
*   Proven success in AI/GenAI implementation in procurement
*   Strong leadership and communication skills in multicultural environments
*   Expertise in SAP Ariba, ECC, S4, and data analytics
*   Fluent in English with an international mindset

**Your Background**

*   Degree from a Business or Engineering school (Information Systems or IT)
*   Master’s in Purchasing (preferred)

You don’t need to be a train enthusiast to thrive with us. We guarantee that when you step onto one of our trains with your friends or family, you’ll be proud. If you’re up for the challenge, we’d love to hear from you!
