### Your role
Are you a designer with a track record of designing meaningful experiences for large scale and complex businesses? Do you enjoy deeply understanding user and business needs, rapidly iterating concepts with users, and then implementing them with technology partners? Do you want to play a key role in transforming our firm into an AI powered and client centric organization?
We're looking for an Experience designer to:
• drive the Front to Back Digital Transformation agenda of the Wealth Management (WM) division by deeply understanding the jobs-to-be-done and motivations of WM Clients, Client Advisors and Operations colleagues
• facilitate co-creation sessions and work closely with product, markets and business SMEs to understand business needs, identify opportunities and use design to drive desired business outcomes
• transform existing journeys and processes by designing for AI and Agentic AI powered experiences
• rapidly test and learn with users and iterate either in design space or in production
• work closely with engineers to deploy quality solutions in production at scale and execute design work with a high level of polish

### Your team
