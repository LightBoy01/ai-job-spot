### Description\nFlora Food Group is a global leader in plant-based food, offering delicious, natural, nutritious, and sustainable alternatives to dairy across four growing categories. With iconic brands like Flora, Becel, Rama, and Violife, they hold leadership positions in over 100 countries.\n\nWe are seeking an ambitious Key Account Manager to drive business development and growth in the **Austrian market**. If you possess an \"Entrepreneur Mindset\" and thrive on successfully managing major clients and implementing growth strategies, this is an opportunity to join a market leader pioneering the global plant-based food trend. This role is based in Vienna, Austria.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Actively manage customer needs and expectations, ensuring strong relationships.\n- Drive forecast planning, monitoring, and ensure high forecast accuracy.\n- Collaborate closely with the Sales Lead CHATIT, Finance, Demand Planning, and Marketing departments.\n- Plan and manage customer budgets to achieve and exceed set revenue and profitability targets.\n- Proactively steer your business by understanding and leveraging Risks & Opportunities.\n- Develop and create negotiation strategies for value-adding measures for 2026.\n\n### Qualifications\n- University degree.\n- Minimum of 3 years of Key Account experience within the FMCG sector.\n- Profound knowledge of the Austrian food retail (LEH, DFH) and local trade structures.\n- Excellent proficiency in Excel, Pivot data, Nielsen, and GFK data analysis.\n- Fluent in both German and English.\n- Proven strong negotiation skills.\n- Demonstrated success in implementing sustainable growth strategies with major customers.\n- A proactive \"Macher\" (doer) and Teamplayer mindset.\n- Ability to perceive challenges as opportunities and transform problems into effective solutions.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a driven Key Account Manager to make a significant impact within a rapidly growing and purpose-driven global company. Flora Food Group is at the forefront of the plant-based revolution, offering a chance to shape the future of food in Austria. The culture fosters dynamism, collaboration, and a proactive approach, valuing individuals who embrace challenges and contribute to shared success. With a strong commitment to diversity and inclusion, this role is ideal for a \"Macher\" who wants to grow with a company dedicated to sustainability and innovation.
