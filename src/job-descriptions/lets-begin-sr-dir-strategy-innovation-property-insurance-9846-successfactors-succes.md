Let's begin! Sr Dir-Strategy &amp; Innovation | Property Insurance (9846)

Requisition ID&nbsp;<b>9846</b>&nbsp;-&nbsp;Posted&nbsp;<b></b>&nbsp;-&nbsp;<b>Remote Worker-USA1-40</b> More (2)

        

        <p>At Moody's, we unite the brightest minds to turn today’s risks into tomorrow’s opportunities. We do this by striving to create an inclusive environment where everyone feels welcome to be who they are—with the freedom to exchange ideas, think innovatively, and listen to each other and customers in meaningful ways. Moody’s is transforming how the world sees risk. As a global leader in ratings and integrated risk assessment, we’re advancing AI to move from insight to action—enabling intelligence that not only understands complexity but responds to it. We decode risk to unlock opportunity, helping our clients navigate uncertainty with clarity, speed, and confidence.</p>
<p>If you are excited about this opportunity but do not meet every single requirement, please apply! You still may be a great fit for this role or other open roles. We are seeking candidates who model our values: invest in every relationship, lead with curiosity, champion diverse perspectives, turn inputs into actions, and uphold trust through integrity.&nbsp;</p>
        
Skills and Competencies
<ul>
<li>15+ years of experience in strategy, product, marketing, or market research within the Property Insurance space.</li>
<li>Deep understanding of market dynamics, customer personas, and workflows in the Property Insurance sector.</li>
<li>Proven ability to translate market insights into actionable business strategies.</li>
<li>Strong leadership and mentoring capabilities, fostering collaboration and high performance.</li>
<li>Exceptional problem-solving and communication skills, with the ability to influence senior stakeholders.</li>
<li>Experience working in Agile environments and managing multiple strategic initiatives simultaneously.</li>
<li>Basic understanding of artificial intelligence concepts, with curiosity and enthusiasm for learnin how AI tools can be used to improve processes and drive efficiency. Interest in exploring AI systems and a willingness to develop awareness of responsible AI practices, including risk management and ethical use.</li>
</ul>

&nbsp;Education
<ul>
<li>Undergraduate degree in a business-related field required.</li>
<li>MBA or other relevant graduate degree preferred.</li>
</ul>

&nbsp;Responsibilities
<ul>
<li>Drive strategic growth and market insight for Moody’s Property Insurance segment.</li>
<li>Analyse and monitor demand and competitive trends impacting Moody’s customers and prospects.</li>
<li>Identify growth opportunities across customer sub-segments and workflows.</li>
<li>Prioritise and deploy product, sales, service, and marketing resources to improve profitability.</li>
<li>Support M&amp;A and investment activities with strategic recommendations.</li>
<li>Inform marketing messages and positioning through market insights.</li>
<li>Leverage deep knowledge of the Property Insurance sub-segment to shape product and go-to-market strategies.</li>
<li>Provide expert insights into industry trends, regulatory changes, and emerging technologies.</li>
<li>Maintain strong understanding of Moody’s solutions and their value propositions.</li>
<li>Collaborate with product strategy, GTM enablement, sales, and marketing teams to align initiatives.</li>
<li>Assess strategic partners and acquisition targets to expand market reach.</li>
<li>Contribute to sales and marketing materials and thought leadership content.</li>
</ul>

About the team
<p>Moody’s Insurance Business Unit shapes the world’s view of risk for insurers, reinsurers, financial services organisations, and the public sector.</p>
<p>Our Risk Models underpin nearly $2 trillion in the property and casualty insurance industry.</p>
<p>We empower organisations to evaluate and manage global risk from natural and man-made catastrophes—including hurricanes, earthquakes, floods, climate change, cyber threats, pandemics, and liability risks.</p>
<p>By joining our team, you will be part of exciting work in AI-powered risk modelling, intelligent automation, and customer-centric design across global markets.<br><br></p>
