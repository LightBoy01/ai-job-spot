<p><strong>Position Overview:</strong></p>
<p>We are seeking a detail-oriented and analytical Junior FP&amp;A Analyst to join our finance team. The ideal candidate will have 2 to 5 years of experience and hold a degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related Technology field. This role plays a critical part in supporting financial planning, budgeting, forecasting, analysis, and the development and maintenance of KPI scorecards to drive informed business decisions. The candidate should demonstrate proficiency in leveraging AI tools and advanced analytics to enhance financial modeling and reporting.&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Key Responsibilities:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>

Assist in the preparation, analysis, and monitoring of monthly, quarterly, and annual budgeting and forecasting processes
</li>
<li>

Develop, build, and maintain KPI scorecards and dashboards to track financial and operational performance metrics
</li>
<li>

Support the creation of detailed financial models to evaluate business performance, risks, and opportunities
</li>
<li>

Analyze financial data to identify trends, variances, and key performance drivers
</li>
<li>

Prepare comprehensive reports and presentations for management to support strategic and operational decision-making
</li>
<li>

Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather, validate, and reconcile financial data inputs
</li>
<li>

Utilize AI and automation tools to streamline financial analysis, reporting, and forecasting accuracy
</li>
<li>

Maintain data integrity and ensure compliance with company policies and accounting standards
</li>
<li>

Assist in ad hoc financial analyses and special projects as needed
</li>
</ul>
