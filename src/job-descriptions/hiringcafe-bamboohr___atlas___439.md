Who we are looking for:

  
We are seeking an experienced AI Solutions Lead with a strong background in AI/ML   
and expertise in delivering scalable AI solutions across diverse problems. The ideal   
candidate should have a proven track record of translating business requirements into   
impactful AI solutions. Prior experience in Business Analysis, Product Management or   
Program Management is a plus. The candidate must have a strong educational

… and hunger towards staying updated on the latest AI/ML trends and

and Agentic AI, and innovative deployment techniques.

ensuring adherence to timelines, budgets, and quality standards.

organizations, helping them leverage AI for enhanced decision-making, and   
operational efficiency.
