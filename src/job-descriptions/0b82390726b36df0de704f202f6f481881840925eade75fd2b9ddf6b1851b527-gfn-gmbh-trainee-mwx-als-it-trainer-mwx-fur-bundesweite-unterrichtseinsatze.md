### Description\
GFN GmbH is a leading IT education provider in Germany, dedicated to supporting individuals in their further education and career reorientation within the IT sector. This is a unique 12-month paid Trainee program specifically designed for aspiring IT Trainers. During this intensive year, you will gain hands-on experience and proactively contribute to the world of reskilling and continuous education, while also getting acquainted with numerous GFN locations nationwide. The program's core focus is to optimally prepare you for your future role as an IT Trainer, culminating in the acquisition of the official IHK trainer qualification (Ausbildereignung).\
\
### Responsibilities\
- Undergo comprehensive and optimal preparation for your future role as an IT Trainer (m/w/x) at GFN.\
- Receive expert professional, methodical, and didactic-pedagogical guidance from experienced colleagues.\
- Participate in an intensive onboarding phase, gaining deep insights into classroom teaching methodologies across various GFN locations.\
- Subsequently, take on and lead your own classes nationwide, delivering instruction both in-person and online within your specialized IT fields.\
\
### Qualifications\
- Demonstrate a reliable and team-oriented working approach.\
- Possess a strong willingness to travel throughout Germany, with arrangements made individually.\
- Exhibit excellent communication skills, with the ability to clearly explain complex topics to others and confidently present in front of groups.\
- Display a genuine affinity and enthusiasm for IT subjects.\
- Bring a relevant technical background, which can stem from further education, a technical university degree, a pertinent apprenticeship, or professional experience.\
- Command very good German language skills, certified at a minimum C1 level.\
- Applicants without a formal IT degree are expressly encouraged to apply.\
- Articulate your motivation for becoming a trainer and, optionally, provide details of any prior experience in teaching, tutoring, coaching, or similar roles within the educational sector.\
\
