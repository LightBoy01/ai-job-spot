**The role:** 

Reporting to the Senior Manager, IT & Operations, as the **Manager, IT Engineering** you recognize the need to build highly scalable and automated solutions for a growing company. You will lead a team of IT Engineers, taking a hands-on approach to implementing and designing complex, AI-powered workflows. Working with stakeholders across the company, you'll develop people-centric solutions using a diverse range of modern tools and technologies to solve everyday problems.

The **Manager, IT Engineering** will:

*   Partner with the Senior Manager, IT & Operations to develop and execute the company’s IT strategy, ensuring alignment with business goals. 
*   Collaborate with senior leadership to assess future IT needs, including representing the IT team, scaling processes, and implementing best practices for security and compliance.
*   Build, mentor, and manage a high-performing IT Engineering team. The IT Engineering team is currently 3 individual contributors and will grow over time. 
*   Ensure the uptime and reliability of shared IT services and hardware, including disaster recovery and business continuity planning.
*   Collaborate with Legal and Security teams to address data privacy and cybersecurity concerns.
*   And more! 

**To be successful, you should have:**

*   Proven track record of leading highly technical teams, preferably in a growing SaaS or technology company, with approximately 6+ yrs of total experience. 
*   Ability to operate as a player/coach. You have excellent team leadership skills, with experience managing systems administration teams, and you can get your hands dirty when needed. 
*   Experience architecting projects centered around automation, using tools such as Zapier, Workato, n8n, Okta Workflows, etc. 
*   Experience applying LLMs and agentic AI to workflow automation or internal tooling. 
*   Knowledge of common scripting languages (eg. Python, Bash, etc.) 
*   Familiarity with public cloud providers such as AWS or GCP.

**It would be really great (but not a deal-breaker!) if you had:**

*   Experience leading the deployment of internal tools (eg. code-based or no-code).
*   Knowledge of SDLC, CI/CD, and IaC.
