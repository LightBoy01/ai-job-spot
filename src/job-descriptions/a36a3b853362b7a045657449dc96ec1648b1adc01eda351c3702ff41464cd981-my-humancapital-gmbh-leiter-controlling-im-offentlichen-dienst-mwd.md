### Description\nOur partner company in Munich is seeking a **Head of Controlling for the public sector (m/w/d)** to join their team as soon as possible. In this pivotal leadership role, you will be responsible for the technical and disciplinary guidance of the controlling team, driving the strategic evolution of controlling processes within the public service context. You will play a crucial part in ensuring financial transparency, optimizing cost structures, and spearheading the digitalization of controlling instruments to support strategic decision-making at the executive level.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Provide technical and disciplinary leadership to the controlling team and take responsibility for the strategic development of controlling processes.\n- Manage budget planning, forecasts, and target-actual comparisons, ensuring financial transparency across all business units.\n- Analyze business key figures, derive recommendations for action, and support management in strategic decisions.\n- Develop impactful reports and dashboards, optimize cost structures, and advance the digitalization of controlling instruments.\n- Coordinate cross-departmental projects, participate in audits, and ensure efficient collaboration between Controlling, Accounting, and Management.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Possess a degree in Business Administration, Finance, Accounting, or a comparable qualification (e.g., Controller (m/w/d) or Balance Sheet Accountant (m/w/d)).\n- Bring several years of professional experience in controlling, ideally with leadership responsibility.\n- Demonstrate profound knowledge of MS Excel, ERP systems (e.g., SAP FI/CO), and modern BI tools (e.g., Power BI, LucaNet, Jedox).\n- Work analytically, results-oriented, and possess a high degree of numerical understanding and strong communication skills.\n- Very good German language skills complete your profile.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned Controlling professional to step into a leadership role within the public sector in Munich. You'll not only manage a team but also significantly influence strategic direction and drive digitalization initiatives. The company offers a supportive and appreciative work culture with flexible hours, home office options, and a strong commitment to your professional growth through continuous learning and development. If you're looking to make a tangible impact and thrive in an environment that values trust and collaboration, this role is for you.
