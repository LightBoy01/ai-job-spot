The company values work-life balance with flexible hours and home office options, promotes a supportive 'Du-Kultur,' and invests heavily in employee growth through structured onboarding and extensive training. If you're a strategic thinker with a passion for digitalization and team leadership, this role offers significant impact and a very positive work environment.
