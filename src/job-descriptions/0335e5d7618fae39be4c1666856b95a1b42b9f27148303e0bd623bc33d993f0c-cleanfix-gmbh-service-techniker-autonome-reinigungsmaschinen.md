This vital role positions you as the primary technical interface for our customers, ensuring the seamless operation and enthusiastic adoption of our cutting-edge autonomous robotic solutions across Germany.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Visit customers on-site to perform technical service, installations, and technical consulting.\n- Conduct demonstrations and provide comprehensive training for our autonomous cleaning solutions.\n- Offer assistance and solutions for all technical inquiries.\n- Act as a crucial liaison between our customers and the internal office team.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Ideally, a successfully completed technical education and proven experience with (cleaning) robotics.\n- Essential: A high willingness to travel throughout Germany, up to 5 days a week.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a dedicated technical specialist eager to work with advanced robotics. You'll be instrumental in supporting our market-leading autonomous cleaning solutions, directly impacting customer success. The role offers a motivating team environment, a company car for private use, modern IT, 30 days of vacation, and a company pension option, though it requires extensive travel across Germany.
