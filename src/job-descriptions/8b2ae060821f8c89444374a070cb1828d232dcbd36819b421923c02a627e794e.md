**The Position**

*   Provide technical expertise and presales support, including gathering customer requirements, preparing solution proposals, delivering presentations, and conducting product demonstrations, with a focus on HPC and AI project delivery
*   Offer technical guidance throughout the sales cycle, collaborating with customers to analyze their business and technical requirements, ensuring the delivery of innovative and impactful AI-driven solutions
*   Develop comprehensive technical proposals, product recommendations, and deployment strategies for AI and HPC-related services
*   Coordinate and lead project kick-offs to ensure smooth transitions from presales to service delivery, effectively meeting project milestones
*   Support business development initiatives by identifying new opportunities, building relationships, and driving customer engagement
*   Provide training on AI technologies and solutions to current and potential customers, enhancing their understanding and adoption
*   Continuously explore and propose innovative ideas, strategies, and technologies to enhance service offerings and differentiate from competitors
*   Stay updated on emerging technologies, industry trends, and best practices in AI development, project management, and HPC, maintaining a competitive edge
*   Perform additional duties and responsibilities as assigned   
     

**The Candidate**

*   Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Information Systems, or other related disciplines
*   8 years of experience, preferably in software development, programme or project management, technology innovation, or AI/data-related initiatives
*   Familiarity with AI concepts, technologies, and trends (e.g., large language models (LLMs), analytics, geospatial data)
*   Proficiency in using reporting tools and business intelligence platforms for pipeline management and impact reporting
*   Exposure to and HPC technologies and data processing will be an advantage
*   Experience in AI development, model training, or deployment and/or knowledge of AI programming frameworks like PyTorch or TensorFlow is a plus
*   Ability to engage credibly with technical and business stakeholders
*   Strong interpersonal, communication, and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence and engage diverse audiences effectively
*   Highly self-motivated, organized, and capable of managing multiple projects and initiatives under tight deadlines
*   Good command of written and spoken English and Cantonese including Mandarin
*   Higher education or professional qualifications with less experience will also be considered

**About Hong Kong Science & Technology Parks Corporation**

Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks Corporation (HKSTP) was established in 2001 to create a thriving I&T ecosystem grooming unicorns, more than 15,000 research professionals and over 2,400 technology companies from 26 countries and regions focused on developing healthtech, AI and robotics, fintech and smart city technologies, etc.

Our growing innovation ecosystem offers comprehensive support to attract and nurture talent, accelerate and commercialise innovation for technology ventures. HKSTP continues contribute in establishing I&T as a pillar of growth for Hong Kong.
