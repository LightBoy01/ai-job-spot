### Description\nWe offer ambitious talents the opportunity to get to know the diverse world of business and management consulting within a six-month trainee program. Join us at our location in the heart of Cologne and actively shape the future of our clients – and your own. This program emphasizes flexibility, personal development through mentoring, and an attractive, performance-based compensation model within a dynamic, young team with flat hierarchies.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Individual Consulting**: Together with your team, you advise demanding private and business clients on wealth accumulation, investment, real estate financing, and pension planning.\n- **Competence Development**: You acquire sound technical and sales knowledge to purposefully develop your consulting expertise.\n- **Business Development**: You independently take on projects and actively contribute your ideas to strategic issues.\n- **Entrepreneurial Thinking**: As a trainee, you act like an entrepreneur within the company and make a significant contribution to the development of new concepts and processes.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You have successfully completed your studies or are about to graduate with convincing results.\n- You are characterized by initiative, goal orientation, and entrepreneurial thinking.\n- You communicate confidently and empathetically – both within the team and in client dialogue.\n- Personal development is not a secondary goal for you, but part of your attitude.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for ambitious graduates looking to dive into the world of consulting. The six-month trainee program in Cologne offers a unique blend of hands-on client work, dedicated mentorship, and significant personal development. With a strong emphasis on entrepreneurial thinking and a supportive, flexible work environment, it's ideal for those eager to make an immediate impact and build a strong foundation for their career.
