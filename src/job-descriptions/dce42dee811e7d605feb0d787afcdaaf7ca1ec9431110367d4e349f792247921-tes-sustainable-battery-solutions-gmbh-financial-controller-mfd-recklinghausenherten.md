**About SK tes Sustainable Battery Solutions GmbH**

SK tes SBS GmbH is part of the international SK Group – a leading Korean corporation for sustainable development. With over 40 locations in more than 20 countries, we operate globally and promote the circular economy.

Our core business includes sustainable reuse and lifecycle management of IT hardware, complemented by innovative solutions for recycling lithium-ion batteries from electric vehicles. This makes an important contribution to resource conservation and reducing environmental impact.

We work for global automotive manufacturers, battery OEMs, and government organizations. For us, sustainability is not a buzzword but the foundation of our actions – in all areas of our company.

Tasks
-----

**Your Role**

Do you love numbers and want to actively shape the financial strategy? As a Financial Controller (m/f/d), you will be responsible for accurate financial statements, cash flow analyses, and the development of efficient financial processes.

**Your Responsibilities**

*   Preparation of monthly and annual financial statements for SBS GmbH and the Dutch subsidiary SBS BV
*   Ensure accurate financial accounting and compliance with all reporting requirements under IFRS and local standards
*   Preparation of regular cash flow analyses and forecasts
*   VAT reporting to tax authorities
*   Collaboration with external auditors and the corporate headquarters for audited financial statements
*   Execution of annual budget planning, financial analyses, and feasibility studies to support strategic decisions
*   Monitoring of corporate governance guidelines and financial processes

Requirements
------------

**Your Profile**

*   Degree in business administration with a focus on controlling/accounting or commercial training with further qualification as a controller (m/f/d)
*   At least 5 years of professional experience in controlling, including 3 years in a responsible position in an international industrial environment
*   In-depth knowledge of IFRS, local accounting principles, and audits
*   Extensive experience in German tax law and group reporting
*   Ideally a qualification such as ACA, CIMA, or ACCA
*   Fluent English skills, both written and spoken
*   A strong analytical and structured working style, combined with high initiative and team orientation
*   Empathetic communication skills to clearly convey ideas and build trusting relationships with stakeholders
*   Resilient and able to deliver excellent results even under time pressure in a dynamic environment
