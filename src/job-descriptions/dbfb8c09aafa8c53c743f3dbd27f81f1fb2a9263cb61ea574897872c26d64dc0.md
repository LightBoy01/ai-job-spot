**About the Role**

Join our Elite Transformation Product Team, led by the Global Mainframe Modernization Solutions Director, and work at the sharp edge of enterprise IT modernization.

In this role, you will not only deliver solutions but also develop new capabilities within our transformation engines, inspired by insights and patterns emerging from real-world migration projects. Your mission is to continually improve performance, automation, scalability, and accuracy — making our modernization tools faster, smarter, and more efficient.
