**Role overview:**
------------------

We are looking for a strategic and forward-thinking Client Solutions Partner with deep expertise in Telecom and Generative AI (GenAI). In this high-impact, client-facing role, you will act as a trusted advisor to leading telecom clients, helping them harness the transformative power of AI and GenAI to solve complex challenges, improve operations, and create new value. You will lead the design, sale, and delivery of tailored AI-driven solutions in close partnership with cross-functional teams.

**Key Responsibilities:**
-------------------------

*   Build and maintain strong, long-term relationships with executive stakeholders in telecom organizations.
    --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    
*   Serve as the primary point of contact for all AI / GenAI / Agentic AI initiatives, establishing yourself as a strategic advisor and innovation partner.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    
*   Guide clients through their AI transformation journey—from initial opportunity discovery to scaled implementation.
    ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    
*   Collaborate with clients to deeply understand their business goals and operational challenges in telecom.
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    
*   Design and propose custom AI/GenAI solutions that drive measurable outcomes—such as customer experience transformation, predictive analytics, or network optimization.
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    
*   Lead the full lifecycle of AI projects, including solution design, scoping, implementation, and post-deployment success measurement.
    ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    
*   Drive consultative sales efforts, identifying new opportunities and expanding AI/GenAI adoption within existing accounts.
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    
*   Work closely with internal engineering, product, and delivery teams to ensure alignment with client needs and solution feasibility.
    -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    
*   Stay ahead of industry trends and emerging technologies in AI, GenAI, Agentic AI, Machine Learning, and their application in the telco industry
    -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    
*   Track and report on solution performance, demonstrating ROI and business impact.
    --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    
*   Provide ongoing education and strategic support to ensure clients continue to realize value from AI investments.
    ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    
*   Advocate for client needs internally, ensuring feedback is heard and acted upon.
    --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
