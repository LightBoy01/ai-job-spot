Our platform is trusted by some of the world's most innovative companies, and we partner closely with customers to design, deploy, and scale AI solutions that deliver measurable impact.

If you thrive in an environment where innovation happens daily and impact is visible, Articul8 is the place to do the best work of your career.

**Role Overview**

We are seeking an **AI/ML Engineer** to design, develop, and deploy AI-driven solutions that solve real-world problems at scale and deliver direct impact for our customers. You will work with machine learning models, algorithms, large language models (LLMs), and AI applications while optimizing performance for production environments.

This role requires expertise in AI/ML modeling, including knowledge of various model types, layer architectures, and training strategies. You should have experience with cloud platforms and be comfortable following software engineering best practices. You will work directly with customers to understand their requirements while collaborating closely with our Customer Success team. You will also engage cross-functionally with Product, Engineering, and Applied Research teams to develop and deploy the best AI solutions.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Design, develop, and deploy AI and machine learning models for production environments.
    
*   **Train, fine-tune, and optimize deep learning models and interface with LLM-based solutions.**
    
*   Engage with customers to understand their needs and transform them into actionable tasks to develop functionality within our products.
    
*   Build algorithms and ML models to analyze diverse structured and unstructured data, including pattern recognition, anomaly detection, forecasting, and computer vision.
    
*   Collaborate with software engineers and the product team to integrate AI capabilities into our applications.
    
*   Develop APIs and microservices to serve AI models in production.
    
*   Stay current with the latest AI models, research, and best practices.
    
*   Ensure ethical AI practices, data privacy, and security compliance.
