### Description\nunivativ is a project and personnel service provider dedicated to connecting students and graduates with exciting roles at renowned companies, making your career development their mission. This specific opportunity is for a (Junior) Customer Advisor in Insurance & Claims Settlement, offering a diverse set of responsibilities. You'll be involved in reviewing insurance applications and claims, managing contracts, and communicating with customers, brokers, and insurers. The role emphasizes autonomous work, contributing to internal quality assurance, and bringing forward ideas for modern, digital processes, primarily conducted in German. This is an ideal chance to gain deep insights into the insurance sector, collaborate with various departments, and foster both your professional and personal growth.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Review incoming insurance applications, claims, and benefit cases, making autonomous decisions on acceptance or rejection.\n- Manage and maintain contract and customer data, ensuring systems are always up-to-date.\n- Communicate extensively, both in writing and by phone, with customers, brokers, and insurers regarding contract contents and claims.\n- Create policies, amendments, and certificates, always adhering to legal and internal guidelines.\n- Support internal quality assurance processes and actively contribute ideas for process optimization and digitalization.\n- Coordinate with adjacent departments like sales, legal, or experts for complex cases.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed commercial training, ideally with an insurance focus (e.g., Insurance and Finance Clerk).\n- Initial practical experience in the insurance or banking sector is an advantage.\n- Business-fluent German language skills at C1 level.\n- Strong service and customer orientation.\n- A diligent and structured way of working.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at univativ offers a fantastic entry point into the dynamic insurance sector, ideal for individuals with a foundational understanding of insurance processes and strong communication skills. The opportunity to work autonomously, contribute to process improvements, and interact with diverse stakeholders provides excellent professional development. With a competitive salary, the option for remote work, a personal career companion, and benefits like corporate shopping, this position clearly demonstrates a commitment to employee well-being and growth. It's a standout opportunity for anyone looking to build a career in a supportive and evolving environment.
