<p>As a Product Design Co-op you’ll dive into exciting, hands-on projects that directly shape the UX/UI of Klaviyo’s platform. You'll tackle meaningful challenges, see your ideas come to life, and contribute to something big. We're all about empowering you to explore, experiment, and even "fail fast" so you can iterate and launch amazing products. Plus, you'll build a strong UX/UI design foundation, connect with awesome people at Klaviyo, and have a blast while doing it.</p>
<p>As a Product Design Co-op, you'll jump right into a team, getting involved in every stage of product development. Imagine crafting stunning data visualizations and interactions that help our customers understand their business like never before, or collaborating with data scientists to build trust and drive adoption of cutting-edge AI features. You'll also sharpen your skills in design critiques with our expert team, constantly improving the overall user experience of our product. We're looking for driven individuals eager to deepen their expertise and make a real difference in the design process.</p>
<p><strong>How you will make an impact:&nbsp;</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Contribute to making Klaviyo’s data experiences delightful for our customers</li>
<li>Bridge the gap between data and customer action</li>
<li>Help our customers understand and optimize their businesses</li>
<li>Transform workflows by putting AI at the center, building smarter systems and ways of working from the ground up.</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Who you are:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Currently pursuing an undergraduate degree with a target graduation date between <strong>May 2027 and May 2028</strong> in Design, Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field.</li>
<li>Demonstrate a portfolio or class projects that showcase your talent in at least two of these areas: crafting compelling data visualizations, designing seamless interactions with delightful transitions and micro-interactions, building intuitive AI experiences, creating motion designs that explain complex ideas, or exploring the worlds of service or behavioral design.</li>
<li>Truly passionate about understanding users and transforming their needs into incredible product experiences.</li>
<li>Fluent in design and data prototyping tools like FigmaMake, V0, R, Python, Snowflake, and more.</li>
<li>Communicates brilliantly and thrives in a fast-paced, collaborative environment, delivering top-notch designs and impactful presentations.</li>
<li>You’ve already experimented with AI in work or personal projects, and you’re excited to dive in and learn fast. You’re hungry to responsibly explore new AI tools and workflows, finding ways to make your work smarter and more efficient.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Ability to work well across complex teams and feel comfortable working with a wide range of customers and stakeholders&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>We’d love to hear from you if you:&nbsp;</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Have taken courses on statistics, databases, or data structures</li>
</ul>
