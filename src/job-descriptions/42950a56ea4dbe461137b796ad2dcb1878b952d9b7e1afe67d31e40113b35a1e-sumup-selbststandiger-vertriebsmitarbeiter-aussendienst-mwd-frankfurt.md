### Description
Join SumUp as an Independent Field Sales Representative and play a crucial role in empowering small businesses. This is an exciting opportunity to earn attractive, uncapped commission by building strong relationships with local entrepreneurs, helping them discover the immense value of SumUp's innovative payment and POS solutions. As a solutions consultant, you'll leverage our comprehensive product suite to meet diverse business needs. We're actively building a highly motivated sales team and are looking for ambitious, passionate, and results-driven individuals with a proven track record. If you're dedicated to helping small businesses thrive, we need your expertise to achieve our mission.

### Responsibilities
As an Independent Field Sales Representative, you will:
*   Proactively identify and acquire new potential customers within your assigned territory, with a primary focus on small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).
*   Conduct engaging in-person meetings and deliver persuasive product presentations to effectively demonstrate the benefits of SumUp's innovative card readers and point-of-sale (POS) solutions.
*   Clearly articulate the value proposition and unique selling points of our products, skillfully addressing customer needs and overcoming objections.
*   Independently generate new leads and strategically nurture your customer base to ensure continuous growth and long-term relationships.

### Qualifications
We're looking for someone who brings:
*   Excellent verbal and written communication skills in German, along with good proficiency in English.
*   An empathetic approach that enables you to build and maintain trusting relationships with clients.
*   A consistent ability to meet and exceed sales targets, delivering outstanding results.
*   Exceptional communication and interpersonal skills essential for building rapport and fostering lasting client partnerships.
*   Solid understanding of the market landscape and a genuine passion for supporting local businesses.
*   Proven experience in outbound lead generation, cold calling, and effectively building sales pipelines.
