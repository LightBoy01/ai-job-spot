**Key Responsibilities:**
**Rapid Solution Building & Go-to-Market (GTM) Focus**
*   **Lead Rapid Deployment:** Own the speed-to-market for new solutions. Creatively use Firebase Studio (or similar rapid application development tools) to build and deploy functional MVPs in days to capture emerging business opportunities.
*   **Business-Driven Development:** Work directly with the Head of Product to translate business goals (new apps, new revenue streams) into tangible, live products, ensuring every solution has a clear path to immediate value.
*   **End-to-End Ownership:** Own the full lifecycle of prototypes, from ideation and design partnership to deployment and initial user testing.
**Technical Balance & AI Integration**
*   **Best Practice Integration:** While moving fast, intentionally maintain best practices for code structure, documentation, and API integration to ensure prototypes are easy to read and debug for future maintenance and scale.
*   **AI Value Creation:** Integrate relevant AI and Machine Learning (ML) services (e.g., Google's Vertex AI, Firebase ML Kit, or internal Carsome models) into the applications to create intelligent, data-driven solutions for workflow automation and operational efficiency.
*   **API Integration:** Work closely with the engineering teams to integrate prototypes with existing iCarAsia and Carsome platform APIs, ensuring robust data consumption and authentication.
**Collaboration & Iteration**
*   **Design Partnership:** Collaborate with designers to ensure MVPs are visually intuitive and adhere to a high standard of user experience (UX).
*   **Knowledge Transfer:** Systematically document and transfer successful MVPs and established best practices to the core engineering team for production hardening and scaling.
**Requirements:**
*   Minimum 3+ years of experience in software development, solutions engineering, or a related technical role.
*   Proven expertise in Firebase Studio (or strong equivalent experience with platforms like Retool, FlutterFlow, or similar low-code/no-code rapid development tools).
*   Solid understanding of AI/ML concepts and practical experience integrating cloud-based AI services into applications.
*   Proficiency in modern web technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) and experience working with RESTful APIs.
*   Experience in an Agile/Scrum environment, with a strong commitment to delivering iterative value.
*   Experience within the automotive, e-commerce, or classifieds industry.
*   Familiarity with the Google Cloud Platform (GCP) ecosystem.
*   Experience transitioning solutions from an MVP stage to a full-scale production environment.
