The Director Finance Transformation & Continuous Improvement is responsible for assisting Chief Tax Officer/VP (CTO) with launching, driving and maintaining Quest’s Lean Six Sigma projects across the entire finance organization. 

The Director reports to the Chief Tax Officer/Finance Continuous Improvement Leader and may be based as a hybrid worker (min 3 days/week) at any Quest U.S. office location.

### Responsibilities

*   Assist CTO with the development and implementation of the finance transformation strategy using a centralized transformation management office focused on the launch of Lean six sigma (“LSS”) mindset across Finance. 
*   Ensure LSS projects should be in alignment with the CFO’s, FLT’s and Quest’s overall objectives and goals. Ensure adoption of AI is part of overall strategy. 
*   Stay up to date on latest AI developments for finance. 
*   Coordinate with AI Finance Council and data management office to ensure all controls are in place to ensure data integrity. 
*   Conduct and drive workshops with key finance areas to further process improvement. 
*   Drive process improvement through follow up workshops and working sessions with various teams across finance. 
*   Conduct trainings on up to date LSS tools for Finance. 
*   Develop and manage tracking tool to capture savings and efficiencies. 
*   Assess current finance processes, systems, and controls to identify areas for enhancement and standardization.
*   Assist with design operating models across – people, process, organization, and systems, agree on the operating models and oversee their implementation.
*   Help CTO drive our multi-year transformation program roadmap that will enable the finance function develop into a best in class finance organziation.
*   Define data-driven frameworks and processes to identify, measure, and manage program success focusing on business value.
*   Deep dive into specific “hot topics” when required and articulate the issues in a succinct, data backed way, including offering options for solutions.
*   Partner with IT, RPA, Finance Technology Committee, AI Leaders  and other functional areas to integrate finance systems and technologies, ensuring seamless data flow and interoperability.
*   Draft use cases and project plans ensuring alignment with project timelines, budgets, and quality standards.
*   Develop and maintain KPIs for all projects. 
*   Report out to Finance Townhall and FLT quarterly

### Qualifications

*   Lean Six Sigma training and certification – Greenbelt preferred. 
*   Continuous improvement mindset – think outside the box.
*   Ability to drive change and best in class practices.
*   Strong collaboration and teambuilding skills. 
*   Willingness to learn new technology as it develops.
*   Bachelor’s degree in finance and/or technology.
*   Strong modeling, excel and powerpoint skills required.
*   Ability to motivate and lead teams in crisis to drive change.
*   Must be able to travel a couple times a month to various Quest locations to conduct LSS workshops.
