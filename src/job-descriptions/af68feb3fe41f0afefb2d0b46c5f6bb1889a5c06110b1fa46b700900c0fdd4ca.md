**About this opportunity - Background**
---------------------------------------

To build the driving automation features of the future, it is important to combine multiple sensor observations. However, it is not always trivial what is the most effective approach to combine different modalities. Therefore, the purpose of this thesis project is to explore the combined usage of different sensor observations (e.g., video, time-series data). The scope of the thesis can be adjusted to suit your master’s program and interests, and you will get to formulate the final research questions in cooperation with us. 

**Scope of the thesis work**
----------------------------

To motivate the choices of your research, we will start with a literature review to understand what methods and procedures have been applied in previous studies to extend or align different modalities (e.g., Imagebind or XFusion). After that, we will refine together what kind of experiments could be interesting on two open-source naturalistic driving datasets. An example approach could be to first evaluate pre-trained foundation models, followed by evaluating (self-supervised) modeling approaches from scratch, and lastly evaluating how to combine the various modalities most effectively for multiple downstream tasks. 

**What you'll bring**

You must be able to program in Python (PyTorch and Transformers), and experience in machine learning is desired. As you will become part of the analytics and artificial intelligence team within the research and development department of Volvo Cars, we expect you to work a few days per week at our office in Gothenburg.
