### Description
Lionflence is a dynamic and young company specializing in Online Marketing and Artist Management, with headquarters in Mülheim an der Ruhr and a Berlin office since August 2022. We empower companies with brand building and develop our own innovative software systems. As a \\\"Circle-of-Marketing,\\\" we offer clients a comprehensive 360° service package, extending beyond the conventional offerings of a marketing agency.

We pride ourselves on a culture of flat hierarchies and flexible structures, providing significant freedom for active participation and personal growth. We offer a responsible position within a highly motivated and high-performing team that values constructive feedback, open communication, and celebrating successes together. Enjoy a balanced work life with 30 days of vacation, supported by company phone and laptop. This is an exciting opportunity to actively shape the future of artist management within a forward-thinking environment.

### Responsibilities
As an Artist Manager, your key responsibilities will include:
- Acting as the primary point of contact for our internal talents.
- Supporting the search for new cooperation partners, creating new offers, and processing incoming inquiries.
- Demonstrating strong negotiation skills for contracts and pricing with providers, service providers, and cooperation partners.
- Collaborating with talents to develop innovative concepts and content formats, and assisting in their realization.
- Serving as the essential interface between talents and our partners, planning their engagements, and ensuring their well-being.
- Identifying and acquiring potential new talents for existing and future formats and broadcasts.
- Jointly working to enable talents to operate with a future-oriented mindset and create new opportunities beyond their immediate artist roles.

### Qualifications
To succeed in this role, you should bring:
- A minimum of 1-3 years of experience in Influencer Marketing or Online Marketing, ideally gained at a Digital, Social Media, or PR agency.
- A strong interest in and keen sense for the latest digital and consumer trends.
- Proficient handling of all common social media networks and online communities.
- Ideally, prior experience in the implementation and/or conceptualization of social media and influencer marketing campaigns.
- High levels of creativity and motivation.
- Fluent German and English language skills, both written and spoken.
