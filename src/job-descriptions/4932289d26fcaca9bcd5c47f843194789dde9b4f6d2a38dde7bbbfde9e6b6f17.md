**Location:** San Francisco, CA (Near Salesforce Tower) **Employment Type:** Full-time **On-site:** 5 days a week in-person

### About the Role

Our client is an AI startup building **foam**, a cutting-edge tool designed to be the highest-signal solution for solving software bugs. They've raised **$2.4M** from notable investors and are focused on making engineering jobs more engaging by automating the debugging process.

They are seeking a **Full Stack Product Engineer**—a generalist who is comfortable **wearing all the hats**. In addition to classical full-stack work (from CSS to the database level), you will be expected to increase the general intelligence of the platform by designing and implementing cutting-edge **AI product features**.

### What You'll Do

*   Serve as a **generalist** engineer, comfortable working across the entire stack.
*   Build **cutting-edge AI product features**, including writing advanced prompts, building custom Generative AI tools, and applying the latest AI techniques.
*   Work on core writing tools, system & user prompts, and general intelligence/denoising capabilities for the platform.
*   Stay up-to-date with the latest Gen AI techniques, tools, and research (blogs, videos) that can be immediately applied to the product.
*   **Work closely with users** (internal or external) to quickly iterate on features.

**Requirements**

*   **5+ years** as a full-stack engineer.
*   **Demonstrated interest in the AI space** and excitement about Generative AI.
*   Experience working closely with users (internal or external).
*   **Startup experience** is required.

### Nice-to-Have

*   Experience building code generation tools, AI agents, and production-level RAG systems _without_ using frameworks (e.g., LangChain).
*   Experience with the core tech stack: **React, AWS, MongoDB, TypeScript, NextJS, Vercel**.

**Benefits**

*   **Salary:** $140K - $190K
*   **Equity:** 0.25% - 2%
*   **Visa Sponsorship:** No H1B sponsorship; TN is acceptable but not preferred; valid work permits are acceptable.
*   **Benefits include:** 100% coverage for Medical, Dental, and Vision; 401K; Unlimited PTO; Pet insurance; and a Travel stipend.
