### Description\nAre you passionate about IT and digital media? Then a three-year apprenticeship as an IT Specialist for System Integration (m/w/d) could be perfect for you! Our IT department is responsible for the procurement, integration, and comprehensive support of information technology across our entire group of companies. This includes managing all IT systems, networks, and software, along with licensing and administration. You will gain hands-on experience in a dynamic environment, engaging in diverse and responsible tasks, including leading independent projects. The apprenticeship officially starts on **August 1st, 2026**.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Planning and implementing IT projects collaboratively within a team\n- Performing first-level support and conducting training sessions for employees\n- Installing and configuring new hardware and software components\n- Providing essential support to our IT team in various administration tasks\n- Managing system permissions and maintaining user accounts\n- Providing technical support for company-internal hardware and software (e.g., notebooks, tablets, mobile phones, printers)\n- Administering and documenting servers and workstations\n- Implementing and rigorously reviewing measures for IT security and data protection\n- Conducting error analysis and troubleshooting in daily operational activities\n\n### Qualifications\n- A genuine enthusiasm for IT and confident handling of digital media\n- A strong willingness to quickly learn new technologies and areas of responsibility\n- A precise, structured, and independent approach to tasks\n- Possess at least a secondary school leaving certificate (mittlere Reife)\n- Good academic grades in Mathematics and German\n- Excellent communication skills and team spirit\n- High levels of motivation, engagement, and a proactive attitude\n- Enjoy working effectively as part of a team\n\n### Author's Take\nThis apprenticeship offers an outstanding entry point into the IT profession, providing a robust foundation in system integration within a highly supportive and team-oriented environment. The company clearly prioritizes investing in its apprentices, evidenced by a commitment to offering practical experience through varied tasks and independent projects, coupled with extensive support for personal and professional development. This includes regular workshops, full coverage for examination preparation courses, and strong prospects for permanent employment post-apprenticeship. It is an ideal opportunity for motivated individuals eager to learn, grow, and become a valuable, long-term contributor to a dynamic IT team.
