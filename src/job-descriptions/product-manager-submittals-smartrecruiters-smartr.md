<strong>Job Description:</strong><p>We’re looking for a <strong>Product Manager</strong> to join Procore’s PM Core team. In this role, you’ll be working to develop and enhance our high-volume, business-critical Project Management tools, essential to the timely and successful delivery of construction projects worldwide. This role is at the intersection of core product strength and future innovation, demanding a leader who can balance the needs of a demanding user base with ambitious, long-term technical vision.</p><p>As a <strong>Product Manager</strong> on the PM Core team, you’ll partner with our Product Design, Engineering, and Revenue teams to manage the product roadmap, translate a bold vision for AI-driven automation into actionable features, and evangelize the value internally and with customers. Initially, you will be focused on executing a key strategic initiative to enable multi-customer collaboration via our Procore Connect platform. If you are excited about solving complex system-level problems and driving industry-defining innovation, come join us at Procore to be part of a team that is building transformative products for the construction industry.</p><p>This position reports into the Group Product Manager, Project Management Core Tools and will be based in our office in Austin, TX. We’re looking for someone to join us immediately</p><p><strong>What you’ll do:</strong></p><ul><li>Own the roadmap, prioritization, and execution for a high-volume workflow tool, expertly balancing the needs of our core market with investment in strategic growth initiatives.</li><li>Partner directly with existing construction customers to understand use case, pain points, and ‘quick wins’ to drive near, and long term adoption and value</li><li>Create compelling demos and partner with our go-to-market teams to position the product and support revenue impact across Procore</li><li>Become well-versed in the construction industry and its diverse challenges to guide and communicate the value you’re creating for customers</li><li>Balance customer empathy with horizontal solution thinking by identifying platform approaches that solve multiple problems at once</li><li>Effectively use qualitative and quantitative data analysis to help inform the decision-making process</li><li>Collaborate with teams across Procore to build collaboration solutions that support multiple use cases and workflow across the Procore platform</li></ul><p><strong>What we’re looking for:</strong></p><ul><li>Minimum 2+ years of experience building and delivering successful SaaS products in a Product Management or equivalent role.</li><li>Strong prioritization skills and the ability to clearly articulate and defend roadmap choices when managing competing requests from various internal teams and customers.</li><li>Experience or enthusiasm for collaborating with globally distributed teams and building alignment across different time zones using clear, effective communication.</li><li>Solid product fundamentals including definition of detailed user stories, managing functional requirements, and establishing measurable metrics for features after launch.</li><li>A passion for technology and automation, with a curiosity for how AI/Machine Learning can be applied to eliminate manual steps in enterprise workflows.</li><li>Experience working with Engineering and Design teams in an agile process to deliver end-user experiences.</li><li>Ability to work collaboratively across the organization with other product teams and stakeholders to collect feedback and influence</li><li>Bachelor's degree or equivalent work experience</li></ul><strong>Additional
