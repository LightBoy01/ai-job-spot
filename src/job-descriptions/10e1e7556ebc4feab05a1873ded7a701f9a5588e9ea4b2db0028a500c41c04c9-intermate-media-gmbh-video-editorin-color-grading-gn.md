### Description\nIntermate, one of the largest social media and influencer agencies in the DACH region, is seeking a skilled Colorist. With over 250 `Mates` across Berlin, Hamburg, Cologne, and remotely, we specialize in developing social-first strategies, producing high-quality content in our own studios, collaborating closely with creators, and managing data-driven campaigns using our proprietary technology. We partner with some of the world's most renowned brands, spanning lifestyle, FMCG, and tech sectors.\n\nAs part of SAMY, a global network of social media and creator marketing agencies, Intermate connects you with over 970 experts across 20 locations, offering international perspectives, projects in approximately 55 markets, and extensive opportunities for professional development beyond national borders.\n\nPlease note: Our application process is partly supported by an AI-powered assistant that conducts the initial introductory conversation. Further information will be provided during your application.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- You will be responsible for the color grading and editing of our video assets, primarily for brand channels.\n- You will edit videos in portrait format (e.g., TikTok, Instagram Reels) and, where applicable, also in landscape format.\n- You will enhance the visual quality of our PGC (Professional Generated Content) formats and ensure a consistent visual look.\n- You will develop and improve our grading workflows, especially for UGC (User-Generated Content) & CGC (Creator-Generated Content).\n- You will work closely with editors, videographers, and the creative team.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You have a completed degree or vocational training in graphic design, communication design, media design, or a comparable qualification.\n- You have at least 2 years of experience in the production, design, and editing of social media content.\n- You are a Colorist with proven experience in Adobe Premiere Pro & DaVinci Resolve.\n- You are skilled at telling stories through images, possessing a keen sense of tension and aesthetics.\n- You bring a strong understanding of visual language, storytelling, and current visual trends.\n- You are a Social Media Native with an excellent grasp of trends.\n- You are highly proficient with the Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Premiere Pro) – ideally also familiar with tools like Figma or Canva.\n- You work in a structured and self-reliant manner, comfortable navigating tools, processes, and methodologies.\n- You are capable of working precisely, reliably, and results-oriented under time pressure.\n- Very good German and English language skills, both spoken and written, complete your profile.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at Intermate, a prominent social media agency within the global SAMY network, presents an exceptional opportunity for a talented Mid-Senior Colorist to significantly shape the visual identity of top-tier brands. The company prides itself on a supportive `Mate` culture that champions professional growth through an annual development budget and international collaboration opportunities. Intermate also strongly prioritizes work-life balance, offering flexible working models, generous remote work options within the EU, and additional paid days off for personal milestones.\n\nBeyond competitive remuneration, the comprehensive benefits package is impressive, including premium Apple tech (MacBook, iPhone), extensive health and wellbeing initiatives (Urban Sports Club, nilo.health for mental support, also for family members), and customizable financial perks like a monthly Givve Card, mobility budget, or pension contributions.
