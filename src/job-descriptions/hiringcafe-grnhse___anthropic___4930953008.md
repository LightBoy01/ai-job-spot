**About Anthropic**
-------------------

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

**About the Role**
------------------

Join Anthropic's Legal Operations team as a dedicated tooling specialist, where you'll build the technical infrastructure that powers legal work at one of the world's leading AI companies. You'll work across the Legal org to create innovative automation solutions, integrate cutting-edge AI tools into legal workflows, and scale our operations to support Anthropic's rapid growth. This role offers the unique opportunity to shape the future of legal operations through technology while working with Claude and other business critical systems.

**Responsibilities:**
---------------------

*   Build and maintain integrations between core legal systems including Brightflag, DocuSign, JIRA, Zip, and Google Workspace
*   Develop and deploy AI-powered automation tools using Claude and Model Context Protocol (MCP) to streamline legal workflows
*   Support the ongoing development and expansion of of knowledge management system
*   Create self-service resources and FAQ systems by analyzing support ticket patterns and user needs
*   Partner with internal engineering and tooling teams on legal-specific automation projects and system requirements
*   Troubleshoot technical issues across legal platforms and coordinate resolution with IT and vendor support teams
*   Design and implement workflow automation solutions using tools like Tines and other no-code/low-code platforms

**You may be a good fit if you:**
---------------------------------

*   Have 5+ years of experience in legal operations, legal technology, or technical roles supporting legal teams
*   Demonstrate strong technical skills including API integration, system administration, and workflow automation
*   Show genuine curiosity about AI/ML applications and hands-on experience with LLM tools (especially Claude!)
*   Possess excellent problem-solving abilities and can translate legal business needs into scalable technical solutions
*   Have experience with legal technology platforms such as contract management systems, e-billing platforms, or document management tools
*   Excel at cross-functional collaboration and can communicate effectively with both technical and legal stakeholders
*   Are self-directed and can manage multiple technical projects simultaneously

**Strong candidates may have:**
-------------------------------

*   Previous experience implementing or administrating legal technology platforms
*   Tines workflow automation certification or equivalent no-code automation platform experience
*   Software development background with API development and system integration experience

**How we're different**
-----------------------

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
