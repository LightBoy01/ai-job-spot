### Description\nDigitale Leute is a leading platform for product management, UX design, and engineering, connecting specialists and leaders in the digital industry through conferences, bootcamps, and a vibrant community. We collaborate with esteemed partners like Airbnb, Porsche Digital, Atlassian, and Rewe Digital to shape the future of software development. Our Digitale Leute School offers practical further education for the tech industry, with a standout program being our Product Design Bootcamp. This intensive 4-month online program is an ideal opportunity for career starters, career changers, or juniors to enter the UX/UI Designer field. Our trainers are experienced professionals from top tech companies, providing you with up-to-date, practical knowledge. Participants will benefit from a compact, German-language online course (also possible alongside a job), a Product Designer / UX/UI Designer certificate, personal mentoring, close supervision, and the chance to work in simulated product teams, just like in real-world practice. Importantly, we actively support you in your job search through our strong hiring network after the bootcamp. For job seekers with an education voucher, participation can be completely free.\n\n### Responsibilities\nWithin the Product Design Bootcamp, you will immerse yourself in hands-on projects and gain practical experience in:\n- **User Research**: Understanding what users truly need to inform design decisions.\n- **Prototyping & Wireframing**: Making ideas visually and interactively tangible.\n- **Usability Testing**: Testing designs, gathering feedback, and iteratively improving solutions.\n- **Interface Design**: Crafting modern, user-friendly interfaces.\n- **Agile Methods**: Working effectively in teams using Scrum & Kanban methodologies.\n- **Project-based Learning**: Engaging in projects designed to simulate real product team environments.\n\n### Qualifications\nTo join our Product Design Bootcamp and embark on your journey as a UX/UI Designer, you should bring:\n- A genuine enthusiasm for design, technology, and digital products.\n- A team-oriented mindset, strong communication skills, and openness to feedback.\n- Good German language skills, as the course and projects are primarily conducted in German.\n- Whether you're a career changer, junior, or looking to switch fields, no prior design knowledge is required – just motivation and a strong willingness to learn.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis Product Design Bootcamp by Digitale Leute offers an exceptional launchpad for aspiring UX/UI Designers. What truly sets it apart is the strong emphasis on practical, project-based learning, guided by industry experts from leading tech companies. The curriculum is comprehensive, covering essential skills from user research to agile methods. Crucially, the commitment to active job search support through their extensive hiring network, coupled with personal mentoring, provides a significant advantage for participants looking to make a confident entry into the digital product world. It's an ideal program for motivated individuals, including career changers, who are ready to dedicate four months to acquiring highly sought-after skills and securing their first role in a dynamic field.
