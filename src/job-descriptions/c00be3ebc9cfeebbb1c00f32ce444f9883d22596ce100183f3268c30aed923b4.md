### Your role

Do you want to shape the future of HR through the power of AI? Do you want to build front-end experiences that make intelligent, people-centric technology possible?  
  
At UBS, we re-imagine the way we work, the way we connect with each other – our colleagues, clients and partners – and the way we deliver value. Being agile will make us more responsive, more adaptable, and ultimately more innovative.  
  
We’re looking for a Frontend Engineer to:  
  
• lead the design and development of intelligent, user-centric digital products and services that support our employees and HR stakeholders — with AI at the core  
• apply and promote modern engineering practices, from analyzing user needs and developing new features to automated testing, deployment, and observability  
• ensure the quality, security, reliability, and compliance of our solutions by applying UBS digital principles and implementing both functional and non-functional requirements  
• drive observability and operational excellence, proactively monitoring production health, resolving incidents, and addressing root causes of risks and issues  
• continuously upskill and explore new technologies and practices, reuse strategic platforms and standards, and make sustainable, forward-looking engineering decisions  
• share your knowledge openly, contribute to hiring and onboarding, and help shape a collaborative, forward-thinking engineering culture

### Your team

You’ll be part of the AI Engineering team within the GOTO Group HRCS Technology organization. Our mission is to simplify and digitize the HR application landscape while delivering AI-powered solutions that enhance the employee experience. Based in New York you’ll work in an agile, autonomous pod alongside product managers, designers, and fellow engineers, with a strong focus on collaboration, innovation, and continuous improvement

### Your expertise

• Bachelor’s degree in engineering or science or foreign equivalent  
• proven leadership in frontend engineering, with expertise in designing and scaling enterprise-grade web platforms using React, Next.js, and JavaScript/TypeScript  
• deep experience building scalable component libraries and UI frameworks aligned with design system guidelines (e.g., using Tailwind CSS, Storybook)  
• proficiency with GraphQL APIs and client libraries (e.g., Apollo Client)  
• practical experience with SSR/SSG/ISR in Next.js, including performance tuning  
• experience with frontend testing tools (e.g., Cypress, Playwright, React Testing Library)  
• skilled at leading frontend engineers through architecture reviews, code reviews, and technical mentoring, fostering a culture of clean, maintainable code.  
• a collaborative communicator — you express ideas clearly in English and adapt technical content for diverse audiences, while working closely with product managers, designers, and engineers in agile settings  
• hands-on experience with AI UI toolkits (e.g., Vercel AI SDK, CopilotKit, Assistant-UI) or frontend integration with LLMs is a strong plus
