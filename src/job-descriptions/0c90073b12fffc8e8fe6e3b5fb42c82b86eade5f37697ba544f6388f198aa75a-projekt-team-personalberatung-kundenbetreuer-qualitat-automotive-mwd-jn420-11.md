### Description\nOur client, a successful company in the automotive supplier industry, specializes in the production of plastic systems. As part of a replacement, they are looking for a **Quality Customer Advisor (m/f/d)** to start as soon as possible. The location is in the metropolitan region of Bremen-Oldenburg.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Ensuring quality requirements according to internal guidelines and customer specifications.\n*   Providing expert support to customers on quality-related topics.\n*   Planning and conducting product, process, and workstation audits.\n*   Analyzing quality problems and initiating and tracking corrective measures.\n*   Blocking and releasing products in case of deviations from specifications.\n*   Conducting special investigations for production problems.\n*   Collaborating on projects with customers and suppliers, especially during new product launches.\n*   Maintaining and further developing Production Control Plans and FMEAs.\n*   Training employees in quality requirements and management systems.\n*   Processing complaints and warranty claims, including damage part analysis.\n*   Monitoring defined quality key figures and targets.\n*   Close cooperation with internal departments for continuous improvement.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed business administration or technical degree (e.g., Industrial Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Quality Management, Production Technology).\n*   Relevant knowledge in quality assurance/quality management or corresponding further training is desirable.\n*   Initial professional experience in supplier management, including process audits.\n*   Proficient in quality techniques and quality planning methods.\n*   Knowledge of common standards ISO 9001, IATF 16949, and VDA 6.3.\n*   Good MS-Office and SAP skills.\n*   Very good German and English language skills, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a key role for a Quality Customer Advisor within a respected automotive supplier, offering a challenging environment for those passionate about maintaining high standards and fostering strong customer relationships. The company provides excellent opportunities for individual growth and development in a supportive culture that values open communication and employee well-being. If you have a solid background in quality management, especially within the automotive sector, and are ready to make a significant impact, this role offers a path for professional advancement.
