**Key Performance Area**

Key Responsibility
==================

Strategy, Planning and Delivery
===============================

*   Develop a holistic organic growth strategy that balances traditional search (Google, Bing, Baidu) with emerging AI-driven search experiences (AI Overviews, ChatGPT integrations, Perplexity, etc.).
*   Implement Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) strategies to ensure IDP and IELTS content is surfaced in AI-generated summaries and responses.
*   Align with business objectives to drive leads, ecommerce transactions, and visibility in both search engine results pages (SERPs) and AI-driven answer boxes.
*   Coordinate with developers, content creators, and marketing teams to deliver a future-proof SEO strategy.

Technical SEO Analysis and Remediation
======================================

*   Ensure all technical elements (crawlability, indexation, sitemaps, structured data, hreflang, redirects) are correctly implemented and maintained.
*   Implement advanced structured data (FAQ, How-to, Course, Event, etc.) to increase chances of inclusion in featured snippets and AI-generated results.
*   Conduct regular SEO + AI readiness audits, assessing how content is interpreted by both search engines and AI models.
*   Explore semantic search optimizations, entity-based SEO, and schema enhancements to align with Google’s Knowledge Graph and AI indexing models.
*   Track, report, and disseminate metrics, including traditional KPIs (rankings, CTR, organic traffic) and emerging ones (AI Overview mentions, conversational query presence).

On Page Optimization

*   Utilise the latest techniques to ensure a high ranking across pages including: 
    *   Site structure and architecture improvements
    *   internal linking optimizations
    *   Identify duplicate content issues
    *   Meta & Heading tags management
    *   Page speed & image optimization
    *   Use of heading, canonical, no-follow and hreflang tags
    *   Manage sitemaps 
    *   Structured data / rich & featured snippets implementation
    *   Language & location tags
    *   Content improvement recommendations based on competitor analysis
    *   Continuously optimize site architecture, internal linking, and semantic markup to strengthen authority and visibility in AI-augmented search.
    *   Ensure content is optimized for both SERPs and AI answer engines, focusing on clear, structured, and authoritative responses to user intent.
    *   Recommend content enhancements informed by LLM output analysis (e.g., identifying where AI surfaces competitor content instead of ours).
