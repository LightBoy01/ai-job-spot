### Description\nK-tronik is seeking a passionate Hardware Developer (m/w/x) specializing in Power Electronics to join their expert team, working with a client in the radio and communication industry. This role focuses on developing cutting-edge HF transformation networks, emphasizing teamwork and future-oriented technologies.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Set up, control, and support an HF measurement station for characterizing a power switch module with PIN diodes for a full-electronic and quasi-stepless HF transformation network for load matching in the HF range.\n- Support the further development of the switch board, as well as the design and commissioning of associated driver circuits.\n- Integrate into the device and carry out qualification and series introduction.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Degree in Electrical Engineering or a comparable field of study/training.\n- Experience in power electronics (especially switched-mode power supplies) and high-frequency technology.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a specialized Hardware Developer passionate about high-frequency power electronics. K-tronik offers a collaborative and supportive environment, prioritizing employee well-being with flexible working hours, generous vacation, regular team events, and extensive individual development opportunities. The role promises engaging work on innovative projects, making it ideal for those looking to advance their expertise in cutting-edge communication technology while enjoying a strong work-life balance and comprehensive support.
