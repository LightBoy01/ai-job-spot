### Description
Studio Schnauze is a dynamic, two-person team based in Düsseldorf, specializing in comprehensive brand and product development. Our diverse clientele ranges from innovative startups to established corporates and renowned brand agencies. We are actively seeking a motivated Working Student (Werkstudent/-in) to join our team at the beginning of 2026. This role offers an exceptional opportunity to gain deep insights into various design and client processes while contributing to a wide array of engaging projects.

### Responsibilities
- Develop creative design concepts that effectively solve our clients' challenges.
- Collaborate closely with the team to craft holistic and compelling brand experiences.
- Translate strategic concepts and systems into persuasive designs for multiple touchpoints, including both print and digital media.
- Quickly immerse yourself in new topics and create diverse design moods for brand systems, digital experiences, typography, imagery, logos, and color palettes.
- Independently plan and execute small sub-projects, taking ownership from conception to completion.

### Qualifications
- Currently enrolled in a degree program related to Communication Design, Graphic Design, or Design Strategy.
- Proficient in industry-standard design and wireframing tools, such as Figma and Adobe Creative Suite, with a keen interest in exploring and utilizing AI tools.
- Possess strong digital affinity, a passion for innovative interactions, compelling brand experiences, and emerging design trends.
- We are looking for a creative individual with an enthusiastic desire for design and a talent for generating ambitious, impactful ideas.

### Author's Take
This is an outstanding entry-level opportunity for an aspiring designer to gain invaluable hands-on experience within an agile and highly collaborative studio. The role promises direct involvement in diverse projects for a varied client base, offering comprehensive exposure to the entire design and client management process. The emphasis on creative problem-solving, independent project execution, and the flexibility of work location make this a truly attractive position for someone eager to make a tangible impact and accelerate their professional growth in brand and product development.
