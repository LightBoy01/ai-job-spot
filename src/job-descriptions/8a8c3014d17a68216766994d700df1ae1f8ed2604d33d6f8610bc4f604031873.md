What will I be bringing to life in this role?
---------------------------------------------

**Your Role:**   

Kinetic is seeking a Graduate AI Engineer with a T-shaped profile to join our Innovation Acceleration team, supporting our AI-led product development initiatives. We're looking for someone with deep expertise in a core AI area complemented by broader knowledge across related fields. This entry-level role offers an exceptional opportunity for a recent graduate to contribute to the integration of artificial intelligence capabilities across our student accommodation and event management platforms, while developing their skills under the mentorship of experienced AI professionals.   
  

As a Graduate AI Engineer, you'll work alongside our Senior Innovation Acceleration Engineer and cross-functional teams to help reimagine our products through innovative AI solutions. You'll assist in identifying opportunities, developing prototypes, and implementing AI features that enhance user experiences and unlock new value for our higher education customers. This role provides a unique chance to shape the future of our products while building a strong foundation for your career in AI engineering.  
**Career Development Pathway** 

This Graduate AI Engineer position is designed as a stepping stone for ambitious professionals seeking to build a career in AI within the SaaS industry. We offer: 

*   A structured development program tailored to your strengths and interests 
*   Opportunities to take on increasing responsibility as you grow 
*   Clear progression path toward more senior AI engineering roles 
*   Cross-functional exposure to build a well-rounded understanding of product development 
*   Support for continued education and professional certifications

We are a shining entity under the expansive umbrella of Constellation Software Inc., a global software powerhouse. Kinetic is a subsidiary of Volaris, which is a part of Constellation. **Responsibilities:** 

**AI Implementation Support**: Assist in the development and deployment of AI solutions within our products. Help implement prompt engineering techniques, test model performance, and contribute to AI feature development.   
  

**Prototype Development**: Build proof-of-concepts and prototypes that demonstrate the potential value of AI approaches to business challenges. Contribute to the evaluation of emerging AI technologies and tools.   
  

**Data Preparation & Analysis**: Support data collection, cleaning, and preparation for AI systems. Assist in analysing performance metrics and user feedback to improve AI features.   
  

**Cross-Team Collaboration**: Work with product managers, engineers, and professional services teams to understand requirements and ensure successful integration of AI components.   
  

**Research & Learning**: Stay current with the latest AI developments. Research potential applications relevant to our higher education customers and share findings with the team.   
  

**Documentation & Knowledge Sharing**: Help create documentation for AI systems and contribute to knowledge-sharing initiatives that build AI literacy across the organisation.   
  

You will collaborate with our established workstreams:   
  

*   **Innovation Acceleration team** - Working directly with the VP of Innovation Acceleration and IA Senior Engineer to develop and implement AI strategies across the business. 
*   **Product Management team** - Assisting with the identification of AI enhancement opportunities and translating customer needs into technical requirements. 
*   **Engineering teams** \- Supporting the integration of AI components with both established products and new development initiatives. 

What will I need to hit the ground running?
-------------------------------------------

**T-Shaped Profile** - We seek candidates with T-shaped skills: deep technical expertise in areas like AI orchestration or systems architecture (vertical bar), combined with broad cross-functional capabilities in customer understanding and business acumen (horizontal bar). This profile enables engineers to drive AI innovation through technical problem-solving while translating business requirements, communicating across departments, and maximising their organisational impact   
  

**Educational Background** - Recent graduate with a degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, or a related field. Demonstrated interest in AI through coursework, projects, or internships.   
  

**Programming Skills** - Proficiency in Python or similar languages. Experience with data processing, analysis, and visualisation. Basic understanding of software development principles.   
  

**AI Knowledge** - Foundational understanding of machine learning concepts, large language models, and AI application patterns. Familiarity with AI frameworks and libraries (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch, Hugging Face).   
  

**Problem-Solving** - Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving abilities. Capacity to approach business challenges with a creative, solution-oriented mindset.   
  

**Learning Agility** - Demonstrated ability to quickly acquire new skills and adapt to evolving technologies. Proactive approach to self-development and learning.   
  

**Communication** - Clear communication skills with the ability to articulate technical concepts to diverse audiences. Strong collaboration and teamwork orientation.   
  

**Project Work** - Evidence of successful completion of academic or personal projects involving AI, machine learning, or data science.   
  

**Desirable skills/experience:** 

*   Experience with cloud services (Azure, AWS, GCP) and their AI/ML offerings 
*   Knowledge of Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) or similar AI implementation approaches 
*   Familiarity with software-as-a-service (SaaS) business models 
*   Understanding of the higher education sector or student accommodation operations 
*   Experience with agile development methodologies 
*   Knowledge of AI ethics considerations and responsible AI implementation 
*   Contributions to open-source projects or AI research
