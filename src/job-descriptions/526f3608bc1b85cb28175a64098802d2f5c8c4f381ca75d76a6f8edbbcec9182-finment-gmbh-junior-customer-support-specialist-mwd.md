### Description\nFinMent is a rapidly growing, state-certified FinTech startup based in Berlin, dedicated to democratizing access to institutional investment knowledge and strategies. Through innovative technology, verified financial expertise, and exclusive strategies previously reserved for institutional investors, FinMent empowers individuals to achieve financial freedom. As a Customer Success Specialist, you will be a vital part of this mission, supporting customers and contributing to a dynamic, success-oriented environment where your personal and professional growth are highly valued.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Support customers with questions regarding the platform, processes, and trading procedures.\n*   Identify and relay customer feedback for enhancing the user experience.\n*   Maintain support documentation and create short help articles/FAQs.\n*   Collaborate closely with the Trading, Coaching, and Analysis teams to provide in-depth answers to customer questions and ensure consistently high service quality.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Possess 6 months to 1 year of practical experience in customer coaching and support, gained through training, internships, or similar activities.\n*   Demonstrate strong self-motivation and a passion for clean, agile process and project management.\n*   Proven ability to take responsibility for your subject area, even outside your comfort zone, tackling tasks proactively, result-oriented, and with swift execution.\n*   Eager to learn and develop, both professionally within the finance sector and personally, actively seeking feedback while keeping the team's success in mind.\n*   Possess very good analytical and structural skills to thoroughly think through scenarios beforehand and implement them systematically.\n*   Ideally, German at a \"native speaker\" level, and good English for conversations.\n*   Ideally, practical work experience with common financial and chart software (e.g., TWS, Tradingview).\n*   *Note: We understand that you may not always meet all requirements. Don't let that deter you.
