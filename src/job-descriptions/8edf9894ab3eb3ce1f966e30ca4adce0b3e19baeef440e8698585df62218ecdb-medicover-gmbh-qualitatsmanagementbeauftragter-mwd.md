### Description\nIHP Institut für Hämostaseologie und Pharmakologie MVZ GmbH is a highly specialized laboratory for human medicine based in Berlin. They offer comprehensive diagnostic and therapeutic analysis for blood coagulation, drug therapy monitoring, abuse assessment, and the analysis of vitamins, intermediates, and hormones to referring physicians.\n\nTo support their Quality Management team in Berlin-Steglitz, IHP is seeking a dedicated Quality Management Officer (m/f/d) to cover a parental leave period.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Maintain and further develop the implemented QM system according to DIN EN ISO 15189.\n- Collaborate professionally within a regional QM team.\n- Organize and accompany external audits.\n- Conduct and document internal audits.\n- Determine and evaluate quality key figures.\n- Review and monitor QM processes (e.g., risk and change management).\n- Provide training to staff.\n- Act as a contact person for all employees regarding QM questions.\n- Support QM projects.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed university degree or training in a medical-technical field.\n- Several years of experience in QM/QS (e.g., ISO, GMP, GCP, GCLP, GLP, accreditation).\n- Leadership experience is desirable.\n- Proven experience with internal and external audits.\n- Possess a structured and well-organized working style.\n- Very good German and good English language skills, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a pivotal role for an experienced Quality Management professional to ensure compliance and drive continuous improvement within a leading specialized medical laboratory. Even as a parental leave cover, the position offers significant responsibility and the chance to contribute to critical healthcare diagnostics. The company emphasizes a collaborative and supportive culture, offering intensive onboarding, attractive benefits including a competitive salary and 27 days of vacation, and a secure, modern workplace. It's an excellent opportunity for someone looking to apply their QM expertise in a dynamic and highly respected environment.
