We are looking for a seasoned technical leader to head our AI Centre of Excellence and elevate our automation capabilities to enterprise-grade standards. As the AI & Automation Solutions Manager, you will be responsible for driving engineering excellence, implementing scalable DevOps practices, and delivering robust AI/Automation solutions. This role blends hands-on technical contribution with strategic leadership, enabling the transformation of innovative prototypes into reliable, maintainable, and secure enterprise solutions.
**About the Role:**
*   Lead and mentor a team of AI/Automation developers, fostering a culture of collaboration, innovation, and continuous improvement.
*   Drive the adoption of software engineering best practices including version control, clean code principles, and CI/CD pipelines.
*   Design and manage multi-environment deployment strategies (Dev → UAT → QA → Prod) with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using tools like Terraform or CloudFormation.
*   Architect scalable, secure, and cost-effective cloud solutions on AWS and GCP, leveraging serverless technologies for flexibility and global reach.
*   Establish robust logging, monitoring, and observability frameworks to support production-grade AI systems.
*   Create reusable components, shared workflows, and configuration management practices to accelerate development and reduce technical debt.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams including business stakeholders, security, compliance, and governance to ensure alignment and enterprise readiness.
*   Translate business requirements into technical specifications and oversee solution design, development, and deployment.
*   Actively contribute to development efforts, troubleshoot complex issues, and prototype emerging technologies to assess fit and value.
**About You:**
*   **Experience**: 8+ years in software development; 3+ years in engineering management; 5+ years in cloud platforms (AWS/GCP); 3+ years in DevOps.
*   **Technical Skills**: Proficient in Python, Java; familiar with AI/ML tools (TensorFlow, PyTorch, Hugging Face); experience with UiPath, Langraph, AWS Agentcore, Google Agentspace, Crew.ai.
*   **Leadership**: Proven ability to mentor teams, implement engineering standards, and drive productivity.
*   **Process Expertise**: Skilled in CI/CD, automated testing, multi-environment deployments, and observability.
*   **Domain Knowledge**: Understanding of AI/ML lifecycle, automation governance, and enterprise software delivery.
*   **Preferred**: Bachelor’s in computer science or related field; AWS/GCP certifications; experience with monitoring tools like DataDog; knowledge of identity and access management systems.
