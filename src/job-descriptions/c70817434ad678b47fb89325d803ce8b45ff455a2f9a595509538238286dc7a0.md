**Welcome to Maastricht University!
**At Maastricht University Brightlands Campus Greenport Venlo, we are looking for two enthusiastic postdoctoral researchers to expand and strengthen research on “Personalised healthy diets through data sciences and artificial intelligence”. Are you excited about the opportunities that data science in nutrition—especially the role of targeted metabolic modelling, machine learning and AI offer in developing effective personalized nutrition interventions? Are you interested in deepening yourself in dietary patterns uncovering what works, for whom, and under which circumstances by analyzing diverse data streams? Do you want to contribute to the development of innovative smart wearables and apps in personalised nutrition and adapting lifestyle advices through dynamic data collection of these smart devices? And does working in an interdisciplinary team focused on healthy and sustainable nutrition within the context of the whole food chain appeal to you? Then these positions at Maastricht University Brightlands Campus Greenport Venlo might be just for you!
We offer challenging Post-doc positions and we ask for highly motivated candidates with a strong background, interest, and affinity for academic research in the area of nutrition and data science and health to further strengthen our research group.
**Post-docs on data science in personalized nutrition**
*   **Our goal:** To develop personalized healthy diets through data science and AI – advancing health promotion while keeping sustainability at the heart of nutrition research.
*   **Your colleagues:** You will be based at Brightlands Campus Greenport Venlo (BCGV) and collaborate closely with Maastricht University and NUTRIM, School of Nutrition and Translational Research in Metabolism. You will join an international, interdisciplinary team that bridges nutrition, health sciences, data science, and sustainable food systems.
**What you do
**For our research line Sustainable Nutrition we are looking for **post-doc** candidates for 4 y positions. In these roles, you will work at Brightlands Campus Greenport Venlo (BCGV). There will be a close collaboration with Maastricht University, NUTRIM, Institute of Nutrition and Translational Research in Metabolism.
For one post-doc with a strong **background in nutrition**, we ask:
*   Strong knowledge in nutrition and metabolism
*   Holistic view on health and sustainability aspects of nutrition
*   Interest in personalised nutrition, including metabolic, socioeconomic and psycho-social determinants of efficacy and adherence to dietary intervention
*   Interest in or affinity with data sciences
For one post-doc with a strong **background in data sciences** we ask:
*   Insights in the most suitable data science techniques (e.g., machine learning, cluster analysis) to answer specific research questions based on available data as a basis for the execution of the dietary intervention trials
*   Ability to apply machine learning, statistical techniques, and AI to analyze data, predict response to diet, and identify signatures determining response to diet.
*   A strong foundation or affinity with statistical modeling, with the ability to translate complex data into actionable insights

**Brightlands Campus Greenport Venlo**
**_Current discussions about food are dominated by healthy and safe food, future farming and the bio-circular economy_****.**
Innovate entrepreneurs, large enterprises, start-ups, scientists and students work together at Brightlands Campus Greenport on innovations for healthy and safe food, the farming of the future and the bio-circular economy. There are also many crossovers with the manufacturing industry, information and communication technology, logistics and the other three Brightlands campuses.
In a chain of laboratories that have been specially constructed to mimic and analyse each step, the journey of food through the body is monitored to determine the impact of food and products on health. This ranges from studies exploring the ways in which we experience taste, scent, or texture to simulations of metabolic processes.
Innovations in the future farming and the bio-circular economy are resulting in higher nutritional values and yields. This means that healthier and safer products can be produced and that we can get the most out of our food.
For further information about Brightlands and the facilities on the campus visit: [https://www.brightlands.com/en/brightlands-campus-greenport-venlo/facilities-agri-food-innovation](https://www.brightlands.com/en/brightlands-campus-greenport-venlo/facilities-agri-food-innovation)
