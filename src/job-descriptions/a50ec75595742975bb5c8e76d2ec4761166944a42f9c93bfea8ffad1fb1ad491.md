Description:

We are seeking a talented **AI Adoption Specialist Developer** to join us in **Cluj-Napoca, Romania** at our EMEA Technology Hub. This is a hybrid role that has a requirement of working at least three days a week in the office. The Technology Readiness and Change Enablement Centre of Expertise (COE) team plays a crucial role in facilitating the adoption of IT technology, including Cloud, Generative AI, and engineering excellence, through engaging design and implementation.
The **AI Adoption Specialist** will develop and deliver customized training programs, create marketing strategies to promote AI adoption, and lead virtual and in-person training sessions. Additionally, you will be responsible for creating engaging and effective content such as e-learnings and videos. This is a great opportunity to empower teams across our organization. The role reports to the AI Adoption Lead within the Technology Readiness and Change Enablement COE.
Marsh McLennan (MMC) has established its EMEA Technology Hub in Cluj, Romania, reflecting our commitment to innovation and excellence. This hub is central to our digital strategy, addressing emerging challenges and enhancing service delivery through advanced technology solutions that empower our clients and promote sustainable growth.
**We will count on you to**
*   Manage internal stakeholders and in a global and cross-cultural setting.
*   Develops and maintains core curricula and customized training, by partnering with internal subject matter experts to identify business goals and target audience’s learning needs, and/or analyzing existing training material.
*   Create compelling marketing materials and campaigns, primarily videos and engaging posts, to promote AI initiatives and increase adoption across the organization.
*   Lead both virtual and in-person training sessions, workshops, and demonstrations to educate employees on GenAI capabilities and best practices.
*   Monitor and report on the effectiveness of training programs and AI adoption metrics.
*   Co-manage MMC Tech Learning Portal, the training delivery platform, and leverage learning data extracted from Workday, MMC Learning Management System (LMS), for training efficacy measurement
**What you need to have**
*   Excellent communication both in English and Romanian
*   Bachelor’s degree with 5+ years of working experience in corporate training.
*   Strong stakeholder management and learning project management experience.
*   Proven working experience in training design, technology, implementation, and measurement.
*   Highly proficient with tools such as Adobe Creative Cloud Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, etc.) and Articulate for creating engaging content.
*   Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to engage diverse audiences.
*   A lifelong learner with an appetite for deepening your information technology knowledge, introducing change, and a team player.
*   Technical proficiency with AI tools, platforms, and related software is a plus
**What Makes You Stand Out**
*   Portfolio demonstrating Articulate 360 and Adobe CC work for video animation and skill assessments.
