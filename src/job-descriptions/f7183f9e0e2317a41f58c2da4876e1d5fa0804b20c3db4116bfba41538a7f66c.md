**Job Description:**

**WHAT YOU’LL DO​** 

The **Junior Project Manager for** Tech will lead the planning, execution, and delivery of high-impact projects involving next-generation technologies. You will work closely with engineering, product, and business teams to ensure the successful delivery of projects, managing resources, timelines, and stakeholder expectations. This role demands a strong blend of technical acumen and project management expertise to guide complex initiatives from inception through to successful completion. 

In the role you are responsible for making things happen - by making the complex easier and bringing people together to create success. You focus on value creation and team empowerment to accelerate on the journey towards common strategic goals and exceptional customer/business results. You create joint plans, drive excellent execution and follow-up, always in tight collaboration with key stakeholders and contributors. 

As a Junior Project you are typically leading a workstream in a project. You are familiar with the Project Management proficiency. You participate in the Project/Program Management Community. 

**Key Responsibilities**

**Planning & definition:**  

*   Scope a defined workstream within a project and set objectives. 
*   Create timeline for the workstream. 
*   Identify risks and develop mitigation strategies. 
*   Define project/program objectives, scope, and deliverables in alignment with the organization’s goals. 
*   Develop detailed project/program roadmaps, timelines, and resource plans. 
*   Identify dependencies, risks, and issues, and develop mitigation strategies. 

**Stakeholder & change management:**  

*   Act as the primary point of contact between senior leadership, sponsors, and project teams. Identify relevant stakeholders and establish a trustworthy relationship. 
*   Communicate project/program status, risks, and outcomes to stakeholders through regular reporting. Coordinate with internal and external stakeholders, including developers, designers, product managers, and senior leadership, to ensure smooth communication and execution. 
*   Manage stakeholder expectations and ensure alignment with program goals. 

**Execution:** 

*   Oversee multiple workstreams/projects under the project/program, ensuring each aligns with the overall objectives. 
*   Drive adoption of agile methodologies and best practices for project execution, continuously improving project delivery processes. 
*   Facilitate sprint planning, daily stand-ups, retrospectives, and other Agile ceremonies in collaboration with engineering teams. 
*   Ensure alignment with compliance, security, and quality standards throughout the project lifecycle. 
*   Work closely with workstream leads/project leads to ensure successful execution and delivery. 
*   Assess, communicate and manage changes that may occur. 

**Functional competence:**  

*   Overall knowledge of fashion retail business process and business models. 
*   Experience in our project management practices and tool (eg Jira, Anaplan, Miro…) 
*   Champion innovation and emerging trends, keeping up to date with industry advancements and recommending new tools or approaches that enhance project success
