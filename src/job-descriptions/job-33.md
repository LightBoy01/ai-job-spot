# Data Scientist (AI/ML focus)

### Description

Deloitte US is seeking a talented Data Scientist with a strong AI/ML focus to join our consulting team. In this role, you will leverage cutting-edge AI and Machine Learning techniques to solve complex business problems for a diverse range of clients across various industries. You will be responsible for developing and deploying data-driven solutions, providing strategic insights, and contributing to the growth of our AI/ML practice.

### Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and implement AI/ML models and algorithms to address client needs.
- Analyze large, complex datasets to extract actionable insights and identify trends.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including business analysts, engineers, and client stakeholders.
- Communicate complex analytical findings and recommendations clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.
- Stay abreast of the latest advancements in AI/ML technologies and apply them to real-world client challenges.

### Qualifications

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, or a related quantitative field.
- Proven experience in data science, with a strong focus on AI/ML applications.
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Python or R, and experience with relevant libraries (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn).
- Strong understanding of machine learning algorithms, statistical modeling, and data visualization techniques.
- Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and communication skills.
- Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, client-facing consulting environment.
