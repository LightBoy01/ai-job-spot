Help us scale Cherry Ventures and our portfolio by finding and hiring the best talent across Europe.

Cherry Ventures is a leading early-stage venture capital firm with offices in Berlin, London, and Stockholm. We back bold entrepreneurs from day zero and actively support them in building world-class teams. To strengthen our own operations and better serve our founders, we are looking for a Talent Acquisition Specialist to join our Berlin office.

This is a hands-on role for someone who enjoys end-to-end recruiting and is happy to take on coordination responsibilities when needed. Working closely with our Executive Director of Talent, you will play a key role in helping our portfolio companies build the best teams across Europe while ensuring a seamless candidate experience.

### **What you’ll do**

*   Drive full-cycle recruitment for roles at Cherry and across our portfolio – from sourcing and outreach to interviews, offers, and closing.  
      
    
*   Source top talent proactively through LinkedIn, networks, and market research, with a focus on senior and hard-to-fill roles.  
      
    
*   Support portfolio founders in building high-performing teams by driving sourcing and recruiting efforts for critical hires.  
      
    
*   Leverage AI and automation tools to boost sourcing efficiency, candidate engagement, and pipeline management.  
      
    
*   Manage coordination tasks such as scheduling, candidate communication, and process tracking to ensure a seamless experience for candidates and hiring teams.  
      
    
*   Contribute to talent projects such as building sourcing playbooks, improving candidate experience, and leveraging data to refine our approach.  
      
    

### **What we’re looking for**

*   3 - 4 years of experience in sourcing, recruiting, or talent acquisition (agency, in-house, or startup background welcome).  
      
    
*   Proven ability to run searches end-to-end while also rolling up your sleeves on coordination tasks.  
      
    
*   Strong LinkedIn and sourcing skills; comfortable with direct outreach and talent mapping.  
      
    
*   Mastery of AI recruitment tools is a must - you know how to harness them to supercharge sourcing and hiring outcomes.  
      
    
*   High proficiency in Greenhouse ATS (or equivalent systems) - you can manage pipelines and processes with ease.  
      
    
*   Experience hiring for startups or VC-backed companies - you understand the pace, ambiguity, and profile of talent these environments demand.  
      
    
*   Very technical role expertise highly appreciated - you can confidently approach and engage engineering, product, and data candidates.  
      
    
*   Excellent organization and communication skills; you’re structured and detail-oriented.  
      
    
*   A collaborative mindset and the ability to build trust with candidates and colleagues.  
      
    
*   International outlook and comfort working in a diverse, fast-paced environment.  
      
    

### **What we offer**

*   A front-row seat to the European startup and VC ecosystem.  
      
    
*   The chance to shape talent operations at Cherry and directly support ambitious founders in building their teams.  
      
    
*   Competitive compensation and benefits.  
      
    
*   A highly international, entrepreneurial, and collaborative team culture.  
      
    
*   An on-site role in our Berlin office.  
      
    

Cherry Ventures is an early-stage VC firm with offices in Berlin, London, and Stockholm. We are a team of former founders and operators, investing across Europe and committed to being the active ingredient behind founders’ growth. We’ve backed more than 120 companies across Europe - including Flix, Auto1, Amboss, Forto, Numa, Moss, and Tacto - and support them not just with capital, but with recruiting, coaching, and operational resources. In 2025, we raised a $500M fund, split between early-stage investments and follow-on capital.
