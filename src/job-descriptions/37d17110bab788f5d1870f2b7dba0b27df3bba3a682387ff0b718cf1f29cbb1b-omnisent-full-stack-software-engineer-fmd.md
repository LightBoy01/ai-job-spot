**Founded in late 2024, Omnisent is a Munich-based acoustic AI company developing next-generation sensing systems that turn sound into real-time intelligence. The team combines deep expertise in physics, machine learning, and mechanical engineering — united by a mission to unlock the untapped intelligence of the acoustic world.**

At the core of Omnisent’s innovation are ultra-low-power sonic devices and the company’s Large Acoustic Model (LAM) — a foundation model trained on the complex world of non-speech audio. This unique combination enables the real-time detection, classification, and localization of acoustic events — whether identifying drones in contested airspace or tracing air leaks in manufacturing systems.

Tasks
-----

*   **Build beautiful, intuitive web applications and dashboards** that transform complex sensor networks into actionable insights.
*   **Develop backend services and APIs** for device provisioning, fleet management, OTA updates, and secure data access.
*   **Collaborate with external UI/UX and engineering partners** to translate concepts into production-ready systems.
*   **Implement secure user provisioning and access control**, including encryption and role-based permissions.
*   **Architect and operate cloud infrastructure** powering Omnisent’s platform, including storage, APIs, authentication, and observability.
*   **Develop internal tools** for test automation, debugging, and quality workflows.
*   **Work closely with embedded, RF, and AI teams** to deliver seamless device–cloud–user integrations.
*   **Contribute to CI/CD pipelines** and maintain high reliability across production environments.
