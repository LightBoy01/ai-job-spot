Your tasks
----------

As KoRo grows and all teams depend on reliable and secure IT, we’re looking for a hands-on IT & Security Operations Specialist to help us keep our environment smooth, secure, and well-organised. You will work closely with our external partners and internal teams, take ownership of day-to-day IT operations, and support our company-wide NIS2 readiness.

*   Provide day-to-day IT support for our hybrid workforce (remote + office), solving issues quickly and with a service mindset
*   Support user lifecycle processes (onboarding, offboarding, access changes) together with P&C
*   Administer our core systems (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, MS Dynamics, selected SaaS tools)
*   Support operations of MDM (with our external partner), device policies, and hardware lifecycle management
*   Maintain and improve our security fundamentals: SSO usage, MFA enforcement, access reviews, documentation, and basic monitoring
*   Support internal teams with tool access, routing requests to the right owners where needed
*   Assist in NIS2 tasks: evidence collection, asset lists, documentation updates, and keeping controls running
*   Keep our office IT environment healthy (network basics, meeting rooms, printers, Wi-Fi, etc.)
*   Keep track of SaaS tools, licenses, and inventories, ensuring minimum security requirements are met
*   Help evolve our processes and documentation as we grow (no enterprise stuff, just simple, clear processes)

Your profile
------------

*   1–3 years experience in IT support, IT operations, or similar hands-on technical role
*   Comfortable working with Google Workspace and/or Microsoft 365 (no need to be a deep expert)
*   Basic understanding of networks, security hygiene, and device management
*   Interested in security and happy to learn about NIS2, SSO, IAM, and compliance basics
*   Good communicator: able to work with P&C, Office Management and all teams in a friendly, helpful way
*   Structured, reliable, and detail-oriented: you like clean processes and checklists
*   Pragmatic mindset: you understand we’re a lean company and prefer simple, effective solutions over heavy enterprise tooling
*   English required, German a plus

What you can expect from us
---------------------------

*   Our diverse team is ready to be enriched and inspired by your ideas: help building the future of food retailing in our fast-growing start-up!
*   Extensive onboardings which will prepare you for your various and challenging tasks
*   Flexible working hours and home office options to ensure a good work-life balance
*   A beautiful and modern office in Berlin Schöneberg, easy to reach by public transport – and we cover the costs of your monthly BVG (public transport Berlin) ticket
*   Find your way (back) to the gym thanks to fitness cooperations with Urban Sports Club or FitX
*   Optimal performance thanks to a Windows laptop or a MacBook.
*   Well equipped office kitchen with fresh fruit, delicious KoRo snacks, coffee and tea
*   20 % discount in our KoRo online store
*   Regular team events and joint activities as well as legendary company parties
