We are looking for a Senior Manager of Marketing Content to join our marketing team. This person will be responsible for developing and executing a comprehensive content strategy that directly supports our go-to-market initiatives. This leader will manage and inspire a team of content creators, ensuring the production of high-quality, impactful content across a variety of formats, including but not limited to: emails, webinars, blogs, articles, events, research papers, podcasts, and more. The ideal candidate possesses a deep understanding of the buyer's journey, a proven track record in B2B content marketing, exceptional leadership and project management skills, and has a deep passion for innovating the content experience, particularly how AI can power the entire content creation and distribution process. 
=================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================================

Your impact
===========

Driving Growth Through Content Strategy:
========================================

*   Develop and refine the content marketing strategy in alignment with overall company objectives and go-to-market plans to drive customer acquisition and customer growth outcomes.
    
*   Oversee the content calendar for a wide range of content types, and manage the content production workflow, from ideation and assignment to editing, approval, and publication, ensuring timely delivery and alignment with strategic priorities.
    
*   Manage the content marketing budget effectively.
    

Cultivating Talent & Collaboration:

*   Lead, coach, and develop a team of content marketing experts, content writers, and copywriters, fostering a collaborative and high-performing environment.
    
*   Collaborate closely with other teams across the organization - product marketing, SMEs, communications/advocacy, and customer and acquisition campaigns--to ensure content effectively supports all key initiatives. 
    

Innovating Content for the Future:

*   Identify opportunities for new and innovative content formats to reach and engage target audiences, including how AI is changing the content experience.
    
*   Track, analyze, and report on content performance metrics, using data to inform future content strategy and optimization.
    
*   Stay informed of industry and competitive trends in content marketing, go-to-market strategies, and relevant content formats.
    

Qualifications
==============

*   At least 5 years of experience in content marketing, with at least 2 years of experience in a leadership or management role.
    
*   Proven experience developing and executing content strategies that support go-to-market initiatives.
    
*   Demonstrated ability to manage and mentor a team.
    
*   Strong understanding of SEO principles and content optimization best practices.
    
*   Excellent writing, editing, and proofreading skills.
    
*   Exceptional project management and organizational skills.
    
*   Strong ability to interpret data to measure content performance.
    
*   Effective communication and interpersonal skills.

*   Shape the content strategy and directly impact company revenue and growth.
    
*   Collaborate with a team of passionate marketers, creatives, and industry leaders, driving innovation and powering the future of real estate.
