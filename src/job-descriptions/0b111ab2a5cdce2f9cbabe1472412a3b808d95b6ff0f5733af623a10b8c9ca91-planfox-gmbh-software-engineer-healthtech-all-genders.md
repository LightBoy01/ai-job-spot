### Description\nAt PLANFOX, we are shaping the digital future of healthcare. We develop smart and networked software solutions that simplify processes, improve information quality, and create more time for what matters – people. Originating from a research collaboration with the University of Augsburg and rooted in the XITASO Group, we combine scientific expertise with technological know-how and a passion for digital health. Join our mission to revolutionize healthcare with user-friendly and practical software.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Collaborate with the team on the full-stack development of our applications using React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL.\n- Take responsibility for the implementation within a completely TypeScript-based codebase.\n- Actively contribute as a member of a small, agile team without rigid roles, shaping our product landscape from the initial idea to successful implementation.\n- Drive the digitalization of healthcare forward with user-friendly and practical software.\n- Develop robust and secure software architectures that meet the highest requirements for data protection and information security when handling sensitive health data.\n- Work in a German-speaking and distributed team.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Possess significant development experience in agile teams and confidently apply it to our product development.\n- Enjoy delving deeply into new technologies and complex technical issues.\n- Have a passion for type-safe programming in a functional style.\n- Bring knowledge of FHIR or other health-tech interfaces and can apply this expertise effectively.\n- Think beyond the code, considering aspects such as security, interface design, infrastructure, and monitoring as integral parts of your work.\n- Approach tasks in a solution- and results-oriented manner, always keeping the big picture in mind.\n- Feel comfortable in a fully remote work environment and work independently and reliably.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a compelling opportunity for a Senior Full-Stack Developer passionate about making a tangible impact in healthcare. PLANFOX offers a unique blend of a supportive startup culture, significant technical challenges in a highly regulated domain, and genuine work-life balance with a fully remote setup.
