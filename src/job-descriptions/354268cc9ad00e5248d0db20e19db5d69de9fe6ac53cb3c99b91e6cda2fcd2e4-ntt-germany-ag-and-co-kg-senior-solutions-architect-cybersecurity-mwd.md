### Description
NTT DATA Inc. is a globally leading company for IT solutions and services, bringing together people, data, and things to create a better and more sustainable future.

**Ready for the next career step?**

**Cybersecurity reimagined – with Cisco and NTT DATA Inc.**

We are further expanding our Security Practice in close partnership with Cisco and are looking for you: an experienced **Senior Solutions Architect - Cybersecurity (m/w/d)**, who develops and positions security architectures that make our customers more secure, productive, and innovative.

### Responsibilities
**Your Role:**
You act as a Trusted Advisor and translate our customers' business requirements into tailored security solutions – based on the Cisco Security portfolio (Secure Access / ZTNA, Duo, Secure Firewall, XDR/Splunk, Hypershield, SSE/SASE) and NTT DATA's services. Together with Sales, Consulting, and Delivery teams, you demonstrate how security creates real business impact.

**What you will do:**
*   Develop security architectures in alignment with business, regulatory, and compliance requirements.
*   Support presales activities: workshops, proposals, solution design, and bid support.
*   Position Cisco Security solutions with clear business value.
*   Conduct risk analyses, threat modeling, and compliance reviews.
*   Work closely with Cisco and internal teams.

### Qualifications
**What you bring:**
*   At least 7 years of experience in information security and security architecture, including presales.
*   Profound knowledge of Cisco Security technologies and Cloud Security.
*   The ability to convince both technical contacts and C-level decision-makers.
*   **Nice to have:** Certifications such as CISSP, CISM, or CRISC, as well as Cisco certifications like CCNP Security or Cisco Security Black Belt, are advantageous.
*   **Must have:** Very good German and English language skills. Residence in Germany.
