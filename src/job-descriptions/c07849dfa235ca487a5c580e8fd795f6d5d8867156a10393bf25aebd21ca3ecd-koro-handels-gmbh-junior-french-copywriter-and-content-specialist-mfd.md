Your tasks
----------

Do you enjoy working with language, and playing with words in a creative and inspiring way? Your passion for creative writing is matched only by your passion for food? Are you the kind of person who loves researching and learning and is eager to shape the voice of a growing brand? Well then, welcome to the KoRo team!

We are looking for a dedicated wordsmith & storyteller who can help us bring our brand personality to life in the French market. In this junior role, you will be responsible for copywriting, localization, SEO and AI content creation & optimization, ensuring our brand voice is engaging, consistent, and tailored to the French audience. You’ll work across multiple channels - from website to marketing campaigns and product descriptions - playing a key role in enhancing our brand visibility and search engine performance. Your responsibilities will include:

Copywriting & Content Creation
------------------------------

*   Develop creative, conversion-driven copy for multi-channel campaigns (ads, web banners, headlines, landing pages)
*   Craft compelling customer retention emails, both campaign-based and automated flows
*   Support in the development of performance marketing and OOH campaigns

SEO/AI Content Creation & Optimization
--------------------------------------

*   Research and write SEO and AI-optimized content, including product descriptions and blog articles
*   Conduct keyword research and integrate high-performing keywords into content
*   Optimize existing content to boost organic rankings and engagement
*   Collaborate with the SEO/GEO team to analyze key performance metrics and implement improvements

Localisation & Translation
--------------------------

*   Adapt and localise product descriptions, blog posts, and marketing materials for the French market
*   Implementation of content and product updates
*   Support with cross-functional translation projects across departments

Brand Voice & Consistency
-------------------------

*   Ensure consistent brand messaging and a native feel in all French-language content across all touchpoints, from website and marketing campaigns to offline materials
*   Shape and refine the brand voice to resonate with the French audience

Your profile
------------

*   You bring **1-3 years of experience** in copywriting or content marketing, ideally in FMCG, lifestyle, or e-commerce.
*   A solid understanding of content and performance marketing, along with broad knowledge of the French media landscape, is essential – plus a strong interest in SEO and AI.
*   Excellent **writing skills** with a strong grasp of tone, brand voice, and audience needs.
*   You speak **excellent French** and a have **very good command of English** for daily communication – any additional language skills are a big plus
*   You live in **Berlin** or nearby or you’re excited to relocate for this role.
*   A **proactive, structured, and collaborative** approach – you plan ahead, communicate clearly, and deliver quality on time.
*   A real passion for crafting copy that informs, inspires, and moves people.
*   Ideally, familiarity with content management systems (e.g., Shopware and Hygraph), Google Analytics, and common SEO tools
*   You can **work independently** and keep a cool head even in stressful situations
*   One of your strengths is creativity as well as strong communication skills and you enjoy working in creative freedom - what matters to us are the results!
*   **Willingness to travel** if needed to support brand activities, events, and partnerships in France
