### Description\nspized, a growing company with 57 employees specializing in customizable sports apparel, is seeking a Creative Concepter (m/f/d) to join their team from January 2026. This role is ideal for a creative mind eager to fully realize their ideas in the ads sector and significantly contribute to the performance of high-budget campaigns. As a Creative Concepter, you will develop engaging ads, foster collaboration between teams, optimize processes, and ensure creative concepts are successfully implemented from ideation to international deployment. Candidates should bring 1-3 years of relevant experience and enjoy a relaxed, sports-oriented work atmosphere.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop high-performing creatives aligned with campaign goals, actively contributing creative ideas through visual briefings and scripts.\n- Serve as an interface, coordinating collaboration between production and content teams, and optimizing creative strategies and assets with the PPC team.\n- Manage and optimize internal pre- and post-production processes, including the administration of creatives in internal databases.\n- Write concise and compelling ad copies designed for high conversion rates.\n- Plan and coordinate briefings for monthly Content Days and weekly ad shootings.\n- Oversee the translation and adaptation of existing briefings for current and new international markets.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 1-3 years of practical experience in marketing or media design, with a passion for creative execution and continuous optimization through testing.\n- Foundational knowledge in performance marketing and content production.\n- Proficiency in using Meta Ads Manager.\n- Excellent German and English language skills; additional languages are a plus.\n- Proven track record of successful content shootings and ability to demonstrate measurable results.\n- Deep understanding of target group-oriented creatives across various platforms.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting opportunity for a proactive and creative professional to significantly impact the digital advertising success of a rapidly growing company in the sports apparel sector. spized offers a vibrant, collaborative, and results-oriented culture, where you'll work with 'A-players' and have the autonomy to drive creative performance. The emphasis on process optimization, internationalization, and high-budget campaigns ensures a challenging yet rewarding environment. With flexible work arrangements, modern office amenities, and a strong focus on team well-being and growth, spized provides an excellent platform for a Mid-Senior Creative Concepter to thrive.
