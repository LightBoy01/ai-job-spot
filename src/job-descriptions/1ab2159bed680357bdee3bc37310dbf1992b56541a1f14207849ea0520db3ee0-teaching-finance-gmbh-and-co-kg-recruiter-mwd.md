### Description\nTeaching Socials is one of the fastest-growing consulting firms in the DACH region, specializing in personal branding for finance and real estate professionals. We are seeking an ambitious Recruiter (m/f/d) to take full ownership of the entire recruiting process. This is a high-impact role focused purely on Talent Acquisition, from outbound sourcing to signed contracts, with clear KPIs, free from HR administration or \"feel-good\" tasks. You will be instrumental in building our \"A-Player\" team to drive reach, authority, and revenue.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Active Sourcing & Talent Scouting:** Utilize platforms like LinkedIn Recruiter, professional networks, communities, and events to proactively identify and engage top talent.\n- **Pipeline Management & Initial Contact:** Conduct initial phone screens, qualification calls, and fit-checks against scorecards to assess candidate suitability.\n- **Job Profile & JD Writing:** Collaborate closely with Hiring Managers to refine job requirements and craft compelling, optimized job descriptions.\n- **Funnel Management:** Oversee the entire selection process, including screening, shortlisting, coordinating and conducting interviews, managing case tasks, and reference checks.\n- **Stakeholder Management:** Ensure close coordination with Hiring Managers, providing clear next steps and regular updates.\n- **Distribution:** Post job openings across relevant job boards and target-group specific channels, and actively build and maintain talent pools.\n- **Reporting:** Prepare weekly reports on sourcing activities, interviews conducted, offers extended, time-to-fill (TTF), and offer acceptance rates.\n- **Process & Tools:** Maintain the ATS/CRM (Recruitee), and continuously develop recruiting templates and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).\n- **Onboarding Handover:** Facilitate a smooth transition of new hires to Operations/Team Lead and provide feedback for recruiting SOPs.\n- **Interview & Selection:** Organize appointments, conduct interviews, and manage the selection process.\n- **Requirements Profiling:** Create precise, measurable requirement profiles in collaboration with departments.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Experience:** 2–4+ years of full-cycle recruiting experience, preferably within an agency, consulting firm, or high-growth environment.\n- **Sourcing Expertise:** Proven track record in outbound sourcing and successfully filling challenging roles.\n- **Interview Skills:** Proficient in various interview methods and selection procedures (e.g., structured interviews, scorecards).\n- **Technical Proficiency:** Familiarity with ATS and talent databases; excellent research skills.\n- **Language Skills:** C1+ level German, with clear written and verbal communication; English proficiency is advantageous.\n- **Mindset:** A data-driven approach to recruiting, steering activities based on KPIs.\n- **What We Don't Look For:**\n    - HR Generalists without active sourcing experience.\n    - Corporate mentality, slow processes, or \"post & pray\" approaches.\n    - Individuals merely seeking \"a job\" instead of striving for visible hiring impact.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a phenomenal opportunity for a driven and results-oriented recruiter who thrives in a fast-paced, high-growth environment. Teaching Socials offers a culture defined by high standards, ambition, ownership, and a direct path to impact. You'll work closely with founders, implement solutions quickly, and be measured by concrete results, not administrative tasks. If you're passionate about pure talent acquisition and want to contribute significantly to building a market-leading team in a dynamic industry, this role offers immense professional growth and a chance to truly make your mark.
