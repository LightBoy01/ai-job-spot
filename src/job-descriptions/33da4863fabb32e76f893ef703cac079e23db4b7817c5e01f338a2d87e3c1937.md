We are looking for **German language experts** to join our annotation review team. In this role, you will contribute to the development of next-generation generative AI by reviewing, refining, and fact-checking linguistic data.

This position is open to candidates with strong German and English skills who are detail-oriented and motivated to work on cutting-edge projects.

**Responsibilities**

*   Review and validate German annotations for accuracy and consistency
    
*   Support prompt creation and ensure linguistic quality
    
*   Apply reliable research and fact-checking methods

*   Strong English communication skills
    
*   Prior experience with annotation or data labeling is a plus
    
*   Familiarity with fact-checking and research
    
*   Ability to complete and pass a short qualifying test
