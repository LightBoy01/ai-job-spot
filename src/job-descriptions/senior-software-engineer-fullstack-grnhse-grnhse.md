Airbyte is the open-source standard for Data Movement. We enable data teams to move data from applications, APIs, unstructured sources, and databases to data warehouses, lakes, AI applications and LLMs. With our approach we are finally solving the need for extensibility and control that every company needs with data.

&nbsp;

So far, our customers, users, and ourselves have built over 15,000 connectors and have had 200,000+ companies install Airbyte. We've raised $181M from the world's top investors (Benchmark, Accel, Altimeter, Coatue, Y Combinator, etc.) and we believe in product-led growth, where we build something awesome that all our users love.

&nbsp;

We’re committed to providing as much context to our current employees and candidates.&nbsp;The Airbyte&nbsp;company handbook is open to all.

&nbsp;

If you find this role exciting, we encourage you to apply even if you think you don’t meet all requirements.





<p><strong>Opportunity</strong></p>
<p>You'll be joining a senior team of frontend engineers who own the Airbyte frontend end to end. We have big plans for expanding our data product to include orchestration, data observability, lineage, and streaming. You will design and build&nbsp; products that will allow Airbyte to be the single pane of glass for data teams and individuals to manage their entire data operation in one tool. These features will be game-changing for our open source community and will be a massive expansion driver for our Enterprise business.</p>
<p>The Airbyte UI is intuitive and powerfully simplifies complex data replication for everyone. It empowers non-technical users to get up and running quickly and equips power users to optimize and monitor their pipelines. In addition to feature development, you will help set the conventions and best practices for the entire frontend codebase.</p>
<p><strong>What You'll Do</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Build new products&nbsp;</li>
<ul>
<li>Including orchestration, data observability, lineage, and streaming</li>
</ul>
<li>Own the Airbyte frontend</li>

<li>Define and promote conventions and best practices for the frontend</li>
<li>Write high-leverage code</li>
<li>Mentor other engineers</li>
</ul>
