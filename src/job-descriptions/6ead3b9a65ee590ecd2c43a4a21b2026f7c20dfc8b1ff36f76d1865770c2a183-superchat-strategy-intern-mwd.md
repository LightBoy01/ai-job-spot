### Description
At Superchat, we are redefining communication between businesses and their customers. Our mission is clear: communicating with a company should be as simple and natural as chatting with friends. Superchat is currently experiencing rapid growth, bringing many exciting opportunities. That's why we're looking for you to join us! This internship offers a unique opportunity to significantly contribute to our scaling efforts, expand our market share, and drive our brand presence. You will take ownership of diverse projects, gaining deep insights into key business functions like Growth, Marketing, Sales, RevOps, and Operational Excellence, all while working closely with our management.

### Responsibilities
*   Scale Superchat and significantly expand our market share by analyzing growth potentials and preparing decision-relevant insights for our management.
*   Develop and implement social media and marketing strategies to strengthen our brand presence and drive organic growth and lead generation for Superchat.
*   Take independent and self-directed ownership of projects across various company departments, leading them to successful completion.
*   Gain deep insights into Growth, Marketing, Sales, RevOps, and Operational Excellence.
*   Get to know the daily business of our teams, in addition to project work and supporting our management.

### Qualifications
*   You are currently studying or have successfully completed a degree in a startup-relevant field (e.g., economics or natural sciences) and are available for a 3- to 6-month internship.
*   You have prior practical work experience in startups, management consulting, or investment banking.
*   You set the highest quality standards for yourself and possess a strong sense of responsibility.
*   You have a strong drive for continuous development and are not afraid to get hands-on.
*   You enjoy working in a highly analytical and structured manner.
*   You possess native-level German language skills and very good written and spoken English skills (Spanish is a plus).

### Author's Take
This is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for an ambitious student or recent graduate eager to make a tangible impact in a hyper-growth startup. Superchat offers a unique blend of high autonomy and strong support through an intensive onboarding program, regular coaching, and feedback. You'll be part of an inclusive, team-spirited culture, celebrating successes and sharing ideas in Berlin. With direct exposure to senior management and critical business functions, this internship is designed to accelerate your professional development and provide unparalleled experience in Product-Led Growth within a well-funded, innovative company. If you're ready to take significant responsibility and contribute to a company redefining customer communication, this role is for you.
