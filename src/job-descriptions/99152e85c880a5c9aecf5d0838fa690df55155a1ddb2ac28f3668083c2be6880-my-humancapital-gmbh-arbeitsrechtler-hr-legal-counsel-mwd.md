### Description\nJoin our partner company in Munich as an **HR Legal Counsel (m/w/d)**. In this crucial role, you will be responsible for national and international labor law matters, including tax and social security law. You will support the HR Director on international projects and HR initiatives, ensuring all actions comply with legal and collective bargaining regulations. This position offers a unique opportunity to contribute to a modern, growing company with a focus on employee well-being and professional development.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Responsible for national and international labor law issues, including tax and social security law.\n- Support the HR Director in planning, coordinating, and implementing international projects and HR measures, ensuring compliance with legal, labor, and collective bargaining regulations.\n- Represent the company in labor court proceedings, create guidelines, works agreements, and labor contract templates, ensuring their legal conformity.\n- Collaborate with the Works Council.\n- Coordinate and manage secondments and international expatriates.\n- Advise and train managers and HR colleagues on all labor law topics, drive the further development of HR processes, and work closely with HR Development.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A degree as a fully qualified lawyer (Volljurist), business lawyer (Wirtschaftsrechtler), or a comparable qualification with a focus on labor law.\n- Several years of professional experience in HR with an international focus; ideally, profound knowledge of tax and social security law, and experience with collective bargaining agreements, e.g., in the metal and electrical industry.\n- Good user knowledge of MS Office.\n- Strong communication skills, diplomatic aptitude, assertiveness, and intercultural competence.\n- Very good German and good English language skills, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced labor law specialist to take on a highly impactful role within a dynamic company in Munich. The organization emphasizes work-life balance with flexible hours and home office options, a professional onboarding process, and significant opportunities for continuous learning and career development. You'll work in a modern environment, contribute to strategic HR initiatives, and play a vital part in fostering a legally compliant and supportive workplace culture with a global reach. If you're looking for a long-term, stable position where your expertise is valued and you can truly make a difference, this role is for you.
