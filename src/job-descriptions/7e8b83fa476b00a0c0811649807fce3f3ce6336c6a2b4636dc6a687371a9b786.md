**In a nutshell**   

---------------------

At Picnic, we’re revolutionizing the way people buy groceries with our innovative and sustainable app-only service. We do almost everything in-house, developing cutting-edge technologies and processes to ensure we continue to grow rapidly without missing a beat. 

Ever since Picnic started, the importance of consistent high-quality product photography was clear to us. That’s why we’ve always shot all our packshots in our own photo studios, eliminating dependency on suppliers and creating the best customer experience. As we look to the future, AI will play an increasingly vital role in optimizing our visual content, making our operations more scalable and future-proof, and freeing up our creatives to do what they love most: be creative. 

We're looking for an enthusiastic product photographer to join our team, someone who is excited to contribute to our evolving visual strategy. If you’re ready to put your photography skills into practice and help shape the future of online grocery visuals, we're looking for you!

**Tricks of the trade**
-----------------------

*   **Ownership:** Have responsibility for the product photography process, ensuring all deliverables are finished on time
*   **Fast-paced:** Shoot high volume of products across many categories, from bakery to drugstore 
*   **Quality:** Produce high quality product photos that align with Picnic's guideline standards, as well as experiment with new photography to improve our strategy
*   **Learn:** Contribute actively to the transition we are making in visual content optimization using AI tools
*   **Equipment:** Maintain necessary camera tools, photography props and studio equipment

**Your superpowers  
**
-----------------------

*   2-5 years of professional experience in e-commerce product photography
*   Advanced technical skills, you are capable of independently building lighting setups
*   Demonstrated experience in handling high volume in-house photography workflows 
*   Experience with AI tools, or eager to learn more about AI tools to enhance creativity and efficiency
*   Excellent knowledge of studio equipment and Capture One, and good Photoshop skills
*   Great attention for detail, ability to notice the small but important things
*   Strong portfolio with relevant product photography examples
*   Degree in a relevant photography field is an advantage
*   Demonstrates flexibility and a can do mentality in a fast-paced work environment
*   Good English communication skills, verbal and written
*   You're based in the Netherlands to be able to work from our beautiful photo studio and office in Amsterdam
