<p><strong>What You'll Do:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Design and develop innovative learning materials that go beyond traditional methods, incorporating eLearning modules, interactive videos, dynamic guides, and immersive content.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Tailor content to highlight DevRev’s value propositions while helping to establish DevRev as a thought leader through developing content with a focus on grassroots user engagement, leveraging real-world scenarios, gamification, and personalized learning paths to enhance retention and practical application.</li>
<li>Collaborate with subject matter experts (SMEs), product teams, and stakeholders to gather requirements and create content that simplifies complex concepts.</li>
<li>Develop certification programmes that will cater to customers, partners and new hires.</li>
<li>Create course outlines, storyboards, and scripts that align with business objectives and learning outcomes.</li>
<li>Use instructional design theories, models, and best practices to develop both customer-facing and internal training programs.&nbsp;</li>
<li>Analyze usage data and user journeys to identify pain points, creating content that aligns with users' needs and meets them at their level for a more tailored learning experience.</li>
<li>Utilize the latest technology and content creation tools to ensure quick turnaround times for project deliverables, enabling high-quality content development while consistently meeting deadlines.</li>
<li>Utilize learning management systems (LMS) and other tools to create and manage training courses.</li>
<li>Continuously assess and improve learning materials based on feedback and evolving product/tech updates.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>What You'll Bring:&nbsp;</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>3-5 years of experience as an Instructional Designer, preferably in a SaaS or product/tech company.</li>
<li>Strong understanding of instructional design models and eLearning development tools.</li>
<li>Experience with content development software (e.g., Articulate) and LMS platforms.</li>
<li>Familiarity with leveraging <strong>AI tools</strong> (e.g., generative AI, conversational agents, AI-powered content assistants) to accelerate content creation, personalize learning paths, and enhance learner engagement.</li>
<li>Ability to work in a fast-paced, agile startup environment with tight deadlines and shifting priorities.</li>
<li>Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with a knack for simplifying complex topics.</li>
<li>Strong attention to detail, creativity, and enthusiasm for producing high-quality, user-centered content.</li>
<li>Self-motivated with the ability to work independently as well as part of a collaborative team.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Bonus Skills:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Experience with video editing, graphic design, or multimedia content creation.</li>
<li>Familiarity with analytics tools to track and improve training effectiveness.</li>
<li>Experience designing or implementing <strong>AI-integrated learning experiences, </strong>such as adaptive learning flows, chat-based learning, or AI skill-building simulations.</li>
</ul>
