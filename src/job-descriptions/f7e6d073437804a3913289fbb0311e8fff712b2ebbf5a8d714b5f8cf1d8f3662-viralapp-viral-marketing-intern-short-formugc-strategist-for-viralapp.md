**Your Mission**

You are our **Viral Marketing Analyst**. Your job is simple: figure out _exactly_ why content is blowing up on short-form platforms, and turn those insights into actionable strategy for us and our users.

Tasks
-----

*   **Viral Trend Hunter:** Identify the newest organic hooks, formats, and strategies dominating TikTok, IG Reels, and YT Shorts.
*   **Deconstruct Strategy:** Analyze the organic marketing playbooks used by top consumer brands and mobile app founders to achieve explosive growth.
*   **Content & Insights:** Use your writing skills to create sharp, concise reports and content that summarize your key findings.
*   **Hands-On Growth:** Bring a **growth hacker mindset** to help us test and execute new organic marketing experiments.

*   **Platform Native:** You don't just use TikTok, IG Reels, and YT Shorts — you _speak the language_ and understand the algorithms better than most.

*   **A Strong Creator:** Excellent writing and content creation skills are essential.
*   **Hands-on Mentality:** You thrive in a fast-paced, startup environment and are ready to jump into execution.

*   **Mastery in a Niche:** Become a globally recognized expert in the highly valuable, rapidly evolving space of performance UGC and viral content.
