1.  **Educational Background:**  
    • A Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related fields.  
    • Candidates with backgrounds in Electrochemistry, Materials Science, or Mechanical Engineering with relevant battery system research or testing experience will also be considered.
2.  **Work Experience:**  
    • 0–5 years of experience in the battery, energy storage, or power electronics industry (candidates with strong AI/ML research background are also encouraged to apply).  
    • Experience or research exposure in battery management systems (BMS), battery testing, system integration, or power management.  
    • Practical or research experience in applying AI/ML methods to battery diagnostics, lifetime prediction, or optimization is strongly preferred.
3.  **Technical Expertise:**  
    • Knowledge of battery operation, performance evaluation, and system-level testing (lithium-ion or other emerging chemistries).  
    • Familiarity with power management, battery testing equipment, or control/system design.  
    • Experience in AI/ML model development, data analysis, or algorithm design related to battery or energy systems is a plus.
4.  **Soft Skills:**  
    • Good English presentation skills with the ability to clearly communicate technical findings to both technical and non-technical audiences.  
    • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English, including the ability to draft clear technical documentation and reports.
5.  **Personal Attributes:**  
    • A proactive learner with a growth mindset, eager to explore and adopt new technologies and methodologies.  
    • Capable of working independently and executing high-quality projects with minimal supervision.  
    • Strong problem-solving, analytical, and critical thinking skills, with the ability to define directions and deliver effective solutions.
6.  **Other Skills:**  
    • Experience with AI/ML tools, battery performance modeling, or system simulation platforms is advantageous.  
    • Familiarity with regulatory and safety standards for battery systems is a plus.  
    • Knowledge of laboratory safety protocols and experience with test equipment or resource management is desirable.
