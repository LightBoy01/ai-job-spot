Working Student, Part-Time, Remote or Cologne / Darmstadt / Düsseldorf / Berlin

Info
----

Who we are
----------

We operate the international [online marketplaces](https://www.kaufland.de/) of Kaufland: Several thousand sellers and millions of products make us one of the fastest growing online marketplaces. Ours is a dynamic corporate culture that merges a start-up mentality with the opportunities offered by a large corporation. We combine knowledge and many years of experience in e-commerce with flat hierarchies and a highly motivated team.

We love flexibility! You have the freedom to choose whether you want to work remotely (within Germany) or at one of our offices in Cologne, Darmstadt, Düsseldorf, or in our co-working space in Berlin. You decide what works best for you!

Day by day, our Tech & Product Team of about 400 experts pursues the goal of creating the best possible customer shopping experience for our online marketplace. Through innovative technical developments, they not only create an outstanding shopping experience but also lay the foundation for an optimal selling experience for our sellers. Learn more about our division, areas, and cross-functional teams [here](https://kaufland-ecommerce.com/en/team/tech/)!

Aufgaben
--------

Your tasks – this is what awaits you in detail
----------------------------------------------

*   You support our development team in building and improving web applications
*   You further develop existing applications and optimize them in terms of design and performance
*   You work hand in hand with software engineers and collaborate with product designers and product managers
*   You implement new features and maintain existing code
*   With the help of testing, code reviews, and pair programming, you ensure the quality and maintainability of the code
*   You actively seek feedback and engage in learning opportunities to enhance your technical skills and understanding of best practices in software engineering

Profil
------

Your profile – this is what we expect from you
----------------------------------------------

*   You are an enrolled student in computer science or a related field
*   You have already had the opportunity to gain hands-on experience in software engineering
*   Ideally, you have some experience with one or more of the following technologies: JavaScript, TypeScript, Vue.js, PHP, or Golang
*   You are open to new challenges and eager to learn from experienced engineers in a supportive environment
*   You demonstrate strong organizational skills and the ability to balance work commitments with your studies effectively
*   You are reliable and proactive, possess excellent communication skills, and thrive in an international, English-speaking team
