### Description\nThis is an exciting part-time Working Student opportunity within Kaufland e-commerce, one of Germany's fastest-growing online marketplaces. Operating with a dynamic culture that blends startup agility with corporate stability, Kaufland's Tech & Product team of 400 experts is dedicated to creating an outstanding shopping and selling experience. This role offers exceptional flexibility, allowing you to work remotely within Germany or from one of their offices in Cologne, Darmstadt, Düsseldorf, or a co-working space in Berlin. As a Working Student, you will contribute directly to the development and optimization of web applications, gaining hands-on experience in a highly collaborative and supportive international environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Support the development team in building and improving web applications.\n- Further develop existing applications and optimize them for design and performance.\n- Work hand in hand with software engineers and collaborate with product designers and product managers.\n- Implement new features and maintain existing code.\n- Ensure the quality and maintainability of code through testing, code reviews, and pair programming.\n- Actively seek feedback and engage in learning opportunities to enhance technical skills and understanding of best practices in software engineering.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Enrolled student in computer science or a related field.\n- Prior hands-on experience in software engineering.\n- Ideally, some experience with one or more of the following technologies: JavaScript, TypeScript, Vue.js, PHP, or Golang.\n- Open to new challenges and eager to learn from experienced engineers in a supportive environment.\n- Strong organizational skills and ability to balance work commitments with studies effectively.\n- Reliable, proactive, with excellent communication skills, thriving in an international, English-speaking team.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis Working Student role at Kaufland e-commerce offers a remarkable opportunity for aspiring software engineers to gain practical experience within a vibrant and flexible environment. The company stands out for its unique blend of startup dynamism and the robust support of a large corporation, providing extensive learning and growth opportunities like language programs, in-house training, and mentorship. The emphasis on work-life balance, mental well-being, and a strong team-player culture, complete with digital onboarding and buddy programs, makes this an ideal place to kickstart your tech career while continuing your studies. The flexibility to choose your work location (remote or various German cities) further enhances its appeal.
