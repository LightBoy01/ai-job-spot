<p><strong>About Us</strong></p>
<p>At Unframe, we build AI-powered products that address real-world challenges. Our platform team is at the core of this mission, developing the infrastructure, reusable components, and internal tools that enable rapid and reliable product delivery.</p>
<p><strong>What You'll Do</strong></p>
<p>As an Applied AI Engineer, you’ll work closely with customers and internal teams to translate business problems into AI-driven solutions. You’ll be hands-on with AI workflows, and help bridge the gap between model capabilities and real-world impact.</p>
<p><strong>Responsibilities</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Implement workflows and configure model behavior for customer solutions.</li>
<li>Build integrations between AI logic and deterministic building blocks.</li>
<li>Work with platform and research teams to surface platform-level improvements.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Requirements</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>4+ years experience in software engineering or research roles.</li>
<li>Experience building or integrating LLM-based solutions.</li>
<li>Ability to rapidly understand complex domains and translate them into structured prompts.</li>
<li>Comfortable working with real-time applications and production systems.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Nice to Have</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Experience working with AI or LLM-based applications.</li>
<li>Experience working with B2B or enterprise customers.</li>
</ul>
<p>If you’re a strong, independent builder who thrives on ownership, cares deeply about quality, and wants to have a real impact — we’d love to meet you.</p>
