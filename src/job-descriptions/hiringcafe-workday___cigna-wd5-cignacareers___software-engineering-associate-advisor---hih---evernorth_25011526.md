Role Title: Software Engineering Associate Advisor Cloud & AI

Position Summary:

Cigna, a leading Health Services company, is seeking a talented and forward-thinking AI Cloud Engineer to join our Data & Analytics organization. This role blends full-stack engineering expertise with cutting-edge AI and cloud technologies. You will be responsible for designing and implementing scalable systems that power intelligent applications, contributing to projects that push the boundaries of what's possible with Artificial Intelligence.

This position requires fluency in critical technologies, a hunger to learn, and a strong sense of ownership and accountability. You’ll work end-to-end—from understanding requirements to deploying production-ready software—while driving automation, continuous improvement, and innovation.

Behaviors of a Full Stack Engineer:

Full Stack Engineers are able to articulate clear business objectives aligned to technical specifications and work in an iterative, agile pattern daily. They have ownership over their work tasks, and embrace interacting with all levels of the team and raise challenges when necessary. We aim to be cutting-edge engineers – not institutionalized developers.

Job Description & Responsibilities:

· Write referenceable & modular code and have experience in all the phases of SDLC

· Hands on experience developing or using CI/CD solutions for application.

· Be inquisitive, analytical, and investigative, all while keeping customer in mind

· Be a planner -time management and the ability to see around the corner is essential

· Take ownership and accountability and have a desire to simplify, automate

· Have a quality mindset, not just code quality but also to ensure ongoing data quality by monitoring data to identify problems before they have business impact

Experience Required:

· 8-11 Years of overall experience in Software Engineering

· Strong proficiency in Python and related frameworks

· Experience with Langchain or similar AI-focused libraries

· Familiarity with RESTful APIs and microservices architecture

· Experience with cloud platforms, preferably AWS

· Solid understanding of database systems and ORM technologies

· Experience with version control systems (e.g., Git)

Experience Desired:

· 2+ years being part of Agile teams – Scrum or Kanban

· 4+ years of hands-on experience in programming language Python. (Python is preferred)

· 1+ years in Web development frameworks like React, Angular, other Node.js based systems, Django, React-native, etc.

· 3+ years of REST APIs

· 4+ years of scripting (Python, Shell etc.)

· 3+ years of experience with any. Enterprise level database (Postgres, Db2, TDV, Redshift, My SQL or any other cloud DB)

· Experience with GitHub/Jenkins

· Troubleshooting skills

· Strong communication skills

· Fluent in BDD and TDD development methodologies

· Experience with AWS /Azure cloud technologies, terraform, CloudFormation is a plus

· Experience with data platforms such as databricks or snowflake

· Knowledge of containerization technologies (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes)

· Familiarity with AI/ML concepts and workflows

· Contributions to open-source projects

Evernorth Health Services, a division of The Cigna Group, creates pharmacy, care and benefit solutions to improve health and increase vitality. We relentlessly innovate to make the prediction, prevention and treatment of illness and disease more accessible to millions of people. Join us in driving growth and improving lives.
