**JOB RESPONSIBILITIES:**

**Design System Contribution & Learning**

*   Assist in maintaining and evolving our design system (typography, colour, spacing, motion, UI components).
*   Support documentation efforts, including guidelines for visual language and component usage.
*   Learn to work with engineering workflows and tools that bridge design to code (e.g., Storybook, Supernova, Git-based handoffs).
*   Explore and apply AI-assisted design tools for prototyping, variant creation, or documentation.

**Product Design & Prototyping**

*   Translate requirements into wireframes, mockups, and prototypes under guidance from senior designers.
*   Collaborate with product managers and engineers to design intuitive, user-friendly interfaces.
*   Participate in rapid prototyping and testing to refine ideas.

**User Research & Experience Support**

*   Help conduct user research (interviews, usability tests, surveys, analytics reviews).
*   Document findings and assist in turning insights into personas, journey maps, and design recommendations.
*   Contribute to validation efforts such as A/B testing or user feedback analysis.

**Collaboration, AI Exploration & Growth**

*   Work closely with senior designers, engineers, and product managers to understand product goals.
*   Present your work in design reviews and be open to feedback and iteration.
*   Stay curious about new tools and technologies, especially AI, and explore how they can improve workflows or user experiences.
*   Contribute to a culture of continuous learning, experimentation, and inclusivity.
