### Description\nAt Stadtritter, we create more than just security technology — we build a strong brand image that radiates trust, professionalism, and clarity. As a marketing all-rounder with a focus on **media design**, you will become part of our creative team and develop visual materials that make our brand visible in everyday life: from print to web to video. If you love precision, uphold high design standards, and enjoy diverse projects, you are exactly right with us.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Design of advertising materials & visual content for print and digital (flyers, brochures, posters, stickers, company documents, apparel graphics, web graphics).\n- Print preparation & quality control: data processing, coordination with printers, checking samples & final results.\n- Further development & maintenance of our corporate design – across multiple brands (e.g., Stadtritter, Alarmanlage, Ever Energy Group, Ever Energy Home,..).\n- Design and maintenance of websites (WordPress), including images, structure & simple layout adjustments.\n- Support for our video production: thumbnails, slides, graphic elements, title cards and layouts for reels & videos.\n- Creation of SEO-friendly visual content (e.g., image texts, structured file naming, alt texts).\n- Collaboration with various departments (Marketing, Sales, Technology) to implement designs precisely.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Excellent knowledge of Adobe Creative Cloud (InDesign, Illustrator, Photoshop).\n- Experience in graphic design, media design, or marketing.\n- Very good German language skills (at least C1).\n- High accuracy, structured way of working, sense of responsibility.\n- On-site work required (no home office).\n- Nice-to-have: Experience with Canva, WordPress, social media, SEO visuals & print production.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role offers a unique opportunity for a passionate media designer to significantly impact a growing brand across diverse platforms. With intensive onboarding, creative freedom, and varied projects, it's ideal for someone who values quality, clarity, and brand identity. The collaborative environment and flat hierarchies promote growth, though the role requires on-site presence. You'll visibly shape how thousands of customers perceive the brand daily, from digital to physical interactions. If you're ready to make a tangible mark and see design as a question of quality and identity, this is an excellent fit.
