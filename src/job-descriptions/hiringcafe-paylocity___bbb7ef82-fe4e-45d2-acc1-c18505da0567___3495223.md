**Summary**

We are seeking a dynamic and visionary Vice President, Growth Marketing Intelligence to lead our marketing organization and drive exponential growth. This role will spearhead data-driven growth and outreach strategies, implement marketing operations that fuel productivity, and develop AI-centric marketing strategies to position Augeo as a leader in engagement technology around the globe. By leveraging evolving AI technologies and fostering cross-functional collaboration, this role will shape Augeo’s storytelling, align marketing initiatives with business objectives, and champion innovation to achieve scalable results in customer acquisition and demand generation.

  

**Responsibilities**

The Vice President, Growth Marketing Intelligence will lead efforts to drive Augeo’s growth by designing innovative, data-driven strategies, optimizing campaigns through advanced analytics, and leading a high-performing team to execute impactful B2B campaigns. This role will deeply integrate AI into all workflows to enhance marketing operations, ensure consistent brand positioning, and foster a culture of experimentation.

*   **Growth Strategies**: Design data-driven plans to boost customer acquisition and demand generation initiatives, identifying new market opportunities and aligning with business goals.
*   **Data Optimization**: Leverage tools like Salesforce CRM & Marketing Cloud, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or Amplitude to track KPIs (CAC, LTV, conversion rates) and optimize campaigns via A/B testing.
*   **Cross-Functional Leadership**: Leverage AI for marketing efficiencies, further advance Augeo’s storytelling, and mentor a team to foster innovation and agility.
*   **B2B Campaign Management**: Oversee end-to-end campaigns across email, social media, PPC, and organic channels, ensuring personalized messaging and brand alignment.
*   **Experimentation & Innovation**: Test new channels and tactics (e.g., viral loops, AI-driven ads) to stay ahead of trends; foster a culture of innovation and creativity across the team.
*   **Budget Management**: Allocate marketing budgets cost-effectively, negotiate with vendors, and forecast outcomes using financial models.
*   **Brand Positioning**: Conduct market research and competitor analysis to refine positioning and amplify reach through industry partnerships, partnering closely with Augeo’s executive team to ensure alignment.
*   **Marketing Operations & AI**: Streamline processes with AI-driven tools to enhance productivity, personalize experiences, and drive data-informed decisions.

**Qualifications**

*   **Experience**: Minimum of 5 years in marketing strategy, demand generation, and team building, with a proven track record of driving growth in a B2B environment.
*   **Education**: A bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications or Organizational Leadership or equivalent experience; MBA preferred.
*   **Technical Skills**: Proficiency in analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude) and experience with AI-driven marketing technologies.
*   **Leadership**: Strong ability to lead and mentor teams, fostering a culture of innovation and collaboration.
*   **Strategic Thinking**: Expertise in developing data-driven growth strategies and translating insights into actionable plans.
*   **Communication**: Exceptional storytelling, communication and messaging skills to align internal and external stakeholders with brand vision.
*   **Innovation**: Passion for experimentation and staying at the forefront of marketing trends and technologies.
