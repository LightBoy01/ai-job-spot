### Description\nWe are a specialized consultancy dedicated to helping industrial and mechanical engineering companies optimize their product data, processes, and systems. Leveraging Siemens Teamcenter, we deliver powerful solutions for robust product structures, sophisticated variant management, streamlined workflows, efficient release processes, and integrated manufacturing operations.\n\nWe are looking for an **experienced PLM Consultant** to join our team. In this role, you will apply your mastery of Teamcenter's technical aspects combined with a deep understanding of processes across product development, engineering, and manufacturing.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Consult, analyze, and implement Teamcenter solutions for our clients.\n- Analyze existing PLM landscapes of our customers.\n- Conceptualize and implement Teamcenter modules (including structure, document, change, and workflow management).\n- Define, adapt, and optimize product structures, variants, and configuration models.\n- Build and maintain **Bill of Material structures** (EBOM, MBOM).\n- Model and maintain **Maximal Bill of Materials**.\n- Support variant management (CTO – Configure to Order / ETO – Engineer to Order).\n- Advise on change processes, versioning, and configuration.\n- Model and optimize release processes.\n- Implement Change Management (ECN, ECO, ECR).\n- Analyze and improve existing workflow implementations.\n- Coordinate with Engineering, Quality, Purchasing, and Manufacturing departments.\n- Consult on data transfer to manufacturing planning / work preparation.\n- Understand MPPR, MPP, or comparable manufacturing structures in Teamcenter.\n- Integrate and interface with CAM environments (NX CAM or external systems).\n- Ensure continuous data flow from Engineering → PLM → Manufacturing.\n- Conduct user and admin training.\n- Create documentation, templates, and best practices.\n- Support Go-Live phases and continuous optimization.\n\n### Qualifications\n**Professional Experience**\n- Several years of practical experience in a **PLM system** (ideally Teamcenter, alternatively: 3DEXPERIENCE, Windchill, SAP PLM, SmarTeam or similar).\n- Very good understanding of **product structures**, variants, and configurations.\n- Knowledge of **EBOM/MBOM**, Maximal Bill of Materials, as well as CTO/ETO processes.\n- Experience with manufacturing data handovers, manufacturing planning, and CAM interfaces.\n- Understanding of engineering processes, CAD data structures, and document management.\n\n**Technical Skills (Advantageous)**\n- Knowledge of NX-integrated processes.\n- Basic scripting or configuration tool knowledge.\n- Experience with Workflow Designer.\n- Knowledge of adjacent systems (ERP, CAD, CAM).\n\n**Personal Attributes**\n- Analytical, structured, process-oriented.\n- Strong in communication and stakeholder management.\n- Enjoys advising, optimizing, and shaping solutions.\n- Solution-oriented, team-player, and customer-focused.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced PLM Consultant passionate about making a tangible impact on complex industrial processes. You'll work on challenging, end-to-end PLM projects from design to manufacturing, with ample scope to specialize in areas like Change Management or advanced BOM structures. The collaborative environment brings you together with expert colleagues, while the company’s commitment to hybrid work, flexible hours, and long-term development ensures a supportive and growth-oriented career path.
