**Job Description:**

**Your contribution to something big:**

*   Research, design, and develop machine learning models (supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning) and deep learning architectures to solve complex business problems.
*   Build end-to-end data pipelines, including data collection, preprocessing, and model deployment.
*   Implement mathematical and statistical techniques (e.g., linear regression, logistic regression, time series analysis, gradient-boosting) efficiently and accurately.
*   Keep up to date with the latest ML/DL research, frameworks, and tools, incorporating relevant advancements into existing workflows.
*   Collaborate with software engineers, data scientists, and product managers to integrate AI solutions into production environments.
*   Perform error analysis and refinements of models to ensure the highest possible performance and reliability.
*   Document processes, architectures, and best practices to maintain clarity and continuity across teams.

**Qualifications:**

**What distinguishes you:**

*   Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field. A Ph.D. is a plus.
*   Strong foundation in mathematical concepts including linear algebra, calculus, probability, and statistics.
*   Proven experience in building and deploying ML models (regression, classification, clustering, etc.) and deep learning architectures (CNNs, RNNs, transformers, etc.).
*   Proficiency in one or more programming languages commonly used in ML/DL (e.g., Python, R, C++); experience with frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, or scikit-learn is highly desirable.
*   Solid understanding of software engineering best practices, including version control (Git), testing, continuous integration, and containerization (Docker, Kubernetes).
*   Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure) for ML model deployment and data storage.
*   Strong problem-solving skills, with an ability to understand complex domains and produce innovative, high-impact solutions.
*   Excellent communication and collaboration skills to work effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
