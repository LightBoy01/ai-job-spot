Role: Junior Python Engineer

Description: We are seeking a Junior Python Engineer with a strong foundation in Python programming and API development to join our dynamic technology team. This role is ideal for an early-career professional who is exploratory by nature, eager to learn and motivated to work on next-generation AI technologies, including Generative AI applications.

  
Key Responsibilities  
•    Design, develop, and maintain Python-based backend services, APIs, and microservices.  
•    Assist in integrating third-party APIs and cloud-based AI services into existing systems.  
•    Collaborate with cross-functional teams to support proof-of-concept and production-ready projects.  
•    Conduct debugging, testing, and performance optimization for APIs and backend services.  
•    Stay current with emerging technologies, particularly in Generative AI ecosystems.

Must Have
---------

•    1 to 2 years of professional experience in software development with a focus on Python.  
•    Proficiency in Python concepts, including object-oriented programming, error handling, and testing.  
•    Strong experience in API development (RESTful APIs, FastAPI).  
•    Familiarity with version control systems (Git) and CI/CD pipelines.  
•    Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.  
•    Good communication skills with the ability to collaborate effectively in a team environment.  
•    Curiosity and willingness to learn new technologies particularly in the Generative AI domain.

Good to have
------------

•    Exposure to cloud platforms (AWS or Azure).  
•    Basic understanding of databases (SQL/NoSQL).  
•    Interest or hands-on exposure to LLMs, LangChain or other Generative AI frameworks.  
•    Familiarity with containerization (Docker) and event-driven architectures.
