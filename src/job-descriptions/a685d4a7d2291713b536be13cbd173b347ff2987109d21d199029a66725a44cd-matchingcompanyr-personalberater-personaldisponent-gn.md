### Description\nThe MatchingCompany® is a professional and sustainable recruitment partner with over 25 years of industry experience, specializing in nationwide placement of skilled and management positions in People Management. We are seeking a dedicated **Recruitment Consultant / Disponent (gn)** on behalf of a client in Bremen to manage the temporary placement (ANÜ) of industrial-technical personnel.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Recruitment of new employees for temporary assignments at client companies.\n- Independent management of the entire personnel process.\n- Sustainable key account management and active new client acquisition.\n- Sourcing candidates through various channels, contract negotiation, personal support, etc.\n- Appropriate placement of employees according to defined client requirements.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed vocational training.\n- Experience in the temporary staffing industry (Arbeitnehmerüberlassung) within the industrial-technical sector.\n- Fluent German language skills, both written and spoken.\n- Enthusiasm for sales and enjoyment of teamwork.\n- High degree of personal responsibility and a strong service-oriented mindset.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced recruitment professional who thrives in a 360° consultant role within the industrial-technical sector. The company offers a stable, permanent employment contract with performance-based pay, annual bonuses, and profit-sharing. You'll benefit from extensive onboarding, continuous professional development, significant creative freedom, and individual growth opportunities. With a minimum of 30 days holiday and comprehensive corporate benefits focusing on health, family, and retirement planning, this role provides a strong foundation for both professional and personal well-being.
