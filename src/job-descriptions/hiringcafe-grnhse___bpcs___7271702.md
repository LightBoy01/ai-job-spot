**In This Role**

You'll join our agile and independent team building a transformative research knowledge reasoning platform using state-of-the-art reasoning and large language models. You'll help revolutionize and democratize data analysis and research insight by transforming complex, time-consuming text reasoning tasks into efficient and trustable processes. This role offers the opportunity to work on cutting-edge AI systems, data analysis, and agent development while contributing to a company-wide knowledge library that impacts organizations across industries.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Collaborate closely with PMs, researchers, architects, and technical leads to understand and refine user requirements for AI systems and research knowledge reasoning while incorporating customer feedback into future designs
*   Develop, optimize, and review code to ensure extensibility, maintainability, and adherence to best practices for large-scale AI platform development
*   Build and deploy large-scale indexes powered by NoSQL, Graph, and ML Vector databases to store and track both human authored and LLM generated research insights
*   Integrate telemetry data collection to monitor AI behavior, performance, and reliability while using this data to refine product design and improve engineering decisions

**Required Qualifications**

*   Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science or related technical discipline with 2-4+ years overall technical engineering experience
*   Minimum 2+ years experience with coding in languages including Python, C#, or TypeScript with large systems software development and support experience, ideally in Azure
*   Minimum 1+ years experience building and deploying LLM application development or RAG (retrieval augmented generation) systems

**Preferred Qualifications**

*   Expertise in designing and implementing LLM Evals with experience in graph databases and NoSQL systems
*   Experience deploying, handling, and scaling infrastructure on cloud platforms with cross-disciplinary team collaboration experience
