We are seeking a motivated Sales Associate to join our sales team as a contractor. The ideal candidate will have excellent communication skills, a passion for sales, and a strong desire to assist our customers with their travel insurance needs so they can look forward to a worry-free traveling experience. You will receive inbound telephone inquiries from customers with the intent of promoting or selling company products and services.

Come work for a company who recognizes that our strength lies in the people we hire.

**Please note:** This is a temporary position contracted for 6 months. At that time, the contract may end, be extended, or potentially transition into a permanent role depending on performance and business needs. 

What you will do

*   Provide telephone support for e-commerce and insurance clients.
*   Identify customer needs and provide optimal or alternative product solutions to meet those needs.
*   Educate potential clients on their insurance options, provide quotes, and close sales.
*   Responsible for in-depth knowledge of products and services offered, learning about products, and staying up to date on new product or policy changes.
*   Accurately enter pertinent customer data and make changes to existing policies.
*   Able to close a sale by addressing customer concerns, demonstrating empathy, and consistently moving the customer towards commitment.
*   Familiarity with AI tools like ChatGPT and similar technologies can help enhance productivity and support innovative problem-solving in daily tasks.

What you will bring

*   Six (6) to twelve (12) months in a customer service or sales environment.
*   Possess TICO certification or willing to become certified.
*   Ability to pay close attention to detail and multi-task.
*   Demonstrate superb customer service skills, a strong customer focus, and commitment to customer satisfaction.
*   Fluency in French is preferred.
