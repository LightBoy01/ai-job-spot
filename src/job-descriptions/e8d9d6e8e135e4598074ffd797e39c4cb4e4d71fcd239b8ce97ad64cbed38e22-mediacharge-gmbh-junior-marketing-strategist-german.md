### Description\nAre you eager to learn how to develop cutting-edge marketing strategies for the world's most ambitious companies? Do you want to understand how top marketers think and collaborate with CMOs and marketing teams of international enterprises? We're aiming to be the #1 strategic marketing partner globally, serving multi-billion-dollar international clients. We're looking for ambitious colleagues who want to take on leadership and responsibility from day one and grow with us. This role offers an immersive learning experience within a highly motivated, international team. We foster strong collaboration, working together in our stylish Munich office from Monday to Thursday, with the option for home office on Fridays, as remote-only work isn't an option for our team-centric approach.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Take responsibility from day one: You will actively shape and optimize campaigns and learn to communicate at the highest level with clients and colleagues.\n- Work within a team of experienced marketers from around the world.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Excellent academic performance.\n- Top English skills & native German proficiency.\n- Analytical thinking and a strong will to perform.\n- Confident demeanor – especially in discussions with CMOs and decision-makers.\n- The ambition to be among the best (and the readiness to learn for it).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for highly ambitious individuals with a strong academic background who are ready to dive headfirst into the world of strategic marketing. The company offers unparalleled hands-on experience with top-tier international clients, coupled with significant growth potential, weekly 1:1 coaching, and competitive compensation (20%+ above industry average). You'll be part of a \"super ambitious\" team with a clear vision, working in a modern office in the heart of Munich. While remote work is limited, the hybrid model and strong in-person collaboration promise a dynamic and supportive environment for accelerated learning and leadership development.
