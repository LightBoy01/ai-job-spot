**In a nutshell**   

---------------------

We're looking for an enthusiastic Retoucher to join our team. If you’re ready to put your Photoshop skills into practice, we are looking for you! We believe our assortment deserves to look at its best in the app. That's why we don't settle for supplier images or standard pack shots. We shoot beautiful images in our in-house photo studios. Our product images should not only be recognizable, they should also look delicious and inspire our customers. 

Capturing images is only half the effort. As Visual Editor, you know that image editing is a crucial phase, where images are refined, enhanced and transformed. As we look to the future, AI will play an increasingly vital role in optimizing our visual content, making our operations more scalable and future-proof, and freeing up our creatives to do what they love most: be creative.  

You’ll be part of the international team, responsible for product and recipe photos in the Dutch, German and French Picnic app. Are you an enthusiastic Retoucher and are you excited to contribute to our evolving visual strategy? Then we’re looking for you!

**Tricks of the trade**
-----------------------

*   **Edit & retouch images:** Retouch and enhance high volume of product and recipes images to correct imperfections and improve overall visual quality
*   **International post production:** Together with our in-house Visual Editors, you will be responsible for post production of images in all countries
*   **Internal & external collaborations:** Work closely with our internal photographers and editors, and our external edit agency to make sure all photos go through the process smoothly and within deadlines 
*   **AI:** Contribute actively to the transition we are making in visual content optimization using AI tools
*   **Configuration:** Ensure all images are correctly configured in our systems
*   **Brainstorm:** Contribute with creative ideas to improve our product presentation

**Your superpowers  
**
-----------------------

*   Degree in Photography, Graphic Design, or similar
*   0-5 years of relevant working experience
*   Good knowledge of Adobe Photoshop, or eager to become a Photoshop pro 
*   Inspiring portfolio with Photoshop examples 
*   Experience with AI tools, or eager to learn more about using AI tools to enhance creativity and efficiency
*   Eye for detail, ability to notice the small but important things
*   Ability to deliver high volume of images, without compromising on quality 
*   Well-organized, ability to work on multiple tasks in a given period
*   Good English communication skills, verbal and written
*   You’re based in the Netherlands to be able to work from our beautiful photo studio and office in Amsterdam
