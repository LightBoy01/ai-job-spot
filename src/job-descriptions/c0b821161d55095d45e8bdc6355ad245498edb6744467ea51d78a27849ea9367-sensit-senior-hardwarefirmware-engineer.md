**SensitHaptics — The #1 Haptics Platform in SimRacing**

**About Us**

SensitHaptics builds the most advanced high-fidelity haptics in SimRacing and competitive gaming — a category we currently lead, as confirmed by countless industry influencers and top-tier reviewers. We’re a DeepTech startup with real commercial momentum: strong revenue traction across the EU and US, rapidly expanding OEM integrations, and a product that has become the benchmark in the eSports sim hardware scene.

If you’re passionate about gaming hardware, embedded systems, and building tech that users _feel_ in their bones, this is the kind of engineering role that rarely shows up: high ownership, zero legacy baggage, massive upside.

You’ll work closely with Product, Hardware, and our OEM/System Builder partners — and you’ll shape the next generation of real-time haptics firmware used by thousands of serious gamers worldwide.

Tasks
-----

*   Scale up the core firmware embedded engineering team
*   Drive the evolution of our firmware platform — new features, performance breakthroughs, and expanded sensory capabilities
*   Work hand-in-hand with our software engineers to perfect the firmware ↔ app interface
*   Architect and maintain a cutting-edge firmware stack prioritized for ultra-low latency, stability, and power efficiency
*   Select and integrate embedded tech, sensors, actuators, and communication protocols that push the limits of gaming haptics
*   Optimize system for component costs and assembly/production costs
*   Oversee code quality, documentation, and testing rigor across the entire firmware pipeline
*   Support certification processes with external testing labs
*   Provide technical support during OEM partner integrations, prototype iteration, and production ramp-up

Requirements
------------

*   Degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Embedded Systems, or a comparable background.
*   German (min B2-level) or English fluency in verbal or written language is required
*   Several years of hands-on experience developing firmware/embedded software — ideally in consumer electronics, audio devices, robotics, or gaming hardware.
*   Deep command of **C/C++**; Python or Rust is a plus.
*   Strong experience with **RTOS**, **BLE**, **USB**, **audio/haptic signal processing**, or **sensor/actuator integration**.
*   Experience building or leading engineering teams is highly beneficial.
*   Solid understanding of agile development (Scrum/Kanban).
*   Clear, concise communicator with strong ownership and a solutions-driven mindset.
*   Passion for gaming, haptics, eSports, and building products that deliver a visceral, unforgettable user experience is a plus.

Benefits
--------

*   Shape the firmware architecture of the **market-leading** haptics platform in SimRacing.
*   High autonomy and direct influence over core product and technology decisions.
*   Fast cycles, flat hierarchy, and a founder-led engineering culture.
*   Flexible work hours and modern engineering tooling.
*   High-end hardware, test gear, and a fully-equipped development environment.
*   Education credits to attend courses or industry tradeshows.
*   Competitive market compensation including employee stock options
