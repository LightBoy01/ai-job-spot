<p>The Head of the Funds &amp; Asset Managers Credit Risk (2LOD) is a senior leadership role responsible for managing a team of credit risk professionals overseeing the credit quality and risk management of a broad range of fund and asset manager products. As BNY accelerates its AI-enabled transformation and evolves its second-line model to be more commercially minded, you’ll combine deep analytical rigor with data-driven insights, guiding smart risk decisions while preserving robust 2LOD governance.</p>
<p>Key Responsibilities</p>
<ul>
 <li>Lead and manage a global team of credit risk professionals, fostering cross-training, mentoring, and succession planning to ensure continuous coverage and professional development.</li>
 <li>Provide expert credit oversight for alternative fund structures and asset managers—including Hedge Funds, Private Equity Funds, regulated funds, BDCs, and Investment Managers—while championing the adoption of AI/ML tools (e.g., predictive scorecards, workflow automation) to enhance efficiency and consistency.</li>
 <li>Oversee credit assessment and approval for a diverse product set: loan finance, subscription finance, structured finance, repo finance, securities lending, and foreign exchange.</li>
 <li>Perform comprehensive analytical financial reviews, determine credit structures and terms, and exercise approval authority in alignment with the Credit Policy Manual—leveraging advanced analytics to flag emerging risks and commercial opportunities.</li>
 <li>Serve as a consultative partner and challenger to the first line (1LOD), reviewing all credit requests, offering guidance on deal structuring, and recommending adjustments to align with risk appetite and revenue-enhancement goals.</li>
 <li>Collaborate with the 1LOD, Chief Credit Risk Officer, Chief Risk Officer, and Risk Analytics (?) teams to align on major credit decisions, escalate complex transactions, and pilot AI-driven credit analytics initiatives.</li>
 <li>Facilitate and chair the quarterly Funds &amp; Asset Managers Portfolio Management Committee, driving portfolio reviews, risk reporting, and data-backed action plans that balance growth and safeguards.</li>
 <li>Contribute to the enhancement of credit evaluation methodologies, scorecards, and risk models; identify and implement process improvements using agile “test-and-learn” sprints.</li>
 <li>Attend client due diligence and site meetings as required to support relationship teams, validate AI-augmented assessments, and uncover opportunities to streamline onboarding.</li>
 <li>Prepare and deliver periodic written and oral updates to regulators on portfolio performance, emerging risks, AI-enabled enhancements, and policy changes.</li>
 <li>Ensure credit policies, procedures, and guidelines remain current—updating them for shifting business needs, regulatory expectations, and the integration of smart-automation tools.</li>
 <li>Participate in quarterly enterprise-wide portfolio review forums, highlighting trends, concentrations, and risk mitigation strategies through interactive dashboards.</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Qualifications</p>
<ul>
 <li>Minimum of 15 years’ experience in credit risk management, including 5+ years in a leadership capacity within a global banking or asset management environment.</li>
 <li>Proven track record of structuring complex credit facilities and managing a diverse portfolio of Alternative Funds, Fund Managers and Non-Bank Financial Institutions.</li>
 <li>Strong understanding of financial models used in managing &nbsp;counterparty credit risk including stress testing.</li>
 <li>Demonstrated experience driving digital or AI/ML transformation projects in a risk-management setting.</li>
 <li>Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Accounting, or related field; MBA or CFA designation preferred.</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;Skills and Competencies</p>
<ul>
 <li>Exceptional analytical and financial-modeling capabilities, with keen attention to detail and comfort using data-visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, PowerBI).</li>
 <li>Commercial acumen: ability to translate risk insight into client-centric funding and hedging solutions.</li>
 <li>Strong leadership, team-management, and talent-development skills, with a “thrive together” mindset.</li>
 <li>Excellent communication and stakeholder-management abilities, including presentations to C-suite and regulators.</li>
 <li>Sound judgment, decisive decision-making, and ability to articulate risk/return trade-offs.</li>
 <li>In-depth knowledge of credit risk frameworks, policies, scorecards, and regulatory expectations (e.g., Basel, CRD, local regulators).</li>
 <li>Digital fluency: familiarity with machine-learning concepts, API integrations</li>
</ul>
