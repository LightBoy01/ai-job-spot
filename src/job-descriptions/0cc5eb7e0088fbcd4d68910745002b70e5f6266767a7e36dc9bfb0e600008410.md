### Description

**About Grey chain:**

Grey chain is a Generative AI-as-a-service, Mobile & Digital Transformation firm dedicated to helping organizations reimagine user experiences through disruptive and transformational thinking. We are trusted by leading organizations like UNICEF, BOSE, KFINTECH, WHO, and many Fortune 500 companies. Our team consists of over 110 product engineering specialists building the next generation of digital products across industries such as Banking & Financial Services, Non-Profits, Retail & eCommerce, Consumer Goods, and Consulting.

**Location:** New Delhi / Remote

We are seeking a Lead AI Engineer to spearhead the development of advanced agentic workflows and large language model (LLM) systems. The ideal candidate will bring deep expertise in agent building, LLM evaluation/tracing, and prompt operations, combined with strong experience deploying these systems at scale.

### Responsibilities

*   Design and build sophisticated agentic workflows leveraging modern frameworks.
*   Develop and maintain robust LLM evaluation, tracing, and prompt operations pipelines.
*   Lead MCP (Model Context Protocol) based system integrations.
*   Deploy and scale AI/ML solutions with enterprise-grade reliability and performance.
*   Collaborate closely with product and engineering teams to conceptualize and deliver high-impact AI solutions.

### Qualifications

*   Proficiency with agentic frameworks such as LangChain, LlamaIndex, or similar.
*   Hands-on experience with LLM evaluation platforms (e.g., Langsmith, Arize, DeepEval).
*   Strong background in prompt engineering and modern prompt operations (PromptOps).
*   Proven expertise in deploying and managing AI/ML models in production environments.
*   Solid programming skills in Python and its relevant AI/ML libraries.
*   Experience with major cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) and MLOps best practices.
*   Excellent problem-solving abilities and strong communication skills.
