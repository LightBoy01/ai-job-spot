### Description\nWelcome to KSP – a team of around 70 diverse personalities across two locations in Düsseldorf and Munich. We cover almost the entire spectrum of tax consulting and auditing, serving clients ranging from regional SMEs to international corporations. What truly sets us apart from other firms of our size?\n\nFirstly, we advise an above-average number of larger and internationally operating companies. Our multilingual team, with dedicated Spanish, Chinese, and Japanese Desks, allows us to advise clients directly in their native language. However, if you prefer to focus on national mandates, you are equally welcome here.\n\nSecondly – and we believe this is even more important – it's the unique personalities behind KSP that shape our firm. Flat hierarchies, short decision-making paths, and a strong sense of togetherness are not just buzzwords; they are lived practice. Everyone knows everyone here, we are on a first-name basis ('per Du'), and we value an open, appreciative working atmosphere – because work is simply more enjoyable when the teamwork is right.\n\nWe believe you shouldn't have to fit into a rigid job description. Instead, we work together to identify your strengths, interests, and areas where you wish to develop. Whether it's accounting, financial statements, or tax declarations, you will take on responsibility in ongoing client support and be a vital part of the team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Independently manage your mandates and handle the preparation and review of ongoing financial accounting.\n- Prepare annual financial statements and tax returns for companies and private individuals.\n- Review tax assessments and assist in appeals procedures together with our qualified professionals.\n- Serve as a contact person for clients, authorities, and offices, also managing technical correspondence.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed training as a tax clerk (Steuerfachangestellter) and/or further education as a tax specialist (Steuerfachwirt) or balance sheet accountant (Bilanzbuchhalter).\n- Possess professional experience from a law firm/consulting environment.\n- Proficient in using DATEV and familiar with digital tools in the professional services sector.\n- Demonstrate a friendly demeanor, team spirit, and a meticulous, reliable working style.\n- Good English language skills, both written and spoken, with the desire to further develop them with the support of our in-house English trainer if needed.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced tax professional seeking significant autonomy and continuous growth within a highly collaborative and supportive environment. KSP distinguishes itself with a familial culture characterized by flat hierarchies, flexible working hours, and a strong emphasis on individual development through tailored training and international exposure. The role offers diverse challenges and a chance to truly own your mandates, making it ideal for someone who values both professional depth and a positive team atmosphere.
