We are seeking an experienced technology leader to join our dynamic and innovative team. As a Consulting Member of Technical Staff, you will play a pivotal role in architecting, designing, developing, and delivering high-quality software solutions for complex clinical systems. Your expertise will be instrumental in shaping the future of our next generation healthcare solutions.  Work is non-routine and complex, involving the application of advanced technical and business skills in developing the next generation in healthcare solutions.

You will have the opportunity to:

*   Drive the vision for technology that truly impacts the world
*   Ability to have immediate impact on developing technology
*   Unlimited growth potential with inspiring work
*   Work with the best minds in the industry
*   Enjoy working in an open, diverse, and productive environment

Qualifications

*   Led software development, delivery, support and maintenance of entire products.
*   Driven engineering, development and/or ops practices within a SW development organization.
*   Strong understanding of software architecture principles, design patterns and best practices.
*   Experience working directly with executive leadership.
*   Experience working collaboratively across multiple development teams to refine requirements, interfaces and functional responsibilities to create full solutions.
*   Experience recruiting, mentoring and training of software developers at all levels.
*   Excellent problem-solving skills with a detail-oriented mindset.
*   Effective communication skills, with the ability to convey technical concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
*   Demonstrated ability to work collaboratively in cross-functional teams.
*   Ability to adapt to fast-paced environments and learn new technologies quickly.
*   Experience in AI driven development and use of AI applications.
*   Experience being the escalation point for systemic and large-scale operational issues.
*   Experience working with distributed systems.
*   Proficiency in multiple programming languages and software development methodologies.
*   Graduate degree or equivalent experience relevant to functional area.
*   Experience in medical records management (EHR, EMR) desired.

For more information on OHAI's mission see [https://www.oracle.com/healthcare/](https://www.oracle.com/healthcare/)

### Responsibilities

As a technology leader you will take an active role in architecting, reviewing and implementing complete software solutions as well as refining our software development practices and procedures. You will:

*   Lead the design and development of complex software systems, ensuring scalability and maintainability.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather requirements and define technical specifications.
*   Work with executive leadership to refine product requirements and establish delivery milestones.
*   Use AI technologies both in SW development and also in applications.
*   Mentor and guide junior developers, providing technical expertise and fostering skill growth.
*   Research emerging technologies and trends to drive innovation and enhance development processes.
*   Understand and design workflows that interact with internal and external Third Party Systems.
*   Develop and maintain technical documentation, including architecture diagrams and API documentation.
*   Be hands-on and ready to lead troubleshooting and debugging efforts of intricate software issues.
*   Contribute to the architectural decisions of projects, ensuring alignment with long-term goals.
*   Collaborate with product managers to define and prioritize features, delivering high-quality software on time.
*   Collaborate with executive leadership to deliver solutions consistent with long-term goals and vision.
