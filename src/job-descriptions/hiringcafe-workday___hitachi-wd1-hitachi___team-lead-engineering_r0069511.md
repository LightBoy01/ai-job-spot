**The opportunity**

We are looking to hire a new manager for our Engineering Department to lead the Primary Engineering Team. 

The Primary Engineering team handles the electromechanical design of substations. Hitachi Energy delivers substations in the range between 11-420kV, both AIS and GIS, for utilities, railway, industry and generation. The Primary team designs the stations electromechanical layout, apparatus, equipment and calculations.  

We seek a dynamic and strategic leader to guide, manage, and coordinate our Primary Engineering team. This role involves enhancing team performance and development to ensure all activities meet necessary standards and fulfil our commitments to customers.

  
**How you'll make an impact**

*   Collaborate with other departments to manage supplies from an engineering perspective, including analyzing resource demand for capacity management.
    
*   Provide insights, develop, and manage team priorities to ensure performance and quality goals are met throughout all project phases.
    
*   Build operational capacity through effective people management and strategic planning, supporting the establishment and deployment of necessary frameworks.
    
*   Ensure processes are in place to capture and share best practices and operational experiences, supporting business strategy and defining specific processes and tools.
    
*   Set goals and objectives for team members to achieve operational results.
    
*   Ensure policies, practices, and procedures are understood and followed.
    
*   Develop the team and build the capacity required by the strategic plan.
    

**Your background**

*   Proven leadership experience
    

*   Bachelor's degree in engineering, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience. 
    
*   Knowledge of substation technology AIS and GIS. 
    
*   Excellent communication, negotiation, and interpersonal abilities.  
    
*   Strategic thinker with problem-solving skills.  
    
*   It is an advantage with experience from the energy sector.   
    
*   Fluent in Norwegian and English.
