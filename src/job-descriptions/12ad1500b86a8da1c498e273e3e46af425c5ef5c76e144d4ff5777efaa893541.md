*   **Work with the team to create simple dashboards or reports.**
*   **Learn how data and AI are used in real-world advertising projects.**
*   **Share your ideas and document what works well with AI tools.**
**What We’re Looking For**
*   **A student or recent graduate in Data Science, Marketing, Computer Science, or a related field.**
*   **Interest in digital advertising and how AI can support data work.**
*   **Basic knowledge of tools like Excel, Python, or SQL is a plus.**
*   **Curious, organized, and willing to learn.**

*   **Basic data visualization (e.g., Power BI, Tableau).**

*   **Hands-on experience with AI and advertising data.**
