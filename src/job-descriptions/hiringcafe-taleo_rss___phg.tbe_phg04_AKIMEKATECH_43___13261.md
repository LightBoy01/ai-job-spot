The Alaka\`ina Foundation Family of Companies (FOCs) is possibly looking for a **Responsible AI Senior Systems Engineer** to provide support for our government customer in **National Capital Region, Washington, D.C.**

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

*   Assist client with the implementation of the Department’ responsible artificial intelligence program and the development of associated tools and products.
*   Collaborate with Product Development Teams on the development of a strategy that incorporates ethical principles across the entire artificial intelligence product development lifecycle.
*   Develop standard methodologies for responsible artificial intelligence product development.
*   Apply deep technical expertise in data science and artificial intelligence methodologies to build ethical AI capabilities and design models; deploy model testing and validation practices that eliminate potential biases and ensure robustness against adversarial attacks.
*   Apply deep technical expertise to integrate RAI tools into DoD infrastructure and ensure interoperability.

**REQUIRED EDUCATION:**

Must have  Bachelor’s Degree from an accredited university in Computer Science or related field.  Master’s Degree from an accredited university in Computer Science or related field (preferred).

  
**REQUIRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

*   Must have  Minimum 5 years of expertise in the development of machine learning and AI infrastructure.
*   Minimum 2 years’ Experience working on priority DoD data analytics and AI platforms, such as JCF or ADVANA.
*   Minimum of 2 years Expertise with Responsible AI Software Tools.

**REQUIRED CITIZENSHIP AND CLEARANCE:**

*   Must be U.S. Citizen
*   Must have an Active Top Secret clearance with SCI Eligibility
