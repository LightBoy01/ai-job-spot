<p>We are seeking an experienced and visionary AI/ML Solution Architect to lead the design, development, and deployment of advanced AI and ML systems across Autonomous Network. In this role, you will play a critical part in driving business transformation through the application of AI/ML and Generative AI technologies. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams, mentor engineers, and define scalable, secure, and innovative architectures aligned with enterprise goals.</p>
<p>If you have a passion for AI, cloud-based ML solutions, and driving scalable innovation, this role is for you.</p>
Responsibilities
<ul><li>Design and architect end-to-end AI/ML system solutions on the Cloud, ensuring scalability, reliability, and maintainability throughout all project phases—from discovery and proof of concept to industrialization, deployment, and ongoing maintenance.</li><li>Collaborate with stakeholders to understand business objectives and translate them into AI-driven solutions aligned with enterprise strategy.</li><li>Propose, implement, and select optimal machine learning algorithms by leveraging advanced statistical tools and data science techniques to uncover insights and generate accurate predictions.</li><li>Ensure security, compliance, and governance of AI solutions in production.</li><li>Research, prototype, and implement state-of-the-art AI/ML techniques to solve complex business problems.</li><li>Act as a technical leader and mentor to AI/ML engineers across projects, guiding junior engineers in defining robust methodologies and evaluating the most effective technical solutions.</li><li>Define and enforce engineering best practices, coding standards, and scalable design patterns. Facilitate technical knowledge sharing sessions, code reviews, and design walkthroughs.</li><li>Built scalable APIs for real-time web application enforcement and integrating applications with AI Pipelines. Collaborated with cross-functional teams to integrate AI-powered features.</li><li>Stay abreast of the latest AI/ML research, tools, and frameworks, and assess their applicability to current projects.</li><li>Prepare technical documentation, including design specifications, test cases, and user manuals.</li><li>Foster a culture of innovation by exploring emerging AI technologies such as reinforcement learning, split learning, or multimodal AI.</li></ul>
Qualifications
<p><b>You have:</b></p><ul><li>Bachelor’s or master’s degree in computer science, Engineering, or a related field, with overall 8+ years of experience, including extensive work in the design, development, and deployment of AI/ML systems.</li><li>Strong expertise in machine learning algorithms, with advanced proficiency in Python, SQL, and relational/non-relational databases.</li><li>Deep understanding of ML, Natural Language Processing (NLP), Large Language Models (LLMs), and applied GenAI solutions.</li><li>Demonstrated experience in designing scalable AI/ML architectures and integrating intelligent systems into production-ready, cloud-native applications.</li><li>Hands-on knowledge of MLOps practices and tools, such as Kubeflow, MLflow, Vertex AI, and LLMOps pipelines.</li><li>Strong grip on Kubernetes, Docker, and microservices-based architecture for deployment and orchestration of AI solutions.</li><li>Exposure to big data ecosystems (Kafka, Spark, Hadoop) and real-time data processing in AI pipelines.</li><li>Proficient in DevOps/MLOps best practices, including CI/CD for ML, automated testing, model versioning, and monitoring.</li><li>Hands-on experience with LLMs and agentic AI frameworks such as LangChain, CrewAI, or AutoGen.</li><li>Proven ability to lead and mentor AI/ML engineering teams, promote technical best practices, and drive delivery excellence.</li></ul><p><b>It would be nice if you also had:</b></p><ul><li>Experience working with&nbsp;OpenAI, Meta LLaMA models, and&nbsp;LangChain-based frameworks for GenAI applications.</li><li>Prior experience developing&nbsp;AI solutions in the telecom domain, with an understanding of domain-specific challenges and data environments.</li><li>Familiarity with&nbsp;Reinforcement Learning,&nbsp;Federated Learning, or&nbsp;Multimodal AI as emerging research applications.</li></ul><p>&nbsp;</p>
