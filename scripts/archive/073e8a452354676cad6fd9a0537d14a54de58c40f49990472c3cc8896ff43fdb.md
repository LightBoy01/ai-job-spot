### Description

At G-P, our leading SaaS-based Global Employment Platform™ is breaking down barriers to global business, enabling clients to expand into over 180 countries and create opportunities for everyone, everywhere. We are shaping the future of global employment and are seeking a Public Relations (PR) Manager to help tell our story in powerful and creative ways.

In this role, you will craft campaigns that not only make headlines but also move the business forward. You will be instrumental in elevating our brand and reinforcing our position as an AI leader within the global employment and HR technology industry, working across channels from traditional media to generative AI search.

### Responsibilities

- Develop and execute creative, high-impact owned and earned media campaigns across digital, social, podcasts, influencers, and more.
- Collaborate with our PR agency and Communications team to pitch journalists and influencers, securing placements that achieve key KPIs including share of voice, SEO, and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).
- Monitor industry news, press trends, and competitor media mentions to identify timely outreach opportunities and proactively develop pitches.
- Own the creation and maintenance of media messaging, briefing documents, and Q&As, simplifying complex ideas into clear, engaging narratives.
- Work directly with G-P spokespeople on media opportunities, including content development, executive interview briefings, and evaluating incoming press and speaking requests.
- Measure and report on the effectiveness and impact of all PR initiatives.
- Partner with our social media team to identify compelling ways to extend PR wins across G-P’s digital channels.
- Explore and apply AI tools to optimize campaign strategies, amplify content, and drive visibility in emerging AI-powered search and media channels.
- Collaborate on paid media initiatives that extend the reach of earned coverage and report on campaign performance to demonstrate impact.

### Qualifications

- A minimum of 3-5 years of experience in B2B PR, with HR tech experience strongly preferred.
- Strong grasp of the technology and AI landscape, with the ability to simplify complex technical concepts into engaging and authentic stories.
- Proven success in building and executing new and traditional media relations strategies, including podcasts.
- Excellent writing and editing skills across various formats, including press releases, bylined articles, blog posts, and presentations.
- A track record of securing media coverage and managing press interactions that support measurable business outcomes.
- Curiosity and creativity to experiment with AI tools for content development, campaign design, and next-gen search optimization.

### Author's Take

**1. The job description emphasizes positioning G-P as an "AI leader." Beyond PR messaging, how deeply is AI integrated into the company's core strategy, and what kind of support can the PR team expect in telling that story authentically?**

That's a fantastic question, and it gets right to the heart of what makes this role so exciting. This isn't just a marketing angle for us; it's a fundamental part of our strategy. Our product and engineering teams are actively integrating AI to streamline every facet of global employment, from smart onboarding to predictive analytics for our clients. As the PR Manager, you won't be just spinning a story; you'll be embedded with the teams building the future of our platform. You'll have direct access to our tech leaders and data scientists, giving you the raw material to craft narratives that are both authentic and groundbreaking. We see this role as a vital translator, turning complex innovation into compelling stories that truly resonate with the market.

**2. How does G-P measure the success of PR beyond traditional metrics, and how much autonomy will this role have in shaping new, creative campaign strategies?**

We're moving beyond vanity metrics. While share of voice is important, we tie our PR success directly to business objectives like lead generation, sales cycle velocity, and brand preference in key markets. We want to see how a feature in a major tech publication influences inbound interest, for example. Regarding autonomy, you're not just being handed a playbook; we're looking for someone to come in and help write new chapters. We foster a 'test and learn' culture and are eager for fresh ideas—whether that's launching a new podcast series, collaborating with unconventional influencers, or pioneering strategies for Generative Engine Optimization. Your creativity will be your biggest asset, and you'll have the support and resources to bring your most innovative ideas to life.
