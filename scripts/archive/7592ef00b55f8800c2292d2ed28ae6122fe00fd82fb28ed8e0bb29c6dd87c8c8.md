**Company Description:**

Daxko powers wellness to improve lives. Every day our team members focus their passion and expertise in helping health & wellness facilities operate efficiently and engage their members.

Whether a neighborhood yoga studio, a national franchise with locations in every city, a YMCA or JCC--and every type of organization in between--we build solutions that make every aspect of running and being a member of a health and wellness organization easier and delightful. 

**Job Description:**

We are seeking a strategic, hands-on **Manager of Product Operations** to drive efficiency, implement actionable insights, and ensure our product teams have the tools and processes to deliver outstanding customer experiences. You’ll lead a small but mighty team, partner closely with Product, Engineering, and Marketing, and play a key role in shaping how our products come to life. This is a unique opportunity to influence cross-functional operations at a growing, mission-driven company.

**What You’ll Do:**

*   Lead, coach, and inspire a team of product operations professionals, fostering growth, collaboration, and high performance.
*   Develop and implement operational processes that streamline workflows, optimize productivity, and scale across our product portfolio.
*   Manage and optimize our product toolstack, including Aha!, Pendo, Dovetail, Jira, and Power BI, ensuring teams get maximum value.
*   Build data collection and reporting systems that provide actionable insights, track key metrics, and inform strategic decisions.
*   Partner with Product Marketing and other teams to support successful product launches, including go-to-market coordination and performance tracking.
*   Champion the Voice of the Customer across all products, ensuring research and feedback inform product decisions.
*   Drive change management initiatives, including training, playbooks, and process adoption, to empower product teams.
*   Leverage AI and automation tools to simplify processes, enhance analytics, and boost team productivity.
*   Travel up to 25% for team or cross-functional collaboration.

**Qualifications:**

**What You Bring:**

*   4+ years in B2B software product operations, product management, or related operational roles, with measurable business impact.
*   2+ years of experience leading and developing teams.
*   Strong analytical skills, including SQL, advanced Excel, and BI tools; ability to turn data into actionable insights.
*   Proven ability to implement and optimize operational processes that improve efficiency in fast-paced environments.
*   Excellent communication and presentation skills; comfortable partnering across multiple teams and levels.
*   Experience with AI or automation platforms to drive operational improvements.

**Nice-to-Haves:**

*   Experience in multi-product or portfolio management environments.
*   Background in health, fitness, or wellness software markets.
*   Familiarity with Aha!, Pendo, Dovetail, Jira, and Power BI.
*   6+ years of experience in product operations or product management.
