### Description

**About FRYTE**

FRYTE is at the forefront of redefining how electric logistics integrates with the energy grid. Based in Munich, we are developing the intelligent software layer that facilitates the critical transition from diesel to electric freight. Our SaaS platform empowers fleet operators, dispatchers, and charge point operators to seamlessly plan, optimize, and monetize electric truck charging, aligning fleet efficiency with overall grid stability. Our audacious vision is to **replace big oil with smart electrons**, and our mission is to **get e-trucks on the road – faster, smarter, more efficiently**.

This isn't merely a coding position; it's a unique opportunity to **own features end-to-end**, significantly shape the architecture of a rapidly scaling product, and build the foundational systems that will electrify an entire industry. You will collaborate directly with our founders, former TUM researchers with extensive expertise in energy and logistics, translating complex technical and operational challenges into elegant, production-grade software solutions.

### Responsibilities

As a Fullstack Engineer at FRYTE, you will:

*   Own fullstack features, guiding them from initial concept through to successful production deployment.
*   Design and implement clean, scalable APIs that form the backbone of our platform.
*   Build performant and intuitive UIs that genuinely empower real-world logistics operators.
*   Work extensively with geospatial data (leveraging PostGIS) and asynchronous systems (built on FastAPI).
*   Champion and push for high quality, reliability, and security across the entire technology stack.
*   Actively contribute to shaping our engineering culture, defining best practices, and influencing the overall technical direction.

### Qualifications

**What You Bring:**

*   A Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a closely related technical field.
*   1–4 years of fullstack experience, demonstrating proficiency in both Python and JS/TypeScript environments.
*   A deep understanding of modern web stacks, including React, FastAPI, and Postgres.
*   Comfort and eagerness to navigate fluidly between backend, frontend, and DevOps responsibilities.
*   A pragmatic, problem-solving mindset, focused on shipping tangible value rather than merely closing tickets.
*   Genuine curiosity and interest in logistics, optimization, or energy systems.

**Bonus Points:**

*   Experience with PostGIS, Mapbox, or scaling products in an early-stage startup environment.

**Our Core Stack:**

*   **Backend:** FastAPI, Postgres, PostGIS
*   **Frontend:** React, TypeScript
*   **Infra:** Docker, GitHub Actions, OpenStack

### Author's Take

**Question 1:** Given FRYTE's ambitious vision to replace big oil with smart electrons, what does 'owning features end-to-end' truly mean for an engineer, and how can they see the tangible impact of their work?

**Answer:** Here at FRYTE, 'owning features end-to-end' isn't just a phrase – it's how we operate. Imagine being the architect and builder of a critical component, from the initial whiteboard sketch with our founders, through designing scalable APIs and intuitive UIs, all the way to seeing it deployed and actively optimizing electric truck routes in real-time. You're not just a cog; you're the engine driving specific parts of our mission. The tangible impact is profound: you'll witness fleet operators efficiently charging hundreds of e-trucks, dispatchers making smarter decisions, and a measurable reduction in carbon emissions, knowing your code made that happen. It’s a direct line from your craft to a greener planet.

**Question 2:** The job description highlights 'autonomy & ownership' and 'deep work' balanced with 'human connection'. How does FRYTE ensure engineers grow professionally in such an environment, and how do team members truly collaborate when embracing an 'asynchronous-first' approach?

**Answer:** It's a delicate but powerful balance. Growth here is less about rigid ladder climbing and more about expanding your mastery and influence. With true autonomy, you're empowered to identify problems, propose solutions, and take the lead on implementing them, pushing your technical boundaries constantly. Our founders, with their deep academic and startup expertise, are highly accessible for sparring and mentorship, not micromanagement. For collaboration in an asynchronous-first environment, it means we lean heavily on well-structured documentation in Notion, clear communication guidelines, and a bias towards written decisions. This frees up our 'human connection' time for genuinely impactful discussions, brainstorming, and building rapport, rather than endless status meetings. It ensures everyone has focused time to innovate, knowing their work is understood and contributes to a collective goal, while still fostering a close-knit, supportive team that truly connects when it matters.
