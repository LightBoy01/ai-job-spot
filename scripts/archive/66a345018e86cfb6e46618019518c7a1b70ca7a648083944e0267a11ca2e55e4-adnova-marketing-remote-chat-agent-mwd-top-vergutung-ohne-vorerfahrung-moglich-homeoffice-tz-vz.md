### Description
Are you looking for a flexible, 100% remote job that you can comfortably do from your laptop or smartphone, regardless of your location, with above-average compensation? Then the position as a **Remote Chat Agent (m/w/d)** is perfect for you!

We offer a unique opportunity to join a multi-award-winning team. You'll benefit from **performance-based income with no upper limit**, **reliable commission payouts**, and **free, comprehensive training** that ensures your successful entry, even without prior experience. Our team members consistently rate us as a "best employer" on Kununu and describe this as "the best job they've ever had." It's no coincidence that we were recognized as a **Top Employer in 2024 and 2025** and also awarded the **Top Salary Seal 2025**. This role is ideal for those seeking flexibility, support, and genuine growth opportunities in a dynamic, global remote environment.

### Responsibilities
As a Remote Chat Agent, your core mission will be to engage actively and authentically with the community of a creator. Your responsibilities include:

*   **Chat Communication:** Engage in lively conversations with fans of a creator, addressing their wishes and interests to build genuine personal connections.
*   **Sales & Content Promotion:** Skillfully present exclusive content within the chat at opportune moments and guide fans through the sales process step-by-step according to our guidelines.
*   **Data Management (CRM):** Maintain and update information regarding fan interests and preferences to personalize interactions.
*   **Profile Management:** Administer the creator's profile according to our instructions, regularly publishing posts and updates to the feed.
*   **Community Engagement:** Actively respond to comments and interactions to enhance the profile's visibility and foster a vibrant community atmosphere.

### Qualifications
We are looking for motivated individuals eager to learn and grow within our supportive team.

**Must-Haves:**

*   **Age Requirement:** You must be at least 18 years old.
*   **German Language Proficiency:** You write confidently and largely error-free in German.
*   **Strong Communication Skills:** You can express yourself clearly and actively shape conversations.
*   **Technical Setup:** You have access to a laptop, PC, or smartphone with a stable internet connection.

**Preferred, but not required:**

*   **Empathy:** The ability to put yourself in others' shoes and respond sensitively to their moods.
*   **Sales Acumen:** A basic understanding of how to persuasively offer products or content.
*   **Prior Experience:** Previous experience in chat communication or digital content sales.
