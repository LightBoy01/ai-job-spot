### Description

Wavestone is seeking a **Senior Solution Architect** for Project RST. This pivotal role focuses on the development and support of a state-of-the-art self-healing Micro-Frontend application, emphasizing AI-First development approaches, robust architecture, seamless integration, and active coaching of the development team.

As a leading consulting firm for strategic transformations, Wavestone prides itself on being a trusted partner for both clients and employees. They operate responsibly, with high performance, and always with a human-centric approach. Wavestone offers a 360-degree portfolio of consulting services, combining first-class industry expertise with a broad spectrum of cross-industry competencies. They encourage interdisciplinary work and out-of-the-box thinking, providing extensive opportunities for partners and freelancers within their projects.

**Key Details:**
*   **Start Date:** December 1, 2025
*   **Duration:** Not specified (Projected until December 31, 2028)
*   **Workload:** 84%
*   **Location:** Remote (primarily) with occasional presence in Frankfurt am Main, Germany.

### Responsibilities

*   Building self-healing Micro-Frontend architectures and infrastructures using modern technologies like React, Vite, and Module Federation.
*   Implementing technical components in applications based on utilized technologies such as Microservice architectures, Spring, AWS, Messaging, and Kotlin, including associated interfaces, modules, and databases.
*   Consulting on and implementing AI-First development approaches to significantly boost development efficiency across all software development areas: Business Engineering, Frontend and Backend development, Testing, and DevOps.
*   Evaluating, integrating, and optimizing AI development tools like GitHub Copilot, Amazon Q Developer, ChatGPT, and other AI-powered development tools within the project context.
*   Designing and implementing AI Agents for specific development tasks and developing strategies for their effective utilization within the DevOps team.
*   Mentoring the development team in the professional use of AI tools, Prompt Engineering, and best practices for AI-powered development.

### Qualifications

**Required Skills & Experience (Must-Haves):**

*   Excellent methodological and technological knowledge in DevOps, with at least 5 years of experience in building DevOps teams with genuine end-to-end responsibility.
*   Demonstrated experience in building self-healing Micro-Frontend architectures and infrastructures using React, Vite, and Module Federation, with at least 4 years of practical experience.
*   Profound experience with Test Driven Development (TDD) in both Backend and Frontend, with at least 6 years of proven practical experience in implementing software solutions using the TDD approach.
*   Experience in designing and implementing reusable CI/CD pipelines and Cloud infrastructure topics, including at least 5 years of practical experience with GitLab CI.
*   At least 1 year of experience in designing and implementing MLOps pipelines (ML Model Training, Deployment, Monitoring).

**Desired Skills & Experience (Nice-to-Haves):**

*   Experience in using AI tools in software development, with at least 2 years of experience in AI within the context of Code Reviews and Specification Driven Development.
*   Experience with infrastructure monitoring and log analysis in relevant software solutions (e.g., EFK Stack, Prometheus, Kibana, Grafana), with at least 5 years of proven practical experience.
*   Experience with professional web frameworks utilizing Domain Driven Design (DDD), with at least 6 years of experience using Spring Boot or comparable frameworks.
*   Practical experience in building OpenShift infrastructures, with at least 5 years of proven practical experience working with OpenShift infrastructures.
*   Excellent programming and architectural knowledge in the Backend, with at least 6 years of experience in Micro-Service architectures based on Kotlin.
