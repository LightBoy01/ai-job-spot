### Description

We are seeking a proactive and analytically-minded Junior Online Marketing Specialist to join our dynamic team. This role is pivotal in developing and optimizing digital campaigns, leveraging cutting-edge AI tools, and collaborating closely with various internal departments to enhance our overall digital footprint. If you have an initial background in online marketing, a passion for data and AI, and an eagerness to innovate, we encourage you to apply.

### Responsibilities

*   Develop digital campaigns with a focus on reach, performance, and visibility.
*   Utilize AI tools for content optimization, automation, and analysis.
*   Monitor traffic, conversions, and relevant marketing KPIs.
*   Collaborate closely with Content, Development, and Product teams.
*   Support the further development of our marketing strategies – from SEO and Content to Performance.

### Qualifications

*   Completed degree in Marketing, Communications, or Business Administration.
*   Initial experience in online marketing or content management.
*   Interest in data, AI, and new digital trends.
*   Analytical thinking, structured working style, and enjoyment of collaboration.
*   Eagerness to rethink things and implement them pragmatically.
