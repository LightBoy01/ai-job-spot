Are you passionate about SEO and AI Search? Ready to make a meaningful impact? Join our team at Klick Health as an SEO Manager, where you will have the opportunity to develop and implement effective SEO/GEO strategies for our clients. Become the go-to contact for client SEO & GEO engagements, diving deep into website analysis, competitor landscapes, and market research to craft innovative strategies for improving organic search & GenAI visibility.  
  
**Key Responsibilities:**  
*   **SEO Strategy Development:** Perform keyword research, analyze website content, competitor landscapes, and market trends to create innovative SEO strategies.
*   **Technical SEO:** Diagnose technical SEO issues, collaborate with development teams on solutions, and ensure websites adhere to SEO best practices.
*   **Performance Measurement:** Monitor and measure both Organic Search and AI Search performance, providing actionable insights to clients and internal teams.
*   **Client Advocacy:** Serve as an SEO/GEO advocate within our Client Services Team, offering educational support and driving new business development initiatives.
*   **Collaboration:** Work closely with content creators, developers, and marketing teams to implement and optimize SEO & GEO strategies.
  
  
**Required Skills and Qualifications:**  
*   **4+years of hands-on SEO experience.**
*   **AI & automation:** Proven track record of applying AI tools and workflows (including ChatGPT) to improve efficiency and impact.
*   **Curiosity:** For all things SEO, GEO, data, analytics and technology.
*   **Proactive Mindset:** Ability to take initiative and work independently while being a collaborative team player.
*   **Communication Skills:** Exceptional communication and storytelling abilities to convey complex SEO/GEO concepts to clients and internal teams..
*   **SEO Tools Proficiency:** Proficient in SEO tools such as Google Search Console, Ahrefs, SEMrush, and Screaming Frog.
*   **Technical SEO Expertise:** Strong understanding of web analytics platforms, technical SEO troubleshooting, and best practices.
*   **Web Development Knowledge:** Knowledge of HTML, CSS, JS, and content management systems.
*   **Structured Data Markup:** Experience with structured data markup and web development best practices is highly valued.
  
  
**Why Join Us?**  
*   **Innovative & Data-Driven Environment:** Be part of a team that encourages creativity and innovation in SEO/GEO strategies. 
*   **Professional Growth:** Embrace new tools (Profound, SparkToro, etc) and methodologies in GEO, and generative AI models.
*   **Collaborative Team:** Work in a supportive and collaborative environment that values teamwork and knowledge sharing.
*   **Impactful Work:** Make a significant impact on our clients' success and contribute to their growth.
*   If you’re passionate about SEO and the future of Search, eager to share your knowledge/continually learn, and ready to make a meaningful impact, we want to hear from you! Apply now and be part of our collaborative team driving superior service and results for our clients.
