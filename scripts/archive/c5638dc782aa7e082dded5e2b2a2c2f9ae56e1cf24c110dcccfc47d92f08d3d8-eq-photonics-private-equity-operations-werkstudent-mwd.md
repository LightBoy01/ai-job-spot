### Description\nEQ Photonics is a leading distributor in the Laser & Photonics sector, leveraging a proactive Buy-and-Build M&A strategy. We collaborate with global market leaders and cater to a broad network of scientific, medical, and industrial clients. Since our establishment in 2000, we have been committed to innovative solutions and sustainable growth, continuously expanding our portfolio and strategically strengthening our market presence through targeted corporate acquisitions.\n\nAs a Working Student (m/f/d) in Private Equity Operations, you will be directly integrated into our Buy-and-Build strategy. You will support our team in the acquisition and integration of new companies, as well as contribute to operational and strategic projects. This role offers an excellent opportunity to gain hands-on experience starting February 1, 2026, with direct involvement in live deal processes, a steep learning curve through close collaboration with our management team, flexible working hours, the option for partial remote work, and a modern environment within a dynamic and growing company.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Conduct market and competitor analyses to identify potential acquisition targets.\n- Contribute to the preparation of investment documents, such as pitch decks, teasers, and investment memoranda.\n- Support the M&A process from initial contact to successful integration.\n- Assist in the further development of our website and the organization of trade fair appearances.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Currently enrolled student (m/f/d) in Economics, Finance, or a comparable field of study.\n- Proficient in using Excel and PowerPoint.\n- Very good German and English language skills, both written and spoken.\n- Demonstrated structured and independent working style, coupled with a quick grasp of new concepts.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a truly exceptional 'Werkstudent' (Working Student) opportunity for ambitious students eager to dive deep into the world of Private Equity and M&A. EQ Photonics offers a rare level of direct involvement in live deal processes and strategic projects, which is invaluable for an entry-level position. The emphasis on a \"high learning curve\" and close collaboration with the management team signals a strong mentorship culture, where proactive individuals can gain profound insights and significantly accelerate their professional development. The added benefits of flexible working hours and remote options reflect a modern, employee-friendly environment. If you're looking to make a tangible impact and learn from seasoned professionals in a rapidly growing photonics company, this role presents an outstanding career stepping stone.
