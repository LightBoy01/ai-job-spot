Le tue mansioni

For the leading MediaWorld third-party Consumer Electronics Marketplace, operated within its leading E-Commerce Platform which enable Italian and international online sellers to sell their products, we are looking for a **Partner manager**
The main activities will be:
Responsible for the growth of the Marketplace by onboarding new Sellers on the platform;
Generate and manage Seller leads of the assigned categories;
Cooperate peers in the network of marketplaces and partners to recruit and grow the contribution of Cross Border Trade to GMV (Gross Merchandise Volume);
Support the Sales / Activation / Seller Support teams in their day-to-day challenges;
Educate Sellers to perform well in terms of Sales and Quality, generate further sales by enhancing Retail media and visibility tools.
