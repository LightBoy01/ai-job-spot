### Description

To elevate our YouTube channel (TäglichTechnik) to the next level, we need your creative support in video editing and, in part, scripting. TäglichTechnik is undergoing a re-alignment, shifting focus towards modern, engaging tech documentaries. We're seeking an enthusiastic and creative individual who is eager to contribute to this exciting new direction.

This role offers incredible flexibility, allowing you to work remotely from anywhere. You'll gain valuable insights into a rapidly growing YouTube channel and benefit from a culture of fast decisions and easy collaboration, free from endless approval loops. Our small team also enjoys semi-annual offsites, fostering connection and shared vision.

### Responsibilities

*   Edit YouTube documentaries with a modern, dynamic style and strong storytelling – no conventional TV look here!
*   Animate sections within videos with subtle, impactful animations.
*   Perfectly synchronize music, sounds, and B-roll to ensure a cohesive and polished final product.
*   Actively contribute new ideas and creative input – your creativity is essential!

### Qualifications

*   A keen sense for excellent flow, timing, and captivating storytelling.
*   Ability to work independently with a meticulous eye for clean, modern edits.
*   Strong willingness to learn and adapt.
