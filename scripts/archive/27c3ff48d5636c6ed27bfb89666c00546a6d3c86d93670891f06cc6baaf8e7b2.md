**Job Description**
-------------------
The **Growth Marketing Specialist (AI Innovation)** is a key driver in embedding AI capabilities across GTM functions to accelerate automation, enable hyper-personalisation, and enhance operational efficiency. Embedded within the Growth Marketing team, this role focuses on introducing **agentic and** **generative AI-powered tools** that seamlessly connect marketing, product, sales and data—transforming day-to-day GTM operations to future-proof the consumer business.
This position is ideal for a technically adept, curious and self-driven individual who thrives in fast-paced environments and is passionate about solving meaningful business challenges through innovation and automation. By implementing scalable, intelligent systems, you will reduce manual effort, unlock productivity gains and reshape how the CGTM department delivers value.
**Responsibilities**
--------------------
### AI Strategy & Opportunity Mapping
*   Identify GTM processes, workflows, and consumer journeys that can be reimagined with _agentic AI solutions_ (e.g., campaign targeting, churn prediction, pricing, bundling, digital journeys).
*   Work with GTM leads to align AI initiatives with core commercial goals such as acquisition, upsell and retention.
*   Prioritise AI use cases with measurable ROI and customer experience uplift.
### Solution Design & Execution
*   Partner with Data Science, Tech, MarTech and AdTech teams to translate business needs into _agentic AI models_, recommendation engines, or automation flows.
*   Evaluate, test and integrate emerging platforms to strengthen campaign agility, audience targeting, and personalisation.
*   Ensure smooth onboarding of tools with clear documentation, enabling self-serve adoption within GTM teams.
### Innovation & Experimentation
*   Stay up-to-date with the latest in _agentic AI_, generative AI, martech and productivity tools, continuously experimenting with new technologies.
*   Advocate for a culture of innovation and digital-first thinking within the CGTM department.
*   Foster a culture of digital-first thinking by piloting new technologies and validating their impact through A/B testing.
*   Champion scalable innovations that reduce manual effort and enhance team productivity.
### Stakeholder Engagement
*   Work closely with internal and external teams to ensure alignment and successful implementation of initiatives.
*   Present findings, recommendations and impact assessments to leadership and relevant stakeholders
**Requirements**
----------------
### Experience & Skills
*   Minimum 3 years of experience in growth marketing, digital operations or a related field.
*   Proven track record of implementing digital tools or AI-powered solutions to improve business processes.
*   Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to assess high-level business challenges and design effective solutions.
*   Comfortable with rapid experimentation and adapting to evolving technologies.
### Technical Proficiency
*   Familiarity with generative agentic AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Jasper, Notion AI, etc.), low-code/no-code automation platforms (e.g., Zapier, Power Automate, Make), and project management tools (e.g., Asana, Trello, Monday.com).
*   Basic understanding of APIs and integrations.
### Personal Attributes
*   Self-motivated, curious, and excited by the opportunity to drive change.
*   Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
*   Flexible and resilient in the face of ambiguity and shifting priorities.
