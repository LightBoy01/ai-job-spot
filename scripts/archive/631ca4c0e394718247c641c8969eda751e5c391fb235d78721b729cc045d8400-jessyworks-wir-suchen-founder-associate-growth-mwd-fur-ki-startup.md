### Description

Hey, we are **Jessy Works** – an AI startup revolutionizing accounting. While tax firms are desperately searching for skilled workers, we provide the solution: digital accounting on autopilot. Our AI assistants handle bookkeeping, routine tasks, and client communication, thereby expanding the capacities of tax firms.

We are currently looking for you as a **Founder Associate Growth (m/w/d)** – with a genuine sales focus and the opportunity to experience firsthand how a high-performance sales team is built.

**Ort:** Hybrid (Munich-based Team) | **Umfang:** 12–20 h/Woche (Part-time) | **Start:** asap

If you're eager to actively dive into sales, take on responsibility, and witness firsthand how modern AI-driven sales processes are established, then read on.

### Responsibilities

**🚀 What awaits you**

*   **Operational Sales:** You support the entire sales cycle – from lead research to preparing for closures.
*   **Research & Outreach:** You identify potential clients (tax firms) and initiate contact with them.
*   **CRM & Follow-ups:** You maintain our sales data in Pipedrive and ensure no lead is left behind.
*   **Sales Execution:** You attend appointments, create follow-ups, and keep communication flowing.
*   **AI-driven Sales:** You work with tools like GPT, Loom, and Pipedrive – no outdated sales, but a modern workflow with AI.
*   **Hands-on Team Building:** You experience live how a sales high-performance team is built from the ground up.

### Qualifications

**🧠 What you bring**

*   Enrolled student (Business Administration, Business Informatics, Tax, or similar).
*   Interest in sales, communication, and economic processes.
*   You are communicative, reliable, and proactive – no script-based selling, but genuine understanding.
*   Structured way of working and a desire for measurable results.
*   Nice-to-have: initial experience with CRM systems (Pipedrive) or AI tools.
*   Bonus: Basic understanding of accounting or tax procedures.
