### Description\nWe are a leading company in the trade and processing of precious metals, placing the highest value on the quality of our products and services. We are looking for an engaged employee (m/f/d) to join our Quality Assurance team, someone passionate about quality and continuous improvement. This is an exciting and varied role within a dynamic, family-run company, offering a long-term perspective, a motivated team, and attractive social benefits.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Support in complaint processing and root cause analysis.\n- Maintenance and administration of testing and measuring equipment.\n- Conducting sampling and statistical evaluations.\n- Contribution to the continuous improvement of product and service quality.\n- Collaboration with other departments to optimize processes and results.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed technical or commercial education, ideally with experience in quality assurance.\n- Initial experience in complaint processing and handling of testing and measuring equipment is an advantage.\n- Good analytical thinking and a structured way of working.\n- Team player, strong communication skills, and a high sense of responsibility.\n- Independent work style and willingness for continuous further education.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role offers a fantastic opportunity for an individual with a passion for quality to make a significant impact in a reputable, family-run business. You'll gain hands-on experience in critical quality assurance processes, work collaboratively across departments, and benefit from a supportive environment that values continuous learning and long-term career development. If you're detail-oriented, analytical, and eager to contribute to excellence, this is an excellent path to grow your QA career.
