### Description\nSK tes Sustainable Battery Solutions GmbH, part of the global SK Group, is a leading international corporation dedicated to sustainable development and the circular economy. Operating in over 20 countries, our core business focuses on the sustainable reuse and lifecycle management of IT hardware, complemented by innovative solutions for recycling lithium-ion batteries from electric vehicles. We work with global automotive manufacturers, battery OEMs, and government organizations, driven by sustainability as the foundation of our actions.\n\nWe are seeking a proactive Financial Controller (m/f/d) who loves numbers and is eager to actively shape our financial strategy. In this pivotal role, you will be responsible for ensuring accurate financial statements, conducting insightful cash flow analyses, and developing efficient financial processes for both SBS GmbH and its Dutch subsidiary. Join us in making a significant contribution to resource conservation and reducing environmental impact.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Preparation of monthly and annual financial statements for SBS GmbH and the Dutch subsidiary SBS BV\n*   Ensure accurate financial accounting and compliance with all reporting requirements under IFRS and local standards\n*   Preparation of regular cash flow analyses and forecasts\n*   VAT reporting to tax authorities\n*   Collaboration with external auditors and the corporate headquarters for audited financial statements\n*   Execution of annual budget planning, financial analyses, and feasibility studies to support strategic decisions\n*   Monitoring of corporate governance guidelines and financial processes\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Degree in business administration with a focus on controlling/accounting or commercial training with further qualification as a controller (m/f/d)\n*   At least 5 years of professional experience in controlling, including 3 years in a responsible position in an international industrial environment\n*   In-depth knowledge of IFRS, local accounting principles, and audits\n*   Extensive experience in German tax law and group reporting\n*   Ideally a qualification such as ACA, CIMA, or ACCA\n*   Fluent English skills, both written and spoken\n*   A strong analytical and structured working style, combined with high initiative and team orientation\n*   Empathetic communication skills to clearly convey ideas and build trusting relationships with stakeholders\n*   Resilient and able to deliver excellent results even under time pressure in a dynamic environment\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a compelling opportunity for an experienced Financial Controller to join an international company at the forefront of sustainable development and the circular economy. The role offers significant responsibility and the chance to directly impact strategic financial decisions within a global context. SK tes emphasizes a supportive, appreciative, and international team culture, complemented by attractive benefits such as flexible working hours, remote work options, and strong development opportunities. Candidates should highlight their experience with international financial standards (IFRS), German tax law, and group reporting. Be prepared to indicate your earliest possible start date and salary expectations in your application.
