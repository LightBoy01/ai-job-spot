We’re seeking a future team member for the role of **Senior Vice President, Application Delivery Services** to join our Fund Services team. This role is in Wroclaw, Poland .

**In this role, you’ll make an impact in the following ways:**

*   Direct teams responsible for supporting accounting applications globally
*   Partner closely with Platform SDG Leads (Registered Funds, ETF & Insurance Acct Services) to achieve common OKR’s
*   Create a globally consistent structure and model which aligns with our Fund Services strategy, co-locating teams in SGLs as much as is practicable
*   Achieve consistency in support models for each primary application (e.g., Eagle, InvestOne)
*   Introduce a metrics-based approach to measuring the functions performance 
*   Identify efficiency opportunities leveraging AI & Digital Tools to streamline processes and automate repetitive non-value add work
*   Enhance the apps ‘user experience’ through the creation of feedback loops, identifying user knowledge gaps and trends
*   Manage financial and human resources for the unit and ensure assigned teams operate effectively to meet unit goals and objectives.
*   Recruit, direct, motivate, and develop staff to maximize individual contributions, professional growth, and effective teamwork.

**To be successful in this role, we’re seeking the following:**

*   Bachelor’s degree in accounting / business / data or the equivalent combination of education and experience.
*   Engineering background with experience managing core Accounting applications
*   7-10 years of total work experience with at least 2-3 years in management preferred.
