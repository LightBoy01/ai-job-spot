### About the Role

We're hiring Sales Representatives to join our fast-growing sales team. You’ll report to the Inside Sales Team Lead and play a key role in growing our customer base across industries and regions. 

This is an entry point into tech sales, with a focus on qualifying inbound leads, running discovery calls, and setting up high-quality opportunities for our Account Executives.

You’ll be the first human contact many prospects have with our company — so your ability to understand their needs and communicate our value will shape the entire sales experience. It’s a great role for someone who wants to learn fast, gain exposure to the full go-to-market motion, and be part of a category-defining company in the AI space.

**The role is based in Paris, in a coworking space, with a hybrid work model (mix of in-office and remote).**  
  
**Responsibilities**

*   Qualify inbound leads and identify high-potential prospects
*   Conduct discovery calls to understand business needs and map them to product capabilities
*   Collaborate with Account Executives to move qualified leads through the sales funnel
*   Manage outreach using tools like HubSpot, LinkedIn, and email sequences
*   Book product demos and hand off warm leads to the sales team
*   Maintain accurate records of activity and pipeline progress
*   Support broader go-to-market initiatives with feedback, ideas, and hustle

**Requirements**

*   A recent graduate or early-career professional eager to break into SaaS or AI sales
*   Curious, driven, and excited about working in a fast-paced, high-growth startup
*   Comfortable speaking with technical and non-technical stakeholders alike
*   Able to clearly communicate value and tailor messaging to different industries
*   Organized, self-motivated, and persistent — with strong follow-up skills
