**Responsibilities**

*   **Requirements Capture & Definition**

*   **Lead the elicitation and documentation of business, user, and system requirements (functional and non-functional).**
*   **Translate operational needs and business drivers into clear, verifiable requirements.**
*   **Ensure requirements are unambiguous, complete, and aligned with programme objectives.**

*   **Requirements Management & Traceability**

*   **Maintain a centralised requirements baseline within approved tools (e.g., DOORS, Jama).**
*   **Ensure traceability between requirements, design, testing, and acceptance criteria.**
*   **Track changes, manage impacts, and support configuration control of requirements.**

*   **Stakeholder Engagement**

*   **Collaborate with Defence users, programme managers, architects, and suppliers to agree and validate requirements.**
*   **Act as a key interface between business stakeholders and technical delivery teams.**
*   **Support workshops, reviews, and decision boards to achieve requirements consensus.**

*   **Assurance & Compliance**

*   **Ensure requirements adhere to MOD standards, JSPs, and the Common Architecture Approach (CAA).**
*   **Support audits, reviews, and assurance activities, providing evidence of requirements compliance.**
*   **Assist in the definition of acceptance criteria and support verification and validation activities.**

*   **Change & Risk Management**

*   **Support impact assessments of change requests against baselined requirements.**
*   **Identify and escalate risks, gaps, or conflicts in requirements early in the lifecycle.**
*   **Ensure programme governance boards have clear and accurate requirements information.**
