### Description
MR.KNOW is a specialist in Business Process Management Software (BPMS), which has been developed since 1996. Our solutions are aimed at Citizen Developers in corporations and large enterprises, with the mission of enabling solutions without programming. The entire MR.KNOW team is driven by the vision of making process management tangible for everyone.

As a Graphic Design Intern (m/f/d) at MR.KNOW, you will have the unique opportunity to work within an established and innovative company, actively shaping the graphic design area during your internship. We encourage you to bring in your own ideas and deliver real value to our company. This role offers deep insights and significant design opportunities, providing tasks that align with your interests and foster your professional development, all within a flexible work environment with direct mentoring from our Marketing department.

### Responsibilities
*   Create presentation templates and prepare graphics and charts.
*   Execute various graphic design tasks, such as creating flyers and roll-ups.
*   Visualize ideas and concepts in the form of wireframes and mockups.
*   Graphically implement marketing campaigns.
*   Support our development team with new features.

### Qualifications
*   Fluent German (minimum C1 level).
*   Unlimited work permit in the EU.
*   Currently enrolled as a student at a German university.
*   Interest in software products.
*   Independent and structured way of working.
*   Excellent PowerPoint skills.
*   Initial experience with design tools like Figma.
