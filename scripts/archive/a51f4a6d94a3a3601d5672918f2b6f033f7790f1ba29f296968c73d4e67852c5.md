**MicroBIM** is a technology company delivering innovative software solutions for the Architecture, Engineering, and Construction (AEC) industry. We’re driven by quality, collaboration, and the goal of transforming how AEC professionals work.
We are currently seeking a **Senior AI Engineer** to join our team and help us build intelligent solutions that push the boundaries of what’s possible in the AEC sector.
**Responsibilities:**
*   Develop AI-driven tools for geometry manipulation, parametric modeling, and generative design in construction applications.
*   Implement machine learning algorithms to enhance efficiency, accuracy, and automation in design processes.
*   Integrate computer vision and deep learning techniques for analyzing and optimizing construction models.
*   Work with large-scale construction datasets to build AI models for predictive analysis and automation.
*   Collaborate with engineers, architects, and construction professionals to develop customized AI solutions.
*   Optimize geometry processing algorithms for real-time and large-scale construction data.
*   Stay updated with advancements in AI, computational design, and automation to introduce innovative solutions in the construction industry.
**Required Qualifications:**
*   Bachelor’s or master’s degree in computer science, Engineering, Mathematics, or a related field (ITI graduates are a plus).
*   Has 4+ years of experience working in AI or related fields.
*   Strong proficiency in C# and Python, with experience in developing computational geometry algorithms.
*   Experience with machine learning frameworks (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn).
*   Knowledge of 3D modeling and parametric design tools such as Autodesk Revit, Rhino, or Grasshopper.
*   Experience in geometry optimization and feature recognition.
*   Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work in a multidisciplinary team.
**Preferred Qualifications:**
*   Experience in Reinforcement Learning, Evolutionary Algorithms, or Generative AI for construction automation.
*   Familiarity with cloud-based AI solutions and BIM automation workflows.
*   Knowledge of robotic process automation (RPA) and digital twin technology in construction.
*   Understanding of graph-based and computational design workflows.
**What We Offer:**
*   Opportunity to work on cutting-edge AI-driven automation in the construction industry.
*   Competitive salary and benefits package.
*   Collaborative and innovative work environment.
*   Professional development and career growth opportunities.
