**About our group:**
--------------------
We are a team of IT and Data professionals at Seagate, responsible for creating and implementing data-driven solutions to support business decisions and operations. Leveraging AI and ML, we enable real-time data analysis, predictive maintenance, anomaly detection, and process optimization. We collaborate with business and machine development teams to harness the full potential of Industry 4.0—real-time control, IoT, Big Data, Analytics, Automation, and Artificial Intelligence—to support the “Smart Factory” initiative and maximize operational excellence.
**About the role - you will:**
------------------------------
As a Software Developer - Analyst II, you will:
*   Work closely with machine developers and factory teams to acquire and process data from machine sensors and production lines.
*   Develop, implement, and support software solutions for data collection, integration, and analysis in a manufacturing environment.
*   Apply AI/ML techniques for anomaly detection, predictive maintenance, and early detection of equipment or process issues.
*   Build, tune, and deploy machine learning models for real-time and batch analysis of sensor and production data.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to design and implement experiments, validate hypotheses, and communicate findings.
*   Support and maintain existing software and analytics solutions, ensuring high quality and minimal disruption to operations.
*   Document technical designs and provide support for development and operations teams.
**About you:**
--------------
We are looking for candidates who:
*   English proficiency both in writing and speaking
*   Have strong programming skills (Python, C# required; Java or other languages a plus).
*   New graduates are also welcome to apply.
*   Are passionate about applying AI/ML to solve real-world manufacturing problems.
*   Demonstrate experience with AI/ML frameworks and libraries (e.g., TensorFlow, Keras, scikit-learn, PyTorch).
*   Are familiar with data acquisition from industrial sensors, IoT devices, or factory automation systems.
*   Have hands-on experience with data preprocessing, model training, evaluation, and deployment.
*   Are comfortable with Unix/Linux environments and version control (Git).
*   Possess strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
*   Are self-motivated, independent, and effective in cross-functional teams.
*   Hold a bachelor’s or master’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, or related fields.
**Your experience includes:**
-----------------------------
*   \[Preferred\] Experience with anomaly detection, predictive maintenance, or root cause analysis in manufacturing.
*   \[Preferred\] Experience with big data platforms and real-time data processing.
