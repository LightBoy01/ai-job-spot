**DNB Corporate Banking**

**Analyst to Customer Analysis Section Energy**

**Do you want to drive the energy transition forward?**

**As an Analyst in CAS Energy, you’ll work closely with the largest companies in the energy sector and help shape the future of the industry.**

**Why join us?**

*   **Immediate impact:** Take on real responsibility from day one and influence major decisions for our clients.
*   **Teamwork & learning:** Collaborate in cross-functional teams with both experienced experts and young talents. You’ll learn from the best and build a strong professional network.
*   **Exciting projects:** Dive into projects within oil and gas, renewable energy, technology, and sustainability. Gain unique insight into how world-leading companies are tackling the energy transition.
*   **Supportive culture:** We offer an inclusive, social, and flexible work environment. We value curiosity, initiative, and support your professional and personal growth.

**About the role**

You’ll be part of a global team of ~80 people in DNB’s Energy Division, covering all major industry segments - oil & gas, renewables, and the energy sources of the future. The Customer Analysis Section (CAS) in Oslo consists of ~18 analysts with diverse backgrounds, working closely with client managers and advisors across the bank.

**As an Analyst, you will:**

*   Analyze customers’ financial and non-financial risk, including ESG risk and how sustainability trends are shaping industries.
*   Drive processes, prepare, and present credit memos for decision makers.
*   Prepare market analyses on various industry segments, sharing insights into customer markets, challenges, and opportunities.
*   Work in client teams to deliver the best financing solutions.

**What we’re looking for**

*   Are you curious, analytical, and ready to make a difference? We want to hear from you if you have:
*   Preferably 3 years or more of relevant experience (accounting, finance, energy, or similar)
*   Solid understanding of and interest in financial modelling and analysis
*   Strong collaboration and communication skills
*   Higher education at master’s degree level with strong results
*   Fluency in Norwegian and English

**What you’ll get**

*   Fast-track career development in the energy sector
*   The chance to work on real challenges that matter for society
*   A workplace that values work-life balance - even when things get busy

**Ready to join us?**
