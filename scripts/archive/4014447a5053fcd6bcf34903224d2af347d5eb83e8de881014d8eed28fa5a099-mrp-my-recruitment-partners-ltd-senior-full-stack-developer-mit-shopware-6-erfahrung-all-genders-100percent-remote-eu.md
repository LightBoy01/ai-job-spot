### Description
Are you seeking a role where transparent communication, clean coding, and high-quality work are not just buzzwords, but core principles? This ambitious e-commerce agency, a team of around 35 passionate individuals, is actively seeking a **Senior Full Stack Developer with a strong focus on Backend** development. Dedicated to shaping the future of digital shopping, the company supports a diverse portfolio of market-leading online shops and is committed to making the internet a more beautiful, faster, and better place.

Forget the 'Monday morning blues' – this role offers 100% remote work, fostering a hands-on, structured, and solution-oriented approach with an emphasis on technical automation and efficiency. You'll find no process chaos here, thanks to efficient digital documentation and streamlined workflows. The company champions a strong team spirit, promotes continuous feedback, and invests in its employees through ongoing professional development and individual goal and compensation systems (including perks like a BahnCard or WalkingPad). You'll engage with a diverse client portfolio, including major brands like Koro, Zalando, and FC Moto, and have the flexibility to define your level of client interaction. They believe in empowering their team, offering constant learning opportunities, and maintaining a vibrant, supportive culture.

### Responsibilities
As a Senior Full Stack Developer with a Backend focus, your key responsibilities will include:

*   Configuring and continuously developing complex Shopware projects.
*   Working across both backend (Python, PHP/Symfony, Git) and frontend (JavaScript, Vue.js, React) technologies.
*   Taking technical ownership within projects and collaborating closely with project managers.
*   Developing innovative ideas and solutions specifically for the e-commerce sector.
*   Actively exploring and learning modern and emerging web technologies to keep solutions cutting-edge.

### Qualifications
To be successful in this role, you should possess:

*   A minimum of 4-5 years of practical experience in full-stack development, with a clear focus on backend.
*   At least 2 years of hands-on experience with Shopware 6.
*   Proficiency in German language at a C1 level or higher.
*   Solid knowledge of PHP (Symfony) and ideally, Python.
*   Proven experience with JavaScript frameworks such as Vue.js or React.
*   Routine in using Git for version control.
*   A strong sense of responsibility and commitment to quality.
*   A solution-oriented and highly independent working style.
*   A Bachelor's degree or equivalent vocational training (Fachinformatiker) in fields like Mathematics, Physics, or Computer Science.
