**SUMMARY**  

At Wiz, we believe great enablement is a growth engine. Our GTM Excellence team supports the learning and development of our Go to Market organization — and we’re looking for a strategic, creative, and AI-curious leader to help us scale. 

As a **Senior Manager,** **Learning Experience Designer**, you’ll lead the curriculum design and execution of impactful learning strategies for our Wiz Revenue team. You’ll collaborate across teams to build tailored, relevant, and engaging experiences — from onboarding to role mastery — using the latest in instructional design and AI-powered tools. 

**WHAT YOU’LL DO** 

**Learning Strategy & Curriculum Design** 

*   Develop and own the learning strategy for multiple roles across the GTM organization
*   Lead end-to-end curriculum design, from onboarding to advanced enablement, grounded in adult learning theory and business priorities
*   Conduct needs assessments to identify gaps and create learning journeys that drive real outcomes 

**Instructional Design & Content Development** 

*   Design and develop blended learning experiences — including eLearning, instructor-led sessions, simulations, job aids, and more
*   Apply visual storytelling and modern instructional techniques to create compelling, accessible content
*   Leverage AI to personalize learning experiences and streamline content creation 

**AI & Innovation** 

*   Use generative AI and learning agents to build scalable, intelligent workflows and enhance learner engagement
*   Stay on the pulse of new technologies, tools, and approaches to continuously evolve our enablement approach 

**Stakeholder Partnership** 

*   Collaborate closely with leadership, product experts, and GTM program managers to align learning with team goals
*   Translate business needs into actionable learning plans with measurable impact 

**Measurement & Iteration** 

*   Define success metrics and collect feedback to improve learning experiences
*   Continuously refine programs based on engagement, performance, and business outcomes  

**WHAT YOU’LL BRING** 

*   8+ years in a learning experience, instructional design, or enablement role — ideally supporting technical or pre-sales teams
*   Proven experience in New Product Release and Product enablement 
*   5+ years of experience building and managing a team of LXD professionals
*   Expertise in adult learning principles and curriculum development
*   Proficiency with AI and learning tools (e.g. ChatGPT, prompt engineering, agents, Articulate Rise, Camtasia, etc.)
*   Familiarity with LMS/CMS systems and modern training formats like microlearning and simulations
*   Strong project management, visual design, and communication skills
*   A collaborative spirit and a builder’s mindset — excited to experiment, iterate, and make learning magical at scale
