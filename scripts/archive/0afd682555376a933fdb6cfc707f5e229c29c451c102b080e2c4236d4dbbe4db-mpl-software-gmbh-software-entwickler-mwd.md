With over 300 active corporate clients and more than 12,000 users across various industries, we know from experience: the sustainable success of CRM and CPQ projects stems from the interplay of people, processes, and solutions – mpl.\n\nOur clients rely on reliability, expertise, and precision. Team spirit, trust, commitment, and enthusiasm define our daily collaboration, both internally and externally. If these values are just as important to you, you'll fit in perfectly.\n\nJoin our team as a Software Developer and collaborate with us to develop modern solutions that make our customers more successful in the long term, either remotely or flexibly on-site. We especially welcome applicants who have already gained initial professional experience and are eager to develop professionally and personally.\n\nAs a Software Developer, you will conceptualize and develop high-quality software solutions, both independently and as part of a team. You will have high standards for code quality and work according to modern development principles to create maintainable, performant, and testable applications. With your expertise, you will actively contribute to the technical evolution of our solutions, enabling our customers to achieve long-term successful CRM and CPQ processes.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop new features and enhance our existing software solutions.\n- Write modular, performant, and testable Clean Code.\n- Analyze requirements and perform technical conception.\n- Collaborate closely with Product Owners and the development team.\n- Support architectural decisions and technological advancements.\n- Actively participate in code reviews and uphold quality standards.\n- Contribute to the continuous improvement of our development processes.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed education or a degree in software/application development or a comparable qualification.\n- First to several years of practical experience with TypeScript, JavaScript, SQL, and REST web services.\n- Ability to analyze technical problems, approach them structurally, and solve them in a solution-oriented manner.\n- Independent, reliable, and structured way of working.\n- Strong customer orientation and enjoyment of teamwork.\n- Openness to new technologies and motivation for continuous professional development.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a Software Developer with initial to several years of experience looking to make a tangible impact on customer success.
