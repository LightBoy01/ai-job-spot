### Description
Are you ready for the next step in IT support? At accompio, you'll work in a highly motivated team, support reputable clients with exciting digitalization topics, and actively shape the future.

accopio is a private equity-backed IT corporate group, forming a strong foundation with around 700 specialists across 20 locations in Germany, Hungary, and Bulgaria. Together, we pursue one mission: to build a powerful expertise hub that opens up new perspectives for our clients and employees.

From IT infrastructure and cloud solutions to DevOps and cyber security, we support companies in their digitalization journey and make their processes future-proof. We rely on modern technologies such as container platforms, public cloud services, next-gen security platforms, and infrastructure automation to create scalable systems with the highest quality and performance.

We emphasize strong collaboration where everyone can actively shape accompio's future. We are currently looking for an IT Support Specialist (m/f/d) in full-time for our Kempten or Lindau locations, working in a hybrid model.

### Responsibilities
*   Qualify incoming support requests and handle initial processing – both technically and organizationally.
*   Independently process standard changes according to defined processes.
*   Reliably resolve simple incidents yourself and escalate complex cases to specialized departments.
*   Monitor monitoring systems and initiate first measures when necessary.
*   Document and communicate clearly and service-orientedly in the ticketing system.

### Qualifications
*   Fluent in German (C1) and good English (B2).
*   Completed IT training (e.g., IT specialist or IT systems electronics technician) and initial experience in a service desk.
*   Technical understanding of IT infrastructure (e.g., virtualization, backup, monitoring, firewalls).
*   Familiarity with Windows servers and ideally experience with ITSM tools like AutoTask.
*   Familiarity with ITIL processes or even an ITIL® Foundation certification.
*   Structured, service-oriented, and reliable work style – even with a high volume of tickets.
*   Friendly, clear, and solution-oriented communication – both on the phone and in writing.
*   Eagerness to learn and develop professionally in the long term.
