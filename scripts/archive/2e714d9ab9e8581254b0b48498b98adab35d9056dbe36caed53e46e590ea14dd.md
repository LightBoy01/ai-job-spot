**What you get to do every day:**  
*   Manage and publish engaging content across web, email, and social channels to keep our brand voice consistent and compelling.
*   Audit, edit, and standardize creative assets, copy, and metadata to ensure a seamless customer experience.
*   Monitor campaign performance metrics (CTR, engagement, conversions) and adjust strategies to maximize results
*   Maintain and update 360 content calendars and dashboards to track deadlines, campaign performance, and publishing accuracy
*   Work closely with marketing, creative, and merchandising teams to support seasonal campaigns, product launches, and promotions.
*   Provide insights and recommendations to refine creative strategy, ensuring data-driven decisions are made on what content performs and why
*   Coordinate asset delivery and ensure content accuracy during peak promotional periods when it matters most.
*   Explore and contribute to the adoption of AI tools in digital marketing, enhancing efficiency and campaign performance
  
  
**What you bring to the role:**  
*   3+ years of experience in digital marketing, content coordination, or publishing.
*   Strong attention to detail with a focus on content accuracy, brand alignment, and consistency.
*   Ability to multitask and manage competing priorities in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.
*   A balance of creativity and analytical mindset - able to interpret performance data, spot trends, and recommend changes
*   Proficiency with Content Management Systems (CMS) and collaboration tools; experience with design or analytics platforms is a plus.
*   Bonus: Experience with design platforms (Canva, Figma, Photoshop) and analytics tools; familiarity with AI-driven marketing tools is a plus
