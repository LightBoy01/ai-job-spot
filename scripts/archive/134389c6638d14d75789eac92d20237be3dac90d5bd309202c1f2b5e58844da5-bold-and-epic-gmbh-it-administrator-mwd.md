### Description
BOLD & EPIC is more than a holding company; we are a network of doers united by passion and innovation to shape the future together. We value flexible working models, personal collaboration, and smart solutions. We are currently seeking an IT Administrator (m/f/d) to join our team, someone who will actively develop our internal IT landscape, co-design IT security and data protection, and technically empower our entire organization.

### Responsibilities
- Administer and further develop our tool and system landscape, with a focus on modern SaaS solutions such as Google Workspace, Notion, and Asana.
- Set up accounts, roles, groups, and permissions to ensure clear and secure structures.
- Guide technical onboarding and offboarding processes for smooth transitions.
- Provide friendly, helpful, and solution-oriented 1st- & 2nd-Level IT support to our team.
- Continuously evolve our IT security and data protection structures, managing security policies and access concepts.
- Collaborate closely with data protection officers and external partners to consistently strengthen our security level.
- Manage our Endpoint Security and Mobile Device Management.
- Handle hardware procurement, installation, and administration, maintaining clean asset and license management.

### Qualifications
- You possess a relevant education, degree, or demonstrable experience in the IT field.
- You have 2-4 years of experience in IT administration or technical support.
- You are familiar with modern cloud tools (SaaS), ideally Google Workspace.
- You have a strong understanding of IT security, data protection (GDPR/DSGVO), and secure IT processes.
- You work independently, in a structured manner, and are service-oriented.
- You have a quick grasp of technical topics and tools.
- You communicate fluently in German and well in English.
- You are willing to work regularly on-site in Stuttgart; hybrid work options are always available.
