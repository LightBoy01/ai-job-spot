### Description\nAre you looking to kickstart new battery storage projects? Then join a strong renewable energy company that implements solar and wind projects nationwide. At the company's Hanover location, tomorrow's projects are being developed.\n\nAs a **Project Manager (m/f/d) Battery Storage with a focus on Approval Planning**, you will be an important part of this pioneering vision, guiding new mega-energy storage projects from the planning concept to the granting of approval – for the common goal of a greener future.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Be responsible for the approval planning of battery storage projects.\n- Participate in the planning concept and prepare application documents in coordination with authorities.\n- Accompany the entire project from the application process to post-demand management.\n- Commission expert opinions, prepare reports, and evaluate project risks.\n- Present projects to municipalities, planning authorities, and stakeholders.\n- Create a condition register and monitor the work of specialist departments.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in Renewable Energies, Environmental Sciences, Industrial Engineering, Administrative Law, or a comparable field.\n- Experience in a comparable position in renewable energies, for example, as a Project Manager for Wind Energy and/or Photovoltaics.\n- Knowledge of the Federal Immission Control Act (BImSchG), building planning law, building law, and related legal areas.\n- Practical experience in the application of project management tools.\n- Very good German and good English language skills.\n- Proficient in MS Office and possibly ELIA.\n- Ability to work reliably both independently and collaboratively in a team.\n- Possess a driver's license and willingness to travel for business.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a pivotal Mid-Senior Project Manager role for an individual passionate about driving the green energy transition through large-scale battery storage projects. The company offers a supportive, flexible environment with excellent benefits, focusing on continuous learning and a strong team culture. If you thrive on navigating complex regulatory landscapes and contributing to a sustainable future, this opportunity in Hanover is worth exploring.
