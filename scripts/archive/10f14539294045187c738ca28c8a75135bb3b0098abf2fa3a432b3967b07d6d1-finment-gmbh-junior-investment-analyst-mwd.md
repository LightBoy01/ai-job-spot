### Description\nJoin FinMent, a rapidly growing, twice government-certified FinTech startup in Berlin, on our mission to democratize institutional investment knowledge and access. We leverage innovative technology, verified financial expertise, and exclusive strategies previously reserved for large institutional investors. As part of our team, you'll play a significant role in empowering individuals to achieve financial freedom. We invest heavily in your personal and professional development, offering a unique opportunity to use our premium products for free (valued up to €36,000 annually) to build your own wealth. If you're passionate about finance and ready to kick-start your career in a dynamic, profitable startup, we want to hear from you!\n\n### Responsibilities\nYou will be actively involved in the analysis, development, and implementation of financial and investment content, while also providing holistic support to our customers and community. You'll have the autonomy to bring your own ideas to life and continuously improve our offerings.\n-   **Market Analysis:** Analyze securities and current market conditions to generate investment ideas and support our trading team and clients.\n-   **Client & Community Engagement:** Serve as the first point of contact, interacting with clients and our investor community, assisting them with questions and challenges in stock trading.\n-   **Customer Consulting:** Develop and manage campaigns focused on customer retention, referrals, and overall satisfaction.\n-   **Content Creation:** Produce simple yet in-depth educational videos on personal finance, investment strategies, and the stock market.\n-   **System Development & Innovation:** Contribute to the continuous development of our trading system, conceptualize and implement new investment/trading strategies, and realize your own innovative ideas.\n-   **Financial Education Advancement:** Partner with us to elevate financial education to a new standard.\nWe are committed to your individual training, fostering your development step-by-step, and guiding you to become an expert, aiming for a long-term collaboration.\n\n### Qualifications\nWe are looking for individuals with a high level of dedication, a strong thirst for financial knowledge, and a desire to evolve into a specialist in the finance sector.\n-   **Experience:** 6 months to 1 year of practical customer support experience, ideally gained through internships or similar roles.\n-   **Passion & Drive:** A genuine enthusiasm for this job, strong self-motivation, and a passion for clean, agile process and project management.\n-   **Startup Mindset:** Proven willingness to take ownership and responsibility for your domain, even when stepping outside your comfort zone. You're results-oriented, proactive, and quick to implement solutions.\n-   **Continuous Learner:** Eager to learn and develop, both professionally within your role and sector, and personally. You actively seek feedback and always keep team success in mind.\n-   **Analytical & Structured:** Possess excellent analytical and structural skills to thoroughly think through scenarios beforehand and implement solutions in a structured manner.\n-   **Language Skills:** Ideally native-level German proficiency and good conversational English.\n-   **Tools:** Practical working experience with common financial and charting software (e.g., TWS, Tradingview) is a plus.\nDon't be deterred if you don't meet every single requirement. If you have a strong desire to work with us, we encourage you to apply!\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an ambitious individual with a foundational understanding of finance and a passion for customer engagement to join a rapidly expanding FinTech leader.
