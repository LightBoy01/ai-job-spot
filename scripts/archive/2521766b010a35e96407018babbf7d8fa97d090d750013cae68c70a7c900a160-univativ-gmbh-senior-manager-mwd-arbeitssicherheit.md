### Description
univativ is a dynamic project and personnel service provider dedicated to connecting talented students and graduates with exciting career opportunities at renowned companies. Our core mission revolves around fostering your professional development and empowering your career journey.

We are currently seeking a motivated "Manager (m/f/d)" for Occupational Safety and Health Protection to join our client, a successful and well-established company in the food industry, located in Schladen-Werla. This is a permanent position, offering an engaging scope of responsibilities where you can significantly contribute and deepen your expertise. This role is ideal for an individual with an engineering background who is eager to apply their knowledge in a responsible environment and actively shape our client's safety culture.

In this pivotal role, you will be instrumental in implementing comprehensive occupational safety and health measures, actively participating in audits, and collaborating closely with a diverse range of internal and external stakeholders. Beyond immediate implementation, you will strategically plan and coordinate essential training and further education programs, ensuring that safety and health principles are deeply embedded within the company's long-term operational framework. If you are passionate about creating a secure and healthy work environment, we encourage you to apply and take the next step in your career!

### Responsibilities
As the "Manager" for Occupational Safety and Health Protection, your key responsibilities will include:

*   **Strategic Planning & Development**: Overseeing the planning, steering, and successful implementation of annual goals in occupational safety and health, thereby actively contributing to the continuous evolution and improvement of the safety culture.
*   **Reporting & Analysis**: Ensuring transparency through diligent reporting and in-depth analysis of relevant key performance indicators (KPIs), from which you will derive and implement effective, preventive safety measures.
*   **Coordination & Awareness**: Preparing and confidently moderating occupational safety committee meetings, and coordinating vital training and advanced education initiatives to strengthen safety and health awareness across the entire company.
*   **Risk Management & Standards**: Utilizing your keen eye for detail to develop thorough risk assessments, accompanying site inspections, and conducting practical instructions—always with the overarching goal of minimizing risks and continuously improving safety standards.
*   **Collaborative Partnerships**: Working in close collaboration with both internal departments and external partners to holistically conceptualize and successfully implement comprehensive occupational safety strategies.
*   **Emergency Preparedness**: Planning and meticulously evaluating emergency drills, ensuring that the organization is optimally prepared and capable of responding effectively in critical situations.
*   **Technical Expertise & Audits**: Contributing your specialized technical knowledge to the safety-related design and development of machinery and plant systems, and competently leading internal as well as external audits with both expertise and foresight.

### Qualifications
To thrive in this impactful role, candidates should possess the following qualifications:

*   **Educational Background**: A successfully completed degree in Mechanical Engineering, Process Engineering, Food Technology, or a comparable technical field.
*   **Specialized Training (Ideal)**: Ideally, you will have completed training as a Safety Specialist ("Fachkraft für Arbeitssicherheit"), preferably with demonstrable experience within an industrial environment.
*   **Professional Experience**: Initial professional experience of at least 6 months in the manufacturing industry, coupled with practical know-how in conducting risk assessments and engaging in project work.
*   **Interpersonal Skills**: Strong assertiveness and exceptional communication skills, enabling you to conduct engaging and practical training sessions and instructions with confidence.
*   **Language Proficiency**: Proficiency in English, both written and spoken, to effectively handle tasks such as translating accident reports or one-pagers into German.
*   **Work Ethic**: An analytical mindset, complemented by an independent, structured approach to work and a high sense of personal responsibility, which collectively round out your professional profile.
