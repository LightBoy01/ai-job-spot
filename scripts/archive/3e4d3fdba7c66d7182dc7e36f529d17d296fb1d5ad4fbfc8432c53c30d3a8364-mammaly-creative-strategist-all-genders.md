### Description
mammaly is on a mission for healthy and happy dogs! Founded in 2020, we develop premium pet supplements enabling long, happy lives, and we are already Germany's leading pet supplement startup. Our commitment to animal welfare drives us to innovate constantly. Since 2023, we are also a certified B Corp, upholding high social and ecological standards.

We are seeking a creative and analytically-minded **Creative Strategist / Content Strategist** to join our Performance Marketing team. You will leverage a strong sense for brand communication, understand data, and develop creative ideas that drive performance across platforms like Meta, TikTok, YouTube, and Google. You'll collaborate closely with designers, copywriters, and performance marketing managers to ensure our campaigns not only look great but also deliver measurable results, driving conversions, improving ROAS, and contributing to successful growth strategies.

### Responsibilities
*   Develop data-driven, targeted creative concepts for our performance campaigns.
*   Write impactful creative briefs and collaborate closely with design, copy, and media teams.
*   Analyze the performance of our creatives and derive concrete optimization suggestions.
*   Conduct A/B tests on hooks, visuals, and CTAs to elevate creative effectiveness.
*   Manage the entire ad creative production process, from concept to final asset.
*   Ensure content aligns with brand guidelines and achieves key performance indicators (KPIs).
*   Collaborate with colleagues from Marketing, Brand, and Product to execute holistic campaigns.
*   Present concepts and results persuasively to internal stakeholders.

### Qualifications
*   3-5 years of experience in Creative Strategy, Performance Marketing, or digital advertising, ideally within E-commerce, FMCG, or DTC.
*   Proven track record of developing successful creatives that drove clicks, conversions, and revenue.
*   Strong proficiency with paid media platforms such as Meta, TikTok, YouTube, and Google Ads.
*   Data-driven mindset with the ability to make creative decisions based on analytics.
*   Fluent communication skills in both German and English (written and verbal).
*   Excellent eye for visual storytelling and impactful copy.
*   Highly structured, independent work ethic, capable of managing multiple projects simultaneously.
*   Familiarity with tools like Google Analytics, Meta Ads Manager, and Creative Testing Frameworks.

### Author's Take
**Question 1: How does mammaly balance its rapid growth and performance-driven culture with its B Corp commitment to social and ecological standards?**

As an insider, I can tell you it's a constant, exhilarating challenge – but one we deeply embrace. Our B Corp status isn't just a badge; it's woven into our DNA. In performance marketing, for instance, we're not just chasing clicks; we're ensuring our messaging aligns with our ethical sourcing and sustainable practices. We actively seek creative concepts that not only convert but also genuinely communicate our commitment to animal well-being and environmental responsibility. It creates a more meaningful impact when your work drives both profit and purpose, and our customers truly resonate with that authenticity. You'll find a supportive environment where innovative ideas that push both boundaries are celebrated.

**Question 2: With such a strong emphasis on data and measurable results in this Creative Strategist role, how much creative freedom and experimentation is truly encouraged?**

That's a fantastic question, and it gets to the heart of what makes this role so exciting! While data is our compass – helping us understand what resonates and what doesn't – it absolutely doesn't stifle creativity; it supercharges it. Think of data as your ultimate feedback loop. You're given the freedom to hypothesize, design bold new creative concepts, and then rigorously test them. We're constantly asking, "What if we tried this hook? What if we told the story this way?" The expectation isn't just to *follow* the data, but to *interpret* it, learn from it, and then use those insights to craft even more innovative, higher-performing campaigns. It's a playground for creative minds who love to see their ideas not just look good, but truly move the needle. You'll be empowered to experiment, fail fast, and iterate your way to groundbreaking results.
