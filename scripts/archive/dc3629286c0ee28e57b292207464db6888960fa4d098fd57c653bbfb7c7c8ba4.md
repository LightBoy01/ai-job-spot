**Oliver Wyman’s global Learning & Development team is hiring a Learning Product Manager to design, deliver and scale high‑impact learning products for our Principal and Partner population. This role sits within Partner Development and will be based in London**
_Please Note: Oliver Wyman/MMC operates a hybrid working policy and we will require the successful candidate to work from the London Baker Street office at least 60% of the time._
**Why this role matters**
*   You will shape the learning experience for our most senior consulting talent — designing in‑person programmes, virtual live sessions, on‑demand curriculum, curated external courseware and blended solutions that drive performance and commercial outcomes.
*   You’ll work closely with Partners, the Partner Development Council, HR, designers, delivery specialists and technology teams to prioritise a portfolio of products, measure impact, and iterate quickly.
*   This is a high‑visibility role with direct influence on leadership capability across the firm.
**What you will be doing:**
*   Own a product roadmap for Partner & Principal learning that aligns to global L&D strategy and business/talent priorities.
*   Conducting needs analysis to identify learning gaps and shape evidence‑based solutions.
*   Collaborating with subject matter experts, designers and stakeholders to define objectives and create Partner‑level scenarios, simulations and coaching interventions.
*   Sourcing, evaluating and managing external content providers and faculty; set SLAs and quality standards tied to learner outcomes.
*   Measuring progression from learning to behaviour and business outcomes; using data to iterate.
*   Exploring AI‑enabled curation, personalization and coaching assistants responsibly; ensure data privacy and ethical use.
*   Running governance and prioritisation with Partner Development Council, HR and regional leaders.
*   Defining and managing end‑to‑end portfolio budget in partnership with Finance.
**What we are looking for you to bring:**
*   4–7 years’ experience in learning product management, organisational development or strategic HR consulting
*   Previous experience in management consulting professional services firm is expected
*   Proven experience designing and managing learning for senior audiences (executive/senior leadership) — ideally with simulations, coaching and peer learning
*   Experience of using AI
*   Strong project management skills and experience managing multiple programmes concurrently.
*   A track record in linking learning interventions to commercial and people outcomes, in a professional services context.
*   Confidence using data and analytics to measure impact and guide decisions.
*   Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills
*   Familiarity with LMS/LXP platforms, authoring tools and agile ways of working
