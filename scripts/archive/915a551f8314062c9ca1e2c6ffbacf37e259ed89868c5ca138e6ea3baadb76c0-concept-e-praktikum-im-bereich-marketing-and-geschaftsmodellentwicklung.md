### Description\nconcept-e is a dynamic young startup dedicated to developing cutting-edge energy systems for medium-sized businesses. Our mission is to empower companies on their journey towards a climate-friendly energy supply. As an intern, you will play a crucial role, combining engaging content creation and marketing tasks with valuable insights into sales and market development.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Customer Acquisition and Market Analysis:** Support in acquiring new customers and conducting comprehensive analysis of market potentials and competitor strategies.\n- **Sales Funnel Design:** Actively contribute to designing and optimizing the sales funnel, from initial contact to conversion. You will learn to craft targeted and needs-based customer engagement strategies.\n- **Communication Strategy Development:** Structure and maintain our essential communication materials, ensuring consistency and clarity.\n- **Marketing Material Preparation:** Develop and design compelling marketing materials (for LinkedIn, website, etc.) that effectively communicate our brand and sustainable mission.\n- **Support in Strategic Decisions:** Conduct research and provide valuable input for current sustainable, cost-effective energy solutions for medium-sized manufacturing companies, as well as contribute to general strategic decisions for the company.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Field of Study:** Currently pursuing a degree in an economics-related field such as Business Administration, Business Psychology, Marketing, Sales, Management, or a comparable course of study.\n- **Interest in Sustainability:** A genuine passion for climate protection and sustainable development. At concept-e, sustainability is not just a buzzword, but a core conviction that drives us.\n- **Analytical Skills:** Demonstrated ability to research, analyze, and clearly present complex information in an understandable manner.\n- **Strong Communication Skills:** Excellent verbal and written communication abilities for effective interaction with stakeholders and the creation of impactful marketing materials.\n- **Independence and Teamwork:** We seek an individual who can work autonomously while also thriving collaboratively within a team. Flexibility and openness to embrace new tasks are highly valued.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis internship at concept-e offers an exceptional opportunity for an aspiring professional passionate about sustainability to gain hands-on, impactful experience within an agile and innovative startup. You'll be deeply integrated into critical business development, marketing, and sales initiatives, receiving mentorship and direct feedback from experienced colleagues. The hybrid work model, combining a vibrant coworking space in Cologne with remote flexibility, underscores a commitment to work-life balance and individual growth. This is a chance to truly make a difference, contributing to a greener future while rapidly developing core business skills in a supportive environment.
