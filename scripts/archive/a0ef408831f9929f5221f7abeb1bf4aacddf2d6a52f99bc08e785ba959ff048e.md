**EY-Consulting –** **AI Enabled Automation** **–** **Senior  -** **Kore.AI**
We are in pursuit of a Conversational AI Expert with a track record of excellence in Kore.ai and Microsoft-based chatbot platforms, including Copilot Studio (formerly known as Power Virtual Agents). The ideal candidate will possess a robust portfolio demonstrating hands-on experience in crafting, evolving, and implementing chatbot applications that engage users in a natural and intuitive dialogue. The role demands not only technical prowess but also a keen analytical mind and the capacity to write sophisticated Python and GenAI code.
**Responsibilities:**
*   Design and develop advanced conversational AI solutions using Kore.ai, Microsoft chatbot platforms, and Copilot Studio.
*   Write and maintain high-quality Python and GenAI code.
*   Integrate chatbot applications with various APIs, backend systems, and cloud services, particularly Azure AI Services.
*   Work closely with cross-functional teams to gather user requirements and provide AI-driven conversational experiences.
*   Perform testing and debugging to ensure the highest level of functionality and user experience.
*   Keep up-to-date with the latest trends and advancements in conversational AI, cloud computing, and Azure AI Services.
*   Offer technical leadership and mentorship to junior developers.
**Requirements:**
*   Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, or related field.
*   Proven experience with Kore.ai and Microsoft chatbot platforms, including Copilot Studio.
*   Strong programming skills in Python and familiarity with GenAI code.
*   Understanding of Natural Language Processing (NLP) and machine learning techniques.
*   Experience with RESTful APIs and web services integration.
*   Good to have: Knowledge of cloud computing and Azure AI Services.
*   Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
*   Effective communication skills, both written and verbal.
*   Ability to work both independently and as part of a collaborative team.
*   3-5 years of Experience
This role offers an excellent opportunity for a seasoned professional who is passionate about AI technology and customer service innovation, and are ready to make a significant impact in a cutting-edge technology environment.
