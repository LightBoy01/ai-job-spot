Most Scrum Master roles keep you focused on ceremonies. Few give you the opportunity to help shape a brand-new team, embed agile ways of working from the ground up, and influence how product innovation comes to life. This role is perfect for someone who thrives in fast-moving, startup-like environments, loves supporting cross-functional teams, and sees agile as a mindset for delivering impact—not just a process to follow.  
  
**Responsibilities**  
*   **Establish Agile Practices**: Stand up team ceremonies and working agreements that fit a fast-moving, startup-style product environment.
*   **Facilitate Delivery**: Lead sprint planning, reviews, retrospectives, and daily stand-ups—keeping the team aligned, focused, and adaptable.
*   **Support the Team**: Remove blockers, shield the team from distractions, and help maintain sustainable pace and focus
*   **Guide Growth**: Coach the team on agile principles at a practical, team-focused level, ensuring practices evolve as the team mature
*   **Enable Collaboration**: Partner with product managers, engineers, business analysts, and designers to refine priorities and keep delivery tied to outcomes
*   **Drive Continuous Improvement**: Foster a culture of reflection, experimentation, and learning to improve team performance and product delivery.
  
  
**Qualifications**  
*   5+ years of experience as a Scrum Master (preferably in SaaS or product-focused companies).
*   Hands-on experience building agile practices in small, fast-growing, cross-functional teams.
*   Strong facilitation and communication skills—able to keep discussions focused, inclusive, and outcome-driven.
*   Practical understanding of agile frameworks (Scrum, Kanban, etc.) with the ability to adapt them pragmatically to context.
*   Track record of helping teams balance speed and quality in delivery.
*   Collaborative mindset, able to work across product, engineering, and business roles.
*   Experience working in regulated industries is preferred. Familiarity with healthcare or life sciences are a bonus.
*   Hands-on experience with AI-driven tools and a strong interest in exploring emerging technologies.
  
  
**Why You’ll Love It**  
*   **Impact:** You’ll help shape how a new product team works from the ground up, directly influencing how we deliver solutions that matter.
*   **Challenge + Purpose**: Work in a startup-style environment inside an established organization—fast-paced, dynamic, and meaningful.
*   **Growth:** Exposure to building agile practices in a new team
*   **Team:** Join a group of product managers, engineers, and analysts who value collaboration, curiosity, and outcomes over process for process’s sake.

If you’re a Scrum Master who asks not only “Are we doing agile right?” but also “How can this team work smarter to deliver real impact?”—you’ll thrive here.
