### Description

This company, with over a century of experience, is a cornerstone of the international energy and rail technology industry. They develop and produce electrical safety components, switchgear, and power supply systems used worldwide, spanning from the railway sector to renewable energies and industrial applications. Their unwavering focus on reliability, precision, and innovation has made them a valued partner to leading industrial and transport companies globally. Working here means actively shaping the energy and mobility solutions of tomorrow in an environment that empowers individuals with responsibility, fosters initiative, and truly embodies quality.

Join the team as a Project Manager (m/f/d) for Switchgear Construction!

**Location**: Rhine-Main area | Mobile working possible | **Start Date**: As per personal availability

### Responsibilities

As a Project Manager, you will take on a pivotal role with a broad range of responsibilities:

*   Assume overall responsibility for switchgear projects, from initial planning through to commissioning.
*   Technically and organizationally manage and monitor direct current (DC) systems.
*   Act as a crucial interface between customers in Germany and the international project team.
*   Coordinate technical alignments with design, engineering, and commissioning departments.
*   Award contracts to subcontractors and oversee customer acceptances both at the factory (CZ) and on-site.
*   Maintain a diligent focus on costs, deadlines, and quality throughout the project lifecycle.
*   Take ownership of customer, claim, and change management processes.

### Qualifications

To succeed in this role, you should bring a combination of technical expertise, project management experience, and soft skills:

*   You are a qualified Technician, Electrical Master, or Engineer with expertise in switchgear construction, protection technology, and potentially secondary technology.
*   You have proven experience in project leadership and the successful execution of customer projects.
*   You communicate clearly, negotiate confidently, and lead teams effectively with a strong overview in both German and English.
*   Contract management, claim management, and billing are familiar concepts to you.
*   You work independently, reliably, and maintain focus and composure even under pressure.
*   Knowledge of VOB, HOAI, and VDE standards would be ideal, but is not strictly mandatory.
*   Proficiency in using EPLAN, AutoCAD, and MS Project is required.
*   Basic knowledge of automation is also a plus.
