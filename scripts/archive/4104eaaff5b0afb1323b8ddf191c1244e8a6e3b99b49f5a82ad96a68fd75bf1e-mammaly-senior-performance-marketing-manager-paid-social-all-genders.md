### Description\nWe are mammaly! - and our mission is healthy and happy dogs! That's why we develop premium supplements for dogs that enable a long and good life. Founded in 2020, mammaly is already the leading Pet Supplement Startup in Germany. We don't rest on our laurels, because animal welfare is close to our hearts: We promote the health and quality of life of our dogs with innovative solutions. Furthermore, we are committed to upholding high social and ecological standards and have therefore been a certified B Corp company since 2023!\n\nPaid Social is one of our strongest growth engines and plays a central role in new customer acquisition for mammaly. For this, we are looking for a Paid Social Marketing Manager (m/f/d) who, with creative power, analytical precision, and deep performance marketing expertise, will elevate our campaigns to the next level, with a clear objective: to sustainably optimize and scalably reduce the Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC). You are a performance-oriented Paid Social professional who not only measures growth but actively shapes it. You are data-driven, love creative tests, are curious about new formats, and love to continuously optimize the funnel. If you want to take responsibility, enjoy making independent decisions, and want to achieve great things together with a strong team, then you are in the right place.\n\n### Responsibilities\n* Development, testing, and optimization of high-performance creatives (especially video & UGC), including ideation, briefing, storyboarding, and analysis of creative performance.\n* Independent scaling and management of our Paid Social campaigns on Meta with a focus on new customer acquisition for mammaly.\n* Continuous optimization of the entire funnel, from ad click to landing page to checkout, based on data-driven learnings.\n* Close collaboration with the Creative & Content Team, Direct Response Copy, and CRO to quickly realize new creative concepts and funnel tests and iteratively develop them further.\n* Competitive market and creative analyses, benchmarking, and trend research to always stay \"ahead of the curve\".\n\n### Qualifications\n**Required:**\n* At least 3 years of hands-on experience in Paid Social Advertising (Meta).\n* Expertise in Direct Response Marketing for D2C Subscriptions or E-Commerce.\n* Very strong analytical skills and confident handling of performance data (e.g., CAC, ROAS, LTV).\n* Experience in the conception and production of performance creatives (video & UGC); you can both develop ideas and clearly brief them.\n* Ability to work efficiently with the Creative Team and designers.\n* Proactive, solution-oriented, and independent way of working.\n* Team orientation and clear, transparent communication.\n* Fluent in German and English.\n\n**Preferred (Nice-to-Haves):**\n* Experience with Figma, Canva, or other creative tools.\n* Experience with automation processes (creative iterations, budgets, reporting).\n* Hands-on experience in Paid Social Advertising on TikTok.\n* Deep interest in current Paid Social trends, algorithm updates, and new ad formats.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a driven Paid Social Marketing Manager to make a significant impact at mammaly, Germany's leading pet supplement startup and a certified B Corp. The role offers high autonomy to drive customer acquisition and optimize campaign performance with a clear focus on sustainability. You'll thrive in a flexible, collaborative environment that values innovation, data-driven decisions, and a strong commitment to animal welfare and social standards. Expect a supportive team, excellent work-from-home flexibility with provided equipment, and a vibrant office in Berlin. This role is perfect for someone passionate about growth, ethical marketing, and making a tangible difference.
