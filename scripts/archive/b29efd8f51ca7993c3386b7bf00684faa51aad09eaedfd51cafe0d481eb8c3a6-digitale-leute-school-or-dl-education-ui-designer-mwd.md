### Description\nDigitale Leute is a leading platform for product management, UX design, and engineering, connecting professionals and leaders in the digital industry through conferences, bootcamps, and a vibrant community. We partner with industry leaders like Airbnb, Porsche Digital, Atlassian, and Rewe Digital to shape the future of software development.\n\nOur Digitale Leute School offers practical further education for the tech industry, with a standout program being our Product Design Bootcamp. This intensive, 4-month online program is the ideal opportunity for individuals looking to start or transition into a career as a UX/UI Designer. Our trainers, hailing from top tech companies, provide cutting-edge, practical knowledge to help you excel. We actively support graduates in their job search through our strong hiring network, and for those with a \"Bildungsgutschein,\" participation can be completely free.\n\n### Responsibilities\n**What you will learn and do in the Bootcamp:**\n*   **User Research:** Learn to understand what users truly need.\n*   **Prototyping & Wireframing:** Make ideas visually and interactively tangible.\n*   **Usability Tests:** Test designs, gather feedback, and improve solutions.\n*   **Interface Design:** Create modern, user-friendly interfaces.\n*   **Agile Methods:** Work with Scrum & Kanban in a team, learning through practical projects just like in real product teams.\n\n### Qualifications\n**Your Profile:**\n*   Enthusiasm for design, technology, and digital products.\n*   Team-oriented, communicative, and open to feedback.\n*   Good German language skills (the course and projects are mostly in German).\n*   Career changer (Quereinsteiger:in), Junior, or career switcher (Umsteiger:in) – no prior design knowledge is required, only motivation and willingness to learn.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for anyone looking to kickstart or transition into a career in UX/UI design. The Digitale Leute School offers a highly practical, mentor-led bootcamp with a strong emphasis on real-world projects and direct support for job placement. With experienced trainers from top tech companies and a valuable hiring network, this program provides a clear pathway into digital product teams. The flexibility of an online, part-time format and potential for free participation via \"Bildungsgutschein\" makes it accessible to a wide range of motivated individuals.
