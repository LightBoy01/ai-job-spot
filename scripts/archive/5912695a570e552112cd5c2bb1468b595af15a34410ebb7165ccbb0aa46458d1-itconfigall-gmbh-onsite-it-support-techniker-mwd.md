### Description\nThis role involves providing comprehensive 1st and 2nd level IT support, ensuring smooth operations for users within an international and innovative work environment. You will be responsible for user assistance, system maintenance, and contributing to an open culture with significant growth opportunities.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- On-site user support for 1st and 2nd level issues.\n- Assistance with incidents and service requests via a ticketing system.\n- Installation, configuration, and replacement of clients, notebooks, and peripherals.\n- Support and administration of mobile devices (smartphones).\n- Assistance with simple tasks on the network infrastructure (e.g., patching, device replacement).\n- Maintenance of documentation and close collaboration with colleagues.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Experience in 1st/2nd level IT support in enterprise environments.\n- Practical experience with ticketing systems, ideally ServiceNow.\n- Knowledge of client support for Windows environments and DELL hardware.\n- Experience with Mobile Device Management, ideally Workspace ONE.\n- Basic knowledge of network technology (patching, switches, access points).\n- Very good German and English language skills, both written and spoken; French is a plus.\n- Service-oriented, communicative, and team-player personality.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced IT support professional to join a dynamic, international team. The role offers a good blend of hands-on technical tasks and direct user interaction, providing pathways for both professional and personal development. If you thrive in an open communication culture with flat hierarchies and enjoy tackling diverse IT challenges, this position will be a great fit.
