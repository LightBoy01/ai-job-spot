### Description\nJoin a growth-oriented online marketing agency dedicated to empowering e-commerce brands to sustainably boost their digital performance. We craft holistic strategies, optimize online shops, and drive continuous growth in revenue, profitability, and brand strength for our diverse clientele. We are seeking a dedicated E-Commerce Manager to professionally support our clients, taking a pivotal role in the operational and strategic development of their online shops.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Provide strategic and operational support for e-commerce clients.\n- Derive actionable insights from data analysis and communicate them clearly to clients and internal teams.\n- Prepare regular performance reports and provide data-driven recommendations for action.\n- Act as a sparring partner for clients and our Performance Marketing Managers.\n- Take ownership for key metrics including Conversion Rate, overall Growth, and Shop Performance.\n- Identify and leverage growth opportunities across the entire customer journey.\n- Execute operational shop management tasks, such as product and category maintenance, and setting up promotions and bundles.\n- Ensure the smooth technical operation of online shops through regular checks on usability, loading times, and accurate tracking.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Proven experience in e-commerce platforms (e.g., Shopify, Shopware, WooCommerce or similar systems).\n- Excellent understanding of shop performance, conversion optimization, and customer-centric shop development.\n- Solid knowledge of web analytics tools and fundamental tracking principles.\n- Basic understanding of UX/UI principles, shop architecture, and conversion optimization strategies.\n- Demonstrated analytical thinking and a highly structured approach to work.\n- Commitment to high-quality standards and a keen eye for detail.\n- A genuine enjoyment of client interaction and adept project management skills.\n- Proactive and solution-oriented mindset.\n- Strong comprehension of digital business models and economic contexts.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis E-Commerce Manager role offers a fantastic opportunity for a proactive and detail-oriented professional to make a significant impact within a dynamic, growth-focused agency. With direct responsibility for client success and shop performance, you'll be empowered to drive tangible results. The company culture stands out with its commitment to continuous learning, flexible work arrangements (remote/hybrid), a generous holiday allowance, and a supportive team environment. If you thrive on client interaction, data-driven strategy, and hands-on shop optimization, this is an ideal environment to grow your career and influence key e-commerce brands.
