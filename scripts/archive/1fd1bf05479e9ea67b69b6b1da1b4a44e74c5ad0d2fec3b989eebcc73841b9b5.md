AI Builder & Automation Intern (US)
===================================
### **Location:** New York City (Hybrid, ~3 days/week in office)
### **Type:** Full-time internship (≈3 months)
### **Start:** October 2025
### **Compensation:** $50,000 - £60,000 per year (prorated for internship length)
### **Reports to:** **Darren Govender, Lead Pricing Actuary**
Why this role
-------------
We’re looking for someone who enjoys building practical automations that make teams faster. You’ll work with our US Sales & Marketing pod to spot repetitive tasks, design simple automated workflows, get them into production, and measure the impact. Your work will help us decide whether to scale this into a permanent capability.
What you’ll do
--------------
*   **Deliver impact quickly.** Ship 2–3 production automations by the end of the internship (e.g., lead enrichment, meeting-notes → CRM updates, follow-up drafting, lightweight reporting).
*   **Build and integrate.** Use platforms like Zapier/Make/n8n and light Python/JavaScript to connect tools (CRM, marketing automation, documents, chat, calendar, data stores) via APIs/webhooks, with sensible error handling and retries.
*   **Make it visible.** Create a simple dashboard (Notion/ or a small web view) that shows runs, success rate, time saved, and common errors. Add basic alerting for failures or missed SLAs.
*   **Iterate with users.** Run two improvement cycles based on usage data and Account Executive feedback. Write short release notes and track adoption and satisfaction.
*   **Enable and document.** Publish short playbooks (how we choose what to automate, security/PII basics, and how to request an automation). Run two internal demos to share what you’ve shipped.
*   **Work smart with stakeholders.** Shadow AEs, map “current vs future” steps, define a small first scope, and ship weekly where possible.
Examples you might tackle
-------------------------
*   Auto-enrich inbound leads and post clean updates to the CRM with an audit trail.
*   Turn meeting notes into structured CRM fields and next actions, with a light review step.
*   Draft prospect follow-ups using recent notes and documents for AE approval.
*   Simple reporting nudges (calendar/CRM hygiene; pipeline health summaries).
What you’ll bring
-----------------
You like to build, ship, and learn quickly. A portfolio, hackathon work, or class projects can be as valuable as formal experience.
**Must-haves**
*   Familiarity with at least one automation platform (Zapier, Make, n8n, Lovable/Replit agents).
*   Comfort working with APIs/webhooks/JSON, plus basic Python or JavaScript for glue code and data transforms.
*   Sensible judgment about when to use rules vs. AI tools; awareness of speed, cost, and accuracy trade-offs.
*   Basic data skills (CSV/JSON handling, SQL) and clear thinking about simple schemas.
*   Reliability mindset: logging, retries/backoff, avoiding duplicate runs, and simple Git/PR workflows.
*   Clear communication: explain trade-offs to non-technical partners and write concise docs.
**Nice-to-haves**
*   Prior internships/projects in Tech/RevOps or sales-adjacent automation.
*   Exposure to modern data stores or vector databases (e.g., Pinecone/Supabase)—not required.
How we’ll define success
------------------------
By ~3 months you’ve:
*   Shipped 2–3 automations to production hitting the targets above.
*   Stood up a simple dashboard and alerts, and completed two data-led iterations.
*   Published playbooks and delivered two enablement sessions (aim for CSAT ≥4/5).
*   Shown hx values in action beyond your core deliverables.
30/60/90 (how we’ll support you)
--------------------------------
*   30 days: Shadow AEs, map 5–7 workflows, pick the top 2 by impact/effort; ship POC #1; set up logging + dashboard skeleton; write a short runbook.
*   60 days: Take POC #1 to GA (adoption ≥50%); ship POC #2; add retries/alerts; first enablement; iteration #1.
*   90 days: 2–3 GA automations with target metrics; dashboard live; playbooks published; iteration #2; tidy handover docs.
Why hyperexponential
--------------------
*   Real impact, fast. Small US squad, tight feedback loops, and measurable outcomes “a startup inside a scale-up.”
*   Great exposure. a16z-backed, exec visibility, and direct access to the US GTM team.
*   Future path. Strong chance of conversion toward RevOps/Enablement (AI-aware) or Automation Engineer. If not, you leave with shipped work, data to show impact, and a strong reference from a fast-growing insurtech.
