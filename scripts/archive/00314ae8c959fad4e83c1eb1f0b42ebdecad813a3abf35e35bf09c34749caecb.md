Main Tasks
----------

**Product Design & Development**

*   Lead design of M+H filtration products, from concept to 2D/3D modeling per customer and internal standards.
*   Drive DFMEA, NPI workflows and functional product release with complete technical documentation.
*   Ensure robust design validation and seamless product launch.

 **Innovation & Continuous Improvement**

*   Champion cost reduction, value engineering and product optimization during serial production.
*   Lead root cause analysis and corrective actions for design and process issues.

**Project & Change Management**

*   Manage cross-functional project execution across the product lifecycle—on time, within budget.
*   Own and execute Technical Change Management (TCM) with full accountability.

**Stakeholder & Communication Excellence**

*   Translate customer requirements into technical solutions; ensure timely resolution of open points.
*   Collaborate with suppliers, customers, and internal teams to align on technical deliverables.
*   Prepare and deliver clear technical documentation and presentations.

**Knowledge Sharing & Compliance**

*   Ensure adherence to M+H standards, norms and patent protection protocols.
*   Share best practices and technical knowledge across teams.

 **Team Leadership**

*   Mentor junior engineers, foster collaboration and build a high-performing team culture.
*   Handle conflict resolution, performance feedback and team motivation.

Your Profile
------------

*   Proficiency in CADtools mainly in CATIA.
*   Understanding of filtration technologies (air, oil, fuel, cabin filters).
*   Knowledge of materials science, especially polymers and composites.
*   Familiarity with design for manufacturability (DFM) and design for assembly(DFA).
*   Familiarity with APQP, PPAP, and ISO/TS 16949 standards.
*   Experience with injection molding, pleating, ultrasonic welding and assembly
*   Exposure to 3D printing/additive manufacturing for prototyping

Experience
----------

**Qualification & Experience:**

*   University degree in Mechanical Engineering.
*   8 - 12  years of relevant work experience
*   Experience in (automotive or similar) product Design & development processes.
