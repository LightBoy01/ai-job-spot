**About Atos**
Atos is a global leader in digital transformation with c. 78,000 employees and annual revenue of c. € 10 billion. European number one in cybersecurity, cloud and high-performance computing, the Group provides tailored end-to-end solutions for all industries in 68 countries. A pioneer in decarbonization services and products, Atos is committed to a secure and decarbonized digital for its clients. Atos is a SE (Societas Europaea) and listed on Euronext Paris.
The purpose of Atos is to help design the future of the information space. Its expertise and services support the development of knowledge, education and research in a multicultural approach and contribute to the development of scientific and technological excellence. Across the world, the Group enables its customers and employees, and members of societies at large to live, work and develop sustainably, in a safe and secure information space.
We welcome an exceptional leader ready to build and expand our consulting business in Germany :
As **Head of Consulting**, you will shape and execute our strategic vision, lead high-impact transformation programs, and cultivate trusted, long-term client relationships across industries. You’ll oversee a diverse team of consultants and partner with senior stakeholders to deliver innovative, data- and AI-driven sustainable solutions.
Key Responsibilities
•    Define and implement the consulting strategy for the German market, aligned with global objectives.
•    Promote offerings such as:
\-    Unlock the value of Data & AI
\-    Drive business transformation
\-    Maximize the potential of platforms and hyperscalers
•    Drive business development, including pipeline growth, proposal leadership, and client acquisition.
•    Lead client engagements across our four core competencies areas:
\-    Digital Strategy, Insight & Experience
\-    Digital Process Automation & Excellence
\-    Digital Technology Strategy & Innovation
\-    Digital Organizational Change Management
•    Support the verticalization of industries through strong industries expertise.
•    Lead client engagements across digital transformation, operational excellence, and strategic advisory.
•    Build and manage a high-performing consulting team, fostering a culture of excellence and innovation.
•    Deploy an AI driven approach in Consulting team
•    Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver integrated solutions across technology and business domains.
•    Speak at industry forums, conferences, and client events; serve as a thought leader in the German market.
Ideal candidate profile
We are looking for a transformation-driven leader, combining creativity, flexibility, and top-tier consulting expertise with decisive decision-making. We expect our new colleague to demonstrate exceptional communication skills, customer focus, stakeholder management, diplomacy in complex organizations, and strong financial acumen—empowering growth, trust, and the realization of the company’s vision.
The ideal candidate will also bring:
•    Strong track record in managing large-scale transformation programs and consulting P&L.
•    Deep understanding of the German market and client landscape.
•    Excellent communication, stakeholder management, and team leadership skills.
•    Fluency in German and English.
•    Entrepreneurial mindset with a passion for innovation and impact.
