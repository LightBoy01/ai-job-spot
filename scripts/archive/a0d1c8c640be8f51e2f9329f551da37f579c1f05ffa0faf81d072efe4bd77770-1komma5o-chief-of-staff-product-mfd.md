### Description\n1KOMMA5° is rapidly building Germany's largest one-stop-shop for the sale, installation, and servicing of interconnected solar panels, heat pumps, electricity solutions, and charging infrastructure. As Chief of Staff – Product, you will be the strategic right hand to the VP Product, playing a central role in driving alignment, operational excellence, and strategic execution across our global product organization. This highly visible and hands-on position combines strategic thinking with deep operational involvement, supporting international collaboration and shaping how our global Product function executes its mission to accelerate the energy transition.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Central Coordination:** Act as the primary point of contact within the Product organization, connecting teams, ensuring effective information flow, and coordinating alignment across departments and international stakeholders.\n- **Project Leadership and Execution:** Lead and coordinate strategic and operational projects with cross-functional and international impact. Drive initiatives from concept to delivery and ensure measurable outcomes.\n- **Site and Market Support:** Analyze operational and product-related data to identify optimization opportunities. Support local implementation of improvements and process enhancements, focusing on Germany and other key markets.\n- **Support for the VP Product:** Provide direct support to the VP Product in day-to-day operations, strategic planning, and administrative tasks, including project preparation, documentation, and coordination (e.g., scheduling and travel planning).\n- **Independent Project Ownership:** Take full ownership of selected initiatives within the Product domain. Contribute proactively to product-related projects and ensure their timely, high-quality execution.\n\n### Qualifications\n- University degree in Engineering, Business, Humanities, or a comparable discipline.\n- Strong attention to detail and organizational skills.\n- Experience in product-related roles (Product Management, Strategy, or Operations) is highly desirable.\n- Ideally, prior exposure to the energy or technology sector.\n- A hands-on, proactive mindset and ability to work independently and responsibly.\n- Reliable, structured, and motivated by the mission of accelerating the energy transition.\n- Excellent command of German and English, both written and spoken.\n- Willingness to travel and thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.\n- Proficient in Excel, PowerPoint, Google Sheets, Google Slides, and other office or analytics tools.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a highly driven individual to join 1KOMMA5°, a rapidly scaling, mission-driven company at the forefront of the energy transition. As Chief of Staff to the VP Product, you'll gain unparalleled exposure to strategic decision-making and operational execution across a global product organization. The role promises significant impact, allowing you to directly contribute to climate goals while working alongside super-smart colleagues in a collaborative, fast-paced environment. With benefits like remote work flexibility, wellness programs, and strong growth potential, it's ideal for someone looking to make a tangible difference in a thriving scaleup.
