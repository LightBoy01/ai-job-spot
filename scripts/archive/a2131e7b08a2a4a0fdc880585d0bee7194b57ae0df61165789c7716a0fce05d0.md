**Job Description:**

The Copperleaf Extensions Infrastructure team builds and maintains the platform that enables internal teams, clients, and partners to develop intelligent, scalable extensions and integrations for the Copperleaf product. This includes standard APIs, extensibility frameworks, partner tooling, and — critically — a growing focus on AI infrastructure. 

We’re looking for a Full Stack Engineer with proven experience in both full-stack development and AI-powered systems. In this role, you’ll work on extensibility frameworks, enhance developer and partner experience, and shape the AI capabilities that will define the next generation of extension development. 

This is a high-impact opportunity for someone who enjoys building powerful platforms that empower others. 

Key Responsibilities 

*   Design and develop robust, secure, and performant software using .NET (C#) and Angular. 
    

*   Build and evolve extensibility infrastructure and RESTful APIs that support integration across the Copperleaf ecosystem. 
    

*   Develop infrastructure and tooling for AI-assisted development 
    

*   Partner with product teams and external stakeholders to deliver extensibility capabilities that are powerful, intuitive, and future-ready. 
    

*   Participate in code reviews, technical design discussions, and mentoring.
