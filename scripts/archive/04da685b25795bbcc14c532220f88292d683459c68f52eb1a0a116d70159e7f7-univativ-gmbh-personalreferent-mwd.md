### Description\nunivativ is a project and personnel service provider offering exciting jobs to students and graduates at renowned companies. Your career is our mission, as your development is important to us.\n\nIn this role, you will assume a central interface function between HR, management, and co-determination bodies. As a trusted point of contact, you will guide change processes, expertly manage employment law topics, and contribute your expertise to strategic personnel planning.\n\nFor our client in the transport sector, we are seeking Project Employees (m/f/d) who are enthusiastic about a modern work environment and innovative topics. In an expert team, you can extensively apply your knowledge and successfully master tasks. This position is based in Frankfurt am Main, for 37.5 hours per week, with a project duration of 18 months, starting as soon as possible.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Serve as the primary point of contact for managers, advising them on all HR-related questions.\n- Guide employment law topics, manage co-determination processes, and participate in organizational changes.\n- Provide early support for all staffing processes and coordinate with relevant committees.\n- Develop recommendations on compensation, personnel planning, and development and career paths.\n- Analyze individual employment law cases with attention to detail, prepare information, and develop suitable solutions.\n- Provide HR data and analyses, deriving concrete recommendations for action.\n- Actively participate in various HR projects, create contracts and agreements, and support change processes.\n- Ensure transparent information flow and represent the interests of your department towards co-determination bodies.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in fields such as Business Law, Economics, Psychology, or as a fully qualified lawyer. Alternatively, a comparable qualification with relevant experience in Human Resources (e.g., Air Traffic Business Administrator with relevant further training).\n- Several years of professional experience in HR is desirable, especially in advisory or strategic functions.\n- Strong knowledge of employment law topics (labor law, works council constitution act, collective bargaining law) and the ability to communicate them clearly.\n- Proficient in SAP HR, MS Office, and common HR tools.\n- Excellent empathy, communication skills, and negotiation confidence.\n- Analytical thinking, conceptual strength, and a solution-oriented approach characterize your work style.\n- Very good German and good English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis Project Employee role at univativ offers a unique opportunity to gain diverse HR experience across various clients in the transport sector, significantly expanding your professional network. The company prioritizes career development, providing a collaborative expert environment where your strategic insights into HR and employment law will be highly valued. Beyond the core responsibilities, you'll benefit from a competitive salary of €50,000 to €55,000 per year, corporate shopping advantages, and fitness discounts. It's an ideal role for an ambitious individual eager to make a tangible impact in a dynamic, project-based setting.
