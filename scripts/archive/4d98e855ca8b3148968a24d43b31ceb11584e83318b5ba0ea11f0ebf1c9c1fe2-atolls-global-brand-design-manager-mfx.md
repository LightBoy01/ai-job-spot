### Description
Atolls stands as the world's largest community-driven shopping platform, actively serving over 20 markets globally. Our mission is to empower millions to make smarter spending decisions by connecting them with the right brands and retailers throughout their discovery, evaluation, and purchase journeys. With a diverse team of over 1,000 professionals across 10 countries, we build products used daily at a global scale, offering employees real ownership and visible impact.

We are seeking a **Global Brand Design Manager (m/f/d)** to join our Design organization. In this pivotal role, you will lead the evolution of Atolls' expansive brand portfolio, which includes prominent names like Mydealz, Dealabs, Hotukdeals, Pepper, Shoop, and iGraal, across multiple international markets. This is an ideal opportunity for a systems-minded creative leader with a deep passion for design scalability, craft excellence, and cross-functional collaboration. You will be responsible for defining and scaling our global brand's visual identity across products, campaigns, and experiences, ensuring a cohesive and high-quality design presence that resonates with users and partners worldwide. This role also champions the integration of AI, automation, and process-driven design, requiring close partnership with product, marketing, commercial, and editorial teams to establish a unified global roadmap that maximizes brand reach, impact, and creative potential.

### Responsibilities
*   Lead and evolve the global brand identity system, scaling the use of our logo, color palette, typography, iconography, imagery, and motion design to ensure cohesive storytelling across all touchpoints.
*   Develop scalable systems, toolkits, and workflows that maintain global consistency while allowing regional flexibility. Define and uphold design principles that drive creative excellence and innovation.
*   Leverage AI and automation to streamline production, improve efficiency, and raise the quality bar at scale.
*   Partner with marketing, product, and design system teams to integrate the brand seamlessly across product, campaign, and experiential environments.
*   Define and drive the global brand design roadmap in collaboration with marketing, product, commercial, and editorial partners, ensuring unified priorities and long-term strategic alignment.
*   Foster a culture of openness, critique, and collaboration—mentoring senior designers and leading creative reviews that strengthen global design quality.
*   Own the delivery of global brand initiatives from concept to execution, translating strategy into scalable outcomes with measurable impact.

### Qualifications
*   4+ years of experience in **Brand Design Leadership at scale.**
*   Proven success defining and evolving global visual identity systems across multiple markets and channels.
*   Deep expertise in brand systems, typography, color, motion design, and visual storytelling.
*   Strong proficiency in design tools and systems, for instance, Figma, Adobe, and others.
*   Experience integrating AI and automation into creative workflows.
*   Exceptional communication, storytelling, and stakeholder management skills.
*   A collaborative, growth-oriented mindset—leading with empathy, clarity, and accountability.
