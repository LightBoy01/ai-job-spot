**About the Company**
---------------------
Our client is at the forefront of the AI revolution, providing cutting-edge infrastructure that's reshaping the landscape of artificial intelligence. They offer an **AI-centric cloud platform** that empowers Fortune 500 companies, top-tier innovative startups, and AI researchers to drive breakthroughs in AI.
Their **mission** is to democratize access to world-class AI infrastructure, enabling organizations of all sizes to bring their bold AI ambitions to life. At the core of their success is a culture that celebrates creativity, embraces challenges, and thrives on collaboration.
*   Industry: Cloud Computing, AI
*   Product/Service: AI-centric cloud platform offering infrastructure optimized for training AI models
*   Company size: ~800 employees
*   Candidate location: UK
**The Opportunity**
-------------------
As a **Venture Partnerships Lead**, you will be at the forefront of expanding our clients’ AI-focused cloud platform throughout the **European** venture landscape. Your mission will be to develop and strengthen relationships with leading VC firms, private equity groups, accelerators, and dynamic startup communities, strategically contributing to the acquisition of high-value customers and enhancing overall market presence. By leveraging your expertise and network, you’ll play a vital role in driving business growth and shaping our clients’ reputation as an essential partner for the region’s most innovative AI ventures.
**What You'll Do**
------------------
*   Develop, manage, and scale partnerships with EU-based VCs, PEs, accelerators, and startup communities
*   Source and onboard high-potential AI startups into the Company’s Startup Program
*   Design and execute ecosystem activations, campaigns, and joint GTM initiatives
*   Create case studies, playbooks, and event programming to strengthen ecosystem adoption
*   Track metrics including startup acquisitions, activation, and revenue contributions
*   Collaborate with global sales, product, and technical teams to ensure successful integration and customer journeys
*   Champion the voice of the EU startup and VC community within the Company
**What You Bring**
------------------
*   7+ years in GTM, partnerships, business development, or sales, with 5+ years in VC/startup ecosystems
*   Proven track record of driving pipeline growth and ecosystem partnerships
*   Deep understanding of venture capital, investment models, and growth strategies in the EU region
*   Direct VC/accelerator/founder experience, hands-on AI/cloud sales/partnerships expertise
*   Deep understanding of AI/ML startups landscape
*   Established Venture Capital network in the EU, especially UK and Israel
*   Excellent communication, stakeholder management, and presentation skills
*   CRM proficiency (HubSpot/Salesforce)
