### Description

Are you an ambitious self-starter eager to shape your own success with maximum flexibility? SumUp empowers small businesses with smart, user-friendly financial solutions, from mobile card payments to comprehensive Point-of-Sale (POS) systems. Our mission is to strengthen entrepreneurs, allowing them to focus entirely on their core business.

As a freelance Field Sales Consultant, you operate as your own boss, with the exciting opportunity to earn attractive, unlimited commissions. Your primary role involves building strong relationships with local businesses and demonstrating how SumUp's innovative solutions can simplify and enhance their operations. You'll be at the forefront of direct sales, offering everything from mobile card terminals to complete payment systems, equipped with all the resources you need to succeed.

This role offers 100% performance-based compensation with no income cap, ensuring your earnings directly reflect your effort and success. You'll enjoy maximum flexibility, deciding when and where you work, while offering top-tier products with significant market potential. Despite your independent status, you'll receive robust training and dedicated sales support, ensuring you're never left alone on your journey to success.

### Responsibilities

*   **Active New Customer Acquisition**: Identify and acquire new local businesses for SumUp, expanding our merchant base.
*   **Personal Meetings and Consulting**: Conduct face-to-face meetings, present SumUp products, and demonstrate their clear benefits and value propositions.
*   **Build Customer Relationships**: Develop and nurture long-term partnerships with merchants by providing excellent service and ongoing support.
*   **Own Customer and Lead Management**: Take full ownership of your client portfolio and lead pipeline, strategically building and managing your business.

### Qualifications

*   A strong passion for sales and an inherent drive for self-motivation are essential.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer service is a significant advantage, but not strictly required.
*   Excellent communication skills and genuine enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Very good German language skills, enabling effective communication with local businesses.
