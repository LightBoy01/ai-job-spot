**Position Specific Description**

The Agile PMO/Technology Strategy & Operations team is seeking an intern to gain hands-on experience in core IT functions while supporting portfolio execution through internal AI (NEE.AI) and analytics. The intern will work with Agile Delivery Managers, Agile Leaders, Scrum Masters, and Technology Leaders to streamline APMO processes, improve tool utilization, and provide insights on technology investment performance and business value. This role offers valuable exposure to agile delivery, technology strategy, and enterprise portfolio management.

**Core Responsibilities**

*   Observe Agile/Delivery ceremonies and portfolio forums; document current processes, tool usage, and pain points, then propose pragmatic improvements
*   Build and maintain prompt libraries and light automations with NEE.AI to accelerate APMO tasks
*   Curate backlogs and roadmaps; create lightweight dashboards (predictability, cycle time, dependency heatmaps) to provide executive-ready transparency
*   Partner with managers to compile budget and forecast snapshots and simple benefits views that connect initiatives to outcomes
*   Assist with communications packs, demos, and training artifacts to promote consistent ways of working and improved tool utilization in TSO
