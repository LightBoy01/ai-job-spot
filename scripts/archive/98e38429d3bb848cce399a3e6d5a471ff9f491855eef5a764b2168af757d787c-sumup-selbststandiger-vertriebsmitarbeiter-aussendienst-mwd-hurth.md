### Description\nJoin SumUp as a freelance Field Sales Consultant and kickstart your career in sales with unlimited earning potential! SumUp empowers small businesses with smart, user-friendly financial solutions, from card payments to POS systems. In this highly autonomous role, you'll be your own boss, building relationships with local businesses and demonstrating how SumUp can simplify their operations. This is a 100% commission-based opportunity where your income directly reflects your drive and success. Enjoy maximum flexibility, comprehensive training, and dedicated sales support while representing top-tier products with high market potential.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Actively acquire new customers: Identify and win local businesses for SumUp.\n*   Conduct personal meetings and consultations: Present SumUp products and demonstrate their benefits.\n*   Build strong customer relationships: Secure long-term partnerships through excellent service.\n*   Manage your own customer and lead pipeline: Independently decide how to build and grow your business.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Passion for sales and strong self-motivation.\n*   Initial experience in sales or customer service is an advantage.\n*   Excellent communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.\n*   Very good German language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an ambitious, self-driven individual eager to embrace an entrepreneurial path in sales. SumUp offers the unique chance to be \"your own boss\" within a supportive ecosystem, providing strong products and comprehensive training. Ideal for those who thrive on autonomy, direct customer impact, and an uncapped commission structure, this role allows you to directly shape your success while empowering local businesses. If you're passionate about sales and ready to build your own client base, this could be your next big step.
