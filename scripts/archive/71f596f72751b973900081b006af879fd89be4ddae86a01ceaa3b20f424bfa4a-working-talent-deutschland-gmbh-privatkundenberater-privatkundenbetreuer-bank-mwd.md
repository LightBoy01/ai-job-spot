### Description\nJoin a modern, regionally rooted financial service provider and take on a responsible role in private customer consulting. We are looking for you as a Private Customer Advisor/Consultant for our locations in Unna or Hamm, via direct placement. This role offers an attractive compensation package, significant benefits like 33 days of holiday, flexible working models, and extensive support for your health, professional development, and family life, all within a welcoming and appreciative environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Holistically advise your private clients throughout all phases of their lives.\n- Actively acquire new clients and strengthen existing relationships.\n- Conduct advisory-focused discussions, identify needs, and develop suitable solutions.\n- Guide your clients into the digital future and utilize modern consulting tools.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed banking apprenticeship (Bankausbildung) or a comparable qualification.\n- Initial experience in private customer consulting is desirable, but not a must.\n- High service and sales orientation, combined with confident communication skills.\n- Proactiveness, commitment, and enjoyment of teamwork.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for individuals looking to build a career in financial advisory within a stable, modern, and regionally connected institution. The emphasis on continuous learning, work-life balance through flexible hours and mobile work, and a comprehensive benefits package (including health, pension, and family support) makes this a highly attractive employer. The \"desirable, but not a must\" for initial experience indicates a supportive environment for those looking to start or grow their advisory career.
