**Job Description:**

Rakuten is a global Internet and e-commerce company with over 1.3 billion registered users worldwide. Our e-commerce platform is the largest of its kind in Japan and among the world's largest by sales. Rakuten has many subsidiaries as well, including well-known brands like Rakuten VIKI, Rakuten Symphony, Rakuten TV, Kobo (e-Reading), and Viber (messaging). For more information, visit http://global.rakuten.com/corp/about/_._

As a Product Management Intern, you will be a motivated and detail-oriented individual with a basic understanding of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML). The incumbent will be involved in various aspects of product management, including competitor analysis and client communication.

**Position Duties**

· Manages all aspects of product development and user experience for existing products.

· Generates insights for our product strategy based on data and user feedback.

· Supports sales and marketing teams in articulating the optimal value propositions for our clients based on market research.

· Responsible for definition and communication of product requirements and specifications

· Drives product execution through an agile delivery process.

**Qualifications**

· Knowledge of MLOps, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence.

· Excellent communication skills.
