We are excited to be seeking an AI Strategic Enablement Lead to drive the adoption and management of AI technologies across the organisation. This pivotal role involves translating strategic vision into operational excellence, coordinating AI-related initiatives, and ensuring alignment across departments.
The successful candidate will lead the development of governance materials, playbooks, and AI tool inventories, fostering a culture of experimentation, capability building, and ethical AI use.
This is a fixed term
part\-time position (0.6 FTE) with a view to moving to full-time.
About the role
You will:
*   Lead the strategic direction of AI initiatives in collaboration with the AI Steering Committee.
*   Coordinate and monitor AI projects across the organisation.
*   Identify and address risks, opportunities, and barriers to progress.
*   Develop and maintain AI governance materials and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.
*   Prepare investment cases and funding proposals for AI initiatives.
*   Stay informed on AI technologies, regulatory changes, and sector best practices.
*   Build and maintain relationships with internal and external stakeholders.
Key Selection Criteria
*   Understanding of AI concepts and their application for social impact and ethical outcomes.
*   Proven ability to lead cross-functional initiatives in resource-constrained environments.
*   Experience in maintaining AI governance frameworks and ensuring compliance with privacy laws.
*   Strong analytical skills and evidence-based decision-making.
*   Excellent communication skills, with the ability to translate complex AI concepts for non-technical audiences.
*   Experience in developing training and guidance materials for responsible AI use.
*   Awareness of AI and NFP sector developments.
Please contact Alison Ramsay if you have any questions regarding this role.
