### Description

At Wolt, we are a Finnish technology company dedicated to creating technology that brings joy, simplicity, and earnings to neighborhoods worldwide. Originating in 2014 with restaurant food delivery, we have rapidly expanded to offer delivery of (almost) everything across over 500 cities in 30 countries. Since joining forces with DoorDash in 2022, we continue our ambitious global expansion, driven by a culture that values self-starters, entrepreneurial spirit, and continuous challenge and growth.

This role is integral to **Wolt Market**, our innovative grocery stores designed to deliver unprecedented convenience and a world-leading customer experience. Operating across 21 countries, Wolt Market enables customers to receive groceries at lightspeed to their doorstep. As a **Category Manager** for Wolt Market Germany, you will report directly to the General Manager and assume full ownership for building and managing the assortment and trading plan, including pricing and promotions, within your assigned categories. This involves developing strong supplier relationships and ensuring optimal category performance across the German market. You will collaborate closely with Wolt Market stores, the Content team, and Supply Chain to ensure the right assortment and consistent stock levels, while also partnering with retail, operations, marketing, and data teams to define and develop necessary reporting, data models, tools, and products to drive local store performance.

### Responsibilities

*   Have full ownership of assigned category performance, including forecasting, monitoring, and reporting on the business.
*   Contribute to driving projects and trading strategy to achieve business goals.
*   Define and continuously update Wolt Market's assortment at a store level to meet customer needs and optimize order economics.
*   Lead effective negotiations with suppliers to establish joint goals, improve cost structure, and sustainably grow the P&L.
*   Influence the design of categories, contribute to new product groups, and improve the customer experience to deliver growth.
*   Create business plans for new opportunities and develop and execute project plans for the launch of new features, incorporating merchandising and pricing strategies.
*   Work cross-functionally with counterparts across in-stock management, marketing, finance, and retail systems to operate and grow the business.
*   Continually improve and automate manual processes to enable the team to scale better as the business grows.

### Qualifications

*   Three (3) years of solid experience in category management, with a proven track record of delivering results.
*   Background in online grocery, traditional grocery retail, or e-commerce, ideally in fast-paced or scaling environments.
*   Strong communication skills and experience in negotiating with both internal and external stakeholders.
*   Excellent analytical skills.
*   Ability to think both strategically and operationally, with strong attention to detail.
*   Experience in ultra-fresh categories (e.g., fruits and vegetables, fresh meat) is a plus.
*   Full professional proficiency in German is a strong plus.
