### Description

Join **Apaleo** and help shape the future of hospitality tech! Apaleo offers the world's most open, API-first property management platform, driving the next generation of hospitality operations. Our modular, AI-powered infrastructure empowers hoteliers to customize their tech stack, automate routine tasks, and deliver seamless, personalized guest experiences through best-in-class apps and adaptive AI agents. If you're passionate about making hospitality smarter, more human, and powered by cutting-edge technology, we invite you to join us.

We are seeking a **Staff Engineer .NET (f/m/d)** with a sharp engineering mind and a strong AI-first mindset to contribute to this exciting journey of building the future of hospitality tech.

### Responsibilities

*   Architect and develop scalable, reliable, and maintainable backend systems within a modern tech environment.
*   Champion architectural decisions and lead design discussions, focusing on long-term impact and cross-team alignment.
*   Mentor engineers and drive technical excellence through collaborative efforts.
*   Proactively identify opportunities for technical innovation, system evolution, and performance improvements, especially leveraging **AI and automation**.
*   Build and maintain internal tools and infrastructure (AWS, Terraform) to enable teams to deliver faster and more reliably.
*   Ensure high platform availability by implementing best practices for observability, logging, and operational excellence.
*   Participate in on-call rotations and act as a reliable problem solver during incidents.

### Qualifications

*   **8+ years of experience** in backend development, with several years in a staff-level or technical leadership role.
*   Expert-level knowledge of **C# (.NET)** and strong hands-on experience with **PostgreSQL** (or solid SQL skills).
*   Proven experience designing and scaling **distributed systems** in cloud-native environments (preferably **AWS**).
*   Comfortable working with containerized services and **infrastructure-as-code** (Docker, ECS, Kubernetes, Terraform).
*   **Hands-on experience with AI-assisted development tools** (e.g., **GitHub Copilot**, **Cursor AI**, **ChatGPT**, **Amazon CodeWhisperer**) to boost productivity, solve complex bugs, or auto-generate boilerplate code and documentation.
*   A **growth mindset** and constant curiosity about how **AI and automation** can accelerate development, improve quality, and reduce toil.
*   Strong advocate for **AI enablement across engineering**, thinking beyond just coding.
*   Excellent collaboration and communication skills, capable of clearly articulating ideas, especially when introducing new technologies like AI to cross-functional teams.
*   A pragmatic approach to problem-solving, understanding when to build custom solutions versus leveraging existing AI tools.
*   Experience with **event-driven systems** and streaming technologies (Kafka is a plus).
