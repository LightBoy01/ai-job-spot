### Description\nSPRIND, the German Federal Agency for Disruptive Innovation, operates with a startup mindset to drive the future. Within the \"Mein Bildungsraum\" (My Education Space) project, our mission is to unlock Germany's digital education potential. We are building an infrastructure that enables learners and educational institutions to digitally manage and securely share data and offerings across the entire educational journey, from early childhood to lifelong learning, ensuring European compatibility. This creates the foundation for a digital and future-proof education landscape.\n\nWe are seeking a PR & Communication Manager (m/f/d) to make our topics and projects visible and secure effective political support. This full-time position, initially fixed until December 31, 2026, with an aim for extension, can be based in Leipzig or Berlin, and/or remote. The goal is to create awareness, build trust, and gain commitments through clear, strategic communication.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop communication strategies and plans, and operatively implement press and public relations for \"Mein Bildungsraum.\"\n- Create thematic plans, editorial guidelines, and storylines for diverse target groups (politics, general public, specialist community).\n- Edit and further develop the project website.\n- Draft and coordinate press releases, respond to press inquiries, and build and maintain media contacts.\n- Produce content for various channels (newsletter, information materials, social media) in coordination with the SPRIND press office.\n- (Co-)create documents such as strategy papers, project fact sheets, and briefings for political stakeholders.\n- Brief and coordinate external service providers (e.g., agencies, illustrators, photographers) and contribute to brand and design development.\n- Provide cross-functional communication consulting for product development, launches, pilot projects, and policy changes.\n- Plan and organize appearances at conferences and trade fairs.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in Communication Science, Political Science, Journalism, or a comparable field.\n- Several years of experience in the communication sector, ideally in political communication (Public Affairs), press work (Public Relations), or strategic marketing.\n- Ability to translate complex topics and political contexts into clear, convincing messages.\n- Strong writing skills and experience in drafting various communication formats (e.g., press releases, background papers, strategy papers, briefings).\n- Proficient in handling social media channels (especially LinkedIn) and editorial content planning.\n- Excellent written and verbal communication skills in German, and the ability to convey complex issues understandably.\n- Experience collaborating with public institutions, ministries, or in the education sector, as well as a good understanding of political and federal structures.\n- Knowledge of Content Management Systems and a basic understanding of brand management and visual communication.\n- Optional: Experience in audio, video, or image editing.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a highly impactful and strategic role within a unique organization that perfectly blends the agility and innovation of a startup with the foundational mission of a federal agency. The successful candidate will be at the forefront of shaping Germany's digital education landscape, making a tangible difference by ensuring critical projects like \"Mein Bildungsraum\" receive the necessary visibility and political backing. SPRIND offers a dynamic environment with diverse tasks, excellent opportunities for professional and personal development, and attractive benefits including flexible working hours and mobile work options. This position is ideal for an experienced and passionate communication professional who thrives on public affairs, strategic storytelling, and contributing to significant societal change.
