### Description\nThis role is a unique opportunity to lead the strategic development and implementation of sales processes for a family-run, medium-sized company with 30 employees, located near Düsseldorf Airport (40472). The company prides itself on a very familial and employee-friendly culture, blending generational experience with modern approaches. You will play a pivotal role in modernizing sales steering and enhancing the company's overall success. This environment fosters a supportive atmosphere with flexible working conditions, including flextime and home office options, as well as attractive benefits such as a gym membership, fresh fruit, coffee, subsidized meals, and employee discounts.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop and implement the sales strategy.\n- Establish transparent process flows across the entire sales department.\n- Develop and introduce measurable key performance indicators (KPIs).\n- Steer team exchanges with clear agendas.\n- Continuously coach sales employees and accompany them to client appointments for potential development.\n- Design and conduct internal training sessions on commercial and sales topics.\n- Build an efficient and simple reporting system, considering that your team is not primarily composed of commercial specialists.\n- Collaborate closely with the owner.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You are a salesperson through and through, know sales from the ground up, and are now dedicated to strategic sales.\n- You possess exceptional interpersonal skills, capable of convincing the company owner of your ideas, while also respecting the individuality of sales employees and enabling comparability.\n- You thrive in a small/medium-sized company, not primarily seeking career advancement, but intrinsically motivated to pursue a common goal with all colleagues: to make the solid company even more successful through modern sales steering.\n- You are fluent in German (C1/C2) and have B1 level English skills.\n- Experience in the food sector would be ideal.\n\n### Author's Take\nThe recruiter was deeply impressed by the company's spirit and the genuine commitment to its employees.
