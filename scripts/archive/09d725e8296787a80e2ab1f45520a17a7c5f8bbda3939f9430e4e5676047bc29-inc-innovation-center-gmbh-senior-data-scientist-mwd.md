### Description\nThe INC Innovation Center is an internationally active one-stop shop for the development and implementation of technology-driven innovations. Our expert team supports clients worldwide – from idea to market launch – with innovative methods, consulting, and software development. Over 450 satisfied clients already benefit from our future-oriented projects in the fields of Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Robotics. Become part of our ambitious team and actively shape the future of AI!\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Development and implementation of Machine Learning and AI solutions in areas such as production, logistics, research & development, and robotics.\n- Conception, training, and implementation of AI agents based on modern tools (e.g., Copilot Studio, AI Foundry, OpenAI APIs, Azure AI Services).\n- Data organization, preparation, and modeling using statistical methods, Machine Learning, and Generative Models.\n- Analysis and interpretation of complex data patterns – identifying correlations, trends, and optimization potentials.\n- Creation of reports, dashboards, and presentations to visualize analysis results.\n- Collaboration in interdisciplinary industrial, research, and development projects.\n- Development of technical concepts for industrial clients in the AI / Machine Learning sector.\n- Support in identifying and evaluating new AI technologies and co-designing innovative prototypes and Proofs of Concept.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Mathematics, Engineering, or a comparable qualification, preferably with a PhD.\n- At least 5 years of professional experience in Data Science, Machine Learning, or AI projects.\n- Experience in the development and integration of AI agent systems (e.g., with LLMs, RAG, Copilot Studio, LangChain, AI Foundry, or similar frameworks).\n- Knowledge in Robotics, Automation, or Control Systems is a plus.\n- Excellent knowledge of statistics, data analysis, and programming (e.g., Python, Pandas, Scikit-Learn, PyTorch, or TensorFlow).\n- Experience in creating technical concepts and project proposals based on client requirements.\n- Analytical, conceptual, and solution-oriented thinking.\n- Enthusiasm for new technological developments and a strong desire to continuously learn and grow.\n- Independent, structured work ethic and strong teamwork skills.\n- Fluent in German and English (negotiation-level proficiency).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned AI/ML professional to join a highly ambitious and innovative team at the forefront of technology. You will work on exciting, interdisciplinary projects, directly impacting international clients and shaping the future of AI, Data Science, and Robotics. The company values continuous learning, offers significant autonomy for your own ideas, and provides excellent benefits including flexible remote work options, professional development, and a supportive, motivated work environment. If you're passionate about pushing technological boundaries and contributing your expertise to impactful projects, this role offers a dynamic platform for growth.
