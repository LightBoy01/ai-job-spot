### Description

Our partner company in Bad Tölz is seeking a dedicated **Private Client Advisor** to join their team as soon as possible. In this role, you will be instrumental in building and maintaining strong financial relationships with a diverse client base, offering comprehensive advice and tailored solutions. This is a crucial position for individuals passionate about financial consulting and committed to long-term client success.

**Benefits of Joining:**
*   **Long-Term Security:** Secure an unlimited permanent employment contract, ensuring stability and a future with a market leader.
*   **Work-Life Balance:** Enjoy a generous 32 days of annual leave, providing ample time for personal pursuits and relaxation.
*   **Commuting Support:** Benefit from a subsidized Deutschlandticket, making your commute more affordable and convenient.
*   **Appreciative Culture:** Thrive in a supportive and positive team environment characterized by a 'Du-culture' (informal address) and strong trust from leadership.
*   **Modern Environment:** Work in a contemporary setting equipped with the latest tools and technology.
*   **Continuous Development:** Access numerous opportunities for further training and professional growth, supporting your career journey.

### Responsibilities

As a Private Client Advisor, you will:
*   Provide holistic support to a fixed customer base, engaging personally, via phone, and through digital channels.
*   Advise clients on current accounts, loans, investments, securities, and pension products, developing individualized financial plans.
*   Take responsibility for active new client acquisition and the expansion of existing customer relationships, aligning with their financial goals.
*   Contribute to target group-oriented marketing campaigns, leveraging modern advisory tools and digital channels to optimize the client experience.
*   Diligently document consultation discussions and outcomes, collaborate closely with internal teams, and contribute to the ongoing development of advisory processes.

### Qualifications

To succeed in this role, you should possess:
*   A professional qualification as a Bank Clerk (Bankkaufmann/m/w/d) or a comparable qualification, such as a Financial Advisor, Bank Customer Advisor, or Insurance and Finance Merchant.
*   Initial professional experience in private client consulting or bank sales, ideally with securities and credit products.
*   Good knowledge of account management, digital advisory formats, and ideally experience with financial and advisory tools.
*   Demonstrated sales talent, strong communication skills, customer orientation, and a solution-oriented, structured working approach.
*   Very good German language skills.
