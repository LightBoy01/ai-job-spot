### Description

Are you a sharp IT System Administrator who is comfortable in Windows environments and has already gained exciting initial insights into Cyber Security? Do you long for a work environment that combines development, appreciation, and team spirit? Then you've found the right place!

We are seeking a proactive and skilled IT System Administrator to join our dynamic team. In this role, you will be instrumental in planning and implementing IT infrastructure projects for small and medium-sized businesses, acting as a trusted advisor and providing reliable support to our diverse client base. You'll manage client relationships from start to finish, with the flexibility to specialize in particular areas or specific solutions based on your interests and expertise. If you're passionate about IT security and eager to grow within a supportive, forward-thinking environment, we want to hear from you.

### Responsibilities

*   Plan and implement robust IT infrastructure projects tailored for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).
*   Provide expert consulting and reliable, end-to-end support to our clients, ensuring their IT systems are efficient and secure.
*   Manage comprehensive client relationships, from initial setup to ongoing maintenance and strategic advice.
*   Identify opportunities for specialization in specific technical areas or niche solutions, pursuing growth aligned with your interests.
*   Actively contribute to the enhancement of IT security measures and best practices across client environments.

### Qualifications

*   Solid experience in IT system administration, with a strong command of Windows environments.
*   Familiarity with Cyber Security concepts; ideally, you have already gained initial practical experience in this field.
*   A strong desire to develop further in a dynamic setting and deepen your knowledge in the area of IT security.
*   Excellent problem-solving skills and a customer-focused approach.
*   Ability to work independently and as part of a collaborative team.
