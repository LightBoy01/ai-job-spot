### Description\nThis role is for a Partner Growth Lead within SumUp's Partnerships Channel, leading the Partner Growth team. This team is central to SumUp's integration and partner strategy, responsible for managing and expanding its partnership portfolio (ISVs, Reseller, Referrals). The core missions are to onboard and grow new integration partners and to accelerate merchant acquisition and revenue growth through collaborative relationships with existing partners and unlocking new business opportunities via SumUp's products. As the Lead, you will elevate this function by defining scalable structures, frameworks, and rhythms for collaboration and measurable success, guiding a team of 7 Partner Growth Managers. You will work closely with Product, Engineering, Revenue, and Partner Acquisition teams to ensure integrations drive impact for merchants across Europe. This position is based in SumUp's Berlin office, operating in an an office-first setup.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Lead and develop the Partner Growth team (7 FTEs), establishing a clear vision, KPIs, and scalable ways of working.\n- Build and implement a standardized onboarding framework for new ISVs, ensuring smooth handovers from Partner Acquisition to Product and Growth teams.\n- Introduce performance management systems to forecast, measure, and improve partner-driven revenue.\n- Create and roll out a commercial playbook for partner prioritization, portfolio segmentation, and scaling activation and growth motions.\n- Partner cross-functionally with Product, RevOps, and Engineering to accelerate integrations and unblock partner initiatives.\n- Strengthen SumUp's market positioning by developing sales materials and narratives that establish SumUp as the preferred payment partner for ISVs.\n- Balance strategic vision with hands-on problem solving, ensuring clarity, accountability, and performance across the team.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 8+ years of commercial experience, ideally in fintech or SaaS, with at least 3 years of people leadership experience.\n- Proven experience in building or scaling partner management structures, KPIs, and playbooks.\n- Data-driven mindset and proficiency with Salesforce, dashboards, and performance analytics.\n- Strong stakeholder management and cross-functional \\\"collaboration skills\\\" – you influence effectively across business and tech teams.\n- Ability to combine strategic thinking with hands-on execution; proactive, resilient, and solution-oriented.\n- Thrives in fast-changing environments and possesses a \\\"growth mindset\\\" – leading by example and driving accountability within the team.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a pivotal leadership role for an experienced individual looking to significantly impact SumUp's growth through strategic partnerships. The company emphasizes a \\\"team-first\\\" attitude, diversity, and continuous learning, offering an annual L&D budget and a clear commitment to an inclusive environment. With an office-first setup in Berlin, the role offers a chance to work on global fintech products. The benefits package is robust, including a corporate pension, generous leave, and even a sabbatical program, highlighting a strong focus on employee well-being and long-term commitment. SumUp encourages applicants to apply even if they don't meet every single criterion, stressing room for learning and development.
