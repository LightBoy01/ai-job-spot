**Director, Executive Recruiting (Global Leadership)**
======================================================
At Okta, we believe the future starts with exceptional leadership. As Director, Executive Recruiting, you will play a pivotal role in attracting and securing the executive talent (VP-level and above) who will shape Okta’s next chapter. In this role, you will build out a consistent, global process for hiring Executive leadership roles and enable the recruiting organization on best-in-class practices for hiring senior leadership.  In this individual contributor (IC) role, you will report to the Vice President, Global Talent Acquisition.
**Role**
--------
*   Serve as a strategic partner to the C-suite and senior leaders, guiding executive hiring decisions across all global business functions (G&A, Technology, Product, GTM).
*   Lead complex, global executive searches end-to-end, leveraging AI, data, and best-in-class recruiting strategies to deliver outstanding results.
*   Act as a cultural ambassador, ensuring every executive hire reflects Okta’s values and enhances our leadership bench.
*   Drive operational excellence in executive recruiting through process design, metrics, and continuous improvement.
**Results**
-----------
*   Deliver a consistent pipeline of diverse, high-quality executive talent that meets business and leadership needs.
*   Achieve timely and successful placement of VP and C-level hires across multiple geographies.
*   Strengthen Okta’s position in the executive talent market, particularly in the security and IAM sectors.
*   Demonstrate measurable improvements in efficiency, candidate experience, and stakeholder satisfaction through the innovative use of AI and data-driven insights.
*   Contribute to a strong leadership culture that accelerates Okta’s growth and long-term success.
**Relationships**
-----------------
*   Build trusted advisory partnerships with the C-suite, executive team, and senior leaders across the globe.
*   Collaborate closely with business partners, HR leaders, and external search partners to ensure alignment on priorities and outcomes.
*   Develop and maintain long-term relationships with top executive talent in the market, positioning Okta as an employer of choice.
*   Act as a visible guardian of Okta’s culture, ensuring leadership hires embody and advance our core values.
**What We’re Looking For**
--------------------------
*   10+ years of executive recruiting experience with a track record of placing VP and C-level talent.
*   Strong foundational experience hiring across business functions (G&A, Technology, Product, GTM).
*   Expertise in leveraging AI and data to drive smarter, more efficient recruiting outcomes.
*   Executive presence with the ability to influence and advise senior leaders confidently.
*   Vast understanding and experience in creating executive compensation packages in the technology sector.
*   History of building scalable, efficient recruiting processes with a focus on operational excellence.
*   Global mindset with experience navigating different markets and hiring practices.
*   High EQ and ability to manage nuance with discretion and credibility.
*   Industry knowledge in security, IAM, or related technology sectors strongly preferred.
*   A collaborative, values-driven leader who elevates Okta’s culture.
*   This hybrid role will be based in San Francisco with a 1 to 2 days/week in-office requirement.
