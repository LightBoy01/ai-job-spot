### Description

Join Apaleo and be part of shaping the future of hospitality tech! Apaleo is at the forefront of the industry, offering the world's most open, API-first property management platform. Our modular, AI-powered infrastructure empowers hoteliers to revolutionize their operations, automate routine tasks, and deliver seamless, personalized guest experiences through best-in-class apps and autonomous AI agents.

We are seeking a **Junior Onboarding Manager- French Speaking (f/m/d)** to join our dynamic team in Munich. This role is perfect for someone driven by the idea of making hospitality smarter, more human, and powered by cutting-edge technology. You won't just support innovation; you'll help drive it, directly shaping an intelligent, AI-first platform used by hospitality teams across the globe. At Apaleo, your ideas power real products, your voice influences direction, and your impact is visible at every level.

We are proud to be an Equal Opportunity and Affirmative Action Employer, committed to fostering a diverse, inclusive, and welcoming workplace. We encourage applications from individuals of all backgrounds, celebrating differences and believing that diversity enriches our team and drives innovation.

### Responsibilities

As a Junior Onboarding Manager, you will:

*   Support customers throughout the onboarding process, ensuring a smooth and successful integration with our system.
*   Collaborate closely with senior onboarding specialists and other internal teams to understand customer needs and provide effective solutions.
*   Guide customers through system setup and configuration, offering essential training and assistance as required.
*   Monitor onboarding progress, track key milestones, and ensure that timelines and deliverables are consistently met.
*   Communicate proactively with internal teams to promptly resolve customer questions and feedback.
*   Continuously learn and proactively take on increasing responsibility as you develop and grow within the role.

### Qualifications

We value candidates who bring:

*   Strong motivation to learn and develop rapidly in a dynamic, fast-paced environment.
*   A proactive and solution-oriented mindset, eager to tackle challenges.
*   Excellent communication, organizational, and multitasking abilities.
*   Some experience or an internship in customer onboarding, support, or software implementation (prior experience in SaaS or the hospitality industry is a significant advantage).
*   **Fluency in both English and French is essential for this role.**
