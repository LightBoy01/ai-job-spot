### Job Description

You will be the Manufacturing Engineering Associate Manager for the manufacturing engineering team. Our team is responsible for overseeing the design, development, and implementation of factory production equipment and manufacturing processes.

  

**What You Will Be Doing**

  

As the Manufacturing Engineering Associate Manager, you will be responsible for leading the development and implementation of manufacturing processes, supervising manufacturing engineers, and ensuring compliance with company and government regulations.

  

Your responsibilities will include, but are not limited to:

  

  
*   Oversight of the design, development, and implementation of factory production equipment and manufacturing processes
  
*   Supervision of manufacturing engineers, including employee recruitment, selection, and training
  
*   Performance assessment and assignment of engineering tasks
  
*   Ensuring compliance with company command media, engineering requirements, and government regulations
  

  

**Why Join Us**

  

We are looking for a collaborative and experienced leader to join our team. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in manufacturing engineering and supervision. This role stands out as an opportunity to lead a team and make a significant impact on our manufacturing operations. We offer a comprehensive benefits package and flexible scheduling options. ### Basic Qualifications

Minimum of a bachelor’s degree from an accredited college in a technical discipline and six years of manufacturing experience or five years of manufacturing experience with a master’s degree in a technical discipline.  
  
History of successfully leading a technical team effort in manufacturing assembly, integration, and/or test operations within a production factory environment.  
  
Must possess and demonstrate Full Spectrum Leadership competencies including (1) delivering results, (2) shaping the future, (3) build effective relationship, (4) energizing the team, and (5) model personal excellence, integrity, and accountability.  
  
Must be a U.S. Citizen with the ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. DoD Secret Security Clearance. Lockheed Martin will assist candidate with obtaining at least an Interim Secret Clearance prior to starting.  
  
Candidate must be able to pass a Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (“ATF”) background check to obtain approval to be an “employee possessor” (of explosives or other ATF-regulated items) as defined by ATF regulations. Failure to obtain and retain employee possessor status may result in revocation of an offer and loss of employment.

### Desired skills

Advanced education with manufacturing experience in ordnance assembly, integration, and test operations.  
  
Experience working with multi-functional teams including members from Quality Assurance, ESH & Ordnance Safety, Facilities Engineering, Production Control & Transportation, Project Engineering & Management and U.S. Government Customer Representatives.  
  
LM Lean Six Sigma Green / Black Belt certification.  
  
Working knowledge of Lockheed Martin Missiles and Fire Control (LM-MFC) production data systems (Winchill MPM-Link, Apriso, SAP), (LM MFC) test equipment and tooling hardware and document control.  
  
Experience with design, fabrication, installation, initial calibration, prove-out and maintenance of special tools and equipment, continuous yield improvement activities, root cause and corrective action analysis, cost reduction (affordability) and post-production depot operation activities.
