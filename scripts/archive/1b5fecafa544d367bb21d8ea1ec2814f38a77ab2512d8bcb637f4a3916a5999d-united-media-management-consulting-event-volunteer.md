### Description\nPrivate Equity Insights organizes premier global events that drive finance and investment innovation by connecting C-level executives, General Partners (GPs), and Limited Partners (LPs) with entrepreneurs and investors. Our conferences gather influential industry players like Blackstone, BlackRock, Apollo, and Carlyle.\n\nWe are seeking ambitious and talented individuals to join a team of 25+ peers as a Volunteer for the leading French Private Equity Conference in Frankfurt. This is a unique opportunity to expand your professional network with over 500 leaders in the private equity sector, develop essential leadership skills, and gain invaluable insights into the operational aspects of a major industry event. Volunteers are fundamental to our success, offering you an \\\"insider's view\\\" into how the world's largest Private Equity community is built.\n\nJoin us at Westin Frankfurt on March 13th, 2026. For a glimpse into our past events, watch our [2023 Milan's highlight video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1w9amNARIROInji1jERo8KzgPLCfemnQM/view?usp=drive_link) or visit our website [here](https://pe-insights.com/private-equity-conferences/france/) for more information.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a Private Equity Conference Volunteer, you will play a crucial role in ensuring the successful execution of the event. Your responsibilities will include:\n- Assisting the Private Equity Insights team in the setup, coordination, and smooth delivery of the conference.\n- Providing support to conference attendees, speakers, and organizers as needed throughout the day.\n- Participating actively in a mandatory 2-hour training session and rehearsals on the afternoon prior to the event.\n- Contributing to a positive and professional environment, helping to manage logistics and guest flow.\n- Representing Private Equity Insights with professionalism and enthusiasm.\n\n### Qualifications\nWe are looking for motivated individuals eager to immerse themselves in the private equity world and gain practical experience. Ideal candidates will possess:\n- A strong interest in the Private Equity sector and a desire to expand professional networks.\n- Ambition to develop soft skills, including leadership, communication, and event management.\n- The ability to work effectively and collaboratively as part of a large volunteer team.\n- Availability to commit to the 2-hour training session the day before and the full conference day on March 13th, 2026.\n- A proactive attitude and willingness to contribute to a high-impact event.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis volunteer opportunity with Private Equity Insights is far more than just helping out; it's a strategic stepping stone for anyone looking to break into or advance within the finance and investment world. You'll gain direct access to over 500 C-level executives, GPs, and LPs, offering \\\"unparalleled networking\\\" that is difficult to achieve elsewhere. The structured individual training in management, along with the chance to observe and contribute to a major international conference, provides invaluable real-world experience. Beyond the professional certificate, you'll benefit from an exclusive dinner with peers and the PEI team, fostering genuine connections. This role is perfect for ambitious individuals ready to leverage a unique, immersive experience to supercharge their career development and build a powerful professional network.
