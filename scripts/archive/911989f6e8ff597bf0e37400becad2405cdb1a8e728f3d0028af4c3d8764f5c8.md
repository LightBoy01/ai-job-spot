**Innovation Engineer\_ AI & Automation– Contract 1 yr.**

**Remote position**

**Are you interested in being part of an innovative team that supports Westinghouse’s mission to provide clean energy solutions? At WECTEC Staffing Services, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Westinghouse Electric Company LLC, we recognize that our employees are our most valuable asset and we seek to identify, attract and recruit the most qualified talent while recognizing and encouraging the value of diversity in the global workplace.**

Employees in this role have responsibility for interpreting, organizing, executing, and coordinating cutting-edge innovation and pilot projects. This includes selecting and exploring diverse subject areas, defining project scope, and implementing novel concepts. Technologies of interest include, but are not limited to, solar PV, energy storage, transmission, and wind.

**Your Day-to-Day:**

*   Subject matter expert (SME) of innovation in assigned subject areas
*   Manage projects without direct reports
*   Research, develop, and implement appropriate automation and other tools to optimize processes
*   Collaborate with and lead multi-disciplinary teams of internal and external industry subject matter experts for problem solving, betterment opportunities, and systems integration
*   Collaborate with business and technology stakeholders to define the strategic roadmap and drive digital innovation
*   Understand industry trends and the practical application of existing, new, and emerging technologies
*   Write and maintain production level code in Python, R, SQL, etc.
*   Design, prototype, and test solutions to energy-related scientific and mathematical problems
*   Support and foster innovative technologies to deliver new ideas that enable business transformation
*   Communicate technical concepts to stakeholders and provide data-driven recommendations
*   Maintain familiarity with industry and company progress and problems in assigned technical areas
*   Perform other job-related duties as assigned

**Who You Are:**

**As a successful candidate, you will bring the following to the team:**

*   Deep understanding of integrated IT environments and cloud architecture, as well as modern software development and deployment practices
*   Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills
*   Ability to manage change and drive transformation within a large organization
*   Experience utilizing APIs following industry best practices
*   Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, R, SQL
*   Cloud & DevOps proficiency
*   Experience with LLM integration, RAG, and prompt engineering
*   Experience with agent orchestration frameworks (LangGraph, LangChain, AutoGen, or similar)
*   Proficient with code repositories like Github

**Educational Requirements**

**Required Qualifications:**

*   Bachelor’s Degree
*   Experience: 5+ years

**Preferred Qualifications**

*   Experience with PowerApps/Power Automate

*   Background in detailed analysis of complex systems involving large datasets
