### Description
Join SumUp as a freelance Field Sales Consultant and kickstart your career in sales with unlimited earning potential! SumUp empowers small businesses with smart, user-friendly financial solutions, from card payments to comprehensive POS systems, allowing entrepreneurs to focus entirely on their core business.

In this highly autonomous role, you'll be your own boss, building relationships with local businesses and demonstrating how SumUp can simplify their operations. Your primary focus will be the direct sale of our solutions, whether it's mobile card terminals or complete payment systems. This performance-based role offers attractive, uncapped commissions, maximum flexibility to work when and where you want, and access to top-tier products with high market potential. We also provide comprehensive training and sales support to ensure you're never alone in your journey.

### Responsibilities
*   **Active New Customer Acquisition**: Identify and win local businesses for SumUp.
*   **Personal Meetings and Consulting**: Present our products and demonstrate their benefits to potential clients.
*   **Build Customer Relationships**: Secure long-term partnerships through excellent service.
*   **Own Customer and Lead Management**: Independently manage and build your client portfolio.

### Qualifications
*   Passion for sales and strong self-motivation.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer support is an advantage.
*   Excellent communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Very good German language skills.
