Are you ready to kick-start your career?

Here at GXO, we are currently recruiting for a **Resource Assistant** to join our team in **Avonmouth**, supporting one of our new business wins! As a Resource Assistant, you will be supporting the planning manager. You will focus on both short and long-term planning of core staffing and temporary agency volumes.

This is a full time, permanent position, predominately working Monday to Friday, 09:00 till 17:00.

**Pay, benefits and more:**

We’re looking to offer a salary of up to **£36,000.00** per annum and 25 days annual leave (plus bank holidays). Your benefits package includes flexible dental insurance plans, a company sponsored pension scheme, a 24/7 online GP service, life assurance and a fully comprehensive employee assistance programme. You’ll also have access to our MyBenefits platform offering a variety of high street discounts, a cycle to work scheme, cashback cards, a saving scheme and much more!

**What you’ll do on a typical day:**

*   Assist in creating and maintaining shift schedules and training plans for warehouse staff.
*   Analyse operational data to forecast resource needs and identify trends.
*   Produce management information reports to support planning decisions.
*   Communicate with warehouse teams and stakeholders to align on staffing and operation charges
*   Support the implementation of planning tools, systems and assist with continuous improvement related to warehouse efficiency

**What you need to succeed at GXO:**

*   Excellent attention to detail with a strong proficiency in excel and other office applications
*   Experience within resource planning, ideally within a fast-paced logistics environment
*   Strong communication skills, both written and verbal
