### Description
We are seeking a highly driven and ambitious Media Buyer to scale our advertising campaigns and consistently discover new winning strategies. You will work directly with the Founder and the Creative Team, taking full ownership of performance outcomes across various platforms. This role offers the flexibility of remote work with flexible hours, fostering an environment of full creative freedom in testing and budget allocation. We operate with a 'no fluff, just numbers' philosophy – if you can scale, you will be compensated like a top performer, with a fixed salary plus a percentage of profit/ROAS, and a compelling bonus structure for long-term collaboration and significant growth potential.

### Responsibilities
-   **Daily Ad Management**: Execute and optimize campaigns across major platforms including TikTok, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), and Google Ads.
-   **Strategic Planning**: Develop comprehensive budget plans, implement robust scaling strategies, and manage advanced retargeting efforts.
-   **Experimentation**: Conduct extensive split-tests, hook-tests, and audience tests to identify top-performing creatives and targeting segments.
-   **Reporting & Optimization**: Analyze campaign performance diligently based on ROAS and other key KPIs, driving continuous optimization and improvement.
-   **Cross-functional Collaboration**: Work closely and synergistically with the Creative Team to generate and iterate on innovative ad concepts.

### Qualifications
-   1+ year of hands-on experience with Paid Ads, specifically within E-Commerce and TikTok platforms.
-   A strong data-driven mindset, with a deep understanding of KPIs, ROAS optimization, and performance analytics.
-   Highly structured work approach coupled with strong self-reliance and personal accountability.
-   Fluent in English with a distinct performance-oriented mindset.
-   **Unique Hiring Process**: The hiring process includes a 1-week test campaign (with a provided budget) to demonstrate your capabilities, followed by a 1-month probationary contract with weekly KPIs. Successful completion of this phase leads to a long-term contract (6-12 months + bonus structure).

We're not about rigid career ladders; we're about recognizing and rewarding performance.
