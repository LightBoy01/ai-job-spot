### **About the Role**
Our Global Marketing Team is seeking a **Senior Video Editor & Motion Designer** with proven expertise in **generative AI for video and motion graphics**.
In this role, you will lead the production of **high-quality video assets**, shape the creative direction of campaigns, and integrate **AI-driven tools and workflows** into the heart of our content creation process.
This is a senior-level position where you will combine **technical mastery, creative vision, and AI expertise** to deliver innovative, impactful, and scalable content for global audiences.
### **Main Responsibilities**
*   Edit and produce **high-end video content** for campaigns across multiple platforms (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, etc.).
*   Design and execute **motion graphics and animations** that enhance brand storytelling.
*   Lead the **integration of generative AI tools** (e.g., Runway, Pika, MidJourney, Veo) into the creative workflow, from ideation to execution.
*   Ensure **polished final outputs** with color grading, sound design, captions, and platform-optimized versions.
*   Collaborate with Creative and Marketing teams to **conceptualize campaigns** that blend traditional craft with AI-powered innovation.
*   Stay at the forefront of **video, design, and AI trends**, proactively applying them to elevate our creative output.
*   **Mentor junior team members** on editing, motion design, and effective use of AI in production.
*   Continuously **optimize workflows**, identifying areas where AI can accelerate processes and expand creative possibilities.
### **About You**
*   6+ years of experience in **video editing and motion graphics**, ideally in creative agencies or global marketing teams.
*   Expert proficiency in **Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects**.
*   Mandatory hands-on experience with **generative AI platforms** for video and motion graphics (e.g., Runway, Pika, Sora, MidJourney, Veo).
*   Strong creative vision with the ability to **merge storytelling, design, and technology**.
*   Track record of delivering **innovative, large-scale video campaigns**.
*   Experience as a **videographer** is a plus.
*   Excellent communication skills in **English and Spanish**.
*   Proactive, curious, and comfortable leading projects in a **fast-paced, global environment**.
*   Portfolio/reel that demonstrates **both traditional editing skills and AI-driven creative work**.
