At Kelsey-Seybold Clinic, our Neurologists diagnose, treat, and manage patients with neurological disorders. They see patients in the office from 8-5pm Monday through Friday. One of the group neurologists takes call during weekday evenings and on weekends every six weeks on average. Kelsey Seybold Clinic has a 24-hour call center and after-hours registered triage nurse to assist in patient care.
