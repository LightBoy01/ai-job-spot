**Why Join Us**

This is your chance to build a marketing engine from the ground up — with the freedom to innovate and the support of leadership who values creativity and results. You’ll put your fingerprints on the growth story of a category-leading brand in a booming health, wellness, and longevity industry.

This role isn’t just about managing campaigns, it’s about shaping the future of our company. You’ll have the opportunity to define strategy, execute with agility, and scale your impact, while working closely with founders who are deeply passionate about the mission.

**About We Inspect**

At We Inspect, we empower people everywhere with the tools and services they need to create healthy homes. Our mission is to provide clarity and confidence during one of the most vulnerable moments in a person’s life: when they suspect their home may be making them sick or preventing them from living optimally.

We’ve pioneered a consultative, data-driven approach to mold inspections, helping thousands of families nationwide regain control of their health and their homes. Our vision is to improve global health and wellness by changing the way people interact with their environments, not only for those struggling with illness but also for those committed to longevity and peak performance.

**Who We Serve**

We partner with individuals and families navigating complex chronic illnesses potentially tied to their living environments, such as mold-related illness, Lyme disease, autoimmune conditions, and chronic fatigue.

We also serve proactive health seekers focused on longevity, performance, and prevention. These clients may not be sick, but they want to ensure their home environment is supporting optimal health, energy, and lifespan.

Many of our clients are referred by functional and integrative medicine practitioners, longevity experts, and wellness professionals. Regardless of their starting point, they come to us seeking answers, clarity, and a trusted guide to help them make their home a safe place to heal and thrive.

**The Opportunity**

We Inspect is entering its next stage of growth, and we’re seeking a mission-driven Marketing Leader to build and scale the full marketing engine of the company. You will own all elements of marketing operations and be responsible for everything from strategy, to implementation, and reporting and optimization across all platforms. 

A marketing leader who can capture attention, tell our story, and turn visibility into revenue.

This is not a “sit in strategy meetings and send briefs” job. It’s a builder + doer role for someone who:

*   Lives where culture and attention live.
*   Can craft and scale multichannel strategies (B2C + B2B).
*   Loves creating content, testing ideas, and iterating fast.
*   Understands brand as story, connection, and trust — not just logos and color palettes.
*   Utilizes AI as an essential tool across all marketing operations. 

You’ll have freedom to test ideas and engage specialists where needed. As the company grows, you’ll shape and expand the marketing team to scale your vision.

**What You’ll Do**

*   **Own the Full Funnel**: Build multichannel strategies across social, web, email, SEO/GEO, paid media, and thought leadership. Use paid not as a crutch, but as a strategic amplifier — for retargeting, audience building, and scaling what’s already working. Then execute directly with a lean team.
*   **Tell the Story & Shape the Brand**: Craft and elevate We Inspect’s brand image by making the company (and its founders) a relatable, trusted authority. Use storytelling, content, and community-building to create authentic connection, build trust, and make our expertise both approachable and memorable.
*   **Hack Attention**: Create and repurpose content for IG, YouTube, TikTok, FB, LinkedIn, Reddit, and wherever the right eyeballs are. Secure podcast appearances, guest features, interviews, and media placements for the founders and company 
*   **Blend Brand + Performance**: Elevate our brand while generating leads and revenue. Balance long-term authority with short-term growth.
*   **Leverage AI & Data**: Use AI to scale output, personalize experiences, analyze data, and speed up iteration. AI is baked into all marketing operations. Track KPIs from awareness → booked inspections.
*   **Lead**: Manage a small, remote team by setting KPIs, coaching talent, and creating accountability in a nimble environment. You’ll have access to external specialists when needed, and the opportunity to expand and shape your own team as the company grows.
*   **Build**: Develop operational systems to support all marketing initiatives. 
*   **Innovate**: Experiment with new platforms, creative formats, and storytelling techniques to stay ahead of cultural shifts.
