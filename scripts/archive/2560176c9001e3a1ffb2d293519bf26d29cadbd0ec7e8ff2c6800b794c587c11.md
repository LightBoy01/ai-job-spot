Job Description:  

The candidate will be involved in all aspects of developing the automated inspection tool. The method for driving an array of MEMS devices under a high-resolution digital camera and optics will be developed for image acquisition. An AI-based image analyzer must be properly trained to recognize passing and failing MEMS devices with an accuracy of greater than 95% to provide an outgoing quality assurance report in a time-efficient manner. Strong and independent problem-solving skills are critical for this role. We will require the candidates to introduce and describe their project development experience in the interview.

Key Job Functions:

*   Developing method of image acquisition
*   Integrate hardware and software for image acquisition
*   Train/develop AI-based image analysis software
*   Determine efficient reporting methodology
*   Analyze/improve system to achieve high accuracy

Required Qualifications

*   GPA over 3.0/4
*   Familiarity with hardware integration
*   Experience in coding using LabView, Python and C+ is a plus.
*   Experience with computer vision related coding project is an asset.
*   Ability of using general electronic testing equipment, such as DMM, power supply, oscilloscope, etc.
*   Applicant with background of microelectronic, engineering physic and computer science will be an asset.
*   4-month co-op session.
*   Working on-site

Education Requirement: 3rd or 4th year undergraduate student.
