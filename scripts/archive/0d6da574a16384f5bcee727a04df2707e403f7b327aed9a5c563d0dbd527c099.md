**Company Description:**

*   **Do you have a passion for complex problem solving?**
*   **Do you want to help New Zealand based and international organisations harness opportunities and deliver greater value?**
*   **Do you want to help New Zealand organisations develop solutions to their most important and complex challenges?**

Deloitte’s Strategy &amp; Business Design team helps organisations navigate today, plan for tomorrow and make the best choices on how to win and deliver value in their chosen markets.

**The team you’ll join**

You’ll join Deloitte’s Strategy &amp; Business Design team, based in Wellington and join a team of innovative strategists with diverse backgrounds and skills, working with some of the largest and most iconic organisations in New Zealand. We help our clients navigate the challenges of today, imagine and prepare for tomorrow, and make the best strategic choices to transform their businesses and drive sustainable growth.

Our projects span developing organisational strategy, to designing and implementing market entry and growth strategies to developing innovative operating models and facilitating transformative change. We are focused on delivering impact, ensuring our work drives results that matter.

The success of our team is built on recruiting the best people who share our core values - a passion for embracing change and making a meaningful difference on every engagement. We value collaboration, curiosity, and energy, blending analysis and insight to tackle our clients’ toughest problems. Continuous learning is at the heart of what we do, offering team members at every level structured development opportunity within a supportive, high-performance environment.

We are proud to foster a diverse and inclusive culture where team members feel valued and empowered to thrive. Our policies promote equal opportunity for all, and our commitment to respect and authenticity ensures individuals can bring their best selves to work.

**Job Description:**

**The impact you’ll make**

*   Collaborating with a team of consultants, guided by Managers, Directors and Partners, to address complex and high-impact client challenges.
*   Working directly with client executives across diverse industries, gaining insight into their most pressing strategic issues and facilitating informed decision making.
*   Developing actionable insights to drive client growth, enhance performance, and establish sustainable competitive advantages.
*   Contributing to project work streams, and assisting in delivering high-quality outcomes
*   Engaging in meaningful collaboration within your team, playing a role in innovation and a shared commitment to excellence.
*   Expanding your knowledge and skills daily through exposure to varied industries, robust tools and methodologies, structured training, and tailored development programmes.

**Qualifications:**

**Your expertise**

*   2-3+&nbsp;years of demonstrated experience working as part of a team on organisational strategy or delivering enterprise level change in the public or private sector.
*   Strong quantitative skills, with the ability to collect, transform, and interpret data to derive insights and tackle complex business problems effectively.
*   Ability to communicate, present and contextualise complex ideas concisely and accurately while adjusting style and tone to suit your audience.
*   Strong interpersonal and communication skills, enabling you to share ideas with varied audiences and foster strong relationships with clients and your team.
*   A proactive approach to learning and growth, with a positive outlook when solving challenging problems and leveraging Deloitte’s network of subject matter experts.
*   An understanding of generative AI and proficiency in tools such as Excel and PowerPoint, with a willingness to adopt new technologies to innovate and enhance client solutions.
