### Description
Are you a creative student eager to make a significant impact on a fast-growing international brand? hey contact heroes, one of the most innovative BPO and Customer Experience service providers in the DACH region, is looking for a **Social Media Content Creator (m/f/d)**. This **Minijob (6-8 hours per week)** role, based in **Hamburg / Remote**, offers a unique opportunity to shape our LinkedIn presence and enhance our brand visibility.

We are an international BPO and Customer Experience service provider with over 550 employees across 17 countries. Our focus is on developing modern, technology-based CX solutions for businesses in e-commerce, telecommunications, energy, mobility, and many other sectors. We prioritize scalability, quality, remote excellence, and the powerful synergy of human talent and advanced technology.

### Responsibilities
As our Social Media Content Creator, you will:
*   Develop and create engaging social media content for LinkedIn, including posts, slides, short video snippets, and share-pics.
*   Support content planning and editorial preparation processes.
*   Research current trends, topics, and best practices in CX, digitalization, Nearshore/Offshore strategies, and leadership.
*   Transform internal content into clear, high-quality LinkedIn formats.
*   Collaborate closely with the management board, marketing, and recruiting teams.
*   *Optional:* Provide live coverage for events, capturing photo and video material.

### Qualifications
To thrive in this role, you should bring:
*   Enrollment as a student, ideally in Communications, Media, Marketing, Journalism, Business Administration, Digital Media, or similar fields.
*   Strong affinity for social media, particularly LinkedIn.
*   A creative eye, excellent language skills, and clear expression.
*   Proficiency in Canva, CapCut, or comparable content creation tools.
*   Reliability, strong self-organization, and enthusiasm for taking initiative and ownership.
*   A keen interest in modern businesses, digitalization, and brand building.
