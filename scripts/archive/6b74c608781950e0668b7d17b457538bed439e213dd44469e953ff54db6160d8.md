**Job Summary**  

We are seeking two motivated and curious **AI Intern (Co-op Student)** to join our team for the January 2026 semester (January - April 2026). In this role, you will work closely with ProCogia’s data science and engineering teams to help **productionize AI agentic workflows**. This is a hands-on opportunity to apply your technical skills in real projects, contribute to cutting-edge solutions, and gain experience in enterprise AI delivery. 

**Key Responsibilities** 

*   Assist in designing, testing, and deploying **AI agentic workflows** for internal and client-facing projects. 

*   Support the integration of LLMs (e.g., Claude, GPT, open-source models) into enterprise systems. 

*   Work with orchestration tools (e.g., MCP, N8N, LangChain) to automate tasks and build reliable workflows. 

*   Collaborate with senior consultants to document and standardize development processes. 

*   Participate in troubleshooting, debugging, and refining workflow performance. 

*   Research emerging tools, frameworks, and best practices in **AI agents, orchestration, and workflow automation**. 

*   Contribute to knowledge sharing through presentations, demos, or documentation. 

**What You Bring** 

*   Currently enrolled in a university program in **Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, or related fields**. 

*   Strong interest in AI/ML, workflow automation, and applied enterprise solutions. 

*   Familiarity with **Python** (required); experience with APIs and RESTful services is a plus. 

*   Exposure to cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP) preferred. 

*   Interest or basic knowledge of LLMs, prompt engineering, or workflow tools (LangChain, N8N, MCP, etc.) is an asset. 

*   Strong problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and eagerness to learn. 

*   Excellent communication and teamwork abilities
