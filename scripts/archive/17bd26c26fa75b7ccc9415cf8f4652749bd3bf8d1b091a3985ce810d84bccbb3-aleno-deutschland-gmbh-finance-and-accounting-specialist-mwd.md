### Description
As a **Finance & Accounting Specialist**, you play a central role in aleno's growth. You ensure that our financial processes function reliably, create transparency, and keep the company on track.

You are the financial hub between accounting, operations, and strategic financial management.
Approximately 80% of your time will be dedicated to operational tasks related to accounting and daily business – you maintain the financial foundations and ensure all processes are correct.
The remaining 20% will be used to drive analyses, reporting, and planning – thereby actively contributing to the further development of our financial processes.
This role is ideal if you bring a solid foundation in accounting and want to develop yourself towards Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A).

### Responsibilities
**Operational:**
- Independent management of the ongoing accounting for the German subsidiary.
- Coordination of accounting for the Swiss parent company and at group level.
- Ensuring all receipts, account assignments, and bookings are correct and complete.
- Interface between Finance and Customer Success for billing and payment-related topics.
- Responsibility for invoicing and dunning processes.
- Collaboration with and technical guidance of a colleague in accounting.
- Support for the introduction of Stripe and ensuring correct booking of all transactions.
- Control and ensure all services are properly invoiced.
- Contribution to monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements and coordination with external specialists.

**Strategic:**
- Contribution to monthly banking and investor reporting.
- Actual-vs-target analyses and support for monthly performance reports.
- Cost analyses and optimization of the cost structure.
- Support in the annual budget process.
- Participation in strategic financial projects.

### Qualifications
- Several years of experience in accounting, ideally from a fiduciary office or an in-house finance function.
- Knowledge of HGB (German Commercial Code) and German tax regulations (experience with DATEV).
- Solid knowledge of accounting systems like Xero or similar tools, and proficiency (experience in Financial Modelling is a plus).
- Analytical and entrepreneurial mindset, detail-oriented work, and ability to act as a strong business partner for other teams.
- Enjoy looking beyond the obvious: you don't just want to book, but to understand, optimize, and further develop processes.
- Proactive, communicative, eager to learn, willing to take responsibility, and open to trying new things.
- Fluent in German and very good English.

**Nice-to-haves:**
- Experience with BI tools (e.g., Power BI, Looker, or Tableau) to leverage data for analysis and decision-making.
- A good understanding of SaaS metrics and their drivers, enabling you to derive clear recommendations for action.
- Experience with Hubspot and Stripe.
