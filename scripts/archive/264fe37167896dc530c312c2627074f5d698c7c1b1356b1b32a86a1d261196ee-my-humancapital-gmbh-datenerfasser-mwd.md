### Description

On behalf of our partner company in Munich, we are seeking a dedicated **Data Entry Specialist (m/f/d)** to join their team as soon as possible. This role is crucial for maintaining accurate records and supporting various administrative functions within a company that truly values its employees. You'll join a supportive environment that prioritizes work-life balance, offers a convenient in-house canteen, provides structured onboarding by experienced colleagues, and numerous opportunities for internal professional development.

### Responsibilities

*   Responsible for comprehensive document management.
*   Pre-sorting and classification of documents.
*   Comparing and correcting data within the system.
*   Supporting the team with various administrative tasks as they arise.

### Qualifications

*   Ideally, you will have initial experience as a Data Entry Specialist (m/f/d) or Administrator (m/f/d).
*   You demonstrate reliability, commitment, diligence, and a high level of motivation in your work.
*   You are comfortable and proficient in using computers.
*   Excellent German and good English language skills complete your profile.
