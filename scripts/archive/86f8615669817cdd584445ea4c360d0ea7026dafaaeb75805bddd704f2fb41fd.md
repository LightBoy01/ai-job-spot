**Sr Backend/LLM Ops Engineer Role at Fuse Finance**
====================================================

[Fuse](https://www.fusefinance.com) is a high-growth New York City startup revolutionizing financial services infrastructure through an innovative low-code tool. Backed by top-tier venture capital, the company is creating a transformative self-service platform for configuration, integration, and automation in financial technology. We are now expanding our capabilities to leverage artificial intelligence in revolutionizing how financial services workflows and integrations are built.

**Position Overview**
---------------------

We are seeking a Senior Backend engineer with hands-on experience with **LLM ops** to spearhead our artificial intelligence agents initiatives, focusing on leveraging generative AI to enhance our low-code platform capabilities. This role combines deep technical expertise in Backend development with practical experience in implementing AI agents solutions, particularly in generating and optimizing low-code workflows, user interfaces, and data schemas.

**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------

The Senior **Backend/LLM ops** Engineer will drive the development and implementation of AI-powered features across our platform. Core responsibilities include:

*   Building and optimizing AI agent pipelines that leverage leading language models and AI services to enhance our low-code platform capabilities.
    
*   Developing sophisticated agentic workflows to ensure high-quality answers of our AI systems, with a particular focus on domain knowledge from low-code data sources.
    
*   Designing and implementing AI agent systems that can effectively generate and validate use case specific workflows, user interfaces, and data schemas.
    
*   Creating robust evaluation frameworks to measure and improve the quality of AI-generated outputs within our platform.
    
*   Collaborating with product and engineering teams to seamlessly integrate AI capabilities into our existing platform architecture.
    
*   Establishing best practices for AI implementation, including monitoring, versioning, and continuous improvement of models.
