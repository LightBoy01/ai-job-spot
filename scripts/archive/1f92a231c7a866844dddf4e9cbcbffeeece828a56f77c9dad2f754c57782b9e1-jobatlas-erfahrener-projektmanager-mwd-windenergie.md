### Description\nJoin a leading renewable energy company based in Mainz, Germany, dedicated to realizing an emission-free future by implementing large-scale solar and wind projects nationwide. As an experienced Project Manager for Wind Energy, you will play a pivotal role in this visionary mission, overseeing significant wind projects from initial planning through to successful completion, ensuring their smooth and efficient execution.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Take full responsibility for the project development and execution of wind farms until completion.\n- Conduct thorough analyses, forecasts, and site security measures for new locations.\n- Develop comprehensive planning concepts and commission necessary expert reports.\n- Contractually secure land properties and manage all aspects of the approval procedures.\n- Regularly communicate project status to stakeholders and navigate the political landscape.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Several years of relevant professional experience in a comparable project management position.\n- A completed degree in Renewable Energies or a related field.\n- Practical experience applying various project management tools.\n- Excellent command of German and good English language skills.\n- Proficiency in MS Office Suite.\n- Proven ability to work reliably both independently and as part of a team.\n- Possession of a driver's license and willingness to undertake business travel.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a seasoned Project Manager passionate about renewable energy to make a tangible impact on Germany's energy transition. The company fosters a supportive and forward-thinking environment, offering not only a robust, regularly adjusted salary but also excellent work-life balance with flexible hours, remote work options, and 30 days of vacation. Furthermore, you'll benefit from paid professional development, a modern office with amenities like daily fresh fruit, coffee, and tea, and attractive financial benefits including subsidies for capital-forming benefits and pension contributions. If you're ready to drive significant wind energy projects and contribute to a sustainable future, this role offers both challenge and reward within a company that truly values its employees.
