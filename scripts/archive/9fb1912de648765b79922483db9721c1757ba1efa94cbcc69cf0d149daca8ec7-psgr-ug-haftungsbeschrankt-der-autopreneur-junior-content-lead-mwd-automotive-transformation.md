### Description

"Der Autopreneur" is a leading business newsletter and podcast focused on automotive transformation, boasting over 33,000 subscribers and 51,000 LinkedIn followers. Founded by Philipp Raasch, an industry veteran with 15+ years of experience (ex-Mercedes), its content is read by decision-makers at OEMs, suppliers, and tech companies.

We are seeking a proactive and detail-oriented individual to become my right hand in content – someone who thinks critically, takes responsibility, and is eager to grow with the platform. This isn't just an assistant role; it's an opportunity to develop into a Junior Content Lead. You will act as our crucial safety net and quality gate, ensuring our voice and standards are consistently met.

This position offers direct mentorship from an industry expert, providing real responsibility where your work directly reaches VPs and board members. You'll gain deep expertise in automotive transformation, building a robust portfolio that will open doors in media, content, or the automotive sector. The role comes with a clear growth trajectory, starting with quality control and podcast support, evolving into taking ownership of new formats and ultimately becoming a Content Lead.

This is a flexible role, ideally suited for a working student or freelancer, requiring 10-20 hours per week with fixed deadlines, and is 100% remote.

### Responsibilities

As a key member of the "Autopreneur" team, your responsibilities will include:

*   **Quality Control (German + English):**
    *   Performing the final check for all newsletters before publication.
    *   Identifying and correcting errors, inconsistencies, and stylistic weaknesses.
    *   Ensuring logical flow, structural integrity, and consistent brand tonality.
    *   Providing support for research and drafting content as needed.
*   **Podcast Production:**
    *   Preparing for interviews: conducting guest research, developing question catalogs, and shaping episode concepts.
    *   Managing post-production: meticulously reviewing and editing audio, optimizing storylines.
    *   Adapting key learnings into concise summaries for the newsletter.
    *   Creating and editing presentations synced with audio for YouTube videos.
*   **LinkedIn Management:**
    *   Publishing posts on behalf of Philipp Raasch.
    *   Responding to comments and actively managing the community.
    *   Cultivating engagement and moderating discussions.
*   **Process Ownership:**
    *   Documenting and continuously optimizing content workflows.
    *   Setting up and refining AI-powered processes using tools like Claude and ChatGPT.
    *   Proactively proposing and implementing improvements across content operations.

### Qualifications

**Must-Have:**

*   Currently a student (4th semester or higher) or a freelancer with relevant content experience.
*   **Demonstrable Content Experience:** A portfolio showcasing internships, projects, or professional work.
*   **Detail-Oriented:** An exceptional eye for detail, ensuring no error or inconsistency goes unnoticed.
*   **Flawless German + English:** Proficient written communication in both languages with a strong sense for appropriate tonality.
*   **AI Power-User:** Routine experience with AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, or similar platforms.
*   **Autonomous:** Proactive mindset; capable of independent thought and action without constant direction.
*   Genuine interest in Automotive, Tech, and Business Transformation.

**Nice-to-Have:**

*   Experience in podcast or video editing.
*   Strong understanding and experience with LinkedIn and general social media best practices.
*   Academic background in Journalism, Communication, or Media Production.
*   Basic knowledge of the automotive industry (e.g., E-Mobility, Software, Autonomous Driving).
