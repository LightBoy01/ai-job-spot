### Description\nThis role is for a skilled Fashion Photographer responsible for the entire photography workflow, from set preparation and styling support to independent execution of diverse shoots (model, bust, still life). The successful candidate will also manage post-production tasks including image sorting, retouching, color adjustments, and quality control, ensuring all visual assets meet client-specific and e-commerce requirements. Collaboration with fashion stylists and account managers is key to delivering high-quality, impactful visuals for major international brands.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Production preparation and set-up, both guided and self-initiated.\n- Provide support to fashion stylists during shoots.\n- Independently execute various types of photo shoots (model, bust, still life).\n- Sort and process image data according to customer-specific guidelines.\n- Perform retouches and color adjustments.\n- Conduct error correction and create visual looks.\n- Ensure quality control and assurance throughout the process.\n- Capture, prepare, and analyze relevant data.\n- Work in close collaboration with fashion stylists and account managers.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed vocational training as a photographer.\n- A minimum of two to three years of professional experience in e-commerce, fashion, or lifestyle photography, and/or in a creative agency with relevant shooting experience.\n- Demonstrated high creative standards and a strong eye for detail.\n- A pronounced service-oriented mindset and an understanding of client needs.\n- Very good understanding of e-commerce principles and visual requirements.\n- High degree of teamwork, flexibility, and resilience.\n- Proficient handling of professional camera equipment and Capture One software.\n- Excellent communication skills in both German and English.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a dedicated Fashion Photographer to thrive in a dynamic, award-winning environment. The role offers significant autonomy in managing diverse shoots for prominent international brands, combined with the support of a motivated and collaborative team. With a focus on high-quality visuals for e-commerce, you'll have a direct impact on brand perception while enjoying a secure, long-term career path. If you possess a keen eye for detail, a creative drive, and excellent communication skills, this role provides an ideal platform to elevate your craft and contribute to exciting campaigns.
