### Description\nThis Entry-Level role places you within the Assortment & Supply Chain team, working in close collaboration with Brand Management. You will gain exciting insights into the strategic development of our international distribution brands. You will contribute to market analysis, brand presence optimization, and strategic decision-making in a dynamic, global environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Support in the development and implementation of brand strategies for our international distribution brands.\n- Analyze market, competitor, and distribution data to derive strategic recommendations.\n- Contribute to the design and optimization of brand presence across various sales channels.\n- Work closely with Brand Management, Marketing, and Supply Chain to operationally implement brand strategies.\n- Support in preparing reports, concepts, and presentations for management.\n- Participate in process and tool optimizations within Supply Chain to contribute to efficient brand management.\n- Ensure and maintain master data in the ERP system as a basis for well-founded strategic decisions.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Currently enrolled in studies in Business Administration, Marketing, Brand Management, Strategy, or Supply Chain Management.\n- Interest in brand building and strategy, combined with an understanding of operational processes in the supply chain.\n- Analytical thinking and a good sense for brand positioning and growth strategies.\n- Excellent MS-Office skills (especially Excel and PowerPoint).\n- Structured, independent, and solution-oriented work style.\n- Strong communication skills, team spirit, and initiative.\n- Very good spoken and written English and German skills.\n- Willingness for occasional business trips.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role offers a fantastic opportunity for an entry-level professional to gain hands-on experience in brand strategy and supply chain management within a dynamic, international, and fast-growing company. You'll be part of a highly motivated, collaborative team with flat hierarchies and a strong \"Du\"-culture, encouraging active participation and personal growth from day one. The company provides a personal development budget, diverse projects, and a supportive environment, including flexible work arrangements (home office), a well-equipped office in Munich, and various perks like subsidized public transport, fitness deals, and freebies. It's an inclusive workplace that values diversity and offers a genuine chance to make a tangible impact and grow your career.
