**Req ID:** 340387 

NTT DATA strives to hire exceptional, innovative and passionate individuals who want to grow with us. If you want to be part of an inclusive, adaptable, and forward-thinking organization, apply now.

We are currently seeking a **Salesforce Business Analyst - Remote Canada role.** 

We are seeking a highly skilled **Salesforce Business Analyst** with expertise across Sales, Service, Experience, AI, CPQ, MuleSoft, and Data Cloud solutions. The ideal candidate will play a pivotal role in bridging business needs with technical solutions, driving digital transformation, and enabling seamless customer and operational journeys.

**Day to Day/Job Function**

*   Partner with business stakeholders to gather, analyze, and document requirements across domains including Customers, Claims, Rebates, Quoting, Approvals, Routings, Products & Pricing, and OEMs.
*   Collaborate with solution architects, developers, and product owners to translate business requirements into functional specifications and user stories.
*   Drive process improvements in Sales, Service, and Experience Cloud environments with a focus on scalability, automation, and AI adoption.
*   Work closely with technical teams on Salesforce CPQ, MuleSoft integrations, and Data Cloud initiatives to deliver seamless data flows and connected customer experiences.
*   Support design and delivery of Einstein AI use cases to improve decision-making, personalization, and predictive insights.
*   Participate in end-to-end testing, ensuring solutions meet business requirements and are aligned with compliance/regulatory needs.
*   Facilitate workshops, requirement sessions, and user training to ensure adoption and value realization.
*   Provide insights on industry best practices, customer experience trends, and future-ready capabilities such as Agentic AI.

**Required Qualifications**

*   8+ years of experience as a Business Analyst in enterprise IT projects.
*   8+ years experience in Salesforce ecosystem (Sales, Service, Experience Clouds) and CPQ functionality.
*   5+ years experience with MuleSoft integration design and Data Cloud data modeling concepts.
*   Familiarity with Einstein AI or other AI-driven business solutions.
*   Strong analytical skills with the ability to map business processes and identify optimization opportunities.
*   Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with experience working in cross-functional teams.
*   Ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver outcomes in a fast-paced environment.

**Preferred Certifications**

*   Salesforce Admin  
      
    

**Preferred Experience**

*   Exposure to Agentic AI and its applications in enterprise business workflows.
*   Knowledge of advanced automation, orchestration, and intelligent routing solutions.
*   Experience in regulated industries with complex workflows (e.g., OEMs, claims, rebates).
*   Salesforce certifications (BA, CPQ Specialist, MuleSoft, or Data Cloud) are a plus 

**Ideal Mindset:**

*   Problem Solver. You are creative but also practical in finding solutions to problems that may arise in the project to avoid potential escalations.
*   Analytical. You like to dissect complex processes and can help forge a path based on your findings.
*   Self-driven. Motivated individual who can efficiently influence delivery in complicated matrix organization."
