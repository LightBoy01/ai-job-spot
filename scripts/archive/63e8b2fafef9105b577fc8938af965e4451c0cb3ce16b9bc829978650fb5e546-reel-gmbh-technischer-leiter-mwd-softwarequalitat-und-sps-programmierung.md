### Description\nReel GmbH is one of the world's leading and pioneering partners for aluminum smelters, nuclear power plants, and other industrial sectors. We offer our customers process-oriented solutions and tailor-made systems in the field of special crane construction, manipulators, and handling equipment from a single source. Integrated into the French REEL INTERNATIONAL and a worldwide corporate and manufacturing network with decades of experience, we are an internationally oriented company with a strong export focus. We value strong customer proximity and open, modern work structures. In this key role, you will be responsible for ensuring the software quality of our special machinery and handling equipment. After successful onboarding, you will take over the leadership of our PLC programmers and contribute significantly to the standardization and optimization of our quality assurance processes.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Ensure software quality assurance measures for special machinery throughout all project phases (planning, software creation, integration tests, etc.).\n- Support simulations, integration tests, and factory acceptance tests. Document test results, create data backups, and deviation reports. Develop improvement suggestions and monitor their implementation.\n- Work on improving and developing quality assurance measures, programming guidelines, checklists, test instructions, and standardizing PLC programming, considering corporate guidelines and specific product requirements.\n- Ensure functionality, reliability, maintainability, usability, and cyber resilience. If necessary, specify test criteria and supplement or expand checklists and test methods.\n- Primarily work with Siemens TIA Portal, common fieldbus systems, PC connectivity, control technology, databases, as well as OPC UA and video technology.\n- After successful onboarding, assume leadership of our PLC programmers, including planning, organizing, and controlling tasks and work processes.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Engineering degree or technical education (m/f/d) in Automation Technology or a comparable qualification, along with several years of professional experience in a similar field.\n- **Technical Know-how:** Competence in PLC programming, automation technology, and software development, as well as a good technical understanding.\n- **Experience in Software Testing:** Knowledge of standard contents, testing methods, and simulation systems.\n- **Organizational and Communication Skills:** Competent organization and motivation of software teams, as well as collaboration with internal and external stakeholders.\n- **IT and Security Knowledge:** Understanding of data security, cyber security, and modern software environments.\n- Fluent German and English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a unique opportunity to join a globally leading company that pioneers tailor-made solutions for critical industries. The role offers a compelling blend of deep technical expertise in software quality assurance for PLC systems and a clear path to leadership, taking charge of the PLC programming team after onboarding. Reel GmbH fosters an open, collaborative, and trusting environment, providing significant autonomy, flexible working conditions, and robust opportunities for professional growth and continuous learning. Benefits include 30 days of vacation, home office options, flexible working hours, employee discounts, and holiday bonuses. It's an ideal role for an experienced automation professional looking to make a significant impact and grow into a leadership position within a supportive and innovative team.
