**Your Role**
The People Analytics team at Nutanix is looking for an analyst with a passion to help leaders make data-driven decisions about our employees. This person will excel at creating visual dashboards that allows clients to quickly understand the story and will also be adept at approaching problems with an analytical mindset and persistence to get to the root of the problem.
The People Analytics Team is responsible for understanding the employee experience across the employee life cycle, leveraging multiple data inputs to measure and accelerate the employee experience, and ultimately, providing leaders with insights and data to help them make informed decisions about our people.
The team leverages data from many sources, including HRIS, ATS (Applicant Tracking System), survey data, and other people program data (e.g., Learning Management System).
*   Develop complex Tableau dashboards that leverage multiple data sources by running discovery sessions to gather client requirements and building visualizations to quickly identify gaps, strengths, and trends across time
*   Partner with a data engineer by communicating and refining Tableau dashboard needs to ensure data sources are structured to support client objectives.
*   Provide ongoing client support, expertly translating client questions into data and insights, managing the project timelines and client expectations, and documenting requirements (e.g., metric definitions)
*   Carefully handle sensitive employee data, ensuring necessary confidentiality and security
*   Analyze unstructured / structured disparate data sources and running statistical analysis to deliver actionable  insights
*   Consult with HR leaders and business leaders across the organization, leveraging data and insights to tell a compelling story and help leaders make data-driven decisions
*   Support HR organization’s data needs by looking for ways to automate data processes and streamline reporting
*   Support enterprise employee listening program by building surveys and updating reporting within the survey tool, and providing ongoing project support, as needed
**What You Will Bring**
