Be an integral part of an agile team that's constantly pushing the envelope to enhance, build, and deliver top-notch technology products.
As a Senior Lead Software Engineer at JPMorgan Chase within the Commercial and Investment Bank, you will play a crucial role in an agile team dedicated to enhancing, building, and delivering trusted, market-leading technology products in a secure, stable, and scalable manner. Your expertise and contributions will promote significant business impact, as you apply your deep technical knowledge and problem-solving skills to address a wide range of challenges across various technologies and applications.
**Job responsibilities**
*   Own day-to-day execution of AI initiatives, ensuring agents and platform features are delivered on time and with impact.
*   Partner with the Technical Product Owner to manage backlog priorities, sprint planning, and team velocity.
*   Clear delivery blockers, coordinate across architects, developers, and pods, and maintain delivery cadence.
*   Track and report progress, risks, and outcomes to the Head of AI and stakeholders.
*   Ensure alignment between business needs and technology execution through demos, reviews, and adoption tracking.
*   Build a culture of accountability, speed, and continuous improvement within the AI delivery team.
*   Collaborate with the Engineering Lead to ensure delivery adheres to governance, security, and compliance standards.
*   Support business and technology stakeholders in identifying, shaping, and executing high-value AI initiatives.
**Required qualifications, capabilities, and skills**
*   Formal training or certification on software engineering concepts and 5+ years applied experience
*   Experience in program or delivery management in technology, data, or AI domains
*   Proven track record for leading cross-functional teams (engineering, product, architecture) to deliver complex initiatives
*   Experience in vendor evaluation and managing vendor relationship, POCs, and integrations,
*   Strong background in agile delivery methods (Scrum, Kanban, or hybrid models), Agile backlog and sprint planning
*   Ability to translate strategic goals into actionable delivery plans
*   Skilled at removing blockers, prioritizing effectively, and driving momentum
*   Strong communication and stakeholder management skills – abile to engage both business and technology leaders
*   Adept at progress tracking, reporting, and risk management
*   Familiarity with AI/ML concepts, platforms, and enterprise adoption challenges
*   Delivery & program management
**Preferred qualifications, capabilities, and skills**
*   Governance, compliance, and security awareness
*   Clear written and verbal communication
*   Problem-solving and decision-making under pressure
