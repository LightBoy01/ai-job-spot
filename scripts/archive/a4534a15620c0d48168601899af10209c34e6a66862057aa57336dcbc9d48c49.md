**About The Role**
------------------

As Fora’s Senior Manager, Editorial & Content, you will oversee all traveler-facing content, shaping this growing brand’s voice and approach to storytelling from the ground up. Reporting to the VP of Brand & Content, you’ll have the rare opportunity to build and scale our nascent editorial presence across channels, with the twin goals of fueling traveler loyalty and demand and establishing Fora as a leading voice in travel content.

**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------

*   Lead strategy and operations for all of our traveler-facing content, including inspirational editorial storytelling, destination content, and special projects such as data-driven reports and the annual Hot List
*   Extend our brand reach by developing signature editorial franchises and tentpoles that attract readers, email subscribers, social followers, backlinks, and media coverage
*   Own strategy and content for our new traveler-facing newsletter, including A/B testing roadmap
*   Write, assign, and edit all of the above, evolving Fora’s editorial voice and always raising the bar on quality
*   Manage a roster of freelance contributors and industry experts
*   Track and report on content performance and use data insights to inform strategy
*   Collaborate cross-functionally both within the marketing team (social media, production) and with other teams (product, product operations, design, PR/comms, partnerships) to ideate and produce high-quality content experiences and other campaigns that delight and engage travelers and the travel-obsessed
*   Responsibly use AI to support your workflow while keeping human creativity at the forefront

**Requirements**
----------------

*   7+ years experience in editorial, journalism, or content marketing, with extensive experience managing freelancers
*   Deep familiarity with the travel space—whether as a writer, editor, marketer, or industry insider—and a network of travel writers and subject matter experts
*   Sharp writer and editor with a finely-tuned ear for voice and a rigorous focus on editorial excellence
*   Creative powerhouse who can dream up a new idea and spin out a dozen different ways to package and execute it
*   Knowledge of current SEO best practices and deep curiosity for how AI is transforming the world of SEO
*   Highly collaborative, organized, and adaptable; the pace of change at a startup doesn’t scare you, it excites you
*   Curious about and excited to explore how emerging AI can support content functions
*   Proficient with SEO and analytics tools; our current stack includes Ahrefs, Google Search Console, GA4, and Omni
