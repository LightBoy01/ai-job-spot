### Description
MedNet EC-REP GmbH, headquartered in Münster, is a leading service provider for regulatory representation and market access in Europe. As a European Authorized Representative, UK Responsible Person, and Swiss Authorized Representative, MedNet EC-REP currently supports approximately 700 manufacturers from outside the EU in achieving fast, secure, and compliant access to the markets of the EU, Great Britain, and Switzerland. The company specializes in medical devices, in-vitro diagnostics, cosmetics, and personal protective equipment, offering comprehensive support for all regulatory requirements. With an experienced team of scientists and engineers, ISO 13485 certification, and tailored services, MedNet EC-REP is a reliable partner for the healthcare industry regarding compliance, market entry, and sustained presence in Europe.

As a Digital Marketing Manager, you will assume central responsibility for MedNet EC-REP's international marketing strategy – from website management and campaign execution to detailed performance analysis.

### Responsibilities
*   **Website & Project Management:** Independent planning, control, and optimization of the corporate website, including target definition, time and resource planning, stakeholder coordination, and success measurement.
*   **SEO, AIO, GEO & Content Marketing:** Development and implementation of holistic strategies for search engine and AI-supported optimization (SEO/AIO/GEO) and content marketing. This includes keyword research, on-page optimization, performance analysis, and continuous content optimization for the website, blog, and other channels.
*   **Performance Marketing:** Conception, implementation, and management of international campaigns via Google Ads, LinkedIn, and email marketing – targeted and data-driven.
*   **Content Creation:** Creation of compelling content such as blog articles, whitepapers, mailings, and campaign texts, tailored to B2B target groups.
*   **Systems & Tools:** Content maintenance using modern Headless CMS (e.g., Payload CMS) and creation of simple HTML adjustments. Utilization of email marketing tools (e.g., BREVO) and management of leads and campaign results in ZOHO CRM.
*   **Analytics & Optimization:** Analysis of campaign performance with tools like Google Analytics (GA4), Clarity, or Umami. Derivation of data-based optimization measures, execution of A/B tests, and conversion tracking.
*   **Cross-functional Collaboration:** Close coordination with specialist departments such as Sales and Regulatory Affairs, as well as regular market and competitor analyses within the international healthcare context.

### Qualifications
*   Completed degree in Marketing, Communications, or a comparable qualification.
*   Several years of professional experience in digital B2B marketing, ideally within a regulated environment (Healthcare, MedTech, or similar).
*   Profound knowledge in Performance Marketing, especially with Google Ads, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, and email marketing.
*   Very good SEO and AIO knowledge, and routine handling of tools like SEMrush, Sistrix, and Google Search Console.
*   Experience in working with web analytics tools (e.g., GA4, Umami) and in data-driven optimization of user journeys.
*   Proficient use of CMS systems (ideally Headless, e.g., Payload CMS), basic HTML knowledge is a plus.
*   Experience in independently creating target-group-specific content.
*   Proficient use of Canva for visual content creation and MS Office (Outlook, Excel, Word, PowerPoint).
*   Very good German and English language skills, both written and spoken.
*   High degree of initiative, structured way of working, and a hands-on mentality.
