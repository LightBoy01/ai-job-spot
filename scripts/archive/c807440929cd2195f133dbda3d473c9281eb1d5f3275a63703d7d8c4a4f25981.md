**Summary**
-----------

We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced Data Scientist to join our Data Engineering and Artificial Intelligence team which is at the forefront of innovation. The ideal candidate will possess a strong understanding of data science principles, advanced technical skills, and the ability to communicate effectively to all levels within the organization. As part of this team, you will have opportunities to use your data science and leadership skills to shape the next generation of AI/ML to truly transform how Bell operates.

Our goal is to leverage the power of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to provide the best service for our customers while accelerating our product roadmaps. The work we do is critical in ensuring that Bell remains the best telecommunications provider in Canada. 

**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------

*   Lead the development of machine learning products and models from inception to production
*   Build scalable, production-grade AI-driven applications leveraging generative AI models (e.g., image/text/video/code generation)
*   Identify areas for AI/ML opportunities and demonstrate to internal clients how AI/ML can improve their business
*   Collaborate with cross functional teams of data scientists, data engineers, and product managers in solving business problems
*   Maintain and expand your knowledge of ML/AI and current technology through training opportunities, conferences, etc.
*   Innovative and solution-focused: Proactively identifies opportunities for improvement and contributes creative solutions to enhance efficiency and effectiveness.
*   Analytical and detail-oriented: Meticulously gathers and analyzes information to effectively identify and resolve complex problems.

**Critical Qualifications**
---------------------------

*   Bachelors degree, or Masters in a discipline such as Mathematics, Statistics, Machine Learning, Data Science, Computer Science or related area 
*   3-6 years of relevant work experience in a hands-on data scientist or related role
*   Algorithms
    *   Advanced knowledge of machine learning techniques: classification, regression, clustering, deep learning, and others
    *   Hands-on experience and expertise with different ML frameworks such as Scikit-Learn, XGBoost, TensorFlow, BQML, SparkML
    *   Exposure to Gen AI tools and frameworks, such as various large language models (LLMs), retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), agentic workflows, and orchestration
*   Coding
    *   Advanced Python development skills
    *   Experience with Google Cloud tools
    *   Experience with web API development for AI integration and prototyping
    *   Experience with CI/CD pipelines
    *   Excellent code design (OOP, Algorithms, and Data Structures)
*   Data
    *   Advanced ability to query, analyze, and visualize data
    *   Proficiency with BigQuery, Teradata, Python libraries, MicroStrategy
    *   Experience in preprocessing of structured and unstructured data
    *   Understanding RDBMS, Distributed, and NoSQL databases

Adequate knowledge of French is required for positions in Quebec.
