### Description\nThis is a hands-on, impactful role within a dynamic tech startup, where you'll collaborate closely with the founders to build and shape the company's financial future from the ground up. Our client is looking for a strategically and operationally strong Finance Manager who is eager to contribute to construction rather than just take over existing structures. This position offers a unique opportunity to evolve from an operational all-rounder into a future leader within the finance department. You will join a modern, growth-oriented company with a true startup character, offering direct influence over processes, short decision-making paths, and significant long-term development opportunities, including progression towards Senior Finance Manager and Head of Finance. The role comes with an attractive compensation package up to €68,000 p.a. and flexible mobile/hybrid work options.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Take full responsibility for accounting, cash flow and liquidity planning, budgeting, and forecasting.\n- Prepare monthly reports, dashboards, and financial analyses for both internal management and external stakeholders.\n- Actively participate in the operational implementation of new finance processes and tools, moving beyond just control to hands-on execution.\n- Optimize and automate finance processes, specifically tailored for a SaaS and startup environment.\n- Work in close collaboration with the founders and external partners (e.g., tax advisors, banks, auditors), directly impacting the company's growth and structural development.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A completed degree in Business Administration, Finance, Controlling, or an equivalent qualification.\n- A minimum of 3 years of professional experience, ideally gained within a SaaS or startup environment rather than a traditional corporate setting.\n- A pronounced hands-on mentality, a proactive approach, and a passion for working autonomously and driving initiatives forward.\n- Proficiency with essential tools such as Stripe and Google Sheets, alongside experience with accounting/controlling software like DATEV Unternehmen Online.\n- Demonstrate an analytical and structured approach to work, maintaining high quality standards, combined with pragmatism and a 'can-do' attitude.\n- Excellent German language skills and good English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a driven Finance professional who thrives in a fast-paced, innovative environment. The role offers unparalleled direct influence on a growing tech startup's financial strategy and operations, working side-by-side with the founders. It's perfect for someone with a strong hands-on mentality eager to build and optimize processes from scratch, rather than just manage existing ones. With clear pathways for career progression to Senior Finance Manager and even Head of Finance, coupled with an attractive salary and flexible work options, this position is ideal for an ambitious individual looking to make a significant impact and grow into a leadership role.
