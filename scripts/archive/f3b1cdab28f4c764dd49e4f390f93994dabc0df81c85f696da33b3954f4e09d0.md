**Job Description:**

*   **AI & Agentic Systems Development**: Design, build, and optimize advanced AI/ML solutions, including Generative AI, LLM-based agents, multi-agent architectures, and autonomous workflows.
*   **Foundation Models & Domain Adaptation**: Fine-tune and adapt foundation models (text, vision, multimodal) for real-world applications across industries.
*   **Computer Vision & Time Series**: Develop and deploy models for image/video analysis, object detection, scene understanding, and predictive time-series forecasting.
*   **AI for Big Data**: Architect AI systems capable of processing massive datasets with distributed computing, streaming pipelines, and large-scale analytics platforms.
*   **Data Engineering for AI**: Build and maintain robust data pipelines for ingestion, transformation, feature engineering, and scalable model training.
*   **AI Software Engineering**: Integrate AI models and pipelines into production-grade software systems using best practices in modular architecture, APIs, microservices, testing, security, and maintainability.
*   **MLOps & AI Engineering**: Lead deployment, monitoring, optimization, and CI/CD automation for AI workloads with strong observability and reliability standards.
*   **Collaboration & Leadership**: Partner with cross-functional teams to deliver impactful AI solutions; mentor and guide junior engineers, fostering a culture of excellence

**Qualifications:**

*   Bachelor’s/Master’s/PhD in Computer Science (+5 years of experience), AI/ML, Data Science, or related field.
*   Strong expertise in AI, ML, deep learning, Generative AI, and LLM fine-tuning.
*   Proficiency in Python and AI/ML frameworks (e.g., PyTorch, TensorFlow, LangChain, LlamaIndex).
*   Experience with **agentic AI** and orchestration frameworks for autonomous systems.
*   Solid knowledge of **computer vision**, **time series analysis**, **AI for big data**, and **AI software engineering integration**.
*   Hands-on experience in **data engineering for AI**, distributed processing (Spark, Databricks), and orchestration tools (Airflow, Prefect).
*   Strong understanding of MLOps principles, scalable deployment, and AI observability.
*   Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and communication skills.
*   Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure), Docker, Kubernetes, and Git workflows.
*   Main responsibility to provide AI solutions and support to develop AI-power tools for MA-AS in Diagnostic area to bring excellent operational efficiency in our GB partner’s daily work and customer experience.
