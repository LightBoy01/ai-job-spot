We currently have a vacancy for a **Data Governance Specialist** fluent in English, to offer his/her services as an expert who will be based in Belgium. The work will be carried out either in the company’s premises or on-site at customer premises. In the context of the first assignment, the successful candidate will be integrated with the Development team of the company that will closely cooperate with a major client’s IT team on site.  

**Your tasks**

*   Design of Data Quality and Health Monitoring processes;
*   Assistance with deployment and configuration of the system;
*   Elaborate on Data governance topics;
*   Implement processes to ensure data accuracy, consistency and reliability across all data sources;
*   Set up a data lineage system to track the data flow from source to target;
*   Implement tools and techniques to monitor data pipelines in real time;
*   Establish and manage a comprehensive data catalogue to provide a centralised repository of metadata;
*   Ensure compliance with GDPR and other data protection regulations;
*   Design and implement data access control mechanisms;
*   Establish data logging standards and practices to ensure comprehensive logging of data usage and changes;
*   Develop and disseminate best practices for data management and governance within the organisation;
*   Develop frameworks and guidelines to ensure our AI applications are ethical and fair.

**Requirements**

*   University degree in IT combined with relevant IT professional experience of 15 years;
*   Excellent experience in data governance, data management or related;
*   Proficiency in data governance tools, data quality tools, data lineage tools and metadata management solutions;
*   Experience with data security standards and technologies;
*   Experience of GDPR, data protection laws, and AI regulations such as the AI Act;
*   Extensive knowledge of data warehouse design & architecture;
*   In depth knowledge of SQL, PostgreSQL and NoSQL;
*   Very good knowledge of ETL tools;
*   Good knowledge of modelling tools;
*   Experience of online analytical data processing (OLAP) and data mining tools;
*   Experience in data quality, data control and monitoring techniques
*   Excellent command of the English language.
