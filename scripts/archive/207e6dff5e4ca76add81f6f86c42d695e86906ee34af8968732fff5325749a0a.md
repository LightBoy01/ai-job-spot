**Role Summary** 

Join our EMEA Digital and Technology Transformation team for an exciting kick-start to your career! You'll get hands-on experience working within a large-scale technology transformation project, develop core project management skills, help keep things running smoothly, and work alongside experienced professionals who want you to grow. From organizing events and creating presentations, to streamlining team processes leveraging automation and AI, this role gives you the chance to learn a lot while supporting Marriott International's tech transformation. If you're ready to dive in and make a difference with us, we can't wait to meet you!
