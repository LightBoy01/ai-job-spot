### Summary
ElevenLabs' founder, Mati Staniszewski, has highlighted that, in the immediate future, AI audio models stand out as the most significant advantage and transformative development currently available within the technological landscape.

### Why It Matters
This statement, though concise, carries substantial weight coming from the founder of a leading AI audio company like ElevenLabs. For AI professionals, it signals that while broader discussions around AI often focus on large language models (LLMs) or general AI advancements, specialized generative AI modalities, specifically high-fidelity audio, are currently at an inflection point. Staniszewski's emphasis on AI audio models as the "biggest advantage and the biggest step change you can have today" underscores their immediate and tangible impact on various industries, from content creation and entertainment to accessibility and customer service.

This insight is critical for AI strategists and product managers. It suggests that integrating advanced AI audio capabilities offers a powerful competitive differentiator *right now*, enabling companies to create novel experiences and efficiencies that are not yet widely commoditized. It encourages a proactive approach to adopting and developing with AI audio, rather than waiting for it to become a standard feature. The underlying implication is that companies that leverage this current "step change" can capture significant market share and innovation leadership before the technology potentially matures and becomes more ubiquitous, as often happens with groundbreaking advancements. It's a call to action for immediate exploration and investment in AI audio solutions as a pathway to significant strategic advantage.
