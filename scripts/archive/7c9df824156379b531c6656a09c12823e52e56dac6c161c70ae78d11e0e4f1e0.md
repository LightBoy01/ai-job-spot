**About the role**

OpusClip Canada is seeking a **Growth Engineer** who combines strong product sense with scrappy 0→1 engineering skills to drive product-led growth. This role is equal parts growth strategy and hands-on building: you’ll deeply understand our users, identify growth opportunities, and rapidly prototype and ship solutions across multiple tech stacks. Expect to work on mini tools, integration apps, SEO/GEO experiments, and mobile features (mobile engineering experience is a strong plus). We’re looking for someone self-driven, resourceful, and impact-obsessed, who thrives in a fast-paced, experimental environment. This is a high-ownership role with a timeline to hire by early November through end of year, with emphasis on finding the right fit over urgency.

**What you'll own**

*   Work with growth marketers and product managers on a daily basis
    
*   Analyze growth opportunities, define and own metrics, drive initiatives and land measurable impact
    

**We're looking for**

*   4–8 years of experience as a Growth Engineer
    
*   Build and ship scrappy 0->1 solutions quickly across multiple tech stacks
    
*   Strong growth mindset & product sense.
    
*   Must perform as a generalist
    

**Nice to have**

*   Strong engineering skills across web and/or mobile (React, Node.js, Swift/Kotlin a plus).
    
*   A good sense of marketing skills.
