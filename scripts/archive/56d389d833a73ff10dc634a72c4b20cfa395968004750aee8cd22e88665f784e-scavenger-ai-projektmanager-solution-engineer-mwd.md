### Description
Scavenger AI is one of Europe's fastest-growing AI startups, VC-backed, recently having closed its seed round, and awarded as the best AI Startup in Germany. We are building a product that redefines Business Intelligence by transforming complex corporate data into understandable, precise, and trustworthy analyses, enabling every employee to make decisions without being a data expert.

As a Project Manager / Solution Engineer, you will be pivotal in bringing our product to life for our customers. You will serve as the central interface between customers, business, and tech, understanding user requirements, translating them into implementation plans, and coordinating both internal and external stakeholders. This is a unique opportunity to take on a key role in a rapidly expanding AI company and help shape the future of Business Intelligence in Europe.

### Responsibilities
*   Take end-to-end ownership for customer projects, from kickoff to successful Go-Live.
*   Define project goals, workstreams, responsibilities, scope, and timelines.
*   Lead structured workshops, onboardings, and regular status updates.
*   Act as the central point of contact ("one face to the customer") ensuring clear communication.
*   Collaborate closely with our Product & Engineering Team, prioritize requirements, and ensure customer feedback seamlessly flows back into the product.
*   Be prepared to intervene when critical situations arise, whether it's related to data logic, analytical questions, or small technical adjustments (e.g., SQL, Python, API checks).

### Qualifications
*   You don't need to be a Data Scientist, but you should feel comfortable in a data and software context.
*   Experience in Project Management, Consulting, Solution Engineering, Business Analytics, or Customer Success (B2B SaaS environment is a plus).
*   Excellent communication and moderation skills (customer-facing & internal).
*   Ability to quickly understand technical issues and explain them in a structured manner.
*   Basic understanding of databases / SQL and initial experience in Python, or a strong willingness to learn technically.
*   Entrepreneurial, solution-oriented mindset.
*   Enjoy designing processes, structuring things, and bringing order to chaos.
*   Very good written and spoken German language skills are required.
