**Your Key Responsibilities**

*   Understand the requirements both functional and non-functional by going through the specifications and with inputs from business analysts and participate in creating high level estimate as well as translating the same to data requirements in order to create a data requirements document and participate effectively in the design, development and testing phases of the project
*   Utilize analytical, statistical, and programming skills to collect, analyze and interpret data sets
*   Create hypothesis scenarios based on the business requirements
*   Create model design artifacts to provide deliverables in line with requirements
*   Develop models and algorithms
*   Review all artifacts (Design, Code, Documentation, Unit test scripts)
*   Develop A/B testing and test model quality
*   Define process and tools to monitor and analyze model performance

Required Qualifications  
Bachelor’s degree or foreign equivalent required from an accredited institution. Will also consider three years of progressive experience in the specialty in lieu of every year of education.  
At least 8 of experience in Information Technology.

At least 4 years of experience in Data Science/AI/ML projects  
Preferred Qualifications  
At least 4 years of experience in AI/ML/DL algorithms, Python, R, Data pipeline,  Data cleansing, Visualization  
Excellent verbal and written communication skills  
Experience in software development life cycle  
Ability to work in team in diverse/ multiple stakeholder environment  
Experience and desire to work in a Global delivery environment.

**主な責務**

*   仕様を精査して機能要件と非機能要件の両方を理解し、ビジネス解析からの情報を得て上位レベルの見積を作成。データ要件文書を作成するためにデータ要件に落とし込み、プロジェクトの設計、開発およびテストの各段階に効果的に参画。
*   解析、統計、プログラミングのスキルを活かしてデータセットを収集、解析、解釈する
*   ビジネス要件に従って仮説シナリオを作成
*   要件に沿った成果物を提供するようモデル設計アーチファクトを作成
*   モデルとアルゴリズムを開発
*   すべてのアーチファクト（設計、コード、文書化、単体試験のスクリプト）をレビュー
*   A/B試験を開発しテストモデル品質を確保

モデルのパフォーマンスを監視解析するためのプロセスとツールを定義
