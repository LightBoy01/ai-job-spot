**About Us:**
-------------

Liberate Innovations Inc. is a Series-A funded AI company focused on revolutionizing the insurance industry through advanced technology solutions. 

We are seeking an AI Agent Engineer to bridge the gap between our cutting-edge products and our customers' technical needs, ensuring successful deployment and integration. In this role, you will work closely with customer success, sales, product, and engineering teams to deliver AI-driven solutions that drive measurable value for our customers.

_**\*Location: Boston or San Francisco (Berkeley) hybrid role (2 day/week in-office)**_ 

**Key Responsibilities:**
-------------------------

*   Collaborate with the sales team to understand customer requirements and design tailored AI-driven solutions that meet their specific business needs.
*   Serve as the technical expert during the post-sales process, providing product demonstrations, technical presentations, and answering detailed customer inquiries.
*   Work closely with customers to assess their current systems and workflows, identifying integration points for our AI solutions.
*   Develop and deliver customized product configurations and prototypes to demonstrate the value and functionality of our solutions.
*   Assist in the implementation process by providing technical guidance, troubleshooting issues, and ensuring seamless integration with customer systems.
*   Act as a liaison between customers and the product development team, communicating customer feedback and helping to shape future product features.
*   Provide post-sales support to ensure customer satisfaction and continued product success.
*   Create and maintain technical documentation, including integration guides, FAQs, and best practices.

**Qualifications:**
-------------------

**Must have**

*   5+ years of experience in a solutions engineering or technical consulting role, preferably within the AI or technology sectors.
*   Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
*   Extreme sense of ownership, urgency, and customer obsession to ensure that you will thrive in our early stage enterprise startup environment
*   Experience with programming languages such as Python, Java, or similar, and familiarity with APIs, data integration, and cloud platforms.
*   Strong technical background with experience in software development, system integration, and cloud-based solutions.
*   Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to clearly explain complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences.
*   Proven ability to manage multiple customer engagements simultaneously, delivering high-quality results under tight deadlines.
*   Strong problem-solving skills and a proactive approach to addressing customer challenges.

**Nice to have**

*   Familiarity with AI, machine learning, and data analytics technologies and how they can be applied in enterprise environments.
*   Experience in the insurance technology or financial services industries.
*   Previous experience working in a startup or high-growth environment.
*   Certifications in cloud platforms such as AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure.
