_The_ _Quality_ _Documentation_ _Specialist_ _is_ _responsible_ _for_ _managing_ the _site_ _documentation_ _management_ _systems_ _and_ _ensuring all_ _documentation_ _standards__,_ _records__,_ _SOPs__,_ _and_ _documented_ _processes_ _meet_ _company_ _and_ _regulatory_ _requirements__._ _This_ _position_ _will_ _be_ _responsible_ _for_ _writing__,_ _implementing_ _and_ _training_ _new_ _or_ _modified_ _quality_ _programs__/__processes_. _This_ _role_ will _support_ _documentation_ _and_ _communication_ _of_ _continuous_ _improvement_ _programs_ _such_ _as_ _management_ _of change__,_ _corrective_ _&_ _preventive_ _action_ _and_ _customer_ _complaint_ _reporting__._ 

_**Key responsibilities of the role include:**_

*   Managing site documentation management systems to ensure compliance with company and regulatory requirements.
*   Writing, implementing, and training new or modified quality programs/processes.
*   Supporting continuous improvement programs like management of change (MOC), corrective & preventive action (CAPA), and customer complaint reporting.
*   Conducting periodic reviews/audits of documentation systems.
*   Leading Good Documentation Practices (GDPs) and training staff.
*   Supporting the product release process by reviewing production documents for compliance.
*   Facilitating the sharing of ideas and best practices.

**Education and Background:**

*   A B.A./B.S. degree in Food Science, Chemistry, Engineering, Microbiology, Biochemistry, or a related field.
*   Italian speaking proficiency (English proficiency preferred).
*   At least 6 months  of experience in a food /pet food manufacturing environment.
*   Proficient knowledge of HACCP, cGMPs, and SPC.
*   Excellent project management, interpersonal, communication, multitasking, and critical thinking skills.
*   Proficiency in Google Suite, Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), and SAP.
