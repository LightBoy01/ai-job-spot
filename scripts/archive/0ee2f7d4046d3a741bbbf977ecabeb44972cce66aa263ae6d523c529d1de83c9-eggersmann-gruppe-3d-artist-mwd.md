### Description
The **Eggersmann Group** is a pioneering expert in **construction, recycling technology, and waste management**. The group is an internationally active association of companies with over 1,300 employees worldwide, characterized by comprehensive know-how, many years of experience, and the efficient use of synergies. As a family business with origins in East Westphalia-Lippe, we take responsibility for people and nature, acting economically, foreseeably, sustainably, and globally. We rely on innovative technologies and dedicated employees who master exciting challenges with enthusiasm.

For our location in **Halle (Westf.)**, we are looking for a **3D Artist (m/f/d)** to join us as soon as possible.

We offer a future-proof workplace with structured onboarding, exciting and varied tasks and projects within a family-like team with flat hierarchies. This permanent contract comes with attractive compensation, flexible working hours, 30 days of vacation, company and team events, free beverages, a fruit basket, subsidized lunch options, and the possibility to lease a JobRad. We also support occupational pension schemes and provide access to a Corporate Benefits Portal, among many other advantages.

### Responsibilities
- Preparing CAD data.
- Visualizing photorealistic images, including scene setups, modeling, texturing, lighting, and animation.
- Image processing, creating composings and illustrations.

### Qualifications
- Successfully completed training in the media sector and at least 2 years of professional experience.
- Sound knowledge of 3ds Max, V-Ray, and After Effects, as well as a good technical understanding.
- Proficient in MS-Office products, especially PowerPoint.
- Ideally, very good knowledge of working with Adobe Creative Suite, particularly InDesign and Photoshop.
- Enjoy working independently and autonomously.
- A team-oriented, reliable, and systematic approach to work.
