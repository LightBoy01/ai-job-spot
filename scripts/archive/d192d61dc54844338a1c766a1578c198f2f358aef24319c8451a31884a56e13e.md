Synopsis
--------

Support the Ground Product & Standards team through active coordination of audits, compliance monitoring, reporting, documentation, and vendor performance tracking to ensure global consistency of Etihad’s hub and ground service guest experience standards.

Accountabilities
----------------

*   Conduct scheduled audits and compliance checks for ground product standards across hub and outstation airports.
*   Prepare and maintain audit reports, track findings, and follow up on corrective actions with responsible departments.
*   Monitor guest feedback, service performance KPIs, and product delivery data to support reporting requirements for management.
*   Assist in the development and update of global service standards, SOPs, product documentation, and operational guidelines.
*   Support vendor performance tracking and data collection related to ground service suppliers and contractors.
*   Coordinate documentation, meeting notes, and process updates related to product development projects and audits.
*   Maintain audit schedules, compliance logs, and ensure timely reporting across the global network.
*   Liaise with Global Airports, Service Delivery, Procurement, and Vendor Partners to ensure compliance with established product standards.

Education & Experience
----------------------

*   Bachelor’s Degree, preferred in Hospitality, Business Administration, or related field.
*   4 years of experience in audit, compliance, ground services, or airport guest experience operations.
*   Experience with service standards documentation, data reporting, and audit preparation.
*   Proficiency in Excel, PowerPoint, and reporting tools; familiarity with data dashboards is an advantage.

**About Etihad Airways**

Etihad Airways, the national airline of the UAE, was formed in 2003 and quickly went on to become one of the world’s leading airlines. From its home in Abu Dhabi, Etihad flies to passenger and cargo destinations in the Middle East, Africa, Europe, Asia, Australia and North America. Together with Etihad’s codeshare partners, Etihad’s network offers access to hundreds of international destinations. In recent years, Etihad has received numerous awards for its superior service and products, cargo offering, loyalty programme and more. All this ties into Etihad’s ambitious Journey 2030 strategy. The airline plans to double its fleet size and triple the number of customers over the next six years as it sets out to be the airline everyone wants to fly!
