### Description

Working at the Theo Müller Group means being part of a regionally rooted family business and an internationally growing conglomerate in the food and logistics sector. With well-known brands in dairy, fish & delicatessen, sauces and dressings, its own packaging company, a transport and logistics company, and its own power plant, the Group stands for diversity, independence, and security.

The employees of the Group share a common vision: to bring taste into people's lives. Accompany us on this journey and secure a job with future prospects.

We are seeking a **Senior System Analyst SAP Authorization and GRC (m/w/d)** to join us in **Aretsried (near Augsburg), Freising (near Munich), or Leppersdorf (near Dresden)**.

### Responsibilities

*   As a Senior Analyst (m/w/d), you will develop and implement new authorization concepts within SAP ABAP, SAP Java, or SaaS-based SAP systems such as S/4, SuccessFactors, Ariba, or SAP BTP.
*   You will also regularly review and optimize existing authorization concepts to adapt them to new business requirements.
*   You will ensure compliance with governance and compliance regulations by reviewing critical authorizations, conducting re-certifications, and supporting internal and external audits.
*   You will conduct license measurements and optimize the distribution of existing SAP licenses to increase cost efficiency.
*   For complex authorization issues, you will provide 3rd level support and act as an interface to internal service units.

### Qualifications

*   You possess a completed relevant degree and at least five years of professional experience in the SAP authorization environment.
*   In recent years, you have gained profound knowledge in SAP ABAP, SAP Java, or SaaS-based SAP systems such as S/4, SuccessFactors, Ariba, or SAP BTP.
*   Personally, you impress with a strong sense of responsibility, excellent communication skills, and pronounced problem-solving competence.
*   Very good German and English language skills, as well as willingness for occasional business trips, complete your profile.
