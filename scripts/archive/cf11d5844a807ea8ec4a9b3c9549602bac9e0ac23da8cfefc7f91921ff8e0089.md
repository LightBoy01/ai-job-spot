This role is a unique opportunity to lead a high-impact business systems organization that supports critical People, Legal, and Finance functions across the company. You will work closely with senior stakeholders to design, implement, and optimize complex business processes and automation initiatives, while fostering a high-performing, inclusive team culture. This is ideal for a visionary leader who thrives in a fast-paced, innovation-driven environment.

**Accountabilities**

*   Lead the Company Pillar Systems organization, scaling platforms, processes, and AI-powered tools to support People, Legal, and Finance functions.
*   Drive the adoption of generative AI capabilities and champion experimentation across business systems.
*   Oversee and optimize core platforms, including ServiceNow, Workday, Workato, and company-wide AI/ML tools.

*   Provide thought leadership in automation, AI integration, and process engineering to enhance efficiency, scalability, and user experience.

*   Extensive technical experience, ideally with a Bachelor’s degree or higher in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field.
*   Proven leadership of multi-disciplinary business systems teams through scaling, transformation, and automation initiatives.
*   Hands-on expertise with core platforms such as ServiceNow, Workday, and Workato; experience running Centers of Excellence is a plus.
*   Strong understanding of People, Legal, and Finance business processes, with experience partnering with senior leadership in these domains.
*   Demonstrated ability to operationalize strategy, establish governance frameworks, and deliver complex, high-volume systems reliably.
*   Passion for AI and generative technologies, with experience experimenting with AI/ML tools and articulating business impact.
*   Excellent coaching, mentoring, and team development skills, with experience fostering inclusive, high-performing teams.
*   Strong communication skills, comfortable building trusted relationships with C-level and VP-level stakeholders.
