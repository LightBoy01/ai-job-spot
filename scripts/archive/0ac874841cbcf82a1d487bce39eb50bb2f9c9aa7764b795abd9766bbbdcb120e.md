### Description

Billigence is a boutique data consultancy with a global reach, dedicated to transforming the way organizations work with data. We design, tailor, and deliver cutting-edge data solutions across AI, data science, cloud platforms, and governance. Our mission is to help clients unlock the full value of their data and embed a scalable, data-driven culture.

We are seeking a highly skilled Data Science Consultant with deep expertise in Microsoft Azure AI services and OpenAI. This role involves designing and delivering advanced AI and machine learning solutions on modern cloud platforms, working closely with client stakeholders to build scalable, intelligent applications. This is an exciting opportunity to apply your technical expertise across ML, Python, Databricks, and CI/CD pipelines, while also leveraging advanced Azure services for search, data storage, and analytics.

### Responsibilities

*   Design, develop, and implement machine learning models and AI-driven solutions.
*   Leverage Azure AI services and OpenAI APIs to build scalable, production-ready applications.
*   Work with Databricks to process, analyze, and transform large datasets.
*   Implement and optimize Azure Search for intelligent information retrieval.
*   Design and maintain Cosmos DB solutions for distributed data storage.
*   Support deployment workflows with robust CI/CD pipelines.
*   Collaborate with engineering teams to integrate AI solutions into front-end applications using JavaScript and React.
*   Provide best practices and thought leadership in AI and data science to clients and internal teams.

### Qualifications

*   Proven experience in Data Science roles with expertise in Azure AI Services and OpenAI.
*   Strong programming skills in Python and applied machine learning.
*   Hands-on experience with Databricks for data engineering and ML workflows.
*   Knowledge of Azure Search and Cosmos DB.
*   Experience building and managing CI/CD pipelines.
*   Front-end development exposure with JavaScript (React preferred).
*   Strong communication skills and ability to work with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
*   Problem-solving mindset and ability to deliver results in fast-paced environments.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "Beyond just delivering a functional model, what does 'success' look like for a consultant in this role at Billigence?"**

That’s a great question because it gets to the heart of what we do. Success for us isn't just a 'project complete' email. It's about six months after we've rolled off an engagement, and you hear from the client that their team is not only using the AI solution we built but are now building their own new features on top of it. It’s seeing the 'aha!' moment when a business leader realizes they can now answer questions they didn't even know they could ask before. We're here to be catalysts. True success is when we've not only solved a technical challenge but have fundamentally empowered the client to become more data-driven on their own. We leave behind a capability, not just a codebase.

**A candidate might ask: "Given the rapid evolution of Azure AI and OpenAI, how does Billigence ensure its consultants stay at the cutting edge and avoid burnout?"**

That's something we're very intentional about. As a boutique firm, we live and breathe this specific tech stack, so our collective knowledge is our biggest asset. We don't just encourage learning; we structure it. We have dedicated internal channels where we share new findings, weekly 'deep dive' sessions on emerging services, and a budget for certifications and conferences. More importantly, our project assignments are strategic. You won't be siloed on a legacy project for a year. We rotate our experts to ensure everyone gets hands-on experience with the latest tools. It's a culture of shared curiosity. We believe the best way to stay ahead is to learn together, and the best way to avoid burnout is to be genuinely excited and challenged by your work.
