### Description\nTechnology excites you? Us too! K-tronik is looking for a Development Engineer for Line Routing (m/f/x) to strengthen our expert team for a client in the automotive & commercial vehicle industry. We look forward to getting to know you personally – and working together with passion and team spirit on exciting projects and future-oriented technologies. This role involves comprehensive development of line systems, focusing on concept creation, integration, and detailed design for heavy truck chassis.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Conduct space analyses within the heavy truck chassis.\n- Develop complete concepts for integrating components and their supply lines, coordinating with all stakeholders (involved development departments, architecture, production, suppliers, quality, sales, service).\n- Document these concepts in Catia V5.\n- Lead the complete development of line systems made from steel, aluminum, plastic, and PA, including their brackets, considering the system environment and requirements.\n- Create installation, assembly, and individual part drawings.\n- Participate in international development project teams, initially as a designer, with potential for a team lead role or similar in Scrum (English only for this team).\n- Work as a designer in the SET team (German only for this team).\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in Mechanical Engineering, Automotive Engineering, Mechatronics, or a comparable technical discipline, or equivalent vocational training.\n- Experience in the design/construction of components and line systems (steel, aluminum, plastic, PA).\n- Knowledge of space analyses in a vehicle environment (especially truck chassis).\n- Ability to develop integration concepts for components and supply lines.\n- Proficiency in Catia V5 for 3D and 2D (concept documentation, installation/assembly/individual part drawings).\n- Experience with PDM systems, bills of materials, and release processes.\n- Understanding of system environment, system requirements, and cross-functional integration.\n- Experience with project work following Scrum methodologies.\n- Fluent in German and English.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a detail-oriented Development Engineer passionate about automotive technology, specifically heavy vehicles. K-tronik offers a strong, supportive culture with flexible working conditions, comprehensive benefits (including 30 vacation days, team events, and corporate benefits), and a clear commitment to individual growth and career planning. The role offers the chance to work on critical integration challenges using Catia V5 and Scrum, with potential for leadership development in an international context. The recognition as a \"Kununu TOP Company\" further underscores a positive employee experience.
