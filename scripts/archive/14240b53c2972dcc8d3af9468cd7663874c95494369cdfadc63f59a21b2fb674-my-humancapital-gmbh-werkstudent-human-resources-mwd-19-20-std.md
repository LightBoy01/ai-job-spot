### Description\nOur partner company in Munich is seeking a **Working Student Human Resources (m/f/d)** for 19-20 hours per week, starting as soon as possible. In this role, you will provide essential administrative support across various HR functions, with a particular focus on employer branding, onboarding, and HR systems. This is an excellent opportunity to gain practical experience in a dynamic HR environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Manage central functional mailboxes.\n- Provide administrative support for the planning, preparation, and execution of onboarding events.\n- Actively participate in the creation of new website, intranet, and presentation content, with a focus on employer branding.\n- Support the testing of the applicant database and undertake minor adjustments in the applicant and program management system.\n- Participate in the evaluation of surveys and the analysis of statistics.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Enrolled student (m/f/d) with an affinity for topics such as communication, content management, HR development, or customer service.\n- Very good knowledge of MS Office; experience with CMS and MS SharePoint is an advantage.\n- Reliable, structured, and team-oriented work style.\n- Very good German and English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic entry-level opportunity for a student looking to kickstart their career in Human Resources. The role offers a diverse range of tasks, providing valuable insights into various HR domains, from talent attraction through employer branding to efficient HR administration and system management. The company emphasizes a flexible, appreciative, and collaborative work culture, backed by professional onboarding, ensuring you'll be well-supported as you learn and grow. Its central Munich location is also a great plus.
