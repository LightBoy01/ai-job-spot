### Description
This part-time Human Resources Student position (19-20 hours per week) in Munich offers a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience within a dynamic HR department. You will play a crucial role in supporting various HR initiatives, focusing on administrative tasks, employer branding, and enhancing both the candidate and employee experience. The role promises a structured onboarding process and the chance to work within a collaborative and appreciative team environment, providing valuable insights into diverse HR functions and enabling you to contribute to real-world projects.

### Responsibilities
*   Manage central functional mailboxes, ensuring professional and timely communication.
*   Provide administrative support for the planning, preparation, and execution of onboarding events, contributing to a positive new hire experience.
*   Actively participate in the creation of new website, intranet, and presentation content, with a strong emphasis on employer branding initiatives.
*   Assist with the testing of the applicant database and perform minor adjustments within the applicant and program management system.
*   Contribute to the evaluation of surveys and the analysis of HR-related statistics, providing data-driven insights.

### Qualifications
*   Currently enrolled as a student with an affinity for topics such as communication, content management, HR development, or customer care.
*   Proficiency in MS Office is required; experience with CMS and MS SharePoint is a plus.
*   Demonstrate reliability, a structured approach to work, and strong team orientation.
*   Possess excellent German and English language skills.
