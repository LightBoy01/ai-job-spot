### Description\nCecon Computer Systems GmbH, founded in 1990, has continuously evolved into a full-service provider with high consulting and solution expertise for clients in Berlin and its surroundings. Through partnerships with leading manufacturers in the system business and specialization in ConnectSmart Kitchen and Digital Pakt Schule products, Cecon is a driver in digitalization. This role offers the opportunity to join a team of continuously developing experts who engage in diverse projects from kick-off to go-live, specializing in areas like Cloud, Storage & Backup, Client, and Network environments. You will manage a fixed client base, providing comprehensive IT support and benefiting from an attractive compensation package, flexible remote work options, and a supportive team environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Act as an expert (technician or engineer) in various projects, from kick-off to go-live.\n*   Specialize in technology areas such as Cloud, Storage & Backup, and Client & Network environments based on your interests.\n*   Provide comprehensive IT support and care for a dedicated client base.\n*   Apply ITIL standards in daily work (certification is not required, but knowledge is beneficial).\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed vocational training or university degree in IT (e.g., IT Specialist for System Integration, Business Informatics) or equivalent professional experience in the field.\n*   Good knowledge of network and server environments, as well as virtual desktop infrastructure.\n*   Strong service-oriented mindset and genuine interest in the role.\n*   Independent working style and open communication.\n*   Class B driver's license.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced IT professional looking to deepen their expertise and take ownership of client relationships within a stable, growing company.
