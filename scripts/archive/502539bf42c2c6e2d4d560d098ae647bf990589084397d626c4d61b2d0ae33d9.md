**Job Description:**

Parsons is looking for an amazingly talented **Manager / Subject Matter Expert (Technology & Systems Support Lead)** to join our team! The Technology & Systems Support Lead will play a critical role in overseeing and managing technology systems, ensuring seamless integration, and providing expert-level support for the Jeddah Central Development Project. This position requires a combination of technical expertise, leadership skills, and a deep understanding of project-specific technology needs. The ideal candidate will act as a Subject Matter Expert (SME) in technology and systems, driving innovation and ensuring operational excellence.

  
**What You’ll Be Doing:**

*   Oversee the implementation, maintenance, and optimization of technology systems and infrastructure for the project.
*   Evaluate and recommend technology solutions to meet project requirements and enhance operational efficiency.
*   Ensure the integration of cutting-edge technologies, including IoT, AI, and smart city solutions, into the project framework.
*   Collaborate with vendors and contractors to ensure timely delivery and installation of technology systems.
*   Provide expert-level support for all technology systems, ensuring minimal downtime and maximum reliability.
*   Develop and implement system monitoring tools and processes to proactively identify and resolve issues.
*   Lead the troubleshooting and resolution of complex technical problems, coordinating with internal teams and external partners as needed.
*   Act as the primary point of contact for technology-related matters, liaising with project stakeholders, contractors, and government entities.
*   Lead a team of IT professionals, providing guidance, mentorship, and performance management.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to align technology strategies with project goals and timelines.
*   Develop and execute a comprehensive technology roadmap for the project, ensuring alignment with the overall vision of the Jeddah Central Development Project.
*   Stay updated on emerging technologies and industry trends, incorporating innovative solutions into the project as appropriate.
*   Manage the technology budget, ensuring cost-effective solutions without compromising quality.
*   Ensure all technology systems comply with local regulations, international standards, and project-specific requirements.
*   Implement robust cybersecurity measures to protect sensitive data and systems from potential threats.
*   Conduct regular audits and assessments to ensure the integrity and security of all technology systems.

**What Required Skills You’ll Bring:**

*   Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field (Master’s degree preferred).

*   Minimum of 15 years of experience in technology management, systems support, or a related role.
*   Proven track record of managing large-scale technology implementations, preferably in urban development or infrastructure projects.
*   Experience in smart city technologies, IoT, and AI integration is highly desirable.

*   Strong technical expertise in IT systems, infrastructure, and emerging technologies.
*   Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
*   Leadership and team management capabilities.
*   Effective communication and stakeholder management skills.
*   Proficiency in project management tools and methodologies.

**Certifications (Preferred):**

*   ITIL (Information Technology Infrastructure Library) Certification.
*   PMP (Project Management Professional) Certification.
*   Relevant cybersecurity certifications (e.g., CISSP, CISM).
