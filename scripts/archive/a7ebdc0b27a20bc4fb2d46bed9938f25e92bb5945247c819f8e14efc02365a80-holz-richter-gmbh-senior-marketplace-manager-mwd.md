### Description
Are you ready to elevate your career? Holz-Richter is seeking a motivated (Senior) Marketplace Manager to strengthen our dynamic E-commerce team.

As one of Germany's largest and most renowned timber trading companies, we offer the opportunity to work in an innovative environment where tradition meets modernity. With over 400 dedicated employees and an impressive operational area exceeding 100,000 square meters, we are consistently on a growth trajectory.

You will become a key part of our E-commerce team, which is already setting new standards with our successful online shop, casando. This role offers exciting projects and the chance to contribute your own ideas within a familial work environment characterized by flat hierarchies and a dynamic team of motivated colleagues. We foster continuous learning with various development opportunities in e-commerce and marketplace management, alongside benefits like flexible working hours, mobile work options, and comprehensive onboarding. If you are eager to drive both online and offline success with fresh ideas, we look forward to getting to know you.

### Responsibilities
*   Manage and optimize our product listings on major marketplaces like Amazon, eBay, and Otto.
*   Maintain product information, pricing, and inventory levels, ensuring accuracy and oversight at all times.
*   Plan and analyze marketing campaigns specifically for our marketplace channels.
*   Evaluate key performance indicators (KPIs), generate reports, and derive actionable optimizations.
*   Monitor market trends and conduct competitive analyses.
*   Handle complaints and customer inquiries within the marketplace environment.
*   Collaborate closely with Product Management, Marketing, and Customer Service to continuously improve processes and listings.

### Qualifications
*   Proven experience in marketplace management, specifically with Amazon and eBay; experience with Otto is a plus.
*   Familiarity with Plentymarkets or a strong willingness to learn and master it.
*   Digitally savvy with a passion for tracking current e-commerce and online trends.
*   Adept at data analysis, structured thinking, and a solution-oriented approach to work.
*   Proactive in contributing ideas, a team player, and an open communicator.
*   Excellent spoken and written German language skills.
