**About the Team**

The Platform Team at OpenAI owns the foundational data and processing stack powering the OpenAI API and products built on top of it. We innovate on the infrastructure needed to serve the latest models and agentic workflows at an unprecedented pace of growth and scale with high performance and reliability. Join us to build and operate the core platform that underpins OpenAI’s products and allows our customers to deploy AI themselves.

**About the Role**

This role is centered on designing and deploying services and infrastructure that empower our models and agents to generate insights and execute tasks, all grounded in authorized data sources and internal systems. You’ll help define the most effective data access patterns to unlock higher model accuracy and task performance. You will own the full lifecycle of your work—from architecture and implementation to production operations and on-call responsibilities.

Success in this role requires a solid understanding of enterprise data management fundamentals, including security, identity and access, performance, and reliability.

**In this role, you will:**

*   Design, build, and maintain data infrastructure and networking systems that connect structured and unstructured data to LLMs in a performant and reliable manner
    
*   Design, build, and maintain secure and compliant systems to process sensitive and valuable data
    
*   Ensure our data platform can scale by orders of magnitude while remaining reliable and efficient
    
*   Integrate data access and authentication with our production model and agent systems
    
*   Collaborate with product, research and forward-deployed engineering teams to fill in the full picture on how to deploy production systems and achieve world-class results on overall task capabilities
    
*   Own the reliability of the systems you build, including participation in an on-call rotation for critical incidents
    

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

*   Have expertise in deploying, configuring, and operating software systems within customer-managed infrastructure and networks
    
*   Are excited to bring the power of AGI to the infrastructure the world runs on today
    
*   Are comfortable with ambiguity and rapid change
    
*   Have an intrinsic desire to learn and fill in missing skills, and an equally strong talent for sharing learnings clearly and concisely with others
