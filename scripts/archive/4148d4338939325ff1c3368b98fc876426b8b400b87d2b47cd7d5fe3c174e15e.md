**POSITION PURPOSE**

The Finance Specialist will oversee and manage the operations and financial activities of an organization within the respective country to ensure all its processes are compliant as per regulations.

**JOB ACCOUNTABILITY**

*   Prepare and present accurate financial reports, including profit and loss statements, balance sheets, and cash flows, to senior management, stakeholders, and regulatory agencies.
*   Develop, implement, and monitor budgets and financial forecasts. It involves analyzing financial data to predict future financial trends and performance.
*   Implement and monitor cost control measures to effectively manage expenses. Identify areas where the company can reduce costs and increase financial efficiency.
*   Establish and maintain an internal control system to protect company assets, ensure accuracy in financial reporting, and prevent fraud.
*   Ensure the company complies with financial regulations and standards. Coordinate with external auditors for annual audits and follow up on their findings or recommendations.
*   Assist in the development of financial strategies and long-term plans that are aligned with company goals. Provide insights and recommendations for financial decision making.
*   Lead and manage finance and accounting teams, providing guidance, training and support to ensure high performance and professional development.
*   Act as a liaison between the finance department and other departments and external stakeholders such as banks, investors and regulatory bodies.
*   Identify and reduce financial risks to the organization by analyzing market trends, economic conditions, and company financial performance.
