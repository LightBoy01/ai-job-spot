### Description

Fetch is America's leading rewards app, empowering millions of users to earn rewards on their everyday purchases and interactions. Backed by major investors and partnered with Fortune 500 companies, we are reshaping the connection between brands and consumers. At Fetch, we champion a people-first culture built on curiosity, adaptability, and a willingness to explore new tools—especially AI—to work smarter and faster.

As the Sales Tools & Systems (Salesforce) Manager, you will be a key player on our Strategy and Operations team. Reporting to the VP of Sales Strategy and Operations, you will own our Salesforce platform and its integrated tech stack. This role is perfect for a strategic problem-solver who excels at managing complex systems, understanding business processes, and fostering cross-functional relationships in a dynamic, fast-paced environment.

### Responsibilities

*   Serve as the product owner for Fetch’s Salesforce instance, its data, and all integrated tools and systems.
*   Lead a small Salesforce team and manage relationships with external consultants.
*   Act as the go-to expert for all things Salesforce, providing guidance on both basic and advanced functionalities.
*   Perform daily administration and customization of our Salesforce org, ensuring seamless integration with other business systems.
*   Design and implement custom objects, fields, reports, dashboards, and automation using flows.
*   Collaborate with various teams to understand their needs, demonstrate new features, and answer system-related questions.
*   Identify and execute opportunities to enhance system efficiency and automate manual processes.
*   Partner with leadership to align Salesforce with business goals and reporting requirements.
*   Translate team requirements into actionable Salesforce development and change management initiatives.
*   Manage and ensure the smooth integration of third-party revenue operations tools (e.g., Gong, ZoomInfo, Outreach, Snowflake).
*   Evaluate, adopt, and champion AI-powered solutions to drive automation, data quality, and system efficiency.
*   Lead large-scale, cross-functional projects from inception to completion.

### Qualifications

**Minimum Requirements:**

*   5+ years of experience as a Salesforce Administrator or Business Analyst.
*   2+ years of experience in team and external relationship management.
*   Proven experience managing complex projects from requirements gathering through User Acceptance Testing (UAT).
*   Expertise in Salesforce's standard and customizable features, including deep knowledge of automation (must be a flow expert) and integration capabilities.
*   Proficiency in developing custom Salesforce elements like objects, fields, reports, and dashboards.
*   Salesforce.com Administrator certification is required.
*   Demonstrated success in gathering business requirements, managing change, and implementing strategic initiatives.
*   Excellent data management skills and a strong understanding of Salesforce data relationships.

**Preferred Requirements:**

*   Salesforce Advanced Administrator Certification.

**A candidate might ask: "The description mentions an 'AI-first environment.' Can you give a concrete example of how this philosophy impacts the Salesforce team's day-to-day work and strategic planning?"**

That's a great question because it gets to the heart of how we operate. For us, 'AI-first' isn't just a buzzword; it's a practical approach to problem-solving. In this role, you're not just maintaining Salesforce; you're actively looking for ways to make it smarter. For example, instead of just building a standard dashboard for our sales team, you might be tasked with evaluating and integrating an AI tool that provides predictive lead scoring or identifies partnership expansion opportunities we might otherwise miss. It means you have the autonomy to experiment with new technologies that can automate data enrichment, improve reporting accuracy, and ultimately give our teams an edge. It’s about being a strategic innovator, not just a system administrator.

**Another great question would be: "As the 'product owner' for Salesforce, how does this role influence broader business strategy beyond just maintaining the system?"**

This role is incredibly central to our revenue strategy. Think of our Salesforce instance as the nervous system for our entire go-to-market organization. You're not just reacting to tickets; you're proactively shaping how we operate. For instance, last quarter, our Salesforce team identified a bottleneck in our partner onboarding process by analyzing data flow and user behavior. Working with sales and partner success leadership, they re-architected the workflow, which directly led to a faster time-to-revenue for new partners. You'll have a seat at the table with leadership, translating their strategic goals into technical solutions and, just as importantly, bringing data-driven insights from the system back to them to influence their next move. Your work directly impacts the company's bottom line and the efficiency of hundreds of your colleagues.
