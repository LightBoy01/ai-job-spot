**Location:** Hybrid (Stoughton, MA)

**Position Overview:**

Shields Health Solutions is seeking a **strategic, results-oriented Project Manager** to join our fast-paced Emerging Technology team. This is a high-impact role for a proactive leader who thrives in dynamic environments and is passionate about driving innovation in healthcare technology.

As Project Manager, you will lead the execution of complex, cross-functional initiatives – _inclusive of the development of our AI Center of Excellence_ – to enhance operational efficiency, improve patient outcomes, and deliver measurable business value. You’ll bring an entrepreneurial mindset, exceptional organizational skills, and a commitment to delivering high-quality project outcomes in a rapidly evolving tech landscape.

**Key Responsibilities:**

*   Lead end-to-end project management for Emerging Technology initiatives – from initial concept through implementation and rollout.
*   Facilitate and document scoping sessions with business stakeholders to translate ideas into actionable project plans.
*   Coordinate seamless handoffs between product and development teams, ensuring alignment and maintaining delivery timelines.
*   Present innovative solutions to internal and external stakeholders, including senior and executive leadership.
*   Monitor project deliverables, timelines, and KPIs, including those related to AI performance metrics, to ensure consistent progress and stakeholder satisfaction.
*   Identify new project opportunities, including those involving AI and automation, lead go/no-go assessments, and develop compelling proposals and presentations.
*   Support the development and evolution of business plans and strategic road maps.
*   Apply creative problem-solving and sound judgment to anticipate challenges and proactively resolve issues.
*   Support the establishment and maintenance of project governance, policies, and control systems to ensure operational excellence and ethical AI practices.
*   Provide clear and concise regular updates on project status, risks, and key decisions to leadership and cross-functional teams.

**Qualifications & Experience:**

*   Minimum 3 years of experience in project management and/or business analysis – with exposure to AI technologies and data-driven projects a strong plus.
*   Proven ability to communicate complex technical concepts to non-technical and executive audiences.
*   Highly motivated, entrepreneurial, and results-driven professional with a collaborative spirit.
*   Strong analytical skills and attention to detail; adept at managing multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.

*   Passion for AI/ML, automation, and other emerging technologies with a proactive approach to learning and experimentation.

**Education & Certifications:**

*   Bachelor’s degree in STEM, Finance, Business Administration, Computer Science, or a related field.
*   Project Management Professional (PMP) or Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM) certification preferred.
*   Additional coursework or certifications in AI, machine learning, or data science is a strong plus.
