**Your future team**

Join Deloitte Luxembourg’s **Intelligent Automation & Agentic AI team**, where innovation meets strategy. Our team operates at the crossroads of business, technology, and user adoption by helping clients reimagine their processes and data flows. Together, we deliver cutting-edge solutions leveraging intelligent automation, AI agents, GenAI, document management, and low-code platforms.

**Your advantages of being one of us**

1.  **Career Growth, Your Way**: Access top-notch training, mentoring programs, and career development opportunities tailored to your ambitions.
2.  **Innovative Work That Matters**: Work on cutting-edge projects that utilize state-of-the-art technology to solve real-world challenges.
3.  **Inclusive, Flexible Culture**: Thrive in a flexible work environment where collaboration and work-life balance take priority.
4.  **Global Impact, Local Connections**: Collaborate with diverse teams worldwide while making an impact in the Luxembourg market.
5.  **Purpose-Driven Work**: Contribute to transformative projects that align with long-term sustainability and innovation goals.

**Your contribution to our success**

1.  **Business Analysis & Use Case Framing**: Identify and prioritize automation, AI, and document management use cases. Run design-thinking workshops to co-create future-proof solutions with measurable impact.
2.  **Process Modernization & Compliance**: Analyze and optimize workflows for HyperAutomation and GenAI integration, while ensuring compliance with GDPR and industry regulations.
3.  **Functional Leadership**: Bridge business requirements and technical solutions, lead User Acceptance Testing (UAT), and define functional governance frameworks.
4.  **Enablement & Engagement**: Lead automation enablement initiatives, translate complex technologies into compelling narratives, and design playbooks and templates for scaling adoption.
5.  **Practice Development**: Mentor junior consultants, foster collaboration among multidisciplinary teams, and support innovation within the Intelligent Automation team.

**Your skills**

**Must-have skills:**

*   *   Master’s degree in **Business, Information Management, Digital Transformation**, or related fields.
    *   4+ years in **consulting, business analysis, digital transformation, or document management roles**.
    *   Deep expertise in document management platforms like **Documentum, SharePoint, OpenText**, etc.

**Nice-to-have skills:**

*   *   Knowledge of **Intelligent Automation (RPA, workflow orchestration)**, AI/GenAI, and low-code platforms.
    *   Experience in **functional analysis, UAT coordination**, and delivering functional documentation.
    *   Multilingual proficiency (English required; French preferred).
