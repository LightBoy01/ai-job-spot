Imagine Your Impact.
The AI Workflow & Automation Architect (Multimedia) reports to the AI Workflow & Automation Manager and focuses on the discovery, evaluation, and integration of AI tools for multimedia content creation (audio, video, imagery). This role is both a supportive and execution-focused position that builds capacity in our creative and curriculum teams to work with external tools while also taking part in the creation of internal tools and workflows that lead to faster, reliable, and reusable internal processes. This role will scout emerging AI media solutions, assess fit and feasibility, build integrations and automations, and provide guidance and training so teams can confidently adopt these capabilities.

Envision Your Experience.
In this role you’ll have the opportunity to:
*   Research & Discovery – Track and assess emerging AI multimedia tools and models (audio, video, imagery) for internal use. Identify where AI can accelerate creative pipelines and reduce manual effort.
*   Evaluation & Integration – Pilot and evaluate tools/APIs for cost, quality, compliance, integration complexity, and reliability. Build automation and connective tissue using Python and workflow platforms (StackAI and AWS Flows) to link services and move data between systems. Create reusable workflow templates and utilities that other teams can adopt.
*   Enablement & Training – Serve as an AI liaison to creative/curriculum teams—share best practices, run trainings, and document usage patterns. Provide guidance on parameters, prompts, file formats, and output standards for consistent results.
*   Operationalization – Partner with the AI Workflow & Automation Manager to productionize successful pilots into supported internal solutions. Document tools, endpoints, configuration, and troubleshooting steps; contribute to a central catalog of approved tools, workflows, and “how-tos.”
*   Collaboration – Work closely with creative content experts and adjacent engineering/ops partners. Focus on enablement and automation opportunities, not direct content creation.
*   Other duties as required.
