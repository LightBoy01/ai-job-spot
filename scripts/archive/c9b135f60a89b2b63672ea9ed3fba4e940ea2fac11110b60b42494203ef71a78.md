**Job Description:**

**Principal Engineer – Gen AI Services**

The Experian Software Solutions (ESS) Analytics team is accelerating Experian's impact by integrating data, technology, and data science to build game-changing products and services for our customers. We are looking for a Principal Engineer to lead the development of our AI platform and products, delivering value to Experian's clients.

**You'll have the opportunity to:**

*   Be the technical authority and visionary for GenAI product architecture and strategy, including the adoption and integration of generative AI models and frameworks.
*   Lead multiple development teams, driving engineering best practices across projects.
*   Architect and oversee the design and implementation of full stack analytics platforms on AWS.
*   Collaborate with senior stakeholders and global teams to maximize the value and influence of Analytics offerings.
*   Guide programs to improve product cost, scalability, and stability, influencing organization-wide technology decisions.
*   Champion a culture of high-quality code, CI/CD automation, and robust enterprise software delivery.
*   Provide technical leadership in resolving complex production issues and guiding root cause analysis.
*   You will report to the Engineering Director.

**Qualifications:**

**Your background:**

*   15+ years of experience in software engineering, with demonstrated experience in object-oriented and asynchronous programming, and technical leadership.
*   Expertise in designing, building, and scaling enterprise-grade platforms.
*   Advanced expertise in full-stack application development, web development concepts, and cloud-native architectures.
*   Deep experience with production software development in React.js, NodeJS, and Python for scalable REST APIs.
*   Experience architecting and deploying solutions using Docker, Kubernetes, and Helm.
*   Experience with scrum, Agile processes, and leading code reviews and pull requests.
*   Hands-on experience with large language models (e.g., OpenAI GPT, Google Gemini, Meta Llama, Anthropic Claude).
*   Familiarity with prompt engineering, model fine-tuning, and evaluation.
*   Experience with frameworks such as LangChain, Hugging Face Transformers, and OpenAI API.
*   Knowledge of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), vector databases (e.g., Pinecone, Weaviate, FAISS), and embedding techniques.
*   Experience building conversational AI, document intelligence, and generative data synthesis solutions.
*   Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or related field.
