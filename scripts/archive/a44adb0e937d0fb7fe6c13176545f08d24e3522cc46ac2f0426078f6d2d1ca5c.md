Description

The Solution Architect will play a pivotal role in advancing our digital and AI strategy. Reporting directly to the CIO, this hands-on role blends architecture, development, and innovation to design and deliver modern digital platforms, AI-driven applications, and data solutions that simplify complexity, drive efficiency, and enhance the customer and employee experience.

  

This role is key to shaping how Betco leverages data, AI, and automation to scale growth, improve operations, and deliver exceptional customer experiences.

Requirements

**Solution Design and Delivery**

*   Architect and build custom applications, integrations, and digital tools that drive measurable business value.
*   Define and champion solution design standards across data, applications, and APIs.
*   Evaluate and recommend new technologies that drive business value.

**AI and Data**

*   Apply AI and automation to simplify processes and enhance decision-making.
*   Lead modern data architecture efforts to improve access to information and insights.
*   Explore and implement agentic AI solutions with clear, high ROI.

**Execution and Leadership**

*   Drive end-to-end delivery of application, AI, and data projects with full ownership and measurable results.
*   Prototype, test, and deliver working solutions using an AI-centric development approach.
*   Ensure adoption and success through close collaboration with business teams.
*   Foster a culture of innovation, accountability, and continuous improvement.

**Collaboration**

*   Partner with other departments to deliver technological solutions that align with growth and efficiency goals.
*   Clear and effective communicator, able to explain technical solutions in business terms and business needs in technical terms.
*   Champion simplicity in design to maximize adoption and minimize training.

**Technical Expertise**

*   Application Development: Node.js, Python, Next.js, React
*   AI & Automation: LLM-centric apps, frameworks, prompting, agentic design
*   Integrations: API-first and event-driven architecture patterns
*   Low-Code Platforms: modern tools that accelerate delivery
*   Data Architecture: Snowflake, dbt, modern modeling, pipelines.

**Mindset and Approach**

*   Demonstrates strong ownership, accountability, and follow-through on commitments.
*   Execution-oriented with strong commitment to delivery and results.
*   Passion for learning and innovation, with interest in emerging areas such as agentic AI.
*   Collaborative and able to guide teams through complexity and ambiguity.
*   Passionate about user experiences that are simple and intuitive.

**Qualifications and Skills**

*   5+ years in solution architecture, application development, or related roles.
*   Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science or related field.
*   Proven track record of delivering production-ready solutions with high ROI.
*   Strong communication skills to connect strategy with execution.
*   Comfortable working in ambiguity and simplifying complexity into actionable steps.
