### Description

**Start your career in sales – 100% commission-based & with unlimited earning potential!**

SumUp is looking for motivated individuals with an entrepreneurial spirit to join us as a freelance Field Sales Consultant. If you're eager to work independently, shape your own success, and manage your time freely, this is the perfect opportunity for you.

SumUp empowers small businesses with smart and user-friendly financial solutions, ranging from card payments to comprehensive POS systems. Our mission is to strengthen entrepreneurs, allowing them to fully focus on their core business.

As a **freelance Field Sales Consultant**, you'll be your own boss, with the exciting potential to earn **attractive, unlimited commissions**! Your primary role involves building relationships with local businesses and demonstrating how SumUp can simplify and enhance their operations. You'll be directly selling our solutions, whether it's mobile card terminals or extensive payment systems, equipped with everything you need to succeed.

**What you can expect:**

*   **100% performance-based compensation** – your income is directly tied to your effort, with no upper limit.
*   **Maximum flexibility** – work whenever and wherever you choose.
*   **Top products with high market potential** – offer compelling solutions to small businesses.
*   **Training & sales support** – we provide you with the resources and guidance you need to thrive.

### Responsibilities

As a Field Sales Consultant, your key responsibilities will include:

*   **Active new customer acquisition** – identify and win local businesses for SumUp.
*   **Personal meetings and consultations** – present our products and demonstrate their clear benefits.

*   **Building customer relationships** – secure long-term partnerships through excellent service and trust.
*   **Independent customer and lead management** – you have the autonomy to decide how you build and manage your business pipeline.

### Qualifications

To be successful in this role, we're looking for individuals with:

*   A passion for sales and strong self-motivation.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer support is an advantage.
*   Excellent communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Very good German language skills.
