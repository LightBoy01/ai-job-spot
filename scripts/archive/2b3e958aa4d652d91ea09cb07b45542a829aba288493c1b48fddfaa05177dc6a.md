**Senior Product Manager – Agentic AI Platforms**   
**Location: Dallas, TX**    
**Experience: 8+ years**   

**Employment Type: Full-time**  

**Role Summary** 

The Senior Product Manager leads the strategy, development, and delivery of AI-driven products and platforms powered by intelligent agents and large language models (LLMs). This role combines **strategic product leadership**, **technical depth**, and **cross-functional collaboration** to bring scalable, enterprise-ready AI capabilities to market. 

**Key Responsibilities** 

Define and prioritize product features and roadmap for AI and automation platforms. 

Partner with engineering to design and scale **multi-agent systems**, **LLM workflows**, and **integration frameworks**. 

Translate technical concepts (e.g., **Model Context Protocols**, **vector databases**, **retrieval-augmented generation**) into clear user value. 

Work directly with enterprise clients to identify needs, define use cases, and ensure successful adoption. 

Collaborate with design, data, and infrastructure teams to build secure, efficient, and user-friendly experiences. 

Track product performance, usage metrics, and feedback to drive continuous improvement and innovation. 

**Qualifications** 

**8–12 years of Product Management experience**, preferably in **SaaS** or **platform-based products**. 

Strong understanding of **LLMs**, **Agentic AI architectures**, and modern **AI development workflows**. 

Familiarity with **cloud platforms**, **API orchestration**, and **identity/security frameworks** (e.g., OAuth2, SSO). 

Experience working with engineering, design, and enterprise stakeholders in agile environments. 

Excellent communication, analytical, and problem-solving skills. 

**Preferred Experience** 

Background in **AI platforms**, **automation**, or **data-driven enterprise software**. 

Exposure to **LangChain**, **LangGraph**, **Semantic Kernel**, or similar orchestration frameworks. 

Proven ability to connect technical depth with strategic business outcomes.
