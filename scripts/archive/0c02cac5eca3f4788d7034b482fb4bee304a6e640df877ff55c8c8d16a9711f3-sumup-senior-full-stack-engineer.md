### Description
As a crucial member of the Field Sales Engineering Team, you will be instrumental in maintaining and enhancing our Global Field Sales processes, directly enabling SumUp's overarching mission. Operating within the Platform Tribe, you'll contribute to developing essential internal developer platforms. Your key role involves designing and building software systems that empower both Sales and Engineering teams to identify optimal solutions and approaches. These solutions will benefit SumUp merchants and internal users alike, ensuring our users can concentrate on delivering value to customers without being burdened by underlying infrastructure concerns.

### Responsibilities
*   Design, build, and maintain services integral to dependable and highly available production systems, adhering to SumUp's microservice architecture.
*   Develop high-quality, scalable code following Platform standards and best practices.
*   Collaborate with stakeholders to pinpoint pain points and collect feature requests.
*   Work closely with Sales, Data, and Software Engineers across Platform and Operations teams.

### Qualifications
*   5+ years of programming experience in Typescript.
*   Prior experience with Fullstack Frameworks such as Next/Remix/Nuxt/Meteor.
*   Proficient in building and consuming RESTful APIs within a microservice environment.
*   Experience with SQL & Event driven architecture, specifically working with Postgres and Kafka.
*   Understanding of fundamental system architecture, software design principles, data modelling, and API design.
*   Collaborative mindset and open to feedback in teamwork settings.
*   Demonstrated pride in engineering work and a strong sense of ownership in tasks.
