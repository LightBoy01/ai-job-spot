### Description\nAre you ready to take responsibility for stable IT structures in a modern industrial environment? For an internationally active company in Bochum, we are looking for you as an **IT Operations Manager (m/f/d)** for direct placement. This is a role where you will decisively develop technologies and achieve a real impact. The company offers a permanent position, significant scope for shaping IT operations, flexible working hours with up to 2 days of home office per week, and tailored professional development opportunities. You'll work in a collegiate environment with short decision paths.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Operational management and continuous development of the entire IT infrastructure.\n- Administration of Windows servers, Active Directory, Exchange, and M365.\n- Support and optimization of the virtualized environment (e.g., Hyper-V).\n- Ensuring a reliable network infrastructure (switches, routers, firewalls).\n- Planning and operation of monitoring, analysis, and security solutions.\n- Implementation of IT security measures and creation of technical concepts.\n- 1st and 2nd-level support for employees at the location.\n- Documentation and collaboration on exciting IT and digitalization projects.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Education as an IT specialist (\\\"Fachinformatiker\\\") or comparable qualification.\n- Professional experience in IT operations or system administration.\n- Very good knowledge of the Microsoft server environment and with M365.\n- Experience in networking and with security solutions.\n- Structured, solution-oriented way of working.\n- Enjoyment of teamwork and communication.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a compelling opportunity for an experienced IT professional who thrives on autonomy and making a tangible impact. The role offers a fantastic blend of operational responsibility and strategic development within an international company committed to modern IT practices. With flexible work options, a supportive culture, and significant investment in professional growth, it's ideal for someone looking to truly influence and shape an organization's digital future. The company values collaborative problem-solving and offers a clear path for continuous learning.
