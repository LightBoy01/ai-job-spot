We’re seeking an experience SEO Outreach Manager to lead and execute off-page SEO strategies across all LiveScore Group brands. You’ll work closely with the Head of SEO to shape and deliver high-impact outreach campaigns.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Develop and execute effective outreach strategies to build relationships with relevant blogs, sites and publishers.
*   Evaluate linking opportunities through research, competitive analysis and third party SEO metrics
*   Work closely with internal teams (Brand, Social, Content, Affiliates) to align campaigns and content with our link building objectives.
*   Coordinate and monitor vendors, tracking performance metrics, link quality, and ROI to optimise overall link building efficiency.
*   Monitor and report on the success of outreach campaigns and keep an up-to-date link inventory.
*   Execute campaigns and keep track of the budget and invoicing process.
*   Monitor and maintain the health of the existing backlinks, including spotting any anomalies in our backlink profile.

**Skills, Knowledge and Experience**

*   Proven experience of working on outreach strategies 
*   Fluent in English with strong verbal and written communication skills; proficiency in additional languages is a plus.
*   Strong understanding of off-page techniques, IPs, subnet analysis and key link quality metrics.
*   Proficient in SEO tools such as aHrefs, Majestic, Linkody and other SEO tools.
*   Familiarity with Generative AI, LLMs, and the evolving search landscape shaped by AI-powered responses.
*   Excellent problem-solving skills, time management and organisation skills.
*   Ability to analyse the success of outreach campaigns and adjust the strategy accordingly.
*   Creative mindset with a flair for generating innovative, forward-thinking outreach ideas.
