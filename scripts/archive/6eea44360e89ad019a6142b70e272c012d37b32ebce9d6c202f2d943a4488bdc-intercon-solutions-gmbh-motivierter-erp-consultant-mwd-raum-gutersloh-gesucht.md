### Description
Our client is a successful mechanical engineering company with approximately 500 employees. With high technical competence and innovative strength, the company develops and produces sophisticated machinery and systems for international customers.

As part of its digital evolution, the ERP landscape is continuously optimized and adapted to business processes. For this, they are seeking an engaged individual who combines IT expertise with a deep understanding of mechanical engineering processes – and enjoys actively driving digitalization and process optimization as an **ERP-Consultant (m/w/d)**.

### Responsibilities
*   Analyze, optimize, and redesign business processes in various corporate divisions (e.g., production, design, materials management, finance, and sales).
*   Independently develop solution concepts and closely coordinate with management and the owner family.
*   Take responsibility for leading projects, including coordinating implementation teams and managing external service providers.
*   Advise on the selection and introduction of suitable ERP modules.
*   Support and mentor users during and after the implementation of new processes and systems.
*   Create project documentation, evaluations, and reports for project management and general management.

### Qualifications
*   Completed degree in Business Informatics, Computer Science, or a comparable qualification, preferably with a technical background.
*   Practical experience in project and change management with strong implementation skills.
*   In-depth understanding of business processes in various industries, ideally in a manufacturing company.
*   Extensive experience in consulting and implementing ERP systems.
*   Strong communication and presentation skills.
*   Team-oriented, independent, and structured way of working.
