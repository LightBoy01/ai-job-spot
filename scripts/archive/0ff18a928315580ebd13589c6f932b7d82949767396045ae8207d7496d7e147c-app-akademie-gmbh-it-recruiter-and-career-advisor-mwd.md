### Description\nApp Akademie trains software developers for modern tech roles. We are looking for an individual who combines **sales competence** with **IT recruiting experience** and understands the dynamics of the tech job market.\n\nYou will be the primary expert contact for prospective students, career changers, and experienced IT professionals who wish to enter the job market or reposition themselves through our courses.\n\nYou will leverage your **recruiting know-how**, **IT understanding**, and **trustworthy consulting skills** to assess:\n*   Who is truly suitable for the course?\n*   Which career paths are realistic?\n*   What skills are missing and how can we bridge these gaps?\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Conduct initial consultations with prospects and assess suitability.\n*   Evaluate technical pre-experience and learning potential.\n*   Guide candidates through the decision-making process.\n*   Inform prospects persuasively and transparently about the program.\n*   Apply your understanding of IT roles and job market requirements.\n*   Monitor the application funnel and propose optimization suggestions.\n*   Further develop screening criteria, interview guidelines, and processes.\n\n### Qualifications\n**Must-have:**\n*   Experience in **Tech Recruiting**, HR, or Talent Acquisition.\n*   Experience in **Sales**, Admissions, or a consulting environment.\n*   Basic understanding of software and digital professions.\n*   Professional interview skills & strong communication abilities.\n*   Structured way of working, ownership, & reliability.\n\n**Nice-to-have:**\n*   Experience in continuing education/EdTech.\n*   Good understanding of current tech stacks (not mandatory, but helpful).\n*   Experience with AVGS, BGS, or publicly funded further education.\n*   Knowledge of moderation, coaching, or conversational diagnostics.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a pivotal role at App Akademie, offering the unique opportunity to blend your sales acumen with tech recruiting expertise to directly impact individuals' career journeys. You'll operate at the intersection of talent acquisition and education, guiding aspiring tech professionals while continuously refining processes. The role offers excellent flexibility with full remote options, a provided tech setup, and strong cross-functional collaboration. Regular team events in Berlin also foster a strong sense of community. If you are passionate about empowering people to enter the tech world and thrive in a dynamic, supportive environment, this is an exciting opportunity.
