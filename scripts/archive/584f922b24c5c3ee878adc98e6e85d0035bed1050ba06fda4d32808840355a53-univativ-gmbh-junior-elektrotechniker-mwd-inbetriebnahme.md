### Description\nunivativ is a dedicated project and personnel service provider committed to fostering the careers of students and graduates by connecting them with exciting opportunities at renowned companies. For an esteemed client in the transport and traffic sector, we are seeking a motivated (Junior) Electrical Engineer to join their team in Salzgitter.\n\nThis role is ideal for individuals passionate about rail vehicles, offering a chance to apply your electrical engineering skills in a dynamic environment. You'll be instrumental in ensuring the functionality and safety of rail vehicles, performing diagnostics, and resolving complex electrical and mechanical issues. Be prepared for a hands-on role that may involve working at heights of up to 4 meters and in areas with restricted accessibility, offering a truly unique and challenging experience.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Perform comprehensive testing of rail vehicles according to predefined instructions.\n*   Independently establish electrical, mechanical, and pneumatic functions for vehicles.\n*   Conduct thorough troubleshooting based on circuit and function diagrams, and independently resolve complex errors (e.g., analysis of pneumatic, electrical, software, and climate systems).\n*   Document all work results, including writing protocols and error lists.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   A completed apprenticeship or further training as an Electronics Technician, or a degree in Electrical Engineering.\n*   Initial experience with electrical components and devices.\n*   Fluent German language skills, both written and spoken.\n*   Willingness to work occasionally at heights of up to 4 meters and in areas with restricted accessibility, as well as readiness for shift work.\n*   Quick comprehension, an independent, and analytical working style.\n*   Possession of a Class B driver's license.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an Electrical Engineer looking to gain specialized experience within the crucial transport and traffic sector. univativ truly prioritizes your development, offering a supportive environment with a personal career companion, diverse training opportunities via their e-academy, and the chance to build a robust professional network through varied client projects. The role itself is hands-on and impactful, perfect for someone who enjoys complex problem-solving and isn't afraid of a dynamic work setting. With an attractive salary, special payments, and a commitment to your professional growth, this position offers a solid foundation for a rewarding career.
