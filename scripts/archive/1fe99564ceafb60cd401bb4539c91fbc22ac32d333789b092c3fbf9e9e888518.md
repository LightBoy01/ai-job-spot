RayzeBio, a Bristol Myers Squibb company, is an innovative clinical-stage radiopharmaceutical company focused on harnessing targeted radioisotopes to improve survival for people with cancer. As a standalone biotech subsidiary, we combine the agility of a startup with the resources and reach of BMS. Our mission is to develop and deliver next-generation medicines for solid tumors through scientific excellence, collaboration, and a highly empowered team culture.
RayzeBio is revolutionizing cancer treatment through targeted radiopharmaceutical therapy (RPT). The Senior Director, Clinical Development Analytics & RPT Orchestration will lead the development and implementation of cutting-edge digital capabilities that accelerate our path from clinical development to commercial success. This role is critical to establishing data-driven decision-making frameworks across our medical organization while building the digital infrastructure necessary for successful RPT commercialization.
**Key Responsibilities**
_Medical Organization Digital Excellence_
*   Serve as the primary digital and analytics partner to the Chief Medical Officer and development teams, establishing comprehensive digital strategies
*   Lead clinical trial analytics initiatives to optimize study design, execution, and decision-making through advanced modeling and real-world data insights
*   Develop and implement Medical Science Liaison (MSL) systems and analytics capabilities, integrating CRM platforms with clinical operations and commercial planning
*   Design and oversee clinical supply chain optimization systems to ensure efficient delivery of radiopharmaceutical therapies to trial sites while minimizing waste
*   Implement AI-driven algorithms for scheduling optimization and predictive analytics to enhance operational efficiency
_RPT Orchestration for Commercial Readiness_
*   Lead digital strategy for building integrated systems for product ordering, scheduling, and fulfillment across the end-to-end patient and product journeys
*   Collaborate with commercial, clinical, manufacturing, and quality teams to establish comprehensive visibility and control systems for product and patient management
*   Design scalable digital infrastructure to seamless site ordering capabilities and waste prevention
*   Develop analytics frameworks to monitor and optimize operational KPIs
*   Implement AI-driven algorithms for scheduling optimization and predictive analytics to enhance operational efficiency
_Digital & Analytics Capabilities for APC3 Team_
*   Lead digital and analytics strategy for newly formed cross-functional strategic ACP3 team
*   Serve as primary digital analytics partner for this high-priority initiative, building integrated data capabilities to maximize ACP3 patient impact and value
*   Design and implement digital solutions that enhance cross-functional collaboration efficiency and accelerate development timelines
*   Identify and deploy AI-solutions that speed up the development of APC3 and expand the number of patients the program can serve
_Team Leadership & Innovation_
*   Build and lead a team of digital analytics professionals, fostering a culture of innovation and continuous learning
*   Establish partnerships with internal data management, IT, and BMS enterprise teams to leverage shared capabilities and ensure alignment
*   Drive adoption of emerging technologies including AI/ML, advanced analytics, and cloud-based platforms
*   Manage vendor relationships and external partnerships to supplement internal capabilities as needed
**Required Qualifications**
*   Bachelor's degree in quantitative discipline (Statistics, Data Science, Engineering, Life Sciences) required; Advanced degree preferred
*   10+ years in digital analytics, data science, or related roles with proven pharmaceutical/biotech experience
*   Demonstrated leadership of analytics teams and successful delivery of enterprise-scale digital transformation initiatives
*   Deep expertise in clinical trial operations, commercial analytics, and healthcare data management
*   Experience with cloud platforms, data architecture, and systems integration
*   Results-Driven: Proven track record of delivering measurable business impact through data-driven insights and operational improvements
*   Collaborative Leader: Skilled at building cross-functional partnerships and influencing without authority across complex organizations
*   Communication Excellence: Ability to present complex analytical concepts to senior leadership and diverse stakeholder groups
**Preferred Qualifications**
*   Experience with radiopharmaceuticals, oncology and prostate cancer strongly preferred
*   Strong background in AI/ML, predictive modeling, and advanced analytics methodologies
**Travel & Location**
*   Travel up to 30% to Indianapolis, Princeton, NJ, and other BMS locations as needed
*   Occasional travel for conferences, partnerships, and stakeholder meetings
