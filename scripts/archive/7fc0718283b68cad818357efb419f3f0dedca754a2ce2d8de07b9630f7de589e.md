ECS is seeking an **_AI Integration Engineer_** to work in our **_Arlington, VA_** office.

We are seeking an accomplished **AI Requirements Engineer** to lead the end-to-end design, development, and operational support of AI-enabled platforms and enterprise applications. The ideal candidate will combine strategic vision, business process analysis, and technical leadership to ensure delivery of secure, scalable, and user-centric solutions. This role requires experience modernizing complex IT systems, collaborating with executive stakeholders, and guiding cross-functional teams through full agile lifecycles—from concept and development through production and ongoing operations and maintenance (O&M). 

**Responsibilities:** 

*   Lead the strategic planning, requirements definition, and delivery of AI solutions that align with enterprise objectives. 
*   Define program vision and roadmap, balancing priorities across multiple stakeholders. 
*   Serve as the primary liaison between technical teams, leadership, and customers, translating business needs into actionable requirements and managing expectations. 
*   Elicit, document, and validate functional and technical requirements, including AI model integration, data flows, and security constraints. 
*   Define and maintain a prioritized product backlog, user stories, and acceptance criteria to ensure timely and high-quality feature delivery. 
*   Collaborate with vendors and internal development teams to integrate commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) products, APIs, and automation tools. 
*   Lead cross-functional agile teams through sprint planning, execution, testing, and production deployment. 
*   Oversee the design and enhancement of workflows, UI/UX improvements, data integrations, and back-end processes. 
*   Ensure system quality through user acceptance testing (UAT), performance monitoring, and continuous improvement cycles. 
*   Manage ongoing operations, maintenance, and annual release cycles to ensure high availability, scalability, and compliance with security requirements. 
*   Track and report key performance indicators (KPIs), such as user satisfaction, adoption rates, and system performance, to inform iterative enhancements. 
*   Streamline business processes through automation, analytics, and AI-driven capabilities to improve efficiency and reduce manual overhead. 
*   Develop standard operating procedures (SOPs) for model retraining, rollback, and root-cause analysis. 
*   Document incident response procedures for AI model failures and anomalies.
