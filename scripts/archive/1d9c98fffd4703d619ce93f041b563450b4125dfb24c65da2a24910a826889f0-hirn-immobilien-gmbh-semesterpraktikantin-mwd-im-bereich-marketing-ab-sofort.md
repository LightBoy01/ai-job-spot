### Description
Hirn Immobilien GmbH, a family-run, medium-sized real estate company, has been a significant presence in the Ulm and Neu-Ulm region for almost 40 years. We offer everything to excite real estate enthusiasts, from classical residential property brokerage and marketing investment projects to the sale of developer projects. As consultants and brokers, we accompany residential project developments, and through Hirn Projekt GmbH, we also realize our own construction projects. Furthermore, we provide market-oriented valuations by in-house experts. We value trust and fair cooperation within the team and with our customers.

This role offers full support for your semester internship, deep insights into comprehensive marketing measures in the real estate industry, and the opportunity to work in a family-run company with a young and motivated team. You will enjoy flexible organization, flat hierarchies, and a fun work environment with perks like drinks, snacks, company events, and office dogs.

### Responsibilities
*   Support our marketing team with all online and offline advertising measures.
*   Graphically implement exposés, postcards, brochures, advertisements, website graphics, and much more, within our corporate identity and brand guidelines.
*   Develop creative designs for marketing our properties.
*   Support the expansion of social media activities.
*   Assist with target group-oriented acquisition measures (e.g., newsletters, online campaigns, print media, etc.).
*   Support the marketing team in preparing our events.
*   Take on varied tasks and manage your own projects.

### Qualifications
*   Currently enrolled in a degree program in graphic design, marketing, information management, corporate communications, communication design, or similar.
*   Love to work creatively and bring bold, fresh ideas to our advertising.
*   Basic knowledge of common graphic and image editing programs (e.g., Adobe CC with InDesign, Photoshop, Illustrator, as well as Canva, etc.).
*   Enthusiasm for teamwork and enjoyment of what you do.
*   Very good German language skills, both written and spoken.
