### Description

**Our Greenery** combines innovative technologies with plants and interior design. Our goal is to enable everyone to grow food in their own homes or for employees within companies – fully automated and sustainably.

We are a tech startup based in Berlin and Brandenburg with the mission to develop more and more products that are intuitive, set new standards in efficiency, and are designed entirely in the spirit of circularity and functional design.

Team is key. We are a collective of individuals driven by the need to change the status status quo and forge new paths. Because the challenges of our time can only be solved together. You will have the opportunity to contribute to one of the most important tasks of our time: the sustainable nutrition of all people. Sounds good? Let's go!

**Your Role as Operations Lead:**
You will be responsible for the smooth functioning of our operational processes – from the production of our indoor gardens and the supply chain to customer support. You ensure that everything runs efficiently, transparently, and reliably.

You will work closely with the management team (Daniel & Florian) and act as the crucial link between production, logistics, service partners, and customers. This is a management role with significant responsibility and decision-making power, offering you the chance to actively shape the company's structures and contribute directly to our impactful mission.

### Responsibilities

*   **1. Production**
    *   Manage the ongoing production of our indoor gardens in collaboration with manufacturing partners.
    *   Plan, conduct quality control, and optimize production processes.
    *   Ensure that delivery times, quality, and costs remain within target parameters.

*   **2. Supply Chain Management**
    *   Responsible for procurement, material availability, and supplier communication.
    *   Build and maintain transparent supplier reporting.
    *   Optimize purchasing conditions and delivery times.

*   **3. Logistics & Fulfillment**
    *   Organize and manage shipping, warehousing, and returns.
    *   Collaborate with freight forwarders, courier services, and service partners.
    *   Establish efficient processes for picking, packing, and tracking.

*   **4. Customer Care**
    *   Work closely with the Customer Care team to ensure a first-class customer experience.
    *   Organize our service partners for maintenance and repairs.
    *   Implement a simple CRM/ticket system for tracking inquiries.

*   **5. Internal Processes & Operations**
    *   Responsible for smooth daily operational workflow.
    *   Continue and track our OKRs.

### Qualifications

**Your Profile:**

*   Several years of experience in operations, production, or supply chain management, ideally in hardware or manufacturing environments.
*   Analytical and solution-oriented thinking with strong implementation skills.
*   Understanding of technical and logistical processes.
*   Independent, structured way of working with a strong hands-on mentality.
*   Passion for sustainability, design, and innovation.
