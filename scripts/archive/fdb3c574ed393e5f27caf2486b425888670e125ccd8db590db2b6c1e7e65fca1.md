### **Role Overview**
As the VP of Engineering, you are the architect and leader of our entire product development organization. Your mission is to build and lead a single, high-performing, AI-first team—spanning engineering, research science, product management, security, and design—to create a generational company. This is a role for a deeply technical, hands-on "player-coach" who is not only a technical architect but also the operational driver of the day-to-day product build. You will set an exceptionally high bar for quality and velocity, fostering a culture of ownership, rapid learning, and relentless innovation.
This role is based in our Palo Alto, CA office. We use an in-person work model and offer relocation assistance.
### **What You'll Do**
*   **Build and Lead a World-Class Team:** Recruit, hire, and mentor A+ talent across all disciplines (Engineering, AI Research, PM, Design, Security). You will cultivate a high-performance culture defined by ownership, high standards, and a focus on growth.
*   **Drive Execution and Velocity:** **Own the operational cadence of the product development team.** You will personally run sprint planning, stand-ups, and retrospectives to ensure tight execution. You will implement and optimize lightweight processes that accelerate development without sacrificing quality, instilling a strong bias for action and an obsession with shipping.
*   **Provide Technical Leadership:** Remain deeply technical and hands-on. You will guide critical architectural decisions, contribute to the codebase, and solve the hardest technical challenges alongside the team, ensuring we build scalable, secure, and reliable AI systems.
*   **Champion an AI-First Culture:** Foster an environment where an "AI-first" mindset is embedded in every part of the development process. You will encourage the rapid adoption of new tools and techniques that enhance productivity and innovation.
### **What You Have**
*   **Proven Leadership Experience:** 15+ years of experience with a clear track record of building and leading high-performing, cross-functional product development teams in a fast-paced, high-growth environment.
*   **Deep Technical Foundation:** You are a deeply technical, hands-on leader, not just a manager of managers. You have a background as a strong engineer and can command the respect of a world-class technical team. Experience with AI/ML products is essential.
*   **Execution Excellence:** A proven track record of shipping high-quality products with exceptional speed and precision. You have a keen eye for detail and instill a culture of excellence and accountability.
*   **World-Class Organizational & Communication Skills:** You create clarity from ambiguity. You can align the entire organization around a vision and communicate effectively with the board, customers, and individual contributors.
*   **Ownership and Growth Mindset:** You embody a true ownership mentality with an intense focus on growth. You are highly adaptable, learn and adopt new technologies quickly, and are relentless in your pursuit of building a generational company.
