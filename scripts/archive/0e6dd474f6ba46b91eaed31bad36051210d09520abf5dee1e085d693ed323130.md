### Description

The Public Service's Graduate Industry Traineeship Programme (GRIT@Gov) is designed to equip fresh graduates with industry-relevant skills and experience, strengthening their employability for full-time roles in the sector.

Join the SkillsFuture Singapore Agency (SSG) as an AI Engineer Trainee within the Data Strategy & Analytics Division (DSAD). In this role, you will be exposed to impactful projects in artificial intelligence and machine learning that directly support SSG's mission: to enable individuals to learn for life, pursue skills mastery, and develop fulfilling careers for a future-ready Singapore.

Working alongside experienced engineers and data scientists, you will receive dedicated mentorship and training opportunities throughout your traineeship. This program is designed to develop both your technical competencies and professional skills, all while aligning with SSG's ACCOLade values of Adaptability, Commitment, Collaboration, Openness, and Learning.

### Responsibilities

**AI Implementation and Technical Development**

*   Assist with AI implementation tasks, including data pipeline automation and model deployment, under the guidance of experienced AI Engineers.
*   Develop foundational competencies in machine learning algorithms, data processing, and model optimization.
*   Support the development and maintenance of comprehensive technical documentation for AI models and workflows to ensure knowledge transfer and system sustainability.
*   Provide technical support for ongoing AI initiatives, such as streamlining data processing workflows and supporting model validation processes.

**AI Governance and Best Practices**

*   Contribute to improving and operationalizing the AI governance framework, ensuring adherence to best practices in model management and compliance.
*   Develop a strong understanding of ethical AI deployment and regulatory requirements.
*   Collaborate with team members to integrate AI models into broader system architectures, demonstrating technical adaptability.
*   Participate in knowledge-sharing sessions to enhance your learning and contribute to the team’s collective expertise.

### Qualifications

*   A relevant academic background or training in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Engineering, Data Science, or a related field.
*   Experience with machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, or generative AI applications gained through academic studies, project work, or internships.
*   Familiarity with the Python programming language and AI frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, or scikit-learn is advantageous.
*   A willingness to learn about cloud platforms (like AWS SageMaker), MLOps practices, and containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes).
*   Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a genuine curiosity for emerging technologies and innovative solutions.
*   Excellent communication skills and a collaborative mindset to work effectively within a team.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "As a trainee, what is the balance between learning and contributing? Will I be working on real, impactful projects, or mostly handling routine tasks?"**

That's a great question, and it gets to the heart of what makes this traineeship different. From day one, you're not just an observer; you're part of the team. While there's a significant focus on structured learning and mentorship, it’s all in the context of projects that matter. You won't be siloed into just 'training' tasks. You might start by assisting a senior engineer with optimizing a data pipeline for a new skills recommendation engine, and by the end of your project, you'll be co-presenting the validation results. We believe the best way to learn is by doing, and our goal is to give you a solid foundation to launch your career. The work here directly impacts how we help Singaporeans navigate their careers, so your contributions, guided by your mentor, have real-world weight.

**Another candidate might ask: "Can you give an example of how the AI projects at SSG connect to the agency's broader mission?"**

Absolutely. Think about the challenge of helping a mid-career professional figure out what new skills they need for the jobs of tomorrow. It's a massive puzzle. One of our core projects involves using natural language processing and machine learning models to analyze job postings and course descriptions at a national scale. Your work could directly contribute to an AI system that identifies emerging skills trends and then recommends personalized learning pathways to individuals. You're not just coding an algorithm; you're building a tool that could empower someone to make a life-changing career move. That's the kind of mission-driven work we do here. It’s deeply collaborative and rooted in our 'Openness' and 'Learning' values, because we’re all working together to solve problems that truly matter for the nation.
