### Description
Welcome to SumUp! Are you ready to take charge of your career, shape your own success, and manage your time freely? Then you've found the right place!

SumUp empowers small businesses with smart and user-friendly financial solutions, ranging from card payments to comprehensive POS systems. Our mission is to strengthen entrepreneurs, allowing them to fully concentrate on their core business.

As a freelance Field Sales Consultant, you are your own boss, with the exciting opportunity to earn attractive, unlimited commissions! You'll build valuable relationships with local businesses and demonstrate how SumUp can simplify their operations. Your main task will be the direct sale of our solutions – whether it's a mobile card terminal or comprehensive payment systems, you'll have everything you need to succeed.

What awaits you:
*   **100% performance-based compensation** – your income depends on your commitment, with no upper limit.
*   **Maximum flexibility** – work whenever and wherever you want.
*   **Top products with high market potential** – compelling solutions for small businesses.
*   **Training & Sales Support** – we won't leave you alone on your journey.

### Responsibilities
*   **Active New Customer Acquisition**: Identify and win local businesses for SumUp.
*   **Personal Meetings and Consulting**: Present our products and demonstrate their benefits.
*   **Build Customer Relationships**: Secure long-term partnerships through excellent service.
*   **Own Customer and Lead Management**: You decide how to build your business.

### Qualifications
*   Passion for sales and self-motivation.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer service is an advantage.
*   Strong communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Very good German language skills.
