**Roles and Responsibilities:**

*   Define and drive the Gen AI COE roadmap, ensuring alignment with business objectives.
*   Develop and execute project plans for AI infrastructure setup, fine-tuning open-source LLMs, Q&A bot UI, and natural language querying.
*   Establish and track KPIs, SLAs, and success metrics for AI-driven solutions.
*   Ensure on-time delivery, scope adherence, and risk mitigation in AI projects.
*   Lead cross-functional teams (ML engineers, software engineers, DevOps, data scientists) for effective AI execution.
*   Act as a trusted advisor to enterprise clients, ensuring continuous value delivery and adoption of Gen AI solutions.
*   Identify opportunities for expansion and additional AI services.
*   Manage stakeholder relationships and set clear expectations for AI project outcomes.
*   Facilitate Agile ceremonies and collaborate with engineering, MLOps, and product teams for seamless AI development.
*   Coordinate infrastructure provisioning, model deployment, and performance optimization with DevOps.
*   Maintain and refine technical documentation, backlogs, and sprint reports.
*   Oversee AI model deployment on cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure) ensuring scalability, security, and cost optimization.
*   Guide teams on AI pipeline automation, CI/CD for ML models, and ML governance best practices.
*   Ensure regulatory compliance and data privacy adherence in AI-driven HCLS solutions.

**Required Skills:**

*   Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Computer Science, AI/ML, Data Science, or related technical field.
*   10+ years in technical program management, AI/ML product delivery, or cloud-based AI solutions.

*   Proficiency in cloud AI platforms (AWS SageMaker or GCP Vertex AI or Azure ML).
*   Knowledge of natural language processing (NLP), prompt engineering, RAG, and Gen AI architectures.

*   Excellent problem-solving, communication, and leadership skills.
