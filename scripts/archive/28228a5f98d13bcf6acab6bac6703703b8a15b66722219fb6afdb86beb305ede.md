**AI Omics Scientist III – Remote PST**

The AI Omics Scientist III is a senior-level contributor responsible for leading the development of scalable AI/ML solutions in support of complex genomic and multi-omics research. This role integrates deep learning, advanced statistical modeling, and biomedical interpretation to accelerate insights in diagnostics, therapeutic development, and clinical decision-making. You will work cross-functionally with scientists, engineers, and clinical teams to design models and deploy them within high-performance computing environments. 

 **Essential Functions**

*   Lead the design and development of machine learning models for omics analysis and clinical AI tools
*   Drive integration of AI with multi-omics datasets, including genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and clinical phenotypes
*   Apply deep learning architectures (CNNs, RNNs, transformers, LLMs) for predictive and generative analysis
*   Contribute to production-ready deployment of models using MLOps and cloud-native tools 
*   Mentor junior scientists, guide project direction, and ensure scientific rigor in AI-driven research
*   Publish high-impact findings and collaborate with external partners on AI method development
*   Ensure reproducibility and interpretability of models in a regulatory and clinical context (e.g., CAP, CLIA)
*   Collaborate with software engineering teams to scale tools to production and integrate with user-facing platforms
*   This position may serve as a technical lead or project owner. While it does not have direct reports, it includes mentoring of junior scientists and cross-functional project coordination 
*   Other duties as assigned

**Qualifications**

*   PhD in Computational Biology, Bioinformatics, AI/ML, or a related field with 4+ years of post-degree experience, or MS with 6+ years of relevant experience
*   Strong grounding in omics technologies (e.g., RNA-seq, WGS, single-cell, proteomics) and biomedical informatics
*   Deep understanding of machine learning theory, statistical modeling, and biological interpretation
*   Expert in Python and advanced ML frameworks (e.g., PyTorch, TensorFlow, JAX, HuggingFace)
*   Proficiency with GPU-based model training, performance tuning, and distributed learning frameworks
*   Experience in MLOps tools (MLflow, DVC), containerization (Docker, Kubernetes), and cloud platforms (AWS/GCP)
*   Deep knowledge of omics-specific processing pipelines and tools (e.g., GATK, Nextflow, Snakemake)
*   Experience with foundational models (e.g., BERT, BioBERT, GPT, AlphaFold, or domain-adapted LLMs)
*   Familiarity with HIPAA-compliant and regulated environments
*   Strong communication skills for both technical and cross-disciplinary collaboration 
*   5+ years applying AI/ML in bioinformatics, biomedical, or healthcare domains
*   Experience leading AI research initiatives or clinical deployment efforts
*   Track record of peer-reviewed publications, conference presentations, or open-source contributions
*   Strong interdisciplinary collaboration across data science, software, and clinical domains
