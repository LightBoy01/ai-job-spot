### Description\nRemmert specializes in automated storage and logistics systems, providing space-saving storage and fully automated movement for sheet metal, long goods, and workpieces. We operate in a future-oriented industry of mechanical engineering, automation, and logistics.\n\nSince 1945, we have been a family-run company, living by principles of strong development prospects, open communication, and short decision-making paths. We are continuously growing and looking for colleagues to actively support our team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Requirements Definition & Procurement Planning:** Close collaboration with sales, engineering, and project management to derive technical specifications, create bills of material, and procurement schedules.\n- **Supplier Selection & Development:** Identification, evaluation, and selection of suitable suppliers, considering quality, ESG, and certification standards. Building and maintaining long-term supplier relationships.\n- **Contract & Price Negotiations:** Drafting and negotiating framework agreements, price structures, and payment terms. Ensuring cost-optimized procurement while meeting technical requirements.\n- **Technical Risk & Quality Management:** Reviewing technical specifications, standards, and compatibility. Collaboration with quality assurance and managing deviations.\n- **Supply Chain Tracking & Deadline Management:** Monitoring delivery dates, coordinating deliveries, and supporting assembly and installation.\n- **Cost Efficiency & Budget Control:** Creating cost overviews, conducting price analyses, and contributing to budget planning.\n- **Documentation & Reporting:** Maintaining procurement documentation and providing regular status reports to project management.\n- **Process Optimization & & Digitalization:** Contributing to the further development of procurement processes and utilizing digital tools for supplier evaluation.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed technical training and commercial further education with a focus on mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, or comparable qualification; ideally a degree in industrial engineering, supply chain management, or relevant advanced training.\n- In-depth knowledge of mechanical engineering, ideally in rack steel construction, load carriers, conveyor technology, and automation components.\n- Experience in project purchasing, especially during the project planning phase.\n- Strong negotiation skills, knowledge of contract law, and supplier management.\n- SAP knowledge (order processing, invoice workflow) is an advantage.\n- Team player, structured working style, and high self-initiative.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity to join a stable, family-owned company in a critical project purchasing role. Remmert offers an unlimited employment contract, 30 days of vacation, and significant flexibility with mobile work and flexible hours. Beyond competitive compensation including profit sharing and other bonuses, the company is deeply committed to employee well-being and growth, providing extensive development opportunities, a catalog for further education, modern equipment, and a supportive culture fostered through regular employee events and comprehensive health management programs. If you are a long-term oriented individual seeking a dynamic role where your contributions directly impact cutting-edge automated logistics systems, this could be your next career move.
