### Description
We’re on a mission to **automate the busy work of running offices** and build the future of hybrid work. Backed by top-tier VCs, PULT is scaling fast.

This role is ideal if you aspire to become a founder yourself, as PULT has already produced three founders and is expanding the "PULT mafia". As our first working student **Founders Associate**, you’ll work side-by-side with our founder and growth team, taking on high-impact projects that will directly shape our commercial and operational success. Your focus will be on discovering and scaling new growth channels, driving international expansion, and automating the engine room of our business.

This is your chance to be **part operator, part entrepreneur, part growth hacker**—and to help build a category-defining company from the ground up. PULT is building the **Automated Workplace Platform** that helps companies solve the toughest challenges of hybrid work: boosting office attendance, optimizing office space, and automating repetitive tasks for workplace teams—powered by AI, automation, and deep integrations. We are one of Hamburg’s fastest-growing early-stage startups, already trusted by customers like Enpal, VEVO, Taxfix, and Deutz AG.

### Responsibilities
In this role, you will be primarily focused on Operations (70%) and Growth (30%).

**Operations (70%)**
*   Design, optimize, and automate internal processes and operations.
*   Help scale our finance setup (accounting, forecasting, business cases).
*   Drive strategic projects across different teams.
*   Support with investor updates and reporting.

**Growth (30%)**
*   Help test, validate, and scale new growth channels to expand our reach.

### Qualifications
We are looking for someone who meets the following criteria:

*   **Academic excellence**: Currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree (Master is preferred) from a top university (Business, Economics, STEM, or related).
*   **Proven track record**: 1-2 internships or jobs at consulting, Investment Banking, startups / VCs—or you’ve built a product yourself once.
*   **High ownership**: You take initiative, move fast, and thrive in ambiguity.
*   **Strong communicator**: Excellent written & verbal skills in English and German.
*   **Impact-driven**: You care about technology and want to build something meaningful.
*   **Entrepreneurial mindset**: Hungry to learn what it takes to run a startup.
*   **Based in Hamburg**: We believe being close to the action is crucial (hybrid possible).
