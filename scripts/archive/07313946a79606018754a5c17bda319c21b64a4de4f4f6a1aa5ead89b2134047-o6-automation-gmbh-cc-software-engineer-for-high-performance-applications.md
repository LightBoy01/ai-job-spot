### Description
If you enjoy diving deep into the architecture of high-performance applications using the low-level language C, this is the right challenge for you. The open62541 library, written in C just like the Linux kernel or PostgreSQL, forms the foundation of this role. We are a team that thrives on solving technical challenges, contributing to Open Source software, and delivering solutions that power industrial applications globally. This role offers the unique opportunity to make foundational contributions to a critical open-source library while growing into a technical leadership position.

### Responsibilities
- Architecture and design of high-performance software systems.
- Making foundational contributions to the open62541 software library.
- Participating in customer projects that utilize the open62541 library.
- Bringing the potential and motivation to grow into a technical leadership role.

### Qualifications
- Proven experience in system architecture and development within a UNIX/Linux environment.
- Strong programming skills in C/C++ and Python.
- Demonstrated experience in performance benchmarking and optimization.
- A team-oriented mindset with a clear willingness to develop into a technical leadership role.
- Excellent communication skills, both in German and English.
- *Optional:* Visible code contributions to open-source projects.
- *Optional:* Experience with operating systems for embedded systems and real-time requirements.
- *Optional:* Knowledge in the field of industrial automation and PLC programming.

### Author's Take
This is an exceptional opportunity for a passionate C developer who wants to make a significant impact on critical industrial applications through open-source contributions. The role is designed for individuals who not only possess deep technical prowess but also aspire to grow into a technical leadership position. The company offers a highly professional and technical culture, working with an exceptionally talented team, and provides clear pathways for career development in a fast-growing environment. If you're looking for a challenging, impactful role where your contributions are valued and you can shape the future of industrial automation, and you meet their selective hiring criteria, this is a role to seriously consider.
