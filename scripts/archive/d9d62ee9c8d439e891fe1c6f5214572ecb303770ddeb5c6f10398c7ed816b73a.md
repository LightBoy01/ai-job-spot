You'll have the opportunity to engage with clients across Europe and the MEA markets, traveling on-site to elevate their connectivity solutions. As part of our team, you'll spearhead consulting business development, driving sales and delivery for key accounts, ensuring clients seamlessly transition to cutting-edge cloud and automated operations. Enjoy comprehensive benefits including professional development, flexible working arrangements, and the chance to influence the future of technology. Bring your expertise and creativity to shape transformative solutions and thrive in this exciting role!
### Responsibilities
*   Lead strategic cloud transformation initiatives leveraging Cloud Technologies (Kubernetes, AWS, GCP, Azure) for client success.
*   Design and implement Cloud Native Core and Automation architecture, including CI/CD, Test Automation, and Orchestration solutions.
*   Apply deep knowledge of Core Networks domain (PaCo, Voice Core, IMS Core) to enhance network performance and reliability.
*   Drive innovation in OSS/BSS, Orchestration, Network Assurance, AI Ops, and 5G slicing to meet evolving customer needs.
*   Collaborate with clients and stakeholders to understand requirements and deliver tailored solutions that exceed expectations.
*   Engage with CxO and Director-level executives, fostering relationships that enhance client partnerships and business opportunities.
*   Mentor and lead multidisciplinary teams in high-pressure environments to ensure successful project delivery and excellence.
*   Travel to customer sites in Europe and MEA, actively participating in hands-on engagements to facilitate on-the-ground transformations.
### Qualifications
*   A BSc in Engineering or Computer Science is required, with an MBA preferred. Candidates should have 20+ years in Telecom/IT or 10+ years in management consulting, with a proven track record in large-scale cloud transformation and automation programs.
*   Strong financial acumen in Telco operations, business case development, and ROI analysis. Deep expertise in Cloud Technologies (Kubernetes, Cloud Native, AWS, GCP, Azure) and Cloud-Based Automation Architecture (CI/CD, Test Automation, Orchestration). Extensive knowledge of Core Networks (PaCo, Voice Core, IMS Core), OSS/BSS, 5G slicing, and AI Ops, with familiarity in TMF, ETSI, and 3GPP standards.
*   Proven experience leading cloud transformation and core network modernization. Adept at engaging CxO and Director-level executives, bridging technical and business discussions to drive strategic decisions. Skilled in digital transformation program execution, cross-functional collaboration, and high-impact stakeholder management.
*   An influential leader and mentor with exceptional communication skills. Proactive, customer-focused, and structured in problem-solving. Thrives in high-pressure environments with a global mindset and willingness to travel.
Ready to shape the future of Cloud & Automation in Telecom? Join us!
