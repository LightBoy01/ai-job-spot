Our team of approximately 60 creative minds excels in authentic storytelling and covers the entire social media marketing funnel – from strategy and content creation to performance marketing and influencer relations. We are a hub for creative thinkers, competent professionals, talented creators, and organizational talents, creating \"entertaining dialogue\" for renowned brands like Kaufland, KFC, and Mövenpick.\n\nAs an integral part of our team, you will gain exciting insights into all areas of modern People & Culture Management. We offer an environment that values independent work and provides the opportunity to implement your own projects. You can expect a workplace defined by team spirit, openness, flat hierarchies, and a commitment to learning. Appreciation, trust, and development are at the core of our collaborative work with experienced HR colleagues.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Actively participate in our **recruiting processes** and learn about **communication with applicants**.\n- Support us in the **operational day-to-day business** across all HR topics, from **posting job advertisements** to **creating contracts and references**.\n- Be a **point of contact** for our employees regarding office and HR questions.\n- Take on your **own projects in the People & Culture area**, for example, in the **planning and implementation of team events**.\n- Support the **development and implementation of modern HR instruments and processes**.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You are an **enrolled student**, ideally with a focus on **Human Resources Management** or a comparable degree program.\n- You have **3 to 6 months available** to gain practical experience.\n- Initial **experience in the HR field** is a plus.\n- You have an **affinity for social media** and an interest in modern HR marketing.\n- You work **independently, are well-structured**, and enjoy contributing your own ideas.\n- You hold a **Class B driving license**.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for an ambitious student passionate about People & Culture and modern HR marketing within a dynamic social media agency.
