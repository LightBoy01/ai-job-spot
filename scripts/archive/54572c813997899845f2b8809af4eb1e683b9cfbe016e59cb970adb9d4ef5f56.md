**Summary**

We’re looking for a strategic Talent Acquisition Consultant (CX & Healthcare) - Greater Sacramento  to lead high-impact recruiting efforts for customer experience and healthcare operations roles in the Greater Sacramento area. While this isn’t a full-cycle role, you’ll drive the recruiting strategy, sourcing, candidate engagement, and hiring manager partnership — with the support of a TA Associate to manage coordination and logistics. If you're a recruiter who can move fast _and_ think smart, we want to hear from you.

**Your role in our mission**

*   Lead sourcing, candidate engagement, and interview management for high-volume CX and healthcare roles, including medical claims and call center positions.
*   Partner closely with hiring managers to define hiring needs, provide market insights, and influence decisions.
*   Create and execute sourcing strategies using job boards, social media, referrals, and talent pools.
*   Use AI tools like Copilot and automation to streamline outreach and improve candidate quality.
*   Collaborate with a TA Associate who will support scheduling, communication, and applicant tracking.

**What we're looking for**

*   3+ years of experience recruiting in high-volume or customer-facing environments.
*   Strong sourcing ability using LinkedIn, Facebook, Indeed, and creative digital outreach.
*   Healthcare recruiting experience preferred, especially in Medical Claims or back-office operations.
*   Familiarity with AI tools, ATS platforms, and high-volume recruiting tech.
*   Excellent relationship-building, communication, and prioritization skills.

**What you should expect in this role**

*   A fast-paced, high-volume recruiting environment where you focus on quality, not just quantity.
    
*   The ability to use modern recruiting tools, including AI and social sourcing.
    
*   Support from a dedicated TA Associate to manage administrative and coordination tasks.
    
*   A collaborative TA team focused on continuous improvement and strategic hiring.
    
*   Based in the Greater Sacramento area with on-site work required as needed (hybrid schedule).
