We focus on space-saving storage and fully automated movement, operating in the future-oriented machine building, automation, and logistics industry. We are continuously growing and seeking dedicated colleagues to strengthen our team. Building and maintaining long-term supplier relationships.\\n- **Contract & Price Negotiations:** Drafting and negotiating framework agreements, price structures, and payment terms. Ensuring cost-optimized procurement while considering technical requirements.\\n- **Technical Risk & Quality Management:** Review of technical specifications, standards, and compatibility. Collaboration with quality assurance and management of deviations.\\n- **Supply Chain Tracking & Deadline Management:** Monitoring delivery dates, coordinating deliveries, and supporting assembly and installation.\\n- **Cost Efficiency & Budget Control:** Preparation of cost overviews, execution of price analyses, and participation in budget planning.\\n- **Documentation & Reporting:** Maintenance of procurement documentation and regular status reports to project management.\\n- **Process Optimization & & Digitalization:** Participation in the further development of procurement processes and the use of digital tools for supplier evaluation.\\n\\n### Qualifications\\n- Completed technical training and commercial further education with a focus on mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, or comparable qualification; ideally, a degree in industrial engineering, supply chain management, or a relevant further qualification.\\n- Sound knowledge in mechanical engineering, ideally in the areas of racking steel construction, load carriers, conveying technology, and automation components.\\n- Experience in project purchasing, especially in the project planning phase.\\n- Strong negotiation skills, knowledge of contract law, and supplier management.\\n- SAP knowledge (order processing, invoice workflow) is advantageous.\\n- Team player, structured way of working, and high degree of initiative.\\n\\n### Author's Take\\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced procurement specialist to join a stable, family-owned company in a thriving industry.
