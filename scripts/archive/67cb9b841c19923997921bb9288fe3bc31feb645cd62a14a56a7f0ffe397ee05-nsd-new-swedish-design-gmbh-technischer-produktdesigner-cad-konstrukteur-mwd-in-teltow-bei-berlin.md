### Description

**Please note: Very good German language skills (at least B2) are required for this position. We kindly ask for your understanding that we will immediately reject all applicants who do not meet this criterion.**

Are you still working, or are you already making a difference?

NSD New Swedish Design GmbH is an independent company that develops and distributes unique supplementary products and accessories for IKEA furniture. We are recognized as the number one in Germany when it comes to 'kaufbare Ikea Hacks.'

Through our shops and brands – New Swedish Design, HOKEA, Kura-Love, schwedenbaby, and Kallax Shop – we enable IKEA customers to use their existing furniture more sustainably and enhance its function and design. If you enjoy realizing products that inspire many people, we warmly invite you to become part of our great team and success story. Together with you, we aim to build a highly professional technical team near Berlin.

### Responsibilities

*   Design and construct components for furniture and living areas, creating production-ready individual part drawings using CAD.
*   Conduct feasibility analyses and research suitable suppliers in collaboration with our purchasing department.
*   Manage the creation of components and assemblies, as well as maintaining existing part data.
*   Inspect sample deliveries and conduct product tests.
*   Create product descriptions for our marketing department and assembly instructions for our customers.
*   Provide technical support to colleagues in related departments for technical questions.

### Qualifications

*   Completed technical education and/or a technical degree.
*   Several years of professional experience in one or more technical fields (e.g., wood, metal, or plastic technology).
*   Ideally, knowledge in the field of furniture and home accessories.
*   Independent, solution-oriented, meticulous, and well-structured working style.
*   Proficiency in technical drawings, CAD, and Office applications.
*   Fluent German language skills (at least B2), good English skills, and a Class B driving license.
