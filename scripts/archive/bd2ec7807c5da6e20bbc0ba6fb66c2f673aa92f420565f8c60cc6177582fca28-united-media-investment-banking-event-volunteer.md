They are seeking ambitious and talented volunteers to join a team of 25+ peers to help build, run, and deliver their prestigious French Private Equity Conference in Munich on September 26th, 2025. This is a unique opportunity to expand your network with over 500 industry leaders, develop your skills in leadership and management, and directly contribute to a major industry event.\n\nYou can gain further insight into the conference experience by watching the [2023 Milan's highlight video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1w9amNARIROInji1jERo8KzgPLCfemnQM/view?usp=drive_link). More information about Private Equity Insights and their conferences is available on their [website](https://pe-insights.com/private-equity-conferences/france/).\n\n### Responsibilities\nVolunteers will play a crucial role in the successful execution of the conference. Key responsibilities include:\n*   Assisting with the setup, logistics, and overall delivery of the French Private Equity Conference alongside a team of peers.\n*   Supporting various event operations on the day of the conference to ensure a seamless experience for attendees.\n*   Actively participating in a mandatory 2-hour training and rehearsal session the afternoon prior to the event.\n*   Contributing to a dynamic and engaging atmosphere for over 500 leaders, entrepreneurs, and investors.\n\n### Qualifications\nWhile specific professional qualifications are not explicitly listed, Private Equity Insights is looking for individuals who are:\n*   Ambitious and talented, with a strong interest in expanding their network within the private equity sector.\n*   Eager to develop soft skills, leadership capabilities, and receive individual training in management.\n*   Motivated team players who are keen to contribute to a high-profile international event.\n*   Available for the conference day (September 26th, 2025) and the preceding 2-hour training session.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis volunteer opportunity with Private Equity Insights offers a truly exceptional gateway into the world of private equity and large-scale event management.
