### Description
As our new Senior IT System Engineer (m/w/d), you will be the technical expert for our hybrid client and server landscape, combining deep technical knowledge with a passion for automation and security. You will operate in a dynamic environment with flat hierarchies and fast decision-making processes, where your expertise will directly contribute to our strategic goals. We believe in providing the latest hardware and software to empower you, alongside opportunities for significant career growth. In addition to an attractive salary and an performance-based bonus, we offer occupational pension schemes and a company mobile phone contract. You'll be joining a highly motivated team that values knowledge sharing and collaborative problem-solving.

### Responsibilities
*   **Autopilot & Provisioning Expert:** Conceive and manage zero-touch rollouts, utilizing Windows Autopilot for new client provisioning.
*   **Professional Software Packaging & Distribution:** Take responsibility for packaging (e.g., MSI, MSIX, IntuneWin) and distributing complex applications and updates within our hybrid environment (SCCM & Intune).
*   **Automated Patching Excellence:** Ensure system security through comprehensive patch management (Windows & 3rd-Party), with critical expertise in Patch My PC for automation and compliance.
*   **Linux Administration:** Administer, maintain, and harden our primarily server-based Linux environments (e.g., Debian, Ubuntu, RHEL), ensuring stable operations.
*   **Network & Security:** As a subject matter expert, analyze network and security issues in client and server environments (Firewall rules, VPN, Conditional Access, Endpoint Protection) and collaborate closely with specialist departments.
*   **3rd-Level Escalation:** Act as the final escalation point for complex incidents related to clients, servers (Windows/Linux), software distribution, and network connectivity.

### Qualifications
*   **Experience:** Approximately 10 years of professional experience in IT system administration, particularly in an enterprise environment.
*   **Endpoint Security & Hardening:** Perfect expert knowledge in Endpoint Security and hardening of server operating systems (Windows and Linux).
*   **Microsoft Technologies:** Profound knowledge in SCCM and expert proficiency in Windows Autopilot and software packaging (MSI, MSIX, IntuneWin) via Intune.
*   **Patch Management:** Expert in using Patch My PC, a core requirement for automating our patching processes.
*   **Linux Expertise:** Good and proven practical knowledge in the administration and patching of Linux servers (Debian, Ubuntu, RHEL).
*   **Network & Security:** Deep understanding of network protocols, firewalls, VPN, and Conditional Access.
*   **Mindset:** Analytical, structured work approach, and a willingness to take responsibility as the final escalation instance.
*   **Languages:** Fluent German (at least C1) and good English skills.
