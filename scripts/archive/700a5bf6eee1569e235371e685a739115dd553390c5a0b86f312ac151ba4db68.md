AVL-AST D.O.O. offers a job in Croatia (HR) as

**Senior System Engineer (f/m/d) with focus on IT Identity Management**
=======================================================================

As an expert (f/m/d) in Identity and Access Management, you will contribute your know-how both to the development of new solutions and to the optimization of existing processes in the IAM area. Your involvement in architecture topics completes your diverse range of tasks.  
Our IAM colleagues focus on collegial teamwork and a lively exchange of experience. Together, we strive for sustainable solutions for our company and place the highest value on quality. You can look forward with a highly motivated team that enjoys tackling challenging tasks!

  

**YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES:**

•    Planning, design and implementation of connections between identity and access management systems (IAM system) and business applications  
•    Definition and further development of IAM processes  
•    Collaboration in different projects regarding the integration of IAM processes  
•    3rd level support for IAM processes and applications  
•    Independent control of suppliers  
 

**YOUR PROFILE:**

•    Completed technical education (LDAP – Final Apprenticeship Examination, Secondary Technical College, University of Applied Sciences) in the field of computer science  
•    Very good knowledge of at least one established IAM tool (Microsoft Identity Manager, OneIdentity or comparable)  
•    At least 5 years of experience in Identity Management or IT Security  
•    Experience in the environment of directory services on premise and in the cloud (e.g. Active Directory, LDAP, Azure AD, AWS IAM)  
•    Experience with federation protocols and access management (SAML, OAuth, Kerberos) advantageous   
•    Scripting and/or programming experience (Powershell, .NET)  
•    Analytical thinking skills, independent and responsible way of working  
•    Communicative and team-oriented personality  
•    Willingness for independent further training  
•    Very good German and good English skills
