**Job Description:**
--------------------
**Job Title:** **Full-stack Developer**
**Location:** **Pune, India**
**Corporate Title: AVP**
**Role Description**
*   The User Experience team delivers end user applications and digital services to Deutsche Bank staff. The team is focused on delivering a modern web & mobile experience specifically built around users’ personas, presenting all apps & services in a clean, consistent, and coherent package. This ensures an optimum client experience on our digital estate. Our technology vision is moving towards secured digitization & automation. To support this an artificial intelligent conversational Virtual Assistance has been introduced to the Bank focusing on IT Support.
*   The UX Engineering team is seeking candidates with strong full-stack development experiences particularly with Javascript/Typescript (Angular) on the front-end. The candidate must have keen interest in AI technologies and be willing to work across multiple platforms and technologies including but not limited to our Angular-based support portal, Kore.AI based chatbot and Microsoft Power Platform. Experience of chatbot development, generative AI prompt engineering, Microsoft Copilot, Power Automate and AI Builder is extremely beneficial.

**Your key responsibilities**
*   Work as part of an Agile software development team to deliver valuable business requirements across the product portfolio which includes the bank’s support portal and AI-supported chatbot.
*   Actively participate in the design and architecture of the applications or components, investigating and proposing appropriate technologies.
*   Develop conversational workflows within the existing chatbot solution.
*   Provide L3 support and troubleshooting for production problems.
*   Follow SDLC guidelines including coding standards and compliance with security controls.
*   The successful candidate will have a clear focus on providing accurate and robust solutions whether working independently or as part of a team whilst delivering in a high-pressure environment.  It is important for the candidate to have enthusiasm, commitment and a positive attitude as well as being a strong team player.  It is also essential for the candidate to have good interpersonal skills and the ability to discuss, analyse and accurately document technical solutions and analyse issues.
