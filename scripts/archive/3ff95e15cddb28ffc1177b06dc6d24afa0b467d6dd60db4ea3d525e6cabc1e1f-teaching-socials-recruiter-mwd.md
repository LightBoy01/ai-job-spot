### Description

**About Teaching Socials**

Teaching Socials is one of the fastest-growing consulting firms in the DACH region and the leading consultancy for personal brands in the finance & real estate sector. We build reach, authority, and revenue – and for that, we need A-players on our team.

**Your Role / Impact**

You will take full ownership of the **entire recruiting department** at Teaching Socials, from outbound sourcing to signed employment contracts. This is not about HR administration or "feel-good" activities – it's **pure Talent Acquisition** with clear KPIs and a direct, measurable impact on our growth.

### Responsibilities

As our Recruiter, you will drive our talent acquisition efforts with precision and proactivity. Your daily tasks and core areas of ownership include:

*   **Active Sourcing & Talent Scouting:** Proactively identifying and engaging top talent via LinkedIn Recruiter, professional networks, communities, and industry events.
*   **Pipeline & Initial Contact:** Conducting initial phone screenings, qualification calls, and fit-checks against defined scorecards to assess candidate suitability.
*   **Job Profiles & JD-Writing:** Collaborating closely with Hiring Managers to define precise and measurable requirement profiles, and crafting compelling job advertisements.
*   **Funnel Management:** Overseeing the entire recruitment funnel, including screening applications, creating shortlists, coordinating and conducting interviews, managing case tasks, and conducting reference checks.
*   **Stakeholder Management:** Maintaining close alignment with Hiring Managers, providing clear communication on next steps, and ensuring a seamless process.
*   **Distribution:** Posting job openings on relevant job boards and targeted channels, while continuously building and nurturing talent pools.
*   **Reporting:** Generating weekly reports on key metrics such as sourcing activity, interviews conducted, offers extended, Time-to-Fill (TTF), and offer acceptance rates.
*   **Process & Tools:** Maintaining and optimizing our ATS/CRM (Recruitee), and proactively developing and refining recruitment templates and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
*   **Onboarding Handover:** Ensuring a smooth transition of new hires to the Operations/Team Lead, and integrating feedback into recruiting SOPs for continuous improvement.

### Qualifications

We are looking for a highly driven and experienced Recruiter who thrives in a fast-paced, high-growth environment.

**Must-Haves:**

*   2–4+ years of proven full-cycle recruiting experience, preferably within an agency, consulting firm, or high-growth company.
*   Demonstrable expertise in **outbound sourcing** and a track record of successfully filling challenging roles.
*   Proficiency in various **interview methodologies & selection processes** (e.g., Structured Interviews, Scorecards).
*   Familiarity with **ATS & talent databases** (e.g., Recruitee) and excellent research skills.
*   **German language skills C1+** (clear written & verbal communication); English proficiency is advantageous.
*   A strong **data-driven mindset** to manage and optimize recruitment processes based on KPIs.

**What We Don't Look For (and what implies you should be):**

*   Individuals who are primarily HR Generalists without strong active sourcing capabilities.
*   A "corporate" mindset characterized by slow processes and a "post & pray" approach to recruiting.
*   Candidates who merely seek "a job" rather than a role with **visible and measurable hiring impact**. We seek individuals who are ambitious, proactive, and committed to making a tangible difference.
