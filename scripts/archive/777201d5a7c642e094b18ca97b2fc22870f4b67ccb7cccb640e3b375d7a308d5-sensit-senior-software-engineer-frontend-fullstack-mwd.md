**About Us**  
SensitHaptics develops leading haptics technology for immersive gaming experiences – especially in SimRacing. Our solutions are considered the benchmark for realism, precision, and latency. We develop and manufacture in Germany and work with OEMs, system builders, and high-end gamers worldwide.  
If you want to build software for a product that gamers can _physically feel_, this is the place for you.

**Your Role**  
You will build the software tools, apps, and integrations that control, configure, and bring our haptics platform to life. You work independently, reliably, and in a structured manner – no micromanagement. You design features end-to-end, collaborate closely with Product, Firmware, Hardware, and UX, and deliver robust, high-performance software.

Tasks
-----

• Develop modern frontend or fullstack software (web apps, desktop tools, control panels)  
• Implement new features in our Sensit Haptics Engine (UI, middleware, backend)  
• Collaborate with Product, Hardware, and UX to define technical requirements  
• Optimize performance (latency, stability, user experience)  
• Develop and maintain APIs, SDKs, and Unity/Unreal integrations for partners  
• Build automated tests, CI/CD pipelines, and developer tooling  
• Provide clean technical documentation + consistent code reviews

Requirements
------------

• Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent experience  
• Strong proficiency in TypeScript, C++, C#, Python, or Rust  
• Experience with modern frontend frameworks (React, Next.js, Electron) and/or backend (Node.js, APIs)  
• Experience with game engine integrations is a plus  
• Familiar with Git, CI/CD, and modern development workflows  
• Highly reliable, clear communicator, self-driven working style  
• Senior-level developer mindset: ownership, prioritization, self-organization  
• Experience with agile methods/Scrum is a plus  
• Excellent German and English skills  
• Passion for gaming, haptics, UI/UX, or high-performance systems

Benefits
--------

• Work on technology that physically transforms the gaming experience  
• Unique blend of hardware + software + tools – rare opportunity to learn and shape products  
• Direct impact, no politics, fast decision-making  
• Opportunity to independently own entire product areas  
• State-of-the-art development environment + access to high-end SimRacing setups  
• Hybrid work possible: mix of Berlin/Potsdam office and remote work by arrangement

Join a team that doesn’t just develop software — but experiences you can _feel_.
