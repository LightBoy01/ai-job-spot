### **Role Summary**
Are you a recent PhD graduate or Postdoctoral researcher passionate about applying cutting-edge AI to solve real-world problems? Join Sonder as an Applied Machine Learning Scientist/Engineer — focusing on LLM Research & Fine-Tuning Engineering, and help us build a new generation of AI systems that directly impact human well-being and safety.
This is a unique opportunity for a PhD-level candidate to apply their deep academic knowledge to a mission-driven environment. This role is distinct from typical AI tooling or RAG-based roles.
We are **not** seeking candidates whose primary experience is in building vector search systems or integrating off-the-shelf APIs. Instead, we require deep, hands-on expertise with the internal workings of large language models. Your work will be instrumental in developing our personalisation engine and fine-tuning state-of-the-art open-source LLMs like LLaMa, Mistral, and DeepSeek. Your research will translate directly into creating specialised LLMs that provide empathetic, life-saving support to our members.
We're looking for someone who thrives on technical challenges and wants to see their expertise translate into a positive, tangible impact.
### **Key Responsibilities**
*   Research, fine-tune, and optimise open-source LLMs using techniques such as PEFT, LoRA, and QLoRA to build our proprietary AI models.
*   Contribute to the design and implementation of our personalisation and recommendation engines to deliver tailored, proactive support.
*   Develop and maintain robust machine learning pipelines for model training, evaluation, and deployment.
*   Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams, including product managers, software engineers, and mental health professionals, to translate research into effective product features.
*   Stay current with the latest advancements in AI and LLM research, and apply new techniques to improve our systems.
*   Publish and/or present research findings at conferences or in academic journals where appropriate, contributing to the broader AI community.
### **Key Qualifications**
*   A recently completed **PhD** or **Postdoctoral experience** in Machine Learning, AI, Computer Science, or a closely related quantitative field.
*   Deep theoretical and practical understanding of Transformer architectures, attention mechanisms, and training dynamics.
*   Hands-on experience fine-tuning open-source LLMs (e.g., LLaMa, Mistral, DeepSeek).
*   Strong computer science fundamentals and coding proficiency in Python.
*   Solid experience with deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
*   A profound passion for applying AI to high-impact, meaningful domains such as mental health and well-being.
**_Candidates with primary experience limited to building Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems or MLOps tooling are not a suitable fit for this specific role._**
### **Nice-to-Have**
