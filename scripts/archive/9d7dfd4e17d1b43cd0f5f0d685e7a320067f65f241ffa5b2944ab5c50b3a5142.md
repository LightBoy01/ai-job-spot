Head (m/f/d) of Global Controlling, Reporting & Projects

Permanent 

**About Heraeus**

Making displays foldable? Helping hearts beat with medical innovations? Or breathing new life into precious metals? As a family-owned global technology group we help our customers to always being one step ahead.

  
**About Heraeus Precious Metals**

With more than 350 years of experience in precious metals processing, Heraeus Precious Metals is the world's leading provider of innovative products and services, such as precious metals trading and recycling. Our products are used in a wide range of industries, for example to reduce climate killers such as nitrous oxide and methane and in the form of pharmaceutical ingredients for the treatment of cancer.

Our innovative strength is driven by our 3,000 employees at more than 15 sites worldwide, who work passionately with our partners to develop pioneering and sustainable solutions.

Our vision: We create innovative precious metals products and services, leading the way to a sustainable future. To achieve this, we have set ourselves ambitious targets.

**The following challenges await you:** 

*   Lead the Global controlling team, overseeing global coordination of budget planning, month-end closing, and forecasting activities across HPM.  
    

*   Build and develop a high-performing team culture, while also representing the HPM CFO in key meetings as required.  
    

*   Responsibility for ESG Reporting for HPM 
    

*   Serve as a strategic advisor and sparring partner to business line leadership and site management, providing financial insights and guidance  
    

*   Act as the primary liaison with the corporate finance departments of the holding company.  
    

*    Own and manage the Business Review Meeting process, ensuring effective preparation, execution, and follow-up.  
    

*   Drive cross-functional initiatives focused on enhancing systems, tools, and processes, with a strong emphasis on digitalization and automation within global finance operations.  
    

*   Continuously improve KPI reporting in collaboration with business line managers and oversee global precious metals controlling and carbon footprint reporting.  
    

*    Lead the global implementation of preventive audit measures, manage global sales controlling including transfer pricing and licensing topics, and ensure tax compliance in close coordination with the tax department.  
    

*   Analyze major international projects, contracts, and joint ventures, and support the evaluation of M&A transactions in partnership with the M&A team. 
    

**Your profile:** 

*   Strong academic foundation with a strategic mindset and solid background in controlling and/or consulting 
    

*   Skilled in designing and enhancing financial systems and tools 
    

*   Clear communicator with the ability to engage senior stakeholders and convey complex issues to various target groups 
    

*   Leadership experience or strong potential to lead 
    

*   Effective collaborator across functions and hierarchies 
    

*   Excellent PowerPoint Skills, MS Office tools in general and IT Savvy 
    

**What you can look forward to at Heraeus Precious Metals:** 

*   Exciting tasks - implement them with a high level of personal responsibility in an ambitious environment with very good opportunities for further development. 
    

*   Shaping sustainability - be part of realizing our vision. 
    

*   Inclusive corporate culture - diversity makes our organization more successful. We promote this, among other things with specific targets, part-time/job-sharing management positions, and training courses. 
    

*   Attractive compensation - in addition to a good pay package, you can expect extensive social benefits from a responsible family-owned company. 
    

*   And much more - mobile working, 30 vacation days or more, travel allowance for public transport, bicycle leasing, ...
