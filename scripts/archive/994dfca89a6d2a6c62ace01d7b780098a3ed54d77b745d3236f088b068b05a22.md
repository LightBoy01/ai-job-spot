Would you like to become part of the most international company in the world? Would you like to make a difference? Then join our team.

European Air Transport Leipzig GmbH (EAT) officially started operations at Leipzig/Halle Airport in spring 2008 as an aircraft maintenance company and registered cargo airline. It is a wholly-owned subsidiary of DHL Group and belongs to the DHL Express division. In cooperation with DHL Air Austria and DHL Air UK Ltd., EAT covers the majority of the European DHL network.

For more information, please visit:  [European Air Transport Leipzig - Hub Leipzig - Deutschland (dhl.com)](https://www.dhl.com/de-de/microsites/express/hubs/hub-leipzig/discover/flugbetrieb.html)

YOUR TASKS
==========

The Maintenance Production Manager LEJ reports directly to the Senior Manager Maintenance and oversees the Line Maintenance crews in Leipzig. He represents the Senior Manager Maintenance.

*   Organizes and coordinates the Line Maintenance activities in LEJ within existing guidelines, procedures, and objectives.
    
*   Manages the maintenance resources and acts as a supervisor for the crew chiefs.
    
*   Responsible for overseeing and operating the hangar with regard to safety and cleanliness.
    
*   Develops and optimizes the maintenance department with a focus on efficient production, workplace organization, and methods.
    
*   Ensures efficient resource management that meets legal and productive requirements.
    
*   Coordinates between the various technical departments.
    
*   Maintains high quality standards in accordance with all applicable regulations and promotes the EAT safety culture.
    
*   Motivates maintenance employees and drives active continuous improvements.
    
*   Supports the development of maintenance strategies and innovative concepts to meet the latest industry standards and process improvements to increase productivity.
    
*   Monitors department-specific key performance indicators, analyzes the causes of deviations, and implements corrective actions.
    
*   Engages in cross-departmental project work.
    
*   Assists the Maintenance Manager in all his tasks and represents him during his absence.
