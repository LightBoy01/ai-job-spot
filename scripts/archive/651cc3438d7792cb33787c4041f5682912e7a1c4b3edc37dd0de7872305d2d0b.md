**Senior Data AI Architect**

Join the Asia Data Office as a Senior Data AI Architect to support the development of innovative data and AI solutions and contribute to fostering a data-driven culture across our regional partners. This role is ideal for individuals passionate about data, AI, innovation, with full-stack AI/ML skills, and understanding the emerging technologies, particularly in areas such as Generative / Agentic AI, MLOps / LLMOps, and various data platforms including Azure Databricks, AI Foundry, Kubernetes Service, and Alibaba Cloud.

**Position Responsibilities:**

*   **AI Applications:**
    
    *   Responsible for the architecture design for data and AI in Aisa Data Office, and collaborate with other stakeholders to ensure the standards are followed and fit for purpose design applied.
        
    *   Participate and drive the architectural discussions on data provisions for AI applications to enhance data processing and analytics capabilities.
        
    *   Create POC solutions independently with emerging AI technologies such as RAG, Agentic AI, MCP, low-code development, etc., and propose the best course of action to adopt new architectural patterns.
        
    *   Design and enhance reusable frameworks to maximize repeatability of solutions and reduce cost & overall time-to-market.
        
    *   Drive and improve CICD / LLMOps / MLOps practices to streamline and automate build, deploy, model management processes using tools such as GitHub Actions, Jenkins, etc.
        
    *   Explore AI capabilities in both Microsoft Azure and AliCloud, and align architectural design and internal processes with multiple cloud providers.
        

*   **Architectural Support and Implementation:**
    
    *   Assist in the design and implementation of scalable, secure data and AI related architectures.
        
    *   Support the resolution of architectural issues and contribute to robust solutions using industry best practices.
        
    *   Facilitate data flow throughout the organization under the guidance of senior architects.
        

*   **Data Management and Integration:**
    
    *   Help develop and maintain component data architectures.
        
    *   Assist in creating templates and complex solution components for software and platform engineers.
        

*   **Application Architecture:**
    
    *   Support the design of application architectures and troubleshoot issues with component architectures.
        

*   **Team Collaboration:**
    
    *   Work with teams towards common objectives, providing support in data architecture and security.
        

*   **Innovation and Learning:**
    
    *   Explore emerging technologies and assess their potential impact.
        
    *   Engage in continuous learning from both successes and failures.
        

*   **Documentation and Reporting:**
    
    *   Document data architecture designs, security policies, and procedures.
        
    *   Report on data solutions and security effectiveness to senior management.
        

**Required Qualifications:**

*   Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Information Technology, or related fields.
    
*   7 years of experience in data platform, Architecture, solution design, engineering, or database administration.
    
*   Proficient in data platform technologies and security practices.
    
*   Ability to align software/platform/infrastructure architecture with business strategies.
    
*   Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
    
*   Able to work independently and manage partnerships and stakeholders
    
*   Insight into the insurance and/or financial sectors is a plus.
    
*   Ability to exercise judgment with limited supervision to achieve tasks.
    
*   Capable of leading and mentoring other engineers & team members to develop skills and best practices.
    
*   Relevant certification such as Azure Solution Architect Expert and/or AI related specialization is beneficial.
