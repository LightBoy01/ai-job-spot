### Description
Are you ready to break free from the conventional 9-5 and embrace a dynamic, flexible work environment? This long-term, part-time project offers you the opportunity to set your own schedule while contributing to the evolution of digital media content. As a Media Search Analyst, you will utilize an innovative web-based evaluation tool to perform comprehensive assessments across diverse media categories. This role is ideal for individuals passionate about digital content and eager to play a key role in revolutionizing the AI ecosystem from the comfort of their home.

### Responsibilities
As a Media Search Analyst, your core responsibilities will include:
*   Conducting comprehensive assessments of various task categories, including music, video, books, podcasts, and home pod evaluations across multiple media domains.
*   Analyzing and evaluating search outcomes for App Store content to ensure accuracy and relevance.
*   Performing research using online tools to assess and validate query accuracy and user intent.
*   Applying market expertise in conjunction with provided guidelines to evaluate the relevance and purpose of task-related information specific to the German market.

### Qualifications
To succeed in this role, you will need to meet the following requirements and successfully pass our standard assessment process:

**Basic Requirements:**
*   **Language Proficiency:** Full professional proficiency in both English and German.
*   **Residency:** Must have been a resident in Germany consecutively for the last year.
*   **Cultural Familiarity:** Strong familiarity with current and historical business, media, sport, news, social media, and cultural affairs in Germany.
*   **Apple Ecosystem Knowledge:** Possession of an email address associated with an Apple ID, along with experience and know-how of Apple products.
*   **Digital Savvy:** Up-to-date familiarity with prevailing trends in applications, music & video, coupled with a keen interest in internet research.
*   **Flexibility:** Ability to work across a diverse set of task types, including news, audio tasks, and relevance evaluations.
*   **Technical Setup:** Daily access to a secure broadband internet connection, a computer, and relevant software.
*   **Work Ethic:** Demonstrated independence, adaptability to innovative methodologies, great attention to detail, and exceptional communication skills.
*   **Education:** While a High School degree or higher certification is preferred, we welcome candidates with exceptional skills and talent.

**Assessment Process:**
Upon application, you will undergo an open-book qualification exam designed to determine your suitability for the position. Our team will provide comprehensive guidelines and learning materials to prepare you for this exam, which you can complete at your convenience within a specified timeframe. Successful candidates will also complete an ID verification. Additional incentives are provided throughout the assessment process.
