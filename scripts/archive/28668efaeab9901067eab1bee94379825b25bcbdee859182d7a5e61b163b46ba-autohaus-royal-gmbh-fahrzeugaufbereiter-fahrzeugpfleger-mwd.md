### Description
Join one of Europe's leading providers in the premium used vehicle market! For over 20 years, our automotive dealership has set standards for quality, modern service, and exclusive vehicles. As an innovative, digitally-oriented company, we are passionately shaping the future of mobility with precision and strong team spirit. We are looking for a dedicated Vehicle Detailer / Cleaning Staff (m/f/d) to join our expanding team in 14974 Ludwigsfelde, starting as soon as possible. This role is crucial in maintaining the immaculate appearance of our high-quality fleet, embodying our commitment to perfection and customer satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
As a Vehicle Detailer, your core responsibilities will include:
*   Being responsible for the meticulous care and cleaning of vehicles, both interior and exterior, ensuring the optical quality control of our premium fleet.
*   Performing professional polishing work, applying high-quality waxes and sealants to achieve a flawless finish.
*   Assisting with various other tasks related to vehicle preparation and detailing as required.

### Qualifications
To succeed in this role, we are looking for candidates with:
*   Experience in vehicle detailing is beneficial, but not a strict requirement; comprehensive on-site training will be provided.
*   A reliable, meticulous, and physically capable approach to work.
*   Possession of a valid Class B driver's license.
*   Good command of the German language.
