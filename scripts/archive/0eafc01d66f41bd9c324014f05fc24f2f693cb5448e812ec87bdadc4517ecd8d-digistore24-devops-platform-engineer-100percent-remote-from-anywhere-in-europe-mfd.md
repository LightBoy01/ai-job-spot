### Description
Digistore24 is one of Europe's fastest-growing tech companies, driven by a powerful mission: to empower individuals by providing software and expertise that enables them to share their knowledge online and build their own businesses. This commitment ensures millions gain access to valuable information, helping them achieve their goals. To sustain this rapid growth, Digistore24 is dedicated to expanding its teams with experts and strong personalities who embody their values, irrespective of their location.

They are currently seeking an experienced Developer or DevOps Engineer to join their dynamic DevOps & SRE team. This is a fully remote position, available exclusively for candidates based in Europe, offering the freedom to work from your home office while contributing to an internationally successful software and education company.

**Important Note:** Proficiency in both English and German is a mandatory requirement for this role.

### Responsibilities
As a vital member of the DevOps & SRE team, your responsibilities will include:

*   **Automation and Infrastructure as Code (IaC):** Automating repetitive tasks, deployments, and system management through scripting, CI/CD pipelines, and infrastructure provisioning to enhance efficiency and reduce human error.
*   **Collaboration with Development and SRE Teams:** Working closely with development and Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) teams to align platform capabilities with application requirements, providing tools and frameworks that promote best practices in CI/CD, testing, and deployment.
*   **Reliability and Performance Optimization:** Continuously improving system uptime and optimizing performance by identifying bottlenecks and refining system architecture.
*   **Capacity Planning and Scaling:** Assessing and predicting system resource requirements (CPU, memory, storage) and implementing auto-scaling solutions to ensure the infrastructure can scale seamlessly with increasing demand, maintaining performance under various conditions.
*   **System Monitoring and Incident Response:** Continuously monitoring system performance, uptime, and reliability using tools like Prometheus, Grafana, or ElasticSearch. This includes detecting and responding to issues proactively, managing incidents, outages, and failures quickly to minimize downtime, and overseeing incident documentation, communication, and post-incident analysis.

### Qualifications
To thrive in this role, you should possess the following:

**Essential Skills & Experience:**
*   Proven professional experience in IT operations, with a minimum of 3 years.
*   Excellent communication skills and grammar in both German and English, with the ability to seamlessly switch between the two languages.
*   Strong experience with Cloud Services (Google Cloud Platform is preferred, AWS experience is also acceptable).
*   Very good experience with Terraform / Terragrunt for infrastructure management.
*   Very good experience with Kubernetes / Container Technology.

**Key Competencies (Your Superpowers):**
*   **Communication Mastery:** The ability to communicate precisely, sensitively, and in a solution-oriented manner with stakeholders, developers, and your team, even under pressure.
*   **Collaboration Wizardry:** Skilled at bringing developers, stakeholders, and operations onto the same page, understanding diverse team challenges, and finding solutions that benefit the entire company.
*   **Automation Sorcery:** A strong advocate for automation, with the ability to implement tools that improve productivity and reduce errors across the team.
*   **Problem-Solving Genius:** Capable of deep-diving into complex problems, identifying root causes, and devising solutions that prevent future incidents.
*   **Self-organization:** Thrives on autonomy, excelling at organizing and structuring complex projects independently, especially in a remote work environment, and taking full ownership.
*   **Values Alignment:** A strong identification with Digistore24's core values.
*   **Proactive Ownership:** The ability to take ownership of tasks and projects, making decisions autonomously without needing to discuss every detail with supervisors or colleagues.
*   **Task Management:** Strong skills in planning and prioritizing your tasks effectively.
*   **Solution-Oriented:** A genuine enthusiasm for finding solutions to complex problems.
