### Description

Together with our partner, a leading global provider of garden and irrigation solutions, we are looking for a **Senior IT Project Lead (m/f/d)** in **Ulm** or **remote**. In this pivotal role, you will actively shape the modernization of warehouse and logistics processes, leading the **migration to the state-of-the-art SAP EWM warehouse management system**. This project will sustainably improve the efficiency and future viability of the entire supply chain.

### Responsibilities

*   Lead the project for migrating from an existing, highly customized system to a modern, SAP-based warehouse management system (SAP EWM).
*   Take responsibility for planning, budget control, schedule and quality management, and reporting to Group IT.
*   Build and functionally lead a hybrid project team comprising internal specialists and external partners, ensuring efficient collaboration.
*   Manage external service providers, coordinate their services, and guarantee compliance with agreements.
*   Work closely with the Logistics and Supply Chain departments, ensuring that business requirements are translated into technical implementation.
*   Regularly coordinate with global stakeholders within Group IT and with related programs to ensure transparency and consistency across all levels.

### Qualifications

*   Extensive experience in leading complex software implementation projects in the ERP sector (ideally SAP).
*   Profound understanding of logistical business processes, particularly in warehouse management and supply chain.
*   Ability to build, functionally lead, and motivate a project team.
*   Fluent in German (native speaker level) and English (at least B2 level), and able to quickly integrate into a complex, global corporate environment.

### Author's Take

This is an outstanding opportunity for a seasoned IT Project Lead to drive a truly impactful transformation. You'll be at the forefront of modernizing global logistics, migrating to SAP EWM – a project with strategic importance for the company's future efficiency and competitiveness. The role offers significant autonomy to build and lead a hybrid team, backed by direct collaboration with the CIO, ensuring a streamlined and efficient process.

The company is highly committed to employee well-being and flexibility, offering a 38-hour week, 30 days of vacation, and the option for up to 100% remote work. Beyond work-life balance, they provide extensive career development opportunities, international career paths, and a comprehensive benefits package covering health, mobility, family support (including an on-site kindergarten), and future security through employer-funded pensions.

If you're ready to take on a critical leadership role in a global organization that values innovation, collaboration, and employee growth, this position in Ulm (or fully remote) offers an exceptional environment to make a lasting impact.
