### Description

**Launch your sales career – 100% commission-based & without income limits!**

Do you have the courage to work independently, shape your own success, and manage your time freely? Then you've come to the right place! SumUp supports small businesses with smart and user-friendly financial solutions – from card payments to POS systems. Our goal is to empower entrepreneurs so they can fully concentrate on their business.

As a **freelance Field Sales Consultant**, you are your own boss and have the opportunity to earn attractive, unlimited commissions! You will build relationships with local businesses and show them how SumUp simplifies their operations. Your main task is the direct sale of our solutions – whether mobile card terminals or comprehensive payment systems, you'll have everything you need to succeed.

### Responsibilities

*   Active new customer acquisition – identify and win local businesses for SumUp.
*   Personal meetings and consulting – present our products and demonstrate their benefits.
*   Build customer relationships – secure long-term partnerships through excellent service.
*   Own customer and lead management – you decide how you build your business.

### Qualifications

*   Passion for sales and self-motivation.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer service is advantageous.
*   Strong communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Very good German language skills.
