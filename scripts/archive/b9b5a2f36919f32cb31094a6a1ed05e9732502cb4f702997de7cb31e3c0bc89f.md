**Data Analyst & Database Domain Expert - AI/ML Focus**

**Company Overview**

Role Overview
=============

We are currently looking for **Hands-on Data Analyst** to transform Foglight monitoring data into actionable insights using AI/ML capabilities, while serving as database domain expert across enterprise platforms.

Responsibilities:

*   Analyze Foglight data to identify trends, anomalies, and performance bottlenecks
*   Design dashboards, reports, and alerts based on Foglight insights
*   Design, prototype, and validate AI/ML models for monitoring solutions
*   Collaborate with PM, QA, and engineering teams on accuracy and reliability
*   Serve as database expert for Oracle, DB2, Databricks, and emerging platforms
*   Provide data-driven recommendations for technical and business decisions
*   Research latest AI/ML advancements and integrate into product features

Qualifications:

Technical Expertise
-------------------

*   Strong experience with Foglight or similar monitoring platforms
*   In-depth knowledge of Oracle, DB2, Databricks, and enterprise databases
*   Proficiency in SQL and database performance tuning
*   Strong ML algorithms, deep learning, and NLP knowledge
*   Proficiency in Python, R, or Java with AI/ML libraries (Prophet)
*   Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) and MLOps pipelines

Core Skills
-----------

*   Solid analytical skills with ability to present complex data clearly
*   Good communication and cross-team collaboration skills
*   Complex dataset analysis and problem-solving
*   Communicating AI concepts to non-technical stakeholders
*   Building dashboards and reports (Power BI, Tableau)

Preferred Qualifications
========================

*   Experience with RDBMS and modern platforms (Snowflake)
*   Database certifications (Oracle OCP, IBM DB2)
*   Cloud data architectures knowledge
*   BS/BA in Computer Science, Information Systems or related field

Additional Requirements
=======================

*   Excellent English (written and spoken)
*   Team collaboration and independent work capability
*   Flexibility for worldwide customer support
*   Quick learning ability for new database systems
