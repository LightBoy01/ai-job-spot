### Description\nFor our esteemed client, a leading full-service provider specializing in SAP S/4HANA, SAP ERP, SAP Payroll, Core HR, SuccessFactors, and SAP ALM, we are actively seeking an experienced Senior SAP Logistics Consultant (m/f/d). This is a permanent position, available for full-time or part-time engagement.\n\nJoin a dynamic team at the forefront of SAP innovation, where you will play a pivotal role in impactful transformation projects, contributing to both client success and the growth of our cutting-edge solutions.\n\n### Responsibilities\n* Lead and execute exciting transformation projects within the SAP Logistics domain.\n* Facilitate and guide workshops for the analysis, conception, implementation, and harmonization of business processes as part of S/4HANA migration, system consolidation, and CarveOut projects.\n* Optionally assume (partial) project management responsibilities and actively support pre-sales activities for new client engagements.\n* Proactively contribute to the strategic development and expansion of our transformation consulting team.\n\n### Qualifications\n* Possess several years of extensive project experience as an SAP Application Consultant, SAP Inhouse Consultant, or SAP Developer, complemented by strong business acumen and in-depth expertise in Logistics.\n* Demonstrate proven customizing experience in at least one module within the SAP Logistics application suite.\n* Prior experience in SAP S/4HANA transformation or implementation projects is highly desirable.\n* Exhibit excellent communication skills in both German and English, coupled with strong problem-solving capabilities and a pronounced customer-oriented approach.\n* Be willing to travel within the DACH region (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) as required for project delivery.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role presents an exceptional opportunity for a Senior SAP Logistics Consultant seeking to drive significant impact in complex, innovative projects. The company distinguishes itself through a profound commitment to employee growth, underpinned by personalized development plans and fully sponsored SAP certifications at the prestigious SAP Academy Walldorf. Operating under a 'challenge and support' (`Fördern und Fordern`) philosophy, you'll thrive in an environment that champions independent, self-reliant work, empowering you to quickly assume responsibility and directly influence organizational development. Beyond professional enrichment, the role emphasizes a healthy work-life balance through flexible working hours and locations, complemented by attractive benefits such as a company car, subsidized lunch, and a job bike. This position is ideal for a skilled professional eager for a long-term, challenging, and rewarding career path within a supportive and forward-thinking consultancy.
