### Description\nJoin PEM Motion and help us realize mobility dreams! We are a dynamic and innovative team that approaches every challenge with courage, a spirit of discovery, and a fresh perspective, always striving to create something exceptional. We embrace a healthy dose of nonconformity, staying grounded in reality while pushing the boundaries of what's possible.\n\nWe are passionate about the future of mobility and committed to implementing groundbreaking concepts. In this role, you'll experience a varied, challenging, and exciting work environment, surrounded by a cohesive team that values and builds upon the strengths and ideas of each individual.\n\nAs a Test Management Specialist, you will be instrumental in ensuring the reliability and performance of critical Battery Management Systems (BMS) components, directly contributing to the development of innovative mobility solutions.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Take responsibility for test management in accordance with ISO26262:2018.\n*   Plan, specify, and execute tests for BMS system, hardware, and software components.\n*   Set up, commission, and maintain HIL (Hardware-in-the-Loop) test benches for BMS function validation.\n*   Create and automate tests using MATLAB/Simulink.\n*   Develop and maintain test plans, test cases, and comprehensive test reports.\n*   Analyze test results in close collaboration with the development team and quality management.\n*   Provide support for reviews, audits, and product releases.\n\n### Qualifications\nTo succeed in this role, you should possess:\n\n*   A completed degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Mathematics, or a comparable technical field.\n*   Sound knowledge of process flows within requirements and test management.\n*   Proven practical experience in the development, execution, automation, and documentation of tests for Battery Management Systems.\n*   Experience in setting up and operating HIL test benches.\n*   Proficiency in using MATLAB/Simulink.\n*   Good command of both English and German languages.\n*   A self-description as customer-oriented, team-capable, responsible, and a strong communicator.\n\nYou will thrive at PEM Motion if you:\n*   Are passionate about the future of mobility.\n*   Enjoy turning ambitious and visionary ideas into reality.\n*   Believe that professionalism and and nonconformism can coexist.\n*   Are comfortable stepping outside your comfort zone
