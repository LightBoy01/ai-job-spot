**What you’ll be doing**
------------------------
*   Gather and prioritize key questions from product, design, engineering and product marketing teams.
*   Plan and conduct practical, impactful research throughout the entire product cycle from inception to completion, to answer the questions posed above and driving impact.
*   Gather insights about customers from a variety of research methods, including in-depth interviews, usability studies, diary studies and surveys etc.
*   Analyze qualitative and quantitative results and convey relevant insights to the appropriate stakeholders in the most engaging and actionable manner.
*   Work closely with product teams to help them use research insights to make Groww better.
*   Actively seek opportunities for expanding capacity or optimising workflow with AI product and tools.
*   Work closely with analytics and data-science to tell a cohesive story about our product.
*   Act as an internal evangelist for customer and product research.
**What you should have**
------------------------
*   2-4 years of industry experience in driving UX research at product teams.
*   Experience with qualitative and quantitative data collection and analysis for generative and evaluative research.
*   Experience managing projects involving cross-functional teams
*   Excellent research execution skills including scoping, moderation, analysis and synthesis.
*   Strong collaboration and communication skills
*   Strong story-telling skills and an ability to frame insights into compelling narratives
*   Familiarity with developing or using product / marketing frameworks to align cross-functional teams.
*   Experience of working independently with minimal supervision.
*   Experimenting with various tools, AI to unlock new use-cases in workflow.
*   Willingness to roll up your sleeves and perform a wide variety of research-related tasks, including recruiting participants, liasioning with agencies, partnering with various teams to collaborate on projects.
