### Description\nAre you passionate about leading teams and delivering first-class IT solutions for clients? As a leader with strong communication skills and a vision for a high-performance team, you will actively shape the future of our Atlassian and Cloud projects as a Consulting Team Lead at XALT. XALT Business Consulting GmbH is an Atlassian Platinum Solution Partner with 100% DevSecOps DNA, realizing projects in areas such as ITSM, Social Intranets, Cloud Infrastructure, IDPs, and Identity Management. We offer a future-oriented, international environment with flexible working hours and remote options, prioritizing continuous learning, creative freedom, and a strong team spirit with an agile startup flair.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Team Leadership & Delivery:** Professional and organizational leadership of the consulting team – planning the deployment of consultants in Atlassian and DevOps projects, ensuring timely, high-quality results, and satisfied customers.\n- **Methodology & Processes:** Defining and optimizing our consulting processes and best practices within the team, establishing corresponding methods, and driving continuous improvements.\n- **Client Escalations:** Direct point of contact for escalations – intervening when issues arise, finding constructive solutions, and strengthening client trust.\n- **Mentoring & Enablement:** Coaching and professional development of consultants – acting as a mentor in project work, actively supporting the team, fostering continuous learning, and ensuring optimal performance.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Education & Experience:** Completed degree in (Business) Informatics, Business Administration, or comparable field, with at least 8 years of professional experience in IT consulting or project management, including several years in a leadership role and demonstrable project success in the IT consulting industry.\n- **Technical Expertise:** In-depth expertise in Atlassian tools (Jira, Confluence, etc.) and good knowledge of cloud technologies and DevOps principles; experience with Identity & Access Management solutions (e.g., SSO, directory services) is a plus.\n- **Methodological Competence:** Fundamental understanding of project management and consulting methods and tools like Jira for project control; ideally familiar with agile methodologies (Scrum/Kanban) and ITIL/ITSM processes.\n- **Client Orientation:** Strong service mindset and quality awareness; experience in projects in the DACH region; proactive assurance of customer satisfaction.\n- **Leadership & Soft Skills:** Proven leadership competence and experience in managing distributed teams; strong communication skills and empathy; a team player with high social competence and organizational talent.\n- **Languages:** Native-level German and fluent English skills, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned IT Consulting Team Lead with a passion for Atlassian and Cloud solutions. XALT offers a highly autonomous, flexible, and growth-oriented environment where you can make a significant impact on team development and client success. The company's commitment to continuous learning, creative freedom, and a supportive culture, combined with flexible work options and a vibrant \"startup flair,\" makes this an ideal role for an innovative and empathetic leader looking to thrive.
