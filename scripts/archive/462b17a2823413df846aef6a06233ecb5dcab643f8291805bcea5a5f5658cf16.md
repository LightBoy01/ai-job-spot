### **ROLE 👩🏽‍💻👨‍💻**

As a Senior Growth Product Manager you will drive sustainable and scalable growth across our customer lifecycle — from acquisition and activation to retention and expansion. In this role, you’ll be at the forefront of our Product-Led Growth (PLG) strategy, leading high-impact experiments and building systems that accelerate customer value and revenue. You’ll work cross-functionally with world-class teams to optimize funnel conversion, unlock self-serve revenue opportunities, and scale our B2B PLG engine.

*   Own the end-to-end PLG growth strategy across acquisition, activation, retention, and expansion.
*   Identify and prioritize the highest-impact growth opportunities based on data, user insights, and market trends.
*   Drive fast-paced experimentation to optimize conversion funnels and unlock new revenue opportunities.
*   Partner with engineering, analytics, PMM, and customer-facing teams to build and iterate on high-performing growth loops.
*   Lead growth pods to execute roadmap initiatives — with clear ownership over experiment planning, execution, and analysis.
*   Influence go-to-market strategy, pricing & packaging, and product-led sales efforts through customer insights and experimentation.
*   Define and track key growth metrics, including activation rates, retention cohorts, LTV/CAC, and expansion revenue.
*   Use data to surface bottlenecks, test hypotheses, and refine the roadmap to maximize business impact.
*   Build feedback loops between product and go-to-market teams to continually improve user onboarding and engagement.

**SOUND LIKE YOU? 🙌**
----------------------

*   Experience: You have 3+ years of experience in growth product management, ideally in B2B SaaS or a product-led environment.
*   You have a proven track record in PLG or self-serve models, with success owning metrics like signup-to-activation, retention, expansion, and LTV/CAC.
*   End-to-End Growth Strategy Ownership: You connect market insight → PLG roadmap → OKRs. You prioritize the highest-impact bets across acquisition, activation, retention, and expansion. You co-author quarterly goals with leadership and revisit them as data shifts.
*   Experimentation & Learning Mindset: You design statistically sound A/B tests, ship fast iterations, learn aloud, and scale wins. You challenge “we’ve always done it this way” when impact warrants.
*   Analytical Insight & Data Fluency: You surface “gold spots” in messy funnels, define crisp KPIs, and tell the story behind the numbers. You move seamlessly from SQL/dashboards to stakeholder-ready narratives.
*   Cross-Functional Leadership & Execution: You rally Design, Eng, Data, PMM, and GTM pods. You clarify scope, trade-offs, and timelines. You coach teams while keeping momentum high.
*   Stakeholder Influence & Communication: You earn trust up, down, and sideways. You frame decisions, secure buy-in, and navigate change with empathy and clarity.
*   Velocity & Bias to Action: You turn insight into shipped value quickly. You align fast, unblock teams, and hit timing windows—delivering quality without drag.
*   Creative Solutioning & Bold Problem Solving: You generate and refine novel growth ideas, test constraints, and find workable alternatives when the “perfect” path isn’t feasible.
*   Customer Insight & Empathy: You build a deep understanding of customer journeys and pain points. You leverage interviews, usage data, and frontline feedback to surface unmet needs and shape high-impact solutions. You champion the voice of the user in growth strategy and prioritization.
