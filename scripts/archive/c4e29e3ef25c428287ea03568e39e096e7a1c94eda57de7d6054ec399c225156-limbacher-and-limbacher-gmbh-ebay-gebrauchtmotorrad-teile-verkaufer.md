### Description
Are you ready to become part of Germany's largest motorcycle center? As an eBay Used Motorcycle Parts Seller at Limbächer & Limbächer GmbH, you will immerse yourself in the world of motorized passion – this time within the exciting cosmos of high-quality used parts. Our mission is to make our customers' mobility dreams come true, and you contribute to this by providing the right motorcycle parts for our online clientele.

You will be the primary contact person for our eBay customers, presenting and organizing our extensive product range, and supporting our team with dedication and passion. With over 250 colleagues by your side, we offer a work atmosphere characterized by innovation, team spirit, and trust. We are committed to growth, honesty, and fairness – and look forward to making motorcycle dreams come true with you, even in the digital marketplace.

If you have an affinity for motorcycles, work in a structured manner, and enjoy online customer contact, you are exactly right for us. Seize the challenge and become part of our community – where courage, reliability, and passion for technology count.

### Responsibilities
- Provide friendly and professional support for customer inquiries regarding used motorcycle parts – via message, phone, or in person.
- Organize, coordinate, and process orders, queries, and shipping procedures.
- Receive, inspect, and document used motorcycle parts, preparing them for sale.
- Create item listings on eBay, including descriptions, photo documentation, and price determination.
- Ensure a smooth sales process through transparent communication with customers and internal teams.
- Process service and complaint cases, and provide support for warranty or goodwill inquiries, if applicable.
- Maintain and update customer and item data, and manage related correspondence.
- Actively participate in measures to enhance customer satisfaction, e.g., through quick feedback and professional support.
- Organize the warehouse area, control incoming goods, and prepare parts for shipping.

### Qualifications
- Very good knowledge of various **motorcycle brands**, models, and years of manufacture (including differences, compatibility, typical weak points).
- Experience in the **accessories and spare parts sector** for motorcycles.
- Proficient in using **warehouse management systems**, merchandise management, and structured parts organization.
- Competent in **researching market-driven prices** (e.g., comparing across different platforms, assessing condition, demand analysis).
- Ability to **correctly identify**, assign parts, and check compatibilities if necessary.
- Experienced in using **eBay**, classifieds, and other online sales platforms.
- Experience in the **professional presentation of items**.
- Knowledge of the **shipping process** (packaging, shipping service providers, tracking).
- Basic knowledge in **customer service, complaint handling**, and professional online communication.
- Proficient in using PC, smartphone, Office programs, and ideally image editing (e.g., for product photos).
