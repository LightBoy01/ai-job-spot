### Description\nEvery is a direct-to-consumer (DTC) food subscription business in Berlin, poised for a significant \"10x jump.\" We operate our own channels and systems, focused on delivering a superior eating experience during the week. As a core lever for sustainable growth, we are seeking a Head of Finance to comprehensively own the finance function. This critical role is responsible for building scalable systems and providing the leadership team with crystal-clear financial insights necessary to navigate rapid expansion \"without chaos.\"\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Own and manage the full finance function, including monthly and annual financial statements, ensuring a clean and efficient close rhythm.\n- Oversee in-house accounting operations using DATEVasp, working closely with the Finance Manager and tax advisors.\n- Drive end-to-end finance operations, focusing on automation, process quality, and continuous improvement.\n- Lead Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A), encompassing forecasting, budgeting, and strategic scenario planning.\n- Proactively manage liquidity, cash flow, and working capital to support high-growth ambitions.\n- Conduct in-depth analysis of unit economics, cost steering, and cross-pillar financial performance.\n- Develop and maintain the data analytics and reporting stack for core financial metrics, in collaboration with the Tech lead.\n- Manage risk, legal, and insurance topics pragmatically, suited for a dynamic scale-up environment.\n- Build, mentor, and lead the finance team to support the company's next stage of growth.\n- Co-own the selection and rollout of an ERP system with the Operations team.\n- Ensure finance becomes \"boringly reliable\" with fast closes and trusted numbers, supporting a robust planning cadence.\n- Enable data-driven decision-making across the organization through insightful dashboards and unit economics.\n- Efficiently manage multi-country complexities, including VAT, customs, and cross-border transactions, minimizing bureaucracy.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 7-10+ years of progressive experience in finance leadership roles, with a strong preference for backgrounds in scale-ups or FMCG/DTC industries.\n- Excellent command of accounting principles, controlling, and FP&A methodologies.\n- Proven \"builder mindset\" with demonstrated experience in process design, automation, and system implementation.\n- Comfortable operating in multi-country setups and thriving amidst high-growth ambiguity.\n- Strong intuitive understanding of cash management and unit economics.\n- Proficiency with finance tools such as DATEVasp, Moss, Easybill, Pathway, and BigQuery.\n- Fluency in both German and English is required.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned finance leader who thrives in a high-growth, hands-on, and strategic environment. As the Head of Finance at Every, you'll be instrumental in shaping the financial infrastructure for a company ready for a 10x jump. The role demands not just financial acumen but a true builder's mindset to automate, optimize, and lead a growing team. If you're passionate about leveraging data to drive strategic decisions in a dynamic DTC scale-up and are comfortable with multi-country complexity, this is your mountain to climb.
