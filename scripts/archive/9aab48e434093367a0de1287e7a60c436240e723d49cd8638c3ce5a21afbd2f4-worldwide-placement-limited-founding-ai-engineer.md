### Description
Join an innovative YC-backed startup in **Berlin, Germany**, at the forefront of **AI Motion Design/Video Technology**. This **full-time** role is **on-site** with **0 home-office days**, and **relocation support is offered**. As the first AI motion designers that create professional animations through conversation, this company is revolutionizing video creation. Unlike existing video-gen tools, their platform generates code-based animations where every parameter can be fine-tuned, offering the control of After Effects with intuitive simplicity. As a key member of a small, elite **founding engineering team (1 position available)**, you will play a pivotal role in building and optimizing AI systems that empower millions of users to create stunning, high-quality animations effortlessly. This is a unique opportunity to shape the future of creative AI and redefine how video content is made.

### Responsibilities
*   Build and optimize AI systems that generate high-quality animations following core motion-design principles (easing, non-linear movement, visual hierarchy).
*   Develop comprehensive evaluation frameworks to assess animation quality, combining automated metrics with expert validation from motion designers.
*   Partner with motion designers and marketers to translate professional design knowledge into AI training data and evaluation criteria.
*   Design and implement fine-tuning pipelines that teach Hera domain-specific animation styles and brand guidelines.
*   Create feedback loops between user preferences, expert input, and model performance to continuously improve animation quality.
*   Research and prototype state-of-the-art approaches for controllable, style-consistent motion-graphics generation.

### Qualifications
*   **Experience:** Minimum 1 year of professional experience in a relevant field.
*   **Language:** C1 English proficiency.
*   **Technical Expertise:**
    *   **Core Technologies:** Hands-on experience with TypeScript, React.js, Next.js, PostgreSQL, and cloud platforms like GCP/AWS. Familiarity with proprietary motion graphics engines is a plus.
    *   **ML Engineering Foundation:** Strong background in training and fine-tuning models (e.g., diffusion models, VAEs, GANs), building robust data pipelines, and implementing comprehensive evaluation frameworks.
    *   **Computer Vision/Graphics:** Practical experience in video generation, image synthesis, or animation-related ML projects, with familiarity in motion/temporal modeling.
    *   **Systems Design:** Proven ability to build end-to-end ML pipelines, from data collection and model training to evaluation and production deployment.
    *   **Research & Implementation:** Capable of understanding and implementing state-of-the-art research papers, with bonus points for domain knowledge in animation/motion graphics.
*   **Mindset & Work Ethic:**
    *   **Driven:** Highly ambitious and "hungry" to build something massive.
    *   **Entrepreneurial:** Takes ownership and has a track record of initiating and building.
    *   **Adaptable:** Thrives in ambiguous environments, comfortable tackling problems without predefined roadmaps or PMs.
    *   **Growth-Oriented:** Committed to continuous personal and professional growth.
    *   **High-Performing:** Approaches work with an "athlete's" mentality – high performance, consistent improvement, and a "whatever it takes" attitude.
*   **Location:** Willingness to work exclusively in-person in Berlin, Germany (relocation support provided).
*   **Cultural Fit (Anti-patterns):** Not a fit if you are particular about work tasks, lack curiosity, prefer highly structured environments, are skeptical of AI, or wish to specialize in only one technology. The company values trajectory ("slope") over past credentials ("intercept").
