We are currently looking for a Heavy Vehicle Apprentice to join our **Townsville Workshop**. As an Apprentice, your primary objective is to actively participate in an apprenticeship program under the guidance of experienced technicians and mentors. This involves learning and mastering the fundamental principles of heavy vehicle mechanics, such as diagnosing mechanical issues, conducting routine inspections, performing scheduled maintenance tasks and utilising diagnostic tools and equipment effectively.

**  
The Opportunity:**

*   Work towards and complete a Cert III Heavy Vehicle Mechanical Technology - actively participate in training programs, workshops and educational opportunities enhance technical skills, expand knowledge and progress towards becoming a qualified Heavy Vehicle Mechanic.
*   Full-time Permanent Position | Monday to Friday.
*   Support technicians in performing routine maintenance tasks such as oil changes, filter replacements, tyre rotations and lubrication to ensure vehicles are operating efficiently.
*   Diagnose and troubleshoot issues whilst learning technical skills.
*   Maintain cleanliness and organisations along with adhering to safety procedures and guidelines.

  

*   A willingness to undertake the formal training requirements of this role.
*   A person who appreciates and enhances a good work-culture and displays exemplary ethics.
*   A genuine interest in the heavy vehicle mechanical trade with a commitment to completing apprenticeship.
*   Eager to learn, develop and grow in their trade, with long term employment prospects beyond your apprenticeship.

**Benefits:**

*   Wide variety of modern fleet & specialised equipment with full size service pit.
*   Uniforms supplied & professionally laundered.
*   Workplace Benefits Program providing discounts to many major retailers.
*   Health Fund discounts for single or family cover.
*   Free access to our Employee Assistance Program, Mental Wellbeing Program and Workplace Banking Program.
*   Recognition programs where you are rewarded for your contribution to the business.

If you encompass the above attributes and feel this position is a good fit for you, the Followmont Team would love to hear from you!
