**Role:** Gen AI Graphic Motion Designer
**Location:** San Francisco, CA
### About the role:
We are seeking a talented and innovative **Gen AI Graphic Motion Designer** to join our creative agency in San Francisco. This role combines cutting-edge AI-powered design skills with traditional motion graphics expertise to create compelling visual content for a leading B2B finance solutions company. The Motion Designer create dynamic motion graphics, animations and videos using both traditional and AI-powered tools, leveraging generative AI tools (including Pencil) to accelerate and enhance the creative process.
### What you will be doing:
*   Develop visually compelling video content for digital platforms, social media, presentations and other platforms
*   Adapt brand guidelines to ensure consistency across all animated content
*   Collaborate with creative teams to execute campaign concepts
*   Stay current with emerging AI design technologies and techniques
*   Present creative concepts to internal teams and clients
*   Optimize workflows by effectively combining AI tools with traditional design processes
*   Work collaboratively with the creative team to establish design systems and templates for AI generation
*   Optimize AI prompts to achieve consistent, on-brand creative outputs
*   Stay current with evolving generative AI technologies and methodologies
*   Create inspiring and exciting content – from repurposing, reformatting of assets to ideation
*   Keenly evaluate and monitor industry trends and brands, research industry developments in branding and marketing to be at the forefront of creative excellence
*   Strong project management skills, teamed with an aptitude for managing multiple varying sized projects seamlessly for a high-profile client
### What you need to be great in this role:
*   Excellent client engagement skills with the ability to proactively organise and influence clients and build strong and effective working relationships
*   An innate creative flair, strong art direction and concepting skills – full of ideas across multi-platforms
*   Strong knowledge of Adobe Creative Suite
*   Strong typography, layout and technical skills with excellent attention to detail
*   An understanding how to work across online banners (static and animated), social media posts (primarily Instagram and Facebook), web design, UI design and storytelling
*   An understanding of responsive design across all digital platforms
*   An understanding of UX and latest Social and Digital technology trends
*   Motion and video skills using After Effects. & Premiere. Storyboarding Animation concepts
*   Working on print-based collateral with great knowledge of branding
*   The ability to talk through your ideas confidently with the team and clients
