Are you a detail-obsessed project doer who loves turning messy inputs into clean, reliable data? Do you thrive in a fast-paced, collaborative environment and enjoy juggling multiple deadlines? Keen to use AI to boost quality and speed? Help Ipsos transform opinions into insight that shapes products, services and policy.

  
**About the role:**

  
Join Ipsos as a Project Executive in our Operations Data Capture Team and help turn people’s opinions and experiences into robust, decision-ready data. Operations gives our researchers global access to respondents across modes with rigorous quality, security, speed and cost-efficiency. In this hands-on role, you’ll manage projects end-to-end - setting up survey templates and scanning, delivering fast, accurate data cleaning and transcriptions, and running thorough quality checks - right through to passing final data. You’ll partner closely with research teams to understand needs, keep timelines and budgets on track, calculate production rates and resourcing, and spot risks early with practical solutions. You’ll collaborate across diverse teams, coach colleagues, and continuously improve how we work. At Ipsos, you’ll make a real impact on everyday products, public services and policy, while growing in a supportive, people-first culture led by researchers, where curiosity, client focus and entrepreneurial spirit are celebrated - and smart tools, including AI, are embraced to help you deliver your best.

  
**About you:**

*   Meticulous, quality-first mindset with a keen eye for detail
*   Organised multi-tasker who keeps complex projects moving
*   Clear communicator and active listener who builds trust
*   Analytical, comfortable interpreting information to drive decisions
*   Adaptable and resilient in a dynamic, deadline-driven environment
*   Collaborative and customer-focused, with a can-do approach
*   Proactive problem-solver who anticipates and mitigates risks
*   Proficient with AI tools and eager to adopt new tech to work smarter

  
**Essential skills:**

*   Proficiency in analysing and interpreting information
*   Strong attention to detail and commitment to quality
*   Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
*   Robust organisational and project management abilities
*   Ability to adapt to change with resilience and flexibility
*   Customer service skills; able to build relationships across the business
*   Effective problem-solving; identifies challenges and develops solutions
*   Supervision skills, including task delegation and time management
*   Knowledge of AI and how it can help with day-to-day tasks

  
**Desirable skills:**

*   English to A-Level standard (or equivalent)
*   Previous verbatim coding experience
*   Experience with survey scanning and template set-up
*   Data cleaning, transcription workflows and quality control
*   Calculating/monitoring production rates and resource planning
*   Background in market research operations or data capture environments
