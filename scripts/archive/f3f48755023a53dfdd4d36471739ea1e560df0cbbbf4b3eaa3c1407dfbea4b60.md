**ABOUT THE ROLE**

As a **Staff FullStack AI Engineer** at WunderGraph, you are a technical leader who defines and drives the architecture of our AI-powered experiences. You will design and ship LLM Agents and Agent Workflows, while shaping the frameworks, practices, and systems that make them scalable, secure, and delightful for our customers.

You will own the technical direction of major AI initiatives across our products, influencing architectural decisions from backend APIs to frontend integration. Your expertise ensures our AI systems are reliable, enterprise-ready, and deliver exceptional developer and user experience.

This is a highly impactful, hands-on role: you’ll be coding, reviewing, mentoring, and collaborating directly with customers while setting the bar for how AI engineering is done at WunderGraph.

**TEAM INTEGRATION**

*   Aligns with the **CTO** on architectural vision and strategic priorities
    
*   Collaborates with product managers, engineering peers, and directly with customers
    
*   Acts as a **mentor and multiplier**, guiding senior and mid-level engineers
    
*   Closes the feedback loop by engaging directly with users through Slack channels and calls  
    

**ROLE OBJECTIVES**

You are successful if you:

*   Drive the **architectural vision** for AI systems across WunderGraph’s stack.
    
*   Take full ownership of complex AI features, from design to production operations.
    
*   Influence the **full-stack experience** by aligning frontend, backend, and AI workflows.
    
*   Ensure security, scalability, and enterprise readiness in everything you ship.
    
*   Elevate the engineering org by mentoring peers, raising standards, and sharing knowledge.  
    

**ROLE TASKS**

The role focuses on, but is not limited to:

**Architecture & Development**

*   Architect and operate the **WunderGraph AI Stack** using Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Node.js.
    
*   Design and implement **backend APIs and services** that power AI workflows.
    
*   Evaluate and guide the adoption of **LLM frameworks and agent architectures**.
    
*   Define patterns and best practices for scalable AI integrations.  
    

**Reliability & Security**

*   Embed **security and compliance** (SOC 2, enterprise standards) into system design.
    
*   Anticipate scaling challenges and design for **resilience and performance**.
    
*   Establish monitoring, observability, and CI/CD standards for AI features.  
    

**Leadership & Mentorship**

*   Provide **technical guidance and mentorship** to senior and mid-level engineers.
    
*   Conduct thoughtful **code and design reviews**, raising quality across the team.
    
*   Document architectural decisions and contribute to open knowledge-sharing.
    
*   Represent WunderGraph in the AI/engineering community through contributions, talks, or content.
    
*   Help us hire and build an exceptional engineering team that sets the standard for the industry  
    

**ROLE REQUIREMENTS**

*   **Staff Level (IC5)** experience architecting and operating complex, user-facing applications in production.
    
*   Deep expertise in **TypeScript, React, Next.js, and Node.js**.
    
*   Strong understanding of **system architecture and API design**.
    
*   Familiarity with **GraphQL and GraphQL Federation is a strong** plus.
    
*   Experience building developer tools, platforms, or products for highly technical users.
    
*   Demonstrated ability to lead technical direction and resolve ambiguous challenges.
    
*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
    
*   Self-starter with a **leader’s mindset**: proactive, decisive, and comfortable driving change.
