TODAY revolutionizes Financial Advice in insurance, banking, and investment by making advisors AI-native. Moreover, TODAY is an AI-native company that works with unparalleled automation. Join our mission to deliver peace of mind at scale as our Founding Fullstack Engineer!

Tasks
-----

*   Architect, build, and own core product features across our entire stack, from **AI** (various LLMs, Voice AI, VAPI, Langfuse), **Python** (optionally **Golang**) on the backend, **Postgres** as a DB and **Microsoft Azure** for the infrastructure.
*   Design and implement scalable **microservice architectures** on a modern infrastructure stack.
*   Development of production-grade **AI products**, including features driven by LLMs, agents, and advanced **audio ML**.
*   Integrate a wide variety of third-party **APIs** to expand our platform's capabilities.
*   Get hands-on with **Voice AI**, building systems for audio analysis, processing, and implementing robust evaluation frameworks for **evals** and **agent orchestration**.
*   Champion a security-first mindset, ensuring the highest standards for **data, application, and infrastructure security**.
*   Establish and drive a comprehensive testing culture, from **unit testing** to contract and penetration testing.

Requirements
------------

*   You have a proven track record of building and shipping **production-grade AI products**, especially in a startup environment.
*   You possess rock-solid, hands-on experience in **Python** (and/or **Golang**), **Postgres** (or MySQL), microservices, and modern infrastructure. **ReactJS** or **Electron** is a big plus too.
*   You are experienced in **API integrations** and can quickly get up to speed with new services.
*   You have deep, practical experience with **LLMs**, **prompt** engineering, **evals.** **Voice AI** and **Audio ML** is a significant plus.
*   You are exceptionally driven with a strong **entrepreneurial mindset** and are excited by the prospect of being a founding member of the team.
*   You are a pragmatic problem-solver who can translate complex requirements into robust, scalable, and secure technical solutions.

In addition to a competitive salary, we offer significant equity.
