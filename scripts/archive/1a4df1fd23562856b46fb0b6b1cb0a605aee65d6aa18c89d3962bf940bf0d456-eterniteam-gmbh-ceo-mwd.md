### Description
EterniTeam addresses Germany's significant challenge in entrepreneurship: business succession. Through a revolutionary approach, we secure the future of established companies, ensuring all stakeholders – departing and succeeding entrepreneurs, as well as employees – benefit from the solution. For this vital mission, we are seeking talented executive leaders to assume the managing directorship and ownership of an established operation with an annual turnover between 2 and 10 million Euros. We do not require personal capital investment; instead, we seek your talent and sustainable commitment.

We pride ourselves on being unbiased talent scouts, utilizing personality profiles and cultural matching to find the ideal fit for each company. We are looking for future CEOs who thrive in leading people and taking full responsibility for results. Our proprietary training program addresses any skill gaps, and our continuous support and community exchange ensure you are never alone on your journey.

### Responsibilities
- Lead an established, sustainably profitable business with an annual turnover between 1 million and 10 million Euros, typically managing 12 to 40 employees.
- Create and/or strengthen a positive company culture and foster a spirit of new beginnings during transition through engaged and empathetic leadership.
- Assume full profit and loss (P&L) responsibility for your company, with a strong focus on income statements and cash flow.
- Implement necessary modernizations with our support, such as introducing improved systems and processes.
- Serve as the primary contact for key customers and/or suppliers, building and expanding crucial relationships.
- Champion employee participation: empower your team by granting them genuine company shares that grow in value each year, ensuring transparent communication to maximize team motivation.
- Collaborate closely with our Operations Team, especially during the initial phase of transition.
- Enjoy significant creative freedom in guiding the company's growth and transformation, while ensuring all employees are actively involved and supportive of the journey.

### Qualifications
- Minimum of 5 years of proven leadership experience managing at least 5 employees, with a minimum of 10 years of overall professional experience.
- Business-fluent German language skills are essential.
- Experience in corporate management or commercial responsibility is a significant plus.
- Industry-specific experience or state-recognized certifications are also a plus.
- A commitment to sustainable engagement: this role is not merely a career step; the company will increasingly become *your* company through growing share ownership each year, requiring a long-term commitment.
- Possess a \\\"hands-on\\\" mentality for tackling challenges and overcoming apparent obstacles – you are in the cockpit of your company.
- Your sense of responsibility – for your company, your team, and yourself – serves as a role model for those around you.
- Demonstrate empathy, emotional intelligence, and self-assured, appreciative communication to build trust in you and your work.
