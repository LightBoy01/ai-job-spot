### Description\nThe Digitale Leute School is the premier platform for product management, UX design, and engineering professionals, dedicated to connecting industry leaders through conferences, specialized bootcamps, and a vibrant community. In collaboration with partners like Airbnb, Porsche Digital, Atlassian, and Rewe Digital, we actively shape the future of software development.\n\nOur flagship offering, the Product Design Bootcamp, is a **praxis-oriented, 4-month online training program** designed to be the ideal entry point for aspiring UX/UI Designers. Taught by experienced trainers from leading tech companies, you'll gain top-aktuelles know-how. Following the bootcamp, we provide **active support in your job search through our robust hiring network**, helping you secure a position in digital product teams. The program is even **completely free for job seekers with a Bildungsgutschein**. Don't miss out – the next course starts soon!\n\n### Responsibilities\nWithin the bootcamp, you will develop essential skills and practical experience in key areas of UX/UI Design, including:\n\n*   **User Research:** Learn to truly understand user needs and behaviors.\n*   **Prototyping & Wireframing:** Master the creation of visual and interactive concepts.\n*   **Usability-Tests:** Conduct tests, gather feedback, and iteratively improve designs.\n*   **Interface Design:** Design modern, user-friendly, and intuitive interfaces.\n*   **Agile Methods:** Work effectively with Scrum & Kanban within a team environment.\n*   **Practical Learning:** Engage in project-based work, simulating real-world product team scenarios.\n\n### Qualifications\nWe are looking for motivated individuals who possess:\n\n*   **Passion:** A strong enthusiasm for design, technology, and digital products.\n*   **Collaboration:** A team-oriented, communicative mindset, open to feedback.\n*   **Language Skills:** Good German language proficiency, as the course and projects are primarily conducted in German.\n*   **Motivation:** Whether you are a career changer (Quereinsteiger:in), a junior professional, or transitioning fields (Umsteiger:in), no prior design knowledge is required – just high motivation and a willingness to learn.\n\n### Author's Take\nThe Digitale Leute Product Design Bootcamp represents an outstanding opportunity for individuals eager to launch or transition into a UX/UI design career. Its compact yet comprehensive 4-month structure, delivered by industry-leading trainers, ensures a highly practical learning experience. The unparalleled benefit of personalized mentoring, hands-on projects in simulated product teams, and crucially, dedicated support for job applications and entry into digital product teams, makes this program incredibly valuable. The accessibility provided by the option for free participation with a Bildungsgutschein further cements its position as a top-tier choice for aspiring designers in Germany.
