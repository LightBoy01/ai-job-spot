**Job Description:**
The AI Strategist will play a pivotal role in transforming business processes and operations into AI-driven workflows, leveraging cutting-edge Agentic AI technologies. This role requires a deep understanding of AI systems capable of autonomous decision-making and interaction, combined with strategic thinking to align AI solutions with business goals. The AI Strategist will collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify opportunities for automation, innovation, and optimization, ensuring seamless integration of Agentic AI into the organization's operations.
**Responsibilities:**
*   **Strategic Planning and Roadmap Development:**
*   Design and implement AI strategies that align with business goals and long-term objectives.
*   Develop and prioritize a roadmap for integrating Agentic AI technologies across departments and workflows.
*   **Agentic AI Development and Integration:**
*   Lead the architectural design of Agentic AI systems capable of autonomous decision-making and task execution.
*   Work with business teams for detailed requirements; work with development teams for implementation. Potentially may also identify outsourced solution as needed.
*   Assist business teams to integrate Agentic AI solutions into existing business processes and operations.
*   **Business Process Transformation:**
*   Analyze current workflows to identify inefficiencies and opportunities for AI-driven automation and optimization.
*   Develop AI-powered workflows that improve operational efficiency, reduce costs, and enhance productivity.
*   **Cross-Functional Collaboration:**
*   Partner with stakeholders across departments to understand business needs and challenges.
*   Act as a liaison between technical teams and business units to ensure successful AI adoption and integration.
*   **Performance Monitoring and Continuous Improvement:**
*   Define metrics for evaluating the effectiveness and performance of AI-driven workflows.
*   Monitor AI systems post-deployment and recommend adjustments to optimize performance and outcomes.
*   **Training and Change Management:**
*   Educate employees and leadership on the capabilities and limitations of Agentic AI systems.
*   Develop training programs to support the adoption of AI-driven workflows and ensure smooth transition to new processes.
*   **Risk Management and Ethical AI Practices:**
*   Identify potential risks associated with Agentic AI deployment and develop mitigation strategies.
*   Ensure AI solutions adhere to ethical guidelines and promote fairness, transparency, and accountability.
**Knowledge and Skills:**
**Technical Expertise:**
*   Strong knowledge of AI systems, particularly Agentic AI, including autonomous agents, reinforcement learning, and decision-making models.
*   Proficiency in AI development tools, frameworks, languages and vector database (e.g., Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, LangChain, AutoGen, ChromaDB, Weaviate).
*   Familiarity with cloud platforms and AI integration technologies (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud AI).
*   Understanding of data pipelines, data governance, and the role of data in AI workflows.
**Strategic and Analytical Skills:**
*   Ability to analyze complex business processes and identify opportunities for AI-driven transformation.
*   Expertise in creating actionable strategies that leverage AI to achieve business objectives.
*   Strong problem-solving skills and ability to translate technical solutions into business value.
**Leadership and Collaboration:**
*   Proven experience leading cross-functional teams and driving successful AI initiatives.
*   Excellent communication skills to bridge the gap between technical teams and business stakeholders.
*   Ability to deliver presentations, reports, and recommendations to senior leadership.
**Change Management and Training:**
*   Experience implementing organizational change initiatives related to AI adoption.
*   Ability to design and deliver training programs for employees to adapt to AI-driven workflows.
**Ethical AI and Risk Management:**
*   Familiarity with ethical AI frameworks and best practices.
*   Experience in managing risks related to AI systems, including bias, transparency, and accountability.
**Education and Experiences Required:**
*   Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, or a related field.
*   5+ years of experience in AI strategy, development, and integration, with a focus on Agentic AI.
*   Proven track record of successfully transforming business processes using AI-driven solutions.
This role is ideal for a forward-thinking strategist who is passionate about leveraging AI to drive innovation, efficiency, and growth in business operations.
