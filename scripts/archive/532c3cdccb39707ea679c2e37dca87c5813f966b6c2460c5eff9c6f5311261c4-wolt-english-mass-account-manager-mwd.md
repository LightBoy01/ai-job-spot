### Description
Wolt, in partnership with DoorDash, is a leading technology company transforming local commerce and delivery across 500+ cities in 30 countries. We focus on building technology that brings joy, simplicity, and earnings to neighborhoods worldwide, starting with restaurant food and now expanding to "almost everything." Our mission-driven culture emphasizes learning, building, and shipping more, challenging individuals while fostering an entrepreneurial spirit.

This role is for a Mass Account Management Manager within Wolt's crucial German restaurant operations. You will be at the heart of supporting a vast and growing portfolio of partners, taking full ownership of their success. The core mission is to elevate operational performance, reduce churn, and scale the adoption of Wolt's commercial tools such as Deals, Promotions, and Ads. This position is ideal for an analytical, data-driven individual who thrives on solving challenges at scale and is passionate about building sustainable growth processes for both Wolt and its partners.

### Responsibilities
*   Manage the operational health and performance of a large group of restaurant partners.
*   Monitor and improve key performance indicators (KPIs) such as availability, active rate, rejection rate, and churn.
*   Design and lead initiatives that drive the adoption of Promotions, Deals, and Ads among partners.
*   Utilize Business Intelligence (BI) tools and dashboards to automate engagement strategies and identify operational gaps.
*   Collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams to enhance tooling, communication, and outreach efforts.
*   Create and implement scalable solutions, and mentor Associates on best practices within account management.

### Qualifications
*   A minimum of 3 years of experience in account management, operations, or growth-oriented roles.
*   Excellent communication skills in both German and English.
*   Strong data and process orientation, with a proven ability to leverage analytics for decision-making.
*   Practical experience working with Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems and Business Intelligence (BI) platforms.
*   Demonstrated ability to take initiative, independently improve systems, and work autonomously.
*   Bachelor's or Master's degree in a relevant field.
