**Start Date:** As soon as possible, until December 1, 2027

univativ is a project and personnel service provider dedicated to connecting students and graduates with exciting opportunities at renowned companies. Your career is our mission, and your professional development is at the core of what we do.

Are you looking to work in a versatile environment where teamwork, organizational talent, and independent action are highly valued? As a **Team Assistant (m/f/d) for Network Construction**, you will step into a crucial role, acting as a vital interface between various teams and the department management, actively shaping daily operational activities. Join our dynamic setting and contribute your strengths to engaging infrastructure and expansion projects.

We are here to kickstart your career! Successfully launch your journey with our client in the energy sector! You'll have the chance to collaborate on thrilling projects within an innovative team, continuously expanding your knowledge and expertise. If this sounds like a compelling career perspective, we encourage you to apply today!

### Responsibilities

*   Serve as the central point of contact for multiple teams in their daily operational business.
*   Collaborate closely with department management by providing active support and assistance.
*   Support teams with both technical and organizational tasks.
*   Manage and oversee personnel changes within projects (onboarding and offboarding).
*   Organize meetings and appointments, utilizing common collaboration tools such as RDB, Jira, and SharePoint.

### Qualifications

*   Completed commercial or technical training combined with initial relevant professional experience.
*   Practical experience in assistant roles, preferably with a focus on project management.
*   Demonstrated ability to work independently and in a structured manner, with proficient use of M365; basic knowledge of SAP and DMS is a plus.
*   High level of commitment coupled with persistence, strong communication skills, self-initiative, and patience.
*   At least business-fluent German language skills, both written and spoken.
