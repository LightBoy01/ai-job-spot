We are **tech transformation** specialists, uniting human expertise with AI to create scalable tech solutions.

With over [7.400](http://7.400) CI&Ters around the world, we’ve built partnerships with more than 1,000 clients during our 30 years of history. Artificial Intelligence is our reality.

  

We’re hiring a Mid-level Python Developer to design, implement, and evolve a multi‑agent AI system alongside data scientists, software engineers, and statisticians. You’ll join client calls in English and help deliver robust, observable, and secure production solutions.

  

**Key responsibilities**

\-Build and orchestrate AI agents (workflows, tools, memory/retrieval, coordination).

\-Integrate LLMs and supporting services (RAG, vector stores, function/tool calling, external APIs).

\-Write tests, add logging/monitoring, and support the release cycle.

\-Partner with data scientists on agent evaluation (offline/online metrics, A/B testing).

\-Document architecture, technical decisions, and best‑practice usage.

  

**Requirements**

\-Strong Python skills applied to AI products.

\-Advanced SQL required; experience with SQL or NoSQL databases and performant queries.

\-Hands‑on experience with LLMs and agents (prompting, tool calling, basic RAG).

\-Knowledge of at least one agent framework: LangGraph, AutoGen, LlamaIndex, Google ADK, or similar.

\-Clear English communication with client stakeholders.

  

**Nice to have**

\-LLM observability (tracing/metrics) and automated evaluation experience.

\-Familiarity with vector stores  and queues/events.

\-Containers/CI/CD and security/guardrails for AI.

  

If you like it, just apply and good luck!
