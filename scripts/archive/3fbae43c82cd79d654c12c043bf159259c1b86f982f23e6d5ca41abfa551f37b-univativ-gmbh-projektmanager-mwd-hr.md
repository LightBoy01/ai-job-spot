### Description

univativ is a dynamic project and personnel service provider dedicated to connecting students and graduates with exciting job opportunities at renowned companies. Our mission is to foster your career, as your development is at the heart of what we do. For our client, a leading company in the energy sector based in Munich, we are looking for a creative and organized individual to develop digital learning concepts and manage e-learning projects from ideation to implementation.

In this role, you will encounter exciting tasks ranging from conceiving innovative learning formats and managing external partners to closely collaborating with internal stakeholders. You will have the unique opportunity to actively shape education within a forward-thinking environment, working 37 hours per week.

### Responsibilities

*   Plan, coordinate, and implement e-learning projects.
*   Coordinate and manage external e-learning agencies.
*   Plan training needs and develop concepts for digital learning formats.
*   Advise clients on media didactics and media technology for the implementation of learning journeys and formats.
*   Communicate and coordinate effectively with various stakeholders, including the digital learning department, clients, agencies, and employees, demonstrating strong communication and implementation skills.
*   Support training management within the Learning Management System.

### Qualifications

*   Successfully completed studies in media didactics, media pedagogy, or similar fields.
*   Ideally, initial experience in project management or e-learning.
*   Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate persuasively with diverse stakeholders.
*   Business-fluent German language skills.
*   Analytical thinking, organizational talent, and creativity.
