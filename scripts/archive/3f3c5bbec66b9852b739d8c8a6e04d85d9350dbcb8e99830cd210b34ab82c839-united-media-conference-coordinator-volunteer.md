### Description\nPrivate Equity Insights organizes premier global events that connect C-level executives, GPs, LPs, entrepreneurs, and investors within the Private Equity sector. We are seeking ambitious and talented individuals to volunteer at the French Private Equity Conference in Frankfurt on March 13th, 2026. This is a unique opportunity to expand your network with over 500 industry leaders, develop valuable skills, and experience the world's largest Private Equity community from an insider's perspective. Volunteers are crucial to the success of our events and will be part of a team of 25+ peers working to deliver a leading industry conference.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a Private Equity Conference Volunteer, you will:\n*   Actively contribute to the planning, running, and delivery of the leading French Private Equity Conference.\n*   Participate in a 2-hour training session and rehearsals the afternoon before the event.\n*   Help create a unique atmosphere and ensure the smooth execution of the conference on the day of the event.\n*   Engage with industry leaders and attendees, supporting their experience throughout the day.\n\n### Qualifications\nWe are looking for individuals who are:\n*   Ambitious and talented, eager to contribute to a high-profile industry event.\n*   Interested in expanding their network within the Private Equity sector.\n*   Keen to develop soft skills, including communication and management abilities.\n*   Team-oriented, ready to collaborate with 25+ peers to achieve common goals.\n*   Available for the full day of the conference (March 13th, 2026) plus 2 hours the afternoon prior for training.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis volunteer opportunity with Private Equity Insights offers an unparalleled chance to immerse yourself in the world of private equity. It's ideal for anyone looking to build a robust professional network, gain practical event management experience, and receive valuable training in a dynamic, high-stakes environment. The promise of direct interaction with industry titans and the camaraderie within a dedicated volunteer team make this a standout opportunity for professional and personal growth. The exclusive access to the post-event dinner is a fantastic bonus for forging lasting connections.
