### Description
This role as an HR Business Partner is pivotal in shaping our organizational culture and supporting our strategic goals. You will act as a trusted advisor to executives and employees, driving initiatives related to talent management, employee development, and fostering an inclusive workplace.

### Responsibilities
*   Provide strategic consulting to executives on topics such as team structure, motivation, recruitment, and development.
*   Support personnel planning as well as talent and succession management.
*   Analyze employee surveys and participate in culture and engagement initiatives.
*   Promote a positive, inclusive work environment.
*   Advise on employee relations, moderate conflicts, and guide sensitive discussions.
*   Identify training needs and participate in the development of programs.
*   Support change and restructuring processes.
*   Utilize relevant HR metrics for recommendations and decisions.
*   Take responsibility for onboarding and offboarding processes, including exit interviews.
*   Participate in HR projects and provide support in the final recruiting phase.

### Qualifications
*   University degree, ideally in Human Resources, Management, or Law, or relevant vocational training with pertinent professional experience and further education.
*   Several years of experience as an HR Business Partner or in a comparable HR function.
*   Empathetic, professional demeanor and experience in conflict moderation.
*   Analytical approach and confident handling of HR data.
*   Strong communication and consulting skills.
*   Proactive, solution-oriented mindset.
*   Very good German and English language skills.
