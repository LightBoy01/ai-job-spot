**The Company**
===============

Wizeline is a global digital services company helping mid-size to Fortune 500 companies build, scale, and deliver high-quality digital products and services.  We thrive in solving our customer’s challenges through human-centered experiences, digital core modernization, and intelligence everywhere (AI/ML and data). We help them succeed in building digital capabilities that bring technology to the core of their business.

**What You Will Bring to the Team**
-----------------------------------

The Tech Partner will bring technology and business domain expertise, becoming a trusted advisor for our customers and offering guidance and mentorship for our Tech Leads and their teams. This role will work with large and strategic customers, collaborating with multiple teams and stakeholders across the client’s organization.   

Tech partners will be responsible for supporting the entire software development life cycle (SDLC) and consulting with our customers to design and implement the best solutions for their business. As a Technology Partner, you will be working closely with other senior technologists, both on our customer’s team and across Wizeline groups, driving strategic decisions, improving our methodologies, and identifying technology trends that will help us deliver customer satisfaction and growth.  

**Your Day-to-Day**
-------------------

*   Technology Strategy

*   Ensure design and components are extensible across multiple projects
*   Define and execute AI adoption strategies and multi-year roadmaps.
*   Mentor technology leaders and teams, driving excellence in AI engineering and delivery.
*   Oversee the design, development, and deployment of AI-powered solutions in collaboration with product, platform, and engineering teams
*   Conduct architecture and design reviews, ensuring AI models and systems meet performance, security, and compliance standards.
*   Define talent profiles and guide team capability building, including AI/ML upskilling programs.
*   Anticipate emerging AI trends and position them as opportunities for client innovation.
*   Document and share knowledge across the organization
*   Envision solutions and choose technologies that will solve our client's problems 
*   Responsible for overall technical performance; following Wizeline Solutions Framework 
*   Drive continuous innovation by exploring and assessing technologies to apply them in business domains
*   Create and execute based on multi-year technology roadmaps for one or more projects. Achieving technical economies of scale across multiple teams

*   Technology Execution 

*   Enable technology best practices across the entire SDLC
*   Define required staff profiles for a project and help the team achieve their full potential, defining performance and training programs 
*   Work in collaborative product and platform teams at both the Wizeline and our client levels
*   Conduct technical design reviews; auditing projects to ensure best practices are followed

*   Coaching and Mentoring

*   Mentor and coach Tech Leads and teams 
*   Influence a large team or multiple projects to achieve goals
*   Complete technical performance assessments for his/her team
*   Define staff profile, performance, and training standards.

**Are You a Fit?**
------------------

To be successful in this role, you will have:

*   10+ years of engineering leadership experience with a strong track record of successful development and delivery of complex, enterprise-class cloud solutions that resulted in material market impact with a clear understanding of software development lifecycle, DevOps, and agile methodologies 
*   Strong expertise in **AI/ML systems** including large language models, MLOps, data pipelines, and multi-agent orchestration.
*   Experience working with UX Designers, Software Engineers, and multidisciplinary teams, on a collaborative process to define product strategy and roadmap 
*   A systems thinker who can reinvent processes and apply AI technologies in pragmatic, value-driven ways.
*   Ability to operate autonomously, lead large-scale AI initiatives across multiple geographies, and manage change effectively.
*   Strong team player who supports their team in all regards and embraces ownership, accountability, and responsibility for the team’s work
*   Outgoing and positive thinking about possibilities, where others see problems you see challenges as opportunities
*   Storyteller with strong English skills who communicates effectively and inspires others with your vision
*   Analytical and problem-solving experience with large-scale platforms, infrastructure, and organizations
*   Effective in setting direction, addressing competing priorities, and managing change
*   A consultative and customer-centric approach to solving challenges with the ability to develop and execute a technical vision with a focus on business value
*   Experience influencing others, advocating for technical excellence while being open to change when needed 
*   Resilient, comfortable  in ambiguous situations with the ability to approach challenges from multiple perspectives
