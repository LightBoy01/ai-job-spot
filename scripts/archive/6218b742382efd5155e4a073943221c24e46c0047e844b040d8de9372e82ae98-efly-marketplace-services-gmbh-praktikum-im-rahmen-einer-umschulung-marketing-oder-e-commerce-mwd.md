### Description\nAs a Marketing Manager, you will calculate and manage our clients' advertising budgets, with the goal of achieving the best possible visibility within a defined budget. You will regularly create evaluations to communicate progress to clients. The strategic positioning of products in desired advertising placements also plays a significant role in this.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Develop SEA Strategies:** Collaborate with our Account Managers to develop targeted SEA strategies and ensure the full advertising potential on Amazon is leveraged.\n- **Keyword Research & Analysis:** Conduct comprehensive keyword research and manage on-page and off-page optimization to maximize product visibility.\n- **Campaign Optimization:** Continuously optimize and improve campaign potential to its fullest.\n- **Data-Driven Measures:** Create analyses and derive concrete measures for further campaign development – always keeping an eye on success with the help of analytical tools.\n- **Reporting:** Prepare regular reports to transparently present campaign results to our clients.\n- **Amazon Seller Central:** Be proficient in using Amazon Seller Central to manage advertising campaigns.\n- **PPC Price Optimization:** Optimize PPC prices based on predefined strategies to ensure optimal budget utilization.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Independent Work:** You are capable of working independently and managing projects autonomously.\n- **Quick Learner:** You have the ability to quickly grasp new topics and find solutions.\n- **Teamwork & Communication Skills:** You enjoy working in a team and possess strong communication skills – both internally and when interacting with clients.\n- **Analytical Understanding:** You love working with numbers and have a good understanding of analytical correlations.\n- **Structured Communication:** You communicate clearly and structured – whether in writing or verbally.\n- **Excel & PowerPoint:** You have very good knowledge of Excel and PowerPoint and know how to effectively use these tools to present results and analyze data.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role offers a fantastic opportunity for a data-driven Marketing Manager to make a significant impact on client success within the dynamic Amazon advertising landscape. With a focus on strategic development, continuous optimization, and transparent reporting, you'll be at the forefront of maximizing visibility and ROI. The company clearly values growth, offering a hybrid work model and a supportive, open team culture, making it an ideal environment for those looking to advance their career and contribute meaningfully.
