### Description

We are seeking a highly experienced and qualified Senior IT Administrator for PTC Creo. In this critical role, you will primarily provide IT support for PTC Creo 10 for one of the world's leading companies with over 3,000 employees globally. You will join a dynamic team, taking on responsibility for parametrisation, configuration, and administration of PTC Creo, acting as the Application Owner for the system.

This position is essential for ensuring smooth operations and supporting engineering and product development teams across local and international offices. You will collaborate closely with various departments to implement and optimize software solutions, with a strong focus on PTC Creo 10 and SAP ECTR. Excellent German and English language skills are imperative for successful collaboration with national CAD engineers in Germany and international teams worldwide.

### Responsibilities

As a Senior IT Administrator and Application Owner for PTC Creo, your responsibilities will include:

*   Configuring and maintaining the PTC Creo environment, including templates, libraries, and user settings.
*   Developing and managing parametric models and design automation tools.
*   Managing user access, licenses, and system updates.
*   Collaborating with engineering and IT teams to ensure seamless integration with PLM and ERP systems.
*   Providing technical support and training to end-users.
*   Defining and enforcing CAD standards and best practices.
*   Taking full responsibility as the Application Owner for PTC Creo, which includes managing vendor relationships and strategic roadmap planning.

**Possible Additional Responsibilities:**

*   Providing support for PLM SAP-ECTR products.
*   Managing output and document control from SAP.
*   Developing and implementing .Net/VB.Net programs for integration with SAP ECTR and PTC Creo.
*   Offering technical support and training to users on CAD and PLM systems.

### Qualifications

To be successful in this role, you should possess the following qualifications:

*   Extensive experience with PTC CAD products, especially PTC Creo 10.
*   Profound knowledge of PLM systems, including their administration, processes, and applications.
*   Demonstrated experience in software installation, customisation, and deployment.
*   Strong problem-solving skills and exceptional attention to detail.

*   Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.
*   Proven capacity for independent work as well as collaborative team efforts.
*   A proactive and eager-to-learn personality.
*   Experience with other CAD software solutions and PLM tools (advantageous).
*   Certifications in relevant technologies or systems (advantageous).
*   Knowledge of database management and SQL.
*   Experience in .Net and VB.Net programming for SAP ECTR and PTC Creo (highly advantageous).
*   A completed degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Engineering, or a related field.
*   Fluency in both German and English.
