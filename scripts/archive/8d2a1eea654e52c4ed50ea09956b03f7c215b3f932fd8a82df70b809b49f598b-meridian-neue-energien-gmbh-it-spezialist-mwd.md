### Description\nMeridian Neue Energien GmbH is a pioneering company dedicated to developing, realizing, and operating large-scale renewable energy projects across Germany and Europe for over 25 years. With a primary focus on solar energy and wind power, we leverage our deep expertise to build a sustainable energy supply for the future. We are seeking a dedicated IT Operations and Network Specialist to join our team in Suhl and ensure the seamless operation and security of our critical application and network infrastructure, directly contributing to the reliability of our renewable energy plants.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Operate and support internal and external applications.\n- Plan and monitor network technology, including WAN connectivity for EEG plants with VPN, and ensure robust network security, adhering to KRITIS requirements.\n- Oversee the operation and monitoring of applications, data flows, and associated databases.\n- Collaborate closely with other IT departments to ensure continuous, trouble-free operations.\n- Manage security and compliance controls, including adherence to relevant standards.\n- Provide essential support to operational management for the inspection, functional testing, maintenance, and upkeep of in-plant network technology.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A completed apprenticeship as an IT Specialist (`Fachinformatiker`) or a comparable education coupled with relevant professional experience.\n- Alternatively, a completed technical degree in fields such as Business Informatics, Electrical Engineering, or Industrial Engineering, or a comparable educational background.\n- **Mandatory:** Proven knowledge and practical experience in IT management, encompassing hardware, software, networks, and databases.\n- **Desired:** Experience with Fieldbus systems and SCADA integration.\n- **Desired:** Basic programming experience (e.g., HTML, PHP).\n- **Desired:** Familiarity with ISO 27001 certification processes.\n- Demonstrated ability to work independently, in a structured, and solution-oriented manner.\n- Excellent communication skills, strong social competence, and organizational strength.\n- A collaborative team player attitude.\n- Possession of a Class B driver's license.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an IT Operations and Network Specialist passionate about contributing to the renewable energy sector. The role offers a blend of critical operational responsibilities, from network security to application support, all within a company with a strong mission and a progressive culture. Meridian Neue Energien GmbH emphasizes employee well-being, growth, and transparent communication, making it an ideal environment for those looking to make a tangible impact while enjoying excellent benefits and a supportive team.
