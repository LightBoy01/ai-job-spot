### Description
At Wolt, we're building the delivery of (almost) everything across 500+ cities in 30 countries. We joined forces with DoorDash in 2022 and are continuously expanding globally. We are looking for proactive and fast-learning part-time Grocery Associates to join our Wolt Market team in Frankfurt. This role is crucial in delivering joy and simplicity to our customers by ensuring efficient grocery operations. If you thrive in a high-growth, challenging, yet fun environment and possess an entrepreneurial spirit, this could be an exciting opportunity to shape the future of retail.

### Responsibilities
- Taking in orders through our Merchant app
- Picking and packing groceries, and handing them to customers and courier partners
- Unloading food and supplies received from vendors
- Managing our internal systems, including precise information about nutrients and allergens
- Maintaining order and cleanliness of the store

### Qualifications
- Proactive and a fast learner with the ability to multitask
- Flexibility with working in shifts in the mornings, evenings, and nights, including weekends and public holidays
- Experience from working in a grocery store is an advantage
- Fluent in English; fluency in German is a plus
- Right to work in Germany
