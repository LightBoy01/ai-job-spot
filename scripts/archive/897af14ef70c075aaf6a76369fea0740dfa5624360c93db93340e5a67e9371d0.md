**Job Description:**

We’re looking for a **Principal CloudOps Engineer** to lead the design, development, and scaling of our next-generation AI platform infrastructure.   
You’ll drive architecture decisions, mentor engineers, and ensure our GenAI platform is highly scalable, reliable, and secure across cloud environments (AWS/GCP/Azure). 

This is a hands-on leadership role at the intersection of CloudOps, MLOps, and GenAI innovation — perfect for someone passionate about building at scale and shaping the future of enterprise AI. 

**Responsibilities:** 

*   Lead architecture and delivery of cloud-native and cloud-agnostic infrastructure for our enterprise GenAI platform. 
    

*   Design, build, and optimize infrastructure to support large-scale AI/ML workloads, including LLMs and GenAI applications. 
    

*   Collaborate closely with Product, Research, and Customer Success teams on roadmap planning and technology evaluation. 
    

*   Set technical best practices for cloud infrastructure, automation, monitoring, and reliability at scale. 
    

*   Mentor and coach engineers; help grow the team’s technical culture. 
    

*   Participate in on-call rotation as needed to ensure platform reliability. 
    

*   Drive continuous improvement in security, cost optimization, and operational excellence. 
    

**Required Qualifications**:

*   8+ years of hands-on experience designing, building, and operating cloud infrastructure on **AWS, GCP, or Azure**. 
    

*   Deep understanding of the **Well-Architected Framework** and cloud-native best practices. 
    

*   Expertise in **infrastructure-as-code** (e.g., Terraform, CloudFormation, Pulumi). 
    

*   Strong background in cloud networking, security, and observability. 
    

*   Experience supporting **AI/ML** or **data-intensive workloads** in production environments. 
    

*   Proven success leading technical projects and mentoring other engineers. 
    

*   Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Engineering, or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience. 
    

**Preferred Qualifications:** 

*   Master’s degree in computer science, Engineering, Data Science, or a related technical field. 
    

*   Professional Cloud Certification (e.g., AWS Solutions Architect, GCP Professional Cloud Architect, Azure Solutions Architect Expert). 
    

*   Experience building **cloud-native MLOps platforms** or managing large-scale AI/ML infrastructure. 
    

*   Strong background in SRE/DevOps principles with automation-first mindset. 
    

*   Previous experience in a **hyper-growth startup** or building **enterprise-grade SaaS** products.
