### Description

Do you want to help shape the future of renewable energies? Your ideas count with us! We are developing the operating system for renewable energies and making the management of corresponding plants easier with the Amperecloud Platform – for a world where green energy secures growth.

As a Working Student in Content Marketing & Social Media, you will work at the intersection of product, marketing, and sales. You will translate complex technologies into clear messages, develop engaging content, and demonstrate why our solution is crucial for the energy transition.

This is a part-time working student contract (at least 20 hours/week) based in Berlin, with a hybrid work model.

### Responsibilities

*   **Impactful Content:** Research topics, write texts, and develop stories for our website, newsletters, whitepapers, and case studies.
*   **Substantial Brand Building:** Collaborate with Product and Sales teams to develop content that strengthens our brand and generates leads.
*   **Social Media Ownership:** Plan, create, and publish posts on channels like LinkedIn, and actively contribute to community management and monitoring.
*   **Content Creation:** Produce authentic photos and videos of the team, product, and customers – on-site, spontaneously, and creatively.
*   **Events and Fairs:** Assist with the preparation and design of our trade fair appearances.
*   **Analysis and Growth:** Measure impact, derive learnings, test new formats, and contribute your own ideas.

### Qualifications

*   **Education:** You are an enrolled student, for example, in communication, business, or engineering sciences.
*   **Experience:** You have initial practical experience in social media, content, or product marketing, or in preparing technical topics. Basic knowledge of Adobe Creative Suite, SEO, B2B communication, or other common graphic and content tools is a plus. Experience with CRM systems like HubSpot rounds off your profile.
*   **Working Style:** You work independently, in a structured manner, and seek solutions rather than just tasks. You combine creativity with a high standard of quality.
*   **Mindset:** You are passionate about strong communication, love good storytelling, and want to actively contribute to the energy transition. You are reliable, eager to learn, and bring your own ideas to achieve measurable progress together with the team.
*   **Language:** You communicate confidently in German and English; Spanish language skills are a plus.

### Author's Take

This is an exceptional opportunity for an ambitious student eager to kickstart their career in content marketing within a purpose-driven, green tech company. Amperecloud offers a vibrant and supportive environment where your ideas genuinely contribute to shaping the future of renewable energies.

You'll benefit from a "steep learning curve" supported by exciting challenges and educational leave, ensuring significant personal and professional growth. The culture emphasizes teamwork and community, fostered through regular offsites and inspiring events in a dog-friendly Berlin office. Flexibility is key, with hybrid work options and flexible hours designed to support your work-life balance. Beyond a competitive package of mobility benefits (JobRad, Deutschlandticket), the company deeply cares about employee well-being, offering mental health support, fitness subsidies, and childcare allowances.

If you're passionate about impactful storytelling, contributing to the energy transition, and thriving in a collaborative, forward-thinking environment, Amperecloud provides the perfect platform to make a real difference and develop your skills.
