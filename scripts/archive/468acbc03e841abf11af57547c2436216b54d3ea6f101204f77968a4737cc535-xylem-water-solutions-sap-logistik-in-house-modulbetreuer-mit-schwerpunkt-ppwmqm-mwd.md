### Description
Xylem is a global water technology corporation with nearly 22,000 employees, dedicated to solving critical water challenges for our customers through innovative technological solutions and our deep expertise. We are the world's leading provider of efficient, innovative, and sustainable water technologies, ensuring that our water is optimally utilized, managed, preserved, and reused.

For our Sensus business unit at the Ludwigshafen location, we are seeking an **SAP Logistics In-House Module Specialist** with a core focus on **Production Planning (PP), Warehouse Management (WM), and Quality Management (QM)**. In this pivotal role, you will be instrumental in optimizing and evolving our SAP logistics processes to support our global mission.

### Responsibilities
*   Independently manage, optimize, and further develop SAP logistics processes.
*   Analyze complex business requirements and engineer tailored SAP solutions.
*   Execute customizing, oversee implementations, and support international rollouts to adapt software to specific company needs.
*   Conduct thorough integration tests to ensure system integrity.
*   Proactively analyze requirements, document processes, and provide expert process consulting to users and key users.
*   Advance the existing solution scope and generate detailed specifications.
*   Independently plan, coordinate, and execute complex international IT sub-projects, including initiatives within our S/4HANA project.
*   Serve as the central point of contact for all tasks related to the PP, WM, and QM logistics modules.
*   Plan, coordinate, and deliver comprehensive training sessions for users and key users, ensuring optimal utilization of SAP solutions.
*   Maintain strict adherence to security, data protection, and compliance regulations.

### Qualifications
*   A degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics, or a closely related field.
*   Many years of practical experience in SAP logistics, encompassing customizing and ABAP development.
*   Profound knowledge of logistics processes and SAP module configuration.
*   Proven experience with both SAP ECC 6 and S/4HANA environments.
*   Demonstrated experience in user support and consulting for key users.
*   Solid project management experience.
*   A proactive, structured, and results-oriented approach to work.
*   Excellent command of both German and English languages.
*   Ability to clearly and effectively communicate complex technical content.
*   A strong service and team-oriented mindset.
