About the Role
--------------

Join the team that is building the next generation of AI-powered mental health tools. As a Staff AI Engineer, you will develop advanced multimodal (voice, text, and vision) systems that power our Mental Health AI Coach and Provider Co-pilot—pushing the boundaries of what’s possible in AI while making mental health care more accessible, personalized, and effective. We’re looking for a driven and curious engineer with strong fundamentals in software engineering and machine learning who is excited to apply technical expertise to real-world impact. In this role, you’ll tackle cutting-edge challenges in AI/ML—from fine-tuning large language models to building robust training pipelines—and see your work directly improve the lives of those seeking care.

What you’ll do
--------------

*   Design, train, fine-tune, and evaluate machine learning and large language models (LLMs) creating hyper-personalized and highly effective Agentic AI Systems.
*   Apply context engineering and prompt engineering techniques to optimize model behavior, improve accuracy, and ensure reliability in production.
*   Develop rigorous evaluation standards that not only measure system performance but also establish the gold standard for efficacy, empathy, and clinical soundness in AI-powered agents.
*   Contribute to production-grade ML systems, including model deployment, monitoring, and optimization.
*   Leverage MLOps tools for experiment tracking, model management, and reproducibility.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate AI into real-world applications, including direct collaboration with clinical partners.
*   Proactively identify blockers, ask the right questions, and drive projects forward in ambiguous environments.
*   Mentor and learn within the team—sharing knowledge on ML techniques, fine-tuning and optimization strategies, and engineering practices.

**What does success look like?**  
----------------------------------

*   **30 days:** Ship a feature or new improvements to AI Agents to production
*   **60 days:** Lead a cross-team functionality development or propose a performance or scaling or testability improvement
*   **90 days:** Lead a system-level architectural improvement or completely new deliverable, propose a clear design, implementation, and rollout plan and execute towards the same.

Who You Are
-----------

*   Strong computer science and math foundations: data structures, algorithms, linear algebra, probability, optimization.
*   Solid understanding of machine learning fundamentals: supervised/unsupervised learning, regression/classification, reinforcement learning, loss functions, optimization methods, and neural network basics.
*   Proven experience fine-tuning large language models for production use.
*   Proficiency in Python and experience building software in production environments.
*   Proficiency with Deep Learning frameworks such as  PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras, JAX, etc. and ability to reason about model architectures and training processes.
*   Experience with production systems and MLOps — ability to design, debug, and maintain reliable software.
*   Demonstrated experience working with LLM/GenAI APIs and tools (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Hugging Face).
