**Drive Exceptional Golf Experiences on Victoria’s Stunning Surf Coast**

Are you passionate about golf and hospitality? Ready to take your career to the next level in one of Victoria’s most picturesque locations? Join RACV Torquay Resort as our Assistant Golf Operations Manager and help shape unforgettable experiences for our members and guests.

**Why RACV Torquay Resort?**

Nestled at the gateway to the iconic Great Ocean Road, RACV Torquay Resort offers breathtaking views, world-class facilities, and a vibrant team culture. As part of RACV—a purpose-driven organisation committed to enriching lives through travel, home services, and sustainable energy—you’ll be joining a team that values impact, innovation, and integrity.

**What You’ll Be Doing**

*   Lead the Game: Support daily golf operations including tee time scheduling, cart management, and course presentation.
*   Create Memorable Events: Coordinate corporate and social golf days, ensuring every detail is delivered flawlessly.
*   Engage & Grow: Assist with membership sales and guest engagement, helping to grow our golf community.
*   Build Connections: Strengthen relationships within the golf industry to enhance RACV’s reputation and identify new opportunities.
*   Innovate & Improve: Contribute ideas to elevate service standards, boost revenue, and streamline operations.

**What We’re Looking For**

*   5–10 years’ experience in a golf shop or golf operations environment.
*   Background in retail, hospitality, or sales.
*   Experience with POS systems and cash handling.
*   Strong communication, problem-solving, and customer service skills.
*   A passion for golf and delivering exceptional guest experiences.

**What You’ll Enjoy**

*   Discounts across RACV’s 10 resorts and clubs.
*   Generous staff benefits within the resort.
*   Free on-site parking.
*   Monthly employee recognition program.
*   Long-term career development opportunities.
*   A supportive, values-driven team culture.

**The RACV difference**

At RACV, we support your ambition. Discover a career where you're supported, valued, and empowered to grow - every step of the way.
