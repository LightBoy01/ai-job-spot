We're Celonis, the global leader in Process Mining technology and one of the world's fastest-growing SaaS firms. We believe there is a massive opportunity to unlock productivity by placing data and intelligence at the core of business processes - and for that, we need you to join us.

**The Team:**

As part of the **Benelux Value Engineering team**, you’ll join a group of data-driven business consultants helping major enterprises across industries like Manufacturing, Financial Services, Energy, and Consumer Goods.

You’ll work closely with our Value Engineers, Principals, and Partner VEs to help customers measure, realize, and scale value using the **Celonis Process Intelligence Platform**. You’ll report directly to **Maarten van der Borden, Director Value Engineering Benelux.**

**The Role:**

As an Intern Value Engineer, you’ll experience the full lifecycle of our customer engagements — from early discovery to quantified business value.   
You’ll analyze process and financial data, build insights with Celonis technology, and help prepare customer-facing materials for workshops, Proofs of Value (PoVs), and AI Value Plans.

You’ll collaborate across Sales, Partners, and the wider Value Engineering organization to turn data into decisions and insight into action.

**The work you’ll do:**

*   Analyze customer data to identify bottlenecks and value potential
*   Quantify business impact and build management-ready presentations  
    Support creation of **AI Value Plans** and **Value Realization reports**
*   Help run **Proofs of Value (PoVs)** and capture success stories
*   Maintain key inputs for the **Customer Health Score** dashboards
*   Prepare content for BNL reviews, QBRs, and Town Halls
*   Collaborate with **partners (Apolix, Ofi, Datalane, KPMG)** on deliverables
*   Contribute to internal tools and automation within the BNL Value Engineering Team

**The qualifications you need:**

*   Student in Business Engineering, Data Science, Management, or related field
*   Strong analytical mindset and proficiency in Excel, SQL, and Python
*   Interest in AI, digital transformation, and management consulting
*   Clear communication and storytelling skills
*   Business fluent in English (Dutch or French is a plus)
*   Available for a **6-month**, full-time internship based in **Amsterdam**

**About Us:**

Celonis makes processes work for people, companies and the planet. The Celonis Process Intelligence Platform uses industry-leading process mining and AI technology and augments it with business context to give customers a living digital twin of their business operation. It’s system-agnostic and without bias, and provides everyone with a common language for understanding and improving businesses. Celonis enables its customers to continuously realize significant value across the top, bottom, and green line. Celonis is headquartered in Munich, Germany, and New York City, USA, with more than 20 offices worldwide.
