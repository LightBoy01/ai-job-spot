### Description\nBRT HARTNER GmbH, an internationally active company with 30 years of experience in planning, production, and distribution of custom machinery for the sorting and recycling industry, is part of the Eggersmann Group, which employs over 1300 people worldwide. We are looking to strengthen our team with a dedicated Commercial Employee (m/f/d) for Accounting / Office Management at our Ibbenbüren location. This part-time role (20-25 hours/week) offers you the chance to actively shape the future of recycling and sorting technology with us.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Execute ongoing accounting duties, including debtor and creditor management and posting.\n- Prepare and process payment transactions and manage the dunning process.\n- Assist with month-end, quarter-end, and year-end financial closings.\n- Support tasks in human resources and process travel expense reports.\n- Perform general accounting and administrative tasks, as well as various assistant duties for the local management.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed commercial vocational training, for example, as a tax clerk (m/f/d).\n- Ideally, you have already gained professional experience within a technically oriented company.\n- Possess sound knowledge in accounting, specifically in financial reporting according to HGB, and experience with ERP or merchandise management systems.\n- Good command of accounting software.\n- Demonstrate a structured and reliable working approach with a high degree of personal initiative.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an experienced commercial professional to join an international leader in the recycling and sorting technology sector. The part-time nature (20-25 hours/week) offers excellent flexibility, while the comprehensive benefits package – including flexible hours, development opportunities, JobRad leasing, and fitness studio membership – underscores a strong commitment to employee well-being and growth. You'll be joining a motivated team in a pleasant working atmosphere, contributing directly to crucial financial and administrative processes that underpin the company's innovative work. If you're looking for a stable role where your accounting and administrative skills can make a real impact, and you appreciate a supportive culture that values work-life balance and continuous learning, this role at BRT HARTNER GmbH is definitely worth exploring.
