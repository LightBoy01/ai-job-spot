Join us at Ventura TRAVEL – a group of specialized travel brands that unleash the adventurer in every person!

[Ventura TRAVEL](http://www.venturatravel.org/) was founded to empower intrapreneurs to build up leading niche travel brands, focused on authentic and responsible travel experiences. We are strongly committed to sustainable tourism principles, to mitigate the environmental and cultural footprint of tourism. As a holding enterprise for other tourism companies and through our head office in Berlin, Germany, we take care of all the across company and international issues concerning all our subsidiary brands. All our brands actively live and share our core values to contribute to a business for the good cause.

_On a daily basis, you will_
----------------------------

*   Act as the central point of contact between our marketing team and our niche travel brands, ensuring alignment and coherence across markets and platforms.
*   Translate brand strategy into actionable marketing tasks: generate ideas, assign tasks (CU master-list), coordinate resources, and follow up to ensure timely execution.
*   Own brand coordination for the UK market launch and support key initiatives for our small-ship cruise platforms and AI-driven FIT specialist "Epic Trips".
*   Monitor ongoing campaigns and marketing projects: track progress, maintain deadlines, flag risks, ensure high quality of deliverables, and close projects once objectives are achieved.
*   Foster strong internal communication: brief brand teams and marketing specialists, share status updates, maintain documentation and support cross-functional collaboration.

_The basics you bring along_
----------------------------

*   Proven experience (2-4 years) in a coordination, account management or brand liaison role, ideally in a marketing or agency setting.
*   Strong project-management skills: ability to manage multiple parallel briefs, keep track of deadlines and deliverables, use task-management tools (e.g. ClickUp, Asana).
*   Solid understanding of marketing fundamentals and brand development (digital campaigns, content, CRM, performance marketing).
*   Excellent communication skills in English; comfort working with UK / English-speaking markets.
*   High adaptability and an open mindset: able to switch between different brands, platforms and target groups, and respond to change quickly.

_You can impress us even more with_
-----------------------------------

*   Experience working directly with Brands or Platforms.
*   Knowledge of the **UK** and/or **US**  tourism sector and/or cruise ship industry.
