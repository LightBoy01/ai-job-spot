**Job Summary:**
----------------

We are looking for a hands-on Production Engineer join the team to help build rack, running benchmark test, and troubleshoot the customized HPC/AI/Cloud infrastructure related products for our customers. Including the in-house total solution implementation and the on-site HPC project deployment. You will be part of a talented team of engineers that demonstrate technical competency, delivering mission critical infrastructure and ensuring high levels of availability, performance, and security.

**Essential Duties and Responsibilities:**
------------------------------------------

**Includes the following essential duties and responsibilities (other duties may also be assigned):**  
• Set up Server/Network/software Configuration.  
• Write customized automation scripts to increase system efficiency   
• Manage and monitor all installed systems and infrastructure  
• Install, configure, test, and maintain operating systems, application software and system management tools  
• Proactively ensure the highest levels of systems and infrastructure availability  
• Monitor and benchmark test application performance for potential bottlenecks, identify possible solutions  
• Draft and maintain technical documentations including drawing/diagram  
• Provide on-site software deployment/customer acceptance verification test

**Qualifications:**
-------------------

• BS/MS degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related subject  
• Minimum 1 year of proven working experience in installing, configuring, and troubleshooting Linux based environments  
• Solid scripting skills (e.g., shell scripts, Python, Perl)  
• Solid networking knowledge (OSI network layers, TCP/IP)  
• Solid language programming knowledge   
• Experience with workload/Resource Managers (e.g., any of Torque, PBS, Slurm)  
• Experience with virtualization and containerization   
• Travel required for on-site projects
