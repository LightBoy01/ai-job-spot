**Your role at Dynatrace**
--------------------------

Dynatrace is seeking a strategic, technically expert, and autonomous Senior Manager of Sales Analytics to lead our data-driven transformation across global sales operations. This is a high-impact role that will shape how Dynatrace leverages data, AI, and advanced analytics to drive sales performance, operational efficiency, and executive decision-making.

You’ll own the sales analytics roadmap, mentor a team of analysts, and partner across departments to deliver scalable, governed, and high-impact analytics. Success in this role requires strong leadership, hands-on technical expertise, fluency in AI concepts, and the ability to operate independently while influencing senior stakeholders.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Define and execute a scalable analytics strategy that delivers actionable insights to sales leadership and field teams.
    
*   Lead and develop a high-performing analytics team, balancing mentorship with hands-on execution.
    
*   Build and optimize advanced Power BI dashboards and predictive models for pipeline forecasting, consumption tracking, opportunity scoring, quota setting, and churn prediction.
    
*   Apply data-science techniques and explore LLMs and AI capabilities to enhance segmentation, forecasting, and enablement.
    
*   Collaborate with Business Systems, the Transformation Office, and Enterprise Data & Analytics teams to drive change management, align on SDLC practices, uphold governance standards, and build certified data products aligned to enterprise metrics and definitions.
    
*   Design frameworks that reduce noise, prioritize effectively, and support cross-functional alignment across Sales Ops, Marketing Ops, Finance, and Strategy.
    
*   Champion analytics adoption through enablement programs such as office hours and dashboard literacy sessions.
    
*   Present insights clearly to senior leadership, simplifying complex datasets for non-technical audiences.
    

**Location:** Remote (Eastern or Central Time Zones)

**What will help you succeed**
------------------------------

*   10+ years of experience in Sales Analytics, Data Science, or Business Intelligence, including at least 3 years in a leadership role.
    
*   Expert-level proficiency in Power BI, including DAX, data modeling, and performance optimization.
    
*   Strong working knowledge of Snowflake, SQL, and Python; experience with LLMs or AI applications is a plus.
    
*   Deep familiarity with Salesforce (SFDC), including reporting, data structures, and integration points.
    
*   Demonstrated success leading change management initiatives and implementing SDLC best practices in enterprise environments.
    
*   Solid understanding of sales processes, including renewals, pipeline management, key metrics, and forecasting methodologies.
    
*   Proven leadership and communication skills—able to build, mentor, and empower analytics teams while influencing executive stakeholders.
    
*   Bachelor’s degree in Analytics, Business, Statistics, or a related field; Master’s degree preferred.
