Join us as an Insight Consultant and turn your passion for human behavior into business strategy. You will be at the heart of our mission, analyzing qualitative data to uncover the ‘why’ behind consumer interactions and translating those findings into powerful, actionable recommendations. Your work will directly influence how leading brands connect with their audiences.
We are looking for someone who is collaborative and thriving when working with others to deliver exceptional outcomes. If you are passionate about how people interact with their peers, brands and the world, and determined to use those insights to shape the future of great brands, you are the one we are looking for.
_As we are part of a global team, please submit your CV in English._
Key Responsibilities
--------------------
**Impactful client delivery**
*   You analyze, interpret, and synthesize qualitative data from online communities, interviews, and group discussions to uncover deep consumer insights.
*   You collaborate closely with local moderators and internal stakeholders to ensure smooth project execution and high-quality data collection.
*   You collaborate with (senior) team members to come up with compelling conclusions and recommendations for clients.
*   You are eager to move projects forward. You assist in the planning, set-up and management of qualitative projects, with support from senior team members.
*   You actively use AI to work more efficiently and effectively. You learn how to use AI in a business relevant way, to contextualize your work and add value to the client.
*   You understand how to use behavioral frameworks and models. With guidance you know how to apply these in your analysis and reporting.
*   You gain experience by presenting internally and support in client presentations.
**Account and commercial management**
*   You gain experience with and exposure to clients by supporting senior consultants with project related tasks and communications.
*   You have a good understanding of the client’s needs and business objectives, you dare to challenge and think along.
Skills, Knowledge and Expertise
-------------------------------
*   A Masters Degree in relevant field (e.g., social sciences, psychology, business, or economics).
*   **Curiosity and Eagerness**: A desire to learn and understand complex human behaviour.
*   **Attention to Detail**: Carefully handle data and research materials.
*   **Communication**: Clear and pro-active communication within the team and towards clients.
*   **Adaptability**: Willingness to adapt to changing project requirements and rapidly evolving industry.
*   **Analytical**: A high affinity with data interpretation and analysis.
*   **Innovative**: Actively experimenting with AI, eager to learn and use new technologies to bring added value for the client.
*   **Collaborative**: You love to achieve and win together!
