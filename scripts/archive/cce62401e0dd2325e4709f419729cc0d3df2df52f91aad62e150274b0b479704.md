**Associate Principal, IT Applications – HCM**

We are seeking a highly skilled and motivated Associate Principal, IT Applications – HCM to join our IT Applications team. This role is responsible for managing and supporting the configuration, maintenance, and optimization of **enterprise-level HCM systems**, with a preferred experience in **SAP SuccessFactors****.**

**Key Responsibilities:**

*   Lead the configuration, administration, and enhancement of **enterprise-level HCM systems**, including but not limited to:

*   Employee Central (Core HR)
*   Performance & Goals
*   Succession & Development
*   Compensation Management
*   Recruiting & Onboarding

*   Collaborate with HR stakeholders to ensure that system configuration aligns with HR policies, business requirements, and compliance standards.
*   Identify opportunities for process improvement, automation, and optimization of HR technologies.
*   Troubleshoot system issues, provide resolution, and coordinate with vendors as needed.
*   Contribute to IT strategy and roadmaps for HCM applications, ensuring alignment with business objectives.
*   Preferred to have technical expertise in system integrations with payroll, Time Management applications, and other enterprise applications.

**Qualifications and Experience:**

*   Strong technical experience in the **configuration of enterprise-level HCM systems****.**
*   Direct experience with **SAP SuccessFactors configuration** is preferred, but not mandatory. Candidates with a proven track record of implementing or supporting similar enterprise HCM platforms are encouraged to apply.
*   Solid understanding of the **HCM life cycle**, including Core HR, Talent Management, Compensation, and related processes.
*   Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills with the ability to interact effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
*   Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, HR Systems, or related field.

This role offers the opportunity to lead **global HCM initiatives** and partner closely with HR to enable digital transformation and operational excellence.
