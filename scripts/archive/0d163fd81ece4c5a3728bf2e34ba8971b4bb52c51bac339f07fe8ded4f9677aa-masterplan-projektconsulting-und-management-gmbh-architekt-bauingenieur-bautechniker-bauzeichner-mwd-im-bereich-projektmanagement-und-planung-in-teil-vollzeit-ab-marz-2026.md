### Description
MasterPlan Projektconsulting und -management GmbH is a nationwide company based in Munich, specializing in project consulting and management. For over 35 years, MasterPlan has been a trusted partner in overseeing, planning, and realizing diverse real estate projects. This includes commercial and office buildings, hotels, residential properties, and special-purpose real estate, guiding them from the initial concept through commissioning and beyond.

### Responsibilities
As a key member of our team, you will contribute to challenging and varied projects, focusing on:
*   Project control and management
*   Consulting for project developers and clients
*   Existing property management
*   Urban planning initiatives
*   Planning phases 1-4 according to HOAI (German Fee Structure for Architects and Engineers)

### Qualifications
To succeed in this role, you should possess:
*   A successfully completed degree in Architecture or Civil Engineering, or a comparable qualification (e.g., building technician/draftsman).
*   At least 3 years of relevant professional experience.
*   Very good German language skills, both spoken and written.
*   A strong understanding of constructive and design principles.
*   Proficiency in MS-Office products.
*   Excellent knowledge and practical experience with ArchiCAD.
*   An independent and goal-oriented approach to work.
*   An authentic and professional demeanor when interacting with clients, developers, and other stakeholders.

### Author's Take
This is an excellent opportunity for a dedicated professional looking to contribute to significant real estate projects within a stable and well-established company. MasterPlan stands out with its commitment to employee well-being, offering a permanent contract, flexible and family-friendly working hours, and the option for home office. The culture is characterized by a flat hierarchy, open communication, and a strong team spirit, described as \"almost like a big family.\" Furthermore, the company actively supports professional and personal development through continuous training and fosters a vibrant work environment with regular team events and social activities. If you value a collaborative, supportive, and growth-oriented workplace, MasterPlan offers a genuinely rewarding career path.
