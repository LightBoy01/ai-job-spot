### Description

At Medtronic, you'll be part of a global team dedicated to solving humanity's most challenging health problems. This role, as a Training & Education Digital Expert, sits within our innovative Virtual Academy (VA) team. Our mission is to transform how we engage with customers by expanding our virtual training and education (T&E) footprint. You will be at the heart of this transformation, responsible for the high-quality operation of the T&E ecosystem for customer training. Your work will directly empower healthcare professionals with the skills they need, advancing healthcare access and equity for all.

### Responsibilities

- Assess training gaps across Operational Units, co-developing annual content plans to create Blended Learning Pathways (BLPs) and other vital training materials.
- Apply your instructional design skills and creativity to develop engaging digital content.
- Manage the Learning/Content Management Systems (LMS/CMS) to ensure seamless delivery and organization of digital content.
- Oversee training data management and generate insightful reports to measure impact and inform future strategy.
- Stay on the cutting edge of technology, leveraging your understanding and experience with AI practices to enhance our training and education programs.

### Qualifications

- A Bachelor’s Degree in a relevant field; experience in the Pharma or Medtech industry is highly preferred.
- Proven ability to work with minimal supervision while managing large-scale projects or processes.
- A strong problem-solver capable of providing and implementing effective solutions.
- Excellent communication and negotiation skills, with the ability to influence decision-making and build consensus.
- A self-driven and highly motivated personality with a passion for continuous learning and development.
- A reliable and collaborative team member who thrives in a team-oriented environment.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "Beyond managing the T&E ecosystem, how will I see my work directly contribute to Medtronic's mission of alleviating pain and restoring health?"**

That's really the core of why we're so passionate about this work. You're not just managing a system; you're building the bridge between our groundbreaking medical technology and the surgeons and clinicians who use it to save lives. Think of it this way: when you develop a more effective virtual training module, a doctor in a remote hospital can learn a complex new surgical technique without ever leaving their community. That means their patients get access to a higher standard of care faster. The data you manage isn't just numbers; it's a direct measure of how many professionals we're empowering. Your work directly scales our ability to restore health, making life-saving knowledge accessible to everyone, everywhere.

**A candidate might also ask: "The description mentions being 'tech-savvy' with AI. How much real opportunity is there to innovate and experiment with new technologies in this role?"**

That's a great question, and the answer is: a lot. Our Virtual Academy team functions like an internal R&D lab for education. We're actively encouraged to look beyond just maintaining the status quo. The mention of AI isn't a future-state wish; we're currently exploring AI-driven personalized learning paths and using predictive analytics to identify training needs before they become critical gaps. If you have an idea for a new tool, a better workflow, or a pilot project using emerging tech, you'll find a very receptive audience. We're looking for someone who won't just run the system, but who will challenge it and help us build the future of medical education. Your curiosity and drive to innovate will be your greatest assets here.
