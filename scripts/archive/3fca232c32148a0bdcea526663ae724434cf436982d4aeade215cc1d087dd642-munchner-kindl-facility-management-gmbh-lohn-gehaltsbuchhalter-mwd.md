### Description

Are numbers at the core of what you do? Do you enjoy working hand-in-hand with dedication and initiative to optimize and develop financial topics in collaboration with a highly motivated team?

Are you seeking an exciting challenge in finance where you can fully leverage your expertise in HGB (German Commercial Code)?

In other words, you are:

*   **Organized**
*   **Goal-oriented**
*   **Number-savvy**
*   **Managerial (in terms of tasks/projects)**

Then we, Münchner Kindl Facility Management GmbH, a modern company acting as a comprehensive service provider in all areas of facility management, have exactly the right job for you. We serve not only a wide range of industries in Munich and across Germany, but also various renowned companies from the hospitality, retail, and automotive sectors. Consequently, a diverse and large team of employees awaits you.

### Responsibilities

*   Perform preparatory accounting tasks
*   Participate in regular analyses and forecasts, as well as key figure systems
*   Manage and maintain the dunning system
*   Act as a point of contact for external service providers (tax advisors, banks, health insurance companies)
*   Reconcile and post accounts receivable and accounts payable
*   Weekly creation of open item lists
*   Manage purchasing activities
*   Create purchasing analyses and overviews
*   Act as a point of contact for our goods service providers
*   Control and manage the company's vehicle fleet
*   Contribute to the optimization of processes and the further development of existing controlling instruments

### Qualifications

*   Completed commercial training, ideally as a Tax Specialist, with experience in financial accounting
*   Experience with DATEV software is an advantage
*   Possess excellent numerical understanding and a quick grasp
*   Ideally, you have gained professional experience in a renowned tax firm or auditing company
*   Very good MS-Office skills are a prerequisite
*   Your meticulous working style distinguishes you – you are only satisfied when the numbers are correct
*   Demonstrate additional initiative, flexibility, and the ability to find solutions in close collaboration with colleagues
