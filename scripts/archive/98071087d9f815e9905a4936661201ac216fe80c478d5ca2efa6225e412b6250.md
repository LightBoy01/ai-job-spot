Moloco is a machine learning company empowering organizations of all sizes to grow and unlock the full value of their unique first-party data, elevating the traditional path to performance advertising. While the largest technology companies have proven the speed and scale of ad targeting using data, that same robust performance powered by machine learning has previously been unavailable beyond their platforms. 

That's where Moloco steps in. With Moloco's powerful combination of cutting-edge machine learning technologies, we play a unique and visible role in shaping the digital economy, all while enabling companies to stay independent and scale.

**The Impact You’ll Be Contributing to Moloco:** 

As a **Senior Strategic Growth Manager** you will partner with MCM (Moloco Commerce Media) customers to help them build out a retail media business. You will lead customer relationships with large retailers and marketplaces, functioning as the product and go-to market expert, educating and advising on business setup and growth, retail media strategy, campaign management and optimization, and how to scale a retail media business.

**The Opportunity:** 

*   Enable our retail and marketplace customers to be successful by growing their ads business; crafting GTM strategies with our MCM clients to help drive incremental ad revenue growth.
*   Identify growth levers and product improvement opportunities.
*   Build and manage strategic customer partnerships with deep understanding of customer verticals.
*   Educate business users and advertisers on performance ads values and operations.
*   Identify actionable growth initiatives and execute flawlessly to achieve goals.
*   Enable decision-making with a data-driven strategy and valuable insights.
*   Translate complex data analysis into simple language for clear communications.
*   Capture customers’ needs and devise optimized and productive solutions.
*   Lead multiple customer meetings and regular business reviews.

**How Do I Know if the Role is Right For Me?** 

*   6-10 years of relevant work experience, ideally gained at a strategy consulting firm and/or Ad-tech company
*   Tech industry and performance advertising/Ad-tech experience. Retail media experience and/or exposure preferred.
*   Outstanding communication skills to build trustworthy relationships with customers.
*   Great co-working skills with multiple stakeholders to accomplish business goals.
*   Experience managing and executing strategic programs.
*   Excellence in problem-solving, logical and out-of-box thinking to meet customer needs.
*   Rigor with data (exposure to ML-based products is a plus).
*   Experience or interest in understanding ML and AI products and communicating value and outcomes to stakeholders
*   Comfort working in a 0-1 environment with willingness to embrace ambiguity.
*   Ownership and passion for continuous growth and creating real value.
*   Detail oriented, and yet willing to embrace ambiguity
*   Ability to adapt to a fast-growing global company.
