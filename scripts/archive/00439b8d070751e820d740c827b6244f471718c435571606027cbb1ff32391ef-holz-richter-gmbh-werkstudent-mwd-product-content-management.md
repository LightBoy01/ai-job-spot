### Description\nAre you eager to gain e-commerce experience and actively contribute to how products are presented online? Then join our new **Product Content Management Team (PCMT)** at Holz-Richter and help us prepare high-quality, informative, and customer-oriented product data for our online shop **casando**. This role offers exciting tasks within a growing online shop, providing valuable insights into a modern e-commerce business. We offer flexible working hours compatible with your studies, the chance to introduce creative ideas and shape processes, and the opportunity to take on significant responsibility in a new team from the outset. Fair compensation and home office possibilities after onboarding are also part of the package.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Support in refining product data.\n- Creation of informative, appealing product listings.\n- Maintenance and optimization of product attributes (dimensions, material, variants, etc.).\n- Ensuring data quality: completeness, consistency, and customer-friendly preparation.\n- Collaboration on content guidelines for product presentation.\n- Collaboration with UX, Marketing, SEO, Marketplace teams, and Category Management to ensure a more consistent and better user experience.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Ongoing studies in Business Administration, E-Commerce, Media/Communication Sciences, or similar.\n- Interest in e-commerce, product data, and content management.\n- Good writing and communication skills.\n- Analytical thinking, meticulous work ethic, and enjoyment of structured data.\n- Proficient in MS Office (especially Excel).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent entry-level opportunity for a student eager to dive into e-commerce and product content management. You'll play a crucial role in shaping the online presence of a growing shop, gaining practical experience, and contributing to a new, dynamic team. The flexible hours and home office option make it ideal for balancing studies with professional development. You'll gain hands-on experience in data quality, content creation, and cross-functional collaboration, making a tangible impact from day one.
