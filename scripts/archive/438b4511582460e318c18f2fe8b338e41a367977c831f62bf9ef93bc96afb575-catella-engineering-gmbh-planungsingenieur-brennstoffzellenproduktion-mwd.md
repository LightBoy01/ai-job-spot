### Description
At Catella Engineering, we specialize in connecting talented engineers with pioneering projects in the automotive sector, focusing on the innovation and assurance of new vehicle technologies. In this project role (Arbeitnehmerüberlassung), you will significantly contribute to shaping the future of manufacturing by applying your expertise in Manufacturing Engineering. Specifically, you will support the planning and optimization of **fuel cell production**, ensuring that innovative technologies transition efficiently and reliably into series production. This is your chance to deploy your skills where the future of mobility is brought to the road.

### Responsibilities
*   Manage release processes for complete seats and components, including coordination and preparation of documentation and release procedures for component and variant bills of material.
*   Verify and correct project-phase-compliant release statuses.
*   Handle change management: create change requests in collaboration with component managers.
*   Coordinate the entire change process from initial recording to implementation.
*   Organize a data hub, including archiving data statuses in respective project phases.
*   Create technical documentation and release documents based on supplier test and inspection reports.
*   Engage in interdisciplinary collaboration with internal partners from development, planning, quality, as well as external suppliers.

### Qualifications
*   Completed degree or comparable education in a technical field.
*   Experience in the administration of development processes, ideally in seat systems and components.
*   Profound knowledge of documentation and release processes within the Daimler Group.
*   Strong teamwork skills, an open personality, and excellent communication abilities.
*   Analytical thinking, a structured approach, as well as self-initiative and goal orientation.
