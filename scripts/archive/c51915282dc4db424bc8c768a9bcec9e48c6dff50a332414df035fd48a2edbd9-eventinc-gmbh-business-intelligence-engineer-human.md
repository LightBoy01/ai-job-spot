### Description\nOur production data resides in various databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite). We are looking for a dedicated Data Expert to build the connective tissue that unifies these diverse data sources into a single, queryable foundation for critical business decisions. This role, reporting directly to the Head of Engineering, is an integral part of our Engineering team. You will collaborate closely with Engineering and Product to ensure data is accessible, reliable, and actionable across all departments. You will own the full data pipeline, from raw production data to delivering actionable business insights.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Extract & Transform**: Connect our production databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite) to our BI stack (Metabase, Salesforce) and write efficient, scalable data pipelines.\n- **Build Custom Tooling**: Extend Metabase and Salesforce with custom JavaScript components to provide specific event management insights beyond off-the-shelf solutions.\n- **Make Data Usable**: Design impactful visualizations and data models that answer key business questions, such as project profitability, time loss in delivery, and resource forecasting.\n- **Ship in Go**: Develop clean, maintainable Go scripts for ETL processes, data validation, and API integrations within our Go-centric data infrastructure.\n- **Explore & Experiment**: Utilize Elixir (Livebook) for rapid prototyping and Rust for performance-critical transformations (optional but encouraged), treating data as an evolving product.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 3+ years of experience working with relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL) in production environments.\n- Strong programming skills in Go, with a proven track record of building backend services or data pipelines.\n- Solid JavaScript experience for custom visualizations or extending BI tools.\n- Experience designing end-to-end data models and ETL pipelines.\n- Demonstrated ability to translate complex business requirements into clear data solutions.\n- Conversational German skills and strong communication skills in English (company language).\n- Exposure to Elixir or Rust (nice-to-have).\n- Experience with Metabase or similar BI tools (nice-to-have).\n- Understanding of event-driven architectures or time-series data (nice-to-have).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting Mid-Senior Data Expert role for a Go-savvy engineer who thrives on ownership and making a tangible impact. You'll be central to transforming raw data into strategic insights for a company that values collaboration, innovation, and employee well-being. The hybrid work model, flexible hours, learning budget, and social team events reflect a supportive culture. The requirement for conversational German adds a unique intercultural dimension to an otherwise English-speaking, technically challenging role focused on building robust data foundations.
