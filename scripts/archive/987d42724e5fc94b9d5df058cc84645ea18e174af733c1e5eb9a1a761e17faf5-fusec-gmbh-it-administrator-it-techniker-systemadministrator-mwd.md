This role involves both remote and on-site work.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Manage, install, and continuously improve the IT infrastructure for our clients.\n- Provide 1st/2nd level IT support, including remote and on-site assistance for ships at berth.\n- Monitor IT systems and proactively identify issues to ensure smooth operations.\n- Develop tailored IT solutions to meet evolving client requirements.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed vocational training or a degree in IT, or comparable professional experience.\n- Strong IT knowledge in MS Windows Server, Active Directory, DNS, DHCP.\n- Knowledge of network administration and network security.\n- Desired skills include VMWare, vSphere, Veeam, Linux (Debian).\n- Passion for technology, high willingness to learn, and loyalty.\n- Good English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a dedicated IT professional to join a maritime IT service provider in Rostock. The role offers the chance to work with diverse IT infrastructures, provide crucial support to clients, including on-site ship visits, and contribute to developing innovative solutions. The company truly values its employees, offering a permanent contract, competitive salary, generous vacation time (up to 30 days), holiday bonuses, travel allowances, and flexible additional benefits like occupational retirement provisions, childcare, or gym memberships. With modern offices by the water, it's a stable and supportive environment for someone passionate about technology and eager to learn.
