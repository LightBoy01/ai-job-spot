### Description\nThis Lead Analyst position is a critical role focused on empowering a team of analysts to deliver significant business impact. You will provide strategic direction, bring clarity to complex problems, and coach your team on technical skills, prioritization, and critical thinking. Your mission is to foster a data-driven culture where evidence guides decisions and impact is the ultimate measure of success, ensuring the team's insights drive meaningful change across the organization.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Guide analysts through ambiguous challenges by creating clarity and defining direction.\n*   Help the team focus on high-value work and avoid low-impact distractions.\n*   Review analyses with an emphasis on clarity, rigor, and business relevance, fostering analysts' judgment.\n*   Stay hands-on with complex analyses, experiments, and strategic deep dives.\n*   Build shared templates, frameworks, and tools to raise the overall quality and consistency of analytics work.\n*   Collaborate with leaders in product, marketing, finance, and operations to define meaningful metrics and align on goals.\n*   Champion strong tracking, documentation, and data modeling practices for a reliable data foundation.\n*   Provide decision-ready insights that include clear recommendations, risks, timelines, and accountable owners.\n*   Create an environment where curiosity, learning, and knowledge sharing are part of daily work.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   **Experience**: 5+ years in data analytics with at least 2+ years in leadership, mentoring, or team guiding roles. Experience across product, marketing, finance, or operations analytics is valuable.\n*   **Leadership and Impact Orientation**: Strong ability to prioritize, structure problems, and focus on high-impact work. Skilled in building alignment and fostering an outcome-driven culture.\n*   **Analytical Skills**: Excellent judgment in problem framing, experiment design, and analytical approach selection. Comfortable reviewing deep dive analyses, models, and experiments from both technical and business perspectives.\n*   **Technical Proficiency**: Robust SQL skills and strong experience in Python. Familiarity with data modeling concepts, experimentation frameworks, dashboarding, and modern data platforms. Able to critically evaluate data quality, instrumentation, and analytical code.\n*   **Communication and Collaboration**: Clear and confident communicator who can influence stakeholders, provide direction, and navigate trade-offs with crisp, decision-oriented insights.\n*   **Work Approach**: Thrives in evolving environments, proactive in identifying gaps and opportunities, and values curiosity, mentorship, and building systemic improvements.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an experienced Lead Analyst passionate about both hands-on analytical challenges and strategic team development. The company culture is highly supportive, emphasizing work-life balance with 30 days of vacation, flexible hybrid work in Hamburg (Mondays/Fridays remote, Tuesdays-Thursdays in office), and a generous annual education budget of €1,500. Beyond the core benefits, they offer unique perks like \"Workation Opportunities,\" wellness programs, and a diverse international team. This role is perfect for someone eager to drive significant business impact through data, mentor a growing team, and continuously develop their own skills in a dynamic, people-centric environment.
