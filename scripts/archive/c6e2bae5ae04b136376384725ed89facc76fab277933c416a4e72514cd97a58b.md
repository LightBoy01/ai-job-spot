**LCI - Energy - CAS - Renewables & Clean Tech**

**Analyst / Corporate Analyst / Senior Corporate Analyst**

**Are you eager to be part of a team at the forefront of the energy transition, working with the largest companies in the energy sector?**

We are now looking for an analyst to cover our customer portfolio focusing on Renewables and New Energies & Utilities.

In the **Energy Division**, you will be part of a global team of ~80 people. We represent an internationally oriented business area in DNB covering most industry segments in the energy sector, with focus on Oil & Gas, Renewables and other energy sources of the future.

We aim to be the preferred financial partner for our customers, enabling growth opportunities as well as providing support in challenging times. In addition to maintaining strong industry competence, we need to stay updated on technological developments and international regulations, as well as understanding key risk elements.

The **Customer Analysis Section (CAS)** in Oslo consists of ~18 analysts with diverse backgrounds and experiences working towards the various industry segments. As an Analyst/Corporate Analyst you will work in teams in close cooperation with the client managers and client advisors for the respective clients/transactions, often in close cooperation with colleagues across other areas of the bank. In addition to an interest in financial analysis and credit products, good cooperation and communication skills are key for the position. We are looking for candidates that are professionally curious, take initiative and drive processes.

The Customer Analysis section has a good working environment with a mix of experienced analysts and young talents. The section has a varying workload and can at times have demanding time limits, but we do emphasize flexibility and a good work-life balance.

**This is an opportunity to develop your career within Energy focusing on:**

*   Analysis of customers’ financial and non-financial risk, including ESG-risk and how trends within sustainable development are affecting industries and our customers.
*   Driving the process, prepare, and present credit memos for decision makers.
*   Gaining and sharing insight into customers’ markets, challenges, and opportunities through preparing market analysis on various industry segments.
*   Working in client teams to provide the best financing solutions to our customers.

**We want to hear from commercial and team-oriented persons with:**

*   Relevant experience from accounting, relevant industries, finance or similar
*   Solid understanding of and interest in financial modelling and analysis
*   Good cooperation and communication skills
*   Higher education at master's degree level with good results
*   Fluent in Norwegian and English

Please enclose a brief motivation letter together with your CV and transcripts. Applications will be assessed on an ongoing basis.
