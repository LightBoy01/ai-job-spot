### Description
This company is at the forefront of AI Motion Design/Video Technology, building the first AI motion designers that create professional, code-based animations through conversation. Unlike traditional tools, their solution offers the precision of professional software with unparalleled simplicity, aimed at solving the most painful and expensive parts of video creation. This full-time, on-site Founding Engineer role is based in Berlin, Germany, with comprehensive relocation support offered.

As a key member of this YC-backed startup, you will join an elite team to redefine how millions of users create video. This position offers a life-changing equity stake and is ideal for an individual passionate about building a massive, impactful product from scratch, with direct influence on both the technological direction and company culture.

### Responsibilities
As a Founding Engineer, you will be instrumental in shaping the product and technology stack, taking on a wide range of high-impact responsibilities:

*   Partner directly with the founders to conceptualize, design, and deliver user-centric product solutions from initial idea to production deployment.
*   Assume end-to-end ownership for the development of full-stack features, ensuring scalable and robust architectural design.
*   Architect, build, and continuously optimize agentic LLM systems, incorporating advanced techniques such as tool-calling, multi-step reasoning, and sophisticated communication protocols.
*   Design and implement comprehensive LLM evaluation frameworks, featuring automated benchmarks, human-in-the-loop validation, and A/B testing methodologies.
*   Proactively optimize LLM inference performance, cost efficiency, and latency across various model architectures.
*   Develop and enhance a high-performance, proprietary motion graphics engine.
*   Drive critical architectural decisions that will underpin and support scalable growth, preparing the product for millions of users.

### Qualifications
The ideal candidate is an ambitious, growth-oriented engineer with a strong bias for action and a desire to build something truly transformative.

**Required Experience & Skills:**

*   Minimum of 3 years of professional work experience, though the company prioritizes trajectory and impact over strict credentials.
*   Proficiency in Full Stack Development, with a core technical stack that currently includes TypeScript, React.js, Next.js, PostgreSQL, and experience with cloud platforms like GCP/AWS. Experience with proprietary motion graphics engines is a plus. The team values learning velocity and adopts new technologies as best serves user and product goals.
*   English language proficiency at C1 level.

**What Makes You a Great Fit:**

*   **Hunger & Drive:** You're not just ambitious but deeply obsessed with the vision of building something massive and impactful.
*   **Entrepreneurial Spirit:** You have a proven track record of building things and demand significant ownership over your work.
*   **Comfort with Ambiguity:** You thrive in environments without rigid roadmaps or dedicated PMs, seeing problems as opportunities to solve.
*   **Growth Mindset:** You're committed to continuous personal and professional growth, understanding it's essential for scaling the company.
*   **High Performance:** You approach work with the discipline of an athlete, focusing on consistent improvement and doing "whatever it takes to win."
*   **On-site Commitment:** You are keen to work in-person in Berlin, with full relocation support provided if needed.
*   **Potential Over Pedigree:** The company hires for "slope, not intercept," valuing future trajectory and potential contribution more than past credentials.
