### Description

This is an exciting Mid-Senior Digital Project Manager role (Professional Level) with a leading digital agency known for its deep UX expertise and international client portfolio. We're looking for a sharp, inventive, and forward-thinking individual to join our team, ideally bringing 3-5 years of focused digital project management experience. This position offers a competitive salary range of **€50,000 - €65,000 annually**, depending on your background.

Our agency, comprising over 100 dedicated professionals, prides itself on delivering high-impact, long-term B2B and B2C projects for renowned international clients – no 'fluff' projects here. UX is deeply embedded in our DNA, championed by one of our founders who is a university professor in the field. We are pragmatic, down-to-earth, approachable, and truly human-centric. Every team member is operationally involved and close to the action. We embrace your individuality with a 'come as you are' philosophy, ensuring you never have to switch between a 'business self' and a 'private self.' We are also deeply passionate about technology, leveraging current innovations like AI for interactive client projects.

**Work Model:** After an initial, fully organized 2-4 week onboarding period at our office, you are welcome to work fully remote from anywhere within Germany. A part-time option (80%) is also negotiable.

### Responsibilities

As a Digital Project Manager, you will be the linchpin connecting our clients and our talented internal teams. Your core responsibilities will include:

*   **Client Communication & Project Management:** Engage keenly, resourcefully, proactively, and with humor, communicating at eye level with clients. You'll discuss projects, ensure everything runs according to plan, and celebrate successful project conclusions with a metaphorical 'banquet.'
*   **Campaign Focus:** Spearhead digital campaigns for leading FMCG brands, some with an international scope. These involve modern, contemporary, and cool activations, often integrated with music events, festivals, and sponsorships.
*   **Digital Measures:** Manage purely digital initiatives such as microsites for branding/marketing games, or other consumer activation & engagement solutions like feature-rich loyalty apps, including music streaming functionalities.
*   **Project Leadership:** Take partial responsibility for B2C projects, sometimes in collaboration with Senior PMs, ensuring a strong communicative role.
*   **Team Steering:** Guide and coordinate our internal teams across Design, UX, and Development.
*   **Comprehensive PM Duties:** Oversee project execution, manage calculations & effort estimations, handle external service provider management, and ensure robust project controlling.

This role is also a great fit if you currently hold an 'Account Manager' title and have a significant project management component in your current role that you enjoy.

### Qualifications

To thrive in this role, you should bring:

*   **Digital Campaign Expertise:** Proven expertise or extensive experience with digital campaign-focused projects is essential.
*   **Professional PM Experience:** A minimum of 3 years of professional Project Management experience in the digital environment. You are accustomed to juggling multiple tasks and methodically pursuing projects – PM should not be 'just a side task' for you.
*   **Agency Background (Plus):** Experience from an online marketing agency or a communication/advertising agency with a digital unit would be a distinct advantage.
*   **Digital Brand Management:** A clear understanding and practical experience with digital brand management.
*   **Language Skills:** Excellent command of English, both written and spoken, is a must-have as it is the official team language (German is also spoken).

**What We Offer Beyond the Basics:**

*   **Flexibility:** Full remote work possible within Germany after onboarding, with negotiable part-time options (80%).
*   **Positive Atmosphere:** Join a team of professional and genuinely nice people; we believe in creating a very pleasant work environment.
*   **Personalization:** Have specific ideas or needs? We encourage you to discuss what's important to you.
