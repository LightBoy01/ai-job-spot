Join us to drive positive, lasting change that moves missions and the government forward!

**You Are:**

As a GenAI for Cloud Application Migration SME Senior Manager, you will play a pivotal role in guiding the client through the complex process of transitioning their applications and infrastructure to AWS. Your expertise will be instrumental in designing and implementing cloud-native solutions, with a strong focus on leveraging AWS services to optimize performance, scalability, and cost-efficiency.  This role demands a deep understanding of cloud migration strategies, including rehosting, re-platforming, and refactoring, as well as the ability to troubleshoot and resolve challenges that arise during the migration process. To excel in this position, you will need hands-on experience with tools and technologies such as GitLab for version control and CI/CD pipelines, use of AI tools like GenWizard and Code Assist to convert legacy applications to modern architecture as well as proficiency in cloud native & microservices application architectures .

**The Work:**

*   Will collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to refactor legacy applications, ensuring they are modernized and fully aligned with cloud best practices
*   Expert hands on experience with programming languages like python and AWS services like EC2, S3, Lambda, EKS, ECS and RDS will be critical to your success, as will your ability to integrate these services into seamless, scalable solutions.
*   Exposure to modern development practices, including AI tools, DevOps methodologies and infrastructure as code, will further enhance your ability to deliver impactful results
*   Your background should include a proven track record of leading cloud migration initiatives, with a focus on application refactoring and modernization using AFS AI tool
*   A strong foundation in cloud-native development is essential, as you will be expected to design and implement microservices architectures and containerized solutions

**Here's What You Need:**

*   Bachelors Degree or 10+ years of industry experience
*   Experience with Generative AI applications for application modernization, agentic frameworks, reverse engineering and forward engineering
*   Experience with Github copilot and AFS AI tools to rapidly assess and accelerate the application migration to the AWS
*   Proficiency with both backend and front end architecture
*   Experience with of modern JavaScript libraries like React, Vue, or Angular and web based applications architectures

**Bonus Points If:**

*   The ability to work closely with diverse set of senior client leadership is essential to provide them confidence in our ability to deliver, present assessment and delivery options, and share progress on delivery. This role offers the opportunity to make a significant impact by driving innovation and enabling organizations to fully realize the benefits of cloud computing
*   Experience working in agile environments and collaborating with diverse teams will be invaluable, as will your ability to communicate complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders
*   Strong analytical mindset and problem-solving skills will help you navigate the challenges of cloud migration, while your commitment to staying current with emerging cloud technologies will ensure you remain a trusted expert in the field

**The Extras:**

*   US Citizenship
*   The ability to obtain and maintain a government clearance may be required for this role
