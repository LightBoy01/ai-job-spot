### Description\nGreenPocket is an agile, green startup specializing in energy management and visualization software, actively contributing to a sustainable, climate-neutral future. With a team of 35 talented colleagues, our mission is to make energy consumption management intuitive through AI-driven software, making it a matter of course for everyone – from companies to private households. This empowers energy providers and corporate clients to effectively reduce both energy consumption and CO2 footprints, transforming the digitalization of the energy sector into a real opportunity. This role offers an opportunity to plan marketing campaigns, coordinate social media, and prepare events within a supportive and dynamic environment. You'll benefit from an intensive onboarding process, continuous learning opportunities, flexible working hours with home office options, regular team events, and a vibrant startup atmosphere.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Social Media Marketing:** Independently manage our social media channels, monitor trends and benchmarks, create evaluations, and proactively develop content.\n*   **Campaign Management:** Actively support the creation of specific target group lists, newsletters, and other marketing materials like PowerPoint presentations for product campaigns.\n*   **Content Creation:** Create postings (including images), blog posts, product texts, and support the implementation of our podcast.\n*   **Event Management:** Be a key part of the planning, preparation, and follow-up for trade fair appearances and specialist congresses, and independently arrange fair appointments for our Sales team.\n*   **Online Marketing:** Monitor the effectiveness of Google Ads and track our monthly inbound leads.\n*   **Brand Building:** Shape the GreenPocket brand through individual, creative projects and foster our growth.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Currently studying Media or Communication Sciences, Event Management, Business Administration (optimally with a focus on Marketing), or a comparable field.\n*   Ideally, you have already gained initial practical experience in marketing or event management – but this is not a must!\n*   You enjoy creating and formulating engaging content.\n*   You are characterized by a quick comprehension and an analytical mindset.\n*   You are communicative and proactively gather the input you need.\n*   You demonstrate a high degree of teamwork and self-initiative.\n*   You are proficient in using common MS Office applications.\n*   You have very good German and English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic entry-level opportunity for a marketing enthusiast eager to make an impact in the green tech sector.
