**Who we are:**

For 75 years, NATO’s mission has been to preserve peace and security in the Alliance for nearly one billion citizens. The NATO Communications and Information Agency (NCIA) and its predecessors have worked tirelessly in providing the means that enable the connectedness and togetherness that keep our Alliance strong. We are the NCI Agency, a team of 3000 civilian and military staff in 29 locations throughout Europe, North America and Asia.

Our technology and cyber experts allow NATO to conduct critical operations, protect NATO’s airspace, make data-driven decisions, defend against cyber-attacks, secure NATO networks and maintain superiority in space. This is all possible because of our greatest force, our people. In order to keep this edge we aim to hire, train and retain the very best staff.

Our staff members represent both the diversity and unity of our Alliance. When you join the NCIA, you will be part of an organization where you can contribute authentically to the mission and purpose of NATO and help us keep our technological edge.

**About the job:**

Based in Brussels, Belgium you will join the Agency as we embark on a journey to transform our IT services to support NATO’s Digital Endeavour. You will responsible for the delivery and day-to-day operations of multiple IT services to customers in remote NATO locations.

We are looking for a driven and enthusiastic Engineer (Digital Infrastructure) who will take on the following roles and responsibilities:

•    Responsible for the onsite IT Support at remote NATO offices.  
•    Provides IT logistic support.  
•    Supports and coordinate Cryptographic activities.  
•    Provides support for network equipment.  
•    Implements and follow up security policies and protocols to protect information according to NATO rules and regulations.   
•    Manages service-based contracts associated with the remote site, evaluate vendor performance and assist in renewing contracts.   
•    Periodically provides on demand support outside of business hours that could require working on site.

For a full list of duties, please review the [job description](https://www.ncia.nato.int/resources/site1/general/employment/jobs25/251630.pdf) on the NCIA career site.

**About you:**

The valuable knowledge and experience that you bring to this role are:

•    A Bachelor’s degree from a nationally recognized/certified university in a related discipline and 2 years post-related experience. Or, a different educational qualification with 6 years extensive and progressive expertise post related experience.  
•    Minimum of 1-year experience providing IT support in conflict zones.  
•    Up-to-date certificate of attendance of hostile environment awareness training.  
•    Experience supporting end user equipment including iPhones and Windows laptops  
•    Experience supporting Microsoft 365   
•    Experience supporting Cisco switches and wireless access points  
•    Experience performing Cryptographic activities  
•    Communication with all level of users, including VIPs (minimum of 1-year experience).  
•    Strong communication and interpersonal skills.   
•    Strong analytical skills and solution-oriented attitude.   
•    Fluency in English, both written and spoken.
