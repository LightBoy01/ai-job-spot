Vice President, Data Scientist
At BNY, our culture allows us to run our company better and enables employees’ growth and success. As a leading global financial services company at the heart of the global financial system, we influence nearly 20% of the world’s investible assets. Every day, our teams harness cutting-edge AI and breakthrough technologies to collaborate with clients, driving transformative solutions that redefine industries and uplift communities worldwide.
Recognized as a top destination for innovators, BNY is where bold ideas meet advanced technology and exceptional talent. Together, we power the future of finance – and this is what #LifeAtBNY is all about. Join us and be part of something extraordinary.
We’re seeking a future team member for the role of Vice President, Data Scientist to join our AI Hub Engineering team. This role is located in Dublin, Ireland and will be working in a hybrid capacity.
In this role, you’ll make an impact in the following ways:
*   Develop AI/ML and Generative AI / LLM models to address challenging and impactful business problems for BNY, from proof of concepts to production-level deliverables, and produce thorough model evaluation and documentation in order to support decision and assess model risk
*   Define, create and maintain actionable insights using data science and statistical analysis. Prepare assessments to provide business decision-supporting facts
*   Thoroughly evaluate models and produce supporting documentation, thoroughly assess analysis results to ensure correct business decision-supporting
*   Develop innovative solutions and produce patent disclosures to demonstrate RD&I
*   Collaborate with colleagues and subject matter experts (SME) to obtain data and domain knowledge needed to conduct advanced reviews
*   Continuously support and develop skills across AI/ML and GenAI and increase the business acumen
*   Stay highly up to date with the latest advances in AI/GenAI, technology innovations and banking industry trends. Share the findings with the team, recommend areas for improvement, and promote adoption of AI tools and platforms
*   Be proactive and autonomous in solving problems, demonstrate a strong sense of ownership and urgency
To be successful in this role, we’re seeking the following:
*   PhD degree in STEM degrees, or equivalent work experience with experience preferred in related fields
*   Experience in the securities or financial services industry is a plus
*   Deep understanding of applied data science, ML, NLP (e.g. feature engineering, supervised/unsupervised learning, predictive analytics, clustering, anomaly detection, sentiment analysis, entity recognition), and GenAI/LLM
*   Good understanding of Responsible AI principles (e.g. Fairness, Accountability, Transparency, Ethics) and AI Governance in finance
*   Strong and proven hands-on experience implementing solutions (e.g. Python, scikit-learn, pandas, keras, TensorFlow/PyTorch, GPT models)
*   Strong communication skills (presenting, writing) and highly collaborative mindset
*   Innovative mindset and creative thinking demonstrated by publications and patents
