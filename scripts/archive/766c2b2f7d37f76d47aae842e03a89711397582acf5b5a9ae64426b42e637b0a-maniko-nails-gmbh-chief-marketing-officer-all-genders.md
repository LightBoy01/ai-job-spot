### Description

Maniko is seeking a visionary **Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)** to lead our rapidly growing beauty brand into its next phase of expansion. Are you passionate about blending data-driven marketing with creative brand building to elevate a fast-growing company?

As CMO, you will be the strategic driving force behind all marketing activities, responsible for developing and executing Maniko's entire marketing strategy – from performance marketing to brand building and international expansion. You will build and lead a high-performance team of 15+ marketing professionals, manage a multi-million Euro budget, and ensure our brand grows sustainably across both eCommerce and retail channels. Collaborating closely with Product, Retail, Operations, and Finance, you will drive measurable success and actively shape the future of our innovative brand.

Maniko is a pioneering, rapidly expanding company in the nail care industry, renowned for our revolutionary self-adhesive nail polish technology. We empower our customers to achieve professional results conveniently at home with high-quality products. Our core values aren't just words; they are lived every day:
*   **Facts over ego:** We prioritize results and data, focusing on team success over individual ego.
*   **Challenge accepted:** Every challenge is viewed as an opportunity for collective growth.
*   **Power to the People:** Every contribution counts. We trust in personal responsibility and empowerment.
*   **Who, if not you?:** We embrace ownership and proactive problem-solving.

This is a unique opportunity to establish Maniko as a leading beauty brand in the European market by combining a data-driven mindset with a hands-on approach, ensuring every marketing Euro contributes measurably to our business development.

### Responsibilities

As Chief Marketing Officer, your key responsibilities will include:

*   **Marketing Strategy & Growth:**
    *   Develop and implement a holistic marketing strategy for sustainable growth across eCommerce and Retail.
    *   Own all marketing KPIs, including CAC, ROAS, CLV, and Brand Metrics.
    *   Drive data-driven reporting and continuous optimization of marketing performance.
*   **Team Leadership:**
    *   Build, lead, and foster the continuous development of a high-performance marketing team.
    *   Oversee hiring, onboarding, and talent development for all marketing functions.
    *   Implement performance management and cultivate an outcome-oriented, collaborative team culture.
*   **Performance Marketing:**
    *   Strategically plan and optimize all performance channels (Paid Social, SEA/SEO, Influencer Marketing, CRO).
    *   Execute efficient new customer acquisition with a focus on sustainable growth and profitable ROAS.
    *   Lead testing and scaling of new marketing channels and formats.
*   **Customer Lifecycle Management:**
    *   Develop and optimize CRM strategies to maximize Customer Lifetime Value.
    *   Create and implement retention programs, including print mailings, events, and loyalty initiatives.
    *   Drive segmentation and personalization of the customer journey.
*   **Brand & Creative Direction:**
    *   Evolve the brand positioning and ensure consistent rollout across all touchpoints.
    *   Provide overarching creative direction for campaigns and brand assets.
    *   Collaborate closely with the Brand & Creative Team for integrated campaign ideas.
*   **International Expansion:**
    *   Develop and execute the Go-to-Market strategy for new international markets.
    *   Conduct market analysis, localization efforts, and build country-specific marketing strategies.
    *   Manage international campaigns and partnerships.
*   **Budget & Analytics:**
    *   Hold full responsibility for the multi-million Euro marketing budget.
    *   Proficiently utilize attribution models and BI tools (Tableau, Looker, Excel).
    *   Champion continuous improvement through data-based decision-making.

### Qualifications

We are looking for an experienced and dynamic leader with:

*   **Experience & Expertise:**
    *   Minimum of 8-10 years of progressive marketing experience, ideally within the Beauty, Fashion, or Consumer Goods sectors.
    *   At least 5 years of proven leadership experience, successfully building and developing high-performing marketing teams.
    *   Demonstrable track record in brand building, market positioning, and campaign development with measurable business impact.
*   **Hard Skills:**
    *   **Performance Marketing:** Deep expertise in Paid Social (Meta, TikTok), Paid Search (Google), Influencer Marketing, and Conversion Rate Optimization.
    *   **Analytics & Data:** Proficient in attribution models, BI tools (Tableau, Looker), Excel, and data-driven reporting.
    *   **International Marketing:** Proven experience in market entry strategies, localization, and multi-market campaign management.
    *   **Budget Management:** Several years of responsibility for multi-million Euro marketing budgets.
    *   **Team Leadership:** A strong track record in hiring, talent development, and leading cross-functional teams.
    *   **Brand Strategy:** Expertise in brand architecture, positioning, and integrated campaign development.
*   **Soft Skills:**
    *   **Strategic Thinking:** Ability to grasp complex contexts and develop long-term strategies.
    *   **Results Orientation:** Clear focus on impact and measurable outcomes with strong execution capabilities.
    *   **Hands-on Mentality:** Willingness to actively contribute operationally in a scale-up environment, not just delegate.
    *   **Data-Driven Mindset:** Decisions are based on data, coupled with the courage for creative experimentation.
    *   **Leadership:** An inspiring leader who motivates, develops, and guides teams to success.
    *   **Excellent Communication:** Business-fluent German and English skills, with strong presentation abilities for C-level and team audiences.
    *   **Collaboration:** Strong cross-functional collaboration, avoiding silo thinking, and a high degree of empathy.
    *   **Creative Problem Solving:** Innovative solution-finding and out-of-the-box thinking combined with pragmatism.

### Author's Take

Here are some insights an insider might share about stepping into the CMO role at Maniko:

**1. "What's the biggest challenge and most rewarding aspect of being CMO at Maniko, and how does the company culture truly support you?"**

"The biggest challenge is undoubtedly balancing our aggressive growth targets with the delicate art of brand building across multiple international markets. We're scaling fast, and maintaining Maniko's unique brand voice and premium feel while optimizing for performance metrics is a constant, exhilarating tightrope walk. You'll be managing a multi-million Euro budget and a diverse team of 15+ professionals, all while expanding our footprint globally. But this challenge is precisely what makes it so rewarding.

You see the direct impact of your strategic decisions – from a new campaign driving unprecedented engagement to a new market launch exceeding expectations. Our culture is your strongest ally. 'Challenge accepted' isn't just a slogan; it's how we operate. When you bring a bold idea to the table, the immediate response isn't 'why?', but 'how can we make this work?'. The 'Facts over ego' mindset means we critically evaluate everything with data, but there's also immense trust in your expertise and leadership. You're empowered to experiment, learn from failures fast, and pivot. This isn't a role where you just execute; you define the playbook. We're a team that genuinely supports each other, always ready to roll up our sleeves and collaborate across departments to achieve our shared vision."

**2. "Beyond the strategic responsibilities, what’s the day-to-day reality of leadership like at Maniko, and how does the company's 'Power to the People' value translate into tangible impact for a C-level executive?"**

"Day-to-day leadership here is dynamic and incredibly engaging. While you'll certainly be deep in strategy, budget allocations, and high-level stakeholder meetings, there's also a strong 'hands-on' expectation. This isn't a purely delegatory role; you're expected to be deeply involved, mentor your team, and sometimes even jump into the details if a critical project demands it. You'll spend a significant amount of time collaborating – not just with your direct reports, but with Product, Retail, and Operations leaders, ensuring a truly integrated approach to growth.

The 'Power to the People' value, especially for a C-level executive, means you have extraordinary autonomy and direct influence. We have flat hierarchies and agile decision-making processes. If you identify an opportunity or a critical strategic shift, you have the direct ear of the founders and fellow C-levels to discuss, debate, and implement rapidly. There's no bureaucratic maze to navigate. Your vision for Maniko's marketing future isn't just heard; it's actively sought, valued, and given the resources to flourish. You're not just managing a department; you're truly helping to steer the entire company's direction and impact the very essence of our brand."
