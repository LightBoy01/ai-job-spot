### Description
Are you looking for a **flexible and enjoyable remote job** that you can perform from anywhere in the world – using just a mobile phone or laptop – while earning an **above-average income**? Then the position as a **Remote Chat Moderator (m/f/d)** is perfect for you.

As a Chat Moderator, you benefit from **unlimited earning potential**, **punctual commission-based remuneration**, and **free, practical training** that enables an **easy entry into the field** even without prior experience. Our employees rate us on Kununu as the **"best job"** and **"best employer"** they've ever had. It's no coincidence that we were awarded **Top Employer in 2024 and 2025**, and received the **Top Salary Seal in 2025**. We are a thriving team of over 350 members, offering 100% remote, worldwide, and anonymous work, flexible hours (part-time or full-time), and 24/7 support.

### Responsibilities
*   **Chat Communication:** You will engage with the fans of a creator, listen actively, and build personal connections. Your goal is to create interesting experiences and fulfill their wishes and expectations.
*   **Sales:** You will strategically offer exclusive content from the creator within the chat, following our clear, step-by-step guidance.
*   **CRM Maintenance:** You will systematically record information, interests, and preferences of the fans.
*   **Profile Management:** You will manage the profile according to our guidelines and regularly publish new posts in the feed.
*   **Community Management:** You will respond to comments and increase the profile's visibility through active interaction.

### Qualifications
*   **Age Requirement:** You must be at least 18 years old.
*   **German Language Skills:** You write confidently and largely error-free in German.
*   **Communication Skills:** You can express yourself well and actively shape conversations.
*   **Technical Equipment:** You have a laptop, PC, or smartphone with a stable internet connection.

**Optional (Plus Points):**
*   **Empathy:** You can easily put yourself in others' shoes and react accordingly in conversations.
*   **Sales Talent:** You have a basic understanding of how to offer products or content effectively.
*   **Experience:** You have previous experience with chat communication or selling exclusive content.
