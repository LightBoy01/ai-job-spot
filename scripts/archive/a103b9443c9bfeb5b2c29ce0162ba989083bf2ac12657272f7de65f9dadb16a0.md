**What You'll Do**
*   **Support the planning, promotion, and execution of events**—from small in-person gatherings to webinars and virtual sessions
*   **Assist with the production and promotion of our podcast**, including scheduling guests, uploading content, and sharing episodes
*   **Manage and grow our** **social media presence**, helping us build community and expand our reach
*   **Collaborate with internal teams to create and launch** **marketing campaigns**, emails, and related assets
*   **Help maintain and update content on our WordPress website**, including layout tweaks, plugin support, and simple backend fixes.
*   **Contribute to the creation and coordination of** **email marketing** campaigns using HubSpot or similar tools
*   **Monitor and report** on campaign and channel performance, offering insights for improvement
*   **Use AI tools and new technologies** to improve marketing efficiency, content quality, and overall impact
*   **Provide hands-on support** across a wide range of general marketing initiatives—from planning to execution
**Who You Are**
*   **Have hands-on experience with WordPress**, including setup, management, front-end customization, back-end troubleshooting, and plugin configuration.
*   **Thrive in fast-paced, collaborative environments**, and bring strong communication skills to team projects and cross-functional work.
*   **Are confident managing** **social media** platforms and know what drives engagement
*   **Are a self-starter** who takes initiative, identifies opportunities, and enjoys solving problems without waiting for instructions.
*   **Welcome feedback** and use it as a tool for personal and professional growth.
*   **Are quick to adopt new technologies and tools**, driving AI and other automation opportunities to enhance modern marketing workflows.
*   **Communicate clearly and confidently**, both in writing and in conversation, across a range of audiences.
*   **Have experience supporting events**, including planning logistics, managing registrations, and helping with promotion for both virtual and in-person formats.
