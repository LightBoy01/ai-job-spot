### Description\n\"The best way to predict the future is to invent it.\" – Alan Kay. At isento (Portuguese for “free”), we live this philosophy by realizing IT projects in Nuremberg, from conception to rollout. As a Software Developer, you will contribute to the development of tailored solutions for our customers' challenges. We see ourselves as a team where mutual reliance, professional and personal support, and shared knowledge are paramount to delivering the best solutions. This role offers an opportunity to work on innovative AI-driven projects within a highly collaborative and supportive environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop prototypes and productive systems based on modern AI methods such as Agentic AI, RAG, or LLM pipelines in client and research projects.\n- Implement robust, cloud-based architectures and integrate AI services into existing IT landscapes.\n- Ensure clear code, conduct unit and integration tests, perform code reviews, engage in knowledge exchange, and practice pair programming as part of your daily routine, alongside conceptualizing new features and coordinating with stakeholders.\n- Bring in your own ideas, take responsibility for your tasks, and actively contribute to the further development of projects.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Passion for software development, AI, and innovative technologies.\n- Experience in implementing AI and software projects up to productive use.\n- Very good knowledge of Python and Machine Learning concepts.\n- Practical experience with common AI frameworks and methods such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, Agentic AI, RAG, MCP, vLLM, LangChain, Prompt Engineering.\n- Experience in cloud technologies, web front-end development, and Java is an advantage.\n- Curiosity, a desire for exchange, and a willingness to share your knowledge to learn and grow together.\n- Good German language skills (at least B2) to ensure confident communication within the team and with stakeholders.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at isento offers an exciting opportunity for a skilled Software Developer passionate about cutting-edge AI technologies. The company deeply values collaboration, professional growth through individualized training, and an exceptional work-life balance, highlighted by flexible hours and extensive remote work options. Beyond the innovative client and research projects in AI, Machine Learning, and Robotics, isento fosters a vibrant team culture with regular social events and has been recognized as a top employer for its friendly and respectful atmosphere. This is an ideal position for someone who wants to take ownership, contribute ideas, and grow within a supportive and forward-thinking environment.
