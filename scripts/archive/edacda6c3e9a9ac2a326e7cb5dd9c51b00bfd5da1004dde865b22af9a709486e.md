**About the Role**

We’re looking for a Senior PM with **5+ years of product management experience**, including **2+ years building or managing AI-powered products**. The ideal candidate combines strategic thinking with strong operational discipline, thrives in cross-functional settings, and can confidently engage with clients, partners, and internal teams to drive AI initiatives from concept through launch.

**Core Responsibilities:**

*   Own the **end-to-end product lifecycle** for key AI initiatives — from discovery to post-launch iteration.
*   Join **client calls and beta programs** to gather structured feedback and ensure high product-market alignment.
*   Conduct **market research and competitive analysis** to inform new opportunities and commercialization strategy.
*   Translate strategy into a **clear, prioritized backlog** with detailed user stories and acceptance criteria.
*   Partner with **engineering, design, data science, and go-to-market teams** to ensure timely, high-quality releases.
*   Champion the **voice of the customer** by collaborating closely with client-facing teams.
*   Drive adoption and measure success across AI products through data-driven insights.

**Required Experience & Skills:**

*   5+ years in product management within B2B SaaS or workflow automation platforms.
*   2+ years managing **AI, ML, or data-intensive products**.
*   Proven success leading **cross-functional delivery** and working directly with clients or external partners.
*   Experience translating business goals into technical execution.
*   Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
*   Strong organizational discipline and ability to handle multiple priorities in parallel.

**Bonus Points:**

*   Experience with **Salesforce** or Salesforce-native applications.
*   Background in legal tech, fintech, or other complex operational domains.
*   Familiarity with LLMs, document intelligence, or data extraction workflows.
