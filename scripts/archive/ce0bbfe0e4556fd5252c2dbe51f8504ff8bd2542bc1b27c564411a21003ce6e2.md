**Job Description:**

The Product Manager (PDM) role is instrumental in guiding Assent's product evolution. Embracing a continuous discovery approach, this role demands deep engagement with customers and users, leveraging insights to shape product strategy and execution. The Product Manager will lead through empowerment, fostering a culture of innovation and collaboration across internal teams to deliver solutions that meet and exceed expectations. This role demands a balance of technical savvy, strategic insight, and a deep commitment to user-centric principles, driving the product forward through informed, collaborative, data-driven decision-making.

Key Requirements & Responsibilities 

*   Collaborate directly with Engineering and Design team members to produce working software solutions
*    Lead team collaboration activities in alignment with established practices
*   Document collaboration outcomes in alignment with established standards
*   Work closely with Senior Product Managers to translate their value-driven feature outcomes into actionable bodies of work (Epics and Stories) for the Delivery Team
*   Understand customer needs, market trends, and potential technological advancements; 
*   Regularly engage with customers, conduct user research, and validate hypotheses through experiments;
*   Work closely with your product Delivery Team, with product management focusing on value and viability, product design leading on usability and engineering representing the feasibility perspective;
*   Manage individual products or specific product features, develop and execute the product roadmap based on market data and insights;
*   Advocate for and implement continuous delivery practices to ensure rapid and reliable product releases;
*   Focus on delivering value to customers through iterative development and learning;
*   Utilize data and analytics to inform product decisions, measure success, and adapt strategies based on actionable insights and performance metrics;
*   Ensure that business objectives are still being met, when dealing with unforeseen change during delivery of scope;
*   Maintain knowledge of new trends and products in the market and in adjacent products;
*   Collaborate with and support internal groups to help launch products and features;
*   Contribute to the correlation of product definitions and market needs;
*   Collaborate with regulatory, marketing and sales to define solutions and understand market;
*   Contribute to product launch activities, and monitor product metrics
