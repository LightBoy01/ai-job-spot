This position is posted by Jobgether on behalf of a partner company. We are currently looking for a **Principal Video Creative Lead** in the **United States**.

In this role, you will lead the creative strategy and production of world-class video advertising campaigns that inspire, engage, and drive measurable results. You’ll combine strategic leadership with hands-on creative execution, personally shaping the vision, design, and delivery of performance-driven video content across platforms. Working with a talented multidisciplinary team, you will elevate storytelling through innovation, AI tools, and data-driven insights. This is an opportunity to set creative benchmarks, influence global marketing impact, and push the boundaries of video excellence in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.

**Accountabilities**

*   Develop and execute a cohesive creative vision for video advertising campaigns that balance performance and brand integrity.
*   Lead end-to-end production of high-impact video ads, including concepting, scripting, filming, editing, color grading, and delivery.
*   Partner with performance marketing teams to design platform-specific strategies and ensure campaigns meet conversion and engagement goals.
*   Direct on-set production activities — including lighting, camera operation, and talent direction — to ensure creative alignment and technical quality.
*   Integrate AI tools throughout the production process to optimize ideation, editing, and dynamic creative personalization.
*   Build scalable systems for high-volume video ad production using modular, templatized approaches.
*   Implement creative testing frameworks, analyze campaign performance data, and refine strategies based on insights.
*   Mentor and inspire creative team members, fostering experimentation, collaboration, and excellence.
*   Stay at the forefront of industry trends and champion new technologies, workflows, and storytelling formats.

**Requirements**

*   8–10+ years of professional experience in video production, with at least 5 years focused on performance marketing and advertising.
*   Proven expertise in creating high-converting video ads and executing full campaign lifecycles from concept to delivery.
*   Deep understanding of multi-platform video advertising ecosystems, including YouTube, social media, programmatic, and CTV/OTT.
*   Expert-level proficiency in **Adobe Creative Suite**, especially **Premiere Pro**, with the ability to perform complex edits independently.
*   Demonstrated experience leveraging **AI-powered video tools** for generation, automation, and personalization.
*   Strong ability to balance creativity with data-driven decision-making, analyzing metrics such as CTR, view rates, and conversions.
*   Skilled across all stages of production — concepting, storyboarding, directing, shooting, editing, sound, and color correction.
*   Experience leading creative reviews, guiding teams toward excellence, and articulating design choices to stakeholders.
*   Strong portfolio showcasing high-performing campaigns and measurable results.
*   Experience in SaaS, technology, or B2B marketing environments is a plus.

**Benefits**

*   Competitive annual compensation range: **$142,000 – $228,000 USD**, with potential for bonuses and restricted stock units (RSUs).
*   Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance plans.
*   Flexible work arrangements, including hybrid and remote options.
*   Access to cutting-edge creative tools and professional development opportunities.
*   Inclusive, collaborative culture that values creativity, innovation, and continuous learning.
*   Opportunities for career advancement and leadership within a globally recognized creative organization.
