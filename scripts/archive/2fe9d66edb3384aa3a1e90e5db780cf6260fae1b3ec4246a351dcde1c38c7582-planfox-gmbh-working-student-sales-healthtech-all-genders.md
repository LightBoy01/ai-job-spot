### Description
Join PLANFOX and help us shape the digital future of healthcare! We develop smart and connected software solutions that simplify processes, improve information quality, and create more time for what matters most – people. Born from a research collaboration with the University of Augsburg and rooted in the XITASO Group, we combine scientific expertise with technological know-how and a passion for digital health.

We offer you:
*   A diverse work environment where you can contribute your own ideas and take on responsibility early.
*   Flexible working hours that can be easily reconciled with your studies.
*   An open start-up culture with flat hierarchies and short decision-making paths.
*   A motivated and warm team that supports you and grows with you.
*   The opportunity to actively contribute to the success of PLANFOX and help shape our customer relationships.
*   A steep learning curve in a scalable software start-up – you'll learn something new every day and actively shape our processes and products.

### Responsibilities
**THESE TASKS AWAIT YOU**

*   Support the Sales team in day-to-day operations.
*   Collaborate in the creation of offers, tenders, and sales concepts.
*   Maintain and update customer databases and internal systems.
*   Prepare and create presentations, sales documents, and reports.
*   Research potential customers, market trends, and competitors.
*   Assist with lead follow-up and the preparation and organization of customer appointments.
*   Support the planning and implementation of marketing and sales campaigns.

### Qualifications
**WHAT DISTINGUISHES YOU**

*   **Hard Skills:** You are currently studying (e.g., Business Administration) and eager to apply your knowledge in practice. You are confident with MS Office and enjoy working with digital tools. Perhaps you already have initial insights into sales, organization, or digital topics, or you are keen to learn in these areas.
*   **Motivation:** You enjoy independently driving topics forward, contributing creative ideas, and actively shaping the digitalization of healthcare and modern sales processes.
*   **Mindset:** You work in a structured and reliable manner, maintain an overview even with multiple tasks, and value meticulousness and quality in your work.
*   **Team Spirit:** You enjoy working in a team, are actively engaged, and value reliable and respectful collaboration at eye level.
*   **Communication:** You possess strong communication skills and actively contribute to collaboration. You can confidently perform your work in German and communicate professionally and friendly.

### Author's Take
Here's a glimpse behind the scenes, from someone who knows the ropes at PLANFOX:

**Q1: This role seems very supportive. How does an entry-level contributor truly impact PLANFOX's mission of transforming healthcare when much of the work is behind the scenes?**

*A: That's a great question, and it gets to the heart of what makes working here so rewarding. While many of your tasks might seem 'supportive,' every single one of them is a vital link in the chain that brings our innovative solutions to healthcare providers. Think of it this way: your meticulous research on market trends directly informs our sales strategy, your polished presentations are often the first impression a potential client gets, and your efficient database management ensures our sales team can operate without a hitch. Without this foundational support, our sales efforts would falter, and our mission to simplify healthcare processes wouldn't reach as many people. You're not just processing information; you're directly enabling the growth that allows us to make a real-world difference in patient care. The impact is tangible, and often, the sales team will share successes where your specific contributions made the difference.*

**Q2: The description mentions a "start-up culture" with "flexible working hours" but also an expectation to "take on responsibility early." How do these elements balance for someone still studying and looking for a steep learning curve, especially in a German context?**

*A: It's a fantastic balance, and it's something we pride ourselves on. The "flexible working hours" aren't just a buzzword; they're genuinely designed to accommodate your academic schedule. We understand that your studies are a priority, and we work together to ensure your work hours complement, rather than conflict with, your university commitments. When it comes to "taking on responsibility early," this isn't about throwing you in the deep end without support. It means we trust you with meaningful tasks from day one, giving you ownership and a real sense of contribution. You'll be mentored by experienced colleagues who are genuinely invested in your growth. In a German context, this means we value clear communication, structured feedback, and a high degree of reliability, even within our agile start-up environment. You get the best of both worlds: the dynamic, fast-paced learning of a start-up, coupled with the clarity and support that ensures you can truly excel and make a significant impact while still pursuing your education.*
