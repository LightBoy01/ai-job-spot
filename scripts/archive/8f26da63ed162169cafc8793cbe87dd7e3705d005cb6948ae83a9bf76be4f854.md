**Ventia** provides facilities management services to the **City of Sydney’s** portfolio of 251 assets, including libraries, leisure centres, depots, commercial buildings, and iconic sites like Sydney Town Hall and the Ian Thorpe Aquatic Centre.

We’re hiring two full-time **Plumbers** to join our growing team, working across Sydney’s CBD to deliver maintenance services at a variety of community and commercial facilities.

**What’s on offer:**

*   Competitive salary with access to corporate discounts
*   Tool of trade vehicle and power tools provided
*   Monday to Friday, 7am–3pm with optional overtime
*   On-call roster approximately every 6 weeks, 10am–6pm. Callouts outside these hours paid at double time (min. 4 hours)
*   Career progression opportunities within the contract and broader Ventia organisation
*   Health & wellness programs including EAP, Healthy Minds and Healthy Bodies
*   Join one of Australasia’s largest essential service providers, listed on the ASX and NZX

  
**What you’ll be doing:**

*   Perform maintenance tasks including inspections, classifications, and repairs across City of Sydney facilities
*   Carry out planned maintenance works including RPZ and TMVs
*   Respond to reactive works such as blocked drains, toilets, hot water system repairs, and gutter/downpipe clearing
*   Communicate effectively with customers and support other trades to ensure high satisfaction
*   Complete administrative tasks including quotes, purchase orders, job dockets, minor estimates, and technical reports  
      
    

**About you:**

*   Certificate III in Plumbing or equivalent trade qualification
*   RPZ (backflow) and TMV experience preferred
*   Experience in roofing maintenance (waterproofing or flashing) highly regarded
*   Valid Driver’s Licence and White Card
*   Willingness to undergo a police check
