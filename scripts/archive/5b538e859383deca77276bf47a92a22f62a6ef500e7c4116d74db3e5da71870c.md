Overview:

This role is a **Senior Recruiter** with the strategic mindset of a headhunter and the agility of a tech-savvy talent partner. This role is ideal for someone who thrives in a fast-paced, AI-first environment and is passionate about identifying, engaging, and securing top-tier talent—especially passive candidates. You will be a critical support in shaping our hiring strategy, executing them and mentoring junior recruiters, and co-creating seamless, tech-enabled recruitment processes that blend automation with deep human insight.

Responsibilities:

_Strategic Recruitment_

*   Lead full-cycle recruitment for managerial, niche, and hard-to-fill roles across the organization.
*   Design and execute outbound sourcing strategies using advanced techniques such as:

*   Boolean logic and X-ray searches
*   Talent mapping and competitive intelligence
*   Deep LinkedIn and GitHub sourcing

*   Build and maintain a strong pipeline of passive candidates through proactive engagement and relationship-building.

_Candidate Screening & Interviewing_

*   Conduct thorough screening and behavioral interviews to assess both technical qualifications and cultural alignment.
*   Evaluate candidates against our core values: Character, Excellence, Ownership, and Teamwork.
*   Collaborate with hiring managers to define role requirements and ensure alignment on candidate profiles.
*   Facilitate structured interviews and debrief sessions to ensure objective, values-based hiring decisions.

_AI-Driven Talent Acquisition_

*   Leverage AI tools and platforms (e.g., sourcing automation, resume screening, chatbots) to streamline and scale recruitment efforts.
*   Continuously explore and implement emerging technologies to enhance candidate experience and recruiter productivity.
*   Champion an AI-first but human-centered approach to hiring—balancing data-driven insights with empathy, intuition and human insights.

_Stakeholder Management_

*   Serve as a strategic advisor to hiring managers and senior leaders, offering market insights and talent strategy recommendations.
*   Facilitate collaborative hiring decisions through structured interviews, scorecards, and data-backed reporting, leveraging the systems of the company, i.e. Recruit IQ (ICIMS) and other pertinent tools.
*   Ensure a high-quality experience for both candidates and internal stakeholders.

_Mentorship & Team Leadership_

*   Mentor and coach junior recruiters, fostering a culture of continuous learning and innovation.
*   Share best practices in sourcing, stakeholder engagement, and use of AI tools.
*   Lead initiatives to improve team performance, diversity hiring, and employer branding.

_Process Optimization & Reporting_

*   Audit and refine recruitment workflows to ensure efficiency, scalability, and compliance.
*   Track and analyze key recruitment metrics (e.g., time-to-fill, quality of hire, source effectiveness) using Recruit IQ (ICIMS) and other pertinent tools.
*   Collaborate with People Ops and HR Tech teams to co-create seamless, tech-enabled hiring processes.

Qualifications:

Required:

*   Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Business, Psychology, or a related field.
*   6+ years of full-cycle recruiting experience, including executive search or headhunting.
*   Proven expertise in outbound sourcing and passive candidate engagement.
*   Strong command of Boolean search, X-ray search, and sourcing automation tools.
*   Comfortable working with AI tools and recruitment technologies; eager to experiment and innovate.
*   Excellent English communication skills, both oral and written.
*   Effective problem-solving skills; capable of negotiating successfully with both stakeholders and candidates.
*   Strong planning and execution abilities.
*   Adaptable to change quickly and effectively.
*   Excellent time management skills.
*   Experience mentoring or leading junior recruiters.

**KNOWLEDGE/SKILLS/ABILITIES** 

Required:

*   Experience in fast-paced, high-growth, tech-driven environments of multi-national corporations
*   Familiarity with ATS, CRM, and AI sourcing platforms (e.g., ICIMS, Workday, Taleo, and the likes).
