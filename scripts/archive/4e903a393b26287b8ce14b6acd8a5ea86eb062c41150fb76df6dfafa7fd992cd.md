**Position Profile**

We are seeking a motivated and enthusiastic Robotics Engineer Intern to join our dynamic team at Ricoh USA for summer 2026. As a Robotics Engineer Intern, you will gain hands-on experience with advanced robotic systems, work directly with cutting-edge technologies, and contribute to projects that push the boundaries of industrial automation.

**Job Duties and Responsibilities**

*   Assist in the design, development, and testing of robotic systems and components, with an emphasis on collaborative robot (cobot) platforms.
*   Contribute to the development of control architectures using ROS2, enabling higher-level autonomy and digital twin integration.
*   Develop and integrate sensor fusion techniques across multiple sensing modalities (e.g., cameras, depth sensors, LiDAR) for perception and safety.
*   Support algorithm development for computer vision, object detection, and pose estimation.
*   Contribute to system development on x86 platforms with NVIDIA GPU acceleration, while also evaluating embedded options (e.g., Jetson Orin) for potential cost, packaging, and performance benefits.
*   Support initiatives using NVIDIA Omniverse for visualization, simulation, and digital twin environments.
*   Support hardware and software integration across robotics, vision, production printers, and workflow systems to deliver cohesive automation solutions.
*   Document project progress and contribute to proof-of-concept demonstrations integrating robotics, vision, and simulation.

**QUALIFICATIONS (Education, Experience, and Certifications)**

*   Working towards - B.S., M.S., or PhD. degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field

*   Students must have a cumulative GPA of 3.0 or higher
*   Students are to successfully complete all required screenings prior to hire

**Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities:**

Required

*   Enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master’s program in Robotics, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Mechanical Engineering, or related field.
*   Strong interest in robotics, automation, and applied AI.
*   Proficiency in Python and C/C++, with experience in robotics and vision libraries (e.g., ROS2, OpenCV, PCL).
*   Understanding of sensors (cameras, LiDAR, depth) and hardware/software integration on robotic platforms.
*   Excellent problem-solving ability and attention to technical detail.
*   Ability to work independently and collaboratively across multidisciplinary teams.

**Preferred Skills**

*   Familiarity with collaborative robots (e.g., UR series) and robotic arm kinematics.
*   Experience with ROS2 middleware, simulation frameworks, and motion planning libraries.
*   Exposure to object detection and pose estimation methods for manipulation and localization tasks.
*   Experience with GPU-accelerated computing on x86 and interest in exploring embedded platforms (e.g., Jetson) for optimized deployments.
*   Knowledge of computer graphics concepts and digital twin applications (e.g., NVIDIA Omniverse).
*   Research or project experience in embodied robotics and reinforcement learning.
