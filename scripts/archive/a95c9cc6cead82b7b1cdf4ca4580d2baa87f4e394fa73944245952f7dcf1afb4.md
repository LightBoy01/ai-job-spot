### **About the Role**

We're looking for a rare breed: a commercially-minded creative who lives at the intersection of design and B2B marketing. This isn't about perfecting one narrow skill; it's about bringing versatility, energy, and zeal to growing our marketing function. 

Reporting to the marketing director, you'll be our brand storyteller, content architect, and strategic partner to sales. You’ll work closely with the Marketing Director, collaborating on marketing initiatives and being expected to step in when they're unavailable; this requires someone who can operate at both the strategic and executional level with confidence. You'll work directly with clinical teams and Isla’s growth & product functions to sharpen our messaging, produce content that converts, and shape how healthcare professionals understand the tangible difference our digital pathway platform makes for patients and clinicians.

You'll be part of a small, tight-knit team where collaboration is everything. We need someone who works well with others, jumps in wherever needed, and understands that no job is too big or too small. 

One day you might be presenting a strategy to the executive team, the next you're formatting a deck or troubleshooting an email campaign, and you're equally energised by both.

This is more than just a role; it's an opportunity to make a tangible impact. We're looking for someone who will roll up their sleeves, go the extra mile, and help us build something meaningful in healthcare.

### What You'll Do

### Client-facing marketing & sales enablement

Work alongside sales to understand prospect pain points, refine our USPs, and test messaging with real clients and NHS Trusts. Create sales collateral, case studies, and other marketing materials that help close deals. 

### Strategic copy creation

You don't need to be a Pulitzer-winning writer, but you know how to structure a compelling narrative. You ask the right questions, extract insights from clinical teams/customers, and craft content (blogs, thought leadership, web copy, email campaigns) that resonates with healthcare decision-makers. AI tools will accelerate first drafts and ideation, freeing you to focus on strategy and storytelling. You are adept at leveraging the latest content format trends to achieve top search rankings and enhance online visibility.

### Creative production & design

Bring visual ideas to life. Whether editing video, creating presentation decks, designing social graphics, or developing campaign assets, you're comfortable in the creative space. You understand design principles enough to produce high-quality work and collaborate effectively with designers or agencies when needed.

### PR & brand storytelling

Draft press releases, build media relationships, and work with NHS trusts to launch partnerships and share impact stories. As part of your role, you’ll also help to manage our digital presence across social and other digital channels, always thinking about how to position Isla as a leader in healthcare innovation.

### Strategic marketing projects 

This role is a strategic partner in our growth, and as it's new, you'll have the autonomy to help shape it. You will not only execute campaigns but also contribute to high-impact initiatives that define our marketing strategy. There will be ample opportunity to get involved in other high-impact projects of interest as you pioneer new martech and channels, streamline processes for efficiency, and ultimately grow into a leadership role with people management responsibilities as the team expands.
