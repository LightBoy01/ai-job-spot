**About the role**

Lendi Group is evolving into an **AI-native fintech**, embedding artificial intelligence at the heart of how we work. This transformation is about more than tools – it’s about empowering our people. By integrating AI across the business, we’re freeing our teams to solve more complex problems, build smarter products faster, and deliver even greater value to our customers.

We're investing heavily in upskilling and reskilling, giving our engineers access to cutting-edge AI capabilities and the freedom to experiment, learn and lead in this new era. If you're curious, collaborative, and excited by what AI can unlock – this is your moment to get involved.

We’re looking for an enthusiastic **Senior Full Stack Engineer** to be part of our multi-disciplinary engineering team that will be designing, developing, and deploying AI systems that drive agentic AI Automation within the property buying and owning, mortgage broking and financial services sector. You will work at the intersection of AI, data, software engineering, and financial services, building cutting-edge solutions that enhance operational efficiency and customer experiences.

You will be reporting to a newly formed team within Lendi Group that is focused on AI driven initiatives, working side-by-side with a high performing, knowledgeable and fun team. This is a unique opportunity to make an impact on our long-term vision to expand our AI capability across the enterprise.

**Job Description:**

*   Help build an aesthetically pleasing and user-friendly interface, specifically designed to empower borrowers.
*   Engage in a partnership with our Product/UX team
*   Take charge of major segments of our system, demonstrating your expertise in both design and delivery. Your role will be pivotal in providing stable, high-quality solutions that stand the test of time. 
*   As an integral member of our greenfield AI focussed engineering team, your role involves building robust and scalable software. Your work will lay the foundation for a resilient and evolving technological framework. 
*   Your commitment to writing clean, maintainable code is crucial. We prioritise practices that support continuous integration and deployment, adhering to the highest standards and guidelines in software development. 
*   Play a leading role in driving the engineering team's rhythm and processes, from planning and resource allocation to design, coding, deployment, and ongoing support. 
*   Embrace full ownership of the code you and your team develop. This includes being proactive in debugging, monitoring, and ensuring the utmost reliability in our production environment.
