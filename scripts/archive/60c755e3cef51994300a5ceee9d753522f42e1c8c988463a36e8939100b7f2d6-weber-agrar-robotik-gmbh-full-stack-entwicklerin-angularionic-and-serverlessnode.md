### Description

We are looking for an experienced Full-Stack Developer to holistically drive our Webaro Cloud Platform – from the modern Angular/Ionic frontend to the scalable serverless backend.

**About the Product/Technology Stack**

*   **Frontend**: Angular (TypeScript), Ionic, RxJS, SCSS, Tailwind, i18n (de/en/fr), Cypress, Karma/Jasmine
*   **Maps/GIS**: Google Maps, GeoJSON/KMZ
*   **Tooling**: Webpack/Angular CLI, ESLint/TSLint (Migration), Protractor (Legacy, replacement planned), ng-swagger-gen
*   **Backend/Integrations**: Serverless (AWS Lambda), REST/OpenAPI, WordPress
*   **CI/CD & Quality**: E2E-Tests (Cypress), Unit-Tests, Linting
*   **Other**: Multi-tenant and subscription variants, Assets/Build optimizations, Internationalization

**Benefits**

*   Modern toolchain, time for quality (tests, reviews, refactoring).
*   Further training/budget, Conference Days.
*   Attractive salary package depending on experience.

**Our Way of Working**

*   Pragmatic Clean Code, clear architecture, focus on Developer Experience.
*   Short decision-making processes, high degree of creative freedom.
*   Remote-friendly, flexible working hours.

### Responsibilities

*   Develop new features in the Angular/Ionic frontend including state management, i18n, and Responsive UI.
*   Maintain and optimize existing modules (e.g., `booking`, `portal`, `settings`) and refactor services like `booking.service.ts`.
*   Implement map and geo-features (GeoJSON/KMZ, Markers/Overlays, Performance).
*   Design and implement serverless backends (AWS Lambda) and REST APIs, connecting via OpenAPI/Swagger-Client.
*   Ensure quality: Unit tests (Karma/Jasmine) and E2E tests (Cypress), replacing Protractor.
*   Perform performance and build optimizations (Lazy Loading, Bundle size, Image optimization, Tailwind/SCSS).
*   Maintain small PHP/WordPress integrations (plugin maintenance) and webhooks.
*   Collaborate with Product/Design, conduct Code Reviews, and drive Continuous Improvement.

### Qualifications

*   Several years of experience with Angular (TypeScript), RxJS, and Ionic.
*   Proficiency in SCSS/Tailwind, Responsive Design, and component architecture.
*   Experience with Serverless (ideally AWS Lambda) and Node.js/REST APIs.
*   Confident in testing (Cypress, Karma/Jasmine) and CI/CD workflows.
*   Practical experience in i18n, Accessibility basics, and Performance tuning.
*   Clean code, review culture, Git workflow, independent working style.
*   Fluent in spoken and written German and English.

**Nice-to-Haves**

*   OpenAPI/Swagger tooling (e.g., `ng-swagger-gen`), API design.
*   Google Maps Platform, GIS fundamentals, GeoJSON/KML/KMZ.
*   Experience with WordPress plugins (PHP) and webhooks.
*   AWS stack (API Gateway, S3, CloudFront), Terraform/Serverless Framework.
*   Migration Protractor → Cypress, E2E strategies.
*   Security know-how (Auth, OWASP Top 10), Payment flows (PayPal/Stripe).

### Author's Take

Here are two questions a candidate might ask, along with an insider's perspective:

**1. Given the mention of refactoring initiatives and the varied tech stack, what is the team's philosophical approach to technical debt and continuous modernization?**

That's a fantastic question, and it really hits at the heart of our engineering philosophy. We're not just building new features; we're meticulously refining our existing platform. The mention of Protractor's planned deprecation, for instance, isn't just about switching tools; it's a testament to our commitment to modern, efficient testing practices. We actively allocate time for refactoring and architectural improvements, seeing them not as chores, but as investments in our future scalability and developer experience. This isn't a place where technical debt accumulates unchecked; it's a dynamic environment where continuous improvement, quality, and a pragmatic approach to clean code are deeply ingrained in our daily workflow. You'll have significant input into these strategic technical decisions.

**2. The job description highlights flexibility, creative freedom, and a remote-friendly environment. How does the team ensure strong collaboration and a cohesive culture amidst such autonomy?**

It's true, we pride ourselves on being remote-friendly and offering a lot of creative freedom, but that doesn't mean we compromise on collaboration or culture. We've cultivated an environment built on trust and clear communication. Our short decision paths mean ideas get heard and implemented quickly, regardless of where you're working from. We leverage modern communication tools effectively, but more importantly, we foster a culture where initiative is rewarded, and peer-to-peer code reviews are a cornerstone for knowledge sharing and maintaining quality. This isn't just a place to code; it's a community where everyone's contribution is valued, and you're empowered to shape not just the product, but also our engineering practices. You're not just writing lines of code; you're building a platform that makes a real impact on our users, and your voice will be instrumental in how we achieve that.
