The **Project Coordinator - Digital Transformation** Intern will work closely with the Information and Security Systems (ISS) Digital Transformation Team in an effort to partner with clinical operations on several digital health projects that provide or facilitate quality remote patient care for our patient populations. The intern’s responsibilities will include (but are not limited to):

*   Participate in project meetings and track key operational milestones
*   Develop metrics, dashboards, and workflow diagrams to support ambulatory project implementation
*   Attend Electronic Medical Records (EMR) training & education
*   Learn about various remote patient monitoring programs, including AI, VR, and other tech-enabled digital health solutions
*   Support the team with the go-live of a new digital health project involving an AI clinical decisions support application and provide on-site support and troubleshooting in clinical settings

### Qualifications

*   Applicants must be currently enrolled in a full-time accredited undergraduate or graduate program at a public or private College/University, and be in good academic standing at their institution 
*   Must be committed to completing the duration of the internship from June 2026 - August 2026
*   Must be committed to work 37.5 hours per week and commit to a minimum of 375 hours for the duration of the internship 
*   Pursuing a degree in Public Health, Health Education, or a similarly related field 
*   Demonstrate strong analytical and problem-solving skills
*   Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite, specifically Excel 
*   Must have strong communication skills (Written & Verbal)
*   Interest in digital health technology and the use of AI/VR in healthcare settings to enable strong patient care outcomes (preferred)

### About Company

Atlantic Health System is at the forefront of medicine, setting standards for quality health care powered by a workforce of more than 18,000 team members and 4,800 affiliated physicians dedicated to building healthier communities, the system offers more than 400 sites of care, including eight award-winning medical centers. Specializing in cardiovascular care, cancer care, orthopedics, neuroscience, pediatrics, women's health and rehabilitation medicine. Atlantic Medical Group, comprised of 1,000 physicians and advanced practice providers, represents one of the largest multi-specialty practices in New Jersey and includes finance, legal, marketing, human resources, talent acquisition, ISS and more. Caring for our patients, our team members and the communities we serve is our central mission.
