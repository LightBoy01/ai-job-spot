### Description\nJoin etalytics, a pioneer in AI-powered energy management for the industrial sector. We leverage cutting-edge IoT, data analytics, and AI technologies to help businesses achieve significant energy savings, reduce CO₂ emissions, and cut costs. As an Intern or Working Student in Backend Software Development, you will be instrumental in advancing our **etaONE Energy Intelligence Platform**. This role offers a unique opportunity to grow professionally and personally within a highly motivated, international team, contributing to impactful projects that shape the future of sustainable energy. This internship is full-time for three months, while working students are expected to be available 12–20 hours per week during the semester. Our office is located in Darmstadt, conveniently accessible by public transport.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs an active member of our skilled development team, you will contribute to the etaONE Energy Intelligence Platform by:\n- Expanding your technical skills through dedicated mentoring, insightful code reviews, and participation in an active developer community, staying current with **Spring Boot** and related technologies.\n- Designing and developing robust backend features utilizing modern frameworks and tools such as Spring Boot (Java/Kotlin), Hibernate, Git, Gradle, PostgreSQL, MQTT, and Docker.\n- Actively supporting the development of new product and solution ideas, bringing your creative suggestions, innovative solution approaches, and potential new technologies to the forefront.\n- Ensuring the highest code quality standards through comprehensive test automation with JUnit, Mockito / MockK, applying test-driven development (TDD) principles, and leveraging static code analysis.\n\n### Qualifications\nTo thrive in this role, you should possess:\n- Ongoing or successfully completed studies in Computer Science, Physics, Mathematics, Engineering, or a comparable technical field.\n- A strong, demonstrable interest in developing high-quality, maintainable, and thoroughly tested code.\n- A keen willingness and proven ability to learn quickly and work effectively both independently and as part of a team.\n- High motivation to take ownership of tasks and contribute to building sustainable software solutions.\n- Excellent English language skills are essential, with German language skills being a valuable plus.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an aspiring backend developer eager to make a real impact on sustainability. etalytics offers a rare blend of meaningful work, cutting-edge technology (AI, IoT, Spring Boot, Kotlin), and a genuinely supportive culture. Interns and working students aren't relegated to trivial tasks; they're integrated into the core development process with mentorship, code reviews, and opportunities to contribute creative solutions. The commitment to growth, flexible hybrid work, and a vibrant international team environment makes this a highly attractive role for those looking to kickstart their career in a purpose-driven tech company. Prepare to learn fast, build robust solutions, and directly contribute to reducing carbon emissions in industrial sectors.
