### Description
We are seeking a proactive and skilled **IT Administrator (m/w/d)** to join our partner company. In this crucial role, you will be a key player in safeguarding the integrity and optimizing the efficiency of our IT systems. Your responsibilities will span comprehensive data management, from robust backup and thorough documentation to diligent archiving and the development of innovative automation solutions. You will contribute to a dynamic environment focused on continuous improvement and operational excellence, directly streamlining our operational processes.

### Responsibilities
*   Monitor and perform data backup for IT systems.
*   Conduct holistic documentation and logging.
*   Manage the transfer of data carriers, including documentation for exchange with external data centers, and ensure regular reorganization.
*   Maintain and verify the readability and completeness of archive media.
*   Archive documents within SAP.
*   Develop solutions for the automation of operational processes.

### Qualifications
*   A degree in Computer Science or a comparable qualification.
*   Ideally, several years of experience in SAP system administration or data backup.
*   Strong analytical and communication skills, coupled with high customer and service orientation.
*   Fluent German and English language skills.

### Author's Take

**A:** Honestly, it's the chance to truly make an impact by driving efficiency. We're not just maintaining systems; we're actively seeking and building solutions to automate repetitive tasks. It’s incredibly rewarding to see your work free up time for more strategic initiatives, knowing you're contributing to a smoother, faster operation. You'll be given the space to explore new technologies and bring fresh ideas to the table, which keeps things constantly engaging and ensures you're always growing professionally.

From day one, you'll be paired with experienced colleagues who are genuinely invested in your success – think of them as your personal guides through our systems and culture. We also have a strong focus on internal training and development. The company encourages us to stay ahead of the curve, offering various opportunities to expand our skills, whether it's through certifications, workshops, or knowledge-sharing sessions. It's a supportive environment where continuous learning isn't just encouraged, it's part of our DNA.
