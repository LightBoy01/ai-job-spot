**Introduction to the job**
---------------------------
Are you passionate about leading data and analytics? Do you have a strong vision on how artificial intelligence will impact high-tech organizations? Do you thrive at the intersection of business, technology, and innovation?
ASML’s D&E sector is seeking a visionary Department Manager to lead its Business Intelligence & Analytics (BI&A) department within the Quality & Business Performance subcluster. This role offers a front-row seat to the strategic transformation of one of the world’s most advanced engineering organizations.
**Role and responsibilities**
-----------------------------
**Job Mission**
As Department Manager, you lead the D&E Business & Analytics capability in order to maximize its impact to the ASML business. In order to do so, together with key stakeholders and based on internal and external trends, you develop a strong vision on the future of BI&A in ASML. You direct and manage the activities of the department, in line with the sector & cluster strategy, technology/product roadmap and policies. You maintain key stakeholder relations inside and outside D&E, including the cross sectoral analytics network. You create an environment in which employees feel engaged and can grow to their full potential.
**Key Responsibilities**
•    Drive the BI&A Capability within D&E. Monitor all relevant internal and external developments, including data sciences, business analytics and AI. In alignment with key stakeholders, define and manage the capability roadmap.
•    As a member of the QBP Cluster MT, contribute to the strategic plan for the cluster. Translate the QBP strategy to an annual operational plan for the BI&A department. Contribute to the success of the cluster and sector by taking ownership beyond the scope of own department.
•    Lead, grow and develop a team of ~35 BI&A professionals, including business analysts, data analysts and artificial intelligence and machine learning specialists, utilizing the relevant HR instruments, service and procedures.
•    Define an optimum organization structure for the department.
•    Manage CAPEX and OPEX according to budget and monitor financial performance versus plans and budgets.
•    Supervise GLs on staffing, prioritization and execution.
**Strategic Focus Areas**
Foster a culture of innovation, collaboration, and continuous improvement:
•    Drive the development of optimal enterprise analytics solutions and algorithms that support strategic decision-making across D&E.
•    Accelerate optimization of key dashboards (e.g., PGP, BOM, IRM) and ensure enterprise data integration.
•    Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI/ML): Leverage AI to enhance predictive insights and operational efficiency.
•    Ensure alignment of BI&A activities with D&E’s strategic business portfolio and sector priorities.
•    Represent BI&A in cross-sectoral initiatives and leadership forums.
**Education and experience**
----------------------------
**Education**
•    Master degree in Data Sciences, Engineering and/or Business.
**Experience**
•    +15 years of experience  in high-tech industry, with at least 10 years in a leadership role and a proven experience in managing international teams.
•    Demonstrated leadership in enterprise data & analytics, data platforms and AI/ML.
•    Strong background in project/program management and large-scale business transformations, especially in semiconductor manufacturing.
•    Comfortable dealing with high-pressure, ambiguous environments.
•    Fluent in English.
**Skills**
----------
*   Business Leadership skills: Passion for technology and data driven decision making, results-driven, manages complexity, strategic decision-making, stakeholder management, innovation, and accountability.
*   People Leadership skills: Visionary, collaborative, talent development, team building, trust-building.
*   Personal skills : Decisive team player, strong communicator, influential without authority, self-starter, professional, solution-oriented, and skilled mentor/coach.
**Other information**
---------------------
**Context of the Role**
ASML is a global leader in creating advanced technology systems for the semiconductor industry, offering a full range of solutions for manufacturing integrated circuits (ICs or chips). Headquartered in Veldhoven, the Netherlands, with sites across the globe. ASML serves major semiconductor manufacturers worldwide. Its Development & Engineering sector employs around 15,000 people, primarily in the Netherlands and the USA, is organized into clusters focused on developing and engineering new and existing products. The Business Intelligence & Analytics (BI&A) team operates within the P&I Quality & Business Performance (QBP) cluster, working collaboratively across all D&E sectors and with IT and other partners.
**Why to Join Us?**
This role offers an unique opportunity to:
•    Be part of a mission-critical transformation in one of the world’s most innovative companies.
•    Lead a high-impact team shaping the future of data-driven decision-making in D&E.
•    Collaborate with top minds in engineering, IT, and business strategy.
