### Description\nRemmert specializes in automated storage and logistics systems, providing space-saving storage and fully automated movement solutions for sheets, long goods, and workpieces. Operating in the future-oriented mechanical engineering, automation, and logistics industry, Remmert has been a family-run company since 1945. This heritage fosters a culture of development, communication at eye-level, and short decision paths. As the company continuously grows, we are seeking dedicated colleagues to join our team.\n\nEmployees benefit from an unlimited employment contract, 30 days of holiday, flexible working hours including mobile work options, and a wide array of special bonuses like profit sharing, employee referral programs, E-Bike leasing, attendance bonuses, and fitness studio subsidies. The company also provides modern equipment (laptop, mobile phone, height-adjustable desk), complimentary coffee, fruit, and vegetables, and invests significantly in employee development through regular feedback, annual reviews, and a comprehensive training catalog. Regular employee events, company pension schemes, and health management programs further enhance the supportive work environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Demand Determination & Procurement Planning:** Collaborate closely with sales, engineering, and project management to derive technical specifications, create bills of materials, and establish procurement schedules.\n*   **Supplier Selection & Development:** Identify, evaluate, and select suitable suppliers, considering quality, ESG, and certification standards. Build and maintain long-term supplier relationships.\n*   **Contract & Price Negotiations:** Draft and negotiate framework agreements, price structures, and payment terms. Ensure cost-optimized procurement while adhering to technical requirements.\n*   **Technical Risk & Quality Management:** Review technical specifications, standards, and compatibility. Collaborate with quality assurance and manage deviations.\n*   **Supply Chain Tracking & Schedule Management:** Monitor delivery dates, coordinate inbound deliveries, and provide support during assembly and installation.\n*   **Cost Efficiency & Budget Control:** Prepare cost overviews, conduct price analyses, and contribute to budget planning.\n*   **Documentation & Reporting:** Maintain procurement documentation and provide regular status reports to project management.\n*   **Process Optimization & Digitalization:** Participate in the further development of procurement processes and the utilization of digital tools for supplier evaluation.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed technical training and commercial further education with a focus on mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, or a comparable qualification; ideally, a degree in Industrial Engineering, Supply Chain Management, or relevant advanced training.\n*   Solid knowledge in mechanical engineering, ideally in rack steel construction, load carriers, conveying technology, and automation components.\n*   Experience in project purchasing, especially in the planning phase.\n*   Strong negotiation skills, knowledge of contract law, and supplier management expertise.\n*   SAP knowledge (order processing, invoice workflow) is an advantage.\n*   Team player with a structured working style and high initiative.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a **Mid-Senior Project Purchaser** to join a stable, growing, family-run company that is a leader in automated storage and logistics. Remmert offers a genuinely supportive culture with significant investment in employee development, work-life balance through flexible arrangements, and a comprehensive benefits package. If you're passionate about contributing to innovative technical projects, value a collaborative environment with clear communication, and are looking for a long-term career path, this role offers a great platform to make a tangible impact within a future-oriented industry. If you are a long-term oriented individual and can imagine being part of the Remmert team, Mona Greve welcomes your application.
