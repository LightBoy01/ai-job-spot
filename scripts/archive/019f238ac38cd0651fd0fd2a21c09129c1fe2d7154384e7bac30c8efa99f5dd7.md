Our Technology Centers focus on creating a space where teams can come together to deliver business solutions that serve our company purpose to save and improve lives. An integral part of our company IT operating model, Tech Centers are globally distributed locations where each IT division has employees to enable our digital transformation journey and drive business outcomes. **Role Overview**

Join our company as a software engineer as we innovate!  Our QE Platform team provides test tools and test infrastructure to enable end users to test.  As a Software Engineer, you will participate in a full stack development activity to design, develop, test, and maintain software systems. Our platform is grounded in AWS and Azure Cloud development of web applications, APIs, and also Python based RobotFramework.

**What will you do in this role** 

*   Take full ownership of the tech stack using python to  design, code, verify, test, and document our Test Automation Framework developed for RobotFramework.
*   Work with a diverse tech stack that includes creation of Docker Containers, GitHub Actions and Workflows,  Python, RobotFramework, RobotFramework Libraries, Databases, SQL, Java, Node, Tosca, AWS and Azure DevOps.
*   Identify improvements to and new practices aligned to Technology Engineering standards & strategy and contribute to the Technology Engineering Practices within the product line where you work.
*   Work within a team atmosphere and collaborate with other practitioners to implement our roadmap.

**What should you have**

*   Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or any technology stream.
*   3+ years of hands-on experience developing Docker Containers, GitHub Actions and Workflows, Python, PyTest and RobotFramework based applications
*   Necessary experience with containerization technologies such as Docker.
*   Experience with version control using Git and CI/CD pipeline automation using GitHub Actions or similar tools to drive the pipeline.
*   Familiarity with AI technologies such as GPT and Copilot.

Our technology teams operate as business partners, proposing ideas and innovative solutions that enable new organizational capabilities. We collaborate internationally to deliver services and solutions that help everyone be more productive and enable innovation.
