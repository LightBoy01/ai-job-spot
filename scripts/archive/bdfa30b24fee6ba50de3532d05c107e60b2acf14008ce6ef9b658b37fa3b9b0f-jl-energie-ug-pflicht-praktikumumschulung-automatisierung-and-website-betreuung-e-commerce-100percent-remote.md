### Description
PV Siegel pioneers innovative Agri-PV technology, seamlessly integrating agriculture and energy generation on the same land. Our vision is a sustainable future where food production and clean energy coexist harmoniously. If you are passionate about sustainability and captivated by technology, we offer incredibly exciting opportunities to expand our digital presence. This role is ideal for individuals eager to learn and make a significant impact.

**What you can expect:**
*   **Deep Insights:** An exciting and varied role in website support and tech product development.
*   **Make a Positive Impact:** Agri-PV truly helps implement the energy transition. You'll be doing good for people and the environment.
*   **100% Remote & Flexible Working Hours:** Because we know that your best output isn't determined by your workplace.
*   **Teamwork:** A young, dynamic team that ensures a pleasant working atmosphere and strong commitment.
*   **Proactive Initiative:** Every individual counts with us, and we look forward to your ideas, solutions, and creative approaches.

### Responsibilities
*   Support **SEO and GEO optimization** to enhance online visibility and regional reach.
*   **Automate workflows with AI**, including identifying efficiency potentials and implementing simple tools.
*   Prepare **customer documents (online & offline)** as part of our marketing activities, such as presentations, brochures, and digital content.
*   Build and **optimize sales processes**, including CRM structuring and developing sales funnels.

### Qualifications
*   Currently pursuing a **business administration vocational training** (Kauffrau/Kaufmann or comparable) OR a **current university degree** in Business Administration, Marketing, Media and Communication Sciences, or a related field.
*   Deep understanding and **proficient handling of AI & LLMs**.
*   **Interest** in e-commerce and digital marketing strategies.
*   **Creativity** and the ability to present complex information clearly and appealingly.
*   **Independent** work style and strong teamwork skills.
*   **Native German** speaker.
*   **[Advantageous:]** Basic knowledge of **SEO & GEO**.
*   **[Advantageous:]** Basic knowledge of **MCP, n8n/Make/Zapier**.
