### Description
Superchat is revolutionizing communication between businesses and their customers. Our mission is to make business communication as simple and natural as talking to your friends. With over 7,500 business customers, Superchat is experiencing rapid growth, backed by $15.6M in Series A funding from leading investors like Blossom Capital and 468 Capital. We offer a comprehensive messaging suite that unifies all communication channels (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and more) in one platform, enabling businesses to easily send newsletters, build customer relationships, and sell products and services more effectively. This role offers significant freedom and the potential to be responsible for Superchat's next revenue leap, embodying true Product-Led-Growth.

### Responsibilities
*   Scale Superchat and significantly expand our market share by analyzing growth potentials and preparing decision-relevant insights for management.
*   Develop and implement social media and marketing strategies to strengthen our brand presence and drive organic growth and lead generation for Superchat.
*   Take on independent and self-determined projects in various company divisions and bring them to a successful conclusion.
*   Gain deep insights into Growth, Marketing, Sales, RevOps, and Operational Excellence.
*   Get to know the daily business of our teams, alongside project work and supporting our management.

### Qualifications
*   Currently studying or have successfully completed a degree in a start-up relevant field (e.g., economics or natural sciences) and are available for a 3- to 6-month internship.
*   Previous practical work experience in start-ups, management consulting, or investment banking.
*   You set the highest quality standards for yourself and bring a high sense of responsibility.
*   You have a strong drive for personal development and are not afraid to get hands-on.
*   You enjoy working highly analytically and structurally.
*   Native-level German language skills and very good written and spoken English skills (Spanish skills are a plus).

### Author's Take
This is an exceptional opportunity for an ambitious student or recent graduate to gain substantial, hands-on experience within a rapidly scaling German startup. Superchat offers a unique environment where interns are given significant autonomy on projects that directly impact the company's growth. The commitment to professional development through strong onboarding, regular coaching, and feedback is a major plus. Coupled with an inclusive culture, strong team spirit, and regular team events, this internship provides not just a learning experience but a chance to make a real difference and celebrate successes together. It's perfect for someone who thrives in a fast-paced, analytical, and supportive environment.
