### Description
We are a growing software company specializing in the development of custom software solutions. To support our continuous growth, we are looking for an HR Administrator to strengthen our HR team in Erlangen.

As an HR Administrator (m/f/d), you will be a central point of contact for our employees, taking responsibility for a wide range of HR tasks. From managing the entire employee lifecycle – from onboarding to offboarding – to creating important documents and contributing to exciting HR projects, you will actively contribute to the further development of our company.

Are you structured, communicative, and enjoy working with people? Then we look forward to getting to know you!

**What We Offer:**
*   Thorough onboarding by a motivated team.
*   Trust-based working hours and flexible work schedules.
*   Flexible home office arrangements.
*   33 days of annual leave.
*   Targeted professional and personal development opportunities.
*   A company bike, Deutschlandticket (Germany-wide public transport ticket), or parking space.
*   Excellent public transport connections.
*   Family-friendly company culture.
*   Subsidy for a premium fitness studio membership.
*   Free drinks, barista coffee, ice cream, fruit, and much more.

### Responsibilities
*   Independent processing and management of personnel administration from onboarding to offboarding (contracts, references, master data maintenance).
*   Support and advice for employees on various questions.
*   Assistance in the implementation of HR projects and processes.
*   Collaboration with the external tax office.

### Qualifications
*   Completed commercial vocational training or a comparable qualification, ideally with further training in human resources management.
*   Initial professional experience in HR administration (at least 1 year).
*   Structured, reliable, and independent way of working with a high service orientation.
*   Excellent communication skills and enjoyment in dealing with people.
*   Proficient use of modern communication tools.
*   Knowledge of Atlassian is an advantage.
*   Excellent German language skills (native speaker level from C1) and good English language skills, both written and spoken.

### Author's Take

"Honestly, it's the feeling that you're truly at the heart of our expansion. We're not just processing paperwork; we're enabling our talent to thrive, which directly fuels our innovation. One day you might be perfecting an onboarding process for a new developer, the next you're contributing to a company-wide initiative to enhance employee well-being. The pace is dynamic, and your direct impact on our culture and operational efficiency is genuinely felt across all departments. You become the go-to person, the trusted advisor, and that's incredibly rewarding in an environment where every employee's contribution counts."

**2. How does the company's family-friendly culture and flexible policies translate into the day-to-day experience for an HR team member?**

"Our commitment to a family-friendly culture isn't just a bullet point in a job ad; it's woven into our daily fabric. As an HR team member, you're not just advocating for these policies; you're living them. We truly embrace flexible working hours and home office options, understanding that life happens. This means you have the autonomy to manage your schedule, which is a huge stress reliever. Beyond that, the collaborative spirit means you're never alone. Whether it's a tricky HR question or a new project, you're supported by a motivated team that genuinely cares about each other's success and well-being. It creates an atmosphere where you feel empowered to do your best work, knowing the company has your back."
