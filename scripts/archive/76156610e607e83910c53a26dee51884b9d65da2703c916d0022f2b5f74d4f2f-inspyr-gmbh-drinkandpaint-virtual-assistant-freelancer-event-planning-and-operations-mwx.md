### Description
Join our young, rapidly growing startup as an Administrative Event Coordinator on a part-time (20 hours/week), freelance basis, starting immediately. This remote-friendly role, with an option for Cologne-based work, is perfect for a passionate, self-driven team player who excels in organization and administrative support. We're looking for a structured individual to reliably support our administrative processes within event management.

While the events are purely digital and require no on-site presence or creative input from your side, your role will be crucial in ensuring their smooth execution. You'll have the chance to contribute your own ideas and build an exciting brand within a fast-paced, dynamic startup environment characterized by quick decisions, open communication, and direct exchange with the founding team. Your sense of responsibility is highly valued here.

### Responsibilities
*   You will be responsible for the administrative organization and seamless scheduling of our digital events.
*   You will take over the structured alignment and timely coordination of all involved internal and external stakeholders during event preparation.
*   You will ensure the continuous optimization of processes and the maintenance of high quality standards to guarantee a problem-free event flow.
*   You will be responsible for the planning, tracking, and documentation of all project-related tasks in close collaboration with the team, keeping a keen eye on timelines and priorities.

### Qualifications
*   You have a completed vocational training or degree in Business Administration, Economics, or a comparable field.
*   You bring at least 2 years of professional experience in project management or a comparable specialized area.
*   You possess excellent German and good English language skills, both written and spoken.
*   You remain calm in stressful situations and are characterized by flexibility and improvisational skills, even under time pressure.
*   Willingness to work outside of regular business hours and on weekends when required.
