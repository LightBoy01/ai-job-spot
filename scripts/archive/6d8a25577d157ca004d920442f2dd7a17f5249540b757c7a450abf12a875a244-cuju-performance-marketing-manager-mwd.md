### Description
**Location:** Hamburg or Remote (Germany) | **Industry:** SportTech | **Start:** as soon as possible | **Languages:** fluent German and English.

\"Make invisible visible!\" CUJU's goal is to revolutionize football scouting and build the world's leading platform for identifying and developing talent. With modern AI technology, CUJU enables every young football talent to be seen – regardless of origin, gender, or social background. We create fair opportunities and transparent paths into professional sports. Our platform connects athletes, clubs, and organizations worldwide to rethink scouting – data-driven, objective, and mobile. Together, we are shaping the next generation of global talent scouting.

As a Performance Marketing Manager, you will drive CUJU's digital growth by creating and optimizing data-driven online marketing campaigns across multiple platforms. In this position, you will be at the center of user acquisition, engagement, and the overall performance marketing strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Develop, implement, and optimize international full-funnel performance marketing strategies in alignment with business goals.
- Plan and manage budget-oriented online campaigns across channels such as Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, and more.
- Perform target group segmentation and explore/test new performance channels and customer journeys to scale user growth.
- Monitor costs per acquisition (CPI, CPL, etc.) and optimize budget allocation.
- Build marketing funnels and campaigns to activate users of the CUJU App.
- Track and analyze campaign performance using KPIs and tools like Looker Studio, Excel, or CRM systems.
- Derive actionable insights and implement continuous improvements.
- Collaborate closely with internal teams (Content, Product, Data, etc.) and external agencies to align campaign goals and execution.
- Conduct market benchmarking, user persona, and competitor analyses to identify new opportunities.
- Support cross-market learning and rollouts based on campaign insights.
- Track campaign performance using key KPIs and conversion rates, reporting regularly to stakeholders with optimization suggestions.
- Conduct A/B testing of content, ads, and landing pages, etc.
- Monitor budget efficiency and propose budget allocation according to objectives.

### Qualifications
- At least 3 years of practical experience in Paid Media/Performance Marketing.
- Profound knowledge of tools like Google Ads, Meta Ads, and LinkedIn Ads Manager.
- Strong analytical mindset with in-depth knowledge of tracking, analytical tools, and Excel.
- Data-driven decision-making skills and experience with performance KPIs.
- A degree in Marketing, Economics, Data Science, or a related field.
- **Bonus:** Experience with CRM systems, Looker Studio, or marketing automation tools.
- Fluent German and English skills (Portuguese an advantage).
- Willingness to occasionally work on-site in Hamburg.
