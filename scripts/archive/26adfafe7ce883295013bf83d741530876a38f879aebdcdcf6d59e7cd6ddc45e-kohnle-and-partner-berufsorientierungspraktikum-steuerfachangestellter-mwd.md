### Description

Kohnle & Partner is described as a distinctive tax law firm, setting itself apart with state-of-the-art technology, digital processes, dynamic growth, and a team of leading experts. With over 45 years of success, the firm has established itself as one of the region's leading practices in Baden, serving companies and private individuals as competent partners, passionate problem-solvers, and innovative forward-thinkers. They are committed to providing top-tier working conditions, including modern offices, above-average compensation, and a highly motivated team, fostering an environment where employees genuinely enjoy their work and recommend the firm.

This "Berufsorientierungspraktikum" (vocational orientation internship) offers a unique opportunity to gain comprehensive insights into the world of tax consulting. It is specifically designed for individuals interested in economic contexts who wish to explore the diverse tasks of a tax assistant. The firm invites you to become part of their team at one of their locations in Karlsruhe, Weingarten, Offenburg, or Karlsbad.

### Responsibilities

During your vocational orientation internship, you will gain practical, hands-on experience and learn about the multifaceted responsibilities of a tax assistant, which include:

*   **Bookkeeping:** Understanding and assisting with various accounting tasks.
*   **Tax Declarations:** Gaining insight into the preparation of tax returns for clients.
*   **Client Support:** Learning how to effectively interact with and support clients.
*   **Professional Exploration:** Discovering the daily routines and challenges of the profession to determine if this exciting and future-proof career path aligns with your interests and aspirations.

Successful completion of the internship offers the direct opportunity to commence an apprenticeship ("Ausbildung") within the firm.

### Qualifications

To be a successful candidate for this internship, you should possess the following:

*   **Educational Background:** A minimum of a secondary school certificate (Realschule), with Abitur (high school diploma) or Fachabitur (subject-specific high school diploma) being a plus.
*   **Strengths:** A good understanding of numbers, meticulousness, and a structured approach to work.
*   **Academic Interests:** An interest in Mathematics, Economics, and German is advantageous.
*   **Soft Skills:** Strong communication skills, reliability, and a team-oriented mindset.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** Basic knowledge of MS Office, particularly Excel and Word, is helpful.
