**FIFO Camp Coordinator – WA based – FIFO 2:1 or 4:2 roster**

DDH1 Drilling is seeking a Camp Coordinator. 

**A bit about DDH1 Drilling** 

DDH1 Drilling is a leading provider of drilling services to the Australian mineral exploration and mining industry. For over 19 years, our reputation has been built on a track record of high performance, quality drilling and our skilled team who make it possible.

When you join DDH1, you become an important part of our team. It’s not all work, we recognise your commitment with events, drilling bonuses and our employee benefits program, saving you money at top Australian retailers. 

DDH1 is part of Perenti Limited, an ASX-listed global mining services group, providing our employees with access unique career development pathways, and world-class opportunities.

**What we are looking for:** 

*   Ability to perform food preparation, cooking duties and other camp maintenance tasks.
*   Previous experience in a similar role would be highly regarded.
*   Working autonomously, you will be required to provide catering services for 12 to 20 people on our exploration-based sites with camp-style accommodation.
*   The candidate will need to have a degree of flexibility due to crew operations running 24 / 7.
*   We are committed to safety and quality in the workplace, and you would be expected to have the same level of focus across your area of responsibility.

**Your primary responsibilities will include, but are not limited to:**        

*   Providing three meals per day for each shift;
*   Ensuring a clean and hygienic workplace by regular maintenance and cleaning of cooking and camp facilities.
*   Proactively use time to prepare for upcoming meals;
*   Proactively contribute to a safety-conscious work environment;
*   Ensuring catering, food service and camp upkeep are managed to meet health, hygiene and quality standards;
*   Ensuring production and timely delivery of meals in line with site requirements;
*   Ensuring stock and stores areas are kept clean, tidy and orderly;
*   Ensuring the kitchen and dining area are kept clean and hygienic at all times;
*   Maintain cleanliness and hygiene standards across common areas, including accommodation and bathrooms
*   Undertake any small tasks to assist the drilling team.

If you meet the above criteria and aspire to work with a leading drilling organisation, apply now.

DDH1 is an equal opportunity employer and welcomes applications from individuals of all genders and cultural backgrounds.
