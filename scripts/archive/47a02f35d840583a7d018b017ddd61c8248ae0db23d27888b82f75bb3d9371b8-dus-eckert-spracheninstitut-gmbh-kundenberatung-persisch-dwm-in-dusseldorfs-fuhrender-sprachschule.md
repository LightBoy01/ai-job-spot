### Description

DÜS Eckert is a leading provider of German and integration courses in Düsseldorf and Essen, having successfully taught German to over 30,000 refugees and migrants since 2004. The organization plays an essential role in supporting active social participation for its participants. Specializing in integration courses and German exams, DÜS Eckert also offers individualized online language courses for innovative companies.

They are looking for a **Customer Advisor specializing in Farsi/Dari/Pashto** to join their growing team full-time (40 hours per week) at their Düsseldorf Hauptbahnhof location. Work hours are Monday to Thursday until 5:30 PM or 7:00 PM, and Fridays until 4:00 PM. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys working with individuals from diverse cultures in a fast-growing social educational enterprise.

### Responsibilities

As a Customer Advisor, you will be the first point of contact for participants, ensuring they feel welcome through your friendly and competent demeanor. Your core duties will include:

*   **First Point of Contact:** Serve as the primary contact for participants, ensuring a welcoming and supportive environment.
*   **Consultation:** Provide optimal guidance to participants regarding their questions about courses and exams, in person, by phone, and via email.
*   **Registration:** Handle the registration of participants for German courses and exams, meticulously processing applications for various federal agencies.
*   **Participant Support:** Address participant concerns and complaints, coordinating solutions in close collaboration with teachers, administration, and school management.
*   **Team Collaboration:** Work closely with other customer advisors and the administration team to ensure seamless operations.

### Qualifications

To succeed in this role, you should possess a blend of language proficiency, interpersonal skills, and professional experience:

*   **Language Proficiency:**
    *   Native-level proficiency (at least C1) in **German** and **Farsi/Dari/Pashto**.
    *   Fluent English skills (at least B1).
*   **Multilingualism (Preferred):** Proficiency in other relevant languages such as Ukrainian, Russian, Arabic, etc.
*   **Social & Intercultural Competence:** A genuine enjoyment of interacting with people, active listening skills, empathy, and comfort in intercultural contexts.
*   **Communication Skills:** Excellent communication abilities, combined with an organized and reliable work ethic.
*   **Professional Experience (Preferred):** Previous experience in customer consulting, customer care, or sales.
*   **Passion for Languages (Optional):** Enthusiasm for language learning.
