**Job Summary:**
================

  
At Iron Mountain, we protect what our customers value most, from the everyday to the extraordinary, while helping them bridge the physical and digital world. Today, we help our customers, including 95% of  
the Fortune 1000, to preserve priceless art, restore classic films, outsource their data centers around the globe and more. Our people have the opportunity to bring their creativity to a workplace that thrives on  
change. Here you will be part of a team that doesn't just embrace what's exceptional. It is exceptional. The Director, Product Engineering role will be a part of the Global Product Engineering team at Iron  
Mountain, reporting to the Engineering leadership - Global Platforms.

  
The ideal candidate will have experience building enterprise-scale technology platforms, and implementing digital solutions for customers globally.

  
As a senior leader in Global Product Engineering, you'll combine your years of proven expertise with a drive to create innovative technology through solid engineering practices. You&#39;ll lead a highly inspired and  
inquisitive team of technologists, data scientists, solution engineers, and architects who are developing and deploying applications to the highest standards.

  
The ideal candidate would have the ability to attract, recruit, and retain the industry's best talent, and have the technical skills to effectively guide and participate in architectural discussions, technology roadmaps,  
and decisions to ensure we build impactful, compelling, and scalable solutions in an incremental fashion. The role is responsible for maintaining our competitive positioning to ensure that we have the best in  
class solution offerings that meet the regional needs for our clients.

  
Success in this role requires strategic and innovative thinking, a proven track record of delivering new customer-facing software products at scale, rigorous analytical skills, and a passion for automation and  
data-driven approaches to solving problems. The ideal leader will consistently lead by example, be a role model, and a strong change agent for your team and the larger technology team.
