About Rockefeller Capital Management:

Rockefeller Capital Management was established in 2018 as a leading independent financial advisory services firm. Originally founded in 1882 as the family office of John D. Rockefeller, the Firm has evolved to offer strategic advice to ultra- and high-net-worth individuals and families, institutions, and corporations from offices in 30 markets throughout the United States, as well as an office in London. The Firm oversees $151 billion in client assets as of December 31, 2024.

Position:

We are seeking a skilled AI/ML Data Engineer with 4–5 years of hands-on experience in data engineering and analytics. The individual should have expertise in at least one of the following platforms: Databricks, Snowflake, or Microsoft Fabric (with preference for Databricks /Microsoft Fabric). The role involves designing and implementing scalable data solutions, ensuring performance, and supporting business needs with reliable and secure data delivery. The role also values exposure to machine learning and AI workflows using Azure or open-source frameworks such as scikit-learn, making a great opportunity to work the intersection of data engineering and applied AI.

Responsibilities:

*   Design, develop, and maintain scalable ETL/ELT pipelines and data models using Microsoft Fabric, Databricks, or Snowflake.
*   Build and optimize lakehouse and warehouse architectures to support analytics and reporting needs.
*   Implement data governance practices including data quality, cataloging, lineage, and access controls.
*   Integrate Azure data services (Data Factory, Synapse, Functions, Logic Apps) into workflows.
*   Support Power BI integration for reporting and visualization across platforms.
*   Contribute to CI/CD pipelines and version control for reliable deployments.
*   Collaborate with analytics and business teams to deliver data-driven solutions.
*   Monitor, troubleshoot, and enhance data pipelines for performance, reliability, and scalability.
*   Apply machine learning workflows using Azure ML or open-source frameworks (e.g., scikit-learn).
*   Stay current with emerging technologies in data engineering and AI.

Qualifications:

*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or related field.
*   4–6 years of hands-on experience in data engineering or development.
*   Experience with at least one major data platform:
    *   Databricks: Spark (PySpark/Scala), Delta Lake, notebooks (preferred).
    *   Microsoft Fabric: Lakehouse, Warehouse, Data Engineering pipelines, Power BI integration (preferred).
    *   Snowflake: SQL, performance tuning, data modeling.
*   Proficiency in SQL and one programming language (Python, Scala, or Java).
*   Exposure to machine learning libraries (e.g., scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch) and ML services (Azure ML, Databricks ML).
*   Familiarity with Azure cloud services, DevOps practices, and CI/CD pipelines.

Skills:

*   Proficiency in SQL and one programming language (Python, Scala, or Java)
*   Experience with Spark and Delta Lake for scalable data processing
*   Hands-on with Microsoft Fabric, Databricks, or Snowflake platforms
*   Familiarity with Azure Data Factory, Synapse, Logic Apps, and other Azure services
*   Integration of Power BI for data visualization and reporting
*   Knowledge of CI/CD pipelines and version control tools (e.g., Git)
*   Exposure to machine learning libraries such as scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or PyTorch
*   Experience with Azure ML or Databricks ML services
*   Strong communication and collaboration skills
*   Problem-solving mindset and ability to work cross-functionally
*   Adaptability and eagerness to learn emerging technologies
*   Ownership and accountability in a mid-level developer capacity
