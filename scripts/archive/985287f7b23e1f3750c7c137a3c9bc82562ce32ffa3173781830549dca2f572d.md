**About the Role**  
Join our team as a Senior Meeting Planner where you'll be the mastermind behind unforgettable events! Your expertise will shine as you manage high-profile client programs, from budgeting to execution, ensuring everything runs smoothly. You’ll be the crucial bridge connecting clients with our talented teams, all while adding value with your insights and creative solutions. If you love making events extraordinary and enjoy a challenge, this is the place for you!

**You’re Good At**

*   Crafting and executing detailed event plans with precision and flair.
*   Managing budgets and communicating financials effectively to clients.
*   Liaising between various teams to ensure seamless event execution.
*   Problem-solving on the fly to keep everything on track.
*   Providing top-tier client service with a friendly touch!

**You Might Also Have**

*   A 4-year college degree and a knack for the details.
*   At least 4 years of experience in Congress/Convention and meeting management.
*   Familiarity with the Pharma industry (a plus!).
*   Proficiency in Microsoft Office, especially Excel and PowerPoint.
*   Strong leadership skills and the ability to motivate teammates.

**You Should Know** 

*   This position operates on Eastern Standard Time
*   This role is hybrid, working both in office and onsite (West Chester, PA or Raynham, MA) 
*   Travel is required for this position
