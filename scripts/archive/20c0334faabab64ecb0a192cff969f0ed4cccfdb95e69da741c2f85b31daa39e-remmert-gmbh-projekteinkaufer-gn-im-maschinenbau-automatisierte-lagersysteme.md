### Description\nRemmert specializes in automated storage and logistics systems. We know how to store sheet metal, long goods, and workpieces in a space-saving manner and move them fully automatically. Mechanical engineering, automation, and logistics — an industry with future prospects!\n\nSince 1945, we have been a family-run company, and we live by those values: development perspectives, communication at eye level, and short decision paths.\n\nWe are continuously growing and are looking for colleagues to actively support our team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Demand Determination & Procurement Planning:** Close collaboration with sales, design, and project management to derive technical specifications, create bills of material, and procurement schedules.\n*   **Supplier Selection & Development:** Identification, evaluation, and selection of suitable suppliers, considering quality, ESG, and certification standards. Building and maintaining long-term supplier relationships.\n*   **Contract & Price Negotiations:** Drafting and negotiating framework agreements, price structures, and payment terms. Ensuring cost-optimized procurement while considering technical requirements.\n*   **Technical Risk & Quality Management:** Reviewing technical specifications, standards, and compatibility. Collaboration with quality assurance and management of deviations.\n*   **Supply Chain Tracking & Schedule Management:** Monitoring delivery dates, coordinating deliveries, and supporting assembly and installation.\n*   **Cost Efficiency & Budget Control:** Creating cost overviews, conducting price analyses, and contributing to budget planning.\n*   **Documentation & Reporting:** Maintaining procurement documentation and providing regular status reports to project management.\n*   **Process Optimization & Digitalization:** Contributing to the further development of procurement processes and the use of digital tools for supplier evaluation.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed technical training and commercial further education with a focus on mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, or a comparable qualification; ideally, a degree in industrial engineering, supply chain management, or relevant further training.\n*   Profound knowledge in mechanical engineering, ideally in the areas of shelving steel construction, load carriers, conveying technology, and automation components.\n*   Experience in project purchasing, especially in the project planning phase.\n*   Strong negotiation skills, knowledge of contract law, and supplier management.\n*   SAP knowledge (order processing, invoice workflow) is an advantage.\n*   Team player, structured way of working, and high self-initiative.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced Project Purchaser to join a stable, family-run company that is a leader in automated logistics systems. Remmert genuinely invests in its employees, offering a comprehensive benefits package including mobile work, flexible hours, profit sharing, and significant support for professional development and continuous learning. The culture emphasizes open communication, short decision paths, and a strong work-life balance with engaging employee events and health programs. If you're looking for a role where your contributions directly impact innovative projects and where long-term growth is actively fostered, this could be your next strategic move.
