### Description

**Our Mission**

encentive develops intelligent energy management solutions that help industrial companies reduce energy consumption and CO₂ emissions, and control their facilities more efficiently. Our platform connects IoT data, AI-based predictions, and optimization algorithms into a scalable system that enables real-time sustainable energy decisions.

As a **Lead Energy Performance Engineer**, you will be the key technical figure at the interface between Engineering, Product, and Customer Success. You will develop prototypes and MVPs in Python, lead a team of Energy Optimization Engineers, and ensure that data-driven innovations are efficiently transferred from practice into our product platform. You will actively promote the professional and personal development of your colleagues.

### Responsibilities

*   **Team Leadership & Mentoring:**
    Lead a team of at least two Energy Optimization Engineers. Foster their development through coaching, code reviews, and structured knowledge transfer. Shape a team culture that combines technical excellence, personal responsibility, and a passion for learning.
*   **Prototyping & MVP Development:**
    Independently develop Python-based MVPs, scripts, and tools that demonstrate new optimization or analysis functionalities. Test and document results, and hand over validated solutions to the Product and Development teams.
*   **Data Analysis & Performance Monitoring:**
    Analyze energy consumption, forecast, and optimization data. Derive recommendations for action, KPI improvements, and new optimization strategies from the results.
*   **Interface Management:**
    Work closely with the Commercial Customer Success and Technical Customer Success Teams to define and implement technical requirements from customer projects. Coordinate with the Product Team for the handover of MVPs and support integration into the development process.
*   **R&D & Innovation:**
    Actively drive research topics such as reference consumption models, new algorithms, or optimization strategies, and develop prototypes or Proof-of-Concepts.

### Qualifications

**Education & Experience**

*   Completed degree in Energy Technology, Process Engineering, Physics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or a comparable field.
*   At least **5 years of professional experience** in energy management, data engineering, optimization, or a comparable technical environment.
*   Initial **leadership experience** or proven competence in mentoring and coaching technical teams.
*   Experience working with **cross-functional teams** (Product, Development, Customer Success).

**Technical Know-how**

*   Very good knowledge of **Python** (pandas, numpy).
*   Experience in **building MVPs, data pipelines, and reporting tools.**
*   Understanding of physical systems such as refrigeration systems, heat pumps, combined heat and power plants (BHKWs), or charging infrastructure.
*   Knowledge of **optimization or forecasting models** (e.g., MILP, heuristic methods, ML).

**Personal Strengths**

*   You **take responsibility** for technical results, team performance, and process quality.
*   You communicate clearly, structured, and **solution-oriented** – both with engineers and with Product and Customer Success teams.
*   You promote **independence, quality, and continuous development** within the team.
*   You work **hands-on and strategically** at the same time.

**Languages**

*   Fluent **German and English** in spoken and written word.
