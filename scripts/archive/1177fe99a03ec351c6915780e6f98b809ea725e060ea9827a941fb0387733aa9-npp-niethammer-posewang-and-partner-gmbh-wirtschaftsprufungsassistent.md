### Description\nNPP Niethammer, Posewang & Partner GmbH is a mid-sized, multidisciplinary auditing and tax consultancy firm located in Hamburg. As a member of the Alliott Global Alliance, the firm is well-equipped to handle cross-border issues for its diverse clientele, which includes mid-sized companies, listed corporations, and pan-European trading companies. Due to ongoing growth, NPP is seeking an engaged Audit Assistant to join their team. This role offers an excellent opportunity to start a career in a modern environment, benefit from personal mentorship, and become part of an international network.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Audit individual and consolidated financial statements.\n*   Prepare and audit annual financial statements in accordance with commercial law.\n*   Participate in tax and business consulting activities.\n*   Maintain direct contact with clients.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   A completed degree in economics or a comparable educational background.\n*   Demonstrated persuasiveness through professional demeanor, high commitment, flexibility, and strong team spirit.\n*   The ability to quickly familiarize oneself with new subject matters and issues.\n*   Proficiency in IT and MS-Office applications, with ideal knowledge of DATEV and Audicon software.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an ambitious individual looking to launch a career in auditing within a supportive and growth-oriented firm. NPP prides itself on a welcoming, familial atmosphere with flat hierarchies where every team member is valued from day one. You'll benefit from extensive professional development support, including a personal mentor, and a broad spectrum of interesting, varied tasks that go beyond traditional audits, often with an international scope. The company truly invests in its people, offering flexible working hours, home office options, 30 days of vacation, and an attractive office in the heart of Hamburg. Beyond the core benefits, perks like daily fresh coffee and fruit, company events, a job bike scheme, and access to FutureBens underscore a commitment to employee well-being and a positive work-life balance. This role offers the chance to develop professionally and personally within a modern and international network.
