### Description

i-flow is a thriving software company specializing in Industrial IoT. We're on a mission to accelerate digital transformation for leading manufacturing clients, enabling robust, data-driven use cases at scale. We're looking for a talented Senior Backend Developer to join our team and make a significant impact on our core platform and customer success.

### Responsibilities

As a Senior Backend Developer, you will:

*   Assume technical ownership for central components throughout the development, testing, deployment, and operation of our internationally deployed IoT software platform.
*   Contribute significantly to the continuous evolution of our core software, with a strong focus on scalability, performance, and ease-of-use. You'll have a genuine influence on key technical and architectural decisions.
*   Actively shape our development roadmap, bringing innovative ideas to the table and taking end-to-end responsibility from initial concept to successful rollout.
*   Continuously advance our technology stack, collaboratively evaluating and integrating new tools, frameworks, and patterns.
*   Engage in a variety of dynamic and interdisciplinary tasks, including conducting technical demonstrations for our clients.

### Qualifications

We're looking for someone who:

*   Possesses 3+ years of experience in backend development with NodeJS, ideally utilizing TypeScript, and has explored lower-level languages such as Go.
*   Demonstrates a keen interest in industrial automation protocols, including OPC UA, MQTT, Modbus TCP, or Siemens S7.
*   Is proficient in leveraging LLMs or other AI tools to enhance daily efficiency, whether for debugging, documentation, or prototyping new ideas.
*   Has proven experience in designing, implementing, and extending REST APIs.
*   Brings initial experience in the deployment and operation of modern software, utilizing technologies like Docker, Kubernetes, GitOps, or classic CI/CD pipelines.
*   Enjoys engaging in technical discussions, takes full responsibility for their code, and consistently delivers pragmatic solutions, even under tight deadlines.
*   Is familiar with modern frontend frameworks such as React, Vue, or Angular, or excels at collaborating closely with frontend teams to ensure seamless interface integration.
*   Has a strong sense for user experience (UX), can clearly articulate complex technical concepts, and persuasively communicate their ideas both within the team and to clients.
*   Holds a degree in Computer Science or a comparable field.
*   Is proficient with relational and NoSQL databases (e.g., MongoDB, MySQL, InfluxDB) for managing transactional data, time-series data, or scalable data storage.

While not expected to master every single technology listed, you should bring solid experience in several of these areas and possess a strong drive for continuous professional development. C2-level German language proficiency and a residency in Germany are required for this role.
