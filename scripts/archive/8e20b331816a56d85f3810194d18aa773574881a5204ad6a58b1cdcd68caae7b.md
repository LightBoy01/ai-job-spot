Your New Role and Team Sanctuary AI, a world leader in building AI-based control systems for humanoid robots, is looking to hire a Director, Product Development & Management to join our team. Reporting to the Chief Product & Technology Officer, this pivotal role will oversee the product management group, driving the development and execution of our product strategy. This role is ideal for a visionary leader who combines deep industry knowledge with a passion for innovative solutions.   
  
**Our Success Criteria**  
*   Work with leadership to develop and implement a comprehensive product strategy and roadmap
*   Lead, mentor, and develop a team of product and program managers, fostering a high-performance culture that delivers on ambitious goals
*   Align product development initiatives with business goals and market needs
*   Thrive in ambiguity and bring structure to complexity. You’ve built processes, not just followed them
*   Support the product development team, ensuring timely delivery of high-quality products
*   Communicate product vision, strategy, and progress to senior management and stakeholders
*   Oversee the end-to-end product development lifecycle, from ideation to launch
*   Collaborate with engineers, designers, and other technical teams to develop product specifications and ensure feasibility
*   Understand and contribute to the technical aspects of product development
*   Ensure the product meets technical specifications and performance standards
*   Define and track key performance indicators to measure success
*   Gather and analyze data to make informed decisions and drive continuous improvement
*   Manage product lifecycle, including updates, improvements, and end-of-life strategies
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams (including engineering, marketing, sales, and customer support) to guide products from conception to launch

**About Sanctuary AI**

Founded in 2018, Sanctuary builds humanoid robots and a novel control system for them that integrates symbolic logic and reasoning with data-driven robot foundation models. We use our robots to collect vision, audio, touch, and proprioception data from the perspective of the robot while they perform real-world work tasks. We use that data to train multimodal robot foundation models. Because our systems are vertically integrated, we can design, deploy, and refine at scale. Our mission is to create the world's first human-like intelligence in general-purpose robots.
