We are looking for a **Senior Account Executive** to join our Health team!  

   
**Responsibilities:**  

*   Proactively, efficiently, and independently manage projects for healthcare and pharma accounts 
*   Day-to-day client contact on projects; keeps teams informed of all updates and progress and identifies any emerging account problems.  
*   Establish relationships with key stakeholders, vendors, and clients and maintain regular contact. Proactively offer and implement new ways to meet client needs. 
*   Knowledge in using social media platforms to drive brand awareness and engagement. 
*   Ability to actively participate in the development and execution of social media plans. 
*   Be a hands-on team player, adding value, sharing creative ideas, and executing digital programs. 
*   Develop accurate project budgets and monitor them effectively.  
*   Prioritize own work, meet deadlines, actively communicate progress and deliverables.  
*   Develop and monitor program plans and budgets for profitability; report status to client and agency management regularly. 
*   Play a key role in account activities, including special events, media events, media relations, social media, desk-side briefings, program implementation, etc. 
*   Stays current on trends in the healthcare industry. 

   
**Qualifications:**  

We’re looking for required skills, which can come from any combination of education and experience. Diversity of thought and background are encouraged. Typical qualifications include: 

*   Bachelor’s degree in Communications, Public Relations, Journalism, English, Marketing, or a related field preferred 
*   Pharma experience 
*   Three to five years of experience in a public relations agency or similar position
*   Proficiency in using AI tools like ChatGPT for research, content creation, and task automation is required.
*   Ability to work in a multi-faceted, fast-paced environment
