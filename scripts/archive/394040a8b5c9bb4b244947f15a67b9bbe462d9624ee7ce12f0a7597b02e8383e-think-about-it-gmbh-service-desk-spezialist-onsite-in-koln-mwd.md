### Description\nJoin the think Group in Cologne as a Service Desk Specialist (m/f/d) and become a valuable member of our Service Desk department as soon as possible. This role is crucial for providing first-level IT support, ensuring our customers receive prompt and effective assistance for various technical issues. We are looking for dedicated individuals who are eager to contribute to a collaborative team and grow their IT expertise.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Provide telephone and remote support to customers as part of 1st Level IT Support, focusing on MS-Office, MS-Teams, MS Windows, cloud-based applications, MyPBx, authorizations, clients (notebooks, FAT-/Thin-Clients), printers, telecommunications, and media technology.\n*   Record detailed information on disruptions and service requests for hardware and software, and document them in the ticketing system (SAP ITSM).\n*   Process support tickets in subsequent steps.\n*   Analyze complex inquiries and escalate them to 2nd Level IT Support.\n*   Inform users about the progress of their support requests and ensure SLA compliance through regular tracking.\n*   Propose improvements to service quality and continuously develop processes.\n*   Maintain the Asset Management system and create Knowledge Base articles.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Successfully completed IT training, e.g., as an IT Specialist for System Integration (Fachinformatiker/-in Systemintegration), or a comparable qualification.\n*   Ideally, experience in IT Service Desk/User Helpdesk, but career changers and working students are also very welcome.\n*   Very good knowledge of Windows 10 and Microsoft Office components.\n*   Good English language skills.\n*   Desirable, but not required: Apple iOS, Blackberry Enterprise Server, Innovaphone PBX, SAP IDM, OPSI, RSA Token Management, Macmon, virtual machines, TeamViewer, media technology.\n*   Process and service orientation.\n*   Strong communication skills and a solution- and customer-oriented mindset.\n*   Organized and independent working style with high motivation.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an aspiring or junior IT professional to kickstart or advance their career in IT support. The think Group clearly values its employees, offering a comprehensive onboarding process, a buddy program, and a generous annual budget for further education, emphasizing continuous personal and professional growth. The inclusion of benefits like fitness studio access, health courses, and bike leasing, alongside team activities, points to a company culture that prioritizes employee well-being and fosters a strong sense of community and work-life balance. If you are a proactive, customer-focused individual looking for a supportive environment to develop your IT skills, this role in Cologne offers a solid foundation and a path for development.
