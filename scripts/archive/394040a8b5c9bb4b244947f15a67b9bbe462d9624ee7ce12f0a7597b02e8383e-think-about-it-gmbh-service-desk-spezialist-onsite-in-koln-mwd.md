### Description
We are looking for a Service Desk Specialist (m/w/d) to join our Service Desk department at our **Cologne** location as soon as possible. Become a part of the think Group and support us in providing first-level IT support to our customers via phone and remote access.

### Responsibilities
*   Provide first-level IT support to customers via phone and remote access, focusing on MS-Office, MS-Teams, MS Windows, cloud-based applications, MyPBx, authorizations, clients (notebooks, FAT-/Thin-Clients), printers, telecommunications, and media technology.
*   Accurately record hardware and software disruptions and service requests, documenting them in the ticketing system (SAP ITSM).
*   Process support tickets.
*   Analyze complex issues and escalate them to second-level IT support.
*   Inform users about the progress of their support requests and ensure SLA compliance through regular tracking.
*   Propose improvements to service quality and continuously develop processes.
*   Maintain the asset management system and create knowledge base articles.

### Qualifications
*   Successfully completed IT training, e.g., as an IT Specialist for System Integration, or a comparable qualification.
*   Ideally, experience in IT Service Desk/User Helpdesk, but career changers and working students are also very welcome.
*   Very good knowledge of Windows 10 and Microsoft Office components.
*   Good English language skills.
*   Desirable but not required: Apple iOS, Blackberry Enterprise Server, Innovaphone PBX, SAP IDM, OPSI, RSA Token Management, Macmon, virtual machines, TeamViewer, media technology.
*   Process- and service-oriented mindset.
*   Strong communication skills and a solution- and customer-oriented approach.
*   Organized and independent working style, high motivation.

### Author's Take
**Q1: How does the Service Desk Specialist role at think Group go beyond just "fixing tickets" to truly impact user productivity and the overall business?**

As an insider, I can tell you this role is far more than a reactive task-list. You're the first line of defense, the voice of calm in a storm, and often the unsung hero who gets our colleagues back on track. Your quick, empathetic, and effective solutions directly translate into uninterrupted work for our entire team. You're not just closing tickets; you're ensuring projects stay on schedule, critical communications flow smoothly, and everyone has the tools they need to succeed. There's immense satisfaction in knowing you've unblocked someone's day, allowing them to focus on their core responsibilities. Plus, your insights from recurring issues are invaluable for shaping our IT strategy and preventing future problems – you're truly at the pulse of our operational health.

The "Buddy-Programm" isn't just a formality; it's a genuine commitment to making sure you feel supported from day one.
