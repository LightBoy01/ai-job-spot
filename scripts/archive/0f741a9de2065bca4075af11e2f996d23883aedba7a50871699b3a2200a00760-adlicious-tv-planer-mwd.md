### Description\nadlicious is a leading Advertising Solutions Provider based in Hamburg, Germany, dedicated to creating the best advertising campaigns for clients by uniquely combining Media, Creative & Tech. With its proprietary Ad Delivery Technology, adlicious enables top brands to deliver personalized messages in real-time across the entire digital customer journey. The company is rapidly growing, currently comprising around 90 talented individuals, and is looking for a proactive TV Planner to join its dynamic team.\n\nThis role is for an engaging TV Planner (m/f/d) with 2-3 years of professional experience, who will be instrumental in integrating TV into the existing digital media portfolio and shaping cross-channel communication strategies.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Process Design & TV Integration:** Support the design and implementation of processes to integrate TV into our existing digital media portfolio.\n*   **National TV Campaign Conception & Planning:** Take responsibility for conceiving and planning national TV campaigns based on target audience, market, and competitor data, optimizing individual KPIs and cost targets.\n*   **Cross-Channel Communication Strategy Integration:** Ensure TV concepts are seamlessly integrated into the respective cross-channel communication strategies.\n*   **Presentation of Recommendations & Planning Results:** Present campaign recommendations and planning results to clients and internal stakeholders.\n*   **Collaboration with Teams:** Work closely with TV purchasing, marketers, and internal teams for the smooth execution of TV campaigns.\n*   **Campaign Monitoring & Reporting:** Manage the monitoring and reporting of campaign key figures, deriving optimization recommendations.\n*   **Market & Technology Observation (Moving Image):** Contribute to internal knowledge management, monitoring current market developments, key figures, and technical advancements in the moving image sector.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   **Experience in TV & Digital Media Planning:** Minimum of 2-3 years of professional experience in TV media planning, ideally complemented by digital media experience.\n*   **Analytical Aptitude & Affinity for Numbers:** Possess strong analytical thinking skills and a confident command of media key figures.\n*   **Proficiency with Planning enjoyment in helping build structures.\n*   **Team Player & Organized:** Be a team player with high service and results orientation, coupled with strong organizational talent.\n*   **Corporate Culture
