### Description\\nAs a new member of our client team, you will work with us on event management within our proprietary system and configure our online shops. The client team is one of currently three self-organized domain teams within ticket i/O IT, which manage their projects autonomously. It currently consists of 7 people, including a Tech Lead, a Product Owner, and five Fullstack Developers.\\n\\nOverall, you can look forward to 30 colleagues in the IT and product team. You will drive new features and the further development of our online ticketing software with us in agile sprints. TypeScript (Node.js) is our programming language of choice, and we rely on the benefits of NestJS in the backend and ReactJS in the frontend. We build and host our application on AWS, creating a microservices-based and scalable infrastructure. Are you ready to join?\\n\\nWho are we? We see ourselves as an innovative company in the ticketing and event industry. We combine years of experience from event, leisure, sports, and trade fair sectors with the best technology to create an intuitive software solution.
