At Parallel Web Systems, we are bringing a new web to life: it’s built with, by, and for AIs. Our work spans innovations across crawling, indexing, ranking, retrieval, and reasoning systems. Our first product is a set of APIs for AIs to do more with web data.

We are a **fully in-person** team based in Palo Alto, CA. Our organization is flat; our team is small and talent dense.

We want to talk to you if you are someone who can bring us closer to living our aspirational values:

1.  **Own customer impact -** _It’s on us to ensure real-world outcomes for our customers._
    
2.  **Obsess over craft -** _Perfect every detail because quality compounds._
    
3.  **Accelerate change -** _Ship fast, adapt faster, and move frontier ideas into production._
    
4.  **Create win-wins -** _Creatively turn trade-offs into upside._
    
5.  **Make high-conviction bets -** _Try and fail. But succeed an unfair amount._
    

**Job:** You will design and build robust API integrations within the rapidly evolving AI ecosystem. You'll be developing seamless connections between our platform and third-party AI tools - including building custom nodes, plugins, or connectors that extend functionality and unlock new workflows.

**You:** Are well-versed in the modern AI ecosystem and excited by emerging tools and APIs. Have hands on experience developing integrations, especially in the context of AI platforms, and care deeply about developer experience and efficient workflows. You are comfortable navigating documentation, debugging APIs, and building from scratch.

Our founder is [Parag Agrawal](https://www.linkedin.com/in/paragagr). Previously, he was the CEO and CTO at Twitter. Our investors include First Round Capital, Index Ventures, Khosla Ventures, and many others.
