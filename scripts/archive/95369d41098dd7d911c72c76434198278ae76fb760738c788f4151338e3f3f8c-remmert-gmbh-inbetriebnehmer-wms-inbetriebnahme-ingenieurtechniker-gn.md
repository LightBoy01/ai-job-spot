### Description\nRemmert specializes in automated storage and logistics systems, expertly managing the space-efficient and fully automated movement of sheets, long goods, and workpieces. We operate in mechanical engineering, automation, and logistics – an industry with future prospects! Since 1945, we have been a family-run company, living by principles of development opportunities, communication at eye level, and short decision-making paths. We are continuously growing and seeking colleagues to actively support our team.\n\nAs a **Commissioning Engineer (gn)**, you will be based at our site in Löhne and travel internationally to our customers with a 50% travel readiness (approx. 100 overnight stays per year).\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Commissioning of software at national and international customer sites\n- Installation, simulation, and testing of software\n- Conducting training sessions\n- Configuration of databases and software\n- Customer workshops for process definition and optimization\n- Roll-out of updates\n- Replacement and adjustment of electrical components\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed vocational training in automation or information technology, or a comparable qualification\n- Good knowledge of application installations (especially Windows)\n- Career starter or with professional experience in the relevant field\n- Affinity for control technology desirable\n- Strong communication skills in German and English\n- Independent and reliable working style\n- A class B driving license\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an adaptable and technically skilled individual eager to work internationally and make a direct impact within the growing field of automated logistics. Remmert, as a stable, family-run business, truly invests in its employees' well-being and growth, offering excellent benefits like hybrid work options, profit sharing, and comprehensive health/pension plans. The role provides unique challenges and rewards for those looking to expand their horizons and contribute to a supportive team environment.
