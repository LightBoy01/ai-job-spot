**About the team:**

At SumUp, we empower millions of small businesses around the world with simple and intuitive financial solutions. Our Data Platform enables the analytics, insights, and data-driven products that drive smarter decisions and help our teams scale efficiently.

We're looking for a **Senior Technical Product Manager – Data Platform** to shape how data is ingested, stored, and accessed across SumUp. In this role, you'll combine technical depth with product vision to build high-impact platform capabilities that support teams company-wide.

This is a highly collaborative role where you'll work closely with engineering, analytics, InfoSec, compliance, and internal data users to develop scalable workflows, evolve our Iceberg-based data lake, and enable self-service tools that unlock the full value of data at SumUp.

  
**What you'll do:**

*   Lead discovery, prioritisation, and roadmap development for key Data Platform initiatives.
*   Work closely with engineering, analytics, security, and compliance to design and implement scalable ingestion and storage solutions.
*   Improve the resilience, reliability, and observability of the platform using data-driven insights and operational signals.
*   Improve platform performance and reliability using operational signals and insights from data.
*   Champion self-service tooling, automated schema handling, data lineage, and platform observability.
*   Monitor and define key success indicators such as adoption, data freshness, latency, reliability, cost efficiency, and error rates.

**You'll be great for this role if you:**

*   Have a proven track record of delivering impactful products in complex technical environments, and you thrive on turning ambiguity into clarity and direction.
*   Bring hands-on experience in data, analytics or software engineering, allowing you to speak the language of engineers and build strong, collaborative partnerships.
*   Use metrics such as reliability, latency and data freshness to inform decisions and continuously improve platform performance.
*   Communicate clearly with both technical and non-technical stakeholders, aligning teams around shared outcomes and influencing without authority.
*   Can balance multiple priorities effectively, delivering short-term value while driving long-term platform strategy forward.
