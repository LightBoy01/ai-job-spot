### Description

The Model Car World Group, encompassing BlueBrixx and Model Car World, stands as a leading force in the European online retail of model cars and clamp bricks. With approximately 400 employees and a strong online presence complemented by growing brand stores across Germany and Europe, the group is dynamic, growth-oriented, and committed to delivering high-quality, diverse products to its dedicated communities.

We are seeking an enthusiastic and experienced **Influencer Marketing & Cooperations Manager (all genders)** to join our Marketing team immediately at our Rüsselsheim am Main location. This full-time, permanent position is crucial for expanding our brand presence and driving growth through strategic partnerships and community engagement.

### Responsibilities

*   Strategically build and operatively implement Instagram Influencer Marketing from the ground up, covering strategy development, creator selection, and execution.
*   Advance our existing YouTube Influencer Marketing efforts in close collaboration with our external agency.
*   Establish a robust technical setup, including tools, databases, and reporting mechanisms, for comprehensive tracking, evaluation, and performance measurement of all cooperation activities.
*   Identify, engage, and nurture relationships with relevant content creators across diverse niches such as model building, lifestyle, technology, clamp bricks, and gaming.
*   Develop and execute impactful brand and e-commerce cooperations aimed at boosting reach, driving revenue growth, and enhancing brand awareness.
*   Proactively acquire new partner companies and cultivate long-term, thematically aligned D2C collaborations to significantly increase sales.
*   Continuously monitor and report on defined Key Performance Indicators (e.g., Reach, Revenue, Customer Acquisition Costs) and implement ongoing optimizations.
*   Collaborate closely with our Social Media, Performance Marketing, and E-commerce teams to ensure seamless execution of cross-channel campaigns.

### Qualifications

*   A completed degree in Marketing, Communication, Media, or an equivalent qualification.
*   Demonstrable multi-year experience in Influencer Marketing within a D2C context, gained both on agency and in-house sides.
*   2-4 years of professional experience in successfully building and managing influencer campaigns.
*   Proven expertise in establishing structures, tools, and processes for professional cooperation management.
*   A deep understanding of the YouTube platform and its creator ecosystem, particularly within special interest and hobby communities.
*   An excellent sense for target groups and communities in areas like model building, technology, pop culture, or related themed worlds.
*   Strong team orientation, excellent communication skills, and a genuine passion for creative, community-driven marketing.
*   An analytical mindset, structured approach to work, and a high degree of self-responsibility.
*   Very good written and spoken German and English language skills.
*   High level of commitment and a strong sense of responsibility.
