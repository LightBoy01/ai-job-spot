### Description

It's time for a transformative change – in the energy transition, in mindsets, and in your career. As a Senior Project Manager for Wind Energy, you will be a pivotal force in Germany's journey towards renewable energy, actively contributing to the widespread adoption of wind power. Your initial major undertaking, commencing in 2026, involves the ambitious construction of 14 new wind power plants in Brandenburg.

This role offers an attractive annual salary ranging from €80,000 to €100,000, complemented by performance-based bonuses (Tantieme). You will benefit from flexible working hours, substantial home office possibilities, and excellent career development prospects within a dynamic sector. Join a dedicated team and independently realize impactful wind park projects across key regions including Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Brandenburg, and Thuringia, truly making a difference in Germany's sustainable future.

### Responsibilities

*   Conceive, plan, and strategically manage complex wind energy projects.
*   Lead negotiations with landowners and local municipalities, and maintain effective communication with relevant public institutions and authorities.
*   Initiate and meticulously oversee environmental permitting processes and competitive tender procedures.
*   Commission, direct, and closely monitor the work of external expert consultants and assessors.
*   Coordinate all necessary discussions and agreements with grid operators regarding grid connection infrastructure.
*   Prepare and manage participation in tender processes for project realization.
*   Develop comprehensive business plans and detailed project cost calculations, accurately assessing the economic viability of each project.
*   Assume overall accountability for the timely, quality-compliant, and cost-effective execution of wind energy projects, from initial planning stages through to final acceptance by investors.
*   Proactively initiate and steer measures for project optimization, collaborating effectively with internal departments and/or external service providers and consultants.
*   Produce thorough project documentation and ensure continuous, transparent internal and external reporting.

### Qualifications

*   A completed university degree in a natural science or engineering discipline, such as Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Geography, or Landscape Planning, or an equivalent professional qualification.
*   A minimum of 5 years of proven experience in project management within the wind energy sector.
*   Excellent proficiency in German, both written and spoken (at least C1 level).
*   Possession of a valid Class B driver's license.

From an insider's perspective, the true excitement isn't just in the engineering marvel of a wind turbine, but in the profound societal impact you create. Imagine seeing a project you've nurtured from concept to completion, knowing that those towering turbines are not just structures, but symbols of progress, quietly powering thousands of homes with clean energy. It's the tangible realization of the energy transition right before your eyes, the constant collaboration with diverse stakeholders – from local communities to government agencies – and the knowledge that every challenge overcome brings us closer to a greener Germany. You're not just managing tasks; you're orchestrating a vital part of our nation's future, and that sense of purpose is truly exhilarating.

**Question 2: How does the company foster career growth and a supportive environment, especially with the hybrid work model?**

Our culture is built on trust and empowerment. As a Senior Project Manager, you'll find that while the responsibilities are significant, you're not left to fend for yourself. We actively promote continuous learning and provide clear pathways for advancing into more strategic or leadership roles – many of our current leaders started exactly where you would. Regarding our hybrid model, it's more than just a policy; it's how we operate. We understand that flexibility allows you to perform your best, so we equip you with the tools and autonomy needed to manage your projects effectively from home or the office. Regular virtual team meetings, collaborative platforms, and a genuinely open communication policy ensure that despite physical distance, we remain a cohesive, supportive unit, always ready to lend expertise or a helping hand.
