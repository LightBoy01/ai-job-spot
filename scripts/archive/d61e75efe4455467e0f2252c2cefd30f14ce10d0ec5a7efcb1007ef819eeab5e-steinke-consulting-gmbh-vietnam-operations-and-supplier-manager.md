We are supporting a rapidly growing German engineering company that specialises in taking electronic products from concept to mass production.

Tasks
-----

About the Role

We are looking for a **Vietnam-based Operations & Supplier Manager** who will be the **key person on the ground**:

*   Manage and develop **suppliers** (focus on HCMC area)
*   Coordinate **freelancers** and support building a local team
*   Drive **day-to-day operations, logistics, and processes**
*   Use **ClickUp** and modern project management to keep work structured and transparent
*   Communicate daily in **English** with the international team

The role starts as **freelance / independent contractor** with a clear **growth path toward a future Country / Subsidiary Manager** position for the right person.

What You Will Do

*   Identify, evaluate, and onboard **suppliers and manufacturing partners** in Vietnam (electronics, mechanics, or related engineering fields)
*   Request and consolidate **quotations, prices, lead times** and track **supplier performance**
*   Visit suppliers on-site around Ho Chi Minh City, follow up on production and quality issues
*   Coordinate **freelancers in Vietnam**: tasks, timelines, hours, quality, HR
*   Use **ClickUp** as the main tool to:
*   Plan and prioritise tasks
*   Track progress and deadlines
*   Maintain templates, SOPs, dashboards, and KPIs
*   Prepare **weekly status updates in English** for the international management team
*   Communicate clearly with local suppliers (Vietnamese) and international stakeholders (English)
*   Act with a high level of **integrity and professionalism** (no kickbacks / side deals)
*   Grow over time into a **leadership role** managing the future local entity and team

Requirements
------------

What We Are Looking For

*   Based in **Vietnam**, ideally in or near **Ho Chi Minh City**
*   **3+ years of experience** in:
*   Supplier management / sourcing / procurement, or
*   Operations / project management in a technical environment, or
*   Manufacturing / production in **electronics or mechanics**
*   **Very good to excellent English** (spoken and written) and fluent Vietnamese
*   Strong **communication skills** and the ability to work with international teams
*   Experience with **ClickUp** or similar tools (Jira, Asana, [Monday](http://monday.com/), etc.) and willingness to become a **ClickUp power user**
*   Strong **project management skills**: structured, reliable, good at tracking tasks, deadlines, and KPIs
*   Used to working **independently**, taking ownership and responsibility
*   Comfortable working as an **independent contractor / freelancer** with your own equipment

Benefits
--------

*   Long-term **freelance collaboration** with competitive hourly compensation
*   Work with a **modern, international engineering company**
*   High level of **autonomy and responsibility** from day one
*   Clear **career perspective** to grow into a **Country / Subsidiary Manager** role in Vietnam
