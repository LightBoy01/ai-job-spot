**Join Ventia’s Wireless Telco team in Brisbane as Contracts Administrator where you can work on meaningful projects that positively impact communities. Applications will be considered from other locations.**

**This is a permanent position offering hybrid working conditions with the opportunity to be part of a dynamic team that plays a critical role in managing our national supply chain and ensuring commercial compliance across high impact projects.** 

  
**What’s on Offer** 

*   $105k - $120k TFR (includes super) depending on skillset and experience
*   Hybrid role offering work life balance. Split your time between our modern West End office (on site gym, coffee station, cereal bar, break out areas and much more) and home
*   Career stability, supportive, collaborative team and the opportunity to develop new skills and professional development
*   Access to a variety of Health and Wellness initiatives - including the EAP, Ventia's Healthy Minds program and Healthy Bodies program
*   Employee benefits such as corporate travel discounts, Novated leases, private health discounts, credit discounts with retailers Australia wide including Woolworths, Coles, Booking.com and Expedia

  
**About the Role**  
As the Contracts Administrator you will

*   Manage onboarding and compliance of subcontractors and suppliers
*   Support commercial activities including contract preparation, variations, claims and governance
*   Collaborate with project teams to develop procurement strategies and resolve commercial issues
*   Maintain accurate commercial records and ensure compliance with relevant legislation and internal policies
*   Assist in dispute resolution, risk management and continuous improvement initiatives
*   Contribute to the development on commercial tools, templates and training

  
**Required Skills and Experience**

*   Tertiary qualifications in Law, Engineering, Quantity Surveying, Commercial Management or related field
*   Experience in a similar role 
*   Strong understanding of subcontracting principles and commercial frameworks
*   Proficiency in Microsoft Office (especially Excel), SharePoint and JIRA
*   Excellent communication, negotiation and stakeholder management skills
*   A collaborative, solutions focused mindset with high attention to detail
