### Description\\nunivativ is a project and personnel service provider that offers students and graduates exciting jobs with renowned companies. Your career is our mission, as your development is important to us.\\n\\nAre you analytically minded and excellent with numbers? Then we are looking for you as a Purchaser (m/f/d). After determining needs and agreeing on conditions, you will manage existing supplier relationships and contribute to the development of purchasing strategy and potential optimization.\\n\\nWe offer you an \"exciting career entry opportunity\" in a project with our client from the environmental/energy sector (E.ON SE). In this position, you can apply your knowledge and gain valuable practical experience. In addition to a promising career start, you will benefit from further training opportunities through free workshops and courses in our univativ academy!\\n\\n### Responsibilities\\n*   As a Purchaser (m/f/d), you are jointly responsible for the support and execution of the \"Procure-to-Pay\" (P2P) process in the operational purchasing department of E.ON SE in Essen.\\n*   You will receive SAP purchase requisitions, review their content, and create purchase orders in the SAP system, including obtaining necessary signatures and sending them to suppliers. Price invoice verification is also part of your duties.\\n*   You will ensure proper setup in SAP and the use of framework agreements within the Supply Chain Organization, and you will communicate/coordinate this with strategic purchasers.\\n*   Within the scope of sending orders, you also have a control and review function regarding requirements important to E.ON for the ordering process (e.g., data protection, human rights, and HSE).\\n*   Furthermore, you are responsible for general SAP adjustments/changes, such as account assignment changes, commitment clean-ups, and for the creation and maintenance of supplier master data.\\n\\n### Qualifications\\n*   You have a completed commercial apprenticeship or comparable qualification.\\n*   Furthermore, you have experience in the \"Procure-to-Pay\" process and strong skills in using SAP, ideally in MM (Materials Management module).\\n*   Your communication strength and experience in dealing with internal stakeholders/demand owners as well as suppliers and colleagues within the Supply Chain Organization will help you navigate a diverse work environment.\\n*   You enjoy working both independently and in a team and react openly and flexibly to changes.\\n*   High process orientation, efficient and structured way of working, and a quick grasp to familiarize yourself with new topics characterize you.\\n*   You have acquired practical knowledge of common MS Office programs in the past and are confident in using these tools.\\n*   You also speak very good German; good English skills are ideally a plus, but not a must.\\n\\n### Author's Take\\nThis role at E.ON, facilitated by univativ, presents an outstanding opportunity for individuals looking to launch or advance their career in operational purchasing within the dynamic energy sector. It combines hands-on SAP experience in the critical Procure-to-Pay process with exposure to strategic compliance areas like data protection and human rights. univativ's strong commitment to employee development, demonstrated by their academy and personal career coaching, makes this an ideal environment for continuous learning and professional growth. Ideal candidates will be analytical, process-oriented, enjoy collaborative yet independent work, and be eager to contribute to a major player in the energy industry.
