**Job Description:**

**About You**

You are passionate about connecting world-class technical talent with exciting opportunities. You thrive in fast-paced environments where creativity, strategy, and precision are essential. You are eager to design sourcing strategies that anticipate business needs, build strong pipelines, and partner with recruiters and hiring managers to bring the best developers, engineers, and technical professionals to the table.

**You Bring to Applaudo the Following Competencies:**

*   Bachelor’s degree in Business, Human Resources, Psychology, or related fields — or equivalent practical experience.
*   **1+ years of experience in sourcing or recruiting** technical talent across global markets.
*   **Mastery in candidate research and sourcing techniques**, including advanced **Boolean search** and market mapping.
*   **Proficiency in LinkedIn Recruiter and other sourcing platforms**, with advanced knowledge of search strings, filters, and pipeline management.
*   Experience leveraging **AI-powered sourcing tools** (e.g., LinkedIn AI, HireEZ, SeekOut, or similar) to expand reach, improve efficiency, and uncover hidden talent.
*   **Strong understanding of global sourcing strategies** and the ability to navigate diverse technical talent pools.
*   Analytical mindset with the ability to distinguish highly skilled developers from average talent.
*   Proactive approach to finding talent through online communities, tech events, and professional networks.
*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
*   Comfortable working with collaboration tools (Slack, Google Suite, ATS).
*   Ability to thrive under pressure, meet deadlines, and adapt quickly to changing priorities.

**You Will Be Accountable for the Following Responsibilities:**

*   Design and execute sourcing strategies to attract top software engineering and technical talent.
*   Build and maintain a steady pipeline of qualified candidates aligned with hiring priorities.
*   Partner with recruiters and hiring managers to understand role requirements and align sourcing efforts with business needs.
*   Use creative sourcing techniques (Boolean search, online communities, and events) to identify potential candidates.
*   Engage candidates through personalized outreach and nurture relationships to maintain long-term interest in Applaudo opportunities.
*   Track, measure, and report sourcing metrics to evaluate pipeline effectiveness.
*   Contribute to the development and continuous improvement of sourcing processes.
*   Collaborate with the Talent Acquisition team to meet and exceed KPIs and hiring goals.
