**Job Description:**

**Your Career**

Palo Alto Networks is expanding its strategy organization and seeking a Lead Strategy for our AI strategy & transformation team. 

The Strategy & Transformation team covers CEO-led initiatives ranging from AI to drive efficiency and effectiveness, margin improvement and central GTM initiatives.  In the short-term, this role will focus on designing & deploying AI at Scale for better Sales and Marketing initiatives and may expand to additional use-cases. The role is beyond strategy and goes deep into execution to own and drive business outcomes. 

As a key leader, you will collaborate with top executives and cross-functional teams (Product, Engineering, Finance, Sales, Enablement, Documentation, IT) to tackle mission-critical business challenges and contribute to our industry-leading growth. This role offers a supportive, dynamic, and meritocratic environment that values innovation, learning, and teamwork. In this role, your contributions will directly impact our strategic success and drive innovation in the constantly evolving security landscape.

**Your Impact**

*   Push the thinking on “art of the possible” future
*   Own and drive impactful business outcomes 
*   Drive transformative change with AI in the organization, wearing multiple hats (strategy, product & program management, execution) to do what it takes to “move the needle”
*   Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to gather insights, co-create deliverables, and drive execution to achieve business goals
*   Manage matrix teams and provide coaching & mentoring to team members
*   Structure and analyze complex business problems, develop hypotheses, experiment with AI and present data-driven recommendations
*   Develop high-quality deliverables for exec-level consumption, including business cases and roadmap/execution plans
*   Support strategy and business planning processes while fostering strategic capabilities across the organization

**Qualifications:**

**Your Experience** 

*   4+ years relevant work experience post-MBA with at least 2 years in management consulting (McKinsey, Bain, BCG, Deloitte, etc.), private equity or top-tier tech strategy or “bizops” functions (Google, Facebook, Amazon, etc).
*   MBA graduate from a top-tier B- school or similar academic credentials or “direct promote” in Consulting or equivalent military experience required 
*   Experience with AI/Gen-AI transformations and B2B GTM is a significant plus
*   Expertise in the Enterprise technology industry is a must, knowledge of cybersecurity industry is a plus but not required
*   Experience in building relationships and leading through influence
*   Experience managing projects and large-scale transformations
*   Effective verbal and written communication ability and comfort with both business and technical discussions with senior audiences
*   Great teammate, willing to passionately roll up their sleeves and work cross-functionally to get things done
*   Self-starter who thrives in fast-paced, high-growth environments with minimal supervision
