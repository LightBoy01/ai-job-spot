### Description\nJoin a rapidly growing company in the renewable energy sector, taking on the pivotal role of Head of Finance. Based near Trier (Föhren), with options for part-time (from 30h) and hybrid work, you will be responsible for the entire financial accounting, from professional leadership to process development. This diverse role focuses on balance sheets within a sustainable environment characterized by flat hierarchies and a strong emphasis on long-term development, modern working conditions, and personal freedom.\n\n### Responsibilities\nYou will lead the financial accounting according to HGB, bringing your expertise to professionalize structures, digitize processes, and develop your team.\n*   Preparation of monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements.\n*   Primary contact for tax advisors, banks, and auditors.\n*   Consolidation, intercompany reconciliations, and tax compliance.\n*   Further development of accounting processes and digital tools.\n*   Close collaboration with the commercial director and related departments.\n*   Optional: Participation in controlling (cost centers, project controlling).\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed commercial training or a degree in finance.\n*   Successfully passed balance sheet accountant examination (Bilanzbuchhalter).\n*   Approximately 10 years of professional experience in accounting, with strong HGB closing certainty.\n*   Team leadership is your passion project? This is the perfect opportunity!\n*   Experience with consolidation, intercompany processes, and ERP systems (e.g., SAP).\n*   Analytical mindset, precise working methods, and a joy for improvement.\n*   Nice to have: Experience with IFRS, project controlling, group structures, or in the renewable energy sector.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an experienced and passionate finance leader to make a significant impact within a rapidly expanding company dedicated to renewable energy.
