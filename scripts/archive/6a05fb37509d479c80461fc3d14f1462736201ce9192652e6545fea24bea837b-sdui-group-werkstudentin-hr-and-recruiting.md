### Description\nSdui, founded in 2018, is one of Europe's fastest-growing EdTech companies, with over 230 international employees across five locations. Their mission is to actively shape the future of education by providing digital solutions for schools, daycares, and regions, enabling simple communication, efficient administration, and individualized, inclusive, intuitive, and data-protection-compliant learning. This role within the People & Culture team offers a purpose-driven job in a startup atmosphere, acting as the first personal contact for applicants and providing an authentic insight into the company's culture. You will learn modern recruiting from A to Z and contribute to Sdui's continued growth, helping to find the best talent to empower the transformation of education.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Matchmaker & Talent Scout:** Actively source top talent for Sdui using platforms like LinkedIn and XING, learning effective talent engagement strategies.\n- **Candidate Experience Manager:** Guide applicants through the entire recruitment process, coordinate interviews, and ensure a welcoming experience from day one.\n- **Real Impact:** Conduct independent initial screening calls, developing expertise in personnel selection.\n- **Projekte & Employer Branding:** Support various HR projects, assist with the onboarding of new \"Sduis,\" and contribute innovative ideas to enhance Sdui's employer brand.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Education:** Currently enrolled in a relevant degree program (e.g., Business Administration, Psychology, Social Sciences) with approximately 12 months remaining until graduation.\n- **Personality:** Naturally sociable, excellent communicator, comfortable with phone and video calls, and possesses strong empathy.\n- **Work Style:** Well-organized, reliable, proactive, and demonstrates a \"can-do\" mentality.\n- **Languages:** Fluent in German and very good English, reflecting the international team environment.\n- **Tech Interest:** Eager to learn and work with new digital tools and platforms, including a willingness to master tools like LinkedIn Recruiter.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a student or entry-level professional looking to kickstart their HR career in a dynamic, purpose-driven EdTech environment. Sdui's commitment to work-life balance, continuous learning, and a supportive, international culture with flat hierarchies makes it an attractive place to grow.
