### Description
Are you a pest control specialist looking for the next step in your career, or do you have sales experience in the food, pharma, hygiene, or horticulture sectors, eager to sell pest control services? This is your chance for a self-determined sales role in Rheinland-Pfalz/Saarland! We are seeking a dedicated **Sales Representative for Pest Control Services (m/f/d)** to join our team in a permanent full-time position. You can look forward to an attractive annual salary of **€60,000 - €80,000 (including commission)**, with approximately **€3,500 fixed monthly + commission**. This role offers a company car for private use, 30 days of vacation, 1-2 days of home office flexibility, and genuine opportunities for career advancement. If you have a passion for sales and true customer proximity, this is the perfect opportunity to make a significant impact.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain your existing customer base.
- Actively acquire new customers and open up additional markets in Rheinland-Pfalz and Saarland.
- Acquire new customers through targeted cold acquisition in defined industries (food and packaging industry, supermarkets, hospitals, pharmaceutical, and industrial clients).
- Collaborate closely with internal sales support and regularly coordinate on offers, customer projects, and sales strategies.
- Independently plan your customer appointments and take responsibility for your sales region.

### Qualifications
- You have a completed commercial apprenticeship OR proven experience in pest control.
- You are proficient in MS Office and ideally have experience with a CRM system.
- You communicate confidently in German, both written and spoken.
- You possess a valid Class B driving license.
