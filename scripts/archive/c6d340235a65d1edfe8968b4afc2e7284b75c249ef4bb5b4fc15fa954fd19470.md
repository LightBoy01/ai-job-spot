**About the Role** 

**Essendon Jaguar Land Rover**  are seeking a knowledgeable and customer-focused **Parts Interpreter** to join our Parts team. This role is responsible for identifying, sourcing, and supplying automotive parts and accessories to both internal departments and external customers, ensuring accuracy and a high level of service. 

*   Accurately interpret and source parts for workshop, retail, and trade customers 
    
*   Process orders, quotes, and invoices in line with dealership procedures 
    
*   Maintain up-to-date knowledge of parts catalogues and manufacturer systems 
    
*   Manage stock levels, incoming deliveries, and returns 
    
*   Provide prompt and professional service to both internal teams and external clients 
    

**Skills & Experience** 

*   Previous experience in a parts interpreter or similar automotive role 
    
*   Strong knowledge of automotive parts and systems 
    
*   Excellent attention to detail and accuracy 
    
*   Ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment 
    
*   Good communication and customer service skills 
    
*   Current driver’s licence
