### Description
FELS Family Office provides comprehensive tools and services for effective wealth management and growth. Our platform integrates investments, tax advisory, and optimization tools, empowering clients to increase returns and reduce costs. Through partnerships with FELS wealth GmbH (a BaFin-licensed securities institution) and Interactive Brokers Group, Inc., clients gain access to a broad spectrum of assets, from traditional securities to tokenized luxury real estate worldwide, enabling the creation of diversified portfolios. Clients also benefit from the expertise of leading investment and tax professionals who deliver educational content, individualized tax structuring advice, and investment ideas.

Our digital platform enables clients to structure their investments in a tax-optimized manner – efficiently, cost-effectively, and automatically – especially for wealth-managing GmbHs.

We are looking for a **Brand Specialist** to join our team as soon as possible. This full-time role, based in Kelkheim or remote, is pivotal in developing and implementing our brand system across websites, sales assets, and campaigns. With a digital-first mindset (Figma, web basics), you will translate complex finance and tax topics into clear communication, manage agencies, and ensure brand consistency and swift execution. Experience with regulated content is a significant plus. Fluency in German and English is required.

### Responsibilities
This role involves a 70% operational and 30% strategic split.

**Strategy & System (30%)**
*   Develop brand mission and narrative, positioning FELS within the dynamic intersection of wealth management and innovation (tokenization, digitalization).
*   Define the complete brand system, including design system (colors, typography, components), tonality/voice, governance rules, and a comprehensive Do/Don’t catalog.
*   Establish architecture and guidelines for our multi-brand/sub-brand landscape (main brand, product brands, events), including naming conventions and co-branding rules.
*   Collaborate with Legal/Tax advisory to define regulatory and tax guardrails (e.g., performance promises, suitability notices, risk disclaimers, differentiation between “education vs. offering”; incorporating MiCAR/eWpG fundamentals) and translate them into reusable language and UI building blocks.

**Operations, Production & Roll-out (70%)**
*   Conduct a comprehensive brand audit & consolidation: Review and harmonize all existing websites, landing pages, social media channels, sales assets, decks, one-pagers, and advertisements.
*   Set up design operations: Create and manage a template library, component kit, and asset repository (e.g., in Notion/Figma); oversee versioning and approval processes.
*   Standardize web presences: Provide clear briefings, art direction, and quality assurance, working closely with Engineering (e.g., Next.js/Vercel/Tailwind) and Content teams.
*   Manage agencies/freelancers: Handle briefings, sourcing, selection (RFP), budget allocation, and quality control, ensuring high-quality output and timely delivery.
*   Produce diverse content assets: Create pitch decks, product sheets (including for tokenized real assets), social media kits, event visuals, and ad packages.
*   Implement Brand QA & Governance: Establish ongoing operations, conduct regular reviews, provide training, and manage a “Brand Helpdesk” for the team and stakeholders.

### Qualifications
*   **Experience:** 5+ years of experience in Brand, Design-Ops, or Brand Management – ideally within tax & financial services, FinTech, Asset/Wealth Management, or a tech environment with regulated content.
*   **Finance Affinity:** Ability to translate complex asset allocation and financial products into clear, compliance-proof messages. A strong interest in tokenization, DLT, eWpG/MiCAR is a significant advantage.
*   **Motivation:** Highly motivated and eager to contribute to the company's vision. Capable of handling pressure and enthusiastic about embracing new challenges.
*   **Operational Strength:** Proven ability to deliver hands-on (Figma/Adobe, Slides, web basics) and manage multiple external streams simultaneously.
*   **Digital-first Brand Understanding:** Deep understanding of design systems, web/UI basics, performance-optimized creatives, and modular templates.
*   **Tool Proficiency (Examples):** Expertise in Figma, Adobe Creative Cloud, GDocs/Slides, Notion, Drive. Bonus points for knowledge of web basics (HTML/CSS), component libraries (e.g., Tailwind), CMS/Headless platforms, Webflow/Framer.
*   **Languages:** Fluency in both German & English is required.

### Author's Take

**Question 1: Given the split between 30% strategic and 70% operational, how will I truly influence the brand's direction and not just execute?**

*Insider's Answer*: That's a sharp question, and it gets right to the heart of this role's unique impact. While the operational component is indeed significant – because we need someone to *get things done* and maintain consistency across every touchpoint – your strategic input is absolutely vital. You won't just be executing; you'll be the architect. Think of the 30% strategy as the blueprint you'll design, covering everything from our core narrative in the evolving wealth management landscape to defining how we speak about innovative topics like tokenization. The 70% operational is then about building, implementing, and refining that blueprint. You'll be setting the standards, creating the reusable language and UI components, and ensuring all agencies and internal teams adhere to your vision. Without your strategic guidance, the operational efforts would lack cohesion and direction. You are the custodian of our brand's future identity, translating complex financial realities into a clear, compelling story for our clients. Your impact will be felt directly in how FELS is perceived and understood in a rapidly changing market.

**Question 2: How does FELS navigate the balance between rapid FinTech innovation (like tokenization) and the inherently conservative, regulated nature of financial services, especially from a brand and communication perspective?**

*Insider's Answer*: This is precisely the fascinating challenge and opportunity at FELS, and it’s why this role is so critical. We're operating in a space where "innovation" often collides with "regulation," and your expertise will be the bridge. Our clients trust us with their wealth, so clarity, transparency, and compliance are non-negotiable. Yet, we're also pioneering new frontiers with tokenized assets and digital platforms. Your role is to translate these cutting-edge concepts into language that is both exciting and reassuring. You'll work directly with our legal and tax experts to define "regulatory guardrails" – not as limitations, but as a framework for responsible innovation. This means crafting messages that educate without over-promising, highlighting benefits while clearly communicating risks, and differentiating between educational content and investment offerings. It's about building a brand that feels both forward-thinking and profoundly trustworthy. We believe that true innovation in finance isn't just about new products, but also about how we communicate them, ensuring our clients feel confident and informed every step of the way. You'll be at the forefront of shaping this delicate, yet crucial, balance.
