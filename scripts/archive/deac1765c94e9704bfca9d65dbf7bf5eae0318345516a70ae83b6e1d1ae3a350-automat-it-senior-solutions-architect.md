### Description
Automat-it, an AWS Premier and Strategic Partner, empowers high-growth startups across EMEA and the US to innovate, scale smartly, and maximize their cloud potential. We provide hands-on DevOps, FinOps, and GenAI support that delivers tangible results, fueling innovation and solving complex challenges daily.

As a **Solutions Architect**, you will be a pivotal member of the pre-sales process, directly engaging with global startup customers and collaborating with account executives. Your mission will be to design and develop cutting-edge AWS solutions tailored to client needs, helping them build the future of tech.

This is a remote position based in **Berlin or Munich**, offering an international work environment where you can grow your skills and shape bold ideas. Automat-it is committed to your professional development, covering professional training and certifications (AWS, FinOps, Kubernetes), providing soft skills training, and fostering a collaborative culture with company events and knowledge sharing. We are dedicated to equal opportunities and believe in the power of a diverse workforce.

### Responsibilities
*   Design, develop, and implement solutions that meet business requirements, creating cost-efficient, highly available, and scalable AWS solutions for customers, including Well-Architected reviews and Statements of Work (SoW).
*   Research and analyze current solutions, proactively initiating improvement plans and innovative strategies.
*   Collaborate effectively with fellow engineers and stakeholders to ensure all solutions are designed and developed in alignment with industry best practices.
*   Actively share your expertise with both customers and the broader cloud community through public speaking engagements such as workshops, conferences, webinars, and other events.
*   Undertake frequent travels, both locally (on-demand for customer/partner meetings and local events) and internationally (at least once per quarter).

### Qualifications
*   3+ years of hands-on experience in an AWS environment, encompassing implementation, migration, maintenance, and end-to-end solution design.
*   2+ years of experience in technical customer-facing roles, such as Customer Engineering, Site Reliability Engineer (SRE), Cloud Architect, or an equivalent position.
*   2+ years of experience in an IT environment with hands-on experience in Kubernetes, CI/CD, and DevOps tools (an advantage).
*   Proven experience in a production environment, developing software with either Python, Go, Java, or equivalent languages.
*   In-depth knowledge of AWS's main services, with a strong emphasis on Kubernetes and Containers.
*   Successful track record of designing robust DevOps and Infrastructure solutions for high-scale production environments in AWS.
*   Solid understanding of Information Security best practices.
*   Currently holding an **AWS Solutions Architect - Associate** certification.
*   Possession of an AWS Solutions Architect Professional or a specialty certification (an advantage).
*   Ability to effectively communicate complex technical designs in both verbal and written formats to customers.
*   Ability to lead an end-to-end engagement collaboratively with the account management team.
*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills in both English and German.
*   Ability to thrive and perform effectively in a fast-paced environment.
