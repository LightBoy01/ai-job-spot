**About the role**
------------------

**Location:** Austria - Vienna, Belgium - Beringen, Belgium - Kallo, Finland - Porvoo, Germany - Burghausen, Sweden - Stenungsund

**Hours:** Full-Time

**Type of Contract:** Permanent

**Application Deadline:** 29/10/2025

**Make HSEQ Real !**

Are you passionate about Health, Safety, Environment & Quality? Do you want to drive group-level energy and environmental management, shape and implement strategies to improve performance, compliance, and cost efficiency across different locations?  
  

In this role of Group Expert Energy & Environment you work closely with location leadership, asset and technical development managers, local energy and climate teams, and group experts to drive continuous improvement !

Your key responsibilities are to:  
  

*   Co-develop and implement group-wide energy, utilities, and environmental strategies ;
*   Set and monitor energy and environmental performance targets aligned with group ambitions ;
*   Ensure reliable, cost-effective energy and utilities supply while reducing environmental footprint ;
*   Lead compliance with ISO 50001 and ISO 14001 certifications and regulatory requirements ;
*   Coordinate energy/environmental assessments, benchmarks, and reporting ;
*   Collaborate with asset development teams on location energy plans and roadmaps ;
*   Facilitate knowledge sharing and provide tools to improve energy and environmental outcomes ;
*   Deliver expert guidance, training, and support to enhance sustainability and HSE compliance.

**About you**
-------------

*   Master’s degree in science, engineering, or equivalent (minimum 5 years university degree) ;
*   Total 10 years’ experience in the chemical process industry with a minimum 5 years in chemical process development, process engineering, or asset development roles ;
*   Strong background in HSEQ management with proven knowledge of production processes ;
*   Knowledge of energy and/or environmental management ;
*   Proactive, analytical, detail-oriented, with solid understanding of energy policy and market structure
*   Effective coach and communicator; able to give and receive feedback, manage conflicts, and inspire teamwork ;
*   Fluent in English (business level) and local language(s).
