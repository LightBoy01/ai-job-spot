With our experience of 300 actively managed corporate clients and 12,000 users across several industries, we know that the sustainable success of CRM and CPQ initiatives results from the optimal interplay of three crucial factors: People, Processes, and Solutions (mpl).\n\nOur clients consistently rely on our reliability, expertise, and precision. Optimal service always has top priority. Collaboration, team spirit, trust, and enthusiastic commitment characterize our daily work.\n\nIf these factors are just as important to you, then you are exactly right with us. We are looking for employees who innovatively and target-oriented shape and promote collaboration internally and externally.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Independently and comprehensively manage the operational execution of ongoing financial accounting (accounts receivable, accounts payable, general ledger) and oversee the external service provider for booking.\n- Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements in collaboration with the external tax advisor.\n- Completely manage payment transactions, dunning processes, and monitor liquidity planning.\n- Your personal drive includes the further development and maintenance of internal controlling.\n- You are passionate about \"ZDF\" (Numbers, Data, Facts). You enjoy analyzing data by regularly preparing reports and analyses for management.\n- You and the management work closely together on budget planning, forecasts, and business evaluations.\n- Optimizations, e.g., of processes/structures in accounting and controlling, are your thing.\n- Your team involvement: You work closely with our colleagues in internal sales and assist with commercial and administrative tasks.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Ideally, you have a completed commercial apprenticeship with further training as a balance sheet accountant or a completed business administration degree with a focus on finance/controlling.\n- You bring several years of professional experience in accounting or controlling, ideally in a medium-sized company.\n- You have a basic understanding of accounting software (e.g., DATEV, Lexware, SAP, or similar) and are proficient in MS Office, especially Excel.\n- You are characterized by analytical thinking, a structured way of working, and a high sense of responsibility.\n- You enjoy achieving and celebrating successes together with the team.\n- You contribute as a team player through your open and communicative demeanor.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role offers a fantastic opportunity for an experienced finance and controlling professional to take on a highly autonomous and impactful position within a company dedicated to digital transformation.
