### Description

At Wolt, we create technology that brings joy, simplicity, and earnings to neighborhoods worldwide. Since starting with restaurant food delivery in 2014, we've expanded to delivering (almost) everything across 500+ cities in 30 countries. In 2022, we joined forces with DoorDash, continuing our global expansion.

Working at Wolt is challenging but exciting. You'll learn, build, and ship more here than at most other companies. If you're a self-starter with drive and an entrepreneurial spirit, this could be the ride of your life.

Are you an experienced, strategic Account Manager with a genuine 'service-first' mentality and an analytical mindset, eager for an exciting role at one of Europe's fastest-growing companies? Let's talk!

Our Account Management team is at the heart of Wolt, ensuring the success of our existing restaurant partners and Wolt in Germany. As Wolt grows, so must our Account Management capabilities.

As an Account Manager, you will manage our most important Small and Medium Business (SMB) partners, increase product utilization, and drive their growth on our platform. This involves leading commercial negotiations, managing cross-departmental projects, closing complex deals in a competitive market, analyzing large datasets, and applying a consultative approach to your portfolio.

### Responsibilities

*   Utilize your business acumen and analytical skills to increase the adoption of Wolt products among your partner restaurants.
*   Lead complex negotiations, strengthen partnerships, and achieve financial targets.
*   Continuously monitor your portfolio's performance, analyze data, and provide data-driven recommendations to your partners.
*   Conduct regular meetings, present business reviews, and maintain a consultative approach in all conversations.
*   Collaborate closely with the Marketing team, plan campaigns, and implement them with your partners.
*   Contribute your experience to the team, share knowledge, and help others grow.
*   Observe the competition and provide feedback to further improve our products.

### Qualifications

**Required:**

*   3-5 years of experience in a customer-facing commercial role, e.g., Account Management, Sales, or Marketing.
*   Strong business acumen and a consultative approach to customer service: You understand industry dynamics, identify opportunities, and develop your accounts and KPIs.
*   A passion for numbers and data, ideally with experience using dashboards like Looker or Power BI, or even SQL knowledge.
*   A sales-oriented mindset, strong communication skills (verbal & written), and enjoy strategic conversations in a dynamic environment.
*   Excellent time management skills and a solution-oriented approach.
*   A knack for effective communication and building rapport.
*   Takes ownership and acts proactively.
*   Proficiency in Excel or Google Sheets.
*   Fluent in German and good English skills.

**Nice to have:**

*   Experience in gastronomy, Food & Beverage, hospitality, or similar industries.
*   Experience with CRM systems.
*   Experience with content creation or marketing.
*   Experience with Google Workspace.
