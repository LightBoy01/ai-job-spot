### Description

WPP is the creative transformation company, a global network of over 100,000 talented people using the power of creativity to build better futures. As we transform into an AI-enabled marketing powerhouse, we recognize that this shift is fundamentally about human behavior, not just technology. 

Our Change and Enablement team is at the forefront of this digital transformation, and we are seeking a Lead Behavioral Designer to be the architect of this change. You will apply deep behavioral science expertise to ethically influence the adoption of our new global platform, embed new mindsets, and drive sustained engagement, particularly around new AI-driven ways of working. As a strategic leader, you will apply cutting-edge behavioral science to unlock the full potential of our technology, shifting mindsets and behaviors across our global workforce to enhance both client and employee experiences.

### Responsibilities

*   **Lead Behavioral Strategy & Intervention Design:** Apply advanced behavioral science frameworks to identify barriers to platform adoption and new AI work methodologies. Design and implement targeted, ethical behavioral interventions, nudges, and choice architectures to significantly increase engagement and sustained usage.
*   **Drive Insight Generation & Collaboration:** Work closely with dedicated researchers to leverage insights from behavioral research (experiments, field studies, qualitative analysis) to deeply understand motivations and barriers to change. Translate these complex insights into actionable design recommendations.
*   **Foster Cross-Functional Integration & Mentorship:** Collaborate with Service Designers to embed behavioral insights into service flows and partner with Instructional Designers to optimize learning outcomes. Mentor and provide strategic guidance to Senior Behavioral Designers.
*   **Measure Impact & Ensure Ethical Practice:** Champion ethical behavioral design, ensuring transparency and integrity in all interventions. Present compelling insights to stakeholders, demonstrating how interventions drive measurable impact on platform adoption, and help define KPIs related to mindset shifts and cultural alignment.

### Qualifications

*   Progressive experience in Behavioral Science/Design, with a proven track record of leading initiatives in large-scale organizational change, internal product adoption, or enterprise-level behavioral transformation.
*   Extensive experience designing and analyzing behavioral interventions, experiments, field studies, and in-depth qualitative analysis.
*   Deep expertise in behavioral economics, cognitive psychology, social psychology, or a related field.
*   Strong analytical and statistical skills, capable of interpreting complex behavioral data to derive actionable insights.
*   Exceptional communication, persuasion, and collaboration skills, with a demonstrated ability to influence behavior through strategic design.
*   Proven ability to translate complex behavioral theories into actionable design solutions that drive measurable mindset shifts, especially for AI ways of working.
*   You are open, optimistic, and extraordinary: inclusive, collaborative, and believe in the power of creativity and technology to pioneer our industry.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "Beyond platform adoption metrics, how will the success of this role be measured in terms of its real-world impact on WPP's creative output and client relationships?"**

That’s a fantastic question because it gets right to the heart of why this role is so critical. We don't see this as just an IT project; it's a creative one. Success isn't just a dashboard showing higher user logins. It's about the stories we'll tell. Imagine one of our global creative teams, previously siloed, now collaborating seamlessly on a major campaign using our new AI-enabled platform. Success is that team's creative director telling us, 'The new workflow, which you helped design, cut our concepting time in half and led to the most innovative pitch we’ve delivered in years.' Your impact will be measured in the quality of our creative product, the speed of our execution, and the 'wow' factor we deliver to our clients. You're not just changing how people click; you're changing how they create.

**A candidate might also ask: "With a network of over 100,000 people, resistance to change is inevitable. What kind of support and autonomy will this role have to navigate that complexity and truly influence behavior at such a massive scale?"**

You've hit on the biggest challenge—and the biggest opportunity. We're fully aware that you can't just mandate a new way of thinking for 100,000 creative minds. That's precisely why this role sits within the Strategic Design function of our Change and Enablement team, not in a traditional IT hierarchy. You will have a direct line to senior leadership who are champions for this human-centric approach. You won't be fighting for a seat at the table; you'll be at the head of it when it comes to behavioral strategy. You’ll have dedicated research partners, close collaborators in service and instructional design, and the autonomy to design and run experiments that challenge our assumptions. We're not looking for someone to follow a playbook; we're looking for the architect who will write it with us.
