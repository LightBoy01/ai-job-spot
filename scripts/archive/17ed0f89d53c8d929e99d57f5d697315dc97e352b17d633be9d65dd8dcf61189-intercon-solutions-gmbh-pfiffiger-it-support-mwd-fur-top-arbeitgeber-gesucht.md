### Description
Are you a friendly, helpful IT Supporter who enjoys optimizing processes and solving technical challenges? Then you've come to the right place!

We offer an environment where your work is valued and you can feel completely at ease. You can expect not only exciting tasks and innovative software solutions, but also great benefits, a motivated team, and a respectful, appreciative collaboration. Become part of a company culture where cohesion, continuous development, and joy in work are highly valued!

*   Comprehensive social benefits
*   Company pension scheme
*   Health promotion initiatives

### Responsibilities
As an IT Supporter, your primary responsibilities will include:
*   Coordinating international IT operations and processes.
*   Providing first-level technical support to users.
*   Actively contributing to the expansion and continuous improvement of IT support services.

### Qualifications
To be successful in this role, you should possess:
*   A completed vocational training or degree in IT / Computer Science.
*   A friendly and communicative personality.
*   Proficient German language skills (C1/C2 level).
*   A strong technical focus on Windows operating systems and Active Directory.
