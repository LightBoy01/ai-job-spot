Join our brand-new team right from the start and be part of something special!

Are you a Registered Veterinary Nurse ready for an exciting new chapter? We're opening the doors to our state-of-the-art, purpose-built surgery in Stockbridge, and we want you to join us on our journey!

The brand-new surgery is situated inside one of our stores in the heart of Stockbridge and has been designed to deliver not only the highest clinical standards, but also a pleasant working environment for the whole team. We pride ourselves on fostering a collaborative and positive work environment where every team member is valued.

Our new practice can offer you the environment, caseload and equipment needed to utilise the skills you’ve gained to date whilst also giving you plenty of opportunity to develop further. We are proactive in continually supporting qualifications and CPD.

**About you and the role**  
We’re looking for a proactive, enthusiastic team player who is keen to work in a progressive, fast paced first opinion practice, with high clinical standards. You’ll be a confident and motivated RVN with some experience behind you, if you are interested in and/or are a clinical coach this would be an advantage. 

The key to your success will be your ability to embrace all aspects of the veterinary nurse role, delivering excellent patient and client care. You'll have the opportunity to help us shape the practice from day one.

**As a brand-new practice, we are happy to offer full or part time hours. We understand the importance of a healthy work/life balance and so we are open to discussions and welcome you to come and talk to us about what shifts would work best for you. There will be some weekend work included but no OOH required.**
