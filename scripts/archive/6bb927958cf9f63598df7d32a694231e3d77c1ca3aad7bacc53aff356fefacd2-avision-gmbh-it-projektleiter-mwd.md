### Description
Avision GmbH is an IT service provider specializing in "Software Revival" – application development and support. We offer an efficient alternative to costly and risky complete re-implementations by modernizing existing legacy software for companies. Our team consists of 150 employees, ranging from seasoned experts with over 25 years of experience to young professionals bringing fresh ideas.

We value personality above background, origin, gender identity, or individual circumstances. We foster a culture of collaboration and respect where every team member has a voice, can develop personally, and feels valued. We also welcome applications from individuals with equal status or severe disabilities.

This position can be performed 100% remotely across Germany. We offer 30+2 days of holiday, a flexible working time account for a great work-life balance, and overtime that can be paid out or taken as time off. Other benefits include Workation & Sabbatical opportunities, individual further training, company pension scheme, Wellpass (50% employer subsidy), JobRad (monthly employer subsidy), sports activities, annual summer and Christmas parties (with partners and children), an annual company trip, a familial working atmosphere, and corporate benefits.

### Responsibilities
*   Oversee project control for the implementation of customer projects, ensuring adherence to deadlines, costs, and contractual compliance.
*   Develop software solutions collaboratively with your project team and in close partnership with our clients.
*   Take responsibility for analyzing functional requirements and technical processes, as well as creating functional and technical concepts.
*   Elaborate on requirements analyses, as well as system and database architectures.
*   Calculate and prepare offers, alongside detailed project planning.
*   Provide technical leadership to project team members and collaborate closely with our software developers.
*   Support the team in the realization and implementation of applications.
*   Continuously cultivate and develop customer relationships as a reliable and competent point of contact.

### Qualifications
*   Successfully completed studies in (Applied/Business) Informatics, Mathematics, Business Administration, or a vocational training as an IT Specialist for Application Development (m/f/d).
*   Several years of professional experience in implementing software projects, as well as in the conception and development of (custom) software solutions.
*   Ideally, experience in project management and in leading project teams.
*   Strong technical understanding and experience in software architecture/programming.
*   Experience with agile project methodologies, e.g., Scrum, is desirable.
*   Enjoy taking responsibility and possess strong decision-making skills.
*   Very good German language skills.
*   An open and flexible personality who enjoys working in a motivated and innovative team.
