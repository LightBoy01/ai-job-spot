The company prides itself on an open, warm, and diverse team culture that fosters collaboration through regular social events, team trips, and a vibrant feedback culture. You'll find a modern, light-filled office in Oberursel, complete with great amenities and connectivity.\n\nThe role provides significant autonomy and avenues for personal and professional development, allowing you to truly make an impact with your ideas.
