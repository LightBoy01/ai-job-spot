**About Resolve AI**
--------------------

*   At Resolve, we’re building Agentic AI that empowers software engineers by automating production engineering and SRE workflows. Our models deeply understand production systems — from code to databases — taking on repetitive, high-pressure tasks and handling critical incidents autonomously, so engineers can focus on building.
    
*   Our founders ([Spiros Xanthos](https://www.linkedin.com/in/spiros/) and [Mayank Agarwal](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mayank-ag/)) are the core creators of OpenTelemetry and led Splunk Observability. They have had 2 successful exits to Splunk and VMware.
    
*   We raised a $35M Seed round from top-tier investors like Greylock, Unusual Ventures, Jeff Dean (Chief Scientist, Google DeepMind), Thomas Dohmke (CEO, GitHub), Matt Garman (CEO, AWS), Reid Hoffman (Founder, LinkedIn) and Fei Fei Li (Professor, Stanford).
    

**What You’ll Do**
------------------

As our first technical recruiter, you’ll help shape the future of Resolve AI by identifying and hiring the exceptional engineers who will build the next generation of AI-driven production systems. You’ll partner directly with the Head of People & Talent and early leadership team to build world-class engineering teams across AI, backend, infrastructure, and more. Your impact will extend far beyond hiring: you’ll define recruiting processes from scratch, bring our talent strategy to life, and directly influence company culture during this foundational stage.

Your responsibilities will include:

*   **Own end-to-end recruiting** for technical roles across engineering, AI Research, Infrastructure, Backend, Full stack, Frontend, Security, and more.
    
*   **Partner closely with founders and hiring managers** to understand business needs, team roadmaps, and evolving role requirements.
    
*   **Build and execute strategic sourcing campaigns** to uncover top-tier, often passive, candidates, using creative outreach, referrals, talent mapping, and events.
    
*   **Deliver an exceptional candidate experience** that reflects Resolve AI’s culture of excellence and high urgency.
    
*   **Define and implement foundational processes**, including interview loops, scorecards, scheduling workflows, and best practices.
    
*   **Share talent market insights** and real-time funnel data to inform hiring decisions and improve conversion metrics.
    
*   **Contribute to our employer brand**, working with design and marketing to craft messaging that attracts world-class builders.
    
*   **Experiment with new ideas**, be it in sourcing channels, or automation tools, to keep us ahead of the curve.
    

**What We’re Looking For**
--------------------------

*   **7+ years of full-cycle technical recruiting experience**, ideally in high-growth startup environments.
    
*   **Proven success hiring across multiple technical domains**, bonus if you’ve supported areas like infra, AI, or backend engineering.
    
*   **Experience recruiting AI/ML or research talent**
    
*   **Strong business acumen and technical intuition**, you don’t need to write code, but you should be able to quickly understand what makes someone excellent at building scalable systems.
    
*   **Highly autonomous and self-starting**, with a founder mindset and bias toward action.
    
*   **Exceptional communication and organizational skills**, with a polished but human touch across written and verbal interactions.
    
*   **A scrappy, resourceful approach**, you know how to deliver results with speed and limited resources.
    
*   **A genuine belief in our mission** to transform production operations using AI, and excitement to work alongside deeply technical builders.
