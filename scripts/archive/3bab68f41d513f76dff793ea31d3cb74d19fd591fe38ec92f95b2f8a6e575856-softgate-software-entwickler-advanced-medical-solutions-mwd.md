### Description
This company is a stable and established player in medical technology, dedicated to developing software that saves lives and shapes the future of the industry. With an impressive average employee tenure of over ten years, they cultivate an international team focused on delivering high-quality software for medical device manufacturers worldwide. The culture is defined by trust, appreciation, genuine development opportunities, flat hierarchies, an agile mindset, and a familial atmosphere. They seek individuals looking for a long-term perspective in a down-to-earth, collaborative environment.

### Responsibilities
*   Design and develop applications and services for modern platforms, including desktop apps, web services, and scalable cloud environments.
*   Actively contribute to the further development of architectural standards (e.g., API design, CI/CD pipelines, security best practices).
*   As an experienced member of the team with solid knowledge of software and applications, drive projects forward.
*   As an experienced part of the development team, take responsibility for tasks and backlog items, work in a structured and quality-oriented manner, and keep an open eye for the progress and development of team colleagues.

### Qualifications
*   Completed degree in Computer Science, Medical Technology, or a comparable qualification.
*   Several years of experience in software development, ideally within the medical technology sector.
*   Practical experience with REST APIs, Microservices, Containerization (Docker/Kubernetes), modern authentication methods like OAuth2/OpenID Connect, and other cloud-native approaches to support the team in developing scalable and future-proof solutions.
*   Proficient in at least one of the programming languages C++ and C#.NET, with familiarity in the other.
*   Routinely work with agile methodologies (Scrum, Kanban) and tools (DevOps).
*   Ability to work independently and support the team.
*   Very good German language skills (at least C1 level) and very good English language skills.

### Author's Take
This is an exceptional opportunity for an experienced software engineer who wants to make a tangible impact by contributing to life-saving medical technology. The company stands out for its remarkable stability, long-term employee commitment, and a genuinely supportive, collaborative culture that fosters continuous growth. With a focus on quality, agile practices, and modern tech stacks, engineers can tackle meaningful challenges while benefiting from excellent work-life balance, including up to 60% remote work, flexible hours, and 30 days of annual leave. The emphasis on trust, open communication, and real development pathways makes this a highly attractive role for those seeking a stable career with purpose and a strong sense of community.
