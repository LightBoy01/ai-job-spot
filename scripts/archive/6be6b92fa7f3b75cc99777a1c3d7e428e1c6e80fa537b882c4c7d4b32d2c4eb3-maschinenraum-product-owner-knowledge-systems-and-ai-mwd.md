### Description
The Maschinenraum is the shared transformation ecosystem for German medium-sized and family businesses, aiming to better understand and more boldly shape future topics together. Collaborating with our 80 member companies such as edding, Stihl, Sennheiser, Vorwerk, and Viessmann, the Maschinenraum systematically drives the transformation of organizations by sharing experiences, knowledge, skills, and resources. Originating from the transformation experience of the Viessmann Group, the Maschinenraum is a neutral platform by the Mittelstand for the Mittelstand.

The Maschinenraum is growing, and we want to rethink how we live knowledge and collaboration within our ecosystem. For this transformation project, we are looking for a Product Owner who will shape this endeavor with strategic foresight and entrepreneurial drive, and bring it to implementation.

As Product Owner, you assume overall responsibility for the strategic and technical development of our new knowledge platform, a product of strategic relevance for the future viability of the Maschinenraum and its member companies.

### Responsibilities
You will shape a central future project that seamlessly integrates into the Maschinenraum system and enables new, technologically supported offerings that create real value for family businesses. Specifically, this means:

*   You work at the intersection of Business Development, Data, Technology, and Customer Experience – with the clear goal of transforming knowledge into impact and collaborating closely with the entire Maschinenraum team.
*   You translate the shared vision into a concrete product concept with a clear roadmap.
*   You orchestrate internal and external stakeholders, from Maschinenraum teams to technology partners, ensuring that strategy, technology, and user needs intertwine.
*   You develop and prioritize use cases that create concrete added value for teams and member companies, and you transfer them into concrete applications together with the team.
*   You are additionally responsible for operational system interfaces and their automations within the tech stack.

### Qualifications
*   Relevant experience in product or project management with data, knowledge, or AI products.
*   Experience in developing or scaling digital platforms or ecosystems is a plus.
*   Experience in cross-system work (e.g., CRM, Community, Data Management, API integrations, and system automations).
*   Strong understanding of business and user logic, as well as stakeholder management.
*   Ideally, experience in building or utilizing business intelligence approaches to derive data-driven decisions.
*   Ability to simplify complex structures and translate them into clear priorities.
*   Structured, analytical, and entrepreneurial way of working with high initiative.
*   Enthusiasm for topics such as knowledge management, data strategy, artificial intelligence, and organizational development.
*   Desire to take responsibility in a dynamic environment and build a future product together with the teams.
