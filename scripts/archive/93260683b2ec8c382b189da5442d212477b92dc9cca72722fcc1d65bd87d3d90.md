**Job Summary:**  

*   Assembler who assists with cabinet assembly of chiller modules including, mounting electrical enclosures, drilling holes for electrical enclosures, installing water tight connections, External VFD mounting, and installation of other major components. Assists with Rough-in process of electrical with creating harnesses  
    

**Responsibilities:**  

*   Learn to read 3-dimesional and 2-dimensional blueprints and schematics to determine dimensions, design and materials needed for performing duties.  
    

*   Run electrical cables and wire (rough-in) 
    

*   Create conduit assemblies and install connectors 
    

*   Assist electrical team with assembly process 
    

*   Install external electrical enclosures 
    

*   Layout the electrical enclosures and drill/punch correct sized holes for fittings 
    

*   Install watertight electrical connections 
    

*   Mount and install VFD and external VFD enclosures 
    

*   Assist with distribution box and Whip installation for beam systems 
    

*   Other departments assignments as needed 
    

**Qualifications:**  

*   Ability to work in a team setting and demonstrate a positive work attitude  
    

*   Actively practices and promotes safety  
    

*   Previous experience with hand tools is a plus  
    

*   Experience in Manufacturing assembly or equivalent preferred  
    

*   Committed to continuous improvement in products, services, and processes.
