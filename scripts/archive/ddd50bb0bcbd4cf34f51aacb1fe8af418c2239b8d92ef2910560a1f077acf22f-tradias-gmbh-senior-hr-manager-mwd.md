### Description\nAt tradias, we are shaping the future of finance as a fully licensed securities trading bank and a leading liquidity provider, regulated by BaFIN and based in Frankfurt. We specialize in connecting traditional financial markets with the world of digital assets, providing institutional investors with secure access to cryptocurrencies and tokenized assets. If you're looking for a dynamic, agile fintech environment where you can truly make an impact and grow, tradias is the place for you. We offer a high degree of autonomy, flexible working hours with home office options, 30 days of annual leave, and an international, modern work environment with flat hierarchies, encouraging continuous development alongside a highly motivated team. This role is crucial in supporting our management and employees in all HR aspects, contributing significantly to our growth in the European crypto and digital assets universe.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Provide administrative and professional support to management on all HR and labor law matters.\n*   Take responsibility for the entire recruitment process in close cooperation with department heads.\n*   Manage and support all operational HR-related topics throughout the Employee Life Cycle.\n*   Organize and maintain personnel records efficiently.\n*   Create essential HR documents, including employment contracts, certificates, and references.\n*   Collaborate closely with Compliance, Legal, and Risk departments to support HR-related regulatory initiatives and audits.\n*   Generate regular reports and presentations on key HR metrics.\n*   Serve as the primary contact person for employees regarding HR-related inquiries.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   A completed degree with a focus on Human Resources or a comparable qualification.\n*   Several years of professional experience in strategic and operational Human Resources Management, ideally within a regulated environment such as financial services, banking, or FinTech.\n*   In-depth expertise in various HR areas, including labor law and talent management.\n*   Demonstrated ability to work independently and in a structured manner.\n*   Excellent communication skills coupled with a high degree of empathy, integrity, and discretion.\n*   A high level of commitment and proactive initiative.\n*   Excellent proficiency in written and spoken German and very good English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an experienced HR professional who thrives in a fast-paced, innovative, and highly regulated environment. tradias offers a unique blend of traditional finance stability with the exciting dynamism of the digital asset space. You'll have significant autonomy and the chance to shape HR processes and culture from the ground up, working alongside industry experts. The emphasis on growth, flexible working, and a flat hierarchy means you can genuinely impact the business and your career trajectory. If you're passionate about talent acquisition, employee development, and navigating complex regulatory landscapes in a pioneering fintech, this role is a perfect match.
