### Description

**Without you? Data chaos. With you? Clarity, speed, impact.**

READO is on its way to becoming the Spotify for books: A social reading platform with rapidly growing reach and bold product ideas. The company is remote-first and led by a young, passionate founding team that has already successfully proven how to scale digital products.

For features like recommendations, feeds, and analytics to truly fly, a strong data foundation is now essential. As a sparring partner to the CTO, you will work at eye-level with the founding team and actively shape the technical future. Now is the perfect time to join so we can fly together 🚀🚀

READO offers an attractive compensation package with room for growth based on your experience, a trusting remote setup (work from anywhere in Germany), and a small, strong team environment that values direct communication and autonomy over micromanagement.

### Responsibilities

*   **Build robust data pipelines** that deliver on their promises – regularly, stably, and traceably.
*   **Set up target data stores for analytics**, e.g., BigQuery – you create the foundation for fast decisions.
*   **Automate quality assurance** – including completeness, freshness, and simple checks.
*   **Structure events & user data** to make them usable for search, feeds, & recommendations.
*   **Reduce data chaos** – from logs to Firestore and Postgres: you ensure a consistent solution.

### Qualifications

*   **SQL & PostgreSQL? Your playground.** Combined with Python, you are at home in the data flow.
*   **You think cloud-native anyway** – Google Cloud is ideal, but AWS or Azure are also a good foundation.
*   **Airflow, dbt & Co.** You bring experience with recurring data jobs and data modeling.
*   **You think in events & pipelines, not in tables** – streaming, APIs & caching are not new territory for you.
*   **Linux know-how** is a plus, as you know that containers don't run by themselves.

### Author's Take

This is an exceptional opportunity for a Senior Data Engineer who thrives in a high-impact, autonomous environment. You'll be joining Reado at a pivotal moment, shaping the entire data infrastructure for a platform aiming to be the 'Spotify for books.' The remote-first culture, coupled with direct access to a passionate founding team, means your work will have immediate and visible influence. Expect to build from the ground up, solve complex data challenges, and contribute strategically to product innovation, all within a small, agile team that values trust, ownership, and direct communication over corporate bureaucracy. If you're passionate about data, growth, and making a tangible impact, Reado offers a unique stage for your talents.
