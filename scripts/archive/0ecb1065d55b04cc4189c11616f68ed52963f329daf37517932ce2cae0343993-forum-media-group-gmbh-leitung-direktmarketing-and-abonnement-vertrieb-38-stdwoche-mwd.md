### Description\nFORUM VERLAG, an international media group with 35 years of history, specializes in expert knowledge across diverse fields like occupational safety, construction, renewable energies, education, medicine, and economics. They continuously expand and optimize their portfolio in print, digital, online, and further education. The company thrives on a passionate team that drives future knowledge management, fostering collaboration, open communication, and continuous growth.\n\nIn this role, you will leverage your expertise in customer relations and sales to take responsibility for direct and subscription sales (print & digital). You will independently manage direct marketing initiatives, optimize sales funnels, and drive subscription growth alongside your team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Responsible for revenue growth and achieving sales targets.\n- Develop and implement effective direct marketing campaigns.\n- Expand and continuously optimize the subscription sales funnel.\n- Analyze KPIs, derive, and implement concrete growth strategies.\n- Plan and control budget and forecast.\n- Monitor market and competition.\n- Lead and develop sales teams.\n- Collaborate closely with marketing, product management, and customer service.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Profound experience in (digital) sales within the media and subscription sector.\n- Strong analytical skills combined with innovation and creativity.\n- Knowledge in the introduction or application of CRM solutions.\n- Excellent organizational and communication skills for interacting with internal and external stakeholders.\n- Cooperative and appreciative leadership style.\n- Native-level German language skills (for the DACH target group).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting Lead role for a sales professional with a strategic mindset and a passion for team leadership within a dynamic media group. The company culture emphasizes collaboration, continuous learning through its own academy, and significant autonomy with \"maximum design freedom\" in a flat hierarchy. With a strong focus on work-life balance (flexible hours, hybrid work, 30 days holiday) and attractive benefits like profit sharing, this opportunity is ideal for someone looking to drive direct and subscription sales growth while developing their team in a supportive and forward-thinking environment.
