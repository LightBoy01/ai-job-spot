**541046 - Registered Nurse - Transition to Emergency Nursing Program**

**Calvary Mater Newcastle**

**Permanent Full-Time**

Are you a Registered Nurse ready to grow your skills and make a real difference in Emergency Medicine?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You’re invited to apply for Calvary Mater Newcastle’s **Emergency Nursing Program**, a year-long, fully supported pathway.

**Why you’d love working with us**

We care for our people by providing safe, equitable, inclusive and welcoming work environment that respects each individual and their contribution to our mission of ‘Being for Others’.

As an Affiliated Health Organisation we provide services to both public and private patients primarily from the Hunter New England area.  Our services include Emergency & Intensive Care, Haematology & Oncology, Clinical Toxicology and Coronary Care.

Our benefits include:

*   Family orientated to accommodate work-life balance
*   Up to $2000 relocation incentive by negotiation
*   Up to $15,900 in salary packaging options
*   Corporate discounts including Private Health Insurance and Tech products
*   Employee Assistance Program and Fitness Passport for you and your family
*   Gender neutral parental leave
*   NSW Health employees can request to have leave entitlement balances transferred to Calvary Mater Newcastle on commencement.

We are an inclusive organisation and actively promote equality of opportunity for all with the right mix of talent, skills and potential. We welcome applications from a wide range of candidates.

**About you**

*   A Registered Nurse authorised to practice by the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency (AHPRA).
*   Recent clinical experience in acute care or completion of a New Graduate Program.
*   Demonstrated knowledge of and willingness to work towards achieving Emergency Department Key Performance Indicators
*   Previous experience working effectively in a multidisciplinary team environment and in periods of high demand and activity

  
**About the role**

Position title: Registered Nurse (Transition to Emergency Nursing)

Position type: Permanent Full-Time

Salary classification: Registered Nurse Year 1-8

Remuneration: $36.39 - $51.09 per hour + superannuation + 17.5 % annual leave loading

Please refer to the position description for role responsibilities and full selection criteria.

This role requires you to be available to work all shifts on a rotating roster including morning, afternoon, night shift and weekends.

**Launch Your Emergency Medicine Nursing Career**   
Click ‘Apply for Job’ and follow the prompts.  _This role requires the successful application to:_

*   _Successfully complete pre-employment checks including reference checks, work rights, criminal history check and WWCC checks to ensure suitability for the role._
*   _Provide evidence of immunisation as required for the role._
