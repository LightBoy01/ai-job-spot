•    SCOPE/PURPOSE OF POSITION:   
Reporting to the Global Directory Services Lead, the Multi-Cloud Active Directory Administrator is a senior technology leader that will function as Subject Matter Expert for Active Directory. This position will function in coordination with IT Architecture, Engineering, Operations, Security, Strategic partners, Technology vendors and Project management teams to facilitate the successful delivery of complex Corning initiatives and priorities  
Responsibilities will include  the administration of the current Corning Active Directory environments, managing aspects of Entra ID, making recommendations for design and process improvement, and automation.   
As a core member of the Corning Multi-Cloud Active Directory team,  the AD Administrator will be responsible for helping direct and guide the delivery of key priorities across multiple stakeholders This position will require the ability to communicate technical problems and solutions to internal IT support teams as well as business units around the globe.
