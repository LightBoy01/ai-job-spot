**ABOUT THE JOB**

We are looking for an Requirements Expert to join the team of Rail & Transit in our offices in Madrid, Bilbao or Barcelona.

At IDOM, we offer you the opportunity to participate in national and international projects as part of a team where you will have the support to push your professional boundaries and improve your skills every day. In a multidisciplinary environment you will be exposed to all the latest technologies and best practices. Your main responsibilities are going to be:

*   Lead the production of Verification and Validation Matrices (VVMs) to support technical design assurance.
*   Ensure that the requirements management approach aligns with project standards and contractual obligations.
*   Report to and liaise with project teams and subcontractors regarding requirements management processes during design, construction, and delivery phases.
*   Develop, configure, and manage the requirements management database.
*   Drive innovation in the requirements management approach.
*   Facilitate tracking and regular reporting on the performance of the requirements management process.
*   Support other disciplines in their interactions with the DOORS database or similar tools.

**ABOUT YOU**

**Apply if you…**

*   You hold a degree in fields related to Civil, Industrial, or Systems Engineering, or a Bachelor's degree in Mathematics.
*   You have at least 2 years of prior experience working on large infrastructure projects, especially in civil or railway engineering, and in the use of DOORS, JAMA, or similar requirements management tools.
*   You have strong communication and collaboration skills.
*   You are capable of working effectively with project delivery teams and building relationships with stakeholders at all levels.
*   You are familiar with industry standards and best practices in requirements management.
*   You have fluent English (minimum level B2/C1, spoken and written); other languages will be valued.
*   You have analytical skills and are proficient in report writing.
*   You are familiar with tools for designing participatory and communication processes (desirable).
*   You are able to work effectively with project delivery teams and build relationships with stakeholders at all levels, and you demonstrate autonomy, flexibility, technical rigor, and an international mindset.
*   And above all... you’re eager to learn and grow in an international company like IDOM.
