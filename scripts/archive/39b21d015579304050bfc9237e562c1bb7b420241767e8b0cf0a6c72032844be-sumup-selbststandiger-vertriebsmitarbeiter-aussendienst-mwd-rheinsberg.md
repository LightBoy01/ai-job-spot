### Description

Are you courageous enough to work independently, shape your own success, and manage your time freely? Then you've come to the right place!

SumUp supports small businesses with smart and user-friendly financial solutions – from card payments to comprehensive POS systems. Our goal is to empower entrepreneurs so they can fully concentrate on their business.

As a **freelance Field Sales Consultant**, you are your own boss and have the opportunity to earn **attractive, unlimited commissions!** You will build relationships with local businesses and show them how SumUp can simplify their operations. Your main task is the direct sale of our solutions – whether it's a mobile card terminal or comprehensive payment systems, you'll have everything you need to succeed.

**What you can expect:**

*   **100% performance-based compensation** – your income depends on your effort, with no upper limit.
*   **Maximum flexibility** – work when and where you want.
*   **Top-products with high market potential** – compelling solutions for small businesses.
*   **Training & sales support** – we won't leave you alone.

### Responsibilities

*   **Active new customer acquisition** – identify and acquire local businesses for SumUp.
*   **Personal meetings and consultation** – present our products and demonstrate their benefits.
*   **Build customer relationships** – secure long-term partnerships through excellent service.
*   **Own customer and lead management** – you decide how to build your business.

### Qualifications

*   Passion for sales and self-motivation.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer service is an advantage.
*   Strong communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Excellent German language skills.

### Author's Take

This opportunity with SumUp invites you to kickstart or advance your career in sales with an entrepreneurial spirit. It's designed for highly motivated individuals who thrive on autonomy, offering a unique path to build your own successful sales business under the banner of a supportive brand. The unlimited commission structure means your earnings are directly tied to your drive and results, providing substantial financial upside.

While operating as your "own boss" is central, SumUp emphasizes that you'll be well-supported with comprehensive training and sales resources. The company's "Job Application Tip" encourages candidates not to feel deterred if they don't meet every single qualification. If you possess a passion for sales, strong self-motivation, and excellent communication skills, and are eager to make a tangible impact on small businesses, this role offers a flexible and rewarding environment to grow your career.
