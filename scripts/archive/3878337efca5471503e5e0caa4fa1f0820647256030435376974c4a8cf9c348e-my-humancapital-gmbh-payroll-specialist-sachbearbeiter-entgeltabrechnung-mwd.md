### Description\nOur partner company in Munich is looking for a **Payroll Specialist / Sachbearbeiter Entgeltabrechnung (m/w/d)**. In this role, you will be responsible for the accurate and timely processing of monthly payroll using SAP HR, serving as a key contact person for employees regarding all payroll-related inquiries, and actively contributing to HR digitalization and process optimization projects.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Perform monthly payroll processing in SAP HR, ensuring accurate handling of all payroll-relevant processes.\n- Serve as a competent contact person for employees regarding inquiries about salaries, social security, and income tax.\n- Maintain and update HR master data in the SAP system, ensuring all payroll-relevant information is always current and complete.\n- Manage topics related to company pension schemes, time management, and special benefits like bike leasing or other additional services.\n- Support year-end closing activities, determine provisions, and actively contribute your expertise to digitalization and process optimization projects within the HR department.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed training as an HR Specialist (\"Personalfachkaufmann (m/w/d)\"), Tax Clerk (\"Steuerfachangestellter (m/w/d)\"), Industrial Clerk (\"Industriekaufmann (m/w/d)\"), Office Management Clerk (\"Kaufmann für Büromanagement (m/w/d)\"), or a comparable commercial qualification with a focus on HR or accounting.\n- Profound knowledge in payroll accounting and experience with SAP HR, SAP HCM, or similar payroll systems.\n- Ideally, knowledge of social security law, income tax law, and company pension schemes.\n- Structured, reliable, and discreet way of working, with a strong affinity for numbers and a pronounced sense of responsibility.\n- Excellent German language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an experienced Payroll Specialist seeking a long-term, stable role within a supportive and forward-thinking company. You'll benefit from a culture that values trust, open communication, and collaboration, reflected in their \"Du-Kultur\" and strong leadership support. The role offers excellent work-life balance through flexible hours and home office options, alongside a modern work environment. With ample opportunities for further education and development, this position is ideal for someone looking to grow their expertise and contribute actively to HR process optimization.
