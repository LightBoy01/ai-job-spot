### Description
Our partner company in Munich is seeking a dedicated and experienced Payroll Specialist / Sachbearbeiter Entgeltabrechnung (m/w/d) to join their team as soon as possible. This is a fantastic opportunity for a professional looking for a long-term, permanent position in a modern and appreciative work environment. You will play a crucial role in ensuring accurate and timely payroll processing while contributing to the ongoing digitalization and optimization of HR processes. The company values work-life balance, offering flexible working hours and the option for home office, fostering a trusting "Du-Kultur" (informal address) within the department. You'll be supported with continuous training and development opportunities to grow your expertise.

### Responsibilities
As a Payroll Specialist, your core responsibilities will include:
*   Executing the monthly payroll in SAP HR, ensuring the correct and timely processing of all payroll-relevant transactions.
*   Serving as a competent and trusted point of contact for employees regarding questions on payroll, social security, and wage tax.
*   Maintaining and updating personnel master data within the SAP system, ensuring all relevant information is current and complete.
*   Overseeing topics related to occupational pension schemes, time management, and special benefits such as bike leasing.
*   Providing support for year-end closing activities, including the calculation of provisions.
*   Actively contributing your expertise to digitalization and process optimization projects within the HR department.

### Qualifications
To be successful in this role, you should possess:
*   A completed vocational training as a Personnel Specialist (Personalfachkaufmann), Tax Assistant (Steuerfachangestellter), Industrial Clerk (Industriekaufmann), Office Management Assistant (Kaufmann für Büromanagement), or a comparable commercial qualification with a focus on human resources or accounting.
*   Profound knowledge in payroll accounting and practical experience with SAP HR, SAP HCM, or similar payroll systems.
*   Ideally, knowledge in social security law, wage tax law, and occupational pension schemes.
*   A structured, reliable, and discreet working style with a strong affinity for numbers and a high sense of responsibility.
*   Very good German language skills.
