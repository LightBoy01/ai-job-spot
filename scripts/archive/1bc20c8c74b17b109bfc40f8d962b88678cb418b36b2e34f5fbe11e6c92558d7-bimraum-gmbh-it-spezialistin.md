### Description\nWe are currently finalizing the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) for an innovative AI solution designed to revolutionize and significantly increase the efficiency of construction planning. Our initial pilot customers are ready and eager to begin live testing starting in February. To ensure we can rapidly implement customer feedback and continuously train our AI models, we are immediately seeking our first technical Co-Founder, who will also serve as our Chief Technology Officer (CTO).\n\nThis is a foundational role for a visionary technical leader ready to take on full ownership of our product's technical direction and make a profound impact in a high-growth market from day one.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Continuously develop features and enhance the user experience (UX) based on genuine customer feedback.\n- Further train and improve our AI modules, leveraging both knowledge-based systems and Large Language Model (LLM) approaches.\n- Assume full code ownership of the MVP and take over overall technical leadership from the current founder.\n- Ensure the stability, performance, and security of our platform.\n- In the medium term, build and lead a small tech team, potentially comprising students or freelancers.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Proven hands-on development experience (Fullstack and/or AI expertise).\n- Ability to deliver high-velocity development while maintaining a solid technical foundation.\n- An entrepreneurial mindset: a focus on what needs to function tomorrow to truly delight customers.\n- Residence in the Stuttgart area or within a maximum 100 km radius is preferred for quick coordination and co-working sessions.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a driven technical leader to join an AI startup as a Co-Founder (CTO) at a critical inflection point. With an MVP ready and pilot customers eagerly waiting, the role offers immediate, tangible impact in the burgeoning construction planning sector. The emphasis on rapid iteration, direct customer feedback, and significant equity ownership (5-7.5% with vesting) signals a true partnership for someone with an entrepreneurial spirit. You'll be tackling a real-world problem in a massive market, building out a cutting-edge AI product, and eventually shaping a tech team. If you thrive on ownership, innovation, and seeing your code make a difference from day one, this role offers unparalleled potential.
