### Description\nThis established mechanical engineering company in the Karlsbad area is seeking an experienced IT System Administrator (m/f/d) – Expert for Infrastructure & Security, for direct placement. This pivotal role involves the comprehensive management, development, and security of the entire IT landscape, ensuring robust and high-performance operations for all IT services.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Manage and further develop the entire IT landscape, including servers, network, storage, and virtualization.\n*   Ensure secure, stable, and high-performance operation of all IT services.\n*   Primarily administer Windows servers and relevant network components.\n*   Significantly contribute to the further development of IT security and compliance structures.\n*   Monitor systems, analyze disturbances, and ensure quick, sustainable resolution.\n*   Accompany infrastructure projects, release and migration initiatives, and rollouts.\n*   Document processes and systems and continuously optimize them.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Successfully completed IT training or a comparable qualification.\n*   Several years of professional experience in administering demanding IT infrastructures.\n*   Very good knowledge of virtualization technologies (e.g., VMware or Hyper-V) and network technology.\n*   Experience with modern cloud environments (Azure, AWS or Google Cloud).\n*   Good knowledge in IT security, including network and endpoint security, and vulnerability analysis.\n*   Routine in handling backup and monitoring tools.\n*   Independent, structured, and solution-oriented way of working, as well as enjoyment of teamwork.\n*   Fluent German and good English skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a seasoned IT System Administrator with a strong background in infrastructure and security to join a stable mechanical engineering company. The role offers significant influence over the IT landscape, from daily operations to strategic project involvement, including cloud adoption and security enhancements. If you thrive in a collaborative environment where your expertise directly contributes to operational excellence and you enjoy both hands-on work and strategic development, this position in Karlsbad is for you.
