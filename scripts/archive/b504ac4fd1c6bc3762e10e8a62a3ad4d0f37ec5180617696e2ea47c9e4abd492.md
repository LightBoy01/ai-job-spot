The Sr. Manager of Technical Enablement will lead Pre-Sales & Partner Technical Sales enablement. This role requires deep technical expertise, strong business acumen, excellent communication skills, and a background in IT, InfoSec, and/or Cloud.

**Responsibilities**

*   Define and track enablement KPIs to demonstrate ROI and business impact
    
*   Partner with Pre-Sales Engineering leadership to design and execute an enablement roadmap
    
*   Assess knowledge gaps and build programs for technical sales, services, success, and partners
    
*   Manage onboarding logistics and serve as the primary point of contact for new technical hires
    
*   Build and manage ongoing training series for technical teams and partners
    
*   Partner with TME to define demo and hands-on lab strategies to support internal and partner skill development
    
*   Oversee enablement tools (CMS, LMS, Confluence, project management systems) and automate processes (APIs, Workato)
    
*   Develop and deliver training programs, labs, and technical summits
    
*   Facilitate ongoing education for technical teams (both technical and soft skills)
    
*   Collaborate cross-functionally with product, engineering, and solutions architecture
    
*   Travel occasionally for meetings, events, and off-sites
    

**Qualifications**

*   Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience
    
*   3–5 years in technical enablement
    
*   Background in InfoSec, IT, or Cloud as SE, SE Manager, Architect, or Practitioner
    
*   Experience in curriculum development, field training, program and project management
    
*   Proficiency with AI tools to support pre-sales processes, performance, and department efficiency 
    
*   Strong executive presence and communication skills
