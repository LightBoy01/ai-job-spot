### Description

Teaching Socials is one of the fastest-growing consulting firms in the DACH region and the leading consultancy for personal brands in the finance & real estate sector. We connect reach, lead generation, and sales in one system – and your playing field is the CRM heart behind it.

As our Senior IT Specialist for Automation, Data & Revenue Operations, you will be responsible for the entire CRM architecture end-to-end: ensuring clean data, smart automations, clear dashboards, and measurable revenue impact. Your ultimate goal is to achieve more speed in sales, less friction in processes, and full transparency in our figures.

In this role, you will be instrumental in:
*   **CRM Management:** Setting up Close/HubSpot to ensure maximum efficiency in client support and sales.
*   **Technical Expertise:** Utilizing AI and automation to eliminate routine tasks and elevate quality across the board.
*   **Data Analysis:** Gaining actionable insights from CRM data and translating them into concrete, implementable measures.
*   **Process Optimization:** Systematically removing friction, reducing lead times, and significantly increasing close rates.
*   **Training & Support:** Empowering the team to confidently use our systems – quickly, pragmatically, and with clear documentation.

### Responsibilities

Your daily ownership will involve:

*   **CRM Management:** Structuring Close & HubSpot, managing pipelines, stages, properties, lead routing, SLA rules, and ensuring impeccable data hygiene.
*   **Automation & AI:** Building flows with Make/Zapier, managing webhooks, implementing scoring models, auto-tasks, and email/WhatsApp sequences; deploying AI snippets (e.g., for qualification tags, summaries).
*   **Integrations:** Connecting Typeform, ManyChat, Calendars, Payment, Ads Platforms, Airtable/Notion – ensuring bidirectional syncs and deduplicated datasets.
*   **Analytics & Dashboards:** Developing conversion chain tracking and creating comprehensive Excel/Sheets/Airtable dashboards, including alerts.
*   **Process Optimization:** Working closely with Sales/Account Management to identify bottlenecks, build Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) & playbooks, and conduct A/B tests.
*   **Training & Support:** Providing enablement for Sales/Account Management through short guides, Loom videos, and playbooks.
*   **Customer CRM Training (Close/HubSpot):** Delivering practical workshops and concise Loom guides on CRM setup, best practices, KPI dashboards, and daily usage, including Q&A sessions.

### Qualifications

We are looking for a highly skilled professional with:

*   Strong marketing tech affinity and deep CRM know-how (preferably Close, also HubSpot).
*   **Proficiency in Make or Zapier** (APIs, Webhooks, error handling, versioning).
*   Very strong **Excel/Sheets skills** (Pivot, Query, Regex, modeling) & advanced Dashboarding capabilities.
*   Experience with **AI applications** for workflow acceleration (e.g., auto-tags, notes, scoring).
*   A clean **data mindset**: skilled in deduplication, mappings, schemas, and naming conventions.
*   Ability to create structured documentation (SOPs, runbooks) & clear communication skills (German C1+).

**Nice to have:**

*   Experience with Airtable/Notion as an Ops-Layer, Typeform/ManyChat, Google Tag Manager / UTM-Attribution.
*   Basic understanding of B2B Sales Funnel & High-Ticket Lead Generation.
