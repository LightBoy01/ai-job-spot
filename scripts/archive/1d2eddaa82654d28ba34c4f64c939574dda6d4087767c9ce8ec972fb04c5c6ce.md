### Job Description

By bringing together people that use their passion for purposeful innovation, at Lockheed Martin we keep people safe and solve the world's most complex challenges. Our people are some of the greatest minds in the industry and truly make Lockheed Martin a great place to work. With our employees as our priority, we provide diverse career opportunities designed to propel development and boost agility. At Lockheed Martin, we place an emphasis on empowering our employees by fostering innovation, integrity, and exemplifying the epitome of corporate responsibility. Your Mission is Ours.  
  
Lockheed Martin Space is seeking a Summer 2026 Intern, HR Business Partner. In this role, you will get exposure to the breadth of the HR discipline through experiential learning in areas such as Total Rewards and Performance Management, Workforce Strategy, Analytics, Talent and Organizational capability, Talent Acquisition, Employee Relations, and Culture, and more! In this role, you will be placed in a teaming environment and work on projects that align with our Company's HR Strategy, as well as obtain hands on experience through projects and client exposure within the National Security Space HR Team.  
  
The successful candidate will have experience and/or knowledge of what the field of Human Resources is, strong interpersonal and communication skills, and a passion to jump in and learn new things.  
  
This position can be located in Denver, CO, or in Valley Forge, PA

### Basic Qualifications

\- Demonstrate an interest and ability to enter the HR field and be pursuing either an undergraduate or advanced degree in HR, labor relations or a related area with specific coursework in HR.  
\- Effective written and verbal communications skills  
\- Strong project management and problem solving skills  
\- Knowledge of Microsoft Office Products  
\- Ability to clearly articulate strong career interest in the field of Human Resources

### Desired skills

\- Confidence in communicating in 1:1 and group settings.  
\- Leadership potential and competencies as evidenced by good citizenship, achievement and involvement in non-academic pursuits such as extracurricular activities, community service, or special projects  
\- Good organization skills and able to handle multiple priorities.
