### Description

Teaching Socials is one of the fastest-growing consulting firms in the DACH region and the leading consultancy for personal brands in the finance and real estate sectors. As an Account Manager, you will be responsible for closing high-value deals ranging from €48,000 to €90,000, as well as securing renewals from existing clients. We are seeking a Senior Sales Consultant / Closer who is passionate about building strong customer relationships and thrives in a sales-driven environment.

### Responsibilities

Your daily tasks will combine independent consulting and account management, specifically advising finance and real estate companies on topics such as content marketing, branding, sales, lead generation, business development, and scaling.

Key responsibilities include:

*   **Negotiating** and **closing** contracts, and **selling** additional services and renewals.
*   **Developing** and **refining** products as well as internal processes.
*   Continuously obtaining **referral** contacts.
*   Ongoing **enhancement** of the **Customer Experience**.
*   **Identifying** potential customers and **business opportunities**.

### Qualifications

We are looking for an absolute A-Player and all-rounder who is ready to go the extra mile. The ideal candidate possesses strong B2B sales experience, excellent communication skills, and a compelling demeanor.

Essential Requirements:

*   4+ years of experience in Sales or Account Management.
*   Proven experience in B2B closing.
*   Highly communicative and enjoys interacting with people.
*   Demonstrated ability to close large deals.

Advantageous Skills & Experience:

*   Team leadership experience (for individuals eager to take on significant responsibility).
*   Experience in the Online Marketing, Agency, or Coaching space.
*   Experience in consulting companies.
*   Background in self-employment, indicating a broad skill-set and high degree of independence.

We are not seeking candidates who primarily:

*   Have worked predominantly in large corporate structures.
*   Have worked mainly in retail sales environments (e.g., car dealerships).
*   Possess limited closing experience in sales.
*   Are merely looking for 'just a job' without a desire for growth.
