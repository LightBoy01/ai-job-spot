**What is Portfolio Administration Team responsible for?**
==========================================================

*   The Portfolio Administration department provides operational support to Institutional, Sub-advised & High Net Worth separate accounts managed by Franklin Templeton. This includes issue resolution, providing accounting and reporting services, and delivering comprehensive account information to our internal and external partners.
    

**What are the ongoing responsibilities of Senior Client Service Representative?**
==================================================================================

*   Ownership of assigned Institutional Separate Account portfolios on behalf of Franklin Templeton Services (FTS).
    
*   Proactively troubleshoot and resolve queries, track issues to completion via collaboration with key groups and individuals globally.
    
*   Support account events such as on-boarding activities, restructuring and participating in client/custodian meetings for custodian changes.
    
*   Develop solutions and deliver complex/custom reports and procedures as requested.
    
*   Actively developing and maintaining robust stakeholder relationships.
    
*   Deliver premiere and seamless client service to internal and external clients such as Sales/Marketing, Client Services, external Institutional Clients, Portfolio Management, Trading and Performance.
    

**What ideal qualifications, skills & experience would help someone to be successful?**
=======================================================================================

*   Possesses problem solving skills, detail orientation and good analytical skills.
    
*   Communicates effectively, builds and maintains relationships with internal teams and business partners.
    
*   Has good organizational skills and ability to prioritize own workloads.
    
*   Demonstrates a keen interest in applying technology and AI tools, with experience in developing, testing and implementing automated processes.
    
*   Is flexible with assigned work; able to work across time zones and expand scope where as needed.
    
*   Holds a Bachelor’s Degree (preferably finance related) and possesses Investment Management industry experience; ideally in Operations for listed and OTC instruments.
