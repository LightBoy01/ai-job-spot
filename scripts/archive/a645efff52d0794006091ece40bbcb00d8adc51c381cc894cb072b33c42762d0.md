C10 Labs is an AI-focused venture studio dedicated to propelling the frontiers of artificial intelligence. Our venture studio model is extending the AI revolution beyond screens to build AI first ventures that will revolutionize healthcare, climate tech, mobility,  energy, and more. We are looking to add the best entrepreneurial and technical minds to our team to provide our proto-ventures with the resources they need to succeed: funds, go to market acceleration, mentorship, and engineering. Our team is passionately committed to building a more intelligent world, join us on our mission!

Position Overview:

  

We are seeking a part-time AI Fellow to join our team in a part-time capacity. This position is more than a job - it's an opportunity to shape the future of AI, molding our portfolio companies into global leaders in the AI space. This role offers a hands-on opportunity to work on challenging projects, collaborate with experienced researchers and engineers, and contribute to the development of groundbreaking AI solutions. If you value working with the brightest minds in the world in areas like climate tech and health then come join us to shape the future of humanity!

  

  

What you'll do:
---------------

*   Define and lead AI initiatives within our portfolio companies, working closely with founders and tech teams.
*   Oversee the application of AI technologies across multiple platforms and products.
*   Share knowledge and insights through regular presentations, workshops, and collaboration sessions with venture and portfolio teams.
*   Advise on best frameworks and tools for our portfolio companies to create solutions.
*   Advise our executive team on AI strategy, risks, opportunities, and investments.
*   Maintain a strong network in the global AI community, staying informed of the latest research and developments.

Some expertise you may have:
----------------------------

*   Product Development Experience:  full product development and bringing cutting edge AI products, platforms, and services to market 
*   Technical Development: Expertise in AI/ML frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, and others. Strong programming full stack skills in languages such as Python, R, or Java, with experience in implementing machine learning models and algorithms.
*   Knowledge of cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud, Azure) for deploying AI solutions at scale.
*   Familiarity with AI model optimization, including hyperparameter tuning, model evaluation, and performance benchmarking.
*   Major plus will be experience with assessing tech readiness in AI projects using commonly used frameworks like TRL, AI Maturity Models, Model Validation Frameworks, SAFE AI, IEEE, IRL. 
*   Excellent communication skills and ability to explain complex AI concepts to non-technical stakeholders.

*   An exciting opportunity to shape the AI landscape and create industry-defining companies.
*   Collaborative, innovative, and intellectually stimulating work environment.
*   Exposure to real-world AI projects and applications.
*   A platform to leverage your AI expertise and make a global impact.

If you are passionate about AI, would love to work with an amazing team, ready to solve challenging problems, and eager to transform industries through innovative technologies, we want to hear from you. Apply now and join us on our mission to shape the future of AI.
