C3 AI (NYSE: AI), is the Enterprise AI application software company. C3 AI delivers a family of fully integrated products including the C3 Agentic AI Platform, an end-to-end platform for developing, deploying, and operating enterprise AI applications, C3 AI applications, a portfolio of industry-specific SaaS enterprise AI applications that enable the digital transformation of organizations globally, and C3 Generative AI, a suite of domain-specific generative AI offerings for the enterprise. Learn more at: **[C3 AI](https://c3.ai/)**

As a Senior Product Manager, you will be instrumental in shaping the future of the C3 Agentic AI Platform — a highly available, scalable infrastructure designed to power agents and AI applications at enterprise scale. You’ll advocate for the operator’s experience, deeply understanding their goals, pain points, and workflows. Your mission: to define and drive a product strategy that delivers world-class cloud infrastructure, enabling seamless deployment and operation of agentic AI solutions. 

**Responsibilities:** 

*   Deliver cloud services that offer developers and data scientists compelling, convenient, and cost-effective access to AI-native infrastructure. 

*   Define and lead the product vision, strategy, and roadmap for the C3 Agentic AI Platform’s cloud infrastructure. 

*   Own the full product lifecycle — from ideation and definition through development, launch, and iteration. 

*   Translate complex technical requirements into clear, prioritized product initiatives, balancing long-term strategic goals with short-term business impact. 

*   Collaborate cross-functionally with engineering, data science, UX, and go-to-market teams to build and deliver exceptional solutions. 

 **Qualifications:** 

*   5\+ years of product management experience, with a strong track record of delivering cloud infrastructure or platform products. 

*   Deep understanding of cloud-native technologies, developer workflows, and distributed systems. 

*   Experience working with AI/ML platforms or data infrastructure is a strong plus. 

*   Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with experience engaging stakeholders across engineering, data science, UX, and go-to-market functions 

*   Proven ability to develop and effectively communicate a product vision, strategy, and roadmap. 

*   Bachelor’s degree required from a 4-year university, with a focus on science, technology, engineering, math, or similar. M.B.A degree preferred**.** 

**Candidates must be authorized to work in the United States without the need for current or future company sponsorship.** 

C3 AI provides excellent benefits, a competitive compensation package and generous equity plan.
