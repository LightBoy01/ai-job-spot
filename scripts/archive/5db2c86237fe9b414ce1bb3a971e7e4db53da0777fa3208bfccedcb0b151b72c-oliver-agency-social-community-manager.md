### Description

Established in 2004, OLIVER is the world's first and only specialist in designing, building, and running bespoke in-house agencies and marketing ecosystems for brands. Partnering with over 300 clients in 40+ countries, our unique model drives creativity and efficiency, delivering tailored solutions that resonate deeply with audiences. As part of The Brandtech Group, we're at the forefront of leveraging cutting-edge AI technology to revolutionize how we create and deliver work, enhancing efficiency, sparking creativity, and driving insightful decision-making.

We are looking for a **Community Manager (German Speaking)** to join our team in **Hamburg, Netherlands**. This is a hybrid role, requiring 2 to 3 days a week on-site at the office, offered as a Fixed-Term Contract (FTC) with a view to extend, on a full-time basis.

In this role, you will work embedded within our alcoholic beverage client's team, driving brand presence through content and community building across various local market social channels, supporting paid initiatives. You will foster and build a social community across Meta, Pinterest, Reddit, YouTube, Twitter, and Snapchat for our client's key alcohol brands. This involves engaging with consumers, conducting social listening to identify trends and insights, and monitoring competitors. The successful candidate will consistently bring best-in-class recommendations to the client's brand and media team, proactively collaborating with internal colleagues and client stakeholders to develop, implement, track, and optimize content across social platforms. Working closely with Brand Managers and the onsite team, you will represent the voice of the consumer, monitoring conversations, posting content, and managing daily social media operations.

### Responsibilities

*   Manage and distribute predominantly paid content across all digital and social channels on a day-to-day basis.
*   Help build, grow, and manage online communities for the client's key consumer brands.
*   Identify and track relevant community metrics, develop monthly reports, and outline key insights and implications.
*   Champion creativity and deliver excellent customer experiences through content planning, working closely with the onsite studio to develop best-in-class content, and partnering with the client's media agency to understand campaign performance and optimize creative.
*   Brief and manage creatives and designers to produce new social assets, ensuring alignment with brand guidelines.
*   Act as a brand guardian to ensure all work adheres to the client's brand standards.
*   Adopt and use the brand's voice to respond to and engage with the user community.

### Qualifications

*   Impeccable and proven written and verbal communication skills in **English and German**.
*   Passion for delivering on-brand and on-brief social communications.
*   Pro-active planning and leadership of creative community initiatives.
*   Practical know-how of how to manage social media channels, especially their intricacies.
*   A thirst to learn more and to discover new and exciting trends within the social space.
*   Experience using analytical/social listening tools such as **Sprinklr** for reporting and to gather insights.
*   The ability to manage and filter workflow as well as organize and prioritize workloads to maximize productivity.
*   Understanding of how to integrate with a client-side team whilst maintaining a top-tier agency service.
*   Experience working with global lifestyle brands – ideally in the alcohol or drink sector.
*   Post-secondary education in communications, journalism, business, marketing or a related field.
*   Experience in online communications, be it via editorial, social, or advertising.

### Author's Take

**1. What's the most exciting aspect of being an embedded Community Manager within a client team, particularly in the alcohol sector, and what unique challenges does it present?**

As an insider, I'd say the thrill comes from truly being a part of the brand's core. Unlike a traditional agency, you're not just a vendor; you're on the front lines, deeply understanding the product, the consumer, and the business goals. For an alcohol brand, this means you get to shape the narrative around exciting, lifestyle-driven products, seeing your work directly impact sales and brand love. The direct feedback loop is incredibly satisfying. However, this immediacy also brings challenges. You need to be agile, balancing the client's vision with OLIVER's innovative approach, all while navigating the strict regulatory landscape of the alcohol industry. It's a high-stakes, high-reward environment where your insights directly influence strategic decisions and help keep the brand relevant and responsible.

**2. How does OLIVER's unique in-house agency model and focus on AI truly empower a Community Manager's growth and daily work, beyond typical agency setups?**

Being part of OLIVER, especially with our backing from The Brandtech Group and our aggressive push into AI, means you're not just managing communities; you're pioneering the future of community engagement. Our in-house model cuts through typical agency bureaucracy, giving you direct access to client stakeholders and faster decision-making, which is fantastic for professional growth. You'll be working with cutting-edge AI tools to inform your social listening, content optimization, and audience targeting, transforming how you derive insights and execute campaigns. This isn't just about efficiency; it's about pushing creative boundaries with data-driven precision. It's an environment where your ideas are valued, you're encouraged to experiment, and you have the resources of a global network to help you become a true expert in the evolving landscape of digital communities.
