### Description
SIKA – Dr. Siebert & Kühn is an expanding "Hidden Champion" with a 120-year history, known for its innovative products and a culture of genuine collegiality, driven by passionate individuals committed to clever solutions and satisfied customers.

With approximately 350 employees, in-house development, and an export quota of 65%, SIKA is a global leader in the manufacturing of series components and sensors for measurement technology in flow, temperature, and pressure. Their diverse customer base spans machine and plant engineering, renewable energies, and climate technology. This specific role is part of a succession plan, offering a clear long-term perspective.

### Responsibilities
*   Manage and develop significant OEM series customers within the attractive sales region of Baden-Württemberg.
*   Strategically acquire new customers in dynamic growth markets such as energy, data center cooling, and industrial plant engineering.
*   Provide technical consultation and present customer-specific measurement technology solutions.
*   Handle comprehensive customer support, including proposal generation, negotiation, and contract closure.
*   Collaborate closely with internal departments such as R&D, inside sales, marketing, and project management.

### Qualifications
*   A seasoned sales professional with experience in technically complex series components, or an applications engineer with strong communication skills.
*   Keen interest in technical customer applications and the ability to articulate technical advantages effectively.
*   Enjoys consulting, taking responsibility, and negotiating – both online and on-site.
*   Proficiency in English and a willingness to travel approximately 25%.
