### Responsibilities

**Key Responsibilities**  
•    Manage AI implementation projects from concept to delivery, ensuring business goals, timelines, and ROI are met.  
•    Translate business requirements into clear technical deliverables and oversee solution execution.  
•    Serve as product manager for the HTS AI chatbot, driving adoption, improvements, and new use cases across business units.  
•    Collaborate with business and IT partners (HR, Finance, Operations, Customer Experience, Security) to identify and prioritize AI opportunities.  
•    Partner with business stakeholders to identify high-impact opportunities for agent automation.  
•    Define best practices, frameworks, and reusable components for enterprise-wide agent development.  
•    Mentor and guide AI engineers and product managers in building and scaling intelligent agent solutions.  
•    Ensure AI solutions are implemented securely, with attention to data privacy, compliance, and user trust.  
•    Lead initiatives to automate ITIL and business workflows, improving service delivery and customer experience.  
•    Monitor performance and adoption of AI solutions, sharing results and lessons learned to drive continuous improvement.  
•    Evaluate and integrate emerging agentic AI platforms to accelerate enterprise adoption.  
•    Manage, mentor, and develop direct report(s), fostering a culture of collaboration and growth.  
•    Act as an advocate for AI-enabled services, clearly communicating business benefits to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.  
**Competencies**  
•    Strong project management skills with attention to scope, deadlines, and quality.  
•    Business and customer focus: ability to understand needs, translate them into solutions, and measure impact.  
•    Analytical and problem-solving skills with a practical, results-oriented mindset.  
•    Clear communication skills, both written and verbal, for diverse audiences, from executives to end users.  
•    Collaborative team player with the ability to influence without formal authority.  
•    Strategic thinking with the ability to align AI initiatives with broader business priorities and long-term value creation.

**Requirements**  
Education & Certifications  
•    Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Business, Data Science, or related field required.  
•    Master’s degree (MBA or MS in AI/Data Analytics) a plus.  
•    ITIL v3+ certification preferred.  
•    Certifications in AI/ML, data science, or automation platforms (e.g., AWS AI/ML, Microsoft Azure AI, Google AI, UiPath) desirable.  
**Experience**  
•    5–7+ years of experience in IT service delivery, project management, or digital transformation.  
•    Proven experience in designing, building, and deploying AI agents in production environments.  
•    Proven success managing enterprise chatbot platforms (Moveworks, IBM Watson, Microsoft Copilot, Zendesk Answer Bot, etc.).  
•    Hands-on expertise with platforms like Microsoft Copilot Studio and Moveworks.  
•    Strong background in enterprise connectors, APIs, and SDKs to enable seamless agent integration with business applications.  
•    Solid understanding of LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), orchestration, and prompt engineering.  
•    Strong background in automation and orchestration tools (UiPath, Automation Anywhere, WorkFusion, etc.).  
•    Hands-on experience with ServiceNow (knowledge management, workflows, ITIL processes).  
•    Demonstrated ability to manage cross-functional projects with measurable business outcomes.  
•    Hands-on experience with SharePoint (administration, integrations).  
•    Experience collaborating with business stakeholders to deliver customer-focused technology solutions.  
•    Prior people management and leadership experience preferred.
