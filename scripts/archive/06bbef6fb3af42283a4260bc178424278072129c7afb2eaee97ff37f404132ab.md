**Job Title:** Business Analyst

**Location:** Barrow-in-Furness. We offer a range of hybrid and flexible working arrangements – please speak to your recruiter about the options for this particular role.

**Salary:** circa £50,000 depending on skills and experience

**What you’ll be doing**

*   Analyse and document business requirements to inform solution selection and deployment plans
*   Ensure process alignment across international partners and internal teams
*   Maintain clear traceability between business needs and technical delivery
*   Provide insight through Business Analysis and process modelling to drive informed decisions
*   Act as a key voice in shaping the digital and business architecture of the SSNA programme

**Your skills and experiences**

Essential:

*   Validated experience in business analysis within multi-layered organisations
*   Good skills in requirements gathering, stakeholder engagement, and traceability
*   Demonstrable experience using Business Analyst tools and techniques to support strategy-making
*   Process modelling and continuous improvement experience
*   Understanding of project delivery within Agile or hybrid methodologies

Desirable:

*   BCS Diploma or qualified through equivalent experience

**Benefits**

As well as a competitive pension scheme, BAE Systems also offers employee share plans, an extensive range of flexible discounted health, wellbeing and lifestyle benefits, including a green car scheme, private health plans and shopping discounts - you may also be eligible for an annual incentive.

**The AUKUS IM&T Enablement Team:**

We’re excited to offer a unique and strategic opportunity for an experienced Business Analyst to join our team supporting the AUKUS programme a ground-breaking partnership between the UK and Australia.

This role is key to establishing enterprise-level collaboration, enabling integrated, secure, and scalable processes across both nations. You’ll be working with stakeholders across the business to translate needs into actionable insights and ensure that requirements are aligned to support delivery of multi-layered solutions. We offer relocation support packages across all Submarines roles, subject to meeting eligibility criteria.
