**Job Description:**

We are seeking a Data Analyst with strong SQL, Python, and data visualization skills to support Assent’s Data Science & AI initiatives. This role combines technical analysis with organizational knowledge-building. You will work with teams across the company to map out available data sources, understand how they are used, and consolidate this knowledge into a central inventory for the Data Science & AI team.

In addition to being the go-to expert on where and how data lives within Assent, you will provide analysis, dashboards, and insights that accelerate model development, monitoring, and evaluation. This is a unique opportunity to shape the foundation of data-driven AI innovation at Assent.

Key Requirements & Responsibilities 

*   Data Discovery & Knowledge Building: Interview teams across the organization to understand what data they use, where it resides, and how it is accessed.
*   Data Inventory & Documentation: Build and maintain a central inventory of datasets, including metadata, ownership, quality checks, and access details.
*   SQL & Python Analysis: Query, clean, and analyze datasets to support exploratory data analysis (EDA), model development, feature engineering, and validation.
*   Apply data cleaning, transformation, and interpolation techniques to handle missing, inconsistent, or incomplete datasets, ensuring reliable downstream use.
*   Insights & Dashboards: Develop reports and dashboards in Tableau, and Sigma to track data quality, dataset usage, and AI/ML performance metrics.
*   Ad-hoc Support: Provide on-demand analyses to support AI/ML experiments, audits, and evaluations.
*   Data Quality Assurance: Monitor and flag issues in data accuracy, completeness, and consistency.
*   Develop methods to reconcile and connect disparate data points across systems to produce coherent, insight-ready datasets.
*   Cross-Functional Collaboration: Serve as a bridge between business teams and the Data Science & AI group, ensuring smooth data access and knowledge transfer.

**Qualifications:**

*   3+ years of professional experience as a Data Analyst, BI Analyst, or in a similar analytical role.
*   Bachelor’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, Business Analytics, or related field.
*   Strong SQL expertise for querying and transforming large datasets.
*   Proficiency in Python (Pandas, Polars, Dask, numpy, matplotlib/seaborn, Jupyter) for analysis and visualization.
*   Experience with Git and version control for managing analytical code and ensuring reproducibility.
*   Demonstrated ability to perform data cleaning, imputation/interpolation, and transformation at scale.
*   Hands-on experience with exploratory data analysis (EDA) and statistical techniques.
*   Experience integrating and connecting data from multiple sources to extract actionable insights.
*   Experience using BI/reporting tools with Tableau, Sigma (Or similar tools like Power BI).
*   Excellent communication and interviewing skills to engage with cross-functional teams and capture data knowledge effectively.
*   Strong organizational skills to build and maintain a living data inventory.
*   Understanding of AI/ML data workflows (feature engineering, model evaluation, monitoring) is a plus.
*   Familiarity with data governance practices and cloud environments (AWS, Snowflake, Azure, or GCP) is an asset.
*   Familiarity with feature stores for reusable AI/ML datasets is an asset.
