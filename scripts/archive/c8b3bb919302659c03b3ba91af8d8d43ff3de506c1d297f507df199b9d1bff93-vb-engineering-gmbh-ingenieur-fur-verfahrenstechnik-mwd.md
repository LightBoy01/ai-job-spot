### Description\\nVB-Engineering GmbH is a young, modern, and future-oriented company specializing in high-quality engineering services for industrial and maritime plants. Efficiency, transparency, quality, and adherence to deadlines are core to our philosophy. We focus on process engineering, energy-generating systems, and marine operations technology, using our expertise to enhance plant efficiency, safety, and performance. We are looking for an experienced, motivated, and technically and personally convincing Process Engineer (m/f/d) to join our interdisciplinary team of engineers, technicians, and specialists. This role offers diverse projects with technical depth, a dynamic team, scope for personal development, flexible working hours, and home office options.\\n\\n### Responsibilities\\n- Develop, optimize, and evaluate process engineering processes and systems.\\n- Create technical documentation, e.g., P&ID diagrams, safety and explosion protection documents.\\n- Conduct process and component validations.\\n- Project planning, as well as schedule and cost monitoring.\\n- Coordinate interfaces between involved trades/departments.\\n- Support on-site commissioning and technical clarifications.\\n\\n### Qualifications\\n- Completed degree in process engineering or a comparable qualification.\\n- Experience in industrial plant construction, ideally in the chemical, refinery, or similar industrial plant sectors.\\n- Proficiency in creating technical documentation.\\n- Proficient in CAD systems, e.g., AutoCAD.\\n- Independent, structured working style, and strong teamwork skills.\\n- Good communication skills in German (and ideally English).\\n\\n### Author's Take\\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for an experienced Process Engineer to join a forward-thinking company that truly values innovation, quality, and its people. You'll tackle challenging, varied projects that push technical boundaries, working within a supportive, dynamic, and interdisciplinary team. The commitment to personal growth, flexible working arrangements including home office, and a long-term career perspective make this a highly attractive role for professionals seeking to make a significant impact while maintaining a healthy work-life balance.
