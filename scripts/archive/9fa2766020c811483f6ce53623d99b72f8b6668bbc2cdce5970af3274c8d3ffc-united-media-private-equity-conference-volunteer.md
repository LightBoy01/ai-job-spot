### Description\nAre you looking to expand your professional network with over 500 leaders, entrepreneurs, and investors in the Private Equity sector, while also developing crucial leadership and soft skills? Private Equity Insights offers you a unique opportunity to volunteer at the leading French Private Equity Conference in Frankfurt on March 13th, 2026.\n\nPrivate Equity Insights orchestrates the largest global events dedicated to fostering finance and investment innovation, bringing together C-level executives, General Partners (GPs), Limited Partners (LPs), entrepreneurs, and investors. Our conferences feature prominent industry players such as Blackstone, BlackRock, Apollo, Carlyle, and KKR. We are seeking ambitious and talented individuals to join a team of 25+ peers, critical to building, running, and delivering this prestigious event. Volunteers are the backbone of Private Equity Insights, and this is your chance to gain an insider's view into how the world's largest Private Equity community is nurtured.\n\nJoin us at the Westin Frankfurt on March 13th, 2026, for an unforgettable experience. For a glimpse into our events, watch our [2023 Milan's highlight video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1w9amNARIROInji1jERo8KzgPLCfemnQM/view?usp=drive_link). Discover more about our conferences on our website [here](https://pe-insights.com/private-equity-conferences/france/).\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a Private Equity Conference Volunteer, you will be an essential part of a collaborative team responsible for the successful execution of the leading French Private Equity Conference. Your contributions will include:\n\n*   Actively participating in a team of 25+ peers to help build, run, and deliver the conference.\n*   Assisting with various on-site tasks to ensure the smooth flow of the event.\n*   Committing to attend a 2-hour training session and rehearsals the afternoon before the event.\n*   Being present and engaged throughout the conference day.\n\n### Qualifications\nWe are looking for individuals who are ambitious and talented, with a keen interest in the private equity sector and a desire to contribute to a high-profile event. While no specific prior experience is required, candidates should possess:\n\n*   A proactive and collaborative team spirit.\n*   Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.\n*   A strong desire to learn, network, and develop professional competencies.\n*   An eagerness to contribute to the success of a major industry conference.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis volunteer opportunity with Private Equity Insights is an exceptional gateway for anyone looking to break into or advance within the finance and investment world, particularly private equity. It's far more than just volunteering; it's a strategic networking and development platform. You'll gain unparalleled access to over 500 industry leaders, develop critical soft skills through direct engagement and individual management training, and build valuable relationships with like-minded professionals. The chance to receive an official certificate, attend an exclusive dinner, and learn the intricacies of running a leading European event makes this a truly unique and impactful experience. This role is perfect for ambitious students or young professionals eager to fast-track their professional growth and expand their industry footprint.
