### Description

i-flow is a successful software company specializing in Industrial IoT, empowering leading clients in the manufacturing industry to accelerate their digital transformation and enable Data-Driven Use-Cases at Scale. We are seeking a Senior Backend Developer to take technical ownership of central components within our internationally deployed IoT software platform. This role offers significant influence over architectural decisions and the opportunity to actively shape our development roadmap, playing a pivotal part in how industry works with data.

### Responsibilities

As a Senior Backend Developer, you will:

*   Take technical ownership for central components across the development, testing, deployment, and operation of our internationally used IoT software platform.
*   Contribute to the continuous development of our core software, focusing on scalability, performance, and ease-of-use, with a genuine impact on technical and architectural decisions.
*   Actively shape our development roadmap, introduce new ideas, and take responsibility from conception to rollout.
*   Continuously evolve our technology stack and evaluate new tools, frameworks, and patterns collaboratively within the team.
*   Undertake diverse and interdisciplinary tasks, including technical customer demonstrations.

### Qualifications

We're looking for someone who:

*   Has **5+ years of experience** in backend development with **NodeJS, ideally based on TypeScript**, and has explored low-level languages like Go.
*   Is familiar with **industrial automation protocols** such as OPC UA, MQTT, Modbus TCP, or Siemens S7.
*   Knows how to strategically leverage **LLMs and AI Agents** for automation, debugging, or creative problem-solving.
*   Has experience in designing, using, and extending **REST APIs**.
*   Brings experience in deploying and operating modern software, whether via **Docker, Kubernetes, GitOps**, or classic **CI/CD pipelines**.
*   Enjoys technical discussions, takes responsibility for their code, and finds pragmatic solutions, even under time pressure.
*   Is familiar with modern frontend frameworks like **React, Vue, or Angular** – or works closely with frontend teams to enable seamless interfaces.
*   Has a good sense for UX, can clearly communicate technical concepts, and convinces with their ideas – within the team or with clients.
*   Holds a degree in Computer Science or a comparable field.
*   Is familiar with relational and NoSQL databases like **MongoDB, MySQL, or InfluxDB** – for transactional data, time series, or scalable data storage.

You don't need to master everything, but you bring solid experience in several of these areas and have the ambition to continuously develop your skills. C2-level German language skills and a residence in Germany are required.
