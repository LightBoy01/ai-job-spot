### Description

Digitl is a young and innovative company at the forefront of marketing and cloud technology. In our collaborative and cross-functional environment, we develop cutting-edge concepts to guide clients across diverse industries into a digitally-driven future. We're looking for a Search Specialist to join us as a Digitl Native, bringing fresh and forward-thinking ideas to be implemented alongside our team of experts and direct Google contacts.

In this role, you will primarily work with key Google tools such as Google Ads, Search Ads 360, and to some extent, Campaign Manager 360. Your core objective will be to elevate our clients to the next level of data-driven campaign performance within Google Ads or the broader Google Marketing Platform.

### Responsibilities

*   Serve as a Paid Search Specialist, advising clients in your domain and delivering strategic recommendations coupled with practical, actionable insights.
*   Translate client needs and objectives into effective development plans and strategies that directly contribute to their business goals.
*   Lead the implementation of projects, guiding clients through the intricacies of the Google Marketing Platform (GMP) and advanced Google Ads setups.
*   Contribute to impactful client presentations and insightful case studies, while continuously staying abreast of industry trends by researching new web technologies and strategies to enrich team knowledge.
*   Actively participate in the development of our AdTech services, expanding the depth of our service offerings and growing our portfolio of digital products with a strong focus on Google Ads.

### Qualifications

*   A degree with a specialization in Marketing, E-commerce, E-business, or Data Science, or a completed vocational training (e.g., as a Marketing Communications Specialist).
*   2–3 years of experience in search engine marketing, with significant practical experience in Google Ads and/or Search Ads 360.
*   Demonstrated hands-on experience working with or for a media agency, or collaborating with industry partners.
*   Proficient in analyzing data, and skilled in creating and editing detailed reports.
*   Excels in both internal team collaboration and external client interactions, communicating clearly and effectively.
*   Keeps up-to-date with the latest industry trends and developments.
*   Approaches new challenges with enthusiasm, a positive attitude, and a strong eagerness to learn.
