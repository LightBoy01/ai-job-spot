### Description
Welcome to Flix, where a modern, dynamic work environment awaits you, complete with fair compensation, diverse development opportunities, and the chance to simplify, make more sustainable, and affordable global travel through innovative technologies.

As a **Payroll Manager** at Flix, you will play a crucial role in ensuring a smooth and reliable payroll process. Collaborating closely with our Payroll Experts, you will support the accurate and timely payment of our employees and actively contribute to payroll projects and process optimizations.

This position is based in Munich and is a **fixed-term contract for 15 months, part-time (20 hours/week)**.

### Responsibilities
*   Prepare the monthly payroll for our employees in Germany, working closely with our external payroll provider.
*   Ensure that all payroll-relevant data – such as new hires, departures, contract changes, benefits, and absences – is completely, accurately, and punctually submitted.
*   Support in clarifying account discrepancies with our financial accounting department.
*   Act as the primary point of contact for employees, HR colleagues, and external partners regarding payroll and employment-related queries.
*   Contribute ideas for optimizing our payroll processes and assist in their implementation.

### Qualifications
*   At least 1 year of experience in preparatory payroll accounting.
*   Good knowledge of payroll tax and social security law.
*   Experience with payroll tools (e.g., DATEV, SAGE, SAP) and HR systems.
*   A structured, reliable, and discreet working style.
*   Very good written and spoken German and English skills.

We believe in looking at the whole picture – even if you don't meet all requirements 100%, but still believe you can make a difference, we encourage you to apply!
