### About Magpie

At Magpie, we envision a world where every child, no matter their background, becomes a confident reader. Our nonprofit team combines research-backed instruction, joyful game design, and cutting-edge AI to tackle America’s reading crisis head-on — with the goal of eradicating illiteracy through a digital-first, student-centered approach.

### Role Summary

We’re seeking an experienced Principal Product Manager to lead the design, execution, and delivery of personalized, AI-enhanced foundational literacy products. This PM will be instrumental in building experiences that meet every child where they are, seamlessly integrate high-fidelity assessments, and deliver engaging, research-based learning moments — all while unlocking actionable insights for educators and families. 

### What You’ll Do:

*   Drive end-to-end product development for personalized learning pathways, AI-powered practice, and formative assessments — from shaping vision to delivering live impact in classrooms.
*   Deeply embed user research and data insights to inform product strategy and prioritize features that maximize student growth and teacher usability.
*   Partner cross-functionally with engineering, academics, design, data science, and implementation teams to deliver high-quality, joyful student experiences that align with our learning science principles. 
*   Integrate data, assessment, and efficacy  rigor and personalization engines to ensure every learner’s progress is tracked, analyzed, and used to adapt their reading journey.
*   Champion a culture of measurement, using clear success criteria, KPIs, and user data to inform decisions and iterate quickly.
*   Communicate vision and progress clearly to leadership, funders, and partner organizations, grounding plans in evidence and user stories.

**Requirements**

### A strong candidate will possess:

✅ Proven product management experience (5–8+ years) shipping student-facing digital learning products at scale — ideally in K–8 literacy or learning science.  
✅ Demonstrated success leading AI- or data-driven personalization features in an edtech or consumer learning context.  
✅ Strong grasp of instructional data, personalization, game and/or assessment design and how to translate assessment data into actionable insights and adaptive experiences.  
✅ Deep user empathy and experience designing for joyful, identity-affirming learning for diverse student communities.  
✅ Ability to align research, design, engineering, and operations to deliver on time, at quality, and with clear metrics.  
✅ Comfort navigating nonprofit mission and revenue trade-offs, balancing short-term product-market fit with long-term vision to eradicate illiteracy.

**Within your first 30 days, you will:**

*   Ramp up on Magpie’s platform, K–2 assessment launch, engineering work processes, and overall product strategy.
*   Build context on assessment models, technical architecture, and data capabilities.
*   Establish relationships across engineering, design, data, and learning science.
*   Identify immediate areas where you can add capacity and impact.

**By day 60, you’re:**

*   Engaging with Magpie’s priorities and aligning product work to critical outcomes.
*   Driving feature assessment strategy by balancing AI-driven, ethical and traditional approaches.
*   Supporting market-facing and in-development components to ensure readiness for release.
*   Beginning to influence key decisions on the learning engine and platform integration.

**By day 90, you will:**

*   Take ownership of core product areas, particularly around data, and using advanced analytics to guide decisions.
*   Lead the planning and execution of upcoming 3–8 assessment builds, positioning the roadmap as a future-forward strategy and a key selling point for Magpie.
*   Drive clarity and alignment across stakeholders to ensure product releases are well-defined, validated, and delivered with confidence.
*   Operate as a product leader, connecting data, engineering, and product strategy to accelerate Magpie’s impact.

**What Success Looks Like:**

*   Thousands more students meet reading proficiency benchmarks via personalized skill pathways.
*   High-quality assessment data seamlessly fuels adaptive instruction and teacher dashboards.
*   Students engage joyfully with literacy practice, building confidence and agency.
*   Partners and internal teams trust and champion your product plans because they see measurable impact.
