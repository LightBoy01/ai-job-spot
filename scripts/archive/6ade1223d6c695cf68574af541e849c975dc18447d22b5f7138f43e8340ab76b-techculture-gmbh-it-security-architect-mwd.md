### Description
Our client is developing a highly critical IT platform – a project with enormous impact and a clear mission to ensure digital sovereignty at the highest level. They are looking for an IT-Security Architect (m/w/d) to strengthen their in-house team, taking responsibility for the development and implementation of security strategies, and providing security-focused consultation for internal projects. This role offers the unique opportunity to contribute to a project of national relevance, with diverse development opportunities and flexible working models.

### Responsibilities
*   Provide security-focused consultation for the implementation of internal IT projects and support the deployment of security measures and controls.
*   Process security-critical data from vulnerability and attack surface management to develop strategic decision templates and recommendations for the organization.
*   Support the analysis and remediation of security vulnerabilities.
*   Co-design and implement information security policies and procedures.
*   Conduct awareness campaigns, and develop and deliver training sessions on security technologies and best practices.

### Qualifications
*   Successfully completed studies in (Business) Informatics, IT Security, or a comparable qualification.
*   Several years of professional experience in IT security or cyber defense.
*   Strong technical understanding in network security, endpoint hardening, or vulnerability management.
*   Profound knowledge of regulatory standards such as IT-Grundschutz, ISO 27001, or NIS-2.
*   Excellent communication and project management skills.
*   Fluent in German and English.
