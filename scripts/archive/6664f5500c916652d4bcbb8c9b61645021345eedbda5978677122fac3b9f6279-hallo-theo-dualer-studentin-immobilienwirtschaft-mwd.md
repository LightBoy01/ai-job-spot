### Description

Are you ready to kickstart your career in modern property management? We are seeking an enthusiastic and motivated individual for a dual study program in Real Estate Management (B.A.) with the IU International University of Applied Sciences. This program combines academic learning with hands-on practical experience at hallo theo, providing a comprehensive understanding of both operational day-to-day tasks and innovative digital projects within the real estate sector. You'll spend two days a week studying and three days a week gaining practical experience in our Berlin office. During lecture-free periods, your practical involvement will be full-time, ensuring a deep dive into the industry.

### Responsibilities

As a Dual Student, you will learn all facets of modern property administration, from operational tasks to digital innovation projects. Your responsibilities will include:

*   Providing comprehensive support in WEG (condominium owners' association) and rental property management.
*   Assisting with and attending owner meetings, and communicating effectively with owners.
*   Preparing and reviewing financial statements and utility bills.
*   Supporting maintenance and modernization projects for various properties.
*   Actively contributing to the ongoing development and optimization of our digital processes and tools.
*   Gradually taking on independent responsibility for sub-areas and your own projects as you gain experience.

### Qualifications

To succeed in this role, you should possess:

*   General higher education entrance qualification (Abitur) or advanced technical college entrance qualification (Fachhochschulreife).
*   A keen interest in real estate, economics, and digital administration.
*   A strong sense of responsibility and excellent organizational skills.
*   A joy for communication and a collaborative team spirit.
*   A proactive attitude, a willingness to contribute ideas, think critically, and continuously learn new things.
