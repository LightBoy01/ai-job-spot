Come and grow a better future with Sappi. Join us as a **Research & Development Chemist** to work at the forefront of innovation and take your skills and knowledge to new levels.

**Join a company focused on innovation**

Sappi makes world-class products for customers in over 150 countries and with seven research-and-development facilities operating worldwide, Sappi leverages cutting-edge technology to create new advances for our products and find fresh solutions for our customers.

**Play your part in world-class R&D**

In our manufacturing facility in Dundee, our people are our inspiration for success. Every idea, every collaboration, every perspective, creates breakthroughs for us all. Here we develop and improve upon our heat-sealable coatings for the food packaging industry.

**Your role on our team**

We are seeking a proactive and technically skilled R&D Chemist to join our team and drive innovation, product development, and process improvement. This role is ideal for a hands-on problem solver with strong scientific expertise and a commitment to safety and quality.

**Key Responsibilities:**

*   Lead R&D experiments, trials, and product development activities.
*   Ensure compliance with safety, quality, and environmental standards.
*   Provide technical support across Production, QC, Sales, and Supply Chain.
*   Translate customer requirements into product specifications.
*   Develop and implement testing methods, SOPs, and staff training.
*   Support operational efficiency, cost savings, and continuous improvement.
*   Stay up to date with regulatory requirements (BRC, EU, FDA) and emerging technologies.

**Qualifications & Experience:**

*   Bachelor’s degree (Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, Materials Science or related).
*   Strong knowledge of formulation science (solutions, extrusion, polymers).
*   Hands-on experience with analytical techniques (e.g. DSC, GC, IR, viscometry).
*   Proficiency in MS Office and data analysis/reporting.
*   Proven track record in polymer chemistry, formulation, and process development.

**Competencies:**

*   Strong scientific, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
*   Excellent communication and presentation abilities.
*   Organised, detail-oriented, and able to prioritise effectively.
*   Collaborative, with a continuous improvement mindset.

This is an exciting opportunity to contribute to cutting-edge R&D while supporting operational and customer-focused goals in a dynamic environment.
