### Description

thinkGROUP is a highly successful system house group, continuously striving for innovation and placing great importance on the ongoing optimization of our internal processes. The use of modern technologies is not an end in itself, but an expression of our innovative spirit and lived customer orientation. Excellent IT service requires optimal systems. Our motivated team gives its best every day to inspire our customers anew.

We are looking for a **Senior IT Administrator (m/w/d)** to join our Technical Consulting department at our **Bochum** location as soon as possible. Become part of the thinkGROUP and support us in this critical role.

After consultation, the position can also be performed from our branch offices in **Münster, Hamburg, Mainz, Nuremberg, or Leipzig**.

We offer a comprehensive benefits package designed to support your well-being and professional growth, including:
*   Home-office flexibility
*   Up to 4 weeks of Workation annually (mobile work from European countries)
*   Extensive onboarding complemented by our Buddy-Program
*   Annual continuing education budget
*   Employee referral bonus of €1,000
*   €200 employer contribution for your prescription glasses
*   Free lunch on "Healthy Wednesday"
*   Employer contribution to asset accumulation
*   High degree of personal development and individual advancement
*   Training opportunities in our in-house fitness studio
*   Bike-leasing offers for you and your family
*   Employee discounts and benefits with numerous cooperation partners
*   Joint leisure activities

Look forward to a team that sticks together, even outside of work. Visit our career page to get a personal impression and learn what distinguishes thinkGROUP.

### Responsibilities

*   Administration and maintenance of our internal IT systems and networks
*   Installation, configuration, and updating of hardware and software
*   Support for employees with IT-related questions and problems
*   Monitoring system performance and performing regular backups
*   Implementation of security measures to protect the IT infrastructure
*   Documentation of IT processes and systems

### Qualifications

*   Completed IT training (e.g., IT specialist for system integration or information electronics technician) or a comparable qualification
*   Professional experience in IT administration
*   Profound knowledge of Windows operating systems and network technologies
*   Experience with virtualization technologies is an advantage
*   Excellent problem-solving skills and an analytical mindset
*   Team player, strong communication skills, and a service-oriented attitude
