### Description\nRemmert specializes in automated storage and logistics systems, focusing on space-saving and fully automated handling of sheet metal, long goods, and workpieces. As a family-led company since 1945, Remmert values open communication, short decision-making paths, and continuous growth. They are seeking a Software Developer to support the new development of their Warehouse Management Software. This role is crucial for a future-oriented industry combining mechanical engineering, automation, and logistics.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Full-stack software development in C# / .NET.\n- Conception and development of new products and solutions.\n- Technical documentation of the software.\n- Active participation in the technical architecture and conception of new features.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed training as an IT specialist for application development (gn) or a comparable qualification.\n- Sound experience in C# development.\n- Experience with Blazor and Entity Framework Core is desirable.\n- Understanding of clean code, modern software architectures, and design patterns.\n- Very good written and spoken German language skills.\n- Good English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a compelling opportunity for a C# Full Stack Developer to join Remmert, a stable, family-led company at the forefront of automated logistics. The role offers significant individual contribution to core products and active involvement in technical architecture. Remmert fosters a highly supportive and employee-centric culture, reflected in generous benefits such as 30 days of holiday, profit sharing, E-Bike leasing, fitness subsidies, and comprehensive health management. The company strongly emphasizes professional development through regular reviews and a dedicated training catalog, ensuring continuous growth. For a long-term-oriented individual seeking a role with real impact and a strong focus on well-being, this position offers an exceptional career path.
