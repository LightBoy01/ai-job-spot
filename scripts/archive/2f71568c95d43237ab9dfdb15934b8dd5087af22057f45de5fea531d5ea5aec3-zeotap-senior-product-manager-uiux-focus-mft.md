### Description

We are seeking a talented and experienced UI/UX-focused Product Manager to join our team. This is a hands-on role where you will be a key driver in defining and delivering exceptional user experiences for our enterprise SaaS product. You will work closely with design, engineering, and business stakeholders to translate user needs into a compelling product vision and strategy.

### Responsibilities

*   Lead the product lifecycle from ideation to launch, with a strong focus on the user interface and user experience of our product.
*   Conduct user research, market analysis, and competitive landscape assessments to identify opportunities for product improvement.
*   Define and prioritize product requirements, user stories, and acceptance criteria.
*   Work collaboratively with UI/UX designers to create intuitive, elegant, and effective designs.
*   Partner with engineering teams to ensure timely and high-quality product delivery.
*   Analyze product performance and user feedback to inform future iterations.
*   Serve as the voice of the user, championing their needs throughout the development process.
*   Adhere to company, privacy, and information security policies and procedures.
*   Complete all assigned awareness training on time.

### Qualifications

**Required:**

*   5-7 years overall experience; with 3+ years of Product Management experience in an enterprise SaaS environment.
*   Proven track record of successfully managing products with a strong focus on UI/UX.
*   Deep understanding of user-centered design principles and methodologies.
*   Excellent communication, presentation, and interpersonal skills.
*   Ability to think strategically and execute with an attention to detail.
*   Experience with agile development methodologies.
*   A portfolio or examples of work showcasing your contributions to successful product launches.

**Preferred:**

*   Experience with marketing tools, such as CRM, email marketing platforms, or analytics tools.
