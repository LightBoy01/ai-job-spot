Tasks
-----

*   Own and continuously improve the full Amazon performance marketing setup.
*   Manage PPC campaigns, analyze performance data and implement scaling measures.
*   Bring a **full-funnel marketing mindset** to Amazon: move beyond pure conversion-driven last-click tactics and build upper & mid-funnel strategies that strengthen brand demand, not only capture it.
*   Collaborate closely with Product & Design to optimize listings, creatives and overall brand presence.
*   Introduce campaign structures and creative concepts that elevate the brand and reduce dependency on low-funnel intent.
*   Build and expand Multichannel Performance (e.g. Google, Pinterest) over time.

Requirements
------------

*   Several years of experience in Amazon PPC & Performance Marketing.
*   Strong understanding of **full-funnel acquisition** (awareness → consideration → conversion) and how to adapt this thinking to Amazon’s historically low-funnel environment.
*   Ideally experience with premium or high-price products on Amazon.
*   Strong brand and design understanding — you think beyond keywords and care about aesthetics & impact.
*   Fluent in English or German; background in D2C or Amazon-focused brands is a big plus.a
