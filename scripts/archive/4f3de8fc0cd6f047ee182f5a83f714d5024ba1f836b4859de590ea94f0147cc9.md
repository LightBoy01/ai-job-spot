### About the Role

Are you a natural leader with a passion for event planning? As our Event Planning Lead, you'll steer a talented team, ensuring every event dazzles and delights while meeting our clients’ expectations. Join us in the heart of downtown Chicago and help us elevate our event experiences to new heights. Let’s make magic happen together!

### You're Good At

*   Leading and inspiring a team of event planners to achieve greatness.
*   Managing the end-to-end event planning process with precision and flair.
*   Optimizing workflows and implementing innovative strategies for seamless operations.
*   Fostering cross-department collaboration for flawless event execution.
*   Ensuring client satisfaction through exceptional service and quality assurance.

### You Might Also Have

*   A Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Hospitality Management, or a related field.
*   Over 5 years of experience in event planning, particularly with complex events.
*   Proven success in operations management and team leadership.
*   Familiarity with event technologies like Cvent or Momentus.
*   A knack for strategic thinking and solving problems creatively in a fast-paced environment.
*   Ability to work onsite in Beaverton, OR.
