Join **Project Spearmint** — a multilingual AI response evaluation project focused on reviewing large language model (LLM) outputs in different languages!

  

**Why Join?**

\- Shape the future of AI in your native language.

\- Work from home with flexible hours.

\- Contribute to advancing cutting-edge language model development.

  

**What You’ll Do**

\- Review short, pre-segmented datasets.

\- Evaluate model-generated replies based on **Tone** or **Fluency**.

\- Read a user prompt and two model replies, then rate each using a five-point scale.

\- Provide short rationales for extreme ratings.

  

**Project Breakdown**

**Batch 1 – Tone**

Determine if replies are helpful, engaging, fair, and appropriately formal. Flag issues such as condescension, bias, or tonal mismatches.

**Batch 2 – Fluency**

Assess grammatical accuracy, clarity, coherence, and natural flow.

  

**Requirements**

\- Native-level fluency in Nepali language.

\- Strong comprehension of English.

\- Residing in Nepal.

\- Can commit to working 25 hours per week.

  

**Payment Details**

This is a project-based opportunity with **CrowdGen**. Payment details will be provided upon selection.

  

  

**How to Get Started**

\- Apply to Project Spearmint.

\- If selected, you will receive an email from CrowdGen to create your account.

\- Log in, reset your password, and complete the setup requirements.

\- Start the qualification process: estimated qualification duration: 30–40 minutes.
