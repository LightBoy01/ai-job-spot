### Description
Our client is a successful mechanical engineering company with approximately 500 employees. With high technical competence and innovative strength, the company develops and produces sophisticated machinery and systems for international customers.

In the course of digital advancement, the ERP landscape is continuously optimized and adapted to business processes. For this, a committed personality is sought who combines IT expertise with a deep understanding of processes in mechanical engineering – and enjoys actively driving digitalization and process optimization as an ERP Administrator.

### Responsibilities
*   Administer and maintain the ERP system.
*   Ensure system availability and optimize performance.
*   Perform regular system updates and patches, as well as adapt and develop interfaces to other systems (e.g., financial accounting, materials management, purchasing).
*   Conduct monitoring and troubleshooting to ensure smooth operation.
*   Manage users, assign rights, and create role and authorization concepts.
*   Adapt and configure the ERP software according to the requirements of specialist departments.
*   Support the implementation of new modules and functionalities.
*   Create and maintain technical documentation.
*   Collaborate with external service providers and resolve technical problems.
*   Train and support end-users in utilizing the ERP system.

### Qualifications
*   Completed vocational training or university degree in computer science, business informatics, or a comparable qualification.
*   Several years of experience in ERP system administration.
*   Solid knowledge of ERP software, ideally in common systems such as SAP, Microsoft Dynamics, or Oracle.
*   Experience in database administration and SQL is an advantage.
*   Strong analytical and technical skills.
*   High problem-solving competence and a structured way of working.
*   Strong communication skills and teamwork ability.
