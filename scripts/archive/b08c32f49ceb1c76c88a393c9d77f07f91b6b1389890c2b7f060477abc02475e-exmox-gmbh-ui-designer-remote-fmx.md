Your mission
------------

As a UI Designer at exmox, you'll join a growing design team to shape the user interface and experience of our mobile-first B2C product, impacting millions of users. You'll create polished, responsive, and accessible interfaces, collaborating with developers and product managers to implement them. You'll own features from concept to handoffs.

Your key responsibilities
-------------------------

*   Design visually engaging UIs for our mobile app, ensuring usability, accessibility, consistency, and scalability.
*   Deliver production-ready assets, specs, prototypes, and documents to developers and support them during implementation.
*   Build, maintain, and own our evolving design system. You will have a proven track record of designing, shipping, and maintaining Figma design systems.
*   Design illustrations, icons, and other graphical elements needed for your designs.
*   Perform design reviews to ensure implementations align with the designs.
*   Collaborate with our UX researcher and data team to gather feedback on your design

Your profile
------------

*   Experience designing for mobile and web, ideally for B2C mobile apps.
*   5+ years of experience working in product teams.
*   A portfolio that demonstrates outstanding UI craft, visual design, especially on mobile.
*   Problem-solving mindset, able to simplify complex ideas into clear UI.
*   Expert-level skills in Figma.
*   Comfortable working in fast-growing teams, shaping systems and processes, and making clear decisions to keep momentum and move projects forward.
*   Proactive, self-driven, and able to bring structure to ambiguity.
*   Strong collaboration and communication skills in agile product teams.
*   Awareness of accessibility and usability principles.
*   Fluent in English; German is a plus.

Nice to have:
-------------

*   Understanding of how to balance visual flair with performance and usability.
*   Experience in the gaming or marketing industry.
*   Awareness of front-end technologies and how your design is going to be implemented.
*   Experience with designing white-label multi-brand products.
*   Skilled at creating transitions and animations that make interfaces feel dynamic.
*   Capable of connecting the Figma design system to the codebase so design updates flow seamlessly into production.

Why us?
-------

*   **Work-Life Balance:** 30 days of paid vacation.
*   **Professional Development:** Annual education budget of €1,500.
*   **Diversity:** Join an international team with diverse cultural backgrounds. _Please note: This is a fully remote position, but requires working within a German or European time zone to ensure smooth collaboration with the team._
