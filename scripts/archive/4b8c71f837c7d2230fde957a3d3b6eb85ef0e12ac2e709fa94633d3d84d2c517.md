This is primarily a remote role targeting someone in the Atlanta, GA or North Canton, OH metropolitan areas. Will require occasional in person meetings in those locations.

We are seeking a dynamic and strategic Corporate Executive Recruiter to lead full-cycle recruitment for executive-level roles (Senior Director and above) across global corporate functions. This high-impact role requires a seasoned recruiting professional with deep expertise in executive search, stakeholder engagement, and talent strategy. You will serve as a trusted advisor to senior leaders and HR partners, ensuring the attraction and selection of top-tier leadership aligned with Diebold Nixdorf’s business goals and cultural values.

### Responsibilities

*   Own and execute end-to-end recruitment for Senior Director, VP, SVP, and C-suite roles across corporate domains including Finance, HR, Legal, Strategy, and Operations.
*   Partner with senior executives and HR Business Partners to define leadership needs, build candidate profiles, and craft tailored search strategies.
*   Develop and implement proactive sourcing approaches using executive networks, competitive intelligence, and advanced research tools.
*   Demonstrate ability to manage relationships with external executive search firms when necessary, with a strong preference for directly identifying and engaging top executive talent.
*   Lead high-touch candidate engagement, assessment, and offer negotiation processes with professionalism, discretion, and speed.
*   Deliver strategic talent insights and market data to inform hiring decisions and long-term workforce planning.
*   Ensure a seamless, confidential, and exceptional candidate experience throughout the executive recruitment lifecycle.
*   Champion Diebold Nixdorf’s employer brand, leadership culture, and values in all candidate interactions.
*   Maintain accurate and compliant records in ATS and HRIS systems, including Oracle Recruiting Cloud, supporting data integrity and reporting excellence.

### Qualifications

*   7+ years of experience in corporate executive recruiting or retained executive search, ideally within a global, matrixed organization.
*   Experience in Manufacturing, Banking and/or Retail is a plus
*   Proven success recruiting for Senior Director-level and above roles across diverse corporate functions.
*   Strong business acumen and ability to influence and advise senior stakeholders.
*   Expertise in managing complex, confidential searches with high levels of discretion and urgency.
*   Proficiency in executive sourcing tools, ATS platforms (e.g., Oracle Recruiting Cloud, Workday, SuccessFactors), and market mapping techniques.
*   Exceptional communication, negotiation, and relationship-building skills.
*   Ability to manage multiple executive searches simultaneously while maintaining quality, speed, and strategic alignment.
*   Familiarity with global hiring practices, compliance standards, and cultural nuances.
