### Description
Wolt is a technology company dedicated to enhancing local life through joyful, simple, and profitable solutions. Starting with restaurant food delivery in 2014, Wolt has expanded to deliver (almost) everything across 500+ cities in 30 countries. Since joining forces with DoorDash in 2022, Wolt continues its ambitious global expansion.

Working at Wolt offers an exciting and challenging environment where individuals are empowered to learn, build, and ship more than in many other companies. It's a place for self-starters with an entrepreneurial spirit.

The German Mass Account Management (MAM) team is crucial in supporting thousands of restaurant partners nationwide. As a Mass Account Manager, you will play a key role in significantly improving the operational success of these partners at scale. You'll be instrumental in ensuring thousands of merchants remain active, available, and engaged with Wolt's commercial tools, leveraging data, structured processes, and scalable communication to drive performance, feature adoption (like Promotions and Ads), and contribute to Wolt's overall growth, all while meticulously maintaining clean and reliable portfolio data.

### Responsibilities
*   Manage the operational health and performance of a large group of restaurant partners.
*   Monitor and improve key performance indicators (KPIs) such as availability, active rate, rejection rate, and churn.
*   Design and lead initiatives that drive the adoption of Promotions, Deals, and Ads among partners.
*   Ensure strong data hygiene and accurate reporting for your portfolio.
*   Support campaign rollouts and help scale best practices across the partner portfolio.
*   Collaborate cross-functionally with other internal teams to achieve shared goals.

### Qualifications
*   3+ years of experience in operations, commercial, or support roles.
*   Excellent communication skills in both German and English.
*   Proficiency in Excel/Google Sheets and CRM platforms.
*   Strong sense of ownership, structured thinking, and meticulous attention to detail.
*   An analytical mindset coupled with a strong drive to continuously improve processes.
