About the job
-------------

In this role, you will be a part of a cross-functional team with a set of skills spanning research, prototyping, and production mobile development that will drive the boundaries of how headsets and glasses unlock new capabilities for users.

You will be working with some of the most cutting-edge technical developments (e.g., generative AI, agentic systems) across all modalities. Work on one of the best form factors for fully uncovering the potential of AI agents. Collaborate with excellent cross-functional partners (Product Management, User Experience, Program Management, and User Experience Research) to develop completely new user interfaces for people to interact with head-worn devices. Work with a fun group of people who enjoy working together to make new experiences come to life.

For decades, the computing revolution has reshaped our world driven by breakthroughs in compute, connectivity, mobile, and now, AI. Google's XR team is at the forefront of the next major leap – the convergence of AI and XR. This is more than just new devices – it's about reimagining how we interact with the world around us. We're building a future where lightweight XR devices pair with helpful AI to augment human intelligence, offering personalized, conversational, and contextually aware experiences.

Responsibilities
----------------

*   Define roadmap for Extended Reality (XR) Interfaces powered by AI conversational agents that supports a healthy balance of innovation, user research, and industry trends to push the boundaries of how users interact with headsets and glasses.
*   Partner closely with the broader Applied AI teams, XR developer team, App teams and Google deepmind teams to align development with real-world use cases and tests.
*   Approach to software design, architecture, and coding standards to ensure the highest quality and scalability of XR's AI-driven system interface.
*   Lead, mentor, and develop top engineering talent across the XR Human Interface team.
