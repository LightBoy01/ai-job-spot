### Description
Speechify is on a mission to ensure reading is never a barrier to learning. Over 50 million people utilize our text-to-speech products—across iOS, Android, Mac, Chrome, and Web—to transform various texts into audio, enhancing reading speed, comprehension, and retention. Recognized by Google as Chrome Extension of the Year and by Apple for its Design award for inclusivity in 2025, Speechify is a leader in its field.

Our diverse team of nearly 200 individuals operates in a 100% distributed setting, with no physical office. Our talent hails from top companies like Amazon, Microsoft, and Google, leading PhD programs, high-growth startups like Stripe, and many are founders themselves.

With the significant growth of our Android app, now the most used text-to-speech app in the Play Store, we are seeking a Senior Android Engineer. This key role is crucial for supporting our expanding user base and spearheading new, exciting projects. We are looking for a strategic thinker who thrives in fast-paced environments, is passionate about making product decisions, and excels at building delightful user experiences. Our flat organization empowers individuals to lead through demonstrated technical prowess and consistent, rapid delivery. Work ethic, robust communication skills, and a drive to win are paramount. Our interview process is designed to be efficient, typically completing technical interviews within one week.

This role offers a unique opportunity to shape the company and product within a fast-growing, entrepreneurial team that encourages risk-taking and intuition. We believe in a hands-off management approach, enabling you to focus on your best work and make a substantial impact in a transformative industry. We offer competitive salaries, a friendly atmosphere, and a strong commitment to a great asynchronous culture. Join us to build products that directly impact and support millions, including those with learning differences like dyslexia, ADD, low vision, and autism, at the exciting intersection of artificial intelligence and audio.

### Responsibilities
*   Own major features, working closely with the design team to ensure high-quality delivery.
*   Strategically shape the future direction and technical roadmap of our Android team.
*   Own, maintain, and continuously improve reliability metrics for key features within the application.
*   Actively participate in cross-functional discussions with Product, Design, and other Engineering teams.
*   Conduct thorough pull request reviews and provide constructive support to other teammates.
*   Effectively handle critical issues and navigate unexpected challenges with agility.
*   Take full ownership of feature releases, including providing nightly builds for the QA team.

### Qualifications
**Required:**
*   5+ years of professional software engineering experience.
*   Strong familiarity with core Android components and SDKs.
*   Demonstrated experience building or significantly contributing to at least one Android application.
*   Acute product design intuition and deep user empathy.
*   A strong drive to push the boundaries of Android UI/UX excellence.
*   A clear understanding of the importance of testing and a practical approach to writing comprehensive tests.
*   Self-driven initiative to improve the app and codebase beyond explicit specifications.
*   Rock-solid experience with Kotlin, Kotlin Coroutines, Kotlin Flow, Dagger 2, MVVM, Clean Architecture, Background Services, Music Player Service, Android Animations, Jetpack Navigation, and JUnit tests.
*   Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
*   A user-oriented approach to problem-solving.
*   Driven by and receptive to continuous feedback from leaders.

**Bonus:**
*   Experience building, maintaining, or contributing to open-source Android projects.
*   Experience with iOS, Web, or NodeJS development.

**Technologies We Use:**
*   Kotlin, Kotlin Coroutines, Kotlin Flow

**Question 1:** "Given Speechify's distributed, flat structure, how does a Senior Android Engineer truly influence the product roadmap and technical direction, beyond just feature implementation?"

We genuinely expect you to bring a product mindset, spotting opportunities, challenging assumptions, and proposing solutions that shape our roadmap. With a hands-off management approach, the impact of your technical excellence and strong communication skills is amplified.
