**About the Opportunity** 

Reporting to the Group Head of Strategy and Architecture, the Head of Innovation and AI will spearhead the development and execution of a forward-thinking, technology-led innovation strategy. This pivotal role is designed to harness emerging technologies—particularly AI—to unlock commercial value, elevate customer experiences, and drive operational efficiency.

As a strategic leader, you’ll collaborate closely with business and technology stakeholders to foster a culture of experimentation and continuous learning. You’ll champion innovation while ensuring alignment with governance, regulatory, and delivery frameworks. From leading proof-of-concepts to embedding an innovation mindset across the organisation, your work will shape how VicRoads prototypes, evaluates, and scales transformative technologies. Driving innovative ideas through qualification in a corporate environment, and defining the metrics for measuring success is pivotal to demonstrating value.

This is a hands-on, commercially focused role within the Strategy and Architecture function, working alongside cross-functional teams in product, engineering, delivery, and commercial. If you're passionate about driving digital transformation and making a tangible impact through innovation and AI, we invite you to lead the charge.

*   **Innovation Strategy & Leadership:** Responsible for leading, developing, and maintaining an innovation strategy that aligns with business objectives, customer needs, and emerging technology trends, supporting both the overarching Technology Strategy and Product Roadmaps. It champions a culture of innovation by fostering curiosity, experimentation, and continuous improvement across the organisation. Additionally, the role plays a key part in enabling VicRoads’ digital transformation through the strategic application of innovation and artificial intelligence.
*   **Technology-led Innovation and Emerging Technology:** Maintain up-to-date knowledge of technology trends to identify innovation opportunities and drives the exploration and adoption of emerging technologies - such as generative AI, machine learning, AI vision, AR, and automation - to enhance customer experience, operational efficiency, and delivery capability.
*   **Governance and Corporate Alignment:** Contribute to the development of VicRoads’ AI framework, support the Architecture function in shaping the Technology Strategy and roadmaps, and establish innovation guardrails that balance agility with compliance. Ensure initiatives align with governance, risk, and regulatory standards, and are measurable through clear KPIs, outcomes, and defined success criteria
*   **Stakeholder Engagement and Influence:** Build trusted relationships across technology and business areas to understand and influence strategic priorities, partners with stakeholders to embed innovation into core processes, and ensure initiatives are commercially focused. Strong focus on communicating effectively across diverse audiences, from technical teams to business leaders

  
**About you**   
  
You’re a strategic and technically skilled professional with a strong foundation in business, computer science, AI, or innovation—supported by relevant qualifications or equivalent industry experience. You bring hands-on expertise in programming languages liek  Python, Java, or Rust, and are familiar with AI tools and frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, LangChain, and AWS AI/ML services. Ideally, you also hold certifications in digital delivery practices (e.g. SAFe, SCRUM) and architecture frameworks.

Your experience spans multiple disciplines including digital product development, engineering, architecture, and data. You’ve led cross-functional teams through prototyping and incubation, and have a proven track record of driving ideas from concept to execution. You’re confident working with emerging technologies such as GenAI, ML, AI Vision, and AR, and have deep experience in strategic planning and business analysis within complex environments.

You’re comfortable engaging with stakeholders from C-suite to engineering, and excel at articulating business value, setting measurable success criteria, and working within agile delivery environments. Your understanding of modern digital design, customer experience, and cloud engineering enables you to contribute meaningfully to innovation and transformation initiatives.
