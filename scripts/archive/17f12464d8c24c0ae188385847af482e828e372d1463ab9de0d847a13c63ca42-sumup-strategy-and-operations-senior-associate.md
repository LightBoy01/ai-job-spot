### Description
At SumUp, we are driven by the purpose of leveling the playing field for small businesses globally. We empower entrepreneurs by providing simple, affordable tools for managing payments, finance, and customer relationships. Our passionate team thrives on human connection, autonomy, and continuous learning, guided by our values: Founder's Mentality, Team First, and We Care. We are committed to building an enduring, people-positive, and disciplined organization that constantly innovates from within, with agility at its core.

As the Strategy & Operations Senior Associate in Global Operations, you will operate at the critical intersection of strategy and execution. This role involves translating high-level priorities into measurable, data-driven initiatives that directly enhance how we serve millions of merchants worldwide. You'll act as a vital connector across teams, ensuring alignment between regional and global goals, managing complex cross-functional projects, and basing all business decisions on solid data and clear business cases. This is a high-visibility position offering direct exposure to senior stakeholders across Operations, Product, Support, and Strategy, providing a unique opportunity to significantly influence SumUp's global operating model and overall customer experience.

### Responsibilities
*   Support leadership in translating the Global Operations strategy into actionable roadmaps, clear objectives, and measurable outcomes that improve efficiency, scalability, and merchant experience.
*   Manage end-to-end delivery of global initiatives — from scoping and planning to execution, tracking, and measurement — ensuring alignment across Product, Support, and Regional Operations teams.
*   Build and present robust business cases and financial models to evaluate new initiatives, balancing merchant impact, feasibility, and ROI.
*   Analyze operational data to track impact, identify trends, and provide actionable recommendations to leadership through structured reporting and executive-ready presentations.
*   Ensure global priorities are consistently and effectively implemented across regions, acting as a strategic connector between HQ and local markets.
*   Partner with diverse stakeholders—including Product, CSM, Support, and Regional Ops—to strengthen strategic alignment and accelerate impact.
*   Contribute to capacity planning and process improvements that enhance operational efficiency and sustainability.

### Qualifications
*   You have 2–4 years of experience in strategy, consulting, operations, financial planning, or program management, ideally in a high-growth tech, fintech, or startup environment.
*   You have strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate data into actionable insights and business recommendations.
*   You're comfortable driving cross-functional initiatives without direct authority, influencing stakeholders, and aligning diverse teams around shared objectives.
*   You can balance strategic thinking with hands-on delivery, thriving in fast-paced environments with competing priorities.
*   You're skilled in building business cases, performing ROI analyses, and managing complex projects end-to-end.
*   You're a clear communicator capable of crafting concise narratives, reports, and presentations that influence senior stakeholders.
*   You bring a structured, detail-oriented approach and are motivated by impact, curiosity, and continuous improvement.

### Author's Take
**1. What's the most exciting challenge this role will tackle in the coming year, and how will it directly impact SumUp's global growth?**

"The biggest thrill here is the sheer scale and complexity of optimizing our global operations. We're growing rapidly across 36 markets, and each brings its own nuances. Your main challenge will be to act as the architect of efficiency, taking our overarching strategy and translating it into concrete, data-backed initiatives that resonate in every region. Imagine identifying a bottleneck in one country, then designing a solution that improves the merchant experience and operational cost-effectiveness across all our territories. You're not just solving problems; you're building the future operating model of a global fintech leader. The impact is direct: your work ensures we can serve millions more small businesses seamlessly, making their financial lives easier and strengthening SumUp's competitive edge worldwide. It’s about being the connective tissue that turns ambitious vision into tangible, global improvements."

**2. Given SumUp's 'Founder's Mentality' and 'Team First' values, how does this role empower an individual contributor to truly make a difference, especially in a global setup?**

"This role is a prime example of our 'Founder's Mentality' in action. While you won't have direct reports, you'll own initiatives from conception to completion, much like a founder would. You're expected to identify opportunities, build the business case, secure buy-in from diverse stakeholders (Product, Support, regional teams), and drive execution. It’s about proactive problem-solving and seeing your ideas translate into global impact. The 'Team First' aspect comes alive through the extensive cross-functional collaboration. You'll constantly be partnering, influencing, and aligning teams across different time zones and cultures. This means a lot of trust is placed in you to lead through influence, fostering a shared sense of purpose. Senior leaders are accessible and genuinely value your insights, creating an environment where a sharp, driven individual contributor can genuinely shape strategic direction and operational excellence."
