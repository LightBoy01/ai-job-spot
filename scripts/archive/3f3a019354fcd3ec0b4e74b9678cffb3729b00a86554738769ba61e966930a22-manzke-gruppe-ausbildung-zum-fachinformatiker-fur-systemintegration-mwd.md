### Description\nTeam player wanted: Are you interested in IT and digital media? Great! Then a three-year apprenticeship as an IT specialist for system integration (m/f/d) could be just right for you. The IT department focuses on the procurement, integration, and support of information technology within our corporate group. It is also responsible for the support of EDP systems, the network, and the software used, including licensing and license management. Apply now for your apprenticeship starting on **01.08.2026**.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Planning and implementation of projects in a team\n- Performing first-level support and employee training\n- Installation of new hardware and software\n- Supporting our IT team with administration\n- Granting system permissions and maintaining user administration\n- Support for in-house hardware and software (laptops, tablets, mobile phones, printers, etc.)\n- Management/documentation of servers and workstations\n- Implementation and review of measures for IT security and data protection\n- Error analysis and troubleshooting in daily business\n\n### Qualifications\n- Enthusiasm for IT and confident handling of digital media\n- Willingness to learn new technologies and tasks\n- Precise and independent working method\n- At least a secondary school certificate (Mittlere Reife)\n- Good grades in Mathematics and German\n- Communication skills\n- Team spirit\n- High motivation and commitment\n- Structured and independent working method\n- Enjoy working in a team\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent Entry-Level opportunity for aspiring IT professionals eager to kickstart their career with a comprehensive three-year apprenticeship. The role offers diverse and responsible tasks, focusing on system integration and IT support. The company strongly invests in its apprentices' growth, providing regular workshops, support for independent projects, and covering exam preparation costs. With good performance, there's even a possibility to shorten the apprenticeship and excellent chances of permanent employment. Beyond professional development, the workplace fosters a collaborative team environment with social events like summer and Christmas parties, apprentice team events, and practical perks like a discounted canteen and free beverages, emphasizing a supportive and engaging company culture.
