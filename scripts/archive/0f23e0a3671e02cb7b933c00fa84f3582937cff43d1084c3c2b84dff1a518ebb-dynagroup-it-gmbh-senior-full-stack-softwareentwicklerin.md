### Description\nDynaGroup IT GmbH, based in Ottobrunn near Munich, is a specialized software development company focused on creating modern web applications, automating business processes, and integrating AI workflows for mid-sized companies and industry. We are looking for an experienced Senior Full-Stack Developer who is passionate about technology, takes ownership, and is eager to collaboratively shape the future with us. This role offers the chance to build innovative solutions within a small, dynamic team, providing ample space for personal ideas and significant impact.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop web and cloud applications using Angular, TypeScript, and Node.js.\n- Undertake development tasks in Python.\n- Create AI workflows and AI agents.\n- Take responsibility for the architecture and quality of entire features or modules.\n- Actively shape and improve our CI/CD and DevOps processes.\n- Collaborate directly with clients.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 5+ years of experience in full-stack development.\n- Excellent knowledge of Angular, TypeScript, and Node.js.\n- Experience with Python, and ideally with Java or .NET.\n- Practical experience with AI agents, automation, or workflow development.\n- Very good German language skills.\n- Availability to start within 2 weeks is preferred.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a Senior Full-Stack Developer who thrives in a technically excellent, collaborative, and fast-paced environment. DynaGroup IT offers a unique blend of direct impact on product architecture and development, working closely with leadership and senior architects. The culture emphasizes personal growth, quick decision-making, and clear communication, all supported by flexible working arrangements including home office options. If you're looking for a role where your contributions are highly valued, and you have a clear path to leadership or architectural roles at your own pace, this is an ideal fit.
