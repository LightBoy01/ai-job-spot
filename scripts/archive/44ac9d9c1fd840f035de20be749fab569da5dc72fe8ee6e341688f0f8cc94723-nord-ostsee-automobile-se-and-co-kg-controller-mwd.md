### Description
Nord-Ostsee Automobile stands as one of Germany's leading automotive trading companies, boasting an impressive portfolio of brands including Mercedes-Benz, AMG, Aston Martin, smart, Hyundai, and Iveco. With a team of over 1,800 dedicated employees spread across 34 centers in Hamburg, Schleswig-Holstein, Brandenburg, and the Hannover region, we are committed to excellence.

We are seeking a highly motivated and detail-oriented Controller (m/f/d) who shares our passion for automobiles and has a strong affinity for numbers. In this pivotal role, you will be instrumental in ensuring the qualitative reporting and analysis that underpins our operational excellence. Your expertise in controlling processes will be key to maintaining the high quality of our services and supporting the company's continued economic success.

### Responsibilities
*   Prepare and oversee monthly and annual reports, as well as forecasts and budgets for reporting purposes.
*   Continuously monitor the adherence to company objectives and the economic efficiency of the business. Develop actionable recommendations for achieving targets and support their implementation.
*   Work in close coordination with cross-functional departments and provide expert advice to specialist areas within cost center controlling.
*   Intensely delve into and continuously develop the area of cost center controlling through thorough analysis.
*   Conduct actual-versus-target comparisons, deviation analyses, and ad-hoc evaluations.
*   Develop and prepare comprehensive reports for the executive management.

### Qualifications
*   Successfully completed a degree in economics with a specialization in Controlling, or a comparable qualification with advanced training.
*   Proven professional experience in Controlling, a general affinity for IT, and profound knowledge of relevant systems.
*   Knowledge of the German Commercial Code (HGB) and tax law is desirable.
*   Demonstrate responsible and conscientious work habits, ensuring flawless execution of controlling tasks.
*   Possess a strong ability to work independently, a solution-oriented mindset, and a very diligent and organized approach.
*   Exhibit discretion and a high level of service orientation.
*   Display analytical and process-oriented thinking, coupled with a keen understanding of business contexts.
