**Job Description:**
_**Position Overview**:  We are seeking a dynamic and passionate AI Engineer Intern to explore the application of AI in enhancing game development processes. The role centers on researching, evaluating, and prototyping AI tools and machine learning models, with a focus on creating and modifying AI-driven content pipelines for game development. Applications include asset generation, automation of production tasks, and intelligent visual recognition. The intern will prototype and integrate AI solutions within Unity and Unreal Engine, focusing on building and optimizing content pipelines to enhance productivity and innovation in game development._
**Key Responsibilities:**
*   Explore and integrate AI-driven content pipelines into production environments (e.g., asset creation, animation, testing automation).
*   Apply AI models and tools within Unity or Unreal Engine as part of the content pipeline, ensuring smooth integration and practical use in game development.
*   Prototype and experiment with machine learning models for 2D/3D visual recognition.
*   Research and evaluate AI tools (market-ready and open-source) relevant to game development.
*   Analyze AI tool performance and recommend improvements for content pipelines and production workflows.
**Qualifications:**
*   Currently a 3rd or 4th year student majoring in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, or Game Development, with a strong passion for applying AI technologies in game development.
*   Ability to work independently on research tasks while collaborating effectively within a team.
*   Strong communication and documentation skills to clearly present research findings and technical insights.
*   Hands-on project experience with Unity, preferably in 3D projects or those involving advanced algorithms (beyond starter level), and directly relevant to the responsibilities of this role.
*   Proficient in programming (C#, C++, Python, or similar), with strong problem-solving and analytical skills.
