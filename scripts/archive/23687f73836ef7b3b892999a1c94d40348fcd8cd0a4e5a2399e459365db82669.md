### **Video Graphics / Editor**

**Full-time | Remote | LATAM / South Africa**

### **About us:**

At The Global Talent Co., we provide incredible opportunities for the top 5% of global marketers. Join our team and become our employee, gaining access to high-paying salaries, stable employment, and the chance to work with cutting-edge tech companies in Europe and the US.

**Your Role:  
**We’re partnering with a **modern property management company** that’s redefining how people experience real estate through technology, creativity, and service excellence.  
They’re a **tech-enabled service provider**, not just another software brand — on a mission to bring a fresh, modern, and engaging edge to an industry ready for change.

They’re now looking for a **Video Graphics / Editor** to help shape their digital voice by creating visually captivating content that educates, entertains, and inspires across multiple online platforms.

### **What You’ll Be Doing:**

*   Edit and produce **short- and long-form video content** for social media and digital channels.  
    
*   Design motion graphics, titles, and animations aligned with brand identity and visual guidelines.  
    
*   Collaborate closely with the founders and marketing team to bring creative concepts to life.  
    
*   Repurpose and optimize clips from podcasts, Q&A sessions, and interviews for digital storytelling.  
    
*   Explore and implement **AI-assisted tools** to enhance creativity and production efficiency.  
    
*   Design static visuals and graphics for use across digital and social campaigns.  
    

### **What You’ll Bring:**

*   Proven experience as a **Video Editor** or **Motion Graphics Designer**.  
    
*   Proficiency in **Adobe Premiere Pro**, **After Effects**, and **Creative Suite**.  
    
*   Strong storytelling and design skills with a sharp eye for visual detail.  
    
*   Experience producing content for **LinkedIn, Instagram, and YouTube**.  
    
*   Curiosity or hands-on experience with **AI-powered design or editing tools**.  
    
*   Comfortable working independently in a fast-moving, digital-first environment.  
    
*   Excellent **English** skills; knowledge of **German is a strong plus**.  
    

### **Nice to Have:**

*   Background in property tech, real estate, or marketing for service-based brands.  
    
*   Familiarity with AI and automation tools (e.g. Runway, Descript, Midjourney).  
    

### **Why You’ll Love This Role:**

*   Be part of a company that’s **transforming a traditional industry through creativity and innovation**.  
    
*   Collaborate directly with founders on brand-defining visual content.  
    
*   Enjoy creative freedom and flexibility within a remote-friendly setup.  
    
*   Work with a team that values fresh ideas, quality storytelling, and modern design.
