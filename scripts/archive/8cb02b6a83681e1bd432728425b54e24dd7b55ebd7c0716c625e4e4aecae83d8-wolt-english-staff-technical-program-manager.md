### Description

At Wolt, we're on a mission to bring joy, simplicity, and earnings to neighborhoods worldwide through innovative technology. Starting with restaurant food delivery in 2014, we've rapidly expanded to encompass the delivery of nearly everything across over 500 cities in 30 countries. Our journey has been further amplified by joining forces with DoorDash in 2022, fueling our ambitious global expansion.

Working at Wolt is an exhilarating experience; it's where you'll be challenged, learn intensely, build extensively, and ship impactful solutions more rapidly than in most other organizations. We seek self-starters with an entrepreneurial spirit who are ready for an exciting ride.

We are looking for a **Technical Program Manager** to play a pivotal role in scaling Wolt's engineering capabilities, enhancing process efficiency, and upholding our unwavering commitment to operational excellence. This role places you at the very heart of a forward-thinking team dedicated to achieving outstanding results. If you possess technical program management expertise and are driven to make a tangible impact, this opportunity is for you.

As a Technical Program Manager, you will be instrumental in fostering seamless collaboration between business, product, and engineering teams. Your core objectives will involve driving complex technical programs that span multiple teams, meticulously defining and tracking key performance metrics, proactively communicating status and risks to stakeholders, and ensuring absolute alignment on strategic priorities across the organization. This role demands broad technical understanding, world-class program management acumen, the ability to win hearts and minds, top-notch orchestration skills, and a cultural approach that makes things happen. You will be data-driven, passionate about the craft of software engineering, a master of some trades and a jack/jill of all. You will advocate for complex technical programs, ensuring alignment and influencing without direct authority, ultimately owning the programs you define, groom, and facilitate.

This role offers flexibility with office or home-based work opportunities and can be based in Berlin (preferred), Helsinki, or Stockholm.

### Responsibilities

*   **Owning Cross-Organizational Technical Programs**
    *   Run technical initiatives across various teams, groups, and business units, ensuring alignment with requirements and overarching business objectives.
    *   Deep dive into ambiguous areas, identify underlying problems, and guide teams in developing scalable solutions.
    *   Manage diverse stakeholders across engineering, product, legal, sales, operations, and account management teams.
    *   Act as a catalyst, significantly boosting the efficiency of collaboration between product, engineering, and business teams.

*   **Helping to Run Engineering at Scale**
    *   Work with various internal teams, engineering, product, and business leaders to define key technical initiatives.
    *   Build alignment with team and organizational roadmaps to incorporate key initiatives, define priorities, and ensure adherence to industry standards, regulations, and internal policies.
    *   Identify crucial metrics, ensure they are up-to-date, and establish escalation or remediation plans for when guardrails are exceeded.
    *   Build business-driven cases for technical programs to advocate for and prioritize them across the organization.

*   **Making the Complex Simple**
    *   Decide and navigate through ambiguity, providing clear direction for everyone in the organization.
    *   Codify key processes, architectures, and strategic choices.
    *   Continuously improve how we run technical programs.
    *   Support teams in building the right things in the right ways.

### Qualifications

We are seeking candidates with the following qualifications:

*   Strong prior experience in a similar Technical Program Manager role within an agile development environment.
*   Demonstrated technical expertise to understand and navigate complex engineering challenges.
*   Exceptional project management skills, capable of orchestrating multi-team initiatives.
*   Outstanding interpersonal and communication skills to influence without authority and manage diverse stakeholders effectively.

### Author's Take

**1. What's the biggest challenge and most rewarding aspect of being a Technical Program Manager at Wolt?**

"The biggest challenge, hands down, is navigating the sheer pace and scope of our growth. We're a rocket ship, and with that comes constant ambiguity and the need to connect dots across rapidly evolving landscapes – from new markets to new product verticals. You're often building the airplane while flying it! But that's also where the immense reward lies. When you successfully untangle a complex, cross-functional initiative that directly contributes to our global expansion or significantly improves our engineering efficiency, the sense of accomplishment is profound. You're not just managing a project; you're actively shaping how a hyper-growth company operates at scale, and seeing your programs go live in dozens of countries is incredibly gratifying."

**2. How does Wolt support its Technical Program Managers in developing their skills and career, especially given the rapid pace?**

"Despite the fast pace, Wolt is surprisingly dedicated to individual growth. For TPMs, this often means being thrown into the deep end with significant ownership, which is the best classroom you can ask for. But it's not sink-or-swim. We have a culture of continuous learning and mentorship, often informal, where experienced leaders are genuinely invested in your success. There's a strong emphasis on documentation and codifying processes to share knowledge, and you're encouraged to proactively seek out learning opportunities, whether that's through internal workshops, external courses, or simply collaborating with our incredibly talented engineers and product managers. If you're proactive about your development, Wolt provides an unparalleled environment to accelerate your career and master the craft of program management in a high-impact setting."
