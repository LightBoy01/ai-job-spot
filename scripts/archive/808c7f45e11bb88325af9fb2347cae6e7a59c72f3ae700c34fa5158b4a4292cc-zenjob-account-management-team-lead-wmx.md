### Description\nZenjob is an AI-powered platform for flexible staffing solutions, efficiently connecting businesses and temporary workers since 2015. Companies use our platform to book readily available employees in real-time, while students use our app to find part-time jobs that perfectly fit their studies. Over 40,000 students and 1,000 partner companies in logistics, retail, and hospitality across all major German cities share our belief that good work is no coincidence; it's the result of innovative technology and genuine passion. Good work starts here.\n\nAs an Account Management Team Lead (m/f/x), you will lead and develop a team of Account Managers to achieve outstanding customer results and sustainable revenue growth. You will be responsible for team performance in terms of customer retention, expansion, satisfaction, and operational excellence, while driving consistent processes and data-driven decisions. You will collaborate cross-functionally to improve our customer lifecycle and ensure high-quality execution in a scalable manner.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Lead, coach, and develop a team of Account Managers with regular 1:1 meetings, performance reviews, and continuous feedback.\n- Set clear goals and workflows for your team; monitor KPIs and conduct pipeline, retention, and risk assessments.\n- Drive customer retention and expansion through structured QBRs, account plans, and proactive measures to minimize churn risk.\n- Ensure operational excellence and consistency in AM processes, documentation, and handovers.\n- Collaborate cross-functionally (e.g., Key Account Management, AM Operations, Finance, Product) to remove obstacles and improve tools and workflows.\n- Continuously standardize and improve AM best practices, playbooks, and content in our knowledge base.\n- Utilize data to prioritize, forecast, and identify growth opportunities or efficiency potentials.\n- Ensure compliance with company policies, data standards, and quality guidelines in all AM activities.\n- Recruit new team members, onboard them, and foster a high-performing, inclusive team culture.\n- Represent Account Management in cross-team initiatives and communicate progress and insights.\n- Your success will be measured by increasing Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), Customer Retention, and Fulfillment rates.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Experience leading or mentoring Account Managers or Customer Success Teams in a B2B context.\n- Strong commercial understanding in retention, expansion, and sales.\n- Demonstrable ability to implement processes, playbooks, and workflows at a team level.\n- Data-driven decision-making; familiarity with dashboards, KPIs, and forecasting.\n- Excellent communication, stakeholder management, and change leadership skills.\n- Ability to coach for performance, provide actionable feedback, and develop talent.\n- Familiarity with cross-functional collaboration and improving tools/processes with partners.\n- Excellent spoken and written communication skills, fluent in German and good English.\n\n*Desired:*\n- Experience with Lifecycle/CLM concepts, segmentation, and various sales techniques.\n- Background in temporary staffing, recruitment services, marketplace, or similar industries.\n- Familiarity with knowledge base management and activation content.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an experienced Account Management Team Lead to make a significant impact within an innovative, AI-powered platform.
