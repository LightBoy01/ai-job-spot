### Description
Founded just a year ago, acemate has already become Germany's leading AI learning platform for students and educators. Tens of thousands of learners and more than twenty renowned universities in four countries, including **TU Munich**, **University of Cologne**, and **WHU**, already rely on us.

**Our Mission:** We democratize access to personalized learning and enable educators to create individualized learning experiences at scale.
**Our Growth:** We have successfully completed a funding round, are growing rapidly, and are already expanding into new regions and markets.
**Our Culture:** We are a young, ambitious team passionately tackling the biggest challenges in education.

Our office is located in Berlin-Mitte. While we value in-person collaboration, we are realistic about commuting times and therefore offer flexible working options, including remote work.

As a **Growth Marketing Manager**, you will be responsible for our online presence on [**Instagram**](https://www.instagram.com/acemate.ai/) and **TikTok**. You will work directly with our CEO, Antong, and have the opportunity to represent our brand on social media, expanding our reach in a data-driven and creative way. This role offers you a unique insight into the early phase of a startup – including the chance to take on responsibility from day one.

### Responsibilities
Your key tasks will include:
*   Supporting the development of social media strategies based on performance data and insights.
*   Planning, producing, and publishing content (Reels, posts, and campaigns) on Instagram and TikTok – based on identified trends and your own format ideas.
*   Observing trends and conducting market analysis for the continuous development of our strategies and best practices.
*   Interacting with our community and active community management.
*   Supporting the strategic and operational management of influencer and ambassador campaigns.
*   Analyzing the performance of your content and deriving measures for optimization.

### Qualifications
We're looking for someone who:
*   Has a good feel for social media and spends far too much time on Instagram / TikTok.
*   Has a sense of humor that resonates with students.
*   Is creative and can inspire our target audience with exciting ideas.
*   Is comfortable in front of the camera and willing to be part of the content themselves.
*   Is numbers-savvy, interested in KPIs, and keen to derive real insights from data.
*   Is eager to work with a young team while taking on independent responsibility.
*   **Bonus:** You bring initial experience in social media marketing or content creation, have already built your own reach, or have experience with viral content.

Even if not all requirements apply to you, we still encourage you to apply.
