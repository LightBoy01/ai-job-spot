### Description
Media Automobile is a specialized performance marketing agency in the automotive sector, part of a larger group serving various industries. We provide full-service solutions to car dealerships, dealer groups, and brands, covering Performance Ads, Social Media, Recruiting Campaigns, and Content Production.

We are seeking a strong personality to manage our full-service clients, steer operational implementation, and provide expert leadership to our team, enabling our CEO to focus entirely on growth and sales. In this pivotal role, you will be directly responsible for ensuring our clients receive stable, professional, and high-quality service. Your mission is to build a robust, scalable fulfillment operation that runs efficiently, becoming the backbone of our service delivery.

### Responsibilities
*   **Client Management (Full-Service):**
    *   Maintain regular client touchpoints and status calls.
    *   Conduct monthly jour-fixe meetings for conceptual and strategic client consultation (e.g., using Mindmaps, Miro, strategic guidelines).
    *   Develop editorial plans and monthly posting roadmaps.
    *   Review and approve creatives, reels, and texts.
    *   Collaborate with the CEO on onboarding new clients.
*   **Team Steering & Quality Assurance:**
    *   Manage task allocation for content (video, reels) and performance marketing.
    *   Provide expert leadership to both our internal fulfillment team and external partners (e.g., videographers).
    *   Ensure deadlines, quality, and output standards are consistently met.
    *   Proactively identify and resolve problems and bottlenecks.
    *   For video productions: Conceptually develop video content (shotlist, topic structure) in collaboration with videographers and the team.
*   **Operational Social Media Expertise:**
    *   Identify relevant topics, hooks, and content ideas.
    *   Create content editorial plans and, if necessary, prepare texts.
    *   Ensure content is modern, trend-conscious, and highly relevant to target groups.
*   **System & Structure Development (First 6 Months):**
    *   Establish and refine SOPs, templates, and standard editorial plans.
    *   Develop clear workflows, roles, and handover processes.
    *   Continuously drive improvement through the adoption of new technologies, AI, and automation.
    *   **Core Goal:** Build a fulfillment "machine that runs without the CEO."

### Qualifications
*   Several years of experience working in an agency environment.
*   Proven experience as an Account Manager, Campaign Lead, or Content Lead.
*   Strong Social Media expertise (Meta, Reels, and current content trends), as well as SEO and SEA knowledge.
*   Confident and professional communication skills with clients.
*   Demonstrated ability to provide expert leadership to a team.
*   Highly structured work approach and an ownership mindset.
*   Nice to have: Experience in the automotive industry.
