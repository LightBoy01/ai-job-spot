### Description
univativ is a project and personnel service provider dedicated to connecting students and graduates with exciting career opportunities at renowned companies. Your career development is our mission and our passion.

We are seeking a diligent VAT Specialist for our client in the energy sector. In this pivotal role, you will operate at the intersection of tax law, operational processes, and IT systems. You will be instrumental in ensuring the tax-compliant implementation of energy industry billing processes and will actively contribute to the continuous development and optimization of existing procedures and systems. This is a chance to work in a modern environment with high relevance for the entire organization, offering a diverse range of tasks in tax law, IT, and process management. We also offer a 30-hour work week to ensure a healthy work-life balance.

If you possess a strong understanding of VAT law, enjoy analytical problem-solving, and have a passion for understanding and shaping complex interrelationships, we encourage you to apply.

### Responsibilities
*   Ensure the fulfillment of VAT requirements within energy industry billing processes and systems (SAP IS-U).
*   Perform VAT verifications in the sub-ledger.
*   Analyze and evaluate VAT-related facts and circumstances, then derive and implement necessary measures in collaboration with specialist departments and group companies.
*   Accompany system and process adjustments in projects to ensure compliance with VAT legal requirements.
*   Conduct analytical quality assurance checks of VAT processes and systems (SAP IS-U).

### Qualifications
*   Completed training as a tax specialist (Steuerfachangestellter) or a degree with a focus on tax law or tax studies.
*   Initial professional experience in a consulting firm or corporate tax department with a focus on VAT is required.
*   Experience in Tax Compliance and confident handling of tax audit processes.
*   Business-fluent German language skills are essential.
*   Experience with SAP (ideally with SAP IS-U) is desirable.
