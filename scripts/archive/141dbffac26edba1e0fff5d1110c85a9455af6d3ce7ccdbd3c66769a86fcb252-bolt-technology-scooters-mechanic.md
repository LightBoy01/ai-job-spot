### Description
Bolt is seeking a highly motivated and hands-on Scooters Mechanic to join our rapidly growing team. You will be instrumental in ensuring that our fleet of e-scooters is consistently maintained to the highest quality and safety standards, directly contributing to our mission of making cities for people, not cars.

At Bolt, we're one of Europe and Africa's fastest-growing tech companies, impacting over 150 million users across 45+ countries. We foster an inclusive environment where everyone is welcome, valuing diversity in all its forms.

In this role, you will quickly and efficiently identify and resolve damages, ensuring every ride is safe and reliable. Your keen eye for detail will be crucial as you perform thorough safety tests to guarantee vehicle roadworthiness, upholding our commitment to a top-notch transport experience.

**What We Offer:**
*   Play a direct role in shaping the future of mobility.
*   Work in fast-moving teams with some of the smartest people in the world.
*   Accelerate your professional growth with unique career opportunities.
*   Take advantage of Bolt credits to ride our scooter and use our ride-hailing services for free.
*   Enjoy various team events to get to know your colleagues.

### Responsibilities
As a Scooters Mechanic, your key responsibilities will include:
*   Ensuring the highest quality for our e-scooters by conducting general repair work and check-ups.
*   Inspecting e-scooters in use and identifying problems within the different parts of the product.
*   Testing e-scooters before releasing them to ensure their quality and safety standards.
*   Putting new e-scooters into service: unpacking, doing quality checks, adding necessary equipment, ensuring safety, and troubleshooting.
*   Assisting with warehouse operations, including inventory and battery management, shipments, and general housekeeping.

### Qualifications
We are looking for someone with:
*   A high level of attentiveness and accuracy in your work.
*   Knowledge and skills to operate various hand and power tools.
*   A proactive approach and willingness to take ownership of challenges, reflected in your hands-on work.
*   Experience in repairing mechanical devices, particularly in the automotive or electronic repair fields.
*   Solid local language skills and experience using smartphones.
*   English skills and a forklift license are considered an advantage.

Experience is highly valued, but what we truly seek is drive, intelligence, and integrity. We encourage you to apply even if you don't meet every single qualification!
