### Summary\nTimeCopilot is introduced as the first open-source agentic framework for time series forecasting. It uniquely combines multiple Time Series Foundation Models (TSFMs) with Large Language Models (LLMs) via a single unified API, automating the entire forecasting pipeline from feature analysis and model selection to cross-validation and forecast generation. The framework is LLM-agnostic, offers natural language explanations, supports direct queries about future predictions, and allows for ensembles across diverse forecasting families. Demonstrating state-of-the-art probabilistic forecasting performance at low cost on the large-scale GIFT-Eval benchmark, TimeCopilot provides a practical foundation for reproducible, explainable, and accessible agentic forecasting systems.\n\n### Why It Matters\nTimeCopilot represents a significant leap forward for AI professionals by showcasing the practical power of agentic AI in a specialized domain. This framework highlights a crucial evolution beyond standalone models: the orchestration of domain-specific foundation models (TSFMs) with the reasoning and interpretative capabilities of general-purpose LLMs. This hybrid approach not only automates complex analytical workflows but also democratizes advanced forecasting, making it accessible to a broader range of professionals beyond expert data scientists. For businesses, this translates into more efficient, accurate, and explainable predictions with reduced operational costs, enabling faster, data-driven decision-making. The emphasis on reproducibility and explainability is particularly vital, fostering trust and facilitating adoption in critical sectors where transparency is paramount. TimeCopilot signals a future where AI systems act as intelligent co-pilots, handling intricate technical tasks while providing human-understandable insights, thereby transforming how organizations leverage predictive analytics and accelerating the integration of sophisticated AI into mainstream operations.
