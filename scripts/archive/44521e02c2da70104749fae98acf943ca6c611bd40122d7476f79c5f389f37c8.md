What we’re looking for

*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
*   3–5 years of relevant experience as a Python Developer, with hands-on exposure to Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs).

*   Technical Skills:

*   Proficient in Python and its frameworks such as Django, Flask, and FastAPI.
*   Strong experience with Generative AI platforms including Azure OpenAI, Gemini, and Amazon Bedrock.
*   Familiarity with AI agent frameworks such as Autodev and Crew.
*   Solid understanding of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and vector databases.
*   Skilled in Python linting and formatting tools: Pylint, Flake8, Black, and MyPy.
*   Experienced with Python testing libraries: PyTest and Tox.
*   Proficient in version control systems, particularly Git.
*   Ability to integrate multiple data sources and databases into a unified system.
*   Understanding of Python’s multi-process architecture and threading limitations.
*   Working knowledge of additional programming languages such as PySpark and SQL.
*   Front-End & Visualization

*   Basic knowledge of front-end technologies including JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS3.
*   At least 1 year of experience building Power BI models and dashboards, with a strong grasp of DAX and visualization best practices.
