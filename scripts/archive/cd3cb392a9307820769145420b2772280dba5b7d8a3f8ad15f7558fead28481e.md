**About Lumen**

Lumen connects the world. We are igniting business growth by connecting people, data and applications – quickly, securely, and effortlessly. Together, we are building a culture and company from the people up – committed to teamwork, trust and transparency. People power progress.

We’re looking for top-tier talent and offer the flexibility you need to thrive and deliver lasting impact. Join us as we digitally connect the world and shape the future.

**The Role**
------------

 As a Lead Software Developer, you will take an active role in guiding teams toward successful project delivery while contributing to the architecture, design, and implementation of cutting-edge software solutions. This position involves increased leadership responsibilities alongside technical expertise.

**The Main Responsibilities**
-----------------------------

• Lead the design and development of complex software solutions using Java, NodeJS, VueJS, and SpringTools.

• Architect and implement modern APIs and MicroApps to support distributed systems.

• Oversee the implementation of CI/CD pipelines with GitHub, optimizing software development workflows.

• Ensure successful deployment and maintenance of cloud-based solutions, driving innovation across the organization.

• Champion DevSecOps practices, establishing robust security protocols and workflows.

• Mentor junior team members and foster a collaborative environment.

• Analyze network topology requirements to ensure seamless integration and scalability of software systems.

**What We Look For in a Candidate**
-----------------------------------

*   8+ years professional experience with a Bachelor's Degree or equivalent education and experience
*   6+ years professional experience with a Master's Degree or equivalent education and experience
*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field. A master’s degree is preferred.
    
*   5–8 years of professional experience in software development, including leading and managing complex projects.
    
*   Preferred Experience with NestJS and advanced cloud technologies.
    
*   Experience with languages: 5+ with Java and some exposure to Python and ability or desire to learn Go.
    
*   Knowledge of generative AI development for forward-thinking software solutions
    
*   Implement AIOps best practices, including CI/CD for model training, validation, deployment, and monitoring 
*   Proven leadership skills with the ability to manage multiple projects and deadlines effectively.
