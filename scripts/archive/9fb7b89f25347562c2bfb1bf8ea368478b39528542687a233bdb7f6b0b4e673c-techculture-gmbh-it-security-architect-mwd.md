### Description\nOur client is developing a highly critical IT platform, a project with enormous impact and a clear mission to ensure digital sovereignty at the highest level. To strengthen their team, they are looking for an IT-Security Architect (m/w/d) to take in-house responsibility for the development and implementation of security strategies, and to provide security consulting for internal projects. This role involves significant national relevance as you will be shaping the security architecture for Germany's sovereign cloud.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Provide security-focused advice for the implementation of internal IT projects and support the deployment of security measures and controls.\n- Process security-critical data from vulnerability and attack surface management to develop strategic decision templates and recommendations for the organization.\n- Support the analysis and remediation of security vulnerabilities.\n- Contribute to the design and implementation of information security policies and procedures.\n- Conduct awareness campaigns, develop, and deliver training sessions on security technologies and best practices.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed studies in (Business) Informatics, IT Security, or a comparable qualification.\n- Several years of professional experience in IT security or cyber defense.\n- Strong technical understanding in network security, endpoint hardening, or vulnerability management.\n- Profound knowledge of regulatory standards such as IT-Grundschutz, ISO 27001, or NIS-2.\n- Excellent communication and project management skills.\n- Fluent German and English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an experienced IT-Security Architect to join a mission-critical project with national significance, directly shaping the security architecture of Germany's sovereign cloud. The role offers substantial responsibility and a chance to make a tangible impact. The company fosters a supportive environment with ample opportunities for professional development and continuous learning. Furthermore, it emphasizes a healthy work-life balance through flexible working models, trust-based hours, and extensive home office options. Combined with attractive compensation and comprehensive benefits, this is a highly compelling role for a security professional looking to contribute to a secure digital future.
