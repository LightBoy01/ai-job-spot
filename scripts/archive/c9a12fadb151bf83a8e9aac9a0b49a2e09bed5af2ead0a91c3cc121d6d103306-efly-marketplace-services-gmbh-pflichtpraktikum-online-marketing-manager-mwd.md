### Description\nIn the role of **Marketing Manager**, you will calculate and manage the advertising budgets of our clients. The goal is to achieve the best possible visibility within a given budget. Regular evaluations are created to communicate work progress to the client. The positioning of products in desired advertising spaces also plays a significant role in this.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **SEA Strategy Development**: Together with our Account Managers, you will develop targeted SEA strategies and ensure that the full advertising potential on Amazon is leveraged.\n*   **Keyword Research & Analysis**: You will conduct comprehensive keyword research and handle on-page and off-page optimization to maximize product visibility.\n*   **Campaign Optimization**: Your goal is to optimally utilize and continuously improve the potential of campaigns.\n*   **Data-Driven Measures**: You will create analyses and derive concrete measures for the further development of campaigns – with the help of analytical tools, you will always keep an eye on success.\n*   **Reporting**: You will create regular reports to transparently present campaign results to our clients.\n*   **Amazon Seller Central**: You are proficient in using Amazon Seller Central and utilize it to manage advertising campaigns.\n*   **PPC Price Optimization**: You will optimize PPC prices based on predefined strategies and ensure that the budget is utilized optimally.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   **Studies**: You are an enrolled student.\n*   **Independent Work**: You are capable of working independently and managing projects responsibly.\n*   **Quick Comprehension**: You have the ability to quickly familiarize yourself with new topics and find solutions.\n*   **Teamwork & Communication Skills**: You enjoy working in a team and possess strong communication skills – both internally and in interaction with clients.\n*   **Analytical Understanding**: You love working with numbers and have a good understanding of analytical relationships.\n*   **Structured Communication**: You communicate clearly and structured – whether in writing or verbally.\n*   **Excel & PowerPoint**: You have very good knowledge of Excel and PowerPoint and know how to effectively use these tools to present results and evaluate data.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis **Entry-Level Marketing Manager** position offers a unique opportunity for an **enrolled student** to gain hands-on
