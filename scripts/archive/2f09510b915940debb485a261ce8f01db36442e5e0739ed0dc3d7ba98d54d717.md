**Why GMF Technology?**

GM Financial is set to change the auto finance industry and is leading the path of embarking on tech modernization – we have a startup mindset, and preserve our small company culture, in a public company environment with financial stability and intense growth over a decade-plus history. We are _data_ junkies and trust in _data_ and insights to advance our business objectives. We take our goal of zero emission, zero collision, zero congestion, and zero friction very seriously. We believe as an auto finance market leader we are in the driver's seat to lead us in the GM EV mission to change the world. We are building global platforms, in LATAM, Europe, China, U.S. and Canada – and we are looking to grow our high-performing team. GMF is comprised of over 10,000 team members globally. Join our fintech culture within a Blue-Chip company where we are changing the way we use technology to support our customers, dealers and business.

The Senior Salesforce Developer will be part of the IO (International Operations) business team providing business application solutions to enhance dealers' experience while doing business with GM Financial. Day-to-day activities will involve developing on the Salesforce.com platform, creating VisualForce Pages/Apex Classes and providing technical documentation. Responsibilities also include researching and implementing new technology solutions, not necessarily limited to the Salesfore.com platform. 

### Responsibilities

**Key Responsibilities:** 

*   Collaborate with internal teams to understand business needs and deliver smart, scalable solutions.
*   Build and enhance Salesforce applications using Apex triggers, classes, Flows, and scheduled jobs.
*   Support Service Cloud processes, including case management and user workflows.
*   Design and implement secure configurations, user permissions, and disaster recovery plans using Own to meet cybersecurity standards.
*   Develop and maintain API integrations—both inbound and outbound—with other enterprise systems.
*   Lead data synchronization efforts between Salesforce and external platforms.
*   Research and implement new technologies to improve system performance and user experience.
*   Champion Salesforce best practices and share knowledge across teams.
*   Facilitate roadmap planning sessions and lead solution design meetings with business and IT stakeholders.
    

### Qualifications

**Experience and Education:** 

*   Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science or related Engineering field; and/or commensurate experience preferred
*   5–7 years of hands-on Salesforce development experience required.
*   Experience with Apex, Lightning Web Components (LWC), Aura, and Salesforce integrations.
*   Familiarity with Service Cloud, Cases, Flows, and platform security (encryption, permissions, disaster recovery).
*   Strong understanding of API integrations (REST, OpenAPI, Event Catalog).
*   Experience with modern UI frameworks (Lightning, Experience Cloud, Angular, React).
*   Proficiency in object-oriented programming (Java, C#, Python, or TypeScript).
*   Knowledge of DevOps tools (Azure DevOps, Terraform, Jenkins) and source control (Git).
*   Exposure to AI tools (ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Einstein AI) and cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, GCP).
*   Test automation experience with tools like Selenium, Postman, or Cypress.
*   Bilingual in English and Spanish or Portuguese is highly preferred.
*   Industry experience in automotive, financial services, or other regulated environments is a plus.
