### Description

Are you ready to become part of Germany's largest motorcycle center? As a Motorcycle Service Advisor (m/f/d) at Motorrad Hintermeyer GmbH, you'll dive into the world of motorized passion. Our mission revolves around making our customers' mobility dreams a reality. You will be the primary point of contact for our customers, supporting our team with dedication and enthusiasm. With over 200 colleagues across the Limbächer Group, we offer a work environment characterized by innovation, team spirit, and trust. We are committed to growth, honesty, and fairness, and we look forward to realizing motorcycle dreams together with you. If you have an affinity for motorcycles and enjoy customer interaction, you've come to the right place. Embrace the challenge and become part of our community – where courage and reliability count.

### Responsibilities

*   Provide competent consultation and support to our service customers, from appointment scheduling and vehicle intake to handover.
*   Independently prepare and explain service and repair offers, including cost and performance overviews.
*   Plan, coordinate, and monitor workshop orders in collaboration with workshop management and technicians.
*   Conduct vehicle inspections and handovers, including visual checks, documentation, and customer consultation.
*   Ensure high customer satisfaction through transparent communication and proactive complaint management.
*   Responsibly process warranty and goodwill claims, as well as complaints, according to manufacturer specifications.
*   Actively sell service packages, accessories, and additional products to enhance customer satisfaction and workshop utilization.
*   Provide technical advice and develop individual maintenance and repair recommendations based on vehicle condition and customer needs.
*   Assure the quality of completed work and approve vehicles after their workshop run-through.
*   Cultivate and expand long-term customer relationships through professional service and proactive customer engagement.

### Qualifications

To excel in this role, you should bring:

*   A completed commercial and/or technical apprenticeship, or relevant experience in sales (experience is desirable but not strictly required).
*   Career changers are explicitly welcome.
*   A friendly and approachable demeanor with a professional appearance.
*   A strong sense of responsibility, reliability, sociability, and excellent communication skills.
*   Enjoyment in customer interaction and sales.
*   A meticulous and precise working style.
*   A genuine passion for motorcycles.
*   A Class A and B driving license is advantageous.

**What We Offer:**

*   A secure and collegial working environment.
*   Interesting, independent, and varied tasks.
*   Structured salary levels and a clear 5-year plan with advancement prospects.
*   A strong team and an employer with a vision.
*   Employee participation in the form of an annual bonus.
*   Company vehicles (car, e-bike, motorcycle).
*   Numerous opportunities for further training and development.
*   Subsidized lunch, free fruits, and beverages.
*   Employer contributions to asset-building schemes (VWL) and pension.
*   Regular employee events and anniversary bonuses.
*   Support with apartment hunting and relocation.
