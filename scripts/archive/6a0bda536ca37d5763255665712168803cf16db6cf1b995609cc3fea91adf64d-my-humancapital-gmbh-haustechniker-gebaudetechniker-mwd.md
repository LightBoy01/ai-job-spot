### Description
Shape tomorrow's healthcare in a secure work environment filled with purpose, stability, and room for initiative. We are seeking a dedicated Building Services Technician / Facilities Technician (m/f/d) on behalf of our partner company in Munich, to start as soon as possible. This pivotal role involves the comprehensive management of our technical building infrastructure and supporting general facility operations, ensuring a seamless and productive environment for all.

### Responsibilities
*   Oversee, manage, and monitor the technical building equipment, including meticulous documentation.
*   Execute internal relocations, minor alterations, and conduct maintenance work on building technology systems.
*   Steer and coordinate external craft firms, supervise maintenance and renovation projects, and support technical acceptances.
*   Prepare and tidy meeting rooms, operate event technology, and provide support for various events.
*   Perform winter services, manage waste disposal, handle driving services, and undertake administrative tasks within fleet management, such as vehicle maintenance and tire changes.

### Qualifications
*   Hold a completed vocational qualification as a Plant Mechanic (Sanitary, Heating, Air Conditioning), Electrician, Building Services Technician, Caretaker, or possess a comparable technical qualification.
*   Ideally possess professional experience in building services, facility management, or maintenance.
*   Maintain a valid Class B driver's license.
*   Demonstrate strong teamwork abilities, excellent communication skills, a service-oriented mindset, and an independent, structured approach to work.
*   Possess proficient German language skills.

### Author's Take
*   **Q: How does a Building Services Technician really make a difference here, especially with the company's mission to 'shape tomorrow's healthcare'?**
    *   **A:** This role is absolutely central, even if it's not directly patient-facing. Imagine a critical meeting where innovations are discussed, or an event where important decisions are made – our spaces need to be perfectly functional, comfortable, and safe. You ensure the technical backbone of our entire operation runs flawlessly, from climate control to power, ensuring our teams can focus on their vital work without interruption. By keeping our facilities in top shape, you're directly enabling the environment where groundbreaking ideas for healthcare are born and implemented. It's about providing the stability and seamless operation that allows our experts to innovate. Your keen eye for detail in maintenance and coordination of external firms helps us avoid disruptions that could impact our mission.

    *   **A:** Absolutely. Here, 'appreciative' isn't just a buzzword. It means you're genuinely seen as a vital part of the team, not just a pair of hands. You'll experience a real 'Du-Kultur,' meaning we're on a first-name basis across all levels, fostering a very direct and open atmosphere. Management trusts your expertise to handle issues autonomously. For instance, when coordinating external firms or tackling a complex repair, you're given the space to make decisions. Your suggestions for improvements are welcomed, not just tolerated. This trust, combined with our flexible working hours and focus on work-life balance, creates an environment where you feel respected, supported, and truly empowered to contribute your best, knowing your efforts are recognized and valued. We value proactive problem-solving and empower you to take ownership.
