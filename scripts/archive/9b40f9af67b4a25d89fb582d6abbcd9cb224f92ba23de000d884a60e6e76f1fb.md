About the role
--------------
The **Supply Chain Domain Architect** plays a pivotal role within our **global architecture team**, which drives **technology decision-making globally** across the enterprise. You will provide strategic direction and architectural leadership across supply chain transformation, modernization, planning, and analytics.
This role oversees a team of architects focused on manufacturing and planning, ensuring that technology decisions are aligned with business goals and are executed with a **real mandate to bring change**.
You will be instrumental in connecting business and IT, translating strategic supply chain objectives into scalable, resilient, and data-driven architecture. A **strong collaborator and connector with business stakeholders**, you will guide enterprise-wide consistency and innovation across the supply chain technology landscape to support agility, resilience, and digital competitiveness.
Key responsibilities
--------------------
*   Define and evolve the end-to-end supply chain architecture vision, principles, and reference models.
*   Design scalable, resilient, and data-driven architectures across planning, manufacturing, logistics, and analytics domains.
*   Lead a team of architects, providing strategic guidance and ensuring alignment with enterprise goals.
*   Drive the modernization of legacy supply chain platforms (e.g., SAP, Oracle, Kinaxis, o9) into future-ready solutions.
*   Collaborate closely with business stakeholders to translate strategy into actionable architecture.
*   Champion enterprise architecture governance, ensuring consistency and quality across initiatives.
*   Represent supply chain architecture in global forums and contribute to enterprise-wide decision-making.
*   Build trusted relationships with senior leaders in IT and the business to drive alignment and adoption.
*   Apply strong analytical and problem-solving skills to navigate complex architectural challenges.
*   Ensure architectural coherence and integration across global business units and IT delivery teams.
*   Promote the use of emerging technologies such as AI/ML, digital twins, and advanced analytics.
*   Operate with a clear mandate to drive real change, turning strategic vision into business impact.
Job requirements
----------------
*   Bachelor\`s or Master\`s Degree in a relevant field.
*   8+ years of experience in a senior architecture role, ideally within a global matrix organization.
*   Deep knowledge of supply chain domains, particularly in planning (S&OP, demand/supply, capacity), manufacturing, procurement, logistics, and analytics.
*   Proven experience in designing end-to-end architectures and leading complex transformation initiatives.
*   Experience working with SAP and familiarity with platforms like Oracle, Kinaxis, or o9 is a plus.
*   Excellent stakeholder management skills — able to build strong relationships and align diverse business and IT teams.
*   Strategic and hands-on approach to modernizing legacy systems and shaping digital, future-ready supply chains.
*   Fluent in English, both written and spoken, with strong communication and influencing skills.
