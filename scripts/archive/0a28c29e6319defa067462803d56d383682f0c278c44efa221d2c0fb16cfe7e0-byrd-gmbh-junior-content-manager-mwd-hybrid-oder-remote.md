### Description
Are you eager to take on responsibility, actively shape processes, and be part of a team that grows and learns every day? As a Junior Content Manager at Byrd Agency, you will be the vital link between our Creative and Catalog teams. Your core mission will be to ensure that our clients' content is consistently compelling, up-to-date, and brand-aligned across all platforms, primarily Amazon.

You'll be instrumental in monitoring brand product portfolios, optimizing workflows, and contributing your own innovative ideas to enhance our efficiency and impact. This role offers a unique opportunity to learn the intricacies of building successful brands on Amazon, from initial product concept to flawless presentation. You'll work with exciting clients and diverse product categories from around the globe, taking on significant responsibility early on. We're committed to your professional and personal development, and you'll play an active role in shaping our agency's growth.

If you thrive in an international environment where initiative is highly valued, team spirit is paramount, and you can truly make a difference, then this is the perfect place for you.

### Responsibilities
*   Serve as the primary interface between our Creative and Catalog teams, ensuring seamless content integration and flow.
*   Monitor and manage our clients' product portfolios, guaranteeing that product pages are always current, complete, and perfectly aligned with brand guidelines.
*   Support the entire lifecycle of Amazon content, including the conception, maintenance, and revision of product listings and brand presences, from A+ Content to Brand Stores.
*   Assist with project coordination and client communication, ensuring tasks are completed efficiently and deadlines are met without a hitch.
*   Actively participate in the optimization of internal processes and workflows, contributing to more efficient cross-departmental operations.
*   Independently conduct product research and market analyses, integrating your valuable insights into our overall content strategy.
*   Identify opportunities for improvement, develop and propose your own ideas, and contribute directly to the ongoing development of our team and the agency.

### Qualifications
*   Completed degree in Economics, Marketing, Communication, or a comparable educational background.
*   Excellent proficiency in both written and spoken German and English.
*   A structured, analytical, and solution-oriented approach to work; you are proactive and bring fresh ideas to the table.
*   Strong organizational skills and a genuine enthusiasm for taking on responsibility.
*   Strategic thinking capabilities and the ability to view challenges and opportunities holistically.
*   Keen interest in optimizing processes and workflows, alongside a desire to contribute to the development of internal structures.
*   High degree of self-initiative, a collaborative team spirit, and a strong desire to learn and grow rapidly within an international setting.
