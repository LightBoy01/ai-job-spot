**About the Role**  
As the Seller Onboarding Manager, you will be the owner of the onboarding experience for all new sellers joining OnBuy. This is not just a support role it’s a business-critical function that connects seller experience to commercial outcomes. You will work cross-functionally to identify friction, drive improvements, and deliver scalable solutions that accelerate seller success and platform growth. Success means demonstrating the ability to connect operational execution with strategic impact, an essential trait for OnBuy.

The ideal candidate will be a cross-functional collaborator, passionate about enabling sellers to thrive on our platform.

**Key responsibilities**

*   Seller Advocacy & Strategic Ownership
*   Act as the internal advocate for new sellers, surfacing feedback and pain points to improve the onboarding experience.
*   Support the Head of Sales in managing the onboarding funnel from activation through to retention.
*   Collaborate with Product and Tech teams to influence roadmap priorities and resolve systemic onboarding blockers.
*   Recommend tooling, automation, and product enhancements that improve onboarding efficiency and seller satisfaction.

_Onboarding Process Management_

Ensure timely completion of all critical onboarding steps, including:

*   Account verification
*   Integration method selection (Manual, ERP/API, CSV)
*   International listing setup
*   Shipping rate validation
*   Product data quality checks
*   Price competitiveness
*   Boost and package education
*   Brand registration
*   Document onboarding progress in Salesforce and create educational content to proactively prevent common seller errors.

In_tegration & Product Activation_

*   Incentivise sellers to list best-selling SKUs immediately post-activation.
*   Provide tailored support for ERP/API and CSV integrations, including documentation and troubleshooting.
*   Collaborate with B2C Marketing to ensure SKU activation within the first month of onboarding.

_Retention & Seller Satisfaction_

*   Design onboarding engagements that promote long-term seller retention.
*   Partner with Sales, Support, and Product teams to proactively address early churn signals.
*   Maintain a 90% satisfaction rate among strategic sellers through personalised support and feedback-driven improvements.
*   Implement and manage onboarding satisfaction surveys in collaboration with B2B Marketing.

_Scalable Onboarding & Automation_

*   Balance high-touch onboarding for strategic sellers with scalable processes for the broader seller base.
*   Leverage automation and AI tools to reduce manual effort while preserving personalisation.
*   Continuously refine onboarding workflows based on performance data, seller feedback, and internal insights.  
    

_Performance Monitoring & Reporting_

*     Monitor onboarding KPIs and integration milestones to track progress and identify areas for improvement.
*     Identify trends in delays or drop-offs and implement root-cause solutions.
*     Provide regular updates to leadership on seller progress, onboarding performance, and program effectiveness.
