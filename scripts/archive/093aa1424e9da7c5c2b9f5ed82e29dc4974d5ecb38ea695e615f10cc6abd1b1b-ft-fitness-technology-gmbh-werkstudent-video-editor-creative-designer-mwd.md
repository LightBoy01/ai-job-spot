### Description

Pullup & Dip is an innovative e-commerce company that develops, produces, and distributes high-quality accessories for bodyweight training (Calisthenics) under its 'Pullup & Dip' brand, and training modules and accessories for climbers under 'CLEVO Climbing'. Our products are utilized by athletes in over 70 countries worldwide.

We are currently looking for a motivated **Video Editor / Creative Designer (m/w/d)** to join our Marketing Team as a **Working Student**.

### Responsibilities

*   Conceive and create engaging video content for promoting our brands and products on popular social networks (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and TikTok).
*   Conduct visual research and collaboratively develop ideas within the team for creative image and video formats (including YouTube videos, Meta Ads, organic Reels, and product videos).
*   Edit and produce high-quality, performance-oriented videos using industry-standard image and video editing tools.
*   Monitor trends and developments in social media to stay current and apply best practices.

### Qualifications

*   You have initial experience in video editing.
*   You are currently pursuing a degree with a focus on Design, Marketing, Media Management, or a similar field.
*   You are proficient in DaVinci Resolve functions such as video editing, color grading, and audio editing to produce visually appealing videos.
*   You possess reliability, precision, high-quality standards, and an affinity for digital media.
*   You have a creative mindset and a good sense of aesthetics and trends.
*   You are a native German speaker and have very good English skills, both written and spoken.
*   **Bonus**: You have basic videographer skills (framing, lighting, sound, etc.) and can operate a compact cine-camera.

If you're a German-speaking student with a keen eye for aesthetics, DaVinci Resolve proficiency, and a desire to see your creative work make a tangible global impact, this role offers an 'attractive compensation' and a secure workplace to kickstart your career in digital media and marketing.
