### Description
This role offers an exciting opportunity to support our Human Resources administration in daily operations. You will be a central point of contact for employee concerns and play a vital part in ensuring smooth HR processes from onboarding to ongoing support. Join a team that values your contributions and offers significant opportunities for professional growth.

Here’s what you can look forward to:

*   **Holiday and Christmas Bonus**: After one year with the company, you will receive additional holiday and Christmas bonuses.
*   **€5 Voucher for momoxfashion.com**: Your wardrobe won't miss out either: every month you'll receive a €5 voucher for momoxfashion.com to treat yourself to new outfits.
*   **Half Day Off for Your Birthday**: On your birthday, you get half a day off – so you can enjoy your day relaxed.
*   **Company Parties**: We celebrate with the entire team at a big summer party and, of course, a Christmas party.
*   **Fresh Fruit**: We regularly provide you with fresh fruit – for a healthy snack during work.
*   **Sports and Fitness Offers**: Every year we participate in various sports events, such as the Leipzig Company Run, Football Cup, Table Tennis Cup, or the LVZ Cycling Festival.
*   **Development**: Seize the opportunity to develop professionally – whether as a specialist or a manager. Together, we'll discover your strengths and discuss your development opportunities with you.
*   **Further Training**: momox is only as good as you are. That's why we provide space and budget for your individual professional development with language courses, internal training, conferences, (online) courses, and more.
*   **Accessibility**: Our location is conveniently reachable in less than 12 minutes. By S-Bahn via Leipzig Messe Bhf. (RE13, S2, S5, S5X) or by bus lines 86, 84, and 82 (stops: Messeverwaltung, Am alten Flughafen). Tram line 16 also brings you to us.
*   **Sustainability**: We love second-hand and integrate our sustainable business idea into daily office life with simple methods such as sustainable consumables, double-sided printing, energy-saving mode on devices, waste separation, etc.

### Responsibilities
*   Support our HR administration in daily operations, including maintaining and creating personnel files, as well as the initial processing of incoming HR-relevant inquiries in DATEV.
*   Organize and supervise HR consultation hours, serving as the central point of contact for employee concerns.
*   Prepare all relevant hiring documents within the onboarding process.
*   As needed, support adjacent teams in other areas of the HR department.
*   Collaborate on internal projects and are encouraged to contribute your ideas.

### Qualifications
*   Successfully completed a commercial apprenticeship or a comparable qualification.
*   Initial experience in human resources, ideally in HR administration.
*   A careful and conscientious working style – you pay attention to details and handle documents and sensitive data responsibly.
*   An independent, reliable working style and a high service orientation, without losing sight of clear processes.
*   Very good German language skills, both written and spoken; additional languages for our multicultural location are definitely a plus.

### Author's Take
This role offers an excellent opportunity for individuals with initial HR experience to delve into operational HR administration within a dynamic and supportive environment. The company clearly values employee well-being, professional growth, and sustainability.
