**Job Description:**

**Why this role is important to us**

Operational Risk is central to how we protect our business, our people, and our customers. You will sit within our Group Risk team and play a part in shaping the strategy and future direction of Operational Risk globally. You'll help embed risk practices, promote innovation, and ensure our frameworks are fit for purpose.

This role is a hybrid role based in Nottingham or London reporting into the Global Head of Operational Risk.

**Work that matters – What you'll do**

*   Lead the development and evolution of our Operational Risk strategy and frameworks
*   Oversee and maintain Group Operational Risk Policies and Standards (e.g., RCSA, Loss Events, Issues Management)
*   Partner with global teams to embed risk practices and escalate concerns
*   Promote innovation through the use of AI and new technologies to enhance risk reporting and insights
*   Lead training and awareness programs to build risk capability across the organisation
*   Collaborate with the Archer Centre of Excellence to enhance risk tooling and systems
*   Produce high-quality executive-level risk reports and updates for senior leadership
*   Ensure quality assurance and governance across all Operational Risk data

**Qualifications:**

**What you'll need to bring to the role & Experian**

*   Experience in Operational Risk within a second line of defence capacity
*   Experience building, evolving, and operating industry-standard Risk Management Frameworks
*   Stakeholder engagement skills advising at senior levels
*   Communicational ability to simplify complex data
*   A practical and balanced approach to applying technical risk knowledge
*   A collaborative continuous improvement mindset
*   A understanding of technology, systems, and the opportunities AI presents
*   Experience working across cultures and global teams

**More about you**

*   You're a strategic thinker who excels in a matrix organisation
*   You're passionate about risk, technology, and driving meaningful change
*   You bring curiosity, innovation, and a continuous improvement mindset
*   You're ready to shape the future of Operational Risk, not just manage it
