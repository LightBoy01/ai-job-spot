**Senior Geology Technician**

Ready to pursue your professional journey with Northern Star? As an ASX 50 global-scale gold miner, we have sizeable operations in Western Australia and Alaska. With unparalleled pathways for advancement and avenues for personal growth, we stand as Australia’s premier gold employer. Your journey starts here.

At Northern Star, we live by our STARR Core Values of Safety, Teamwork, Accountability, Respect and Results. These Values are not just words on paper; they are the cornerstone of our Company culture and guide everything we do. You will play a pivotal role in upholding these Values and contributing to our collective success.

We are seeking an experienced **Senior Geology Technician** to join our **KCGM Operations.**  This **residential** role is on an **8/6** **roster** working **11-hour** days.

**Here’s what awaits you:**

*   Assist in the supervision & leadership of a fully functional core processing facility.
*   Complete regular audits and inspections to ensure maintenance of mobile and fixed plant.
*   Assist with the development and maintenance of relevant Standard Operating Procedures.
*   Provide in-house training and ongoing mentoring of Geology Technicians.
*   Process, sample, and collect data from diamond drill core.
*   Sample and collect data from Reverse Circulation, Blast Hole, Rotary Air Blast, Aircore and auger drillholes.
*   Ensure timely and accurate sample dispatch to assay laboratories.
*   Coordinate daily activities, liaising with mining and geology teams.
*   Develop strong working relationships with members of the Operations, Growth and Discovery Geology teams.
*   Hands-on role with approximately 60% of the role requiring direct leadership with your team.

**To thrive in this role, you’ll need:**

**Experience:**

*   Minimum **3** years' experience in a field assistant/technician role
*   Computer literacy with the ability to use Microsoft office products.
*   Excellent numeracy skills.
*   Good communication skills, written and verbal.
*   Strong attention to detail, ability to plan daily activities and meet deadlines.
*   Leadership skills
*   Flexibility to adapt to improved procedures and practices.

**Qualifications**: _essential_

*   Current "C" Class Western Australian Driver's Licence
*   Current approved and recognised forklift ticket.
*   Current First Aid HLTAID011

**Qualifications**: _desirable_

*   Current “LR” Class Western Australian Driver's Licence
*   Certificate IV WHS – BSB41419
*   WHS Statutory Appointment
