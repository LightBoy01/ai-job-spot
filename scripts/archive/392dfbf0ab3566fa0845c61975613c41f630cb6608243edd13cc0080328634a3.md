Solution & Security Consultant

  

Patsnap is a global, pre-IPO company that transforms the way organizations harness their Intellectual Property and Research & Development productivity. Our platform revolutionizes how IP and R&D teams collaborate across the entire innovation lifecycle, using domain-specific AI to accelerate the creation of market-ready products.

  

Our products span global patent databases, scientific journals, technical literature, and custom enterprise solutions. By combining cutting-edge AI, deep semantic search, advanced analytics, and workflow automation, our platform empowers R&D, innovation, and IP teams across industries like engineering, life sciences, technology, and finance.

  

With over 12,000 customers worldwide, including some of the biggest names in innovation, Patsnap is at the forefront of technological advancement. Our $300M Series E funding round brings our valuation to a $1 billion unicorn status, and we still have a remarkable amount of growth ahead.

  

We have a vibrant and diverse team with offices in Singapore, Toronto, London, and Shanghai. Our hyper-growth trajectory is powered by our people, and we are extremely proud of our company-wide vision, work ethic, and entrepreneurial spirit. We are committed to fostering an inclusive environment where talent thrives and ideas bloom. 

  

**Role Summary:**

  

As a Solution & Security Consultant at Patsnap, you’ll be the bridge between our product and our buyers by building lightweight demos, presenting compelling stories that win trust, and translating security choices into plain language. If you love crafting decks, running live demos, and partnering with sales to close deals, this role is for you!

  

_This is a hybrid position in our Downtown Toronto office with the expectation to work from the office once a week._

**What You'll be Doing:**  
*   Lead discovery calls to capture problems, users, data sources, and success criteria.
*   Build demos and proof-of-concepts (e.g., RAG, small agents, lightweight automations) and tailor them to the customer's workflow.
*   Lead crisp demos and executive-friendly walkthroughs; craft short narratives, handle Q&A, and land clear value.
*   Maintain client security & trust. Map data flows, access patterns, and retention; support customer security questionnaires and evidence gathering.
*   Partner with sales by working with Account Executives on win plans, next steps, and follow-ups; keep notes and dates tight.
*   Produce one-pagers, FAQs, and simple diagrams others can reuse.
*   Translate customer needs into tickets and coordinate with product/engineering.
  
  
**About You: (We’re hiring for potential and learning velocity - no need to tick every box)**  
*   0-4 years of work experience. New grads are welcome.
*   Computer Science degree or equivalent is preferred.
*   Presentation confidence. You’re clear, concise, and engaging with slides and live demos.
*   AI curiosity. Comfortable discussing embeddings, prompting, and the idea behind RAG (or eager to learn fast).
*   Technical fundamentals. Familiar with HTTP/JSON APIs, Git basics, and reading logs; can write (or want to learn) small scripts in Python or JavaScript.
*   Security mindset. Understand security and architecture fundamentals and concepts
*   Strong writing. You can turn complexity into crisp docs.
*   Domain interest. Some understanding of IP/patents is a plus.
  
  
**Nice to Haves:**  
*   Hands-on with LangChain/LlamaIndex, OpenAI or local LLMs, and a vector store (e.g., pgvector).
*   Cloud basics (AWS or similar).
*   Diagramming in Mermaid or draw.io
*   Familiarity with SOC 2 / ISO 27001 concepts or the shared responsibility model.
*   Patent/literature search exposure.
*   Customer-facing internships (support, tutoring, teaching assistant, sales/SE).
