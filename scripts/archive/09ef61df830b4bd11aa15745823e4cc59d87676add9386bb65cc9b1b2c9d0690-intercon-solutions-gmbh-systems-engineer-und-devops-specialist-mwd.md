### Description
Are you passionate about developing and operating modern IT infrastructure? Do you envision a system role where operations and automation complement each other effectively, and where you can truly shape and innovate rather than just process tickets? If so, this role is for you.

We are an established software and technology company that has been developing solutions for industrial SMEs for decades. Our product landscape includes ERP web applications, platform services, and our own private cloud. We are looking for you to join our System Operations and DevOps team to further develop our systems. In this diverse role, you will combine administration, automation, observability, and technical consulting in a modern environment.

### Responsibilities
*   Further development, operation, and automation of our system landscapes.
*   Support for Unix, Linux, and Windows systems.
*   Participation in the development and maintenance of our private cloud.
*   Setup, maintenance, and optimization of monitoring and logging solutions (e.g., Prometheus and Grafana).
*   Automation of deployments and build processes (e.g., with Jenkins).
*   Maintenance and optimization of databases like PostgreSQL.
*   Technical support for complex system questions internally and in customer projects.
*   Contribution to modern DevOps processes such as CI/CD and infrastructure automation.
*   Involvement in future topics like AI-supported system optimization.

### Qualifications
**Your Technical Profile:**
*   Completed IT education or university degree.
*   Experience in the administration of Unix/Linux systems, ideally complemented by Windows Server knowledge.
*   Basic knowledge of networking, virtualization, and firewalls.
*   Experience in at least one area such as monitoring, CI/CD, databases, or scripting.
*   Willingness to learn new technologies such as containerization or AI automation.

**Your Personal Profile:**
*   High willingness to learn and develop.
*   Structured and reliable working style.
*   Enjoyment of technical problem-solving.
*   Clear communication and team orientation.
*   Low travel readiness, as the activity is predominantly internal or remote.
