Overview:

**Mid Data Scientist All-Purpose SME**  

**Clearance Required:** **Active TS/SCI**

**Location: Springfield, VA or St Louis, MO**

**Experience: 5 years of experience focusing on the concepts and applications of data analytics, computer science and programming, data modeling, Artificial intelligence and machine learning.**

**Travel: 25 - 30%**

**_As a Mid Data Scientist All Purpose SME_**  – join S2 Analytical Solutions and the customer to facilitate the mission of national security and humanitarian leadership through working to develop GEOINT skillsets at NGA's college.

**Job Overview:**

Instructor position that will support course delivery, course maintenance, and provide technical expertise to the development of new learning solutions associated with the assigned College Branch’s focus areas.  The position will instruct the NGC GEOINT Curriculum via multiple delivery methods (classroom, blended, synchronous/asynchronous online, video, and/or performance support).

Responsibilities:

### Duties:

*   A Mid-level Data Science SME shall be able to build upon the knowledge taught at the introductory GEOINT Tradecraft learning level (1000), in order to effectively handle fundamental (2000) GEOINT Tradecraft learning level tasks.
*   At this level the SME shall have a thorough understanding of the concepts and skills related to Data Science and can apply these skills in routine and none routine situations.
*   They shall be able to apply tradecraft skills through problem-based exercises culminating in exams.
*   They shall demonstrate solid Data Science tradecraft skills and an understanding of how the Data Science tradecraft contributes to addressing key intelligence questions.
*   A Mid-Level Data Science SME is primarily focused on providing support to Fundamental (2000) GEOINT Tradecraft Learning Level courses, with a secondary focus on Intermediate (3000) GEONT Tradecraft Learning Level courses, both levels within the Data Science content area.

Qualifications:

### Required Experience:

*   Minimum 5 years of experience focusing on the concepts and applications of data analytics, computer science and programming, data modeling, Artificial intelligence and machine learning.

### Desired Education:

*   A bachelor’s degree from an accredited (i.e., regional, national, or institutional) academic institution, related to their assigned area of expertise (i.e., Computational Thinking, Programming, Statistics, or Data Visualization).
*   Department of Defense (DoD) Basic Instructor Certification
