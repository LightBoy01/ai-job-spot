### Description
We are seeking a dedicated and experienced **Bankkaufmann / Customer Advisor (m/w/d)** for our partner company located in the vibrant heart of Munich. This is a long-term, permanent employment opportunity where your expertise will be highly valued. You will join a modern work environment that champions work-life balance through flexible working hours and the option for home office. The company fosters an appreciative work culture, characterized by a direct 'Du-Kultur' (informal address) and a strong sense of trust from leadership, providing a supportive backdrop for your professional growth and success.

### Responsibilities
*   Advise and support private and corporate clients comprehensively on all aspects of financing, investment, provision, and payment transactions.
*   Identify individual customer needs and develop tailored financial solutions to build and strengthen long-term customer relationships.
*   Conduct account consultations, investment discussions, and credit applications, guiding your clients competently through all phases of banking business.
*   Actively participate in customer acquisition, existing customer care, and sales promotions to achieve ambitious sales targets.
*   Contribute your expertise to digitalization initiatives and process optimizations within customer service to ensure modern and efficient operational workflows.

### Qualifications
*   Completed training as a Bankkaufmann (m/w/d), Finanzassistent (m/w/d), Sparkassenkaufmann (m/w/d), or a comparable qualification in the financial sector.
*   Profound knowledge in private customer business, financial consulting, credit, and investment products.
*   Ideally, proven experience in a customer advisory role within a bank or Sparkasse.
*   Confident and friendly demeanor, an pronounced service orientation, and enjoyment in interacting with people.
*   Excellent German language skills.

### Author's Take
**Q1: How does the team foster innovation, particularly in areas like digitalization and process optimization, and what opportunities would I have to genuinely contribute to these advancements?**

This isn't a bank resting on its laurels; it's actively seeking to evolve. Your insights into process optimization and digitalization won't just be heard, they'll be actively sought. Imagine suggesting a new digital tool for client interaction that gets implemented, or streamlining a credit application process that significantly improves customer experience. The leadership here genuinely believes in empowering its teams to drive change, seeing your daily interactions with clients as a valuable source of practical innovation. This isn't a place where ideas get lost in bureaucracy; it's where practical innovation thrives, directly impacting how customers experience banking in the digital age.

**Q2: The job description mentions a 'wertschätzende Arbeitskultur' and 'Weiterentwicklung'. Could you elaborate on how these are lived daily and what specific development paths are typical for a Customer Advisor here?**

The 'Du-Kultur' isn't just a formality here; it's a reflection of genuine mutual respect. You'll find a flat hierarchy where leadership trusts you to manage your client relationships and take initiative. Feedback is constructive, and support is readily available, especially when navigating complex client cases or new financial products. For your development, think beyond just courses: they actively encourage shadowing senior colleagues, taking on specialized client portfolios, or even participating in internal working groups focused on specific market trends like sustainable finance. The goal is not just to keep you up-to-date, but to empower you to become a specialist in areas you're passionate about, whether it's wealth management or specific corporate financing solutions, ensuring your career grows alongside the company's vision.
