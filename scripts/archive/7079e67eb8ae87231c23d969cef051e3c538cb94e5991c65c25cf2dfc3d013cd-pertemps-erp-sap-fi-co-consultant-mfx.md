### Description
Our client, a world and industry-leading Biotech company in Germany, is embarking on a pivotal greenfield SAP S/4HANA implementation project in Berlin. They are seeking experienced and passionate SAP FICO Consultants to play a crucial role in this transformation. This B2B contract opportunity, ranging from 6 to 12+ months, requires deep knowledge of Treasury & Cash Management, proficiency in configuration and user functionalities, and the ability to communicate effectively at all levels, including Board and C-level, in both business-level German and English.

### Responsibilities
*   Requirement analysis & definition
*   Design & Configuration
*   Coaching and mentoring

### Qualifications
*   Over 10 years of SAP Finance Implementation and S/4HANA FICO module roll-out experience.
*   Strong understanding of integration with other SAP modules.
*   Comprehensive overview of all sub-modules within TR/CM/GL/AP/AR/Asset Management.
*   Demonstrated ability for cross-department collaboration.
*   Proficiency in executing necessary configuration changes.
*   Adept at discussing and understanding new requirements raised by Business users.
*   Experience in preparing end-user manuals.
