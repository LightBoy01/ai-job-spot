**Multimedia Content Engineer – AI & UX**
Ground-breaking products deserve to be accompanied by world-class technical information. Dell Technologies customers expect nothing less. That’s where our Content Engineering team comes in. Working side-by-side with editors and illustrators, subject matter experts and stakeholders, the team creates clear, concise and easy-to-understand content for multiple channels. It takes an ability to research, analyze and synthesize complex subject matter. And it’s all underpinned by an understanding of how our technologies work and how they benefit customers. As a Multimedia Content Engineer – AI & UX, you will be responsible for designing and delivering high-quality technical content that enhances user experience and leverages AI-driven tools and automation.
Join us to do the best work of your career and make a profound social impact as a **Multimedia Content Engineer – AI & UX** on our **Content Engineering** Team in **Singapore**.
**What you’ll achieve**
You will leverage your expertise in visual storytelling, modern UX trends, and AI-driven automation to create compelling and interactive content experiences. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to create engaging, interactive, and intelligent documentation that supports modern product ecosystems. This role requires a strong blend of technical knowledge, creative media production, and strategic thinking to elevate the information experience for premium hardware products.
**You will:**
*   Develop and deliver high-impact visual and interactive content tailored for premium hardware products. Design and test AI prompts to improve product documentation and internal workflows.
*   Serve as on-screen talent for feature videos and teardown procedures, ensuring clarity and engagement. Integrate automation tools and AI technologies to streamline content creation and delivery.
*   Create scripts and storyboards for product feature videos and technical teardown demonstrations. Ensure consistency, accuracy, and brand alignment across all content formats and platforms.
*   Apply modern UX trends to enhance the usability and appeal of product documentation and media.
*   Collaborate with engineering, product, and marketing teams to gather technical information and transform it into user-friendly content.
**Take the first step towards your dream career**
Every Dell Technologies team member brings something unique to the table. Here’s what we are looking for with this role:
**Essential Requirements**
\- Proficiency in multimedia content creation including video production, editing, and script writing.
\- Excellent communication and presentation skills, including on-camera presence.
\- Strong understanding of modern UX principles and interactive content design.
\- Experience with AI tools, prompt engineering, and automation technologies for content development. Collaborative mindset with experience working across cross-functional teams
\- Strong technical writing and editing skills. Ability to translate complex technical information into accessible and engaging content.
