### Description
Our games are more than just code – they are dynamic worlds that you actively help shape with your eye for detail. You ensure that our players can dive into captivating and flawless adventures every day. Whether it's our massive MMOs or our innovative payment software – here, you won't just tick off lists, but actively secure the highest quality standards in our products with your own creative test cases. Are you ready to bring your passion for games and your keen eye for detail to take the player experience to the next level? Then apply now at our Regensburg location and help shape the future of our online worlds.

### Responsibilities
*   Unleash your creativity in testing and create your own test plans and cases to put our worlds through their paces.
*   Collaborate with colleagues to manually test and continuously improve monsters, NPCs, quests, and combat systems.
*   Be part of diverse and exciting projects and delve deep into game development through exchange with other teams.
*   Plan, coordinate, and monitor tests with players to ensure the best possible gaming experience.
*   Set up and maintain test environments, and apply your talent for scripting and optimizing processes.

### Qualifications
*   Possess a heart for gaming and are ready to immerse yourself in the world of software testing and quality assurance.
*   Bring IT affinity and technical understanding, are familiar with Linux, and eager to delve deeper into it.
*   Have patience, motivation, and the drive to tackle new challenges repeatedly.
*   Work analytically and structured to continuously improve the quality of our games.
*   Possess a quick comprehension and are passionate about continuous self-development.
