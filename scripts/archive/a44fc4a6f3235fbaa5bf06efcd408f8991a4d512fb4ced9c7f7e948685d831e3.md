Your Tasks
----------
*   *   You are responsible for developing and driving exciting solutions in the areas of data engineering, analytics and machine learning (ML) in order to boost the relevance of search algorithm for the customers browsing through category pages, brand pages or triggering a full-text search in one of the online shops or apps from the MediaMarktSaturn group.
*   You collaborate with data scientists, engineers and analysts to design and deliver ML powered solutions, including hybrid search models, NLP, recommendations, anomaly detection and other advanced techniques, with a strong focus on data preparation, search relevance, KPI tracking, and analytics to ensure customers quickly find exactly what they need.
*   You own and drive the product roadmap and backlog for ML driven search relevance, aligned with business and enterprise strategy.
*   You partner with other product owners, search managers, analysts and stakeholders across countries to capture business requirements and translate them into actionable search improvements.
*   You translate company strategy into actionable tasks in the area of search and build a solid bridge between business units using the search engine (and its features) and the DevOps team developing the product
Your Profile
------------
*   *   You have completed studies in informatics, business informatics, computer science physics, mathematic or any related discipline.
*   You have proven understanding of Machine Learning algorithms and/or processes related to the model development lifecycle and want to learn more. For example, in the areas of search intent recognition/prediction, KPI anomaly detection & reporting, etc.
*   You possess a great level of technical understanding and can grasp new tasks and highly complex subjects quickly, but you are able to “think business” in an environment full of interdependencies.
*   Knowledge of ML frameworks, SQL and/or Python are a plus.
*   Experience in Agile product development; background in e-commerce and search technologies is an advantage.
*   You are reliable, motivated and bring solid communication skills, the right team spirit, the ambition to be innovative and to take search relevance to the next level and the flexibility of working both remotely and/or in our offices in Munich and Ingolstadt.
*   You are fluent in English. German is a plus.
About Us
--------
MediaMarktSaturn is Europe’s leading consumer electronics retailer. It sees itself as a partner, daily companion and navigator for its customers in the digital world. Its over 1,000 MediaMarkt and Saturn stores are closely integrated with their online shops. MediaMarktSaturn has a total headcount of more than 60,000.
MediaMarktSaturn Technology is working on making Europe's number one consumer electronics retailer the technology leader in its sector. To this end, several hundred developers, UX designers and system architects using state-of-the-art technology work hand in hand with business owners in agile teams to develop customer-focused technology solutions for more than 6 million customers every day in 11 countries.
**Technology Hub located in Barcelona**, is one of the service units of MediaMarktSaturn Technology to deliver adequate staffing and engineering skills for the agreed deliveries of the global deployment plan for the entire group.
At MediaMarktSaturn, we help millions of customers discover and buy the right tech products every day. Search is at the heart of that experience. As Product Owner for our Search Machine Learning team, you will shape how our customers find products.
If you are a data scientist or machine learning specialist eager to transition into product ownership or a product owner who has good technical understanding and wants to develop into the dynamic world of machine learning, this is the ideal opportunity to apply your expertise at scale.
