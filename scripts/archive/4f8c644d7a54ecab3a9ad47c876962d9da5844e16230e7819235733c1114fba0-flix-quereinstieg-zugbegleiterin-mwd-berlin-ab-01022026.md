### Description
Join FlixTrain as a Train Attendant and become a crucial part of delivering an exceptional travel experience across Germany. At FlixTrain, we believe in creating secure workplaces with above-average compensation and excellent benefits, where your contributions genuinely make a difference. You'll thrive in a dynamic environment, shaping the future of mobility while providing our passengers with a safe, pleasant, and memorable journey.

Our passionate team is dedicated to offering first-class service with a smile, strong organization, and unwavering team spirit. We foster a culture of trust and personal responsibility, giving you the freedom to implement your own ideas and actively shape your career path. As we continue our international expansion, you'll have the unique opportunity to make a tangible impact – for us, our passengers, and the future of sustainable travel.

Beyond a competitive monthly fixed salary and market-standard allowances, FlixTrain offers an attractive package including 12 free Flix travel vouchers annually, discounted travel for friends and family, and a fixed shift plan for a predictable work-life balance. We prioritize your well-being with free coaching and therapy sessions via OpenUp, and support your growth through paid training, internal programs, and mentorship. Enjoy additional perks like discounted Urban Sports Club membership, JobRad, and access to a corporate benefits portal.

### Responsibilities
As a Train Attendant, your core responsibilities will include:
*   Providing friendly and competent advice to our passengers throughout their train journey.
*   Checking tickets and assisting with on-board ticket sales when required.
*   Ensuring order, cleanliness, and safety across all train carriages.
*   Serving as the central point of contact for all passenger questions and concerns.
*   Actively coordinating on-board operations and assisting with passenger information.
*   Proactively contributing to a positive and enjoyable travel experience for every guest.
*   Participating in the continuous improvement of our service processes.

### Qualifications
We are looking for a dedicated and service-oriented individual who meets the following:
*   Ideally, prior experience in customer service, train attendance, or similar public-facing roles.
*   Proficiency in German language is essential; initial English skills are a strong advantage.
*   A professional and well-groomed demeanor, coupled with strong communication and service-oriented personality.
*   The ability to remain calm, collected, and maintain an overview, even in fast-paced or stressful situations.
*   Flexibility for various shifts, which may include nights or weekends. Your shift schedule will be provided at least 3 weeks in advance for better planning.
*   Willingness to occasionally stay overnight in a hotel after a shift, with all travel, food, and lodging expenses covered by FlixTrain.
*   A readiness to travel, with the exciting opportunity for free Germany-wide travel for both professional and private purposes.

We understand that everyone brings unique experiences. Even if you don't meet every requirement, we encourage you to apply. We look forward to hearing from you!
