### Description\nFC Viktoria Berlin is seeking a passionate intern for a 3-month program focused on match day organization, communication, and fan support. Join a vibrant \"football start-up with a stance\" that's redefining professional football through a modern, inclusive, and female-led approach. This is an opportunity to contribute to a club committed to building a strong community, promoting female empowerment, and establishing sustainable structures on and off the field, as its women's team prepares for the 2. Bundesliga from the 2025/26 season. The role offers a blend of on-site work in Berlin and remote flexibility, starting immediately.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Match Day Organization & Fan Support:**\n  - Assist with the preparation and execution of home games.\n  - Contribute to entrance procedures, ticketing, and merchandise stands.\n  - Support fan activities and match-day programs around the game.\n  - Help shape the fan experience at Stadion Lichterfelde.\n- **Communication & Social Media:**\n  - Assist with social media tasks (e.g., content preparation, community inquiries, photo documentation).\n  - Support in responding to emails regarding ticketing, the club shop, or general inquiries.\n  - Contribute to activities for partner activation (e.g., preparing materials, communicating with partners).\n- **Organization & Daily Operations:**\n  - Support administrative tasks in the club's office.\n  - Assist with the preparation and execution of events, meetings, or team activities.\n  - Gain insights into various processes and structures within club operations.\n- **On top:**\n  - Opportunity to gain insights into other departments such as Marketing, Sales, and Sponsoring, based on interest and project availability.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Passion for football, events, and communication.\n- Availability for a 3-month compulsory or voluntary internship.\n- Reliability, self-initiative, and good organizational skills.\n- Willingness to be on-site for specific home match days (Sundays between 11:00 - 16:00 on Dec 14, Feb 01, Feb 15, Mar 15, Mar 29, Apr 26).\n- First experiences in event, office, or support areas are a plus.\n- A Class B driver's license is an advantage.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional entry-level internship for someone eager to immerse themselves in the world of professional football, particularly within a pioneering, female-led organization. FC Viktoria Berlin offers a unique blend of start-up dynamism and a strong social purpose, focusing on community and empowerment. Interns will gain invaluable hands-on experience across critical operational areas, from match day logistics and fan engagement to communication and administrative tasks, all while being part of a supportive team that values new ideas. This role is perfect for a proactive individual looking to make a tangible impact and build a foundation for a career in sports management, events, or communication.
