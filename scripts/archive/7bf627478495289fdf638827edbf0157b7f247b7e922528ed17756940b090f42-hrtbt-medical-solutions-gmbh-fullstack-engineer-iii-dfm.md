### Description\nHeartbeat Medical is seeking a **Fullstack Engineer** to join their highly collaborative, cross-functional product and engineering team in Berlin, Germany (hybrid setup). This role is pivotal in shaping their digital platform and services for patients and medical professionals, contributing across the full stack – backend, frontend, and infrastructure. As a health-tech company, the role places critical emphasis on testing, safety, and reliability. The successful candidate will take ownership of implementing unit and integration tests, ensuring high code quality standards, and directly impacting hundreds of patients weekly in their mission to make healthcare more patient-centric and digitally supported globally. The position is available full-time or part-time (min. 30 hours/week) with a salary range of €65,000–€75,000 per year, depending on experience.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Develop, test, and maintain scalable features across the full product stack.\n*   Contribute to system architecture and infrastructure decisions.\n*   Improve and maintain CI/CD pipelines and containerized deployments.\n*   Write clean, testable code and perform code reviews.\n*   Support an increasing use of AI tools within the engineering workflow.\n*   Collaborate with Product Managers, designers, engineers, and leadership to deliver product improvements.\n*   Participate in work planning (using Kanban and Scrum mixtures) and retrospectives.\n*   Occasionally interact with customers or users to clarify technical questions or issues.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Strong experience in **JavaScript/TypeScript**, covering both backend (Node.js) and frontend (React, Vue, or similar) development.\n*   Strong understanding of **core web fundamentals** and framework-less development.\n*   Experience working with **SQL** and **relational databases** (Postgres experience is a plus).\n*   Comfort deploying and maintaining **containerized applications** (e.g., Docker).\n*   Ability to write testable, robust software with unit and integration tests.\n*   Excellent communication skills in English, with an openness to learning basic German (support for classes provided).\n*   Comfortable in a dynamic, fast-paced, and purpose-driven environment.\n*   Proactive, hands-on, and team-oriented
