**About League**

Founded in 2014, League is the leading healthcare consumer experience (CX) platform, powered by artificial intelligence (AI), reaching more than 63 million people around the world and delivering the highest level of personalization in the industry. Payers, providers, and consumer health partners build on League’s platform to deliver high-engagement healthcare solutions proven to improve health outcomes. League has raised over $285 million in venture capital funding to date, powering the digital experiences for some of healthcare’s most trusted brands, including Highmark Health, Manulife, Medibank, and Shoppers Drug Mart.

**The Role  
  
**

As an **Intermediate Product Designer**, you are primarily responsible for solving hard problems by creating best-in-class, usable and delightful design work. You work collaboratively with Product Management and Engineering to create the best solutions to problems, and balance delivering high quality experiences to our customers quickly and iteratively. You work with other Designers to provide feedback on their work, and ensure the team is creating a cohesive and consistent design system.

**What you will do**

*   Solve complex design problems through simple, beautiful design
*   Advocate for great user experience across all teams (member, employer, integrations)
*   Share and iterate on designs based on customer interviews, peer feedback and UX research insights
*   Be involved in all aspects of product development, working closely with product managers, designers, and developers on feature sets spanning across our product shelf
*   Create high fidelity designs and prototypes for new projects
*   Contribute to our design system while leveraging existing standards for product consistency

**What you bring**

*   3+ years hands-on experience in Product Design and/or Design Strategy.
*   Strong experience designing cross-platform (Responsive Web, iOS, Android) digital products.
*   Strong portfolio demonstrating user-centered design thinking across solutions and platforms.
*   Experience collaborating in cross-functional, agile environments.
*   Exposure to the healthcare industry (preferred, but not required)
*   AI Fluency
