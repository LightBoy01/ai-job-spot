**Kognitos** is at the forefront of revolutionizing the trillion-dollar hyper-automation market. Our mission is to redefine how software is built and maintained by leveraging cutting-edge multi-agent automation platforms. We are pioneering advancements in agentic workflows, enabling machines to reason, plan, and execute tasks in a deterministic fashion.

Our approach elevates debugging to English, bypassing traditional programming languages that struggle to adapt to runtime surprises. Kognitos is building "HAL"—but the friendly, helpful kind—to create systems that align AI safety with business value, solving challenges in efficient fine-tuning, multimodal reasoning, and real-world adaptability.

Our team includes top engineers from Google, Microsoft, Meta, Amazon, Pure Storage, and leading tech startups. Many of our engineers have studied at IIT, Georgia Tech, Carnegie Mellon, Stanford, UC Berkeley, University of Washington, UIUC, Caltech, and the University of Michigan.

With offices in San Jose, CA, and Bangalore, India, we’re building a global center of excellence in AI automation.

At Kognitos, you won’t just build software—you’ll help shape the future of AI-driven automation.

**Stop marketing hype. Start marketing a revolution.**

Kognitos is building the future of AI automation, turning plain English from business users directly into powerful, operations-ready AI. It’s a category-defining technology, but in a market drowning in noise, our story needs a powerful voice to cut through.

That’s where you come in.

### **What You'll Do:**

*   **Own the Content Playbook:** You will develop and execute a comprehensive content strategy that establishes Kognitos as the undisputed leader in AI automation, blending product-led growth (PLG) tactics with insightful, brand-building narratives.
    
*   **Become an Influential Face of Kognitos:** You will be our primary creator, specializing in short-form video. You'll script, shoot, edit, and host engaging content for LinkedIn, X(Twitter), YouTube, and other key channels, making our product and its value crystal clear.
    
*   **Translate the Technical into the Tangible:** Work side-by-side with our founders, engineers, product, and product marketing teams to deeply understand Kognitos. Your mission is to distill that complexity into clear, concise, and captivating content for a B2B audience.
    
*   **Master an AI-First Workflow:** You’ll live at the cutting edge, using AI-driven tools (e.g., GPT-4, Descript, Midjourney, CapCut) not as a crutch, but as a creative partner to accelerate your workflow and amplify your output.
    
*   **Measure What Matters:** Go beyond vanity metrics. You’ll own the analytics for your content, connecting your work directly to lead generation, pipeline influence, and brand awareness, and constantly iterating based on data.
    
*   **Collaborate to Win:** Partner with the broader marketing and sales teams to ensure your content fuels every stage of the funnel, from top-of-funnel awareness campaigns to bottom-of-funnel sales enablement assets.
    

### Who You Are (Qualifications):

*   **Experience Level:** 5-8 years in a content creation and strategy role, with a proven track record within a B2B SaaS or enterprise technology company. Experience in a high-growth startup environment is a major plus.
    
*   **Demonstrable Impact:** You must have a strong portfolio of work, with an emphasis on video content. We want to see examples of your creations and hear you articulate the strategy behind them and the business results they drove.
    
*   **MarTech Proficiency:** Extensive experience with modern marketing and sales platforms. You should be comfortable navigating tools like Hubspot, Salesforce, HootSuite, Google Analytics, and SEO tools like SEMrush and Ahrefs.
    
*   **AI-Native Toolkit:** Hands-on expertise with an AI-first content workflow. You should have extensive experience using generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT for scripting), video/audio editors (e.g., ElevenLabs, Descript, CapCut), and image generators (e.g., Veo3, Midjourney) to accelerate your creative process.
    
*   **On-Camera Excellence:** Exceptional communication skills, with a natural and engaging on-camera presence. Experience hosting webinars, product demos, or social media videos is essential.
    
*   **Analytical Mindset:** Proficiency in using analytics to measure content performance, track key KPIs (like engagement, pipeline influence, and conversions), and make data-driven decisions to optimize your strategy.
    
*   **Education:** A Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business, Communications, Journalism, or a related field.
    

### **You might be a perfect fit if...**

*   You live on platforms like LinkedIn and X(Twitter) and have strong instincts for what kind of content performs and why.
    
*   You believe that "show, don't tell" is the golden rule of marketing complex products.
    
*   You’d rather spend an hour workshopping an idea with an engineer than a day in a pointless meeting.
    
*   You see content as a product that should be built, shipped, and iterated on with the user in mind.
    
*   You thrive on autonomy and want to be measured by your impact, not your activity.
    

If you’re ready to stop just contributing to a brand and start building one, apply now.
