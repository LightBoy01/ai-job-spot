### Description\nPrivate Equity Insights is seeking enthusiastic volunteers for their leading French Private Equity Conference in Munich on September 26, 2025. This unique opportunity allows individuals to expand their professional network with over 500 leaders, entrepreneurs, investors, and general/limited partners from the Private Equity sector, while also developing valuable leadership and soft skills. Volunteers will join a team of 25+ peers to help build, run, and deliver a pivotal industry event, gaining an insider's view into how the world's largest Private Equity community is fostered. This is an excellent chance to contribute to a high-profile event and connect with major industry players like Blackstone, BlackRock, and KKR. For a glimpse into previous events, watch the [2023 Milan's highlight video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1w9amNARIROInji1jERo8KzgPLCfemnQM/view?usp=drive_link) and visit our [website](https://pe-insights.com/private-equity-conferences/france/) for more details.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a Private Equity Conference Volunteer, you will play a crucial role in the successful execution of the event. Key responsibilities include:\n- Actively assisting in the setup and logistical preparation of the conference.\n- Supporting the Private Equity Insights team in running and delivering the event on the conference day.\n- Participating in a mandatory 2-hour training session and rehearsal the afternoon before the event.\n- Contributing to a dynamic and engaging atmosphere for attendees and speakers.\n- Collaborating effectively with a team of 25+ fellow volunteers.\n\n### Qualifications\nWe are looking for ambitious and talented individuals eager to contribute to a premier industry event. Ideal volunteers will possess:\n- A strong desire to expand their professional network within the Private Equity sector.\n- Eagerness to develop soft skills, including management, communication, and teamwork.\n- A proactive, enthusiastic, and collaborative attitude.\n- Availability to commit for the full day of the conference (September 26, 2025) at the Marriott Hotel City West in Munich, plus a 2-hour session the afternoon prior.\n- An interest in gaining insights into the operations of a leading global finance conference.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a truly exceptional volunteer opportunity for anyone looking to break into, or further their career in, the private equity and finance world. Far more than just an event support role, it offers unparalleled access to C-level executives and influential investors – direct access that would be incredibly difficult to secure otherwise. The promise of individual training in management, official certification, and an exclusive post-event dinner are significant benefits that underscore the value Private Equity Insights places on its volunteers. This role is a strategic investment in your network and skill set, providing a concentrated burst of high-level exposure and practical experience over a short, impactful period. It's a stepping stone for future career opportunities and a chance to build genuine relationships with like-minded professionals.
