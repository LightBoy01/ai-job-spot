### Description\nEuraTel, an innovative telecommunications company based in Leipzig, is looking for **Telephone Customer Advisors (m/f/d)** to join our team. You'll have the flexibility to work from home, at our service center, or in a hybrid model, on a full-time or part-time basis (20-40 hours/week).\n\nEuraTel develops and supports forward-thinking communication solutions, including service center operations for national and international clients in diverse sectors such as door and gate automation, healthcare, photovoltaics, and innovative security systems against telephone fraud and spam. \n\nAs part of our dynamic Service Center team (approx. 30-40 employees), you'll enjoy a secure, permanent employment contract and attractive compensation, including a fixed salary plus performance bonuses. We also offer additional support such as subsidies for commuting costs or childcare. Our culture is characterized by flat hierarchies, a collegial environment, and regular team events to foster a great working atmosphere. We believe in your personal growth, providing careful onboarding, team support, and regular training opportunities to keep you up-to-date and ensure career changers have a smooth start. While our service center operates 24/7, requiring occasional late (approx. 8 PM - 10 PM) and weekend shifts, we are committed to accommodating your preferred working hours. For those working on-site, our modern offices are located in Gohlis Arkaden Leipzig, easily accessible by public transport or car.\n\n### Responsibilities\nYour customer support activities may include a variety of areas, such as:\n- Providing customer service for large healthcare providers.\n- Operating service and support hotlines for hotel booking systems.\n- Handling fault reports and order processing for automatic doors and gate systems.\n- Engaging in many other diverse customer interaction tasks.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Technical Know-how**: Proficient use of PC and MS Office applications.\n- **Language Skills**: Excellent verbal and written communication skills.\n- **Personal Skills**: A friendly, confident, and competent demeanor, high level of commitment, and a passion for professional communication and telephoning.\n- **Customer Orientation**: A strong service orientation and empathetic customer approach are second nature to you.\n- **Experience**: Ideally, you already have initial experience in customer service or a call center environment.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for individuals passionate about customer service, offering a highly flexible work environment with options for home office and part-time hours. EuraTel stands out with its commitment to employee well-being, providing comprehensive training, strong team support, and clear paths for personal development, making it ideal for both experienced professionals and career changers. The diverse range of customer industries ensures a dynamic and engaging role where every day brings new challenges and opportunities to make a direct impact. If you value a supportive culture, work-life balance, and continuous learning, this role at EuraTel could be your next great step.
