**Do you enjoy designing and building public cloud solutions for customers?**

**Do you enjoy working with a diverse multinational team of engineering talents?**

**Join the Technical Solutions Architect team**

Akamai is working to simplify the way people work in the cloud. The team's mission is to accelerate innovation by making computing simple, scalable, accessible, and affordable for all. We provide the infrastructure and services that enable developers to build next generation applications.

**Shape the future of the Internet**

As a presales solutions engineer (TSA), you'll define Cloud/Linode solutions during the sales lifecycle. You'll guide customers through strategic and technical Cloud transformation decisions while driving Linode service adoption. You'll serve as a thought-leader in cloud computing within Akamai and externally.

As a Technical Solutions Architect II, you will be responsible for:

*   Driving Linode opportunities as a cloud pre-sales engineer, supporting accounts, and owning technical sales efforts.
*   Ensuring customer success and Linode consumption within the integrated account team.
*   Driving innovation in solutions and products while promoting trends and influencing the global product roadmap.
*   Building technical expertise in Cloud Computing/Linode to support Akamai's overall business growth.

**Do what you love**

To be successful in this role you will:

*   Possess extensive professional experience including relevant experience in customer-facing technical roles with cloud-related organizations.
*   Demonstrate extensive experience in architecting IaaS solutions for customers. Hold professional certifications from AWS/GCP/Azure/Oracle and/or CNCF.
*   Show hands-on expertise in Kubernetes, cloud networking, database, storage, AI, IaC, gaming, or media.
*   Show expertise and practical skills in open-source software like Apache, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Redis, Nginx, Terraform.
*   Communicate complex technical content effectively through writing, speaking, and presenting to diverse audiences.
