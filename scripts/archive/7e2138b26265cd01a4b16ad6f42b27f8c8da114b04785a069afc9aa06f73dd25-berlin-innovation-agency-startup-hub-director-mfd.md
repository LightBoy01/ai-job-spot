### Description\nWe are a Berlin-based boutique agency at the intersection of corporate transformation and startup entrepreneurship. Through our three units BIA, HelloCircle, and ClimateX Hub, we support established organizations in their transformation while also helping startups build impactful businesses.\n\nIn the Corporate Programs area (BIA & HelloCircle), we develop tailored change, AI, and leadership programs for companies. In the Startup Hub (ClimateX), we focus on accelerator and soft-landing programs, as well as co-innovation and venture clienting, to specifically connect established companies with innovative startups.\n\nOur core team of less than ten people works from Berlin-Mitte and is supported by a network of over 40 experienced freelancers. We develop innovative programs with partners such as LiveEO, GetYourGuide, Novo Nordisk, Roland Berger, and public funding bodies like the Berlin Senate and various federal ministries. Our Startup Hub is a very dynamic environment, and we are looking for a **Startup Hub Director** who will lead the hub across all programs and actively shape its future direction.\n\nYou will develop new thematic areas such as AI and Future of Work, build additional ClimateTech Entrepreneurship programs, and take holistic responsibility for the hub – from project acquisition and operational program steering to grant management.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop the cross-program hub agenda together with the team and connect startup programs with our corporate innovation themes.\n- Coordinate the implementation of our programs, work closely with the Program Manager team and our freelancers, and ensure transparent planning and program controlling.\n- Actively participate in the programs – through startup scouting, event development and moderation, workshop facilitation, and coaching of founders.\n- Engage in the startup ecosystem, identify new project opportunities, and build partnerships with ministries, investors, universities, corporates, and other stakeholders.\n- Support project acquisition through the joint development of funding applications and the creation of proposals for corporate tenders.\n- Oversee project administration within the framework of grant management, including invoicing, reporting, and compliance with all grant-related regulations.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You have at least 5 years of experience with startup programs.\n- You bring solid experience (min. 3 years) in grant/funding management.\n- You know the startup and impact scene well and navigate confidently within the ecosystem.\n- You have experience in corporate-startup innovation.\n- You work hands-on, quickly take responsibility, and act independently.\n- You possess strong project management and communication skills.\n- You demonstrate a positive attitude and build strong relationships with partners.\n- You speak German at a native level and are fluent in English.\n- You have a Bachelor's or Master's degree.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a highly impactful and dynamic Director role within a growing Berlin-based boutique agency, perfect for a leader passionate about connecting startups and corporates, particularly in the ClimateTech space. The company prides itself on an ambitious, collaborative, and learning-oriented culture, offering significant autonomy and mentoring from an experienced founder. You'll be instrumental in shaping the future of the Startup Hub, working with inspiring partners, and making a real difference. Beyond the challenging work, they offer attractive benefits including 30 days of vacation, premium hardware, flexible work options (office + home office), and a genuine commitment to family-friendliness, ensuring a sustainable and rewarding career. The team is eager to onboard the new Director as soon as possible, by March 2026 at the latest.
