### Description

Are you passionate about working with numbers and seeking a diverse accounting position? We are recruiting on behalf of our client, a medium-sized company in the commercial-technical sector, for a dedicated **Accounting Clerk (m/f/d)**.

This role offers a varied and challenging range of tasks within a collegial team. You'll benefit from an open-ended employment contract, long-term assignments with client takeover options, and a supportive environment that prioritizes your professional growth. The company provides regular job-related training to ensure a long-term perspective, along with attractive benefits like holiday and Christmas bonuses, and increasing vacation entitlement up to 30 days.

### Responsibilities

*   Handle the **booking of ongoing business transactions**
*   Review, record, and **control invoices**
*   Maintain master data and manage the **cash book**
*   Organize **payment transactions** and support **dunning procedures**
*   Prepare documents for **payroll accounting**

### Qualifications

*   Completed **commercial training with an additional qualification in accounting** or **tax specialization**
*   Experience in **accounting**, with experience in the construction industry being **an advantage**
*   Proficiency in **MS Office**, especially Excel
*   Knowledge of accounting software (e.g., DATEV, PDS) is **desirable, but not mandatory**
*   Independent, structured, and reliable work ethic
*   Team-oriented, communicative, and diligent
*   Very good German language skills, both written and spoken

### Author's Take

**1. Given this is a medium-sized company in the commercial-technical sector, how does an Accounting Clerk directly contribute to the company's operational efficiency and strategic goals?**

*From an insider's perspective, this role is far more than just processing numbers; it's the financial backbone of our operations. In a technical environment where projects run on tight schedules and budgets, your meticulous work in booking transactions, controlling invoices, and managing the cash book directly impacts our project liquidity and profitability. By ensuring accurate financial records and smooth payment processes, you enable our technical teams to focus on their core work, secure supplier relationships, and ensure timely payroll for everyone. Your efforts provide the reliable data that management relies on for crucial decision-making, helping us identify opportunities for growth and maintain financial health in a competitive market.*

**2. The description mentions a 'collegial team' and opportunities for 'regular professional development.' Could you elaborate on the daily team dynamics and how the company supports continuous learning and career growth for an Accounting Clerk?**

*Absolutely. Our accounting team thrives on collaboration and mutual support. You won't just be handed tasks; you'll be part of a supportive group where questions are encouraged, and knowledge sharing is common. On a daily basis, we work closely, helping each other navigate complex cases and ensuring everything runs smoothly. Regarding development, the company is genuinely invested. We regularly participate in workshops, and there's support for external courses, especially for software like DATEV or PDS, should you wish to deepen those skills. The goal isn't just to keep up but to excel. We see this as a long-term journey, and the diverse range of tasks you'll handle naturally expands your expertise, giving you a clear path to becoming a more senior specialist within our organization. It's about growing with the company.*
