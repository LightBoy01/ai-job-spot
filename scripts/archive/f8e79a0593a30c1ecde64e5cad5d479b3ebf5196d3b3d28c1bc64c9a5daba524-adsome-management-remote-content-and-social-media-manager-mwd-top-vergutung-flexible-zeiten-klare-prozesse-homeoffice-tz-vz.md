### Responsibilities
*   **Trend Research**: Identify viral trends and successful short-video formats that align with our creators.
*   **Personal Branding Development**: Develop or optimize an authentic and recognizable personal brand.
*   **Content Briefings**: Create clear and structured scripts so creators know exactly how to produce content.
*   **Posting & Account Management**: Publish content and ensure social media profiles are always current, appealing, and strategically maintained.
*   **Performance Analysis & Optimization**: Analyze content and statistics, derive optimization potential, and adapt strategies precisely.

### Qualifications
*   Proven success in online marketing, social media management, or influencer marketing.
*   An eye for virality and successful content formats.
*   A results-oriented mindset with a focus on measurable performance.

**Optional (Bonus Points):**
*   Experience in copywriting or Paid Ads / Performance Marketing / Media Buying.
*   Video editing skills.
