### Description

Join our Global IT Organization as we help shape the future through digital, data, and AI. We are seeking an innovative and strategic leader for the position of Director IT, Digital Partner – Americas Businesses. Reporting directly to the Global Chief Information Officer, this is a pivotal role in accelerating our digital transformation journey by reimagining the future of our business to create unparalleled value for our business, patients, and customers.

In this role, you will collaborate with senior leaders across our Service and Product Businesses, Marketing, Sales, Human Resources, and Legal/Privacy functions. You will lead the charge in exploring and implementing advanced technologies like AI, digital product modeling, and real-time predictive analytics to transform our corporate functions and commercial execution. This position is based in our Libertyville, IL office and operates in a hybrid work environment, requiring three days on-site per week.

### Responsibilities

*   **Strategic Leadership & Partnership:** Partner closely with the CIO and executive leaders to lead a multi-year technology transformation. Build deep, trusted relationships with business leaders to understand their goals and co-create a future-state technology roadmap that drives business performance.
*   **Technology & Platform Management:** Provide strategic direction for key platforms including Salesforce, Veeva, SAP SuccessFactors, Azure, and PowerBI. Design and implement modern, secure, and scalable architectures that enhance analytics, AI capabilities, and system integrations.
*   **Innovation & Transformation:** Foster a culture of innovation and collaboration, establishing IT as a strategic catalyst for change. Drive an enterprise-wide shift toward a digital-first, AI-first, data-driven culture, leveraging emerging technologies to solve business challenges.
*   **Team Development & Leadership:** Lead, inspire, and develop a high-performing global IT team. Evolve the organization toward an agile, product-oriented operating model to deliver our Digital Roadmap and drive unprecedented value through technology.
*   **Governance, Compliance & Finance:** Establish comprehensive frameworks for data quality, security, and compliance, ensuring adherence to medical device industry regulations (e.g., 21 CFR part 11, ISO:13485). Own and manage departmental budgets and key vendor relationships.

### Qualifications

*   Bachelor's degree or higher in Information Technology, Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field; MBA or Master of Science is preferred.
*   15+ years of overall experience in IT, with at least 10 years in platform or product implementation roles.
*   Proven track record of developing and delivering platform strategies across multiple functions in a global, highly regulated company (life sciences experience is strongly preferred).
*   Deep architectural experience in Cloud Platforms (especially Azure), integrations with microservices, APIs, and reusable assets.
*   Advanced knowledge of software development lifecycles (Waterfall, Agile, Scrum), DevOps, and change management.
*   Extensive knowledge of technology trends, including AI/GenAI, omnichannel experiences, and customer-facing digital products.
*   Exceptional leadership skills with a history of inspiring technical teams and driving large-scale transformation.
*   Excellent communication, presentation, and interpersonal skills, with the ability to influence executives and translate complex technical concepts for non-technical audiences.
*   Ability to travel up to 25% as needed.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "Beyond modernizing technology, what is the deeper, human-centric goal of this digital transformation, especially given Hollister's mission to make life more rewarding and dignified?"**

That's the core of it, isn't it? For us, technology is never just about efficiency for its own sake. Because we're an employee-owned company, our mission is personal. This transformation is about closing the distance between us and the people who rely on our products. When we use AI to predict a supply need or streamline an order process, we're not just optimizing a workflow; we're giving a patient or a caregiver one less thing to worry about. It's about using data to create a more personalized, empathetic experience, ensuring someone feels understood and supported, not like a case number. This role isn't just about implementing Salesforce or Azure; it's about architecting a system of care that lives up to our promise of dignity.

**A candidate might also ask: "This role is described as a 'change catalyst.' What are the biggest cultural hurdles to overcome in shifting to a digital-first mindset, and how does the leadership team actively support navigating these challenges?"**

That's a very insightful question. The biggest hurdle in any established company, especially a regulated one, is moving from 'this is how we've always done it' to 'what's possible if we try something new?' Our leadership, right up to the CIO, understands this deeply. The support isn't passive; it's active sponsorship. We don't expect this role to simply push technology; we expect you to be a storyteller and a coach. You'll have the executive backing to create pilot programs, to test and learn without the fear of failure, and to demonstrate tangible value. Your job is to connect the dots for people—to show how a new CRM feature helps a sales rep better serve a clinic, or how a new data model helps us create better educational materials for patients. The leadership team provides the air cover; your role is to lead the charge on the ground, bringing people along on the journey by focusing on the 'why' behind the change.
