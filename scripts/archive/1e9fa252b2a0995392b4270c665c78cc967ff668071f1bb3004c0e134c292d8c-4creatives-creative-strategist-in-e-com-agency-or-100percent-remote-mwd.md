### Description
Are you frustrated by bad e-commerce ads flooding your Instagram feed? So are we! If you're passionate about writing direct response ads that truly hit the mark, disrupting various e-commerce niches with your creativity, and exercising maximum creative freedom, then this is the place for you.

We are 4creatives, a dynamic creative agency looking for a **Creative Strategist** to join our 100% remote team. You'll be responsible for producing high-converting ads for leading e-commerce brands like Alpensattel, Tierliebhaber, BitterLiebe, and many more – moving beyond generic content to deliver truly effective creatives. This role offers the opportunity to work with a different brand from diverse niches every day, fostering continuous learning and skill development, all while enjoying absolute flexibility and the freedom to manage your own time remotely.

### Responsibilities
- **Deep Dive Audience Research:** Immerse yourself in target audiences, analyze their true triggers, and develop high-performing ad angles.
- **Test Processes & Strategies:** Plan which formats, hooks, and offers to test – whether it's podcasts, UGC, VSLs, or advertorials, you decide.
- **Write Converting Scripts:** Direct response copywriting is your daily business. You'll write scripts for TikTok & Meta Ads that generate not just clicks, but actual revenue.
- **Collaboration with Editors:** Provide feedback on edits to ensure ads align with your vision.
- **Analysis & Optimization:** Monitor results, identify what works, and scale successful strategies.
- **Test New Trends and Formats:** Stay up-to-date with the latest trends and introduce fresh ideas that make a tangible difference.

### Qualifications
- **1-2 years of experience** in Creative Strategy for Meta Ads within an agency or e-commerce brand, or possess exceptional talent with a killer portfolio.
- **Proven Direct Response Copywriting Skills** – writing scripts that sell is second nature to you.
- **Creativity & Performance Mindset** – you love testing new ideas while always keeping ROI in sight.
- **Self-reliance & Drive** – you thrive without close supervision, preferring to shape your own work and continuously improve.
- **Team Spirit & Humor** – you enjoy working closely with a supportive team, not taking yourselves too seriously, but always with a focus on performance.
