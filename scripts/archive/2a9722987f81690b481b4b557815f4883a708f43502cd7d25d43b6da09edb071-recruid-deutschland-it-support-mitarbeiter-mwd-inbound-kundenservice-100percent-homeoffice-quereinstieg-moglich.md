### Description

Join a team dedicated to providing modern, human-centric customer service. We empower companies to offer swift and understandable support, free from technical jargon. This role is perfect for individuals who enjoy interacting with people, possess patience, and are fascinated by technology. Even if you're a career changer (Quereinsteiger) without prior experience, you'll find a supportive environment here.

We offer a secure job with great prospects, providing opportunities to develop and grow into the IT sector. You'll benefit from a fixed hourly wage, fair bonus possibilities, intensive onboarding with experienced coaches, flexible working hours (ideal for early birds or night owls), and a 100% remote home office setup. Our supportive team is truly committed to having your back.

For those seeking greater responsibility and a higher salary, we offer a dedicated career development path. Within 9 to 12 months, we can guide you towards a position with a significantly higher annual salary, tailored to your ambitions and development.

### Responsibilities

As a Customer Service Specialist, your core responsibilities will include:

*   Clearly explaining technical solutions in an easy-to-understand manner for all customers.
*   Assisting customers with a wide variety of questions and issues.
*   Guiding customers step-by-step through problem-solving processes until a resolution is reached.

### Qualifications

We're looking for individuals who bring the following to our team:

*   A genuine enjoyment of customer contact and a passion for solving technical challenges.
*   A basic understanding of technology or a strong curiosity and willingness to learn about IT.
*   A proactive learning attitude and a strong sense of team spirit.
*   Proficiency in German language skills.

### Author's Take

**1. What's the true impact of this role beyond just resolving technical issues? How do I make a real difference for our customers?**

"Beyond troubleshooting, you're essentially a bridge-builder. Our customers often come to us feeling frustrated or confused by technology. Your job isn't just to fix a problem, but to simplify a complex world for them, to reassure them, and to empower them with knowledge. Imagine the relief and gratitude when someone finally understands how to use a feature that was baffling them, or when they feel heard and respected. You're not just solving a ticket; you're cultivating trust and loyalty, making technology less daunting and more accessible. It's about delivering a truly 'human' experience in a digital age, and that's incredibly rewarding."

**2. The description mentions significant career development and higher salaries within 9-12 months. What does that growth path *actually* look like, and how does the company actively support it?**

"This isn't just a promise; it's a structured program we're genuinely passionate about. When we talk about growth, we mean a clear, individualized development plan. We pair you with experienced coaches who act as mentors, not just trainers. You'll gain deeper technical expertise, potentially specialize in certain areas, and learn advanced problem-solving and communication techniques. Our 'Leitung Karriereentwicklung' (Head of Career Development) isn't just a title; it's a dedicated resource. They'll work with you one-on-one, identifying your strengths and ambitions, and charting a realistic path to promotion. This could involve stepping into a more specialized support role, becoming a senior agent, or even moving into training or team lead positions. We're committed to promoting from within, investing in our people, and providing the tools and opportunities for you to sculpt a truly impactful career here."
