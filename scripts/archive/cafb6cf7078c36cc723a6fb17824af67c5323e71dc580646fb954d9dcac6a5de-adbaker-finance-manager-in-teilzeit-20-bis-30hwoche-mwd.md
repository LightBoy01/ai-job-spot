### Description\nThis position is for a part-time Finance specialist (m/w/d) at ADBAKER, a leading Facebook & Instagram Performance Marketing Agency in the DACH region. The role involves supporting the CEO and independently managing, structuring, and developing the entire finance department, including daily operational tasks. It's an opportunity for someone passionate about shaping processes and taking responsibility in a dynamic and challenging environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Close collaboration with the CEO** as the central finance contact person within the company.\n- **Independent management of all operational financial processes**, including invoice verification, preparatory accounting, invoicing, accounts payable management, and payment transactions.\n- **Accounts receivable management**: monitoring open items, maintaining customer data, preparing and following up on dunning runs.\n- **Interface to internal and external stakeholders**, e.g., management, tax advisors, customers, and partners.\n- **Further development and optimization of finance processes** to ensure efficient operations.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Several years of experience in **Finance**, accounting, or preparatory bookkeeping.\n- Proficient use of common tools.\n- Structured, meticulous, and responsible working style.\n- High degree of initiative, proactivity, and enjoyment in improving processes.\n- Very good German language skills and good English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an experienced and proactive finance professional to take ownership of an entire function within a fast-growing, dynamic performance marketing agency. The emphasis on independent management and process optimization, coupled with direct support for the CEO, suggests a role with significant impact and autonomy. ADBAKER clearly values employee well-being, offering a modern loft office, numerous perks like warm lunches, office yoga, and cool team events, creating an engaging and supportive work environment. If you thrive on improving systems and contributing strategically, this part-time role offers an excellent blend of challenge and flexibility.
