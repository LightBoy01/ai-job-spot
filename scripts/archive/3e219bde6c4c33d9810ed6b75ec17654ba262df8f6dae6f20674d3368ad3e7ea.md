United Wholesale Mortgage (UWM), the leading wholesale mortgage lender in the nation, is seeking a talented and experienced Prompt Engineer to join our innovative AI team. The ideal candidate will possess a strong background in natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML), specifically in designing and refining prompts for large language models (LLMs). As a Prompt Engineer, you will bridge the gap between human needs and AI capabilities by crafting prompts that elicit accurate, relevant, and high-quality responses from AI systems, ultimately enhancing our mortgage-related AI solutions.

WHAT YOU WILL BE DOING:

*   Create and refine text prompts to guide AI systems toward desired outputs for various tasks, such as text generation, translation, question answering, and creative writing
    
*   Develop prompts that enhance the performance and reliability of AI models in the mortgage industry
    
*   Continuously test prompts to assess their effectiveness and make necessary adjustments to improve performance
    
*   Utilize data-driven approaches to evaluate prompt outcomes and refine strategies accordingly.
    
*   Analyze prompt performance data to understand how different prompts impact AI behavior and outcomes
    
*   Generate insights and recommendations based on data analysis to optimize prompts for specific use cases
    
*   Keep abreast of the latest advancements in AI technology and prompt engineering techniques.
    
*   Experiment with new prompt strategies and technologies to drive innovation in mortgage AI solutions
    
*   Help users interact with AI systems more effectively by refining prompts and improving the overall user experience
    
*   Tailor prompts to recognize the capabilities and limitations of AI models, ensuring realistic and relevant interactions
    
*   Create and maintain comprehensive documentation, tutorials, and best practices for prompt engineering
    
*   Share knowledge and insights with team members and stakeholders to promote effective prompt engineering practices
    

WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU:

**Must Have:**

*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, AI, or a related field
    
*   Minimum of 5 years of experience in prompt engineering, NLP, or AI model optimization
    
*   Proven experience in designing and refining prompts for LLMs
    
*   Strong analytical skills and the ability to interpret complex data sets
    
*   Excellent communication and collaboration skills
    
*   Ability to stay updated with the latest AI advancements and apply them effectively
    

**Nice To Have:**

*   Master’s degree in Computer Science, AI, or a related field
*   Experience in the mortgage or financial services industry
    
*   Familiarity with cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure) and AI/ML tools
    
*   Knowledge of ethical considerations in AI and prompt engineering
