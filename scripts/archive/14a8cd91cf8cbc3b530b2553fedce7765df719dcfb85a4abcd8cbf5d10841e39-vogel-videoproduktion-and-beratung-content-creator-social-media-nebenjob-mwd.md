### Description
We are looking for new employees (m/f/d) to support us in creating and designing daily content for our clients. As a young and growing company, we're seeking individuals who are ready to roll up their sleeves, contribute actively, and grow with us. We pull together in the same direction and are excited to welcome passionate individuals to our team in the media industry.

### Responsibilities
*   Planning and execution of images and videos for our social media profiles (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube)
*   Review and revision of recorded material
*   Video editing and processing of content
*   Conception of new content ideas

### Qualifications
*   You are self-taught, have completed a degree/apprenticeship, or have already gained experience in an agency or through self-employment.
*   You have a basic technical understanding of image and video.
*   You are creative, a team player, and have an eye for detail.
*   You have clean and error-free expression in German.
*   You work independently, organize & finalize your own projects on time.
*   You are quick to grasp new concepts.
*   You have the ability to follow existing processes.

### Author's Take
This is a fantastic opportunity for a creative content specialist who thrives in a dynamic, collaborative, and growth-oriented environment. The company emphasizes internal development with dedicated training in video, lighting, and sound technology, alongside clear career advancement possibilities. You'll work with modern Apple equipment on diverse, hands-on productions, and your ideas are genuinely valued. The culture is open, supportive, and offers perks like a beverage flat-rate and the flexibility of partial remote work (though proximity to Düsseldorf is still desired). If you're a self-starter with a passion for digital content and eager to make a tangible impact, this role offers a great platform to develop your skills and grow within a young, ambitious team.
