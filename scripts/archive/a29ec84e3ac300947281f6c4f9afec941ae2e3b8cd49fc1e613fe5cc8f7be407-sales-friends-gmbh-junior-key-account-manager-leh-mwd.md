### Description\nSales Friends is an owner-managed trade and sales agency driven by a genuine passion for brands and the food retail sector. Our mission is to make A-brands visible at the Point of Sale (PoS) with heart, intellect, and a dedicated team.\n\nWe are looking for a **Junior Key Account Manager (m/w/d)** to join our team in **Rellingen near Hamburg**. This role is an excellent opportunity to take responsibility, shape strategic partnerships, and advance your career in sales, contributing to the strategic growth of our strong brands.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Support & Collaboration**: Work closely with management (Geschäftsführung and VKL), independently manage initial clients (MBU, regions), and further develop strategic retail concepts with our customers.\n*   **Brand Performance Monitoring**: Analyze sales, market shares, and PoS performance, then derive concrete measures to enhance visibility and sales.\n*   **Peer-Level Communication**: Cultivate contacts with decision-makers in retail and collaborate effectively with our field service teams.\n*   **Coordination & Implementation**: Plan sales promotion activities (VKF), coordinate listings and placements, and ensure smooth processes between retail, brand, and field service.\n*   **Strategic Growth**: Collaborate with the team to develop ideas on how to position our A-brands even more strongly in the market.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   **Commercial education or degree**, e.g., in Business Administration (BWL), Marketing, or Sales.\n*   **Experience in Key Account Management, Trade Marketing, or Field Sales**, ideally within the FMCG (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods) or LEH (food retail) environment.\n*   **Initial contacts** with central buyers.\n*   **Strong communication and analytical skills**: Ability to handle numbers and inspire people.\n*   **Self-initiative & Team Spirit**: Structured, reliable work style, and enthusiasm for collaborative development.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at Sales Friends offers a fantastic entry point for an ambitious individual looking to grow their career in Key Account Management within the dynamic FMCG and food retail sector. As an owner-managed agency, Sales Friends provides a familial and highly collaborative environment where teamwork and open communication are valued. You'll benefit from intensive onboarding and mentorship, allowing you to quickly take on significant responsibility and contribute directly to the success of leading A-brands. With generous vacation policies and a commitment to professional development, this position promises a supportive culture for learning, impact, and a healthy work-life balance.
