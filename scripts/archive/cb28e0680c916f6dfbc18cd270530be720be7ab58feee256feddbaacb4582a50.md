**About the Role**

Do you thrive on solving complex, logic-based puzzles? Are you a fast learner who enjoys transforming chaos into clarity? We are looking for an **Automation Specialist** to join our Intelligent Document Processing team. This role focuses on developing, optimizing, and maintaining document parsing templates - particularly using platforms like ABBYY FlexiCapture and Kodexa AI - to automate the extraction of utility bill data.

**Key Responsibilities**

This is a hands-on, detail-oriented role that blends technical precision with critical thinking.

*   Design and maintain AI based data extraction templates.
*   Analyze and understand diverse bill formats to ensure reliable and scalable automation.
*   Collaborate with product and operations teams to resolve data issues and optimize output.
*   Participate in Agile ceremonies and contribute to planning, QA, and delivery.
*   Apply strong logic skills to troubleshoot automation flows and identify improvements.
*   Develop an understanding of utility billing patterns, vendor nuances, and industry data formats.
*   Leverage modern AI tools (including large language models) to accelerate development, enrich data interpretation, and improve automation workflows.
*   Craft and refine prompts to guide AI outputs - particularly when working within AI-assisted platforms like Kodexa or other document intelligence tools.
