### Description\nUrban Sports Club, now part of Wellhub, is Europe's leading platform dedicated to inspiring active, healthy lives by offering access to thousands of fitness and wellness experiences across Europe. With the recent union with Wellhub, we're building the world's largest wellbeing ecosystem, connecting 39,000 corporate clients and 97,000 wellness partners across 18 countries. This is an exciting time to join a global team making a real impact in a fast-growing market.\n\nWe are building the most innovative Creative Studio in the wellbeing industry, a place where human creativity and machine intelligence work together. As a Senior AI Creative Expert, you will be instrumental in strengthening the intelligence layer of our Creative Studio. This involves embedding AI, testing, and creative measurement across all campaigns, channels, and departments. You'll combine design craft, systems thinking, and technological curiosity to empower our teams to create smarter, faster, and more insight-driven work. This is an expert-level, hands-on Individual Contributor role, not a people management position, ideal for someone with deep creative experience and a passion for applying AI to real business challenges. You will report to the Head of Creative Studio and collaborate closely with Growth, Brand Marketing, Content Intelligence, Product Design, and external creative partners.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Build and maintain AI workflows, prompt libraries, and automation scripts that accelerate creative production and quality.\n- Explore emerging tools (text-to-image, motion, layout, video, localization) and translate them into practical applications for design and content teams.\n- Define brand-safe guardrails and ensure AI-assisted work consistently reflects the USC identity.\n- Define creative KPIs and measurement frameworks with Growth, Brand, to link creativity to performance outcomes, and summarizing insights in monthly reports.\n- Support Performance Marketing and CRM teams with scalable, data-informed creative solutions and collaborate with Content Intelligence on aligning visual experimentation with narrative and message testing.\n- Curate and document the Studio's AI tool stack; establish governance and standards.\n- Conduct short trainings and internal demos to upskill designers, writers, marketers and collaborate with external creative partners to optimize asset production, localization, and quality assurance.\n- Build dashboards and trackers linking creative decisions to campaign engagement and ROI and help the wider Marketing organization understand which creative elements drive the strongest business results.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You have at least 7 years experience in digital design, creative direction, motion, or marketing technology — ideally spanning both brand and performance environments.\n- You are hands-on fluency with modern AI tools (Midjourney, Runway, Firefly, ChatGPT, Sora, etc.) and automation workflows.\n- You have a strong ability to interpret creative performance data (CTR, CVR, engagement metrics) and convert insights into visual direction.\n- You are an expert of Adobe Creative Cloud and Figma; working knowledge of collaboration and data tools (Notion, Sheets, Looker Studio a plus).\n- You have a strategic and structured mindset with high curiosity for innovation and experimentation.\n- You conduct excellent collaboration and communication skills across creative, technical, and business teams.\n- You bring excellent communication skills in English, German is a plus.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a truly unique opportunity for a Senior AI Creative Expert to shape the future of creative production within the dynamic wellbeing industry. You'll be at the forefront of integrating AI into a global platform, directly impacting how millions of users engage with health and fitness. Urban Sports Club, now part of Wellhub, offers a vibrant, inclusive culture that prioritizes employee well-being, continuous learning with a dedicated budget, generous flexible work options, and a fantastic L Pro Membership. If you're an innovative, hands-on expert passionate about leveraging AI for creative excellence and business impact, this role provides an unparalleled chance for growth and contribution in a company that truly values its people.
