**Company Description:**

At MedTrainer, we are transforming healthcare compliance and education with innovative technology solutions. Our platform simplifies compliance, credentialing, and learning management, helping healthcare organizations across the U.S. improve operations and focus on what matters most—patient care.

We’re looking for a **Healthcare Regulatory & Compliance Specialist I (HRCS I)** to join our Compliance team and contribute to the growth of our Compliance Library and knowledge base.

**Job Description:**

As a **Healthcare Regulatory & Compliance Specialist I**, you will:

*   Develop and update compliance templates, policies, checklists, competency assessments, and other related resources.
*   Research, interpret, and link federal and state regulations, accreditation standards, and evidence-based practices to MedTrainer solutions.
*   Create and maintain compliance documents, ensuring accuracy, consistency, and adherence to formatting and metadata standards.
*   Apply A.I. tools to enhance the creation, adaptation, and organization of compliance materials.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure the timely delivery of compliance projects and initiatives.
*   Take initiative to improve internal processes and adapt quickly to new technologies and priorities.

**Qualifications:**

What We’re Looking For

**Education & Certifications**

*   Bachelor’s degree in a related field (Required).
*   Master’s degree (Preferred).

**Experience**

*   Experience with healthcare compliance or privacy programs involving interpretation and application of regulations and standards of care.
*   Experience developing and/or implementing policies, procedures, or compliance resource documents.
*   Familiarity with A.I. tools for personal or professional use (Preferred).
*   Significant A.I. experience may be considered in lieu of healthcare knowledge if the candidate demonstrates the ability to learn and adapt quickly.

**Knowledge & Skills**

*   General understanding of healthcare regulations, compliance practices, and policy development.
*   Strong analytical and critical-thinking skills to interpret and apply regulations effectively.
*   Exceptional attention to detail, especially in context, format, and consistency.
*   Excellent English communication skills (speaking, reading, and writing).
*   Proficiency with Microsoft Office and adaptability to learn new software.
*   Highly organized, proactive, and able to work independently in a remote environment.
