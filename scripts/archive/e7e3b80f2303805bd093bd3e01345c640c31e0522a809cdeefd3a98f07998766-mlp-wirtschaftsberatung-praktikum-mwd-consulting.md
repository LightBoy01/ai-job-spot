### Description\nAre you eager to gain practical insights into financial consulting, develop an entrepreneurial mindset, and grow both personally and professionally? Then our internship program is exactly what you need! At MLP Wirtschaftsberatung, we offer you the chance to experience the diverse world of holistic financial planning and business consulting up close – right in the heart of Cologne.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Learn Customer Consulting**: Support our team in advising private clients – from analysis and planning to strategy development.\n- **Understand Market & Products**: Gain insights into topics such as investment, wealth accumulation, retirement planning, real estate financing, and business protection.\n- **Theory Meets Practice**: Apply theoretical knowledge from your studies in real consulting situations and receive feedback at eye level.\n- **Independent Work**: Gradually take on your own areas of responsibility and actively contribute to projects.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Currently studying Business Administration, Economics, Business Psychology, or a comparable field – career changers are also welcome.\n- Interest in economic contexts and topics such as finance, investment, or entrepreneurship.\n- Structured, independent work style with a high willingness to learn.\n- Eager to develop and take your first steps in professional life – possibly with a long-term perspective.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis internship at MLP Wirtschaftsberatung offers an excellent opportunity for students to dive deep into the world of financial and business consulting. With a strong emphasis on practical experience, mentorship, and personal development, it provides a solid foundation for a future career. The flexible hours and potential for a permanent role post-internship make this a particularly appealing entry point into a professional career in finance, supported by experienced professionals in a vibrant city.
