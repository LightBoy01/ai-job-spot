### Description

Are you planning your perfect career launch and seeking the ideal entry point into the media and creative world? If you are equally passionate about video, storytelling, and AI technologies and want to get to know RTL Deutschland, Germany's leading entertainment company, then our 18-month Trainee Program for Trailer Producing and AI Artist in Cologne is exactly what you're looking for.

This program is designed for you to experiment, learn, and simultaneously take responsibility for exciting projects. Through close collaboration with our Creative and Marketing teams, you will gain a comprehensive insight into the world of producing and AI innovation. After the program, you will be perfectly prepared for a career in the future of creative media production, with potential roles such as Creative Producer, Junior Producer, Digital Content Producer (with a focus on AI), AI Specialist in Video Production, or Innovation Manager for AI in Media Production.

### Responsibilities

*   Gain comprehensive insights into producing and AI innovation by collaborating closely with our creative and marketing teams.
*   Receive training in trailer editing (Adobe Premiere Pro), storytelling, and build upon your initial AI experiences (e.g., Runway, ChatGPT).
*   Develop creative campaign ideas and implement AI-supported trailer productions using tools like Impact Amplifier, Runway, Midjourney, and ChatGPT.
*   Support the conception and execution of video and marketing projects.
*   Experiment with modern AI tools for motion design, image manipulation, and animation (from Suno to VEO3 to AI plug-ins in After Effects).
*   Independently manage complete projects from idea to final delivery, integrating AI into your workflows.
*   Take responsibility for project tasks and act as the interface between creative and tech teams.

### Qualifications

*   You have recently completed or are about to complete a degree in Media, Communications, Film, or a comparable field.
*   You have initial practical experience in video production, marketing, or media (e.g., through internships or as a working student).
*   You are proficient with at least one editing program (Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or DaVinci Resolve) and have basic knowledge of motion/image editing (After Effects, Photoshop).
*   You possess a fundamental understanding of how to use AI tools (Runway, Impact Amplifier, Midjourney, ChatGPT, etc.).
*   You have a solid understanding of brand management, target audience communication, and digital media.
*   You are a creative storyteller with a talent for dramaturgy and emotional visual language.
*   You have a strong technical interest and a desire to dive deep into new AI technologies.
*   You are a strong communicator with a confident demeanor, organizational talent, independence, and a flexible, team-oriented mindset.
*   You have fluent German (C2) and good English (B2) language skills.

**Application Note:** To apply, we ask you to produce a video of a maximum of 1 minute in which you briefly introduce yourself and address how you have used AI in the past. Please upload the video to a service like Dropbox and include the link in a document with your application.

### Author's Take

**Q1: The title "AI Artist" is fascinating. Beyond just learning to operate new tools, how does RTL truly foster creative artistry in this role, and what does success look like for a trainee blending these two worlds?**

"That's a great question, and it gets to the heart of what we're trying to build here. We see AI not as a replacement for creativity, but as a super-powered co-pilot. Success for a trainee isn't just about mastering the prompts for Midjourney or Runway; it's about developing a unique 'AI-augmented creative fingerprint.' We want you to bring your vision, your sense of timing, your understanding of what makes a story resonate emotionally. The AI is your paintbrush, your camera, your effects suite—all rolled into one. We foster this by encouraging experimentation. Some of your wildest ideas might not work, and that's okay. We celebrate the learning that comes from pushing the boundaries. Success, by the end of the 18 months, is when you're not just executing tasks, but you're proactively pitching ideas that wouldn't have been possible a year ago, seamlessly blending your innate storytelling talent with the incredible potential of these new technologies. You'll have become the go-to person who can bridge the gap between a wild creative concept and a stunning, deliverable reality."

**Q2: The program description lists several potential career paths. Can you share an example of how a former trainee leveraged this program to carve out their specific niche and make a tangible impact at RTL?**

"Absolutely. I'm thinking of a trainee from a recent cohort who started with a solid foundation in video editing but was intensely curious about generative video. The program gave her the space and resources to go deep. She started by using an emerging AI tool for a small internal branding project—something low-stakes. But her work was so compelling and innovative that a senior producer noticed. Soon, she was invited to contribute to a major campaign for one of our flagship shows. She ended up creating a sequence that became a signature part of the promotion, something the team simply didn't have the time or resources to create traditionally. She’s now a Junior Creative Producer, specializing in AI-driven visuals. Her story is a perfect example of our culture: this program isn't just a rigid curriculum. It's a launchpad. We give you the tools and the network, and if you bring the curiosity and drive, you can create your own trajectory and make a visible impact much faster than you would in a traditional entry-level role."
