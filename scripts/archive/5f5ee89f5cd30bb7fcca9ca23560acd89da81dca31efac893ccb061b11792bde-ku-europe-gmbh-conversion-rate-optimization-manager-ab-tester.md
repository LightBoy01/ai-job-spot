### Description

We’re seeking a detail-oriented and data-driven Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Specialist / A/B Tester to join our growing team. You’ll be responsible for analyzing user behavior, running A/B and multivariate tests, and implementing strategies to improve conversion rates across our digital platforms.

### Responsibilities

*   Plan, execute, and analyze A/B and multivariate tests to optimize landing pages, checkout flows, and user experience.
*   Use data and insights to identify growth opportunities and improve website conversion rates.
*   Collaborate with marketing, design, and development teams to implement and test hypotheses.
*   Monitor user behavior through analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics, Hotjar, GA4, or similar).
*   Create detailed reports on test performance and insights.
*   Continuously refine and prioritize testing roadmaps to drive measurable results.

### Qualifications

*   Minimum 3 years of professional experience in conversion rate optimization, A/B testing, and digital experimentation.
*   **SUBMISSION OF PORTFOLIO REQUIRED:** We require proof that you have conducted A/B tests in the past. Theoretical knowledge is not sufficient. You must have hands-on experience with A/B testing in WordPress/WooCommerce and Shopify, including testing different product prices.
*   Hands-on experience with testing tools.
*   Understanding of UX principles, customer journeys, and e-commerce funnels.
*   Experience collaborating with designers and developers to implement test variations.
*   Strong attention to detail, data interpretation, and communication skills.
*   Ability to work independently in a remote environment.
*   Must be available to work in the CET time zone.
*   Good command of English (written and spoken).
