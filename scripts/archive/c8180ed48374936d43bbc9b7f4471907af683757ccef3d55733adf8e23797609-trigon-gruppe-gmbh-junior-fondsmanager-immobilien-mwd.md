### Description

Trigon Unternehmensgruppe is a well-established real estate group with decades of success in the development, realization, and management of residential, commercial, and hotel properties in central city locations. They expertly manage approximately 4,000 residential and 300 commercial units, including parking spaces, ensuring the long-term value preservation of their real estate portfolio through dedicated facility and building technology management.

They are currently seeking a highly motivated **(Junior) Real Estate Fund Manager (m/w/d)** for a permanent full-time position to strengthen their engaged team. In this exciting role, you will assume responsibility for their closed real estate funds and actively contribute to the sustained success of their growing real estate portfolio.

### Responsibilities

*   Support fund management in the operational and administrative supervision of real estate funds.
*   Organize and manage the secretariat, ensuring a smooth and efficient flow of information.
*   Coordinate appointments, manage follow-ups, and ensure timely adherence to deadlines.
*   Serve as a key interface between the executive management, internal departments, and external partners.
*   Process entries in the commercial and land registers, and provide support for corporate law matters.
*   Prepare, organize, and meticulously minute shareholder meetings and advisory board meetings.
*   Undertake general administrative tasks to support the efficient functioning of fund management operations.

### Qualifications

*   A completed degree, for example, in Business Administration (BWL), Real Estate Management, or Business Administration.
*   Initial to several years of professional experience in fund or investment management is highly desirable.
*   Good knowledge of closed real estate funds and their specific regulatory requirements.
*   Demonstrated high degree of independence, structured working approach, and reliability.
*   Strong communication skills and a genuine enjoyment of collaborative teamwork.
