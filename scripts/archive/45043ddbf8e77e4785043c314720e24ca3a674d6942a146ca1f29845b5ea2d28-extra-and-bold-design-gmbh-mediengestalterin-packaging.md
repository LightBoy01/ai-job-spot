### Description\nExtra & Bold is a dedicated packaging design agency for the food retail and FMCG sectors. We embrace a holistic approach to packaging, from the initial concept to final implementation. Our specialized teams leverage clear processes and modern technology to create and realize packaging solutions that are compelling on the shelf and resonate with consumers. We focus on well-conceived design processes, precise artwork creation, and technical production preparation to ensure maximum brand impact and efficient time-to-market. Our culture is defined by respectful collaboration, flat hierarchies, and diverse projects for renowned clients, all within an environment that fosters creativity, innovation, efficiency, and team spirit. We offer a responsible and varied role focusing on consumer goods, a supportive interdisciplinary team, flexible working hours through a flextime model, and the option for 100% home office or working at our modern office location.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Create packaging designs in accordance with corporate design guidelines or layout specifications.\n*   Independently produce final artwork and print-ready data for various packaging materials (e.g., labels, folding cartons, cans, trays, flow wraps).\n*   Implement language and variant adaptations.\n*   Technically review and prepare data according to the requirements of different printing processes (flexo, gravure, offset).\n*   Apply client-specific guidelines and legal requirements (e.g., LMIV, Cosmetics Regulation).\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed vocational training as a Media Designer for Digital and Print or a comparable degree.\n*   Several years of professional experience in artwork creation and prepress.\n*   At least 2 years of experience in the packaging sector (within the last 5 years).\n*   Very good knowledge of Adobe Illustrator.\n*   Experience with Esko software (e.g., Deskpack, ArtPro+) is an advantage.\n*   In-depth knowledge of packaging printing and the requirements of various printing processes.\n*   Structured, precise way of working and a high degree of quality awareness.\n*   Good communication skills, team orientation, and a high level of personal responsibility.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a skilled Packaging Designer who thrives on technical precision and creative impact within the FMCG sector. Extra & Bold offers a truly supportive and flexible environment, evident in their emphasis on respectful collaboration, flat hierarchies, and the highly attractive 100% home office option. You'll tackle diverse projects for well-known brands, applying your expertise from concept to print-ready data. If you're a meticulous, team-oriented professional with a passion for packaging and a desire for continuous growth, this role provides an excellent blend of autonomy, challenge, and work-life balance.
