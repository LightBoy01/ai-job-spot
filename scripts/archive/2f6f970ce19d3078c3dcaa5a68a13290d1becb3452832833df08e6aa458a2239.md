**Company Description:**

**LabV** is a Munich-based software company and a member of the NETZSCH Group, specializing in AI-powered data management solutions for materials research, quality control, and process optimization. Founded in 2022, LabV offers a cloud-based platform designed to transform industrial laboratories into intelligent data hubs, enhancing efficiency and product quality across various sectors.

**Job Description:**

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Develop and maintain frontend applications using **React**, **Next.js**, and **TypeScript**
    
*   Collaborate with designers and product teams to implement high-quality UI/UX
    
*   Lead or mentor other developers in the team, promoting best practices and code quality
    
*   Work closely with stakeholders to understand business needs and translate them into technical solutions
    
*   Contribute to the continuous improvement of the development process and toolset
    
*   Leverage tools like **Figma** for design handoff and feedback
    
*   Explore and integrate emerging tools, including basic AI-assisted development tools (e.g., **Cursor**)
    

**Qualifications:**

**Requirements**

*   5+ years of experience in frontend development
    
*   Strong proficiency in **JavaScript**, **TypeScript**, **React**, and **Next.js**
    
*   Experience leading or mentoring teams (formally or informally)
    
*   Solid understanding of **UI/UX principles** and hands-on experience with **Figma**
    
*   Familiarity with **AI development tools** like **Cursor**, or an interest in exploring them
    
*   **B2B experience**, preferably in **non-digital industries** (manufacturing, logistics, healthcare, etc.)
