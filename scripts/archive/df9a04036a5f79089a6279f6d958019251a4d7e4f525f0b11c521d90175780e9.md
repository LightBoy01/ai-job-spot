**Product Owner**

_Join our Product Management team in shaping the future of AI-powered shopping experiences across our global software platform._

**The Company You’ll Join**

At Rebuy, we're on a mission to revolutionize shopping with intelligent, personalized experiences that wow customers around the globe. As a fully remote team, we power some of the fastest growing DTC brands like Aviator Nation, Liquid Death, Magic Spoon, Blenders, Laird Superfoods, Primal Kitchen, and many more.

We believe in ownership, drive, and empathy and strongly uphold that every team member plays a vital role in shaping the future of intelligent commerce. Our culture thrives on collaboration, creativity, and genuine passion. We don’t just build great tech - we build lasting partnerships, a strong community, and a place where people love to work.

**The Team You’ll Join**

We're a tight-knit, driven team that’s obsessed with team work and excellence. In this role, you’ll be reporting to John Erck, who is our CEO & Head of Product. If you thrive on curiosity, crave a challenge, and never stop learning, you’ll feel right at home here. Our flat structure means no red tape, just real impact. Everyone rolls up their sleeves and contributes directly to our mission.

We reward initiative, celebrate high performance, and believe leadership is earned through action. Strong work ethic, smart prioritization, and clear communication are must-haves. Whether you're flying solo or teaming up across departments and with customers, you'll play a key role in moving us forward.

**The Problems You’ll Solve**

Rebuy and its team members continually strive to create a high-spirited, intentional work environment that stresses performance productivity as well as collaboration and merit.

As a Product Owner**,** you’ll be joining our small and powerful team! You will be a valuable asset to the Product team by utilizing your excellent and innate product instincts and killer prioritization skills. You will find yourself working collaboratively across the org, with departments such as Engineering, Sales, Marketing and Support to ensure customer initiatives and roadmap deliverables are being met.

_In this role, you will:_

*   Participate in all phases of the product life cycle from requirements definition through delivery to production.
    
*   Monitor and evaluate product progress at each stage of the process and communicate with the applicable stakeholders to build a shared understanding of project statuses along with managing expectations accordingly.
    
*   Collaborate with key stakeholders to define requirements and understand the business justification and supporting use-cases.
    
*   Create, maintain and prioritize the product backlog for the development team(s).
    
*   Act as a liaison with the product team and end-users to deliver updates.
    
*   Interface directly with customers to demo new functionality, assist with feature implementation and configuration, solicit feedback, and understand their challenges.
    
*   Conduct supporting research and competitive analysis; where applicable.
    

**Who You Are**

We’re stoked to meet you and get to learn more about you, your experience and your interest in joining our team.

_Must haves:_

*   3+ years of product management or product-oriented engineering role.
    
*   Identify new product opportunities, create and own product roadmap from concept to launch.
    
*   Hands-on experience designing and managing third-party API integrations (GraphQL & REST).
    
*   Familiarity with data extraction and transformation concepts.
    
*   Strong backlog and delivery discipline, and familiarity with Agile/Scrum environments.
    
*   Accomplished working effectively with cross functional teams, leading discussions and collaborating with key stakeholders.
    
*   Solid technical background with understanding and/or hands-on experience in software development and web technologies.
    
*   Experience maintaining documentation for both internal developers and any potential external consumers.
    
*   Excellent listening, communication and interpersonal skills, both written and verbal.
    

_Nice to haves:_

*   Working knowledge of SQL and basic data modelling.
    
*   Proficiency with AI-first tooling (ChatGPT, [v0.dev](http://v0.dev) or equivalents).
    
*   An excitement to help shape the future of intelligent ecommerce and be part of a fast-growing SaaS company.
    
*   Strong ability to work independently and collaboratively with internal and external teams.
    
*   Passion to problem solve and willingness to roll up your sleeves to get the job done.
    
*   Experience working within a remote environment.
