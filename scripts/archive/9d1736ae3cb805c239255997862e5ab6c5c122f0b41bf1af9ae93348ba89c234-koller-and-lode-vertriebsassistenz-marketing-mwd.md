### Description\nKoller & Lode is a leading partner for craft businesses, helping them secure more orders and attract suitable regional employees. With a modern approach, they ensure full order books and strong teams for their clients. Located in Würzburg-Lengfeld, they are seeking motivated individuals to join their team as an **Inside Sales Representative**. This role is ideal for someone passionate about learning sales from the ground up and contributing to the success of craft businesses.\n\n### Responsibilities\n* Proactively acquire potential new customers.\n* Initiate first contact with prospective clients.\n* Conduct initial discussions to understand customer needs and identify opportunities.\n* Reach out to existing contacts within the system via telephone.\n* Plan, prepare, and follow up on appointments for the strategy consultants.\n\n### Qualifications\n* Strong desire and willingness to learn sales fundamentals from scratch.\n* Ability for quick comprehension and a high degree of self-discipline.\n* Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written, with a keen eye for detail.\n* Impeccable and error-free written German language skills.\n* Capacity to adhere to and effectively follow established company processes.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding entry-level opportunity for an ambitious individual eager to kickstart a career in sales. Koller & Lode offers a highly supportive environment, evident through their structured onboarding with a personal mentor and excellent, company-funded further training opportunities. The attractive salary package, modern tech (Apple MacBook), and a vibrant team culture with regular events highlight their commitment to employee well-being and growth. The chance to quickly advance and gain exclusive expert knowledge makes this role a unique launchpad for long-term professional development. If you're disciplined, communicate well, and ready to learn, this team offers the perfect platform to thrive and make a tangible impact on local craft businesses.
