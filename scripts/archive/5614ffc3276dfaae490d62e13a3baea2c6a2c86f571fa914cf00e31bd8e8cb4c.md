**Job Title:** Deloitte GenAI Director

**Location:** Pan India

**Experience:** 10+ Years

**Company:** Deloitte

**Job Summary:**

As a Director in Deloitte’s GenAI practice, you will spearhead the development and deployment of cutting-edge generative AI solutions, driving innovation and business transformation for clients across industries. You will lead cross-functional teams, shape AI strategies, and ensure the delivery of scalable, responsible, and impactful AI initiatives aligned with organizational goals.

**Key Responsibilities:**

*   Lead the strategic vision and execution of generative AI (GenAI) initiatives across Deloitte and client organizations.
*   Drive innovation in AI by leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs), generative models, and related AI technologies to solve complex business problems.
*   Oversee the design, development, and implementation of scalable GenAI solutions and frameworks.
*   Collaborate with clients, partners, and internal teams to identify AI opportunities and develop tailored AI strategies.
*   Ensure ethical AI practices, governance, and compliance in all GenAI projects.
*   Build and mentor high-performing teams of AI experts, data scientists, and engineers.
*   Represent Deloitte as a thought leader in AI through industry forums, conferences, and publications.
*   Manage stakeholder relationships at senior levels and drive business growth through AI service offerings.

**Required Qualifications:**

**Education:**

*   Master’s or PhD in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, or related field.

**Experience:**

*   10+ years of experience in AI/ML, with significant exposure to generative AI and LLMs.
*   Proven leadership experience managing large-scale AI projects and teams.
*   Strong background in AI strategy development and execution.

**Skills:**

*   Expertise in AI/ML frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, etc.) and programming languages such as Python.
*   Deep understanding of NLP, large language models, and generative AI technologies.
*   Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) and AI infrastructure.
*   Excellent strategic thinking, communication, and stakeholder management skills.
*   Strong focus on responsible AI, ethics, and compliance.

**Preferred Qualifications:**

*   Experience in consulting or professional services firms.
*   Track record of driving AI innovation and business transformation.
*   Familiarity with emerging AI technologies and industry trends.
*   Strong publication or patent record in AI or related fields is a plus.
