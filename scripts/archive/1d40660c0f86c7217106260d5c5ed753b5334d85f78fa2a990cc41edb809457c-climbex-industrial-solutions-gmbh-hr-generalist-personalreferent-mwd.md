### Description
Climbex has been a leader in industrial cleaning services for over 25 years, specializing in tailored solutions for industrial production facilities, biogas fermenters, and automated crude oil tank cleaning. With more than 22 years of specific experience, we are committed to the highest standards of efficiency, safety, and sustainability, leveraging modern technology and an experienced team to deliver reliable and professional cleaning solutions that meet our customers' demanding requirements.

Our core values drive everything we do:
*   **Safety & Quality**: Upholding the highest standards and efficient processes for superior results.
*   **Sustainability**: Employing resource-efficient and environmentally friendly procedures.
*   **Team Spirit & Responsibility**: Valuing every voice, fostering self-responsibility, continuous learning, and respectful collaboration.

We are seeking an HR-Generalist / Personalreferent (m/w/d) to join our team as soon as possible, contributing to our continued success by supporting our most valuable asset: our people. We offer a fair compensation, an unlimited employment contract, employee events, and extensive internal and external training opportunities. At Climbex, you'll find flat hierarchies, open communication, and a motivated team, with further benefits continuously being planned.

### Responsibilities
As an HR-Generalist / Personalreferent, your key responsibilities will include:
*   Providing comprehensive support and advice to employees and management on all HR-related topics.
*   Independently managing the full employee lifecycle administration, from onboarding to offboarding (including contracts, references, and certificates).
*   Assisting in the recruitment and successful onboarding of new team members.
*   Organizing and facilitating personnel development and further training initiatives.
*   Maintaining and administering HR master data and HR systems efficiently.
*   Actively participating in HR projects and internal process optimization (e.g., digitalization, employer branding, employee retention).

### Qualifications
To be successful in this role, you should possess:
*   A completed commercial apprenticeship with a focus on HR, a degree in HR, or a comparable qualification.
*   Several years of experience in human resources, ideally as an HR Generalist or Personnel Officer.
*   Proficiency in MS-Office; experience with HR software is an advantage.
*   Excellent German language skills (both written and spoken); English skills are desirable.
*   Strong communication and organizational abilities, coupled with a proactive, hands-on mentality.
*   An independent, reliable, and solution-oriented approach to your work.
*   A team-oriented attitude, resilience, and a high degree of responsibility.
