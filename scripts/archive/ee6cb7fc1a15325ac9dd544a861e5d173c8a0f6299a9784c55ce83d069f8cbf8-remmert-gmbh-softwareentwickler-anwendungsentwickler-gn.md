We excel in designing space-saving, fully automated solutions for mechanical engineering, automation, and logistics – a future-proof industry. We foster a culture of open communication, short decision paths, and continuous development, always seeking colleagues who want to contribute to our growing team. As a Software Developer (m/f/d), you will be crucial in the new development of our Warehouse Management Software. Remmert truly invests in its employees, offering not only continuous development opportunities through regular feedback and a comprehensive training catalog but also exceptional benefits that promote work-life balance and well-being. From generous leave and company pension schemes to health management and regular team events with family, Remmert demonstrates a strong commitment to its people.
