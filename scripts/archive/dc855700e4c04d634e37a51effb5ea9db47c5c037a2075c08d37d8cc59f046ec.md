**Get to Know Us**

[Horizon3.ai](http://Horizon3.ai) is a fast-growing, remote cybersecurity company dedicated to the mission of enabling organizations to proactively find, fix and verify exploitable attack vectors before criminals exploit them. Our flagship product, the NodeZeroTM platform, delivers production-safe autonomous pentests and other key assessment operations that scale across the largest internal, external, cloud, and hybrid cloud environments. NodeZero has been adopted by organizations of all sizes, from small educational institutions to government agencies and Global 100 enterprises. It is used by IT Ops/SecOps teams, consulting pentesters, and MSSPs and MSPs.

We are a fusion of former U.S. Special Operations cyber operators, startup engineers & operators, and formerly frustrated cybersecurity practitioners. We're committed to helping solve our common security problems: ineffective security tools and false positives, resulting in alert fatigue, blind spots, "checkbox” security culture, cybersecurity skills shortage, and the long lead time and expense of hiring outside consultants. Collectively, we are a team of learn it alls, committed to a culture of respect, collaboration, ownership, and results.

As a remote first company, we require minimum 25Mbps consumer grade broadband connection.

**What You’ll Do**

We’re seeking a visionary **Head of Brand & Creative** — a bold storyteller, brand architect, and creative leader who understands AI at a deep level and can translate it into market-defining narratives that command attention and drive belief.leader who thrives at both setting strategy and ensuring operational excellence with an eye on AI.

You’ll be responsible for defining, amplifying, and protecting the [Horizon3.ai](http://Horizon3.ai) brand — ensuring we are not just seen as an AI company, but as **the AI-first security company**. You’ll own the creative direction, brand architecture, and narrative strategy that puts us in the **AI hype zone — with credibility, speed, and style.**

You’ll also help transform how marketing operates — **building an AI-native creative and brand organization** that runs at a **10:1 efficiency ratio**, using AI to automate, augment, and accelerate every part of the marketing lifecycle.

### **Key Responsibilities**

**Brand Strategy & Positioning**

*   Architect the evolution of [Horizon3.ai](http://Horizon3.ai)’s brand to clearly position us as the AI-first leader in cybersecurity.
    
*   Build a bold, differentiated narrative that connects [Horizon3.ai](http://Horizon3.ai)’s technology and mission to the larger AI revolution.

### **Creative Direction & Storytelling**

*   Lead all aspects of brand creative — from identity systems and visual design to campaign concepts, product launches, and thought leadership.
    
*   Create emotionally resonant, high-impact content that earns attention and drives influence across audiences (customers, media, investors, and talent).
    
*   Drive an editorial and visual standard that reflects innovation, confidence, and authenticity — the tone of a true AI pioneer.
    

**AI Driven Marketing Transformation**

*   Reimagine how brand and creative teams operate by integrating AI tools and workflows across every discipline — copywriting, design, video, and operations.

**Brand Experience & Integration**

*   Ensure brand coherence across every touchpoint — digital, product, events, campaigns, and internal communications.
    
*   Partner with growth, product marketing, and field teams to align creative strategy with GTM objectives.
    
*   Lead brand guardianship to maintain consistency as the company scales globally.
    

**Leadership & Collaboration**

*   Build, mentor, and inspire a world-class creative team that combines strategic thinking, design excellence, and technical curiosity.
