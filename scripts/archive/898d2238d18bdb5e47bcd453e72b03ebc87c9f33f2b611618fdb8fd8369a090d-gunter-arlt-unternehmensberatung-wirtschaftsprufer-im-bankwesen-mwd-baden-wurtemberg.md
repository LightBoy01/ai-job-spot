### Description\nThis organization actively shapes the banking sector and contributes significantly to the stability of the financial system. They are seeking an experienced **Chartered Accountant (Wirtschaftsprüfer)**, full-time or part-time, to join a central auditing function. This role offers a versatile and responsible scope of tasks with deep insights into the structures and processes of regional credit institutions, providing a secure environment with substantial freedom for personal responsibility, flexibility, and engagement.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Independently conduct annual financial statement audits at credit institutions within the association's region.\n- Provide technical support to the field audit service and contribute to the further development of auditing concepts.\n- Supervise specific technical areas and collaborate closely with interdisciplinary teams.\n- Represent the auditing department in working groups, projects, and expert committees.\n- Act as a lecturer at specialist conferences and seminars in cooperation with financial industry educational institutions.\n- After the initial onboarding phase, assume responsibility as the primarily designated Chartered Accountant for several institutions.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully passed the **Chartered Accountant (Wirtschaftsprüfer) exam** and possess several years of practical experience in auditing credit institutions.\n- Demonstrate a structured, analytical, and goal-oriented approach to work, even when facing complex issues.\n- Possess the ability to work independently while also exhibiting a strong team spirit.\n- Show high communication skills, organizational talent, and social competence.\n- Be willing to undertake occasional travel within the region.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a seasoned Chartered Accountant looking to make a substantial impact within the financial sector. The role is positioned within an organization that prioritizes stability, individual responsibility, and continuous professional development. With flexible working hours, options for mobile work, and comprehensive training, the company clearly invests in its employees' well-being and growth. The chance to not only conduct audits but also to shape concepts, represent the department, and even lecture, offers a unique blend of deep technical work and thought leadership. It's a secure and dynamic environment for experts who value both autonomy and collaborative excellence.
