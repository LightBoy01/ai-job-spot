### Description\nDigistore24 is one of Europe's fastest-growing tech companies, driven by a mission to empower people to share their knowledge online and fulfill their entrepreneurial dreams. We are expanding our teams sustainably, focusing on experts and strong personalities who share our values, regardless of location. We are looking for an IT Support Specialist who enjoys supporting our global team in both German and English, has experience with modern technology, and can clearly explain technical matters.\n\nThis role offers the stability of a highly successful German high-tech company that has preserved its start-up spirit. You'll enjoy 100% home office, provided you have stable internet, regular on-the-job training, and the chance to work with an international team that values strong cohesion and holds regular team events across Europe. We offer modern technical equipment, autonomy from day one, an informal \"du\" culture, and no dress code.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Provide professional IT support to employees worldwide via a ticket system.\n- Standardize and unify company services, enhancing password security and implementing 2FA.\n- Integrate utilized SaaS services into Okta.\n- Identify and eliminate redundant services to achieve cost reductions.\n- Manage (Mobile) Device Management and ensure IT security across all services.\n- Work independently and take ownership within the ITSM team.\n- Develop, evolve, and maintain company-wide IT services to high quality standards.\n- Continuously develop and maintain IT services with a focus on data protection, legal certainty, user-friendliness, automation, and security.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A genuine passion for user support and efficiently solving problems for our team.\n- Ability to maintain an overview of numerous services and simplify them effectively.\n- Proficiency in monitoring and analyzing existing services and systems.\n- A solution-oriented approach to work with a keen eye for detail.\n- Exceptional communication and empathy skills.\n- **MUST HAVE:** Proficient knowledge of operating systems including Windows, Linux, and MacOS.\n- **MUST HAVE:** Native or C2 level fluency in both German and English.\n- **Nice to have:** Prior experience with Single-Sign-On systems, ideally OKTA.\n- **Nice to have:** Knowledge of IT security principles, such as 2FA, Single-Sign-On, Audits, and Compliance.\n\n**This job is NOT for you
