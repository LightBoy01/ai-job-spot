### Description\nDataSolid GmbH operates modern, scalable IT infrastructures, including both Windows and Linux-based systems for our corporate group. This internship offers a unique opportunity to actively participate in a critical infrastructure transformation project: migrating our existing virtualization environments from VMware to Proxmox. You will gain hands-on experience managing and migrating real, productive systems, working closely with experienced professionals in a dynamic and innovative technology environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Build and Configuration of a Hosted Linux System**\n  - Installation & basic configuration (Debian/Ubuntu)\n  - Network, storage, and security configuration\n  - Setup of Proxmox VE as a virtualization platform\n- **Migration of Existing VMware Environments to Proxmox**\n  - Analysis of the existing infrastructure\n  - VM conversion (Windows, Linux)\n  - Testing, optimization, and documentation of the migration\n- **Migration of an Ubuntu-based Open-Source ERP System**\n  - Analysis of the existing PHP-based ERP instance\n  - Preparation and execution of the migration\n  - Backup & transfer of databases (MySQL/MariaDB)\n  - System validation and functional testing after migration\n- **Migration and Setup of Standard Windows Clients**\n  - Preparation of images\n  - Driver and network configuration\n  - Integration into existing networks after migration\n- **Documentation & Knowledge Transfer**\n  - Creation of technical documentation\n  - Handover to the internal IT team\n\n### Qualifications\n- Enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's program in Computer Science, Business Informatics, IT Systems Engineering, or a comparable field\n- Basic knowledge in Linux and Windows administration\n- Initial experience with virtualization (e.g., VMware, VirtualBox, KVM, Proxmox)\n- Interest in server and infrastructure topics\n- Advantageous, but not mandatory:\n  - Basic knowledge in PHP or web server technologies\n  - Experience with MySQL/MariaDB\n- Analytical thinking, structured working style, and eagerness to work with real systems\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an IT student eager to gain hands-on experience with critical infrastructure projects. You'll be instrumental in a major virtualization migration, working autonomously on modern systems while collaborating with seasoned experts. The role offers flexible hours, potential remote work, and excellent chances for a full-time position, making it a powerful start to your career in system engineering. Kickstart your career at DataSolid GmbH and expand your knowledge in a dynamic technology team!
