Udemy Business is at the forefront of global workforce transformation, partnering with organizations around the world to close skills gaps and drive business outcomes through learning. As part of Udemy, we’re committed to helping people and organizations thrive in a rapidly changing world.

**About the Role**

We are seeking a strategic, innovative, and data-driven Director of Growth & Retention to serve as the Product Line Owner for critical metrics driving the success of Udemy Business. This leader will own and accelerate growth and retention across our global portfolio, with a mandate to maximize ARR and NDRR. You will be instrumental in defining our enterprise product strategy, leading key product initiatives, and crafting seamless upgrade sequences and packaging solutions for our customers.

You will lead a team of talented product managers, work cross-functionally with senior partners in design, research, sales, and customer success, and influence both direct and indirect teams toward shared business goals. You will champion a culture of innovation, inclusion, and career development while translating strategy into tangible results.

**What You’ll Do**

*   Serve as the Product Line Owner for Growth & Retention across Udemy Business, responsible for key business metrics (ARR, NDRR, adoption, and engagement).
*   Develop and execute product strategies that drive net new growth, upsell, and retention within a global B2B environment.
*   Own packaging and pricing strategy in close collaboration with Sales, Marketing, and Finance, leveraging segmentation and market insights to design value-based offerings and seamless upgrade paths.
*   Partner closely with research teams, engaging directly with customers to deeply understand industry needs, business context, and user behavior; use these insights to inform and validate product strategy.
*   Use data, experiments, and customer feedback to define and iterate on product initiatives that accelerate adoption and deliver measurable ROI for customers.
*   Partner deeply with design, user research, engineering, and GTM teams to deliver exceptional enterprise customer experiences.
*   Drive global product launches and enhance enterprise readiness, with an understanding of the unique needs of multinational organizations.
*   Serve as a thought partner and trusted leader to executive stakeholders, delivering clear recommendations, dashboards, and business cases to support investment decisions.
*   Lead, develop, and inspire a team of product managers, fostering a high-performance, inclusive team culture, and investing in career growth.
*   Influence broader cross-functional teams and establish alignment across key partners to ensure execution of growth and retention strategies at scale.
*   Stay informed about emerging technologies (including AI and automation) and trends in learning and talent tech, proposing ways to leverage innovation for measurable business impact.

**About You**

*   8+ years of product management experience in B2B SaaS organizations, including at least 3 years in a leadership role managing product teams.
*   Proven success developing and executing growth and retention strategies with demonstrable impact on ARR and NDRR.
*   Expertise in packaging, pricing, customer segmentation, and lifecycle management for enterprise software.
*   Highly data-driven, with demonstrated ability to turn insights into action and experimentation at scale.
*   Strong facilitator and influencer, able to align cross-functional partners and present clearly to executive audiences.
*   Experience working with global or multi-region products and navigating the complexity of launching and scaling in diverse markets.
*   Passion for developing people and building a healthy, high-performing culture.
*   Familiarity with AI and its application in enterprise/SaaS contexts.
*   Experience with learning, HR tech, or workforce transformation technology is highly preferred.
*   Excellent organizational, strategic thinking, and communication skills.
*   Ability to flex working hours on a part time basis to achieve partial overlap with stakeholders in US
