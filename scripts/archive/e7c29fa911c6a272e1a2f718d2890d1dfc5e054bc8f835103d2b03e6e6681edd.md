Position Overview 

_Are you a life-long learner, curious about the world around you?_  

_Are you passionate about inclusion and the profound impact of global learning?_  

_Are you ready to bring your unique skills to a mission-driven organization?_ 

If so, we would love to welcome you to our global community! 

We are searching for a Data Governance Analyst to join IFSA. This role will oversee the management, integrity, and security of student and partner data within IFSA's global study abroad programs. In this role, you will establish and maintain data policies, ensure regulatory compliance (e.g., FERPA, GDPR), and support teams in using data effectively and ethically.  

The Institute for Study Abroad (IFSA) was founded in 1988 as a provider of study abroad programming for U.S. undergraduates. With student success in mind, the organization creates global learning environments designed to foster the development of critical perspectives, knowledge and skills essential for students to thrive in the future. Offering more than 100 programs in 19 countries, we draw upon theory and evidence-based practice to create a variety of program models and flexible academic options in alignment with the needs of partners and students. Our commitments to inclusive excellence, intercultural agility, individualized learning and enduring impact are present in every aspect of our programs. We are committed to building an inclusive and equitable organization and we look for positive-minded, adaptable people from diverse communities and cultures to help us transform our sector and change the lives of the students we serve.  

In this position, your responsibilities would include: 

*   Data Quality & Integrity
    *   Define and implement data quality standards and processes.
    *   Identify and resolve data inconsistencies across platforms.
    *   Monitor and maintain accurate student, partner, and academic records.
*   Policy & Compliance
    *   Develop data governance policies in alignment with privacy regulations (e.g., FERPA, GDPR, HIPAA, EUAI Act).
    *   Work with legal and compliance teams to manage data-sharing agreements with global institutions.
*   Data Stewardship
    *   Act as a liaison between departments (e.g., Enrollment, Academics, IT) to ensure data accountability.
    *   Document data flows and manage metadata repositories or dictionaries.
*   Education & Training
    *   Train staff on data handling best practices and data protection responsibilities.
    *   Create clear documentation for data procedures and governance protocols.
*   Technology & Tools
    *   Collaborate with IT to manage permissions, access control, and data architecture in systems such as CRMs, student information systems, or study abroad platforms.
    *   Support system audits and participate in data migration or integration projects as needed.
*   Other duties and projects, as assigned

Requirements, Work Experience and Education 

*   Bachelor’s degree in Data Management, Information Systems, Higher Education Administration, or related field.
*   2+ years of experience in data governance, compliance, or data stewardship.
*   Familiarity with education data regulations (FERPA, GDPR) is preferred.
*   Experience with CRMs, data visualization tools, or student information systems is a plus.
*   Familiarity with AI Platforms at the enterprise level preferred.
*   Demonstrated project coordination and workflow management skills, with the ability to track multiple deadlines, prioritize tasks, and maintain clear documentation
*   Keen attention to detail, particularly for compliance-oriented tasks
*   Tech fluency, adaptability and openness in learning new platforms
*   Demonstrated ability to work independently with a proactive approach to problem-solving and follow-up, especially in asynchronous environments
*   Strong oral and written communication skills and mature approach to communication
*   Ability to work effectively with multiple stakeholders from diverse communities and cultures 
*   Fluency in English, both written and verbal, to effectively collaborate across international teams.
*   Comfortable working in a mission-driven, student-centered environment.
*   Able to balance structure and flexibility in a fast-paced, small-team setting.
*   Interest in international education or global mobility is a plus.
*   Existing work authorization for the UK and physically reside in the UK
