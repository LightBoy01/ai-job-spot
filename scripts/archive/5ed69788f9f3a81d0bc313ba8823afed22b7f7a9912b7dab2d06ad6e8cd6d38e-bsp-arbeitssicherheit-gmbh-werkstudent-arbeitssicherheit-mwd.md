### Description

**Work safely. Act sustainably. Grow together.**

BSP Arbeitssicherheit GmbH is a vital part of the BERATER group – a modern network of consulting firms specializing in occupational safety, fire protection, environmental protection, and management systems. At BSP, you won't just find exciting projects, but also a highly motivated team dedicated to creating safe and sustainable working environments.

As a Working Student, you will be an integral part of our team, supporting daily operations and gaining deep, practical insights into the field of occupational safety. This role offers a unique opportunity to apply your academic knowledge in a real-world setting, contributing directly to the safety and well-being of workplaces.

### Responsibilities

*   Support in the maintenance and further development of risk assessments, training materials, and other essential documentation.
*   Actively participate in the organization and follow-up of instruction sessions and training courses.
*   Assist with the preparation of site inspections and audits.
*   Create and maintain evaluations, presentations, and reports.
*   Perform general administrative tasks within the occupational safety department.

### Qualifications

*   You are an enrolled student, ideally in Occupational Safety, Environmental Management, Engineering, or a related field of study.
*   Initial knowledge or experience in occupational safety is a plus.
*   You demonstrate a structured, diligent, and independent approach to your work.
*   You are proficient in MS Office applications and comfortable using various digital tools.
