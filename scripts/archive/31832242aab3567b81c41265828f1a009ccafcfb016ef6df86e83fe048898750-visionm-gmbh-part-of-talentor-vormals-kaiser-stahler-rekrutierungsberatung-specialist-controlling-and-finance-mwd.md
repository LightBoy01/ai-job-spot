### Description\nOur client, a medium-sized and internationally active group subsidiary, is seeking a \"Specialist Controlling & Finance\". If you have already gained initial experience in finance and controlling and wish to grow further in an international environment, this role offers an ideal opportunity to actively contribute and shape processes. The company boasts an excellent market position and reputation, offering attractive growth prospects with significant scope for personal contribution. Employees also benefit from diverse social benefits and flexible working models, including a home office component.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Production controlling, including the development and maintenance of modern controlling tools and reports for optimal management of the dynamic production environment.\n- Creation of standard cost roll-ups, precise variance analyses, and ensuring correct cost allocation.\n- Identification and analysis of deviations between target and actual values, clarification of inconsistencies and differences, proposal and coordination of necessary corrections.\n- Examination of production processes, identification of cost reduction and optimization potentials, derivation of concrete recommendations for action and tracking their implementation.\n- Collaboration in the planning, execution, and evaluation of inventories.\n- Support for financial accounting, e.g., with booking business transactions, payment runs and dunning, account reconciliation, and master data maintenance.\n- Participation in the creation of various statistics, evaluations, and other financial accounting tasks within daily operations.\n- Function as a business partner for cost center managers and department heads by providing support in financial matters.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You hold a relevant degree or commercial training with corresponding experience.\n- Ideally, you bring at least 2-3 years of professional experience in finance and/or controlling, a strong understanding of numbers, and an interest in production processes.\n- You have very good knowledge of Office tools and ERP systems, especially SAP FI/CO (preferably experience with PP/MM).\n- You speak fluent German and have good English skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced finance and controlling professional to join a stable, internationally renowned company. The role offers significant scope for impact, focusing on optimizing critical production processes and acting as a key business partner. With attractive growth prospects, a supportive culture that values contribution, and modern flexible working options, this position is ideal for someone looking to advance their career in a dynamic and international setting.
