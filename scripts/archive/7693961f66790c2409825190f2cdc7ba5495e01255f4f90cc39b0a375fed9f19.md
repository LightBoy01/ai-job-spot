### About the Role

We’re looking for a highly analytical and versatile finance professional to join our team as a Financial Analyst. 

This role will partner directly with the Director of Finance and support company-wide decision making — from forecasting and board prep to financial tooling and workflow automation.

### Responsibilities

FP&A and Financial Analysis

*   Build and maintain financial models to support budgeting, forecasting, and scenario planning
*   Produce clear, reliable reporting for internal stakeholders and help prepare materials for board and investor updates
*   Conduct ad-hoc analysis to support company-wide initiatives — examples include pricing, hiring plans, vendor evaluation, and SaaS metrics

Finance Operations and Tooling

*   Improve how we track and access core financial and operational metrics across systems
*   Support the monthly close process in collaboration with our Controller and Bookkeeper
*   Identify opportunities to streamline workflows and reporting — and help evaluate and implement new tools to support that

Cross-Functional Support

*   Apply a quantitative lens to decisions across GTM, Product, and People teams
*   Communicate insights clearly — helping stakeholders across the company make better, faster decisions

**Requirements**

*   Strong analytical thinking, attention to detail, and a high bar for accuracy — you care about getting the numbers right and explaining them clearly

*   Experience in a finance-related role (e.g. FP&A, banking, accounting, ops, or similar) is a plus, but not required — we’re open to early-career candidates who are sharp and hungry to learn

*   Comfortable working in Google Sheets/Excel to build and audit models and reports from scratch

*   Tech-savvy and quick to learn — especially comfortable using AI tools to extend your capabilities and tackle things you haven’t done before

*   Takes agency in an unstructured, fast-paced environment — you proactively identify problems, own your work, and follow through without needing a playbook

*   Willing to get into the details — you’re not above chasing down accounting inconsistencies, double-checking data at the source, or doing hands-on work to make sure the numbers hold up
