### Description
Since 1979, iphas Pharma-Verpackung GmbH has been a trusted partner in the pharmaceutical industry, dedicated to developing and delivering high-quality packaging solutions. Our core mission is to continuously refine our processes and achieve optimal results in collaboration with our partners. We're not just about quality on paper; we live it every day – structured, practical, and with a clear overview.

We are actively seeking proactive individuals who think critically, take ownership, and are eager for genuine professional development. This role is pivotal in ensuring our unwavering commitment to quality is upheld across all operations. We offer a permanent position with performance-based compensation, a modern workplace, and a supportive, familial atmosphere characterized by short decision-making paths and direct communication.

### Responsibilities
As our new Quality Management and Process Specialist, you will be instrumental in upholding and enhancing our high standards. Your key responsibilities will include:

*   Taking full ownership of and further developing our Quality Management System (QMS) in accordance with ISO 9001, and expertly guiding our transition to ISO 15378.
*   Serving as the central interface connecting all processes and departments, actively championing and driving continuous improvement initiatives.
*   Analyzing deviations, formulating pragmatic solutions, and ensuring the sustainable implementation of corrective and preventive actions.
*   Conducting thorough internal audits, meticulously accompanying external audits, and acting as a primary point of contact for customers and certification bodies.
*   Documenting all changes comprehensively, diligently monitoring quality-relevant processes, and maintaining impeccable order, traceability, and structure across all areas.
*   Additionally, overseeing the IT/EDP domain, coordinating external service providers, and contributing to the advancement of our digital systems.
*   Ensuring new employees are onboarded smoothly and efficiently with clear processes, comprehensive checklists, and precise documentation.
*   Managing organizational tasks within Human Resources, such as administering leave requests, sick leave notifications, and time tracking.
*   Collaborating closely with the management team to ensure all these crucial processes function seamlessly and transparently.

### Qualifications
We are seeking a highly organized, analytical, and proactive individual who is passionate about quality. Your profile should ideally include:

*   A deep enthusiasm for quality management, organization, order, and structure, coupled with a genuine desire to understand how everything interconnects.
*   Strong analytical thinking, a structured approach to work, and an excellent eye for detail.
*   The ability to remain composed and effective, even when juggling multiple priorities simultaneously.
*   A collaborative spirit, a solution-oriented mindset, and a preference for finding pragmatic solutions over engaging in theoretical discussions.
*   A preference for working in a dynamic office and team environment, as we operate without a home office model to foster direct, efficient, and structured collaboration.
*   **Career changers are explicitly welcome!** If you are motivated, eager to take on responsibility, and believe quality is no accident, we are committed to providing you with all the necessary knowledge and training.

**What we value:**
*   You take ownership and proactively seek solutions, rather than excuses.
*   You are driven to understand, improve, and meticulously document processes.
*   You work conscientiously, systematically, and with a high standard for quality.
*   You bring energy, precision, and passion to your work, constantly striving for daily improvement.
*   We are looking for "A-players" – individuals who consistently deliver above average performance.
