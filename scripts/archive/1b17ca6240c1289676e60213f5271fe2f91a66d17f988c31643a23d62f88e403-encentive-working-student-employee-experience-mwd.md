### Description
Encentive is an innovative SaaS startup with offices in Hamburg, Berlin, and Neumünster, dedicated to empowering industrial companies to significantly reduce energy costs and CO₂ emissions. Our intelligent energy management platform, flexOn, autonomously optimizes and controls corporate electricity consumption, ensuring energy is utilized precisely when it's green and cost-effective.

We are seeking a proactive and enthusiastic Working Student to be the heart of our new Hamburg office. This pivotal role involves ensuring seamless daily operations, fostering a vibrant and positive employee experience, and providing crucial administrative support to our HR team, directly contributing to our unique company culture.

### Responsibilities
*   Ensure the seamless day-to-day operation of our Hamburg office, from organizing logistics to procuring essential materials.
*   Actively contribute to creating a welcoming and positive work atmosphere, ensuring all employees feel comfortable and supported in our new office space.
*   Plan and meticulously organize a variety of engaging events, including team activities, after-work gatherings, and internal company functions.
*   Provide comprehensive administrative support to our HR team, assisting with vital processes such as employee onboarding, managing contractual documentation, and facilitating internal communications.
*   Proactively contribute and implement your own creative ideas to continuously enhance and elevate our overall employee experience.

### Qualifications
*   Currently an enrolled student, ideally pursuing a degree in economics, communications, or social sciences.
*   Possess a genuine passion for organization, communication, and a strong desire to create a positive and supportive work environment for others.
*   Demonstrate a structured, reliable, and highly proactive approach to tasks, eager to take initiative.
*   Exhibit excellent team spirit, strong commitment, and a consistently positive demeanor.
*   Fluent in both German and English.
