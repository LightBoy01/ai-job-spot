### Description
This role is for a Head of Finance & Controlling at an established real estate developer and manager based in the Esslingen area, boasting over 100 employees. The company is seeking an experienced leader to oversee its finance and controlling operations, offering a permanent, full-time position with excellent benefits including up to two days of home office per week after successful onboarding, flexible working hours, and opportunities for professional development. The ideal candidate will be an integral part of the leadership team, contributing to the company's financial strategy and operational excellence in a collaborative and growth-oriented environment.

### Responsibilities
- Participate significantly in the preparation of monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements, leveraging experience in real estate accounting.
- Provide hands-on support to the team in ongoing financial accounting for multiple real estate entities and asset accounting.
- Lead, mentor, and develop a team of six employees, both functionally and disciplinarily.
- Oversee weekly cash forecasting and liquidity planning.
- Act as a key sparring partner for strategic investment planning.
- Serve as a competent point of contact for all balance sheet-related topics.
- Collaborate with the team on medium and long-term planning, as well as actual-vs-target comparisons.
- Manage the preparation and follow-up of shareholder resolutions.
- Drive continuous process improvement within Finance & Controlling and contribute to key projects, including SAP migration.
- Be the primary contact for the management and external partners (e.g., auditors, tax advisors) regarding all finance and controlling inquiries.

### Qualifications
- At least four years of professional experience in a comparable leadership position, including team leadership.
- A minimum of four years of experience within the real estate industry.
- Proven experience in independently preparing monthly and annual financial statements, or significant contributions to their creation.
- Proficient user knowledge of SAP is required.
- Excellent communication skills in German, both written and spoken.
- A strong team player with a positive attitude and a desire to collaborate effectively with colleagues.
- A proactive mindset focused on continuous professional development. The company encourages applications even if not all requirements are fully met.
