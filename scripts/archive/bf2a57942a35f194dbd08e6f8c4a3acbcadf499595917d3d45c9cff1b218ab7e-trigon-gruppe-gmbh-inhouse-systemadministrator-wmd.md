### Description
Die Trigon Unternehmensgruppe has been successfully developing, realizing, and managing residential and commercial properties and hotels in central city locations for decades. We oversee approximately 4,000 residential and 300 commercial units, including parking spaces, and ensure the long-term value preservation of our real estate portfolio through our facility management and building technology.

You will ensure the smooth operation and security of our network and server systems at our centrally located site. Strengthen our IT team and take responsibility for the configuration, monitoring, support, and further development of our IT infrastructure. You will also be the point of contact for technical questions and support employees with IT-related concerns.

This is a permanent full-time position offering significant development potential within a professional and collegial team that will warmly welcome you. We provide attractive compensation commensurate with your qualifications, a challenging role with high personal responsibility, and a supportive work environment. Our benefits package includes a flextime model, 30 days of annual leave (for a 5-day week) plus additional time off at Christmas and New Year, a job ticket, and complimentary drinks and fresh fruit at the workplace.

### Responsibilities
*   Creation and rollout of Windows 10 images
*   Setup, configuration, and maintenance of Windows Server (versions 2008–2019)
*   Support for Exchange Servers (2010–2016) and Active Directory
*   Operation and configuration of Watchguard Firewall
*   Management of Hipath Manager E (telephone systems)
*   Configuration of Cisco routers
*   Setup and support of network infrastructure (switches, WLAN, routers)
*   Maintenance and support of DATEV applications, EBICS, and Sage software
*   Implementation of IT projects and creation of technical documentation
*   Point of contact for the entire Trigon Group

### Qualifications
*   Completed IT apprenticeship or a comparable qualification
*   Professional experience in a similar position (e.g., as a System or Network Administrator)
*   Very good German language skills, both written and spoken
*   Linux knowledge, especially with FreeBSD / FreeNAS
*   Basic knowledge of HTML and PowerShell scripting
*   High willingness to learn and quick comprehension
*   Independent and service-oriented work style
