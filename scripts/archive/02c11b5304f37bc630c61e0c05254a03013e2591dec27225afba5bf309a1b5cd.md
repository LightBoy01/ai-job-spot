### Description

Join the growing success of American Iron & Metal (AIM), a recognized global leader in the metal recycling industry with over 125 sites and 3,500 employees worldwide. For over 86 years, we have thrived through the dedication of our employees and the trust of our customers. We're proud to be a multifaceted company with divisions in metal recycling, construction waste, solder manufacturing, demolition, and car recycling, all while doing good for the environment to help create a greener, more sustainable future for everyone.

We are looking for a leader to spearhead our Business Intelligence and AI strategy. This role is crucial for transforming our vast operational data into actionable insights that will drive our business forward and enhance our environmental impact.

### Responsibilities

*   **Strategy & Leadership:** Develop and implement a comprehensive BI strategy aligned with organizational goals. Lead, mentor, and manage a team of BI developers, analysts, and data engineers.
*   **Culture & Adoption:** Drive the adoption of AI and cultivate a data-driven culture among business users.
*   **Project Management:** Manage resources, timelines, and priorities for all data-related initiatives to maximize business value and efficiency.
*   **Technical Oversight:** Oversee the creation of impactful dashboards, reports, and visualizations. Develop advanced analytics solutions, including predictive, prescriptive, AI, and machine learning models.
*   **Data Governance & Architecture:** Ensure the integrity, consistency, and security of all data. Implement and enforce data governance policies. Supervise the design and deployment of data warehouses and data lakes.
*   **Collaboration:** Work with cross-functional teams to gather requirements and translate stakeholder needs into effective BI solutions. Present analyses and insights to senior management in a clear and actionable manner.
*   **Innovation:** Stay current with industry trends in BI and AI, recommending improvements to optimize the impact of our solutions and fostering a culture of continuous learning.

### Qualifications

*   Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, IT, Business Administration, or a related field (Master's degree is an asset).
*   7+ years of experience in BI or analytics, including 3+ years in a management capacity.
*   Hands-on experience with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.
*   Proficiency with BI tools and Microsoft Azure services (Data Factory, Data Lake, Synapse).
*   Strong programming skills in Python, DAX, Power Query, or R.
*   Solid experience with SQL, data modeling, and data warehousing.
*   Deep knowledge of ETL processes, large datasets, data governance, security, and quality assurance.
*   Excellent communication, presentation, and problem-solving skills.
*   Experience with cloud-based BI solutions (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud).
*   Experience with project management methodologies (Agile, Scrum).
*   Knowledge of advanced AI/ML tools and experience in a manufacturing environment are considered assets.

### Author's Take

**1. AIM has an impressive 86-year history. As you look to build a more data-driven culture, what is the biggest opportunity for this role in driving that change across a well-established organization?**

"That's a fantastic question because it gets to the heart of this role. You're right, we have a deep legacy built on operational expertise and strong relationships. The biggest opportunity isn't to replace that, but to amplify it with data. Think of it this way: our top traders and operators have an incredible intuition for the market. This role is about building the tools to validate, challenge, and scale that intuition. The opportunity is to become a strategic partner to the business, showing them the 'art of the possible' with their own data. You won’t just be delivering reports; you'll be fundamentally changing how a global leader makes decisions, from optimizing logistics in our recycling plants to predicting scrap metal prices. It’s a chance to build a modern data legacy within a company that's already a giant in its field."

**2. The description mentions contributing to a 'greener, more sustainable future.' Can you give a concrete example of a project this BI team might tackle that directly supports that environmental mission?**

"Absolutely. This isn't just a corporate tagline for us; it's our core business. A key project we envision for this team is creating a predictive model for our recycling intake. Right now, we manage massive volumes of material, and optimizing the sorting and processing is critical. By using historical data, market trends, and even weather patterns, your team could build a model to forecast the type and quantity of materials arriving at our sites. This would allow us to schedule staff more efficiently, optimize machinery run-time to reduce energy consumption, and ultimately increase the percentage of materials we successfully recycle. Every percentage point you help us improve has a direct, measurable impact on reducing landfill waste. You're not just analyzing numbers; you're analyzing how to make our planet cleaner."
