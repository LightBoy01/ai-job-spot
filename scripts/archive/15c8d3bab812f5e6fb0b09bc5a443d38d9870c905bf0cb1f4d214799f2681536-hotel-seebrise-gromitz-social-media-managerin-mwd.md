### Description\nThe Hotel Seebrise Grömitz, a charming 3-star garni hotel located just 200 meters from the beautiful Ostsee beach, is seeking a creative and social media-savvy **Social Media Manager (m/f/d)**. This is a part-time Minijob role, totaling 8.5 hours per week, starting in April 2026. You will be responsible for capturing the unique moments and relaxing atmosphere of the hotel and translating them into engaging digital content across various platforms, working where others dream of vacationing.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Manage and maintain our social media channels (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn).\n*   Support the planning and implementation of marketing campaigns.\n*   Create engaging posts, stories, short videos, and accompanying texts.\n*   Monitor and analyze social media activities.\n*   Capture and edit photo and video material using both an iPhone camera and a DSLR camera.\n*   Provide general support in the marketing department.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Initial experience in social media management.\n*   A keen sense for trends and engaging content.\n*   Organized, structured, and reliable working style.\n*   Dependability & team spirit.\n*   Creativity & enjoyment in writing and designing.\n*   Confident communication skills, both written and verbal.\n*   Basic knowledge of image editing or tools like Canva is an advantage.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic entry-level opportunity for a passionate and creative individual to kickstart their career in social media marketing within a picturesque environment. The role offers a superb work-life balance with flexible hours, the exciting prospect of home-office after six months, and a competitive hourly wage. What truly stands out is the company's commitment to a familial team atmosphere, flat hierarchies, and investment in employee growth through internal training and open space for personal ideas. If you're looking to make a real impact, develop your skills, and enjoy working right by the sea, this role at Hotel Seebrise Grömitz is an ideal fit.
