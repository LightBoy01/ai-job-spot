**Your work profile.** 

As an Associate Director in our **SAP** Team, you’ll build and nurture positive working relationships with teams and clients with the intention to exceed client expectations: - 

**SAP Datasphere should have:** 

*   Hands-on experience in creating fact models, dimension views, analytical datasets, and entity relationships in SAP Datasphere.
*   Building semantic models, hierarchies, and calculated measures aligned to financial KPIs in SAP Datasphere.
*   Strong knowledge of connecting SAP S/4HANA, SAP BW/4HANA, SAP ECC, and external databases into SAP Datasphere.
*   Experience with federation, replication, and virtualization methods in SAP Datasphere.
*   Enabling SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC) reporting through Datasphere datasets.
*   Designing governed semantic layers for consistent enterprise-wide reporting.
*   Strong hands-on experience in creating stories, dashboards, and advanced visualizations.
*   Proficiency in designing interactive filters, drill-downs, linked analysis, and input controls.
*   Expertise in building financial reports (P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Variance Analysis).
*   Creating calculated measures, restricted key figures, and custom hierarchies.
*   Managing live connections and import connections with SAP S/4HANA, BW/4HANA, and Datasphere.
*   Optimizing story performance for large datasets (query design, model size, cache).
*   Applying best practices for reporting speed, design, and user experience.
*   Integrating SAC with SAP Datasphere, SAP S/4HANA, BW/4HANA, and external data sources.
*   Consuming data via OData services, APIs, and flat file uploads.
*   Extending SAC with custom widgets and scripting-based enhancements.
