### Description\nAre you ready to take the next step in your career? Then apply to Computerra and become part of our team! In our dynamic and motivated team, collaboration, enjoyment of work, and innovation come first. With us, you can expect exciting challenges, creative projects, and plenty of room for your personal growth.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Implement IT systems for our clients (M365 Intune, Exchange Online), Cloud Backups, and other modern IT solutions, and independently perform server migrations if you wish.\n- Monitor and maintain our clients' IT infrastructure and configure switches, routers, and firewalls (Securepoint, Sophos, or similar).\n- Manage and configure servers, networks, and storage solutions.\n- Analyze and troubleshoot hardware, software, and network problems – structured and solution-oriented.\n- Set up IT workstations, install software, and support peripheral devices.\n- Monitor backups, implement security measures, and react to security-relevant incidents.\n- Maintain technical records and documentation.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A completed IT education (e.g., IT specialist for system integration) or a comparable qualification.\n- Knowledge of Windows server operating systems and services such as Active Directory, DNS, and DHCP.\n- Experience with virtualization (e.g., Hyper-V, VMware, or Proxmox).\n- Driving license class B.\n\n**Nice-to-have (but not mandatory):**\n- Knowledge in the administration of Microsoft 365 products (e.g., Entra, Exchange, Intune).\n- Experience in setting up backup systems.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at Computerra offers a dynamic environment for an experienced IT System Integrator who thrives on independent problem-solving and client-focused solutions. With a strong emphasis on collaboration, continuous growth, and a great work-life balance facilitated by flexible hours and home office options, it's an excellent opportunity for professionals looking to implement modern IT solutions and contribute to a supportive team culture. The company also provides attractive benefits including corporate perks, modern equipment, and regular team events to foster camaraderie.
