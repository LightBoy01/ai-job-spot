### Description\nonest. is a pioneering startup revolutionizing the caffeine beverage market with the world's first truly honest caffeine lifestyle drink. Unlike competitors, onest. uses only three natural ingredients – carbonated water, real fruit juice, and natural caffeine from guarana seeds – free from concentrates, artificial flavors, sweeteners, or colors. The company has successfully completed its first funding round and is now seeking passionate individuals to help build a globally recognized brand and a movement for authenticity. This part-time role offers a unique opportunity to directly contribute to marketing strategy and execution, working closely with the founders to establish onest. as the go-to brand for a generation seeking quality, taste, and a healthy lifestyle without the stigma of traditional energy drinks.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Coordination & Support**: Support social media interns, ensure smooth operational workflows, and coordinate content activities.\n- **Marketing Strategy**: Collaborate directly with the founders on strategic questions related to brand positioning, campaign development, and marketing experiments.\n- **Operational Marketing**: Actively participate in campaigns, event organization, promotions, and influencer collaborations.\n- **Analysis & Reporting**: Evaluate campaign performance and derive actionable optimizations.\n- **Cross-functional Support**: Provide marketing support for daily business needs across sales, B2B initiatives, and investor relations.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Fluent German language skills (C1/C2 level) in both written and spoken communication.\n- Currently pursuing or holding a degree in Marketing, Communication, Media, Business Administration, or a related field.\n- Initial experience in social media, campaign management, or event marketing.\n- Strong organizational and communication skills.\n- Genuine interest in startups, consumer brands, and digital marketing.\n- Ideally residing in or near Konstanz.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an ambitious junior marketer or student eager to make a significant impact within a rapidly growing startup. You'll gain unparalleled hands-on experience across the entire marketing spectrum, from strategic planning with the founders to direct execution of campaigns and content. The flexible 20-hour work week is perfectly tailored for those balancing studies, offering direct mentorship from the leadership team. If you're passionate about building a brand from scratch, challenging industry norms, and want to be part of a movement that values honesty and authenticity, this role provides a unique platform for accelerated growth and real responsibility from day one.
