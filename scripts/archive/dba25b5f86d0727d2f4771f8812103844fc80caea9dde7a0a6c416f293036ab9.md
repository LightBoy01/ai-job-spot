At Webflow, our mission is to bring development superpowers to everyone. As the pioneer of the Website Experience Platform (WXP), we’re redefining how teams Build, Manage, and Optimize for the web — combining visual development, powerful content management systems, AI-driven personalization, seamless hosting, and end-to-end analytics in a single, unified platform. With AI at the core, Webflow helps teams move faster, create more performant digital experiences, and scale without heavy engineering support. From independent designers and creative agencies to global enterprises, hundreds of thousands of organizations use Webflow to turn ideas into reality — and to power what’s possible on the web.

Our Solutions Engineering team plays a vital role by partnering closely with our customers as well as with Sales, Customer Success, and Product to ensure mutual, long-term success. We aim to deeply understand our customers’ strategic objectives and technical requirements. We architect and demonstrate innovative solutions that unlock meaningful business value for our customers. Additionally, we champion the voice of our customers by understanding market trends and influencing product direction.

We’re looking for a **Senior Solutions Engineer, Corporate** to inspire our customers by helping them understand the art of what’s possible with the Webflow platform. You will tell our differentiated story that shows how our solution helps them meet their business objectives.

*   Reporting to the Senior Manager, Solutions Engineering

As a Senior Solutions Engineer, Corporate you’ll … 

*   Partner with 3-4 Account Executives & support multiple active customer opportunities across our Corporate segment, proactively communicating with stakeholders to ensure on-time solution delivery
*   Guide customers through the evaluation process of Webflow ensuring that our solution meets key business objectives and technical requirements
*   Conduct deep discovery, solution design, technical validation and business case development
*   Present effective stories through demonstrations that highlight the benefits of using Webflow to solve key customer objectives and challenges
*   Lead hands-on workshops and trials where appropriate
*   Influence product direction for short & long-term success in Corporate by representing the customer voice
*   Support cross-functional initiatives that are key to advancing Webflow’s mission of bringing development superpowers to everyone
*   Travel up to 25% -  primarily for onboarding, industry events, and internal offsites

In addition to the responsibilities outlined above, at Webflow we will support you in identifying where your interests and development opportunities lie and we'll help you incorporate them into your role.

**About you:**
==============

You’ll thrive as a Senior Solutions Engineer, Corporate if you:

*   Have at least 3+ years of experience as a Solutions Engineer, ideally in the web development space
*   Possess strong written, visual and verbal communication skills
*   Take a consultative approach and are passionate about helping customers solve problems
*   Consistently demonstrate strong storytelling within the context of discovery and/or product demo sessions
*   Exhibit an advanced understanding of APIs and web programming languages — HTML, CSS & JavaScript
*   Creatively problem solve with AI where appropriate
*   Enjoy working autonomously, creatively, and kindly with your teammates while empowering and supporting those around you
*   Practice radical candor with your colleagues to communicate clearly
*   Demonstrate excellent coaching, writing, discovery and presentation skills
*   Lead with enthusiasm for earning customer trust and deepening customer relationships
*   Are comfortable working in a fast-paced environment and adaptable to changing priorities
*   Stay curious and open to growth — actively building fluency in emerging technologies like AI to unlock creativity, accelerate progress, and amplify impact.
