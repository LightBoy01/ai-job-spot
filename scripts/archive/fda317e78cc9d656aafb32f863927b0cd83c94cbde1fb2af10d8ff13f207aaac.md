**ABOUT THE ROLES:  
  
**

*   Location: CBD
*   Full-time 24/7 availability
*   Roster: 12-hour rotating shifts (2 weeks of days and 2 weeks of nights - mix of weekdays and weekend
*   Level 4 pay rate with team leader allowance 

Wilson Security is seeking an all-rounded Team Leader to manage a team of Security Officer/Control Room Operators for an iconic multifaceted complex located in the CBD. This is a dynamic, fast-paced environment that is subject to change and requires a driven and passionate person with a strong desire to make a difference.

This position is primarily undertaking and managing various functions such as Control Room, Facility Management support, Patrols and Customer Service within a large precinct.

You will be supported and mentored by an experienced Security and Facility Management team who wants to see you succeed and are committed to delivering a premium service to our customers, tenants, and visitors.

**DUTIES & RESPONSIBILITIES:**

*   Availability to work 12-hour shifts, including nights/weekends (24/7 availability)
*   Provide operational support to the facility management team to deliver and exceed service expectations.
*   Produce high-quality reports and documentation.
*   Ensure Contractor Compliance through Site Inductions and Review and Process of Correct Access/Work Permits
*   Have a flexible and focused work ethic.
*   Be proactive in Safety and Security Risk identification and issue resolution.
*   Train, guide, mentor a team of Security Officers
*   Assess all alarms, incidents, emergencies and provide the appropriate response.
*   Be well presented and well-spoken
*   Have good IT skills

**WHO WE ARE LOOKING FOR:**  
  

*   Strong customer service skills and the ability to maintain resilience within a fast-paced environment.
*   Understanding of Building/Facilities Management and Contractor Compliance will be highly regarded
*   Intermediate computer skills and use of Microsoft Office programs
*   Experience using Fire (FIP, EWIS, ASE) and Security Systems (CCTV, Access, DVM) will be highly regarded
*   Access Control and (BMS) Building Management Systems (previous experience of Gallagher and Honeywell systems will be highly regarded)
*   Excellent interpersonal and verbal/written communication skills
*   Client-centric approach with a positive attitude to change and continuous improvement.
*   Leadership skills, the ability to drive and lead your team
*   Previous experience as a Chief Warden will be highly regarded.

**Qualifications & Licenses**

*   Valid Victorian Security Licence (Control Room Endorsed)
*   Current First Aid Certificate
