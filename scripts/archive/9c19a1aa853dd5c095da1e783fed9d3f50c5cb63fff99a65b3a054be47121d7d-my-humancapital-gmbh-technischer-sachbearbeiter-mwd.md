### Description
Our partner company in Augsburg is seeking a **Technical Specialist (m/w/d)** to join their team as soon as possible. This pivotal role involves the comprehensive management and coordination of technical services, ensuring the smooth operation and upkeep of facilities. You will be instrumental in maintaining a modern work environment, supported by a professional onboarding process and opportunities for continuous development, all within a company that values work-life balance with flexible hours in the heart of Augsburg.

### Responsibilities
*   Oversee and organize all maintenance and servicing measures.
*   Coordinate internal and external service providers, supervising minor construction and repair projects, and ensuring their timely completion.
*   Serve as the primary communication point for internal departments, external partners, and users.
*   Conduct on-site appointments, thoroughly document findings, and initiate necessary follow-up actions.
*   Process orders and invoices efficiently, contributing significantly to seamless technical operations.

### Qualifications
*   Hold a completed technical or commercial education with a technical specialization (e.g., Technician, Master Craftsman, Real Estate Specialist, or a comparable qualification).
*   Possess proven experience in organizing and supporting technical measures, ideally within maintenance, construction, or facility management.
*   Be proficient in standard IT applications, including MS Office; knowledge of SAP and AiBATROS is a plus.
*   Demonstrate an independent, structured, and reliable work ethic, coupled with strong organizational and communication skills.
*   Fluent German language skills, both written and spoken, are essential to your profile.
