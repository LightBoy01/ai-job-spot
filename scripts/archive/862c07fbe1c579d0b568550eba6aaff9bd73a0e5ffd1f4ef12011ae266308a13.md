Perion is a global advertising technology company delivering solutions to the biggest brands and publishers around the globe across search, social media and display, video, CTV, and programmatic DOOH.

Home to an award-winning technology solution –– with our unique data-driven AI/ML based technologies, we deliver and optimize hundreds of terabytes of data and billions of events per day. We’re working with dozens of sources to provide a superior experience across screens and platforms, including mobile, video, social and native.

Perion Algos is a technology-driven team within the Perion Networks group, focused on building high-performance advertising algorithms and systems. We blend robust backend data processing with sleek frontend interfaces to make complex data easily actionable for our users.

We're on the lookout for a **Fullstack Developer** who leans more toward the frontend side of things — someone who loves crafting beautiful, responsive UIs but is also comfortable diving into Python-based backends.

### **What You’ll Do**

*   Develop and maintain modern web interfaces using **React + TypeScript**
*   Build and integrate backend services in **Python**
*   Collaborate with product, and data teams to deliver seamless user experiences
*   Contribute to architectural and UI/UX discussions
*   Help shape our developer experience and coding standards

### **What We’re Looking For**

**Must-haves:**

*   Solid experience in **React (with TypeScript)**
*   Comfortable with backend work in **Python**
*   Familiar with REST APIs and frontend/backend integration
*   Strong communication skills and a collaborative mindset
*   High adaptability 

**Nice-to-haves:**

*   Experience with **SQLAlchemy** and **Pydantic**
*   Familiarity with **Airflow** and data workflows
*   An eye for UI/UX or experience working closely with designers
*   Experience in ad tech

*   **Top-tier health insurance** with **Alan**

*   **Remote-friendly culture**, as well as permanent offices in **Paris** or **Montpellier**
