You know your way around Power Platform – and you’re ready to dive into the world of AI? Then you’ve come to the right place. In our projects, you’ll develop applications that not only automate processes but also create real value using Microsoft technologies like Copilot Studio and Azure AI.
**What you do with us**
*   You analyze our clients’ business processes, identify optimization potential and design tailored digital solutions.
*   You plan, design, and build custom low-code applications using Power Apps, Power Automate and other Power Platform components.
*   You implement process automation to increase efficiency and generate tangible business value.
*   You design and develop smart agent-based applications using Copilot for M365, Copilot Studio, or Azure AI – tailored to each client’s needs.
*   You always keep an eye on compliance, governance and data protection to ensure secure, sustainable solutions.
**When you fit in with us**
*   You’ve worked on Power Platform solutions from the idea to go-live and know what it takes to deliver successful projects.
*   You bring solid experience with Power Apps (Canvas and ideally Model-driven) and Power Automate (Cloud Flows, ideally Desktop Flows/RPA).
*   You’ve already had first touchpoints with Copilot or have built custom GPTs or agents.
*   You have a solid understanding of low-code principles and are curious about current tech trends in the Microsoft ecosystem – especially around Microsoft 365, Copilot and automation.
*   You enjoy working independently, bring a collaborative spirit and are driven by one goal: creating smart, future-ready solutions for our clients.
*   You are fluent in both German and Englisch
