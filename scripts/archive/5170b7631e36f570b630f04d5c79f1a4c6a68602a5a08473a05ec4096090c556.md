Brandtech+ (formerly known as OLIVER+) is a global team of creative thinkers, tech-savvy trendsetters, and production pros specialising in film, CGI, automation, AI, motion design, and digital/print content. We partner with over 300 clients in 40+ countries and counting. Our focus is to connect clients with high-quality solutions, talent and ambitious opportunities worldwide.  

As a part of **The Brandtech Group**, we're at the forefront of leveraging cutting-edge AI technology to revolutionise how we create and deliver work. Our **AI solutions** enhance efficiency, spark creativity, and drive insightful decision-making, empowering our teams to produce innovative and impactful results.

**Role:** Senior GenAI Motion Designer

**Location:** Remote (México)

**Language:** Proficiency in English is required as we are a global business

**About the role:**

Awareness and adoption of GenAI technology has transformed advertising, creating entirely new opportunities, and challenges for creative teams. We’re looking for a GenAI Motion Designer to deliver a wide range of different marketing material, from social assets such as GIFs or Instagram posts, to cutdowns of TVCs, 2D animation, graphics that include motion such as supers or icons, and various graphic elements, based on the provided assets. You will be trained on our AI platform, Pencil, and will be using Pencil along with a range of other AI tools to ensure you deliver high-performing, innovative ads. 

The ideal candidate will have a strong background in motion graphics and editing, inclusive of grading and sound design. Experience in 2D animation workflows, from concept development and storyboarding through to final execution is essential. A good knowledge or history of work in Social Media as well as AI is desirable. 

Ultimately, as the GenAI Motion Designer, you will create and edit video content, ensure high-quality production, and collaborate with the creative team to meet project goals, through the use of AI as well as traditional tools. 

**What you'll be doing:** 

*   Working closely with GenAI Art Directors/Creators/PM’s to bring projects and work from concept to final delivery.
*   Create and adapt high-quality content leveraging AI and traditional tools.  
*   Produce visual assets that align with client's creative standards and effectively scale creative production across brands and channels.
*   Expand the creative output of the team. Confidence in ideation along with the ability to create bespoke high-end animation and video edits is essential.
*   Take projects from brief to completion, injecting new ideas and creativity - all in close adherence to the client's brand guidelines.
*   Strong skills in mastering, editing, remastering and adjusting master assets for various crops and formats are required.
*   The ability to own your creative projects, craft assets that meet briefs and design standards and inspire client with your creative input.
*   Pitch fresh approaches and concepts confidently.
*   Oversee and manage the preparation of all finished files that will comply with the correct output specifications. 

As a business we're committed to ensuring everyone on our team is fully trained and confident in using Pencil AI, and other approved AI tools. A wide range of internal resources are available to make sure our teams stay ahead of the AI curve: training, webinars, newsletters, AI communities etc. 

You are expected to maintain an active AI proficiency and keep up to date by: 

*   Completing ongoing, structured internal AI training including Pencil.
*   Continuously up-skilling using our approved AI tools including Pencil.
*   Stay up to date with evolving AI trends through our newsletters, webinars, hackathons, AI communities etc
*   Collaborate and contribute to AI knowledge-sharing sessions embracing our culture of Learning. 

**What you'll need:** 

*   8+ years experience as a motion designer in an advertising agency, in-house creative group, or related design studio.
*   Proven portfolio of motion design work, representing both traditional and AI-assisted projects. AI can be personal projects or Client related work.
*   A strong willingness to adapt current workflow to include GenAI.
*   Proficiency in Adobe Creative Cloud (After Effects, Premiere Pro, Photoshop, Illustrator).
*   Familiarity with GenAI tools such as ChatGPT, Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, Veo3, etc. (Training on our specific platform, Pencil, will be provided.)
*   Comfortable with fundamental GenAI techniques like model selection, creative prompting, and collaborative AI-driven ideation.
*   Any hands-on experience delivering real-world creative using GenAI (desirable).
*   Strong communication skills (written and verbal), and a collaborative attitude.
*   Optimism about the evolving role of GenAI in creativity, with adaptability and a solution-oriented mindset.
*   Awareness of legal/ethical considerations in using AI-generated assets
