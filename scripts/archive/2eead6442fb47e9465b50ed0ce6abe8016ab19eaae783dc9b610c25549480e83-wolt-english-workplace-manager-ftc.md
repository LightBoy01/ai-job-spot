### Description
Wolt is a fast-growing Finnish technology company renowned for building local delivery platforms, connecting customers with restaurants, grocery stores, and other retail outlets. Since its inception in 2014, Wolt has expanded into over 500 cities across 30 countries, and in 2022, joined forces with DoorDash to further its global reach. Working at Wolt is characterized by excitement and challenge, offering an environment where individuals can learn, build, and ship more than in many other organizations.

The Wolt Workplace team plays a crucial role in ensuring the functionality, comfort, safety, sustainability, and efficiency of all Wolt offices worldwide. As an integral part of the wider Real Estate & Facilities teams, their mission is to remove any friction in the physical space, enabling employees to be their most successful.

Wolt is seeking an experienced Workplace Manager for a 12-month fixed-term contract, covering a maternity leave. This role, based in Berlin with responsibility for satellite offices across Germany, is pivotal in shaping and running the workplace experience and operations for the country. The successful candidate will report to the Global Workplace & Facilities Manager and will be a creative, problem-solving individual adept at combining people, technology, and space to create an exceptional environment for employees, visitors, and contractors, all while optimizing resources. This is a hands-on, customer-focused position in a dynamic, fast-paced, and growing environment.

### Responsibilities
*   Serve as the primary point of contact and Directly Responsible Individual (DRI) for landlords, vendors, and local stakeholders across all German workplaces.
*   Manage a small dedicated workplace team and oversee vendor relationships.
*   Implement day-to-day policies, procedures, and programs to ensure well-managed and maintained buildings, prioritizing functionality, comfort, safety, sustainability, and efficiency.
*   Lead and manage all workplace programs, including but not limited to food & beverages, lockers, mail services, space planning, reception experience, and workplace technology.
*   Oversee all site-specific workplace projects and initiatives aimed at improving the physical office, involving the creation of playbooks, operational guidelines, and physical enhancements.
*   Streamline office accessibility and services, ensuring ease of navigation and use, while innovating and automating to enable self-service where appropriate.
*   Promote high employee satisfaction by ensuring prompt responses to concerns, addressing environmental, health, and safety matters, and maintaining quality programs, extending the same service expectation to team members and vendors.
*   Collaborate with local and global stakeholders to develop a detailed understanding of Wolt's business and service requirements.
*   Build strong relationships with business leaders and local teams to deliver workplace experience strategies that align with their functions.
*   Coordinate activities, financials, vendors, and staffing for current and future building, infrastructure, and operations needs, including owning the country facilities operating budget.
*   Establish, maintain, and manage vendor relationships, implementing clear Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and contract management processes.
*   Provide information and training to Vendor Partners, such as Receptionists and Facilities Managers, on Wolt values and practices.
*   Act as the on-site stakeholder liaison during the planning and construction phases of office expansions.
*   Manage space planning, tracking, move coordination, and new hire space accommodations.
*   Plan, execute, and report on team onsite events, and support ad-hoc company-wide event needs within the local office, including creating processes for large internal events and coordinating workplace-specific event tasks.
*   Collaborate with Global Real Estate, Facilities, IT, Safety, Security, and Sustainability teams to roll out global policies and initiatives across Wolt.

### Qualifications
*   Multi-year professional experience in Office, Workplace, or Facilities Management within a global organization operating in a fast-paced and high-growth environment.
*   Demonstrated strong and passionate customer focus.
*   Proven experience in vendor management.
*   Ability and willingness to travel to support all in-country locations, especially during office relocations and openings.
*   Event Management experience is a bonus.
*   A proactive attitude, enjoying taking ownership and solving problems independently.
*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English, with German proficiency being an advantage.
*   Ability to work autonomously in a fast-moving environment, coupled with strong relationship building and stakeholder management skills, and a true growth mindset for continuous learning and development.
*   Willingness to participate in First Aider and Fire Warden Training and readiness to provide assistance in emergencies.
*   Comfortable collaborating remotely and dynamically using digital tools such as GSuite, Slack, and Zoom.
