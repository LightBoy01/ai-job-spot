### Description

Hi, we are Junico – Germany's largest community for unique freelancers. We empower freelancers to change the world with their skills! 🌈

Our community thrives on honesty, helpfulness, and mutual respect. We believe in open exchange, active participation, and transparency – because freelancers are not just users of Junico; they are what makes Junico. 🙌

We connect exciting projects that freelancers are truly passionate about, creating the perfect match between talent and passion. 🔥 If you're passionate about a more flexible, fairer, and sustainable world of work like us, and eager to be part of a startup founded in 2019 and self-financed to this day, you should apply *asap* as a Working Student in Sales & Business Development!

As a Working Student in Sales & Business Development, you will identify the needs of companies and ensure that the best freelance experts are found for each project. Thanks to you, projects will be filled quickly, and major companies will become Junico fans! 🙏

### Responsibilities

As a key part of our Sales & Business Development team, your responsibilities will include:

*   **Account Management & Customer Care:**
    *   Identify the needs of our corporate clients, find suitable freelancers from our community, and support the entire placement process – from initial contact and onboarding to long-term customer retention.
    *   Manage inbound leads, conduct conversations with freelancers, assist with contract negotiations, and help optimize our CRM and sales funnel. With your keen sense of people and structures, you actively contribute to perfectly matching companies and freelancers.
*   **Business Development & Process Optimization:**
    *   Support administrative tasks in payment processing and finance, develop and improve internal processes – especially in support, to simplify efforts for customers – and contribute to both operational and strategic support.
    *   Additionally, you will assist with partner management and work on developing new, efficient processes to further scale Junico.

### Qualifications

To succeed in this role, you should possess the following:

*   You are currently studying and have already gained initial practical experience in Account Management, Business Development, or Sales.
*   You are motivated to work **3-5 days per week for 4-6 hours each** (Monday + Friday from home office, other days in the office).
*   You have the "**Sales Spirit**."
*   You are excited about the **startup world**.
*   You aspire to be an **expert in what you do**.
*   You work **goal-oriented**, **structured**, and question tasks when you believe a better result can be achieved through a different approach.
*   You take **full responsibility** for your tasks and are the first to recognize impending problems.
*   You **enjoy working** because you want your actions to have an impact – all while keeping a cool head in stressful situations.
*   You can **motivate your colleagues**, enjoy working in a team, and **communication** is your ace up the sleeve.

### Author's Take

This is an exceptional opportunity for a motivated student to gain significant practical experience within a dynamic, self-financed startup. Junico champions a highly collaborative, transparent, and respectful culture, where working students are fully integrated and their contributions are truly valued from day one.

The company offers a strong commitment to employee well-being and growth, evident in benefits like competitive pay (starting at €15/hour with room for growth), flexible working arrangements (home office days, workations), comprehensive training and development opportunities (in-house library, learning platforms), and a flat hierarchy that encourages open communication. Beyond the core responsibilities, you'll be contributing to a meaningful mission of shaping a fairer and more flexible future of work.

The hiring process is swift and transparent, promising feedback within 48 hours. They're looking for someone who doesn't just do the job but truly owns it and brings a "Sales Spirit" – someone who can confidently articulate "why we'd be crazy not to invite you for an interview." If you're eager to make a tangible impact and grow within a supportive, forward-thinking team, Junico is definitely worth exploring.
