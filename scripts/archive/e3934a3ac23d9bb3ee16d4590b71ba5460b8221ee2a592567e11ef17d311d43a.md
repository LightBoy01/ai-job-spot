**Duties and Responsibilities**  
*     
    

Including but not limited to:

  

AI Strategy & Use Case Development

*   Identify high-impact use cases where AI can solve creative, marketing, and educational challenges.
*   Participate in vendor research and selection.
*   Build a roadmap of AI innovation initiatives that touch content production, media buying, education delivery, and brand storytelling.

AI Tool Deployment & Workflow Engineering

*   Deploy and maintain AI systems, AI agents, knowledge bases, and automation workflows.
*   Build systems that integrate with our stack: Shopify, Klaviyo, Meta Ads, Recharge, and more.

Generative Creative Systems

*   Automate generation of copy, imagery, and video while preserving brand integrity.
*   Collaborate with Creative Directors to build prompt libraries, tone systems, and smart brief generators.
*   Enable the creative team to produce more output with less friction through AI tooling.

Marketing Performance Automation

*   Deploy AI for personalization at scale in paid media, email/SMS, and product recommendations.
*   Use prediction models for retargeting, churn prevention, and post-purchase retention.
*   Partner with media and data teams to drive attribution clarity and funnel efficiency through AI-enhanced insights.

Brand & Education Enablement

*   Build internal tools and knowledge agents for onboarding, training, and internal brand alignment.
*   Develop AI-powered education platforms that deliver dynamic content to stylists, retailers, or end consumers.
*   Ensure knowledge continuity across creative and educational content using intelligent retrieval systems.
  
  
**Qualifications**  
*   4–7+ years applying AI in creative, marketing, or tech-enabled environments.
*   Technical fluency in LLMs, prompt engineering, APIs and AI workflows.
*   Deep understanding of the generative AI landscape—text, image, audio, and video tools.
*   Proven ability to ship AI-powered products or internal tools from MVP to scale.
*   Hands-on experience integrating AI into platforms like Meta Ads, Klaviyo, Recharge, Shopify, Notion, Airtable, or Slack.

Preferred Qualifications

*   Prior work in DTC, beauty, wellness, lifestyle, or content-rich brand environments.
