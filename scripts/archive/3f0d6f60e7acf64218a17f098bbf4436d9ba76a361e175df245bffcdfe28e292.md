**About The Role:**
*   The **Senior Partner Manager** will report directly to the Industry Lead and will be responsible for building new business relationships within the mid-market space.
This is a full-time role that can be held from one of our US offices or remotely in the United States.
**Role Responsibilities:**
*   Activate new partners and brands and expand into new business units within existing partners. You will be responsible for contributions to revenue targets within your industry team.
*   Work closely and collaboratively with Industry Lead and Account Managers on offer details, programming, and calibration.
*   With oversight, craft proposals and meet with prospects to share Fetch’s vision, strategy, and value proposition.
*   Leverage insights and data visualization in proposals to _show_ brand partners the power of Fetch in driving their business forward.
*   Externally influence decision-makers on Fetch’s partner teams, showing brands the power of Fetch and guiding them to full partnership. Internally, you’re supporting other Fetch sellers' efforts.
*   Demonstrate knowledge of Fetch's offerings and tech/product/marketing pipeline. You know where Fetch is and where we're going with the depth to get new partners/brands to full partnership.
*   Remain up-to-date on media/marketing industry trends, network, and demonstrate your subject matter expertise in the mid-market space to prospective partners.
**Minimum Requirements:**
*   5+ years of experience in partnerships, sales support, or a related field.
*   Direct experience in digital media, advertising, or consumer data.
*   Strong organizational and execution skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities.
*   Proficient understanding of how Fetch ad solutions drive business outcomes (including key KPIs).
*   Strong communication and presentation skills, with the ability to engage clients and collaborate across teams.
*   Familiarity with AI-driven decision-support tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini AI) or automation tools (e.g., Zapier).
*   Experience drafting proposals and order forms from concept to completion.
*   Exposure to cross-functional collaboration to align campaign strategy and execution.
*   Demonstrated ability to forecast revenue and create gap-to-quota plans.
**Preferred Requirements:**
*   Experience with Salesforce or similar CRM platforms.
*   Direct client vertical experience in mid-market industry is preferred.
*   Comfort working in dynamic or evolving environments.
