**The opportunity:**
*   We’re on the lookout for a **Senior AI Engineer** to join our dynamic ITS- AI team and lead the design and delivery of transformative AI solutions. If you're passionate about building intelligent systems that are scalable, secure, and drive firm value — this is your opportunity to make a real impact.
**Day to day you will:**
*   Architect and implement advanced AI systems using LLMs, RAG, NLP, and MLOps on platforms like Azure, Copilot Studio, and KPMG Workbench.
*   Build and deploy intelligent copilots and AI services with robust CI/CD pipelines, testing frameworks, and monitoring tools.
*   Seamlessly integrate AI with enterprise platforms including M365, APIs, and data platforms, ensuring top-tier security and performance.
*   Champion Trusted AI governance — from bias assessments to explainability and audit logging.
*   Lead technical efforts within cross-functional delivery teams, shape product roadmaps, and support pre-project work with solution design and estimation.
*   Mentor engineers, drive technical excellence, and develop reusable assets and accelerators.
**About you:**
*   Deep expertise in AI/ML engineering, with 5+ years of hands-on experience in enterprise-grade AI architecture and deployment.
*   Strong understanding of cloud platforms (especially Azure), M365 integrations, and modern development practices.
*   A passion for ethical AI, with a track record of implementing governance frameworks.
*   Proven leadership in technical delivery, stakeholder engagement, and mentoring.
*   A mindset for continuous improvement, innovation, and customer-centric design.
*   Proven ability to collaborate effectively in cross-functional teams, communicate clearly, and adapt quickly in a fast-paced, agile environment
