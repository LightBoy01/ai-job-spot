### Description\nOur client is a family-owned company established over 20 years ago, headquartered in Artland. This medium-sized, owner-managed business offers a wide range of services for animals. Empathy and love for animals go hand-in-hand with responsibility and appreciation, which has been the foundation for enormous growth in recent years. They offer exciting opportunities at more than 50 national and international locations.\n\nAs a communicative interface between stakeholders, specialist departments, and IT, you will organize and coordinate digital projects, ensuring that ideas transform into innovative solutions. To expand our client's digitalization efforts, we are looking for a **Project Manager Digital Projects (m/w/d)** for the main location, effective immediately.\n\nIf you bring structure, technical understanding, and a passion for design, you've come to the right place!\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Responsible for the entire portfolio of digital projects, from idea to Go-Live.\n- Prioritization of projects, resource steering, and monitoring of time, budget, quality, and target achievement.\n- Gathering and structuring requirements, then translating them into technical concepts.\n- Serving as the communicative link between IT, specialist departments, and management, ensuring seamless collaboration.\n- Regular creation of reports/KPIs and presentation of results to management and executive board.\n- Leading pilot projects, fostering innovations, and integrating Cloud, AI, and automation into the company.\n- Supporting teams with agile and hybrid project methods and moderating workshops.\n- Ensuring the integrability, scalability, and maintainability of solutions.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A degree in Business Informatics, Computer Science, Business Administration, or a comparable qualification.\n- Several years of experience in (digital) project management, ideally with a focus on software or ERP projects.\n- A profound understanding of technical solution architectures, interfaces, and system integration.\n- Familiarity with agile and classical project methods, as well as confident stakeholder management.\n- A structured, solution-oriented working style, paired with analytical thinking and organizational talent.\n- Strong communication skills, empathy, and enthusiasm for digitalization and innovation – you can inspire teams and actively shape change.\n- Experience collaborating with external service providers is a plus.\n- Ideally, you also bring experience in change or innovation management, a certification (e.g., Scrum Master, Product Owner), or practical knowledge in data flows, API design, or ERP systems.\n- German and English at least C1 level.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting opportunity for a seasoned Digital Project Manager to significantly impact the digital transformation of a successful, family-owned company with a strong ethical foundation. The role offers substantial autonomy and influence, allowing for the leadership of key innovation initiatives, including Cloud, AI, and automation. The company culture, with its emphasis on trust, flexibility, and a pet-friendly environment, combined with continuous learning opportunities, makes this an attractive proposition for someone who thrives on challenge and values a supportive workplace. The \"Du\"-culture and hybrid work model further highlight a modern and employee-centric approach.
