**What will you be doing?**

As innovators in Big Data, Circana seeks passionate client advocates with strong analytic skills to advance solutions that best build the client's strategic vision. The Newell Team at Circana works with one of the most iconic companies in the industry. This role reports to the Vice President in Client Development and works with the client directly to demonstrate how data can impact and improve clients’ businesses and across all functional teams. The position provides the opportunity to make an impact, leveraging fact-based insights and analysis, while developing the knowledge and skills required for continued success in data driven solutions.

**Job Responsibilities**

*   **Account and Business Management**: understand client organizations and align Circana solutions with their business objectives; drive revenue by developing and executing strategic account plans; collaborate cross functionally and cross-vertically to deliver client-centric solutions.
*   **Industry Expertise and Analytic Capability**: leverage proprietary data tools, analytic skills, and AI to analyze large, complex data sets to identify and communicate key retail trends and their implications to clients
*   **Client Relationship Development**: manage daily client interactions, ensuring responsiveness and value delivery; educate and train clients to maximize the use of Circana’s data and tools; present insights and recommendations in client meetings to support strategic decisions. Builds rapid rapport with key client contacts and across both CPG & GM functions within the highly matrixed Circana organization.
*   **Problem Solving & Adaptability**: address client challenges with urgency, creativity, and positive approach; willingness to learn new data sets and platforms to deliver value to our clients. Serves as a passionate advocate for the client and in so doing advocates solutions that will best build the client’s business. Leads effective problem solving with client and internal team

**Requirements**

*   Bachelor’s degree
*   3 to 5 years of prior CPG experience in a role which utilizes data and analysis to add value to clients. Interest in Market Research, consumer insights, account management, or category management preferred
*   Strong expertise in all MS Office products, Excel, PowerPoint, & Word
*   Demonstrated ability to work across various data sets and reporting tools as well as capability in analytics, business math, and AI/LLMs; demonstrated expertise in translating data and analysis into relevant implications.
*   Advanced interpersonal skills and strong orientation towards building client relationships.
*   Excellent time management and organizational skills; able to set priorities and meet deadlines in a fast-paced, multitasking environment.  Relentless adherence to deadlines.
*   Very strong oral and written communication skills; confident in presenting to groups.
*   Highly detail oriented with commitment to total quality and data accuracy.
*   Proactive and collaborative work style Seeks out and engages learning opportunities/apply key learning Builds and leverages mutually beneficial relationships and networks internally.
*   Demonstrated strong understanding of client issues. Ability to develop creative ways to address business issues.
*   Financial management-manages to budget, understands account financial dynamics, suggests ways to be more efficient.
*   Experience with and curiosity about relevant industries a plus.
