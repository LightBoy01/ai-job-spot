### Description\nDÜS Eckert is the leading provider of German and integration courses in Düsseldorf and Essen, having successfully taught German to over 30,000 refugees and migrants since 2004, significantly contributing to their active societal participation. Specializing in integration courses, German exams, and customized online language courses for innovative companies, DÜS Eckert is expanding its team.\n\nWe are seeking a **full-time Customer Advisor (40 hours per week) for Farsi/Dari/Pashto speakers** at our Essen main station location. You will work Monday to Thursday until 5:30 PM or 7:00 PM, and Fridays until 4:00 PM. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys working with participants from diverse cultures in a fast-growing social education company.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **First Point of Contact:** Serve as the initial point of contact for our participants, ensuring they feel comfortable at DÜS Eckert through a friendly and competent approach.\n- **Consultation:** Provide optimal advice to participants regarding their questions about our courses and exams, in person, by phone, and via email.\n- **Registration:** Register participants for German courses and exams, carefully processing applications for various federal offices.\n- **Participant Concerns:** Address participant concerns and complaints, coordinating solutions in close collaboration with teachers, administration, and school management.\n- **Teamwork:** Work closely with other customer advisors and the administration team.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Languages:** Native-level proficiency (at least C1) in **German** and **Farsi/Dari/Pashto** is essential. Fluent English (at least B1) is also required.\n- **Multilingualism:** Proficiency in additional relevant languages such as Ukrainian, Russian, Arabic, etc., is a plus.\n- **Social and Intercultural Competence:** Enjoy interacting with people, be an attentive listener, empathetic, and comfortable in intercultural contexts (essential).\n- **Communication:** Possess excellent communication skills, work in an organized and reliable manner (essential).\n- **Professional Experience:** Prior experience in customer consulting, customer service, or sales is preferred.\n- **Passion for Languages:** Enthusiasm for language learning is optional but welcomed.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity to join a purpose-driven organization that makes a tangible difference in people's lives. DÜS Eckert prides itself on an outstanding team culture, where colleagues are friendly, competent, and committed to mutual support and continuous learning. New hires receive thorough onboarding and ongoing internal and external training opportunities. The company provides modern equipment and excellent accessibility, with offices directly at the main station, and supports commuting costs. Crucially, DÜS Eckert champions diversity as its foundation, welcoming applicants from all backgrounds and explicitly stating zero tolerance for discrimination, creating a truly inclusive and welcoming environment for both employees and participants. This role is perfect for someone passionate about language, culture, and social impact, seeking a supportive and growth-oriented workplace.
