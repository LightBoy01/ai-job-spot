At Instructure, we believe in the power of people to grow and succeed throughout their lives. Our goal is to amplify that power by creating intuitive products that simplify learning and personal development, facilitate meaningful relationships, and inspire people to go further in their education and careers. We do this by giving smart, creative, passionate people opportunities to create awesome. 

  

And that's where you come in:

  

  

As the Senior Manager, Content Marketing, your role will be responsible for helping to craft, execute, and measure content marketing plans designed to drive greater visibility & interest in the Instructure brand, and its product portfolio within the education/learning spaces.

  

Your role will also be instrumental in developing new content marketing tactics to showcase the deep expertise of Instructure’s thought leadership team, strengthening our position as a leader in the learning space, and to differentiate Instructure from our competitors.

  

As a member of the Content Marketing/Social Team, you will work closely with channel experts and internal content creators to craft compelling, channel-appropriate content in service of the larger content marketing strategy.

  

This role reports to the Senior Director, Content Marketing. 

  
  
As the Senior Manager, Content Marketing, your role will be responsible for helping to craft, execute, and measure content marketing plans designed to drive greater visibility & interest in the Instructure brand, and its product portfolio within the education/learning spaces. Your role will also be instrumental in developing new content marketing tactics to showcase the deep expertise of Instructure’s thought leadership team, strengthening our position as a leader in the learning space, and to differentiate Instructure from our competitors. As a member of the Content Marketing/Social Team, you will work closely with channel experts and internal content creators to craft compelling, channel-appropriate content in service of the larger content marketing strategy. This role reports to the Senior Director, Content Marketing.   
  

This range reflects our target hiring range, with flexibility based on experience, skills, and market factors.

  
  
**What you will do:**  
*   Support the development and implementation of content marketing strategies that align with business goals and customer needs.
*   Lead in developing best practices for content creation and distribution by channel.
*   Monitor, analyze, and frequently communicate content performance, establishing KPIs and benchmarks; recommending areas to optimize/improve along the content marketing planning timeline.
*   Maintain strong relationships with stakeholders for cohesive execution of content marketing campaigns.
*   Develop approaches that tap into the strengths of AI-based search engines, in order to drive wider visibility of our content.
*   Aid in content creation across written, video, and audio-based media.
*   Work with internal community teams to gather valuable customer insights that may help steer future content marketing strategies.
*   Support Senior Director, Content Marketing in crafting content marketing reporting/leadership presentations.
  
  
**What you will need to know/have:**  
*   4-6 years content marketing experience in a SaaS-based organization.
*   Proven experience in creating B2B content marketing plans (Education vertical preferred) with strength in written content creation.
*   Knowledge of Gemini, Copilot, and other AI-powered search engines, and the role content marketing plays within them.
*   Degree in Marketing, Journalism, or Communications, or equivalent experience.
*   Paid digital media experience (preferred).
