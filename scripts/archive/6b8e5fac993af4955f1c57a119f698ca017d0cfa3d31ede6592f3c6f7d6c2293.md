AVL Hungary Kft. offers a job in Hungary (HU) as

**Accounts Payable Specialist**
===============================

**RESPONSIBILITIES**

*   Handle supplier calls and emails, resolving invoice and payment issues.
*   Receive, scan, and process invoices in line with company procedures.
*   Manage non-compliant documents and update workflow systems.
*   Post and block invoices for payment; handle recurring and non-PO transactions.
*   Process supplier and intercompany payments, including urgent requests.
*   Reconcile supplier statements, investigate discrepancies, and provide status updates

**REQUIREMENTS**

*   Bachelor's degree
*   3-5 years of relevant professional experience
*   Experience in IFRS reporting or other international GAAP´s
*   Profound SAP knowledge
*   Good knowledge MS Office (EXCEL and WORD)
*   English language knowledge, German is an advantage
*   Ability to work in a team, customer orientation, good communication skills
*   Analytical and decision-making skills
*   Willingness to travel abroad 

**WE OFFER**

*   Group accident insurance, private medical insurance, free fruits
*   Flexible working time, home office possibility (60%)
*   International projects, business trips
*   Travel reimbursement
*   Relaxation and fitness room
*   Cafe and restaurant in the office building
*   Company buses between Budapest and Érd

#### **ABOUT AVL HUNGARY KFT.**

AVL Hungary Kft., founded in 2002, has become a leading research and development center in almost all areas of powertrain systems in recent years by combining the 100-year tradition of the Hungarian vehicle industry and the innovative activities of the global company.

The Hungarian company's team of more than 500 professionals works in a high-tech development environment not only in the headquarters in Érd, but in Budapest, Zalaegerszeg and Kecskemét. The team is operating primarily in the fields of R&D - planning, simulation, testing, software development, calibration, and production planning.

The results of the work of our colleagues and the technical innovations created by AVL are used in passenger cars, heavy vehicles, construction machinery and ship industries of five continents: AVL Hungary participates in national and international projects, works with the most modern tools, and develops the powertrains of the future.
