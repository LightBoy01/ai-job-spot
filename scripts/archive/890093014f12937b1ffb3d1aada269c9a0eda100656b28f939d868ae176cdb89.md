**About the role**
------------------

We are seeking a strategic and results-driven **Manager – Procurement** to lead our procurement function and drive value across our supply chain. This role is pivotal in ensuring cost-effective purchasing, supplier relationship management, and compliance with procurement policies and procedures.  This role will be based at our **Brisbane office**.

**What you will be doing**
--------------------------

*   Developing and implementing procurement strategies aligned with business objectives.
*   Leading sourcing initiatives and negotiating contracts to achieve optimal value.
*   Managing supplier performance, risk, and compliance.
*   Collaborating with internal stakeholders to understand procurement needs and deliver solutions.
*   Monitoring market trends and identify opportunities for cost savings and innovation.
*   Ensuring procurement activities comply with legal and ethical standards.
*   Leading, mentoring, and developing a high-performing procurement team.

**What you will bring**
-----------------------

*   Relevant tertiary qualifications in Business, Supply Chain, or a related field.
*   Proven experience in procurement management, preferably in mining or heavy industry.
*   Strong negotiation, analytical, and strategic thinking skills.
*   Excellent communication and stakeholder engagement abilities.
*   Proficiency in procurement systems and tools (e.g. SAP).
