### Description\nOur client, located in Munich, is seeking a dedicated and experienced HR Specialist (m/f/d) to join their team. This role is crucial for maintaining efficient HR operations, ensuring accurate data management, and providing comprehensive support throughout the employee lifecycle. The position offers a flexible work arrangement, including part-time hours (20-30 hours/week) and home office possibilities, within a collaborative and growth-oriented environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Manage and maintain employee personnel files and HR data within the company's systems.\n- Prepare and issue essential HR documents, including employment contracts, references, certificates, and other personnel-related correspondence.\n- Support the onboarding process for new employees, ensuring a smooth and welcoming integration into the company.\n- Coordinate appointments, manage communication with internal and external stakeholders, and handle general administrative tasks within the HR department.\n- Actively participate in HR process optimization initiatives and take on special assignments or project-based topics as required.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed commercial training (e.g., Industrial Clerk, Office Management Clerk, Wholesale Clerk), ideally with a focus on Human Resources, or an equivalent qualification.\n- Proven experience in HR administration, specifically including the creation of employee references.\n- Demonstrated structured, meticulous, and discreet work ethic, coupled with the ability to work independently, proactively, and goal-oriented.\n- Ideally, experience or knowledge of SAP HR systems.\n- Excellent German language skills (very good written and spoken).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced HR Specialist looking for a part-time role with significant flexibility and a supportive company culture. The emphasis on professional onboarding, continuous learning, and flat hierarchies suggests an environment where employees are valued and encouraged to grow. With the chance to contribute to process optimization and enjoy home office options, this role offers a well-rounded package for someone who thrives in a collaborative and dynamic HR setting while maintaining a healthy work-life balance.
