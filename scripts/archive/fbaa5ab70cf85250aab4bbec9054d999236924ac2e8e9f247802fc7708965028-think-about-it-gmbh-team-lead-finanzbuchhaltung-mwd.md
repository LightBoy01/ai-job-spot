### Description\nthinkGROUP is a successful system house group constantly striving for innovation and placing great value on the continuous optimization of internal processes. The use of state-of-the-art technologies is not an end in itself but an expression of our innovative spirit and lived customer orientation. Excellent IT service requires optimal systems. Our motivated team gives its best every day to inspire our customers anew.\n\nWe are looking for you to join us as soon as possible as a new team member at our **Bochum** location. Become part of the think Group and support us as **Team Lead Financial Accounting (m/f/d)**. This role offers the chance to actively shape the future of our financial processes while benefiting from extensive professional development opportunities and a flexible, supportive work environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Functional and organizational leadership of the accounting team\n- Ensuring timely and proper bookkeeping\n- Preparation of monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements according to HGB (German Commercial Code)\n- Responsibility for accounts receivable, accounts payable, and payment transactions\n- Further development and optimization of accounting processes\n- Support with budget planning, forecasts, and business analyses\n- Collaboration with auditors, tax advisors, and internal departments\n- Active participation in the digitalization and automation of accounting processes\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed commercial training with further education as a certified accountant (Bilanzbuchhalter) or a degree in economics\n- Several years of professional experience in financial accounting, ideally in an IT or service environment\n- Initial leadership experience or a clear desire to take on a leadership role\n- Very good knowledge of HGB and proficient use of accounting and ERP systems\n- Analytical understanding, high reliability, and a structured way of working\n- Strong communication skills, team spirit, and enthusiasm for change and process optimization\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for an ambitious finance professional to step into a leadership role within an innovative IT system house. The thinkGROUP offers a dynamic environment where you can actively drive the digitalization and optimization of financial accounting processes. With a strong commitment to employee development, demonstrated by an annual training budget and individual promotion, alongside a focus on work-life balance through hybrid work and \"workation\" options, this role truly empowers its employees. Beyond professional growth, the company fosters a strong team spirit, offering numerous benefits from a fitness studio to bike-leasing and social activities, making it an attractive workplace for those seeking both challenge and support.
