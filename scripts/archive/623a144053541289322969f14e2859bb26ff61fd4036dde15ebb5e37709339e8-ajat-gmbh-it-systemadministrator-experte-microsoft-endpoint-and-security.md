### Description
AJAT GmbH is a dynamic technology, information, and internet company with a small, dedicated team of 1-10 employees. Our corporate culture is built on values such as challenge, courage, creativity, and determination. We emphasize education, empathy, empowerment, and equality, while fostering honesty, openness, and personal responsibility. Passion, performance, and respect are as crucial to us as selflessness, social justice, and accountability. Sustainability and teamwork are central to our trust-driven work.

We are looking to strengthen our team with an IT System Administrator Expert for Microsoft Endpoint & Security. This position demands profound knowledge and extensive experience in Microsoft technologies, alongside the ability to effectively implement and manage security solutions. Join a team that values innovation and excellence, working collaboratively towards our company goals.

### Responsibilities
*   **Hybrid Endpoint Architecture:** You will be the architect behind our entire Microsoft Endpoint Manager environment, independently administering, optimizing, and developing our infrastructure, comprising **SCCM (MECM)** and **Microsoft Intune**.
*   **Autopilot & Provisioning Expert:** You will design and manage our zero-touch rollouts, with Windows **Autopilot** as your standard tool for deploying new clients.
*   **Professional Software Packaging & Distribution:** You will be responsible for **packaging** (e.g., MSI, MSIX, IntuneWin) and distributing complex applications and updates within the hybrid environment.
*   **Automated Patching Excellence:** You will ensure system security through comprehensive patch management (Windows & 3rd-Party). Your expertise in **Patch My PC** will be critical for automation and compliance.
*   **Linux Administration:** You will manage and maintain our primarily server-based Linux environments (e.g., Debian/Ubuntu/RHEL), ensuring their operation and security.
*   **Network & Security:** As a subject matter expert, you will analyze network and security issues in client and server environments (Firewall rules, VPN, Conditional Access, Endpoint Protection) and collaborate closely with relevant departments.
*   **3rd-Level Escalation:** You will serve as the final escalation point for complex incidents related to clients, servers (Windows/Linux), software distribution, and network connectivity.

### Qualifications
*   **Experience:** A minimum of 5 years of professional experience as a System Administrator with a demonstrable focus on Endpoint Management and infrastructure.
*   **Microsoft Expert Knowledge (Must-Have):** In-depth practical experience and expert-level understanding of the following technologies:
    *   **Microsoft Intune** (device configuration, compliance policies, app deployment)
    *   **Windows Autopilot** (zero-touch deployment, ESP)
    *   **SCCM / MECM** (co-management, OS deployment, application catalog)
    *   **Patch My PC** (setup, configuration, troubleshooting)
*   **Packaging & Scripting (Must-Have):** Proficiency in **software packaging** and scripting with **PowerShell** to implement complex requirements.
*   **German Language Skills (Must-Have):** Fluent German language skills, both written and spoken (minimum C1 level), are a mandatory prerequisite for documentation and internal communication.
*   **Linux Knowledge (Important):** Solid practical experience in administering common Linux distributions.
*   **Profound Expertise:** Very good knowledge in IT security (Endpoint hardening, Defender for Endpoint) and related network technologies (TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, VLAN, Firewall management).
*   **Location:** You will primarily work at our office location in **Cologne**.
