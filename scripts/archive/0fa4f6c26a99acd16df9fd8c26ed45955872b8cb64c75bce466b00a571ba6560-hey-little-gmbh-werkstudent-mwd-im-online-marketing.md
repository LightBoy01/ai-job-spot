This role offers an exciting entry point into a mission-driven team, focused on transforming how families approach baby product consumption. First experience with Content Management Systems.\n    - Basic knowledge of SEO and SEA.\n    - Familiarity with common marketing tools.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for a motivated working student passionate about digital marketing and sustainability.
