### Description

This role is located in **Munich, Germany**, and operates within a **hybrid work environment**, requiring presence in the office 3+ days/per week.

[Tulip](https://tulip.co/) is a leader in frontline operations, dedicated to equipping workforces with connected apps that enhance quality, improve efficiency, and enable end-to-end traceability across operations. Companies of all sizes and industries leverage Tulip's cloud-native, no-code platform to implement composable solutions for critical operational challenges, including error-proofing, boosting productivity, real-time data capture and analysis, and continuous improvement.

A spinoff of MIT, Tulip is headquartered in Somerville, MA, with additional offices in Germany and Hungary. Focused on human-centric solutions for industrial environments, Tulip is actively disrupting the Manufacturing Execution System (MES) category. The company has been recognized as a World Economic Forum Global Innovator and named one of Energage's Top Workplaces USA, as well as one of Built In Boston's "Best Places to Work" and "Best Midsize Places to Work" for 2024.

**Working at Tulip:**

Tulip is building a strong, diverse team that values hard work, families, and personal well-being. We understand that even great candidates experience imposter syndrome, and we encourage you to apply even if you don't match every single requirement. 

Benefits of working with us include:

*   Direct impact on product and culture
*   Company equity
*   Competitive benefits package, including commuter benefits, learning and development benefits, and team events
*   Catered lunches 3 days a week
*   Flexible work schedule and unlimited vacation policy
*   Virtual company events and happy hours

Tulip is an equal opportunity employer committed to building an inclusive community that transforms frontline operations. ### Responsibilities

*   Consult customers and prospects to prepare their companies for digital transformation.
*   Work closely with sales executives to effectively progress opportunities through the pipeline and secure the \"technical win\".
*   Develop, present, and deliver high-impact technical demonstrations of the Tulip solutions on a daily basis (Demos, technical presentations, technical discussions, etc.).
*   Clearly articulate the benefits of Tulip's applications to all levels, including line of business managers, \"C\" level executives, and IT.
*   Provide comprehensive technical pre-sales support to Tulip's channel partners and strategic partners, including developing and training personnel on product demonstrations, product positioning, and competitive overviews to leverage our free trial in many cases in tandem with the strategic seller.
*   Identify and collaborate with prospect technical stakeholders to align around Tulip's solution and secure commitments needed to ensure a \"technical close\".
*   Manage and maintain internal requests using our internal systems.
*   Provide input to other cross-functional departments pertaining to prospect requests and product enhancements as needed.
*   Facilitate brainstorming sessions and consensus building.
*   Perform needs gathering and requirement analysis for new customers.
*   Analyze and track reseller competencies and technical delivery.
*   Help develop and answer RFx (RFI, RFP, RFQ), technical assessments, and technical/security questionnaires.
*   Lead in the scoping and estimation of implementation projects delivered by the Tulip Professional Services Team.

### Qualifications

**Desired Profile:**

*   Experience in Manufacturing or Industrial operations, or a strong interest in this field.
*   Passion for Digital Transformation in the Manufacturing space, with a focus on driving value for customers.
*   Proactive in improving systems at scale, implementing best practices, and enhancing methodology and quality of execution within the Digital Transformation Engineering Team.
*   A natural team player who thrives in an agile environment.
*   Eager to work cross-functionally to improve Tulip's exposure and decrease the sales cycle.
*   Lives in the Munich Area and can speak, write, and comprehend German fluently.

**Required Skills & Experience:**

*   BS degree in Engineering (Mechanical, Industrial, Chemical, Computer Science). MSC degree is a plus.
*   5-10 years of enterprise SaaS pre-sales experience.
*   10+ years of valuable experience in Manufacturing or Industrial operations.
*   Experience in selling to or working with manufacturing companies to facilitate their digital transformation journey.
*   Excellent business writing, presentation, and communication skills.
*   Fluency in both English and German is required.
*   Team player with strong interpersonal skills and the ability to take a leadership role to eventually manage a team.
*   Passion for technology and the ability to speak fluently about current trends related to SaaS, PaaS, IIoT, and cloud solutions.
*   Detail-oriented individual with the ability to quickly assimilate and apply new concepts, business models, and technologies.
*   Travel: This role will involve some travel, typically between 10-50%, depending on demand.

**Preferred Experience:**

*   Experience with systems such as MES/MoM, LES/LIMS, WMS, QMS, ERP, SPC, IIoT, etc.
