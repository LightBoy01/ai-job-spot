### Description\nJoin a small, ambitious team in Berlin-Mitte, part of a boutique agency and academy focused on innovation management, organizational transformation, and leadership development. We empower Europe's innovators and leaders to create real impact. As a Learning Designer, you will be instrumental in developing and enhancing our learning programs in innovation, AI, and leadership. You'll craft high-quality learning experiences, from on-site trainings to innovative online courses and comprehensive capability-building programs, making complex topics accessible and practical in close collaboration with our consulting team. Our clients include leading companies like LiveEO, GetYourGuide, Novo Nordisk, and Roland Berger.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Conceptualize end-to-end learning experiences, including program architectures, storytelling, and session agendas, implementing them using various LMS tools.\n- Develop curricula for innovation, leadership, change, and AI transformation in collaboration with consultants and subject matter experts.\n- Translate expert knowledge into clear, accessible, and inspiring learning content (theory, exercises, case studies, peer-coaching elements, workbooks, etc.).\n- Facilitate workshops, group coaching, and training formats, both online and on-site.\n- Support program operations, ensure customer-oriented participant management, and continuously improve content based on feedback.\n- Conduct user research and needs analyses to adapt programs to client requirements.\n- Assist in preparing proposals for new clients and projects.\n- Contribute creatively to the development of new formats.\n\n### Qualifications\n- At least 2 years of experience in Learning Experience Design or a comparable role.\n- Proven ability to develop curricula and creative in-person and digital formats.\n- Strong skills in storytelling, structuring, and creating learning content.\n- Confident facilitation abilities (e.g., peer coaching, training sessions).\n- Passion for topics such as Leadership, Innovation, Transformation, and AI.\n- Excellent team, communication, and collaboration skills.\n- Creative, solution-oriented working style with high speed and flexibility.\n- Technical affinity and proficiency in using LMS, authoring tools, and AI tools.\n- Fluent in German and English.\n- Bachelor's or Master's degree in a relevant field (e.g., Education, Psychology, Business, Design).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a creative and impact-driven Learning Designer to join a dynamic Berlin-based boutique agency. You'll work with an ambitious, collaborative team dedicated to fostering continuous learning and growth, both for clients and internally. The role offers significant influence in shaping cutting-edge programs in AI, leadership, and innovation for major European organizations, combined with competitive benefits, flexible work, and direct mentorship from experienced founders. If you're passionate about transforming complex knowledge into engaging experiences and thrive in a fast-paced environment, this role promises significant professional development and the chance to make a tangible difference.
