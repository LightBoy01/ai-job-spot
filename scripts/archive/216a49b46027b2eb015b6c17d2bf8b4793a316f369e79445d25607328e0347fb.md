**About the Role**

We are seeking a highly skilled and motivated Senior Software Engineer to join our innovative AI team. This is a critical role in developing and enhancing Litify's AI-driven products. You will be instrumental in building scalable software solutions and contributing to a mature AI product line.

**Key Responsibilities:**

*   Design and develop AI-enabled platforms and features directly integrated into the Litify Practice Management platform.
*   Translate business requirements into scalable technical architectures.
*   Contribute to the automated accuracy assessment for ongoing testing and improvement of AI models.
*   Work with Anthropic Claude LLMs within a closed AI ecosystem via Amazon Bedrock.
*   Ensure client data remains in a private, walled-off cloud environment and is not used to train broader models.
*   Collaborate with product, engineering, design, and QA to refine scope, validate acceptance criteria, and establish technical solutions within our SCRUM workflow.
*   Provide technical expertise and business acumen to deliver best-in-class technology solutions.

**Qualifications:**

*   10+ years of experience building scalable software solutions and integrating enterprise platforms.
*   Strong background in technical architecture and solution engineering.
*   Proficiency in AI, Node, AWS Cloud, Azure Cloud, Java/J2EE, JavaScript, ExtJS, Spring Boot.
*   Experience with Salesforce solutioning, data analytics, and infrastructure modernization.
*   Ability to lead cross-functional teams and present complex solutions to senior stakeholders.
*   Familiarity with Langfuse, Langchain, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, MySQL, Weaviate, ChromaDB, Reactjs, HTML CSS, and JavaScript.

**Preferred Qualifications:**

*   Familiarity with Langfuse, Langchain
*   Experience in the legal domain.
*   A proven record of simplifying technology adoption for both technical and non-technical audiences.
