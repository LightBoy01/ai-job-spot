### Description
Logistica OS is an AI-native software company on a mission to revolutionize the logistics industry by bringing efficiency and intelligence to workflows. Having successfully closed their pre-seed funding from leading VCs, they are now scaling their business and seeking motivated individuals to join their team.

They are looking for **Founders Associate Interns** to take on significant responsibility and ownership from day one. This high-impact role involves leading and supporting projects across the entire organization, providing a comprehensive learning experience within an early-stage startup. Working directly with the founders and leadership team, the intern will not only contribute ideas but also actively help drive the company's vision forward.

### Responsibilities
*   **Project Management**: Work simultaneously on diverse and important projects involving multiple internal and external stakeholders.
*   **Growth Experiments**: Own and drive new growth initiatives with the potential to transform the company's trajectory.
*   **Operational Playbooks**: Implement and improve operational processes across the organization to enable scalable growth.
*   **Entrepreneurial Drive**: Exhibit a solutions-driven mindset, capable of thinking creatively and outside the box.

### Qualifications
*   Excellent communication skills in English, both written and verbal.
*   First-hand experience in a fast-paced environment such as a startup, investment banking, or consulting.
*   Exceptional analytical skills, a data-driven mindset, and a proven ability to take ownership of impactful projects.
*   High motivation to contribute to building a startup from the ground up.
*   A hands-on mentality and a willingness to go above and beyond to achieve results.
*   Critical thinking and strategic insight to evaluate and implement optimal outcomes.
