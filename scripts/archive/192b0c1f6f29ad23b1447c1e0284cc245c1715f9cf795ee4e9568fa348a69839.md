**About the Role:**
===================

**Grade Level (for internal use):**

09

**The Team:**

Join S&P Global's AI engineering team as a Mid-Level AI Developer, where you'll design, develop, and deploy production-ready AI applications for global financial markets. You'll collaborate with experienced technologists to build intelligent platforms using cutting-edge Generative AI and NLP technologies, while accelerating your career in a fast-paced, innovation-driven environment.

**The Impact:** This role is essential for the Market Intelligence Group of S&P as it ensures the development of new software solutions and continuous improvement and stability of our existing applications. Role will enable our clients with a seamless user experience and access to up-to-date data, ultimately bolstering their confidence in our services and reinforcing our competitive position in the market.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   **Production AI Development:** Design, develop, and deploy production-ready AI applications using LLMs, RAG architectures, and modern ML frameworks for internal and external clients.
*   **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Partner with product managers, QA engineers, and data platform teams to deliver scalable, high-quality AI solutions that directly impact business outcomes.
*   **Full-Stack AI Engineering:** Build end-to-end AI systems using Python, modern ML frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch), cloud services (AWS/Azure), and vector databases.
*   **Quality & Best Practices:** Write clean, maintainable code following SOLID principles, participate in code reviews, and contribute to CI/CD pipelines (Azure DevOps or similar).
*   **Continuous Learning:** Stay up-to-date with emerging AI technologies; participate in innovation initiatives, proofs of concept, and team brainstorming sessions.
*   **Documentation & Transparency:** Document features and technical decisions in Jira/Confluence to ensure clarity and accountability.
*   **Production Support:** Lead feature integration, provide L2/L3 support for AI systems in production, and troubleshoot complex issues to maintain 99.9% platform reliability.

**Required Qualifications**

*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related technical field.
*   4-7 years of software development experience.
*   1+ years of hands-on experience developing and deploying AI/ML solutions, including Generative AI, NLP, or related domains preferred.
*   Proficiency in Python (C# or JavaScript/Angular experience is a plus).
*   Proven experience with cloud platforms (AWS or Azure) and modern CI/CD tools (Azure DevOps, Jenkins, or similar)
*   Hands-on experience with OpenAI APIs, RAG patterns, and vector database implementations
*   Experience with microservices architecture and containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)
*   Strong analytical thinking and communication skills with ability to translate business requirements into technical solutions

**Preferred Skills**

*   Experience with model deployment platforms (MLflow, Kubeflow, or SageMaker)
*   Knowledge of prompt engineering, fine-tuning techniques, and LLM optimization
*   Familiarity with financial markets or financial data processing
*   Understanding of AI ethics, responsible AI practices, and model governance
*   Knowledge of security and compliance considerations for enterprise AI systems
*   Track record of innovation through hackathons, research publications, or open-source contributions.
