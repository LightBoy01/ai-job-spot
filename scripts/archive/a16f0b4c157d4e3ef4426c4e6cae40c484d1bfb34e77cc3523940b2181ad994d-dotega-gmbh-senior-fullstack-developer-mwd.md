### Description\nDo you enjoy taking responsibility and thinking in solutions instead of tickets? As a Senior Fullstack Developer (m/f/d), you will make architectural decisions, develop complex features, and ensure code quality and structure – with significant freedom to actively shape our platform. This role offers the chance to take ownership and build a product with real impact. You'll also benefit from company stock options, an accessible workplace, professional development opportunities, a company laptop, a fitness subscription, flexible working hours, and complimentary drinks.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- You take responsibility for the architecture and code quality of our platform.\n- You develop features in both frontend and backend (Java/Spring Boot & Angular).\n- You further expand our platform, focusing on scalability, stability, and performance.\n- You act as a sparring partner for the team, coaching/mentoring and assisting with technical decisions.\n- You work closely with Product & Design, actively contributing your experience.\n- You implement solutions pragmatically, ensuring we ship quickly, cleanly, and sustainably.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Several years of experience in developing modern web applications (fullstack).\n- Excellent knowledge of Java / Spring Boot and Angular (or comparable frameworks).\n- Strong technical understanding of architecture, cloud & scalability.\n- You love clean code, clear structures, and enjoy thinking one level deeper.\n- Strong communication skills, team-oriented, with a natural leadership mentality.\n- Bonus (not mandatory): Experience with CI/CD, Docker/Kubernetes, or cloud platforms (AWS/GCP/Azure).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting opportunity for a seasoned Fullstack Developer who thrives on ownership and technical leadership. The role offers substantial autonomy to influence architectural decisions and shape a critical product, moving beyond just ticket resolution. With a focus on scalability and quality, alongside a collaborative team culture and attractive benefits like flexible hours and stock options, it's a perfect fit for someone looking to make a significant impact and grow professionally.
