Your career is our mission, and your development is truly important to us.\n\nAre you passionate about vehicle technology and eager to actively shape the future of chassis and brake components? Bring your expertise to the table and design modular solutions for global vehicle platforms.\n\nOur client, a global player in the automotive industry, is currently seeking project-based reinforcement. You can expect a broad range of responsibilities and a friendly work atmosphere. If this sounds interesting to you, then become a part of Univativ and turn your dreams into reality!\n\nThis full-time project role (40 hours/week) is based in Stuttgart within the automotive sector, with an expected duration until January 1, 2028, and a salary range of €74,000 to €84,000 per year. We welcome both entry-level and experienced professionals.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   You will be responsible for the development and support of chassis and brake components across all vehicle series, including axles, steering, suspension/damping, brake control systems, as well as wheels and tires.\n*   You will also collaborate within the team for service and parking brake systems, focusing on the conception, design, testing, and release of all brake components within the overall system.\n*   Designing modular braking solutions for new platforms, considering global market requirements, is also part of your responsibilities.\n*   Additionally, you will undertake tasks such as design and CAD space analyses, test planning, and vehicle build supervision.\n*   Test execution, data evaluation, and report generation complete your task profile.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   A completed degree in Mechanical Engineering, Automotive Engineering, Mechatronics, or a comparable qualification.\n*   Profound knowledge in vehicle technology with a focus on brakes, experience in component design, test execution and evaluation, and proficient use of project management tools.\n*   Experience with Siemens NX is an advantage, along with proficiency in MS-Office.\n*   Independent, structured working method with high initiative and willingness to learn complex technical topics.\n*   International travel readiness and driving licenses Class BE and C1E.\n*   At least business-fluent German and English skills, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an ambitious Engineer passionate about automotive technology to make a significant impact on future vehicle platforms.
