### Description\nNeraya Agency specializes in providing professional, efficient, and empathetic outsourced customer support via email and chat for a diverse client base. We are dedicated to delivering friendly, solution-oriented, and reliable service, believing that exceptional results come from individuals who approach their work with joy and a strong sense of self-responsibility. We are looking for passionate Freelancers who excel in clear communication, enjoy independent work, and value open, respectful collaboration. This is a fully remote, freelance role offering significant autonomy and ample opportunity for personal initiative and growth.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Expertly handle customer inquiries via email and chat, directly representing our clients.\n- Communicate effectively with customers in German, and occasionally in English. Translation tools are permitted and encouraged.\n- Maintain meticulous documentation, ensuring all interactions are friendly, solution-oriented, and align with client brand guidelines.\n- Operate with a high degree of independence and self-organization, consulting leadership for complex issues when necessary.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A reliable, independent, and structured approach to work, with strong organizational skills.\n- Excellent written communication skills in German (friendly, clear, error-free). Basic English is a valuable asset.\n- A genuine enjoyment for communication and a natural inclination to help others resolve queries effectively.\n- Comfort and ability to thrive in an open, trusting, and collaborative remote work environment.\n- Previous customer support experience is beneficial but not required, as comprehensive training is provided.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis freelance opportunity with Neraya Agency is ideal for those seeking substantial autonomy and the flexibility of remote work. It provides a clear path for income growth, enabling freelancers to significantly scale earnings by taking on more clients. Neraya cultivates a deeply trusting and supportive environment, offering new and experienced professionals the tools and guidance needed to excel in customer support. The balance of independent work with a robust support network makes this an appealing long-term partnership for self-starters focused on fair communication and mutual respect.
