**Principal Technical Content Developer**

Ground-breaking products deserve to be accompanied by world-class technical information. Dell Technologies customers expect nothing less. That’s where our Content Engineering & Translation team comes in. Working side-by-side with editors and illustrators, subject matter experts and stakeholders, the team creates clear, concise and easy-to-understand content for multiple channels. It takes an ability to research, analyze and synthesize complex subject matter. And it’s all underpinned by an understanding of how our technologies work and how they benefit customers.

Join us to do the best work of your career and make a profound social impact as a **Technical Content Developer 4** on our **Content Engineering & Translation** team in **Bangalore**.

  
**What you’ll achieve**

Dell provides the technology that transforms the way we all work and live. But we are more than a technology company — we are a people  
company. We inspire, challenge and respect every one of our over 100,000 employees. We also provide them with unparalleled growth and  
development opportunities. We can’t wait for you to discover this for yourself as a Principal Technical Content Developer on our Content  
Engineering & Translation team.  
  
**You will:**

*   Help define and evangelize Dell’s global content vision and future roadmap
    
*   Champion advanced practices in API-first documentation, UX writing, and/or AI-driven knowledge delivery
    
*   Build and nurture a culture of innovation and excellence
    
*   Influence executive stakeholders with data-driven content insights.
    

  
**Take the first step towards your dream career**  
Every Dell Technologies team member brings something unique to the table. Here’s what we are looking for with this role:  
  
**Essential Requirements**

*   12+ years in technical structured authoring (DITA, XML, Markdown), with proven leadership at scale 
    

*   Recognized expertise in information experiences, API/UX content, AI-assisted knowledge systems, and/or content transformation 
    

*   Strong executive presence and ability to influence at C-level and senior stakeholders 
    

*   Visionary mindset with ability to link content strategy to business outcomes 
    

**Desirable Requirements**

*   Deep expertise in LLMs, prompt engineering, taxonomy/ontology, and knowledge graphs, with a proven ability to influence predictive, personalized, and AI-optimized content ecosystems at enterprise scale.
