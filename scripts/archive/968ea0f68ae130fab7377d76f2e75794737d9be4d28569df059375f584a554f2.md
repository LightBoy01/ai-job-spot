**What We Do:**

Florence software advances cures by helping the world’s most important research sites do their best work. Our solutions are now used by over 30,000 research teams in 70 countries around the world—we’re the most widely deployed site workflow tool in the industry. By the end of the decade, we’ll double the pace at which new medicines get to market by doubling the output of trial site teams. To date, we were named a Deloitte Fast 50 business, G2 Category Leader, an Inc. & AJC best place to work, and an Inc. 5000 company five years in a row. 

At Florence, we are committed to make the world a better place by accelerating research while providing an environment for our employees where they can be happy in their lives, enjoy their jobs, and grow. 

### **What You’ll Bring to the Team**

As the VP of Product, AI & Workflow Automation – Clinical Trials, you’ll define and lead Florence’s Site Portfolio and products strategy — shaping the vision, roadmap, and execution that drive the digital transformation of clinical trials.

As a member of the COO team, you’ll partner closely with engineering, design, marketing, sales, and customer experience to ensure our products don’t just meet market needs — they redefine what’s possible in clinical research.

This is a hands-on, high-impact role for a product leader who thrives at the intersection of **strategy, innovation, and execution**.

### **You Will:**

#### **Strategy & Vision**

*   Define and drive Florence’s global product strategy and multi-year roadmap across all customer segments — Sites, Sponsors, and CROs.
*   Align product priorities with Florence’s mission to automate and simplify clinical trial operations.
*   Translate customer insights and market dynamics into strategic direction and tangible product outcomes.

#### **Customer & Market Insight**

*   Build deep relationships with customers, users, and partners to uncover emerging needs and opportunities.
*   Identify and anticipate macro-trends in life sciences and technology that will shape the future of clinical operations.
*   Champion a customer-centric mindset throughout the organization.

#### **Execution & Delivery**

*   Lead the end-to-end lifecycle of Florence’s SaaS platform — from concept to launch to adoption.
*   Partner cross-functionally with engineering, design, marketing, and revenue teams to deliver products that drive engagement, retention, and long-term value.
*   Establish and track product metrics and OKRs that guide performance and decision-making.

#### **Team Leadership**

*   Recruit, coach, and scale a world-class product management organization.
*   Foster a culture of accountability, curiosity, and innovation.
*   Develop your team’s skills and careers through coaching and structured growth opportunities.

#### **Thought Leadership**

*   Serve as a strategic thought partner on Florence’s executive team.
*   Act as a public evangelist — sharing Florence’s story, purpose, and vision with customers, partners, and industry audiences.

**An Ideal Candidate Has:**

*   12+ years of product management experience, with 5+ years in senior or executive leadership roles.
*   Proven success leading SaaS products from concept to market adoption in a high-growth environment.
*   Experience operating in or adjacent to regulated industries such as life sciences, healthcare, or fintech.
*   Demonstrated ability to scale product organizations globally and manage distributed teams.
*   Strong executive presence and communication skills — able to inspire, align, and influence across all levels.
*   Track record of defining and delivering strategies that drive growth, adoption, and customer value.
*   Combination of large-company scale and startup agility is ideal.
