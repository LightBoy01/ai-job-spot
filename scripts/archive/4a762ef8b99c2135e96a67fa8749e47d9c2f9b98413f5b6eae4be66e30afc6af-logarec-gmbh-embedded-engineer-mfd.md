  impacting worker safety and operational efficiency.

### Description
Join a dynamic team focused on pushing the boundaries of embedded technology for critical applications. This role encompasses the entire lifecycle of embedded software and hardware development, with a specific emphasis on camera-based systems. You will play a crucial part in developing, testing, implementing, and debugging cutting-edge solutions, ensuring their efficiency, stability, and scalability across a diverse range of high-stakes industries.

### Responsibilities
*   Manage the entire lifecycle of embedded software and hardware development.
*   Develop, test, implement, and debug camera embedded software with a strong focus on hardware functionality, functional safety, cybersecurity, and explosion protection.
*   Ensure the efficiency, stability, and scalability of system resources across various industries including maintenance, railway technology, aerospace, logistics, energy, and security & defense technology.

### Qualifications
*   Solid practical experience in Embedded C/C++, Python, FPGA programming (VHDL/Verilog), and Jetson platforms.
*   Proven knowledge in PCB design (KiCad/Altium or similar), high-speed interfaces, and MIL-STD compliant development processes.
*   Expertise in cryptographic security (AES-256, Secure Boot, FIPS), real-time systems, and multi-sensor fusion (camera/LIDAR/GPS).
*   Proficient German or very good English skills.
