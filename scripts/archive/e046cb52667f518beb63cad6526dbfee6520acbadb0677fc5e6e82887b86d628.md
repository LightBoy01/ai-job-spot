Lockheed Martin Space is seeking a Mechanical Engineer Summer 2026 Intern for our Englewood, Colorado site. In this role, you will manage hardware components within a ballistic missile, provide technical solutions, and own a component throughout different phases of the product life-cycle (qualification, production, service support, aging, etc.). The successful candidate will have experience and/or knowledge of mechanical engineering and design, propulsion, and project management. Must be a US Citizen; this position will require a government security clearance. Clearance is not required to start. This position is located at a facility that requires special access.

### Basic Qualifications

\- Currently enrolled in a BS degree or higher in one of the following majors: Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Material Science Engineering, or a related degree from an accredited university or college.  
  
\- Experience in mechanical design  
\- Proficiency in Microsoft Office  
  
\- Must be a US Citizen and eligible for a government security clearance.

### Desired skills

Experience in the following:  
\- Rocket or Aerospace Clubs  
\- Formula SAE, Formula Baja  
\- Machining Experience  
\- Other hands on engineering experience  
\- Any combination of exposure to: engineering project management, supplier management, annual budget proposals, technical evaluations, tracking and development of cost/schedule  
\- Exposure to or participation with failure investigations, root cause analysis, and/or corrective action dispositions
