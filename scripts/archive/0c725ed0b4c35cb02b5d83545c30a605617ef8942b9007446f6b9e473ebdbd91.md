**About this opportunity:**
Are you looking for a thesis project that puts you at the forefront of AI innovation? This master’s thesis invites you to join Ericsson’s mission to reimagine the supply chain through Agentic AI — autonomous, goal-oriented AI systems — and contribute to breakthrough technical architectures. You will focus on enhancing the Excess Management solution currently under development by integrating AI agents into Ericsson’s global supply chain operations. Working alongside technical architects, supply chain experts, and AI researchers, you will help design future-ready architectures that align with Ericsson’s IT standards and strategic vision.
**What you will do:**
*   Apply cutting-edge Machine Learning and Agentic AI concepts to optimize supply chain operations.
*   Embed AI capabilities into scalable, robust technical solutions.
*   Evaluate and improve the Excess Management solution with practical, actionable enhancements.
*   Design architectures anticipating future AI agents, aligned with enterprise IT standards.
*   Bridge technical designs with long-term business goals to deliver measurable value.
**The skills you bring:**
*   Strong understanding of supply chain processes.
*   Proficiency in Machine Learning, AI concepts, and hands-on solution development with structured data.
*   Analytical thinking and proven research skills.
*   Ability to connect deep technical insights with strategic business outcomes.
*   Collaborative, curious, and results-oriented mindset
If you’re ready to take your research from theory to impact, apply today and help shape the future of AI-powered supply chain solutions.
**Why join Ericsson?**
At Ericsson, you´ll have an outstanding opportunity. The chance to use your skills and imagination to push the boundaries of what´s possible. To build solutions never seen before to some of the world’s toughest problems. You´ll be challenged, but you won’t be alone. You´ll be joining a team of diverse innovators, all driven to go beyond the status quo to craft what comes next.
