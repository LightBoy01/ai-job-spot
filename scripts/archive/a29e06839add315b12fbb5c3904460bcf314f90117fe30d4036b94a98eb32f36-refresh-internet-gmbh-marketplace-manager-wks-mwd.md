### Description\nTo further strengthen our team, we are looking for a motivated Working Student in Marketplace Management who will support us in operational and strategic marketplace business and gain valuable insights into the dynamic world of e-commerce. This role offers a unique opportunity to contribute to a growing company within an innovative and family-like environment, fostering learning and personal development.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Support in daily operational marketplace business (e.g., Amazon, eBay, and Kaufland).\n- Maintenance and optimization of product listings, including text, image, and price updates.\n- Analysis of sales figures, conversion rates, as well as competitor and market trends.\n- Collaboration in the creation and implementation of marketplace campaigns.\n- Support in quality assurance, e.g., checking product data, inventory levels, and delivery times.\n- Close collaboration with the E-Commerce and Marketing team to improve performance.\n- Opportunity to contribute own ideas for process optimizations or new listings.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Enrolled student (m/f/d), ideally in Business Administration, Marketing, E-Commerce, Communication Sciences, or a similar field of study.\n- First experiences in e-commerce or on online marketplaces are an advantage, but not a must.\n- Analytical mindset and affinity for numbers as well as digital tools.\n- Structured, meticulous, and independent way of working.\n- Very good German language skills (written and spoken); good English skills are a plus.\n- Proficient in MS Office, especially Excel; experience with shop or marketplace tools is an advantage.\n- High motivation, willingness to learn, and enjoyment of dynamic, digital work environments.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a motivated student eager to dive into the dynamic world of e-commerce. The company offers a highly supportive, innovative, and family-like work environment characterized by flat hierarchies and a strong emphasis on fostering creative ideas and continuous learning. With flexible working hours, an attractive compensation package, and benefits like free refreshments, regular team events, and employee discounts, this role provides an ideal foundation for gaining practical experience and making a real impact in a growing digital business.
