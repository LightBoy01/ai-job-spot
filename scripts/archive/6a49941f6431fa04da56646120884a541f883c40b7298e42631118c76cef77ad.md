Lila Sciences is the world’s first scientific superintelligence platform and autonomous lab for life, chemistry, and materials science.  We are pioneering a new age of boundless discovery by building the capabilities to apply AI to every aspect of the scientific method.  We are introducing scientific superintelligence to solve humankind's greatest challenges, enabling scientists to bring forth solutions in human health, climate, and sustainability at a pace and scale never experienced before. **Your Impact at Lila**
Lila Sciences is excited to offer an internship opportunity designed to immerse you in the development and enhancement of our cutting-edge automated workcells. As an intern, you will play a critical role in identifying and resolving operational challenges while contributing to the innovation that drives our mission of scientific superintelligence. This position will provide you with valuable hands-on experience in a dynamic and collaborative setting, helping you develop a diverse set of skills applicable to your future career in science and technology.
**What You'll Be Building**
*   Identify, categorize, and resolve issues related to automated Workcells.
*   Troubleshoot software and electro-mechanical systems effectively, learning new technologies swiftly and documenting the processes for team knowledge sharing.
*   Proactively identify potential failure points and lead initiatives to mitigate them.
*   Develop automated processes or tools to help achieve Lila's goals.
*   Collaborate on significant projects to gain practical experience and make a tangible impact, including projects focused on proactive issue identification, preventive maintenance, and AI integration into troubleshooting operations.
**What You’ll Need to Succeed**
*   Currently pursuing or have recently completed a degree in a STEM field or a related discipline.
*   Demonstrates curiosity and a strong hands-on approach to learning and problem-solving.
*   Ability to rapidly learn and master a diverse array of technical subjects.
*   Working knowledge of software principles
*   Demonstrated proficiency in mechanical and technical tasks, with hands-on experience with physical equipment and components.
*   Strong problem-solving abilities and effective collaboration and communication skills.
*   Adaptable and receptive to feedback, fostering growth and development in a dynamic work environment.
*   Contributes positively to team dynamics, encouraging and enhancing the performance of peers.
**Bonus Points For**
*   Experience with predictive modeling and statistical analysis for issue identification.
*   Familiarity with AI-based systems and integration into operational processes.
*   Previous exposure to database design and flowchart development.
