### Description
univativ is a project and personnel service provider dedicated to connecting students and graduates with exciting jobs at renowned companies, making career development its core mission. Our client, a global player in the automotive industry, is seeking project-based reinforcement for their team. This role focuses on ensuring comprehensive compliance in hazardous material management within aftersales logistics. You can expect a broad spectrum of tasks, a friendly work atmosphere, and the flexibility to work remotely. This is an excellent opportunity for experienced professionals to contribute to environmental sustainability within a dynamic corporate environment.

### Responsibilities
- Ensure compliance in hazardous material management for aftersales logistics by implementing all relevant legal obligations for hazardous substances.
- Review and evaluate safety data sheets for products containing hazardous substances.
- Contribute to the further development of the environmental management system according to ISO 14001 and the implementation of the environmental sustainability strategy.
- Support the development of new methods, procedures, and processes to improve and minimize risks in hazardous material management.
- Provide expert advice to aftersales logistics on all hazardous material issues and actively participate in project groups to ensure compliance with corporate and legal environmental protection requirements.

### Qualifications
- Completed degree in natural sciences with a specialization in environmental protection/environmental management or a comparable field.
- Profound knowledge of regulatory details in the area of hazardous substances and products, especially European hazardous substance law (REACh, CLP, etc.).
- Expertise in the creation, review, and evaluation of safety data sheets.
- Good knowledge of MS Office (especially Excel and PowerPoint), SharePoint, and Access.
- Fluent in both German and English.
- Analytical, networked, process-oriented, and strategic thinking.
- Strong service orientation, persuasiveness, and assertiveness.
