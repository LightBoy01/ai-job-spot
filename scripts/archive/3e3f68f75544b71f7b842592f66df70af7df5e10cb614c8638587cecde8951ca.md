Fisher & Paykel is an iconic global brand with a strong local heritage. Our origins inspire our culture of curiosity and passion to develop solutions designed around people - these are some of the core elements that make us unique.  We are driven by our values of being trusted, sustainably minded, innovative, generous, and curious. **Senior SEO & Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) Lead – Global Impact | Digital**

We’re looking for a senior search leader to shape the future of our brand visibility — across traditional search and Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) for AI-driven discovery.

Why this role?

*   Lead global & local strategies optimised for Google SGE, Bing Copilot, Perplexity, and more.
*   Build entity-rich, AI-ready content that ranks in generative answers.
*   Influence product, UX, and content strategies at a senior level.

What you’ll do

*   Drive integrated SEO & GEO strategies across all markets.
*   Ensure multi-modal readiness: text, voice, image, and video.
*   Lead AI search performance optimisation and reporting.

What you’ll bring

*   7–10+ years in SEO/digital with proven GEO expertise.
*   Track record of success in AI search visibility.
*   Thrives in change with a relentless curiosity for what’s next in search.

Apply now and lead us to the front of the AI search race.
