### Description\nQuasiris GmbH develops the **Quasiris Search Cloud (QSC)**, a modern AI-powered search solution for online shops and digital platforms. Our team of experienced software developers and architects makes complex technologies simple and accessible, successfully implementing projects with strong partners for renowned clients.\n\nTo further develop our products visually and functionally, we are looking for someone who thinks, designs, and technically implements **User Experience** and **modern Interfaces**. You will be the **missing link between frontend and backend** – or between frontend and our full-stack teams. You understand technical requirements, but at the same time, you think from a user's perspective and bring both together. You will develop modern UI/UX concepts for our Search Cloud tools and then implement them yourself in the frontend.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Analyze requirements in collaboration with Product & Backend teams.\n- Conceptualize **UX flows**, wireframes, and design system components.\n- Design modern, clear **User Interfaces** for our products and dashboards.\n- Implement your concepts using **Vue.js**, HTML/CSS, and JavaScript.\n- Coordinate with Backend/Fullstack developers to optimally utilize API structures.\n- Continuously improve the usability of existing interfaces.\n- Take significant creative freedom and responsibility for visual product development.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Degree in Computer Science, Media Informatics, UX Design, Digital Product Development, or similar.\n- Experience in both UI/UX Design AND Frontend Development.\n- Technical understanding of Backend APIs (REST/JSON), data models, and workflows.\n- Practical knowledge in **Vue.js** (or React/Angular).\n- Proficient in **Figma** or similar design tools.\n- Structured way of working, a feel for user guidance, and an eye for detail.\n- Eagerness to work both **conceptually** and **hands-on**.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a versatile UI/UX professional who loves both design and development. Quasiris offers significant creative freedom and direct impact on a core product with a clear vision. You'll be part of a small, motivated team that values quality and pragmatism, operating with flexible hours and home-office options. It's an ideal environment for someone looking to truly shape a product's visual and functional experience from concept to code.
