**Job Title:** Associate Director – Generative AI (GenAI)

**Location:** Pan India (Preference for Bengaluru)

**Experience:** 12+ Years

**Company:** Deloitte

**Job Summary:**

Deloitte is seeking an experienced Associate Director to lead Generative AI and advanced data science initiatives within the Technology & Transformation practice. The candidate will drive innovation in AI/ML and GenAI applications across industries, manage teams, and deliver scalable AI solutions using foundation models and large language models (LLMs).

**Key Responsibilities:**

*   Lead design, development, and deployment of GenAI-powered enterprise applications leveraging LLMs (e.g., GPT-4, Claude, Gemini).
*   Oversee prompt engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and embedding-based search solutions.
*   Manage projects spanning summarization, content generation, chatbots, copilots, document understanding, and personalization use cases.
*   Integrate GenAI solutions with enterprise systems and cloud platforms (Azure, AWS, GCP).
*   Utilize AI/ML platforms and tools including LangChain, LlamaIndex, Hugging Face Transformers, Pinecone, FAISS, and Weaviate.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams including data scientists, engineers, and business stakeholders to define problem statements and GenAI architectures.
*   Ensure responsible AI practices—governance, bias mitigation, explainability, and traceability—are embedded in solutions.
*   Lead technical discussions with clients and mentor junior team members.
*   Contribute to development of GenAI accelerators, assets, and intellectual property within Deloitte’s AI practice.
*   Support pre-sales, solutioning, and proposal development related to GenAI offerings.

**Required Qualifications:**

**Education:**

*   Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, AI/ML, Data Science, or related fields.

**Experience:**

*   12+ years in AI/ML, data science, or related roles, with 3+ years hands-on experience in Generative AI, LLMs, or NLP solutions.
*   Proven experience managing teams and delivering AI-driven enterprise solutions.
*   Strong expertise in statistical analysis, machine learning, deep learning, and analytical models (e.g., marketing mix models, segmentation, demand forecasting).
*   Experience deploying AI/ML and GenAI solutions on cloud platforms like Azure, AWS, or GCP.

**Skills:**

*   Deep knowledge of transformer models, embeddings, fine-tuning, and prompt engineering.
*   Expertise in architecting scalable GenAI pipelines and integrating them with enterprise systems.
*   Strong leadership, communication, and stakeholder management skills.

**Preferred Qualifications:**

*   Certifications in AI/ML or cloud platforms (Azure AI Engineer, AWS ML Specialty).
*   Familiarity with LLMOps tools for model tracking, evaluation, and monitoring.
*   Experience working in regulated industries such as BFSI, healthcare, or legal.
*   Prior consulting experience with client-facing roles.
