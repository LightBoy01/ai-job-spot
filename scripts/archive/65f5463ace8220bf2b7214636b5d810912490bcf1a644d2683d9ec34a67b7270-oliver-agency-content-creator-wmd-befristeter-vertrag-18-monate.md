### Description
Established in 2004, **OLIVER** is the world's first and only specialist in designing, building, and running bespoke in-house agencies and marketing ecosystems for brands. We partner with over 300 clients in 40+ countries, leveraging a unique model that drives creativity and efficiency, allowing us to deliver tailored solutions that resonate deeply with audiences.

As a part of **The Brandtech Group**, we are at the forefront of leveraging cutting-edge AI technology to revolutionize how we create and deliver work. Our **AI solutions** enhance efficiency, spark creativity, and drive insightful decision-making, empowering our teams to produce innovative and impactful results.

We are seeking a **Content Creator** (fixed-term contract - 18 months) to join our team in Hamburg, Germany. This role requires an on-site presence of at least 2-3 days per week.

As our Content Creator, you will be the creative voice behind some of the country's most beloved nostalgic food brands. You will create impressive foodie content that brings these icons to life on social media platforms, from enticing recipe videos to trend-oriented storytelling. You will work closely with Social Media Managers, Strategists, Influencer Managers, and Performance Teams to develop creative content that resonates with target audiences and has a significant impact across various markets.

### Responsibilities
**Content Creation & Production:**
*   Create mouth-watering, platform-specific foodie content for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and emerging channels.
*   Shoot and edit short videos, static posts, Reels, and Stories that make our brands stand out in the feed.
*   Collaborate with designers and videographers to produce high-quality, easily digestible content.

**Trend Spotting & Innovation:**
*   Stay consistently up-to-date on foodie and social media trends, from viral TikTok recipes to new content formats.
*   Boldly experiment with new tools, transitions, and storytelling techniques to make your content indispensable.

**Collaboration & Integration:**
*   Work closely with Social Strategists and Influencer Managers to align content with campaign goals.
*   Collaborate with Performance Marketing Teams to optimize content for paid amplification.

**AI-Driven Content Ideation:**
*   Utilize generative AI tools to explore creative variations and increase efficiency in content workflows.
*   Use AI-driven insights to inform content planning and creative decisions.

### Qualifications
*   2–4 years of experience in social content creation, ideally in FMCG or multi-brand environments.
*   Native speaker in German (C2) and fluent in English (spoken and written).
*   In-depth knowledge of social media platforms, content formats, and best practices.
*   Proficiency in design and editing tools (Adobe Creative Suite, Canva, CapCut).
*   Familiarity with AI-powered tools for content ideation and optimization.
*   Excellent storytelling skills and the ability to adapt tone and style across various brands.
*   Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously.
*   Experience working with multiple markets and understanding cultural nuances.
