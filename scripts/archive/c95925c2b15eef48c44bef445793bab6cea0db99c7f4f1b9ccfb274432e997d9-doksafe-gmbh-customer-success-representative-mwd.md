### Description\ndokSAFE GmbH is a Leipzig-based software company specializing in the SaaS solution ACTAPORT. We are dedicated to revolutionizing the work environment in law firms with our innovative law firm software. We are looking for an empathetic and skilled Customer Success Representative (m/f/d) to join our team, starting January 1, 2026, on an unlimited contract.\n\nIn this role, you will be the crucial interface between our valued customers and our internal teams. With your keen eye for improvements and commitment to customer satisfaction, you will actively contribute to the continuous development of our services and ensure our customers don't just use the ACTAPORT platform, but remain enthusiastic and engaged long-term. If you love empowering people to achieve their goals and possess a knack for explaining complex matters clearly, we encourage you to apply!\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Act as an expert in customer management and the primary point of contact for our ACTAPORT customers, advising them on process implementation within ACTAPORT and integrated third-party partner tools.\n- Handle customer inquiries with empathy and efficiency via email, chat, and individually arranged support appointments, providing personalized assistance during their onboarding process.\n- Collaborate closely with customers to build trust, help them achieve their desired goals, and cultivate strong, long-term relationships and loyalty.\n- Serve as the voice of our customers, actively collecting their suggestions for optimization and providing valuable internal feedback for product and service improvements.\n- Beyond direct customer interaction, contribute to the expansion of our internal and external knowledge bases and support sales colleagues with your expertise.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You consistently demonstrate a customer- and solution-oriented approach to your work, combined with reliability and strong independence.\n- You are highly organized and possess excellent time management skills.\n- Very good German language skills (C-Level) and strong communication abilities (both verbal and written) are essential for empathetic interactions with both customers and colleagues.\n- Ideally, you have at least one year of experience in customer service; however, career changers are heartily welcome and encouraged to apply.\n- Initial experience with CRM applications and ticketing systems is a definite advantage.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a proactive and customer-focused individual to join a progressive software company that is truly transforming the legal tech landscape. The Customer Success Representative role offers significant influence on customer satisfaction and product evolution, all within a supportive and inclusive team environment. dokSAFE GmbH champions work-life balance, flexible and hybrid work options, and provides comprehensive benefits, including substantial personal and professional development opportunities. It's an ideal setting for those looking to make a tangible impact and grow their career in customer success, even for talented career changers eager to learn and contribute.
