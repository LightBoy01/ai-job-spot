### Description
Our client is a successful mechanical engineering company with approximately 500 employees, renowned for its high technical competence and innovative strength. They develop and produce sophisticated machinery and equipment for a diverse international clientele.

In line with their commitment to digital advancement, the company is continuously optimizing its ERP landscape and adapting it to evolving business processes. They are seeking a dedicated ERP Administrator (m/f/d) who can combine strong IT expertise with a deep understanding of mechanical engineering operations. This role is perfect for someone passionate about actively driving digitalization and process optimization within a dynamic environment.

### Responsibilities
*   Administer and maintain the ERP system.
*   Ensure system availability and optimize performance.
*   Perform regular system updates and patches, as well as adapt and develop interfaces to other systems (e.g., financial accounting, materials management, purchasing).
*   Conduct monitoring and troubleshooting to guarantee smooth operations.
*   Manage users, assign rights, and create role and authorization concepts.
*   Adapt and configure the ERP software according to the requirements of specialist departments.
*   Support the implementation of new modules and functionalities.
*   Create and maintain technical documentation.
*   Collaborate with external service providers and resolve technical issues.
*   Provide training and support to end-users on ERP system utilization.

### Qualifications
*   Completed vocational training or university degree in computer science, business informatics, or a comparable qualification.
*   Several years of experience in ERP system administration.
*   Profound knowledge of ERP software, ideally in common systems such as SAP, Microsoft Dynamics, or Oracle.
*   Experience in database administration and SQL is an advantage.
*   Strong analytical and technical skills.
*   High problem-solving competence and a structured approach to work.
*   Strong communication skills and ability to work in a team.

### Author's Take
**Question:** How will my contributions as an ERP Administrator directly impact the company's ambitious digital transformation goals, and what opportunities are there for professional growth within this evolving landscape?

**Answer:** Here, you're not just maintaining a system; you're truly at the heart of our digital evolution. We're continuously optimizing our ERP landscape, and your insights will be crucial in shaping how our core business processes run – from finance to logistics. You'll be bridging the gap between IT and our specialist departments, seeing the tangible impact of your work on efficiency and innovation for our international clients. As we grow and integrate new technologies, you'll have ample room to develop your expertise, potentially delving deeper into specific modules, interface development, or even mentoring new team members.

**Answer:** We truly believe a stable system starts with a supported team. While the ERP is vital, we prioritize an open and appreciative culture, reflected in our flexible working hours and generous 30 days of annual leave. We understand that life happens, and we empower our employees to manage their time effectively. We also invest in robust monitoring tools and clear processes to minimize urgent issues, ensuring you're not constantly firefighting. This isn't just about 'benefits'; it's about building a sustainable, long-term workplace where you feel valued and have the space to thrive both professionally and personally.
