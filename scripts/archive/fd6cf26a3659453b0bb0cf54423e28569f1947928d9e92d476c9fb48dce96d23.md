**About Pryon:** 

We’re a team of AI, technology, and language experts whose DNA lives in Alexa, Siri, Watson, and virtually every human language technology product on the market. Now we’re building an industry-leading knowledge management and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) platform. Our proprietary, cutting-edge natural language processing capabilities transform unstructured data into meaningful experiences that increase productivity with unmatched accuracy and speed.

  

  

**The Opportunity:**

  

We are seeking an exceptional Strategic Partnerships Manager to lead our high-performing partner team and own some of our most important accounts — including NVIDIA and other hyperscaler and global alliance partners.

  

This player-coach role is ideal for a commercial and technically sophisticated operator who can guide senior partner managers while also directly managing high-value, complex partnerships. You will be responsible for driving strategy, operational execution, and revenue outcomes across key partner ecosystems — including cloud providers, global consultancies, systems integrators, and AI infrastructure companies.

  

You’ll be expected to provide executive-level stewardship, sales coaching, and technical insight — while rolling up your sleeves to manage partner relationships yourself, especially in high-impact accounts like NVIDIA.

*   **Leadership & Team Management**
*   Directly manage a team of 3–5 Strategic Partner Managers
*   Coach ICs on deal strategy, execution, and technical positioning
*   Set performance goals, measure outcomes, and drive accountability
*   Foster collaboration across Product, Sales, Solutions, and Marketing

  

*   **Executive-Level Partner Management**
*   Own strategic relationships with high-stakes partners, including NVIDIA
*   Lead executive alignment, joint planning, and multi-threaded partner engagement
*   Navigate technical and commercial complexity across large enterprises
*   Drive Pryon’s presence in hyperscaler and AI-native partner ecosystems

  

*   **Strategic Planning & Execution**
*   Define partner strategy aligned to company priorities (e.g., GCP, McKinsey, Leidos)
*   Operationalize go-to-market initiatives and co-sell motions with partners
*   Build and track joint business plans, pipeline goals, and revenue impact
*   Collaborate with leadership to prioritize and allocate team capacity

  

*   **Technical Acumen & Enablement**
*   Act as executive sponsor for proof-of-value projects with strategic partners
*   Support solutioning, technical validation, and RFP processes as needed
*   Ensure team is fluent in Pryon’s RAG and NLP capabilities
*   Elevate partner enablement programs and technical collateral
  
  
**What You'll Need to Be Successful:**  
*   **Experience & Track Record**
*   10+ years in enterprise partnerships, strategic alliances, or B2B sales leadership
*   Experience leading and scaling a team of senior ICs or partner managers
*   Proven ability to close $1M+ deals and manage long-cycle complex partnerships
*   Background in AI, cloud infrastructure, enterprise SaaS, or NLP platforms
*   Experience working with or within companies like NVIDIA, AWS, GCP, or Microsoft preferred

  

*   **Technical Competence**
*   Strong understanding of AI/ML, GenAI, RAG, and knowledge management systems
*   Able to guide solution design and discuss integrations with engineering counterparts
*   Familiarity with cloud-native architectures and partner ecosystems

  

*   **Leadership & Communication**
*   Inspiring team leader with a bias for action, clarity, and accountability
*   Adept at influencing across functions and levels, including CxO audiences
*   Exceptional communicator, both externally and internally
