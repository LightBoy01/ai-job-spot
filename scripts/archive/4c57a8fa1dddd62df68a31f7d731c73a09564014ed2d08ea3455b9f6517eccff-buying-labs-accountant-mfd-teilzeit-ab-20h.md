### Description
\"Buying Labs is the leading global partner for sourcing and distributing premium consumer goods! We are a modern, international company that values structure, figures, and team spirit. For our Finance team in Hamburg, we are seeking dedicated and experienced part-time support – someone who enjoys structured, precise work, loves working in a team, and feels at home in accounting. We are immediately looking for an Accountant (m/f/d) for our Hamburg office on a part-time basis (from 20 hours per week).\"

### Responsibilities
- Support our accounting team in accounts payable and receivable, ensuring our figures are always clean and up-to-date.
- Prepare invoices, manage payment runs, and maintain relevant accounting data in DATEV and Excel.
- Participate in the preparation of our monthly financial reports, from KPI preparation to final reconciliations.
- Organize and manage dunning and receivables, reliably keeping an eye on open items.
- Perform data checks and reconciliations between DATEV, Excel, and our internal tools to ensure everything runs correctly.
- Support analyses related to our finance and company data, and actively contribute to making processes smarter and more efficient.

### Qualifications
- Successfully completed commercial apprenticeship or a degree with a finance focus.
- Relevant professional experience in finance or accounting, acquired through permanent employment, apprenticeship, or internships.
- A strong understanding of numbers and enjoyment of careful, structured, and detail-oriented work.
- A reliable, organized way of working and a true team player mentality.
- Enjoyment of teamwork.
- Proficient in Microsoft Office, especially Excel, as well as DATEV.
- Fluent German and good English skills for collaboration in an international environment.
