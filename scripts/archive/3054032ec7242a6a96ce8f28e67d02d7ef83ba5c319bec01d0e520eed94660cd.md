**MATERIALS COACH  
DETROIT, MICHIGAN – DETROIT GRATIOT PLANT**

This role is responsible for providing oversight and management of shipping and receiving of materials, materials planning, inventory control activities, and employee engagement in a Just in Time, automotive environment.

Incumbents in this classification are primarily responsible for providing day-to-day guidance and support to High-Performance, Materials Team Members regarding planning, shipping, and receiving of materials based on usage trends, trigger points and reorder quantities.

**The Role:**

Work will include, but not be limited to:

*   Overseeing the performance of Materials Team Members (through Team Leaders) for consistent results of timely parts unloading storage, retrieval, and delivery to assigned lines.
*   Enforcing Environmental, Health, and Safety rules and regulations.
*   Maintaining excellent employee relations by inspiring and motivating plant staff to perform to their highest level of efficiency.
*   Providing training, direction, encouragement, and motivation to the Materials Team in the execution of their daily assignments.
*   Resolving team issues and collaborating to assist Team Members with problem/conflict resolution.
*   Monitoring inventory levels and processing procedures.
*   Overseeing the distribution of materials throughout the plant and ensuring deliveries meet plant needs
*   Ensuring uniform systems, procedures and training are in place to enhance the productivity and efficiency of the Materials Team.
*   Evaluating processing procedures and activities of the Materials Team and recommending/implementing improvements and modifications for higher efficiencies.
*   Partnering to optimize warehouse space while supporting operations.
*   Participating in the selection of Materials Team Leaders/Members and providing/coordinating proper training.
*   Effectively developing, mentoring to give Team Members opportunities for personal and professional growth.
*   Building and maintaining positive working relationships at all levels of the organization.
*   Monitoring maintenance of company equipment and ensuring vehicles are in good working condition- to include partnering for the coordination of availability of equipment.
*   Assisting with the cycle counting process and participating in physical inventory events to maintain inventory accuracy.
*   Participating in inter-departmental teams working towards continuous process improvement and elimination of waste.
*   Maintaining and implementing departmental procedures to ensure compliance with TS16949 standards.

**Your Qualifications:**

*   Bachelor’s degree in a related field.
*   3-5 years’ experience in materials and/or logistics.
*   Must be able to work extended periods of time and rotating shift hours.
*   Must be able to get certified in forklift and other powered vehicles.
*   Must be familiar with JIT Manufacturing Practices and AS400.
