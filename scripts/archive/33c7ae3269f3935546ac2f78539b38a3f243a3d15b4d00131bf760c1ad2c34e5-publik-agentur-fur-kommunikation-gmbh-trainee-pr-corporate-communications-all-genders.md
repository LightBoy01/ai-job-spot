### Description
Have you just completed your studies, are you passionate about communication, eager to learn consulting from the ground up, and excited about the **#agencylife**? Then Publik is the right place for you!

As one of the leading agencies in the Rhine-Neckar metropolitan region, we successfully implement communication strategies for renowned companies across various industries. Whether print or digital, B2B or B2C, internal or external communication, our team of experienced consultants and designers always finds a strategically sophisticated, creative, and sustainable solution for our clients' needs.

### Responsibilities
As a Trainee at Publik, you can expect to:

*   Get to know our entire range of services, including PR, Content Marketing, Corporate Communications, Online Marketing, and Social Media.
*   Learn how to advise clients in a way that truly helps them progress.
*   Take on responsibility early and support clients on their path to success.
*   Participate in numerous workshops and training sessions to build your knowledge and skills.
*   Work with modern AI tools and learn to use them for research, text drafts, and content formats.
*   You'll never be alone: experienced mentors will be by your side at all times.

### Qualifications
What we expect from you as a Trainee:

*   You are a Bachelor's or Master's graduate in Media, Communication, or a comparable field of study.
*   You have already gained experience in corporate communication, e.g., through internships or working student positions in agencies or companies.
*   You have initial experience in copywriting.
*   You enjoy working in a team. If you are open-minded and curious, you'll be a great fit for us!
