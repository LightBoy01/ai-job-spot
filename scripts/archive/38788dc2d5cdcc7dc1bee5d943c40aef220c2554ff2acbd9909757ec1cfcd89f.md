### Job Description

This Mid-level electrical engineering position requires,  
  
• Mid level having technical responsibility for interpreting, organizing, executing, budgeting and coordinating complex assignments  
  
• Plans and develops projects concerned with unique or controversial problems which have an important effect on major CDM Smith programs  
  
• Maintains liaison with individuals and units within or outside organization with responsibility for acting independently on technical matters pertaining to specialty field  
  
• Representing CDM Smith in conferences to resolve important questions and to plan and coordinate work  
  
• Acting as a mentor for Junior level Engineers  
  
• Understand and adherence to the CDM Smith Core values, Ethics & Code of professional conduct and familiar with compliance aspects. Understand and adherence to the Leadership Competence as defined by CDM Smith  
  
• Actively participating or assisting in new business development by contributing to strategic planning, marketing, and business operations planning.  
  
• Planning, developing, coordinating, and directing a large and important project or several small projects with many complex features  
  
• Refining your project management skills Coordinating and reviewing the work of a small staff of engineers (typically 3 to 5)  
  
• Visualizes and assumes responsibility for new business development activities within areas of expertise  
  
• Requires the ability and expertise to use advanced techniques and the modification and extension of theories, precepts and practices of Electrical design resulting from progressive experience.

### Minimum Qualifications

• Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in electrical engineering  
  
• 15+minimum years of experience in detailed design for high voltage substation projects through 345kV including:-  
• Protective relay schemes  
• Relay Schematics and wiring diagrams  
• Experience with Transmission and Distribution Type Substations.  
• Experience with AIS Substation.  
• Grounding and Lightning protection design  
• AC/DC Systems including Battery Calculations  
• Specification of Substation Outdoor Equipment including Detailed BOM.  
  
• Outside Yard – Equipment layout, Electrical Clearance Diagrams  
  
• Experience with SCADA and other Communication systems – (RTU Replacements, Fiber Equipment, Line Carrier Relay equipment)  
  
• Knowledge of NESC  
  
• Experience with Supervising and coordinating the work of CADD Operators, technicians and others assisting in specific assignments and Review of 3D model developed in AutoCAD, Revit etc.,  
  
• Preparation of Electrical one-line drawings, Site and building plan drawings to show the Electrical equipment layout.  
  
• Coordination with other disciplines at different stages to identify the problems with the Design progress.  
  
• Experience with Performing short circuit, coordination and arc flash studies using power systems analysis software (preferably SKM).  
  
• Experience with leading medium to large scale electrical design projects preferably involving power distribution, lighting, communications, fire alarm, and security systems design.  
  
• Experience with reviewing shop drawings and assisting with general construction services.  
  
• Experience with developing the design criteria, specifications and standards  
  
• Performing independently on all assignments with minimum technical/administrative instructions.  
  
• Able to devise new approaches to problems encountered and coordinates various technical aspect of work.  
  
• Able to plan, investigate, conduct work requiring judgement in the independent evaluation, selection, and substantial adaptation and modification of standard techniques procedures and criteria.  
  
• Able to participate to a limited degree in new business development in establishing and monitoring budgets to assess profitability.  
  
• Able to get updated the ongoing changes in the field of Electrical engineering and motivate the junior level staff to get into the track.  
  
• Able to perform project management function small and moderate sized projects.  
  
• Able to plan, develop, coordinate and direct several large and important projects or a project of major scope and complexity  
  
• Able to conceive, plan and conduct research in problem areas of considerable scope and complexity, problems must be approached through a series of complete and conceptually related studies, are difficult to define, require unconventional or novel approaches and sophisticated research techniques; may be assisted by other engineers/scientists or technicians.  
  
  
• Requires good communication skill and Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite (MS Word, MS Excel and MS Power Point)

### Preferred Qualifications

• Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering.  
  
• Working knowledge of the National Electrical Code (NFPA 70).  
  
• Experience in designing electrical systems for water/wastewater and other industrial/ facilities (building) projects  
  
• Professional Engineering certification from accredited agencies is preferred  
  
• Experience with reviewing the drawings/ models developed using Revit.
