### Description\nRemmert specializes in automated storage and logistics systems. We are experts in space-saving storage and fully automated movement of sheet metal, long goods, and workpieces. Mechanical engineering, automation, and logistics represent an industry with significant future prospects!\n\nFounded in 1945, we are a family-run company that embodies values such as development perspectives, communication at eye level, and short decision-making paths. We are continuously growing and seeking motivated colleagues to actively support our team.\n\nAs a **Commissioning Engineer (gn)**, you will primarily be based at our site in Löhne and travel internationally to our customers with a 50% travel readiness (approximately 100 overnight stays per year).\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Commissioning of software at national and international customer sites.\n*   Installation, simulation, and testing of software.\n*   Conducting training sessions for clients.\n*   Configuration of databases and software systems.\n*   Leading customer workshops for process definition and optimization.\n*   Performing roll-outs of system updates.\n*   Replacement and precise adjustment of electrical components.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed vocational training in automation or information technology, or a comparable qualification.\n*   Good knowledge of application installations, especially on Windows operating systems.\n*   Open to career starters or candidates with professional experience in the relevant field.\n*   An affinity for control technology is highly desirable.\n*   Strong communication skills in both German and English.\n*   Independent and reliable work ethic.\n*   Possession of a Class B driving license.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a technically skilled individual who thrives on problem-solving and international travel. Remmert offers a stable, family-oriented work environment with a strong commitment to employee growth and well-being. The company's benefits package is exceptionally comprehensive, including 30 days of holiday, hybrid work options, profit sharing, an e-bike leasing program, and extensive support for professional development and health. For someone looking to make a significant impact in the automation industry, collaborate with diverse clients globally, and grow within a supportive company culture, this role as a Commissioning Engineer offers immense potential. The blend of on-site work and international travel ensures a dynamic and engaging professional journey.
