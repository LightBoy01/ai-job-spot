### Description\nYou want to actively shape the digital sales of an internationally established company and strategically contribute your expertise in E-commerce? Then an varied role with scope for design, high visibility, and the opportunity to comprehensively develop digital growth potentials awaits you here. Seize the chance to take on a key position in the expansion of modern online sales channels.\n\nOur client is a globally active, dynamically growing company in the pharma-oriented environment. You benefit from a modern work culture, international projects, and an organization that actively promotes digital innovations. Look forward to a professional environment that combines responsible tasks, flexibility, and development opportunities.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- You are responsible for the implementation and expansion of the global E-commerce strategy in the DACH region.\n- You manage the development and expansion of our marketplace activities and further develop our E-commerce customers.\n- You take full responsibility for the support and maintenance of our Amazon activities, including optimizing product presentation, monitoring relevant KPIs, and managing sales-promoting measures.\n- You manage central Key Accounts in E-commerce and implement the multi-channel strategy in close cooperation with marketing.\n- You manage digital marketing measures on marketplaces and with E-commerce partners and are responsible for the maintenance and updating of product data as well as listing and content management.\n- You create regular KPI analyses, reports, and presentations for national and international stakeholders.\n- You report to the Business Unit Lead with a direct interface to global E-commerce management.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You have a completed degree – preferably in Marketing, E-commerce, Digital Management or a comparable field.\n- You bring practical experience from E-commerce or online marketing, ideally from a similar position.\n- You are familiar with marketplace structures like Amazon and relevant tools for performance analysis.\n- You impress with customer orientation, strong communication skills, and teamwork ability.\n- You work structured, prioritize confidently, and manage several projects simultaneously with sovereignty.\n- You are business fluent in spoken and written German and English (at least C1 level).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned E-commerce professional to take on a highly visible and strategic role within a dynamically growing, international company. With direct reporting to the Business Unit Lead and connections to global management, this position offers significant influence over digital sales strategy in the DACH region. The modern work culture, flexibility, and strong emphasis on professional development make it an ideal environment for those looking to drive innovation and advance their careers in a collaborative, global setting. The attractive compensation package further underscores the value placed on this key role.
