Do you enjoy direct contact with users and customers? Ideally, practical experience in **user support** or **B2B software training**.\n*   Proficiency in creating multimedia content (video, audio, illustration, image editing).\n*   Knowledge of common processes for introducing and operating software systems.\n*   Interest in **AR technologies** and **industrial applications**.\n*   Independent, proactive, and structured way of working.\n*   Excellent German language skills (C2) and good English language skills (B2).\n\n**You are a great fit if you:**\n*   See yourself as a trustworthy service provider and enjoy helping our customers.\n*   Want to bring problem-solving skills and strong communication to our team and enjoy communicating with people.\n*   Have an interest and talent in knowledge transfer and writing texts.\n*   Possess a quick comprehension and are good at structuring and organizing.\n*   Are technically proficient and want to engage with Augmented Reality solutions in industrial quality inspection.\n\n### Author's Take\nVisometry offers a unique opportunity to shape the customer experience for cutting-edge industrial AR/XR solutions. You'll be continuously onboarded by an expert team, with ample opportunities to develop into a true specialist in pioneering technologies and enjoy continuous further training. The role promises interesting and varied work with renowned national and international clients, allowing for independent work and personal development as you actively contribute to shaping support structures.\n\nLocated just a short walk from Darmstadt city center, the company embraces a vibrant startup atmosphere with modern, well-equipped offices, complimentary drinks, coffee, and regular team events.
