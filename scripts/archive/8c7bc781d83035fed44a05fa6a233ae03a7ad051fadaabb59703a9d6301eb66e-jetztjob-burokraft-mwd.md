### Description
Seize an exciting opportunity for a varied role within an established yet modern company in the food industry. We are looking for an Office Clerk (m/f/d) to bring their organizational talent, reliability, and enthusiasm for administrative tasks to our dedicated administrative team. This role offers a secure position within a family-owned business, supported by structured onboarding and a truly appreciative work environment. Enjoy a range of benefits including occupational pension schemes, regular vouchers, commuting subsidies, employee discounts on our products, and gifts for special occasions.

### Responsibilities
*   **Organization:** Processing general correspondence, as well as telephone and email communication.
*   **Coordination:** Supporting order processing, scheduling, and invoice creation.
*   **Communication:** Acting as a contact person for customers, suppliers, and internal departments.
*   **Data Management:** Managing and updating customer, supplier, and product data.
*   **Document Management:** Filing, archiving, and processing administrative procedures.
*   **Support:** Assisting management and other departments in daily operations.

### Qualifications
*   **Education:** Completed commercial training (e.g., office administrator, industrial clerk, or similar).
*   **Experience:** Professional experience in administration or clerical work, ideally in a manufacturing environment.
*   **Work Style:** Structured, independent, and meticulous approach to tasks.
*   **Skills:** Proficient in MS Office; ERP experience is an advantage.
*   **Personality:** Team-oriented, reliable, and strong communication skills.
