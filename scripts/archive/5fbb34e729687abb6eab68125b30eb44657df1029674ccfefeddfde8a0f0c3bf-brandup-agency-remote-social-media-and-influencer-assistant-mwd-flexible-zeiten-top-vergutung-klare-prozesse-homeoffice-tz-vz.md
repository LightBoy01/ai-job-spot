### Description
Are you looking for a **remote social media job** with **attractive compensation**, **flexible working hours**, and **clearly structured processes**? Then the position as a **Remote Social Media & Influencer Assistant (m/f/d)** is perfect for you.

Join a company where you benefit from **unlimited earning potential**, **punctual commission payments**, and **free training** that optimally prepares you for your tasks – even without prior experience. You'll be part of a **motivated, results-oriented team** where you can apply your knowledge in **digital marketing and influencer management** to actively **shape the Creator Economy**, **achieve visible results**, and be **directly rewarded** for your contributions.

Our team members describe the job as "unique" and the working environment as "fair, supportive, and inspiring." It's no coincidence that we have been repeatedly recognized for **excellent working conditions** and **above-average salaries**. We boast over 350 team members worldwide and are committed to continuous development and a strong, cohesive culture.

### Responsibilities
As a Remote Social Media & Influencer Assistant, your core tasks will include:

*   **Trend Research:** Monitor current social media trends, identify viral formats early, and select suitable ideas for our creators.
*   **Content Strategy & Briefings:** Develop clear, creative concepts and briefings to guide creators in their content execution.
*   **Personal Branding Support:** Assist in building an authentic, recognizable brand for creators and ensure consistent communication across all channels.
*   **Account Management & Posting:** Regularly publish content, keep profiles up-to-date, and ensure a professional, appealing presentation.
*   **Analysis & Optimization:** Evaluate performance data, identify areas for improvement, and strategically adjust approaches to increase reach and engagement.

### Qualifications
To succeed in this role, you should bring:

*   **Experience in online marketing, social media management, or influencer marketing** – you understand what makes content successful.
*   **A strong sense for virality and current trends** – you recognize formats that work and know how to implement them.
*   **A results-oriented approach** – you work data-driven and always keep performance in mind.

**Nice-to-have (but not essential):**
*   Experience in **copywriting**, **paid ads**, **performance marketing**, or **media buying**.
*   Basic knowledge of **video editing** or optimizing short-form video content.
