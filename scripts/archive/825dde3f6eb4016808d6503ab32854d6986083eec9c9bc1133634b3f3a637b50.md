**Would you like to impact the digital experiences of millions of users?**

**Do you enjoy solving large scale distributed systems problems?**

**Join our Edge Apps Engineering team!**

Our team tests all Products and Technologies across the Edge Apps Engineering group. We develop automation infrastructure for product testing and collaborate with the rest of the Engineering teams to qualify features. We are knowledgeable about all Delivery products and focus on System integration testing replicating real world scenarios.

**Partner with the best**

As a Software Development Engineer in Test, you will be tasked with solving critical software automation and verification problems in a distributed systems environment. Working with developers and other QA teams you will become an expert in system-wide automation infrastructure.

As a Software Development Engineer in Test, you will be responsible for:

*   Contributing to system test plans to ensure highly effective test coverage & automation.
*   Creating and deploying automated system tests to replicate real world scenarios.
*   Improving testing methodologies and procedures to enhance test automation efficiency.
*   Reviewing product & system design requirements to verify accuracy of test plans and test cases.

**Do what you love**

To be successful in this role you will:

*   Have a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or its equivalent.
*   Possess experience in developing test automation in Python.
*   Be passionate about solving large scale distributed systems problems.
*   Have exposure or interest in working with internet technologies such as TCP/IP, HTTP, HTTPS, DNS, etc.
*   Be passionate about solving QA challenges using emerging technologies such as AI and Cloud Native solutions.
