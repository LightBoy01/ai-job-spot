### Description

flatexDEGIRO AG operates Europe's leading and fastest-growing online brokerage platform. Leveraging modern, in-house, state-of-the-art technology, we offer customers of our flatex and DEGIRO brands a wide range of independent products with execution on Top TIER 1 exchanges. Our technological advantage, high efficiency, and strong economies of scale allow us to continuously improve our service offering and set leading standards in product, price, and platform quality.

With more than 3 million customer accounts and over 63 million securities transactions processed in 2024, flatexDEGIRO stands as the largest retail online broker in Europe. In times of banking consolidation, increasing digitalization, and the inherent need for millions of Europeans to take control of their financial future, flatexDEGIRO is excellently positioned for further profitable growth.

Curious about what it's like to work at flatexDEGIRO AG? Visit us on Instagram @lifeatflatexdegiro and get to know the great people who make us who we are! If you can see yourself as part of this team, we look forward to your application!

### Responsibilities

As part of your role, you can expect to:

*   Gain insights into the Securities Processing / Portfolio Transfers department of an international MDAX group.
*   Support the Portfolio Transfers team in daily business, for example, by recording new portfolio transfers or reconciling internal accounts/deposits.
*   Apply your analytical skills in reconciling booked capital measures.
*   Ensure quality standards within Portfolio Transfers & Settlement.

### Qualifications

What we are looking for:

*   You have completed at least the first two semesters of your studies with a focus on Business Administration or Economics (University, University of Applied Sciences, or similar higher education) and have an affinity for securities.
*   You are proficient in MS Office programs and possess broad knowledge in business administration and/or finance.
*   You bring strong analytical thinking and an understanding of commercial contexts.
*   You are characterized by team spirit, flexibility, and above-average commitment.
*   You enjoy challenges and are highly motivated.
