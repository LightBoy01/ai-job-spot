### Description\nAs a **Lead Data & AI Platform Engineer – Azure Data Engineering**, you will define the technical vision and strategy for data platforms on Azure. This role involves coordinating multiple projects, developing reference architectures, and ensuring robust governance and compliance. You will actively drive the adoption of new Azure features such as Fabric, Mirroring, and AI support, and represent the company to customers and partners. A flexible hybrid/remote setup is possible for candidates residing in Germany (primary residence required).\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Strategic planning and further development of the data platform, including defining standards and roadmaps for lakehouse architectures on Azure.\n- Management of the deployment of Microsoft Fabric, Azure Databricks, Delta Lake mirroring, and Unity Catalog, alongside evaluating innovations like Lakeflow and AI-based pipeline design for productive use.\n- Conducting comprehensive risk and compliance assessments, ensuring adherence to data protection, governance, and security policies.\n- Providing technical leadership to data engineering teams, encompassing coaching and crucial knowledge transfer.\n- Managing stakeholder communication in both German and English, and actively participating in pre-sales and technical workshops.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 7+ years of experience in data engineering and architecture, with proven expertise in large-scale Azure data solutions.\n- Demonstrated leadership skills, strong stakeholder management capabilities, and the ability to implement strategic programs.\n- In-depth knowledge of data governance, compliance, and secure cloud architectures, specifically with experience in Purview, Unity Catalog, and DataOps.\n- Fluent in German and very good English skills, coupled with strong overall communication abilities.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned Azure Data & AI Platform Engineer to take on a highly strategic and impactful leadership role. The position offers significant autonomy to define the technical vision, drive the adoption of cutting-edge Azure technologies (like Fabric and AI-based solutions), and lead a talented data engineering team. With a hybrid/remote work option in Germany, it combines flexibility with the demands of ensuring robust data governance, managing key stakeholders, and representing the company externally. The requirement for fluency in German and strong English underscores the importance of communication in this forward-thinking and dynamic environment.
