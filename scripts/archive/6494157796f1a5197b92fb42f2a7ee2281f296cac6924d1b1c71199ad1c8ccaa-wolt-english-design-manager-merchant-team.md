### Description

About Wolt:
At Wolt, we're building technology that brings joy, simplicity, and earnings to neighborhoods worldwide, expanding our delivery services beyond restaurant food to (almost) everything across 500+ cities in 30 countries. Having joined forces with DoorDash in 2022, we continue to dream big and grow globally. Working at Wolt is exciting and challenging; it's an environment where you'll learn, build, and ship more, surrounded by fun and opportunities for self-starters with an entrepreneurial spirit.

We are seeking a sharp, execution-driven **Design Manager** to join our **Merchant team** in Berlin or Stockholm. This team is pivotal in building self-serve, scalable 0→1 products for merchants across 23+ countries, empowering them to onboard, grow, and succeed independently. We're looking for a leader who can lead from the front, comfortable driving strategy, inspiring their team under pressure, and seeing ambiguity as an opportunity.

At Wolt, we've evolved our approach to design leadership, moving from:
*   Managing process to **leading with conviction and momentum**.
*   Raising the bar through direction and example, rather than just coaching on craft.
*   Driving clarity and vision from first principles, rather than aligning on frameworks.
*   **Shaping strategy** alongside product and engineering peers, not just interpreting it.

We seek someone who embodies taste, speed, agency, and autonomy—a leader capable of making bold calls, building alignment quickly, and executing at a high level without constant direction.

### Responsibilities

As a Design Manager, your key responsibilities will include:

*   **Build and Lead:** Leading the design team within the Merchant area, building its strategy, shaping execution, and mentoring a high-performing team.
*   **Raise the Bar:** Setting and upholding a high standard for design quality, velocity, and craft, encompassing not only visual design but also product thinking, storytelling clarity, and interaction polish.
*   **Drive Strategy:** Collaborating closely with group leads and product counterparts to actively define *what* should be built, not just *how* to build it.
*   **Move the Work:** Guiding the team through ambiguous 0→1 product development spaces, cutting through noise, unblocking delivery, and bringing strategic ideas to life through rapid execution.
*   **Coach and Elevate:** Developing and growing designers through hands-on feedback, dedicated career support, and performance management, fostering an environment that promotes both craft and pace.
*   **Leverage AI:** Harnessing AI to accelerate design exploration, enhance user insights, and increase execution velocity, empowering the team to utilize AI as a creative and strategic partner for optimizing workflows and driving data-informed decisions.
*   **Act with Urgency:** Working autonomously, proactively driving change, and prioritizing impact over process, understanding that velocity is a critical strategic advantage.

### Qualifications

We are looking for candidates who possess:

*   3+ years of proven experience leading design teams in product-led organizations, ideally within SaaS, platform, or marketplace environments.
*   A sharp eye for detail and a deep appreciation for high-quality, user-centered design principles.
*   A strong track record of building and shipping successful B2B or consumer tools across various platforms (desktop + mobile).
*   The ability to effectively shape vision and drive execution, comfortably setting strategic direction while also being willing to roll up your sleeves and get hands-on.
*   Demonstrated leadership in highly ambiguous, fast-paced environments.
*   Exceptional storytelling, cross-functional influence, and stakeholder management skills.
*   Practical experience designing for or applying AI in product workflows.
*   A mindset characterized by urgency, experimentation, and continuous improvement.
