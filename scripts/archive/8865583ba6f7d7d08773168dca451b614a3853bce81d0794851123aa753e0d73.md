### **About Our Client**

Our client is a **no-code drag-and-drop platform** designed to make building and deploying AI workflows fast and intuitive. Their platform enables companies to **visually design AI pipelines**, connecting data sources with powerful Large Language Models (LLMs) like ChatGPT to automate complex business processes.

Their customers use the platform to build solutions such as:

*   **Chatbots & Assistants:** AI agents that interact with users, answer questions, and complete tasks using internal data and APIs.
*   **Document Processing:** Applications that summarize, extract insights, and answer questions from any document, no matter the length.
*   **Database Querying:** The ability to ask GPT-like models questions directly about Notion, Airtable, Postgres, and other databases.
*   **Content Creation:** Generating tags, summaries, and transforming data and documents across various platforms.

**Role Overview:**

We’re looking for a **dynamic Sales Specialist** to spearhead our startup’s growth. This is a pivotal role focused on **prospecting and outreach**, helping us build and scale our sales pipeline from the ground up. As one of our first non-engineering hires, you’ll play a key part in **shaping and executing our growth strategy**, driving new business opportunities, and connecting with top-tier clients.

### **Key Responsibilities :**

*   **Prospecting & Outreach:** Identify and engage potential clients through strategic initiatives such as LinkedIn, email outbound campaigns, Sales Navigator, cold calling, events, and conferences.
*   **Use tools such** as Apollo, Instantly, and Sales Navigator to build and manage outreach campaigns.
*   **Meeting Generation:** Book qualified meetings for the sales team — you won’t need to run the meetings yourself, just make sure the right doors are opened.
*   **Relationship Building:** Establish and nurture connections with key decision-makers, including executives, directors, and procurement teams.
*   **Content Creation Support:** Assist in creating engaging sales content like short product videos, tutorials, case studies, and targeted email campaigns to communicate the value of our AI solutions. (Note: advanced content creation is a nice-to-have, not a requirement.)
*   **Support basic** sales enablement initiatives, such as short demo videos or outreach content, when needed.

**Requirements**

**Experience and skills :**

*   **Experience:** 3+ years in business development or sales roles, ideally within B2B SaaS or AI technology sectors.
*   **Outbound Expertise:** Hands-on experience with LinkedIn, email outbound tools, and Sales Navigator.
*   **Technical Curiosity:** While deep AI expertise isn’t required, you’re comfortable learning enough to confidently communicate product value and translate technical concepts into clear, compelling narratives.
*   **Creative & Proactive:** A self-starter who enjoys solving problems, managing multiple initiatives, and finding new ways to connect with potential clients.
*   **Proven track record** of booking meetings with large enterprises and managing complex sales cycles.
*   **Excellent English** communication and presentation skills.
*   **Hands-on experience** using modern sales tools (e.g., Apollo, Instantly, HubSpot, LinkedIn Sales Navigator).

### **Nice to Have :**

*   Basic familiarity with AI tools — you don’t need to be an expert, but curiosity and willingness to learn go a long way.
*   Experience with simple content creation, such as producing short videos or demos to showcase a product (advanced skills are a plus but not required).
