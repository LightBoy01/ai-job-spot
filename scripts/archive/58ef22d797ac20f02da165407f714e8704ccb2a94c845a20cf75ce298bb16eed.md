**Project Manager - Software / AI / Technology**  
**Experience: 10+ years**    
**Industry: Software / AI / Technology**    
**Employment Type: Full-Time**    
**Location: Dallas, TX**

**Job Description:**  

        We are looking for a dynamic and detail-oriented **Project Manager** with excellent communication skills to join our team in Chennai. The ideal candidate will be responsible for planning, executing, and closing projects while effectively managing teams, timelines, and stakeholder expectations.  

**Key Responsibilities:**  

Manage end-to-end project lifecycle including planning, execution, monitoring, and closure.  

Communicate project status, risks, and dependencies clearly to stakeholders and senior leadership.  

Coordinate across cross-functional teams to ensure project goals are met on time and within scope.  

Identify project risks and develop mitigation strategies.  

Lead regular meetings with internal teams, clients, and stakeholders to ensure alignment.  

Prepare and maintain comprehensive project documentation, plans, and reports.  

Manage changes in project scope, schedule, and costs using appropriate verification techniques.  

Drive accountability and foster a collaborative and productive team environment.  

**Required Skills & Qualifications:**  

10+ years of experience as a Project Manager or in a similar role.  

Strong verbal and written communication skills — able to manage stakeholders effectively at all levels.  

Proven experience managing multiple projects simultaneously.  

Proficiency in project management tools (e.g., JIRA, MS Project, Trello, Asana).  

Solid understanding of Agile/Scrum and Waterfall methodologies.  

Strong problem-solving, leadership, and organizational skills.  

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Business, or related field (PMP or Agile certification is a plus).
