Why join us?:

As a Senior Associate or Special Counsel, you’ll work closely with senior lawyers to deliver strategic and pragmatic legal advice across a diverse portfolio of **personal injury** and **property liability** matters. You’ll be involved in everything from coverage advice and dispute resolution to mentoring junior lawyers and driving business development.

This opportunity will be based in our Melbourne CBD office on a full-time, hybrid basis. All your technology needs will also be provided for!

Responsibilities:

*   Manage complex litigated and unlitigated files
    
*   Provide coverage advice to insurers (local and London Underwriters)
    
*   Draft and review legal documents including pleadings and settlement agreements
    
*   Appear in court and settlement conferences; instruct counsel
    
*   Liaise with external stakeholders including clients, counsel, and experts
    
*   Prepare evidence and submissions
    
*   Mentor junior team members and support training initiatives
    
*   Stay current with legal developments and industry trends
    
*   Contribute to business development and uphold firm policies
    

What will help you succeed in this role?:

*   Minimum 4 years post-admission experience in personal injury litigation
    
*   Public liability experience required; property defence experience ideal
    
*   Strong general litigation background
    
*   Excellent communication and drafting skills
    
*   Proficiency in Microsoft Office and document/time management systems
    
*   Willingness to adopt AI and new technologies
    
*   Collaborative mindset and proactive attitude
    
*   High ethical standards and attention to detail
