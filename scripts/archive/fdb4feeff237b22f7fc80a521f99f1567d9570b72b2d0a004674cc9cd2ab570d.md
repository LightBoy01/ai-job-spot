### **What you’ll do:**

As Senior Backend Engineer for our San Francisco-based Product Intelligence team, you will work closely with the product owner, designer and engineering team to shape the design and implementation strategies. Your responsibilities cover the entire development process from refining user stories, participating in scrum ceremonies, mentoring folks on the team, and naturally, writing and deploying enterprise-grade production code. 

The backend services are written in Java, Kotlin, and Rust, but this role is to develop a new Python-based backend service.  While knowledge of the other languages will be helpful, the focus for this position is to build a REST/JSON-based Python Service, including API and database schema design, which processes large scale data and uses AI/ML to analyze enterprise process data.

In this role, you will join a cross-functional team that values trust, respect, and diversity.

We are looking for people who share our passion for creating products. To thrive in this role, you are someone who appreciates close collaboration, open communication, and feedback.

### **Description**

●       Participate in the entire cycle of product development, from first ideas and brainstorming sessions to continuously delivering improvements based on customer data and feedback

●       Enhancements of existing applications as well as the inception of new microservices

●       Collaborate with Principal Engineers and other Lead Engineers to define long-term technical strategies in accordance with business needs

●       You will mentor less-experienced peers and be mentored by more-experienced peers

●       Act as functional lead for members of the Process Intelligence team by providing technical feedback, guidance, and mentoring
