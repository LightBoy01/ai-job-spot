Charlotte Pipe and Foundry, located in Huntsville, AL, is looking for a Pipe Operator. Charlotte Pipe and Foundry is a leading manufacturer of pipe and fittings. We are the only manufacturer that offers a complete system of cast iron, PVC, CPVC, ABS and FlowGuard Gold® pipe and fittings for residential and commercial plumbing systems and industrial applications. Investment in the latest tooling and machinery make it possible for us to meet increasing demand for our products and to provide the highest quality pipe and fittings in the industry. We offer a safe and productive work environment. We pay competitively based on performance and provide excellent benefits.

Charlotte Pipe and Foundry is seeking a full-time Pipe Operator. This position is responsible for monitoring the extruder and down-stream equipment and make adjustments as required, assist Maintenance associates during changeovers. Also, weigh pipe, check quality of the pipe, and operate strapper tool to secure pipe on wood boards.

**Essential Duties and Responsibilities:** include the following. Other duties may be assigned

Remove pipe from extruders.

Inspect and identify poor quality pipe using micrometer, pie tape, etc.

Package pipe per standards with strapping tool, weigh pipe and move to designated storage area with forklift.

Keep work area up to 5S housekeeping standards including operating sweepers as necessary.

Complete production quality records as required.

Set up cooling tanks and make required adjustments.

Assist operator or maintenance in repair of equipment and tool and die change.

Perform minor repairs on pipe racks, printers, etc.

Maintain constant state of alertness and ability to safely perform job duties.

Constantly communicate with extruder operator

Other duties as may be assigned by the supervisor.
