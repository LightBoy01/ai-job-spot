This is an invitation to be part of establishing a highly qualified boutique in the SAP consulting industry from the very beginning, where your ideas have a direct impact on the success of the startup.\n\nWe offer an attractive salary package with 30 days of vacation, flexible working hours including home office options, and an overtime account where you can choose between payout or additional time off.
