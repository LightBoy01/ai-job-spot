**Job Summary**
===============
As a DevOps Engineer at the AI Testing & Workflow Validation team, you will design, test, and maintain the systems and pipelines that support AI-driven workflows. You’ll ensure prompt-powered features are deployed, monitored, and validated reliably at scale across multiple environments. This role blends expertise in prompt engineering with deep knowledge of infrastructure automation, CI/CD, and system observability.
**We are seeking highly experienced professionals with at least 7 years of hands-on expertise in the areas described.**
**What you will do**
====================
*   Develop and maintain CI/CD pipelines for prompt-driven workflows and AI-generated code validation.
*   Create infrastructure-as-code and automation scripts (e.g., Terraform, Ansible) to provision, configure, and monitor validation environments.
*   Build tools and frameworks for continuous prompt testing, performance tracking, and failure analysis.
*   Implement observability and alerting for prompt workflows, capturing metrics such as latency, accuracy, and error rates.
*   Integrate security and compliance checks into the prompt development lifecycle, especially for generated code execution.
*   Collaborate with engineering, QA, and platform teams to ensure prompt workflows are seamlessly integrated into existing systems.
**What you will bring**
=======================
*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field, or equivalent experience.
*   Hands-on experience with DevOps tools and practices, including CI/CD systems (Jenkins, GitLab CI), container orchestration (Kubernetes), and infrastructure-as-code.
*   Proficiency in scripting languages such as Python or Bash.
*   Strong understanding of cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) and hybrid cloud environments.
*   Experience with system monitoring, logging, and performance tuning.
*   Excellent analytical and troubleshooting skills in complex, distributed environments.
**Nice-to-Have**
================
*   Experience deploying or managing machine learning models in production environments.
*   Familiarity with prompt-engineering principles and AI workflow validation.
*   Knowledge of security scanning, compliance automation, and canary or A/B testing for AI systems.
