**About the role:**

Join Origin as a **Senior** **Grid Modelling Engineer** and help connect Australia’s next generation of energy projects to the grid. You’ll lead technical modelling, collaborate with experts, and work with industry regulators to deliver smart, future-ready solutions. If you thrive on solving complex challenges and driving innovation, we’d love to hear from you.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Develop and manage complex grid and generator performance models to meet Generator Performance Standards for new energy projects.
*   Select and oversee specialist consultants for grid modelling, including GPS packages, MLF assessments, and constraints analysis.
*   Lead technical negotiations and maintain strong relationships with AEMO, TNSPs, DNSPs, and state-based authorities.
*   Provide expert advice and approvals on grid connection strategies and future grid options.
*   Conduct detailed analysis of current and future grid configurations to identify optimal connection points.
*   Support project development and acquisition due diligence by assessing connection risks and opportunities

**What will you bring?**

*   Engineering degree with extensive relevant experience in the Australian energy market.
*   Proven expertise in grid connection processes and regulatory frameworks within the National Electricity Market.
*   Strong understanding of AEMO’s Integrated System Plan and future grid technologies.
*   Demonstrated ability to manage technical consultants and deliver high-impact modelling outcomes.
*   Proficiency in industry-standard software tools such as **PSCAD, PSS®E, DigSILENT PowerFactory**, and other grid simulation and modelling platforms.
