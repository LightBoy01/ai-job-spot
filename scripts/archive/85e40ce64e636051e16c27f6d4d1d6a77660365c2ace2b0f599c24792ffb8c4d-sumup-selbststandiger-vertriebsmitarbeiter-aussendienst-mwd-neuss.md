### Description\nSumUp empowers small businesses with smart, user-friendly financial solutions, from card payments to POS systems, enabling them to focus entirely on their core business. As a freelance Field Sales Consultant, you are your own boss, building relationships with local businesses and demonstrating how SumUp simplifies their operations. This role offers attractive, unlimited commission based purely on your performance, along with maximum flexibility to work when and where you want. You'll be selling top-tier products with high market potential and will receive comprehensive training and sales support to ensure your success.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Actively acquire new customers: identify and win local businesses for SumUp.\n- Conduct personal meetings and provide consulting: present our products and demonstrate their benefits.\n- Build customer relationships: secure long-term partnerships through excellent service.\n- Manage your own customers and leads: you decide how to build your business.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Passion for sales and strong self-motivation.\n- First experience in sales or customer service is advantageous.\n- Strong communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.\n- Very good German language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a self-driven, entrepreneurial sales professional eager to shape their own success. SumUp offers unparalleled flexibility and unlimited earning potential through a 100% commission-based model, making it ideal for those who thrive on independence and performance. While highly autonomous, the company provides robust training and sales support, ensuring you're well-equipped to empower small businesses with leading financial solutions. Don't hesitate to apply even if you don't meet every single qualification – SumUp values potential and a willingness to learn and develop your career.
