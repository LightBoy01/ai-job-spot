We pride ourselves on individual care, personalized advice, and consistent customer orientation to deliver tailored solutions.\n\nWe are currently seeking an enthusiastic Working Student for Social Media & Marketing to join our team in Dortmund as soon as possible. If you're passionate about Instagram, LinkedIn, and developing creative content, this is your chance! If you're looking to make a tangible impact and expand your expertise beyond the classroom, this role provides a fantastic launchpad.
