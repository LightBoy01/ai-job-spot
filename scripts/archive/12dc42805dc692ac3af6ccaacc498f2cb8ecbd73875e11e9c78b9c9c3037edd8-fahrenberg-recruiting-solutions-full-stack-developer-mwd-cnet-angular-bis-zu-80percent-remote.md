### Description

Are you looking to develop software that truly makes an impact in real-world applications, beyond mere proof-of-concepts? Join a dedicated 5-person full-stack team working on an established time tracking and access control solution trusted by hundreds of companies daily. This role offers significant autonomy within a modern .NET and Angular technology stack, emphasizing clear architecture and close team collaboration.

You'll be part of a larger engineering hub of over 100 developers and engineers at the North German headquarters, collectively building innovative software and smart-tech solutions. The environment is professional, collegial, and refreshingly free from corporate bureaucracy.

The company values work-life balance, offering up to 80% remote work, flexible hours (39-hour week with flextime), 13 monthly salaries (including holiday and Christmas bonuses), and 31 vacation days plus special leave. Further benefits include company pension, Hansefit, bike leasing, subsidized canteen, team events, modern IT equipment, open communication, and a long-term perspective within a stable organization.

### Responsibilities

*   Further develop and maintain web and cloud applications based on .NET (Core / 9) and Angular 20.
*   Implement new features and REST APIs in close coordination with the Product Owner and various business departments.
*   Contribute to architectural modernizations, participate in code reviews, and advance containerization efforts (Docker, Azure DevOps).
*   Optimize performance and code quality, strictly adhering to Clean Code principles.
*   Actively engage in knowledge exchange within the team and contribute to technical decision-making.

### Qualifications

*   Several years of experience in web and full-stack development with .NET (C#) and Angular / TypeScript.
*   Solid knowledge of REST APIs, Entity Framework, and SQL databases.
*   Experience with Docker and Azure DevOps, with ideally initial exposure to Kubernetes.
*   Strong analytical thinking skills and an understanding of complex business logic (e.g., time models, shift systems).
*   Fluent German language skills (C1-level) essential for effective team and customer communication.
*   Enjoy working collaboratively in a team and commit to regular on-site exchange (at least 1 day per week in Leer, ideally 2 days).
