**Rhino Operator** 

Ready to pursue your professional journey with Northern Star? Northern Star Mining Services (NSMS) is an in-house contractor that specialises in providing underground mining services to its parent entity, ASX 50 listed Northern Star Resources Ltd (ASX: NST). As a global-scale gold miner, we have sizeable operations in Western Australia and Alaska. With unparalleled pathways for advancement and avenues for personal growth, we stand as Australia’s premier gold employer. Your journey starts here.At Northern Star, we live by our STARR Core Values of Safety, Teamwork, Accountability, Respect and Results. These Values are not just words on paper; they are the cornerstone of our Company culture and guide everything we do. You will play a pivotal role in upholding these Values and contributing to our collective success.

We have an exciting opportunity for experienced Rhino Operators to join our team who work between several of our Underground operations located in the Goldfields. Contribute to the company's success through high precision drilling and partnering with underground team members to ensure targets are achieved. FIFO from Perth available.

**Positon Details** 

*   7/7 Lifestyle Roster
*   FIFO Ex-Perth or Kalgoorlie Residential
*   Permanent Full-time

**Experience** 

*   Previous experience as a Rhino Operator highly advantageous
*   Raisebore experience may be considered

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Operate and maintain Rhino Drill Rig

*   Interpret and execute Rhino drilling as per supplied plan.

*   Transport equipment between sites as required
