### Description

Ninox is actively seeking a Senior Software Engineer with profound experience in building complex products, meticulous attention to detail, and exceptional communication skills. As a pioneering AI-enhanced low-code platform, Ninox empowers businesses to regain ownership of their processes and operate with increased efficiency by enabling anyone to create database applications for internal systems like CRM, ERP, and multi-project management without writing a single line of code.

Joining Ninox means collaborating with an inspiring, multicultural team on a sophisticated product within a rapidly expanding company. You will have a unique opportunity to shape the future of business software, directly impacting the daily operations of thousands of Ninox customers and contributing to the next major version of our cutting-edge AI-enhanced platform.

### Responsibilities

*   Apply and combine your technical expertise, best practices, and the latest technologies to build and scale our React/TypeScript frontend and Node.js/TypeScript backend, powering our cross-platform app (Web, iOS, iPadOS, macOS, Android).
*   Own features end-to-end, encompassing the design of responsive UIs and robust APIs, optimizing database performance, and managing deployment pipelines.
*   Collaborate closely with fellow Engineering team members, UX Designers, Content Writers, Product Managers, and other stakeholders to deliver new iterations of our product.
*   Elevate our automated testing strategy, monitoring, and alerting solutions to detect defects early and ship fixes rapidly, ensuring high quality and reliability for our thousands of reliant small and medium businesses.
*   Adhere to the "You build it, you run it" philosophy, taking full ownership and pride in the entire lifecycle of your code.
*   Champion clean code, advocate for robust architecture, and provide insightful, actionable code reviews.
*   Lead critical technical initiatives such as Application Architecture, Design Patterns, and Refactoring.
*   Actively mentor junior colleagues and contribute to the collective growth and knowledge transfer within the team.

### Qualifications

*   A degree in Computer Science or a comparable professional qualification.
*   7+ years of experience as a software engineer, with a strong focus on JavaScript/TypeScript for backend development and React for frontend development.
*   Demonstrated ability to transfer knowledge and learnings across various technical stacks and engineering disciplines.
*   Expertise in engineering practices, including the implementation of Test-Driven Development (TDD) and Behavior-Driven Development (BDD) paradigms for frontend development, alongside proficiency in relevant frameworks and libraries like Jest.
*   A strong belief in automation, coupled with practical experience in driving deployments, automated testing, and verification processes. Familiarity with Docker containers and cloud application hosting is essential.
*   A passion for clean code and clean architecture, evidenced by the ability to provide excellent and actionable code reviews.
*   Proven involvement in and leadership of topics such as Application Architecture, Design Patterns, and Refactoring.
*   A deep understanding of performance optimization, with the ability to build highly scalable, performant client-side applications for both web and native platforms.
*   A genuine team player, eager to mentor junior colleagues and contribute to the overall growth and success of the group.

### Author's Take

**1. Given the focus on 'owning features end-to-end' and the mention of working with an 'AI-enhanced low-code platform', how does Ninox balance rapid iteration with the need for robust, scalable architecture, especially when integrating new AI capabilities?**

As an insider, one of the most exciting challenges – and rewards – here is precisely that balance. We're not just bolting on AI; we're fundamentally rethinking how businesses interact with their data. Owning features end-to-end means you’re deeply involved in architectural decisions that make this integration seamless and scalable. It's a blend of agile development for quick wins, backed by rigorous design discussions and continuous integration that ensures stability for our thousands of customers. You'll see your code directly empower businesses, making complex tasks simple, and that real-world impact is incredibly motivating.

**2. The job mentions mentoring junior colleagues and 'growing as a group'. What does professional growth and mentorship truly look like at Ninox, beyond just technical skills?**

Our 'grow as a group' philosophy is central to our DNA. It’s not just about pushing code; it’s about elevating everyone around you. Mentorship here is organic and encouraged – from pairing sessions and internal tech talks to our transparent feedback culture. We believe in empowering you not just as an engineer, but as a leader who shapes product direction and even company practices. The founders are highly accessible, and new ideas are always welcomed. You're encouraged to step up, own initiatives, and even challenge existing norms if it means building a better product or a stronger team.
