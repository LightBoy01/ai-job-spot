### Description

Claudius Wurth Agrarberatung is a specialized consulting firm dedicated to supporting agricultural businesses. Our team assists farmers with crucial business management challenges, from farm succession planning to implementing innovative business models like direct marketing.

As a Working Student in Content Creation (m/f/d), you will have the unique opportunity to apply your creative skills within our small, dynamic team. You will be instrumental in creating and optimizing content that educates our clients on current agricultural trends and groundbreaking solutions. This role offers close collaboration with our seasoned experts, providing invaluable insights into the intricacies and opportunities of modern agriculture. If you possess a passion for agriculture and a talent for crafting engaging content, we invite you to join us.

**What We Offer:**
*   A responsible position with ample room for personal initiative.
*   Flexible working hours and the option for home office.
*   A modern work environment characterized by short decision-making processes.
*   A highly motivated and collegial team atmosphere.
*   Excellent opportunities for both professional and personal development.

### Responsibilities

*   Support the creation of content for our website and other communication channels, working closely with the consulting team.
*   Research current trends and developments in agricultural consulting to produce relevant and engaging content.
*   Contribute to the development of presentations and other materials for client meetings and internal training sessions.
*   Create and maintain social media posts to boost target audience engagement and enhance brand awareness.
*   Assist in the planning and execution of content marketing campaigns to promote Claudius Wurth Agrarberatung's services.

### Qualifications

*   Currently enrolled in a degree program in Agricultural Sciences, Business Administration, or a comparable field of study.
*   Initial experience in content creation and a keen interest in agricultural topics.
*   Excellent German language skills, both written and spoken.
*   Proficiency in MS Office and ideally experience with Content Management Systems.
*   Independent work style, reliability, and creativity.
