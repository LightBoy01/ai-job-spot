### Description
This role as an **Intern / Working Student for Automation & AI** at VISPIRON SYSTEMS places you at the forefront of digital transformation within the automotive and energy sectors. VISPIRON SYSTEMS, an R&D service provider founded in 2002 and based in Munich, specializes in accelerating vehicle development cycles and reducing warranty risks for clients through a unique combination of deep automotive system understanding, state-of-the-art test infrastructure, and project management expertise. They empower their clients to bring new technologies to series maturity in record time and with the highest quality.

As part of a team driven by curiosity, solution-orientation, and a passion for technology, you will be a key contributor to optimizing internal processes and client projects by analyzing, automating, and integrating intelligent AI systems. The company prides itself on being inspiring, open to new ideas, a strong team player, and fostering an environment where individuals take responsibility and make a tangible difference. They are solution-oriented, innovative, and enjoy taking on challenges together. This position offers an excellent opportunity to apply your academic knowledge in a dynamic, high-impact environment, whether as a full-time intern or a working student.

### Responsibilities
As an Intern / Working Student for Automation & AI, you will be instrumental in driving efficiency and innovation through intelligent automation. Your core responsibilities will include:

*   **Process Analysis & Automation:** Systematically identify, evaluate, and prioritize repetitive tasks across the company and in customer projects, aiming to significantly reduce manual efforts and process costs, striving for a minimum of 5 analyzed processes per month and over 30% time savings per automated process, with 100% documentation completeness.
*   **Tool-based Implementation:** Design and implement automated workflows using cutting-edge tools such as n8n, Make, Zapier, or custom scripts. This involves comprehensive API, webhook, and database integration to ensure robust and highly available solutions, targeting 3-5 automated workflows per month with over 90% availability and zero critical errors after rollout.
*   **KI-Integration & Intelligent Process Optimization:** Leverage AI models for advanced data analysis, pattern recognition, and decision support. This could involve developing solutions like chatbots, text classification, document analysis, or predictive automation, with a goal of conceiving and prototypically implementing at least 2 AI use-cases, achieving over 20% quality improvement through AI-supported automation, and over 90% user satisfaction.
*   **Monitoring & Continuous Improvement:** Proactively monitor, evaluate, and refine existing automations and AI models. Your goal will be to ensure maximum stability, quality, and computational efficiency, aiming for less than 1% error rate in productive automations, providing monthly performance reports with actionable recommendations, and achieving over 10% process improvement after optimization cycles.
*   **Documentation & Knowledge Transfer:** Maintain thorough and understandable documentation for all workflows, AI models, and project outcomes to ensure scalability and clarity, striving for 100% traceable documentation and conducting 2 internal knowledge sessions per quarter with over 90% positive team or project leadership evaluation.
*   **Project Support & Collaboration:** Provide direct support to ongoing client projects, working closely with project leads, IT, and various departments to implement practical automation and AI solutions that meet project deadlines and client satisfaction goals, targeting 100% adherence to project deadlines, over 90% customer satisfaction (based on feedback), and zero open support tickets after handover.

### Qualifications
To succeed in this role, you should possess a blend of methodological rigor, personal drive, and relevant technical foundations:

**Methodological Competencies:**
*   Strong analytical thinking and a structured approach to process mapping.
*   Demonstrated responsibility, solution-orientation, with a keen eye for efficiency and sustainability.
*   Ability to articulate complex processes in a simple and understandable manner.

**Personal Attributes:**
*   High level of initiative, eagerness to learn, and genuine enthusiasm for automation, AI, and digitalization.
*   Excellent communication skills and a team-oriented mindset.
*   Meticulous, strong documentation skills, and a commitment to quality.

**Technical & Educational Background:**
*   Currently enrolled in a degree program in Computer Science, Business Informatics, Data Science, Industrial Engineering, or a comparable field.
*   Foundational knowledge or a strong interest in automation tools such as n8n, Make, Zapier, or Power Automate.
*   Initial exposure to AI technologies, including experience with ChatGPT-APIs, OpenAI, Hugging Face, or Python libraries like Pandas, scikit-learn, or LangChain.
*   A good understanding of digital business processes and API interfaces.
*   Very good German language skills (C1/C2) in both spoken and written communication are essential.
