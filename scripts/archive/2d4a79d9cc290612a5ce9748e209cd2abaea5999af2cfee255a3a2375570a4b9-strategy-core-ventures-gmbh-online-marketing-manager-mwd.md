### Description

Are you more than just a campaign manager? Do you generate *real* leads, understanding audiences, hooks, creatives, and performance metrics? At SC Ventures, we're not just looking for someone to 'manage ads'; we want someone who takes full control of funnels and is accountable for one clear KPI: Cost per Lead (CPL). We build scalable lead machines for certified education providers (AZAV), not just social media reports.

This is a hands-on role where you'll dive into Facebook & TikTok Ads, tracking, funnels, landing pages, creatives, and copywriting. If you deliver results, there's a clear path for you to develop into the Head of Performance, taking on budget responsibility and building a team.

We're searching for someone who is driven by numbers but remains creative, and who is eager to grow – both professionally and in their career trajectory. If you're ready to make a significant impact and thrive in an environment where your performance directly shapes the company's success, then you fit right in.

### Responsibilities

*   Plan, launch, and scale performance campaigns for Facebook & TikTok, exclusively focused on lead generation.
*   Build landing pages and funnels, optimizing forms, conversion flows, and follow-up sequences.
*   Analyze data, identify patterns, and derive concrete optimizations – no flying blind.
*   Develop ad copy, hooks, and creatives independently (or with support if needed).
*   Set up tracking & events precisely (Meta Pixel, CAPI, UTM, Event-Tracking, etc.).
*   Work closely with sales & advisory teams, managing the inflow of qualified leads.

### Qualifications

*   **At least 2–3 years of experience in Facebook Ads (Lead Generation)** – not e-commerce, not awareness campaigns. Pure lead generation.
*   Ideally: **Experience with TikTok Ads**, alternatively, a strong willingness to quickly build this expertise.
*   **Funnel thinking** – you see the complete process, not just clicks & CTRs.
*   **Copywriting & creative understanding** – you know how to grab attention and generate clicks.
*   **Basic tech skills in landing page construction** (e.g., WordPress / GHL / Webflow / Funnelbuilder).
*   Bonus: **Google Ads or SEO experience**.
