**About the Job**
Join Allianz Technology Spain as a Strategic Operations Intern and gain hands-on experience in strategic projects, process improvement, and automation within the Spain Hub. This internship offers a unique opportunity to connect and implement global initiatives locally while developing leadership skills in a dynamic and innovative environment.
**What you do**
*   Assist in the planning and execution of strategic projects aimed at enhancing local operations as well as collaborating with cross-functional teams to support project goals and deliverables.
*   Gain insights into the day-to-day operations of the Spain Hub and support operational tasks and initiatives to ensure efficiency and effectiveness.
*   Work closely with the Spain Hub to connect and implement global initiatives whilst facilitating communication and coordination between local and global teams.
*   Identify opportunities for process improvement and propose actionable solutions.
*   Assist in the development and implementation of process optimization strategies.
*   Support automation efforts to streamline operations and enhance productivity as well as learning and applying tools and technologies to automate routine tasks.
*   Participate in leadership development programs, assessments and engage in activities that foster leadership skills and personal growth.
**What you bring**
*   Strong interest in operations management and process improvement.
*   Excellent communication and interpersonal skills with the ability to work collaboratively within a team and adapt to a fast-paced environment.
*   Fluency in English, both written and spoken.
*   Take initiative, demonstrate a willingness to learn and contribute and show eagerness to explore new ideas and approaches.
*   Exhibit reliability and accountability in tasks and projects whilst striving for high-quality work and continuous improvement.
*   Detail-oriented and organized with the ability to deal with many projects simultaneously.
*   Currently pursuing a degree in Business Administration, Information Technology, or related fiel
*   Basic knowledge of AI technologies, principles, and their practical use.
**What we offer**
*   A modern and open space office located in Barcelona (Poblenou)
*   Flexible working hours and an excellent hybrid working model
*   You will receive a laptop and any material you need for your work.
*   Activities nearly every week (afterworks, games night, skating, padel and many more!)
*   Job opportunities in a global company.
