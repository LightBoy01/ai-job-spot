### Description\nAre you passionate about technology? So are we! We are looking for a Software Developer (m/w/x) for the backend of an RF calibration tool to join our expert team at K-tronik, working with a client in the radio and communications industry. We look forward to getting to know you personally and working together with passion and team spirit on exciting projects and future-oriented technologies.\n\nK-tronik offers a friendly and collegial working atmosphere with flexible working hours and 30 days of flexible vacation planning. Benefits include regular team events, recognition as a Kununu TOP Company, corporate benefits (employee offers), and free beverages. The company provides individual further training opportunities and 360° support, encompassing feedback discussions, career planning, and relocation assistance.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop backend software in C++ for a PC program designed to remotely control measurements and calibrate RF metrology devices.\n- Work with build and deployment systems, specifically CMake and Gitlab-CI.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Strong proficiency in C++.\n- Good command of English.\n- Experience with CMake.\n- Foundational understanding of RF concepts (e.g., phase shift, frequency response, group delay).\n- Practical experience with CI/CD pipelines, particularly GitLab.\n- Proficiency in Python.\n- Experience with Unit Tests and Mocking.\n- Optional skills: Multithreading, Performance Analysis, experience with SCPI (Standard Commands for Programmable Instruments).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a skilled C++ Backend Developer eager to work at the intersection of software engineering and RF technology. K-tronik offers a highly supportive and employee-centric environment, emphasizing work-life balance, continuous learning, and a strong team spirit. If you thrive on technical challenges in a specialized domain and value a company that genuinely invests in its people, this role provides both impact and significant growth potential.
