### Description
_Position at Imagine Learning_
Great Purpose. Great People. Great Opportunities.
At Imagine Learning, we Empower Potential \- in students, educators, and each other. As the leading provider of digital-first K–12 curriculum solutions, we’re committed to igniting learning breakthroughs that spark curiosity, creativity, and confidence. Every day, our team designs innovative tools that help educators personalize instruction and engage students in meaningful ways. We’re shaping the future of learning through our Curriculum-Informed AI™ approach - technology that understands instructional context, respects educator judgment, and transforms how teachers personalize learning at scale to unlock each student’s potential. Together, we’re reimagining what’s possible and transforming education.
That same spirit of innovation and purpose drives our team culture. At Imagine Learning, you’ll find opportunities for meaningful work, continuous growth, and connection with passionate colleagues who care deeply about making a difference. We celebrate collaboration, embrace change, and believe in lifelong learning - for our students and ourselves.
Imagine Learning offers flexible work arrangements across the U.S. and internationally. U.S.-based employees located in or around Tempe, AZ (HQ), Austin, TX, Petaluma, CA, Rock Rapids, IA, or Bloomington, MN, typically work on a hybrid schedule at one of our office locations.
Imagine Your Impact.
The Senior Product Manager facilitates and participates in problem and solution discovery, product design, implementation, and go-to-market for software solutions. This position works within the Product, Engineering & Design teams to help discover and define customer and user needs, jobs to be done, desired outcomes, and the priority of them as the team gathers more data and learns from that data.

Envision Your Experience.
In this role you’ll have the opportunity to:
*   Inform the strategy for the reporting and AI-driven insights components across our portfolio of core curriculum, supplemental/intervention, and assessment products.
*   Use data to drive identification and prioritization of product initiatives including market research, user research, competitor analysis, product analytics, and client/field feedback.
*   Maintain an in-depth understanding of the K-12 education market landscape.
*   Develop outcomes-based roadmaps that deliver customer and business value over a defined period of time.
*   Collaborate to develop an engaging experience for our students with enhanced motivation features, personalization, language support, and robust student tools.
*   Work with key stakeholders to identify solutions that can be leveraged by products throughout our portfolio.
*   Translate solutions into detailed requirements and acceptance criteria for the engineering teams.
*   Prioritize product enhancements and maintenance activities within the product backlog.
*   Triage urgent issues in collaboration with support and development teams.
*   Provide training and documentation on product updates to internal teams.
*   Collaborate with development teams to ensure that functional intent is achieved.
*   Assure product quality before the product gets into the hands of the customer.
*   Other duties as required.
Share Your Expertise.
Experience, education, and qualifications essential for success in this role, include:
*   Bachelor’s degree in sales, marketing, business, or similar field and at least 5 years of experience as a Product Manager; or an equivalent combination of education and experience.
*   Knowledge of childhood education is required.
*   Proficiency in the EdTech market is a plus.
*   Experience building reporting and insights features.
*   Knowledge of building AI-driven features a plus.
*   A high degree of technical fluency and comfort.
*   Effective listening, diplomacy and tact, and collaboration skills.
*   Excellent organizational, time and project management skills; ability to effectively multi-task while meeting demanding deadlines.
*   Strategic ability: anticipating future opportunities and consequences, demonstrated innovative approach to work.
*   Ability to work independently but also operate efficiently and effectively within a collaborative team environment.
*   Willingness and ability to travel as needed, up to 10%.
