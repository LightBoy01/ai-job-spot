****About the Role****

We’re looking for a ****Creative Lead, Influencer Marketing****—a creative strategist who understands the inner workings of influencer campaigns and knows how to transform brand objectives into high-performing, social-first content.

This is not just a “creative ideas” role. We’re seeking someone with a deep understanding of influencer marketing campaigns (whether through direct management, creative strategy, or a related role) who can pair strategic expertise with sharp creative instincts. You’ll be the bridge between strategic goals and creative execution, ensuring our content is set up for success.

  

* * *

  

### ****Core Responsibilities****

****Creative Strategy & Concepting****

*   Lead brainstorms to develop social-first, trend-driven creative concepts for influencer campaigns.  
      
    
*   Build custom content briefs, shot lists, scripts, and art direction that balance storytelling with brand objectives.  
      
    
*   Evaluate influencer content for performance drivers—signature shticks, hooks, brand integration, pacing—and deliver actionable feedback that strengthens the story arc.

*   Review influencer concepts to ensure content is optimized for success, providing actionable feedback that elevates their creative.  
      
    
*   Apply the right creative lens depending on the client’s industry and strategy (e.g., fashion may call for editorial, cinematic storytelling, while CPG may require playful, trend-led, social-first content).  
      
    

****Client & Influencer Partnership****

*   Present creative concepts and strategies to clients with confidence and clarity.  
      
    
*   Serve as the go-to creative contact on assigned accounts, ensuring client alignment and strong creative execution.  
      
    
*   Join creative calls with influencers to brainstorm, refine, and enhance content ideas.  
      
    
*   Review influencer deliverables with a sharp eye for what makes content authentic, engaging, and entertaining.  
      
    

****Campaign Integration****

*   Collaborate with account management, paid, and insights teams to ensure creative vision is executed seamlessly.  
      
    
*   Maintain consistency of narrative, tone, and quality across all campaign deliverables.  
      
    
*   Incorporate best practices for each platform to maximize content performance.  
      
    
*   Support influencer sourcing when needed by reviewing or recommending creators, applying a keen eye for style, vibe, storytelling ability, and brand fit.  
      
    

****Operational Leadership****

*   Contribute to process improvements, leveraging AI tools to enhance creative workflows.  
      
    
*   Manage multiple campaigns simultaneously, maintaining a high standard of quality and timeliness.  
      
      
    

* * *

  

### ****Experience & Expectations****

*   ****3–5 years of experience**** in influencer marketing or creative strategy (agency experience strongly preferred).  
      
    
*   Demonstrated ability to lead client-facing creative conversations.  
      
    
*   Strong portfolio of social-first creative work (non-“adsy,” engaging, authentic).  
      
    
*   Strategic thinker able to translate data, audience insights, and platform best practices into actionable creative.  
      
    
*   Deep understanding of creative across social-first platforms and current trends.  
      
    
*   Exceptional communication skills with clients, creators, and internal teams.  
      
    
*   Ability to work both independently and collaboratively to deliver results.  
      
    

****Bonus Points For:****

*   Experience with humor- or skit-based content (many of our top-performing campaigns are comedy-driven).  
      
    
*   Familiarity with AI tools, Google Slides, and project management platforms.  
      
      
    

* * *

  

### ****What Success Looks Like****

*   Clients and team view you as a trusted creative partner who elevates the work (and our brands) through sharp creative execution.  
      
    
*   Influencers consistently deliver content that feels authentic, entertaining, and high-performing.  
      
    
*   Campaign creative runs smoothly across teams, meeting both brand and performance KPIs.  
      
    
*   You balance strategic rigor with creative flair—never “flighty creative,” but always inspiring.  
      
    
*   You apply the right creative lens across industries, knowing when to be aspirational, humorous, or hyper-social-first.  
      
    
*   You help identify creators who not only match the brand but also deliver best-in-class, scroll-stopping content.
