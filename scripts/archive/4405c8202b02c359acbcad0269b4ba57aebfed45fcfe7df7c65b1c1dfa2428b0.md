Harmonia Holdings Group, LLC is an award-winning, rapidly growing federal government contractor committed to providing innovative, high-performing solutions to our government clients and focused on fostering a workplace that encourages growth, initiative, creativity, and employee satisfaction.  

Harmonia is seeking a Senior Project Management Support Specialist / Managment Consultant (Data Analytics, AI/ML) to join our cloud data management and analytics program for a large federal customer. This role combines project management support with data consulting responsibilities. The selected candidate will help plan and track high-level work efforts, prepare executive presentations, and coordinate Agile delivery activities. In addition, they will act as a data consultant to initiative teams, supporting their onboarding to the enterprise cloud platform and helping them understand and effectively use available data, analytics, and AI/ML services.

The ideal candidate is detail-oriented, collaborative, and comfortable shifting between project coordination and client-facing data enablement.

**Responsibilities**

*   Support project managers with Agile planning, scheduling, and tracking of enterprise-level work efforts.
*   Develop, edit, and format PowerPoint presentations, reports, and dashboards for leadership and stakeholders.
*   Facilitate Agile ceremonies and maintain project documentation, including action items, risks, and dependencies.
*   Serve as a data consultant for initiative teams onboarding to the cloud data management and analytics platform.
*   Provide guidance on available platform services, including the enterprise data lake, analytics tools, and AI/ML/NLP capabilities.
*   Support governance by documenting use cases, ensuring compliance with platform standards, and assisting with data access requests.
*   Collaborate with cloud engineers, data architects, and analytics teams to resolve onboarding issues and support customer adoption.
*   Contribute to knowledge management by creating reference guides, training materials, and process documentation.

Required Qualifications
=======================

*   Bachelor’s degree in Business, Information Systems, Data Analytics, or a related field (or equivalent professional experience).
*   10+ years of experience in project management support, Agile delivery, or data/analytics
*   Strong proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite (PowerPoint, Excel, Word) and collaboration tools (e.g., Teams, SharePoint, Jira, Confluence).
*   Familiarity with cloud data platforms and services (Azure preferred).
*   Excellent communication and stakeholder-facing skills, with the ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical audiences.
*   S. Citizenship (ability to meet federal contract requirements).

Preferred Qualifications
========================

*   Experience working with enterprise cloud platforms (Azure data services, Databricks).
*   Familiarity with data governance, analytics, or AI/ML use cases in large
*   Agile or project management certifications (CSM, PMI-ACP, PMP).
*   Experience supporting federal government or large enterprise IT
