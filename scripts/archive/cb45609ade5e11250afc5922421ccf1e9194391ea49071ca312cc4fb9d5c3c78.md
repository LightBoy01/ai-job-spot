**\[What the role is\]**
The GRaduate Industry Traineeship Programme within the Public Service (GRIT@Gov) aims to provide fresh graduates with opportunities to gain industry-relevant skills and experience that can strengthen their employability to take up full time roles in the sector.
**\[What you will be working on\]**
Looking for a job that actually makes a difference?
**Join us at the Forefront of Shaping Healthier Lives**
**Overview**
The Health Promotion Board’s vision is to make Singapore a nation of healthier people.
Come be a part of this journey if you’re passionate about creating boundary-pushing work that drives behavioral change.
**Scope**
You will be a part of HPB’s Research & Development team, which strives to advance health promotion efforts through the translation of research to practice. You will play an important role in charting the next generation of health promotion by distilling learnings and insights from cutting-edge research, rich data and novel technologies for deployment in nation-scale population platforms.
As a key member of **_Research & Development_** team, you will work in a cross-functional matrix team and contribute to high-impact projects in priority growth areas.
You will leverage multi-dimensional health and lifestyle data from mobile applications and wearables to generate actionable insights using a variety of AI and data science techniques.
You will develop predictive models that provide citizens with personalised feedback on their lifestyle choice to drive healthier behaviours.
You will develop compelling data-driven narratives for impactful storytelling to both internal and external stakeholders.
You will support the translation of data-driven insights, algorithms and predictive models to practice in the real-world through HPB programmes.
**\[What we are looking for\]**
An ideal candidate will be a dynamic and self-motivated individual who possesses the following competencies and traits:
*   Degree in data science, computer science, computer engineering, or a related discipline
*   Proficiency in Python and SQL.
*   Expertise in statistical techniques of multivariate modelling and advanced analytics (e.g. machine learning, k-means cluster)
*   Knowledge in classical AI and generative AI and associated concepts is preferred.
*   Strong sense of intellectual curiosity to learn and seek continuous learning.
*   Ability to work cohesively in fast-paced teams and navigate stressful, ambiguous situations in a positive manner.
*   Strong communication skills, especially in communicating technical findings to a non-technical audience
*   Tenacity, resilience and adaptability in tackling challenges.
