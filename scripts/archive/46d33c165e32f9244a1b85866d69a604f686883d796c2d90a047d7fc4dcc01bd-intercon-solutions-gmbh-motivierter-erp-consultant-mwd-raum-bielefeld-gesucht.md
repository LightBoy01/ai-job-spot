### Description
Our client is a successful mechanical engineering company with approximately 500 employees. Renowned for its high technical competence and innovative strength, the company develops and produces sophisticated machinery and systems for an international customer base.

In line with ongoing digital advancement, the ERP landscape is continuously optimized and adapted to evolving business processes. For this pivotal journey, we are seeking a dedicated **ERP Consultant** who passionately connects deep IT expertise with a profound understanding of mechanical engineering operations. This role is instrumental in actively driving digitalization and process optimization across the organization, working within an innovative and economically solid company known for its open and appreciative culture, offering a secure and long-term workplace with attractive benefits.

### Responsibilities
- Analyze, optimize, and redesign business processes across various company departments, including production, design, materials management, finance, and sales.
- Independently develop robust solution concepts and ensure close coordination with the management board and the owner family.
- Take responsibility for leading end-to-end projects, including the coordination of internal implementation teams and the steering of external service providers.
- Provide expert consulting for the selection and successful introduction of suitable ERP modules.
- Offer comprehensive support and guidance to users both during and after the implementation of new processes and systems.
- Create detailed project documentation, evaluations, and reports for project leadership and overall management.

### Qualifications
- A completed degree in Business Informatics, Computer Science, or a comparable qualification, preferably complemented by a technical background.
- Proven practical experience in project and change management, demonstrating strong implementation capabilities.
- An in-depth understanding of business processes across various industries, ideally gained within a manufacturing or producing company.
- Extensive hands-on experience in the consulting and implementation of complex ERP systems.
- Excellent communication and presentation skills, capable of engaging with diverse stakeholders.
- A team-oriented, highly independent, and structured approach to work.
