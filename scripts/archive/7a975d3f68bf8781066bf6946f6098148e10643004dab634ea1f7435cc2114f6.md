Enterprise Technology at Ford is at the forefront of shaping the future of mobility. This is an unparalleled opportunity to leverage advanced technology, redefine the transportation landscape, enhance customer experiences, and profoundly improve people's lives. Join us to challenge your IT expertise and analytical skills, contributing to the creation of intelligent vehicles.
At Ford Motor Company, we champion the belief that freedom of movement fuels human progress. We seek diverse problem-solving skills to tackle unique challenges and offer you the freedom to define and achieve your aspirations. With our ambitious plans for the future of mobility, you'll find a wealth of opportunities to accelerate your career and help us define tomorrow's transportation.
As a Full Stack Software Engineer, you will collaborate with a broad array of talented software engineers to build amazing software that will transform the Ford Reservation Services into the standard for providing an excellent digital experience for Service appointments for vehicles. Innovate, problem-solve, and build new features and exciting capabilities to support launches of customer-centric features.
### Responsibilities
**What you’ll be able to do:**
*   Develop Ford Reservation Services iteratively in fast pacing always relevant environment
*   Create features based on regular user interviews and fast feedback
*   Work in an environment where you can learn and share knowledge openly
*   Define, foster, and advance your team’s culture, norms, and practices
*   Maintain a healthy work-life balance while revolutionizing automotive
### Qualifications
**You'll Have…**
*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering or related field OR a combination of education and equivalent experience
*   7+ years of experience and proficiency with:
*   front-end languages such as JavaScript/Typescript, and a familiarity with other languages such as Java
*   front-end frameworks such as React, Angular, VueJs
*   backend framework like Nest.js, GoLang Api development and Spring boot
*   5+ years of understanding, practicing, and advocating for clean code principles such as test-driven development (TDD), self-documenting code, continuous refactoring, and Extreme Programming (XP)
*   Enthusiastic about AI Driven coding with the help of tools like Github Co-Pilot, Cursor AI or Windsurf
*   Strong understanding of Fullstack architecture principals
*   Working knowledge of CI/CD with popular tools like Jenkins, Tekton, Github Actions etc
*   Strong understanding of any public cloud provider like GCP, AWS or Azure
**Even Better, You May Have…**
*   Master’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering or related field OR a combination of education and equivalent experience
*   Experience with web and mobile accessibility standards (WCAG compliance), tools, and testing (e.g. screen-readers, axe, user interviews)
*   Experience with Behavior Driven Development
*   Mechanical and engineering empathy
*   A desire to collaborate closely with UI/UX designers
*   Self Starter and have keen interest of problem solving
*   Have skillset of leading a team and bringing along the junior engineers
