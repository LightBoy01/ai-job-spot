**About The Role:**

We're scaling fast and need a hands-on Senior Software Engineer to join our Public Investor Engineering team who can develop full stack applications end-to-end from architecture to delivery. The ideal candidates possesses 3+ years of architecting and developing full-stack applications and 4+ years working on web applications built with React/JavaScript/TypeScript, Python/Dash, and AWS.

**This is a remote-friendly opportunity that can sit in NYC (where our headquarter is located), one of our office hubs (Austin, Miami, Denver, Mountain View, or Seattle), or anywhere else in the US.** However, depending upon where the remote work is performed, income could be subject to New York State tax withholding. 

**As a Senior Software Engineer, You Will:**

*   Architect and deliver key features of our Public Investor tooling ecosystem - including both internal tools and external-facing applications, such as our Public Investor Portal.
*   Serve as a subject matter expert across the whole stack, and report directly to the Senior Engineering Manager, who will provide hands‑on mentorship in cutting‑edge web tools and techniques.
*   Leverage AI tools like Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT to improve personal and team productivity, code quality, and documentation.
*   Collaborate with other application engineers and PMs on evolving our application platform for scale, testability, and reliability.
*   Produce clear documentation: create playbooks, docs, and training materials to institutionalize best practices.

**You Are Likely To Succeed If:**

*   You have **4+ years** of hands‑on software development experience with a proven track record designing, building, and maintaining customer‑facing web applications supporting data analytics use cases.
*   You have hands‑on, production‑level expertise with AWS, Python, JavaScript, and React; familiarity with Django and Plotly Dash is a strong plus.
*   You have an AI‑accelerated mindset — hands‑on experience piloting or institutionalizing developer‑productivity tools such as Claude Code and Cursor.
*   You are able to navigate ambiguity — you are able to understand key business problems and translate them into concrete engineering outcomes
*   You are a highly motivated self-starter on the initiatives you work on and are eager to constantly learn new technologies.
*   You have exceptional verbal and written communication skills.
*   You hold a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, STEM, or a related technical discipline.
