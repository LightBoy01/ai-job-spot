We are looking for dedicated IT System Administrators (m/f/d) for various attractive IT companies across Germany. This role involves taking responsibility for modern IT infrastructures, either for internal company IT or for client systems. We facilitate permanent employment (not temp agency work). If you're currently in IT support and eager to advance into system administration, you are highly encouraged to apply! We are here to support your next career step.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Administer, maintain, and further develop servers, networks, and IT systems.\n- Install and configure hardware and software.\n- Manage user accounts and permissions.\n- Oversee cloud environments.\n- Ensure IT security, backups, and system availability.\n- Support IT projects, migrations, and rollouts.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree or vocational training in IT (e.g., IT Specialist for System Integration) or comparable professional experience.\n- Initial or several years of experience in system administration (IT supporters with initial admin experience are also welcome!).\n- Knowledge of Windows or Linux server environments.\n- Independent, structured way of working and a desire for continuous development.\n- Good German language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for IT professionals looking to advance their careers in System Administration across Germany.
