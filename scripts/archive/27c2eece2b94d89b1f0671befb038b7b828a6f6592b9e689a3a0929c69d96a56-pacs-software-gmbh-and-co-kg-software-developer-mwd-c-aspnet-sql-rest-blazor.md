### Description
Are you passionate about technically advancing an established software product and contributing to exciting, future-oriented projects? Then join our team and help shape the future of PACS Project Controlling and Project-ERP Software!

PACS is a leading project software used by numerous companies, primarily for digitizing project processes, tracking time and travel expenses, and ensuring seamless billing – all with modern interfaces to other systems.

We develop our software with passion, innovation, and genuine team spirit.

Together, we are now embarking on a new development phase. We are fundamentally modernizing our existing ASP.NET application and bringing it into the future as a powerful **Blazor WebApp** – with a new architecture, optimized performance, and expanded possibilities for integrating **AI technologies**.

For this, we are looking for you to strengthen our Development Team! Look forward to modern tools and an exciting daily work routine – and the chance to truly contribute and shape the product.

### Responsibilities
As a **Software Developer (m/w/d)** at PACS, you will work on the further development of our PACS software for project controlling & project ERP – an established solution used daily by thousands of users worldwide.

You will contribute new ideas, develop innovative features, and help us shape the technical transition from **ASP.NET** to **Blazor** – including exciting topics around **REST APIs** and **AI**.

Your detailed tasks include:

*   **Development of new functions and features** for our standard software
*   **Maintenance and further development** of the existing codebase _(ASP.NET, C#, SQL)_
*   **Implementation, support, and maintenance of interfaces** – especially **REST-based APIs**
*   **Active participation in the modernization of our existing software architecture** – particularly in the migration from ASP.NET to Blazor
*   **Implementation of customer-specific extensions and integrations** in close cooperation with the Product Team
*   **Collaboration on AI projects** – e.g., automations or intelligent analyses for process optimization
*   **Close collaboration with experienced colleagues** from development and support to create stable and performant solutions

### Qualifications

*   You have **good knowledge of C# and ASP.NET**, ideally also experience with **Blazor**
*   You have **experience with Microsoft SQL Server** and **relational databases**
*   You are familiar with **REST APIs** and **interface development**
*   You value **clean, well-structured code** and high code quality
*   You work **analytically, structured, and self-motivated**
*   You have a **completed degree** in (Business) Informatics, Mathematics, or a comparable education
*   You have **good German language skills**, and **English language skills** are a plus
*   You are **open to new technologies** and interested in actively contributing to and developing topics like **AI**
*   You enjoy working in a **team**, sharing your knowledge, and contributing your own ideas

### Author's Take
This role offers a unique opportunity for a motivated Software Developer to play a pivotal part in a major technological transformation.
