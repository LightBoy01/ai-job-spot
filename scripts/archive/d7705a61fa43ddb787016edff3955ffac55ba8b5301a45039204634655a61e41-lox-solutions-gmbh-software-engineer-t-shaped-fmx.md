Tasks
-----

**Who we’re looking for 👀**

A T-shaped mind with a maker’s heart.  
Someone who can dive deep into software architecture _and_ connect the dots across product, design, and business.

You bridge the “tech–biz gap” without even noticing.  
You understand why something works technically – and why it should exist at all.  
You don’t hide behind job titles or silos. You build, discuss, align, and deliver.

Not a code-only purist. Not a strategy-only thinker.  
You’re both – and that’s your superpower.

------------

**What you’ll do here 💪🏻**

Create software that scales, products that matter, and systems that actually make sense.  
Turn fuzzy ideas into working solutions.  
Shape the product vision _and_ its technical reality.

You’ll:

*   design architectures that enable rapid iteration and long-term resilience
*   collaborate with cross-functional teams from design to business to AI
*   balance trade-offs between user value, speed, and scalability
*   make technology choices that move ventures forward – not sideways

Whatever moves the needle – even if it’s not on the roadmap yet.  
We don’t talk about agility.  
We deliver it – turning ideas into working products.
