### Description\nSilberkraft is a German e-commerce company specializing in high-quality solutions for pest control, mold prevention, and household products. Our focus is on effective, responsibly formulated products sold through our own online shop and various marketplaces like Amazon, Shop-Apotheke, Otto, and Hornbach. We operate in a data-driven and customer-oriented manner, continuously developing our product lines and marketplace presence. This role offers an excellent opportunity to gain deep insights into e-commerce processes and contribute to our growth from a 100% remote setup, working closely with the founding and marketing teams within a flexible, flat organizational structure.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Support operational marketplace management across platforms such as Amazon, Shop-Apotheke, Otto, and Hornbach.\n*   Maintain and optimize existing product listings, including titles, bullet points, descriptions, images, and search terms.\n*   Implement SEO strategies tailored for various marketplaces.\n*   Assist with PPC optimization, covering campaign setup, keyword research, monitoring, and reporting.\n*   Conduct price and competitor analyses.\n*   Manage inventory levels and monitor for out-of-stock risks.\n*   Coordinate effectively with logistics, production, and content teams.\n*   Regularly evaluate key performance indicator (KPI) dashboards, including Conversion Rate, CTR, TACOS, and Buybox share.\n*   Contribute to launch strategies for new products.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   A degree in Business Administration, Economics, Marketing, E-commerce, or a comparable field.\n*   Strong analytical thinking skills and comfort working with data.\n*   A fundamental understanding of online marketplaces and their operational mechanics.\n*   Prior experience with Amazon Seller Central, PPC tools, or feed management systems is a plus.\n*   Demonstrated structured working approach and high level of self-motivation.\n*   Excellent German language proficiency (very good Deutschkenntnisse).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic Junior role for someone looking to dive deep into the world of e-commerce, specifically marketplace management. The 100% remote setup, flexible working environment, and flat hierarchies speak to a modern, progressive culture. Candidates will benefit from close collaboration with the founding team and marketing, gaining hands-on experience and taking responsibility for their own small projects. The emphasis on data-driven work and continuous optimization means a steep learning curve and tangible impact on the business. If you're analytically minded, self-motivated, and fluent in German, this is an exceptional opportunity to kickstart your career in a dynamic e-commerce company.
