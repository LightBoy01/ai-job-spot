**Duties and Responsibilities**  
*   Ensure a consistent and high-end retouching is delivered across all campaigns.
*   Assist in the development of the creative styles and assist the team in designing sets for all the in-house shoots.
*   Serve as the main Re-toucher and Lead all the in house photography efforts for the day to day operations of the department.
*   Create engaging content across all platforms and mediums.
*   Partner with the video editing team on campaigns with video and still components to ensure a cohesive campaign is created.
*   Prepare and Shoot all video content for branded and marketing teams: This includes product videos, lifestyle videos, transformation videos, interviews, scripted content, and tv advertisements. (Hi-fi and Lo-fi content; pro cameras and cell phone content)
*   Must ensure audio recording is also of high quality whenever shooting with dialogue
*   Work closely with Editing staff to ensure shooting for efficient editing
*   Work with Marketing Directors and Creative Directors to execute and bring to life the content that has been envisioned by them. 
*   Work well with production team to create seamless production processes
*   Direct and guide on screen talent, as well as stage and set products for product shots
*   Research new trends, techniques, platforms, and tools to innovate and enhance the quality of our video content
*   Provide high-quality assets in multiple formats depending on expected consumption.
*   Stay up to date with popular shooting trends
*   Maintain organized studio and up to date inventory of equipment
*   Other duties as assigned by leadership
  
  
**Qualifications**  
*   5+ years of experience in Creative Production, Project Management, or Creative Operations, preferably within a DTC (Direct-to-Consumer) E-commerce or beauty/CPG brand environment.
*   Proven expertise in managing the full production lifecycle for both photo and video assets, from concept and scheduling through post-production delivery.
*   Expert proficiency with workflow and project management tools (ShortCut, Airtable, Asana, Jira, or similar platforms) for task delegation, tracking, and capacity planning.
*   Strong technical understanding of digital media specifications and optimization required for distribution across e-commerce platforms, Paid Social (Meta, TikTok), and other digital channels.
*   Demonstrated ability to manage and track production budgets, negotiate effectively with external vendors, freelancers, and creative agencies, and maintain usage rights documentation.
*   Exceptional organizational skills and a highly proactive, problem-solving mindset to anticipate and resolve bottlenecks in a fast-paced creative environment.
*   Excellent cross-functional communication skills to effectively bridge creative teams (Art Directors, Editors) and business stakeholders (Marketing, Product, Finance).
*   Expertise in leveraging Generative AI tools (like Midjourney or DALL-E) to rapidly prototype concepts and enhance creative output.
*   Proficiency in integrating and managing AI-powered workflow tools to automate asset tagging, versioning, and performance-based content iteration.
*   Proven experience in designing and implementing AI-driven creative systems (e.g., prompt engineering, style guide automation) to maintain brand consistency at scale.
*   Experience partnering with AI/data teams to utilize creative performance data for informed decision-making and content optimization.
