**ABOUT MAPLE**
Founded in 2015, Maple is a fast-growing health tech company with a vision to power the future of healthcare by building a connected and superior experience for patients, doctors, nurse practitioners and other types of health providers.
We offer virtual care services across multiple distribution channels, including Direct-to-Consumer ("B2C"), Employers and Private Insurers ("B2B") and Public Sector Institutions ("B2I"). With a growing network of 2,000+ healthcare providers and nearly 7 million Canadians with access to our services, Maple is one of Canada’s fastest-growing virtual care companies.
We have established an entrepreneurial culture centered around our purpose to support people’s health and well-being and to strengthen the healthcare system. We attribute our success to our team, who has helped us achieve numerous noteworthy awards including: LinkedIn's Top 10 Startups, Deloitte Canada’s Technology Fast 50, Globe & Mail's Top Growing Companies in Canada, North America’s Inspiring Workplaces, and Glory Professional’s The Power 50: Canada’s Most Impactful Companies.
**THE POSITION**
We’re seeking a Vice President (VP) of Engineering to lead, empower, and inspire our software engineering team — driving innovation and excellence as we continue building one of Canada’s most impactful virtual healthcare platforms. Reporting directly to the CTO, you’ll be accountable for the performance and growth of our engineers, ensuring we deliver high-quality, reliable, and innovative products at scale.
As VP of Engineering, you’ll set the engineering vision, evolve our engineering practices, and ensure architectural decisions support scale and reliability — with your primary focus on organizational health, delivery excellence, and operational outcomes. You’ll partner closely with Product, Design, Data, and Operations to deliver solutions that meet real-world healthcare needs while fostering a culture of high standards, accountability, and continuous improvement.
At Maple, engineering leadership is hands-on and product-aware. We expect our VP of Engineering to manage and scale teams, shape org design and guild structure, guide hiring strategies, and champion technical excellence across the company. You will also play a key role in advancing Maple’s position as an early AI adopter in healthcare technology — embedding AI-driven innovation into our products, processes, and culture to enhance patient care, provider experience, and operational efficiency.

**WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE**
In your first 90 days, you’ll immerse yourself in Maple’s technical landscape and business priorities, building strong relationships cross-functionally, including with engineering, product, and executive peers. You’ll assess the current team structure, delivery practices, and platform health, identifying key strengths and areas for improvement. Early wins will include removing blockers for active projects, implementing clear decision-making frameworks, and setting a shared vision for engineering excellence.
Over the next 12–18 months, you will have positioned our engineering organization for scale — embedding AI as a core driver of product innovation and engineering efficiency. This includes accelerating AI adoption across our stack, optimizing processes, developing future leaders, and implementing sustainable practices that enable long-term success. You’ll have driven measurable improvements in delivery speed, predictability, and quality while reducing technical debt. Engineering health metrics will be in place and will regularly inform strategy. You will also have established and operationalized Maple’s engineering operating system — delivering measurable improvements in stability, throughput, and developer experience. You’ll have built a culture where engineers take pride in their craft, product teams move quickly with confidence, and Maple is recognized as both a destination employer for top engineering talent.
