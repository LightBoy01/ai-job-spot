**Job Description:**
We are seeking a **Project Manager** to join our Innovation team, who will lead high-impact AI+Software Development projects from concept to execution with precision, urgency, and ownership.
**Project Ownership & Delivery**
• Translate high-level business problems into well-scoped project goals and timelines
• Drive seamless execution across teams, ensuring timely delivery and accountability
• Take full ownership of outcomes, escalating roadblocks while staying laser-focused on results
**Stakeholder Engagement & Team Coordination**
• Work cross-functionally with internal stakeholders and external vendors
• Facilitate communication flow, manage expectations, and lead by influence
• Own project documentation, from tracking updates to executive-level reporting
**Agility & Continuous Improvement**
• Monitor and adapt to changes in scope, schedule, and resources
• Track performance metrics to ensure success and impact
• Identify process gaps and propose actionable improvements
**Qualifications:**
**What we look for**
• **Experience:** Minimum 3 years managing cross-functional projects end-to-end
• **Expertise:** Skilled in Agile, Scrum, or other PM frameworks and tools
• **Mindset:** Resourceful, proactive, and thrives in high-pressure, fast-paced environments
• **Focus:** Deep customer empathy and relentless commitment to solving real problems
• **Communication:** Able to navigate complexity, build trust, and drive clarity
• **Values:** Critical thinker, honest communicator, growth-oriented, dependable
