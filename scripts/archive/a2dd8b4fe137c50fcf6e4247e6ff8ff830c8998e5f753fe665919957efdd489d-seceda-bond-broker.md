-----

*   Continuously monitor client portfolios and recommend adjustments based on changing market conditions and client objectives.
*   Collaborate with other financial professionals to develop comprehensive investment strategies tailored to individual client requirements.

*   Minimum of 3 years experience in bond trading or brokerage
*   Strong knowledge of financial markets and instruments, particularly bonds
*   Excellent analytical and quantitative skills
*   Effective communication and negotiation abilities
