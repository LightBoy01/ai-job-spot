### Description\nStart to Finish is the #1 recruiting boutique for e-commerce startups in the DACH region, helping fast-growing brands like Snocks and SellerX hire key talent. Our client, a fast-growing DTC streetwear brand, is known for its high product velocity, complex textile processes, and international production setup. This Product Lead role is pivotal, responsible for managing the full product lifecycle from sample development to mass production across all apparel categories. The successful candidate will professionalize the production setup, accelerate timelines, and strategically expand the company’s international manufacturing network, requiring deep experience in textile production, strong operational execution, and the ability to scale complex supply chains.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Manage the full process from sample development to large-scale production\n*   Oversee sampling for core products (T-shirts, hoodies, sweaters, caps) and growing categories (denim, outerwear, accessories)\n*   Coordinate complex printing, embroidery, washing, dyeing, and finishing processes\n*   Ensure consistent Sample → Production timelines of 1–2 months\n*   Build new production lines and expand the international supplier network (Asia, Uzbekistan, Southeast Asia)\n*   Manage offshore factories, production partners, and external agencies\n*   Oversee purchasing, supply chain planning, cost control, and quality assurance\n*   Work closely with Design, Marketing, and Operations on feasibility and timelines\n*   Support the development of new materials and technical product categories\n*   Establish documentation, compliance, and production standards\n*   Travel internationally to production partners as needed\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Several years of experience in apparel production, garment technology, or textile manufacturing\n*   Strong end-to-end understanding of fabrics, construction, printing, and finishing\n*   Proven ability to manage **high-volume productions** (5,000–30,000+ units)\n*   Experience working with international suppliers and offshore manufacturing\n*   Track record of establishing or scaling new production setups is a strong plus\n*   Excellent supplier management and project coordination skills\n*   Ability to manage multiple timelines and product categories simultaneously\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned apparel production expert to take on a highly autonomous and impactful \"Product Lead\" role within a fast-paced DTC streetwear brand. If you thrive on building, professionalizing, and scaling global supply chains, and you're passionate about influencing product quality, speed, and innovation, this remote-first position offers direct strategic impact and significant growth potential. The company fosters a collaborative environment where quick decision-making and your expertise will directly drive the brand's international expansion.
