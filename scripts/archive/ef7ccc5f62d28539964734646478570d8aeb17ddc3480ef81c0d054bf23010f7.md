Career Opportunities: Sr. Principal Data Architect (49451)
==========================================================

Requisition ID **49451** - Posted  - **Architecture - ROW**

**OPENTEXT - THE INFORMATION COMPANY**

OpenText is a global leader in information management, where innovation, creativity, and collaboration are the key components of our corporate culture. As a member of our team, you will have the opportunity to partner with the most highly regarded companies in the world, tackle complex issues, and contribute to projects that shape the future of digital transformation.

**AI-First. Future-Driven. Human-Centered.**

At OpenText, AI is at the heart of everything we do—powering innovation, transforming work, and empowering digital knowledge workers. We're hiring talent AI can't replace to help us shape the future of information management. Join us.

OpenText is hiring a Senior Principal Data Architect to lead architectural strategy and design for our transformational Data Cloud initiative – to turn enterprise content and application data into trusted, AI-ready data products.

**Your Impact:**

You’ll report to the SVP, Chief Architect and be responsible for architecting the Data Cloud solution that bridges enterprise information management with agentic AI. This is a great opportunity to shape the future of enterprise data strategy and build the semantic, scalable data platform that drives decision-making and agentic AI across the enterprise.

**What the role offers:**

As a Senior Principal Data Architect, you will:

*   Develop a unified data architecture integrating structured and unstructured data, eliminating silos and boundaries.
*   Define data product specifications with standardized data contracts and governance controls.
*   Design AI-driven knowledge graphs from large repositories of unstructured content.
*   Architect AI-native data pipelines for contextual search and agentic workflows via AI platforms.
*   Create accuracy enhancement frameworks to reduce hallucinations and ensure verifiable results.
*   Lead strategic customer pilots in legal, healthcare, and financial services with clear ROI demonstration.

**What You Need to Succeed:**

*   10+ years of enterprise data architecture experience designing large-scale platforms for Fortune 500 companies.
*   Expertise in modern data architecture patterns, data processing frameworks (Spark, Kafka), and data formats (Delta/Iceberg).
*   Strong background in knowledge graphs, semantic technologies (RDF, SPARQL, Neo4j), and vector search.
*   Proven experience in data governance and lifecycle management, including catalogs, lineage, data quality, and privacy controls.
*   Solid AI/ML foundation with hands-on experience delivering production-grade AI solutions (LLMs, RAG, Agentic AI, MCP).
*   Deep understanding of multi-cloud architecture (AWS, Azure, GCP) and hybrid cloud strategies.
*   Demonstrated leadership in shaping technical vision and driving enterprise-wide architecture initiatives.
*   Exceptional communication skills with the ability to influence stakeholders and lead cross-functional teams in complex B2B environments.

**One Last Thing**

OpenText is more than a company—it’s a global community driven by trust, innovation, and purpose. We're shaping the Data Cloud to redefine the future of work, empowering you to influence strategy, technology, and outcomes at scale.

Join a culture built on ethics, collaboration, and growth. With competitive compensation and benefits, we offer more than a job—we offer the chance to make a real impact.
