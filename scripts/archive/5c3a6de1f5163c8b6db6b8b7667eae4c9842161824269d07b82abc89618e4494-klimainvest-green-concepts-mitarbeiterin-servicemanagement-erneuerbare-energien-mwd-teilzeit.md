### Description
Join KlimaInvest Green Concepts GmbH, a successful team dedicated to advancing the energy transition and climate protection, particularly in Germany, since 2009. We provide green electricity and gas to energy providers and public utilities, and support the compensation of unavoidable CO2 emissions. With our expertise, we pave the way for our customers towards greater climate protection and sustainability.

We are looking for a **Service Management Specialist for Renewable Energies (m/f/d) - Part-time** to strengthen our committed team in Hamburg's Hafencity. As part of our service and project team, you will serve as the central interface between our customers, partners, and internal departments. Your role is to ensure that processes related to evidence management, contract management, and customer communication run smoothly and continuously improve, always with the goal of ensuring transparency and quality in all operations. This position is offered on a part-time basis (30 hours per week).

### Responsibilities
*   **Manage Certification Processes:** Take responsibility for the accurate recording, documentation, and execution of cancellations and decommissioning in external registers. This includes managing compensation for natural gas emissions and proofs of origin for green electricity, thereby ensuring traceable and verified climate protection services.
*   **Coordinate Inquiries:** Effectively coordinate incoming internal and external inquiries, prioritize them, and maintain communication threads between various departments.
*   **Oversee Contract Management:** Create, manage, and dispatch contracts for our climate partners. Maintain an overview of contract templates and provide support during the signing process and with technical inquiries.
*   **Conduct Customer Feedback Analysis:** Plan and execute customer surveys, analyze the feedback received, and derive actionable measures to optimize our services.
*   **Contribute to Operational Excellence:** Actively engage in cross-departmental operational topics and support the team with strong organizational skills and a high commitment to quality.
*   **Drive Continuous Improvement:** Apply your keen eye for quality and efficiency to ensure compliance with our standards and actively promote the continuous improvement of our operational processes.

### Qualifications
*   **Education:** Successfully completed a degree (e.g., Business Administration, Environmental/Sustainability Management, Economics) or possess comparable commercial training.
*   **Experience:** Bring initial practical experience, ideally within the commercial sector.
*   **Work Style:** Demonstrate a structured, independent, and diligent approach to work. Be able to maintain an overview across multiple parallel tasks and confidently set priorities.
*   **Communication & Service:** Possess strong communication skills and a deep understanding of service, enabling you to confidently manage internal and external inquiries and provide solution-oriented answers.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** Feel confident in using digital tools; ideally, you have prior experience with platforms like Qualtrics, CRM systems, or contract management solutions.
*   **Mindset:** Exhibit a high degree of responsibility and a strong commitment to quality standards, along with a genuine enjoyment in actively shaping and improving processes.
*   **Language Skills:** Hold very good German language skills, both written and spoken, and demonstrate excellent expressive abilities.
*   **Team Fit:** Be a team player, open-minded, and possess a good sense of humor to round out your profile.
