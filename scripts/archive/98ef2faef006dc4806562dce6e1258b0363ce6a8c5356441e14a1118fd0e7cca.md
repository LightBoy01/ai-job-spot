**About The Role:**

Own the design, build, and production release of AI/ML and LLM systems on Databricks. Set standards, lead cross-team delivery, and land high-impact features end to end.

**What you’ll do**

*   Architect and ship pipelines on Databricks (data → features → train/eval → serve → monitor) using Python and Spark.
*   Productionize LLM use cases: RAG, evaluation/guardrails, latency & cost optimization, caching, and rollout.
*   Lead design reviews, mentor engineers, and drive multi-quarter initiatives with Data, Platform, and Product.
*   Drive ambiguity and ownership: Own 0→1 charters and hold deprecation/migration authority across services.
*   Handle data governance and access controls; keep systems reliable and compliant.

**This is a fully remote opportunity based in India. Standard work hours are from 11am to 8pm IST.**

**You Are Likely To Succeed If:**

*    8+ years building and shipping AI/ML systems; proven Staff-level scope and cross-team impact.
*   Expert Python + Spark; strong Databricks/AWS/Azure experience (Delta, MLflow, Jobs/Workflows; Model Serving a plus).
*   Hands-on LLM production work (RAG, evals/safety, serving, tracing, cost/perf tradeoffs).
*   Solid system design, CI/CD, containers, and monitoring.
