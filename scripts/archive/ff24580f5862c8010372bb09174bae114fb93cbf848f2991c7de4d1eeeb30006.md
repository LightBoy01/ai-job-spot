It’s a strange thing to say, because us humans are capable of incredible things. And at Medibank, we know our greatest potential lies in the people who work with us.

**Join Our Strategy and Analytics Team**

Are you passionate about turning ambiguity into clarity, driving change, and delivering impact through strategy, AI, and collaboration? We’re looking for a thoughtful problem solver to join our Commercial Performance Squad.

**Why This Role Matters**

This isn’t just about data—it’s about strategic transformation. You’ll help shape how we work by applying structured problem solving, AI-enabled solutions, and collaborative delivery to unlock smarter decisions and better outcomes.

**What You’ll Be Doing**

*   **Strategic Impact**: Work on high-priority challenges that shape the future of our business and how we operate.
*   **AI-Driven Transformation**: Support the delivery and adoption of AI solutions that uplift performance and drive innovation.
*   **Collaborative Delivery**: Align stakeholders, unblock decisions, and orchestrate change across teams.
*   **Creative Problem Solving**: Use structured thinking to bring clarity to complex problems and turn insights into action.
*   **Cross-Business Exposure**: Partner with diverse teams and share your work with senior leaders.

**Who You Are**

You bring a strategic mindset and a collaborative spirit. With experience in Management Consulting, in-house Strategy, or Digital Transformation, you’re comfortable navigating ambiguity and driving outcomes.

You’re skilled in building business cases, scenario modelling, and applying emerging technologies like AI and automation to real-world problems. You thrive in team environments, communicate with clarity, and know how to get things done.

**Bonus Points If You Have:**

*   Experience implementing AI or automation solutions.
*   A track record of driving tech adoption or uplifting legacy systems.
*   Familiarity with data platforms and emerging technologies.
