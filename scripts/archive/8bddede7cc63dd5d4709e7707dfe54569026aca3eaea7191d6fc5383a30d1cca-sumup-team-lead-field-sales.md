### Description\nSumUp is a global fintech leader empowering over 4 million small businesses in 30+ countries with simple, affordable, and innovative payment solutions. As a Team Lead for Field Sales in Germany, you will play a pivotal role in strengthening SumUp's national footprint. Your mission is to lead, grow, and inspire a crucial network of 20-30 self-employed Field Sales Consultants (FSCs), serving as the strategic link between them and SumUp's sales leadership. This role ensures high performance, gathers direct market insights, and significantly contributes to helping small merchants adopt SumUp's products and succeed in their business journeys.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Lead and Grow National Sales Network:** Recruit, manage, and provide comprehensive support to a network of 20-30 self-employed Field Sales Consultants across Germany.\n- **Drive Hands-on Sales Success:** Actively engage in selling SumUp's POS and payment products to local businesses, gaining direct, invaluable market insights.\n- **Coach and Enable Team:** Deliver continuous training, conduct performance reviews, and provide on-the-ground support to empower FSCs to consistently achieve their sales targets.\n- **Analyze and Optimize Performance:** Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), meticulously analyze sales data, and design targeted initiatives to boost customer acquisition and retention.\n- **Shape German Strategy:** Collaborate closely with SumUp's HQ, sharing local market intelligence, designing specific market initiatives, and actively scaling the company's presence nationwide.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Proven experience in field sales, with a strong preference for backgrounds in fintech, POS systems, merchant services, or telecom.\n- Demonstrated ability to manage teams of 10+ freelancers, consultants, or commission-based sales professionals across multiple regions.\n- An entrepreneurial, hands-on leadership style, with a clear passion for ownership and delivering tangible impact.\n- A profound understanding of the unique needs of small business owners and a track record of building lasting customer relationships.\n- Strong analytical skills, coupled with proficiency in CRM tools such as Salesforce or Tableau.\n- Fluent communication skills in both German and English.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an experienced and entrepreneurial sales leader to significantly shape SumUp's growth in Germany. The role offers a powerful blend of strategic leadership—building and mentoring a large network of self-employed consultants—and hands-on sales, ensuring direct market understanding and credibility. SumUp clearly emphasizes autonomy, impact, and continuous professional growth, backing it with a truly competitive compensation package that includes a base salary, uncapped commissions, Virtual Stock Options (VSOP), and a car allowance. Beyond financial incentives, the company demonstrates a strong commitment to work-life balance and employee well-being through a generous Learning & Development budget (€2,000/year), a 30-day sabbatical after three years, fully remote work flexibility, and a comprehensive suite of benefits including corporate pension matching, various mobility options, subsidized sports club memberships, and childcare support. If you are passionate about empowering small businesses and thrive in a dynamic, high-impact leadership position, this role provides a unique platform to drive substantial change and accelerate your career within a global fintech leader.
