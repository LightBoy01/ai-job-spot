**Our Team**
------------
Cimpress Open excels at serving customers via third-party channels. We partner with companies that have strong customer relationships with end-customers who need mass-customized print products. Cimpress Open pursues opportunities with digital design software partners and established ecommerce marketplace platforms.
We are seeking a Senior AI Designer to own the end-to-end creation of Amazon listing visuals using AI-driven tools. This role is pivotal in scaling our creative output and producing high-quality, brand-compliant images for both custom and non-custom SKUs at speed. You will combine AI expertise with advanced design skills to deliver compelling, conversion-focused assets that align with our brand standards and marketplace requirements for both the EU and US regions.
**What You Will Do**
--------------------
*   Create Amazon-ready images (main, lifestyle, editor-style) for custom and non-custom products using AI tools, with potential expansion to other marketplaces in the future.
*   Develop reusable mockups and templates for listing layouts and advertising campaigns to support rapid scaling.
*   Produce seasonal and promotional variants aligned with campaign and merchandising calendars.
*   Apply brand overlays, design polish, and custom elements to maintain Cimpress brand standards.
*   Train and refine AI prompts for product-specific outputs, reducing manual rework and improving accuracy.
*   Create high-impact visuals for A+ Content, A/B testing, and advertising banners to improve CVR and CTR, including cross-sell assets and listing optimizations.
*   Generate dynamic visuals for seasonal events and promotional calendar initiatives with quick turnaround and consistent quality.
*   Collaborate with the Product team to identify, evaluate, and integrate new AI tools that can enhance creative workflows and scale production capabilities.
**Your Qualifications**
-----------------------
*   3–5 years of design experience specifically within e-commerce, retail, or product marketing environments, with at least 2 years hands-on experience in AI-driven image creation.
*   Proven track record in creating high-quality visuals for e-commerce and marketplaces.
*   Familiarity with Amazon marketplace requirements, merchandising strategies, image optimisation, and advertising best practices is a must.
*   Proficiency with AI platforms (Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, Leonardo or any other) and Adobe.
*   Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator).
*   Experience building scalable, brand-compliant templates for high-volume launches.
*   Attention to detail and the ability to work quickly without compromising quality.
*   Fluency in English is required; additional languages such as German, French, Italian, or Spanish are a plus.
*   Eagerness to learn and adapt in a fast-paced, data-driven environment.
