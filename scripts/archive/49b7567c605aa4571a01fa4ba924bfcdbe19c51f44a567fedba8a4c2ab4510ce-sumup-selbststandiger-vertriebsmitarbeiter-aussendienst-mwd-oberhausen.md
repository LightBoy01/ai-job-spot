### Description

Are you ready to **start your career in sales**, taking charge of your own success and managing your time freely? SumUp is looking for highly motivated individuals to join our team as **Freelance Field Sales Consultants**.

At SumUp, we empower small businesses with smart and user-friendly financial solutions, from mobile card payments to comprehensive POS systems. Our mission is to strengthen entrepreneurs, allowing them to focus entirely on their core business.

As a **freelance Field Sales Consultant**, you are your own boss, with the exciting opportunity to earn **attractive, unlimited commissions**! Your primary role will be to build relationships with local businesses, demonstrate how SumUp solutions can simplify their operations, and directly sell our innovative products, including mobile card terminals and complete payment systems. We provide you with everything you need to succeed in this dynamic role.

### Responsibilities

*   **Active New Customer Acquisition**: Identify and acquire local businesses as new SumUp clients.
*   **Personal Meetings and Consultations**: Present our products and demonstrate their benefits through engaging, in-person consultations.
*   **Build Customer Relationships**: Secure long-term partnerships by providing excellent service and support.
*   **Independent Customer and Lead Management**: Strategically build and manage your own pipeline and client base.

### Qualifications

*   A genuine passion for sales and strong self-motivation.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer service is an advantage, but not strictly required.
*   Excellent communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Very good German language skills.
