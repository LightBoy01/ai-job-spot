### Description\nCIRCL is a production studio for photo and film content, managing over 300 shooting days annually, creating campaigns, content formats, and brand presences for diverse clients. We approach projects holistically: from idea and concept to final execution. We are looking for a Creative Project Manager (f/m/d) Film Production to strengthen our team starting in January. In this role, you will not only structure and plan our productions but also think proactively and creatively. You will serve as the central interface between clients, creation, and production, reporting directly to the Production Lead and collaborating closely with our project management team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Conceive and manage film and video productions for content marketing and advertising.\n*   Oversee projects end-to-end – from briefing, planning, and shooting to final delivery.\n*   Keep a close eye on budget, timings, and resources.\n*   Coordinate all trades (camera, lighting, location, makeup, casting, post-production).\n*   Plan and organize shooting days, including call sheets and set workflows.\n*   Optionally take on set management yourself or bring in suitable production management.\n*   Ensure clear communication between the internal team, external partners, and clients.\n*   Implement presentations confidently and convincingly – whether in PowerPoint, Keynote, or similar tools.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   3-5 years of experience in project management (agency, production, or studio), ideally in film/video.\n*   Excellent skills in organization, budgeting, and time planning.\n*   Strong understanding of film/video production and on-set processes.\n*   Proficiency in project management tools and office software.\n*   Fluent in German and English, both written and spoken.\n*   Confident presentation skills and a good sense of structure, visuals, and storytelling.\n*   Initial experience with AI tools like ChatGPT, Midjourney, or similar – not a must, but a real plus.\n*   A structured, solution-oriented working style and enjoyment of a fast-paced, production-oriented environment.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting opportunity for a Mid-Senior Creative Project Manager to join CIRCL, a dynamic studio at the forefront of content creation. The role offers significant autonomy and creative input, allowing you to shape projects from conception to completion. With a focus on a collaborative and appreciative team environment, state-of-the-art facilities, and a willingness to embrace new technologies like AI, CIRCL fosters continuous growth and offers ample room to develop processes. If you thrive in a fast-paced, hands-on production setting with diverse clients, this role, based in Düsseldorf, provides a perfect platform to make a tangible impact.
