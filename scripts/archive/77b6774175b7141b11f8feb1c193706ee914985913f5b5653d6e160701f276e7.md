We are looking for an experienced, resourceful, and highly independent AI Deployment Manager to join our growing AI team based in the United States. You will play a critical role in ensuring the successful deployment of our AI products within client environments, working at the intersection of product, operations, IT, and customer success. This role requires a startup mindset, hands-on project leadership, and a proven ability to manage complex deployments involving multiple stakeholders, including clients’ IT and security teams. You will be responsible for end-to-end project management – from planning to go-live – ensuring our AI tools are seamlessly integrated into client operations. Experience with Smartsheet is a must, as you will own and manage project tracking, reporting, and dashboards. 

**Why join us?** 

*   Join a mission-driven company building cutting-edge AI solutions 
*   Work with global clients and top-tier professionals in a dynamic, remote-first team 
*   Help shape how AI is delivered and scaled across the world 
*   Competitive compensation, flexible working hours, and real impact 

Primary Job Responsibilities
----------------------------

*   Lead the deployment of AI software solutions across global client operations, ensuring high-quality, timely delivery
*   Own project planning and tracking using Smartsheet – including dashboards, Gantt charts, task assignments, and stakeholder updates
*   Act as the bridge between internal product teams, customer success, and client-side IT/security stakeholders 
*   Identify project risks, manage dependencies, and resolve blockers to ensure deployment success
*   Support client readiness, coordinating activities such as user testing, training, and go-live planning 
*   Ensure compliance with client security, privacy, and infrastructure requirements
*   Collaborate with internal teams to continuously improve deployment playbooks and best practices

Skills and Qualifications
-------------------------

*   5+ years of experience in project management or customer success roles, ideally within a startup or tech environment
*   Proven track record managing software deployments into enterprise environments, preferably in AI, SaaS, or IT consulting
*   Expert-level proficiency with Smartsheet (building dashboards, workflows, and project plans)
*   Highly autonomous and self-directed, with excellent organizational and problem-solving skills
*   Strong communication and stakeholder management abilities, including working with technical and non-technical teams 
*   Familiarity with IT and security processes (e.g., data privacy, InfoSec reviews, SSO, integrations) during software deployments 
*   Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, global, and remote-first environment
*   Fluent in English; additional languages a plus  

Preferred Qualifications
------------------------

*   Certification in project management (e.g., PMP, Prince2, Agile/Scrum) 
*   Experience working with AI or automation technologies 
*   Experience working with multinational clients in BPO, CX, or enterprise environments
