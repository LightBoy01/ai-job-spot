### Description\nSumUp is looking for highly motivated individuals to \"Start your career in sales\" as a freelance Field Sales Consultant. In this role, you act as your own boss, with the freedom to manage your time and achieve unlimited, performance-based commission. You will be responsible for building relationships with local businesses, introducing them to SumUp's smart and user-friendly financial solutions – from mobile card terminals to comprehensive payment systems – and demonstrating how these products can simplify their operations. This position emphasizes maximum flexibility, strong products with high market potential, and robust training and sales support to ensure your success in empowering small businesses.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Actively acquire new customers by identifying and winning local businesses for SumUp.\n- Conduct personal meetings and consultations to present SumUp products and demonstrate their practical benefits.\n- Build and maintain strong customer relationships to ensure long-term partnerships through excellent service.\n- Independently manage your own customer portfolio and leads, deciding on the best strategies to build your business.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Possess a strong passion for sales and a high degree of self-motivation.\n- First experience in sales or customer support is advantageous but not strictly required.\n- Demonstrate excellent communication skills and enjoy direct interaction with customers.\n- Have very good German language skills, essential for effective communication in the local market.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for ambitious, self-driven individuals eager to build an entrepreneurial career in sales. The 100% commission model offers true unlimited earning potential, directly rewarding your effort and success. SumUp provides excellent training and ongoing sales support, ensuring that even though you're an independent consultant, you're well-equipped and not left alone. The flexibility to work on your own terms is a significant perk, making it ideal for those who thrive on autonomy. While prior sales experience is a plus, the emphasis on starting a career suggests that enthusiastic candidates with strong communication skills and a passion for helping small businesses can excel here, provided they have very good German language skills.
