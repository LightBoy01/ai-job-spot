**Your role at Dynatrace**
--------------------------

Our Sales Development Representatives partner with our Account Executives across EMEA to build and implement an effective sales pipeline strategy. You will be engaging within the **Public Sector** market to drive the adoption of our market leading products and will need to be able to think strategically in order to adjust your approach depending on each customers unique needs. We have a newly created position for a **Spanish** speaking Sales Development Representative to join our team selling into the **Iberian** market.

This is a hybrid position, candidates should be commutable to our Barcelona office 4 days p/week. 

**Responsibilities**:

*   Drive new logo acquisitions and build sales pipeline and generate fully qualified sales opportunities through proactive outbound prospecting into target organisations.
*   Be creative and strategic with your sales approach by using all tools available (Sales Nav, Outreach, Salesloft etc.)
*   Building sales pipeline through qualifying inbound leads gained from events, downloads, trials and webinars.
*   Gather sales intelligence around customers situation, needs, issues and decision process.
*   Ensure quality data on prospects is updated and maintained within Salesforce.
*   Become fully emersed with Dynatrace’s products and its value add to customers in order to leverage our position against competitors.
*   Conduct product demonstrations for customers.

**What will help you succeed**
------------------------------

*   **Fluency in Spanish is essential**, additional fluency in **Portuguese** is highly desirable
*   Prior experience within a sales focus environment 
*   Able to think strategically and adapt sales approach to various prospects.
*   A driven, ambitious individual who thrives on meeting and exceeding sales targets.
*   Able to build effective internal working relationships with stakeholders such as Sales and Marketing
*   Experience of Salesforce is desirable but not essential.
