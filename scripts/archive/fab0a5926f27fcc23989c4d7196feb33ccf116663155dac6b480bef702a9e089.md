### Position: Web Analytics Specialist

**Location:** Riyadh – Head Office  
**Department:** Omni Team  
**Reports to:** Head of Omni  
**Type:** Full-time

* * *

### Role Purpose

As a Web Analytics Specialist at ABYAT, you will play a key role in shaping our ECommerce analytics strategy. Your insights will drive performance enhancements, improve customer journeys, and empower data-driven decision-making across our digital platforms. This is your chance to bring together analytical excellence and business acumen to influence outcomes at scale.

* * *

### Job Description

#### Site Performance & Conversion Optimization

*   Own the performance analysis of ABYAT’s ECommerce sites.
    
*   Identify and recommend opportunities to increase conversion rates and maximize revenue.
    
*   Track and optimize micro-conversions to enhance user flows and streamline the path to purchase.
    

#### Strategic Insight & Measurement

*   Design and implement a robust measurement framework to support both site and cross-functional teams.
    
*   Provide actionable insights and strategic recommendations based on site analytics and key business drivers.
    
*   Analyze the impact of content, features, and campaigns on customer behavior and business outcomes.
    

#### Collaboration Across Teams

*   Work closely with the Marketing Analytics team to connect campaign performance with user behavior.
    
*   Support the transition to a multi-touch attribution model.
    
*   Partner with the Data Analytics team to build and maintain real-time dashboards for key KPIs.
    

#### Innovation & Best Practices

*   Stay ahead of industry trends, tools, and methodologies.
    
*   Drive the continuous evolution of ABYAT’s web analytics capabilities through innovation and best practices.
    
*   Apply A/B testing and web performance optimization techniques to improve digital experiences.
    

* * *

### Qualifications

*   Minimum of 4 years of experience in web/digital analytics, ideally within an ECommerce setting.
    
*   Expertise in Google Analytics (GA4), including tagging and tracking implementation.
    
*   Strong understanding of user behavior analysis and conversion funnels.
    
*   Hands-on experience with multi-touch attribution models.
    
*   Proficient in A/B testing and web performance optimization.
    
*   Skilled in data visualization tools such as Tableau, Looker, or similar platforms.
    
*   Advanced proficiency in Excel and SQL for data modeling and manipulation.
    
*   Excellent analytical thinking and problem-solving skills.
    
*   Strong communication and storytelling abilities, especially in translating complex data into impactful narratives.
    
*   Self-starter with excellent organizational skills and adaptability in a dynamic environment.
