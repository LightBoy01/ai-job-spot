**SECURITY SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM - WILSON SECURITY DARWIN**

No Experience Needed | Up to $100,000 per year with overtime | Fully funded Certificate II in Security Operations  
   
Wilson Security is offering a fully paid Certificate II in Security Operations and a direct pathway into long-term employment.

  
   
ABOUT WILSON:  
As one of the largest providers of security services in Australia and New Zealand, our valued employees are supported by a highly experienced management team, industry-leading expertise and a strong local and national infrastructure operating 24 hours a day.  
   
Our vision is to be the most trusted provider of integrated Security Solutions and our purpose, is to build a safer future for everyone. Wilson Security is committed to shaping the future of security by deploying innovative and sustainable solutions that creates enduring value for our people, customers and the communities we operate in.  
About the Program  
Wilson Security is offering a unique Security Scholarship Program for Australian citizens in Darwin who are ready to start a full-time career in the security industry.  
No previous experience in security is required – with the right attitude, we can teach it!  
We understand that the cost associated with training and licensing can be a barrier, so we’re removing that hurdle. Wilson Security will fully fund the entry requirements for 10 successful applicants who are committed to joining our team in a full-time capacity.

  
What’s Included – Over $3,000 in Value  
•    Fully funded Certificate II in Security Operations  
•    12-month Security Licence registration  
•    National name and fingerprint check (Criminal History Check)  
•    Baseline Security Clearance  
•    Full uniform, including boots  
•    Four-on, four-off roster providing excellent work-life balance  
•    Comprehensive on-the-job training – no prior experience required

  
What We’re Offering  
•    The potential to earn up to $100,000 per year with overtime and allowances  
•    Long-term career opportunities within Defence and other major security contracts  
•    Ongoing mentoring, training, and clear career progression pathways  
•    A supportive team environment with a national leader in security services  
Who We’re Looking For  
•    Australian citizens  
•    Reliable, professional individuals with a strong work ethic  
•    Candidates able to commit to full-time employment once licensed

  
Eligibility  
•    Must be an Australian citizen  
•    Must be able to obtain a security licence (medical and background checks apply)  
•    Must be committed to completing training and joining Wilson Security as a full-time Security Officer  
   
Return of service obligation may apply\*
