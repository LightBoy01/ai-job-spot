**Job Description:**

We’re seeking a **Principal AI Infrastructure Engineer** to architect, scale, and optimize the backend infrastructure powering our enterprise GenAI platform.   
In this hands-on leadership role, you’ll design distributed systems for AI/ML at scale, optimize infrastructure for large model workloads, and drive innovation at the intersection of cloud engineering and AI. This is a high-impact opportunity to shape the infrastructure behind next-generation GenAI products. 

**Responsibilities:**

*   Lead the architecture and delivery of highly scalable, cloud-native, and cloud-agnostic infrastructure supporting AI/ML workflows. 
    

*   Design and implement backend systems optimized for GenAI workloads, including large-scale model training, tuning, and serving. 
    

*   Partner with Product, Applied Research, and Engineering teams to influence platform direction and technical strategy. 
    

*   Champion operational excellence in automation, security, networking, observability, and cost optimization. 
    

*   Mentor engineers, promote best practices, and help grow a strong technical culture. 
    

*   Participate in on-call rotation as needed to ensure platform reliability and incident management. 
    

**Required Qualifications** 

*   8+ years of experience building and operating scalable, high-performance cloud infrastructure on **AWS, GCP, or Azure**. 
    

*   Expertise with **Infrastructure as Code** (e.g., Terraform, CloudFormation). 
    

*   Practical experience with **containerization and orchestration** technologies (Docker, Kubernetes). 
    

*   Strong background in **distributed systems, network architecture, and security best practices**. 
    

*   Familiarity with **service mesh** (e.g., Istio, Linkerd) and **API gateways** (e.g., Kong, Traefik). 
    

*   Proficiency in scripting and automation (e.g., Python, Bash). 
    

*   Proven ability to lead technical initiatives and mentor engineers. 
    

*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field, or equivalent experience. 
    

**Preferred Qualifications** 

*   10+ years of infrastructure engineering experience, including large-scale AI/ML or data platform deployments. 
    

*   Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field. 
    

*   Experience building and optimizing infrastructure for **training, fine-tuning, and deploying LLMs** in production. 
    

*   Knowledge of orchestration frameworks like **Flyte**, **Kubeflow**, or **MLflow**. 
    

*   Deep understanding of SRE/DevOps principles, including automation, observability, and incident response at scale. 
    

*   Previous experience in a **high-growth startup** or **enterprise AI/SaaS** environment.
