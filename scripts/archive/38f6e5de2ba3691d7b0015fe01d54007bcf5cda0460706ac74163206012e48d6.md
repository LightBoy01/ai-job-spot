**Job Description:**

**About you:**

You are someone who wants to influence your own development. You’re looking for a company where you have the opportunity to pursue your interests and be able to grow professionally.

**You bring to Applaudo the following competencies:** 

*   Strong skills in the Python ML stack (scikit-learn, pandas, NumPy).
*   Hands-on experience with LLM integration (OpenAI API, LangChain, fine-tuning workflows).
*   Knowledge of forecasting models (regression, time-series, probabilistic approaches).
*   Expertise in confidence scoring systems (rule-based + ML hybrids).
*   Solid background in model evaluation and bias/error analysis.
*   Ability to design and implement data pipelines for training and inference.
*   Familiarity with MLOps practices (model versioning, retraining pipelines, monitoring).
*   Experience with HuggingFace Transformers.
*   Knowledge of vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, FAISS).
*   Exposure to CI/CD and automation tools for ML workflows (a plus).
*   Good conversational English (B2 or higher).

**You will be accountable for the following responsibilities:** 

*   Design and implement ML and LLM-powered solutions that solve real-world business challenges.
*   Build and optimize pipelines for training, fine-tuning, inference, and monitoring of AI models.
*   Develop and evaluate forecasting models across business domains.
*   Create robust confidence scoring and model validation systems.
*   Integrate LLMs into applications through APIs, LangChain, and retrieval-augmented generation setups.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless integration of AI models into products and workflows.
*   Conduct bias/error analysis to ensure fairness, reliability, and transparency in AI solutions.
*   Maintain and optimize vector database solutions for retrieval and search tasks.
*   Stay up-to-date with the latest AI/ML advancements and proactively propose improvements.
*   Mentor peers and contribute to a culture of excellence in AI/ML practices.
