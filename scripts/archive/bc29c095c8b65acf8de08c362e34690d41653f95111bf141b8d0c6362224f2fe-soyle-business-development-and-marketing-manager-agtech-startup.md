We are looking for a Business Development & Marketing Manager to join our founding team. This role can be part-time or full-time and offers real ownership and impact shaping the growth of our product in the agriculture sector.

Tasks
-----

*   Develop and implement go-to-market strategies targeting farmers, cooperatives, and agribusiness partners in Germany
*   Build, manage, and grow pipelines of farmer and partner leads through outreach, pilot projects, and marketing campaigns
*   Organize farmer events, demos, and campaigns to increase adoption and gather feedback
*   Collaborate with founders to improve product-market fit based on farmer and partner insights
*   Track key growth metrics and maintain simple sales and marketing reporting

Requirements
------------

*   Native-level German fluency and strong English communication skills
*   Experience in business development, sales, marketing, or account management, preferably in startups
*   Preferably experience in agriculture, farmer advisory, or working with agribusinesses
*   Entrepreneurial mindset with strong ownership, commitment, and ability to work independently
*   Willingness to work in a dynamic early-stage startup environment
*   Valid Class B driving license

Benefits
--------

*   Founding team role with ownership and long-term growth potential
*   Stock options to share in the future success and growth of Soyle
*   Opportunity to impact sustainable farming at scale
*   Flexible work arrangements (part-time/full-time, remote options possible)
*   Be part of a mission-driven company with a positive environmental impact

Ready to bring your business development and marketing skills to the forefront of AgTech innovation? Join Soyle’s founding team to support farmers in adopting cutting-edge AI technology and making agriculture more sustainable. Apply now to be part of our exciting journey!
