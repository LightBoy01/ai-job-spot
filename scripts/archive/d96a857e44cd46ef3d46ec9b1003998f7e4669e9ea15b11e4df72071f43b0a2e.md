**About this opportunity
**
At the department of device research at Ericsson, we have the objective to host a few master thesis projects during the spring period (January to June) 2026. We are seeking master's students who are team players and enthusiastic about communication technologies and extended reality (XR) and can help us research and implement environment-aware immersive communications in XR and understand the effects on user experience, latency and compute requirements and technology challenges in related use cases.
**What you will do
**
The scope of the master thesis includes but is not limited to the following topics:
\- Fusion of camera-based and radio-based positioning
\- Computer vision for immersive communications
\- Multisensory XR and haptics
\- Spatial audio for XR immersive communications
**The skills you bring
**
We are looking for two self-motivated and creative students per project with the following qualifications:
Master student in an Engineering field preferably within Electronics Engineering, Computer Science or Engineering Physics with AR/VR.
Fluency in English
Excellent analytical skills
Hands-on programming experience in Python and/or Unity
Some experience with computer vision
Some experience with machine learning and AI
