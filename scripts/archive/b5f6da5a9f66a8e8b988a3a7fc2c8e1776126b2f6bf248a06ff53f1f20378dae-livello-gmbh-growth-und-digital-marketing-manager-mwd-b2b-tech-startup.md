### Description\nLivello stands for the future of **Autonomous Retail** – smart mini-stores, available 24/7, for self-service, made in Germany. We are looking for an all-rounder Marketing talent with **AI power and a Growth Mindset** to grow our brand and excite new customers. Livello develops intelligent, autonomous **Smart Kiosks** – fully automated vending stations that independently recognize, manage, and sell products. Our mission is to provide people everywhere with quick access to healthy foods and everyday products – **24/7, everywhere.** Our vision is to shape the future of **\"Automated Autonomous Retail\"** and bring our technology to every company, hotel, and public building. If this sounds like you, let’s build the future of retail – together.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Lead Generation & Campaign Management:** You will conceptualize, automate, and optimize multi-channel campaigns (email, LinkedIn, SEO, SEA, PR, events) to generate B2B leads for our Sales Team.\n*   **Social Media & PR:** You will plan and produce creative content for LinkedIn, Instagram & Co., identify topics from tech, IoT, and food innovation, and position Livello in relevant media.\n*   **AI Automation and Content Creation:** You will utilize tools like **ChatGPT, Nano Banana, Midjourney, Sora, Runway, Gemini,** etc., to create compelling texts, images, videos, and research.\n*   **SEO & Website Optimization:** You will maintain our website, develop new landing pages for industry-specific use cases (e.g., offices, hotels, clinics, fitness, events), and improve our search engine ranking.\n*   **Outbound & Partner Marketing:** You will research, identify, and acquire partners (e.g., trade fair collaborations, food brands, vending operators, resellers), prepare Cold Email campaigns, and open new distribution channels.\n*   **Event & Trade Fair Management:** You will organize trade fair appearances, handling planning, branding, and follow-up leads.\n*   **Analytics & Reporting:** You will track campaign performance, funnel KPIs, conversion rates, and report to management.\n*   **CRM & Marketing-Ops:** You will work closely with Sales, manage leads in the CRM (e.g., Pipedrive, HubSpot), develop automations, and segment target groups based on data.\n\n###
