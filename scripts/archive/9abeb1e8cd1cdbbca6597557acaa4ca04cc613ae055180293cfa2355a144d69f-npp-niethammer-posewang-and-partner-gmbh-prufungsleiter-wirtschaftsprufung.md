### Description\nNPP Niethammer, Posewang & Partner GmbH is a mid-sized, multidisciplinary auditing and tax consulting firm. Our centrally located office in Hamburg welcomes colleagues and clients alike. As a proud member of the Alliott Global Alliance, we are perfectly positioned to efficiently resolve cross-border issues. Our diverse clientele includes medium-sized companies, listed corporations, pan-European trading companies, and various other service providers. Due to our continuous growth, we are actively seeking dedicated colleagues. You will be warmly welcomed and become an important part of our team from day one. Join our dynamic team as an Audit Lead and experience personalized consultation at eye level, professional excellence, and a modern work environment in Hamburg. Become a part of NPP!\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Lead & organize annual and consolidated financial statement audits (HGB & IFRS).\n*   Provide technical leadership and professional development for your audit team.\n*   Independently manage complex consulting & expert appraisal activities.\n*   Engage in interdisciplinary collaboration with our tax advisors on exciting projects.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Proven experience as an Audit Lead in financial auditing.\n*   Proficiency with DATEV & Audicon, and a genuine interest in digital processes, are a distinct advantage.\n*   Profound expertise in accounting, valuation, and auditing techniques.\n*   An open, personable individual with strong communication skills.\n*   Demonstrated team spirit, reliability, and self-initiative.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an experienced Audit Lead looking to take on significant responsibility within a growing and supportive firm. NPP offers a leadership role where you'll not only manage critical audits for a diverse client base but also play a key part in the technical development of your team and contribute to interdisciplinary projects. The company culture stands out with its emphasis on a relaxed, familial atmosphere, flat hierarchies, and strong appreciation for employee contributions. With benefits like flexible working hours, home office options, 30 vacation days, a personal mentor, and intensive support for professional development, NPP clearly invests in its people. This role is ideal for a professional seeking challenging work, genuine impact, and a truly employee-centric environment in the heart of Hamburg.
