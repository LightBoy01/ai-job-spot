### Description
Bots & People is seeking a highly motivated and structured **Growth Marketing Generalist** with a specialization in Account-based Marketing, Field Marketing, or Content. This entrepreneurial role offers the opportunity to take full ownership of the growth engine, scaling awareness within the enterprise segment and driving revenue by converting target customers.

Bots & People empowers enterprise teams through virtual live sessions and blended learning journeys, focusing on critical areas such as AI, data, personal productivity, and low-code solutions. You will join a high-growth environment, working closely with senior leadership to shape the future of enterprise learning in the exciting field of Generative AI.

### Responsibilities
As the Growth Marketing Generalist, you will be instrumental in building and optimizing our marketing efforts, driving our enterprise growth engine. Your key responsibilities will include:

*   **Pipeline Creation:** Develop and execute strategies for enterprise pipeline creation, targeting a named account list aligned with our Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) and revenue targets.
*   **Events & Webinars:** Plan, host, and optimize a portfolio of owned webinars and events, including evaluating and staffing external conferences (e.g., Learntec), driving pre-event outreach, managing on-site presence, and ensuring post-event conversion.
*   **ABM & Outbound:** Collaborate closely with the sales team to design and implement personalized Account-Based Marketing campaigns, encompassing tactics like physical mailers, custom landing pages, and crafting follow-up cadences. Opportunity to engage in initial customer conversations via email or LinkedIn.
*   **Paid Social:** Take end-to-end ownership of LinkedIn and Meta campaigns, managing audiences, creative briefs, offers, budgeting, and experimentation, with support from a freelance performance marketing expert.
*   **Content & Campaign Calendar:** Operate a reliable and on-time content engine, overseeing the consistent delivery of blog posts, newsletters, and LinkedIn content.
*   **Measurement & Revenue Architecture:** Track bowtie-style funnel metrics, maintain UTMs, and manage lead reporting with a pragmatic approach, focusing on key indicators like target account engagement, qualified pipeline value, conversion rates, and experiment velocity.
*   **Collaborative Goal Setting:** Define success metrics for the initial 6-12 months alongside the team, focusing on impact areas such as Tier-1 account engagement, Marketing Qualified Accounts (MQAs), and sourced pipeline.

### Qualifications
We are looking for a highly capable individual with a blend of strategic thinking and hands-on execution skills.

**Required:**

*   **Experience:** 4-8 years of experience in B2B growth, demand generation, Account-Based Marketing (ABM), or field marketing, specifically within startups or scale-ups, including hands-on outbound targeting towards enterprise clients.
*   **AI Mindset:** An AI-first mindset, comfortable leveraging the latest AI technologies to enhance marketing efforts.
*   **Copywriting & Messaging:** Strong proficiency in copywriting and crafting compelling messages for email, LinkedIn, and landing pages, including the ability to write effective cadences and talk tracks.
*   **Structured Operator:** A highly organized and structured approach to work, with the ability to plan ahead, maintain a visible calendar/roadmap, and consistently meet deadlines.
*   **Language Skills:** Excellent command of English and good proficiency in German.
*   **Event Enthusiasm:** Eagerness to attend and organize both external and internal onsite events.

**Nice to Have:**

*   **Platform Competence:** Familiarity with LinkedIn Campaign Manager and Meta Ads, including the ability to brief creatives and manage experiments.
*   **Tool Fluency:** Competence with CRM/automation platforms, analytics tools, data enrichment services, and webinar/event management software.
*   **Framework Knowledge:** Familiarity with "Winning by Design" concepts, such as SPICED and the Bowtie funnel.
