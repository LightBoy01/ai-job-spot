Mercedes-Benz Group AG is one of the most successful automotive companies in the world. As part of Mercedes-Benz AG, the manufacturer ranks among the leading providers of premium and luxury passenger cars and vans. Join us in shaping the future of Mercedes-Benz Vans Operations and become part of our team.

The department Operational Excellence, Digitalization, Lean & Green is responsible for strategic alignment, transformation, efficiency management, and project coordination. Together, we drive digitalization, innovation, sustainability, and Lean Management initiatives across the global production network for all organizational units.

Our team consists of six highly motivated colleagues who are looking forward to your support. We are responsible for optimizing operational efficiencies and managing transformation and efficiency programs within VAN/O. We serve as the central competence center for Lean & MPS methods and lay the foundation for best practice sharing and exchange in Centers of Competence. Our goal is to support and empower the plants and cross-functional areas in achieving their targets. We work smart, passionately, and collaboratively, valuing direct and regular communication at eye level. Constructive and honest interaction is important to us. Mobile working is regularly practiced. You will take responsibility for global sub-projects and diverse, exciting tasks with significant impact on the entire organization. We place high value on solution orientation and a partnership-based approach with colleagues and stakeholders.

* * *

**Responsibilities:**

*   Lead cross-plant production optimization projects based on Lean Management principles and ensure long-term project success on-site.
*   Coordinate MPS activities at all VAN/O locations in collaboration with plant management and local MPS offices.
*   Act as key account for cross-plant Centers of Competence (CoC) in body shop, paint shop, assembly, intra- logistics, and maintenance, actively promoting best practice exchange.
*   Advise and support production management regarding Lean Management, MPSadv, SFMdigital, and standard KPIs at VAN sites.
*   Ensure close collaboration with local MPS offices.
*   Collaborate closely with colleagues and interfaces in VAN Operations, Mercedes-Benz Cars, and Powertrain, and actively contribute to MPS Cooperation to foster synergies and innovation.
*   Implement a cross-hierarchical improvement culture and actively drive the change process.
*   Prepare and conduct strategic and/or operational (hybrid) workshops across all hierarchy levels to effectively advance projects and initiatives.
*   Create decision templates and project progress reports for steering committees and top management, including professional and audience-appropriate presentation of content.
*   Drive the introduction and implementation of standardized methods, KPIs, and processes within VAN/O.

  

**Professional Qualifications:**

*   Degree in mechanical engineering or a comparable field with solid knowledge of production systems and processes.
*   Several years of professional experience in production and/or Lean Management, as well as in executing optimization projects, ideally in a production environment.
*   Certification as Lean Consultant L(M)C / MPS Expert or willingness to obtain it.
*   In-depth methodological knowledge in project management and Lean Management.
*   Profound knowledge of Lean Management; comprehensive understanding of the Mercedes-Benz Production System (MPS) are a plus
*   Fluent in German and English; Spanish skills are a plus.

**Personal Qualifications:**

*   Independent and constructive working style with the ability to develop viable solutions with stakeholders.
*   Methodological expertise in project management and Lean Management.
*   Experience in the automotive sector with a focus on production and Lean Management.
*   Quick comprehension and outstanding analytical and conceptual skills to understand and explain complex relationships.
*   Strong team orientation, reliability, and problem-solving skills even in challenging situations.
*   Excellent moderation, communication, and presentation skills with the ability to convey complex topics clearly, structured, and visually appealing.
*   High mobility and willingness to travel for project assignments.
