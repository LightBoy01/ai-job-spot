As a Software Developer, you will contribute to crafting tailored solutions for client challenges. At isento, we are a true team, relying on each other both professionally and personally. We believe in mutual support to achieve our shared goal: delivering the best solutions for our customers. This role offers the chance to be part of a supportive environment where your contributions directly impact client success.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop new web applications, modernize and extend existing systems, migrate applications to the cloud, or work on research and AI topics, depending on the project.\n- Engage daily in writing clean, understandable code, conducting Unit and Integration Tests, Code Reviews, knowledge sharing, and Pair Programming.\n- Participate in the conception of new features and coordinate with stakeholders.\n- Take joint responsibility with your team for the entire software lifecycle, from conception, development, and testing to deployment and monitoring.\n- Actively contribute your own ideas, show initiative, and drive topics forward with responsibility and foresight.\n- Work with technologies like Angular in the frontend, and Java, Spring Boot, and/or Python in the backend (familiarity with all is not required; projects will align with your strengths).\n\n### Qualifications\n- A passion for software development.\n- Professional experience in software projects with an understanding of scalable and maintainable solutions.\n- Proficiency in at least one of the following programming languages: Java, Python, or TypeScript.\n- Eagerness to learn new technologies; prior experience in Spring Boot, Angular, databases, AWS, CI/CD, cloud technologies, Reinforcement Learning, LLMs, or IT Security is a plus, but not strictly required.\n- Curiosity, a desire for exchange, and a willingness to share knowledge for mutual learning and growth.\n- Good German language skills (minimum B2) for confident communication within the team and with stakeholders.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at isento offers a compelling opportunity for a mid-level software developer who thrives in a highly collaborative and supportive team environment.
