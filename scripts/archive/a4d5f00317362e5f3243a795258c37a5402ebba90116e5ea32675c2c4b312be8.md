MOO helps the world’s most ambitious businesses showcase their brands beautifully. From our iconic business cards to a growing range of custom products, we blend intuitive design with craftsmanship to create moments of delight. As we enter a new era, expanding our platform, driving sustainability, and reimagining our brand, we’re seeking a creative leader who can shape what’s next. The Creative Director leads MOO’s in-house creative team at the heart of our brand. This role combines strategic vision with hands-on leadership, inspiring world-class work across every touchpoint. You’ll set the creative direction, guide storytelling and execution, and build a culture where great ideas thrive. You’ll also modernise how we work, introducing smart tools, scalable systems, and creative partnerships that let the team focus on the work that matters most.  
  
**Key Responsibilities**  
*   Define and lead MOO’s creative vision and strategy with clarity and originality.
*   Help bring our brand story to life consistently across all channels, from our site experience to digital campaigns to social content.
*   Inspire and lead and nurture a multi-disciplinary global team, fostering collaboration, inclusivity, and creative excellence.
*   Use insight, customer understanding, and emerging trends to shape ideas that connect emotionally and deliver results.
*   Create partnerships and processes that scale creative output without compromising quality.
*   Embed modern tools and workflows, including AI, to increase efficiency and free up time for deeper creative thinking.
*   Collaborate with leaders across the business to bring ideas to life that grow awareness, engagement, and loyalty.
  
  
**Requirements**  
*   Extensive experience in senior creative leadership, either in-house or at an agency.
*   A strong portfolio that demonstrates strategic thinking, storytelling, and design excellence.
*   Proven experience developing creative strategies grounded in customer insight and adaptable to diverse audiences.
*   Ability to inspire and lead creative teams while influencing stakeholders across the organisation.
*   Familiarity with scalable production models and digital-first creative workflows.
*   A strategic mindset that balances creativity with commercial understanding.
*   Experience in a B2B or design-led environment is a plus.
