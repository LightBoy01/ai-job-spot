We are looking for an Insight Manager to drive transformative change for our clients through expert research and analysis. You will manage a range of quantitative and qualitative projects, from design to delivery, ensuring they precisely meet client objectives.
The ideal candidate is a proactive project manager with a sharp eye for detail, skilled at translating data into actionable insights. You thrive in a team environment while taking ownership of your work, building trusted relationships with both clients and colleagues to achieve strategic goals.
Key Responsibilities
--------------------
**Impactful client delivery**
*   You are considered a trusted research project manager.
*   You are eager to move projects forward and play a key role in the planning, set-up and management of projects of increasing complexity (e.g., multinational, multi-methodology, etc.), with support from senior team members.
*   You are actively interested in the use of AI to work more efficiently and effectively, while maintaining a strong ‘human’ touch. You learn how to use AI in a business relevant way, to contextualize your work and add value to the client.
*   You understand how to use behavioral frameworks and models. With guidance you know how to apply these in your analysis and reporting.
*   You have experience in presenting research projects to clients, likely with senior support as needed.
**Account Management**
*   You will be comfortable having a lot of client contact and being the lead project manager, as well as supporting senior consultants with project-related tasks, communications, and enacting client strategies.
*   You have a good understanding of the client’s needs and business objectives, and are open to being able to challenge and think along.
Skills, Knowledge & Expertise
-----------------------------
*   A bachelor's degree in a related field (e.g., social science, psychology, business, economics) or equivalent practical experience.
*   Around 5 years of professional research experience.
*   **Curiosity and Eagerness**: A desire to learn and understand complex human behavior.
*   **Proactive and organized:** Being able to juggle a lot of moving parts.
*   **Communication**: Clear and pro-active communication within the team and towards clients.
*   **Adaptability**: Willingness to adapt to changing project requirements and rapidly evolving industry.
*   **Analytical:** A high affinity with data interpretation and analysis.
*   **Innovative**: Actively experimenting with AI, eager to learn and use new technologies to bring added value for the client.
*   **Collaborative**: You love to achieve and win together!
Job Benefits
------------
*   You will join a pioneering company in a dynamic and innovative industry that is re-inventing itself.
*   You will grow as a marketing insights consultant, and thrive on the cross road of AI capabilities and deep human understanding.
*   Aspirational brands will seek your guidance.
*   Competitive salary and local benefits.
