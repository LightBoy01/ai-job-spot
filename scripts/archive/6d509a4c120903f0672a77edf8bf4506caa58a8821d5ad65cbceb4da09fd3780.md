Senior Software Engineer (Python/PHP) - Vilnius, Lithuania

Job Type

Full-time

Description

**About AccuSourceHR** 

AccuSourceHR™ is a full-service employment screening organization headquartered in Phoenix, Arizona. Since 1999, we've combined innovative technology with high-quality, US-based client care. Our culture is built on collaboration, continuous learning, and a commitment to excellence, ensuring our team members feel valued, supported, and empowered.

  

**Role Summary** 

Are you passionate about building innovative, customer-facing applications? AccuSourceHR is seeking a Senior Software Engineer to join our Innovation team. You’ll work on cutting-edge solutions using Python, PHP, Vue.js, and AI technologies, helping us disrupt the market and make a meaningful impact in the communities we serve. 

**Key Responsibilities** 

*   **D**evelop web application using PHP, Python and Vue.js with MySQL Databases.
*   **E**ngineer solutions in AWS cloud environments. 
*   **V**isualize and implement AI-powered features.
*   **E**nsure software is scalable, secure, and maintainable.  
*   **L**everage automation and write clean, testable code.
*   **O**rganize documentation and foster cross-team collaboration.
*   **P**rovide mentorship and support team growth.

Requirements

Required Education/Experience:

*   Minimum: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field (or equivalent experience). 
*   5–7 years of experience in web application development. 
*   3+ years of experience in front-end development (JavaScript, Vue.js, or similar frameworks). 
*   Strong experience in backend programming using Python or PHP, including database and REST API development. 

Preferred/Bonus Experience:

*   Exposure to AI-driven development, including tools such as OpenAI, Copilot, or JetBrains AI Assistant. 
*   Experience integrating LLMs into applications. 
*   Familiarity with cloud-based services such as AWS (SageMaker, Bedrock, Lambda) or equivalent platforms. 
*   Experience with asynchronous frameworks (Aiohttp, FastAPI, or similar). 

Tech Stack:

*   Languages & Frameworks: Python, PHP, JavaScript, Vue.js, HTML5, CSS3. 
*   Backend & Database: REST APIs, MySQL or other relational databases. 
*   AI & Cloud (Preferred): OpenAI, Copilot, JetBrains AI Assistant, AWS. 
*   Tools & Methodologies: Git, Agile/Scrum. 

**Benefits** 

*   Work with a team of curious, kind, and driven humans. 
*   Hybrid work setup (because flexibility matters) - up to 3 days remote. 
*   Free parking at the office. 
*   Snacks, coffee, and good vibes always stocked. 
*   Private health insurance. 
*   Space to grow, experiment, and make a real impact. 

**Salary** 

4,300-5,000 EUR gross/month (depending on experience) 

  

**Physical Requirements** 

*   Ability to commute to our Vilnius office 2 times / week. 
*   A dedicated and ergonomic workspace at home conducive to focused work, including proper seating and desk setup. 
*   Access to a stable and reliable high-speed internet connection. 
*   Adequate lighting and minimal background noise to support professional video calls and meetings. 
*   Ability to lift and carry up to 5 pounds occasionally, for tasks such as setting up a workstation or equipment.
