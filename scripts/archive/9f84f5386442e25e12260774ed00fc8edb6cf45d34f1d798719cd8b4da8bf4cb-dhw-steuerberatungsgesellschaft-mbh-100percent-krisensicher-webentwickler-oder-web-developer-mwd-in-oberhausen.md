### Description\nDHW Steuerberatungsgesellschaft, a modern tax consulting firm based in Oberhausen, Germany, is seeking a dedicated Web Developer to strengthen its digital presence. Despite being a tax firm, DHW has a strong passion for e-commerce and recognizes the critical importance of a first-class web presence. To achieve this, DHW is moving away from external agencies and \"freelancers\", seeking an in-house \"Web-Macher\" (Web Maker) who values short communication paths and quick decisions.\n\nThis is a unique opportunity for a pragmatic web specialist to take ownership of DHW's online platforms. The role offers a crisis-proof, permanent position within an extremely modern and digitally oriented firm, alongside attractive benefits such as home office options, flexible working hours, significant freedom in project implementation, and the choice of your own hardware. You'll be joining a humorous and uncomplicated team dedicated to innovation and efficiency.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Be responsible for the further development, adaptation, and maintenance of DHW's WordPress websites.\n- Create engaging landing pages and effective funnels.\n- Implement smaller web automations, for instance, via API/Webhooks, to streamline processes.\n- Continuously monitor and optimize SEO, PageSpeed, mobile responsiveness, and overall website performance.\n- Act as a technical sparring partner for the Marketing team and Management, providing expert guidance and insights.\n- (Optional) Provide support in broader marketing and sales activities.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You are a pragmatic \"Web-Macher\" – focused on practical web implementation rather than being a classical IT developer.\n- Possess very good knowledge and hands-on experience in: WordPress, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP.\n- Have experience working with Custom Code, Child-Themes, and WordPress Hooks.\n- Demonstrate an eye for design and a solid understanding of UI/UX principles.\n- Ideally, you also have experience with Progressive Web App (PWA) concepts.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptionally interesting role that defies typical expectations for a tax consulting firm. DHW's commitment to e-commerce and digital excellence clearly positions them as a forward-thinking employer. The opportunity to be the sole in-house web expert means significant autonomy and direct impact on the company's digital strategy and growth. The benefits package, particularly the home office option, flexible hours, and choice of hardware, speaks volumes about their modern, employee-centric culture. This is a fantastic fit for a Mid-Senior web developer who thrives on hands-on work, enjoys a high degree of freedom, and wants to contribute directly to a dynamic and digitally-driven environment.
