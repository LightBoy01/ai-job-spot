### Description

**Are you an organizational talent with foresight?**

For our client, the business consultancy **T.Eilers & Partner**, we are seeking a dedicated **Assistant for Project and Office Management (m/f/d)**. This is a flexible remote opportunity.

T.Eilers & Partner specializes in the holistic **consulting of small and medium-sized businesses** and their **private households**. Our goal is to **optimally structure our clients' income and expenses** to ensure sustainable economic success.

We place great emphasis on **structured processes**, **efficient workflows**, and **clear communication** – and this is exactly where you come in!

### Responsibilities

*   Support project management in daily operations.
*   Organize and coordinate internal processes and external appointments.
*   Prepare and maintain project documents, reports, and presentations.
*   Communicate with clients, partners, and internal teams.
*   Assist with administrative tasks in office management.

### Qualifications

*   Organizational talent and a good sense for priorities.
*   Independent and structured way of working.
*   Strong communication skills and team spirit.
*   Proficient in MS Office and digital tools.
*   Initial experience in project or office management is an advantage – but not a must!

### Author's Take

This role at T.Eilers & Partner offers a fantastic opportunity for an organized and proactive individual to be the backbone of a dynamic consultancy. You'll enjoy significant flexibility, with options for remote work or in a modern office environment, and can even choose part-time, full-time, or freelance engagement (e.g., Virtual Assistant). The company prides itself on flat hierarchies, quick decision-making, and a supportive, motivated team that values your ideas and contributions.

With a focus on structured processes and client success, you'll have the chance to work on exciting projects and truly grow your skills. The compensation ranges from €530 to €3,150 monthly, depending on your qualifications and the hours you commit (starting from 8-15 hours/week). If you're looking for a role where your organizational skills directly contribute to the success of small and medium-sized businesses in a flexible and growth-oriented environment, this could be your next step!
