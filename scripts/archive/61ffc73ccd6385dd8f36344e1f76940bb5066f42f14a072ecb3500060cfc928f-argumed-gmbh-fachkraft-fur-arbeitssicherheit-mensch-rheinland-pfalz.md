### Description

Join us in transforming an entire industry! Argumed's goal is to become Europe's leading provider in occupational health and safety. With a hybrid combination of a digital product portfolio and highly qualified specialists, we help employers keep their employees safe and healthy. We work daily to further digitize the industry and prepare it for the future.

To expand our expert team at Argumed, we are looking for a **Safety Specialist or Safety Engineer (m/f/d)** who will work from a home office and on-site with clients (7-9 field visits per month in your local area):

### Responsibilities

*   Take responsibility for the entire basic support process for our clients, from initial inspection and creation of risk assessments to participation in training sessions and instructions, and planning and preparation of ASA meetings.
*   Advise and support our clients on all matters related to occupational safety and health.
*   Be an attentive point of contact for your clients and support them with their daily challenges, e.g., BEM (occupational reintegration management) or statements to professional associations.
*   Organize and conduct training sessions and instructions in the field of occupational safety.
*   Investigate and analyze workplace accidents, record and evaluate investigation results, and develop preventative measures.
*   Supervise projects, if qualified, in areas such as hazardous substance management and fire protection.
*   Be a sparring partner: Help our digitalization team with creative ideas and your expertise to offer our clients digitally supported and tailored services, and to make your own work easier every day.

### Qualifications

*   You are a certified Safety Specialist or Safety Engineer (m/f/d).
*   You are open to new and digital approaches and solutions in occupational safety and may have already gained experience in this regard.
*   You have no problem working with little supervision, making independent decisions, and taking responsibility for them.
*   You possess the necessary willingness to travel to optimally advise clients on-site.
*   You work meticulously, have an eye for detail, and know how to maintain the balance between practice and theory.
*   You find it easy to understand client needs and pragmatically identify the best solution.
*   You speak German at a native level (Bonus: very good English).
