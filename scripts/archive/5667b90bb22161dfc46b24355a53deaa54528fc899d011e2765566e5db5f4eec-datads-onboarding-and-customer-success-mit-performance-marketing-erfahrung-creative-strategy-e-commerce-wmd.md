### Description

At **DatAds**, we've set ourselves an ambitious goal: to elevate the ad creative process to the next level. Our software acts as the **bridge** between data-driven **Performance Marketing and innovative Creative Teams**. This enables Performance Marketers and Creative Designers to collaborate optimally and create more successful ad campaigns through AI analyses.

We work with some of the largest eCommerce brands and marketing agencies, including Holy, yfood, ahead, Rosental, Pammys, Beilmann, Perspective, and many more. Are you ready to become part of a fast-growing **Marketing SaaS startup** and **actively shape** the journey of DatAds?

### Responsibilities

*   **Customer Success & Consulting:** Support and development of our eCom & Agency customers. You will analyze existing setups, identify optimization potential, and help integrate DatAds more effectively into their daily processes.
*   **Customer Support:** Conduct regular calls with clients, provide support via Slack, and actively assist with strategic questions concerning their creatives and campaigns.
*   **Upselling & Reactivation:** Identify growth opportunities with existing customers and proactively engage inactive or former leads via our CRM.
*   **Communication & Content:** Create texts, emails, and short updates for customer retention, success communication, and to support sales activities.
*   **Collaboration & Initiative:** Close coordination with the two founders, Max and Daniel, contributing your own ideas, and continuously improving our internal processes.

### Qualifications

*   At least **2 years of experience in Performance Ads** (e.g., Meta, Google, LinkedIn, or TikTok) – both in operational campaign management and strategic setup.
*   Ideally, **experience in an agency** or as a **freelancer** – you understand the dynamics, responsibility, and initiative that this entails.
*   **Hands-on mentality** – you tackle tasks instead of just analyzing, and find pragmatic solutions.
*   **Growth Mindset & Self-motivation:** You want to see results, take responsibility, and build something.
*   Confident, approachable demeanor in customer communication – both written and in video calls.
*   Fluent German and good English, both written and spoken.
