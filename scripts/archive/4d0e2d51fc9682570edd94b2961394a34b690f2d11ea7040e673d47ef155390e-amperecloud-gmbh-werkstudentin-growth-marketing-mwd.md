### Description

Are you looking to shape the future of renewable energies? At Amperecloud, your ideas count! We're developing the operating system for renewable energies, making the management of related facilities easier with the Amperecloud Platform – for a world where green energy secures growth.

As a Working Student in Growth Marketing with an analytical focus, you will work at the intersection of marketing, data analysis, and sales. Together with our Business Development team, you'll develop data-driven strategies to measurably drive growth. You'll quickly take on responsibility, work on real growth levers, and see the immediate impact of your work on team decisions. This is a part-time working student contract (at least 20 hours/week) based in Berlin, with a hybrid work model.

### Responsibilities

*   **Campaign Management:** You'll support the planning and implementation of multichannel campaigns – from social media to performance ads – and work with the team to develop data-driven measures to target potential customers along the funnel and convert them into clients.
*   **Conversion Optimization:** You'll assist in the conception and optimization of landing pages, ads, and customer journeys to continuously improve the conversion rate.
*   **Growth & Testing:** You'll contribute your own creative approaches to identify new growth potentials and continuously test and optimize marketing measures.
*   **Data Analysis:** You'll analyze marketing data such as CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost), Conversion Rates, and CLV (Customer Lifetime Value), deriving concrete recommendations for future actions.
*   **Visualization & Reporting:** You'll prepare analyzed data clearly and visualize results in dashboards – either directly in the CRM system or using tools like Power BI – to make key metrics transparent for the team.
*   **CRM & Data Quality:** You'll ensure high data quality within the CRM system, maintain existing datasets, and support the further development of data-based processes.
*   **Cross-functional Collaboration:** You'll work closely with the Sales and Product teams to link marketing and sales data and derive joint insights.

### Qualifications

*   **Education:** You are an enrolled student in Business, Marketing, Data Science, or a related field and eager to understand how systematic growth works in the B2B sector.
*   **Experience:** You have initial practical experience in digital marketing or a growth environment – ideally in a SaaS or start-up context. Experience with CRM systems, preferably HubSpot, is a plus. Knowledge of Power BI or comparable analytical tools is desirable but not a prerequisite.
*   **Work Style:** You enjoy working with data, think analytically, and approach tasks in a structured and results-oriented manner.
*   **Focus/Interests:** You are interested in performance channels like LinkedIn, Google Ads, SEO, or email marketing and want to understand how their interplay enables growth.
*   **Mindset:** You are motivated, eager to learn, and want to actively contribute to the energy transition. As a team player, you communicate clearly, bring your own ideas, and take responsibility to achieve measurable progress together with the team.
*   **Language:** You communicate confidently in German and English; Spanish skills are a plus.
