### Description\nThis role is central to our financial operations, focusing on the meticulous processing of incoming invoices and the creation of accident damage claims. You will be the primary contact for billing inquiries, ensuring accuracy and timely resolutions, while also contributing to process optimization within a supportive, flexible, and dynamic team environment. We encourage initiative and offer significant scope for personal and professional development.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Systematic processing of all incoming invoices from partners and suppliers, including necessary reclamations.\n- Creating invoices to accident damages (e.g., repair, rental car, and expert costs) for commercial key accounts.\n- Maintaining an overview of all processes and handled cases using our status and task system.\n- Ensuring the dispatch of all necessary documents for claims settlement to customers, insurances, lawyers, and leasing companies.\n- Supporting the pre-posting of incoming invoices as needed.\n- Recording necessary receivables and provisions for period-based revenue and cost of goods sold accrual.\n- Acting as the first point of contact for customer billing questions and requesting missing invoices and documents.\n- Supporting the optimization of our billing processes.\n- Creating credit notes and invoice corrections.\n- Serving as the interface to our accounting department, e.g., for clarifying queries.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Demonstrably organized, transparent, and structured approach to work.\n- Prudent, reliable, and able to maintain an overview even in stressful situations.\n- A strong commitment to the quality of your work.\n- Possession of a commercial education, ideally with experience in accident damage billing.\n- Ability to independently form a complete picture of situations to arrive at a balanced assessment.\n- Capable of coordinating necessary decisions with team colleagues or supervisors when required.\n- Good numerical comprehension and logical understanding of relationships.\n- Excellent German language skills, both written and spoken.\n- High degree of initiative and independence.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a detail-oriented and proactive individual with a commercial background to play a crucial role in a company's financial operations. The emphasis on mobile work, flexible hours, and a supportive team environment, coupled with opportunities for process optimization and personal development, makes this an attractive proposition. You'll be thoroughly onboarded and empowered to make a real impact, becoming a key interface for customers and internal accounting.
