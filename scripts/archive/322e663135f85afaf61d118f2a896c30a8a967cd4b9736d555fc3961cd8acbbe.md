**Job Title:** Data & AI Business Partner (SM&C)
**Sector**: Strategic Affairs
**Department**: Data & AI Department
**Section:** Compliance & Quality Monitoring
**Job Purpose**
The Data & AI Business Partner (SM&C) acts as a strategic liaison between the Data & AI Department and the Strategic Marketing & Communications (SM&C) Sector. The role ensures the effective integration of data-driven insights, advanced analytics, and AI solutions to enhance marketing strategies, optimize communications effectiveness, and strengthen Abu Dhabi’s positioning as a global cultural and tourism hub. By understanding SM&C’s specific needs and translating them into actionable data and AI initiatives, the role drives innovation, campaign effectiveness, audience engagement, and evidence-based decision-making across all marketing and communications programs.
**Key Responsibilities**
**Strategic Partnership:**
*   Serve as the primary point of contact between the Data & AI Department and the SM&C Sector.
*   Align SM&C priorities with enterprise-level data and AI strategies.
*   Identify opportunities for leveraging data and AI to improve marketing reach, brand awareness, and communications impact.
**Data & AI Enablement:**
*   Translate business requirements into AI and analytics solutions in collaboration with Data Science and Analytics teams.
*   Support the design, development, and delivery of sector-specific dashboards, campaign performance models, and audience insights reports.
*   Promote adherence to data governance, quality, and responsible AI practices across the SM&C Sector.
**Advisory & Engagement:**
*   Provide strategic advice to SM&C leadership on applying AI and analytics to measure campaign ROI, audience segmentation, media performance, and sentiment analysis.
*   Build strong partnerships with internal and external communications stakeholders to embed data-driven decision-making.
*   Act as a change agent, fostering adoption of AI and analytics within marketing and communications functions.
**Performance Monitoring & Reporting:**
*   Monitor and report on the performance of SM&C-specific data and AI initiatives.
*   Ensure timely submission of strategic reports and KPIs in line with enterprise reporting systems (e.g., ADPM, ECAS, ADEO).
*   Provide insights and recommendations to support proactive leadership decision-making.
**Qualifications & Experience**
*   Bachelor’s degree (master’s degree preferred) in Data Science, Computer Science, Business Analytics, Artificial Intelligence, Marketing Analytics, or a related field.
*   5 to 7 years’ relevant experience in data analytics, AI, or business partnering, ideally within government, marketing, communications, or media-related sectors.
*   Demonstrated ability to translate business requirements into effective data/AI solutions.
*   Proven track record in stakeholder management and cross-functional collaboration.
*   Strong project management experience with the ability to deliver impactful solutions.
*   In-depth knowledge of AI/ML applications, analytics, and data governance.
*   Experience in developing reports and dashboards to support strategic decision-making.
*   Familiarity with marketing KPIs (campaign reach, ROI, engagement, sentiment) and international benchmarks is an advantage.
**Skills**:
*   Excellent relationship management skills, with a strong ability to influence and partner with senior leaders and business stakeholders.
*    Ability to collaborate effectively across teams and work in a matrix environment.
*   A data-driven mindset with the ability to analyze complex data and translate insights into actionable business strategies.
*   Proven ability to think strategically and develop long-term solutions.
*   Proactive: Able to identify opportunities and act on them without constant guidance.
*   Strategic Visionary: Thinks beyond the immediate project to identify long-term goals and challenges.
*   Business-oriented: Always focused on how data and technology can drive business success.
*   Strong Communicator: Able to clearly articulate complex concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences.
