**AI Security Architect, Security - Seattle**

**Why We Have This Role**

We create software that the world’s best brands use to deliver exceptional frontline experiences, build high-performing teams, and design products people love. Serving over 20,000 clients globally, we are more than a platform—we are the creators and stewards of the Experience Management category. This AI Security Architect role is essential to designing, building, and maintaining robust security architectures for our Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) systems and their underlying platforms. Your work will proactively identify and mitigate unique security risks associated with the AI/ML lifecycle, including model integrity, data poisoning, adversarial attacks, and system robustness.

**How You’ll Find Success**

*   Takes initiative, understanding the expected outcome and context to work entrepreneurially to achieve goals.
*   Highly communicative and influences effectively, building trust at the team level and across stakeholders.
*   Demonstrates strong analytical skills to proactively identify and mitigate complex AI/ML security risks.
*   Possesses a continuous learning mindset, staying current with rapidly evolving AI/ML technologies and security threats.
*   Collaborates effectively with diverse technical and non-technical teams, fostering a security-conscious culture.
*   Exhibits a passion for solving challenging problems and driving innovative security solutions for AI/ML systems.

**How You’ll Grow**

*   Lead cross-functional security initiatives for cutting-edge AI/ML projects, expanding your influence and expertise across the organization.
*   Mentor junior security engineers and contribute to the development of Qualtrics' overall AI security strategy and best practices.
*   Engage with industry leaders and participate in conferences, contributing to the broader AI security community and bringing back valuable insights.
*   Drive the adoption of new security technologies and methodologies, continuously evolving our defenses against emerging AI threats.

**Things You’ll Do**

*   Design and architect end-to-end security for the entire ML/AI pipeline, developing reference architectures for various deployment patterns, and ensuring compliance
*   Perform threat modeling for AI/ML systems, identifying vulnerabilities in data poisoning, model evasion, extraction, and integrity; define and implement data privacy, DLP, and encryption standards for sensitive training and inference data; and collaborate with Data Scientists and ML Engineers to integrate security-by-design principles from initial concept.
*   Implement and monitor technical controls for model integrity, governance, and AI security, including access control and bias detection.
*   Ensure AI systems comply with industry standards, privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), and internal Responsible AI policies, working with Legal and Compliance to document and validate technical adherence to AI governance frameworks.

**What We’re Looking For On Your Resume** 

*   Minimum BA/BS degree. Advanced degree (Masters, PhD) in Computer Science, Data Science, Cyber Security, or a related quantitative field is preferred. 
*   Minimum of 10 years in Information Security, with at least 4 years focused on securing AI/ML systems.
*   Deep understanding of the ML/AI lifecycle and its associated security risks.
*   Expertise in cloud security platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP) and services related to AI/ML (e.g., SageMaker, Vertex AI).
*   Strong knowledge of data security principles, including encryption, masking, and tokenization.
*   Deep understanding of AI specific threats, including adversarial ML, data poisoning, prompt injection and model inversion and inference attacks and associated defensive measures 
*   Strong knowledge of application security architecture best practices and architecture patterns 
*   Relevant security certifications such as CISSP, CSSLP, or specialized AI security certifications are preferred.
*   Knowledge of AI related security related frameworks and standards globally  
    Excellent communication skills, with the ability to translate complex security concepts into clear requirements for both technical and non-technical audiences.
*   Proven ability to work collaboratively across diverse teams (Data Science, Engineering, Product, Legal).
*   Strong problem-solving skills and a proactive approach to emerging AI security challenges.

**What You Should Know About This Team**

*   The Security Architecture team is a collaborative, high-performing group dedicated to protecting Qualtrics and its customers through proactive risk management and compliance.
*   We work closely with legal, security, product, and customer success teams, as well as external auditors and partners.
*   The team embraces strategic risk-taking and continuous learning.
*   You will be positioned as a key enabler of business success through security assurance and compliance excellence.
