**Job Description:**

**Your Career**

You will have the opportunity to build a career solving network security challenges leveraging advanced machine learning techniques on exabytes of data. You will help create and deliver the most advanced AI and Machine Learning enabled solutions to detect and remediate network issues, discover threats, provide intelligence and protect devices.

You have

*   Deep technical experts and thought leaders that help accelerate adoption of the very best engineering practices, while maintaining knowledge on industry innovations, trends and practices
*   Visionaries who deliver on critical business needs and are recognized across the company as go-to engineering resources on given domains
*   Role models and mentors who exemplify the best of Palo alto Networks culture - They do the right thing even when it’s hard, treat challenges as a chance to learn and provide honest opinions so the team can improve
*   Leaders who can communicate cogently with hands-on engineers as well as executives

**Qualifications:**

**Your Experience** 

*   M.S. or Ph.D degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics or related field or equivalent military experience required
*   10+ years industry experience in Machine Learning techniques and data analytics
*   10+ experience in design, algorithms and data structures - Expertise with one or more of the following languages is must - Java, Python, Golang
*   Proven leadership skills with an ability to deliver under tight deadlines
*   Proven ability to collaborate with many cross-functional teams including Product Management and Design teams with an emphasis on end-to-end delivery
*   Proven track record in solving difficult real world problems by applying Machine Learning techniques
*   Experience with deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch and TensorFlow, NLP, Large Language Models, Generative AI, Langchain, Transformer models or related areas is a big plus
*   Experience with LLaMA is a big plus
*   Thorough understanding of ML Ops best practices, ability to design, test, measure algorithms/models and orchestrate - Experience working with Google Vertex AI platform will be a plus
*   Excellent communication skills with the ability to influence at all levels of the organization
*   A self driven individual contributor and an excellent team player
