**About the role:**

This is a fantastic opportunity to join our dynamic Data Capture team at Ipsos on a 12-month fixed-term contract. In this role, you will be at the heart of our operations, transforming open-ended responses into structured data that uncovers deep consumer insights and sentiments. You'll work on a variety of projects, from fast-paced, quick-turnaround tasks that require efficiency and adaptability, to detailed tracking studies and complex public consultations that influence significant policies and laws. Your day-to-day will involve engaging with research teams, managing projects from start to finish, creating code frames, and ensuring the highest data integrity through quality checks. This position offers a unique chance to enhance your project management skills and collaborate with diverse teams across our organization, all while contributing to our mission of providing a total understanding of society, markets, and people.
