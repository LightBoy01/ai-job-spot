### Description
We are a diverse, ambitious team based in the heart of Berlin, passionate about data privacy, generative AI, and innovative startups. We are seeking an experienced Software Engineer to join us in changing the world of video data. This role is crucial for enhancing our real-time image-processing pipelines, primarily utilizing GStreamer and Nvidia DeepStream technologies. You will develop performance-critical features at the core of our visual computing applications, collaborating closely with product and Deep Learning teams to design scalable and adaptive architectures. We value experimentation, continuous improvement through practices like pair programming and code reviews, and a culture of flat hierarchies with a positive feedback loop. If you're looking for a challenging position with real impact from day one within a collaborative and growth-oriented environment, this is the opportunity for you.

### Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and maintain robust software solutions based on deep learning models.
- Work closely with business and engineering teams to iteratively deliver high-quality solutions.
- Embrace experimentation, iteration, and adaptability, quickly validating ideas and shipping high-performance prototypes.
- Continuously explore emerging technologies and innovative ways to optimize speed and intelligence.
- Contribute to a culture of continuous improvement by actively participating in pair programming, code reviews, and retrospectives, ensuring scalable, production-grade visual computing infrastructure.

### Qualifications
**Required:**
- At least 3 years of professional experience as a full-time Software Engineer.
- Strong proficiency in C++ and Python.
- Solid understanding of software design and architecture principles.
- Experience in image processing technologies (e.g., OpenCV).

**Desirable:**
- Interest or prior exposure to multimedia processing frameworks, specifically GStreamer or Nvidia DeepStream.
- Practical experience with Docker.
- Experience with the Meson build system.
- CUDA knowledge.
- Familiarity with Jenkins or other CI/CD systems.
