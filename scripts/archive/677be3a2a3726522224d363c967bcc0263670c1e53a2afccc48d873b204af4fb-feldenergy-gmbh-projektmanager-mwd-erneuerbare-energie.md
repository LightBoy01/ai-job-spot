### Description\nfeld.energy is more than just a company – we are a movement! We help farmers and landowners cleverly combine agriculture and energy production. With our innovative Agri-Photovoltaic solutions, we create dual land use – for food production and clean energy. This way, we shape the energy transition in rural areas – sustainably, economically, and future-oriented.\n\nAs a Project Manager (m/w/d), you take responsibility for projects at the intersection of renewable energy, agriculture, and construction law. You accompany our Agri-PV projects from the initial idea to approval or turnkey handover – with a clear goal: to make sustainable energy solutions a reality.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- As Project Manager (m/w/d), you take responsibility for our Agri-Photovoltaic projects – from the initial idea to approval or turnkey handover.\n- Planning, control, and prioritization of project milestones are part of your daily routine – you always keep budgets, timings, and goals in sight.\n- In exchange with municipalities, authorities, farmers, and external partners, you advance projects diplomatically and solution-oriented.\n- You accompany construction law procedures and ensure that all requirements are implemented transparently and on schedule.\n- Furthermore, you develop strategies for acceptance and participation in communities and the public.\n- You evaluate the potential for dual use of agricultural land – for example, for animal husbandry or arable farming under PV modules – and integrate these into project planning.\n- You efficiently manage interdisciplinary teams of planners, experts, lawyers, and agronomists – with clear communication and a focus on results.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed studies or training in civil engineering, environmental technology, agricultural sciences, renewable energies, or comparable fields.\n- Initial experience in project development, land-use planning, and/or the photovoltaic industry.\n- Good knowledge of regulatory and approval processes is desirable.\n- Independent, structured, and solution-oriented way of working.\n- Strong communication skills and diplomatic aptitude in dealing with various stakeholders.\n- At least business-fluent German language skills are essential.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at feld.energy offers a unique opportunity to drive the energy transition through innovative Agri-Photovoltaik solutions. The company fosters a collaborative, sustainable, and growth-oriented culture with excellent development opportunities, regular team events, and a focus on work-life balance (27 days off). It's an ideal position for a proactive Project Manager eager to make a tangible impact at the intersection of renewable energy and agriculture, managing complex projects with diverse stakeholders.
