### Description

Deep Digital is actively seeking a highly skilled and experienced Senior Data Engineer for a remote opportunity based in Spain. The ideal candidate will possess a proven track record in the successful implementation, optimization, and maintenance of critical data integration and historian systems. This pivotal role requires an individual with in-depth knowledge of industrial processes, data historian best practices, and a strong capability to lead and mentor junior team members. You will be instrumental in advanced technical responsibilities and collaborate extensively with cross-functional teams to ensure the seamless operation and integrity of our data systems.

### Responsibilities

*   Lead the design, implementation, and optimization of robust data historian solutions.
*   Drive efforts to integrate historian systems with diverse control systems and third-party applications.
*   Conduct comprehensive performance analysis and implement strategic improvements for continuous enhancement.
*   Design and configure complex data tags to precisely meet project-specific requirements.
*   Develop custom interfaces and connectors to significantly enhance data integration capabilities.
*   Develop and implement advanced security measures to safeguard historical data integrity.
*   Ensure stringent compliance with industry standards and regulations, taking a lead role in audits and assessments.
*   Investigate and resolve complex issues pertaining to data collection, storage, and retrieval.
*   Provide crucial technical leadership and mentorship to junior engineers within the team.
*   Maintain comprehensive documentation for all configurations, installations, and troubleshooting procedures.
*   Comply with Deep Digital's Quality Management System requirements, performing work according to process documents and maintaining accurate records.

### Qualifications

*   Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
*   Between 3 and 5 years of hands-on experience in the design, implementation, and leadership of projects involving systems such as AVEVA PI, AspenTech IP.21, AspenTech Inmation, Inductive Automation Ignition, and PTC Kepware.
*   In-depth knowledge of data integration and historian system architecture and components.
*   Strong understanding of industrial processes and automation systems.
*   Expertise with data contextualization, visualization, and reporting platforms.
*   Proficiency in scripting languages (e.g., PowerShell) for automation tasks.
*   Previous experience in mentoring and leading a technical team.
*   Excellent communication skills.

### Author's Take

*Here at Deep Digital, being a Senior Data Engineer isn't just about lines of code or system architecture; it's about being a foundational pillar for our client's operational intelligence. Your strategic impact comes from two key areas: first, ensuring the absolute reliability and scalability of our data historian systems, which are the heartbeat of our industrial clients' decision-making. Secondly, and perhaps more profoundly, it's about shaping our future technical leadership. Mentoring junior engineers isn't just a task; it's an investment in our collective expertise and a chance for you to cultivate the next generation of data engineering talent. You'll directly influence how we approach complex data challenges and elevate our overall technical game, ultimately translating into tangible business value for our clients and the growth of our team.*

*That's a fantastic question, and one we prioritize deeply. Even though we're remote-first within Spain, we've cultivated a vibrant and highly collaborative culture. We leverage best-in-class communication tools and practices, ensuring daily stand-ups, regular syncs, and dedicated 'deep dive' sessions keep everyone connected and aligned. Professional development is baked into our DNA; we actively encourage certifications, conferences, and internal knowledge-sharing workshops, understanding that staying at the forefront of technology benefits everyone.
