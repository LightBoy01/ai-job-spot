**Requisition ID:**  90184 

NextEra Analytics offers energy consulting services using industry-leading scientific analysis for planning, siting, forecasting and optimizing all forms of energy projects. Our optimization and analytics platforms integrate open-source technologies to leverage massive, diverse sets of utility operating data. This enables rapid development of operational solutions. Applying expertise in advanced mathematics, data and physical sciences, we solve some of the hardest problems facing the energy industry.  
 

**Position Specific Description**

We are seeking a Product Manager to lead high-impact AI Energy product initiatives. In this role, you will set the product direction and strategy, oversee roadmaps for products, and drive innovation. The Product Manager will play a pivotal role in delivering NextEra Analytics' product vision, acting as the interface between the business and development teams, to deliver AI driven products that define the future of renewable energy. 

Job Overview
------------

Employees in this role will be responsible for defining and executing the product vision, strategy, and roadmap for critical products. You will oversee the entire product lifecycle, from ideation to launch and continuous improvement, ensuring alignment with business objectives and market needs.

Job Duties & Responsibilities
-----------------------------

*   Formulate and communicate a compelling product vision and strategy that aligns with NextEra’s goals and market opportunities
*   Develop and maintain a comprehensive product roadmap, ensuring alignment with business priorities and stakeholder expectations
*   Collaborate closely with engineering, data science, marketing, and other teams to drive product initiatives and deliver best-in-class solutions
*   Engage with customers, partners, and stakeholders to understand their needs, gather feedback, and ensure the product meets and exceeds their expectations
*   Lead Agile development processes, including sprint planning, backlog prioritization, and managing cross-functional teams to ensure timely and high-quality product deliveries
*   Define and track key performance indicators to measure product success and drive continuous improvement
*   Conduct market research and competitive analysis to identify trends, opportunities, and potential risks related to product development
*   Provide regular updates to leaders and stakeholders on product progress, key achievements, and strategic direction

Performs other job-related duties as assigned

*   Experience: 5+ years
