Pax8 is looking for an **Operations Data Analyst** to join our growing AMER Operations team and help shape the future of data-driven decision-making.
### 💡 Why This Role Matters
As the **Operations Data Analyst**, you’ll be the first hire supporting a high-volume, high-impact analytics function. You will be helping uncover insights across five business units. Your work will directly influence strategic decisions, optimize processes, and unlock growth opportunities.
This is a remote-friendly role with a supportive manager who empowers team members to lead and take ownership. You’ll be joining a small but mighty team, collaborating with stakeholders.
* * *
### 🧠 What You’ll Do
*   **Build Data Products & Monitor Performance (30%)**
*   Create dashboards, reports, and semantic models in Power BI to track KPIs and OKRs.
*   Ensure timely refreshes and quality checks.
*   **Optimize Processes & Solve Problems (30%)**
*   Deliver data-backed improvements and root-cause analyses using SQL, Redshift, and AI tools.
*   Work from a prioritized backlog set by the AMER BI Manager.
*   **Collect & Analyze Data (20%)**
*   Gather data from multiple sources and conduct descriptive analysis to answer stakeholder questions.
*   **Collaborate & Strategize (15%)**
*   Translate business questions into clear analytical requirements.
*   Demo data products and capture feedback for continuous improvement.
*   **Ensure Data Integrity (5%)**
*   Apply validation and QA checks.
*   Maintain documentation and adopt best practices for modeling and AI-assisted workflows.
* * *
### 🧰 Tools You’ll Use
*   **SQL, Redshift, Power BI**
*   **AI tools** for querying, documentation, and testing
*   **ERP systems** like NetSuite
* * *
### 🎯 What Success Looks Like
*   **30 Days**: Learn the data structure, modeling approach, and tools.
*   **90 Days**: Begin building and delivering with support.
*   **6 Months**: Operate independently, tackle complex data problems, and collaborate with data engineering.
* * *
### 🌱 Growth & Development
You’ll be the first person in this new team, with opportunities to grow into a senior analytics role. From onboarding to ongoing learning, you’ll have access to development resources and mentorship to accelerate your career.
