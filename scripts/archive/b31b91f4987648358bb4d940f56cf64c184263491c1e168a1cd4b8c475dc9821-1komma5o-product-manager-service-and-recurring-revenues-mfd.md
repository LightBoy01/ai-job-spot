1KOMMA5°
--------

We are looking for you to join our tech team in Berlin, Munich, or Hamburg. 1KOMMA5° is creating Germany's largest one-stop-shop for the sale, installation, and servicing of solar panels, heat pumps, electricity solutions, and charging infrastructure. And the best part? They are all interconnected!

Become a part of our mission and learn about our Product & Tech team!

Deine Position
--------------

You will own the development and scaling of our next-generation service offerings — transforming one-time energy installations into long-term customer relationships and sustainable revenue streams. You’ll design service products that connect hardware, software, and energy solutions into a seamless customer experience, driving profitability and retention across our global markets.

This role sits at the intersection of **Product, Operations, and Commercial**, with the mission to build scalable, profitable, and innovative services that extend the lifetime value of every customer in our ecosystem.

Your Responsibilities:
----------------------

*   **Define Product Vision and Strategy:** Shape the roadmap for our global **service and recurring-revenue portfolio**, including warranty, maintenance, monitoring, and digital service products. Align business models with customer value and operational efficiency.
*   **Drive Product Lifecycle:** Lead the end-to-end product process — from market research and concept validation to rollout, optimization, and scaling across regions.
*   **Enable Recurring Revenue Models:** Develop offerings and pricing structures that create sustainable, predictable revenue. Ensure operational executability, profitability, and a strong customer experience.
*   **Cross-functional Collaboration:** Work closely with **Engineering, Market Operations, and Commercial teams** to translate insights and operational feedback into scalable product solutions.
*   **Customer and Market Focus:** Leverage customer feedback, competitive analysis, and market trends to continuously refine and expand our service offerings.
*   **Operational Excellence:** Define and monitor KPIs to measure adoption, margin performance, and service quality. Identify levers to improve efficiency, cost structure, and customer satisfaction.
*   **Innovation and Ownership:** Take ownership for building our **service-driven ecosystem** and push boundaries to make 1KOMMA5° a market leader in integrated energy solutions.

Dein Profil
-----------

*   Degree in **Business Administration, Industrial Engineering, Energy Management**, or a related field
*   **2+ years of experience** in **Product Management** or **Business Development**, ideally in the **energy, technology, or SaaS sector**
*   Strong understanding of **recurring revenue models**, pricing mechanics, and customer value creation
*   Proven track record of turning **customer needs and operational insights** into scalable product solutions
*   Excellent **communication and stakeholder management** skills across functions and hierarchies
*   Entrepreneurial, structured, and **execution-driven mindset** with a passion for ownership
*   Fluent in **English and German**
