### Description
Our client is a well-established German company, a leading partner and component supplier to the manufacturing industry, boasting triple-digit employee numbers and over 100 million Euros in annual revenue. Headquartered in North Rhine-Westphalia with excellent transport links, the company benefits from a very strong market position, recent strategic acquisitions, and a broad product portfolio, all signaling robust future growth. This expansion necessitates a strategic HR approach, which this central role is designed to deliver.

Based in the greater Dortmund area, this versatile position is pivotal in shaping the ongoing development of the company and its employees, with the ultimate goal of becoming an "Employer of Choice." The role embraces a modern hybrid working model, requiring approximately 30% office presence.

### Responsibilities
*   **Strategic HR Development & Talent Promotion**
    *   Identify and develop High Potentials and Critical Talents across the global organization.
    *   Conceive and implement targeted development programs for leaders and technical specialists.
    *   Build and maintain a global Learning & Development Roadmap aligned with the corporate strategy.
*   **Global Information Management & Knowledge Organization**
    *   Further develop and implement concepts for the digitalization of knowledge management.
    *   Introduce and support AI-powered tools to enhance learning and development processes.
    *   Foster a learning organization through the establishment of robust knowledge-sharing structures.
*   **Process Optimization & Global Integration**
    *   Support the integration within the Norres Baggerman Group through exchange programs, learning platforms, and global initiatives.
    *   Analyze existing processes with a focus on efficiency, transparency, and digital usability.
    *   Provide operational support for a specific employee group and assist with back-office tasks.

### Qualifications
*   A successfully completed university degree in Human Resources, Business Psychology, or a comparable field.
*   Several years of professional experience in Learning & Development, Talent Management, or Organizational Development, preferably from comparable international companies.
*   Demonstrated strong process understanding and experience in the digitalization of HR processes.
*   A clear affinity for AI-based solutions and modern digital learning platforms.
*   Proven experience working within agile, dynamic organizational environments.
*   Excellent project management capabilities.
*   Exceptional communication skills in both German and English.
*   Strong empathy, intercultural competence, and robust communication abilities.
*   A passion for shaping an international environment and actively driving change.
*   High degree of independence, self-motivation, and proactivity.
*   Strong identification with your area of responsibility.
