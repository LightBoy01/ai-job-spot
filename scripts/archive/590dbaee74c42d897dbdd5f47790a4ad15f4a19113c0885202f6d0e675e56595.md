Chiswick Park, London | Hybrid working

Lead with purpose. Protect what matters. Shape the future.

At Starbucks, we’re more than coffee. We’re a global community built on trust, quality and connection. We’re looking for a strategic leader to drive food safety and quality across our 43 EMEA markets — someone who can balance innovation with compliance, and lead with both heart and expertise.  

**What you’ll do:**

*   Set the vision for food safety and quality across a diverse region made up of both company owned and licensed stores.
*   Have a global reporting line with peers across the globe and a seat at the extended leadership table within the local region.  
*   Lead a high-performing team and collaborate across global functions.
*   Ensure our products meet the highest standards — from supplier to store.
*   Influence industry standards and regulatory frameworks.
*   Drive innovation in supply chain and product development.

**What you’ll bring:**

*   Deep expertise in food safety, quality assurance and regulatory affairs.
*   Proven leadership across multi-site or international operations.
*   Strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
*   A proactive, agile mindset with a passion for excellence.

**Why Starbucks?**

*   Flexible hybrid working and a culture of belonging.
*   Opportunities to grow, lead and make a lasting impact.
*   A brand that puts people first — from our partners to our customers.
