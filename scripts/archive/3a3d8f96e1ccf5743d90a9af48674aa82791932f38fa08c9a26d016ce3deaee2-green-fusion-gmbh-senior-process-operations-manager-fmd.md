### Description\nGreen Fusion is at the forefront of digitalizing the energy transition, developing software that optimizes heating systems in the real estate sector. Our mission is clear: to combat climate change by reducing emissions and energy consumption through advanced digitalization and automation.\n\nAs a (Senior) Process Operations Manager in Engineering, you will be pivotal in driving this mission. You will own the entire end-to-end building optimization process, ensuring clarity, speed, and predictability within this critical cross-functional workflow as we continue to scale our impact and accelerate the energy transition.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Own and optimize the end-to-end optimization funnel, covering remote and on-site workflows, including readiness, prioritization, execution, and verification.\n*   Maintain the definitive source of truth for optimization processes and lead weekly funnel reviews, sequencing, and progress tracking.\n*   Manage approvals, exceptions, and cross-team escalations, ensuring clearly defined SLAs and timely communication.\n*   Publish and communicate weekly KPIs, such as throughput, approval times, data completeness, and exceptions, providing transparency across the organization.\n*   Support annual, quarterly, and weekly planning by generating data-driven forecasts for optimization throughput.\n*   Partner closely with Sales and Customer Success to ensure seamless onboarding of new deals into the optimization funnel, validating readiness, controller coverage, and constraints.\n*   Collaborate with System Engineering and Product teams to assess technical feasibility, resolve blockers, define next steps, and identify opportunities for process improvement or automation, while maintaining operational ownership.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   4–6+ years of proven experience in operations, delivery management, or process ownership, ideally within a tech, SaaS, or IoT environment.\n*   Exceptional ability to own and manage complex cross-functional processes end-to-end, from onboarding through planning, execution, and verification.\n*   Strong analytical skills, comfortable working with data, including funnel KPIs, dashboards, and reporting to drive informed decisions.\n*   Effective communicator, capable of translating information seamlessly between Product, Engineering, Customer Success, Sales, and Operations teams.\n*   Demonstrated ability to independently prioritize, sequence work, and make thoughtful trade-off decisions in a dynamic environment.\n*   Adept at navigating change and bringing clear structure to complex work, maintaining effectiveness during high-volume periods and acting with urgency when required.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a truly impactful role for an experienced Process Operations Manager passionate about driving real change in the energy sector. Green Fusion offers a unique opportunity to directly contribute to combating climate change while working within a flexible, growth-oriented, and highly collaborative environment. With strong emphasis on continuous learning, team well-being, and a clear vision for market leadership, this position is ideal for someone who thrives on owning complex processes, leveraging data, and working with a motivated team to scale a solution that genuinely makes a difference. If you're looking to combine your operational expertise with a powerful mission, this is an outstanding opportunity to shape the future of energy transition.
