**In one sentence**
-------------------

We are seeking an experienced AI/ML Engineer with knowledge in generative/agentic AI to grow our AI-powered automation projects, including a scalable LLM backend, data orchestration platform, and production-grade ML models—all while coordinating with our team of AI/ML specialists.

**What will your job look like?**
---------------------------------

•    Aid in the progress of our AI-driven automation tools, integrating LLM and expanding our data ingestion platform.   
•    Deploy, fine-tune and benchmark foundational LLM models for production, focusing on accuracy, scalability, and efficiency.   
•    Build RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines and focus on prompt engineering strategies to boost model relevance and adaptability.   
•    Design and build LLM-based agents with function-calling tools for specific, task-oriented AI solutions.   
•    Use LLM APIs (like OpenAI, Anthropic) for language tasks, frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex) for rapid development, and platforms (Langfuse) for real-time monitoring.  
•    Collaborate with cross-functional teams to align AI/ML applications with business goals, also providing technical insights to non-technical stakeholders.   
•    Manage data preprocessing, feature engineering, and model selection using large datasets and distributed computing.   
•    Follow MLOps best practices (CI/CD, versioning, containerization with Docker) to ensure streamlined operations and scalable model serving.   
•    Stay updated on AI/ML trends and technologies to drive continuous innovation. 

**All you need is...**
----------------------

Must-haves:

  
• A Bachelor’s or Master’s in Computer Science, Machine Learning, or a related field, with specialized AI/ML training.  
• 2-4 years of hands-on work with Large Language Models (LLMs), including model development, fine-tuning, and deployment.  
• Strong skills in prompt engineering and LLM agent development, optimizing prompts and building intelligent agents for complex tasks and external tool interactions.  
• Experience building RAG pipelines and integrating tools to improve retrieval, indexing, and context understanding.  
• Advanced proficiency in Python and key AI/ML libraries for model building and automation. Excellent English communication skills, with the ability to translate tech into plain language.  
• A team-player mindset, proactive problem-solving skills, and an eagerness to innovate in a fast-paced, cross-functional environment.

Good to Have:

  
• Experience with both hosted LLM APIs (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic) and open-source models (e.g., LLaMA, Mistral, Qwen), as well as frameworks like LangChain and LlamaIndex.  
• Familiarity with monitoring platforms like Langfuse for real-time observability.  
• Knowledge of containerization (Docker) and orchestration (Kubernetes) for building scalable ML/LLM services.  
• Hands-on experience with advanced RAG techniques, data indexing, and embedding models.  
• Background in AI-driven automation and information extraction for building data-driven applications. 

**Why you will love this job:**
-------------------------------

• The chance to serve as a specialist in software and technology.  
• You will take an active role in technical mentoring within the team.  
• We offer a wide range of stellar benefits, including health and life insurance as well as paid time off, sick time, and parental leave!
