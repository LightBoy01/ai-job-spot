**About the Role**  
We are seeking a **Senior Applied AI Engineer** to design, deploy, and optimize production-grade AI systems powering [Sully.ai](http://Sully.ai)’s real-time voice and medical agent technologies. This role is deeply technical and end-to-end — from data to model to service — and will help us build reliable, low-latency ML infrastructure integrated directly into healthcare workflows.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Design, deploy, and maintain end-to-end AI/ML systems (data → model → inference → service).
    
*   Build robust evaluation, monitoring, and feedback pipelines for production ML.
    
*   Integrate LLMs, retrieval systems, and semantic/voice pipelines into customer-facing products.
    
*   Collaborate cross-functionally with Product and Infra to translate ML trade-offs into product outcomes.
    
*   Drive experimentation speed and model reliability through CI/CD, metrics, and instrumentation.
    

**Hard Requirements**

*   Demonstrable track record deploying AI/ML models in production.
    
*   Strong software foundation (TypeScript, APIs, distributed systems).
    
*   Familiarity with ML frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow, Hugging Face or similar).
    
*   Experience building inference, evaluation, and monitoring pipelines.
    
*   Excellent communication and collaboration skills across technical and non-technical teams.
    

**Nice-to-Have**

*   Experience with LLMs, embeddings, or RAG pipelines in SaaS or startup environments.
    
*   Familiarity with telephony APIs (Twilio/SIP/VoIP) and real-time ASR/NLU pipelines.
    
*   Experience in healthcare, call center, or real-time conversational AI systems.
    

**First-Month Focus**

*   Deploy and optimize a low-latency inference pipeline (&lt;0.5 s target).
    
*   Audit and improve evaluation metrics, drift detection, and guardrail coverage.
    
*   Partner with Product to define success metrics for reliability and responsiveness in voice workflows.
    

**Success OKRs (90 Days)**

*   Deliver production-ready LLM or voice AI systems integrated with Sully’s core stack.
    
*   Establish automated evaluation and monitoring tied to user feedback loops.
    
*   Reduce model latency and error rate by &gt; 30% while maintaining accuracy benchmarks.
    

**Culture Fit**

*   Persistent, driven problem solver.
    
*   Willing to push back on leadership to defend quality/timelines.
    
*   Thrives in high-ambiguity, fast-paced startup environments.
