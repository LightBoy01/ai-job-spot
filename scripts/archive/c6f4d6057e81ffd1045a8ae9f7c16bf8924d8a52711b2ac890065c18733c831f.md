**About the Role**

You.com is building the **AI Search Infrastructure for Enterprises** - the decision backbone that enables companies to reason, decide, and act with the accuracy of their best experts. We’re seeing strong traction with mid-market and enterprise customers across consulting, media, financial services, and tech. To accelerate growth, we’re hiring a **Head of Demand Generation** to own pipeline creation, campaign execution, and revenue marketing.

This is not a role for playbook followers. We need a scrappy, strategic operator who can scale demand gen from the ground up - building a repeatable growth engine that blends **ABM, outbound programs, digital, content, and events**. You’ll partner with Sales, Product Marketing, and Brand to create measurable pipeline impact.

**Responsibilities**

*   Own the demand generation function end-to-end: campaign design, execution, and optimization across all channels.
*   Build **ABM programs** targeting C-level execs (CIO, CISO, Heads of AI/Strategy) in key verticals.
*   Scale digital performance marketing: LinkedIn, Google SERP, programmatic, podcasts, and scrappy experiments.
*   Partner with Sales to align MQL → SQL → Opportunity progression; continuously optimize conversion.
*   Define KPIs, dashboards, and attribution models to measure ROI across campaigns.

**Requirements**

*   8+ years of B2B demand gen experience; proven track record in **enterprise SaaS or AI/infra markets**.
*   Deep experience with **enterprise pipeline marketing, and outbound integration**.
*   Comfortable rolling up sleeves (building campaigns, optimizing ads, writing copy) while scaling a team.
*   Strong partner to Sales, with an obsession for pipeline, CAC, and conversion metrics.
*   Ability to balance **strategic planning and scrappy execution**.
*   Bonus: Experience marketing to technical and consulting audiences.
