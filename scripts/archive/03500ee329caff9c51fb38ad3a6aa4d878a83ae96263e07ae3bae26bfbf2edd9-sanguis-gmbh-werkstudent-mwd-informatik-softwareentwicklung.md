### Description\nSanguis, a dynamic young Health-Tech startup based in Düsseldorf, is revolutionizing the distribution of blood products between clinics. Through digital integration, advanced AI, and modern software architecture, Sanguis is developing a central platform aimed at making medical care safer and more efficient. We are seeking a motivated student developer to join our innovative team and contribute to a product with significant societal impact.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Collaborate on the technical development of the Sanguis platform (Frontend & Backend).\n*   Assist with integrating our platform into hospital systems (e.g., via FHIR or Swisslab/Nexus interfaces).\n*   Build and test data models, APIs, and integrations.\n*   Independently implement smaller development projects, directly influencing product decisions.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's degree program in (Medical) Informatics or a comparable field, ideally in an advanced semester.\n*   Basic knowledge in one or more programming languages (e.g., Python, TypeScript, Node.js, React).\n*   Demonstrated interest in Health-Tech, hospital IT, or AI-driven systems.\n*   Strong initiative, eagerness to learn, and a 'can-do' attitude (Machermentalität).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an ambitious student developer to gain hands-on experience at a fast-growing Health-Tech startup. You'll be working on a product that genuinely impacts society by improving healthcare efficiency. Sanguis offers a flexible and hybrid work environment, allowing you to balance your studies with meaningful work. With significant autonomy and a supportive, young team, you'll have ample room to learn, grow, and make your mark on product development. If you're passionate about technology and healthcare and possess a proactive mindset, Sanguis provides an ideal launching pad for your career.
