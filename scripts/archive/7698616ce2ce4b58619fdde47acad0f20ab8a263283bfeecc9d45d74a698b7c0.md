### **About the Role**

We are seeking a motivated **AI Agent Development Intern** to join our Go-to-Market Department. This role focuses on designing and building intelligent agents that streamline workflows, enhance cross-team collaboration, and improve operational efficiency. The intern will work closely with both technical and business stakeholders to identify bottlenecks, automate repetitive processes, and deliver AI-powered solutions.

### **Key Responsibilities**

*   **Research & Development :** Explore AI agent frameworks, LLMs, and automation tools to identify the best fit for department needs. Prototype, test, and iterate on AI agent solutions.
    
*   **Process Automation :** Analyze existing workflows in the Services Department to pinpoint inefficiencies. Build AI agents to automate repetitive tasks such as data entry, reporting, knowledge retrieval, or customer case triaging.
    
*   **Integration & Implementation :** Connect AI agents with existing enterprise tools, knowledge bases, and communication platforms (e.g., Slack, CRM, ticketing systems).Ensure smooth integration between services and support teams through automation.
    
*   **Documentation & Reporting:** Maintain clear documentation of AI agent design, capabilities, and usage guidelines. Provide regular progress updates to the Services leadership team.
    

### **Required Skills & Qualifications**

*   Currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Data Science, AI/ML, or related field.
    
*   Familiarity with Python and/or JavaScript for building AI/automation solutions.
    
*   Basic understanding of APIs, system integrations, and workflow automation.
    
*   Exposure to **LLMs (e.g., OpenAI, LangChain, Rasa, etc.)** or conversational AI tools.
    
*   Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
    
*   Excellent communication skills and ability to collaborate with cross-functional teams.
    

### **Preferred Skills (Nice-to-Have)**

*   Prior experience with AI agents, chatbots, or automation projects.
    
*   Knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) for deploying AI solutions.
    
*   Familiarity with ticketing systems (ServiceNow, Jira, Zendesk) or CRM platforms (Salesforce, HubSpot).
    
*   Understanding of prompt engineering and fine-tuning AI models.
    

### **What You’ll Gain**

*   Hands-on experience in applying AI to solve real-world business challenges.
    
*   Exposure to enterprise workflows and service operations.
    
*   Mentorship from senior engineers and business leaders.
    
*   Opportunity to contribute to impactful automation initiatives that shape the future of our --Go-to-Market Department.
