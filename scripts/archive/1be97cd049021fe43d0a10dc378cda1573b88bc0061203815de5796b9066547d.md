We are seeking a dynamic and visionary leader to join our team as **Sr. Director / VP of Data Strategy and ML/AI**. This role is responsible for shaping and driving our enterprise-wide data strategy—leveraging data as a strategic asset to deliver actionable insights, enable innovation through ML/AI, and support scalable, secure, and trustworthy data practices.  
  
This high-impact leadership role requires cross-functional collaboration across Product, Engineering, Operations, Finance, and Compliance. You will be the steward of our data ecosystem, championing a culture of data excellence, insight-led decision making, measurable business outcomes, and embedded ML/AI capabilities.

What that means day to day
--------------------------

**Architecture & Platform Leadership:**  
\- Architect a unified and scalable enterprise data platform supporting structured and unstructured data.  
\- Build a modern Data Lakehouse/Warehouse as a single source of truth.  
\- Implement reporting and dashboarding solutions that empower internal teams and customers.  
\- Ensure platform reliability, observability, and security.  
\- Promote reusable, curated datasets and self-service analytics across teams.  
\- Collaborate with BI, Engineering, and business stakeholders to enhance analytics delivery.  
\- Leverage cloud platforms and modern pipeline tooling (e.g., Snowflake, Databricks, Azure, AWS).  
  
**ML/AI Strategy & Execution:**  
\- Lead the design and deployment of scalable ML/AI capabilities on top of the data infrastructure to enhance our applications and business processes.  
\- Deliver high-impact initiatives such as predictive modeling, advanced analytics, and intelligent automation.  
\- Collaborate with Product and Engineering to embed ML/AI into core application features and workflows.  
\- Establish success metrics and a governance model for AI deployments.  
  
**Data Strategy & Governance:**  
\- Define and execute a modern, enterprise-wide data strategy aligned with business objectives.  
\- Foster a culture of trusted data stewardship, literacy, and transparency.  
\- Establish data governance practices including change control, provenance tracking, and auditability.  
\- Build and evolve a robust data integration, reporting, and analytics platform using a Data Lakehouse, Warehouse, or Data Mart architecture.  
  
**Cross-Functional Collaboration:**  
\- Align data initiatives with Product, Engineering, and Operations roadmaps.  
\- Act as a unifying force across departments to eliminate silos and improve insight delivery.  
\- Lead enterprise reporting initiatives, benchmarking, and data-driven transformation.  
\- Ensure measurable outcomes from all data and analytics programs.

What you’ll need to be successful
---------------------------------

**Required Qualifications:**  
\- Degree educated in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, or related field; Master's/PhD preferred.  
\- 10+ years in data strategy, analytics, or ML/AI leadership roles.  
\- Proven experience with platforms like Snowflake, Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, etc.  
\- Deep expertise in data architecture, pipeline orchestration, and real-time analytics.  
\- Ability to influence executive stakeholders and communicate complex ideas clearly.  
\- Strong leadership and organizational change skills.  
  
**Preferred Qualifications:**  
\- Experience in high-growth or private equity-backed environments.  
\- Familiarity with SaaS, compliance, or workforce management platforms.
