As a Technical Support Engineering Lead, you’ll manage and grow our support engineering team, ensuring our customers get world-class technical assistance and guidance. You’ll balance hands-on problem-solving with leadership responsibilities, driving improvements in processes, tooling, and knowledge-sharing while mentoring and enabling your team.
