### Description\nJoin our dynamic team as a Consultant Trainee, where you'll embark on a journey of significant professional growth within a supportive and entrepreneurial environment. This role offers the unique opportunity to work with demanding private and business clients, contributing actively to their financial success while developing your own expertise across various financial domains. We are looking for motivated individuals eager to make a tangible impact and shape the future of our services.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Individual Client Consulting**: Collaborate with your team to provide comprehensive advice to demanding private and business clients on wealth accumulation, investment strategies, real estate financing, and pension provisions.\n- **Competence Building**: Proactively acquire in-depth technical and sales knowledge, continuously developing your consulting expertise through structured learning and practical application.\n- **Business Development**: Take ownership of projects and actively contribute your innovative ideas to strategic questions, playing a key role in shaping our business direction.\n- **Entrepreneurial Contribution**: Operate with an entrepreneurial mindset within the company, making significant contributions to the development of new concepts and optimization of existing processes.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You have successfully completed a university degree or are in the final stages of completion with impressive academic achievements.\n- You demonstrate strong initiative, goal orientation, and an inherent entrepreneurial spirit.\n- You possess excellent and empathetic communication skills, effective both within a team and in direct client interactions.\n- You view personal development not merely as a goal, but as a fundamental part of your professional attitude and drive.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis Consultant Trainee role offers an exceptional entry point for ambitious graduates eager to dive into financial consulting. The emphasis on \"entrepreneurial thinking\" and independent project ownership suggests a company that truly empowers its junior talent to make a difference from day one, rather than just observing. The attractive benefits package, particularly the \"above-average, performance-related income\" combined with robust \"mentoring & further training,\" paints a picture of a company that invests heavily in its people's growth and rewards success. The promise of a \"dynamic environment\" with a \"motivated, young team\" and \"flat hierarchies\" indicates a vibrant, collaborative culture where personal development and open communication are highly valued. If you're looking for a role where you can shape your career path, contribute strategically, and see your impact firsthand, this opportunity appears to be an ideal fit.
