**AI Solution and Data Architect - Marketing Technology & Operations**

**Why We Have This Role**

We are looking for an AI Solution and Data Architect to join our Marketing Technology and Operations team. In this hybrid role, you'll blend deep technical expertise with product management, focusing on AI-driven marketing solutions that transform our MarTech ecosystem. You’ll work closely with engineering, marketing, and product teams to drive innovation, enable scalable technology platforms, and deliver impactful customer experiences and business results. Your work will directly shape how Qualtrics leverages AI to unlock new levels of marketing effectiveness and operational efficiency.

**How You’ll Find Success**

*   Expert in designing and implementing AI-powered marketing solutions that automate and personalize at scale
*   Skilled at modernizing complex MarTech stacks with numerous integrations, ensuring reliability and performance
*   Strong communicator who influences cross-functional teams and senior leaders with technical and product insights
*   Entrepreneurial mindset to lead discovery, drive strategy, and deliver product roadmaps grounded in data
*   Adept at building automation workflows that improve data quality and reduce manual effort
*   Proven ability to manage compliance, security, and governance in marketing technology environments
*   Experienced mentor who cultivates team growth and architectural best practices

**How You’ll Grow**

*   Lead high-impact AI innovation projects at the intersection of marketing and technology
*   Expand your product management skills driving roadmaps and strategic initiatives
*   Deepen expertise in MarTech integrations and automation with cutting-edge platforms and tools
*   Develop your leadership influence with senior executives and multi-disciplinary teams
*   Represent Qualtrics in industry forums on AI-powered marketing technologies

**Things You’ll Do**

*   Design and deploy AI-driven marketing solutions leveraging GPT-4, Claude, and custom ML models to improve campaign outcomes and operational efficiency
*   Transform and modernize our marketing technology stack with over 25 integrations across AI-enabled platforms
*   Build enterprise-grade integration frameworks standardizing APIs, authentication, and audit trails for marketing systems
*   Serve as a trusted technical advisor to leadership, guiding architectural decisions and software lifecycle assessments
*   Lead automation initiatives that empower citizen developers and improve data quality across the marketing funnel
*   Define and execute product strategies that prioritize AI enhancements to marketing tools based on data insights
*   Ensure all solutions comply with privacy, security, and regulatory requirements such as GDPR and CCPA

**What We’re Looking For On Your Resume**

*   8+ years as a solution architect with at least 3 years focused on marketing technology environments
*   Proven programming skills in Python, JavaScript, Java, and SQL, plus hands-on experience with AI/ML frameworks and large language models
*   Deep knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS, Azure), data engineering tools (Snowflake, ETL/ELT), and marketing automation & CRM systems
*   3+ years of product management experience, driving strategy and executing roadmaps
*   Strong leadership experience mentoring technical teams and influencing senior stakeholders
*   Demonstrated ability to translate technical capabilities into tangible business value
*   Preferred: Experience with Qualtrics Experience Management or similar platforms, MarTech consolidation, cost optimization, and compliance frameworks
