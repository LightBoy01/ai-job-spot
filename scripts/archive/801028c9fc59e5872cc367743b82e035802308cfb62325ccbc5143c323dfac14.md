**Job Description:**
**About the role**
The Principal Product Designer will be a highly accomplished individual contributor delivering impactful design outcomes for AI-enabled experiences that enhance hirer efficiency through intelligent automation and AI-driven insights. This role sits within the Connect team focusing on hirer automation and works closely with cross-functional teams including technology and product management partners, with thought leadership responsibilities across the broader Design organisation.
**Responsibilities**
*   Design and prototype AI-enabled features that streamline hirer workflows across multiple marketplace touchpoints
*   Create innovative interaction patterns for human-AI collaboration, ensuring AI recommendations are contextually relevant and actionable
*   Develop rapid prototypes to test feature concepts with hiring teams, validating usability and business impact in fluid environments
*   Establish design principles for AI transparency, explainability, and trust-building in hiring contexts
*   Optimise end-to-end hirer journeys, identifying key intervention points where AI can reduce cognitive load and improve decision quality
*   Work on complex projects including research, analysis, trend analysis and innovative AI-powered proofs of concepts
*   Drive improvements to design system, tooling and ways of working with the design system team
*   Build prototypes and communication pieces that articulate design outcomes with strong storytelling skills
*   Share output regularly with broad audiences to drive alignment across teams and senior leadership on hirer experience improvements
**Qualifications:**
**Skills and experience**
*   Portfolio demonstrating AI/ML-enabled product design experience including prompt design capabilities
*   Proven experience designing human-AI interaction patterns and interfaces for complex user workflows
*   Track record working in cross-functional teams with data scientists, ML engineers, and product managers to shape feasible AI experiences
*   Experience with rapid prototyping tools and methodologies for testing AI concepts with users in iterative environments
*   Background in UX research methodologies, particularly for validating feature usability and business impact metrics
*   Knowledge of AI/ML principles including transparency, explainability, algorithmic bias considerations and error recovery strategies
*   Experience working within design systems and contributing to interaction pattern libraries for scalable solutions
*   Experience in B2B or marketplace products, particularly in hiring, recruitment, or talent acquisition domains
*   Understanding of fairness, bias, transparency, and privacy considerations when designing AI-driven user experiences
*   Ability to design for complex systems where AI, data, and user interaction create integrated feedback loops
