### Description\nTogether with our partner, we are looking for an **Executive Program Manager (all genders)** in a key position within Customer Solutions. In this role, you will be responsible for the overarching overall leadership of highly complex, business-critical large-scale projects.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Strategic overall responsibility for business-critical large-scale projects of national or international relevance, encompassing multiple business units and stakeholder groups.\n*   Management of a complex project organization with up to 60 members, including central project management, the PMO, and various sub-project leads.\n*   Stakeholder management at the top-management level.\n*   Efficient steering of external service providers and internal, cross-departmental teams.\n*   Ensuring adherence to budgets, timelines, and resource specifications.\n*   Establishing and ensuring governance and risk management.\n*   Utilizing modern delivery models and a clear KPI framework to implement and secure project success.\n*   Coaching and development of (sub-)project managers and sharing knowledge to strengthen company-wide project management competence.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Many years of experience in complex project environments, ideally in the public sector.\n*   Expertise demonstrated by relevant PMI certification (ideally PgMP®) or similar.\n*   Experience in leading projects at the management level.\n*   Strong analytical skills and excellent organizational talent.\n*   High assertiveness and resilience in interdisciplinary and multicultural teams.\n*   Completed university or technical college degree or similar desirable.\n*   Fluent German and English language skills (negotiation level).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a pivotal executive-level role for a seasoned Program Manager looking to take on significant strategic responsibility within a dynamic global organization. The opportunity to lead highly complex, business-critical projects with national and international impact, combined with the chance to mentor future leaders, makes this an incredibly impactful position. The company emphasizes a modern, flexible work environment, offering excellent work-life balance benefits and a flat hierarchy, signaling a culture that values employee well-being and professional growth. Ideal for someone with strong leadership, strategic vision, and a desire to drive large-scale transformation.
