### Description\nThe INC Innovation Center operates as an international one-stop-shop for the development and implementation of technology-driven innovations. With six global locations, we support companies from the initial idea to the realization of market-ready products and services. As part of our international Future Robotics activities, we offer dedicated students the opportunity to contribute to exciting projects in robotics, artificial intelligence, and digital business models, either as part of an internship or a master's thesis.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Participate in the technological and strategic analysis of current trends in robotics (e.g., humanoid, collaborative, and modular systems).\n- Support in defining and evaluating robotics use cases for industrial, logistics, or service sectors.\n- Contribute to the validation and implementation of prototypes (e.g., integration of AI systems or sensor solutions).\n- Support hands-on projects in the INC Applied Robotics Hub (testing, evaluation, documentation).\n- Research global robotics markets, technologies, and relevant start-ups.\n- Participate in workshops and study trips (e.g., Europe, China, USA – depending on project phase).\n- Support in concept development and documentation for pilot projects and customer presentations.\n- **Optional:** Develop a master's thesis on topics such as technology evaluation, prototyping processes, supplier-fit analyses, or business model validation.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Currently studying Industrial Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, Robotics, Computer Science, Innovation Management, or a comparable field of study.\n- Strong interest in robotics, AI, and digital business models.\n- Initial experience in technology analysis, product development, or research is an advantage.\n- Possess analytical, conceptual, and interdisciplinary thinking skills.\n- Demonstrate an independent, structured, and solution-oriented work style.\n- Good knowledge of MS Office applications.\n- Very good written and spoken German and English skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for ambitious students eager to make a tangible impact in the rapidly evolving field of robotics. The INC Innovation Center offers a unique, international environment where you'll be directly involved in cutting-edge projects, working alongside a motivated, interdisciplinary team. You'll gain hands-on experience in the Applied Robotics Hub, contributing to real-world prototypes and market research. The role provides access to leading robotics partners, research institutions, and international networks, fostering unparalleled growth. With flexible working hours and the option to develop a practically relevant master's thesis under expert supervision, this position is ideal for those who want to actively shape the future of robotics and bridge the gap between academic theory and practical innovation. If you're ready to bring future technologies to life, this is your chance.
