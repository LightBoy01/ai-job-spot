### Description\nAre you passionate about technology? So are we! We are looking for an EE Hardware Tester - Inhouse & Supplier Qualification (m/f/x) to strengthen our expert team at our client in the Automotive & Commercial Vehicle industry. This is a permanent position with K-tronik. Join us to work on exciting projects and future-oriented technologies with passion and team spirit.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Conducting tests for 24-V electronics and hardware in the automotive sector.\n- Planning and executing hardware tests as well as environmental/qualification tests.\n- Error analysis and support in root cause identification.\n- Interpretation of test norms and standards.\n- Creation of professional test reports.\n- Coordination of engineering and test activities, and exchange with specialist teams.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in Electrical Engineering, Automotive Engineering, Mechatronics, or a comparable technical field, or equivalent training.\n- Expert knowledge in the development and testing of 24-V automotive electronics and hardware.\n- Experience in hardware tests, environmental/qualification tests, and troubleshooting.\n- Proficient in handling and interpreting test norms.\n- Very good German and English language skills.\n- System knowledge in the automotive sector, e.g., infotainment, displays, cameras, radar, general control units (ECUs).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced EE Hardware Tester with a passion for automotive electronics to join a highly regarded company. K-tronik offers a supportive and friendly work environment, emphasizing work-life balance through flexible hours and generous vacation. The role provides significant individual growth potential with continuous learning opportunities and 360° support, making it ideal for someone looking to make a direct impact on cutting-edge vehicle technology while developing their career in a collaborative setting.
