### Description
GFN GmbH is an IT-specialized education provider dedicated to supporting individuals throughout Germany in their professional development and reorientation. This 12-month trainee program offers a direct path to becoming an IHK-certified trainer, immersing you in the world of vocational retraining and continuing education where you can proactively shape the future. We believe in your potential and your strengths!

### Responsibilities
*   You will receive optimal preparation from day one for your future role as a trainer (m/f/x) at GFN.
*   Our experienced colleagues will provide expert guidance, both professionally and methodologically-didactically, on your journey to becoming an instructor.
*   Following the program, you will lead online and in-person courses in your specific technical field.

### Qualifications
*   You are our ideal trainee (m/f/x) if you are characterized by a reliable and team-oriented work style.
*   Have you always been good at explaining things to others and enjoy being \"on your own stage\"? Do you demonstrate an affinity and enthusiasm for IT topics?
*   Do you bring a technical background, for instance, from further education, a technical degree, a relevant apprenticeship, or professional experience?
*   Do you possess very good German language skills (minimum C1 level)?

Even if you don't have a formal IT degree, we still encourage you to apply – we value your individual qualifications. Please include a few sentences about your motivation to become a trainer (m/f/x) and (optionally) your previous experience as a trainer (m/f/x), tutor (m/f/x), coach (m/f/x), or in a similar educational role.

### Author's Take
This is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for individuals passionate about IT and education, seeking a structured path to become a professional trainer. GFN offers a highly supportive environment with dedicated mentorship and a comprehensive paid training program that culminates in an IHK qualification. The extensive benefits, including 30 days of vacation, a JobRad scheme, fitness subsidies (EGYM Wellpass), a subsidized Deutschland-Ticket, and an internet allowance, underscore a strong commitment to employee well-being and work-life balance. It's an ideal role for someone who thrives in a collaborative, diverse, and growth-oriented culture, eager to make a tangible impact in vocational education.
