**Role**\- AI Data Architect
**Company-** StatusNeo (Bangalore)
**About StatusNeo:**
StatusNeo is a modern technology consulting and services firm helping enterprises transform with **AI, Cloud, Data, and Digital Engineering**. We partner with global clients to deliver innovative and scalable solutions that drive real business outcomes.
### **Role Overview**
We are seeking an experienced **AI Data Architect (8+ years)** to design and implement robust, scalable, and secure data architectures powering AI/ML solutions. You will play a critical role in defining data strategies, enabling advanced analytics, ensuring governance, and optimizing infrastructure for AI-driven applications.
### **Key Responsibilities**
*   Design and implement **end-to-end data architectures** for AI/ML workloads.
*   Define **data strategy, governance, and frameworks** for structured, semi/unstructured data.
*   Architect scalable **pipelines, warehouses, and lakehouses** optimized for AI/ML.
*   Collaborate with **Data Scientists, ML Engineers, and business teams** to translate requirements into solutions.
*   Ensure **data security, compliance, lineage, and metadata management**.
*   Optimize **data platforms** for performance, scalability, and cost efficiency.
*   Guide teams on best practices for **AI/ML data lifecycle integration**.
*   Stay ahead by evaluating **emerging tools & technologies** in the Data & AI ecosystem.
### **Required Skills & Experience**
*   **8+ years of experience** in data engineering/architecture, with **3+ years as Data Architect** (AI/ML focus).
*   Strong expertise in **cloud platforms** (AWS, Azure, GCP).
*   Hands-on experience with **lakehouse platforms** (Databricks, Snowflake, Delta Lake, BigQuery, Synapse).
*   Proficiency in **data pipeline frameworks** (Apache Spark, Kafka, Airflow, DBT).
*   Strong knowledge of **ML data lifecycle** – feature engineering, versioning, pipelines, MLOps.
*   Familiarity with **data governance, security & compliance standards**.
*   Proficiency in **SQL & Python** with distributed data systems.
*   Exposure to **AI/ML platforms** (SageMaker, Vertex AI, Azure ML) is a plus.
*   Strong problem-solving and **stakeholder management** skills.
📍 **Location:** Bangalore
