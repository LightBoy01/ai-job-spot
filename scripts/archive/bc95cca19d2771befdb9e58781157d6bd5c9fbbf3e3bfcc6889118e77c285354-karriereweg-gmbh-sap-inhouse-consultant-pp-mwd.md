### Description\\nOur client, a leading international manufacturer in Mobility Solutions, is seeking an experienced SAP In-house Consultant with a focus on Plan to Produce (PP). This position is a crucial part of a strategic transformation process, offering both operational responsibility and active participation in shaping future process and system landscapes. Your core focus will be on elevating production planning and manufacturing processes within the SAP system to a new level. The role is based in the greater Kassel area, with the flexibility of two remote days per week. You will take on a central role in advancing a highly modern SAP system landscape, enjoying technical responsibility close to both operational and strategic issues, and significant scope for shaping processes in a growth-oriented environment.\\n\\n### Responsibilities\\n- Responsibility for production-related SAP processes: analysis, further development, and harmonization of workflows related to planning, control, and manufacturing.\\n- Customizing and system adaptation in the PP module, including configuration of relevant functions and technical implementation support with internal development teams.\\n- Process support along the manufacturing chain – with interface relevance to MM, Logistics, and Quality Assurance.\\n- Coordination of technical requirements from specialist departments and daily business support.\\n- Collaboration on international SAP projects, including rollouts and system integration.\\n- Maintenance of process-relevant master data, participation in documentation, and knowledge transfer.\\n\\n### Qualifications\\n- A completed degree or comparable qualification in IT, Production, or Logistics.\\n- Several years of experience in supporting production-related processes with SAP – particularly in the PP module, ideally with experience in ECC 6.0 EHP 8 on a HANA basis.\\n- Knowledge of related modules such as MM, EWM, or QM is an advantage.\\n- Customizing experience and understanding of technical concepts in the SAP context (e.g., interfaces, simple ABAP implementations, XML).\\n- Structured, independent working style and the ability to communicate complex processes clearly.\\n- Very good German and good English language skills.\\n\\n### Author's Take\\nThis is a compelling opportunity for a seasoned SAP PP expert eager to drive significant impact within an international manufacturing leader. The role offers a unique blend of strategic influence and hands-on technical work, allowing you to shape a modern SAP landscape. With a collaborative culture, short decision paths, flexible remote work options, and a clear growth mandate, it's an ideal environment for an individual contributor looking for professional growth and tangible results in a supportive setting.
