**How You'll Make An Impact:**

As a Senior Product Marketing Manager you will help craft positioning and messaging, and support the execution of marketing strategies for the Boomi Enterprise Platform with a focus on engaging data and analytics leaders as well as hands-on data professionals. This role requires a solid understanding of product marketing, the Data Management landscape, and agentic AI technologies. The ideal candidate brings experience in product or solution marketing, preferably within the Data or AI vendor space.

**What You'll Do:**

*   Craft compelling, differentiated positioning and messaging by developing a deep understanding of target buyers and their needs
    
*   Produce high-quality customer-facing assets, including presentations, datasheets, case studies, videos, and demos, to effectively communicate the value of our solutions to potential buyers
    
*   Support the execution of marketing programs and sales enablement 
    
*   Conduct competitive analysis to identify opportunities for growth and differentiation
    
*   Collaborate with product management to drive launches and act as the voice of the buyer to help influence roadmap decisions
    
*   Present at customer-facing physical events and webinars as a subject matter expert
    

**The Experience You Bring:**

*   Bachelor's degree in marketing, communications, engineering or other related programs
    
*   8+ years of product or solution marketing experience
    
*   Experience in the B2B SaaS/ Software technology space
*   Demonstrated ability to understand and market data technology products to a technical and business audience
    
*   A strong understanding of real-life, Agentic AI use cases
    
*   Excellent communication skills, with the ability to craft compelling stories and articulate complex technical details in a clear, concise manner
    
*   Strong analytical skills and experience with market research, competitive analysis, and marketing metrics
    
*   Advanced writing skills using complicated technical concepts to describe a clear and impactful story
    
*   Ability to work collaboratively across teams, including product management, sales, and customer success
    

**Bonus Points If You Have:**

*   Familiarity with RAG processes and effective data feeding for AI Agents
    
*   Experience working for an AI Vendor
*   Knowledge of data engineering challenges and metadata usage
