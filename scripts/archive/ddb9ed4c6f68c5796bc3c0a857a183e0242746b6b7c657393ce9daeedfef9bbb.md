We are looking for passionate people who are about driving innovation at the intersection of automation, AI, and integration. At AppsFlyer, you’ll do more than just build solutions, you’ll be a key part of our Information Systems (IS) team, focusing on internal solutions that empower our organization from within. We offer a collaborative, global environment where continuous learning and personal growth are part of our culture. If you’re ready to make a real impact and be at the forefront of technological advancement within our internal teams, we’d love to have you on board!
**What you'll do:**
*   Lead Automation and AI Projects: Take ownership of implementing automation, AI, and integration solutions across the organization.
*   Support Existing Processes: Maintain and enhance current automated workflows and integration processes to ensure they remain efficient and effective.
*   Internal Client Requests: Act as the primary contact for internal stakeholders, gathering requirements and translating them into scalable automation and AI solutions.
*   Knowledge Sharing: Serve as a subject matter expert for the team and the organization in automation, AI, and integration domains, helping to educate and upskill colleagues.
**What you have:**
*   A bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or Industrial Engineering and Management is preferred, or at least 3 years of proven experience in automation or experience with building impactful AI agents.
*   Experience with IPAAS tools (Like Make, Workato, N8N, etc.)
*   Experience with REST API.
*   Experience in Python / JS.
*   Familiar with the AWS tools like S3, RDS. Lambda, Athena.
*   Familiar with SQL
*   Excellent English (spoken and written)
**Bonus Points:**
*   B.S. in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent experience in software and/or a technologically relevant field.
*   DB Architecture understanding.
