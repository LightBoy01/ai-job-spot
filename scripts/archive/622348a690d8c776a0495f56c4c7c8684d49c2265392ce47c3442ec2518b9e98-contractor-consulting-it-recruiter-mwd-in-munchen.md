### Description
Do you want to do more than just manage resumes – rather, bring people and projects together that truly fit? Then you are in the right place with us! Here, you don't work "just anywhere," but in one of the leading partner networks for highly qualified IT freelancers in the DACH region.

As an IT Recruiter (m/f/d), you will become part of a motivated recruiting team that finds the best IT specialists with passion, speed, and a real commitment to quality.

### Responsibilities
*   **Meaningful Recruiting**: You bring top talent together with exciting digital projects – quickly, purposefully, and with a commitment to quality.
*   **End-to-End Responsibility**: From initial contact to interviews and presenting candidates to clients – you shape the entire candidate journey.
*   **Collaboration over Competition**: Close cooperation with sales, freelancers, and partners – all at eye level.
*   **Valued Expertise**: You develop a deep understanding of tech trends, role profiles, and markets.
*   **A Team That Truly Works**: You work with people who want to make a difference – with drive and enjoyment in their work.

### Qualifications
*   First to several years of experience in recruiting – ideally in an IT or agency environment.
*   A strong intuition for people, job matching, and the "hidden skills" behind resumes.
*   Passion for communication, combined with a high quality standard and initiative.
*   Interest in digital technologies, IT trends, and evolving skillsets.
*   Structured, solution-oriented work – even when things need to move quickly.
*   Very good German language skills and good English language skills, both written and spoken.
