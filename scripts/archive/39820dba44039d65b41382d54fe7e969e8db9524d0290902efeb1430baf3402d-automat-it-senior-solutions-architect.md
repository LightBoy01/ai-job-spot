### Description
Automat-it is a premier AWS Partner, specializing in empowering high-growth startups to accelerate their pace, scale intelligently, and maximize their cloud investments. We provide hands-on DevOps, FinOps, and GenAI support, driving tangible results across EMEA and the US, tackling complex challenges daily and fueling innovation.

In this pivotal pre-sales role, you will collaborate directly with global startup customers and cross-functionally with account executives to architect and develop robust AWS solutions. This is an opportunity to grow your skills, contribute to bold ideas, and help shape the future of technology.

**Work location**: Remote from Berlin/Munich.

Automat-it is dedicated to cultivating a workplace that fosters equal opportunities for all. We believe a diverse workforce is vital to our success, and our recruitment focuses on your experience and skills, recognizing the unique value you bring to our team.

### Responsibilities
*   Design, develop, and implement solutions that meet business requirements, ensuring they are cost-efficient, highly available, and scalable for customers. This includes conducting Well-Architected reviews and preparing Statements of Work (SoW).
*   Research and critically analyze existing solutions, proactively initiating and driving improvement plans.
*   Collaborate extensively with fellow engineers and various stakeholders to guarantee that solutions are designed and developed in alignment with industry best practices.
*   Actively share your expertise with our customers and the broader cloud community by participating in public speaking engagements such as workshops, conferences, webinars, and other relevant events.
*   Undertake frequent travel, both locally (on-demand, to meet with customers and partners, and attend local events) and internationally (at least once per quarter).

### Qualifications
*   3+ years of hands-on experience in an AWS environment, encompassing implementation, migration, maintenance, and end-to-end solution design.
*   2+ years of experience in technical customer-facing roles, such as Customer Engineering, Site Reliability Engineer (SRE), Cloud Architect, or equivalent.
*   Proven experience in a production environment, developing software with Python, Go, Java, or equivalent languages.
*   In-depth knowledge of AWS's main services, with a strong emphasis on Kubernetes and Containers.
*   Demonstrated success in designing DevOps and Infrastructure for high-scale production environments within AWS.
*   A solid understanding of Information Security best practices.
*   Certified AWS Solutions Architect - Associate.
*   Ability to effectively communicate complex technical designs in both verbal and written formats directly to customers.
*   Ability to lead an end-to-end engagement collaboratively with the account management team.
*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills in both English and German.
*   Ability to thrive and deliver results in a fast-paced environment.

**Advantageous Qualifications:**
*   2+ years of experience in an IT environment, including hands-on work with Kubernetes, CI/CD, and various DevOps tools.
*   AWS Solutions Architect Professional or a specialty certification.
