**Job Description:**

*   Lead executive alignment workshops across strategic and global accounts
    
*   Defines value messaging across C-suite buying committees.
    
*   Lead discovery workshops, shape customer requirements, and influence deal strategy to drive technical wins.
    
*   Deliver compelling and customized product demonstrations aligned to map digital transformation using industry use cases, ROI models, and value storytelling.
    
*   Collaborate with AEs, product teams, and solution architects to shape scalable, secure, and future-ready solutions.
    
*   Contribute to competitive strategies, offer technical insights, and proactively mentor junior team members (IC1–IC3).
    
*   Translate complex customer needs into clear solution architectures, including integrations, APIs, and security considerations.
    
*   Champion technical excellence and industry best practices through presentations, internal enablement, and customer engagements.
    
*   Advises leadership on innovation, GTM
    

**Qualifications:**

*   10+ years of experience in Solution Engineering, preferably in SaaS and enterprise software.
    
*   You have an established playbook on using AI to present better,  operationalize projects faster, and articulate value clearer
    
*   Understands LLM's and can guide customers, team members and products on foundational AI principles
    
*   Strong understanding of ITSM/ESM domains; familiarity with platforms such as ServiceNow, Halo, Jira, or similar.
    
*   Strong knowledge of ITSM and AI  maturity adoption frameworks and integrating ITIL.
    
*   Comfortable operating independently in high-stakes, complex enterprise scenarios; able to lead strategy, technical discovery, and deal execution without daily oversight.
    
*   Deep knowledge of APIs, webhooks, integrations, and infrastructure components—able to propose and explain solutions without needing additional support.
    
*   Skilled in coaching and mentoring peers; committed to team development and cross-functional collaboration.
    
*   Adept at aligning customer needs with Freshworks’ platform capabilities, identifying gaps, and working with internal teams to drive innovation.
    
*   Confident and engaging communicator with experience influencing C-level stakeholders and diverse technical audiences.
    
*   Strong time management skills—capable of owning multiple deals and workstreams concurrently.
    
*   Experience leading complex solution design involving custom apps, third-party integrations, and platform architecture.
    
*   Demonstrated leadership in internal initiatives, mentoring, or representing your company at industry events.
    
*   Bachelor’s degree preferred; equivalent experience accepted.
