### Description

Join Uniserv's Customer Data Hub Team as a Frontend Developer, where you'll be a vital contributor to the continuous development and improvement of our Master Data Management solutions. We are an agile, predominantly remote team that thrives on collaboration and innovation. You'll be instrumental in expanding our existing applications with exciting new features, working closely with Product Owners, Backend, QA, Consulting, and Managed Solution teams.

At Uniserv, we foster an environment of continuous learning and growth. You'll have the chance to evolve your skills and explore new tools and technologies. We believe in creative problem-solving within an agile framework, supported by a comprehensive onboarding process that ensures a smooth start. We provide modern work equipment, including a laptop and Office 365, to support your work, wherever you are. Beyond the day-to-day, we celebrate successes together at various team events throughout the year. Our culture is built on appreciation, helpfulness, and open communication, making #TeamUniserv a truly strong and supportive community.

### Responsibilities

*   Be a key part of our agile and predominantly remote Customer Data Hub Team, actively contributing to the development and continuous improvement of our Master Data Management solutions.
*   Extend existing applications with new and exciting features, collaborating closely with Product Owners, Backend and QA teams, as well as our Consulting and Managed Solution teams.
*   Implement (Figma) designs into functional prototypes, always ensuring high performance, quality, and scalability of our applications.
*   Ensure our solutions meet the highest quality standards through rigorous testing (e.g., with Jest, Vitest, or React Testing Library) and thorough code reviews.

### Qualifications

*   Profound knowledge and experience in React development, coupled with familiarity with current software development methodologies and practices.
*   Proficiency in TypeScript, Redux, and RTK-Query.
*   Experience with container technologies (Kubernetes, Docker) is a plus, but not a strict requirement – the more touchpoints, the better.
*   You are characterized by a meticulous and self-reliant working style, consistently upholding very high-quality standards.
*   An absolute team player, you value the synergistic effects of excellent collaboration.
*   Ability to communicate fluently in English, as part of our team operates from Cluj (Romania).
