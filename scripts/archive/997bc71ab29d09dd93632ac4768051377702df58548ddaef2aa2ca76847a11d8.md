This role reports into the Global Privacy Counsel.

**Job Description:**

We are seeking to hire a **Head of AI Governance** to lead Informa’s Responsible AI efforts and contribute to the evolution of our AI Policy Framework for the Informa Group. Sitting within Informa’s Privacy Team, the role will involve working closely with our technology teams building and deploying leading AI technologies and our business teams using these tools.  Additionally, this role will work with the Privacy Team, the Commercial Data Governance Team, and our broader Legal & Governance colleagues. The key aim being to ensure that we make the most of our opportunities with AI whilst ensuring our development and uses of AI technologies are transparent, ethical, and compliant with relevant regulations, and that colleagues across Informa understand their responsibilities when developing, procuring, and/or deploying AI-based solutions.

The role suits passionate, proactive, goal-oriented candidates who are intellectually curious, thrive in complicated but ambitious organisations, and who are happy to roll up their sleeves and be very hands-on in developing, implementing, and evangelising Responsible AI across Informa. The role is well-suited to versatile candidates with a background in AI Governance, Data Ethics, Privacy Compliance & Operations, etc. People with heart and ambition, and a strong mix of emotional and professional intelligence, will be successful in the role.  This role will also require strong collaboration and coordination skills with the ability to influence a broad set of senior stakeholders.

**Your Role:**

*   Lead the development and deployment of Informa’s Responsible AI programme (inc. policies, standards, resourcing, training, processes, technologies, etc.)
*   Be Informa’s internal expert advisor on the regulatory, policy, and technical matters relating to Responsible AI.
*   Manage and evolve our AI impact assessment workflow; reviewing assessments for compliance, flagging risks and documenting mitigations, aligning and integrating with parallel assessments (e.g. privacy and cybersecurity) to simplify compliance.
*   Develop standard metrics and report on the operationalization of Informa’s AI Governance Framework
*   Develop and maintain a master register of AI deployments across Informa, documenting the use cases, their risks, reporting criteria, etc.
*   Actively participate in the AI Council to advise on policy and regulatory developments
*   Regularly report on responsible AI implementation and risk management to senior management
*   Lead the development and delivery of training & communication plan for roll-out of responsible AI in Informa, focused on continually increasing AI governance maturity across the enterprise
*   Be the resident expert for the Legal & Governance (L&G) team to help us make the most of AI tools. Lead the identification of AI use cases for L&G function and be the conduit to technical teams to ensure the L&G team’s use cases are prioritized and delivered.
*   Continually horizon scan for policy, regulatory, and legal developments in the field of Responsible AI, ensuring that Informa’s AI implementations are compliant with internal AI policies as well as relevant laws and regulations.  Attending events to represent Informa, provide thought leadership and knowledge share.
*   Work with Informa’s Global Privacy Counsel to advise business stakeholders on legal requirements relating to AI.
*   Liaise closely with Privacy Team members on aspects of AI regulation that are interlinked with privacy compliance.

**Qualifications:**

**Who we’re looking for**

Ideal candidates will be individuals whose qualities include:

*   **Minimum of 8 years of experience in governance roles.**  Experience may not have been exclusively focused on AI governance, but should have been in a related field requiring the consideration of technical, legal, and commercial factors to assess risk and advise business clients (e.g. privacy and data protection advisory work).
*   **Deep understanding Responsible AI challenges:** the technical, policy, and regulatory risks that AI technologies raise for Informa customers and colleagues in a multitude of jurisdictions around the world
*   **Understanding of AI systems and capabilities:** the technologies, the emerging field, and how these are deployed within an enterprise
*   **Get things done:** highly organised, resourceful and action-oriented with experience driving focus, clarity and prioritisation in a fast-paced environment
