With over a decade of innovation, we protect the integrity of the digital advertising ecosystem through cutting-edge, real-time technology. Our success is driven by a passionate, collaborative team that thrives on solving complex challenges and making an impact.\n\nAs a **Sales Executive** focusing on the **DACH region**, particularly the German digital advertising market, you will be pivotal to GeoEdge's continued growth. This is a high-visibility, high-impact role requiring deep market knowledge, international experience, and a hands-on, consultative sales approach. You will lead strategic sales efforts, cultivate high-value pipelines, and build enduring relationships with key stakeholders across the adtech ecosystem.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Build, manage, and own a comprehensive sales pipeline, including lead generation, outbound outreach, prioritization, and key metric tracking.\n- Lead the full sales cycle, from prospecting to closing, across Germany and the broader DACH region.\n- Cultivate strong relationships with decision-makers at publishers, SSPs, platforms, and media companies.\n- Maintain accurate pipeline forecasting and performance reporting.\n- Adapt GeoEdge’s global value proposition into impactful, localized sales narratives.\n- Collaborate cross-functionally with product, marketing, and customer success teams to ensure long-term client growth.\n- Provide crucial insights into regional market trends and customer needs to help shape strategic and product direction.\n- Represent GeoEdge at key industry events throughout Europe, both virtually and in person.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 4+ years of B2B sales experience within the programmatic advertising ecosystem, with a proven track record of success in the German and DACH markets.\n- Demonstrated ability to manage the full sales cycle and consistently achieve revenue goals.\n- Strong existing network across publishers, SSPs, and media platforms in the region.\n- Experience pitching and closing complex deals with senior stakeholders (C-suite, VP, and Director levels).\n- Strategic mindset complemented by excellent relationship-building skills.\n- Native or fluent proficiency in both **German and English**, with outstanding verbal and written communication abilities.\n- Consultative, client-focused sales approach with strong interpersonal and presentation skills.\n- Bachelor’s degree required;
