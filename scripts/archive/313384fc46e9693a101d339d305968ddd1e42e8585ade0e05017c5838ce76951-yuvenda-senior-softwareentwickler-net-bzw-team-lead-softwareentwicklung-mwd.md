### Description\nShape the future of our retail software solutions – technologically modern, with the opportunity to drive AI-supported development company-wide. As a Senior Developer or Team Lead for Software Development, you will significantly influence the technological advancement of our platforms. Concurrently, you will introduce your knowledge of AI in software development, promoting modern working methods and AI-based tools within the team and across the company. This role offers the unique chance to blend hands-on development with strategic leadership, particularly in pioneering AI integration.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop modern software solutions for the retail sector (web, app, and desktop applications).\n- Take technical responsibility from conception to go-live.\n- Introduce and establish AI-supported development practices within the company.\n- Evaluate suitable AI tools, best practices, and workflows.\n- Manage and execute projects within the German retail environment.\n- Conduct analysis, specification, testing, and documentation.\n- Assume team leadership in development, mentoring junior developers.\n- Co-create a modern engineering and team culture.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Several years of experience in software development (.NET-based).\n- Good understanding of modern software architectures.\n- Affinity for AI in software development.\n- Structured way of working, analytical thinking, hands-on mentality.\n- Team player, strong communication skills, and sense of responsibility.\n- Experience in leading teams or strong motivation for this step.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned developer passionate about innovation and leadership, especially with a focus on AI. The role offers significant autonomy to shape the company's future tech landscape and lead a team of 5-10 developers. With a modern, hybrid work culture offering 2 days home office per week, flexible arrangements, and strong support for professional development, it's an ideal environment for someone looking to make a substantial impact and grow into a pivotal leadership position.
