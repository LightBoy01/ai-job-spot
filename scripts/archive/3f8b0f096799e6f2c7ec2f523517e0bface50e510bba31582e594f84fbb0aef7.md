**The Role:**

We’re seeking a **Senior Analyst, AI Automation** with deep experience in AI, data-driven automations, and operational tooling to drive innovation across both internal systems and client-facing financial products. Reporting to the SVP of Enterprise AI Strategy and Engineering, this hybrid role will support the strategy, design, and delivery of AI-powered solutions — from enhancing internal operational efficiency through automation to building external products that deliver differentiated insights to our clients. You’ll sit at the intersection of AI/ML, product strategy, and financial services, working closely with engineering, operations, and go-to-market teams to execute a roadmap that scales our business and advances our leadership in private market intelligence. 

* * *

**Location: This position is remote in Denver, CO. We intend to open an office in Denver and once we do, we will require hybrid attendance onsite at least 9 days each month.**

**Responsibilities:**

*   Contribute meaningfully to the strategy and execution for AI-powered internal tools and automation across departments (Marketing, People, Finance, Sales, Operations, etc.)
*   Partner with business stakeholders to identify manual or inefficient processes and translate them into scalable AI-enabled workflows and automation-first solutions
*   Define and prioritize the internal tools roadmap in collaboration with cross-functional leads and IT
*   Build lightweight, no-code tools (e.g. GPT, internal APIs, Agents)
*   Own end-to-end delivery: from user discovery, requirements, building, and post-launch adoption
*   Drive internal adoption and training to ensure your tools are used and valuable
*   Drive integration of machine learning and large language model (LLM) capabilities into both internal systems and market-facing offerings
*   Collaborate with data, engineering, design, operations, and compliance teams to ensure solutions are secure, compliant, and deliver measurable business value
*   Define and track product success metrics such as user adoption, operational time savings, data accuracy, error reduction and cost optimization
*   Promote adoption of internal tools through training, change management, and ongoing optimization

**Qualifications:**

*   5-7 years of experience as an analyst.
*   Experience working with low-code tools (e.g., Retool, Zapier, Airtable, Workato,n8n) and coordinating with engineers on custom tooling
*   At least 2 years of experience working in AI analytics including data-driven products and/or automation solutions
*   Proven track record launching both internal business systems/tools and external financial services products
*   Strong understanding of AI/ML concepts, data pipelines, and cloud-based architectures
*   Experience conducting process analysis and translating business needs into scalable system designs
*   Excellent English-communication skills with the ability to influence across technical and non-technical teams

**Preferred Qualifications** 

*   Experience with financial market data products, investment lifecycle processes, or private/public market structures.
*   Strong understanding of operational processes across GTM, Finance, HR, and Support
*   Experience conducting process analysis to inform system design, tool selection, and automation opportunities
*   Excellent problem-solving skills; thrives on process improvement and system design
*   Experience in a high-growth startup or fast-moving environment 

**Location: This position is remote in Denver, CO. We intend to open an office in Denver and once we do, we will require hybrid attendance onsite at least 9 days each month.**
