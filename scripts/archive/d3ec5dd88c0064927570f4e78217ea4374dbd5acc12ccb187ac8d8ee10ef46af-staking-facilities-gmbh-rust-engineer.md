**(f/m/x) - Hybrid in Munich or Remote in EU Time Zone - Blockchain / DeFi**

If you’re an engineer who gets excited about building from the ground up and creating the kind of systems that will power the next wave of blockchain trading, this is your chance to get in early.

Tasks
-----

You’ll work directly with our Trading Lead as the first dedicated Engineer in this new venture. Expect deep technical challenges, real ownership, and the thrill of helping define how capital markets evolve on-chain within one of Europe’s most respected blockchain companies.

*   Design and implement backend services in Rust, optimized for throughput, reliability, and low latency
*   Build systems that connect on-chain and off-chain components, from smart contract interactions to market data ingestion and execution logic
*   Collaborate closely with infrastructure and validator teams to leverage bare-metal performance and low-latency access to blockchain networks

*   Contribute to architecture design sessions, helping define how we scale a multi-chain trading platform
*   Collaborate with experienced engineers across Staking Facilities to extend node infrastructure and on-chain APIs
*   Shape technical direction, influence tooling and processes, and grow with the trading unit as it scales

Requirements
------------

*   Proficient in Rust, with experience writing safe, performant, and maintainable code
*   You’re excited about Web3, decentralized finance, and the emerging internet of capital markets.
*   Comfortable working independently and taking ownership in an early-stage setup
*   Curious and self-motivated, you enjoy learning new systems, exploring blockchain tech, and tinkering with complex problems
*   Based in Europe or nearby time zones (CET overlap)
*   Fluent in English, with strong communication and collaboration skills
*   Familiar with concepts like order books, limit orders, and exchange mechanics, or eager to learn
*   Existing experience with smart contracts, on-chain transactions would be ideal
