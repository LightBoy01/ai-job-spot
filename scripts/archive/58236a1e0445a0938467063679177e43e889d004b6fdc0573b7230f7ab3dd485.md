**ABOUT THE JOB**

We are looking for a RAMS Specialist to join the team of Rail & Transit in our offices in Madrid, Bilbao or Barcelona.

At IDOM, we offer you the opportunity to participate in national and international projects as part of a team where you will have the support to push your professional boundaries and improve your skills every day. In a multidisciplinary environment you will be exposed to all the latest technologies and best practices. Your main responsibilities are going to be:

*   Functional and interface safety analysis
*   Safety requirements tracking
*   Test Specification review
*   Test Results Reports review
*   Verification Reports preparation
*   Safety Reports preparation
*   Reliability Studies, FMECA, FTA preparation
*   RAMS Plan preparation
*   Safety Plan preparation
*   Support for AsBo documentation

**ABOUT YOU**

**Apply if you…**

*   You hold a degree in Civil, Industrial, or similar Engineering, as long as it relates to the areas of responsibility. A Master's or Postgraduate degree specifically related to RAMS management in railway projects will be valued.
*   You have at least 3 years of experience in the railway RAMS engineering sector, railway inspection, interoperability, or Regulation 402 (Common Safety Methods). Previous experience in the railway sector and RAMS activities is a plus.
*   You have fluent English (minimum level B2/C1, spoken and written); other languages will be valued.
*   You are familiar with interoperability (TSI).
*   You have strong analytical skills and are proficient in report writing.
*   You are a team player with autonomy, flexibility, technical rigor, and an international mindset.
*   And above all... you’re eager to learn and grow in an international company like IDOM.
