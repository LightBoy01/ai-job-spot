### Description\nEveryone should hear well to live well. This is what we believe in at audibene, driven by the purpose of changing lives of our customers. As one of the fastest-growing health-technology companies, audibene/hear.com has revolutionized the hearing care industry, helping over 200,000 customers since 2012.\n\nAs a Service Administrator, you will join the Product & Engineering team to help expand audibene's internal service ecosystem beyond Salesforce and Business Central into custom-built microservices. This role is crucial for ensuring data accuracy across internal systems, maintaining integrations with partners and core platforms, and supporting key business processes like customer and contract management. It's a unique opportunity for someone who thrives at the intersection of system administration, data management, and process design, contributing to the evolution of a vital digital ecosystem.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Administer and maintain business-critical data, including customer records, contracts, and process information across internal systems.\n*   Ensure seamless integration and synchronization between Salesforce, Business Central, and custom-built microservices.\n*   Monitor and validate data flows, identify discrepancies, and coordinate fixes with engineering and product teams.\n*   Support external partners directly, manage access, resolve issues, and track feedback.\n*   Create and follow up on bug reports or improvement requests based on partner and stakeholder input.\n*   Collaborate with Product, Operations, and Engineering teams to define admin processes and data workflows.\n*   Contribute to the design of admin tools, dashboards, or lightweight frontends for better data visibility and control.\n*   Champion best practices for data quality, operational excellence, and process scalability.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Experience in system administration, data operations, or integration management in a multi-system environment (Salesforce, ERP, or similar).\n*   Strong understanding of data flows, APIs, and integrations; comfortable discussing technical topics with engineers.\n*   Excellent attention to detail, accuracy, and organizational skills.\n*   Strong communication skills, able to work effectively with both internal stakeholders and external partners.\n*   A proactive mindset, identifying issues and driving process improvements.\n*   Familiarity with SQL, REST APIs, or basic scripting for data validation or analysis.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a detail-oriented and proactive Service Administrator to make a significant impact at audibene, a rapidly growing health-tech company. You'll be at the heart of their evolving digital ecosystem, ensuring data integrity and seamless integrations that directly support their mission of changing lives. The role offers a collaborative environment, a hybrid work model (4 days in office, 1 day remote), and a strong commitment to personal development through training and coaching. With perks like a Deutschland-Ticket, Urban Sports Club membership, and a dog-friendly office, audibene clearly values employee well-being and a positive team culture. If you enjoy solving complex data challenges and contributing to process design in a mission-driven company, this role offers both technical depth and meaningful impact.
