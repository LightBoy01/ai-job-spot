VTS is the commercial real estate industry's only technology platform that unifies owners, operators, brokers, and tenants to improve the way space is marketed, leased, and managed. Our Leasing & Asset Management products — VTS Lease, VTS Market, and VTS Data — are the flagship solutions that power leasing execution, deal management, and portfolio insights across the globe.
As Lead Product Manager for Leasing & Asset Management, you will fully own this core segment, guiding the strategy and execution of our market-defining products. You will lead two product managers (with potential to grow the team) and shape a roadmap that strengthens our category leadership, expands adoption, and delivers measurable value to owners, operators, and brokers.
Reporting directly to the SVP of Product, you will partner closely with Engineering, Design, Data Science, and our Revenue organization to ensure product investments drive business impact. You will set the vision for how leasing and asset management professionals work smarter and faster with AI-enabled workflows, portfolio-level insights, and seamless integrations.
In this role you will deliver high-impact releases that accelerate deal velocity, improve portfolio performance, and enhance decision-making. You will also be responsible for defining strategic growth plans for VTS Lease, Market, and Data, ensuring they scale in alignment with customer needs and company revenue goals.
**_\*\* Please note that this opportunity is located in New York, NY, and requires this hire to work from our office four days a week. There will be periodic travel to customers (approx 10-20%) \*\*_**
**Here is what you can expect as a Lead Product Manager:**
*   **Own the Leasing & Asset Management segment strategy & roadmap**, ensuring delivery of high-value capabilities that drive leasing outcomes and asset performance.
*   **Lead and grow a team of product managers**, managing two PMs today
*   **Drive adoption, retention, and expansion** by partnering with GTM, Customer Success, and Implementation teams to bring products to market and deliver customer success.
*   **Collaborate cross-functionally** with Engineering, Design, Revenue, Data Science, and external partners to deliver best-in-class solutions.
*   **Identify and deliver AI-driven enhancements** that streamline deal execution, improve forecasting, and generate actionable insights.
*   **Run discovery** with leasing teams, asset managers, and portfolio managers to prioritize the most impactful problems to solve.
*   **Mentor and coach** product managers, elevating product craft, strategic thinking, and execution.
**To succeed in this role you have:**
--------------------------------------
*   6+ years of Product Management experience, with at least 2 years in a **lead-level or equivalent role**.
*   Proven success **owning a product segment** or portfolio with multi-squad delivery.
*   Experience in **B2B SaaS** with enterprise-scale web products (marketplace, workflow, or analytics).
*   Strong track record of delivering products that **combine workflow execution with data-driven insights**.
*   Ability to **balance platform vision with customer-driven priorities**.
*   Excellent communicator with strong influencing skills across technical and business stakeholders.
*   Comfortable navigating ambiguity and driving alignment in complex enterprise environments.
*   Ideally you have a background delivering **AI-powered features** in production, and familiarity with **data products, analytics, and enterprise-scale implementations**.
*   Experience in **proptech, commercial real estate, or financial technology** is a plus.
