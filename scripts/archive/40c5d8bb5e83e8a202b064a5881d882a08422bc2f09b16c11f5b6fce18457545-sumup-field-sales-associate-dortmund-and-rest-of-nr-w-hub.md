### Description

**Sell with Purpose. Inspire with Technology.**

At SumUp, we empower small businesses with modern solutions for card payments and point-of-sale systems: simple, efficient, and flexible. Our devices enable merchants to securely accept payments anywhere – in-store, on the go, or at the weekly market.

As a **Field Sales Representative (m/f/d)** at SumUp, you will receive a fixed base salary along with the opportunity for an **unlimited, performance-based bonus structure**. Your success is in your hands and will be rewarded. Simultaneously, you will enjoy **full flexibility in daily planning** and take responsibility for your own sales territory.

### Responsibilities

*   Actively approach local merchants within your sales territory.
*   Present modern payment products clearly and practically.
*   Confidently handle objections and find individual solutions.
*   Build and maintain your own customer portfolio – long-term and personal.
*   Collaborate with a dedicated, goal-oriented Sales Team.

### Qualifications

*   Motivation to be directly in the market and achieve results.
*   Basic technical understanding and the ability to explain smart products simply.
*   Solution-oriented thinking, self-initiative, and high motivation.
*   Initial sales experience is an advantage, but passion and eagerness to learn are more important.

### Author's Take

SumUp is an international Fintech company dedicated to developing simple, powerful financial products that empower small businesses worldwide to realize their vision. They value diversity, an open company culture, and emphasize performance, commitment, and mutual respect.

This role offers a unique blend of autonomy and support, ideal for a driven individual ready to make a tangible impact. You'll benefit from:

*   **Financial Rewards:** A competitive fixed salary complemented by an uncapped commission structure, directly honoring your performance.
*   **Flexibility & Growth:** Full control over your daily schedule, comprehensive onboarding, ongoing training, and an individual professional development budget to foster your continuous learning.
*   **Well-being & Perks:** Access to mental health support, an Employee Referral Program, employee discounts with over 700 partners, and an Urban Sports Club membership.

SumUp encourages applicants to apply even if they don't meet every single criterion, emphasizing that passion and a willingness to learn are highly valued. This is an opportunity to shape your success while supporting real entrepreneurs in their growth journey.
