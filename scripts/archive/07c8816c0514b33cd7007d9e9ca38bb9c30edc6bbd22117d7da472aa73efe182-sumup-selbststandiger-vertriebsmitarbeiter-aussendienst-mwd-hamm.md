### Description

**Launch your sales career – 100% commission-based & with no income cap!**

Are you courageous enough to work independently, shape your own success, and manage your time freely? Then you've come to the right place! SumUp empowers small businesses with smart and user-friendly financial solutions, ranging from card payments to comprehensive POS systems. Our goal is to strengthen entrepreneurs, enabling them to focus entirely on their core business.

As a **freelance Field Sales Consultant**, you are your own boss and have the opportunity to earn **attractive, unlimited commissions**! You will build relationships with local businesses and demonstrate how SumUp can simplify their operations. Your primary task involves the direct sale of our solutions – whether it's a mobile card terminal or comprehensive payment systems, you'll have everything you need to succeed.

**What awaits you:**

*   **100% performance-based compensation** – your income depends on your effort, with no upper limit.
*   **Maximum flexibility** – work whenever and wherever you want.
*   **Top products with high market potential** – compelling solutions for small businesses.
*   **Training & sales support** – we won't leave you on your own.

### Responsibilities

*   **Active New Customer Acquisition** – Identify and acquire local businesses for SumUp.
*   **Personal Meetings and Consultation** – Present our products and demonstrate their benefits.
*   **Build Customer Relationships** – Secure long-term partnerships through excellent service.
*   **Own Customer and Lead Management** – You decide how you build your business.

### Qualifications

*   Passion for sales and strong self-motivation.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer service is an advantage.
*   Excellent communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Very good German language skills.
