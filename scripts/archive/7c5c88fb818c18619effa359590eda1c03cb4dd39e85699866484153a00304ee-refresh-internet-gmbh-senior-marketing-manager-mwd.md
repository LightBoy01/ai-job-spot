### Description\nAre you ready to **Own the Funnel and Drive the Growth**? As a Senior Marketing Manager, you will be pivotal in shaping the company's digital marketing landscape and brand presence. This strategic role offers overall responsibility for marketing initiatives across all digital channels, focusing on scalability, brand awareness, and sustainable revenue growth. You will play a key part in defining the future of our brands, particularly our private labels, through innovative strategies and close collaboration.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Hold overall responsibility for the marketing strategy across all digital channels, including Performance Marketing, CRM, Social Media, and Content.\n- Conceive and implement scalable marketing campaigns that enhance brand awareness and generate sustainable revenue growth.\n- Collaborate closely with the E-commerce, Purchasing, and Private Label teams.\n- Identify new growth potentials, market trends, and digital tools.\n- Further develop the company's brand positioning and establish a consistent, modern brand presence across all touchpoints.\n- Serve as a strategic sparring partner for the management board on all key marketing strategy questions.\n- Strategically develop our private labels, including brand positioning, product storytelling, and Go-to-Market concepts.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A completed degree in Marketing, Communications, Business Administration, or a similar field.\n- Several years of professional experience in marketing, ideally within e-commerce or a digital, performance-oriented environment.\n- Proven experience in developing and leading brands, ideally including private labels.\n- A deep understanding of digital channels, consumer insights, and modern brand management principles.\n- Strategic foresight combined with a pragmatic, hands-on mentality.\n- Demonstrated creativity, strong communication skills, and decisiveness.\n- Excellent German and English language proficiency.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a driven Senior Marketing Manager to make a significant impact in a rapidly growing and innovative company. You'll be empowered with overall strategic responsibility and direct influence on key business areas like private labels. The culture is described as \"family-friendly\" with flat hierarchies, offering ample room for creative ideas and personal growth. Beyond a competitive salary, the company genuinely invests in employee well-being with flexible working hours, modern offices, numerous perks like free drinks and snacks, regular team events, and attractive benefits such as employee discounts and a Job-Bike. If you're looking for a role where your strategic vision and hands-on approach directly contribute to tangible growth and brand evolution, this could be your next big move.
