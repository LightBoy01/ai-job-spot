### Description
At ju:niz Energy, we believe **no storage means no energy transition.** For over 10 years, ju:niz Energy has been a pioneer in grid-serving large-scale battery storage systems. Our systems stabilize the grid, create flexibility, and secure a climate-neutral energy supply. What sets us apart: We invest in our projects – and retain them in our portfolio for the long term.

To optimally steer our growth financially and strategically, we are looking for a **Teamlead Investments & Project Finance (m/w/d)**. In this central role, you will lead a small, high-impact team and take responsibility for our business cases, the financial controlling of our battery storage projects, and the structuring and management of our project financings. You will work closely with the CFO, Development, Delivery, and Finance teams, ensuring our projects are economically strong, scalable, and future-proof.

### Responsibilities
*   Develop, evaluate, and maintain business cases across all project phases – from initial idea to operational readiness.
*   Take responsibility for the controlling and reporting of our battery storage projects during the development, construction, and operational phases.
*   Structure and support project financings, as well as ongoing management of existing financing structures (including communication with banks).
*   Build and further develop a standardized evaluation and reporting framework for all projects and entities.
*   Consolidate technical and commercial data into insightful decision-making documents for management and investors.
*   Contribute to the further development of long-term financial and investment strategies (“Monaco-Modell”).
*   Ensure close coordination with the Development, Delivery, Operations, and Energy Trading departments.
*   Lead, coach, and develop your team.

### Qualifications
*   Completed degree in Financial Economics, Energy Economics, Business Mathematics, Industrial Engineering, or a comparable education.
*   At least 5 years of professional experience in Project Finance, Business Planning, Investment, or Controlling – ideally within the energy or infrastructure sector.
*   Profound knowledge in Financial Modelling, Cashflow Analysis, Project Valuation, and Economic Feasibility Studies.
*   Experience in structuring and negotiating financings (equity and debt, banks, investors).
*   Proficient in Excel, BI-tools (e.g., PowerBI, Tableau), and ideally basic knowledge of Python or SQL.
*   Strong analytical skills, structured thinking, and high results orientation.
*   Excellent communication skills, pragmatic approach, and ability to present complex topics clearly and persuasively.
*   Independent, proactive work style, coupled with strong leadership qualities.
*   Very good German and English language skills.
