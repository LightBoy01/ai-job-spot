### Description\nAre you ready to transform numbers into impactful decisions? Join a leading social enterprise in Essen, Germany, which operates vital social facilities nationwide for young people, homeless individuals, and refugees. As a (Senior) Financial Controller, you will be a crucial link between finance and management, bringing your experience to bear and influencing strategy directly. This role is not just about analysis; it's about proactively shaping strategy and business development by identifying opportunities and mitigating risks, allowing you to act as a true architect of financial success and social impact.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Advise management on financial decisions and ensure transparency in achieving objectives.\n- Develop and optimize pricing and cost structures.\n- Take responsibility for budget planning, forecasts, cash reporting, and ongoing controlling throughout the year.\n- Monitor revenue realization, invoicing, and receivables.\n- Build strong business relationships and foster cooperative collaboration.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed business administration degree (e.g., in Business Administration, Economics, or Accounting).\n- Minimum of 5 years of professional experience in a comparable position, ideally within an international service company.\n- Proven experience collaborating with executives and working within contract and offer environments.\n- Excellent knowledge of Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A).\n- Demonstrated entrepreneurial thinking, integrity, and strong teamwork skills.\n- Very good command of both German and English languages.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptionally impactful Senior Financial Controller opportunity within a mission-driven social enterprise. The role offers substantial influence over strategic financial decisions that directly contribute to the company's vital work in supporting vulnerable communities across Germany. The organization prioritizes professional development through coachings and further education, offers flexible working hours, a secure, permanent contract, and a competitive compensation package that values your expertise. It's an ideal fit for an experienced finance professional eager to combine their analytical skills with a meaningful social cause in a dynamic and supportive environment.
