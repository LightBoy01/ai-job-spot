### Description\nAre you passionate about not just supporting clients, but actively leading their projects to success? Do you want to design solutions that drive overall strategy and create real added value? Do you love managing processes, deadlines, and meticulously reviewing results? Then you've come to the right place!\n\nWe are looking for ambitious Project Managers who want to grow beyond the classic PM role. As a Consultant Projects & Solutions, you will be the central interface between client, team, and project goal. Additionally, you will have the opportunity to not only implement projects operationally but also to think strategically, take on responsibility, and shape exciting healthcare projects that create real impact.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Lead client meetings and provide strategic advice: You are the central point of contact for our healthcare clients.\n- Oversee and evaluate marketing and sales materials: From conception to final delivery, you ensure that all materials align with the communication strategy.\n- Conduct quality control: You check roll-ups, ads, and all communication media for customer identity and ensure 100% alignment.\n- Develop solutions with clients: Together, you will create assets, ads, social postings, and other measures.\n- Manage internal teams: You coordinate creation, timelines, and budgets, ensuring seamless alignment.\n- Steer projects from A to Z: From offer creation to implementation and final delivery, you maintain an overview.\n- Ensure customer centricity: You are the guardian of brand consistency and strategic alignment.\n- Your focus: You are the central interface with a strong agency focus, bringing together client needs, strategic requirements, and operational implementation – while ensuring the highest quality standards.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Agency Experience:** You bring several years of experience from an agency environment and understand the dynamics that shape projects, clients, and teams there. Experience in the healthcare or pharmaceutical sector is a big plus.\n- **Client Consulting & Account Management:** You know how to build, trustfully maintain, and strategically develop client relationships. You keep both client needs and agency goals in mind, confidently manage projects and contacts, and act as a reliable interface between the client and the internal team.\n- **Project Management Competence:** From the initial offer to budget and resource planning to final delivery, you manage projects in a structured and proactive manner. You keep deadlines, to-dos, priorities, and coordination rounds securely under control, ensuring high quality.\n- **Design Evaluation Competence:** You can professionally assess layouts, graphics, and other client materials and evaluate whether they align technically, aesthetically, and strategically with client needs.\n- **Very good German language skills in written and spoken:** You communicate clearly, professionally, and flawlessly, both in presentations and client meetings, as well as in emails, offers, or briefings.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a highly dynamic and impactful role for an ambitious Project Manager looking to grow into strategic consulting. The company offers exceptional growth opportunities with dedicated mentoring, transparent development plans, and an \"excellence bonus\" for strong performance. You'll be empowered to take responsibility from day one, contributing to significant healthcare projects in a collaborative environment that values initiative, interdisciplinary teamwork, and flexible work arrangements including home office. Even if you don't tick every box, they encourage you to apply and convince them of your potential.
