### Description\nThe TIM Company GmbH is a rapidly growing marketing and consulting team specialized in medical tourism and performance marketing. We operate data-driven, fast-paced, and with high-quality standards. This Entry-Level position offers direct insights into real campaigns, ensuring a steep learning curve where good ideas are implemented immediately. We are a multicultural team with clear processes, yet ample room for individual responsibility. Ideal for students or those seeking practical entry into online marketing, this role offers flexible working hours, remote possibilities (with optional meetings in Berlin), quick onboarding, and the chance for long-term collaboration.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Daily monitoring and quick checks of our landing pages (errors, links, display, content).\n- Recording, maintaining, and entering data into Sheets/CRM (e.g., leads, reports, content lists).\n- Creative collaboration in the social media area (ideas, post preparation, content plan contribution).\n- Support with video, ad, and text drafts (hooks, captions, variants, briefing contribution).\n- Keyword analysis in Google Ads (checking search terms, clustering, exclusion, marking potentials).\n- Daily monitoring of KPIs in Google Ads and Social Media and reporting of anomalies.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Very reliable, structured way of working and daily availability for short checks.\n- Basic understanding of online marketing / social media / performance (basics are sufficient – we provide training).\n- Proficient use of Google Sheets or Excel.\n- Good German language skills (written and spoken); English is a plus.\n- Eagerness to learn, quick comprehension, and enthusiasm for practical implementation.\n- Initial experience with Google Ads, Meta Ads, landing pages, Canva, or CapCut.\n- A feel for social media trends / short videos.\n- Interest in KPI tracking and optimization.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an aspiring online marketing professional, particularly those with an academic background or eager to kickstart their career with practical, hands-on experience. The TIM Company offers a highly supportive yet autonomous environment, perfect for learning and immediate impact. With a focus on real campaigns, clear processes, and flexible work arrangements, this role is ideal for someone who is reliable, detail-oriented, and ready to dive deep into performance marketing. If you're looking for a place where your contributions matter from day one and your ideas are quickly brought to life, this role in a data-driven, multicultural team is for you.
