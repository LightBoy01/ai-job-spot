### Description
We are seeking a dedicated and organized **HR Administrator (part-time)** to join our client's team in Rostock. In this pivotal role, you will be the backbone of our HR operations, ensuring smooth administrative processes and contributing to a positive employee experience. This position offers flexibility with a 20- to 30-hour work week and includes the option for home office.

### Responsibilities
*   Maintain and manage personnel files and HR data meticulously within our HR systems.
*   Draft and prepare essential HR documents, including employment contracts, certificates, references, and other personnel-related paperwork.
*   Support the onboarding process for new employees, ensuring a welcoming and seamless integration into the company.
*   Coordinate appointments, manage communication with both internal and external stakeholders, and handle general administrative tasks within the HR department.
*   Actively participate in process optimization initiatives and take on special projects or tasks as needed to enhance HR efficiency.

### Qualifications
*   Successfully completed a commercial apprenticeship (e.g., Industrial Clerk, Office Management Clerk, Wholesale Clerk, or a specialization in HR) or possess a comparable qualification.
*   Ideally, you bring at least two years of professional experience in HR administration, human resources, or as an HR Assistant.
*   Proficiency in HR software and a strong command of MS Office programs are required.
*   Demonstrate a structured, meticulous, and discreet approach to your work; you are also characterized by your independent, proactive, and goal-oriented nature.
*   Excellent German language skills are essential to effectively communicate and manage documentation.
