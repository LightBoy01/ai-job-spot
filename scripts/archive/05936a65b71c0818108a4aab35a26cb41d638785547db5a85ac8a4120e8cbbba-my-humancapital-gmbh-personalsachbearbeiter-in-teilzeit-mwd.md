### Description\nOn behalf of our client in Rostock, we are seeking a dedicated and organized **Part-time HR Administrator (m/f/d)** to join their team as soon as possible. This role is ideal for someone looking to contribute to a supportive and flexible work environment, managing essential HR administrative tasks and contributing to a positive employee experience.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a Part-time HR Administrator, your core responsibilities will include:\n* Maintaining and managing personnel files and HR data within the company's systems.\n* Preparing and issuing employment contracts, certificates, references, and other HR-relevant documents.\n* Supporting the onboarding process for new employees to ensure a smooth and welcoming integration.\n* Coordinating appointments, handling communication with internal and external stakeholders, and managing general administrative tasks within the HR department.\n* Actively participating in process optimization initiatives and taking on special tasks and project-related topics as required.\n\n### Qualifications\nTo be successful in this role, you should possess:\n* A completed commercial apprenticeship (e.g., Industrial Clerk, Office Management Clerk, Wholesale Clerk, or a specialization in Human Resources) or a comparable qualification.\n* Ideally, at least two years of professional experience in HR administration, human resources, or as an HR Assistant.\n* Proficiency in HR software and strong command of MS Office programs.\n* A structured, meticulous, and discreet working style.\n* The ability to work independently, proactively, and goal-oriented.\n* Excellent German language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis opportunity offers a fantastic blend of administrative HR duties with a chance to genuinely impact employee experience and departmental efficiency. The emphasis on flexibility, with part-time hours (20-30 hours/week) and home office options, makes it highly appealing for those seeking a healthy work-life balance. You'll be joining a team that values collaboration, professional development, and quick decision-making in a flat hierarchy. If you're a detail-oriented HR professional with a proactive mindset and strong German language skills, this role provides an excellent platform to grow and contribute within a supportive environment. The chance to contribute to process optimization means your ideas for improvement will be valued, making this more than just a routine administrative position.
