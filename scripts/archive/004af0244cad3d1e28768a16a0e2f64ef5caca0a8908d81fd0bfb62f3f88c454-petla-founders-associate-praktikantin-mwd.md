### Description
Petla is a rapidly growing Berlin-based startup with a clear mission: to rethink veterinary care. We offer a platform for online appointment bookings, enabling veterinary clinics and practices to digitalize their processes – from appointment scheduling and anamnesis to customer communication. The result: less administrative effort, more satisfied pet owners, and more time for medicine.

We are looking for a Founder’s Associate Intern (m/f/d) to strengthen our team. As a Founder’s Associate, you will work directly with the founding team and gain deep insights into all areas of a fast-growing startup.

### Responsibilities
- **Business Development:** Identify, approach, and qualify new practices and partners. Build lead lists, manage initial conversations, and support collaborations with associations or networks.
- **Customer Success & Onboarding:** Guide new customers from the first demo to successful platform usage and assist with setting up online appointment booking.
- **Marketing, SEO & Traffic Generation:** Contribute to landing pages (Framer), blog articles, SEO, Google Business Profiles, and lead generation campaigns. Support Google Ads, content assets, and performance data analysis.
- **Community Building:** Support the organization of info sessions, webinars, small events, and the establishment of an active veterinarian community.
- **Operational Work in the Founder Team:** Support research, market analyses, process and structure development, KPI tracking, and smaller strategy projects.

### Qualifications
- You are currently studying in a business, technical, or a related field.
- You have already gained experience in startups, venture capital, or consulting.
- You work independently, learn quickly, and are flexible in a dynamic environment.
- And most importantly: You have a heart for animals! :)
