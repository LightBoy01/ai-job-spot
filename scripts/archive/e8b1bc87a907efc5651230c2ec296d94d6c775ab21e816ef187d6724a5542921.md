**Department overview:**  
Nomura's Finance department oversees the company's financial operations and provides strategic guidance for business decision-making. **Role description:**

We are seeking an experienced and strategic AEJ Finance COO. This pivotal role will work closely with the CFO to drive finance strategy, manage change initiatives, and strengthen control & oversight and operational excellence across the finance function. The ideal candidate will have a strong technical background in finance, exceptional leadership and influencing skills, and the ability to navigate complex organizational structures. This challenging role offers the opportunity to play a crucial part in shaping the future of finance operations and driving strategic initiatives. 

If you are a strategic thinker with a passion for finance transformation and have the skills to influence a high-performing team in a complex, global environment, we encourage you to apply for this exciting position.

**Responsibilities:** 

*   Finance Strategy and Transformation:
    *   Define and Dive Transformation program for Finance
    *   Organisational Redesign, Operating Model Optimization
    *   Automation / Digitization of data and reporting
    *   Outsourcing Strategy
    *   AI implementation in finance 
*   Change Management and Project Oversight:
    *   Regional strategic legal entity optimization project
    *   Manage finance book of work and oversee project portfolios
    *   Track progress, establish accountability and timelines for initiatives
    *   Coordinate efforts within finance and across other functions (Risk, Operations, Business, Technology)
*   Process Improvement and Re-engineering:
    *   Collaborating with functional leads to review and challenge existing processes
    *   Ensure efficiency and control in all finance processes
    *   Partner with technology teams for automation initiatives
*   Finance Control and Oversight:
    *   Oversee the control framework for the entire finance function operational risk, RCSAs, and internal audit issue follow-ups
    *   Control heatmaps and escalation processes
    *   Documentation of processes and controls
    *   Proactively assess and stress-test the control environment
    *   Oversee and coordinate new business approval processes
    *   Business Continuity Management
*   Cross-functional Collaboration:
    *   Navigate complexities and manage conflicts within the finance department
    *   Act as a decisive referee in case of stalemate
*   Executive Communication and Reporting:
    *   Assist CFO in preparing presentations and finance updates monthly communications to the broader finance team
    *   To senior regional management and headquarters and board meetings
*   Process Improvement and Re-engineering:
    *   Collaborate with functional leads to review and challenge existing processes
    *   Ensure efficiency and control in all finance processes
    *   Oversee process documentation efforts
    *   Partner with technology teams for automation initiatives
*   Budget Management & Cost Optimization:
    *   Planning Budgeting (resources & projects); Cost management; Headcount management
    *   Identify & implement cost save opportunities
*   People Management and Development:
    *   Serve as the main point of contact for HR Business Partners
    *   Conduct talent mapping in collaboration with the Finance Management Team
    *   Drive people engagement and communication strategy 

**Skills, experience, qualifications, and knowledge required:**

*   Minimum 15 years of experience in technical finance roles (Product / Financial Control, Treasury etc.), with at least 5 years in a senior leadership position including in similar COO role in the financial industry
*   Experience leading large-scale transformation projects relating to operating model redesign, outsourcing, process re-engineering and digitization / automation
*   Experience with financial systems and technologies, including AI and automation tools
*   Leadership skills with ability to influence and collaborate across corporate support and business stakeholders at all levels
*   Outstanding communication and interpersonal skills to influence broader finance organization
*   Excellent executive presentation, storytelling, analytical and problem solving skills
