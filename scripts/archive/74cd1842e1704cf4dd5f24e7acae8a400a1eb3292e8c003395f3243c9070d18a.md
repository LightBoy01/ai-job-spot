**About the Role**

We are seeking a **high-potential Technical Business Analyst** based in **Hyderabad** who is passionate about technology and excited to work at the intersection of business needs and IT delivery. In this role, you will play a critical part in delivering impactful solutions within our Life & Health reinsurance business. This is not your average BA role — you’ll be deeply involved in **process improvements**, data analytics, **quality assurance**, and driving forward automation and transformation initiatives.

**Key Responsibilities**

**Business Analysis**

*   Partner with business stakeholders to gather, analyze, and document detailed business and system requirements.
*   Translate business needs into clear and concise technical specifications.
*   Facilitate workshops and stakeholder interviews to validate solution design and requirements.
*   Support data dictionary and metadata governance processes for analytical and operational datasets.
*   Work closely with developers, architects, and data engineers to align delivery with business expectations.

*   Define test scenarios, acceptance criteria, and detailed test cases in coordination with business and technical teams.
*   Perform hands-on functional, regression, and data testing of new features and data pipelines.
*   Coordinate UAT activities and obtain business signoffs.
*   Investigate defects and data inconsistencies, working closely with dev teams for resolution.
*   Help shape and enforce automated testing practices and CI/CD pipelines.
*   Be a proxy to business users in testing the application

**Qualifications**

*   Bachelor's degree in information systems, Computer Science, Business, or related field.
*   4-12 years of experience as a Business Analyst or Software Quality Assurance Analyst, preferably in insurance/reinsurance or financial services.
*   Working knowledge of **SQL**, data validation, and working with structured datasets (e.g., actuarial or treaty data).
*   Familiarity with tools such as **JIRA, Azure DevOps, Postman**, or automation frameworks (e.g., Selenium, PyTest) is a plus.
*   Demonstrated understanding in requirements gathering, test planning, test execution, and defect management.
*   Experience with reinsurance or insurance operations is highly desirable.

**Key Traits**

*   **Tech-forward mindset**: Curious, adaptable, and enthusiastic about learning and leveraging new tools.
*   **Detail-oriented**: Thorough with requirements and testing to ensure accurate, high-quality delivery.
*   **Communicative**: Comfortable bridging business and technical stakeholders with confidence and clarity.
*   **Proactive problem solver**: Capable of independently investigating issues and proposing solutions.
*   **Collaborative team player**: Values feedback, continuous improvement, and cross-functional teamwork.
*   **Global mindset**: Appreciates being part of a diverse team from around the world.

**Why Join Us**

*   Be part of a growing **Hyderabad-based** team supporting global reinsurance initiatives.
*   Work with one of the world's leading reinsurance firms, deeply involved in shaping the future of Life & Health products.
*   Join a collaborative, innovation-driven IT team that values experimentation, quality, and continuous learning.
*   Gain exposure to both legacy systems and modern cloud-native architectures.
*   Develop professionally through internal training, mentorship, and challenging project work.
