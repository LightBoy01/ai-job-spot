We are now looking for a suitable person to fill the following position in the **School of Nursing and Health Sciences**:

  

****Major Duties and Responsibilities****

The appointee will be responsible mainly for the following:

*   Involving in the daily operation of Clinical Nursing Education Centre and Clinical Simulation Education Units;
*   Assisting in preparation and facilitation of teaching and learning activities of School of Nursing and Health Sciences;
*   Assisting in purchasing orders and keeping an inventory;
*   Ensuring smooth running and laboratory safety; and
*   Performing any other duties as assigned.

**The appointee will be required to work under different schedules due to specific operational requirements which might be scheduled on weekday or weekends.**

****Candidates****

Candidates should possess the following qualifications, experience and competence:

*   A Higher Diploma or above in Nursing Studies or equivalent (practiced as Enrolled Nurse or Registered Nurse);
*   A good command of written and spoken English & Chinese;
*   Good computer proficiency;
*   Good communication, interpersonal and organisational skills; and
*   Working experience in providing simulation training or handling medical equipment procurement is an advantage.

Candidates may be invited to attend a written test.

**Terms and Conditions for Appointment**

An attractive remuneration package, including basic salary, an end-of-contract gratuity, and a discretionary incentive payment, where applicable, will be offered to the right candidates.
