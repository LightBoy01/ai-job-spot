### Description\nAt Preventicus GmbH, we are dedicated to creating the health solutions of tomorrow, today. We develop innovative mobile and wearable products for the healthcare sector, focusing on the prevention of strokes.\n\nThis internship offers you a comprehensive insight into **Software Testing across Web, Mobile, and Wearables** (Smartwatches). You will learn to test software based on specifications and contribute to real-world projects. Upon successful completion, there is an opportunity to develop a topic for a Bachelor's or Master's thesis, or to continue working with us long-term as a working student. This internship is available immediately for a minimum duration of 2 months.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Support the QA team in ensuring the quality of web and mobile applications.\n- Create, maintain, and execute test cases for functional, system, and regression tests.\n- Conduct development-accompanying testing for B2C and B2B products in mobile and web-based applications.\n- Independently log, process, and maintain tickets in Jira.\n- Prepare requirements and test case reviews, as well as test reports.\n- Assist in the implementation of automated functional tests.\n- Collaborate with the development team to identify defect impacts and support root cause analyses.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Currently enrolled as a student in Computer Science, Business Informatics, or a comparable field.\n- Strong interest in test-driven software development.\n- Technical understanding of automated test processes.\n- Familiarity with technologies such as JavaScript, TypeScript, PHP, Git, and Jira is a plus.\n- Excellent communication skills, high motivation, and a strong sense of initiative.\n- Structured and methodical working approach.\n- Very good German and English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent entry-level opportunity for a student passionate about making a real impact in healthcare technology. Preventicus offers a supportive and collaborative environment, with dedicated mentorship and clear pathways for continued growth, whether through a thesis project or a student worker role. You'll gain hands-on experience in critical software testing for cutting-edge web, mobile, and wearable health products, all while contributing to a vital mission of stroke prevention. The focus on knowledge transfer and a positive team culture makes this an ideal place to start your career in software quality assurance.
