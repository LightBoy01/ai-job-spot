### Description
Stadtritter protects people, buildings, and assets with modern security technology – from alarm systems to video surveillance. We aim to digitize our processes, data flows, and systems to make our services faster, safer, and simpler. As a Digitalization Manager, you will shape our digital strategy and play a crucial role in making our company fit for the future. If you are passionate about making a difference and implementing digitalization hands-on, you're a perfect fit for us.

**Location:** Berlin (until April) / from April: Potsdam-Babelsberg • Full-time • No remote work.

### Responsibilities
*   Develop and implement the digital strategy in close collaboration with management.
*   Analyze and digitally map all company processes – from sales to technology and service.
*   Introduce and manage Zoho One as the central data and process platform (CRM/ERP).
*   Identify digital tools and innovative integration possibilities (e.g., automations, interfaces, digitalization).
*   Monitor digitalization progress using KPIs, reports, and dashboards.
*   Lead and coordinate digitalization projects across various departments.
*   Support teams through the change process – from training to implementation and change management.
*   Assess the feasibility of AI functionalities and gradually introduce new digital technologies.

### Qualifications
*   Professional experience in digitalization, process management, or system implementation.
*   Understanding of operational processes and inter-departmental interface communication.
*   Knowledge of CRM/ERP systems (experience with Zoho One is a plus).
*   Analytical thinking, conceptual strength, and a structured, methodical approach to work.
*   Strong communication skills, empathy, and openness to change processes.
*   Very good German language skills, both written and spoken.
