The Royal Melbourne Hospital is one of Australia’s leading public healthcare providers. Join the team. Build a career. We are looking for a passionate and dedicated individual to join The Royal Melbourne Hospital.

**Administrative Assistant - Respiratory and Sleep Medicine**   
**The Royal Melbourne Hospital** 

*   **Part Time Ongoing Position**
*   **64 hours per fortnight**
*   **End to end administrative management of outpatient referrals**

We are a team committed to working with all stakeholders to ensure effective and efficient delivery of services to a diverse population of patients.   The Respiratory and Sleep Medicine Department administrative team enjoys a collaborative working environment where staff enjoy a great place to work and where the department meets patient expectations as a great place to receive care.

**Your new role:**

The Respiratory and Sleep Medicine department is seeking an Administrative Assistant. Reporting to the Administrative Team Leader, the Administrative Assistant is a key member of the customer service team and is responsible for the end-to-end administrative management of the patient's outpatient episode of care, including but not limited to

*   Referral management
*   Appointment scheduling
*   Waitlist management and auditing
*   Phone, online and health hub enquiries
*   Front desk reception duties

**About you:**

*   You have acute hospital experience with a collaborative working style
*   You like to work autonomously and as part of a team
*   You have effective communication skills and a passion for continued professional development.

**Benefits:**

*   Be part of a supportive and values-driven workplace culture
*   Career growth opportunities, ongoing training, and professional development
*   Access to Health & Wellbeing programs including Fitness Passport, Employee Assistance Program (EAP), and Staff Peer Support
*   Salary packaging, on-site car parking, and great public transport access
