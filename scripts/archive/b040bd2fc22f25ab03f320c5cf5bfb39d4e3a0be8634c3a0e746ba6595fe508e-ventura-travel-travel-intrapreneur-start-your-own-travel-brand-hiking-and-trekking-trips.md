### Description

Are you a passionate trailblazer with a deep love for the outdoors, ready to turn your vision into a thriving business? Ventura TRAVEL invites you to become a **Travel Intrapreneur**, an entrepreneurial leader who will conceptualize, launch, and scale your very own niche hiking travel brand. This unique "company within a company" model empowers you to lead with full autonomy, supported by Ventura TRAVEL's robust infrastructure—including expert teams in marketing, finance, IT, and operations.

We're seeking experienced professionals with an entrepreneurial spirit, strong ethical compass, and extensive knowledge of hiking tourism. You'll focus on crafting extraordinary trekking and hiking tours across the world's most breathtaking landscapes, delivering truly superior experiences that replace ordinary mass tourism with authentic adventures.

Ventura TRAVEL is a global network of tourism experts and a highly recognized employer, ranked #14 in Germany's Best Employers 2024 by _ZEIT_ and _Kununu_, and #2 in Tourism in Germany by _Most Wanted Employer 2025_. We foster a culture of freedom and trust, where you're supported, not micromanaged, and encouraged to collaborate with a diverse, international team. You'll benefit from a flexible workspace, whether in our vibrant Berlin office or remotely, and have access to existing customer bases eager for your unique offerings.

To learn more about the Intrapreneur program, explore these resources:

### Responsibilities

As a Travel Intrapreneur, you will:

*   **Grow your hiking brand:** Develop and scale your new hiking-focused travel brand into a success story.
*   **Build partnerships:** Create strong relationships with local guides, mountain lodges, and regional partners to craft authentic hiking experiences.
*   **Design unforgettable tours:** Develop meaningful trekking itineraries—from gentle nature walks to challenging multi-day treks.
*   **Optimize your website:** Ensure your brand's website inspires adventurers with authentic stories, expert content, and beautiful hiking imagery.
*   **Strategize marketing:** Collaborate with Ventura TRAVEL's Marketing team to attract the right audience for your hiking adventures.
*   **Delight travelers:** Put hikers at the center of everything you do, offering world-class service and attention to detail.
*   **Lead your team:** Build and lead a motivated team that will help you grow your hiking brand sustainably.

### Qualifications

**Required Skills & Experience:**

*   **Entrepreneurial mindset & passion for hiking:** You live and breathe travel, adventure, and the great outdoors.
*   **Leadership & project management:** Strong leadership skills and the ability to manage complex projects efficiently.
*   **Communication & networking:** Excellent communication skills and the ability to connect with partners, guides, and hikers alike.
*   **Language skills:** Proficiency in English (company language).
*   **Application requirement:** All applications must include a CV in English.

**Preferred (Nice-to-Haves):**

*   **Tourism expertise:** Several years of experience in hiking or outdoor travel operations.
*   **Analytics savvy:** Good understanding of Google Analytics, Data Studio, or similar tools.
*   **Team leadership:** Prior experience leading or mentoring teams.
*   **Destination knowledge:** Fluency in the language(s) of your hiking destinations or target markets.
*   **CRM experience:** Familiarity with systems like HubSpot.
*   **Additional skills:** Any other expertise that could benefit your hiking brand or Ventura TRAVEL as a whole.
*   **Additional languages:** German, French, Spanish, etc., are a plus.
