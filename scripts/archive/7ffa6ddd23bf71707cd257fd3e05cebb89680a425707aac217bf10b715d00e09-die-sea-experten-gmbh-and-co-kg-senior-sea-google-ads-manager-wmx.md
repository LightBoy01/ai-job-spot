### Description
Join Die SEA-Experten, a leading Google Ads agency in the DACH region, recognized as a Google Partner with over 15 years of experience. Our team of 20 dedicated professionals specializes in driving measurable revenue, qualified leads, and sustainable growth for businesses through expertly crafted and continuously optimized SEA campaigns. As a Senior SEA Manager, you will be at the forefront of strategic campaign execution, client relationship management, and contributing to the growth of both our clients and our team. We pride ourselves on data-driven strategies, highest quality standards, and continuous performance optimization in a dynamic and ambitious environment.

### Responsibilities
*   **Strategic Campaign Management**: Plan, implement, and optimize Google Ads campaigns across Search, Display, Video, and Performance Max, with a strong focus on performance, efficiency, and ROI.
*   **Independent Client Management**: Take full ownership of client relationships, including in-depth analysis, expert consulting via phone & video calls, and continuous development of their SEA strategy.
*   **Data-Driven Recommendations**: Derive actionable, data-based recommendations by identifying market trends, potential growth areas, and new opportunities for clients.
*   **Collaborative Project Execution**: Work closely with the team and management to efficiently implement complex client projects.
*   **Mentoring & Knowledge Transfer**: Support the team in skill development and actively share your extensive know-how to elevate collective expertise.

### Qualifications
*   **Extensive Experience**: At least 4 years of proven experience in Google Ads / SEA (Search, Display, YouTube, Performance Max).
*   **Performance Marketing Expertise**: Deep understanding of performance marketing principles and a strong data-driven approach to work.
*   **Strategic & Analytical Mindset**: Ability to recognize patterns, comprehend market dynamics, and clearly interpret complex data to inform strategy.
*   **Strong Communication & Client Focus**: Confidently advise clients at decision-maker level and build trust through your expertise and communication skills.
*   **Independent Work Ethic**: Structure your projects autonomously and proactively contribute ideas for further development and improvement.
