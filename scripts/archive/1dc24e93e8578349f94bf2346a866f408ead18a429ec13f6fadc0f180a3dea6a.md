**About the role**
We are seeking a detail-oriented, confident, and experienced individual to join our team as a Business and Technical Support Analyst. The Business and Technical Support Analyst will serve as a vital link between business needs and technical solutions, combining analytical thinking and AI-driven approaches with hands-on support to ensure smooth operations, effective communication, and continuous improvement across systems and processes.
**Key Responsibilities**
------------------------
**A. Innovation Technology Business Analyst**
\- Gather and analyze business requirements to identify opportunities for innovation and process improvement.
\- Translate business needs into clear innovation technical support deliverables and propose effective solutions.
\- Collaborate closely with KPMG departments to drive discussions, align objectives, and identify effective solutions.
**B. Technology Support**
\- Provide first-level support for internal applications, tools, and automation solutions.
\- Assist in designing, testing, and optimizing AI prompts for internal tools.
\- Support on solution design for innovation technology solutions that address business needs and drive digital enablement.
**C. Project Management Support**
\- Support to track progress and ensure timely follow-up on action items with KPMG departments.
\- Act as a bridge between business team and technical teams to ensure clear communication.
\- Maintain accurate documentation of project updates, requirements, and deliverables.
**Qualifications & Experiences**
--------------------------------
*   Bachelor's degree in information technology, Business Information System, Innovation Technology, Economics or a related field.
*   Minimum 1 year in a hybrid role involving business analysis, technical support, or project coordination. Exposure to AI tools, automation, or solution design is a plus.
*   Be able to think both as a user and a business owner.
*   Good analytical and critical thinking.
*   Good presentation and documentation skills.
*   Good communication, and stakeholder management skills.
*   Passionate about exploring innovative technologies and translating knowledge into practical, value-driven solutions.
*   Skilled in the use of Microsoft Office.
