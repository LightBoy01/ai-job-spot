### Description
Join our IT Administration team at a dynamic tech consulting company with offices in Germany (NRW and Berlin) and a subsidiary in Vietnam. Our corporate group develops customized software and cutting-edge AI solutions for a diverse international client base.

In this pivotal role, you will be the cornerstone of our stable IT infrastructure, taking full responsibility for its administration and continuous development. You will manage the implementation and maintenance of Microsoft 365, oversee critical cloud infrastructures, ensure reliable hardware and software provisioning, and provide essential IT support to all employees. You will work closely with our IT service providers, actively contributing to the ongoing optimization of internal IT processes and collaborating on a future-proof IT strategy with management. If you are passionate about technical excellence, thrive in a dynamic environment, adopt a structured approach, and can clearly communicate complex technical concepts in both German and English, we encourage you to apply.

### Responsibilities
As a key member of our Corporate IT team, your responsibilities will include:

*   **Infrastructure Management:** Directly manage our internal IT infrastructure and services.
*   **Microsoft Cloud Expertise:** Take full ownership of our Microsoft Cloud infrastructure, including MS365, EntraAD, and Exchange concepts.
*   **Hardware & Software Provisioning:** Install and configure hardware systems and software applications essential for employee productivity.
*   **System Lifecycle Management:** Support and manage changes within our internal IT system landscapes throughout their entire lifecycle (e.g., rollout, migration, change, and release management).
*   **Strategic IT Development:** Administer and continuously develop our IT infrastructure, including co-developing the overarching IT strategy with management and establishing robust internal IT structures.
*   **Compliance & Security:** Observe and enforce prescribed compliance rules across the company.
*   **Vendor Collaboration:** Collaborate effectively with our external IT service providers.
*   **IT Procurement:** Procure and manage IT equipment.
*   **User Support:** Act as the primary point of contact for IT support, resolving IT questions and problems for all employees.

### Qualifications
We are looking for a candidate with a strong foundation and practical experience:

*   **Education:** Successfully completed vocational training or a degree in computer science, system engineering, or a comparable qualification.
*   **Professional Experience:** Demonstrated professional experience and strong knowledge in operating and administering Microsoft products, specifically:
    *   AzureAD / GPOs (Microsoft 365)
    *   SharePoint / OneDrive Online (Microsoft 365)
    *   Exchange Online (Microsoft 365)
    *   MS Teams
*   **Hardware & Software:** Experience with current hardware and software, including troubleshooting and fault rectification.
*   **IT Security:** Basic knowledge of IT security principles for secure service setup.
*   **Microsoft Ecosystem (Ideal):** Ideally, knowledge of Microsoft's partner program and licensing.
*   **Automation (Plus):** Know-how in the MS Power Platform for automating routine administration tasks is a significant plus.
*   **Heterogeneous Environment:** Experience in administering Macs and Windows computers in a mixed environment, along with skills in automated deployment of laptops.
*   **Mobile Device Management:** Knowledge of MS Intune.
*   **Problem-Solving:** Strong ability to independently develop solutions with high problem-solving competence.
*   **Work Style:** Excellent technical understanding, service- and solution-orientation combined with an analytical, structured, and diligent working style.
*   **Collaboration:** Enjoys close collaboration with service providers, clients, other developers, and the team; adaptable to different constellations.
*   **Language Skills:** Fluent in German and English, capable of clearly communicating technical and complex contexts to various stakeholders (management or client level).

### Logistics & Work Model

*   **Travel Readiness:** Occasional business trips within the DACH regions (by agreement with the client).
*   **Work Model:** Hybrid (2 days office & 3 days mobile work), with fully on-site options available; arrangement is individual and dependent on current company work phases. Ideally, you can come to our office in Kamp-Lintfort twice a week.
*   **Working Hours:** 32+ hours per week, weekdays.
*   **Start Date:** By individual arrangement.
*   **Contract Type:** Unlimited.
