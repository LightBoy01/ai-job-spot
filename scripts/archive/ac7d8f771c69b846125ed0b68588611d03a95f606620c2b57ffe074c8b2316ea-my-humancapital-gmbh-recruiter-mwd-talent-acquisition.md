### Description
Join the MHC-Crew, a company recognized by Focus Online as the fastest-growing in Southern Germany, dedicated to shaping the future of work by supporting numerous companies in the Munich area throughout the entire Employee Life Cycle. As a Recruiter (m/w/d), you will be a core part of the recruiting team, quickly taking ownership of your own projects. At MHC, teamwork is paramount, and success is celebrated together, ensuring a fun and engaging environment. This role is for someone eager to actively shape the future of MHC, bring new ideas, and thrive in a dynamic, supportive atmosphere.

### Responsibilities
*   **Career-Coaching:** Engage with applicants, primarily by phone, to guide them through exciting new job opportunities and plan individual career paths. Act as the primary point of contact for all questions regarding the application process.
*   **Job-Coaching:** Organize interview appointments and meticulously prepare candidates for specific job interviews. You will also be responsible for optimizing resumes.
*   **Active Sourcing:** Utilize direct search methods to identify and uncover top talent. Proactively and precisely approach potential candidates and conduct initial telephone interviews.
*   **Feedback Discussions:** Conduct essential feedback discussions with candidates after they have met our partner companies, complementing the interview preparation.
*   **Idea Management:** Your input is highly valued! MHC is continuously evolving, and you will actively contribute to its future by bringing in your ideas and suggestions (e.g., in employer branding, process design, etc.).

### Qualifications
Beyond a successful education/degree or relevant professional experience, you will also convince us with the following character traits:
*   **Positive Mindset:** You love learning new things and taking on responsibility. You're eager to step out of your comfort zone and grow beyond yourself.
*   **Focus:** You know what you want and possess the necessary assertiveness and a high degree of self-initiative.
*   **Eagerness to Learn:** You understand that mistakes are part of learning. You are courageous, enjoy trying new things, and always think solution-oriented.
*   **Enthusiasm:** Positive vibes are never in short supply with you. You inspire others with your energy and are always up for a laugh.
