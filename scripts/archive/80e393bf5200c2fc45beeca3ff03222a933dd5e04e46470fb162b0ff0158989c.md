As a Mentor with C10 Labs, you will play a pivotal role in guiding and accelerating early-stage startups across multiple industries. We pride ourselves on fostering a dynamic AI ecosystem that promotes creativity, execution, and growth. Mentors are seasoned leaders—founders, operators, investors, or technologists—who provide high-impact guidance to founders navigating the journey from idea to scale.

  
You’ll have a front-row seat to breakthrough innovation, with opportunities to become **early advisors, board members, or fractional CxOs** within our portfolio companies. You’ll also gain access to exclusive C10 programs, events, and our global network of investors, corporates, and academics.  
  

  

Key Responsibilities
--------------------

*   Provide strategic mentorship to portfolio companies, leveraging your **domain expertise, operating experience, and network** to drive meaningful outcomes.  
      
    
*   Support founders in refining business strategies, market positioning, product roadmaps, and go-to-market approaches.  
      
    
*   Act as a trusted advisor—helping startups overcome challenges, seize opportunities, and build resilient organizations.  
      
    
*   Share perspectives on **market trends, customer needs, regulatory landscapes, and competitive dynamics** across industries.  
      
    
*   Participate in workshops and cohort programming, enhancing founders’ skills in areas such as fundraising, business development, product scaling, and team building.  
      
    
*   Facilitate access to potential partners, investors, and customers within your network.  
      
    
*   Provide ongoing feedback and insights to the C10 investment and operating teams to strengthen our venture-building approach.  
      
    

  

Skills, Knowledge and Expertise
-------------------------------

*   Proven track record as a founder, operator, investor, or industry leader in enterprise, health, bio, mobility, robotics, fintech, or related domains.  
      
    
*   Experience mentoring or advising early-stage or high-growth startups.  
      
    
*   Strong professional network and ability to make meaningful introductions to investors, partners, and customers.  
      
    
*   Knowledge or familiarity with of **state-of-the-art (SOTA) AI models** (e.g., foundation models, multimodal AI, agentic systems) and their **applications across industries**.  
      
    
*   Understanding of venture capital, startup ecosystems, and the dynamics of scaling companies.  
      
    
*   Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to deliver constructive feedback and strategic guidance.  
      
    

  

Benefits
--------

  

*   Shape the trajectory of the next generation of AI-driven companies.  
      
    
*   Work in an intellectually stimulating and collaborative environment.  
      
    
*   Leverage your expertise to make a lasting impact on founders and industries.  
      
    
*   Gain access to exclusive C10 global professional network, events and a broad, cross-sector network of innovators and investors.  
      
    
*   Explore opportunities for deeper engagement with ventures as advisor, board member, or fractional executive.
