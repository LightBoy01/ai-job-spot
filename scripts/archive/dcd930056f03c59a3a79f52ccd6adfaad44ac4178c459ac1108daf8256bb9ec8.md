**Location 📍:** Sydney, Australia (Hybrid - 3 Days In Office)

**About Us** 🚀

At Relevance AI, our mission is to empower anyone to delegate work to the AI workforce.

We’re building a new type of automation platform, for anyone to create and use AI agents that can replicate human quality work, decision making and collaboration.

We are scaling fast to meet unprecedented customer demand and we need exceptional talent to help us grow. If you’re ready to be part of a trailblazing company at the cutting edge of AI, this is the place for you.

**The Role 🥳**

We’re seeking talented AI engineers (fullstack) to join our team and build next-generation AI-powered applications. This role would be ideal for individuals who are excited by the intersection of software development and applied AI.

This is not a traditional ML engineer/ MLops role. Your day to day will be varied and you’ll work on building real, user-facing AI features—powering workflows, tools, and experiences that leverage large language models and agents across the product. Expect to dive deep into prompt engineering, AI-powered backend services, and full stack implementations (APIs, microservices, and frontends alike).

You’ll collaborate closely with a visionary product and engineering team, contribute to our open-source SDKs, expand internal agent frameworks, and shape the future of AI agent interfaces and infrastructure.

**Your impact💥**

*   **Develop Agents and Multi-agent systems:** Design and implement AI agent and extend existing agents with new capabilities. This includes managing the agent’s context (using techniques like sub-agents, retrieval based context management, sliding context windows, etc.).
    
*   **Develop Tools for agents:** Develop complex tools for agents like computer use, browser use, etc.
    
*   **Multi-agent orchestration and tool calling:** Research and develop systems on how agents should collaborate, and how agents should call tools.
    
*   **Fullstack development:** Build and maintain production-grade APIs and AI-powered features, from backend services to user-facing experiences.
    
*   **Evaluate and improve:** Evaluate AI’s performance using evaluations and tests to then iterate on prompts, agent tools and agents orchestration to improve output quality and reliability.
    

**What We’re Looking For** 🧠

*   4+ years in full-stack software development, ideally developing practical and production-ready AI applications or features that utilise Gen AI/ LLMs / RAG.
    
*   Strong programming skills in Python and Javascript, including experience using AI-assisted coding tools.
    
*   Deep understanding of foundational models, prompt engineering, and fine-tuning techniques.
    
*   Understanding of software design, service architecture, and scalable systems.
    
*   Passion for building AI-enhanced user experiences, not just theoretical ML solutions.
    
*   Prior start-up or tech company experience is a plus—especially where you’ve worn multiple hats or shaped early products.
    
*   Bonus: Side projects or open-source contributions involving building AI agents, RAG pipelines, or open-source LLM tooling.
    

**Our Tech-Stack:** Javascript • Typescript (Node.js) • Python • React/ Vue.js • Express.js • LLMs
