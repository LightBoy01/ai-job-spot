**Job Description:**

**The Opportunity:** 

Mattel Games Studios is seeking an analytical and results-driven Performance Marketing Manager to join our growing mobile marketing team. In this role, you will be responsible for acquiring high-quality users across our diverse portfolio of mobile games. You will leverage your deep expertise in performance marketing, campaign optimization, and ASO to develop and execute robust growth plans. As a core member of the growth team, you'll collaborate closely with creative and analytics specialists, managing substantial budgets to deliver impactful results and drive global expansion for Mattel’s iconic brands. 

**What Your Impact Will Be:** 

*   Plan, execute, and optimize multi-channel UA and retargeting campaigns across Meta, Google UAC, TikTok, Apple Search Ads, DSPs, and emerging channels like CTV and influencer partnerships. 
    

*   Manage multi-million-dollar monthly budgets, collaborating with Marketing Analytics on strategic allocation to maximize profitable growth and LTV/ROAS targets. 
    

*   Design and execute A/B and multivariate tests on audiences, bids, and funnels, translating results into scalable performance gains. 
    

*   Partner with creative teams to develop, test, and scale high-performing static, video, UGC, and experimental formats informed by performance insights. 
    

*   Increase app store visibility and conversion through keyword optimization and asset A/B testing (icons, screenshots, videos) using Sensor Tower and Data.ai. 
    

*   Analyze and interpret attribution data from MMP (Singular) and privacy-first frameworks (SKAN, Privacy Sandbox) to guide strategic decisions. 
    

*   Collaborate with product, live-ops, monetization, and analytics teams to align UA and re-engagement with in-game events, content releases, and monetization goals. 
    

*   Build and maintain strategic relationships with platform reps, network partners, and agencies to secure betas, negotiate deals, and explore new traffic sources. 
    

*   Continuously monitor trends in UA, growth marketing, and competitor strategies to proactively test and scale new channels and formats. 
    

**Qualifications:**

**What We’re Looking For:**

*   3–5 years of experience managing UA and retargeting campaigns for mobile F2P games, with a proven track record of delivering profitable growth. 
    
*   Demonstrated expertise in multi-channel media buying across major advertising platforms (Meta, Google UAC, Apple Search Ads, TikTok, DSPs, mobile ad networks). 
    
*   Proven ability to manage and strategically allocate multi-million-dollar monthly budgets to maximize LTV and achieve ROAS targets. 
    
*   In-depth knowledge of App Store Optimization (ASO), including keyword strategy, metadata refinement, creative A/B testing, and conversion rate optimization. 
    
*   Hands-on experience working within privacy-centric frameworks (SKAdNetwork, Privacy Sandbox) and leveraging MMP data (Singular, AppsFlyer, Adjust) to measure and optimize performance. 
    
*   Highly iterative marketer with a data-driven, “test-and-learn” mindset, comfortable operating in fast-moving, ambiguous environments. 
    
*   Exceptional communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to clearly present data-driven insights and strategies to cross-functional teams and executives. 
    
*   Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Economics, Business, Analytics, or a related field (or equivalent experience). 
    
*   Experience with AdMon is a plus. 
    
*   Demonstrated a growth mindset by staying curious and continuously learning, embracing challenges, and improving themselves.
