### Description\nWe are seeking a dedicated Private Client Advisor / Bank Client Advisor (m/w/d) for our partner company in Frankfurt am Main. In this pivotal role, you will be responsible for the holistic support of a loyal client base, engaging with them personally, via phone, and digitally. You will advise on a comprehensive range of financial products, develop tailored financial plans, actively acquire new clients, and contribute to marketing initiatives to optimize the client experience.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Provide holistic support for a fixed client base – personally, by phone, and digitally.\n- Advise clients on current accounts, loans, investments, securities, and pension products, developing individual financial plans.\n- Take responsibility for active new client acquisition and the expansion of existing client relationships, tailored to their financial goals.\n- Collaborate on target-group-oriented marketing campaigns, utilizing modern advisory tools and digital channels to optimize the consulting experience.\n- Meticulously document consulting discussions and results, work closely with internal teams, and contribute to the further development of advisory processes.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed training as a Bankkaufmann (m/w/d) or a comparable qualification (e.g., Finanzberater, Kundenberater Bank, Kaufmann für Versicherungen und Finanzen).\n- Initial professional experience in private client consulting or bank sales, ideally with securities and credit products.\n- Good knowledge of account management, digital advisory formats, and ideally experience with financial & advisory tools.\n- Demonstrated sales talent, strong communication skills, customer orientation, and a solution-oriented, structured working style.\n- Excellent German language skills are essential.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a proactive and client-focused financial professional ready to thrive within a market-leading organization in Frankfurt am Main. The company offers a highly supportive 'Du-Kultur' where collaboration and trust are paramount. You'll benefit from an exceptional work-life balance with 32 days of holiday and a subsidized Deutschlandticket, alongside a modern work environment equipped with the latest tools. Crucially, the role comes with significant opportunities for continuous professional development, ensuring long-term career growth and stability. If you're passionate about building lasting client relationships and contributing to innovative financial solutions, this role provides the perfect platform.
