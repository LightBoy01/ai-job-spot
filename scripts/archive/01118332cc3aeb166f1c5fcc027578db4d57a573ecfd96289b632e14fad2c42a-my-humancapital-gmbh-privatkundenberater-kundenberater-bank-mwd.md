### Description
On behalf of our partner company in Frankfurt am Main, we are seeking a **"Private Client Advisor / Bank Client Advisor (m/f/d)"** to join their team as soon as possible. In this pivotal role, you will be responsible for the comprehensive and holistic support of a dedicated customer base, engaging with clients personally, over the phone, and through digital channels. You will be instrumental in developing individual financial plans and driving both new client acquisition and the expansion of existing relationships.

### Responsibilities
*   Provide holistic management and advice to a dedicated client base, interacting personally, via phone, and digitally.
*   Advise clients on a range of financial products, including current accounts, loans, investments, securities, and pension products, to develop tailored financial plans.
*   Actively engage in new client acquisition and strategically expand existing customer relationships, aligning with their financial objectives.
*   Collaborate in designing target-group-oriented marketing campaigns, leveraging modern advisory tools and digital platforms to enhance the consulting experience.
*   Diligently document all consultations and outcomes, work closely with internal teams, and contribute to the continuous improvement of advisory processes.

### Qualifications
*   Completed training as a Bank Clerk (m/f/d) or a comparable qualification, such as a Financial Advisor (m/f/d), Bank Client Advisor (m/f/d), or Insurance and Finance Clerk (m/f/d).
*   Initial professional experience in private client advisory or bank sales, ideally with a focus on securities and loan products.
*   Possess strong knowledge of account management and digital advisory formats, with a preference for experience using financial and advisory tools.
*   Demonstrate strong sales acumen, excellent communication skills, a customer-centric approach, and a solution-oriented, structured working style.
*   Hold very good German language skills.

### Author's Take
**Question 1: How does this role truly make a difference in clients' lives, beyond just managing accounts?**

That's a fantastic question, and it gets right to the heart of what we do here. You're not just processing transactions; you're truly shaping financial futures. Imagine guiding a young couple through the complexities of their first mortgage, helping a seasoned professional plan for a secure retirement, or enabling a small business owner to access the capital they need to grow. Every conversation is an opportunity to build trust, understand deep-seated financial aspirations, and then craft bespoke strategies that genuinely empower our clients. The impact is tangible, personal, and incredibly rewarding – you're a financial partner, not just a service provider.

**Question 2: The job description mentions a 'valuing work culture' and 'trust from leadership.' What does that look like in daily practice?**

It's more than just buzzwords; it's woven into the fabric of our daily interactions. Our 'Du-culture' (first-name basis) isn't just a formality; it reflects a genuinely flat hierarchy and open lines of communication. Leadership here empowers us to own our client relationships, trusting us to make informed decisions that benefit both the client and the bank. This means you have a significant degree of autonomy in how you manage your portfolio and approach your workday. We foster an environment where ideas are encouraged, collaboration is key, and successes are celebrated together. If you have an innovative idea to enhance a process or improve a client's experience, your input is genuinely valued and can lead to real change. It's a place where you feel respected, heard, and truly part of a team striving for collective excellence.
