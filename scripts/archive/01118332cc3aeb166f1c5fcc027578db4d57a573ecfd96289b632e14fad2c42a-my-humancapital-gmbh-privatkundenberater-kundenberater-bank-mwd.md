### Description\n\nOn behalf of our partner company in Frankfurt am Main, we are seeking a dedicated **Private Customer Advisor / Bank Customer Advisor (m/f/d)** to join their team at the earliest possible date. This is an exciting opportunity to become a vital part of a market-leading financial institution, offering long-term stability and a modern, supportive work environment. As an advisor, you will play a crucial role in empowering clients to achieve their financial goals through comprehensive and personalized guidance.\n\n### Responsibilities\n\n*   Provide holistic advice and support to a dedicated portfolio of private customers, utilizing personal, telephone, and digital channels.\n*   Advise clients on a wide range of financial products, including checking accounts, loans, investments, securities, and pension plans, developing tailored financial strategies.\n*   Actively engage in new customer acquisition and strategically expand existing client relationships, aligning with their specific financial objectives.\n*   Contribute to the design and execution of target group-oriented marketing initiatives, leveraging modern advisory tools and digital platforms to enhance the client experience.\n*   Meticulously document all consultations and outcomes, collaborate closely with internal teams, and contribute to the continuous improvement of advisory processes.\n\n### Qualifications\n\n*   Successfully completed training as a Bank Clerk (Bankkaufmann/frau) or an equivalent qualification, such as a Financial Advisor, Bank Customer Advisor, or Insurance and Finance Clerk.\n*   Possess initial professional experience in private customer consulting or bank sales, ideally with a focus on securities and credit products.\n*   Demonstrate strong knowledge of account management, digital advisory formats, and ideally experience with various financial and consulting tools.\n*   Exhibit proven sales talent, excellent communication skills, strong customer orientation, and a solution-oriented, structured approach to work.\n*   Proficiency in German at a very high level (very good Deutschkenntnisse) is essential.\n\n### Author's Take\n\nThis role offers a fantastic blend of client interaction, strategic financial planning, and personal growth within a highly stable and modern banking environment. If you're looking for a long-term career where your contributions are valued, and your work-life balance is respected, this is a prime opportunity. The company stands out with its commitment to employee well-being, offering a generous 32 days of holiday and subsidized public transport. You'll thrive in a supportive 'Du-Kultur' where trust from leadership and positive collaboration are key. With ample opportunities for further education and development, you're not just taking a job; you're building a future at a market leader known for its crisis-proof stability. This is ideal for someone passionate about making a real difference in clients' financial lives while continuously advancing their own professional journey.
