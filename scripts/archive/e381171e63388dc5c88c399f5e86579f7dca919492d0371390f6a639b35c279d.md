**The Job You Will Perform:**

Miscellaneous labor associates report to the production supervisor or superintendent for the department to which they are assigned.  The Position is primarily HOUSEKEEPING in a production environment.

\- following all safety rules and practices;

            - performing assigned housekeeping duties to insure that production needs are met;

            - assisting other associates as needed;

            - accurately using tape measures and other measurement and test equipment;

            - accurately completing time sheets, reports, lists, and other records;

            - keeping the supervisor informed of any problems and/or unusual situations;

            - maintaining work areas to plant standards of housekeeping;

            - maintain general housekeeping throughout the plant;

            - complete daily assignments/task, per Housekeeping Log in a timely manner;

            - must learn to operate a fork truck under I.P. Requirements.

            - maintaining teamwork with machine crews and other associates; and

            - performing other duties as assigned by supervision.

**The Skills You Will Bring:**

**Education & Work Experience**

The qualifications for miscellaneous labor associates are the ability to perform the duties/responsibilities of the job with or without accommodation; the ability to read, write, and speak English; the ability to accurately read a measuring tape and ruler; the ability to use basic shop math; the ability to learn; and the ability to follow instructions, ask questions, and work effectively as a member of a team.

Must pass AON GM Pre-Assessment with Recommended Status 

Stable, verifiable work history in similar environment
