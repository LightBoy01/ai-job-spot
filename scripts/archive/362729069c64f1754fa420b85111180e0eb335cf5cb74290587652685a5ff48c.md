Overview:

Pwrteams is currently looking to hire a **Junior Research Analyst for** **Nortal**, our mother company, to join their Central Research Desk - a fast-moving internal service that supports regions and teams across Sales, Delivery, and Strategy with on-demand research and insights. ​

​

You will work closely with stakeholders across the organization to deliver high-impact research that informs proposals, client engagements, and strategic decisions.​

​

**Nortal** is a strategic innovation and technology professional services company with an unparalleled track record of delivering successful transformation projects for over 25 years. With a deep understanding of technology, strategy, and human behaviour, we craft digital solutions that drive meaningful change and deliver results. Our projects have touched more than half a billion people's lives worldwide from Seattle to Riyadh.​

​

**This is a part-time role, ideal for someone early in their career who is curious, analytical,** **and eager to learn.**

Responsibilities:

*   Respond to research requests submitted via Jira, ensuring timely and high-quality delivery​
*   Conduct desk research and information gathering using internal knowledge bases, external sources (e.g. Gartner), and public data​
*   Collect and synthesize information on markets, technologies, competitors, and regulations from reliable sources​
*   Summarize findings into clear, actionable insights tailored to business needs​
*   Translate research findings into concise, presentation-ready slides and one-pagers; apply templates and visual standards consistently
*   Maintain and update the CRD SharePoint page and internal documentation​
*   Support internal marketing of the desk (e.g. newsletter content, success stories)​
*   Organize and tag sources, templates, and outputs to enable easy reuse​
*   Share regular updates, flag risks early, and present results clearly in non-technical language​
*   Collaborate with Sales Operations and Delivery teams to understand evolving needs

Qualifications:

*   Strong analytical and writing skills​
*   Ability to synthesize information from multiple sources​
*   Strong written communication and presentation skills​
*   Comfortable working independently and managing multiple requests​
*   Proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word, PowerPoint, Excel)​
*   Fluent in English (written and spoken)  
      
    **Brownie points:**  
      
    
*   Experience with research tools (e.g. Gartner, Statista, LinkedIn Insights)​
*   Familiarity with Jira or other ticketing systems​
*   Interest in business strategy, sales, or consulting​
*   Previous internship or work experience in a research or analyst role​
*   Basic knowledge of using AI tools for research (summarization, extraction, first drafts) and awareness of their limitations​
*   Well organized and reliable: able to scope small tasks, estimate effort, and deliver on time
