### Description\n\nKSP is a dynamic firm comprising approximately 75 professionals across two locations in Düsseldorf and Munich, offering the full spectrum of tax consulting and auditing services. We cater to a diverse clientele, ranging from regional mid-sized companies to international corporations. What truly sets us apart is our focus on larger and internationally positioned companies, supported by a multilingual team that advises clients directly in their native languages. However, if your preference lies with national mid-sized clients, you'll find an equally strong fit here.\n\nBeyond our client focus, our unique culture is paramount: flat hierarchies, short decision-making paths, and a strong sense of togetherness are not just buzzwords but lived realities. Everyone knows everyone, we're on a first-name basis, and we highly value an open, appreciative working atmosphere – because work is simply more enjoyable when the team spirit is right.\n\nAs a Senior Auditor (Wirtschaftsprüfer), you will serve as an expert point of contact for clients, teams, and decision-makers. You will independently lead annual and consolidated financial statement audits, advise mid-sized companies and international subsidiaries, and contribute your expertise to special projects. Your approach will be practical, solution-oriented, and committed to delivering measurable quality.\n\n### Responsibilities\n\n- Holistic client support for mid-sized companies and international corporate subsidiaries, primarily in industry, trade, and services.\n- Lead and be responsible for annual and consolidated financial statement audits according to HGB and IFRS, from project execution to results presentation.\n- Conduct Due Diligences, company valuations, and special audits independently or as part of a team.\n- Opportunity to contribute and develop your own specializations, for example, in sustainability reporting, internal quality assurance, or financial services audits.\n\n### Qualifications\n\n- Successfully completed a degree in economics or business administration and passed the German Public Auditor (Wirtschaftsprüfer) exam.\n- Relevant professional experience in auditing and consulting companies.\n- Profound knowledge of HGB and IFRS, coupled with strong business acumen.\n- Structured, analytical work style with a strong client-oriented approach.\n- Fluent communication skills in German and English. Optionally, further develop your English skills with the support of our in-house English trainer.\n\n### Author's Take\n\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a certified and experienced Senior Auditor seeking a challenging yet deeply supportive environment. KSP stands out with its focus on diverse, often international, clients, providing ample scope for complex audit work and specialized project contributions. The company's culture is a major draw, emphasizing flat hierarchies, strong teamwork, and genuine appreciation, coupled with attractive benefits like above-average compensation, flexible working hours, and extensive individual development opportunities. If you value a modern workplace, a familial team, and a commitment to well-being (including sports and team events), KSP offers a compelling career path where you can truly grow and make an impact.
