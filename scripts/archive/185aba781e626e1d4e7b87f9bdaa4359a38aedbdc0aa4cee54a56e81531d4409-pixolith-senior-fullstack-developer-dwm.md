### Description
As a Senior Fullstack Developer, you assume a key technical role connecting customers, project management, the development team, and the demands of modern e-commerce platforms. Complex shop systems (especially Shopware 6) are familiar territory for you: You think in stable architectures and translate technical challenges into powerful, scalable solutions.

You operate where code, concept, and business converge – with the aspiration to develop platforms that convincingly unite customer experience, performance, and economic benefit. \"Value instead of effort\" is the compass that guides you.

You work in a cross-functional squad of digital experts – fast, self-reliant, solution-oriented. Silos? Not your mindset. You want project success, not boundaries of responsibility.

### Responsibilities
- Continuously develop and evolve Shopware platforms.
- Develop clean, performant, and extensible plugins and themes.
- Smartly integrate external systems, APIs, and data flows.
- Solve technical challenges collaboratively within the team.
- Ensure code quality, standards, and reviews with attention to detail.
- Keep documentation, technical guidelines, and developer notes concise and up-to-date.
- Monitor and evaluate e-commerce trends, new tools, and Shopware innovations.
- Advise clients, conduct workshops, and share knowledge.
- Provide support for technical questions as part of good teamwork.

### Qualifications
- Solid proficiency in PHP, JavaScript, MySQL.
- Deep knowledge of Shopware 6 in its core, plugins, and frontend.
- Mastery of HTML, CSS (SASS/LESS), and frameworks like Vue or React.
- Think in object-oriented principles and design patterns.
- Work in a solution-oriented, goal-driven, and structured manner.
- Live a collaborative mindset and a cooperative spirit.
- Naturally curious, eager to learn, and willing to share knowledge.
- Naturally accept criticism, reflect on it, and act independently.
- Capable of moderating, presenting, and advising.
- Possess open communication, clarity, and empathy.
- Naturally prioritize, work efficiently, and remain flexible.
- Proactivity, self-organization, and personal responsibility are your foundation.
