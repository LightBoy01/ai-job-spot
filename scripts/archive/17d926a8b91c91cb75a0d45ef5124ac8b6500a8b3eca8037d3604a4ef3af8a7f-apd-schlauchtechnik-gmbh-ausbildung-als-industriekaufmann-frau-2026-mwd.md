### Description
Since 1953, SCHAUENBURG Ruhrkunststoff GmbH has been a leading producer of flexible spiral hoses and hose lines for diverse applications, often in areas where hoses are not typically expected. As a proud part of the Schauenburg Hose Technology Group, we bundle versatile competencies in hose technology. We are not an anonymous corporation but a family-like and engaged team, and we are looking for you to join us as an Apprentice Industrial Clerk (m/f/d) for 2026. In this role, you will gain comprehensive insights into all crucial commercial areas and actively contribute to various departments.

### Responsibilities
- You will support various departments and gain insights into all important commercial areas.
- You will process orders, offers, and requisitions, ensuring everything runs smoothly.
- You will assist with bookkeeping and learn how invoices are correctly created and checked.
- You will interact with customers and suppliers – via email, telephone, or directly on-site.
- You will contribute your ideas to improve workflows and make processes more efficient.

### Qualifications
- You have your Abitur or Fachabitur (or are currently working towards it).
- You are keen on economic topics and want to understand how companies truly operate.
- You enjoy working in a team and contribute with fresh ideas.
- You can deal well with people and remain organized even when things are busy.
- You are reliable, curious, and approach your tasks intelligently.
