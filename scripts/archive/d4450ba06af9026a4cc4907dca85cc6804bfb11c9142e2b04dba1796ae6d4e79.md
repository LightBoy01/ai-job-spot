Scholarship Description
-----------------------

One Scholarship . Infinite Possibilities 

  
As the world’s most awarded airline, SIA is looking for scholarship candidates who are passionate about aviation, have strong academic records with co-curricular achievements and leadership qualities. As a SIA Scholar, you will get the opportunity to be rotated across different fields such as commercial, operations and planning based on business requirements.  Overseas opportunities are also available through the Trainee Station Manager and Overseas Manager Schemes. Gain access to a wealth of opportunities for your professional and personal development today!   
   
We invite candidates who are currently doing their undergraduate studies in local autonomous universities  
   
**Course of Study**  
•    All Courses except Architecture, Biomedical, Biotechnology, Dentistry, Law, Medicine, Pharmacy

**Eligiblity**  
•    All Nationalities

•    Undergraduates currently studying in local autonomous universities (i.e. NUS, NTU)  
•    Completed at least 1 academic semester, and not in their final year of study  
•    Strong academic records in University/Secondary School / Institute of Technical Education / Polytechnic / Junior College / International Baccalaureate School (where applicable)  
•    Strong leadership qualities as demonstrated in Co-Curricular Activities (CCAs)   
  

**Bond Period**

•    1 to 2 academic years remaining: 2-year bond  
•    More than 2 academic years remaining: 3-year bond

**Value of Awards**

•    More than 2 academic years remaining: SGD 50,000  
•    1 to 2 academic years remaining: SGD 30,000  
•    Overseas Exchange Allowance  
•    Internship attachments to SIA

**Submission Deadline**  
•    10 March 2026
