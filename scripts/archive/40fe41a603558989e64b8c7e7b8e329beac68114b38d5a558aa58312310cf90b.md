Our Digital Experience team are recruiting for an AI Search & SEO Lead to join them on a permanent basis. Reporting to our Head of Digital Experience, you’ll be tasked with planning and maintaining an integrated optimisation strategy across SEO, GEO, AEO and AIO, to maximise discoverability of our Penguin digital estate.
This role will suit someone who already has solid experience as a SEO Analyst or Lead and is curious, analytically minded and with the ability to adapt to the rapidly evolving search landscape.
**About the department**
The Digital Experience team sits within the Brand department which houses a number of central areas of specialist expertise which support and work closely with our nine publishing houses to connect our books and authors with readers. The department, made up of around 70 people, includes experts from digital marketing, brand, communications, consumer insights and more.
The iconic Penguin brand – the only consumer-facing brand in publishing – sits at the heart of what we do. We have an exciting opportunity to leverage the power of our brand to help new and different readers discover our books and to champion the causes we care about.
**The role**
You will build and maintain systems, protocols and performance indicators in line with our Brand principles, to ensure our content and platforms across our owned channels are optimised to create a seamless user experience.
**Some of your responsibilities include:**
*   Planning and maintaining an integrated SEO strategy in collaboration with our Head of Digital Experience, encompassing SEO, GEO, AEO and AIO.
*   Defining technical, content and AI-specific optimisation best practices, collaborating with our content, product and development teams to implement relevant updates.
*   Guiding content creators in structuring pages and assets for both human and AI readability.
*   Implementing URL, taxonomy, structured data, redirection, content, canonical and AI retrievability strategies.
*   Setting SEO priorities and targets for all Penguin properties based on commercial opportunities and AI ecosystem impact.
*   Performing keyword and prompt-oriented research, incorporating semantic and persona-based approaches that benefit both traditional and AI systems.
*   Owning and maintaining SEO performance dashboards and prepare research and reports to share back key findings with internal stakeholders.
*   Staying up to date with search and AI trends; testing emerging tools and techniques that boost discoverability in generative and answer-based platforms
*   Collaborating cross-functionally to create and refine experiences designed for AI consumption – search, chatbots, voice experiences, and more.
**What you’ll bring**
**Essential criteria:**
*   Significant experience as an SEO Lead or Manager, ideally in digital-first or content-heavy environments.
*   Proven success in SEO best practises and development.
*   Expertise with web analytics and SEO tools including SEMRush, Moz, Ahrefs and Google Search Console and Adobe Analytics.
*   An understanding of emerging AI-powered search technologies (e.g. Google SGE, Bing Copilot, ChatGPT browsing).
*   Working knowledge of GEO, AEO and AIO practices.
*   Familiarity with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Google Data Studio.
*   Strong analytical skills; capable of interpreting both traditional SEO metrics and new AI search signals.
*   Be a critical thinker and problem solver with strong communication and collaboration skills.
*   The ability and willingness to commit to our hybrid working model (below).
