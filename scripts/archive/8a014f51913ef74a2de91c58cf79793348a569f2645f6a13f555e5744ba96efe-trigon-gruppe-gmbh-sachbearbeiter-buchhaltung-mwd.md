### Description\nTrigon Unternehmensgruppe is a long-standing, successful company specializing in the development, leasing, and management of residential, commercial, and hotel properties in central city locations. With approximately 4,000 residential and 300 commercial units, we ensure the long-term value preservation of our real estate portfolio through our in-house facility management.\n\nTo strengthen our accounting team in Berlin, we are seeking an engaged Accounting Clerk to join us as soon as possible. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys working with numbers and structured processes.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Independently manage property accounting.\n- Prepare invoices and support year-end financial statements in collaboration with tax advisors.\n- Maintain and update master data.\n- Process dunning procedures and accounts receivable management.\n- Assist with general administrative tasks.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed commercial training or a comparable qualification.\n- Initial professional experience in real estate or property accounting is an advantage.\n- Analytical mindset, strong affinity for numbers, and good process understanding.\n- Team-oriented, structured, and conscientious work ethic with a high degree of personal responsibility.\n- Proficient in MS Office; ideally, knowledge of common real estate management software.\n- Business acumen and a strong sense of responsibility.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a dedicated Accounting Clerk to join a successful real estate group. The role offers significant personal responsibility and development potential within a professional and collegial team. Trigon values work-life balance, providing a flexible working model, 30 days of annual leave plus additional days off for Christmas and New Year's Eve, a Deutschlandticket, and complimentary drinks and fresh fruit. This position promises a challenging yet supportive environment for growth.
