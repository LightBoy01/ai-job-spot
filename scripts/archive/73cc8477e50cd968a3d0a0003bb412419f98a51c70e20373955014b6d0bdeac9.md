Join our Internal Audit team as a Data Analyst Intern. You’ll support audit projects by analyzing data, building dashboards, and identifying risks and trends that drive better decision-making.

### Responsibilities

Key Responsibilities:

*   Analyze and validate data to support audit objectives. 
*   Build dashboards and visualizations (e.g., Power BI, Tableau). 
*   Assist in automating audit analytics and reporting. Identify trends, anomalies, and potential risks. 
*   Present findings clearly to audit stakeholders.

### Qualifications

Qualifications – External

Requirements:

*   Pursuing a degree in Management Information Systems, Data Science, Computer Science. 
*   Strong analytical and communication skills. Familiarity with Excel, SQL, Python, or BI tools. 
*   Interest in internal audit and data-driven insights. 
*   Interest in or experience with automation tools and AI tools a plus Preferred Experience with data platforms like Snowflake or Microsoft Fabric. 

What You’ll Gain: 

*   Hands-on experience in audit analytics. Mentorship from experienced professionals. 
*   Insight into risk, controls, and corporate governance.
