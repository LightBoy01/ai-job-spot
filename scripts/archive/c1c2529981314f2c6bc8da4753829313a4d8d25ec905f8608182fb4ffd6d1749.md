**Senior Director - Reporting, Data Science, AI Lead**
**Barcelona, Spain.**
**Role Overview**
Lead the enterprise-wide delivery of business intelligence and AI solutions that drive data-informed decisions. This role unifies reporting and data science to deliver trusted insights, predictive and prescriptive modelling, and trustworthy AI, providing sustainable and measurable business value.
**Your Key Responsibilities**
*   Own enterprise BI and reporting services, providing accurate, timely, and user-friendly reports and dashboards.
*   Champion data visualization best practices and manage the pipeline of reporting requests and projects.
*   Lead the identification, development, and deployment of enterprise data science and AI use cases. Ensure alignment with business context, proper integration into business processes, and delivery of intelligent systems that address real business problems.
*   Establish MLOps practices, ensuring models remain reliable and effective over time. Set up processes for model monitoring, maintenance, and version control, guaranteeing AI solutions remain accurate and trustworthy.
*   Implement model governance frameworks, ensuring ethical, compliant, and safe use of AI and analytics. Work with relevant teams to enforce guidelines around data privacy, fairness, and transparency.
*   Foster innovation in AI techniques and develop reusable AI assets.
*   Enable and support the organization to perform analysis independently through self-service tools.
**We offer.**
*   Enterprise-wide influence – shape the future of reporting and AI across all business units with high-impact, data-driven solutions.
*   Innovation with purpose – develop and deploy cutting-edge AI models and BI tools that solve real business challenges.
*   A collaborative ecosystem – work with digital partners, business leaders, and external experts to deliver cohesive, trusted analytics.
*   A culture of trust and ethics – lead with integrity, ensuring AI solutions meet the highest standards of fairness, transparency, and compliance.
*   Growth through leadership – Drive the full lifecycle of analytics products.
*   Empowerment through enablement – champion self-service analytics and elevate data literacy across the organisation.
**You bring:**
*   Proven leadership – experience managing large analytics teams and delivering enterprise-scale BI and AI solutions.
*   Technical depth – strong background in data science, reporting, MLOps, and model governance frameworks.
*   A strong sense of accountability – you take initiative, speak up, and see things through. You are responsible and focus on what matters.
*   An innovator’s instinct – you’re always looking for better, smarter, more sustainable ways to make an impact.
*   A collaborative spirit – you’re at your best when working across teams, functions and countries.
*   Curiosity and an open mind – because you believe progress starts with asking, “Is there a better way?”
