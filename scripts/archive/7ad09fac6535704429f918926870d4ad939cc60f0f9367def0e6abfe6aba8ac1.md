This role offers the opportunity to leverage your cloud-native deployment skills and experience with modern AI platforms to build scalable ML pipelines, LLM-based applications, and intelligent agent frameworks. You will play a key role in accelerating the delivery of innovative solutions for telecom, enterprise, and next-generation autonomous networks. If you thrive in a challenging and rewarding environment, we encourage you to apply.

### Responsibilities

*   Employ MLOps best practices for CI/CD, model deployment, and monitoring.
*   Focus on fine-tuning, prompt engineering, RAG pipelines, and LLM monitoring.
*   Enhance AI/ML systems with Agentic AI frameworks for autonomous decision-making and workflow automation.
*   Handle structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data through ingestion, preprocessing, and feature engineering.
*   Work with cross-functional teams to translate use cases into production-ready deployments.
*   Utilize platforms like GCP (Vertex AI), RedHat OpenShift AI, and Kubeflow across multi-cloud/hybrid environments.

### Qualifications

**You have:**

*   Bachelor's/Master's degree in computer science, Data Engineering, AI/ML, or related field.
*   10+ years in AI/ML engineering, with 5+ years focused on MLOps.
*   Proficient in Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, and SQL.
*   Experience with MLOps pipelines like Kubeflow, MLflow, and Vertex AI.

**It would be nice if you also had:**

*   Experience with Ab-initio data management platforms.
*   Familiarity with telecom data products and autonomous networks.
*   Knowledge of vector databases and retrieval—augmented generation (RAG).
*   Contributions to open-source AI/ML or GenAI frameworks.
