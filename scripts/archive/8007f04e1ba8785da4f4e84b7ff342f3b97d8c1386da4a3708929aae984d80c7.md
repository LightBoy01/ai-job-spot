Overview:

Join Nortal, a global technology consulting company driving digital transformation in governments, healthcare, and enterprises, impacting over half a billion lives worldwide.

We are a global technology consulting company with more than 2,700 employees, delivering transformative digital solutions for leading companies and governments. With over 25 years of experience, we are a trusted digital transformation partner in the public sector, healthcare, and enterprise domains, combining global expertise with local insights to deliver value at scale and speed.

We are seeking a highly skilled Data Consultant to join our Data & AI Studio, serving as a subject matter expert for the Americas region. This role is a blend of delivery leadership and client engagement, focusing on driving impactful outcomes across data strategy, modern data platforms, AI/ML/GenAI adoption, and data governance. 

The ideal candidate combines strong technical expertise with business acumen and executive presence. You will advise C-level and VP-level stakeholders, deliver high-value strategy engagements, and ensure alignment between business goals and technology solutions. 

Beyond delivery, you will support business development activities—contributing to proposals, RFP responses, partnership development, and client pre-sales calls. This position is suited for someone who thrives at the intersection of technology, strategy, and client-facing consulting. 

Responsibilities:

Delivery & Advisory 

*   Lead and deliver data strategy engagements, mapping business priorities to actionable technology roadmaps. 
    

*   Advise clients on modern data platforms (Microsoft Fabric, Azure, Databricks, Snowflake, etc.) and AI/ML/GenAI adoption. 
    

*   Design and execute frameworks for data governance, quality, and compliance. 
    

*   Facilitate executive-level workshops, aligning C-suite and VP stakeholders around strategic outcomes. 
    

*   Translate complex technology concepts into business value language. 
    

Business Development & Partnerships 

*   Support proposal and RFP development, including scoping, solution design, and effort estimation. 
    

*   Join sales and pre-sales calls, positioning our Data & AI services to client executives. 
    

*   Contribute thought leadership, case studies, and best practices to strengthen our market presence. 
    

*   Help identify and grow partnerships with cloud and AI ecosystem providers (e.g., Microsoft, Google, AWS, OpenAI). 
    

Qualifications:

*   8–12 years of experience in data consulting, strategy, or solution delivery. 
    

*   Proven track record leading data strategy and platform projects for enterprise or public-sector clients. 
    

*   Strong knowledge of data architectures, governance, AI/ML lifecycle, and emerging GenAI trends. 
    

*   Ability to interface confidently with C-level and VP stakeholders; excellent communication and facilitation skills. 
    

*   Blend of technical fluency (cloud, data platforms, analytics, AI/ML) and business strategy insight. 
    

*   Experience supporting business development (proposals, RFPs, partnerships). 
    

*   Bachelor’s degree required; Master’s degree in Data, Business, or Technology-related field preferred.
