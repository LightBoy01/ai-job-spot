**In this role, you'll be expected to:**
*   **Manage daily office operations**, including receiving deliveries, maintaining supplies, restocking snacks and coffee, and creating a welcoming environment.
*   **Coordinate with contractors** for office maintenance and upgrades (HVAC, flooring, kitchen, parking, etc.).
*   **Handle asset management**—assigning equipment like laptops and monitors, updating our Asset Management Module, and applying QR labeling.
*   **Support health and safety initiatives** by participating in Joint Health & Safety Committee meetings and implementing workplace safety improvements.
*   **Greet visitors**, sign them in, and ensure they’re connected with their hosts.
*   **Provide administrative support to leadership** including managing calendars, scheduling meetings, preparing agendas, distributing reminders, and overseeing meeting logistics.
*   **Book travel** (flights, hotels, transport) and prepare detailed itineraries, for both in-office and remote team members attending off-site events or conferences.
*   **Assist in coordinating company events** alongside leadership team members.
*   **Document and share meeting outcomes** using AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT transcripts), maintain SharePoint records, update Excel trackers, and send action reminders.
*   **Share weekly office updates** at Monday team meetings (e.g., building work, maintenance, parking, or road closures).
*   **Use and support tools including Microsoft Office Suite** (Outlook, Word, Excel, Teams), SharePoint, BIStrainer, Expedia, and AI tools.
**You might be the right fit if you:**
*   **Are organized** and detail-oriented, with excellent time management skills.
*   **Take initiative** and solve problems before they arise.
*   **Have strong interpersonal and communication skills**, both written and verbal.
*   **Have experience** in administrative, operations, or executive support roles.
*   **Are proficient in Microsoft Office** tools like Outlook, Excel, Word, and Teams.
*   **Feel confident using everyday technology** and have a genuine desire to learn new tools.
*   **Are curious about AI tools** like ChatGPT, even if you haven’t used them yet.
*   **Love helping people** and keeping things running smoothly behind the scenes.
**Bonus points if you**
*   Experience with AI-powered tools.
*   Familiarity with SharePoint or asset tracking systems.
*   Previous experience coordinating office facilities or renovations.
