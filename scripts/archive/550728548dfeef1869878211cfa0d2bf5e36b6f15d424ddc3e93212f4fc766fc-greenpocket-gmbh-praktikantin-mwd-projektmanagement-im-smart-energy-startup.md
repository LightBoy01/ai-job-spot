### Description\nAre you eager to dive into project management, taking on topics independently and with responsibility? Do you enjoy focusing on project controlling, analysis, and resource planning? Then kickstart your career at GreenPocket now!\n\nWe are an agile, green startup specializing in energy management and visualization software, actively shaping a sustainable, climate-neutral future. Our mission is to make energy consumption management intuitive with AI-driven software, making it second nature for everyone – businesses and private households alike. We empower energy providers and corporate customers to effectively reduce both energy consumption and their carbon footprint. With our 35 talented colleagues, we are turning the digitalization of the energy industry into a real opportunity.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Project Management**: As a full-fledged team member, you will work on project planning, execution, and post-processing for renowned companies in the energy sector, telecommunications, and IT industries.\n*   **Stakeholder Management**: You will collaborate closely with product and project management, sales, and development, gaining comprehensive insights into the operations of a software startup.\n*   **Innovation Management**: You will independently bring your own ideas into various project processes.\n*   **Project Controlling**: You will assist in project controlling, contributing to the timely and financially sound execution of projects.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   You are an enrolled student, at least in your 3rd semester, studying Business Administration, Business or Media Informatics, or a comparable field.\n*   Ideally, you bring initial experience in project management, IT, administration, or controlling.\n*   You enjoy evaluations, creating statistics, or project planning, and possess a basic technical understanding.\n*   Initial knowledge of software applications like JIRA and Confluence is desirable.\n*   You are characterized by an analytical mindset, independent action, and structured work.\n*   You have good German and English language skills, as well as good MS Office skills, especially Excel.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for a motivated student to gain hands-on project management experience within a dynamic, purpose-driven green tech startup. GreenPocket offers a highly supportive environment, emphasizing intensive onboarding and continuous learning, ensuring you can quickly develop both personal and professional skills. The company culture values work-life balance, offering flexible hours and home office options, alongside regular team events. If you're passionate about sustainability and eager to make a tangible impact while growing in a collaborative and innovative team, this role provides the perfect platform to launch your career.
