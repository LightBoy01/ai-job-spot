**About the Team:**

LEDH (Loss & Exposure Data Handling) is part of Data Curation and Reporting under Standard Underwriting, Corporate Solutions. We work with colleagues in various functions and locations and take ownership of the Data management responsibilities needed to support deal costing, development and refinement of our costing framework. Corporate Solutions is in a dynamic phase of growth, and we are continuously updating our costing models in support of new products and growing markets. We are seeking individuals who are attracted to work and contribute to a dynamic and growing environment. The role is to support Actuarial services and underwriting by extracting and analyzing claims/loss information of existing and potential clients of commercial business which serves as the basis for performing various analysis and be used further in the value chain for calibration of better rates and for risk assessments. Planning, evaluating, and implementing improvements in business processes and practices; quality assurance and project management.

**About the Role:**

*   Performing end to end processing of loss and exposure data of the companies' part of the respective portfolios.
*   In-depth analysis to update/correct the existing details of the companies
*   Backlog clearance of the respective portfolios.
*   Document management and other administrative tasks in your area of responsibility.
*   Providing analytical insights and producing high-quality work.
*   Proactively maintaining and expanding own expertise level.
*   Identifying process pain points and working with the team to improve the process.
*   Performing any adhoc tasks.
*   Research and Development skills
*   Automation

*   Data Governance Frameworks (DAMABOK, DCAM)
*   Data Quality Management

Experienced data management professional with expertise in data governance, compliance, and data quality frameworks, ensuring regulatory alignment and business value will be an added advantage.

**Project Management:** Supporting of Industry specific projects (small and low complex) assigned which involves:

*   Document the industry specific requirement before the project kick off
*   Coordinating/liaising with key project team members mainly the Underwriters and Actuaries
*   Internal data handling and reporting
*   Deliver project status report to the LEDH management team and project team members on a required/periodic basis
*   Documentation of key project findings and learnings
*   QC as and when required.
*   UAT
