**SEO Specialist**
==================

**Part-time | Remote | LATAM / South Africa**

### **About us:**

At The Global Talent Co., we provide incredible opportunities for the top 5% of global marketers. Join our team and become our employee, gaining access to high-paying salaries, stable employment, and the chance to work with cutting-edge tech companies in Europe and the US.

**Your Role:  
**We’re looking for a sharp, data-driven SEO Specialist to join our in-house team at The Global Talent Co. You’ll take full ownership of our organic growth engine, driving both traditional SEO (technical, on-page, content, and link building) and pioneering new strategies using large language models (LLM SEO). Your mission: increase our visibility in search, attract high-intent candidates and clients, and experiment with AI-driven approaches to stay ahead of the curve.

**Responsibilities:**

*   Conduct SEO audits across technical, on-page, and off-page dimensions to uncover opportunities  
    
*   Perform keyword research and competitor analysis to guide content strategy  
    
*   Optimize site structure, metadata, internal linking, schema, and Core Web Vitals  
    
*   Collaborate with content and marketing teams to produce SEO-driven blogs, landing pages, and resources  
    
*   Build and manage link-building and outreach initiatives to strengthen domain authority  
    
*   Track and report on keyword rankings, organic traffic, and lead generation  
    
*   Diagnose and resolve crawl, indexation, and site performance issues  
    
*   Experiment with LLM-driven SEO, using AI tools for keyword clustering, topic ideation, and meta generation  
    
*   Evaluate and refine AI-assisted content to ensure quality, compliance, and SEO alignment  
    
*   Stay on top of search algorithm updates, AI-driven SEO trends, and industry best practices  
    
*   Present findings and recommendations to the leadership team with clear action steps  
    

**Requirements:**

*   3–6 years of hands-on SEO experience, including technical, content, and link-building  
    
*   Strong proficiency with SEO tools (Ahrefs, SEMrush, Google Search Console, Screaming Frog, etc.)  
    
*   Solid understanding of keyword research, semantic SEO, and content optimization  
    
*   Experience experimenting with LLMs / AI tools in SEO workflows (content ideation, clustering, prompt engineering)  
    
*   Proven track record of improving organic visibility, keyword rankings, and conversions  
    
*   Analytical mindset with experience using Google Analytics, Looker Studio, or similar tools  
    
*   Comfortable working autonomously in a part-time, remote-first environment  
    
*   Organized, proactive, and able to manage multiple priorities within limited hours  
    
*   Bonus: Background in recruitment marketing, talent acquisition, or B2B SaaS marketing
    

If you're a talented marketer looking to take your career to the next level, The Global Talent Co. is your gateway to a bright future in the thriving tech industry. Join us in promoting remote work and unlocking the potential of global talent.
