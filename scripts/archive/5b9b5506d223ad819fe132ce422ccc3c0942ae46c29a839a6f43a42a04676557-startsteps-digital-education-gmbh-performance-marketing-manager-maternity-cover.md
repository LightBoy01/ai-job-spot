**Type:** Full-time

**Location:** Berlin Hybrid

**Start:** ASAP

**Duration:** 12 months

**Employment Type:** 1 year full time contract. Freelance contracts or agency contracts will not be considered for this role.

**About StartSteps**

We help people in Germany upskill for tech roles through industry-aligned training and employer partnerships.

**Role Summary**

You will own paid acquisition across Meta, Google, and TikTok, manage tracking and reporting, and drive predictable lead volume for our tech-career programs funded by Agentur für Arbeit.

Tasks
-----

**Paid Acquisition**

*   Plan, launch, and optimize lead-gen campaigns across Meta, Google, TikTok
*   Manage TOFU/MOFU/BOFU funnels across multiple programs (Orientation, Mentoring, SAP, ServiceNow, AWS, etc.)
*   Build and test creatives; brief UGC creators and editors; ensure fast and cost-effective content production
*   Manage budgets, pacing, and KPIs (CPL, CPA, quality)

**Data, Tracking & Analytics**

*   Maintain tracking setups across all platforms and Pipedrive
*   Ensure clean UTM flow from landing pages to CRM
*   Troubleshoot pixels, events, and CAPI issues
*   Build and maintain Looker/Sheets dashboards (ads + CRM)  
    Report on performance, attribution, and funnel conversion

**Cross-Team Collaboration**

*   Align with Sales on lead quality
*   Coordinate with Program Managers and Partners on new launches
*   Support the launch of webinars and landing pagesWork closely with the Social Media team to align content, messaging, and creative needs across paid and organic

Requirements
------------

**Requirements**

*   Strong experience in Meta, Google, TikTok performance marketing (minimum of 2 years)
*   Proven experience in lead generation, funnel management, and tracking leads accurately in CRM platforms.
*   Solid tracking + attribution skills (UTMs, pixels, CAPI, events)
*   A creative eye for hooks, storytelling, and high-performing ad angles
*   Experience writing short-form ad scripts and briefing content creators
*   Independent, driven, and confident taking full ownership of campaigns with no supervision
*   Hands-on, entrepreneurial mentality, someone who can figure things out, move fast in a startup environment, and confidently outsource tasks if needed.

**Nice to Have**

*   Experience with Pipedrive
*   Familiarity with Bildungsgutschein-funded programs
*   Comfort working with UGC creatorsBasic understanding of tech-career pathways
