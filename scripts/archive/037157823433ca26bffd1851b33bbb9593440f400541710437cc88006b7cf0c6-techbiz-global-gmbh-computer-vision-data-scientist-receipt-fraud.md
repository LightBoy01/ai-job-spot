### Description\nAt TechBiz Global, we connect top talent with leading clients. We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced Data Scientist with expertise in fraud detection to join one of our prominent clients' innovative teams. This role offers a unique opportunity to apply advanced machine learning and data analysis techniques to combat fraud, grow within a dynamic environment, and influence critical product direction.\n\n### Responsibilities\n1. Analyze large-scale receipt data for fraud patterns and anomalies.\n2. Develop statistical methods to detect subtle inconsistencies in receipt data.\n3. Design feature engineering strategies combining OCR, visual embeddings, and behavioral signals.\n4. Build and optimize ML models for fraud detection using collected data points.\n5. Develop fraud scoring algorithms that combine multiple detection signals and model outputs.\n6. Implement threshold optimization strategies balancing precision and recall for different risk levels.\n7. Design comprehensive fraud scoring systems.\n8. Develop weighted scoring mechanisms adaptive to fraud types and retailer patterns.\n9. Create interpretable scoring frameworks for manual review teams.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 4+ years as a data scientist with experience in fraud detection.\n- Strong expertise in hypothesis testing, time series, and anomaly detection.\n- Hands-on experience with classification, ensemble methods, and deep learning (scikit-learn, XGBoost, PyTorch/TensorFlow).\n- Computer Vision - Strong experience with image processing and embedding, specifically EfficientNet and FAISS, is a plus.\n- Experience with high-volume transaction processing and real-time decision systems.\n- Knowledge of retail/e-commerce fraud patterns preferred.\n- Familiarity with document fraud techniques and anti-fraud methodologies.\n- Part-time commitment with flexible hours.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a high-impact role for an experienced Data Scientist passionate about leveraging cutting-edge AI/ML, including GenAI, to solve complex fraud challenges. The client offers a flexible remote work environment, a diverse global team, and significant opportunities to influence product direction and company growth. If you thrive in an innovative setting and want to contribute directly to safeguarding platforms with advanced technology, this position is an excellent fit.
