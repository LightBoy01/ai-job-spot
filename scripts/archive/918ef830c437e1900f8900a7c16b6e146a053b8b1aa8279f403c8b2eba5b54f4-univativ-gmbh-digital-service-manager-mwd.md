### Description\nUnivativ is a project and personnel service provider that connects talented individuals like you with exciting opportunities at renowned companies. Our mission is your career development. We are seeking a skilled and proactive Product Owner (m/f/d) to join our client in the dynamic energy sector in Würzburg. If you are familiar with Product Ownership, Design Thinking, and customer journey mapping, and possess strong analytical and conceptual abilities, this role offers the chance to make a significant impact. You will thrive in an innovative team, contributing to critical projects, continuously expanding your expertise, and shaping the future of energy solutions.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Hold technical ownership for the IT/OT Platform and Incident Detection.\n- Collaborate closely with the Business Product Owner to align priorities.\n- Prioritize and manage the backlog with a strong focus on technical topics.\n- Define and further develop the technical product vision and roadmap.\n- Provide technical consulting for new features and change requests, from ideation to go-live.\n- Ensure transparent communication regarding critical incidents, feature status, and roadmap with specialist departments, IT, and external partners.\n- Be responsible for technical KPIs (stability, performance, availability) and continuous optimization.\n- Plan and control IT costs and IT staffing (internal team / service providers).\n- Ensure the technical scalability and future viability of solutions.\n- Maintain and further develop the service description (scope, SLAs, responsibilities).\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree, e.g., in Business Informatics, Computer Science, Software Engineering, Industrial Engineering, or Electrical/Energy Engineering with a strong IT focus.\n- Experience as a Product Owner, IT Project Manager, or System Engineer is desirable.\n- Ideally, experience in the energy sector (Redispatch 2.0, grid operation, asset management, OT/IT integration).\n- Proficiency in agile methodologies (Scrum, Kanban); certifications such as CSPO/PSPO, Scrum Master, or ITIL are a plus.\n- Ability to communicate clearly and appropriately for the audience – with developers as well as with specialist departments, management, and external partners.\n- High level of personal responsibility, enjoyment of ownership, and the will to shape products long-term rather than just processing tickets.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting Mid-Senior Product Owner role offering deep technical ownership within the critical energy sector. The position requires a blend of strategic vision for technical products and hands-on backlog management, emphasizing stability, scalability, and future-proofing. Candidates will thrive by leveraging their agile expertise and communication skills to navigate complex technical landscapes and diverse stakeholder groups, particularly within energy-specific domains like Redispatch 2.0. Univativ provides a supportive environment focused on continuous learning, well-being, and work-life balance, making this an ideal opportunity for a proactive individual looking to make a tangible impact on cutting-edge IT/OT platforms.
