**The Role**
------------

Rogo's customer success associates are former bankers and investors who guide top financial institutions on how to apply AI in their day-to-day workflows and long-term strategy. You’ll lead client engagements from onboarding through upsell, building consultative relationships and tailoring demos that resonate with investors and deal teams. You’ll contribute to bottom-line growth through upsell identification and churn mitigation.  

**What You Will Own**
---------------------

*   Lead product onboardings and workshops tailored to the context of various financial firms, showcasing Rogo's features and benefits relevant to each prospective client’s potential use cases.
    
*   Developing 1:1 relationships with users across accounts to build trust, uncover customer needs, and ensure each user sees tangible value from the product, ultimately driving higher adoption and long-term revenue retention.
    
*   Establish yourself as a credible expert in solving customers’ specific problems (e.g., public company financial research, M&A advisory, company diligence workflows).
    
*   Drive bottom-line revenue success by using customer performance metrics to 1) identify and deliver on upsell opportunities and 2) identify and prevent customer churn.
    
*   Partner with the Head of Customer Success to outline key success strategies for retaining and expanding SMB and Mid Market accounts
    
*   Collaborate with the sales team to develop and implement more effective strategies and synthesize customer feedback for the product team through a financial services lens.
    

**What You Will Need**
----------------------

*   1-2+ years of front-office experience at a top-tier investment bank, private equity firm, or hedge fund.
    
*   Outstanding project management skills; extremely organized and detail-oriented.
    
*   Strong presentation and communication skills, whether impromptu on a whiteboard or using prepared presentations and demos.
    
*   Desire to be in a heavily customer-facing role
    
*   Curiosity about AI’s potential to transform financial services.
    

### **Bonus**

*   Early experience at a hyper-growth startup

*   LLM knowledge (can you explain how a transformer works?)
