### Description
Particula is the leading rating and monitoring platform for digital assets, dedicated to making this market more accessible, secure, and transparent for institutional investors. We empower issuers, trading facilities, banks, and asset managers to build trust, mitigate risks, and optimize capital allocation. Joining us means shaping the future of the financial world with us!

We are seeking a highly motivated and detail-oriented Research Analyst with a strong background in financial analysis and credit ratings. In this pivotal role, you will be instrumental in advancing our cutting-edge rating and monitoring platform for digital assets. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure our methodologies remain at the forefront of industry standards and deliver actionable insights that directly impact our clients and the evolving digital asset landscape.

### Responsibilities
*   Aggregate and analyze complex data sets to extract meaningful insights, ensuring the accuracy and reliability of all collected data.
*   Prepare and author comprehensive rating reports, encompassing conceptualization, in-depth data analysis, and clear presentation of findings.
*   Coordinate internal requirements and constraints with various stakeholders, including developers, marketing, sales, and external partners, to align product development with business objectives and customer needs.
*   Uphold the highest quality standards for all research activities and data aggregation, ensuring full compliance with relevant laws and regulations.
*   Proactively identify and pursue opportunities for continuous improvement in data collection, automation, analytics, and reporting processes, staying abreast of industry best practices.
*   Enhance our holistic rating framework by continuously analyzing market conditions and trends in digital assets, thereby ensuring the accuracy and relevance of our metrics.

### Qualifications
*   Minimum 5+ years of proven experience in the financial industry, with a strong emphasis on credit risk and credit ratings.
*   Demonstrated expertise in blockchain technology, smart contracts, and tokenized assets, including practical knowledge of Real-World Asset (RWA) applications in digital finance.
*   Proficiency in data analytics, with hands-on experience leveraging analytical tools and methodologies to extract actionable insights. Familiarity with Agile and Scrum frameworks for collaborative project delivery is a plus.
*   Deep understanding of financial markets, investment strategies, and the regulatory frameworks governing digital assets, including emerging standards for compliance and risk management.
*   Exceptional communication and collaboration skills, with a proven track record of effective teamwork with cross-functional teams and diverse stakeholders.
*   Advanced written and verbal communication skills in English, capable of producing clear, comprehensive reports and presentations.
*   Strong analytical and strategic thinking skills, adept at developing and executing plans based on market research, industry trends, and data-driven insights.
*   Excellent problem-solving abilities and high adaptability to new market developments, technological advancements, and evolving industry best practices.
*   *Preferred:* Experience with structured finance risk management practices.
