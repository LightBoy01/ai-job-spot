### Description\nAs part of MediaMarktSaturn Deutschland, Deutsche Technikberatung is on a mission to free Germany from tech frustration. This role offers you the chance to be a key player in this innovative endeavor, leveraging your technical expertise to empower customers with modern technology. From explaining the latest smartphones to installing cutting-edge Smart Home systems and tackling Cyber Security concerns, you'll ensure that everyone can stress-free embrace the digital world.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Provide expert technical assistance and education to customers, making them proficient in all technical matters, from explaining the newest smartphones to installing innovative Smart Home technologies.\n- Reliably manage and update your availability in the scheduling calendar three weeks in advance to ensure timely assignment of customer appointments.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Possess a strong passion for technology and be the go-to person for technical questions in your personal network.\n- Demonstrate excellent communication skills, an open demeanor, and a customer-oriented approach.\n- Be reliable and meticulous in managing your scheduling availabilities.\n- **A personal vehicle is mandatory** for this role, as you will be traveling to customer locations.\n- Have very good command of the German language, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at Deutsche Technikberatung, backed by Europe's strongest consumer electronics retailer, offers a truly unique proposition for tech enthusiasts. The standout feature is the unparalleled flexibility, allowing you to dictate your work schedule, a benefit highly rated by current employees (4.94/5 stars). Coupled with a strong emphasis on work-life balance (4.5/5 stars), continuous professional development, and an attractive, transparent compensation model that includes hourly pay, performance bonuses, and mileage, it’s an ideal fit for someone passionate about simplifying technology for others. If you're a communicative, reliable, and tech-savvy individual looking for autonomy and growth within a supportive, mission-driven environment, this opportunity to make a tangible impact on customers' lives is not to be missed.
