### Description\nThis is an exciting opportunity for a highly experienced and responsible IT System Administrator to take on a lead role in managing and optimizing client IT environments. You will be a critical partner to clients, ensuring robust IT operations, driving strategic improvements, and potentially leading small teams on-site. This role demands a proactive problem-solver with strong technical skills across diverse systems and the ability to translate technical insights into actionable business decisions.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Take full responsibility for managing and optimizing client IT systems, including Windows Servers, firewalls, networks, and specialized application servers.\n*   Lead on-site teams for clients as needed, distributing tasks and ensuring successful project outcomes and operational excellence.\n*   Oversee daily IT operations for clients and, where required, support project teams working on major system transformations.\n*   Develop and maintain comprehensive documentation for client systems and configurations.\n*   Identify, analyze, and present key performance indicators (KPIs) and factual data to clients to support strategic decision-making.\n*   Provide responsive support to clients, including participation in a team-covered on-call rotation.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Successfully completed vocational training or a university degree in IT, or an equivalent qualification, combined with many years of practical professional experience as a responsible IT System Administrator.\n*   Demonstrated expertise across a broad range of IT operational technologies, including Windows Server environments, firewalls, networking, and various applications, with the ability to quickly adapt to new systems.\n*   Proven ability to perform structured problem analysis, develop effective solutions, and communicate complex technical information clearly to users and stakeholders.\n*   Proficiency in developing custom solutions, configuring systems, writing scripts, and automating routine tasks to enhance efficiency.\n*   Strong analytical skills to derive meaningful insights from system data and present them effectively to stakeholders for decision-making.\n*   Solid understanding of project management principles, with the ability to structure and lead both projects and teams.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a challenging yet highly rewarding role for a seasoned IT professional eager to make a significant impact across various client environments. The opportunity to lead teams, drive automation, and influence strategic decisions positions this role beyond typical system administration. If you thrive on solving complex problems, enjoy client interaction, and are looking for a company that values both technical expertise and leadership potential within a supportive team, this role offers a clear path for growth and influence.
