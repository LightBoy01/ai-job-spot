**About the role**
As a Physical Scientist at Lubrizol, you’ll join a dynamic R&D team where your expertise in applied chemistry will directly contribute to the development and deployment of cutting-edge technologies and solutions. This role offers a unique opportunity to apply physical and chemical principles to real-world challenges, collaborate across disciplines, and support business-critical projects. From advancing corrosion and e-mobility initiatives to generating intellectual property and partnering with internal teams, you’ll play a vital role in shaping innovation and delivering impactful results across the organisation.
**About you**
You bring a strong foundation in physical chemistry, a passion for applied science and a continuous improvement mindset based on Artificial Intelligence, potential for automation, and digitalisation. You’re analytical, collaborative, and eager to contribute to cross-functional teams. Your ability to communicate complex concepts and generate meaningful insights sets you apart.
**You will also bring:**
*   A degree in physical chemistry or a related scientific field
*   Experience in applied chemistry within industrial or research environments
*   Strong problem-solving and critical thinking skills
*   An interest in AI, automation, and digitalisation
*   Ability to collaborate effectively across technical teams
*   Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal
*   A passion for innovation, mentoring, and continuous learning
