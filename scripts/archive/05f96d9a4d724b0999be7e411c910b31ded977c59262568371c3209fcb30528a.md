### Description

The Director of Clinical Quality & Analytics will combine operational rigor, advanced analytics, and clinical curiosity to ensure we deliver safe, high-quality, and patient-centered care. This role is for a leader who thrives on turning complexity into clarity, loves building scalable systems, and believes that data is the currency of good decisions. You will shape the future of clinical quality and innovation at Midi, lead analytics at the intersection of AI and patient experience, and build systems that improve outcomes for both patients and clinicians.

### Responsibilities

*   **Clinical AI & Innovation:**
    *   Own the analytics pipeline for our scribe AI tools, translating raw outputs into clear, actionable reports and dashboards.
    *   Develop and analyze data on clinician-patient interactions, identifying training opportunities, operational barriers, and key clinical trends.
    *   Lead process improvement initiatives to strengthen quality, reduce care variation, and measurably improve patient outcomes.
    *   Partner with Product and Operations teams to seamlessly integrate AI-driven findings into clinical workflows.
*   **Quality Program Oversight:**
    *   Own Midi’s comprehensive Quality program, including manual chart audits, the expansion of automated audits, and compliance oversight.
    *   Monitor patient safety events, adverse outcomes, and near-miss reporting, integrating all findings into a continuous improvement feedback loop.
    *   Lead weekly AI-powered Practice Pattern reviews to ensure adherence to clinical protocols and identify coaching opportunities.
*   **Clinical Program & Protocol Development:**
    *   Collaborate with clinical leadership to develop and refine evidence-based clinical protocols.
    *   Design structured 'algorithms of care'—decision pathways that improve consistency and outcomes across our services.
    *   Tailor protocols for distinct clinical services, including menopause, weight management, primary care, and future complex care offerings.
*   **Cross-Functional Leadership & Data Analytics:**
    *   Serve as a key partner to Product, Operations, and Clinical leaders, bridging technical and clinical teams to foster agile development.
    *   Contribute to strategic planning as Midi expands its services, ensuring quality remains paramount.
    *   Champion a data-first philosophy, using analytics to allocate resources, identify inflection points of value, and build frameworks that translate data into actionable insights for the entire organization.

### Qualifications

*   8+ years of experience in clinical operations, health analytics, quality management, or health services research.
*   Demonstrated success leading data-rich clinical programs and translating complex findings into tangible operational improvements.
*   Proven ability to bridge technical and clinical teams, driving agile, scalable, and impactful innovation.
*   A highly energetic and innovative mindset with a talent for designing simple solutions to complex challenges.
*   Obsessed with efficiency and accountability; you respect deadlines, thrive in a fast-paced environment, and consistently hold yourself and others to high standards.
*   Deep intellectual curiosity and a passion for continuous learning.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "The description mentions leveraging AI for clinical quality. How mature are these AI systems today, and what is the vision for their evolution and integration into the daily clinician workflow?"**

That’s a great question, and it gets to the heart of what makes this role so exciting. Right now, our AI tools are fantastic at descriptive analytics—they give us a clear picture of what's happening in our patient interactions. Your first big challenge will be to take us from *descriptive* to *prescriptive*. We envision a future where our systems don't just report on practice patterns but proactively offer real-time decision support to clinicians, flag potential safety issues before they occur, and automate the most tedious parts of documentation. You won't just be analyzing data; you'll be the architect of a smarter, more supportive clinical environment, working directly with our tech and clinical teams to build the feedback loops that make this vision a reality. This role is about closing the gap between a cool AI feature and a tool that genuinely makes a clinician's day better and a patient's outcome safer.

**A candidate might also ask: "As Director, how will the insights I generate directly influence Midi Health's broader strategic decisions, particularly as the company expands into more complex care?"**

Honestly, this isn't a role where your work ends up in a dusty report that no one reads. The dashboards and analyses you create will be front-and-center in our weekly leadership meetings. When we discuss launching a new service line for complex care, your data on patient outcomes and operational efficiency will be the foundation of that conversation. You'll be the one telling us where we're excelling and, more importantly, where the data reveals an opportunity to improve. Think of this position as the connective tissue between our clinical promise, our operational reality, and our future strategy. Your insights will directly shape our product roadmap, our clinician training programs, and our overall approach to growth, making this one of the most visible and impactful roles in the company.
