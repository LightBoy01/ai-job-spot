**Position Overview**

We are looking for an **Automotive Functional Safety Architect – Silicon (SoC/NPU/AI Accelerators)** to define and lead the safety architecture of our automotive-grade AI chips.

This is a **critical technical leadership role** focused on **semiconductor-level functional safety**. You will design and implement safety strategies compliant with **ISO 26262, SOTIF, and ISO/SAE 21434**, ensuring our NPUs, CPUs, and SoCs meet the highest standards of automotive safety and reliability.

You will also **serve as the technical lead in external engagements**, including with **automotive partners, customers, and European consortia**, representing Axelera AI’s functional safety strategy and driving alignment on safety requirements across the ecosystem.

**Important**: This role requires **hands-on experience with functional safety at the silicon level**—not at the vehicle, ECU, or system level. You’ll be working directly with hardware and RTL teams to embed fault-tolerant, safety-compliant features deep into the design.

**Key responsibilities:**

*   **Functional Safety Architecture**: Define and own the **ISO 26262-compliant safety architecture** for AI-enabled SoCs (ASIL B to D), with a focus on chip-level implementation.
    

*   **Hardware Safety Design**: Work with RTL and SoC architects to integrate fault-tolerant safety mechanisms, including ECC, lockstep, BIST, watchdogs, and other diagnostic features.
    

*   **Safety Analysis**: Lead **HARA**, **FMEDA**, **FTA**, and **DFA** across hardware and system levels to support robust diagnostic coverage and compliance.
    

*   **Safety Concepts**: Develop and validate technical and functional safety concepts, including requirements allocation, safety goals, and system-level safety paths.
    

*   **Cross-Functional Collaboration**: Partner with internal hardware, software, and systems teams to ensure that safety requirements are implemented and validated across the stack.
    

*   **External Leadership**: Act as **technical lead in partner and customer engagements**, including participation in **European automotive consortia**, ensuring alignment of external requirements with internal safety strategy.
    

*   **Certification Process**: Support external audits and liaise with certification bodies for ISO 26262 and SOTIF compliance.
    

*   **Cybersecurity & SOTIF Integration**: Incorporate **ISO/SAE 21434** and **ISO/PAS 21448 (SOTIF)** standards into architecture and development, particularly for AI/ML-based systems.
    

*   **Safety Documentation**: Contribute to and review the **safety case**, safety plan, safety manual, and traceability artifacts.
    

*   **Process & Tooling Improvement**: Help define and enhance internal safety development processes, methodologies, and tooling infrastructure.

*   Proficiency in safety analysis methods such as **FMEDA**, **HARA**, **FTA**, and **DFA**.
