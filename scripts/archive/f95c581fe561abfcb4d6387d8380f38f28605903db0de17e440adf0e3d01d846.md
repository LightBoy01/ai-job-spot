About the Position
------------------

Are you ready to take on a role where no two days are the same? As a **Personal Assistant** at John Holland, you’ll be at the heart of our Infrastructure business unit, supporting multiple members of the Senior Leadership Team based in our Pyrmont office.

This is more than just a support role — you’ll be a trusted partner, ensuring the smooth operation of day-to-day activities while contributing to the success of some of Australia’s most iconic infrastructure projects. From managing busy schedules and coordinating high-level meetings to preparing reports and liaising with clients, you’ll play a pivotal role in keeping everything running seamlessly.

If you thrive in a fast-paced environment, enjoy building strong relationships, and love the challenge of juggling multiple priorities, this is your opportunity to make a real impact in a dynamic and rewarding role.

Key Responsibilities include:
-----------------------------

*   Manage the day-to-day activities of the General Managers and provide ad hoc support to their teams
*   Manage financial records such as expenses, invoices, and purchaser requisitions
*   Oversee and coordinate Management diaries, which will include scheduling and calendar planning.
*   Coordinate all aspects of internal and client meetings, preparing agendas, typing minutes, and distributing to the relevant stakeholders
*   Provide Administrative support, such as preparation of correspondence, presentations, marketing material and reports for current and potential customers
*   Monitor and respond to communications on behalf of the General Manager
*   Coordinate travel and accommodation arrangements.
*   Communication with client representatives, external vendors, subcontractors, and community stakeholders as needed

To be successful in this key role, you must have:
-------------------------------------------------

*   Able to thrive in a dynamic and busy environment
*   A highly organised individual who can prioritise multiple conflicting deliverables
*   An excellent people person with well-developed communication skills
*   Able to treat sensitive information with a high level of confidentiality
*   Proficient computer skills and knowledge of relevant software such as Microsoft Office Suite
