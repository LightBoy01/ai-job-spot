**About the Department**
========================

  
**Franklin Templeton Technology (FTT)** drives the technology strategy and delivers innovative technology solutions for Franklin Templeton (FT), a global investment leader delivering innovative multi-asset solutions across public and private markets. FT integrates asset allocation, manager research, and implementation to drive portfolio construction, execution, and strategic oversight. Joining FT means working in a collaborative, growth-oriented environment that values innovation in investment technology.

As a **Lead Data Scientist**, you will design and implement advanced **AI and machine learning (ML)** solutions to enhance decision-making and automation. You’ll work with cutting-edge technologies, including **Generative AI** and **large language models (LLMs),** supporting FT’s mission to modernize investment processes and insights. Collaboration with data engineers, quantitative researchers, and investment professionals will be key to operationalizing scalable, high-impact AI systems. This position will be located in our San Ramon, CA office; we may consider a remote work location for certain designated areas within the U.S.

**How you will add value**
==========================

_Core Responsibilities_

*   AI & ML Development: 
    
    *   Build, train, and deploy ML and AI models to solve business challenges and improve investment decisions.
        
    *   Apply Generative AI, LLMs, and deep learning to automate analysis and reporting.
        
    *   Implement models using MLOps practices for scalability and reliability.
        
    *   Continuously monitor and refine performance.
        
*   Data Engineering:
    
    *   Develop automated pipelines for collecting, cleaning, and validating structured and unstructured data.
        
    *   Partner with engineering teams to ensure robust data governance and accessibility.
        
*   Analytics & Insights:
    
    *   Perform statistical and exploratory analysis to uncover trends and opportunities.
        
    *   Use AI-driven analytics to support portfolio construction, risk management, and client insights.
        
    *   Present findings clearly through dashboards and visualizations.
        
*   Collaboration & Innovation:
    
    *   Translate business needs into AI-based solutions in partnership with portfolio managers and product teams.
        
    *   Research emerging AI methods (e.g., retrieval-augmented generation, vector search) and prototype new applications.
        
    *   Contribute to FT’s AI capability development and best practices.
        

**What will help you be successful in this role**
=================================================

_Experience, Education & Certifications_

*   Bachelor’s or Master’s in Computer Science, Statistics, Engineering, or related field
    
*   2–4 years of experience in data science, AI, or ML (experience with LLMs preferred)
    
*   Strong Python and SQL skills; experience with PyTorch, TensorFlow, or scikit-learn
    
*   Familiarity with AWS, Azure, or GCP; knowledge of MLOps frameworks
    
*   Excellent problem-solving, communication, and cross-functional collaboration skills
