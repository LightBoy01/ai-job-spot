**What role you will play in our team**
---------------------------------------

This Ops Tech Engineer will support facilities engineering in production assets across the globe. Work location is Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

**What you will do**
--------------------

The key responsibilities for the Ops Tech Engineer will be:

*   Develop comprehensive understanding of process systems and the integration of systems.
*   Collaborate with multi-discipline teams to build facility surveillance plans and review findings with global site teams.
*   Perform real-time process surveillance monitoring, optimization, remote troubleshooting, and reporting. Identify deviation and provide resolution to Operations team.
*   Use analytical tools for data driven analysis for enhanced visualization and identification of actionable insights.
*   Assess, categorize, and allocate downtime and perform facility mass balance.
*   Identify, map, and monitor facilities constraints. Identify options to improve/ control constraints.
*   Troubleshoot & optimize equipment/ processes including heat exchangers, separators, pump/compression units, fractionation units, acid gas treatment units, water treatment units, refrigeration and gas liquefaction units, process safety and process control systems.
*   Perform root cause analysis investigations for equipment failures or process upsets.
*   Accountable to deliver engineering packages to site for procurement and construction by working closely with Contractor, Business Unit and other engineering disciplines.
*   Support Management of Change for facilities modifications including development of scope and completion of engineering deliverables for both routine maintenance and turnaround scopes.
*   Identify and progress capacity enhancement, reliability improvement and cost reduction initiatives to improve unit profitability.
*   Develop and modify process simulation models using standard process modeling software (i.e., Aspen HYSYS, HTRI, etc.)
*   Provide coaching and mentoring to early career engineers.
*   Support commissioning / start-up / turnaround activities at site as needed.

**About You**
-------------

**Skills and Qualifications**

*   Bachelor’s degree in chemical/petroleum or mechanical engineering from a recognized university
*   Minimum of 5 years of work experience within the oil and gas industry (Upstream, LNG processing, Refining, or Petrochemical) in Process Engineering, Operations Support or Technical Services role(s).
*   Experience and competency in various oil and Gas Industry Process Design and Process Safety standards.
*   Experience in performance Monitoring of cryogenic units including cold boxes, turbo-expanders, fractionation and LNG trains or cold section of Gas cracker.
*   Analyze process data to identify inefficiencies, fouling, or deviations in cryogenic operations.
*   Recommend and implement process improvements to enhance energy efficiency and product recovery.
*   Support troubleshooting of cryogenic heat exchangers, molecular sieve units, and refrigeration systems.
*   Strong understanding of thermodynamics and phase separation.
*   Hands-on experience with LNG storage tanks, boil-off gas systems, and loading arms.
*   Strong interpersonal skills and written/ verbal communication skills in English
*   Proficient in Microsoft Office suite software program
*   Willing to work in shifts (1.30 PM - 10.30 PM IST) or (9:00 AM -6:00 PM IST)
*   Tool proficiency: Aspen Hysys/HTRI/PRO-I|
*   Strong organizational, planning/ coordination, interface, and technical judgment skills
*   Demonstrated experience and proficiency in mentoring early career process engineers
*   Ability to work both independently and collaboratively with multi-disciplinary teams
*   Willing to work full-time from Bengaluru based office location
*   Willing to work flexible working hours to provide around-the-clock support for global affiliates
*   Willing and able to travel nationally and/or internationally as required.
