### Description\nWelcome to Schüttflix Group, where we've laid the foundation for the digitalization of the construction site. Our mission is to make the construction industry more sustainable and low-emission by increasing recycled building materials and implementing intelligent material flow management. For this unique vision, we need a unique team. Join us to actively shape a sustainable future! As a key member of the People & Culture team, you will support all recruiting activities and contribute to an outstanding candidate experience, playing a vital role in building our innovative team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Recruiting Rockstar:** Support all recruiting activities, from defining requirement profiles to participating in interviews and independently managing smaller (sub-)projects.\n*   **Matchmaker:** Identify and attract new talents for Schüttflix through various platforms.\n*   **Experience Designer:** Ensure an exceptional candidate experience throughout the entire application process, in true Schüttflix style.\n*   **Communication Professional with an Overview:** Be responsible for creating job advertisements and coordinating with all stakeholders and external service providers.\n*   **All-rounder in the People & Culture Team:** Support diverse topics related to employee experience, including onboarding measures, internal events, and participation in P&C initiatives.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   **Education:** A degree, preferably in Economics, Human Resources Management, Psychology, or a comparable qualification.\n*   **Know-How:** Initial practical experience in a professional context is an advantage (e.g., through internships or part-time jobs).\n*   **Personality and Working Style:** You are enthusiastic about recruitment and modern HR topics and are always willing to go the extra mile.\n*   **Communication Skills:** Very good communication skills in German to effectively interact with various teams (English language skills are a nice-to-have).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an entry-level People & Culture professional eager to make a tangible impact within a dynamic, mission-driven company. Schüttflix is not just digitalizing construction; they are actively driving sustainability in a critical industry. The role offers broad exposure to all facets of recruiting and employee experience, making it ideal for someone looking to develop a diverse HR skill set. The culture emphasizes collaboration, growth, and a hands-on approach, promising strong support for your professional development. If you're passionate about talent acquisition, modern HR practices, and contributing to a greener future, this role provides an exciting platform to learn and grow.
