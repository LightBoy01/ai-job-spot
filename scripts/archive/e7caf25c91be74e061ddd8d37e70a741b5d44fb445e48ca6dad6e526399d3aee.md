Career Opportunities: Master thesis: MLIR based compiler transformation for AI applications (773996)

Requisition ID **773996** - Posted  - Country/Area (1) - state/province (1)

Join our Team
-------------

**About this opportunity**

We’re looking for an enthusiastic MSc student to join our team and contribute to developing MLIR-Based Compiler frameworks for executing AI applications.

Today there is a high demand to run ML models efficiently (Inference) across diverse hardware platforms from high performance GPUs to resource constrained edge devices. However, there is a significant semantic gap, that exists between high-level model frameworks (PyTorch, JAX, TF etc.) and the low-level instructions executed on diverse hardware. This gap results in suboptimal performance. The **MLIR (Multi-Level Intermediate Representation)**, framework has emerged as a powerful, modular and extensible infrastructure designed to bridge this gap unifying compiler design for heterogeneous compute systems.

This thesis investigates the optimization of AI workloads by leveraging MLIR-based compiler transformations. The core of this work involves the design, implementation, and evaluation of novel or improved optimization passes within the MLIR ecosystem, to reduce memory overhead, and enhance data locality and hardware utilization. In this thesis, you can explore custom optimization passes as part of our transformation passes. Besides, this thesis examines the **Quant** and **SparseTensor** dialects in MLIR. These dialects handle the necessary abstractions into the compiler's **Intermediate Representation (IR)** to enable **quantization** and **pruning** optimizations, respectively. This is a unique opportunity to gain deep, practical expertise in the highly sought-after fields of compilers and high-performance computing for AI. 

**What you will do**

*   Conduct a literature review to identify relevant optimizations tailored to our use cases.
*   Design, implement, and evaluate the performance in heterogeneous targeted platform.
*   Benchmark compiler performance against our currently implemented methods.
*   Build an end-to-end prototype for deployment in an industrial setting.
*   Summarize your work in a written thesis and present your results to the team.

**The skills you bring:**

We are looking for a master’s student who:

*   Is very curious about new technology, and a quick learner with open mind.
*   Is currently enrolled in a quantitative MSc program such as Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, or a related field.
*   Has a strong understanding of MLIR or LLVM compiler techniques.
*   Has hands-on experience with Python programming and common ML libraries (NumPy, Pandas, scikit-learn, etc.).
*   Has hands-on experience with embedded programming.
*   Ideally has practical experience with deep learning frameworks (e.g., JAX, TensorFlow, PyTorch)
*   Communicates well in English and enjoys working in a collaborative environment.
