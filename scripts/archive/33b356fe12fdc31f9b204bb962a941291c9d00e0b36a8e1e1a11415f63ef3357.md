Role profile:
-------------
Are you an experienced software engineer with a passion for productionising data science solutions? Do you want to build innovative solutions for financial markets and professionals by co-developing with teams of brilliant, collaborative software engineers? If so, we’d love to talk to you.
Role summary:
-------------
As Lead Software Engineer, you will drive the development of high-impact, scalable AI software solutions. Our engineering teams are multi-functional and cover the full software lifecycle, and we cooperate closely with machine learning experts.
We are looking for backend developers with a proven ability deliver high-quality code that aligns with business goals, and manage project timelines. You will apply your problem solving skills and experience to ensure your team successfully ships efficient software.
What you'll be doing:
---------------------
This role combines hands-on technical leadership with strategic oversight in a high-impact environment focused on innovation and continuous development.
*   Contribute to the design, architecture and development of scalable state-of-the-art AI software solutions, ensuring alignment with business objectives and technical standards.
*   Collaborate closely with Data Science and software engineering teams to define project requirements, set technical direction, and establish development roadmaps.
*   Establish and apply coding standards to maintain robust and clean code.
*   Solve complex technical issues, optimize development workflows, and provide strategic insights to stakeholders to support project goals and timelines.
What you'll bring:
------------------
Essential skills:
-----------------
*   Extensive experience in software engineering with a focus on designing and implementing high-performance, scalable systems.
*   Proficiency in Object-Oriented programming in Python for backend development.
*   Expertise in cloud technologies (emphasis on Azure and/or AWS) and deployment practices, ensuring seamless and scalable delivery.
*   Proven ability to establish and uphold coding and design standards to ensure high-quality, maintainable code.
*   Effective collaboration skills, with experience working with multi-functional teams and communicating technical requirements to both technical and non-technical team members.
Desirable skills:
-----------------
*   Experience with DevOps practices, including CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, and monitoring, to optimise development workflows and improve deployment reliability.
Education
---------
Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Physics or related field, or equivalent work experience
