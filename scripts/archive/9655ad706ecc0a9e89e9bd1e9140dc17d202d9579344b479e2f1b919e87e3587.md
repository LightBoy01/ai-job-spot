About the Role:
---------------

We’re hiring engineers focused on **AI Engineering Practices** to help our teams harness AI to accelerate development velocity, code quality, and developer experience. As a **Sr. Staff Engineer**, you’ll operate with a broader organizational scope: shaping how AI is adopted across multiple groups, setting long-term technical direction, and ensuring that platform improvements and tiger team initiatives scale into company-wide practices.

You’ll partner with DevOps to advance our developer platform, join **tiger teams with product engineers** to tackle high-impact codebase refactoring, and drive cross-org alignment on AI adoption strategy. Beyond hands-on contributions, you’ll mentor Staff Engineers, influence technical leaders across squads, and raise the bar for how we use AI to build reliable, scalable systems.

You Will:
---------

*   Define and drive org-level initiatives that integrate AI into developer experience, platform infrastructure, and engineering workflows.
    
*   Partner with DevOps leadership to establish a strategic roadmap for AI-enabled platform capabilities.
    
*   Lead tiger teams in tackling complex codebase refactoring and modernization efforts, modeling effective AI-assisted development practices.
    
*   Influence cross-org architecture and system design by ensuring AI integration supports scalability, reliability, and operational excellence.
    
*   Identify and institutionalize AI adoption patterns into company-wide standards, frameworks, and tooling.
    
*   Mentor and coach Staff and Senior Engineers, fostering professional growth and building a strong AI practices community across engineering.
    
*   Partner with senior engineering and product leadership to align AI adoption with long-term business strategy and technical vision.
    
*   Act as an internal thought leader, guiding responsible AI use and contributing to the continuous evolution of engineering practices.
    

You Have:
---------

*   12+ years of professional software engineering experience, including technical leadership with org-wide or company-wide impact.
    
*   Track record of influencing architecture and technical strategy across multiple groups or domains.
    
*   Strong hands-on coding skills and experience delivering production systems at scale.
    
*   Deep understanding of AI/ML tools and APIs (LLMs, code assistants, AI testing tools, etc.) and their practical application to developer workflows.
    
*   **Expertise in one or more of the following: backend technologies (Node.js, Kotlin, Java, Python, Go), frontend technologies (React, TypeScript, Next.js), or mobile (React Native, Swift, Kotlin/Android).**
    
*   Significant experience with CI/CD workflows, cloud infrastructure (AWS, Docker, Kubernetes), and developer platform tooling.
    
*   Proven ability to set long-term technical direction, anticipating future growth, complexity, and use cases.
    
*   Demonstrated success mentoring senior-level engineers and shaping engineering culture.
    
*   Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to influence senior leadership and build cross-org alignment.
    

You'll Thrive Here If You:
--------------------------

*   Shape cross-org engineering practices and technical strategy, not just squad-level initiatives.
    
*   Champion responsible AI adoption that accelerates delivery while ensuring long-term quality and maintainability.
    
*   Lead through influence, building trust with engineering leaders and executives alike.
    
*   Balance innovation with pragmatism, making decisions that account for scale and sustainability.
    
*   Actively coach and grow other technical leaders, multiplying your impact across the org.
    
*   Take initiative to identify systemic challenges and deliver solutions that raise the bar for the entire engineering organization.
