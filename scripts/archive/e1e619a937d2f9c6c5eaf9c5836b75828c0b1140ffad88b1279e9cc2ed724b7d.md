**Want to know how a facility management company is powered by technology?**

At ISS Tech Team we are investing in innovative and integrated technological solutions for the facility management industry with a tech team headquarted in Copenhagen, Warsaw and Porto. Our Porto hub is the first space dedicated specifically to software development and acts as a powerhouse where our employees test and implement products for over 40,000 clients and 350,000 placemakers around the world.

**Location:** Porto, Portugal (Hybrid)

**Work Regime:** Hybrid

**What you’ll do:**

*   Develop new features, and address maintenance and continuous improvement of existing software with a strong focus on maintainability and quality; 
*   Deep in conversations regarding the creation of fresh components and services that seamlessly blend with both internal and external services, overseeing their execution; 
*   Actively contribute to the documentation of codebase architecture, design decisions, and best practices, ensuring that knowledge is effectively shared and maintained within the team; 
*   Actively participate in code reviews and provide constructive feedback to peers, fostering a collaborative and knowledge-sharing environment within the development team. 

**Who you’ll work with:**

*   **Software Development Team:** You'll collaborate closely with other developers to share knowledge, tackle challenges together, and ensure code consistency and quality; 
*   **Product Owners and Designers:** You'll work closely with product managers and designers to understand user needs, translate them into technical requirements, and ensure the final product meets expectations; 
*   **QA Engineers:** You'll work with QA testers to identify and fix bugs, ensuring software functionality and user experience are optimized; 
*   **DevOps Engineers:** You'll collaborate with DevOps engineers to streamline the software development lifecycle, including deployment, automation, and infrastructure management. 

**What makes you fit**

**You should have:**  

*   2+ years of experience as a Fullstack Developer, with expertise in Node.js and React. 
*   Strong knowledge of TypeScript, ensuring robust and maintainable code. 
*   Experience working with relational databases, particularly PostgreSQL. 
*   Deep understanding of APIs and integrations, including RESTful APIs, authentication mechanisms (OAuth, JWT), and third-party service integrations. 
*   Familiarity with cloud services, such as Azure, for hosting and managing applications. 
*   Problem-solving mindset with the ability to work in a collaborative, cross-functional team environment. 
*   Excellent English communication skills, both written and spoken. 

**Nice to have:** 

*   Experience with CI/CD pipelines to streamline deployment processes. 

*   Knowledge of frontend performance optimizations and best practices. 
*   Understanding of microservices architecture and best practices for scalable applications.
