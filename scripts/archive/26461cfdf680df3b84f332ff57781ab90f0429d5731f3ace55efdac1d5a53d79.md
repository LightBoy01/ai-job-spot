**Job Description:**
We are seeking an experienced **Full Stack ServiceNow Platform Developer** with strong expertise in **AI/ML integration** to advance our enterprise IT solutions. This role focuses on **designing, developing, and automating ITSM and ITOM workflows** on ServiceNow, while embedding **AI-driven capabilities** for predictive analytics, intelligent routing, and decision-making.
The ideal candidate will bring **hands-on ServiceNow development experience**, strong full-stack skills, and proven expertise in **integrating AI/ML models and automation** into enterprise workflows.
**What you get to do in this role:**
*   Lead **ServiceNow development** across frontend and backend components with advanced debugging and optimization.
*   Design and configure **ITSM/ITOM modules** (Incident, Change, Request, Problem, Event Mgmt, CPG, Certificate Mgmt, Discovery).
*   Build and automate workflows using **Flow Designer, Integration Hub, Client Scripts, Business Rules, and Virtual Agent**.
*   Implement **Now Assist** and **Conversational AI/Virtual Agent** to enhance self-service and reduce manual workload.
*   Develop and maintain **AI-driven observability solutions**, deriving insights from logs, metrics, and traces.
*   Create **REST/SOAP API integrations** between ServiceNow and external AI/ML platforms, observability tools, and enterprise apps.
*   Design **end-to-end workflows** across IT infrastructure domains (Network, Applications, Security) enhanced by AI capabilities.
*   Manage **CMDB data models and relationships** to support accurate AI-powered insights.
*   Automate data ingestion, event correlation, and AI-driven resolution workflows.
*   Ensure adherence to **ITIL best practices** and **enterprise security standards** in AI-enabled solutions.
*   Establish governance and transparency for **AI-driven decisions**.
*   Continuously enhance **ServiceNow performance, scalability, and automation accuracy** to drive ITSM effectiveness.
**Qualifications:**
**To be successful in this role you have:**
*   6+ years of combined experience in **ServiceNow development** and **object-oriented programming (Java/JavaScript/Python)**.
*   Minimum 3 years of **hands-on ServiceNow development** experience.
*   Advanced proficiency in **JavaScript, Glide scripting, Business Rules, UI policies, and ServiceNow APIs**.
*   Strong knowledge of **data structures, design patterns, performance/scalability optimization**.
*   Experience with modern UI frameworks (Angular, React).
*   Practical expertise in **AI/ML model integration, AI Ops, and workflow orchestration with Agentic AI**.
*   Solid understanding of **ITIL processes** across Incident, Change, Request, and Problem Management.
*   Familiarity with **Cloud platforms (Azure/AWS)** and **AI/ML frameworks** (e.g., Azure OpenAI).
*   Proven experience integrating **external AI models** via REST/SOAP APIs.
*   Strong problem-solving and analytical skills to optimize ITSM/ITOM operations.
**Qualifications**
*   **ServiceNow certifications**: CSA, CIS-ITSM, CIS-EM, CIS-Discovery (CTA preferred).
*   Full-stack skills: **React, CSS/SCSS, Python, Django, REST APIs**.
*   Hands-on with **AI/ML models, RPA tools, and predictive intelligence solutions**.
*   Familiarity with **ServiceNow ITOM, CMDB, and automation frameworks** for IT service optimization.
