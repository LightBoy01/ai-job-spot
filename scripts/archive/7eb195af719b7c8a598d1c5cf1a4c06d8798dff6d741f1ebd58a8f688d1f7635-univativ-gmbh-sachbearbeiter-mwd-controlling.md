Your career is our mission, and your development is at the heart of what we do.\n\nDo you possess a keen eye for detail and excel at bringing order to numbers, processes, and projects? As a Controlling Specialist (m/f/d), you will provide precise commercial support for construction and network projects, overseeing everything from order creation and invoice verification to project post-calculation. You will proactively monitor cost developments, identify risks early, and actively contribute to shaping processes.\n\nWe are here to kickstart your career! Successfully launch your professional journey with our client in the dynamic energy sector! You'll join an innovative team with the opportunity to work on exciting controlling projects and continuously expand your knowledge. If this sounds like a fascinating career prospect, we encourage you to apply today!\n\n### Responsibilities\n- As a Controlling Specialist (m/f/d), you will create orders, generate purchase requisitions, continuously update them, and reliably track their status.\n- You will ensure the correct commercial billing of services rendered and, as a Controlling Specialist (m/f/d), ensure transparent processes.\n- In project controlling, you will verify invoices, update orders, and always keep a close eye on cost developments in network construction and building projects.\n- You will prepare commercial project status information, coordinate it, and make it available to involved stakeholders.\n- As a Controlling Specialist (m/f/d), you will actively participate in project post-calculations and support analyses aimed at improving planning accuracy.\n- You will identify commercial opportunities and risks, contribute to the further development of commercial processes and procedural instructions, thereby sustainably strengthening project governance.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed vocational training as an Industrial Clerk (Industriekaufmann/-frau) or a degree in Business Administration, Economics, Industrial Engineering, or a comparable qualification.\n- Ideally, experience in project controlling and sound knowledge of Excel.\n- Proficient use of common Office applications and SAP systems.\n- Strong self-initiative, assertiveness, and an independent working style.\n- Willingness for occasional business trips and possession of a Class B driver's license.\n- At least business-fluent German language skills, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a detail-oriented individual looking to grow their career in controlling within the energy sector. univativ positions itself as a strong career partner, offering a supportive environment for professional development, exposure to diverse projects, and a chance to build a valuable professional network. The role itself provides significant responsibility in financial oversight and process improvement, making it ideal for someone who enjoys hands-on involvement in project finance and has a keen eye for optimizing operations. The attractive salary and benefits, combined with the focus on continuous learning, make this a compelling next step for both entry-level and experienced professionals.
