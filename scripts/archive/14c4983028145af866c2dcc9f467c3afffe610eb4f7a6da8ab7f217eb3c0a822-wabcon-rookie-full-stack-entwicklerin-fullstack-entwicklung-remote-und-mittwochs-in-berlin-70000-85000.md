### Description
Join our client where you will contribute to critical decisions that propel Germany forward. As a Fullstack Developer, you will design and implement IT systems that enhance the modernity, security, and efficiency of central administrative processes, spanning from the user interface to the database. Our client offers you responsible tasks, modern working conditions, diverse development opportunities, and a dedicated team committed to shaping the digital future. Be part of projects that truly matter.

### Responsibilities
*   Perform Frontend and Backend development.
*   Design and implement APIs (REST, GraphQL).
*   Conduct code reviews and automated tests.
*   Independently implement user stories within development sprints.
*   Identify opportunities for refactorings and other code optimizations (Frontend and Backend).
*   Proactively identify and introduce new tasks to further develop and improve applications.
*   Independently and with guidance, familiarize yourself with the extremely complex and extensive domain knowledge of the federal budget.
*   Continuously further your knowledge in domain expertise and technology, and integrate these insights into projects.

### Qualifications
*   Profound knowledge in Frontend development (Vue/Vuetify, React, JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, CSS).
*   Profound knowledge in Backend development (Node.js, GraphQL, Java, Spring).
*   Good knowledge of PostgreSQL.
*   Experience with testing frameworks (e.g., Jest, Vitest, Robot Framework, JUnit).
*   Basic understanding of software architecture.
*   Proficient in Clean Code principles.
*   Very good German language skills.
