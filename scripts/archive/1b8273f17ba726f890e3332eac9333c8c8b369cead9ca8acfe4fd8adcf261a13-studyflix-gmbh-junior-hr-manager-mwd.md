### Description\nStudyflix is the largest free e-learning and career platform in the DACH region, serving over 6 million users. Our mission is to provide students with free access to high-quality education and the best job opportunities, while also being a game-changer for our corporate clients in the \"War for Talents\".\n\nWe are looking for a **(Junior) HR Manager (m/w/d)** to join our team in Augsburg. In this full-time, permanent role, you will be a crucial interface where HR, operations, and organization converge. You will support our team and management, ensuring smooth processes and creating real value for the entire company through your attention to detail and passion for structuring, improving workflows, and maintaining daily operations. This is an immediate start position.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs our (Junior) HR Manager, your key responsibilities will include:\n- Acting as the primary point of contact for all personnel-related matters for our employees and coordinating between various departments.\n- Driving various HR projects, such as personnel development initiatives, supporting management, and strengthening our employer branding.\n- Managing interfaces and actively creating synergies within the broader Bertelsmann Group.\n- Ensuring accurate preliminary payroll accounting, maintaining time management systems, and handling contract management.\n- Overseeing and optimizing various processes via Personio, including onboarding and offboarding, data quality, and automated workflows.\n- Organizing daily office operations, maintaining internal services, and managing office equipment and infrastructure.\n\n### Qualifications\nTo succeed in this role, you should bring:\n- A very well-completed university degree or equivalent qualification, ideally with a focus on HR, economics, organization, or administration.\n- A structured, reliable, and precise working style, with a strong understanding of processes and a pragmatic, solution-oriented mindset.\n- Initial experience in an HR or office environment, for example, in personnel administration, contract management, time management, or using HR tools like Personio.\n- A service-oriented approach, clear communication skills, and a passion for supporting employees and bringing different teams together.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an ambitious individual looking to kickstart their HR career in a dynamic and fast-growing e-learning company. As a Junior HR Manager at Studyflix, you won't just be handling administrative tasks; you'll be at the heart of operational excellence, directly impacting employee experience and contributing to strategic HR projects from day one. The role offers significant personal scope, a supportive team environment, and the chance to shape processes within a company that is making a real difference in education and talent acquisition. With flexible home-office options and a suite of attractive benefits, Studyflix clearly values its employees and provides a great platform for professional growth.
