### Description
With our client, you'll be working at the forefront of Germany's digital transformation, shaping the future of public administration. As a Product Owner, you assume responsibility for delivering innovative digital solutions that enable modern, secure, and efficient IT systems.

You excel at understanding user needs, prioritizing requirements, and guiding interdisciplinary teams with a clear vision and an agile mindset. In this pivotal role, you significantly contribute to transforming ideas into functional, practical products that noticeably simplify the daily lives of citizens and public administration.

Our client offers you responsible tasks, modern working conditions, diverse development opportunities, and a dedicated team that collaboratively designs the digital administration of tomorrow. Join us and shape projects that truly matter.

### Responsibilities
*   **Team Leadership:** You manage the development team, set clear priorities, and ensure the efficient implementation of requirements.
*   **Technical Alignment:** In close collaboration with the project architect, you ensure a perfect match between business functionalities and technical implementation.
*   **Functional Concepts:** You translate business requirements into clear, actionable solutions.
*   **Requirements Management:** You prioritize, document, and structure both functional and non-functional requirements.
*   **Project Planning:** You actively plan and manage projects, consistently keeping company objectives in mind.
*   **Team Collaboration:** Together with other Product Owners, you ensure a unified and effective product strategy.

### Qualifications
*   Strong communication skills and excellent German language proficiency (at least C1 level).
*   Successfully completed studies in a relevant field, ability to solve problems independently, and a preference for structured work.
*   Ability to capture stakeholder requirements and translate them into clear conceptual designs.
*   Experience with agile methodologies such as Scrum and Kanban.
*   Proficient in using tools like Jira and Confluence.
*   Experience in planning and executing releases in agile teams.
*   Basic understanding of software development, especially Microservices and distributed systems.
