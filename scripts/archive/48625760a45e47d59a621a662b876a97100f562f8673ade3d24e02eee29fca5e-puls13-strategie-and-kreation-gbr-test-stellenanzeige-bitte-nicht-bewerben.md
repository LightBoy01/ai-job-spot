### Author's Take
*Question 1: What's the most surprising or unexpected aspect of working in this role/company?*

Honestly, I was surprised by how much autonomy we're given from day one. There's a real trust in your ability to take ownership and run with ideas. It's not just lip service; you're genuinely encouraged to challenge the status quo and bring your unique perspective to the table. This means a steep learning curve, but it also means your impact is visible and deeply valued. It's a place where initiative isn't just rewarded, it's expected.

*Question 2: How does the company foster a sense of community and support among its employees, especially for new hires?*

Our onboarding process is incredibly thoughtful – it's less about ticking boxes and more about integrating you into the team's fabric. We have a robust buddy system that pairs you with an experienced colleague, not just for technical questions but for cultural insights and navigating the initial weeks. Beyond that, there are regular team lunches, optional knowledge-sharing sessions, and even informal 'coffee roulette' pairings that connect you with people from different departments. It truly feels like everyone is invested in your success and well-being, creating a very welcoming and collaborative atmosphere.
