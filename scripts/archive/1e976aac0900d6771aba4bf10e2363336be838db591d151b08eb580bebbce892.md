C10 Labs is hiring a **founder-type operator** to launch and lead C10 Labs’ **NYC studio**. This person will anchor our operations in New York, oversee venture builds, drive programmatic execution (including a cohort-based AI incubator), and serve as a strategic point of contact for our ecosystem partners.

  
This is both a **strategic** and **hands-on** role for someone who thrives in early-stage environments, is fluent in AI and emerging tech trends, and knows how to operationalize complex programs while cultivating talent and partnerships.

What you'll do:
---------------

**Studio Buildout & Launch**

*   Lead the setup and launch of C10 Labs NYC, including physical space, initial team recruitment, and onboarding.
*   Stand up systems and workflows to support multiple parallel venture builds.
*   Coordinate with real estate partners, vendors, and civic/economic development entities.

**AI Incubator & Program Management**

*   Design and execute a high-impact incubator program in partnership with our Cambridge team: founder sourcing, onboarding, curriculum, milestone tracking, workshops, and demo days.
*   Deliver program curriculum, monitor performance, capture learnings, and continuously iterate to improve the founder experience.
*   Manage mentor networks, partner programming, and founder resources.

**Talent & Ecosystem Engagement**

*   Build strong ties with NYC-based founders, technologists, academic researchers, and operators.
*   Lead startup recruitment and ideation events to source promising AI ventures.
*   Represent C10 Labs at conferences, university events, and community forums.

**Partnerships & Strategic Outreach**

*   Serve as liaison to NYC-based partners.
*   Interface with academic innovation hubs to access translational research and recruit top AI talent.
*   Explore public-private collaboration models to amplify C10’s studio platform.

**Fund & Leadership Coordination**

*   Report KPIs and key metrics to C10 Labs’ GPs and studio leadership.
*   Contribute to investment diligence and portfolio company evaluation.
*   Ensure alignment of NYC operations with broader fund and studio strategy.

Who you are:
------------

*   7+ years in fast-paced, entrepreneurial roles in applied AI, product, VC platform ops, venture studios, or accelerators.
*   Deep familiarity with the NYC innovation ecosystem—startups, VCs, research universities, civic programs.
*   Technical foundation in AI/ML, data science, or engineering (coding not required, but understanding of technical concepts and new applications is key)
*   Demonstrated success launching or scaling early-stage products, programs, or ventures.
*   Experience supporting technical founders and building structure in ambiguous environments.
*   Strong operational toolkit: financial modeling, venture formation, process design, stakeholder management.
*   Track record of building partnerships with corporates, universities, or civic organizations.
*   Exceptional communication, leadership, and program management skills.

**Preferred**

*   Prior experience running or helping scale an incubator, accelerator, or venture programs.
*   Familiarity with the latest tools and practices for AI product iteration (e.g., LLM tooling, vector databases).
*   Experience engaging with academic tech transfer or translational research groups.

What we offer:
--------------

*   Base salary of 160,000-200,000 and equity in our portfolio of ventures, fully-paid benefits, and a flexible work environment.
*   Front-row seat to the creation of transformative AI companies at the earliest stages.
*   Opportunity to shape C10 Labs’ expansion in NYC from the ground up.
*   Access to C10’s extensive network of researchers, operators, investors, and partners.
*   A mission-driven team committed to accelerating science, impact, and human ingenuity.

**C10 Labs** is a venture studio and fund focused on building and backing AI-driven startups tackling critical challenges in healthcare, energy, industrial systems, and scientific discovery. Our team runs accelerator programming and co-creates companies at the earliest stages by embedding deep technical expertise with hands-on operational support.  
  

Headquartered in Cambridge, MA C10 Labs is expanding its presence to **New York City**—one of the most vibrant ecosystems for applied AI. As we grow, we are seeking bold, entrepreneurial leaders to help shape the future of AI innovation. Join us today!
