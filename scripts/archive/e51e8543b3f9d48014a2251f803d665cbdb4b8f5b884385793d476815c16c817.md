**Job Responsibilities**
------------------------

*   Act as first level support for the process dicing team. 
*   Support daily wafer dicing operation that includes wafer mounting, saw dicing, inspection duties. 
*   To support in shifty lot disposition activities  
*   Follow through the established work instructions and work procedures on all work performed. 
*   Work with responsible engineers in dicing operation for quality control, process control and continuous improvements in dicing processes and procedures. 
*   Document and report any nonconformal event and assist root cause analysis. 
*   Attending to day-to-day dicing issues, perform troubleshooting. 
*   Coordinate with equipment team on maintaining equipment in good operating condition and in calibration. 
*   Assist in providing briefing, guidance and training to production team in regard to dicing process and control 
*   Participate in project DOE (Design of Experiment) initiatives by process owners and suggest improvement ideas, potentially helping to develop and implement them. 
*   Actively demonstrate a passion for continuous learning. 
*   Any Other ad hoc duties as assigned. 

**Job Requirements**
--------------------

*   Minimum Higher NITEC or Diploma in engineering or related fields. 
*   Entry level candidates are welcome to apply.  
*   Must be able to commit to perform 12 hours rotating shift and work in cleanroom environment. 
*   Good communication and documentation skills 
*   Strong problem solving and systems issues troubleshooting skills 
*   Good Team Player and able to work independently 

**Following Requirements Are a Plus** 

*   Experience with Accretech and Disco mechanical and stealth Dicers 
*   Hands on with manual dicing process (Semi-Auto Cutting) 
*   Knowledge of SPC and FDC metrology systems 
*   Use of digital microscope and metrology tools 
*   Basic computer proficiency using Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel & PowerPoint) 
*   Strong communication skills coupled with basic arithmetic knowledge. 
*   Ability and desire to work in a team environment
