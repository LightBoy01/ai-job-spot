**Job Description:**
For the Manager, Data Science & AI Innovation role in the CEMEA Data Science team, we are seeking a visionary leader and strategic thinker to champion our next-generation AI-driven initiatives within the region. As a Data Science Manager specializing in Generative AI and Agentic AI solutions, you will lead cross-functional teams, drive innovation in autonomous AI systems, and develop cutting-edge AI agents that transform how Visa and our clients approach data-driven decision making. This role is dedicated exclusively to working on key client accounts based in the GCC market while pioneering the future of AI in financial services.
**Principal Responsibilities**
*   **Strategic AI Leadership & Innovation**
*   Lead the design, development, and implementation of GenAI models and autonomous AI agents that revolutionize cards and payments analytics
*   Drive the strategic vision for AI and analytics within the organization, aligning with overall business goals and Visa's innovation roadmap
*   Identify and champion opportunities for agentic AI automation solutions across client workflows, from procurement to payment processing
*   Develop and execute comprehensive AI strategies that leverage multi-agent orchestration frameworks to optimize complex business processes
*   Lead implementation of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems and multi-agent workflows for enhanced decision-making capabilities
*   **Team Leadership & Talent Development**
*   Build, lead, and mentor a high-performing team of data scientists, AI engineers, machine learning specialists, and agentic AI developers
*   Manage and grow talent within the team, providing guidance on advanced AI methodologies, LLM fine-tuning, and agent-based system development
*   Foster a culture of innovation and continuous learning, ensuring team stays current with latest advancements in GenAI, LLMs, and agentic frameworks
*   **Client Engagement, Solution Architecture & Project Management**
*   Partner with global teams, business leaders, and technology partners to design and deliver GenAI solutions driving measurable business value.
*   Lead consultative C-suite engagements and manage AI projects end-to-end, ensuring on-time, within-scope delivery with strong governance.
*   Champion ethical AI practices, model risk management, and compliance with Visa’s analytics, privacy, and global standards.
**Qualifications:**
Basic Qualifications
*   10 or more years of relevant work experience with a Bachelor's Degree or at least 2 years of work experience with an Advanced degree (e.g. Masters, MBA, JD, MD) or 0 years of work experience with a PhD
Preferred Qualifications
*   10 or more years of work experience with a Bachelor's Degree or 4 or more years of relevant experience with an Advanced Degree (e.g. Masters, MBA, JD, MD) or up to 3 years of relevant experience with a PhD
*   Minimum of 8+ years of expertise in applying Machine Learning and AI solutions to business problems, with at least 4+ years in leadership roles managing data science or AI teams
*   Master's or PhD degree in Computer Science, AI/Machine Learning, Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or related quantitative field
*   Good understanding of Payments and the Banking industry, including card verticals such as consumer credit, consumer debit, prepaid, small business, commercial and co-branded product
*   Expert knowledge of data, market intelligence, business intelligence, and AI-driven tools and technologies, with demonstrated ability to incorporate new techniques to solve business problems
*   Proven ability to lead technical teams in designing and implementing complex AI architectures while translating business requirements into scalable AI solutions
*   Hands-on experience with agentic AI frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, or LlamaIndex for building autonomous AI agents and multi-agent orchestration systems
Technical Expertise
*   Expertise in vector databases, embedding models, semantic search technologies, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems for intelligent information processing
*   Experience in drafting solution architecture frameworks that rely on API’s and micro-services
*   Expert proficiency in Python and AI-specific libraries (Transformers, LangChain) with additional programming skills for comprehensive AI solution development
*   Deep knowledge of transformer architectures, attention mechanisms, reinforcement learning, and modern deep learning techniques for building sophisticated AI agents and GenAI applications
*   Advanced understanding of distributed computing environments and big data platforms optimized for AI workloads (Hadoop, Spark, Elasticsearch) with experience in API development and microservices architecture
*   Comprehensive knowledge of data governance, privacy-preserving AI techniques, model risk management, and ethical AI practices for secure and responsible AI system deployment
