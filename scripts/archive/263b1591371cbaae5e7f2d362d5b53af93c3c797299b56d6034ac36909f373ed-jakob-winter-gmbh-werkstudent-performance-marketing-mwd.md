### Description

Hello, we are Jakob Winter!

As a company, we do many things, but everything revolves around cases and molded parts. We develop our own brands and distribute them through dealers or in direct-to-consumer eCommerce. Additionally, we work for globally successful brands, acting as their production partner.

As a Working Student in Social Media (m/f/d), you will be responsible for our brands: Uniqhorse (Equestrian Sports), Jakob Winter 1886 (Music), and Winter Guncases (Weapons).

Our company departments are as diverse as we are ourselves, and all specialized teams work hand-in-hand. Our super nice and international team collaborates closely across our two locations in Marienberg and Groß-Gerau. You will be part of a growing environment and have the chance to actively shape our future through your initiative and motivation. Are you in?

**What we offer:**

*   **Growth and Development:** Work in an ambitious, medium-sized family business with long-term opportunities for your talents and continuous development. We provide avenues for constant growth through on-the-job training, knowledge sharing with colleagues, and e-learning. You'll receive regular feedback and development planning.
*   **Motivated Team Culture:** Be part of a highly motivated and committed team with incredibly kind colleagues.
*   **Impact & Autonomy:** The opportunity to constantly develop, take responsibility, and contribute to our continuous improvement. We value self-starters who love to develop ideas and try them out, truly making a difference.

### Responsibilities

You see yourself as a Social Media all-rounder, always up-to-date with new platforms, changes in the social media landscape, and have a knack for trends and markets to highlight our brands across the internet. Your key responsibilities include:

*   **Performance Marketing:** You will create paid campaigns on Meta, Google, and TikTok. You will also monitor these campaigns and generate weekly reports.
*   **Social Media Marketing:** You will manage content postings and develop content and ad strategies.
*   **Creatives:** Additionally, you will develop ideas for creatives in ads and posts, collaborating with our content team to bring them to life.

### Qualifications

*   **Self-Starter Mindset:** You are an absolute self-starter. You find motivation in working independently, allowing you to truly make things happen. You love developing ideas and simply trying them out.
*   **Digital & Social Media Affinity:** Your affinity for digital and social media is immense. You feel comfortable online and ideally are active yourself in the social media space or have gained significant experience here.
*   **Impact-Oriented & Reliable:** As a medium-sized company, every employee counts. You are driven to make a difference and advance us. You also work very carefully and reliably.

**Helpful Additional Qualifications:**

*   Availability of at least 16 hours/week on average.
*   Very good English skills – additional language skills are a plus.
*   Experience in photography, video production, and image editing.
