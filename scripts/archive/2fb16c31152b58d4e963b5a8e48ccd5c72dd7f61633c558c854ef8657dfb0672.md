**The AI orchestration of your wildest imagination.**

n8n is the open AI workflow orchestration platform built for the new era of AI. We give technical teams the freedom of code with the speed of no-code, so they can automate faster, smarter, and without limits. Backed by a fiercely inventive community and 500+ builder-approved integrations, we’re changing the way people bring systems together and scale ideas for impact.

Since our founding in 2019, we’ve grown into a diverse team of over 160 - working across Europe and the US, connected by a shared builder spirit and with our centre of gravity in Berlin. Along the way, we’ve:

*   Cultivated a community of more than 650,000 active developers and builders
    
*   Earned 145k+ GitHub stars, making us one of the world’s Top 40 most popular projects
    
*   Been ranked as one of Europe’s most promising privately held SaaS startups (4th in Sifted’s 2025 B2B SaaS Rising 100)
    
*   Raised $90m in funding from leading investors - from Sequoia’s first German seed to our recent Series B
    
*   And are grateful for our 94 eNPS score (most companies would call 70 excellent)
    

  
That’s the company we’ve built. Now we’d love to see what _you_ can build. If you’re applying, try n8n out - whether you’re technical or not - and share a screenshot of your first workflow with us. The easiest place to start is here:[app.n8n.cloud/register](http://app.n8n.cloud/register).

At n8n, we’re always excited to connect with exceptional product managers and product designers who are passionate about building intuitive, powerful, and user-centric experiences.

This Product & Design Talent Pool is not for a specific open role but it’s a way for us to stay in touch with talented professionals who could be a strong fit for future opportunities in **Product Management, Product Design, and UX Research**. And yes, we do check this regularly.

We’re particularly interested in connecting with **past founders, relatively technical profiles, and AI optimists** who want to push the boundaries of what’s possible in workflow automation.

**What You’ll Bring**

*   Extensive experience driving product vision, strategy, and execution, ideally in fast-growing environments.
    
*   A strong product sense and design craft, with a proven ability to balance user needs and business impact.
    
*   You use AI daily in your work as a PM and you know how to code
    
*   Comfort working across functions, aligning teams, and turning complex challenges into simple, elegant solutions.
    
*   A curious, data-informed mindset with the creativity to imagine new possibilities.
    
*   Enthusiasm for emerging technologies, especially automation and AI-driven innovation.
    

**What We Value**

*   A product-first mindset rooted in empathy for users.
    
*   Former founders & Entrepreneurs.
    
*   Curiosity about workflow automation, orchestration, and the future of AI.
    
*   Ownership and adaptability in fast-changing contexts.
    
*   Forward-thinking creativity and optimism about what technology can enable.
