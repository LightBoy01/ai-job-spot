### **The role**

We are looking for a Recruiter to join our high-performing GTM Talent Acquisition team. This person will own hiring across key commercial functions (Sales, Marketing, Partnerships, etc.) and play a strategic role in building a world-class GTM team to drive our hyper-growth journey.  
This role is ideal for a recruiter with a strong IT/tech background, deep understanding of the cloud and AI market, and a passion for business partnering and stakeholder influence. You’ll be expected to own and lead hiring for your assigned business area, act as a trusted advisor to senior stakeholders, and drive data-informed hiring strategies.

**Your responsibilities will include:** 

*   Own full-cycle recruitment for GTM roles across Sales, Marketing, Customer Experience, Partnerships, and other commercial functions.
*   Act as a strategic talent partner to business leaders, hiring managers (HMs), and GTM executives—advising on market trends, talent availability, and hiring strategies.
*   Build and manage robust talent pipelines for both immediate needs and long-term GTM scaling.
*   Translate business objectives into effective recruitment plans, aligning hiring to growth and revenue goals.
*   Use data, funnel analytics, and market insights to identify challenges, forecast risks, and influence hiring decisions.
*   Take ownership of search strategy, including outreach, engagement, interviewing, offer negotiation, and closing.
*   Facilitate high-quality candidate experiences with a DEI-first approach.
*   Contribute to continuous improvement of processes, tools, and employer branding initiatives across GTM functions.

**We expect you to have:** 

*   6+ years of recruiting experience, with at least 3 years focused on GTM roles within high-growth tech or IT companies.
*   Deep understanding of cloud infrastructure, AI, or enterprise tech markets—including knowledge of talent landscapes, competitive trends, and GTM structures.
*   Demonstrated experience partnering with senior business stakeholders and influencing hiring decisions through data and market insights.
*   Proven ability to own and scale a function or business unit, acting as the go-to talent expert for that area.
*   Strong stakeholder management skills—collaborative, consultative, and solutions-oriented.
*   Excellent communication skills—able to craft compelling narratives and present complex insights with clarity.
*   Experience with ATS tools (e.g., Greenhouse) and sourcing platforms
*   Passion for building and iterating in a fast-paced, ambiguous startup environment.
