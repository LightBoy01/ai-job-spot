### Description\nAs a Sales Manager at Spire, you will be accountable for the success of your business within a specific region or vertical, owning the performance of Spire's cutting-edge space-based data portfolio. You are expected to quickly grasp the external business environment and focus on building strong partnerships with prospects, guiding them through the sales process to become loyal customers. A key aspect of this role is continuously elevating the customer experience and advocating for their needs internally.\n\nSpire is looking for a candidate who demonstrates a clear track record of professional growth and a strong desire for continued development. You will take ownership of customer activities across operational, tactical, and strategic dimensions. The ideal candidate possesses a perfect command of English, can synthesize complex topics concisely, and has a keen eye for long-term value creation. You will thrive collaborating with lean, agile cross-functional teams, providing direction, and driving initiatives to generate new business pipelines and enhance customer experience.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Identify outbound prospects, understand their business needs, and engage them in 1:1 dialogue.\n*   Drive outbound New Business pipeline generation, meeting sales targets through rigorous prospection and opportunity qualification.\n*   Manage the end-to-end customer experience across the entire Spire Sales Process.\n*   Ensure internal processes and products are updated based on customer and industry insights you periodically gather.\n*   Conduct financial analysis of business opportunities to meet and exceed revenue and profitability targets, building a pipeline of accounts from prospecting to deal closure, typically over 90 days, targeting six-digit sales. You will take ownership and accountability of your quota targets and provide data-centric forecasting of deal outcomes.\n*   Maintain a thorough understanding of the industry, business trends, events, and continuously monitor both industry and competitor developments.\n*   Understand customer needs and use that knowledge to advocate for, and drive delivery of, improvements that provide Spire customers with an unparalleled experience.\n*   Build your own industry network, positioning yourself as a respected resource.\n*   Coordinate across cross-functional teams and communicate with internal and external stakeholders, all while meeting tight deadlines.\n*   Possess a complete understanding of internal and external variables impacting your business, including owning forecasting, analyzing data, and reporting on business performance.\n*   Closely collaborate with SDRs and the Marketing Team to develop, execute, and provide feedback on marketing campaigns to drive new business.\n*   Responsible for keeping accurate customer and pipeline data on CRM for reporting and forecasting.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Experience driving internal cross-team collaboration.\n* international environment.\n*   Degree in business, commercial or sales from a top-tier university.\n*   Experience driving strategic and complex initiatives through cross-functional teams.\n*   Track
