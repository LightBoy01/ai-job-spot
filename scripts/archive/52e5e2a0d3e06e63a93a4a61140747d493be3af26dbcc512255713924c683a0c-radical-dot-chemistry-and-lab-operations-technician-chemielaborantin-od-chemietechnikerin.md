### Description

Radical Dot is seeking a dedicated **Chemistry & Lab Operations Technician (m/w/o)** to join our R&D team. Your mission will be to help advance our pioneering process for converting mixed plastic waste into valuable chemical building blocks, contributing directly to cutting-edge sustainable chemistry.

This is an interdisciplinary role, blending classical analytical, research, quality control, process, and synthesis lab work, offering many varied and exciting tasks. You will work hands-on in the lab, supporting our team of scientists and engineers in developing, optimizing, and scaling our chemical recycling technology. Your expertise in **analytical chemistry and maintenance** will be crucial for ensuring high-quality data generation, reliable results, and smooth lab operations.

As an early-stage climate-tech start-up, this is a unique opportunity to grow your skills in an agile, collaborative, and fun environment while making a significant impact.

### Responsibilities

As a Chemistry & Lab Operations Technician, your key responsibilities will include:

*   **Maintaining lab equipment** (e.g., reactors, analytical instruments), performing calibration, and ensuring thorough documentation to guarantee data integrity and compliance. This includes maintaining preventative maintenance logs, performing simple troubleshooting, and coordinating vendor interactions.
*   **Keeping our lab running smoothly** by managing consumables, gases, and inventory with reorder points, and maintaining tidy, audit-ready benches.
*   Conducting **analytical measurements** using various methods such as GC-FID, titrations, XRF, GPC, and TGA to characterize products, intermediates, and feedstocks.
*   Supporting the R&D team in **sample preparation and standard reaction work-ups**, including the use of rotavaps, flashing setups, and filtration techniques.
*   Preparing, handling, and analyzing **polymer, chemical, and liquid samples** in strict accordance with established methods and safety standards.
*   Assisting with **method development** and continuous improvement of analytical workflows, including writing and updating Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
*   Collaborating closely with the team to interpret results and optimize experimental processes.
*   Supporting SOP adherence, risk assessments, waste stream handling, and regular Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) checks.

### Qualifications

**Required Skills and Experience:**

*   Vocational training (e.g., Chemietechniker, CTA, Chemielaborant/in, or equivalent) and/or a degree in **chemistry, chemical engineering, or a related field**.
*   **3+ years of professional experience** in a relevant laboratory setup as a **chemical laboratory technician, analytical chemist, or a similar role**.
*   Experience operating and maintaining bench-scale **pressure reactors** (e.g., Parr reactors) and familiarity with standard maintenance and operation.
*   Hands-on experience in **analytical chemistry** using techniques such as GC, HPLC, IC, and spectroscopy.
*   A solid understanding of **laboratory safety standards** and best practices for handling chemicals.
*   Excellent **organizational skills**, meticulous attention to detail, and a self-driven attitude.
*   Proficiency in **English** is required; **German** is a strong advantage.

**Ideal Mindset:**

*   **Hands-on and precise**, with a genuine passion for experimental work.
*   **Curious and problem-solving**, eager to understand chemical processes and analytical results.
*   **Team-oriented**, thriving in a collaborative and interdisciplinary environment.
*   **Safety-conscious** and methodical in all lab activities.
*   **Motivated by impact** and driven to contribute to sustainable solutions for plastic waste.
*   **Adaptable & willing to learn**, ready to grow with a fast-moving start-up environment.
*   **Ambitious**, seeking opportunities for growth into different roles over time.
