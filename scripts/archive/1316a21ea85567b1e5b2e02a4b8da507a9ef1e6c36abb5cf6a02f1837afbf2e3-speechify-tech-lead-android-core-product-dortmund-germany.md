### Description

Speechify's core mission is to eliminate reading as a barrier to learning, empowering individuals to consume information effortlessly. With over 50 million users, Speechify's text-to-speech products transform PDFs, books, Google Docs, news articles, and websites into audio, enabling users to read faster, more efficiently, and retain more information. Our comprehensive suite of products includes iOS, Android, Mac, Chrome Extension, and Web Apps, and has received significant industry recognition, including Google's Chrome Extension of the Year and Apple's Design Award for Inclusivity in 2025.

We are a thriving, 100% distributed company with nearly 200 talented individuals globally, proud to have no physical office. Our team comprises exceptional professionals from leading tech companies like Amazon, Microsoft, and Google, top PhD programs such as Stanford, high-growth startups like Stripe, Vercel, and Bolt, alongside many successful founders. 

As our Android app continues its rapid growth, becoming the most used text-to-speech app in the Play Store, we are seeking a **Senior Android Engineer**. This pivotal role is crucial for supporting our expanding user base and spearheading exciting new projects that will drive the product forward. We are looking for a strategic thinker who thrives in fast-paced environments, is passionate about making significant product decisions, and excels at building delightful user experiences. Our flat organizational structure empowers individuals to become leaders through exceptional technical skills and consistent, rapid delivery of results. A strong work ethic, solid communication, and an obsession with winning are paramount.

### Responsibilities

*   Take ownership of major features within the Android app, collaborating closely with the design team to deliver high-quality user experiences.
*   Actively contribute to shaping the future direction and technical roadmap of the Android team.
*   Own, maintain, and continuously improve reliability metrics for key features.
*   Participate in cross-functional discussions with Product, Design, and other Engineering teams.
*   Conduct thorough pull request reviews and provide constructive support to teammates.
*   Proactively address critical issues and effectively manage unexpected technical challenges.
*   Take full ownership of feature releases, including providing nightly builds for the QA team.

### Qualifications

**Required Skills & Experience:**

*   5+ years of professional software engineering experience.

*   Proven experience building or contributing significantly to at least one Android application.
*   Exceptional product design intuition and a deep sense of user empathy.
*   A strong drive to push the boundaries of Android UI/UX.

**Bonus Points:**

### Author's Take

**Question 1**: How does the 100% distributed nature of Speechify's team impact daily collaboration and the overall development process for a Senior Android Engineer, especially when driving new features and UI/UX innovations?

**Answer 1**: Coming from an insider's perspective, our distributed setup isn't just a perk; it's fundamental to how we operate and innovate. It means a high degree of trust and autonomy. You'll find yourself leading initiatives, owning features from concept to launch, and relying heavily on robust asynchronous communication and clear documentation. We're very intentional about over-communicating in writing, leveraging tools, and structuring our sprints to minimize blockers. For UI/UX, this means you're empowered to truly dive deep and iterate, with feedback cycles being deliberate and well-documented. It fosters a culture where the best ideas win, regardless of where they originated, and it truly accelerates individual growth as you learn to lead with clarity and impact from afar.

**Question 2**: Speechify's mission is deeply rooted in accessibility. How does a Senior Android Engineer's work directly translate into impactful improvements for users with learning differences, and what opportunities are there for innovation in this space?

**Answer 2**: This is where the role really shines. Your code isn't just a feature; it's a bridge to knowledge for millions. As a Senior Android Engineer, you're directly building the tools that empower users with dyslexia, ADHD, low vision, and other learning differences to consume information. This means every UI tweak, every performance optimization, every new listening mode you implement, has a tangible, life-changing impact. The opportunities for innovation are immense – think about leveraging the latest AI in audio processing, enhancing reader controls for specific cognitive needs, or creating entirely new ways for users to interact with text. It's a challenging but incredibly rewarding space where your technical skills directly contribute to a more inclusive world. You're not just coding; you're enabling millions to learn and grow, which is a powerful motivator.
