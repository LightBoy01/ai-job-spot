### Description
CEYLAN GmbH, a leading manufacturer of döner kebab cutting machines and gastronomy equipment, is seeking an experienced Finance & Controlling Specialist. Since 1995, we have been synonymous with quality, innovation, and excellent customer service, setting industry standards with brands such as CEYLAN®, Devran®, Heran® Seymen®, and SARAN®. Our mission is to provide first-class gastronomy equipment worldwide, focusing on sustainability and resource-saving technologies.

In this pivotal role, you will significantly contribute to our financial stability and success. We offer a supportive work environment that values personal growth and the opportunity to work within a dynamic, innovative, and trusting team. We foster long-term partnerships and together, we realize the gastronomic visions of our customers.

### Responsibilities
*   Prepare and monitor budget plans as well as financial forecasts for the company.
*   Analyze financial performance and generate regular reports for senior management.
*   Ensure compliance with financial guidelines and regulatory requirements.
*   Optimize internal controlling processes to improve cost control and increase efficiency.
*   Collaborate with other departments to support financial decisions and strategic planning processes.

### Qualifications
*   Completed degree in Business Administration with a focus on Finance, Controlling, or an equivalent qualification.
*   Several years of professional experience in finance and controlling, ideally within the manufacturing industry.
*   Profound knowledge in the preparation and analysis of financial reports and budget management.
*   Experience with common controlling and accounting software solutions.
*   Strong analytical thinking skills, high affinity for numbers, and the ability to clearly communicate complex matters.
