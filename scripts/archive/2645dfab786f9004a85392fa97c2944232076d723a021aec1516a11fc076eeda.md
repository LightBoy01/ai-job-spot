When

4 May 2026 to 4 September 2026

Who are you
-----------

You are a PhD Student at an Australian University and residing in Australia and graduating after September 2026.

We are
------

Dolby's Advanced Technology Group in Sydney creates cutting edge audio, video and multimodal technologies that drive next generation experiences. Our interests are broad: interactive media using Machine Learning/AI, Data Science of media, cloud implementations of media processing and delivery, and explorations of audio video content creation for immersive media such as XR. In this internship you will spend four months with Dolby Australia researchers to investigate a cutting-edge topic. We will work with you in the first two weeks of the project to refine the research topic and develop a plan that leverages your competencies to produce novel research that is fun and exciting. We will encourage publication of the output of your project in an appropriate conference or journal.

Technical Background
--------------------

*   AI/Machine learning systems preferably with application to audio, video, text, and multimedia
*   Multimodal/cross-modal representation learning
*   Trustworthy and explainable AI
*   Generative AI for media creation
*   3D deep learning (including NERF, Gaussian Splatting, etc.)
*   Spatial Computing for 6DoF Multimedia
*   Perceptual and interactive multimedia computing
*   Human-Computer Interfaces and AI applications
*   Multimodal and Immersive Audio and Video creation

Project Areas
-------------

*   Live events or sports
*   Data provenance, attribution and authenticity
*   Creating and controlling audio, video, textual, or multimedia content
*   Enhancement of education and learning
*   Future devices, e.g. smart glasses and holographic displays
*   Next-generation experiences in Automotive

What the internship is about
----------------------------

*   We will work with you and your PhD supervisor to define a project that relates to your PhD.
*   We highly encourage publication of the results of your project.
*   Work closely with Dolby research teams to better understand industry-based research.
*   Where appropriate, hone your skills in AI/Machine learning or the appropriate creative tools.

Skills and Specialty Areas
--------------------------

We are interested in PhD Research candidates from Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Science, Music Technology, Media Technology, or Media Content Creation. We are not prescriptive of skill sets, but it is useful if the Research intern has ability in using appropriate tools, e.g, a project in machine learning may require programming Python or Matlab or a project in XR creation may use MaxMSP or Final Cut/Logic Pro. Most importantly, we are looking for the ability to creatively explore and design experiments to uncover new and interesting learnings in media.
