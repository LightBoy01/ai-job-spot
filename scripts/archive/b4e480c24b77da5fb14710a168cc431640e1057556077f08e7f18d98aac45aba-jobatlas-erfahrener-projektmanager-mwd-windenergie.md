### Description\nAre you an expert in renewable energies? Then we need your power! Join a strong company dedicated to implementing solar and wind energy projects across the country, based in Leer. As an experienced Project Manager (m/f/d) for Wind Energy, you will be a crucial part of our visionary mission to achieve an emissions-free future. You will oversee major wind projects from initial planning through to successful implementation, ensuring their smooth execution.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Be responsible for project development and its process until the completion of wind farms.\n- Conduct analyses, forecasts, and securing of project sites.\n- Create planning concepts and commission necessary expert reports.\n- Contractually secure land plots and manage approval procedures.\n- Regularly exchange information on project status and provide political accompaniment for projects.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Several years of relevant professional experience in a comparable position.\n- Completed degree in Renewable Energies or a comparable field.\n- Practical experience in the application of project management tools.\n- Very good German and good English language skills.\n- Proficient in MS Office.\n- Reliable work ethic, both independently and as part of a team.\n- Driver's license and willingness to travel for business.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting opportunity for an experienced Project Manager to significantly contribute to the energy transition. The company offers a supportive environment with excellent benefits, including a robust salary, flexible working hours, mobile work options, 30 days of annual leave, and paid professional development. You'll be part of a team driving large-scale wind projects, directly impacting a sustainable future. If you're passionate about renewable energy and project management, this role provides the perfect platform to make a real difference.
