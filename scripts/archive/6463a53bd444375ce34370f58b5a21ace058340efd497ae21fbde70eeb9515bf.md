**Position Overview**

We’re looking for a Sales Specialist to drive outbound prospecting and lead generation for our partner’s AI platform. As one of their first sales hires, you’ll play a key role in building the top of the funnel and shaping the company’s go-to-market strategy. This role suits someone who’s entrepreneurial, target-driven, and eager to grow in a dynamic startup environment.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Conduct full-time outbound prospecting through LinkedIn, email, and calls to identify and engage potential leads.
*   Book and schedule qualified meetings with prospective clients.
*   Use tools such as Apollo, Instantly, and Sales Navigator to build and manage outreach campaigns.
*   Track and maintain lead activity, metrics, and engagement data accurately.
*   Collaborate with leadership to refine outreach strategies and improve conversion rates.
*   Support basic sales enablement initiatives, such as short demo videos or outreach content, when needed.
*   Maintain strong communication and professionalism in every client interaction.

**Requirements**

**Must-Have:**

*   Minimum of 3 years of experience in outbound sales, business development, or lead generation.
*   Proven track record of booking meetings and exceeding outreach targets.
*   Excellent English communication and presentation skills.
*   Hands-on experience using modern sales tools (e.g., Apollo, Instantly, HubSpot, LinkedIn Sales Navigator).
*   Comfortable working in U.S. time zones (ET or PT).
*   Highly proactive and self-driven, with strong ownership and attention to detail.

**Nice-to-Have:**

*   Experience in SaaS, tech, or AI-related industries.
*   Familiarity with generative AI tools and basic sales content creation.
*   Previous experience in a startup or fast-scaling environment.
