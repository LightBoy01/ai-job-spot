### Description
The company's work is driven by a passion for authentic content, compelling stories, and inspiring narratives, always aiming to touch people and encourage reflection. They are seeking a talented Video Editor (Cutter) with a keen eye for timing, trends, and aesthetics, who enjoys collaboratively transforming raw material into captivating content.

### Responsibilities
*   Cut, shorten, and optimize video content for platforms like Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and others.
*   Create Reels, Shorts, and Story formats with a consistent and engaging look & feel.
*   Adapt video formats (e.g., portrait/landscape, subtitles, sounds, transitions).
*   Collaborate closely and directly with the Creator on content selection and implementation.
*   Support creative idea development and content planning.

### Qualifications
*   First experience in video editing or ongoing studies in media, film, communication, design, or a comparable field.
*   Proficiency with CapCut, Adobe Premiere Pro, or Final Cut Pro.
*   Understanding of social media trends, viral dynamics, and storytelling.
*   Creativity, a good eye for aesthetics, and a strong sense of timing.
*   Independent, reliable work ethic, and strong communication skills.
