### Description
Nara is an innovative Multi-Agent AI platform designed for enterprises, with the mission to automate complex business processes and significantly boost employee productivity and satisfaction. We are seeking a dedicated Senior Software Developer to join our team, taking end-to-end responsibility for features within our TypeScript monorepo.

In this role, you will collaborate closely with our Product, Design, and Platform teams, actively shaping architectural decisions and contributing to a scalable and maintainable codebase. This is a full-time, permanent position based in Würzburg, Germany, offering a remote-friendly setup within Germany with optional onsite or hybrid arrangements. We are looking for someone to start immediately.

### Responsibilities
As a Senior Software Developer, your key responsibilities will include:
*   Conceiving and implementing features across our web and service layers, leveraging technologies such as Next.js 15, Node.js, and TypeScript.
*   Developing robust, typed APIs and real-time flows using WebSockets, and integrating with various external services.
*   Designing and implementing data models and complex queries with PostgreSQL and Prisma.
*   Delivering high-quality, testable, and modular software with a strong emphasis on type-safety, and actively participating in code reviews.
*   Contributing to the evolution of our CI/CD pipelines, linting configurations, type-checking processes, and build systems within our pnpm monorepo.

### Qualifications
To succeed in this role, you should possess:
*   Proven professional experience with TypeScript and Node.js.
*   Strong experience with modern web development stacks, including React and Next.js, and working with strictly typed APIs.
*   Proficiency in relational databases, with practical experience in PostgreSQL.
*   A high standard for software quality, demonstrated through practices like thorough testing, linting, and CI/CD.
*   Good German language skills are required, as the team operates in both German and English.
