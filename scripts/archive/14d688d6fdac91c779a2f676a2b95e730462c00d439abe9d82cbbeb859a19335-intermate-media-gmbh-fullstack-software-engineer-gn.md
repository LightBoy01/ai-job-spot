### Description\nIntermate is one of the largest social media and influencer agencies in the DACH region, with over 250 employees across Berlin, Hamburg, Cologne, and remote locations. They specialize in developing social-first strategies, producing content in their studios, collaborating with creators, and managing data-driven campaigns using proprietary technology for renowned brands across lifestyle, FMCG, and tech sectors. As part of SAMY, a global agency network with over 970 experts in 20 locations, Intermate offers international perspectives and significant growth opportunities.\n\n**Please note:** The application process for this role is partly supported by an AI-powered assistant, which will conduct the initial introductory interview.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop, implement, and maintain software solutions.\n- Contribute to design and architectural decisions in the team based on Ports & Adapters and Event-Driven Architecture principles.\n- Write and optimize clean, scalable, and maintainable object-oriented code (OOP).\n- Write unit, integration, acceptance, and smoke tests to ensure software quality.\n- Identify, analyze, and resolve software bugs.\n- Create and maintain technical diagrams and documentation.\n- Analyze requirements and develop functional specifications.\n- Work closely with design and Head of Product teams to develop user-centric applications within a cross-functional team.\n- Participate in code reviews to ensure adherence to development standards.\n- Apply agile development methods like SCRUM.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Proficiency in the following technologies:\n  - **Backend**: NodeJS, Express, TypeScript, JavaScript\n  - **Frontend**: React, MobX (State Management), Webpack (Builder)\n  - **Testing**: Cypress, Mocha, Jest, Istanbul\n  - **Tools**: Jira, Zabbix, AWS, Sentry, Mixpanel, Figma\n- Strong interest in agile working methods and initial experience with SCRUM.\n- High-quality standards, interest in writing clean code, and a willingness to test it.\n- Strong communication skills in English.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at Intermate offers an excellent opportunity for a software developer to thrive in a dynamic, global social media agency.
