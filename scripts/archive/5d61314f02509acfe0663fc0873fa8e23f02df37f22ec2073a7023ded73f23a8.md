**Company Description:**

Informa Festivals marks an exciting new chapter in B2B events, bringing some of the world's most influential creative, technology, and business festivals together. We’re combining prestigious events like Cannes Lions, Black Hat, Money20/20, GDC, and London Tech Week with cutting-edge marketing intelligence platforms and advisory services to deliver unmissable experiences and insights.

Our festival-led approach reimagines traditional B2B events, creating powerful, immersive experiences that drive innovation and industry advancement. Building on decades of expertise and powered by Informa's international reach, we're shaping the next generation of business festivals.

**Job Description:**

**This role is based in our 5 Howick Place office.**

The Strategy Manager is a critical member of the Informa Festivals strategy team, responsible for developing and validating strategic initiatives. The role will develop vertical-level strategic thinking, manage key projects and programmes, and deliver a comprehensive growth agenda that includes market analysis, market penetration, product innovation, geo-expansion, 365-expansion, festivalisation and business development through acquisition and partnership.

**Key Responsibilities**

**Strategic Vision & Planning**

*   Deliver the vertical strategy process, including the development of a five-year ambition, three-year plan and annual budget.
*   Define key growth levers, strategic roadmap, and objectives to drive sustainable growth.
*   Support the brand strategy by identifying key markets, audiences, customers, products, GTM (Go-to-Market) strategies, geographic and 365 (non-event) opportunities.

*   Maintain an external focus on market and competitors to identify industry developments, trends and innovations, and derive implications and recommendations.
*   Conduct in-depth market analysis to inform the vertical’s long-term strategy and to identify opportunities for business growth and improvement.

**Product Innovation**

*   Support product innovation, getting new products ideated, validated, planned and launched.

**Business Development**

*   Contribute to the division business development agenda, including M&A pipeline development and target evaluation.
*   Assist on live deals, overseeing assessment, strategic thesis formulation, business modeling, due diligence, and combination planning.

**Intelligence & Analytics**

*   Deliver divisional Business Intelligence, building capabilities in insights and analytics related to strategy, brand, vertical and divisional performance.

**Key Capabilities**

*   **Commercial Judgement:** Demonstrates significant commercial acumen and applies strategic and commercial judgement whilst maintaining a sense of pace to ensure recommended actions are well-considered, executable, and deliver meaningful results.
*   **Relationship building:** Excels at partnering with other areas of the business, so they are seen as indispensable thought partners and co-owners of the vertical growth agenda.
*   **Disruption and Innovation:** Embraces and drives change and encourages innovative thinking and ways of getting things done for the greater good. Open to new ideas and willing to challenge the status quo to drive commercial and strategic progress.
*   **Growth mindset:** Embodies a growth and learning mindset, focusing on continuous improvement and learning from success and failure and helping others do the same. Adaptable and resilient, ready to take on challenges and turn them into opportunities.
*   **Humility and collaboration:** Embodies humility with a genuine willingness to share knowledge, listen actively, and collaborate with others. Creates an inclusive environment where diverse perspectives are valued and integrated into decision-making processes.

**Qualifications:**

**Education & Experience**

*   Proven experience in a senior strategic role, especially at a consulting firm.
*   Strong understanding of market analysis, strategic planning, and execution.
*   Experience of acquisitions, partnerships, and transformation projects is a plus.
*   Familiarity with intelligence, data analytics, and programme management frameworks.
*   An MBA or equivalent advanced degree is preferred.
*   Strong PowerPoint and Excel skills are a must; an early adopter of AI.
