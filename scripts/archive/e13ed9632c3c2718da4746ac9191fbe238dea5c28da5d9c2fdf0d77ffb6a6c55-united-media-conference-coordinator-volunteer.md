### Description\nAre you eager to expand your professional network with over 500 leaders, entrepreneurs, investors, and general/limited partners in the Private Equity sector, while simultaneously developing valuable leadership and event management skills? This is your chance to join Private Equity Insights, an organization renowned for hosting the largest global events that foster finance and investment innovation.\n\nPrivate Equity Insights connects C-level executives, GPs, LPs, entrepreneurs, and investors, bringing together major industry players like Blackstone, BlackRock, Apollo, Carlyle, CVC, KKR, and Permira. We are seeking ambitious and talented individuals to join a team of 25+ peers to help build, run, and deliver the leading French Private Equity Conference.\n\nOur volunteers are the backbone of our events; \"Private Equity Insights would not exist without our volunteers. Simple as that.\" Join us to gain an insider's view of how the world's largest Private Equity community is built.\n\nThe conference will take place at the Marriott Hotel City West in Munich on the 26th of September 2025. You can get a better sense of our events by watching the [2023 Milan's highlight video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1w9amNARIROInji1jERo8KzgPLCfemnQM/view?usp=drive_link) and find more information on our website [here](https://pe-insights.com/private-equity-conferences/france/).\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a Private Equity Conference Volunteer, you will be integral to the successful execution of the event, with responsibilities including:\n- Actively participating in a mandatory 2-hour training session and rehearsals the afternoon before the conference.\n- Assisting the Private Equity Insights team and a group of 25+ peers in the setup, running, and delivery of the conference.\n- Contributing to the creation of a seamless and engaging experience for 500+ high-profile attendees, including leaders, entrepreneurs, and investors.\n- Supporting various logistical and operational aspects of the event on the conference day.\n\n### Qualifications\nWe are looking for individuals who possess:\n- Ambition and talent, with a proactive approach to learning and networking.\n- A strong desire to expand their professional network within the Private Equity and broader finance sectors.\n- Eagerness to develop soft skills, leadership capabilities, and competencies in communication and event management.\n- The ability to collaborate effectively as part of a dynamic team of peers.\n- Commitment to attend the pre-conference training session and dedicate the full day of the conference to the role.\n- An interest in gaining valuable insights into the Private Equity industry and large-scale event organization.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis isn't merely a volunteer opportunity; it's a golden ticket to unparalleled networking, hands-on learning, and exclusive access within the elite world of Private Equity. For a minimal time commitment (2 hours pre-event training + conference day), you gain the chance to connect with 500+ industry leaders, receive individual management training, and earn an official volunteer certificate.\n\nThe exclusive invitation to the 2025 Private Equity Insights Dinner after the event offers a unique chance for deeper connections with like-minded professionals and the Private Equity Insights team. This role is a significant resume booster and an invaluable experience for anyone aspiring to build a career in finance, investment, or event management, offering a true insider's perspective on how a leading industry event is orchestrated.
