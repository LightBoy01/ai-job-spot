### Your role

Are you an expert when it comes to tools like Automation Anywhere? Do you have a proven ability to solve complex issues? Do you have a track record of improving processes?  
  
We’re looking for a Tech Engineer to:  
• Support and maintain the mission critical Robotics Process Automation (RPA) central platform across several technical domains  
• Establish support excellency across our all our products and processes provided to our automation business globally  
• Interact with automation business clients for support and request management  
• Deploy controlled releases through the environment chain up to production  
• Define workarounds for known errors and initiate process improvements

### Your expertise

• Bachelor’s and/or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering or related technical discipline  
• Hands-on experience with Automation Anywhere products at a medium to large enterprise, including knowledge of the administration of the AA Control Room (CR, API, BLM, etc.) and robot code development  
• Incident, Change and Problem Management experience, including experience of system monitoring integration and application performance management (APM) platforms: e.g. Azure Monitor / Application Insights, Cisco AppDynamics, Microsoft System Center Operation Manager (SCOM), Splunk  
• Strong troubleshooting skills at Windows desktop level, including Microsoft Office products and ability to understand and improve automation logic or code  
• Proficient with the administration and support of Windows client and server operating systems, including good experience with Cloud platforms like Microsoft Azure  
• Solid network and Microsoft MSSQL database administration and support knowledge, able to read and write TSQL queries  
• Good knowledge of scripting languages, e.g. HTML, Microsoft Visual Basic for Application (VBA), PowerShell, VBScript  
• ITIL Knowledge and certification are desired
