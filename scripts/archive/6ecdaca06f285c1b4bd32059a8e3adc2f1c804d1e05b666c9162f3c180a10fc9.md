**Purpose of the role**

*   Senior Analyst will be responsible for period end close activities, ensures accuracy and timeliness in processing all **general ledger entries, fixed assets transaction, bank transactions, financial and management reporting, accounts reconciliation, statutory audit and tax computation** while adhering to WPP group policies and company procedures. He/She is responsible to regularly track and resolve outstanding issues and escalate to relevant parties as necessary.

**Job Description**

*   Ensure accuracy and timeliness in processing general ledger entries, fixed assets transaction, banking transaction, financial and management reporting, accounts reconciliation, statutory audit and tax computation in accordance with SLAs
*   Work closely with WPP Operating Units (OpCo) Finance to understand current activities and ensure all changes are reflected in financial statement.
*   Collaborate with OpCo to clear aged balance sheet items.
*   Regularly track and resolve outstanding issues being identified and escalate to relevant parties as necessary.
*   Liaise with internal/external stakeholders as and when necessary
*   Ensure service level agreements (SLA) and monthly performance and productivity KPIs are met
*   Ensure the ownership for all applicable internal controls related to RTR processes.
*   Proactively identify, propose and implement continuous process improvement opportunities in existing processes within the team
*   Participate in continuous process improvement projects and plans
*   Involvement in ad hoc activities or project.
*   Perform review work on analyst
*   Perform knowledge transfer with new joiner
*   Works independently within guidelines and policies, and receives minimal guidance.
*   Acts as a resource for colleagues with less experience and provides functional guidance.

**Qualifications**

*   Bachelor Degree in Accounting or related field, and/or professional accounting qualification (ACCA/CPA/MIA etc.)
*   2 - 3 years of experience in accounting/ finance functions.
*   Experience in Audit and tax is an added advantage.
*   Having supervisory experience is an added advantage.
*   Exposure to MNC working environment and Consolidated Group Accounts will be added advantage

**Skill and Competencies**

*   Good communication skill and able to communicate effectively in English
*   Knowledge in financial accounting principles, processes and accounting standard such as IFRS.
*   Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills
