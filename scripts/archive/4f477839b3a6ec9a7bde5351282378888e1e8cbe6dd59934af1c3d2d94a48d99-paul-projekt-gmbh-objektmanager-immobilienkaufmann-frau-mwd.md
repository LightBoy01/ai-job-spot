### Description
Paul Projekt GmbH is an established service company in the real estate sector in Central Hesse, Germany. With over 20 years of experience in property management, we support real estate owners and investors in the administration, marketing, development, and rental of residential and commercial buildings. Our well-trained employees are characterized by high professional competence.

We offer an interesting and diverse range of tasks, comprehensive onboarding, and a pleasant team with a very collaborative working atmosphere. You can expect fulfilling work and performance-based remuneration.

### Responsibilities
*   Organizing and conducting viewings, concluding rental agreements, handing over and taking over rental units.
*   Processing terminations, including pre-inspections, handovers, and takeovers.
*   Acquiring prospective tenants through advertisements on internet portals.
*   Assessing and arranging apartment modernizations upon tenant changes.
*   Preparing utility bill statements.
*   Communicating with owners, tenants, caretakers, craftspeople, insurance companies, as well as other service providers and authorities.
*   Planning, commissioning, and coordinating repairs and maintenance measures.
*   Recording, reporting, and tracking (warranty) defects.
*   Property inspections and managing necessary measures.
*   Handling building technology, contracts, and services.

### Qualifications
*   Commercial vocational training as a real estate agent (Immobilienkauffrau/-mann) or comparable qualification, or several years of professional experience in the administration, marketing, development, and rental of residential and commercial buildings, or a degree in real estate economics.
*   Independent, responsible, and structured way of working.
*   Proficient in MS-Office applications.
*   Experience in using social media marketing to increase efficiency.
*   Excellent customer and service orientation.
*   Convincing, polite demeanor.
*   Car driver's license.
