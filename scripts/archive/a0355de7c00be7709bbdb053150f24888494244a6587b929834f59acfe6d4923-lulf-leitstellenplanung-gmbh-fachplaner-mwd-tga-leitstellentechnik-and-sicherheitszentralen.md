### Description\nLülf+ and its specialized subsidiary, Lülf+ Leitstellenplanung GmbH, are leading consultants and planners in hazard prevention, focusing on the conception, planning, and realization of state-of-the-art control centers, security centers, and alarm infrastructures. We serve public and private sector organizations, ensuring safety, redundancy, and future viability in critical infrastructure projects across Germany.\n\nAs a Specialist Planner (m/w/d) for Technical Building Equipment with a focus on Control Center Technology and Security Centers, you will be a key part of our interdisciplinary team. This full-time, immediate-start position involves supporting challenging projects for entities ranging from industrial and airport control centers to municipal emergency centers.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Lead the planning and expert coordination of control and security centers through all HOAI service phases (LP 1–9).\n- Conduct thorough stock analyses, feasibility studies, and develop comprehensive technical concepts and service specifications.\n- Design advanced control, alarm, and redundancy concepts for security-critical systems.\n- Facilitate workshops, provide expert client consultation, and coordinate effectively with authorities and other specialist trades.\n- Oversee projects from the initial design phase through execution, including meticulous construction supervision and documentation.\n- Uphold the highest quality standards by adhering to structured processes, norms, and critical industry standards (e.g., DIN EN 50518, DIN EN 50600, ISO 27001 BSI-Grundschutz).\n\n### Qualifications\n- A completed (engineering) degree or certification as a state-certified technician in Informatics, Electrical Engineering, Telecommunications, Rescue Services, or a related technical field.\n- A minimum of 3 years of professional experience in Technical Building Equipment (TGA) planning, with a strong preference for experience in control center technology or security-relevant systems.\n- Solid understanding of the HOAI structure and established planning procedures.\n- Proficiency in MS Office applications; familiarity with CAD or Visio is a plus.\n- Excellent German language skills, both written and verbal.\n- Demonstrated communication strength, exceptional organizational skills, and a proactive, solution-oriented, and structured approach to work.\n- Possession of a Class B driver's license is required.\n- Desirable: Proven experience from previous professional projects or through voluntary/full-time involvement with authorities and organizations having security tasks (e.g., fire department or aid organization).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a high-impact Mid-Senior level opportunity for a dedicated Technical Building Equipment planner with a passion for critical infrastructure. Lülf+ offers a stimulating environment where your work directly contributes to public safety and the resilience of essential services across Germany. The company prides itself on a collaborative, flat-hierarchical culture that encourages individual autonomy and continuous professional development through targeted training and certifications. With flexible working hours, home office options, and a strong team culture focused on quality and innovation, this role is perfect for a detail-oriented professional seeking to make a significant difference in a vital sector.
