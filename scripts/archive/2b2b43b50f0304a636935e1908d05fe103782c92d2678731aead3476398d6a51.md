**About this role**

The Global Marketing & Digital Wealth organization at BlackRock is a dynamic function that plays a meaningful role in helping drive the firm’s cross-functional initiatives. We specialize in a range of functions to provide campaigns, advertising, communications, training/education material, investment, and events. We are supported by global centers of excellence spanning content strategy, research & analytics, and digital platforms & experiences.

  
**Your team:**  
 

The COO Team provides business management and oversight of the Global Marketing & Digital Wealth organization, including measurement and insights, talent strategy, operating and organizational effectiveness, and risk management.

We are seeking a Strategic Initiatives Lead based in New York or San Francisco.

**Your Role and Responsibilities:**
-----------------------------------

*   Work closely with the COO of Global Marketing & Digital Wealth on presentations for leadership meetings such as BlackRock’s Board of Directors and Executive committees.
    
*   Prepare memos and talking points for business objectives, priorities/updates and other strategic topics in Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR)
    
*   Synthesize information into concise communications tailored for audiences (executives, partners, teams).
    
*   Support and project-manage cross-functional strategic initiatives; build and manage project plans, overseeing interdependencies and risks.
    
*   Support internal communications and engagement strategies for initiatives.
    
*   Translate data and insights into actionable strategies aligned with business priorities.  
     
    

**You have...**
---------------

*   5 ore more years of experience in strategy, consulting, corporate development, chief-of-staff/COO support, or financial services/asset management.
    
*   The ability to lead multiple priorities simultaneously while being detail-oriented
    
*   A knack for being adaptable and proactive, with strong problem-solving and critical-thinking skills.
    
*   Professional maturity, discretion, and sound judgment when handling sensitive information.
    
*   Interest in financial services, digital wealth, and marketing strategy.
    
*   AI experience in content creation, presentations and idea generation
    

  
**Core Skills:**

*   **Executive communication:** Strong writing and presentation skills; proven ability to create high-quality materials for senior and board-level audiences.
    
*   **Strategic thinking:** Ability to structure ambiguous problems, develop frameworks, and synthesize insights into clear recommendations.
    
*   **Analytical ability:** Comfortable working with data, distilling key trends, and connecting analysis to strategic implications.
    
*   **Project management skills:** Demonstrated success leading cross-functional initiatives, managing timelines, and driving accountability.
    
*   **Stakeholder management:** Skilled at building trusted relationships across senior leaders and diverse teams.
