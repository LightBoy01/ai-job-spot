### Description
fleetster is a leading SaaS company based in Munich, dedicated to revolutionizing mobility with smart solutions for global fleets and car-sharing services. Our sophisticated software platform is at the core of our operations, empowering over 100,000 users across 28 countries in 9 languages. We are a team of smart, driven individuals committed to redefining the future of mobility and building a better tomorrow.

As we continue to expand our innovative product suite and ensure top-tier quality, we are actively seeking a **Senior Full-Stack Engineer (m/f/d)** to join our dynamic team in Munich. This is an exciting opportunity to contribute significantly to our architectural evolution and make a tangible impact on our global product.

### Responsibilities
As a Senior Full-Stack Engineer, your key responsibilities will include:

*   Planning and developing new features and functionalities within our collaborative international implementation team (approximately 15 colleagues).
*   Actively contributing to the renewal of our core architecture by introducing modern tools and frameworks, gradually migrating and enhancing existing software components.
*   Investigating and meticulously analyzing bug reports, providing expert assistance to our support team in resolving critical customer issues.

### Qualifications
To succeed in this role, we are looking for a candidate with:

*   **Experience**: 5+ years of hands-on professional development experience. Experience in B2B, SaaS, and fleet management domains is considered a significant advantage.
*   **Technical Proficiency**: Proven experience with our current tech stack, including:
    *   **Frontend**: React / Redux, Typescript / JavaScript, Cypress / Jest, Blueprint (UI Library).
    *   **Backend**: NodeJS, Express (with a strategic move towards NestJS), TypeScript (some legacy JavaScript), MongoDB, AWS (various services).
*   **Communication**: Strong English communication skills, both verbal and written, to thrive in our international environment.
*   **Location**: A strong preference for candidates living in or near Munich, as we champion a true hybrid working culture that values in-person team connection.
