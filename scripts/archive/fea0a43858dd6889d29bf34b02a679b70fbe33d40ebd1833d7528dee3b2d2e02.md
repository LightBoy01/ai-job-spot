Main Tasks
----------

• Full procurement of production and non-production materials for the plant – excluding logistics scope  
• Achieving local procurement targets based on defined KPIs  
• Continuous communication with suppliers and identification of new suppliers  
• Ensuring compliance with and enforcement of procurement processes  
• Managing RFQs, tenders, and conducting auctions  
• Approval of purchase orders  
• Coordinating the work of the local procurement team  
• Implementing cost control and cost efficiency at the site  
• Keeping contracts and procurement documentation continuously updated  
• Ensuring compliance with legal and corporate regulations  
• Preparing reports and analyses for senior management

Your Profile
------------

• First procurement contact for the site in all local, commercial, and supplier-related tasks, including escalations  
• Support for the site (especially supply chain management) for all procurement-related topics  
• Interface management between the site and the responsible regional procurement lead, including all procurement functions  
• Ensure the active involvement of Procurement via the respective Procurement lead or Strategic Purchasing  
• Core team member of the “Change Committee”, responsible for driving local supplier and procurement-related change projects  
• Invoice resolutions (first-level price issues)
