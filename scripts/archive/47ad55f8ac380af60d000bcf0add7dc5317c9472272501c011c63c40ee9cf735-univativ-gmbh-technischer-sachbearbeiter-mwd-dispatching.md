### Description

This role is with univativ, a project and personnel service provider dedicated to connecting students and graduates with exciting opportunities at renowned companies. Your career development is our mission and our passion.

This is a project-based role from **January 1, 2026, to December 1, 2026**, with the **option for extension and potential permanent takeover**. It's a **38-hour per week** position based in **Bad Waldsee** within the **energy sector**, open to both **entry-level and experienced professionals**. The annual salary ranges from **€56,000 to €62,000**. (Project ID A202551533_1S)

Do you excel at ensuring that orders reach their destination quickly, coordinated, and reliably? As a **Technical Clerk (m/f/d) in Dispatching**, you will be responsible for the acceptance, coordination, and follow-up of both internal and external orders. Your key tasks will include planning the deployment of operational staff, managing their daily assignments, and ensuring that any disruptions are efficiently resolved within regular working hours.

We are committed to giving your career a significant boost! You'll kickstart your journey with our client in the energy sector, where you'll join an innovative team. Here, you'll have the opportunity to contribute to exciting projects and continuously expand your knowledge. Does this sound like a compelling career path? Then we encourage you to apply today!

As part of our commitment to your growth, we offer diverse on- and off-the-job training opportunities, including various certifications and individualized further education, to help you become an expert. We also offer the flexibility to **work remotely up to 50%** of the time. You can also benefit from our "Recruit a Friend" program, offering an attractive bonus of up to €2,000.

### Responsibilities

*   You will receive internal and external orders, taking charge of work preparation and structured follow-up.
*   The coordination of orders will be your responsibility, ensuring everything is in the right place at the right time.
*   As a Technical Clerk (m/f/d), you will plan and efficiently manage the deployment of operational staff.
*   You will confidently coordinate disruptions during regular working hours, ensuring their swift and reliable resolution.

### Qualifications

*   You have completed vocational training as a network technician, master electrician, or possess a comparable qualification.
*   Experience in power grid operation is an advantage.
*   You are proficient and confident in using common IT applications.
*   An independent and systematic approach to work, flexibility, and strong teamwork skills are among your strengths.
*   You possess at least business-fluent German language skills.
