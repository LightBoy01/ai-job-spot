**Twin Health** 

At Twin Health, we empower people to improve and prevent chronic metabolic diseases, like type 2 diabetes and obesity, with a new standard of care. Twin Health is the only company applying AI Digital Twin technology exclusively toward metabolic health. 

We start by building a dynamic model of each person’s metabolism — drawing on thousands of data points from CGMs, smartwatches, and meal logs — that maps their personal path to better health. Guided by a dedicated clinical care team, our members have lowered their A1C below the diabetes range, achieved lasting weight loss, and reduced or even eliminated medications, all while living healthier, happier lives.

**Opportunity**

We are looking for an AI Applications Engineer to join our team and help shape the next generation of deeper activity experiences. This is an exciting opportunity to work at the intersection of backend engineering and AI, collaborating closely with backend, mobile, and AI engineers, as well as clinical teams, to build innovative solutions. Your work will empower members with personalized experiences that enhance engagement and support overall metabolic well-being.

**Responsibilities** 

*   Contribute to the design, development, and deployment of AI agents and tools that streamline clinical decision-making and enhance clinical workflows.
*   Explore and implement full-stack architecture for AI-agentic systems, from context engineering to API design and deployment.
*   Develop and maintain robust, scalable backend services and integrations (using Python, Java Spring Boot, SQL) that serve as the foundation for our AI applications.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to help translate clinical and operational needs into technical specifications and prototypes.
*   Follow best practices for developing reliable, secure, and scalable AI applications, ensuring all solutions meet compliance standards such as HIPAA.
*   Monitor and optimize the performance of AI systems in production to handle growth and ensure a seamless user experience.
*   Take on additional responsibilities as needed, including on-call rotations.
*   Regular travel to our Mountain View headquarters for planning workshops and team collaboration sessions.
*   Other duties as assigned

**Qualifications** 

*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a STEM-related field.
*   2+ years of software engineering experience, with a foundation in building backend services.
*   Demonstrated experience (professional or personal projects) in developing AI-powered applications using Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT, Claude, or Gemini.
*   Familiarity with prompt engineering, vector databases, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
*   Proficiency in Java and/or Python and experience developing microservices.
*   Experience with SQL databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL).
*   Ability to work across disciplines, collaborating effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
*   Strong communication skills and a proactive approach to problem-solving.
*   Experience in healthcare, biosensors, or related fields is a plus but not required.
*   This remote opportunity based out of the U.S. Applicants must be authorized to work for any employer in the U.S. We are unable to sponsor or take over sponsorship of an employment Visa at this time.
