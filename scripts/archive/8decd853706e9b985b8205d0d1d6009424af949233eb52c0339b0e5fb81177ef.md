**Your Career**

At Federated Wireless, you’ll work on cutting-edge wireless tech, geospatial analytics, and AI-driven optimization—real innovation. Join a team that values creativity, impact, and making wireless networks smarter.

We’re looking for a talented Full Stack Engineer and collaborative problem solver to take ownership of our applications. You will be responsible for independently designing, developing, and maintaining these robust and scalable solutions through the entire agile life cycle. The ideal candidate is passionate about building high-performance, stable software and is committed to staying current with the latest technologies and trends in web development and AI/ML.

**Your Impact**

*   Design, develop, test, and document Cloud-based as well as stand-alone AI-driven micro-services with support for RESTful APIs and UI
*   Integrate AI-powered features into applications
*   Configure automated system integration through CI/CD
*   Track different aspects of development and testing work in an Agile process
*   Create automated tests leveraging AI/ML 
*   Follow security guidance in the development process as well as in SW design

**Your Experience**

*   BS or MS in Computer Science, or related fields
*   Proficient in programming in Python and/or Java
*   Proficient in database: Mongo and/or PostgreSQL
*   Experience in front-end GUI and back-end server development
*   Experience in UI technologies such as JavaScript, React, Redux, HTML5, CSS/SASS
*   Experience with AI/ML frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-Learn, or OpenAI APIs.
*   Familiarity with AWS Cloud and native services such as EC2, S3
*   Familiarity with MLOps best practices for AI deployment and monitoring
*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills
*   Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills
*   Proactive and takes initiative to identify and address potential problems or opportunities
*   Highly adaptable and comfortable working in a fast-paced, dynamic environment
*   Self-motivation, teamwork and experience working in a fast-paced agile environment are highly desired

Federated Wireless is unable to provide F1-OPT or other visa sponsorship.

**Our Commitment** 

We’re trailblazers who dream big, take risks, and drive innovation. Our collaborative team spirit enables the best and brightest minds to align on common goals. We will stand behind you to enable your success.

**Location**

DC or Boston Metro areas. Onsite at either our Arlington, VA or Boston, MA office.

*   401(k) Plan with employer match.
*   Subsidized commuter benefits.
*   Paid time off, holidays, and flexible work options.
*   Opportunities for professional growth and development.
