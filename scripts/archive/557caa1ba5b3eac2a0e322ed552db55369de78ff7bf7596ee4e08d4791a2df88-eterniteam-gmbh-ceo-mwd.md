### Description

EterniTeam is solving Germany's biggest entrepreneurial challenge: **corporate succession.** With a revolutionary new approach, we secure the succession of approximately 1 million businesses that need to arrange their handover this decade alone. Our model ensures that all parties benefit: the outgoing and succeeding entrepreneurs, and the employees.

For this vital mission, we are seeking talented **Executive Leaders** to take over the management and ownership of an established business with an annual turnover between 2 and 10 million Euros. We don't expect you to invest your own capital; instead, we seek your talent and long-term commitment.

We consider ourselves unbiased talent scouts. To find the ideal solution for each corporate succession, we utilize personality profiles and cultural matching. The perfect fit for each company is determined individually during the placement process. We are looking for future Managing Directors who thrive in leading people and taking full responsibility for results. Our proprietary training program closes any potential skill gaps, and our post-placement support assists with specific tasks and through continuous exchange.

### Responsibilities

*   Lead an established, sustainably profitable business with a turnover between 1 million and 10 million Euros, typically employing 12 to 40 people.
*   Create and/or strengthen a positive company culture and a spirit of change through engaged and empathetic leadership.
*   Assume full profit & loss (P&L) responsibility for your company, with a focus on income statements and cash flow.
*   Introduce necessary modernization with our support, for example, by implementing improved systems and processes.
*   Act as the primary contact for key customers and/or suppliers, building and expanding crucial relationships.
*   Champion company participation: just like you, all your employees will receive real shares in the company that grow in value each year. This facilitates your start, and you ensure the necessary motivational boost reaches the team through transparent communication.
*   Collaborate closely with our Operations Team, especially during the initial transition period.
*   Enjoy creative freedom in growing and transforming the company, ensuring all employees are actively involved in the journey.

### Qualifications

*   Minimum of 5 years of proven leadership experience managing at least 5 employees, with a total of at least 10 years of professional experience.
*   Business-fluent German language skills are essential.
*   Experience in corporate management or significant business/P&L responsibility is a strong plus.
*   Industry-specific experience or state-recognized certifications are each a plus.
*   Commitment to a long-term engagement: taking on this role is not merely a career step; the company, through growing shares, becomes more and more 'your' company each year, implying a long-term commitment.
*   Possess a "hands-on" mentality for tackling tasks and overcoming apparent obstacles – you are in the cockpit of your company.
*   Your sense of responsibility – for your company, your team, and yourself – serves as a role model for those around you.
*   Your empathy, emotional intelligence, and confident, appreciative communication build trust in you and your work.
