### Description

EterniTeam is solving Germany's most pressing entrepreneurial challenge: business succession. With a new, revolutionary approach, we secure the future of the estimated one million companies that need to arrange their succession this decade. Our model ensures that all parties – outgoing entrepreneurs, incoming leaders, and employees – benefit from a seamless transition.

For this vital mission, we are seeking talented executive leaders to take over the management and ownership of established, sustainably profitable businesses with annual revenues between 2 and 10 million Euros. We don't require personal capital investment; instead, we seek your talent and enduring commitment. We see ourselves as unbiased talent finders, utilizing personality profiles and cultural matching to ensure the ideal fit between a leader and a company.

### Responsibilities

As a future CEO, you will thrive in leading people and taking full responsibility for results. Your core responsibilities will include:

*   Leading an established, sustainably profitable business, typically with 12 to 40 employees and an annual turnover between 1 million and 10 million Euros.
*   Creating and/or strengthening a positive company culture and fostering a spirit of change through engaged and empathetic leadership.
*   Holding full profit & loss (P&L) and cash flow responsibility for your company.
*   Introducing necessary modernization initiatives with our support, such as implementing improved systems and processes.
*   Acting as the primary point of contact for key customers and suppliers, actively building and expanding these crucial relationships.
*   Advocating for company participation: similar to your own role, you will ensure all employees receive real shares in the company, which grow in value annually, facilitating a motivational boost through transparent communication.
*   Collaborating closely with our Operations Team, especially during the initial phase of transition and change.
*   Exercising significant freedom in shaping the company's growth and evolution, while ensuring all employees are actively engaged and brought along on the journey.

### Qualifications

To excel in this entrepreneurial leadership role, you should possess:

*   A minimum of 5 years of proven leadership experience managing at least 5 employees, with a total of at least 10 years of professional experience.
*   Business-fluent German language skills.
*   Experience in corporate management or comprehensive business responsibility is a significant plus.
*   Specific industry experience or state-recognized certificates are also a plus.
*   A strong commitment to long-term engagement – this is not merely a career step; the company will increasingly become *your* company through growing shares each year, representing a long-term commitment.
*   A "hands-on" mentality when tackling tasks and overcoming perceived obstacles – you are in the cockpit of your company.
*   Exemplary sense of responsibility – for your company, your team, and yourself – serving as a role model for those around you.
*   Strong empathy, emotional intelligence, and confident, appreciative communication skills to build trust in yourself and your work.
