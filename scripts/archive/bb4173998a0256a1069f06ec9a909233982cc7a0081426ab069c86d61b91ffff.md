Sells a subset of product or services directly or via partners to large, enterprise accounts in the oil & gas industry.
Preferred location:  Los Angeles, Seattle, Portland, Phoenix, San Francisco
### Responsibilities
Primary job duty is to sell technology software products and related services in a defined, largely greenfield territory. Identifies, qualifies and closes new opportunities. Manages accounts including the entire sales process from business development prospecting and specifications through contract negotiations, signing, and post-sales support. Leverages the Oracle sales model to maximize revenue growth and increase local market share. Builds and expands business partner revenue and self sufficiency.
