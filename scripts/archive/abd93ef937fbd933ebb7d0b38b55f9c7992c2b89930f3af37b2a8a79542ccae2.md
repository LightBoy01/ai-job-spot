**What's the role?**

News Corp Australia is looking for a **Head of Domain Architecture Data & AI**, a leadership position responsible for overseeing the architecture of data and AI for News Corp Australia. This role is in charge of designing, implementing, and maintaining data & AI architecture strategies and solutions that align with the organisation's goals and objectives within that particular domain. The responsibilities include developing data & AI models and architectures, establishing data & AI governance policies, ensuring data & AI security and compliance, and optimising data for efficient data management and analysis. The head will have **direct reports of Data & AI Architects** and will collaborate with other domain teams and stakeholders to integrate data & AI architecture with other systems and processes across the organisation.

**Responsibilities:**

*   **Lead the Data & AI Architecture Strategy** – Oversee the overall architecture direction, roadmap, and design for all initiatives under the Data & AI domain, ensuring alignment with enterprise and global technology strategies.
    
*   **Develop and Govern Architectural Standards** – Establish and maintain architecture standards, patterns, and best practices that drive efficiency, reusability, and agility across systems and processes.
    
*   **Drive Innovation and Emerging Technologies** – Provide thought leadership in leveraging emerging technologies, including Generative AI, to deliver strategic business value.
    
*   **Collaborate and Align with Stakeholders** – Partner with enterprise architects, domain teams, and business leaders to ensure architecture solutions meet organisational goals and deliver measurable outcomes.
    
*   **Optimise Performance and Delivery** – Ensure architecture initiatives achieve key performance objectives such as cost efficiency, data utilisation, scalability, and speed to market.
    
*   **Build and Lead High-Performing Teams** – Foster a culture of innovation and continuous improvement through mentoring, training, and developing a skilled, empowered architecture team.
    

**Who are you?**

*   10+ years of architectural and design experience with a strong background in delivering modern data and AI solutions.
    
*   5+ years of technical experience across data platforms, AI/ML, analytics, and technologies such as data warehouses, data lakes, GCP, and CDP/DMP tools.
    
*   Proven ability to establish Data and AI roadmaps that align with business goals and enable innovation.
    
*   Demonstrated success in influencing stakeholders and driving business strategy through technology.
    
*   Strong focus on delivering scalable, efficient, and cost-effective architectural solutions that enhance data utilisation and business performance.
    
*   Collaborative and forward-thinking, fostering continuous improvement and exploration of emerging technologies.
