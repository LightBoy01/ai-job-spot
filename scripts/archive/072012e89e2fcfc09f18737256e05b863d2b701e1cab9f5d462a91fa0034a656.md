### Description

As the Lubrizol Surface Science (LSS) Process Technology Regional Lead, you will be at the forefront of reimagining what's possible in manufacturing. You'll play a critical role in developing next-generation, safe, and sustainable processes that create a significant competitive advantage. This position is central to driving our 'Most Effective Technology' process (METp), a framework for continuous evolution: METBase (our current standard), METPlus (the best achievable today), and METPrime (our vision for the future). You will lead the charge in advancing our technology to METPlus and Prime levels, while simultaneously partnering with global operations to maximize the efficiency of our current METBase. With a focus on coatings, personal care, or polymerization chemistry, you will guide process scale-ups, support new product launches, and champion global productivity initiatives within a cross-functional, collaborative team.

### Responsibilities

**Technology & Productivity Leadership:**
*   Collaborate with R&D and third-party partners (Tollers) to build a pipeline of METPlus and Prime process technologies.
*   Develop and manage MET dashboards and KPIs to quantify performance, measure potential, and track the value delivered by facility upgrades.
*   Identify and secure necessary pilot process equipment and technologies to accelerate learning and development.
*   Lead scale-up activities, ensuring rigorous data generation to confirm product quality and process capability.
*   Champion Lean and Design for Six Sigma methodologies to drive systematic improvements and implement effective controls.
*   Conduct product and process FMEAs to proactively manage and mitigate risks to process integrity and operator safety.
*   Ensure compliance and execution of Technology License Agreements with external partners.
*   Lead the execution of high-impact productivity projects and leverage insights across the global organization.

**Team Development & Mentorship:**
*   Lead the hiring, development, and mentoring of engineering talent to build a world-class, 21st-century engineering team.
*   Explore and integrate opportunities in Digitization, AI, and Generative AI to enhance engineering capabilities.
*   Foster a culture of creativity and agility, promoting effective brainstorming, lean start-up thinking, and rapid learning cycles.
*   Elevate the team's core competencies in engineering fundamentals, unit operations, Six Sigma methodologies, and risk assessment (FMEA).
*   Ensure the team demonstrates proficiency in first-principles thinking, project management, and technical documentation.

**Manufacturing & Operations Support:**
*   Work closely with plant operations to ensure global METBase performance standards are consistently achieved.
*   Provide expert support for regional product localization efforts as required.

### Qualifications

**Required:**
*   Bachelor of Science degree in Chemical Engineering or an equivalent from an accredited university.
*   A minimum of 7 years of experience in chemical manufacturing and/or process development.
*   A minimum of 3 years of experience managing and developing engineers.
*   Working knowledge of personal care, coatings, or polymerization chemistry.
*   Demonstrated success in driving change and delivering results within a matrix organization.
*   A proven record of developing teams to reach their full potential.
*   Excellent communication, networking, interpersonal, and leadership skills.
*   Strong project management skills and a continuous improvement mindset.

**Considered a Plus:**
*   An advanced degree in Chemical Engineering, Materials Science, Colloid Science, or Chemistry.
*   Subject matter expertise in personal care chemistry, manufacturing processes, and associated unit operations.
*   DMAIC or DFSS Six Sigma Green Belt or Black Belt certification with a history of practical application.
*   Project management certifications (e.g., PMP).

### Author's Take

**Q: The 'Most Effective Technology' (METp) framework sounds ambitious. Can you give me a practical example of how this role moves METp from a concept to a tangible business outcome?**

That's a great question because it gets to the heart of what makes this role so impactful. Think of METp as our roadmap from 'good' to 'game-changing.' Last year, a team was struggling with a METBase process for a key polymer that had high variability and waste. The leader in a role like this one didn't just tweak the existing parameters. They partnered with R&D to explore a novel mixing technology they'd seen at a conference—that was the seed of METPlus. They then led the pilot trials, built the business case, and secured the capital. The result was a 15% reduction in cycle time and a superior, more consistent product. Now, as the new lead, your challenge is to define METPrime for that same product line. Is it a continuous process instead of batch? Can we integrate a novel catalyst? You’re not just an optimizer; you’re the architect of our future state, empowered to explore, validate, and implement the technologies that will define our market leadership for the next decade.

**Q: The job description emphasizes developing a '21st Century Engineer' team. What does that mean in practice, and how does Lubrizol support its leaders in fostering that kind of innovative environment?**

We see a '21st Century Engineer' as more than just a technical expert. They are a creative problem-solver, a data scientist, and a collaborative innovator all in one. My role is to give my team the tools and, more importantly, the psychological safety to be all of those things. In practice, this means we dedicate time for 'what if' projects where failure is just a data point, not a penalty. We’ve invested in AI-powered simulation software that lets our junior engineers run dozens of virtual experiments in a single afternoon. As a leader here, you're not just delegating tasks; you're coaching your team on how to ask better questions. You're supported by a leadership culture that values calculated risks and actively removes bureaucratic hurdles. We want you to build a team that isn't afraid to challenge the status quo, and we'll give you the autonomy and resources to make that a reality.
