We are looking for a self-motivated engineer to join us in this role. This engineer will be
===========================================================================================
*   Deeply exposed to broad-based view of how Probe operations runs
*   Expected to identify opportunities for operations and process improvements through data analytics & propose solutions to realize gains from the identified opportunities
*   Expected to drive implementation of these solutions through both improvements to business process & deploying software solutions.
**Job Responsibilities :**
==========================
*   Enhance Probe’s capability to perform real-time reporting, monitoring and flagging of Production issues using Tableau & customized software solutions
*   Develop innovative software solutions for Engineering & Production teams to reduce turn-around time in resolving issues
*   Implement code to automate material tracking, product disposition & validation to ensure high-quality products for end-customers
*   Collaborate with the worldwide Probe community on projects with significant impact to Probe
**Job Requirements :**
======================
**_Education_**
*   Bachelor’s degree in Electronic Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science or equivalent combination of education and experience is preferred
**_Experience, Skills, Knowledge and Abilities_**
*   Candidates must have strong programming skills, regardless of programming languages.
*   Candidates with strong grasp of Perl, Linux, SQL, Tableau skills preferred
*   Candidates must have studied relevant courses including Computer Science/Engineering, Machine Learning and Programming.
*   Candidates must have relevant internship experience such as software engineer or data science related internship.
*   Candidates must have relevant project experience including software development, web development and AI-related projects.
*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills
*   Successfully demonstrated teamwork skills with a strong focus on effective team dynamics and strong problem solving skills and the ability to conduct root cause analysis and elimination
*   Proven ability to deliver results while working under pressure
