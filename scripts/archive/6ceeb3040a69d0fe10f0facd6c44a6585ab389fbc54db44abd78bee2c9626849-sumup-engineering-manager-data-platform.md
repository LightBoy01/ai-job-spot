**About the Data Platform Team:**

At SumUp, our Data Platform team is evolving from a multi-team setup towards a unified, initiative-based operating model. As part of the Platform Tribe, we're responsible for delivering scalable, high-impact data infrastructure that supports both operational and analytical workloads. Our engineers work in cross-functional initiative groups, collaborating closely with Product Managers and Engineering Managers to drive ownership and delivery of key milestones.

We're on a journey to strengthen our capabilities across data engineering, streaming services, and infrastructure—all with the goal of building "One Data Platform" that accelerates business impact across SumUp.

This is a unique opportunity to make a strategic and technical impact, enabling both our internal teams and customer-facing channels to operate at scale with greater intelligence and efficiency.

**What you'll do:**

*   Lead the Data Platform function through a strategic transition to an initiative-based operating model, enhancing focus and impact.
*   Drive collaboration and alignment across the Data Platform and broader organisation—including Machine Learning, Data Science, Data Analytics, and Engineering teams—to deliver a robust operational and analytical data infrastructure.
*   Provide technical leadership, define clear goals, and ensure high-quality execution across data engineering, streaming services, and infrastructure initiatives.
*   Partner closely with Product Managers to define milestones, manage priorities, and ensure accountability within initiative groups.
*   Coach and support engineers and team members in their professional growth, fostering both technical development and delivery excellence.
*   Lead strategic topics such as Kafka & PCI, Machine Learning operations, observability, and cost efficiencies (e.g., Snowflake optimization).
*   Work closely with Platform Infrastructure teams and lead key forums to drive alignment with stakeholders.  
      
    

**You'll be great for this role if you have:**

*   6+ years of experience in Software/Backend and Data Engineering, including at least 2 years of hands-on technical leadership and experience leading cross-functional teams.
*   Hands-on experience maintaining critical data infrastructure, such as Kafka and streaming processing services (e.g., Apache Flink, Spark).
*   A product-oriented mindset with the ability to drive high-impact engineering work, identifying opportunities for automation, simplification, and cost optimisation.
*   The ability to translate engineering concepts into business outcomes.
*   Strong experience coaching and mentoring engineers and team members, removing blockers, and building high-performing, collaborative teams.
*   The ability to communicate clearly and influence effectively across technical and non-technical stakeholders (e.g., Platform Infrastructure, Payments, Risk ML teams), fostering alignment and knowledge-sharing.
*   A pragmatic approach to challenges, thriving in ambiguity, and leading with a team-first focus to deliver sustainable outcomes.
