### Description
BOLD & EPIC TRANSFORM is an expert in the digitalization of German medium-sized enterprises. We specialize in making business processes more efficient, transparent, and scalable using the innovative ERP solution **Haufe X360**, ensuring our clients are future-proof. As part of the BOLD & EPIC network, we approach transformation holistically, combining modern technology with a deep understanding of mid-sized structures. Our core values are **trust, equality, friendliness, curiosity, and flexibility**, both with clients and within our team. We embrace agile methodologies, direct communication, and flat hierarchies. Our work environment is hybrid and flexible, fostering a supportive team committed to mutual growth.

We are seeking a **Finance Consultant - ERP (m/w/d)** to strengthen our team immediately.

### Responsibilities
*   Analyze and optimize accounting processes, developing financially sound and process-driven solutions.
*   Provide expert and systematic consultation to clients in Finance & Accounting, particularly during the implementation and further development of Haufe X360.
*   Configure the ERP system in the finance module (e.g., tax logic, chart of accounts, posting templates, open item logic) and support clients through to the productive launch.
*   Create requirements analyses and tailored solution concepts for finance departments, contributing to quality assurance through posting tests, open item reconciliations, and error analyses.
*   Design and implement automation solutions within Haufe X360 to enhance the efficiency of financial processes.
*   Train and support users to ensure successful integration and utilization of Haufe X360.
*   Ensure seamless integration of financial accounting with other Haufe X360 modules, such as production, logistics, and sales.
*   Support the optimization of reporting and data transparency within Haufe X360 accounting.
*   Assist project management with your technical expertise, both in daily operations and client interactions.
*   Document your work clearly and systematically for traceability by team members and clients.

### Qualifications
*   Completed degree in Business Administration, Finance, or a comparable education.
*   At least **5 years of experience** in ERP system implementation consulting, specifically in finance – ideally with Haufe X360 (alternatively, e.g., SAP Business One, DATEV Unternehmen online, Lexware, etc.).
*   Profound understanding of financial processes and their requirements for ERP systems (e.g., financial accounting, controlling, liquidity management).
*   Experience in analyzing and optimizing financial processes and implementing them in ERP systems.
*   Technical affinity and the ability to translate complex requirements into technical solutions.
*   Analytical and systematic thinking: You recognize connections between processes, system logic, and accounting results.
*   Good understanding of special tax cases (e.g., §13b UStG, intra-community services, foreign bookings).
*   Excellent communication skills, both in collaboration with IT teams and in direct client consultation.
*   Very good German and English language skills, both written and spoken.
*   Hands-on mentality: You don't just think, you also implement – using Excel, import tools, debug logic, or templates.
