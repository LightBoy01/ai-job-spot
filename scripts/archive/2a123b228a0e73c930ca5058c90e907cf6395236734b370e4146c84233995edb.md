**Position Overview**

Lear is looking for his Assistant Controller at the Velizy technical center.

He/she will be in charge of the controlling activities for the Electrical division. He/she will be reporting to the Finance Manager, with a strong dotted line with the Finance Controller.

**Responsibilities will include (but may not be limited to):**

*   Controlling and reporting activities such as:

*   Reporting the month end figures, analyzing the variances vs Forecast, the profitability per project Plan and CAPEX spending.
*   Complying with the reporting deadlines / ensure records are in accordance with French/US gaaps.
*   Ensuring that fixed assets and relevant depreciation comply with French/US gaaps/ controlling CAPEX expenditures.

*   Revenue & costs analysis (incl overheads) related to engineering & tooling programs through regular meetings with engineering teams such as:

*   Controlling engineering and tooling billing process
*   Headcount & costs/Allocations to programs
*   Risk & opportunities weekly assessment

*   Follow up of all related treasurery movements:

*   Weekly cash flow analysis actuals & forecast through regular contacts with Accounting, Purchasing, HR & Sales Depts

*   Maintain internal control & ensure SOX compliance in activities such as:

*   Completing the Monthly close package on time
*   Preparing Month end reconciliations & analysis
*   Ensuring SOX Controls & testing are effective

**To be a success in this role you must have:**

*   Accounting/controlling degree. Minimum bachelor, Master preferred.
*   Minimum 4/5 years of experience preferably in international environments, experience in engineering finance items is a plus.
*   Strong understanding of accounting entries
*   Organized, synthetic, team player and highly effective communication.
*   Knowledge of ERP and reporting systems (QAD & HFM are a plus), Excel.

**Language Skills:**  

*   The candidate needs to have great skills both verbal and written in English and French.

**Work Location: Velizy**
