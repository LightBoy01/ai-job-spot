**About Us / Who you will join:**

Allianz Consulting is the internal consulting division of the Allianz Group. Based in Munich, Paris, Milan, Barcelona, Trivandrum, Pune, Singapore, New York and Minneapolis, our dedicated global teams of over 600 consultants share a joint vision: to be the consulting partner of choice for Allianz Group core segments, transforming the future of Allianz from within. We bring deep industrial and functional expertise to fulfill our clients' needs, ranging from strategy, market management, sales and distribution, IT, organization, claims and operations to central functions and Agile methodologies.

Allianz Consulting's structure focuses on competencies and delivery. Therefore, we are set-up in individual Core Service Clusters and Growth Areas:

*   Life

*   _Sales_
*   _Productivity_
*   _Resilience_
*   _Sustainability_
*   _Central Functions_

**Key responsibilities / You will get to:**

*   You will lead AI projects, manage small teams, develop data and AI strategies, and ensure compliance with regulatory standards such as the EU AI Act
*   Stay updated on date and AI trends, recommend innovative solutions, and communicate effectively with stakeholders to align data and AI initiatives with business objectives
*   Ability to handle diverse and new topics in a fast paced consulting environment take ownership when required
*   Develop and own new Service Offerings and Topics that would support our global clients
*   Manage communications and project timelines
*   Contribute to business development and client pitches under the guidance of an Engagement Manager
*   Help increase Allianz Consulting expertise within our strategic areas of development
*   Contribute to internal projects within Allianz Consulting, including internal knowledge sharing and team events

**Key requirements / What we hope you bring:** 

*   University degree or equivalent experience, preferably in Computer Science or Information Technology Degrees or similar degrees
*   5 to 10 years of experience in data and AI relevant topics in consulting
*   Knowledge of AI, Agentic Tools, MCP , data architecture and governance related topics
*   Experience in assesing AI solution feasibilitys and provide strategic guidance on AI adoption with a solid understanding of real-world AI and data applications 
*   Ability to evaluate GenAI use cases potentials, especially in insurance or adjacent industries, by assessing data maturity, feasibility, and business value
*   Your experience in strategic advisory, project management, change management, business transformation or initiative development and piloting AI solutions
*   Good knowledge of AI governance , data protection, and compliance frameworks , including GDPR, EU AI Act, DORA and cybersecurity regulations
*   Your mastery of working and communicating with a broad range of audiences and seniority levels
*   An affinity for solving problems and a pronounced capacity for analytical thinking to help you organize and prioritize your tasks
*   Your enthusiasm for working in a diverse and international team
*   You possess excellent English and German language skills, which you will utilize in preparing and delivering presentations and reports
*   Solid understanding of the Microsoft 365 ecosystem, including PowerPoint, Excel, Power BI, Power Apps and Power Automate
*   Your curiosity for or knowledge of insurance and financial services and consulting methodologies, tools, and techniques
