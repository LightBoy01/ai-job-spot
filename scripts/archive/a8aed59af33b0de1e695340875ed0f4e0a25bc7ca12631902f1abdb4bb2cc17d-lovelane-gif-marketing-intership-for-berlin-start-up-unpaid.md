While it's important to note this is an unpaid internship without a path to a paid position, the benefits of creative freedom, direct collaboration with the founder, and exposure to real-world brand building in a flexible, remote setting are substantial. It's ideal for someone looking to build their portfolio, understand startup operations from the inside, and make a tangible impact on a product aimed at fostering positive relationships. The emphasis on practical experience over formal qualifications makes it accessible to a wide range of motivated candidates.
