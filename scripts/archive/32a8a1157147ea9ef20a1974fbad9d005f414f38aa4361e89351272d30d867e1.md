**ABOUT US**
------------

Boostlingo is a mission-driven, fast-growing language technology company focused on defining and developing the next generation of interpretation technology solutions. Being at the forefront of this ground-breaking technology enables us to connect people across the globe and break down communication barriers.   

Boostlingo’s technology innovation empowers our customers and the people they serve to communicate without barriers and increases language access for all. We offer products and tools for interpreter management, video remote Interpretation (VRI) and over the phone interpretation (OPI) delivery, interpreter scheduling, simultaneous interpretation, multilingual events, and monthly subscriptions for on-demand interpretation.   

Headquartered in Austin, TX, our global, hybrid teams are distributed across offices in San Francisco, Austin, Copenhagen, Manila, and Ireland.

**ABOUT THE ROLE**
------------------

As a UX/UI Designer at Boostlingo, you will design product experiences that transform the way users interact with language and AI. Reporting directly to the Chief Product Officer, you will collaborate closely with Product Management, Engineering, and Customer Success teams to create user journeys that are simple, effective, and delightful. You will play a critical role in ensuring our AI-powered workflows are approachable, intuitive, and human-centered. 

We are growing rapidly and are looking for a creative, user-centered UX/UI Designer to join our Product team.  

This role will focus on shaping intuitive, engaging, and accessible user experiences for our next-generation AI-enabled products. 

**WHAT WILL BE EXPECTED OF THIS ROLE****:**
-------------------------------------------

_**User-Centered Research & Insights**_ 

*   Conduct user interviews, surveys, and usability tests to deeply understand the needs of multilingual end users, interpreters, and enterprise clients. 
*   Analyze user behavior data and translate insights into design improvements that enhance adoption and retention. 
*   Create personas, empathy maps, and customer journey maps to ensure designs are grounded in real-world user needs. 
*   Define and document company-wide UX/UI design processes, tools, and review practices.   
     

_**UX Strategy & Information Architecture**_ 

*   Define end-to-end user journeys, especially around AI-enhanced workflows such as AI-assisted interpreting, translation, or workflow automation. 
*   Develop wireframes, user flows, site maps, and task analyses to simplify complex language workflows. 
*   Establish interaction models that balance human input and AI-driven recommendations for intuitive, trustworthy experiences.   
     

_**UI Design & Visual Systems**_ 

*   Design polished, accessible interfaces that align with Boostlingo’s brand and product vision. 
*   Build and maintain a design system that ensures consistency across products and platforms. 
*   Build scalable design frameworks, from research to handoff. 
*   Deliver prototypes and high-fidelity mockups for stakeholder reviews and engineering implementation.   
     

_**AI Product Experience Design**_ 

*   Partner with product and engineering to design explainable AI features that inspire user confidence. 
*   Craft micro-interactions and feedback loops to help users understand and trust AI outputs. 
*   Experiment with conversational UI, predictive flows, and adaptive interfaces that leverage machine learning models.   
     

_**Collaboration & Cross-Functional Partnership**_ 

*   Work directly with the Chief Product Officer to translate strategic product vision into concrete design deliverables. 
*   Partner with Product Managers to define requirements and prioritize design initiatives. 
*   Collaborate with engineers to ensure smooth design handoff, technical feasibility, and quality execution. 
*   Advocate for UX best practices across departments, serving as a voice of the customer.   
     

_**Iteration, Testing & Optimization**_ 

*   Plan and run A/B tests, usability studies, and heuristic evaluations to validate design decisions. 
*   Continuously refine product experiences based on data, feedback, and evolving AI capabilities. 
*   Establish KPIs for design success (e.g., task completion rate, time on task, adoption of AI features).   
     

_**Leadership in Design Culture**_ 

*   Act as the primary design authority across all product lines. 
*   Partner with executives to embed design thinking in product strategy 
*   Promote design thinking across the company and mentor team members in design best practices. 
*   Contribute to thought leadership on AI and UX in language technology. 
*   Stay current on industry trends in AI, SaaS, language access, and accessibility to keep Boostlingo products competitive and innovative.
