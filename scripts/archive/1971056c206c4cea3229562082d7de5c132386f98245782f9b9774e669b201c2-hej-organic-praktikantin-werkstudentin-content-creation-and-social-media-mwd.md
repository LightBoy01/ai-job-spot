### Description

Do you love social media, trends, and great content? Do you enjoy getting creative both in front of and behind the camera – whether on TikTok, Instagram, or Pinterest? Then we are looking for exactly you!

As a Working Student in Content Creation, you will support us in making our brand visible – with engaging formats, memorable ideas, and a large dose of creativity. Together, we want to accelerate the transition to sustainable care and tell a new story of natural cosmetics.

Start: **From January**

### Responsibilities

*   Develop creative content ideas and implement them for TikTok, Instagram, and Pinterest (photo and video).
*   Actively participate in on-camera content creation, bringing your unique personality.
*   Plan and publish content according to the editorial calendar and support community management.
*   Monitor relevant trends, sounds, and creators.
*   Support performance analyses and derive learnings for new content formats.
*   Collaborate closely with the marketing team to make our brand tangible across social media.

### Qualifications

*   Currently enrolled at a university or technical college in Marketing, Communication, Business Administration, or a comparable field.
*   Initial experience with content creation on TikTok/Instagram/Pinterest – either privately or for brands.
*   No camera shyness and a passion for experimenting with new ideas.
*   A passionate user of social networks, especially Instagram, TikTok & Pinterest, with a keen eye for design, beauty, and aesthetics.
*   Excellent communication skills coupled with a structured and reliable work approach.
*   Independent, proactive work ethic and an interest in sustainable beauty & lifestyle.
*   Ability to commit for at least 6 months (preferably longer) as part of a mandatory internship or working student period.

### Author's Take

We're not looking for someone just to execute tasks; we're looking for a creative mind to *contribute*. You'll be deeply embedded in the marketing team, and your input on trends, sounds, and formats is not just welcomed, it's expected. We thrive on experimentation. If you see a TikTok trend that aligns with our brand, we want you to pitch it, produce it, and see how it performs. This isn't just about making content; it's about shaping our digital voice and driving real engagement with a brand that genuinely cares about sustainability. Your ideas will directly contribute to making our mission tangible and exciting for our community."

**Question 2:** "The description mentions accelerating the shift towards sustainable care and a new era of natural cosmetics. How does this specific Content Creation role directly contribute to that larger mission, beyond just general brand visibility?"

**Answer 2:** "This role is absolutely pivotal to our sustainable mission. It’s not just about visibility; it's about education and inspiration. You'll be the one translating complex concepts of sustainable beauty, ingredient transparency, and eco-conscious living into bite-sized, engaging stories on platforms where people actively seek inspiration. Imagine creating a TikTok series that demystifies sustainable sourcing, or an Instagram story that showcases the true impact of natural ingredients. You're not just creating content; you're building a community around a shared value system, encouraging conscious consumption, and ultimately, accelerating that shift we envision. Your creative storytelling has the power to turn curious followers into passionate advocates for a more sustainable future. It's a chance to make a real, tangible impact with your creativity."
