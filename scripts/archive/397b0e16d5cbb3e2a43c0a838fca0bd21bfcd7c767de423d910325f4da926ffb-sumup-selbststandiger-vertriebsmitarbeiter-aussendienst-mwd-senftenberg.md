### Description

SumUp empowers small businesses with smart and user-friendly financial solutions, ranging from mobile card payments to comprehensive POS systems. Our mission is to strengthen entrepreneurs, allowing them to focus entirely on their core business. As a freelance Field Sales Consultant, you are your own boss, responsible for building relationships with local businesses and demonstrating how SumUp's innovative solutions can simplify their daily operations.

This role offers attractive, uncapped, 100% performance-based commissions, meaning your income directly reflects your dedication and success. You'll enjoy maximum flexibility, choosing when and where you work, while selling top-tier products with high market potential. SumUp provides comprehensive training and continuous sales support to ensure you have all the tools necessary to thrive in this independent, impactful position.

### Responsibilities

*   Actively acquire new customers: Identify and win local businesses for SumUp's solutions.
*   Conduct personal meetings and consultations: Present our products and clearly demonstrate their benefits to potential clients.
*   Build and maintain strong customer relationships: Foster long-term partnerships through excellent service and follow-up.
*   Manage your own customer and lead pipeline: Independently strategize and decide how to build and expand your business.

### Qualifications

*   Possess a strong passion for sales and high self-motivation.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer service is a significant advantage.
*   Demonstrate excellent communication skills and enjoy direct customer interaction.
*   Maintain very good German language skills (written and spoken).

### Author's Take

This is an outstanding opportunity for highly self-motivated individuals eager to forge their own path in sales with truly uncapped earning potential. The role epitomizes flexibility and autonomy, empowering you to be a genuine 'entrepreneur within an entrepreneur.' SumUp provides robust training and continuous support, alongside market-leading products, to help you succeed. However, candidates must be comfortable with a purely performance-based compensation model, where income is directly tied to effort and results. It's perfectly suited for those with an entrepreneurial spirit who thrive on direct customer engagement and are passionate about making a tangible difference for small businesses. While prior sales experience is beneficial, a strong drive, excellent communication skills, and a proactive approach are paramount to excelling in this dynamic role.
