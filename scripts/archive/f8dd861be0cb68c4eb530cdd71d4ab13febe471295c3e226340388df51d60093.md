**Summary** 

We’re looking for a **Senior Growth Marketing Manager** to own our paid social strategy for North America. You’ll be responsible for driving measurable pipeline and revenue impact by managing budgets with precision, optimizing for performance, and pushing innovation through experimentation and AI-powered insights. This role is perfect for a marketer who thrives at the intersection of analytics, creativity, and technology, with a deep understanding of the security market.

**What you’ll do**

*   Own and scale North America’s paid social strategy across LinkedIn, Meta, Reddit, X, and Quora with pipeline as the goal.  
      
    
*   Manage and optimize budgets to maximize ROAS, with a track record of scaling to $XM+ in ad spend.  
      
    
*   Develop and define strategic audience segments, leveraging platform-specific tools and data-driven insights to precisely target and engage high-value prospects.  
      
    
*   Leverage AI to improve copy, create ads, automate budget allocation, and prioritize leads.  
      
    
*   Analyze and present performance data to uncover insights, influence strategy, and inform future investments.  
      
    
*   Build and maintain an experimentation roadmap to test targeting, creative, messaging, and channel strategies.  
      
    
*   Collaborate closely with PMM, Brand, Campaigns, and ABM teams to align on messaging, offer, and creative testing.  
      
    
*   Stay ahead of trends and maintain insight into the evolving paid social landscape, including AI-driven ad platforms, privacy changes, and new formats.

**What you’ll bring**

*   Proven expertise in paid social, with deep knowledge of platforms (LinkedIn, Meta, Reddit, X, Quora).  
      
    
*   Hands-on experience managing paid social campaigns, from initial setup through to optimization and reporting. You know how to leverage A/B testing, creative testing, and audience targeting to maximize campaign performance.  
      
    
*   Experience in the cybersecurity or B2B SaaS space, with an understanding of security buyers and the cloud market.  
      
    
*   A data-driven mindset, comfortable with analyzing large data sets and translating findings into actionable strategies.  
      
    
*   Hands-on experience with AI tools for creative generation, performance optimization, and workflow automation.  
      
    
*   Excellent communication skills to convey complex insights simply, clearly, and persuasively.  
      
    
*   A self-starter attitude with the ability to thrive in a fast-moving, high-growth environment.

If your experience is close but doesn’t fulfill all requirements, please apply. Wiz is on a mission to build a special company. To achieve our goal, we are focused on hiring Wizards with different backgrounds, perspectives, and experiences.
