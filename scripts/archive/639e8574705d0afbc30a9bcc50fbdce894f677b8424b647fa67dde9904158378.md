**Job Description**
-------------------
Role Overview
We’re looking for a data leader who thrives at the intersection of product, marketing, and behavioral analytics. As our Digital AI Application & Data Innovation Lead, you will co-lead the experimentation engine that powers both GlobeOne and GOMO’s growth loops — turning engagement data into decision-ready insights, behavioral diagnostics, and AI-personalized lifecycle strategies.
You’ll partner with Growth Managers, and our Applied Behavioral Scientists to uncover what drives adoption, stickiness, and monetization — and build the AI-backed tools and intelligent triggers that deliver measurable lift across every user journey.
This is a rare opportunity to work inside a growth operating model that doesn’t just report dashboards — it defines the next best move. If you think in models, design experiments, and build with system-level scale in mind, we’d love to meet you.
****Key Responsibilities****
============================
****Growth & Behavior Modeling****
*   Diagnose friction and habit drop-offs by analyzing digital behavioral data across both apps.
*   Translate growth levers into quantitative models to identify where to intervene — and why it matters.
*   Co-create segmentation frameworks (e.g., activation cohorts, journey states) used by Growth Architects and Growth Marketing teams.
****Digital AI & Data Activation****
*   Work with our AI and CEP stack (e.g., OfferFit, Snowflake, Braze) to personalize app experiences based on behavior, preferences, and telco data.
*   Embed predictive triggers and nudges into lifecycle journeys — from app opens to top-ups and high-stakes drop-offs.
****Experimentation & Learning Loops****
*   Design and evaluate A/B tests that uncover root causes of low retention, conversion, or engagement.
*   Identify “growth truths” beyond statistical wins — using experimentation to drive insight generation, not just performance lifts.
****Strategic Storytelling****
*   Turn insight into business decisions — not noise. Collaborate with Product, Brand, Growth, and Experience teams to bring clarity to ambiguity.
Contribute to cross-team learning culture, sharing behavioral findings, cohort patterns, and campaign impact analyses.
