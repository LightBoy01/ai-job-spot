### Description\n\nThe company aims to make European agriculture resilient and future-proof through innovative Agri-PV solutions. The next strategic step is to build a Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) division, creating storage capacity with farmers and energy providers for a stable, decentralized energy system. Your mission is to build this BESS solutions division from the ground up – strategically, technically, and operationally – developing scalable models for storage projects on agricultural land in collaboration with the founding team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n\n*   **Strategy**: Develop the BESS vision and roadmap in close coordination with founders and investors.\n*   **Business Development**: Identify and evaluate suitable locations, establish partnerships with manufacturers, aggregators, and direct marketers.\n*   **Project Development**: Conduct technical and economic feasibility analyses, integrate with Agri-PV and Virtual Power Plants (VPP).\n*   **Regulatory & Market Analysis**: Monitor relevant laws, funding programs, and tenders (e.g., §14a EnWG, storage in EEG, electricity market 2.0).\n*   **Team Building**: Select and lead an interdisciplinary team comprising technical, operations, and finance professionals.\n*   **Communication**: Serve as an internal sparring partner for management and an external interface to authorities, grid operators, and investors.\n\n### Qualifications\n\n*   Proven experience in building energy storage projects.\n*   Deep knowledge in BESS, electricity market design, flexibility marketing, and grid-related topics.\n*   An entrepreneurial mindset with strong implementation skills.\n*   Understanding of technical planning, project development, and regulatory requirements.\n*   Strong communication skills in both German and English.\n*   A hands-on mentality and enthusiasm for building a new business area within a startup.\n\n### Author's Take\n\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a visionary leader to establish a critical new business division within a purpose-driven startup. You will be instrumental in developing the BESS strategy and operations from scratch, with direct impact on Europe's energy future. The role offers significant autonomy, the chance to lead an interdisciplinary team, and genuine equity participation. The company fosters a culture of innovation, sustainability, and collaboration, providing excellent professional development opportunities and promoting work-life balance through generous leave and team events. It's ideal for an entrepreneurial expert in BESS eager to drive tangible change in a dynamic, high-growth environment.
