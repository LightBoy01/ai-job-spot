### Description

We are seeking a talented Videographer for Ads to bolster our marketing agency, focusing primarily on video editing but also encompassing filming. We are looking for an individual with proven experience in ad creation and a strong understanding of marketing principles.

We are a boutique scaling agency dedicated to helping selected coaching, consulting firms, and personal brands grow from seven- to eight-figure annual revenues. Marketing is our essential tool to empower our clients' customers. We partner exclusively with market leaders who possess a grand vision and are committed to creating sustainable change in the world.

### Responsibilities

*   Regularly edit ads and promotional videos.
*   Film promotional videos for our partners and our in-house podcast.
*   Edit podcast episodes.
*   Edit images and visuals for ads.
*   Utilize AI for the creation of AI-generated images and videos for ads, etc.
*   Occasional travel for recording podcasts, promotional videos, etc.
*   Manage and oversee our in-house podcast/recording studio.

### Qualifications

*   Proven experience in ad creation and editing ads across various niches and platforms.
*   Deep understanding of what makes promotional videos effective: compelling hooks, fast-paced editing, and emotional resonance.
*   An intuitive grasp of mood and music, capable of infusing videos with emotion to maximize engagement and click-through rates.
*   A general understanding of marketing principles. Ideally, you have experience running ads yourself, and you comprehend performance marketing and its synergy with branding.
*   A highly visual individual with a keen eye for detail, instantly spotting suboptimal framing or lighting in a video.
*   Technically adept, enjoy experimenting with software and AI, and eager to explore new tools and techniques.
*   Experience beyond basic AI tools like ChatGPT, specifically with advanced software such as Google VEO 3, or similar cutting-edge technologies.
*   Enthusiasm for a varied role where you can unleash your creativity and witness direct, measurable results.
*   Strong communication skills and a collaborative spirit: you will work closely with our Media Buyer and Copywriter to continuously develop fresh, high-performing advertising creatives.
*   Speed and efficiency: accustomed to fast-paced work in an agency environment with multiple parallel projects, demonstrating quick comprehension.
*   And **the crucial factor**: You possess a **High-Performance Mindset** – you are not satisfied with mediocrity but strive for outstanding results, and you inspire the same from your colleagues.
