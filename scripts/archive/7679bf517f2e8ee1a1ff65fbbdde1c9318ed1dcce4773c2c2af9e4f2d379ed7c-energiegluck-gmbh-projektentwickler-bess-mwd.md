### Description\nEnergieglück develops large-scale battery energy storage systems (BESS) from 1 to 700 MW, managing projects from initial site idea to construction readiness. Our work is built on three pillars: grid connection, building law, and land acquisition. We are seeking reinforcement for the building law and land acquisition pillars.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Independently bring our BESS projects to a construction-ready state.\n- Manage approval procedures according to §34/§35 BauGB (German Building Code) and coordinate external planners, experts, and authorities.\n- Negotiate with landowners and secure land through lease or purchase agreements.\n- Monitor deadlines and ensure comprehensive project documentation.\n- When capacity allows, acquire new sites and prepare grid connection requests – technical processing is handled by our specialist team.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Several years of experience in project development, ideally from the real estate, infrastructure, or renewable energy sectors.\n- Profound knowledge of building law (§34, §35 BauGB) and experience with approval procedures at the municipal level.\n- Strong negotiation skills in dealing with landowners and authorities, even in complex situations.\n- Independent and structured work style – you proactively find tasks even when contacts are unavailable.\n- Team player in a small, dynamic startup environment.\n- Fluent German language skills (for communication with authorities).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a highly impactful role at Energieglück, offering the chance to directly contribute to the energy transition by bringing large-scale battery storage projects to fruition. You'll operate with significant autonomy, collaborate closely with management, and navigate complex legal and negotiation challenges. The company culture prioritizes flexibility with remote-first work and flexible hours, provides modern equipment, and offers a steep learning curve within a dynamic startup. This role is ideal for an experienced project developer who thrives on responsibility, seeks measurable impact, and values a supportive environment with opportunities for professional growth and shared success, all while enjoying excellent work-life balance and job security.
