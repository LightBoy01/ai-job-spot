### Description\nJoin SumUp, a leading international Fintech company dedicated to empowering small businesses worldwide with simple, powerful financial products. As a Field Sales Representative (m/w/d), you'll be at the forefront of this mission, directly engaging with local merchants to introduce them to modern, efficient, and flexible card payment and POS solutions. This role offers the unique combination of a fixed basic salary alongside an uncapped, performance-based bonus structure, putting your success directly in your hands. You'll enjoy full flexibility in planning your day and taking ownership of your sales territory, all while contributing to a company that values diversity, open culture, performance, engagement, and mutual respect. SumUp is committed to supporting your growth with comprehensive onboarding, training, and a dedicated further education budget, ensuring you have the tools to thrive and make a tangible impact.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Actively engage and approach local merchants within your designated sales territory.\n- Present modern payment products in a clear, understandable, and practical manner.\n- Skillfully handle objections and develop tailored solutions for individual customer needs.\n- Build and nurture your own customer portfolio, fostering long-term and personal relationships.\n- Collaborate effectively with a dedicated and target-oriented sales team.\n\n### Qualifications\n- High motivation to be directly in the market and achieve results.\n- Basic technical understanding and the ability to explain smart products simply.\n- Solution-oriented thinking, strong initiative, and inherent motivation.\n- First sales experience is an advantage; however, passion and willingness to learn are considered more important.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a dynamic opportunity for an ambitious individual contributor who thrives on autonomy and direct impact. SumUp offers an enticing package with a fixed salary foundation complemented by an uncapped commission structure, truly rewarding high performance. The emphasis on full flexibility in daily planning is a significant draw, allowing you to manage your territory effectively. Beyond the financial incentives, SumUp clearly invests in its employees with robust onboarding, continuous training, and mental health support, all within a culture that champions diversity and mutual respect. If you're passionate about supporting small businesses, possess a proactive mindset, and are eager to learn and grow in a fast-paced environment, this role provides an excellent platform to build a successful career while making a real difference.
