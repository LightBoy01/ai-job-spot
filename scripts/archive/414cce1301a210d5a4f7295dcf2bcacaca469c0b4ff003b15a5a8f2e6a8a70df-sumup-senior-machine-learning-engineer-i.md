### Description

The Global Operations team at SumUp is dedicated to building the best customer experience in the fintech industry through effortless service for merchants. We are seeking a skilled and passionate Senior Machine Learning Engineer to join our talented AI team. This role is crucial for developing and optimizing state-of-the-art AI models and algorithms that will significantly enhance our customer support services.

As a Senior Machine Learning Engineer, you will leverage your expertise in machine learning, deep learning, and AI models to drive our AI-driven initiatives. You will collaborate closely with cross-functional teams including backend engineers, data scientists, and product managers, translating business requirements into innovative AI solutions. Your contributions will directly shape the future of our support products, keeping SumUp at the forefront of technological advancements in AI.

### Responsibilities

*   Architect, design, develop, and deploy AI solutions and systems in production environments, ensuring reliability, high performance, and scalability.
*   Collaborate and communicate closely with data scientists, product managers, developers, and other business stakeholders to integrate state-of-the-art AI solutions into Customer Support, enhancing customer experience and improving operational efficiency.
*   Develop and maintain ML infrastructure and pipelines to support efficient data processing, model training, and serving.
*   Optimize and fine-tune machine learning models to improve accuracy, efficiency, and scalability.
*   Collect, preprocess, and clean large text datasets to ensure high-quality input for model training and evaluation.
*   Embrace software development principles, best practices, and industry standards, including version control, CI/CD processes, and unit testing frameworks, as part of your daily work.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless integration of machine learning solutions into software applications and platforms.

### Qualifications

*   Bachelor's degree in Machine Learning, Computer Science, or an engineering-related field.
*   5+ years of proven experience working as a Machine Learning Engineer, focusing on building and deploying scalable machine learning or AI solutions and data-driven systems.
*   Excellent software development engineering skills to design computationally effective solutions and ensure maintenance in large-scale production environments (including data version control, model serving, continuous monitoring & alerting).
*   Experience building and deploying ML models using cloud services (AWS, GCP, or Azure).
*   Expert in Python and familiarity with MLOps tools (e.g., MLflow, Kubeflow, Airflow, Langfuse).
*   Experience with machine learning workflow orchestration and algorithm optimization, feature engineering pipelines, data ingestion, and transformation.
*   Good understanding of data pipelines, APIs, containers (Docker), and version control (Git).
*   Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with strong attention to detail.
*   Working proficiency and communication skills in verbal and written English.
