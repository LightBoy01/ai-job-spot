### Description

Our client in Munich is seeking a dedicated **Property Operating Expense Accountant (m/f/d)** to join their team as soon as possible. In this vital role, you will be responsible for ensuring accurate and compliant billing for property operations.

### Responsibilities

*   Independently prepare operating and heating cost statements.
*   Check consumption values for plausibility and adjust advance payments as necessary.
*   Review and develop billing concepts, and monitor operationally relevant data.
*   Participate in the creation of budgets and business plans, as well as process optimizations.

### Qualifications

*   Completed commercial apprenticeship or a comparable qualification.
*   Ideally, initial professional experience in this field.
*   Possess an independent, structured, solution- and results-oriented work style.
*   Demonstrate a very good understanding of numbers coupled with an analytical approach.

### Author's Take

**1. How does this role contribute beyond just accurate accounting? What's the bigger picture impact?**

From an insider's perspective, this isn't just about crunching numbers; it's about the financial health of our properties and the satisfaction of our tenants. Your precise work on operating and heating cost statements directly influences transparent communication with residents and ensures regulatory compliance. Beyond that, by checking plausibility and participating in budget planning, you're actively contributing to cost efficiency and identifying areas for process improvement that can save significant resources. It's a role that demands meticulous attention but offers a tangible impact on both the bottom line and tenant relations, making you a key player in maintaining trust and operational excellence.

**2. What kind of team environment can a new Property Operating Expense Accountant expect, and what opportunities are there for professional growth?**

Our client fosters a supportive and collaborative environment. You'll be joining a team where precision and a proactive mindset are highly valued. While you'll be handling your tasks independently, there’s always a team of experienced professionals ready to collaborate on complex cases or brainstorm improvements. Given your involvement in process optimization and budget planning, you'll have opportunities to deepen your expertise in financial analysis and property management. The company encourages continuous learning, so whether it's staying updated on new regulations or developing more efficient billing concepts, your growth will be supported, allowing you to truly shape your career path within a dynamic industry.
