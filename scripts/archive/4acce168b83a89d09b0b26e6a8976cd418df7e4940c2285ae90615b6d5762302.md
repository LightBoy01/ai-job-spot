**Get to Know Us**

Horizon3.ai is a fast-growing, remote cybersecurity company dedicated to the mission of enabling organizations to proactively find and fix and verify exploitable attack vectors before criminals exploit them. Our flagship product, the NodeZeroTM platform, delivers production-safe autonomous pentests and other key assessment operations that scale across the largest internal, external, cloud, and hybrid cloud environments. NodeZero has been adopted by organizations of all sizes, from small educational institutions to government agencies and Global 100 enterprises. It is used by ITOps/SecOps teams, consulting pentesters, and MSSPs and MSPs. 

We are a fusion of former U.S. Special Operations cyber operators, startup engineers, and formerly frustrated cybersecurity practitioners. We're committed to helping solve our common security problems: ineffective security tools, false positives resulting in alert fatigue, blind spots, "checkbox” security culture, cybersecurity skills shortage, and the long lead time and expense of hiring outside consultants. Collectively, we are a team of learn it alls, committed to a culture of respect, collaboration, ownership, and results.

**What We're Looking For:**

We're looking for an Applied AI Engineer with deep experience deploying emerging AI technology in production and a demonstrated interest in security. You will have a major impact in how we add value to our customers by using emerging AI technologies and exposing their vulnerabilities. You will help design and implement AI solutions—from crafting components for offensive security operations to contributing to our autonomous offensive testing capabilities—that surface vulnerabilities across the AI application stack and outputs, partnering closely with senior engineers to translate research into reliable, scalable systems. This position requires practical expertise in creating, implementing, launching, machine learning systems and an interest in attacking them.

**Essential Functions**

*   Build sophisticated AI systems capable of discovering vulnerabilities and streamlining penetration testing methodologies
    
*   Research and implement AI-driven vulnerability detection and exploitation methods
    
*   Automate exploitation workflows using AI technologies
    
*   Lead complete development lifecycles for AI features from concept through deployment
    
*   Design and implement advanced testing capabilities that detect vulnerabilities in AI-native applications
    
*   Research and target emerging attack vectors specific to applications developed with AI and implement detection methods
    
*   Implement scalable solutions that can use AI to exploit traditional CVEs, credential-based attack paths, and data pilfering vectors
    
*   Guide technical conversations, assess code quality, and facilitate design meetings while sharing knowledge to enhance team capabilities
    
*   Support and guide less experienced team members, fostering their professional development
    

**Competencies/Requirements**

*   3+ years proven track record building and deploying AI/ML solutions in live environments
    
*   Expert-level Python programming abilities
    
*   You’ve shipped and maintained AI/ML systems, not just built prototypes, and ideally in multiple domains
    
*   Demonstrated interest in cybersecurity and penetration testing
    
*   Knowledge of AI frameworks and machine learning libraries (such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, Hugging Face, OpenAI APIs, and LangChain)
    
*   Experience with ML data preprocessing techniques including normalization, feature engineering, and dataset preparation for model training and fine tuning
    
*   Experience with cloud AI platforms such as AWS Bedrock, AWS SageMaker, and Azure AI, with ability to implement and optimize solutions across environments
    
*   Strong analytical skills and ability to clearly communicate complex security concepts
    
*   Comprehensive understanding of various database technologies, including relational and non-relational systems
