### Description
GFN GmbH is a leading IT-specialized educational provider in Germany, empowering individuals through further education and career reorientation nationwide. We are offering a unique 12-month paid Trainee Program designed to transform you into an IHK-certified Trainer (m/w/x). From day one, you will dive into the dynamic world of retraining and further education, actively shaping learning experiences. We believe in your strengths and provide a supportive, prejudice-free, and appreciative environment where diversity thrives amongst both our participants and the GFN team. This program offers comprehensive preparation, expert mentorship, and practical training experiences to equip you for a rewarding career delivering online and in-person IT courses.

### Responsibilities
As a Trainee Trainer (m/w/x) at GFN, you will:
*   Receive optimal preparation for your future role as an IT trainer from your very first day.
*   Benefit from professional and didactic-methodological guidance from our experienced colleagues.
*   Engage in a year of practical training experiences, learning the craft of a trainer through dedicated online further education.
*   Regularly exchange insights and collaborate with your fellow trainees.
*   Be comprehensively prepared for and supported in obtaining your IHK Ausbildereignung (trainer certification).
*   Upon successful completion of the program, independently conduct online and in-person training sessions within your specialized IT field.

### Qualifications
We are looking for a Top-Trainee (m/w/x) who possesses:
*   A reliable and team-oriented work style.
*   A natural ability to explain complex topics clearly and a passion for presenting.
*   Strong affinity and enthusiasm for IT subjects.
*   A technical background, which could stem from further education, a technical degree, relevant apprenticeship, or professional experience.
*   Excellent German language skills (minimum C1 level).
*   **Important Note**: Even if you don't have a finished IT degree, we encourage you to apply if you bring individual qualifications and a strong motivation to teach.
*   Please include a few sentences about your motivation to become a trainer and (optionally) detail any prior experience as a trainer, tutor, coach, or in a similar educational role.
