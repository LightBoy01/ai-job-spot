### Description
This is an opportunity to join a successful and versatile technology company specializing in software, IT, and cloud environments. The company prides itself on a culture built on teamwork and enthusiasm, offering a familiar work atmosphere with flat hierarchies, modern offices, and cutting-edge technology. They provide an an attractive compensation package with various benefits and guarantee a secure, permanent position. The organization uses state-of-the-art IT infrastructure, firewall, database, server, and cloud technologies. The company is committed to employee growth, offering extensive training and development programs designed to advance individuals from Junior to Senior levels, making it an ideal place regardless of your current career stage.

### Responsibilities
- Take on the role of Information Security Officer and strategically develop the existing Information Security Management System (ISMS).
- Accompany the ongoing ISO 27001 certification process, manage necessary measures, and ensure permanent embedding of standard requirements within the company.
- Advise management and departments on information security risks and measures, translating requirements into concrete actions for development, Managed IT Services, and Business Applications.
- Maintain and refine existing guidelines, processes, and security concepts, monitor their compliance, and coordinate risk analyses for internal systems and customer environments.
- Ensure information security is embedded throughout the entire software development and operational process, e.g., through Secure Development Practices, hardening, and authorization concepts.
- Act as the central contact person for information security matters, support customer and ISO audits, and organize training and awareness measures for employees.

### Qualifications
- Completed degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics, or a comparable qualification.
- Several years of practical experience in building, operating, or certifying an ISMS according to ISO 27001.
- Ideally, additional knowledge of other standards and frameworks (e.g., BSI IT-Grundschutz, OWASP, NIST).
- Good understanding of IT infrastructures, networks, cloud environments, and software development processes.
- Ideally, certifications in the field of information security (e.g., ISO 27001 Lead Implementer/Lead Auditor, CISM, CISSP, or similar).
- Knowledge of relevant legal frameworks, especially GDPR and requirements for data processing agreements.
- Strong analytical thinking skills and the ability to communicate complex issues appropriately for the audience.
- High consulting and communication competence and enthusiasm for actively driving information security within the company.
