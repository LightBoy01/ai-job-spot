### Description\nAre you ready to join Felix and significantly elevate Naughty Nuts' social media presence? We're seeking a **Social Media Content Creator & Brand Face** who is not only creative, but boldly authentic and brimming with personality. This unique Werkstudent or Minijob role offers the chance to become a core part of our brand, both in front of and behind the camera, shaping our digital voice and engaging with our community. If you're based in or near Cologne and eager to unleash your creativity, take ownership, and make a real impact, we want to hear from you!\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Feature alongside Felix in videos for Naughty Nuts' main social media channels, embodying the brand's energetic and cheeky personality.\n- Actively participate in brainstorming, help organize shoots, and assist during filming to ensure high-quality content production.\n- Take full ownership of your dedicated Naughty Nuts social media accounts, developing and implementing unique content ideas. These will become your creative playground with initial guidance from Felix.\n- Provide ad-hoc support for tasks like influencer communication or giveaway organization as part of a small, agile team, ensuring these do not detract from primary content creation.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Be at least 18 years old and reside in or near Cologne.\n- Be seeking a flexible Werkstudent (working student) or Minijob (marginal employment) position.\n- Thrive in front of the camera, possessing strong self-confidence and a witty personality. We'll help you overcome any shyness!\n- Demonstrate a passion for content creation. While prior experience (professional or personal) is a plus, we prioritize personality and cultural fit, so even without experience, we encourage you to apply.\n- Be a self-starter comfortable working autonomously and leading your own projects, while also valuing collaborative support.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis isn't just a job; it's an invitation to unleash your inner influencer and leave a tangible mark on a dynamic brand. Naughty Nuts is seeking a creative powerhouse to become a co-conspirator, blending seamlessly into their energetic, fun-loving culture. The role offers an incredible blend of **autonomy and support**: you'll manage your \"own babies\" (social accounts) with flexible hours and remote work options, while still being part of a close-knit team that values collaboration and social events. The company clearly prioritizes personality over a perfect CV, presenting a fantastic growth opportunity for a bold individual. Expect a workplace that prioritizes fun, flexibility, and tangible perks like Job-Rad, Urban Sports, and delicious Naughty Nuts products. If you're a natural entertainer ready to rock social media, this is your stage – make sure that 90-second video shines!
