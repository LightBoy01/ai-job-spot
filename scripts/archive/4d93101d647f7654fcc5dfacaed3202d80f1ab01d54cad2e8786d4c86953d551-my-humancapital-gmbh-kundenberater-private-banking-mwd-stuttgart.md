### Description\nThis role as a **Private Banking Client Advisor (m/w/d)** in Stuttgart involves supporting Senior Advisors in the comprehensive care and consultation of demanding private clients and institutional investors. You will be instrumental in nurturing existing relationships, actively participating in new client acquisition, and developing tailored investment strategies to expand the client base and ensure long-term partnerships.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Support Senior Advisors in the holistic care and consultation of demanding private clients and institutional investors.\n- Maintain existing client relationships in the high-net-worth private client segment and actively contribute to building and expanding long-term partnerships.\n- Participate in the acquisition of new clients, accompany sales activities, and support the strategic expansion of the assigned client base.\n- Conduct target- and closing-oriented consulting discussions, identify client needs, and develop tailored investment strategies, considering risk profiles and regulatory requirements.\n- Take on tasks in various Private Banking projects and contribute own ideas for process optimization and client retention.\n\n### Qualifications\n- First professional experience in a banking environment, ideally in Private Banking, Wealth Management, or asset management.\n- Proficiency in MS Office, especially Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.\n- High service orientation, strong communication skills, and a confident demeanor in customer contact.\n- Ability to contribute to various Private Banking projects and introduce ideas for process optimization and client retention.\n- Very good German language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an ambitious professional looking to grow within Private Banking in a supportive and modern environment. The company emphasizes a strong work-life balance through flexible hours and home office options, coupled with a highly appreciative and trusting \"Du-Kultur.\" With a focus on continuous development and a central Stuttgart location, this role offers a chance to make a significant impact while advancing your career with long-term security.
