**ROLE TITLE: Strategic Change Management Senior Consultant (NYC, ATL, CHI, SFO)**

_**About the team**_

_Our Workforce & Organization team at Invent goes to market with a diverse set of capabilities and sector expertise, now enhanced by cutting-edge AI-driven Change Management. We leverage advanced AI technologies to drive precision and scale in organization purpose and strategy, workforce transformation, change management, employee experience, and HR transformation._

_Our team specializes in accelerating change adoption, personalizing training experiences through gamification and adaptive learning, and generating data-driven insights for decision-making. By integrating AI-powered analytics to define and monitor change KPIs, we enable organizations to navigate transformation with agility and confidence. With a forward-thinking mindset, we combine human-centered design and intelligent automation to deliver innovative, technology-enabled change strategies that align with evolving business goals._

**YOUR ROLE**

An ideal candidate is motivated to deliver high-quality work to drive customer satisfaction. This person uses their intellect, insight, and ability to build creative solutions with clients and colleagues while embracing a collaborative mindset. Key qualifications include:

•         Experience in leveraging AI tools and platforms to drive change adoption across large organizations.

•         Define and track change adoption KPIs using AI-powered analytics and dashboards.

•         Design and implement AI-based training programs, including gamification and adaptive learning paths.

•         Utilize AI to personalize change management strategies based on stakeholder behavior and feedback.

•         Apply data-driven insights from AI models to continuously improve change adoption and engagement.

**YOUR PROFILE**

We would love to find a candidate who:

•         Demonstrated ability to integrate AI technologies into change management frameworks.

•         Skilled in using AI to differentiate tactical change (e.g., technology implementation) from strategic transformation (e.g., cultural or mission shifts).

•         Proven success in managing stakeholders through AI-enhanced communication and engagement strategies.

•         Ability to lead AI-enabled change initiatives that align with organizational goals and values.
