### Description\nThis role is for a (Senior) Data Engineer in Berlin, offering a hybrid work model. The successful candidate will work closely with the Head of Data Products & Analytics and expert teams to develop innovative solutions and execute the data strategy. The mission involves managing data lakes/warehouses, developing infrastructure, supporting reporting, optimizing data quality, and standardizing data availability for various client data sources.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Data Lake & Warehouse Management**: Develop processes and structures for the Data Warehouse and Lakehouse, ensuring high data quality and fast, reliable data delivery to business stakeholders across the entire Data Value Chain.\n- **Data Operations & Infrastructure**: Collaborate with DevOps and Security teams to develop and implement data infrastructure in a hybrid environment (on-premise and Cloud), actively shaping data architecture and programmatic infrastructure provisioning.\n- **Reports & Analyses**: Support the creation of detailed reports and in-depth analyses to drive the data strategy, quickly identifying bottlenecks and error sources, and independently implementing requirements for stable and secure data supply.\n- **Data Quality & Optimization**: Actively contribute to improving data quality and flows, identify optimization potential, and implement solutions for continuous improvement of the data infrastructure.\n- **Data Availability & Standardization**: Implement data connections using scripts and platform methods, and be part of the team standardizing data supply within the organization for consistent and reliable data availability, with a focus on integrating diverse client data sources and formats (e.g., with Python, Airflow, MS Fabric).\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Academic Foundation**: Completed studies in Computer Science, Informatics, Mathematics, or a related field, with proven practical application of theoretical knowledge.\n- **Technical Expertise**: Comprehensive knowledge in SQL, Python, statistics, and database design, mastering the entire data pipeline, and continuously developing efficient solutions for data process optimization.\n- **Entrepreneurial Spirit**: Proactive approach in a dynamic environment, developing results-oriented solutions that promote both short-term and long-term business goals.\n- **Communication Skills**: Ability to present complex technical topics clearly and understandably in both German and English, effectively conveying them to all relevant stakeholders.\n- **Practical Mindset**: Characterized by a pragmatic, goal-oriented approach, efficiently implementing projects with a constant focus on scalability and sustainability.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a compelling opportunity for a skilled Data Engineer to join a profitable company with a The role offers significant autonomy to shape the data landscape, utilizing modern technologies in a flexible hybrid work environment.
