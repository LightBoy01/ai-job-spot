As an AVP at UWM, you will work in a fast paced, results driven environment. In this position, you have a passion to revolutionize the field. This is an innovative technology environment and you will lead, inspire, and support teams towards building state-of-the-art AI solutions.

WHAT YOU WILL BE DOING:

*   Lead, inspire, and manage a high-performing AI team responsible for developing and enhancing generative AI models.
*   Conduct day-to-day operations and project management, ensuring all team activities align with project goals and timelines.
*   Be involved in the lifecycle of AI model development - from conceptualization, designing, training, and testing, to refinement and deployment.
*   Collaborate cross-functionally with data science, engineering, and business teams to implement AI requirements and solutions.
*   Monitor the performance and reliability of AI models, making necessary adjustments for optimal results.
*   Stay informed on the latest advancements in AI technology to help drive innovation and competitive advantage.

WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU:

**Required:**

*   Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, or a relevant field.
*   Hands-on experience with OpenAI's GPT-3, or similar AI platforms, for development of generative AI models.
*   Understanding of machine learning techniques, concepts, and AI model development.
*   Proven track record in managing teams within an Agile/Scrum development environment.

**Preferred:**

*   In-depth experience in the fintech industry.
*   Experience with cloud-deployed AI solutions like GCP, AWS, or Azure.
*   Demonstrated ability to handle complex AI-related projects efficiently.
*   Exceptional leadership, organization, communication, and interpersonal skills.
