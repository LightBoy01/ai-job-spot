### Description
Superchat is revolutionizing business-to-customer communication, aiming to make it as simple and natural as talking to friends. As a rapidly growing company, Superchat is looking for dedicated support in the form of a Growth & Marketing Intern to help scale operations and significantly increase market share. This role offers the opportunity to drive key marketing strategies, implement independent projects, and gain deep insights into various critical business functions within a dynamic startup environment.

### Responsibilities
- Scale Superchat and significantly increase market share by analyzing growth potential and preparing decision-relevant insights for management.
- Develop and implement social media and marketing strategies to strengthen brand presence and drive organic growth and lead generation for Superchat.
- Take on independent and self-directed projects in various business areas and bring them to a successful conclusion.
- Gain deep insights into Growth, Marketing, Sales, RevOps, and Operational Excellence.
- Learn about the daily operations of our teams, in addition to project work and supporting management.

### Qualifications
- Currently studying or have successfully completed studies in a startup-relevant field (e.g., economics or natural sciences) and are available for a 3- to 6-month internship.
- Previous practical work experience in startups, management consulting, or investment banking.
- Possess the highest quality standards for yourself and demonstrate a strong sense of responsibility.
- Strong drive for continuous development and a willingness to be hands-on.
- Enjoy working in a highly analytical and structured manner.
- Native level German and very good English skills, both written and spoken (Spanish skills are a plus).
