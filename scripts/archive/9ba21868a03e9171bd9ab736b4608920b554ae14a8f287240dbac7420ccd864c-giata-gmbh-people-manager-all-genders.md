### Description\nThis part-time Junior People & Culture Specialist role offers an exciting opportunity to contribute to the entire employee lifecycle within a flexible, hybrid work environment. You will be instrumental in fostering an open feedback culture and supporting employees, benefiting from a trusting and dynamic company culture that values independent work and offers excellent work-life balance through flexible hours, remote options, and \"Work from anywhere\" weeks. The position comes with an unlimited contract and attractive benefits like a Deutschlandticket, Urban Sports Club membership, and various discounts.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Accompany the entire Employee Lifecycle – from recruiting to the further development of our employees.\n- Use data and key figures as a basis for well-founded decisions, combining this approach with a personal, human touch.\n- Be the point of contact for our employees on all People-related topics and promote an open feedback and company culture.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Education or degree in Human Resources Management / (Business) Psychology.\n- Initial professional experience in the People field (e.g., as a working student, intern, or in a junior role).\n- Native German speaker, fluent English.\n- Hands-on mentality and a desire to drive things forward.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an ambitious individual with initial HR experience looking for a part-time, flexible role. The company clearly invests in its employees' well-being and growth, offering a supportive environment where you can make a tangible impact on the employee experience. The hybrid model, 'Work from anywhere' policy, and focus on trust make it particularly attractive for those seeking work-life balance in a dynamic setting.
