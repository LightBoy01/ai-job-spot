**Mining Engineer**

We're seeking a skilled **Mining Engineer** to join our **Open Pit Team** at **KCGM**, based in **Kalgoorlie** on a **residential 8/6 roster** (11-hour shifts).

Reporting to the **Senior Mine Engineer**, you'll help shape daily mine plans and support safe, efficient production. With ongoing asset growth, this is a prime opportunity for someone eager to advance their career in a dynamic environment.

**Here's what awaits you:**

*   Coordinate pit and ancillary designs to align with weekly plans.
*   Support Weekly Scheduling Engineers and the Senior Mining Engineer.
*   Lead planning and execution of weekly, monthly, and quarterly mine plans.
*   Manage weekly reconciliations, identifying risks and opportunities.

**To thrive in this role, you'll need:**

**Essential**

*   **Tertiary qualification in Mining Engineering.**
*   Minimum 3 years' experience in open pit hard rock mining.
*   Proficiency in Deswik CAD and Scheduler.
*   Advanced Microsoft Office skills.
*   Strong accountability and leadership mindset.
*   Valid WA "C" Class Driver's Licence.

**Preferred**

*   Experience with drill and blast design software and scheduling a drill fleet to meet blasting requirements.
*   Understanding of the environmental effects of operating and blasting near a community.
*   Experience with mining contractors or operators.
*   Familiarity with large teams and production coordination.
*   Power BI experience.
