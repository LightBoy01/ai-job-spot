### Description\nunivativ is seeking an IT Network/System Technician (m/w/d), also functioning as an IT Support Specialist, for a global chemical and pharmaceutical client in Frankfurt am Main. This role is ideal for individuals ranging from entry-level to experienced professionals who are proficient in Windows environments and eager to manage demanding IT systems. You will be instrumental in ensuring the smooth operation of client systems and providing global user support, becoming a vital part of a company developing complex innovations for tomorrow.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Ensure the smooth operation of client systems and provide user support in an international environment.\n- Offer global user support, including troubleshooting, analyzing, and classifying disruptions, and providing remote assistance (Windows, MS Office, Lotus Notes).\n- Coordinate and manage external service providers (Dell, PandCom, Ricoh) and track open issues.\n- Handle helpdesk functions, including call intake and ticket creation.\n- Manage SAP user passwords, set up project directories with AD user groups, and install/configure software.\n- Provide IT support for approximately 1,400 computers for 1,200 internal/external employees and support plasma donor centers.\n- Ensure professional support for internal and external users in German and English, leveraging organizational strength and communication skills.\n- Maintain stable, efficient, and service-oriented IT operations.\n- Manage permissions and approvals, showcasing reliability in IT support.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in Computer Science or a comparable qualification.\n- Solid knowledge of client support (Windows, MS Office, Lotus Notes).\n- Experience with software distribution systems, ideally Empirum.\n- Proficient in setting up and managing user accounts (Active Directory, Lotus Notes, VPN).\n- Experience in global user support.\n- Knowledge of archiving systems (e.g., d.3), including permission and password management.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a motivated IT professional to join a leading global player in the chemical and pharmaceutical industry through univativ. The role offers a comprehensive experience in IT support, working with an international user base and cutting-edge systems. With a clear focus on employee development, flexible working hours, and defined career paths towards specialist or leadership roles, this position is perfect for someone looking to grow their career in a supportive and innovative environment in Frankfurt.
