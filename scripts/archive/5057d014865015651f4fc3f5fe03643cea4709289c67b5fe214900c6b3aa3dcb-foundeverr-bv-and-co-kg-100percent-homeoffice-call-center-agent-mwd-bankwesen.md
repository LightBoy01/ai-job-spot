### Responsibilities

As a Call Center Agent, your main tasks will include:

*   Answering incoming customer calls promptly and professionally.
*   Expertly handling inquiries related to leasing and financing contracts, vehicle registration documents (Kfz-Briefe), and accident processing for Volkswagen Financial Services.
*   Processing requests concerning payments, master data changes, revocations, and authorizations with accuracy.
*   Careful verification and processing of incoming documents.
*   Conducting claims management effectively.
*   Advising customers on contract extensions and other relevant services.
*   Consistently creating exceptional customer experiences through friendly and competent service.

### Qualifications

To succeed in this role, you should bring:

*   Initial professional experience in customer service or the automotive industry.
*   Ideally, a commercial education.
*   Fluent German language skills, both spoken and written (C2-level), to effectively communicate with customers.
*   Very good PC and Microsoft Office skills for efficient task management.
*   A genuine enjoyment of communication and telephony, with a customer-focused attitude.

#### Requirements for your home office setup:

*   A reliable broadband internet connection (DSL, VDSL, or cable).
*   An internet speed of at least 16 Mbit/s download and 1 Mbit/s upload.
*   A quiet, lockable room with an ergonomic workstation to ensure productivity and privacy.
*   Residence in Germany.
