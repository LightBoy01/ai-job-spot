### Description\nDo you want to actively shape the energy transition and contribute your technical expertise in an \"innovative, high-growth environment\"? Then become part of Nexentury and develop with us pioneering energy solutions that secure tomorrow's energy supply.\n\nNexentury GmbH specializes in the development, construction, financing, integration, and operation of solar power plants and battery storage systems in Germany and Europe.\n\nTo strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated and qualified Technical Manager (m/f/d) with experience in renewable energy projects, with a special focus on Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), at our Starnberg or Eschbach (near Freiburg) locations.\n\nAs a Technical Manager for Renewable Energies (focus on BESS), you will assume overall technical responsibility for our storage and PV/hybrid projects – from the early concept phase through planning and construction to successful commissioning.\n\nYou will combine technical development, project control, and quality responsibility, ensuring that our projects are implemented optimally both technically and economically.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Overall technical responsibility for battery storage and renewable energy projects (especially ground-mounted PV, floating PV, moor PV).\n- Management of the entire project lifecycle: from technical conception, planning, and engineering to implementation and commissioning.\n- Evaluation and selection of sites from technical and economic perspectives.\n- Development, review, and optimization of technical designs (layout, dimensioning, system architecture, interfaces) for BESS and PV/hybrid projects.\n- Coordination of external planners, experts, and EPC partners, as well as alignment with internal departments (development, financing, operations).\n- Technical support for approval procedures (including environmental and expert opinions).\n- Creation and coordination of grid connection concepts with grid operators, as well as support for grid connection planning.\n- Support for economic project evaluation (CAPEX/OPEX estimation, technical risks, optimization potential).\n- Technical support for tenders and contract negotiations with suppliers and service providers.\n- Construction and implementation support including quality and schedule control, acceptance tests, and performance checks.\n- Contribution to the further development of Nexentury's BESS and project strategy from a technical perspective.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in Energy Technology, Electrical Engineering, Industrial Engineering, or a comparable technical field.\n- At least two years of relevant professional experience in the technical project planning and implementation of battery storage and ideally also PV or other renewable energy projects.\n- Profound knowledge of relevant norms, laws, approval procedures, and regulations (e.g., EEG, grid connection guidelines, environmental and nature protection requirements).\n- Experience in collaborating with grid operators, authorities, experts, and other external stakeholders.\n- Structured, independent working style and routine in technical project management.\n- Team player with the ability to manage complex coordination processes with internal and external stakeholders in a goal-oriented manner.\n- Very good German language skills (written and spoken), good English language skills are an advantage.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a highly impactful Mid-Senior role at Nexentury, offering the chance to be at the forefront of the energy transition. You'll take overall technical responsibility for critical BESS and PV/hybrid projects, from conception to commissioning, in an innovative and high-growth environment. The company emphasizes a dynamic team, flat hierarchies, and quick decision-making, which means your expertise will directly shape pioneering energy solutions. This role is perfect for someone with strong technical project management skills in renewable energies who thrives in a collaborative, fast-paced setting and wants to make a tangible difference in securing future energy supply.
