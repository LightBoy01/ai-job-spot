### Description\nThis interim Project Leader role, based in Braunschweig with significant remote work flexibility, focuses on steering critical projects within the pharmaceutical industry. You will be responsible for overseeing complex initiatives from planning through successful completion, with a particular emphasis on decommissioning (Rückbau) and asset recovery in a highly regulated environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Steer and monitor projects to successful completion.\n- Plan, organize, and implement decommissioning and asset recovery measures for inventory.\n- Support the coordination, restructuring, and strategic development of processes.\n- Lead and motivate employees, ensure clear task distribution, and foster efficient teamwork.\n- Ensure smooth internal communication and adherence to project goals through close coordination.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A qualification as a Project Leader, Technician, Engineer, or a comparable background such as a Business Administrator with a focus on Project Management.\n- Experience in executing projects within a regulated environment, ideally with GMP relevance.\n- Ideally, knowledge in decommissioning, asset recovery, or infrastructural restructuring.\n- Strong leadership, organizational skills, and communication abilities.\n- Fluent in spoken and written German and English.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a compelling interim opportunity for a seasoned Project Leader with a passion for driving complex projects in the pharmaceutical sector. The role offers significant autonomy, a high degree of home office flexibility, and a chance to make a tangible impact on process development and team leadership. The company prides itself on a supportive, respectful, and collaborative culture that values work-life balance and professional growth. If you thrive in a regulated environment and are ready to steer critical initiatives, this role promises an engaging and rewarding experience.
