### Description

At Flix, we offer a dynamic work environment with competitive pay, strong growth opportunities, and a tech-driven approach to making travel more accessible, sustainable, and affordable. We are seeking an experienced Project Manager to join our rapidly evolving Customer Service team.

Flix Customer Service is leveraging advanced technology to enhance customer experience and operational efficiency. As a CS Project Manager, you will lead strategic initiatives focused on driving automation, AI adoption, and process optimization across global markets. This role is ideal for a self-starter who thrives in dynamic, unstructured environments, enjoys solving complex problems, and delivers data-driven solutions in a fast-paced, ever-changing landscape.

You will frequently be pulled into high-priority projects spanning multiple teams, diverse initiatives, and a wide range of stakeholders, making a significant impact on our global operations.

### Responsibilities

*   Lead end-to-end delivery of cross-functional projects with a focus on CS processes and automation, from defining the problem and hypothesis through execution and rollout.
*   Streamline and optimize CS processes to improve operational efficiency and scalability across markets.
*   Collaborate with Operations, IT, and Customer Experience teams to align solutions with business strategy and customer needs.
*   Maintain and enhance CS content governance, ensuring documentation and records remain accurate and up to date.
*   Co-lead and contribute to automation and AI initiatives, including chatbots, IVR enhancements, and real-time customer service solutions.
*   Conduct quick turn analyses to identify trends, define KPIs, and deliver actionable insights for long-term CS success.
*   Support new market launches and ensure CS content and systems are ready for expansion.

### Qualifications

*   Bachelor's degree in business, STEM, or a related field; Master's preferred.
*   Proven track record in leading complex, cross-functional projects from concept to completion.
*   Experience in customer service, operations, or automation-related environments.
*   Familiarity with CRM and contact center technologies (e.g., Salesforce, Twilio, IVR, WFM, chatbot platforms).
*   Strong analytical skills with proficiency in data tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI, or similar).
*   Hands-on experience with AI tools, low/no-code platforms, or process automation systems.
*   Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, comfortable leading discussions across global teams.

We recognize that everyone carries a unique set of valuable skills and experiences. If you think you could have an impact even though you don't meet 100% of the requirements, we still encourage you to apply. We want to hear from you!

**NB**: Please submit your resume in English!
