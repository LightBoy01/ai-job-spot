Role Details
------------

A Procurement Category Officer at Vinarchy plays a vital role in managing the procurement process across indirect   
procurement categories   
This position is based in our fun and dynamic Adelaide CBD office, with the flexibility to work from home.    
Reporting into the Procurement Manager ANZ, the role is responsible for supporting and managing procurement   
activities  across indirect categories, including fleet administration, goods, services, and related support functions.    
You will assist in executing category strategies, managing supplier relationships, negotiating contracts, and ensuring all   
procurement aligns with Vinarchy’s commercial objectives.

Your Key Accountabilities
-------------------------

**• Category Strategy Development:** Support and implement procurement strategies specific to the wine category. Understand product specifications, market shifts, and emerging trends to ensure cost-effective and high-quality   
outcomes.    
**• Supplier Management:** Identify, evaluate, and manage supplier partnerships. Negotiate pricing and commercial   
terms to secure optimal value and service.    
**• Procurement Process Oversight:** Manage end-to-end procurement activities — from sourcing to delivery — while   
ensuring full compliance with policies and regulations.    
**• Cost Management:** Monitor costs, review financial data, and identify savings opportunities without compromising   
product quality.    
**• Stakeholder Collaboration:** Work cross-functionally with Production, Sales, Marketing, and Finance to align   
procurement plans with business needs.    
**• Risk Management:** Anticipate and mitigate risks in the supply chain through proactive analysis and planning.    
**• Performance Measurement:** Develop and monitor KPIs to assess supplier performance, procurement efficiency, and   
overall category health.    
**• Sustainability and Compliance:** Drive ethical sourcing and sustainability goals in alignment with company values and   
industry standards.
