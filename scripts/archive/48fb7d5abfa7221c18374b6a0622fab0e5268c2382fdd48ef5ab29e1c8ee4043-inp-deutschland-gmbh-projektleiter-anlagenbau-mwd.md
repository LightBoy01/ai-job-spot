### Description\nAs **Project Manager Plant Engineering (m/f/d)**, you will shape projects with technical demands and strategic foresight. You will combine engineering, commercial thinking, and customer orientation to create well-conceived solutions – from offer creation to commissioning. Freelancers are also welcome.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Conception and steering of technical projects from offer creation to commissioning\n- Implementation of project-specific requirements into internal processes and workflows\n- Coordination of the project team and support for the collaboration of all involved parties\n- Coordination and follow-up of tasks with engineering, purchasing, construction site, and commissioning\n- Overview of project progress, schedules, quality, and general conditions\n- Communication with customers, service providers, and internal interfaces during project execution\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed studies in process engineering, plant engineering, mechanical engineering, or a comparable qualification\n- Several years of professional experience in project management in plant or mechanical engineering, ideally with a process engineering focus\n- Proficient use of MS Office and common project management tools\n- Strong customer orientation, assertiveness, and a structured, proactive way of working\n- Willingness to travel according to project phase requirements\n- Fluent German and English skills, both written and spoken\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced Project Manager to lead innovative and sustainable projects in the energy and plant engineering sector. The company offers a high degree of autonomy, significant individual development opportunities, and a supportive team culture with flexible working hours and attractive benefits. It's ideal for someone who thrives on technical challenges, values collaboration, and wants to make a tangible impact on future energy solutions.
