### Description

**Are health, sugar-free nutrition, and longevity your passion? Become my creative right hand!**

I'm seeking a **Junior Content Creator** to craft engaging content for Instagram and beyond. In the past two years, I've successfully built my Instagram account to nearly 160K followers. Now, I'm looking for an imaginative individual to support me, observe trends, and make exciting health content visible.

This is a unique opportunity to shape a thriving health and wellness brand. You'll enjoy significant creative freedom, planning and executing videos, developing scripts, and infusing your unique aesthetic into our content. You'll delve deep into topics like sugar-free nutrition, longevity, and biohacking, continuously expanding your professional skills in content creation and brand communication.

We offer a flexible work schedule of 10-12 hours per week, with hybrid options in Munich and in-person meetings approximately twice a month. You'll work with modern tools like CapCut Pro and AI tools, benefit from direct feedback, and collaborate closely in a personal, open, and focused small team atmosphere. This role promises a long-term creative partnership with ample space for learning, experimenting, and networking within the health and wellness industry.

### Responsibilities

*   **Project Planning & Execution:** Independently plan projects in coordination with me, including research, scriptwriting, and video editing (CapCut Pro).
*   **Idea & Concept Development:** Develop creative concepts, observe current trends, and continuously enhance content aesthetics.
*   **AI-Driven Efficiency:** Utilize AI tools to optimize workflow and content production.
*   **Collaboration:** Engage in close collaboration with me, including joint shoots in Munich as needed (1-2 times per month).
*   **Social Media Content Creation:** Produce engaging carousel posts using Canva and contribute to the creative evolution of content.
*   **Daily Tasks:** Conduct research, write scripts, edit and process videos, and continuously generate new ideas.
*   **Structured Output:** Aim to produce approximately 3 content pieces per week.
*   **Content Planning:** Participate in regular meetings for topic and content planning.
*   **Note:** Community management is not required for this role.

### Qualifications

*   **Education/Experience:** Degree or vocational training in Media, Communication, Design, or comparable experience in Social Media Content Creation.
*   **Language Skills:** Fluent in German, with a minimum B2 level in English.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** Skilled in video editing with CapCut Pro (or similar tools), Canva, and basic knowledge of AI tools.
*   **Soft Skills:** Creative, proactive, organized, reliable, strong visual sense for design, and affinity for social media.
*   **Experience Level:** Suitable for candidates from Beginner to Intermediate level; leadership experience is not required.
