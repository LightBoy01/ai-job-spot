Overview:

Quest is an award-winning IT management software provider offering a broad selection of solutions that solve some of the most common and most challenging IT problems. We are currently interviewing for a Vice President, Financial Planning & Analysis to join our team based in Austin, Texas. **This position will be an in-office role based in our Austin, TX office located near The Domain.  Only candidates currently residing in the Austin area will be considered.**

The VP of FP&A position will be responsible for partnering key parts of the business organization and its leaders. Reporting to President & CFO, [Ashish Joshi](https://nam12.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.linkedin.com%2Fin%2Fashijoshi%2F&data=05%7C02%7CNancy.Marshall%40quest.com%7C82c57842fafd4d1ba14108de0201efee%7C91c369b51c9e439c989c1867ec606603%7C0%7C0%7C638950404514212460%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=zHW5yO9sZM%2BxwPAf7JL2v4juIvBbzsP7OGjVBmZDj7w%3D&reserved=0), position is a unique opportunity to play a crucial role in building a strong financial foundation. As a finance team member in the organization, this highly visible role provides financial leadership to areas of the business driving all financial processes and providing hands-on financial and analytical support.

As the VP of FP&A, you will partner with the CFO and executive team to drive strategic decision-making and resource allocation through insightful analytics and operational rigor. You’ll lead a high-performing FP&A team, work cross-functionally with departments including Sales, Marketing, Product, and Engineering, and help define the roadmap to scalable profitability.

Responsibilities:

This is a hands-on, in-depth analysis role intended to drive data-based decisions to improve efficiency, productivity, and profitability. The ideal candidate will have an Enterprise SAAS background with a strong understanding of financial planning and analysis principles, experience in developing and maintaining financial models, developing near and long-term plans and strong communication skills. **PE background is highly recommended.** 

*   Own company-wide planning, forecasting, and long-range financial modeling to support Quest’s long-term growth and scale objectives.
*   Act as a strategic advisor to the CFO and CEO on business performance, financial risks, and investment opportunities.
*   Drive alignment of financial goals with overall company OKRs and KPIs.
*   Build models that measure and predict AI adoption, product usage, and monetization metrics.
*   Partner with stakeholders to support GTM strategies, pricing models, and capacity planning.
*   Translate financial data into clear business insights and recommendations for senior stakeholders.
*   Deliver executive-level dashboards, board reports, and variance analysis with a focus on strategic insights.
*   Ensure timely and accurate monthly/quarterly forecasting and reporting.
*   Build, mentor, and scale the FP&A team; establish best-in-class processes, systems, and tools and familiarity with the latest enterprise tools – Anaplan, Power BI etc.
*   Foster a high-performance, collaborative culture focused on continuous improvement and data integrity.

Qualifications:

*   BA/BS in Finance Economics or quantitative field; MBA or Masters preferred.
*   20+ years of progressive FP&A experience in a PE backed or public company.
*   Strong background in SaaS and/or AI business models with deep knowledge of recurring revenue financials.
*   Exceptional analytical, modeling, and communication skills.
*   Strong executive presence and interpersonal skills with proficiency in distilling complex business information into consumable and actionable takeaways.
*   Systems thinker. Experience in designing and implementing new systems and tools
*   Comfortable operating in a fast-paced, ambiguous, and highly dynamic environment.

**Personal Characteristics**

*   Entrepreneurial with a high level of energy, dedication, and an unrelenting drive to succeed.
*   Inspirational and disciplined leader who inspires and optimizes effectiveness among diverse teams.
*   Embodies the highest ethical standards and is accountable, transparent, and team oriented.
*   A motivated self-starter who can set strategy and drive tactical-level execution.
*   Intellectual, yet comfortable making decisions in ambiguous situations.
*   Action-oriented but maintains highly collaborative working relationships with others.
