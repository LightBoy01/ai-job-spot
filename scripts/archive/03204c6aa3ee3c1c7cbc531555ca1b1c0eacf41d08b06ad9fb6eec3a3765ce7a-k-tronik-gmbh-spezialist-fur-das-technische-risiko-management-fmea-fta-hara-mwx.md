### Description\nK-tronik is seeking a passionate and team-oriented **Technical Risk Management Specialist (FMEA, FTA, HARA)** to join their expert team, working full-time for a client in the Automotive & Commercial Vehicles sector. This role offers the opportunity to engage with exciting projects and future-oriented technologies, contributing to innovation with dedication and collaborative spirit.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Be responsible for conducting risk analyses (FMEA, FTA, HARA) accompanying the development process.\n- Coordinate, moderate, and document FMEA, FTA, and HARA workshops for new developments, particularly in accordance with functional safety requirements (ISO26262).\n- Be responsible for status reporting in the relevant project committees.\n- Actively participate in the process optimization of FMEA, FTA, and HARA methods within MAN Truck & Bus SE and in cross-brand corporate working groups.\n- Define procedures for FMEA, FTA, and HARA methodologies in guidelines, work instructions, and standard specifications.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Degree in Engineering (University/FH).\n- At least 5 years of experience in implementing technical risk management methods in the automotive industry.\n- Several years of operational experience in conducting FMEAs, FTAs, and HARAs in automotive development projects.\n- Possess excellent communication, coordination, and moderation skills.\n- Convince through strong customer orientation and a solution-oriented working approach.\n- Exhibit initiative, goal-orientation, and team spirit.\n- Operational experience with APIS-IQ FMEA and Isograph Fault Tree tools, along with knowledge of Functional Safety (ISO26262).\n- Knowledge regarding the development of mechatronic systems in the automotive industry.\n- Further experience with additional methods for technical risk management (e.g., FMEDA) is an advantage.\n- Proficient German and English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a highly impactful **Senior Individual Contributor** role for an experienced Technical Risk Management Specialist passionate about functional safety in the automotive industry. The position offers significant responsibility in shaping safety methodologies across an entire corporate group, beyond just project execution. K-tronik emphasizes a supportive, collaborative, and growth-oriented culture with excellent work-life balance (flexible hours, 30 vacation days, team events) and extensive professional development opportunities. This makes it an attractive prospect for those looking to drive innovation in automotive safety and make a tangible difference in product reliability and compliance.
