### Description\nAre you a passionate sales expert with a strong sense for market trends and customer needs? Do you enjoy building business relationships and securing long-term success? Then we have the perfect challenge for you!\n\nWe are looking for an **Account Executive (m/f/d) - Health (Healthcare)** to join our growing team. You will leverage your expertise and energy to expand our market position and sustainably grow our customer base. This role is about shaping the digital health of tomorrow. In the healthcare sector, digital sovereignty and data protection are paramount. You will support the digitalization of processes, applications, and data flows for clinics, research institutions, and public health authorities with our secure IT solutions, all with the goal of improving patient care. If you are proactive, goal-oriented, and ready to take responsibility, we look forward to getting to know you!\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Acquire and manage customers in the healthcare sector – e.g., clinics, public health offices, research institutions – for our IT services: Hosting, IT outsourcing, software development, security, and AI solutions.\n*   Initiate projects, serve as the central point of contact in the sales cycle, and collaborate closely with our healthcare IT consulting teams.\n*   Identify trends such as interoperable data platforms, AI-supported diagnostics, and IT security in the healthcare environment, and translate them into sales opportunities.\n*   Build long-term customer relationships, articulate regulatory requirements (e.g., data protection, medical technology compliance), and highlight added values like digital sovereignty.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Sales experience in the healthcare sector OR strong interest and understanding of healthcare IT (e.g., clinics, medical technology, research).\n*   Good knowledge of IT security, data protection, software solutions, and AI in the healthcare context.\n*   Strong customer orientation, negotiation skills, and enjoyment in building solutions and consulting business.\n*   Willingness to travel, good German and English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a driven Account Executive who is passionate about making a tangible impact in the critical healthcare sector. The company offers a highly flexible and supportive work environment, emphasizing work-life balance through remote options, flexible hours, and a kindergarten subsidy. With a strong focus on professional development, team spirit, and comprehensive well-being benefits, including an on-site gym and health days, this role provides an excellent platform for long-term growth and contribution to digital health innovation.
