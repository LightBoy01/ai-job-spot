### Description\nGFN GmbH is an IT-specialized education provider that supports individuals across Germany in their further education and career reorientation. This 12-month paid Trainee Program is your springboard to becoming an IHK-certified IT Instructor (Ausbilder). You will experience the world of retraining and further education firsthand, actively shaping the future of IT specialists. GFN GmbH is one of Germany's largest IT education providers, committed to facilitating successful entry into the future-oriented IT sector through targeted further education.\n\n### Responsibilities\nFrom day one, you will receive optimal preparation for your future role as an instructor (m/f/x) for our IT Specialists in System Integration and/or Application Development (IHK) (m/f/x) at GFN. Our experienced colleagues will provide professional, methodological, and didactic guidance throughout your journey. After an intensive onboarding phase, you will take over the mentoring and support of our trainees. You will serve as the primary contact for all questions regarding their practical final exams and will conduct classes specifically designed to prepare them for these crucial assessments.\n\n### Qualifications\nTo be our ideal Trainee (m/f/x), you should possess:\n*   A reliable and team-oriented work ethic.\n*   A completed apprenticeship or retraining as an IT Specialist in System Integration or Application Development (m/f/x) (or a comparable qualification).\n*   A natural ability to explain complex topics to others and a passion for presenting.\n*   A strong affinity and enthusiasm for IT subjects.\n*   Interest in topics such as final exams, examination requirements, deadlines, admission criteria, company project work, and similar aspects.\n*   Initial experience in training or mentoring apprentices.\n*   Very good German language skills (at least C1 level).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis Trainee Program at GFN GmbH offers an exceptional entry-level opportunity for passionate IT professionals looking to transition into education. It provides a structured, fully paid year of comprehensive training, culminating in the highly valued IHK Instructor Aptitude Certificate. The role is heavily supported by experienced mentors and a professional tandem partner, ensuring a smooth and guided journey. GFN's commitment to employee well-being is evident through its generous benefits package, including 30 days of vacation, corporate discounts, JobRad leasing, EGYM Wellpass, an internet allowance, a subsidized Deutschland-Ticket, and group accident insurance. This position is perfect for someone eager to share their IT knowledge, make a tangible impact on others' careers, and thrive in an inclusive, supportive, and growth-focused environment. It's a direct path to a rewarding career as an IT instructor within a leading educational institution.
