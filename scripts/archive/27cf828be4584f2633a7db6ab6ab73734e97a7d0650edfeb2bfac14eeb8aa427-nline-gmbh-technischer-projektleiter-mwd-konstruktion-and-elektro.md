### Description
nLine GmbH, founded in 2023 as a subsidiary of Wahtari GmbH and based in Haar near Munich, specializes in the development of innovative AI-based systems for visual quality assurance. We provide 360° all-in-one solutions that are utilized by renowned manufacturers of cables, wires, pipes, hoses, and ropes. Our solutions are distinguished by their high accuracy, versatility, and quick, straightforward commissioning. Furthermore, we place great importance on the highest level of data security, ensured by our internally developed software and hardware.

We are seeking a dedicated and experienced Project Lead to drive the technical success of our cutting-edge AI vision systems, taking a pivotal role in shaping our product delivery and team development.

### Responsibilities
*   Lead and coordinate technical projects from the concept phase through to series production readiness.
*   Provide technical leadership and motivation to the project team.
*   Take responsibility for achieving project deadlines, cost targets, and quality objectives.
*   Ensure close collaboration with the design, development, and electrical engineering departments.
*   Actively contribute to the technical design and optimization of systems and components.
*   Support the continuous development of standards, processes, and tools within the organization.
*   Facilitate effective communication with customers, suppliers, and internal interfaces.

### Qualifications
*   A completed technical degree (e.g., Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, Electrical Engineering, or a comparable field).
*   Several years of professional experience in design and/or project management.
*   Proficient knowledge of electrical engineering, ideally with interfaces to mechanical components.
*   Proven leadership competence and a genuine enjoyment in leading and developing teams.
*   A structured, solution-oriented, and independent working style.
*   Strong communication skills and excellent organizational talent.
