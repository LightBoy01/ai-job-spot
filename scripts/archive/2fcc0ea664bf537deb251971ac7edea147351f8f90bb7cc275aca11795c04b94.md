Description

The **R&D Engineer** will design, test and commercialize advanced fabrics and composite materials that deliver superior thermal insulation, heat dissipation, sealing performance and temperature regulation in extreme environments. This position will continually develop new innovative products, growing the Company and solidifying Davlyn’s position as the leading manufacturer of best-in-class protection solutions for the energy, industrial and transportation markets. The R&D Engineer must be curious, willing to take risks and possess an entrepreneurial mindset. This position combines materials science expertise, hands-on textile engineering skills and a passion for performance-driven product innovation.

  

  

**Davlyn Group** is a fast growing, privately owned technical textile company based in Spring City, PA, just outside of Philadelphia, with additional locations in Norristown, PA, Independence, VA and Juarez, Mexico. Through our Amatex, Darco Southern, Davlyn Manufacturing, and Norfab businesses, our products and engineering services are used by thousands of customers in over 50 countries to help keep heat in its place, improve safety, and increase productivity. DGH is a leading manufacturer of technical textiles, fabrics and yarns used in the appliance industry, industrial markets, and personal protective equipment (PPE).

  

  

**ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS:**

Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions.

*   Research & Innovation:
*   Lead R&D projects for the Industrial segment within Davlyn Group, including fiber selection, yarn design, nonwoven/fabric construction, braiding, weaving, twisting, coatings, laminations, and composite systems.
*   Investigate and apply emerging materials such as fiberglass, aluminized fabrics, ceramic fibers, phase change materials (PCMs), aerogels, nanofibers, reflective barriers, advanced ceramics, and thermal coatings to enhance thermal performance.
*   Designing & Prototyping
*   Rapidly develop prototypes using weaving, knitting, nonwoven, braiding, twisting, and/or lamination processes.
*   Develop and maintain the internal lab and external partners to iterate and validate material performance. Applications include oven gaskets, Automotive protective textile sleeves/wraps, aerospace and space exploration insulation, as well as high-density ropes and tapes for industrial furnaces.
*   Translate thermal performance targets into material specifications and construction parameters, develop these, and process samples.
*   Testing & Validation
*   Plan and execute laboratory and field testing for thermal conductivity, insulation value (Clo/R-value), heat flux, moisture vapor transmission, flammability, durability, cyclic thermal exposure, and mechanical performance under heat.
*   Analyze test data, identify trends, and recommend design improvements.
*   Ensure compliance with relevant industry standards (ISO, ASTM, EN, NFPA, UL, MIL, FAA, Automotive).
*   Cross-Functional Collaboration
*   Partner with product design, manufacturing, supply chain, and quality teams to ensure materials meet performance, scalability, and cost requirements.
*   Collaborate with customers, external labs, internal leadership, and partners to validate performance requirements and support certifications.
*   Participate in and lead the Stage Gate process for Davlyn Group.
*   Communicate technical findings to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
*   Project Management
*   Define project scope, timelines, and deliverables.
*   Prepare technical documentation, material specifications, and reports.
*   Support scale-up from prototype to full-scale production, including pilot-line trials.
*   Deliver projects on time and in scope with a bias for action.
*   Opportunity to lead engineers/technicians and contribute to project budgets.

  

  

Requirements

**EDUCATION/EXPERIENCE**

*   Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Textile Engineering, Materials Science, Polymer Science, Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, or related field.5+ years of progressive experience in a related Manufacturing environment.
*   3+ years of R&D experience in performance textiles, preferably with a focus on thermal management.
*   Hands-on experience with fiber/yarn production, textile construction, coatings, and lamination processes.  
*   Familiarity with thermal performance testing methods and equipment. 
*   Experience in adjacent industries (aerospace, defense, protective fabrics, polymers, composites) strongly valued.

  

**SKILLS**

*   Excellent problem-solving abilities and a collaborative mindset.
*   CAD, AI platforms, spreadsheets, Microsoft Office.
