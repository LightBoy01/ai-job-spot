The team is passionate about supporting innovative startups from across Germany with mentoring, workshops, and expert consultations.\n\nThis is an exciting opportunity to join the STARTPLATZ Family and contribute to its mission of discovering new markets and spreading the STARTPLATZ spirit throughout Germany. You'll gain access to a world full of fascinating startups, innovators, and corporates, with a large, Germany-wide network. The company fosters a hands-on mentality, offering early responsibility where your contributions directly influence STARTPLATZ's success.
