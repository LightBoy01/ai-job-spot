**Do you enjoy designing and building public cloud solutions for customers?**

**Do you enjoy working with a diverse multinational team of engineering talents?**

**Join the Technical Solutions Architect team**

Akamai is working to simplify the way people work in the cloud. The team's mission is to accelerate innovation by making computing simple, scalable, accessible, and affordable for all. We provide the infrastructure and services that enable developers to build next generation applications.

**Shape the future of the Internet**

As a Technical Solutions Architect, you'll support the sales lifecycle by defining Cloud/Linode solutions as presales engineer. You will guide customers' Cloud transformation journey strategically and technically, driving adoption and consumption of Linode services. In addition, you'll also be the thought-leader in cloud computing both outside and within Akamai.

As a Senior Technical Solutions Architect, you will be responsible for:

*   Driving Linode opportunities as a public cloud pre-sales engineer.
*   Owning Linode accounts and sales opportunities from the technical side, working closely with assigned sales counterparts.
*   Driving customer success and consumption of Linode, as part of the integrated account team.
*   Bringing cloud professional service delivery experience will be added advantage.
*   Driving solution innovation by evangelizing new use cases, shaping trends, and influencing roadmaps with global product managers.
*   Building technical thought-leadership in Cloud Computing/Linode to support overall Akamai business growth.

**Do what you love**

To be successful in this role you will:

*   Have 7+ years' professional experience, including 5+ years in customer-facing technical roles with cloud vendors or integrators.
*   Demonstrate expertise in architecting IaaS solutions and hold professional-level certifications from AWS, GCP, Azure, Oracle, or CNCF.
*   Demonstrate hands-on expertise across at least three domains, including Kubernetes, cloud networking, databases, AI/GPU, or media stacks.
*   Demonstrate knowledge and hands-on skills in common open-source software such as Apache, MySQL/PostgreSQL, Redis, Nginx, Terraform, etc.
*   Demonstrate excellent written, verbal, and presentation skills, effectively communicating complex technical content to technical and non-technical audiences.
