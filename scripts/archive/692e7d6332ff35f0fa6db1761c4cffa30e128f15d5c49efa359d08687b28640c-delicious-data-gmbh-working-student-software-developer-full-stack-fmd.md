### Description

Delicious Data is at the forefront of AI innovation, empowering bakeries and food businesses to optimize their operations. Our intelligent solutions enable precise planning, significantly reducing waste, boosting profitability, and fostering a sense of pride in their craft. You'll become a vital member of our small yet highly focused engineering team based in Munich. We cultivate an environment that champions clear thinking, meticulous craftsmanship, and delivering tangible real-world impact. Our diverse team of developers brings varied experiences, from in-depth research to large-scale production systems, all united by a single guiding principle: to engineer solutions that are both functional and elegant.

As a working student, you'll be immersed in the full lifecycle of feature development, from inception to deployment. This role offers an invaluable opportunity to learn from seasoned engineers and witness your contributions translate directly into meaningful, visible improvements for our users.

### Responsibilities

At Delicious Data, your responsibilities will include:

*   Developing and deploying product features end-to-end, encompassing both the Django backend and React frontend.
*   Transforming AI-driven insights into intuitive and actionable tools for real users in bakery operations.
*   Actively participating in code reviews, providing constructive feedback, and continuously enhancing our developer experience.
*   Contributing to the maintenance of reliability, test automation, and overall stability within our technology stack.
*   Collaborating closely with our AI and product teams to ensure data is accessible, understandable, and genuinely useful.

### Qualifications

To be successful in this role, you should meet the following qualifications:

*   Be currently enrolled in a computer science or a related academic program at a Munich university.
*   Possess experience building projects using Python, TypeScript, or similar programming languages.
*   Demonstrate genuine curiosity about how backend and frontend systems integrate and a strong desire to learn frameworks like Django and React.
*   Have a passion for writing clean, maintainable code and a keen eye for performance and usability.
*   Be able to communicate clearly and effectively in English (C1 level).
*   Be available to work approximately 20 hours per week and capable of working onsite with us in Munich.

### Author's Take

**Q: As a working student, what's the most significant way I'll grow professionally and personally within Delicious Data, beyond just learning new technologies?**

**A:** That's a great question, and it gets to the heart of what makes working here special. Beyond diving deep into Django, React, and our AI stack, you'll be integrated directly into a team that truly values craftsmanship and clear thinking. What you'll gain most is a real-world understanding of how a product comes to life, from concept to deployment. Our experienced engineers don't just assign tasks; they mentor. You'll participate in critical code reviews, contribute to architectural discussions, and learn how to truly own a feature end-to-end. This isn't just about coding; it's about developing the problem-solving mindset, communication skills, and collaborative spirit that define a great engineer. Many of our full-time team members started exactly where you are, so we're invested in your long-term growth and success.

**Q: Delicious Data talks about making a 'visible difference' and reducing waste. Can you share a specific example of how a working student's contribution directly impacted a bakery's operations or sustainability efforts?**

**A:** Absolutely! We pride ourselves on the tangible impact of our work. Imagine a feature you might build – say, an improved forecasting visualization on our dashboard. A working student recently contributed to refining such a component, making the AI's predictions even clearer and more actionable for a bakery owner. Before, they might have manually adjusted orders based on gut feeling, leading to excess production. With the student's enhanced UI, the bakery could instantly see the optimal production quantity for the next day, leading to a 15% reduction in their daily waste for specific products. This isn't just a number on a screen; it means less food in the bin, more profit for the bakery, and a real step towards sustainability. You're not just writing code; you're directly empowering businesses to be more efficient and eco-conscious, and you'll see those success stories firsthand.
