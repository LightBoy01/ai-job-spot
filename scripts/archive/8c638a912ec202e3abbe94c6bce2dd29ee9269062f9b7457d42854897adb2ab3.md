**Job Description:**

We’re looking for an **Executive Talent Management Program Manager** to join Procore’s Talent Team. In this role, you’ll lead the design and continuous improvement of our most critical executive talent systems, transforming them from standalone programs into scalable, high-impact products.

As an **Executive Talent Management Program Manager**, you’ll partner with senior HR and business leaders to shape, deliver, and optimize our executive talent strategy. Use your strategic thinking, product management mindset, and data-driven approach to create systems that enhance leadership capability, drive organizational effectiveness, and prepare Procore’s next generation of executives. If you approach Talent Management with an AI-first, Product mindset, this is the role for you—apply today and help us build the future of executive leadership.

This position reports into the Head of Talent Management and is a hybrid role based in our Denver, CO office (1 day in-office, 4 days from home). We’re looking for someone to join us immediately.

**What You’ll Do**

*   Own and manage the Executive Talent Product Portfolio, including Leadership Principles, Succession Planning, Talent Reviews, C-Suite Assessments, Executive Promotions, and Executive Onboarding.
    
*   Define and execute a multi-year product roadmap that aligns with Procore’s business strategy and leadership needs.
    
*   Apply Product Management and Agile principles to HR—gather Voice of the Customer feedback, design MVPs, iterate, and measure adoption and impact.
    
*   Partner with key stakeholders (People Partners, Talent team, business leaders) to deliver high-impact products that strengthen Procore's executive talent density
    
*   Leverage analytics to track success metrics such as adoption, efficiency, and ROI, and provide performance updates to senior leaders.
    
*   Identify and pilot AI or automation opportunities that enhance the fairness, speed, and scalability of executive talent processes.
    
*   Build and maintain governance and communication frameworks that ensure alignment across the business and HR teams.
    

**What We’re Looking For**

*   Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Business, or a related field.
    
*   5+ years of experience in Talent Management, HR, or related fields, with direct exposure to executive or senior leadership audiences.
    
*   Proven experience applying Product Management, Agile, or UX principles to HR program design and execution.
    
*   Strong analytical and strategic thinking skills with a bias toward simplicity, automation, and measurable outcomes.
    
*   Exceptional communication and consulting skills, with the ability to influence at the C-suite and VP level.
    
*   Demonstrated ability to manage multiple priorities, build strong stakeholder relationships, and lead projects end-to-end.
    
*   Curiosity and comfort exploring AI and data-driven solutions to modernize HR processes.
