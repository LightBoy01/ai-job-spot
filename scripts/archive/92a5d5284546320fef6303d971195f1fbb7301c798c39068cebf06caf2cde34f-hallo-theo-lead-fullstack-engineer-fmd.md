### Description\nAs a Lead Fullstack Engineer at hallo theo, you will be instrumental in designing, building, and operating the core systems of our AI-native property management platform. This hands-on role involves working across the entire stack, from intuitive frontends to robust backend services and data flows, taking full ownership of critical user journeys. You will apply a solution-oriented mindset, strong attention to detail, and empathy for users to transform ambiguous problems into simple, impactful product experiences, significantly shaping the future of property management.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Own the end-to-end delivery of features and services, from discovery and technical design through implementation, testing, rollout, and monitoring in production.\n- Build and evolve reliable backend and frontend components (APIs, services, UIs) that seamlessly integrate with our web tech stack and data layer.\n- Continuously improve our architecture and codebase by refactoring, defining clear interfaces, and ensuring the platform remains secure, observable, and maintainable.\n- Collaborate closely with Product, Design, and Operations teams to define scope, break down complex problems, and make pragmatic trade-offs between speed and quality.\n- Act as a technical sparring partner and mentor for other engineers, actively supporting good engineering practices and fostering a healthy engineering culture.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 6–8 years of experience in software engineering, ideally within product-driven environments like startups or scale-ups, demonstrating a strong track record in complex and uncertain projects.\n- Possess a strong product mindset, thinking in terms of user journeys and outcomes rather than just tickets, and comfortable making trade-offs under ambiguity.\n- Solid understanding of Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), and current AI trends, coupled with a motivation to apply them pragmatically in a real product.\n- Proficient in at least one modern programming language and experienced in a web tech stack (e.g., Python and SQL, alongside modern web frameworks and APIs).\n- Experience operating services in the cloud (e.g., AWS, GCP, or Azure), including CI/CD, logging, monitoring, and basic security practices.\n- Business-fluent German (C1 or higher) and strong English skills, essential for working with German-speaking stakeholders and an international team.\n- **Nice to have:** Experience in domains with complex operations or workflows, such as proptech, fintech, marketplaces, or B2B SaaS.\n- **Nice to have:** Prior experience in a \"tech lead\" capacity, involving leading projects or streams, even without formal line management.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a Lead Fullstack Engineer to make a significant impact at hallo theo. You'll be at the forefront of integrating AI into real-world property management, working with a clear, ambitious vision and a tech-first operating model. The role offers high autonomy, encouraging you to shape not only the platform but also the engineering practices and culture. With an emphasis on mentorship and collaboration within an interdisciplinary team, this position is ideal for someone passionate about solving complex problems, driving innovation, and directly influencing the future of an industry.
