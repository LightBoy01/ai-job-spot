**JOB ACCOUNTABILITY:**
The service manager is responsible for leading and continuously enhancing NOV's aftermarket service operations in the Caspian region. The role requires ensuring operational excellence, meeting customer demands, maintaining financial performance, and driving innovative practices aligned with global industry standards.
**ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES:**
**Service Leadership:**
*   Lead and develop a dynamic offshore service team to effectively support regional customers.
*   Develop and implement strategic service objectives aligned with NOV’s global aftermarket vision.
*   Actively participate in shaping industry best practices by engaging in professional forums and contributing to NOV’s global aftermarket strategy.
*   Drive customer satisfaction initiatives and foster a strong customer-focused culture.
*   Oversee financial management, including forecasting, budgeting, and analysing departmental profitability.
*   Ensure service capability and resource allocation align with current and projected market demands.
*   Cultivate personnel development, training programs, and structured succession planning.
*   Collaborate closely with cross-functional and regional management teams to drive business growth.
*   Regularly assess market competition and identify opportunities for expanding business with key clients.
*   Maintain proactive, clear communication with leadership, ensuring awareness of both opportunities and potential risks.
**Planning – Productivity – Scheduling:**
*   Evaluate operational outcomes and implement continuous improvement initiatives.
*   Accurately scope project requirements and manage service execution effectively.
*   Analyse operational data to optimize resource utilization and enhance efficiency.
*   Implement structured processes and monitor KPIs to measure and improve service delivery.
*   Drive innovation by identifying and implementing advanced technical solutions, digital tools, and data-driven processes to enhance operational excellence.
**Human Resources and Governance:**
*   Ensure compliance with all applicable regional and international laws governing offshore service operations.
*   Establish and manage operational processes, ensuring clear structures and communication within the service department.
*   Manage, mentor, and develop direct reports to adhere to NOV standards and policies.
*   Address and resolve any operational or personnel issues proactively.
*   Oversee recruitment, training, performance evaluation, and disciplinary procedures as required.
*   Champion a diverse and inclusive team culture, ensuring equal opportunities for professional development and advancement.
**Punctuality & Professionalism:**
*   Ensure adherence to operational timelines and deadlines.
*   Establish priorities clearly, fostering a disciplined and professional work environment.
*   Demonstrate resilience and agility in crisis situations, managing operational contingencies swiftly and effectively to minimise downtime and maintain client confidence
**HSE & Quality Assurance:**
*   Champion NOV’s HSE culture, ensuring rigorous adherence to safety protocols and practices.
*   Promote active use of SWA (Stop Work Authority) and encourage safety awareness at all organisational levels.
*   Monitor and maintain high standards of housekeeping and workplace safety.
*   Adhere strictly to NOV's quality assurance standards, policies, and procedural guidelines.
*   Support NOV’s commitment to sustainable offshore operations by advocating environmentally responsible practices and continuous improvement in HSE performance.
**QUALIFICATIONS, EXPERIENCE, & SKILLS:**
**Qualifications:**
*   Qualification in Engineering or a relevant technical discipline.
*   Additional qualifications or experience in business management are highly desirable.
**Experience:**
*   Minimum of 5 years in junior-to-middle management, preferably within the Oil & Gas industry.
*   Strong working knowledge of NOV Rig Equipment product lines and services.
*   Experience in either mechanical, electrical, or automation technical disciplines is beneficial.
**Skills and Competencies:**
*   Strong computer literacy (Microsoft Excel, Word, Outlook & AI tools).
*   Familiarity with technical documentation and engineering drawing systems.
*   Proficient with ERP systems and project management practices.
*   Excellent leadership, communication, and interpersonal skills.
*   Proven ability to independently lead and develop teams effectively.
*   Exceptional customer relationship management capabilities.
