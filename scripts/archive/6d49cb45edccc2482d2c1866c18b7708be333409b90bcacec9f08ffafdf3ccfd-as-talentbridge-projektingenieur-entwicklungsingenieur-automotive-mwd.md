### Description\nThis role involves developing and optimizing passive vehicle safety systems, focusing on frontal and side impact protection for vehicles. The successful candidate will leverage simulation and in-house testing, coordinate with various stakeholders to meet legal and consumer protection requirements, and analyze results for client presentations. It's a critical position at the forefront of automotive safety innovation.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Develop subsystems of the overall restraint system in the area of frontal or side protection using simulation and in-house testing.\n*   Coordinate occupant protection-relevant components to meet various legal and consumer protection requirements.\n*   Define corresponding functional requirements for components, considering customer requirements and the cost model.\n*   Manage calculations and coordinate prototypes.\n*   Accompany the respective test setup and execution.\n*   Analyze and present results to the client.\n*   Define measures to achieve objectives and coordinate them with the department and the client.\n*   Gather and evaluate market-relevant information and present it to the departments.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Degree in Mechanical Engineering, Automotive Engineering, Industrial Engineering or comparable fields, alternatively a technician (m/f/d) with relevant professional experience.\n*   Experience in passive vehicle safety is an advantage.\n*   Experience with the creation of CAD and CAE models in the automotive industry.\n*   Product FMEA according to VDA Volume 4.\n*   Very good English language skills desirable.\n*   CAD and CAE knowledge.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a technically proficient engineer with a passion for automotive safety. The role offers a stimulating environment focused on innovation and continuous improvement, where your analytical skills in simulation and testing will directly contribute to developing cutting-edge occupant protection systems. The emphasis on collaboration, client interaction, and the pursuit of progress suggests a fulfilling path for someone who thrives in a dynamic, growth-oriented setting.
