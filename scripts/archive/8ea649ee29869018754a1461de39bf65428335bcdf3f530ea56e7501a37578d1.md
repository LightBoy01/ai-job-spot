Applications are currently being accepted for the position of Category Development Manager, Sun, Wipes & Fem Care.

##### **ROLE SUMMARY**

The Category Development Manager leads the development and implementation of category plans and insights for internal cross-functional teams and external customers.  This includes providing analytical support, insights, and actionable recommendations that support the achievement of both the retailer and EPC’s sales and profit objectives.

**RESPONSIBILITIES**

*   Develop category consumption projections at key points in the annual and strategic planning processes and be the authority in front of customer on future category trends.
*   Fully leverage a broad range of insights (shopper/consumer/economic/retailer) into the category management process.
*   Fully leverage analytical strength in creating compelling fact-based solutions for customer and brand plans.
*   Deliver thorough and well-supported analysis to the customer regarding assortment and merchandising recommendations.
*   Champion the category vision and strategy process for EPC categories.
*   Align category development effort and priorities with marketing and sales strategy and support the identification of market opportunities.

**EDUCATION & QUALIFICATIONS**
==============================

*   University Business degree with 5+ years of category management or market/consumer analytics experience, preferably in a CPG environment.
*   Excellent computer skills, including spreadsheet, database and presentation software.
*   Experience with Nielsen data (i.e. MarketTrack, Strategic Planner) and Plan-o-gram software.
*   Knowledge of Numerator, LDIA, Stackline, and/or other Retailer tools is an asset.
*   Prior sales/trade/marketing experience in a CPG environment is an asset.

**SKILLS & EXPERIENCE**
=======================

*   Strong understanding of the role of insights, analytics and shopper marketing in Category Management.
*   Experience with category management theory & analysis and solid understanding of financial analysis principles is strongly preferred. 
*   Experience with OMNI/eCommerce insights and trends is an asset.
*   Strong, proactive collaborator comfortable interacting at all levels within the organization. A team player who thrives in a team-oriented environment.
*   Solid project management and organizational skills to prioritize and multi-task.
*   Highly developed analytical, story-telling and problem-solving skills.
*   Self-starter, able to work independently on projects.
*   Excellent communication, presentation, and interpersonal skills at all levels both within and outside the organization.
