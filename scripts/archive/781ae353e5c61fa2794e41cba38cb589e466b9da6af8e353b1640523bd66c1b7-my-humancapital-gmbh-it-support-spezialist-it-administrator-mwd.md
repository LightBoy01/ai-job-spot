### Description\nOur client in Munich is looking for an **IT-Support Specialist / IT-Administrator (m/w/d)** to join their team as soon as possible. In this role, you will be a key player in ensuring the smooth operation and support of their IT infrastructure and end-user devices.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Responsible for the installation, setup, and support of IT end devices within the defined lifecycle process.\n*   Set up network connections for end devices and peripheral equipment, ensuring their seamless integration into existing systems.\n*   Provide 2nd-level support to users, analyze technical issues, and perform independent troubleshooting.\n*   Execute technical tasks autonomously while adhering to internal guidelines and quality standards.\n*   Take on representation duties within the IT team during absences and vacation planning.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   A completed degree in Computer Science or vocational training as an IT Specialist for System Integration, or a comparable IT qualification with several years of professional experience.\n*   Solid experience in IT support and end device management, ideally in larger or security-relevant IT environments.\n*   Knowledge in the use and administration of software distribution tools such as Microsoft Intune, ACMP, or Baramundi.\n*   Structured, solution-oriented, team-player with a proactive approach.\n*   Very good German language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced IT professional to join a supportive team in Munich. The role offers a great balance of independent problem-solving and collaborative teamwork within a modern IT environment. With benefits like flexible working hours, home office options, and a strong emphasis on continuous learning and a flat hierarchy, it's a fantastic place to grow your career while maintaining a healthy work-life balance.
