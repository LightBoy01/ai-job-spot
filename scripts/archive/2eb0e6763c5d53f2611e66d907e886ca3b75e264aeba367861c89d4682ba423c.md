**Job Description:**

**Overview of the role**

The Program Manager will play a pivotal role in driving SLGS’s strategic and transformational agenda. This role combines strategic execution, cross-functional leadership, and program delivery excellence. The incumbent will be responsible for conceptualizing, orchestrating, and delivering programs that create measurable business impact, client value, and organizational capability uplift.

This is a unique opportunity for a professional who can think strategically, influence cross-functionally, and execute with precision — helping reimagine Sun Life Global Solutions as a digital, data-led, and AI-powered organization.

**Key Responsibilities**

1\. Strategy Execution & Program Leadership

*   Translate SLGS’s strategic priorities into actionable multi-workstream programs with defined business outcomes.
*   Serve as the portfolio lead for SLGS initiatives spanning technology, operations, and corporate functions.
*   Partner with functional heads and global stakeholders to ensure alignment and sponsorship.

2\. Cross-Functional Orchestration

*   Act as the strategic liaison across business groups, technology, and enablement functions, ensuring integrated execution.
*   Manage governance, cadence, and decision forums for critical initiatives.
*   Drive risk, dependency, and change management across workstreams.

3\. Innovation & Technology Enablement

*   Co-lead ideation and incubation of technology-enabled solutions, including GenAI and automation opportunities.
*   Partner with Engineering, Data, and Digital teams to accelerate experimentation and scale deployment.
*   Evaluate emerging technologies and contribute to SLGS’s innovation roadmap.

4\. Performance Management & Reporting

*   Establish robust program management frameworks, metrics, and dashboards for executive visibility.
*   Deliver crisp, outcome-oriented reporting for SLGS Leadership and global stakeholders.
*   Track value realization and ensure sustained adoption post-deployment.

**Key Skills & Experience**

*   10–15 years of experience in program management, strategy execution, or transformation roles (preferably in BFSI, GCC, or consulting).
*   Proven success managing large, cross-functional initiatives with measurable business impact.
*   Strong understanding of technology transformation, digital enablement, and AI/GenAI concepts.
*   Excellent executive communication and stakeholder management skills.
*   Comfort working in an agile, high-growth, and globally matrixed environment.

**Ideal Candidate Attributes**

*   Strategic Thinker: Connects enterprise vision with actionable execution pathways.
*   Outcome-Oriented: Focused on measurable impact, not just activity.
*   Innovator: Curious about emerging tech and capable of converting ideas into scalable pilots.
*   Connector: Strong collaborator who can influence without authority.
*   Change Champion: Embodies a growth mindset and drives reinvention across teams.

**Success Measures**

*   Delivery of strategic programs on time, within scope, and achieving business impact.
*   Acceleration of innovation and GenAI adoption within SLGS.
*   Improved stakeholder satisfaction and cross-functional alignment.
*   Clear visibility of portfolio performance through enhanced reporting and governance.
