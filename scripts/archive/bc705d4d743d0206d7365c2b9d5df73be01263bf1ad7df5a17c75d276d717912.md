**Job Description:**
**Job Description: Senior Supply Chain Automation Developer**
**Location:** Hyderabad, India
**About NEXTracker:** We’re building a high-impact Center of Excellence inside one of the fastest-growing clean energy companies in the world. Our goal is to amplify productivity across the supply chain by automating work, surfacing insights, and building intelligent solutions that scale. If you love creating elegant technical solutions that make people’s work easier and better, you belong here.
**What You’ll Do:** As a Sr. Supply Chain Automation Developer, you’ll build and deploy automations, apps, and analytics that drive immediate business value. You’ll work in fast-paced sprints to deliver prototypes and iterate on solutions that streamline operations, reduce waste, and scale decision-making. You’ll sit at the intersection of process and tech, solving pain points through elegant, user-centric solutions.
**Key Responsibilities:**
*   Design and build low-code/no-code applications and workflows using the Microsoft Power Platform (Power Automate, Power Apps, Power BI)
*   Rapidly prototype solutions to address operational bottlenecks using Python, SQL, and AI tools where applicable
*   Model, transform, and integrate supply chain data to enable smart automation and decision support
*   Focus deeply on UI/UX to ensure adoption and usability of solutions
*   Collaborate closely with process engineers and SMEs to understand business context and iterate solutions
**Ideal Profile:**
*   Creative problem solver who adapts quickly and loves shipping fast, high-quality solutions
*   Deep experience in technical development, workflow automation, and low-code/no-code platforms
*   Excellent communication skills with an ability to align technical output to business needs
*   Enjoys navigating ambiguity and solving messy real-world operational challenges
**Preferred Qualifications:**
*   15+ years experience building digital solutions in supply chain, operations, IT, or consulting environments
*   Proven experience with Microsoft Power Platform, RPA tools, forms development, and Python/SQL
*   Experience applying AI in process automation (e.g. GPT prompts, document parsing, chatbots, etc.)
*   Strong UI/UX sensibilities
*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or equivalent experience
**Why Join Us?** This is a role where you’ll ship visible solutions fast, improve how hundreds of people work, and directly shape how the CoE delivers value to the business. NEXTracker is where technologists come to solve meaningful problems with lasting impact.
At Nextracker, we are leading in the energy transition, providing the most comprehensive portfolio of intelligent solar tracker and software solutions for solar power plants, as well as strategic services to capture the full value of solar power plants for our customers. Our talented worldwide teams are transforming PV plant performance every day with smart technology, data monitoring and analysis services.
For us at Nextracker, sustainability is not just a word. It's a core part of our business, values and our operations. Our sustainability efforts are based on five cornerstones: People, Community, Environment, Innovation, and Integrity. We are creative, collaborative and passionate problem-solvers from diverse backgrounds, driven by our shared mission to provide smart solar and software solutions for our customers and to mitigate climate change for future generations.
