### Description

Quintus Studios is a passionate creator of video content, spanning shorts and long-form formats for TV and digital platforms, reaching national and global audiences. We are dedicated to storytelling, building video worlds, and fostering communities with relevance and impact, reaching billions globally. "Content is king. Quintus is content."

The company is structured into three key units:
*   **Quintus Channel Network**: One of the world's largest independent factual channel networks on YouTube, Facebook, and Snapchat.
*   **Quintus Productions**: Focuses on content financing and production activities.
*   **Quintus Distribution**: Our global licensing division, distributing Quintus Originals and external productions to broadcasters, streaming services, and VOD partners worldwide.

The TV and video landscape is rapidly evolving, creating exciting opportunities and ensuring a dynamic work environment. Join our team in Berlin as a Brand Partnerships Manager (m/f/d), available in part-time or full-time.

As a **Brand Partnerships Manager**, you will be responsible for building brand partnerships and sponsorship deals related to our content and productions. Given our concepts often aim to combine broad reach with significant impact, a strong focus will be on partners who, beyond successful commercialization, also represent values like sustainability in environment and society. Working closely with colleagues from Development and Content Management, you will establish this department from scratch, creating an additional content financing avenue for Quintus. We are looking for an individual passionate about creative video projects that entertain and inform millions, loves working with people, and has proven experience in Branded Entertainment – whether from the marketer, agency, production, or brand side. This role is not about programmatic advertising; it's about finding creative ways to connect content and brands for mutual value. An existing relevant network is a significant advantage.

### Responsibilities

Your detailed responsibilities include:

*   Analyzing the broader Quintus content ecosystem for potential smart brand integrations.
*   Participating in the development process of new concepts, ensuring that new ideas not only have the potential for broad reach but also creative opportunities for brand partner involvement.
*   Creating professional pitch decks, researching potential brand partners in our core markets (primarily Germany/Europe, with future international expansion), identifying ways to approach them, and convincing them to collaborate.
*   Building and nurturing a network of relevant brand partners for Quintus.
*   Independently managing the entire process, from initial contact, through the offer phase and negotiations, to closing deals.
*   After contract signing, coordinating the necessary steps with internal teams and supporting the brand partner throughout the cooperation.

### Qualifications

We are looking for passionate individuals eager to contribute their skills and dedication to advance Quintus as a company. We also value the following qualifications:

**Experience**

*   2-3 years of professional experience in Branded Entertainment or similar roles – from the marketer, agency, production, or brand side (ideally in digital entertainment).
*   Sound knowledge of relevant KPIs for brands and experience with the entire process (how to approach brands, what's important to them, successful negotiation strategies).
*   An existing network of potential brand partners in Germany.

**Skills**

*   Fluent in German and English (written and spoken).
*   Strong communication skills and professional conduct, both with internal teams and external partners.
*   Excellent research, organizational, and time management skills.
*   Analytical and creative mindset – with the talent to identify market trends, opportunities, and draw strategic conclusions.
*   Ability to work in a dynamic environment and manage multiple processes in parallel.

**Personal Attributes**

*   You are a people magnet; people enjoy spending time with you, and you love meeting new people, networking, and building long-term, trusting friendships and partnerships.
*   You are passionate about your job.
*   You possess a solution-oriented mindset – you seek solutions and ways forward rather than dwelling on problems.
*   You have a proactive, entrepreneurial mindset.
*   You are a team player and highly reliable.
