**Senior Associate – Full-Stack Engineer**
At BNY, our culture allows us to run our company better and enables employees’ growth and success. As a leading global financial services company at the heart of the global financial system, we influence nearly 20% of the world’s investible assets. Every day, our teams harness cutting-edge AI and breakthrough technologies to collaborate with clients, driving transformative solutions that redefine industries and uplift communities worldwide.
Recognized as a top destination for innovators and champions of inclusion, BNY is where bold ideas meet advanced technology and exceptional talent. Together, we power the future of finance – and this is what #LifeAtBNY is all about. Join us and be part of something extraordinary.
We’re seeking a future team member for the role of **Senior Associate,Full-Stack Engineer** to join our Corporate Engineering team. This role is located in **Pune.**
In this role, you’ll make an impact in the following ways:
*   Lead the design and implementation of full stack solutions using modern frameworks and technologies.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define, design, and deliver new features.
*   Ensure code quality, security, and maintainability through best practices and code reviews.
*   Mentor junior developers and contribute to team knowledge sharing.
*   Troubleshoot, optimize, and enhance existing applications for performance and scalability.
*   Participate in Agile ceremonies and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives.
To be successful in this role, we’re seeking the following:
*   Bachelor’s degree in computer science engineering, or related Engineering.
*   Proven experience as a Full Stack Developer in enterprise environments.
*   Proficiency in front-end technologies (e.g., React, Angular, HTML, CSS, JavaScript).
*   Proficiency in back-end technologies (e.g., Java, .NET, Node.js, Python).
*   Experience with databases (SQL).
*   Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.
*   Excellent communication and documentation skills.
*   Knowledge of DevOps practices and CI/CD pipelines
*   Exposure to AI tools and solutions.
