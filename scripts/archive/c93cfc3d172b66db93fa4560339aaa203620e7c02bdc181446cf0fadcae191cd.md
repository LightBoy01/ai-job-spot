Intetics Inc., a leading American technology company specializing in custom software application development, distributed professional teams creation, software product quality assessment, and “all-things-digital” solutions, is looking for a Senior AI Engineer to lead for our AI systems. You will be responsible for maintaining and optimizing our existing integrations, advising on new features, and guiding the long-term AI strategy. This role requires strong hands-on expertise in large language models (LLMs), fine-tuning, and production deployment at scale.  
  
·       **Key Responsibilities:**

o   Maintain and improve our AI infrastructure (currently Azure OpenAI fine-tuned GPT models).

o   Design, fine-tune, and deploy advanced LLM solutions tailored to business needs.

o   Build and optimize APIs and workflows using **FastAPI,**

·       **LangChain/LangGraph**.

o   Collaborate with other teams to define AI-driven features.

o   Provide expert guidance on cloud (AWS & Azure) architecture for scalable AI deployments.

o   Stay up to date with the latest in generative AI, LLM research, and best practices.

o   Act as an AI team leader and mentor in AI-related initiatives.

**Requirements**

o   5+ years of hands-on experience in AI/ML engineering, with a strong focus on **LLMs**.

o   Proven experience with **Azure OpenAI** (or OpenAI APIs) and model fine-tuning.

o   Proficiency in **Python**, **FastAPI**, and AI frameworks (LangChain, LangGraph, etc.).

o   Strong understanding of cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure) and scalable deployment.

·       Experience leading AI/ML projects from prototype to production.
