### Description
Tulip, a pioneering leader in frontline operations, spun out of MIT, is revolutionizing industrial environments with its cloud-native, no-code platform. We empower companies worldwide to equip their workforce with connected apps, driving higher quality work, improved efficiency, and end-to-end traceability across operations. Recognized as a World Economic Forum Global Innovator and a Top Workplace, Tulip fosters a dynamic, fast-paced scale-up environment.

Based in Munich, Tulip operates on a hybrid model, requiring 3+ days in the office per week. We are seeking a proactive, organized, and detail-oriented student passionate about international B2B marketing to join our team for 20 hours/week, starting at the beginning of the Winter Semester 2025. This role is crucial for supporting Tulip's international growth through essential localization efforts, campaign execution, event preparation, and creative asset development, requiring collaboration across diverse regions and functions.

### Responsibilities
As an International Marketing Student, your key responsibilities will include:

*   Localizing and performing quality assurance on critical marketing assets such as website content, e-books, and ad copies, specifically for German or French markets.
*   Preparing operational performance reports to provide insights that support campaign optimization.
*   Conducting targeted audience and company research, often utilizing platforms like LinkedIn, to inform strategic initiatives.
*   Researching potential vendors and events, and submitting internal program requests to facilitate marketing activities.
*   Supporting the end-to-end execution of various marketing initiatives, including demand generation, lead generation, brand awareness, and Account-Based Marketing (ABM) campaigns.
*   Collaborating closely with the Creative Team to finalize layouts and develop engaging ad creatives using existing templates.

### Qualifications
To be successful in this role, you should possess the following qualifications:

**Required:**
*   Native fluency in either German OR French.
*   Currently enrolled in a degree program in marketing, communications, business, or a related field.
*   Strong English communication skills, essential for working effectively in our international team.
*   Highly organized, reliable, and a collaborative team player.
*   Demonstrated curiosity around data and analysis.
*   A genuine interest in working within a dynamic scale-up environment.

**Preferred:**
*   Prior internship experience in marketing or a related field.
*   Basic familiarity with marketing and operational tools such as Salesforce, Marketo, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, Canva, Jira, or Confluence.
