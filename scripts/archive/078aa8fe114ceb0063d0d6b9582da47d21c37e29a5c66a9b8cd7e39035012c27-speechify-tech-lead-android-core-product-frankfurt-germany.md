### Description
Speechify's core mission is to eliminate reading as a barrier to learning, empowering over 50 million people to convert any text – from PDFs and books to Google Docs and websites – into audio. This enables users to read faster, absorb more, and retain information more effectively through our highly acclaimed text-to-speech products available on iOS, Android, Mac, Chrome, and Web platforms. Our innovative approach has earned us prestigious recognition, including Google's Chrome Extension of the Year award and Apple's Design award for inclusivity in 2025.

We are a diverse, globally distributed team of nearly 200 professionals, operating in a 100% remote setting with no physical office. Our team includes top-tier engineers, AI research scientists, and other experts from industry leaders like Amazon, Microsoft, and Google, graduates from leading PhD programs such as Stanford, and talent from high-growth startups like Stripe, Vercel, and Bolt, alongside many successful founders.

Due to the significant growth of our Android app, which has become the most-used text-to-speech application in the Play Store, we are seeking a **Senior Android Engineer**. This is a pivotal role for someone who thinks strategically, thrives in fast-paced environments, is passionate about making impactful product decisions, and excels at building delightful user experiences. We foster a flat organizational structure where individuals can become leaders by demonstrating exceptional technical skills and consistently delivering fast, high-quality results. A strong work ethic, solid communication skills, and an obsession with winning are paramount to our success. Our interview process is designed to be efficient, typically concluding within one week.

Joining Speechify means contributing to a life-changing product that directly impacts and supports millions, including individuals with learning differences such as dyslexia, ADD, low vision, concussions, and autism. You'll operate within one of the fastest-growing sectors of tech – the intersection of artificial intelligence and audio – enjoying a fast-growing environment, an entrepreneurial mindset, hands-off management, competitive salaries, and a commitment to building a great asynchronous culture.

### Responsibilities
As a Senior Android Engineer, you will:
*   Take full ownership of major features, collaborating closely with our design team to deliver high-quality implementations within the app.
*   Actively shape the future strategic direction and technical roadmap of our Android team.
*   Own, maintain, and continuously improve reliability metrics for key product features.
*   Engage in cross-functional discussions with Product, Design, and other Engineering teams to drive initiatives.
*   Conduct thorough code reviews and provide support and mentorship to teammates.
*   Proactively handle critical issues and adapt to unexpected technical challenges.
*   Manage the release cycle for features, including providing nightly builds for the QA team.

### Qualifications
We are looking for a candidate who possesses:
*   5+ years of professional software engineering experience.
*   Solid familiarity with core Android components and architecture.
*   Proven experience building or significantly contributing to at least one shipped Android application.
*   Strong product design intuition and a deep sense of user empathy.
*   A drive to push the boundaries of Android UI/UX, creating innovative and engaging interfaces.
*   A clear understanding of the importance of testing and effective strategies for writing robust tests.
*   Self-motivation to continuously improve the app and codebase, often exceeding outlined specifications.
*   Rock-solid expertise in: Kotlin, Kotlin Coroutines, Kotlin Flow, Dagger 2, MVVM, Clean Architecture, Background Services, Music Player Service, Android Animations, Jetpack Navigation, and JUnit tests.
*   Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal.
*   A user-oriented approach to problem-solving.
*   A proactive attitude towards receiving and acting upon continuous feedback.

**Bonus Points:**
*   Experience building, maintaining, or contributing to open-source Android projects.
*   Experience with iOS, Web development, or NodeJS.

### Author's Take

**Q1: How does a Senior Android Engineer truly impact the product and user experience at Speechify, especially given the "flat organization" and "hands-off management"?**

From an insider's perspective, this role is far from just executing tasks; it's about true ownership and direct influence. Because we're a flat organization with hands-off management, you're expected to be a leader through your technical skills and results. You won't just be handed specs; you'll be shaping them, collaborating directly with design and product teams from conception to launch. Imagine owning a major new feature, seeing it through development, making key architectural decisions, and then witnessing millions of users benefit from your work within weeks. Our products directly impact individuals with learning differences, so every decision you make has a tangible, positive effect on someone's daily life. This isn't just coding; it's about innovating and delivering an experience that genuinely changes how people read and learn, backed by the freedom to challenge norms and improve beyond the obvious.

**Q2: What's the company culture like for a fully distributed team, particularly regarding collaboration and professional growth?**

Our 100% distributed setup means we've built a culture around asynchronous communication, trust, and a shared passion for our mission. Collaboration is incredibly intentional – it's about clear communication, well-documented decisions, and leveraging tools to stay connected without the overhead of endless meetings. You'll find yourself working alongside brilliant minds from diverse backgrounds (ex-Amazon, Stripe, founders) who are all driven by impact. For growth, it's a meritocracy: show excellent technical skills and deliver results consistently, and you'll find pathways to leadership, whether that's leading complex projects, mentoring peers, or driving strategic initiatives. We're a fast-growing company at the intersection of AI and audio, so the learning curve is steep, and the opportunities for personal and professional development are immense. If you thrive on autonomy, impactful work, and being part of a team obsessed with winning and user delight, you'll find your home here.
