Description

**Full Time | Onsite**

  

**Summary of Duties:** This individual will be primarily responsible for the support of Portfolio Managers in matters such as investment research, trading, operational/administrative support functions and bank technology. The analyst will also be responsible for assisting with special projects as assigned

  

**Essential Functions:**

1.  Conduct due diligence on existing and prospective investments
2.  Collaborate with portfolio managers on research, trading and operational/admin support
3.  Create and maintain models in trading system
4.  Support model rebalancing activities
5.  Become a student of financial markets
6.  Active participant in equity and fixed income committee meetings
7.  Function as liaison with fund managers and schedule reviews as appropriate
8.  Incorporate AI to increase efficiency and scale within the Trust Department
9.  Become subject matter expert on bank technology systems
10.  Assume additional responsibilities as requested

Requirements

 **Required Skills/Experience:**

*   Undergraduate degree
*   2-5 years of experience
*   Strong analytic skills - detail oriented
*   Strong technology/software skills
*   Familiarity with AI utilization
*   Passion for learning/understanding investments and financial markets
*   Demonstrated commitment to professional development
