### Description

1KOMMA5° is rapidly building Germany's largest one-stop-shop for integrated home energy solutions, encompassing solar panels, heat pumps, electricity solutions, and charging infrastructure, all interconnected to create a seamless energy ecosystem. We are actively seeking a visionary Product Lead to join our dynamic tech team in Berlin, Munich, or Hamburg, and become a pivotal part of our mission to accelerate the global energy transition.

### Responsibilities

*   **Define the Global Utility Product Vision:** Set and steward the global product vision and strategy for 1KOMMA5°’s utility business — covering metering, tariffs, trading, and flexibility services. Drive the long-term roadmap for how we provide, manage, and scale energy across markets.
*   **Lead and Grow your Product Team & Stakeholders:** Manage and mentor a growing team of Product Managers responsible for key product domains within our utility stack. Foster a culture of ownership, collaboration, and innovation across commercial, technical, and operational areas.
*   **Standardize and Scale Core Systems and Processes:** Develop and implement global standards for systems, technical setups, and partner integrations. Ensure our retail energy products operate consistently and efficiently across markets — from tariff design to billing and data exchange.
*   **Drive Operational Excellence Across Energy Markets:** Oversee market operations processes including metering, trading, and market communication. Work cross-functionally with Engineering, Energy Operations, and Trading teams to embed operational excellence into product design.
*   **Monitor Performance and Deliver Business Impact:** Define and track KPIs across product performance, operational efficiency, customer outcomes, and profitability. Use data to inform strategic decisions and continuous improvements in the utility portfolio.
*   **Align Internal and External Stakeholders:** Collaborate with internal teams (Engineering, Commercial, Energy Operations) and external partners (utilities, DSOs, market operators) to ensure alignment, compliance, and shared success.
*   **Anticipate Market and Regulatory Developments:** Stay ahead of regulatory, commercial, and technological trends in energy retail and flexibility markets. Translate emerging opportunities and requirements into actionable product initiatives.
*   **Lead Commercial and Go-to-Market Success:** Partner with Sales, Marketing, and Market Leads to position and scale our energy products globally. Ensure our energy offerings deliver measurable value to customers and strengthen 1KOMMA5°’s market leadership.

### Qualifications

To excel in this role, you should possess a compelling combination of leadership, deep domain expertise, and strategic acumen:

*   **Leadership & Experience:**
    *   Minimum 5+ years of product, commercial or operational leadership in the energy sector (ideally energy retail/utility, tariffs, services) across multiple markets.
    *   Proven experience managing product managers or cross-functional teams and owning a complex product domain with both business and operational/technical dimensions.
    *   Experience building or scaling products in energy retail, tariffs, metering/trading integration or flexibility services.

*   **Domain & Market Expertise:**
    *   Deep understanding of energy retail business — tariffs, pricing models, metering (roll-out and operations), trading/settlement, market communications (Marktkommunikationsprozesse) — across multiple countries.
    *   Strong familiarity with regulatory frameworks and market operations in the energy sector.
    *   Exposure to flexibility services, distributed energy resources (DERs), and digital energy services is highly advantageous.

*   **Technical & Operational Acumen:**
    *   Comfortable engaging with technical teams on system architecture, data flows, integration of tools and processes (metering, billing, trading, partner platforms).
    *   Strong operational discipline, ability to translate operational/process complexity into scalable product models.

*   **Commercial & Strategic Mindset:**
    *   Excellent commercial awareness with strong analytical skills: able to define and interpret KPIs, build business cases, prioritize product investments, and measure ROI.
    *   Strategic thinker who can also roll up sleeves and deliver in a fast-paced, evolving environment.

*   **Leadership & Communication:**
    *   Excellent communication and interpersonal skills in English and German.
    *   Able to influence and align internal and external stakeholders.
    *   Proven ability to lead people, build strong teams, mentor product managers, and drive cross-functional collaboration.

*   **Personal Attributes:**
    *   Entrepreneurial, proactive, and comfortable operating in a high-growth, dynamic context.
    *   Passionate about the energy transition, renewable energy, and changing how energy is delivered and consumed globally.
