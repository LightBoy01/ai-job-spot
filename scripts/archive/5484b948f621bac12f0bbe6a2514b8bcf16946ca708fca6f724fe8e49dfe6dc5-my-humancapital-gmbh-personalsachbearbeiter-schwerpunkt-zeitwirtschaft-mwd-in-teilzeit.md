### Description

On behalf of our esteemed partner company in Munich, we are seeking an **HR Specialist with a focus on Time Management (m/f/d)** to join their team as soon as possible in a part-time capacity (30-hour week). This role offers a unique opportunity to contribute to a market-leading organization known for its crisis security and commitment to employee well-being. Located in the heart of Munich with excellent transport links, you'll be welcomed into a modern working environment equipped with the latest technology. The company places a strong emphasis on work-life balance, offering not only flexible hours but also home office options. Furthermore, they are dedicated to your professional growth, providing numerous training and development opportunities to help you advance your career.

### Responsibilities

*   As an HR Specialist focusing on Time Management, you will maintain attendances, absences, working time models, and quotas within the SAP system.
*   You will monitor vacation entitlements and ensure reliable and accurate payroll processing.
*   You will independently process sick leave and return-to-work notifications, ensuring complete documentation.
*   Furthermore, you will serve as the central point of contact for employees and managers regarding all SAP time management inquiries.
*   You will ensure daily compliance with labor law regulations and company agreements.

### Qualifications

*   You have successfully completed a commercial apprenticeship, for example, as an Industrial Clerk (m/f/d), Office Clerk (m/f/d), or Clerk for Office Management (m/f/d).
*   Ideally, you possess initial experience with SAP and with the legal framework of labor law, such as the Working Hours Act and company agreements.
*   Preferably, you have knowledge of the collective bargaining agreement for public service (TVöD).
*   You impress with a confident, friendly demeanor and work in a structured and independent manner.
*   Proficient German language skills, both written and spoken, complete your profile.
