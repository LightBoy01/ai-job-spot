### Description\nAre you ready to take charge of your career in sales, working independently, shaping your own success, and managing your time freely? SumUp is looking for a **Freelance Field Sales Consultant** who is self-motivated and passionate about empowering small businesses.\n\nSumUp supports small businesses with smart and user-friendly financial solutions, from card payments to comprehensive POS systems. Our mission is to empower entrepreneurs, allowing them to focus entirely on their core business.\n\nAs a Field Sales Consultant, you are your own boss, building relationships with local businesses and demonstrating how SumUp can simplify their operations. Your primary task involves the direct sale of our innovative solutions, whether mobile card terminals or extensive payment systems, giving you everything you need to succeed. This role offers **attractive, unlimited commissions** based purely on your performance. You'll also benefit from maximum flexibility, working when and where you want, with top products that have high market potential, and comprehensive training and sales support to ensure you're never alone.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Active New Customer Acquisition**: Identify and win local businesses for SumUp.\n*   **Personal Meetings and Consultation**: Present our products and demonstrate their benefits to potential clients.\n*   **Build Customer Relationships**: Secure long-term partnerships through excellent service and ongoing support.\n*   **Own Customer and Lead Management**: You have the autonomy to decide how you build and manage your business pipeline.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   A strong passion for sales and a high degree of self-motivation.\n*   First experience in sales or customer service is advantageous.\n*   Excellent communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.\n*   Very good German language skills are essential.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for an ambitious, self-starter looking to build a flexible and highly rewarding sales career. With 100% commission-based earnings and no upper limit, your income is directly tied to your drive and success. SumUp provides the product, training, and support, while you bring the entrepreneurial spirit and passion for helping small businesses. It's ideal for those who thrive on autonomy, enjoy building relationships, and are eager to leverage a strong product in a growing market.
