### Description\nJoin mammaly, Germany's leading Pet Supplement Startup and a certified B Corp, on our mission for healthier, happier dogs! Founded in 2020, we're rapidly growing, and Paid Social is a key engine for our success in new customer acquisition.\n\nWe're looking for a performance-driven Paid Social Marketing Manager (m/w/d) to elevate our campaigns to the next level. You'll combine creative power, analytical precision, and deep performance marketing know-how with the clear goal of sustainably optimizing and scaling Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).\n\nIf you are a data-driven professional who loves creative testing, is curious about new formats, and thrives on funnel optimization, this is your opportunity. We seek someone eager to take on responsibility, make autonomous decisions, and achieve great things with a strong, collaborative team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop, test, and optimize high-performing creatives (especially video & UGC), including ideation, briefing, storyboarding, and analysis of creative performance.\n- Independently scale and manage our Paid Social campaigns on Meta, with a primary focus on new customer acquisition for mammaly.\n- Continuously optimize the entire funnel, from ad click to landing page to checkout, based on data-driven learnings.\n- Collaborate closely with the Creative & Content Team, Direct Response Copy, and CRO to rapidly implement new creative concepts and funnel tests, and develop them iteratively.\n- Conduct competitive market and creative analyses, benchmarking, and trend research to always stay \"ahead of the curve.\"\n\n### Qualifications\n**Required:**\n- At least 3 years of hands-on experience in Paid Social Advertising (Meta).\n- Expertise in Direct Response Marketing for D2C subscriptions or e-commerce.\n- Very strong analytical skills and proficiency in handling performance data (e.g., CAC, ROAS, LTV).\n- Experience in conceptualizing and producing performance creatives (video & UGC); ability to both develop ideas and brief them clearly.\n- Ability to work efficiently with the Creative Team and designers.\n- Proactive, solution-oriented, and autonomous work style.\n- Team-oriented with clear, transparent communication.\n- Fluent in German and English.\n\n**Nice-to-Haves:**\n- Experience with Figma, Canva, or other creative tools.\n- Experience with automation processes (creative iterations, budgets, reporting).\n- Hands-on experience in Paid Social Advertising on TikTok.\n- Deep interest in current Paid Social trends, algorithm updates, and new ad formats.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at mammaly offers a fantastic opportunity for a skilled Paid Social Marketing Manager to make a significant impact on a rapidly growing, mission-driven B Corp. The company champions an incredibly flexible work environment, allowing 100% remote work (within Berlin/surroundings) and providing high-end equipment. You'll be empowered to drive growth through data-backed creative strategies and continuous optimization, working closely with a supportive, collaborative team. It's an ideal fit for someone passionate about pet wellness, data-driven marketing, and a culture that values both performance and purpose.
