**Job Description & Summary**
At PwC, we value every individual's role in shaping our future. We're looking for candidates who excel in their roles and embody our core behaviours of Trusted Leadership and Distinctive Outcomes. Trusted Leadership focuses on building trust, delivering sustained results, and showing courage and honesty. Distinctive Outcomes emphasize excellence, collaboration, and inclusiveness. We seek self-aware, collaborative individuals committed to continuous learning and delivering quality and integrity.
At PwC, our people in software and product innovation focus on developing cutting-edge software solutions and driving product innovation to meet the evolving needs of clients. These individuals combine technical experience with creative thinking to deliver innovative software products and solutions.
In technology delivery at PwC, you will focus on implementing and delivering innovative technology solutions to clients, enabling seamless integration and efficient project execution. You will manage the end-to-end delivery process and collaborate with cross-functional teams to drive successful technology implementations.
Responsibilities:
*   Innovative Solution Development: Apply your deep understanding of full stack development to create cutting-edge web and mobile applications that incorporate AI and data analytics, driving innovation in our Deals services.
*   Work collaboratively with the Deals client teams to understand strategic objectives and translate these into actionable development initiatives, ensuring alignment with PwC's broader goals.
*   Engage in the entire application lifecycle, focusing on coding, debugging, and providing robust, scalable solutions.
*   Keep up-to-date with the latest industry trends and technologies, particularly those related to AI, to continuously improve our development practices and solutions.
*   Provide technical leadership and mentorship to junior developers, promoting a culture of excellence and innovation.
*   Ensure the highest standards of security and privacy are maintained in all developed solutions, in line with PwC's commitment to client confidentiality and regulatory compliance.
Requirements:
*   A Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
*   At least 5+ years of hands-on experience in full stack development, with a portfolio that showcases web and mobile applications.
*   Proficiency in front-end technologies (e.g., HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Tanstack) and back-end languages/frameworks (Node.js, NestJS, experience with Python preferable).
*   Experience with AI and machine learning technologies, demonstrating the ability to incorporate these into practical applications.
*   Solid understanding of database technologies (SQL, NoSQL) and cloud platforms (Azure, Google Cloud).
*   Familiarity with Agile development methodologies and tools (Git, JIRA).
*   Excellent problem-solving abilities, strong communication skills, and a collaborative spirit, especially in working closely with the Deals Transformation team to align development projects with strategic objectives.
Joining the Deals Technology team offers a unique opportunity to be part of pioneering solutions at the forefront of technology and deals advisory services, shaping the future of deal-making at PwC.
