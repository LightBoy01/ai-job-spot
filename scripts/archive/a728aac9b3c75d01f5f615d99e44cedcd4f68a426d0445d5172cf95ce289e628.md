**About Tru Identity**

Tru Identity is using AI to make importing into the US simple. We help importers, marketplaces and logistics providers automate all the messy compliance work that slows trade down — saving time, money and endless frustration. We’ve already built a great product, have happy customers and are growing fast with government contracts secured. 

Tru Identity is reimagining how trade compliance works; starting with the $4 trillion worth of goods imported into the U.S. every year. Joining now means you’ll be part of a small, sharp team with huge impact. **About the position** 

We’re looking for a seasoned **Backend Engineer** to lead architecture and development of scalable, intelligent systems. In this role, you’ll design and build APIs and distributed systems in Python/Django, manage async workflows with Celery, and deploy production-grade services on AWS. You’ll also contribute to AI-powered features using tools like OpenAI, LangChain, and vector databases.

**Requirements** 

*   Lead backend architecture and development — Design and build scalable APIs, services, and async systems using Python, Django and Celery
*   Own infrastructure and deployment — Manage and optimize AWS environments (EC2, ECS, Lambda, RDS) and maintain reliable CI/CD pipelines
*   Develop intelligent, AI-powered features — Integrate tools like OpenAI, LangChain, and vector databases to deliver production-grade AI functionality
*   Develop async processing systems and distributed task queues
*   Integrate AI capabilities using OpenAI, LangChain, or vector databases
*   Lead and mentor junior engineers through code reviews and technical guidance
*   Build CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, Bitbucket, Jenkins)
*   Write clean, tested, and maintainable code
*   Participate in Agile ceremonies and help drive technical strategy

**Experience**  

*   5+ years of deep backend development experience
*   Eligible to obtain US government clearance (background check required)
*   Expert in Python/Django with focus on performance and scalability
*   Skilled in Celery and async task processing
*   Proficient in AWS infrastructure and cloud management
*   Experience designing and deploying AI features using LLMs, LangChain, and RAG pipelines
*   Hands-on expertise deploying, scaling and monitoring AI/ML models in production
*   Strong CI/CD experience (GitHub Actions, Bitbucket, Jenkins)
*   Familiar with observability tools (Sentry, Prometheus, etc.)
*   Knowledge of microservices and event-driven architectures
*   Experienced building systems and processes from scratch
*   Background in startup and fast-paced environments
*   Contributions to open source or technical writing

**Highly Preferred**
