### Description\nThis role is for an experienced Network & IT Security Engineer eager to drive technical success and innovation within a dynamic and collaborative environment. You will be responsible for ensuring stable network operations and strategically evolving our clients' IT infrastructures. Your expertise will be crucial in designing, implementing, and maintaining cutting-edge network and security solutions, working closely with a diverse team and acting as a key technical point of contact. The company fosters an innovative culture that prioritizes performance, collegial support, personal accountability, and continuous development. Employees benefit from a range of perks including 30 days of vacation, flexible working hours, mobile work options, opportunities for career advancement and training, and regular team events.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Building and maintaining customer network infrastructures, with a focus on HPE Aruba.\n*   Implementing, migrating, and maintaining firewall solutions (e.g., Sophos, Forcepoint).\n*   Setting up and connecting proxy services (HTTP/HTTPS, DNS, Mail, etc.).\n*   Designing, building, and maintaining WLAN infrastructures.\n*   Contributing to the development of new security options for clients.\n*   Providing technical support for project proposal creation and implementation planning.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   A completed IT apprenticeship or a degree in (Business) Informatics.\n*   Proven professional experience in Network & IT Security, ideally as a System Engineer or (technical) Consultant.\n*   In-depth knowledge of essential network and security technologies (Layer 2 & 3, Routing/Switching, [Next Generation] Firewalls, VPN/Remote Access, IDS/IPS) and application protocols.\n*   Good knowledge of Windows and ideally Linux operating systems, plus basic Active Directory (AD) and DNS skills.\n*   Very good command of German and good command of English, both written and spoken.\n*   Residency in or willingness to relocate to the Hamburg metropolitan region.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a dedicated Mid-Senior Network & IT Security professional to make a significant impact within a growing German IT services company. The role offers a blend of hands-on technical work and strategic client engagement, allowing for both technical depth and broader influence. The company clearly values its employees, offering a comprehensive benefits package that supports work-life balance (flexible hours, mobile work, \"abends zuhause\" guarantee for field staff) and professional growth (career and further education). The collaborative team environment and flat hierarchies suggest a culture where expertise is valued and individuals can truly own their projects. Strong German language skills are essential, reflecting the local client focus.
