### Description

**Start your career in sales – 100% commission-based & without income limit!**

Are you courageous enough to work independently, shape your own success, and manage your time freely? Then SumUp is the right place for you! SumUp empowers small businesses with smart, user-friendly financial solutions, from card payments to POS systems. Our mission is to strengthen entrepreneurs, allowing them to fully concentrate on their core business.

As a **freelance Field Sales Consultant**, you are your own boss, with the opportunity to earn attractive, unlimited commissions! You'll build relationships with local businesses and demonstrate how SumUp can simplify their operations. Your main task is the direct sales of our solutions – whether it's a mobile card terminal or comprehensive payment systems, you'll have everything you need to succeed.

**What awaits you:**

*   **100% performance-based compensation** – your income depends on your commitment, with no upper limit.
*   **Maximum flexibility** – work when and where you want.
*   **Top products with high market potential** – compelling solutions for small businesses.
*   **Training & sales support** – we won't leave you alone.

### Responsibilities

*   Actively acquire new customers – identify and win local businesses for SumUp.
*   Conduct personal meetings and consultations – present our products and demonstrate their benefits.
*   Build strong customer relationships – secure long-term partnerships through excellent service.
*   Manage your own customer base and leads – you decide how you build your business.

### Qualifications

*   Passion for sales and strong self-motivation.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer service is an advantage.
*   Excellent communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Very good German language skills.
