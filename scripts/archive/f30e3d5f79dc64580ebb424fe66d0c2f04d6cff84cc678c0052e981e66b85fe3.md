**This early careers full-time finance internship position with mergers and acquisitions will be available starting in June 2026**

**\*\*UnitedHealth Group is not able to offer visa sponsorship now or in the future for this position\*\***

As a M&A Analyst Intern, you’ll join a team responsible for executing highly strategic acquisitions and investments to support the enterprise’s overall mission. The role positions motivated students on dynamic, high-performing teams to develop critical thinking, sophisticated financial modeling, and extensive communication skills within an exciting and fast-paced work environment. As a Summer Analyst, you’ll learn basic financial forecasting and business valuation expertise and develop tactical transaction management skills by participating in live transactions, while also learning about UHG’s businesses and overall strategy.

**Primary Responsibilities:** 

*   Gaining exposure to building financial forecasts, including but not limited to, projecting a target’s future financial performance
*   Deploying different valuation methodologies (DCF, precedent transactions, trading comparables and LBO) and performing various return metric calculations (EPS accretion / dilution analysis and IRR)
*   Supporting deal teams in financial due diligence on potential acquisition opportunities
*   Researching publicly available information on different health care sectors, including health care technology, care delivery, pharmacy services, insurance and care services, among others
*   Assisting with the coordination of large, internal diligence teams comprised of operational and legal subject matter experts
*   Providing support for special projects (e.g., new industry research, strategic financial analyses and new business initiatives, among others)
*   Participating in informational sessions to increase knowledge about UHG’s businesses

You’ll be rewarded and recognized for your performance in an environment that will challenge you and give you clear direction on what it takes to succeed in your role as well as provide development for other roles you may be interested in. 

  

**Required Qualifications:** 

*   Must be actively enrolled in an accredited college / university during the duration of the internship (internships are not intended for graduating seniors) with an anticipated graduation date of Spring 2027
*   Basic knowledge of financial modeling and valuation techniques
*   Must be eligible to work in the U.S. without company sponsorship, now or in the future, for employment-based work authorization (F-1 students with practical training and candidates requiring H-1Bs, TNs, etc. will not be considered)

**Preferred Qualification:**

*   Minimum 3.5 cumulative GPA
