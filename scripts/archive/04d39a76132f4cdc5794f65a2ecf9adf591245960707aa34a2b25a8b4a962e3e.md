**Followmont Transport** is currently seeking highly motivated Drivers to join our growing **Mackay Pick up and Delivery team**. Operating a Heavy Rigid tautliner truck to complete multidrop pick ups and deliveries throughout Mackay, this is a great opportunity to join a supportive and passionate team.

**The Role:** 

*   Full Time - Permanent positions
    
*   Monday to Friday 
    
*   Work to deliver high levels of service to our local customers by completing general freight deliveries and pickups.
    
*   Forklift and Pallet Jack operation – loading and unloading general freight.
    
*   Adherence company policies, procedures and industry legislative obligations 
    

**About you:**

*   Committed to safety, teamwork and providing quality customer service.
    
*   Hold a current current HR Truck Licence
    
*   Forklift Licence + Experience (desirable)
    
*   Ability to pass a pre-employment medical, including a drug and alcohol screen
    
*   Willingness to complete Criminal History Check if successful
