**SAP Solution Architect: Presales and Delivery**

We have a growing SAP practice and we require experienced individuals to join our fast-growing team. We operate across different industry sectors bringing SAP expertise to our client engagements, working closely with our strategy, industry and technology colleagues to Win and Deliver ground-breaking SAP programs.

To support us growing our team, we are looking for a SAP Solution Architect who, will be responsible for owning and driving the delivery of the solution for SAP proposal to customers, participate in evaluations and assessments and also guide Delivery teams when required.

**Responsibilities:**

*   Help transform leading organizations and communities around the world by making solution recommendations considering scope and client requirements.
*   Design Technical and Solution Architecture.
*   Gather non-functional requirements, undertake project planning and management activities.
*   Be responsible for solution design, configuration, and customization of SAP products
*   Technical analysis and documentation of SAP systems.
*   Provide experienced advice on SAP functionality, organizational change and other advisory services.
*   Suggest solutions to customer based on best practice.
*   Provide guidance on test plans and end-user documentation.

**Requirements:**

·         10-12 years SAP experience

·         Hands on experience in any SAP Functional Module or Technical area in at least 6 implementations

·         Experience of S/4HANA Conversion

·         Understanding of SAP integrations within the SAP eco system as well as integrations with non SAP systems

·         Understanding of Automation, Cloud, IOT and AI and ability to assimilate the same in the solution

·         Bachelors or masters degree

·         Excellent Communication and Presentation skills
