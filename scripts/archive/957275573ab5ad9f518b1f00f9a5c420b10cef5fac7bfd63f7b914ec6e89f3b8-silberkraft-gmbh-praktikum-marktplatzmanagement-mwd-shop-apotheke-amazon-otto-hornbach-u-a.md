### Description\nSilberkraft is a German e-commerce company specializing in high-quality solutions for pest control, mold prevention, and household products. Our focus is on effective, responsibly formulated products sold through our own online shop and major marketplaces like Amazon, Shop-Apotheke, Otto, and Hornbach. We combine profound expertise with data-driven work and clear customer orientation. At Silberkraft, product development, performance marketing, marketplace optimization, and logistics converge, working quickly, pragmatically, and growth-oriented.\n\nThis is a mandatory 3-month remote internship, with an immediate start date.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Support in operational marketplace management on Amazon, Shop-Apotheke, Otto, Hornbach, and other platforms.\n- Maintain and optimize existing product listings (titles, bullet points, descriptions, images, search terms).\n- Implement SEO measures on the respective marketplaces.\n- Assist with PPC optimization: campaign setup, keyword research, monitoring, and reporting.\n- Conduct price and competitor analyses.\n- Regularly evaluate KPI dashboards (Conversion Rate, CTR, TACOS, Buybox share, etc.).\n- Contribute to launch strategies for new products.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Studies in Business Administration, Economics, Marketing, E-commerce, or comparable fields.\n- Analytical thinking and not afraid of numbers.\n- Basic understanding of online marketplaces and how they work.\n- Advantageous: First experiences with Amazon Seller Central, PPC tools, or feed management systems.\n- Structured working style and high self-motivation.\n- Very good German language skills, good English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an aspiring e-commerce professional to gain hands-on experience within a dynamic, fully remote German company. You'll get deep insights into marketplace operations, work closely with the founding and marketing teams, and take ownership of your projects. The flat hierarchies, modern tools, flexible work environment, and focus on practical learning make it an ideal setting for growth and skill development in the fast-paced world of e-commerce.
