**Our Story:**

At DECK 13 Interactive, we’re more than just a game development studio—we’re a diverse, passionate community of creators dedicated to crafting unforgettable gaming experiences. Since our founding in 2001, in the heart of Frankfurt, Germany, we’ve been pushing the boundaries of what’s possible in PC and console games. Now part of the PulluP Entertainment group, our team of around 100 talented professionals continues to shape the future of gaming.  
We’re the minds behind titles like _Lords of the Fallen_ , _The Surge_ series, and _Atlas Fallen_ , known for their rich worlds and immersive gameplay. But we believe the best games are still ahead of us, and that’s where you come in.

To continue our success story, we are now looking for you to join our team as our new **UI/UX Designer (f/m/x).**

**Your Quest:**

*   Drive design, tuning, implementation and polish of UI features which support the project’s game and creative directions
*   Coordinate interdepartmentally with artists, sound designers, programmers and animators to deliver high-quality fully integrated features within the defined deadlines
*   Develop and improve generic logics and systems to empower an efficient and agile design workflow
*   Support the development of gameplay features by providing expert insight and solutions to UX problematics
*   Demonstrate a high level of ownership and responsibility in your day-to-day work and across disciplines
*   Resourcefully navigate constraints and challenges by finding creative solutions to work around them

**Your Toolkit:**

*   Significant experience with visual scripting systems, like Unreal Engine Blueprints
*   Strong experience of Unreal Engine 5, particularly of UI-related tools and systems
*   2+ years of experience in the field of UI and/or UX Design
*   Deep knowledge of user experience in interactive application
*   Experience with standard industry software such as Photoshop and Illustrator
*   Analytical thinking with the capacity to quickly understand game design rules, mechanics, balancing concepts and techniques
*   Ability to understand and communicate technical issues from an aesthetic point of view
*   Team Player with strong interpersonal and communication skills with willingness to learn new techniques and share knowledge openly
*   Experience in the gaming industry
*   Full professional English proficiency

**Nice-to-Have Qualifications:**

*   Proficiency in C#, C++ or any other programming language in a strong plus
*   Good knowledge of 3rd person melee combat games is a strong plus
*   Experience driving the UX design of a project from pre-production to release is a strong plus
*   Working knowledge of Graphics Mathematics (Vectors, trigonometry, matrices, Boolean logic, etc) is a strong plus
*   Knowledge of rendering pipelines
*   Knowledge of a shader language, experience writing and maintaining shaders
*   Basic animation understanding
