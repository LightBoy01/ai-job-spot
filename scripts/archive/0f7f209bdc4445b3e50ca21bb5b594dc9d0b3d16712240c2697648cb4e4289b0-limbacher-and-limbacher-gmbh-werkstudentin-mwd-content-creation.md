### Description\
Are you ready to unleash your creativity? As a Working Student Content Creator (m/f/d) at Limbächer Reutlingen, you will become part of Germany's largest motorcycle center – and the engine behind powerful stories that inspire our community. With over 200 colleagues by your side, you'll immerse yourself in a world full of innovation, precision, and genuine motorcycle passion. If you have a knack for strong brands, modern storytelling, and visual trends, and love transforming emotions into content, then you've come to the right place. We are looking for someone who shares our values of courage, fairness, and team spirit and is eager to make a difference. Together, we continue to grow and shape the future of (e-)mobility – and you'll tell the stories that make it happen. Join our team and kickstart your creative career!\
\
### Responsibilities\
- Actively collaborate within the Marketing department.\
- Film/photograph motorcycles, e-bikes, and employees for content and promotional materials.\
- Edit and cut recorded files.\
- Plan, editorially develop, and create postings for content and promotional materials.\
- Provide general support to the Marketing team in their current activities.\
\
### Qualifications\
- Currently enrolled at a college or university, studying Marketing, Design, Communication, Photography, or a comparable field.\
- Possess a driver's license and a personal car.\
- Open-minded, eager to learn, committed, reliable, and a team player.\
- Strong interest in online marketing.\
- Creative, detail-oriented, and able to visually implement others' ideas.\
- Ideally, proficient with Adobe Creative Suite (especially Premiere Pro) and experienced in photography/videography.\
\
### Author's Take\
This is an excellent entry-level opportunity for a student passionate about motorcycles and e-mobility to gain hands-on experience in content creation and digital marketing. Working within Germany's largest motorcycle center, the successful candidate will play a crucial role in shaping the brand's narrative and engaging a broad community. The role offers a blend of creative freedom, technical skill development (especially with Adobe Creative Suite), and valuable teamwork experience within a dynamic, growth-oriented environment that values innovation and passion. The added benefits like flexible hours and potential for home office make it particularly attractive for students.
