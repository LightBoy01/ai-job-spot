**Job Description**

**What is the opportunity?**

Capital Markets Quantitative & Technology Services - Data AI and Research Technology (DART) team is hiring a hands-on Data & AI Platform Engineer to advance Data & AI capabilities that power Research Digitization, Banking and Global Market solutions.

**What will you do?**

*   Engineer advanced Data & AI capabilities to power Research Digitization, Banking and Global Market solutions
    
*   Evaluation and subsequent adoption of AI Observability & Monitoring solutions, Vector databases , inference engines and other technologies in AI ecosystem.
    
*   Deploy, manage, performance tune and scale containerized applications using Kubernetes. Architect clusters leveraging fit for purpose hardware for AI workloads
    
*   Provide subject matter expertise in distributed and parallel computing. Analyze ML and data processing workloads to identity latency contributors, inefficient compute utilization and provide remediation recommendations
    
*   Automate AI infrastructure provisioning
    
*   Create reports for AI infrastructure usage and cost reports
    
*   Collaborate with Enterprise Architecture, Data & AI governance teams to ensure full alignment of platform with enterprise data strategy, AI governance frameworks, security policies, compliance and architecture standards.
    
*   Present platform architecture artifacts to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
    

**What do you need to succeed?**

**Must Have**

*   Solid understanding of Hybrid Data & AI platforms with emphasis on security, access control compute/storage architecture, AI workloads, cost visibility, monitoring and DevOps enablement.
    
*   2+ Years experience with Databricks.
    
*   Hands on experience in Data Engineering and applied Machine Learning.
    
*   Deep understanding of database systems and distributed computing
    
*   Experience building, scaling, managing and monitoring ML pipelines
    
*   Strong Proficiency in Python, SQL, Kubernetes, ML Flow and  Spark
    
*   Proficient in CI/CD principles, version control and best practices for deploying AI workloads to production.
    
*   Experience with Terraform.
    

**Nice to Have**

*   Good understanding of Classic ML Principles and Generative AI
    
*   Working experience with LLMs, Agents
    
*   Degree in Computer Science or Engineering
