**Key Responsibilities**

*   Assist Principal Manager Nursing in planning of designated unit(s) to meet statutory requirement, international standards, hospital direction and department goals
*   Promote clear understanding of the mission, vision and values of the hospital in relation to patient and staff
*   Develop professional standards, operational guidelines, work processes and quality improvement programmes to ensure safe delivery of patient care and smooth operation of the unit(s)
*   Lead and supervise a nursing and support service team within the unit(s) and act as resource and referral agent on clinical expertise
*   Manage and respond to client needs proactively and conduct daily operation activities to enhance teamwork and safety culture within the unit (s)
*   Plan and implement quality improvement initiatives and to monitor infection control practices in the unit(s)
*   Monitor nursing appearance and professional conduct of staff of the assigned unit(s)
*   Perform departmental budget and managing resources, including manpower, space, facilities and consumable items
*   Maintain and promote continuous education and specialty development
*   Monitor environment which promotes safety, security and optimal health for both clients and staff of the assigned unit(s)
*   Shift duties required
*   Perform any other duties assigned by supervisor(s)

  

**Job Requirements**

*   Holder of a Diploma or Bachelor's Degree in Nursing, Master's degree or above in Nursing, Leadership, Management, Healthcare Studies or related disciplines is preferred
*   Holder of a valid Registered Nurse Practising Certificate issued by The Nursing Council of Hong Kong, post-registration specialty qualification is preferred
*   Holder of valid certificate of Basic Life Support (BLS), Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) and Paediatrics Advanced Life Support (PALS) is preferred
*   At least 8 years post-registration experience of which 5 years should be in specialty settings
*   Experience in supervising a nursing team or commissioning of healthcare facilities is preferred
*   Excellent people management and communication skills
*   Demonstrable personal commitment in providing safe and quality patient care services
*   Proficient in project management and quality improvement processes
*   Good command of written and spoken English and Chinese
*   Proficient in MS Office applications
