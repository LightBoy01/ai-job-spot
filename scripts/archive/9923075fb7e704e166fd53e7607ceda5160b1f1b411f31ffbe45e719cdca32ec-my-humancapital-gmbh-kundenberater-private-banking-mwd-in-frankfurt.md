### Description\nOn behalf of our partner company in Frankfurt, we are seeking a dedicated **Private Banking Client Advisor (m/f/d)** to join their team as soon as possible. This is a long-term opportunity within a permanent employment contract, offering a modern work environment in the heart of Frankfurt with the latest equipment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Support Senior Advisors in the holistic care and advice of demanding private clients and institutional investors.\n- Maintain existing client relationships in the high-net-worth private client segment and actively contribute to building and expanding long-term partnerships.\n- Participate in the acquisition of new clients, accompany sales activities, and support the strategic expansion of the assigned client base.\n- Conduct goal- and closing-oriented consulting discussions, identify client needs, and develop customized investment strategies considering risk profiles and regulatory requirements.\n- Take on tasks in various Private Banking projects and contribute your own ideas for process optimization and client retention.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Initial professional experience in a banking environment, ideally in Private Banking, Wealth Management, or asset management.\n- Proficiency in using MS Office, especially Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.\n- High service orientation, strong communication skills, and a confident demeanor in client contact.\n- Excellent German language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role offers a fantastic blend of support and independent contribution within a highly reputable private banking environment. The company truly values its employees, fostering an appreciative work culture characterized by a collaborative 'Du' culture and significant trust from leadership. With flexible working hours and partial home office options, work-life balance is clearly prioritized. Furthermore, the commitment to professional development is evident through numerous training and growth opportunities, making this an ideal position for someone looking to build a lasting career in private banking.
