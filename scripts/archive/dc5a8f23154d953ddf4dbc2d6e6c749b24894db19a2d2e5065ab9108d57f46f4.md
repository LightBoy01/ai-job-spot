**About the team + role**
-------------------------
We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world’s biggest financial problems. We’re looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn’t a place for complacency, it’s where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We’re a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.
The Customer Care team’s mission is to transform how Robinhood supports its customers by integrating AI technologies that scale empathetic, accurate, and timely support.
As a Staff Software Engineer, you’ll be at the forefront of AI innovation—designing, deploying, and scaling generative AI chatbot solutions that redefine customer support! Your work will directly influence how millions interact with our platform.
This role is based in our **Menlo Park, CA** office, with in-person attendance expected at least **3** days per week.
At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.
**What you’ll do**
------------------
*   Design and implement scalable generative AI solutions that elevate our customer care experience using advanced LLMs, natural language processing, and ML tools.
*   Collaborate with AI/ML engineers, PMs, and CX stakeholders to ensure seamless integration of chatbot features across the Robinhood ecosystem.
*   Experiment with and prototype new capabilities, driving improvements in response quality, reliability, and personalization.
*   Mentor other engineers and contribute to a culture of innovation, learning, and high-impact delivery.
*   Champion best practices around testing, monitoring, security, and compliance in all AI initiatives.
**What you bring**
------------------
*   10+ years of software engineering experience, with significant depth in AI/ML, NLP, and chatbot development.
*   A proven track record of working across product and engineering teams to ship impactful, user-facing solutions.
*   Experience mentoring and leading other engineers while fostering collaboration and technical growth.
*   Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to navigate complex technical challenges in a fast-paced environment.
*   Deep interest in generative AI advancements and their application to customer service technologies.
*   Bonus: familiarity with the financial services industry.
