As a Senior Account Manager, you will be responsible for managing Wolt's largest SMB merchants, driving the adoption of Wolt's products, and fostering their growth on the platform. This challenging role requires a strategic, analytical, and service-first mindset to thrive in a dynamic, competitive environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Wolt's Products:** Leverage business acumen and analytical abilities to increase the adoption of Wolt's products and services among restaurant partners in your portfolio.\n- **Strategic Partnerships:** Drive complex commercial negotiations to strengthen partnerships with key restaurant partners, ensuring financial targets are met.\n- **Portfolio Performance:** Continuously monitor your portfolio's performance, interpret complex data sets, and provide data-driven consultancy to your partners.\n- **Business Reviews:** Conduct periodic meetings, present business reviews, and lead discussions with a consultative approach.\n- **Collaboration:** Work closely with the Marketing team to plan and execute campaigns in conjunction with restaurant partners.\n- **Mentorship:** Act as a senior presence and source of knowledge within the team, contributing to the development of others through training and mentorship.\n- **Market Analysis:** Actively monitor the competitive landscape and provide feedback to influence Wolt's product improvements.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Fluency in German and good English skills.\n- 3–5 years of experience in Account Management, Sales, Category / Vendor Management, or
