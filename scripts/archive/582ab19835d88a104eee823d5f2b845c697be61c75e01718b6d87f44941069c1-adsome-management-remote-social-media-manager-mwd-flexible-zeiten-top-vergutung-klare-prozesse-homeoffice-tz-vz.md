### Description
Are you looking for a **Remote Social Media Management job** with **attractive compensation**, **flexible working hours**, and **clear processes**? Then SheX is the right place for you.

At **SheX**, you'll benefit from **unlimited income**, **punctual commission payments**, and **free training** for a smooth start. Join a **motivated, performance-oriented environment** where your **marketing know-how** drives the **Creator Economy**, achieves **sustainable results**, and is **directly rewarded**.

Our employees consistently rate us on **Kununu** as the **"best job"** and **"best employer"** they've ever had, reflected in our **Top Employer awards for 2024 and 2025** and the **Top Salary Seal in 2025**. This role offers **100% remote work** from anywhere, **flexible full-time or part-time hours**, **clear structures**, and **proven processes** for efficient work. You'll also receive **free online training**, **transparent contracts**, **short onboarding**, **anonymous work options**, **development opportunities**, and **24/7 support** within a **supportive team climate**.

### Responsibilities
*   **Trend Research:** Identify current viral trends and successful short-video formats that perfectly match our Creators.
*   **Personal Branding:** Develop or enhance an authentic, consistent, and recognizable brand identity.
*   **Content Briefings:** Create clear, structured scripts and guidelines to enable the Creator to implement content effectively.
*   **Posting & Account Management:** Publish content and ensure social media profiles are always up-to-date, visually appealing, and strategically maintained.
*   **Performance Analysis & Optimization:** Evaluate content and statistics, identify areas for improvement, and specifically adapt the strategy.

### Qualifications
*   Demonstrable experience in **Online Marketing**, **Social Media Management**, or **Influencer Marketing**.
*   A keen eye for **viral content** and successful format ideas.
*   **Results-oriented mindset** with a focus on measurable outcomes.

**Beneficial:**
*   Knowledge in **Copywriting**, **Paid Ads**, **Performance Marketing**, or **Media Buying**.
*   Basic knowledge of **video editing**.
