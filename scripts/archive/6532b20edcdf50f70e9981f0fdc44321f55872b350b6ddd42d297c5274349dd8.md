**EMEIA Indirect Tax Compliance - Manager**

**The company**

EY Global Delivery Services (GDS) is the hub of innovation, a technology powerhouse, a global talent center and, a driver of operational excellence within EY, propelling EY’s purpose of building a better working world.  With a robust network spread across 9 countries and 20 cities, GDS’s dynamic network of highly talented professionals leverage data, AI, and cutting-edge technology to enhance EY's wide array of services in assurance, consulting, tax, strategy, and transactions.  
Join us at EY GDS Hungary to shape your future with confidence.   
Our indirect tax compliance professionals support large multinational clients with their indirect tax obligations in EMEIA and globally. Dealing with the indirect tax compliance requirements is the heart of the operations but this goes hand in hand with VAT advisory and technology support which we provide or coordinate with our specialist teams. We apply a client centric approach meaning that our professionals are organized per client, and not per country or region, to maximize the knowledge of our client’s business.  
 

**The opportunity**

  
With our breadth of service offerings you have the opportunity to develop your career through a variety of experiences, mentoring and formal learning to ensure our employee value proposition – Whenever you join, however long you stay, the exceptional EY experience lasts a lifetime – is a reality.   
Join our fast growing Indirect Tax Compliance and Reporting team as a Manager in Hungary and you will have the opportunity to work on EY’s largest multi-country client engagements. We deploy strong multi-skilled teams working seamlessly across countries and service lines, with major hubs in UK, Ireland, Belgium, Netherlands, Germany, Poland, Romania and Hungary.   
 

**Your key responsibilities**

  
As a Manager, you will be working on a selection of clients where you are the “VAT controller” for the VAT/GST compliance outsourced to EY, including:

•    Act as central point of contact – liaise with your international clients and colleagues with respect to regional and global VAT and VAT compliance.

•    Monitoring, and giving feedback on, the quality of our clients’ indirect tax data (using AI and other technologies) and reports of your clients.

•    Reviewing and approving indirect tax returns, and considering ways to maximise cash for your clients.

•    Follow up on legislative changes and business changes in order to ensure that our client’s systems and/or processes are updated.

•    Identify issues and opportunities to continuously improve the VAT compliance processes and our client’s data.

•    Coordinate VAT compliance advisory, technology, controversy and audit support.

•    Coordinate with other VAT controllers and report to the client’s Process Owner at EY (Senior Manager or above).

•    Supervise, coach and lead team members.

•    Broaden the relationship network with existing clients and participate in commercial initiatives together with Senior Managers or Partners .

•    Proactively communicate and collaborate with other sub-service lines and identify and facilitate cross-selling opportunities .

•    Proposing improvement possibilities to raise effectiveness and efficiency of the work processes.

•    Potential short to medium term secondments to client sites based in your home country.

**In addition, you will get a chance to work on a broader strategic initiative such as:**

•    Process: design innovative compliance solutions, including AI and generative AI; regulatory monitoring, etc.

•    Technology: automation, AI, data analytics, dashboarding, etc.

•    People: wellbeing initiatives, training programs, mentoring and team development, recruitment, etc.

•    Go to market: positioning of new services and solutions, marketing of existing services, etc.

  
**Qualifications and experience**

•    Experience in data interrogation.

•    Basic understanding of other compliance activities such as statutory accounting and corporate tax compliance

•    Understanding of IT-systems and VAT/GST reporting solutions
