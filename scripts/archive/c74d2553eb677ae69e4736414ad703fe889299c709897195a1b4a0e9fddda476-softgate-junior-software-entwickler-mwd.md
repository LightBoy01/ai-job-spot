### Description
softgate, an established and stable company, may not boast all the flashy perks of some startups, but it offers something far more valuable: an average employee tenure exceeding 10 years. This speaks volumes about an environment where people enjoy working for the long haul, driven by conviction. We prioritize trust, appreciation, and genuine development opportunities over short-term promises. If you're seeking a long-term perspective within a down-to-earth, collaborative setting, you'll find your home with us.

Your work makes a tangible difference. As part of a small team of experienced colleagues, you will play a key role in further developing our Document Management System (DMS) as a strategic product. Our diverse client base ranges from small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) to globally renowned brands. We are committed to sustainably supporting the continuous education and development of our employees.

### Responsibilities
You will work within a team of developers focused on our digitalization and document management solutions. You will collaborate closely with experienced developers to implement changes and features in line with customer requirements and our product vision.

*   Continuously develop and modernize our DMS.
*   Work closely with experienced developers.
*   Gain insights into frontend and/or backend development, depending on your qualifications and our shared goals.
*   Participate in further training in relevant technologies.

### Qualifications
*   Completed degree in Computer Science or a comparable field – OR –
    Completed vocational training as an IT Specialist (Application Development) with initial professional experience.
*   Experience in software development with Java or C#.
*   Experience with HTML/CSS, React/Vue/Angular, MS DevOps, Git, Maven, MS SQL / Oracle / PostgreSQL is advantageous.
*   Experience with Windows Server, AD/LDAP, Linux, QMS according to ISO 9001, QA, Testing, Docker/Kubernetes, KeyCloak is advantageous.
*   You enjoy working independently and are a team player.
*   Very good German language skills (at least C1 level) and good English language skills.

### Author's Take
softgate presents an excellent opportunity for a developer looking for stability, genuine growth, and a collaborative environment. With an impressive average employee tenure, the company clearly values its people, offering a culture built on trust, appreciation, and open communication ("Du-Kultur"). They actively encourage employees to contribute ideas and shape the company's direction.

Work-life balance is a priority, supported by 30 days of annual leave, flexible working hours, and the option to work up to 60% remotely. Continuous professional development is strongly emphasized through targeted training and internal career opportunities. Beyond the core benefits, employees enjoy perks like corporate discounts, ergonomic workstations, a beverage flat rate, engaging team events, employee profit sharing, and options for parking or a leasing bike. This role is ideal for a proactive team player eager to make a long-term impact on a strategic product within a supportive and well-established German company.
