### Description
Kohnle & Partner is a distinctive tax law firm that truly sets the standard for top-tier working conditions. With state-of-the-art technology, digital processes, dynamic growth, leading experts, and premium offices, they don't just talk about ideal workplaces – they actively create them.

For over 45 years, their excellent working environment and dynamic team have been the foundation of their success, establishing them as one of the leading firms in the Baden region. They serve businesses and private individuals as competent partners, passionate problem-solvers, and innovative future-thinkers.

Join their team at one of their locations in Karlsruhe, Weingarten, Offenburg, or Karlsbad.

As a Tax Specialist (Steuerfachangestellter), your role extends far beyond merely juggling numbers. Through ongoing training and development ("Ausbildung"), the firm supports your personal and professional growth, enabling you to comprehensively and competently advise clients on complex tax and business management matters.

### Responsibilities
*   Preparation of small tax returns and diverse financial accounting tasks.
*   Assistance with the preparation of annual financial statements.
*   Reviewing tax assessments and corresponding with the tax authorities.
*   Conducting research activities.
*   Supporting the firm's digital transformation initiatives.
*   Engaging in additional areas that you enjoy and where your strengths lie.

### Qualifications
*   You are communicative and enjoy working with people.
*   You are someone who follows through on words with actions.
*   Openness to new ideas and changes.
*   You have high standards for the quality of your work.

**Desirable:**
*   Technical college entrance qualification (Fachhochschulreife) / general higher education entrance qualification (allgemeine Hochschulreife) or a secondary school leaving certificate (Realschulabschluss) with good grades.
*   Initial experience with MS-Office applications, especially Word and Excel.
