### Description\nSchum Unternehmensgruppe is a nationally and internationally active trading and service company based in Würzburg, and one of the leading non-food specialists for chain retail. With over 3000 employees and 145 years of experience, Schum also operates one of Germany's most successful and expansive retail concepts with 300 of its own EuroShops. We are looking for a full-time **IT Service Desk Specialist (w/m/d)** to join our team immediately.\n\nThis role offers a comprehensive onboarding process, flexible working hours, and targeted further training opportunities, ensuring a supportive environment for your professional growth within our family-run company. Additional benefits include an attractive, performance-based compensation, company pension, free coffee/tea/water, subsidized canteen, free parking, and bike leasing.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Independently process support requests via the ticketing system, analyze hardware, software, and network problems, and provide remote support.\n- Set up and configure workstations and end devices, as well as plan and execute rollouts.\n- Maintain technical documentation and the knowledge database for transparent processes and effective team knowledge exchange.\n- Conduct practical training and support employees in the use of new technologies.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Several years of experience in IT support and excellent familiarity with ITSM processes (ITIL an advantage).\n- A completed IT education (e.g., IT Specialist for System Integration) or a comparable qualification.\n- Strong teamwork and communication skills, coupled with a customer-oriented service mentality.\n- Ability to work in a structured manner and maintain composure and clarity in demanding situations.\n- Very good German language skills (written and spoken); good English skills are desirable.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a dedicated IT Service Desk Specialist to join a well-established, family-owned company with a strong commitment to employee development and work-life balance. You'll play a crucial role in maintaining the IT infrastructure for thousands of employees and hundreds of retail locations, making a tangible impact on daily operations. The comprehensive onboarding, continuous training, and supportive culture make this an ideal environment to grow your career in IT support.
