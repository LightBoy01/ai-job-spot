Join HubSpot’s DPG UX Team: Designing for Developers
----------------------------------------------------

The Developer Product Group builds the tools necessary for HubSpot’s growing customer base to get the most out of their HubSpot accounts. We own and deliver a best-in-class set of developer tools that unlock added power, data, features, and insights for HubSpot’s customers through custom-built apps, extensions, and integrations. Our tooling provides external developers with everything they need—from support to ramp up quickly to the tools necessary to build and test apps, integrations, workflows, and CMS customizations. We continuously partner with teams across HubSpot to unlock new and powerful ways to customize and extend the scaling HubSpot product.

What Sets DPG UX Apart?
-----------------------

*   **Craft & Impact:** You build enterprise-grade developer tools that empower HubSpot's developer ecosystem to create rich experiences for millions of users worldwide. With a builder's mindset and passion for exceptional developer experiences, you use design and systems thinking to prototype cohesive end-to-end workflows.
*   **AI & Data**: Our teams lead the charge in AI innovation, from launching game-changing connectors for ChatGPT and Claude to pioneering HubSpot's MCP Server that enables millions to work at the speed of AI. We leverage data to diagnose complex challenges while using design excellence to craft elegant solutions.
*   **End-to-End Ownership:** Shape a comprehensive platform serving both internal teams and external developers through UI extensions, OAuth flows, custom workflow actions, and cutting-edge agentic tools. Your ecosystem thinking spans the entire HubSpot universe, connecting diverse use cases across customers and products.
*   **Global Scale & Foundation:** Craft foundational systems for world-class SaaS that directly impacts hundreds of thousands of developers and users across the globe.
*   **Innovate What's Next:** Build the future of developer experiences and AI-enhanced workflows by providing vibe-coding experiences for developers and non-developers alike, plus next-generation MCP servers that redefine how HubSpot customers get work done.

What You’ll Do
--------------

*   **Design for Enterprise Developer Tooling:** Navigate the feature-rich and highly technical landscape of development platforms with confidence. Demonstrate foundational knowledge of developer workflows and toolchains—IDEs, APIs, CLI systems, CI/CD pipelines, and version control should be well understood and used to inform design decisions.
*   **Join a Builder’s Culture:** Embrace the philosophy that a prototype is worth a thousand meetings. Maintain a bias toward building, consistently using tools like Cursor, V0, and Lovable to create proof-of-concepts that demonstrate possibilities and accelerate stakeholder understanding.
*   **Lead with a Clear Vision**: Synthesize complex narratives into bite-sized strategic insights. Lead from the front with a proactive industry perspective, knowing how to activate partners and teams around blue-sky visions that push organizational boundaries and unlock new opportunities.

What You’ll Bring To HubSpot
----------------------------

*   **Proven Product Design Craft Expertise**: To stand out in the application process, share a [portfolio](https://product.hubspot.com/blog/portfolio-reviews-done-right) that showcases depth of craft, systems thinking, and measurable impact on users and the business, especially in complex, scalable, or platform environments.
*   **Collaborative Leadership**: A history of influencing outcomes across disciplines by building trust, driving alignment, and championing user-centric solutions.
*   **Systems and Scale Thinking**: Passion for connecting the dots across products and platforms, designing experiences that grow with our customers and business needs.
*   **Adaptability & Change Readiness**: Comfort with ambiguity and a proactive mindset in the face of change. You’re energized by new challenges and thrive in a dynamic, evolving environment.
*   **AI & Automation Curiosity**: Excitement to work with (and continue learning about) AI and automation—whether to elevate customer experiences or optimize how we design and deliver.
*   **Growth Mindset**: A genuine drive to constantly learn, share knowledge, and help yourself and others get better, every day.

We Welcome You—Wherever You Are in Your Journey
-----------------------------------------------

We know great designers come from many backgrounds. If you’re unsure if you “check all the boxes,” apply anyway. Impact, learning, and growth drive our team—help us make design stronger for everyone.

**About HubSpot**

HubSpot (NYSE: HUBS) is an AI-powered customer platform with all the software, integrations, and resources customers need to connect marketing, sales, and service. HubSpot's connected platform enables businesses to grow faster by focusing on what matters most: customers. 

At HubSpot, bold is our baseline. Our employees around the globe move fast, stay customer-obsessed, and win together.
