_**Purpose of Role**_

Responsible for leading the integration and optimization of creative technologies to support scalable, efficient, and AI-enabled content production. Works closely with Design, Copy, Project Management, and Emerging Technology teams to ensure Creative Services operates as a modern, technology-forward function and to define AI governance guidelines related to creative workstreams and tools. 

_**Key Responsibilities and Expectations**_

*   Leads the configuration, integration, and continuous improvement of creative technologies (e.g., Wrike, Adobe Creative Cloud, Runway, Canva).
*   Supports automation of production workflows, asset templates, briefs, and approvals.
*   Collaborates with IT, Emerging Tech, and external partners to support tool onboarding, training, and integration.
*   Monitors developments in creative technologies to ensure the team remains at the leading edge of awareness and adoption, building use cases to demonstrate the value of  expanding the technology stack.
*   Pilots and implements emerging AI and automation tools for design, copy, and video production workflows.
*   Establishes and maintains template and prompt libraries that support distributed content creation.
*   Analyzes usage data and gathers team feedback to inform tool enhancements and training priorities.
*   Ensures the scalability and sustainability of creative systems to support growth and distributed content development needs.
*   Contributes to the development of a Creative Technology Roadmap.
*   Serves as the lead for creative AI governance, collaborating with Legal & Compliance to establish standards including guidelines for ethical use, legal disclosures, and training protocols.
*   Performs other duties as assigned.

_**Reports To**_

*   VP, Channel Marketing

_**Qualifications - Experience/Skills/Competencies**_

*   Minimum 5 years in a creative operations, marketing technology, or digital production role.
*   Hands-on experience with creative tools and platforms such as Adobe Creative Cloud, Wrike, Figma, Canva, and generative AI technologies (e.g., Firefly, ChatGPT, Runway).
*   Strong understanding of automation workflows and creative tool integrations.
*   Proven ability to collaborate across Creative, Marketing, and IT/Emerging Tech teams.
*   Experience with configuring and optimizing project management platforms.
*   Familiarity with prompt engineering, template development, and distributed content enablement.
*   Excellent problem-solving, communication, and training skills.
*   Strong organizational and documentation habits to support scalable systems.
*   Passion for emerging technology, innovation, and continuous learning.
*   Experience contributing to or leading governance initiatives is a plus.

_**Qualifications - Education - Required**_

*   Bachelor's Degree

_**Qualifications - Education - Field(s)/Profession(s)**_

*   Design Technology, Digital Media, Marketing, Information Systems or related field.

**_Qualifications - Education - Preferred_**

*   Wrike or Adobe Creative Cloud certification
