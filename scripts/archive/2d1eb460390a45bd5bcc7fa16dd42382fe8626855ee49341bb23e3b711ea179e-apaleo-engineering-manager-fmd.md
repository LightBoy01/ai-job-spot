Bonus: experience with event-driven architectures (Kafka).\n- A pragmatic, ownership-driven mindset with a passion for healthy teams and long-term quality.\n- Passion and experience in AI-driven SDLC.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis Engineering Manager role at Apaleo offers a unique opportunity to lead and shape the future of hospitality technology within an AI-first environment.
