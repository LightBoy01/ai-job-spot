*   Investigate electronic application and systems problems, analyze causal mechanisms, recommend appropriate actions, and own problem resolution with documentation.
*   Apply and improve electronic application and systems development processes, including system specification, modeling, simulation, calibration, DVP&R, and troubleshooting.
*   Collaborate with customers, technical and product teams, manufacturing engineers, suppliers, and stakeholders to deliver recommendations leading to quality product decisions.
*   Make decisions in areas of system design, requirements, calibration, and testing that impact subsystem quality and performance.
*   Review work of less-experienced developers/testers to ensure robust, reusable, and efficient design.

*   Coordinate and direct work amongst technicians and student employees; mentor and transfer knowledge to junior engineers.
*   Deliver independent execution of established and emerging work processes while continuously developing technology or product knowledge.
*   Facilitate a 12-week applied data science learning experience for engineers and technical staff.
*   Coach participants in using tools like Databricks, Amazon Q, Palantir, and ChatGPT for agent-based automation.
*   Guide learners in both code-based and low/no-code workflows to increase accessibility and impact.
*   Support learners in using Python-based tools (e.g., Pandas, Scikit-learn) and low-code platforms (e.g., Power BI, Dataiku).
*   Integrate LLM use cases such as prompt-driven analytics, code generation, and auto-documentation.
*   Review learner projects and provide personalized feedback to strengthen data science maturity.
*   Maintain a supportive coaching environment and collaborate to improve curriculum and resources.
