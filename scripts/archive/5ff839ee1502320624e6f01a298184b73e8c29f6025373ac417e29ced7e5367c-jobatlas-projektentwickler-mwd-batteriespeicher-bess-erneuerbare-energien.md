### Description\nThis strong renewable energy company, specializing in solar and wind power across Germany, is seeking a **Project Developer (m/w/d) for Battery Storage (BESS)**. Based in Mainz, you will be responsible for the end-to-end project management of future battery storage projects, from site acquisition to turnkey handover. This role requires a commitment to the company's vision and willingness to travel regularly to project sites across Germany.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Identify, coordinate, and manage battery storage projects.\n- Accompany BESS projects from the initial idea to turnkey handover.\n- Conduct site analyses, secure land, and develop planning concepts.\n- Evaluate project risks and support approval procedures.\n- Regularly report on project status and collaborate closely with internal departments.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Relevant professional experience in a comparable position and with battery storage systems, for example, as a Project Developer in photovoltaics, solar, wind, or renewable energies.\n- Completed technical degree, for example, in electrical engineering, energy technology, renewable energies, mechanical engineering, or industrial engineering.\n- Experience with project management tools and MS Office.\n- Good understanding of business contexts.\n- Strong communication skills making you an effective negotiation partner.\n- Fluent German and good English language skills.\n- Willingness to travel nationwide to visit project sites.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an experienced Project Developer passionate about renewable energies to play a pivotal role in Germany's energy transition. The company offers a highly supportive environment with excellent benefits, including flexible working hours, remote work options, 30 days of holiday, and significant investment in professional development. It's a chance to take full ownership of impactful projects from start to finish within a modern and employee-focused culture.
