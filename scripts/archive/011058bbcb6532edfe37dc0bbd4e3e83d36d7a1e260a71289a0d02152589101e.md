### Description

At Fetch, we're building America’s Rewards App, a platform used by millions of people every month to earn rewards on brands they love. We empower consumers to live rewarded throughout their day, having delivered over $1 billion in rewards and earning more than 5 million five-star reviews. Backed by major investors and partnered with Fortune 500 companies, Fetch is reshaping how brands and consumers connect.

We are looking for a **Head of Core ML Products** to lead the innovation of our AI-driven systems. This role is central to decoding and enriching the billions of data points we ingest from receipts and other user behaviors. You will oversee the technologies that power nearly every aspect of the Fetch app, from personalized shopping recommendations to campaign measurement and user safety. You'll be joining at a critical inflection point as we leverage new technologies to redefine our products and workflows, ensuring our Core ML systems are highly accurate, performant, and integral to helping over 12.5 million users Live Rewarded.

### Responsibilities

*   Own the vision, strategy, and execution for our receipt scanning, product matching, catalog enrichment, and user trust/safety products, which power both consumer and B2B experiences.
*   Collaborate cross-functionally with our core ML and AI teams, including our Chief AI Officer, LLM Labs, Sales Operations, and Go-To-Market to deliver scalable, accurate, user-centric, and compliant solutions.
*   Work across the Product Org to ensure systems capturing receipt and behavioral intelligence are deeply embedded into consumer experiences and brand outcomes (e.g., targeting, measurement).
*   Build the intelligence and rules-engine that power anomalous behavior detection and user education, establishing brand trust within the Fetch ecosystem.
*   Lead and grow a high-performing team, providing guidance on product craft and technical partnership while directly owning strategic initiatives.
*   Use data, experimentation, and instincts to drive prioritization, define success metrics, and course-correct as needed.
*   Immerse yourself in the Fetch consumer and partner experiences to become an expert in your subject area.
*   Take complete ownership and responsibility for outcomes, and be ready to solve tough problems in a fast-paced environment.

### Qualifications

*   A strong track record of building and scaling ML/AI/LLM-driven, data-intensive systems, ideally involving product catalogs, taxonomy design, or computer vision.
*   7+ years of product management experience.
*   2+ years of experience managing PMs or cross-functional pods.
*   Adept at navigating highly technical conversations with engineering and ML leaders while keeping user outcomes at the center.
*   Skilled in experimentation and data interpretation, including hypothesis-driven development and continuous learning.
*   Confident in setting strategy, framing tradeoffs, and aligning stakeholders across product, engineering, analytics, ops, and executive teams.
*   Ability to thrive in ambiguity and move quickly, defining your own roadmap and making bold bets when necessary.
*   Comfort guiding teams through system migrations, technical debt paydown, or large-scale data infrastructure evolution.
*   Experience with consumer or retail data platforms, such as in ecommerce, loyalty, ads, or retail tech.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "The description mentions Fetch is at an 'inflection point' with new technologies. Could you elaborate on what this means for the Core ML Products team and what the biggest opportunity will be in the next 12-18 months?"**

That’s a great question because it gets to the heart of why this role is so exciting right now. For years, our ML focus was on flawlessly executing a core task: understanding what's on a receipt. We've become world-class at that. The 'inflection point' is our shift from just *understanding* data to *predicting* and *personalizing* with it. The opportunity for this leader isn't just to make our receipt scanning 1% more accurate; it's to use our massive dataset to fundamentally change the user experience. Imagine the app not just rewarding you for what you bought, but proactively helping you discover products you'll love or find offers that genuinely make your life easier. Your team will be the engine for that transformation, moving from a reactive data processing function to the predictive core of the entire Fetch ecosystem. You’ll have the C-suite's full support to experiment, build, and redefine what's possible.

**A candidate might also ask: "This role owns user trust and safety systems, which can seem at odds with a fast-paced, experimental culture. How does Fetch balance building robust safety nets with the need to innovate quickly?"**

It’s a balance we think about every day, and we don't see them as opposing forces. At Fetch, trust is the currency of our relationship with users. We believe that innovation without trust is unsustainable. So, instead of having a 'growth team' and a 'safety team' that are firewalled from each other, we build with a safety-first mindset from day one. For this role, it means you're not just the 'rules police.' You're a strategic partner in product development. When a team wants to launch a new feature, they come to you and your team not for a 'yes/no' but for a 'how.' How do we build this so it's resilient to bad actors? How do we communicate it so it's transparent to users? This approach allows us to move fast because we're not constantly putting out fires; we're building fire-resistant systems from the ground up, making trust a core feature, not an afterthought.
