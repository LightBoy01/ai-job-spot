### Description

Booking Holdings (NASDAQ: BKNG) is the world's leading provider of online travel and related services, with a mission to make it easier for everyone to experience the world. Our family of brands—including Booking.com, KAYAK, Priceline, Agoda, Rentalcars.com, and OpenTable—serves customers in over 220 countries.

As part of a major Finance Transformation, we are redesigning our global finance services to be more consistent, efficient, and technology-driven. We are seeking an RTR (Record-to-Report) Senior Manager based in our Finance Center of Excellence (COE) in Bangalore to lead this charge. This is not just an operational role; you will be at the heart of shaping the future of RTR, driving digital transformation, embedding global standards, and leading a high-performing team through a period of exciting change.

### Responsibilities

*   **Lead Transformation:** Partner with the GBS RTR Director at HQ to drive the Global Finance Transformation Agenda, embedding new ways of working, digital tools, and automation.
*   **Operational Oversight:** Oversee end-to-end RTR operations, ensuring the accuracy and timeliness of the financial close and supporting statutory and US audits.
*   **Process Transition:** Manage the seamless transition of new processes into the COE, ensuring smooth onboarding and successful stabilization.
*   **Champion Standardization:** Drive process harmonization, standardization, and automation to achieve efficiency gains and improve KPIs across global markets.
*   **Innovate with Technology:** Utilize technologies like SAP, Blackline, RPA, and AI/ML to re-imagine RTR delivery and create best-in-class solutions.
*   **Business Partnership:** Act as a key business partner to global stakeholders, providing insights, foresight, and governance that extends beyond transactional support.
*   **Team Development:** Coach and develop a team of people managers, fostering agility, resilience, and a transformation-focused mindset.
*   **Problem Resolution:** Serve as the final escalation point for complex accounting challenges, ensuring pragmatic and globally aligned resolutions.

### Qualifications

*   **Education:** A Bachelor’s degree in Finance/Accounting is required. A CPA/CA is preferred.
*   **Experience:**
    *   15+ years of progressive finance experience with strong domain expertise in Record-to-Report (RTR).
    *   Proven success leading finance transformation or other large-scale change management projects.
    *   Experience working within a shared services or Global Business Services (GBS) model for a multinational organization.
    *   Strong people management experience, including leading other managers and large, global teams.
    *   Solid grounding in US GAAP/IFRS, SOX compliance, and ERP systems (SAP and Blackline are mandatory; OneStream/Hyperion is a plus).
*   **Skills:**
    *   Exceptional program and project management capabilities, especially in transformation initiatives.
    *   Demonstrated ability to manage ambiguity and confidently lead teams through change.
    *   Excellent stakeholder management and influencing skills across various levels and cultures.
    *   A proactive problem-solver who is equally comfortable designing new solutions and running stable operations.

### Author's Take

**1. How does the company support this role in balancing the immediate pressures of a smooth financial close with the ambitious long-term goals of finance transformation?**

"That's a fantastic question because it gets right to the core of this role. Think of it less as a balancing act and more as an integration. Leadership here understands that transformation isn't a side project—it *is* the project. You're not just being asked to keep the lights on while also finding time to innovate; you're being empowered to redesign the entire electrical grid. You'll have a direct line to the GBS Director at HQ, ensuring your strategic initiatives have global visibility and support. We've structured the team so that your direct reports—the other managers—are empowered to own the day-to-day operational excellence, freeing you up to focus on the strategic architecture of our future. Your success isn't measured by just closing the books on time, but by how you fundamentally improve the *way* we close the books."

**2. What is the current team's appetite for change, and what are the key cultural levers this role can pull to foster a transformation-first mindset?**

"You'd be inheriting a team that is incredibly sharp and technically skilled. They've been a crucial part of our success so far, but they're hungry for a leader who can show them what's next. Their appetite for change is high, but they're looking for a clear vision and a roadmap they can believe in. That's where you come in. The biggest cultural lever you have is empowerment. This isn't about pushing a top-down mandate. It’s about bringing the team into the design process, giving them ownership over automation projects, and celebrating the 'smart failures' that lead to breakthroughs. Your role is to be the coach and strategist, turning their deep process knowledge into fuel for innovation. By connecting their work directly to the global impact on Booking Holdings, you'll shift the mindset from 'executing tasks' to 'building the future of finance.'"
