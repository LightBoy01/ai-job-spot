**Job Description:**
Role Overview Visa is seeking a seasoned and highly motivated Director, Technical Product Manager within VAS Product Development. VAS Product Development drives the diversification of our revenue with products that differentiate our network and deliver valuable solutions across other rails.
We are seeking a Director,Technical Product Manager who is a self-starter, ready to learn and experiment, and able to work both independently and as part of a team.
The ideal candidate will possess the technical expertise to navigate both business and technology requirements, be comfortable operating in ambiguous situations, and assist in prioritizing delivery in line with product strategy. They should also have the ability to catalyze change and enhance efficiency within the overall delivery process.
Essential Functions:
*   Lead the development and management of the well defined requirements, roadmap, feature sets, and success criteria for pilots, while handling any associated issues or risks.
*   Collaborate with internal teams and external partners to gather high-level requirements, convert them into feature specs, prioritize, and drive execution across development teams in different regions.
*   Lead and coordinate teams across multiple countries to ensure seamless execution.
*   Knowledge of AI/ML technologies as well as AI/ML product development is a must.
*   Advocate for internal and external customer needs within the engineering team, influencing solutions design and priorities.
*   Establish milestones in alignment with client needs to ensure timely product delivery.
*   Represent the product team in engineering meetings, providing clarification on features and requirements.
*   Develop high-level technical solutions for pilots, identifying any external dependencies and technology risks.
*   Collaborate with engineers on design artifacts and Quality Assurance test plans.
*   Conduct User Acceptance Testing, Bug bashes, and hackathons with key stakeholders.
*   Advise the product team on technical feasibility during the development lifecycle.
*   Provide updates to executive management, highlighting any technical issues.
*   Manage multiple concurrent projects, ensuring quality and timely delivery.
*   Demonstrate a strong passion for applying GenAI solutions proactively in undertaking technical tasks as needed to facilitate process improvements.
*   Keep abreast of industry trends and the competitive and regulatory landscape.
This is a hybrid position. Expectation of days in office will be confirmed by your hiring manager.
