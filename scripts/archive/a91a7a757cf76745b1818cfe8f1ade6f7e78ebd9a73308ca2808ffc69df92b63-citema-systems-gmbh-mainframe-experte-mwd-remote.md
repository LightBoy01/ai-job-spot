### Description\nAt citema, we actively shape the digital transformation, advising clients in Cyber Security, AI, and Blockchain, while also implementing system- and security-relevant software projects. We foster a professional, collegial environment with a \"Du-Kultur,\" valuing team spirit and open communication. As a successful and continuously growing company, we offer diverse opportunities in a dynamic field.\n\nFor our client in the insurance sector, we are seeking an experienced Mainframe Expert (m/f/d) to support the operations and z/OS application management of Mainframe systems in the life insurance domain. The department ensures stable operations and supports development environments for smooth and efficient system usage.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Ensure ongoing operation of Mainframe systems under z/OS.\n- Support and maintenance of development and test environments.\n- Setup and maintenance of compilers, tools, and development infrastructures.\n- Monitoring, control, and optimization of batch and TPS processes.\n- Planning and control of job chains with Tivoli Workload Scheduler.\n- Execution of error analyses, troubleshooting, and performance tuning.\n- Support for deployments, release preparations, and technical tests.\n- Collaboration with application development within an agile Release Train.\n- Contribution to the further development and automation of operational processes.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Several years of experience in the **Mainframe environment** (z/OS, DB2, CICS).\n- Experience in Mainframe Application Management or application operations.\n- Proficiency in **Batch processes**, job control (especially Tivoli Workload Scheduler), and performance tuning (TPS).\n- Knowledge in setting up and maintaining development environments.\n- Knowledge of COBOL and/or PL/1 is a plus.\n- Experience in application development is a plus.\n- Ideally, experience in agile environments (SAFe, Scrum).\n- Analytical, structured, and solution-oriented way of working.\n- Excellent spoken and written German is required for effective communication in the project environment and with our German-speaking clients.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis Senior Mainframe Expert role at citema offers a unique opportunity to contribute significantly to the stability and evolution of critical systems within the insurance sector. The company truly values a collaborative \"Du-Kultur\" and strong team spirit, promoting open communication and individual growth. Beyond the core responsibilities, you'll find ample development prospects, participate in shaping innovative future technologies, and benefit from a flexible, hybrid \"New Work\" model, ensuring a great work-life balance. It's a chance to apply deep technical expertise in a dynamic environment that bridges classic software with cutting-edge digital transformation initiatives like AI and Blockchain, all while working with challenges that truly make work enjoyable.
