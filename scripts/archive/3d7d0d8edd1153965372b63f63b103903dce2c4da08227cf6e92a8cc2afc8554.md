As a Tech Lead Manager (TLM), you will be in a Onsite role that combines senior technical leadership with people management. You will act as a "multiplier," balancing hands-on work with team leadership.  This will be to work on our AI Transform product, more details here: FloQast Transform  
  
**What You’ll Do**  
*   Own the architecture and execution of major systems.
*   Lead by example in coding, code reviews, and design.
*   Manage a small team of 3–6 engineers.
*   Balance hands-on work with team leadership (approximately 50/50).
*   Coach your team on engineering practices and technical growth.
  
  
**What You’ll Bring**  
*   8+ years of software engineering experience.
*   Proven track record of delivering complex system initiatives with minimal oversight.
*   Ability to improve the quality, performance, or scalability of a module or product you own.
*   Experience driving consistent improvement in code quality and technical decisions.
*   A history of fostering a strong mentorship culture in technical areas.
*   Clear influence in architecture across teams within a product.
  
  
**Nice To Haves/Other**  
*   Experience with LangGraph/LangChain or similar, vector databases, and the pros/cons of various LLMs
  
  

About FloQast:

FloQast is the leading Accounting Transformation Platform in accounting workflow automation created by actual former accountants for accountants. By streamlining and modernizing daily accounting tasks, FloQast helps teams collaborate more effectively and complete their work with greater efficiency and precision. This cloud-based, AI-powered software is trusted by over 3,000 accounting teams, including those at Snowflake, Twilio, Instacart, and The Golden State Warriors—and continues to grow. Our mission is to continuously elevate the accounting profession, enhancing both its practice and perception.
