### Description
**Join Roometric and help us digitize real estate to decarbonize cities!**

At Roometric, we're developing **TwinCloud**, an innovative digital tool that empowers housing companies to map and analyze their building stock using iPhones and advanced 3D scans. Our mission is clear: to enable smart renovations and drive large-scale decarbonization efforts across existing buildings.

We're proud to collaborate with **major industry players**, including Europe's largest housing provider and a significant public housing company in Berlin. They trust us because we swiftly combine deep technological expertise with practical, impactful execution.

As we scale rapidly, we're searching for a talented individual like you to join our team!

### Responsibilities
As a Flutter Engineer, you will:

*   Build and ship **production-ready features in Flutter** for both mobile and web platforms.
*   Drive features from initial idea to full implementation within a rapid **2-week cycle**.
*   Work hands-on within a **modern, lean codebase** that's approximately one year old.
*   Collaborate closely with our tight-knit team of 6 professionals.
*   Deliver high-quality work efficiently, **minimizing time spent in meetings**.
*   Maintain clear and effective communication in an **async-first, remote setup**.

### Qualifications
**Our Tech Stack:**

*   **Flutter** (your primary tool – requiring 2+ years of experience)
*   Native iOS (Swift) – a valuable "nice to have"
*   Azure Cloud, GitLab, VS Code / IntelliJ

**What we're looking for in you:**

*   Master's degree or higher.
*   Minimum of 2 years of full-time Flutter development experience in a professional company setting.
*   A pragmatic mindset, demonstrated by writing clean, stable code that solves real-world problems.
*   Proficiency in using AI tools like GitHub Copilot or ChatGPT as an integral part of your development workflow.
*   A strong sense of ownership and commitment to delivery – you complete what you start.
*   Comfortable and effective working within a small, high-output team environment.
*   Excellent and clear communication skills in English (required).
