Convolutional recurrent neural networks (ConvRNNs) emerge as the most effective encoders, both for tactile categorization and for producing representations that closely match activity in rodent somatosensory cortex, revealing a linear link between task performance and neural alignment. Notably, self-supervised contrastive ConvRNN models achieve neural fits comparable to supervised training, indicating that label-free learning can capture biologically relevant tactile representations.
