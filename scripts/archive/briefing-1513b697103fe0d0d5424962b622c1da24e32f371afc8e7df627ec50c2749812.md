### Summary\nTraditional 3D mesh representations, often relying on iso-surfaces, inherently compromise geometric fidelity due to their need for water-tightening or rendering optimization. This paper introduces \"Faithful Contouring,\" a novel sparse voxelized representation that overcomes these limitations. It directly supports high resolutions (2048+) for arbitrary meshes without requiring conversion to field functions or iso-surface extraction, achieving near-lossless fidelity by meticulously preserving sharpness and internal structures even for complex geometries. Beyond representation, the method incorporates a dual-mode autoencoder, enabling scalable and detail-preserving 3D shape reconstruction. Extensive experiments demonstrate that Faithful Contouring significantly outperforms existing methods, achieving distance errors at the $10^{-5}$ level for direct representation and dramatically improving Chamfer Distance (93% reduction) and F-score (35% improvement) for mesh reconstruction, solidifying its superior fidelity for 3D learning tasks.\n\n### Why It Matters\nThis research marks a significant leap forward in 3D representation, with profound implications across numerous AI-driven industries. For professionals in gaming, VR/AR, medical imaging, industrial design, and digital twins, the ability to achieve 'near-lossless fidelity' at unprecedented resolutions (2048+) fundamentally changes what's possible. Current 3D pipelines often involve tedious manual clean-up and optimization to compensate for fidelity loss in conversion or representation. Faithful Contouring eliminates these compromises by bypassing the problematic iso-surface extraction step, meaning designers and engineers can work with highly accurate, sharp geometric data from the outset. This not only streamlines workflows but also enables the creation of truly photorealistic and functionally precise digital assets.\n\nFurthermore, for AI and deep learning practitioners focused on 3D, this technology is a game-changer. More accurate and detailed input data directly translates to more robust and precise 3D object recognition, generation, and manipulation models. Imagine generative AI producing 3D assets that are production-ready without extensive post-processing, or medical AI systems reconstructing organs with sub-millimeter precision. The inclusion of a dual-mode autoencoder further amplifies its impact by providing a scalable solution for high-fidelity reconstruction, making it practical for large-scale datasets and complex learning tasks. This innovation moves us closer to a future where digital 3D environments and objects are indistinguishable from reality, paving the way for advancements in everything from autonomous robotics to synthetic data generation.
