### Description

We are seeking an experienced and innovative AMD/XILINX Field Application Engineer to join our dynamic technical team in Israel. This is a unique opportunity for an engineer with a background in R&D to engage directly with customers in the high-tech market, tackling challenging projects and driving the adoption of cutting-edge FPGA and SoC technologies.

### Responsibilities

*   Provide expert technical support and design guidance to customers utilizing AMD/Xilinx FPGAs, SoCs, and associated development tools.
*   Collaborate closely with the sales team to identify opportunities, drive design wins, and support customers throughout the entire lifecycle, from concept to production.
*   Deliver compelling technical presentations, workshops, and hands-on training sessions to showcase the power of AMD/Xilinx technologies.
*   Serve as the primary technical liaison between customers, Avnet Silica, and AMD/Xilinx, ensuring customer feedback is effectively communicated to shape future product development.
*   Stay current with AMD/Xilinx product roadmaps, Vivado/Versal tools, and emerging industry trends to guide customers toward the most innovative and effective solutions.

### Qualifications

*   A minimum of 5 years of experience as a development engineer, with hands-on experience working with programmable components (FPGAs).
*   A proactive attitude, high energy, and strong motivation, complemented by excellent interpersonal skills.
*   Proven ability to thrive in a collaborative team environment.
*   Proficiency in technical English (both written and spoken).
*   **Advantageous:** Experience in developing embedded computer systems.
*   **Advantageous:** Familiarity with development environments for implementing artificial intelligence-based systems.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "Beyond closing design wins, what is the real, long-term impact of a Field Application Engineer in this role?"**

That's a fantastic question because it gets to the heart of what makes this job so rewarding. You're not just a technical support resource; you are a strategic partner and the crucial bridge between our most innovative customers and the core AMD/Xilinx engineering teams. When you're on-site helping a customer solve a complex problem, the insights you gather—the tool limitations they hit, the features they wish they had—don't just disappear into a ticket queue. We actively channel that feedback to influence the next generation of silicon and software. You get to see your real-world experience directly shape the product roadmap. The real impact is becoming a trusted advisor whose expertise helps a customer build something revolutionary, knowing you played a key part in both their success and the evolution of our technology.

**Another great question would be: "Can you describe the dynamic between the FAE and the sales team? How is success truly measured?"**

Think of it as a true partnership. The sales team might open the door, but the FAE is the one who builds the house. Our sales team relies on you to establish technical credibility and to dream with the customer, architecting solutions that solve their biggest challenges. Success isn't just a number on a sales report. It's measured by the depth of the technical relationship you build. It's the 'aha' moment in a workshop when a customer's engineering team realizes what's possible. It's receiving an email months later about how your guidance on an architecture choice saved them from a major production issue. We celebrate the complex problems solved and the trust earned, because we know that's what leads to sustainable, long-term success for everyone.
