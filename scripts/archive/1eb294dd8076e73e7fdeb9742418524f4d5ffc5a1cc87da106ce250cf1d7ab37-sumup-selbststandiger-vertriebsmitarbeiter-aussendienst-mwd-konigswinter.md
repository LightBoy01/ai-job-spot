### Description

**Launch Your Sales Career – 100% Commission-Based & Unlimited Income!**

Are you brave enough to work independently, shape your own success, and manage your time freely? Then you've come to the right place! SumUp supports small businesses with smart and user-friendly financial solutions – from card payments to POS systems. Our goal is to empower entrepreneurs so they can fully concentrate on their business.

As a **freelance Field Sales Consultant**, you are your own boss and have the opportunity to earn **attractive, unlimited commissions**! You will build relationships with local businesses and show them how SumUp simplifies their operations. Your main task is the direct sales of our solutions – whether mobile card terminals or comprehensive payment systems, you will have everything you need to succeed.

**What Awaits You:**

*   **100% performance-based compensation** – your income depends on your effort, with no upper limit.
*   **Maximum flexibility** – work when and where you want.
*   **Top products with high market potential** – compelling solutions for small businesses.
*   **Training & Sales Support** – we won't leave you alone.

### Responsibilities

*   **Active new customer acquisition** – identify and win local businesses for SumUp.
*   **Personal meetings and consulting** – present our products and demonstrate their benefits.
*   **Build customer relationships** – secure long-term partnerships through excellent service.
*   **Own customer and lead management** – you decide how you build your business.

### Qualifications

*   Passion for sales and self-motivation.
*   First experience in sales or customer care is advantageous.
*   Strong communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Very good German language skills.
