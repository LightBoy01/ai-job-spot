**The Role**  

Adobe is seeking a UI Software Engineer to build the next generation of AI-driven consumption experiences for Adobe Analytics, Adobe's industry-leading analytics platform. The Adobe Analytics platform processes 30+ billion transactions daily and delivers reporting data to customers via a sophisticated data visualization interface.  

The UI Engineer will participate in all aspects of the software development lifecycle including the architecture, design, development, & maintenance of many products and tools. This position is based in our Lehi, UT offices within the Digital Experience Business Unit of Adobe. 

**What You'll Do** 

*   Participate in the product development process working multi-functionally with product management, user experience team, and our customers 
    

*   Support Adobe's shift toward more agile product development processes with rapid prototyping and iterative development 
    

*   Solve complex business and technical problems involved with enterprise-scale data sets 
    

*   Implement robust data visualizations for mobile and web-based user interfaces that delight our customers 
    

**What You Need to Succeed**  

*   Excellent interpersonal skills and communication skills 
    

*   Ability to show deep empathy and interest in customer problems and needs 
    

*   Proficiency with React frameworks 
    

*   Proven experience in web development languages like TypeScript, and JavaScript. 
    

*   Familiarity with integrating Data Visualizations in User Interfaces. 
    

*   Some experience in integrating UI with AI Services. 
    

*   Proficiency with git. 
    

*   B.S in Computer Science, or related field 
    

*   2 to 3 years professional software engineering experience
