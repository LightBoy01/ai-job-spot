### Description
AVUS GmbH, established in 1993, is a nationally recognized service provider specializing in medico-psychological diagnostics and an officially accredited assessment center for driving aptitude. Operating across 14 locations in Germany, we are currently seeking dedicated **Customer Service and Office Management Employees** (full-time and part-time, min. 30h/week) to join our branch in Düsseldorf immediately.

This role offers an engaging and varied activity within a modern, future-oriented service company. You will become an integral part of a dynamic, collegial, and interdisciplinary team that values a supportive work atmosphere, flat hierarchies, and efficient decision-making processes. We are looking for individuals who excel in customer interaction and administrative tasks, eager to contribute to a vital public service.

### Responsibilities
As a Customer Service and Office Management Employee, your core responsibilities will include:

*   Engaging in personal and telephone customer contact, serving as a competent and friendly point of contact.
*   Providing comprehensive informational and organizational support to our clients, including lawyers, therapists, and authorities, throughout and after their assessment appointments.
*   Efficiently handling general administrative duties to ensure smooth office operations.
*   Processing emails and other correspondence with professionalism and attention to detail.
*   Maintaining and updating customer master data with accuracy.
*   Taking responsibility for cash management and performing invoice auditing.

### Qualifications
To thrive in this role, we are looking for candidates who possess the following qualifications and attributes:

**Required:**
*   A completed vocational training as a specialist in office management or a comparable qualification.
*   Excellent German language skills, both written and spoken.
*   Proficiency in Microsoft communication systems and data processing programs, including Office and Outlook.

**Desired Skills & Experience:**
*   We warmly welcome career changers from medically related fields (e.g., MTA, PTA, nurse, medical assistant, psychologists) who are enthusiastic about new challenges.
*   A demonstrated ability to work independently, in a well-organized, and engaged manner.
*   Strong customer and service orientation, coupled with genuine enjoyment in interacting with people.
*   Comfort and enjoyment in telephoning, with a desire to support individuals in challenging life situations.
*   A keen interest in a stable and long-term collaboration with our company.

A: "That's a really important question that highlights the human aspect of our work. We understand that our clients often come to us during challenging times, seeking clarity on crucial matters like driving aptitude. To ensure our team is well-prepared, we provide thorough, personalized onboarding. It’s not just about learning processes; it's about developing empathy and understanding the unique circumstances our clients face. You'll be welcomed into a highly supportive, interdisciplinary team where collaboration is key. If you ever encounter a particularly challenging situation or just need to debrief, a colleague or team leader is always there to listen and offer guidance. We truly foster an environment where mutual support is paramount, ensuring that while you're helping others, you also feel completely supported yourself."

A: "Absolutely, and this is one of the most enriching aspects of working with us, particularly for those with a medical background or a natural curiosity. While your primary role is crucial customer service and office management, you're at the frontline of a fascinating and complex field.
