### Summary
Aaron Levie, CEO of Box, envisions a transformative future for enterprise software where existing SaaS platforms continue to manage core business workflows, while intelligent AI agents operate as an overlying layer, enhancing and automating these foundational processes. This perspective highlights a strategy of augmentation rather than wholesale replacement for established enterprise solutions.

### Why It Matters
This vision from a prominent SaaS CEO signals a crucial evolutionary phase for enterprise technology, deeply impacting AI professionals. It underscores a paradigm shift from AI merely *automating tasks* to AI *orchestrating complex workflows* atop existing infrastructure. For AI practitioners, this isn't just about building better models; it's about designing highly capable, context-aware agents that can seamlessly integrate, interpret, and interact with a diverse ecosystem of enterprise SaaS applications. The 'riding on top' metaphor implies a need for robust API strategies, sophisticated natural language understanding for interacting with UI elements, and a deep understanding of business logic embedded within SaaS platforms.

This future demands a focus on agentic system design, where AI entities possess autonomy, memory, and the ability to make decisions within defined parameters to achieve business objectives. This will necessitate advancements in areas like multi-agent collaboration, ethical AI governance, explainable AI for auditability within enterprise contexts, and robust security protocols for agents handling sensitive data. Furthermore, it opens up new avenues for innovation in how enterprise data is leveraged—agents will become key consumers and producers of insights, driving unparalleled efficiency and personalized experiences. Professionals in AI must pivot towards developing not just intelligent algorithms, but intelligent *entities* that can dynamically adapt and contribute value across the entire enterprise workflow, fundamentally reshaping the competitive landscape for both AI solution providers and traditional SaaS vendors.
