### Description\nTriviar is seeking a visionary **Head of Sales (m/w/d)** to take overall responsibility for the sales strategy, team development, and scalable growth of its unique experience platform across Germany. You will be instrumental in establishing Triviar as a leading brand for local experiences, from creative workshops like pottery to unique events like alpaca hikes, by building strong partnerships and driving expansion into new cities and markets. This role is crucial for defining and executing a data-driven sales strategy, optimizing processes, and leading a growing team to achieve sustainable growth and cement Triviar's position as a premier experience platform in the DACH region.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Strategic Sales Scaling:** Oversee the entire sales strategy, from identifying attractive providers to successful expansion into new cities and markets.\n- **New Partner Acquisition:** Excite providers about the platform and develop a scalable process covering the entire journey from initial contact to successful course launch.\n- **Strong Partner Relationship Building:** Strategically manage active providers, strengthen collaborations, and ensure their sustainable growth on Triviar.\n- **Data-Driven Decision Making:** Utilize CRM tools, analytics, and automations to make sales processes measurable, efficient, and scalable, continuously developing them further.\n- **Lead a High-Performing Team:** Build and grow a sales team, set clear goals, coach employees, and foster a culture focused on performance and collaboration.\n- **Shape Growth:** Collaborate closely with the management team to identify opportunities, influence strategic decisions, and actively shape Triviar's overall expansion strategy.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **Completed Degree:** E.g., in Business Administration, Management, or a comparable qualification.\n- **Several Years of Sales Experience:** Ideally within a startup or scale-up environment.\n- **Strategic Sales Competence:** Ability to recognize market opportunities, build scalable processes, and drive growth based on data.\n- **Strong Communication Skills:** Proficient in convincing others, building, and maintaining long-term relationships.\n- **Data-Driven Mindset:** Confident in using KPIs, CRM tools, and analytics to guide decisions.\n- **Leadership Qualities:** Experience in team leadership or a natural talent for motivating and developing people.\n- **Hands-on Mentality:** Works independently, solution-oriented, and actively contributes to shaping growth.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an ambitious Sales Leader to make a significant impact within a dynamic and rapidly growing startup. As Head of Sales, you will have the autonomy to build and scale the sales function from the ground up, shaping the strategy and leading a new team. Triviar offers a vibrant startup culture with flat hierarchies, flexible hybrid work options, and attractive benefits including company participation and generous discounts on their unique experiences. If you are passionate about building scalable sales structures, leading a team, and contributing to the strategic direction of an innovative platform, this role promises immense growth potential and direct influence on the company's success.
