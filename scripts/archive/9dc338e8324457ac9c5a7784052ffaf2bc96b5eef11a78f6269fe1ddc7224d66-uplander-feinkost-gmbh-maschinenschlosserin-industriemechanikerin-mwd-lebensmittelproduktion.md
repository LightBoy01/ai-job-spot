### Description\nJoin an owner-managed, technically modern manufacturer specializing in innovative \"Better-For-You\" bowls, spreads, and snacks. We serve a diverse clientele, from startups to major food retailers, covering the entire value chain from raw materials to final products. We blend technical innovation with a passion for healthy eating, individually tailoring solutions for every customer. As a key member of our technical team, you will ensure the seamless operation and optimization of our production facilities, directly contributing to our commitment to quality and efficiency. Our team looks forward to your application.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Performing maintenance, upkeep, and repair work on production facilities and filling lines.\n- Analyzing and troubleshooting technical malfunctions during ongoing operations.\n- Planning and implementing optimizations to increase plant availability.\n- Supporting the installation of new machines and modifications.\n- Documenting according to food law requirements (hygiene, occupational safety, quality assurance).\n- Close cooperation with production, engineering, and quality management.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed apprenticeship as a machine fitter, industrial mechanic, mechatronics technician, or similar.\n- Professional experience in the food, chemical, beverage, or pharmaceutical industry is an advantage.\n- Proficient handling of pneumatic and mechanical systems; basic electrical knowledge desirable.\n- Structured, independent way of working and a strong sense of responsibility.\n- Team player with solution-oriented communication skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a skilled Mechatronics Technician or Industrial Mechanic to join a forward-thinking, owner-managed company at the forefront of innovative \"Better-For-You\" food production. The role offers stability with a permanent contract, an attractive salary, a modern work environment with flat hierarchies, and genuine development paths. If you thrive in a dynamic setting where your work directly impacts product excellence and operational efficiency, and you possess a strong sense of responsibility and teamwork, this could be your next exciting career step.
