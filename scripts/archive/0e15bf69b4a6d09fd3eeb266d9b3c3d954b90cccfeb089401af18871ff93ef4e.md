### Description

Optum is a global Fortune 5 healthcare organization dedicated to helping millions of people live healthier lives through technology-driven care. As a Principal Data Analyst on our Emisar team, you will be at the forefront of this mission, developing advanced analytical products and solutions for our pharmaceutical partners. You will leverage your deep expertise to uncover key insights from complex data, directly contributing to improved health outcomes and advancing health equity on a global scale. This is an opportunity to make a tangible impact, supported by the immense resources, career development opportunities, and stability of one of the world's leading healthcare companies. This role offers a hybrid work model for individuals based in the Republic of Ireland, blending time between our Dublin office and a home-based office.

### Responsibilities

*   Lead the end-to-end delivery of robust, sustainable, and scalable data solution projects, collaborating closely with IT and business partners.
*   Perform and oversee analytics in line with organizational best practices, ensuring cross-functional alignment on methodologies.
*   Anticipate future business requirements and proactively design, develop, and implement solutions to meet them.
*   Champion and execute data quality initiatives to ensure the integrity and reliability of our insights.
*   Drive the development of new concepts, technologies, and products to meet emerging customer needs.
*   Influence senior management decisions that shape the business's strategic direction.
*   Effectively communicate complex ideas and drive recommendations by telling compelling stories with data.
*   Oversee Data Operations activities, including supporting Power BI reports and analysis.
*   Spearhead AI/ML initiatives to unlock new analytical capabilities.
*   Manage and resolve data queries and issues from pharmaceutical users within agreed-upon SLAs.
*   Lead and mentor other Data Analysts within the Emisar team, fostering their growth and development.

### Qualifications

**Required:**
*   Bachelor's Degree (BA, BS) in a relevant field such as IT, Computer Science, Data Analytics, Data Science, Engineering, Economics, or Business, or equivalent professional experience.
*   Proven experience in data and analytics, including roles in product development and/or product management.
*   Demonstrated experience leading or owning the delivery of complex solution projects with accountability for the work of other team members.
*   Technical proficiency in Power BI, Python, SQL, and MS Excel.
*   Solid experience with predictive modeling and advanced analytics techniques.
*   Strong data visualization skills using tools like Tableau or similar platforms.

**Preferred:**
*   Hands-on experience with AI and Machine Learning projects.
*   Experience working within an agile delivery model.
*   Familiarity with transactional data in industries such as Healthcare, Financial Services, Banking, or Telecommunications.
*   Excellent communication skills with the ability to navigate and influence stakeholders across a highly matrixed organization.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "The description mentions improving health outcomes. Could you share a specific example of how a Principal Analyst's work translates into a real-world impact on patient care?"**

That's really the core of what we do. For instance, last year, a Principal Analyst led a project analyzing anonymized prescribing patterns and demographic data across a specific region. The model they developed identified a previously unnoticed correlation between certain environmental factors and a lag in patients refilling critical medication. These insights weren't just an interesting report; they directly informed a targeted outreach program, run by our pharmaceutical partner, that provided at-risk individuals with personalized reminders and educational resources. We saw a measurable increase in medication adherence in that group within six months. As a Principal, you're not just crunching numbers; you're the one connecting the dots and creating the narrative that leads to interventions like that. You're the bridge between raw data and a healthier life for someone you'll never meet.

**A candidate might also ask: "As a Principal, how much autonomy is there to explore new technologies or analytical approaches, versus executing on a predefined roadmap?"**

That's a great question, and it gets to the heart of what this role is about. We see our Principals as our technical and strategic trailblazers. While we certainly have roadmaps and commitments to our partners, a significant part of your role is to challenge and evolve that roadmap. We expect you to be our 'eyes on the horizon.' If you believe a new ML library or a different cloud-native tool could solve a problem 10x more effectively, you're given the space and resources to build a proof-of-concept and a business case. There’s a direct line to leadership to pitch these ideas. It's not about being a cog in a machine; it’s about being the architect of the next-generation machine, guiding the team, and ensuring our analytical capabilities are not just current, but years ahead of the curve.
