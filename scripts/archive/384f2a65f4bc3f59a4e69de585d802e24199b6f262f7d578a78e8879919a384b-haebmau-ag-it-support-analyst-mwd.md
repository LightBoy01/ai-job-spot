### Description\nAre you passionate about IT and have you already gained your *initial professional experience* in IT support and administration? Do you want to contribute and grow within the dynamic environment of a *lifestyle communications agency*? Join our IT team and collaboratively manage the Digital Workplace for our colleagues in Munich and Berlin. You'll enjoy *significant decision-making freedom* and the necessary pragmatism. Beyond daily routine tasks, you'll contribute your ideas and suggestions, *actively shaping our IT landscape*!\n\nIf this sounds like you, we look forward to your application as an **IT-Support Analyst (m/f/d)**!\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Provide 1st to 3rd level IT service for end-users (remote and on-site).\n- Manage hardware and software (installation, configuration, and maintenance) for client support, as well as Mobile Device Management (deployment, administration, and support of mobile devices), particularly with Microsoft Intune and macOS systems like Mosyle or JAMF.\n- Install, troubleshoot, and manage users for Windows and macOS operating systems in a client environment.\n- Administer Microsoft 365 environments, including Exchange Online, Entra ID (Azure AD), and related services.\n- Perform network administration tasks such as IP configuration, printer connection, local network troubleshooting, and user/rights management with Active Directory.\n- Design and implement your own projects (changes, new introductions).\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed vocational training as an IT specialist for system integration or a comparable qualification, OR at least 2 years of relevant practical professional experience and/or further training.\n- You work reliably, independently, and solution-oriented.\n- You enjoy organizing your own workday, are flexible, and can prioritize effectively.\n- You are a true team player with a high degree of personal responsibility and strong communication skills.\n- Fluent in German and possess very good English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an IT professional with initial experience to truly make an impact in a vibrant agency setting. The role offers substantial autonomy, allowing you to not only provide comprehensive support but also to actively shape the company's IT infrastructure through your own projects. With a focus on continuous learning, flexible hybrid work, and a supportive, collaborative culture, this position is ideal for someone looking for significant growth and the freedom to innovate.
