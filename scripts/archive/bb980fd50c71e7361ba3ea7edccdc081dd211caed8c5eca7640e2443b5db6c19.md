**Role:**  Senior Machine Learning Engineer  
**Experience Level:**  3+ years  
**Employment type:**  Full Time  
**Location:** Remote (USA)

**Job Summary**  
We are seeking a highly skilled and innovative Machine Learning Engineer with a deep specialization in Generative AI and Agentic AI. This role is crucial for designing, developing, and deploying advanced AI solutions that leverage the power of large language models (LLMs) to create intelligent, autonomous agents capable of performing complex tasks, generating creative content, and driving significant business value.  
The ideal candidate will possess extensive hands-on experience across the entire Generative AI lifecycle, from advanced LLM fine-tuning technique to implementing sophisticated agentic workflows using frameworks like LangChain and LangGraph. You will be instrumental in translating cutting-edge research into robust, scalable, and impactful enterprise-grade solutions.

**Roles & Responsibilities:**  
 

**Generative AI & LLM Development**

*   Development, and optimization of enterprise-grade Generative AI solutions, focusing on leveraging LLMs for various applications.
*   Apply advanced techniques for LLM fine-tuning, including but not limited to PEFT (Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning), SFT (Supervised Fine-Tuning), QLoRA, and DPO (Direct Preference Optimization), to enhance model performance, efficiency, and domain specificity.
*   Implement sophisticated prompt engineering strategies (e.g., Chain of Thought, Few-Shot, Tree of Thoughts, Self-Refine, RAG) to maximize LLM reasoning, creativity, and alignment with complex business requirements.
*   Develop and integrate Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) systems to enhance LLM knowledge and reduce hallucinations.

  
**Agentic AI System Design & Implementation**

*   Develop and deploy robust and scalable Agentic AI systems capable of autonomous decision-making, task execution, and complex problem-solving.
*   Utilize and contribute to agentic frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, and AutoGen to build sophisticated multi-agent systems and orchestrate complex workflows.
*   Design and implement mechanisms for agent memory (short-term, long-term, episodic) and context management to enable more coherent and effective agent interactions.
*   Empower agents with advanced tool-use capabilities, integrating with external APIs, databases, and proprietary systems to extend their functionality.
*   Develop and optimize A2A (Agent-to-Agent) communication protocols and coordination strategies for efficient and reliable interaction between autonomous agents.

**Research & Innovation**

*   Actively research and integrate the latest advancements in Generative AI, LLMs, and Agentic AI, including:
*   Self-Correction & Self-Improvement: Design agents capable of identifying and rectifying their own errors, learning from past interactions, and continuously improving performance.
*   Emergent Behavior & Adaptability: Develop agents that exhibit emergent behaviors in complex environments and adapt to unforeseen circumstances.
*   Cognitive Architectures: Explore and implement advanced architectures for human-like reasoning, planning, and decision-making within agent systems.
*   Explainability & Interpretability (XAI): Develop methods to make agent reasoning and LLM outputs transparent and understandable.
*   Ethical AI & Alignment: Ensure the development of fair, unbiased, and responsible Generative and Agentic AI systems.

**Deployment, Scaling & MLOps**

*   Deploy Generative and Agentic AI models and systems at scale, optimizing for latency, throughput, robustness, and cost-efficiency on cloud platforms.
*   Implement and manage CI/CD pipelines for AI solutions, ensuring seamless integration, testing, and deployment.
*   Monitor model performance, agent behavior, and system health in production environments, implementing strategies for continuous improvement and retraining.

  
**Required Skills & Qualifications:**

*   3–5 years of hands-on experience in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence.
*   Proven track record in designing, developing, and deploying enterprise-grade Generative AI solutions.
*   Extensive experience with LLM fine-tuning techniques (e.g., PEFT, SFT, QLoRA, DPO) and prompt engineering for complex tasks.
*   Strong experience in building and deploying complex agentic workflows and multi-agent systems.

**Technical Skills:**

*   Expert proficiency in Python and relevant ML/DL frameworks (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch, JAX).
*   Deep practical experience with Generative AI models, including various LLM (e.g., Transformers, GPT, Llama).
*   Mandatory expertise with Agentic AI frameworks such as LangChain and LangGraph.
*   Experience with other agentic workflow tools like CrewAI, AutoGen, or similar.
*   Strong understanding of MLOps principles and practices for deploying and managing AI models in production.
*   Cloud expertise (GCP - Preferred) for model deployment, scaling, and infrastructure management.
*   Solid background in Natural Language Processing (NLP) tasks (e.g., text generation, summarization, question answering, semantic search).
*   Familiarity with reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, and decision-making algorithms.
