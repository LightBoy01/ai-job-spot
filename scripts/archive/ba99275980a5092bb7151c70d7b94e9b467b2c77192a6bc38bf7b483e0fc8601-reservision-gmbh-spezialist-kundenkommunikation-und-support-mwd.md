### Description\nWe are a young company revolutionizing the world of guest management tools. With **RESERViSiON**, many businesses in gastronomy and hospitality rely on a management system that impresses with its efficiency, user-friendliness, and noticeable relief in daily operations.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- As a Support Specialist (m/f/d), you accept incoming customer inquiries by phone and in writing, classify them, and process them systematically.\n- You keep customer needs in mind and strive for timely problem solutions; you independently reproduce errors and effectively prepare insoluble problems for further processing by IT.\n- You support our new customers when starting with our products and take over the training and setup of our **RESERViSiON world**.\n- Furthermore, you identify the needs of our existing customers to not only ensure the best service but also to prevent churn. For this, you conduct regular customer discussions.\n- As the first point of contact for our customers, you collect customer wishes and thus contribute to the further development of our products; furthermore, you work closely with your colleagues from e.g., sales, marketing, IT, and administration.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed commercial or information technology training or comparable.\n- You have initial experience in support or B2B customer consulting, preferably with reference to software applications.\n- You enjoy interacting with customers, are empathetic, and communicate professionally and target-group oriented.\n- You have an analytical mindset and a self-organized way of working.\n- You are characterized by a high affinity for IT and confident handling of common software applications.\n- Good English skills, with which you can also support international business customers, are an advantage.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity to join a young, innovative company revolutionizing guest management. The role offers a responsible and diverse set of tasks where you'll be a key interface between customers and product development. You'll benefit from a highly motivated team, flat hierarchies, and a culture that values flexible working hours and home office. This position is ideal for someone passionate about customer interaction, problem-solving, and contributing directly to product evolution in a supportive environment.
