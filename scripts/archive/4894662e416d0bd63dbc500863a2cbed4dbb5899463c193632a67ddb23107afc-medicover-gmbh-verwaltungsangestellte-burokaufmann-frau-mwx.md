### Description
Medicover Humangenetik, a leading provider of tailored diagnostics in human genetics, is seeking a dedicated Administrative Employee (Verwaltungsmitarbeiter:in) to join their team in Berlin-Lichtenberg. This full-time, permanent position offers an opportunity to contribute to vital healthcare services within a dynamic and future-oriented company.

Medicover operates accredited laboratories in Munich and Berlin, covering a wide range of diagnostic fields including human genetics, laboratory medicine, transfusion medicine, microbiology/virology, and pathology. With a multidisciplinary team of experienced specialists, they provide expert consultation and access to genetic counseling through various practices across Germany, including Berlin, Hannover, Augsburg, Kempten, Cologne, and Potsdam, as well as via telemedicine.

### Responsibilities
*   Review booking-relevant documents.
*   Act as the primary contact person for commercial matters.
*   Factually review invoices and release payments upon instruction from the commercial management.
*   Generate invoices related to services provided for health insurance companies.
*   Serve as the point of contact for personnel questions and act as the interface between employees and the decentralized HR department.
*   Provide support to the site management with administrative and organizational tasks.

### Qualifications
*   Completed commercial training (Kaufmännische Ausbildung) with at least 3 years of professional experience, preferably in the medical field.
*   Proficient computer skills, including 10-finger typing.
*   Demonstrated independent work ethic and strong comprehension skills.
*   A strong emphasis on teamwork.
*   Excellent command of the German language, both written and spoken.

*Even if you don't meet all criteria, we encourage your application!*
