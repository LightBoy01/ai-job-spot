### Description
As a strategic leader at FlixTrain, you will significantly shape the commercial development of our services. You will be responsible for the offer portfolio, expand our network, and ensure a consistent alignment between demand, capacity, and economic goals. Your team will create the foundational decision-making for growth and profitability, spanning from long-term expansion strategies to the annual timetable.

### Responsibilities
- **Offer and Demand Strategy:** Ensure reliable demand and revenue forecasts, develop models with your team, and base decisions on sound assumptions.
- **Strategic Network and Expansion Planning:** Develop target network scenarios, evaluate their economic viability, and derive recommendations for expansion, fleet strategy, and long-term investments.
- **Capacity and Offer Design:** Define commercial guidelines for the annual timetable, ensuring capacities, traffic intensities, and seasonal specifics are harmonized in close coordination with operational planning.
- **Revenue Planning & Monitoring:** Take charge of revenue planning, develop monitoring systems for early deviation detection, and ensure a seamless interface with Yield Management and Pricing.
- **Leadership & Team Building:** Build and develop a team of analysts and offer planners, fostering analytical strength and a learning-oriented approach.
- **Stakeholder Collaboration:** Work closely with operational offer planning, Yield/Pricing, Public Affairs, and Marketing to consistently align offers, demand, regulation, and customer segments.

### Qualifications
You bring an entrepreneurial mindset, strong analytical skills, and leadership experience. Additionally, you possess:
- Several years of experience in offer planning within rail transport or aviation.
- A distinct understanding of mobility behavior and mode choice.
- A passion for tackling complex challenges with a goal to continuously improve the company.
- Proven success in developing teams, managing diverse profiles, and fostering a culture built on clarity, learning, and responsibility.
- Knowledge of regulatory processes is helpful but not strictly required.
At Flix, we value a holistic perspective – even if you don't meet 100% of the requirements but believe you can make a significant impact, we encourage you to apply!
