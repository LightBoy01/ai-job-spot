### Description\
As a Customer Success Manager, you ensure that bank budgets transform into **real impact in the region** – helping local associations, initiatives, and projects receive the support they deserve faster. Your purpose: **empowering local communities** by ensuring donation, sponsorship, and foundation funds flow digitally, securely, and effectively.\
\
**mykori** is a software for funding processes. Regional banks, in particular, use us to relieve their Marketing & Communication teams, transparently manage funding, and smoothly guide applicants – without email chaos and with clean documentation. Our customers are teams across Germany that proactively foster their region.\
\
Your job with us goes beyond \"just onboarding\": You are responsible for the success of our banks **from signed contract to enthusiastic fan**, building a scalable CS area and thus leaving your unique footprint in the foundation of mykori.\
\
### Responsibilities\
**_Operational: End-to-End Responsibility for Customer Success_**\
*   **Lead Onboardings (remote & on-site):** You plan and moderate onboardings – initially with Tim, later independently. This includes preparation, configuration, team training, and the first steps after go-live.\
*   **Build & Maintain Relationships:** You are the constant point of contact for your banks: regular check-ins, sparring at eye level, expectation management, and actively asking questions before problems escalate.\
*   **Bring Feedback into the Product:** You translate customer experiences into clear requirements, prioritize with us, and are the voice of the customers in product and dev rounds.\
\
**_Structural: Developing a Scalable Foundation_**\
*   **Enablement & Documentation:** You create short how-tos, checklists, and possibly videos, so customers can increasingly manage things independently – and our support is relieved.\
*   **Systematize Customer Success:** You develop check-in formats, playbooks, health logics, and simple \"who does what when?\" structures that are scalable – instead of just solving everything ad-hoc.\
*   **Expansion & Growth from Existing Customers:** Together with you, we will further develop this field: You will first help us systematically identify and structure expansion potential (further use cases, more users, additional modules) – and gradually build a setup with us where upsell opportunities can be properly prepared and closed.\
\
### Qualifications\
*   Initial experience in **Customer Success**, Project Management, Consulting, or a comparable role – ideally in a B2B/SaaS environment.\
*   **Strong in dealing with customers:** You can engage people, speak plainly, and remain appreciative.\
*   **Structured & process-driven:** You think in workflows, love clear responsibilities, and have no problem with \"building things yourself first.\"\
*   **Basic technical understanding** (web applications, interfaces, data): You don't need to code, but you need to understand roughly how things interact – and be able to explain that to others.\
*   **Ownership-Mindset:** You don't just want to operate an existing machine, but design it with us – including the responsibility that comes with it.\
*   **Valid driver's license (Class B)** and willingness to travel for on-site onboardings.\
*   Negotiation-level German, fluent English is a plus.\
