Ericsson Inc. does not sponsor US work authorizations for this job position including H-1B, O-1, L-1 and TN. Ericsson also does not hire F-1’s working on EAD for this position.

**About this Opportunity:**   
We are seeking a highly organized and results-driven Project Manager to lead the execution of the Ethics and Compliance Self-Testing exercise across all Customer Units (CUs) within MACS, as well as managing the MACS OKR strategy execution. In this role, you will manage end-to-end planning, coordination, and delivery of self-testing activities, ensuring alignment with Ericsson’s Ethics & Compliance program. This position requires strong leadership, strategic project management skills, and a proactive approach to driving initiatives forward while collaborating across departments.

##### **What you will do:**

*   Develop and maintain detailed project plans and timelines for the Ethics and Compliance Self-Testing exercise.
*   Define scope, milestones, resource allocation, and risk mitigation strategies to ensure timely and effective completion of project objectives.
*   Facilitate communication across multiple teams and departments, ensuring alignment of goals, expectations, and deliverables.
*   Engage stakeholders to promote understanding, buy-in, and adoption of self-testing processes across MACS CUs.
*   Deliver actionable insights and updates to leadership in clear, concise formats.
*   Track project progress, adherence to timelines, and outcomes using structured monitoring frameworks.
*   Ensure timely completion of corrective actions, providing oversight and support until successful implementation.
*   Manage Responsible Business Governance deliverables related to quality, environment, occupational health and safety and information security.
*   Explore AI/Automation to support sustainability of Ethics and Compliance
*   Identify improvement opportunities and projects that enhance compliance and performance effectiveness.
*   Introduce workflow enhancements to streamline the testing exercise and support broader organizational goals.
*   Lead the development of MACS OKR framework.
*   Drive OKR Governance across the MA.
*   Own reporting of OKR progress on a monthly cadence.
*   Prepare comprehensive reports summarizing project outcomes and actionable recommendations for leadership.
*   Maintain detailed documentation to support decision-making and compliance reporting requirements.
*   Provide support to the PaT Business Performance team, contributing to broader project initiatives as bandwidth allows.

##### **The skills you bring:**

*   Bachelor’s or Mater's degree in Business Administration, Project Management, Compliance, or a related discipline.
*   Minimum of 5 years of experience managing cross-functional projects in a business environment.
*   Proven ability to lead initiatives involving diverse stakeholders and deliver complex project deliverables on time.
*   Proficiency in project management tools and reporting software is preferred.
*   Experience leveraging AI to solve complex business problems.
*   Experience managing projects within compliance or risk management frameworks, particularly in large organizations.
*   Ericsson knowledge: understanding of Ericsson organizational design, global stakeholders, and operating model.
*   Understanding of Security, Ericsson Health and Safety, Compliance, Sustainability targets and operating model.
*   Proven ability to manage timelines, resources, and deliverables for multiple concurrent projects.
*   Adept at structuring and maintaining project workflows to enhance efficiency and impact.
*   Ability to inspire and coordinate cross-functional teams to achieve shared objectives.
*   Project stakeholder management: ability to facilitate collaboration across multiple global and local organizations.
*   Skilled at presenting complex information in a clear, executive-ready format to varied audiences.
*   Strong ability to work across departments and foster a collaborative project environment.
*   Confidence in delivering insights with professionalism and clarity to senior leadership.
*   Demonstrated ability to identify gaps, propose pragmatic solutions, and drive iterative process improvement.
*   Ability to align project initiatives with organizational goals and compliance frameworks.
*   Ensure accuracy and precision in deliverables and progress tracking.
*   Fundamental understanding of Large Language Models and their applicability to current workflows.
*   Fundamental understanding of Agentic workflows in automating current processes.

Your Health  
Ericsson offers excellent health benefits including the choice of three medical plan options and a dental plan option that allow an employee to select the level of coverage that suits their needs. Employees will receive company credits in an amount equal to the cost that Ericsson pays toward the cost of their medical and dental premiums for themselves and eligible covered dependents.

Your Financial Security

We invest in both your short and long-term financial wellbeing. The Ericsson US 401(k) Plan offers an automatic 3% company contribution and Ericsson matches $1 for every $1 you put into the 401(k) Plan on the first 3% of your eligible pay, plus 50 cents on every $1 on the next 2% of eligible pay. When you contribute at least 5% of eligible pay, you are receiving Ericsson’s full matching contributions. Matching and company automatic contributions stop when your total eligible pay for the year reaches the IRS limits. Employees will also receive company credits in an amount equal to the cost of basic life insurance and basic accidental death and dismemberment coverage, as well as short-term and long-term disability coverage. Employees also have the option to participate in Ericsson’s Stock Purchase Plan.

Your Time  
Your work-life balance is important to us. New employees are provided a minimum of 15 days of accrued vacation, up to 3 personal days per year, 11 annual holidays, 8 hours of volunteer time, and 80 hours of sick time annually. Please note paid time off is pro-rated based on the employee’s start date. Furthermore, Ericsson provides up to 16 weeks of paid maternity leave and 6 weeks of parental or adoption leave at 100% of pay.

Additional Benefits  
Ericsson offers many other company-paid benefits such as financial wellness programs, educational assistance, matching gifts, and recognition programs.
