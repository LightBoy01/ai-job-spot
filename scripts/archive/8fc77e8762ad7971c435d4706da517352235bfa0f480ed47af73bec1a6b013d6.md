The ROLE

As part of the AMS People Ops team, the HR Business Partner will be responsible for designing and delivering HR strategies and solutions that directly support our Go-to-Market (GTM) functions.

Entrusted as a strategic partner to GTM leadership, this role will focus on stabilizing and scaling high-performing teams, aligning talent strategies to business priorities, and ensuring seamless integration with Delivery through close collaboration with Delivery HRBPs.

The HRBP will serve as a trusted advisor, ensuring our GTM organization has the talent, structure, and engagement needed to meet ambitious growth targets.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

**Strategic Business Partnership**

*   Serve as a trusted advisor to GTM leadership in developing and executing people strategies that enable business growth.
*   Partner with Delivery HRBPs to align workforce planning and execution across Sales commitments and Services readiness, supporting smooth operations.
*   Support the success of the 2-in-a-box model by ensuring consistent HR partnership and alignment across GTM and Delivery.

**Employee Engagement & Performance**

*   Lead performance management processes for GTM, ensuring a focus on accountability, coaching, and development.
*   Champion employee engagement initiatives by executing surveys, facilitating feedback sessions, and leading action planning.
*   Provide leadership and guidance on recognition, rewards, and retention strategies to strengthen morale and minimize attrition.

**Talent Development & Workforce Planning**

*   Partner with GTM leaders to identify talent needs, assess talent strength, and create succession pipelines.
*   Collaborate with Talent Acquisition to ensure hiring strategies meet GTM growth goals and align with customer market needs.
*   Drive professional development initiatives and support leadership effectiveness across GTM teams.

**Employee Relations & Policy Guidance**

*   Advise GTM leaders and managers on employee relations matters, company policies, and compliance with U.S. labor laws.
*   Manage and resolve employee relations cases with sound judgment and discretion.
*   Identify and mitigate risks related to turnover, compliance, or organizational alignment.

**Collaboration & HR Leadership**

*   Contribute to the AMS People Ops team by sharing insights, best practices, and building scalable HR solutions.
*   Use data and analytics to generate insights on workforce trends, inform decisions, and track HR program effectiveness.
*   Stay current on HR trends, employment law, and best practices to deliver relevant, practical guidance.

WHAT IT TAKES

*   Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Business, or related field (Master’s preferred).
*   8+ years of progressive HR experience; 3+ years directly as an HR Business Partner.
*   Experience supporting GTM or customer-facing organizations (Sales, Marketing, Customer Success) required.
*   Strong knowledge of U.S. labor laws, HR policies, and compliance requirements.
*   Familiarity with HRIS systems (SuccessFactors a plus).
