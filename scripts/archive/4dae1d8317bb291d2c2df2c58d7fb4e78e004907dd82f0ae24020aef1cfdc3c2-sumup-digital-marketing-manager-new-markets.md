  That's a crucial point, and it's something we constantly navigate. Our core

  global provides the foundational tools and brand guidelines, and local teams
  provide the cultural intelligence and market data to refine and optimize,
  ensuring SumUp feels like it was built for *them*.

### Description\nSumUp empowers small businesses globally with powerful, easy-to-use financial solutions. This Digital Marketing Manager role sits within the Global Expansion team, focusing on defining and executing go-to-market and growth strategies for new and emerging markets like Australia, Mexico, and Canada. It's a hands-on, high-impact position for a strategic operator who thrives in ambiguity, building growth engines from scratch by acquiring, activating, and retaining early merchants through data-driven experiments and scalable playbooks.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Lead go-to-market growth across new and emerging markets, driving user acquisition and early activation.\n- Develop and own the full-funnel growth strategy, including budgeting, forecasting, and performance tracking across multiple channels.\n- Manage and optimize paid acquisition campaigns (Google, Meta, Programmatic, Affiliates) to drive measurable results with lean budgets.\n- Build and execute CRM and lifecycle flows to improve onboarding, engagement, and retention in the first year post-launch.\n- Partner with Product, Data, and Finance to design reporting, attribution, and performance dashboards, optimizing for payback and ROI.\n- Run rapid tests and experiments to validate product-market fit, identify growth loops, and localize positioning per geography.\n- Lead external partners and agencies while documenting playbooks and learnings for future market rollouts.\n- Collaborate with local and global teams to ensure smooth transitions from pilot phase to full-scale operations.\n\n### Qualifications\n- 5–8+ years of experience in growth and performance marketing, ideally within fintech, SaaS, or fast-paced digital businesses.\n- Hands-on and analytical, comfortable with campaign setup, funnel tracking, and tools like Excel/Google Sheets, GA4, Google Analytics, Firebase, and BI dashboards (Tableau, Looker, Power BI or similar).\n- Managed six-figure marketing budgets and demonstrated measurable impact on acquisition and retention metrics.\n- Combines strategic thinking with operational grit, able to build frameworks and execute fast in ambiguous environments.\n- A natural collaborator, skilled at influencing cross-functional stakeholders across Product, Partnerships, and Data.\n- Thrives in international settings, adapting quickly to new markets and cultural contexts.\n- Bonus: experience launching products in emerging markets or regulated industries (payments, banking, e-commerce).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a high-impact role for an experienced Digital Marketing professional passionate about launching and scaling products in new international markets. The opportunity to shape global expansion strategies for a company committed to empowering small businesses, combined with a generous benefits package and a focus on work-life balance (sabbatical, time off), makes this a truly compelling position for a strategic operator who thrives in fast-paced, ambiguous environments.
