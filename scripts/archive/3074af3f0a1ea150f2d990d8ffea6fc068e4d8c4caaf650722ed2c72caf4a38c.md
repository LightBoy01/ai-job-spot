**What you will do:**
The Role's Key Responsibilities and Tasks
As an eDOer, you will have clear objectives, great challenges and a clear overview of how your work contributes to the global company project and its customers. As a People Systems & Process Manager in the People Analytics & Projects team you will be in charge of:
System Administration & Configuration:
*   Serve as an administrator for our main HRIS, Cornerstone, and other HR systems.
*   Configure and maintain modules, troubleshoot issues, and ensure system integrity.
*   Conduct thorough QA and UAT for all system changes and updates.
*   Collaborate with vendors to resolve technical issues and stay informed on new software versions and features.
*   Create and maintain detailed documentation for system configurations, procedures, and troubleshooting.
Process Improvement & Project Management:
*   Proactively identify and analyze inefficiencies within People Team processes.
*   Design, develop, and implement solutions to improve workflows, reduce manual effort, and increase data accuracy.
*   Leverage AI technologies to enhance productivity and foster innovation across the team.
*   Lead and coordinate pilots and other projects from start to finish, managing stakeholders and timelines independently.
*   Drive the automation of HR processes, including potential implementation and management of AI applications.
*   Maintain data quality and integrity across all HR systems. Support the design, implementation, and maintenance of integrations between HR systems to ensure seamless integration with the tools.
