**Hydraulic Technician | Mechanical Fitter**

BTP has exciting opportunities for experienced, detail-oriented, and trade-qualified **Hydraulic Mechanical Fitters** to carry out stripping, assessing, rebuilding, and final testing of hydraulic cylinders in our spacious and safety-focussed Hazelmere workshop. 

**About The Role**

*   Australian recognized Automotive or Engineering trade qualification 
*   Demonstrated experience with Caterpillar and Komatsu component repairs mainly hydraulic cylinders. 
*   All valid and relevant qualifications, certificates, and licenses (RPLs) 
*   Ability to meet all site access requirements (including, but not limited to, Medical, DAS, NPC & valid Driver’s Licence) 

**About You**

*   You’re at the top of your game and like to be challenged.
*   Competent computer skills with experience using Caterpillar SIS preferred.
*   Passionate about quality of work ensuring functionality and reliability exceeds customer expectations.
*   Knowledge and application of Occupational Health and Safety in the workplace.
