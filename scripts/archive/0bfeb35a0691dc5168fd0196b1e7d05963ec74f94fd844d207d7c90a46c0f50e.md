### Description

This role is a pivotal client-facing position focused on ensuring the successful delivery and management of Oracle's cloud solutions. As the primary delivery contact, you will be responsible for overseeing the entire lifecycle of managed services for key accounts, from project execution and incident management to strategic governance and risk mitigation. You will leverage your deep functional expertise in Oracle's SaaS/PaaS product suite to act as a trusted advisor, guiding customers through complex challenges and ensuring they derive maximum value from their investment in Oracle technology.

### Responsibilities

- **Account Management & Governance:** Oversee account management and establish a robust delivery governance model with customers at both management and executive levels.
- **Project & Service Delivery:** Lead managed services projects across the Oracle product footprint (SaaS/PaaS), ensuring delivery aligns with contract terms, resource utilization is effective, and financial objectives are met.
- **Incident Management:** Serve as the primary point of contact for major incidents, managing customer expectations and communications across various Oracle business units through to resolution.
- **Technical & Functional Guidance:** Provide functional-level support, review solution design documents and Root Cause Analysis (RCA) reports, and leverage deep product knowledge to solve complex, non-routine problems.
- **Strategic Collaboration:** Work closely with sales, delivery teams, and customers to identify optimal solutions, manage contract renewals, and uncover new opportunities through periodic account planning and reviews.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Contribute to internal initiatives aimed at improving Oracle’s delivery processes and developing new tools and methodologies.
- **Team Mentorship:** Guide and mentor junior team members, helping them develop their skills and grow professionally within the organization.

### Qualifications

- **Career Level:** This is a senior individual contributor role (IC4).
- **Domain Expertise:** Strong functional understanding of finance processes (Order-to-Cash, Procure-to-Pay) and expertise in Oracle Fusion SaaS products, particularly in Customer Experience (CX), Supply Chain Management (SCM), or Human Capital Management (HCM). Experience with other ERP products is also valuable.
- **Customer Focus:** Proven experience in a customer-facing role with the ability to quickly assess critical situations, mobilize organizational resources, and manage expectations effectively under pressure.
- **Operational Excellence:** A mindset geared towards continuous improvement, utilizing methods, tools, and utilities to optimize operational processes and delivery.
- **Learning Agility:** A demonstrated ability to stay current with emerging business models, application solutions, product features, and technology trends to add daily value to customers.
- **Strategic Acumen:** A comprehensive understanding of a customer’s industry, organizational structure, and strategic objectives, with the ability to identify critical success factors and influence their technology roadmap.

### Author's Take

**1. Beyond the contract terms and SLAs, what does true success look like in this role, and how does it directly impact the customer's business?**

"That's a fantastic question because it gets to the heart of what we do. Hitting our contractual metrics is the baseline, the absolute minimum. Real success is when you've moved from being a 'vendor' to a 'strategic partner.' It’s the moment a client stops bringing you predefined problems and starts bringing you their business goals. For example, last quarter, we were helping a customer streamline their supply chain. By not just resolving tickets but truly understanding their operational bottlenecks, we proactively proposed a solution using a feature in their SCM suite they weren't even aware of. It ended up reducing their order fulfillment time by 15%. That's success. It's when you see your work directly translate into a tangible business outcome for the customer, making their operations smoother and their own customers happier. That's when you know you're making a real impact."

**2. The role involves being the primary point of contact for major incidents. Can you describe the team's culture and the support system in place when managing these high-stakes, stressful situations?**

"It's intense, there's no doubt about it, but you are never, ever alone. The culture here is incredibly collaborative, especially when the pressure is on. When a major incident kicks off, it's not about finding who to blame; it's about 'all hands on deck' to find the solution. As the primary contact, your job is to orchestrate, communicate, and keep the customer steady. Behind you, you have a direct line to our top-tier remote delivery engineers, product specialists, and even architects who will swarm the problem with you. I've been on calls where a senior director cleared their schedule to help us navigate an internal resource roadblock. There's a deep sense of shared ownership. We celebrate the wins together, and more importantly, we tackle the crises together. It forges a really strong bond within the team and gives you the confidence to lead the customer through anything."
