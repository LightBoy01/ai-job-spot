### Description
This position is located in Munich, Germany, operating under a hybrid model that requires at least 3 days per week in the office. Please submit your CV in English, as it is the corporate language.

Tulip, a market leader in frontline operations, empowers companies worldwide by equipping their workforce with connected apps. This enhances work quality, improves efficiency, and ensures complete operational traceability across all departments. Businesses of all sizes and industries rely on Tulip's cloud-native, no-code platform and its modular solutions to tackle some of the most urgent manufacturing challenges: securing processes, boosting productivity, capturing and analyzing real-time data, and driving continuous improvements.

A spin-off from MIT, Tulip is headquartered in Somerville, Massachusetts, with additional offices in Germany and Hungary. Focused on modular, human-centered solutions for industrial environments, Tulip is revolutionizing the MES market and has been recognized as a Global Innovator by the World Economic Forum. Tulip was also named one of the "Top Workplaces USA" by Energage, and one of Built In Boston's "Best Places to Work" and "Best Midsize Places to Work" for 2024.

Tulip is looking for a proactive and passionate software developer who thrives on evolving products and delighting users. You are a curious learner, adept at both independent and collaborative teamwork, with a product-centric mindset and strong empathy for end-users. You embrace challenges, enjoy exploring new technologies, navigate complex tech stacks comfortably, and possess a strong passion and curiosity for artificial intelligence. You excel in dynamic, fast-paced work environments.

### Responsibilities
*   Write and maintain clean, high-quality, and efficient code according to project specifications.
*   Conduct comprehensive testing and ensure smooth deployments of programs and systems.
*   Communicate technical information clearly and effectively within the team.
*   Collaborate closely with fellow developers to design and optimize algorithms and flowcharts.
*   Integrate various software components and external programs.
*   Proactively troubleshoot, debug, and optimize existing software solutions.
*   Analyze user feedback to drive product evolution.
*   Propose and implement strategic software improvements.
*   Create and maintain technical documentation for internal purposes.
*   Contribute to innovative products in the field of Conversational AI.

### Qualifications
**Required Skills:**
*   3-5+ years of experience in software development, ideally within a SaaS company.
*   Excellent proficiency in TypeScript is required.
*   Experience with API design and integration (RESTful Services).
*   Solid understanding of unit testing, integration testing, and debugging.
*   Ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a team.

**Desired Skills:**
*   Familiarity with our tech stack, including React, Redux, Node.js, Golang, Elixir, Electron, MongoDB, Postgres, and Docker.
*   Experience with machine tools and/or in the manufacturing industry.
*   Startup experience.
*   Understanding of database design and optimization.
*   Knowledge of CI/CD pipelines and automated deployment strategies.
*   Understanding of Prompt Engineering and LLM customization.
