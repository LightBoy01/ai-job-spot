**WHY CREAMY?**

Creamy Fabrics is not just about clothing. It's about attitude, expression and true empowerment.

We are more than just a label - we are a movement for diversity, visibility and responsibility. We create fashion that not only fits, but also takes position. And that starts with the people who work with us.

**YOUR IMPACT**

As a **Working Student in Influencer Research**, you’ll be part of our Social Team and lay the foundation for successful influencer collaborations.

You’ll help ensure that our acquisition and management teams work with the most relevant, inspiring and on-brand creators in the UK.

Your mission: to identify the right influencers across social media platforms – from TikTok to Instagram to YouTube – and contribute directly to how Creamy Fabrics grows and shines internationally.

  
  

*   Research and identify suitable UK-based influencers who align with our brand values.
    

*   Evaluate creator profiles based on audience, style and brand fit.
    
*   Maintain and update influencer data in our internal database with precision and structure.
    
*   Collaborate closely with our influencer acquisition team to provide high-quality creator lists.
    
*   Keep up to date with trends, platform developments and niche communities within the UK market.
    

**WHY YOU’RE A GREAT FIT**

*   You’re passionate about social media and love discovering new creators.
    
*   You work quickly, precisely and reliably – data quality matters to you.
    
*   You have a great eye for aesthetics, trends and authentic brand alignment.
    
*   You speak and write **fluent English** and understand the UK social media landscape.
    
*   You’re structured, love clear processes and feel comfortable working with digital tools.
