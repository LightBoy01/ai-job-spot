### Description
Speechify's core mission is to eliminate reading as a barrier to learning, impacting over 50 million users with its innovative text-to-speech products across various platforms (iOS, Android, Mac, Chrome, Web). Recognized by Google as Chrome Extension of the Year and by Apple for its inclusive design, Speechify operates with nearly 200 distributed team members globally, drawing talent from leading tech companies and academic programs.

This pivotal role as a Senior Android Engineer is critical to supporting our rapidly growing Android user base – currently the most used text-to-speech app in the Play Store – and driving new, exciting projects forward. We are seeking a strategic thinker who thrives in a fast-paced environment, is passionate about making product decisions, and excels at building delightful user experiences.

Speechify prides itself on a flat organizational structure, empowering individuals to emerge as leaders through technical excellence, consistent and rapid delivery, strong work ethic, solid communication, and an obsession with winning. Our interview process is designed to be efficient, aiming for completion within one week, and involves several technical discussions.

### Responsibilities
*   Own major features and collaborate closely with our design team, taking full responsibility for delivering high-quality features.
*   Influence and shape the future direction of our Android team and product.
*   Own, maintain, and improve reliability metrics for key features.
*   Actively participate in cross-functional discussions with Product, Design, and other Engineering teams.
*   Review pull requests and provide support to fellow teammates.
*   Address critical issues and effectively manage unexpected challenges.
*   Take ownership of feature releases, including providing nightly builds for the QA team.

### Qualifications
*   5+ years of software engineering experience.
*   Strong familiarity with core Android components.
*   Demonstrable experience building or significantly contributing to at least one Android application.
*   Solid product design intuition and deep user empathy.
*   A strong drive to push the boundaries of Android UI/UX.
*   A clear understanding of the importance of tests and effective testing methodologies.
*   Self-driven initiative to improve the app and codebase beyond explicit specifications.
*   Rock-solid experience with:
