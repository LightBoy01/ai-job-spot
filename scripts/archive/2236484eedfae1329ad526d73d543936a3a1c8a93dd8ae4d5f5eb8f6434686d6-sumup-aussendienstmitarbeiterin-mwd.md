### Description
"Sell with purpose. Inspire with technology."

At SumUp, we empower small businesses with modern solutions for card payments and point-of-sale systems – simple, efficient, and flexible. Our devices enable merchants to securely accept payments anywhere, whether in-store, on the go, or at a weekly market.

As a **Field Sales Representative (m/f/d)** at SumUp, you'll not only receive a fixed base salary but also the opportunity for an **unlimited, performance-based bonus structure**. Your success is in your hands and will be rewarded. You'll also enjoy **full flexibility in daily planning** and take full responsibility for your own sales territory.

### Responsibilities
*   Actively engage with local merchants in your sales territory.
*   Present modern payment products clearly and practically.
*   Confidently handle objections and find individual solutions.
*   Build and nurture your own customer portfolio – for long-term, personal relationships.
*   Collaborate with a dedicated, goal-oriented sales team.

### Qualifications
*   Motivation to be out in the market and achieve results.
*   Basic technical understanding and the ability to explain smart products simply.
*   Solution-oriented thinking, self-initiative, and high motivation.
*   Initial sales experience is a plus, but passion and willingness to learn are more important.

**What SumUp Offers You:**
*   Fixed salary plus uncapped commission – performance is directly rewarded.
*   Flexible daily planning: You decide how you organize your workday.
*   Comprehensive onboarding, training, and an individual further education budget.
*   Access to mental health support and an Employee Referral Program.
*   Employee discounts with over 700 partners, plus an Urban Sports Club membership.
