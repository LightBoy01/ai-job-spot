**Regulatory Junior Manager** 

Based in Budapest 

Purpose of the role
-------------------

Support the management of Wizz Air Group’s regulatory affairs and traffic rights portfolio. Contribute to the company’s expansion into new, regulation-sensitive markets by assisting in securing necessary operating permissions and maintaining compliance with aviation authorities. 

Responsibilities
----------------

*   Assist in coordinating efforts to obtain market access in new countries and jurisdictions under the guidance of senior team members 
*   Support the administration and tracking of the Group’s traffic rights, including applications for designations, extra-bilateral rights, and other regulatory approvals 
*   Manage the Foreign Airline Registration process for entering new countries 
*   Secure seasonal route permits and conduct revisions as needed 
*   Participate in initiatives to improve and streamline traffic rights processes, including automation and documentation enhancements 
*   Collaborate with Commercial teams to align regulatory activities with Wizz Air’s strategic directions 
*   Support the development and maintenance of professional relationships with aviation regulators and Civil Aviation Authorities across the network 

Experience
----------

1-3+ years overall working experience preferably in Commercial or Operational areas of an airline 

Hard skills
-----------

Good understanding of aviation regulatory landscape and traffic rights 

Education
---------

Bachelor’s or Master’s degree 

Language
--------

Fluent spoken and written English 

Skills and competencies
-----------------------

*   Strong analytical and problem-solving skills 
*   Good oral and written presentation skills 
*   Business mindset and ability to synthesize information into recommendations 
*   Teamwork and strong interpersonal skills 
*   Flexibility to travel as required away from Head Office 
*   Other Requirements: 
*   Ability to live and work in Budapest, Hungary
