_When joining PerkinElmer, you select an experienced and trusted leader in scientific solutions, with the support of a global service network and distribution centers, providing the right solution, at the right time, to meet critical customer needs.  With over an 80+ year legacy of advancing science and a mission of innovating for a healthier world, our dedicated team collaborates closely with commercial, government, academic and healthcare customers to deliver our broad portfolio of analytical solutions, and OneSource services._
**Job Title**
-------------
Manager IT - HR Functions
**Location(s)**
------------------
Pune
**Key Responsibilities:**
**Service Ownership & Operational Excellence**
*   Accountable for **stable, reliable, and secure operations** across all enterprise applications, ensuring adherence to **defined SLAs and KPIs**.
*   Implement robust **IT Service Management (ITSM)** processes for incident management, change control, and problem resolution.
*   Drive **proactive monitoring** and **continuous service improvements** for critical enterprise applications.
*   Own and manage **application lifecycle planning**, including upgrades, patches, compliance audits, and end-of-life transitions.
**Business Relationship Management**
*   Serve as **trusted partner** to senior leadership in IT & business functions using **solutions like Workday, Payroll, Time & Attendance, Concur etc** ensuring IT solutions align with functional needs and business strategies.
*   Facilitate **regular business reviews** to capture evolving requirements, share technology updates, and solicit feedback.
*   Foster a **collaborative and transparent partnership** between IT and business units to maximize business value from technology investments short term to long term.
**Innovation, Automation & Transformation**
*   Identify opportunities to **drive process automation, data transparency, and self-service capabilities** within the enterprise functions.
*   Champion the adoption of **emerging technologies** (AI/ML, RPA, advanced analytics) where they add measurable business value.
*   Evaluate and lead **cloud migrations, integration platform adoption (e.g., Boomi), and process reengineering** initiatives.
*   Lead efforts to **harmonize global processes** through technology standardization across regions.
**Vendor & Partner Management**
*   Manage relationships with **vendors** across the enterprise applications landscape, ensuring strong performance, innovation contributions.
*   Establish a structured **vendor governance framework**, including regular performance review, resourcing needs and joint innovation initiatives.
*   Stay abreast of **industry trends, technology advances, and best practices** to continuously evolve the application portfolio.
