  Honestly, the biggest hurdle is often the resistance to change and the

  are used to highly customized on-premise solutions. In this role, you're not
  just a technical implementer; you're a true partner. We spend a lot of time in
  "Fit-to-Standard" workshops, guiding clients through the SAP Best Practices,
  showing them the immense value of standardization, and demystifying the public
  cloud roadmap. It's incredibly rewarding when you see a client's 'aha!'
  moment, understanding how these modern processes genuinely transform their
  business for the better. We're empowered to design those compelling solutions,
  and the collaborative nature of our international teams means you always have
  a network to tap into for diverse perspectives.
