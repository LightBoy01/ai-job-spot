### Description\nSumUp empowers small businesses worldwide with intuitive payment solutions and digital tools for their daily operations. To expand our presence across German cities and regions, we are seeking dedicated freelance Field Sales Consultants. In this self-employed, commission-based role with no income limit and flexible hours, you will directly present and sell our products to local merchants. We offer a performance-oriented commission model with realistic earning potential, comprehensive training in products and sales strategies, and access to modern sales tools and demo equipment. You'll benefit from high self-responsibility, flexible scheduling, and opportunities to participate in local events, supported by regular exchange within a growing network of Field Consultants.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Actively engage with small and medium-sized businesses in your assigned territory (e.g., retail, gastronomy, mobile service providers).\n- Conduct on-site sales discussions directly in stores, on the street, or at local events.\n- Advise clients on suitable SumUp solutions and perform product demonstrations.\n- Support the onboarding of new customers and guide them through their first successful transaction.\n- Gather market and competitor information to continuously optimize our sales strategy.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Experience in sales, ideally with a focus on small businesses or in field sales.\n- Excellent communication and persuasion skills, coupled with a passion for direct customer interaction.\n- Independent, structured work ethic with a strong entrepreneurial mindset.\n- High degree of self-motivation and resilience, even when facing rejection or fluctuating daily conditions.\n- Existing local networks and good knowledge of the respective region are a significant advantage.\n- Must possess a self-employment trade license or be willing to become self-employed.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for highly self-motivated and entrepreneurial sales professionals to build a flexible and rewarding career. SumUp offers a truly independent role with uncapped commission potential, coupled with robust support through comprehensive training, modern tools, and a collaborative network of peers. If you thrive on direct customer engagement, enjoy empowering small businesses, and are prepared for the autonomy and challenges of a commission-based field role, this position provides a clear path for growth and significant impact. SumUp's encouraging \"Job Application Tip\" also highlights their commitment to nurturing talent, making it an attractive option for driven individuals ready to learn and develop.
