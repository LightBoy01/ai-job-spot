**About the Role:** Are you ready to unleash your storytelling superpowers? As a Senior PR Specialist at BCD Meetings & Events, you’ll be the driving force behind our public relations adventures. Develop exciting narratives, nurture media relationships, and amplify our unique voice in the industry! Join us in a dynamic, remote environment where you'll shape our PR strategy and help tell the incredible stories that set us apart.

**You're Good At:**

*   Leading media relations to showcase our brand and boost visibility.
*   Cultivating strong relationships with journalists and internal stakeholders.
*   Identifying and crafting compelling stories that resonate.
*   Pitching creative ideas and securing impactful media coverage.
*   Tracking PR success and evaluating media impact like a pro.

**You Might Also Have:**

*   5+ years of experience in PR, communications, or media relations.
*   Exceptional writing and storytelling abilities, with a keen eye for detail.
*   Proven experience juggling multiple projects and deadlines.
*   A proactive mindset for identifying opportunities and solving challenges.
*   A collaborative spirit that thrives in a remote, global team setting.
