**Job Description:**

We are searching for an **AI/ML Technical Lead** to drive project delivery excellence, manage stakeholder relationships, and champion innovative AI/ML initiatives.

**Key Responsibilities:**

*   AI/ML Technical Lead end-to-end delivery of software development projects, ensuring adherence to scope, timeline, budget, and quality standards Develop and maintain comprehensive project plans, risk registers, and delivery roadmaps
*   Coordinate cross-functional teams including developers, engineers, analysts, and subject matter experts
*   Oversee Scrum Master and ensure optimal implementation of Agile methodologies
*   Monitor project health metrics and proactively address potential issues or roadblocks
*   Provide strategic direction while Scrum Master handles daily ceremonies and team facilitation
*   Government Client Relationship Management to Serve as primary point of contact for government clients and stakeholders
*   Facilitate regular client communications, status updates, and milestone reviews Ensure compliance with government regulations, security requirements, and contracting standards
*   Manage client expectations and negotiate scope changes or timeline adjustments
*   Build and maintain strong working relationships across multiple levels of government organizations
*   Navigate complex government procurement and approval processes
*   Innovation Leadership - AI/ML Initiatives Identify opportunities to integrate AI/ML solutions into existing projects and workflows
*   Lead development of AI/ML proof of concepts and pilot programs
*   Collaborate with technical teams to evaluate emerging technologies and their practical applications
*   Present innovative solutions to government clients and secure buy-in for advanced technology initiatives
*   Stay current with AI/ML trends, government technology priorities, and industry best practices
*   Drive digital transformation initiatives within client organizations
*   Team Leadership & Development Mentor and develop project team members across technical and non-technical disciplines
*   Foster collaborative team environment focused on continuous improvement and innovation
*   Coordinate resource allocation and capacity planning across multiple concurrent projects
*   Conduct performance evaluations and provide professional development guidance Required

**Must have:**

*   Bachelor’s degree in engineering, computer science, data science, or related field
*   3 years of technical project management experience
*   7 years in software delivery with government clients (federal, state, or local)
*   Demonstrated experience leading AI/ML initiatives or proof of concept development

**Nice to have:**

*   Master's degree
*   Proven track record of successful software project delivery in complex environments
*   Strong understanding of software development lifecycle and methodologies
*   Experience with AI/ML technologies, frameworks, and implementation strategies
*   Familiarity with government technology standards and compliance requirements Knowledge of DevSecOps, cloud platforms, and modern development tools Understanding of data science principles and analytics platforms Certifications (Preferred)
*   Project Management Professional (PMP) or equivalent AI/ML related certifications (AWS ML, Google Cloud ML, etc.)
*   Exceptional communication and presentation skills
*   Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities
*   Ability to translate technical concepts for non-technical stakeholders
*   Experience managing budgets and contract deliverables
*   Proven ability to work in fast-paced, dynamic environments
*   Strong leadership and team management capabilities

**Security Clearance:**

*   An active TS/SCI federal security clearance is required
