### Description

Are you looking for a role where you can take on responsibility and truly make a difference? Do you want to work in a motivated environment where collaboration, openness, and continuous development are highly valued?

Then you've come to the right place! We are a dynamic, growing company that develops innovative solutions to successfully position products on Europe's most important online marketplaces. Our team of around 100 passionate individuals ensures that our customers achieve their goals efficiently and sustainably.

To strengthen our IT team, we are immediately looking for an enthusiastic **System Administrator (m/f/d)** to join us full-time. In this role, you will be crucial in maintaining and evolving our IT infrastructure, directly supporting our mission to shape digital commerce in Europe.

We offer a friendly, helpful team, a hybrid work model (4 days on-site, 1 day home office) for optimal balance, excellent fort- and further training opportunities, modern IT equipment, and a structured onboarding process. You'll also benefit from short decision-making paths, plenty of room for your own ideas, free beverages, fresh fruit, and on-site parking.

### Responsibilities

*   Manage and further develop the IT infrastructure (client, server, and network systems).
*   Administer and monitor Linux and Windows servers.
*   Ensure IT security, system availability, and network performance.
*   Provide technical support to employees for various questions and issues.
*   Plan and implement IT projects (upgrades, migrations, automations).
*   Perform regular backups and maintain system documentation.

### Qualifications

*   Completed vocational training as an **IT Specialist for System Integration** or an equivalent qualification.
*   At least **3 years of professional experience** in system administration.
*   Excellent knowledge of **Windows 10/11** and **Linux and Windows servers**.
*   Experience with **network storage solutions** (e.g., QNAP NAS).
*   Strong team spirit, proactive attitude, and a solution-oriented approach.
*   Very good German and English language skills, both written and spoken.
