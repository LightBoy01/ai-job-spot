### Description
BRANDCIRCLE is a dynamic and rapidly growing E-Commerce M&A advisory boutique specializing in facilitating M&A deals for online retailers, typically with annual revenues between €2 million and €25 million.

As BRANDCIRCLE, we provide strategic and operational consulting for the sale of assets and/or company shares for e-commerce entrepreneurs, aiming to achieve the highest possible sale value for our clients. For our alumni – clients who have successfully sold with us – we also manage the acquisition of other brands/companies. This involves activities ranging from initial acquisition and negotiation to document review and successful contract conclusion.

We are committed to offering our clients the best possible advisory experience. We achieve this through excellent results in business sales, and for us, a trusting, open, and reliable collaboration with our clients is paramount.

We are a results-driven company that operates on the principle of "Hire for attitude, train for skill" and will train you in all relevant areas! Therefore, it is advantageous if you speak our language: "E-Commerce M&A." We are fully convinced of the benefits of 100% remote work in our daily operations, guaranteeing high flexibility! If you are eager to "speak E-Commerce M&A" with us and dedicate yourself fully to our clients, then you've found the right place!

### Responsibilities
As a Financial Analyst, you will play a crucial role in supporting our clients' M&A transactions. Your responsibilities will include:

*   Analyzing, conceptualizing, and controlling our clients' financials, often in the millions.
*   Optimizing financial and sales strategies for our clients.
*   Strategically and operationally structuring the sale of our clients' businesses.
*   Supporting the sales process of our active client mandates to potential buyers.
*   Conducting in-depth research within the sales process for our clients, gaining deep insights into the sale of e-commerce companies.

### Qualifications
We are seeking a proactive and detail-oriented Financial Analyst to join our team. The ideal candidate will possess a blend of strong analytical skills, an affinity for numbers, and excellent communication abilities.

**Hard Skills:**
*   Practical experience in interpreting profit and loss statements (BWAs) and balance sheets.
*   A degree in Business Administration (BWL), Economics (VWL), or Business and Economics (WiWi) is advantageous.
*   Understanding of Amazon FBA and online retail is beneficial.
*   Experience with software programs such as Unternehmen-Online, Notion, and Asana.
*   Very good proficiency in MS Office and Google Workspace.
*   Native-level German language skills.
*   Fluent English language skills.

**Soft Skills:**
*   High perseverance, ambition, and self-confidence.
*   Strong affinity for numbers and a preference for working with them.
*   Excellent communication and presentation skills.
*   Open and trustworthy demeanor with a high degree of loyalty.
*   Quick comprehension and a solution-oriented mindset.
*   Structured and conscientious working approach.
*   Eager to learn and seeking challenges.
*   Positive outlook and attitude towards life.
*   Willingness to go the extra mile when necessary.
