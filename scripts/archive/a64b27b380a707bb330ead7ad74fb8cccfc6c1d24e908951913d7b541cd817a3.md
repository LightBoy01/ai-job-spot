At Serco, we believe in creating safe, dignified, and welcoming environments for individuals navigating the asylum system. Through our partnership with Asylum Accommodation and Support Services (AASC), we're proud to provide essential — offering not just accommodation, but compassion, respect, and a fresh start.  
  
We’re currently looking for a dedicated Cleaner to help us maintain safe and hygienic homes across Warrington. This is far more than a cleaning role — it’s your chance to play a part in something bigger, supporting individuals and families during one of the most challenging times in their lives.  
  
 

**What we offer:**

*   Annual salary of £24,330
    
*   Company van provided for work-related travel
    
*   Full-time role (37.5 hours/week) with flexible hours Monday–Friday, between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM
    
*   **Pension:** 6% contributory pension scheme
    
*   **Discounts:** Discounts across around 1,000 retailers, from food and clothing to days out, entertainment and travel.
    
*   **Health and wellbeing:** Employee Assistance Programme, Bupa Anytime HealthLine, wellbeing app, 30% off at Serco-managed leisure centres, and 24/7 access to a virtual GP service
    
*   **Training and development:** Over 1,100 internal courses available, funding for qualifications and apprenticeships (including L3 Team Leader and L5 Operations Manager apprenticeships), mentoring opportunities, and a focus on internal progression
    

  
 

**What you’ll be doing:**

As a Cleaner, you’ll be responsible for ensuring that accommodation provided to asylum seekers is clean, safe, and welcoming. This includes:

*   Cleaning both dispersed homes and communal areas
    
*   Traveling across Liverpool and occasionally other regions
    
*   Taking pride in high standards of cleanliness and presentation
    

  
 

**What we’re looking for:**

*   A valid UK driving licence — we’ll provide the vehicle
    
*   Great communication and IT skills
    
*   A team player who’s also confident working independently
    
*   Previous experience in cleaning households or similar environments
    
*   Experience of cleaning larger households is desirable, but not essential
