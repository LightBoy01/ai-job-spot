### Description
Famedly, founded in 2019, is a dynamic, remote-first startup dedicated to revolutionizing healthcare through privacy-compliant digital communication. We develop messaging software for healthcare facilities, aiming to improve treatment outcomes by fostering seamless communication among doctors, nurses, and therapists. Our solutions boost staff productivity and integrate smoothly with primary systems.

As a **Senior Rust Developer (m/f/d)**, you'll join an experienced team working on multiple open-source projects. Famedly is committed to leveraging open-source software, primarily Matrix and FHIR, to modernize healthcare. Your primary focus will be on a specific greenfield Rust project, offering a chance to build from the ground up. Additionally, our unique team exchange program provides opportunities to contribute temporarily to other open-source initiatives, broadening your impact and experience.

### Responsibilities
*   Design, build, and maintain scalable, high-performance Rust applications and services.
*   Collaborate effectively with your team to deliver high-quality software solutions.
*   Ensure the highest standards of code quality, comprehensive test coverage, and thorough documentation.
*   Proactively identify and resolve performance and scalability issues within our systems.

### Qualifications

**Required Skills & Experience:**
*   A strong understanding that communication is paramount for a successful developer.
*   Proven team player who can learn quickly and apply creative problem-solving.
*   5+ years of professional software development experience.
*   3+ years of hands-on experience in Rust development.
*   3+ years of experience in automated testing.
*   Demonstrable experience in API and library design.
*   Familiarity with DevOps methodologies, practices, and Git.

**Preferred/Bonus Qualifications:**
*   A degree in Computer Science or a related field (while a plus, your skills and practical experience are valued more).
*   Technical writing background.
*   Experience with microservices and event-driven architectures.
*   Experience with Rust FFI (Foreign Function Interface).
*   Knowledge of the Matrix protocol.
*   German language proficiency (C1 certificate).

*We encourage you to share your GitHub/GitLab profile with us to showcase your work.*
