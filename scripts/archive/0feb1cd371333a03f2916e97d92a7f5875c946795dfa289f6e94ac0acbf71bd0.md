Simple Technology Solutions is looking for a **Principal** **Data, AI/ML Engineer** to add to our team.  

**Quick Position Overview:**  

*   US Citizenship is required 
*   Bachelor's Degree is required 
*   Minimum of 15 years' position related experience is required  

**The Role:**  

Simple Technology Solutions is seeking a **Principal Data, AI/ML Enginee**r to join our growing team. You will play a vital role in designing and implementing innovative solutions that leverage artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data analytics to drive business growth. 

**The Principal Data, AI/ML Engineer at STS will:** 

*   Collaborate extensively with stakeholders to deeply understand business requirements, translating them into robust technical solutions that advance the enterprise's objectives.
*   Spearhead the design and development of AI/ML projects, crafting intricate yet scalable data pipelines, model training mechanisms, and deployment strategies.
*   Assume responsibility for the meticulous oversight of data lifecycle management, ensuring seamless collection, preprocessing, and quality assurance to uphold data integrity.
*   Pioneering the development of machine learning models, from prototyping to rigorous training and validation, employing state-of-the-art algorithms and methodologies.
*   Continually refine and optimize model performance, utilizing sophisticated techniques to meet and exceed predefined benchmarks.
*   Implement robust data handling protocols and security measures, adhering meticulously to industry standards and internal policies.
*   Forge strong partnerships with data scientists, engineers, product managers, and executives, orchestrating cohesive project execution across teams.
*   Provide diligent oversight and support for deployed AI/ML solutions, ensuring their sustained performance and reliability.
*   Offer guidance and mentorship to junior team members, fostering their professional growth while serving as a beacon of technical excellence.
*   Maintain an active presence within the AI/ML community, staying at the forefront of emerging technologies, research, and industry best practices. 

**Education and Experience:** 

_Required_ 

*   Bachelor's or Master’s degree in computer science, Data Science, Engineering, or a closely related discipline.
*   Over 15 years of hands-on experience in architecting AI/ML solutions, demonstrating a deep understanding of diverse machine learning algorithms and methodologies.
*   Strong command of programming languages such as Python, R, Java, or Scala, with a proven track record of building robust and scalable solutions.
*   In-depth familiarity with leading AI/ML frameworks, including TensorFlow, PyTorch, or Scikit-learn, leveraging them to develop cutting-edge models and solutions.
*   Strong SQL abilities and experience with massive relational database systems.
*   Comprehensive knowledge of big data technologies such as Hadoop, Spark, or Kafka, harnessing their power to manage and analyze vast datasets effectively.
*   Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS and Azure is highly valued for this role, particularly in leveraging their capabilities to deploy and scale AI/ML solutions.  
*   Proficiency in Agile methodologies and related tools is advantageous for effective project management and collaboration.
