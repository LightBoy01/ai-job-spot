### Description\nAs a Working Student in Marketing with a focus on Social Media, your primary role will be to manage and further develop our social media activities for both samedi and our clients. You'll create target-group-specific content, develop editorial plans, and oversee the planning and distribution of content across various platforms. This includes monitoring trends, contributing creative ideas, and actively shaping our social media presence.\n\nAdditionally, you will support website maintenance, the execution of marketing campaigns, and the organization and promotion of events and trade fairs. In digital marketing, you'll assist with keyword analyses and research for Google SEA, as well as market and competitor analyses to gain insights into target groups and industry trends. Your creative and analytical approach will be crucial in continuously evolving our marketing strategies.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Manage social media content, including video editing and image processing.\n- Develop editorial plans and schedule social media posts across platforms.\n- Regularly maintain our website using the content management system.\n- Support the planning and execution of marketing campaigns.\n- Assist with the organization and promotion of events, trade fairs, and workshops.\n- Provide support for Google SEA, including keyword analyses and optimizations.\n- Contribute to market research, focusing on target groups, competitors, and market needs.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Currently enrolled in a degree program in Business Administration with a focus on Marketing & Communications, or a comparable field of study.\n- Interest in the healthcare sector and initial practical experience in marketing are a plus.\n- Basic knowledge of tools like Canva, CapCut, various social media platforms, and common content management systems.\n- Enjoyment of writing engaging texts.\n- Creativity, strong teamwork skills, and a good sense for visual design and effective communication.\n- Affinity for digital trends and social media.\n- Excellent German language skills and good English language skills, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting entry-level opportunity for a working student passionate about digital marketing and making a real impact in the healthcare sector. samedi, a leading e-health company, offers a dynamic and creative environment where you'll gain hands-on experience in social media, content management, and campaign support. The role provides a a fantastic blend of creative and analytical tasks, with the added benefit of a flexible hybrid model, trust-based working hours, and a dog-friendly office. For ambitious students, there's a clear path for professional growth and potential long-term collaboration after graduation, all while contributing to a mission that genuinely improves health access and patient outcomes.
