### Responsibilities

**About the Role**

We are seeking a **Senior Principal Software Engineer** with deep expertise in **Generative AI** to lead the design and development of next-generation AI applications. This is a hands-on, architecture-driven role for a seasoned technologist who thrives in regulated environments and is passionate about building secure, scalable, and intelligent systems.

You will be responsible for independently prototyping, coding, and validating advanced AI solutions using agentic patterns, multi-agent frameworks, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures. You’ll also play a key role in evangelizing architectural best practices and driving innovation across the organization.

**In this role,** _**you will:**_

*   Architect and build production-grade Generative AI applications using modern agentic and multi-agent design patterns.
*   Independently develop proof-of-concepts (PoCs) and prototypes to validate new ideas and technologies.
*   Design and implement RAG pipelines, integrating LLMs, vector databases, and orchestration frameworks.
*   Ensure AI systems meet privacy, governance, and compliance standards, especially in highly regulated industries such as finance.
*   Evaluate and select appropriate GenAI platforms (e.g., Azure AI Foundry, Databricks Mosaic AI) based on project needs.
*   Lead architectural reviews and influence enterprise-wide technology decisions.
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to align AI solutions with business goals and regulatory requirements.
*   Mentor engineers and promote a culture of innovation, quality, and accountability.
*   Stay current with emerging AI technologies, evaluation frameworks, and industry trends.
