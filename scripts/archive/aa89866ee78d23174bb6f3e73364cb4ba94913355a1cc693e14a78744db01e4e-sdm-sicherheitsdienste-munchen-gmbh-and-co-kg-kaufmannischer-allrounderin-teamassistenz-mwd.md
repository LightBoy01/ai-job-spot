### Description
For many years, we have stood for reliability, discretion, and the highest quality standards – both in security services and in our daily teamwork. As a 100% subsidiary of the listed sdm Group, we handle operational tasks within the group.

To strengthen our administration, we are looking for a dedicated Commercial All-rounder / Team Assistant (m/f/d) who will support our Managing Director and the team with organizational talent, clear structure, and foresight. This is a versatile and responsible role aimed at actively relieving the team and allowing the management to focus on core tasks.

### Responsibilities

**Organization & Coordination**
*   Planning and managing appointments for the Managing Director and the team
*   Travel planning, including bookings and expense reports
*   Structured office organization and management of daily office operations

**Communication & Correspondence**
*   Processing of emails, phone calls, and written correspondence
*   Creation, formatting, and review of documents, presentations, and reports
*   Taking minutes at meetings and tracking To-Dos

**Administration & Accounting**
*   Creation and review of invoices, and processing of expense reports
*   Maintenance of databases, attendance lists, and business key figures
*   Support with budget monitoring and cost control

**Support for the Team & Management**
*   Creation of PowerPoint presentations, reports, and analyses
*   Actively relieve the team
*   Goal: "Keeping the Managing Director and the team free to focus on core tasks."

### Qualifications

**Your Profile – what you should bring**
*   Completed commercial training or a comparable qualification
*   Ideally, initial professional experience (1–2 years) in an assistant role, in a professional service environment (e.g., M&A, Consulting, Legal, Administration)
*   Very good German and English language skills, both written and spoken
*   Proficient in MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
*   Loyal, dynamic, and open to new challenges
*   Systematic, reliable, and able to set priorities

### Author's Take
This opportunity with a 100% subsidiary of the sdm Group offers a stable and secure career path in a growing segment of the security industry. The company strongly emphasizes a supportive team culture characterized by flat hierarchies, open communication, and mutual reliance. New hires can expect continuous professional development through regular training and practical advanced education, fostering both personal and professional growth.

Beyond the core work, the company invests in employee well-being and cohesion, offering attractive compensation with performance-related bonuses, complimentary drinks and snacks, and engaging team events and workshops. This role is ideal for a proactive and organized individual eager to be an integral part of a dynamic team and make a tangible impact by supporting key leadership functions. If you're ready for the next step in a collaborative and growth-oriented environment, this could be an excellent fit.
