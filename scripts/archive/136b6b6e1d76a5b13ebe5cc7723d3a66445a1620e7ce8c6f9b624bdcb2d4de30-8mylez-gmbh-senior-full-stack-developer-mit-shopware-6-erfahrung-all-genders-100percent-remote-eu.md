### Description

Are you tired of half-baked code and endless meetings? We are an ambitious e-commerce agency with around 35 dedicated individuals, shaping the future of digital shopping and supporting market-leading online shops. We are looking for a **Senior Full Stack Developer with a strong backend focus** who truly values transparent communication, clean coding, and high-quality work—not just as buzzwords.

This is a **100% remote** role where a structured, solution-oriented, and hands-on approach is key, with a strong emphasis on technical automation and efficiency. We pride ourselves on having no process chaos, with digital and efficient documentation and workflows. Join us and help make the internet more beautiful, better, and faster every day.

### Responsibilities

*   Configure and further develop complex Shopware projects.
*   Work with a mix of backend and frontend technologies: **Python, JavaScript (Vue.js, React), PHP (Symfony), and Git**.
*   Take technical responsibility in projects and collaborate closely with project managers.
*   Develop innovative ideas for the e-commerce sector.
*   Explore and learn modern and new web technologies.

### Qualifications

*   At least **4-5 years of practical experience** in full-stack development, with a **focus on backend**.
*   Minimum **2 years of experience with Shopware 6**.
*   Proficiency in **German at C1 level** or higher.
*   **Profound knowledge in PHP (Symfony)** and ideally Python.
*   Experience with **JavaScript frameworks** such as Vue.js or React.
*   Routine in **using Git**.
*   Strong sense of responsibility and quality awareness.
*   Solution-oriented and **independent working style**.
*   Bachelor's degree or vocational training as an IT specialist in fields such as Mathematics, Physics, or Computer Science.
