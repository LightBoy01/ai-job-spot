### Description\nUnivativ is a project and personnel service provider dedicated to connecting students and graduates with exciting opportunities at renowned companies. Our mission is your career, and your development is at the heart of what we do. Are you looking for a position where you can fully leverage and further develop your expertise? Then we are looking for you as a Project Coordinator (m/f/d) for On-Board Diagnostics (OBD)! Our client is a prestigious company in the automotive industry. From day one, you will be an integral part of an innovative team, tackling complex and challenging tasks. If we've sparked your interest, we look forward to receiving your application!\n\n### Responsibilities\n- You create and maintain model series-specific project schedules and milestones.\n- You also take over budget and resource planning.\n- You evaluate cross-functional OBD maturity levels.\n- Coordinating internal and external interfaces, including reporting, is part of your tasks.\n- In addition, you manage external service providers.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in Automotive Engineering, Mechatronics, Electrical Engineering, or a comparable qualification.\n- Experience in On-Board Diagnostics, project management, and maturity level assessment.\n- Knowledge of relevant legislation (OBD, Euro-7, etc.).\n- Proficient use of diagnostic/evaluation tools such as INCA/MDA and DiagRA is an advantage.\n- At least business-fluent German and English skills, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a Project Coordinator with a background in automotive engineering and On-Board Diagnostics to join a prestigious company in Affalterbach. The role offers a competitive annual salary of €75,000 - €85,000, along with the flexibility of partial remote work. Univativ emphasizes employee growth with diverse e-academy training opportunities and a collaborative, innovative team environment. If you're passionate about complex OBD projects and stakeholder coordination, and value professional development, this role promises significant impact and career advancement.
