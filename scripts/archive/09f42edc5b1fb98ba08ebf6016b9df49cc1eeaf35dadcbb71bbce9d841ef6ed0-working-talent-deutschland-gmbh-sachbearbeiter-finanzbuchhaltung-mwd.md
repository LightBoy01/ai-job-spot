### Description\nThis is an opportunity for a **Financial Accountant (m/f/d)** to join an established, owner-managed B2B company in Essen with over 100 years of history. The role offers technically demanding tasks within a stable environment and a well-integrated team, supported by a hybrid work model (up to 2 days home office). The company is also planning a move to modern new office spaces in Essen by 2026, providing a forward-thinking work environment. This direct placement offers a permanent position with short decision paths and close collaboration with management, providing a unique blend of stability and modernization.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Collaborate in ongoing financial accounting within a group environment.\n- Post business transactions in SAP (accounts payable, accounts receivable, general ledger).\n- Take responsibility for fixed asset accounting.\n- Prepare VAT advance returns.\n- Assist with monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements.\n- Perform account reconciliations and clarify open items.\n- Work closely with accounts receivable and accounts payable departments.\n- Engage in professional exchange with commercial management and support ad-hoc topics.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Commercial training, ideally with further qualification as a certified balance accountant (Bilanzbuchhalter) or equivalent.\n- Several years of experience in financial and/or fixed asset accounting, preferably in a group context.\n- **Mandatory** secure handling of SAP in accounting.\n- Very good knowledge of accounting principles and VAT.\n- Structured, reliable work ethic and team orientation.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role presents a compelling opportunity for a skilled Financial Accountant looking for stability combined with forward-thinking development. Joining a company with a century-long legacy offers a secure, permanent position within a deeply experienced team. The hybrid work model and upcoming move to modern offices underscore a commitment to employee well-being and a progressive work environment. Direct involvement with commercial leadership and short decision paths mean your contributions will be valued and impactful. This position is ideal for someone who thrives in a collaborative setting, values work-life balance, and wants to grow within a respected, evolving enterprise.
