Job Summary
-----------

We are seeking an exceptional Senior Software Engineer to join our team and drive innovation in our cloud-based test platform used in manufacturing for storage products testing. This is a unique opportunity to blend cutting-edge AI capabilities with robust cloud infrastructure while making a direct impact on product quality and manufacturing efficiency.

Responsibilities
----------------

*   Design, develop, and maintain our cloud-based test platform for storage product manufacturing
*   Integrate AI and machine learning capabilities to enhance testing intelligence, predictive analytics, and automation
*   Architect scalable cloud solutions that support high-volume manufacturing test operations
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams including manufacturing, quality assurance, and product engineering
*   Optimize platform performance, reliability, and scalability
*   Implement best practices in software development, testing, and deployment
*   Mentor junior engineers and contribute to technical decision-making

*   AI/ML Development: Strong knowledge in AI model development, integration, and deployment in production environments
*   Cloud Platforms: Strong expertise in cloud-based solutions (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
*   Programming Languages: Strong C#, C++, or Java skills for application and web service development
*   Experience with JavaScript primarily Node.js and Python
*   Experience with SQL database stored procedures, administration, and desig
