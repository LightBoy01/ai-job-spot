Forging Future food champions.

At Oyster Bay, we believe the way we grow, prepare, and enjoy food defines our future. It shapes our health, our thinking, our memories, and how we connect with one another on this one planet we all share.

That is why we team up with the rare founders who see the future of food - and back them to build a successful champion that disrupts the food system for the better. Both economically and ecologically.

Tasks
-----

If you are extremely ambitious, yet know what you dont know and want to make a meaningful difference with your work - this one may be for you. We are continiously looking for C-Level Talent for our growing portfolio of impact-driven companies.

Thus - leave us your CV and if we have an opening that fits your profile, we will reach out.

Requirements
------------

For our founders & C-Level executives in our portfolio we expect an extreme level of dedication and passion to their respective company. Great companies are not built by leaning back.

Likely open positions in our portfolio include:

\- Chief Marketing Officer

\- Chief Operating Officer

\- Chief Financial Officer

Benefits
--------

Any C-Level executive in our portfolio has a meaningful stake in the respective company. The individual compensation package is up to the portfolio.

The location will of course depend on the specific portfolio company.
