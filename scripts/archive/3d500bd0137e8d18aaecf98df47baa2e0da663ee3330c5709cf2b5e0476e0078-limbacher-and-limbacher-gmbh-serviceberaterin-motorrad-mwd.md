### Description
Limbächer & Limbächer GmbH, Germany's largest motorcycle center, is seeking a passionate Motorcycle Service Advisor (m/f/d) to join our team of over 200 colleagues. Dive into the world of motorized passion, where we turn our customers' mobility dreams into reality. As the first point of contact for our clients, you'll bring dedication and enthusiasm to support our team. We offer an innovative, team-oriented, and trusting work environment, built on growth, honesty, and fairness. If you have an affinity for motorcycles and enjoy customer interaction, this is the place for you. Embrace the challenge and become part of our community, where courage and reliability are key.

### Responsibilities
*   Provide expert advice and support to service customers, from appointment scheduling and vehicle intake to handover.
*   Independently prepare and explain service and repair quotes, including cost and service overviews.
*   Plan, coordinate, and monitor workshop orders in collaboration with the workshop management and technicians.
*   Conduct vehicle intake and handover, including visual inspections, documentation, and customer consultations.
*   Ensure high customer satisfaction through transparent communication and proactive complaint management.
*   Responsibly process warranty and goodwill claims, as well as complaints, according to manufacturer specifications.
*   Actively sell service packages, accessories, and additional products to enhance customer satisfaction and workshop utilization.
*   Offer technical advice and create individual maintenance and repair recommendations based on vehicle condition and customer needs.
*   Ensure the quality of completed work and approve vehicles after workshop processing.
*   Cultivate and expand long-term customer relationships through professional service and proactive customer engagement.

### Qualifications
*   Completed commercial or technical vocational training and/or
*   Experience in sales is desirable but not mandatory.
*   Career changers are welcome.
*   Friendly and approachable demeanor with a neat appearance.
*   Strong sense of responsibility, reliability, sociability, and communication skills.
*   Enjoy working with customers and sales.
*   Meticulous and precise working style.
*   Passion for motorcycles.
*   Driving licenses classes A and B are advantageous.
