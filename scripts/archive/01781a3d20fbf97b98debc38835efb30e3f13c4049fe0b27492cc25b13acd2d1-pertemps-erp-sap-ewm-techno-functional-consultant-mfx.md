### Description\nAre you ready to shape the future of logistics at the intersection of process and code? Join one of Europe's most innovative supply chain organizations as an SAP EWM Techno-Functional Consultant. Based in Cologne, Germany, with a flexible hybrid work model (approximately 60% remote), you will deliver major implementation and optimization projects for international clients. This role empowers you to own processes end-to-end, translating complex business needs into robust technical solutions and building the enhancements that drive significant impact. This is your opportunity to combine your deep EWM knowledge with strong ABAP skills in a forward-thinking environment where logistics, technology, and innovation converge.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Lead and support complex SAP EWM implementations and rollouts for international customers.\n*   Analyze and optimize warehouse/logistics processes, translating business needs into robust technical solutions.\n*   Configure SAP EWM (processes, RF, integrations) and develop ABAP enhancements (exits/BADIs, reports, forms).\n*   Build and maintain interfaces (IDoc/OData/REST), troubleshoot issues, and perform debugging/unit testing.\n*   Take ownership of work packages, ensuring quality and on-time delivery.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   3+ years hands-on SAP EWM experience (process design & configuration).\n*   Strong ABAP development skills (OO ABAP, enhancements, BADIs, performance/debugging); exposure to Fiori/UI5 is a plus.\n*   Bonus: experience across WM, SD, or MM and integration topics (EDI, PI/PO, CPI).\n*   Clear communication in English; German is a plus.\n*   Degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or similar.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an experienced SAP EWM Techno-Functional Consultant looking for significant ownership and growth within a dynamic, international team. The company offers a vibrant culture focused on continuous learning and professional development, underscored by a highly attractive hybrid working model. With a strong team culture, a modern workplace, and competitive compensation up to €90,000 depending on experience, you'll find an environment where your skills have a direct and measurable impact on cutting-edge logistics projects. If you thrive on solving complex problems and driving innovation, this role is for you.
