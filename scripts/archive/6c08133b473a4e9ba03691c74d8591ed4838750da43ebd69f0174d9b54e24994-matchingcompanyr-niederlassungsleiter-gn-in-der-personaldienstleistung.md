### Description\nThe MatchingCompany® specializes in professional and sustainable recruitment, with over 25 years of experience in placing specialist and management positions in People Management across Germany. We are looking for a **Branch Manager (Niederlassungsleitung)** for our client in Neuss, responsible for the **placement (ANÜ and PERM) of industrial-technical personnel.** This is a key operational leadership role that requires keeping all threads together and acting as the external ambassador for the branch.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Overall responsibility for the branch and acting as its \"ambassador\" to the outside world.\n*   Responsible leadership of operational business, including budget and revenue responsibility.\n*   New customer acquisition, as well as cultivation and expansion of existing customer relationships.\n*   Organizational, administrative, and personnel management of the team focused on personnel and customer consulting (disposition).\n*   Planning and control of relevant sales activities.\n*   Coordination of collaboration with all local departments and headquarters.\n*   Ensuring compliance with all labor law, personnel-related, and legal provisions.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Successfully completed vocational training.\n*   Several years of experience in the personnel services industry as a Branch Manager (Niederlassungsleitung) within the framework of temporary employment (Arbeitnehmerüberlassung) for industrial-technical personnel.\n*   Business management and human resources knowledge, especially in leading employees.\n*   Fluent German language skills, both written and spoken.\n*   High sales affinity, strong determination, and proactive work style.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a pivotal opportunity for an experienced leader in the personnel services sector to take full ownership of a branch in Neuss. The role offers significant autonomy, budget responsibility, and the chance to shape a team focused on placing industrial-technical personnel. With a strong emphasis on continuous development, generous benefits, and a supportive company culture, The MatchingCompany provides an excellent platform for a driven individual with a proactive sales mindset to make a substantial impact and contribute directly to the company's success. If you're looking for a role where you can truly lead and grow, this could be your perfect match.
