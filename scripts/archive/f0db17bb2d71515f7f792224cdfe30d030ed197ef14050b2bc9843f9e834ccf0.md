**Graphic Designer - Nas Company**
Location: Phillippines
Nas Company is an ecosystem of products that help people feel more connected.
Both online and offline. In the millions.
We are a team of 100 people from 28 countries who are market leaders in the creator economy, reaching 300 Million people globally every month.
Nas Company has 5 main products:
Education: Nas Academy
Technology: Nas.io
Media: Nas Daily (65 Million followers!)
Events: Nas Summit
Hotels: Nas House
**Key Responsibilities:**
*   Take full ownership of design projects, from conceptualization and branding to visual execution.
*   Design a range of visual assets, including pitch decks for business presentations, landing page assets for product launches, and community assets.
*   Collaborate with the content team to design high-quality, photo-manipulated thumbnails and statics for various social media channels.
*   Design promotional materials that effectively promote our products and enhance brand visibility.
*   Stay up-to-date with the latest design trends and best practices, adapting as needed to maintain relevance and competitiveness.
**Qualifications:**
*   Proven 3 years of experience in graphic design/motion graphics, preferably in a social media environment.
*   A strong portfolio of illustrations or other graphics
*   Proficient in Adobe Creative Suite (specifically Photoshop and Illustrator). Familiarity with Figma, Framer and any 3D modelling software, is a plus point.
*   Familiarity with print design, pop-up structures, and exhibition booth designs.
*   Proficiency in using AI image design tools, such as Midjourney, is highly recommended.
*   A keen eye for aesthetics and attention to detail.
*   Excellent communication skills and ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams in a fast-paced environment.
