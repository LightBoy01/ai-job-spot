**Lead the Way in Station Cleaning – Day Shift Role Along the Mandurah Line**  
**_Full-Time | Great Pay | Weekdays Only | Supportive Team_**  
Ventia is looking for a reliable and motivated Leading Hand Cleaner to join our team in a full-time, weekday role.   
If you take pride in maintaining clean public spaces and want your evenings and weekends free, this opportunity offers the perfect balance.  
Start and finish your day at Perth Train Station, driving a company van along the Mandurah Line to ensure each station is clean, safe, and welcoming for commuters.

**Role Details**  
**Position:** Cleaning Team Leader  
**Hours:** Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM  
**Weekly Pay:** $1,014.70 plus Superannuation  
**Location:** Begin and end your shift at Perth Train Station, driving the company van along the Mandurah Line to clean each station.  
**Contract:** Full-Time until end of July 2026 (with potential extension)

**Key Responsibilities**

*   Clean public areas, platforms, restrooms, and station facilities
*   Perform routine tasks: sweeping, mopping, dusting, emptying bins, restocking supplies
*   Operate pressure washers and scrubber machines for deep cleaning
*   Monitor supplies, report issues, and follow safety procedures
*   Lead the cleaning team, maintain standards, and communicate with management
