At ING the landscape has become increasingly complex with more applications added every day. We depend and rely on each other to deliver a reliable service to our customers that demands the very best of us.
Every single day, our engineers typically look at metrics, traces and logs to assess the health of their application, all of which need to be pulled from different sources and interfaces. MDPL provides a solution where they can combine data with just a few clicks but being connected and dependent on a large chain of applications raises the need for insights beyond their application to run a reliable service.
Platform Engineering is responsible for monitoring and observability data services, such as providing components for onboarding, data storage, dashboarding, alerting and offering observability insights to all ING engineers globally. You will support the observability core team in running the core capabilities and increasing observability maturity across ING.
The team
MDPL v2 Core squad is an international team of highly qualified DevOps engineers. You will join a team where the product is constantly evolving and introducing new features. The platform is based on several components developed in Go(lang). The components are deployed on ING’s private cloud (RedHat OpenShift) as well as the public cloud (MS Azure (AKS))
Roles and responsibilities
We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced Senior Platform Engineer to join our team in a DevOps environment. The successful candidate will have a strong background in Observability and a deep understanding of Open Telemetry, and experience working in a complex, enterprise-level environment.You will have a leading role in the squad regarding designing, implementing, and testing new and improved functionality of the products. You understand the entire lifecycle of an application from refinement up until release to production, as well as the operational aspects like monitoring, alerting, and resolving incidents.
*   Drive improvements in our current Observability solution
*   Enable ING teams globally to have a frictionless experience in storing and exposing the Telemetry data for their applications.
*   Incorporate AI features to our Observability solution
*   Extend the solution to run On-premises as well as Public Cloud.
*   Use the latest technologies to improve the usability of our solution.
