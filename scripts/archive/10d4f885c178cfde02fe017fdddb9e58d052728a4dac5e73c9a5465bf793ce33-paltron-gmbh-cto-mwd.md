### Description
The partner operates as an independent unit within one of Germany's largest media groups. As an HR-Tech holding company with its own investment capital, it pursues a consistent \"Greenfield\" approach, combining corporate resources with an agile mindset and the structure of a private equity player. They are looking for a CTO in Munich (alternatively Berlin or Essen).

Your mission is to not only integrate innovative companies in the recruiting market but also to sustainably develop them through technological excellence. You are responsible for the challenging balancing act between restructuring legacy systems and building a completely new, data-driven target architecture. As the technological leader, you will unite all portfolio companies on a scalable platform, thereby creating true value.

### Responsibilities
- You are responsible for the technological vision and building the automated multi-channel control of a platform that efficiently distributes budget across various channels using algorithms.
- You manage technical due diligence and Post-Merger Integration (PMI) of acquired companies, deciding on the restructuring, remediation, or re-development of tech stacks.
- You lead tech teams (currently a mix of internal and external employees across various locations) and develop a scalable organization, exemplifying a \"hands-on\" mentality and actively intervening operationally during critical bottlenecks or escalations.
- You act as a technical sparring partner for the Board, the CEOs within the parent company, and the extended shareholder group, confidently presenting complex matters at eye-level to implement technological agendas group-wide.
- You foster a culture of entrepreneurial thinking and drive innovation, with the option to support the spin-off of new ventures (e.g., in AI recruiting).

### Qualifications
- Several years of experience in a CTO, VP Engineering, or comparable leadership role, ideally in scaling environments or restructuring situations (M&A / PMI experience is a big plus).
- Deep technical understanding of modern IT architectures (e.g., Microservices, API-first, Cloud/AWS, AI) and ideally experience from data-driven environments such as e-commerce, AdTech, or FinTech.
- Assertiveness to make independent decisions, implement necessary technological cuts, and drive change in traditional structures.
- Strong communication skills, ability to discuss technically complex topics at eye-level with various stakeholders, and the sensitivity to build bridges in a corporate environment.
- Fluent in German and English, and willingness to travel occasionally within Germany.
