### Description

Shape your future in a globally oriented law firm – where challenging mandates and personal development go hand in hand.

On behalf of our client in Munich, we are looking for an **Accounts Receivable Accountant (m/f/d)** to join us at the earliest possible date.

In this pivotal role, you will be a key player in our financial operations, ensuring the smooth processing of invoices and contributing to the overall financial health of a dynamic legal practice. Our firm prides itself on a supportive and collaborative environment, fostering professional growth and offering a clear path for development within a flat hierarchical structure.

#### What We Offer:

*   **Work-Life Balance:** Enjoy regulated working hours with partial home office options, supporting a healthy balance between your professional and personal life.
*   **Team Spirit:** Become part of a friendly and collegial company culture that values teamwork and open communication.
*   **Development:** Benefit from numerous internal opportunities for professional and personal further education and training.
*   **Work Environment:** Work in modern office spaces located in the vibrant city of Munich.
*   **Events:** Look forward to unique team events that strengthen camaraderie and build lasting relationships.

### Responsibilities

As an Accounts Receivable Accountant (m/f/d), you will be responsible for:

*   Taking ownership of domestic and international invoicing.
*   Supporting monthly and annual financial statements, as well as ongoing financial accounting.
*   Processing and correcting recorded time entries in the billing system.
*   Creating and analyzing revenue reports and assisting with forecast reporting.
*   Reviewing the dunning process, coordinating revenue discussions, and collaborating closely with the Compliance team.

### Qualifications

To be successful in this role, you should bring:

*   A successfully completed commercial vocational training, e.g., as an Industrial Clerk (m/f/d), Office Administrator (m/f/d), Foreign Language Correspondent (m/f/d), or an equivalent qualification.
*   Ideally, professional experience as an Accountant (m/f/d), Accounts Receivable Accountant (m/f/d), or in a comparable position.
*   Reliability, a structured, and independent working style.
*   Proficiency in standard MS Office programs.
*   Very good German and good English language skills.
