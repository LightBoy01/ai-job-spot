### Description
Shape your future in a globally oriented law firm where challenging mandates and personal development go hand-in-hand. On behalf of our client in Munich, we are seeking a dedicated Accounts Receivable Accountant (m/f/d) to join their team as soon as possible. This role offers the opportunity to significantly contribute to the financial operations of a dynamic firm, working with both domestic and international invoicing and supporting crucial financial closing processes within a supportive environment that values work-life balance, team spirit, and continuous growth.

### Responsibilities
*   Take responsibility for domestic and international invoicing.
*   Support monthly and annual financial closings, as well as ongoing financial accounting.
*   Process and correct recorded time entries in the billing system.
*   Generate and analyze revenue reports, and contribute to forecast reporting.
*   Review the dunning process, coordinate revenue discussions, and collaborate closely with the Compliance team.

### Qualifications
*   Successfully completed commercial vocational training, e.g., as an industrial clerk, office clerk, foreign language correspondent, or a comparable qualification.
*   Ideally, you have gained professional experience as an accountant, accounts receivable accountant, or in a similar position.
*   You demonstrate reliability, a structured, and independent working style.
*   Proficiency in common MS Office programs.
*   Very good German and good English language skills.
