**Company Description:**

For 30+ years, Aussie has helped 1.5 million Australians achieve homeownership. Today, we’re also helping them find, buy, and own — not just with loans, but with buyer’s agents, conveyancing, and more.

Backed by a trusted brand, award-winning support, and cutting-edge tech, we’re equipping brokers to grow stronger, faster, and smarter.

**Job Description:**

As an Aussie Mobile Broker, you’ll run your own business with the full backing of Australia’s #1 mortgage brand. Work remotely and flexibly—supporting clients by phone, video or in-person on your terms.

We’ll power your growth with a consistent stream of high-intent appointments, fulfilment support, and AI-driven tools that eliminate admin and supercharge productivity.

This is a self-employed, commission-based opportunity, where you will:

*   Work with pre-qualified customer appointments booked directly into your calendar
    
*   Guide customers from first contact to loan settlement, with processing handled for you
    
*   Harness Aussie’s pioneering tech platform to connect with our centralised Client Solutions team, who handle loan processing and admin on your behalf.
    
*   Earn extra income by connecting customers with additional products and services under the Aussie umbrella, including buyer’s agents and conveyancing.
    

**Qualifications:**

To thrive as an Aussie Mobile Broker, you’ll need:

*   Cert IV in Finance & Mortgage Broking (or willingness to obtain—we’ll support you)
    
*   Sales, broking, or customer service experience
    
*   Motivation to grow a high-performing, flexible business
    
*   Passion for helping Australians achieve property ownership
    
*   Unrestricted Australian working rights
