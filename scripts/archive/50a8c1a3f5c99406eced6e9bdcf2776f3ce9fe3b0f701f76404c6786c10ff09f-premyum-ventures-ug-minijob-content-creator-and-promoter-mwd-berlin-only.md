### Description
Whiffy is an innovative young project based in Berlin, pioneering the first **mobile fragrance add-on for smartphones**. Our mission is to capture attention, create unique experiences, and leverage social media in a fresh, creative way. We're looking for **authentic Berliners** who are passionate about **content creation, street promotion, and genuine vibes** to join our Creator Team. This is an exciting opportunity to bring Whiffy to life where Berlin thrives – in parks, at events, on the streets, and across social media feeds.

### Responsibilities
As a Content Creator, you will be responsible for making Whiffy visible and engaging across Berlin's vibrant landscape:
*   Produce short, creative videos for **TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts**.
*   Create street-level content in Berlin, including interviews, reactions, and spontaneous clips at iconic locations like Kotti, Mauerpark, Späti, and more.
*   Actively **promote Whiffy at local events**, pop-ups, and festivals.
*   Execute small public space activations such as distributing stickers and giveaways (always with official permission).
*   Develop **POVs, product tests, challenges, or skits** – feel free to use your unique style, even from home.

Compensation is performance-based:
*   **€15** for short, simple clips.
*   **€30** for more elaborate stories and videos.
*   **€60** for group videos, surveys, or street campaigns.

### Qualifications
We're looking for someone who fits the following profile:
*   You are communicative, embody an urban spirit, and are comfortable in front of the camera.
*   You have experience with, or a strong interest in, social media and video content creation.
*   You enjoy working independently, bringing in your own ideas, and actively implementing them.
*   You are at least 18 years old and reside in Berlin or the surrounding area.
*   You are flexible with your time, able to commit approximately 8–10 hours per week (which may include evenings or weekends).
