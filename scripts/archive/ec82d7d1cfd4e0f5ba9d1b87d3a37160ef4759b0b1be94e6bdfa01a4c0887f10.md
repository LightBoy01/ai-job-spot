**More about us**
-----------------

Come be part of the Human Resources / Talent Acquisition organization of ExxonMobil’s Global Business Center in Buenos Aires, with about 2800 employees and 20 years, providing support to an extensive portfolio of businesses across the globe.

Our people are what makes the difference, we strive to provide long-term rewarding careers for our employees by offering challenging assignments, with varying degrees of complexity and breadth of opportunities in the different services that we offer. We are interested in people with growth mindset and intellectual curiosity.

**What role you will play in our team**
---------------------------------------

Execute Talent Acquisition processes effectively, including interview scheduling & logistics, job offer extensions, pre-employment, onboarding processes before Day 1, reporting, candidate communications and data collection.

**What you will do**
--------------------

*   Coordinate the talent acquisition administrative process over daily interactions with candidates, vendors and other parties to ensure an effective and efficient delivery of services
*   Coordinate right to work assessments for candidates where applicable
*   Coordinate internal verifications and endorsements for Medical Assessments, background checks, and pre-offer verifications
*   Process candidates and 3rd party payments as applicable
*   Update process flows and information in company designated Applicant Tracking System
*   Identify bottlenecks process and support remediation of service breaks

**About you**
-------------

We are looking for someone who has the following skills and qualifications, along with strong interest in becoming expert in Talent Acquisition processes.

*   Bachelor degree in: Business administration, Human resources
*   Languages: Advanced English, Portuguese (non-exclusive)
*   Attention to Detail: Ensuring accuracy in scheduling, documentation, and compliance.
*   Collaboration & ability to effectively work across multiple organizations, teams and vendors
*   Organizational Skills: Managing multiple tasks and deadlines efficiently.
*   Communication Skills: Clear and effective communication with candidates, hiring managers, and team members.
*   Problem-Solving Abilities: Addressing and resolving issues that arise during the recruitment process.
*   Customer Service Orientation: Providing a positive experience for candidates and internal stakeholders.
*   Experience in dealing with Talent acquisition processes/ systems preferred.
