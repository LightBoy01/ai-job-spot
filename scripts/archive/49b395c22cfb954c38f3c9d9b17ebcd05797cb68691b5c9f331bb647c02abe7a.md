**BE THE CEO OF YOUR JOB** 

As the Director of Insurance, you are responsibile for the strategic oversight and administration of the company’s insurance programs and claims management within the Risk Management Department. The position ensures that all insurance initiatives are fully aligned with enterprise risk management (ERM) objectives, while also advancing the integration of innovative technologies—including artificial intelligence tools such as ChatGPT—to enhance reporting, analytical capabilities, and organizational communication.

  

Your ability to own your work and drive success will make a direct impact on the communities we serve. 

  

**YOUR KEY RESPONSIBILITIES** 

*   Leads a team accountable for the performance and results of a function, industry and/or region.
*   Receives work in the form of objectives and strategically determines how to use resources to meet deadlines and goals.
*   Drives a culture of open communication.
*   Lead procurement, renewal, and administration of corporate insurance programs.
*   Makes recommendations regarding insurance renewals, budget allocations, policy endorsements, coverage structures, and claim strategies.
*   Provides input on large claim settlements, risk transfer methods, and broker/carrier relationships.
*   Integrate insurance strategies into the ERM framework and ensure accurate ERM reporting and dashboards.
*   Leverage AI tools, including ChatGPT, to improve reporting, streamline workflows, improve efficiency and enhance communication.
*   Monitor claims activity, analyze loss trends, and prepare executive-level reports.
*   Manage insurance budgets, allocations, and related financial reporting.
*   Supervise, mentor, and develop the Risk Management Specialist.
*   Serve as primary liaison with brokers, insurers, consultants, and internal stakeholders.
*   Provide strategic recommendations to the SVP of Risk Management on coverage, limits, and risk transfer strategies.
