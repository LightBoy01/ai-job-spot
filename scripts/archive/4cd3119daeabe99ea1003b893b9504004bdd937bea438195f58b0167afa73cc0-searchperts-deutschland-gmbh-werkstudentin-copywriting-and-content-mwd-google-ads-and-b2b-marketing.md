### Description\nSearchperts is a specialized Google Ads agency for e-commerce and medium-sized businesses, aiming to be Europe's most powerful Google Ads agency. Our core values are \"Built to Impress,\" \"Steady Growth,\" and \"Zero Ego.\" As a Working Student Copywriter, you will support our team in crafting high-performing texts for both Google Ads campaigns and our internal B2B marketing efforts.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Create ad texts for Google Search & Performance Max (headlines, descriptions, assets) following clear frameworks.\n*   Assist with landing page texts, hooks, and value propositions.\n*   Contribute to our internal content: LinkedIn posts, newsletter drafts, scripts for YouTube/Ads.\n*   Conduct research on clients, products, and target audiences to optimize copy.\n*   Maintain and structure our internal copy library (best practices, snippets, test ideas).\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Currently enrolled as a student (Communication, Marketing, Media, Business Administration, or similar field).\n*   Excellent German language skills, both written and spoken, with a clear and precise expression.\n*   Passion for language, headlines, and advertising texts; ideally, you have initial writing samples.\n*   Structured, reliable work ethic, capable of independent work within a small team.\n*   Interest in Performance Marketing (Google Ads, E-Commerce) and a strong willingness to learn.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding entry-level opportunity for a student eager to make a tangible impact in performance marketing. You'll gain hands-on experience in a dynamic, \"Office-First\" environment in Düsseldorf, working directly with management to receive clear feedback and accelerate your learning curve. The role offers significant responsibility within a small team, ensuring your contributions have a real impact on client performance. With flexible hours to accommodate your studies and a competitive hourly wage, it’s a perfect chance to apply your passion for language and marketing in a fast-paced, growth-oriented agency.
