### Description

**Sell with purpose. Inspire with technology.**

At SumUp, we empower small businesses with modern solutions for card payments and point-of-sale systems: simple, efficient, and flexible. Our devices enable merchants to securely accept payments anywhere, whether in-store, on the go, or at the weekly market.

As a **Field Sales Representative (m/f/d)** at SumUp, you'll receive a fixed base salary plus the opportunity for an **unlimited, performance-based bonus structure**. Your success is in your hands and will be rewarded. At the same time, you'll enjoy **full flexibility in your daily planning** and take responsibility for your own sales territory.

### Responsibilities

*   Actively approach local merchants in your sales territory.
*   Present modern payment products in a clear and practical manner.
*   Confidently handle objections and find individual solutions.
*   Build and maintain your own customer portfolio – long-term and personal.
*   Collaborate with a dedicated, goal-oriented Sales team.

### Qualifications

*   Motivation to be directly in the market and achieve results.
*   Basic technical understanding and the ability to explain smart products simply.
*   Solution-oriented thinking, self-initiative, and motivation.
*   Initial sales experience is an advantage; however, passion and willingness to learn are more important.
