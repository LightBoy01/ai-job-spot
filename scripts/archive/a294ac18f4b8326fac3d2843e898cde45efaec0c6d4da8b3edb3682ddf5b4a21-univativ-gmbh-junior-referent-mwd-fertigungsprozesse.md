### Description\nunivativ is a project and personnel service provider dedicated to offering exciting job opportunities to students and graduates at renowned companies, with a strong focus on fostering individual career development. This internal position within MAT3's Process Engineering team is ideal for individuals who have successfully completed their education in mechanical engineering, mechatronics, or vehicle technology, or who are looking to transition directly into full-time work to apply their theoretical knowledge in a practical setting.\n\nYour primary responsibilities will include maintaining and updating work plans and instructions within the PLM system, supporting the implementation and approval of changes in the ERP software to ensure current manufacturing data, and actively participating in change management tasks. You will also contribute to the introduction and optimization of digital manufacturing processes and assist process engineers and production teams in implementing digital solutions. A key aspect of your role will be the review and validation of changes to guarantee data quality before final release.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Process and update work plans and instructions within the PLM system.\n- Support the implementation and approval of changes in the ERP software to ensure manufacturing data remains current.\n- Handle tasks within change management tools and contribute to the introduction and optimization of digital manufacturing processes.\n- Assist process engineers and manufacturing teams in the analysis and implementation of digital solutions.\n- Review and validate changes to ensure data quality before final release.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed education in mechanical engineering, vehicle technology, mechatronics, or materials and process engineering.\n- Practical or theoretical experience in the design of manufacturing processes.\n- Initial knowledge of a CAD program is desirable.\n- Proficient German language skills (C1 level) and fluent English skills, both written and spoken (B2).\n- Time flexibility, along with a reliable and conscientious work ethic.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional entry-level opportunity for aspiring professionals looking to kickstart their career in process engineering within a dynamic environment. univativ and MAT3 are deeply committed to employee development, offering extensive targeted training and clear pathways to specialist or leadership roles. The hybrid work model, allowing up to two days remote work, coupled with a competitive salary and the chance to gain diverse industry experience across different companies and projects, makes this a highly attractive position for those eager to make a tangible impact and grow professionally.
