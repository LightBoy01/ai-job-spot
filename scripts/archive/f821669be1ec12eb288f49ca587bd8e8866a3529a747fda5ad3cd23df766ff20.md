We have an exciting opportunity at Penguin Random House for a **Legal Operations Associate, Litigation**, to support our Legal Department! This team is responsible for tackling a wide variety of challenges associated with being a global publisher.  

The ideal candidate has prior experience as a litigation paralegal; experience with or the ability to master e-discovery tools, AI-based operations tools, and internal systems; excellent organizational and communication skills, especially with respect to communicating and managing deadlines; and experience with managing legal operations (including internal processes, workflow, and systems). 

**Specific responsibilities include:**  

*   **Drafting**: Prepare the first draft of various litigation related documents, including responses & objection to subpoenas, interrogatory responses, settlement agreements, and responses to cease & demand letters. Familiarity with AI based tools like GCAI to support this workstream is a plus. 

*   **E-discovery**: Collect and prepare documents for production in connection with ongoing litigation and liaise with our e-discovery vendor(s).  

*   **Project management**: Take charge of organizing and proactively communicating (1) key matter deadlines; (2) records of prior document productions; (3) internal tracking documents. Act as the first point of contact for external vendors and manage vendor agreements.  

*   **Legal technology**: Assist with legal technology maintenance by mastering internal systems, troubleshooting basic issues, and supporting the implementation of new tools and processes.  

*   **Operational leadership**: Identify process inefficiencies within the legal department. Propose, develop and assist in implementing process improvements, including testing and implementing new technologies as appropriate.  

**Please apply if you meet the following qualifications:** 

*   Excellent organizational skills and strong attention to detail 

*   Ability to effectively prioritize and work on multiple projects at a time 

*   Excellent verbal and written communication skills 

*   Proven ability to work independently as well as part of a larger team 

*   “Growth Mindset”- Be comfortable taking initiative to use resources to find solutions to issues not yet encountered in order to grow our legal operations functions throughout the Legal Department, not just for litigation 

*   Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite, including Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and AI tools 

*   Minimum 2 years of working in a legal, technology, publishing, or media office environment; working as a paralegal (paralegal certificate not required), or legal operations a plus

**The salary range for this position is $56,000-$60,000.  All positions are currently eligible for annual profit award or bonus, subject to Company results.**  

 **This is a hybrid position with in-office responsibilities. We will consider candidates who can work from a commutable distance to our offices at 1745 Broadway, which includes the tri-state area (NJ, CT, & PA).**
