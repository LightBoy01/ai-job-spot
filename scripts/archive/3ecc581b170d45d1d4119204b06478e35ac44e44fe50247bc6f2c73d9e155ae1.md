Join the leader in entertainment innovation and help us design the future. At Dolby, science meets art, and high tech means more than computer code. As a member of the Dolby team, you’ll see and hear the results of your work everywhere, from movie theaters to smartphones. We continue to revolutionize how people create, deliver, and enjoy entertainment worldwide. To do that, we need the absolute best talent. We’re big enough to give you all the resources you need, and small enough so you can make a real difference and earn recognition for your work. We offer a collegial culture, challenging projects, and excellent compensation and benefits, not to mention a **Flex Work** approach that is truly flexible to support where, when, and how you do your best work.

The **Advanced Technology Group** (ATG) is the research division of the company. ATG’s mission is to look ahead, deliver insights, and innovate technological solutions that will fuel Dolby’s continued growth. Our researchers have a broad range of expertise related to computer science and electrical engineering, such as AI/ML, algorithms, digital signal processing, audio engineering, image processing, computer vision, data science & analytics, distributed systems, cloud, edge & mobile computing, computer networking, and IoT.

**About the Role**  
Sight and sound shape over 90% of human experience. At ATG, we believe data-driven technologies can transform these experiences—making them more personalized, resilient, and immersive. We’re seeking a visionary research leader with deep expertise in **AI, Data Science, and Distributed Systems** to drive innovation across our advanced technology initiatives.

**What You’ll Do**

As a senior leader, you will:

*   **Lead strategic research programs** focused on scalable data platforms, immersive media, collaborative systems, and intelligent visualization—spanning domains such as social media, enterprise communication, cinema, mobile, and gaming.
*   **Architect distributed systems** for training and deploying AI models at scale, with a focus on edge computing, data efficiency, and real-time responsiveness.
*   **Drive innovation** in AI/ML, natural language processing, data mining, and cognitive systems—balancing long-term vision with near-term deliverables.
*   **Mentor and grow world-class research teams**, fostering a culture of curiosity, rigor, and impact.
*   **Collaborate with global leadership** to shape ATG’s research portfolio, influence technology strategy, and align with business goals.
*   **Champion bottom-up innovation**, supporting researchers in developing novel ideas and translating them into high-impact technologies.
*   **Represent ATG externally**, contributing to academic and industry thought leadership through publications, talks, and strategic partnerships.

**What You Bring**

**Required Qualifications:**

*   PhD in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
*   12+ years of research experience in **Distributed Systems**, **Edge Computing**, and **AI Infrastructure**, with a proven track record of leading impactful initiatives.
*   Strong publication record in top-tier venues (e.g., NeurIPS, ICML, VLDB, SIGMOD, CVPR, ACL, KDD).
*   Deep expertise in one or more of the following:
    *   Distributed databases and storage systems
    *   Scalable AI/ML model deployment
    *   Natural language processing and computer vision
    *   Multimedia systems and network optimization
    *   Data mining, knowledge discovery, and visualization

**Leadership Competencies:**

*   **Strategic Vision**: Ability to anticipate technological shifts and craft research agendas that position ATG at the forefront of innovation.
*   **Influence & Collaboration**: Trusted advisor across engineering, product, and executive teams. Builds consensus and drives alignment.
*   **Team Building**: Proven success in recruiting, mentoring, and retaining top research talent. Skilled in translating business goals into team objectives.
*   **Execution Excellence**: Balances bold innovation with disciplined execution. Makes data-informed decisions and manages risk effectively.
*   **Communication**: Exceptional scientific writing and presentation skills. Comfortable briefing executives and engaging with external stakeholders.
