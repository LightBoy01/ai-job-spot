**Job Title:** Digital GBUs Commercial performance GenAI Product Owner

**Location:** Cambridge, MA

**About the Job**

At Sanofi, we’re committed to providing the next-gen healthcare that patients and customers need. It’s about harnessing data insights and leveraging AI responsibly to search deeper and solve sooner than ever before. 

Sanofi has recently embarked on a vast and ambitious digital transformation program. A cornerstone of this roadmap is the acceleration of its data transformation and of the adoption of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) solutions, to accelerate R&D, manufacturing, and commercial performance and bring better drugs and vaccines to patients faster, to improve health and save lives. 

We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced Analytics and AI Solution Product Owner who will work in collaboration with the central data GenAI team, focusing on leading a set of specific GenAI-based solutions to address performance analytics & insights challenges. The ideal candidate will possess a strong background in advanced analytics & AI/Gen AI, great leadership abilities, and a passion for driving innovation through data-driven solutions. You will play a critical role in shaping our Advanced analytics & AI/GenAI solution development, delivery, and overall digital transformation; implementing best practices (including responsible AI), and driving strategic initiatives to ensure the reliability, scalability, continuous improvement, and challenging the status quo. 

**Our vision for digital, data analytics, and AI** 

Join us on our journey to enabling Sanofi’s digital transformation through becoming an AI-first organization. This means: 

*   **AI Factory - Versatile Teams Operating in Cross-Functional Pods:** Utilizing digital and data resources to develop AI products, bringing data management, AI, and product development skills to products, programs, and projects to create an agile, fulfilling, and meaningful work environment. 
    

*   **Leading Edge Tech Stack:** Experience building products that will be deployed globally on a leading-edge tech stack. 
    

*   **World-Class Mentorship and Training:** Working with renowned leaders and academics in machine learning to further develop your skillsets. 
    

As a Product Owner, you will be a leader and act as a mini-CEO for this digital product, leading product strategy with the business, planning product roadmap, driving product delivery with the delivery team, and measuring/optimizing business impact.  

We are an innovative global healthcare company with one purpose: to chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives. We’re also a company where you can flourish and grow your career, with countless opportunities to explore, make connections with people, and stretch the limits of what you thought was possible. Ready to get started?

**Main Responsibilities:**

*   Lead the Development and Delivery of analytics, AI, and GenAI-based products or solutions to address Commercial Performance & Insights AA needs.
    
*   Own the product from a "What" and "Why" perspective & inspire the team, and bring a unique vision and perspective for what is possible
    
*   Define, create, and communicate product roadmap aligned with business objectives and supported by business cases, market research, customer data and competitive analysis
    
*   Be accountable for delivering incremental value every sprint & manage backlog prioritization of work
    
*   Drive the product vision and delivery via collaboration with GBUs and GTMC Teams: Work closely with GBU and GTMC (Go to Market Capabilities) teams to understand their requirements and help develop efficient and effective payer strategies using analytics and AI solutions, including GenAI components.
    
*   Optimize Use Cases: Participate in the assessment and recommendation of commercial use cases for analytics and AI solutions, ensuring alignment with strategic goals.
    
*   Lead and implement the Product Vision: Driving the design and implementation of the end-to-end product vision and delivery of the product, and helping to define the overall Commercial Advanced Analytics Strategy.
    
*   Standardize Digital Products: Leading the standardization process and delivering digital products with a focus on commercial analytics and AI portfolio.
    
*   Synergize with Central GenAI Team: Collaborate with the central GenAI team to develop products and solutions that leverage reusable components and ensure synergy across projects.
    
*   Manage Product Roadmap: Assist in managing the product roadmap, including budget planning, resource allocation, and feasibility assessments.
    
*   Ensure Performance and Value: Support efforts to ensure digital product performance and value realization.
    
*   Stakeholder Collaboration: Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather requirements, drive technology strategy, and deliver tailored reporting solutions.
    
*   Experience in the commercial performance analytics & insights and commercial excellence function, and hands on experience on complex GenAI product development is a must
    

**Ways of working**   

*   You will be a member of the Global Digital GBU Advance Analytics & AI Team.  
    
*   Own the product from a "What" and "Why" perspective & inspire the team and bring a unique vision and perspective for what is possible
    
*   You will foster partnerships and close collaboration between cross-functional teams (GBU Brand teams, Digital, business operations, market teams) and drive structured ways of working between teams to provide maximum value and efficiency to business teams.  
    
*   You will work closely and constructively with other development teams e.g., Accelerator, AI COEs, Data Engineering COE, Responsible AI COE) whether inside or outside the company.  
    
*   You will empower your teams to set up lean but effective teams and efficient local governance.   
    
*   You will build and develop a team that uses complementary strengths and develop novel solutions to core business problems
    
*   You will work closely with senior leadership for capability development + strategy formation on future evolution on predictive/prescriptive and agentic commercial analytics
