We’re looking for a user-centric designer to ensure design consistency and quality across our projects. In this role, you’ll refine and maintain existing UIs and design systems, making sure everything stays product-accurate and visually cohesive. You’ll also help communicate design updates clearly through lightweight presentations or annotated prototypes when needed.

If you’re detail-oriented, creative, and up to date with UX/UI trends—with experience in Figma, Adobe Suite, and AI tools—apply today and join our team.

**In this role you will:**

*   Ensure design accuracy, consistency, and adherence to high standards across all deliverables.
*   Refine and maintain existing UIs and design systems to guarantee visual and functional consistency across products.
*   Communicate design updates and improvements clearly through quick reviews, walkthroughs, or annotated prototypes when needed.
*   Actively participate in feedback sessions and incorporate input seamlessly into design refinements.
*   Manage multiple ongoing tasks, meeting deadlines while maintaining attention to detail and quality.
*   Stay current with design tools, software updates, and evolving UX/UI best practices.  
    Proactively identify and suggest user-centered improvements grounded in UX principles.
*   Leverage AI tools to streamline design workflows and enhance efficiency.

**Skills**

**Hard Skills:**

*   Proficiency in Figma (medium to advanced level)
*   Advanced proficiency in Adobe Suite, including editing
*   Knowledge of AI tools (intermediate level)
*   Bilingual in English (written and spoken)
*   Strong presentation and storytelling skills, including walkthroughs and high-fidelity prototypes
*   In-depth understanding and experience with large, stable design systems
*   Intermediate knowledge of UX with the ability to propose solutions
*   Interest in software/hardware trends and user experience innovation

**Soft Skills:**

*   Meticulous attention to detail
*   Excellent time management and organizational skills, with the ability to adapt to changing project needs
*   Strong communication and collaboration skills
*   Adaptable and open to diverse approaches and project types
*   Proactive, solution-oriented and able to work independently

**Experience**

*   2 to 4 years of proven UI experience.
*   Bachelor's degree in Graphic Design, Art Direction, or a related field.
*   A strong portfolio showcasing UI design principles applied in real-world projects, with an emphasis on UI-specific work.
