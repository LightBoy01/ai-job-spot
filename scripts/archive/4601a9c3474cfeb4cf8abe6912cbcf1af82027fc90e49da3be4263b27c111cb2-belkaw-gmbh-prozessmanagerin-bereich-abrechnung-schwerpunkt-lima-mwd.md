### Description\nAs a **Process Manager for Billing with a focus on LIMA (m/f/d)** at BELKAW, you will play a pivotal role in optimizing and transforming our billing processes for B2C and B2B customer segments. This involves ensuring efficiency, compliance with regulatory and audit requirements, and driving digital transformation towards a modern, future-proof system landscape. Your work will directly contribute to excellent process performance and the strategic development of billing within new market models, including process governance through defined standards and KPIs. This position involves working across various BELKAW service locations, including Bergisch Gladbach, Burscheid, Leichlingen, and Lindlar.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Conduct holistic analysis of existing billing processes for energy, water, and heat for BELKAW and its subsidiaries, identifying improvement potential and implementing comprehensive process optimizations in collaboration with adjacent departments, specifically within the LIMA context.\n- Deeply utilize and administer the LIMA billing software, including error analysis, parametrization, process monitoring, and the technical development of the system.\n- Develop, document, and implement efficient process flows to enhance data quality, transparency, and traceability, including establishing reports and key performance indicators (KPIs) for ongoing assessment of process quality and efficiency.\n- Provide training and support to employees on the usage of LIMA and adapted process flows.\n- Support the introduction of new billing system functionalities and digital tools within the LIMA environment.\n- Actively participate in cross-departmental projects to implement regulatory, IT, and process-related topics.\n- Design maximally customer-oriented processes in the context of the energy transition, considering both upstream and downstream process flows.\n- Contribute to the integration of LIMA into adjacent system landscapes to ensure continuous end-to-end processes.\n- Identify and implement automation potential (e.g., RPA, workflow tools) to increase efficiency.\n- Set up and maintain dashboards and KPIs for real-time monitoring of process quality.\n- Monitor and implement regulatory changes within the system, and prepare for and accompany internal or external audits.\n- Contribute to change management initiatives related to system introductions and process adjustments.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed commercial or IT-related vocational training, ideally with further training in process management.\n- Several years of professional experience in billing within the energy industry.\n- Very profound specialist expertise in LIMA billing software, including experience in system maintenance, analysis, and process design.\n- Proficient in MS-Office, especially Excel (Pivot tables, data analysis).\n- Strong analytical and process-oriented thinking, coupled with an enjoyment of working with data and systems.\n- Independent, structured, and solution-oriented working style.\n- High communication and team skills, along with a cross-departmental mindset.\n- Strong sense of responsibility and accuracy, particularly when handling sensitive data.\n- Experience in audit-proof work is advantageous.\n- Resilience and flexibility.\n- Experience with process automation, data analysis tools, or Business Intelligence systems is advantageous.\n- Understanding of customer experience and service quality in the context of billing.\n- Good moderation and presentation skills for effectively conveying complex process correlations.\n- Agile mindset and ideally experience with agile methodologies (e.g., Scrum, Kanban).\n- Enthusiasm for innovation and the ability to critically question existing practices and develop new solutions.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a critical and highly impactful role for a seasoned Process Manager with deep expertise in the LIMA billing system within the energy sector. BELKAW is looking for a proactive, analytical, and innovative individual contributor who can not only optimize existing processes but also drive the digital transformation and strategic evolution of their billing landscape. The emphasis on collaboration, regulatory compliance, and customer-centricity, combined with an agile mindset, suggests a dynamic environment where your contributions will directly shape the company's future in a rapidly changing market. This role offers a unique opportunity to blend technical LIMA expertise with strategic process design and automation, making it ideal for someone eager to make a significant operational and strategic difference.
