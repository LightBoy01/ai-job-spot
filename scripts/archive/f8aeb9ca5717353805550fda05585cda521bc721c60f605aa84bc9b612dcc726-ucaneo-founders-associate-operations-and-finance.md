Your mission
------------

As our Founder’s Associate, **your** **mission is to keep Ucaneo running smoothly behind the scenes.** You’ll oversee critical internal processes — from financial planning and grant coordination to operational administration such as inventory and visa support. You’ll help shape our company culture through well-organized events and smooth team operations. Working closely with the founders, you’ll build scalable systems that drive clarity, efficiency, and sustainable growth across the organization.

Key Responsibilties

*   **Financial Control** – manage company spending and invoicing through our financial tool, support budgeting and reporting cycles, and maintain up-to-date business plans and financial models.
*   **Grant & Funding Management** – prepare financial documentation for public grants and funding programs, ensuring compliance, timely submissions, and accurate reporting.
*   **Asset & Inventory Management** – establish and maintain an organized inventory of company assets, lab and office equipment, and procurement records.
*   **People & Operations** – support HR administration, onboarding, and visa processes while coordinating procurement, contracts, and vendor relations.
*   **Culture & Events** – organize company events, offsites, and team travel, fostering collaboration and a strong team culture across the organization.

Your profile
------------

Skills & Experience

*   Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Finance, Economics, or a related field.
*   2–3 years in a Finance, Controlling, or Operations role, ideally in a startup or fast-paced environment.
*   Strong proficiency in financial modeling and spreadsheet analysis, familiarity with expense management systems, and solid understanding of key SaaS metrics and business fundamentals. Advanced Excel/Google Sheets skills (mandatory).
*   Strong numerical and problem-solving abilities; data-driven decision-maker.
*   Highly structured, detail-oriented, proactive, and able to communicate complex information clearly.
*   Fluency in German and strong working proficiency in English. Workstyle and culture
*   Fast learning and quick to grasp complex topics
*   A team player with a hands-on mentality
*   Authentic and reflective self

*   Good listener and effective communicator in cross-sectional team
*   Curious and open-minded Other
