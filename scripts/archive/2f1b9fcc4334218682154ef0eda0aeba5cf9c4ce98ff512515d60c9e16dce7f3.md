**Primary Purpose:**

The Senior Account Manager will be responsible for driving sales, support business development, retail presentation, training and brand building activities of Kate Spade New York in SEA International. He/she is a strategic thinker who is able to solve problems with an analytical approach, provide recommendations and take decisions. He/she is able to gather and challenge information from different sources to make clear and impactful decisions in a timely manner. This role requires a hands on individual and will develop strong business relationships externally and internally to gain support and cooperation to drive the business forward.

**The successful individual will leverage their proficiency in Account Management to...**

1.  Own and drive the business

*   Responsible for achieving sales: Retail comp and total sales; wholesale shipments and for delivering on business development objectives
*   Act as market expert. Report on all key market developments including overall consumer and regional market trends and partner with Consumer Research and distributors on any consumer research activities
*   Own and Drive accounts orientation as well as continued learning and education
*   Build & nurture relationship with distributors, landlords, travel retail operators and airport authorities and influence them so that they understand and further embrace the Kate Spade brand vision
*   Manage Distributor Relationships for accounts. Day-to-day liaison between Kate Spade and the Distributor to ensure alignment of Kate Spade brand objectives.
*   Own in-store execution: Product Assortment, Visual Merchandising in partnership with Regional VM, Environment, and Staff Knowledge
*   Own monthly and quarterly business reviews
*   Own ensuring self and direct report has knowledge of product and all Kate Spade standards
*   Manage execution of key strategies (new introductions, product category initiatives, sales promotion, VM enhancements)
*   Collaborate to Develop multi-year business strategy for regions

2.  Sales and Marketing

*   Develop seasonal sales strategies, initiatives around key holidays to drive incremental business
*   Partner with Finance to develop annual plans, monthly estimates and OTB as well as risks & opportunities.
*   Partner with Buying Teal to optimize product offer
*   Identify and champion communication to Brand Director, Buying Team and Global Merchandising of large product development opportunities
*   Drive Seasonal Markets:

*   Communicate Kate Spade strategy, initiatives and goals
*   Ensure alignment with distributors

*   Guarantee receipt of purchase orders within established time frames
*   Manage inventory levels by continuously monitoring delete schedules and sell-through. Negotiate with accounts optimal inventory levels. Provide recommendation.
*   Support the Regional Marketing team building & implementing Marketing strategy & budget with each partner in alignment with the Kate Spade Global Campaign & contractual agreements aiming to maximizing sales results and driving Kate Spade Brand Building
*   Support the Marketing team to drive CRM programs including partnering with distributors to develop systematic name capture and leverage databases to drive Sales and Customer loyalty

3.  Business Development

*   Support Regional Director & GM in business development activities and processes, Partner with Architecture, Construction and VM on development and execution of New Doors as well as renewal/expansion of existing doors
*   Monitor development/expansion of the competition in respective markets with Architecture, Construction team and Partners.

4.  Business Management

*   Participate in Project team meetings, coordinate execution and implementation cross functionally
*   Manage issues on the ground and drive resolution with the Distributor.
*   Frequent travel to key markets to support retail execution, training and to build distributors relationships

**The accomplished individual will possess...**

*   7+ years’ work experience with International Wholesale and Retail business
*   Ability to Travel 50% of the time to different countries in SEA
*   Ability to successfully work with multiple stakeholders
*   Strong communication and influencing skills
*   Proven ability to drive business initiatives and dynamic business models
*   Experience developing and leading a high performance team
*   English and Mandarin fluency is required
*   Strong collaborator and team player
