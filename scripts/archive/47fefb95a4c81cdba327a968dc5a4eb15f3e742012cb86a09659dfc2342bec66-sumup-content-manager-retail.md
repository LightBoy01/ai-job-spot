### **About SumUp**

Small business owners are at the heart of everything we do. That's why we're creating powerful, easy-to-use financial solutions to help them run their businesses with confidence. ### **Team Description**

You'll be part of SumUp's **Marketing Development team**, focusing on driving growth through our **Retail and Partnerships** acquisition channels. This team leads marketing initiatives that drive merchant acquisition and partner engagement through new product launches and innovative campaigns with clear, high-quality, and compelling content across all assets, including retail packaging and POSM, co-branded campaigns, and digital channels.

In this role, you'll help shape how SumUp shows up in stores and with partners — from the way we tell our product stories on shelves and Amazon, to how we collaborate with industry partners. Reporting to our **Marketing Development Lead**, you'll work cross-functionally with Product Marketing, Trade Marketing, Business Development, Design and Brand to create content that effectively connects our brand to merchants, retailers, and partners at every touchpoint. This role begins with a two-year fixed-term contract, with potential for extension based on future business needs.

### **What You'll Do**

*   Own the **content planning and execution** for SumUp's Retail & Partnerships channels — across packaging, in-store materials, Amazon listings, partner pages, and co-branded campaigns  
      
    
*   Develop and maintain **messaging frameworks and narratives** tailored to retail merchants, verticals, and partner audiences  
      
    
*   Create **retail packaging and in-store content** that communicates product value clearly and drives merchant conversion  
      
    
*   Produce **partner and co-branded content**, including landing pages, onboarding materials, integration content, and promotional assets  
      
    
*   Manage **Amazon channel content**, crafting optimized listings, A+ content, and FAQs aligned with SEO and conversion best practices  
      
    
*   Support **event-related communications**, from booth messaging and flyers to follow-up campaigns  
      
    
*   Partner with **Localization** teams to ensure retail and partnership content is accurately adapted and consistent across markets  
      
    
*   Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders (Marketing, Design, Product) to ensure alignment across all content initiatives and touchpoints  
      
    

### **You'll Be Great for This Role If**

*   You have **3–6 years of experience** in retail content, B2B content, content strategy, or partner marketing — ideally in **FMCG, consumer electronics, or e-commerce**
*   You're skilled at **writing clear, engaging copy** for both retail and digital channels, balancing creativity with commercial impact  
      
    
*   You have **experience with marketplace environments** (Amazon strongly preferred) and know how to optimize listings for visibility and conversion  
      
    
*   You excel at **navigating co-branding and partnership requirements**, aligning multiple stakeholders and maintaining brand integrity  
      
    
*   You're **organized, detail-oriented, and proactive**, with the ability to manage several content projects simultaneously  
      
    
*   You're **data-informed** and eager to use performance insights to improve content effectiveness  
      
    
*   You have **native-level English fluency** and strong cross-functional collaboration skills
