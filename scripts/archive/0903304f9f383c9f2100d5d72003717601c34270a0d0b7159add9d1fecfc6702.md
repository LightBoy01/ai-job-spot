### Description

you.com is an AI-powered search and productivity platform designed to empower users with personalized, efficient, and trustworthy search experiences. We are on a mission to create the most helpful search engine in the world by combining advanced AI models with user-first principles like transparency, privacy, and user control. We’re building a team of innovators and problem-solvers who are passionate about shaping the future of AI and technology.

As a Senior AI Engineer on our Enterprise Solutions team, you will be at the forefront of this transformation. AI is revolutionizing how enterprises operate by surfacing hidden knowledge and automating complex workflows. In this role, you’ll act like a startup CTO for our customers, turning this vision into robust, production-ready AI solutions that ship fast and deliver measurable wins. You will get a front-row seat to the practical application of AI across the economy and play a key role in defining and shaping its future.

### Responsibilities

*   **Build and Ship:** Design and develop enterprise-grade AI applications, primarily in Python. Run comprehensive evaluations to validate models and package solutions for deployment on Kubernetes, AWS, or customer on-premises clusters.
*   **Work with Customers:** Lead discovery sessions to understand client needs, guide pilot projects from conception to completion, and ensure successful, high-impact deployments. This role is mostly remote with occasional on-site workshops.
*   **Run and Improve:** Monitor system performance and reliability post-deployment. Enhance core services for logging, billing, and authentication, and build internal tooling to automate repetitive tasks and improve efficiency.
*   **Share What You Learn:** Act as a crucial feedback loop to the core product team. Share insights on common patterns, customer pain points, and reusable modules to influence the future direction of the you.com AI platform.

### Qualifications

**Required:**

*   5+ years of experience writing solid, production-quality software.
*   Hands-on experience with LLMs and a solid understanding of machine learning fundamentals.
*   Strong customer empathy: the ability to listen, ask insightful questions, and translate real-world pain points into intuitive technical designs.
*   A product-minded approach with a focus on outcomes, comfort in shaping scope with product managers, and the ability to define success metrics and make trade-offs to accelerate learning.
*   Excellent communication skills, with the ability to engage effectively with executives and engineers both remotely and on-site.
*   A versatile problem-solver who thrives in ambiguous environments and enjoys rapid, continuous learning.

**Nice to Have:**

*   Experience in forward-deployed, solutions architect, or other product-oriented technical roles.
*   Exposure to regulated industries such as healthcare or finance.
*   Proficiency with Infrastructure as Code (e.g., Terraform, Pulumi) and experience across multiple cloud platforms.
*   Experience integrating with ERP, CRM, or other large enterprise systems.
*   Experience mentoring junior engineers or leading small technical teams.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "The description mentions acting like a 'startup CTO' for our customers. Can you elaborate on what that autonomy looks like in practice and how it differs from a more traditional forward-deployed engineering role?"**

That's a great question because it gets to the heart of what makes this role special. In many companies, a 'forward-deployed' role means you're primarily an integrator, fitting a pre-built product into a customer's environment. Here, 'startup CTO' is our shorthand for true ownership. From the first discovery call, you're not just listening for keywords; you're co-designing the solution. You have the autonomy to decide on the architecture, select the right models, and build the necessary tooling to solve the customer's core problem, not just the one they initially describe. It means you might spend one week whiteboarding a novel workflow with a client's VP of Operations and the next week deep in Python code making it a reality. You're not just a cog in a machine; you're the architect, the builder, and the trusted technical advisor, all in one.

**A candidate might also ask: "How is feedback from the Enterprise Solutions team actually incorporated into the core AI platform? What does that process look like, and can someone in this role truly influence the company's roadmap?"**

Absolutely. This isn't a role where your findings get tossed into a backlog abyss. The feedback loop is direct and constant. We have dedicated weekly syncs where the solutions team presents 'field reports'—what's working, what's not, and what we had to custom-build that really should be a platform feature. I've personally seen a custom data ingestion script I wrote for a finance client become the basis for a new, standardized connector in the core product two quarters later. The product managers see us as their eyes and ears, and because we can demonstrate real customer impact with our solutions, our voice carries significant weight in roadmap planning. When you show that a feature you developed unblocked a major deal or solved a widespread problem, people listen. You're not just using the platform; you're actively co-creating it.
