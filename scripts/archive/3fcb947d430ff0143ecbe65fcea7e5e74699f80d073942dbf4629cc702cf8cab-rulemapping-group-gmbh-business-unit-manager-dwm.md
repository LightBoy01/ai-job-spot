### Description
Rights Tech, based in Berlin, stands at the forefront of innovation where law meets technology. Our mission is to simplify, accelerate, and clarify complex bureaucratic processes for everyone. Leveraging our unique Rulemapping-Method and Rule Based AI, Rights Tech develops services designed to support dispute resolution bodies under European Digital Laws. We are a subsidiary of the Rulemapping Group, alongside Rulemapping Solutions GmbH.

We invite you to join us in driving this vision forward and revolutionizing the world of rules and laws!

### Responsibilities
As the Head of Rights Tech, you will lead a pivotal business unit with significant impact. Your responsibilities will include:

*   **Strategic Leadership**: Serve as the Head of Rights Tech, establishing it as a full-service provider for dispute resolution bodies across the EU, specifically addressing the Digital Services Act (DSA).
*   **Financial Oversight**: Hold full Profit & Loss (P&L) responsibility for Rights Tech GmbH, ensuring financial health and growth.
*   **Business Development & Sales**: Drive business development and sales efforts, identifying new market opportunities and crafting tailored solutions for EU dispute resolution bodies.
*   **Key Account Management**: Cultivate and strategically develop existing customer relationships, managing the entire customer lifecycle.
*   **Network Expansion**: Build a robust network of potential customers and key stakeholders within the legal and regulatory tech ecosystem.
*   **Inter-company Collaboration**: Collaborate closely with and steer the Product and Service Management teams at our sister organization, Rulemapping Solutions (our Tech Service Provider), to optimally position customer-centric solutions and ensure seamless implementation and long-term customer satisfaction.
*   **Brand Advocacy**: Represent Rights Tech's offerings at industry events and across social media channels.
*   **Team Building**: Establish and lead a high-performing team focused on Sales and Service Management.
*   **Service Excellence**: Oversee all service processes and the full-service management for B2B clients, encompassing Service Desk operations, Service Requests, SLA Management, Change, Incident, and Problem Management.
*   **Performance Optimization**: Proactively measure success by defining Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), analyzing user feedback, implementing continuous optimization strategies, and providing feedback to the Product and Service Provider teams.

### Qualifications
To excel in this challenging yet rewarding role, you should possess:

*   **Business Acumen**: Exceptional understanding of business economics, particularly concerning budget responsibility, profitability analyses, and the management of key business figures (P&L).
*   **Regulatory Expertise**: Deep knowledge of EU regulatory frameworks, especially in the context of the Digital Services Act (DSA), combined with practical experience in implementing technology-supported processes to ensure legal compliance.
*   **Scaling Experience**: Proven track record in building and scaling business units or organizational entities, specifically focused on delivering digital services to public or quasi-state institutions.
*   **Leadership & Stakeholder Management**: Demonstrated competence in leading interdisciplinary teams and adeptly managing complex stakeholder structures, including governmental authorities, private sector clients, and platform operators.
*   **Technical & Operational Foundations**: Solid understanding of digital platform architectures, operational process design, and the successful implementation of service excellence in technology-driven B2G (Business-to-Government) or B2B environments.
*   **Strategic & Diplomatic Skills**: High strategic thinking capability, coupled with a solution-oriented, assertive personality and strong diplomatic skills.
*   **Language Proficiency**: Excellent proficiency in both German and English (C1 level or higher).
