### Description\nCatella Investment Management (CIM), part of the Catella Group with nearly 500 employees across 12 European countries, is a leading platform for the creation and portfolio management of European residential and commercial real estate funds. With approximately 100 employees, CIM manages about €10 billion in assets under management (AuM), including project developments, invested in over 150 locations across 15 European countries.\n\nWe are immediately seeking a **Fund Controller (m/f/d)** to strengthen our Fund Controlling Team. This is a permanent, full-time (40h) position, based in **Berlin** or **Munich**.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Responsible for the implementation and monitoring of the \"Planning and Control Cycle\" (budgeting, monthly closing, annual reports, and forecasting) for SPVs and funds.\n- Analysis of fund and property performance, including performance attribution analysis.\n- Acting as a sparring partner for the Head of Fund Controlling, Fund Managers, and Transaction & Asset Managers.\n- Responsibility for liquidity management within the fund sector.\n- Contact person and coordination of foreign service providers, such as Company Administrators, Tax Advisors, Auditors, Catella Group alliance partners, KVGs, and AIFMs.\n- Support for the Transaction Team in acquisitions and dispositions (asset and share deals).\n- Coordination and execution of the financing process with banks.\n- Preparation of documents for the investment committee or investor presentations.\n- Coordination of corporate tax and other tax declarations.\n- Support in SPV Management (annual closing, audit, corporate law issues).\n- Monitoring of investment limits according to KAGB.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed degree in economics or business administration with a focus on finance and/or real estate.\n- 3 to 5 years of professional experience in controlling international real estate funds, other funds, or with an auditing firm.\n- Profound knowledge in accounting.\n- Good knowledge of accounting principles (IFRS, HGB, Tax GAAP).\n- Experience with public funds and related regulations is desirable.\n- Excellent communication skills.\n- Structured, solution-oriented, and independent working style.\n- Analytical thinking and ability to handle complex matters.\n- Proficient in Microsoft Office.\n- Fluent in German and very good English skills, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a compelling Mid-Senior opportunity for a diligent Fund Controller looking to make a significant impact within a leading European real estate investment platform. The role offers high levels of responsibility and engaging, demanding tasks within a dynamic, friendly, and motivated team. Catella Investment Management fosters an open corporate culture with clear, transparent communication and short decision-making paths. Candidates can expect extensive professional and personal development opportunities, both internally within the international Catella Group and through external training programs, supporting continuous career growth.
