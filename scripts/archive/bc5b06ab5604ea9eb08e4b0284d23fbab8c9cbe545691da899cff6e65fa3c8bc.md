We, the BMW Group, offer you an interesting and varied internship in the field of Data & AI Transformation. Our team at BMW Group IT drives the data and AI transformation across all business areas. Together, we apply cutting-edge AI technologies and focus on building productive, scalable applications in the fields of data science and artificial intelligence. During your internship, you will support us in ensuring top performance and contribute to shaping innovative digital solutions for the future of mobility.

*   You will support implementing generative AI applications.
*   Furthermore, you help to collaborate closely with various business units for continuous improvements.
*   In addition, you contribute to developing software based on a state-of-the-art public cloud data and AI platform.
*   Moreover, you will assist in gaining expertise for large scale AI applications.
**What should you bring along?**
*   Studies in computer science or a related field.
*   Experience with programming in Python, Java or similar languages.
*   Experience with public cloud platforms, APIs and databases.
*   Strong interest in emerging technologies and their evaluation and adoption.
*   Interest in conversational AI, generative AI and intelligent search engines.
*   Business-fluent English or German skills.
