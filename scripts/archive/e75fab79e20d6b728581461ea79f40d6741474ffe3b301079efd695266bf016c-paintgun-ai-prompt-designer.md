### Description
Paintgun is a dynamic creative agency on a mission to drive innovation by transforming growth plans into high-performing paid ads. Specializing in bold, visually captivating content, we partner with brands to deliver impactful campaigns that inspire, engage, and deliver results. At Paintgun, creativity meets strategy to redefine what’s possible in digital advertising.

We’re now expanding our creative frontier into the world of generative AI — and we’re looking for curious, creative minds to join us. We are seeking a passionate and experimental **AI Prompt Designer** to collaborate with our team on a freelance basis. This is an artist-led research role designed for those who live and breathe generative tools. If you spend your days experimenting with image, video, music, and language models — and love pushing creative boundaries — this is for you.

### Responsibilities
*   Explore and experiment with generative tools across image, video, music, and text.
*   Develop visually and conceptually strong outputs through thoughtful prompting.
*   Create moodboards, motion studies, and creative experiments that inspire future projects.
*   Document your process and share findings with the team.
*   Collaborate closely with artists, creatives, and strategists to shape new workflows and ideas.

### Qualifications
*   Strong creative taste and a sharp eye for design, aesthetics, and storytelling.
*   Deep interest in generative AI tools (Midjourney, Runway, Pika, Suno, ChatGPT, etc.).
*   Curiosity for the intersection of art, technology, and culture.
*   Self-motivated, experimental mindset — you enjoy learning through doing.
*   No formal degree required; show us your work, prompts, or projects that reflect your taste and curiosity (**attach portfolio**).
