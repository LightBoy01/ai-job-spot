### Description

REA Group is on a mission to change the way the world experiences property. As a purpose-driven and collaborative company, we've been recognized as one of Australia’s “Top 5” Best Workplaces for two consecutive years. Our people are the key to our success, fostering a thriving culture of high performance and care.

This role sits within the Content & Growth team, the storytelling division of REA Group. We are a dynamic group of journalists, content marketers, economists, and PR professionals dedicated to keeping Australians informed about the property market with reliable data and insightful stories.

As a Content Producer, you will be creating data-driven editorial content specifically for Property.com.au, Australia’s 3rd largest digital property platform. This research-focused platform aims to give users a complete, detailed picture of any property across the country, and your content will be central to achieving that goal.

### Responsibilities

*   Write data-driven editorial content for Property.com.au.
*   Collaborate with our economists, analysts, and editorial team to develop compelling data-driven angles for articles.
*   Leverage AI and automation tooling to surface market insights.
*   Maintain a content calendar and employ strong planning skills.
*   Provide engaging and timely content for weekly/monthly consumer communications.
*   Collaborate with the property news editor to sub-edit and build content from external contributors.
*   Upload and manage the delivery of content through our CMS.

### Qualifications

*   3+ years of experience in editorial content production and journalism.
*   A background in news, property, or economic reporting.
*   Ability to interpret data to tell a compelling story for consumers.
*   Demonstrated ability to write consumer-friendly and insightful content.
*   Experience using AI tooling.
*   Proven ability to build positive and collaborative relationships with internal stakeholders.
*   Knowledge of best practice SEO content production.
*   Experience using content management systems.
*   Proven ability to multi-task and work to tight deadlines.
*   Strong attention to detail and excellent written and verbal communication skills.
*   An understanding of how to best engage digital audiences and use the latest technologies to drive traffic.

### Author's Take

**A candidate might ask: "Beyond just writing articles, how does this Content Producer role directly influence the strategy and success of Property.com.au?"**

That’s a great question because it gets to the heart of what makes this role so exciting. You're not just a writer in a silo; you're the translator and storyteller for our most powerful asset: our data. You’ll be working hand-in-hand with our economists and data analysts, who uncover incredible trends about the market. Your job is to take that complex information and weave it into clear, compelling narratives that empower everyday Australians to make one of the biggest decisions of their lives. Your insights directly shape how users perceive the market and inform the platform's content strategy, making Property.com.au the go-to source for trusted, comprehensive property research. You're essentially the bridge between raw data and consumer confidence.

**Another great question would be: "The description mentions leveraging AI and automation. What does the culture of innovation look like day-to-day, and how does the team embrace new technologies?"**

We see AI not as a replacement for creativity, but as a powerful partner. In practice, this means we're actively experimenting with tools that can help us sift through massive datasets to find the 'needle in a haystack'—that one surprising insight that becomes the foundation of a fantastic story. Your day might involve using an AI tool to spot emerging suburb trends, which you then verify and enrich with your journalistic skills and human perspective. The culture here is very much about curiosity and learning. We run 'Hackdays' where you can bring big ideas to life, and there's a real encouragement to test, learn, and share what you discover. We believe the best content comes from combining the scale of technology with the irreplaceable nuance of a skilled storyteller.
