**Senior Business Operations Manager**
--------------------------------------

**Remote Location: San Francisco Bay Area or Seattle, WA**
----------------------------------------------------------

**Position Summary**
--------------------

HackerOne is seeking a Senior Business Operations Manager to be a force multiplier for our business. This is a high-impact and high-visibility role at the intersection of strategy and execution - you’ll work closely with cross-functional leaders to translate vision into operational reality and solve complex, high-leverage strategic problems that are among the company’s most pressing priorities.

Whether you’re diagnosing root causes of an issue, re-architecting a process, or supporting the execution of a critical project, your work will create clarity where it doesn’t yet exist, uncover insights, and accelerate decision-making. You'll use first-principles thinking to solve complex problems, leverage data to drive decisions, and spearhead cross-functional initiatives from framing and analysis to planning and execution. This role reports to our VP of Business Operations.

At HackerOne, we embrace a Flexible Work approach that gives us the freedom to do our best work while also fostering the connections and community that make us stronger. Reflecting this philosophy, this is a remote role targeted for candidates within ~50 miles of San Francisco, CA (preferred) or Seattle, WA. We believe this balance of proximity and flexibility gives Hackeronies the chance to occasionally come together – fostering collaboration, connection, and in-person moments that enrich our culture – while still preserving the benefits of remote work.

**What You Will Do**
--------------------

*   Drive cross-functional strategic initiatives - from opportunity framing and analysis to planning and outcome realization.
    
*   Diagnose, optimize, or build processes to improve cross-functional execution and drive scale.
    
*   Collaborate and act as a trusted thought partner with cross-functional teams to ensure strategic clarity and operational alignment across the organization.
    
*   Monitor business performance across teams and products - identify key trends, root cause issues, and surface actionable insights to inform strategic decisions.
    

**Minimum Qualifications**  
----------------------------

*   6+ years relevant work experience in management consulting, business operations, or strategy in enterprise technology.
    
*   Skilled in first principles problem solving: Able to quickly figure out how things work, break down complex issues to their core, and build effective, pragmatic solutions.
    
*   Strong analytical and business acumen: Hands-on with sourcing evidence and analyzing data to inform decisions while balancing speed, judgment, and impact.
    
*   AI-first thinking: Proactively seek ways to improve workflows with AI or redesign workflows to be AI-native, and leverage AI tools to improve execution.
    
*   Experience driving cross-functional initiatives to impactful outcomes with clarity & adaptability in ambiguous or fast-evolving contexts.
    
*   Skilled in executive-level communication, translating complex information into clear, persuasive narratives and presentations that influence stakeholders across all levels and functions.
    
*   Proven ability to multiply impact by leading through influence, enabling ownership, building trust, and collaborating across teams.
    

**Preferred Qualifications**
----------------------------

*   Experience working in the cybersecurity industry, with a strong understanding of customer needs and business models.
    
*   Technical expertise with database and analytics tools.
