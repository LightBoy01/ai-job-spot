**About the role:** 

We are seeking a skilled Sr Software Engineer to join our Cloud Centre of Excellence Team((**Data & AI/ML Team)**. This role focuses on building and scaling data-driven and AI/ML workloads on AWS, leveraging cutting-edge cloud services, automation, and infrastructure-as-code practices. The ideal candidate will have hands-on expertise in AWS services, GenAI (Bedrock), ML (SageMaker), automation scripting, and modern DevOps tools. 

**What you’ll do:**  

*   Collaborate with a cross-functional team of application developers, operations engineers, architects to understand complex product requirements and translate them into automated solutions that you build. 
    

*   Collaborate with Data Scientists, ML Engineers, and Cloud Architects to optimize solutions for performance, cost, and scalability. 
    

*   Ensure best practices in cloud security, compliance, and monitoring are followed. 
    

*   Support automation of operational tasks and proactive monitoring of AI/ML workloads. 
    

*   Lead technical discussions to ensure solutions are designed for successful deployment, security, and high availability in the cloud 
    

*   Design, implement, and maintain server, storage, network, and security infrastructure as code. 
    

*   Build reusable pipelines for application deployments. 
    

*   Write and maintain code for automating the creation of scalable/resilient systems/infrastructure with a focus on immutability and containers. 
    

*   Develop, implement, and test automated data backup and recovery, and disaster recovery procedures across multiple regions. 
    

*   Write and maintain clear, concise documentation, runbooks and operational standards including infrastructure diagrams. 
    

*   Drive Automation efforts across the organization utilizing Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform, Configuration Management, and Continuous Integration (CI) / Continuous Delivery (CD) tools such as Jenkins. 
    

*   Design and deploy scalable, highly available, and fault tolerant distributed systems. 
    

*   Continuously identify, adopt, & refine best practices. 
    

*   Educate/mentor product teams and junior engineers. 
    

****What you will need:****

MS or BS degree in computer science or related focus, or equivalent experience with 4-7 yrs in AWS cloud, infrastructure management and automation developments. 

****Must have:****

*    Hands-on expertise in AWS services: Bedrock, SageMaker, EMR, Lambda, IAM, S3, Step Functions, CloudWatch, Serverless services etc.  
    

*   Experience with Terraform (IaC) for provisioning and managing cloud resources. 
    

*   Good knowledge of CI/CD tools (Jenkins) and pipeline automation. 
    

*   Familiarity with AI/ML concepts, MLOps workflows, and cloud-based ML lifecycle management. 
    

*   Understanding of Agentic AI concepts and experience in implementing autonomous/Agent driven AI workflows.  
    

*    Strong programming skills in Python, proficiency in Shell scripting. 
    

****Who you are:****

*   Bachelor’s degree or foreign equivalent degree in Computer Science or a related field required
    
*   Excellent communication and prioritization skills.
    
*   Able to work independently or within a team proactively in a fast-paced AGILE-SCRUM environment.
    
*   Owns success – Takes responsibility for successful delivery of the solutions.
    
*   Strong desire to improve upon their skills in software development, frameworks, and technologies
