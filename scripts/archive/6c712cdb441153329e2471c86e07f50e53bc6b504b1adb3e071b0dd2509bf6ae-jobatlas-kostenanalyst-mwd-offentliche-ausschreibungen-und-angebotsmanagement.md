### Description

Are you looking to enable strategic decisions with your analyses? Join a leading social enterprise based in **Essen, Germany**, that operates social facilities across Germany, providing care for youth, homeless individuals, and refugees. This is an impactful opportunity to contribute to a socially relevant mission.

As a **Cost Analyst (m/f/d) – Public Tenders and Offer Management**, you will be instrumental in enabling sound business decisions on a solid economic foundation. With your keen eye for detail, analytical expertise, and understanding of complex financial relationships, you will be the specialist for financial modeling, calculation, and economic evaluation of bids within the public sector.

### Responsibilities

*   Conduct financial models, risk, opportunity, and sensitivity analyses, presenting them visually.
*   Prepare business analyses to inform offer, cost, and pricing strategies.
*   Ensure up-to-date knowledge of current requirements, relevant legislation, and procurement guidelines in the public sector.
*   Develop client financial reports using the internal financial model, coordinate them, and clarify open questions.
*   Review accounting treatments according to IFRS in coordination with the group finance department.
*   Support the implementation of cost-efficient model reviews and peer reviews to ensure compliance and integrity.
*   Contribute to data preparation and data modeling to support new business proposals and cross-functional projects.

### Qualifications

*   **Required Skills & Experience:**
    *   Completed degree in Finance and relevant professional experience.
    *   Profound knowledge in financial reporting and financial modeling.
    *   Knowledge of detailed cost modeling processes, TUPE (Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment)) and comparable European regulations.
    *   Experience with corporate governance processes and Microsoft Excel.
    *   Excellent analytical skills, a quick grasp of new concepts, and clear, effective communication.
    *   Thorough and detail-oriented work ethic – both independently and as part of a team.
    *   Ability to remain calm and effective even in stressful situations.
    *   Fluent German language skills (at least C2).
*   **Benefits:**
    *   Permanent full-time employment directly with the company.
    *   An unlimited employment contract.
    *   A strong salary that truly values your work.
    *   Flexible working hours with flexitime.
    *   A responsible role in a socially relevant environment.
    *   An exciting corporate group environment with intercultural exchange.
    *   Access to coaching, further training opportunities, and 29 vacation days.
