### Description\nJoin our partner company in Munich as a Freight Forwarding Agent (m/w/d). In this key role, you will be responsible for orchestrating the seamless flow of national and international goods, ensuring efficiency, cost-effectiveness, and customer satisfaction. This is a long-term, permanent position within a modern environment that values work-life balance and employee development.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Organize and manage the smooth flow of national and international goods.\n- Plan and dispatch transports in coordination with suppliers, customers, and transport companies, considering deadlines, costs, and efficiency.\n- Conduct price and condition negotiations with freight forwarders, compare offers, and actively contribute to the optimization of logistical processes.\n- Communicate with all involved parties in German and English, ensuring transparent order processing.\n- Create, review, and manage transport and customs documents, ensuring complete documentation of all shipping processes.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed vocational training as a Freight Forwarding Agent (Speditionskaufmann), Clerk for Freight Forwarding and Logistics Services, or a comparable qualification such as Industrial Clerk or Wholesale and Foreign Trade Clerk.\n- Ideally, professional experience in transport dispatch, freight forwarding, or logistics management.\n- Good knowledge of MS Office.\n- Structured work style, ability to maintain an overview in hectic phases, and strong organizational skills.\n- Very good German and good English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced logistics professional to take on a crucial role in a company that truly values its employees. With flexible working hours, a central Munich location, and a supportive \"Du-Kultur\" where trust and development are paramount, it offers a stable and enriching career path. The focus on long-term commitment and continuous learning makes it ideal for someone looking to grow within a positive and modern work environment.
