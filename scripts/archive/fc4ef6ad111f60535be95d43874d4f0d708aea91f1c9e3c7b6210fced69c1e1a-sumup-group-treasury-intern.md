* * *

**About the Team**
------------------

Our **Group Treasury Team** sits at the centre of SumUp's global financial operations — managing liquidity, funding, and risk across more than 30 markets. We ensure that the company's financial resources are safe, optimally allocated, and support our fast-paced growth.

As a **Group Treasury Intern**, you'll play a hands-on role in supporting daily treasury activities, helping to strengthen our global cash management and forecasting processes. You'll gain exposure to complex financial operations, collaborate with international teams, and contribute to projects that directly influence SumUp's financial strategy.

[Berlin (Landing Page)](https://www.sumup.com/careers/offices/berlin/)

* * *

**What You'll Do**
------------------

*   Support the Group Treasury team in **day-to-day operations** such as payment processing and funding transfers.
    
*   Contribute to **cash flow forecasting** and analysis across SumUp's global legal entities.
    
*   Assist in **bank relationship management**, helping to maintain accurate records and improve internal tools (e.g., transition of documentation into Notion).
    
*   Collaborate on defining and enhancing **hedging strategies and investment policies**.
    
*   Partner with global teams including **FP&A, Controlling, Accounting, Tax, and Latin America Treasury** to ensure smooth treasury operations.
    
*   Drive or support **process improvement projects**, identifying opportunities to optimise workflows and automation.
    
*   Conduct **treasury-related analyses** that inform wider financial and business strategy.
    

* * *

**You'll Be Great for This Role If**
------------------------------------

*   You're genuinely interested in **Finance and Treasury**, and excited to learn in a fast-moving fintech environment.
    
*   You bring a **problem-solving mindset** and strong analytical skills.
    
*   You're confident working with data and can translate numbers into insights.
    
*   You're detail-oriented, well-organised, and proactive about driving improvements.
    
*   You're eager to collaborate with international teams and build your professional network.
    
*   _(Bonus)_ You have some exposure to **SAP S/4HANA, SAC, or Treasury Management Systems (TMS)**.
