### Description\nAre you an experienced professional in IT validation projects eager to take on significant responsibility? Your expertise in the GxP environment is highly sought after here, where you will actively shape the future of Computerized System Validation (CSV) in the pharmaceutical and medical technology sectors. In this role, you will independently lead compliance and CSV projects, develop tool-supported validation processes, and apply your knowledge in validating both cloud-based and on-premise systems. You will work closely with clients and leverage innovative technologies to achieve these goals.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Strategically plan and independently lead IT compliance and CSV projects within regulated industries.\n- Develop, implement, and optimize tool-supported validation processes.\n- Conduct GAP and risk analyses, ensuring adherence to GxP guidelines (FDA, EU-GMP, GAMP5).\n- Validate cloud or on-premise IT systems in agile or classic project environments.\n- Consult clients on regulatory requirements and continuous improvements in validation processes.\n- Utilize and contribute to the introduction of innovative technologies, such as AI-supported validation or modern ALM tools.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A minimum of 3-5 years of professional experience in the GxP environment and strong knowledge of regulatory requirements (EU/US, FDA CFR Title 21 Part 11, EU GMP Annex 11/15, GAMP5).\n- Proven experience in independent project work, including the coordination and strategic alignment of validation projects.\n- Familiarity with agile methodologies (SCRUM) and experience with tools like JIRA and Confluence.\n- A structured, analytical approach to work and a high degree of quality awareness.\n- Excellent communication skills in German (C1-level) and English.\n- Experience with ALM tools or AI-supported validations is a plus.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role offers a fantastic opportunity for an experienced IT Validation specialist to truly make an impact within a stable, family-friendly company with a 30-year track record. EXCO prioritizes employee well-being and growth, offering flexible working hours, mobile work options, and comprehensive support through onboarding, individual coaching, and further education. Beyond attractive compensation, benefits like kindergarten subsidies and company events highlight a culture that values work-life balance and collaboration. This is a chance to lead innovative projects in a critical and crisis-proof industry, contributing to the future of GxP compliance.
