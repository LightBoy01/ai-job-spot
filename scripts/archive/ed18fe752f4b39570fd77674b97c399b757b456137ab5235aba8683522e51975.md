We are seeking an experienced **Learning and Content Program Manager** to own and optimize our Learner and Content Management platform. In this role, you will implement content governance frameworks, lead cross-functional projects, and drive platform adoption for our global Go-to-Market (GTM) teams. You will work closely with Sales, Marketing, Product, and Operations stakeholders to ensure our enablement solutions are streamlined, scalable, and aligned with business priorities. If you are passionate about content management, process automation, and enabling measurable business impact, we invite you to join our team. 

**What You’ll Do** 

*   **Organize platform content** for easy access and search. 
    

*   **Enforce content governance** through regular audits and archiving. 
    

*   **Lead projects** for onboarding, certifications, and proposals. 
    

*   **Align platform strategy** with GTM goals and stakeholder needs. 
    

*   **Track adoption and engagement**; share insights with leadership. 
    

*   **Automate workflows and templates** to boost efficiency. 
    

*   **Handle content requests and quality control** for consistency. 
    

*   **Train and support users** on platform best practices. 
    

*   **Drive feature adoption and integrations** with teams and vendors. 
    

*   **Maintain documentation** and resolve complex user issues. 
    

**What You’ll Bring** 

*   **Experience managing enablement platforms** (Docebo & Seismic preferred) 
    

*   **Strong organizational, analytical, and communication skills** 
    

*   **Strong project and program management skills**—including the ability to oversee multiple projects, set deadlines, and coordinate stakeholders effectively 
    

*   **Forward-thinking mindset** with a passion for enablement technology 
    

*   **Solid AI knowledge** and AI Engineering experience 
    

*   **Ability to manage multiple projects and stakeholders** 
    

*   **Familiarity with sales enablement and content governance best practices**
