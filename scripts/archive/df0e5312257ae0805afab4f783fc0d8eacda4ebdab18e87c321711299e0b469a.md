**About the Role**

We are seeking a **Director of Engineering** to lead and scale our Security Operations Automation and AI-driven engineering functions. This is a **senior leadership role** at the intersection of **cybersecurity, AI agents, and intelligent automation**, where you will drive innovation and execution for next-generation Security Operations capabilities.

Your teams will be responsible for designing, building, and scaling **cloud-native, AI-powered, and autonomous systems** that redefine how Security Operations Centers (SOCs) operate. From **agentic frameworks, reasoning systems, and ML optimization** to **system test, automation, and detection-as-code pipelines**, you will guide strategy, build exceptional teams, and deliver technical excellence at scale.

This role requires a **visionary leader** with deep technical expertise and proven experience building **high-performing engineering organizations** in **cybersecurity and AI/SaaS domains**.

**Key Responsibilities**

*   **Strategic Leadership & Vision**
    *   Define and execute the long-term engineering strategy for Security Operations Automation with a focus on AI, intelligent agents, and large-scale automation.
    *   Partner with executive leadership and cross-functional leaders (Product, Research, Threat Intelligence, Security Operations, Data Science) to align engineering priorities with business outcomes.
*   **Team Building & People Leadership**
    *   Build, scale, and inspire a **world-class engineering organization** with a focus on hiring, mentoring, and retaining top technical talent.
    *   Foster a culture of **innovation, collaboration, and accountability** across global teams.
*   **AI & Security Engineering**
    *   Lead the design and delivery of **AI-powered automation systems**, including reasoning agents, MCP (Model Context Protocol) integrations, LangChain/LangGraph-based frameworks, and autonomous task execution engines.
    *   Drive innovation in **machine learning optimization, detection pipelines, and autonomous decision-making** to accelerate incident response and reduce manual SOC workloads.
    *   Oversee the development of secure, scalable, and reliable software in a **SaaS environment**, leveraging containers, Kubernetes, and cloud-native platforms.
*   **Technical Excellence & Execution**
    *   Own end-to-end delivery of automation and AI-driven features: response playbooks, enrichment services, agent-based orchestration, and intelligent testing frameworks.
    *   Ensure engineering quality, scalability, and operational excellence through best practices in architecture, agile methodologies, and continuous improvement.
    *   Collaborate on integrating AI/ML with **system test and automation functions** to validate SOC workflows at scale.
