### Description\nArgus Search is exclusively recruiting a motivated Head of Accounting for a top company in Berlin, specializing in HR and finance placements. This permanent position offers an exciting, future-proof environment.\n\nThe role comes with an attractive salary package, ample opportunities for professional and personal development, and the freedom to contribute ideas. You'll enjoy a modern workplace with excellent transport links and a flexible hybrid work concept.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Leadership of the accounting team (currently 6 employees)\n- Responsibility for monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements according to HGB and US-GAAP\n- Independent preparation of consolidated financial statements\n- Ensuring proper bookkeeping and participation in account reconciliation and maintenance\n- Monitoring general ledger accounting\n- Responsibility for tax matters\n- Contact person for auditors, tax advisors, and internal departments\n- Optimization and further development of accounting processes and support for digitalization projects\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed commercial training, ideally with further qualification as an accountant (Bilanzbuchhalter) or a degree in economics, business administration, or a comparable qualification with a focus on accounting/finance\n- Several years of professional experience (10+ years) in accounting, ideally in a comparable position\n- Profound knowledge in the preparation of monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements according to HGB (ideally also US-GAAP)\n- Profound knowledge of German tax law\n- Proficient in MS Office, especially Excel, and experience with ERP systems and accounting software\n- Analytical and structured way of working with a high affinity for numbers and problem-solving skills\n- Self-initiative, responsibility, and a reliable, diligent way of working\n- Fluent German and English skills, both written and spoken\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a stellar opportunity for an experienced accounting professional ready to step into a leadership role with significant impact. The emphasis on process optimization, digitalization, and team leadership, combined with a flexible hybrid work model and clear growth paths, makes this an exceptionally attractive position for someone looking to shape the future of finance within a dynamic company in Berlin.
