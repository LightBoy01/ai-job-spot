**Why work at Nebius  
**Nebius is leading a new era in cloud computing to serve the global AI economy. We create the tools and resources our customers need to solve real-world challenges and transform industries, without massive infrastructure costs or the need to build large in-house AI/ML teams. Our employees work at the cutting edge of AI cloud infrastructure alongside some of the most experienced and innovative leaders and engineers in the field.

**Where we work  
**Headquartered in Amsterdam and listed on Nasdaq, Nebius has a global footprint with R&D hubs across Europe, North America, and Israel. The team of over 800 employees includes more than 400 highly skilled engineers with deep expertise across hardware and software engineering, as well as an in-house AI R&D team.

### **The role** 

We are seeking a senior product leader to help us shape and grow a new data platform for AI builders. This is a chance to join early, work hands-on with engineering and design, and influence how ML engineers and researchers manage data, train AI models, and build agents. You will bring product leadership and clarity by setting direction, validating ideas, and driving execution while collaborating closely with other leaders across product, engineering, and go-to-market. Your work will be key in making the platform a meaningful differentiator for our customers and partners. 

You’re welcome to work in our colocation in **Modiin, Israel**.

**Your responsibilities will include:** 

*   Define and execute the vision and roadmap for the data platform, aligning with company goals.
*   Engage directly with users and partners to understand needs, validate ideas, and drive product–market fit. 
*   Collaborate with engineering, research, design, and go-to-market teams to deliver high-quality product outcomes.
*   Monitor the market landscape to identify trends, competitive moves, and new opportunities.
*   Lead product experiments and rapid iteration cycles — from prototype to production-ready solutions.
*   Support go-to-market efforts by equipping sales, solutions, and marketing with the right product context.

**We expect you to have:** 

*   5+ years of product management experience, with a focus on developer-first products or data  
    infrastructure.
*   Proven track record of launching impactful products in fast-moving markets, working closely with  
    engineering and design.
*   Strong understanding of AI, data management, and cloud-native architectures; hands-on technical  
    background is a plus.
*   Degree in computer science, engineering, or a related field, with the ability to dive deep into technical  
    concepts.
*   Skilled at driving cross-functional initiatives and navigating complex stakeholder environments.
*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills — able to clearly explain technical concepts to both  
    technical and non-technical audiences.

**It will be an added bonus if you have:** 

*   Leadership experience in a high-growth technology company.
*   People management experience (building and mentoring product teams).
*   Exposure to generative AI products, workflows, or research environments.

**What we offer** 

*   Competitive salary and comprehensive benefits package.
*   Opportunities for professional growth within Nebius.
*   Hybrid working arrangements.
*   A dynamic and collaborative work environment that values initiative and innovation.

We’re growing and expanding our products every day. If you’re up to the challenge and are excited about AI and ML as much as we are, join us!
