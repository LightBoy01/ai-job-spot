### Description
Join a pioneering company at the forefront of the energy transition, specializing in photovoltaic systems, smart meters, and electric vehicle charging infrastructure. We are seeking a dedicated Technical Customer Support Specialist to be the primary point of contact for our B2C and B2B customers. In this vital role, you'll ensure the smooth operation of installed systems, provide expert technical guidance, and contribute to optimizing our support processes, all within a supportive and innovative environment. This is an opportunity to make a tangible impact by facilitating the adoption of sustainable energy solutions and driving customer satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
*   Serve as the main point of contact for B2C and B2B customers following contract completion and system installation (PV systems, smart meters, charging infrastructure).
*   Handle technical customer inquiries and issues efficiently via phone, email, or our dedicated ticketing system.
*   Coordinate effectively with project managers, partner management, and, if necessary, the grid department to resolve complex issues.
*   Provide essential support for technical clarifications, troubleshoot error messages, and manage maintenance requests.
*   Actively contribute to the continuous optimization and improvement of internal customer support processes and workflows.

### Qualifications
*   Completed technical training (e.g., electrician, mechatronics technician, or similar qualification) or equivalent professional experience.
*   Profound technical understanding in the fields of photovoltaics and electromobility.
*   Excellent communication skills, a structured and organized approach to work, and a genuine enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Proficiency in using digital tools, including CRM systems and ticketing software.
*   A collaborative team player with a hands-on mentality and a strong commitment to quality.
