**About our team**
------------------
BCR is the place where you learn, grow, and contribute to shaping the future of banking. We are recognized as a school of excellence in banking, a top employer in the banking sector, and a team open to learning, where every colleague feels seen and valued.
We are an organization that values people and supports them in having a life that brings fulfillment, security, and balance.
The Data, Analytics & AI Division is in charge to transform BCR in a data driven organization being responsible for end-to-end delivery of AI, BI & Advanced analytics solutions, data governance and quality. We combine business and technical knowledge at the same time, and we coordinate and define data strategy for the entire organization.
We are looking for a Product Owner to take charge of the Business Growth Data vision, initiatives, roadmap and its excellent implementation team, in order to maximize the product value and features.
**How you will contribute**
---------------------------
*   Be a trusted partner for business representatives and stakeholders, collaborating closely to understand their needs and turning them into real added value for your data product;
*   Clearly define and communicate the Product Goal, keeping everyone aligned and inspired;
*   Lead the team to design and deliver the product roadmap, ensuring visibility and transparency at every step;
*   Create and manage the product backlog, prioritizing features based on business value and regulatory requirements; confidently add or decline change requests that don’t fit the product vision;
*   Oversee the product across its full lifecycle, actively engaging in planning sessions, refinements, reviews and retrospectives;
*   Monitor product objectives through analytics and status reports, anticipating and addressing issues before they affect key results;
*   Build trust and transparency by making the team’s progress and potential impediments visible, and work side-by-side with stakeholders to make the product shine;
*   Act as a leader and role model — the team’s flag carrier and shield — creating the focus and space they need to excel.
We believe great products are built not only with skills and passion, but also with openness and resilience.
Bring your curiosity, your courage to embrace change, and your ability to stay focused even when challenges arise.
**What will help you be successful**
------------------------------------
*   Bachelor's degree in economics, technical, mathematics or computer science;
*   Good knowledge regarding banking products and processes. Minimum 2 years’ experience in banking;
*   Good knowledge of Data Management and Data Warehouse software development implementation flow.
*   Minimum 2 years’ experience working in a collaborative environment, delivering data frameworks and BI solutions;
*   Minimum 2 years’ experience in delivering complex projects in agile frameworks;
*   Exceptional communication and organizational skills;
*   Team player with leadership skills and great talent for motivating people and make them work on a common purpose.
