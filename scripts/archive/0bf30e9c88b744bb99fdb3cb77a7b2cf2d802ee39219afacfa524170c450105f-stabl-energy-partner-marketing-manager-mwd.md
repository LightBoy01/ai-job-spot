### Description
As a Partner Marketing Manager (m/f/d) at STABL Energy, you will be instrumental in elevating our partner marketing to an unparalleled level, extracting maximum value from our strategic alliances. Through innovative co-marketing campaigns, engaging partner events, and the development of a smart partner portal, you will empower our sales and installation partners, fostering stronger connections and greater success. Together, we are poised to dominate the European market for sustainable and reliable battery storage systems, accelerating the adoption of renewable energies and significantly contributing to grid stability.

STABL Energy, founded in 2019, is recognized as one of the most innovative startups in the energy transition. We are proud winners of the ees Award 2022 and were named among the Top 100 energy startups worldwide. Supported by leading tech VCs from Germany and Switzerland, we are actively shaping the future of energy, offering a dynamic environment where your ideas drive real impact.

### Responsibilities
*   Build and nurture a robust partner network in Germany and Austria, with the strategic vision to expand into further European countries.
*   Conceive and execute co-marketing activities, webinars, and partner events to foster growth and revenue with and through our partners.
*   Develop and manage our partner portal in close collaboration with cross-functional teams, including Sales and Operations, ensuring seamless coordination and effective implementation.
*   Gather best practices, compelling success stories, and valuable partner feedback to continuously improve our offerings.
*   Analyze partner activities and performance data, identify optimization potentials, and contribute to the ongoing refinement of our partner strategy.

### Qualifications
*   Completed degree in Marketing, Business Administration, or a comparable field.
*   2-3 years of experience in indirect sales partner marketing or B2B marketing, ideally within the energy sector, PV, or storage domain.
*   Experience collaborating with EPCs, PV installers, or planning offices is a distinct advantage.
*   Demonstrated knowledge in event management, co-marketing, and various digital marketing channels.
*   Possess strategic thinking capabilities combined with a proactive, hands-on mentality.
*   Excellent proficiency in both German and English languages.
