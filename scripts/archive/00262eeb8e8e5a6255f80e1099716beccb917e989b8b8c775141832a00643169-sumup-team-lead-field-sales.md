### Description\nSumUp is a global fintech leader empowering over 4 million small businesses in 30+ countries with simple, affordable, and innovative payment solutions. We're seeking a dynamic Team Lead – Field Sales to strengthen our footprint across Germany. This fully remote, full-time role involves leading, growing, and inspiring a network of self-employed Field Sales Consultants (FSCs) to help small merchants adopt SumUp's products and succeed. You will be the strategic link between our field consultants and SumUp’s sales leadership, ensuring high performance and real impact.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Lead and grow our national sales network by recruiting, managing, and supporting 20–30 self-employed Field Sales Consultants across Germany.\n- Drive hands-on sales success by actively selling SumUp's POS and payment products to local businesses, gaining direct market insights.\n- Coach and enable your team through continuous training, performance reviews, and on-the-ground support to help FSCs hit their targets.\n- Analyse and optimise performance by monitoring KPIs, analysing sales data, and designing initiatives to increase acquisition and retention.\n- Shape the German strategy by working closely with HQ to share local insights, design market initiatives, and scale our presence nationwide.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Proven experience in field sales, ideally within fintech, POS, merchant services, or telecom.\n- Demonstrated ability to manage 10+ freelancers, consultants, or commission-based sales teams across different regions.\n- An entrepreneurial, hands-on leader who thrives on ownership and impact.\n- Strong understanding of small business owners' needs and how to build long-term customer relationships.\n- Excellent analytical skills and confidence working with Salesforce, Tableau, or other CRM tools.\n- Fluency in both German and English is required.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an experienced sales leader to make a significant impact by shaping and scaling SumUp's field presence across Germany. The role offers substantial autonomy, a chance to lead and mentor a team of entrepreneurial consultants, and direct contribution to the success of small businesses. With a competitive compensation package, comprehensive benefits including a generous L&D budget and sabbatical, and a strong emphasis on work-life flexibility, SumUp demonstrates a clear commitment to empowering its employees while fostering a mission-driven, supportive environment. If you're passionate about growth and empowering others, this role provides a unique blend of strategic leadership and hands-on market engagement.
