### Description\nOur client is a technology leader in the healthcare industry with over 1000 employees. This role involves developing firmware for critical medical devices, utilizing C/C++ on STM32 microcontrollers, and working with modern wireless communication technologies. You will be instrumental in creating essential documentation and participating in the full product lifecycle of these life-saving technologies.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop firmware for controlling medical devices using C and C++ on STM32 microcontrollers.\n- Program communication protocols and current radio technologies used by microcontrollers.\n- Create essential documentation required for medical products.\n- Accompany and participate in the entire product lifecycle.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Successfully completed a degree in electrical engineering or a comparable field.\n- Several years of professional experience in firmware development.\n- Strong proficiency in C++ and proven experience in the design and architecture of embedded systems.\n- Developed system understanding of the overall product and its development processes.\n- Fluent in both German and English.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a seasoned Firmware Developer with a strong C++ background to make a significant impact in the critical and highly regulated medical technology sector.
