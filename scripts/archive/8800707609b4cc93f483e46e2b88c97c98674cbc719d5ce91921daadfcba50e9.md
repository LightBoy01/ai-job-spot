Darigold is actively seeking a dynamic Talent Acquisition Partner to play a pivotal role in driving organizational excellence and fostering engagement. In this key position, you will connect vibrant talents with our manufacturing teams spanning across the Pacific Northwest.

As a proactive contributor, you won't just deliver full-cycle recruiting outcomes; you'll actively enhance our employer brand and contribute to cultivating a workplace where employees are not only valued but genuinely engaged. Embrace the exhilarating challenge of innovating our hiring processes and identifying opportunities to amplify our employer brand impact.

The ideal candidate is results-oriented, possesses a continuous improvement mindset, exudes creativity, is dedicated to exceptional customer service, embraces innovation, and embodies a "Commitment to Excellence" attitude.

**What You Will Do**

*   Collaborate seamlessly with hiring managers to attract and hire exceptional talent, covering entry-level to mid-level roles within our organization. Your role is instrumental in building a diverse and talented workforce.
*   Explore new and creative strategies for attracting top talent by partnering with hiring managers to understand the complex and unique talent needs of the organization.
*   Elevate the recruitment journey with enthusiasm, professionalism, and an unwavering commitment to delivering a top-tier, end-to-end hiring experience for every candidate.
*   As a Talent Adviser, collaborate closely with hiring managers. Serve as a strategic guide, offering valuable counsel on hiring decisions, and navigate the intricacies of offer negotiations with enthusiasm and expertise.
*   As a Talent Brand Ambassador, infuse excitement, professionalism, and unwavering passion into the mission. Elevate Darigold's talent brand through active involvement in local organizations, job fairs, industry events, and robust support for social media initiatives.
*   Collaborate with key stakeholders and the talent acquisition team by actively contributing to special projects and hiring initiatives. Identify opportunities to streamline the overall recruitment workflow, ensuring efficiency and effectiveness in talent acquisition processes.

field1

**What You Bring:**

*   Bachelor's degree in human resource management, IO Psychology, Business, or another related field of study; 4-6 years of relevant recruitment experience in lieu of a bachelor’s degree.
*   Experienced in utilizing LinkedIn, Indeed resume search, text messaging, and crafting engaging messages.
*   Showcasing a proven ability to excel in high-volume environments, supporting a variety of roles in manufacturing and operations while building great relationships with hiring managers and candidates.
*   Commitment to high-quality work and maintaining the confidentiality of sensitive information.
*   Excellent relationship-building, consultative, and collaboration skills.
*   Ability to prioritize and deliver results in a changing environment.
*   Self-directed and organized.
*   Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to effectively communicate with all levels of employees, including senior leadership.
*   Technology-savvy: Utilizing advanced technology, including AI tools, and exhibiting proficiency in Microsoft Office products.

**Nice to Have’s**

*   Experience recruiting in food or beverage manufacturing environments; dairy preferred
