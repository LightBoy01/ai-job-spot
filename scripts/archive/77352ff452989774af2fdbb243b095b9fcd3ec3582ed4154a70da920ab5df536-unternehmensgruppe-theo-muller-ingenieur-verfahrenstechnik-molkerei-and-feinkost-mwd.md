### Description\nUnternehmensgruppe Theo Müller, a regionally rooted family business with an international presence in Food and Logistics, is seeking a **Process Engineering Engineer for Dairy & Delicatessen (m/w/d)**. With well-known brands across dairy, fish & delicatessen, sauces & dressings, packaging, transport & logistics, and its own power plant, the group stands for diversity, independence, and security. Employees share a vision to \"bring taste into people's lives.\" This role offers a future-proof job based in Aretsried (near Augsburg), Freising (near Munich), or Leppersdorf (near Dresden).\n\n### Responsibilities\n* Develop group-wide process engineering concepts, standards, and guidelines for production processes.\n* Analyze existing processes, identify optimization potential, and develop innovative solutions for increased efficiency and sustainability.\n* Evaluate new technologies and create technical roadmaps for the future direction of our plants.\n* Support plants with accompanying planning, implementation, and review of process engineering projects.\n* Advise sites on strategic decisions and ensure compliance with defined standards.\n* Accompany investment projects primarily in the concept phase and assess their technical feasibility.\n\n### Qualifications\n* Completed engineering degree, ideally supplemented by technical vocational training.\n* Several years of experience in the food industry (preferably dairy, delicatessen, or beverages).\n* Strong analytical and conceptual skills, as well as technical understanding of complex processes.\n* Experience in developing technical standards and strategic planning.\n* Strong communication skills and ability to build internal and external networks.\n* Willingness to travel (approx. 30% in Europe) and fluent German and English skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned Process Engineering Engineer to make a significant impact within a stable, family-owned company that boasts international growth and a diverse portfolio. Theo Müller prioritizes employee well-being and professional development, offering attractive benefits like 30 days of holiday, flexible working, health programs, and even exclusive access to company vacation homes. The role offers strategic influence across multiple sites, focusing on innovation, efficiency, and sustainability. If you thrive on developing group-wide standards and driving technical roadmaps in the food industry, this is a role where your expertise will be valued and rewarded.
