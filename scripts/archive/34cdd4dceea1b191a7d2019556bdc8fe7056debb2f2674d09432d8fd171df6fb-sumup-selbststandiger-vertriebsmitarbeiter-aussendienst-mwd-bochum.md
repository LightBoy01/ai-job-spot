### Description
As an Independent Field Sales Representative, you have the opportunity to earn attractive, uncapped commission by building relationships with small businesses and helping them understand the value of SumUp products. With our extensive product package, you can act as a solutions consultant and offer all in-house solutions.

We are building a motivated sales team and are looking for individuals who have a passion for sales, are ambitious, and have a proven track record of success. Our aspiration is to help small businesses succeed, and we rely on your support to achieve this goal.

### Responsibilities
*   Search for and acquire potential customers in your assigned territory, with a particular focus on small and medium-sized enterprises.
*   Conduct in-person meetings, demonstrating the benefits of SumUp's innovative card reader and POS solutions through compelling product presentations.
*   Effectively communicate the value of our product and unique selling points, addressing customer needs and objections with finesse.
*   Actively generate new leads and cultivate your customer base by expanding it independently.

### Qualifications
*   Excellent verbal and written communication skills in German, along with good English proficiency.
*   Your empathetic nature allows you to build trusting relationships with your customers.
*   Ability to consistently achieve sales targets and deliver outstanding results.
*   Possess excellent communication and social skills that enable you to build trust and maintain long-term customer relationships.
*   Solid knowledge of the market and a genuine passion for supporting local businesses.
*   Experience in outbound lead generation, cold calling, and building lead pipelines.

### Author's Take
SumUp is actively seeking a driven Independent Field Sales Representative, also known as a Freelancer Vertrieb, Sales Consultant, or Gebietsverkaufsleiter selbstständig, who is passionate about empowering small businesses through innovative payment solutions. This is an individual contributor role with uncapped commission potential, ideal for a self-starter with a proven sales track record in outbound lead generation and cold calling.

SumUp is an Equal Employment Opportunity Employer, proudly committed to diversity and inclusivity.
