### Description\nVACUFLEX GmbH is an internationally active company and a market leader in the development and manufacture of plastic hoses and hose lines. Far from being an anonymous corporation, VACUFLEX prides itself on a familial and committed team. We are looking for a dedicated Tool Mechanic (m/f/d) to join our team, and entry-level candidates are warmly welcomed! This is an excellent opportunity to grow your skills in a supportive environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Manufacture tools according to technical drawings, ensuring every detail is precise.\n*   Produce turned and milled parts using conventional machines, working accurately and reliably.\n*   Set up and competently operate CNC turning and milling machines.\n*   Ideally, you bring CAD/CAM programming knowledge, or you are eager to develop your skills in this area.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   You have successfully completed vocational training as a Cutting Machine Operator, Tool Mechanic, or hold a comparable qualification.\n*   You possess experience in conventional turning and milling.\n*   CNC turning and milling are among your strengths, or you are motivated to further expand your expertise here.\n*   You can confidently read technical drawings and work precisely according to them.\n*   You demonstrate analytical thinking, find effective solutions, and maintain an overview even with complex tasks.\n*   You work in a structured, careful manner with a strong awareness for quality.\n*   You are a team player who enjoys working collaboratively to achieve common goals.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at VACUFLEX GmbH offers a fantastic opportunity for a Tool Mechanic, especially for those at an entry level, to thrive within a supportive and collaborative team environment. The company emphasizes a familial culture with flat hierarchies and short communication paths, fostering an atmosphere where colleagues are genuinely helpful. Beyond the engaging technical work, VACUFLEX demonstrates its commitment to employee well-being through generous benefits like a permanent contract, 30 days of annual leave, and perks such as JobRad leasing or discounted gym memberships. This is a role where precision, teamwork, and continuous learning are highly valued, making it ideal for someone looking to build a long-term career in a stable, market-leading company.
