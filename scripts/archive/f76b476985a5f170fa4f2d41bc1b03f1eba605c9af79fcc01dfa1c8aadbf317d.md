**Security Officer - South Australia**

**ABOUT WILSON:**

Wilson Security is the leading provider in the provision of security services across Australia and New Zealand. Our services are supported by a highly experienced management team, industry-leading expertise and a strong local and national structure. We are committed to becoming Australasia’s leading provider of integrated Security Solutions. Wilson Security has an established Blue Chip national client portfolio which is in a period of significant growth.

**ABOUT THE ROLE:**

*   Location: Various locations across Adelaide CBD
*   General Guard and Concerige Officer roles available
*   Casual and Part Time Opportunities available

Wilson Security is expanding in Adelaide, securing additional contracts that strengthen our presence in the Whole of Adelaide.  This growth has created new roles within our Adelaide team, focused on enhancing response and support capabilities for our existing teams and clients.

These positions are crucial to our success in the region and will be supported by our Operations Team and Duty Managers. They offer the opportunity to work in response roles, handling events such as protests and emergency management, with training across multiple locations and a variety of positions.

**DUTIES & RESPONSIBILITIES:**

*   Providing strong Customer Service solutions to visitors, contractors & client
*   Gatehouse duties
*   Proactively monitoring CCTV and Access Control Systems
*   Conducting building patrols to actively report and mitigate security & safety risk
*   Identifying and reporting hazards and building faults for rectification
*   Providing support to tenants and Facilities
*   Managing contractors and deliveries through dock management
*   Responding to emergency situations
*   Contractor management duties
*   Ad-hoc duties

**WHO WE ARE LOOKING FOR:**

*   Strong Customer Service Skills
*   Proactive and problem-solving mindset
*   Willing to support and step up as required into other roles
*   Ability to work well in a team or autonomously
*   Ability to work in a rotational roster (Nights/Days/Weekends)
*   Australian Citizen or hold Unlimited Work Rights
*   Previous experience in Corporate Real Estate Contracts (highly desirable)
*   Demonstrated ability to prepare and write reports
*   Demonstrated ability in the use of Computers and Security related systems (Access Control, CCTV, Microsoft Programmes)
*   Availability to and physical fitness to walk and stand for long periods
*   Valid SA Security License (including Monitoring Subclass)
*   Crowd Control endorsement will be highly desirable
*   Current First Aid & CPR Certificate

Wilson Security is an employer of choice, engaging with our people both online and in person.
