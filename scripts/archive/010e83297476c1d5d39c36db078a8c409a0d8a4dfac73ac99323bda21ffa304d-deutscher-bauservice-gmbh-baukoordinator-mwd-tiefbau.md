### Description\nAre quality and safety on civil engineering construction sites important to you? Do you enjoy serving as an interface between clients, municipalities, and commissioned civil engineering companies? Then you've come to the right place! We are successful because we act as a team, solution-oriented and innovatively. Become part of our motivated team and take off with us! This role involves comprehensive responsibility for civil engineering projects, from initial technical execution to final acceptance, ensuring legal compliance and efficient resource management.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Be responsible for the construction site from technical execution to acceptance.\n- Ensure legal compliance on construction sites and adherence to construction plans.\n- Coordinate and lead site meetings, and keep minutes.\n- Create and check quantity surveys, and ensure the efficient and economical use of construction materials and equipment.\n- Always keep an eye on quality and construction progress, and document the work.\n- Manage and coordinate commissioned external companies and review their quantity surveys.\n- Support permit applications and communication with authorities.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You are a certified Foreman or Technician (m/f/d), have a Master in Road or Civil Engineering, or are a Civil Engineer (m/f/d), or possess a comparable education/degree, e.g., in horticulture and landscaping.\n- You already have many years of professional experience in civil engineering and road construction, or comparably in horticulture and landscaping.\n- You have proficient MS Office skills.\n- You bring knowledge of relevant regulations (DIN18300, ZTV, RSA21, VOB).\n- You have very good German language skills and at least a Class B driving license.\n- Willingness to travel (not on weekends) is a prerequisite.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for an experienced civil engineering professional to take on significant responsibility in a dynamic, team-oriented environment. The company values innovation and offers a modern \"start-up flair\" with great benefits like a company car, flexible hours, mobile work options, and extensive support for employee well-being and growth. If you are passionate about quality, safety, and managing complex projects while contributing to a motivated team, this role is for you.
