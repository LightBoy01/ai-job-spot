_Velkommen om bord_ – Welcome aboard!

At NORSAN, a highly motivated and international team with Norwegian roots, we pride ourselves on being manufacturers of the finest Omega-3 products, guided by a crew rich in Omega-3 expertise.

Our mission is to inform and educate about Omega-3 fatty acids and their positive effects on the body, while simultaneously developing and offering the best Omega-3 products for optimal nutritional supply.

How do we achieve this? We, the NORSAN crew – a sailing team of clever minds – approach our work with dedication, creativity, and fun. We support each other, stepping in when the waves are highest. We are a strong team, focused on our shared goal, keeping our course and sails taut even in strong winds.

### Description
The wind is favorable, and our Hamburg team needs your support to visually articulate our mission and products. This role offers an exciting opportunity to immerse yourself in a dynamic, international environment, contributing your creative skills to a company dedicated to health and well-being. You will be a vital part of our visual communication efforts, helping to translate scientific information into engaging and accessible formats.

### Responsibilities
As a creative force on deck, your tasks will include:

*   Supporting our team in the creative preparation of trade fairs and events – for example, by designing roll-ups, flyers, or postcards.
*   Maintaining and developing graphic elements such as infographics and pictograms.
*   Helping to visualize reports and presentations attractively and understandably – whether with diagrams, icons, or smart layouts.
*   Creating and editing corporate presentations.

### Qualifications
What we expect from you:

*   You are currently studying Marketing, Communication or Media Sciences, Graphic/Media Design, or a similar field.
*   Excellent German language skills and good English skills, both written and spoken.
*   Ideally, you have gained initial experience in marketing or design projects, for example, through internships, university projects, or your own creative work.
*   You are familiar with Canva, Adobe Creative Cloud, or similar graphic tools, and you can create convincing presentations with PowerPoint.
*   You have a good eye for design, typography, and current visual trends, and you enjoy contributing your creative flair.
*   You possess a proactive, reliable, conscientious, and independent working style, and you are eager to support the medical field service area with your expertise.
