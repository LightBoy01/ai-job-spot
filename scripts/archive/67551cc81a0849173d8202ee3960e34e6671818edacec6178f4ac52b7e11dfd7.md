Job Title: Senior Business Analyst – Digital Transformation (Asset-Heavy Industries)

  

Company Overview:

At Codvo, software and people transformations go hand-in-hand. We are a global empathy-led technology services company. Product innovation and mature software engineering are part of our core DNA. Respect, Fairness, Growth, Agility, and Inclusiveness are the core values that we aspire to live by each day.

  

We continue to expand our digital strategy, design, architecture, and product management capabilities to offer expertise, outside-the-box thinking, and measurable results.

  

About the Role:

We are seeking a seasoned Senior Business Analyst with a strong background in digital transformation and a deep understanding of asset-heavy industries such as oil & gas, manufacturing, or utilities. This role is ideal for a strategic thinker who excels in analyzing business needs, managing stakeholders, and driving digital initiatives through agile methodologies.

  

Key Responsibilities:

\- Conduct detailed business analysis to identify opportunities for digital transformation.

\- Collaborate with cross-functional teams including product managers, engineering, operations, and business units.

\- Gather and document business requirements, user stories, and process flows.

\- Facilitate Agile/Scrum ceremonies and support sprint planning and backlog grooming.

\- Ensure strategic alignment of business analysis activities with organizational goals.

\- Evaluate and recommend AI-enabled solutions to enhance operational efficiency.

\- Perform market research and competitive analysis to inform business strategies.

\- Support change management and training initiatives related to new digital solutions.

  

Qualifications:

\- Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, IT, or Business.

\- MBA or equivalent advanced degree preferred.

\- 10+ years in the digital industry.

\- 5+ years of experience in business analysis.

\- 3+ years of experience in asset-heavy industries (e.g., oil & gas, utilities, manufacturing).

  

Skills & Competencies:

\- Strong proficiency in Agile/Scrum methodologies.

\- Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.

\- Expertise in business analysis and translating insights into actionable requirements.

\- Ability to ensure strategic alignment across teams and initiatives.

\- Exposure to AI-enabled solutions and data-driven decision making.

\- Comfortable working in fast-paced, ambiguous environments with a high degree of autonomy.
