### Description\nAre you an expert in renewable energies, ready to bring fresh impetus to a strong company dedicated to solar and wind projects nationwide? We are seeking a passionate **Senior Wind Project Developer (m/f/d)** with a primary focus on permitting. In this autonomous and mobile role, you will be instrumental in developing and steering wind energy projects from initial concept through successful permitting and implementation, primarily operating in and around Oldenburg. This position requires enthusiasm for renewable energies, a proactive approach, and a readiness to travel to project sites.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Be responsible for the development and steering of wind energy projects with a focus on permitting.\n- Identify suitable locations, negotiate, and finalize land use agreements.\n- Prepare and coordinate approval documents according to BImSchG and communicate with authorities.\n- Evaluate technical and economic project parameters in collaboration with specialist departments.\n- Monitor regulatory market developments and derive recommendations for action.\n- Work closely with project stakeholders such as authorities, experts, and planning offices.\n- Mentor and support junior colleagues in their daily work.\n\n### Qualifications\n- At least 5 years of relevant professional experience as a wind energy project developer with completed projects in permitting.\n- Completed university degree in a technical field, ideally with a focus on energy supply and renewable energies, e.g., urban and regional planning, environmental science, engineering.\n- Very good knowledge of planning and permitting law, especially BImSchG, as well as contract and lease law.\n- Convincing and confident demeanor with a lot of energy and a will to succeed.\n- You are from the Oldenburg region and are ideally well-networked there.\n- Willingness to travel to supervise projects on-site.\n- Very good German and good English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an outstanding opportunity for a seasoned wind energy professional to make a significant impact within a robust and forward-thinking company. The role offers substantial autonomy and flexibility, including mobile work, flexible hours, and a company car for private use, emphasizing a great work-life balance. Beyond a competitive salary, the company invests in your success with an in-house training program and provides a platform to mentor emerging talent. If you are deeply passionate about renewable energy, thrive in a challenging regulatory environment, and want to directly contribute to Germany's energy transition, this role provides the perfect blend of responsibility, growth, and meaningful work.
