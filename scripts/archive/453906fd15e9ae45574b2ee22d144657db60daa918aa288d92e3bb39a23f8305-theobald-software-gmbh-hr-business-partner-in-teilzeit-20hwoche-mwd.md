Our team of over 60 enthusiastic and innovative employees, located at two sites, is dedicated to making the integration of SAP into various third-party systems simple and reliable. Our headquarters in Stuttgart thrives on team spirit, curiosity, and open collaboration. Here, experience, innovative power, and unconventional thinking converge to transform complex challenges into smart solutions for our customers.\n\nWe offer you the opportunity to bring your skills and creativity to our successful and continuously growing company. You'll enjoy a constructive work atmosphere where you can autonomously dedicate yourself to challenging tasks. We support flexible working hours and provide an attractive, performance-based compensation package. Our culture is characterized by flat hierarchies and a strong team spirit within a relaxed yet constructive work environment.
