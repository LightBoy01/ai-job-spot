### Description
Our client is a dynamic and growing company comprised of experienced software developers, dedicated to helping other businesses achieve their digital objectives. As an all-round IT partner, the team specializes in developing innovative apps, web applications, microservices, and device controls. They meticulously tailor their services to meet customer needs, ensuring the highest quality through continuous development and a strong emphasis on teamwork. The current client focus is primarily within the energy sector, undertaking holistic projects from initial concept through to implementation and deployment.

They are seeking enthusiastic individuals—from young professionals to seasoned seniors—who possess a genuine passion for software development and collaborative problem-solving. Key attributes include a keen interest in continuous improvement, an openness to embracing new challenges, and a desire to work respectfully and collaboratively within a team. Both technical and personal development are actively encouraged and fostered within the organization.

### Responsibilities
*   Collaborate to define your project focus, whether it's specific languages, technology stacks, or your role within the development process, given the diverse nature of projects.
*   Conceive and implement new features, consistently focusing on delivering customer value (e.g., smart automations, robust update systems, sensor integration, intuitive user interfaces).
*   Analyze and resolve bugs, alongside continuously improving existing codebase.
*   Regularly conduct team code reviews to uphold and secure high code quality standards.
*   Engage in close collaboration with the product management of their clients, following SCRUM methodologies.
*   Actively contribute to shaping software architecture and making critical decisions regarding tools, frameworks, and structural approaches.
*   Participate in and contribute to Continuous Integration and Deployment processes.

### Qualifications
*   Possess strong analytical thinking skills and a pragmatic approach, readily embracing new challenges.
*   Bring a minimum of three years of professional experience in backend development, with full-stack experience being an advantage.
*   Demonstrate routine proficiency with web technologies and at least one object-oriented programming language (e.g., Java, C#, Python).
*   Exhibit a solid understanding of containers, test frameworks, databases, and build systems.
*   Be proficient in using Git and CI/CD tools (e.g., GitLab CI, GitHub Actions, Angular or Jenkins), and be accustomed to collaborative team environments.
*   Have Clean Code principles and Design Patterns as an integral part of your daily development practice.
*   Hold a completed degree in fields such as Computer Science, Mathematics, or possess equivalent professional qualifications.
*   Be fluent in both German and English.
