### Description\nJoin Flix, a dynamic and modern travel tech company, where you'll contribute to making global travel simpler, more sustainable, and affordable through innovative technologies. As a **Payroll Manager (m/f/d)**, you will play a crucial role in ensuring a smooth and reliable payroll process for our German employees. Working closely with our Payroll Experts, you will support the accurate and timely remuneration of our employees and actively participate in payroll projects and process optimizations.\n\nThis position is based in Munich and is a **fixed-term contract for 15 months, part-time (20 hours/week)**.\n\nWe believe in looking at the bigger picture; even if you don't meet all requirements 100%, but believe you can make an impact, we encourage you to apply!\n\n### Responsibilities\n* Prepare monthly payroll for our employees in Germany, working closely with our external Payroll Provider.\n* Ensure that all payroll-relevant data (e.g., new hires, terminations, contract changes, benefits, and absences) is complete, accurate, and submitted on time.\n* Support the clarification of account discrepancies with our financial accounting department.\n* Act as the primary contact person for employees, HR colleagues, and external partners regarding payroll and employment-related questions.\n* Contribute ideas for optimizing our payroll processes and support their implementation.\n\n### Qualifications\n* At least 1 year of experience in preparatory payroll accounting.\n* Good knowledge of income tax and social security law.\n* Experience with payroll tools (e.g., DATEV, SAGE, SAP) and HR systems.\n* Structured, reliable, and discreet working style.\n* Very good German and English language skills, both written and spoken.\n\n### Author's Take\nFlix offers an exciting opportunity for a detail-oriented Payroll Manager to make a significant impact within a rapidly evolving global travel tech company. This role is ideal for someone looking to deepen their payroll expertise in a collaborative and supportive environment, even in a part-time, fixed-term capacity.\n\nThe emphasis on process optimization and direct stakeholder communication means you'll be more than just a \"number-cruncher\"; you'll be a key player in ensuring smooth operations. Flix's commitment to employee well-being, growth, and a hybrid work model showcases a progressive culture. The \"Work from Anywhere\" option (up to 60 days/year) and various development programs highlight their dedication to fostering a flexible and learning-rich environment where every contribution counts.\n\nIf you're passionate about payroll, enjoy a dynamic workplace, and want to contribute to a company focused on sustainable travel, this role offers a great platform to grow and make a tangible difference.
