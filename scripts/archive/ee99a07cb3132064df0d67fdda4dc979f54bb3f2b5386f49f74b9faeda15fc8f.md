Perion is a global advertising technology company delivering solutions to the biggest brands and publishers around the globe across search, social media and display, video, CTV, and programmatic DOOH.

Home to an award-winning technology solution –– with our unique data-driven AI/ML based technologies, we deliver and optimize hundreds of terabytes of data and billions of events per day. We’re working with dozens of sources to provide a superior experience across screens and platforms, including mobile, video, social and native.

Perion Algo (formerly Greenbids) is reshaping how brands and agencies optimize media investments across YouTube, Meta, and programmatic with AI-driven solutions that deliver better outcomes with simplicity and precision. As part of Perion, we operate at the intersection of technology, media quality, and performance to drive measurable results for advertisers globally.

We’re hiring a **Media Trader, AI Solutions** to lead strategy, activation, and optimization for high-impact campaigns across **YouTube and Meta** in the US market on a **Hybrid** basis out of the New York Metro area. You’ll own the full trading lifecycle—from architecture and QA to advanced optimization and insights—while partnering tightly with Sales and Client Success to drive retention, margin, and scalable growth. You’re hands-on in platforms, love structured testing, and know how to translate business goals into smart media and creative levers powered by AI.

**What You’ll Do**
------------------

*   **Own end-to-end trading** for YouTube & Meta: campaign design, launch, pacing, optimization, QA, brand safety, and reporting.
*   **Translate objectives into strategy:** map KPIs (VTR, CPV, CPM, ROAS, attention/quality metrics) to bidding, audiences, placements, and creative frameworks.
*   **Optimize with rigor:** daily pacing and margin management, flighting, budget re-allocation, bid/budget automation, frequency control, inventory curation.
*   **Run structured experiments:** build a testing roadmap (audience, creative, supply, bidding), define success criteria, and codify repeatable wins into playbooks.
*   **Leverage AI workflows:** apply Perion Algo recommendations and insights to simplify ops, improve results, and surface opportunities for scale and upsell.
*   **Upsell signals** **→** **action:** proactively identify expansion (new lines, formats, geos, objectives) and partner with Sales/CSM on proposals and QBRs.
*   **Raise the bar on quality:** implement checklists, pre-launch QA, brand suitability, measurement consistency, and data hygiene across platforms.
*   **Tell a compelling story:** convert performance data into clear narratives and next steps for clients; present in weekly/bi-weekly calls and QBRs.
*   **Enable the team:** mentor traders, share best practices, and contribute to internal tools, macros, and SOPs that make the team faster and more accurate.  
      
    

**What You Bring**
------------------

*   **3-5+ years** hands-on media trading across **YouTube (Google Ads/DV360)** and **Meta Ads Manager** for US advertisers (brand and/or DR).
*   Proven ownership of **large, multi-line campaigns** (six- to seven-figure budgets), with strong results on KPI delivery, margin management, and scale results.
*   Expert understanding of digital KPIs & mechanics (CPM, CPV, CTR, VTR, ROAS, reach, incrementality, attention/quality signals) and how to move them.
*   Comfort with **auction dynamics, learning phases, creative testing frameworks**, and supply curation (inventory, placements, brand safety).
*   Advanced **Excel/Google Sheets** (pivots, lookups; basic modeling). **Nice to have:** SQL or Python for analysis/automation; Looker/Tableau/GA4.
*   Strong communication: you can brief creatives, align stakeholders, and present crisp insights and recommendations to senior clients.
*   **Startup mindset:** bias to action, high ownership, process discipline, and comfort in a fast-moving environment backed by the scale of Perion.
