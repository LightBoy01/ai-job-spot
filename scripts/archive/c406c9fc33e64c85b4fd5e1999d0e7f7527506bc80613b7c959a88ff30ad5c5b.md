**About the role**

The role requires to design, coordinate and lead recreational activities and experiences for guests at Doha Beach Club fostering a fun and vibrant atmosphere. This role supports guests enjoyment, strengthens community engagement and enhances the overall guest experience whilst safeguarding guest wellbeing and promoting brand identity through high energy and creative activities.

**Accountabilities:**

Operational

Perform other department duties related to his / her position as directed by the Head of the Department

*   Lead the planning, coordination, and execution of daily recreational programs for guests ensuring all activities run on time and meet quality standards.
*   Manage the anime staff including scheduling, performance feedback and provide a collaborative environment.
*   Ensure guest safety and wellbeing by enforcing safety protocols.
*   Deliver exceptional guest experiences by customizing activities to suit diverse guests needs, ages and cultural background while upholding brand standards.
*   Monitor attendance and guest feedback; analyze data to improve offerings and drive guest satisfaction.
*   Collaborate with other departments to plan and execute themed events and promotions that align with the Doha Beach Club marketing and operational goals.
