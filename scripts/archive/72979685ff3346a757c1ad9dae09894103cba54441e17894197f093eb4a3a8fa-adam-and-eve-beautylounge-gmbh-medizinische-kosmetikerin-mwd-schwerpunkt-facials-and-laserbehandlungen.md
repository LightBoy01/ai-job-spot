### Description\nAdam & Eve Beautylounge is seeking a dedicated and passionate **Medical Cosmetician (m/f/d)** who wishes to apply their expertise and enthusiasm for skin health in a modern, high-quality environment. You will find a stylish ambiance, state-of-the-art technologies, and a team that combines professionalism with warmth. Be a part of shaping the future of beauty with us.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Conduct professional **facial treatments** including skin analysis, deep cleansing, peelings, and personalized skincare consultations.\n- Apply **laser technologies** (e.g., for permanent hair removal, BBL, skin rejuvenation).\n- Create individualized treatment and skincare plans.\n- Provide expert advice to our clients on skincare, treatments, and products.\n- Ensure the highest hygiene standards and professional documentation.\n- Actively contribute to an excellent client experience.\n\n### Qualifications\n**Hard Skills:**\n- Completed training as a **medical or state-recognized cosmetician**.\n- Experience with **laser devices** (e.g., for hair removal and BBL treatments).\n- Sound knowledge of **facials and skin analysis**.\n- Proficient in using modern cosmetic devices.\n- Understanding of skin physiology and medical-aesthetic applications.\n\n**Soft Skills:**\n- Passion for skincare and aesthetics.\n- High service orientation and a well-groomed, professional appearance.\n- Empathy and client understanding.\n- Team player, responsible, and proactive.\n- Interest in further education and new technologies.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a skilled Medical Cosmetician to join a forward-thinking and supportive team at Adam & Eve Beautylounge. The role offers a chance to work with the latest technologies in a luxurious setting, providing ample opportunities for continuous professional development and specialization in medical aesthetics. With an attractive compensation package, employee discounts, and a warm, collaborative team environment with flat hierarchies, this position promises both professional growth and a fulfilling client-focused career.
