### Description

At RaiseNow, we believe in using technology for good. As our IT Support (f/m/d), you'll be instrumental in empowering our 90 employees across more than 5 countries, ensuring our systems are secure, up-to-date, and flawlessly maintained. This role offers the unique opportunity to leverage your hardware and software expertise within a remote-first, international team, directly contributing to a meaningful cause.

This is a part-time position, with possible working hours of 20 hours per week. You have the flexibility to work from our Berlin office or completely remotely within Germany.

You will be the first point of contact for all IT topics, ensuring our team members have the reliable infrastructure and support they need to thrive. Beyond technical support, your mission is to conscientiously manage IT assets, own critical tools, and uphold our IT security standards, all while making a positive impact with your work.

### Responsibilities

*   **Daily Support:** Provide first and second-level technical support for hardware, software, and system issues, managing tickets with clear, comprehensive, and swift communication.
*   **Asset Management:** Systematically track, manage, and maintain IT assets across RaiseNow, including the installation, configuration, and maintenance of laptops and other IT equipment.
*   **Tool Management:** Support company-wide rollouts of new tools, updates, and system changes. This includes taking ownership of Google Workspace and providing expert support for various SaaS and productivity software (e.g., Slack, Asana, Zoom).
*   **Ownership & Compliance:** Maintain and enhance IT documentation (e.g., FAQs, setup guides, asset inventory). Manage user accounts, access rights, and hardware setup for on- and offboarding processes, ensuring strict compliance with IT security policies and best practices.
*   **Proactive Communication:** Cultivate proactive, clear communication with colleagues and external service providers through various channels, including tickets, chat, and IT training, to ensure a smooth and efficient IT environment.

### Qualifications

*   Relevant professional experience in a similar IT support field.
*   Proficiency with both macOS and Mobile Device Management (MDM) solutions, such as Kandji.
*   Experience working in a UNIX environment and with Google Workspace.
*   Familiarity with LDAP, SCIM, and Single Sign-On (SSO) technologies.
*   Practical experience with Asset Management and SaaS Management Platforms, including integration with third-party tools.
*   Up-to-date knowledge of current hardware and software trends.
*   C1 level English skills, both spoken and written.
