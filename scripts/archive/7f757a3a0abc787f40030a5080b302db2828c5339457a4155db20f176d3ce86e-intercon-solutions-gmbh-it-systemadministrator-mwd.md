### Description

Are you looking for a role where you can take on real responsibility and truly make a difference? Do you want to work in a motivated environment that values collaboration, openness, and continuous development?

If so, you've come to the right place! We are a dynamic, growing company dedicated to developing innovative solutions that successfully position products on Europe's most important online marketplaces. Our passionate team of around 100 professionals ensures our clients achieve their goals efficiently and sustainably.

To strengthen our IT team, we are immediately seeking an engaged, full-time **System Administrator (m/w/d)**.

### Responsibilities

*   Manage and further develop the entire IT infrastructure, including client, server, and network systems.
*   Administer and monitor both Linux and Windows servers.
*   Ensure robust IT security, high system availability, and optimal network performance.
*   Provide technical support and assistance to employees for all IT-related questions and issues.
*   Plan and implement various IT projects, such as system upgrades, migrations, and automation initiatives.
*   Perform regular data backups and diligently maintain comprehensive system documentation.

### Qualifications

*   Completed vocational training as an **IT Specialist for System Integration** or a comparable qualification.
*   A minimum of **3 years of professional experience** in system administration.
*   Excellent knowledge of **Windows 10/11**, as well as **Linux and Windows server environments**.
*   Demonstrated experience with **network storage solutions** (e.g., QNAP NAS).
*   Possess strong team spirit, self-initiative, and a solution-oriented approach to problem-solving.
*   Very good German and English language skills, both written and spoken.

### Author's Take

**What kind of impact can a System Administrator truly make here, beyond just keeping the lights on?**

"As an insider, I can tell you that your work as a System Administrator here is far from just routine maintenance. Our company is all about innovation in e-commerce, and every upgrade, every optimized server, every successful automation project you implement directly empowers our product teams to build those cutting-edge solutions faster and more reliably. You're not just supporting; you're actively shaping the digital infrastructure that underpins our growth across European marketplaces. We value your input on new technologies and processes, and with our short decision-making paths, your ideas can quickly turn into impactful improvements."

"It's genuinely collaborative. When a complex issue arises, it's never just 'your problem.' We jump in to help each other, share knowledge, and brainstorm solutions together – whether it's troubleshooting a tricky network configuration or planning a major server migration. The '4 days on-site, 1 day home office' model perfectly supports this: we get solid team interaction and hands-on collaboration during the week, with the flexibility for focused work or personal matters on the remote day. Plus, the commitment to professional development isn't just a bullet point; we actively encourage and support taking courses or certifications, ensuring your skills evolve as rapidly as our technology stack."
