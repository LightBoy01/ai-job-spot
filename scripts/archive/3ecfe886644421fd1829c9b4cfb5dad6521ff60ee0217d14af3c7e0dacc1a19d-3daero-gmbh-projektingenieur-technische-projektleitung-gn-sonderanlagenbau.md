### Description\n3D-aero GmbH, a technology company based in Hamburg with approximately 60 employees, has been a proud part of MTU Aero Engines AG – one of the world's leading companies in the aviation industry – since December 2024. We develop customized automation solutions for aircraft production and other industrial sectors. We utilize optical sensor technology and modern industrial robots together with AI-based data processing to develop tailor-made systems for our customers.\n\nWe are looking for a **Project Engineer / Technical Project Lead (gn)** to bring innovative special machines and robot solutions to life, starting immediately. If you are passionate about technology and want to shape projects from idea to implementation, then you are exactly right with us – apply now and become part of the 3D-aero team!\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Project Management**: Take technical project leadership from kick-off to commissioning, making technical decisions and ensuring quality, deadlines, and costs.\n*   **Customer Communication & Coordination**: Act as the central point of contact for our national and international customers, as well as internally for all involved specialist departments. Conduct kick-offs, alignments, and project meetings, keeping all relevant stakeholders coordinated – in-house and on-site at the customer.\n*   **Practical Implementation**: In close coordination with our specialist departments, independently plan, implement, and conduct in-house tests of our custom-built systems.\n*   **Proactive Development**: Actively contribute to technical solutions, bring in your own ideas, and help shape the growing team.\n*   **Service**: Ensure smooth operation even after installation and acceptance, in collaboration with our service team.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Successfully completed degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, or Mechanical Engineering.\n*   Several years of professional experience in technical project management: from design to commissioning of robot systems or special machinery.\n*   Ideally, specialized knowledge in sensor technology, industrial image processing, or sealing.\n*   Solution-oriented, structured, and independent working style.\n*   Willingness to undertake occasional business trips within Germany and abroad.\n*   Very good German and good English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting opportunity for a seasoned Project Engineer to drive innovation in industrial automation at 3D-aero, now bolstered by its integration with MTU Aero Engines AG. The role offers significant autonomy in managing projects from concept to completion, engaging directly with international clients, and contributing actively to solution development. The company fosters a supportive and respectful environment, emphasizing personal growth, flexible working, and generous benefits like 30 days of leave and public transport subsidies, underscoring a strong commitment to employee well-being and professional development.
