C3 AI (NYSE: AI), is the Enterprise AI application software company. C3 AI delivers a family of fully integrated products including the C3 Agentic AI Platform, an end-to-end platform for developing, deploying, and operating enterprise AI applications, C3 AI applications, a portfolio of industry-specific SaaS enterprise AI applications that enable the digital transformation of organizations globally, and C3 Generative AI, a suite of domain-specific generative AI offerings for the enterprise. Learn more at: **[C3 AI](https://c3.ai/)**

C3 AI is hiring a Senior Director, Strategic Solutions focused on US Federal and International sales, specifically targeting U.S. Indo Pacific Command and allied nations and militaries. This is a quota-based sales opportunity with a rapidly growing leader in Enterprise AI software. You will work directly with C-level customers and partners in the federal government to define high-value AI use cases and develop AI application roadmaps. Your mission: drive adoption of our solutions while helping federal agencies achieve their digital transformation goals.

**Responsibilities**

*   Achieve or exceed assigned sales quota through focused activities
*   Build and maintain enterprise-level customer relationships
*   Develop and execute a comprehensive sales strategy targeting US Federal and International agencies, with a focus on Indo PACOM region.
*   Consult with federal agencies to define and implement digital transformation strategies using AI technologies
*   Forecast and maintain a robust pipeline to achieve business unit goals
*   Collaborate with internal teams (business development, engineering, products, marketing) to deliver tailored solutions
*   Represent C3 AI in strategic partnerships and alliances
*   Engage in strategic selling and ensure ongoing customer success
*   Travel up to 60% (or more) to customer sites based on business needs

**Qualifications**

*   Bachelor’s degree in engineering, science, computer science, or related field (Master’s or MBA preferred)
*   Proven track record in SaaS solution sales or 5+ years in US Federal and International sales, ideally with focus on direct commercial sales and a knowledge of foreign military sales.
*   Strong understanding of federal procurement processes (GSA Schedules, GWACs, IDIQs)
*   Deep knowledge of AI, machine learning, big data, analytics, and their government applications
*   Experience building relationships with senior-level decision-makers in federal agencies
*   Technical fluency in AI, machine learning, cyber, and cloud technology stacks
*   Exemplary written and oral communication skills
*   Strong multi-tasking, planning, and organizational abilities
*   Must be local to or willing to relocate to Redwood City, CA
*   Must be authorized to work in the U.S. without current or future sponsorship
*   Top Secret (TS) Clearance preferred
