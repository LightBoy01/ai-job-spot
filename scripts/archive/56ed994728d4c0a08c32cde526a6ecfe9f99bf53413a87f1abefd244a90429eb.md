Mining Engineer

We have a **career defining opportunity** for a **Mining Engineer** to join our high performing Underground Technical Services team at our **KCGM Operations** in the **new Fimiston Underground Mine**. This opportunity is offered on **8/6 roster, 12-hour days**, residentially based in Kalgoorlie-Boulder – the Heart of the Goldfields.

It is an exciting time to be part of the biggest growth story in Australian Underground Gold Mining at the Fimiston Underground Operation, with experience to be gained that will be challenging and rewarding, but ultimately a career-shaping experience as a turning point professionally.

Reporting to the Senior Mining Engineer and Underground Manager, the primary role is for the safe design and delivery of all day-to-day engineering functions – including data management, drill & blast plans, short term planning and scheduling, mine ventilation, mining projects and maintenance and improvement of mining systems, as well as other requirements within the Mining Engineering team.

**Your main responsibilities will include:**

*   Produce accurate and clear work instructions for mining activities.
*   Ensure issued instructions are clear, correct, and understandable.
*   Production Designs, QA/QC of production activities
*   Assist in short term planning and scheduling, including development of weekly schedules to ensure priority deadlines are met for production activities.
*   Work closely with operations and survey to prioritise and execute production and development activities.
*   Reporting for all mining activities and compliance against schedule.
*   Undertake improvement projects to optimise mining practices.

**Required skills and experience:**

*   Tertiary Qualifications in Mining Engineering.
*   Current "C" Class Western Australian Drivers Licence (Manual).
*   Experience with mine design software - Deswik.
*   Underground mining engineering experience in a mechanised hard-rock underground mine.
*   Good communication skills.
*   Flexibility to adapt to improved procedures and practices.
*   St Johns Senior First Aid certificate (desirable).
*   Awareness of the Work Health & Safety Act and Regulations (desirable).
*   Underground time completed (desirable).
