### Description
Urban Sports Club is Europe's leading platform for sports and wellness, dedicated to inspiring active, healthy lives by offering access to thousands of fitness and wellness experiences with a single membership. We believe our people are at the core of our success and offer a vibrant environment for growth and innovation.

We are seeking a **Staff Android Engineer** with a passion for crafting exceptional user experiences in an international setting. You will become a vital part of our cross-functional teams, working on our flagship application to develop innovative features and refine the member check-in experience. This pivotal role involves not only designing and implementing advanced Android applications in Kotlin but also taking significant architectural ownership, guiding the evolution from hybrid to native development, and ensuring seamless alignment across Android and iOS platforms. You will work closely with engineers, product managers, and designers to achieve platform cohesion and define the future of mobile excellence at Urban Sports Club.

### Responsibilities
*   Design and build advanced applications in Kotlin for the Android platform.
*   Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to define, design, and deploy new features, ensuring a cohesive product experience.
*   Write comprehensive unit tests to ensure robustness, address edge cases, and enhance application usability and reliability.
*   Proactively identify and resolve bugs, continuously working to improve overall application performance.
*   Continuously discover, evaluate, and integrate new technologies and best practices to maximize development efficiency and innovation.
*   Drive critical architectural decisions and coordinate efforts across both Android and iOS teams to ensure consistent and high-quality implementations.

### Qualifications
*   A degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience, with at least 8 years of professional Android development experience.
*   Strong expertise in Kotlin and the Android SDK, demonstrated through hands-on experience across various Android versions and screen sizes.
*   Proven skill in designing intuitive Android UIs, applying established design principles, patterns, and best practices.
*   Extensive experience with RESTful APIs, offline storage solutions, maps APIs, and performance tuning techniques to build scalable and reliable applications.
*   Familiarity with cloud messaging APIs, push notifications, CI/CD practices, and effective utilization of open-source libraries to enhance development workflow.
*   Exceptional ability to translate complex business requirements into clear technical solutions and communicate effectively across diverse teams to ensure alignment and impact.

### Author's Take
*   **Question:** Given the mention of architectural ownership and guiding the shift from hybrid to native, what are the immediate technical challenges or strategic initiatives this role would tackle in the first 6-12 months?
    *   **Answer:** That's a fantastic question, and it gets to the heart of this role's impact. In the first year, you'd be pivotal in solidifying our native Android foundation. We're talking about not just migrating existing hybrid components but also architecting new features with a native-first mindset. This includes defining robust module boundaries, potentially exploring modern UI frameworks like Jetpack Compose for future features, and establishing clear technical standards that empower our feature teams while ensuring overall app performance and scalability. Your influence here won't just be on code; it will shape how we build mobile for years to come.
