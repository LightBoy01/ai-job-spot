### Description

Livy Care is at the forefront of healthcare innovation, offering an AI-powered room assistance (Ambient-AI) and productivity platform designed for care management. Our technology transforms continuous room and activity data into actionable insights and automated documentation, shifting care teams from reactive alarm processing to proactive care orchestration. This results in fewer emergencies, optimized time, and enhanced resource utilization for our clients in the DACH region. The platform significantly deepens integrations within existing alarm and clinic IT infrastructures, thereby increasing stickiness and delivering measurable improvements in safety, efficiency, and resource allocation. Our next phase involves widespread auto-documentation, where care events are automatically captured, contextualized, and integrated directly into Electronic Medical Records (EMR) and reporting systems.

As a Field Engineer at Livy Care, you will be the technical backbone and trusted voice for our customers. Your primary responsibility is to clarify IT requirements, configure our state-of-the-art devices, meticulously set up WLAN environments, independently analyze and resolve technical issues, and ensure seamless operation. You will work closely with our clients' IT departments, manage on-site installations, and contribute directly to the successful deployment and ongoing stability of our solutions. This full-time, hybrid role based in Berlin involves significant travel (approx. 30-50%) across the DACH region, starting as soon as possible.

### Responsibilities

*   **Technical Customer Point of Contact (IT-first):** Serve as the main technical contact for IT departments, guiding them from initial preparation through to ongoing operation via phone and remote sessions.
*   **WLAN & Network Management:** Conduct site checks, parameterize, and troubleshoot complex WLAN and network issues (including SSID, VLAN, 802.1X, DHCP/DNS, Routing/NAT, Firewall rules, QoS). Document all network requirements comprehensively.
*   **Device Commissioning & Configuration:** Manage the rollout, perform over-the-air (OTA) firmware updates, handle device provisioning, and ensure secure key management.
*   **Error Analysis & Troubleshooting:** Execute systematic diagnostics across Layers 1-7, perform log analysis, monitor system performance, and create detailed runbooks. Resolve issues independently, escalating to the engineering team when necessary.
*   **SSH & Linux-CLI Expertise:** Conduct status checks, verify services (systemd/journalctl), perform network traces (tcpdump), and script recurring tasks using SSH and Linux command-line interface.
*   **On-site Installations & Support:** Participate in site surveys and installation appointments, coordinate with technical stakeholders, and perform final function tests to guarantee successful deployments.
*   **Hardware Interaction:** Perform basic work on cables and connectors, including soldering if required, demonstrating comfort with hardware proximity.
*   **Internal Collaboration:** Act as a critical interface, working closely with Product and Engineering teams, providing essential field feedback, and contributing to our knowledge base and documentation.
*   **Compliance & Security:** Adhere strictly to data protection and IT security guidelines, ensuring clean handovers and maintaining audit-proof protocols.

### Qualifications

To excel in this role, you should possess:

*   **Networking Mastery:** Proven proficiency in WLAN/802.11 and fundamental network principles (TCP/IP, VLAN, 802.1X, DHCP/DNS/NTP, Firewalls, PoE, QoS/Multicast).
*   **Advanced Network Security:** Expertise in network encryption & certificates, including WPA2-/WPA3-Enterprise, 802.1X (EAP-TLS/PEAP), RADIUS/NPS, certificate-based WLAN (client/server), PKI/CA (incl. CSR), key/certificate lifecycle, CRL/OCSP, rollout via MDM/Intune and SCEP/EST, and secure TLS configuration (TLS 1.2/1.3, mTLS).
*   **Linux/CLI Proficiency:** Routine in Linux/CLI operations, including SSH, shell basics, log analysis, container basics, certificates/PKI. Ideally, knowledge in Bash or Python scripting.
*   **Process Definition & Execution:** Demonstrated ability to independently set up checklists, escalation paths, and canary/rollback processes, alongside a strong understanding of SLAs.
*   **Customer Obsession & Communication:** Exceptional service and customer orientation, clear and effective communication with IT teams, and proven ability to troubleshoot under pressure.
*   **Mobility & Language:** Willingness to travel extensively (30-50%) for customer engagements across the DACH region. A Class B driver's license is an advantage. Fluency in both German and English is essential, and you must be on-call capable.

**Nice to Have:**

*   Experience with **IoT/Edge** technologies (e.g., MQTT, REST, Zigbee, Device Management).
*   Relevant certifications such as **CCNA/CWNA/Network+** or IT security basics.
*   Prior experience in field rollouts, particularly within Health/MedTech/Industrial environments, and familiarity with WLAN planning tools.
