Customers run their businesses on the Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) cloud, leveraging an ever-expanding set of industry-leading cloud-based services such as security, networking, compute, storage, and database. OCI's Block Volumes service operates at massive scale, delivering exabytes of data across 60+ regions.

As part of the OCI storage performance team, you will be part of the team focused on improving storage performance for object, file and block implementations. In this role, you will be engaging with performance analysts and with functional subject matter experts to improve system performance. •    Evaluate the performance of cloud storage solutions, identify inefficiencies, and design cost-effective solutions.

We are looking for hands-on engineers with expertise and passion for solving complex problems in distributed systems, large-scale storage, and scaling services to meet future growth. If this is you, you can be part of the team that drives the best-in-class Storage platform into its next development phase. These are exciting times for the service - we are growing fast and delivering innovative, enterprise-class features to satisfy various customer workloads. 

### Responsibilities

•    Evaluate the performance of cloud storage solutions, identify inefficiencies, and design cost-effective solutions.  
•    Collaborate with SMEs within OCI storage to architect performance improvements that align with the strategic direction of the platform.    
•    Implement proposed changes at proof of concept scale to validate performance gain and system risk.    
•    Lead a team of engineers through final implement and roll out to OCI storage regions.    
  
Qualifications:  
•    5 to 10 years distributed service engineering experience in a software development environment  
•    Development experience in a modern programming language, such as Java, C++, C#  
•    Knowledge of Linux Systems Internals and Storage Protocols like (iSCSI, NVMe-oF, NFS, SMB).  
•    Experience working closely with architects, principals, product, and program managers to deliver product features on time and with high quality.  
•    Good knowledge of data structures, algorithms, operating systems, and distributed systems fundamentals.  
•    Demonstrable technical leadership and mentorship skills  
•    Hands-on experience in software performance profiling, analysis, and optimization.   
•    Experience with industry leading benchmarking tools such as FIO, Crystaldiskmark, IOR, MLPerf, VDBench, HammerDB, SysBench etc.  
  
Preferred Qualifications:  
•    BS degree in Computer Science or related field (MS preferred)  
•    Hands-on experience developing services on a public cloud platform (e.g., AWS, Azure, GCP)  
•    Experience driving security reviews, documentation, UX reviews, and working with Product Managers from inception through launch  
•    Building continuous integration/deployment pipelines with robust testing and deployment schedules  
•    Proven experience in distributed system or cloud infrastructure performance.  
Nice to Have:  
•    Experience with cloud storage services such as OCI, AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.  
•    Strong SQL skills and database performance tuning.  
•    Familiarity with containerization and orchestration tools like Kubernetes.  
•    Experience with application profiling and workload simulation.
