### Description\nJoin a leading energy company in Cologne that has reliably supplied around 900,000 people with electricity and positive energy for over 20 years. As a Senior Brand Strategist, you will be driven by a forward-looking mindset, aiming to make brands tangible and truly inspire people. In this pivotal role, you will take responsibility for the strategic positioning of the brand, shaping its corporate identity, design, and campaigns. You will translate market research into compelling communication projects and define the strategic framework for a consistent brand presence across all touchpoints, actively contributing to a positive, sustainable future narrative that inspires individuals towards their own energy transition.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Act as a strategic partner to the Head of Brand Marketing, supporting strategic and technical decisions related to brand positioning, identity, and direction.\n- Steer the strategic positioning of the brand, ensuring its continuous development and clear differentiation from the group's sales brand.\n- Engage with digitalization strategy topics and actively shape a positive, sustainable future narrative that inspires people towards their own energy transition.\n- Deputize for the Head of Brand Marketing and take on operational team leadership tasks, including planning and moderating dailies, planning & refinements, preparing team retrospectives or annual plans, and consolidating various project strands.\n- Oversee the management of strategy and market research agencies, working closely with the company's market researchers and corporate teams.\n- Advise the campaign team on the strategic and creative direction of campaigns and all communication activities, supporting the operationalization of the strategic positioning.\n- Develop guidelines and serve as a continuous strategic sparring partner for CI/CD colleagues, supporting the implementation of the unique brand look, corporate language (Tone of Voice), and Sound DNA.\n- Act as an impulse generator and sparring partner for all operational teams regarding brand strategic integration and verification of all market activities for brand fit (e.g., social media, performance, partner selection).\n- Particularly focus on brand experience at digital touchpoints such as the app and website, supporting and guiding their owners with clear Brand Experience Principles and Guidelines.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Hold a degree in Marketing, Media, Business Administration, or a comparable field; however, your practical experience (\"Doing\") is key.\n- Be a true strategist, capable of convincing others through well-founded arguments, clear discussions, and presentations with a stringent storyline.\n- Possess a passion for creation and bold ideas, believing a strategy only makes sense when brought to life.\n- Demonstrate visible project successes and several years of professional experience in leading a strong brand.\n- Understand that successful brand management requires not only strategic skill but also a significant portion of intuition for the effective use of innovation and creativity.\n- As a professional, especially in digital marketing disciplines, make far-reaching, data-driven decisions with an analytical-conceptual \"doer\" mentality, incorporating market research knowledge.\n- Be able to inspire others with your enthusiasm and excel in a team with high self-motivation and assertiveness.\n- Believe good ideas are not just a gut feeling: you investigate thoroughly and draw correct conclusions based on tests and data.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis Senior Brand Strategist role offers an exciting opportunity to shape the future of a well-established energy brand deeply committed to sustainability. The company truly walks the talk on work-life balance and flexibility, offering self-managed hours, hybrid work options, and even a subsidy for home office equipment.
