**About Resolve AI**
--------------------

*   At Resolve, we’re building Agentic AI that empowers software engineers by automating production engineering and SRE workflows. Our models deeply understand production systems — from code to databases — taking on repetitive, high-pressure tasks and handling critical incidents autonomously, so engineers can focus on building.
    
*   Our founders ([Spiros Xanthos](https://www.linkedin.com/in/spiros/) and [Mayank Agarwal](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mayank-ag/)) are the core creators of OpenTelemetry and led Splunk Observability. They have had 2 successful exits to Splunk and VMware.
    
*   We raised a $35M Seed round from top-tier investors like Greylock, Unusual Ventures, Jeff Dean (Chief Scientist, Google DeepMind), Thomas Dohmke (CEO, GitHub), Matt Garman (CEO, AWS), Reid Hoffman (Founder, LinkedIn) and Fei Fei Li (Professor, Stanford).
    

**What You’ll Do**
------------------

You will be the voice of Resolve AI across social channels—building and maintaining a presence that feels impossible to ignore. This role is both strategic and hands-on:

*   **Own and manage the content calendar** for LinkedIn, X, and emerging platforms (potentially Reddit, Substack).
    
*   **Identify and jump on relevant industry trends** to inject a bold Resolve AI perspective.
    
*   Collaborate cross-functionally with Recruiting, Product, Sales, and Marketing to generate content from customer stories, product launches, culture moments, and thought leadership.
    
*   **Proactively engage in online discussions** to build the brand’s presence and spark debate in the software engineering and AI community.
    
*   **Monitor conversations across platforms** to find entry points for high-impact commentary and trend participation.
    
*   **Develop lightweight but high-quality** assets (text posts, carousels, short-form videos, reels) that balance speed with creativity.
    
*   **Use AI tools and workflows to scale** ideation, asset creation, and experimentation.
    

**What We’re Looking For**
--------------------------

*   **Proven track record** of growing engagement and impressions for a B2B SaaS or tech brand.
    
*   **Finger on the pulse of AI**, software engineering culture, and broader tech trends—knows what’s happening _now_.
    
*   **Creative urgency**: able to turn ideas into live content within hours, not days.
    
*   **Skilled at fostering conversations**, not just publishing announcements.
    
*   **Experience with AI-powered creative workflows** (e.g., generating ideas, scripts, visuals).
    
*   **Cross-functional collaborator** who can work with leadership, engineers, and marketers seamlessly.
    
*   **Comfortable both creating original content** and curating voices from the community.
