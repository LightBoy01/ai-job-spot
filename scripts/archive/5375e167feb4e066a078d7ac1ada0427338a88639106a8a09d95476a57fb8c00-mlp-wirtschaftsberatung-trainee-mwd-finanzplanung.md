### Description
At MLP Wirtschaftsberatung, we offer ambitious talents the unique opportunity to dive into the diverse world of financial and business consulting through a six-month trainee program. Join us in the vibrant heart of Hamburg and actively shape the future of our clients – and your own.

This isn't just a job; it's a launchpad for your career. You'll benefit from exceptional flexibility and autonomy to design your working hours, ensuring a healthy work-life integration. We offer attractive, performance-based earning potential that rewards your dedication and success. Your growth is paramount, supported by personalized mentoring from experienced professionals and a comprehensive training program. You'll thrive in a dynamic, motivated, and youthful team characterized by flat hierarchies and an open communication culture.

### Responsibilities
As a Consultant Trainee, you will:
*   Provide individual consultation alongside your team to demanding private and business clients across wealth management, investments, real estate financing, and retirement planning.
*   Build comprehensive technical and sales expertise to systematically develop your consulting capabilities.
*   Independently manage projects and actively contribute your ideas to strategic questions.
*   Embody an entrepreneurial mindset, acting as an "entrepreneur within the company" and significantly contributing to the development of new concepts and processes.

### Qualifications
To succeed in this role, you should have:
*   A successfully completed university degree or be on the verge of graduation with compelling academic achievements.
*   A strong sense of initiative, goal-orientation, and an entrepreneurial mindset.
*   Confident and empathetic communication skills, both within a team and in client dialogues.
*   A deep commitment to personal development, seeing it not as a secondary goal, but as an integral part of your professional ethos.
