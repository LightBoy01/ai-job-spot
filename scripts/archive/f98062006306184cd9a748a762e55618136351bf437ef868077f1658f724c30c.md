We are hiring! 

We’re on the lookout for an experienced **Cost Accountant** to manage and coordinate the finance and cost management accounting functions within the Mill, including Pulp, Utilities and Paper departments.

**As a Cost Accountant, you will be responsible for:**

*   Investigates and propose changes to the accounting system.
*   Ensure that all activities comply with health, safety, and environmental requirements.
*   Compiles the monthly fixed and variable costs forecast and compares actuals/results.
*   Ensure the availability of accurate weekly and monthly returns and cost management information.
*   Ensures that all accounting deadlines are met and that provisions and costs are accounted for monthly.
*   Ensures accuracy of costing data in the system for the Mill.
*   Submits daily, weekly and monthly SAP FI/CO reports to Mill Managers accompanied by explanations on variances.
*   Manages profitability initiatives and assist the Mill and Plants with AD HOC profitability studies.
*   Develop and submit proposals for new policies, procedures, and expanded services to the Management Accountant.
*   Ensure the availability of accurate production data and statistics, variable production costs, and management reporting.
*   Prepares annual budgets by updating the bill of materials and cost based on the proposed production plan and assists the Management Accountant with the overall budget process.
*   Assists the Management Accountant with month end close and financial reporting.
*   Assists the Management Accountant with financial forecasts for the Mill (RFC, FC1, FC2, FC3).
*   Driving Real-Time Costing for the mill. 
*   Ensures the joint venture accounting records are accurate, complete and current. 

**What are we looking for?** 

*   Relevant accounting degree (majors in cost and management accounting will be advantageous)
*   Minimum 2 years experience in a costing environment
*   SAP
*   Proficient in Excel
