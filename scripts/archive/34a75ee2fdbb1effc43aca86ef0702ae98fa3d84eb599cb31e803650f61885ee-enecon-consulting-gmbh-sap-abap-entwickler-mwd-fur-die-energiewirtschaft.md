### Description

ENECON Consulting GmbH stands for tailored system integration in the energy sector. Our team combines a deep understanding of industry-specific processes with proven SAP expertise, bringing this specialized knowledge to every project. Our goal is to drive the digitalization of our customers' business models, unlock efficiency potentials, and create sustainable solutions for a future-proof energy industry.

If you want to actively shape the future of the energy sector and contribute your SAP expertise to demanding projects, we look forward to getting to know you!

### Responsibilities

*   Developing and customizing SAP applications with a focus on ABAP/ABAP Objects within the SAP IS-U environment.
*   Designing and implementing technical solutions, as well as performing customizing within the IS-U module.
*   Implementing interfaces and WebServices for SAP and third-party systems.
*   Analyzing requirements and transforming them into efficient solutions for our customers.
*   Bringing experience in at least two of the following modules: IS-U, FI-CO, SD/MM, FI, EDI, HCM, EWM, HR, PS.
*   Ideally possessing experience with S/4HANA, Fiori, UI5, and IDoc.
*   Processing incidents and changes in 2nd and 3rd-level support.

### Qualifications

*   Completed degree in (Business) Informatics or a comparable education with relevant professional experience.
*   Several years of experience in **SAP development**, ideally within the **SAP IS-U** environment.
*   Profound knowledge in **ABAP/ABAP Objects**, with desirable experience in **IDEX, PI**, or other interfaces.
*   Strong analytical skills and excellent communication skills.
