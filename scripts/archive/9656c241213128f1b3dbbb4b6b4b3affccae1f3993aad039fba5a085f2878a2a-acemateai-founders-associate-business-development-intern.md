### Description\
acemate is pioneering the leading AI learning & teaching platform for higher education institutions, experiencing significant traction and rapid growth. Founded less than a year ago, acemate has quickly become Germany's foremost AI learning platform for higher and further education, trusted by top-tier institutions including the University of Cologne and WHU.\
\
**Our Mission:** We aim to democratize access to personalized learning, empowering educators to design and deliver scalable, personalized learning experiences without replacing them.\
**Our Growth:** With secured pre-seed funding, we are rapidly expanding into new regions and markets, beyond our current focus on higher education.\
**Our Culture:** We are a young, driven team passionate about solving education's toughest challenges. Our \"builder culture\" prioritizes ad-hoc discussions and maximizes focus on coding and creating, minimizing scheduled meetings. We operate in a small, high-performing team environment that values learning, autonomy, and flexibility, including remote work options, alongside frequent team events and parties.\
\
### Responsibilities\
As a Founder's Associate Intern / Working Student, you will work directly with our CEO (Antong) and CPO (Paul) on high-impact initiatives spanning strategy, sales, marketing, product, operations, and growth. Your key tasks will include:\
\
*   Driving strategic initiatives, from market expansion and pricing to new partnerships and product launches.\
*   Supporting the sales cycle by identifying promising leads, preparing sales calls, and advancing opportunities.\
*   Boosting our B2C presence through social media (organic, paid, influencer), SEO/SEA, referral loops, and ambassador programs.\
*   Turning data into action by analyzing product data and user behavior to identify growth levers and test monetization ideas.\
*   Building our brand by contributing to content, events, community engagement, and thought leadership.\
*   Taking charge of internal operations by improving workflows, processes, and scaling internal systems across finance, investor relations, and tooling.\
\
### Qualifications\
We are seeking an individual who meets the following criteria:\
\
*   **Relevant experience:** You have experience in startups, consulting, VC, investment banking, or other fast-paced, high-performance environments.\
*   **Strategic & hands-on:** You possess the ability to think broadly and strategically, yet are equally ready to roll up your sleeves and execute.\
*   **Analytical mindset:** You are comfortable with numbers, structured thinking, and adept at converting data into actionable decisions.\
*   **Excellent communicator:** You are fluent in both German & English, and confident in leading conversations with users, customers, and decision-makers.\
*   **Commitment to excellence:** You are driven to exceed expectations when it truly matters.\
*   **AI-native:** You seamlessly integrate AI into nearly every workflow, using it as a tool to enhance your thinking, not replace it.\
*   **Availability:** You are available for 3-6 months and can work in Berlin.\
\
