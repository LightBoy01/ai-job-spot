MaintainX is the world's leading Asset and Work Intelligence platform for industrial and frontline environments. We are a modern IoT-enabled cloud-based tool for reliability, safety, and operations on physical equipment and facilities. MaintainX powers operational excellence for 12,000+ businesses including Duracell, Univar Solutions Inc., Titan America, McDonald's, Brenntag, Cintas, Xylem, and Shell.

We recently completed a $150 million Series D funding round, bringing our total funding to $254 million and valuing the company at $2.5 billion.

We are currently seeking a talented and motivated Director of Public Relations to join our team.  
  
As the Director of Public Relations you’ll be responsible for shaping and executing a comprehensive PR strategy that drives conversation and awareness while elevating MaintainX's brand. The ideal candidate is a creative and critical thinker with a proven track record in implementing strategic PR programs, has built-in media contacts, and is excitement about joining a company that is the category leader. 

**What you’ll do:**

*   Build and maintain trusted relationships with top‑tier business, trade, and industry media to advance awareness, reputation, and our corporate narrative.
*   Lead earned and paid media programs—including media sponsorships—that reinforce corporate reputation and brand positioning.
*   Build executive visibility: create thought‑leadership platforms and provide strategic counsel on speaking engagements, panels, and awards to align with corporate priorities and amplify key messages.
*   Develop clear, accessible media materials (press releases, key messages, and talking points) tailored to audience needs.
*   Deliver media training and coaching to executives, preparing them to engage confidently and effectively in media and public forums.
*   Collaborate with product, HR, partnerships, and marketing to ensure cohesive messaging and maximize media opportunities.
*   Plan and lead communications and media strategy for product launches, key events, and milestone announcements.
*   Measure and report weekly and quarterly PR performance; use data‑driven insights and external benchmarking to refine strategy for reach, engagement, and reputational impact.
*   Test emerging technologies and continually experiment with AI to streamline workflows, improve efficiency, and increase the accuracy of data, metrics, and reporting.

**About you:**

*   Bring 8+ years of public relations experience (in‑house or agency).
*   Have a background in manufacturing or other industrial sectors (highly preferred).
*   Demonstrate exceptional strategic writing skills—crafting corporate messages, standby statements, executive talking points, and briefing materials—and produce high‑quality content independently.
*   Think creatively and implement innovative communication platforms and tools, including leading‑edge PR strategies.
*   Bring strong press relationships and an active media network.
*   Present confidently with meticulous attention to detail.
*   Respond to crises and challenging situations quickly and professionally.
*   Thrive in a fast‑paced environment.
*   Stay at the forefront of how AI is reshaping the media landscape and apply those insights to strategy, pitching, and measurement.
*   Have working knowledge of Muck Rack (preferred).
