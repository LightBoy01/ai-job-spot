### Description
We are seeking a dynamic and results-driven Manager to lead our Meetinn and Customer Service teams. This pivotal role involves overseeing, coaching, and developing two key customer-facing departments. You will be instrumental in driving strategic pricing, identifying growth opportunities through customer analysis, and ensuring seamless cross-functional collaboration with departments such as Marketing, Legal, Finance, Sales, and Operations. This position offers a high degree of creative freedom, an appreciative work climate, and attractive development opportunities within a modern, future-oriented company that embraces an open "Du-Kultur" (informal address) at all levels. We offer flexible working hours, partial home office, and a strong commitment to employee well-being and professional growth.

### Responsibilities
*   **Leadership & Team Development**: Oversee, coach, and further develop the Meetinn and Customer Service teams.
*   **Interface & Coordination**: Work closely with all relevant departments (including Marketing, Legal, Finance, Sales, Operations).
*   **Pricing & Offer Management**: Regularly review and adjust price structures to ensure competitiveness and profitability.
*   **Customer Analysis & Business Development**: Analyze existing customers and opportunities to identify growth potential.
*   **Marketing & Promotion**: Develop and implement targeted promotional activities in collaboration with the Marketing team.
*   **Service Provider Management**: Select, manage, and quality control external partners and service providers.
*   **Reporting & Tools**: Utilize **Amadeus Delfi**, **Salesforce**, and **Power BI** for analysis and steering of relevant KPIs.

### Qualifications
*   A degree in Business Administration or a comparable education – **alternatively, several years of relevant professional experience in a similar position**.
*   Professional experience in Customer Service, Sales, or Conference Management, ideally with leadership responsibility.
*   Strong passion for sales and enjoyment in acquiring, retaining customers, and exploiting potential.
*   Results-orientation and entrepreneurial thinking with a clear focus on revenues, margins, and target achievement. Additionally, resilience and stress resistance are part of your DNA.
*   Analytical skills and a very good understanding of sales strategies, market mechanisms, and customer needs.
*   Proficient in CRM and BI tools (ideally Salesforce, Power BI).
*   Strong communication skills, empathy, a hands-on mentality, and enjoyment of cross-functional collaboration.
*   Very good German language skills, both written and spoken.
