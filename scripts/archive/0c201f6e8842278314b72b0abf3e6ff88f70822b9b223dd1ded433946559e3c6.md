**In one sentence**
-------------------

The Principal Consultant specializing in Presales at Amdocs Studios plays a pivotal role in driving transformation for enterprise clients using the Amdocs Studios technology portfolio. This position is responsible for originating, developing, and closing high-impact transformation deals by leveraging modern technologies and innovations in Cloud, AI, data, and platform modernization. The role requires deep technical expertise, strong solution-selling skills, and the ability to translate complex business challenges into actionable technology strategies.

**What will your job look like?**
---------------------------------

_Key Responsibilities:_
-----------------------

*   Architect and present high-impact, composable technology solutions that address diverse and complex client challenges across cloud, AI, data, and application modernization.
*   Lead technical discovery workshops and requirements-gathering sessions with C-level, business, and technical stakeholders to align business objectives with technology outcomes.
*   Design and develop proof-of-concept environments, prototypes, and live demonstrations to showcase Amdocs Studios’ technical capabilities and value.
*   Author end-to-end architectural blueprints, solution designs, and technical documentation to support client decision-making and successful project delivery.
*   Scope, size, and define work packages for Studio-led engagements, ensuring alignment with client requirements, delivery capabilities, and best practices in modern engineering.
*   Collaborate with business development and delivery teams to produce compelling proposals, RFP/RFI responses, and sales collateral, and contribute thought leadership through blogs and industry presentations.

**All you need is...**
----------------------

_**What you will bring to the role:**_

*   **Consultative Sales and Solution Architecture:** **Strong consultative skills with the ability to map business challenges to technical solutions**
*   **Technical Solution Selling:** Proven track record in selling and solutioning complex engagements including: cloud platform and data solutions, application modernization and automation.
*   **Transformation Expertise**:&nbsp; Experience solutioning or delivering transformation programs with measurable outcomes.
*   **Technical Domain Leadership:** &nbsp;Significant Experience with Software Engineering (Data, Cloud, AI)
*   **Excellent Communication:** Superior written, verbal and presentation skills for technical and executive audiences

Amdocs Studios Principal Consultants combine technical depth, commercial acumen, and a passion for innovation to drive client transformation agendas, build market presence, and accelerate growth.

-------------------------------

*   Form strong relationships with the leading Hyperscalers
*   People first organisation
*   Competitive Salary and Annual Incentive Plan
*   Unlimited Paid Annual Leave
