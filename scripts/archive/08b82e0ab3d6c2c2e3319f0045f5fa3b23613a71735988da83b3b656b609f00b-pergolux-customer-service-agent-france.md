### Description\nPERGOLUX is a rapidly growing e-commerce company in the Home & Garden sector, specializing in the design, manufacture, and sale of high-quality pergolas. Founded in Norway, PERGOLUX has expanded its presence to over 15 countries across Europe, Australia, North America, and South America, constantly seeking new growth opportunities. We are passionate about outdoor living products and dedicated to making our customers' dreams possible. Join our high-performance, international team, where you'll collaborate with amazing colleagues in a dynamic environment focused on continuous growth and customer satisfaction.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Handle complex customer inquiries and escalations professionally across multiple channels (phone, email, chat)\n- Provide expert advice for technical and installation questions\n- Independently manage cancellation requests, claims, returns, and refunds, including the verification and accuracy of issues\n- Collaborate closely with transport service providers, logistics, warehouse, and product development teams\n- Ensure high customer satisfaction and improvement of our NPS\n- Mentor and support junior team members\n- Data maintenance and process optimization to increase efficiency and service quality\n\n### Qualifications\n- Excellent communication skills in French (C2) and proficiency in English\n- Experience in customer service (at least 1-2 years), ideally in e-commerce or a similar industry\n- Strong communication skills, along with a solution-oriented and empathetic working style\n- Enjoyment of working in a dynamic team and a strong customer service mindset\n- Good self-organization and time management skills are required to work efficiently\n- IT affinity and the ability to quickly adapt to new systems and tools. Familiarity with Zendesk, Shopify, and Aircall preferred\n- Residence in Frankfurt/Berlin or the surrounding area\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at PERGOLUX offers a fantastic opportunity for an experienced Customer Service Specialist to make a significant impact within a rapidly expanding international e-commerce company. Beyond handling complex customer interactions, you'll be instrumental in driving customer satisfaction (NPS improvement) and optimizing key processes. The expectation to mentor junior team members highlights the company's commitment to fostering a supportive and growth-oriented environment, where professional development is a strong focus. PERGOLUX embraces a hybrid work model (80% on-site in Frankfurt am Main or Berlin, 20% remote) and cultivates a vibrant team spirit through regular employee events. If you possess excellent French and English communication skills, 1-2 years of relevant experience, and a proactive, solution-oriented mindset, you'll thrive in this dynamic and collaborative setting. The company is actively looking for candidates to start as soon as possible, offering employee discounts, access to corporate benefits, and free drinks and snacks to enhance the work experience. It's an ideal role for someone looking to grow their career in a high-performance, international team.
