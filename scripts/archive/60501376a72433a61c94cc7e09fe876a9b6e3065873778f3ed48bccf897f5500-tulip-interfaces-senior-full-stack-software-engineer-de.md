### Description
This role is based in **Munich, Germany**, operating within a hybrid work environment that requires 3+ days in the office per week.

Tulip is a global leader in frontline operations, dedicated to empowering workforces with connected apps that drive higher quality, improved efficiency, and end-to-end traceability. Through its cloud-native, no-code platform, Tulip enables companies of all sizes and across diverse industries to implement composable solutions. These solutions address critical operational challenges such as error-proofing processes, boosting productivity, capturing real-time data, and fostering continuous improvement.

A spin-off from MIT, Tulip is headquartered in Somerville, MA, with additional offices in Germany and Hungary. The company is at the forefront of disrupting the Manufacturing Execution System (MES) category with its focus on composable, human-centric solutions for industrial environments. Tulip has earned recognition as a World Economic Forum Global Innovator and has been named one of Energage's Top Workplaces USA and one of Built In Boston's "Best Places to Work" and "Best Midsize Places to Work" for 2024.

**About You:**
You are a passionate builder eager to push products forward and create delightful user experiences. You possess a strong product mindset, empathy for the end-user, and a continuous desire to learn new technologies. Thriving in dynamic, fast-paced environments, you are comfortable navigating a large technology stack and contributing across different parts of the platform. A genuine curiosity and passion for artificial intelligence will be a significant asset in this role.

### Responsibilities
As a Software Engineer, you will:
*   Work on innovative products at the forefront of conversational AI.
*   Write and maintain clean, high-quality, and efficient code that aligns with project specifications.
*   Execute comprehensive testing and seamless deployment of programs and systems.
*   Communicate technical information clearly and effectively with fellow team members.
*   Partner with developers to collaboratively design and refine software algorithms and flowcharts.
*   Successfully integrate various software components and external third-party programs.
*   Proactively troubleshoot, debug, and enhance existing software solutions.
*   Collect and analyze user feedback to inform product evolution.
*   Propose and implement strategic improvements to our software.
*   Develop and maintain thorough technical documentation for internal reference and reporting.

### Qualifications
**Required Skills:**
*   5-7+ years of software engineering experience, ideally at a SaaS enterprise software company.
*   Strong proficiency in TypeScript.
*   Familiarity with API design and integration (RESTful services).
*   Proficiency in unit testing, integration testing, and debugging practices.
*   Ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a team.

**Preferred Skills (Nice to Have):**
*   Familiarity with our tech stack, including React, Redux, Node.js, Golang, Elixir, Electron, MongoDB, Postgres, and Docker.
*   Experience with machine tools and/or manufacturing industry.
*   Background working in startups.
*   Understanding of database design principles and optimization.
*   Knowledge of CI/CD pipelines and automated deployment strategies.
*   Understanding of prompt engineering and LLM customization.
