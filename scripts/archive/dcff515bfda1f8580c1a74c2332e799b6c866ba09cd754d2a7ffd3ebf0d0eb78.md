Join the CrowdGen team as an Independent Contractor for Project Rowan and earn while shaping the future of AI!

  

Are you detail-oriented and passionate about improving AI technology? Project Rowan gives you the opportunity to contribute directly to refining chatbot performance by evaluating and comparing AI-generated responses. Your insights will help ensure chatbots provide accurate, clear, and useful answers to real user questions.

  

Position Overview:

As an AI Response Evaluator, your role will involve reviewing and scoring chatbot answers, as well as comparing multiple responses to determine which best meets the user’s intent. Your evaluations will guide future improvements in AI training and reliability.

  

Key Responsibilities:

• Assess chatbot responses for accuracy, clarity, relevance, and completeness.

• Compare two AI-generated answers and select the stronger response.

• Provide consistent and objective feedback through the evaluation platform.

  

Requirements:

• Strong reading comprehension and critical thinking skills.

• Ability to follow detailed evaluation guidelines.

• Access to a reliable computer and internet connection.

• Commitment to completing all assigned tasks within deadlines.

  

Please Note: By applying, you confirm that you understand the time commitment required.

This is a project-based independent contractor role with CrowdGen. If selected, you’ll receive an email invitation to create a CrowdGen account using your application email. You’ll need to log in, reset your password, complete the setup steps, and then proceed with your application for Project Rowan.
