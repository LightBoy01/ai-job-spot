### Description\nJoin a fast-growing company revolutionizing fleet management with a data-driven approach, passionate about efficiency and transparency. Leveraging our proprietary AI platform and strong partner networks, we measurably optimize fleet processes, reduce operating costs, minimize downtime, and create financial security for all stakeholders. We are expanding our finance and controlling structures to ensure stability, trust, and sustainable growth.\n\nOur culture is defined by:\n*   **Precision, Stability & Transparency**: We deliver first-class financial, payment, and trust management.\n*   **Data-Driven Decisions**: Reliable figures, clear reports, and complete documentation form the basis of our choices.\n*   **Efficiency**: We optimize processes with passion – structured, smart, and solution-oriented.\n*   **Open Feedback Culture**: We continuously learn and grow, both as a team and an organization.\n*   **Uncompromising Quality**: Punctuality, diligence, and compliance are non-negotiable.\n\n**Your Mission**: As a Finance & Controlling Manager (m/w/d) specializing in trust processes, you will ensure our financial and payment structures operate smoothly, securely, and in full compliance with regulations. You will be responsible for transparent financial flows, fiduciary transactions, and precise, audit-proof reporting, building trust internally, with clients, and with auditing bodies.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Manage, maintain, and monitor client trust accounts.\n*   Control and execute fiduciary deposits and withdrawals according to contractual specifications.\n*   Ensure complete documentation of all client trust transactions and prepare account overviews and proofs.\n*   Perform regular account reconciliation and reporting for management, clients, and auditing bodies.\n*   Execute and monitor system-based payment runs, including file import, validation, and approval.\n*   Clarify returns, error messages, and reconciliations between payment runs and trust accounts.\n*   Support general payment transactions and ensure adherence to internal compliance and control guidelines.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Completed commercial training or a degree in Finance, Controlling, or Accounting.\n*   Experience with trust accounts, payment transactions, or financial processes – ideally in a regulated environment.\n*   Proficient in accounting or payment transaction systems (SFirm and Diamant are a plus).\n*   High level of accuracy, responsibility, and a strong understanding of compliance and segregation of duties.\n*   Enjoy structuring processes, eliminating sources of error, and creating transparency.\n*   Solution-oriented approach, pragmatism, and team spirit.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a pivotal role for a detail-oriented finance professional passionate about precision and compliance within a fast-growing, innovative company. If you thrive in an environment that values data, efficiency, and continuous personal growth, and offers a flexible, open work culture with attractive benefits like performance-oriented compensation, company participation, and various mobility perks, this is an excellent opportunity to make a significant impact on transforming the fleet industry. The company strongly invests in its employees' development and fosters a collaborative, forward-thinking spirit.
