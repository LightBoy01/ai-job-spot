About the role:
===============

The Director of Content Lifecycle Transformation (CLT) will lead the USBU CLT transformation by shaping, executing and collaborating with key cross functional partners to implement the CLT roadmap—a cornerstone for achieving organizational content goals and delivering exceptional customer experiences. As a key member of the Marketing Operations & Solutions Leadership Team (LT), the Director will be responsible for driving continuous transformative changes, fostering a culture of agility, efficiency and growth, and scaling organizational capabilities in alignment with related and ongoing initiatives such as Content Innovation. The ideal candidate will embark on this journey as an independent contributor, focused on delivering immediate priorities in partnership with the Head of Marketing Operations & Solutions. Over time, the scope of this role will expand to include leadership of the CLT team to drive the longer-term vision.

The objectives of the CLT role include:

*   Unify functions, initiatives, and capabilities under a cohesive strategy to ensure alignment and direction across the USBU.
    
*   Direct centralized governance and strategic oversight of the CLT roadmap to standardize and scale capabilities.
    
*   Champion enterprise -wide cross-functional collaboration to enhance communication, create synergies, and enable efficient knowledge and resource sharing.
    
*   Leverage unified resources and streamlined processes to rapidly scale capabilities, maximize impact, and drive efficiency.
    

How you will contribute:
========================

**Strategic Planning and Execution**

*   Lead the vision and strategy for the USBU content personalization goals
    
*   Design, deploy & manage roadmap implementation  for consistent execution with designated leaders and clear success/scale criteria
    
*   Connect CLT roadmap and Global workstreams, such as CI, to ensure an ongoing E2E view of capabilities and tech
    
*   Serve as primary strategic partner to other organizational efforts such as the Content Innovation team
    
*   Identify and liaise on technical architecture needs
    
*   Manage agencies and budget effectively
    

**Change Management**

*   Drives the global to local strategy across all pillars
    
*   Translates the org/transformation vision to a change mgmt. strategy representing diverse stakeholders/perspectives
    
*   Establish the goals, scope and approach to execute on the roadmap
    
*   Curate and disseminate USBU CLT communication's plan
    
*   Centralize USBU cross-brand initiatives and timelines into a single repository
    
*   Ensure collaboration & feedback loops across stakeholders' groups
    

**Governance**

*   Design Governance Framework: develop the overall governance framework, including principles, roles, responsibilities and decision-making processes
    
*   Stakeholder Engagement & Alignment: Engage stakeholders across the organization to ensure alignment with business goals, address concerns, and secure commitment to the governance structure
    
*   Communication & Reporting: Implement regular communication channels and reporting processes to keep governance participants and stakeholders informed
    
*   Continuous Improvement: Review and refine the governance framework and processes based on feedback, changing needs, and lessons learned
    

**Training**

*   Needs Assessment: Identify skill gaps and training needs through surveys, interviews, and performance analysis
    
*   Translates functional training needs and plans (e.g. Marketing, Marketing Operations, MLR) into the development of training programs
    
*   Leads functional training development with CI shared resource
    
*   Identifies and leads the development of SOPs, work instructions, and FAQs
    
*   Compliance and record-keeping
    

**Measurement & Optimization**

*   Establish CLT measurement plan & reporting cadence
    
*   Evaluate training effectiveness
    
*   Determine and deploy testing capability plans (Pilot, POC, test and learn)
    
*   Report progress on pilot, capability and technical milestones
