### Description\nWelcome to the Schüttflix Group! We are specialists in waste management and a proud part of the Schüttflix Group, driving the circular economy forward. We supply construction sites with recycled materials, making the construction industry more sustainable. To ensure everything runs smoothly and sustainably, we are looking for a **Branch Manager (m/f/d)** with experience in the construction materials or waste management industry for our hub in **Duisburg**. This is a pivotal leadership role where you will take overall responsibility for the organization and management of all areas and employees at our operational site, significantly contributing to our mission.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs our Branch Manager, you will be instrumental in leading our operations:\n-   **Site Master:** You will assume overall responsibility for the organization and leadership of all areas and employees at our operational site.\n-   **Operations Architect:** You will plan, steer, and control operational and business processes with the goal of designing them efficiently and sustainably.\n-   **Environmental Law Expert:** You will be responsible for the preparation, execution, and monitoring of all approval procedures, ensuring compliance with legal requirements.\n-   **Future Designer:** You will develop the annual economic plan and a viable medium-term plan, implementing them consistently.\n\n### Qualifications\nTo succeed in this role, you should bring:\n-   **Experience:** You have experience in a leadership position and are ideally familiar with the waste management industry.\n-   **Expertise:** You possess sound knowledge of waste and environmental law.\n-   **Working Style:** You tackle tasks independently and work diligently.\n-   **Communication:** You have a confident, friendly demeanor, along with strong communication and presentation skills.\n-   **Entrepreneurial Thinking:** You act strategically, recognize economic contexts, and proactively develop solutions to combine ecological and economic goals.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a seasoned leader passionate about sustainability to make a significant impact at the Schüttflix Group. You'll join an authentic company culture that is down-to-earth, passionate, and committed, where teamwork and mutual respect are paramount. The role offers substantial autonomy to shape the future of the Duisburg branch, driving both operational excellence and environmental stewardship. Beyond the satisfying challenges, Schüttflix provides a highly attractive compensation package, including contributions to company pension schemes, capital-forming benefits, and various private health supplementary insurances. Furthermore, you'll benefit from top-notch IT equipment, flexible vacation, a company car for private use, a company bike, gym memberships, employee discounts, and discounted train tickets – truly a comprehensive suite of benefits designed for your well-being and success. If you're ready to lead a vital operation with a purpose, this role offers both growth and genuine impact.
