### Job Description

At ADM, we unlock the power of nature to provide access to nutrition worldwide. With industry-advancing innovations, a complete portfolio of ingredients and solutions to meet any taste, and a commitment to sustainability, we give customers an edge in solving the nutritional challenges of today and tomorrow. We’re a global leader in human and animal nutrition and the world’s premier agricultural origination and processing company.   
  
We are looking for an experienced Process Engineer to join our team in the Adana plant.   
  
**GENERAL QUALIFICATIONS**

*   Bachelor's degree in Chemical Engineering,
*   Fresh graduate or 1 – 2 years of work experience,
*   Good command of English
*   Good knowledge of MS Office programs,
*   Willing to work in a team,
*   Problem-solving and analysis skills,
*   Researcher, follower, careful and result-oriented,
*   Successful in human relations,
*   Residing or able to live in Adana,

**JOB DESCRIPTION**

*   Monitoring of daily process parameters and continuity of production within specs
*   Ensuring compliance with occupational safety, food safety, HACCP, and GMP standards,
*   Ensuring the shift order according to the production plan,
*   Managing the staff and facility working in the shift,
*   Recording production data in the SAP system
*   Generating monthly production and efficiency reports in SAP
*   Generating reports on chemicals and consumables in SAP system
