### Description
Wolt is a technology company focused on creating joy, simplicity, and earnings through delivery services across over 500 cities in 30 countries. Since joining forces with DoorDash in 2022, Wolt continues its global expansion, emphasizing a challenging yet exciting work environment where team members are encouraged to learn, build, and ship more. The company values self-starters with drive and an entrepreneurial spirit.

The Merchant Services team is central to Wolt's mission, ensuring merchant success by providing a seamless experience. This role is for a proactive team member within the newly established Post Activation department. This department is vital for ensuring that newly onboarded restaurants on the Wolt platform perform excellently. This involves assisting partners with proper equipment training, guiding them on order acceptance improvement, and applying innovative thinking to continuously enhance partner performance.

### Responsibilities
As a proactive team member in Post Activation, you will be responsible for:

*   Analyzing partner performance data and acting on key performance indicators to drive improvement.
*   Monitoring multiple workflows related to partner performance enhancement and tracking progress using Monday.com.
*   Conducting in-person training for newly onboarded restaurant partners on hardware and app usage.
*   Proactively engaging with partners through both written and verbal communication to resolve issues and provide support.
*   Collaborating effectively with cross-functional teams, clearly formulating requests and tasks.
*   Continuously refining and improving approaches for partner and vendor communication, coaching, and training strategies.

### Qualifications
We are looking for a candidate with:

*   Prior experience in partner support or a communication-heavy role.
*   A positive demeanor coupled with a good amount of patience.
*   Excellent organizational skills, including the ability to multitask and track numerous open tickets across multiple workflows.
*   Outstanding verbal and written communication skills in both **German and English**.
*   A keen eye for detail, understanding that every small aspect contributes to success.
*   An \"outside the box\" mindset, ready to propose creative solutions to issues and improve workflows.
*   Experience in gastronomy or the food industry is a plus.
*   Familiarity with Google Suite is a plus.
