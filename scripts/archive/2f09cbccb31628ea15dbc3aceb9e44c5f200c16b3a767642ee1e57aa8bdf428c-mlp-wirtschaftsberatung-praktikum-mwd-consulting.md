### Description\nDo you want to gain practical insights into financial consulting, learn entrepreneurial thinking, and develop yourself personally and professionally? Located in Berlin, it provides hands-on experience, direct mentorship, and exposure to a wide range of financial topics. The emphasis on practical application, personal development, and the potential for a long-term career path post-internship makes this a highly valuable opportunity for those looking to build a solid foundation in the industry. The flexible hours are a significant bonus for students. If you're eager to learn and contribute in a dynamic environment, this role could be your perfect start.
