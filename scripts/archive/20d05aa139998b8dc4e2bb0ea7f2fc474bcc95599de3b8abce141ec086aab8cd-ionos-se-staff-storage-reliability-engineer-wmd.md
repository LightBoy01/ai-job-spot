### Description\nJoin IONOS, a leading European provider of cloud infrastructure, cloud services, and hosting solutions. We foster a collaborative culture with open structures, flat hierarchies, and a strong team spirit, believing that work and fun are compatible. We are rapidly growing and seeking a **Staff Storage Reliability Engineer** to join us.\n\nIn this role, you will be responsible for our global, **public Object Storage platform**, built on Ceph. You will develop scalable solutions and collaborate closely with our Operations team to build, deploy, and maintain these critical platforms.\n\nOur Ceph platform manages double-digit petabytes across multiple locations, experiencing rapid growth. It's a critical component of our internal and public infrastructure. You will actively contribute to its continuous development, enhance and maintain our production environments, and ensure sustained availability, performance, and security as we scale.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Ceph Deployment and Management:** Provision, configure, and operate Ceph clusters, managing storage pools, placement groups, and other core components.\n- **Performance Optimization:** Tune Ceph for optimal performance, eliminate bottlenecks, and ensure efficient resource utilization.\n- **Automation:** Develop and implement automation strategies for Ceph deployments, upgrades, and maintenance tasks.\n- **Troubleshooting and Problem Solving:** Diagnose and resolve complex technical issues related to Ceph storage, often in collaboration with other teams.\n- **Collaboration:** Cooperate closely with development teams, system administrators, and other stakeholders to integrate Ceph into various systems and applications.\n- **Staying Up-to-Date:** Monitor the latest Ceph developments, new features, and best practices, actively participating in the Ceph community to share knowledge.\n\n### Qualifications\n- **5+ years** of experience as a Senior Linux Engineer or Site Reliability Engineer, with a deep and broad understanding of Linux systems and networks.\n- Profound knowledge of Ceph architecture and administration.\n- Experience with cloud storage technologies (File, Object, Block).\n- Practical experience with automation tools (e.g., Ansible) and monitoring/observability solutions.\n- Familiarity with cloud platforms and container technologies (e.g., Docker).\n- Excellent troubleshooting, problem-solving, communication, and collaboration abilities.\n\n### Author's Take\nIONOS provides a dynamic and supportive environment for highly skilled engineers, valuing collaboration, personal growth, and work-life balance through its hybrid work model and flexible hours. Engineers will work on challenging, large-scale systems within Europe's cloud infrastructure, with ample opportunities for continuous learning and community engagement. The company fosters an informal culture with strong team spirit and a commitment to employee well-being, offering benefits such as subsidized canteens, modern offices, and health programs.\n\nAs a leading European digitization partner for over six million small and medium-sized businesses (SMEs) across 18 markets, IONOS offers comprehensive web presence, productivity, and cloud solutions. The company is committed to diversity and welcomes applications from all backgrounds.
