**The Impact You'll Make**
--------------------------

As an AI Specialist, you will be at the forefront of transforming enterprise operations through intelligent automation and data-driven decision-making. Your work will directly influence how we leverage AI to unlock efficiencies, elevate strategic insights, and create scalable value across our ERP systems. By championing innovation and continuous improvement, you’ll help shape a future-ready organization that embraces change and thrives on technological advancement.

**What You’ll Be Doing**
------------------------

*   Partnering with cross-functional teams to uncover operational challenges and identify high-impact AI opportunities aligned with business goals.
*   Ideating and prioritizing AI use cases across Finance, Supply Chain, Sales, and other domains to enhance ERP performance and decision-making.
*   Leading proof-of-concept (PoC) initiatives to validate AI solutions, measure outcomes, and refine implementation strategies.
*   Assessing the technical feasibility of AI solutions within existing ERP infrastructure, ensuring scalability, security, and long-term viability.
*   Designing a robust AI adoption framework that includes governance, data strategy, and integration pathways tailored to ERP systems.
*   Translating complex technical insights into actionable business recommendations for senior leadership.
*   Staying ahead of emerging AI trends and technologies, continuously exploring their relevance to enterprise transformation.

**What We're Looking For**
--------------------------

We’re seeking a curious, adaptable, and forward-thinking AI professional who thrives in dynamic environments and is passionate about solving real-world problems with cutting-edge technology. You should bring:

*   10+ years of IT experience, with at least 3 years in AI/ML development and deployment.
*   Strong command of supervised, unsupervised, and deep learning techniques.
*   Proficiency in Python, cloud platforms (Azure/AWS/GCP), and integration tools.
*   Expertise in Prompt Engineering for LLMs, Context Engineering, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
*   Solid understanding of MLOps practices including CI/CD, model monitoring, and versioning.
*   Familiarity with data pipelines, APIs, and system integration.
*   Experience with NLP, object detection, and deep learning frameworks.
*   Awareness of AI governance, ethics, and compliance.

We value a growth mindset—someone who is eager to learn, experiment, and evolve with the technology landscape.

**What You'll Get from Us**
---------------------------

*   A collaborative environment that encourages experimentation, learning, and innovation.
*   Opportunities to work on impactful projects that shape the future of enterprise AI adoption.
*   Support for continuous learning and professional development through access to cutting-edge tools and resources.
*   A culture that values agility, curiosity, and resilience—where your ideas and contributions are recognized and celebrated.
*   The chance to grow your career while helping others grow theirs, in a team that believes in shared success and long-term vision.
