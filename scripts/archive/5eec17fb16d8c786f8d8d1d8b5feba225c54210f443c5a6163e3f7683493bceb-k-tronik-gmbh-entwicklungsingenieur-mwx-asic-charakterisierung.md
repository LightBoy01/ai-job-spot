### Description\nAre you passionate about technology? So are we! We are looking for a Development Engineer (m/f/x) for ASIC Characterization to strengthen our expert team at our client, a company in the radio and communication industry. This is a permanent position with K-tronik. We look forward to getting to know you personally and working together with passion and team spirit on exciting projects and future-oriented technologies.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Perform characterization and validation of mixed-signal ASICs, RFICs, and MMICs.\n- Develop, set up, and optimize measurement setups for analyzing high-frequency and mixed-signal parameters.\n- Conduct and evaluate RF measurements on coaxial and wafer samples in the micro- and millimeter-wave range.\n- Automate characterization measurements, data acquisition, and evaluation using MATLAB, Python, or comparable tools.\n- Analyze and document measurement results and derive recommendations for design and test development.\n- Create cost and time estimations.\n- Support external test houses with series releases and qualification measurements.\n- Collaborate closely with design, test, packaging, production, and users to validate performance targets and ensure technological excellence.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree in Electrical Engineering, Communication Technology, Microelectronics, or a comparable technical education with a focus on RF technology.\n- Good knowledge of RF measurement technology, calibration, and de-embedding techniques.\n- Experience in characterizing semiconductor components (ASICs, MMICs, RF components).\n- Proficient use of measurement systems (e.g., VNA, SMU, oscilloscope, climate chamber, etc.).\n- Knowledge of automation and data analysis (Python, MATLAB or similar).\n- Analytical thinking, structured way of working, and enjoyment of complex technical challenges.\n- Communication and teamwork skills, as well as fluent German and English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting opportunity for an experienced ASIC Characterization Engineer to work on cutting-edge technologies within the radio and communication sector. K-tronik offers a truly supportive and collaborative environment, recognized as a Kununu TOP Company, emphasizing work-life balance with flexible hours and generous vacation. Candidates will benefit from individual development opportunities, comprehensive career support, and regular team events, fostering both professional and personal growth. If you are passionate about high-frequency technology, analytical challenges, and working in a friendly, team-oriented culture, this role provides an excellent platform to make a significant impact.
