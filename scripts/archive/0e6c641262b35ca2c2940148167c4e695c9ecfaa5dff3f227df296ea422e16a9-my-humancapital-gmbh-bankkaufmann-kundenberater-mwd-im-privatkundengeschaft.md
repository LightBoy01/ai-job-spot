### Description\n\nOur partner company in Munich is seeking a dedicated and experienced **Bankkaufmann / Kundenberater (m/w/d)** to join their private banking team. This role focuses on comprehensive client consulting and service, offering a long-term, unlimited employment opportunity starting as soon as possible.\n\n### Responsibilities\n\nAs a **Bankkaufmann / Kundenberater (m/w/d)** in private banking, you will:\n* Provide personal and comprehensive advice to clients on all financial and investment topics.\n* Conduct structured consultations on various products including checking accounts, online banking, building savings, insurance, pension plans, investments, and private loans – both in-person and digitally.\n* Coordinate with internal specialists or external partners, such as insurance advisors and construction financing experts, for specialized client inquiries.\n* Actively strengthen customer loyalty and contribute to long-term customer satisfaction through your excellent customer orientation and strong communication skills.\n\n### Qualifications\n\nTo be successful in this role, you should possess:\n* A completed vocational training as a Bankkaufmann (m/w/d), Finanzassistent (m/w/d), Kaufmann für Versicherungen und Finanzen (m/w/d), or a comparable commercial qualification with a focus on financial services.\n* Ideally, experience in private customer consulting and a consultant qualification according to § 34f GewO or WpHG.\n* Strong communication skills, a customer-oriented approach, and a confident demeanor in both personal and digital client interactions.\n* Proficiency in common MS-Office programs.\n* Good German language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\n\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a client-focused financial professional looking for a stable and supportive environment. The company offers a long-term, unlimited contract and prioritizes employee well-being with flexible working hours and home office options. New team members benefit from comprehensive onboarding with experienced colleagues, fostering a truly collaborative atmosphere. There's a strong emphasis on professional development, with numerous opportunities for further training and career advancement. You'll work in a dynamic, versatile environment characterized by flat hierarchies, quick decision-making, and appreciative teamwork, all within a centrally located office in Munich with excellent transport links. If you thrive on building lasting client relationships and are eager to grow, this role offers a compelling path.
