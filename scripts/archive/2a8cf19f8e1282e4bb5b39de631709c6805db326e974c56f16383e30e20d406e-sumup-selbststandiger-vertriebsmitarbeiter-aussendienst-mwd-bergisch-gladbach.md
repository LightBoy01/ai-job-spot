### Description

Are you an ambitious self-starter with the courage to work independently, shape your own success, and manage your time freely? Then this **Field Sales Consultant** role at SumUp is for you! We empower small businesses with smart, user-friendly financial solutions, from card payments to comprehensive POS systems, helping entrepreneurs focus entirely on their core business.

As a freelance Field Sales Consultant, you'll be your own boss, building relationships with local businesses and demonstrating how SumUp can simplify their operations. This is a direct sales role focused on our mobile card terminals and complete payment solutions. You'll benefit from a 100% performance-based compensation model with unlimited earning potential, maximum flexibility to work when and where you want, and access to top-tier products with high market potential. We also provide essential training and sales support to ensure your success.

### Responsibilities

*   **Active New Customer Acquisition**: Identify and win over local businesses for SumUp.
*   **Personal Meetings & Consulting**: Present our products and demonstrate their value in face-to-face interactions.
*   **Build Customer Relationships**: Secure long-term partnerships through excellent service.
*   **Own Customer & Lead Management**: Independently manage your client base and sales pipeline.

### Qualifications

*   Passion for sales and strong self-motivation.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer service is an advantage.
*   Excellent communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Very good German language skills.

### Author's Take

This is a compelling opportunity for highly motivated, entrepreneurial individuals who thrive on independence and direct results. SumUp offers a unique blend of complete autonomy—being 'your own boss'—with the backing of a well-established company providing high-potential products, training, and support. The 100% commission structure means your income is directly tied to your effort and success, offering genuinely unlimited earning potential for top performers. While the role demands significant self-discipline and initiative for new customer acquisition and relationship building, the flexibility to work when and where you want provides an attractive work-life balance. This role is ideal for experienced sales professionals or ambitious individuals with initial sales experience looking to take full ownership of their career and financial growth within a supportive ecosystem dedicated to empowering small businesses.
