As a family-led enterprise, it upholds values of trust, transparent communication, and partnership. The b.i.g. gruppe management gmbh is seeking a full-time **HR Specialist (Personalreferent)** to join their team in Halle (Saale) immediately. This role is crucial in reinforcing the company's commitment to being there for its employees, embodying the family's strength.\\n\\n### Responsibilities\\n- Serve as the primary contact person for employees throughout their entire \"employee lifecycle,\" providing independent and self-responsible consultation and support.\\n- Actively contribute to fostering a \"Feel Good\" atmosphere for employees.\\n- Collaborate closely with management to drive the development and improvement of HR processes.\\n- Provide comprehensive HR support for apprentices, from recruitment to successful completion, in close coordination with vocational trainers.\\n- Assist the team in the planning and implementation of personnel development measures.\\n- Maintain regular communication with chambers regarding vocational training and further education topics.\\n- Continuously optimize existing HR processes and lead various HR projects.\\n- Prepare all labor law relevant documents.\\n\\n### Qualifications\\n- Successfully completed a degree with a specialization in Human Resources Management or a comparable commercial education with further qualification as a certified HR Specialist (Personalfachkaufmann/frau).\\n- Several years of professional experience in human resources management, coupled with strong knowledge of labor law, social law, and social security law.\\n- Proven professional experience in supervising and mentoring apprentices.\\n- Possession of a trainer qualification (Ausbildereignung / AdA-Schein).\\n- Demonstrated high communication skills, strong service orientation, and excellent social competence.\\n- A self-reliant, structured, and team-oriented working approach is essential.\\n- Strong organizational, negotiation, and persuasion skills.\\n- Valid driver's license Class B (or old Class 3).\\n\\n### Author's Take\\nThis is a highly impactful Mid-Senior level HR Specialist role within a stable, family-led company that deeply values its employees and their development.
