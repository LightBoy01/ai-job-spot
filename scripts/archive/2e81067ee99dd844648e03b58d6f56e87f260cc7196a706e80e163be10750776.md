**Job Description:**

**Purpose:**

Nimble recognizes the potential power of AI/Machine Learning approaches to accelerate the discovery of novel peptide therapeutics, and we believe that the ability of our platform to generate massive data sets uniquely positions Nimble to be the leaders in this area. We are seeking a highly motivated Machine Learning Scientist to join Nimble to help drive our efforts to develop Machine Learning methods to accelerate peptide drug discovery. The successful candidate will be responsible for identifying and developing novel algorithms and pipelines and collaborating extensively with computational and experimental scientists to iteratively optimize the approach to Generative-AI enabled hit discovery.   

**Responsibilities:**

*   Develop machine learning and computational chemistry methods to enable discovery of peptides and/or peptide containing scaffolds that bind to target proteins.
*   Develop group to leverage various computational chemistry tools and machine learning architectures to accelerate design of peptide-based molecules.
*   Lead deployment of machine learning algorithms across various therapeutics areas.
*   Build data sets and develop novel strategies to quantify model performance.
*   Present results at internal and external scientific conferences.
*   Routinely demonstrates scientific initiative and creativity in research or development activities.
*   Highly autonomous and productive in performing laboratory research or method development, requiring only minimal direction from or interaction with supervisor.
*   Formulate conclusions and design follow-on experiments based on multidisciplinary data.
*   May initiate new areas of investigation that are scientifically meaningful, reliable, and can be incorporated directly into a research or development program.
*   A primary author of publications, presentations, regulatory documents and/or primary inventor of patents.
*   Understand and adhere to corporate standards regarding code of conduct, safety, appropriate handling of materials, controlled drug and radioactive compounds, GxP compliance, and animal care where applicable.
*   Direct mentorship of others

**Qualifications:**

**Qualifications:**

*   Bachelor’s Degree or equivalent education and typically 10 years of experience, Master’s Degree or equivalent education and typically 8 years of experience, PhD and no experience necessary.
*   Possess thorough theoretical and practical understanding of own scientific discipline.
*   Applied experience toward a quantitative science (e.g., Chemistry, Biology, Biochemistry, etc.) required
*   1+ year of experience applying deep learning frameworks (academic or industry) 
*   Experience using deep learning frameworks (PyTorch) to build and train models, apply transfer learning, and/or fine tune models
*   Fluent in Linux command-line, python (or other programming language if applied to deep learning), and git
*   Extensive experience working with large chemical and biological datasets, including graph, sequence, and structure-based data
*   Experience using cloud / compute / GPU clusters
*   Experience with protein structure modeling, design, or prediction algorithms ranging from Physics-based to deep learning architectures
*   Record of machine learning research excellence as evidenced by conference presentations or journal publications
*   Ability to work collaboratively on projects with multiple contributors

**Preferred:**

*   Experience developing or applying deep learning approaches for protein / peptide design
*   Experience developing novel strategies to quantify model performance, especially for diffusion and transformer-based models
*   Experience using experiment tracking platforms (e.g. MLFlow, TensorBoard, Neptune, Weights & Biases)
*   Experience contributing to drug discovery efforts
