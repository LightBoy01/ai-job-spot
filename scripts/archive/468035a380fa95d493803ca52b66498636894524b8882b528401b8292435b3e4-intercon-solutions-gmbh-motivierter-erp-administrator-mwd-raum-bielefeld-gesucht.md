### Description
Our client is a successful mechanical engineering company with approximately 500 employees. With high technical competence and innovative strength, the company develops and produces sophisticated machinery and systems for international customers.

In the course of its digital evolution, the ERP landscape is continuously being optimized and adapted to business processes. For this important endeavor, they are seeking an engaged personality who combines IT expertise with a deep understanding of mechanical engineering processes – and genuinely enjoys actively driving digitalization and process optimization. This is an excellent opportunity for an ERP Administrator to make a significant impact within a stable, innovative, and economically sound organization that values an open and appreciative corporate culture.

### Responsibilities
As an ERP Administrator, you will be crucial in ensuring the smooth and efficient operation of the company's core systems. Your key responsibilities will include:

*   Administering and maintaining the ERP system.
*   Ensuring system availability and optimizing performance.
*   Conducting regular system updates and patches, as well as adapting and developing interfaces to other systems (e.g., financial accounting, materials management, purchasing).
*   Monitoring and troubleshooting to guarantee seamless operation.
*   Managing users, assigning rights, and creating comprehensive role and authorization concepts.
*   Adapting and configuring ERP software according to the requirements of various specialist departments.
*   Supporting the implementation of new modules and functions.
*   Creating and maintaining technical documentation.
*   Collaborating with external service providers to resolve technical issues.
*   Providing training and support to end-users on how to effectively utilize the ERP system.

### Qualifications
To succeed in this role, you should bring a strong blend of technical skills and practical experience:

*   Completed vocational training or a university degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics, or a comparable qualification.
*   Several years of proven experience in the administration of ERP systems.
*   Profound knowledge of ERP software, ideally with familiarity in common systems such as SAP, Microsoft Dynamics, or Oracle.
*   Experience in database administration and SQL is considered a valuable advantage.
*   Demonstrated analytical and technical abilities.
*   High problem-solving competence and a structured approach to work.
*   Strong communication skills and a team-oriented mindset.
