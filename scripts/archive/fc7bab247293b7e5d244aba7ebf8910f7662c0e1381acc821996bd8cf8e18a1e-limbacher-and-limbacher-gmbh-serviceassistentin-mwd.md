### Description

Are you ready to become part of Germany's largest motorcycle center? As a Motorcycle Service Assistant (m/f/d) at Limbächer & Limbächer GmbH, you'll immerse yourself in the world of motorized passion. Here, everything revolves around making our customers' mobility dreams come true. You will be the first point of contact for our customers and support our team with dedication and passion.

With over 250 colleagues by your side, we offer a working atmosphere characterized by innovation, team spirit, and trust. We are committed to growth, honesty, and fairness, and we look forward to realizing motorcycle dreams together with you. If you have an affinity for motorcycles and enjoy customer contact, you've come to the right place. Embrace the challenge and become part of our community – where courage and reliability count.

### Responsibilities

*   Friendly and professional support for our service customers, from appointment scheduling to vehicle pick-up.
*   Organization, coordination, and allocation of workshop appointments in collaboration with the service advisor and workshop team.
*   Assistance with vehicle reception and handover, including preparation of documents and recording of relevant information.
*   Creation of service orders and administrative support for quote generation and invoice preparation.
*   Ensuring a smooth workflow in the service area through active communication and coordination processes.
*   Telephone and in-person customer support, as well as handling general customer inquiries.
*   Support with warranty and goodwill claims, as well as complaint processes in coordination with the service advisor.
*   Maintenance and updating of customer data, as well as processing correspondence and documentation.
*   Active participation in customer satisfaction initiatives, including customer reception, feedback collection, and care in the waiting area.
*   Support in the sales and accessories area, e.g., for service campaigns or follow-ups to foster customer loyalty.

### Qualifications

*   Completed commercial and/or technical training, or experience in sales (desirable, but not strictly required).
*   Career changers are welcome.
*   Friendly and pleasant demeanor, as well as a neat appearance.
*   Sense of responsibility, reliability, sociability, and communication skills.
*   Enjoy working with customers and in sales.
*   Diligent and precise working style.
*   Passion for motorcycles.
*   Class A and B driver's licenses are an advantage.
