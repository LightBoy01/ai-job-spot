**About the Opportunity**

We have an exciting opportunity to join Metcash as a **Retail Design Manager**. The Retail Design Manager will be responsible for delivering industry best practice and innovative retail designs for our Metcash Food & Grocery retailers. This will be achieved through effective management and co-ordination between Metcash, the Retailer and Developer.

**In this Role** 

*   Design and document new and refurbished supermarket fit-outs
*   Manage project approvals, ensuring alignment with Metcash standards and timelines
*   Coordinate design and construction documents across internal and external teams
*   Review third-party plans to ensure optimal store outcomes
*   Ensure compliance with regulations and tenancy requirements
*   Collaborate with stakeholders including developers, consultants, and internal teams
*   Conduct site investigations / measure-ups and prepare Existing Conditions, Feasibility and Concept Plans
*   Analyse store layouts to enhance customer experience and operational efficiency

**You’re Likely a Match If:**

*   Tertiary qualifications in Architecture or Interior Design.
*   Experience in supermarket or food retail design (preferred).
*   Proficiency in Autodesk Revit and AutoCAD (Revit family creation a plus).
*   Skills in Enscape or similar presentation tools.
*   Strong portfolio showcasing relevant 2D/3D design, documentation, and presentation.
*   Solid understanding of supermarket and/or food retail design requirements, including equipment, layout, operations, food safety, services (MEP), and compliance (BCA/NCC/DDA/AS).
*   Strong communication skills and a collaborative mindset.
*   A creative, innovative mindset with strong stakeholder and time management skills
