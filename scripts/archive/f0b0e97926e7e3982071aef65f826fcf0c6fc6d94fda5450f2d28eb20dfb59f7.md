Followmont Transport is looking for a reliable and detail-focused Local HR Driver to join our PUD team. If you take pride in consistent, high-quality work and value being part of a stable and supportive workplace, this could be the perfect role for you.

At Followmont, we know our success comes from people who do their work with care, accuracy, and professionalism. You’ll be trusted to handle the safe pick-up and delivery of a variety of freight, including manual handling of parcel freight, all with a strong focus on adhering to our established rules and regulations.

What You’ll Be Doing:

*   Completing local pickups and deliveries of general freight (including manual handling).
    
*   Managing deliveries with accuracy and efficiency, following established procedures.
    
*   Working at a steady pace in a stable and structured environment.
    
*   Maintaining high standards of safety and compliance at all times.
    
*   Adhere to established rules, regulations, and processes to ensure smooth operations.
    

This role will suit someone who:

*   Values consistency, reliability, and accuracy in their work.
    
*   Prefers a structured, steady-paced environment with clear processes.
    
*   Enjoys being part of a stable, supportive team but can still work independently.
    
*   Has a cooperative, professional approach and a passion for high-quality work.
