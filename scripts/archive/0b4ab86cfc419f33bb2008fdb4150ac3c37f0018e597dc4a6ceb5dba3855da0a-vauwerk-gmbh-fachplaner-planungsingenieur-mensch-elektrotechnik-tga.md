### Description
VAUWERK GmbH is an interdisciplinary team of architects, planners, and building services engineers, dedicated to developing intelligent planning, sustainable energy concepts, and modern building technology. We create economic, future-proof, and energy-optimized solutions for buildings. We are currently seeking an engaged Electrical Engineer to technically shape and responsibly develop our projects within the electrical engineering domain.

### Responsibilities
- Assuming technical planning responsibilities in electrical engineering, contributing to the technical and energetic excellence of challenging construction projects.
- Planning, dimensioning, and designing electrical systems for new construction and existing projects.
- Developing holistic supply concepts, including photovoltaic systems, storage systems, and charging infrastructure.
- Participating in all service phases (LP 1–9) according to HOAI (German Fee Schedule for Architects and Engineers).
- Creating planning documents, specifications, circuit diagrams, and calculations.
- Close coordination with project management, clients, and other specialist disciplines – up to commissioning.

### Qualifications
- Completed degree in Electrical Engineering, Energy Technology, Building Services Engineering, or Building Technology. Alternatively: a relevant electrical engineering vocational training with further qualification as a Techniker, Meister, or Fachplaner.
- Professional experience in planning electrical systems within the construction sector.
- Proficient in relevant norms, planning, and calculation tools.
- Strong interest in innovative building technology and sustainable energy solutions.
- Structured working style, excellent communication skills, and team-oriented approach.
- German language proficiency at a minimum C1 level.
