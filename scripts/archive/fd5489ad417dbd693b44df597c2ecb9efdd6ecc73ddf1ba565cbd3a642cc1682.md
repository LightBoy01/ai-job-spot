**About the Team**

Okta’s Business Technology organization powers the internal systems that drive how we operate, scale, and innovate. At the heart of this transformation is the Business Technology Shared Services team — building secure, enterprise-grade capabilities that embed GenAI and automation into the fabric of our business. From virtual agents and document intelligence to smart workflow platforms and AI-enabled service delivery, we’re creating foundational systems that support the next generation of productivity, self-service, and secure operations across Okta.

#### **The Opportunity**

As the Senior Manager of AI Engineering, you will lead an engineering team of Full Stack and AI/ML Engineers focused on building and operationalizing Applied AI and Agentic platforms that serve internal users across Okta. You'll be responsible for the delivery of complex, cross-functional projects and will work closely with engineering and product leadership to implement the long-term technical direction. You will play a key role in shaping how AI and automation are responsibly adopted across the enterprise.

This is a leadership role for a technical manager who can **coach and mentor a team**, manage project delivery, and ensure the execution of secure, intelligent platforms with broad business impact.

**What You’ll Do:**

*   **Manage and lead a high-performing engineering team** responsible for delivering AI/automation platforms, services, and internal tools
*   Partner with product, architecture, data science, and security leaders to **translate vision and direction into actionable plans** for the scalable, responsible adoption of GenAI technologies.
*   **Oversee end-to-end delivery of cross-functional projects**, including intelligent agents, AI-assisted workflows, and self-service automation frameworks.
*   Help implement and contribute to the evolution of Okta’s internal GenAI platform strategy, including **governance, experimentation frameworks, architecture patterns, and infrastructure.**
*   Uphold and champion engineering excellence standards across architecture, code quality, testing, CI/CD, observability, and platform reliability.
*   Collaborate with stakeholders across Business Technology and the enterprise (IT, HR, Finance, Customer Support, etc.) to **ensure technical execution aligns with business outcomes.**
*   **Coach and mentor team members**, fostering an inclusive, collaborative, and purpose-driven team culture.
*   Help develop and enforce technical standards for responsible AI use, security, and privacy within internal AI systems.

**What You’ll Bring**

*   10+ years of software engineering experience with at least 3+ years of engineering leadership (including people management).
*   Proven success leading high-impact teams and projects involving platform development, distributed systems, and cloud-native services.
*   Hands-on experience with AI/ML or GenAI technologies, including integration of LLMs, NLP systems, orchestration frameworks, and vector-based search.
*   Strong foundation in system architecture, secure development practices, and enterprise platform engineering.
*   Ability to influence stakeholders and drive alignment across engineering, product, data, and security organizations.
*   Track record of building and scaling teams and developing engineering talent.
*   Excellent written and verbal communication, with comfort navigating technical and non-technical conversations.
*   A strategic mindset, customer orientation, and a bias for impact and innovation.

**Why Join Okta:**

*   **Implement the AI Platform Vision:** Play a defining role in how GenAI and automation shape the future of work at Okta.
*   **Lead with Impact:** Build platforms used by thousands of employees and trusted across critical business functions.
*   **Grow a Great Team:** Mentor a passionate, mission-driven team committed to excellence, learning, and purpose.
