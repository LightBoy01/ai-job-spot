### Description\nAre you eager to redesign supply chain planning processes with state-of-the-art SAP technologies and guide companies through their digital transformation? This role offers the perfect opportunity. Our client is a globally leading consulting firm with over 340,000 employees across more than 50 countries. They specialize in supporting companies worldwide with digital transformations, leveraging the latest SAP technologies such as S/4HANA and Integrated Business Planning (IBP).\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Advise on the introduction and optimization of SAP IBP (e.g., Demand, S&OP, Supply Chain Control Tower).\n- Participate in national and international projects throughout the entire lifecycle – from roadmap definition to go-live.\n- Design and implement supply chain planning processes, including integration with adjacent SAP modules (PP, SD, MM, Inventory).\n- Lead workshops, create solution concepts, and take technical ownership within projects.\n- Support the continuous development of the consulting portfolio and coach junior colleagues.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Several years of project experience with SAP IBP, ideally across multiple end-to-end implementations.\n- Strong knowledge of Demand and Supply Planning, S&OP, and integration with S/4HANA.\n- Experience in customizing and designing planning processes.\n- Analytical and solution-oriented mindset, strong consulting skills, and excellent presentation abilities.\n- Very good proficiency in German and English; willingness to travel.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a seasoned SAP IBP Consultant to join a leading global firm. The role offers significant impact, working on complex international projects with cutting-edge technologies. The emphasis on continuous learning, career development, flexible work arrangements, and a supportive global network makes this a highly attractive position for those looking to advance their expertise and contribute to major digital transformations.
