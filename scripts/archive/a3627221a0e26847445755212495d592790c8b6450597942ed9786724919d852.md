Are you a strategic visionary in media and partnerships, eager to amplify brand visibility and spearhead growth? Do you excel in dynamic environments that leverage media strategy and thought leadership to make a meaningful impact? If so, we want to hear from you!

**About the role:**

Join Ipsos as the Director of Media & Partnerships within our Marketing and Communications department. This role offers the opportunity to significantly influence our brand's visibility, thought leadership, and business expansion. As a key player in a team renowned for its expertise across various sectors and service lines, you will be responsible for developing media strategies, managing press relations, positioning thought leadership, and cultivating partnerships that contribute to elevating Ipsos’ impact.

**About you:**

*   Proven expertise in media relations and strategy development.
*   Strong network-building capabilities.
*   Leadership acumen with a proactive mindset.
*   Proficient in AI use and adoption.
*   Adaptability and strategic thinking.

**Essential skills:**

*   Media strategy development and execution.
*   Leadership in dynamic environments.
*   Excellent communication skills.
*   Public relations expertise.

**Desirable skills:**

*   Background in communications/media.
*   Budget management experience.
*   Understanding of research methodologies.
*   Passion for media and communications.

Ipsos is one of the world’s largest research companies and currently the only one primarily managed by researchers, ranking as a #1 full-service research organization for four consecutive years. Simply speaking, we help the biggest companies solve some of their biggest problems, serving more than 5000 clients across the globe by providing research, data, and insights on their target markets.
