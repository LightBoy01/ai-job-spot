### Description\nRemmert, a long-standing family-led company since 1945, is a specialist in cutting-edge automated storage and logistics systems for sheet metal, long goods, and workpieces. We are known for our innovative solutions that optimize space and enable fully automatic movement of materials. As we continue our growth trajectory in the future-oriented machine building, automation, and logistics industry, we are seeking a dedicated Commissioning Engineer (gn) to join our team. Based in Löhne, Germany, this dynamic role involves significant international travel (approximately 50% or 100 overnight stays per year) to our diverse customer base, offering a unique opportunity to shape the future of industrial automation.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Lead the on-site commissioning, installation, simulation, and testing of our advanced software solutions for national and international clients.\n- Conduct comprehensive training sessions for customers, ensuring successful adoption and utilization of our systems.\n- Configure databases and software applications to meet specific client requirements.\n- Facilitate customer workshops focused on process definition and optimization.\n- Manage the roll-out of system updates and perform necessary exchanges and adjustments of electrical components.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A completed vocational training in automation or information technology, or a comparable relevant qualification.\n- Proven expertise in application installations, particularly within Windows environments.\n- This role is open to both career starters eager to learn and professionals with relevant experience in commissioning or automation.\n- A strong affinity for control technology is highly desirable.\n- Excellent communication skills in both German and English are essential for international collaboration and customer interaction.\n- Demonstrated ability to work independently and reliably.\n- Possession of a Class B driving license.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a driven individual looking to make a significant impact in the automation sector. Remmert offers a stable, family-oriented environment with a strong commitment to employee growth, well-being, and work-life balance. The extensive travel provides a unique chance to experience diverse international settings while working on sophisticated, cutting-edge technology. With robust benefits including hybrid work options, generous leave, numerous bonuses, and extensive development programs, Remmert clearly values its team members. This role is ideal for someone who thrives on technical challenges, customer interaction, and desires a career path with genuine long-term prospects within a supportive and innovative company.
