### Description\nAre you an IT System Administrator seeking a new challenge in NetApp Storage & Networking? Our partner company in Hamburg is looking for an **IT System Administrator (m/f/d)** to join their team as soon as possible.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   As an IT System Administrator, you will be responsible for the stable and secure operation of NetApp Storage systems in an enterprise environment and manage NetApp Object Storage platforms.\n*   In network administration, you will configure, integrate, and operate complex LAN, WAN, and WLAN infrastructures, as well as remote sites and VPN connections.\n*   To increase efficiency, you will automate IT processes and workflows using PowerShell scripts.\n*   You will support the preparation and execution of internal IT security audits in accordance with ISO 27001, contributing to compliance with regulatory requirements.\n*   Furthermore, you will actively participate in connecting and setting up new locations to continuously expand the company's IT infrastructure.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   You have vocational training as an IT Specialist (System Integration), a completed degree in Computer Science, or a comparable qualification – alternatively, you possess professional experience as a Network Administrator, IT System Technician, or IT Administrator.\n*   You have practical experience with virtualized platforms such as VMware ESX and Horizon, as well as in the administration of Windows and Linux servers.\n*   Ideally, you bring knowledge in Storage, Microsoft Active Directory (including GPOs), and network technologies such as VLAN, WLAN, QoS, or VoIP.\n*   Team orientation, a service-oriented mindset, analytical thinking, and a solution-oriented working style characterize you.\n*   Good German language skills complete your profile.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role offers an excellent opportunity for an experienced IT System Administrator to take on significant responsibility within a stable, market-leading company in Hamburg. The organization stands out for its progressive approach to employee well-being, offering an excellent work-life balance with a 39-hour week and substantial home office flexibility. The culture is highly appreciative and collaborative, fostering a 'Du' (informal) environment built on trust from management. Beyond the modern work environment and state-of-the-art equipment, the company is deeply committed to employee growth, providing numerous training and development opportunities. If you're looking for a role where your expertise in NetApp Storage and network administration will be valued, and where you can truly grow in a supportive and secure environment, this is an outstanding opportunity.
