### Description
KRUU, a leading photobox brand in Europe and the USA, is on a mission to help people capture, share, and relive magical moments through innovative hardware and smart software. To steer their product world holistically and drive the next chapter of growth, they are seeking a Head of Product. This pivotal role combines two key functions: serving as the central Product Owner for all KRUU products (photobox, app, and internal tools), and leading the newly formed Product department, establishing processes, managing OKRs, and fostering a professional product culture. This position requires deep operational product ownership initially, with a clear path to build and lead a growing team and department. The Head of Product will be instrumental in bringing clarity, focus, and priorities to the product landscape, preventing chaos, enabling speed, and preparing the company for significant growth.

### Responsibilities
*   **Product Ownership:**
    *   Hold complete Product Ownership for all KRUU products until individual Product Owners and Product Managers are hired.
    *   Act as the central Backlog Owner for the photobox, app, and internal tools.
    *   Prioritize and translate product requirements transparently into the backlog, preventing unprioritized topics from circulating.
    *   Lead cross-functional alignment between Growth, Operations, and Management.
    *   Collaborate closely with the development team to ensure they can focus fully on coding, improving speed, quality, and clear priorities.
*   **Leadership & Structure:**
    *   Lead and develop two part-time colleagues currently managing OKR coaching, App Project Management, and App Product Ownership.
    *   Build out the Project & Product department, establishing clear roles and processes.
    *   Improve the OKR process, ensuring clarity in goals and priorities, and aligning all teams with company objectives.
    *   Oversee impact checks after releases, monitor KPIs, and derive key learnings.
    *   Cultivate a professional product culture centered on data, customer insights, and clarity.

### Qualifications
*   Several years of experience as a Product Owner for digital products (mandatory).
*   Deep understanding of digital product development (coding not required, but understanding is key).
*   Ability to organize, prioritize complex information, and translate it into clear stories.
*   Very strong communication and moderation skills.
*   Structured, focused, ownership-minded, and pragmatic approach.
*   High degree of independence, decisiveness, and clear prioritization.
*   Excellent German and English language skills.
