### Description\nGHOST OFFICE is a full-service implementation agency based in Berlin, specializing in bringing creative concepts to perfect execution. We step in where creative agencies and marketing departments leave off, focusing on adaptation and flawless implementation across various media.\n\nThis **Senior Project Manager** role, available **full-time, starting immediately, with a hybrid work model (Berlin/remote)**, places you at the heart of our team, specifically serving a large German financial group. It's a pivotal position that blends classic marketing and communication project management with an active, strategic advisory role in client relationships. You'll keep projects on course while actively contributing to client meetings, planning, and strategic development.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Confidently, friendly, and reliably advise our clients.\n- Steer all project phases between the client, creative teams, and production.\n- Keep track of multiple jobs simultaneously, managing timings, capacities, and priorities.\n- Demonstrate expertise in both digital and classic media, including print advertising, online media, newsletters, digital marketing portals, and embrace topics like AI & automation.\n- Handle typical daily PM tasks: briefings & alignments, cost inquiries & calculations, timing & production planning, coordination of copywriters, graphic designers, and external service providers, and creation of client presentations, controlling lists, or research analyses.\n- Proactively think for your clients – identify needs, introduce new impulses, and actively shape discussions during virtual and on-site meetings in Berlin.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree or relevant training in Marketing/Communication, Media, or Advertising.\n- **Several years of professional experience in an agency environment** with a focus on project management / client consulting.\n- Strong structure, organizational talent, and reliability.\n- A good eye for design, text, and brands.\n- Team player with strong communication skills, empathy, and a desire for genuine client relationships with a pronounced customer and service orientation.\n- Proficient in MS Teams and standard Office applications.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a proactive and detail-oriented Senior Project Manager to join a dynamic, owner-managed agency. Ghost Office offers a refreshing blend of impactful project work for a major client, a culture that values \"familiäres Miteinander,\" flat hierarchies, and a strong emphasis on work-life balance and flexibility through hybrid work. If you thrive on independently steering complex projects, advising clients strategically, and embracing new challenges like AI, while also valuing a supportive and modern work environment in the heart of Berlin, this role is a perfect fit. It's a chance to significantly shape outcomes and grow within a forward-thinking team.
