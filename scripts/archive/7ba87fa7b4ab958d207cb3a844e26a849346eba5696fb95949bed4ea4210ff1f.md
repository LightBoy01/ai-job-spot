**What you will be doing**

As a strategic technology leader within Operations, this role co-owns the future-state roadmap alongside Business Technology, aligning operational priorities with broader digital initiatives. It goes beyond traditional product ownership by driving systems convergence, data-led decision-making, and the adoption of future-focused digital enablers.

The role also serves as a key advisor and escalation point for technology risks, system changes, and capability uplift across operational systems and infrastructure.

Your key responsibilities are:

*   Lead the definition and co-ownership of the operations digital technology roadmap in partnership with Business Technology. 
*   Act as senior technology partner to ORAT, Terminal, Airfield, Baggage, and Operations teams in shaping the selection and embedding of digital systems.
*   Identify and champion opportunities for future-facing technology investment (AI, automation, predictive analytics)
*   Ensure lifecycle resilience planning (BCP, DR, asset lifecycle)
*   Support the design and operationalisation of integrated performance dashboards, real-time insights, and decision support tools
*   Collaborate with internal teams and external partners to co-design and influence change management strategies, ensuring successful adoption of operational technologies.
*   Act as the operational product owner across key workstreams such as check-in, customer experience, and digital systems, while managing the check-in kiosk product and related vendor contracts.
*   Plan and prioritise technology investments, ensure operational requirements like capacity and resilience are met, and provide expert support during crisis situations involving digital tools.
*   Manage vendor performance to ensure accountability, delivery to service levels, and continuous improvement.

**What you’ll bring to the role**

At Auckland Airport, we are a busy hive of activity, and a lot of what we look for is how you would align with our AKL values All In | Tātou tatou – How we feel, Know How | Kōkiri Tahi – How we think and Let’s Go | Karawhiua – How we act.

To ensure your success in this role, you will need:

*   Bachelor's degree in Information Systems, Technology, Operations Management or Business, Aviation
*   Demonstrated knowledge of digital systems used in operational environments through formal training or equivalent experience.
*   Familiarity with IT governance or service management frameworks (e.g. ITIL Foundation or equivalent exposure).
*   Strong understanding of IT processes, infrastructure and network components.
*   Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to have a wide overarching view of multiple data flows, inputs, integrations and outputs.
*   Minimum 15 years’ experience in operational technology, systems management, product management and digital programme delivery in a complex, multi-stakeholder environment
*   Prior experience of operating within an airline and / or ground handler environment.
*   Proven ability to lead digital system lifecycle from roadmap development through to operational implementation and optimisation
*   Strong understanding of digital systems relevant to airport operations (e.g. check-in, baggage, security, mobility, biometrics)
*   Experience working across business and technology functions to co-develop solutions, resolve delivery issues, and ensure operational fit.
*   Strong vendor management experience including contract oversight, performance management, and service-level adherence.
*   Ability to manage competing priorities and deliver in high-pressure operational contexts.
*   Budgeting and planning skills.
*   Excellent relationship-building skills at all levels of the organisation.
