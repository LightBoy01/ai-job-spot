### Description\nWe are a B2B Social Media Agency dedicated to making people visible, giving experts a voice, and transforming complex business topics into understandable, honest, and authentic video content. We are a young team that works quickly and clearly, and we are eager to grow. That's precisely why we are looking for someone who is truly excited about taking on responsibility, leading clients, and personal development. With you, our clients are in the best hands. If you want responsibility, enjoy working with people, and prefer to lead rather than administer, then this role is for you. You will grow quickly, learn a lot, and have a real impact.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   **Strategic Consulting & Client Leadership**\n    *   Charmingly and confidently advise clients (digitally & on-site).\n    *   Clearly convey LinkedIn strategies.\n    *   Provide security, inject humor, build relationships.\n    *   Lead with clarity to avoid \"wish-list\" chaos.\n*   **Project Coordination & Quality Assurance**\n    *   Fast, reliable & professional client communication.\n    *   Manage approximately 30 parallel client accounts.\n    *   Clearly communicate results and feedback to all stakeholders.\n    *   Prepare and convincingly present reports.\n*   **Selling Results & Placing Upsells**\n    *   Emotionally charge successes and inspire clients for further development.\n    *   Identify upsell potentials (\"This would now be useful for you...\").\n    *   Charmingly and consultatively place additional offerings.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   **Strong Communication Skills:** You can engage people, understand them, and guide them through conversations.\n*   **Standing & Clarity in Consulting:** You can friendly, but firmly, state what makes sense – and what doesn't.\n*   **Professional Experience:** Ideally, you have completed a degree in Business Administration or a comparable field of study.\n*   **Structure & Reliability:** You organize approx. 30 clients calmly, neatly, and without chaos.\n*   **Business Understanding or Interest:** You can empathize with business models and understand client strategies.\n*   **Affinity for Digital Tools and AI:** CRM, LinkedIn, Sales Navigator, Outreach Automation, ChatGPT.\n*   **Willingness to Learn & Ambition:** You want to grow, take on responsibility, and improve.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role offers an exceptional opportunity for significant personal and professional growth. You will become exceptionally proficient in communication, gain in-depth knowledge of LinkedIn, learn to lead people effectively, and deeply understand B2B business models. Beyond the learning, the company provides flexible working hours, a stylish office in Mainz's Zollhafen or home office options, and a young, dynamic team of high-performers (aged 22-32) eager to support your ideas. With 1-on-1 mentoring, exciting \"Worcation\" opportunities, cool team events, and a top company culture, this is a place where you can truly thrive and make a real impact with a brilliant team.
