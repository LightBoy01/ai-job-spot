**About the Role**
------------------

As a _Content/Community Manager_, you’ll _manage the French social media accounts of a global leader in the tech industry, one of the largest companies in the world._ We are looking for someone who is passionate about seeking new opportunities to create impactful work.

### **Responsibilities:**

*   Lead the day-to-day exchanges and conduct weekly meetings with the client
*   Update the internal leadership team on a weekly basis
*   Orchestrate the social editorial calendar week-on-week
*   Participate in the strategic recommendations, be responsible for and present creative recommendations, and consult on monthly performance reports, providing qualitative analyses.
*   Handle community management for the social media accounts
*   Guide social apprentices/interns
*   Stay up to date with social media feature updates, best practices, trends, and insights
*   Brief and follow up artistic/motion designers

**About You**
-------------

### **The essentials:**

*   Native French speaker/writer, professional spoken/written proficiency in English
*   Experience across the following social media platforms: Facebook, X, YouTube (including Shorts & Community), Instagram, and TikTok
*   Understanding of social media KPIs
*   Creative and proactive
*   Strong organisational skills
*   Autonomous with team spirit
*   Follows internet/pop culture

### **Not a must, but a plus:**

*   Experience working with the Google Suite and Slack
*   Previous experience working on tech or AI clients and/or an interest in tech or AI
