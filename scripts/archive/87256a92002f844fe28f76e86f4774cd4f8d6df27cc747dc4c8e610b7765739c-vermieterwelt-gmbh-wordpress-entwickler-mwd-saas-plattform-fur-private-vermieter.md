### Description\nVermieterwelt is the leading digital marketplace and SaaS platform for private landlords in Germany, offering digital products, subscriptions, and partner integrations. Their core platform, a central cockpit, allows landlords to manage various services like property administration, rent control, and utility bill settlement.\n\nThis is a key role focused on expanding and defining the future of their WordPress-based SaaS platform, contributing to a product that digitally redefines the daily life of landlords across Germany.\n\n### Responsibilities\n**Platform & Product Development**\n*   Further develop our WordPress-based SaaS platform with a focus on WooCommerce.\n*   Develop and extend custom plugins and Gutenberg components.\n*   Implement stable purchase and subscription processes based on WooCommerce Subscriptions.\n*   Integrate external partner products and CRM systems (e.g., HubSpot) via REST/JSON APIs and webhooks.\n*   Build scalable structures for new digital products and services.\n\n**Technical Responsibility**\n*   Ensure smooth self-service functions in the cockpit (e.g., invoices, documents, subscription management, utility bill settlement, rent control).\n*   Maintain and optimize interfaces between frontend, backend, and databases.\n*   Optimize performance and security for a fast, reliable user experience.\n*   Co-design a consistent and seamless User Experience/Design in close collaboration with product management and marketing.\n\n**Our Tech Stack includes:**\n*   WordPress (PHP 8+), Custom Plugins, Gutenberg\n*   WooCommerce with Subscriptions & Payment\n*   WP REST API, JSON, Webhooks\n*   MySQL, Git, CI/CD\n*   Cockpit as the central user interface with features like property management, rent control, utility bill settlement.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Several years of experience in WordPress development (Custom Plugins, Hooks/Filters, Gutenberg).\n*   Profound knowledge of PHP 8+ and MySQL.\n*   Experience in building cockpits / customer portals (e.g., SaaS interfaces, customer accounts, admin areas).\n*   Practical experience with WooCommerce, especially WooCommerce Subscriptions and payment integrations (Stripe, PayPal, SEPA).\n*   Proficient in using REST-APIs, JSON, and Webhooks.\n*   Basic understanding of UX design and an eye for user-friendly interfaces.\n*   Affinity for AI-powered solutions and a willingness to adopt new technologies early (\"AI first\" mindset).\n*   **Nice-to-have:** HubSpot integrations, React/TypeScript (for interactive modules), Docker & CI/CD.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a pivotal role for a seasoned WordPress developer eager to make a significant impact on a rapidly growing SaaS platform. Vermieterwelt offers a dynamic, agile environment where innovation, particularly with an \"AI first\" mindset, is highly valued. The opportunity for direct collaboration with management and various departments, combined with flexible/hybrid work arrangements and ample room for personal ideas, makes this an attractive position for someone who wants to shape the future of digital property management in Germany. You'll be working on a platform that genuinely simplifies complex tasks for thousands of users.
