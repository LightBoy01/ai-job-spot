### Description

Casando, the online shop of Holz-Richter, is a renowned provider in the **Home & Garden** sector. Our products are far from ordinary – best described as **consultation-intensive, bulky, and durable**. From garage doors and parquet floors to regular doors and six-meter-long terrace planks, we distribute classic wood and building material trade products online.

To further expand our online success, we are immediately looking for an enthusiastic **Working Student in Performance Marketing / SEA**. You will actively shape our advertising presence, optimize campaigns, and eagerly explore new ideas.

### Responsibilities

*   Support the strategic and operational management of our Google Ads accounts (Search, Display, PMax).
*   Assist in the conception, implementation, and monitoring of ongoing SEA campaigns according to defined performance goals.
*   Take on reporting tasks and support the analysis of campaign results.
*   Work closely with the Online Marketing team and adjacent departments such as Data Analytics and Product Management.
*   Monitor current trends in online marketing and contribute your own ideas for new campaigns or test approaches.

### Qualifications

*   You are an enrolled student in Marketing, Business Administration, Communications, Media, or a comparable field of study.
*   Initial experience with Google Ads, Meta Ads, or other performance marketing channels is an advantage – if you haven't worked with them yet, we will teach you everything necessary.
*   You possess strong analytical skills and enjoy working with numbers and data.
*   You work in a structured, independent manner and bring a high degree of willingness to learn.
*   Very good German language skills, both written and spoken, complete your profile.

### Author's Take

This is an outstanding **entry-level opportunity** for a motivated student eager to delve into the practical world of performance marketing. Casando offers a unique challenge, dealing with **consultation-intensive and durable products** within the Home & Garden sector, which means your contributions will have a tangible impact on a distinct customer journey.

You'll gain hands-on experience with Google Ads, contribute to campaign strategy and analysis, and collaborate with diverse teams like Data Analytics and Product Management. The company explicitly states they will "teach you everything necessary" if you lack prior experience, highlighting a supportive environment focused on your growth.

Beyond the stimulating work, Casando offers **flexible working hours** to accommodate your studies, a **motivated team**, and the possibility of **home office after onboarding**. If you're analytical, structured, curious, and ready to learn and implement new ideas, this role provides a solid foundation for your career in online marketing.
