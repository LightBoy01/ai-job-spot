**Responsibilities**

*   Ensures all construction activities are completed prior to taking up the system for commissioning.
*   Prepares and plans the deployment of workers to fulfill the commissioning schedule.
*   Ensures the preparation of procedures and guidelines for systematic commissioning and start-up of equipment.
*   Initiate and manage the commissioning by Coordinating between the company and OEM.
*   Highlight operational issues with regards to machinery and equipment.
*   Ensure that OEM meets the Contractual agreement accordingly.
*   Audit work site regularly and ensure that proper housekeeping is adhered to.
*   Supervise onsite commissioning activities
*   Coordinate work with other Trades
*   Prepare and maintain commission records of site observations, testing processes and checklist.
*   Track and review progress through HAT meetings.
*   Attend progress meeting to keep up status and close issues with customers.
*   Ensures that all safety and precautionary requirements and procedures are followed during the commissioning process.
*   Compile and update commissioning report/ records.
*   Assist with Identification and set right commissioning and malfunction issues.
*   Access the requirements of special tools and equipment required for commissioning and initiate procurement if required.
*   Review the tools and equipment used for Safety and Efficiency.
*   Develop and update RA accordingly and enforce on-site and conducted weekly safety briefing with supervisors and workers

**Requirements**

*   Degree or Diploma in Naval Architecture/Structural/Marine/Electrical/Electronic Engineering or other electrical/offshore/electronic related studies.
*   Naval Institute Trained
*   Shipboard or Preferably 3 years or more of working experiences in similar capacity.
*   No experience can be considered for junior role.
*   Knowledge of Class requirements & Quality Standard requirements
*   Trade Dependent.
*   Must be able to understand Marine/Electrical & Automation Systems Equipment related schematic & arrangement drawings
*   Knowledge and skills in Marine/Electrical & Automation systems equipment's operational requirements.
*   Adequate Computing Knowledge as well as use of Microsoft Office and e-mail.
