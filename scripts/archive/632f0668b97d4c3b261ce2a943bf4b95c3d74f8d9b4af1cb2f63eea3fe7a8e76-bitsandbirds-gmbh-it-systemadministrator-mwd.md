### Description\nWe are looking for an experienced IT System Administrator (m/f/d) for a fast-growing software company in Hamburg.\n\nOur client is one of the market leaders for holistic enterprise communication solutions, experiencing strong growth – and with the company, the demands on internal IT are also growing. As new solutions emerge and a proprietary Public Cloud is on the horizon, one thing is paramount: an infrastructure that supports ongoing operations. Stable, scalable, professional.\n\nThis is where you come in: As an IT System Administrator (m/f/d), you ensure availability, security, and performance – within the existing setup, with a focus on Windows environments and hybrid infrastructure operations. You will be part of an experienced, helpful team that takes collective responsibility – without hierarchy, but with a lot of expertise.\n\nIf your strengths lie in daily operational IT, but you are open to further development – e.g., towards Cloud or Linux – you will find the perfect environment here.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Operate and maintain the existing hybrid infrastructure – from classic Windows servers to the Azure Cloud.\n- Ensure platforms, storage, and backup systems run stably by monitoring, maintaining, and optimizing them.\n- Monitor systems with modern monitoring solutions, analyze sources of error, and efficiently resolve technical disruptions.\n- Handle topics such as firewalling, user & license management, and virtualization (e.g., with Hyper-V or Proxmox) as part of your daily business.\n- Work closely with the team, IT management, and neighboring departments – in a structured yet agile environment.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Solid experience in Windows system administration – Microsoft 365, Exchange, and Intune are your daily tools.\n- Routine management of networks, firewalls, and monitoring solutions – always keeping an eye on the essentials.\n- Ability to work independently, structured, and reliably – even when many topics are running simultaneously.\n- Initial experience with Azure or Linux (e.g., SUSE Rancher) is a plus – but not a must.\n- Virtualization (e.g., with Proxmox or Hyper-V) and a basic understanding of hybrid IT structures complete your profile.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role offers a fantastic opportunity for an experienced IT System Administrator to join a market-leading, rapidly growing software company. You'll play a crucial role in maintaining and evolving a robust hybrid infrastructure while contributing to the exciting development of new solutions and a public cloud. The company fosters a highly collaborative environment with an experienced team, prioritizing professional development through training and certifications. Beyond a stable and impactful position, you'll benefit from an attractive overall package, including comprehensive benefits for health, well-being, and work-life balance, all within a supportive culture that values team spirit. This is an ideal fit if you thrive in operational IT, seek continuous growth, and want to contribute significantly to a company's success.
