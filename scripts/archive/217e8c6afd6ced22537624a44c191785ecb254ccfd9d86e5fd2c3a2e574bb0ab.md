**Trades Assistant**

**Location:** Port Kembla

**Job Details:** Onsite – Permanent, Full-Time

**Working Hours:** Monday to Friday between 6am and 3pm

Do you want to develop hands-on skills, grow your career, and thrive in a supportive, workshop-based team? Join **AutoNexus Port Kembla**, part of Inchcape’s global network.

You’ll play a key role in preparing vehicles for delivery, working with a diverse team across workshop, yard, and wharf operations. This is a great opportunity to build your automotive experience while contributing to a business that’s transforming the industry and delivering great customer outcomes.

**What You’ll Do**

*   _Install accessories to various OEM vehicles (e.g. AV kits, reverse cameras, and other accessories)_
*   Carry out pre-delivery preparation including washing, fuelling, and tagging
*   Move and process vehicles before dealership handover
*   Fit FOTON trucks and apply Data Dots (Subaru)
*   Work across workshop, yard, and wharf environments

**About You**

To be successful in this role, you’ll be:

*   Mechanically minded with basic car knowledge
*   Detail-oriented and able to follow processes precisely
*   A strong communicator who works well in a team
*   Able to drive manual vehicles (Full Australian Driver’s Licence required)
*   Comfortable working in a fast-paced workshop environment

**We Offer You**

*   A safe, well-equipped workshop and great working conditions
*   Opportunities to learn new skills and grow into leadership roles
*   Access to **Inchcape employee benefits**, including wellness initiatives and staff events
*   A diverse and inclusive team culture that values collaboration and respect
*   Career pathways within Inchcape’s global network
