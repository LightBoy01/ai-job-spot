### Description
Our partner company is actively seeking a dedicated and experienced Tax Manager for Reporting to join their team as soon as possible. In this pivotal role, you will be instrumental in coordinating the group-wide tax reporting processes, ensuring compliance with IFRS and HGB, and identifying crucial optimization opportunities. You will serve as a central figure, liaising with both domestic and international group entities and external auditors, making a significant impact on the company's financial integrity and strategic tax planning.

### Responsibilities
*   Coordinate group-wide tax reporting and conduct thorough reviews and audits.
*   Calculate group taxes under IFRS for quarterly, annual, and consolidated financial statements, including the preparation of required disclosures.
*   Engage in strategic tax planning, proactively identify optimization opportunities, and perform detailed variance analyses.
*   Address complex tax-related questions arising from company transactions and meticulously analyze their implications for group tax positions.
*   Serve as the central point of contact for all tax-related communication with domestic and international group companies, as well as external auditors.

### Qualifications
*   Completed university degree in Business Administration, Economics, or a related field, with a specialization in Auditing, Accounting, or Taxation.
*   Extensive professional experience in Tax Accounting & Reporting is highly desirable.
*   Profound knowledge of IFRS and HGB accounting principles, alongside a strong grasp of tax law.
*   Possess exceptional analytical skills and a highly structured, detail-oriented approach to work.
*   Fluent in both German and English, enabling effective communication across diverse stakeholders.
