### Description
On behalf of our partner company in Munich, we are looking for an **SAP Solution Manager (m/f/d)** to join their team as soon as possible. In this pivotal role, you will be responsible for advising, supporting, and further developing ALM systems, playing a key part in the strategic enhancement of their IT landscape.

### Responsibilities
*   Advise, support, and further develop ALM systems such as SAP Solution Manager 7.2, Focused Build, Cloud ALM, and JIRA.
*   Conduct system and process analyses, and take ownership of customizing activities.
*   Actively support Demand, Change, Deploy, and Release Management processes.
*   Contribute to projects in the areas of IT Service Management and Document Management Systems (e.g., OpenText).
*   Create concepts and provide project management support for successful implementation.

### Qualifications
*   Completed degree in (Business) Informatics or a comparable qualification, e.g., as an IT Consultant or SAP Consultant (m/f/d).
*   Several years of professional experience in the relevant field.
*   Excellent knowledge of SAP Solution Manager, ALM, as well as Change and Test Management.
*   Experience with IT infrastructures, Cloud products, and IT Service Management tools rounds off your profile.
*   Very good German and English language skills.
