### Description\ncasando, the online shop of Holz-Richter, is a renowned provider in the \"Home & Garden\" sector. We specialize in selling high-value, consultation-intensive, bulky, and durable products online, ranging from garage doors and parquet flooring to six-meter long terrace planks. We are looking for a Digital Analyst (m/f/d) to strengthen our online shop and marketing activities. This role is crucial for transforming data into actionable insights, further developing our tracking and reporting, identifying trends, and ensuring measurable e-commerce performance.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Be responsible for the analysis, evaluation, and interpretation of our casando online shop's performance across all digital channels.\n- Develop dashboards and reports to measure and optimize traffic, conversion rates, customer journeys, and campaign performance.\n- Collaborate closely with Online Marketing, UX, IT, and Product Management teams to derive data-based recommendations.\n- Ensure quality assurance and further development of our tracking setup (e.g., Google Tag Manager, GA4, Consent Management).\n- Identify trends, potentials, and weaknesses, and derive concrete optimization measures for the shop, campaigns, and customer experience.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Professional experience in Digital Analytics, Web Analysis, or Performance Marketing.\n- Profound knowledge of Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, and ideally Looker Studio.\n- Strong analytical understanding and the ability to present complex data clearly and understandably.\n- Experience with conversion tracking or attribution models is a plus.\n- Structured, independent work style and enjoyment in driving data-driven decisions.\n- Very good German language skills (written and spoken) and team-player attitude.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a Digital Analyst looking to make a significant impact within a growing e-commerce company specializing in unique, \"heavyweight\" products. casando offers a dynamic, family-like work environment with flat hierarchies, where your ideas are truly valued.
