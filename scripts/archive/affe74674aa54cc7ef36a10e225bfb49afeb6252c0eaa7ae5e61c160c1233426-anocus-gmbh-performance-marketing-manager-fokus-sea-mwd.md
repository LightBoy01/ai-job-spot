### Description
We aspire to be the go-to partner for Search Engine Advertising (SEA) for e-commerce companies with extensive product ranges in the DACH region. We firmly believe that many online shops haven't yet unlocked their full potential due to suboptimal support, and we're here to change that. Our core values emphasize personal development, operational and strategic excellence, and transparency. We are committed to fostering both the professional and personal growth of our team members, ensuring exceptional results for our clients.

If you're passionate about enabling e-commerce businesses to achieve profitable growth and want to make a direct impact, then this is the place for you!

Headquartered in beautiful Hamburg, our fully remote team of 6 individuals is spread across Germany, with the option to work from our Hamburg office. We hold regular multi-day in-person meetings for collaborative work, workshops, and teambuilding activities. We prioritize strategic consultation, which is why each Account Manager oversees a maximum of 5 clients. This approach ensures you can dedicate quality time to maximize client success, rather than simply processing large volumes.

As a Performance Marketing Manager, you will be instrumental in delivering outstanding results for your clients, working independently and collaboratively within a two-person project team.

### Responsibilities
*   Manage a portfolio of existing e-commerce clients, providing both operational and strategic guidance for their development. This includes setting up, managing, measuring, and optimizing Google Ads (and Microsoft Ads) campaigns, and ideally, Web Analytics measures, thereby significantly contributing to client success.
*   Independently conceive and plan highly profitable performance marketing strategies. Leverage your expertise in Google Search, Shopping, PMAX, Demand Gen, YouTube, and Display to efficiently boost your clients' revenues.
*   Conduct regular client meetings independently, or collaboratively with our CEO (Sergej) or SEA Team Lead (Tolga) for key accounts, to align on strategy and identify new growth opportunities.
*   Analyze and evaluate campaign performance, deriving actionable recommendations from comprehensive reports.
*   Monitor market developments and identify new opportunities to further expand performance marketing activities.
*   Analyze your clients' business structures, understand key economic performance indicators, and develop performance-oriented solutions based on this understanding.
*   Demonstrate strong independence, enjoy collaborating with various clients, and possess a keen eye for detail.
*   Commit to continuous learning, acquiring the latest industry knowledge, and applying it effectively to your client projects.

### Qualifications
*   A minimum of 2 years of practical experience, ideally within an agency setting, in performance marketing with a focus on SEA and Google Ads (Search, Shopping, PMAX campaigns, etc.).
*   Proven practical experience in client communication from previous roles.
*   Proficient in Google Ads, Google Merchant Center, and Google Analytics. Experience with Looker Studio and Google Tag Manager is a bonus.
*   Possess a customer-oriented, independent, structured, and goal-driven work ethic with a strong service mentality.
*   Excellent analytical and evaluation skills are paramount; you will analyze campaigns and make data-driven decisions.
*   Your confident demeanor, poised approach, and strong communication skills define you, making you exceptionally successful in client contact and management.
*   Nice to have: A degree in economics or a comparable field/training.
*   Fluent in both spoken and written German and English (German is mandatory due to client interaction being in German).

### Author's Take
This is an exciting opportunity to join a growing agency where you can actively shape processes and contribute directly to decision-making, escaping rigid, outdated structures. You'll experience a steep learning curve, benefiting from extensive SEA and Web Analytics expertise, ongoing professional development, and annual attendance at industry fairs or training sessions. The commitment to a maximum of 5 clients per manager allows for deep client immersion and long-term strategic partnerships, fostering significant growth for both clients and the agency.

You'll thrive in an environment with flat hierarchies, direct collaboration with the CEO, and clear career advancement opportunities. The company supports a flexible, remote-first work model (within Germany, with EU travel options) complimented by a well-equipped home office setup including Apple MacBook and high-quality peripherals. Enjoy flexible working hours (39-hour week with core hours 10:00-15:00, Mon-Fri) and generous holiday allowance (30 days + birthday off + half days on Dec 24/31, totaling up to 32 days). Benefits also include access to a comprehensive internal knowledge Wiki and an Urban Sports Club membership.

We are looking for someone eager to work in a dynamic environment, contribute their expertise, and help shape the agency's future. Internal exchange and continuous learning are key to our collective success. If you're ready to make a tangible impact and grow professionally and personally, we encourage you to apply!
