### Description
If you have a knack for numbers, are passionate about online marketing, and enjoy working in a young, flexible team, we are looking for exactly you to join us immediately. This role is central to our e-commerce client success, focusing on strategic online marketing across various platforms.

### Responsibilities
*   Managing Amazon accounts for our production and e-commerce clients.
*   Building and optimizing advertising campaigns across Amazon, Google, Bing, and YouTube.
*   Conducting keyword research and market analysis for new campaign preparation.
*   Optimizing conversion-focused landing pages and online shops.
*   Planning and executing promotions and sales campaigns.
*   Onboarding new marketing channels, including tracking implementation.
*   Performing website and product analysis using Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, providing actionable recommendations.
*   Creating briefings for other stakeholders (IT, graphics, copy) and reviewing results.
*   Analyzing and optimizing the overall portfolio to increase ROAS (Return on Ad Spend).
*   Engaging in other tasks strongly related to e-commerce marketing.

### Qualifications
*   Advanced knowledge of Microsoft Excel and other Office applications.
*   Ideally, experience in managing Amazon accounts, Google Ads, website creation, or social media presence.
*   A passion for challenges and the ambition to grow daily, bringing this enthusiasm to your work.
*   A high degree of independence and self-responsibility.
*   Solution-oriented approach and the ability to master tricky situations confidently.
*   The ability to independently acquire knowledge and share it with others.
*   Friendliness and self-confidence in dealing with clients and business partners.
