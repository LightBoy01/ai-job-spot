**Essential Duties and Responsibilities**

_Customer Engagement & Strategy_

*   Serves as a strategic advisor to enterprise customers and partners on technology strategy, architecture, and innovation.
*   Leads executive-level discussions on emerging technologies, industry trends, and digital transformation strategies.
*   Translates customer business goals into technology solutions that leverage the company's products and services.

_Thought Leadership_

*   Represents the company at industry events, conferences, webinars, and in customer briefings.
*   Collaborates with Marketing and Product teams to create white papers, blogs, and other thought leadership content.
*   Maintains a strong presence in the tech community and build trusted relationships with key industry influencers.

_Product and Engineering Collaboration_

*   Provides customer feedback to Product and Engineering teams to influence roadmap priorities and product enhancements.
*   Partners with internal R&D teams to validate new technologies and guide solution development.

_Sales Enablement & Support_

*   Supports the sales process by participating in customer meetings, solution architecture, and proposal development.
*   Assists with complex solution design and proof-of-concept development.
*   Helps qualify and shape strategic deals by aligning technology capabilities with business needs.

_Technology Vision_

*   Monitors trends in the technology landscape and recommend how they can be applied within the company’s strategy.
*   Drives innovation through strategic partnerships and experimental projects with key customers.

_General_

*   Demonstrates and actively promotes an understanding and commitment to the mission of Logicalis through performing behaviors consistent with the organization's values.

*   Maintains a working knowledge of applicable Federal, State, and Local laws and regulations as well as policies and procedures of Logicalis in order to ensure adherence in a manner that reflects honest, ethical and professional behaviors.

*   Supports and conducts self in a manner consistent with customer service expectations.

**Supervisory Responsibilities**     

This job has no supervisory responsibilities.
