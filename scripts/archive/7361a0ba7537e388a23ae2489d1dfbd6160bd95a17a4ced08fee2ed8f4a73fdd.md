**Brief introduction - Role Summary/Purpose:**

The Moulding and Moulding Automation Team Leader is a hands-on, working leader responsible for overseeing the safety, quality, and efficiency of a production shift within the moulding department. This role has a particular focus on managing and troubleshooting automated systems, including robotic cells, vision systems, and other ancillary equipment. The Team Leader is a key driver of continuous improvement, working to optimize both manual and automated processes to meet and exceed production targets.  
  

**Responsibilities:**

*   **Team Leadership and Management:**
    

*   Lead, mentor, and train a team of moulding operators and automation technicians on shift.
    
*   Assign daily tasks and manage workflow to ensure production schedules are met.
    
*   Conduct shift handovers, providing clear communication on production status, issues, and upcoming requirements.
    
*   Provide feedback, conduct performance evaluations, and address disciplinary issues in accordance with company policy.  
      
    

*   **Production and Quality Control:**
    

*   Ensure the production of high-quality products on time and in full, adhering to all quality standards.
    
*   Monitor production metrics, including output, cycle times, and scrap rates, and take immediate action to address any deviations.
    
*   Perform regular quality checks on products and work with the Quality Assurance team to resolve defects and conduct root cause analysis.
    
*   Ensure all production documentation and reports are accurately completed.  
      
    

*   **Automation and Technical Oversight:**
    

*   Act as the first point of contact for troubleshooting and resolving technical issues with moulding machines and, most importantly, the integrated automation cells.
    
*   Perform basic programming and adjustments on robots (e.g., sprue pickers, part handling robots) and other automation equipment.
    
*   Collaborate with the Maintenance and Engineering teams to diagnose complex equipment failures and implement long-term solutions.
    
*   Assist in the setup, testing, and validation of new moulds and automated equipment for new program launches.  
      
    

*   **Process Improvement:**
    

*   Drive continuous improvement projects on the shop floor, focusing on enhancing safety, reducing waste, and improving efficiency (e.g., Lean Manufacturing, Six Sigma, 5S).
    
*   Identify and propose improvements to automation processes and equipment to increase productivity and reduce manual labor.
    
*   Analyze production data to identify trends and opportunities for optimization.  
      
    

*   **Safety and Compliance:**
    

*   Enforce all safety protocols, procedures, and regulations 
    
*   Lead safety initiatives, conduct risk assessments, and ensure a clean, organized, and safe working environment.
    
*   Report and investigate all safety incidents and near misses, driving corrective and preventive actions.
    

**Required  Qualifications:**

*   **Experience:**
    

*   Proven experience in a manufacturing environment, preferably with plastic or rubber injection molding.
    
*   Minimum of 3 to 5 years of experience in a supervisory role.
    
*   Demonstrated experience with industrial automation, including robotics, PLCs, and vision systems.  
      
    

*   **Technical Knowledge:**
    

*   Strong understanding of moulding processes, machine operation, and troubleshooting.
    
*   Knowledge of various plastic resins and their properties.
    
*   Proficiency in setting up and adjusting moulding machines and ancillary equipment.
    
*   Hands-on experience with automated systems, including basic programming and troubleshooting.  
      
    

*   **Leadership and Communication:**
    

*   Excellent interpersonal and communication skills to effectively lead a diverse team and interact with cross-functional departments (Maintenance, Quality, Engineering).
    
*   Strong problem-solving and analytical abilities to address complex technical and operational challenges.
    
*   Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and make quick, data-driven decisions.  
      
    

*   **Physical Demands:**
    

*   Ability to stand and walk for long periods.
    
*   Ability to work in a manufacturing environment that may be loud and have varying temperatures.  
      
    

*   **Education:**
    

*   B.E (Mechanical/Electrical and Electronics/Polymer) with minimum 3 yrs. of experience Or D.M.E/D.E.E.E/CIPET with minimum 6 Years in Operation/ Production Department
