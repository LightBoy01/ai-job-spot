The TIENS Group Co., Ltd. is a multinational conglomerate, integrating industrial capital, business capital and financial capital with its scope of business covering biotechnology, health management, hotel and tourism industry, education & training, e-commerce and finance investment, etc. With its business reaching over 224 countries, TIENS Group has set up branches in 117 countries & regions and has established strategic alliances with top ranked enterprises from many countries. TIENS Europe Region is currently active on more than 20 markets.

Tasks
-----

*   Verify and record daily sales, collections, and accounting transactions.
*   Maintain accurate digital and physical accounting books and documentation.
*   Prepare and submit internal financial reports to the Head Office.
*   Manage tax declarations, payments, and ensure compliance with local fiscal regulations.
*   Process supplier invoices, branch expenses, and commission payments.
*   Conduct monthly physical inventory counts and reconcile results.
*   Maintain and update inventory and fixed asset registers.
*   Support fiscal, corporate, and audit filings as required.
*   Coordinate with the Head Office Finance team to ensure policy alignment and reporting consistency.

Requirements
------------

*   Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
*   Minimum 3–5 years of experience in accounting or finance (preferably in branch or regional coordination roles).
*   Solid understanding of accounting principles, tax regulations, and financial reporting.
*   Proficiency in accounting software (e.g., SAP, QuickBooks, or equivalent) and MS Excel.
*   Strong attention to detail, analytical thinking, and organizational skills.
*   Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities under tight deadlines.
*   Excellent communication and coordination skills for cross-functional and regional collaboration.​
