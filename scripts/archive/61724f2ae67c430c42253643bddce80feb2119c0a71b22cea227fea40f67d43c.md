**About Team & About Role:**

Rubrik is revolutionizing data security with Zero Trust Data Protection™, enabling organizations to safeguard their most critical assets from cyber threats, operational disruptions, and data loss. As the leader in data security and cloud management, Rubrik empowers enterprises to simplify and secure their digital infrastructure.

The **Senior Director of People Operations** is a pivotal executive leadership position, responsible for shaping the strategic direction and operational excellence of Rubrik’s global HR infrastructure. This role is a key member of the People Leadership team and reports to the VP & Head of HR. The ideal candidate will be a visionary leader willing to be very hands-on to drive the transformation of our operational functions into a world-class strategic asset that supports and enables our ambitious growth objectives.

**What You’ll Do:** 

*   **Strategic Leadership and Organizational Development:** Direct and inspire a global team to execute on a strategic vision for People Operations. This includes fostering a high-performance culture, mentoring managers across different regions, and ensuring operational alignment with the company’s strategic goals and core values.
*   **Global Program Management and Transformation:** Provide executive oversight and strategic insight for critical global programs, including our contingent workforce, immigration, and M&A integration initiatives. Lead the charge on centralizing and standardizing these functions to mitigate risk and improve visibility.
*   **Technology and Process Innovation:** Pioneer the modernization of our HR service delivery model. This will involve moving from reactive, email-based support to a proactive, scalable, and technology-driven platform utilizing advanced tools, including AI, to enhance the employee experience.
*   **Data Integrity and System Optimization:** Act as the steward of our people data, championing a culture of accuracy and leveraging our core HRIS (Workday) to drive efficiency and enhance employee self-service capabilities.

**Strategic & Operational Oversight:** 

*   **HR Helpdesk:** Drive the global strategy for employee support, focusing on scaling the HR Helpdesk through technology and process optimization. Transition from an email based ticket model to Jira + AI solutions and robust knowledge base.
*   **Onboarding, Offboarding & Alumni Relations:** Partner closely with the TA Operations team to ensure a smooth hand-off from offer/hiring to onboarding.  Take ownership of the entire employee lifecycle from onboarding to departure. Drive operational excellence in onboarding and offboarding.  
*   **Data Management:** Champion data integrity across all HR managed systems. Ensure processes are designed to capture accurate data and that the team maintains up-to-date process information for Workday transactions.  Drive Workday enhancements with the HCM team to reduce transaction time and move some transactions to self-service where appropriate. 
*   **Compliance & Policy:**  Partner closely with the Legal team to manage policy updates, and support key company compliance programs such as employee handbook acknowledgement, I-9 audits, etc.  Working to streamline delivery and developing creative solutions to drive 100% compliance.
*   **Contingent Workforce Management:** Jump in to support and provide strategic insights to an inflight project to fully centralize a global program for managing contingent workers. This includes strategy for engagement, onboarding/offboarding processes, risk mitigation, and system implementation to ensure visibility and consistency.
*   **Immigration:** Provide strategic oversight of the Global Immigration & Mobility program, partnering with the Immigration Manager to ensure processes are optimized to support future growth and enhance the employee experience.
*   **M&A:** Act as the primary People Operations lead for all M&A activities. Project manage all operational workstreams from due diligence through integration, ensuring a seamless and positive experience for acquired employees. Review and enhance M&A process documentation and educate stakeholders.  _Note: many of these responsibilities may be managed by a Senior Manager level direct report._

**Experience You’ll Need:** 

*   Bachelor's degree in a relevant field.
*   12-15+ years of progressive HR leadership experience, with extensive experience managing global or cross-functional teams.
*   Demonstrated expertise in large-scale project leadership, particularly in areas such as M&A due diligence, global contingent workforce programs, and immigration policy.
*   Proficiency with Workday is required, with a history of leveraging technology to drive operational efficiency and improve the employee experience.
*   Exceptional leadership, communication, and executive presence, with the ability to influence stakeholders at all levels of the organization.
*   The ability to work onsite in Palo Alto 3+ days per week and accommodate a flexible schedule for global team collaboration.
