****About the role****

*   We’re hiring a ****Strategy Manager**** to join our growing team and help shape the future of influencer marketing. You’ll bring cultural insight and creative strategy to life through storytelling, source high-quality creators who align with client goals, and support innovation across campaigns, client renewals, and agency marketing efforts.

****What you'll do****

  

🔍 ****Market Intelligence Support****

*   Research platforms, brands, and verticals
*   Analyze data pulled from social listening tools (e.g., Infegy, Brandwatch) to craft compelling, data-driven campaign narratives 
*   Identify algorithm-centric cultural trends shaping content and influencer strategy

📊 ****Strategy Support****

*   Build data-rich decks for pitches and campaigns
*   Translate data into compelling narratives
*   Source high-quality, relevant creators within the social landscape
*   Craft compelling campaign concepts that offer creative excellence + demonstrate innovative thinking
*   Align creator ecosystems and platform behaviors with campaign goals
*   Confidently present strategies to clients, articulating insights and recommendations in a clear, persuasive way
*   Act as a consultative partner to clients, brainstorming live and shaping strategic approaches in real time

🤖 ****AI & Content Intelligence****

*   Continuously explore and apply innovative AI-driven processes to elevate proposals and client deliverables
*   Support initiatives around trendspotting and creator discovery using AI

🤝 ****Cross-Functional Collaboration****

Work closely with multiple teams to deliver strategic impact across the business:

*   ****Creative:**** Support ongoing client renewals with trend and creator insight
*   ****New Business:**** Contribute to RFPs with standout strategy and creator POVs
*   ****Marketing:**** Provide strategic input and support for agency webinars and thought leadership content  
    ****Product:**** Partner with internal stakeholders on evolving agency tech and UI needs

****Agency-Wide****: Support case study upkeep to ensure best-in-class examples of our work are captured and published

  

****Qualifications****

✅ 6–8 years experience in strategy, insights, consumer research, influencer marketing, or similar  
✍️ A strong writer and storyteller who brings clarity to complexity  
🧠 Curious, proactive, and scrappy—you love turning noise into signal  
📈 Familiar with tools like Infegy, YouGov, SparkToro, TikTok Creative Center, Reddit, ChatGPT  
📊 Strong with Google Slides or PowerPoint  
🔮 Bonus: Trend forecasting, creator discovery, or early-stage AI experience  
🏢 Bonus: Experience at a startup, creative agency, or marketing-tech company

🎨 Major Bonus: Strong visual design skills
