### Description\nSIDES is a successful SaaS company with ambitious growth goals and a clear focus on digital innovations in the gastronomy and delivery industry. As a Founders Associate (m/w/d), you will work directly with our CEO and cross-functionally with our teams on new growth projects. You think strategically, develop ideas further, and implement them into initial prototypes.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- You work closely with the CEO and teams on the development and implementation of strategic growth initiatives.\n- You conceptualize new product ideas or MVPs and implement initial click-dummies or code prototypes (“Vibe Coding” – pragmatic, explorative, fast).\n- You conduct market and data analyses, evaluate opportunities, and develop hypotheses.\n- You coordinate cross-functional projects – from the idea phase through testing to the initial go-to-market.\n- You contribute your perspective to decision-making processes, becoming a sparring partner for the founder team.\n- You will prospectively develop into a (Product) Leader who independently drives projects.\n\n### Qualifications\n- You have a strong interest in digital business models, tech products, and data-driven growth.\n- You bring initial experience from a startup, tech, or consulting environment, or have already worked on your own projects.\n- You have a “hands-on” mentality, think agile, and can flexibly adapt to new topics.\n- You are able to sketch ideas, develop MVPs, and build initial prototypes with no-code or low-code tools or simple code (e.g., HTML/CSS/JS, Python, or Flutter).\n- You are a strong communicator, eager to learn, and enjoy working independently.\n- Fluent proficiency in spoken and written German and English.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis Founders Associate role at SIDES offers an exceptional opportunity for an ambitious individual to work directly with the CEO on strategic growth initiatives within the dynamic gastronomy and delivery SaaS sector. It emphasizes rapid prototyping, data-driven decision-making, and significant individual ownership. The company deeply values personal growth and continuous learning, providing a clear development path towards a leadership role, supported by regular feedback, training opportunities, and a collaborative environment that even prioritizes well-being with a mental health app. This is ideal for someone eager to make a tangible impact and grow rapidly in a high-potential startup.
