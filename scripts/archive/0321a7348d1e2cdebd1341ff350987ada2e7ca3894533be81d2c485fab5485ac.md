**Key Responsibilities**

*   *   Perform daily warehouse duties, inclusive but not limited to organising inventory records, monitoring inventory and analysing utilisation, etc.
*   Liaise with relevant stakeholders in the supply chain to ensure the right goods are delivered efficiently
*   Maintain inventory records on information system 
*   Gather feedback from users and assist Senior Store Officer in developing Standard Operating Procedures and protocols
*   Shift duties required
*   Perform any other duties assigned by supervisor(s)

  

**Job Requirements**

*   Form 5 or above
*   Knowledge of Business / Purchasing / Supply Chain Management is an advantage
*   At least 1 year' relevant work experience, preferably in hospital settings, applicant with less experience will be considered as Store Assistant
*   Good command of written and spoken English and Chinese
*   Good communication and customer service skills
*   Computer literate

**Compensation and Employment Terms**  
 

We provide a unique employment experience for people seeking careers in hospital and healthcare industry; and offer competitive remuneration packages to the successful candidates. Appointment will be made on a 3-year contract with contract-end gratuity, renewable subject to good performance and mutual agreement.
