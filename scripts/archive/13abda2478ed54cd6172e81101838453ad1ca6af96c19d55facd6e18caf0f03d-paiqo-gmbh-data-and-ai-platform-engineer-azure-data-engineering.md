### Description\nAs a **Data & AI Platform Engineer – Azure Data Engineering**, you will independently design and implement scalable data pipelines on Azure. This role focuses on utilizing cutting-edge Microsoft Fabric/OneLake, Azure Databricks, and Delta Lake technologies to deliver reliable data foundations for advanced analytics and AI initiatives. This position offers a hybrid/remote setup, with the requirement of having your primary residence in Germany.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Design, develop, and maintain complex data pipelines (batch & streaming) using Azure Data Factory, Azure Databricks, and Delta Lake.\n*   Integrate innovative Azure features, such as mirroring (zero-ETL replication), to ensure continuous synchronization of operational databases with OneLake and Microsoft Fabric, establishing a unified lakehouse architecture.\n*   Implement robust DataOps practices, including CI/CD pipelines with Azure DevOps/GitHub, comprehensive monitoring via Azure Monitor, and automation leveraging Delta Live Tables or Lakeflow.\n*   Guarantee data quality, enforce strong data governance, and manage access control using tools like Azure Purview and Unity Catalog.\n*   Collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams to continuously optimize the data platform for performance and cost-efficiency.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   Possess several years of demonstrable experience in developing sophisticated data platforms on Azure, specifically with Azure Data Factory, Azure Databricks, and Microsoft Fabric.\n*   Demonstrate in-depth knowledge of SQL, Python/Spark, and Delta Lake technologies.\n*   Be familiar with Azure Monitoring, data catalog solutions (e.g., Azure Purview), and CI/CD principles within a DevOps environment.\n*   Exhibit strong problem-solving capabilities and excellent communication skills, coupled with the ability to work independently and drive projects.\n*   Command very good proficiency in both German and English languages.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exciting opportunity for a seasoned Azure Data Engineer who thrives on autonomy and innovation. You'll be instrumental in shaping a cutting-edge data platform, leveraging the very latest in Microsoft Fabric and OneLake, to power critical analytics and AI solutions. The emphasis on DataOps, CI/CD, and robust data governance means you'll be building not just pipelines, but a mature, scalable, and reliable data ecosystem. The role offers the flexibility of hybrid/remote work within Germany and promises a challenging environment where your expertise in Azure data technologies and passion for independent problem-solving will be highly valued.
