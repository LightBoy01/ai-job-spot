**What you will do:**  
*   Design and implement AI/ML solutions that enhance our logistics platform, focusing on scalable, production-ready systems that deliver immediate value
*   Lead the development of multi-agent AI systems that improve delivery optimization and customer experience
*   Build and optimize data pipelines that power our AI systems, ensuring efficient token usage and system performance
*   Rapidly prototype and iterate on AI-driven features, maintaining a balance between innovation and practical implementation
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify opportunities for AI implementation and drive product improvements
*   Take ownership of AI initiatives from conception to production, including monitoring, optimization, and iteration
*   Drive technical decisions around AI architecture, tooling, and best practices

  

  
  
**What you need to have:**  
*   4+ years of software engineering experience, with demonstrated expertise in AI/ML implementations
*   Strong foundation in TypeScript and [Node.js](http://Node.js), with experience or willingness to work with AI frameworks in the TypeScript ecosystem (e.g., LangChain, LangGraph)
*   Practical experience with multi-agent AI systems and agentic AI frameworks
*   Proven track record of deploying production-ready LLM code and optimizing token usage at scale
*   Experience with AI/ML visibility tooling and monitoring systems
*   Strong data analysis skills and experience working with large-scale datasets
*   Expertise in prompt engineering, RAG implementations, and LLM chain design
*   Ability to write scalable, yet scrappy code that balances innovation with practical business needs
*   Demonstrated bias towards action and rapid iteration
*   Low-ego, product-centric mindset with a focus on delivering customer value
*   Experience working in fast-paced startup environments
*   Proficiency with our core tech stack (React, React Native, Postgres, Redis)

  

  
  
**Bonus points for:**  
*   Experience building and maintaining AI agent orchestration systems
*   Track record of implementing AI features that directly improved business metrics
*   Experience with advanced LLM optimization techniques and custom model fine-tuning
*   Experience integrating multiple AI providers and managing fallbacks
*   Familiarity with AI testing and evaluation frameworks

**Who are we?**

  

We are Curri and our mission is to be the way the world delivers construction and industrial supplies. Curri provides on-demand, last-mile logistics for construction and adjacent industries with our nationwide fleet of cars, trucks, and flatbeds. Curri was founded in 2018 and was a part of the YC S19 Batch. Employees work out of our office in Ventura, CA, but the majority of our fast-growing start-up operates remotely, with over 100 employees spread across the United States. We're solving a massive, global problem of inefficiency in the construction industry through innovative technology and AI-driven solutions. We imagine a world of efficient construction sites resulting in a net win for the entire world. Find out more at [curri.com](http://curri.com).
