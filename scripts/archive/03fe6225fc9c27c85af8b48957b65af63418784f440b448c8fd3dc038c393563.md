**Company Description:**

Daxko powers wellness to improve lives. Every day our team members focus their passion and expertise in helping health & wellness facilities operate efficiently and engage their members.

Whether a neighborhood yoga studio, a national franchise with locations in every city, a YMCA or JCC--and every type of organization in between--we build solutions that make every aspect of running and being a member of a health and wellness organization easier and delightful. 

**Job Description:**

At Daxko, we’re on a mission to power health, fitness, and wellness communities worldwide. As our first dedicated Senior Product Manager for AI, you’ll lead the charge in shaping how artificial intelligence transforms the way YMCAs, JCCs, Boys & Girls Clubs, fitness centers, and boutique studios serve their members.

This is a rare opportunity to define the vision, strategy, and execution of AI capabilities that will impact millions of people. You’ll work side by side with engineering and product leaders across our portfolio, building scalable AI features that drive smarter operations, better member experiences, and stronger business outcomes.

**What You’ll Do:**

*   Lead the end-to-end vision, strategy, and roadmap for Daxko’s AI platform.
*   Partner with engineering teams to design and deliver scalable AI capabilities that work across multiple products.
*   Identify high-value AI use cases with product managers and turn them into customer-focused solutions.
*   Define architecture principles and standards to ensure our AI solutions are reliable and future-proof.
*   Measure adoption, performance, and business outcomes to maximize the impact of AI investments.
*   Stay ahead of AI and ML industry trends, bringing fresh insights back to the organization.
*   Champion AI across the company — from launch planning to internal evangelism.

**Qualifications:**

**What Makes You a Great Fit:**

*   Proven AI/ML product management experience with a track record of delivering real business impact.
*   Strong technical acumen — comfortable working directly with engineers on complex AI/ML challenges.
*   Strategic yet hands-on, able to balance big-picture vision with day-to-day execution.
*   Skilled at cross-functional leadership and bringing diverse teams together.
*   Passion for translating AI capabilities into customer value and measurable outcomes.

**Minimum Qualifications:** 

*   Bachelor’s in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, or related field; or equivalent experience
*   6+ years in B2B Product Management (2+ years with AI/ML, platforms, or technical products).
*   Experience delivering AI/ML technologies, APIs, or cross-product platforms.
*   Excellent communication and presentation skills.
*   Willingness to travel up to 25%.

**Preferred Qualifications:** 

*   SaaS or multi-product portfolio experience.
*   Industry experience in health, fitness, or wellness.
*   Certifications in AI/ML, data science, or related fields.
*   Hands-on experience with AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud AI services.
*   Success scaling AI/ML solutions in high-growth environments.

**Additional Information:**
