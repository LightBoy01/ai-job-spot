### Description\nJusthome is radically rethinking mortgage financing in the age of AI. The process of buying a home in Germany is often overly complicated due to a lack of transparency, rising interest rates, and fragmented communication between buyers, brokers, financial advisors, and banks.\n\nJusthome is developing an intelligent platform to address these issues directly. We are redesigning the interface between all participants in mortgage financing with data-driven decision support, intelligent automation, and a digital user experience that sets new standards. Our solution is already live, with over 150 broker companies and thousands of prospective buyers actively using our platform to find the best financing options, accelerate processes, and simplify complex decisions.\n\nTo further expand our AI initiatives and scale our platform, we are looking for an experienced Full-Stack Developer to strengthen our existing engineering team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Develop our platform across the entire stack – from our customer-facing frontend to our ML-supported scoring and AI-powered document management.\n*   Work closely with Product, Design, and Sales to improve the experience for brokers, customers, and financial advisors, actively contributing your own ideas.\n*   Co-design our technical architecture to be scalable, robust, and future-proof.\n\n### Qualifications\n*   3-5 years of experience in professional software development (ideally already Full-Stack).\n*   Solid knowledge of React (frontend), Go (backend), and MongoDB (database).\n*   Experience with microservice architectures and modern cloud environments (AWS, Azure, or GCP).\n*   Strong interest – or initial practical experience – in AI tools, LLMs, or Machine Learning APIs.\n*   You enjoy developing scalable solutions in depth but are also enthusiastic about rapid iterations and MVPs using low-code tools.\n*   Fluent German language skills, very good English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a Mid-Senior Full-Stack Developer to make a tangible impact from day one.
