**Position Overview:**

We are seeking a **Senior Consultant – AI, Data, and Emerging Technologies** to join the Parsons X team. This consultative role bridges **technology strategy and solution delivery**, helping clients define and implement data-centric, AI-enabled approaches to complex infrastructure challenges.

The ideal candidate brings a blend of **technical depth, strategic insight, and business acumen**—comfortable leading client discussions on AI and data strategy, designing architecture roadmaps, and driving innovation initiatives across domains such as cognitive cities, smart mobility, and critical infrastructure resilience.

**Key Responsibilities:**

**Strategic Advisory & Client Engagement**

*   Serve as a trusted advisor to clients on AI, data strategy, and emerging-technology adoption.
*   Facilitate workshops, discovery sessions, and roadmap development aligned with client business and operational goals.
*   Translate complex technical concepts into actionable strategies and communicate insights clearly to executive and non-technical stakeholders.

**Solution Architecture & Integration**

*   Lead design of integrated AI, data, and analytics architectures leveraging cloud, IoT, and cybersecurity principles.
*   Develop scalable frameworks for data management, governance, and interoperability across platforms and partners.
*   Support solution prototyping and pilot deployments that validate business value through Parsons’ **thin-slice innovation** approach.

**Technical Leadership**

*   Provide thought leadership across AI/ML, data analytics, automation, and digital twin technologies.
*   Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams—engineers, software developers, data scientists, and domain SMEs—to deliver end-to-end solutions.
*   Contribute to Parsons’ internal innovation programs, technical communities, and proposal development efforts.

**Business Growth & Industry Leadership**

*   Partner with Parsons X leadership to identify new business opportunities, cultivate client relationships, and support go-to-market strategies.
*   Develop thought-leadership content, white papers, and presentations to strengthen Parsons’ position in the AI, data, and digital-infrastructure space.
*   Represent Parsons X at conferences, industry events, and client forums to showcase capabilities and drive market visibility.
*   Actively contribute to pipeline development through networking, client outreach, and innovation pursuits.

**Continuous Innovation & Learning**

*   Stay abreast of emerging trends in AI, edge computing, cybersecurity, and connected infrastructure.
*   Share insights across the organization to foster a culture of continuous learning and innovation.

**Qualifications:**

**Education & Experience**

*   Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, or related field **preferred**; equivalent professional experience accepted in lieu of formal education.
*   8+ years of progressive experience in AI, data analytics, or digital transformation, with at least 3 years in a client-facing or consultative capacity.
*   Demonstrated success designing or implementing data-driven solutions in transportation, mobility, or critical-infrastructure sectors.
*   Experience integrating across disciplines—data, AI/ML, systems architecture, cybersecurity, and cloud technologies.

**Technical Expertise**

*   Proficiency with AI/ML frameworks (TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn) and modern data architectures.
*   Strong knowledge of data platforms and tools (Databricks, Snowflake, Hadoop, Spark).
*   Familiarity with cloud ecosystems (AWS, Azure, GCP) and DevSecOps principles.
*   Working understanding of cybersecurity, data governance, and interoperability frameworks.
*   Knowledge of smart-city, digital-twin, or IoT ecosystems highly desirable.

**Professional Skills**

*   Exceptional communication and presentation abilities; adept at bridging business and technical domains.
*   Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
*   Collaborative mindset with ability to lead in virtual, cross-functional environments.
*   Entrepreneurial orientation with interest in business growth and client development.

**Certifications (Preferred)**

*   Certified AI/ML, Data Science, or Cloud Architecture credentials (AWS, Azure, GCP).
*   PMP, TOGAF, or similar enterprise-architecture certification a plus.
