**Material Handler I at TSI**

Under general supervision, this position is responsible for receiving and issuing materials, cycle counting, and stowing and picking material within the stockroom. A Material Handler I will be assigned to a specific area in receiving or the stockroom. 

**What Will You Do?**

In order to grow and build a successful career with TSI, you will be responsible for:

*   Supply assigned work cell(s) with the parts needed to support the production build.
*   Process the appropriate transactions into SAP.
*   Receive, locate, and change location of parts.
*   Pull jobs, replenish bins, and put away product in the stockroom.
*   Process sub-contract orders.
*   Process stock returns.
*   Follow all safety guidelines and report unsafe conditions to supervisor.
*   Completes other assignments and special projects as requested.
