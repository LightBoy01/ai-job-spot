### Description
Like It Media is a leading Native Advertising agency and a key partner to renowned e-commerce companies. We empower brands to achieve significant scale through data-driven direct-response marketing across prominent platforms such as Outbrain, Taboola, Newsbreak, and MGID. Our core focus is on driving measurable performance, fostering creativity, and ensuring continuous growth.

This role offers exceptional freedom and responsibility, allowing you to fully manage your campaigns from end-to-end. We champion a remote and flexible work model where your results speak louder than your presence. You'll be equipped with top-tier gear (Apple or Windows, your choice) and thrive in a creative, dynamic team environment characterized by flat hierarchies, rapid decision-making, and ample space for your innovative ideas. We believe in fair compensation with substantial upside: an attractive fixed salary complemented by an unlimited, performance-based commission. Furthermore, we are deeply invested in your professional development, offering intensive skill-building opportunities in performance marketing, funnel design, and creative strategy.

This position is tailored for 'Macher' – proactive individuals who are ready to take full ownership, deliver tangible results, and live and breathe performance. If you're eager to transform your expertise, creativity, and ambition into measurable growth, then let's scale together.

### Responsibilities
*   **Full Campaign Ownership:** Independently manage your own native advertising campaigns with five-figure daily budgets, ensuring a strong performance orientation.
*   **Performance-Driven Marketing:** Plan, scale, and execute rigorous A/B testing with a clear focus on maximizing revenue, ROI, and overall growth.
*   **Creative Strategy Development:** Innovate and develop compelling headlines and creative assets designed to optimize click-through rates and conversion metrics.
*   **Data & Performance Analysis:** Conduct daily evaluations of relevant KPIs to provide a data-backed foundation for all creative and strategic decisions.
*   **Continuous Testing & Optimization:** Relentlessly test new funnels, ad concepts, and creatives to consistently discover and implement the next performance breakthrough.

### Qualifications
*   **Native Advertising Professional:** Proven experience managing four- to five-figure daily budgets and a track record of successfully scaling campaigns.
*   **Performance-Driven Mindset:** A genuine passion for achieving results, adept at interpreting complex data, and committed to continuously refining strategies.
*   **Creative Thinker:** Possess a keen eye for emerging trends, a knack for engaging storytelling, and an understanding of psychological triggers to develop high-performing ads.
*   **Strong Analytical Acumen:** View numbers and KPIs not as complexity, but as essential tools for precise optimization and strategic guidance.
*   **Ownership & Proactivity:** Demonstrate a strong sense of responsibility, act proactively, and maintain an unwavering focus on delivering measurable outcomes.
*   **Technical Proficiency:** Familiarity with tracking methodologies, reporting tools, and leveraging AI tools to enhance efficiency and effectiveness.
*   **Hands-on Approach:** You are eager to test, measure, optimize, and celebrate the impact of your efforts.
