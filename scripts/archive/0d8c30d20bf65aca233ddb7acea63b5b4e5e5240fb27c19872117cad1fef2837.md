**Position Summary**

We are seeking a skilled Senior Machine Learning Engineer to join our team. In this role, you will design, develop, and optimize state-of-the-art deep learning-based recommendation systems.

**Role and Responsibilities**

*   Design, develop, and optimize state-of-the-art deep learning-based recommendation systems.
    
*   Utilize Azure infrastructure and Databricks to implement MLOps and AI functionalities for scalable machine learning solutions.
    
*   Build domain specific recommendation models fitting to gaming and media domains.
    
*   Stay updated on the latest advancements in recommendation algorithms and machine learning techniques.
    

**Skills and Qualifications**

*   Preferred : Ph.D in Machine Learning or Computer Science or related field.
    
*   5+ years of experience in ML, NLP, or AI engineering, ideally with hands-on LLM experience
    
*   Strong programming skills in Python, TensorFlow, or PyTorch.
    
*   Experience with data analysis, biz insight report, model training, and evaluation.
    
*   Familiarity with MLOps practices and tools, including Databricks.
    
*   experience with On-device model development 
    
*   Expertise in deep learning-based recommendation systems.
    
*   Experience with Azure infrastructure and Databricks for MLOps and AI capabilities.
    
*   Proficiency in developing specialized recommendation and machine learning models within gaming and media domains.
