**Short Description:**    
  
We are looking for a professional who is passionate about technology and problem-solving using technology whether it is a simple issue that enables a user to work faster or a solution that improves efficiency/productivity for a big team, it gives me immense satisfaction. The candidate should be a keen learner and should keep oneself updated with technological improvements and new technologies. **Role:** Senior Consultant  
  
**Location:** Melbourne

**Roles and Responsibilities:**

*   Play a crucial role in aligning the technical work with the business objectives. 
    

*   Collaborate with the Scrum master and determine which story tickets from the product backlog will be sent into the next agile sprint. 
    

*   Well equipped with the programming expertise and experience. 
    

*   Give technical guidance, communicate directly with team members and support them in their respective tasks.  
    

*   Assist the development team in resolving technical issues. 
    

*   Work collaboratively with development and QA teams to establish and reinforce automation development processes. 
    

*   Assist in the planning, deployment, and management of user acceptance testing effort. 
    

*   Training junior team members, participating in code review etc. 
    

  
**Essential:**  
 

*   Tertiary Qualification in IT / Computer Science / related discipline.

  
 **Preferred:**

*   Java, Spring framework, Springboot , Kafka, Aws services, JPA 
    

*   Rest assure framework 
    

*   Exposure on azure and aws deployments 
    

*   Able to manage Devops responsibilities, Ci/Cd pipelines.. 
    

*   Exposure on deployment tools: Jenkins. 
    

*   Exposure working with deployment tools gradle 
    

*   Testin Framework: Junit, Mockito, Component testing. 
    

  
  
**Additional Skills:**

*   Excellent customer interfacing skills.
*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
*   Strong attention to detail and outstanding analytical and Problem-solving skills.
