### Description\nRemmert specializes in automated storage and logistics systems, mastering space-saving storage and fully automated movement of sheet metal, long goods, and workpieces. The mechanical engineering/automation/logistics sector is a future-oriented industry, and Remmert has been a family-run company since 1945, embodying values such as development perspectives, communication at eye level, and short decision-making paths. As they continue to grow, Remmert is seeking a dedicated Project Purchaser to strengthen their team.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- **Requirements Analysis & Procurement Planning**: Close collaboration with sales, design, and project management to derive technical specifications, create bills of materials, and establish procurement schedules.\n- **Supplier Selection & Development**: Identification, evaluation, and selection of suitable suppliers, considering quality, ESG, and certification standards. Building and maintaining long-term supplier relationships.\n- **Contract & Price Negotiations**: Drafting and negotiating framework agreements, price structures, and payment terms. Ensuring cost-optimized procurement while considering technical requirements.\n- **Technical Risk & Quality Management**: Reviewing technical specifications, standards, and compatibility. Collaboration with quality assurance and management of deviations.\n- **Supply Chain Tracking & Deadline Management**: Monitoring delivery dates, coordinating deliveries, and supporting assembly and installation.\n- **Cost Efficiency & Budget Control**: Preparing cost overviews, conducting price analyses, and contributing to budget planning.\n- **Documentation & Reporting**: Maintaining procurement documentation and providing regular status reports to project management.\n- **Process Optimization & Digitalization**: Contributing to the further development of procurement processes and utilizing digital tools for supplier evaluation.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed technical training and commercial further education with a focus on mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, or a comparable qualification; ideally, a degree in industrial engineering, supply chain management, or a relevant further education.\n- In-depth knowledge of mechanical engineering, ideally in the areas of shelving steel construction, load carriers, conveyor technology, and automation components.\n- Experience in project purchasing, especially in the project planning phase.\n- Strong negotiation skills, knowledge of contract law, and supplier management.\n- SAP knowledge (order processing, invoice workflow) is an \"advantage\".\n- Team player, structured way of working, and a high degree of self-initiative.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at Remmert offers a fantastic opportunity for an experienced Project Purchaser to join a stable, family-run company that values long-term commitment and employee growth. The culture emphasizes open communication, professional development through continuous learning, and a supportive environment with excellent work-life balance benefits like flexible hours and mobile work options. You'll contribute directly to cutting-edge automated logistics projects while enjoying a comprehensive package of perks, from profit-sharing to health and wellness programs. It's a chance to build a meaningful career in a future-proof industry.
