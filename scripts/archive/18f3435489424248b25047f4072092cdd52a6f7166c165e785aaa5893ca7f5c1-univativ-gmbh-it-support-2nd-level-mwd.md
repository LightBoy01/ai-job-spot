### Description

Univativ is a project and personnel service provider that offers students and graduates exciting jobs at renowned companies. Your career is our mission, as your development is close to our hearts. Are you at home in the Windows world and your project experience means nothing can easily faze you? Do you want to support sophisticated IT systems and implement new technologies to create customized solutions for clients? Then our position as an IT Network/System Technician (m/w/d) is exactly the right fit for you!

Our client is a globally successful pharmaceutical company headquartered in Frankfurt am Main. With 3000 employees worldwide, they work daily on an excellent product portfolio. In this project, an exceptional, innovative work environment and diverse topics await you. What are you waiting for? Start your successful professional career with our client in the pharmaceutical industry!

### Responsibilities

*   Receive and process incidents and service requests in 1st and 2nd level support.
*   Provide user support in the client area (remote and onsite).
*   Perform error analysis and resolve disruptions in the Microsoft environment (Windows, MS Office, hardware, network).
*   Manage SAP user administration and SAP CRM.
*   Set up, install, and configure end devices (PCs, notebooks, mobile devices).
*   Administer and support mobile end devices, including handling manufacturer inquiries (Apple, Blackberry).
*   Support IT projects such as rollouts, upgrades, and testing activities.
*   Document all processes in the Helpdesk system.
*   Support remote access solutions (RAS/VPN).
*   Conduct quality assurance and user support for new applications.
*   Handle call reception, prioritization, and forwarding of tickets.
*   Communicate with users as the single point of contact.
*   Provide quick and competent support to users with a service-oriented approach.
*   Ensure seamless IT support and high user satisfaction.
*   Participate in shift operations if required.

### Qualifications

*   Degree in Computer Science, vocational training as an IT Specialist for System Integration, or a comparable qualification.
*   Several years of professional experience in IT support, ideally in 2nd level support.
*   Profound knowledge of Microsoft Windows operating systems and Microsoft 365 / Office 365.
*   Experience with ITIL processes (Incident, Problem, Change Management).
*   Routine in using remote support tools and ticketing systems (e.g., ServiceNow, Jira).
*   Ability for independent error analysis and structured provision of solutions.
*   Strong service and customer orientation as well as communication skills.
*   Analytical thinking and high problem-solving competence.
*   Team player combined with independent and responsible work ethic.
*   Resilience and stress resistance – even with high ticket volumes.
*   Very good written and spoken German, as well as good English.
