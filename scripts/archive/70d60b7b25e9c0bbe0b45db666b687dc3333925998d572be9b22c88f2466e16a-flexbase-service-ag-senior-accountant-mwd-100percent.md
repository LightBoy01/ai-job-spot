### Description\nAre numbers your comfort zone, even when complex? Do you maintain an overview of intricate financial statements and enjoy deriving clear decisions from analyses? Then you might be the person we're looking for! As a Senior Accountant at FlexBase, you will take responsibility for professional and audit-proof accounting in a dynamic startup environment. You will independently manage our monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements, ensuring balance sheet clarity and compliance with OR and Swiss GAAP FER. As our processes are still being built, you can expect a hands-on role with significant scope for shaping and direct impact on the development of our Finance department.\n\nAt FlexBase, you won't find a corporate setup, ready-made processes, or long approval chains. Instead, you'll work in our small, dynamic team to jointly build professional accounting. Your role is hands-on, broad, responsible, and offers considerable room for maneuver. Our promise: A workplace that combines technology and sustainability. Join FlexBase and help us shape the future in Laufenburg – home to the world's largest Redox-Flow battery storage and a state-of-the-art AI data center. Together, we drive innovations for a clean, reliable energy future!\n\nWe offer a challenging and varied activity where you can work independently and contribute your ideas, with opportunities for further development, whether through working with new technologies or individual training. We communicate at eye level and approach each other openly in our teams. You'll enjoy great responsibility and direct influence on building our entire finance landscape, working in an exciting technology and energy environment. We provide close collaboration in a small, motivated team, flexible working conditions, modern tools, and support for further education, along with attractive additional benefits (cafeteria, fitness center, massage, company laptop/phone). Your ideas and commitment are warmly welcome!\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Independent preparation of monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements for all five individual companies according to OR / Swiss GAAP FER.\n- Responsibility for general ledger, sub-ledgers, and all balance sheet and income statement reconciliations.\n- Execution of intercompany recharges, project activations, and accruals.\n- Point of contact for audit firms.\n- Support with tax declarations, VAT settlements, and group-wide reporting requirements.\n- Establishment and further development of lean, reliable accounting processes.\n- Process responsibility for purchasing and sales (P2P / O2C).\n- Maintenance and quality assurance of master data.\n- Establishment and monitoring of a pragmatic Internal Control System (ICS).\n- Project lead for the introduction of Microsoft Dynamics Business Central.\n- Collaboration on other finance and automation projects.\n- Creation and further development of the Accounting Manual and internal guidelines.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed education as a federally certified specialist in finance and accounting (eidg. dipl. Fachfrau/Fachmann Finanz- und Rechnungswesen) or comparable education (university/FH).\n- At least 5 years of experience in financial accounting, ideally within a corporate group.\n- Proficiency in financial statements according to OR / Swiss GAAP FER.\n- Experience with Microsoft Dynamics Business Central; experience in ERP implementations is a plus.\n- Very good analytical skills and high affinity for numbers, systems, and processes.\n- Independent, structured, and quality-oriented way of working.\n- High sense of personal responsibility, pragmatism, and enjoyment of tasks in a rapidly developing environment.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a Senior Accountant eager to make a significant impact within a fast-growing, innovative startup at the nexus of sustainable energy and AI. The role offers unparalleled autonomy and direct influence in shaping the entire finance landscape from the ground up, moving away from rigid corporate structures. Candidates will thrive in a hands-on, collaborative environment where their ideas are valued, and continuous learning is supported. If you're a pragmatic, analytical professional looking to combine your accounting expertise with pioneering technology and enjoy the challenge of building processes rather than just maintaining them, FlexBase offers a truly rewarding career path.
