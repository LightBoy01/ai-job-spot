### Description\nOneTutor is a rapidly expanding EdTech startup dedicated to revolutionizing online tutoring through immersive, AI-powered solutions, creating real-world impact and fostering meaningful innovation. As an early Software Engineer, you will work closely with the founders, playing a pivotal role in shaping the core product and contributing to a cutting-edge platform designed to empower learners and educators. This role offers a unique opportunity to take ownership of projects and grow into a full-stack engineer within a dynamic and supportive environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Take full ownership of either backend or frontend projects, with a strong encouragement to expand into the complementary area and grow into a full-stack role.\n*   **Backend Focus**: Develop robust APIs, model data, and utilize NestJS/TypeScript to build and integrate sophisticated AI-driven features.\n*   **Frontend Focus**: Design and craft intuitive React components, seamlessly integrate with backend services, and continually optimize the user experience.\n*   Collaborate extensively with Product and engineering peers throughout the entire software development lifecycle, from design and development to shipping, testing, and maintenance of features.\n*   Actively participate in code reviews and engage in optional pair programming to foster knowledge sharing and ensure high code quality.\n\n### Qualifications\nWe prioritize ability, impact, and a growth mindset over strict years of experience or formal degrees. We're looking for passionate individuals ready to make a significant contribution.\n\n**Must-have skills:**\n*   Strong experience in **either** React/modern frontend tooling **or** backend development (NestJS/Node, REST/GraphQL).\n*   A high level of motivation and eagerness to learn the complementary side of the stack to grow into a full-stack engineering role.\n*   Proven ability to thrive in high-agility environments, demonstrating adaptability and proactiveness.\n*   A strong desire to contribute to a culture of ownership, paired programming, and continuous improvement.\n*   Specific experience in **at least one** of the following:\n    *   NestJS + TypeScript (including REST or GraphQL API design).\n    *   React + TypeScript (with a solid understanding of hooks and component architecture).\n*   Excellent communication and collaboration skills, proficient in either English or German.\n\n**Tech at a glance (our current stack):**\nTypeScript, NestJS, PostgreSQL, React + Tailwind, RTK Query, LangChain, AWS, Kubernetes\n\n**Nice-to-have skills:**\n*   Working experience or a strong curiosity around AI technologies, including LLMs, LangChain, Embeddings, RAG pipelines, and Vector Databases.\n*   Familiarity with prompt engineering or fine-tuning techniques for AI models.\n*   Prior experience working effectively in lean, disciplined startup or agile team environments.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a motivated Software Engineer to join OneTutor as an early hire, directly impacting the future of AI-powered EdTech.
