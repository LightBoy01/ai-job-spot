### Description\nWelcome to Terra One, a rapidly expanding company dedicated to developing and operating large-scale battery storage systems in Germany. Our mission is to accelerate the expansion of renewable energy infrastructure by providing innovative solutions, with a critical focus on establishing a flexible and environmentally friendly power grid. We achieve this through our battery storage solutions and proprietary AI-based energy trading in the European market.\n\nThe energy trading unit is a highly technical team comprising veteran software engineers, leading AI experts, and power trading professionals. We are seeking a talented Systems Engineer to build, maintain, and optimize our proprietary real-time trading system and its supporting backend and infrastructure components. This role offers the opportunity to make a significant impact on the energy transition within a fast-paced, complex, and mission-driven environment.\n\n### Responsibilities\n*   Build, maintain, and optimize Terra One's proprietary real-time trading system and all supporting backend and infrastructure components.\n*   Continuously improve low-latency, high-throughput data processing pipelines handling gigabytes of live data.\n*   Identify and resolve performance bottlenecks in an event-driven system architecture.\n*   Develop new system functionality, including integrations with additional exchanges and external battery management systems.\n*   Implement and enhance in-memory processing, caching, and distributed messaging to ensure system scalability.\n*   Strengthen and maintain monitoring systems for both technical and business KPIs, including alerting, observability, and reporting.\n*   Drive continuous improvement of security, deployment processes, reliability, and incident management for our 24/7 production systems.\n*   Collaborate closely with trading, data engineering, and machine learning teams to design and implement new execution flows and system enhancements.\n*   Take ownership of your work, iterate quickly, and make informed decisions.\n*   Participate in an on-call rotation for nights and weekends, responding to incidents within defined timeframes (with additional compensation).\n\n### Qualifications\n*   University degree in computer or natural sciences, mathematics, or economics.\n*   Experience with backend programming in backends, IoT, or similar).\n*   Strong experience with event-driven architectures, especially Kafka, and distributed system design.\n*   Deep knowledge of in-memory operations and caching technologies.\n*   Proven experience identifying, profiling, and resolving performance bottlenecks in real-time systems.\n*   Experience with Docker and Kubernetes in production environments.\n*   Experience integrating external APIs and working with streaming, microservice, or event-driven architectures.\n*   Understanding of energy markets or energy trading is a strong plus.\n*   Background or strong understanding of trading systems or private trading (crypto/stocks/FX) is a plus.\n*   Understanding of server administration, network security, and container hardening practices is a plus.\n*   Ability to work in a live production environment with monitoring, alerting, and incident response responsibilities.\n*   Strong communication skills and ability to work effectively in a team.\n*   Fluent in English; German is a plus.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a highly skilled and resilient Systems Engineer who thrives on tackling complex, real-time
