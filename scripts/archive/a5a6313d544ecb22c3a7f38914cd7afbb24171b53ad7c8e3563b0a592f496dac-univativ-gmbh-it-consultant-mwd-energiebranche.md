### Description\nunivativ is a project and personnel service provider offering exciting jobs to students and graduates at renowned companies. Your career is our mission, as your development is close to our hearts.\n\nAre you experienced in requirements or product management, familiar with document management systems, and feel at home in application administration and configuration? Then we are looking for exactly you as an IT Service Manager (m/f/d)! In this role, you will be responsible for the maintenance and further development of the Document Management System (DMS), ensuring system availability and managing user administration. You will also analyze requirements from various departments and train users to ensure optimal use of the DMS. The primary language for this job is German, so you must have fluent German language skills at C1 level.\n\nOur renowned client in the energy sector aims to conserve natural resources, recover valuable raw materials, and reduce the CO2 balance. You will be part of an innovative team from the start, working on complex tasks. If you are looking for a job with great development opportunities, apply now and start your successful career! Your main workplace is Helmstedt, but you also have the option to occasionally work at the Hannover location.\n\nKey benefits include an annual salary between €50,000 and €72,000, a permanent position, the flexibility to work remotely 2 days per week, 33 days of annual leave, and a public transport subsidy. Further benefits cover company pension scheme, childcare subsidy, and diverse development opportunities.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- As IT Service Manager, you will be responsible for the further development and maintenance of the Document Management System (DMS).\n- You will continuously ensure the functionality and system availability of the DMS and manage permissions and user control.\n- Additionally, you will be responsible for gathering and analyzing requirements from various facilities and the central office.\n- Training users in the use of the DMS and answering any queries also falls within your area of responsibility.\n- Finally, monitoring and managing service levels are part of your duties.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A completed degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics, or a comparable field.\n- Practical experience working as an IT Service Manager or in a similar role.\n- Basic knowledge of Document Management Systems.\n- Initial experience in the administration and configuration of M365 Services, especially MS-SharePoint, is desirable.\n- Fluent German language skills at C1 level.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a dedicated IT professional, whether you are starting your career or bringing some experience, to take ownership of a critical Document Management System within a purpose-driven energy company. The role offers a great blend of technical responsibility, user interaction, and system evolution, all within a supportive and innovative team environment. The employer truly values its employees, evident from the generous benefits package including remote work options, extensive vacation, and robust support for professional development and work-life balance. If you are proficient in German and eager to make a tangible impact, this role provides significant growth potential and a chance to contribute to sustainable initiatives.
