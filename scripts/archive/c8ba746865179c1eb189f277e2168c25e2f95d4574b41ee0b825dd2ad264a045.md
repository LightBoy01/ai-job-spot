Are you a reliable and experienced **Class 2 Driver** looking for a secure, full-time role with early starts and regular hours? Join our dedicated team working on the **Hovis contract** in **Washington** and be part of a trusted name in food logistics.

You will be working on a **full-time**, **permanent** basis, on a ‘**5 days out of 7’** with a start time of **02:00 am,** so you can enjoy peaceful roads for smooth driving. This role is contracted to 48 hours per week, providing you with a stable and consistent workload.

**Pay, benefits and more:**

*   An annual salary of **£36,575.05**
*   Holiday pay and Workplace pension
*   A 24/7 online GP service, life assurance and a fully comprehensive employee assistance programme
*   Access to our MyBenefits platform offering a variety of high street discounts, cashback cards, a saving scheme and much more!
*   Access to GXO University – home to a wealth of online training courses, meaning your development never ends!

**What you’ll do on a typical day:**

*   Carrying out **multi-drop deliveries** of bread and bakery products to retail stores and distribution points
*   Ensuring **timely and safe delivery**, providing excellent customer service at every drop
*   **Maintaining a high standard of customer service** during all deliveries
*   Performing **vehicle checks** and keeping accurate **delivery records**
*   **Performing occasional warehouse duties** as needed

**What you need to succeed at GXO:**

*   Hold a valid Class 2 licence
*   Less than six points on your licence (as well as no DD, DR, DG, BA, CD, AC, LC or TT endorsements)
*   An up-to-date CPC and Digital Tachograph

*   A strong work ethic, reliability and a commitment to safety and service
