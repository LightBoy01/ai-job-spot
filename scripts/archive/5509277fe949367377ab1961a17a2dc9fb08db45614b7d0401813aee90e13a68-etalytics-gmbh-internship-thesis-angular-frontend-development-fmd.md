### Description\netalytics is seeking a highly motivated Frontend Angular Intern to join their international team in Darmstadt. This full-time, three-month internship offers a unique opportunity to contribute to smart solutions that reduce energy consumption and CO₂ emissions. As an integral part of the development team, you will gain hands-on experience in modern web development, build real features for the Energy Intelligence Platform _etaONE_, and take your Angular skills to the next level.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Build user-friendly, high-performance frontend features with maintainable, tested, and well-documented code in Typescript/HTML/SCSS.\n- Work closely with product and API teams to bring ideas to life.\n- Grow your technical expertise through mentoring, code reviews, and continuous learning about the latest Angular and related web technologies.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Currently studying computer science, business informatics, or a related field.\n- Eagerness to learn and grow in developing web applications and single-page applications (SPAs).\n- First experience with Angular is a plus.\n- Interest in learning quality standards and developing an understanding of testing methods.\n- Very good English skills required; German skills are a plus.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent entry-level opportunity for a student eager to kickstart their career in frontend development with a purpose. etalytics offers a supportive, collaborative environment with a strong focus on mentorship, continuous learning, and professional growth. Interns will gain practical experience with Angular and modern web technologies, contributing directly to impactful, sustainability-focused projects. The hybrid work model and flexible hours underscore a commitment to work-life balance, making this an attractive role for those looking for meaningful work and a strong team culture.
