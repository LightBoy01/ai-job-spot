### Description
thinkGROUP is a successful system house group constantly striving for innovation and placing great importance on the continuous optimization of internal processes. The use of modern technologies is not an end in itself, but an expression of our innovative spirit and lived customer orientation. Excellent IT service requires optimal systems. Our motivated team gives its best every day to delight our customers anew.

We are looking for you to join us as soon as possible as a team member at our **Bochum** location. Become part of the thinkGROUP and support us as a **Working Student Business Development X Controlling (m/f/d)**. This role offers a unique opportunity to gain deep insights into operational controlling, business process automation, and strategic financial planning within a dynamic and innovative environment. You will be instrumental in shaping future controlling concepts and value driver models for the company.

### Responsibilities
As a Working Student, you will be involved in various key areas:

*   **Operational Controlling**
    *   Gain a comprehensive insight into operational controlling (e.g., merchandise management system, SAP ERP, Excel).
    *   Independently take on routine tasks such as report generation, data maintenance, and the creation of variance analyses.

*   **Power BI and Business Process Automation**
    *   Familiarize yourself with existing BI dashboards for the commission evaluation project.
    *   Support the finalization and implementation of business process automation for the monthly sales commission evaluation.
    *   Ensure accompanying documentation and sustainable storage of the development process.

*   **Business Unit and Corporate Controlling Design**
    *   Evaluate existing controlling key figures.
    *   Develop a comprehensive controlling concept at both Business Unit and Corporate levels.
    *   Implement the developed controlling concept in Power BI.
    *   Present and discuss the controlling concept with the management.
    *   Lead the implementation of the new controlling concept.

*   **Development of a Value Driver Model Concept**
    *   Set up a project concept for a value driver model.
    *   Independently derive potential value drivers across all company levels.

*   **OKR Management System**
    *   Support in optimizing the OKR (Objectives and Key Results) structure and its implementation.

### Qualifications
To succeed in this role, you should possess the following:

*   Enrolled student (m/f/d) in Economics, IT, Business Informatics, or a comparable field of study.
*   Strong analytical understanding and a structured, solution-oriented working style.
*   Proficient in PowerPoint and Excel – ideally with prior experience in Power BI.
*   A high degree of personal motivation and a proactive attitude.

### Author's Take
**Question 1: Given this is a 'Working Student' role, how significant is the impact a student can truly make, especially with tasks like developing new controlling concepts or value driver models?**

That's a fantastic question, and it gets right to the heart of what makes thinkGROUP unique. While you'll certainly handle routine tasks – which are crucial for gaining foundational insight – your primary impact will come from the conceptual development and strategic projects. Think of it less as 'student work' and more as being an integral part of shaping our financial and operational future. You won't just be collecting data; you'll be evaluating existing metrics, developing *new* controlling concepts that directly impact business units and the entire corporation, and presenting these to our management. Imagine the feeling of seeing your value driver model being discussed at a leadership meeting! We believe in empowering our talent, regardless of their current career stage, to bring fresh perspectives and drive tangible change. Your work here isn't just theory; it's about practical implementation that will inform real-world decisions and contribute to our continuous innovation.

**Question 2: The job description mentions a 'motivated team' and 'shared leisure activities.' What's the actual day-to-day culture like for a student joining the Business Development X Controlling team?**

Our culture is probably one of our biggest strengths, especially for new joiners. You're not just a number or an extra pair of hands; you're truly part of the team from day one. That 'motivated team' isn't just PR – we genuinely support each other. You'll experience a robust onboarding with a dedicated 'Buddy' who's there to guide you, not just professionally but also culturally. Whether it's our 'Healthy Wednesday' lunches, getting together for leisure activities, or just grabbing a coffee, we foster an environment where you feel comfortable, valued, and encouraged to learn and contribute.
