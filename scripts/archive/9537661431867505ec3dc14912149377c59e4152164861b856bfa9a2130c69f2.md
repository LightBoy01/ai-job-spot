### **Staff Product Manager - Data Lifecycle**

### Location: Bangalore 

### About Rubrik 

Rubrik (NYSE: RBRK) is on a mission to secure the world’s data. With Zero Trust Data Security™, we help organizations achieve business resilience against cyberattacks, malicious insiders, and operational disruptions. Rubrik Security Cloud, powered by machine learning, secures data across enterprise, cloud, and SaaS applications. We help organizations uphold data integrity, deliver data availability that withstands adverse conditions, continuously monitor data risks and threats, and restore businesses with their data when infrastructure is attacked.

### **About the Product Management Team:**

The Product Management team at Rubrik drives forward Rubrik’s vision, ensuring we continue to deliver on our market-leading platform as we address our customers’ most challenging data management & security needs. Our mission is to deliver the best application aware backups, helping customers to secure their data, while ensuring that we do not compromise on the end to end user experience we are known for.

### About the Role:

Rubrik is looking for a strategic, experienced Staff Product Manager with a track record of driving success and innovation. The Product Manager will lead the strategy and execution for one of the most critical parts of Rubrik’s product line: The Data Life Cycle (DLC) system. This core system is the heart of Rubrik’s award-winning cyber resilience offering. Via DLC, users configure what data is protected, how it is protected, when protection happens, etc. This crucial system underpins over $1 billion in Annual Recurring Revenue. The Product Manager will combine AI features, customer research, and innovative strategy to build the next generation of DLC. 

### What You’ll Do:

*   Own the product strategy, roadmap, and execution of Rubrik’s Data Life Cycle system.
*   Meet with customers and prospects to understand their problems and build solutions that they will love.
*   Research and understand the broader IT ecosystem that Rubrik’s products fit into, including regulatory, infosec, and financial considerations.
*   Perform market/competitive analysis, prioritize product features and clearly articulate decisions and tradeoffs.
*   Represent Rubrik in front of customers in both pre-sale and post-sale situations
*   Collaborate with Rubrik’s Design and Engineering teams to rapidly create high-quality solutions that will endure for many years to come.
*   Interface with all of Rubrik’s Product Management teams, understanding their needs and building requisite capabilities into the DLC system.
*   Communicate strategy and roadmaps to the broader company, building alignment around key initiatives.

### Experience You’ll Need:

*   8+ years of product leadership experience, demonstrating strong planning, communication, organizational, and influence skills.
*   13+ years of overall work experience.
*   Prior Product Management experience in one or more of the following areas:

*   Enterprise IT infrastructure
*   Data backup & recovery solutions
*   Cyber security

*   Ability to understand complex technical systems at a deep level.
*   Creativity to find new and better ways to solve complex customer problems.
*   Business acumen and the ability to build business cases, communicate effectively and influence others.
*   Thorough knowledge of software development methods and processes.
*   Ability to be self-motivated towards the achievement and measurement of established goals.
*   Aptitude to develop and maintain effective working relationships.
*   Familiarity with modern AI tooling and techniques, including agents, context engineering, fine tuning, and evaluations.
