**BTP** forms part of the **Perenti Group** and is one of Australia’s largest suppliers of machinery rental, parts and services to the mining, construction and earth-moving industries. Our capabilities include machine rebuilds, Service Exchange components, Site based and Field Service labour and machinery rental.  **BTP** has supported the mining, earth-moving, and construction industries for over 30 years and with locations in key mining areas **BTP** is currently expanding its capabilities and capacity to better support customers and our people. 

**Management Accountant – BTP Hazelmere**

The Management Accountant will be responsible for ensuring the accuracy, integrity, and timely provision of financial data to both Finance and non-Finance teams. This role will also play a key part in driving improvements to accounting systems and processes, enhancing both efficiency and compliance.

Reporting directly to the Senior Finance Manager, the successful candidate will be a proactive, self-motivated individual who consistently strives for excellence. If you are a driven self-starter with a commitment to achieving outstanding results, we encourage you to apply and become a valued member of the BTP team.

 **About You**

*   Tertiary Accounting Qualification
*   CA/CPA qualified desirable but not essential
*   Minimum 5+ years of Management Accounting experience, preferably in Mining Services or Manufacturing
*   Proficient in the use of Coupa and PRONTO (preferable)
*   Excellent technical accounting skills and analytical skills
*   Proven experience in driving process improvements and system enhancements
*   Ability to interpret, analyse and reconcile financial information 
*   Able to work collaboratively and independently   
*   Excellent verbal and written communication skills essential.
*   Ability to multitask, prioritise with excellent time management skills.
*   Professional discretion plus excellent interpersonal skills.

**About The Role**

*   Assist the month-end / year-end close process, ensuring accurate and timely financial reporting
*   Ensure compliance with accounting standards and company policies.
*   Balance sheet reconciliations
*   Assist audit coordination for Mining Services with Perenti Corporate Finance Team
*   Maintain the accuracy and integrity of financial data
*   Identify process automation and efficiency improvements
*   Support ERP system enhancements and implementation projects
*   Assist with monthly reporting pack preparation
*   Assist with budgeting and forecasting reporting cycles
*   Work closely with operational and commercial teams to provide financial insights
*   Maintain the Fixed Assets Register for Accounting and Taxation purposes with respect to additions, disposals, transfers and splits of assets.
