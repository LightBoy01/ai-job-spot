### Description
Our partner company, a leading research institute located in the vibrant heart of Munich, is actively seeking a **Specialist for Foreign Trade Law (m/w/d)** to join their team as soon as possible. This pivotal role involves contributing to the establishment and continuous development of a risk-oriented, science-focused compliance framework. The institute prides itself on a supportive environment, fostering work-life balance with flexible hours, providing modern facilities, and offering ample opportunities for professional development.

### Responsibilities
*   Contribute significantly to the establishment and ongoing development of a risk-oriented, science-related compliance structure.
*   Create, update, and standardize practical guidelines, templates, recommendations, and checklists for operational use.
*   Provide crucial support in evaluating questions pertaining to export control and foreign trade law.
*   Design and conduct comprehensive training programs and awareness campaigns for employees.
*   Collaborate effectively with various internal stakeholders (e.g., Customs, Purchasing) and external partners such as authorities and consultants.

### Qualifications
*   A completed degree (e.g., in Business, Law, or Social Sciences) or an equivalent qualification.
*   Proven knowledge in foreign trade law, export control law, and ideally in the broader compliance field.
*   Ideally, a basic technical understanding and practical experience in developing and delivering target-group-oriented training sessions.
*   Strong communication skills, a pronounced ability to work in a team, analytical thinking, and a solution-oriented, structured approach to complement your legal profile.
*   Excellent German language skills are essential.

*As an insider, I'd say this role isn't just about policing rules; it's about enabling groundbreaking science safely. Think of yourself as a strategic partner to our researchers. They're pushing boundaries, and you're the one who understands how to navigate the complex international legal landscape to ensure their discoveries can be shared, their collaborations can flourish, and sensitive knowledge is protected, all while minimizing risks. Your impact isn't just compliance; it's facilitating innovation. You're building the very structure that allows our scientists to focus on their work, confident that they're operating within best practices. You'll be at the forefront of shaping how a leading institution manages its global scientific footprint, making sure our advancements benefit the world without unintended consequences. It’s a real challenge, but incredibly rewarding.*
