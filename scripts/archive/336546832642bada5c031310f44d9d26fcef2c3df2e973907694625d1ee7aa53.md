**BMW Financial Services Farnborough – Customer Journey – 13 Month Placement (July 2026)**
**What awaits you?**
*   Monitor and support Process Automation Governance/RPA review cycles and dashboards; assist with maintaining Jira and Confluence to support Agile delivery.
*   Assist in building and evaluating ML models (predictive analytics, anomaly detection, forecasting) and learning data science techniques to generate business value.
*   Support automation initiatives from initial assessments to RPA assessments, including the creation of PDDs/SDDs and development requirements.
*   Help with stakeholder engagement on Process Automation, Agentic AI, and AI topics; collaborate with Data Management, Product Owners, Agile Masters, and senior staff.
*   Promote data science awareness across the business, participate in knowledge sharing, and contribute to a data-driven transformation.
*   Learn and contribute to a customer-focused mindset, assist with change management, stay curious about emerging AI/automation techniques, and help deliver tangible outcomes.
**What should you bring along?**
*   Currently studying a university degree in Data Science/Analytics/AI Governance, aiming for a 2:2.
*   Communication, influence, facilitation, collaboration, and a proactive, professional mindset (outgoing, inquisitive, confident; ability to adapt approach and challenge the status quo).
*   Strong analytical and numerical skills with data‑focused thinking; proficient in data handling, cleaning, feature extraction, and basic analytics concepts.
*   Data science foundation: basic programming skills (Python or R) with familiarity in libraries (pandas, scikit‑learn) and SQL querying; ability to work at both detailed and high level.
*   Multi‑tasking and prioritisation: able to work under pressure, manage priorities, and handle multiple activities simultaneously.
*   MS Office proficiency (Word, PowerPoint, Excel) and a demonstrated willingness to learn about AI, automation, and data handling.
**What can you look forward to?**
*   **Great Pay** – A competitive annual salary of £25,300, 27 days holiday per annum (pro rata to your contract) and an attractive pension scheme.
*   **Rewarding Work-Life Balance** – Contracted working hours are 37.5 hours a week.
*   **Exciting Additional Benefits** – You will have the opportunity to enjoy other employee benefits, a subsidised on-site restaurant and access to our Advantages scheme which gives you a range of offers and discounts.
