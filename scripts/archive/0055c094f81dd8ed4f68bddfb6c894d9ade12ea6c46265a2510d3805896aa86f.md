Erste Group was founded in 1819 as the first Austrian savings bank and today it is one of the largest banking groups in Central and Eastern Europe (CEE). As an attractive employer, Erste Group offers interesting career opportunities in an international environment.

The unit "Balance Sheet Hedging & Cover Pool" is part of the Balance Sheet Management division and is responsible for key areas including covered bonds and cover assets, securitization, credit claims, and hedge accounting.

**Your Tasks**
--------------

*   Support quarterly reporting for synthetic securitizations (e.g. investor, ECB, ESMA, loan by loan level)
*   Monitor monthly RWA impacts and significant risk transfer during the lifetime od securitization transactions
*   Maintain pricing parameters and process payment instructions
*   Contribute to project-related tasks

**Your Background**
-------------------

*   Ongoing university studies with a focus on banking, economics, business informatics
*   Strong analytical skills and a keen interest in securitization and balance sheet management
*   Proficient in MS Office; basic knowledge of SAS and SQL is a plus
*   Fluent in German and English
