### Description
Teaching Socials is one of the fastest-growing business consultancies in the DACH region and a leading advisor for personal brands within the finance and real estate sectors. We are seeking a high-impact Performance Copywriter (m/w/d) to take ownership across the entire marketing funnel. This role is crucial for crafting compelling copy from the initial hook to the final Call-to-Action, spanning webinars, Video Sales Letters (VSLs), ad campaigns, and viral scripts for platforms like Reels, Shorts, and YouTube. You will be instrumental in driving measurable results and shaping powerful narratives for our clients.

### Responsibilities
As our Performance Copywriter, you will own the development and optimization of critical content, including:

*   **Offer & Positioning Refinement:** Sharpening value propositions, establishing proof, managing risk reversal, and defining pricing narratives.
*   **Webinars & VSLs:** Creating outlines, scripts, slide texts, story arcs, objection handling strategies, and compelling CTAs.
*   **Funnel Texts:** Developing engaging copy for landing pages, opt-ins, email sequences (nurture, show-up, close), thank-you pages, and order bumps.
*   **Ads & Creatives:** Crafting powerful headlines, primary texts, and hooks for Meta, YouTube, and LinkedIn, including variations and testing plans.
*   **Viral Scripts:** Designing hooks, cold opens, retention beats, and CTAs for Reels, Shorts, and YouTube, in close coordination with editing and social media teams.
*   **Research & Proof:** Conducting market and persona research, building objection libraries, and transforming case studies/testimonials into persuasive copy.
*   **Testing & Analytics:** Executing A/B tests, analyzing key metrics like CTR, CR, watch-time, and drop-off rates, deriving hypotheses, and rapidly iterating based on data.

### Qualifications
To succeed in this high-impact role, you should possess:

*   A **proven track record** in performance-driven copywriting, ideally from an agency, consulting firm, or high-ticket sales environment.
*   A comprehensive **portfolio** showcasing your work across webinars/VSLs, landing pages, ads, **and** social scripts.
*   Demonstrable **results** (e.g., increased CTR/CR, reduced CPL, improved watch-time).
*   **German C2 proficiency** (stylistically confident), with a clear, precise writing style free of superfluous words.
*   Strong command of structured copywriting principles (Hook–Problem–Solution–Proof–Offer–CTA) and effective objection handling.
*   An understanding of **finance/real estate contexts** or a strong willingness to rapidly acquire this knowledge.

**Nice to Have:**

*   Experience with **Direct Response** and **Longform Sales Letters**.
*   Basic understanding of **tracking/attribution** (e.g., UTMs) and familiarity with tools like **Notion/Airtable**.
