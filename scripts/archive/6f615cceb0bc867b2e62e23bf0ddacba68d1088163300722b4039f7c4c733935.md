**Description**
---------------
Are you passionate about transforming supply chains with AI? Join our Digital Supply Chain team as a Supply Chain Co-op, where you’ll gain hands-on experience in AI-driven analytics, process automation, and decision intelligence advance planning systems. You’ll work on real-world projects that integrate AI into planning, forecasting, and inventory optimization—making a measurable impact on operational efficiency. **This Co-op will be 6 months in duration from Jan-June 2026.**
**What You’ll Gain:**
Mentorship from industry leaders in digital supply chain transformation.
Exposure to cutting-edge AI applications in a high-tech manufacturing environment.
Opportunity to contribute to strategic initiatives in Agentic AI and digital planning.
A chance to make a real impact on how supply chains are optimized and automated.
**Responsibilities**
--------------------
*   Partner with planners and IT to automate supply chain workflows using AI tools and data from Kinaxis, SAP, Excel, and other enterprise systems.
*   Support the development and deployment of AI solutions for supply/demand pegging, KPI tracking, and execution efficiency.
*   Analyze large datasets (10M+ rows) to identify trends, risks, and optimization opportunities.
*   Contribute to predictive modeling and process automation that improve planner productivity and decision-making.
**Required Experience and Skills**
----------------------------------
*   Currently enrolled in a  Master’s program in Supply Chain, Engineering, or Business Analytics.
*   Strong foundation in AI/ML concepts and familiarity with tools used in supply chain optimization.
*   Proficient in statistical analysis, Excel (pivot tables, vlookups), and SQL
**Desired Experience and Skills**
---------------------------------
*   Experience with planning platforms such as Blue Yonder (BY), Kinaxis, or SAP is preferred.
*   Excellent communication skills and ability to work across cross-functional teams.
