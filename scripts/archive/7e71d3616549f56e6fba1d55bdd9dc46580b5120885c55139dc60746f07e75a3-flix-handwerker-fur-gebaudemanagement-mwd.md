### Description\nJoin Flix in Munich and be part of a modern, dynamic work environment where you'll contribute to making global travel easier, more sustainable, and affordable through innovative technology. We're looking for a hands-on skilled tradesperson to ensure the smooth technical operation of our headquarters, support daily activities, and manage renovation projects. This role is crucial for maintaining our facilities and supporting our mission. We offer a fair salary, diverse development perspectives, attractive travel benefits, and comprehensive health & well-being programs.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Ensure smooth technical operations (infrastructure, utilities, security).\n- Act as the first point of contact for all technical inquiries.\n- Perform minor repairs and electrical work, and coordinate external tradespeople, suppliers, and landlords.\n- Support renovation projects and routine maintenance.\n- Assemble furniture and assist with office moves and internal floor changes.\n- Monitor compliance with fire safety regulations (occupational safety) and maintain documentation.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed technical vocational training (e.g., electrician, building services technician, or similar) with at least 2 years of relevant professional experience.\n- Solid knowledge of building maintenance, electrical engineering, and general craftsmanship.\n- Strong communication skills for interacting with service providers and landlords.\n- Fluent in German (C1+); English is a plus.\n- Proficient in MS Office; experience with AutoCAD or ticketing systems is a plus.\n- Organized, solution-oriented, and resilient.\n- A team player with physical fitness (e.g., ability to lift up to 15 kg for safety).\n- We value a holistic view – if you don't meet every requirement 100% but believe you can make a difference, we encourage you to apply!\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an experienced and hands-on professional to join a rapidly growing international company.
