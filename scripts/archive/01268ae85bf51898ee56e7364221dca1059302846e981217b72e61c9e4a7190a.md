**What you can expect**

As a Principal AI Product Manager for AI Agent Development, you will leverage cutting-edge technologies like Large Language Models, build robust APIs, and create developer-centric solutions to deliver exceptional value to our customers. As a key technical leader, you will drive innovative experiences through code-first approaches, working closely with developers, customers and partners to meet their unique business needs.

**About the Team**

The AI Product Management team is at the forefront of AI innovation, defining platform strategy and integrating advanced AI/ML capabilities across our solutions. As a key leader, you will drive innovative experiences, working closely with customers and partners to meet specific business requirements.

**Responsibilities**

*   Shaping end-to end product direction through data-driven insights, code prototyping, and deep technical customer engagement.
    
*   Identifying the type of training data required to enhance AI models and improve product performance.
    
*   Leading product growth and Go-to-Market strategy for AI agent solutions and developer APIs, targeting ambitious YoY revenue growth.
    
*   Delivering scalable architectures, SDKs, and developer enablement plans to drive enterprise adoption.
    
*   Orchestrating cross-functional initiatives across research and engineering, developer relations, and technical sales teams.
    
*   Driving technical partnerships and API integrations with key platform providers.
    

**What we’re looking for**

*   Have a Bachelor's in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field.
    
*   Possess a minimum of 8 years of experience as a Product Manager with deep expertise in enterprise software, AI technologies, and API development.
    
*   Demonstrate a track record in Generative AI, LLMs, and AI applications for business users.
    
*   Have experience presenting to executives (internal and customer executives) with consultative professional business acumen.
    
*   Have a developer-centric mindset and experience with at least one modern programming language (Python, JavaScript, Go, etc.).
