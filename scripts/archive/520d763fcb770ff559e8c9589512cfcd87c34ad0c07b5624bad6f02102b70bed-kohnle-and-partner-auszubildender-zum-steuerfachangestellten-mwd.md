### Description
Kohnle & Partner is a distinctive tax firm that truly walks the talk when it comes to top-tier working conditions. With state-of-the-art technology, digital processes, dynamic growth, and leading experts, we offer an environment built on success and innovation. For over 45 years, our highly motivated team has been the foundation of our success, establishing us as one of the leading firms in the Baden region. We are dedicated partners, passionate problem-solvers, and innovative future-thinkers for both companies and private individuals.

Join our dynamic team at one of our locations in Karlsruhe, Weingarten, Offenburg, or Karlsbad.

As a Tax Assistant (Steuerfachangestellter), your role goes far beyond merely juggling numbers. We are committed to supporting your personal and professional growth, enabling you to take the next step and become a comprehensive and competent advisor to our clients on all tax and business administration matters.

### Responsibilities
*   Preparation of smaller tax declarations and diverse financial accounting.
*   Preparation of and support for annual financial statements.
*   Review of tax assessments and correspondence with the tax authorities.
*   Conducting research activities.
*   Supporting digital transformation initiatives within the firm.
*   Engaging in other areas that spark your interest and leverage your strengths, fostering a tailored development path.

### Qualifications
*   You are communicative and enjoy working collaboratively with people.
*   You are proactive and translate ideas into action.
*   You demonstrate openness towards new ideas and embrace change.
*   You maintain high standards for the quality and precision of your work.

**Desirable, but not essential:**
*   A higher education entrance qualification (Fachhochschulreife / allgemeine Hochschulreife) or a secondary school leaving certificate (Realschulabschluss) with good grades.
*   Initial experience with MS-Office applications, particularly Word and Excel.
