**Position Summary**

Why join our team?  
  
Samsung is a world leader in AI innovation in our product portfolio. From embedded AI tech in our SmartPhones to the recently launched Vision AI for our TV range we are leading the world in tech innovation. Using AI as a tool in the workplace is no longer considered a luxury – it is swiftly becoming a must have. Joining our team as an AI Specialist offers the opportunity to work on high-impact initiatives, driving meaningful change and making a real difference in the world through the power of AI.  
  
We are seeking an AI Specialist to join our team and lead the usage of 3rd party innovative artificial intelligence solutions. Your expertise in machine learning, deep learning, and data analysis will be crucial in gaining oversight and maximizing the adoption and implementation of transformative AI applications. You will work closely with the EDO leadership team to conceptualise, design, test, and deploy AI projects that drive innovation and provide value in our daily work life, at all times being in close cooperation with our regional Subsidiaries and Samsung´s HQ in Suwon.  
  
Join us and be a part of shaping the future of AI

**Role and Responsibilities**

**Your key responsibilities**

*   Develop and implement AI commands and outcomes to improve system performance and user experience.
    
*   Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify and address AI-related challenges and opportunities.
    
*   Research and stay current on AI technologies and best practices to inform project decision-making.
    
*   Design and conduct experiments to evaluate the effectiveness of AI solutions and optimize for desired outcomes.
    
*   Create and maintain local documentation for AI systems, including data models, processes, and performance metrics.
    
*   Provide technical expertise and support for AI-related projects, including troubleshooting and resolution of issues.
    
*   Communicate complex AI concepts and findings to non-technical stakeholders in a clear and understandable manner.
    

**What we need for this role**

This AI Specialist role will have a strong educational background, fundamental programming skills, and the willingness to learn and grow within a team. Candidates will benefit from a supportive and nurturing work environment where they can receive guidance from experienced team members. There will be plenty of opportunity for learning, skill development, and on-the-job training

*   Proficiency in machine learning algorithms and deep learning techniques.
    
*   Experience in implementing and maintaining AI systems and tools.
    
*   Strong programming skills in languages such as Python, R, or Java.
    
*   Knowledge of data mining and statistical analysis methods.
    
*   Ability to develop and optimize AI models for specific use cases.
    
*   Experience with natural language processing and computer vision.
    
*   Familiarity with cloud platforms and AI frameworks.
    
*   Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.
    
*   Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.
    
*   Continuous learning and staying updated on AI advancements and trends.
