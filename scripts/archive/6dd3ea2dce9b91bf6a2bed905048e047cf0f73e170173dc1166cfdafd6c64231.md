Scopely is looking for an AI Artist (Generalist) to join our Bingo Bash team in Bangalore on a hybrid basis!
At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.
The Bingo Bash team is home to  bingo, cards, slots  and more! Here you will work on a top-grossing F2P game with significant and growing daily revenues, and help create fresh content for our players.
### What You Will Do
*   Model Design & Curation:
*   Design, fine-tune, and curate lightweight style/character/prop LoRAs (or similar adapters) aligned with Bingo Bash’s look and live-ops themes
*   Build and maintain a model registry (“model zoo”) with clear usage notes, examples, and version history
*   Source, tag, and sanitize training/reference datasets with complete licensing and provenance tracking
*   Output Quality Ownership:
*   Define and enforce quality checklists for readability, silhouette clarity, color/contrast, brand motifs and IP safety
*   Perform inpainting/outpainting, de-noising, line-cleaning, and upscaling; deliver layered PSD/PNG assets ready for animation, VFX, and UI
*   Run in-engine/preview checks (mockups on cards, tiles, icons) to validate scale, legibility, and compression
*   Workflow & Pipeline Efficiency:
*   Build reusable workflows (ComfyUI/A1111 or equivalent) with control/pose/depth guidance and batch queues
*   Integrate with DCC and engine pipelines (Photoshop/Krita, Illustrator, Blender, Unity/Unreal) including naming conventions, pivots, margins, and atlases
*   Cross-Team Collaboration:
*   Partner with Art Direction, Concept/UI Artists, Tech Art, and Producers to map briefs into visual targets and acceptance criteria
*   Share prompt packs, workflow templates, and guides; enable artists on promptcraft and workflow hygiene
*   Collaborate with Marketing/UA teams on key art and variants for A/B testing when required
*   Compliance & Risk Management:
*   Ensure licensed/consented training materials; maintain audit trails for models and datasets
*   Review likeness/IP/bias risks periodically and document mitigations in coordination with Legal and PM
### What We're Looking For
*   Strong portfolio demonstrating art fundamentals (composition, color, style consistency)
*   Practical experience with generative art tools (Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI, A1111, or equivalents)
*   Proficiency with tools such as Photoshop, Krita, Illustrator (Blender a plus)
*   Understanding of game asset pipelines and optimization for mobile
*   Familiarity with dataset curation, tagging, and model training workflows
*   Collaborative mindset with clear communication skills
*   Strong attention to detail and ability to maintain brand/style fidelity
*   Curiosity and adaptability to experiment with new creative workflows
### Bonus Points
*   Familiarity with Unity for integrating assets
*   Exposure to 3D modeling tools (Blender, ZBrush)
*   Interest in mobile game development and Agile methodologies

### About Us
Scopely is a global interactive entertainment and mobile-first video game company, home to many top, award-winning experiences such as "MONOPOLY GO!," “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “Stumble Guys,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” and “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” among others.
