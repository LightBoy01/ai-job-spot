### **Role Overview**

The Project Manager will drive operational excellence across our content production pipeline, ensuring the efficient delivery of high-quality content to our clients. This role demands exceptional organizational skills, a strong eye for process optimization, and the ability to coordinate effectively across multiple stakeholders. You’ll work closely with Content Leads and writers to streamline workflows and uphold consistent delivery standards.

### **Key Responsibilities**

### **Client & Workflow Management (60%)**

*   Own and streamline communication between clients and internal content teams.
*   Manage content approvals and feedback loops with clarity and timeliness.
*   Proactively update Notion boards and timelines to reflect status changes.
*   Ensure timely delivery of high-quality content.
*   Monitor Slack for client and team updates; collaborate with other PMs to ensure full coverage across accounts.

### **Internal Operations & Special Projects (40%)**

*   Onboard new clients and team members into internal systems.
*   Maintain and improve operational dashboards, automations, and tooling (primarily in Notion).
*   Collaborate with leadership on special projects and troubleshooting initiatives.
*   Partner with tech teams to coordinate system updates.
*   Keep internal documentation current and contribute to scalable process improvements.

### **Onboarding Timeline (First 30 Days)**

*   **Weeks 1–2:** Shadow existing PMs, train on Notion, tools, and observe real client workflows.
*   **Weeks 3–4:** Co-manage accounts under supervision; begin handoff of responsibilities.
*   Exposure to both AM-heavy and PM-heavy shifts to ensure coverage across time zones.
