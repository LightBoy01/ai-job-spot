**About Us:** 

Our public service is being called upon to deliver a unified, seamless and effective digital experience for all New Zealanders.  As a member of the Digital Services branch you will help champion this change.  

The Digital Services Branch works across the public service to connect, influence and support the system to take advantage of new digital opportunities. It also supports the Government Chief Digital Officer (GCDO) to drive the government’s digital transformation and position government to succeed in a changing and evolving digital age. 

The Branch is here to deliver on the expectations of the government’s Strategy for a Digital Services. More information can be found here: [https://www.digital.govt.nz/digital-government/strategy/](https://www.digital.govt.nz/digital-government/strategy/) 

**About the role:** 

The All of Government Services Delivery Group (AoGSD) is responsible for a broad Portfolio of All of Government ICT services and digital products to over 300 government agencies. AoGSD works closely with the supply market to deliver modern digital and ICT services across government, through common capability agreements, cloud framework agreements and marketplace.govt.nz contracts. Over $1 billion annually goes through our Portfolio. 

The Technical Specialist – ICT Capabilities provides expert level technical assistance and advice in the development and deployment of AoG ICT Capabilities – including Common Capabilities, Marketplace, Framework Agreements, Cloud Services, and the Common Process Model.  The Technical Specialist will provision effective technical consultancy and product advice for both BAU support and project/change work covering ICT Common Capabilities, Marketplace, Framework Agreements, Cloud Services, plus other services – such as Artificial Intelligence (AI) as required.  

The role will include engagement with internal stakeholders, external agencies, and suppliers to support and facilitate effective delivery of business outcomes. 

In collaboration with the AoG Services Delivery team, the Technical Specialist ensures a cohesive and integrated approach to development, change and delivery, enabling a complete digital and commercial products and portfolio ecosystem view.

### About Company

The Digital Services branch is responsible for strategy and enabling functions that lead and contribute to digital maturity for digital customer-centred services, across both the public sector and Te Tari Taiwhenua. Being digitally mature means being focused not only on technology, but also governance, capability, and other attributes to deliver outcomes and better value for customers. The branch supports and enables agencies and Te Tari through providing strategy, advice, and delivery to progress modern and adaptive public services.
