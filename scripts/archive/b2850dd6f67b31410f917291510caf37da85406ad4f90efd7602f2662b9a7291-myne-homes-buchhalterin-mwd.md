### Description
MYNE Homes is revolutionizing access to dream holiday properties through an innovative co-ownership model. Founded by serial entrepreneurs and backed by renowned European business angels (including founders of Auto1, Smava, and Numa), we are on a fast track to becoming the next Proptech Unicorn. We are rapidly expanding and seeking a dedicated **Accountant (m/f/d)** to join our dynamic Accounting Team. In this role, you will take ownership of ongoing financial accounting tasks, gaining unique insights into the diverse and exciting real estate sector.

This is a unique chance to actively build and shape one of the leading Proptech startups. You will work directly with experienced founders and high-caliber investors from the technology and real estate world within a motivated, results-oriented team. We offer a culture of flat hierarchies, short decision paths, and significant freedom for your own ideas, all while enjoying a competitive salary and the usual startup perks, including stays in our dream holiday properties!

### Responsibilities
As an Accountant at MYNE Homes, your core responsibilities will include:

*   Managing financial accounting across the entire corporate group, encompassing personal and capital companies.
*   Handling payment transactions and efficiently managing bank accounts.
*   Assisting with the preparatory work for monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements.
*   Reconciling general ledger accounts, debtors, and creditors to ensure accuracy.
*   Collaborating effectively with other departments and promptly clarifying inquiries.
*   Working in close cooperation with national and international tax consulting firms.
*   Gaining valuable insights into international accounting standards and practices.
*   Performing intercompany reconciliations to maintain accurate group financials.
*   Conducting cost center accounting to support financial analysis.
*   Conceptualizing and optimizing accounting processes to enhance efficiency.

### Qualifications
To succeed in this challenging and rewarding role, you should possess:

*   A minimum of 3-5 years of professional experience in accounting.
*   A successfully completed vocational training as an Accountant (`Buchhalter/in`) or Tax Clerk (`Steuerfachangestellte/-r`), or an equivalent qualification. An additional qualification (e.g., Certified Accountant `Bilanzbuchhalter/Finanzbuchhalter`) is a strong advantage.
*   Extensive experience and proficiency in using DATEV or Odoo accounting software.
*   Prior professional experience in the real estate industry is considered ideal.
*   A meticulous, precise, and structured approach to your work.
*   Strong organizational skills combined with a proactive, hands-on mentality.
*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills in both German and English.
*   Good proficiency in MS-Office applications (Word, Excel, and PowerPoint).
