### Description
Are you tired of half-baked code and endless meetings?

We are seeking a **Senior Full Stack Developer with a strong focus on Backend** who genuinely values transparent communication, clean coding, and high-quality work, not just as buzzwords. As an ambitious e-commerce agency with about 35 dedicated individuals, we're shaping the future of digital shopping and managing market-leading online shops. Join us and help make the internet more beautiful, better, and faster every day.

### Responsibilities
*   **Configure and further develop** complex Shopware projects.
*   Take **technical responsibility** in projects and work closely with project managers.
*   **Develop innovative ideas** for e-commerce solutions.
*   Explore and learn modern and new web technologies.

### Qualifications
*   A minimum of **4-5 years of practical experience** in Full-Stack Development, **with a focus on Backend**.
*   At least **2 years of experience with Shopware 6**.
*   **Proficient in German (C1 level)**.
*   **Solid knowledge of PHP (Symfony)** and ideally Python.
*   Experience with **JavaScript frameworks** like Vue.js or React.
*   Routine in **using Git**.
*   Demonstrated sense of responsibility and quality awareness.
*   Solution-oriented and **independent work style**.
*   **Bachelor's degree** or vocational training as an IT specialist in areas such as Mathematics, Physics, & Computer Science.

### Author's Take
This is an excellent opportunity for a Senior Full Stack Developer who thrives in a **100% remote, structured, and solution-oriented environment**. The company prides itself on **efficient, digital processes** with "no process chaos," focusing on technical automation. They foster a culture of continuous learning with **daily training opportunities** and highly value **feedback and team spirit**, even incorporating fun elements like "Nerfguns and funny memes." You'll work on a diverse portfolio of **80-90 active clients**, including well-known brands like Koro and Zalando, providing varied and challenging projects. The tech stack is modern, featuring Python, JavaScript (Vue.js, React), PHP (Symfony), and Git, with opportunities to explore new technologies. Plus, they offer **individual goal and salary systems**, showing a commitment to employee well-being and growth. If you're looking for autonomy, impact, and a supportive team, this role is a compelling fit.
