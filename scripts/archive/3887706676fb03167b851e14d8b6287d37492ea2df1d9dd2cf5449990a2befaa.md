**The Role**
------------

The successful candidate will drive outcomes and support in all aspects of the project commercial and contract functions, including but not limited to:

*   Formation and management of RFQ packages and Subcontractor tendering;
*   Administration of subcontract packages, including management of variations, EOT and daywork claims;
*   Perform cost control and reporting duties for end of month reporting and client contract reports;
*   Attend weekly subcontractor meetings representing Civmec’s commercial interests as required;
*   Drafting and submission of notices of delay, extensions of time, subcontracts and or supplier agreements required for the project;
*   Provide support to the project team in the preparation of relevant notices within designated timeframes;
*   Notify clients of any issues, special circumstances or breaches of contract;
*   Liaising with subcontractors to ensure all relevant insurances are current and legal (i.e. workers compensation)
*   Revise and retain contract registers highlighting payment conditions payment claims; and
*   Maintain company standards of documentation and records for Quality Assurance purposes;

**About You**
-------------

To be successful in the role you will possess the following:

*   Tertiary Qualification in a relevant field is desired but not necessary
*   Minimum 3-4 years’ experience in a role as a Contract Administrator or Quantity Surveyor;&nbsp;
*   Previous experience within the construction industry is essential;
*   Strong, clear and effective communication skills, both written and verbal; and
*   Strong understanding of commercial management and project controls.
