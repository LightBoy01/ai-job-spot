**About the Opportunity**

We have an exciting opportunity to join Metcash as a **Credit Officer** on a full-time basis located in Crestmead, Queensland.  The Credit Officer will be responsible for effectively and efficiently administering credit department activity to ensure ALM cash flow is maximised; ALM’s credit risk is minimised, and customer service is enhanced.   

**In this Role**

*   Manage a large, aged customer ledger with focus around driving down debtor days and balances to optimise financial performance
*   Ensuring efficient risk management to align with both business and customer expectations.
*   Maintain credit limits and trading terms
*   Investigation of unreconciled payments
*   Coordinate order pool management, ensuring timely release of orders to meet logistical deadlines.
*   Resolve disputes effectively and cultivate strong customer relations through proactive communication and problem-solving.
*   Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders within a large, national team to achieve collective objectives.
*   Support continuous improvement initiatives within the credit function

**You’re Likely a Match If**

*   Proficient in administrative tasks and customer service, demonstrating meticulous attention to detail.
*   Exceptional written and verbal communication skills for effective interaction.
*   Strong time management abilities, adept at prioritizing tasks efficiently.
*   Self-motivated, confident, and proactive, fostering a positive work environment.
*   Experienced in a similar role, adept at building and maintaining relationships with stakeholders and negotiating favourable business outcomes within a fast-paced, collaborative setting.
