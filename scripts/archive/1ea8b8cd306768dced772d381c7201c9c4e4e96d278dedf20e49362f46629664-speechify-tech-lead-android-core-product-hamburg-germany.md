### Description
Speechify's core mission is to eliminate reading as a barrier to learning. With over 50 million users, Speechify's text-to-speech products transform PDFs, books, Google Docs, news articles, and websites into audio, empowering users to read faster, more efficiently, and with greater retention. The product suite includes iOS, Android, Mac, Chrome Extension, and Web apps, with recent accolades including Google's Chrome Extension of the Year and Apple's Design award for inclusivity in 2025.

Speechify operates as a 100% distributed organization with nearly 200 team members globally, including talent from leading companies like Amazon, Microsoft, Google, and high-growth startups like Stripe. We foster a flat organizational structure where technical excellence and consistent, fast delivery allow anyone to become a leader. A strong work ethic, solid communication skills, and an obsession with winning are highly valued.

As our Android app continues its rapid growth, becoming the most used text-to-speech app in the Play Store, we are seeking a Senior Android Engineer. This pivotal role will be instrumental in supporting our expanding user base and spearheading new, exciting projects to drive our product forward. We're looking for a strategic thinker who thrives in fast-paced environments, is passionate about making product decisions, and excels at building delightful user experiences.

### Responsibilities
*   Own major features, collaborating closely with the design team to deliver high-quality functionality within the app.
*   Contribute significantly to shaping the future direction of our Android team and product.
*   Maintain and improve reliability metrics for critical features.
*   Actively participate in cross-functional discussions with Product, Design, and Engineering teams.
*   Conduct thorough code reviews and provide mentorship and support to teammates.
*   Address critical issues and navigate unexpected technical challenges.
*   Manage feature releases, including providing nightly builds for the QA team.

### Author's Take
Here are a couple of questions a prospective candidate might ask, along with an insider's perspective:

**1. Given Speechify's mission to break down reading barriers for millions, how does a Senior Android Engineer directly contribute to this impact, and what's the most exciting challenge in achieving that?**

*"That's a fantastic question, and it really gets to the heart of what makes working at Speechify so rewarding. As a Senior Android Engineer here, you're not just writing code; you're fundamentally enhancing how millions of people access information and learn. Imagine a student with dyslexia finally being able to engage with their textbook, or a busy professional digesting a long article during their commute. Your work directly enables that. The most exciting challenge, and where you'll make the biggest impact, is in pushing the boundaries of what's possible with text-to-speech technology on Android. This means optimizing performance for diverse devices, crafting intuitive UIs that are accessible to everyone (including those with learning differences), and integrating cutting-edge AI to make the audio experience feel incredibly natural. You'll be solving real-world problems for a diverse user base, and the direct feedback and appreciation from our users are incredibly motivating. It's a unique blend of technical challenge and profound social impact."*

**2. Speechify prides itself on a 100% distributed, flat organization with 'hands-off management.' How does this structure foster senior-level ownership and innovation, especially when tackling complex Android challenges, and what kind of support system exists for distributed teams?**

*"Our distributed, flat structure is actually our superpower when it comes to ownership and innovation, especially for senior engineers. Here, 'hands-off' doesn't mean you're left adrift; it means you're empowered to lead. If you have an idea that can significantly improve the Android app – a new architectural pattern, a performance optimization, or a feature enhancement – you're expected to champion it. You'll have direct access to product, design, and even leadership to make your case and then own its implementation. This rapid decision-making cycle and direct influence over the product are incredibly liberating and accelerate innovation.

For support, we've built an asynchronous culture that's highly effective. We rely on clear documentation, robust communication tools, and a culture of proactive sharing. You'll be working alongside incredibly talented engineers who've come from top tech companies and startups, and the expectation is that we all support each other through code reviews, technical discussions, and collaborative problem-solving. We prioritize results and impact over micromanagement, trusting our senior engineers to drive projects forward with autonomy. It's an environment where your technical expertise and initiative are not just valued, but essential to our success, and your voice truly shapes the product's future."*
