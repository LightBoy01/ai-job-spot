### Description\nAt TechBiz Global, we are seeking a motivated Junior / Intermediate QA Engineer for one of our top clients. This part-time, remote role offers an exciting opportunity for growth within an innovative and supportive environment.\n\nCommencing January 1, 2026, this position requires a commitment of approximately 12 days per month (3 days/week) on an indefinite contract basis, renewed quarterly. You will be instrumental in scaling up testing capacity, collaborating closely with developers, product owners, and fellow QA team members to uphold product quality across the entire development lifecycle.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Write, maintain, and execute comprehensive test cases, test plans, and bug reports.\n- Perform functional, regression, integration, and exploratory testing.\n- Work with the QA lead and engineering team to identify, reproduce, and track software defects.\n- Contribute to continuous improvement of QA processes and best practices.\n- Assist with early-stage test automation tasks, including basic scripting and framework usage.\n- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand requirements and ensure high-quality deliverables.\n- Participate in release validation and support ongoing quality initiatives.\n- (Optional/Preferred) Conduct Accessibility testing, ensuring compliance with relevant guidelines (e.g., WCAG).\n\n### Qualifications\n- Experience creating and executing test cases, test plans, and bug reports.\n- Basic knowledge of test automation concepts and scripting languages—Python preferred.\n- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a high attention to detail.\n- Good communication skills and the ability to work effectively in a collaborative team environment.\n- Ability to work independently and manage time within a part-time schedule.\n\n**Nice to Have:**\n- Experience with Accessibility testing (e.g., WCAG, screen readers, keyboard navigation).\n- Familiarity with test automation frameworks or CI/CD pipelines.\n- Experience with issue-tracking or test-management tools (e.g., Jira, TestRail).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role presents a unique opportunity for a Junior or Intermediate QA Engineer to make a significant impact within a flexible, part-time, and fully remote setup. The emphasis on collaboration, continuous improvement, and the chance to contribute to early-stage test automation makes this an ideal environment for professional growth. The company clearly values a supportive, team-oriented culture and offers long-term engagement, making it a great fit for someone seeking influence and balance.
