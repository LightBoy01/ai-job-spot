### Description\nRemmert specializes in automated storage and logistics systems for sheet metal, long goods, and workpieces, ensuring space-saving storage and fully automated movement. We operate in a future-proof industry: mechanical engineering, automation, and logistics.\n\nEstablished in 1945, we are a family-run company committed to fostering development opportunities, communication at eye level, and short decision-making paths. We are continuously growing and looking for colleagues to actively support our team.\n\nAs a **Commissioning Engineer (gn)**, you will be based in Löhne, Germany, and travel internationally (approx. 50% travel, or about 100 overnight stays per year) to our customers' sites.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Commissioning of software at national and international customer sites\n- Installation, simulation, and testing of software\n- Conducting training sessions\n- Configuration of databases and software\n- Leading customer workshops for process definition and optimization\n- Rolling out software updates\n- Exchanging and adjusting electrical components\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed vocational training in automation or information technology, or a comparable qualification\n- Good knowledge of application installations, especially Windows\n- Entry-level or professional experience in the relevant field\n- Affinity for control technology is desirable\n- Strong communication skills in German and English\n- Independent and reliable working style\n- Class B driving license\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for someone passionate about high-tech automation and enjoys a dynamic, international role. Remmert stands out as a family-run company that truly values its employees, offering extensive development perspectives, open communication, and a strong work-life balance facilitated by hybrid work, 30 days of vacation, and comprehensive benefits like profit sharing, e-bike leasing, and health management. The combination of international travel and a supportive, growth-oriented environment makes this an attractive long-term career path for a motivated Commissioning Engineer.
