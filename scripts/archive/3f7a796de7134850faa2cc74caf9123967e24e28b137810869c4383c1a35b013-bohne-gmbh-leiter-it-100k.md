### Description
An internationally leading logistics company is seeking an experienced IT Lead to drive digital transformation. You will lead a team of experts, shaping modern IT solutions for interconnected business processes within a global service provider. This role is pivotal in maintaining operational excellence while pioneering new technologies.

The company is a historically grown family business known for its appreciative culture, excellent working atmosphere, and strong emphasis on collegiality and collaboration. They demonstrate a high readiness to invest in modern IT infrastructure and technologies, offering considerable creative freedom in a digitalization-focused environment. You'll benefit from short decision paths, direct access to executive management, and long-term career prospects in a successful mid-sized enterprise. While remote work is not offered, you'll find a modern office setup with genuine team proximity, flexible working hours (core hours 10 am - 3 pm), free parking, and various team-building events and amenities.

### Responsibilities
*   Ensure the smooth operation of all IT systems (network, workstations, mobile devices) and be responsible for budget, assets, and operational resources.
*   Develop and maintain a reporting platform for process data analysis and lead initiatives for automation and efficiency improvement.
*   As a team leader, manage a team of experts, coordinate external service providers, and directly collaborate with executive management.
*   Establish new technologies and oversee their implementation, always with a focus on quality, security, and scalability.

### Qualifications
*   Completed degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics, or a comparable qualification.
*   Several years of leadership experience and an above-average technical understanding in infrastructure, applications, and Business Intelligence.
*   Knowledge of modern architectural approaches, Cloud and BI tools, and experience working in international, agile teams.
*   Excellent communication skills in German and English, high degree of initiative, and willingness to travel.
