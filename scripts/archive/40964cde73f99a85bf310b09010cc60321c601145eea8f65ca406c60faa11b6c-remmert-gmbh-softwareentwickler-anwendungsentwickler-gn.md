### Description\nRemmert is a specialist in automated storage and logistics systems, providing innovative solutions for space-saving storage and fully automated movement of sheet metal, long goods, and workpieces. Established in 1945, Remmert is a continuously growing, family-run company that prioritizes development opportunities, open communication, and streamlined decision-making. We are seeking a dedicated Software Developer to contribute to the new development of our Warehouse Management Software.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Full-stack software development using C# / .Net.\n- Conception and development of new products and solutions.\n- Technical documentation of software.\n- Active participation in the technical architecture and conception of new features.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed training as an IT specialist for application development or a comparable qualification.\n- Solid experience in C# development.\n- Experience with Blazor and Entity Framework Core is desirable.\n- Strong understanding of clean code, modern software architectures, and design patterns.\n- Excellent German language skills (written and spoken).\n- Good English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis role at Remmert offers a compelling opportunity for a C# developer looking to make a tangible impact within a stable, family-owned company. The focus on automated logistics systems means you'll be working on advanced, real-world applications. Remmert clearly invests in its employees, offering a permanent contract, 30 days of vacation, and a wide array of benefits including profit sharing, e-bike leasing, health management, and a generous company pension scheme. The emphasis on continuous development, regular feedback, and a supportive, collaborative environment (complete with regular team events and family-friendly activities) makes this an attractive long-term career destination for someone who values growth and a strong company culture.
