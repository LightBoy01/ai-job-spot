**Job Description**

**What is the opportunity?**

*   Capital Markets Quantitative & Technology Services - Data AI and Research Technology (DART) team is looking to hire a Data & AI Solution Architect to lead the design secure, scalable and cost-effective data & AI solutions for building Data Products, Signals and AI Agents for Investment Banking, Global Markets, and Investment Research.
    

**What will you do?**

*   Work directly with the product teams to capture detailed requirements and develop end-to end detailed solution architecture specifications covering both functional and non-functional aspects of the product.
    

*   Collaborate with Enterprise Architecture, Data & AI governance teams to ensure full alignment of solution architecture with enterprise data strategy, AI governance frameworks, security policies, compliance and architecture standards.
    

*   Partner with engineering teams to validate that the delivered solution is as per the prescribed architecture specification.
    

*   Present architecture artifacts to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
    

**What do you need to succeed?**

**Must Have**

*   5+ years’ industry experience, including 2 years as a Solutions Architect focused on large scale Data and AI projects and 3 Years experience as a Software Engineer.
    

*   Proven experience in architecting hybrid cloud solutions that leverage both on-premises and cloud services for compute and storage, aligned with performance, compliance and scalability goals.
    

*   Solid experience collaborating with Engineering teams, Product and Program management, Data and AI governance groups.
    

*   Strong Proficiency in Python, SQL, Identity and Access management solutions.
    

*   Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
    

**Nice to Have**

*   Experience with Agentic Architectures
    
*   Hands on experience in Data Engineering and applied Machine Learning.
    
*   Degree in Computer Science or Engineering
    
*   Experience with Azure Databricks, Kubernetes
