### Description
This role is for a dedicated Fiber Optic Measurement Technician who will play a crucial part in ensuring the reliability and performance of our fiber optic networks. The ideal candidate will be responsible for precise measurements, documentation, and the coordination of fiber optic connections, contributing directly to our service quality.

### Responsibilities
*   Perform bidirectional OTDR measurements of fiber optic routes using launch/receive fibers at 1310/1550nm.
*   Create detailed measurement protocols and ensure thorough documentation within the change management process and our MegaNet system.
*   Independently plan fiber optic measurement tasks, including scheduling and coordination of appointments.
*   Execute switching, commissioning, and acceptance procedures for fiber optic connections.
*   Coordinate and follow up on the rectification of any identified defects, ensuring timely resolution.

### Qualifications
*   A completed vocational training in telecommunications/electrical engineering or equivalent professional knowledge.
*   First professional experience in optical transmission technology.
*   Possess a structured, independent, and reliable approach to work.
*   Strong communication and coordination skills.
*   Hold a valid driver's license (Class B/C1).
