**Company Description:**
**About Mirantis**
Mirantis is the Kubernetes-native AI infrastructure company, enabling organizations to build and operate scalable, secure, and sovereign infrastructure for modern AI, machine learning, and data-intensive applications. By combining open source innovation with deep expertise in Kubernetes orchestration, Mirantis empowers platform engineering teams to deliver composable, production-ready developer platforms across any environment—on-premises, in the cloud, at the edge, or in sovereign data centers. As enterprises navigate the growing complexity of AI-driven workloads, Mirantis delivers the automation, GPU orchestration, and policy-driven control needed to manage infrastructure with confidence and agility. Committed to open standards and freedom from lock-in, Mirantis ensures that customers retain full control of their infrastructure strategy.
We serve global leaders including Adobe, PayPal, Liberty Mutual, Splunk, and Volkswagen.  Learn more at **[www.mirantis.com](https://www.mirantis.com).**
**Job Description:**
We are looking for a skilled Kubernetes DevOps Engineer to drive end-to-end custom integration across our k0rdent-ai platform. You will collaborate with our engineering teams to design and deliver scalable AI infrastructure on Kubernetes environments.
Your responsibilities will include contributing to open-source projects, making strategic architectural decisions, and partnering with external organizations to integrate cutting-edge solutions. Working within an agile framework, you will directly impact how enterprises deploy and manage AI workloads at scale.
**Main Responsibilities**
*   Design, implement, and document Kubernetes hardened integrations that connect with enterprise customer environments
*   Build comprehensive automation pipelines for infrastructure provisioning and service deployment across hybrid cloud architectures
*   Provide operational support by diagnosing, triaging, and resolving complex system issues with clear prioritization based on customer business impact
*   Work with core cloud-native technologies and AI frameworks including k0rdent, k0s, k0smotron, Kubeflow, Kserve, vLLM, Nvidia AI Enterprise, Kubevirt, Harbor, ArgoCD, Grafana monitoring platforms and more
*   Partner closely with customer development teams to ensure successful application deployment and optimization on Kubernetes infrastructure
**Qualifications:**
*   Strong Kubernetes expertise as a user and operator
*   Strong knowledge of hardened, high-quality Kubernetes integration
*   General experience with Linux, virtualization, networking, and storage
*   Overall system architecture, scalability, reliability, and performance experience
*   Experience in debugging, diagnosing, and troubleshooting complex production software
*   Experience in developing and maintaining services built on microservice architecture
*   Experience in large enterprise IT ecosystem
*   Preferably a university/master degree in Mathematics, Electronics, Telecommunications, or Software Engineering
*   Excellent communication in English - written and spoken
**Nice to Have:**
*   OpenStack experience - familiarity with the APIs as a user
*   Experience handling talks at events and running webinars
*   Experience in onboarding applications to the cloud
*   Production operations experience
*   Experience with design, development, testing, and monitoring of large-scale and data-intensive systems
*   Experience with operating AI-related workloads
