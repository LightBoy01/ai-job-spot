### Description

**Start your career in sales – 100% commission-based & without income limits!**

At SumUp, we believe in empowering small businesses with smart and user-friendly financial solutions, from card payments to comprehensive POS systems. Our mission is to strengthen entrepreneurs so they can focus entirely on their business.

Are you brave enough to work independently, shape your own success, and manage your time freely? Then you've come to the right place!

As a freelance Field Sales Consultant, you are your own boss and have the opportunity to earn attractive, unlimited commissions! You'll build relationships with local businesses and show them how SumUp can simplify their operations. Your main task is the direct sale of our solutions – whether it's mobile card terminals or extensive payment systems, you'll have everything you need to succeed.

**What you can expect:**

*   **100% performance-based compensation** – your income depends on your effort, with no upper limit.
*   **Maximum flexibility** – work when and where you want.
*   **Top products with high market potential** – compelling solutions for small businesses.
*   **Training & sales support** – we won't leave you alone.

### Responsibilities

*   **Active new customer acquisition** – identify and win local businesses for SumUp.
*   **Personal meetings and consultation** – present our products and demonstrate their benefits.
*   **Build customer relationships** – secure long-term partnerships through excellent service.
*   **Own customer and lead management** – you decide how you build your business.

### Qualifications

*   Passion for sales and self-motivation.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer service is an advantage.
*   Strong communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Very good German language skills.
