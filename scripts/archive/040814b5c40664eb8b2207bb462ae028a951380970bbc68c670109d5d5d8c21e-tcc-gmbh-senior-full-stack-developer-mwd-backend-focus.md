### Description\nWelcome to TCC! TCC GmbH was founded in Hamburg in 2020. Today, over 50 dedicated employees work towards a common goal: sustainably improving medical care through digital innovations. As a **certified manufacturer of software as a medical device** and provider of medical services, we develop an **AI-powered telemedicine platform** that enables cross-sector care and specifically supports medical professionals. Our solutions combine clinical expertise with technological excellence – for data-driven, quality-oriented, and future-proof medicine.\n\n### Responsibilities\nAs a Technical Lead, you will be responsible for:\n*   **Technical Leadership of an International Development Team**: Coaching and coordinating external developers within a distributed, cross-functional team, ensuring uniform processes and quality standards.\n*   **Architecture and Conception of Enterprise Solutions**: Designing clean, scalable system architectures and, in coordination with our Product Management team, creating well-founded technical concepts from scratch.\n*   **Implementation of a Robust Secure Development Lifecycle**: Establishing and improving security processes throughout the entire development chain.\n*   **Highest Level Quality Assurance**: Ensuring excellent test coverage and medical-compliant documentation standards.\n*   **Hands-on Development of Complex Backend Systems**: Actively participating in development and staying technically up-to-date.\n\n### Qualifications\nWe are looking for someone with:\n*   At least 5 years of experience in enterprise software development with a focus on processes, quality, and security.\n*   Expertise in Java/Kotlin as a basic requirement for backend development.\n*   Experience in technical team leadership and concept creation – you can independently develop, document, and communicate complex technical concepts.\n*   Profound understanding of Secure Development – from Threat Modeling to secure implementation.\n*   Knowledge of test automation and CI/CD – you live \"Testing first\" and automated deployments.\n*   Basic frontend knowledge (Vue/React) desirable – for understanding the entire application landscape.\n*   Strong communication skills in German and English – for successful coordination of international teams.\n*   Passion for quality and Clean Code – especially important in the regulated medical environment.\n\n### Author's Take\nTCC offers an exciting opportunity to contribute to a critical, future-proof industry by leveraging cutting-edge AI in telemedicine. The role itself is a blend of hands-on technical contribution and leadership, perfect for someone who thrives on building scalable, secure enterprise solutions and mentoring a diverse team. The company emphasizes a culture of innovation, quality, and professional growth, all while prioritizing employee well-being through flexible working hours and a hybrid model. If you're passionate about making a tangible impact on healthcare through technology, value a supportive and challenging environment, and are looking for a role with significant influence, TCC is certainly worth exploring. The attractive compensation and 30 days of vacation further sweeten the deal, recognizing the high caliber of talent they seek.
