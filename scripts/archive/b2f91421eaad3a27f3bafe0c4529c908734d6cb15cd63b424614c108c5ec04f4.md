**Internal Communications Lead**

**St Andrew’s Healthcare**

**Fixed-term contract (1 year), with possibility of extension**

**Location: Northampton, with flexible working**

**Salary: Circa £50,000, depending on experience**

**About St Andrew’s Healthcare**

St Andrew’s is a charity that specialises in complex mental health.

With hospitals in Northampton, Birmingham and Essex, we’ve been caring for some of the most vulnerable people for nearly 200 years - giving them hope for a better future.

While society has come a long way in recognising mental health, those with the most complex needs are still too often overlooked. That’s where St Andrew’s steps in.

**The Role**

This is a unique opportunity to use your communication skills to make a meaningful difference every day - shaping how internal communications are delivered across the charity during a time of significant organisational and cultural change.

As Internal Communications Lead, you’ll help colleagues navigate this change, ensuring they feel informed, supported, and connected. This is a role with real purpose, and one where you’ll see the impact of your work every day with both colleagues and patients.

Reporting to the Director of Communications & External Affairs, you’ll join a small, dedicated team working at the heart of decision-making at St Andrew’s.

**The Person**

This role is suited to someone who thrives in a fast-paced environment and is comfortable working through significant change, uncertainty, and challenge.

You’ll bring substantial internal communications experience - gained in-house in the commercial, charity, or public sector. You’ll have a strong track record of supporting organisational change, with business partnering experience.

You’re a self-starter who takes personal responsibility for your work and delivery. You use initiative to solve problems and overcome challenges, with meticulous attention to detail. You’re also a team player - collaborative, and someone who acts with integrity (there’s no place for egos in the St Andrew’s Communications Team).

**Skills and Experience**

*   **Communications**: Exceptional written communication skills, with the ability to tailor engaging messages for different audiences, including senior leaders, front-line colleagues, and patients.
*   **Strategic Planning**: Proven ability to develop and implement communications strategies across diverse and complex stakeholder groups.
*   **Business Partnering**: Experience of business partnering with project teams to support the delivery of key programmes of work.  
*   **Project Management**: Skilled in planning and delivering communications campaigns, particularly those focused on organisational and cultural change.
*   **Creative Flair**: Able to develop and execute creative ideas and solutions to engage audiences.
*   **Channel Management**: Experienced in creating engaging content and managing internal channels such as town halls, internal magazines, intranet, and social platforms.
*   **Digital**: Confident using communication tools and platforms, with a good understanding of emerging technologies including AI.

**Reward**

  
We offer an excellent benefits package including: Pension; Sickness policy on par with the NHS; Access to the Blue Light Card and Health Service Discounts schemes; 27 days annual leave (plus bank holidays); Life cover; Electric Car lease scheme; Cycle to work scheme; Healthcare cash plan; Free parking; Paid DBS application; Access to free vocational qualifications. On-site facilities such as gym and access to cafés all set within beautiful grounds. 

**Closing date:** **26th October** **(we encourage early applications as interviews will be planned when we have suitable candidates)**

**Start Date: ASAP**
