### Description

On behalf of our partner company in Munich, we are looking for a **Labor Lawyer / HR Legal Counsel (m/f/d)** to join as soon as possible. In this pivotal role, you will be responsible for addressing national and international labor law matters, including tax and social security law, acting as a crucial legal expert within the HR department.

### Responsibilities

*   Manage and resolve national and international labor law issues, encompassing tax and social security law considerations.
*   Provide essential support to the HR Director in the planning, coordination, and implementation of international projects, as well as personnel-related measures, ensuring compliance with legal, labor, and collective bargaining regulations.
*   Represent the company in labor court proceedings.
*   Draft and ensure the legal compliance of guidelines, works council agreements, and employment contract templates.
*   Collaborate effectively with the works council.
*   Coordinate and oversee international assignments and expatriates.
*   Advise and train managers and HR colleagues on all labor law topics.
*   Actively drive the further development of HR processes and work closely with the HR development team.

### Qualifications

*   You possess a degree as a fully qualified lawyer (Volljurist), business lawyer (Wirtschaftsrechtler), or a comparable qualification with a strong focus on labor law.
*   You have several years of professional experience in Human Resources with an international context; ideally, you have profound knowledge of tax and social security law, and experience with collective agreements (e.g., in the metal and electrical industry).
*   You demonstrate good user knowledge of MS Office applications.
*   You are characterized by strong communication skills, diplomatic acumen, assertiveness, and intercultural competence.
*   Excellent written and spoken German and good English language skills complete your profile.
