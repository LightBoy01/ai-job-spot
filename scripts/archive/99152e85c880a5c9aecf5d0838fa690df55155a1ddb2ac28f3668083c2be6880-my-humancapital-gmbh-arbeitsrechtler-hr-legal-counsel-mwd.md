### Description\nOur partner company in Munich is seeking an experienced Labor Lawyer / HR Legal Counsel (m/f/d) to join their team. In this pivotal role, you will be responsible for handling national and international labor law matters, providing crucial legal expertise to HR and management, and ensuring compliance across all personnel-related activities.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Handle national and international labor law issues, including tax and social security law.\n- Support the HR Director in planning, coordinating, and implementing international projects and personnel-related measures, ensuring compliance with legal, labor, and collective bargaining regulations.\n- Represent the company in labor court proceedings, draft guidelines, works agreements, and employment contract templates, ensuring their legal conformity.\n- Collaborate with the works council and coordinate/supervise national and international expatriate assignments.\n- Advise and train managers and HR colleagues on all labor law topics, drive the further development of HR processes, and work closely with HR development.\n\n### Qualifications\n- A degree as a fully qualified lawyer (Volljurist), business lawyer (Wirtschaftsrechtler), or a comparable qualification with a specialization in labor law.\n- Several years of professional experience in HR with an international focus; ideally, sound knowledge of tax and social security law and experience with collective agreements (e.g., in the metal and electrical industry).\n- Good user knowledge of MS Office.\n- Strong communication skills, diplomatic tact, assertiveness, and intercultural competence.\n- Very good written and spoken German and good English language skills.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an excellent opportunity for a skilled Labor Lawyer / HR Legal Counsel to make a significant impact within a growing international company. The role offers long-term stability with an unlimited permanent contract, a supportive work environment, and a strong emphasis on work-life balance through flexible hours and home office options. Located centrally in Munich, the company provides professional onboarding, a modern workplace, and ample opportunities for professional development and continuous learning. If you are passionate about labor law and thrive in an international context, this role offers a rewarding career path.
