**THE ROLE**

**Division: Travel Retail**

**Job Title: Digital Intern**                                                                  

**Reporting to: Digital & E-Commerce Manager**

**About the Role**

Through this role, you will be able to gain an understanding of: digital marketing & e-commerce in the environment of Travel Retail Asia Pacific, gaining exposure to best-in-class digital practices across fragrance, makeup & skincare category.

**KEY RESPONSIBILITIES**

In this role you will be responsible for:

**Digital Campaign Planning & Execution**

*   Design & execute effective digital amplification programmes (e.g. livestreams, social media, display advertising) that drives commercial sales growth
*   Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams (e.g. marketing, commercial) to ensure digital campaigns are aligned with the IMC & commercial activities.
*   Track & report campaign performance to evaluate effectiveness of activation & formulate actionable insights for future.

**Omnichannel Execution & In-Store Digitalisation**

*   Work on O2O (online to offline and vice versa) campaigns (e.g. hotel bouncebacks, social media redemption mechanics, etc.) with internal and external partners.
*   Collaborate with global brand & digital tech teams to develop & adapt digital tools that elevate consumers’ shopping experiences
*   Implement guidelines in partnership with cross functional teams (marketing, merchandising, commercial, training) to ensure successful implementation in stores and promotional spaces across APAC region.
*   Track commercial performance & gather feedback from commercial teams to further improve subsequent variations of tools.

**CRM**

*   Leverage retailer’s customer database to create direct communications strategy and plan mechanics for targeted conversion.
*   Track channel, campaign, and creative performance to enhance future iterations.  

**Competitor Analysis Review & Digital Upskilling**

*   Evaluate competitors’ digital & e-commerce activities across the region & share best practices/innovations from competitor brands, providing actionable insights for brands
*   Assist in upskilling extended team’s digital capabilities through providing insights into industry’s latest trends & happenings.

**WHAT YOU’LL BRING**

**_ESSENTIAL:_**

*   Undergraduates who can commit **for at least 6 months** (Jan 2026 to Jun 2026) for a **full-time internship,** including a **2-week handover period in Dec 2025**.

*   Skilled in the use of MS Office tools including Outlook (or related email tool) Excel, Word and PowerPoint.
*   Effectively bilingual in English and Chinese. Must be able to speak and type in Chinese.
*   Self-motivated and self-starter, with strong ownership and learner mindset.
*   Possesses intellectual curiosity, with an appetite for problem solving and comfortable with changes and uncertainty.   
*   Adaptable and articulate, with the ability to work in a fast-paced environment and interacting with a myriad of internal and external stakeholders.
*   Strong interest in beauty.

**LEARNING OUTCOMES**

1.  Acquire digital campaign management skills, that includes:
    1.  Digital strategy formulation (guided by line manager)
    2.  Optimisation in campaign execution (targeting strategies, creative choice, commercial promotions)
2.  Increase digital competency through understanding industry’s latest happenings & trends
3.  How to bring actionable insights with real commercial impact through data analysis
