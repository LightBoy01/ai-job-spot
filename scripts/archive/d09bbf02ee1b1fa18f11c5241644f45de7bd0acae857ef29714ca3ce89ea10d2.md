Dive in and do the best work of your career at DigitalOcean. Journey alongside a strong community of top talent who are relentless in their drive to build the simplest scalable cloud. If you have a growth mindset, naturally like to think big and bold, and are energized by the fast-paced environment of a true industry disruptor, you’ll find your place here.  We value winning together—while learning, having fun, and making a profound difference for the dreamers and builders in the world. 

We're seeking a senior-level designer to join our Growth and Digital Experience team—with a focus on AI and  Growth—who is passionate about simplifying and unifying the experience from our marketing site through our onboarding flow and into our AI/ML products. Reporting to the Creative Director, you'll become a vital part of a vibrant Growth and Digital Experience team that champions an inclusive and supportive environment. Design at DigitalOcean is not just wireframing around complex challenges; we're spearheading the development of simple, powerful tools across our entire ecosystem.

**What You’ll Do:**
-------------------

*   **Identify and act quickly:** Move rapidly from opportunity to concept to design, creating multiple options to maintain momentum.
*   **Deliver impact:** Lead and contribute to projects that drive significant value for customers and the business.
*   **Design user flows:** Create complete user flows that address current and future needs.
*   **Core team member:** Influence growth and AI strategy and execution as part of the Digital Experience team with Product Managers, Engineers, and other Designers.
*   **Communicate clearly:** Articulate and exhibit design perspectives and solutions to complex problems for non–desinger audiences.
*   **Collaborate technically:** Work with engineering to resolve front-end issues and enhance product quality.
*   **Drive KPI improvements:** Hypothesize how design can impact key performance indicators and solve product and brand challenges.
*   **Advance design practices:** Implement best practices and introduce industry-leading tools and methods.
*   **Foster relationships:** Maintain strong cross-functional relationships to ensure seamless collaboration.
*   **Contribute to our design system:** Collaborate to introduce new elements and share innovations in the design library.
*   **Spearhead innovation:** Design intuitive and cohesive user experiences that are engaging and straightforward. Collaborate with Product Managers, Designers, and Engineers to create impactful interactions and experiences that exceed user expectations.
*   **Empower users:** Focus on human-centered design principles to enhance user capabilities and respect their needs, driving satisfaction and aligning with strategic goals.
*   **Ensure relevance:** Create contextually relevant experiences for users of varying technical proficiency, addressing the evolving needs of developers and their teams.
*   **Foster enjoyment:** Make development delightful by creating engaging and enjoyable experiences, encouraging continuous innovation and positivity across the team.

**What You’ll Add to DigitalOcean:**
------------------------------------

*   **AI-expertise:** AI is ever changing and evolving at a very rapid pace. Your experience working with AI, designing for AI, and selling it will prove vital for this role. 
*   **Cloud and/or Platform Experience:** Experience designing for cloud infrastructure or platform products, with an understanding of how developers and technical teams adopt, scale, and integrate these solutions.
*   **Experience and versatility:** Bring relevant experience from AI platform companies, cloud infrastructure companies, and the like. Work on both broadly defined concepts and narrowly defined tactical deliverables.
*   **Dynamic engagement:** Thrive on engaging with a wide range of projects across the DigitalOcean ecosystem.
*   **T-shaped skills:** Deep expertise in product design, with broad skills including visual design, development, research, and usability testing, among others.
*   **Effective communication and team player:** Excel in verbal and written communication, works well within a team, combining opinionated perspectives with humility.
*   **Accessibility advocacy and user-centricity:** Prioritize accessible design and understand the needs of both technical and non-technical users.
*   **Organization and self-motivation:** Maintain highly developed organizational skills and take full responsibility for your work, knowing when to seek guidance.
*   **Lifelong learning and tech-savvyness:** Stay updated with the latest in design systems, continuously pursue learning, and have a keen interest in technical products and their design challenges. Having worked closely with developers or for developer companies is a big +.
*   **Stakeholder savvyness and adaptability:** Gain buy-in from multiple stakeholders, keep them informed about impactful changes, and adjust strategies and roles to meet business demands.
