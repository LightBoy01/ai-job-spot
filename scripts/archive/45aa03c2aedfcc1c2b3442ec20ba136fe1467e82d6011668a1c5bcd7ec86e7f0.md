**What you’ll do**

As Director, Engineering, you’ll lead one of our software development teams. You will be responsible for supporting the career development of the engineers on your team and you will be building and deploying core backend services and frontend applications that are part of the LILT platform. You’ll work closely with our product management and design organizations to ensure we are building the right software for our customers from the private and public sectors, the translator community, and our services team in a reliable and scalable fashion.

We are looking for candidates who have a strong technical background and a solid product vision and can help guide teams and design organizational structure and processes but aren't afraid of rolling up their sleeves and getting down in the weeds on a technical challenge. You'll need to be open to experimenting, and iterating on processes as the company grows. You'll need to be empathic, supportive, and excited to teach as well as learn.

**Our Story**
=============

Our founders, Spence and John met at Google working on Google Translate. As researchers at Stanford and Berkeley, they both worked on language technology to make information accessible to everyone. While together at Google, they were amazed to learn that Google Translate wasn’t used for enterprise products and services inside the company.The quality just wasn’t there. So they set out to build something better. LILT was born.

LILT has been a machine learning company since its founding in 2015. At the time, machine translation didn’t meet the quality standard for enterprise translations, so LILT assembled a cutting-edge research team tasked with closing that gap. While meeting customer demand for translation services, LILT has prioritized investments in Large Language Models, human-in-the-loop systems, and now agentic AI.

With AI innovation accelerating and enterprise demand growing, the next phase of LILT’s journey is just beginning.
