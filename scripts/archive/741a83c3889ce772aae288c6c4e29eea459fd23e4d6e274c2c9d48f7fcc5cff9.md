**Shape the Digital Future of Canadian Communities**

  
**Join our team and what we'll accomplish together**

Drive transformative growth as the strategic architect behind TELUS' nationwide fibre expansion - one of Canada's most ambitious connectivity initiatives. You'll masterfully connect big-picture national marketing vision with authentic, grassroots community engagement, creating meaningful impact where it matters most.

Own the entire journey from innovative concept to successful market launch, while building lasting relationships that accelerate our footprint across diverse territories. This role offers the unique opportunity to blend strategic thinking with hands-on execution, directly influencing how millions of Canadians experience next-generation connectivity.

  
**What makes this role exceptional:**

*   Lead high-impact initiatives that transform entire communities
*   Bridge corporate strategy with real-world community needs
*   Drive measurable growth in Canada's fastest-expanding telecom program
*   Build your expertise across the full marketing and expansion lifecycle
*   Make a tangible difference in connecting underserved areas

  
Ready to leave your mark on Canada's digital infrastructure while advancing your career in one of the country's most dynamic growth areas?

*   Ensure seamless strategy-to-execution alignment across the organization

*   Lead localized demand generation 
*   Manage stakeholder relations with influencers

*   Deliver customized channel playbooks with training and advertising oversight
