**Responsibilities:**

*   Responsible for day to day Diesel workshop management for repairs of commercial/ naval vessels
*   Ensure engine overhaul processes are carried out effectively and productively in order to meet customer specifications and requirements
*   Monitor and guide engineers and technicians to ensure they are strictly compliant to respective engine protocols, Critical Process Inspection (CPI), task sheets and processes stated in Diesel workshop handbook
*   To oversee department overall projects to achieve engine delivery deadlines, targets and quality assurance.
*   Strictly implement yard safety regulations and  promote safety awareness by leading as role model

**Requirements:**

*   Diploma or Degree in Marine or Mechanical Engineering
*   Preferably 5-7 years of working experiences in yard or OEMs for diesel engines
*   Able to perform troubleshooting on pneumatic, hydraulic, piping and basic electrical system with minimum guidance
*   Able to handle mutliple projects for engine repairs and overhauls
*   Knowledge on Engine overhauling and applied mechanics 
*   Familiarity with Engine Warranty, Claims and Job Acceptance related processes
*   Able to supervise a team in both workshop and onboard vessels
