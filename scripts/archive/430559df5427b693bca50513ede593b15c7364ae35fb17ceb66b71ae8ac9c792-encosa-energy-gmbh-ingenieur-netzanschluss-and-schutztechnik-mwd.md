### Description\nJoin encosa to actively shape the energy transition with technical precision and a holistic approach. encosa, short for \"energy cost savings,\" empowers companies to reduce electricity costs and avoid CO2 emissions through advanced battery storage systems and intelligent software. As a full-service provider, we handle everything from feasibility studies and grid connection to installation and operation, ensuring projects are safe, compliant, and on schedule.\n\nAs an Engineer for Grid Connection & Protection Technology (m/w/d), you will take full responsibility for the entire grid connection work package, from initial feasibility to commissioning. You will manage grid operator processes, coordinate necessary tests, conduct technical verifications, and serve as the central interface for project management and grid operators.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Prepare and submit registrations and technical data packages to grid operators.\n- Proactively manage and respond to queries from grid operators.\n- Plan and be responsible for verification procedures according to **VDE-AR-N 4105** and **VDE-AR-N 4110**.\n- Define **SLD** (Single Line Diagrams), protection concepts, CT (Current Transformer) specifications, and remote control technology.\n- Coordinate external partners for protection tests (e.g., FAT, SAT).\n- Maintain the timeline for grid milestones; manage dependencies and risks.\n- Consolidate approvals, test protocols, and certificates until commissioning.\n- Continuously develop standards and templates for future projects.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Degree in **Electrical Engineering** or a comparable qualification (e.g., Master craftsman, Technician).\n- Several years of experience in grid connection – ideally with battery storage or renewable energy (EE) systems.\n- Profound knowledge in protection technology, relevant standards, and grid operator processes.\n- Proficient in reading and creating SLDs and technical specifications.\n- Experience with certification processes, testing procedures, FAT (Factory Acceptance Test), and SAT (Site Acceptance Test).\n- Strong communication skills for interactions with grid operators and authorities.\n- German language proficiency at least **C1**.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a skilled Engineer in Grid Connection and Protection Technology to make a significant impact within the rapidly evolving energy sector. encosa offers a high-impact role where you'll lead real energy projects with a strong technical focus on grid connection, implementation, and certification. The company embraces a dynamic startup culture characterized by open communication and team spirit, alongside a flexible hybrid work model based in Munich. With an attractive compensation package including a fixed salary, bonus, and VSOP, and clear development opportunities towards Senior Project Lead or Operations Management, this role is ideal for those seeking responsibility, growth, and a chance to contribute directly to the energy transition.
