At our partner firm, you can largely define your area of responsibility based on your personal strengths and preferences.

*   Preparation and review of annual financial statements.
*   Preparation of tax declarations for individuals and corporations.
*   Special tax advisory projects based on individual qualifications, e.g., in inheritance tax law or corporate restructuring tax law.
*   Support during tax audits.
*   Team leadership and personnel responsibility (optional).
*   Further individually selectable areas of activity that bring you joy.

For a successful match, it would be beneficial if you possess the following:

*   Meticulous, efficient, and conscientious way of working.
*   Open to a digital way of working.
*   Understanding of leadership and responsibility towards employees (optional).
*   A successfully completed tax advisor examination.
*   Passion for tax topics.
