### Description
SumUp is looking for highly motivated, self-driven individuals to join their team as a freelance Field Sales Consultant. This is a unique opportunity to kickstart your sales career on a 100% commission basis with unlimited earning potential!

SumUp empowers small businesses with smart, user-friendly financial solutions, ranging from card payments to comprehensive POS systems. Our mission is to strengthen entrepreneurs, allowing them to fully concentrate on their core business.

As a freelance Field Sales Consultant, you'll operate as your own boss, building relationships with local businesses and demonstrating how SumUp can simplify their operations. You'll be responsible for the direct sale of our solutions, equipped with everything you need for success, from mobile card terminals to complete payment systems. This role offers maximum flexibility – work when and where you want – combined with top-tier products boasting high market potential. While you're independent, SumUp ensures you're not alone, providing comprehensive training and ongoing sales support.

### Responsibilities
*   **Proactive New Customer Acquisition:** Identify and win local businesses for SumUp.
*   **Personal Meetings and Consultation:** Present our products and demonstrate their value and benefits.
*   **Customer Relationship Building:** Secure long-term partnerships through excellent service.
*   **Independent Customer and Lead Management:** Take full ownership of how you build and manage your business.

### Qualifications
*   Passion for sales and strong self-motivation.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer service is an advantage.
*   Excellent communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Very good German language skills.
