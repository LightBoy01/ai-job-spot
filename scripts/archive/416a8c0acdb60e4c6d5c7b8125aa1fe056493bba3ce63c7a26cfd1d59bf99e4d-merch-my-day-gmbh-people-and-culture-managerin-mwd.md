### Description
Merch my Day is more than just merchandise – we create brands. As a young, growing company focused on high-quality brand merchandise for artists, companies, and personalities, we connect creative visions with smart e-commerce.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a **People & Culture Manager (m/w/d)** who will actively shape and develop our employees and company culture. Join Merch my Day GmbH as a People & Culture Manager (m/w/d) and actively make a difference!

**What we offer:**
*   A creative, dynamic environment with flat hierarchies
*   The chance to actively shape the HR structures of a growing company
*   Room for personal responsibility and co-creation of the company culture
*   Access to a motivated team, partner networks, and high-quality resources
*   Fair compensation, flexible working hours, and benefits

### Responsibilities
*   Taking responsibility for the entire HR strategy, from recruiting and onboarding to talent development
*   Building and maintaining a modern, positive company culture that excites and retains employees
*   Implementing HR processes, policies, and systems for a growing company
*   Advising and supporting managers on personnel matters, employee reviews, and conflict resolution
*   Planning and conducting personnel development measures, workshops, and training
*   Analyzing HR key figures and deriving measures to optimize employee satisfaction and performance

### Qualifications
*   Completed studies in Human Resources, Business Administration, or a comparable qualification
*   Several years of experience in the HR sector, ideally with leadership experience
*   Sound knowledge of labor law, recruiting, and personnel development
*   Experience with HR software and digital tools
*   Excellent communication skills and empathy
*   Strategic thinking, a hands-on mentality, and enjoyment in building structures within a growing company
