### Description
HR work needs creativity, heart, and passion! As an HR Generalist, do you share this view? In line with our company's development, we are looking for an HR Generalist to support our newly founded sister company, Gamma Communications Sales Germany GmbH. You will be the first point of contact for all sales employees, responsible for recruiting our open sales vacancies, administration, and supporting the trainees across the entire group.

### Responsibilities
- Organizational support for trainees within the group for vocational training programs: IT Specialist for System Integration & Application Development.
- First point of contact for all HR-related matters for employees and our sales management.
- Maintenance and administration of personnel master data and contract creation.
- Management of digital personnel files and HR documents.
- Organization of offboarding and onboarding processes.
- Full recruiting in collaboration with our sales management.
- Preparation of payroll.
- Participation in HR projects and process improvements within the HR department.

### Qualifications
- Completed commercial training or a degree with an HR focus.
- 3-5 years of experience in the HR field, ideally in recruiting and personnel administration.
- Initial experience with an HR system (ideally Dayforce or comparable).
- Enjoyment of working with trainees and collaborating with the IHK (Chamber of Industry and Commerce).
- High service orientation, strong communication skills, and team player.
- Structured, meticulous, and independent way of working.
- High reliability, trustworthiness, and discretion.
- Willingness to work at least 2-3 days a week from our beautiful office in Karlsruhe.
