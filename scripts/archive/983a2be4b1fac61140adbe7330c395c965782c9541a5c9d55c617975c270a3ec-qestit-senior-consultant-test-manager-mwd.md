Furthermore, we cultivate a family-like, open, and appreciative corporate culture with plenty of room for personal growth.\n\n### Responsibilities\n* Prepare, plan, and manage tests in relevant project phases with various stakeholders.\n* Manage bug fixes and test releases, and set up reports on test progress.\n* Responsible for creating and implementing training sessions for the test management tool and processes.\n* Responsible for defining an overarching test strategy and creating technical test cases.\n* Actively develop sustainable customer relationships with your expertise.\n* Act as a mentor, supporting your colleagues with your experience at all times.\n\n### Qualifications\n* A minimum of 5 years of experience in software testing.\n* Profound knowledge of processes and methods, as well as practical experience in software development and project management (fundamental test process, defect management, Scrum).\n* Characterized by a strong customer and service orientation, excellent communication skills, and a team player mentality.\n* Willingness to travel for business (your trolley is ready).\n* Fluent German language skills (C1) and ISTQB certification are your ticket to our team.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for an experienced Senior Test Manager looking to make a significant impact within a growing international company. The role offers a blend of strategic responsibility, technical execution, and mentorship, all within a supportive and growth-oriented culture. With flexible work options and a strong emphasis on continuous learning, this position is ideal for someone passionate about quality assurance and career development.
