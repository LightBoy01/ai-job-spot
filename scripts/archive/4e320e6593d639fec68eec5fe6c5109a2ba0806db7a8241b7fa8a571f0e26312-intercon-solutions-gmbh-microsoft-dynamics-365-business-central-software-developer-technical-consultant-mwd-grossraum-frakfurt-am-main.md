### Description
Our client is a family-owned, internationally active medium-sized company headquartered in the Koblenz area. For nearly 80 years, the company has been a leading manufacturer of fall protection devices and safety solutions for use in sports and industry. With approximately 950 employees worldwide (including about 500 at the main location) and branches and production sites in six countries, the company combines tradition, innovation, and international growth.

To further expand their ERP team, they are immediately seeking an experienced **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Software Developer / Technical Consultant** for a permanent position. This role is crucial for advancing their global ERP landscape.

### Responsibilities
As a key member of the ERP team, your responsibilities will include:

*   Actively participating in the international **rollout of Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (BC23)**.
*   **Supporting, further developing, and maintaining** the ERP application.
*   Analyzing functional requirements and translating them into robust technical solutions.
*   **Independently developing** in **AL** (and C/AL for the existing NAV environment).
*   Conceiving and implementing **adaptations and extensions** to the system.
*   **Optimizing and automating** critical business processes.
*   Collaborating closely with internal NAV developers and the IT department, especially on topics related to **SQL Server, .NET, and C#**.
*   Providing **2nd-level support**, conducting user training, and creating comprehensive documentation.

### Qualifications
We are looking for a highly motivated individual with:

*   A completed degree in (Business) Informatics, a software development apprenticeship, or equivalent professional experience.
*   Profound knowledge in **Microsoft Dynamics NAV / Business Central** and practical experience with **AL and C/AL development**.
*   Familiarity with **SQL**, **databases**, **object-oriented programming**, and **Microsoft Office**.
*   A strong understanding of commercial processes and their representation within ERP systems.
*   An independent, structured, and solution-oriented approach to work.
*   Excellent teamwork skills, a strong motivation for continuous learning, and effective communication abilities.
*   Very good command of German and good command of English, both written and spoken.

**Answer 1:** This role is at the heart of our global strategy. You're not just writing code; you're building the backbone for our international growth. With BC23, we're standardizing processes across six countries, improving efficiency, and enabling data-driven decisions that directly support our market leadership in safety solutions. The exciting challenge lies in harmonizing diverse business requirements into a cohesive ERP system, ensuring seamless integration, and truly seeing your work empower our teams worldwide. You'll be a vital part of shaping how we operate globally, making a tangible difference to our operational excellence.

You'll find flat hierarchies and open communication, meaning your ideas are heard and valued. We encourage an independent, solution-oriented approach, but you're never alone; you'll work closely with experienced NAV developers and the broader IT department, learning from each other. The company strongly believes in continuous professional development, offering regular training opportunities. Plus, with flexible hours, home office options, and a focus on work-life balance, we ensure you have the space to thrive both professionally and personally within our supportive, family-like environment.
