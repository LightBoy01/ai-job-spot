### Description\nExoMatter is seeking an exceptional Talent Acquisition Manager (f/m/x) to drive ambitious growth within a deep tech and software startup based in Munich. A spin-off of the German Aerospace Center (DLR) and part of the UnternehmerTUM family, ExoMatter already has a product on the market with paying customers (e.g., CMBlu, BSH) and a strong upward trajectory.\n\nThe company's mission is to accelerate sustainable innovation by helping companies discover and design new materials faster through data, simulations, and AI. They are looking for someone who is equally convinced that efficient materials are crucial for tackling the climate crisis, is a \\\"maker\\\" who wants to turn visions into reality, and thrives in a dynamic team that values trust, feedback, and responsibility.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Own and continuously improve the entire hiring process, from defining needs and sourcing to ensuring an exceptional candidate experience and building a scalable recruiting infrastructure.\n- Write job postings and clarify requirements for new hires in close collaboration with hiring managers.\n- Screen CVs for fit with company values and position requirements.\n- Set up, manage, and lead screening interviews as the first interview stage; document results and manage the funnel.\n- Support later stages of the interview process by coordinating interview schedules with hiring managers.\n- Track and analyze hiring metrics (time-to-hire, source efficiency, funnel conversion) to identify improvement opportunities.\n- Collaborate with Marketing to strengthen the employer brand through social media, events, and content highlighting the mission and team.\n- Occasionally carry out active sourcing of talent, for instance, through LinkedIn.\n- Occasionally collaborate with external recruiters.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Hold a degree in Human Resources, Business, Psychology, or a related field, or have gained equivalent practical experience in recruiting.\n- Possess 3+ years of full-cycle recruiting experience, ideally in a startup or scaleup company and with an international team.\n- Have excellent people skills and can easily convey company values and identify candidates matching them.\n- Be a positive and outgoing person who doesn't mind speaking with people all day.\n- Live in the Munich area or be willing to move there (support with finding housing is provided).\n- Be willing to work at least 50% of your time on-site.\n- Be fluent in spoken and written English & German.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is a fantastic opportunity for a motivated Talent Acquisition Manager to make a significant impact within a purpose-driven deep tech startup. ExoMatter offers a unique blend of challenging work accelerating sustainable innovation, a supportive culture emphasizing trust and feedback, and an outstanding commitment to work-life balance that goes \\\"far beyond industry standard.\\\" With direct ownership over the entire hiring process, you'll play a crucial role in building the team that drives the company's ambitious growth. The hybrid work model, relocation support, and competitive package (including virtual stock options) make this role particularly attractive for someone seeking a high-impact position in a fast-paced yet employee-centric environment.
