### Description
Join FlixTrain, where we offer secure jobs, above-average pay, and excellent additional benefits. You'll have the chance to develop in a dynamic environment where your impact truly matters. As a Train Attendant at FlixTrain, you make a significant difference by ensuring our passengers enjoy a safe and pleasant travel experience – from ticket control to active support throughout their journey. Be part of our dedicated team, helping us create a first-class rail travel experience. We're passionate about providing the best service to our guests with a smile, strong organization, and great team spirit.

### Responsibilities
As a Train Attendant, you will be responsible for:
*   Friendly and competent advice for our passengers during the journey.
*   Controlling tickets and assisting with on-board ticket sales.
*   Ensuring order, cleanliness, and safety throughout the train.
*   Serving as the central point of contact for passenger questions and concerns.
*   Actively coordinating on-board processes and supporting passenger information.
*   Proactively shaping a positive travel experience for our guests.
*   Contributing to the continuous improvement of our service processes.

### Qualifications
We are looking for individuals who bring:
*   Ideally, experience in customer service, as a train attendant, or in similar fields.
*   Good German language skills are essential, with basic English skills being an advantage.
*   A service-oriented, communicative personality with a consistently well-groomed appearance.
*   The ability to remain calm and maintain an overview, even in stressful moments.
*   **Flexibility:** Willingness to work individual shifts, including nights or weekends. Your shift schedule will be provided at least 3 weeks in advance for better planning.
*   **Willingness to Stay Overnight:** Occasionally, you will stay overnight in a hotel after your shift – FlixTrain covers travel, food, and lodging.
*   **Willingness to Travel:** You will have the opportunity to travel nationwide for free for both business and private purposes.

We understand that everyone brings unique experiences. Even if you don't meet all requirements, we encourage you to apply. We look forward to hearing from you!

**What we offer:**
*   **Compensation:** A fair monthly fixed salary of €2,800.00, plus market-standard bonuses.
*   **Travel Benefits:** 12 free Flix travel vouchers + 12 discount vouchers for friends & family, along with free nationwide travel for employees.
*   **Flexible Working Models:** A fixed shift schedule for better planning and work-life balance.
*   **Health & Well-being:** Free coaching & therapy sessions via OpenUp.
*   **Learning & Development:** Paid training, internal courses, and mentoring programs.
*   **Employee Discounts:** Urban Sports Club (85% off), Jobrad (company bike), and a Corporate Benefits portal with discounts across various areas of life.
*   **Team Culture:** A diverse, supportive work environment with a strong team spirit and room for your own ideas.
