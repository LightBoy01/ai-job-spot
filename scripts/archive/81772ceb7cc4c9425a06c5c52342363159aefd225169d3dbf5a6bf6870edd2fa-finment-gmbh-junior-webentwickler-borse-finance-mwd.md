### Description\
FinMent is a rapidly growing, state-certified FinTech startup in Berlin, dedicated to democratizing institutional investment knowledge and access. Our mission is to help individuals make their money work more efficiently using innovative technology, validated financial expertise, and exclusive strategies previously available only to institutional investors. As a key member of our product development team, you will play a crucial role as a Fullstack Programmer (Frontend & Backend), contributing to the core of our financial platforms. We offer a dynamic startup environment where you can kickstart and accelerate your career, with the unique benefit of free access to our products, empowering you to generate your own stock market income and achieve financial freedom (a service clients pay up to €36,000 for).\
\
### Responsibilities\
- Be an integral part of the holistic development process for our financial platforms, identifying and developing new features related to stock trading and financial independence.\
- Develop the next generation of investment systems for private investors within a dynamic startup environment.\
- Support and enhance our financial platform across both frontend and backend.\
- Optimize all internal company processes and algorithms.\
- Actively participate in and shape the Scrum process as a member of an agile team, from planning to retrospectives.\
\
### Qualifications\
- High motivation, a strong thirst for knowledge in financial topics, and a desire to develop into a specialist in the financial sector.\
- Aspiration to be an expert in your field, committed to continuous self-development, finding quick solutions to challenging tasks, and delivering strong ideas.\
- Passion for programming and a commitment to clean code principles.\
- Proficiency in fundamental technologies like JavaScript, React.js, PHP, HTML5, and CSS, demonstrating a precise and detail-oriented work ethic.\
- A versatile programmer with a hands-on mentality, eager to continuously learn and tackle new challenges.\
- Willingness and ability to take full responsibility for your area of expertise, fostering rapid personal growth.\
- Disciplined, autonomous, and results-oriented in your approach to work.\
- Ideally, C1 level German; good English for conversations.\
- We understand that not everyone meets every single requirement. If you are truly passionate about working with us, we encourage you to apply!\
