An exciting opportunity for a **Fitter** to join our fun, collaborative, and hardworking Defence Maintenance Contract team based at **Wallangarra, QLD**, **just north of Tenterfield, NSW.** This is an opportunity to join Ventia in a role which provides a corridor for a great lifestyle outside of work! 

**About the role** 

As a Fitter, you will use your highly detailed workmanship to inspect, repair, and recondition military hardware. As a qualified Fitter, it will be your responsibility to provide direct embedded support and teamwork with Defence and Defence training activities to ensure hardware is functional and training objectives are achieved.

*   Perform maintenance, servicing, repairs, and preventative maintenance on military equipment, hardware and Defence armaments
*   Safely operate machinery to repair, manufacture or modify required parts
*   Certify plant and equipment as serviceable and fit for purpose
*   Provide occasional support to various Defence Bases across the state assisting with Armory maintenance. All travel, accommodation and Food paid for!

**About you**  

*   Possess a Trade Certificate III Mechanical Fitting
*   High Risk Forklift Licence- required
*   Hold a valid Australian Driver’s License
*   MR or HR License – Desirable
*   High Risk Dogging & Rigging, Overhead Crane, and Confined Space Entry - highly regarded, or must be willing to obtain
*   Physically fit and enjoy hands-on work
*   You must be an Australian citizen and able to maintain a Defence Security Clearance
