### Description

Our team is at the heart of our success, and joining us means embracing new challenges and dynamic action every day. At RHG, we champion your entrepreneurial spirit and teamwork, fostering an inclusive and supportive culture where we collectively strive to create unforgettable moments daily. Be yourself, bring your unique perspective to the team, and unlock exciting career opportunities within one of the world's most dynamic hotel companies.

We are looking to strengthen our team as soon as possible with a **Technical Lead (m/f/d)**. Do you enjoy finding creative solutions and taking initiative? If you are passionate about guest service and building maintenance, then come join us and say "Yes I Can!".

### Responsibilities

As our Technical Lead, you will be responsible for:
*   Leading and scheduling the technical team, including creating shift, vacation, and duty rosters.
*   Conducting performance reviews with employees in the technical department.
*   Implementing periodic employee training on fire safety procedures.
*   Overseeing and managing the maintenance of all technical systems and facilities.
*   Ensuring the optimal economic efficiency of operational facilities and technical systems.
*   Coordinating defect rectification and and repairs in accordance with inspection regulations.
*   Reviewing and analyzing water and energy consumption data.
*   Implementing energy-saving measures and training employees on energy-conscious resource management.
*   Implementing and adhering to all applicable technical and operational standards.
*   Taking responsibility for maintaining (occupational) safety throughout the entire hotel.
*   Obtaining quotes, calculating, planning, and monitoring maintenance and repair work.
*   Controlling costs, monitoring, and approving internal renovation and conversion projects.

### Qualifications

To succeed in this role, you should possess:
*   Proven experience in property maintenance with excellent problem-solving capabilities.
*   Exceptional management skills, demonstrating a practical, hands-on approach and leading by example.
*   A deep commitment to delivering outstanding guest service, coupled with a genuine passion for the hospitality industry.
*   The ability to identify creative solutions, provide expert advice, and offer valuable recommendations.

For instance, if you identify a more efficient way to manage preventive maintenance or a better training module for your team, you'll be encouraged to develop and execute it. Your voice is valued in cross-departmental meetings, as your insights are crucial for everything from guest comfort to compliance. Support from leadership isn't just a buzzword – it's tangible. We expect you to lead, and we give you the tools and trust to do it effectively.*
