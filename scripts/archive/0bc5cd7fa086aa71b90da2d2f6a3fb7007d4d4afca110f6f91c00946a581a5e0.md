### Description

Ebury is a global fintech firm dedicated to empowering businesses to expand internationally through tailored and forward-thinking financial solutions. Since our founding in 2009, we’ve grown to a diverse team of over 1,700 professionals across more than 40 offices and 29 markets worldwide. Joining Ebury means becoming part of a collaborative and innovative environment where your contributions are valued and you play a key role in shaping the future of cross-border finance.

As a Product Manager for Money Flows, you will join a high-performing, product-led organization where your expertise will drive strategic initiatives for our core payment systems. This hybrid role (4 days in-office, 1 day remote) is central to our mission. You will be responsible for implementing new payment schemes and optimizing our Money Flows infrastructure, ensuring the performance and continuous evolution of our accounts, payments, and collection products.

### Responsibilities

*   Lead the end-to-end implementation of new payment schemes or connectivity to banks/partners, translating complex rules and API specifications into clear requirements and designing comprehensive payment flows.
*   Collaborate on and contribute to the product strategy for the Money Flows Platform, focusing on areas like payment journeys, exotics, operational process improvements, and automation.
*   Assess complex problems and needs using critical thinking and data analysis, identifying opportunities for predictive models, including LLM and AI.
*   Design innovative solutions and processes from scratch to address identified challenges, managing their end-to-end delivery and ensuring continuous improvements to existing features.
*   Support Technology teams throughout the development process, identifying and resolving issues proactively before releases.
*   Define, track, and monitor KPIs for all product initiatives, making data-driven decisions and communicating performance to relevant stakeholders.
*   Manage the payment backlog for one or multiple Tech teams and actively participate in all agile ceremonies.
*   Develop a deep understanding of internal and external stakeholder needs to deliver successful product outcomes.
*   Support product commercialization efforts, including proposition development, business case formulation, stakeholder training, and marketing collateral creation.
*   Stay informed of market, industry, and regulatory trends in the payment and FinTech space to guide Ebury's product decisions.

### Qualifications

**Required:**

*   A minimum of 3 years of experience in product strategy and execution within top-tier environments (corporate or startups), or a high-growth FinTech/neobank.
*   Proven experience implementing payment schemes in the APAC region (e.g., BECS, NPP, CHIPS, etc.) and a solid understanding of the SWIFT network.
*   Experience as a Technical Product Owner within the Payment industry, capable of translating rulebooks or API specifications into actionable requirements.
*   A track record of successfully delivering product solutions in a fast-paced, agile organization.
*   Exceptional organizational, project management, and analytical skills, with the ability to distill complex problems into manageable parts.
*   Strong communication (verbal and written) and interpersonal skills, with the ability to influence stakeholders without direct authority.
*   Proactive with a history of grit, determination, and resourcefulness, coupled with a strong desire for professional development.
*   Domain knowledge or a keen interest in Payments or FX.
*   Business fluent in written and spoken English.

**Nice to Have:**

*   Hands-on experience using Jira.
*   Knowledge of all agile ceremonies.
*   Experience working in a high-growth or scale-up environment.
*   Experience with LLM and AI models.

### Author's Take

**1. Beyond implementing payment schemes, what is the tangible impact of optimizing the Money Flows platform for Ebury's clients?**

That’s a great question because it gets to the heart of why this role is so critical. On the surface, we're building financial plumbing—connecting payment rails and optimizing routes. But the real impact is far more human. Think of a small e-commerce business in Spain that wants to source goods from Australia. Before, the complexity and cost of that transaction could have been a major barrier. The work we do on the Money Flows team directly lowers that barrier. When you successfully implement a new, faster, or cheaper payment scheme like NPP in Australia, you’re not just checking a box on a roadmap. You’re giving that Spanish entrepreneur a real competitive advantage and the confidence to go global. Your work here directly translates into our clients' success stories, which is an incredibly rewarding feedback loop.

**2. The description mentions a 'product-led organization.' How does that philosophy actually play out in the day-to-day, especially when balancing new strategic features with technical debt or operational fires?**

Being 'product-led' here is less of a buzzword and more of a cultural reality. It means that as a Product Manager, you are empowered and trusted to be the expert in your domain. Your day-to-day isn't about taking orders from the top; it's about building a compelling, data-backed vision for your product area and bringing your stakeholders along on that journey. When it comes to balancing priorities, that's where your influence and analytical skills come in. You'll work directly with engineering leads, data analysts, and commercial teams to make those tough calls. We don't shy away from difficult conversations. If you can build a strong business case—whether it’s for a new AI-driven routing model or for dedicating a sprint to refactoring a critical service—you will get the buy-in. It's a high-autonomy, high-accountability environment where your ability to articulate the 'why' is just as important as defining the 'what'.
