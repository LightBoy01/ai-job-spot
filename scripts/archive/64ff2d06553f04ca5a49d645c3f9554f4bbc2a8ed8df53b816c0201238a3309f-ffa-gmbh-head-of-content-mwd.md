### Description\nFFA GmbH is a rapidly growing company representing brands such as \"Quantum Leap Fitness\" and \"Sjard Fitness.\" Since its founding in 2015, we have been transforming the lives of our customers with innovative products that enhance health and quality of life, now serving one of the largest German-speaking fitness communities. Our product portfolio includes self-developed dietary supplements sold in our online shop, as well as digital and physical information products.\n\nAs **Head of Content (m/w/d)**, you will be the creative force behind our entire content ecosystem. You will lead a team of 14 content specialists (Creative Manager, Social Media, Video, Graphics, SEO) and be responsible for the strategic direction and operational excellence across all content channels. In close collaboration with our Head of Performance Marketing, you will ensure that our marketing budget achieves maximum impact through outstanding, conversion-optimized content. Health Claims).\n*   Develop relevant content KPIs, establish performance reporting, and continuously optimize all content initiatives.\n*   Ensure efficient, cross-channel utilization of all assets and maximize content efficiency.\n\n**Operational Execution**\n*   Manage the entire Ad Creative Production (Static, Video, UGC) for Facebook, Instagram, and Google.\n*   Build and manage a scalable content pipeline for continuous A/B and performance testing.\n*   Plan, coordinate, and monitor social media content productions.\n*   Ensure quality assurance for all content assets across all touchpoints and brand standards.\n\n**Team Leadership**\n*   Provide disciplinary and technical leadership to an interdisciplinary team of 14 employees in five content areas (Creative, Social Media, Video, Graphics, SEO).\n*   Foster efficient cross-functional collaboration and a dynamic, creative, yet performance-oriented team culture.\n*   Responsibly manage the content budget and oversee external partners and service providers.\n\n### Qualifications\n\n**Must-haves**\n*   Minimum of 5 years of professional experience in content marketing, ideally in a D2C/e-commerce or performance Ability to combine creative excellence with data-driven performance optimization.\n*   Excellent understanding of both Brand Building and Performance Marketing – you master both disciplines
