### Description\nWe are seeking a highly skilled Senior Embedded Software Engineer to develop highly reliable industrial and IoT solutions on modern ARM platforms. This role offers a unique opportunity to contribute to highly innovative projects, directly integrate your ideas into serial products, and actively shape technologies for Industry 4.0. You will be a key player in a specialized development team, working in a modern office with flexible hours and a strong emphasis on work-life balance.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Develop firmware for ARM-based platforms (e.g., Nordic nRF, STM32) from conception to series production.\n- Integrate complex communication stacks (DECT NR+, LTE-M/NB-IoT, CAN, Ethernet, PROFINET) for industrial applications.\n- Perform driver and hardware integration in close collaboration with the hardware team.\n- Develop on RTOS (e.g., Zephyr, FreeRTOS) or bare-metal, as well as embedded Linux services for gateways.\n- Ensure high code quality through rigorous reviews, CI/CD pipelines, and automated tests.\n- Establish secure OTA update mechanisms for products deployed in the field.\n- Contribute to architectural decisions and mentor junior developers.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Minimum of 6–7 years of experience in embedded development with C (C++ is a plus).\n- Broad project background, ideally across multiple embedded environments.\n- Proficiency with Git, CMake/Make, Continuous Integration, and Code Reviews.\n- Experience with RTOS or bare-metal, combined with strong Linux fundamentals.\n- Solid knowledge of communication protocols (CAN/CAN FD, Ethernet, PROFINET) and bus/frame analysis.\n- Structured, reliable working style, and excellent German and English language skills.\n\n**Nice-to-haves:**\n- Experience with Embedded Linux Userland for Gateways.\n- Scripting skills (Python, Bash) for tooling or tests.\n- Practical experience in wireless IoT/Industrial technologies (DECT NR+, LTE-M/NB-IoT).\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for a Senior Embedded Software Engineer looking to make a significant impact on industrial and IoT innovation. The company truly values its employees, demonstrated by a 38.5-hour work week, flexible working hours with no core times, and home office options. You'll work with cutting-edge technology, have a budget for the latest hardware and software, and benefit from a clear career path that can lead to product category leadership or strategic technology roles. The supportive, modern office environment and regular team events highlight a culture that balances high performance with employee well-being and collaborative growth. If you're passionate about shaping the future of industrial technology and enjoy a vibrant team culture, this role is a perfect fit.
