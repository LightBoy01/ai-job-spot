### Description

Are you ready to kickstart your career in sales with unparalleled autonomy and unlimited earning potential? SumUp is looking for driven individuals to join their team as **freelance Field Sales Consultants**. This is an opportunity for those who thrive independently, are passionate about shaping their own success, and want to manage their time freely.

SumUp empowers small businesses with smart, user-friendly financial solutions, ranging from card payments to comprehensive POS systems. Their mission is to strengthen entrepreneurs, allowing them to focus entirely on their core business. As a Field Sales Consultant, you'll be your own boss, building relationships with local businesses and demonstrating how SumUp's innovative solutions can simplify their operations and drive growth. You'll have access to top-tier products with high market potential, ensuring you have everything you need to succeed.

**What You Can Expect:**

*   **100% performance-based compensation** – your income is directly tied to your efforts, with no upper limit.
*   **Maximum flexibility** – work when and where you want, dictating your own schedule.
*   **Top products with high market potential** – compelling solutions designed for small businesses.
*   **Comprehensive training & sales support** – you won't be left alone; SumUp provides the resources to help you thrive.

### Responsibilities

As a freelance Field Sales Consultant, your core responsibilities will include:

*   **Active New Customer Acquisition**: Identify and acquire new local businesses for SumUp's services.
*   **Personal Meetings & Consulting**: Conduct in-person meetings to present products and demonstrate their tangible benefits.
*   **Building Customer Relationships**: Foster long-term partnerships through excellent service and consistent engagement.
*   **Own Customer & Lead Management**: Independently manage your client portfolio and lead pipeline, taking full ownership of your business growth.

### Qualifications

To excel in this autonomous and rewarding role, we're looking for individuals with:

*   A strong passion for sales and inherent self-motivation.
*   Initial experience in sales or customer support is advantageous but not strictly required.
*   Excellent communication skills and a genuine enjoyment of direct customer interaction.
*   Very good German language skills are essential for engaging with the local market.
