### Description\
Join us in shaping the future of connection technology! BC Deutschland GmbH, a leading specialist in connection technology and part of the renowned Cooper Turner Beck Group, is looking for reinforcements for its team. Based in Hamm and Ennepetal, we are active throughout the German-speaking world and supply customers worldwide with high-quality special screws for all industrial sectors.\
\
We offer you the unique opportunity to contribute your skills in a dynamic environment that combines the advantages of a medium-sized company with the high quality standards of a globally active corporation. Benefit from our many years of experience, flexibility, and strong service orientation. If you are passionate about innovative solutions in connection technology and want to become part of a successful team, then you've come to the right place!\
\
### Responsibilities\
**Network Administration:**\
*   Installation, configuration, and maintenance of network hardware (routers, switches, firewalls)\
*   Troubleshooting and optimization of network connections and performance\
*   Knowledge and practical experience with network protocols (TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, VPN)\
*   In-depth knowledge of Watchguard firewalls, Aruba switches, and HP switches (desirable)\
*   Telecommunications technology (telephone system)\
\
**Virtualization:**\
*   Planning, implementation, and management of virtual machines and environments\
\
**Cloud Technologies:**\
*   Knowledge and experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud)\
*   Particularly in-depth experience with Microsoft Azure (desirable)\
*   Migration of on-premise solutions to the cloud (if applicable)\
*   Management of cloud resources and costs\
\
**Microsoft 365:**\
*   Extensive experience in managing Microsoft 365 Tenants\
*   Configuration and maintenance of user accounts, licenses, and security policies\
*   Support for end-users with questions and problems related to Microsoft 365\
\
### Qualifications\
*   Completed vocational training or degree in computer science or a comparable qualification\
*   Several years of professional experience in IT administration of networks and systems\
*   Profound knowledge of Windows and Linux operating systems as well as virtualization technologies\
*   Experience with IT security solutions and backup strategies\
*   Good German and English language skills\
*   Strong problem-solving skills and a structured way of working\
\
