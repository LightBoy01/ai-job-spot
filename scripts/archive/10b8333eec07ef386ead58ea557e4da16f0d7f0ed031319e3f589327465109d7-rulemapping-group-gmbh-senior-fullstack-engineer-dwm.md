### Description
Rulemapping Solutions, based in Berlin, stands for innovation at the intersection of law and technology. Our mission is to simplify, accelerate, and make complex bureaucratic processes transparent for everyone. With our unique Rulemapping method and Rule AI, we develop solutions that relieve companies of bureaucratic burden, facilitate access to justice for people, and rethink administration.

As a Senior Fullstack Engineer (m/f/d), you will work closely with other developers, our Product Owner, and the VP Product and Technology in the Core Team. You will actively shape the technological advancement of our SaaS platform, bringing modern web technologies, high code quality, and technical understanding into harmony. Rulemapping Solutions is a subsidiary of the Rulemapping Group.

### Responsibilities
* Develop modern, performant, and maintainable frontend components with React, Panda CSS, and TanStack Router.
* Implement and maintain scalable REST backends in NestJS – including clean architecture principles and clear API designs.
* Work with PostgreSQL, taking responsibility for database design, query optimization, and migrations.
* Integrate Keycloak for authentication and securely connect the Rulemapping Automation Backend.
* Collaborate closely with UX/UI (Figma) and Product Management to develop user-centric and high-quality features.
* Utilize modern AI tools (especially Claude Code) for code generation, refactoring, testing, and accelerating the development process.
* Ensure high code quality – through linters, formatters (ESLint, Prettier), testing (Vitest, Jest, Playwright), and structured code reviews.
* Contribute to building and maintaining our CI/CD pipelines (e.g., GitHub Actions, Cloud Deployment).
* Actively participate in code reviews, architectural decisions, and knowledge sharing within the Engineering team.
* Continuously educate yourself and evaluate new technologies to keep our platform technically modern and long-term scalable.

### Qualifications
* Several years of experience in fullstack development of modern web applications, ideally with a degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics, Software Engineering, or equivalent practical expertise – self-taught developers with demonstrable experience are expressly welcome.
* Deep understanding of TypeScript, React, and PostgreSQL. Knowledge of Python is a plus.
* Proficient in State Management, APIs, Authentication (Keycloak), and Frontend Performance Optimization.
* Fundamental understanding of software architectures, data flows, and API designs.
* Experience with Vite, testing frameworks (Vitest, Playwright), and common software development tools (ESLint, Prettier).
* Basic understanding of CI/CD pipelines and cloud environments.
* Work independently, think pragmatically, write clean code, and enjoy collaborative team development.
* Strong willingness to learn and passion for staying up-to-date with new technologies and trends.
