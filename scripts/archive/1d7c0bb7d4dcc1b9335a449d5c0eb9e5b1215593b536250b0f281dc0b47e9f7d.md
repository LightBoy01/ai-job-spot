**Job Description:**

We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced Product Manager, Data Products, to join our dynamic team to build business-enabling products that are powered by data. As a Product Manager, Data Products, you will be responsible for driving the development, delivery, management, and success of data-driven products across the enterprise. You will work closely with cross-functional teams, including data scientists, engineers, designers, and business stakeholders, to define product strategies, prioritize features, and deliver impactful data solutions. This role requires a deep understanding of data analytics, data management, agile product management principles, AI technology and a passion for driving data-centric innovation.

**Responsibilities:**

*   Product Development: Lead cross-functional agile teams to design, develop, and deliver data products, ensuring high quality, scalability, and user satisfaction.
*   Product Strategy: Collaborate with stakeholders and the Data Strategy team to define and execute a data product strategy aligned with business goals and market needs.
*   Product Roadmap: Develop and maintain a comprehensive product roadmap, ensuring alignment with overall business objectives and incorporating feedback from various stakeholders.
*   Requirements Gathering: Work closely with business users, data scientists, and engineers to understand data-related requirements and translate them into actionable product features.
*   Data Analysis: Conduct data analysis to identify insights, trends, and opportunities that can be leveraged to enhance the product offering.
*   Data Management: Translate the business requirement into logical data modeling using a deep understanding of data management principles to streamline data quality and process efficiency
*   Stakeholder Management: Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to gather feedback, manage expectations, and communicate product updates and enhancements.
*   Product Performance Tracking: Define and monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) to assess the success of data products and drive continuous improvement efforts.
*   Market Research: Stay up to date with industry trends, emerging technologies, and competitive landscape to identify potential product enhancements or new opportunities.
*   Data Governance: Partner with the data governance team to ensure compliance with data privacy regulations and maintain data integrity, security, and confidentiality.
*   Leadership: Mentor individual contributors, foster a collaborative work environment, and promote a culture of data-driven decision-making.

**Qualifications:**

*   Bachelor's degree in a relevant field (Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, or equivalent). A Master's degree is preferred.
*   Proven experience (7 years) in product management, preferably in data-focused roles or industries.
*   Strong understanding of data analytics, data engineering, and data visualization concepts.
*   Solid experience with agile development methodologies and product management frameworks.
*   Proficiency in data storage, manipulation, analysis, and visualization tools such as SQL, Snowflake, Python, Tableau or similar.
*   Excellent communication and interpersonal skills to effectively collaborate with diverse teams and stakeholders.
*   Strong problem-solving and analytical thinking abilities with a keen attention to detail.
*   Demonstrated ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver projects within defined timelines.
*   Experience with data governance, data privacy regulations, and best practices is a plus.
*   Familiarity with AI technologies and LLM frameworks.
