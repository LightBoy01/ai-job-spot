### Description\nDo you want to understand how a company truly works – from strategy to customer projects? Then you've come to the right place.\nAs a Founders Associate (f/m/x), you will work directly with our founding team, gaining deep insights into corporate strategy and hands-on experience with our customers. You will take responsibility for your own projects, support the management of marketing and growth initiatives, and lay a robust foundation for your further development, for example, in project leadership for growth projects. This role offers a steep learning curve and significant room to shape initiatives from day one.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Collaborate directly with the founding team to shape strategic initiatives, internal projects, and customer topics.\n- Engage in client project work, supporting the planning, control, and implementation of marketing and growth projects.\n- Conduct 'light' Account Management, assisting in nurturing client relationships, preparing reports, and coordinating measures.\n- Contribute to Strategy & Organization by developing internal processes, building structures, and enhancing efficiency.\n- Support Finance & Operations by creating business plans, financial models, and reports, and optimizing financial processes.\n- Participate in Business Development, helping to develop new service offerings and explore new markets.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Completed degree (Bachelor/Master) in Business Administration, Economics, Communications, Business Informatics, or a comparable field.\n- Initial professional experience (0-2 years) in a dynamic environment (e.g., consulting, marketing, startup, VC).\n- Structured and independent working style with a high sense of responsibility.\n- Strong communication skills and enthusiasm for direct client interaction.\n- Analytically strong, detail-oriented, and simultaneously pragmatic.\n- Hands-on mentality and a desire to take on operational responsibility.\n- Fluent in German and English.\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an ambitious individual with 0-2 years of experience who is eager to dive deep into the inner workings of a company. Working directly with the founders provides unparalleled exposure to strategy, operations, and client management across various functions. The role promises a steep learning curve, significant autonomy, and a clear path for growth into leadership positions. If you thrive in a dynamic, fast-paced startup environment and want to make a tangible impact from day one, this Founders Associate role in Berlin is a perfect launchpad for your career.
