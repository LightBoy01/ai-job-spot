### Description\nWe are looking for a Chief Revenue Officer (all genders) for our portfolio company reev.\n\n**About reev:** Our platform connects intelligent charging infrastructure with green energy, aiming to make 100% renewable electricity available at every semi-public parking space. As the market leader in Germany with over 7,000 B2B customers, reev is poised for significant growth in the e-mobility sector. We seek driven, hands-on individuals who want to actively shape the future of mobility.\n\nAs our CRO, you will own the entire revenue journey – from new business and partners to existing customers. You will lead our Sales & Customer Growth teams across DACH, France, and Benelux, setting clear goals and building scalable structures for long-term success. Your ultimate goal is to make reev the market leader for Charging & Energy, actively shaping the commercial vision and contributing to company-wide strategy and board discussions.\n\n### Responsibilities\n- Hold full responsibility for revenue, growth, and customer success across all markets, building an international high-performance team with ambition, focus, and data-driven execution.\n- Further develop our channel strategy with electrical wholesalers, strengthen strategic partnerships with hardware manufacturers, and open new sales channels across Europe.\n- Establish structures for key accounts, win strategic enterprise customers, and ensure reev is the preferred partner in every tender and project.\n- Ensure existing customers continuously gain more value from reev through new features, licenses, and services, leveraging clear up- and cross-sell strategies.\n- Align Sales, Customer Success, Marketing, and Finance in one shared rhythm, with clear goals, KPIs, and ownership across all teams.\n\n### Qualifications\n- Proven track record of scaling SaaS revenue from 5M€ to 50M€ ARR in comparable growth environments.\n- Experience driving growth through partner networks, direct sales, and customer expansion.\n- Strategic thinker with a data-driven mindset and operational depth.\n- Deep understanding of complex B2B ecosystems, ideally in SaaS, energy, or infrastructure.\n- Strong communication skills, able to engage confidently with customers, partners, and investors.\n- Mastery of commercial KPIs – pipeline, NRR, conversion, and predictive metrics.\n- Inspirational leader who builds clarity, ambition, and ownership across teams.\n- Fluent in English; German or French are a strong plus.\n- *Note: The company encourages applications even if not every single requirement is met and some tasks might be new challenges.*\n\n### Author's Take\nThis is an exceptional opportunity for an Executive-level revenue leader to make a significant impact at reev, a market leader in Germany's rapidly growing e-mobility sector. The role offers full ownership of the revenue journey and the chance to shape global commercial strategy. reev fosters a high-performance, data-driven culture that values ownership, collaboration, and continuous learning, supported by a €1,000 annual learning budget and mental well-being benefits. The company is deeply committed to diversity and individual growth, encouraging applicants from all backgrounds. If you're an ambitious, hands-on leader passionate about green energy and scaling a successful SaaS platform, this role presents a compelling blend of strategic influence, operational execution, and a supportive, forward-thinking environment.
