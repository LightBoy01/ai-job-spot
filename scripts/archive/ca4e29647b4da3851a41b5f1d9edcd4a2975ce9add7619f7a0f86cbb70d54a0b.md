**Location:** Hybrid (Stoughton, MA)

**Position Overview:**

Shields Health Solutions is seeking a **strategic, results-oriented Business Analyst** to join our fast-paced Emerging Technology team. As a BA on the Emerging Tech team, you’ll be at the heart of innovation – translating ideas into impactful solutions. You’ll collaborate closely with business stakeholders to define requirements, lead user acceptance testing, analyze issues, and guide successful rollouts. Acting as a strategic bridge between business and technology, you’ll help deliver meaningful outcomes and contribute to the success and growth of the Emerging Technology project portfolio – _including the development of our AI Center of Excellence._

The ideal candidate is **a clear communicator, a tactical thinker, and a data-savvy problem solver.** You’ll bring an entrepreneurial mindset, strong analytical skills, and a passion for people and complex data to deliver insights that keep stakeholders aligned in a rapidly evolving tech landscape.

**Key Responsibilities:**

*   Define and present functional and technical requirements to both business and IT stakeholders.
*   Generate complex user stories and develop clear acceptance criteria to support UAT.
*   Investigate and resolve issues through detailed analysis and collaboration.
*   Deliver weekly updates to stakeholders via streamlined sprint metrics and KPI reporting.
*   Identify and implement improvements in reporting, processes, and infrastructure to enhance team efficiency.
*   Uncover dependencies and blockers, providing actionable recommendations to mitigate risk.
*   Maintain comprehensive documentation – including workflows, data governance and implementation guides – in collaboration with IT and business teams.
*   Meticulously prepare and maintain all project artifacts such as functional requirements, action items, decision points, test cases, project timelines, gap analysis, meeting notes and agendas.
*   Facilitate project status meetings with stakeholders and cross-functional teams to ensure alignment and progress.
*   Proactively suggest enhancements to existing reports, metrics, and analytics to better measure business performance.
*   Support automation efforts for KPI reporting and UAT processes to drive scalability and consistency.

**Qualifications & Experience:**

*   Minimum 3 years of experience as a business analyst – exposure to AI technologies and data-driven projects a strong plus.
*   Proven ability to communicate complex technical concepts to non-technical and executive audiences.
*   Driven by a passion for emerging technologies such as AI, machine learning, and automation, with a proactive mindset towards continuous learning and hands-on experimentation.
*   Comfortable leading workshops, discovery sessions, and UAT coordination.
*   Must be a highly motivated, results-driven professional with an entrepreneurial attitude who can thrive in a team-oriented, fast-paced environment.
*   Ability to translate complex technical concepts into business-friendly language.
*   Strong organizational skills to manage multiple priorities and deliverables.
*   Proficient Excel and SQL skills preferred.
*   Familiarity with data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau), and/or UI design tools (e.g., Figma) a plus.

**Education & Certifications:**

*   Bachelor’s degree in STEM, Finance, Business Administration, Computer Science, or a related field.
*   Additional certifications are preferred.
*   Additional coursework or certifications in AI, machine learning, or data science is a strong plus.
