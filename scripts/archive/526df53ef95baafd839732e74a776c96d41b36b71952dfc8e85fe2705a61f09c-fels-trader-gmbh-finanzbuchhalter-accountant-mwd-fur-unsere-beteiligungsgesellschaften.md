### Description
Are you an independent, numbers-savvy individual eager to take on significant responsibility? We are seeking a reliable and structured personality to independently manage the accounting for several companies, including smaller participations, within a dynamic environment. This role offers substantial autonomy and the chance to shape financial processes within a modern, growth-oriented company with exciting investments. We champion flexible working hours, remote options, and short decision paths, fostering a culture where your contributions directly impact our collective success.

### Responsibilities
*   **Financial Accounting**: Independently manage the accounting for several companies, including accounts receivable, accounts payable, and general ledger postings.
*   **Tax Tasks**: Prepare VAT pre-registrations, support with tax declarations, and communicate with tax advisors and financial authorities.
*   **Payment Transactions & Dunning**: Post bank statements, monitor open items, and manage dunning processes.
*   **Closures & Reporting**: Support with monthly and annual financial statements, and create analyses and reports.
*   **Process Optimization**: Participate in the further development of efficient processes in accounting and financial reporting.

### Qualifications
*   Completed business administration degree or comparable education in finance or accounting.
*   Several years of experience in accounting, ideally with knowledge of HGB / RechKredV (German Commercial Code / Accounting Ordinance for Credit Institutions) and understanding of processes in a banking or financial services environment.
*   Independent, structured, and diligent working style – you work autonomously and reliably.
*   Interest in tax topics (VAT, capital gains tax, etc.).
*   Proficient in MS Office (especially Excel).
*   Experience with DATEV or comparable accounting software is an advantage.
*   Very good German and English language skills (at least C1 level).
