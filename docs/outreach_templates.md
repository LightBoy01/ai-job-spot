# Outreach Templates: The Decentralized Referral Engine

Use these templates to pitch the AI Job Spot widget to high-value partners. Remember the golden rule: **Give Value First.** You are not asking for a link; you are providing a free tool that improves their website.

---

## 1. The "University Career Center" Approach
**Target:** Career Services Directors, Department Admins, Student Success Coordinators.
**Goal:** Get the widget embedded on a "Student Resources" or "Job Board" page.

**Subject:** Resource for [University Name] CS students (Auto-updating AI Job List)

I was looking at the [Department/Resource Page Name] today and noticed you have a section for career resources.

I run a live data feed of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning jobs, and I’ve built a free tool specifically for university resource pages. It’s a simple "widget" that displays the 5 most recent, verified AI entry-level/internship roles.

**Why it’s useful for [University Name] students:**
*   **Always Fresh:** It updates automatically every hour. No manual maintenance required for your team.
*   **High Quality:** We verify every listing (no spam, no dead links).
*   **Free:** It’s a community tool, completely free for educational institutions.

You can see a preview of how it looks here: https://aijobspot.online/share

