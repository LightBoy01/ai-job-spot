## 1. The Premise: The $5 Trillion Friction Problem

The global economy has a multi-trillion-dollar problem: **The Friction of Trust.**

Hiring is the bedrock of this problem.
- **Resumes** are unverified, self-reported claims (cheap to fake).
- **Interviews** are subjective, theatrical performances (low fidelity).
- **Credentials** are lagging indicators (university degrees don't prove current coding skill).

The result is a "Lemon Market" where high-quality talent cannot reliably signal their worth, and employers waste billions filtering noise.

**The trillion-dollar opportunity is to create a high-fidelity signal of competence that is costly to fake but effortless to generate.**

---

## 2. The Evolution of the Solution

Our thinking has evolved through rigorous "Red Teaming" of previous iterations:

*   **V1 (The Trust Score):** A "Credit Score for Humans."
    *   *Failure Mode:* Dystopian, biased, and susceptible to Goodhart's Law (people gaming the metric).
*   **V2 (The Notary Service):** Connecting to private data (Jira, Internal GitHub) to verify claims.
    *   *Failure Mode:* **The Privacy Wall.** Companies will never allow a third-party API to scan their internal, proprietary data to help an employee leave.
*   **V3 (The Protocol of Contribution):** A cryptographic attestation of **public** and **open** impact.
    *   *Limitation:* Only captures Open Source (1% of work).
*   **V4 (The Silver Medalist):** The "Trojan Horse" extraction of private interview data.
    *   *Mechanism:* We capture the verified performance data of the "Runner Up" candidates by automating the rejection workflow for recruiters.
    *   *Status:* **The Winning Strategy.**

## 3. The Solution: The "Silver Medalist" Bridge to the Protocol

We are not just building a better LinkedIn. We are building a **Standard for Contribution Attribution.**

### The Core Mechanism: The "Trojan Horse"

We cannot ask companies to "verify" candidates (V2). We must give them a tool they *need* that happens to verify candidates as a side effect.

1.  **The Hook:** A "Rejection Automator" extension. It turns messy interview notes into polite, safe emails in 1 click. Recruiters install it to save time.
2.  **The Extraction:** As the AI processes the notes, it extracts structured data: *"{CandidateHash} passed Python Screen (Level: High) at {CompanyHash}."*
3.  **The Asset:** We build the "Shadow Graph" of verified talent that was "good enough to interview but not hired."
4.  **The Protocol:** Eventually, this data feeds into the `git-attest` ledger, combining Public Code (OSS) with Private Performance (Interviews).

### Why this Works (The "Unfakeable" Signal)
*   **Costly to Fake:** You cannot fake passing a technical screen at a real company.
*   **Effortless to Generate:** The recruiter *already* wrote the notes. The extraction is passive.
*   **Trustless:** We don't need the company's permission to "partner." We just need one recruiter to use the tool.

## 4. The Roadmap to Trillions (The Spearhead Strategy)

We cannot index the private world (yet). We must win the **Open Source** world first, and then use the **Silver Medalist** strategy to bridge the gap.

### Phase 1: The Open Source Resume (Years 1-2)
*   **Target:** The top 1% of developers who contribute to OSS.
*   **Product:** A "Passive Portfolio." You connect your GitHub. We generate a verified report.
*   **Value:** Elite developers get a "Gold Card" that lets them bypass technical screens.

### Phase 2: The "Silver Medalist" Extraction (Years 2-4)
*   **Problem:** Most work is private.
*   **Solution:** **"The Rejection Automator."**
*   **Mechanism:** We distribute the free tool to recruiters. It silently aggregates anonymized performance data from rejected candidates.
*   **Incentive:** Recruiters save 5 hours/week. We get the data.
*   **Monetization:** We re-market the "Silver Medalists" (verified, high-quality runners-up) to other companies.

### Phase 3: The Global Protocol (Years 5+)
*   **Expansion:** We extend the protocol beyond code.
    *   *Design:* Cryptographic signing of Figma files.
    *   *Legal:* Cryptographic signing of Contracts.
*   **End State:** A decentralized, verified graph of *who did what*.

We are moving from **"Verification of Claims"** (V2) to **"Attestation of Artifacts"** (V4).

We stop asking people to *tell* us what they did.
We build the index that *shows* what they built.
