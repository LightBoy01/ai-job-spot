Here is a specific, "insane" traffic growth strategy tailored for a small job board in the emerging AI space.
It avoids the common advice (e.g., "post on LinkedIn," "start a newsletter," "SEO for 'AI jobs'") and focuses on a high-leverage Programmatic SEO play that exploits the specific nature of the current AI boom.
The Strategy: The "Micro-Tool" Programmatic Dominance
The Core Insight:
The "AI" job market is too broad and competitive. However, the "AI Tooling" market is exploding. Thousands of new AI tools (LangChain, Midjourney, Pinecone, Jasper, Hugging Face, LlamaIndex, Stable Diffusion, etc.) are launching or gaining traction.
 * Big job boards (Indeed, LinkedIn) optimize for "Python Developer" or "Machine Learning Engineer."
 * They DO NOT optimize for "LangChain Developer," "Pinecone Vector Database Expert," or "Midjourney Prompter."
The Trick:
Don't build a job board for "AI Jobs." Build a programmatic engine that automatically generates a landing page for every single specific AI tool that hits the market, capturing the "long-tail" traffic before anyone else does.
Step-by-Step Execution:
1. The Data Source (The "Scrape")
Monitor directories like There’s an AI for That, Product Hunt, or GitHub Trending to get a daily list of growing AI tools. You want the specific software names, not general skills.
2. The Programmatic Page Generation
Use a no-code tool (like Webflow, Bubble, or WordPress with WP All Import) to automatically generate thousands of landing pages with this structure:
 * URL: yourdomain.com/hire-expert-[tool-name]-developers
 * Title: "Hire [Tool Name] Experts & Freelancers" or "Remote [Tool Name] Jobs"
 * H1: "The #1 Job Board for [Tool Name] Specialists"
3. The "Fake It 'Til You Make It" Content (The "Hook")
Since you won't have jobs for "Obscure AI Tool #45" yet, you need to solve the "Empty Board" problem while still getting the SEO traffic:
 * Dynamic Content: Use OpenAI's API to generate a unique 300-word description for each page: "What is [Tool Name]?", "Why hire a [Tool Name] expert?", and "Average Salary for [Tool Name] Developers." Google loves this definition-style content for new terms.
 * The Backfill: On these empty pages, programmatically display broader "AI Engineer" or "Python" jobs from a job feed aggregator (like ZipRecruiter or Jobs2Careers backfill feeds) so the page isn't empty.
 * The Call to Action (CTA): "Are you an expert in [Tool Name]? Join our Talent Collective." (This builds your candidate database before you have employers).
4. The Result
When a founder or CTO Googles "Hire LangChain developer" or "AutoGPT expert," your specific, highly relevant page will rank #1 because Indeed and LinkedIn don't have a page for that specific tool yet. You capture the high-intent traffic for free.
3 "Insane" Emerging AI Niches to Target
To make the strategy above work, focus your "Tool" pages around these three specific, high-growth/low-competition buckets:
1. "Agentic" AI Workflow Architects
 * The Niche: Companies don't just want "chatbots" anymore; they want autonomous agents that do things.
 * Keywords/Tools to Target: LangChain, AutoGPT, BabyAGI, Zapier AI Actions, Semantic Kernel.
 * Why: This is the plumbing of the future AI web. Demand is skyrocketing, supply is low.
2. The "RAG" Stack (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
 * The Niche: Connecting LLMs to private company data (building "ChatGPT for our internal docs").
 * Keywords/Tools to Target: Pinecone, Weaviate, ChromaDB, LlamaIndex, Unstructured.io.
 * Why: Every enterprise is currently trying to hire for this specific stack to avoid data privacy issues.
3. AI Compliance & "Red Teaming"
 * The Niche: With the EU AI Act and US regulations looming, companies need people to break/test models for bias and security.
 * Keywords/Tools to Target: Credo AI, Arthur AI, Fiddler AI, TruEra.
 * Why: This is "Compliance" but for tech—it's unsexy to devs but critical for corporations, meaning high willingness to pay for job posts.
One "Side-Door" Booster: The "Reverse" Salary Database
If the programmatic SEO is the Traffic Engine, this is the Viral Engine.
Create a "Real-Time AI Salary Transparency" page.
 * Instead of asking companies to post salaries, create a simple form: "I am a [Role] working with [Tool]. I make $[Amount]."
 * Visualize this data: "Developers using LangChain earn 20% more than developers using TensorFlow."
 * The Growth Hack: Post this specific chart to Reddit (r/MachineLearning, r/OpenAI) and Twitter. Developers will click to see if they are underpaid. You get massive traffic, and then retarget them with "See [Tool] Jobs."
Summary of the Workflow
 * Identify 50+ emerging AI tools (not skills).
 * Generate 50+ landing pages targeting "Jobs/Hire" for those specific tools.
 * Populate with AI-written definitions + backfilled general jobs.
 * Capture traffic -> Convert to Email/Talent Collective.
