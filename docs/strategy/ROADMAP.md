# Product Roadmap: The Path to the Protocol

**Status:** Draft
**Date:** 2025-12-03

This roadmap outlines the strategic evolution from a "Niche Job Board" to a "Global Standard for Contribution."

---

## Phase 1: The Open Source Resume (Current - Month 6)
**Goal:** Solve the "Cold Start" problem for elite AI Engineers.
**Strategy:** Prove that a "Passive Portfolio" is superior to a manually written resume.

*   **Q4 2025 (Now):** Verified Portfolio MVP (GitHub Integration).
    *   *Status:* Complete.
    *   *Key Feature:* "Executive Dossier" with verified public claims.
*   **Q1 2026:** The "Impact" Engine.
    *   *Objective:* Move beyond simple "stars" and "commit counts."
    *   *Feature:* Semantic analysis of merged PRs. Detect *what* was built (e.g., "Implemented RAG pipeline," "Optimized CUDA kernel").
    *   *Feature:* "Gold Card" access. Users with >X verified impact get instant interview invites.
*   **Q2 2026:** The "Stack" Graph.
    *   *Feature:* Infer tech stack proficiency not by keywords, but by dependency analysis of repos they contribute to.

---

## Phase 1.5: The "Silver Medalist" Wedge (Month 6 - Month 18)
**Goal:** Capture high-fidelity data from the private market without asking permission.
**Strategy:** The "Rejection Automator" (Trojan Horse).

*   **Q3 2026:** The Browser Extension (Utility).
    *   *Launch:* A free Chrome/Outlook extension for recruiters.
    *   *Value Prop:* "Turn messy notes into safe rejection emails in 1 click."
    *   *Secret:* It structures the rejection data (Skill: Python, Result: Pass, Reason: Budget) and hashes the identity.
*   **Q4 2026:** The "Shadow Graph" (Data).
    *   *Milestone:* Aggregate 100,000 "Silver Medalist" profiles (candidates who passed screens but weren't hired).
*   **Q1 2027:** The "Second Chance" Marketplace.
    *   *Monetization:* Allow recruiters to "unlock" the Silver Medalists of other companies. "Show me candidates who passed the Google Technical Screen last month."

---

## Phase 2: The Corporate Bridge (Month 18 - Year 3)
**Goal:** Unlock the 90% of work that happens in private.
**Strategy:** Zero-Knowledge (ZK) Proofs.

*   **Year 2:** The "Local Agent" (CLI).
    *   *Problem:* Companies won't let us scan their Jira/GitLab.
    *   *Solution:* A CLI tool (`aijobspot-verify`) users run locally on their work machine.
    *   *Mechanism:* It scans the local `.git` folder, calculates stats (lines of code, complexity, test coverage) *locally*, and sends only a cryptographic proof to our servers. "User X proved they wrote Y complexity code."
*   **Year 2:** The "Enterprise Audit."
    *   *Pivot:* Sell this tool to CTOs as a way to measure their *own* team's velocity. The byproduct is verified credentials for their staff.

---

## Phase 3: The Protocol (Year 3+)
**Goal:** `git-attest` becomes a standard.
**Strategy:** Decentralize the verification.

*   **Year 3:** The Protocol Standard.
    *   Submit `git-attest` as an open standard.
    *   Allow *any* platform (GitHub, GitLab, Linear) to sign contributions automatically.
    *   AI Job Spot becomes just one "Indexer" of this global ledger.

---

## The Trillion Dollar Question: "Is it a myth?"

The "Trillion Dollar" outcome relies on one specific constraint: **Effortless Truth.**

*   **The Myth:** If it requires the user to "curate," "build," or "maintain" a portfolio, it will fail. It becomes just another LinkedIn—a noisy, performative game.
*   **The Reality:** It only works if it is a **byproduct of labor**.
    *   The developer pushes code -> *The credential is updated.*
    *   The designer saves a Figma file -> *The credential is updated.*
    *   The lawyer signs a DocuSign -> *The credential is updated.*

If we can achieve **Passive Generation** (Phase 2), the trillion is real. If we stay in **Active Curation** (Phase 1), it is a nice business, but not a protocol.
