THE KILL SHEET: Why This Will Fail
A "Pre-Mortem" analysis. These are the probabilistic reasons you will likely fail to reach $1 Trillion.
1. The "Platform Risk" Guillotine (Probability: 60%)
The Failure: Your entire V1/V2 relies on the GitHub API (or LinkedIn/Salesforce APIs).
 * The Scenario: You get popular. Microsoft (owner of GitHub and LinkedIn) notices you are extracting their data to build a competitive hiring layer.
 * The Kill: They change their Terms of Service: "Use of API for third-party hiring scores is prohibited." Or they simply ban your API keys.
 * Result: Your product breaks overnight. You have 0 revenue.
 * The Fix: You must diversify data sources immediately (GitLab, Jira, StackOverflow) and aggressively lobby for "User Data Sovereignty" (GDPR Article 20 - Data Portability).
2. The "Goodhart's Law" Collapse (Probability: 40%)
The Failure: Measurement corrupts behavior.
 * The Scenario: Developers realize that "Number of PRs" increases their Trust Score.
 * The Behavior: People start breaking 100-line commits into 100 1-line commits. They spam unnecessary code changes. They merge useless branches.
 * The Kill: Employers realize a "High Score" just means "Spammer," not "Expert." Trust in your signal evaporates.
 * Result: The protocol becomes a joke.
 * The Fix: The algorithm must be "Quality Weighted" (e.g., code that stayed in production for 6 months > code that was reverted).
3. The "Cold Start" Freeze (Probability: 30%)
The Failure: The Two-Sided Market Trap.
 * The Scenario: Recruiters won't pay because there aren't enough verified devs. Devs won't verify because no recruiters are looking.
 * The Kill: You burn through your cash trying to market to both sides and run out of runway.
 * Result: A cool side project that never scales.
 * The Fix: The "Spearhead Strategy" (Focus ONLY on Web3 Freelancers first, where trust is non-existent and pain is high).
4. The "Black Mirror" Backlash (Probability: 20%)
The Failure: Social Rejection.
 * The Scenario: A viral tweet says: "This company is turning human creativity into a dystopian credit score. It's dehumanizing."
 * The Kill: Developers boycott the platform. High-quality talent refuses to sign up out of principle.
 * Result: You are left with only desperate, low-quality candidates.
 * The Fix: Branding is critical. Never use the word "Score." Always use "Verified Portfolio" or "Proof of Work." Frame it as empowering the worker against the bias of the resume.

 * Is it easy? No. You are fighting Microsoft (Data ownership) and Culture (How we define worth).
